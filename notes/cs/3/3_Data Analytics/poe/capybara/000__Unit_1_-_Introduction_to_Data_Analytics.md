## Unit 1 - Introduction to Data Analytics

Data Analytics is the process of examining data sets to draw conclusions about the information they contain, with the aid of specialized systems and software. It is a crucial part of modern-day business, as it enables companies to improve their decision-making process by analyzing large data sets. Here are some important points to keep in mind when it comes to data analytics:

- Data analytics is used to analyze large data sets and draw insights that can help businesses make better decisions.
- Data analytics involves different techniques such as data mining, statistical analysis, and predictive modeling.
- With data analytics, companies can gain a competitive advantage by identifying patterns and trends in their data that would otherwise go unnoticed.
- Data analytics can be applied to different areas of business, including marketing, finance, and operations.
- The tools and software used for data analytics include SQL, R, Python, and Tableau.
- Data analytics is not limited to just large corporations. Small businesses can also benefit from data analytics by using tools that are affordable and easy to use.
- Data analytics requires a combination of technical skills and business knowledge. It is important to have a good understanding of the data being analyzed, as well as the business objectives that the analysis is intended to support.
- Data analytics can be used for different purposes, including descriptive analytics (what happened), diagnostic analytics (why did it happen), predictive analytics (what will happen), and prescriptive analytics (what should we do about it).
- Data analytics can also help companies identify and mitigate risks, by identifying potential issues before they become problems.
- Finally, data analytics is an ongoing process. As businesses grow and change, they must continue to analyze their data in order to stay competitive and make informed decisions.
