### CLIQUE and ProCLUS for the notes of the Unit 5 - Frequent Itemsets and Clustering in the subject of Data Analytics

In the field of Data Analytics, the process of clustering is used to group together similar data points. Two popular algorithms used for clustering are CLIQUE and ProCLUS. Let's understand these algorithms in detail:

#### CLIQUE
- CLIQUE stands for Clustering with Local Subspace Affinity.
- It is an efficient clustering algorithm that works well with high dimensional data.
- CLIQUE uses a density-based approach to clustering.
- The algorithm works by finding dense regions in the data space and then grouping them together.
- CLIQUE has two important parameters: the number of dimensions and the minimum number of points that a cluster should contain.
- The algorithm is useful for finding clusters of different sizes and shapes.

#### ProCLUS
- ProCLUS is another clustering algorithm that works well with high dimensional data.
- It is a popular algorithm for clustering large datasets.
- ProCLUS uses a subspace clustering approach.
- The algorithm works by first identifying the most important dimensions in the data and then clustering the data points in those dimensions.
- ProCLUS has two important parameters: the number of subspaces and the minimum number of points that a cluster should contain.
- The algorithm is useful for finding clusters of different sizes and shapes.

Both of these algorithms are useful for clustering high dimensional data. While CLIQUE is a density-based approach, ProCLUS is a subspace-based approach. The choice of algorithm depends on the specific requirements of the problem at hand.
