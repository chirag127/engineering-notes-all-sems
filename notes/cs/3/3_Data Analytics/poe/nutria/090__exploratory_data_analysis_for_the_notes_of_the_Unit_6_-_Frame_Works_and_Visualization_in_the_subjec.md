
### Exploratory Data Analysis for the Notes of Unit 6 - Frameworks and Visualizations in Data Analytics

1. Exploratory data analysis (EDA) is a process of exploring and analyzing data to gain insights and discover patterns.
2. It involves summarizing the data, visualizing it, and detecting outliers and anomalies.
3. EDA is a form of unsupervised machine learning, meaning that it does not require labels or predetermined categories.
4. It is used to identify relationships between variables, detect trends, and detect patterns.
5. Data visualization is an important part of EDA, as it helps to identify patterns and trends in the data.
6. Data visualization tools such as scatter plots, line graphs, and heat maps can be used to explore relationships between variables.
7. EDA can also be used to identify outliers and anomalies in the data.
8. Outliers can be identified by looking for points that are far away from the main cluster of data points.
9. Anomalies can be identified by looking for data points that do not fit the pattern of the other data points.
10. EDA can also be used to identify correlations between variables.
11. Correlations can be identified by looking for patterns in the data, such as a positive or negative relationship between two variables.
12. Finally, EDA can be used to identify causality between variables.
13. Causality can be identified by looking for relationships between variables that are consistent over time.
