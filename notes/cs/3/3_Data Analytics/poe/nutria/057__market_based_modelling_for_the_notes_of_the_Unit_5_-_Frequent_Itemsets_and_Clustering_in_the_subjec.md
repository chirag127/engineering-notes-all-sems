
### Market Based Modelling for Unit 5 - Frequent Itemsets and Clustering in Data Analytics

* Market-based modelling is a technique used to identify patterns in consumer behaviour and preferences by analysing the data associated with market transactions.
* Market-based modelling can be used to identify consumer segments, trends in consumer behaviour, and the relationships between different products and services.
* Market-based modelling can be used to develop strategies to target specific consumer segments, design effective pricing strategies, and identify potential opportunities for new products and services.
* Frequent itemsets are sets of items that occur frequently together in a dataset. They can be used to identify patterns in consumer behaviour and preferences.
* Clustering is a technique used to group similar data points together. It can be used to identify consumer segments, identify relationships between different products and services, and develop strategies to target specific consumer segments.
