
### Need for the Notes of the Unit 2 - Data Analytics Lifecycle in the Subject of Data Analytics

1. Data analytics is a process of collecting, organizing, analyzing, and interpreting data to gain insights and make informed decisions.
2. Data analytics helps organizations to identify trends, patterns, and correlations in their data, which can be used to inform decisions and strategies.
3. Data analytics can help organizations to understand customer behavior and preferences, optimize operations, and improve products and services.
4. Data analytics can also be used to detect fraud and other anomalies in data sets.
5. Data analytics can be used to improve customer experience and loyalty by providing personalized services and products.
6. Data analytics can help organizations to better understand their markets and develop effective marketing campaigns.
7. Data analytics can be used to predict customer behavior and identify opportunities for growth.
8. Data analytics can be used to improve decision-making and business performance.
