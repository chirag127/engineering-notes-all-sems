
### Unit 1 - Introduction to Data Analytics

1. Data analytics is the process of collecting, organizing, and analyzing data to gain insights and make better decisions.
2. Data analytics can be applied to a wide range of fields, including business, finance, healthcare, education, and science.
3. Data analytics can be used to identify trends, predict outcomes, and uncover patterns in data.
4. Data analytics can be used to improve business operations, customer service, marketing strategies, and product development.
5. Data analytics involves the use of various tools, techniques, and technologies to collect, store, process, and analyze data.
6. Data analytics requires a strong understanding of statistics, mathematics, and computer science.
7. Data analytics is used to identify patterns and relationships in data, and to draw conclusions from those patterns.
8. Data analytics can be used to develop predictive models and to optimize processes and operations.
9. Data analytics can be used to gain insights into customer behavior and preferences, to better understand markets, and to develop new products and services.
