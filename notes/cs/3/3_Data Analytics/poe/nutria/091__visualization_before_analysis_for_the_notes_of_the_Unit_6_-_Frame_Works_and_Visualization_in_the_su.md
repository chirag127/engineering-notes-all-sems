
### Visualization Before Analysis

* Visualization is an important step in the data analytics process that helps to identify trends and patterns in data.
* It is important to visualize data before attempting to analyze it because it can help to identify outliers and other anomalies that may affect the analysis.
* Visualization also provides an easy way to compare different datasets and explore relationships between different variables.
* Visualization can be done using a variety of tools, such as bar graphs, line graphs, scatter plots, and heat maps.
* Different types of visualizations can be used to represent different types of data, such as quantitative, categorical, and temporal data.
* It is also important to consider the context of the data when selecting a visualization tool. For example, if the data is temporal, a line graph may be more appropriate than a bar graph.
* Additionally, it is important to consider the audience when selecting a visualization tool. Different types of visualizations may be more effective for different audiences.
