
### S3 for the Notes of the Unit 6 - Frameworks and Visualizations in the Subject of Data Analytics

1. S3 stands for Simple Storage Service and is an object storage service provided by Amazon Web Services (AWS).
2. It is used for storing and retrieving data from a cloud-based storage system.
3. S3 provides a secure, durable, and highly available storage platform for data of any size.
4. It is a pay-as-you-go service with no minimum fee and no setup or maintenance costs.
5. S3 is designed to provide 99.999999999% durability and 99.99% availability of objects over a given year.
6. It supports a variety of data types such as text, images, videos, and audio files.
7. S3 is used for data archiving, backup and disaster recovery, and data analytics.
8. It also supports serverless computing and data warehousing.
9. S3 is integrated with other AWS services such as Amazon Athena, Amazon EMR, and Amazon Redshift.
10. It is also integrated with a variety of third-party services such as Hadoop, MapReduce, and Spark.
