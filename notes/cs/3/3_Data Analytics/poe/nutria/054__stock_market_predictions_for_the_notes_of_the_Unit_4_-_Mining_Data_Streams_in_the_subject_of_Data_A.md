
### Stock Market Predictions for Unit 4 - Mining Data Streams in Data Analytics

1. Stock market prediction is the process of predicting the future direction of stock prices.
2. Market prediction models use historical data and other factors such as economic indicators and sentiment to predict future stock prices.
3. Machine learning algorithms such as artificial neural networks and support vector machines are often used to build stock market prediction models.
4. Data streams, such as news and social media, can provide valuable insights into the stock market.
5. Mining data streams can provide useful information for stock market prediction models.
6. Data streams can be mined using techniques such as natural language processing and sentiment analysis.
7. Stock market prediction models can be evaluated using metrics such as accuracy, precision, recall and F1 score.
8. Stock market prediction models can be used to inform investment decisions.
