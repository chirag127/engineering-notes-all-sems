
### Unit 5 - Frequent Itemsets and Clustering in Data Analytics

1. Frequent Itemset Mining (FIM) is a data mining technique used to identify items that frequently occur together in a dataset. It is used to discover associations between items in a dataset, such as items that are frequently bought together in a store or items that are often mentioned in the same document.

2. Clustering is a data mining technique used to group similar objects together. It is used to identify patterns in data and to group objects that have similar characteristics. Clustering can be used to identify customer segments, to identify similar documents, or to identify similar images.

3. Association Rule Learning (ARL) is a data mining technique used to identify relationships between items in a dataset. It is used to identify associations between items in a dataset, such as items that are frequently bought together in a store or items that are often mentioned in the same document.

4. Classification is a data mining technique used to assign objects to predefined classes. It is used to identify patterns in data and to classify objects into predefined categories. Classification can be used to identify customer segments, to classify documents, or to classify images.

5. Anomaly detection is a data mining technique used to identify objects that are different from the other objects in a dataset. It is used to identify outliers in a dataset, such as customers who spend significantly more than other customers or documents that are significantly different from other documents.
