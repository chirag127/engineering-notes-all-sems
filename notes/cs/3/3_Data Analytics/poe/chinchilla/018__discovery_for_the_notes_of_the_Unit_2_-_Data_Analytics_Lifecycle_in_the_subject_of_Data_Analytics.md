### Discovery for the Notes of the Unit 2 - Data Analytics Lifecycle in the Subject of Data Analytics

In the Data Analytics Lifecycle, the first step is Discovery. In this step, we aim to understand the business problem and the data available to us. The following points explain the process of discovery in detail:

1. Identify the Business Problem: The first step in the discovery process is to identify the business problem. This involves understanding the business question, the stakeholders involved, and the objectives of the project.

2. Gather Data: Once we understand the business problem, we need to gather data relevant to the problem. This can be done by exploring internal and external data sources. We also need to ensure that the data is of good quality and sufficient for our analysis.

3. Data Exploration: After gathering the data, we need to explore it to gain insights into the data. This involves looking at the data distribution, identifying trends, and finding any anomalies or outliers in the data.

4. Data Cleaning: Once we have explored the data, we need to clean it to remove any errors or inconsistencies. This involves handling missing data, removing duplicates, and dealing with outliers.

5. Data Transformation: After cleaning the data, we need to transform it to make it suitable for analysis. This involves converting data types, scaling variables, and creating new variables.

6. Data Visualization: Once the data is cleaned and transformed, we can create visualizations to gain insights into the data. This can help us identify patterns, trends, and relationships in the data.

7. Hypothesis Generation: Based on the insights gained from the data visualization, we can generate hypotheses about the business problem. This involves formulating a hypothesis and testing it using data.

8. Risk Assessment: Finally, we need to assess the risks associated with the project. This involves identifying potential risks and developing plans to mitigate them.

In conclusion, the discovery process is critical in the Data Analytics Lifecycle as it helps us understand the business problem and the data available to us. By following the steps outlined above, we can ensure that we have a good understanding of the data and can generate insights that will help us solve the business problem.
