### Introduction to R for the notes of the Unit 6 - Frame Works and Visualization in the subject of Data Analytics.

1. R is a programming language and software environment for statistical computing and graphics. It is widely used among statisticians and data miners for developing statistical software and data analysis.

2. R is an open source language under the GNU General Public License. It runs on a wide variety of UNIX platforms, Windows and MacOS.

3. Some of the features of R are:

- It has a wide variety of statistical and graphical techniques.
- It is highly extensible through functions and extensions.
- It provides large, coherent, integrated collection of interpolative software tools.
- It is available as Free Software under the terms of the Free Software Foundation's GNU General Public License.

4. Some of the advantages of R are:

- It is freely available and open source.
- It has a large community of users and developers.
- It has graphical facilities for data analysis and display either on-screen or in hardcopy formats or on-line graphics.
- It has tools for both classical and Bayesian statistical modelling.
- It provides interfaces to other languages like C/C++, Java, .NET, Python, etc.

5. Some of the disadvantages of R are:

- It has a steep learning curve.
- It lacks graphical user interface.
- It has limitations in data handling capabilities.
- The package ecosystem can be difficult to navigate.
- The relay on community support can be unreliable at times.

6. R has a wide variety of applications in fields like finance, bioinformatics, social sciences, public health, etc. It is extensively used in various domains for data cleaning and manipulation, numerical simulation, statistical modelling, data visualization, and machine learning.
