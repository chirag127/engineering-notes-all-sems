### Market Based Modelling

1. Market based modelling is the process of analyzing market data to understand patterns and trends. It helps in making business decisions and predictions.
2. Some of the techniques used in market based modelling are:
- Frequent itemset mining: To find associations between products that are frequently bought together. Eg: Beer and diapers.
- Clustering: To segment customers with similar attributes or behaviors into clusters. This helps in targeted marketing.
- Sequential pattern mining: To find patterns in sequences of customer purchases or website clicks. Eg: A customer buying products A, B and C in sequence.
3. The data used for market based modelling is transactional data which contains information about customer purchases, clicks, ratings, reviews etc. This data is then cleaned and analyzed using algorithms to get actionable insights.
4. The insights obtained from market based modelling can help in increasing sales, recommending products, personalized marketing, detecting fraud etc. However, the models need to be continuously retrained as customer behaviors evolve over time.

The above points cover the key aspects of market based modelling for understanding the process and applications. The notes can be used as reference material to learn and understand the concept in depth.
