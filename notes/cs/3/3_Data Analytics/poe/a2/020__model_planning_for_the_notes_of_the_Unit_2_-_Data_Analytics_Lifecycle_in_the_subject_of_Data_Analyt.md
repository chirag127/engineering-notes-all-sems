### Model Planning for Unit 2 - Data Analytics Lifecycle

1. Problem Definition
- Clearly define the business problem. What are you trying to solve or achieve?
- Frame the problem in a measurable and quantifiable manner. What metrics will be used to evaluate a solution?

2. Data Collection
- Determine what data is available and relevant to solving the problem.
- Evaluate if additional data needs to be collected. If so, figure out the best way to collect it.

3. Data Preparation
- Clean and preprocess the data by handling missing values, inconsistent formats, outliers, etc.
- Ensure the data is suitable for modeling by standardizing ranges, normalizing distributions, etc.

4. Modeling
- Choose an initial set of models that could solve the problem.
- Train and evaluate the models and select the best one.
- Tune and optimize the best model.

5. Evaluation
- Evaluate the final model's performance against the defined metrics and problem goals.
- Determine if the results are satisfactory or if further iterations of modeling and optimization are needed.

6. Deployment
- Deploy the final model to production to enable decision making or predictions.
- Continue monitoring model performance and retrain as needed.

The above points cover the key steps in the data analytics lifecycle to plan and build a model to solve a defined business problem. Following a structured lifecycle approach leads to more robust and optimized data science solutions.
