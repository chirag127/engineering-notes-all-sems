### Exploratory Data Analysis for Unit 6 - Frameworks and Visualization

1. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) is the numerical and visual examination of data to understand its main characteristics and detect patterns.
2. EDA is a fundamental step in data analysis to gain insights and understand the data before building models.
3. Some key aspects of EDA are:
- Univariate Analysis: Analyzing individual variables by visualizing their distributions to find outliers or trends.
- Bivariate Analysis: Analyzing the relationship between two variables using scatter plots or joint distributions.
- Multivariate Analysis: Analyzing multiple variables simultaneously to find correlations or patterns.
4. Common EDA techniques are:
- Summary statistics like mean, median, mode, standard deviation, percentiles, etc.
- Data visualizations like histograms, box plots, scatter plots, heat maps, correlation matrices, etc.
5. The goals of EDA are:
- Detect outliers and anomalies
- Check assumptions of statistical models
- Form hypotheses and derive insights
- Prepare data for modeling by preprocessing if required
