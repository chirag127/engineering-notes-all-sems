## Unit 2 - Data Analytics Lifecycle

1. Business Understanding
- Identify business objectives and problems.
- Determine key metrics and KPIs to measure.
- Understand the context and significance of the problem.

2. Data Understanding
- Identify and locate relevant data.
- Assess the quality and suitability of the data.
- Identify any missing or incomplete data.

3. Data Preparation
- Extract, transform and load the data.
- Clean and transform data as required.
- Apply data integration techniques.

4. Modeling
- Choose an analytical modeling technique.
- Train and evaluate multiple models.
- Optimize model parameters for best performance.

5. Evaluation
- Evaluate the analytical results and model performance.
- Compare results to key metrics and business objectives.
- Present findings and recommendations to stakeholders.

6. Deployment
- Integrate analytical results into operational systems.
- Monitor model performance over time and retrain as needed.
- Continue cycling through the lifecycle to improve analytics.
