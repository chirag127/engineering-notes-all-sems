### Filtering Streams

- Filtering is an important step in data stream processing to extract useful information and reduce the data volume.
- Some common filtering techniques for data streams are:

1. Window-based filtering: Data within a sliding window of time/tuples is analyzed to filter out unwanted tuples. Eg: Considering only latest 5 minutes of data.
2. Tuple-based filtering: Each tuple is evaluated against a condition and filtered out if it does not satisfy the condition. Eg: Filtering out tuples with speed less than 30.
3. Pattern matching filters: Look for specific patterns in the stream and filter out tuples that do not match the patterns. Eg: Filtering tuples that do not have a specific sequence of values.

- Filters can be pipelined to apply multiple filters sequentially to achieve complex filtering logic.
- The filter conditions/logic can be dynamically updated to account for evolving patterns in the data stream.
