### Descriptive Statistics for Unit 6 - Frameworks and Visualization

1. Mean - Average of the data. calculated by sum of all values divided by total number of values.
2. Median - Middle value of the ordered data. If two middle values, take the average of two.
3. Mode - Most frequent value in the data.
4. Range - Difference between maximum and minimum values.
5. Variance - Average of squared differences from the mean.
6. Standard Deviation - Square root of variance. Measure of spread/dispersion of data.
7. Quartiles - Divide data into 4 equal parts. Q1 is 25th percentile, Q2 is 50th percentile/median, Q3 is 75th percentile.
8. Interquartile Range - Difference between Q3 and Q1. Ignore outliers and capture middle 50% of data.
9. Percentiles - Divide data into 100 equal parts. pth percentile is value where p% of data is below that value. For example, 80th percentile is value below which 80% of data lies.
10. Box plot - Visualization of minimum, first quartile, median, third quartile and maximum. Helps visualize spread and detect outliers.

The above points cover the key descriptive statistics and concepts to analyze the spread and distribution of data. These can be calculated for a data set and visualized using histograms, box plots, etc. to gain insights into the data.
