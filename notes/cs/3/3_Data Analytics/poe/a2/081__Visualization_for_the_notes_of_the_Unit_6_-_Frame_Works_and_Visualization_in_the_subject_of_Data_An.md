### Visualization for the notes of the Unit 6 - Frame Works and Visualization in the subject of Data Analytics

1. Data Visualization is the presentation of data in a pictorial or graphical format. It enables decision makers to see analytics presented visually, so that they can grasp difficult concepts or identify new patterns.

2. Some of the key types of data visualization are:

- Bar charts: To compare categories of data.
- Line charts: To show trends over time.
- Pie charts: To show proportions of a whole.
- Scatter plots: To show relationships between variables.
- Heat maps: To show data density or intensity.
- Maps: To show geographical data.

3. Tools for data visualization include:

- Matplotlib for basic plotting.
- Seaborn for more advanced visualizations.
- Tableau for an easy to use commercial tool.
- Power BI for a business intelligence tool.
- D3.js for interactive web visualizations.

4. Benefits of data visualization:

- Identify patterns and trends.
- Communicate information more effectively.
- Gain insights faster.
- Enable better decision making.
- Present data in an engaging and relatable way.

5. Key principles for effective data visualization:

- Show comparisons and relationships.
- Use appropriate graphs for the data.
- Keep it simple and minimize clutter.
- Use consistent design and annotations.
- Choose color appropriately.
- Ensure axes and scales are appropriate.
- Provide context for the data.
