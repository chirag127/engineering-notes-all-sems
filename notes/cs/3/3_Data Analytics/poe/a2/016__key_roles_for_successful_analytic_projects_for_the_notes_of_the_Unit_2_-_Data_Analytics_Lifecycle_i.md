### Key Roles for Successful Analytic Projects

1. Business Owner - The business owner is responsible for identifying the business problem or opportunity and determining if analysis is needed. They provide context for the problem and outline key questions to be answered.
2. Data Owner - The data owner is responsible for identifying, acquiring, and preparing the data necessary to address the key questions. They ensure the data is accessible, cleaned, and in a format ready for analysis.
3. Analyst - The analyst is responsible for analyzing the data to find insights and patterns that address the key questions. They select appropriate analytical techniques, create visualizations and summaries, and interpret results.
4. Communicator - The communicator is responsible for conveying the insights and recommendations from the analysis to the appropriate stakeholders. They tailor the message to the audience and facilitate action based on the insights.

For a successful analytic project, these key roles must work together effectively to understand the problem, gather and prepare the data, conduct insightful analysis, and communicate results and recommendations. Strong communication and collaboration across roles is essential to an impactful project.
