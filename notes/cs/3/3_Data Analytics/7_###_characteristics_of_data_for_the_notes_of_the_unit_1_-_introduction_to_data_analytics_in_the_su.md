### characteristics of data for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Data Analytics in the subject of Data Analytics

Data characteristics:
1. Type: categorical, numerical, ordinal, interval, ratio
2. Distribution: normal, skewed, uniform
3. Outliers: presence and impact
4. Missing values: presence and handling
5. Scale: ratio, interval, ordinal
6. Trends: linear, non-linear, seasonality
7. Correlation: positive, negative, no correlation
8. Association: causality, independence
9. Noise: random, systematic
10. Complexity: simple, complex relationships.
