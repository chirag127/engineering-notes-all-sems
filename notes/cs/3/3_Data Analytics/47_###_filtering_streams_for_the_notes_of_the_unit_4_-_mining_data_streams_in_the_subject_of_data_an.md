### filtering streams for the notes of the Unit 4 - Mining Data Streams in the subject of Data Analytics

The filtering streams section of the Unit 4 - Mining Data Streams in the subject of Data Analytics should cover the following topics:

1. Definition of filtering streams (the process of selecting data from a data stream based on certain criteria)
2. Overview of filtering streams (why it is needed, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
3. Characteristics of filtering streams (speed, accuracy, scalability)
4. Techniques for filtering streams (sampling, windowing, clustering)
5. Overview of sampling (the process of selecting a subset of data from a data stream for analysis)
6. Overview of windowing (the process of dividing a data stream into time-based segments for analysis)
7. Overview of clustering (the process of grouping similar data points in a data stream)
8. The role of filtering streams in data analytics (reducing data volume, improving performance, enabling real-time analysis)
9. Applications of filtering streams in different fields (finance, healthcare, marketing)
10. Limitations of filtering streams (bias, subjectivity, measurement error)
11. Techniques for overcoming limitations (validation, reliability testing, sensitivity analysis)
12. Further reading and resources (books, journals, online resources)

Filtering streams is an important component of the subject of Data Analytics. Understanding the principles and techniques of filtering streams helps students to develop the skills required to manage and analyze large amounts of data in real-time. The hands-on practice and further reading resources will help students to develop their skills and deepen their understanding of the material.
