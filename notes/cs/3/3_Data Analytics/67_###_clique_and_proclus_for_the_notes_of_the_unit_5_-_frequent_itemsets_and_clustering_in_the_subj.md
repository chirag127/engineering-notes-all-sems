### CLIQUE and ProCLUS for the notes of the Unit 5 - Frequent Itemsets and Clustering in the subject of Data Analytics

CLIQUE:
- CLIQUE is a frequent itemset mining algorithm.
- It is used to discover patterns in large datasets.
- CLIQUE works by dividing the dataset into sub-databases and finding frequent itemsets in each sub-database.
- CLIQUE is efficient and scalable, making it a popular choice for data mining.

Proclus:
- Proclus is a clustering algorithm.
- It is used to group similar objects in a dataset into clusters.
- Proclus works by finding the density-connected clusters in the data.
- Proclus is efficient and effective, and is often used in data mining and machine learning applications.
