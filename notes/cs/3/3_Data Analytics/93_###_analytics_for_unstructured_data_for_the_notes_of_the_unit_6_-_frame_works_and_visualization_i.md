### analytics for unstructured data for the notes of the Unit 6 - Frame Works and Visualization in the subject of Data Analytics

Unit 6 of the Data Analytics course focuses on Frameworks and Visualization, including the aspect of analytics for unstructured data.

Unstructured data refers to data that does not have a predefined data model or structure, such as text, images, and audio. Analyzing unstructured data is a complex and challenging task, as it requires the use of techniques and tools that can extract meaningful information from the data.

There are several techniques and tools used for analytics of unstructured data, including:
- Text analytics, which involves the analysis of text data, such as sentiment analysis, topic modeling, and named entity recognition.
- Image analytics, which involves the analysis of image data, such as object recognition and image classification.
- Audio analytics, which involves the analysis of audio data, such as speech recognition and speaker identification.

In the context of the Data Analytics course, students in Unit 6 will learn about analytics for unstructured data as part of the discussion on Frameworks and Visualization. This may include working with examples of unstructured data, analyzing the benefits and limitations of analytics for unstructured data, and learning how to implement analytics for unstructured data in a data analytics project.
