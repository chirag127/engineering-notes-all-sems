### Case studies for the notes of the Unit 4 - Mining Data Streams in the subject of Data Analytics

Unit 4 of the Data Analytics course focuses on Mining Data Streams, including the aspect of case studies.

Data stream mining is a subfield of data mining that deals with the analysis of data streams, which are continuous, high-volume, and fast-moving data sources. Case studies are real-world examples of data stream mining problems and solutions, and provide an opportunity for students to apply the concepts and techniques learned in the course to real-world data.

Examples of data stream mining case studies include:
- Monitoring network traffic to detect and prevent cyber attacks
- Detecting credit card fraud in real-time
- Analyzing data from wearable devices to monitor health and fitness
- Monitoring stock prices to make investment decisions

In the context of the Data Analytics course, students in Unit 4 will learn about data stream mining through case studies. This may include working with real-world data, applying data stream mining techniques, and analyzing the results.
