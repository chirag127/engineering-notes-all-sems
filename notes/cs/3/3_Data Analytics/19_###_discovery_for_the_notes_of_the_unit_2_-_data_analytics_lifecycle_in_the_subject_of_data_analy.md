### discovery for the notes of the Unit 2 - Data Analytics Lifecycle in the subject of Data Analytics

Data Analytics Lifecycle:
1. Problem Definition: Identifying the business problem to be solved through data analytics.
2. Data Collection: Gathering relevant data from various sources.
3. Data Preparation: Cleaning, transforming, and pre-processing data to make it usable for analysis.
4. Data Exploration: Analyzing the data to understand its characteristics and relationships.
5. Modeling: Developing mathematical models to solve the problem defined in step 1.
6. Evaluation: Assessing the performance of the model and making necessary improvements.
7. Deployment: Implementing the model in a production environment to solve the problem.
8. Monitoring: Regularly monitoring the model's performance and making necessary updates.
