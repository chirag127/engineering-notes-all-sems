### Introduction to R for the notes of the Unit 6 - Frame Works and Visualization in the subject of Data Analytics

Unit 6 of the Data Analytics course focuses on Frameworks and Visualization, including the aspect of Introduction to R.

R is a programming language and software environment for statistical computing and graphics. It is widely used in data analytics, data science, and machine learning, and is known for its rich libraries and packages for data analysis and visualization.

In the context of the Data Analytics course, students in Unit 6 will learn about Introduction to R as part of the discussion on Frameworks and Visualization. This may include learning the basics of the R programming language, working with R data structures and functions, and learning how to use R to perform data analysis and visualization.
