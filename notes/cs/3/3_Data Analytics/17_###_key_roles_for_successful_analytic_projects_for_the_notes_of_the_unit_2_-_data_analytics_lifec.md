### key roles for successful analytic projects for the notes of the Unit 2 - Data Analytics Lifecycle in the subject of Data Analytics

Key roles in successful analytic projects: 
1. Project Manager: responsible for defining project scope, timeline, budget, and ensuring project delivery. 
2. Data Analyst: responsible for collecting, cleaning, and transforming data for analysis. 
3. Business Analysts: responsible for understanding business requirements and translating them into analytics projects. 
4. Data Scientist: responsible for developing models, algorithms, and visualizations to extract insights from data. 
5. Technical Lead: responsible for ensuring technical feasibility and providing technical guidance. 
6. Stakeholders: responsible for providing input and feedback on project requirements and results. 
7. Subject Matter Experts: responsible for providing domain knowledge and ensuring accuracy of results.
