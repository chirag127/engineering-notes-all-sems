### Need for the notes of the Unit 2 - Data Analytics Lifecycle in the subject of Data Analytics

The notes of Unit 2 - Data Analytics Lifecycle in the subject of Data Analytics are important because they provide a comprehensive overview of the entire data analytics process. This process includes the following stages:

1. Data Collection
2. Data Preparation
3. Data Exploration and Visualization
4. Model Building
5. Model Deployment and Monitoring

Having detailed notes of each stage will help students understand the importance of each stage and how they contribute to the overall success of a data analytics project. Furthermore, the notes will provide a roadmap for students to follow when working on their own data analytics projects, ensuring that they cover all the necessary steps and avoid common pitfalls. Ultimately, having detailed notes of the data analytics lifecycle will help students become more proficient and effective data analysts.
