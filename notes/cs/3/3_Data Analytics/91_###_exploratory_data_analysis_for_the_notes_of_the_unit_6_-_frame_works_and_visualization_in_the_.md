### exploratory data analysis for the notes of the Unit 6 - Frame Works and Visualization in the subject of Data Analytics

Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) is a process of analyzing data in order to summarize its main characteristics and uncover relationships and patterns in the data. It is an important step in the data analytics process, as it helps to understand the data and identify potential problems and opportunities.

EDA involves several steps, including:

1. Data cleaning: The first step is to clean the data, removing any missing or incorrect data.

2. Data visualization: The next step is to visualize the data, using techniques such as histograms, scatter plots, and box plots, among others.

3. Data summarization: The final step is to summarize the data, using techniques such as mean, median, and standard deviation, among others.

EDA is an iterative process, and it is often necessary to repeat the steps multiple times in order to gain a complete understanding of the data.
