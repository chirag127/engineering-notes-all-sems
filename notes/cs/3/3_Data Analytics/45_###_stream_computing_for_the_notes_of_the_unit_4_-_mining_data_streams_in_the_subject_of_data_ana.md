### stream computing for the notes of the Unit 4 - Mining Data Streams in the subject of Data Analytics

Stream computing refers to processing of data in real-time as it is generated, rather than in batch mode. It is used in applications where data is generated continuously, such as stock market data, social media data, and IoT sensor data. Stream computing systems must handle high velocity, high volume, and high variety of data. They use algorithms such as filtering, aggregation, and windowing to analyze the data in real-time. The results of the analysis can be used for immediate decision-making, such as triggering an alert or updating a dashboard. Stream computing is often used in combination with big data technologies such as Hadoop and Spark for large-scale data processing.
