### stochastic search methods for the notes of the Unit 3 - Data Analysis in the subject of Data Analytics

Stochastic search methods are optimization algorithms that use random sampling to find the global optimum of a function. They are often used in data analysis to find the best parameters for a model. Examples include:

1. Random search: randomly samples parameter values from a predefined distribution.

2. Simulated Annealing: uses a cooling schedule to control the randomness of the search, allowing for a balance between exploration and exploitation.

3. Genetic algorithms: use principles of natural selection and genetic recombination to evolve a population of candidate solutions.

4. Particle swarm optimization: models the behavior of a swarm of particles, each representing a candidate solution, to explore the search space.

5. Bayesian optimization: uses a probabilistic model to guide the search, balancing exploration and exploitation based on the uncertainty of the model.

These methods can be used to optimize various types of models, including linear regression, logistic regression, neural networks, and more. They can also be used to optimize complex, non-linear models, where traditional optimization methods may fail.
