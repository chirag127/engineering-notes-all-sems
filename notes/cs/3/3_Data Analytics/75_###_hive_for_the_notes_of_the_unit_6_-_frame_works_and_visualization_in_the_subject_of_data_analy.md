### Hive for the notes of the Unit 6 - Frame Works and Visualization in the subject of Data Analytics

The Hive section of the Unit 6 - Frameworks and Visualization in the subject of Data Analytics should cover the following topics:

1. Definition of Hive (an open-source data warehousing framework for Hadoop)
2. Overview of Hive (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
3. Characteristics of Hive (scalable, flexible, SQL-like interface)
4. Hive architecture (metastore, driver, executors)
5. Overview of metastore (a centralized repository for metadata)
6. Overview of driver (coordinates query execution)
7. Overview of executors (run the tasks in parallel)
8. Hive data types (primitive, complex)
9. Hive data storage (internal tables, external tables)
10. Hive query language (HiveQL, similar to SQL)
11. Hive functions (built-in, user-defined)
12. Hive performance tuning (indexing, partitioning, bucketing)
13. The role of Hive in data analytics (large-scale data processing, data warehousing, business intelligence)
14. Applications of Hive in different fields (marketing, finance, healthcare)
15. Limitations of Hive (complexity, performance, security)
16. Techniques for overcoming limitations (optimizing queries, using Tez or Spark as execution engine, using Ranger for security)
17. Further reading and resources (books, journals, online resources)

Hive is an important framework for the subject of Data Analytics. Understanding the principles and techniques of Hive helps students to develop the skills required to process and analyze large amounts of data in a scalable and flexible manner. The hands-on practice and further reading resources will help students to develop their skills and deepen their understanding of the material.
