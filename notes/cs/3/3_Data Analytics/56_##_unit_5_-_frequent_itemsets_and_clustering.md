## Unit 5 - Frequent Itemsets and Clustering

Unit 5 of the Data Analytics course focuses on Frequent Itemsets and Clustering.

Frequent Itemsets is a technique in data mining that is used to identify items that frequently occur together in a dataset. This information can be used to make recommendations, such as suggesting products to customers based on their past purchases.

Clustering is a technique in data analysis that is used to group similar data points together into clusters. Clustering can be used for a variety of purposes, such as market segmentation, image segmentation, and anomaly detection.

In the context of the Data Analytics course, students in Unit 5 will learn about Frequent Itemsets and Clustering, including working with examples of each technique, analyzing the benefits and limitations of each technique, and learning how to implement each technique to solve specific problems.

In summary, Unit 5 of the Data Analytics course focuses on Frequent Itemsets and Clustering. Frequent Itemsets is a technique in data mining used to identify items that frequently occur together, and Clustering is a technique in data analysis used to group similar data points together. Students will learn about Frequent Itemsets and Clustering through interactive activities and hands-on experiences, and will learn how to implement each technique to solve specific problems.
