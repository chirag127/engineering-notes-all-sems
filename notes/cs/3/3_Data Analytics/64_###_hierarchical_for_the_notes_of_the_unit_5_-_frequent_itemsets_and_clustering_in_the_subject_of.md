### hierarchical for the notes of the Unit 5 - Frequent Itemsets and Clustering in the subject of Data Analytics

Unit 5 of the Data Analytics course focuses on Frequent Itemsets and Clustering, including the aspect of hierarchical clustering.

Hierarchical clustering is a type of clustering algorithm that creates a hierarchy of clusters, where each cluster is a subset of the previous cluster. Hierarchical clustering can be divided into two types: agglomerative and divisive.

Agglomerative hierarchical clustering starts with each data point as its own cluster, and then iteratively combines the closest clusters until all data points are in a single cluster.

Divisive hierarchical clustering starts with all data points in a single cluster, and then iteratively splits the cluster into smaller clusters until each data point is in its own cluster.

Hierarchical clustering is useful for exploring the relationships between data points, and can be used in a variety of applications, such as image segmentation and gene expression analysis.

In the context of the Data Analytics course, students in Unit 5 will learn about hierarchical clustering as part of the discussion on Clustering. This may include working with examples of hierarchical clustering, analyzing the benefits and limitations of hierarchical clustering, and learning how to implement hierarchical clustering in a data analysis algorithm.
