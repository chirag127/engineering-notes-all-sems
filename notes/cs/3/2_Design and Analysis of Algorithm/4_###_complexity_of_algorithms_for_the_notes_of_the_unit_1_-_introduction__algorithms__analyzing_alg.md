### Complexity of Algorithms for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction: Algorithms, Analyzing Algorithms, Complexity of Algorithms, Growth of Functions, Performance Measurements, Sorting and Order Statistics - Shell Sort, Quick Sort, Merge Sort, Heap Sort, Comparison of Sorting Algorithms, Sorting in Linear Time. in the subject of Design and Analysis of Algorithm

Unit 1 of the Design and Analysis of Algorithm course focuses on the introduction to algorithms, including the aspect of Complexity of Algorithms.

Complexity of Algorithms refers to the amount of resources (such as time and memory) that an algorithm requires to solve a problem. The complexity of an algorithm can be analyzed in terms of the size of the input data and the amount of computational resources required to solve the problem.

There are two main measures of complexity: time complexity and space complexity. Time complexity refers to the amount of time an algorithm takes to solve a problem, while space complexity refers to the amount of memory an algorithm requires to solve a problem.

In the context of the Design and Analysis of Algorithm course, students in Unit 1 will learn about the Complexity of Algorithms as part of the introduction to algorithms. This may include working with examples of algorithms to analyze their time and space complexity, learning techniques for analyzing the complexity of algorithms, and learning how to compare the complexity of different algorithms.
