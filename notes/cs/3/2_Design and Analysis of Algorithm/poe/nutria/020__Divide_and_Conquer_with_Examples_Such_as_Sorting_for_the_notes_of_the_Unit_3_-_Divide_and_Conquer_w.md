
### Divide and Conquer with Examples Such as Sorting 

Divide and Conquer is a technique used to design algorithms. It involves breaking a problem into smaller sub-problems and solving each of them. This technique can be used to solve many problems, such as sorting, matrix multiplication, convex hull, and searching. 

Examples of Divide and Conquer algorithms include: 

* Sorting algorithms such as quicksort, mergesort, and heapsort 
* Matrix multiplication algorithms such as Strassen's algorithm 
* Convex hull algorithms such as Jarvis March 
* Searching algorithms such as binary search 

### Greedy Methods with Examples Such as Optimal Reliability Allocation 

Greedy methods are a type of algorithm that makes the best decision at each step without considering the future. They are often used to solve optimization problems, such as finding the shortest path or the most reliable allocation. 

Examples of Greedy algorithms include: 

* Optimal reliability allocation algorithms such as the Hungarian algorithm 
* Knapsack problem algorithms such as the 0-1 knapsack problem 
* Minimum spanning tree algorithms such as Prim's and Kruskal's algorithms 
* Single source shortest path algorithms such as Dijkstra's and Bellman-Ford algorithms
