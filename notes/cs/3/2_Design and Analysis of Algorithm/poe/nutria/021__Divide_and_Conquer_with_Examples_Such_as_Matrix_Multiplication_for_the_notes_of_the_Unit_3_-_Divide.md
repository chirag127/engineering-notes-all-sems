
### Divide and Conquer with Examples Such as Matrix Multiplication

Divide and Conquer is a well-known algorithmic paradigm which works by recursively breaking down a problem into two or more sub-problems of the same or related type, until these become simple enough to be solved directly.

Examples of Divide and Conquer algorithms include Sorting, Matrix Multiplication, Convex Hull and Searching.

### Greedy Methods with Examples Such as Optimal Reliability Allocation

Greedy methods are algorithmic paradigms which work by making the locally optimal choice at each stage with the hope of finding a global optimum.

Examples of Greedy algorithms include Optimal Reliability Allocation, Knapsack, Minimum Spanning Trees – Prim’s and Kruskal’s Algorithms, Single Source Shortest Paths - Dijkstra’s and Bellman Ford Algorithms.
