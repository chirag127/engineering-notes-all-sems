

### Growth of Functions

* Growth of functions is a concept in computer science that describes the rate at which the running time of an algorithm increases as the size of the input increases. 
* It is used to compare the relative efficiency of different algorithms.
* There are three common growth functions: linear, quadratic, and logarithmic. 
* Linear growth functions have a running time that increases linearly with the size of the input. 
* Quadratic growth functions have a running time that increases quadratically with the size of the input. 
* Logarithmic growth functions have a running time that increases logarithmically with the size of the input.
* Algorithms with linear or logarithmic growth are considered to be more efficient than those with quadratic growth. 
* Shell Sort, Quick Sort, Merge Sort, Heap Sort and Sorting in Linear Time are all examples of algorithms with logarithmic growth. 
* The comparison of sorting algorithms is an important part of the design and analysis of algorithms.
