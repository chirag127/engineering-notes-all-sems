NP-completeness and approximation algorithms are two important concepts in the field of algorithm design and analysis. NP-completeness refers to a class of problems that are considered to be computationally intractable, meaning that there is no known algorithm that can solve these problems in a reasonable amount of time.

Approximation algorithms, on the other hand, are algorithms that are designed to solve NP-complete problems by finding a solution that is close to the optimal solution, but not necessarily the exact optimal solution. These algorithms are used when it is not possible to find the exact optimal solution in a reasonable amount of time.
