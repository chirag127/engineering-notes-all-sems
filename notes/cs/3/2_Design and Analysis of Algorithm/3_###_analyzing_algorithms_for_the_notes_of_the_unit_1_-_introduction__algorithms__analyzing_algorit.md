Unit 1 of the Design and Analysis of Algorithms course focuses on the Introduction, including the aspect of Analyzing Algorithms.

Analyzing algorithms is a process of evaluating the performance of algorithms in terms of their time and space complexity. The goal of analyzing algorithms is to determine the efficiency of algorithms, and to compare different algorithms to determine the best one for a given problem.

There are several methods for analyzing algorithms, including counting the number of basic operations performed by the algorithm, using mathematical models to estimate the running time of the algorithm, and measuring the actual running time of the algorithm on a computer.

In the context of the Design and Analysis of Algorithms course, students in Unit 1 will learn about Analyzing Algorithms as part of the discussion on Introduction. This may include working with examples of analyzing algorithms, analyzing the benefits and limitations of different methods for analyzing algorithms, and learning how to analyze algorithms to determine their efficiency.
