1. Sorting the components by their reliability: The components are sorted in descending order of their reliability, so that the most reliable components are considered first.

2. Allocating the budget: The budget is allocated to the components one by one, starting with the most reliable component, until the budget is exhausted.

3. Computing the system reliability: The reliability of the system is computed based on the reliability of the components and the budget that has been allocated to each component.
