### Haptic Displays for the notes of the Unit 2 - HARDWARE TECHNOLOGIES FOR 3D USER INTERFACES in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

Unit 2 of the Augmented & Virtual Reality course focuses on Hardware Technologies for 3D User Interfaces and covers the aspect of Haptic Displays.

Haptic displays are devices that provide haptic feedback, or touch feedback, to the user. In a virtual reality or augmented reality environment, haptic displays can provide the user with a sense of touch, allowing them to interact with virtual objects as if they were real.

There are several types of haptic displays, including force feedback devices, vibrotactile devices, and electro-tactile devices. Force feedback devices use actuators to apply forces to the user's hand, simulating the sensation of touch. Vibrotactile devices use vibration to provide haptic feedback, simulating the sensation of touch. Electro-tactile devices use electrical stimulation to activate the nerves in the skin, simulating the sensation of touch.

Haptic displays can provide a more immersive and interactive experience in virtual and augmented reality environments, allowing the user to touch, feel, and manipulate virtual objects. Haptic displays can also be used for a variety of applications, including gaming, training, and product design.

In the context of the Augmented & Virtual Reality course, students in Unit 2 will learn about haptic displays as part of the discussion on Hardware Technologies for 3D User Interfaces. This may include working with examples of haptic displays, analyzing the benefits and limitations of haptic displays, and learning how to design and implement haptic displays.
