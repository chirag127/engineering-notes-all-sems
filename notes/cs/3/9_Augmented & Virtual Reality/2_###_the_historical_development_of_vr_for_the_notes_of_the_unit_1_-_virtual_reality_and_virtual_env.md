### The historical development of VR for the notes of the Unit 1 - VIRTUAL REALITY AND VIRTUAL ENVIRONMENTS in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

The Historical Development of Virtual Reality section of the Unit 1 - Virtual Reality and Virtual Environments in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality should cover the following topics:

1. Definition of Virtual Reality (a simulated environment that can be interacted with in a seemingly real or physical way)
2. Overview of Virtual Reality (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
3. The history of Virtual Reality (the evolution of VR technology from its inception to the present day)
4. Early VR systems (1960s-1980s, including the first VR systems, head-mounted displays, and data gloves)
5. 1990s-2000s (the development of VR systems with improved graphics, interaction, and immersion)
6. 2010s-present (the advent of consumer VR systems, such as Oculus Rift and HTC Vive)
7. Key players in the development of VR (researchers, companies, institutions)
8. Limitations of early VR systems (cost, technical limitations, limited applications)
9. Advancements in VR technology (improved graphics, interaction, and immersion)
10. VR and Industry Applications (how VR is used in various industries, such as gaming, education, and medicine)
11. Further reading and resources (books, articles, online resources)

The Historical Development of Virtual Reality is an important component of the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality. Understanding the evolution of VR technology helps students to appreciate the progress that has been made and the challenges that still need to be addressed. The hands-on practice and further reading resources will help students to develop their skills and deepen their understanding of the material.
