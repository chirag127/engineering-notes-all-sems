### Lights and Cameras for the notes of the Unit 4 - SOFTWARE TECHNOLOGIES in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

Lights:
- Illuminate the scene in AR/VR environments to add realism
- Can be static or dynamic, controlled by software algorithms
- Types: ambient, directional, point, spot

Cameras:
- Capture images/video of the real world to be used in AR/VR
- Can be static or moving, determined by the application
- Types: monocular, stereo, RGB-D, 360-degree
- Essential for AR/VR tracking and mapping of the environment.
