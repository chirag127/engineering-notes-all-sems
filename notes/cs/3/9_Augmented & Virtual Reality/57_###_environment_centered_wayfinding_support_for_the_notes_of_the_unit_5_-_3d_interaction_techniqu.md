### Environment Centered Wayfinding Support for the notes of the Unit 5 - 3D INTERACTION TECHNIQUES in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

Environment Centered Wayfinding Support is a technique used to help users navigate and find their way within virtual environments. It is based on the idea of providing users with information and support that is centered around the virtual environment, rather than the user.

The following are some of the key concepts related to Environment Centered Wayfinding Support:

1. 3D Maps: 3D maps provide a visual representation of the virtual environment, including the layout of the environment and the location of objects and landmarks.

2. Navigation Aids: Navigation aids provide users with information and support to help them navigate the virtual environment, such as markers, arrows, or signs.

3. Contextual Information: Contextual information provides users with information about the virtual environment, such as the names of objects, locations, or landmarks.

4. Wayfinding Strategies: Wayfinding strategies are techniques used to help users find their way within the virtual environment, such as following a path, using landmarks, or using a map.

5. User Feedback: User feedback provides users with information about their progress and location within the virtual environment, such as a compass or GPS.

In this unit, you will learn about Environment Centered Wayfinding Support, and you will learn how to use 3D maps, navigation aids, contextual information, wayfinding strategies, and user feedback to help users navigate and find their way within virtual environments.

This unit will provide a foundation for understanding the principles and practices of wayfinding in virtual environments, and for exploring the various concepts and techniques used in the field of Augmented and Virtual Reality.
