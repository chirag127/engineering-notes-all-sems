### Augmented reality methods for the notes of the Unit 8 - Augmented and Mixed Reality in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

Augmented Reality (AR) methods:
1. Image recognition and tracking: using a camera to recognize and track images in real-time.
2. GPS and compass: using GPS and compass data to determine the position and orientation of the device.
3. Marker-based AR: using special markers to trigger AR content.
4. Markerless AR: using computer vision algorithms to recognize and track objects in the real-world without markers.
5. Projection-based AR: projecting virtual objects onto real-world surfaces.
6. Superimposition-based AR: overlaying virtual objects onto the real world in real-time.
