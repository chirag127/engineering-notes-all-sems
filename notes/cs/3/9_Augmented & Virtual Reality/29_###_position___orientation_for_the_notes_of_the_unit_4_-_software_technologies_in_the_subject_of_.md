### Position / Orientation for the notes of the Unit 4 - SOFTWARE TECHNOLOGIES in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

Position and orientation are two important concepts in augmented and virtual reality. 

Position refers to the location of an object in 3D space, relative to a coordinate system. It is typically represented by a set of x, y, and z coordinates. 

Orientation refers to the direction in which an object is facing, also in 3D space. It is typically represented by a set of rotations around the x, y, and z axes. 

In the context of augmented and virtual reality, position and orientation are used to determine the placement and movement of virtual objects in the real world. They are also used to track the movement and orientation of physical objects, such as a user's head or hand, in virtual environments. 

Both position and orientation are important for creating realistic and immersive experiences in augmented and virtual reality. They are used to create a sense of presence and to ensure that virtual objects behave as expected in the real world.

Overall, understanding position and orientation is crucial for developing software technologies in the field of augmented and virtual reality.
