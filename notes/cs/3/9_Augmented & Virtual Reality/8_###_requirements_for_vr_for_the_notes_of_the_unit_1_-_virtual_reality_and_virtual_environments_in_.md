### Requirements for VR for the notes of the Unit 1 - VIRTUAL REALITY AND VIRTUAL ENVIRONMENTS in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

Virtual Reality (VR) is a technology that creates a simulated environment that can be experienced by a user through a headset or other device. To create a high-quality VR experience, the following requirements must be met:

1. Display: A high-resolution display is necessary to create a clear and immersive VR experience.

2. Tracking: Accurate tracking of the user's head and hand movements is necessary to provide a seamless and responsive VR experience.

3. Graphics: High-quality graphics are necessary to create a realistic and believable VR environment.

4. Audio: High-quality audio is necessary to create an immersive VR experience, and it should be spatialized to match the user's movements in the VR environment.

5. Interactivity: The VR environment should be interactive, allowing the user to interact with objects and environments in a natural and intuitive way.

6. Comfort: The VR headset should be comfortable to wear for extended periods of time, and it should not cause discomfort or motion sickness.

In this unit, you will learn about the requirements for VR, and you will learn how to create a high-quality VR experience. You will also learn about the concepts of display, tracking, graphics, audio, interactivity, and comfort, and you will learn how these concepts are used in VR.

This unit will provide a foundation for understanding the principles and practices of VR, and for exploring the various concepts and techniques used in the field of Augmented & Virtual Reality.
