### Real-time computer graphics for the notes of the Unit 1 - VIRTUAL REALITY AND VIRTUAL ENVIRONMENTS in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

The Real-time computer graphics section of the Unit 1 - Virtual Reality and Virtual Environments in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality should cover the following topics:

1. Definition of Real-time computer graphics (interactive computer graphics that are updated in real-time)
2. Overview of Real-time computer graphics (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
3. Graphics Pipeline (the process of transforming 3D models into 2D images)
4. Overview of Graphics Pipeline (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
5. Graphics Processing Units (GPUs) (specialized hardware that performs the calculations required for real-time graphics)
6. Overview of GPUs (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
7. Graphics APIs (Application Programming Interfaces) (programming interfaces that provide access to GPU functionality)
8. Overview of Graphics APIs (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
9. Shading (the process of adding color and texture to 3D models)
10. Overview of Shading (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
11. Lighting (the process of adding light to 3D models)
12. Overview of Lighting (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
13. Shadows (the effect of light and object occlusion on the appearance of 3D models)
14. Overview of Shadows (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
15. Real-time computer graphics and Virtual Reality (how real-time computer graphics are used in virtual reality)
16. Limitations of Real-time computer graphics (computational complexity, performance, accuracy)
17. Techniques for overcoming limitations (using advanced techniques, improving accuracy, etc.)
18. Further reading and resources (books, articles, online resources)

Real-time computer graphics is an important component of the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality. Understanding the principles and techniques of real-time computer graphics helps students to develop the skills required to design and implement virtual environments. The hands-on practice and further reading resources will help students to develop their skills and deepen their understanding of the material.
