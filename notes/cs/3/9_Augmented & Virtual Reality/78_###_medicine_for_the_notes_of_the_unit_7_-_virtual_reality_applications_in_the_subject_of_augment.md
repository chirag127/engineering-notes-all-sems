### Medicine for the notes of the Unit 7 - VIRTUAL REALITY APPLICATIONS in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

Virtual Reality (VR) has been applied to various fields in medicine, including diagnosis, therapy, training, and education. The following are some of the key concepts related to VR in medicine:

1. Diagnosis: VR can be used to diagnose medical conditions, such as visualizing internal organs or simulating symptoms.

2. Therapy: VR can be used to treat medical conditions, such as exposure therapy for phobias or pain management.

3. Training: VR can be used to train medical professionals, such as practicing surgical procedures or learning anatomy.

4. Education: VR can be used to educate patients and the general public about medical conditions and treatments.

5. Telemedicine: VR can be used to provide remote medical consultations, such as virtual visits with a doctor or specialist.

6. Research: VR can be used to conduct medical research, such as studying the effects of drugs or treatments on the body.

In this unit, you will learn about VR applications in medicine, and you will learn how VR is used to diagnose, treat, train, educate, and conduct research in the field of medicine. You will also learn about the key concepts related to VR in medicine, including diagnosis, therapy, training, education, telemedicine, and research, and you will learn how these concepts are used in VR applications in medicine.

This unit will provide a foundation for understanding the principles and practices of VR in medicine, and for exploring the various concepts and techniques used in the field of Augmented and Virtual Reality.
