### Virtual environments for the notes of the Unit 1 - VIRTUAL REALITY AND VIRTUAL ENVIRONMENTS in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

The Virtual Environments section of the Unit 1 - Virtual Reality and Virtual Environments in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality should cover the following topics:

1. Definition of Virtual Environments (computer-generated environments designed to simulate real or imagined environments)
2. Overview of Virtual Environments (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
3. Types of Virtual Environments (immersive, non-immersive, augmented, etc.)
4. Overview of Types of Virtual Environments (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
5. Virtual Reality Head-Mounted Displays (HMDs) (devices used to display virtual environments)
6. Overview of Virtual Reality Head-Mounted Displays (HMDs) (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
7. Input Devices (devices used to interact with virtual environments)
8. Overview of Input Devices (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
9. 3D Modeling (the process of creating 3D objects and environments for virtual environments)
10. Overview of 3D Modeling (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
11. Virtual Environment Design (the process of designing virtual environments)
12. Overview of Virtual Environment Design (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
13. Virtual Environment Applications (how virtual environments are used in various industries)
14. Limitations of Virtual Environments (computational complexity, lack of realism, etc.)
15. Techniques for overcoming limitations (using advanced techniques, improving accuracy, etc.)
16. Further reading and resources (books, articles, online resources)

Virtual Environments are an important component of the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality. Understanding the principles and techniques of virtual environments helps students to develop the skills required to design and implement virtual reality systems. The hands-on practice and further reading resources will help students to develop their skills and deepen their understanding of the material.
