### marker-less tracking for augmented reality for the notes of the Unit 8 - Augmented and Mixed Reality in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

Unit 8 of the Augmented & Virtual Reality course focuses on Augmented and Mixed Reality and covers the aspect of marker-less tracking for augmented reality.

Marker-less tracking is a technique used in augmented reality to track the position and orientation of objects or the environment without the use of physical markers. Instead of using markers, marker-less tracking relies on computer vision algorithms to identify and track features in the environment, such as edges, corners, and textures.

Marker-less tracking is an important aspect of augmented reality, as it allows for more natural and immersive experiences by eliminating the need for physical markers. Marker-less tracking can be used in a variety of applications, including mobile augmented reality, head-mounted displays, and projection-based augmented reality.

In the context of the Augmented & Virtual Reality course, students in Unit 8 will learn about marker-less tracking for augmented reality as part of the discussion on Augmented and Mixed Reality. This may include working with examples of marker-less tracking, analyzing the benefits and limitations of marker-less tracking, and learning how to implement marker-less tracking in augmented reality applications.
