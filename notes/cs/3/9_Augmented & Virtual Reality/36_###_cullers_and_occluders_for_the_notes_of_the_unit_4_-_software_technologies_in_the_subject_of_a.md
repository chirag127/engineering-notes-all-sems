### Cullers and Occluders for the notes of the Unit 4 - SOFTWARE TECHNOLOGIES in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

Cullers and Occluders are technologies used in Augmented and Virtual Reality to optimize the rendering of 3D graphics.

Cullers are algorithms that determine which objects are visible and which are not, based on the camera's position and the position of the objects in the 3D space. By only rendering the visible objects, cullers improve the performance of the system, reducing the amount of processing required to render each frame.

Occluders are objects in the 3D scene that block the view of other objects, creating the illusion of depth and occlusion. Occluders are used to improve the realism of the scene and to reduce the number of objects that need to be rendered, further improving performance.

Both Cullers and Occluders are important components of Augmented and Virtual Reality systems, helping to create more immersive and believable experiences for users.
