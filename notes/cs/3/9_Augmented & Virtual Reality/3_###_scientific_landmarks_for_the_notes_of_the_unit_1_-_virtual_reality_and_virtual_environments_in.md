### Scientific landmarks for the notes of the Unit 1 - VIRTUAL REALITY AND VIRTUAL ENVIRONMENTS in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

Unit 1 of the Augmented & Virtual Reality course focuses on Virtual Reality and Virtual Environments and covers the aspect of scientific landmarks.

A scientific landmark is a significant achievement or discovery in a particular field of study that advances the understanding of the field and opens up new avenues for research.

In the field of virtual reality and virtual environments, there have been several scientific landmarks that have shaped the development of the field. Some of these landmarks include:

1. The first head-mounted display (HMD) - The first HMD was developed in the 1960s, and paved the way for the creation of immersive virtual environments.

2. The development of data gloves - Data gloves were developed in the 1980s and allowed users to interact with virtual environments in a more natural and intuitive way.

3. The creation of the CAVE - The CAVE (Cave Automatic Virtual Environment) was created in the 1990s and was one of the first immersive virtual reality systems.

4. The development of VRML - VRML (Virtual Reality Modeling Language) was developed in the 1990s and allowed for the creation of interactive 3D virtual environments on the web.

5. The creation of the Oculus Rift - The Oculus Rift was created in the 2010s and was one of the first commercially successful virtual reality headsets.

In the context of the Augmented & Virtual Reality course, students in Unit 1 will learn about these scientific landmarks as part of the discussion on Virtual Reality and Virtual Environments. This may include working with examples of the scientific landmarks, analyzing the impact of the scientific landmarks on the field of virtual reality and virtual environments, and learning how the scientific landmarks have influenced the development of the field.
