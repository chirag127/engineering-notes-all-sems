### Science for the notes of the Unit 7 - VIRTUAL REALITY APPLICATIONS in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

Science of Virtual Reality:
- VR technology uses computer-generated simulations to create a 3D environment that can be interacted with in a seemingly real way.
- VR systems use a combination of hardware (e.g. VR headset, gloves) and software to create a sense of presence in the virtual environment.
- VR can be used in a variety of fields such as gaming, education, therapy, and more.

Applications of Virtual Reality:
- Gaming: VR games immerse players in a virtual world, providing a unique gaming experience.
- Education: VR can be used to create interactive and engaging learning experiences, such as virtual field trips.
- Therapy: VR can be used in therapy to treat conditions such as anxiety and post-traumatic stress disorder.
- Training: VR can be used to simulate real-world scenarios for training purposes, such as in the military or medical fields.
- Design & Engineering: VR can be used to visualize and test designs in a virtual environment before committing to physical prototypes.
