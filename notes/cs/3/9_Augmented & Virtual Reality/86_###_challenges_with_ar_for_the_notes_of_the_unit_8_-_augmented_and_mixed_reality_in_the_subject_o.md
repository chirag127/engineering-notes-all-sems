### Challenges with AR for the notes of the Unit 8 - Augmented and Mixed Reality in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

1. Technical Limitations: AR requires high processing power and specialized hardware, making it difficult to implement on older devices.

2. Interoperability: AR technologies are still developing and standards are not yet fully established, leading to compatibility issues between devices and applications.

3. User Adoption: AR requires users to have specialized hardware and software, making it difficult for widespread adoption.

4. Cost: AR technology can be expensive to develop and implement, making it difficult for smaller businesses to adopt.

5. Privacy and Security: AR raises concerns about privacy and security, as it can potentially collect and store sensitive information about users and their environment.

6. User Experience: AR can be difficult to use, especially for those who are not familiar with technology, leading to a poor user experience.

7. Content Development: AR requires specialized skills for content creation, making it difficult for businesses to create and distribute AR experiences.
