### Education for the notes of the Unit 7 - VIRTUAL REALITY APPLICATIONS in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

The Education section of the Unit 7 - Virtual Reality Applications in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality should cover the following topics:

1. Definition of VR in Education (the use of virtual reality technology in education)
2. Overview of VR in Education (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
3. Types of VR in Education (e.g. simulations, games, virtual field trips, etc.)
4. Overview of Types of VR in Education (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
5. Benefits of VR in Education (e.g. engagement, retention, accessibility, etc.)
6. Overview of Benefits of VR in Education (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
7. Limitations of VR in Education (e.g. cost, complexity, compatibility, etc.)
8. Techniques for overcoming limitations (e.g. using advanced techniques, improving accuracy, etc.)
9. VR in Education and Industry Applications (how VR is used in various industries)
10. Further reading and resources (books, articles, online resources)

VR in Education is an important component of the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality. Understanding the principles and techniques of VR in education helps students to develop the skills required to design and implement VR systems for educational purposes. The hands-on practice and further reading resources will help students to develop their skills and deepen their understanding of the material.
