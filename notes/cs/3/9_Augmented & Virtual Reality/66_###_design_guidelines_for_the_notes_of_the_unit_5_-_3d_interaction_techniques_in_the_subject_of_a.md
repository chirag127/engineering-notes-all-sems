### Design Guidelines for the notes of the Unit 5 - 3D INTERACTION TECHNIQUES in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

Unit 5 of the Augmented & Virtual Reality course focuses on 3D Interaction Techniques and covers the aspect of Design Guidelines.

Design Guidelines are a set of principles and best practices that are used to design and develop virtual and augmented reality experiences. Design Guidelines can include recommendations for the use of color, text, and other visual elements, as well as recommendations for the use of sound, touch, and other sensory elements.

Design Guidelines can be used to ensure that virtual and augmented reality experiences are consistent, intuitive, and accessible to users. They can also be used to improve the overall quality and usability of virtual and augmented reality experiences.

In the context of the Augmented & Virtual Reality course, students in Unit 5 will learn about Design Guidelines as part of the discussion on 3D Interaction Techniques. This may include working with examples of Design Guidelines, analyzing the benefits and limitations of different Design Guidelines, and learning how to apply Design Guidelines to virtual and augmented reality experiences.
