### LODs for the notes of the Unit 4 - SOFTWARE TECHNOLOGIES in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

Level of Detail (LOD) refers to the level of complexity and detail in 3D models used in virtual and augmented reality applications. 

1. LOD 0: Lowest level of detail, basic shape and structure.
2. LOD 1: More detail added, including basic textures and shading.
3. LOD 2: Further detail added, including more complex textures and materials.
4. LOD 3: Highest level of detail, including realistic textures, materials, and lighting.

LODs allow for optimized performance in AR/VR applications by reducing the amount of data that needs to be processed and displayed. Higher LODs require more processing power and resources, but provide a more immersive and realistic experience.

It is important to choose the appropriate LOD for a given AR/VR application, balancing the trade-off between performance and visual quality.
