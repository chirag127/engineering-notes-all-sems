### Interaction - Simple for the notes of the Unit 4 - SOFTWARE TECHNOLOGIES in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

Interaction-Simple refers to the user's ability to interact with virtual objects or environments in a straightforward and intuitive manner. It involves basic actions such as pointing, clicking, and dragging, and is often used in AR/VR applications for navigation, selection, and manipulation of virtual objects. Interaction-Simple is a critical aspect of AR/VR design, as it determines the level of user engagement and immersion in the virtual environment. It requires the use of appropriate input devices such as hand-held controllers, touch screens, or gestures to enable users to interact with the virtual world. Interaction-Simple should be designed to be intuitive, responsive, and consistent to enhance the user experience in AR/VR applications.
