### 3D Manipulation tasks for the notes of the Unit 5 - 3D INTERACTION TECHNIQUES in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

3D Manipulation tasks are techniques used to interact with and manipulate objects in 3D virtual environments. The following are some of the key concepts related to 3D Manipulation tasks:

1. Translation: Translation refers to the movement of an object along a straight line, such as moving an object left or right, up or down, or forward or backward.

2. Rotation: Rotation refers to the movement of an object around an axis, such as rotating an object around its x-axis, y-axis, or z-axis.

3. Scaling: Scaling refers to the changing of an object's size, such as making an object larger or smaller.

4. Grabbing: Grabbing refers to the act of selecting and holding an object, such as picking up a virtual object with a hand-held device.

5. Pinching: Pinching refers to the act of selecting and manipulating an object with two fingers, such as pinching a virtual object to resize it.

6. Flicking: Flicking refers to the act of quickly moving an object, such as flicking a virtual object to move it across the screen.

In this unit, you will learn about 3D Manipulation tasks, and you will learn how they are used to interact with and manipulate objects in virtual environments. You will also learn about the key concepts related to 3D Manipulation tasks, including translation, rotation, scaling, grabbing, pinching, and flicking, and you will learn how these concepts are used in 3D interaction.

This unit will provide a foundation for understanding the principles and practices of 3D interaction, and for exploring the various concepts and techniques used in the field of Augmented and Virtual Reality.
