### mobile projection interfaces for the notes of the Unit 8 - Augmented and Mixed Reality in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

Mobile Projection Interfaces (MPI) are AR interfaces that use projection technology to display digital content on real-world objects. MPIs are typically implemented using smartphones or tablets and can be used to create interactive and immersive AR experiences. 

Features of MPIs:
1. Projection of digital content on real-world objects
2. Interactivity with projected digital content
3. Real-time integration with the environment
4. Context-aware content
5. Portable and accessible through smartphones or tablets
6. User-friendly and intuitive interface.
