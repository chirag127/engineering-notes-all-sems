
## Unit 7 - VIRTUAL REALITY APPLICATIONS

1. Virtual Reality (VR) is a computer-generated simulation of a three-dimensional image or environment that can be interacted with in a seemingly real or physical way by a person using special electronic equipment. 
2. VR applications can be used to simulate physical activities such as flying, driving, and sports. 
3. VR can also be used to create immersive educational experiences, such as virtual field trips, and to explore virtual worlds. 
4. VR has been used in a variety of industries, including gaming, entertainment, business, and healthcare. 
5. In healthcare, VR can be used to treat mental health issues, such as anxiety and phobias, as well as physical rehabilitation. 
6. VR technology is constantly evolving and new applications are being developed all the time. 
7. There are a variety of hardware and software options available for creating and using virtual reality applications. 
8. To create a VR experience, developers must first create 3D models, which can be done using 3D modeling software. 
9. Once the 3D models are created, they must be programmed using a game engine, such as Unity or Unreal Engine. 
10. After the game engine is programmed, the VR experience can be tested and refined. 
11. VR experiences can be viewed on a variety of hardware, including headsets, PCs, and mobile devices.
