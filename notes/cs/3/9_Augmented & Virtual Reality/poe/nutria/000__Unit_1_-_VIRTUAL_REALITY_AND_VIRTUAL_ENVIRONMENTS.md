
## Unit 1 - VIRTUAL REALITY AND VIRTUAL ENVIRONMENTS

1. Virtual Reality (VR) is a computer-generated environment that simulates a three-dimensional, interactive world. 
2. It is created using a combination of hardware and software, and can be experienced through a head-mounted display (HMD), a controller, and other peripherals. 
3. Virtual environments (VEs) are computer-generated simulations of real-world environments, such as a battlefield or a city. 
4. VEs can be used for training, education, entertainment, and even for scientific research. 
5. VR and VEs can be used to create immersive experiences that allow users to interact with the environment in real time. 
6. VR and VEs are also used to create virtual worlds, where users can explore and interact with the environment. 
7. VR and VEs can be used to create realistic simulations of real-world scenarios, allowing users to experience them as if they were actually there. 
8. VR and VEs can also be used to create virtual experiences that are not possible in the real world, such as flying or exploring outer space. 
9. VR and VEs are being used in a variety of industries, such as healthcare, education, entertainment, and military. 
10. VR and VEs are becoming increasingly popular and are expected to become even more widespread in the future.
