
### Design Guidelines - Theoretical Foundations of Wayfinding 

1. Wayfinding is the process of understanding and navigating a physical environment. It is a crucial part of user experience in augmented and virtual reality.

2. Wayfinding should be designed to help users orient themselves in the virtual environment and make informed decisions about their journey.

3. Effective wayfinding requires a combination of visual and spatial cues, such as signs, landmarks, and physical barriers.

4. Wayfinding should be designed to be intuitive, so that users can easily identify and understand the cues.

5. Navigation should be designed to be efficient, so that users can quickly and accurately move from one point to another.

6. Wayfinding should be designed to be flexible, so that users can easily adapt to changes in their environment.

7. Wayfinding should be designed to be accessible, so that users of all abilities can navigate the environment.

8. Wayfinding should be designed to be context-sensitive, so that users can understand how their environment is structured.
