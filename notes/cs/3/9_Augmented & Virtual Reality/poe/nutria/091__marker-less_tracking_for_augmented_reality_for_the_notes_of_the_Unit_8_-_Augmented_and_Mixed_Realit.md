
### Marker-less Tracking for Augmented Reality

Marker-less tracking for augmented reality is a technology that allows for tracking of objects in the physical world without the use of markers. It is a key component of augmented reality (AR) and mixed reality (MR) applications, as it enables the tracking of physical objects in the real world and the virtual world. 

Marker-less tracking is typically achieved by using sensors such as cameras, lidar, and infrared. These sensors are used to detect and track the physical objects in the environment. Additionally, marker-less tracking can also be done using computer vision algorithms such as feature detection and tracking. 

Marker-less tracking is used in a variety of applications, including gaming, entertainment, industrial automation, and medical applications. In gaming, marker-less tracking enables the tracking of physical objects such as controllers and other objects in the environment. In entertainment, marker-less tracking can be used to track the movement of actors and other objects in the environment. In industrial automation, marker-less tracking can be used to track the movement of robots and other objects in the environment. In medical applications, marker-less tracking can be used to track the movement of medical instruments and other objects in the environment. 

Marker-less tracking is also used in augmented reality and mixed reality applications. In augmented reality, marker-less tracking enables the tracking of physical objects in the environment, allowing for the virtual objects to be overlaid on the physical environment. In mixed reality, marker-less tracking enables the tracking of physical objects in the environment, allowing for the virtual objects to be blended with the physical environment. 

Marker-less tracking is an important technology for augmented reality and mixed reality applications, as it enables the tracking of physical objects in the environment and the blending of virtual objects with the physical environment.
