
### Interaction Techniques for 3D Manipulation
1. Selection: Selection is the process of selecting an object or region of interest, which can be done in a variety of ways. Selection can be done with a mouse, keyboard, or other input device.
2. Transformation: Transformation is the process of manipulating the position, orientation, and scale of an object or region of interest. This can be done with a mouse, keyboard, or other input device.
3. Navigation: Navigation is the process of moving around a 3D environment. This can be done with a mouse, keyboard, or other input device.
4. Interaction: Interaction is the process of manipulating objects in a 3D environment. This can be done with a mouse, keyboard, or other input device.
5. Gestures: Gestures are the process of using hand and body movements to interact with a 3D environment. This can be done with a Leap Motion, Kinect, or other gesture-based device.
6. Augmented Reality: Augmented Reality (AR) is the process of overlaying digital content onto the real world. This can be done with a mobile device or other AR-enabled device.
