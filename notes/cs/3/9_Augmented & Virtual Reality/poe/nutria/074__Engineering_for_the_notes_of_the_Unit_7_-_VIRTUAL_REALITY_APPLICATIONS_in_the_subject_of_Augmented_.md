
### Engineering for the notes of the Unit 7 - VIRTUAL REALITY APPLICATIONS in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

1. Virtual Reality (VR) is a computer-generated simulation of a three-dimensional environment that can be interacted with in a seemingly real or physical way.
2. It is used in a variety of applications, including gaming, education, entertainment, and healthcare.
3. VR technology involves the use of headsets, controllers, and other peripherals to create a fully immersive experience.
4. VR applications are designed to provide users with a sense of presence and immersion in a virtual environment.
5. The development of VR applications requires a thorough understanding of the physics of the virtual environment, as well as the design principles of user interaction.
6. VR applications can be developed for a variety of platforms, including mobile devices, desktop computers, and dedicated gaming consoles.
7. VR applications can be used to simulate a wide range of scenarios, from medical or military training to virtual tourism.
8. VR applications can also be used to create interactive experiences, such as interactive movies or virtual concerts.
9. The development of VR applications requires a strong understanding of 3D graphics, as well as experience with software development.
