
### Challenges with AR for the Notes of Unit 8 - Augmented and Mixed Reality

1. Cost: Augmented Reality (AR) technology is still relatively new and expensive to implement, meaning that it can be cost-prohibitive for many companies and organizations.

2. Data Storage: AR requires a significant amount of data to be stored and managed, which can be difficult for companies with limited resources.

3. Security: AR technology is vulnerable to cyber attacks, as the data it stores can be easily accessed by hackers.

4. Interoperability: AR applications are often developed to work with specific hardware and software, meaning that they may not be compatible with other platforms.

5. Privacy: AR can potentially be used to collect data about users without their knowledge, which could lead to privacy concerns.

6. User Experience: AR can be difficult to use and understand, which can lead to a poor user experience.

7. Accuracy: AR applications can be inaccurate, meaning that the information they provide may not be reliable.

8. Scalability: AR applications may not be able to scale to meet the needs of larger organizations or businesses.
