
### Haptic Displays

Haptic displays are devices that allow users to experience touch sensations in virtual environments. They are used to provide tactile feedback in augmented and virtual reality applications, such as gaming and training simulations.

- Haptic displays can be divided into two main categories: active and passive. 
- Active haptic displays use motors or actuators to generate forces and vibrations that can be felt by the user. 
- Passive haptic displays use physical objects that can be touched, such as buttons or joysticks.
- Haptic displays can be used to provide tactile feedback for 3D user interfaces, such as those used in augmented and virtual reality applications. 
- Haptic displays can also be used to create immersive virtual environments, such as those used in gaming and training simulations. 
- Haptic displays can be used to provide feedback to users, such as confirming a button press or providing feedback on a successful action. 
- Haptic displays can also be used to create tactile sensations, such as the sensation of touching an object or feeling a vibration.
