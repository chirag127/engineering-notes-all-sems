
### Science for the Notes of the Unit 7 - Virtual Reality Applications in the Subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

1. Virtual Reality (VR) is an interactive computer-generated experience taking place within a simulated environment. It immerses the user in an artificial 3D world, allowing them to interact with virtual objects and environments.
2. Augmented Reality (AR) is a technology that overlays digital information and images on top of the physical world. It is used to enhance the user’s view of the real world with virtual objects, images, and text.
3. Mixed Reality (MR) combines elements from both VR and AR, allowing users to interact with both physical and virtual objects in the same environment.
4. VR, AR, and MR technologies are used in a variety of industries, including gaming, entertainment, education, healthcare, manufacturing, and more.
5. VR applications can be used to simulate dangerous or hazardous environments without putting people in danger.
6. AR applications can provide users with additional information about their surroundings, such as directions or product details.
7. MR applications can be used to create immersive experiences, such as virtual tours or interactive museum exhibits.
8. VR, AR, and MR technologies are constantly evolving, and new applications are being developed all the time.
