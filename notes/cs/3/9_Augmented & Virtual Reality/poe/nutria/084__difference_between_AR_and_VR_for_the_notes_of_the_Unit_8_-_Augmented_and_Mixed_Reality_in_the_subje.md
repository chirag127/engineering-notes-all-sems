
### Difference between Augmented Reality (AR) and Virtual Reality (VR)

1. Augmented Reality (AR) is a technology that superimposes a computer-generated image on a user's view of the real world, providing a composite view. It is used to enhance the user's experience of the real world. AR is used in a variety of applications, including entertainment, education, and navigation.

2. Virtual Reality (VR) is a technology that creates a simulated environment that the user can interact with. It is designed to provide an immersive experience, allowing the user to explore and interact with a virtual world. VR is used for a variety of applications, including gaming, entertainment, and training.

3. The primary difference between AR and VR is the type of experience they provide. AR enhances the user's experience of the real world, while VR creates a completely simulated environment.
