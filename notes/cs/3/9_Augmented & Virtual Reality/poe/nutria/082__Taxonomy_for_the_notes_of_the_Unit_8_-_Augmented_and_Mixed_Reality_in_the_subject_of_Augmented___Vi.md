
### Taxonomy for the Notes of Unit 8 - Augmented and Mixed Reality in the Subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

1. Augmented Reality (AR): Augmented Reality is a technology that superimposes a computer-generated image on a user's view of the real world, thus providing a composite view.
2. Mixed Reality (MR): Mixed Reality is a hybrid of Augmented Reality and Virtual Reality, allowing users to interact with both digital and physical objects in a single environment.
3. Virtual Reality (VR): Virtual Reality is an artificial environment that is created with software and presented to the user in such a way that the user suspends belief and accepts it as a real environment.
4. Augmented Virtuality (AV): Augmented Virtuality is a form of Mixed Reality where users are immersed in a virtual environment that is enhanced by the presence of physical objects.
5. Projection-based Augmented Reality (PbAR): Projection-based Augmented Reality is a form of Augmented Reality where real-world objects are projected onto a physical surface or object.
6. Marker-based Augmented Reality (MbAR): Marker-based Augmented Reality is a form of Augmented Reality where visual markers are used to identify and track physical objects.
7. Location-based Augmented Reality (LbAR): Location-based Augmented Reality is a form of Augmented Reality where the user's location is used to provide contextual information about the surrounding environment.
8. Wearable Augmented Reality (WAR): Wearable Augmented Reality is a form of Augmented Reality where the user wears a device that provides a real-time view of the environment.
