
### Objects - Geometry for the notes of the Unit 4 - SOFTWARE TECHNOLOGIES in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

* Objects in Augmented and Virtual Reality are three-dimensional models of real-world objects.
* Geometry is the mathematics of shapes and forms in space. It is used to define and manipulate objects in 3D space.
* Geometric primitives are the basic shapes from which more complex shapes can be built. These primitives are points, lines, planes, and polygons.
* Basic transformations such as translations, rotations, and scaling can be used to modify the position and size of objects in 3D space.
* Advanced transformations such as shearing and mirroring can be used to create more complex objects.
* 3D modeling is the process of creating 3D objects from scratch using geometric primitives and transformations.
* 3D animation is the process of creating realistic motion and behavior in 3D objects.
* 3D rendering is the process of creating a realistic image of a 3D scene.
