
### Available software in the market for the notes of the Unit 4 - SOFTWARE TECHNOLOGIES in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

* Unity3D: A powerful and popular game engine used to create 3D virtual and augmented reality applications. It is free to use for personal projects and has a wide range of tools and features to help create immersive experiences.

* Unreal Engine: Another popular game engine used for virtual and augmented reality development. It is a paid engine but is known for its high-quality graphics and capabilities.

* Vuforia: A platform for developing augmented reality applications. It has a wide range of features, such as image recognition, 3D object tracking, and cloud-based services.

* ARKit: Apple's augmented reality platform, which is available on iOS devices. It has a range of features, such as motion tracking, facial recognition, and object recognition.

* ARCore: Google's augmented reality platform, which is available on Android devices. It has a range of features, such as environmental understanding, motion tracking, and light estimation.
