
### Manipulation Techniques and Input Devices for 3D Interaction Techniques

* 3D interaction techniques involve the use of a variety of manipulation techniques and input devices.
* Manipulation techniques include direct manipulation, indirect manipulation, and gesture-based manipulation.
* Direct manipulation involves the user manipulating a 3D object directly with their hands or a device such as a stylus or mouse.
* Indirect manipulation involves the user manipulating a 3D object indirectly by using a controller or joystick.
* Gesture-based manipulation involves the user using hand gestures to manipulate a 3D object.
* Input devices used for 3D interaction include controllers, mice, keyboards, touchscreens, and motion tracking devices.
* Controllers are typically used for indirect manipulation, while mice, keyboards, and touchscreens are typically used for direct manipulation.
* Motion tracking devices such as the Microsoft Kinect are used for gesture-based manipulation.
