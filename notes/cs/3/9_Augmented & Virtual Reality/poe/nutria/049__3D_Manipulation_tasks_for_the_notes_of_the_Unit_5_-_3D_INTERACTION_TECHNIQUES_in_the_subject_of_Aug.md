
### 3D Manipulation tasks

1. Translations: Translations are the simplest form of 3D manipulation. They involve moving an object in one, two, or three dimensions.
2. Rotations: Rotations involve rotating an object in one, two, or three dimensions.
3. Scaling: Scaling involves changing the size of an object.
4. Reflection: Reflection involves mirroring an object across a plane.
5. Shearing: Shearing involves moving an object along an axis.
6. Morphing: Morphing involves changing the shape of an object.
7. Deformation: Deformation involves changing the shape of an object in a non-uniform way.
8. Splitting: Splitting involves cutting an object into two or more pieces.
9. Combining: Combining involves merging two or more objects into one.
10. Interpolation: Interpolation involves creating a smooth transition between two objects.
