### Objects - Geometry

In augmented and virtual reality, objects are the fundamental building blocks of the virtual environment. Geometry refers to the shape and structure of these objects. In this section, we will discuss the different types of geometry used to create objects in AR and VR.

- **Points:** Points are the simplest type of geometry. They are represented as a single coordinate in space and have no size or shape. Points are commonly used to represent particles or to create point clouds.

- **Lines:** Lines are made up of two points connected by a straight line. They have length and direction but no thickness. Lines are often used to create wireframe models or to represent paths and trajectories.

- **Polygons:** Polygons are two-dimensional shapes made up of three or more connected lines. They have a defined area and can have a variety of shapes, including triangles, squares, and circles. Polygons are commonly used to create flat surfaces or to define the boundaries of 3D objects.

- **Meshes:** Meshes are three-dimensional objects made up of interconnected polygons. They have volume and can have a variety of shapes, including cubes, spheres, and cylinders. Meshes are the most common type of geometry used in AR and VR applications.

- **Curves:** Curves are smooth, continuous lines that can be used to create organic shapes or to define the path of an object. Curves can be represented using mathematical equations or by a series of connected points.

- **Splines:** Splines are a type of curve that is defined by a set of control points. They are commonly used in 3D modeling to create complex shapes and surfaces.

Understanding the different types of geometry used to create objects in AR and VR is essential for developers and designers working in this field. By choosing the right type of geometry for a particular use case, they can create more realistic and immersive virtual environments.
