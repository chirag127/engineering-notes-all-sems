### Design Guidelines for the notes of the Unit 5 - 3D INTERACTION TECHNIQUES in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

Here are some important design guidelines to keep in mind while taking notes on 3D interaction techniques in Augmented & Virtual Reality:

- Organize your notes in a logical and consistent manner. Use headings, subheadings, and bullet points to structure your notes and make them easy to read and navigate.
- Include examples and illustrations to help clarify your notes. Visual aids such as diagrams, flowcharts, and screenshots can be particularly helpful in understanding complex interaction techniques.
- Highlight key concepts and important information in your notes. This will make it easier to review and remember the material later on.
- Take note of any relevant research or studies that support the effectiveness of certain interaction techniques. This will provide additional context and depth to your notes.
- Be sure to include any relevant limitations or constraints of the interaction techniques. This will help you to understand the potential challenges and drawbacks of using these techniques in real-world applications.
- Finally, review and revise your notes regularly to ensure that they are accurate and up-to-date. This will help you to retain the information and apply it effectively in future projects and assignments.

By following these guidelines, you can create comprehensive and effective notes on 3D interaction techniques in Augmented & Virtual Reality that will serve as a valuable reference for your studies and future work in this field.
