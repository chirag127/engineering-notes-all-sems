### Augmented reality methods for the notes of the Unit 8 - Augmented and Mixed Reality in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

Here are some important points to understand the augmented reality methods for the notes of the Unit 8 - Augmented and Mixed Reality in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality:

- **Marker-Based Augmented Reality:** This method involves using a printed marker or image as a reference point for the augmented reality content to be overlaid on top. The device's camera recognizes the marker and overlays the digital content on top of it.

- **Markerless Augmented Reality:** This method doesn't require a marker for the augmented reality content to be overlaid. Instead, it uses the device's camera to detect and track objects and surfaces in the real world to overlay the digital content.

- **Projection-Based Augmented Reality:** This method involves projecting digital content onto real-world surfaces such as walls, floors, or tables. The content is projected in a way that it appears to be a part of the real world.

- **Superimposition-Based Augmented Reality:** This method involves overlaying the digital content on top of the real-world objects. The device's camera detects the object, and the digital content is superimposed on top of it.

- **Outlining-Based Augmented Reality:** This method involves outlining the real-world objects with digital content to create a 3D effect. The device's camera detects the object's outline and overlays the digital content on top of it.

- **Location-Based Augmented Reality:** This method involves using the device's GPS and compass to overlay digital content based on the user's location. This method is commonly used in location-based games and apps.

- **Recognition-Based Augmented Reality:** This method involves recognizing real-world objects using image or object recognition technology. The device's camera detects the object, and the digital content is overlaid on top of it.

- **Handheld Augmented Reality:** This method involves using a handheld device such as a smartphone or tablet to view and interact with the augmented reality content. The device's camera detects and tracks the real-world objects, and the digital content is overlaid on top of it.

These are some of the important augmented reality methods to understand for the notes of the Unit 8 - Augmented and Mixed Reality in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality. By understanding these methods, you can better understand how augmented reality works and how it can be used in various applications.
