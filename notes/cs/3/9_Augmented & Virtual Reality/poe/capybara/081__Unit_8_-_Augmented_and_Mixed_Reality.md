## Unit 8 - Augmented and Mixed Reality

In this unit, you will learn about Augmented and Mixed Reality. These technologies have been gaining in popularity in recent years and are becoming more and more prominent in various industries. Here are some important points to keep in mind:

- Augmented Reality (AR) is a technology that overlays digital information onto the physical world. This technology is usually used through a mobile device, such as a smartphone or tablet, and can be used in a variety of applications, such as gaming, education, and marketing.

- Mixed Reality (MR) is a combination of both the physical and digital worlds. This technology allows users to interact with digital objects in a physical space. Mixed Reality is often used in gaming and entertainment, as well as in industries such as healthcare and education.

- Both Augmented and Mixed Reality have the potential to revolutionize industries such as education, healthcare, and entertainment. These technologies can be used to provide immersive experiences that enhance learning and engagement.

- Augmented and Mixed Reality can also be used in marketing to provide interactive and engaging experiences for consumers. For example, a company can use AR to create an interactive product demonstration or to provide virtual try-on experiences for customers.

- Implementation of Augmented and Mixed Reality technologies requires specialized skills and knowledge. Developers must have a deep understanding of computer vision, 3D modeling, and user experience design.

- The future of Augmented and Mixed Reality is bright, with the potential for these technologies to become even more advanced and integrated into our daily lives. As technology continues to evolve, we can expect to see more and more innovative applications of these technologies.
