### Gesture Commands for the Notes of Unit 5 - 3D Interaction Techniques in the Subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

In 3D interaction techniques, gestural commands are a vital aspect of the user interface. Gestural commands are actions performed by the user, such as waving a hand or pointing a finger, that are interpreted by the system to perform an action in the virtual or augmented environment. Here are some important gestural commands to take note of:

- **Pointing**: Pointing is a common gesture used in 3D interaction techniques. It involves extending a finger towards an object or location in the virtual or augmented environment. This gesture is used to select objects or locations, move objects, or trigger events.

- **Gestures with Two Hands**: Gestures with two hands are used to perform actions that require more than one input. For example, a two-handed pinch gesture can be used to zoom in or out on an object or location in the environment. A two-handed swipe gesture can be used to rotate an object or view the environment from a different angle.

- **Waving**: Waving is a simple gesture that can be used to perform a variety of actions. For example, waving can be used to activate a menu or trigger an event.

- **Circling**: Circling is a gesture that involves moving a finger or hand in a circular motion. This gesture is commonly used to rotate an object or view the environment from a different angle.

- **Grabbing**: Grabbing is a gesture used to pick up or move objects in the virtual or augmented environment. This gesture involves closing the hand around an object and moving it to a new location.

- **Pushing and Pulling**: Pushing and pulling are gestures used to move objects in the virtual or augmented environment. These gestures involve moving the hand or arm forwards or backwards to move the object in the desired direction.

In conclusion, gestural commands are an essential part of 3D interaction techniques in augmented and virtual reality. By mastering these gestures, users can navigate and interact with the environment more efficiently and effectively.
