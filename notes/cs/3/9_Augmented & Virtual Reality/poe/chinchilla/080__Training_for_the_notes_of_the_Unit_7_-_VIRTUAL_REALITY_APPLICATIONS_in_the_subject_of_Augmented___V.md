### Training for the notes of the Unit 7 - VIRTUAL REALITY APPLICATIONS in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

Virtual Reality (VR) is an immersive technology that creates a simulated environment for the users. VR technology has evolved significantly in recent years, and its applications are widespread in various industries. In this unit, we will be discussing the applications of VR and how it is revolutionizing different fields. Here are some essential points that you need to consider while preparing notes for Unit 7:

- Understand the basics of VR technology: Before diving into the applications of VR, it is crucial to understand the underlying technology. VR technology uses a combination of hardware and software to create a simulated environment. The hardware includes a headset, controllers, and sensors, while the software includes programming languages, game engines, and 3D modeling tools. Understanding the basics of VR technology will help you grasp the applications better.

- Gaming and Entertainment: Gaming is the most popular application of VR technology. VR games provide an immersive experience to the players by creating a simulated environment. The entertainment industry is also using VR technology for creating virtual concerts, movies, and theme park rides.

- Education and Training: VR technology is transforming the education and training industry. VR can provide a simulated environment for students to learn and practice complex procedures. It is also being used in the military for training soldiers and in the healthcare industry for medical training.

- Healthcare: VR technology has revolutionized the healthcare industry by providing a simulated environment for medical procedures. VR is being used for pain management, phobia treatment, and mental health therapy.

- Real Estate and Architecture: VR technology is being used in the real estate and architecture industry to provide virtual tours of properties. It helps potential buyers to get a better understanding of the property before making a purchase. Architects can also use VR technology to create virtual models of their designs and get client feedback.

- Manufacturing and Engineering: VR technology is being used in manufacturing and engineering for product design and prototyping. It provides a simulated environment for engineers to test the product before creating a physical prototype, which saves time and resources.

- Conclusion: VR technology is revolutionizing different industries, and its applications are widespread. Understanding the basics of VR technology and its applications in different fields is crucial for anyone interested in this field.
