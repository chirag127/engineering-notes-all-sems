### Taxonomy for the notes of the Unit 8 - Augmented and Mixed Reality in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

When studying Augmented and Mixed Reality, it is important to understand the various types and categories that exist within this field. This taxonomy will help you organize and categorize your notes, and understand the different types of technologies and applications within this field.

#### Types of Augmented Reality

1. Marker-Based AR: This type of AR uses a predefined marker or image to trigger a digital overlay on top of the real-world environment.

2. Markerless AR: This type of AR does not require a predefined marker or image to trigger a digital overlay. Instead, it uses real-time tracking to detect and augment the environment.

3. Projection-Based AR: This type of AR projects digital content onto real-world surfaces, such as walls or floors.

4. Superimposition-Based AR: This type of AR replaces a portion of the real-world environment with digital content, creating a blended view of the physical and virtual worlds.

#### Types of Mixed Reality

1. Spatial Computing: This type of MR creates a fully immersive digital environment that can be interacted with in real-time.

2. Transparency-Based: This type of MR overlays digital content onto the real-world environment, but allows users to see and interact with both simultaneously.

3. Object-Based: This type of MR allows digital content to interact with real-world objects, creating a seamless blend between the physical and virtual worlds.

#### Applications of Augmented and Mixed Reality

1. Gaming and Entertainment: AR and MR are widely used in gaming and entertainment industries to create immersive and interactive experiences.

2. Education and Training: AR and MR can be used to create interactive educational and training materials, allowing learners to engage with content in a more meaningful way.

3. Industrial and Manufacturing: AR and MR can be used in industrial and manufacturing settings to improve efficiency and accuracy by providing workers with real-time information and instructions.

4. Healthcare: AR and MR can be used in healthcare settings for training, diagnosis, and treatment purposes, allowing medical professionals to visualize and interact with patient data in new ways.

By understanding these different types and applications of Augmented and Mixed Reality, you can better categorize and organize your notes, and gain a deeper understanding of this exciting and rapidly evolving field.
