### Augmented reality methods for the notes of the Unit 8 - Augmented and Mixed Reality in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality.

In this unit, we will be discussing various methods of augmented reality that can be used to enhance the user's experience. Augmented reality is a technology that overlays digital content onto the real world, creating a mixed reality experience. Here are some of the methods that we will be covering:

- Marker-based AR: This method of AR involves using a visual marker, such as a QR code or an image, to trigger the display of digital content. The marker is detected by the AR system, which then overlays the digital content on top of it. Marker-based AR is commonly used in advertising, gaming, and education.

- Markerless AR: Unlike marker-based AR, markerless AR does not require a visual marker to trigger the display of digital content. Instead, it uses a combination of sensors, such as GPS, accelerometers, and gyroscopes, to detect the user's location and orientation. Markerless AR is commonly used in navigation, tourism, and architecture.

- Projection-based AR: This method of AR involves projecting digital content onto a real-world surface, such as a wall or a floor. The content is then overlaid onto the surface, creating an interactive experience. Projection-based AR is commonly used in advertising, events, and entertainment.

- Recognition-based AR: This method of AR involves using object recognition technology to detect and track real-world objects, such as a product or a landmark. The AR system then overlays digital content onto the object, creating an interactive experience. Recognition-based AR is commonly used in advertising, education, and tourism.

- Superimposition-based AR: This method of AR involves overlaying digital content onto a real-world scene, without the need for a visual marker or object recognition technology. The AR system uses computer vision algorithms to detect and track real-world objects, and then overlays digital content onto the scene. Superimposition-based AR is commonly used in gaming, entertainment, and education.

In conclusion, these are some of the methods of augmented reality that we will be covering in this unit. By understanding these methods, you will be able to create immersive and interactive AR experiences that enhance the user's engagement and enjoyment.
