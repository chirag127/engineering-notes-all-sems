### Lights and Cameras

1. Lights
- Lights are used to illuminate the objects in a scene.
- Different types of lights used are:
-- Ambient light - Provides overall illumination to the scene.
-- Directional light - Light comes from a specific direction simulating light from the sun or moon.
-- Point light - Light emanates from a single point in all directions.
-- Spot light - Creates a cone of light with a specific inner angle.

2. Cameras
- Cameras are used to render the 3D scene onto a 2D plane.
- Different types of cameras used are:
-- Perspective camera - Provides a perspective view as seen by a human eye. Objects farther away appear smaller.
-- Orthographic camera - Provides a parallel projection with no perspective. Objects appear the same size regardless of distance.
-- Cinematic camera - Provides controls to simulate camera movements like in films. Can be orbited, panned, trucked, etc.
