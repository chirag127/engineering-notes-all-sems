### Travel Techniques for the notes of the Unit 5 - 3D INTERACTION TECHNIQUES in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

1. Walking - The user can walk around the virtual environment. This is the most natural form of travel but requires a large physical space.
2. Flying - The user can fly through the air to quickly travel around the environment. This gives a sense of freedom but can be disorienting or unrealistic depending on the application.
3. Teleporting - The user can instantly transport to a new location. This is very fast but can be discontinuous and disrupt the sense of presence. It requires a mechanism to specify the new location such as a menu or graphics.
4. Vehicle - The user can travel around in a vehicle such as a car, spaceship or other entity. This can make the experience more engaging or purposeful but may be complex to control and simulate.
5. Portal - The user can walk through portals to instantly move between two locations. This can be an efficient mechanism but may not always be plausible depending on the virtual environment and application.
