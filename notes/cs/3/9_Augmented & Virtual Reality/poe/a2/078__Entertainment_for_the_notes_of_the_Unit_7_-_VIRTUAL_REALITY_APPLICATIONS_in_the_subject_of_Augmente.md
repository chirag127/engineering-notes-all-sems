### Entertainment

- Gaming: VR headsets are primarily used for gaming. Popular VR games include Beat Saber, Superhot, Job Simulator, etc. The immersive experience of VR makes gaming more engaging and realistic.
- Video streaming: VR can be used to stream 360-degree videos that provide an immersive viewing experience. YouTube and other platforms have content tailored for VR headsets.
- Cinema: VR can be used to simulate the experience of watching a movie in a cinema hall. The user can watch the movie on a huge virtual screen in a virtual cinema hall. Some streaming services like Netflix have apps for watching content in VR.
- Social connectivity: VR headsets allow people to connect and interact with each other virtually via avatars. This enables virtual meetups, parties, concerts, etc. where users can socialize and share experiences. Apps like VRChat, Rec Room, AltspaceVR, etc. are designed for virtual social interaction.
- Entertainment apps: There are various dedicated entertainment apps for VR like Wonderglade (mini-games), The Thief of Time (interactive stories), The Night Cafe: An Immersive Van Gogh Art Experience, etc. providing diverse experiences.

The above points cover some of the major entertainment applications of virtual reality. The immersive nature of VR lends itself well to entertainment and enables simulated experiences that would otherwise not be feasible or accessible to most users. However, the high costs of VR devices and content are still barriers to widespread adoption of VR for entertainment.
