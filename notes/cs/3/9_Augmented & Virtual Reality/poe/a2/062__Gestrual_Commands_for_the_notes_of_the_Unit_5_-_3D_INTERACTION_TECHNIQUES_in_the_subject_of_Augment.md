### Gestural Commands for the notes of the Unit 5 - 3D INTERACTION TECHNIQUES

1. Hand Gestures
- Pinching: Bringing two fingers together to zoom in and spreading them apart to zoom out.
- Rotating: Moving two fingers in a circular motion to rotate an object.
- Swiping: Quickly moving fingers across a surface to pan or scroll.

2. Arm Gestures
- Reaching: Extending arm forward to reach for a distant object. As arm gets closer to object, the rate of movement decreases.
- Pointing: Extending index finger to point at objects or directions.
- Throwing: Mimicking throwing motion to throw objects. Force and direction of throw depends on motion of arm.

3. Whole Body Gestures
- Leaning: Leaning body in a direction to navigate through environments or inspect objects.
- Turning: Turning body in a direction to change view or navigate.
- Dodging: Quickly moving body to the side to dodge objects.
- Stepping: Taking steps forward, backward or to the side to navigate in environments.
