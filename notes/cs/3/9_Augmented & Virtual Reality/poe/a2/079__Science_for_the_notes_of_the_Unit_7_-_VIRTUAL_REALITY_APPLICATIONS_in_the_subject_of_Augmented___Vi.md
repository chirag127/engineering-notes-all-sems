### Science for the notes of the Unit 7 - VIRTUAL REALITY APPLICATIONS

1. Virtual Reality in Healthcare
- VR is used for training healthcare professionals through simulated surgeries and medical procedures. This allows them to practice complex surgeries and procedures without risk to patients.
- VR exposure therapy is used to treat patients with anxiety, phobias, PTSD, etc. The patients are exposed to simulated environments and scenarios to help overcome their fears and anxiety in a controlled manner.
- Pain management is aided using VR as it can act as a distraction and relax the person, thereby reducing the perception of pain.

2. Virtual Reality in Education
- VR allows students to explore complex concepts and places which may not be possible otherwise through field trips. For example, the human circulatory system, space travel, etc.
- Hands-on training and learning is enabled through simulated scientific experiments and engineering applications. Students can understand the theory as well as get practical experience through VR.
- VR storytelling allows students to be part of an immersive learning experience, thereby enhancing learning outcomes and retention.

3. Virtual Reality in Business
- VR can be used for training employees on complex machinery and processes in a safe and cost-effective manner. The employees can get hands-on practice without the resources being actually available.
- Product visualization allows customers to get a feel of the product and experience it before purchasing. This can help businesses in marketing and sales.
- Virtual meetings and conferences allow people across the globe to come together in a shared virtual space. This can reduce travel and enable collaboration with reduced costs.

4. Entertainment with Virtual Reality
- VR headsets are used to play immersive games across various genres like action, horror, puzzle, etc. The motion controllers allow the user to physically interact with the virtual world.
- VR can allow you to experience your favorite movies and TV shows as an active participant rather than just a viewer.
- Live events and performances can be enjoyed from the comfort of one's home through VR. One can have an amazing virtual experience of concerts, sports events, and other events.
