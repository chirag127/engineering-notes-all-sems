### Taxonomy for the notes of the Unit 8 - Augmented and Mixed Reality

1. Augmented Reality
- Augmented Reality is the integration of digital information with the user's environment in real time.
- It is used to enhance the elements of the real world using sound, video, graphics, etc. while still allowing the user to interact with the real world.
- Components: Computing system, Tracking system, Display system, Rendering system, Interaction system
- Applications: Education, Entertainment, Business, Healthcare, Engineering, etc.

2. Mixed Reality
- Mixed Reality (MR) is the merging of real and virtual worlds to produce new environments and visualizations where physical and digital objects co-exist and interact in real time.
- It operates at the intersection of AR and VR and extends experiences from one world into the other.
- Components: Computing system, Tracking system, Display system, Rendering system, Interaction system
- Applications: Industry, Healthcare, Sports, etc.

3. Differences between AR and MR
- In AR, the real-world environment is augmented with digital information whereas in MR, the user's perception of the real world is digitally enhanced.
- MR blends the physical and virtual worlds into a continuum while AR enhances the real world.
- MR requires more advanced and complex technology than AR.
