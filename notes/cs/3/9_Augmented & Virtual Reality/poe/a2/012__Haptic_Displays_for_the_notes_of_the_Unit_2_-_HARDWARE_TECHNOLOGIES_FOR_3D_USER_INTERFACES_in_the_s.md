### Haptic Displays

- Haptic displays provide force feedback to the user through touch. They add the sense of touch to augmented/virtual reality experiences.
- They consist of actuators and sensors that apply forces, vibrations, and motions to the user and detect the user's motions and forces, respectively.
- Types:
    - Vibrotactile displays: Produce vibrations via eccentric rotating mass motors or piezoelectric actuators. Provide sensation of texture.
    - Force-feedback displays: Use motors and actuators to apply forces to user. Enable sensation of shape and weight. Require complex mechanics and software control.
    - Ultrasonic displays: Use ultrasonic vibrations to create sensations of texture and touch. Enable mid-air haptic feedback without contact.
- Challenges:
    - Delivering realistic and useful haptic effects.
    - Reducing friction, inertia, and weight of mechanical components.
    - Synchronizing visual and haptic feedback.
    - Safety considerations to avoid excessive forces.
- Applications:
    - Add haptic feedback to AR/VR experiences, simulations, teleoperation, etc. to enable enhanced perception and immersion.
    - Allow interaction with 3D data, remote environments, and virtual objects.
