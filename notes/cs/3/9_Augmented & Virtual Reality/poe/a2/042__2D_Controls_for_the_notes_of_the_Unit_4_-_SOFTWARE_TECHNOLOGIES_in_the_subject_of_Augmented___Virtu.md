### 2D Controls for the notes of the Unit 4 - SOFTWARE TECHNOLOGIES in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

1. Touchscreen:
- Direct touch input on a touch-sensitive display surface.
- Used to directly interact with on-screen objects.
- Multi-touch allows multiple fingers used simultaneously for gestures to control scale, rotate, move etc.

2. Mouse:
- Cursor control device that translates 2D movement across a surface into corresponding movement of an on-screen pointer.
- Click-and-drag interactions on on-screen objects.
- Scroll-wheel for zooming and panning.

3. Trackball:
- Rotating sphere to control an on-screen pointer.
- Can be stationary with finger movements controlling pointer.
- Facilitates simultaneous navigation and selection.

4. Pointing stick (nub/trackpoint):
- Protruding stick or nub in center of keyboard keys.
- Used to control pointer on screen with subtle finger movements.
- Does not require hand movement between keyboard and additional pointing device.
