### Challenges with AR

1. Technical challenges:
- Hardware limitations: AR requires advanced hardware with powerful sensors, displays, and processors which increase the cost of AR devices and limit widespread adoption.
- Limited field of view: The AR displays have a limited field of view which restricts the immersive experience. The narrow field of view makes it difficult to overlay digital information on the real world.
- Registration and tracking: Accurate registration and tracking of the physical and digital worlds is difficult to achieve. The AR system should seamlessly integrate the real and virtual worlds but even minor errors in tracking and registration can create a broken user experience.

2. Content creation challenges:
- Lack of skills: There is a lack of skills and expertise in creating AR content like 3D assets, interactions, and experiences. Content creation requires knowledge of new tools and programming languages which makes it difficult for most users and businesses to create their own AR content.
- High costs: The costs of creating AR content are very high due to the specialized skills and tools required. The high costs limit the availability of AR content and mass adoption of AR technology.

3. User experience challenges:
- Social issues: AR can lead to issues like distracting or annoying other people nearby with AR displays and experiences. It can also be perceived as weird or inappropriate by others in social situations.
- Privacy and security: There are privacy and security concerns with AR as the technology tracks user data and captures information about the physical environment. The personal and sensitive data can be vulnerable to security risks and misuse.
- Health and safety: There are potential health and safety risks with AR related to coordination, navigation, and adverse psychological effects. Extended use of AR technology can lead to issues like eyestrain, headaches, and impaired motor skills. The risks need to be studied in more detail to enable safe and ethical use of AR.
