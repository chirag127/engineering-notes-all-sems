### Evaluating AR Systems

1. Tracking: How accurately and robustly does the system track the user and objects/anchors in the real world? Does it lose tracking easily? How wide of an area can it track?
2. Display: What is the field of view of the display? How is occlusion handled (objects blocking other objects)? What is the resolution and refresh rate of the display? How is the display attached to the user?
3. Interaction: What types of input does the system accept? Are natural and intuitive interactions enabled? How precise and accurate is the input handling?
4. Performance: How fast does the system respond to user input/changes in the environment? How powerful are the processing components? How well does the system handle resource intensive tasks like complex graphics or physics simulations?
5. Immersion: How immersive is the experience? How well does the system blend real and virtual elements? How believable and engaging is the overall experience? How comfortable is the user experience, e.g. do visuals/audio cause strain, is the equipment ergonomic?
6. Content Creation: How easy is it to create/modify content for the system? What tools/APIs are available? How much 3D expertise is required? Can average users create content or customize experiences?
7. Industrial Design: How portable, lightweight, and discreet is the hardware? How wearable is the system? Does the design cater to a specific use case? How suitable is the system for use in various environments?
8. Safety and Practicality: How safe is the system to use and operate? Are there any tripping/collision hazards or other risks? Is the system practical for common use cases? What are the system requirements and costs? How disruptive is the system to use in public/at work?
