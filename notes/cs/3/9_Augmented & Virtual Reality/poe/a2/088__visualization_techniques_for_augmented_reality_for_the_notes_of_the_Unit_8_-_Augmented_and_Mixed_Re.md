### Visualization Techniques for Augmented Reality

1. Marker-based Augmented Reality:Uses visual markers like QR codes or Image targets to identify the real world objects and overlay digital information on them. The markers are used for tracking the location and pose of the objects. Example: AR apps that provide information when you scan a QR code.

2. Markerless Augmented Reality:Does not require any visual markers to identify and track real world objects. Computer vision techniques are used to detect and track objects, planes and features in the real environment. Examples: Pokémon Go, IKEA Place app that can place true-to-scale 3D models in your space.

3. Model-based Tracking:Real world 3D models or maps are used for tracking the location and pose of objects. The virtual objects are then overlaid on the map. SLAM algorithms can also be used to build 3D maps on-the-fly and track locations.

4. Location-based or GPS Augmented Reality:Leverages location data and GPS coordinates to provide an augmented reality experience. The apps detect your location and show relevant AR content. Examples: Apps that show nearby points of interest, restaurants, transit details etc.

The above points cover the key visualization techniques used in augmented reality applications. The choice of a technique depends on factors like computing power required, need for markers, accuracy of tracking etc. A combination of these techniques is also used in many AR apps to provide a rich user experience.
