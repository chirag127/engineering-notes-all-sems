### Difference between AR and VR:

1. Augmented Reality (AR): AR overlays digital information on the real world via devices like smart glasses or smartphone apps. It augments the real world by adding digital information on top of what the user sees in the real world. Users can still see the real world, but digital information is overlaid on top of it.

2. Virtual Reality (VR): VR creates an entirely immersive digital environment that users experience through headsets or multi-projector systems. In VR, the user is isolated from the real world and immersed in a simulated reality. The user cannot see the real world, only the simulated digital world.

3. Interaction with the real world: In AR, users can still interact with the real world as they see it, but in VR the real world is completely blocked out and the user can only interact with the simulated digital world.

4. Data input: AR uses data from cameras and sensors to determine the position and orientation of objects in the real world. VR does not need this data since it does not interact with the real world. VR only needs to track the user's position and orientation.

5. Usage: AR is used for applications like navigation, education, business, and gaming where some interaction with the real world is required. VR is used for fully immersive experiences like gaming, entertainment, and simulation where isolation from the real world is necessary.
