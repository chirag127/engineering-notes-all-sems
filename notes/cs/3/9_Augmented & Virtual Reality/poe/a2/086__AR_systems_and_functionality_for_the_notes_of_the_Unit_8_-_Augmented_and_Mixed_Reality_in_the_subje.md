### AR systems and functionality for the notes of the Unit 8 - Augmented and Mixed Reality

1. Augmented Reality systems combine the real and virtual worlds by overlaying digital information on the real world via devices like smart glasses, head-mounted displays or through mobile screens.

2. The key components of an AR system are:

- Tracking system: To track the user's location and orientation. Uses sensors and cameras.
- Rendering system: To generate the graphics and overlays the virtual objects on the real world. Uses GPU and rendering algorithms.
- Display system: To present the augmented view to the user. Uses head-mounted displays, smart glasses or mobile screens.

3. The functionality of an AR system includes:

- Recognizing and tracking fiducial markers, faces, objects, gestures, etc.
- Displaying 3D models, animations, information, etc. overlaid on the real world.
- Allowing user interaction with the augmented elements via touch, voice or gestures.
- Anchoring augmented elements to a fixed position in the real world.
- Responding to changes in the environment and updates to augmented elements.

4. The applications of AR include education, engineering, healthcare, entertainment, navigation, etc. AR enables an enhanced and interactive experience of the real world.
