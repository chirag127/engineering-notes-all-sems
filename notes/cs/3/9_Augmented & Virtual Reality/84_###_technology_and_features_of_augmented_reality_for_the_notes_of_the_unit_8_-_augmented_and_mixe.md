### technology and features of augmented reality for the notes of the Unit 8 - Augmented and Mixed Reality in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

Augmented Reality (AR) is a technology that enhances the real world with digital content, such as 3D models, animations, videos, and images. AR is achieved by overlaying digital content on the real world through devices like smartphones, tablets, and AR glasses.

Features of AR:
1. Overlaying digital content on the real world
2. Interactivity with digital content
3. Real-time integration with the environment
4. Context-aware content
5. Portable and accessible through devices
6. User-friendly and intuitive interface.
