### Interaction - Simple

Interaction in the context of Augmented and Virtual Reality refers to the ways in which users can interact with the virtual environment and its elements. Simple interactions are the most basic and fundamental ways of interacting with the virtual world. These include:

1. **Gaze**: This is the most basic form of interaction where the user can look at virtual objects to interact with them. This can be achieved through head tracking, where the system tracks the movement of the user's head to determine where they are looking.

2. **Pointing**: This involves using a handheld controller or hand gestures to point at virtual objects to interact with them. This can be achieved through the use of motion controllers or hand tracking technologies.

3. **Selection**: This involves selecting virtual objects to interact with them. This can be achieved through various methods such as clicking a button on a controller, making a hand gesture, or using voice commands.

4. **Manipulation**: This involves manipulating virtual objects by moving, rotating, or scaling them. This can be achieved through the use of motion controllers or hand tracking technologies.

These simple interactions form the foundation of more complex interactions in Augmented and Virtual Reality. They allow users to interact with the virtual world in intuitive and natural ways, enhancing the overall user experience.
