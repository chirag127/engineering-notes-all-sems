### Beyond Text and Number Entry

Unit 5 - 3D INTERACTION TECHNIQUES in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

- Beyond text and number entry, there are a variety of other input methods that can be used in 3D interaction techniques.
- These methods include gesture recognition, voice recognition, and eye tracking.
- Gesture recognition involves interpreting hand and body movements to control virtual objects or navigate virtual environments.
- Voice recognition allows users to interact with virtual environments using spoken commands.
- Eye tracking involves tracking the movement of the user's eyes to determine where they are looking and can be used to control virtual objects or navigate virtual environments.
- These input methods can provide a more natural and intuitive way for users to interact with virtual environments and can enhance the overall user experience.
- It is important to consider the strengths and limitations of each input method when designing 3D interaction techniques for virtual environments.
