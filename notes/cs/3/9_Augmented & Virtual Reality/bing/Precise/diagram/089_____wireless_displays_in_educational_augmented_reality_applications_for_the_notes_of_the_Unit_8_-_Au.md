### Unit 8 - Augmented and Mixed Reality: Wireless Displays in Educational Augmented Reality Applications

1. **Introduction**: Wireless displays can be used to transmit uncompressed stereoscopic video signals in real time. Two output devices, a stereoscopic head-mounted display (HMD) and a TFT display module, have been adapted to act as wireless receivers to display wirelessly streamed AR content.

2. **Advantages**: Wireless displays have several advantages for educational AR applications. They can be used to deliver user-specific views or context-sensitive information to each user privately. This is particularly useful when many users collaborate in a virtual environment.

3. **Examples**: Two educational AR applications have been presented to demonstrate and test wireless displays. A number of teaching scenarios have been described where teachers and students greatly benefit from the use of wireless displays .

4. **Challenges**: The use of wired displays can pose several challenges, such as the number of cables and the risk of users stumbling over them. Movement and interaction can also be restricted, and the number of displays/users may have to be limited. Wireless displays can help overcome these challenges.

5. **Conclusion**: Wireless displays have been shown to be a valuable tool in educational AR applications, providing several benefits and overcoming challenges posed by wired displays. Further development and evaluation of these displays is ongoing.
