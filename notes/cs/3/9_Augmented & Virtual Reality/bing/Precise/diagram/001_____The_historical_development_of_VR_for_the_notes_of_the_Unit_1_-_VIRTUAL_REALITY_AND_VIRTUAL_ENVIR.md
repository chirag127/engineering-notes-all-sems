### The historical development of VR

1. The development of virtual reality (VR) technology can be traced back to the 1950s .
2. The first VR head-mounted display (HMD) system, The Sword of Damocles, was invented in 1968 by computer scientist Ivan Sutherland and his student Bob Sproull .
3. Morton Heilig is considered one of the pioneers of VR, having created the first immersive cinema in 1962 .
4. The term “virtual reality” was popularized by Jaron Lanier in the 1980s .
5. The role of the federal government, particularly the Department of Defense and the National Science Foundation, was significant in the early research and development of VR technology in the United States .
6. VPL Research developed a range of VR equipment in the 1980s .
7. Between 1986-1989, Furness developed a flight simulator known as the Super Cockpit, which featured computer-generated 3D maps, advanced infrared and radar imagery, and real-time audio and visual feedback for the pilot .
