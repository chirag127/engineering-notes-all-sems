### LODs for the notes of the Unit 4 - SOFTWARE TECHNOLOGIES in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

- LODs stands for Level of Detail.
- It is a technique used in computer graphics to optimize the rendering of 3D models.
- LODs are used to reduce the complexity of a 3D model as it moves away from the viewer or occupies less space on the screen.
- This is done by creating multiple versions of the same model with varying levels of detail.
- The most detailed version is used when the model is close to the viewer, while less detailed versions are used as the model moves further away.
- This technique can significantly improve the performance of a 3D application by reducing the number of polygons that need to be rendered.
- LODs are commonly used in video games, virtual reality, and augmented reality applications.
- There are several algorithms and techniques used to generate LODs, including mesh simplification, discrete LODs, and continuous LODs.
- The choice of LOD generation technique depends on the specific requirements of the application and the characteristics of the 3D model.
