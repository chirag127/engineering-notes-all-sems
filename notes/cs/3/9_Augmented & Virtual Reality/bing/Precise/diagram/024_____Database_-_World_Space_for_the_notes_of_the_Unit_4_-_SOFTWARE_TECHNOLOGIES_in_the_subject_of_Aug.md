### Database - World Space

Unit 4 - SOFTWARE TECHNOLOGIES in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

1. A database is an organized collection of data, stored and accessed electronically.
2. In the context of Augmented and Virtual Reality, a database can be used to store information about the virtual world, including objects, their properties, and their locations in the virtual space.
3. The world space refers to the virtual environment in which the objects and characters in an AR or VR experience exist.
4. The world space is defined by a coordinate system, which provides a way to specify the position and orientation of objects within the virtual environment.
5. A database can be used to keep track of the state of the virtual world, including the positions and orientations of objects, as well as any changes that occur over time.
6. This information can be used to render the virtual world and to enable interactions between objects and characters within the virtual environment.
7. There are several types of databases that can be used in AR and VR applications, including relational databases, object-oriented databases, and graph databases.
8. The choice of database technology will depend on the specific requirements of the AR or VR application, including the complexity of the virtual world, the number of objects and characters, and the types of interactions that are supported.
