### Tessellated Data

Tessellated data refers to the process of dividing datasets of polygons, sometimes called vertex sets, into suitable structures for rendering. This is commonly used in computer graphics, where data is tessellated into triangles for real-time rendering . Tessellation techniques are often used to manage datasets of polygons and divide them into suitable structures for rendering .

Tessellation can also be used to automatically create a dataset of appropriately sized bins for a full study area . The tessellation can be of various shapes, including triangles, squares, diamonds, and hexagons .

Tessellated designs are not limited to computer graphics and can also appear on textiles, whether woven, stitched, or printed. Tessellation patterns have been used to design interlocking motifs of patch shapes in quilts .
