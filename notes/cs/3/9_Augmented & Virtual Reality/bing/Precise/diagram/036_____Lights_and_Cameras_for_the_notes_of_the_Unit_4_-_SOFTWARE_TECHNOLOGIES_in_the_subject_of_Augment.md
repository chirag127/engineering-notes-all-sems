### Lights and Cameras

#### Unit 4 - SOFTWARE TECHNOLOGIES

#### Augmented & Virtual Reality

1. **Lights** are essential components in Augmented and Virtual Reality as they help to create realistic and immersive environments.
2. **Directional lights** simulate light sources that are infinitely far away, such as the sun. They produce parallel light rays and cast shadows.
3. **Point lights** emit light in all directions from a single point in space. They are useful for simulating light sources such as light bulbs.
4. **Spotlights** emit light in a cone shape and are useful for simulating light sources such as flashlights or stage lights.
5. **Cameras** are also essential components in Augmented and Virtual Reality as they provide the user's viewpoint and perspective.
6. **Perspective cameras** simulate the way the human eye sees the world, with objects appearing smaller as they get further away.
7. **Orthographic cameras** do not take distance into account and objects appear the same size regardless of their distance from the camera.
8. **Camera properties** such as field of view, aspect ratio, and near and far clipping planes can be adjusted to achieve the desired effect.
