### Architecture for the notes of the Unit 7 - VIRTUAL REALITY APPLICATIONS in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

1. Virtual Reality (VR) architecture refers to the use of VR technology in the design and planning of buildings and structures.
2. VR architecture allows architects and designers to create and visualize 3D models of their designs in a virtual environment.
3. This technology enables architects to experiment with different design options, materials, and lighting conditions in a virtual space before construction begins.
4. VR architecture can also be used to create virtual tours of buildings, allowing clients and stakeholders to experience the design before it is built.
5. VR technology can also be used for training and education purposes, allowing students to explore and interact with virtual architectural models.
6. The use of VR in architecture can improve communication and collaboration between architects, clients, and contractors, and can help to reduce errors and costs during the construction process.
7. VR architecture is an emerging field, and new developments in VR technology are likely to have a significant impact on the way architects design and plan buildings in the future.
