### Bounding Volume

- A bounding volume is a shape that completely encloses an object or a group of objects.
- Bounding volumes are used to simplify collision detection and other intersection tests.
- Common types of bounding volumes include spheres, axis-aligned bounding boxes (AABBs), and oriented bounding boxes (OBBs).
- The choice of bounding volume depends on the shape of the object and the desired trade-off between accuracy and computational efficiency.
- Bounding volumes can be organized into hierarchies to further speed up intersection tests.
- Bounding volume hierarchies (BVHs) are commonly used in computer graphics, physics simulation, and other fields where fast intersection tests are important.
