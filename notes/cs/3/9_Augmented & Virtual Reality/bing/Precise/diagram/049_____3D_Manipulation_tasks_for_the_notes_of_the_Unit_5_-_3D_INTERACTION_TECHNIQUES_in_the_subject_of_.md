### 3D Manipulation tasks for the notes of the Unit 5 - 3D INTERACTION TECHNIQUES in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

1. 3D manipulation is a fundamental task for many different scientific datasets, such as particle data in physics and astronomy, fluid data in aerography, and structured data in medical science.
2. Application-Specific Manipulation Tasks: Canonical tasks can fail to capture important task properties for real applications. For example, positioning a medical probe relative to virtual models of internal organs in a VR medical training application. Techniques must capture and replicate minute details of such manipulation tasks.
3. Broad definition of 3D Manipulation Tasks: any act of physically handling objects with one or two hands. Narrower definition: spatial rigid object manipulation (shape preserving).
4. Canonical 3D manipulation tasks include select, position, and rotate. Variables that affect manipulation include distance to object, object size, required translation distance, amount of rotation, object's density, and others. The design of manipulation techniques depends on interaction tasks and variables.
