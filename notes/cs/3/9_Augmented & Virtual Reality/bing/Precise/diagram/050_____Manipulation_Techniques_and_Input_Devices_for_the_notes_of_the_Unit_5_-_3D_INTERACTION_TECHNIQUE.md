# Manipulation Techniques and Input Devices for 3D INTERACTION TECHNIQUES in Augmented & Virtual Reality

- Manipulation techniques define how input data modify the properties of virtual objects in augmented environments where virtual content overlaps reality .
- Interaction techniques for mobile AR often use the multi-touch capabilities of the device's display for object selection and manipulation .
- To provide full 3D manipulation by touch in an integral way, existing approaches use complex multi finger and hand gestures .
- Three main interaction technique categories applicable in handheld mobile AR are touch-based interaction, mid-air gestures-based interaction, and device-based interaction techniques .
- The proposed interaction technique offers high precision when selecting and manipulating distant objects thanks to the zooming-based idea .
