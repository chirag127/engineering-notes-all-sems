### Environment Centered Wayfinding Support

- Wayfinding is the cognitive aspect of navigation in 3D environments.
- The effectiveness of wayfinding depends on the number and quality of the wayfinding cues or aids provided.
- There are two types of wayfinding aids: User-centered and Environment-centered.
- User-centered wayfinding aids make use of human perception.
- Environment-centered wayfinding aids depend on the virtual world.
- Environment-centered wayfinding support is a technique used in 3D interaction in Augmented and Virtual Reality.
- It is important for software and hardware developers to understand the interaction principles and techniques peculiar to a 3D environment.
- This understanding builds on usability experience with 2D user interfaces but also involves new and unique challenges and opportunities.
- Virtual Reality is an important method to analyze people's behavior during wayfinding.
