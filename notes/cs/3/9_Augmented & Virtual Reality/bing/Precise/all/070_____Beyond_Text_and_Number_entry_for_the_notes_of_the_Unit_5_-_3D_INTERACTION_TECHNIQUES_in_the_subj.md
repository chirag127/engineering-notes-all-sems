### Beyond Text and Number entry

Unit 5 - 3D INTERACTION TECHNIQUES in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

- Beyond text and number entry, there are several other ways to interact with virtual and augmented reality environments.
- These include gesture recognition, voice commands, and eye tracking.
- Gesture recognition involves interpreting hand and body movements to perform actions in the virtual environment.
- Voice commands allow users to speak commands to interact with the virtual environment.
- Eye tracking involves tracking the movement of the user's eyes to determine where they are looking and to perform actions based on their gaze.
- These techniques allow for more natural and intuitive interaction with virtual and augmented reality environments.
- They also provide more options for users with disabilities or limitations in their ability to use traditional input methods such as keyboards and mice.
- The development and implementation of these techniques is an active area of research in the field of virtual and augmented reality.
