# Unit 1 - VIRTUAL REALITY AND VIRTUAL ENVIRONMENTS

## Computer Graphics

- Computer graphics is the field of visual computing, where one utilizes computers both to generate visual images synthetically and to integrate or alter visual and spatial information sampled from the real world.
- Computer graphics is responsible for displaying art and image data effectively and meaningfully to the user.
- It is used to produce illustrations for reports or to generate slide presentations for lectures.
- It is also used for the production of motion pictures, for the design of magazines, newspapers, books, and other publications, and for the display of information in a wide variety of fields, including science, medicine, engineering, and business.
- Computer graphics is used in the development of video games, simulations, and virtual reality environments.
- The field of computer graphics includes a wide variety of techniques and technologies, including 2D and 3D modeling, animation, rendering, and image processing.
- The use of computer graphics in virtual reality environments allows for the creation of immersive and interactive experiences, where users can interact with virtual objects and environments in a realistic manner.
- Computer graphics plays a crucial role in the development of virtual reality technology, as it is responsible for generating the visual content that users see and interact with in virtual environments.
