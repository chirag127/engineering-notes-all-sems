# Unit 1 - VIRTUAL REALITY AND VIRTUAL ENVIRONMENTS

### Virtual environments

- A virtual environment is a computer-generated simulation of a three-dimensional world that can be interacted with in a seemingly real or physical way by a person using special electronic equipment.
- Virtual environments can be used for a variety of purposes, including entertainment, education, training, and research.
- Virtual environments can be created using a variety of technologies, including computer graphics, virtual reality headsets, and motion tracking systems.
- Virtual environments can be designed to simulate real-world environments or to create entirely new and imaginative worlds.
- Virtual environments can provide users with a sense of presence, allowing them to feel as though they are actually inside the virtual world.
- Virtual environments can be used to study human behavior and cognition in a controlled setting, allowing researchers to conduct experiments that would be difficult or impossible to carry out in the real world.
- Virtual environments can also be used to train individuals in various tasks, such as operating complex machinery or performing medical procedures, in a safe and controlled environment.
- Virtual environments can provide a platform for social interaction, allowing users to communicate and collaborate with others in a virtual space.
