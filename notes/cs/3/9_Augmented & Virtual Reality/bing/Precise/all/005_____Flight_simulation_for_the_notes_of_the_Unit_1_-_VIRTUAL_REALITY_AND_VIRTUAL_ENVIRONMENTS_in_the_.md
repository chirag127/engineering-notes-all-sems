### Flight Simulation

Flight simulation is the use of specialized software and hardware to create a realistic experience of flying an aircraft. It is used for a variety of purposes, including training pilots, testing aircraft designs, and conducting research.

1. **Training**: Flight simulators are used to train pilots in a safe and controlled environment. They can simulate various weather conditions, emergency situations, and other scenarios that a pilot may encounter while flying. This allows pilots to practice their skills and gain experience without the risk of an actual flight.

2. **Testing**: Flight simulators are also used to test new aircraft designs. Engineers can use the simulator to evaluate how the aircraft will perform under different conditions and make adjustments to the design before building a physical prototype.

3. **Research**: Flight simulators are used by researchers to study various aspects of flight, such as aerodynamics, human factors, and air traffic control. The data collected from these simulations can be used to improve aircraft design, safety, and efficiency.

Flight simulation is an important tool in the field of aviation, providing a safe and cost-effective way to train pilots, test new designs, and conduct research. It is a key component of the virtual reality and virtual environments unit in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality.
