# Scientific landmarks for the notes of the Unit 1 - VIRTUAL REALITY AND VIRTUAL ENVIRONMENTS in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

1. 1962: Morton Heilig's Sensorama, an arcade-style theatre cabinet, is one of the earliest known examples of immersive, multi-sensory technology.
2. 1968: Ivan Sutherland creates the first head-mounted display (HMD), called the Sword of Damocles.
3. 1987: Jaron Lanier coins the term "virtual reality" and founds VPL Research, which develops and sells VR goggles and gloves.
4. 1991: Virtuality Group launches a range of arcade games and machines, allowing players to experience VR.
5. 1993: SEGA announces the SEGA VR headset for arcade games and the Mega Drive console.
6. 1995: Nintendo releases the Virtual Boy, a 3D gaming console that uses a VR-style head-mounted display.
7. 2010: Palmer Luckey creates the first prototype of the Oculus Rift, a consumer-targeted VR headset.
8. 2014: Facebook acquires Oculus VR for $2 billion.
9. 2016: The release of several consumer VR headsets, including the Oculus Rift, HTC Vive, and PlayStation VR, marks the beginning of the current wave of VR technology.
10. 2018: The release of standalone VR headsets, such as the Oculus Go and the Lenovo Mirage Solo, eliminates the need for external devices to power the VR experience.
