# Unit 1 - VIRTUAL REALITY AND VIRTUAL ENVIRONMENTS

1. **Virtual Reality (VR)** is a computer-generated simulation of a three-dimensional environment that can be interacted with in a seemingly real or physical way by a person using special electronic equipment, such as a helmet with a screen inside or gloves fitted with sensors.
2. **Virtual Environments (VE)** are computer-generated, interactive, three-dimensional environments in which the user is immersed. VEs can be used for a variety of purposes, including training, education, entertainment, and therapy.
3. VR and VE technologies have advanced significantly in recent years, with the development of more sophisticated hardware and software, and the increasing availability of high-speed internet connections.
4. VR and VE can be experienced through a variety of devices, including head-mounted displays, motion controllers, and haptic feedback devices.
5. The use of VR and VE has the potential to revolutionize many industries, including gaming, education, healthcare, and tourism.
6. However, there are also concerns about the potential negative effects of VR and VE, such as addiction, social isolation, and the potential for desensitization to violence.
7. As VR and VE technologies continue to evolve, it is important for researchers, developers, and policymakers to consider both the potential benefits and risks associated with their use.
