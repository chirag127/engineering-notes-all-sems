### Auditory Displays

Auditory displays are an important component of 3D user interfaces for virtual environments. The main goals of auditory displays are localization, the generation of 3D sound, and sonification, which is the transformation of certain types of information into sound . Auditory output is especially useful in collaborative environments.

The perception of the spatial properties of a sound field is an important component of the overall perception of real sound fields. The location of a sound source is a variable that can be used to increase the information transfer rate achieved with an auditory display. It has been a central research focus in the simulation of acoustic environments for virtual environment (VE) systems .

Auditory displays and auditory user interfaces involve a diverse collection of approaches, including art, design, science, and research. New theories, frameworks, methods, and applications are being developed in this field .

Display devices, or output devices, present information to one or more of the user’s senses through the human perceptual system. The majority of them are focused on stimulating the visual, auditory, or haptic (i.e., force and touch) senses .
