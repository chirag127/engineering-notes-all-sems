# Unit 8 - Augmented and Mixed Reality

### Technology and Features of Augmented Reality

Augmented Reality (AR) is the technology that superimposes an image onto a user’s view of the real world and enhances it with sound, touch, and even smell. It is a combination of the real scene viewed by the user and a virtual scene generated by the computer .

AR incorporates three features:
1. A combination of digital and physical worlds.
2. Interactions made in real-time.
3. Accurate 3D identification of virtual and real objects .

Augmented reality offers a better way to design, curate, and deliver consumable instructions by overlaying digital content in real-world work environments . It is commonly used in electronic first-person shooter games to add environmental, health, and other information to players’ viewpoints .

Various militaries have begun to experiment with adding similar overlays to real soldiers using personal head-mounted visors . AR can also be integrated into employee training and education in retail and manufacturing, offering step-by-step instructions and allowing trainers to provide more effective training .
