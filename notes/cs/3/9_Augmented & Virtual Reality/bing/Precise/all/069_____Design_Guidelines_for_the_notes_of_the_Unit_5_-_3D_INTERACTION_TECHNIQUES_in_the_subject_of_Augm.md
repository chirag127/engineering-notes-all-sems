# Design Guidelines for the notes of the Unit 5 - 3D INTERACTION TECHNIQUES in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

1. Start by defining what 3D interaction techniques are and their importance in the field of Augmented and Virtual Reality.
2. Discuss the different types of 3D interaction techniques, such as selection, manipulation, navigation, and system control.
3. For each type of 3D interaction technique, provide examples and explain how they are used in Augmented and Virtual Reality applications.
4. Discuss the design considerations for 3D interaction techniques, such as usability, efficiency, and user experience.
5. Provide guidelines for designing effective 3D interaction techniques, such as considering the user's physical abilities and limitations, providing clear feedback, and minimizing cognitive load.
6. Conclude by summarizing the key points and emphasizing the importance of well-designed 3D interaction techniques in enhancing the user experience in Augmented and Virtual Reality applications.
