### Interaction - Simple

Interaction in the context of software technologies, particularly in Augmented and Virtual Reality, refers to the ways in which users can interact with the virtual environment and its elements. Simple interactions are those that are easy to understand and perform, and do not require extensive training or practice.

Some examples of simple interactions in Augmented and Virtual Reality include:
- **Gaze-based interaction**: This involves the user looking at a specific point or object in the virtual environment to trigger an action or event.
- **Hand gestures**: Simple hand gestures, such as pointing or waving, can be used to interact with virtual objects or navigate the virtual environment.
- **Voice commands**: Users can issue voice commands to perform actions or interact with virtual objects.
- **Controller input**: Physical controllers, such as gamepads or motion controllers, can be used to perform simple interactions in the virtual environment.

These simple interactions allow users to easily and intuitively interact with the virtual environment, enhancing their immersion and overall experience. They are commonly used in a wide range of applications, from gaming and entertainment to education and training.
