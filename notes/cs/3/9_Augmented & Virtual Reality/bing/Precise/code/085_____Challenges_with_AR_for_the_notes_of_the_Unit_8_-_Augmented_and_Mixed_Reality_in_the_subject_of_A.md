### Challenges with AR

1. **Hardware limitations:** AR requires powerful hardware to run smoothly, which can be expensive and not easily accessible to everyone.
2. **Limited content:** There is a limited amount of AR content available, which can limit the user experience.
3. **User experience:** AR can be difficult to use and navigate, which can lead to a frustrating user experience.
4. **Privacy concerns:** AR can collect a large amount of data about the user, which raises privacy concerns.
5. **Safety concerns:** AR can distract users from their surroundings, which can lead to safety concerns.
6. **Technical challenges:** AR technology is still in its early stages and there are many technical challenges that need to be overcome.
