## Unit 1 - VIRTUAL REALITY AND VIRTUAL ENVIRONMENTS

1. **Virtual Reality (VR)** is a computer-generated simulation of a three-dimensional environment that can be interacted with in a seemingly real or physical way by a person using special electronic equipment, such as a helmet with a screen inside or gloves fitted with sensors.
2. **Virtual Environments (VE)** are computer-generated, interactive, three-dimensional environments in which a user is immersed. VEs can simulate real-world environments or create entirely new, imaginary environments.
3. VR and VE technologies have advanced significantly in recent years, with improvements in computer processing power, graphics, and display technologies.
4. VR and VE have a wide range of applications, including gaming, education, training, therapy, and design.
5. There are several types of VR systems, including head-mounted displays (HMDs), CAVE systems, and desktop systems.
6. VR and VE can create a sense of presence, where the user feels as though they are actually inside the virtual environment.
7. There are several challenges associated with VR and VE, including motion sickness, the need for high-quality graphics and realistic interactions, and the high cost of some VR systems.
8. Despite these challenges, VR and VE have the potential to revolutionize many fields and provide new opportunities for learning, entertainment, and exploration.
