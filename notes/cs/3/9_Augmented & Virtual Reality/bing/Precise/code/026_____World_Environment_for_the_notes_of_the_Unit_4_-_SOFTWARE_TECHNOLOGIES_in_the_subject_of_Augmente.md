### World Environment

Unit 4 - SOFTWARE TECHNOLOGIES in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

- Virtual Reality (VR) is a technology that allows users to experience a 3D-simulated environment through a medium like a VR display or a headset.
- Augmented Reality (AR) enables users to experience a perfect blend of the digital world and physical elements to create an artificial environment.
- AR focuses on the interaction between the user and the virtual information registered in a 3-D space.
- Both VR and AR technologies are rapidly expanding across industry, academia, and government and intertwining the physical and digital worlds.
- Connecting more people to AR and VR technologies could transform everything from education and healthcare to mining and tourism.
- AR overlays digital content onto representations of the real environment, using smartphones, tablets, or AR glasses.
