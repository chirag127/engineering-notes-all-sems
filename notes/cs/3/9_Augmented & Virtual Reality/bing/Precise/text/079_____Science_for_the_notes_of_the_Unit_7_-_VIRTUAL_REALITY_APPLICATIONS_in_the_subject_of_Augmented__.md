### Science for the notes of the Unit 7 - VIRTUAL REALITY APPLICATIONS in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

1. Virtual Reality (VR) is a computer-generated simulation of a three-dimensional environment that can be interacted with in a seemingly real way by a person using special electronic equipment.
2. VR has a wide range of applications in various fields, including science.
3. In the field of science, VR can be used for scientific visualization, allowing scientists to interact with and explore complex data sets in a more intuitive and immersive way.
4. VR can also be used for scientific training, allowing students and researchers to practice and learn in a safe and controlled environment.
5. Another application of VR in science is remote collaboration, allowing scientists from different locations to work together in a shared virtual environment.
6. VR can also be used for scientific outreach and education, allowing the general public to experience and learn about scientific concepts in a more engaging and interactive way.
7. Overall, VR has the potential to revolutionize the way science is conducted, taught, and communicated.
