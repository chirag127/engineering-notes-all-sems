### LODs for the notes of the Unit 4 - SOFTWARE TECHNOLOGIES in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

- LODs stands for **Level of Detail**.
- It is a technique used in computer graphics to manage the complexity of 3D models.
- It involves displaying different levels of detail for a 3D model depending on its distance from the camera.
- This helps to maintain a high frame rate and reduce the load on the graphics hardware.
- In the context of Augmented and Virtual Reality, LODs can be used to optimize the performance of the application.
- There are several techniques for implementing LODs, including mesh simplification, texture mapping, and impostors.
- Choosing the right LOD technique depends on the specific requirements of the application and the hardware it is running on.
