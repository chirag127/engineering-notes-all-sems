### 3D Manipulation tasks for the notes of the Unit 5 - 3D INTERACTION TECHNIQUES in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

- 3D manipulation is a fundamental task for many different scientific datasets, such as particle data in physics and astronomy, fluid data in aerography, and structured data in medical science.
- Canonical tasks can fail to capture important task properties for real applications. For example, positioning a medical probe relative to virtual models of internal organs in a VR medical training application. Techniques must capture and replicate minute details of such manipulation tasks.
- 3D Manipulation Tasks can be defined broadly as any act of physically handling objects with one or two hands or narrowly as spatial rigid object manipulation (shape preserving).
- Canonical 3D manipulation tasks include select, position and rotate. Variables that affect manipulation include distance to object, object size, required translation distance, amount of rotation, object's density, and others. The design of manipulation techniques depends on interaction tasks and variables.
