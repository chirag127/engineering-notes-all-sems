### Tools for the notes of the Unit 5 - 3D INTERACTION TECHNIQUES in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

1. **3D Modeling Software:** Tools such as Blender, Maya, and 3ds Max can be used to create 3D models for use in virtual and augmented reality environments.
2. **Game Engines:** Game engines such as Unity and Unreal Engine provide a platform for developing interactive 3D content for virtual and augmented reality applications.
3. **Input Devices:** Devices such as motion controllers, haptic gloves, and hand tracking systems can be used to interact with virtual and augmented reality environments.
4. **Head-Mounted Displays:** Head-mounted displays such as the Oculus Rift, HTC Vive, and Microsoft HoloLens provide an immersive experience by displaying virtual and augmented reality content directly in front of the user's eyes.
5. **Tracking Systems:** Tracking systems such as the Oculus Insight and Lighthouse can be used to track the position and orientation of the user and input devices in virtual and augmented reality environments.
