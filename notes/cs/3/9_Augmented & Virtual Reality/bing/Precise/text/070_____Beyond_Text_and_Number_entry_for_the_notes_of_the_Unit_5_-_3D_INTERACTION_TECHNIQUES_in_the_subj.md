### Beyond Text and Number Entry

Unit 5 - 3D INTERACTION TECHNIQUES in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

- Beyond text and number entry, there are several other ways to interact with 3D environments in augmented and virtual reality.
- One such technique is gesture recognition, where the system can recognize and interpret specific hand or body movements as commands.
- Another technique is voice recognition, where the system can understand and respond to spoken commands.
- Eye tracking is also used, where the system can track the user's eye movements and use them as input.
- Haptic feedback is another technique, where the system can provide physical feedback to the user through vibrations or other sensations.
- These techniques allow for more natural and intuitive interactions with 3D environments in augmented and virtual reality. They can also enhance the user's sense of presence and immersion in the virtual world.
