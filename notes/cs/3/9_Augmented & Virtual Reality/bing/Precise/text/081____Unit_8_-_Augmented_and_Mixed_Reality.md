## Unit 8 - Augmented and Mixed Reality

1. **Augmented Reality (AR)** is a technology that overlays digital information onto the real world, enhancing the user's perception of their environment.
2. **Mixed Reality (MR)** is a technology that combines elements of both virtual and augmented reality, allowing users to interact with digital objects in the real world.
3. AR and MR technologies have a wide range of applications, including gaming, education, and marketing.
4. AR and MR technologies are rapidly evolving, with advancements in hardware and software making these experiences more immersive and interactive.
5. AR and MR technologies have the potential to revolutionize the way we interact with the world around us, providing new opportunities for learning, entertainment, and communication.
