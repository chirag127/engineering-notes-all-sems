### Available software in the market for the notes of the Unit 4 - SOFTWARE TECHNOLOGIES in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

- **Unity** is a popular software for developing augmented reality applications. It offers an AR Foundation package that allows developers to work with augmented reality platforms in a multi-platform way within Unity .
- **Vuforia Engine** is another popular software development kit (SDK) for developing augmented reality applications. It enables developers to create engaging 3D product demonstrations and applications, connecting people to products through an immersive AR experience .
- There are also resources available for bringing AR and VR to the classroom, such as the 23 resources listed by the International Society for Technology in Education (ISTE) .
