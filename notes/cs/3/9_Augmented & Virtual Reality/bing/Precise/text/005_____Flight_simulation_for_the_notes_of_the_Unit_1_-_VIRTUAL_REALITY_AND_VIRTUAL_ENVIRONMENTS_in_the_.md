### Flight Simulation

Flight simulation is the use of specialized software and hardware to simulate the experience of flying an aircraft. It is used for a variety of purposes, including pilot training, aircraft design, and research.

1. **Pilot Training:** Flight simulators are used to train pilots in a safe and controlled environment. They can simulate various flight conditions and scenarios, allowing pilots to practice their skills and prepare for real-life situations.

2. **Aircraft Design:** Flight simulation software can be used to test and evaluate the design of new aircraft. Engineers can use the software to simulate the performance of the aircraft under different conditions and make adjustments to the design as needed.

3. **Research:** Flight simulation can also be used for research purposes. For example, researchers can use flight simulators to study the effects of turbulence on aircraft and develop new technologies to improve flight safety.

Flight simulation is an important tool in the field of aviation, providing a safe and cost-effective way to train pilots, test new aircraft designs, and conduct research. It is a key component of the virtual reality and virtual environments unit in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality.
