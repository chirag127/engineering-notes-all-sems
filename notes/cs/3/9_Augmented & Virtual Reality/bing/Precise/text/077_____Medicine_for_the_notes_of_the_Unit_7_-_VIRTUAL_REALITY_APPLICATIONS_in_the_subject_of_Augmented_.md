### Medicine for the notes of the Unit 7 - VIRTUAL REALITY APPLICATIONS in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

Virtual Reality (VR) is being used in medicine in a variety of applications. Some of the most common uses for VR in medicine include:

1. **Medical training:** VR is being used to train medical professionals, including doctors and nurses, in various procedures and techniques. This allows for a more immersive and realistic training experience .

2. **Emergency training:** VR is also being used to train medical professionals in emergency situations, allowing them to practice their skills in a safe and controlled environment .

3. **Treating mental illnesses:** VR is being used to help diagnose and treat mental illnesses such as anxiety and depression .

4. **Telemedicine:** VR is being used in telemedicine to allow doctors to remotely examine and treat patients .

5. **Rehabilitation and physical therapy:** VR is being used in rehabilitation and physical therapy to help patients recover from injuries and illnesses .

6. **Mental wellness:** VR is being used to promote mental wellness and relaxation .

These are just a few examples of the many ways in which VR is being used in medicine. The technology is constantly evolving, and new applications are being developed all the time.
