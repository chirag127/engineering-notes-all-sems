### Mobile Projection Interfaces

Mobile projection interfaces are a type of interface that allows users to interact with digital content in the physical world. These interfaces use small-scale projectors that can be integrated into mobile devices such as smartphones, allowing users to project digital imagery into physical space virtually anywhere and anytime.

One of the major limitations of Augmented Reality (AR) is that it always needs some type of interface device, whether it is a smartphone or head-mounted AR glass, to interact with AR content. This can limit its usage. Projection-based Augmented Reality is a solution to this limitation.

With a built-in projector, not only can the graphical scale of the applications be increased, but the range of possibilities for developing mobile augmented reality applications will also widen.

Mobile Augmented Reality (MAR) integrates computer-generated virtual objects with physical environments for mobile devices. MAR systems enable users to interact with MAR devices, such as smartphones and head-worn wearables, and perform seamless transitions from the physical world to a mixed world with digital entities.

Mixed Reality (MR) is the use of both Virtual Reality (VR) and Augmented Reality (AR) technologies to create an environment where physical and virtual objects can exist and interact in real-time. MR overlays images or videos over a screen showing reality through a mobile camera, smart glasses, or headsets.
