### Scientific landmarks for the notes of the Unit 1 - VIRTUAL REALITY AND VIRTUAL ENVIRONMENTS in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

1. 1962: Morton Heilig's Sensorama, an arcade-style theatre cabinet, is one of the earliest known examples of immersive, multi-sensory technology.
2. 1968: Ivan Sutherland, with the help of his student Bob Sproull, created the first virtual reality head-mounted display (HMD) system.
3. 1987: Jaron Lanier, founder of the visual programming lab (VPL) Research, popularized the term "virtual reality".
4. 1991: Virtuality Group launched a range of arcade games and machines, which allowed people to experience virtual reality by wearing VR headsets and using joysticks.
5. 2010: Palmer Luckey, founder of Oculus VR, developed the first prototype of the Oculus Rift, a virtual reality headset.
6. 2014: Facebook acquired Oculus VR for $2 billion.
7. 2016: The release of consumer versions of virtual reality headsets such as the Oculus Rift, HTC Vive, and PlayStation VR marked the beginning of the current wave of virtual reality.
8. 2018: The release of standalone virtual reality headsets such as the Oculus Go and the Lenovo Mirage Solo, which do not require a PC or a smartphone to operate, made virtual reality more accessible to the general public.
