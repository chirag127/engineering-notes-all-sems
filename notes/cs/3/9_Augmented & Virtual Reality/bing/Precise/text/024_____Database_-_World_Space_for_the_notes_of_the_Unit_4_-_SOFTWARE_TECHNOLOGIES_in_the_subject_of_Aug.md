### Database - World Space

1. A database is a structured collection of data that is stored and organized in a way that allows for easy retrieval and manipulation of the information.
2. In the context of Augmented and Virtual Reality, a database can be used to store information about the virtual world, including objects, their properties, and their relationships to one another.
3. World space refers to the virtual environment in which the objects and characters in an AR or VR experience exist.
4. A world space database can be used to keep track of the positions, orientations, and other properties of objects in the virtual world.
5. This information can be used to render the virtual world in real-time, allowing for a seamless and immersive experience for the user.
6. The use of a database in AR and VR can also facilitate the sharing of information between multiple users, allowing for collaborative experiences in a shared virtual space.
7. There are several types of databases that can be used in AR and VR, including relational databases, object-oriented databases, and graph databases.
8. The choice of database will depend on the specific needs of the application, including the complexity of the data and the desired level of performance.
