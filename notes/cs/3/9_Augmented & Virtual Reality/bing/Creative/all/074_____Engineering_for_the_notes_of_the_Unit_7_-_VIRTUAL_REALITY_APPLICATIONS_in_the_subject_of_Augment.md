# Engineering

## Unit 7 - Virtual Reality Applications

### Virtual Reality in Engineering

- Virtual reality (VR) is the use of computer modeling and simulation that enables a person to interact with an artificial three-dimensional (3D) visual or other sensory environment.
- VR applications immerse the user in a computer-generated environment that simulates reality through the use of interactive devices, such as headsets, controllers, gloves, etc.
- VR technology is used with special software (an application) and hardware (the VR headset) that immerse engineers in a virtual environment.
- VR engineering includes the use of 3D modelling tools and visualisation techniques as part of the design process .
- This technology enables engineers to view their project in 3D and gain a greater understanding of how it works .
- Plus they can spot any flaws or potential risks before implementation .
- VR can also be used to conduct experiments, test scenarios, train staff, and collaborate with other engineers remotely.

#### Examples and Applications of VR in Engineering

- In design and engineering, VR can be used to model and design new hardware technologies, such as engines, machines, vehicles, etc.
- An environment will be created with different engineering parts being accessible.
- Engineers can manipulate, assemble, and disassemble the parts in VR, as well as test their functionality and performance.
- VR can also be used to create realistic simulations of the operating conditions and environments of the engineered products, such as weather, terrain, traffic, etc.
- This can help engineers to evaluate the feasibility, safety, and efficiency of their designs.
- VR can also be used to train engineers and technicians on how to operate, maintain, and repair the engineered products in a safe and cost-effective way.
- For example, VR can be used to train pilots, drivers, mechanics, etc.
- VR can also be used to facilitate collaboration and communication among engineers and other stakeholders, such as clients, managers, suppliers, etc.
- VR can enable engineers to share their designs and ideas in a virtual space, where they can interact and provide feedback in real time.
- VR can also be used to present and demonstrate the engineered products to potential customers and investors, as well as to educate and inform the public about the engineering projects.
