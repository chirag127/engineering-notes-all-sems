## Unit 7 - Virtual Reality Applications

### What are virtual reality applications?

- Virtual reality applications are applications that make use of virtual reality (VR), an immersive sensory experience that digitally simulates a virtual environment.
- VR applications immerse the user in a computer-generated environment that simulates reality through the use of interactive devices, such as goggles, headsets, gloves, or body suits.
- VR applications enable the user to explore and interact with virtual surroundings, and in some cases, also their physical environment.

### What are some examples of virtual reality applications?

- VR applications can be used for various purposes, such as entertainment, education, business, health, art, and social interaction.
- Some examples of VR applications are:

  - Google Expeditions: A VR app that allows users to go on virtual field trips to different places, such as museums, landmarks, and natural wonders.
  - Allumette: A VR app that tells a story of love, sacrifice, and a bond between a mother and her child in a fantastical city in the clouds.
  - Colosse: A VR app that showcases a real-time, interactive story about a colossal creature and its environment.
  - Titans of Space Plus: A VR app that takes users on a guided tour of the solar system and beyond, with detailed information and realistic visuals.
  - Google Earth VR: A VR app that lets users explore the world from a new perspective, flying over cities, mountains, and oceans.
  - Kingspray Graffiti VR: A VR app that allows users to create and share graffiti art on realistic walls, trains, and rooftops.
  - Littlstar: A VR app that offers a platform for watching and creating immersive 360-degree videos and photos.
  - Ocean Rift: A VR app that simulates an underwater world, where users can swim with various marine animals and explore different habitats.
  - The Foo Show: A VR app that features interviews with creators and artists, as well as interactive segments that showcase their work.
  - Tilt Brush: A VR app that lets users paint in 3D space, using different brushes, colors, and effects.
  - Virtual Desktop: A VR app that allows users to use their computer in VR, with multiple monitors, custom backgrounds, and various settings.
  - Within: A VR app that offers a collection of immersive stories, documentaries, and experiences from different genres and creators.
  - Fulldive VR: A VR app that provides a social network for VR users, where they can watch and upload VR content, chat with friends, and play games.

### What are some benefits and challenges of virtual reality applications?

- VR applications can offer many benefits, such as:

  - Enhancing the user's sense of presence, immersion, and engagement in the virtual environment.
  - Providing a safe and cost-effective way to simulate and train for various scenarios, such as medical procedures, military operations, or disaster response.
  - Enabling the user to access and experience places, events, and cultures that are otherwise difficult or impossible to reach.
  - Supporting the user's creativity, expression, and learning through interactive and personalized features.
  - Facilitating the user's social interaction and collaboration with other users across distances and boundaries.

- VR applications can also pose some challenges, such as:

  - Causing the user to experience physical discomfort, such as nausea, dizziness, or eye strain, due to the mismatch between the visual and vestibular cues.
  - Exposing the user to potential psychological risks, such as anxiety, addiction, or dissociation, due to the intensity and realism of the virtual environment.
  - Raising ethical and legal issues, such as privacy, consent, and ownership, regarding the use and creation of VR content and data.
  - Requiring high-quality and compatible hardware and software, as well as reliable and fast internet connection, to ensure a smooth and satisfying VR experience.
