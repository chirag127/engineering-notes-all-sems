# Requirements for VR

- VR stands for virtual reality, which is a simulated environment that immerses the user in a realistic or imaginary world using a head-mounted display (HMD) and other devices.
- VR requires a high-performance PC or laptop that can handle the graphics, audio, and tracking demands of VR applications.
- VR also requires compatible VR hardware, such as HMDs, controllers, sensors, and cables, that can connect to the PC or laptop and provide the user with input and output capabilities.
- The specific requirements for VR may vary depending on the VR platform, application, and hardware used, but some general guidelines are:

## Minimum Requirements for VR
- These are the basic requirements for running VR applications at low to medium settings and frame rates. They may not provide the best VR experience, but they can still work for some VR applications and hardware.
- 64-bit processor and operating system
- OS: Windows 8 or 10
- Processor: Intel Core i5-4590 / AMD FX 8350 or equivalent
- Memory: 8 GB RAM
- Graphics: NVIDIA GeForce GTX 970 / AMD Radeon R9 290 or equivalent
- Hard Drive: At least 100 GB of free space, preferably SSD
- Two USB 3.0 ports
- HDMI 1.4 or DisplayPort 1.2
- Compatible VR hardware, such as Oculus Rift, HTC Vive, or Windows Mixed Reality

## Recommended Requirements for VR
- These are the optimal requirements for running VR applications at high to ultra settings and frame rates. They can provide a smooth and immersive VR experience, but they may also be more expensive and power-hungry.
- 64-bit processor and operating system
- OS: Windows 10 or 11
- Processor: Intel Core i7-10700K / AMD Ryzen 9 3900X or equivalent
- Memory: 16 GB RAM
- Graphics: NVIDIA GeForce RTX 2080 / AMD Radeon RX 5700 XT or equivalent
- Hard Drive: At least 250 GB of free space, preferably SSD
- Three USB 3.0 ports
- HDMI 2.0 or DisplayPort 1.4
- Compatible VR hardware, such as Valve Index, Oculus Rift S, or HTC Vive Pro

## How to Check if Your PC is VR Ready
- There are several ways to check if your PC meets the requirements for VR, such as:
- Using online tools, such as the SteamVR Performance Test, the Oculus Rift Compatibility Tool, or the Windows Mixed Reality PC Check, that can scan your PC and give you a score or a pass/fail result based on your hardware and software specifications.
- Comparing your PC specifications to the minimum and recommended requirements of the VR platform, application, and hardware you want to use, and making sure you have enough power, memory, storage, and ports to run them.
- Upgrading your PC components, such as the CPU, GPU, RAM, or SSD, if they are below the minimum or recommended requirements, or if you want to improve your VR performance and quality.
