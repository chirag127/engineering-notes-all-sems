# Taxonomy for the notes of the Unit 8 - Augmented and Mixed Reality

- Taxonomy is a system of classification that organizes and categorizes entities based on their characteristics and relationships.
- Augmented reality (AR) is a technology that superimposes virtual information or objects onto the real environment, enhancing the user's perception and interaction with reality.
- Mixed reality (MR) is a technology that merges the real and virtual worlds, creating a hybrid environment where physical and digital objects coexist and interact.
- A taxonomy of AR and MR applications can help to understand the different types, purposes, features, and challenges of these technologies, as well as to guide their design, development, and evaluation.
- Based on the literature review, a possible taxonomy of AR and MR applications can include the following facets and categories:

  - **Educational purpose**: The intended learning outcome or objective of the application, based on the Bloom's taxonomy of educational objectives . Examples of categories are: knowledge, comprehension, application, analysis, synthesis, and evaluation.
  - **Output technology**: The type of device or system that displays the virtual information or objects in the AR or MR environment, based on the Milgram's taxonomy of mixed reality visual displays. Examples of categories are: head-mounted display, handheld display, projection display, spatial display, and shape-changing display.
  - **Visual guidance**: The type of visual cue or feedback that helps the user to perceive, understand, and interact with the virtual information or objects in the AR or MR environment, based on the Langlotz's taxonomy of visualization techniques in AR. Examples of categories are: annotation, augmentation, overlay, occlusion, and deformation.
  - **Patient type**: The type of patient or user that the application is designed for, based on their medical condition, disability, or impairment, based on the Ribeiro's taxonomy of AR and MR applications to support physical rehabilitation. Examples of categories are: stroke, spinal cord injury, cerebral palsy, Parkinson's disease, and amputation.
  - **Medical purpose**: The type of medical goal or benefit that the application aims to achieve or provide for the patient or user, based on the Ribeiro's taxonomy of AR and MR applications to support physical rehabilitation. Examples of categories are: assessment, diagnosis, treatment, prevention, and education.
