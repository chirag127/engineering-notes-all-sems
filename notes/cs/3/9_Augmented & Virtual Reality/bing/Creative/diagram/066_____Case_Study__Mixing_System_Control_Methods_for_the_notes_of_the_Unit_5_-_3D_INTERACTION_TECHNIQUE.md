### Case Study: Mixing System Control Methods for the notes of the Unit 5 - 3D INTERACTION TECHNIQUES in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

- The case study is based on the multiscale simulation of mixing processes using 3D-parallel, fluid-structure interaction techniques.
- The simulation involves the use of a simplified fictitious domain method coupled with a level-set approach to represent the rigid moving boundaries, such as screws and rotors, as well as free surfaces.
- The simulation also requires the use of 3D interaction techniques for common tasks such as selection, manipulation, navigation, and system control .
- The 3D interaction techniques are designed and evaluated according to the principles and strategies of human-computer interaction, such as compatibility, naturalness, multimodality, and two-handedness   .
- The case study demonstrates the benefits and challenges of using 3D interaction techniques for complex and realistic applications, such as mixing processes, that require high performance and accuracy .
