### 3D Mice

- A 3D mouse is a pointing and control device specialized for movement in virtual 3D (three-dimensional) environments.
- It provides this functionality through a variety of methods including accelerometers, multi-axis sensors, and infrared sensors.
- Used in tandem with a traditional mouse, it allows individuals to employ a two-handed approach to their work.
- 3D mice are the preferred tools for designers in many industries because they greatly improve user-comfort, and productivity.
- 3D mice can offer better ways of navigating around objects, letting you jump seamlessly between views or rotate around to make precise alterations.
- Some examples of 3D mice are 3DConnexion SpaceMouse Pro, Razer Naga Trinity, and Logitech MX Vertical .
