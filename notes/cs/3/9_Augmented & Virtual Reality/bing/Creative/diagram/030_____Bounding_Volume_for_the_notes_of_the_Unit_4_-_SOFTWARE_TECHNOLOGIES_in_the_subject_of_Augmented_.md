### Bounding Volume

- A bounding volume is a closed volume that completely contains an object or a set of objects in 3D space.
- Bounding volumes are used to improve the efficiency of geometrical operations, such as ray tracing, collision detection, and visibility tests .
- Bounding volumes can be simple shapes, such as spheres, boxes, cylinders, or convex hulls, or more complex shapes, such as octrees, kd-trees, or BSP trees.
- Bounding volumes can be organized into hierarchies, where each node contains a bounding volume that encloses its children nodes. This allows for fast traversal and culling of objects that are not relevant for the operation.
- Bounding volumes are useful for augmented reality applications, as they can help to detect and track 3D objects in the real world, and to render virtual objects that interact with them .
