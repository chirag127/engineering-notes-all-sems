### Flight simulation

- Flight simulation is the use of computer software and hardware to create a realistic and immersive experience of flying an aircraft in a virtual environment.
- Flight simulation can be used for various purposes, such as:
  - Training and education: Flight simulation can help pilots and students learn and practice the skills and procedures of flying, without the risks and costs of real flights. Flight simulation can also provide feedback and assessment of the performance and progress of the learners.
  - Entertainment and recreation: Flight simulation can offer a fun and engaging way of exploring the world and experiencing the thrill of flying, without the need of a license or a plane. Flight simulation can also support multiplayer and social interactions, such as flying with friends or joining online communities.
  - Research and development: Flight simulation can enable researchers and developers to test and evaluate new designs and technologies of aircraft, flight systems, and environments, without the limitations and constraints of physical prototypes and experiments.
- Flight simulation can be implemented using various technologies, such as:
  - Virtual reality (VR): VR is a technology that creates a fully immersive and interactive 3D environment that simulates the real world or a fictional one. VR can provide a high level of realism and presence for flight simulation, as the user can see, hear, and feel the virtual environment through a VR headset and other devices, such as controllers, gloves, pedals, or seats.
  - Augmented reality (AR): AR is a technology that overlays digital information and graphics onto the real world, enhancing the user's perception and interaction with the environment. AR can provide a mixed reality experience for flight simulation, as the user can see both the real and the virtual elements of the flight, such as the cockpit, the instruments, the scenery, and the weather.
  - Desktop simulation: Desktop simulation is a technology that uses a computer monitor and a keyboard, mouse, or joystick to display and control the flight simulation. Desktop simulation can provide a low-cost and accessible way of flight simulation, as the user can use a standard computer and peripherals, without the need of a VR headset or other devices. However, desktop simulation can also have lower realism and immersion than VR or AR.
