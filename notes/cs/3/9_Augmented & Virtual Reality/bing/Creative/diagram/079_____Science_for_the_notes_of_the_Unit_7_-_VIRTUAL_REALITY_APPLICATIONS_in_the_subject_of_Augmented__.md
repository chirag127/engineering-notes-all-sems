## Unit 7 - Virtual Reality Applications

### What are virtual reality applications?

- Virtual reality applications are applications that make use of virtual reality (VR), an immersive sensory experience that digitally simulates a virtual environment.
- VR applications immerse the user in a computer-generated environment that simulates reality through the use of interactive devices, such as goggles, headsets, gloves, or body suits.
- VR applications enable the user to explore and interact with virtual surroundings, and in some cases, also their physical environment.

### What are some examples of virtual reality applications?

- VR applications can be used for various purposes, such as entertainment, education, business, health, art, and social interaction.
- Some examples of VR applications are:

  - Google Expeditions: A VR app that allows users to go on virtual field trips to different places, such as museums, landmarks, and natural wonders.
  - Allumette: A VR app that tells a story of love, sacrifice, and a bond between a young girl and her mother in a fantastical city in the clouds.
  - Colosse: A VR app that showcases a real-time, interactive story about a colossal creature and its environment.
  - Titans of Space Plus: A VR app that takes users on a guided tour of the solar system and beyond, with detailed information and realistic visuals.
  - Google Earth VR: A VR app that lets users fly over and explore any place on Earth, from cities to mountains to oceans.
  - Kingspray Graffiti VR: A VR app that allows users to create and share graffiti art on realistic walls and surfaces.
  - Littlstar: A VR app that offers a platform for watching and sharing 360-degree videos and photos, as well as live streams and VR cinema.
  - Ocean Rift: A VR app that simulates an underwater world, where users can swim with various marine animals and explore different habitats.
  - The Foo Show: A VR app that features interviews and conversations with creators and artists, as well as interactive segments that showcase their work.
  - Tilt Brush: A VR app that lets users paint in 3D space with virtual brushes and colors, and share their creations with others.
  - Virtual Desktop: A VR app that enables users to use their computer in VR, with multiple monitors and customizable environments.
  - Within: A VR app that offers a collection of immersive stories and experiences, from documentaries to animations to music videos.
  - Fulldive VR: A VR app that provides access to various VR content, such as games, videos, photos, and social media.

### What are some benefits and challenges of virtual reality applications?

- VR applications can offer many benefits, such as:

  - Enhancing the user's sense of presence and immersion in the virtual environment.
  - Providing a safe and controlled way to experience and learn new things, such as skills, cultures, and perspectives .
  - Offering a fun and engaging way to entertain and express oneself, as well as connect with others .
  - Improving the user's well-being and health, such as by reducing stress, pain, and anxiety, and promoting relaxation, fitness, and rehabilitation .

- VR applications can also pose some challenges, such as:

  - Causing physical and psychological discomfort, such as nausea, dizziness, eye strain, headache, and fatigue, due to the mismatch between the user's sensory inputs and outputs.
  - Raising ethical and social issues, such as privacy, security, consent, and responsibility, regarding the use and impact of VR on the user and others.
  - Requiring high costs and technical expertise to develop and maintain VR hardware and software, as well as to ensure their quality and compatibility .
