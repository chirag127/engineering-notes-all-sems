# Tessellated Data

- Tessellation is the dividing of datasets of polygons (sometimes called vertex sets) presenting objects in a scene into suitable structures for rendering.
- Tessellation techniques are often used to manage datasets of polygons and divide them into suitable structures for rendering.
- Tessellation can be used to create more detailed and smooth surfaces from coarse models, by subdividing the polygons into smaller ones.
- Tessellation can also be used to create regular grids or bins for spatial analysis, such as density estimation, clustering, or interpolation .
- Tessellation can be of different shapes, such as triangles, squares, diamonds, hexagons, or transverse hexagons.
- Tessellation patterns have applications in various fields, such as textiles, quilting, origami, art, and architecture.
