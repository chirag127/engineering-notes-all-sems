## Unit 8 - Augmented and Mixed Reality

- Augmented reality (AR) and mixed reality (MR) are both immersive technologies that enhance the perception of reality by adding or manipulating digital elements.
- AR overlays virtual objects on the real-world environment, such as displaying information, graphics, or animations on a smartphone screen or a head-mounted display (HMD)  .
- MR not only overlays but also anchors virtual objects to the real world, allowing them to interact with the physical environment and the user, such as placing a hologram on a table or a wall   .
- AR and MR can be used for various purposes, such as education, entertainment, gaming, tourism, health care, industrial design, and more.
- AR and MR devices can range from smartphones and tablets to wearable devices, such as HMDs, smart glasses, or contact lenses.
- AR and MR applications can use different techniques to track the user's position, orientation, and movement, such as GPS, inertial sensors, cameras, or depth sensors.
- AR and MR applications can also use different methods to render and display the virtual objects, such as image-based, marker-based, or markerless techniques .
- AR and MR are different from virtual reality (VR), which immerses the user in a fully artificial digital environment, blocking out the real world  .
