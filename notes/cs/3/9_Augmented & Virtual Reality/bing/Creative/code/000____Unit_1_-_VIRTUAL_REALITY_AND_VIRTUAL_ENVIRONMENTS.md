## Unit 1 - VIRTUAL REALITY AND VIRTUAL ENVIRONMENTS

- Virtual reality (VR) is a technology that creates an immersive and interactive simulation of a three-dimensional environment, using computer-generated graphics, sounds, and other sensory inputs.
- Virtual environments (VEs) are the specific settings or scenarios that are simulated in VR, such as a virtual classroom, a virtual museum, or a virtual battlefield.
- VR and VEs have various applications in education, entertainment, training, health care, tourism, and more.
- VR and VEs can be experienced using different types of devices, such as head-mounted displays (HMDs), projection systems, haptic devices, and motion platforms.
- VR and VEs can be classified into different categories, based on the level of immersion, realism, interactivity, and social presence they provide.
- VR and VEs can have various benefits and challenges for users, such as enhancing learning outcomes, increasing motivation, improving spatial skills, inducing presence, causing cybersickness, creating ethical issues, and requiring high costs and technical skills.
