### Tracking Devices for 3D User Interface Input Hardware

- Tracking devices are hardware that measure the position, orientation, and/or motion of a sensor or a user in 3D space.
- Tracking devices are essential for 3D user interfaces, as they enable the user to interact with the virtual environment or the 3D content using natural and intuitive gestures and movements.
- Tracking devices can be classified into different types based on the technology they use, such as magnetic, mechanical, acoustic, inertial, optical, or hybrid tracking.
- Tracking devices can also be categorized based on the location of the sensor, such as handheld, user-worn, or direct human input devices.
- Some examples of tracking devices are:

  - Motion trackers: measure the 3D position and orientation of a sensor attached to a user's body part, such as the head, hand, or foot. They can provide 6 degrees of freedom (DOF) input, which allows the user to move and rotate objects in 3D space. Motion trackers can use different technologies, such as magnetic, inertial, or optical tracking .
  - Eye trackers: measure the direction of the user's gaze by detecting the corneal reflections or the electric potential differences of the eye. They are passive input devices that can be used to determine the user's attention, interest, or intention .
  - Data gloves: measure the hand posture and gestures of the user by detecting the bending of the fingers or the contact between the fingertips. They can provide high-fidelity input for manipulating virtual objects or performing symbolic actions .
  - 3D mice: measure the 3D position and orientation of a device that the user physically moves in 3D space. They can be handheld or user-worn, and can provide 6 DOF input for navigating or manipulating 3D content .
  - 3D gesture controllers: measure the 3D position and motion of the user's hand or fingers in the air by using electrical near-field (E-field) sensing. They can provide 3D gesture input for controlling 3D content or applications without touching any device.
