### Tools for the notes of the Unit 5 - 3D INTERACTION TECHNIQUES in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

- 3D interaction techniques are methods of interacting with 3D virtual objects or environments using input devices such as hand gestures, trackers, controllers, or voice commands .
- 3D interaction techniques can be classified into four categories: selection, manipulation, navigation, and system control .
- Selection techniques are methods of specifying one or more 3D objects for further interaction, such as pointing, ray-casting, go-go, or image plane .
- Manipulation techniques are methods of changing the position, orientation, size, or shape of 3D objects, such as direct manipulation, virtual hand, HOMER, or 3D widgets .
- Navigation techniques are methods of moving the user's viewpoint or the virtual environment, such as walking, flying, steering, or teleporting .
- System control techniques are methods of accessing menus, options, or commands, such as voice, gesture, or gaze .
- 3D interaction techniques can be designed and evaluated based on various criteria, such as task performance, usability, user preference, or presence .
- 3D interaction techniques can be applied to various domains and applications of augmented and virtual reality, such as education, training, gaming, visualization, or design .
