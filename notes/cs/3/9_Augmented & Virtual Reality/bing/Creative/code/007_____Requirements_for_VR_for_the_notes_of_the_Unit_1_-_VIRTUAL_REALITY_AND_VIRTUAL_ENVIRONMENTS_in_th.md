### Requirements for VR

Virtual reality (VR) is a technology that creates immersive and interactive simulations of real or imaginary environments using computer-generated graphics, sound, and motion. VR requires specialized hardware and software to deliver realistic and responsive experiences to the users.

Some of the requirements for VR are:

- **VR headset**: A device that covers the eyes and ears of the user and displays stereoscopic images and sounds that correspond to the user's head movements and position. VR headsets can be tethered to a PC or console, or standalone with built-in processors and batteries. Some examples of VR headsets are Oculus Rift, HTC Vive, Valve Index, PlayStation VR, and Oculus Quest.
- **VR controllers**: Devices that allow the user to interact with the virtual environment using hand gestures, buttons, triggers, joysticks, or touchpads. VR controllers can be tracked by the VR headset, external sensors, or cameras. Some examples of VR controllers are Oculus Touch, HTC Vive Controllers, Valve Index Controllers, PlayStation Move, and Oculus Quest Controllers.
- **VR sensors**: Devices that track the position and orientation of the VR headset and controllers in the physical space and translate them to the virtual space. VR sensors can be built-in the VR headset, external, or a combination of both. Some examples of VR sensors are Oculus Insight, HTC Vive Base Stations, Valve Index Base Stations, PlayStation Camera, and Oculus Quest Cameras.
- **VR PC or laptop**: A computer that runs the VR software and renders the VR graphics and audio. VR PC or laptop must have a high-performance processor, graphics card, memory, and storage to handle the high demands of VR. Some of the recommended specifications for a VR PC or laptop are  :

  - **Processor**: Intel Core i5-6300HQ or higher / AMD Ryzen 9 3900x or higher
  - **Graphics card**: NVIDIA GeForce GTX 980 or higher / AMD equivalent or higher
  - **Memory**: 8 GB RAM or higher
  - **Storage**: SSD or better
  - **Operating system**: Windows 8 or higher
  - **Ports**: Two USB 3.0 ports and HDMI 1.4 or higher

- **VR software**: Applications that create and run the VR content and experiences. VR software can be games, simulations, educational, social, or entertainment. VR software can be downloaded from online platforms such as Steam VR, Oculus Store, PlayStation Store, or Viveport. Some examples of VR software are Half-Life: Alyx, Beat Saber, Google Earth VR, VRChat, and The Lab.
