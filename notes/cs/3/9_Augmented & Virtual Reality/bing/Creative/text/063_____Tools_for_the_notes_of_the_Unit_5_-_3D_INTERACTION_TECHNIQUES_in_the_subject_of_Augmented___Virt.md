### Tools for the notes of the Unit 5 - 3D INTERACTION TECHNIQUES in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

- 3D interaction techniques are methods of manipulating and interacting with 3D objects and environments in virtual reality (VR) and augmented reality (AR) applications.
- 3D interaction techniques can be classified into four categories: selection, manipulation, navigation, and system control.
- Selection techniques are used to select or point at 3D objects in the virtual or augmented scene, such as ray-casting, cone-casting, or image-based techniques .
- Manipulation techniques are used to move, rotate, scale, or deform 3D objects, such as direct manipulation, virtual hand, or gesture-based techniques .
- Navigation techniques are used to change the viewpoint or position of the user in the virtual or augmented environment, such as walking, flying, or teleporting techniques.
- System control techniques are used to access menus, options, or commands in the virtual or augmented application, such as voice, gaze, or gesture-based techniques.
- 3D interaction techniques can be implemented using various input devices, such as trackers, gloves, controllers, or cameras, depending on the level of immersion, realism, and naturalness required  .
- 3D interaction techniques can be evaluated using various criteria, such as performance, usability, user satisfaction, or presence, depending on the task, application, and user group.
