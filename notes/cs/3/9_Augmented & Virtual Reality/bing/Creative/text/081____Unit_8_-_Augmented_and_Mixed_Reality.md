## Unit 8 - Augmented and Mixed Reality

- Augmented reality (AR) is a technology that overlays virtual objects or information on the real-world environment, enhancing the user's perception of reality.
- Mixed reality (MR) is a technology that allows real and virtual objects to interact in a shared environment, creating a hybrid of reality and imagination .
- Both AR and MR are immersive technologies, meaning they create a sense of presence and engagement for the user, but they differ in the degree of immersion and interaction they offer.
- AR and MR can be experienced through various devices, such as smartphones, tablets, head-mounted displays, smart glasses, or projectors, depending on the application and the user's preference.
- AR and MR have many potential applications in various domains, such as education, entertainment, gaming, health, tourism, art, industry, and more.
- Some examples of AR and MR applications are:
  - Pokemon Go, a popular mobile game that uses AR to display virtual creatures in the real world.
  - HoloLens, a head-mounted device that uses MR to project holograms that can be manipulated and interacted with.
  - IKEA Place, an app that uses AR to let users visualize how furniture would look in their homes.
  - Magic Leap, a company that develops MR devices and content that blend digital and physical worlds.
  - Google Translate, an app that uses AR to translate text in real time using the camera.
  - Microsoft Mesh, a platform that uses MR to enable collaborative and shared experiences across devices and locations.
