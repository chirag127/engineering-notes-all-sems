### World Environment for the notes of the Unit 4 - SOFTWARE TECHNOLOGIES in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

- Augmented reality (AR) is a technology that enhances the real-world environment with digital visual elements, sounds, and other sensory stimuli via holographic technology.
- Virtual reality (VR) is a technology that creates immersive experiences that isolate users from the real world, usually via a headset device and headphones.
- AR and VR are shaping up to be future technological battlegrounds, as they can transform various domains such as education, healthcare, entertainment, tourism, and renewable energy .
- AR and VR require software technologies that can generate realistic and interactive 3D graphics, audio, and haptics, as well as track the user's position, orientation, and gestures.
- Some of the software technologies that are used for AR and VR are:
  - Game engines: These are software frameworks that provide the core functionalities for developing and running 3D games and simulations. Examples are Unity, Unreal Engine, and Godot.
  - Rendering engines: These are software components that handle the process of converting 3D models, textures, lighting, and shading into 2D images that can be displayed on a screen or a headset. Examples are OpenGL, DirectX, and Vulkan.
  - Spatial audio engines: These are software components that simulate the sound propagation and localization in a 3D environment, taking into account the source, listener, and obstacles. Examples are Steam Audio, Google Resonance Audio, and Oculus Audio.
  - Haptic feedback engines: These are software components that generate tactile sensations that correspond to the virtual objects and events, such as vibration, force, and temperature. Examples are HaptX, Ultraleap, and bHaptics.
  - Tracking and recognition engines: These are software components that use sensors, cameras, and machine learning to detect and track the user's head, eyes, hands, body, and facial expressions, as well as recognize objects, scenes, and gestures in the real world. Examples are OpenCV, ARCore, and ARKit.
- AR and VR software technologies are constantly evolving and improving, thanks to the advances in artificial intelligence, cloud computing, 5G, and edge computing .
- AR and VR software technologies pose various challenges and opportunities for developers, users, and society, such as usability, accessibility, privacy, security, ethics, and sustainability.
