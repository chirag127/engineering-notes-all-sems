### Tracking Devices for 3D User Interface Input Hardware

- Tracking devices are hardware that measure the position, orientation, and/or motion of a sensor or a user in 3D space.
- Tracking devices are essential for 3D user interfaces, as they enable the user to interact with the virtual environment or the 3D application using natural and intuitive gestures.
- Tracking devices can be classified into different types based on the technology, the number of degrees of freedom (DOFs), the body part being tracked, and the form factor .
- Some of the common types of tracking devices are:

  - **Motion trackers**: These devices track the movement of a sensor or a user in 3D space, using various technologies such as magnetic, mechanical, acoustic, inertial, optical, or hybrid. Motion trackers can have 3 DOFs (for position only) or 6 DOFs (for position and orientation) depending on the application. Motion trackers can be attached to handheld devices, such as 3D mice, or worn by the user, such as head-mounted displays or data gloves.
  - **Eye trackers**: These devices track the gaze direction of the user, using cameras or electrodes, to determine where the user is looking. Eye trackers are purely passive input devices, as they do not require any user action, and can be used for attention-based interaction or eye-based communication.
  - **Data gloves**: These devices track the hand posture and gestures of the user, using bend sensors or pinch sensors, to enable natural and expressive interaction. Data gloves can have 5 DOFs (for each finger) or more, depending on the number of sensors and joints. Data gloves can also provide haptic feedback to the user, such as vibration or force.
  - **Direct human input**: These devices track the signals generated by the human body, such as brain waves, muscle activity, or heart rate, to enable implicit or physiological interaction. Direct human input devices can use electroencephalography (EEG), electromyography (EMG), or electrocardiography (ECG) to measure the signals. Direct human input devices can be used for emotion recognition, biofeedback, or brain-computer interfaces.

- Tracking devices have various advantages and disadvantages, depending on the type, such as accuracy, latency, range, robustness, cost, and usability. Therefore, the choice of tracking device depends on the application requirements and the user preferences.
