### Environment Centered Wayfinding Support

- Wayfinding is the cognitive process of defining a path through an environment, using and acquiring spatial knowledge, and being aided by natural and artificial cues .
- Wayfinding is important because it helps us better understand our environment, instead of being overwhelmed by a lot of small details.
- Environment centered wayfinding support is the design of a virtual environment that supports wayfinding by providing cues, landmarks, maps, and other features that help the user orient and navigate.
- Some examples of environment centered wayfinding support are:
  - Using consistent and distinctive visual elements, such as colors, textures, shapes, and lighting, to create regions and boundaries in the virtual environment .
  - Providing global and local landmarks that are easily recognizable and memorable, such as buildings, statues, signs, and natural features .
  - Providing maps and compasses that show the user's location, orientation, and destination in the virtual environment .
  - Providing feedback and guidance, such as arrows, trails, sounds, and voice instructions, that help the user follow the desired path or correct errors .
  - Providing multiple levels of detail and scale, such as overview and zoom, that allow the user to switch between different perspectives and levels of abstraction in the virtual environment .
- Environment centered wayfinding support can improve the user's performance, satisfaction, and spatial learning in the virtual environment .
