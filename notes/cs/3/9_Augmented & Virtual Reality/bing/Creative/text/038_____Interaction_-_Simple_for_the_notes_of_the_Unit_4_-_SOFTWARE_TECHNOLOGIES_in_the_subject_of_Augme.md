### Interaction - Simple

- Interaction is the process of communicating and exchanging information between a user and a system, such as augmented reality (AR) or virtual reality (VR).
- Interaction can involve different modalities, such as visual, auditory, haptic, somatosensory and olfactory, depending on the system and the user's preferences.
- Interaction can be classified into two types: direct and indirect.
  - Direct interaction is when the user manipulates the system or the virtual content directly, such as using gestures, voice commands, touch screens, controllers or trackers.
  - Indirect interaction is when the user manipulates the system or the virtual content indirectly, such as using menus, buttons, keyboards, mice or joysticks.
- Interaction can also be classified into two modes: immersive and non-immersive.
  - Immersive interaction is when the user is fully immersed in the virtual environment and has a high sense of presence and realism.
  - Non-immersive interaction is when the user is partially immersed in the virtual environment and has a low sense of presence and realism.
- Interaction can have different goals, such as navigation, manipulation, selection, annotation, collaboration, education, entertainment, etc.
- Interaction can have different challenges, such as latency, accuracy, feedback, usability, accessibility, etc.
- Interaction can have different design principles, such as consistency, affordance, visibility, feedback, etc.
- Interaction can have different evaluation methods, such as user testing, usability testing, user satisfaction, etc.
