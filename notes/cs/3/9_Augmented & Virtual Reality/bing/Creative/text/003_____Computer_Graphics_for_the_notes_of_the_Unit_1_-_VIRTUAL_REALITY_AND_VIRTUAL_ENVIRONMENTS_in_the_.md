### Computer Graphics for the notes of the Unit 1 - VIRTUAL REALITY AND VIRTUAL ENVIRONMENTS in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

- Virtual reality (VR) is the use of computer graphics systems in combination with various display and interface devices to provide the effect of immersion in the interactive 3D computer-generated environment .
- We call such an environment a virtual environment (VE), which can simulate or create any scenario that can be imagined, from realistic to fictional .
- VR applications immerse the user in a VE that simulates reality through the use of interactive devices, which send and receive information from the computer graphics system.
- The main components of a VR system are:
  - A computer graphics system that generates and renders the 3D scenes of the VE.
  - A display device that presents the 3D scenes to the user's eyes, such as a head-mounted display (HMD), a projection screen, or a monitor.
  - An interface device that allows the user to interact with the VE, such as a joystick, a glove, a tracker, or a voice recognition system.
  - A sound system that provides auditory feedback to the user, such as speakers, headphones, or a microphone.
  - A network system that enables communication and collaboration among multiple users in the same or different VEs, such as the internet, a LAN, or a WAN.
- The main challenges of VR are:
  - Creating realistic and convincing 3D scenes that can respond to the user's actions and inputs in real time.
  - Providing a high level of immersion and presence to the user, which means the user feels as if they are actually in the VE and not aware of the artificiality of the VR system.
  - Avoiding or minimizing the negative effects of VR, such as cybersickness, disorientation, or ethical issues.
