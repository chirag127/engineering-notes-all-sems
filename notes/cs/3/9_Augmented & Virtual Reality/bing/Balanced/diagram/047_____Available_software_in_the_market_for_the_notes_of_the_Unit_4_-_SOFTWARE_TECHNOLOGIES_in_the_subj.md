### Available software in the market for the notes of the Unit 4 - SOFTWARE TECHNOLOGIES in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

- Augmented reality (AR) and virtual reality (VR) are two technologies that create immersive and interactive experiences for users.
- AR overlays digital content on the real world, while VR creates a simulated environment that replaces the real world.
- Both AR and VR require software tools to design, develop, and deploy applications and content.
- Some of the available software in the market for AR and VR are:

  - **Adobe Aero**: A software that allows users to create and share AR experiences without coding. Users can import 3D models, animations, and images, and use gestures and voice commands to interact with them. Adobe Aero also integrates with other Adobe products, such as Photoshop and Illustrator.
  - **Tvori**: A software that enables users to create VR animations and stories using a VR headset and controllers. Users can manipulate objects, characters, and scenes in a 3D space, and export their creations to various platforms, such as YouTube, Facebook, and Steam.
  - **Masterpiece Studio Pro**: A software that combines two tools, Masterpiece Creator and Masterpiece Motion, to help users create and animate 3D models for VR and AR. Users can sculpt, paint, and rig their models, and export them to various formats, such as FBX, OBJ, and USDZ.
  - **Vuforia Engine**: A software that provides a platform for developing AR applications for various devices, such as smartphones, tablets, and headsets. Users can use features, such as image recognition, object tracking, and spatial mapping, to create engaging AR experiences. Vuforia Engine also supports integration with other software, such as Unity and Unreal Engine.
  - **Unity 3D**: A software that is widely used for developing games and interactive content for VR and AR. Users can use a graphical user interface or a scripting language to create and edit scenes, objects, and behaviors. Unity 3D also supports various platforms, such as Windows, Android, iOS, and Oculus .
  - **Oculus Medium**: A software that is designed for creating 3D sculptures and models using an Oculus headset and controllers. Users can use various tools, such as brushes, stamps, and layers, to shape and color their creations. Oculus Medium also allows users to export their models to other software, such as Blender and Maya.
  - **SketchFab**: A software that is used for managing and sharing 3D assets and AR and VR content. Users can upload, view, and edit their 3D models, and embed them on websites, social media, and other platforms. SketchFab also offers a library of over 4 million 3D models that users can browse and download.
  - **ARKit**: A software that is used for developing AR applications for iOS devices. Users can use features, such as face tracking, plane detection, and image recognition, to create realistic and interactive AR experiences. ARKit also supports geolocation anchors for cities, businesses, and landmarks.
  - **Kudan**: A software that is used for developing AR and VR applications for various platforms, such as Android, iOS, and Windows. Users can use features, such as markerless tracking, 3D rendering, and gesture recognition, to create immersive and dynamic AR and VR experiences. Kudan also offers a cloud service for hosting and streaming AR and VR content.
  - **TeamViewer**: A software that offers solutions for implementing both AR and VR. Users can use TeamViewer Pilot to provide remote assistance and guidance using AR annotations and live video. Users can also use TeamViewer Frontline to create and access digital workflows and instructions using smart glasses and AR.
  - **BUNDLAR**: A software that enables users to create and manage AR content and applications. Users can use a web-based editor to upload and edit 3D models, images, videos, and audio, and assign them to AR triggers, such as QR codes, GPS locations, and images. Users can also use a mobile app to view and interact with the AR content.
