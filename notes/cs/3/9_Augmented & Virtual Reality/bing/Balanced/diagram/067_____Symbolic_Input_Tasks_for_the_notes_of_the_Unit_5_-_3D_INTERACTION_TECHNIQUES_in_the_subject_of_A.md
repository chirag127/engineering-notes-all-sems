### Symbolic Input Tasks for the notes of the Unit 5 - 3D INTERACTION TECHNIQUES in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

- Symbolic input is the task in which users communicate symbolic information (text, numbers, and other symbols or marks) to the system .
- Symbolic input is often used for tasks such as writing email messages, entering numbers into a spreadsheet, composing documents, and so on.
- Symbolic input is challenging in 3D interaction environments because of the lack of standard input devices, the difficulty of text entry and recognition, and the trade-off between accuracy and speed.
- Symbolic input techniques can be classified into four categories  :
  - Keyboard-based techniques: using physical or virtual keyboards to enter text or symbols, such as miniature keyboards, chord keyboards, or soft keyboards.
  - Pen-based techniques: using a stylus or a finger to write or draw symbols on a surface or in the air, such as handwriting recognition, graffiti, or sketching.
  - Gesture-based techniques: using hand or body movements to perform gestures that represent symbols, such as sign language, finger spelling, or symbolic gestures.
  - Speech-based techniques: using voice commands or natural language to speak symbols to the system, such as speech recognition, dictation, or voice menus.
- Symbolic input techniques can be evaluated based on criteria such as input speed, error rate, user satisfaction, learnability, and usability.
- Symbolic input techniques can be combined or integrated with other 3D interaction techniques, such as selection, manipulation, or system control, to create more complex or natural interactions.
