### Design Guidelines for the notes of the Unit 5 - 3D INTERACTION TECHNIQUES in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

- 3D interaction techniques are methods of interacting with 3D virtual objects or environments in augmented reality (AR) or virtual reality (VR) applications  .
- 3D interaction techniques can be classified into four categories: selection, manipulation, navigation, and system control .
- Selection techniques are methods of specifying one or more target objects in the 3D scene, such as ray-casting, pointing, or gaze-based selection .
- Manipulation techniques are methods of changing the position, orientation, size, or shape of the selected objects, such as direct manipulation, virtual hand, or gesture-based manipulation  .
- Navigation techniques are methods of changing the viewpoint or location of the user in the 3D scene, such as walking, flying, or teleporting .
- System control techniques are methods of accessing menus, options, or commands in the 3D application, such as voice, gesture, or device-based control .
- 3D interaction techniques should follow some design guidelines to ensure usability, efficiency, and user satisfaction, such as :
  - Match the interaction technique to the task and the context of use
  - Provide feedback and feedforward to the user
  - Minimize the cognitive and physical load of the user
  - Support natural and intuitive interaction
  - Avoid occlusion and clutter in the 3D scene
  - Consider the trade-offs between realism and abstraction
  - Evaluate the interaction technique with appropriate methods and metrics
