### Technology and features of augmented reality

- Augmented reality (AR) is the technology that expands our physical world, adding layers of digital information onto it.
- Unlike virtual reality (VR), AR does not create the whole artificial environments to replace real with a virtual one. AR appears in direct view of an existing environment and adds sounds, videos, graphics to it.
- AR incorporates three features: a combination of digital and physical worlds, interactions made in real time, and accurate 3D identification of virtual and real objects.
- AR offers a better way to design, curate, and deliver consumable instructions by overlaying digital content in real-world work environments.
- AR can be experienced through various devices, such as smartphones, tablets, head-mounted displays (HMDs), smart glasses, and projectors.
- AR can be classified into three types based on the level of immersion and interaction: marker-based AR, markerless AR, and projection-based AR.
- Marker-based AR uses a camera and a visual marker, such as a QR code or a barcode, to detect the position and orientation of the marker and overlay a digital image or animation on it.
- Markerless AR uses sensors, such as GPS, accelerometer, gyroscope, and compass, to track the location and orientation of the device and overlay digital information based on the user's context and preferences.
- Projection-based AR uses a projector to cast digital images or animations onto a physical surface, such as a wall or a table, and allows the user to interact with them using touch or gestures.
- AR can be applied to various domains, such as education, entertainment, gaming, tourism, retail, manufacturing, healthcare, and military   .
- AR can enhance the learning experience by providing interactive and immersive content, such as 3D models, animations, simulations, and games.
- AR can create engaging and immersive entertainment and gaming experiences by adding digital elements to the real world, such as characters, objects, and effects .
- AR can provide useful and relevant information to tourists, such as landmarks, directions, reviews, and translations, by overlaying them on the real environment.
- AR can improve the customer experience and sales performance in retail by allowing customers to try on products, such as clothes, accessories, and furniture, virtually before buying them.
- AR can enhance the productivity and quality of manufacturing by providing workers with step-by-step instructions, feedback, and troubleshooting tips, by overlaying them on the real equipment or process.
- AR can improve the diagnosis and treatment of patients in healthcare by providing doctors and nurses with real-time data, such as vital signs, medical records, and images, by overlaying them on the patient's body or environment.
- AR can increase the situational awareness and combat effectiveness of soldiers in military by providing them with real-time information, such as enemy location, terrain, and health, by overlaying them on their visors or goggles.
