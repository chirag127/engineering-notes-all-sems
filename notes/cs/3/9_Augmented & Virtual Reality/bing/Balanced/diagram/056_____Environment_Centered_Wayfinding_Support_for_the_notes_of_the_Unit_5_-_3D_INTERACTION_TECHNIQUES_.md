### Environment Centered Wayfinding Support

- Wayfinding is the cognitive process of defining a path through an environment, using and acquiring spatial knowledge, and being aided by natural and artificial cues .
- Wayfinding is important because it helps us better understand our environment, instead of being overwhelmed by a lot of small details.
- Environment centered wayfinding support is the design of a virtual environment that supports wayfinding by providing cues, landmarks, maps, and other features that help the user navigate and orient themselves.
- Some examples of environment centered wayfinding support are:
  - Using consistent and meaningful colors, textures, and shapes to distinguish different areas or regions of the environment .
  - Providing signs, labels, arrows, or icons that indicate directions, distances, or destinations .
  - Creating landmarks or points of interest that are easily recognizable and memorable, and that can serve as reference points for the user .
  - Providing maps or mini-maps that show the layout of the environment, the user's location and orientation, and the possible paths or routes .
  - Providing feedback or guidance to the user when they are lost, confused, or need help, such as highlighting the correct path, showing a compass, or giving verbal instructions .
- Environment centered wayfinding support can improve the user's sense of presence, immersion, and engagement in the virtual environment, as well as their spatial memory and learning .
- Environment centered wayfinding support can also help the user transfer their spatial knowledge from the virtual environment to the real world, or vice versa, if the environments are similar or related.
