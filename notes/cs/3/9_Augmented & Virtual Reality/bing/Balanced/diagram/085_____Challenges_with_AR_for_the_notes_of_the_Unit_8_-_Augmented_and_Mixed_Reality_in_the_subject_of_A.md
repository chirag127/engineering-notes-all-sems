### Challenges with AR

Augmented reality (AR) is a technology that enhances the real world with digital information and media, such as 3D models, videos, sounds, and texts. AR has many potential applications in various domains, such as education, entertainment, health, tourism, and industry. However, AR also faces some challenges that limit its adoption and development. Some of the main challenges are:

- **Hardware quality**: Successful adoption of AR depends on the experience that you provide to the end user. Hardware quality matters, especially when you think about mobile AR solutions. The devices need to have high-resolution cameras, sensors, processors, batteries, and displays to support AR features and functions. However, many current devices are not optimized for AR and may have low performance, limited battery life, or poor compatibility. Moreover, there are no AR headsets available for consumers yet, and the existing ones, such as Microsoft HoloLens and Meta 2, are expensive, bulky, and uncomfortable to wear for long periods of time .

- **Content availability**: Content is definitely one of the biggest challenges in AR adoption. Without quality content, no hardware can make AR a success. Organizations that are trying to include AR in their internal work functions need to think about 3D content availability for that technology. Creating 3D models, animations, and interactions for AR is not easy and requires specialized skills and tools. Moreover, the content needs to be relevant, engaging, and updated to meet the user's needs and expectations. However, there is a lack of standardized formats, platforms, and guidelines for AR content creation and distribution, which makes it difficult to share and reuse AR content across different devices and applications .

- **Awareness**: One of the most difficult challenges about AR is educating the broader market. Consumers are not exposed to AR regularly and do not see its wide-reaching applications in their daily lives. However, there are plenty of AR experiences available today that just lack exposure in the consumer market. For example, AR can be used to enhance shopping, gaming, navigation, learning, and entertainment. However, many people are not aware of these possibilities and do not know how to access and use AR applications. Therefore, there is a need for more marketing, promotion, and demonstration of AR to increase its awareness and adoption among the general public .

- **Technical issues**: Technical issues with AR technology affect the functionality of the learning activity and may include any of the following: Hardware Issues — From devices malfunctioning to problems connecting to a local server, hardware issues may inhibit a learner’s ability to access and complete their activity. Software Issues — Software issues may include bugs, glitches, or errors that prevent the AR application from running smoothly or displaying the correct information. Network Issues — Network issues may include slow or unstable internet connection, bandwidth limitations, or security restrictions that affect the data transfer and communication between the AR device and the server. User Interface Issues — User interface issues may include poor design, usability, or accessibility of the AR application, which may confuse, frustrate, or discourage the user from interacting with the AR content.

- **Ethical, legal, and social issues**: Ethical, legal, and social issues are also important challenges that need to be addressed when implementing AR technology. Some of these issues are: Privacy — AR may pose a threat to the user's privacy, as it may collect, store, and share personal or sensitive data, such as location, biometrics, preferences, or behavior, without the user's consent or knowledge. Moreover, AR may also invade the privacy of others, as it may capture and display their images, identities, or information without their permission or awareness. Security — AR may expose the user to security risks, such as hacking, phishing, or malware, that may compromise the user's device, data, or identity. Moreover, AR may also be used for malicious purposes, such as espionage, sabotage, or cyberattacks, that may harm the user or others. Safety — AR may endanger the user's safety, as it may distract, confuse, or mislead the user, causing accidents, injuries, or errors. Moreover, AR may also affect the user's health, as it may cause eye strain, headaches, nausea, or fatigue, due to prolonged or improper use of AR devices.
