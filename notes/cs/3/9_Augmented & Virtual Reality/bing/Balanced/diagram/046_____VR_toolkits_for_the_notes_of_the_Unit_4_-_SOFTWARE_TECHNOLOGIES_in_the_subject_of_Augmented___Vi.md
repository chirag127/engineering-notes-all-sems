### VR toolkits

- VR toolkits are software libraries or frameworks that provide developers with tools and components to create VR applications or games.
- VR toolkits can help with tasks such as rendering, input, interaction, audio, physics, UI, and more.
- VR toolkits can also abstract away the differences between various VR platforms and devices, making it easier to port VR applications across different systems.
- Some examples of VR toolkits are:

  - **VRTK (VR Toolkit)**: A free and open-source collection of useful and reusable solutions for common problems found when building for VR in Unity . VRTK offers features such as locomotion, grabbing, pointers, UI, haptics, and more.
  - **VRToolkit**: A modular shader created for ReShade to enhance the clarity and sharpness in VR while keeping the performance impact minimal . VRToolkit offers features such as sharpening modes, color correction, contrast enhancement, and more.
  - **VR Performance Toolkit**: A tool for measuring and optimizing the performance of VR applications on Windows. VR Performance Toolkit uses the Oculus SDK and NVAPI to collect and display metrics such as frame rate, latency, GPU utilization, and more.
  - **OVR Toolkit**: A utility application that allows users to view and interact with the desktop in VR. OVR Toolkit lets users place desktop windows around the VR world, use mouse and keyboard input, switch between windows, and more.
