### World Environment for the notes of the Unit 4 - SOFTWARE TECHNOLOGIES in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

- Augmented reality (AR) is an enhanced, interactive version of a real-world environment achieved through digital visual elements, sounds, and other sensory stimuli via holographic technology.
- Virtual reality (VR) is an immersive experience that isolates users from the real world, usually via a headset device and headphones designed for such activities.
- Mixed reality (MR) is a combination of AR and VR that allows users to interact with both real and virtual objects in a shared environment.
- The world environment refers to the physical, social, and cultural context in which AR, VR, and MR applications are developed and deployed.
- The world environment influences the design, development, and evaluation of AR, VR, and MR applications in various ways, such as:
  - The availability and accessibility of hardware, software, and network infrastructure for different users and regions.
  - The ethical, legal, and social implications of using AR, VR, and MR technologies for different purposes and audiences.
  - The environmental impact and sustainability of AR, VR, and MR technologies in terms of energy consumption, waste generation, and carbon footprint.
  - The user experience and usability of AR, VR, and MR applications in terms of immersion, interaction, and engagement.
  - The educational, health, and entertainment benefits and challenges of AR, VR, and MR applications for different learners, patients, and consumers.
- The world environment is constantly changing and evolving due to technological innovations, social trends, and global events, which pose new opportunities and challenges for AR, VR, and MR applications.
- The world environment requires AR, VR, and MR developers and researchers to adopt a multidisciplinary and collaborative approach that considers the needs, preferences, and expectations of various stakeholders and users.
