### Real-time computer graphics

- Real-time computer graphics is a sub-field of computer graphics that focuses on producing and analyzing images in real time .
- Real time means that the images are generated and displayed at a rate that matches the perception of human vision, typically 30 frames per second or more.
- Real-time computer graphics can be used for various applications, such as graphical user interfaces, video games, simulations, virtual reality, augmented reality, and image analysis .
- Real-time computer graphics requires efficient algorithms and data structures, as well as specialized hardware, such as graphics processing units (GPUs), to achieve high performance and quality .
- Real-time computer graphics can also adapt to different display technologies, such as autostereoscopy, which allows for 3D viewing without glasses.
