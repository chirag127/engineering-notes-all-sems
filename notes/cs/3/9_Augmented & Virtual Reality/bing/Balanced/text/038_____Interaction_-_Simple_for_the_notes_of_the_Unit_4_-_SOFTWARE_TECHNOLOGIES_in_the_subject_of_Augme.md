### Interaction - Simple

- Interaction is the process of communicating and exchanging information between a user and a system, such as augmented reality (AR) or virtual reality (VR)  .
- Interaction can be classified into two types: input and output .
  - Input is the way the user provides commands, data, or feedback to the system, such as gestures, voice, or controllers .
  - Output is the way the system delivers content, information, or feedback to the user, such as graphics, sound, or haptics .
- Interaction can also be categorized by the level of immersion, which is the degree to which the user feels present and engaged in the virtual or augmented environment  .
  - Immersion can be influenced by factors such as field of view, resolution, latency, interactivity, and realism  .
  - Immersion can be measured by subjective (e.g., questionnaires) or objective (e.g., physiological) methods  .
- Interaction can also be influenced by the design principles and guidelines that aim to enhance the user experience and usability of AR or VR systems  .
  - Design principles and guidelines can cover aspects such as affordances, feedback, consistency, simplicity, and accessibility  .
  - Design principles and guidelines can vary depending on the context, purpose, and target audience of the AR or VR system  .
