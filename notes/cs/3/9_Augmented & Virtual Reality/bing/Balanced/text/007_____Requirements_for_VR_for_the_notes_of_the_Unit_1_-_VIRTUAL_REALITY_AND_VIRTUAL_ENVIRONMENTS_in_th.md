### Requirements for VR

- VR is the simulation of a three-dimensional environment that can be interacted with by a user through a VR device such as a headset, controller, or glove.
- VR requires the following components to create a realistic and immersive experience for the user:
  - A VR device that can track the user's head and body movements and provide feedback to the user's senses, such as vision, hearing, touch, and sometimes smell and taste.
  - A VR application that can generate and render the virtual environment and the objects within it, as well as handle the user's input and output.
  - A VR platform that can support the VR device and the VR application, such as a computer, a console, a smartphone, or a cloud service.
  - A VR network that can connect the VR device, the VR application, and the VR platform, as well as other VR users and devices, such as the internet, a local area network, or a wireless network.
- VR requires the following characteristics to achieve a high level of presence and immersion for the user:
  - High resolution and frame rate to provide clear and smooth visuals.
  - Low latency and high bandwidth to minimize the delay and lag between the user's actions and the VR feedback.
  - Wide field of view and stereoscopic vision to cover the user's natural range of sight and depth perception.
  - Spatial audio and haptic feedback to create realistic and directional sounds and sensations.
  - Accurate and consistent tracking and calibration to align the user's physical and virtual positions and orientations.
  - Intuitive and natural interaction and navigation to enable the user to manipulate and explore the virtual environment and objects.
