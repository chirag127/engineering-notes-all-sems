## Unit 1 - VIRTUAL REALITY AND VIRTUAL ENVIRONMENTS

- Virtual reality (VR) is a technology that creates an immersive and interactive simulation of a three-dimensional environment, using computer-generated graphics, sounds, and other sensory inputs.
- Virtual environments (VEs) are the specific settings or scenarios that are simulated in VR, such as a virtual classroom, a virtual museum, or a virtual battlefield.
- VR can be experienced using various devices, such as head-mounted displays (HMDs), gloves, controllers, treadmills, or haptic suits, that provide visual, auditory, tactile, or kinesthetic feedback to the user.
- VR can have various applications and benefits, such as education, entertainment, training, therapy, or social interaction, depending on the design and purpose of the VE.
- VR can also pose some challenges and risks, such as technical limitations, ethical issues, health effects, or social implications, depending on the quality and usage of the VR system.
