# VR toolkits

- VR toolkits are software libraries or frameworks that provide various features and functionalities for developing VR applications or enhancing VR experiences.
- VR toolkits can help developers to create VR content faster, easier, and more efficiently, by offering solutions for common VR problems, such as input, interaction, locomotion, UI, audio, physics, etc.
- VR toolkits can also help users to improve the clarity, sharpness, and performance of VR graphics, by applying shaders, filters, or optimizations to the VR output.
- Some examples of VR toolkits are:

  - **VRTK (VR Toolkit)**: A free and open-source collection of reusable scripts and prefabs for Unity, that covers many aspects of VR development, such as controllers, pointers, grabbable objects, teleportation, UI, etc.  
  - **VRToolkit**: A modular shader for ReShade, that enhances the clarity and sharpness of VR graphics, by applying different sharpening modes, color correction, and anti-aliasing, while minimizing the performance impact.  
  - **VR Performance Toolkit**: A tool for measuring and optimizing the performance of VR applications, by using the Oculus SDK and NVAPI to access low-level VR and GPU data, such as frame times, latency, resolution, etc. 
  - **OVR Toolkit**: A utility application for viewing and interacting with the desktop in VR, by placing desktop windows around the VR world, and allowing mouse input, virtual keyboard, and window switching.
