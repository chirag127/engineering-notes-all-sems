# Unit 7 - Virtual Reality Applications

- Virtual reality applications are applications that make use of virtual reality (VR), an immersive sensory experience that digitally simulates a virtual environment.
- VR applications require software and hardware to create and display the virtual environment, and to enable the user to interact with it through various devices, such as goggles, headsets, gloves, or body suits .
- VR applications can be used for various purposes, such as entertainment, education, business, health care, tourism, art, and social interaction .
- Some examples of VR applications are:
  - Google Expeditions, which allows users to explore different places and cultures in VR, such as the Great Barrier Reef, the Taj Mahal, or the International Space Station.
  - Allumette, which is a VR animated film that tells the story of a young girl and her mother in a fantastical city in the clouds.
  - Colosse, which is a VR story that follows a giant creature and its interactions with a human tribe.
  - Titans of Space Plus, which is a VR tour of the solar system and beyond, with detailed information and realistic visuals of the planets, moons, and stars.
  - Google Earth VR, which lets users fly over and explore any location on Earth in VR, with 3D imagery and street view.
  - Kingspray Graffiti VR, which is a VR simulation of graffiti art, where users can spray paint on various surfaces and environments, with realistic physics and effects.
  - Littlstar, which is a VR media platform that offers a variety of content, such as 360-degree videos, live events, documentaries, and movies.
  - Ocean Rift, which is a VR aquatic safari that lets users swim with various marine animals, such as dolphins, sharks, turtles, and whales.
  - The Foo Show, which is a VR talk show that features interviews with creators and artists, and showcases their work in VR, such as video games, movies, and comics.
  - Tilt Brush, which is a VR painting application that lets users create 3D art in VR, with a variety of brushes, colors, and effects.
  - Virtual Desktop, which is a VR application that allows users to use their computer in VR, with multiple monitors, customizable backgrounds, and support for various games and applications.
  - Within, which is a VR storytelling platform that offers a range of immersive experiences, such as documentaries, animations, music videos, and interactive stories.
  - Fulldive VR, which is a VR social network that lets users browse and share VR content, such as photos, videos, and games, and interact with other users in VR.
