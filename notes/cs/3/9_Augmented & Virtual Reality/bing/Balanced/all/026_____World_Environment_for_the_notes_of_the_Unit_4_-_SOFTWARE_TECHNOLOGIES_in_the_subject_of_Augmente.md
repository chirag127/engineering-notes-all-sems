# World Environment

- The world environment is the virtual space where the user interacts with the augmented or virtual reality application.
- The world environment consists of three main components: the scene, the camera, and the lighting.
- The scene is the collection of objects, models, animations, sounds, and other elements that make up the virtual world.
- The camera is the device that captures the user's point of view and renders the scene on the display device.
- The lighting is the set of light sources and shadows that affect the appearance and realism of the scene.
- The world environment can be created using various software tools and frameworks, such as Unity, Unreal Engine, A-Frame, and others.
- The world environment can be designed to achieve different levels of immersion, interactivity, and realism, depending on the goals and requirements of the application.
- The world environment can be influenced by the user's input, such as gestures, voice commands, eye tracking, and others.
- The world environment can also be affected by the real environment, such as the user's location, orientation, and movement, using sensors and tracking technologies.
