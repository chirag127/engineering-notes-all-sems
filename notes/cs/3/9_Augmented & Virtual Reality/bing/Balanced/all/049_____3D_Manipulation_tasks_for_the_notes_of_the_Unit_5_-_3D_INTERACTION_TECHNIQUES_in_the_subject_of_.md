# 3D Manipulation Tasks

- 3D manipulation tasks are the tasks of physically handling objects in a 3D environment with one or two hands.
- 3D manipulation tasks can be broadly classified into three basic categories: selection, positioning, and rotation .
- Selection is the task of acquiring or identifying a particular object or subset of objects from the entire set of objects available .
- Positioning is the task of changing the 3D position of an object .
- Rotation is the task of changing the orientation of an object .
- Scaling is the task of changing the size of an object.
- 3D manipulation tasks can be performed using various techniques and input devices, such as mouse, keyboard, joystick, glove, tracker, etc  .
- 3D manipulation techniques and input devices can be evaluated based on several criteria, such as number of control dimensions, integration of control dimensions, force vs. position control, accuracy, speed, ease of use, etc  .
- 3D manipulation tasks are essential for 3D user interfaces, as they allow users to interact with virtual objects and environments in a natural and intuitive way.
