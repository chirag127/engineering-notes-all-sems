# Tessellated Data for the notes of the Unit 4 - SOFTWARE TECHNOLOGIES in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

- Tessellation is the dividing of datasets of polygons (sometimes called vertex sets) presenting objects in a scene into suitable structures for rendering.
- Tessellation can improve the visual quality and performance of augmented reality (AR) applications by generating more detailed and realistic models of the physical environment.
- Tessellation can also enable the visualization of complex and large-scale data in AR by creating immersive and interactive representations of the data.
- Tessellation can be done in different ways, such as using regular grids, adaptive grids, or subdivision surfaces.
- Tessellation can be performed on the CPU, the GPU, or a combination of both, depending on the application requirements and the available hardware.
- Tessellation can be influenced by various factors, such as the level of detail, the curvature, the distance, the lighting, and the texture of the objects.
- Tessellation can be used for various purposes in AR, such as:
  - Augmented assembly for tessellated structures, where AR can provide real-time guidance and feedback for the construction of complex geometries.
  - Visualizing standardized model-based design and inspection data in AR, where AR can display the relevant information and annotations for the engineering tasks.
  - Using VR to step inside your data, where AR can create immersive and interactive environments for data analysis and exploration.
