# User Centered Wayfinding Support

- Wayfinding is the process of navigating and orienting oneself in an unfamiliar environment.
- Wayfinding support is the provision of cues, signs, maps, landmarks, and other aids that help users find their way.
- User centered wayfinding support is the design and evaluation of wayfinding systems that consider the needs, preferences, and abilities of different users .
- User centered wayfinding support is important for 3D interaction techniques because:
  - 3D environments can be complex, large, and dynamic, posing challenges for spatial cognition and navigation.
  - 3D interaction techniques can affect the user's field of view, presence, and sense of direction, which can influence wayfinding performance and satisfaction.
  - Different users may have different levels of familiarity, spatial ability, and cognitive style, which can affect their wayfinding strategies and preferences .
- User centered wayfinding support can be achieved by:
  - Conducting user research to understand the wayfinding goals, tasks, and challenges of different user groups .
  - Designing wayfinding systems that are consistent, clear, intuitive, and adaptable to different user needs and contexts .
  - Evaluating wayfinding systems using both objective and subjective measures, such as accuracy, efficiency, satisfaction, and spatial knowledge .
  - Iterating and improving wayfinding systems based on user feedback and data .
