## Unit 7 - Virtual Reality Applications

### Introduction

- Virtual reality applications are applications that make use of virtual reality (VR), an immersive sensory experience that digitally simulates a virtual environment.
- VR uses software and hardware to immerse a user, and potentially other viewers, in a digital environment. They get to explore and interact with virtual surroundings, and in some cases, also their physical environment.
- VR applications require interactive devices, such as goggles, headsets, gloves, or body suits, that send and receive information and create a stereoscopic or 3D view of the simulated environment.

### Examples of VR Applications

- VR applications can be used for various purposes, such as entertainment, education, business, health, and social interaction. Some examples of VR applications are:

  - Google Expeditions: A free app that allows users to go on virtual field trips to different places, such as museums, landmarks, and natural wonders, using a smartphone and a VR viewer.
  - Allumette: A free app that tells a story of love, sacrifice, and a bond between a mother and her child in a fantastical city in the clouds, using VR animation and interactive storytelling.
  - Colosse: A free app that showcases a real-time VR storytelling experience, where the user's gaze influences the narrative and the environment, using hand-drawn animation and spatial audio.
  - Titans of Space Plus: A paid app that takes users on a guided tour of the solar system and beyond, using VR graphics and narration to create a sense of scale and awe.
  - Google Earth VR: A free app that lets users explore the world from a new perspective, using VR to fly over cities, landmarks, and natural wonders, and even go back in time to see historical imagery.
  - Kingspray Graffiti VR: A paid app that allows users to create realistic graffiti art on various surfaces, using VR spray cans, nozzles, and colors, and share their creations with other users online.
  - Littlstar: A free app that provides access to a library of VR content, such as movies, documentaries, music videos, and live events, using VR streaming and playback technology.
  - Ocean Rift: A paid app that simulates an underwater world, where users can swim with various marine animals, such as sharks, dolphins, turtles, and whales, using VR graphics and sound effects.
  - The Foo Show: A free app that features a talk show in VR, where users can join the host and guests as they explore and discuss various topics, such as video games, movies, and science, using VR interaction and animation.
  - Tilt Brush: A paid app that lets users paint in 3D space, using VR brushes, colors, and effects, and share their artworks with other users online.
  - Virtual Desktop: A paid app that enables users to use their computer in VR, using VR display and input devices, and customize their virtual environment with various themes and options.
  - Within: A free app that offers a collection of VR stories, such as documentaries, animations, and interactive experiences, using VR storytelling and immersive media.
  - Fulldive VR: A free app that integrates various VR features, such as browsing, watching, gaming, and socializing, using VR interface and functionality.
