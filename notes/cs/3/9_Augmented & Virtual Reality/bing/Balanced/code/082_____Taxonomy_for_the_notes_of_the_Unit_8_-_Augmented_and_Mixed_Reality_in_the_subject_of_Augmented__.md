# Taxonomy for the notes of the Unit 8 - Augmented and Mixed Reality

- Taxonomy is a system of classification that organizes and categorizes concepts or objects based on their characteristics and relationships.
- Augmented reality (AR) is a technology that enhances the perception of the real world by overlaying virtual elements (such as images, sounds, or texts) on the user's view.
- Mixed reality (MR) is a technology that combines the real and virtual worlds by allowing the user to interact with both physical and digital objects in a seamless way.
- A taxonomy of AR and MR applications can help to understand the different types, purposes, and features of these technologies, as well as to identify the challenges and opportunities for their development and use.
- Based on the literature review, a possible taxonomy of AR and MR applications can be composed of the following facets and categories:

  - **Purpose**: The main goal or objective of the application, such as education, entertainment, health, or industry.
  - **Content**: The type and format of the virtual elements that are displayed to the user, such as 2D or 3D graphics, audio, video, or text.
  - **Display**: The device or medium that is used to present the virtual elements to the user, such as head-mounted displays, handheld devices, projectors, or glasses.
  - **Tracking**: The method or technique that is used to align and register the virtual elements with the real world, such as markers, sensors, cameras, or GPS.
  - **Interaction**: The way the user can manipulate or control the virtual elements or the application, such as gestures, voice, touch, or buttons.
  - **Environment**: The physical or virtual context or setting where the application is used, such as indoors, outdoors, or hybrid.
  - **User**: The characteristics and preferences of the target audience or user group of the application, such as age, gender, education, or disability.

- An example of an AR application using this taxonomy is:

  - **Purpose**: Education
  - **Content**: 3D graphics and text
  - **Display**: Handheld device (smartphone or tablet)
  - **Tracking**: Markers (QR codes or images)
  - **Interaction**: Touch and buttons
  - **Environment**: Indoors (classroom or museum)
  - **User**: Children or students

- An example of an MR application using this taxonomy is:

  - **Purpose**: Entertainment
  - **Content**: 3D graphics and audio
  - **Display**: Head-mounted display (HMD)
  - **Tracking**: Sensors and cameras
  - **Interaction**: Gestures and voice
  - **Environment**: Hybrid (real and virtual)
  - **User**: Adults or gamers

- This taxonomy is not exhaustive or definitive, and it can be modified or extended according to the specific needs or criteria of the application or the user.
