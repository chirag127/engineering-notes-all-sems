### World Coordinate

- World coordinate is a system of coordinates that defines the position and orientation of objects in a virtual or augmented reality scene.
- World coordinate is usually defined by a three-dimensional Cartesian coordinate system, where the origin is an arbitrary point in the scene, and the axes are aligned with the directions of north, east, and up.
- World coordinate is also known as global coordinate or scene coordinate, and it is independent of the user's viewpoint or device orientation.
- World coordinate is useful for defining the locations and orientations of static objects, such as buildings, landmarks, or furniture, that do not change relative to the scene.
- World coordinate can also be used to define the initial position and orientation of dynamic objects, such as avatars, vehicles, or animals, that can move or rotate within the scene.
- World coordinate can be transformed to other coordinate systems, such as camera coordinate, device coordinate, or local coordinate, by applying translation, rotation, and scaling operations. These transformations are usually represented by matrices or quaternions.
