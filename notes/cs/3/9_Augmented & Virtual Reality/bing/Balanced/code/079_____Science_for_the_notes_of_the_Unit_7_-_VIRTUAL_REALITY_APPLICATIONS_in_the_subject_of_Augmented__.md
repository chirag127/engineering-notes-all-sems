## Unit 7 - Virtual Reality Applications

- Virtual reality applications are applications that make use of virtual reality (VR), an immersive sensory experience that digitally simulates a virtual environment.
- VR uses software and hardware to immerse a user, and potentially other viewers, in a digital environment. They get to explore and interact with virtual surroundings, and in some cases, also their physical environment.
- VR applications require interactive devices, such as goggles, headsets, gloves, or body suits, that send and receive information and create a stereoscopic or multisensory display for the user.
- VR applications can be used for various purposes, such as entertainment, education, business, health, art, and social interaction  .
- Some examples of VR applications are:
  - Google Expeditions: A free app that allows users to experience virtual field trips to different places, such as museums, landmarks, and natural wonders.
  - Colosse: A free app that showcases a stunning animated story in VR, where the user can influence the outcome by looking at different characters.
  - Titans of Space Plus: A paid app that takes users on a guided tour of the solar system and beyond, with realistic and scaled-down models of planets, moons, and stars.
  - Google Earth VR: A free app that lets users explore the world in VR, from cities to mountains, and even fly over the globe.
  - Kingspray Graffiti VR: A paid app that allows users to create graffiti art on realistic walls, with various spray cans, colors, and effects.
  - Littlstar: A free app that streams 360-degree videos and VR content from various sources, such as National Geographic, Discovery, and Red Bull.
  - Ocean Rift: A paid app that simulates an underwater world, where users can swim with various marine animals, such as sharks, dolphins, and turtles.
  - The Foo Show: A free app that features interviews with game developers, artists, and celebrities in VR, where users can join the conversation and explore the virtual sets.
  - Tilt Brush: A paid app that lets users paint in 3D space, with various brushes, colors, and effects, and share their creations online.
  - Virtual Desktop: A paid app that enables users to use their computer in VR, with multiple monitors, custom backgrounds, and 3D environments.
  - Within: A free app that offers a variety of VR experiences, such as documentaries, animations, music videos, and games, from various creators and partners.
  - Fulldive VR: A free app that integrates various VR features, such as browsing, social media, video player, camera, and gallery, in one platform.
