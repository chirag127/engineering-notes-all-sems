## Unit 7 - VIRTUAL REALITY APPLICATIONS

- Virtual reality applications are applications that make use of virtual reality (VR), an immersive sensory experience that digitally simulates a virtual environment.
- Virtual reality uses software and hardware to immerse a user, and potentially other viewers, in a digital environment. They get to explore and interact with virtual surroundings, and in some cases, also their physical environment.
- Virtual reality applications can be classified into different categories, such as:
  - Entertainment: VR applications that provide fun and engaging experiences, such as video games, movies, concerts, etc. Examples: Allumette, Colosse, Kingspray Graffiti VR, etc.
  - Education: VR applications that facilitate learning and training, such as medical or military simulations, virtual tours, etc. Examples: Google Expeditions, Titans of Space Plus, Ocean Rift, etc.
  - Business: VR applications that enhance productivity and collaboration, such as virtual meetings, data visualization, remote work, etc. Examples: Virtual Desktop, Tilt Brush, The Foo Show, etc.
  - Social: VR applications that enable social interaction and communication, such as chat rooms, social networks, etc. Examples: Littlstar, Within, Fulldive VR, etc.
- Virtual reality applications require interactive devices, such as goggles, headsets, gloves, or body suits, that send and receive information and create a sense of presence and immersion in the virtual environment.
- Virtual reality applications can have various benefits, such as:
  - Providing a safe and controlled environment for learning and training.
  - Enhancing creativity and innovation.
  - Offering new and diverse experiences and perspectives.
  - Improving accessibility and inclusivity.
  - Reducing costs and environmental impacts.
