### Interaction - Simple

Interaction is the process of communicating and exchanging information with other entities, such as humans, computers, or the environment. Interaction can be achieved through various modalities, such as speech, gesture, touch, or gaze. Interaction can also be classified into different levels of complexity, such as simple, complex, or natural.

Simple interaction is the most basic form of interaction, where the user performs a single action or command to achieve a specific goal or task. Simple interaction does not require a lot of cognitive effort or feedback from the system, and it is often suitable for simple or repetitive tasks, such as selecting an option, clicking a button, or entering a keyword.

Some examples of simple interaction in augmented reality (AR) and virtual reality (VR) are:

- Using a controller or a hand gesture to point and select a virtual object or menu item in VR or AR.
- Using a voice command to activate a function or query a system in VR or AR, such as "What is that?" or "Play music".
- Using a touch screen or a physical button to trigger an action or switch between modes in VR or AR, such as taking a photo or changing the view.
- Using a gaze direction or a head movement to control a cursor or a camera in VR or AR, such as looking at an object or turning around.

Simple interaction can be effective and efficient for simple tasks, but it can also have some limitations, such as:

- It can be difficult to perform complex or nuanced tasks with simple interaction, such as drawing, editing, or manipulating virtual objects.
- It can be challenging to provide rich and meaningful feedback with simple interaction, such as confirming, correcting, or explaining the system's actions or responses.
- It can be awkward or uncomfortable to use simple interaction in some contexts or environments, such as public places, noisy situations, or limited spaces.

Therefore, simple interaction should be used where it is most appropriate and suitable for the user's needs, preferences, and goals, and where it can provide a satisfying and enjoyable user experience.
