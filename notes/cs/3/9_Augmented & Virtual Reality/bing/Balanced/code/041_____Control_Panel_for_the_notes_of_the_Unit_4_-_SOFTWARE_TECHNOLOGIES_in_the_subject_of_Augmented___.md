### Control Panel for the notes of the Unit 4 - SOFTWARE TECHNOLOGIES in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

- A control panel is a user interface that allows the user to interact with a machine, system, or device, such as a computer, a smartphone, or a car.
- A control panel can be physical, such as a dashboard, a keyboard, or a touch screen, or virtual, such as a graphical user interface (GUI), a voice command, or a gesture recognition system.
- A control panel can also be augmented or virtual, meaning that it can be projected or displayed on a different surface or environment than the actual machine, system, or device, using augmented reality (AR) or virtual reality (VR) technologies.
- AR and VR are software technologies that create immersive and interactive 3D experiences for the user, by overlaying digital information or objects on the real world (AR), or by creating a simulated world (VR).
- AR and VR control panels can have several advantages over traditional control panels, such as:
  - They can be customized and adapted to different user preferences, needs, and contexts.
  - They can provide more information and feedback to the user, such as data visualization, instructions, alerts, and guidance.
  - They can enhance the user's perception and awareness of the machine, system, or device, and its surroundings, such as by highlighting important features, showing hidden components, or detecting hazards.
  - They can reduce the user's cognitive load and physical effort, by simplifying the interaction, reducing the number of steps, or enabling hands-free or remote control.
- Some examples of AR and VR control panels are:
  - SmartPanel by Augumenta, which is an AR user interface for machine control, that can be projected on any surface or wearable device, and can be controlled by gestures or voice commands.
  - VR and XR command and control by Mechdyne, which is a VR and mixed reality (MR) system for monitoring and controlling complex operations, such as military, emergency, or industrial scenarios, that can be accessed from a virtual control room or a field location.
  - SketchFab by SketchFab, which is an AR and VR platform for managing and sharing 3D assets, such as models, scenes, or animations, that can be viewed and edited on various devices, such as smartphones, tablets, or headsets.
