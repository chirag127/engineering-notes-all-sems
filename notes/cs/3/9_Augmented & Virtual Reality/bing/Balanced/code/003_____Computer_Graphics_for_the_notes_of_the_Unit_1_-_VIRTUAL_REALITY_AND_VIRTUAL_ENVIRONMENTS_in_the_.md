### Computer Graphics for the notes of the Unit 1 - VIRTUAL REALITY AND VIRTUAL ENVIRONMENTS in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

- Virtual reality (VR) is the use of computer graphics systems in combination with various display and interface devices to provide the effect of immersion in the interactive 3D computer-generated environment . We call such an environment a virtual environment (VE).
- A virtual environment can simulate reality or create a fictional world that can be explored and manipulated by the user. The user can perceive the virtual environment through different senses, such as vision, hearing, touch, and sometimes smell or taste .
- The main components of a VR system are:
  - A computer that generates the graphics and sounds of the virtual environment.
  - A display device that presents the virtual environment to the user, such as a head-mounted display (HMD), a projection screen, or a monitor.
  - An input device that allows the user to interact with the virtual environment, such as a joystick, a glove, a mouse, a keyboard, or a voice recognition system.
  - A tracking device that measures the position and orientation of the user or a part of the user's body, such as the head, the hand, or the eye.
  - A feedback device that provides sensory feedback to the user, such as a haptic device, a speaker, or a fan.
- The main challenges of VR are:
  - Creating realistic and convincing graphics and sounds that can immerse the user in the virtual environment.
  - Providing fast and accurate tracking and feedback that can match the user's movements and actions.
  - Avoiding or minimizing negative effects of VR, such as cybersickness, disorientation, or fatigue.
  - Designing user-friendly and intuitive interfaces that can enhance the user's experience and performance in the virtual environment.
  - Evaluating the effectiveness and usability of VR for different applications and domains, such as education, entertainment, health, or engineering.
