### AR systems and functionality

- AR systems are systems that combine real and virtual worlds, allow real-time interaction, and align virtual and real objects accurately.
- AR systems can be classified into three types based on the display technology: head-mounted displays (HMDs), handheld displays, and spatial displays.
- AR systems can also be categorized based on the tracking technology: marker-based, markerless, and hybrid.
- AR systems can be used for various applications, such as education, entertainment, gaming, tourism, healthcare, engineering, and architecture  .
- AR systems can provide benefits such as enhanced learning, improved collaboration, increased engagement, and reduced errors.
- AR systems can also pose challenges such as technical limitations, user acceptance, privacy, and ethical issues .
