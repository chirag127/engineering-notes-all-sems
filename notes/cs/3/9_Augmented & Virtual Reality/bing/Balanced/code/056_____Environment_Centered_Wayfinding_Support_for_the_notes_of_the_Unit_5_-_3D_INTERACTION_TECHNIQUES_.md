# Environment Centered Wayfinding Support

- Wayfinding is the cognitive process of defining a path through an environment, using and acquiring spatial knowledge, and being aided by natural and artificial cues .
- Wayfinding is important because it helps us better understand our environment, instead of being overwhelmed by a lot of small details.
- Environment centered wayfinding support is the design of a virtual environment that supports wayfinding by providing cues, landmarks, maps, and other features that help the user navigate and orient themselves.
- Some examples of environment centered wayfinding support are:
  - Using consistent and meaningful colors, textures, shapes, and symbols to distinguish different areas and objects in the virtual environment .
  - Providing clear and visible signs, labels, arrows, and icons that indicate directions, distances, and destinations .
  - Creating recognizable and memorable landmarks that serve as reference points and orientation aids .
  - Providing maps, compasses, or other tools that show the user's location, orientation, and relation to other parts of the virtual environment .
  - Using sound, haptics, or other modalities to convey spatial information and feedback .
- Environment centered wayfinding support can improve the user's sense of presence, immersion, engagement, and satisfaction in the virtual environment, as well as reduce the cognitive load, frustration, and disorientation .
