### Voice Commands for the notes of the Unit 5 - 3D INTERACTION TECHNIQUES in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

- Voice commands are a type of system control technique that allow users to interact with 3D environments by speaking phrases into a microphone  .
- Voice commands are useful for actions that require a single activation, such as selecting an object, changing a mode, or issuing a command  .
- Voice commands are also suitable for hands-free and natural interaction, especially when the user's hands are occupied or the input device is not convenient  .
- Voice commands can be combined with other input modalities, such as gestures, to create multimodal interfaces that enhance the user's expressiveness and flexibility  .
- Voice commands have some limitations, such as the need for users to learn the available phrases, the accuracy and reliability of voice recognition, the noise and privacy issues, and the lack of feedback and affordance  .
- Voice commands can be implemented using various techniques, such as rule-based, statistical, or neural network approaches, depending on the complexity and domain of the application  .
- Voice commands can be evaluated using various metrics, such as recognition rate, response time, user satisfaction, and task performance  .
