### Architecture for the notes of the Unit 7 - VIRTUAL REALITY APPLICATIONS in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

Architecture is the design and construction of buildings and structures, and it is an important application area for virtual reality (VR). The following are some of the key concepts related to VR in architecture:

1. Virtual Reality Modeling: Virtual reality modeling is the process of creating a 3D model of a building or structure in VR, allowing architects and designers to visualize and interact with their designs.

2. Virtual Reality Walkthroughs: Virtual reality walkthroughs are interactive simulations of buildings and structures, allowing architects and designers to experience their designs as if they were physically present.

3. Virtual Reality Prototyping: Virtual reality prototyping is the process of testing and refining building designs in VR, allowing architects and designers to identify and resolve design issues before construction begins.

4. Collaboration: VR in architecture allows architects and designers to collaborate in real-time, regardless of location, making it easier to share ideas and make decisions.

5. Client Presentations: VR in architecture allows architects and designers to present their designs to clients in a more immersive and interactive way, allowing clients to better understand and experience the design.

6. Sustainability: VR in architecture allows architects and designers to evaluate the sustainability of their designs, such as energy efficiency and environmental impact, in a virtual environment.

In this unit, you will learn about the application of VR in architecture, and you will learn how VR is used in the design and construction of buildings and structures. You will also learn about the key concepts related to VR in architecture, including virtual reality modeling, virtual reality walkthroughs, virtual reality prototyping, collaboration, client presentations, and sustainability, and you will learn how these concepts are used in architecture.

This unit will provide a foundation for understanding the principles and practices of VR in architecture, and for exploring the various concepts and techniques used in the field of Augmented and Virtual Reality.
