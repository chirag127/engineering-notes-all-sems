### visualization techniques for augmented reality for the notes of the Unit 8 - Augmented and Mixed Reality in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

Augmented Reality (AR) visualization techniques:
1. Marker-based AR: Uses predefined 2D or 3D markers to trigger AR content.
2. Location-based AR: AR content is triggered based on GPS, compass, or accelerometer data.
3. Image recognition AR: AR content is triggered by recognizing specific images or objects.
4. Projection-based AR: AR content is projected onto real-world surfaces.
5. Superimposition-based AR: AR content is superimposed over the real world.
6. Object recognition AR: AR content is triggered by recognizing specific objects.
7. Prototype AR: AR content is displayed in a virtual prototype of a product.
8. Spatial AR: AR content is placed in the real world in a specific location.
