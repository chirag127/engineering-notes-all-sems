## Unit 8 - Augmented and Mixed Reality

Unit 8 - Augmented and Mixed Reality is a technology that enhances or alters the physical world with virtual information. It involves the integration of digital information into the physical world.

Augmented Reality (AR) adds virtual information to the real world, while Mixed Reality (MR) blends the physical and virtual worlds. AR uses a device's camera to display virtual objects on top of the real world, while MR uses holograms to create virtual objects that interact with the real world.

Examples of AR applications include Snapchat filters, IKEA Place, and Pokemon Go. MR examples include Microsoft's HoloLens and Magic Leap.

Both AR and MR have potential applications in many fields, including education, gaming, retail, and healthcare. For example, AR can be used to provide real-time information in a museum, MR can be used to train surgeons in a simulated environment.
