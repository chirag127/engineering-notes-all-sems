### difference between AR and VR for the notes of the Unit 8 - Augmented and Mixed Reality in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

Unit 8 of the Augmented & Virtual Reality course focuses on Augmented and Mixed Reality and covers the aspect of the difference between AR and VR.

Augmented Reality (AR) and Virtual Reality (VR) are two distinct technologies that are used to create immersive experiences. AR enhances the real world by adding virtual elements to it, while VR creates a completely artificial environment that replaces the real world.

In AR, virtual elements are overlaid onto the real world, and the user can interact with both the real world and the virtual elements. AR is typically experienced through a smartphone or tablet, using the device's camera to view the real world and display virtual elements.

In VR, the user is fully immersed in a virtual environment, and the real world is not visible. VR is typically experienced through a head-mounted display (HMD), which provides a stereoscopic view of the virtual environment.

In terms of applications, AR is often used for information and entertainment purposes, such as providing information about a product or location, or for gaming. VR is often used for simulation and training purposes, such as for flight simulation or medical training.

In the context of the Augmented & Virtual Reality course, students in Unit 8 will learn about the difference between AR and VR as part of the discussion on Augmented and Mixed Reality. This may include working with examples of AR and VR, analyzing the benefits and limitations of each technology, and learning how to choose the appropriate technology for a given application.
