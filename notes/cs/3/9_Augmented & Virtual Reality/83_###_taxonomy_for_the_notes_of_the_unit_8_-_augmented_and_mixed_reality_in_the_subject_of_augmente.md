### Taxonomy for the notes of the Unit 8 - Augmented and Mixed Reality in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

Taxonomy of Unit 8 - Augmented and Mixed Reality:

1. Augmented Reality (AR): Definition, Types (Marker-based, Markerless, Projection-based, Superimposition-based)

2. Mixed Reality (MR): Definition, Types (Projection-based, Superimposition-based)

3. AR/MR Applications: Gaming, Education, Healthcare, Retail, Industry, etc.

4. AR/MR Technologies: Head-mounted displays, Smart Glasses, Mobile AR, Spatial Computing

5. AR/MR Development Tools and Platforms: Unity, ARKit, ARCore, Vuforia, Hololens, etc.

6. AR/MR Interaction Techniques: Gesture recognition, Voice control, Touch control, etc.

7. AR/MR Limitations and Challenges: Technical, User Experience, Privacy, etc.

8. AR/MR Future Trends: 5G, Edge Computing, AI, etc.
