### Flight simulation for the notes of the Unit 1 - VIRTUAL REALITY AND VIRTUAL ENVIRONMENTS in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

Unit 1 of the Augmented & Virtual Reality course focuses on Virtual Reality and Virtual Environments and covers the aspect of Flight Simulation.

Flight simulation is a type of virtual environment that simulates the experience of flying an aircraft. In a flight simulation, the user interacts with a virtual aircraft in a virtual environment, using controls and displays that are similar to those found in a real aircraft. The user can fly the aircraft in a variety of conditions, including different weather conditions, and can experience the effects of the flight on the aircraft and the environment.

Flight simulation is used for a variety of purposes, including training pilots, testing aircraft designs, and exploring the behavior of aircraft in different conditions. Flight simulation can provide a safe and controlled environment for pilots to practice their skills, without the risks associated with flying a real aircraft. Flight simulation can also be used to test aircraft designs, by simulating the behavior of the aircraft in different conditions and evaluating its performance.

In the context of the Augmented & Virtual Reality course, students in Unit 1 will learn about flight simulation as part of the discussion on Virtual Reality and Virtual Environments. This may include working with examples of flight simulation, analyzing the benefits and limitations of flight simulation, and learning how to design and implement flight simulation systems.
