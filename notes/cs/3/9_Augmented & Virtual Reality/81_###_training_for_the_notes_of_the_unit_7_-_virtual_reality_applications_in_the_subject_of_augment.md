### Training for the notes of the Unit 7 - VIRTUAL REALITY APPLICATIONS in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

Unit 7 - Virtual Reality Applications:
- Introduction to Virtual Reality (VR)
- VR hardware and software
- VR applications in various fields (e.g. gaming, education, healthcare, tourism, etc.)
- VR design principles and best practices
- VR development tools and platforms
- Challenges and limitations of VR technology
- Future trends in VR

Note: This unit provides an overview of Virtual Reality technology and its applications in various fields. It covers the hardware and software components of VR, design principles, development tools, and challenges. The unit concludes with a discussion of future trends in VR.
