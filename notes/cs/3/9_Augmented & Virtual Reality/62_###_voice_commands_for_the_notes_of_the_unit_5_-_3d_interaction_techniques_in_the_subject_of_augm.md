### Voice Commands for the notes of the Unit 5 - 3D INTERACTION TECHNIQUES in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

Voice Commands are a type of interaction technique used to control virtual environments using spoken language. The following are some of the key concepts related to Voice Commands:

1. Speech Recognition: Speech recognition is the process of converting spoken language into text, and it is a key component of voice command systems.

2. Natural Language Processing: Natural language processing is the process of analyzing and understanding spoken language, and it is used to interpret voice commands and respond appropriately.

3. Voice Commands: Voice commands are specific phrases or words used to control virtual environments, such as "Open door" or "Turn left".

4. Context Awareness: Context awareness is the ability of a voice command system to understand the context in which a voice command is given, such as the location of the user or the state of the virtual environment.

5. Error Correction: Error correction is the ability of a voice command system to recognize and correct errors in spoken language, such as mispronunciations or background noise.

6. Personalization: Personalization is the ability of a voice command system to adapt to the user's preferences and habits, such as recognizing the user's voice or preferred voice commands.

In this unit, you will learn about Voice Commands and how they are used to control virtual environments. You will also learn about the key concepts related to Voice Commands, including speech recognition, natural language processing, voice commands, context awareness, error correction, and personalization, and you will learn how these concepts are used in voice command systems.

This unit will provide a foundation for understanding the principles and practices of Voice Commands, and for exploring the various concepts and techniques used in the field of Augmented and Virtual Reality.
