### World Environment for the notes of the Unit 4 - SOFTWARE TECHNOLOGIES in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

World environment refers to the physical and biological systems that make up the planet Earth. It includes the atmosphere, oceans, forests, wildlife, and other natural resources. Human activities, such as industrialization and deforestation, have a significant impact on the world environment. Climate change, pollution, and loss of biodiversity are among the major environmental challenges facing the world today. Augmented and virtual reality technologies have the potential to help address these challenges by providing new ways to visualize and understand environmental data, and by enabling more sustainable practices in industries such as agriculture, energy, and transportation.
