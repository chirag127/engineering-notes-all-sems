### Hardware Controls for the notes of the Unit 4 - SOFTWARE TECHNOLOGIES in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

Hardware Controls are the physical devices used to interact with virtual environments in Augmented and Virtual Reality (AR/VR) systems. The following are some of the common hardware controls used in AR/VR:

1. Head-Mounted Displays (HMDs): HMDs are the most common type of hardware control used in AR/VR, and they are worn on the head to provide a visual display of the virtual environment.

2. Controllers: Controllers are handheld devices used to interact with virtual environments, and they can include motion controllers, touch controllers, and gamepads.

3. Tracking Systems: Tracking systems are used to track the movements of the user and the hardware controls, allowing for accurate and responsive interaction with the virtual environment.

4. Input Devices: Input devices include keyboards, mice, and other devices used to input data into the AR/VR system.

5. Sensors: Sensors such as accelerometers, gyroscopes, and magnetometers are used to track the movements and orientation of the hardware controls.

In this unit, you will learn about the hardware controls used in AR/VR, and you will learn about the key concepts and components of hardware controls, including Head-Mounted Displays, Controllers, Tracking Systems, Input Devices, and Sensors.

This unit will provide a foundation for understanding the principles and practices of hardware controls in AR/VR, and for exploring the various concepts and techniques used in the field of Augmented and Virtual Reality.
