### enhancing interactivity in AR environments for the notes of the Unit 8 - Augmented and Mixed Reality in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

Unit 8 - Augmented and Mixed Reality:
- Enhancing Interactivity in AR Environments:
  1. Multi-touch and gesture-based interaction
  2. Voice and speech recognition
  3. Object recognition and tracking
  4. Spatial mapping and tracking
  5. Haptic feedback
  6. Eye-tracking

Note: Interactivity is an important aspect of AR environments, as it allows users to engage with digital content in a natural and intuitive way. This unit covers various techniques for enhancing interactivity in AR, including multi-touch and gesture-based interaction, voice and speech recognition, object recognition and tracking, spatial mapping and tracking, haptic feedback, and eye-tracking.
