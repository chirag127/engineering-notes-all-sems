### Symbolic Input Tasks for the notes of the Unit 5 - 3D INTERACTION TECHNIQUES in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

The Symbolic Input Tasks section of the Unit 5 - 3D Interaction Techniques in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality should cover the following topics:

1. Definition of Symbolic Input Tasks (tasks performed using symbolic input, such as text or numbers)
2. Overview of Symbolic Input Tasks (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
3. Types of Symbolic Input Tasks (e.g. text input, number input, etc.)
4. Overview of Types of Symbolic Input Tasks (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
5. Design Guidelines for Symbolic Input Tasks (principles used to guide the design of symbolic input tasks)
6. Overview of Design Guidelines for Symbolic Input Tasks (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
7. Limitations of Symbolic Input Tasks (e.g. accuracy, complexity, compatibility, etc.)
8. Techniques for overcoming limitations (e.g. using advanced techniques, improving accuracy, etc.)
9. Symbolic Input Tasks and Industry Applications (how symbolic input tasks are used in various industries)
10. Further reading and resources (books, articles, online resources)

Symbolic Input Tasks is an important component of the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality. Understanding the principles and techniques of symbolic input tasks helps students to develop the skills required to design and implement virtual environments. The hands-on practice and further reading resources will help students to develop their skills and deepen their understanding of the material.
