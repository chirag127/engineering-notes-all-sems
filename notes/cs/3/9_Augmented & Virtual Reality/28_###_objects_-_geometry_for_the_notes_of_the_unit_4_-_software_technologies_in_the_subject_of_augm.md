### Objects - Geometry for the notes of the Unit 4 - SOFTWARE TECHNOLOGIES in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

Objects in Geometry for Augmented & Virtual Reality:

- Points: Represented as a single coordinate in 3D space
- Lines: Connect two points, represented by a start and end point
- Planes: Flat surfaces defined by 3 points in 3D space
- Polygons: 2D shapes with multiple sides, can be regular or irregular
- Solids: 3D objects with volume, examples include cube, sphere, cylinder
- Transformations: Changes to position, orientation, or size of an object in 3D space
- Modeling: Creating objects in 3D space using software tools
- Meshes: Representation of objects as interconnected polyggonal faces
- Textures: 2D images applied to surfaces of 3D objects to add detail and realism

Note: These concepts are fundamental to understanding how objects are represented and manipulated in augmented and virtual reality environments.
