### AR systems and functionality for the notes of the Unit 8 - Augmented and Mixed Reality in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

AR (Augmented Reality) systems add virtual information to the real world environment. They can be experienced through devices like smartphones, tablets, smart glasses, and head-mounted displays. AR systems use computer vision, sensors, and displays to overlay digital content on the real world.

Functionalities of AR systems include:

1. Object recognition: AR systems can recognize objects in the real world and add virtual information to them.

2. Image tracking: AR systems can track images in the real world and add virtual information to them.

3. Location-based AR: AR systems can add virtual information to the real world based on the user's location.

4. Spatial mapping: AR systems can create a 3D map of the real world and add virtual information to it.

5. Interaction: AR systems can allow users to interact with virtual information in the real world.

6. Multimodal input: AR systems can accept input from multiple sources, such as touch, voice, gestures, and more.

7. Networked AR: AR systems can connect to the internet and access information from online sources.

8. Persistence: AR systems can store virtual information in the real world and make it accessible to users at a later time.
