JPEG-LS uses a combination of prediction, context modeling, and entropy coding to achieve high compression ratios, and is particularly well-suited for images with smooth transitions between colors. JPEG-LS is widely used in the medical imaging, remote sensing, and digital photography industries, and is a standard part of many image and document formats, including DICOM.
