### Bi-level image compression-The JBIG standard for the notes of the Unit 3 - Coding a sequence in the subject of Data Compression

The Bi-level Image Compression-The JBIG Standard section of the Unit 3 - Coding a Sequence in the subject of Data Compression should cover the following topics:

1. Definition of Bi-level Image Compression (compression of binary images)
2. Overview of Bi-level Image Compression (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
3. The JBIG Standard (e.g. a standard for bi-level image compression)
4. Overview of The JBIG Standard (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
5. JBIG Algorithm (e.g. the steps involved in implementing the JBIG standard)
6. Overview of JBIG Algorithm (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
7. JBIG Performance (e.g. how well the JBIG algorithm performs in compressing bi-level images)
8. Overview of JBIG Performance (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
9. JBIG Applications (e.g. applications that use the JBIG standard)
10. Overview of JBIG Applications (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
11. Further reading and resources (books, articles, online resources)

Bi-level Image Compression-The JBIG Standard is an important component of the subject of Data Compression. Understanding the principles and techniques of bi-level image compression and the JBIG standard helps students to develop the skills required to design and implement effective data compression solutions for binary images. The hands-on practice and further reading resources will help students to develop their skills and deepen their understanding of the material.
