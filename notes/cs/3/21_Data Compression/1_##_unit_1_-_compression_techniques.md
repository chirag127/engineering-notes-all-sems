## Unit 1 - Compression Techniques

The Unit 1 - Compression Techniques section of the subject of Real Time Systems should cover the following topics:

1. Definition of Compression Techniques (methods used to reduce the size of data)
2. Overview of Compression Techniques (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
3. Types of Compression Techniques (e.g. lossless compression, lossy compression, etc.)
4. Overview of Types of Compression Techniques (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
5. Lossless Compression Algorithms (e.g. Huffman coding, Run Length Encoding, etc.)
6. Overview of Lossless Compression Algorithms (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
7. Lossy Compression Algorithms (e.g. Discrete Cosine Transform, Fractal Compression, etc.)
8. Overview of Lossy Compression Algorithms (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
9. Compression Techniques in Real-Time Systems (e.g. the use of compression techniques in real-time systems)
10. Overview of Compression Techniques in Real-Time Systems (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
11. Compression Techniques in Industry Applications (how compression techniques are used in various industries)
12. Further reading and resources (books, articles, online resources)

Compression Techniques is an important component of the subject of Real Time Systems, especially in the context of real-time communication and data storage. Understanding the principles and techniques of compression helps students to develop the skills required to design and implement effective real-time systems that use compression. The hands-on practice and further reading resources will help students to develop their skills and deepen their understanding of the material.
