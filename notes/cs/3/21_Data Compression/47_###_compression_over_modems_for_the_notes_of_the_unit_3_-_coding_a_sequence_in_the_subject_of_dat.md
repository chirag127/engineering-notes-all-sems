### Compression over Modems for the notes of the Unit 3 - Coding a sequence in the subject of Data Compression

The Compression over Modems section of the Unit 3 - Coding a Sequence in the subject of Data Compression should cover the following topics:

1. Definition of Compression over Modems (compression of data transmitted over modems)
2. Overview of Compression over Modems (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
3. Modem Compression Techniques (e.g. Huffman coding, run-length coding, etc.)
4. Overview of Modem Compression Techniques (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
5. Modem Compression Performance (e.g. how well modem compression techniques perform in compressing data transmitted over modems)
6. Overview of Modem Compression Performance (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
7. Modem Compression Applications (e.g. applications that use modem compression techniques)
8. Overview of Modem Compression Applications (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
9. Compression over Modems and the Huffman coding algorithm (e.g. how the Huffman coding algorithm is used in compression over modems)
10. Overview of Compression over Modems and the Huffman coding algorithm (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
11. Further reading and resources (books, articles, online resources)

Compression over Modems is an important component of the subject of Data Compression. Understanding the principles and techniques of compression over modems helps students to develop the skills required to design and implement effective data compression solutions for data transmitted over modems. The hands-on practice and further reading resources will help students to develop their skills and deepen their understanding of the material.
