2. Golomb Codes: Golomb codes are a type of entropy coding that are used to compress integer data. Golomb codes are based on the idea of dividing the integers into groups, and using a fixed-length code for each group.

3. Fixed-Length Codes: Fixed-length codes are codes that have a fixed length, regardless of the size of the data being compressed. Fixed-length codes are used in Golomb codes to compress integer data.

4. Variable-Length Codes: Variable-length codes are codes that have a length that varies based on the size of the data being compressed. Variable-length codes are used in other entropy coding techniques, such as Huffman coding.
