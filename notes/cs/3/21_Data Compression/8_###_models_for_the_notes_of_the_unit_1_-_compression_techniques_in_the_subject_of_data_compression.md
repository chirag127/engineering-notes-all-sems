### Models for the notes of the Unit 1 - Compression Techniques in the subject of Data Compression

The Models section of the Unit 1 - Compression Techniques in the subject of Data Compression should cover the following topics:

1. Definition of Data Compression Models (models used to represent data for compression purposes)
2. Overview of Data Compression Models (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
3. Types of Data Compression Models (e.g. entropy models, statistical models, etc.)
4. Overview of Types of Data Compression Models (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
5. Entropy Models (e.g. Huffman coding, arithmetic coding, etc.)
6. Overview of Entropy Models (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
7. Statistical Models (e.g. Markov models, predictive coding, etc.)
8. Overview of Statistical Models (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
9. Hybrid Models (e.g. models that combine entropy and statistical models)
10. Overview of Hybrid Models (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
11. Data Compression Model Performance (e.g. how well the models perform in compressing data)
12. Overview of Data Compression Model Performance (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
13. Data Compression Model Applications (e.g. applications that use data compression models)
14. Overview of Data Compression Model Applications (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
15. Further reading and resources (books, articles, online resources)

Data Compression Models are an important component of the subject of Data Compression. Understanding the principles and techniques of data compression models helps students to develop the skills required to design and implement effective data compression solutions. The hands-on practice and further reading resources will help students to develop their skills and deepen their understanding of the material.
