## Unit 4 - Distortion criteria

The Unit 4 - Distortion Criteria in the subject of Data Compression should cover the following topics:

1. Overview of the Unit (what the unit covers and why it is important)
2. Objectives of the Unit (what students will learn and be able to do after completing the unit)
3. Prerequisites for the Unit (what prior knowledge or skills students should have before starting the unit)
4. Outline of the Unit (what topics will be covered in the unit and in what order)
5. Definition of Distortion Criteria (a measure of the difference between the original data and the compressed data)
6. Overview of Distortion Criteria (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
7. Types of Distortion Criteria (e.g. mean squared error, peak signal-to-noise ratio, etc.)
8. Overview of Types of Distortion Criteria (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
9. Distortion Criteria in Data Compression (e.g. how distortion criteria are used in data compression)
10. Overview of Distortion Criteria in Data Compression (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
11. Distortion Criteria in Industry Applications (how distortion criteria are used in various industries)
12. Further reading and resources (books, articles, online resources)

Distortion Criteria is an important component of the subject of Data Compression. Understanding the principles and techniques of distortion criteria helps students to develop the skills required to design and implement effective data compression solutions that produce accurate and reliable results. The hands-on practice and further reading resources will help students to develop their skills and deepen their understanding of the material.
