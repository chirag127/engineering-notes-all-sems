Move-to-front coding:
- Move-to-front coding is a lossless data compression technique.
- It is based on the idea of maintaining a list of symbols and moving frequently-occurring symbols to the front of the list.
- The symbols are then encoded by their position in the list, with the most frequently-occurring symbols having the shortest encoding.
- Move-to-front coding is simple to implement and can provide good compression for datasets with repeating patterns.
