### Markov models for the notes of the Unit 1 - Compression Techniques in the subject of Data Compression

Markov models are a type of mathematical model used in data compression to represent the probability of a sequence of events. The following are some of the key concepts related to Markov models:

1. Probability: Markov models use probability to represent the likelihood of a sequence of events. Probability is a measure of the likelihood of an event occurring, and is used to model the behavior of a system.

2. Sequence of Events: Markov models represent the probability of a sequence of events, which are a series of events that occur in a specific order. The sequence of events can be used to model the behavior of a system, such as the behavior of a data stream.

3. Markov Chain: A Markov chain is a mathematical model that represents a sequence of events and the probability of each event occurring given the previous event. Markov chains are used to model the behavior of a system, such as the behavior of a data stream.

4. Markov Process: A Markov process is a mathematical model that represents a sequence of events and the probability of each event occurring given the entire history of the system. Markov processes are used to model the behavior of a system, such as the behavior of a data stream.

5. Hidden Markov Models: Hidden Markov models are a type of Markov model that are used to model the behavior of a system when the state of the system is hidden. Hidden Markov models are used in data compression to model the behavior of a data stream, and to predict the next symbol in the data stream.

6. Applications: Markov models are used in a variety of applications, including data compression, speech recognition, and more. In data compression, Markov models are used to model the behavior of a data stream and to predict the next symbol in the data stream, which can be used to improve the efficiency of data compression algorithms.
