### Models for the notes of the Unit 4 - Distortion criteria in the subject of Data Compression

The Models section of the Unit 4 - Distortion criteria in the subject of Data Compression should cover the following topics:

1. Definition of Models (mathematical representations used in data compression)
2. Overview of Models (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
3. Types of Models (e.g. statistical models, transform models, etc.)
4. Overview of Types of Models (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
5. Modeling Techniques (e.g. techniques used to create models)
6. Overview of Modeling Techniques (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
7. Model Performance (e.g. how well models perform in compressing data)
8. Overview of Model Performance (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
9. Models and Data Compression (e.g. how models are used in data compression)
10. Overview of Models and Data Compression (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
11. Models in Industry Applications (how models are used in various industries)
12. Further reading and resources (books, articles, online resources)

Models are an important component of the subject of Data Compression, especially in the context of distortion criteria. Understanding the principles and techniques of models helps students to develop the skills required to design and implement effective data compression solutions. The hands-on practice and further reading resources will help students to develop their skills and deepen their understanding of the material.
