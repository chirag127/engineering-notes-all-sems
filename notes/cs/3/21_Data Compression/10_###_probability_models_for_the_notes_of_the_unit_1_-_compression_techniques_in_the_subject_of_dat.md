### Probability models for the notes of the Unit 1 - Compression Techniques in the subject of Data Compression

4. Overview of Types of Probability Models (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
5. Probability Modeling Techniques (e.g. techniques used to create probability models)
6. Overview of Probability Modeling Techniques (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
7. Probability Modeling in Data Compression (e.g. how probability models are used in data compression)
8. Overview of Probability Modeling in Data Compression (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
9. Probability Modeling in Industry Applications (how probability models are used in various industries)
10. Further reading and resources (books, articles, online resources)

Probability Models are an important component of the subject of Data Compression. Understanding the principles and techniques of probability models helps students to develop the skills required to design and implement effective data compression systems. The hands-on practice and further reading resources will help students to develop their skills and deepen their understanding of the material.
