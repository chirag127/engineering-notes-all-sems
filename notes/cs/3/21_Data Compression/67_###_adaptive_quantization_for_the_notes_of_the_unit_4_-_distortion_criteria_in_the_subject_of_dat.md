Unit 4 - Distortion criteria in the subject of Data Compression will cover the concept of Adaptive Quantization.

Adaptive Quantization is a data compression technique that adjusts the quantization step size based on the input signal. The quantization step size is chosen such that the distortion between the original signal and the quantized signal is minimized. Adaptive Quantization is used in many applications, including image and audio compression, to reduce the amount of data that needs to be stored or transmitted while maintaining the quality of the signal.
