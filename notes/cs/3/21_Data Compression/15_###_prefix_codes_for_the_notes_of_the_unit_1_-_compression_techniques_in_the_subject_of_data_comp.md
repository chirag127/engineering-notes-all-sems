### Prefix codes for the notes of the Unit 1 - Compression Techniques in the subject of Data Compression

The Prefix Codes section of the Unit 1 - Compression Techniques in the subject of Data Compression should cover the following topics:

1. Definition of Prefix Codes (a type of coding scheme used in data compression)
2. Overview of Prefix Codes (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
3. Properties of Prefix Codes (e.g. prefix-free, uniquely decodable, etc.)
4. Overview of Properties of Prefix Codes (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
5. Huffman Coding (e.g. a specific type of prefix code used in lossless data compression)
6. Overview of Huffman Coding (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
7. Huffman Coding Algorithm (e.g. the algorithm used to implement Huffman coding)
8. Overview of Huffman Coding Algorithm (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
9. Huffman Coding Performance (e.g. how well Huffman coding performs in data compression)
10. Overview of Huffman Coding Performance (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
11. Prefix Codes in Data Compression (e.g. how prefix codes are used in data compression)
12. Overview of Prefix Codes in Data Compression (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
13. Further reading and resources (books, articles, online resources)

Prefix Codes is an important component of the subject of Data Compression, especially in the context of lossless data compression. Understanding the principles and techniques of prefix codes helps students to develop the skills required to design and implement effective data compression techniques that use prefix codes. The hands-on practice and further reading resources will help students to develop their skills and deepen their understanding of the material.
