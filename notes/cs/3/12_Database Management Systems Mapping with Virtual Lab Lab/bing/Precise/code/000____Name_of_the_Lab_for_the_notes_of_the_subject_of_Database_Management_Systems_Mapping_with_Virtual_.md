## Name of the Lab for the notes of the subject of Database Management Systems Mapping with Virtual Lab Lab

- The Virtual Lab for Database Management Systems Mapping is a platform that provides an interactive learning experience for students studying the subject of Database Management Systems.
- The lab is designed to provide students with hands-on experience in working with databases and performing various operations on them.
- The lab provides a virtual environment where students can practice creating, modifying, and querying databases.
- The lab also provides a range of tutorials and exercises that help students understand the concepts of database management systems and how they are used in real-world scenarios.
- The lab is an excellent resource for students who want to learn more about database management systems and gain practical experience in working with them.
