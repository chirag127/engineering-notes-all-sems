## Database Management Lab for the subject of Database Management Systems Mapping with Virtual Lab Lab

- Database Management Systems (DBMS) is a software system that enables users to define, create, maintain, and control access to the database.
- A database is an organized collection of data, stored and accessed electronically.
- The DBMS provides various functions that allow entry, storage, and retrieval of large quantities of information and provides ways to manage how that information is organized.
- The Virtual Lab for Database Management Systems provides an online platform for students to learn and practice the concepts of DBMS.
- The Virtual Lab includes various modules that cover the topics of database design, SQL, normalization, transaction management, and indexing.
- The lab provides a hands-on learning experience for students, allowing them to apply the concepts they have learned in a practical setting.
- The Virtual Lab is an effective tool for students to prepare for exams and gain a deeper understanding of the subject of Database Management Systems.
