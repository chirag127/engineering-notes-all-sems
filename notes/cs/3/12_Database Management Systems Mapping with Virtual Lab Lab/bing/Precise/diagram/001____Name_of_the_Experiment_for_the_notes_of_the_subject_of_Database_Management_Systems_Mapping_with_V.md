## Name of the Experiment for the notes of the subject of Database Management Systems Mapping with Virtual Lab Lab

- The experiment is designed to help students understand the concepts of Database Management Systems and how they can be mapped with a Virtual Lab.
- The experiment involves the use of a virtual lab environment to simulate the process of creating and managing a database.
- Students will learn how to create tables, insert data, and perform queries on the database using SQL commands.
- The experiment also covers the use of various database management tools and techniques, such as indexing and normalization.
- By completing this experiment, students will gain a better understanding of how databases are used in real-world applications and how they can be effectively managed using a virtual lab environment.
