## Name of the Lab for the notes of the subject of Database Management Systems Mapping with Virtual Lab Lab

- The lab for the subject of Database Management Systems Mapping with Virtual Lab is a virtual laboratory that provides an interactive environment for students to learn and practice database management concepts.
- This lab allows students to work with various database management systems and tools, and to perform tasks such as creating, querying, and updating databases.
- The virtual lab provides a safe and controlled environment for students to experiment and learn without the risk of damaging real systems.
- The lab includes a variety of resources, such as tutorials, exercises, and quizzes, to help students learn and practice database management concepts.
- By using the virtual lab, students can gain practical experience and develop skills that are essential for working with databases in the real world.
