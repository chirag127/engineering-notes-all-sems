### Railway Reservation System for the notes of the Unit 12 - Mini project (Design & Development of Data and Application ) for following in the subject of Database Management Systems Lab

Unit 12 - Mini Project: Design & Development of Data and Application:
- Railway Reservation System:
  1. Overview: A system for managing the reservation of railway tickets, including the booking and cancellation of tickets.
  2. Requirements: Identifying the requirements for the system, including data storage, user interface, and functionality.
  3. Design: Designing the database and application, including data models, user interface, and workflow.
  4. Development: Developing the database and application, including coding, testing, and debugging.
  5. Deployment: Deploying the system in a production environment, including installation, configuration, and testing.
  6. Maintenance: Maintaining the system, including fixing bugs, adding new features, and updating data.

Note: The railway reservation system is a complex and challenging project that requires a deep understanding of database management and application development. This unit covers the key concepts and steps involved in designing, developing, deploying, and maintaining a railway reservation system, including requirements gathering, design, development, deployment, and maintenance.
