In this unit, we will be discussing the mini project on Design and Development of Data and Application. - The project will involve the use of programming languages, database management systems, and web development tools. You will need to have a good understanding of these technologies in order to complete the project successfully.

- This mini project will provide you with valuable hands-on experience in designing and developing data and application systems. It will also help you to develop your problem-solving skills and enhance your understanding of programming and web development.

In conclusion, the mini project on Design and Development of Data and Application is an important component of this course. It will allow you to apply the skills and knowledge you have gained throughout the course to a real-world project, and provide you with valuable experience in designing and developing data and application systems.
