Unit 5 - Creating Cursors:
- Overview of Cursors: A database object used to retrieve and manipulate data row by row.
- Types of Cursors: Static, Dynamic, Forward-only, Keyset-driven
- Creating a Cursor:
  1. DECLARE CURSOR: Statement used to define a cursor.
  2. OPEN CURSOR: Statement used to open the cursor and retrieve data.
  3. FETCH: Statement used to retrieve data from the cursor one row at a time.
  4. CLOSE CURSOR: Statement used to close the cursor.
  5. DEALLOCATE CURSOR: Statement used to deallocate the cursor and release resources.

Note: Cursors are a powerful tool for retrieving and manipulating data in a database. This unit covers the key concepts and steps involved in creating a cursor, including declaring a cursor, opening the cursor, fetching data, closing the cursor, and deallocating the cursor. The unit will also cover the different types of cursors and their use cases.
