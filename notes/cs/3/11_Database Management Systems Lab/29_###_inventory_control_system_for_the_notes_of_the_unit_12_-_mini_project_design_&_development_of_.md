### Inventory Control System for the notes of the Unit 12 - Mini project (Design & Development of Data and Application ) for following in the subject of Database Management Systems Lab

An Inventory Control System is a software application that helps businesses manage their inventory and stock levels. The following are some of the key concepts related to Inventory Control Systems:

1. Stock Management: Inventory Control Systems help businesses manage their stock levels, including tracking the movement of goods in and out of the warehouse.

2. Order Management: Inventory Control Systems help businesses manage their orders, including tracking the status of orders and generating reports on order history.

3. Barcode Scanning: Inventory Control Systems often use barcode scanning to quickly and accurately track the movement of goods in and out of the warehouse.

4. Real-time Inventory Tracking: Inventory Control Systems provide real-time inventory tracking, allowing businesses to see their current stock levels at any time.

5. Reporting: Inventory Control Systems provide reporting capabilities, allowing businesses to generate reports on inventory levels, order history, and other key metrics.

6. Integration: Inventory Control Systems can be integrated with other systems, such as accounting systems, to provide a comprehensive view of a business's operations.

In this unit, you will learn about Inventory Control Systems, and you will learn how they are used to manage inventory and stock levels. You will also learn about the key concepts related to Inventory Control Systems, including stock management, order management, barcode scanning, real-time inventory tracking, reporting, and integration.

This unit will provide a foundation for understanding the principles and practices of Inventory Control Systems, and for exploring the various concepts and techniques used in the field of Database Management Systems Lab.
