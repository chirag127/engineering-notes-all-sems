Unit 11 - Automatic Backup and Recovery of Files:
- Overview: The importance of backing up files and maintaining data integrity.
- Steps for Automatic Backup:
  1. Determine the files to be backed up.
  2. Choose a backup method: Full backup, incremental backup, or differential backup.
  3. Schedule the backup: Automate the backup process to occur at regular intervals.
  4. Store the backup: Store the backup in a secure location, such as an external hard drive or cloud storage.
- Steps for Recovery:
  1. Determine the files to be recovered.
  2. Choose a recovery method: Restore from backup or use a recovery tool.
  3. Execute the recovery: Restore the files from the backup or use the recovery tool.
  4. Verify the recovery: Verify that the recovery was successful and that the files are accessible.

Note: Automatic backup and recovery of files is a critical component of database management. This unit covers the key concepts and steps involved in backing up and recovering files, including determining the files to be backed up or recovered, choosing a backup or recovery method, scheduling the backup, storing the backup, executing the recovery, and verifying the recovery.
