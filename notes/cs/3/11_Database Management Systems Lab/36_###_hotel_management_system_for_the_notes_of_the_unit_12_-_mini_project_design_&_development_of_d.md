### Hotel Management System for the notes of the Unit 12 - Mini project (Design & Development of Data and Application ) for following in the subject of Database Management Systems Lab

Unit 12 - Mini Project: Design & Development of Hotel Management System:
- Overview: A project that involves designing and developing a database and application system for managing a hotel.
- Key components:
  1. Guest information: Storing information about guests, including name, address, contact information, and reservation details.
  2. Room information: Storing information about rooms, including room type, availability, and rate.
  3. Reservation system: Allowing guests to make and manage reservations.
  4. Billing system: Generating and managing bills for guests, including room charges, taxes, and other fees.
- Steps for completing the mini project:
  1. Define the project requirements and scope.
  2. Design the data model and database schema.
  3. Develop the application and implement the data model.
  4. Test the application and data model to ensure they meet the requirements.
  5. Refine the design and implementation as needed.
  6. Deploy the hotel management system in a production environment.

Note: The mini project is an opportunity to apply the concepts and skills learned in the course to a real-world scenario. This unit covers the key steps involved in designing and developing a hotel management system, including defining the requirements and scope, designing the data model and database schema, developing the application and data model, testing and refining the design, and deploying the hotel management system in a production environment.
