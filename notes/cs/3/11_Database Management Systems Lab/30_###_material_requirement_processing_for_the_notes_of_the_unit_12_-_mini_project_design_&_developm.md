### Material Requirement Processing for the notes of the Unit 12 - Mini project (Design & Development of Data and Application ) for following in the subject of Database Management Systems Lab

Unit 12 - Mini Project (Design & Development of Data and Application):
- Material Requirement Processing:
  1. Overview: Material Requirement Processing (MRP) is a system used to manage the procurement of materials needed for production.
  2. Steps:
    a. Determine material requirements: Analyze the production plan to determine the materials required for production.
    b. Check inventory levels: Check the current inventory levels of the materials required for production.
    c. Generate purchase orders: Generate purchase orders for materials that are not in inventory.
    d. Track material deliveries: Track the delivery of materials to ensure that they are received in a timely manner.
    e. Update inventory levels: Update the inventory levels of materials to reflect the receipt of new materials.

Note: Material Requirement Processing (MRP) is a critical component of production management, as it helps to ensure that the necessary materials are available for production. This unit covers the key concepts and steps involved in MRP, including determining material requirements, checking inventory levels, generating purchase orders, tracking material deliveries, and updating inventory levels.
