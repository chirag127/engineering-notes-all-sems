### Hospital Management System for the notes of the Unit 12 - Mini project (Design & Development of Data and Application ) for following in the subject of Database Management Systems Lab

Unit 12 - Mini Project (Design & Development of Data and Application):
- Hospital Management System:
  1. Overview: A system for managing patient data, appointments, billing, and other aspects of hospital operations.
  2. Requirements: Collection of functional and non-functional requirements for the system, such as data storage, security, and user interface.
  3. Data Modeling: Creation of an Entity-Relationship diagram to represent the data and relationships in the system.
  4. Database Design: Design of the database schema, including tables, columns, keys, and relationships.
  5. Application Development: Development of the user interface and application logic, including forms, reports, and procedures.
  6. Testing and Deployment: Testing of the system to ensure it meets requirements, followed by deployment in a production environment.

Note: A Hospital Management System is a critical component of modern healthcare, as it enables the efficient and effective management of patient data and hospital operations. This unit covers the key concepts and steps involved in designing and developing a Hospital Management System, including requirements gathering, data modeling, database design, application development, testing, and deployment.
