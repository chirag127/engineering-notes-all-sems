### Weiler and Atherton polygon clipping for the notes of the Unit 2 - Transformations in the subject of Computer Graphics

Unit 2 of the Computer Graphics course focuses on Transformations, including the aspect of Weiler-Atherton Polygon Clipping.

Weiler-Atherton Polygon Clipping is a computer graphics algorithm used to clip a polygon against a specified clipping window. The algorithm works by dividing the polygon into smaller pieces, and then determining which pieces lie inside the clipping window and which pieces lie outside. The algorithm then discards the pieces that lie outside the clipping window, and returns the pieces that lie inside.

The Weiler-Atherton Polygon Clipping algorithm is a general-purpose polygon clipping algorithm that can handle complex polyggon shapes and clipping windows. It is widely used in computer graphics, computer-aided design (CAD) programs, and video games.
