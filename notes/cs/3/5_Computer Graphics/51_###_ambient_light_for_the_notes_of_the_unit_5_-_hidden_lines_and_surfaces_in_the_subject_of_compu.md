### Ambient light for the notes of the Unit 5 - Hidden Lines and Surfaces in the subject of Computer Graphics

Ambient light is a type of lighting used in computer graphics to simulate the general illumination in a scene. Ambient light is a uniform, low-level light that illuminates all objects in a scene equally, regardless of their position or orientation.

The following are some of the key concepts related to ambient light in computer graphics:

1. Ambient Light Color: The ambient light color is the color of the ambient light in a scene. This color can be specified as an RGB value, or as a color name.

2. Ambient Light Intensity: The ambient light intensity is the brightness of the ambient light in a scene. The ambient light intensity can be specified as a value between 0 and 1.

3. Ambient Light Model: The ambient light model is a mathematical model used to simulate the ambient light in a scene. The ambient light model takes into account the ambient light color and intensity, as well as the material properties of the objects in the scene.

4. Ambient Light and Shading: Ambient light is often used in conjunction with other lighting models, such as diffuse shading and specular shading, to create a more realistic representation of a scene.

You will also learn about the advantages and disadvantages of ambient light, and you will learn how to choose the appropriate lighting model for a specific task.
