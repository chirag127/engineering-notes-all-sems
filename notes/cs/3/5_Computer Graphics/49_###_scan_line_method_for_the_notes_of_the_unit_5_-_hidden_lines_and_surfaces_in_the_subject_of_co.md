### Scan line method for the notes of the Unit 5 - Hidden Lines and Surfaces in the subject of Computer Graphics

Unit 5 of the Computer Graphics course focuses on Hidden Lines and Surfaces, including the aspect of the Scan Line Method.

The Scan Line Method is a computer graphics algorithm used to determine which parts of a 3D scene are visible and which parts are hidden. The algorithm works by scanning the scene from top to bottom and from left to right, and determining which parts of the scene are visible at each scan line.

The Scan Line Method is a fast and efficient algorithm that is well-suited for use in real-time graphics applications, such as video games and interactive simulations. The algorithm is also commonly used in computer-aided design (CAD) programs, where it is used to determine the visibility of objects in a 3D scene.

In the context of the Computer Graphics course, students in Unit 5 will learn about the Scan Line Method as part of the discussion on Hidden Lines and Surfaces. This may include working with examples of the Scan Line Method, analyzing the benefits and limitations of the algorithm, and learning how to implement the Scan Line Method in a computer graphics program.
