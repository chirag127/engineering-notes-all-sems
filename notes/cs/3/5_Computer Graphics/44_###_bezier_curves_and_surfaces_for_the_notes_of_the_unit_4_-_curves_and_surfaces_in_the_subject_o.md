### Bezier curves and surfaces for the notes of the Unit 4 - Curves and Surfaces in the subject of Computer Graphics

The Bezier curves and surfaces section of the Unit 4 - Curves and Surfaces in the subject of Computer Graphics should cover the following topics:

1. Definition of Bezier curves (smooth curves defined by control points)
2. Overview of Bezier curves (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
3. Bezier curve representation (representing Bezier curves using control points and polynomials)
4. Bezier curve properties (smoothness, degree, endpoints, etc.)
5. Bezier curve generation (generating Bezier curves using control points and polynomials)
6. Definition of Bezier surfaces (smooth surfaces defined by control points)
7. Overview of Bezier surfaces (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
8. Bezier surface representation (representing Bezier surfaces using control points and polynomials)
9. Bezier surface properties (smoothness, degree, endpoints, etc.)
10. Bezier surface generation (generating Bezier surfaces using control points and polynomials)
11. Bezier curves and surfaces in computer graphics (how Bezier curves and surfaces are used in computer graphics to represent objects)
12. Limitations of Bezier curves and surfaces (complexity, accuracy, etc.)
13. Techniques for overcoming limitations (using efficient algorithms, improving accuracy, etc.)
14. Further reading and resources (books, articles, online resources)

Bezier curves and surfaces is an important component of the subject of Computer Graphics. Understanding the principles and techniques of Bezier curves and surfaces helps students to develop the skills required to represent smooth curves and surfaces in computer graphics. The hands-on practice and further reading resources will help students to develop their skills and deepen their understanding of the material.
