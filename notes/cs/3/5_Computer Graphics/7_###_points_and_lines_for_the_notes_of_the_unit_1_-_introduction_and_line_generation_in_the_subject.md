### Points and lines for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction and Line Generation in the subject of Computer Graphics

Computer graphics is the field of study that deals with the representation, manipulation, and display of images and visual information on a computer. Points and lines are basic building blocks of computer graphics, and they are used to create more complex shapes and images.

The following are key concepts related to points and lines in computer graphics:

1. Points: Points are single pixels or dots on a computer screen, and they are used to define the position of an object in two-dimensional (2D) or three-dimensional (3D) space.

2. Lines: Lines are defined by two points, and they are used to connect points and create shapes.

3. Line Generation: Line generation is the process of generating a line between two points on a computer screen.

4. Line Drawing Algorithms: Line drawing algorithms are mathematical algorithms used to generate lines on a computer screen, including the Bresenham's line algorithm and the DDA (Digital Differential Analyzer) algorithm.

In this unit, you will learn about the basic concepts of points and lines in computer graphics, and you will learn about the different line generation algorithms used to generate lines on a computer screen. You will also learn about the importance of points and lines in computer graphics, and you will learn how to use points and lines to create more complex shapes and images.

This unit will provide a foundation for understanding the principles and practices of computer graphics, and for exploring the various concepts and techniques used in the field of computer graphics.
