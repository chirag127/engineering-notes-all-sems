### Sutherland Hodgeman polygon clipping for the notes of the Unit 2 - Transformations in the subject of Computer Graphics

The Sutherland-Hodgman polygon clipping section of the Unit 2 - Transformations in the subject of Computer Graphics should cover the following topics:

1. Definition of polygon clipping (the process of removing parts of a polygon that lie outside of a specified region)
2. Overview of polygon clipping (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
3. Overview of Sutherland-Hodgman algorithm (a popular polygon clipping algorithm)
4. Steps of Sutherland-Hodgman algorithm (how the algorithm works, step by step)
5. Clipping against a single edge (how to clip a polygon against a single edge)
6. Clipping against multiple edges (how to clip a polygon against multiple edges)
7. Input and output polyggon representation (representing polyggon vertices as points or vectors)
8. Handling degenerate cases (clipping polyggon with vertices on the clipping edge)
9. Limitations of Sutherland-Hodgman algorithm (complexity, performance, accuracy)
10. Techniques for overcoming limitations (using efficient algorithms, reducing the number of vertices, etc.)
11. Applications of polygon clipping (in fields such as computer graphics, engineering, and physics)
12. Further reading and resources (books, articles, online resources)

The Sutherland-Hodgman polygon clipping is an important component of the subject of Computer Graphics.
