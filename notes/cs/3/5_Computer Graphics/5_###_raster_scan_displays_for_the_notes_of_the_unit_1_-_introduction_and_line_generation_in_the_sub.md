### Raster scan displays for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction and Line Generation in the subject of Computer Graphics

The Raster scan displays section of the Unit 1 - Introduction and Line Generation in the subject of Computer Graphics should cover the following topics:

1. Definition of Raster scan displays (a display technology that uses a rectangular array of pixels to represent an image)
2. Overview of Raster scan displays (how they work, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
3. Pixel (the smallest unit of an image in a raster scan display)
4. Resolution (the number of pixels in a raster scan display)
5. Bit depth (the number of bits used to represent the color of a pixel)
6. Color depth (the number of colors that can be represented by a raster scan display)
7. Refresh rate (the number of times per second the image is redrawn on the screen)
8. Raster scan display devices (CRT, LCD, OLED, etc.)
9. Overview of CRT (Cathode Ray Tube, a type of raster scan display)
10. Overview of LCD (Liquid Crystal Display, a type of raster scan display)
11. Overview of OLED (Organic Light Emitting Diode, a type of raster scan display)
12. Raster scan display and computer graphics (how raster scan displays are used to display images generated by computer graphics)
13. Limitations of raster scan displays (resolution, color depth, refresh rate, etc.)
14. Techniques for overcoming limitations (choosing a high-resolution display, increasing color depth, etc.)
15. Further reading and resources (books, articles, online resources)

Raster scan displays is an important component of the subject of Computer Graphics. Understanding the principles and techniques of raster scan displays helps students to develop the skills required to display images generated by computer graphics. The hands-on practice and further reading resources will help students to develop their skills and deepen their understanding of the material.
