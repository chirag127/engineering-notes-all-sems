4. Blending functions (functions used to control the shape of Blobby objects)
5. Overview of blending functions (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
6. Constructive Solid Geometry (CSG) (a method of combining simple shapes to create complex objects)
7. Overview of Constructive Solid Geometry (CSG) (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
8. Blobby objects and Constructive Solid Geometry (CSG) (how Blobby objects can be represented using Constructive Solid Geometry (CSG))

10. Limitations of Blobby objects (complexity, performance, accuracy)
11. Techniques for overcoming limitations (using efficient algorithms, reducing the number of spheres, etc.)
12. Further reading and resources (books, articles, online resources)

Blobby objects is an important component of the subject of Computer Graphics.
