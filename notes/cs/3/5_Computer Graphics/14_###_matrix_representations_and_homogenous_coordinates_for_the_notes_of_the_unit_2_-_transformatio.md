### Matrix representations and homogenous coordinates for the notes of the Unit 2 - Transformations in the subject of Computer Graphics

The Matrix representations and homogenous coordinates section of the Unit 2 - Transformations in the subject of Computer Graphics should cover the following topics:

1. Definition of matrix representation (a mathematical representation of a linear transformation using a matrix)
2. Overview of matrix representation (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
3. Matrix multiplication (the process of multiplying two matrices to produce a new matrix)
4. Overview of matrix multiplication (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
5. Matrix inverse (the inverse of a matrix, which when multiplied by the original matrix results in the identity matrix)
6. Overview of matrix inverse (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
7. Definition of homogeneous coordinates (a system of coordinates used to represent points and vectors in computer graphics)
8. Overview of homogeneous coordinates (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
9. Homogeneous coordinates and matrix representation (how homogeneous coordinates can be represented using matrices)
10. Homogeneous coordinates and transformations (how transformations can be represented using homogeneous coordinates and matrices)
11. Translations (the movement of points and objects in a given direction)
12. Rotations (the rotation of points and objects around a given axis)
13. Scaling (the changing of the size of points and objects)
14. Shearing (the deformation of points and objects along a given axis)
15. Further reading and resources (books, articles, online resources)

Matrix representations and homogenous coordinates are important components of the subject of Computer Graphics. Understanding these concepts helps students to develop the skills required to represent and manipulate points and objects in computer graphics. The hands-on practice and further reading resources will help students to develop their skills and deepen their understanding of the material.
