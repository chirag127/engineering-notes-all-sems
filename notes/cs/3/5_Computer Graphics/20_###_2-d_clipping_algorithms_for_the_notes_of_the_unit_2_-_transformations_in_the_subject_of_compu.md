### 2-D Clipping algorithms for the notes of the Unit 2 - Transformations in the subject of Computer Graphics

The 2-D Clipping algorithms section of the Unit 2 - Transformations in the subject of Computer Graphics should cover the following topics:

1. Definition of 2-D Clipping (the process of removing parts of a 2-D object that are outside a specified region)
2. Overview of 2-D Clipping (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
3. Types of 2-D Clipping algorithms (Cohen-Sutherland, Liang-Barsky, etc.)
4. Overview of Cohen-Sutherland algorithm (a simple 2-D clipping algorithm)
5. Overview of Liang-Barsky algorithm (an efficient 2-D clipping algorithm)
6. Clipping window (the region in which a 2-D object is displayed)
7. Clipping boundary (the boundary of the clipping window)
8. Clipping codes (representing the position of a point relative to the clipping boundary)
9. Clipping algorithms and computer graphics (using clipping algorithms to display 2-D objects within a specified region)
10. Limitations of 2-D Clipping algorithms (complexity, performance, etc.)
11. Techniques for overcoming limitations (choosing an efficient algorithm, optimizing performance, etc.)
12. Further reading and resources (books, articles, online resources)

2-D Clipping algorithms is an important component of the subject of Computer Graphics. Understanding the principles and techniques of 2-D Clipping algorithms helps students to develop the skills required to display 2-D objects within a specified region. The hands-on practice and further reading resources will help students to develop their skills and deepen their understanding of the material.
