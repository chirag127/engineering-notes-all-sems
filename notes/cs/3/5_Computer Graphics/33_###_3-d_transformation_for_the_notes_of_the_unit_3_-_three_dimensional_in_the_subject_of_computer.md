### 3-D Transformation for the notes of the Unit 3 - Three Dimensional in the subject of Computer Graphics

The 3-D Transformations section of the Unit 3 - Three Dimensional in the subject of Computer Graphics should cover the following topics:

1. Definition of 3-D Transformations (mathematical operations applied to objects in 3-D space)
2. Overview of 3-D Transformations (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
3. Types of 3-D Transformations (translation, rotation, scaling, shearing, reflection)
4. Overview of 3-D Translation (moving an object to a new position in 3-D space)
5. Overview of 3-D Rotation (rotating an object about a point in 3-D space)
6. Overview of 3-D Scaling (resizing an object in 3-D space)
7. Overview of 3-D Shearing (distorting an object in 3-D space)
8. Overview of 3-D Reflection (flipping an object over a plane in 3-D space)
9. 3-D Transformation matrices (mathematical representations of 3-D transformations)
10. Composing 3-D Transformations (combining multiple 3-D transformations into a single matrix)
11. 3-D Transformation order (the order in which transformations are applied to an object in 3-D space)
12. Inverse 3-D Transformations (reversing the effect of a 3-D transformation)
13. Limitations of 3-D Transformations (accuracy, computational complexity)
14. Techniques for overcoming limitations (using efficient algorithms, reducing the number of transformations)
15. Further reading and resources (books, articles, online resources)

3-D Transformations is an important component of the subject of Computer Graphics. Understanding the principles and techniques of 3-D Transformations helps students to develop the skills required to manipulate objects in 3-D space. The hands-on practice and further reading resources will help students to develop their skills and deepen their understanding of the material.
