## Unit 4 - Curves and Surfaces

The Unit 4 - Curves and Surfaces in the subject of Computer Graphics should cover the following topics:

1. Definition of curves (mathematical representations of smooth lines)
2. Overview of curves (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
3. Types of curves (Bezier, B-spline, NURBS, etc.)
4. Overview of Bezier curves (a type of curve defined by control points)
5. Overview of B-spline curves (a type of curve defined by control points and knots)
6. Overview of NURBS curves (a type of curve defined by control points and weights)
7. Definition of surfaces (mathematical representations of smooth, two-dimensional shapes)
8. Overview of surfaces (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
9. Types of surfaces (Bezier, B-spline, NURBS, etc.)
10. Overview of Bezier surfaces (a type of surface defined by control points)
11. Overview of B-spline surfaces (a type of surface defined by control points and knots)
12. Overview of NURBS surfaces (a type of surface defined by control points and weights)
13. Surface rendering (the process of displaying surfaces on a computer screen)
14. Overview of surface rendering (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
15. Surface shading (the process of adding shading and texture to surfaces)
16. Overview of surface shading (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
17. Limitations of curves and surfaces (complexity, performance, accuracy)
18. Techniques for overcoming limitations (using efficient algorithms, reducing complexity, etc.)
19. Further reading and resources (books, articles, online resources)

Curves and Surfaces is an important component of the subject of Computer Graphics. Understanding the principles and techniques of curves and surfaces helps students to develop the skills required to represent and display smooth, two-dimensional shapes on a computer screen. The hands-on practice and further reading resources will help students to develop their skills and deepen their understanding of the material.
