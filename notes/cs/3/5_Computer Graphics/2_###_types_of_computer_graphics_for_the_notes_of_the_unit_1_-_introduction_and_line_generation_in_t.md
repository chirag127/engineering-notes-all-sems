### Types of computer graphics for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction and Line Generation in the subject of Computer Graphics

Unit 1 of the Computer Graphics course focuses on Introduction and Line Generation, including the aspect of Types of Computer Graphics.

Computer graphics is the study of digital image creation, manipulation, and display. It involves the use of algorithms and software to create, edit, and render digital images and animations.

There are several types of computer graphics, including:
- Raster graphics, which are also known as bitmap graphics, and are made up of pixels arranged in a grid. Raster graphics are commonly used for digital images and web graphics, as they can be easily edited and resized.
- Vector graphics, which are made up of mathematical equations and geometric shapes, and are resolution-independent. Vector graphics are commonly used for logos, illustrations, and technical drawings, as they can be easily scaled and edited without losing quality.
- 3D graphics, which are used to create 3D models, animations, and simulations. 3D graphics are commonly used in fields such as gaming, film, and product design.
- Interactive graphics, which are used to create interactive visual experiences, such as games, simulations, and virtual reality applications.

In the context of the Computer Graphics course, students in Unit 1 will learn about Types of Computer Graphics as part of the discussion on Introduction and Line Generation. This may include working with examples of each type of computer graphics, analyzing the benefits and limitations of each type of computer graphics, and learning how to choose the appropriate type of computer graphics for a particular project.
