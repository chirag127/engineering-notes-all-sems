### Random scan displays for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction and Line Generation in the subject of Computer Graphics

The Random scan displays section of the Unit 1 - Introduction and Line Generation in the subject of Computer Graphics should cover the following topics:

1. Definition of random scan displays (a type of display that uses a cathode ray tube to draw images)
2. Overview of random scan displays (how they work, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
3. Line generation in random scan displays (how lines are drawn on the screen)
4. Line drawing algorithms (DDA, Bresenham's, etc.)
5. Overview of DDA (digital differential analyzer) algorithm (a simple line drawing algorithm)
6. Overview of Bresenham's algorithm (a more efficient line drawing algorithm)
7. Character generation in random scan displays (how characters are drawn on the screen)
8. Character generation algorithms (bitmap, stroke, etc.)
9. Overview of bitmap character generation (storing characters as bitmaps)
10. Overview of stroke character generation (defining characters as a series of lines)
11. Limitations of random scan displays (resolution, color depth, refresh rate, etc.)
12. Techniques for overcoming limitations (interlacing, anti-aliasing, etc.)
13. Further reading and resources (books, articles, online resources)

Random scan displays is an important component of the subject of Computer Graphics. Understanding the principles and techniques of random scan displays helps students to develop the skills required to create and display images and text on a screen. The hands-on practice and further reading resources will help students to develop their skills and deepen their understanding of the material.
