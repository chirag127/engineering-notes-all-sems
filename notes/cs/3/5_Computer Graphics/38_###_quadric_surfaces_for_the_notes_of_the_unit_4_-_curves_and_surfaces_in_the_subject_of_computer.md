### Quadric surfaces for the notes of the Unit 4 - Curves and Surfaces in the subject of Computer Graphics

Unit 4 of the Computer Graphics course focuses on Curves and Surfaces, including the aspect of Quadric Surfaces.

Quadric Surfaces are a family of 3D surfaces that can be defined by a second-degree polynomial equation. These surfaces include common shapes such as spheres, cylinders, cones, and hyperboloids, and are widely used in computer graphics, computer-aided design (CAD) programs, and video games.

Quadric Surfaces are defined by a matrix representation, which consists of a set of coefficients that describe the shape of the surface. These coefficients can be used to calculate the position and normal vector of a point on the surface, and to determine the intersection of a ray with the surface.

In the context of the Computer Graphics course, students in Unit 4 will learn about Quadric Surfaces as part of the discussion on Curves and Surfaces. This may include working with examples of Quadric Surfaces, analyzing the benefits and limitations of Quadric Surfaces, and learning how to implement Quadric Surfaces in a computer graphics program.
