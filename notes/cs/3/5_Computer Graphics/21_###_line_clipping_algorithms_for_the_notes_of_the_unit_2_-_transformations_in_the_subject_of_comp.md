### Line clipping algorithms for the notes of the Unit 2 - Transformations in the subject of Computer Graphics

Line clipping algorithms are mathematical procedures used to determine which parts of a line are visible and which parts are outside of a specified viewing area. In computer graphics, line clipping is used to optimize the display of lines by removing the parts of the line that are outside of the viewing area, and only displaying the parts of the line that are visible.

There are several algorithms for line clipping, including:

1. Cohen-Sutherland Algorithm: A widely used algorithm that uses a four-part code to determine which parts of a line are inside or outside of a specified viewing area.

2. Liang-Barsky Algorithm: An algorithm that uses a parametric approach to determine which parts of a line are inside or outside of a specified viewing area.

3. Cyrus-Beck Algorithm: An algorithm that uses a geometric approach to determine which parts of a line are inside or outside of a specified viewing area.
