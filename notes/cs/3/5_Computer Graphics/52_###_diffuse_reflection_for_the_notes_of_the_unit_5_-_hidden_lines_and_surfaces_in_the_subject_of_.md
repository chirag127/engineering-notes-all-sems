### Diffuse reflection for the notes of the Unit 5 - Hidden Lines and Surfaces in the subject of Computer Graphics

Unit 5 of the Computer Graphics course focuses on Hidden Lines and Surfaces and includes the aspect of Diffuse Reflection.

Diffuse Reflection is a type of light reflection that occurs when light is scattered evenly in all directions from a surface. Diffuse reflection is responsible for the soft, even illumination of objects that are not highly reflective.

In computer graphics, diffuse reflection is used to simulate the appearance of objects in a 3D scene. This can be accomplished through the use of shading algorithms, such as Phong Shading, that take into account the position of the light source, the surface normal, and the material properties of the object to calculate the diffuse reflection of the light.

In the context of the Computer Graphics course, students in Unit 5 will learn about Diffuse Reflection as part of the discussion on Hidden Lines and Surfaces. This may include working with examples of diffuse reflection, analyzing the benefits and limitations of diffuse reflection, and learning how to implement diffuse reflection in a computer graphics program.
