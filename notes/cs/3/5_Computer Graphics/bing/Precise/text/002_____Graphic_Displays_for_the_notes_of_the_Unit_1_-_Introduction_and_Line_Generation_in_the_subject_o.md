### Graphic Displays

1. Graphic displays are devices that allow a computer to output visual information to a screen or other display medium.
2. There are several types of graphic displays, including CRT (Cathode Ray Tube), LCD (Liquid Crystal Display), LED (Light Emitting Diode), OLED (Organic Light Emitting Diode), and Plasma displays.
3. Each type of display has its own advantages and disadvantages, and the choice of display depends on factors such as cost, power consumption, image quality, and durability.
4. CRT displays were the most common type of display for many years, but have largely been replaced by LCD and LED displays due to their lower power consumption and smaller size.
5. LCD displays use liquid crystals to control the passage of light through the display, while LED displays use light emitting diodes to produce light.
6. OLED displays use organic compounds to produce light, and offer high contrast ratios and fast response times.
7. Plasma displays use ionized gas to produce light, and are known for their high contrast ratios and wide viewing angles.
8. In computer graphics, the display is an important component as it is the primary means of presenting visual information to the user.
