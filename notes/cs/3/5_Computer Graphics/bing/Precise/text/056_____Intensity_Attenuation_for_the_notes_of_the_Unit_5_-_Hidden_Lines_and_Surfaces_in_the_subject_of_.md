### Intensity Attenuation

Intensity attenuation is the decrease in the intensity of light as it travels through a medium. In computer graphics, this concept is used to simulate the effect of light traveling through the air or other transparent materials.

1. The intensity of light decreases as it travels further from the light source.
2. The rate of attenuation depends on the properties of the medium through which the light is traveling.
3. In computer graphics, intensity attenuation is often modeled using an attenuation function, which specifies how the intensity of light decreases with distance.
4. Common attenuation functions include linear, exponential, and quadratic functions.
5. Intensity attenuation is an important concept in the rendering of realistic images, as it helps to create the illusion of depth and distance in a scene.
