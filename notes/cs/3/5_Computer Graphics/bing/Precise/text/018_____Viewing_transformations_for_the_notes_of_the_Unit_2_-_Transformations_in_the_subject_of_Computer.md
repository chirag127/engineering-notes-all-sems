### Viewing Transformations

Viewing transformations are used in computer graphics to manipulate the view of a scene. They are an essential part of the rendering pipeline and are used to transform the coordinates of objects from world space to screen space.

1. **World Space**: World space is the coordinate system in which the objects in the scene are defined. It is a 3D space that represents the real world.

2. **Viewing Transformation**: The viewing transformation is used to transform the coordinates of objects from world space to view space. It is also known as the camera transformation, as it defines the position and orientation of the camera in the scene.

3. **Projection Transformation**: The projection transformation is used to project the 3D view space onto a 2D plane, known as the screen space. This transformation is responsible for creating the illusion of depth in the final image.

4. **Viewport Transformation**: The viewport transformation is used to map the coordinates of the screen space to the actual pixel coordinates of the display device. This transformation is necessary to ensure that the final image is displayed correctly on the screen.

In summary, viewing transformations are used to manipulate the view of a scene in computer graphics. They are an essential part of the rendering pipeline and are used to transform the coordinates of objects from world space to screen space. The viewing transformation, projection transformation, and viewport transformation are the three main types of viewing transformations. They are used to define the position and orientation of the camera, project the 3D view space onto a 2D plane, and map the coordinates of the screen space to the actual pixel coordinates of the display device, respectively.
