### Introductory concepts of Spline for the notes of the Unit 4 - Curves and Surfaces in the subject of Computer Graphics

- A spline is a piecewise-defined polynomial function that is used to represent smooth curves.
- Splines are commonly used in computer graphics to create smooth curves and surfaces.
- The term "spline" originally referred to a flexible strip of wood or metal that was used to draw smooth curves by hand.
- In computer graphics, a spline is defined by a set of control points and a set of basis functions.
- The most commonly used splines in computer graphics are B-spline and Bezier curves.
- B-spline curves are defined by a set of control points and a set of basis functions, which are determined by the degree of the curve and the knot vector.
- Bezier curves are a special case of B-spline curves, where the knot vector is chosen such that the curve passes through the first and last control points.
- Splines can be used to create smooth curves and surfaces by combining multiple curve segments.
- The smoothness of the resulting curve or surface is determined by the degree of the spline and the continuity conditions at the control points.
- Splines are widely used in computer graphics for modeling, animation, and rendering. They are also used in other fields such as engineering, architecture, and industrial design.
