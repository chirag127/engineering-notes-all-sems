### 3-D Geometric Primitives

In the subject of Computer Graphics, Unit 3 - Three Dimensional, one of the topics is 3-D Geometric Primitives. Here are some key points to note:

1. 3-D geometric primitives are the basic building blocks used to create 3D models and scenes in computer graphics.
2. Common 3-D geometric primitives include points, lines, polygons, and polyhedra.
3. Points are represented by a set of 3D coordinates (x, y, z) and are used to define the vertices of other primitives.
4. Lines are defined by two points and can be used to create wireframe models.
5. Polygons are flat, 2D shapes defined by a series of points connected by lines. They can be used to create more complex 3D models by defining the faces of polyhedra.
6. Polyhedra are 3D shapes defined by a series of polygons connected at their edges. They can be used to create solid models of objects.
7. These primitives can be combined and manipulated using various modeling techniques to create complex 3D models and scenes.
