### Diffuse Reflection

Diffuse reflection is a type of reflection that occurs when light hits a rough or matte surface. The light rays are scattered in many different directions, rather than being reflected in a single direction like in the case of specular reflection. This type of reflection is commonly observed in everyday life, for example, when light is reflected off a piece of paper or a wall.

Some key points to remember about diffuse reflection are:

- Diffuse reflection occurs when light hits a rough or matte surface.
- The light rays are scattered in many different directions.
- Diffuse reflection is commonly observed in everyday life.
- Diffuse reflection is different from specular reflection, where light is reflected in a single direction.
