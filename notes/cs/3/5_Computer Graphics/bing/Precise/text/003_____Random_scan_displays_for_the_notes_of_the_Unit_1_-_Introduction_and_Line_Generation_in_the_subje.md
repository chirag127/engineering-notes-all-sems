### Random Scan Displays

- Random scan displays, also known as vector displays, are a type of computer graphics display system.
- They are used to draw lines and shapes on the screen by directing the electron beam of the CRT (Cathode Ray Tube) to the desired locations.
- The beam is deflected to the desired end points of the line and the intensity is turned on to draw the line.
- The beam is turned off while moving to the next location to prevent unwanted lines from being drawn.
- Random scan displays are well suited for line drawings and CAD (Computer Aided Design) applications.
- They are not well suited for displaying raster graphics, such as photographs or video, as the beam must be directed to each pixel individually, which can be time-consuming.
- Random scan displays have largely been replaced by raster scan displays, which are better suited for displaying a wider range of graphics.
