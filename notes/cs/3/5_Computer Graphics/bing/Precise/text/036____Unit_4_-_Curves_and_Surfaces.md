## Unit 4 - Curves and Surfaces

1. **Curves** are one-dimensional objects that can be represented mathematically using parametric equations.
2. **Surfaces** are two-dimensional objects that can be represented mathematically using parametric equations or implicit equations.
3. **Parametric equations** describe the position of a point on a curve or surface as a function of one or more parameters.
4. **Implicit equations** describe the relationship between the coordinates of a point on a surface.
5. **Bezier curves** are a type of parametric curve commonly used in computer graphics and animation.
6. **NURBS** (Non-Uniform Rational B-Splines) are a type of curve or surface representation that can represent a wide range of shapes, including conic sections and freeform curves.
7. **Subdivision surfaces** are a type of surface representation that can be used to model smooth, organic shapes.
8. **Interpolation** is the process of constructing a curve or surface that passes through a given set of points.
9. **Approximation** is the process of constructing a curve or surface that is close to a given set of points, but does not necessarily pass through them.
