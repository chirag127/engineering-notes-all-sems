## Unit 3 - Three Dimensional

Unit 3 - Three Dimensional is a unit in a course on computer graphics, and it covers the basics of three-dimensional (3D) graphics and 3D transformations.

In this unit, you will learn about the basics of 3D graphics, including the different types of 3D graphics, the different types of 3D displays, and the different types of 3D graphics software. You will also learn about 3D transformations, including translation, rotation, and scaling, among others.

The following are some of the key topics covered in Unit 3 - Three Dimensional:

1. Overview of 3D Graphics: An introduction to the basics of 3D graphics, including its history, applications, and fundamental concepts.

2. Types of 3D Graphics: An overview of the different types of 3D graphics, including wireframe models, solid models, and surface models.

3. Types of 3D Displays: An overview of the different types of 3D displays, including stereoscopic displays, autostereoscopic displays, and holographic displays.

4. Types of 3D Graphics Software: An overview of the different types of 3D graphics software, including 3D modeling software, 3D animation software, and 3D rendering software.

5. 3D Transformations: An overview of the basics of 3D transformations, including translation, rotation, and scaling, among others.

In this unit, you will gain a solid understanding of the basics of 3D graphics and 3D transformations, and you will be prepared to explore more advanced topics in the field of computer graphics in later units.
