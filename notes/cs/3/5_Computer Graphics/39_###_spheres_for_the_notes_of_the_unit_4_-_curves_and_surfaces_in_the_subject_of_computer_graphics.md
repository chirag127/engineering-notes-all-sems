### Spheres for the notes of the Unit 4 - Curves and Surfaces in the subject of Computer Graphics

Unit 4 of the Computer Graphics course focuses on Curves and Surfaces, including the aspect of Spheres.

A sphere is a 3D geometric object that is defined as the set of all points that are equidistant from a given center point. Spheres are commonly used in computer graphics to create 3D models of objects such as balls, planets, and other rounded shapes.

To create a sphere in computer graphics, it is necessary to define a center point and a radius, and then generate a set of points that are equidistant from the center point. There are several algorithms that can be used to generate the points that make up a sphere, including the Midpoint Sphere Algorithm and the Marching Cubes Algorithm.

In the context of the Computer Graphics course, students in Unit 4 will learn about Spheres as part of the discussion on Curves and Surfaces. This may include working with examples of spheres, analyzing the benefits and limitations of spheres, and learning how to create spheres in a computer graphics program.
