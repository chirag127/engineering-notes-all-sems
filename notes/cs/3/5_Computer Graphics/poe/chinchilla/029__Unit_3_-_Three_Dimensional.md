## Unit 3 - Three Dimensional

In this unit, we will be exploring the properties and characteristics of three-dimensional shapes and objects. Here are some key points to keep in mind:

- Three-dimensional objects, also known as 3D objects, have length, width, and height.
- The surface of a 3D object can be described as a collection of 2D shapes, such as squares, rectangles, triangles, and circles.
- The volume of a 3D object is the amount of space it occupies in three dimensions. It is measured in cubic units, such as cubic centimeters or cubic inches.
- Some common 3D shapes include cubes, spheres, cylinders, cones, and pyramids. Each of these shapes has its own unique properties and formulas for calculating its volume and surface area.
- A net is a 2D representation of a 3D object that can be folded into its 3D form. Nets can be useful for visualizing 3D shapes and for calculating their surface area and volume.
- Transformations, such as rotations, translations, and reflections, can be used to manipulate 3D objects and change their orientation in space.
- 3D modeling software, such as AutoCAD, Blender, and SketchUp, can be used to create and manipulate virtual 3D objects on a computer.
- The study of three-dimensional shapes and objects is important in many fields, including architecture, engineering, art, and computer graphics.

By understanding the properties and characteristics of 3D shapes and objects, we can better visualize and manipulate them in our work and personal lives. It is important to have a strong foundation in this topic for success in many different fields.
