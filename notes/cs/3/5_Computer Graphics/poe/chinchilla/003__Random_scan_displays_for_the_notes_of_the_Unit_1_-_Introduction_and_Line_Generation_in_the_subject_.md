### Random Scan Displays for the Notes of Unit 1 - Introduction and Line Generation in Computer Graphics

In computer graphics, random scan displays are used to display images on the screen. These displays are also known as vector displays or stroke displays. They work by drawing lines between specified points on the screen.

Here are some key points to keep in mind when studying random scan displays for Unit 1 - Introduction and Line Generation in Computer Graphics:

- Random scan displays use vectors to draw images on the screen. Vectors are lines that connect two points.
- A vector display can only draw lines, not filled shapes or colors.
- The display is controlled by an electron beam that moves across the screen, drawing lines as it goes.
- The beam is guided by magnetic coils that control its position.
- The image is created by repeatedly drawing lines between specified points to create a complete image.
- These displays were used in early computer systems, but have largely been replaced by raster displays.
- Vector displays are still used in some specialized applications, such as in the aerospace industry.

When studying line generation in computer graphics, it is important to understand how lines are drawn on the screen. Here are some key points to keep in mind:

- Lines in computer graphics are represented by mathematical equations.
- The two most common methods for line generation are Bresenham's algorithm and the midpoint algorithm.
- Bresenham's algorithm is a rasterization algorithm that is used to draw lines on a raster display.
- The midpoint algorithm is a more general algorithm that can be used on both raster and vector displays.
- Both algorithms work by calculating the pixel positions that the line passes through and then coloring those pixels.
- The choice of algorithm depends on the specific needs of the application and the display being used.

Overall, understanding random scan displays and line generation is an important part of learning computer graphics. By mastering these concepts, you will be able to create and manipulate images on the screen with precision and accuracy.
