## Unit 4 - Curves and Surfaces

In this unit, we will be discussing curves and surfaces, which are fundamental concepts in computer graphics and geometry. We will explore their properties, mathematical representations, and various techniques used to manipulate and create them.

### Curves

Curves are one-dimensional geometric objects that can be defined by a set of points or mathematical equations. In computer graphics, curves are used to represent smooth, continuous shapes such as circles, ellipses, and splines. Some of the important types of curves that we will study in this unit are:

- Bezier curves: These are curves defined by a set of control points, which are used to control the shape and curvature of the curve. Bezier curves are commonly used in computer graphics for their simplicity and flexibility.

- B-spline curves: These are curves defined by a set of control points and a knot vector, which specifies the degree and continuity of the curve. B-spline curves are widely used in computer-aided design and manufacturing (CAD/CAM) applications.

- NURBS curves: These are non-uniform rational B-spline curves, which are a mathematical extension of B-spline curves. NURBS curves are commonly used in computer graphics and industrial design for their ability to represent complex shapes with high precision.

### Surfaces

Surfaces are two-dimensional geometric objects that can be defined by a set of points or mathematical equations. In computer graphics, surfaces are used to represent smooth, continuous shapes such as spheres, cylinders, and toruses. Some of the important types of surfaces that we will study in this unit are:

- Bezier surfaces: These are surfaces defined by a set of control points, which are used to control the shape and curvature of the surface. Bezier surfaces are commonly used in computer graphics for their simplicity and flexibility.

- B-spline surfaces: These are surfaces defined by a set of control points and a knot vector, which specifies the degree and continuity of the surface. B-spline surfaces are widely used in CAD/CAM applications.

- NURBS surfaces: These are non-uniform rational B-spline surfaces, which are a mathematical extension of B-spline surfaces. NURBS surfaces are commonly used in computer graphics and industrial design for their ability to represent complex shapes with high precision.

### Techniques for Manipulating Curves and Surfaces

In order to create complex shapes and manipulate curves and surfaces, various techniques are used in computer graphics. Some of the important techniques that we will study in this unit are:

- Deformation: This is the process of changing the shape of a curve or surface by applying transformations such as scaling, rotation, and translation.

- Interpolation: This is the process of constructing a curve or surface that passes through a set of given points.

- Approximation: This is the process of constructing a curve or surface that approximates a given set of points with a certain degree of accuracy.

- Subdivision: This is the process of dividing a curve or surface into smaller segments, which can be used to create more complex shapes and surfaces.

### Conclusion

In this unit, we have discussed the fundamental concepts of curves and surfaces, their mathematical representations, and various techniques used to manipulate and create them. By understanding these concepts and techniques, we can create complex shapes and surfaces in computer graphics with high precision and flexibility.
