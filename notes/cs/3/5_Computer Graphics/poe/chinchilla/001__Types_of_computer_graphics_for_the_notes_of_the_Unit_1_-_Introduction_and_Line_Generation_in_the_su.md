### Types of Computer Graphics

Computer graphics can be broadly classified into two categories: 

1. **Raster graphics:** Raster graphics, also known as bitmap graphics, are formed by pixels or small squares of color. The pixels are arranged in a grid to form an image. Raster graphics are resolution-dependent, which means the quality of the image is dependent on the number of pixels per inch (PPI) or dots per inch (DPI). 

2. **Vector graphics:** Vector graphics are formed by mathematical equations and are resolution-independent. Vector graphics are created using geometric shapes such as lines, curves, and polygons. They are scalable and can be resized without loss of quality. 

Within these two broad categories, there are several types of computer graphics:

- **2D Graphics:** Two-dimensional graphics are used to represent objects and images in a two-dimensional space. 2D graphics can be either raster or vector graphics. Examples of 2D graphics include logos, icons, illustrations, and digital paintings.

- **3D Graphics:** Three-dimensional graphics are used to represent objects and images in a three-dimensional space. 3D graphics are usually created using specialized software and can be either raster or vector graphics. Examples of 3D graphics include video games, animated movies, and architectural renderings.

- **Computer-aided design (CAD):** CAD is a type of computer graphics used in engineering and architectural design. It is used to create detailed 3D models of products, buildings, and other structures.

- **Virtual Reality (VR):** Virtual reality is a type of computer graphics used to create immersive, three-dimensional environments. VR is often used in video games and simulations.

- **Augmented Reality (AR):** Augmented reality is a type of computer graphics used to overlay digital information onto the real world. AR is often used in mobile apps and marketing.

In conclusion, computer graphics is a vast field with many different types of graphics. Understanding the different types of computer graphics is essential for professionals in the field of computer graphics and anyone interested in creating digital art, animations, or designs.
