### Viewing pipeline for the notes of the Unit 2 - Transformations in the subject of Computer Graphics

Unit 2 of the Computer Graphics course focuses on Transformations, including the aspect of Viewing Pipeline.

The Viewing Pipeline is a series of steps that are performed to convert 3D models into 2D images that can be displayed on a screen. The Viewing Pipeline is an essential component of computer graphics, as it determines how 3D models are transformed, projected, and displayed on the screen.

The Viewing Pipeline typically includes the following steps:
- Modeling Transformation, which involves transforming 3D models from their local coordinate system to a common world coordinate system.
- Viewing Transformation, which involves transforming the world coordinate system to a camera coordinate system, to determine the view of the scene from a particular viewpoint.
- Projection Transformation, which involves projecting the 3D models onto a 2D plane, to create a 2D image of the scene.
- Viewport Transformation, which involves transforming the 2D image from the projection plane to the screen, to determine the final display of the image on the screen.

In the context of the Computer Graphics course, students in Unit 2 will learn about the Viewing Pipeline as part of the discussion on Transformations. This may include working with examples of the Viewing Pipeline, analyzing the benefits and limitations of the Viewing Pipeline, and learning how to use the Viewing Pipeline to display 3D models on the screen.

The Viewing Pipeline is a series of steps that are performed to convert 3D models into 2D images that can be displayed on a screen, and includes the Modeling Transformation, Viewing Transformation, Projection Transformation, and Viewport Transformation. Students will learn about the Viewing Pipeline through interactive activities and hands-on experiences, and will learn how to use the Viewing Pipeline to display 3D models on the screen.
