### Curve clipping for the notes of the Unit 2 - Transformations in the subject of Computer Graphics

Curve clipping is the process of removing parts of a curve that are outside a specified region, called the clipping window. In computer graphics, curve clipping is used to display only the parts of a curve that are visible within the bounds of the display screen.

The following are some of the key concepts related to curve clipping:

1. Clipping Window: A clipping window is a rectangular region that defines the bounds of the display screen.

2. Curve Types: Different types of curves, including lines, circles, and Bezier curves, can be clipped.

3. Clipping Algorithms: Several algorithms can be used to perform curve clipping, including the Cohen-Sutherland algorithm and the Liang-Barsky algorithm.

4. Clipping Regions: Different types of clipping regions, including rectangular regions and circular regions, can be used to define the bounds of the display screen.
