### Composite transformations for the notes of the Unit 2 - Transformations in the subject of Computer Graphics

The Composite transformations section of the Unit 2 - Transformations in the subject of Computer Graphics should cover the following topics:

1. Definition of composite transformations (combining multiple transformations into a single transformation)
2. Overview of composite transformations (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
3. Translation and rotation (two common types of transformations)
4. Scaling (resizing an object)
5. Shearing (distorting an object by slanting it)
6. Matrix representation of transformations (representing transformations as matrices)
7. Matrix multiplication (combining multiple matrices into a single matrix)
8. Order of transformations (the order in which transformations are applied affects the result)
9. Matrix representation of composite transformations (representing composite transformations as matrices)
10. Applying composite transformations to objects (using composite transformations to transform objects in computer graphics)
11. Implementing composite transformations in code (writing code to apply composite transformations in programming languages)
12. Limitations of composite transformations (complexity, performance, accuracy)
13. Techniques for overcoming limitations (optimizing code, using efficient algorithms, etc.)
14. Further reading and resources (books, tutorials, online resources)

Composite transformations are an important component of the subject of Computer Graphics. Understanding the principles and techniques of composite transformations helps students to develop the skills required to transform objects in computer graphics. The hands-on practice and further reading resources will help students to develop their skills and deepen their understanding of the material.
