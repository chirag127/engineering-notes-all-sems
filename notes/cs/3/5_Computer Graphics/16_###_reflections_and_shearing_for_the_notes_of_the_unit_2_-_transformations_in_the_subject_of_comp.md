### Reflections and shearing for the notes of the Unit 2 - Transformations in the subject of Computer Graphics

Unit 2 of the Computer Graphics course focuses on Transformations, including the aspect of Reflections and Shearing.

Reflection is a transformation in computer graphics that involves flipping an object over a specified axis, creating a mirror image of the original object. Reflection is commonly used in computer graphics to create symmetry, and can be achieved by multiplying the object's coordinates by a reflection matrix.

Shearing is a transformation in computer graphics that involves distorting an object along a specified axis, creating a slanted or skewed image of the original object. Shearing is commonly used in computer graphics to create perspective effects, and can be achieved by multiplying the object's coordinates by a shearing matrix.

In the context of the Computer Graphics course, students in Unit 2 will learn about Reflections and Shearing as part of the discussion on Transformations. This may include working with examples of Reflections and Shearing, analyzing the benefits and limitations of these transformations, and learning how to use Reflections and Shearing to transform objects in computer graphics.
