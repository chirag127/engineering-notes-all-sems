## Unit 1 - Introduction and Line Generation

Unit 1 - Introduction and Line Generation is the first unit in a course on computer graphics, and it provides an overview of the field of computer graphics, including its history, applications, and fundamental concepts.

In this unit, you will learn about the basics of computer graphics, including the different types of graphics, the different types of displays, and the different types of graphics software. You will also learn about the basics of line generation, including the different types of lines, the different methods of line generation, and the different algorithms used in line generation.

The following are some of the key topics covered in Unit 1 - Introduction and Line Generation:

1. Overview of Computer Graphics: An introduction to the field of computer graphics, including its history, applications, and fundamental concepts.

2. Types of Graphics: An overview of the different types of graphics, including raster graphics and vector graphics.

3. Types of Displays: An overview of the different types of displays, including CRT displays, LCD displays, and OLED displays.

4. Types of Graphics Software: An overview of the different types of graphics software, including raster editors, vector editors, and 3D modeling software.

5. Line Generation: An overview of the basics of line generation, including the different types of lines, the different methods of line generation, and the different algorithms used in line generation.

In this unit, you will gain a solid understanding of the basics of computer graphics and line generation, and you will be prepared to explore more advanced topics in the field of computer graphics in later units.
