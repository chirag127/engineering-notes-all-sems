### Basic transformation for the notes of the Unit 2 - Transformations in the subject of Computer Graphics

The Basic Transformations section of the Unit 2 - Transformations in the subject of Computer Graphics should cover the following topics:

1. Definition of transformations (mathematical operations applied to objects in a 2D or 3D space)
2. Overview of transformations (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
3. Types of transformations (translation, rotation, scaling, shearing, reflection)
4. Overview of translation (moving an object to a new position in 2D or 3D space)
5. Overview of rotation (rotating an object about a point in 2D or 3D space)
6. Overview of scaling (resizing an object in 2D or 3D space)
7. Overview of shearing (distorting an object in 2D or 3D space)
8. Overview of reflection (flipping an object over a line in 2D or 3D space)
9. Transformation matrices (mathematical representations of transformations)
10. Composing transformations (combining multiple transformations into a single matrix)
11. Transformation order (the order in which transformations are applied to an object)
12. Inverse transformations (reversing the effect of a transformation)
13. Limitations of transformations (accuracy, computational complexity)
14. Techniques for overcoming limitations (using efficient algorithms, reducing the number of transformations)
15. Further reading and resources (books, articles, online resources)

Basic Transformations is an important component of the subject of Computer Graphics. Understanding the principles and techniques of transformations helps students to develop the skills required to manipulate objects in 2D or 3D space. The hands-on practice and further reading resources will help students to develop their skills and deepen their understanding of the material.
