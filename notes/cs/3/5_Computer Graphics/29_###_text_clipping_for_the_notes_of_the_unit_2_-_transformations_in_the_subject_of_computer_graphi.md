### Text clipping for the notes of the Unit 2 - Transformations in the subject of Computer Graphics

Text clipping is a process in computer graphics that involves removing or hiding parts of text that are outside a specified region or area. Text clipping is often used in computer graphics to ensure that text is properly displayed within a specific area, such as a window or a viewport.

The following are some of the key concepts related to text clipping in computer graphics:

1. Clipping Region: The clipping region is the area or region within which text is displayed. Any text that falls outside the clipping region is clipped, or removed.

2. Clipping Algorithms: Clipping algorithms are mathematical procedures used to determine which parts of text are inside or outside the clipping region, and to remove or hide the parts of text that are outside the clipping region.

3. Cohen-Sutherland Algorithm: The Cohen-Sutherland algorithm is a widely used text clipping algorithm that uses a four-bit code to represent the location of a point with respect to the clipping region.

4. Liang-Barsky Algorithm: The Liang-Barsky algorithm is a text clipping algorithm that uses a parametric equation to represent lines and to determine which parts of text are inside or outside the clipping region.
