### Introductory concepts of Spline for the notes of the Unit 4 - Curves and Surfaces in the subject of Computer Graphics

The Introductory concepts of Spline section of the Unit 4 - Curves and Surfaces in the subject of Computer Graphics should cover the following topics:

1. Definition of spline (a smooth curve that is used to approximate a set of data points)
2. Overview of spline (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
3. Types of spline (linear, quadratic, cubic, B-spline, etc.)
4. Overview of linear spline (a simple spline that connects data points with straight lines)
5. Overview of quadratic spline (a spline that uses parabolic curves to connect data points)
6. Overview of cubic spline (a spline that uses cubic curves to connect data points)
7. Overview of B-spline (a spline that uses a set of basis functions to approximate data points)
8. Spline representation (representing splines using mathematical functions)
9. Interpolation and approximation (the difference between using splines to interpolate data points and to approximate data points)
10. Spline fitting (the process of finding the best spline to fit a set of data points)
11. Limitations of spline (complexity, accuracy, etc.)
12. Techniques for overcoming limitations (using efficient algorithms, reducing the number of data points, etc.)
13. Applications of spline (in fields such as computer graphics, engineering, and physics)
14. Further reading and resources (books, articles, online resources)

Introductory concepts of Spline is an important component of the subject of Computer Graphics. Understanding the principles and techniques of spline helps students to develop the skills required to approximate and fit data points in a computer graphics system. The hands-on practice and further reading resources will help students to develop their skills and deepen their understanding of the material.
