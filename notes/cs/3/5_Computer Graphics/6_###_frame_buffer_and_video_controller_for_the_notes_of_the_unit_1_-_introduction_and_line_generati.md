### Frame buffer and video controller for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction and Line Generation in the subject of Computer Graphics

Unit 1 of the Computer Graphics course focuses on the Introduction and Line Generation, including the aspect of Frame Buffer and Video Controller.

Frame Buffer is a memory area in a computer's graphics card that stores the image data that is to be displayed on the screen. The image data is stored as a collection of pixels, each with its own color and intensity values, and is updated continuously as the image changes.

A Video Controller is a component in a computer's graphics card that is responsible for controlling the display of the image data stored in the Frame Buffer. The Video Controller reads the image data from the Frame Buffer, processes it, and sends it to the display device (such as a monitor) for display.

In the context of the Computer Graphics course, students in Unit 1 will learn about Frame Buffer and Video Controller as part of the discussion on Introduction and Line Generation. This may include working with examples of Frame Buffer and Video Controller, analyzing the benefits and limitations of these components, and learning how to use Frame Buffer and Video Controller to generate and display graphics.
