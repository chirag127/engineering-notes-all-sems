## E-R Modeling from the Problem Statements for the Software Engineering Mapping with Virtual Lab Lab in the subject of Software Engineering

- E-R modeling, or Entity-Relationship modeling, is a technique used to represent data in a structured and organized manner.
- It is commonly used in the design of databases and information systems.
- The E-R model consists of entities, attributes, and relationships.
- Entities represent real-world objects or concepts, such as a customer or an order.
- Attributes represent the characteristics or properties of an entity, such as a customer's name or an order's date.
- Relationships represent the associations between entities, such as a customer placing an order.
- E-R diagrams are used to visually represent the E-R model, using shapes and lines to represent entities, attributes, and relationships.
- E-R modeling is an important step in the process of designing a database or information system, as it helps to ensure that the data is organized in a logical and efficient manner.
