## Modeling UML Use Case Diagrams and Capturing Use Case Scenarios

- **UML (Unified Modeling Language)** is a standard language for specifying, visualizing, constructing, and documenting the artifacts of software systems.
- **Use Case Diagrams** are used to represent the functionality of a system from the user's point of view.
- A **Use Case** represents a distinct functionality of a system, a component, or a class.
- **Actors** are the users of a system, and are represented as stick figures in a Use Case Diagram.
- **Use Case Scenarios** are textual descriptions of the flow of events of a use case.
- To capture a Use Case Scenario, the following steps can be followed:
  1. Identify the actors and use cases involved in the scenario.
  2. Write the steps of the scenario from the perspective of the primary actor.
  3. Include any alternate or exceptional flows that may occur.
  4. Review the scenario to ensure that it is complete and accurate.
- Use Case Diagrams and Scenarios are useful tools in the Software Engineering process, as they help to ensure that the system being developed meets the needs of its users.
