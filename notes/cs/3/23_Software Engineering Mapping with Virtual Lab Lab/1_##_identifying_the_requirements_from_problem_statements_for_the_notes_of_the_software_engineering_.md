## Identifying the Requirements from Problem Statements for the notes of the Software Engineering Mapping with Virtual Lab Lab in the subject of Software Engineering

The Identifying the Requirements from Problem Statements section of the Software Engineering Mapping with Virtual Lab Lab in the subject of Software Engineering should cover the following topics:

1. Definition of Requirements (the characteristics or features that a software system must have in order to meet the needs of its stakeholders)
2. Overview of Requirements (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
3. Definition of Problem Statements (a description of a problem or issue that needs to be addressed by a software system)
4. Overview of Problem Statements (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
5. Steps for Identifying Requirements from Problem Statements (e.g. analyzing the problem, identifying stakeholders, defining objectives, etc.)
6. Overview of Steps for Identifying Requirements from Problem Statements (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
7. Techniques for Identifying Requirements from Problem Statements (e.g. brainstorming, use cases, interviews, etc.)
8. Overview of Techniques for Identifying Requirements from Problem Statements (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
9. Tips for Effective Requirements Identification (e.g. involving stakeholders, documenting requirements, etc.)
10. Overview of Tips for Effective Requirements Identification (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
11. Further reading and resources (books, articles, online resources)

Identifying the requirements from problem statements is an important component of the software engineering process. Understanding the principles and techniques of requirements identification helps students to develop the skills required to create software systems that meet the needs of their stakeholders. The hands-on practice and further reading resources will help students to develop their skills and deepen their understanding of the material.
