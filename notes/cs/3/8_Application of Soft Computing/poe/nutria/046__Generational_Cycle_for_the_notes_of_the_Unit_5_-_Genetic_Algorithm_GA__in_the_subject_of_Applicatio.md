
### Generational Cycle for the notes of the Unit 5 - Genetic Algorithm(GA) in the subject of Application of Soft Computing

1. Genetic Algorithm is a type of evolutionary algorithm which is used to solve complex optimization problems.
2. It is based on the principles of natural selection and genetics, where the solutions to a problem are represented as a set of individuals in a population.
3. The population is evolved over time by applying genetic operators such as selection, crossover and mutation.
4. Selection is the process of selecting the fittest individuals in a population for reproduction.
5. Crossover is the process of combining two individuals to produce a new individual.
6. Mutation is the process of randomly changing the values of an individual in a population.
7. The Generational Cycle is the main loop of a genetic algorithm which consists of the following steps:
    - Initialization: The initial population is randomly generated.
    - Evaluation: The fitness of the individuals in the population is evaluated.
    - Selection: The fittest individuals are selected for reproduction.
    - Crossover: Two individuals are combined to produce a new individual.
    - Mutation: Random changes are made to the values of an individual.
    - Replacement: The new individual is added to the population.
8. The process is repeated until the desired result is achieved.
