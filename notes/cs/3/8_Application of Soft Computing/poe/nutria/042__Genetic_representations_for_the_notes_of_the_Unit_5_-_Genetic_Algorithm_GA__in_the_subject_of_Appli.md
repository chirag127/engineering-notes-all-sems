
### Genetic Representations for Unit 5 - Genetic Algorithm (GA) in Application of Soft Computing

1. Genetic Algorithms (GAs) are a form of evolutionary algorithms that use biological principles such as mutation, crossover, and selection to solve problems.
2. GAs are used to solve optimization problems by searching for the optimal solution in a given search space.
3. GAs use a population of possible solutions, referred to as individuals, which are represented by a string of symbols.
4. The symbols used in the representation of individuals are called genes.
5. The structure of the gene string is referred to as the chromosome.
6. The chromosome is made up of a series of genes that are connected together in some form of order.
7. The order of the genes in the chromosome determines the behavior of the individual.
8. The gene string is then used to calculate the fitness of the individual, which is a measure of how well the individual performs in a given problem.
9. The individuals with the highest fitness values are selected for reproduction and mutation.
10. The process of reproduction and mutation is repeated until a satisfactory solution is found.
