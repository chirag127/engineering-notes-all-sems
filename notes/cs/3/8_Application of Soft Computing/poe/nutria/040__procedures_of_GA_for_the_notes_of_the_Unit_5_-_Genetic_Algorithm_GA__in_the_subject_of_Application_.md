

### Procedures of GA for the Notes of the Unit 5 - Genetic Algorithm(GA) in the Subject of Application of Soft Computing

1. Genetic Algorithms (GA) are a type of evolutionary algorithms inspired by natural selection and genetics. They are used to solve optimization problems.

2. GA works by creating a population of possible solutions to a given problem, and then applying genetic operators such as crossover, mutation, and selection to the population to evolve new generations of solutions.

3. The solutions in the population are represented as chromosomes, which are strings of bits, numbers, or other symbols.

4. The fitness of each solution is evaluated, and the best solutions are selected to reproduce and create the next generation.

5. The process is repeated until a solution with an acceptable fitness is found.

6. GA can be used to solve a wide range of problems, from finding the shortest path between two points to designing complex electronic circuits.
