
### Artificial Neuron and its Model 

1. Artificial Neurons are computational models inspired by biological neurons. 
2. They are used to create artificial neural networks, which are used to simulate the functioning of a biological brain. 
3. Artificial neurons are composed of many components, such as input layers, output layers, weights, and activation functions. 
4. The input layers receive signals from other neurons, which are then processed by the weights and activation functions to produce an output signal. 
5. The output signal is then sent to other neurons in the network. 
6. The weights and activation functions are adjusted during the learning process to optimize the performance of the network. 
7. Artificial neural networks can be used for a variety of tasks, such as image recognition, natural language processing, and decision-making. 
8. They are also used for robotics, medical diagnosis, and financial forecasting.
