
### Perception and Convergence Rule for the Notes of the Unit 1 - Neural Networks-I (Introduction & Architecture) in the Subject of Application of Soft Computing

1. Neural networks are a set of algorithms that are designed to recognize patterns. They are used in a wide variety of applications, from predicting stock prices to recognizing handwritten digits.
2. The basic structure of a neural network consists of an input layer, one or more hidden layers, and an output layer.
3. The input layer takes in data and passes it to the hidden layers, which process the data and pass it to the output layer.
4. The output layer produces a result based on the data that was processed by the hidden layers.
5. The goal of a neural network is to find the optimal weights and biases that will produce the best possible result.
6. This is done through the process of training, where the weights and biases are adjusted to minimize the error between the predicted result and the actual result.
7. The process of training is repeated until the error is minimized and the neural network has converged on a solution.
8. Once the neural network has converged, it can be used to make predictions on new data.
