

### Working Principle of Genetic Algorithm (GA) 

1. Genetic Algorithm (GA) is a search-based optimization technique inspired by the theory of natural selection and genetics. 
2. It is used to solve complex optimization problems by mimicking the process of natural selection. 
3. GA is a stochastic optimization algorithm that works on a population of candidate solutions. 
4. It uses the principles of selection, crossover and mutation to generate new solutions from the existing population.
5. The selection process involves selecting the fittest individuals from the current population for reproduction. 
6. The crossover process involves combining the genetic information of two parent solutions to create a new solution. 
7. The mutation process involves randomly changing the genetic information of a solution. 
8. The new solutions are evaluated and compared with the existing population to determine their fitness. 
9. The process is repeated until a solution with an acceptable level of fitness is found or a predetermined number of generations have been evaluated.
