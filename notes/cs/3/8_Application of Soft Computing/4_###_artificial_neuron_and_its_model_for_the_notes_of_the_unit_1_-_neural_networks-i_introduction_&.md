### Artificial Neuron and its model for the notes of the Unit 1 - Neural Networks-I (Introduction & Architecture) in the subject of Application of Soft Computing

An artificial neuron, also known as a perceptron, is a simple mathematical model that is used to simulate the behavior of a biological neuron. The artificial neuron receives input signals, processes them, and produces an output signal. The processing of the input signals is performed by a set of weights that are multiplied with the inputs and then summed. The result is then passed through an activation function, which determines the output of the neuron.

The artificial neuron model consists of the following components:

1. Inputs: The inputs are the signals that are received by the neuron.

2. Weights: The weights are the parameters that control the strength of the connection between the inputs and the neuron.

3. Bias: The bias is a constant value that is added to the weighted sum of inputs.

4. Summation: The weighted inputs are summed to produce a scalar value.

5. Activation Function: The activation function is a non-linear function that maps the weighted sum of inputs to the output of the neuron.

6. Output: The output of the neuron is the result of passing the weighted sum of inputs through the activation function.

The artificial neuron model is the basic building block of artificial neural networks. The outputs of multiple artificial neurons can be connected to form a network, which can be used to model complex relationships between inputs and outputs.
