### perception and convergence rule for the notes of the Unit 1 - Neural Networks-I (Introduction & Architecture) in the subject of Application of Soft Computing

Perception and convergence rule are two fundamental concepts in neural networks.

1. Perception: A perception is a single processing unit in a neural network that takes inputs, performs a computation, and outputs a signal. It is the basic building block of a neural network.

2. Convergence Rule: A convergence rule is a method used to adjust the weights in a neural network so that the network's output approaches the desired output. The most commonly used convergence rule is the gradient descent algorithm, which adjusts the weights in the direction of the negative gradient of the error function.

The convergence rule is used to train the neural network by minimizing the error between the predicted output and the desired output. The weights are updated iteratively until the error is sufficiently small or the maximum number of iterations is reached. The convergence rule is a critical component of neural networks as it determines how the network learns from the data and improves its predictions over time.
