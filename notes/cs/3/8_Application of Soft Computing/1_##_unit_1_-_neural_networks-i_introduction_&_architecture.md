## Unit 1 - Neural Networks-I (Introduction & Architecture)

Unit 1 - Neural Networks-I (Introduction & Architecture) is the first unit of a course on Neural Networks. It provides an introduction to the fundamental concepts of neural networks and covers the architecture of these models.

1. Introduction: The introduction provides an overview of the history and development of neural networks and the main applications of these models. It also covers the basic concepts of artificial neurons, activation functions, and the architecture of neural networks.

2. Architecture: The architecture section covers the different types of neural networks, such as feedforward, recurrent, and convolutional neural networks. It also covers the design and implementation of neural network models, including the number of layers, the number of neurons in each layer, and the activation functions used in each layer.

The unit provides a foundation for further study of neural networks and their applications in various fields, including computer vision, speech recognition, and natural language processing. It is an important unit for students interested in learning about artificial intelligence and machine learning.
