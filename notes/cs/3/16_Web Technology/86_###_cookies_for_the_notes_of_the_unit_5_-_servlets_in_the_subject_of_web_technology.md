### Cookies for the notes of the Unit 5 - Servlets in the subject of Web Technology

The Cookies section of the Unit 5 - Servlets in the subject of Web Technology should cover the following topics:

1. Definition of Cookies (small text files stored by a web browser on a user's device)
2. Overview of Cookies (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
3. Cookies and HTTP (e.g. sending and receiving cookies in HTTP requests and responses)
4. Overview of Cookies and HTTP (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
5. Cookies and Session Management (e.g. using cookies to track user sessions)
6. Overview of Cookies and Session Management (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
7. Cookies and Security (e.g. securing cookies, avoiding cookie-based attacks)
8. Overview of Cookies and Security (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
9. Cookies in industry applications (how cookies are used in various industries)
10. Further reading and resources (books, articles, online resources)

Cookies are an important component of the subject of Web Technology. Understanding the principles and techniques of Cookies helps students to develop the skills required to create and implement web applications that use cookies for session management and other purposes. The hands-on practice and further reading resources will help students to develop their skills and deepen their understanding of the material.
