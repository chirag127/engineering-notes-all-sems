### Connecting to Internet for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction in the subject of Web Technology

Connecting to the Internet refers to the process of establishing a connection between a device and the Internet, allowing the device to access and exchange information with other devices connected to the Internet. The following are some of the key concepts related to connecting to the Internet:

1. Internet Service Provider (ISP): An Internet Service Provider (ISP) is a company that provides Internet access to customers, typically through a wired or wireless connection.

2. Broadband: Broadband is a high-speed Internet connection that allows for the transfer of large amounts of data at high speeds.

3. Dial-up: Dial-up is a type of Internet connection that uses a telephone line to connect to the Internet. Dial-up is typically slower than broadband connections.

4. Wi-Fi: Wi-Fi is a type of wireless Internet connection that allows devices to connect to the Internet without a physical connection.

5. Ethernet: Ethernet is a type of wired Internet connection that uses a cable to connect to the Internet.

6. Router: A router is a device that connects multiple devices to the Internet and allows them to share the same Internet connection.

In this unit, you will learn about connecting to the Internet and the key concepts related to it, including Internet Service Providers, broadband, dial-up, Wi-Fi, Ethernet, and routers.

This unit will provide a foundation for understanding the principles and practices of web technology, and for exploring the various concepts and techniques used in the field of web development.
