### Factory Methods for the notes of the Unit 3 - Scripting in the subject of Web Technology

Unit 3 - Scripting in the subject of Web Technology may cover Factory Methods, a design pattern used in object-oriented programming.

A Factory Method is a static method that creates objects, typically by calling a constructor for a class. In object-oriented programming, Factory Methods are used to encapsulate the creation of objects, making it easier to manage and maintain code.

In the context of Unit 3 - Scripting in the subject of Web Technology, students may learn about Factory Methods and how they are used in object-oriented programming, including JavaScript. This may include working with examples of Factory Methods, analyzing the syntax and structure of Factory Methods, and learning how to use Factory Methods to create objects in JavaScript.

In summary, in Unit 3 - Scripting in the subject of Web Technology, students may learn about Factory Methods, a design pattern used in object-oriented programming. They may learn about how Factory Methods are used in object-oriented programming, including JavaScript, and how to use Factory Methods to create objects in JavaScript.
