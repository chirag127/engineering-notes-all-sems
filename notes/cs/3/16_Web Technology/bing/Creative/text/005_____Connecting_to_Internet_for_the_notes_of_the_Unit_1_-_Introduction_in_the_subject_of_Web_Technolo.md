### Connecting to Internet

- The internet is a global network of computers and devices that communicate with each other using standard protocols.
- To connect to the internet, you need an internet service provider (ISP) that provides you with access to the network, such as America Online or Earthlink.
- There are different ways to connect to the internet, depending on the type of device and the available technology. Some of the common methods are:

  - Wi-Fi: This is a wireless connection that uses radio waves to transmit data between devices and a router. A router is a device that connects multiple devices to a single internet connection. You can use Wi-Fi to connect to the internet from your laptop, smartphone, tablet, or other devices that have a Wi-Fi adapter. To use Wi-Fi, you need to be within the range of a router that is connected to the internet. You also need to know the network name (SSID) and password (if any) of the router. You can find Wi-Fi networks in many public places, such as cafes, libraries, airports, and hotels .
  - Ethernet: This is a wired connection that uses a cable to connect your device to a router or a modem. A modem is a device that converts the signals from the internet into a format that your device can understand, and vice versa. You can use Ethernet to connect to the internet from your desktop computer, laptop, or other devices that have an Ethernet port. To use Ethernet, you need to have a cable that is compatible with your device and the router or modem. You also need to plug the cable into the appropriate ports on both ends .
  - Dial-up: This is an outdated connection that uses a phone line and a modem to connect your device to the internet. You can use dial-up to connect to the internet from your desktop computer or laptop that has a modem and a phone jack. To use dial-up, you need to have a phone line that is not in use, and an ISP that offers dial-up service. You also need to dial a specific number to connect to the ISP, and enter your username and password. Dial-up is very slow and unreliable compared to other methods of connecting to the internet.

- Once you are connected to the internet, you can use a web browser to access the web. The web is a collection of documents and resources that are stored on servers and identified by URLs. A server is a computer that hosts and delivers web content to clients. A client is a device that requests and receives web content from servers. A URL is a unique address that specifies the location and name of a web resource, such as https://www.wikipedia.org. A web browser is a software application that allows you to view and interact with web content, such as Google Chrome or Mozilla Firefox.
