### Introduction for the notes of the Unit 3 - Scripting in the subject of Web Technology

- Scripting is a technique of writing programs that are executed by another program, such as a web browser or a server, rather than by the operating system directly.
- Scripting languages are often interpreted, rather than compiled, which means they are translated into executable code at run time, rather than beforehand.
- Scripting languages are usually high-level, meaning they provide a lot of abstraction and convenience features, such as dynamic typing, garbage collection, and built-in data structures.
- Scripting languages are often used for tasks that require user interaction, such as web development, automation, data analysis, and testing.
- Some examples of popular scripting languages are JavaScript, Python, Ruby, PHP, and Perl.
