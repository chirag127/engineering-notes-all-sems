### History of Web and Internet

- The **Internet** is a global system of interconnected computer networks that use the Internet protocol suite (TCP/IP) to communicate and exchange data.
- The **Web** is a collection of documents and resources that are linked by hyperlinks and can be accessed via the Internet using a web browser.
- The Internet originated in the **1970s** as a project of the U.S. Department of Defense (DoD) to create a network that could survive a nuclear attack. The network was called ARPANET and connected four computers at different universities.
- The Internet expanded in the **1980s** with the development of the TCP/IP protocols, which allowed different types of networks to interoperate. The Internet also became more accessible to the public with the introduction of personal computers and modems.
- The Web was invented in **1989** by Tim Berners-Lee, a British scientist working at CERN, a European research organization. He proposed a system of hypertext that would allow users to access and share information across the Internet.
- The Web became popular in the **1990s** with the release of the first web browser, Mosaic, which made the Web easy to use and navigate. The Web also enabled the emergence of new applications and services, such as e-commerce, social media, and online education.
