### Cookies

Cookies are small text files that are stored on a user's computer by a web server. They are used to store information about the user's activity on a website. Here are some key points about cookies:

1. Cookies are used to store information about a user's preferences and activity on a website.
2. They are sent from the web server to the user's browser and stored on the user's computer.
3. When the user visits the website again, the browser sends the cookies back to the server, allowing the server to recognize the user and customize the user's experience.
4. Cookies can be used for a variety of purposes, such as authentication, tracking user behavior, and personalizing content.
5. There are two types of cookies: session cookies and persistent cookies. Session cookies are temporary and are deleted when the user closes their browser. Persistent cookies remain on the user's computer until they expire or are deleted by the user.
6. Cookies can be managed by the user through their browser settings. Users can choose to accept or reject cookies, or to be notified when a cookie is being sent.
