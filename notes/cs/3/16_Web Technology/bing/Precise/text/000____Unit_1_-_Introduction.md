## Unit 1 - Introduction

1. The introduction is the first part of any written work, speech, or presentation.
2. It serves to introduce the topic, provide context, and engage the reader or audience.
3. A good introduction should be clear, concise, and interesting.
4. It should provide enough background information to help the reader or audience understand the topic.
5. The introduction should also include a thesis statement or main idea that will be developed in the body of the work.
6. The length of the introduction may vary depending on the type of work and the intended audience.
7. It is important to revise and edit the introduction to ensure that it effectively introduces the topic and engages the reader or audience.
