### Introduction to Internet services and tools

1. The Internet is a global network of interconnected computer networks that use the standard Internet protocol suite (TCP/IP) to link devices worldwide.
2. The Internet provides a wide range of services and tools that enable users to communicate, share information, and access resources.
3. Some of the most popular Internet services include email, instant messaging, social networking, online shopping, and online gaming.
4. Email is a service that allows users to send and receive electronic messages over the Internet.
5. Instant messaging is a service that enables real-time text-based communication between two or more users over the Internet.
6. Social networking is a service that allows users to create and maintain online profiles, connect with friends and family, and share information and media.
7. Online shopping is a service that allows users to purchase goods and services over the Internet.
8. Online gaming is a service that allows users to play games with other users over the Internet.
9. In addition to these services, the Internet also provides access to a wide range of tools and resources, including search engines, online encyclopedias, and online news sources.
10. Search engines are tools that allow users to search for information on the Internet by entering keywords or phrases.
11. Online encyclopedias are resources that provide comprehensive information on a wide range of topics.
12. Online news sources are resources that provide up-to-date news and information on current events.
