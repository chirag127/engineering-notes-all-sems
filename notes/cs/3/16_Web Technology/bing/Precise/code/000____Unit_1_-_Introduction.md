## Unit 1 - Introduction

1. The introduction is the first part of any written work, whether it is an essay, a research paper, or a book.
2. The purpose of the introduction is to provide the reader with a clear understanding of the topic being discussed and the context in which it is being presented.
3. The introduction should provide background information on the topic, including any relevant historical, social, or cultural context.
4. The introduction should also include a clear statement of the main argument or thesis of the work.
5. The introduction should be engaging and interesting to the reader, encouraging them to continue reading the rest of the work.
6. The introduction should be well-organized and structured, with a clear and logical progression of ideas.
7. The introduction should be concise and to the point, avoiding unnecessary detail or tangential information.
8. The introduction should set the tone for the rest of the work, establishing the author's voice and perspective.
