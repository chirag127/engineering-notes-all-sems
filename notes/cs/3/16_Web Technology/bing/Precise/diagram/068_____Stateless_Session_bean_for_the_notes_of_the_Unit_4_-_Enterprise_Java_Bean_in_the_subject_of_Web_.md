### Stateless Session Bean

- Stateless session beans are enterprise beans that do not maintain conversational state with clients.
- They are used to perform independent operations for clients.
- Stateless session beans are typically used to perform operations that are short-lived and do not require conversational state.
- Stateless session beans can be shared among multiple clients, which can improve performance and scalability.
- Stateless session beans are created by the container when needed and are removed when they are no longer needed.
- Stateless session beans can be pooled by the container to improve performance.
- Stateless session beans can be accessed by multiple threads concurrently, so they must be thread-safe.
- Stateless session beans can be used to implement business logic, such as calculations or data processing.
- Stateless session beans can also be used to access external resources, such as databases or messaging systems.
- Stateless session beans can be used in combination with other types of enterprise beans, such as entity beans or message-driven beans, to implement complex business logic.
