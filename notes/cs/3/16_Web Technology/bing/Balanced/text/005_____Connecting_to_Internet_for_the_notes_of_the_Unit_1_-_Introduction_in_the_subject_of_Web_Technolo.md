### Connecting to Internet

- The Internet is a global network of interconnected computers that communicate using standardized protocols.
- To connect to the Internet, a computer needs a network interface card (NIC), a modem, a router, and an Internet service provider (ISP).
- A NIC is a hardware device that allows a computer to send and receive data over a network. It has a unique address called a MAC address that identifies the computer on the network.
- A modem is a device that converts digital signals from a computer into analog signals that can be transmitted over a phone line, cable, or wireless network. It also converts analog signals back into digital signals for the computer.
- A router is a device that connects multiple computers or networks and directs the flow of data between them. It has a unique address called an IP address that identifies the router on the Internet.
- An ISP is a company that provides access to the Internet for a fee. It assigns an IP address to each computer or router that connects to its network and provides a domain name service (DNS) that translates domain names into IP addresses.
- To connect to the Internet, a computer sends data to its modem, which converts it into analog signals and sends it to the ISP over a phone line, cable, or wireless network. The ISP routes the data to the destination computer or router using its IP address. The destination modem converts the data back into digital signals and sends it to the destination computer. The process is reversed for data coming from the Internet to the computer.
