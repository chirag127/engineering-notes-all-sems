## Unit 1 - Introduction

- This unit provides an overview of the course objectives, topics, and assessment methods.
- The course aims to teach the basic concepts and principles of artificial intelligence (AI), such as agents, search, knowledge representation, reasoning, planning, learning, and natural language processing.
- The course also introduces some of the applications and ethical issues of AI, such as expert systems, robotics, computer vision, and artificial neural networks.
- The course requires some background in mathematics, logic, and programming, as well as an interest and curiosity in AI.
- The course assessment consists of quizzes, assignments, and a final exam, which will test the students' understanding and ability to apply the concepts and techniques learned in the course.
- The course follows the textbook "Artificial Intelligence: A Modern Approach" by Stuart Russell and Peter Norvig, which is widely used and respected in the field of AI. The textbook covers most of the topics in the course, but some additional readings and resources will be provided as well.
- The course is divided into 10 units, each covering a different aspect of AI. The units are:

  - Unit 1: Introduction
  - Unit 2: Intelligent Agents
  - Unit 3: Search
  - Unit 4: Knowledge Representation and Reasoning
  - Unit 5: Planning
  - Unit 6: Learning
  - Unit 7: Natural Language Processing
  - Unit 8: Expert Systems
  - Unit 9: Robotics
  - Unit 10: Computer Vision and Artificial Neural Networks

- The course schedule and deadlines are available on the course website, which also contains the lecture slides, assignments, quizzes, and other materials. The course website is the main source of information and communication for the course, so students are expected to check it regularly and follow the instructions and guidelines given there.
- The course instructor and teaching assistants are available for consultation and feedback via email, office hours, and online forums. Students are encouraged to ask questions, participate in discussions, and seek help when needed.
- The course is designed to be challenging, engaging, and rewarding for students who are interested in AI and want to learn more about it. The course hopes to inspire students to pursue further studies or careers in AI, or to apply AI to their own fields and problems.
