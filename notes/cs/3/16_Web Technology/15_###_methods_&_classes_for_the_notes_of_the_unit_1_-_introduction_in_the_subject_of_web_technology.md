### Methods & Classes for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction in the subject of Web Technology

Unit 1 - Introduction in the subject of Web Technology will cover Methods and Classes, fundamental concepts in object-oriented programming.

A method is a block of code that performs a specific task. In object-oriented programming, methods are used to encapsulate the behavior of objects. Methods are defined within classes, and objects are instances of classes.

A class is a blueprint for creating objects. Classes define the structure and behavior of objects, and they provide a way to create objects that have the same structure and behavior. In object-oriented programming, classes are used to define the structure and behavior of objects, and to create objects that have the same structure and behavior.

In the context of Unit 1 - Introduction in the subject of Web Technology, students will learn about Methods and Classes, and how they are used in object-oriented programming. This may include working with examples of Methods and Classes, analyzing the syntax and structure of Methods and Classes, and learning how to use Methods and Classes to develop web-based applications.

In Unit 1 - Introduction in the subject of Web Technology, students will also learn about the basics of object-oriented programming, including inheritance, polymorphism, and encapsulation, and how these concepts are related to Methods and Classes. They will also learn about the benefits and limitations of object-oriented programming, and how to use object-oriented programming to develop web-based applications.

In summary, in Unit 1 - Introduction in the subject of Web Technology, students will learn about Methods and Classes, fundamental concepts in object-oriented programming. They will learn about the syntax and structure of Methods and Classes, the basics of object-oriented programming, and the benefits and limitations of object-oriented programming, and will learn how to use Methods and Classes to develop web-based applications.
