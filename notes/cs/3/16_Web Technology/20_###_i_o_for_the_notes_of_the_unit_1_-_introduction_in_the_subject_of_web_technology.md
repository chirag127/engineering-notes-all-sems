### I/O for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction in the subject of Web Technology

I/O (Input/Output) refers to the process of reading and writing data to and from a computer or device. The following are some of the key concepts related to I/O in web technology:

1. Input: Input refers to the process of reading data into a computer or device, such as reading data from a keyboard, mouse, or file.

2. Output: Output refers to the process of writing data from a computer or device, such as displaying data on a screen or writing data to a file.

3. File Input/Output: File Input/Output refers to the process of reading and writing data to and from files. This can include reading and writing text files, images, videos, and other types of data.

4. Network Input/Output: Network Input/Output refers to the process of reading and writing data over a network, such as the Internet. This can include reading and writing data to and from web servers, email servers, and other networked systems.

5. Streams: Streams are a way of reading and writing data in a continuous flow, rather than reading and writing data in discrete chunks. Streams are commonly used in web technology for reading and writing data over networks.

6. Buffers: Buffers are a type of temporary storage used in I/O operations. Buffers are used to hold data temporarily while it is being read or written to a device, such as a disk or network.

In this unit, you will learn about I/O and the key concepts related to it, including input, output, file input/output, network input/output, streams, and buffers.

This unit will provide a foundation for understanding the principles and practices of web technology, and for exploring the various concepts and techniques used in the field of web development.
