### History of Web and Internet for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction in the subject of Web Technology

The History of Web and Internet section of the Unit 1 - Introduction in the subject of Web Technology should cover the following topics:

1. Definition of the Internet (a global network of computers connected to each other through standardized communication protocols)
2. Overview of the Internet (what it is, when it was created, its evolution and growth)
3. The birth of the Internet (e.g. ARPANET, the first computer network, etc.)
4. Overview of the birth of the Internet (what it was, when it was created, its significance)
5. The evolution of the Internet (e.g. TCP/IP, the World Wide Web, etc.)
6. Overview of the evolution of the Internet (what it was, when it happened, its significance)
7. The birth of the World Wide Web (e.g. Tim Berners-Lee, the first website, etc.)
8. Overview of the birth of the World Wide Web (what it was, when it happened, its significance)
9. The evolution of the World Wide Web (e.g. HTML, CSS, JavaScript, etc.)
10. Overview of the evolution of the World Wide Web (what it was, when it happened, its significance)
11. The growth of the World Wide Web (e.g. the rise of e-commerce, social media, etc.)
12. Overview of the growth of the World Wide Web (what it was, when it happened, its significance)
13. The future of the World Wide Web (e.g. new technologies, trends, etc.)
14. Overview of the future of the World Wide Web (what it is, when it will happen, its significance)

The history of the Web and Internet is an important component of the subject of Web Technology. Understanding the evolution and growth of the Internet and World Wide Web helps students to appreciate the significance of these technologies and their impact on society. The hands-on practice and further reading resources will help students to develop their skills and deepen their understanding of the material.
