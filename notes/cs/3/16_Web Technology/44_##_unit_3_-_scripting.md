## Unit 3 - Scripting

The Scripting section of Unit 3 in the subject of Web Technology should cover the following topics:

1. Definition of Scripting (the use of scripting languages to automate tasks)
2. Overview of Scripting (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
3. Types of Scripting Languages (e.g. JavaScript, Python, etc.)
4. Overview of Types of Scripting Languages (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
5. Scripting in Web Development (e.g. client-side scripting, server-side scripting, etc.)
6. Overview of Scripting in Web Development (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
7. Scripting Techniques (e.g. event handling, dynamic content creation, etc.)
8. Overview of Scripting Techniques (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
9. Scripting Best Practices (e.g. code organization, error handling, etc.)
10. Overview of Scripting Best Practices (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
11. Scripting in industry applications (how Scripting is used in various industries)
12. Further reading and resources (books, articles, online resources)

Scripting is an important component of the subject of Web Technology. Understanding the principles and techniques of scripting helps students to develop the skills required to automate tasks and create dynamic web pages. The hands-on practice and further reading resources will help students to develop their skills and deepen their understanding of the material.
