### Networking for the notes of the Unit 3 - Scripting in the subject of Web Technology

Unit 3 - Scripting in the subject of Web Technology may cover Networking, a fundamental concept in computer science and web technology.

Networking refers to the practice of connecting computers and other devices together to share resources and exchange information. In the context of web technology, networking is used to connect web servers, web browsers, and other devices to the internet, enabling users to access and share information and services.

In the context of Unit 3 - Scripting in the subject of Web Technology, students may learn about the basics of networking, including the different types of networks, such as local area networks (LANs), wide area networks (WANs), and the internet, and the different protocols used to communicate between networks, such as the Transmission Control Protocol/Internet Protocol (TCP/IP).

They may also learn about the different technologies used to connect networks, such as routers, switches, and firewalls, and the different services provided by networks, such as email, file sharing, and web hosting.

In summary, in Unit 3 - Scripting in the subject of Web Technology, students may learn about Networking, a fundamental concept in computer science and web technology. They may learn about the basics of networking, including the different types of networks, protocols, technologies, and services used to connect networks and exchange information.
