3. Types of Session Tracking (e.g. URL rewriting, cookies, etc.)
4. Overview of Types of Session Tracking (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
5. Session Tracking in Servlets (e.g. using session tracking in servlets, etc.)
6. Overview of Session Tracking in Servlets (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
7. Session Tracking and Security (e.g. protecting session data, etc.)
8. Overview of Session Tracking and Security (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
9. Session Tracking and Performance (e.g. optimizing session tracking performance, etc.)
10. Overview of Session Tracking and Performance (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
11. Session Tracking in industry applications (how session tracking is used in various industries)
12. Further reading and resources (books, articles, online resources)

Session Tracking is an important component of the subject of Web Technology. Understanding the principles and techniques of session tracking helps students to develop the skills required to create and implement effective web applications. The hands-on practice and further reading resources will help students to develop their skills and deepen their understanding of the material.
