### Interface Servlet and the Servlet Life Cycle for the notes of the Unit 5 - Servlets in the subject of Web Technology

A Servlet is a Java class that runs on a web server and provides dynamic content to web clients. The following are some of the key concepts related to Servlets and the Servlet Life Cycle:

1. Interface Servlet: The Servlet interface defines the methods that must be implemented by a Servlet, including the init(), service(), and destroy() methods.

2. Servlet Life Cycle: The Servlet Life Cycle is the sequence of events that occur when a Servlet is created, initialized, and destroyed. The Servlet Life Cycle includes the following phases:

- Load and Initialize: The Servlet is loaded into memory and its init() method is called.

- Service Requests: The Servlet's service() method is called to process requests from clients.

- Destroy: The Servlet's destroy() method is called when the Servlet is being shut down.

3. init() Method: The init() method is called when the Servlet is first loaded into memory, and is used to initialize the Servlet.

4. service() Method: The service() method is called to process requests from clients, and is used to generate dynamic content for the client.

5. destroy() Method: The destroy() method is called when the Servlet is being shut down, and is used to clean up any resources used by the Servlet.

In this unit, you will learn about the Servlet interface and the Servlet Life Cycle, including the init(), service(), and destroy() methods, and the phases of the Servlet Life Cycle.

This unit will provide a foundation for understanding the principles and practices of Servlets and for exploring the various concepts and techniques used in the field of Web Technology.
