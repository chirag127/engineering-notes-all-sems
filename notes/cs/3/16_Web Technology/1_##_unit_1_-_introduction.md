## Unit 1 - Introduction

The Unit 1 - Introduction of the subject of Software Engineering should cover the following topics:

1. Definition of Software Engineering (the process of designing, developing, and maintaining software systems)
2. Overview of Software Engineering (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
3. History of Software Engineering (the evolution of software development and its impact on the industry)
4. Overview of the History of Software Engineering (key events, milestones, and trends)
5. Software Engineering Process Models (e.g. waterfall, agile, etc.)
6. Overview of Software Engineering Process Models (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
7. Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC) (e.g. requirements gathering, design, implementation, testing, etc.)
8. Overview of Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC) (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
9. Software Development Methodologies (e.g. Scrum, Kanban, etc.)
10. Overview of Software Development Methodologies (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
11. Software Engineering in industry applications (how Software Engineering is used in various industries)
12. Further reading and resources (books, articles, online resources)

The Unit 1 - Introduction is a crucial component of the subject of Software Engineering. Understanding the principles and techniques of software engineering helps students to develop the skills required to design, develop, and maintain software systems. The hands-on practice and further reading resources will help students to develop their skills and deepen their understanding of the material.
