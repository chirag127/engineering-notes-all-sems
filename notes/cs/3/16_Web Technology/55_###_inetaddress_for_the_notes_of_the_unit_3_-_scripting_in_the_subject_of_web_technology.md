### InetAddress for the notes of the Unit 3 - Scripting in the subject of Web Technology

The InetAddress section of the Unit 3 - Scripting in the subject of Web Technology should cover the following topics:

1. Definition of InetAddress (a class in the Java programming language that represents an IP address)
2. Overview of InetAddress (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
3. InetAddress Methods (e.g. getByName, getLocalHost, etc.)
4. Overview of InetAddress Methods (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
5. InetAddress and Networking (e.g. resolving host names, connecting to servers, etc.)
6. Overview of InetAddress and Networking (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
7. InetAddress and Sockets (e.g. using InetAddress with sockets)
8. Overview of InetAddress and Sockets (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
9. InetAddress and Internet Addresses (e.g. IPv4, IPv6)
10. Overview of InetAddress and Internet Addresses (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
11. InetAddress in industry applications (how InetAddress is used in various industries)
12. Further reading and resources (books, articles, online resources)

InetAddress is an important component of the subject of Web Technology. Understanding the principles and techniques of InetAddress helps students to develop the skills required to create and implement networked applications. The hands-on practice and further reading resources will help students to develop their skills and deepen their understanding of the material.
