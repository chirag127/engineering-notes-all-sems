### Images for the notes of the Unit 2 - Web Page Designing in the subject of Web Technology

In the world of web page designing, images play a crucial role in creating an engaging and attractive website. Here are some important points to keep in mind when using images in web page designing:

- Choose the right format: The most commonly used image formats for web pages are JPEG, PNG, and GIF. JPEG is best for photographs, PNG is ideal for graphics with transparent backgrounds, and GIF is perfect for animated images.
- Optimize image size: Large images can slow down your website's loading time, so it's essential to optimize your images for web use. You can use image compression tools to reduce the file size without compromising the quality of the image.
- Use high-quality images: Blurry or pixelated images can ruin the look of your website, so it's essential to use high-quality images. You can either take your own photographs or use stock images from reputable websites.
- Add alt text: Alt text is a brief description of the image that appears when the image cannot be displayed. Alt text helps search engines understand the content of the image and improves the accessibility of your website.
- Consider image placement: The placement of images on your website can affect the overall design and user experience. You can use images to break up large blocks of text, draw attention to important information, or create a visual hierarchy.
- Be mindful of copyright: It's important to ensure that you have the legal right to use any images on your website. You can either take your own photographs, use royalty-free images, or purchase a license to use copyrighted images.

By following these tips, you can effectively use images to enhance the design and functionality of your website. Remember to always consider the user experience and accessibility when incorporating images into your web page design.
