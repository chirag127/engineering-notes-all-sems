### Manipulating for the notes of the Unit 4 - Enterprise Java Bean in the subject of Web Technology

1. Enterprise JavaBeans (EJB) is a server-side software component architecture for modular construction of enterprise applications.
2. EJB supports transactions, security, scalability, multithreading, and pooling.
3. EJB containers provide services to enterprise beans and manage their life cycle.
4. The two types of enterprise beans are:
- Session beans: Used for task-oriented applications. Two types: stateless and stateful session beans.
- Message-driven beans: Used to process Java Message Service (JMS) messages asynchronously.
5. Clients access enterprise beans through interfaces. Enterprise beans access resources such as databases through JNDI.
6. Annotations or XML deployment descriptors are used to configure enterprise beans.
7. The EJB timer service allows enterprise beans to schedule tasks to occur at a specific time or after a specific interval.
