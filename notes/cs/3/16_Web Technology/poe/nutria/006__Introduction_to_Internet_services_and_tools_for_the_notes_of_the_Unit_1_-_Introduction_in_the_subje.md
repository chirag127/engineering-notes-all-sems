
### Introduction to Internet Services and Tools

1. The internet is a global network of computers that enables users to share information and access services. 
2. The World Wide Web (WWW) is a system of interlinked hypertext documents accessed through the internet. It is the most popular way to access information online.
3. Search engines are programs that search for information on the internet. Examples include Google, Bing, and Yahoo.
4. Email is a service that allows users to send and receive messages electronically.
5. Social media are online platforms that allow users to communicate and share content. Examples include Facebook, Twitter, and Instagram.
6. Online forums are websites where users can discuss topics and ask questions.
7. Online shopping is a service that allows users to purchase goods and services over the internet.
8. Cloud computing is a service that allows users to access data and applications over the internet.
9. Web hosting is a service that allows users to store and access websites on the internet.
10. Domain names are used to identify websites on the internet.
