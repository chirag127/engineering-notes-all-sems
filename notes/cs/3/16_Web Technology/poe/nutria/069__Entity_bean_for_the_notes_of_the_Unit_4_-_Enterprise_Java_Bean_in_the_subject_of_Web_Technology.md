
### Entity Bean

* Entity beans are Java objects that represent persistent data in a database. 
* Entity beans are used to store and retrieve data from a database. 
* Entity beans can be used to create, read, update, and delete (CRUD) records in the database.
* Entity beans are divided into two types: Container Managed Persistence (CMP) and Bean Managed Persistence (BMP). 
* CMP beans are managed by the container and the container handles the persistence of the bean. 
* BMP beans are managed by the bean itself and the bean is responsible for saving and retrieving its data. 
* Entity beans are typically used in applications that require a high degree of transactional integrity. 
* Entity beans can be used to create distributed applications, with the bean being deployed on multiple servers.
