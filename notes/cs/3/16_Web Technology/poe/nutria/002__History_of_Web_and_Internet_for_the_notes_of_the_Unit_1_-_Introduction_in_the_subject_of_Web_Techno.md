
### History of Web and Internet

* The first concept of the World Wide Web was proposed by British scientist Tim Berners-Lee in 1989.
* The World Wide Web was first made available to the public in 1991.
* The Internet was created in the late 1960s and early 1970s as a way to connect computers at different universities.
* In 1990, the World Wide Web was developed as a way to access information on the Internet.
* The first web browser was released in 1993 and was called Mosaic.
* In 1994, the first search engine, WebCrawler, was released.
* In 1995, the first online store was created.
* In 1998, Google was launched, revolutionizing the way people search for information.
* In 1999, the first online auction site, eBay, was launched.
* In 2000, the dot-com bubble burst, signaling the end of the internet boom of the late 1990s.
* Since then, the internet has continued to evolve and become an integral part of everyday life.
