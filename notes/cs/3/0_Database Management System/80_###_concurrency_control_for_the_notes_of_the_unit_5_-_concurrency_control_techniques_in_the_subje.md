### Concurrency Control for the notes of the Unit 5 - Concurrency Control Techniques in the subject of Database Management System

Concurrency control is a critical component of database management systems that ensures that multiple transactions are executed in a manner that preserves the consistency of the database. In the Unit 5 - Concurrency Control Techniques in the subject of Database Management System, students learn about the principles and techniques of concurrency control. The notes for this unit should include the following topics:

1. Definition of concurrency control (the ability to manage the execution of multiple transactions in a manner that preserves the consistency of the database)
2. Overview of concurrency control techniques (locking, timestamp ordering, conflict serializability)
3. Techniques for implementing locking (shared locks, exclusive locks, lock granularity)
4. Techniques for implementing timestamp ordering (assigning timestamps to transactions, resolving conflicts)
5. Techniques for implementing conflict serializability (detecting and resolving conflicts between transactions)
6. Overview of deadlocks (circular wait, starvation, detection and resolution)
7. Hands-on practice with programming exercises (implementing concurrency control, testing and debugging)
8. Further reading and resources (books, online tutorials, forums)

Concurrency control is an essential component of database management systems and is critical for preserving the consistency of the database. Understanding the principles and techniques of concurrency control helps students to develop the skills required to manage transactions effectively and efficiently in a variety of contexts. The hands-on practice and further reading resources will help students to develop their skills and deepen their understanding of the material.
