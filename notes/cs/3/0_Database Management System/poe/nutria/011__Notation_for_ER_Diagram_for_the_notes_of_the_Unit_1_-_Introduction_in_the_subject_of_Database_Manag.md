
### Notation for ER Diagram

1. Entities: Entities in an ER diagram are represented by rectangles. Each entity is uniquely identified by a set of attributes, which are represented by ovals.

2. Relationships: Relationships in an ER diagram are represented by diamond shapes. A relationship can be one-to-one, one-to-many, or many-to-many.

3. Attributes: Attributes are represented by ovals. Each attribute is associated with an entity.

4. Cardinality: Cardinality is represented by lines connecting entities and relationships. It indicates the number of instances of one entity that can be associated with one instance of another entity.

5. Primary Keys: Primary keys are represented by underlined attributes. They are used to uniquely identify an entity.

6. Foreign Keys: Foreign keys are represented by double-lined attributes. They are used to link two entities together.
