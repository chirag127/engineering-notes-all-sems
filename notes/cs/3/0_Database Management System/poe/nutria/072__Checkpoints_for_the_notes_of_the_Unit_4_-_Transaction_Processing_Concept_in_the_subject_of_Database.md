
### Checkpoints for the Notes of Unit 4 - Transaction Processing Concept in Database Management System

1. Transactions are units of work that are performed within a database and can be thought of as a single logical operation.
2. A transaction is an atomic unit of work that must be completed in its entirety or not at all.
3. A transaction must be isolated from other transactions in order to ensure data integrity.
4. The ACID properties are the four key characteristics of a transaction: atomicity, consistency, isolation, and durability.
5. Atomicity requires that all operations within a transaction must be completed or none of them will be.
6. Consistency ensures that a transaction will not leave the database in an inconsistent state.
7. Isolation ensures that the results of a transaction are not visible to other transactions until the transaction is complete.
8. Durability ensures that the effects of a transaction will persist even if the system fails.
9. The two-phase commit protocol is a method of ensuring that transactions are properly committed or rolled back in a distributed system.
10. The transaction log is the record of all transactions that have been performed in the database.
