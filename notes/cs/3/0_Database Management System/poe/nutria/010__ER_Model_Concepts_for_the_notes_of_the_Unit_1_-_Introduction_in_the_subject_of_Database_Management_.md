
### ER Model Concepts for the Notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction in the Subject of Database Management System

1. Entity-Relationship (ER) model is a data modeling technique used to represent the entities and their relationships in a database.

2. An entity is an object, person, place, event, or concept that is represented in the database.

3. A relationship is the connection between two entities.

4. An ER diagram is a graphical representation of the entities and their relationships.

5. A primary key is a unique identifier for an entity.

6. A foreign key is a primary key of one entity that is used to reference another entity.

7. Normalization is the process of organizing a database to minimize redundancy and improve data integrity.

8. A database schema is the structure of a database, including the tables, fields, and relationships.
