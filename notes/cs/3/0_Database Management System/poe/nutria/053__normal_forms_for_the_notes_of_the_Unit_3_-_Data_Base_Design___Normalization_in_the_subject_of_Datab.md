
### Normal Forms for Unit 3 - Database Design & Normalization
1. Normalization is the process of organizing data in a database in a way that minimizes redundancy and dependency.
2. Normalization typically involves dividing large tables into smaller, more manageable tables and defining relationships between them.
3. Normalization is an important concept in database design, as it helps reduce data redundancy and improve data integrity.
4. The most common normal forms are the first, second, and third normal forms.
5. The first normal form (1NF) is the most basic form of normalization and involves organizing data into tables with columns and rows.
6. The second normal form (2NF) requires that all non-key attributes are dependent on the primary key.
7. The third normal form (3NF) requires that all non-key attributes are dependent on the primary key and that there are no transitive dependencies.
8. The fourth normal form (4NF) requires that all non-key attributes are dependent on the primary key and that there are no multivalued dependencies.
9. The fifth normal form (5NF) requires that all non-key attributes are dependent on the primary key and that there are no join dependencies.
