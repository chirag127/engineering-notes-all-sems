### Validation Based Protocol for the notes of the Unit 5 - Concurrency Control Techniques in the subject of Database Management System

The Validation Based Protocol section of the Unit 5 - Concurrency Control Techniques in the subject of Database Management System should cover the following topics:

1. Definition of validation-based protocol (a concurrency control technique that ensures the consistency of the database by validating transactions before they are executed)
2. Overview of validation-based protocol (how it works, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
3. Techniques for implementing validation-based protocol (locking, timestamp ordering, conflict serializability)
4. Overview of locking (shared locks, exclusive locks, lock granularity)
5. Overview of timestamp ordering (assigning timestamps to transactions, resolving conflicts)
6. Overview of conflict serializability (detecting and resolving conflicts between transactions)
7. Hands-on practice with programming exercises (implementing validation-based protocol, testing and debugging)
8. Further reading and resources (books, online tutorials, forums)

Validation-based protocol is a critical component of concurrency control in database management systems. Understanding the principles and techniques of validation-based protocol helps students to develop the skills required to manage transactions effectively and efficiently in a variety of contexts. The hands-on practice and further reading resources will help students to develop their skills and deepen their understanding of the material.
