### Conflict & View Serializable Schedule for the notes of the Unit 4 - Transaction Processing Concept in the subject of Database Management System

Conflict and view serializable schedule are two important concepts in transaction processing in database management systems. 

1. Conflict: Conflict occurs when two or more transactions attempt to modify the same data at the same time. Conflicts can cause data inconsistencies and errors, and they must be resolved in order to ensure the consistency and integrity of data in a database.

2. View Serializable Schedule: A view serializable schedule is a schedule of transactions that is equivalent to a serial execution of the transactions. A view serializable schedule ensures that the results of the transactions are equivalent to the results of a serial execution of the transactions, and it is used to prevent conflicts and ensure the consistency and integrity of data in a database.

In this unit, you will learn about conflict and view serializable schedule in transaction processing, including the causes of conflict, the methods for resolving conflict, and the importance of view serializable schedules in ensuring the consistency and integrity of data in a database. This will provide a foundation for understanding the principles and practices of transaction processing, and for exploring the various concepts and techniques used in database management systems.
