## Unit 1 - Introduction

Unit 1 - Introduction is the first unit of a course or program that provides an overview of the subject matter and sets the foundation for the rest of the course. The main objectives of Unit 1 - Introduction are to:

1. Provide a general overview of the subject matter: This includes a brief description of what the course will cover, the main topics and themes, and the overall structure of the course.

2. Set the context: This involves providing background information on the subject matter and explaining why it is important and relevant to the students.

3. Establish learning objectives: This includes outlining what the students will learn and be able to do by the end of the course, and how the course will help them achieve their goals.

4. Provide a roadmap for the rest of the course: This involves outlining the main topics and themes of each unit, and how they fit together to form a coherent whole.

5. Establish expectations for student engagement: This includes outlining what is expected of the students in terms of participation, preparation, and assessment.

In conclusion, Unit 1 - Introduction is an essential component of any course or program, as it sets the foundation for the rest of the course and provides students with a clear understanding of what they will learn and be able to do by the end of the course.
