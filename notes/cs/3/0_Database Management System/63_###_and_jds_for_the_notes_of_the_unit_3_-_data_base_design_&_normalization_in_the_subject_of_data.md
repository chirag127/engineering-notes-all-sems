### and JDs for the notes of the Unit 3 - Data Base Design & Normalization in the subject of Database Management System

Unit 3 of the Database Management System course focuses on Data Base Design & Normalization, including the aspect of Job Descriptions (JDs).

Job Descriptions (JDs) are detailed descriptions of the duties, responsibilities, and requirements of a specific job or position. JDs are used in the hiring process to help identify the ideal candidate for a job, and to ensure that the job requirements and responsibilities are clearly defined.

In the context of the Database Management System course, students in Unit 3 may learn about JDs as part of the discussion on Data Base Design & Normalization. This may include working with examples of JDs, analyzing the use of JDs in the hiring process, and learning how to create effective JDs for different types of jobs and positions.
