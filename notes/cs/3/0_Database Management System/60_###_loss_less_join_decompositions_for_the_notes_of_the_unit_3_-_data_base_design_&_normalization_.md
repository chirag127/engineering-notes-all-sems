### loss less join decompositions for the notes of the Unit 3 - Data Base Design & Normalization in the subject of Database Management System

Lossless join decomposition is a process in database design and normalization that involves breaking down a relation into two or more smaller relations, while ensuring that the original relation can be reconstructed from the smaller relations. This is accomplished by ensuring that the join of the smaller relations produces the original relation, without any loss of information.

Lossless join decomposition is used to reduce data redundancy and improve data consistency in a database. It is a key step in the normalization process, and it is used to eliminate repeating groups and partial dependencies in a database.

The general process of lossless join decomposition involves:

1. Identifying the functional dependencies in the relation.
2. Breaking down the relation into smaller relations based on the functional dependencies.
3. Ensuring that the join of the smaller relations produces the original relation, without any loss of information.
