### Multiple Granularity for the notes of the Unit 5 - Concurrency Control Techniques in the subject of Database Management System

Unit 5 of the Database Management System course focuses on Concurrency Control Techniques, including the aspect of Multiple Granularity.

Multiple granularity refers to the level of detail at which concurrency control is applied in a database system. In a database system, concurrency control is used to ensure that multiple transactions can access and modify data in a database simultaneously without causing conflicts or inconsistencies.

Multiple granularity refers to the granularity, or level of detail, at which concurrency control is applied. For example, concurrency control can be applied at the level of individual data items, or at the level of entire transactions. The choice of granularity depends on the specific requirements of the database system and the types of transactions that will be executed.

In the context of the Database Management System course, students in Unit 5 will learn about multiple granularity as part of the discussion on Concurrency Control Techniques. This may include working with examples of different levels of granularity, analyzing the benefits and challenges of different levels of granularity, and learning how to choose the appropriate level of granularity for a specific database system.
