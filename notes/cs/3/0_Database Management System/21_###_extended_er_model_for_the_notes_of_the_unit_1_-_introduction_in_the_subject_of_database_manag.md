### Extended ER Model for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction in the subject of Database Management System

The Extended ER Model is an extension of the Entity-Relationship (ER) Model and is used to represent the more complex relationships between entities in a database management system. In the context of Unit 1 - Introduction in the subject of Database Management System, the Extended ER Model is used to represent the relationships between entities such as students, courses, and instructors.

1. Entities: The entities in the Extended ER Model are the objects or concepts that are stored in the database. In the context of Unit 1, these entities may include students, courses, and instructors.

2. Attributes: Attributes are the properties or characteristics of entities. For example, a student entity may have attributes such as name, student ID, and major.

3. Relationships: Relationships are the connections between entities. In the context of Unit 1, relationships may include a student taking a course, or an instructor teaching a course.

4. Cardinality: Cardinality refers to the number of instances of one entity that are associated with a single instance of another entity. For example, a student may take many courses, but a course may be taken by only one student. This relationship is known as a one-to-many relationship.

5. Weak Entities: Weak entities are entities that cannot exist without being related to another entity. For example, a grade may not exist without being related to a student and a course.

6. Inheritance: Inheritance is a relationship between entities where one entity is a subclass of another entity. For example, a graduate student may be a subclass of a student entity.

7. Aggregation: Aggregation is a relationship between entities where one entity is a part of another entity. For example, a course may be a part of a department.

By using the Extended ER Model, a database designer can represent the complex relationships between entities in a database management system. This helps to ensure that the data is stored in a logical and organized manner, making it easier to retrieve and manipulate.
