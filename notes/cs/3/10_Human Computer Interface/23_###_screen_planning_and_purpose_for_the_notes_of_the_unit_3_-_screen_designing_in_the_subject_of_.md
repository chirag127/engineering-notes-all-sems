### Screen planning and purpose for the notes of the Unit 3 - Screen Designing in the subject of Human Computer Interface

Unit 3 - Screen Designing:
- Screen Planning: The process of determining the content and organization of a screen for a particular task or application.
- Purpose of Screen Planning:
  1. To ensure that the screen is organized and easy to understand.
  2. To maximize the use of screen space and reduce clutter.
  3. To ensure that all necessary information is included on the screen.
  4. To ensure that the screen is aesthetically pleasing and consistent with the company's brand.

Note: Screen planning is a critical step in the design of effective and user-friendly Human-Computer Interfaces (HCI). This unit covers the process of screen planning and the purpose of screen planning, including the organization and clarity of the screen, the use of screen space, the inclusion of necessary information, and the aesthetic appeal of the screen.
