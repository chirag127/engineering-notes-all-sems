### Human interaction speeds for the notes of the Unit 2 - Design process in the subject of Human Computer Interface

Unit 2 - Design Process:
- Human Interaction Speeds:
  1. Perception: The time it takes for a user to perceive and process information on the screen.
  2. Response time: The time it takes for a user to respond to a prompt or request on the screen.
  3. Latency: The time it takes for a system to respond to a user's input.

Note: Human interaction speeds are important factors in the design of user interfaces, as they determine how quickly and effectively users can perform tasks and interact with the system. This unit covers the key concepts of perception, response time, and latency, and how they impact the design of user interfaces.
