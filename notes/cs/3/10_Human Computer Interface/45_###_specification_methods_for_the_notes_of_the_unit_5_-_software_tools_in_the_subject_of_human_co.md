### Specification methods for the notes of the Unit 5 - Software tools in the subject of Human Computer Interface

Unit 5 - Software Tools:
- Specification Methods:
  1. Natural Language: Descriptions of the system using everyday language.
  2. Flowcharts: Graphical representations of the system's processes and workflows.
  3. State Transition Diagrams: Diagrams that show the states and transitions of the system.
  4. Data Flow Diagrams: Diagrams that show the flow of data within the system.
  5. Entity-Relationship Diagrams: Diagrams that show the relationships between entities in the system.

Note: Specification methods are used to describe and define the behavior and functionality of a system. This unit covers the key specification methods, including natural language, flowcharts, state transition diagrams, data flow diagrams, and entity-relationship diagrams. The appropriate specification method will depend on the specific needs and goals of the system and the stakeholders involved.
