### definition for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction in the subject of Human Computer Interface

Unit 1 - Introduction:

- Definition of Human-Computer Interface (HCI): HCI refers to the way in which humans interact with computers and digital devices.

- Types of HCI: Graphical User Interfaces (GUI), Command Line Interfaces (CLI), Natural User Interfaces (NUI), etc.

- Importance of HCI: HCI is critical for the design and development of effective and user-friendly digital devices and systems.

- Objectives of HCI: The objectives of HCI include improving the usability, efficiency, and satisfaction of users when interacting with digital devices and systems.

Note: This unit provides an overview of the field of Human-Computer Interface and its importance in the design and development of digital devices and systems. It covers the definition, types, and objectives of HCI, and provides a foundation for the study of HCI in the course.
