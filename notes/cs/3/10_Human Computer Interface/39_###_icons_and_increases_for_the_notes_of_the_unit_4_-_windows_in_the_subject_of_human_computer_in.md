### Icons and increases for the notes of the Unit 4 - Windows in the subject of Human Computer Interface

Unit 4 of the Human Computer Interface course focuses on Windows and covers the aspect of Icons and Menus.

Icons are small graphical images that represent applications, files, and other objects in a graphical user interface (GUI). Icons provide a visual representation of objects and allow users to quickly identify and access the objects they need.

Menus are lists of options or commands that are used to access and manage objects in a GUI. Menus can be accessed using a mouse, keyboard, or other input device, and can be organized into hierarchical structures to make it easier for users to find the options and commands they need.

In the context of the Human Computer Interface course, students in Unit 4 will learn about Icons and Menus as part of the discussion on Windows. This may include working with examples of Icons and Menus, analyzing the benefits and limitations of Icons and Menus, and learning how to design and implement Icons and Menus in a GUI.
