### speech recognition digitization and generation for the notes of the Unit 5 - Software tools in the subject of Human Computer Interface

Unit 5 - Software Tools:
- Speech Recognition: Technology that allows users to control computer systems and applications using voice commands.
- Digitization: The process of converting analog information into digital form, making it easier to store, process, and transmit.
- Text Generation: The process of automatically generating text using algorithms and machine learning models.

Note: Speech recognition, digitization, and text generation are important software tools that can enhance the user experience and provide new ways of interacting with computer systems and applications. This unit covers the key concepts and techniques of speech recognition, digitization, and text generation and their role in creating effective and user-friendly interfaces.
