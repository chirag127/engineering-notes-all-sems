### importance of 8 human characteristics human for the notes of the Unit 2 - Design process in the subject of Human Computer Interface

Unit 2 - Design Process:
- Importance of Understanding Human Characteristics:
  1. Perception: How humans perceive and interpret information
  2. Attention: How humans allocate attention to different aspects of the interface
  3. Memory: How humans store and retrieve information
  4. Problem solving: How humans approach and solve problems
  5. Motor skills: How humans move and interact with the interface
  6. Emotion: How emotions influence the user experience
  7. Culture: How cultural background affects user behavior and preferences
  8. Experience: How prior experience affects user behavior and expectations

Note: Understanding human characteristics is critical for designing effective and user-friendly Human-Computer Interfaces (HCI). This unit covers the importance of considering perception, attention, memory, problem solving, motor skills, emotion, culture, and experience in the HCI design process.
