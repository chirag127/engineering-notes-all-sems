## Unit 3 - Screen Designing

Unit 3 - Screen Designing:
- Overview of screen design
- Principles of screen design
- Types of screen layouts
- Designing for different screen sizes and resolutions
- Designing for accessibility and usability
- Best practices for screen design

Note: Screen design is a critical aspect of human-computer interface design, as it determines how users interact with digital devices and systems. This unit covers the key concepts and principles of screen design, including screen layouts, designing for different screen sizes and resolutions, designing for accessibility and usability, and best practices for screen design.
