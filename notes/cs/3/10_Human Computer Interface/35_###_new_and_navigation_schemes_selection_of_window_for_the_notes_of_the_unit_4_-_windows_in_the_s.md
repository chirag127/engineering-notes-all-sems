### New and Navigation schemes selection of window for the notes of the Unit 4 - Windows in the subject of Human Computer Interface

Unit 4 - Windows:
- New and Navigation Schemes:
  1. New Scheme: A method for creating new windows or documents within an application.
  2. Navigation Scheme: A method for navigating between windows or documents within an application.
  3. Selection of Window: The process of selecting the appropriate new and navigation scheme for a particular application, based on the needs and goals of the user and the business.

Note: New and navigation schemes are critical components of window design in Human-Computer Interfaces (HCI), as they determine how users create and navigate between windows and documents within an application. This unit covers the key concepts of new and navigation schemes, and provides guidelines for selecting the appropriate new and navigation scheme for a particular application.
