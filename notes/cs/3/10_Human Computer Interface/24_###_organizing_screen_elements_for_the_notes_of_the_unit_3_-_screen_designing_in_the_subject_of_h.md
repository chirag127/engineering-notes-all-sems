### organizing screen elements for the notes of the Unit 3 - Screen Designing in the subject of Human Computer Interface

Unit 3 - Screen Designing:
- Organizing Screen Elements:
  1. Hierarchy: Arranging elements in order of importance, with the most important elements at the top and the least important at the bottom.
  2. Alignment: Aligning elements with each other and with the overall design of the screen.
  3. Proximity: Grouping elements that are related to each other to create a visual connection.
  4. Repetition: Repeating design elements, such as color, font, and imagery, to create a consistent and cohesive look.
  5. Contrast: Using contrasting elements, such as color and size, to draw attention to important elements.

Note: Effective screen design requires a well-organized layout of screen elements. This unit covers the key principles of organizing screen elements, including hierarchy, alignment, proximity, repetition, and contrast.
