### statistical graphics for the notes of the Unit 3 - Screen Designing in the subject of Human Computer Interface

The Statistical Graphics section of the Unit 3 - Screen Designing in the subject of Human Computer Interface should cover the following topics:

1. Definition of Statistical Graphics (visual representation of data, used to communicate information)
2. Overview of Statistical Graphics (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
3. Types of Statistical Graphics (e.g. bar charts, line charts, scatter plots, etc.)
4. Overview of Types of Statistical Graphics (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
5. Design Principles for Statistical Graphics (e.g. clarity, simplicity, functionality, etc.)
6. Overview of Design Principles for Statistical Graphics (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
7. Limitations of Statistical Graphics (e.g. complexity, accuracy, compatibility, etc.)
8. Techniques for overcoming limitations (e.g. using advanced techniques, improving accuracy, etc.)
9. Statistical Graphics in Industry Applications (how statistical graphics are used in various industries)
10. Further reading and resources (books, articles, online resources)

Statistical Graphics is an important component of the subject of Human Computer Interface. Understanding the principles and techniques of statistical graphics helps students to develop the skills required to design and implement effective visual representations of data. The hands-on practice and further reading resources will help students to develop their skills and deepen their understanding of the material.
