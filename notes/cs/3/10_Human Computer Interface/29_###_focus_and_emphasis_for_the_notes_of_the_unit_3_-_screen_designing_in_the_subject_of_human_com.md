### focus and emphasis for the notes of the Unit 3 - Screen Designing in the subject of Human Computer Interface

Unit 3 - Screen Designing:
- Focus and Emphasis:
  1. Focus: Drawing the user's attention to the most important element on the screen.
  2. Emphasis: Enhancing the visual impact of an element to make it stand out.

Techniques for creating focus and emphasis:
  1. Contrast: Using color, size, and texture to create contrast between elements.
  2. Proximity: Grouping related elements together to create a visual hierarchy.
  3. Alignment: Aligning elements to create a visually balanced composition.
  4. Repetition: Repeating design elements to create a consistent and cohesive look.
  
Note: Focus and emphasis are important concepts in screen design, as they help to guide the user's attention and create a visually appealing and effective interface. This unit covers the techniques for creating focus and emphasis, including contrast, proximity, alignment, and repetition.
