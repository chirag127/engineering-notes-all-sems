### A brief history of Screen design for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction in the subject of Human Computer Interface

Screen Design: A brief history

1. Early Computers: In the early days of computing, screens were limited to simple text displays and lacked graphics capabilities.

2. Introduction of Graphical User Interfaces (GUI): In the 1980s, Apple introduced the Macintosh computer with a graphical user interface, which allowed users to interact with the computer using icons and images.

3. Web Design: With the rise of the internet, web design became an important aspect of screen design, with designers creating visually appealing and user-friendly websites.

4. Mobile Devices: With the advent of smartphones and tablets, screen design shifted to accommodate smaller screens and touch-based interfaces.

5. Responsive Design: With the increasing use of multiple devices, responsive design emerged as a way to create websites and applications that adapt to different screen sizes and resolutions.

Note: Screen design has evolved over the years to accommodate changing technology and user needs, with a focus on creating visually appealing and user-friendly interfaces.
