### Visually pleasing composition for the notes of the Unit 3 - Screen Designing in the subject of Human Computer Interface

Unit 3 - Screen Designing:
- Visually Pleasing Composition:
  1. Balance: A harmonious arrangement of elements in a design.
  2. Proximity: The placement of elements close to each other to indicate a relationship.
  3. Alignment: The arrangement of elements along a common line or axis.
  4. Contrast: The use of differences in color, size, and texture to draw attention and create visual interest.
  5. Repetition: The repetition of design elements to create a sense of unity and consistency.

Note: Visually pleasing composition is an important aspect of screen design, as it helps to create an attractive and engaging interface. This unit covers the key principles of visually pleasing composition, including balance, proximity, alignment, contrast, and repetition.
