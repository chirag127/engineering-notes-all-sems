### characteristics for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction in the subject of Human Computer Interface

Unit 1 - Introduction:
- Characteristics of a Good User Interface:
  1. User-centered: Focuses on the needs and goals of the user.
  2. Intuitive: Easy to understand and use without extensive training.
  3. Consistent: Follows established conventions and standards.
  4. Efficient: Allows users to complete tasks quickly and easily.
  5. Customizable: Allows users to personalize the interface to their needs and preferences.
  6. Accessible: Supports users with disabilities and different abilities.

Note: The characteristics of a good user interface are critical for ensuring the effectiveness and usability of a computer system. A user-centered, intuitive, consistent, efficient, customizable, and accessible user interface is essential for a positive user experience.
