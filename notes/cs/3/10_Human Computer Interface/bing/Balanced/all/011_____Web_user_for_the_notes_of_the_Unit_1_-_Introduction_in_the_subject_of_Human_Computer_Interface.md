## Human Computer Interface (HCI)

- HCI is the study of interaction between people (users) and computers  .
- HCI deals with the design, execution and assessment of computer systems and related phenomenon that are for human use .
- HCI can be used in all disciplines wherever there is a possibility of computer installation.
- HCI aims to improve the interactions between users and computers by making computers more usable and receptive to the user's needs.
- HCI also has a long term goal of designing systems that minimize the barrier between the human's cognitive model of what they want to accomplish and the computer's understanding of the user's task.

## Foundations of HCI

- The foundations of HCI consist of three main components: the human, the computer and the interaction.
- The human component involves the study of human characteristics such as input/output channels, memory, reasoning and problem solving.
- The computer component involves the study of computer characteristics such as devices, memory, processing and networks.
- The interaction component involves the study of interaction models, frameworks, ergonomics, styles, elements, interactivity and paradigms.

## Scope and Challenges of HCI and Interaction Design

- HCI and Interaction Design are interdisciplinary fields that draw from various disciplines such as computer science, psychology, sociology, design, engineering and more.
- HCI and Interaction Design face various challenges such as dealing with complex and dynamic systems, understanding and supporting diverse users and contexts, designing for multiple and evolving platforms and devices, and evaluating and measuring user experience and satisfaction.
- HCI and Interaction Design also have various opportunities such as creating new and innovative forms of interaction, enhancing human capabilities and well-being, supporting social and collaborative activities, and contributing to societal and environmental issues.
