# Definition

- Human Computer Interface (HCI) is the study of how people interact with computers and other technologies.
- HCI involves designing, evaluating, and implementing user interfaces that are efficient, effective, and satisfying for the users.
- HCI also considers the social, organizational, and cultural aspects of human-computer interaction, as well as the ethical and legal implications.
- HCI is an interdisciplinary field that draws from computer science, psychology, design, engineering, and other disciplines.
- HCI aims to improve the quality of human life and the productivity of work by enhancing the usability and accessibility of computer systems and applications.
