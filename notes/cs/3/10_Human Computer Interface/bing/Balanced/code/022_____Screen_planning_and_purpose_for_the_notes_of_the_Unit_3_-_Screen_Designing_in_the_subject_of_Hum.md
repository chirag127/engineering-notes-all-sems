### Screen planning and purpose

- Screen planning is the process of designing and organizing the layout, content, and functionality of a user interface for a digital product, such as a website, app, or software.
- Screen planning helps to achieve the following purposes:
  - To define the goals and scope of the product and its features.
  - To identify the target users and their needs, preferences, and expectations.
  - To create a consistent and coherent user experience across different screens and devices.
  - To optimize the usability, accessibility, and aesthetics of the product.
  - To facilitate the development and testing of the product by providing a clear and detailed blueprint.
- Screen planning involves the following steps:
  - Research: Gathering and analyzing information about the product's domain, users, competitors, and best practices.
  - Ideation: Generating and evaluating ideas for the product's concept, content, and functionality.
  - Wireframing: Sketching the basic structure and layout of each screen, showing the key elements and interactions.
  - Prototyping: Creating a low-fidelity or high-fidelity mockup of the product, simulating its look and feel and behavior.
  - Testing: Evaluating the prototype with real or potential users, collecting feedback and identifying issues and improvements.
  - Iteration: Refining and revising the prototype based on the testing results and stakeholder input, until the desired outcome is achieved.
