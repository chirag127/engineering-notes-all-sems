### Benefits of good design for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction in the subject of Human Computer Interface

- Good design can enhance the usability, efficiency, and satisfaction of the users who interact with a system or product.
- Good design can also improve the accessibility, inclusivity, and diversity of the user population, by considering the needs and preferences of different groups and individuals.
- Good design can reduce the errors, frustrations, and costs associated with poorly designed systems or products, and increase the trust, loyalty, and satisfaction of the users.
- Good design can also have positive impacts on the environment, society, and economy, by promoting sustainability, social responsibility, and innovation.
