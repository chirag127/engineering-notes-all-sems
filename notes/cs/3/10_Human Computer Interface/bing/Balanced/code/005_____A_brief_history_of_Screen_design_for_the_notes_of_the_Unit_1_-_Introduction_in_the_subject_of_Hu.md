### A brief history of screen design

- Screen design is the process of creating visual interfaces for digital devices, such as computers, smartphones, and tablets.
- Screen design has evolved over time, influenced by technological developments, user needs, and aesthetic trends.
- Some of the major milestones in the history of screen design are:

  - The first screens were cathode ray tube (CRT) displays, which were attached to computers in the 1950s and 1960s. They could only display monochrome text and simple graphics, and had low resolution and contrast.
  - In the 1970s, graphical user interfaces (GUIs) emerged, which allowed users to interact with icons, menus, and windows using a mouse and a keyboard. The first GUI was developed by Xerox PARC, and later popularized by Apple and Microsoft .
  - In the 1980s and 1990s, screen design became more diverse and sophisticated, as different types of devices and applications emerged, such as personal computers, video games, mobile phones, and the World Wide Web. Screen designers experimented with color, typography, animation, and multimedia .
  - In the 2000s and 2010s, screen design was influenced by the rise of touchscreens, social media, and mobile apps. Screen designers focused on usability, accessibility, and responsiveness, and adopted flat design, minimalism, and skeuomorphism as design styles .
  - In the 2020s and beyond, screen design is expected to face new challenges and opportunities, such as virtual reality, augmented reality, voice interfaces, and artificial intelligence. Screen designers will need to consider new dimensions of interaction, emotion, and ethics.
