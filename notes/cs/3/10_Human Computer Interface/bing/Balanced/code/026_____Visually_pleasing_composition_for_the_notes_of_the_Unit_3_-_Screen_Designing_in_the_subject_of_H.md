### Visually pleasing composition for the notes of the Unit 3 - Screen Designing in the subject of Human Computer Interface

- A visually pleasing composition is a way of arranging the elements of your notes in a harmonious and attractive way that conveys the message clearly and quickly.
- A visually pleasing composition can help you to improve your memory, understanding, and creativity by using different colors, shapes, fonts, symbols, and images .
- Some of the principles of a visually pleasing composition are:

  - Balance: Distribute the visual weight of your notes evenly on the page, using symmetry or asymmetry, to create a sense of stability and order.
  - Contrast: Use different colors, sizes, shapes, or textures to create interest and highlight the important points of your notes.
  - Alignment: Align the elements of your notes along a common edge, center, or axis, to create a sense of unity and coherence.
  - Repetition: Repeat the same elements, such as colors, fonts, symbols, or images, to create a sense of consistency and rhythm.
  - Proximity: Group the related elements of your notes close together, and separate the unrelated elements, to create a sense of hierarchy and organization.
  - White space: Leave some empty space around the elements of your notes, to create a sense of clarity and focus.

- Some of the methods of creating a visually pleasing composition for your notes are:

  - Mind mapping: Use a central idea and branch out the related subtopics, keywords, or images, using different colors and shapes, to create a visual representation of your notes.
  - Boxing: Use boxes or rectangles to organize the elements of your notes into categories, subcategories, or bullet points, using different colors and fonts, to create a visual structure of your notes.
  - Sketchnoting: Use sketches, doodles, icons, or diagrams, along with text, to create a visual summary of your notes, using different colors and styles, to create a visual story of your notes.
  - Presentation: Use slides to display the elements of your notes in a sequential and logical order, using different colors, fonts, images, or animations, to create a visual narration of your notes.

- Some of the tips for creating a visually pleasing composition for your notes are:

  - Use a grid or a ruler to align the elements of your notes and create a neat and tidy layout.
  - Use a color scheme or a color wheel to choose the colors that complement or contrast each other and create a harmonious and appealing effect.
  - Use different fonts or font sizes to emphasize the important words or sentences on your notes and create a hierarchy and a focus.
  - Use symbols, abbreviations, or images to avoid long sentences and create a concise and clear expression of your notes.
  - Use headings, subheadings, or labels to organize the elements of your notes and create a structure and a flow.
  - Use arrows, lines, or connectors to link the elements of your notes and create a relationship and a logic.
