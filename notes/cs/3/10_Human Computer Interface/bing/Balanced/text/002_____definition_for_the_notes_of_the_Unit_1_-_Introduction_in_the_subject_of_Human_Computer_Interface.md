### Definition for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction in the subject of Human Computer Interface

- Human Computer Interface (HCI) is the study of how people interact with computers and other digital devices.
- HCI covers the design, evaluation, and implementation of interactive systems that support human activities and goals.
- HCI is an interdisciplinary field that draws from computer science, psychology, design, engineering, and social sciences.
- HCI aims to create user-friendly, efficient, and enjoyable interfaces that meet the needs and preferences of different users and contexts.
- HCI also considers the ethical, social, and environmental implications of technology use and design.
