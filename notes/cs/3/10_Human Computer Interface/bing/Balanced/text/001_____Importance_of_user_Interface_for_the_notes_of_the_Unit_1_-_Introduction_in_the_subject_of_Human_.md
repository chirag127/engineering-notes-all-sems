### Importance of user interface for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction in the subject of Human Computer Interface

- User interface (UI) is the point at which human users interact with a computer, website or application.
- UI enables users to effectively control the computer or device they are interacting with.
- A successful UI should be intuitive, efficient, and user-friendly.
- UI is important to meet user expectations and support the effective functionality of the site or app.
- A well-executed UI facilitates effective interaction between the user and the program, app or machine through contrasting visuals, clean design and responsiveness.
- UI design contributes greatly to the positive user experience, which can attract and retain customers, boost customer satisfaction and loyalty, and increase revenue.
- UI design should follow some qualities, such as clarity, consistency, familiarity, feedback, efficiency, and aesthetics.
- Human Computer Interaction (HCI) is the study of how humans interact with computers and technologies.
- HCI and UI are closely related, as HCI informs the design of UI and evaluates its usability and effectiveness.
- HCI is important to keep humans at the center of the systems and technologies we build, and to ensure that they are accessible, inclusive, and ethical.
