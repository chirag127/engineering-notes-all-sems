### Speech Recognition, Digitization, and Generation

- Speech recognition is the process of converting spoken audio into text or commands that can be understood by a computer system  .
- Speech digitization is the process of converting analog speech signals into digital data that can be stored, transmitted, or manipulated by a computer system .
- Speech generation is the process of converting text or commands into synthetic or natural speech that can be output by a computer system  .

Some of the topics covered in this unit are:

- Speech technology: the different types of speech and audio technologies that can be used for human-computer interaction, such as discrete-word recognition, continuous-speech recognition, voice information systems, speech generation, and non-speech auditory interfaces .
- Speech input: the methods and devices for capturing and processing speech signals, such as microphones, sound cards, speech codecs, feature extraction, and feature vectors .
- Speech output: the methods and devices for producing and delivering speech signals, such as speakers, headphones, speech synthesizers, speech corpora, and speech quality evaluation .
- Speech recognition models: the algorithms and techniques for recognizing and interpreting speech signals, such as acoustic models, pronunciation dictionaries, language models, and decoders .
- Speech generation models: the algorithms and techniques for generating and modifying speech signals, such as text-to-speech, speech-to-speech, speech compression, and speech enhancement  .
- Speech applications: the examples and use cases of speech and audio technologies for various domains and purposes, such as voice assistants, speech transcription, speech translation, speech authentication, and speech emotion recognition  .
