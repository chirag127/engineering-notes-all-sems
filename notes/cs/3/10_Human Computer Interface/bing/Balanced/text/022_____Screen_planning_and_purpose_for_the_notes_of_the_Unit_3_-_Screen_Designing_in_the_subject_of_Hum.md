### Screen planning and purpose

- Screen planning and purpose is the process of designing and organizing the elements of a screen or display that is used as part of human-computer interaction (HCI).
- HCI is the study, planning, and design of how people and computers work together so that a person's needs are satisfied in the most effective way.
- Screen planning and purpose involves the following steps:
  - Define the goals and objectives of the screen, such as the tasks, functions, and information that the user needs to accomplish or access.
  - Identify the target users and their characteristics, such as their skills, preferences, expectations, and limitations.
  - Analyze the context of use, such as the environment, devices, and constraints that affect the screen design.
  - Design the screen layout, structure, and navigation, such as the arrangement, grouping, and sequencing of the screen elements, and the ways the user can move between them.
  - Design the screen elements, such as the text, graphics, icons, buttons, menus, and controls that convey the information and functionality of the screen.
  - Design the screen aesthetics, such as the color, font, style, and animation that enhance the visual appeal and usability of the screen.
  - Evaluate the screen design, such as the testing, feedback, and iteration that ensure the screen meets the goals, objectives, and user needs.
- Screen planning and purpose aims to create screens or displays that are :
  - Intuitive: the screen should be easy to understand and use, without requiring excessive learning or memorization.
  - Usable: the screen should be effective, efficient, and satisfying for the user, without causing errors or frustration.
  - Accessible: the screen should be inclusive and adaptable for different users, devices, and situations, without excluding or discriminating anyone.
  - Aesthetic: the screen should be attractive and engaging for the user, without compromising the functionality or usability.
