### Benefits of good design

- Good design can improve the usability, efficiency, and satisfaction of the users who interact with a system or product.
- Good design can also enhance the aesthetics, appeal, and brand identity of a system or product, making it more attractive and desirable for the users and the stakeholders.
- Good design can reduce the errors, frustrations, and costs associated with poorly designed systems or products, which can negatively affect the user experience and the reputation of the system or product.
- Good design can also increase the accessibility, inclusivity, and diversity of the users who can use a system or product, by considering their needs, preferences, and limitations.
- Good design can also support the goals, values, and ethics of the users and the stakeholders, by aligning the system or product with their expectations and standards.
