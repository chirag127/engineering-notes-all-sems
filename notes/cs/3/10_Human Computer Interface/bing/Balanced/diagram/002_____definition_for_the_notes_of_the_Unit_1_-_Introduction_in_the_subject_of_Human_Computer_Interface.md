# Unit 1 - Introduction

## Definition of Human Computer Interface

- A device that allows interaction between human being and a computer is known as a "Human-computer Interface (HCI)".
- HCI is also a field of research that studies the design and use of computer technology, focusing on the interfaces between people (users) and computers.
- The human-computer interface can be described as the point of communication between the human user and the computer.
- The flow of information between the human and computer is defined as the loop of interaction, which has several aspects, such as:
  - Input: the way the user provides commands or data to the computer, such as keyboard, mouse, touch screen, voice, etc.
  - Output: the way the computer presents results or feedback to the user, such as monitor, printer, speaker, etc.
  - Dialogue: the sequence of actions and responses between the user and the computer, such as menus, buttons, icons, etc.
  - Model: the representation of the system state and functionality that the user and the computer share, such as files, folders, windows, etc.
- Usability of the human-computer interface is the degree to which the design makes using the system effective, efficient, and satisfying.
- HCI is situated at the intersection of computer science, behavioral sciences, design, media studies, and several other fields of study.
