### Design goals for the notes of the Unit 3 - Screen Designing in the subject of Human Computer Interface

- The notes should cover the following topics:
  - The principles of screen design, such as layout, alignment, balance, consistency, and contrast.
  - The guidelines for choosing and using colors, fonts, icons, and graphics on screens.
  - The techniques for designing effective menus, dialogs, forms, and controls on screens.
  - The methods for evaluating and testing screen designs, such as heuristic evaluation, usability testing, and user feedback.
- The notes should be concise, clear, and organized, using headings, subheadings, bullet points, and tables to present the information.
- The notes should include examples, diagrams, and screenshots to illustrate the concepts and show good and bad practices of screen design.
- The notes should provide references and links to additional resources for further reading and learning.
