# Unit 1 - Introduction

## What is Human Computer Interface (HCI)?
- HCI is the study of how people interact with computers and other technologies.
- HCI aims to design, evaluate, and improve the usability, accessibility, and user experience of computer systems and applications.
- HCI involves multiple disciplines, such as computer science, psychology, design, and engineering.

## Why is HCI important?
- HCI can enhance the productivity, efficiency, and satisfaction of users and organizations.
- HCI can prevent or reduce errors, frustrations, and risks associated with human-computer interaction.
- HCI can foster creativity, innovation, and social impact through human-centered design.

## What are the main components of HCI?
- HCI consists of three main components: the user, the computer, and the interaction.
- The user is the human who uses the computer system or application for a specific goal or task.
- The computer is the device or software that provides the functionality and interface for the user.
- The interaction is the process of communication and exchange of information between the user and the computer.

## What are the main challenges of HCI?
- HCI faces many challenges, such as:
  - Understanding the needs, preferences, and abilities of diverse users and contexts.
  - Designing intuitive, effective, and enjoyable interfaces that match the user's expectations and goals.
  - Evaluating the usability, accessibility, and user experience of computer systems and applications.
  - Adapting to the rapid changes and developments in technology and society.
