### Understanding business junctions for the notes of the Unit 2 - Design process in the subject of Human Computer Interface

- Business junctions are the points where different business functions interact with each other or with external entities, such as customers, suppliers, or regulators.
- Business functions are the activities that a business performs to achieve its goals, such as marketing, sales, production, accounting, etc.
- The design process of human computer interface (HCI) is the systematic and iterative approach to create user-friendly and effective interfaces between humans and computers  .
- The design process of HCI involves the following steps:
  - Identify the problem and the user needs
  - Define the requirements and constraints
  - Generate and evaluate alternative solutions
  - Implement and test the chosen solution
  - Collect feedback and refine the solution
- The design process of HCI is guided by the following principles:
  - User-centered design: The user is the focus of the design process and their needs, preferences, and expectations are taken into account throughout the process.
  - Usability: The interface should be easy to learn, use, and remember, and should provide feedback, error prevention, and recovery mechanisms.
  - Accessibility: The interface should be accessible to a diverse range of users, including those with disabilities, and should comply with relevant standards and guidelines.
  - Aesthetics: The interface should be visually appealing, consistent, and coherent, and should convey the intended message and mood.
  - Functionality: The interface should provide the required functionality and performance, and should support the user's tasks and goals.
  - Security: The interface should protect the user's privacy and data, and should prevent unauthorized access or manipulation.
