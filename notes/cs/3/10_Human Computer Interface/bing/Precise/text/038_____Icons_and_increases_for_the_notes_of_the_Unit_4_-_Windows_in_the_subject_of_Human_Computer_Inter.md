### Icons and increases for the notes of the Unit 4 - Windows in the subject of Human Computer Interface

- Icons are graphical representations of objects, actions, or concepts.
- They are used to convey information quickly and efficiently, without the need for text.
- In the context of Windows, icons are used to represent files, folders, applications, and other system objects.
- Icons can be customized to suit the user's preferences, and can be organized into groups for easier access.
- Increases refer to the use of visual cues, such as size, color, or animation, to draw attention to important information or actions.
- In the context of Windows, increases can be used to highlight important system notifications, or to indicate the progress of a task.
- The use of icons and increases can improve the user experience by making it easier to navigate and interact with the system.
