### Pointing Devices

Pointing devices are input devices that allow a user to interact with a computer by moving a cursor or pointer on the screen. Here are some common types of pointing devices:

1. **Mouse**: A mouse is a handheld device that is moved across a flat surface to control the movement of the cursor on the screen. It typically has one or more buttons for clicking and a scroll wheel for scrolling.

2. **Touchpad**: A touchpad is a flat, touch-sensitive surface that is typically found on laptop computers. The user moves their finger across the touchpad to control the movement of the cursor on the screen.

3. **Trackball**: A trackball is similar to a mouse, but instead of moving the entire device, the user moves a ball that is mounted on the top of the device to control the movement of the cursor on the screen.

4. **Stylus**: A stylus is a pen-like device that is used to interact with touchscreens. The user can tap, swipe, and draw on the screen using the stylus.

5. **Joystick**: A joystick is a lever that is mounted on a base and is used to control the movement of the cursor on the screen. It is commonly used for gaming and flight simulation.

These are some of the common pointing devices used in Human-Computer Interaction. Each device has its own advantages and disadvantages, and the choice of device depends on the user's needs and preferences.
