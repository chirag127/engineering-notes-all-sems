### Design goals for the notes of the Unit 3 - Screen Designing in the subject of Human Computer Interface

1. The notes should provide a comprehensive understanding of the principles and techniques of screen designing.
2. The notes should cover the key concepts of screen layout, color, typography, and graphics.
3. The notes should include examples and case studies to illustrate the application of screen design principles in real-world scenarios.
4. The notes should provide practical tips and best practices for designing effective and user-friendly screens.
5. The notes should be well-organized and easy to follow, with clear headings and subheadings to guide the reader.
6. The notes should be written in a clear and concise language, avoiding technical jargon as much as possible.
7. The notes should include diagrams and illustrations to help explain complex concepts and ideas.
8. The notes should be regularly updated to reflect the latest developments and trends in the field of screen design.
9. The notes should include references to additional resources for further reading and exploration.
10. The notes should be designed to facilitate self-study and independent learning, with exercises and review questions to help the reader test their understanding of the material.
