## Unit 1 - Introduction

1. The introduction is the first section of any written work.
2. It provides the reader with an overview of the topic and sets the stage for the rest of the content.
3. The introduction should be clear, concise, and engaging.
4. It should provide the necessary background information and context for the reader to understand the rest of the work.
5. The introduction should also include a thesis statement, which outlines the main argument or purpose of the work.
6. The introduction is an important part of any written work, as it sets the tone and provides the reader with a roadmap for the rest of the content.
