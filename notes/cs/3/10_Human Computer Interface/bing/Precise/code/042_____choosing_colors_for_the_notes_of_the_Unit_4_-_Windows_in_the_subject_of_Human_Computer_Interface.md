### Choosing Colors for the Notes of the Unit 4 - Windows in the Subject of Human Computer Interface

1. When choosing colors for your notes, it is important to consider the **readability** and **legibility** of the text. 
2. Use **high contrast** colors, such as black text on a white background, to make the text easy to read.
3. Avoid using **bright or neon** colors as they can be **distracting** and make it difficult to focus on the content.
4. Consider using **color coding** to organize and categorize information. For example, you could use different colors to represent different topics or sections within the unit.
5. Keep in mind that some people may have **color vision deficiencies**, so it is important to choose colors that are easily distinguishable for all users.
6. Test your color choices by **printing** out your notes or **viewing** them on different devices to ensure that the colors are easily readable and effective in conveying information.
