### Information Retrieval on Web

Information retrieval is the process through which a computer system can respond to a user's query for text-based information on a specific topic. It is one of the most important problems in the domain of natural language processing (NLP) .

In the context of screen designing for human-computer interaction, information retrieval on the web is an important consideration. The design goals for screen designing include screen planning and purpose, organizing screen elements, ordering of screen data and content, screen navigation and flow, visually pleasing composition, amount of information, focus and emphasis, and presentation of information simply and meaningfully  .

Information retrieval on the web can be used to enhance the user experience by providing relevant and easily accessible information. It is important to consider technological considerations in interface design when incorporating information retrieval on the web .
