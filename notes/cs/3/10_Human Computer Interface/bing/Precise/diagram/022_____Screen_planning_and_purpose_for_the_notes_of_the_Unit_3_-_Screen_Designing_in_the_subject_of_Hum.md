### Screen planning and purpose

Screen planning and purpose is an important aspect of screen designing in the subject of Human Computer Interface. It involves the following points:

1. **Defining the purpose of the screen**: The first step in screen planning is to define the purpose of the screen. This involves identifying the tasks that the user will perform on the screen and the information that the screen will display.

2. **Determining the layout of the screen**: Once the purpose of the screen has been defined, the next step is to determine the layout of the screen. This involves deciding on the placement of the various elements on the screen, such as text, images, and buttons.

3. **Choosing the appropriate screen elements**: The next step is to choose the appropriate screen elements, such as text, images, and buttons, that will help the user to perform the tasks and access the information on the screen.

4. **Designing the screen for usability**: The screen should be designed for usability, which means that it should be easy for the user to understand and use. This involves using clear and concise language, providing helpful feedback, and making the screen easy to navigate.

5. **Testing the screen**: Finally, the screen should be tested to ensure that it meets the needs of the user and is easy to use. This involves conducting user testing and making any necessary changes to the screen based on the feedback received.

In summary, screen planning and purpose is a crucial step in the screen designing process, as it helps to ensure that the screen is effective in meeting the needs of the user. It involves defining the purpose of the screen, determining the layout, choosing the appropriate screen elements, designing the screen for usability, and testing the screen.
