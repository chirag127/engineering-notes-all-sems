### Choosing Colors for the Notes of Unit 4 - Windows in the Subject of Human Computer Interface

1. Choose colors that are easy on the eyes and do not cause strain, such as pastel colors or light shades of blue, green, or yellow.
2. Use a consistent color scheme throughout your notes to help with organization and recall.
3. Consider using different colors to highlight important information or to differentiate between different types of information.
4. Avoid using too many colors, as this can be overwhelming and make it difficult to focus on the information.
5. Test out different color combinations to see what works best for you and your learning style.
