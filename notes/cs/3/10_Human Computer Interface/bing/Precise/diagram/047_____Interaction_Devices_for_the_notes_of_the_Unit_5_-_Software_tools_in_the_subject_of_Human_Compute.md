### Interaction Devices

Interaction devices are hardware components that allow users to interact with a computer system. These devices can be classified into two categories: input devices and output devices.

#### Input Devices
Input devices are used to enter data and instructions into a computer system. Some common input devices include:

1. Keyboard: A keyboard is a device that allows users to enter text and commands into a computer system.
2. Mouse: A mouse is a pointing device that allows users to move a cursor on the screen and select items.
3. Touchscreen: A touchscreen is a display that can detect the presence and location of a touch within the display area.
4. Microphone: A microphone is a device that converts sound waves into an electrical signal that can be interpreted by a computer system.
5. Scanner: A scanner is a device that captures images of documents or photos and converts them into digital data.

#### Output Devices
Output devices are used to display or present information to the user. Some common output devices include:

1. Monitor: A monitor is a display device that presents visual information to the user.
2. Printer: A printer is a device that produces a hard copy of digital data on paper or other media.
3. Speakers: Speakers are devices that convert electrical signals into sound waves that can be heard by the user.
4. Projector: A projector is a device that projects an image onto a large screen or wall.

Interaction devices are essential components of a human-computer interface, allowing users to interact with a computer system in a natural and intuitive manner. They play a crucial role in the usability and accessibility of a computer system.
