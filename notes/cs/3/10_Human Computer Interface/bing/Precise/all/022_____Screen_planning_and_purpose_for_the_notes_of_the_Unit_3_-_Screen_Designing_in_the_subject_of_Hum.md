# Screen Planning and Purpose

Screen planning and purpose is an important aspect of screen design in the field of Human-Computer Interface. It involves the process of determining the goals and objectives of a screen, and designing it in a way that effectively meets those goals.

1. **Defining the purpose of the screen**: The first step in screen planning is to clearly define the purpose of the screen. This involves identifying the tasks that the user will perform on the screen, and the information that the screen will display.

2. **Determining the target audience**: The next step is to determine the target audience for the screen. This involves identifying the characteristics of the users who will be using the screen, such as their age, education level, and technical proficiency.

3. **Designing the layout**: Once the purpose of the screen and the target audience have been determined, the next step is to design the layout of the screen. This involves deciding on the placement of the various elements on the screen, such as text, images, and buttons.

4. **Choosing the appropriate visual elements**: The visual elements of the screen, such as the color scheme, font, and images, should be chosen carefully to effectively convey the intended message to the user.

5. **Testing and refining the design**: Finally, the screen design should be tested with users to ensure that it effectively meets its intended purpose. Based on the feedback from the users, the design can be refined and improved.

In summary, screen planning and purpose is a crucial step in the screen design process, as it helps to ensure that the screen effectively meets the needs of the user and achieves its intended goals.
