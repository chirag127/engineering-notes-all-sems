# Unit 1 - Introduction: The Concept of Direct Manipulation

Direct manipulation is an approach to interfaces in computer science, human-computer interaction, and interaction design. It involves the continuous representation of objects of interest together with rapid, reversible, and incremental actions and feedback .

- Direct manipulation is a style of Human Machine Interaction (HMI) design that features a natural representation of task objects and actions.
- It promotes the notion of people performing a task themselves (directly) not through an intermediary like a computer.
- Direct manipulation is one of the central concepts of graphical user interfaces (GUIs) and is sometimes equated with “what you see is what you get” (WYSIWYG).
- These interfaces combine menu-based interaction with physical actions such as dragging and dropping in order to help the user use the interface with minimal learning.
