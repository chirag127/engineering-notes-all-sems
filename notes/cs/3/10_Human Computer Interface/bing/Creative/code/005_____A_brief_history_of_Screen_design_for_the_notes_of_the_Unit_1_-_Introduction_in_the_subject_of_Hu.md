### A brief history of screen design

- Screen design is the process of creating visual interfaces for devices such as computers, smartphones, tablets, etc.
- Screen design involves aspects such as layout, typography, color, graphics, animation, interactivity, and usability.
- Screen design has evolved over time, influenced by technological developments, user needs, and design trends.
- Some of the major milestones in the history of screen design are:

  - **1964**: The first working LCD (liquid crystal display) and PDP (plasma display panel) are invented, paving the way for flat screen technologies.
  - **1970s**: The first graphical user interfaces (GUIs) are developed, using icons, menus, windows, and pointing devices to interact with the screen.
  - **1980s**: The Macintosh and Windows operating systems popularize the desktop metaphor, using folders, files, and trash bins to organize information on the screen.
  - **1990s**: The World Wide Web and the Internet Explorer browser introduce the concept of web pages, using hyperlinks, images, and multimedia to present information on the screen.
  - **2000s**: The emergence of mobile devices and touchscreens, such as the iPhone and the iPad, require new design principles and gestures, such as swiping, pinching, and tapping, to interact with the screen.
  - **2010s**: The rise of social media, streaming services, and cloud computing, create new types of screen content and experiences, such as feeds, stories, and live videos.
  - **2020s**: The advancement of artificial intelligence, augmented reality, and virtual reality, offer new possibilities and challenges for screen design, such as voice, face, and eye recognition, holograms, and immersive environments.

- Screen design is a dynamic and evolving field, that requires constant learning and adaptation to the changing needs and expectations of users and technologies.
