## Unit 2 - Design process

- The design process is a series of steps that engineers, designers, and problem-solvers use to create solutions for various challenges or needs.
- The design process can vary depending on the context, the discipline, and the type of problem, but it generally involves some common stages, such as:
  - Define the problem: Identify the need, the goal, the constraints, and the criteria for success.
  - Research and brainstorm: Gather information, generate ideas, and explore possibilities.
  - Develop and test solutions: Select the most promising idea, create a prototype, and evaluate its performance and feasibility.
  - Communicate and refine: Present the solution, get feedback, and make improvements.
  - Implement and review: Put the solution into practice, monitor its outcomes, and reflect on its effectiveness and impact.
- The design process is not linear or fixed; it is iterative and flexible. It allows for revisiting and revising previous stages, as well as adding new ones, as the problem and the solution evolve.
- The design process is a collaborative and creative endeavor that requires critical thinking, problem-solving, and communication skills. It also involves ethical and social considerations, such as the impact of the solution on the environment, the society, and the users.
