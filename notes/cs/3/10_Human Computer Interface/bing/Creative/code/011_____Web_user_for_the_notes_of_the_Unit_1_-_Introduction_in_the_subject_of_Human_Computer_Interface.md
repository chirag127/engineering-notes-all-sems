### Web user for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction in the subject of Human Computer Interface

Human Computer Interface (HCI) is the study of how people interact with computers and how to design, implement and evaluate computer systems that are usable, effective and satisfying for human users  .

Some of the topics covered in Unit 1 - Introduction are:

- The scope and challenges of HCI and Interaction Design
- The human: input/output channels, memory, reasoning and problem solving 
- The computer: devices, memory, processing and networks 
- Interaction: models, frameworks, ergonomics, styles, elements, interactivity and paradigms  
- Visual representation: segmentation and variables of the display plane, modes of correspondence
- Text and gesture interaction: keyboards, mice, touchscreens, speech recognition and synthesis
- The basic and long term goals of HCI 
- The importance of user feedback and evaluation  

Some of the key points to remember are:

- HCI is an interdisciplinary field that draws from computer science, psychology, design, engineering and other disciplines  
- HCI aims to understand the needs, preferences and abilities of human users and to design computer systems that match them  
- HCI involves both the design of the user interface (the part of the system that the user sees and interacts with) and the evaluation of the user experience (the quality and satisfaction of the user's interaction with the system)  
- HCI is a dynamic and evolving field that responds to new technologies, new contexts and new challenges  
- HCI is a user-centered approach that puts the user at the center of the design process and involves the user in the evaluation and improvement of the system
