## Human Computer Interface (HCI)

- HCI is the study of interaction between people (users) and computers  .
- HCI was previously known as the man-machine studies or man-machine interaction .
- HCI deals with the design, execution and assessment of computer systems and related phenomenon that are for human use .
- HCI can be used in all disciplines wherever there is a possibility of computer installation.

## Foundations of HCI

- The foundations of HCI consist of three main components: the human, the computer and the interaction.
- The human component involves the study of human capabilities and characteristics, such as input/output channels, memory, reasoning and problem solving.
- The computer component involves the study of computer hardware and software, such as devices, memory, processing and networks.
- The interaction component involves the study of models, frameworks, ergonomics, styles, elements, interactivity and paradigms of human-computer communication.

## Scope and Challenges of HCI and Interaction Design

- HCI and interaction design are interdisciplinary fields that draw from various disciplines, such as psychology, computer science, engineering, design, sociology and anthropology.
- HCI and interaction design aim to create systems that are effective, efficient, satisfying, safe, accessible and enjoyable for the users.
- HCI and interaction design face various challenges, such as the diversity of users, contexts and tasks, the rapid changes in technology and society, the ethical and social implications of computing, and the evaluation of user experience.
