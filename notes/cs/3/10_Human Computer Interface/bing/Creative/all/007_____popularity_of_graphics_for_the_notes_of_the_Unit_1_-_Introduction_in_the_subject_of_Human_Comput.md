# Popularity of graphics for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction in the subject of Human Computer Interface

- Graphics are visual representations of information, such as images, icons, diagrams, charts, etc.
- Graphics can enhance the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction in the subject of Human Computer Interface by:
  - Attracting the attention of the readers and increasing their interest in the topic .
  - Simplifying the complex concepts and processes related to human-computer interaction, such as the interaction cycle, the design principles, the evaluation methods, etc.
  - Providing examples and illustrations of different types of graphical user interfaces (GUIs) and their advantages and disadvantages .
  - Facilitating the recall and retention of the key points and concepts by using visual cues and associations .
- Graphics are popular for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction in the subject of Human Computer Interface because:
  - They are consistent with the nature and scope of the subject, which is about the graphical, visual, informational and interactive representations for human-computer interaction.
  - They are compatible with the modern technologies and devices that support GUIs, such as computers, smartphones, tablets, etc. .
  - They are appealing and engaging for the learners, who are accustomed to the use of graphics in various media and applications .
