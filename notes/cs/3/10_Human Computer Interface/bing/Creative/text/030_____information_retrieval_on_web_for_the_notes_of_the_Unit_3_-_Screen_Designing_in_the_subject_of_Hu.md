### Information retrieval on web

- Information retrieval (IR) is the process of finding and accessing relevant information from a large collection of documents, such as the web.
- IR involves three main components: the user, the query, and the document collection.
- The user expresses their information need as a query, which is a formal statement of keywords or concepts.
- The document collection is a set of texts that may contain the information the user is looking for.
- The IR system matches the query to the document collection and returns a ranked list of documents that are most likely to satisfy the user's information need.
- IR systems use various methods and models to represent, index, and retrieve documents, such as Boolean, vector space, probabilistic, and language models.
- IR systems also use various sources of evidence to rank documents, such as the content, the structure, the links, the quality, and the user feedback of the documents.
- IR systems face many challenges and opportunities in the web context, such as the scale, the diversity, the dynamics, the noise, the heterogeneity, and the personalization of the web.
- IR systems aim to provide effective, efficient, and user-friendly web search services that can help users find, access, and use the information they need.
