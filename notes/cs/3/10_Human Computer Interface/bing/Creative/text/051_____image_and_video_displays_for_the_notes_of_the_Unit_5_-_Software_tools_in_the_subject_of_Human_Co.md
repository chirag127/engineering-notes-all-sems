### Image and Video Displays for the Notes of the Unit 5 - Software Tools in the Subject of Human Computer Interface

- Image and video displays are types of **screenless displays** that project images or videos directly onto the user's retina, the environment, or the air.
- Image and video displays can be used for various purposes, such as enhancing the user's perception, providing information, creating immersive experiences, or enabling communication.
- Image and video displays can be classified into three categories based on the distance between the user and the display: **close view**, **intermediate view**, and **distant view**.
- Close view displays are those that are worn or held by the user, such as **virtual reality goggles**, **augmented reality glasses**, or **smartphones**. These displays provide a high-resolution and personalized view of the images or videos, but may also cause discomfort, fatigue, or isolation.
- Intermediate view displays are those that are placed within the user's reach, such as **desktop monitors**, **laptops**, or **tablets**. These displays allow the user to interact with the images or videos using input devices, such as **mouse**, **keyboard**, or **touchscreen**. These displays are suitable for tasks that require concentration, accuracy, or collaboration.
- Distant view displays are those that are located far from the user, such as **projectors**, **smartboards**, or **holograms**. These displays create a large and shared view of the images or videos, but may also suffer from low-resolution, occlusion, or distortion.
- Image and video displays can also be classified based on the type of interaction they support: **passive**, **reactive**, or **proactive**.
- Passive displays are those that do not respond to the user's actions, such as **television**, **cinema**, or **digital signage**. These displays are mainly used for entertainment, education, or advertising purposes.
- Reactive displays are those that respond to the user's actions, such as **video games**, **video conferencing**, or **video-based human-computer interfaces**. These displays use **cameras**, **microphones**, or **sensors** to capture the user's gestures, voice, or facial expressions, and map them to commands, feedback, or emotions.
- Proactive displays are those that anticipate the user's actions, such as **smart assistants**, **personalized recommendations**, or **context-aware applications**. These displays use **artificial intelligence**, **machine learning**, or **data mining** to analyze the user's behavior, preferences, or context, and provide relevant, timely, or personalized information or services.
