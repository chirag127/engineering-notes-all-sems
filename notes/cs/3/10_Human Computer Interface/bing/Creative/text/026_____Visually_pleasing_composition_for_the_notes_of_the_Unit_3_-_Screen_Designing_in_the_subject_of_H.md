### Visually pleasing composition for the notes of the Unit 3 - Screen Designing in the subject of Human Computer Interface

- A visually pleasing composition is a way of arranging the elements of your notes in a harmonious and attractive way that conveys your message clearly and quickly.
- A visually pleasing composition can help you to improve your memory, attention, creativity, and understanding of the topic.
- Some of the principles and techniques of creating a visually pleasing composition for your notes are:

  - Use a grid system to organize your notes into sections and columns. A grid system can help you to align your elements, create balance, and establish hierarchy.
  - Use white space to create contrast, focus, and clarity. White space is the empty area between and around your elements. It can help you to avoid clutter, emphasize important information, and guide the eye of the reader.
  - Use colors to create mood, emotion, and interest. Colors can help you to highlight key points, categorize information, and convey meaning. You can use a color wheel to choose complementary, analogous, or triadic colors for your notes.
  - Use shapes to create structure, variety, and symbolism. Shapes can help you to group information, create boundaries, and represent concepts. You can use geometric shapes (such as circles, squares, and triangles) or organic shapes (such as curves, waves, and blobs) for your notes.
  - Use icons to illustrate key points and help you retain more information. Icons are simple and recognizable symbols that can help you to summarize, simplify, and visualize information. You can use icons from Canva's library or create your own icons for your notes.
  - Use fonts and font sizes to create hierarchy, emphasis, and readability. Fonts are the styles of letters and numbers that you use for your notes. Font sizes are the measurements of how big or small your fonts are. You can use different fonts and font sizes to distinguish headings, subheadings, body text, and captions for your notes.
  - Use symbols, abbreviations, and images to avoid long sentences and save space. Symbols are signs or marks that stand for something else. Abbreviations are shortened forms of words or phrases. Images are pictures or drawings that represent something. You can use symbols, abbreviations, and images to convey information quickly and efficiently for your notes.
