### Human interaction with computers

- Human-computer interaction (HCI) is the field of study that focuses on optimizing how users and computers interact by designing interactive computer interfaces that satisfy users’ needs.
- HCI is a multidisciplinary subject covering computer science, behavioral sciences, cognitive science, ergonomics, psychology, and design principles .
- HCI researchers observe the ways humans interact with computers and design technologies that allow humans to interact with computers in novel ways.
- HCI is also sometimes termed human–machine interaction (HMI), man-machine interaction (MMI) or computer-human interaction (CHI).

### Design process in HCI

- The design process in HCI is the systematic and iterative approach to creating user interfaces that are usable, effective, and enjoyable .
- The design process in HCI typically involves the following steps :
  - **Problem definition**: Identify the goals, needs, and requirements of the users and the system.
  - **User and task analysis**: Understand the characteristics, preferences, and behaviors of the users and the tasks they perform with the system.
  - **Design alternatives**: Generate and evaluate different possible solutions to the problem, using sketches, wireframes, prototypes, etc.
  - **Implementation**: Build and test the chosen solution, using appropriate tools and methods.
  - **Evaluation**: Assess the usability, effectiveness, and satisfaction of the solution, using various techniques such as user testing, heuristic evaluation, etc.
  - **Iteration**: Repeat the previous steps until the solution meets the desired criteria and standards.
