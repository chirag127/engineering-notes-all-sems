# Unit 1 - Introduction

## What is Human Computer Interface (HCI)?

- HCI is the study of how people interact with computers and other technologies
- HCI aims to design, evaluate, and improve the usability, accessibility, and user experience of computer systems
- HCI involves multiple disciplines, such as computer science, psychology, design, and engineering

## Why is HCI important?

- HCI can enhance the productivity, efficiency, and satisfaction of users and organizations
- HCI can prevent or reduce errors, frustrations, and risks associated with computer use
- HCI can foster creativity, innovation, and social impact through human-centered design

## What are the main components of HCI?

- Users: the people who use or are affected by computer systems
- Tasks: the goals, activities, and contexts of computer use
- Systems: the hardware, software, and interfaces of computer systems
- Evaluations: the methods and metrics for assessing the quality and effectiveness of computer systems

## What are some examples of HCI applications?

- Desktop and mobile applications
- Web and social media platforms
- Virtual and augmented reality systems
- Games and entertainment systems
- Educational and learning systems
- Health and wellness systems
- Assistive and adaptive technologies
- Ubiquitous and wearable computing

## What are some challenges and trends in HCI?

- Diversity and inclusion: designing for diverse and marginalized user groups and contexts
- Ethics and privacy: ensuring the security, trust, and responsibility of computer systems and data
- Sustainability and social good: addressing the environmental and societal impacts of computer systems and technologies
- Artificial intelligence and machine learning: enhancing the intelligence, autonomy, and collaboration of computer systems and users
- Multimodal and natural interaction: supporting the use of multiple senses and modalities, such as speech, gesture, and touch, for computer interaction
- User-generated content and co-design: empowering users to create, share, and customize their own content and experiences
