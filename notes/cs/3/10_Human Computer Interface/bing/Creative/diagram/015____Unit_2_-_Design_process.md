## Unit 2 - Design process

The design process is a series of steps that engineers, designers, and problem-solvers use to create solutions for various challenges. The design process can be applied to any field or domain, such as product design, software development, architecture, education, etc. The design process typically involves the following stages:

- **Define the problem**: Identify the need, goal, or challenge that motivates the design. Analyze the context, constraints, and criteria of the problem. Research existing solutions and relevant information.
- **Generate ideas**: Brainstorm possible solutions for the problem. Use creativity and divergent thinking to explore different options. Sketch, model, or prototype the ideas to visualize them.
- **Evaluate and select ideas**: Compare and contrast the ideas based on the criteria and constraints of the problem. Test and refine the ideas to improve their feasibility and effectiveness. Choose the best idea or combine elements from different ideas.
- **Implement the solution**: Plan and execute the steps to implement the chosen solution. Use appropriate tools, materials, and methods to realize the solution. Document and communicate the process and the outcome.
- **Review and reflect**: Evaluate the solution based on the feedback from the users, clients, or stakeholders. Assess the strengths and weaknesses of the solution and the design process. Identify the opportunities for improvement and further development.
