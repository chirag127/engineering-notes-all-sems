### Definition

- Human Computer Interface (HCI) is the study of how people interact with computers and other technologies.
- HCI involves the design, evaluation, and implementation of interactive systems that are usable, efficient, and satisfying for the users.
- HCI also considers the social, ethical, and environmental implications of technology use and design.
- HCI draws from multiple disciplines, such as computer science, psychology, design, communication, and sociology.
- HCI aims to improve the quality of human life and work through better technology design and use.
