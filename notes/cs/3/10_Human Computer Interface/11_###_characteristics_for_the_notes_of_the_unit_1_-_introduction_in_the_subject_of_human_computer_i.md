### Characteristics for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction in the subject of Human Computer Interface

Unit 1 - Human Computer Interface:
- Characteristics of Human Computer Interfaces (HCI):
  1. User-centered design: HCI focuses on creating interfaces that are easy and intuitive for users to interact with.
  2. Direct manipulation: HCI allows users to directly manipulate objects on the screen.
  3. Feedback: HCI provides immediate feedback to users, allowing them to understand the results of their actions.
  4. Customizability: HCI allows users to customize their interfaces to their individual needs and preferences.
  5. Consistency: HCI uses consistent design elements and behaviors to reduce user confusion and increase efficiency.
  6. Accessibility: HCI is designed to be accessible to a wide range of users, including those with disabilities.

Note: HCI is the study of how people interact with computers. This unit covers the key characteristics of HCI, including user-centered design, direct manipulation, feedback, customizability, consistency, and accessibility.
