### understanding business junctions for the notes of the Unit 2 - Design process in the subject of Human Computer Interface

Unit 2 - Design Process:
- Understanding Business Junctions:
  1. Problem definition: Identifying the problem or challenge that the system is designed to address.
  2. Stakeholder analysis: Identifying and analyzing the stakeholders who will be impacted by the system.
  3. Business requirements: Identifying the business requirements for the system, including goals, objectives, and constraints.
  4. User research: Conducting research to understand the needs and behaviors of the users who will be using the system.
  5. Competitor analysis: Analyzing the strengths and weaknesses of competing systems in the market.
  6. Market analysis: Analyzing the market for the system, including trends, opportunities, and challenges.

Note: Understanding business junctions is an important aspect of the design process, as it helps ensure that the system meets the needs of the business and the users. This unit covers the key steps in understanding business junctions, including problem definition, stakeholder analysis, business requirements, user research, competitor analysis, and market analysis.
