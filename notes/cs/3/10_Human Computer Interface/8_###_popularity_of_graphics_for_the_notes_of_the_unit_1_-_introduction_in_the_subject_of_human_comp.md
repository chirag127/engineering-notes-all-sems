### popularity of graphics for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction in the subject of Human Computer Interface

Unit 1 - Introduction to Human Computer Interface:
- Popularity of Graphics:
  1. Graphical User Interfaces (GUIs) have become the dominant form of user interface due to their ease of use and visual appeal.
  2. Graphics have revolutionized the way we interact with computers and have made computing accessible to a wider audience.
  3. The use of graphics has allowed for the development of more intuitive and user-friendly interfaces, such as icons, buttons, and visual representations of data.

Note: The popularity of graphics in human-computer interfaces has had a significant impact on the development of computing. Graphics make it easier for users to interact with computers and understand information, and have played a key role in the evolution of user interfaces.
