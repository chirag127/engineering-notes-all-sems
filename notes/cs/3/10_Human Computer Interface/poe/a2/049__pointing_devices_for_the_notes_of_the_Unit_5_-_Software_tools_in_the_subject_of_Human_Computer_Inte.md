### Pointing Devices

1. Mouse
- Physical device that moves on a surface to control the cursor position on the screen
- Buttons to select, drag, drop objects or perform other actions
- Most common pointing device, suitable for tasks requiring high precision

2. Trackball
- stationary ball device rolled by thumb or fingers to control cursor
- Takes up less space than a mouse but can be less precise
- Well suited for confined workspaces or those with limited desk space

3. Touchscreen
- Direct interaction with the display screen to control the interface
- Finger or stylus taps, swipes and gestures control the interface
- Intuitive to use but can be less precise than physical devices for detailed controls or selections
- Common on mobile devices and tablets, also used in some laptops and desktop setups

4. Touchpad (Trackpad)
- Small, flat, touch-sensitive pad typically below the keyboard
- Uses finger swipes, taps and gestures to control the cursor and interface
- Compact but can be less comfortable or precise than a physical mouse for some tasks
- Found on most laptops as an alternative built-in pointing device

5. Joystick
- Hand-operated lever or stick to control the cursor or other interface elements
- Typically used for gaming to control direction and speed
- Limited use for general desktop interface control due to bulk and specificity of purpose
