### Screen planning and purpose for the notes of the Unit 3 - Screen Designing in the subject of Human Computer Interface.

- Screen planning involves determining the number, type, and organization of screens needed to implement a system.
- The purpose of screen planning is to define the user's path through the interface and ensure that the necessary functionality is allocated to the appropriate screens.
- Consider the complexity of the task and the amount of information to be displayed. Group related information and functions.
- Minimize the number of screen transitions required to complete a task. Consistency in screen organization and layout makes learning and memorization easier for the user.
- Clearly communicate the screen's purpose through screen titles, subtitles, and layout.
- Ensure that the information display and controls are appropriate for the variety of users and tasks.
- Screens and their elements should be designed to accommodate universal usability, including accessibility guidelines.
- Test the screen design to identify and remedy any problems with the user's comprehension, navigation, or ability to complete tasks.
