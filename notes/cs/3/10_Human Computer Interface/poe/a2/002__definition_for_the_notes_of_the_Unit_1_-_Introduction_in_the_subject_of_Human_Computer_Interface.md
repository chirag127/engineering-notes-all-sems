### Definition for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction in the subject of Human Computer Interface:

1. Human Computer Interface (HCI) is the study of how humans interact with computers and to what extent computers are or are not developed for successful interaction with human beings.
2. The main aim of HCI is to understand and create interfaces and interactions that are understandable, usable and accessible to users with a wide variety of backgrounds and abilities.
3. The process of human-computer interaction involves study of cognitive, social and ergonomic aspects of the relationship between humans and computers. It is interdisciplinary field requiring knowledge from computer science and human sciences like psychology, sociology, anthropology.
4. The core goals of HCI are usefulness, ease of use, efficiency, learnability, memorability, error minimization and satisfaction.
5. There are three perspectives to study HCI - socio-organizational, cognitive and computer science perspective. The socio-organizational perspective focuses on social issues surrounding technology use. The cognitive perspective focuses on mental processes involved in interface use. The computer science perspective focuses on system engineering principles.
