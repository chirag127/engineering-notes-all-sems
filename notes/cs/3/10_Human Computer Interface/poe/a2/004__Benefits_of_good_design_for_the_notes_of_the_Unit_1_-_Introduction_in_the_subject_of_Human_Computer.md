### Benefits of good design for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction in the subject of Human Computer Interface.

1. Organized and systematic: Good note design involves structuring the content in an organized manner using headings and subheadings. This makes the notes systematic and easy to understand, navigate and remember.
2. Focuses attention: Effective use of formatting like bold, italics, highlighting important terms draws attention to core concepts and topics. This makes learning and revision more focused and effective.
3. Enhances comprehension: Diagrams, images, examples and illustrations supplement the text and make abstract concepts more comprehensible. This enhances understanding and retention of the topics.
4. Useful for reference: Well-designed notes with proper formatting, page numbers, indexes and tables of contents can serve as a useful reference material for quick lookup of topics and concepts. This saves time in revision and retrieval of information.
5. Provides continuity: Notes that are designed using a consistent style and structure across chapters and sections provide flow and continuity. This makes the learning experience integrated and the notes easier to understand.

In summary, a good design for notes increases their utility and makes learning and reference more efficient and fruitful. The additional effort required to create well-designed notes is worthwhile for effective learning and comprehension.
