### Image and Video Displays

- Images are commonly used to convey information in a visually-oriented manner. Some ways images are used in interfaces:

- Logos - to represent a company, product or service
- Icons - to represent actions, files, devices, etc.
- Illustrations - to visually display concepts and processes
- Photos - to display people, places, objects, etc.

Considerations when using images:

- Appropriateness - choose images that appropriately convey the intended message
- Aesthetics - consider how images look and feel, and how they integrate with the overall interface style
- Format - provide images in appropriate formats, sizes and resolutions for the interface
- Accessibility - include alternative text for images to support users with visual disabilities

- Video is increasingly being utilized in interfaces to demonstrate processes, provide tutorials or instructions, showcase products or services, and more. Some things to consider with video:

- Keep videos short in length (under 2 minutes if possible)
- Clearly communicate the key message or purpose at the beginning
- Make controls easy to use (play, pause, rewind, captions, etc.)
- Choose an appropriate resolution, format, and size for your interface
- Include captions and transcripts for accessibility

This covers the key points regarding the use of images and videos in interfaces for effective communication and usability. Images and videos are powerful but must be applied appropriately with consideration for aesthetics, format, accessibility and more.
