### Presentation Information for Unit 3 - Screen Designing Notes

1.  Layouts
- Divide the screen into sections using grids/frames to organize the elements
- Ensure proper alignment and spacing between sections and elements
- Ensure proper grouping of related elements
- Ensure proper flow of information as per the sequence to be followed

2.  Navigation
- Provide clear navigation options for moving between screens/sections
- Use consistent navigation options throughout for easy understanding
- Label navigation options appropriately
- Ensure navigation options are always visible and accessible

3.  Typography
- Choose fonts suitable for digital display
- Choose appropriate font sizes for easy readability
- Ensure proper contrast between text and background colors
- Use formatting like bold, italics, underline, color, etc. appropriately to highlight important text

4.  Color
- Choose color schemes that are pleasant and easy on the eyes
- Ensure proper contrast between colors for easy visibility
- Use colors to group related elements or highlight important elements
- Ensure color schemes are consistent across screens for a uniform feel

5.  Images
- Choose images that complement the content
- Optimize images to reduce load time
- Provide alternative text for images
- Ensure images are accessible to color blind users
- Ensure proper contrast between image colors and background

6.  Content
- Ensure content is concise and to the point
- Use simple and easy to understand language
- Organize content logically in the sequence to be followed
- Avoid unnecessary elements that clutter the screen
- Ensure all content is accessible to users with disabilities

7.  Responsiveness
- Design screens to work on multiple devices and screen sizes
- Use relative units for dimensions so that elements scale appropriately
- Have a mobile-first approach in responsive design
- Hide unnecessary elements on smaller screens to avoid clutter
- Test the design on multiple devices to ensure proper responsiveness
