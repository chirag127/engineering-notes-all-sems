### Graphical System for the Notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction in the Subject of Human Computer Interface

In the field of Human Computer Interface, it is important to have a graphical system that can assist in taking notes and organizing information. Here are some key points to consider when using a graphical system for note-taking:

- **Visual Hierarchy:** A graphical system should incorporate visual hierarchy, which means that information should be presented in a way that makes it easy to understand the importance and relationship between different pieces of information. This can be achieved through the use of font size, color, and style.

- **Mind Mapping:** Mind mapping is a popular technique for note-taking that involves creating a visual representation of information. This involves starting with a central idea and branching out into related ideas, creating a map of interconnected concepts. Mind mapping can be done on paper or using software.

- **Sketchnoting:** Sketchnoting is another technique for visual note-taking that involves incorporating sketches and drawings into notes. This can be particularly useful for capturing ideas and concepts that are difficult to express through words alone.

- **Color Coding:** Color coding can be an effective way to organize information and make it easier to understand. Different colors can be used to represent different categories or types of information, making it easier to identify and remember key points.

- **Icons and Symbols:** Icons and symbols can be used to represent concepts and ideas in a graphical system. This can be particularly useful for creating a visual shorthand that allows you to quickly identify and understand different pieces of information.

By incorporating these techniques into a graphical system for note-taking, it is possible to create a more effective and efficient way of organizing information and understanding key concepts in the field of Human Computer Interface.
