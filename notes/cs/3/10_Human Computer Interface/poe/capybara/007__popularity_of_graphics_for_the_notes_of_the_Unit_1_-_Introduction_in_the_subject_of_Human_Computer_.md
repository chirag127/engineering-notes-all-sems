### Popularity of Graphics for the Notes of Unit 1 - Introduction in the Subject of Human Computer Interface

The use of graphics in human-computer interface (HCI) has become increasingly popular over the years. Here are some reasons why:

- **Enhanced User Experience:** Graphics can make interfaces more visually appealing and engaging, allowing users to enjoy the experience of using a system more. This can also help increase user retention and satisfaction.

- **Improved Comprehension:** Graphics can aid in the comprehension of complex information. They can help break down complex data into digestible pieces and make it easier for users to understand.

- **Increased Efficiency:** Graphics can also help users complete tasks more quickly and efficiently. For example, a well-designed graphic can help users quickly identify important information or navigate through a system more easily.

- **Accessibility:** Graphics can be particularly beneficial for users with disabilities. For example, a user with a visual impairment may be able to better understand information presented in a graphic form than in a text-based format.

- **Versatility:** Graphics can be used in a variety of contexts, from data visualization to instructional design. This makes them a valuable tool for many different types of interfaces and applications.

In summary, graphics are a popular and useful tool in HCI. They can enhance user experience, aid in comprehension, increase efficiency, improve accessibility, and be used in various contexts.
