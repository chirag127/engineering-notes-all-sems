## Unit 2 - Design process

Design process is a systematic approach to problem-solving that involves defining, ideating, prototyping, and testing solutions. It is a critical component of any successful design project. Here are some key points to keep in mind when working through the design process:

- **Define the problem:** The first step in any design process is to define the problem you are trying to solve. This involves understanding the needs and wants of your target audience, as well as any constraints that may impact your design.

- **Ideate:** Once you have defined the problem, it's time to generate ideas for potential solutions. This is where brainstorming comes into play. The goal is to come up with as many ideas as possible, even if they seem far-fetched or unrealistic.

- **Prototype:** After you have generated a list of potential solutions, it's time to start prototyping. This involves creating rough sketches, wireframes, or even physical prototypes of your ideas. The goal is to create something tangible that can be tested and refined.

- **Test:** Once you have a prototype, it's time to test it. This involves gathering feedback from potential users, stakeholders, and other members of your team. The goal is to identify any flaws or areas for improvement, and then iterate on your design.

- **Iterate:** The design process is not a linear one. Instead, it involves a cycle of defining, ideating, prototyping, testing, and iterating. After you have tested your prototype and gathered feedback, it's time to go back to the drawing board and refine your design.

By following these key points, you can ensure that your design process is both efficient and effective. Remember, the goal is to create a solution that meets the needs of your target audience while also addressing any constraints or limitations. With a systematic approach to problem-solving, you can achieve this goal and create a successful design project.
