### Design goals for the notes of the Unit 3 - Screen Designing in the subject of Human Computer Interface:

- The notes should cover the fundamental principles of screen designing such as usability, accessibility, and user experience.
- The notes should explain the different types of interface components used in screen designing such as buttons, text fields, dropdowns, and checkboxes, along with their respective functionalities.
- The notes should provide examples of good screen designs and explain the reasoning behind their effectiveness.
- The notes should cover the importance of consistency in screen designing, including the use of consistent fonts, colors, and alignment.
- The notes should explain the concept of hierarchy in screen designing, including the use of visual cues such as size, color, and position to guide the user's attention.
- The notes should provide guidelines for designing responsive screens that adapt to different screen sizes and orientations.
- The notes should cover the importance of user testing in screen designing and provide examples of testing methodologies that can be used.
- The notes should include a section on accessibility in screen designing, including guidelines for designing screens that are accessible to users with disabilities.
- The notes should cover the use of prototyping tools such as wireframes and mockups in screen designing, and provide examples of popular tools.
- The notes should provide a list of resources for further reading and learning on the topic of screen designing in human-computer interaction.
