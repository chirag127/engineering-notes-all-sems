## Unit 1 - Introduction

In this unit, we will introduce you to the basics of the course and what you can expect to learn throughout the duration of the program. Here are some key points to keep in mind:

- This course is designed to provide you with a comprehensive understanding of the subject matter, including its history, current state, and future prospects.
- The course will be delivered through a combination of lectures, readings, assignments, and exams.
- You will be expected to actively participate in class discussions and group work.
- The course will cover a wide range of topics, including but not limited to the following:
  - Theoretical foundations of the subject matter
  - Methodologies and techniques used in the field
  - Current trends and issues
  - Applications and case studies
- By the end of the course, you should be able to:
  - Demonstrate a solid understanding of the subject matter
  - Analyze and evaluate research literature in the field
  - Apply relevant methodologies and techniques to real-world problems
  - Communicate effectively about the subject matter
- To succeed in this course, you will need to:
  - Attend all lectures and complete all readings and assignments on time
  - Actively engage in class discussions and group work
  - Seek help from the instructor or teaching assistants when needed
  - Demonstrate academic integrity by citing sources properly and avoiding plagiarism
- Finally, it is important to note that this course is just the beginning of your journey in this field. There is always more to learn, and we hope that this course will inspire you to continue exploring and contributing to the subject matter.
