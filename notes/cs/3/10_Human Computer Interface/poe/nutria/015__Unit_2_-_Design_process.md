
## Unit 2 - Design Process

1. Design process is an iterative process of creating a product or service that meets customer needs. 
2. It involves gathering customer feedback, ideation, prototyping, testing, and refining. 
3. The design process begins with understanding customer needs and the context of use, followed by developing ideas and concepts. 
4. Prototyping is the process of creating a scaled-down version of the product or service to test ideas and concepts. 
5. Testing is the process of evaluating the prototype with users to gather feedback and refine the design. 
6. The design process is an iterative process, meaning that it is repeated until the desired outcome is achieved.
