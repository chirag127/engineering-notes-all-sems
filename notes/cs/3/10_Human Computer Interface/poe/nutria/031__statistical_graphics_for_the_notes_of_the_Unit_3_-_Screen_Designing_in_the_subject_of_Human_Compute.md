
### Statistical Graphics

1. Statistical graphics are visual representations of data that can be used to quickly identify patterns and trends in a dataset.
2. Common types of statistical graphics include bar charts, line graphs, scatter plots, histograms, and box plots.
3. Statistical graphics can be used to compare different datasets, identify relationships between variables, and draw conclusions from data.
4. Statistical graphics are an important tool for data analysis, as they can provide a visual representation of complex data that is easier to understand than text or numbers alone.
5. Statistical graphics can also be used to communicate data to others, as they can be used to create presentations or reports that are easy to understand and interpret.
