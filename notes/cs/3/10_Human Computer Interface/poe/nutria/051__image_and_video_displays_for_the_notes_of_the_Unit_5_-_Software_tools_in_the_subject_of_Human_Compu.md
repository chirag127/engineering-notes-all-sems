

### Image and Video Displays

Image and video displays are used to present information to users in a visual format. Examples of image and video displays include:

- Graphical User Interfaces (GUIs): GUIs are used to display information in a graphical format, such as icons, menus, and windows.
- Video Displays: Video displays are used to present information in a video format, such as movies and television shows.
- Image Displays: Image displays are used to present information in a still image format, such as photographs and illustrations.
- Virtual Reality: Virtual reality is used to present information in a 3D environment, such as games and simulations.
- Augmented Reality: Augmented reality is used to present information in an augmented environment, such as overlaying digital information onto the real world.
