
## Unit 1 - Introduction

1. Introduction is the beginning of any study material. It is an overview of the topic and provides a general understanding of the subject. 
2. It is important to understand the basics of the topic before delving into the details. 
3. Introduction should explain the purpose of the material, the topics that will be covered, and the expected outcomes. 
4. It should also provide an outline of the material and the structure of the material. 
5. Introduction should also provide an overview of the key concepts that will be discussed and the relationships between them. 
6. It should also provide a summary of the main points and the conclusion. 
7. Finally, it should provide a list of resources that can be used to further explore the topic.
