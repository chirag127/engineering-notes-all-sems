
### Unit 3 - Screen Designing

* Screen designing is the process of designing the graphical user interface (GUI) of a computer system, including the layout of menus, buttons, text, and other graphical elements. 
* It is important to consider the usability of the interface when designing a screen, as it should be easy to use and intuitive. 
* There are several techniques that can be used to create a good user interface, such as prototyping, user testing, and heuristic evaluation. 
* It is also important to consider the user's needs when designing a screen, as different users may have different requirements. 
* Good screen design should also take into account the aesthetics of the interface, as this can affect the user's experience. 
* Additionally, it is important to consider accessibility when designing a screen, as it should be accessible to all users, regardless of their physical or cognitive abilities. 
* Finally, it is important to consider the technical aspects of screen design, such as the programming language used and the hardware requirements.
