### ordering of screen data and content for the notes of the Unit 3 - Screen Designing in the subject of Human Computer Interface

Unit 3 - Screen Designing:
- Ordering of Screen Data and Content:
  1. Hierarchical: Organizing information from most important to least important.
  2. Spatial: Organizing information based on its location on the screen.
  3. Temporal: Organizing information based on the order in which it was created or accessed.
  4. Logical: Organizing information based on its relationship to other information.

Note: The ordering of screen data and content is critical for creating effective and user-friendly interfaces. This unit covers the different methods for ordering screen data and content, including hierarchical, spatial, temporal, and logical ordering. The appropriate method for ordering screen data and content will depend on the specific needs and goals of the user and the system.
