### Multimedia for the notes of the Unit 4 - Windows in the subject of Human Computer Interface

Unit 4 - Windows:
- Multimedia:
  1. Audio: Sound recordings or playback of music or speech.
  2. Video: Moving images or playback of recorded or live video.
  3. Images: Still images or graphics, including photographs, illustrations, and charts.
  4. Text: Written or typed information, including headings, body text, and captions.
  5. Interactivity: The ability for users to interact with multimedia elements, such as clicking on links or buttons.

Note: Multimedia elements, such as audio, video, images, text, and interactivity, can enhance the user experience and add depth and interest to a user interface. This unit covers the different types of multimedia elements used in user interfaces and the role they play in creating engaging and effective interfaces.
