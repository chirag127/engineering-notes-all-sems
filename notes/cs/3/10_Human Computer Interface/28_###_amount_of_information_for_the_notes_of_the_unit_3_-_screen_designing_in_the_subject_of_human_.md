### amount of information for the notes of the Unit 3 - Screen Designing in the subject of Human Computer Interface

The amount of information section of the Unit 3 - Screen Designing in the subject of Human Computer Interface should cover the following topics:

1. Definition of amount of information (the amount of data or information displayed on a screen)
2. Overview of amount of information (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
3. Factors affecting the amount of information (e.g. screen size, user goals, task complexity, etc.)
4. Overview of factors affecting the amount of information (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
5. Designing for the amount of information (the process of designing screens to display the appropriate amount of information)
6. Overview of designing for the amount of information (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
7. Techniques for managing the amount of information (e.g. summarization, filtering, etc.)
8. Overview of techniques for managing the amount of information (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
9. Limitations of managing the amount of information (e.g. information loss, user confusion, etc.)
10. Techniques for overcoming limitations (e.g. using advanced techniques, improving accuracy, etc.)
11. Further reading and resources (books, articles, online resources)

The amount of information is an important component of the subject of Human Computer Interface. Understanding the principles and techniques of managing the amount of information helps students to develop the skills required to design effective user interfaces. The hands-on practice and further reading resources will help students to develop their skills and deepen their understanding of the material.
