### Clustering for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of Machine Learning Techniques

Unit 1 of the Machine Learning Techniques course focuses on Introduction and covers the aspect of Clustering.

Clustering is a type of unsupervised learning in which the goal is to group similar objects into clusters, or groups, based on their features. Clustering is used in a variety of applications, including image segmentation, customer segmentation, and anomaly detection.

There are several algorithms that can be used for clustering, including k-means, hierarchical clustering, and density-based clustering. The choice of algorithm depends on the specific requirements of the problem and the type of data being analyzed.

In the context of the Machine Learning Techniques course, students in Unit 1 will learn about Clustering as a type of unsupervised learning. This may include working with examples of Clustering, analyzing the benefits and limitations of Clustering, and learning how to use Clustering algorithms to group similar objects into clusters.
