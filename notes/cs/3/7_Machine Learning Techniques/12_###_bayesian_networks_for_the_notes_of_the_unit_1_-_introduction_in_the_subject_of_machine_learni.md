### Bayesian networks for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of Machine Learning Techniques

Unit 1 of the Machine Learning Techniques course focuses on Introduction and covers the aspect of Bayesian networks.

A Bayesian network is a graphical model that represents the probabilistic relationships between variables in a system. The nodes in a Bayesian network represent variables, and the edges represent the relationships between variables. Bayesian networks can be used to perform probabilistic inference, which is the process of computing the probabilities of variables given the observed data and the relationships between variables.

Bayesian networks are particularly useful for modeling complex systems with many variables, as they provide a compact representation of the relationships between variables and can be used to perform efficient probabilistic inference.

In the context of the Machine Learning Techniques course, students in Unit 1 will learn about Bayesian networks as part of the introduction to the course. This may include working with examples of Bayesian networks, analyzing the benefits and limitations of Bayesian networks, and learning how Bayesian networks can be used to perform probabilistic inference.
