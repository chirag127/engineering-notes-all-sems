1. Determine the purpose and goals of the smart speaker.

3. Gather and organize the notes on ANNs and Machine Learning Techniques.
4. Develop a conversational UI for the smart speaker.
5. Integrate the notes and conversational UI into the chosen platform.
6. Test and refine the smart speaker.
