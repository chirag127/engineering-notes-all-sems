### SOM Algorithm and its variant for the notes of the Unit 4 - ARTIFICIAL NEURAL NETWORKS in the subject of Machine Learning Techniques

Unit 4 of the Machine Learning Techniques course focuses on Artificial Neural Networks and covers the aspect of the Self-Organizing Map (SOM) Algorithm and its variants.

The Self-Organizing Map (SOM) Algorithm is a type of unsupervised learning algorithm used for dimensionality reduction and visualization of high-dimensional data. The algorithm creates a map of neurons, where each neuron represents a cluster of similar data points. The neurons are organized in a two-dimensional grid, and the algorithm updates the weights of the neurons to minimize the difference between the input data and the neurons.

There are several variants of the SOM Algorithm, including the Growing Neural Gas (GNG) Algorithm, the Neural Gas Algorithm, and the Adaptive Resonance Theory (ART) Algorithm. These variants differ in the way that the map of neurons is updated and the way that the clusters are formed, but they all aim to produce a map of neurons that represents the structure of the data.

This may include working with examples of the SOM Algorithm and its variants, analyzing the benefits and limitations of the SOM Algorithm and its variants, and learning how to use the SOM Algorithm and its variants for dimensionality reduction and visualization of high-dimensional data.
