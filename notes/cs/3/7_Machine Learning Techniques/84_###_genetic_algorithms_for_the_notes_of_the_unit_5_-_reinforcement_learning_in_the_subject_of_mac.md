### GENETIC ALGORITHMS for the notes of the Unit 5 - REINFORCEMENT LEARNING in the subject of Machine Learning Techniques

Genetic Algorithms (GAs) are a type of optimization algorithm inspired by the process of natural selection. They are used to find the optimal solution to a problem by iteratively evolving a population of candidate solutions. The solutions are represented as chromosomes, which encode the parameters of the solution. The fitness of each solution is evaluated using a fitness function, and the best solutions are selected to produce the next generation. The process of evolution involves genetic operations such as mutation, crossover, and selection. The algorithm continues until a satisfactory solution is found or a stopping criterion is met. GAs are commonly used in problems where the search space is large and traditional optimization methods are not feasible.
