### Types of Learning for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of Machine Learning Techniques

1. Supervised Learning: In supervised learning, the algorithm is trained on labeled data to learn a function that maps inputs to outputs. For example, the algorithm is trained on thousands of images of dogs and cats, labeled as "dog" or "cat", to learn how to classify new images.

2. Unsupervised Learning: In unsupervised learning, the algorithm is trained on unlabeled data to find hidden patterns or clusters in the data. For example, the algorithm may be trained on purchasing data to cluster customers with similar buying patterns.

3. Reinforcement Learning: In reinforcement learning, the algorithm learns by interacting with a dynamic environment. The algorithm determines the optimal way of achieving the highest reward through trial-and-error using feedback from the environment. For example, the algorithm may learn to play a game by determining the best sequence of actions that lead to winning.

The above points cover the major types of machine learning - supervised learning, unsupervised learning and reinforcement learning. The choice of algorithm depends on the type of problem and availability of labeled or unlabeled data.
