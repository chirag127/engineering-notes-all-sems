### Genetic Algorithm for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of Machine Learning Techniques

1. Genetic algorithms are a part of evolutionary computing, which is a rapidly growing area of artificial intelligence.
2. Genetic algorithms are inspired by Darwin's theory of natural selection. They are adaptive heuristic search algorithms premised on the evolutionary ideas of natural selection and genetic recombination.
3. The basic concept of genetic algorithms is to mimic the process of natural evolution to solve optimization problems.
4. Genetic algorithms work on a population of candidate solutions to a problem. These candidates are encoded as chromosomes and evolve over generations to find the best solutions.
5. The major steps involved in a genetic algorithm are:
 - Representing the solutions as chromosomes.
 - Creating the initial population.
 - Evaluating the fitness of each chromosome.
 - Performing selection to choose the fittest chromosomes.
 - Performing crossover or recombination to produce new offspring.
 - Performing mutation on the offspring.
 - Replacing the least-fit population with the new offspring.
 - Repeating steps 3-7 until the termination condition is met.
