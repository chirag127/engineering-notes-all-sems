### Information gain for the notes of the Unit 3 - DECISION TREE LEARNING

1. Information gain is a concept used to select the best attribute to split the training data.
2. It measures the effectiveness of an attribute in classifying the training data.
3. The attribute with the highest information gain is selected as the splitting criterion.
4. Information gain is based on the decrease in entropy after splitting the data on an attribute.
5. Entropy is a measure of impurity of an attribute. The higher the entropy, the more impure the attribute.
6. By splitting on the attribute with the highest information gain, we can get the purest subsets.
7. This process is repeated on each subset until the subsets are pure or contain instances of only one class.
8. The tree produced this way is called a greedy tree. It may not be the optimal tree but produces reasonably good results.
9. The information gain criterion chooses attributes with the highest number of outcomes/values first. This may lead to overfitting.
10. Pruning and other techniques are used to produce simpler trees and avoid overfitting.
