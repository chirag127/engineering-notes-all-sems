### Issues in SVM for the notes of the Unit 2 - REGRESSION in the subject of Machine Learning Techniques

1. SVM is not suitable for large data sets as it uses all the training points in its optimization problem. This leads to scalability issues for large datasets.
2. SVM cannot directly provide probability estimates. Plug-in methods can be used but they are not reliable and accurate.
3. The choice of kernel is an important aspect of SVM. Different kernels lead to different decision boundaries and there is no systematic way to choose a kernel. It requires experimentation and domain knowledge.
4. SVM is a binary classifier. Multi-class classification problems require combining multiple binary classifiers which increases the complexity. One vs Rest and One vs One are commonly used approaches but they have their own limitations.
5. There is no straightforward way to incorporate the outlier/anomalous data points in the training process of SVM. Most of the SVM formulations assume that all training data is consistent and from the same distribution.
