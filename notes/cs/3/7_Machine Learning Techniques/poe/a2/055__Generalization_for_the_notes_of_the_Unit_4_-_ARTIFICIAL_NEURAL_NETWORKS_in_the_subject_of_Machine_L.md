### Generalization

1. Generalization refers to the ability of a machine learning model to perform accurately on new, previously unseen data.
2. A model that exhibits good generalization will be able to make predictions on new data that are close to the true values.
3. Generalization depends on:
- Having a model with the right capacity (not underfitting or overfitting)
- The training data being representative of the range of possible inputs
- Regularization to reduce overfitting
4. Cross-validation and testing on a held-out test set are ways to estimate the generalization performance of a model.
5. Bias and variance are the two main reasons a model may have poor generalization performance:
- High bias means the model is not flexible enough to capture the patterns in the data.
- High variance means the model is overly sensitive to the training data and overfits.
6. Early stopping is a regularization method that stops training once the validation loss starts to increase in order to avoid overfitting.
