
### Case Study of CNN for Diabetic Retinopathy 
1. Artificial Neural Networks (ANNs) are a type of machine learning technique that is used to solve complex problems. 
2. CNNs are a type of ANN that are used to analyze visual data such as images, videos, and other types of data. 
3. Diabetic Retinopathy is a condition that affects the eyes of people with diabetes and can lead to vision loss.
4. CNNs can be used to detect and diagnose Diabetic Retinopathy by analyzing medical images of the eye.
5. CNNs are able to identify features in the image that are indicative of the condition, such as changes in the blood vessels or the appearance of lesions. 
6. CNNs are able to detect Diabetic Retinopathy with an accuracy of up to 90%, which is higher than the accuracy of other methods such as manual inspection. 
7. CNNs can also be used to predict the progression of the condition and may be used in the future to develop treatments for Diabetic Retinopathy.
