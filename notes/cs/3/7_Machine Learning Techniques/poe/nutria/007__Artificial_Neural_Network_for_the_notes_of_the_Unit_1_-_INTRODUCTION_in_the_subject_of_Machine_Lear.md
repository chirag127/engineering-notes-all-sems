
### Artificial Neural Network 

* Artificial Neural Network (ANN) is a type of artificial intelligence that is modeled after the human brain. It is a network of interconnected neurons, which are used to process data and make decisions. 
* ANNs are used to solve complex problems that are difficult for traditional programming techniques. 
* ANNs are composed of multiple layers of neurons and connections between them. Each layer processes the data and passes it to the next layer. 
* The input layer receives data from the outside environment. This data is then processed by the hidden layers and output layer. 
* The output layer produces the desired output based on the input data. 
* The connections between the neurons are weighted, which means that the strength of the connection between two neurons affects the output of the network. 
* The weights of the connections are adjusted during the training process, in order to optimize the network's performance. 
* ANNs are used in a variety of applications, such as computer vision, natural language processing, and robotics.
