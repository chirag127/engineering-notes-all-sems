
### Mutation for the Notes of the Unit 5 - REINFORCEMENT LEARNING in the Subject of Machine Learning Techniques

1. Mutation is a process of randomly changing the values of parameters in a Reinforcement Learning algorithm.
2. It is used to explore different parameter values, as well as to explore different solutions to a problem.
3. Mutation can be used to improve the performance of an algorithm by introducing randomness and exploration.
4. Mutation can also be used to increase the diversity of the solutions found by an algorithm.
5. Mutation is often used in combination with other techniques such as hill-climbing or genetic algorithms.
6. Mutation can be used to explore a wider range of solutions than would be possible with a single algorithm.
7. Mutation is a powerful tool for exploring complex problems in Reinforcement Learning.
