
### History of ML

* Machine Learning (ML) is a field of Artificial Intelligence (AI) that focuses on developing computer algorithms that can learn from data and improve their performance over time. 
* ML algorithms have been around since the 1950s and have been used in a variety of applications, such as computer vision, natural language processing, and robotics.
* In the 1980s, ML algorithms began to be applied to a wider range of problems, such as financial forecasting and medical diagnosis. 
* In the 1990s, ML algorithms were further developed to be more efficient and accurate, and to be able to handle large datasets.
* Today, ML algorithms are being used in a variety of applications, such as predicting stock prices, recognizing objects in images, and understanding natural language. 
* ML algorithms are also being used to develop autonomous systems, such as self-driving cars and robots. 
* ML algorithms are constantly evolving, and new algorithms are being developed to solve increasingly complex problems.
