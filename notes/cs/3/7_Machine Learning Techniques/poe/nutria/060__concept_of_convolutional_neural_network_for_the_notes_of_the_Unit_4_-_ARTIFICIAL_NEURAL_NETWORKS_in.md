
### Concept of Convolutional Neural Network

1. Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) are a type of Artificial Neural Network (ANN) that are used to analyze visual imagery.
2. CNNs are inspired by the visual cortex of the brain, which is composed of neurons that have receptive fields.
3. CNNs are composed of several layers of neurons, each of which has a receptive field.
4. The first layer of a CNN is the input layer, which is composed of neurons that receive input from the environment.
5. The second layer is the convolutional layer, which is composed of neurons that perform convolution operations on the input.
6. The third layer is the pooling layer, which is composed of neurons that perform pooling operations on the output of the convolutional layer.
7. The fourth layer is the fully-connected layer, which is composed of neurons that are connected to all other neurons in the network.
8. The fifth layer is the output layer, which is composed of neurons that produce the output of the network.
9. CNNs are used in a variety of applications, including image classification, object detection, and image segmentation.
