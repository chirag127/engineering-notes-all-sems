
### Self-deriving Car

Self-deriving cars are vehicles that are able to autonomously navigate their environment using artificial neural networks (ANNs). ANNs are used to process sensory input from cameras, radar, and other sensors, and to make decisions about how the car should respond to its environment. Self-deriving cars are able to learn from their environment and adapt their behavior to changing conditions.

* ANNs are composed of interconnected nodes, which are organized into layers. 
* Each node in the network is connected to the nodes in the previous and next layer, and the weights of the connections are adjusted to optimize the network's performance. 
* The input layer of the network receives data from the car's sensors and the output layer provides instructions to the car's actuators. 
* The weights of the connections between the nodes are adjusted as the car learns from its environment, allowing the car to adapt its behavior.
* Self-deriving cars use supervised learning, in which the car is provided with labeled data (e.g., images of cars, pedestrians, and other objects) and the network is trained to recognize the objects in the data. 
* The car is also able to use reinforcement learning, in which the car is rewarded for performing certain actions and punished for performing others.
* Self-deriving cars are able to make decisions in real-time, allowing them to respond quickly and accurately to their environment. 
* This technology has the potential to revolutionize the automotive industry, allowing cars to drive themselves with little or no human intervention.
