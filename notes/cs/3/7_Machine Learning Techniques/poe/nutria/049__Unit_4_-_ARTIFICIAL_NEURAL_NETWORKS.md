
## Unit 4 - ARTIFICIAL NEURAL NETWORKS

1. Artificial Neural Networks (ANNs) are computational models inspired by the structure and functions of biological neural networks. 
2. ANNs are composed of a large number of interconnected processing elements (neurons) which work together to solve a given problem. 
3. Each neuron is connected to other neurons in the network, and they exchange information with each other by sending signals. 
4. The signals propagate through the network, and the neurons process the information to produce an output. 
5. The output of the network is determined by the weights of the connections between the neurons and the activation functions of the neurons. 
6. The weights and activation functions are adjusted during the training process to improve the accuracy of the output. 
7. ANNs can be used for a variety of tasks, including pattern recognition, classification, and prediction. 
8. ANNs are widely used in many fields, such as computer vision, natural language processing, and robotics.
