
### Genetic Algorithm 

* Genetic Algorithms (GAs) are a form of evolutionary computing which mimics the process of natural selection by applying the principles of evolution to optimize a problem.
* GAs are a type of Machine Learning technique which uses a population of solutions to a problem and applies a set of operators to evolve the population over time.
* GAs are used to solve optimization problems, where the goal is to find the best solution to a given problem.
* GAs operate by creating a population of candidate solutions and then applying a set of operators such as mutation, crossover and selection to evolve the population.
* The selection operator is used to select the best solutions from the population and the crossover operator is used to combine two solutions to create a new solution.
* The mutation operator is used to introduce random changes to the population in order to explore new solutions.
* GAs are used to solve a wide range of problems, from scheduling problems to parameter optimization.
