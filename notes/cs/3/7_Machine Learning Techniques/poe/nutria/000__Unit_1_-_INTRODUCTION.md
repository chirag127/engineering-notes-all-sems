
## Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION

1. Introduction is the first step to understanding a particular topic. It provides an overview of the topic and its key concepts. 
2. It outlines the goals and objectives of the topic, as well as the context in which it is studied. 
3. Introduction also includes a brief history of the topic, its importance, and any relevant research or theories related to it. 
4. Finally, introduction should provide a summary of the main points of the topic, as well as any important questions or issues that may arise during its study.
