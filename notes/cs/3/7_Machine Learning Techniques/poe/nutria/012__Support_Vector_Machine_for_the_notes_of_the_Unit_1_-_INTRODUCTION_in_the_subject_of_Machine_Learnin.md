

### Support Vector Machine

* Support Vector Machines (SVMs) are supervised learning models used for classification and regression tasks.
* SVMs are based on the concept of finding a hyperplane that best divides a dataset into two classes.
* The goal of SVM is to find the best separating hyperplane with the largest margin between the two classes.
* SVMs are powerful and efficient models for linear and non-linear classification problems.
* SVMs can handle high dimensional data, such as text and images, with ease.
* SVMs are also robust to noise, meaning that they can still make accurate predictions even when the data is noisy or incomplete.
* SVMs can also be used for regression tasks, where the goal is to predict a continuous value instead of a class label.
* In addition, SVMs are also capable of performing kernel trick, which allows them to transform the data into a higher dimensional space to find a better separating hyperplane.
