### Artificial Neural Network for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of Machine Learning Techniques

Artificial Neural Network (ANN) is a framework that simulates the functioning of the human brain to process information. ANN is extensively used in various applications such as computer vision, speech recognition, natural language processing, and many more. In this unit, we will discuss the following points related to ANN:

- The concept of neurons: Neurons are the fundamental building blocks of ANN. They receive inputs, process them, and produce outputs. We will study the structure of neurons and their functioning.

- Types of neurons: There are different types of neurons such as input, hidden, and output. We will discuss their roles in ANN.

- Activation functions: Activation functions are used to introduce non-linearity in the output of neurons. We will study different activation functions such as sigmoid, ReLU, and tanh.

- Architecture of ANN: ANN can have different architectures such as feedforward, recurrent, and convolutional. We will discuss their structure and applications.

- Training of ANN: ANN is trained using a process called backpropagation. We will study the backpropagation algorithm and its variants such as stochastic gradient descent and mini-batch gradient descent.

- Regularization techniques: Regularization techniques are used to prevent overfitting of ANN. We will study different techniques such as L1 and L2 regularization.

- Applications of ANN: ANN is extensively used in various applications such as image classification, speech recognition, and natural language processing. We will discuss some of the applications of ANN.

In conclusion, ANN is a powerful framework that simulates the functioning of the human brain to process information. It has numerous applications in various fields and is continually evolving. Understanding the concepts of ANN is crucial for building efficient machine learning models.
