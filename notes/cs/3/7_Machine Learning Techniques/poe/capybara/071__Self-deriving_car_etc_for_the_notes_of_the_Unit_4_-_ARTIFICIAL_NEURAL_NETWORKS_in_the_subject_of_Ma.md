### Self-deriving car and its relation to Artificial Neural Networks

Here are some key points to understand the concept of Self-deriving car and its relation to Artificial Neural Networks:

- Self-deriving cars are autonomous vehicles that can drive themselves without human intervention. They use a combination of sensors, cameras, and artificial intelligence to navigate roads and make decisions.
- Artificial Neural Networks (ANNs) are a subset of machine learning that are designed to mimic the way the human brain works. ANNs are composed of multiple interconnected nodes that process information and make decisions based on that information.
- Self-deriving cars use ANNs to process information from their sensors and cameras. The ANNs can recognize objects on the road, such as other vehicles, pedestrians, and traffic signals, and make decisions about how to navigate around them.
- ANNs are particularly well-suited to the task of self-driving cars because they are very good at recognizing patterns in data. Self-driving cars generate huge amounts of data, and ANNs can process this data quickly and accurately to make decisions in real-time.
- One of the challenges of developing self-driving cars is ensuring that they are safe and reliable. ANNs can help with this by continuously learning and adapting to new situations. As self-driving cars encounter new scenarios on the road, ANNs can update their decision-making processes to handle these situations more effectively.
- Overall, the use of ANNs in self-driving cars has the potential to revolutionize the transportation industry by making driving safer, more efficient, and more accessible to people who are unable to drive themselves.
