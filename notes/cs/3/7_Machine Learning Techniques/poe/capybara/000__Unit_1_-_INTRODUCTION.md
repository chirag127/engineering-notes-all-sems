## Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION

In this unit, we will cover the following topics:

- Definition of Artificial Intelligence (AI) and its history
- Types of AI
- Applications of AI
- Advantages and disadvantages of AI

### Definition of Artificial Intelligence (AI) and its history

- AI is a branch of computer science that involves the development of intelligent machines that can perform tasks that typically require human intelligence, such as visual perception, speech recognition, decision making, and language translation.
- The term "Artificial Intelligence" was coined in 1956 by John McCarthy, who is considered the father of AI.
- AI has its roots in the early 20th century, when scientists began exploring the concept of machine intelligence.
- The first AI program was developed in 1951 by Christopher Strachey, who created a checkers-playing program on the Ferranti Mark I computer.

### Types of AI

- There are two main types of AI: narrow AI and general AI.
- Narrow AI, also known as weak AI, is designed to perform a specific task, such as facial recognition or language translation.
- General AI, also known as strong AI, is designed to perform any intellectual task that a human can do, and can learn and adapt to new situations.

### Applications of AI

- AI has numerous applications across various fields, such as healthcare, finance, education, and transportation.
- In healthcare, AI is used for medical imaging, drug discovery, and patient monitoring.
- In finance, AI is used for fraud detection, investment analysis, and trading.
- In education, AI is used for personalized learning and student assessment.
- In transportation, AI is used for autonomous vehicles and traffic management.

### Advantages and disadvantages of AI

- Advantages of AI include increased efficiency, accuracy, and productivity, as well as the ability to perform tasks that are too dangerous or difficult for humans.
- Disadvantages of AI include job displacement, privacy concerns, and the potential misuse of AI for nefarious purposes.
- It is important to balance the benefits of AI with its potential risks and to ensure that AI is developed and used responsibly.
