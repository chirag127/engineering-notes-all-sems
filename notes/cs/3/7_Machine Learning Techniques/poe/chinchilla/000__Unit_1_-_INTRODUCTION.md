## Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION

In this unit, we will introduce the topic and provide an overview of the course. It is important to understand the basics before diving deeper into the subject matter. Therefore, we will cover the following topics:

- What is the course about? We will provide a brief overview of the subject matter and explain its significance in the real world.
- Course objectives: We will outline the main objectives of the course and what students can expect to learn by the end of it.
- Course structure: We will provide an overview of the course structure and how it will be delivered to students. This includes the number of lessons, assignments, and assessments.
- Learning outcomes: We will highlight the expected learning outcomes of the course and how they will benefit students in their future endeavors.
- Resources: We will provide a list of resources that students can refer to in order to enhance their learning experience. This may include textbooks, online resources, and other relevant materials.
- Assessment criteria: We will explain how students will be assessed in this course and what criteria will be used to evaluate their performance.

It is important to note that this course is designed to be challenging and will require a significant amount of effort and dedication on the part of the student. However, it is also designed to be engaging and rewarding, and we hope that students will find it both interesting and informative.

We encourage students to actively participate in the course and to ask questions whenever they need clarification on any topic. We believe that active engagement is key to successful learning, and we look forward to working with students to help them achieve their goals.
