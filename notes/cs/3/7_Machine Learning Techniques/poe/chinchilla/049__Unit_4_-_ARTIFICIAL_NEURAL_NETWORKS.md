## Unit 4 - ARTIFICIAL NEURAL NETWORKS

Artificial Neural Networks (ANN) are a class of computational models that are inspired by the structure and function of biological neural networks. ANNs are used in a wide range of applications such as image and speech recognition, natural language processing, and autonomous vehicles.

In this unit, we will discuss the following topics related to Artificial Neural Networks:

1. Introduction to Artificial Neural Networks:
    - Definition and brief history of Artificial Neural Networks.
    - Advantages and disadvantages of using ANNs.

2. Neuron Model:
    - Structure and function of a neuron.
    - Types of neurons.
    - Activation functions.

3. Learning Algorithms:
    - Supervised learning algorithms (Backpropagation, Gradient Descent).
    - Unsupervised learning algorithms (Self-Organizing Maps, Kohonen Networks).
    - Reinforcement learning.

4. Types of Neural Networks:
    - Feedforward Neural Networks.
    - Recurrent Neural Networks.
    - Convolutional Neural Networks.

5. Applications of Artificial Neural Networks:
    - Image and speech recognition.
    - Natural Language Processing.
    - Autonomous Vehicles.
    - Stock market prediction.
    - Medical diagnosis.

6. Limitations of Artificial Neural Networks:
    - Overfitting.
    - Interpretability.
    - Computational resources.

7. Future of Artificial Neural Networks:
    - Advancements in hardware and software.
    - Integration with other technologies.

In conclusion, Artificial Neural Networks are a powerful tool for solving complex problems in various fields. Understanding the structure and function of ANNs, learning algorithms, and their applications can be beneficial for researchers, engineers, and developers. However, we must also be aware of their limitations and work towards improving their performance and interpretability.
