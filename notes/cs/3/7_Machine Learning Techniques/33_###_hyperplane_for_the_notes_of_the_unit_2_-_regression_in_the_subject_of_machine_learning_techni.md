### Hyperplane for the notes of the Unit 2 - REGRESSION in the subject of Machine Learning Techniques

Hyperplane is a concept in linear algebra and machine learning that refers to a subspace of a high-dimensional space. In machine learning, hyperplanes are used to separate data into different classes, and they play a key role in many supervised learning algorithms, including support vector machines (SVMs) and linear discriminant analysis (LDA).

The following are some of the key concepts related to hyperplanes:

1. Linear Separation: Hyperplanes are used to separate data into different classes by finding a linear boundary between the classes.

2. Maximum Margin: The maximum margin is the distance between the hyperplane and the closest data points, and it is used to measure the quality of the separation.

3. Support Vectors: Support vectors are the data points that are closest to the hyperplane, and they play a key role in support vector machines (SVMs).

4. Linear Discriminant Analysis (LDA): Linear Discriminant Analysis (LDA) is a supervised learning algorithm that uses hyperplanes to separate data into different classes.

5. Non-Linear Separation: Hyperplanes can also be used to separate data into different classes in non-linear spaces by mapping the data into a higher-dimensional space and finding a linear boundary in the higher-dimensional space.

You will also learn about the key concepts related to hyperplanes, including linear separation, maximum margin, support vectors, linear discriminant analysis (LDA), and non-linear separation.

This unit will provide a foundation for understanding the principles and practices of hyperplanes, and for exploring the various concepts and techniques used in the field of machine learning.
