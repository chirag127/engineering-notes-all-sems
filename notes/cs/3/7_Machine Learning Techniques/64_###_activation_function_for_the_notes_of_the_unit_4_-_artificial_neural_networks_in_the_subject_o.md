### Activation function for the notes of the Unit 4 - ARTIFICIAL NEURAL NETWORKS in the subject of Machine Learning Techniques

Activation functions are mathematical operations applied to the inputs of each neuron in an artificial neural network to determine the output.

1. Sigmoid: maps any input to the range of 0 to 1, ideal for binary classification problems.

2. ReLU (Rectified Linear Unit): sets negative inputs to 0, speeds up training and prevents vanishing gradients.

3. Tanh (hyperbolic tangent): maps inputs to the range of -1 to 1, often used in recurrent neural networks.

4. Softmax: maps inputs to a probability distribution over multiple classes, ideal for multi-class classification problems.

The choice of activation function depends on the specific problem and network architecture.
