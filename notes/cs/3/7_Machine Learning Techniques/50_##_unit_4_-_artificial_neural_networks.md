## Unit 4 - ARTIFICIAL NEURAL NETWORKS

Artificial Neural Networks (ANNs) are machine learning models inspired by the structure and function of the human brain. They are used for a variety of tasks, including classification, regression, and pattern recognition.

The following are some of the key concepts related to Artificial Neural Networks:

1. Neurons: Neurons are the basic units of an ANN, and they are used to represent the relationships between the inputs and outputs of the network.

2. Weights: Weights are the parameters of an ANN, and they are used to control the strength of the connections between neurons.

3. Activation Functions: Activation functions are mathematical functions used in ANNs to determine the output of a neuron, and they are used to introduce non-linearity into the network.

4. Backpropagation: Backpropagation is an algorithm used to train ANNs, and it involves updating the weights of the network to minimize the error between the predicted and actual outputs.

5. Overfitting: Overfitting is a phenomenon in which an ANN fits the training data too closely, and it is used to make predictions about the outcome of a particular event.

6. Regularization: Regularization is a technique used to prevent overfitting in ANNs, and it involves adding a penalty term to the error function to discourage the network from fitting the training data too closely.

In this unit, you will learn about Artificial Neural Networks, and you will learn how to use ANNs for a variety of tasks, including classification, regression, and pattern recognition. You will also learn about neurons, weights, activation functions, backpropagation, overfitting, and regularization, and you will learn how these concepts are used in ANNs.

This unit will provide a foundation for understanding the principles and practices of Artificial Neural Networks, and for exploring the various concepts and techniques used in the field of machine learning.
