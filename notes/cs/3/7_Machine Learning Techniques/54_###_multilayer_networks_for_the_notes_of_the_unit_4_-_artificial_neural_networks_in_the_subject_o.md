### Multilayer networks for the notes of the Unit 4 - ARTIFICIAL NEURAL NETWORKS in the subject of Machine Learning Techniques

Multilayer networks, also known as feedforward neural networks, are composed of multiple interconnected layers of artificial neurons. They are used for supervised learning tasks, such as classification and regression. Each layer processes input data and passes the output to the next layer. The input layer receives the raw input data, while the output layer provides the prediction. The intermediate layers, known as hidden layers, learn complex representations of the input data. The connections between neurons are weighted, and the weights are updated during training based on the error between the predicted output and the true output. Activation functions are used to introduce non-linearity into the network. Popular activation functions include sigmoid, tanh, and ReLU. The number of hidden layers and the number of neurons in each layer are hyperparameters that can be tuned to optimize performance.
