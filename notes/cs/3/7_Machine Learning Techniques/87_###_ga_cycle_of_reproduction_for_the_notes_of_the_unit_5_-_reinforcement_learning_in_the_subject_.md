### GA cycle of reproduction for the notes of the Unit 5 - REINFORCEMENT LEARNING in the subject of Machine Learning Techniques

The genetic algorithm (GA) cycle of reproduction is a key component of genetic algorithms, which are optimization algorithms inspired by the process of natural selection. The GA cycle of reproduction involves the following steps:

1. Selection: In the selection step, individuals from the current population are selected to be parents for the next generation based on their fitness, which is a measure of how well they solve the problem.

2. Crossover: In the crossover step, pairs of parents are combined to create offspring, which are new individuals that inherit traits from their parents.

3. Mutation: In the mutation step, small random changes are made to the offspring to introduce new traits into the population.

4. Evaluation: In the evaluation step, the fitness of each individual in the population is determined, and the individuals are ranked based on their fitness.

5. Replacement: In the replacement step, the individuals in the population are replaced with the offspring, and the cycle of reproduction is repeated.

You will also learn about the key concepts related to the GA cycle of reproduction, including selection, crossover, mutation, evaluation, and replacement.
