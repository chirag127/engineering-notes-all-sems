### Decision surface for the notes of the Unit 2 - REGRESSION in the subject of Machine Learning Techniques

Unit 2 of the Machine Learning Techniques course focuses on Regression and covers the aspect of Decision Surface.

A decision surface is a graphical representation of the boundary between different classes in a classification problem. The decision surface is a surface in the feature space that separates the different classes, and the goal of a classifier is to find the decision surface that best separates the classes based on the training data.

In the context of regression, the decision surface is a surface in the feature space that separates the different values of the target variable. The goal of a regression algorithm is to find the decision surface that best fits the training data, and to use this surface to make predictions on new, unseen data.

In the context of the Machine Learning Techniques course, students in Unit 2 will learn about Decision Surface as part of the discussion on Regression. This may include working with examples of decision surfaces, analyzing the benefits and limitations of different decision surfaces, and learning how to choose the appropriate decision surface for a given problem.
