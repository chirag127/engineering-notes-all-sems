### Gradient descent and the Delta rule for the notes of the Unit 4 - ARTIFICIAL NEURAL NETWORKS in the subject of Machine Learning Techniques

Gradient Descent:
- It is an optimization algorithm used to minimize the cost function in machine learning.
- It works by iteratively adjusting the parameters of a model in the direction of the negative gradient of the cost function.
- The magnitude of the adjustment is determined by the learning rate, which controls the size of the step taken in the direction of the negative gradient.

Delta Rule:
- It is a rule for updating the weights in a single-layer perceptron, a type of artificial neural network.
- The delta rule calculates the error between the predicted output and the true output, and adjusts the weights in proportion to the error.
- The delta rule is a special case of the more general backpropagation algorithm, which is used to train multi-layer neural networks.
