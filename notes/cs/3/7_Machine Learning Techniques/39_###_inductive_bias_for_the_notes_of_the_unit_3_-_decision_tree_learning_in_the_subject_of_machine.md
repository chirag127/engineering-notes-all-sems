### Inductive bias for the notes of the Unit 3 - DECISION TREE LEARNING in the subject of Machine Learning Techniques

The Inductive Bias section of the Unit 3 - Decision Tree Learning in the subject of Machine Learning Techniques should cover the following topics:

1. Definition of Inductive Bias (the assumptions made by a learning algorithm about the underlying pattern in the data)
2. Overview of Inductive Bias (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
3. Inductive Bias and Decision Tree Learning (how inductive bias affects the performance of decision tree learning algorithms)
4. Types of Inductive Bias (preferential bias, representational bias, computational bias)
5. Overview of Types of Inductive Bias (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
6. Preferential Bias (the bias introduced by the choice of data to be used for training)
7. Overview of Preferential Bias (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
8. Representational Bias (the bias introduced by the choice of representation for the data)
9. Overview of Representational Bias (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
10. Computational Bias (the bias introduced by the choice of computational model)
11. Overview of Computational Bias (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
12. Inductive Bias and Overfitting (how inductive bias can lead to overfitting)
13. Inductive Bias and Underfitting (how inductive bias can lead to underfitting)
14. Techniques for avoiding inductive bias (using cross-validation, regularization, etc.)
15. Further reading and resources (books, articles, online resources)

Inductive Bias is an important component of the subject of Machine Learning Techniques. Understanding the principles and techniques of inductive bias helps students to develop the skills required to design and implement decision tree learning algorithms. The hands-on practice and further reading resources will help students to develop their skills and deepen their understanding of the material.
