### Artificial Neural Network

- An artificial neural network (ANN) is a **computational model** based on the structure and functions of **biological neural networks**.
- It consists of a **network of nodes** or **artificial neurons** that can process and transmit information.
- Each node has an **input**, an **output**, a **weight**, and a **threshold**.
- The input is the **signal** or **data** that the node receives from other nodes or external sources.
- The output is the **signal** or **data** that the node sends to other nodes or external sources.
- The weight is the **strength** or **importance** of the connection between nodes.
- The threshold is the **minimum** or **maximum** value of the input that triggers the node to produce an output.
- The nodes are arranged in **layers**, such as an **input layer**, one or more **hidden layers**, and an **output layer**.
- The input layer receives the **raw data** or **features** from the external sources.
- The hidden layer performs the **computation** or **transformation** of the data using **activation functions**.
- The output layer produces the **final result** or **prediction** of the network.
- The network can **learn** from the data by **adjusting** the weights and thresholds using **learning algorithms**.
- The network can **solve** complex problems that are **difficult** for traditional algorithms, such as **image recognition**, **natural language processing**, **speech synthesis**, etc.
- The network is a **subset** of **machine learning** and is at the **heart** of **deep learning** algorithms.
