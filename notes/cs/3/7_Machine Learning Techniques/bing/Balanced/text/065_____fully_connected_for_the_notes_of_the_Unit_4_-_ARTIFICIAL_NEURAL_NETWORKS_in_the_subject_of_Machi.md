### Fully Connected Neural Network

- A fully connected neural network is a type of artificial neural network where all the nodes or neurons in one layer are connected to all the neurons in the next layer.
- A fully connected layer is a function from ℝ m to ℝ n that applies a linear transformation to the input vector through a weights matrix.
- The major advantage of fully connected networks is that they are “structure agnostic” i.e. there are no special assumptions about the input data, such as spatial or temporal dependencies.
- The major disadvantage of fully connected networks is that they are computationally expensive and prone to overfitting, especially when the input data is high-dimensional, such as images or videos.
- Fully connected networks are often used as the final layers of a deep neural network, after convolutional or recurrent layers, to produce the output predictions .
- Fully connected networks can be implemented using various frameworks, such as TensorFlow, PyTorch, Keras, etc.
