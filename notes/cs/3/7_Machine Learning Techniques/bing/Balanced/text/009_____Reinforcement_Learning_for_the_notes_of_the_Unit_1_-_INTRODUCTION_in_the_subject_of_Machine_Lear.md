### Reinforcement Learning

- Reinforcement learning is the science of decision making .
- Reinforcement learning involves no supervisor and only a reward signal is used for an agent to determine if they are doing well or not .
- Time is a key component in RL where the process is sequential with delayed feedback .
- Reinforcement learning is a computational approach to learning whereby an agent tries to maximize the total amount of reward it receives when interacting with a complex, uncertain environment .
- Reinforcement learning has been successfully applied in many domains, including systems such as AlphaZero, that learnt to master the games of chess, Go and Shogi.
