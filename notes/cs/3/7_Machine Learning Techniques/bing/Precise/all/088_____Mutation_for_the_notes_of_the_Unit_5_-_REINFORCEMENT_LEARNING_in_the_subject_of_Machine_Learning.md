# Mutation in Reinforcement Learning

- Mutation is a concept in reinforcement learning that refers to the process of introducing changes or variations in the behavior of an agent.
- Mutation can be used to enable faster convergence and natural exploration, which are key to successful reinforcement learning .
- Mutation can be used in combination with other techniques, such as deep neuroevolution, to leverage the parallel evaluations of individuals in the population .
- Mutation can also be used in the context of moving target defense (MTD), a proactive defense technology that can reduce the risk of vulnerabilities being exploited by attackers .
- Mutation testing is a technique used to evaluate the performance of reinforcement learning systems by introducing changes in reward rules, exploration and exploitation, and experience replay .
- Maximum Mutation Reinforcement Learning is a technique that combines the scalability of genetic methods with the data efficiency and optimal control of reinforcement learning .
