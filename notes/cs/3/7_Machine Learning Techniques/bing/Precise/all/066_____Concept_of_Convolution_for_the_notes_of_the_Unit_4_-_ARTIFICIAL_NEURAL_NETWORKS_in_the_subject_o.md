### Concept of Convolution for the notes of the Unit 4 - ARTIFICIAL NEURAL NETWORKS in the subject of Machine Learning Techniques

1. Convolution is a mathematical operation that is used to extract features from an input signal or image.
2. It is commonly used in image and signal processing, as well as in the field of artificial neural networks, particularly in convolutional neural networks (CNNs).
3. The basic idea behind convolution is to slide a small filter or kernel over the input signal or image, and compute the dot product between the filter and the portion of the input signal or image that it is currently overlapping.
4. This operation is repeated for every possible position of the filter, resulting in a new output signal or image that represents the extracted features.
5. The filter or kernel is typically a small matrix of weights that is learned during the training process of the neural network.
6. The convolution operation can be thought of as a weighted sum, where the weights are determined by the filter and the input signal or image is weighted according to its local neighborhood.
7. Convolution can be used to perform various tasks, such as edge detection, blurring, sharpening, and noise reduction in images.
8. In the context of artificial neural networks, convolutional layers are used to extract features from the input data, which can then be used by subsequent layers to make predictions or decisions.
