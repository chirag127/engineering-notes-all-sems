### Inductive Bias

- Inductive bias refers to the set of assumptions that a learning algorithm makes to predict outputs for new inputs.
- In the context of decision tree learning, inductive bias is the preference for certain types of trees over others.
- The inductive bias of a decision tree learning algorithm can be influenced by factors such as the tree construction algorithm, the attribute selection measure, and the pruning method.
- Common attribute selection measures used in decision tree learning include information gain, gain ratio, and gini index.
- Pruning methods, such as reduced error pruning and cost complexity pruning, can also affect the inductive bias of a decision tree learning algorithm by reducing the size of the tree and removing branches that may overfit the data.
- The choice of inductive bias can have a significant impact on the performance of a decision tree learning algorithm, and it is important to choose an appropriate bias for the given problem and data set.
