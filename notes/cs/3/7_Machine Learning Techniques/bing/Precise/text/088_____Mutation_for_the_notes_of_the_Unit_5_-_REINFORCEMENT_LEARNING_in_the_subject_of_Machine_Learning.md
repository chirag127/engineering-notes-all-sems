### Mutation in Reinforcement Learning

- Mutation is a concept in Reinforcement Learning (RL) that leverages on the parallel evaluations of individuals in the population and natural mutation.
- This deep neuroevolution enables faster convergence and natural exploration, which are key to successful Reinforcement Learning.
- Mutation can be used in various applications, such as mutating malware to bypass AI-powered classifiers while keeping its functionality intact.
- It can also be used in the context of moving target defense (MTD), a proactive defense technology that reduces the risk of vulnerabilities exploited by attackers.
- Mutation testing is a technique used to evaluate the quality of RL systems by introducing faults into the system and observing its behavior.
- Maximum Mutation Reinforcement Learning is a method that combines the scalability of genetic methods with the data efficiency and optimal control of RL.
