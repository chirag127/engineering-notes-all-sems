# Unit 5 - Reinforcement Learning
## Genetic Algorithms

- Genetic algorithms are a type of optimization algorithm that use principles of natural selection and genetics to find solutions to problems.
- They work by representing potential solutions to a problem as a population of individuals, each with a set of characteristics (or genes) that determine their fitness for the problem at hand.
- The algorithm then applies genetic operators such as selection, crossover, and mutation to evolve the population over multiple generations, with the goal of finding the fittest individual (i.e. the best solution to the problem).
- Selection involves choosing the fittest individuals from the population to reproduce and pass their genes to the next generation.
- Crossover involves combining the genes of two individuals to create one or more offspring with characteristics from both parents.
- Mutation involves randomly changing the genes of an individual to introduce new characteristics into the population.
- Genetic algorithms are commonly used in optimization problems where the search space is large and complex, and where traditional optimization methods may struggle to find good solutions.
- They have been applied to a wide range of problems, including scheduling, routing, and machine learning.
