### Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION: History of Machine Learning

- Machine learning was first conceived from the mathematical modeling of neural networks. A paper by logician Walter Pitts and neuroscientist Warren McCulloch, published in 1943, attempted to mathematically map out thought processes and decision making in human cognition.
- The term “Machine Learning” was coined in 1952 by Arthur Samuel, a computer scientist at IBM and a pioneer in AI and computer gaming. He designed a computer program for playing checkers.
- In 1956, the Dartmouth Workshop is widely considered to be the founding event of artificial intelligence as a field.
- In 1957, Frank Rosenblatt – at the Cornell Aeronautical Laboratory – combined Donald Hebb’s model of learning with the McCulloch-Pitts neuron to create the Perceptron, an algorithm for pattern recognition.
- Machine learning is a subfield of artificial intelligence that gives computers the ability to learn without explicitly being programmed.
