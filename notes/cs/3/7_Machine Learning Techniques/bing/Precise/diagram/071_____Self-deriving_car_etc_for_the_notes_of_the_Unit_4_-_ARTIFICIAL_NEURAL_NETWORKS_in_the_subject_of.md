### Unit 4 - ARTIFICIAL NEURAL NETWORKS
#### Self-driving cars

- Self-driving cars, also known as autonomous vehicles, use a combination of sensors, cameras, and artificial intelligence to navigate and drive the vehicle.
- Artificial Neural Networks (ANNs) are a key component in the development of self-driving cars.
- ANNs are used to process the data collected by the sensors and cameras, and make decisions based on that data.
- The decisions made by the ANNs can include things like steering, accelerating, and braking.
- ANNs are trained using large amounts of data, allowing them to learn and improve their decision-making over time.
- The use of ANNs in self-driving cars allows for a more efficient and safer driving experience.
