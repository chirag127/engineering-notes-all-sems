### Mutation

- Mutation is a process of altering the parameters or structure of an agent or a policy in reinforcement learning (RL) to generate new variants that can explore the state-action space more effectively .
- Mutation can be inspired by natural evolution, where genetic operators such as crossover and mutation are applied to a population of individuals to produce offspring that inherit some traits from their parents and introduce some variations .
- Mutation can also be guided by deep learning techniques, such as generative adversarial networks (GANs), where a generator network tries to produce mutated samples that can fool a discriminator network that distinguishes between original and mutated samples.
- Mutation can be used to enhance the scalability, diversity, robustness, and generalization of RL agents or policies, especially in complex and dynamic environments  .
- Mutation can also be used to test the security and reliability of RL systems, by generating adversarial or faulty samples that can challenge the performance or functionality of the system .
- Mutation can be controlled by various hyperparameters, such as mutation rate, mutation strength, mutation probability, mutation distribution, etc., that affect the quality and quantity of the mutated samples  .
- Mutation can be evaluated by various metrics, such as fitness, diversity, novelty, similarity, etc., that measure the effectiveness and efficiency of the mutation process   .
