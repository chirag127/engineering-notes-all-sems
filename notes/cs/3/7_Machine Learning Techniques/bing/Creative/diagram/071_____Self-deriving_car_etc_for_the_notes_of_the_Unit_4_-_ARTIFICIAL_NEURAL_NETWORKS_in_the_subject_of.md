### Self-driving car artificial neural networks

- Self-driving cars are vehicles that can operate autonomously without human intervention, using sensors, cameras, maps, and software to perceive the environment and control the vehicle .
- Artificial neural networks (ANNs) are computational models that mimic the structure and function of biological neurons, with layers of interconnected nodes that process and transmit information .
- ANNs are used by self-driving cars for various tasks, such as image recognition, object detection, lane detection, semantic segmentation, depth estimation, path planning, and decision making   .
- ANNs can learn from large amounts of data and adapt to changing situations, making them suitable for self-driving cars that face complex and dynamic scenarios on the road .
- ANNs can also be combined with other machine learning techniques, such as reinforcement learning, evolutionary algorithms, and generative adversarial networks, to improve the performance and robustness of self-driving cars  .
- Some examples of self-driving car companies that use ANNs are Tesla, Waymo, Uber, and Baidu .
