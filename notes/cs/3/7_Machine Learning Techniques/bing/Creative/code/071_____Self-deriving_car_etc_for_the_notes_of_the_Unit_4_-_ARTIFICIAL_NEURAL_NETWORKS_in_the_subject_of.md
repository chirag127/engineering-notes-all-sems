### Self-driving car artificial neural networks

- A self-driving car is a vehicle that can operate autonomously without human intervention, using sensors, cameras, radars, and artificial intelligence to perceive the environment and navigate safely .
- Artificial neural networks (ANNs) are computational models that mimic the structure and function of biological neurons, with layers of interconnected nodes that process and transmit information  .
- ANNs are widely used in self-driving cars for tasks such as image recognition, object detection, lane detection, semantic segmentation, depth estimation, path planning, and control     .
- ANNs can learn from large amounts of data and adapt to changing situations, making them suitable for complex and dynamic environments such as roads  .
- ANNs can also be biologically inspired, such as the nematode-inspired neural network (N2Net) that imitates the nervous system of a simple worm to process information efficiently and robustly.
- ANNs face some challenges in self-driving cars, such as high computational cost, energy consumption, data quality, interpretability, safety, and ethical issues  .
- ANNs are constantly evolving and improving, with new architectures, algorithms, and hardware that aim to overcome these challenges and enable more intelligent and reliable self-driving cars   .
