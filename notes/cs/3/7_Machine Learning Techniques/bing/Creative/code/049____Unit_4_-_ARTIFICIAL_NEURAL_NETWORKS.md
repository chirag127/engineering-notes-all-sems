# Unit 4 - ARTIFICIAL NEURAL NETWORKS

- Artificial neural networks (ANNs) are **computational models** inspired by the **biological neural networks** that constitute animal brains.
- ANNs are composed of **layers** of **nodes** or **artificial neurons** that can **process** and **transmit** information .
- Each node has an **input**, an **output**, and an **activation function** that determines the output based on the input and a **threshold**.
- Each node is **connected** to other nodes by **weights** that represent the **strength** of the connection.
- ANNs can **learn** from **data** by **adjusting** the weights and thresholds through **learning algorithms** .
- ANNs can **approximate** complex **nonlinear functions** that are generally unknown or difficult to model .
- ANNs are a subset of **machine learning** and are at the heart of **deep learning** algorithms.
- ANNs have many **applications** in various fields, such as **image and voice recognition**, **natural language processing**, **robotics**, **data mining**, **medical diagnosis**, and more .
