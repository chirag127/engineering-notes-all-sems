### Bayesian belief networks for the notes of the Unit 2 - REGRESSION in the subject of Machine Learning Techniques

Bayesian Belief Networks (BBN) are probabilistic graphical models that represent the relationships between variables and their probabilities. BBN is used to model uncertainty and to make predictions based on uncertain inputs.

The following are some of the key concepts related to Bayesian Belief Networks:

1. Probabilistic Graphical Models: Probabilistic Graphical Models (PGMs) are graphical models that represent the relationships between variables and their probabilities, and they are used to model uncertainty.

2. Nodes: Nodes in a Bayesian Belief Network represent variables, and they are used to represent the relationships between variables and their probabilities.

3. Edges: Edges in a Bayesian Belief Network represent the relationships between variables, and they are used to represent the dependencies between variables.

4. Conditional Probability Tables: Conditional Probability Tables (CPTs) are tables that represent the probabilities of variables given their parents in a Bayesian Belief Network, and they are used to make predictions based on uncertain inputs.

5. Inference: Inference is the process of making predictions based on uncertain inputs in a Bayesian Belief Network, and it is performed using the probabilities represented in the CPTs.

In this unit, you will learn about Bayesian Belief Networks, and you will learn how to use BBN to model uncertainty and make predictions based on uncertain inputs. You will also learn about nodes, edges, conditional probability tables, and inference in BBN.

This unit will provide a foundation for understanding the principles and practices of Bayesian Belief Networks, and for exploring the various concepts and techniques used in the field of machine learning.
