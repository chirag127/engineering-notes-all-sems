### Convolutional Layers for the notes of the Unit 4 - ARTIFICIAL NEURAL NETWORKS in the subject of Machine Learning Techniques

Convolutional Layers are a type of layer in artificial neural networks commonly used in image classification tasks. They apply filters to the input image to extract features such as edges, textures, and objects. The filters slide over the input image, computing dot products between their weights and the overlapping region of the image. The result of this operation is a feature map, which is then passed through activation functions such as ReLU to introduce non-linearity. Pooling layers are often used after convolutional layers to reduce the spatial dimensions of the feature maps, making the network more computationally efficient. Convolutional layers are essential in deep learning for image recognition tasks, as they allow the network to learn hierarchical representations of the input data.
