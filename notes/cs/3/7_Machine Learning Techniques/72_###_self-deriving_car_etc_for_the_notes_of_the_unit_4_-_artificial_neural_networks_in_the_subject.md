### Self-deriving car etc for the notes of the Unit 4 - ARTIFICIAL NEURAL NETWORKS in the subject of Machine Learning Techniques

Self-driving cars use Artificial Neural Networks (ANNs) for decision-making. ANNs are inspired by the structure and function of the human brain, and are trained to identify patterns in data.

In self-driving cars, ANNs are used to process sensory data from cameras, lidar, radar, and other sensors to make decisions about steering, acceleration, and braking.

The training process involves feeding the ANN large amounts of data, such as images and sensor readings, and adjusting the weights of the connections between neurons in the network to minimize errors in predictions.

Once trained, the ANN can make predictions in real-time, allowing the car to make decisions about how to navigate its environment.

Some challenges in implementing ANNs in self-driving cars include ensuring the network is robust to different driving conditions and that it can handle the real-time processing requirements of the system.

Overall, ANNs play a crucial role in enabling self-driving cars to make informed decisions and navigate their environment safely and efficiently.
