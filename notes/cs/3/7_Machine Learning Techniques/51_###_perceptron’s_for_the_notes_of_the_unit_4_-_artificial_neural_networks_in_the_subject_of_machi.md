### Perceptron’s for the notes of the Unit 4 - ARTIFICIAL NEURAL NETWORKS in the subject of Machine Learning Techniques

The Perceptrons section of the Unit 4 - Artificial Neural Networks in the subject of Machine Learning Techniques should cover the following topics:

1. Definition of Perceptrons (a type of artificial neural network that consists of a single layer of artificial neurons)
2. Overview of Perceptrons (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
3. Artificial Neurons (units in an artificial neural network that process information)
4. Overview of Artificial Neurons (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
5. Perceptron Algorithm (an algorithm for training perceptrons)
6. Overview of Perceptron Algorithm (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
7. Perceptron Learning Rule (the rule used to update the weights of the artificial neurons)
8. Overview of Perceptron Learning Rule (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
9. Limitations of Perceptrons (linear separability, limited representation power)
10. Techniques for overcoming limitations (using advanced techniques, improving accuracy, etc.)
11. Perceptrons and Industry Applications (how perceptrons are used in various industries)
12. Further reading and resources (books, articles, online resources)

Perceptrons are an important component of the subject of Machine Learning Techniques. Understanding the principles and techniques of perceptrons helps students to develop the skills required to design and implement artificial neural networks. The hands-on practice and further reading resources will help students to develop their skills and deepen their understanding of the material.
