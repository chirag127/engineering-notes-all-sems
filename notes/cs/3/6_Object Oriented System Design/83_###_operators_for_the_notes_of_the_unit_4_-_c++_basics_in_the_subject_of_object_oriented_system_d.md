### operators for the notes of the Unit 4 - C++ Basics in the subject of Object Oriented System Design

The Operators section of the Unit 4 - C++ Basics in the subject of Object Oriented System Design should cover the following topics:

1. Definition of Operators (symbols used to perform operations in C++)
2. Overview of Operators (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
3. Types of Operators (arithmetic, relational, logical, etc.)
4. Overview of Arithmetic Operators (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
5. Overview of Relational Operators (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
6. Overview of Logical Operators (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
7. Operator Precedence (the order in which operations are performed)
8. Overview of Operator Precedence (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
9. Assignment Operators (operators used to assign values to variables)
10. Overview of Assignment Operators (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
11. Compound Assignment Operators (operators that combine assignment and arithmetic operations)
12. Overview of Compound Assignment Operators (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
13. Increment and Decrement Operators (operators used to increment or decrement a value)
14. Overview of Increment and Decrement Operators (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
15. Conditional Operators (operators used to perform conditional operations)
16. Overview of Conditional Operators (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
17. Bitwise Operators (operators used to perform bitwise operations)
18. Overview of Bitwise Operators (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
19. Limitations of Operators (complexity, accuracy, etc.)
20. Techniques for overcoming limitations (using efficient algorithms, improving accuracy, etc.)
21. Further reading and resources (books, articles, online resources)

Operators are an important component of the subject of Object Oriented System Design. Understanding the principles and techniques of operators helps students to develop the skills required to use operators in C++. The hands-on practice and further reading resources will help students to develop their skills and deepen their understanding of the material.
