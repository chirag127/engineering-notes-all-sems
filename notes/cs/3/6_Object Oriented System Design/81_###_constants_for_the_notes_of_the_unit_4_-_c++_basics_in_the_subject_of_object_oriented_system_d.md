### constants for the notes of the Unit 4 - C++ Basics in the subject of Object Oriented System Design

The Constants section of the Unit 4 - C++ Basics in the subject of Object Oriented System Design should cover the following topics:

1. Definition of Constants (values that cannot be changed during program execution)
2. Overview of Constants (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
3. Types of Constants (integer, floating-point, character, etc.)
4. Overview of Types of Constants (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
5. Declaring Constants (using the "const" keyword to declare a constant)
6. Overview of Declaring Constants (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
7. Constants and Naming Conventions (naming conventions for constants)
8. Overview of Constants and Naming Conventions (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
9. Constants and Scope (the scope of a constant)
10. Overview of Constants and Scope (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
11. Constants and Performance (how constants impact system performance)
12. Limitations of Constants (complexity, accuracy, etc.)
13. Techniques for overcoming limitations (using efficient algorithms, improving accuracy, etc.)
14. Further reading and resources (books, articles, online resources)

Constants are an important component of the subject of Object Oriented System Design. Understanding the principles and techniques of constants helps students to develop the skills required to declare and use constants in C++ programs. The hands-on practice and further reading resources will help students to develop their skills and deepen their understanding of the material.
