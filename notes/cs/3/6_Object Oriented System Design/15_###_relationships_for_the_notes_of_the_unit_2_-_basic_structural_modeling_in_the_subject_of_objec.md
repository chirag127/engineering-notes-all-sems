### Relationships for the notes of the Unit 2 - Basic Structural Modeling in the subject of Object Oriented System Design

The Relationships section of the Unit 2 - Basic Structural Modeling in the subject of Object Oriented System Design should cover the following topics:

1. Definition of Relationships (the connections between objects in a system)
2. Overview of Relationships (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
3. Types of Relationships (aggregation, composition, inheritance, etc.)
4. Overview of Aggregation (a relationship in which one object is a part of another object)
5. Overview of Composition (a relationship in which one object is completely contained by another object)
6. Overview of Inheritance (a relationship in which one object is a specialized version of another object)
7. Overview of Association (a relationship in which two objects have a mutual dependency)
8. Overview of Dependency (a relationship in which one object depends on another object)
9. Representing Relationships (using UML diagrams, flow charts, etc.)
10. Relationships and Object Oriented Design (how relationships are used in object oriented design)
11. Relationships and System Design (how relationships are used in system design)
12. Limitations of Relationships (complexity, accuracy, etc.)
13. Techniques for overcoming limitations (using efficient algorithms, improving accuracy, etc.)
14. Further reading and resources (books, articles, online resources)

Relationships is an important component of the subject of Object Oriented System Design. Understanding the principles and techniques of relationships helps students to develop the skills required to represent and manipulate relationships in object oriented systems. The hands-on practice and further reading resources will help students to develop their skills and deepen their understanding of the material.
