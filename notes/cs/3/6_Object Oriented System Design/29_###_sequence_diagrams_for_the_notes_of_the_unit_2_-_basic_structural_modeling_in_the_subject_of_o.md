### Sequence Diagrams for the notes of the Unit 2 - Basic Structural Modeling in the subject of Object Oriented System Design

Unit 2 of the Object Oriented System Design course focuses on Basic Structural Modeling, including the aspect of Sequence Diagrams.

Sequence Diagrams are a type of UML (Unified Modeling Language) diagram that are used to represent the flow of messages and interactions between objects in a system. Sequence Diagrams are used to model the interactions between objects in a system, including the order in which messages are sent and received, and the relationships between objects.

In the context of the Object Oriented System Design course, students in Unit 2 will learn about Sequence Diagrams as part of the discussion on Basic Structural Modeling. This may include working with examples of Sequence Diagrams, analyzing the benefits and limitations of Sequence Diagrams, and learning how to use Sequence Diagrams to model the interactions between objects in a system.
