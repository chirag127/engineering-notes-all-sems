### polymorphism in collaboration Diagrams for the notes of the Unit 2 - Basic Structural Modeling in the subject of Object Oriented System Design

The Polymorphism in Collaboration Diagrams section of the Unit 2 - Basic Structural Modeling in the subject of Object Oriented System Design should cover the following topics:

1. Definition of Polymorphism (the ability of an object to take on multiple forms)
2. Overview of Polymorphism (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
3. Polymorphism in Object Oriented Programming (how polymorphism is implemented in object oriented programming languages)
4. Collaboration Diagrams (UML diagrams that show the relationships between objects and their behavior)
5. Overview of Collaboration Diagrams (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
6. Representing Polymorphism in Collaboration Diagrams (how polymorphism is represented in collaboration diagrams)
7. Polymorphism and Object Oriented Design (how polymorphism is used in object oriented design)
8. Polymorphism and System Design (how polymorphism is used in system design)
9. Limitations of Polymorphism (complexity, performance, accuracy)
10. Techniques for overcoming limitations (using efficient algorithms, reducing complexity, etc.)
11. Further reading and resources (books, articles, online resources)

Polymorphism in Collaboration Diagrams is an important component of the subject of Object Oriented System Design. Understanding the principles and techniques of polymorphism helps students to develop the skills required to represent and manipulate polymorphism in object oriented systems. The hands-on practice and further reading resources will help students to develop their skills and deepen their understanding of the material.
