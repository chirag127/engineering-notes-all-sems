### Basic Behavioural Modeling for the notes of the Unit 2 - Basic Structural Modeling in the subject of Object Oriented System Design

The Basic Behavioural Modeling section of the Unit 2 - Basic Structural Modeling in the subject of Object Oriented System Design should cover the following topics:

1. Definition of Behavioural Modeling (the process of modeling the behavior of objects in a system)
2. Overview of Behavioural Modeling (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
3. State Machines (models that describe the behavior of objects in different states)
4. Overview of State Machines (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
5. State Transitions (changes in the state of an object)
6. Overview of State Transitions (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
7. Event-Driven Behaviour (behavior that is triggered by events)
8. Overview of Event-Driven Behaviour (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
9. Statecharts (UML diagrams that describe the behavior of objects)
10. Overview of Statecharts (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
11. Activities (models that describe the behavior of objects in terms of activities)
12. Overview of Activities (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
13. Behavioural Modeling and Object Oriented Design (how Behavioural Modeling is used in Object Oriented Design)
14. Behavioural Modeling and System Design (how Behavioural Modeling is used in System Design)
15. Limitations of Behavioural Modeling (complexity, accuracy, etc.)
16. Techniques for overcoming limitations (using efficient algorithms, improving accuracy, etc.)
17. Further reading and resources (books, articles, online resources)

Basic Behavioural Modeling is an important component of the subject of Object Oriented System Design. Understanding the principles and techniques of Behavioural Modeling helps students to develop the skills required to model the behavior of objects in a system. The hands-on practice and further reading resources will help students to develop their skills and deepen their understanding of the material.
