### Deployment for the notes of the Unit 2 - Basic Structural Modeling in the subject of Object Oriented System Design

The Deployment section of the Unit 2 - Basic Structural Modeling in the subject of Object Oriented System Design should cover the following topics:

1. Definition of Deployment (the process of deploying a system to a production environment)
2. Overview of Deployment (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
3. Deployment Architecture (the components and relationships of a deployment architecture)
4. Overview of Deployment Architecture (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
5. Deployment Topology (the physical and logical arrangement of components in a deployment architecture)
6. Overview of Deployment Topology (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
7. Deployment Scenarios (typical deployment scenarios, such as single-node, multi-node, etc.)
8. Overview of Deployment Scenarios (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
9. Deployment Strategies (strategies for deploying a system, such as blue-green, rolling, etc.)
10. Overview of Deployment Strategies (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
11. Deployment Testing (testing a system before and after deployment)
12. Overview of Deployment Testing (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
13. Deployment Automation (automating the deployment process)
14. Overview of Deployment Automation (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
15. Limitations of Deployment (complexity, performance, accuracy)
16. Techniques for overcoming limitations (using efficient algorithms, reducing complexity, etc.)
17. Further reading and resources (books, articles, online resources)

Deployment is an important component of the subject of Object Oriented System Design. Understanding the principles and techniques of Deployment helps students to develop the skills required to deploy a system to a production environment. The hands-on practice and further reading resources will help students to develop their skills and deepen their understanding of the material.
