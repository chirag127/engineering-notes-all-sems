### iterated messages for the notes of the Unit 2 - Basic Structural Modeling in the subject of Object Oriented System Design

The Iterated Messages section of the Unit 2 - Basic Structural Modeling in the subject of Object Oriented System Design should cover the following topics:

1. Definition of Iterated Messages (repeated communication between objects in a system)
2. Overview of Iterated Messages (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
3. Iterated Messages in Object Oriented Design (how iterated messages are used in object oriented design)
4. Iterated Messages in System Design (how iterated messages are used in system design)
5. Representing Iterated Messages (using UML diagrams, flow charts, etc.)
6. Types of Iterated Messages (synchronous, asynchronous, etc.)
7. Overview of Synchronous Messages (messages that are sent and received at the same time)
8. Overview of Asynchronous Messages (messages that are sent and received at different times)
9. Iterated Messages and Performance (how iterated messages impact system performance)
10. Limitations of Iterated Messages (complexity, accuracy, etc.)
11. Techniques for overcoming limitations (using efficient algorithms, improving accuracy, etc.)
12. Further reading and resources (books, articles, online resources)

Iterated Messages is an important component of the subject of Object Oriented System Design. Understanding the principles and techniques of iterated messages helps students to develop the skills required to represent and manipulate iterated messages in object oriented systems. The hands-on practice and further reading resources will help students to develop their skills and deepen their understanding of the material.
