## Unit 2 - Basic Structural Modeling

1. Basic Structural Modeling is the process of creating a representation of a physical structure using mathematical and computational tools.
2. This representation can be used to analyze the behavior of the structure under different loading conditions and to design the structure to meet specific performance criteria.
3. The basic steps in structural modeling include defining the geometry of the structure, specifying the material properties, applying loads and boundary conditions, and solving the resulting equations to determine the response of the structure.
4. Common techniques used in structural modeling include finite element analysis, boundary element analysis, and analytical methods.
5. Structural modeling is an important tool in the design of buildings, bridges, aircraft, and other structures, and is used to ensure that these structures are safe, efficient, and able to withstand the loads and forces to which they will be subjected.
