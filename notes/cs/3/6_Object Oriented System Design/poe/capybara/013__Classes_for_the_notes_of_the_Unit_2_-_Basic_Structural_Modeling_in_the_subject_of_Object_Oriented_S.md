### Classes for the notes of the Unit 2 - Basic Structural Modeling in the subject of Object Oriented System Design

In the study of Object Oriented System Design, the concept of structural modeling is crucial. It involves the creation of a blueprint or model of a software system's structure, which helps in understanding the system's behavior and its various components. 

To create a structural model, we need to use classes. Classes are a fundamental building block of object-oriented programming and are used to define objects and their properties. In this unit, we will be discussing the classes that are used in Basic Structural Modeling. 

Here are the classes that you should be familiar with:

1. **Class Diagrams** - Class diagrams are used to show the relationships between classes and the properties and methods of those classes. It is one of the most commonly used diagrams in structural modeling.

2. **Object Diagrams** - Object diagrams are used to show the objects of a class and their relationships with other objects. They are a snapshot of a system at a particular point in time.

3. **Sequence Diagrams** - Sequence diagrams are used to show the interactions between objects and the order in which those interactions occur. They are used to model the behavior of a system.

4. **Collaboration Diagrams** - Collaboration diagrams are used to show the interactions between objects and the messages that they send to each other. They are used to model the communication between objects.

5. **State Diagrams** - State diagrams are used to model the behavior of an object and the transitions between different states of that object. They are used to represent the lifecycle of an object.

6. **Activity Diagrams** - Activity diagrams are used to model the flow of activities in a system. They are used to represent the steps that are taken to complete a task.

By understanding and using these classes, you will be able to create effective structural models for software systems. These models will help you to understand the behavior of the system and its various components, and will aid in the development of high-quality software systems.
