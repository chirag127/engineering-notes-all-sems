### Architectural Modeling

Architectural modeling is the process of creating a high-level abstract representation of a software system's architecture. It aims to provide a clear, concise and understandable view of the system's structure and organization. 

Architectural modeling is an essential aspect of Object-Oriented System Design, and it helps software developers understand and manage the complexity of the software system. Here are some key points to keep in mind:

- Architectural modeling is created using diagrams that represent the system's structure and organization, such as UML (Unified Modeling Language) diagrams or block diagrams.
- The purpose of architectural modeling is to provide a visual representation of the software system's structure and organization, making it easier for developers to understand and communicate with stakeholders.
- Architectural modeling helps developers identify potential issues and risks associated with the software system's architecture, such as scalability or maintainability issues.
- Architectural modeling is an iterative process that involves refining and improving the system's structure and organization over time as the software system evolves.

In conclusion, architectural modeling is an essential aspect of Object-Oriented System Design that helps software developers understand and manage the complexity of the software system. It provides a clear, concise and understandable view of the system's structure and organization, making it easier for developers to communicate with stakeholders and identify potential issues and risks associated with the software system's architecture.
