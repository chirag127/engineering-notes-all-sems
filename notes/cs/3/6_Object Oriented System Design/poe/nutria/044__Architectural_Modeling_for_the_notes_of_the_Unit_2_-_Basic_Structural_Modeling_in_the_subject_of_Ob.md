

### Architectural Modeling

* Architectural modeling is the process of creating a visual representation of a system's structure.
* It involves the use of diagrams to illustrate the relationships between components, components and their environment, and other aspects of the system.
* Architectural models can be used to represent the structure of a system, its behavior, and the interactions between components.
* Architectural models can be used to analyze and evaluate the performance of a system, and to plan for future changes and development.
* Architectural models can also be used to communicate design decisions and to document the system.
* Architectural models can be used to identify potential problems and risks, and to identify areas for improvement.
