
### Physical Packaging for the Notes of the Unit 3 - Object Oriented Analysis in the Subject of Object Oriented System Design

1. Physical packaging is an important part of the object-oriented system design process. It involves the packaging of the objects and their relationships into a physical form that can be deployed and used in a system. 
2. Physical packaging involves the selection of the appropriate structure for the system, such as a database, a library, a distributed system, or a web application. 
3. It also involves the design of the physical components, such as the hardware, the software, the communication protocols, and the security measures. 
4. Physical packaging should be done in such a way that it meets the requirements of the system and ensures that the system is secure and reliable. 
5. It should also be done in such a way that it is easy to maintain and update the system over time. 
6. The physical packaging should also take into account the scalability and performance of the system, as well as the cost of the system.
