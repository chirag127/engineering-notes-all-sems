
### Component Diagrams

- Component diagrams are used to represent the structure of components in a system. 
- They are used to show the relationships between the components of a system, such as classes and objects.
- Component diagrams are composed of a set of components and the relationships between them. 
- Components can be represented in different ways, such as classes, objects, packages, components, and services. 
- Component diagrams can also show the dependencies between components, as well as the interfaces between components.

### Deployment Diagrams

- Deployment diagrams are used to represent the physical deployment of the components in a system. 
- They are used to show the physical components of a system, such as hardware, software, and networks. 
- Deployment diagrams are composed of a set of nodes and the relationships between them. 
- Nodes can be represented in different ways, such as physical machines, virtual machines, and cloud services. 
- Deployment diagrams can also show the connections between nodes, as well as the communication protocols between nodes.
