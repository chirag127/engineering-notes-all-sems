
### The Importance of Modelling for Object Oriented System Design

1. Object-oriented system design is a method of designing computer systems that is based on the concept of objects and their relationships.
2. Modelling is an important part of the object-oriented system design process, as it helps to identify the objects and their relationships.
3. Modelling is also useful for understanding the behaviour of the system, and for predicting its performance.
4. Modelling can be done using a variety of techniques, such as object-oriented analysis and design, object-oriented programming, and object-oriented databases.
5. Modelling can also be used to identify potential problems and to create solutions that can be implemented in the system.
6. Modelling can also help to identify the most efficient way to implement the system, and to ensure that the system meets the requirements of the user.
