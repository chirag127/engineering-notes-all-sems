### Documenting design considerations for the notes of the Unit 3 - Object Oriented Analysis in the subject of Object Oriented System Design

The Documenting Design Considerations section of the Unit 3 - Object Oriented Analysis in the subject of Object Oriented System Design should cover the following topics:

1. Definition of Design Considerations (factors that impact the design of a system)
2. Overview of Design Considerations (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
3. Types of Design Considerations (functional, non-functional, technical, etc.)
4. Overview of Functional Design Considerations (factors that impact the functionality of a system)
5. Overview of Non-Functional Design Considerations (factors that impact the performance, security, etc. of a system)
6. Overview of Technical Design Considerations (factors that impact the technical aspects of a system)
7. Documenting Design Considerations (how design considerations are documented)
8. Design Considerations and Object Oriented Analysis (how design considerations impact object oriented analysis)
9. Design Considerations and System Design (how design considerations impact system design)
10. Limitations of Documenting Design Considerations (complexity, accuracy, etc.)
11. Techniques for overcoming limitations (using efficient algorithms, improving accuracy, etc.)
12. Further reading and resources (books, articles, online resources)

Documenting Design Considerations is an important component of the subject of Object Oriented System Design. Understanding the principles and techniques of documenting design considerations helps students to develop the skills required to analyze and document the design of a system. The hands-on practice and further reading resources will help students to develop their skills and deepen their understanding of the material.
