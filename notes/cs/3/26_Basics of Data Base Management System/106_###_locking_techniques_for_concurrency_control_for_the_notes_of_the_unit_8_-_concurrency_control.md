### locking techniques for concurrency control for the notes of the Unit 8 - Concurrency Control Techniques in the subject of Basics of Data Base Management System

Unit 8: Concurrency Control Techniques in Basics of Database Management System:
- This unit covers the study of concurrency control techniques in database management systems. 
- Topics may include:
  - Overview of concurrency control and its role in database management.
  - Study of transaction processing concepts, including atomicity, consistency, isolation, and durability (ACID).
  - Analysis of locking techniques, including shared locks, exclusive locks, and optimistic locking.
  - Implementation of locking techniques, including the use of lock tables, lock timeout, and deadlock detection.
  - Study of other concurrency control techniques, including multi-version concurrency control (MVCC) and timestamp-based concurrency control.
  - Comparison of concurrency control techniques with other database management systems, such as NoSQL databases.
  - Best practices for concurrency control, including performance optimization and security considerations.
