### DML in PL/SQL for the notes of the Unit 6 - PL/SQL in the subject of Basics of Data Base Management System

Unit 6: PL/SQL in Basics of Database Management System:
- This unit covers the study of PL/SQL in database management systems.
- Topics may include:
  - Overview of PL/SQL and its role in database management.
  - Study of PL/SQL syntax, including the use of variables, loops, and conditional statements.
  - Analysis of PL/SQL data types, including scalar data types, composite data types, and LOB data types.
  - Implementation of PL/SQL procedures, functions, and triggers, including the use of DDL and DML statements.
  - Study of PL/SQL exceptions and error handling, including the use of exception handling blocks and predefined exceptions.
  - Comparison of PL/SQL with other database programming languages, such as T-SQL and Java.
  - Best practices for PL/SQL programming, including performance optimization and security considerations.
