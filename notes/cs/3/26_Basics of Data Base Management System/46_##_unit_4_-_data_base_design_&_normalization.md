## Unit 4 - Data Base Design & Normalization

Unit 4 - Database Design & Normalization in the subject of Basics of Database Management System:

Unit 4 - Database Design & Normalization in the subject of Basics of Database Management System covers the process of designing and normalizing a database. Database design is the process of creating and organizing the structure of a database to meet the needs of an application or system. Normalization is the process of organizing the data in a database to reduce data redundancy and improve data integrity.

The following are the key concepts covered in Unit 4 - Database Design & Normalization:

1. Database Design: The process of creating and organizing the structure of a database to meet the needs of an application or system. This includes identifying the entities, attributes, and relationships in the database, and creating a data model using the Entity Relationship Model.

2. Normalization: The process of organizing the data in a database to reduce data redundancy and improve data integrity. Normalization involves dividing the data in a database into separate tables, and establishing relationships between the tables to ensure that the data is consistent and accurate.

3. Normal Forms: Normal forms are the levels of normalization that a database can achieve, and are used to measure the degree of normalization in a database. There are several normal forms, including First Normal Form (1NF), Second Normal Form (2NF), Third Normal Form (3NF), and so on.

4. Normalization Techniques: Normalization techniques are the methods used to normalize a database, and include techniques such as functional dependencies, normalization by elimination, and normalization by decomposition.

In conclusion, Unit 4 - Database Design & Normalization in the subject of Basics of Database Management System covers the process of designing and normalizing a database. The key concepts covered in this unit include database design, normalization, normal forms, and normalization techniques. Understanding these concepts is important for designing effective and efficient databases that meet the needs of an application or system.
