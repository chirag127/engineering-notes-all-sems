
### Reduction of an ER Diagram to Tables

1. An ER diagram is a graphical representation of entities and their relationships. It is used to model the data in a database.
2. The process of translating an ER diagram into tables is known as reduction.
3. The first step in reducing an ER diagram is to identify the entities and their attributes.
4. The next step is to identify the relationships between the entities.
5. Once the entities and attributes have been identified, the tables can be created.
6. The tables should contain the attributes of the entities and any foreign keys that are needed to represent the relationships.
7. All tables should have a primary key that is used to uniquely identify each record.
8. The last step is to normalize the tables to ensure that the data is stored in the most efficient way possible.
