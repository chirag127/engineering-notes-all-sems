
### Third Normal Forms

* Third Normal Form (3NF) is a normal form used in database normalization. It is used to reduce the redundancy and complexity of a database.
* A relation is in 3NF if and only if it is in 2NF and every non-primary key attribute is non-transitively dependent on the primary key.
* In other words, all attributes must depend directly on the primary key, and not on other non-key attributes.
* 3NF ensures that all attributes are fully dependent on the primary key.
* This helps to minimize the amount of redundant data stored in the database and improves the performance of queries.
* 3NF also helps to ensure the integrity of the data by preventing accidental or intentional modification of the data.
