
### Set-Theoretic Operations

1. **Union** - Combines the tuples of two relations and eliminates duplicates.
2. **Intersection** - Retrieves the tuples that are common in two relations.
3. **Difference** - Retrieves the tuples that are present in one relation but not in the other.
4. **Product** - Combines the tuples of two relations and creates a Cartesian product.
5. **Selection** - Retrieves tuples that satisfy a given condition.
6. **Projection** - Retrieves the columns of a relation that are specified in the query.
7. **Join** - Combines tuples from two relations that satisfy a given condition.
