

### Generalization for the Notes of the Unit 2 - Data Modeling using the Entity Relationship Model in the Basics of Database Management System

1. Generalization is a process in which entities or relationships can be broken down into more general categories. 
2. Generalization is the process of taking a group of related entities or relationships and reducing them into a single, more general entity or relationship. 
3. Entity Relationship Model (ERM) is a graphical representation of the entities and relationships in a database. 
4. ERM is used to design and create databases by representing the entities and relationships between them. 
5. Entities are represented as rectangles and relationships as lines connecting the entities. 
6. Attributes are used to describe the properties of an entity or relationship. 
7. Cardinality is a way of representing the number of entities related to each other in a relationship. 
8. Generalization hierarchies are used to represent the different levels of generalization. 
9. Super-types and sub-types are used to represent the generalization hierarchy. 
10. Super-types are more general entities and sub-types are more specific entities.
