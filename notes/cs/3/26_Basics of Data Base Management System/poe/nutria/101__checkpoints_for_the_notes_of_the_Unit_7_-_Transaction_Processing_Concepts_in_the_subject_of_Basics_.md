

### Checkpoints for the Notes of the Unit 7 - Transaction Processing Concepts in the Subject of Basics of Data Base Management System

1. Transactions are units of work that access and modify data in a database.
2. A transaction must be atomic, meaning that either all of its operations are completed or none of them are.
3. A transaction must be consistent, meaning that it must leave the database in a valid state.
4. A transaction must be isolated, meaning that the operations of one transaction should not interfere with the operations of another transaction.
5. A transaction must be durable, meaning that its effects must persist even if the system fails.
6. A transaction must be serializable, meaning that the results of concurrent transactions should be the same as if the transactions were executed serially.
7. The ACID properties (Atomicity, Consistency, Isolation, and Durability) are the four key characteristics of a transaction.
8. The two-phase commit protocol is a technique for ensuring the durability of a transaction.
9. The two-phase locking protocol is a technique for ensuring the isolation of a transaction.
10. The optimistic concurrency control protocol is a technique for ensuring the serializability of a transaction.
