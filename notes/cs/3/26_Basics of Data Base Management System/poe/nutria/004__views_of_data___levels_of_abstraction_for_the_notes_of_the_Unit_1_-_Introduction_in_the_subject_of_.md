
### Views of Data – Levels of Abstraction

* Data can be viewed from different levels of abstraction. 
* The three levels of abstraction are: 
    1. Physical level: This is the lowest level of abstraction and describes how data is stored. 
    2. Logical level: This level describes what data is stored. 
    3. View level: This level describes how the data is seen by different users. 
* The physical level is concerned with how the data is stored in the database. It includes the physical storage structure, access paths, and other physical aspects. 
* The logical level is concerned with what data is stored in the database. It includes the logical structure of the data, the relationship between different data items, and the constraints that must be satisfied. 
* The view level is concerned with how the data is seen by different users. It includes the user’s view of the data, the security and authorization aspects, and the integrity constraints.
