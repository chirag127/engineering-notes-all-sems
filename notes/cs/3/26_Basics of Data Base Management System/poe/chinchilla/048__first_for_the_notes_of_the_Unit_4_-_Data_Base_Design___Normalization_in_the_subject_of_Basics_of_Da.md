### Unit 4: Data Base Design & Normalization

In this unit, we will be focusing on the process of designing a database and normalizing it to improve its efficiency and effectiveness. Here are the key topics we will cover:

1. Introduction to Database Design
   - Importance of good database design
   - Steps in database design process
   - Conceptual, logical, and physical database design

2. Entity Relationship (ER) Modeling
   - Definition of entities, attributes, and relationships
   - ER diagrams and symbols
   - Types of relationships (one-to-one, one-to-many, many-to-many)

3. Normalization
   - Definition and principles of normalization
   - First Normal Form (1NF), Second Normal Form (2NF), Third Normal Form (3NF)
   - Benefits and drawbacks of normalization

4. Denormalization
   - Definition and purpose of denormalization
   - When to use denormalization
   - Techniques for denormalization

5. Advanced Database Design Concepts
   - Hierarchical, network, and object-oriented database models
   - Data warehousing and data mining
   - Emerging trends in database design

By the end of this unit, you should be able to understand the importance of good database design, know the steps involved in designing a database, be able to create ER diagrams, and understand the principles of normalization and denormalization. You will also be familiar with advanced database design concepts and emerging trends in the field.
