### Generalization

- Generalization is a bottom-up approach in which two or more lower level entities are combined to form a higher level entity .
- In generalization, the higher level entity inherits the properties of all the lower level entities .
- Generalization is used to represent a common set of attributes and relationships among a group of entities.
- Generalization is represented by a triangle with the word "is-a" above it  .

![Generalization Example](https://www.studytonight.com/dbms/images/generalization.png)

- In the above example, the entities Student and Teacher are generalized into a higher level entity Person, which has the common attributes name, age and address.
