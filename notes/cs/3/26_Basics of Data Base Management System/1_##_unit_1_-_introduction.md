## Unit 1 - Introduction

Unit 1 - Introduction is the first unit in a course or program that provides an overview of the content to be covered. It typically covers the following topics:

1. Objectives:
The objectives of Unit 1 - Introduction are to provide an overview of the course or program, including the topics to be covered, the skills to be developed, and the goals to be achieved.

2. Course Outline:
The course outline of Unit 1 - Introduction provides an overview of the topics to be covered in the course or program, including the content of each unit, the learning outcomes, and the assessment methods.

3. Prerequisites:
The prerequisites of Unit 1 - Introduction are the knowledge, skills, and experience that students are expected to have before starting the course or program.

4. Assessment Methods:
The assessment methods of Unit 1 - Introduction describe how students will be evaluated, including exams, assignments, projects, and other forms of assessment.

5. Learning Outcomes:
The learning outcomes of Unit 1 - Introduction describe what students will be able to do at the end of the course or program, including the knowledge, skills, and attitudes they will have developed.

In conclusion, Unit 1 - Introduction is the first unit in a course or program that provides an overview of the content to be covered. It includes the objectives, course outline, prerequisites, assessment methods, and learning outcomes of the course or program.
