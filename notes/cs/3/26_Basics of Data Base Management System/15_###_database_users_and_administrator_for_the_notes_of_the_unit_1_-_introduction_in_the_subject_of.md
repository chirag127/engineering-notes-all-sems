### database users and administrator for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction in the subject of Basics of Data Base Management System

Unit 1 - Introduction in the subject of Basics of Data Base Management System covers the following topics related to database users and administrators:

1. Database Users:
Database users are individuals who access and use the data stored in a database. They can be grouped into two categories: end-users and application users.

2. End-Users:
End-users are individuals who directly interact with the database, either through a graphical user interface (GUI) or through a command-line interface (CLI). They may be employees, customers, or other stakeholders who use the database to perform their work.

3. Application Users:
Application users are individuals who access the database through an application, such as a web application or a desktop application. They may be employees, customers, or other stakeholders who use the database to perform their work.

4. Database Administrators:
Database administrators (DBAs) are individuals who are responsible for the management and maintenance of a database. They are responsible for tasks such as creating and maintaining database tables, ensuring the security and privacy of the data stored in the database, and optimizing the performance of the database.

5. DBA Tasks:
The tasks performed by DBAs include:
1. Installing and configuring the database management system (DBMS).
2. Creating and maintaining database tables, views, and indexes.
3. Ensuring the security and privacy of the data stored in the database.
4. Optimizing the performance of the database.
5. Monitoring the performance of the database and tuning it as needed.
6. Backing up the database and restoring it in the event of a failure.

In conclusion, Unit 1 - Introduction in the subject of Basics of Data Base Management System covers the basics of database users and administrators. The unit covers the two categories of database users (end-users and application users) and the role of database administrators (DBAs) in managing and maintaining a database. The unit provides a general overview of the tasks performed by DBAs, including installing and configuring the DBMS, creating and maintaining database tables, ensuring the security and privacy of the data, optimizing performance, and backing up the database.
