### Introduction for the notes of the Unit 6 - PL/SQL in the subject of Basics of Data Base Management System

Unit 6: PL/SQL in Basics of Database Management System:
- This unit covers the study of PL/SQL, a procedural language for managing relational databases.
- Topics may include:
  - Overview of PL/SQL and its role in database management.
  - Study of PL/SQL syntax, including the use of variables, control structures, and exception handling.
  - Analysis of PL/SQL blocks, including anonymous blocks, stored procedures, and functions.
  - Implementation of PL/SQL commands, including the creation and modification of database objects.
  - Study of PL/SQL cursors, including the use of implicit and explicit cursors.
  - Comparison of PL/SQL with other procedural languages, such as Java and Python.
  - Best practices for PL/SQL programming, including performance optimization, security considerations, and error handling.
