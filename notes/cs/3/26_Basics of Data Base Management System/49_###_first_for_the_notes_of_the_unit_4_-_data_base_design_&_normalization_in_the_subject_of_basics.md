Unit 4 - Database Design & Normalization in the subject of Basics of Database Management System:

Unit 4 - Database Design & Normalization in the subject of Basics of Database Management System covers the process of designing and normalizing a database. The goal of database design is to create a database that is efficient, accurate, and scalable.

The first step in database design is to gather and analyze the requirements for the database, including the types of data that will be stored, the relationships between the data, and the constraints on the data. This information is used to create a conceptual model of the database, which is a high-level representation of the data and relationships.

Once the conceptual model has been created, the next step is to convert the conceptual model into a logical model, which is a more detailed representation of the data and relationships. The logical model is used to define the tables, relationships, and constraints in the database.

Once the logical model has been created, the next step is to normalize the database. Normalization is the process of organizing the data in the database to reduce redundancy and ensure data integrity. Normalization involves dividing the data into separate tables, and defining relationships between the tables.

In conclusion, Unit 4 - Database Design & Normalization in the subject of Basics of Database Management System covers the process of designing and normalizing a database. The first step in database design is to gather and analyze the requirements for the database, and to create a conceptual and logical model. The next step is to normalize the database to reduce redundancy and ensure data integrity. Understanding the process of database design and normalization is essential for anyone working with databases.
