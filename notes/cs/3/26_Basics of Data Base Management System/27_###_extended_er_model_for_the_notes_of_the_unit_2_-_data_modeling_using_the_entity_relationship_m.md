1. Entity Relationship (ER) Model:
The ER model is a data modeling technique that is used to represent the relationships between entities in a database. An entity is a real-world object or concept that is represented in the database, while a relationship is a connection between two or more entities.

2. Extended ER Model:
The extended ER model is an extension of the basic ER model that includes additional features, such as:
1. Inheritance: A mechanism for representing the relationship between a parent entity and its child entities.
2. Specialization: A mechanism for representing the relationship between a general entity and its specialized entities.
3. Aggregation: A mechanism for representing the relationship between a whole entity and its component entities.
