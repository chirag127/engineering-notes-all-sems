### database triggers for the notes of the Unit 6 - PL/SQL in the subject of Basics of Data Base Management System

Unit 6: PL/SQL in Basics of Database Management System:
- This unit covers the study of PL/SQL, a procedural language for managing relational databases.
- Topics may include:
  - Overview of PL/SQL and its role in database management.
  - Study of PL/SQL syntax, including the use of variables, loops, and conditional statements.
  - Analysis of PL/SQL blocks, including anonymous blocks and stored procedures.
  - Implementation of PL/SQL commands, including the creation and execution of stored procedures and functions.
  - Study of database triggers, including the use of triggers for data validation, auditing, and automation.
  - Comparison of PL/SQL with other database management languages, such as T-SQL and PLSQL.
  - Best practices for PL/SQL programming, including performance optimization, security considerations, and error handling.
