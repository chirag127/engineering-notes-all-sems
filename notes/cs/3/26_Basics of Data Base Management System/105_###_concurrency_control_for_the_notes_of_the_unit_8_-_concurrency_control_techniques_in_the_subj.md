### Concurrency control for the notes of the Unit 8 - Concurrency Control Techniques in the subject of Basics of Data Base Management System

Unit 8: Concurrency Control Techniques in Basics of Database Management System:
- This unit covers the study of concurrency control techniques in database management systems.
- Topics may include:
  - Overview of concurrency control and its role in database management.
  - Study of transaction properties, including atomicity, consistency, isolation, and durability (ACID).
  - Analysis of concurrency control techniques, including locking, timestamping, and optimistic concurrency control.
  - Implementation of concurrency control techniques, including the use of lock management and deadlock detection algorithms.
  - Study of advanced concurrency control techniques, including multi-version concurrency control (MVCC) and serializable snapshot isolation (SSI).
  - Comparison of concurrency control techniques with other database management systems, such as NoSQL databases.
  - Best practices for concurrency control, including performance optimization and security considerations.
