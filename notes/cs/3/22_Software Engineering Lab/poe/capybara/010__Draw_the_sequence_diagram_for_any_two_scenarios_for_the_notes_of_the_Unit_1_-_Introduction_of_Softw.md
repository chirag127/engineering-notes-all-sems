### Sequence Diagrams for Two Scenarios in Software Engineering Lab

In software engineering, sequence diagrams are used to describe the interactions between objects or components in a system. Here are two scenarios for which we can draw sequence diagrams to better understand the processes in Software Engineering Lab's Unit 1 - Introduction:

1. Scenario 1: Student Submission of Assignment

- The student logs in to the Lab's system and selects the Unit 1 - Introduction assignment.
- The system retrieves the assignment details and presents them to the student.
- The student uploads the completed assignment to the system.
- The system validates the submission and sends a confirmation message to the student.

2. Scenario 2: Instructor Review of Assignment

- The instructor logs in to the Lab's system and navigates to the Unit 1 - Introduction assignment.
- The system retrieves the submitted assignments and presents them to the instructor.
- The instructor selects an assignment and reviews it.
- The instructor provides feedback on the assignment and submits it back to the system.
- The system stores the feedback and sends a notification to the student.

In both scenarios, the sequence diagrams can be drawn to illustrate the interactions between the various objects or components involved in the process. By visualizing these interactions, we can better understand the flow of information and actions in the system, which can help us identify potential issues and improve the overall efficiency of the process.

To draw a sequence diagram, we use the following symbols and notations:

- Objects: Represented by rectangles with the name of the object inside.
- Lifelines: Represented by vertical lines that extend from the object and show its lifespan.
- Messages: Represented by arrows that show the flow of communication between objects, with the message name above the arrow.

Using these symbols and notations, we can create diagrams that clearly show the sequence of events and interactions between objects in the system. By practicing drawing sequence diagrams for various scenarios, we can improve our understanding of software engineering concepts and become better equipped to design and develop efficient and effective software systems.
