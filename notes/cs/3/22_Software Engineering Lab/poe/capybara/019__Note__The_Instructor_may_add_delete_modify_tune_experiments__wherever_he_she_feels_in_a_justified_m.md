### Introduction to Software Engineering Lab

In the Software Engineering Lab, students will learn practical skills related to software development. As part of the lab, the instructor may add, delete, modify or tune experiments to ensure that students receive the most up-to-date and relevant skills in software engineering. 

Here are some of the topics that may be covered in the lab:

- **Software Development Process:** Students will learn about the software development life cycle (SDLC) and the different methodologies that can be used for software development. They will also learn about project planning, requirements gathering, design, coding, testing, and maintenance.
- **Software Design Patterns:** Students will learn about common software design patterns such as creational, structural, and behavioral patterns. They will also learn how to apply these patterns to real-world software development problems.
- **Software Testing:** Students will learn about different types of software testing such as unit testing, integration testing, and system testing. They will also learn about test-driven development (TDD) and behavior-driven development (BDD) methodologies.
- **Software Configuration Management:** Students will learn about version control systems (VCS) such as Git, and how to use them to manage software projects. They will also learn about continuous integration (CI) and continuous delivery (CD) practices.
- **Agile Software Development:** Students will learn about agile software development methodologies such as Scrum, Kanban, and Lean. They will also learn about agile practices such as sprint planning, daily stand-ups, and retrospectives.

Overall, the Software Engineering Lab provides students with practical experience in software development and helps prepare them for a career in the software industry. The instructor may add, delete, modify or tune experiments to ensure that students have the most relevant and up-to-date skills in software engineering.
