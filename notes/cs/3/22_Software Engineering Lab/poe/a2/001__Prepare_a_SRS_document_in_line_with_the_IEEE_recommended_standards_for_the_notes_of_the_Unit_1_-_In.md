### Software Requirements Specification for Unit 1 - Introduction to Software Engineering Lab

1. Introduction
- Purpose: The purpose of this SRS is to specify the requirements for the notes of Unit 1 - Introduction to Software Engineering Lab.
- Scope: The scope of this SRS is limited to the topics to be covered in Unit 1 of the Software Engineering Lab.
- Definitions, Acronyms, and Abbreviations:
    - SRS - Software Requirements Specification
    - IEEE - Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers

2. Overall Description
- The notes will cover the following topics:
    - Introduction to Software Engineering
    - Software Development Life Cycle Models
        - Waterfall model
        - Spiral model
        - Agile model
    - Requirements Analysis and Specification

3. System Features
- The notes will contain explanations for the topics along with examples.
- The notes will be structured in a easy to understand format with headings and points.
- The notes will contain diagrams and images to aid understanding wherever necessary.
- The notes will contain practical examples and assignments at the end of each topic.

4. External Interface Requirements
- The notes will not have any external interfaces or dependencies. All concepts and explanations will be self-contained within the notes.

5. System Features
- The notes will be written in simple and straightforward language devoid of complex technical terms.
- The notes will have a formal tone and will not contain any emojis or informal language.

6. Other Non-Functional Requirements
- The notes will be prepared in line with the IEEE standards for software requirements specifications.
- The notes will be reviewed for accuracy and correctness before finalizing.
- The notes will be engaging, comprehensive, and practical in nature.
