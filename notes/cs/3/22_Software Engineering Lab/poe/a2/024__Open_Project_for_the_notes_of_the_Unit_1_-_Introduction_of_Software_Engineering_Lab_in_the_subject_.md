### Open Project for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction of Software Engineering Lab in the subject of Software Engineering Lab.

1. Introduction
- Software Engineering Lab aims to provide students with hands-on experience in Software Engineering concepts and practices.
- Students will work on programming projects that demonstrate software engineering principles and techniques.
- The lab experiments are designed to complement the theory being taught in the Software Engineering course.

2. Learning Objectives
- Apply software engineering principles, methods, and tools to the software development process.
- Experience and evaluate various software development life cycle models.
- Analyze given problem statements and specifications and develop software solutions.
- Use an Issue/Bug tracking system and Version Control Systems effectively.
- Employ programming and scripting languages to implement software solutions.
- Work as a team to develop software solutions and experience team-based software development.

3. Pedagogy
- The coursework is a mix of lectures, demonstrations, and hands-on practice. 
- Projects form an integral part of the course and students work in teams to analyze problems and develop software solutions. 
- Revision sessions and discussions provide opportunities to clarify doubts and share experiences.
