### Classify them as weak and strong classes for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction of Software Engineering Lab in the subject of Software Engineering Lab:

1. Weak classes:
- Loosely coupled: The classes are loosely connected and are less dependent on each other. The changes in one class do not affect the other class.
- Have few attributes and methods: The weak classes contain few attributes and methods as they are specialized in performing a specific task.
- Low cohesion: The weak classes exhibit low cohesion as the attributes and methods are not strongly related to each other.

2. Strong classes:
- Highly cohesive: The strong classes demonstrate high cohesion as the attributes and methods are strongly connected and focused on a single task.
- Tightly coupled: The strong classes are tightly coupled as they are highly dependent on each other. The changes in one class affect the other classes.
- Have many attributes and methods: The strong classes consist of many attributes and methods to perform a variety of related tasks.

The above points summarize the key differences between weak and strong classes. Weak classes are loosely connected and specialized while strong classes are highly cohesive and exhibit tight coupling. The selection between weak and strong classes depends on the requirements and design of the software.
