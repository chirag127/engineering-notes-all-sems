
### Draw the State Chart Diagram for the Notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction of Software Engineering Lab in the Subject of Software Engineering Lab

1. A state chart diagram is a graphical representation of the states and transitions of a system. 
2. It is used to represent the behavior of a system in response to external stimuli. 
3. A state chart diagram consists of states, transitions, and events. 
4. States are represented by circles, transitions by arrows, and events by labels on the arrows. 
5. Transitions represent the movement of the system from one state to another. 
6. Events are the stimuli that cause the transitions to occur.
