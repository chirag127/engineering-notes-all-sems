
### Draw the Use Case Diagram and Specify the Role of Each of the Actors for the Notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction of Software Engineering Lab in the Subject of Software Engineering Lab

1. **Actors**: Actors are entities that interact with the system. In the context of the Unit 1 - Introduction of Software Engineering Lab, the actors are students, teachers, and administrators. 
2. **Use Case Diagram**: A use case diagram is a visual representation of the system's interactions with its actors. In the context of the Unit 1 - Introduction of Software Engineering Lab, the use case diagram can be used to represent the interactions between the students, teachers, and administrators. 
3. **Role of Each Actor**: 
    - **Students**: Students are responsible for completing the tasks assigned by the teacher, such as completing assignments and taking tests. 
    - **Teachers**: Teachers are responsible for assigning tasks to the students, such as assigning assignments and creating tests. 
    - **Administrators**: Administrators are responsible for managing the system, such as setting up user accounts and monitoring usage.
