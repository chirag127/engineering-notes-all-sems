
### GanttProject for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction of Software Engineering Lab in the subject of Software Engineering Lab

1. GanttProject is a free open source project management software that helps to plan, track, and manage projects.
2. It provides a graphical representation of tasks and their dependencies, as well as a timeline view of the project.
3. It allows users to create tasks and assign them to resources, set dependencies between tasks, and track progress.
4. It also provides a variety of reports and charts to help analyze the project's progress.
5. GanttProject is a useful tool for software engineering teams to plan and manage their projects, as it allows them to visualize the project's timeline, track progress, and adjust tasks and resources accordingly.
