### Libra

- Libra is a software tool that can be used to measure the mammographic breast density from digital mammograms.
- Mammographic breast density is a risk factor for breast cancer and can affect the accuracy of mammography screening.
- Libra can process both raw and processed images and generate area-based measurements of breast density.
- Libra uses edge-detection algorithms to segment the breast region and fuzzy c-means clustering to classify the pixels into dense and non-dense tissue.
- Libra can be downloaded from https://www.cbica.upenn.edu/sbia/software/libra/ and installed on Windows, Linux or Mac OS platforms.
- Libra can be used for research and educational purposes, but not for clinical diagnosis or treatment.
