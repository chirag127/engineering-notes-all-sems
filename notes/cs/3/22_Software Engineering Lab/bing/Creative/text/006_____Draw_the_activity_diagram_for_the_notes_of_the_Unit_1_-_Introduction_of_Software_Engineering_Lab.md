### Draw the activity diagram for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction of Software Engineering Lab in the subject of Software Engineering Lab

- An activity diagram is a graphical representation of the flow of actions and transitions in a system. It shows the dynamic behavior of the system in response to events and conditions.
- An activity diagram consists of nodes and edges. Nodes represent actions or states, and edges represent transitions or flows between them.
- To draw an activity diagram for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction of Software Engineering Lab, we can follow these steps:

  - Identify the main activities and states involved in the process of creating and accessing the notes.
  - Identify the start and end points of the process, and mark them with a solid circle and a bullseye, respectively.
  - Identify the decision points and branching conditions in the process, and mark them with a diamond shape.
  - Identify the synchronization and concurrency points in the process, and mark them with a thick horizontal or vertical bar, respectively.
  - Identify the sub-activities and partitions in the process, and mark them with a rounded rectangle and a dashed line, respectively.
  - Draw the edges between the nodes, and label them with the triggers or guards, if any.
  - Verify the correctness and completeness of the diagram.
