### Unit 1 - Introduction of Software Engineering Lab

1. The instructor has the authority to add, delete, modify, or tune experiments in a justified manner.
2. These changes may be made to the notes of Unit 1 - Introduction of Software Engineering Lab in the subject of Software Engineering Lab.
3. The purpose of these changes is to improve the learning experience for students.
4. It is important for students to stay up to date with any changes made by the instructor.
5. The Introduction of Software Engineering Lab provides a foundation for understanding the principles and practices of software engineering.
6. The experiments in this unit are designed to provide hands-on experience with software engineering concepts and techniques.
7. Students are encouraged to actively participate in the experiments and ask questions to deepen their understanding of the subject matter.
