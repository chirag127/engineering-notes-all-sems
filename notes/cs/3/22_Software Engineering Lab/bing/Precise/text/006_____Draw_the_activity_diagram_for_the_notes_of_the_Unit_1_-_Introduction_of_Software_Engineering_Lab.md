### Activity Diagram for Unit 1 - Introduction of Software Engineering Lab

An activity diagram is a graphical representation of a workflow or process. It shows the flow of control from one activity to another and can be used to model the behavior of a system or a business process.

Here is an example of an activity diagram for the notes of Unit 1 - Introduction of Software Engineering Lab in the subject of Software Engineering Lab:

1. Start
2. Read the notes for Unit 1
3. Understand the concepts
4. Take notes and summarize the key points
5. Review the notes and clarify any doubts
6. Practice the exercises and examples
7. End

This diagram shows the sequence of steps that a student might follow when studying the notes for Unit 1 of the Software Engineering Lab course. The activities are represented by rounded rectangles and the flow of control is indicated by arrows. The start and end points are represented by filled circles.
