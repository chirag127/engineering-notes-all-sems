### Libra

- Libra was a cryptocurrency created by Facebook, designed to be a simple, low-fee stablecoin to be used around the world .
- It was renamed into Diem at the end of December 2020 and wound down in January 2022 .
- Libra crypto is classified as stablecoin, backed with a reserve of assets .
- It will include cash, bank deposits and short-term government securities in various currencies from reputable central banks .
- The US Dollar, Euro and British Pound are expected to be included in the basket .
