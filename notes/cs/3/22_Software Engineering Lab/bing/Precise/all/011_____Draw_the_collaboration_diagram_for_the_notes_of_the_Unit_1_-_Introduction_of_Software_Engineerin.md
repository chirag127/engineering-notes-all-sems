### Collaboration Diagram for Unit 1 - Introduction of Software Engineering Lab

A collaboration diagram, also known as a communication diagram, is a visual representation of the interactions between objects in a system. It shows the relationships between objects and the messages they send to each other.

Here are the steps to draw a collaboration diagram for the notes of Unit 1 - Introduction of Software Engineering Lab in the subject of Software Engineering Lab:

1. Identify the objects involved in the system. These could include the notes, the student, the teacher, and the course material.
2. Determine the relationships between the objects. For example, the student reads the notes, the teacher creates the notes, and the course material is used to create the notes.
3. Draw the objects as rectangles and label them with their names.
4. Draw the relationships between the objects as arrows, with the arrowhead pointing towards the object receiving the message.
5. Label the arrows with the messages being sent between the objects.
