# Activity Diagram for Unit 1 - Introduction of Software Engineering Lab

An activity diagram is a graphical representation of a workflow or process. It shows the flow of control from one activity to another within a system. 1. Start
2. Read the notes of Unit 1 - Introduction of Software Engineering Lab
3. Understand the concepts
4. Take notes
5. Review the notes
6. End

This diagram shows the flow of activities involved in studying the notes of Unit 1 - Introduction of Software Engineering Lab. The process starts with reading the notes, followed by understanding the concepts and taking notes. The final step is to review the notes before the process ends.
