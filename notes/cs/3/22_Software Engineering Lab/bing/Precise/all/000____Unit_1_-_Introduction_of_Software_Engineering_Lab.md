## Unit 1 - Introduction of Software Engineering Lab

1. **Software Engineering** is the application of engineering principles to the design, development, and maintenance of software.
2. The goal of software engineering is to produce high-quality software that meets the needs of its users, within budget and on time.
3. The **Software Engineering Lab** is a practical course that provides hands-on experience in software development.
4. In the lab, students will learn and apply software engineering principles and techniques to real-world projects.
5. The lab will cover topics such as requirements analysis, design, coding, testing, and maintenance.
6. Students will work in teams to develop software projects, using tools and methodologies commonly used in the industry.
7. The lab will provide students with the opportunity to apply the theoretical concepts learned in the software engineering course to practical problems.
8. The lab will also help students develop skills such as teamwork, communication, and project management, which are essential for a successful career in software engineering.
