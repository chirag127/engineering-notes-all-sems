### Unit 1 - Introduction of Software Engineering Lab

- Software Engineering Lab is a subject that focuses on the practical application of software engineering principles and techniques.
- The experiments in this lab are designed to provide hands-on experience in various aspects of software development.
- The instructor has the authority to add, delete, modify, or tune experiments in a justified manner.
- This flexibility allows the instructor to tailor the experiments to the needs of the students and the course.
- It is important to note that changes to the experiments may occur throughout the course.
