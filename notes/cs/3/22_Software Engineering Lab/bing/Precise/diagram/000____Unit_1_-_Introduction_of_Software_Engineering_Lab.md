## Unit 1 - Introduction of Software Engineering Lab

Software engineering is the systematic approach to the development, operation, and maintenance of software. It involves the application of engineering principles to the design, development, and testing of software to ensure that it is reliable, efficient, and maintainable.

In a software engineering lab, students learn and apply various software engineering concepts and techniques, such as:

1. Requirements analysis: The process of gathering and defining the requirements of the software system.
2. Design: The process of defining the architecture, components, interfaces, and other characteristics of the software system.
3. Implementation: The process of writing and testing the code that implements the design.
4. Verification and validation: The process of ensuring that the software meets the requirements and performs as expected.
5. Maintenance: The process of making changes to the software to fix defects or add new features.

The goal of a software engineering lab is to provide students with hands-on experience in applying software engineering principles and techniques to real-world problems. This helps students develop the skills and knowledge necessary to become effective software engineers.
