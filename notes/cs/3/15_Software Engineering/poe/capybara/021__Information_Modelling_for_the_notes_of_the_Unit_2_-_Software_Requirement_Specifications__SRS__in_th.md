### Information Modelling for the notes of the Unit 2 - Software Requirement Specifications (SRS) in the subject of Software Engineering

Information Modelling is a crucial part of Software Requirement Specifications (SRS). It is the process of representing the real-world information domain of the problem domain in a structured and organized format. The purpose of Information Modelling is to provide a clear understanding of the system's requirements and functionalities to the stakeholders.

Here are some key points to understand Information Modelling for the notes of Unit 2 - SRS:

- Information Modelling is a process of creating a conceptual model of the information domain of the system.
- The conceptual model is a high-level representation of the system's requirements and functionalities that is used to communicate with stakeholders.
- Information Modelling is done using various techniques such as Entity-Relationship Diagram (ERD), Data Flow Diagram (DFD), and Unified Modelling Language (UML).
- ERD is a graphical representation of entities and their relationships, while DFD is a graphical representation of data flow through a system.
- UML is a general-purpose modelling language that is used to model various aspects of the system, such as use cases, classes, and sequence diagrams.
- Information Modelling helps in identifying the system's data requirements and their relationships, which is essential for designing the database schema.
- Information Modelling also helps in identifying the system's processes and their interactions with the data, which is essential for designing the system's architecture.

In conclusion, Information Modelling is a critical process in Software Requirement Specification (SRS) that helps in representing the system's information domain in a structured and organized format. It is essential for communicating the system's requirements and functionalities to the stakeholders and is done using various techniques such as ERD, DFD, and UML.
