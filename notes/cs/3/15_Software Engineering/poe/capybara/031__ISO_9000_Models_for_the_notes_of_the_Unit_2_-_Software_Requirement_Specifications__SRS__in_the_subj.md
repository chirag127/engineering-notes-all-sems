### ISO 9000 Models

ISO 9000 is a set of international standards that provide guidelines for Quality Management Systems (QMS). The ISO 9000 standards are widely used in the software industry to ensure that the software development process meets the customer's requirements and quality standards. ISO 9000 Models are important for the notes of Unit 2 - Software Requirement Specifications (SRS) in the subject of Software Engineering. Here are some key points about ISO 9000 Models:

- ISO 9000 Models provide a framework for establishing, implementing, maintaining, and improving a QMS. The models specify the requirements for an effective QMS and provide guidance on how to achieve them.

- The ISO 9000 Models focus on customer satisfaction and continuous improvement. The models aim to ensure that the customer's requirements are met and that the organization is continually improving its processes and products.

- ISO 9000 Models are applicable to all types of organizations, including small and large organizations, public and private sector organizations, and service and manufacturing organizations.

- ISO 9000 Models consist of three standards: ISO 9000, ISO 9001, and ISO 9004. ISO 9000 provides an overview of the ISO 9000 series of standards, while ISO 9001 specifies the requirements for a QMS that can be certified. ISO 9004 provides guidance on how to improve the performance of a QMS.

- ISO 9000 Models are based on the Plan-Do-Check-Act (PDCA) cycle. The PDCA cycle is a continuous improvement process that consists of four stages: Plan, Do, Check, and Act. The PDCA cycle helps organizations to improve their processes and products by identifying and addressing problems and opportunities for improvement.

- ISO 9000 Models require organizations to document their processes and procedures. Documentation is important for ensuring that processes are repeatable and that the organization can maintain quality standards over time.
