### Elicitation for the notes of the Unit 2 - Software Requirement Specifications (SRS) in the subject of Software Engineering

Software Requirement Specifications (SRS) is the process of documenting the requirements of a software system. Elicitation is the process of gathering and eliciting information from stakeholders to understand their needs and requirements. Here are some important points to keep in mind while eliciting information for SRS:

- Identify the stakeholders: Identify all the stakeholders involved in the software development process. This can include customers, users, developers, testers, project managers, and other stakeholders.

- Determine the information needed: Determine what information is needed from the stakeholders to develop the software system. This can include functional requirements, non-functional requirements, constraints, and other information.

- Choose the elicitation techniques: Choose the appropriate elicitation techniques based on the stakeholders and the information needed. This can include interviews, surveys, questionnaires, focus groups, and other techniques.

- Prepare for the elicitation: Prepare the necessary documents and materials for the elicitation process. This can include questionnaires, interview questions, and other materials.

- Conduct the elicitation: Conduct the elicitation process with the stakeholders. Remember to be professional, courteous, and respectful to all stakeholders.

- Record the elicitation results: Record the results of the elicitation process in a clear and concise manner. This can include notes, transcripts, and other documentation.

- Review the elicitation results: Review the elicitation results with the stakeholders to ensure that all requirements have been captured and understood.

- Validate the elicitation results: Validate the elicitation results with the stakeholders to ensure that the requirements are accurate, complete, and consistent.

- Prioritize the requirements: Prioritize the requirements based on their importance and impact on the software system.

- Document the requirements: Document the requirements in the SRS document. Make sure that the requirements are clear, concise, and unambiguous.

Elicitation is a critical process in developing a software system. By following these important points, you can ensure that all requirements are captured and understood, and that the software system meets the needs of the stakeholders.
