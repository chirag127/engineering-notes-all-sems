### Preventive for the notes of the Unit 5 - Software Maintenance and Software Project Management in the subject of Software Engineering

1. Software Maintenance
- Refers to the activities required to modify a software system after delivery to correct or improve its capabilities or ensure continued operation.
- Includes all modifications to correct faults, to improve performance, or to adapt the software to a changed environment.
- It consumes more than 50% of software project resources, and it's necessary for continued usefulness of software.
- Three types: corrective (bug fixes), adaptive (external changes), perfective (improvements).

2. Software Project Management
- Planning, scheduling, monitoring, and controlling software projects.
- Ensures that projects meet objectives like delivered on time, on budget, and with expected features and quality.
- Includes organizing people and allocating resources to develop software.
- Four key activities: initiating, planning, executing, and closing.
- Challenges: schedule and budget pressures, increasing complexity, high risk of failure, and constantly changing requirements.
