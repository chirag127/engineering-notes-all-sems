### Resource Allocation Models

- Fixed Price Model: In this model, the project is delivered at a fixed price. The risk is borne by the vendor. The client knows the exact cost but the vendor may have to bear losses if the project takes more effort than estimated.
- Time and Material Model: In this model, the client pays for the hours spent by the team and the material used. The risk is borne by the client. The exact cost is not known in the beginning but clients have more control over the project.
- Incremental Funding Model: In this model, funds are released in increments. Each increment is based on the achievement of certain pre-defined milestones. This limits the risk for the client. The cost may exceed budget if project takes longer than estimated.

The above models can be used for allocating resources effectively based on factors like risk appetite, budget constraints and desired control levels. An appropriate model can be selected based on the nature and requirements of the project.
