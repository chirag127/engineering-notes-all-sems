### Perfective Maintenance for the notes of the Unit 5 - Software Maintenance and Software Project Management in the subject of Software Engineering.

- Perfective Maintenance: It involves restructuring the software architecture, rewriting software modules, and modifying interfaces to improve performance, maintainability, and flexibility.
- It is also known as preventive maintenance as it prevents expected future problems.
- It requires detailed planning and scheduling as it involves major changes to the software.
- It improves the software quality and flexibility to accommodate new or changed requirements.
- It reduces software entropy or deterioration and keeps the software adaptable to the changing environment.
- It requires significant effort and cost as it involves redesigning and re-implementing the software.
- It should be done when the benefits of the improved software quality and flexibility outweigh the cost of change.
