### Cost of Maintenance for the notes of the Unit 5 - Software Maintenance and Software Project Management in the subject of Software Engineering

1. Cost of correction: The cost involved in correcting the errors or bugs found in the software. This includes the cost of detecting and removing the bugs.
2. Cost of adaptation: The cost involved in modifying the software to adapt to the changed requirements. This includes the cost of analyzing the changes required, designing the changes, and implementing the changes.
3. Cost of enhancement: The cost involved in enhancing the software with new features to provide more functionality or to improve the performance. This includes the cost of analyzing the new requirements, designing the enhancements, and implementing the enhancements.
4. Frequent changes and modifications lead to increased maintenance costs which can become prohibitive if not managed properly. Proper management of software maintenance involves balancing the costs with benefits to ensure cost-effectiveness.
5. Factors affecting maintenance cost: The complexity of software, frequency of changes required, availability of proper documentation and the maintainers' expertise, etc. affect the maintenance cost.
