### Static Testing Strategies for the notes of the Unit 4 - Software Testing in the subject of Software Engineering.

1.  Code Review - Manually inspecting the source code to find defects by a person other than the author. Helps find logical errors and improves code quality.
2.  Inspection - A formal type of review involving a group of people reviewing the work product to detect defects in a systematic way. Can be done at various stages of SDLC.
3.  Walkthrough - Step-by-step presentation by the author of a work product to a group of reviewers to gather feedback and defects. Focuses on understanding the product.
4.  Structured Walkthrough - A controlled walkthrough process with defined roles, objectives, and procedures to systematically identify defects.

The strategies help evaluate the software artifacts early in SDLC to minimize defects and reduce cost of fixing them later. They promote correctness, quality, and shared understanding of the work product.
