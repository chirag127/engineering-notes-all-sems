
### ISO 9000 Models for the notes of the Unit 2 - Software Requirement Specifications (SRS) in the subject of Software Engineering

1. ISO 9000 is a family of standards related to quality management and quality assurance. It is designed to help organizations ensure that their products and services meet customer and regulatory requirements.

2. The ISO 9000 series of standards consists of three main models: the Quality Management System (QMS), the Quality Assurance System (QAS) and the Quality Control System (QCS).

3. The Quality Management System (QMS) is the main model used to ensure quality management and quality assurance. It consists of five stages: planning, implementation, monitoring, evaluation and improvement.

4. The Quality Assurance System (QAS) is the second model used to ensure quality management and quality assurance. It consists of four stages: design, implementation, assessment and improvement.

5. The Quality Control System (QCS) is the third model used to ensure quality management and quality assurance. It consists of three stages: inspection, testing and reporting.

6. In the Software Requirement Specifications (SRS) stage, the Quality Management System (QMS) is used to ensure that the requirements are clearly specified and that the software meets the customer's requirements.

7. The Quality Assurance System (QAS) is used to ensure that the software meets the customer's requirements and that the software is properly tested before it is released.

8. The Quality Control System (QCS) is used to inspect the software and to ensure that it meets the customer's requirements.
