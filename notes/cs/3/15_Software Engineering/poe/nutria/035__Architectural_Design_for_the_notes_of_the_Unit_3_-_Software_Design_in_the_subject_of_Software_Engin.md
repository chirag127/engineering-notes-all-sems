
### Architectural Design for the notes of the Unit 3 - Software Design in the subject of Software Engineering

1. Architectural design is a process for creating software systems that are both efficient and maintainable.
2. The first step in the process is to identify the system's requirements, which includes understanding the user's needs and the system's purpose.
3. The next step is to create a high-level design that outlines the overall structure of the system and its components.
4. This high-level design should include diagrams and descriptions of the system's components and their interactions.
5. Once the high-level design is complete, the next step is to create a detailed design for each component.
6. This includes creating data models, flow diagrams, and pseudo-code.
7. Finally, the system should be tested to ensure that it meets the user's needs and is reliable and secure.
