
### SQA Plans for the notes of the Unit 2 - Software Requirement Specifications (SRS) in the subject of Software Engineering

1. Software Requirement Specifications (SRS) provide a detailed description of the software system to be developed and the requirements that the system must satisfy.
2. SRS documents include a number of different sections, including a purpose statement, a list of stakeholders, a list of functions and features, and a list of constraints.
3. The SRS should also include a description of the system architecture, a list of assumptions and dependencies, and a list of use cases.
4. The SRS should be reviewed and verified by stakeholders to ensure that it meets their requirements.
5. The SRS should also be reviewed by the software development team to ensure that it is complete and accurate.
6. Quality Assurance (QA) plans should be developed to ensure that the SRS meets the quality requirements of the stakeholders.
7. QA plans should include test plans, test cases, and defect tracking processes.
8. QA plans should also include a process for validating requirements, verifying design, and verifying code.
9. QA plans should also include a process for tracking and resolving defects.
10. QA plans should be reviewed and updated regularly to ensure that the software system meets the requirements of the stakeholders.
