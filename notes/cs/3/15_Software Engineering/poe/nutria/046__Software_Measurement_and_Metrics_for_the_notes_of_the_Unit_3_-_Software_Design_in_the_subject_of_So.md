

### Software Measurement and Metrics

* Software measurement is the process of quantifying the attributes of software products, software processes, and software components.
* Metrics are used to measure the quality and performance of software. Common metrics include lines of code, defects per KLOC (thousand lines of code), and function points.
* Metrics can be used to compare the quality of different software products and to measure the effectiveness of the software development process.
* Metrics can also be used to guide the design process, to identify potential problems, and to assess the impact of changes.
* Metrics can be used to compare different versions of the same software product, or even different implementations of the same software product.
* Metrics can also be used to compare different software products, or to compare different software development processes.
