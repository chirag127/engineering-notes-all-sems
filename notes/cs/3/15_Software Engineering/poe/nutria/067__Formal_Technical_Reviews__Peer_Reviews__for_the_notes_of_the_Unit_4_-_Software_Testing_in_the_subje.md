
### Formal Technical Reviews (Peer Reviews)

* Formal Technical Reviews (FTRs) are a process used in software engineering to ensure the quality of a software product or process.
* FTRs involve a team of experts in the field of software engineering who review the product or process and provide feedback on its quality and effectiveness.
* FTRs are an important part of the software development lifecycle, as they can help identify potential issues before they become major problems.
* FTRs can include reviews of code, designs, user interfaces, and other aspects of the software.
* FTRs are typically conducted by a team of peers, who provide their feedback and opinions in a formal setting.
* FTRs are important for ensuring that a software product meets the requirements of its users and is of the highest quality.
