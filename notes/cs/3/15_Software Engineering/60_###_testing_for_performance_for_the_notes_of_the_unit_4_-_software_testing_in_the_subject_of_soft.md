3. Types of Performance Testing (e.g. load testing, stress testing, etc.)
4. Overview of Types of Performance Testing (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
5. Performance Testing Techniques (e.g. benchmark testing, profiling, etc.)
6. Overview of Performance Testing Techniques (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)

10. Overview of Performance Testing Metrics (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)

Testing for Performance is an important component of the subject of Software Engineering. The hands-on practice and further reading resources will help students to develop their skills and deepen their understanding of the material.
