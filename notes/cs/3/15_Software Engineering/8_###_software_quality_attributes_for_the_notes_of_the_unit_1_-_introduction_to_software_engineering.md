### Software Quality Attributes for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Software Engineering in the subject of Software Engineering

Software Quality Attributes are characteristics that describe the properties of a software system that are important to its stakeholders. These attributes are used to evaluate the quality of a software system and to help ensure that it meets the needs of its users.

In the context of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Software Engineering in the subject of Software Engineering, students will learn about the various software quality attributes and how they are used to evaluate the quality of a software system. This may include working with examples of software quality attributes, analyzing the benefits and limitations of different software quality attributes, and learning how to design and implement software systems that meet the needs of their stakeholders.

Some common software quality attributes include:

1. Usability: The ease with which users can learn and use the software.
2. Performance: The speed and efficiency with which the software performs its intended tasks.
3. Reliability: The ability of the software to perform its intended tasks without failure.
4. Security: The ability of the software to protect sensitive information and prevent unauthorized access.
5. Maintainability: The ease with which the software can be modified and updated to meet changing requirements.
6. Scalability: The ability of the software to handle increasing amounts of data and users.

In the Unit 1 - Introduction to Software Engineering, students will learn about these and other software quality attributes, and will learn how to evaluate the quality of a software system based on these attributes. They will also learn about the trade-offs that are involved in designing software systems that meet the needs of their stakeholders, and will learn how to make informed decisions about the quality attributes that are most important for their software systems.

In summary, in the Unit 1 - Introduction to Software Engineering in the subject of Software Engineering, students will learn about software quality attributes and how they are used to evaluate the quality of a software system. They will learn about common software quality attributes, such as usability, performance, reliability, security, maintainability, and scalability, and will learn how to design and implement software systems that meet the needs of their stakeholders.
