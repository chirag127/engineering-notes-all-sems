### Software Quality Assurance (SQA) for the notes of the Unit 2 - Software Requirement Specifications (SRS) in the subject of Software Engineering

Software Quality Assurance (SQA) is the process of evaluating the quality of a software system during and after its development. The goal of SQA is to identify and prevent defects and to ensure that the software system meets its requirements and is of high quality.

Unit 2 - Software Requirement Specifications (SRS) in the subject of Software Engineering will cover SQA and the techniques used to evaluate the quality of a software system. This may include working with examples of SQA techniques, analyzing the benefits and limitations of different SQA techniques, and learning how to apply SQA techniques to real-world software development projects.

Some common SQA techniques include:

1. Review and inspection: Conducting reviews and inspections of the software system and its documentation to identify defects and ensure that the software system meets its requirements.
2. Testing: Conducting tests of the software system to identify defects and ensure that the software system meets its requirements.
3. Verification and validation: Verifying that the software system meets its requirements and validating that the software system is of high quality.
4. Metrics: Collecting and analyzing metrics about the software system, such as code quality, performance, and reliability, to evaluate the quality of the software system.

In the Unit 2 - Software Requirement Specifications (SRS) in the subject of Software Engineering, students will learn about these and other SQA techniques, and will learn how to choose the most appropriate SQA technique for a given software development project. They will also learn about the challenges involved in SQA, such as ensuring that all defects are identified and prevented, and ensuring that the software system meets its requirements and is of high quality.

In summary, in Unit 2 - Software Requirement Specifications (SRS) in the subject of Software Engineering, students will learn about Software Quality Assurance (SQA), the process of evaluating the quality of a software system during and after its development. They will learn about common SQA techniques, such as review and inspection, testing, verification and validation, and metrics, and will learn how to choose the most appropriate SQA technique for a given software development project.
