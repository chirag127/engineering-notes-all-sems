### Feasibility Study for the notes of the Unit 2 - Software Requirement Specifications (SRS) in the subject of Software Engineering

A Feasibility Study is a preliminary evaluation of a software development project that determines whether it is technically, economically, and operationally feasible to proceed with the project. The following are some of the key concepts related to Feasibility Study:

1. Objectives: The objectives of a Feasibility Study are to determine the viability of a software development project and to identify any technical, economic, or operational constraints that may affect the project.

2. Technical Feasibility: Technical Feasibility involves evaluating the technical aspects of a software development project, including the availability of hardware and software resources, the compatibility of the proposed software with existing systems, and the ability of the development team to implement the proposed software.

3. Economic Feasibility: Economic Feasibility involves evaluating the financial aspects of a software development project, including the costs of development, the expected benefits, and the return on investment.

4. Operational Feasibility: Operational Feasibility involves evaluating the operational aspects of a software development project, including the ability of the software to meet the needs of the users, the ability of the software to integrate with existing systems, and the ability of the software to meet the requirements of the organization.

5. Stakeholder Analysis: Stakeholder Analysis involves identifying and evaluating the needs and expectations of the stakeholders, including the customers, users, development team, and management.

6. Recommendations: The Feasibility Study concludes with recommendations on whether to proceed with the software development project, and if so, what changes should be made to the project plan.

In this unit, you will learn about Feasibility Study and how it is used in software development. You will also learn about the key concepts related to Feasibility Study, including technical feasibility, economic feasibility, operational feasibility, stakeholder analysis, and recommendations.

This unit will provide a foundation for understanding the principles and practices of software development, and for exploring the various concepts and techniques used in the field of Software Engineering.
