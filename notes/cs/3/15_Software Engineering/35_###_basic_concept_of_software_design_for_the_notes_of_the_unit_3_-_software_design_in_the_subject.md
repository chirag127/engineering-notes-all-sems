### Basic Concept of Software Design for the notes of the Unit 3 - Software Design in the subject of Software Engineering

The Basic Concept of Software Design section of the Unit 3 - Software Design in the subject of Software Engineering should cover the following topics:

1. Definition of Software Design (the process of defining the architecture, components, modules, interfaces, and data for a software system)
2. Overview of Software Design (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
3. Phases of Software Design (e.g. conceptual design, architectural design, detailed design, etc.)
4. Overview of Phases of Software Design (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
5. Design Techniques (e.g. object-oriented design, data-driven design, etc.)
6. Overview of Design Techniques (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
7. Design Patterns (reusable solutions to common design problems)
8. Overview of Design Patterns (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
9. Design Principles (e.g. SOLID, DRY, etc.)
10. Overview of Design Principles (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
11. Design Documentation (the process of documenting the design of a software system)
12. Overview of Design Documentation (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
13. Limitations of Software Design (e.g. complexity, difficulty in making design decisions, etc.)
14. Techniques for overcoming limitations (e.g. using advanced techniques, improving accuracy, etc.)
15. Software Design in industry applications (how Software Design is used in various industries)
16. Further reading and resources (books, articles, online resources)

The Basic Concept of Software Design is an important component of the subject of Software Engineering. Understanding the principles and techniques of software design helps students to develop the skills required to design and implement effective software systems. The hands-on practice and further reading resources will help students to develop their skills and deepen their understanding of the material.
