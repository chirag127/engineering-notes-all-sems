### Requirement Engineering Process for the notes of the Unit 2 - Software Requirement Specifications (SRS) in the subject of Software Engineering

The Requirement Engineering Process section of the Unit 2 - Software Requirement Specifications (SRS) in the subject of Software Engineering should cover the following topics:

1. Definition of Requirement Engineering (the process of identifying, documenting and managing the requirements of a software system)
2. Overview of Requirement Engineering (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
3. Phases of Requirement Engineering (e.g. requirements gathering, requirements analysis, requirements validation, etc.)
4. Overview of Phases of Requirement Engineering (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
5. Requirements Gathering Techniques (e.g. interviews, questionnaires, observation, etc.)
6. Overview of Requirements Gathering Techniques (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
7. Requirements Analysis Techniques (e.g. use case analysis, requirement traceability, etc.)
8. Overview of Requirements Analysis Techniques (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
9. Requirements Validation Techniques (e.g. inspection, testing, etc.)
10. Overview of Requirements Validation Techniques (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
11. Limitations of Requirement Engineering (e.g. complexity, compatibility, etc.)
12. Techniques for overcoming limitations (e.g. using advanced techniques, improving accuracy, etc.)
13. Requirement Engineering in industry applications (how Requirement Engineering is used in various industries)
14. Further reading and resources (books, articles, online resources)

The Requirement Engineering Process is an important component of the subject of Software Engineering. Understanding the principles and techniques of Requirement Engineering helps students to develop the skills required to design and implement effective software development processes. The hands-on practice and further reading resources will help students to develop their skills and deepen their understanding of the material.
