### Decision Tables for the notes of the Unit 2 - Software Requirement Specifications (SRS) in the subject of Software Engineering

In the Unit 2 - Software Requirement Specifications (SRS) in the subject of Software Engineering, students will learn about Decision Tables, a technique for representing and analyzing complex decision logic.

A Decision Table is a tabular representation of a system's decision logic. It consists of a set of conditions and actions, and it is used to represent the relationships between the conditions and the actions. Decision Tables are used to represent complex decision logic in a clear and concise manner, and they are a useful tool for specifying and testing software requirements.

In a Decision Table, each row represents a unique combination of conditions, and each column represents a different action. The cells in the table specify the relationship between the conditions and the actions. For example, if a particular combination of conditions is met, then a specific action will be taken.

In the Unit 2 - Software Requirement Specifications (SRS) in the subject of Software Engineering, students will learn how to use Decision Tables to represent and analyze complex decision logic. This may include working with examples of Decision Tables, analyzing the benefits and limitations of Decision Tables, and learning how to use Decision Tables to specify and test software requirements.
