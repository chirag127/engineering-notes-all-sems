### Elicitation for the notes of the Unit 2 - Software Requirement Specifications (SRS) in the subject of Software Engineering

Elicitation is the process of gathering information about the requirements of a software system from stakeholders. It is a critical step in the software development process, as it helps to ensure that the software system meets the needs of its users and stakeholders.

Unit 2 - Software Requirement Specifications (SRS) in the subject of Software Engineering will cover the elicitation process and the techniques used to gather information about the requirements of a software system. This may include working with examples of elicitation techniques, analyzing the benefits and limitations of different elicitation techniques, and learning how to apply elicitation techniques to real-world software development projects.

Some common elicitation techniques include:

1. Interviews: Conducting face-to-face or phone interviews with stakeholders to gather information about their requirements.
2. Surveys: Sending out surveys to stakeholders to gather information about their requirements.
3. Focus groups: Holding focus groups with stakeholders to gather information about their requirements.
4. Prototyping: Creating prototypes of the software system to gather information about the requirements from stakeholders.
5. Observation: Observing stakeholders as they use similar systems to gather information about their requirements.

In the Unit 2 - Software Requirement Specifications (SRS) in the subject of Software Engineering, students will learn about these and other elicitation techniques, and will learn how to choose the most appropriate elicitation technique for a given software development project. They will also learn about the challenges involved in eliciting requirements, such as understanding the needs of stakeholders, dealing with conflicting requirements, and ensuring that all requirements are captured.

In summary, in Unit 2 - Software Requirement Specifications (SRS) in the subject of Software Engineering, students will learn about elicitation, the process of gathering information about the requirements of a software system from stakeholders. They will learn about common elicitation techniques, such as interviews, surveys, focus groups, prototyping, and observation, and will learn how to choose the most appropriate elicitation technique for a given software development project.
