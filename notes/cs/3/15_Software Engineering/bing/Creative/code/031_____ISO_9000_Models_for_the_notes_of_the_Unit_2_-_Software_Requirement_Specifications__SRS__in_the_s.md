# ISO 9000 Models

- ISO 9000 is a family of standards that provide guidelines and tools for quality management and quality assurance in various domains, including software engineering .
- ISO 9000 is based on seven quality management principles that help organizations ensure that their products or services are aligned with customer needs. The seven principles are:
  - Customer focus
  - Leadership
  - Engagement of people
  - Process approach
  - Improvement
  - Evidence-based decision making
  - Relationship management
- ISO 9001 is the only standard in the ISO 9000 family that can be certified to, although this is not a requirement. It specifies the requirements for a quality management system that an organization can use to demonstrate its ability to consistently provide products and services that meet customer and regulatory requirements.
- ISO/IEC/IEEE 90003 is a standard that provides guidance for organizations in the application of ISO 9001 to the acquisition, supply, development, operation and maintenance of computer software and related support services. It covers topics such as software quality management, software life cycle processes, software configuration management, software verification and validation, software reviews and audits, software testing, software documentation, and software measurement .
- ISO 9000 certification is a process of verifying that an organization's quality management system conforms to the requirements of ISO 9001. It involves an external audit by an accredited certification body that evaluates the organization's policies, procedures, processes, and records. The benefits of ISO 9000 certification include improved customer satisfaction, reduced costs, increased efficiency, enhanced credibility, and competitive advantage .
- CMMI (Capability Maturity Model Integration) is another model that originated in software engineering and aims to improve the performance and quality of software processes. It defines five levels of maturity that reflect the degree of process standardization, optimization, and improvement. The five levels are:
  - Initial
  - Managed
  - Defined
  - Quantitatively Managed
  - Optimizing
- ISO 9000 and CMMI are complementary models that can be used together to achieve software quality goals. ISO 9000 provides a generic framework for quality management, while CMMI provides specific practices and guidelines for software process improvement. Both models emphasize the importance of customer focus, process approach, measurement, and continuous improvement .
