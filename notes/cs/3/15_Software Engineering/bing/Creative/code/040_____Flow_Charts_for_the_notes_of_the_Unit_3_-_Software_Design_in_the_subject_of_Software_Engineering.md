### Flow Charts

- A flow chart is a graphical representation of the sequence of steps or actions of a process, algorithm or workflow.
- A flow chart can show the inputs, outputs, decisions, loops, branches, and subroutines of a system or program.
- A flow chart can help to design, document, analyze, and improve a system or program.
- A flow chart can also help to communicate the logic and functionality of a system or program to others.

#### Advantages and Disadvantages

- Some of the advantages of flow charts are:

  - They are easy to understand and follow.
  - They can help to visualize the logic and structure of a system or program.
  - They can help to identify errors and inefficiencies in a system or program.
  - They can help to facilitate communication and collaboration among stakeholders.

- Some of the disadvantages of flow charts are:

  - They can become complex and cluttered for large and complicated systems or programs.
  - They can be time-consuming and costly to create and maintain.
  - They can be inconsistent and ambiguous due to different symbols and notations.
  - They can be limited in expressing some aspects of a system or program, such as data structures, concurrency, and exception handling.
