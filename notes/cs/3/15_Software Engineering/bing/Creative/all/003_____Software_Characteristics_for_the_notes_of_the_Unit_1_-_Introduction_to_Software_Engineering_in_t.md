# Software Characteristics

Software is a set of instructions, data or programs used to operate computers and execute specific tasks. Software can be classified into different types based on its functionality, complexity, purpose, and quality. Software characteristics are the attributes or features that describe the quality and performance of the software.

According to the ISO/IEC 25010:2011 standard, software quality can be measured by eight main characteristics, each composed of sub-characteristics. These are:

- **Functional suitability**: Refers to the degree of performance of the software against its intended purpose. It includes the sub-characteristics of functional completeness, functional correctness, and functional appropriateness.
- **Reliability**: Refers to the ability of the software to provide desired functionality under the given conditions. It includes the sub-characteristics of maturity, availability, fault tolerance, and recoverability.
- **Operability**: Refers to the degree of ease and efficiency with which the software can be used by the intended users. It includes the sub-characteristics of appropriateness recognizability, learnability, user error protection, user interface aesthetics, and accessibility.
- **Performance efficiency**: Refers to the degree of resource utilization and responsiveness of the software. It includes the sub-characteristics of time behavior, resource utilization, and capacity.
- **Security**: Refers to the degree of protection of the software from unauthorized access, use, modification, or destruction. It includes the sub-characteristics of confidentiality, integrity, non-repudiation, accountability, and authenticity.
- **Compatibility**: Refers to the degree of interoperability and coexistence of the software with other systems or components. It includes the sub-characteristics of co-existence and interoperability.
- **Maintainability**: Refers to the degree of ease and cost-effectiveness with which the software can be modified or corrected. It includes the sub-characteristics of modularity, reusability, analyzability, modifiability, and testability.
- **Transferability**: Refers to the degree of ease and cost-effectiveness with which the software can be adapted or transferred to different environments or platforms. It includes the sub-characteristics of adaptability, installability, replaceability, and portability.

These characteristics can be used to evaluate the quality of software and to guide the software development and maintenance processes.
