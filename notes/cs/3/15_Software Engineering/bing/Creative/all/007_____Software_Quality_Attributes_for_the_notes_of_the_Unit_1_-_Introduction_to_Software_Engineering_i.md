# Software Quality Attributes

- Software quality attributes are the characteristics of software that affect its performance, usability, reliability, maintainability, security, etc.
- Software quality attributes are also known as non-functional requirements, quality characteristics, quality factors, or quality criteria.
- Software quality attributes are important because they influence the user satisfaction, the development cost, the operational cost, and the business value of software.
- Software quality attributes can be classified into two categories: external and internal.

## External Quality Attributes

- External quality attributes are the ones that are visible to the users and stakeholders of software. They are also called product quality attributes or user quality attributes.
- External quality attributes include:

  - **Functionality**: The degree to which software meets the specified requirements and provides the expected services.
  - **Usability**: The degree to which software is easy to learn, use, and understand by the intended users.
  - **Reliability**: The degree to which software performs consistently and correctly under normal and abnormal conditions.
  - **Efficiency**: The degree to which software uses the minimum amount of resources (such as time, memory, CPU, etc.) to achieve the desired results.
  - **Portability**: The degree to which software can be transferred from one environment to another with minimal effort or adaptation.
  - **Compatibility**: The degree to which software can interoperate with other systems or components without conflicts or errors.
  - **Scalability**: The degree to which software can handle increasing or decreasing workloads without degrading its performance or quality.
  - **Availability**: The degree to which software is accessible and operational when needed by the users or stakeholders.
  - **Security**: The degree to which software protects the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of the data and resources from unauthorized access or misuse.

## Internal Quality Attributes

- Internal quality attributes are the ones that are not directly visible to the users and stakeholders of software, but affect the external quality attributes. They are also called process quality attributes or developer quality attributes.
- Internal quality attributes include:

  - **Modularity**: The degree to which software is composed of independent and cohesive modules that can be changed or replaced without affecting other modules.
  - **Reusability**: The degree to which software or its components can be reused in other software or contexts with minimal modification or adaptation.
  - **Testability**: The degree to which software or its components can be verified and validated by using automated or manual testing techniques.
  - **Maintainability**: The degree to which software or its components can be modified or corrected to fix errors, improve quality, or adapt to changing requirements or environments.
  - **Readability**: The degree to which software or its components can be easily understood by the developers or other stakeholders by using clear and consistent naming, formatting, documentation, etc.
  - **Extensibility**: The degree to which software or its components can be extended or enhanced to provide new or improved functionality or quality.
  - **Robustness**: The degree to which software or its components can handle unexpected or erroneous inputs or situations without crashing or producing incorrect results.
  - **Flexibility**: The degree to which software or its components can be configured or customized to meet the specific needs or preferences of the users or stakeholders.
