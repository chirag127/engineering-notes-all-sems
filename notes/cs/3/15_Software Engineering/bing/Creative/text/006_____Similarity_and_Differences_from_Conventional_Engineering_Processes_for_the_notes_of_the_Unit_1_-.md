### Similarity and Differences from Conventional Engineering Processes for Software Engineering

- Conventional engineering processes are the methods and practices used to design, build, and maintain physical systems or products, such as bridges, buildings, machines, etc.
- Software engineering processes are the methods and practices used to design, build, and maintain software systems or products, such as applications, websites, databases, etc.
- Similarities:
  - Both are getting automated slowly.
  - Both require in-depth knowledge of their field.
  - Both follow some principles and standards to ensure quality and reliability.
  - Both involve planning, analysis, design, implementation, testing, and maintenance phases.
- Differences:
  - Software engineering is only related to programming, but conventional engineering is related to thinking, designing, and building tasks.
  - Software engineering products are intangible and abstract, but conventional engineering products are tangible and concrete.
  - Software engineering products are more prone to changes and errors, but conventional engineering products are more stable and robust.
  - Software engineering products have higher complexity and diversity, but conventional engineering products have lower complexity and diversity.
  - Software engineering products have shorter life cycle and faster development, but conventional engineering products have longer life cycle and slower development.
  - Software engineering products have more opportunities of foreign settlement, but conventional engineering products have higher government sector opportunity.
  - Software engineering products have a typical office job, whereas conventional engineering products have a more physically active role.
