### Categories of Maintenance

Software maintenance is the process of modifying and updating a software product after its delivery to correct faults, improve performance, or adapt to changing environments. Software maintenance can be classified into four categories, namely:

- **Corrective maintenance**: This type of maintenance involves fixing errors or bugs that are observed when the software is in use. These errors may be due to design flaws, coding mistakes, hardware failures, or user input errors. Corrective maintenance aims to restore the software to its normal functioning state   .
- **Adaptive maintenance**: This type of maintenance involves modifying and updating the software to run on new platforms, operating systems, hardware, or software environments. Adaptive maintenance aims to keep the software compatible and usable in changing contexts   .
- **Perfective maintenance**: This type of maintenance involves enhancing and improving the software to meet new or changing user requirements, such as adding new features, improving user interface, or optimizing performance. Perfective maintenance aims to increase the software's functionality, usability, and quality   .
- **Preventive maintenance**: This type of maintenance involves detecting and preventing potential errors or problems before they occur, such as refactoring code, improving documentation, or applying coding standards. Preventive maintenance aims to reduce the risk of future faults, increase the maintainability, and extend the software's lifespan   .
