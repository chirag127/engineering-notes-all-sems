### Information Modelling for the notes of the Unit 2 - Software Requirement Specifications (SRS) in the subject of Software Engineering

- Information modelling is a process of representing the concepts, relationships, constraints, rules, and operations of a domain of discourse in a formal way .
- Information modelling is used to specify the data semantics and structure of a software system, and to facilitate communication and understanding among stakeholders .
- Information modelling can be done at different levels of abstraction, such as conceptual, logical, and physical .
- Information modelling can use different notations and techniques, such as entity-relationship (ER) diagrams, class diagrams, data flow diagrams, etc .
- Information modelling can benefit from using standard languages and frameworks, such as Unified Modeling Language (UML), Integration DEFinition for information modeling (IDEF1X), etc.
- Information modelling can help to identify and resolve data quality issues, such as redundancy, inconsistency, incompleteness, etc .
- Information modelling can support the development and maintenance of software systems, such as database design, data integration, data analysis, etc .
