## Unit 5 - Software Maintenance and Software Project Management

### Software Maintenance

- Software maintenance is the process of modifying and updating software after it has been delivered to the customer.
- Software maintenance can be classified into four types: corrective, adaptive, perfective, and preventive.
- Corrective maintenance is the process of fixing errors or bugs that are discovered in the software after its delivery.
- Adaptive maintenance is the process of modifying the software to cope with changes in the environment, such as new hardware, operating systems, or standards.
- Perfective maintenance is the process of enhancing the software to improve its performance, usability, or functionality.
- Preventive maintenance is the process of modifying the software to prevent future problems or errors, such as improving the documentation, code structure, or testing procedures.
- Software maintenance can be performed using different strategies, such as patching, versioning, or reengineering.
- Patching is the process of applying small changes or fixes to the software without changing its overall structure or functionality.
- Versioning is the process of releasing new versions of the software that incorporate major changes or enhancements to the software.
- Reengineering is the process of redesigning and redeveloping the software from scratch or using existing components, to improve its quality, maintainability, or adaptability.

### Software Project Management

- Software project management is the process of planning, organizing, directing, and controlling the activities and resources involved in developing software.
- Software project management can be divided into five phases: initiation, planning, execution, monitoring and control, and closure.
- Initiation is the phase where the project scope, objectives, deliverables, and stakeholders are defined and agreed upon.
- Planning is the phase where the project schedule, budget, quality, risk, communication, and procurement plans are developed and documented.
- Execution is the phase where the project deliverables are produced and verified according to the project plan.
- Monitoring and control is the phase where the project progress, performance, and quality are measured and compared with the project plan, and corrective actions are taken if necessary.
- Closure is the phase where the project deliverables are delivered to the customer, the project documentation is finalized, and the project resources are released.
- Software project management can be performed using different methodologies, such as waterfall, agile, or hybrid.
- Waterfall is a sequential and linear methodology that follows the project phases in a fixed order, and requires complete and detailed specifications before starting the development.
- Agile is an iterative and incremental methodology that follows the project phases in a flexible and adaptive manner, and requires frequent and continuous feedback from the customer and stakeholders during the development.
- Hybrid is a combination of waterfall and agile methodologies that follows the project phases in a customized and balanced manner, and requires a mix of upfront and ongoing specifications and feedback during the development.
