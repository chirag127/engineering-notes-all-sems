### Need for Maintenance

- Software maintenance is the process of modifying and updating software after it has been delivered to the end user.
- Software maintenance is necessary for various reasons, such as:
  - To correct errors or bugs that were not detected during testing or that emerged after deployment.
  - To improve the performance, reliability, security, or usability of the software.
  - To adapt the software to changing requirements, user feedback, or environmental conditions.
  - To enhance the functionality or features of the software to meet new or emerging needs.
  - To comply with new standards, regulations, or laws that affect the software.
  - To prevent the software from becoming obsolete or incompatible with other systems or platforms.
- Software maintenance can be classified into four types, according to the purpose and nature of the changes:
  - Corrective maintenance: This involves fixing errors or defects that cause the software to malfunction or deviate from its specifications.
  - Adaptive maintenance: This involves modifying the software to cope with changes in the external environment, such as hardware, operating system, or user interface.
  - Perfective maintenance: This involves improving the quality or performance of the software, such as enhancing its functionality, usability, or efficiency.
  - Preventive maintenance: This involves modifying the software to prevent potential problems or reduce the complexity or cost of future maintenance.
