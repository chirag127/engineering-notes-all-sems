### Software Risk Analysis and Management

- Software risk is the probability of occurrence for uncertain events and their potential for loss within an organization.
- Software risk analysis is the process of identifying, categorizing, and measuring software risks .
- Software risk management is the sequence of steps that help a software team to understand, analyze, and manage uncertainty.
- Software risk management consists of the following steps:
  - Risk identification: The process of finding out the possible risks that may affect the software project objectives. This can be done by using techniques such as brainstorming, checklists, interviews, etc.
  - Risk analysis: The process of assessing the level, likelihood, and impact of each risk. This can be done by using techniques such as probability-impact matrix, risk exposure, risk priority number, etc.
  - Risk planning: The process of developing strategies to avoid, reduce, transfer, or accept the risks. This can be done by using techniques such as risk avoidance, risk mitigation, risk transfer, risk acceptance, etc.
  - Risk monitoring: The process of tracking and reviewing the status of the risks and the effectiveness of the risk plans. This can be done by using techniques such as risk audits, risk reviews, risk reports, etc.
  - Risk control: The process of implementing the risk plans and taking corrective actions when needed. This can be done by using techniques such as risk triggers, contingency plans, escalation procedures, etc.
- Software risk management can help to improve the quality, reliability, and security of the software, as well as to reduce the cost, time, and effort of the software development .
- Software risk management can be supported by using software risk management tools that provide features such as risk identification, analysis, planning, monitoring, and control. Some examples of software risk management tools are MetricStream, RiskyProject, Active Risk Manager, etc.
