### Perfective Maintenance

- Perfective maintenance is one of the types of software maintenance that focuses on improving the functionality, usability, performance, and quality of the software system  .
- Perfective maintenance is not driven by errors or problems, but by customer feedback, user requirements, market changes, or business goals  .
- Perfective maintenance involves adding new features, enhancing existing features, optimizing code, removing obsolete or unused features, or updating documentation   .
- Perfective maintenance aims to keep the software relevant, competitive, and satisfying for the users and stakeholders .
- Perfective maintenance requires a clear understanding of the user needs, the system design, the development process, and the testing methods .
- Perfective maintenance can be challenging, costly, and time-consuming, as it may introduce new errors, dependencies, or complexities in the software system .
- Perfective maintenance can be beneficial, as it can increase the user satisfaction, the system efficiency, the software quality, and the software lifespan   .
