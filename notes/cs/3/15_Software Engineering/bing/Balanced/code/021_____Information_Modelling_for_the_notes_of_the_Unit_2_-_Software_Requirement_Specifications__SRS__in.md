### Information Modelling for the notes of the Unit 2 - Software Requirement Specifications (SRS) in the subject of Software Engineering

- Information modelling is the process of creating a representation of the data and information used by a software system.
- Information modelling helps to identify and define the data entities, attributes, relationships, constraints, and operations that are relevant to the system's functionality and quality.
- Information modelling can be done at different levels of abstraction, such as conceptual, logical, and physical.
- Information modelling can use different notations and techniques, such as entity-relationship diagrams, class diagrams, data dictionaries, and data flow diagrams.
- Information modelling can be used for various purposes, such as:
  - To communicate and document the data and information requirements of the system to the stakeholders.
  - To validate and verify the completeness, consistency, and correctness of the data and information requirements of the system.
  - To facilitate the design, implementation, testing, and maintenance of the data and information components of the system.
  - To support the analysis and evaluation of the data and information quality, security, performance, and scalability of the system.
- Information modelling is an iterative and incremental process that involves the following steps:
  - Identify the data and information entities and their attributes that are relevant to the system's scope and objectives.
  - Identify the relationships and associations among the data and information entities and their attributes.
  - Identify the constraints and rules that govern the data and information entities, attributes, and relationships.
  - Identify the operations and functions that manipulate and process the data and information entities, attributes, and relationships.
  - Represent the data and information entities, attributes, relationships, constraints, and operations using a suitable notation and technique.
  - Review and refine the information model based on feedback from the stakeholders and the system's requirements.
