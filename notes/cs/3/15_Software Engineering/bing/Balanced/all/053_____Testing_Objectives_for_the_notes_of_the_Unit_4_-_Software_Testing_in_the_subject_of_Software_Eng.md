# Testing Objectives

- The main objective of testing is to ensure that the software meets the specified requirements and satisfies the intended purpose.
- Testing also aims to detect and correct any defects or errors in the software that may affect its functionality, reliability, usability, performance, security, or maintainability.
- Testing helps to improve the quality of the software by identifying and eliminating the sources of faults and failures.
- Testing provides confidence and assurance to the stakeholders that the software meets their expectations and needs.
- Testing verifies and validates the software against the design specifications and the user requirements.
- Testing facilitates the delivery and deployment of the software by ensuring that it is ready for use and meets the acceptance criteria.
- Testing supports the maintenance and evolution of the software by ensuring that the changes and enhancements do not introduce new defects or degrade the existing quality.
- Testing also helps to comply with the standards and regulations that may apply to the software domain or industry.
