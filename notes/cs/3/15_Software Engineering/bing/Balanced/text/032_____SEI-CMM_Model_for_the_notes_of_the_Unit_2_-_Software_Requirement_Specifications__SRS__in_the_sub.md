### SEI-CMM Model

- SEI stands for Software Engineering Institute, a research and development center at Carnegie Mellon University that provides guidance and best practices for software engineering.
- CMM stands for Capability Maturity Model, a framework that describes the key elements of an effective software process and the levels of maturity of an organization's software development capability.
- The SEI-CMM Model was developed in 1986 based on a study of data collected from organizations that contracted with the U.S. Department of Defense.
- The SEI-CMM Model has five levels of maturity, each representing a stage of improvement in the software process:
  - Level 1: Initial. The software process is unpredictable, poorly controlled, and reactive. There are no defined or standardized processes, and the success of projects depends on individual efforts and heroics.
  - Level 2: Repeatable. The software process is disciplined, and projects can track cost, schedule, and functionality. Basic project management processes are established, and previous successes can be repeated.
  - Level 3: Defined. The software process is well-defined, understood, and consistent across the organization. The software process is documented, standardized, and integrated into a common process framework. The organization has a set of software engineering and management standards and guidelines that are tailored for each project.
  - Level 4: Managed. The software process is quantitatively measured and controlled. The organization collects and analyzes data on the quality and performance of the software process and products. The organization sets quantitative quality goals and uses them to guide process improvement.
  - Level 5: Optimizing. The software process is continuously improved based on quantitative feedback and data analysis. The organization identifies and implements best practices, and proactively changes and adapts the software process to meet changing needs and goals.
- The SEI-CMM Model helps organizations to assess their current level of maturity, identify the key process areas that need improvement, and implement the best practices that are appropriate for their context and goals.
- The SEI-CMM Model is widely used and recognized as a standard for software process improvement, and has been adopted by many organizations in various domains and industries.
