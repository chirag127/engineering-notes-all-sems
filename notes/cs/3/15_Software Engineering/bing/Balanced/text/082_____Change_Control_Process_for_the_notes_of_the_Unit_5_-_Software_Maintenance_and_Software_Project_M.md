### Change Control Process

Change control is the process that a company uses to document, identify and authorize changes to an IT environment. It reduces the chances of unauthorized alterations, disruption and errors in the system. Change control is also known as change management in software engineering.

The change control process consists of the following steps :

- **Change request initiation**: In this step, a change is requested by a stakeholder, a user, a developer or a manager. The change request should describe the problem or the need, the proposed solution, the benefits and the risks of the change, and the priority and urgency of the change.
- **Change request assessment**: In this step, the change request is reviewed and assessed by a change control board (CCB) or a change manager. The CCB or the change manager is responsible for approving, rejecting or deferring the change request based on the criteria such as feasibility, cost, impact, alignment with the project scope and objectives, and compliance with the standards and regulations.
- **Change request analysis**: In this step, the approved change request is analyzed in detail by the project team or the change implementer. The analysis should include the estimation of the resources, time, budget and quality required for the change, the identification of the dependencies and the conflicts with other changes or requirements, the definition of the acceptance criteria and the testing plan, and the documentation of the change specifications and the implementation plan.
- **Change request implementation**: In this step, the change is implemented according to the plan by the project team or the change implementer. The implementation should follow the best practices and the guidelines of the software development life cycle (SDLC), the quality assurance (QA) and the configuration management (CM). The implementation should also be monitored and controlled by the CCB or the change manager to ensure that the change is delivered on time, within budget and with the expected quality.
- **Change request evaluation**: In this step, the change is evaluated by the CCB or the change manager, the project team or the change implementer, and the stakeholder or the user. The evaluation should include the verification and the validation of the change against the acceptance criteria and the testing plan, the measurement of the benefits and the risks of the change, the identification of the lessons learned and the best practices, and the documentation of the change results and the feedback.

The change control process is an essential part of the software engineering process, as it helps to manage the changes in a systematic, consistent and transparent way. It also helps to ensure that the changes are aligned with the project scope and objectives, and that they deliver value to the stakeholders and the users. The change control process can be adapted to different software development methodologies, such as waterfall, agile or hybrid.
