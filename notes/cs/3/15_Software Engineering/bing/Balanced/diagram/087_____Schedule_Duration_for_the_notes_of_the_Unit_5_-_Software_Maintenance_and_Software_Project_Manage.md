### Schedule/Duration

- Schedule is the plan that defines the activities, milestones, and resources for a software project.
- Duration is the amount of time required to complete a software project or a specific activity within it.
- Schedule and duration are important factors for software project management, as they affect the cost, quality, and scope of the software product.
- Schedule and duration are influenced by various factors, such as:
  - The size and complexity of the software product
  - The requirements and specifications of the software product
  - The skills and experience of the software team
  - The availability and reliability of the software tools and resources
  - The risks and uncertainties involved in the software project
  - The stakeholder expectations and feedback
- Schedule and duration can be estimated using various techniques, such as:
  - Expert judgment: Based on the opinions and experience of the software team or other experts
  - Analogous estimation: Based on the comparison of the software project with similar past projects
  - Parametric estimation: Based on the use of mathematical models and formulas that relate the software project characteristics to the schedule and duration
  - Bottom-up estimation: Based on the aggregation of the estimates of the individual tasks and activities of the software project
  - Top-down estimation: Based on the decomposition of the software project into smaller and manageable units and estimating them
- Schedule and duration can be monitored and controlled using various tools and techniques, such as:
  - Gantt chart: A graphical representation of the software project schedule that shows the start and end dates of the activities and their dependencies
  - Network diagram: A graphical representation of the software project schedule that shows the sequence and duration of the activities and their dependencies
  - Critical path method: A technique that identifies the longest path of dependent activities in the software project schedule and determines the minimum possible duration of the software project
  - Earned value management: A technique that measures the progress and performance of the software project by comparing the planned and actual schedule and duration
  - Schedule variance: A measure of the difference between the planned and actual schedule of the software project
  - Schedule performance index: A measure of the efficiency of the software project schedule
