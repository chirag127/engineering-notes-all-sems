### Information Modelling

- Information modelling is a process of creating a representation of the data and information requirements of a system or domain.
- Information modelling can help to identify and document the data entities, attributes, relationships, constraints, and operations that are relevant for the system or domain.
- Information modelling can also help to define the data flow, data structure, data manipulation, and data validation within the system or domain.
- Information modelling can be done at different levels of abstraction, such as conceptual, logical, and physical.
- Information modelling can use different techniques and notations, such as entity-relationship diagrams, class diagrams, data dictionaries, and data flow diagrams.
- Information modelling can support the software requirement specification (SRS) by providing a clear and consistent description of the data and information aspects of the system or domain.
- Information modelling can also facilitate the communication and collaboration among the stakeholders, such as users, developers, analysts, and testers.
