### Formal Technical Reviews (Peer Reviews)

- A formal technical review (FTR) is a software quality assurance activity performed by software engineers and others  .
- The objectives of FTR are :
  - To uncover errors in function, logic, or implementation for any representation of the software, such as requirements, design, code, test cases, etc.
  - To verify that the software meets the specified requirements and conforms to the standards and guidelines.
  - To improve the software development process by identifying and eliminating the root causes of defects.
  - To provide feedback and suggestions for improvement to the software developers and managers.
  - To serve as a training ground for junior engineers to learn from the different approaches and techniques of software engineering.
- The FTR process consists of the following steps  :
  - Planning: The review leader selects the review team, assigns roles and responsibilities, schedules the review meeting, and distributes the review materials to the reviewers.
  - Preparation: The reviewers study the review materials and identify potential issues, questions, and comments. They also prepare a checklist of items to be verified during the review.
  - Review meeting: The review team meets to discuss the review materials and resolve the issues. The review leader moderates the meeting and ensures that the review objectives are met. The review scribe records the minutes of the meeting and the list of action items.
  - Rework: The software developers perform the necessary corrections and improvements based on the review feedback and action items.
  - Follow-up: The review leader verifies that the rework has been done correctly and closes the review. The review leader also prepares a review report that summarizes the review results and recommendations.
- The FTR team typically consists of the following roles  :
  - Review leader: The person who plans, organizes, and conducts the review. The review leader is usually a senior software engineer or a software quality assurance engineer.
  - Reviewer: The person who evaluates the review materials and provides feedback and suggestions. The reviewer is usually a peer software engineer or a domain expert.
  - Author: The person who created the review materials and is responsible for the rework. The author is usually the software developer or the software tester.
  - Scribe: The person who records the review meeting and the action items. The scribe is usually a junior software engineer or a software quality assurance engineer.
  - Manager: The person who oversees the review process and provides support and resources. The manager is usually the software project manager or the software quality assurance manager.
- The FTR benefits include    :
  - Improved software quality by detecting and removing defects early in the software development life cycle.
  - Reduced software development cost and time by avoiding rework and testing in the later stages.
  - Enhanced software reliability and maintainability by ensuring compliance with the standards and guidelines.
  - Increased software productivity and efficiency by sharing knowledge and best practices among the software engineers.
  - Strengthened software team collaboration and communication by fostering a culture of constructive feedback and mutual learning.
