### SQA Plans

- Software quality assurance (SQA) is a method of ensuring that all software engineering processes, methods, activities and work items are monitored and conform to established standards .
- Standards may include ISO 9000, CMMI model, ISO15504, or a combination of these standards along with others .
- SQA plan is a document that describes the scope, approach, resources, and schedule of the SQA activities for a software project .
- SQA plan should also define the roles and responsibilities of the SQA team, the tools and techniques to be used, the metrics and reports to be generated, and the quality objectives and criteria to be met .
- SQA plan should be aligned with the software requirement specification (SRS) and the software development plan (SDP) of the project .
- SQA plan should be reviewed and approved by the project stakeholders and updated as the project evolves .
- SQA plan should cover the following aspects of the software project :
  - Quality management: how the quality standards and processes will be defined, implemented, and controlled
  - Quality assurance: how the quality of the software work products and processes will be verified and validated
  - Quality control: how the defects and non-conformities will be identified and corrected
  - Quality improvement: how the quality performance and feedback will be measured and analyzed for continuous improvement
