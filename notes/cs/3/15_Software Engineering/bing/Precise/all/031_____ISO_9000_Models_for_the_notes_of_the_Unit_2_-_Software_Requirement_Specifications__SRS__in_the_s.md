# ISO 9000 Models

ISO 9000 is a series of international standards that define the requirements for a Quality Management System (QMS). The standards were developed by the International Organization for Standardization (ISO) and are designed to help organizations ensure that they meet the needs of their customers and other stakeholders.

The ISO 9000 family of standards includes several different models, including:

1. **ISO 9001:** This is the most widely used standard in the ISO 9000 family. It specifies the requirements for a QMS that can be used by any organization, regardless of its size or the industry it operates in.

2. **ISO 9004:** This standard provides guidelines for organizations that want to go beyond the requirements of ISO 9001 and achieve sustained success. It focuses on the long-term success of the organization and takes a broader view of quality management.

3. **ISO 19011:** This standard provides guidelines for auditing management systems, including QMSs. It covers the principles of auditing, managing an audit program, and conducting management system audits.

These standards can be used by organizations to develop and implement a QMS that is tailored to their specific needs. By following the requirements of the ISO 9000 standards, organizations can demonstrate their commitment to quality and continuous improvement. This can help them to improve customer satisfaction, reduce costs, and increase efficiency.
