### SQA Plans for the notes of the Unit 2 - Software Requirement Specifications (SRS) in the subject of Software Engineering

- SQA stands for Software Quality Assurance.
- SQA is a process that ensures that the software being developed meets the specified requirements and standards.
- SQA plans are documents that outline the procedures and standards that will be used to ensure the quality of the software being developed.
- SQA plans are an important part of the software development process, as they help to ensure that the final product is of high quality and meets the needs of the users.
- SQA plans should be developed early in the software development process, and should be updated as the project progresses.
- SQA plans should include information on the standards and procedures that will be used to ensure the quality of the software, as well as the roles and responsibilities of the team members involved in the SQA process.
- SQA plans should also include information on the tools and techniques that will be used to measure and improve the quality of the software.
- SQA plans should be reviewed and approved by all stakeholders, including the development team, project managers, and customers.
