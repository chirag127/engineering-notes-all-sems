### Cost of Maintenance

Software maintenance is an essential part of the software development life cycle. It involves the process of modifying and updating software after delivery to correct faults, improve performance, or adapt to a changing environment. The cost of software maintenance can vary depending on several factors, including the type of software, the number of users, and the required maintenance activities.

- Generally, software maintenance costs around $5000 per month or $60000 per year. However, it can be less or more depending on the development team you're working with and other factors.
- The cost of software maintenance is high compared to other elements of the Software Development Life Cycle like designing, testing, and implementation. A study has found out that the cost of maintenance can be as high as 60% of the total cost of the software development process.
- The cost of software maintenance can include module independence, programming language, programming style, program validation and testing, documentation, and configuration management techniques.
- Some costs are reasonably constant, such as the cost of operations, support, and corrective maintenance, while others will vary, such as license variations, upgrades, and enhancements.
