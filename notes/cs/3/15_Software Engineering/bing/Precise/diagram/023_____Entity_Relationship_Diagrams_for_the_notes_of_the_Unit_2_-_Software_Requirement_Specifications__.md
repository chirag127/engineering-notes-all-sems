### Entity Relationship Diagrams

Entity Relationship Diagrams (ERD) are a graphical representation of the relationships between entities in a database. They are used in the design of database systems and are a key component of the Software Requirement Specifications (SRS) in the subject of Software Engineering.

- ERDs are used to model the data requirements of a system and to identify the relationships between different entities.
- Entities are objects or concepts that are represented in the database, such as customers, products, or orders.
- Relationships are the associations between entities, such as a customer placing an order or a product being part of an order.
- Attributes are the properties of entities, such as the name of a customer or the price of a product.
- ERDs can be used to identify the cardinality of relationships, which specifies how many instances of one entity can be associated with instances of another entity.
- ERDs can also be used to identify the optionality of relationships, which specifies whether an instance of one entity must be associated with an instance of another entity.
- ERDs are an important tool for database design, as they help to ensure that the data requirements of a system are accurately represented and that the relationships between entities are correctly defined.
