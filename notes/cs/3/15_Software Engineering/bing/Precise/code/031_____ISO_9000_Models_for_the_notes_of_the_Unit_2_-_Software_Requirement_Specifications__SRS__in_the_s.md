### ISO 9000 Models

ISO 9000 is a series of international standards that define the requirements for a Quality Management System (QMS). The standards are published by the International Organization for Standardization (ISO) and are designed to help organizations ensure that they meet the needs of customers and other stakeholders while meeting statutory and regulatory requirements related to a product or service.

The ISO 9000 family of standards includes:

- **ISO 9001:** This standard specifies the requirements for a QMS and is the only standard in the family that can be certified to. It is based on the Plan-Do-Check-Act (PDCA) cycle and provides a framework for organizations to follow in order to ensure that they consistently provide products and services that meet customer and regulatory requirements.

- **ISO 9000:** This standard provides the fundamental concepts and terminology used in the ISO 9000 family of standards. It is not a certifiable standard, but rather provides a foundation for understanding the other standards in the family.

- **ISO 9004:** This standard provides guidance on how to achieve sustained success through a QMS. It is not a certifiable standard, but rather provides a framework for organizations to follow in order to continuously improve their processes and achieve long-term success.

- **ISO 19011:** This standard provides guidelines for auditing management systems, including the principles of auditing, managing an audit program, and conducting management system audits.

The ISO 9000 family of standards is applicable to organizations of all sizes and types, and is widely recognized as a best practice for managing quality. Organizations can choose to implement a QMS based on the ISO 9000 standards in order to demonstrate their commitment to quality and to provide assurance to customers and other stakeholders that their products and services meet the highest standards of quality.
