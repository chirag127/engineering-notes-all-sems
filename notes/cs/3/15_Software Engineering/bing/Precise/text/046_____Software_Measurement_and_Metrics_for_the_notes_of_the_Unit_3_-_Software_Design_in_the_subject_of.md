### Software Measurement and Metrics

Software measurement is the process of quantifying the characteristics of software products and processes. It is used by software engineers to gain insight into the design and development of work products, and to assist in strategic decision-making as a project proceeds .

Software metrics are used to measure software processes, products, and projects. There are three types of software metrics: process metrics, product metrics, and project metrics .

- **Process Metrics:** These metrics are used to measure the software development process. They help in estimation, quality control, productivity assessment, and project control throughout a software project .

- **Product Metrics:** These metrics are used to evaluate the state of the product, trace risks, and uncover potential problem areas. They allow software engineers to assess the quality of the design and include architectural design metrics, component-level design metrics, and so on  .

- **Project Metrics:** These metrics are used to measure the project and its characteristics. They help in project planning, monitoring, and control .

Software metrics can be used by software development teams to communicate the status of software development projects, pinpoint and address issues, and monitor, improve, and better manage their workflow .
