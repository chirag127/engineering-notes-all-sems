### ISO 9000 Models

ISO 9000 is a set of international standards for quality management and quality assurance. The standards provide a framework for organizations to ensure that their products and services meet customer requirements and that quality is consistently improved. The ISO 9000 family of standards includes:

1. **ISO 9000:2015** - This standard provides the fundamental concepts, principles, and vocabulary for quality management systems.
2. **ISO 9001:2015** - This standard specifies the requirements for a quality management system. It is the only standard in the ISO 9000 family that can be used for certification.
3. **ISO 9004:2018** - This standard provides guidelines for achieving sustained success through a quality management approach.
4. **ISO 19011:2018** - This standard provides guidelines for auditing management systems, including the principles of auditing, managing an audit program, and conducting management system audits.

Organizations can use the ISO 9000 standards to demonstrate their ability to consistently provide products and services that meet customer and regulatory requirements. The standards can be applied to any organization, regardless of its size or the industry it operates in. The standards are based on the principle of continual improvement and focus on customer satisfaction, leadership, involvement of people, process approach, system approach to management, factual approach to decision making, and mutually beneficial supplier relationships.
