### Decision Tables

Decision tables are a precise yet compact way to model complicated logic. Decision tables, like flowcharts and if-then-else and switch-case statements, associate conditions with actions to perform, but in many cases do so in a more elegant way.

Here are some key points to remember about decision tables:

1. A decision table is a table that lists all possible combinations of conditions and the corresponding actions to be taken.
2. Decision tables are used to model complex logic in a clear and concise manner.
3. Decision tables can be used to represent business rules, system requirements, or any other decision-making process.
4. Decision tables are particularly useful when there are multiple conditions that must be evaluated in order to determine the appropriate action.
5. Decision tables can be used to simplify and clarify complex if-then-else or switch-case statements in computer programs.
6. Decision tables can be easily converted into decision trees, which are another way to represent complex logic.
