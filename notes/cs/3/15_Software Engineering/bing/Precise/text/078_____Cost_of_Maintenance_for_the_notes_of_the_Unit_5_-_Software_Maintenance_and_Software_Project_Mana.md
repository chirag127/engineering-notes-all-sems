### Cost of Maintenance

- Reports suggest that the cost of maintenance is high. A study on estimating software maintenance found that the cost of maintenance is as high as 67% of the cost of the entire software process cycle. On average, the cost of software maintenance is more than 50% of all SDLC phases.

- The cost of software maintenance is high compared to other elements of the Software Development Life Cycle like designing, testing, and implementation. A study has found out that the cost of maintenance can be as high as 60% of the total cost of the software development process.

- Be ready to pay from $70 to $320 per month. Generally, software maintenance costs around $5000 per month or $60000 per year. Still, it can be less or more depending on the development team you're working with and other factors.

- The maintenance efforts require about 60% of the total life-cycle cost for a typical software product. However, maintenance costs vary widely from one application domain to another.

- Depending on the software type, the number of users, and the required activities, the monthly software maintenance cost may vary from $5,000 to $50,000+.

- The cost of software maintenance can include module independence, programming language, programming style, program validation and testing, documentation, and configuration management techniques.
