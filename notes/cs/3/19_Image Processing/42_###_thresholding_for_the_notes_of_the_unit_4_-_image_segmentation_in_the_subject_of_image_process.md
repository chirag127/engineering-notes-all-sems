### Thresholding for the notes of the Unit 4 - IMAGE SEGMENTATION in the subject of Image Processing

Unit 4 - IMAGE SEGMENTATION in the subject of Image Processing will cover the concept of Thresholding, a fundamental technique used for image segmentation.

Thresholding is a technique used to segment an image into multiple regions based on the intensity values of the pixels. Thresholding is used to separate the foreground and background of an image, or to separate different objects in an image.
