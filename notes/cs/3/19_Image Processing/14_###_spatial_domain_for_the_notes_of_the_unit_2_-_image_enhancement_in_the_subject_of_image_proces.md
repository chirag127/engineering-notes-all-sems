### Spatial Domain for the notes of the Unit 2 - IMAGE ENHANCEMENT in the subject of Image Processing

Unit 2 - Image Enhancement in the subject of Image Processing will cover the concept of spatial domain and its role in image enhancement.

Spatial domain refers to the representation of an image as a 2D array of pixels, where each pixel represents the intensity or color value of the image at that location. Image enhancement in the spatial domain involves processing the image in the spatial domain to improve its quality or to extract information from the image.

In the context of Unit 2 - Image Enhancement in the subject of Image Processing, students will learn about the spatial domain and its role in image enhancement. This may include working with examples of image enhancement in the spatial domain, analyzing the techniques used for image enhancement in the spatial domain, and learning how to use the spatial domain for image enhancement.
