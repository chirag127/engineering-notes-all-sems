## Unit 4 - IMAGE SEGMENTATION

Image segmentation is the process of partitioning an image into meaningful regions or objects. The following are some of the key concepts related to image segmentation:

1. Image Representation: Image segmentation starts with the representation of an image, which can be represented in various forms such as intensity values, color values, and more. The choice of representation depends on the specific requirements of the image segmentation task.

2. Thresholding: Thresholding is a simple and widely used technique for image segmentation, which involves dividing an image into two or more regions based on a threshold value for the intensity or color values of the pixels.

3. Region-Based Segmentation: Region-based segmentation is a technique for image segmentation that involves dividing an image into regions based on the properties of the pixels, such as intensity, color, texture, and more.

4. Edge-Based Segmentation: Edge-based segmentation is a technique for image segmentation that involves dividing an image into regions based on the edges or boundaries between the regions.

5. Clustering: Clustering is a technique for image segmentation that involves grouping the pixels in an image into clusters based on their similarity.

6. Graph-Based Segmentation: Graph-based segmentation is a technique for image segmentation that involves representing the image as a graph and dividing the graph into regions based on the properties of the graph.

7. Performance Evaluation: Performance evaluation is the process of measuring the effectiveness of an image segmentation technique, and can be performed using metrics such as accuracy, precision, recall, and more. Performance evaluation is important for comparing different image segmentation techniques and for selecting the best technique for a given image.

In this unit, you will learn about image segmentation, including image representation, thresholding, region-based segmentation, edge-based segmentation, clustering, graph-based segmentation, and performance evaluation.

This unit will provide a foundation for understanding the principles and practices of image segmentation, and for exploring the various concepts and techniques used in the field of image processing.
