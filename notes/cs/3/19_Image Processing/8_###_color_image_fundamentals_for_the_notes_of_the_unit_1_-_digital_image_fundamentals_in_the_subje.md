### Color image fundamentals for the notes of the Unit 1 - DIGITAL IMAGE FUNDAMENTALS in the subject of Image Processing

Unit 1 - DIGITAL IMAGE FUNDAMENTALS in the subject of Image Processing will cover the basics of color image fundamentals.

Color images are digital images that contain color information, and are used in a variety of applications, including photography, video, and graphics. Color images are typically represented using three channels, red, green, and blue (RGB), which are combined to produce a wide range of colors.

In the context of Unit 1 - DIGITAL IMAGE FUNDAMENTALS in the subject of Image Processing, students will learn about the basics of color image fundamentals, including the RGB color model and how it is used to represent color images, and the different color spaces used to represent color images, such as the RGB color space, the hue-saturation-value (HSV) color space, and the lab color space.

In Unit 1 - DIGITAL IMAGE FUNDAMENTALS in the subject of Image Processing, students will also learn about the different techniques used to process color images, including color correction, color enhancement, and color manipulation, and will learn about the different applications of color image processing, such as computer vision, image analysis, and computer graphics.
