### Image Restoration for the notes of the Unit 3 - IMAGE RESTORATION in the subject of Image Processing

The Image Restoration section of the Unit 3 - IMAGE RESTORATION in the subject of Image Processing should cover the following topics:

1. Definition of Image Restoration (the process of removing degradation from an image)
2. Overview of Image Restoration (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
3. Types of Image Degradation (e.g. noise, blur, etc.)
4. Overview of Types of Image Degradation (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
5. Image Restoration Techniques (e.g. inverse filtering, Wiener filtering, etc.)
6. Overview of Image Restoration Techniques (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
7. Image Restoration Algorithms (e.g. the algorithms used to implement image restoration techniques)
8. Overview of Image Restoration Algorithms (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
9. Image Restoration Performance Evaluation (e.g. metrics used to evaluate the performance of image restoration techniques)
10. Overview of Image Restoration Performance Evaluation (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
11. Image Restoration in Industry Applications (how image restoration is used in various industries)
12. Further reading and resources (books, articles, online resources)

Image Restoration is an important component of the subject of Image Processing. Understanding the principles and techniques of image restoration helps students to develop the skills required to design and implement effective image processing solutions. The hands-on practice and further reading resources will help students to develop their skills and deepen their understanding of the material.
