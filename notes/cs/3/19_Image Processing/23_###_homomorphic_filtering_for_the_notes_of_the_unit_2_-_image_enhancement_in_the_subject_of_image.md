### Homomorphic filtering for the notes of the Unit 2 - IMAGE ENHANCEMENT in the subject of Image Processing

Homomorphic filtering is a technique used in image processing to improve the visibility of details in an image by removing noise and preserving the underlying structure of the image. The following are some of the key concepts related to homomorphic filtering:

1. Image Modeling: Homomorphic filtering is based on a mathematical model of the image, which represents the image as a combination of low-frequency and high-frequency components.

2. Noise Reduction: Homomorphic filtering is used to reduce the noise in an image by removing the high-frequency components, which are typically associated with noise, while preserving the low-frequency components, which are associated with the underlying structure of the image.

3. Brightness Correction: Homomorphic filtering is used to correct the brightness of an image by adjusting the low-frequency components, which are associated with the overall brightness of the image.

4. Non-Linear Filtering: Homomorphic filtering is a non-linear filtering technique, as it involves transforming the image into the frequency domain, applying a filter to the frequency-domain representation, and then transforming the image back into the spatial domain.

5. Image Enhancement: Homomorphic filtering is used to enhance the visibility of details in an image by removing noise and correcting the brightness, which can be useful for improving the quality of the image for further processing or for visual inspection.

In this unit, you will learn about homomorphic filtering, including image modeling, noise reduction, brightness correction, non-linear filtering, and image enhancement.

This unit will provide a foundation for understanding the principles and practices of homomorphic filtering, and for exploring the various concepts and techniques used in the field of image processing.
