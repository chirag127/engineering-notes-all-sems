### MPEG for the notes of the Unit 5 - IMAGE COMPRESSION AND RECOGNITION in the subject of Image Processing

MPEG (Moving Picture Experts Group) is a group of experts who develop international standards for audio and video compression. The following are some of the key concepts related to MPEG:

1. Compression Standards: MPEG develops international standards for audio and video compression, including MPEG-1, MPEG-2, MPEG-4, and more. These standards provide a common framework for compressing and decompressing audio and video data, and are widely used in various applications, such as digital television, digital video, and more.

2. Video Compression: MPEG standards provide a framework for compressing video data, which can be used to reduce the size of video files and to transmit video data over networks. MPEG video compression is based on various techniques, such as inter-frame compression, intra-frame compression, and more.

3. Audio Compression: MPEG standards provide a framework for compressing audio data, which can be used to reduce the size of audio files and to transmit audio data over networks. MPEG audio compression is based on various techniques, such as perceptual coding, psychoacoustic modeling, and more.

4. Multimedia Applications: MPEG standards are widely used in various multimedia applications, such as digital television, digital video, and more. These applications use MPEG standards to compress and decompress audio and video data, and to transmit audio and video data over networks.

5. Interoperability: MPEG standards provide a framework for interoperability, which is the ability of different systems to work together. Interoperability is important for ensuring that audio and video data can be transmitted and played back on different systems, regardless of the manufacturer or platform.

In this unit, you will learn about MPEG, including compression standards, video compression, audio compression, multimedia applications, and interoperability.

This unit will provide a foundation for understanding the principles and practices of MPEG, and for exploring the various concepts and techniques used in the field of image processing.
