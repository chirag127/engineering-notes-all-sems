## Unit 5 - IMAGE COMPRESSION AND RECOGNITION

Unit 5 - IMAGE COMPRESSION AND RECOGNITION in the subject of Image Processing will cover the concepts of Image Compression and Image Recognition, two important techniques used in the field of Image Processing.

Image Compression refers to the process of reducing the size of an image without sacrificing its quality. Image Compression is used to reduce the amount of data required to store or transmit an image, and to improve the speed of image processing.

Image Recognition refers to the process of automatically recognizing objects or patterns in an image. Image Recognition is used for a variety of applications, including image classification, object detection, and pattern recognition.

In the context of Unit 5 - IMAGE COMPRESSION AND RECOGNITION in the subject of Image Processing, students will learn about Image Compression and Image Recognition, including the different types of Image Compression techniques, such as lossless and lossy compression, and the different types of Image Recognition techniques, such as template matching and feature-based recognition. This may include working with examples of Image Compression and Image Recognition, analyzing the syntax and structure of Image Compression and Image Recognition, and learning how to use Image Compression and Image Recognition for image processing.

In Unit 5 - IMAGE COMPRESSION AND RECOGNITION in the subject of Image Processing, students will also learn about other aspects of Image Processing, such as image enhancement, image restoration, and image segmentation, and will learn how these techniques can be used in conjunction with Image Compression and Image Recognition for image processing.

In summary, in Unit 5 - IMAGE COMPRESSION AND RECOGNITION in the subject of Image Processing, students will learn about Image Compression and Image Recognition, two important techniques used in the field of Image Processing. They will learn about the different types of Image Compression and Image Recognition techniques, and how to use Image Compression and Image Recognition for image processing, and will learn about other aspects of Image Processing and how these techniques can be used in conjunction with Image Compression and Image Recognition for image processing.
