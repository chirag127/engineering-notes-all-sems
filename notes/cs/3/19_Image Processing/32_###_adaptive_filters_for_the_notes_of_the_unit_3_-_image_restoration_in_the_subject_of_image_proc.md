### Adaptive filters for the notes of the Unit 3 - IMAGE RESTORATION in the subject of Image Processing

Adaptive filters are filters that adjust their parameters based on the local characteristics of the image, and are used in image processing to restore degraded images. The following are some of the key concepts related to adaptive filters:

1. Image Degradation: Adaptive filters are used to restore degraded images, which are images that have been degraded by various factors, such as noise, blur, and compression.

2. Local Characteristics: Adaptive filters adjust their parameters based on the local characteristics of the image, such as the mean and variance of the intensity values of the pixels in a neighborhood around each pixel.

3. Non-Linear Filtering: Adaptive filters are typically non-linear filters, as they involve transforming the image into the frequency domain, applying a filter to the frequency-domain representation, and then transforming the image back into the spatial domain.

4. Image Restoration: Adaptive filters are used to restore degraded images by removing noise and restoring the underlying structure of the image, which can be useful for improving the quality of the image for further processing or for visual inspection.

5. Noise Reduction: Adaptive filters are used to reduce the noise in an image by adjusting the parameters of the filter based on the local characteristics of the image, which can be useful for improving the visibility of details in an image or for correcting for noise problems.

In this unit, you will learn about adaptive filters, including image degradation, local characteristics, non-linear filtering, image restoration, and noise reduction.

This unit will provide a foundation for understanding the principles and practices of adaptive filters, and for exploring the various concepts and techniques used in the field of image processing.
