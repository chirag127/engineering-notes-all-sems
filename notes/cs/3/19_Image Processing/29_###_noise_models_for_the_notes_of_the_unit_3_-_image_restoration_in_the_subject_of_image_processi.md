### Noise models for the notes of the Unit 3 - IMAGE RESTORATION in the subject of Image Processing

Noise models are mathematical representations of the random variations in the intensity values of the pixels in an image. The following are some of the key concepts related to noise models:

1. Types of Noise: There are several types of noise that can affect digital images, including Gaussian noise, salt and pepper noise, speckle noise, and more. Each type of noise has its own characteristics, and the choice of noise model depends on the specific type of noise present in the image.

2. Noise Characteristics: Noise models are used to describe the statistical properties of the noise in an image, including the mean and variance of the noise, and the distribution of the noise. Understanding the noise characteristics is important for selecting the appropriate noise reduction technique.

3. Noise Reduction: Noise reduction is the process of removing or reducing the noise in an image, and the choice of noise reduction technique depends on the specific type of noise and the noise characteristics. Common noise reduction techniques include filtering, thresholding, and more.

4. Image Restoration: Image restoration is the process of removing or reducing the degradation in an image, and noise reduction is an important component of image restoration. Image restoration can improve the visual quality of an image and make it more suitable for further processing and analysis.

5. Performance Evaluation: Performance evaluation is the process of measuring the effectiveness of a noise reduction technique, and can be performed using metrics such as mean squared error, peak signal-to-noise ratio, and more. Performance evaluation is important for comparing different noise reduction techniques and for selecting the best technique for a given image.

In this unit, you will learn about noise models, including types of noise, noise characteristics, noise reduction, image restoration, and performance evaluation.

This unit will provide a foundation for understanding the principles and practices of noise models, and for exploring the various concepts and techniques used in the field of image processing.
