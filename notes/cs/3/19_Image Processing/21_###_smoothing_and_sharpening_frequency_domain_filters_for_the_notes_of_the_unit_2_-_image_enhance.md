Smoothing filters are used to reduce noise and other unwanted details in an image, while sharpening filters are used to enhance the details in an image. Both smoothing and sharpening filters can be implemented in either the spatial domain or the frequency domain.

In the frequency domain, smoothing filters are implemented by removing high-frequency components from the image, while sharpening filters are implemented by adding high-frequency components to the image. The frequency domain representation of an image is obtained using the Fourier transform, which provides a representation of the image in terms of its frequency components.
