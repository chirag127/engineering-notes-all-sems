Band reject filters are filters used in image processing to remove specific frequency components from an image, while preserving other frequency components. The following are some of the key concepts related to band reject filters:

1. Image Modeling: Band reject filters are based on a mathematical model of the image, which represents the image as a combination of frequency components.

2. Frequency Domain Processing: Band reject filters operate in the frequency domain, which is a representation of the image in terms of its frequency components.

3. Frequency Selectivity: Band reject filters are selective in terms of the frequency components they remove, and are designed to remove specific frequency bands from the image, while preserving other frequency components.

4. Image Restoration: Band reject filters are used in image restoration to remove specific types of noise, such as periodic noise, from an image, while preserving the underlying structure of the image.

5. Non-Linear Filtering: Band reject filters are non-linear filtering techniques, as they involve transforming the image into the frequency domain, applying a filter to the frequency-domain representation, and then transforming the image back into the spatial domain.

6. Image Enhancement: Band reject filters can also be used for image enhancement, as they can be used to remove specific types of noise, such as periodic noise, from an image, which can improve the visibility of details in the image.
