### Steps in Digital Image Processing for the notes of the Unit 1 - DIGITAL IMAGE FUNDAMENTALS in the subject of Image Processing

Unit 1 - DIGITAL IMAGE FUNDAMENTALS in the subject of Image Processing will cover the steps involved in digital image processing.

Digital image processing is the process of performing operations on digital images to extract meaningful information or to modify the images in some way. The steps involved in digital image processing can vary depending on the specific application, but typically include the following:

1. Image acquisition: This is the first step in digital image processing, where the image is captured or acquired from a source such as a camera, scanner, or file.

2. Image enhancement: This step involves improving the visual quality of the image, such as increasing contrast or removing noise.

3. Image restoration: This step involves removing distortions from the image, such as blurring or degradation.

4. Image analysis: This step involves extracting meaningful information from the image, such as detecting edges or identifying objects.

5. Image compression: This step involves reducing the size of the image to reduce storage and transmission requirements.

6. Image recognition: This step involves recognizing patterns in the image, such as identifying objects or faces.

7. Image synthesis: This step involves creating new images, such as combining multiple images or generating synthetic images.

In the context of Unit 1 - DIGITAL IMAGE FUNDAMENTALS in the subject of Image Processing, students will learn about the steps involved in digital image processing, including image acquisition, image enhancement, image restoration, image analysis, image compression, image recognition, and image synthesis. This may include working with examples of digital image processing, analyzing the syntax and structure of digital image processing, and learning how to perform digital image processing for various applications.

In summary, in Unit 1 - DIGITAL IMAGE FUNDAMENTALS in the subject of Image Processing, students will learn about the steps involved in digital image processing, including image acquisition, image enhancement, image restoration, image analysis, image compression, image recognition, and image synthesis, and will learn how to perform digital image processing for various applications.
