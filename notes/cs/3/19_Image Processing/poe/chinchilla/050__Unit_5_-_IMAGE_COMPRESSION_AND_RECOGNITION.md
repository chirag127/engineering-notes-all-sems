## Unit 5 - IMAGE COMPRESSION AND RECOGNITION

In this unit, we will be discussing the concepts of image compression and recognition. These topics are crucial in the field of computer vision and image processing, and are used in a wide range of applications, including image and video compression, image recognition, and object detection.

### Image Compression

1. Image compression is the process of reducing the size of an image file while retaining its quality.
2. There are two types of image compression: lossless and lossy.
3. Lossless compression methods preserve all the original data in the image and are suitable for images that cannot afford to lose any data, such as medical images.
4. Lossy compression methods, on the other hand, discard some of the original data in the image to achieve a smaller size, and are suitable for images that can tolerate some loss of data, such as photographs.
5. Some popular image compression techniques include JPEG, PNG, and GIF.

### Image Recognition

1. Image recognition is the process of identifying and labeling objects in an image.
2. It is a form of artificial intelligence that uses machine learning algorithms to analyze and identify patterns in images.
3. Image recognition is used in various applications, such as self-driving cars, security systems, and medical imaging.
4. The process of image recognition involves several steps, including feature extraction, classification, and object detection.
5. Feature extraction involves identifying the key features of an image, such as edges, corners, and textures.
6. Classification involves categorizing the image based on its features, such as identifying whether it is a dog or a cat.
7. Object detection involves identifying the location and boundaries of objects in the image.

In conclusion, image compression and recognition are essential concepts in the field of computer vision and image processing. Understanding these topics is crucial for developing efficient and effective image processing systems.
