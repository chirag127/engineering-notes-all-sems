### RGB, HSI models for the notes of the Unit 1 - DIGITAL IMAGE FUNDAMENTALS in the subject of Image Processing

In the field of image processing, color models play a vital role in representing and manipulating the color information of digital images. Two widely used color models are RGB and HSI models. Let's take a closer look at each of them.

#### RGB Model

RGB stands for Red, Green, and Blue. It is an additive color model in which colors are created by adding different intensities of red, green, and blue light. The RGB model is widely used in digital imaging and is the color model used by most displays such as computer screens, TVs, and smartphones.

Following are some key points to keep in mind about the RGB model:

- In the RGB model, each pixel in a digital image is represented by three values, each corresponding to the intensity of red, green, and blue light.
- The intensity of each color ranges from 0 to 255, with 0 being the absence of that color and 255 being the maximum intensity.
- The RGB model is an additive model, meaning that when we add all three primary colors (red, green, and blue) at their maximum intensities, we get white color.
- The RGB model is not very intuitive for color manipulation, as changing the intensity of one color can affect the other colors as well.

#### HSI Model

HSI stands for Hue, Saturation, and Intensity. It is a cylindrical color model that represents colors based on their hue, saturation, and intensity values. The HSI model is more intuitive for color manipulation than the RGB model.

Following are some key points to keep in mind about the HSI model:

- In the HSI model, each pixel in a digital image is represented by three values - Hue, Saturation, and Intensity.
- Hue represents the actual color of the pixel, such as red, blue, or yellow.
- Saturation represents the intensity of the color, with 0 saturation representing a shade of gray and 100 saturation representing the most vivid color.
- Intensity represents the brightness of the pixel, ranging from 0 (black) to 100 (white).
- The HSI model is more intuitive for color manipulation, as changing the hue or saturation values does not affect the intensity value.

In conclusion, both RGB and HSI models are important in digital image processing. While the RGB model is widely used for displays, the HSI model is more intuitive for color manipulation. Understanding these color models is essential for manipulating and processing digital images.
