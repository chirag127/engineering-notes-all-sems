### Band pass Filters for the notes of the Unit 3 - IMAGE RESTORATION in the subject of Image Processing

In the field of Image Processing, Band pass filters play a crucial role in image restoration. They are used to enhance the images by focusing on the frequency domain of the image. In this section, we will discuss the key aspects of Band pass filters and their types.

#### What are Band pass Filters?
Band pass filters are digital filters that allow the frequencies in a specific range to pass through while blocking the frequencies outside the range. They are used to remove noise or unwanted frequencies from an image while retaining the essential features. They are particularly useful in image restoration tasks, where the objective is to improve the quality of the image.

#### Types of Band pass Filters
There are different types of Band pass filters, and each type has a unique set of characteristics. The following are some of the commonly used Band pass filters in image restoration:

1. Ideal Band pass Filter: This filter allows only the frequencies in a specific range to pass through while blocking all other frequencies. It is an ideal filter in theory, but in practice, it is difficult to implement due to the Gibbs phenomenon.

2. Butterworth Band pass Filter: This filter is designed to have a smooth transition between the passband and stopband, which makes it less susceptible to the Gibbs phenomenon. It is a popular choice in image processing due to its simplicity and effectiveness.

3. Gaussian Band pass Filter: This filter uses a Gaussian function to attenuate the frequencies outside the passband. It is useful in image processing tasks that require a smooth and gradual attenuation of frequencies.

#### Applications of Band pass Filters
Band pass filters have numerous applications in image processing, and some of them are listed below:

1. Image Restoration: Band pass filters are used to remove noise and enhance the image quality.

2. Image Compression: Band pass filters are used to compress the image data by removing the frequencies outside the passband.

3. Feature Extraction: Band pass filters are used to extract specific features from an image by focusing on a particular frequency range.

In conclusion, Band pass filters are an essential tool in image restoration tasks. They allow us to enhance the image quality by removing unwanted frequencies while retaining the essential features. Understanding the types and applications of Band pass filters is crucial for anyone working in the field of Image Processing.
