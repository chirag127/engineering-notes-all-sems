### Properties for the notes of the Unit 3 - IMAGE RESTORATION in the subject of Image Processing

In the Unit 3 of Image Processing, you will learn about image restoration, which is the process of improving the quality of an image that has been degraded or corrupted. Here are some properties to keep in mind when taking notes for this unit:

- **Understand the degradation process**: Before restoring an image, it's important to understand how it was degraded or corrupted. This can help you choose the appropriate restoration method and parameters. Degradation can occur due to various factors such as noise, blur, and compression.

- **Choose the appropriate restoration method**: There are several restoration methods available, each with its own advantages and disadvantages. Some common methods include inverse filtering, Wiener filtering, and total variation regularization. You should understand the basic principles behind each method and when to use them.

- **Consider the trade-off between noise reduction and detail preservation**: Image restoration often involves reducing noise in the image, but this can also result in loss of detail. It's important to strike a balance between noise reduction and detail preservation based on the specific application.

- **Choose appropriate parameters for the restoration method**: Most restoration methods require the selection of parameters that control the trade-off between noise reduction and detail preservation. You should understand the effect of each parameter and how to choose appropriate values.

- **Evaluate the quality of the restored image**: After restoring an image, it's important to evaluate the quality of the result. This can be done using various metrics such as peak signal-to-noise ratio (PSNR) and structural similarity index (SSIM). You should understand these metrics and how to interpret their results.

- **Consider the limitations of image restoration**: Image restoration is a challenging task and has its limitations. It's important to understand the limitations of the restoration methods and the factors that can affect the quality of the restored image.

By keeping these properties in mind, you can take effective notes and develop a solid understanding of image restoration in the context of Image Processing.
