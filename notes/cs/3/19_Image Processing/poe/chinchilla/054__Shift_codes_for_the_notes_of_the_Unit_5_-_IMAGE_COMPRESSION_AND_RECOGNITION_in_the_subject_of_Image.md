#### Image Compression

- Image compression is the process of reducing the size of an image file to save storage space or to transmit the image more efficiently over a network.
- Lossy and lossless are the two main types of image compression. Lossless compression maintains the original quality of the image, while lossy compression sacrifices some of the image's quality to achieve a smaller file size.
- The most popular lossless image compression formats are PNG and GIF, while JPEG is the most popular lossy image compression format.
- The JPEG compression process involves dividing the image into small blocks, transforming them into the frequency domain using the Discrete Cosine Transform (DCT), and quantizing the coefficients to reduce the number of bits needed to represent them.
- The amount of compression achieved by JPEG depends on the quality level selected, with higher quality levels resulting in larger file sizes.

#### Image Recognition

- Image recognition is the process of identifying objects, people, or other features in an image using computer algorithms.
- Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) are the most popular type of algorithm used for image recognition. They are designed to recognize patterns in images by learning from large datasets of labeled images.
- The process of training a CNN involves feeding a large dataset of labeled images into the network and adjusting the weights of the network's layers to minimize the error between the predicted and actual labels.
- Transfer learning is a technique that involves using a pre-trained CNN as a starting point for a new recognition task. By retraining only the last few layers of the network, transfer learning can significantly reduce the amount of training data and time needed to achieve high accuracy.
- Image recognition has many practical applications, including facial recognition, object detection in self-driving cars, and medical image analysis.
