### Steps in Digital Image Processing for the notes of the Unit 1 - DIGITAL IMAGE FUNDAMENTALS in the subject of Image Processing

Digital image processing is the process of manipulating digital images using mathematical algorithms. The following are the steps involved in digital image processing:

1. Image Acquisition - This is the first step in digital image processing. It involves the collection of raw data from an image source such as a camera, scanner or any other digital device.

2. Pre-processing - This step involves enhancing the quality of the acquired image. This includes adjusting the brightness, contrast, and sharpness of the image. The pre-processing step also involves removing noise and unwanted artifacts from the image.

3. Segmentation - Image segmentation is the process of dividing an image into multiple segments or regions. This is done to analyze each segment separately and apply different processing techniques to them. 

4. Feature Extraction - After segmentation, the next step is to extract features from the image. These features can include color, texture, shape, and size. The extracted features are used for classification, object detection, and recognition.

5. Object Recognition - Object recognition is the process of identifying objects in an image. This is done by matching the extracted features with a pre-defined database of objects.

6. Image Enhancement - Image enhancement is the process of improving the quality of an image. This includes sharpening, smoothing, and adjusting the brightness and contrast of the image.

7. Image Restoration - Image restoration is the process of recovering a degraded image. This involves removing noise, blurring or other distortions from the image.

8. Compression - Image compression is the process of reducing the size of an image without compromising its quality. This is done to reduce storage space and transmission time.

9. Analysis - Image analysis is the process of extracting information from an image. This includes measuring the size, shape, and color of objects in the image.

In conclusion, digital image processing is a complex process involving multiple steps. Each step is important in order to produce high-quality images that can be used for a variety of applications.
