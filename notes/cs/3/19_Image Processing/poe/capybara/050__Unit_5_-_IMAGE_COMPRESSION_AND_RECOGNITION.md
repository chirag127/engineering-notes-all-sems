

## Unit 5 - IMAGE COMPRESSION AND RECOGNITION

In this unit, we will learn about image compression and recognition. This is an important topic in the field of computer science and technology. Here are some points that you need to keep in mind while studying this topic:

### Image Compression

- Image Compression is the process of reducing the size of an image without losing its quality or important details.
- There are two types of image compression techniques: Lossless and Lossy.
- Lossless compression techniques preserve all the details of an image while reducing its size.
- Lossy compression techniques remove some of the details of an image to reduce its size.
- Popular image compression techniques include JPEG, PNG, and GIF.

### Image Recognition

- Image Recognition is the process of identifying objects or patterns in an image.
- It is an important technique used in various fields, including computer vision, robotics, and artificial intelligence.
- Image Recognition can be achieved through various algorithms and techniques, including Machine Learning and Deep Learning.
- Some popular image recognition applications include facial recognition, object detection, and handwriting recognition.
- Image recognition has various real-world applications, including self-driving cars, medical diagnosis, and security systems.

In conclusion, image compression and recognition are important topics in the field of computer science and technology. By understanding the concepts and techniques involved in these fields, you can develop advanced applications and systems that can revolutionize various industries.
