### Band pass Filters for the notes of the Unit 3 - IMAGE RESTORATION in the subject of Image Processing

Band pass filters in image processing are used for several purposes, such as restoring blurred images, enhancing images, and sharpening images. In this section, we will discuss the concept of band pass filters and their applications in image restoration.

1. **What are Band Pass Filters?**
   - Band pass filters are a type of filter that allows a specific range of frequencies to pass through while blocking all other frequencies.
   - They are used to filter out noise and unwanted frequencies in images.
   - The passband of a band pass filter ranges from a lower frequency to a higher frequency, and the stopband is the range of frequencies that are blocked.

2. **Types of Band Pass Filters**
   - Butterworth Filter: It is a type of filter that has a smooth frequency response and is commonly used for image restoration and enhancement.
   - Gaussian Filter: It is a type of filter that uses a Gaussian function to determine the weight of the filter coefficients. It is commonly used for noise reduction and image smoothing.
   - Laplacian Filter: It is a type of filter that enhances the edges in an image by highlighting the areas with a high rate of change in intensity.

3. **Applications of Band Pass Filters in Image Restoration**
   - Deblurring: Band pass filters can be used to restore blurred images by removing the frequencies that cause blurring.
   - Denoising: Band pass filters can be used to remove noise from an image by filtering out the high-frequency components that correspond to noise.
   - Sharpening: Band pass filters can be used to enhance the edges in an image by amplifying the high-frequency components that correspond to the edges.

4. **Conclusion**
   - Band pass filters are a powerful tool in image processing for restoring, enhancing, and sharpening images.
   - The choice of filter depends on the specific application and the characteristics of the image.
   - By using band pass filters, it is possible to improve the quality of images and make them more visually appealing.
