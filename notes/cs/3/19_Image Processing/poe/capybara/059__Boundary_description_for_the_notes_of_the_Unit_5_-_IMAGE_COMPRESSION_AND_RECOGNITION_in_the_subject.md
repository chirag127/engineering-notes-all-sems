### Boundary description for the notes of the Unit 5 - IMAGE COMPRESSION AND RECOGNITION in the subject of Image Processing

The Unit 5 of Image Processing deals with the concepts of Image Compression and Recognition. To understand these concepts better, it is important to understand the boundaries of this unit. 

Here are some boundary descriptions for the notes of Unit 5:

- **Image Compression:** This topic deals with the various methods used to compress digital images. The boundaries of this topic include:
  - Understanding the types of image compression techniques i.e., Lossless and Lossy Compression
  - Learning the algorithms for each type of compression
  - Understanding the factors that affect the image quality after compression
  - Understanding the trade-offs between image size and image quality
  - Understanding the applications of image compression in various fields such as medical imaging and satellite imaging

- **Image Recognition:** This topic deals with the various methods used to recognize the content of digital images. The boundaries of this topic include:
  - Understanding the basics of image recognition
  - Learning the different types of image recognition techniques such as Pattern Recognition, Object Recognition, and Face Recognition
  - Understanding the algorithms used in each technique
  - Understanding the factors that affect the accuracy of image recognition
  - Understanding the applications of image recognition in various fields such as security and surveillance

- **Comparison between Image Compression and Recognition:** This topic deals with the comparison between the methods used for image compression and recognition. The boundaries of this topic include:
  - Understanding the similarities and differences between image compression and recognition
  - Analyzing the algorithms used for both techniques
  - Understanding the trade-offs between image size and image recognition accuracy
  - Understanding the applications of both techniques in various fields

Overall, the boundaries of Unit 5 - IMAGE COMPRESSION AND RECOGNITION in the subject of Image Processing are focused on understanding the algorithms, techniques, and applications of both image compression and recognition. It is important to have a clear understanding of these boundaries to excel in this unit and have a better understanding of the subject as a whole.
