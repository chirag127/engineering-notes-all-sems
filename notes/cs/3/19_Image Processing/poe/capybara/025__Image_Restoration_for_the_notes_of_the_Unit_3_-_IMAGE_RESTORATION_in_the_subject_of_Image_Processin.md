### Image Restoration

Image restoration is the process of improving the quality of an image that has been degraded due to various factors such as noise, blur, and compression artifacts. The goal of image restoration is to produce a visually appealing image that is as close to the original as possible. In this unit, we will explore various techniques used in image restoration.

#### Image degradation

Before we dive into image restoration, it is important to understand the different types of image degradation. Image degradation can be broadly classified into two categories:

1. **Spatial degradation:** Spatial degradation occurs due to blur, noise, and other factors that affect the spatial domain of the image. 

2. **Frequency degradation:** Frequency degradation occurs due to compression artifacts and other factors that affect the frequency domain of the image.

#### Image restoration techniques

There are various techniques used in image restoration. Some of the commonly used techniques are:

1. **Spatial domain filtering:** Spatial domain filtering is used to remove noise and blur from an image. This technique involves applying a filter mask to the image to remove unwanted noise and blur.

2. **Frequency domain filtering:** Frequency domain filtering is used to remove compression artifacts and other types of frequency degradation. This technique involves transforming the image to the frequency domain using Fourier transform and then applying a filter to remove unwanted artifacts.

3. **Wavelet-based restoration:** Wavelet-based restoration is a technique that uses wavelets to decompose an image into different scales and orientations. This technique is useful in restoring images that have complex structures and textures.

4. **Iterative restoration:** Iterative restoration is a technique that involves iteratively refining an image to improve its quality. This technique is useful in restoring images that have low signal-to-noise ratio.

#### Conclusion

Image restoration is an important technique used in image processing to improve the quality of degraded images. In this unit, we explored various techniques used in image restoration such as spatial domain filtering, frequency domain filtering, wavelet-based restoration, and iterative restoration.
