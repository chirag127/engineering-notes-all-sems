

### Wiener Filtering for Image Restoration

1. Wiener filtering is a method of image restoration that uses a filter to reduce noise in an image.

2. Wiener filtering is based on the concept of signal-to-noise ratio (SNR). The filter is designed to reduce the noise in the image while preserving the original signal.

3. Wiener filtering is a linear filter, meaning it operates on the values of the pixels in the image.

4. Wiener filtering is a non-adaptive filter, meaning it does not change its parameters based on the image.

5. Wiener filtering is a statistical filter, meaning it uses statistical methods to determine the filter parameters.

6. Wiener filtering is an iterative process, meaning it requires multiple iterations to achieve the desired result.

7. Wiener filtering can be used to reduce noise in an image, sharpen an image, and enhance edges.

8. Wiener filtering is often used in medical imaging, such as X-ray and MRI, to reduce noise and improve the clarity of the images.
