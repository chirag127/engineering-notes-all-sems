
### Fourier Descriptor for the Notes of Unit 5 - IMAGE COMPRESSION AND RECOGNITION

1. Fourier Descriptors are a set of mathematical equations used to describe a shape or object.
2. They are used in image compression and recognition as a way to represent an image in a compact form.
3. The Fourier Descriptor is a set of coefficients that represent the shape of an object.
4. The coefficients are calculated by taking the Fourier transform of the image.
5. The Fourier Descriptor is then used to compare two images and determine whether they are similar or not.
6. The Fourier Descriptor is also used in image recognition systems to identify objects in an image.
7. The Fourier Descriptor is an efficient way to represent an image and is used in many applications such as medical imaging, facial recognition, and object recognition.
