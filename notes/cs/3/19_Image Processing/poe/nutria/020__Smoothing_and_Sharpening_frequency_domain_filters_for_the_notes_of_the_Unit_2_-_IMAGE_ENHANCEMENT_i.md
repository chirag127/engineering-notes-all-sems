
### Smoothing and Sharpening frequency domain filters for the notes of the Unit 2 - IMAGE ENHANCEMENT in the subject of Image Processing

* Smoothing filters are used to reduce the amount of noise in an image by blurring it. This is done by reducing the magnitude of the high frequency components of the image.
* Sharpening filters are used to increase the contrast of an image by increasing the magnitude of the high frequency components of the image.
* Frequency domain filters are applied to the Fourier transform of an image.
* The Fourier transform is a mathematical transformation which converts an image from the spatial domain to the frequency domain.
* The frequency domain representation of an image is composed of sine and cosine waves which represent the high and low frequency components of the image.
* Smoothing filters are typically Gaussian or Butterworth filters.
* Sharpening filters are typically Laplacian or High-boost filters.
* The parameters of a frequency domain filter (e.g. cutoff frequency, sharpness, etc.) can be adjusted to achieve the desired effect.
