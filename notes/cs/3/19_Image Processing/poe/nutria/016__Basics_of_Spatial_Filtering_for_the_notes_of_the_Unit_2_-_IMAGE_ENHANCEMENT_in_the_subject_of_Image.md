
### Basics of Spatial Filtering

Spatial filtering is a type of image processing technique used to enhance or modify an image. It is based on the idea of applying a mathematical operation to each pixel in an image, resulting in a modified image with improved features.

Spatial filtering can be used to:

- Sharpen or blur an image
- Increase or decrease contrast
- Reduce noise
- Remove small objects from an image
- Detect edges and lines

Spatial filters are typically applied to an image using a convolution matrix, which is a small matrix of numbers. This matrix is then multiplied by each pixel in the image, resulting in a modified image. The matrix is usually designed to enhance certain features in the image, such as edges or lines.

Spatial filtering is used in many areas of image processing, such as medical imaging, facial recognition, and satellite imagery. It is also used to improve the quality of digital photographs.
