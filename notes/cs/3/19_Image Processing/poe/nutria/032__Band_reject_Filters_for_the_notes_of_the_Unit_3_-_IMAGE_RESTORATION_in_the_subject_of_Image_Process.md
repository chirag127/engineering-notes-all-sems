
### Band Reject Filters

Band reject filters are used to reduce the frequency components of an image in a given range of frequencies. They are also known as band-stop or notch filters.

* Band reject filters are designed to reject frequencies within a certain range while allowing other frequencies to pass through the filter.
* Band reject filters are used to reduce the noise in an image, specifically in regions where the noise is concentrated in a certain frequency range.
* Band reject filters are useful for removing periodic noise, such as moiré patterns, from an image.
* Band reject filters can be used to reduce the contrast of an image in a certain frequency range, while preserving the detail in other frequency ranges.
* Band reject filters can be used to reduce the visibility of certain features in an image, such as edges or textures.
* Band reject filters can be used to reduce the visibility of certain objects in an image, such as text or logos.
