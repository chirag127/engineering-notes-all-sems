
### Image Sensing and Acquisition

Image sensing and acquisition is the process of capturing and recording digital images. This process involves the use of specialized hardware, such as cameras and scanners, to capture and store images in digital form.

The following are some key concepts related to image sensing and acquisition:

- Image sensors: Image sensors are a type of electronic device that captures and records digital images. The most common type of image sensor is a charge-coupled device (CCD) or complementary metal-oxide semiconductor (CMOS).

- Resolution: Resolution refers to the amount of detail that can be captured in an image. Higher resolution images contain more detail and are of better quality than lower resolution images.

- Exposure: Exposure is the amount of light that is allowed to enter the camera when taking a photograph. Exposure is measured in terms of shutter speed, aperture, and ISO.

- Color depth: Color depth is the number of bits used to represent a single pixel in an image. Higher color depths result in more accurate and detailed images.

- Image formats: Image formats are the types of files used to store digital images. Common image formats include JPEG, GIF, PNG, and TIFF.
