
### Inverse Filtering for the Notes of the Unit 3 - IMAGE RESTORATION in the Subject of Image Processing

1. Inverse filtering is a method of restoring a degraded image by applying a filter to the Fourier transform of the image.
2. The filter used in inverse filtering is the inverse of the filter that is used to degrade the image.
3. The inverse filter must be applied to the Fourier transform of the degraded image in order to restore it to its original form.
4. Inverse filtering is an effective method of image restoration when the degradation process is known.
5. However, it is prone to noise amplification and can result in artifacts in the restored image if the degradation process is not accurately known.
6. Inverse filtering can be used to restore images that have been degraded by motion blur, additive noise, and other types of distortion.
7. Inverse filtering is also used in medical imaging to reduce noise and improve the quality of images.
