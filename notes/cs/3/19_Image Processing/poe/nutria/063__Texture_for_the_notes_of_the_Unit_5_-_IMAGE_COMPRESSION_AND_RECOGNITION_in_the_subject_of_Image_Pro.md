
### Texture

Texture is a visual element of an image that can be used to identify objects and distinguish them from their surroundings. In image processing, texture can be used to improve the quality of an image, enhance its features, and even recognize objects.

- Texture is a measure of the variation in intensity of an image.
- It can be used to identify objects, distinguish them from their surroundings, and improve the quality of an image.
- Texture can be used to recognize objects and distinguish them from their surroundings.
- Texture can be used to enhance features in an image, such as edges, lines, and shapes.
- Texture can be used to compress an image, by removing redundant information from the image.
- Texture can be used to detect motion in an image.
- Texture can be used to recognize objects in an image.
- Texture can be used to identify objects in an image and distinguish them from their surroundings.
