
### RGB, HSI models for the notes of the Unit 1 - DIGITAL IMAGE FUNDAMENTALS in the subject of Image Processing

* RGB (Red, Green, Blue) is a color model used in digital imaging that uses three primary colors to represent a range of colors.
* HSI (Hue, Saturation, Intensity) is an alternative color model that uses three components to represent a range of colors. The hue component is based on the wavelength of light, while the saturation and intensity components are based on the amount of light.
* Both RGB and HSI models are used in digital image processing to represent and manipulate images.
* RGB is the most commonly used color model in digital imaging, as it is the most intuitive and easy to understand.
* HSI is often used for more advanced image processing tasks, such as color correction and color segmentation.
* Both models can be used to represent a variety of color spaces, such as sRGB, Adobe RGB, and CMYK.
