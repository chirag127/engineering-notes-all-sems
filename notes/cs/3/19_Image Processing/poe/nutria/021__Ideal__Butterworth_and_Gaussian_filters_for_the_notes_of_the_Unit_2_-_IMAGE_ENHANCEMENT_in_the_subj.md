
### Ideal, Butterworth and Gaussian Filters

Ideal, Butterworth and Gaussian filters are used in image enhancement to produce a desired effect on an image.

* Ideal filters: Ideal filters are used to reduce noise and sharpening of an image. They are used to enhance the edges of an image by sharpening the edges and reducing the noise.

* Butterworth filters: Butterworth filters are used to reduce the noise and enhance the edges of an image. They are used to reduce the noise and sharpen the edges of an image.

* Gaussian filters: Gaussian filters are used to reduce noise and blur an image. They are used to reduce the noise and blur the edges of an image.
