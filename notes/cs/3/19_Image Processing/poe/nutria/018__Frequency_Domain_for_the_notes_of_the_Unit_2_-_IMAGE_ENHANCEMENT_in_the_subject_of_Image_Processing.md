
### Frequency Domain for the Notes of Unit 2 - Image Enhancement in Image Processing

1. Frequency domain is a way of representing an image as a set of frequencies, rather than its individual pixels. 
2. It is used to analyze the frequency content of an image, which can be used to enhance or modify the image.
3. In the frequency domain, an image can be represented as a set of sine and cosine waves of different frequencies.
4. The frequency domain representation of an image can be obtained by applying a Fourier transform to the image.
5. The Fourier transform decomposes an image into its constituent frequencies, which can be used to identify features in the image.
6. Image enhancement techniques in the frequency domain include sharpening, noise reduction, and contrast enhancement.
7. Sharpening is used to increase the contrast between edges in an image, while noise reduction is used to reduce the amount of noise present in an image.
8. Contrast enhancement is used to increase the overall contrast of an image.
9. Frequency domain image enhancement techniques are usually more computationally efficient than their spatial domain counterparts.
10. However, they are also more sensitive to noise and can introduce artifacts into the image if not used carefully.
