
### Mean Filters for Unit 3 - Image Restoration 

* Mean filters are a type of linear filter used in image processing which take the average of the neighbouring pixels to reduce noise.
* They are used to reduce the noise in an image by blurring it and smoothing out the edges.
* Mean filters are typically applied to greyscale images, but can also be applied to colour images.
* The size of the filter is important, as a larger filter will result in more blurring.
* A mean filter can also be used to sharpen an image by subtracting the blurred image from the original image.
* Mean filters can be used to detect edges in an image by subtracting the blurred image from the original image. This will result in the edges being enhanced.
