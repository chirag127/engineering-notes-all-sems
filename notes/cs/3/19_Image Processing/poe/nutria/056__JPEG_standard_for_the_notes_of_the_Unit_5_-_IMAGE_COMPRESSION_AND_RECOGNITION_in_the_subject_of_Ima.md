
### JPEG Standard for Unit 5 - Image Compression and Recognition

* JPEG (Joint Photographic Experts Group) is a standard for image compression and recognition used in digital imaging.
* JPEG is a lossy compression technique that reduces the amount of data required to represent an image.
* JPEG uses a quantization process to reduce the number of colors in an image. This process is known as color subsampling.
* JPEG also uses a variety of techniques to reduce the amount of data needed to represent an image, such as Discrete Cosine Transform (DCT) and Huffman coding.
* JPEG is widely used in digital cameras, web applications, and image editing software.
* JPEG images are typically saved in the .jpg or .jpeg file format.
