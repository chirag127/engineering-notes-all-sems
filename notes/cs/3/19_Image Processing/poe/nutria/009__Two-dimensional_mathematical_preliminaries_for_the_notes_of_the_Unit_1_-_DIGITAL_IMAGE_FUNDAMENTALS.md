
### Two-dimensional mathematical preliminaries for the notes of the Unit 1 - DIGITAL IMAGE FUNDAMENTALS in the subject of Image Processing

1. Two-dimensional (2D) images are represented using a discrete set of pixels arranged in a rectangular grid. 
2. The intensity of each pixel is usually represented by a numerical value, which is often referred to as the gray level of the pixel.
3. The gray levels of the pixels can be used to represent the intensity of the image.
4. In order to represent a color image, three channels are used: red, green, and blue (RGB). 
5. The color of each pixel is determined by combining the values of the three channels.
6. The resolution of an image is determined by the number of pixels in the image.
7. The size of an image is determined by the dimensions of the rectangular grid.
8. The brightness of an image is determined by the average gray level of the pixels in the image.
9. The contrast of an image is determined by the range of gray levels in the image.
10. The sharpness of an image is determined by the amount of detail in the image.
