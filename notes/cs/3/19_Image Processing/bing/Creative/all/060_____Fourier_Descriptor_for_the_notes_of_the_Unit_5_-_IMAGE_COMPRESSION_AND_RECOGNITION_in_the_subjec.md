# Fourier Descriptor

- Fourier descriptor is a method used in object recognition and image processing to represent the boundary shape of a segment in an image.
- Fourier descriptor is based on the Fourier series, which is a mathematical tool to decompose a periodic function into a sum of simple sinusoidal functions.
- Fourier descriptor can be derived from the Fourier transform of the boundary coordinates of the image segment, which are represented as complex numbers.
- Fourier descriptor can be made invariant to translation, scale, rotation and starting point by applying some normalization techniques.
- Fourier descriptor can capture the essential information about the contour of the image segment and discard the noise and details.
- Fourier descriptor can be used for shape-based image retrieval, shape complexity analysis, shape classification and shape matching .
