### Steps in Digital Image Processing

Digital image processing is the process of manipulating digital images using computer algorithms. It can be used for various purposes, such as enhancing, restoring, analyzing, compressing, and transmitting images. The basic steps involved in digital image processing are  :

- **Image acquisition**: This involves capturing an image using a digital camera or scanner, or importing an existing image into a computer. The image is then converted into a digital form, using analog-to-digital converters, if necessary.
- **Image enhancement**: This involves improving the visual quality of an image, such as increasing contrast, reducing noise, and removing artifacts. The goal is to make the image more suitable for a specific application or task.
- **Image restoration**: This involves removing degradation from an image, such as blurring, noise, and distortion. The goal is to restore the image to its original or ideal state, using prior knowledge or models of the degradation process.
- **Image segmentation**: This involves dividing an image into meaningful regions or objects, based on some criteria, such as color, intensity, texture, or shape. The goal is to simplify or change the representation of an image for further analysis or processing.
- **Image representation and description**: This involves encoding the segmented regions or objects into a compact and meaningful form, using features, descriptors, or models. The goal is to reduce the complexity and dimensionality of the image data for efficient storage, retrieval, or recognition.
- **Image recognition**: This involves identifying or classifying the regions or objects in an image, based on their features, descriptors, or models. The goal is to assign a label or category to each region or object, such as face, car, dog, etc.
- **Image compression**: This involves reducing the size of an image, while preserving its quality or information content. The goal is to save storage space or bandwidth for storing or transmitting images.
- **Image transmission**: This involves sending or receiving an image over a communication channel, such as a network or a wireless link. The goal is to ensure the reliability and security of the image data during transmission.
