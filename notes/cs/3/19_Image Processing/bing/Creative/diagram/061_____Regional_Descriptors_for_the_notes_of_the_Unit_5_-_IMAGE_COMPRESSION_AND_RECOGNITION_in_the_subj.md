### Regional Descriptors

- Regional descriptors are numerical or symbolic features that describe the properties of a segmented region in an image .
- Regional descriptors can be classified into two categories: external and internal .
- External descriptors are based on the shape and boundary of the region, such as perimeter, compactness, orientation, eccentricity, etc  .
- Internal descriptors are based on the reflectivity and texture of the region, such as mean, variance, entropy, histogram, etc  .
- Regional descriptors can be used for various purposes, such as object recognition, classification, matching, retrieval, etc  .
- Regional descriptors can be computed using different methods, such as moments, Fourier descriptors, chain codes, etc  .
