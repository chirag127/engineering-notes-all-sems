### Components for the notes of Unit 1 - DIGITAL IMAGE FUNDAMENTALS

- **Definition and examples of digital images**: A digital image is a representation of a two-dimensional scene using a finite set of discrete values, called pixels or picture elements. Examples of digital images are photographs, maps, charts, diagrams, etc.
- **Elements of digital image processing systems**: A digital image processing system consists of four main components: an image acquisition device, such as a camera or a scanner, an image processing and analysis unit, such as a computer or a software, an image storage and display device, such as a monitor or a printer, and an image communication device, such as a modem or a network.
- **Image sampling and quantization**: Image sampling is the process of converting a continuous image into a discrete image by dividing it into a grid of pixels. Image quantization is the process of assigning a finite number of values to each pixel, usually based on its brightness or color. The sampling rate and the quantization level determine the spatial and intensity resolution of the digital image, respectively.
- **Image enhancement techniques**: Image enhancement is the process of improving the appearance or quality of an image by modifying its pixel values. Some common image enhancement techniques are contrast stretching, histogram equalization, noise filtering, sharpening, smoothing, etc.
- **Image restoration methods**: Image restoration is the process of recovering an original image from a degraded image by removing or reducing the effects of noise, blur, distortion, etc. Some common image restoration methods are inverse filtering, Wiener filtering, deconvolution, etc.
- **Image compression methods**: Image compression is the process of reducing the amount of data required to represent an image by exploiting its redundancy or irrelevancy. Some common image compression methods are lossless compression, such as run-length encoding, Huffman coding, etc., and lossy compression, such as transform coding, vector quantization, etc.
- **Image segmentation techniques**: Image segmentation is the process of dividing an image into meaningful regions or objects based on some criteria, such as pixel intensity, color, texture, shape, etc. Some common image segmentation techniques are thresholding, edge detection, region growing, region splitting and merging, etc.
- **Image representation and description methods**: Image representation is the process of selecting a suitable data structure to store the segmented image regions or objects, such as boundary, chain code, polygon, etc. Image description is the process of extracting features or attributes from the image regions or objects, such as area, perimeter, centroid, orientation, etc.
- **Image recognition and classification methods**: Image recognition is the process of identifying or labeling the image regions or objects based on their features or attributes, such as face recognition, character recognition, etc. Image classification is the process of assigning the image regions or objects to predefined classes or categories, such as medical diagnosis, land use, etc.
