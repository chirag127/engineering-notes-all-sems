### Boundary description for the notes of the Unit 5 - IMAGE COMPRESSION AND RECOGNITION in the subject of Image Processing

- Image compression is the process of reducing the amount of data required to represent an image, while preserving its quality and information content.
- Image recognition is the process of identifying and classifying objects, faces, scenes, or activities in an image, using machine learning algorithms and models.
- The notes of Unit 5 cover the following topics:

  - The need and benefits of image compression, such as saving storage space, bandwidth, and transmission time.
  - The types and characteristics of image compression, such as lossless and lossy compression, entropy coding, predictive coding, transform coding, and vector quantization.
  - The standards and formats of image compression, such as JPEG, JPEG 2000, GIF, PNG, and TIFF.
  - The principles and applications of image recognition, such as feature extraction, feature matching, classification, and recognition.
  - The methods and techniques of image recognition, such as template matching, correlation, edge detection, corner detection, histogram of oriented gradients, scale-invariant feature transform, convolutional neural networks, and deep learning.
  - The challenges and limitations of image recognition, such as occlusion, illumination, pose, scale, rotation, and noise.
