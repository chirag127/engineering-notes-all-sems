### Steps in Digital Image Processing

- **Image acquisition**: This is the first step of digital image processing, where an image is captured by a sensor (such as a camera) and converted into a digital form.
- **Image enhancement**: This is the second step of digital image processing, where the image is processed to improve its appearance or quality for a specific application or purpose.
- **Image restoration**: This is the third step of digital image processing, where the image is processed to remove or reduce the effects of noise, blur, distortion, or other degradation factors that may have occurred during image acquisition or transmission.
- **Image compression**: This is the fourth step of digital image processing, where the image is reduced in size or complexity to save storage space or bandwidth, or to facilitate faster processing or transmission.
- **Image segmentation**: This is the fifth step of digital image processing, where the image is divided into regions or segments that have some common characteristics or features, such as color, texture, shape, or intensity.
- **Image representation and description**: This is the sixth step of digital image processing, where the image segments are represented and described by suitable features or descriptors that can be used for further analysis or recognition.
- **Image recognition and interpretation**: This is the seventh and final step of digital image processing, where the image features or descriptors are used to identify or classify the objects or scenes in the image, or to infer some meaning or information from the image.
