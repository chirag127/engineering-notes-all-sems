## Unit 3 - IMAGE RESTORATION

Image restoration is the process of removing or reducing image degradation caused by various factors, such as noise, blur, and degradation due to imaging system. The following are some of the key concepts related to image restoration:

1. Degradation Model: The first step in image restoration is to model the degradation process, which can be caused by various factors such as noise, blur, and degradation due to imaging system. The degradation model is used to determine the best restoration technique to use.

2. Noise Reduction: Image restoration can be used to reduce noise in an image, which can be caused by various factors such as electronic noise, quantization error, and more. Noise reduction techniques can be used to remove or reduce the impact of noise on the image quality.

3. Blur Correction: Image restoration can be used to correct for blur in an image, which can be caused by various factors such as camera shake, lens aberrations, and more. Blur correction techniques can be used to restore the sharpness of the image.

4. Inverse Filtering: Image restoration can be used to perform inverse filtering, which is a process of restoring the original image by reversing the degradation process. Inverse filtering techniques can be used to restore the image quality by removing or reducing the impact of degradation.

5. Constrained Least Squares: Image restoration can be used to perform constrained least squares, which is a process of restoring the image by minimizing the difference between the original image and the degraded image subject to constraints. Constrained least squares techniques can be used to restore the image quality by removing or reducing the impact of degradation.

6. Regularization: Image restoration can be used to perform regularization, which is a process of restoring the image by adding a regularization term to the objective function. Regularization techniques can be used to restore the image quality by removing or reducing the impact of degradation while preserving the details of the image.

In this unit, you will learn about image restoration, including degradation model, noise reduction, blur correction, inverse filtering, constrained least squares, and regularization.

This unit will provide a foundation for understanding the principles and practices of image restoration, and for exploring the various concepts and techniques used in the field of image processing.
