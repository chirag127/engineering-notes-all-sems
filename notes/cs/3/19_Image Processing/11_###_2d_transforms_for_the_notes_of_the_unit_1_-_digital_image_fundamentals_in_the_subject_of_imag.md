### 2D transforms for the notes of the Unit 1 - DIGITAL IMAGE FUNDAMENTALS in the subject of Image Processing

The 2D Transforms section of the Unit 1 - Digital Image Fundamentals in the subject of Image Processing should cover the following topics:

1. Definition of 2D Transforms (mathematical operations used to manipulate images)
2. Overview of 2D Transforms (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
3. Types of 2D Transforms (e.g. Translation, Rotation, Scaling, Shearing)
4. Overview of Types of 2D Transforms (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
5. Transform Matrices (e.g. matrices used to represent 2D transformations)
6. Overview of Transform Matrices (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
7. Transform Pairs (e.g. pairs of transforms used to achieve a desired transformation)
8. Overview of Transform Pairs (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
9. Image Warping (e.g. the process of transforming an image to a different shape)
10. Overview of Image Warping (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
11. Image Registration (e.g. the process of aligning two or more images)
12. Overview of Image Registration (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
13. 2D Transforms in industry applications (how 2D Transforms are used in various industries)
14. Further reading and resources (books, articles, online resources)

2D Transforms are an important component of the subject of Image Processing, especially in the context of Digital Image Fundamentals. Understanding the principles and techniques of 2D Transforms helps students to develop the skills required to manipulate images effectively. The hands-on practice and further reading resources will help students to develop their skills and deepen their understanding of the material.
