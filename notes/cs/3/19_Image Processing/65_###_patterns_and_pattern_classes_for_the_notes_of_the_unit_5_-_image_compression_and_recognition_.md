### Patterns and Pattern classes for the notes of the Unit 5 - IMAGE COMPRESSION AND RECOGNITION in the subject of Image Processing

The Patterns and Pattern Classes section of the Unit 5 - IMAGE COMPRESSION AND RECOGNITION in the subject of Image Processing should cover the following topics:

1. Definition of Patterns (repeated or recurring shapes, structures, or sequences in images)
2. Overview of Patterns (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
3. Definition of Pattern Classes (categories of patterns based on their properties and characteristics)
4. Overview of Pattern Classes (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
5. Image Pattern Recognition (e.g. the process of identifying patterns in images)
6. Overview of Image Pattern Recognition (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
7. Image Pattern Recognition Algorithms (e.g. algorithms used to implement image pattern recognition)
8. Overview of Image Pattern Recognition Algorithms (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
9. Image Pattern Recognition Performance (e.g. how well image pattern recognition algorithms perform in recognizing patterns in images)
10. Overview of Image Pattern Recognition Performance (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
11. Image Pattern Recognition Applications (e.g. object recognition, image classification, etc.)
12. Overview of Image Pattern Recognition Applications (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
13. Image Pattern Recognition in Industry Applications (how image pattern recognition is used in various industries)
14. Further reading and resources (books, articles, online resources)

Patterns and Pattern Classes are important components of the subject of Image Processing, especially in the context of Image Compression and Recognition. Understanding the principles and techniques of patterns and pattern classes helps students to develop the skills required to design and implement effective image pattern recognition solutions. The hands-on practice and further reading resources will help students to develop their skills and deepen their understanding of the material.
