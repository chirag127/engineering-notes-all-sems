### Notch Filters for the notes of the Unit 3 - IMAGE RESTORATION in the subject of Image Processing

The Notch Filters section of the Unit 3 - IMAGE RESTORATION in the subject of Image Processing should cover the following topics:

1. Definition of Notch Filters (filters used to remove specific frequencies from a signal)
2. Overview of Notch Filters (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
3. Notch Filter Design (e.g. the process of designing a notch filter to remove specific frequencies from a signal)
4. Overview of Notch Filter Design (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
5. Notch Filter Types (e.g. Infinite Impulse Response (IIR) filters, Finite Impulse Response (FIR) filters)
6. Overview of Notch Filter Types (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
7. Notch Filter Applications (e.g. image denoising, image deblurring, image restoration)
8. Overview of Notch Filter Applications (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
9. Notch Filters in Industry Applications (how notch filters are used in various industries)
10. Further reading and resources (books, articles, online resources)

Notch Filters are an important component of the subject of Image Processing, especially in the context of Image Restoration. Understanding the principles and techniques of notch filters helps students to develop the skills required to design and implement effective image restoration solutions. The hands-on practice and further reading resources will help students to develop their skills and deepen their understanding of the material.
