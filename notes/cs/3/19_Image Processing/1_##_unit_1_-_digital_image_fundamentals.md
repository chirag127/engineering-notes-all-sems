## Unit 1 - DIGITAL IMAGE FUNDAMENTALS

Unit 1 - Digital Image Fundamentals in the subject of Image Processing will cover the basics of digital images and how they can be processed and analyzed.

A digital image is a representation of an image in a digital form, typically as a matrix of pixels. Digital images can be captured using digital cameras, scanned from photographs or other images, or generated using computer graphics software.

In the context of Unit 1 - Digital Image Fundamentals in the subject of Image Processing, students will learn about the basics of digital images, including the structure and representation of digital images, and the different types of digital images, such as grayscale and color images. This may include working with examples of digital images, analyzing the structure and representation of digital images, and learning how to process and analyze digital images.

In Unit 1 - Digital Image Fundamentals in the subject of Image Processing, students will also learn about the different components of digital images, including pixels, color channels, and image resolution, and will learn how to manipulate and analyze digital images using image processing algorithms and techniques.

In summary, in Unit 1 - Digital Image Fundamentals in the subject of Image Processing, students will learn about the basics of digital images and how they can be processed and analyzed. They will learn about the structure and representation of digital images, the different types of digital images, and the different components of digital images, and will learn how to manipulate and analyze digital images using image processing algorithms and techniques.
