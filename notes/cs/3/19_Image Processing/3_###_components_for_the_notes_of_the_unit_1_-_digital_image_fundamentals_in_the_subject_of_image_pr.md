### Components for the notes of the Unit 1 - DIGITAL IMAGE FUNDAMENTALS in the subject of Image Processing

Unit 1 - DIGITAL IMAGE FUNDAMENTALS in the subject of Image Processing will cover the various components of digital images and their role in image processing.

The components of digital images include pixels, color channels, and image resolution. Pixels are the smallest unit of a digital image and are used to represent the colors and intensity of the image. Color channels are used to represent the colors in an image and can be red, green, and blue (RGB) or hue, saturation, and value (HSV). Image resolution refers to the number of pixels in an image and is used to determine the quality and detail of the image.

In the context of Unit 1 - DIGITAL IMAGE FUNDAMENTALS in the subject of Image Processing, students will learn about the various components of digital images, including pixels, color channels, and image resolution. This may include working with examples of digital images, analyzing the components of digital images, and learning how to use the components of digital images for image processing.

In Unit 1 - DIGITAL IMAGE FUNDAMENTALS in the subject of Image Processing, students will also learn about the basics of image processing, including the types of image processing techniques, the goals of image processing, and the tools and technologies used for image processing.

In summary, in Unit 1 - DIGITAL IMAGE FUNDAMENTALS in the subject of Image Processing, students will learn about the various components of digital images, including pixels, color channels, and image resolution, and will learn about the basics of image processing, including the types of image processing techniques, the goals of image processing, and the tools and technologies used for image processing.
