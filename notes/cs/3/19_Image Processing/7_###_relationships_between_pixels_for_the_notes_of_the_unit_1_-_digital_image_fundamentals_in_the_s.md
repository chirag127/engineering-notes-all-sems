### Relationships between pixels for the notes of the Unit 1 - DIGITAL IMAGE FUNDAMENTALS in the subject of Image Processing

Relationships between Pixels:
- Pixels are the smallest unit of a digital image.
- Relationships between pixels can be used to process and analyze images.
- Common relationships between pixels include spatial relationships, such as adjacency and distance, and intensity relationships, such as brightness and color.
- These relationships can be used to perform tasks such as edge detection, segmentation, and feature extraction in image processing.
