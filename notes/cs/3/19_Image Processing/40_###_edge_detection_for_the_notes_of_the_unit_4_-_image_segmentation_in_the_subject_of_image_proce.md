### Edge detection for the notes of the Unit 4 - IMAGE SEGMENTATION in the subject of Image Processing

Unit 4 - IMAGE SEGMENTATION in the subject of Image Processing will cover the concept of Edge Detection, a fundamental technique used for image analysis.

Edge Detection refers to the process of detecting the boundaries between different objects or regions in an image. Edge Detection is a crucial step in image analysis, as it allows for the extraction of meaningful information from the image, such as object boundaries and shapes.

In the context of Unit 4 - IMAGE SEGMENTATION in the subject of Image Processing, students will learn about Edge Detection, including the different types of Edge Detection techniques, such as gradient-based Edge Detection and Laplacian of Gaussian (LoG) Edge Detection, and how to use Edge Detection for image analysis.
