### spaces, tabs and new lines.

Spaces, tabs and new lines are whitespace characters used in text formatting.

Spaces: A space is a blank character used to separate words or characters in a text string.

Tabs: A tab is a character that advances the cursor to the next tab stop. It is used to align text in columns.

New lines: A new line is a character that advances the cursor to the next line. It is used to separate paragraphs or lines of text.

These characters are used to create structured and readable text in computer programming and text editing.
