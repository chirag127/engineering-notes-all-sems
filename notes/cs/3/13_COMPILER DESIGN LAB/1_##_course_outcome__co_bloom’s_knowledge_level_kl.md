## Course Outcome ( CO) Bloom’s Knowledge Level (KL)

CO is a statement that defines what a student should know or be able to do after completing a course. Bloom's KL is a taxonomy that classifies learning objectives into six categories, ranging from remembering information to evaluating and creating. The six categories are: Remembering, Understanding, Applying, Analyzing, Evaluating, and Creating. COs are often written at the higher levels of Bloom's KL to indicate that students will be expected to apply, analyze, evaluate, and create, rather than just remember or understand information.
