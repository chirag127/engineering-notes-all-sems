### At the end of course , the student will be able to:

- Understand the key concepts and theories in the course material
- Analyze and apply the knowledge and skills acquired to real-world situations
- Communicate effectively about the course material
- Demonstrate critical thinking and problem-solving abilities related to the course content
- Evaluate and synthesize information from various sources 
- Collaborate with others in group projects or discussions 
- Reflect on personal learning and growth in the subject matter.
