## DETAILED SYLLABUS

A detailed syllabus is a comprehensive outline of the topics, course objectives, and expectations for a class or program. It typically includes:

- Course description and objectives 
- Schedule of topics and assignments 
- Grading policy 
- Textbook and materials 
- Attendance and participation requirements 
- Evaluation criteria 
- Any special requirements or policies 

The detailed syllabus serves as a contract between the instructor and students, providing a clear understanding of what will be covered and expected in the course.
