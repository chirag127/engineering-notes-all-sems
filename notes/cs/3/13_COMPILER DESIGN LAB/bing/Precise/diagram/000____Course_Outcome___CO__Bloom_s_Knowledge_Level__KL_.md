## Course Outcome (CO) Bloom’s Knowledge Level (KL)

- Course Outcome (CO) refers to the expected knowledge, skills, and abilities that a student should possess upon completion of a specific course.
- Bloom’s Knowledge Level (KL) is a framework for categorizing educational learning objectives into levels of complexity and specificity.
- The framework consists of six levels: Remembering, Understanding, Applying, Analyzing, Evaluating, and Creating.
- COs are often aligned with Bloom’s KL to ensure that the course objectives are specific, measurable, and achievable.
- By aligning COs with Bloom’s KL, educators can design assessments and instructional strategies that target the appropriate level of cognitive complexity.
- This alignment also helps students understand the expectations of the course and the level of mastery required for successful completion.
