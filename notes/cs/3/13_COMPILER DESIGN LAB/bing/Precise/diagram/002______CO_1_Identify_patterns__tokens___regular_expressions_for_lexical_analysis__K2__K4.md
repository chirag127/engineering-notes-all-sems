#### CO 1 Identify patterns, tokens & regular expressions for lexical analysis. K2, K4

Lexical analysis is the process of converting a sequence of characters into a sequence of tokens. It is the first phase of the compilation process. The main objective of lexical analysis is to identify the tokens, patterns, and regular expressions in the given input.

- **Tokens:** Tokens are the smallest units of a program that have meaning to the compiler. They are the building blocks of a program. Examples of tokens include keywords, identifiers, constants, operators, and punctuation symbols.

- **Patterns:** Patterns are the rules that define the structure of tokens. They are used to specify the format of the tokens that the lexical analyzer should recognize. For example, the pattern for an identifier may specify that it must start with a letter and can be followed by any number of letters, digits, or underscores.

- **Regular Expressions:** Regular expressions are a powerful tool for describing patterns. They are used to specify the patterns that the lexical analyzer should recognize. A regular expression is a sequence of characters that defines a search pattern. These patterns are used to match character combinations in strings.

In summary, lexical analysis involves identifying the tokens, patterns, and regular expressions in the given input. These elements are essential for the compilation process and play a crucial role in the overall functioning of the compiler.
