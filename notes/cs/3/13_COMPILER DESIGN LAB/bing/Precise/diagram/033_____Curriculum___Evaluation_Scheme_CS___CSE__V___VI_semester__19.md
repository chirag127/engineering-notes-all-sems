### Curriculum & Evaluation Scheme CS & CSE (V & VI semester) 19

The curriculum and evaluation scheme for Computer Science (CS) and Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) for the fifth and sixth semesters is designed to provide students with a comprehensive understanding of the core concepts and practical skills in the field.

1. The curriculum includes a range of subjects, including advanced programming, data structures and algorithms, computer architecture, operating systems, and database management systems.
2. The evaluation scheme is designed to assess students' understanding of the theoretical concepts and their ability to apply them in practical situations.
3. The scheme includes a combination of written exams, practical exams, and project work.
4. The written exams test students' understanding of the theoretical concepts, while the practical exams and project work assess their ability to apply these concepts in real-world scenarios.
5. The curriculum and evaluation scheme are regularly updated to keep pace with the latest developments in the field of computer science and engineering.
