### Electronic mail for the notes of the Unit 7 - Application Layer in the subject of Computer Networks

Electronic mail (email) is a widely used application in computer networks that enables users to send and receive messages. The following are some of the key concepts related to email:

1. Email Clients: Email clients are software applications used to send and receive email, such as Microsoft Outlook and Gmail.

2. Email Servers: Email servers are computer systems that store and manage email messages, and are responsible for delivering email to the recipient's email client.

3. Simple Mail Transfer Protocol (SMTP): SMTP is the protocol used by email clients to send email messages to email servers, and by email servers to deliver email messages to other email servers.

4. Post Office Protocol (POP): POP is a protocol used by email clients to retrieve email messages from email servers.

5. Internet Message Access Protocol (IMAP): IMAP is a protocol used by email clients to retrieve email messages from email servers, and to manage email messages on the server.

6. Email Attachments: Email attachments are files that are sent along with email messages, such as documents and images.

In this unit, you will learn about email in computer networks, including email clients, email servers, Simple Mail Transfer Protocol (SMTP), Post Office Protocol (POP), Internet Message Access Protocol (IMAP), and email attachments.

This unit will provide a foundation for understanding the principles and practices of email and for exploring the various concepts and techniques used in the field of computer networks.
