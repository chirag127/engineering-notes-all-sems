### forwarding and delivery for the notes of the Unit 5 - Network Layer in the subject of Computer Networks

Unit 5 - Network Layer in the subject of Computer Networks will cover the concepts of Forwarding and Delivery, two important processes in the design and implementation of computer networks.

The Network Layer is responsible for forwarding and delivering data between devices on a network. Forwarding refers to the process of routing data from one device to another, while delivery refers to the process of ensuring that data is delivered to its intended destination.

In the context of Unit 5 - Network Layer in the subject of Computer Networks, students will learn about Forwarding and Delivery, including how to design and implement computer networks that use these processes. This may include working with examples of computer networks that use Forwarding and Delivery, analyzing the syntax and structure of computer networks, and learning how to use Forwarding and Delivery to optimize the performance of computer networks.

In Unit 5 - Network Layer in the subject of Computer Networks, students will also learn about the benefits and limitations of using Forwarding and Delivery, and will learn about alternative approaches to network design and implementation, such as using different routing algorithms.
