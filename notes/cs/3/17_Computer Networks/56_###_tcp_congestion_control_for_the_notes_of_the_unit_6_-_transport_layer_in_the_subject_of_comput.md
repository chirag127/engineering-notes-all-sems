### TCP Congestion control for the notes of the Unit 6 - Transport Layer in the subject of Computer Networks

The TCP Congestion Control section of the Unit 6 - Transport Layer in the subject of Computer Networks should cover the following topics:

1. Definition of TCP Congestion Control (a mechanism used to regulate data flow in a network)
2. Overview of TCP Congestion Control (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
3. TCP Congestion Control Algorithms (e.g. Additive Increase Multiplicative Decrease (AIMD), etc.)
4. Overview of TCP Congestion Control Algorithms (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
5. AIMD (Additive Increase Multiplicative Decrease) Algorithm (e.g. how it regulates data flow in a network)
6. Overview of AIMD Algorithm (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
7. TCP Congestion Control and Network Performance (e.g. optimizing TCP congestion control for better network performance)
8. Overview of TCP Congestion Control and Network Performance (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
9. TCP Congestion Control in industry applications (how TCP congestion control is used in various industries)
10. Further reading and resources (books, articles, online resources)

TCP Congestion Control is an important component of the subject of Computer Networks. Understanding the principles and techniques of TCP congestion control helps students to develop the skills required to design and implement effective computer networks. The hands-on practice and further reading resources will help students to develop their skills and deepen their understanding of the material.
