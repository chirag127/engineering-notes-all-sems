### Goals and applications of networks

#### Unit 1 - Introductory Concepts in the subject of Computer Networks

1. **Resource Sharing**: One of the primary goals of computer networks is to enable the sharing of resources among different devices. This can include sharing of hardware resources such as printers or storage devices, as well as software resources such as files or applications.

2. **Communication**: Networks facilitate communication between individuals and organizations. This can include sending and receiving emails, instant messaging, video conferencing, and more.

3. **Collaboration**: Networks enable collaboration between individuals and teams, allowing them to work together on projects and share information in real-time.

4. **Data Management**: Networks can be used to manage and organize data, making it easier to access, store, and retrieve information.

5. **Distributed Processing**: Networks can be used to distribute processing tasks among multiple devices, allowing for more efficient use of resources and faster processing times.

6. **Remote Access**: Networks enable remote access to resources, allowing individuals to access information and applications from any location.

7. **Scalability**: Networks can be designed to be scalable, allowing for the addition of new devices and resources as needed.

These are some of the primary goals and applications of computer networks. They play a crucial role in enabling communication, collaboration, and resource sharing among individuals and organizations.
