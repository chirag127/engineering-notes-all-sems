### Network devices and components

Unit 1 - Introductory Concepts in the subject of Computer Networks

1. **Network Interface Card (NIC)**: A hardware component that connects a computer to a network.
2. **Hub**: A device that connects multiple devices on a network and forwards data packets to all connected devices.
3. **Switch**: A device that connects multiple devices on a network and forwards data packets to the intended recipient.
4. **Router**: A device that connects multiple networks and forwards data packets between them.
5. **Modem**: A device that modulates and demodulates signals to enable data transmission over a communication line.
6. **Firewall**: A network security system that monitors and controls incoming and outgoing network traffic based on predetermined security rules.
7. **Wireless Access Point (WAP)**: A device that allows wireless devices to connect to a wired network.
8. **Repeater**: A device that amplifies and retransmits a signal to extend the range of a network.
9. **Bridge**: A device that connects two networks and forwards data packets between them.
10. **Gateway**: A device that connects two networks using different protocols and translates data packets between them.
