## Unit 2 - Physical Layer

The physical layer is the first layer of the OSI model. It is responsible for the transmission and reception of raw data between a device and a physical transmission medium. The physical layer is responsible for the following tasks:

1. **Data encoding:** The physical layer encodes data into signals that can be transmitted over the physical medium.
2. **Transmission medium:** The physical layer defines the characteristics of the transmission medium, such as the type of cable, connectors, and signaling methods.
3. **Data rate:** The physical layer determines the data rate, or the speed at which data is transmitted over the physical medium.
4. **Signal generation:** The physical layer generates the signals that are transmitted over the physical medium.
5. **Synchronization:** The physical layer synchronizes the transmission and reception of data between devices.
6. **Error detection and correction:** The physical layer can detect and correct errors that occur during transmission.

The physical layer is an essential component of any network, as it provides the foundation for data transmission. It is responsible for converting digital data into signals that can be transmitted over a physical medium, and for ensuring that data is transmitted and received accurately and efficiently. The physical layer is a complex and critical part of any network, and its proper functioning is essential for the overall performance and reliability of the network.
