### Unit 3 - Link Layer: Framing

1. Framing is the process of encapsulating data into a frame or packet for transmission over a link layer.
2. The link layer is responsible for providing a reliable connection between two directly connected nodes.
3. The frame contains the data to be transmitted, as well as control information such as error detection and correction codes, and addressing information to identify the source and destination nodes.
4. The frame format and the methods used for error detection and correction vary depending on the link layer protocol used.
5. Common link layer protocols include Ethernet, Wi-Fi, and PPP.
6. The link layer may also provide flow control to prevent the sender from overwhelming the receiver with data.
7. The link layer is responsible for detecting and possibly correcting errors that may occur during transmission.
8. The link layer may also provide mechanisms for retransmitting lost or corrupted frames.
