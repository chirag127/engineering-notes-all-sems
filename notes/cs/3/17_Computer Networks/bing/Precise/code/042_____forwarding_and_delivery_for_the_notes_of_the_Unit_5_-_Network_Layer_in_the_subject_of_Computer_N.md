### Forwarding and Delivery

Forwarding and delivery are two important concepts in the network layer of the OSI model in computer networks.

1. **Forwarding**: Forwarding refers to the process of moving a packet from an incoming link to an outgoing link within a router. The router uses its forwarding table to determine the outgoing link for the packet based on its destination address.

2. **Delivery**: Delivery refers to the process of transmitting a packet from the source host to the destination host. This involves forwarding the packet through multiple routers along the path from the source to the destination.

In summary, forwarding is the process of moving a packet within a router, while delivery is the process of transmitting a packet from the source to the destination. Both forwarding and delivery are essential for the successful transmission of data in a computer network.
