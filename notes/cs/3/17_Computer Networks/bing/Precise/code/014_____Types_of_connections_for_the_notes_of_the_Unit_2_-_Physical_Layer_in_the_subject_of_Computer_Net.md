### Types of connections for the notes of the Unit 2 - Physical Layer in the subject of Computer Networks

There are two main types of connections in computer networks: **point-to-point** and **multipoint**.

1. **Point-to-point connection**: This type of connection is established between two devices or nodes. It can be either a dedicated link or a circuit-switched link. A dedicated link is a permanent connection between two nodes, while a circuit-switched link is established only when needed and is terminated when the communication is complete.

2. **Multipoint connection**: This type of connection is established between more than two devices or nodes. It can be either a shared link or a packet-switched link. A shared link is a permanent connection between multiple nodes, while a packet-switched link is established only when needed and is terminated when the communication is complete.

These are the two main types of connections in computer networks. They are used to establish communication between devices or nodes in a network. The type of connection used depends on the requirements of the network and the devices or nodes involved.
