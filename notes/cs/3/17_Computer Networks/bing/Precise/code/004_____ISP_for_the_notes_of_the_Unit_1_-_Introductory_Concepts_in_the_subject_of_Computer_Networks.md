### ISP

ISP stands for Internet Service Provider. It is a company that provides internet access to customers by providing a connection to the internet. ISPs can provide internet access through various technologies such as DSL, cable modem, fiber, wireless, or satellite.

- ISPs are responsible for providing internet access to customers and ensuring that the connection is reliable and fast.
- ISPs can offer different plans and packages to customers, depending on their needs and usage.
- ISPs can also offer additional services such as email, web hosting, and virtual private networks (VPNs).
- ISPs are regulated by government agencies to ensure that they provide fair and reliable services to customers.
- ISPs play a crucial role in the internet ecosystem by connecting users to the global network and enabling communication and information exchange.
