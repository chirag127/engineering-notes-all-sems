### Goals and applications of networks

#### Unit 1 - Introductory Concepts in the subject of Computer Networks

1. **Sharing of resources**: One of the primary goals of a computer network is to enable the sharing of resources among multiple devices. This can include sharing of hardware resources such as printers, scanners, and storage devices, as well as software resources such as applications and data.

2. **Communication**: Networks facilitate communication between devices and users. This can include sending and receiving emails, instant messaging, video conferencing, and more.

3. **Collaboration**: Networks enable collaboration among users, allowing them to work together on projects and share information in real-time.

4. **Data storage and management**: Networks provide a centralized location for storing and managing data, making it easier for users to access and share information.

5. **Remote access**: Networks allow users to remotely access resources and data, enabling them to work from anywhere with an internet connection.

6. **Scalability**: Networks can be easily scaled to accommodate growth, allowing new devices and users to be added as needed.

7. **Security**: Networks provide a means of securing data and resources, through the use of firewalls, encryption, and other security measures.

These are some of the primary goals and applications of computer networks. They play a crucial role in enabling communication, collaboration, and the sharing of resources among multiple devices and users.
