## Unit 2 - Physical Layer

The physical layer is the first layer of the OSI model. It is responsible for the transmission and reception of raw data between a device and a physical transmission medium. Some of the key functions of the physical layer include:

1. Defining the physical characteristics of the transmission medium, such as the type of cable, connectors, and signaling methods.
2. Encoding and decoding data into electrical, optical, or radio signals.
3. Transmitting and receiving data over the physical medium.
4. Establishing and terminating connections between devices.
5. Managing the flow of data to prevent congestion and ensure reliable transmission.

The physical layer is responsible for converting the digital data from the upper layers into a format that can be transmitted over the physical medium. This process is known as encoding. The physical layer also performs the reverse process, decoding the received signals back into digital data.

The physical layer is also responsible for managing the flow of data between devices. This includes controlling the rate at which data is transmitted, detecting and correcting errors, and ensuring that data is transmitted reliably.

Overall, the physical layer plays a crucial role in enabling communication between devices by providing the means for transmitting and receiving data over a physical medium. It is the foundation upon which all other layers of the OSI model are built.
