### Services for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introductory Concepts in the subject of Computer Networks

- A service is a set of functions or capabilities that a layer provides to the layer above it.
- The services provided by a layer can be divided into two categories: connection-oriented and connectionless.
- Connection-oriented services establish a connection between two devices before any data is transmitted.
- Connectionless services do not require a connection to be established before data is transmitted.
- The services provided by a layer can also be divided into two categories: reliable and unreliable.
- Reliable services ensure that data is transmitted without errors and in the correct order.
- Unreliable services do not guarantee error-free or in-order transmission of data.
- The services provided by a layer can also be divided into two categories: acknowledged and unacknowledged.
- Acknowledged services provide a mechanism for the sender to receive confirmation that the data has been received by the receiver.
- Unacknowledged services do not provide any confirmation that the data has been received by the receiver.
- The services provided by a layer can also be divided into two categories: flow-controlled and non-flow-controlled.
- Flow-controlled services provide a mechanism for the sender to regulate the rate at which data is transmitted to the receiver.
- Non-flow-controlled services do not provide any mechanism for the sender to regulate the rate at which data is transmitted to the receiver.
