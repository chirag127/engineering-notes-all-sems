### Framing for the notes of the Unit 3 - Link layer in the subject of Computer Networks

1. The link layer is responsible for transferring data between two directly connected nodes.
2. Framing is the process of encapsulating data into frames for transmission over the link layer.
3. Frames typically include a header, payload, and trailer.
4. The header contains information such as the source and destination addresses, and the type of data being transmitted.
5. The payload is the actual data being transmitted.
6. The trailer typically contains error-detection and/or error-correction information to ensure the integrity of the data.
7. Different link layer protocols use different framing methods, such as character stuffing, bit stuffing, or length-based framing.
8. The link layer is responsible for detecting and possibly correcting errors that may occur during transmission.
9. The link layer may also provide flow control to prevent the sender from overwhelming the receiver with data.
10. The link layer is an important part of the overall network architecture, providing a reliable and efficient means of transmitting data between directly connected nodes.
