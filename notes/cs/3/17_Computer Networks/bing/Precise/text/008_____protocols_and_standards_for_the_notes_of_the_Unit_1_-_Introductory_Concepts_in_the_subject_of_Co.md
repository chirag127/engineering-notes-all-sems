### Protocols and Standards

1. **Protocols** are a set of rules and standards that govern the communication between devices on a network.
2. These rules define how data is transmitted, formatted, and processed between devices.
3. Protocols are essential for ensuring that devices can communicate with each other and exchange information accurately and efficiently.
4. Some common protocols used in computer networks include TCP/IP, HTTP, FTP, and SMTP.
5. **Standards** are agreed-upon guidelines that ensure consistency and interoperability between different devices and systems.
6. Standards are developed by organizations such as the International Organization for Standardization (ISO) and the Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers (IEEE).
7. Standards help to ensure that devices from different manufacturers can work together on the same network.
8. Some common standards used in computer networks include Ethernet, Wi-Fi, and Bluetooth.
