### Types of connections for the notes of the Unit 2 - Physical Layer in the subject of Computer Networks

There are two main types of connections in computer networks: point-to-point and multipoint.

1. **Point-to-point connection**: A point-to-point connection is a direct link between two devices. This type of connection is typically used for dedicated links between two devices, such as a computer and a printer, or between two routers. Point-to-point connections can be either full-duplex or half-duplex.

2. **Multipoint connection**: A multipoint connection, also known as a broadcast connection, is a link between multiple devices. This type of connection is typically used in local area networks (LANs) where multiple devices share a common communication channel. Multipoint connections can be either full-duplex or half-duplex.

In summary, point-to-point connections are used for dedicated links between two devices, while multipoint connections are used for shared communication channels between multiple devices. Both types of connections can be either full-duplex or half-duplex.
