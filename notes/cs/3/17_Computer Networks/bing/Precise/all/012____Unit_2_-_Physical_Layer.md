## Unit 2 - Physical Layer

The physical layer is the first layer of the OSI model. It is responsible for the transmission of raw data bits over a physical medium. The physical layer is concerned with the following:

1. Physical characteristics of interfaces and media: This includes the mechanical, electrical, and functional specifications of the interfaces and transmission media.

2. Representation of bits: The physical layer defines how bits are encoded and decoded into electrical, optical, or radio signals.

3. Data rate: The physical layer determines the transmission rate of data, measured in bits per second.

4. Synchronization of bits: The physical layer provides synchronization of the bit stream by providing a clock signal.

5. Line configuration: The physical layer determines the way in which two or more devices are connected to a link.

6. Physical topology: The physical layer defines the physical layout of the network, such as a bus, ring, or star topology.

7. Transmission mode: The physical layer determines the direction of data flow, such as simplex, half-duplex, or full-duplex.

The physical layer is responsible for the actual transmission of data over a physical medium. It converts the digital data into a form that can be transmitted over the physical medium, such as electrical, optical, or radio signals. The physical layer also provides error detection and correction to ensure the reliable transmission of data.
