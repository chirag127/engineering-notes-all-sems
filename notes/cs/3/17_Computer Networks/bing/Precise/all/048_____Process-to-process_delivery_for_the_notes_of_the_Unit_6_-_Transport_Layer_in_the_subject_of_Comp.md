### Process-to-process delivery

Process-to-process delivery is a function of the transport layer in computer networks. It is responsible for delivering data from one process to another on different hosts. Here are some key points to remember:

1. The transport layer uses port numbers to identify different processes on the same host.
2. The transport layer provides logical communication between processes on different hosts.
3. The transport layer can provide reliable or unreliable delivery of data, depending on the protocol used.
4. The transport layer can provide flow control and congestion control to ensure efficient delivery of data.
5. The transport layer can provide error control to detect and correct errors in the transmitted data.
6. The transport layer can provide multiplexing and demultiplexing of data from multiple processes.
