### ISP

An Internet Service Provider (ISP) is a company that provides internet access to customers. ISPs connect customers to the internet by providing them with a connection to their network, which is connected to the larger internet network.

Here are some key points to remember about ISPs:

1. ISPs can provide internet access through various technologies, including DSL, cable, fiber, and satellite.
2. ISPs may offer different plans with varying speeds and data allowances.
3. ISPs may also offer additional services, such as email, web hosting, and virtual private networks (VPNs).
4. The quality and reliability of an ISP's service can vary, and it is important to research and compare options before choosing an ISP.
5. ISPs are regulated by government agencies, which set rules and standards for their operation.
