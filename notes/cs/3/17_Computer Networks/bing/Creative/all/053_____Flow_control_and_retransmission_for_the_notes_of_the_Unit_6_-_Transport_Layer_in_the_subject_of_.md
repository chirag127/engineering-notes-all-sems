# Flow control and retransmission for the notes of the Unit 6 - Transport Layer in the subject of Computer Networks

- The transport layer is the fourth layer of the OSI model that ensures the reliable delivery of messages across a network and provides error-checking and data flow control mechanisms.
- The transport layer also performs packetization, which is the process of dividing a message into smaller segments and numbering them for identification and reassembly.
- Flow control is the technique of regulating the rate of data transmission between two nodes to prevent data loss or congestion. Flow control can be implemented at the sender side, the receiver side, or both.
- Retransmission is the technique of resending the corrupted or lost packets to ensure the reliability of data transfer. Retransmission can be triggered by various events, such as timeout, duplicate acknowledgment, or negative acknowledgment.
- The transport layer protocols that provide flow control and retransmission are TCP (Transmission Control Protocol) and SCTP (Stream Control Transmission Protocol). UDP (User Datagram Protocol) does not provide these services.
