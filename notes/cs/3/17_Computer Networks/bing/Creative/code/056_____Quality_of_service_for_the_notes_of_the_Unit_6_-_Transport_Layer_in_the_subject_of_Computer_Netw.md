### Quality of service for the notes of the Unit 6 - Transport Layer in the subject of Computer Networks

- Quality of service (QoS) is the ability of a network to provide reliable service to the traffic over various technologies and applications  .
- QoS is important for the transport layer because it determines what type of service to provide to the session layer and the users of the network.
- QoS can be affected by various factors such as bandwidth, delay, jitter, packet loss, and error rate  .
- QoS can be improved by using some techniques that can control the network traffic and prioritize the critical applications  .
- The four common techniques to improve QoS are   :
  - Scheduling: It is the process of deciding which packet to send next from a queue of packets waiting to be transmitted. Scheduling can use different algorithms such as first-come first-served (FCFS), priority queueing (PQ), weighted fair queueing (WFQ), etc. to assign different priorities to different packets based on their QoS requirements   .
  - Traffic shaping: It is the process of regulating the rate of packet transmission according to the available bandwidth and the QoS requirements of the applications. Traffic shaping can use techniques such as leaky bucket, token bucket, etc. to smooth out the bursty traffic and avoid congestion   .
  - Admission control: It is the process of deciding whether to accept or reject a new flow request based on the current network conditions and the QoS requirements of the existing and new flows. Admission control can use techniques such as measurement-based, parameter-based, or policy-based to evaluate the feasibility of a new flow request   .
  - Resource reservation: It is the process of allocating network resources such as bandwidth, buffer, etc. to a flow before it starts transmitting. Resource reservation can use protocols such as resource reservation protocol (RSVP), integrated services (IntServ), differentiated services (DiffServ), etc. to establish and maintain QoS guarantees for a flow   .
- These techniques can be used in combination or separately to improve the QoS of the transport layer and the network as a whole   .
