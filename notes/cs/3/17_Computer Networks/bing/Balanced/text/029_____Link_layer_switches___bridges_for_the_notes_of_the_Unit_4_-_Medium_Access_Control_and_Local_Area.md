### Link layer switches & bridges

- Link layer switches and bridges are network devices that operate at the data link layer (layer 2) of the OSI model and forward data frames based on MAC addresses.
- Link layer switches and bridges can connect multiple LANs (local area networks) together to form a larger LAN. This process of aggregating networks is called network bridging .
- Link layer switches and bridges can also filter and isolate traffic within a LAN, reducing collisions and improving performance .
- Link layer switches and bridges differ in their functionality and complexity. Switches are more advanced than bridges and can have multiple ports, support VLANs (virtual LANs), and perform routing at the network layer (layer 3) by incorporating IP addresses. Such switches are commonly known as layer-3 switches or multilayer switches .
- A wireless access point is a specialized bridge that creates a link between a wired and a wireless network.
