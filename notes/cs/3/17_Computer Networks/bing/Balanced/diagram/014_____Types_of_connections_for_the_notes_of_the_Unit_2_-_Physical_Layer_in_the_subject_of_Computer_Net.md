### Types of connections for the notes of the Unit 2 - Physical Layer in the subject of Computer Networks

- The physical layer is the lowest layer of the OSI model that deals with the transmission and reception of raw bits over a physical medium.
- The physical layer defines the characteristics of the physical medium, such as the type of cable, connectors, voltage levels, modulation schemes, etc.
- The physical layer also defines the physical topology of the network, which is the arrangement of devices and cables in a logical or geometric shape.
- There are different types of physical topologies, such as bus, mesh, star, ring, tree, and hybrid.
- Bus topology: It is a linear arrangement of devices connected by a single cable, called the bus. Each device has a unique address and can send or receive data to or from any other device on the bus. The bus has two terminators at both ends to prevent signal reflection. The advantage of bus topology is its simplicity and low cost. The disadvantage is that a break in the cable or a faulty device can affect the whole network.
- Mesh topology: It is a network where every device has a point-to-point connection with every other device. This provides high reliability and security, as data can take multiple paths to reach the destination. The disadvantage of mesh topology is the high cost and complexity of wiring and maintenance.
- Star topology: It is a network where each device has a dedicated point-to-point connection with a central controller or a hub. The hub acts as a relay for data transmission and can perform functions like amplification, filtering, or multiplexing. The advantage of star topology is that a failure of a device or a cable does not affect the rest of the network. The disadvantage is that the hub is a single point of failure and a bottleneck for the network.
- Ring topology: It is a network where devices are connected in a circular fashion by a single cable. Each device receives data from its predecessor and passes it to its successor. Data travels in one direction only, either clockwise or anticlockwise. The advantage of ring topology is that it eliminates the need for a central controller and reduces the number of cables. The disadvantage is that a break in the cable or a faulty device can disrupt the entire network.
- Tree topology: It is a network where devices are connected in a hierarchical structure, similar to a tree. The root node is the main controller of the network, and the other nodes are either intermediate nodes or leaf nodes. The advantage of tree topology is that it can support a large number of devices and can be easily expanded. The disadvantage is that the root node is a single point of failure and a bottleneck for the network.
- Hybrid topology: It is a network that combines two or more of the above topologies to suit the specific needs of the network. For example, a star-bus topology is a network where several star networks are connected by a bus. The advantage of hybrid topology is that it can provide the benefits of different topologies and can be customized for different scenarios. The disadvantage is that it can be complex and difficult to design and manage.
- Examples of physical layer protocols include fiber cables, integrated services digital networks, Ethernet, universal serial bus, Bluetooth, controller area network, etc.
