# Basic Internetworking

- Basic internetworking is the process of connecting different physical networks to form a logical network, such as the Internet .
- Basic internetworking is implemented in the network layer (layer 3) of the OSI model or the Internet layer (layer 2) of the TCP/IP model .
- The network layer is responsible for routing packets of data across different networks, using network-layer addresses, such as IP addresses .
- The network layer also provides services such as fragmentation, error control, congestion control, and quality of service.
- Basic internetworking requires devices such as routers, switches, gateways, and firewalls to interconnect different physical networks and manage the traffic between them .
- Basic internetworking enables communication and data exchange between different types of networks, such as LANs, WANs, MANs, PANs, etc .
- Basic internetworking can be classified into three types, depending on who administers and who participates in them: intranet, extranet, and internet.
  - Intranet: a private network that is accessible only to authorized users within an organization.
  - Extranet: a private network that is accessible to authorized users from outside an organization, such as customers, partners, or suppliers.
  - Internet: a public network that is accessible to anyone with an Internet connection.
