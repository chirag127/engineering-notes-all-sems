### ISP for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introductory Concepts in the subject of Computer Networks

An Internet Service Provider (ISP) is a company that provides Internet access to customers. The following are some of the key concepts related to ISPs:

1. Types of ISPs: ISPs can be classified into several types, including dial-up, broadband, and mobile.

2. Dial-up: Dial-up is the oldest type of Internet access and uses a telephone line to connect to the Internet.

3. Broadband: Broadband is a high-speed Internet connection that uses a cable or digital subscriber line (DSL) to connect to the Internet.

4. Mobile: Mobile ISPs provide Internet access through cellular networks and are used by smartphones and other mobile devices.

5. Internet Access: ISPs provide Internet access to customers, allowing them to connect to the Internet and access websites, email, and other online services.

6. Network Services: ISPs may also provide additional network services, such as email hosting, web hosting, and virtual private networks (VPNs).

In this unit, you will learn about ISPs and the key concepts related to them, including types of ISPs, dial-up, broadband, mobile, Internet access, and network services.

This unit will provide a foundation for understanding the principles and practices of ISPs and for exploring the various concepts and techniques used in the field of computer networks.
