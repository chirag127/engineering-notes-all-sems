### Quality of service for the notes of the Unit 6 - Transport Layer in the subject of Computer Networks

Unit 6 - Transport Layer in the subject of Computer Networks will cover the concept of Quality of Service (QoS), a set of techniques used to manage the flow of data in a computer network.

Quality of Service (QoS) refers to the set of techniques used to manage the flow of data in a computer network and to ensure that data is transmitted with a certain level of reliability and performance. QoS is used to prioritize different types of data traffic, such as voice or video, and to ensure that these types of data are transmitted with a higher level of reliability and performance than other types of data.
