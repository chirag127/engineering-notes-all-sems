## Unit 2 - Physical Layer

Unit 2 - Physical Layer in the subject of Web Technology will cover the basics of the physical layer of the Internet, including the hardware and infrastructure used to support the Internet.

The physical layer of the Internet refers to the underlying hardware and infrastructure used to support the Internet, including the network of computers, servers, and other devices that make up the Internet.

In Unit 2 - Physical Layer, students will learn about the components of the physical layer of the Internet, including computer hardware, networking equipment, and other devices. They will also learn about the infrastructure used to support the Internet, including the network of computers, servers, and other devices that make up the Internet.

In addition, students will learn about the various types of networking equipment used to support the Internet, including routers, switches, and other networking devices. They will learn about the role of each of these devices in supporting the Internet, and will learn how to configure and manage these devices.

In summary, in Unit 2 - Physical Layer, students will learn about the basics of the physical layer of the Internet, including the hardware and infrastructure used to support the Internet. They will learn about the components of the physical layer of the Internet, including computer hardware, networking equipment, and other devices, and will learn about the infrastructure used to support the Internet, including the network of computers, servers, and other devices that make up the Internet.
