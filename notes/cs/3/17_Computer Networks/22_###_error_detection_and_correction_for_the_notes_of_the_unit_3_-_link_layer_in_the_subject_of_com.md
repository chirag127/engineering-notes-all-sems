### Error Detection and Correction for the notes of the Unit 3 - Link layer in the subject of Computer Networks

The Error Detection and Correction section of the Unit 3 - Link layer in the subject of Computer Networks should cover the following topics:

1. Definition of Error Detection and Correction (techniques used to detect and correct errors in data transmission)
2. Overview of Error Detection and Correction (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
3. Types of Errors (e.g. bit errors, burst errors, etc.)
4. Overview of Types of Errors (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
5. Error Detection Techniques (e.g. parity checks, checksum, etc.)
6. Overview of Error Detection Techniques (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
7. Error Correction Techniques (e.g. forward error correction, error correction codes, etc.)
8. Overview of Error Correction Techniques (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
9. Error Detection and Correction in industry applications (how error detection and correction is used in various industries)
10. Further reading and resources (books, articles, online resources)

Error Detection and Correction is a crucial component of the subject of Computer Networks. Understanding the principles and techniques of error detection and correction helps students to develop the skills required to design and implement reliable and efficient computer networks. The hands-on practice and further reading resources will help students to develop their skills and deepen their understanding of the material.
