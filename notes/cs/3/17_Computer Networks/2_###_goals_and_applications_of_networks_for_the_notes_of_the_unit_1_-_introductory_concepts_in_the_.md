### Goals and applications of networks for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introductory Concepts in the subject of Computer Networks

The goals and applications of networks are diverse and range from simple resource sharing to complex communication systems. The following are some of the key concepts related to the goals and applications of networks:

1. Resource Sharing: Resource sharing is one of the primary goals of networks, and allows multiple devices to access and use shared resources, such as printers, files, and databases.

2. Communication: Communication is another key goal of networks, and enables the exchange of information between devices and users.

3. Distributed Computing: Distributed computing is the use of multiple devices to perform complex tasks, such as data processing and simulations.

4. Remote Access: Remote access allows users to access resources and communicate with other devices from remote locations, such as from home or while traveling.

5. E-Commerce: E-Commerce is the buying and selling of goods and services over the internet, and is one of the most important applications of networks.

6. Social Networking: Social networking is the use of networks to connect people and share information, such as through social media platforms like Facebook and Twitter.

In this unit, you will learn about the goals and applications of networks and the key concepts related to them, including resource sharing, communication, distributed computing, remote access, e-commerce, and social networking.

This unit will provide a foundation for understanding the principles and practices of networks and for exploring the various concepts and techniques used in the field of computer networks.
