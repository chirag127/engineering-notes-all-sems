
### ISP for the Notes of Unit 1 - Introductory Concepts in the Subject of Computer Networks

1. An Internet Service Provider (ISP) is an organization that provides access to the Internet for its customers.

2. ISPs offer a range of services, such as dial-up, cable, DSL, and fiber-optic services.

3. ISPs are responsible for providing the necessary infrastructure and equipment to connect customers to the Internet.

4. ISPs also provide customer support and technical assistance to their customers.

5. ISPs are regulated by the Federal Communications Commission (FCC) in the United States.

6. ISPs are required to comply with the regulations set forth by the FCC. 

7. ISPs must also adhere to certain privacy and security standards.

8. ISPs are also responsible for ensuring that their customers have access to a reliable and secure connection to the Internet.
