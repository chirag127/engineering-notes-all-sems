
### Categories of Networks

1. Local Area Networks (LANs): These are networks that are confined to a relatively small area, such as a single building or campus.

2. Metropolitan Area Networks (MANs): These are networks that span a city or metropolitan area, such as a group of buildings in a school district.

3. Wide Area Networks (WANs): These are networks that span a large geographic area, such as multiple cities, states, or countries.

4. Wireless Networks: These are networks that use wireless technology, such as Wi-Fi or Bluetooth, to connect devices.

5. Virtual Private Networks (VPNs): These are networks that use encryption to secure private data transmissions over public networks.
