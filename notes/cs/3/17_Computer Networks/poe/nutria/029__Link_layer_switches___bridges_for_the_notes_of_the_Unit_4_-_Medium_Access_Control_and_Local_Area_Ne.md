
### Link Layer Switches & Bridges

* Switches are used to connect two or more network segments and are used to create a LAN. 
* Bridges are used to connect two or more LANs together. 
* Switches and bridges are used to create a single, larger network. 
* Switches are typically used at the link layer of the OSI model, while bridges are used at the data link layer. 
* Switches and bridges can be used to filter traffic, prioritize traffic, and prevent data collisions. 
* Switches use a variety of methods to forward packets, such as store-and-forward, cut-through, and fragment-free. 
* Bridges use a variety of methods to forward packets, such as transparent bridging and source-route bridging. 
* Switches and bridges can be used to create virtual LANs (VLANs). 
* VLANs are used to segment a network into multiple broadcast domains. 
* Switches and bridges can be used to implement Quality of Service (QoS). 
* QoS is used to prioritize certain types of traffic, such as voice and video.
