
### Network Structure and Architecture

1. Networks are composed of two or more computers connected together to share information, resources, and services.
2. Network architecture is the design of a computer network. It defines the physical and logical components of the network, as well as the communication protocols and methods used to access the network.
3. Network architectures can be divided into two main categories: client-server and peer-to-peer.
4. Client-server networks are centralized networks where one or more computers (called servers) provide services to other computers (called clients).
5. Peer-to-peer networks are decentralized networks where each computer acts as both a client and a server.
6. The most common type of network architecture is the layered architecture, which consists of layers of protocols and services that build upon each other.
7. The Open Systems Interconnection (OSI) model is a seven-layer model that describes how data is transmitted over a network.
8. The Transmission Control Protocol/Internet Protocol (TCP/IP) is a four-layer model that is commonly used in today's networks.
9. Network topologies describe the physical layout of a network. Common topologies include bus, star, ring, and mesh.
10. Network devices such as routers, switches, and bridges are used to connect computers and other devices on a network.
