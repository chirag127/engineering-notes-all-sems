
### Point-to-point Networks

Point-to-point networks are networks that establish a direct connection between two endpoints. This type of network is used to provide a dedicated link between two computers or two networks. It is typically used for short-distance communication, such as between two computers in the same room or between two networks in the same building.

The most common type of point-to-point network is a Local Area Network (LAN). A LAN is typically used to connect computers and other devices in a single location, such as an office or home. It is also used to connect two different locations, such as two offices in different cities.

Point-to-point networks are also used to connect two different networks, such as two different LANs in different locations. This type of network is known as a Wide Area Network (WAN). A WAN is typically used to connect two networks that are geographically separated, such as two offices in different cities or two different countries.

Point-to-point networks can also be used to connect two computers or networks over the Internet. This type of network is known as a Virtual Private Network (VPN). A VPN is typically used to provide a secure connection between two computers or networks over the Internet.

Point-to-point networks are also used to connect two computers or networks over satellite. This type of network is known as a Satellite Network. A Satellite Network is typically used to provide a connection between two computers or networks in remote locations, such as two offices in different countries.
