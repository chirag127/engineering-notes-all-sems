
### Multiplexing for the notes of the Unit 6 - Transport Layer in the subject of Computer Networks

* Multiplexing is the process of combining multiple data streams into one, allowing multiple users to send and receive data over a single communication channel.
* Multiplexing is used to increase the efficiency of data transmission, as it allows multiple users to share the same physical communication channel.
* There are two types of multiplexing: Time Division Multiplexing (TDM) and Frequency Division Multiplexing (FDM).
* In TDM, each user is allocated a fixed amount of time to send data, while in FDM, each user is allocated a fixed frequency range to send data.
* The Transport Layer of the OSI model is responsible for the multiplexing and demultiplexing of data.
* The Transport Layer is responsible for providing end-to-end communication between two hosts, and it does this by multiplexing and demultiplexing data.
* The Transport Layer also provides error-correction and flow control, ensuring data is delivered reliably and efficiently.
