

### Services for the Notes of the Unit 1 - Introductory Concepts in the Subject of Computer Networks

1. Network Services: Network services are the functions that are provided by the network to its users. These services may include file transfer, remote login, email, web hosting, and other services.

2. Protocols: Protocols are the rules and standards that govern the communication between two or more devices on a network. Examples of protocols include TCP/IP, UDP, and HTTP.

3. Network Topology: Network topology is the physical or logical arrangement of computers and other devices on a network. Examples of network topologies include bus, star, and mesh.

4. Network Security: Network security is the practice of protecting a network from unauthorized access. Security measures may include firewalls, encryption, and authentication.

5. Network Performance: Network performance is the measure of a network's ability to deliver data to its users. Performance can be affected by factors such as bandwidth, latency, and jitter.
