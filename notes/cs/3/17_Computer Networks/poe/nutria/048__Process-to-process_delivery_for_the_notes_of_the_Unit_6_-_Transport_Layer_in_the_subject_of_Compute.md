
### Process-to-process delivery for the notes of the Unit 6 - Transport Layer in the subject of Computer Networks

1. Process-to-process delivery is a communication service that is provided by the Transport layer of the OSI model. 
2. It is responsible for the reliable delivery of data between two processes that are running on different hosts.
3. Process-to-process delivery is provided by the Transport layer protocols, such as TCP and UDP. 
4. TCP is a connection-oriented protocol that provides reliable delivery of data by using a 3-way handshake and acknowledgements. 
5. UDP is a connectionless protocol that does not guarantee reliable delivery of data, but is faster than TCP. 
6. The Transport layer also provides the services of segmentation and reassembly, multiplexing and demultiplexing, and flow control. 
7. Segmentation and reassembly is the process of breaking up data into smaller chunks and then reassembling them at the destination. 
8. Multiplexing and demultiplexing is the process of combining multiple data streams into one data stream and then separating them at the destination. 
9. Flow control is the process of controlling the rate at which data is transmitted between two hosts. 
10. The Transport layer also provides the services of congestion control, which is the process of controlling the amount of data that is sent over the network at any given time.
