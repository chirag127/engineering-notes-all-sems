### Electronic mail for the notes of the Unit 7 - Application Layer in the subject of Computer Networks

Electronic mail, commonly known as email, is one of the most widely used applications on the internet. It is an electronic message that can be transmitted from one computer to another over a network. Email is an essential tool for communication in the digital age and has revolutionized the way we communicate.

Here are some important points to understand electronic mail:

- Email is one of the applications of the Application Layer in the OSI model of computer networks. It uses the Simple Mail Transfer Protocol (SMTP) for sending emails and the Post Office Protocol (POP) or Internet Message Access Protocol (IMAP) for receiving emails.
- An email message consists of three parts: the header, the message body, and any attachments. The header contains information about the sender, recipient, and other email details. The message body contains the actual content of the email. Attachments can be added to an email message to send files or documents.
- Email addresses are used to identify the sender and recipient of an email message. An email address consists of two parts: the username and the domain name. For example, in the email address john.doe@gmail.com, "john.doe" is the username and "gmail.com" is the domain name.
- Email clients are software applications that allow users to access and manage their email accounts. Popular email clients include Microsoft Outlook, Gmail, and Apple Mail. These clients provide features like composing and sending emails, organizing email messages, and managing contacts and calendars.
- Spam is unsolicited email that is sent to a large number of recipients. It is a common problem with email and can be prevented by using spam filters and being cautious about opening emails from unknown senders.
- Email security is an important aspect of using email. Email can be encrypted to protect the content of the messages from being read by unauthorized users. Digital signatures can be used to verify the authenticity of the sender and ensure that the message has not been tampered with.

In conclusion, electronic mail is an important application of the Application Layer in computer networks. It has revolutionized the way we communicate and has become an essential tool for communication in the digital age. Understanding the basics of email and its features is important for effective communication and email security.
