### Switching techniques and multiplexing for the notes of the Unit 2 - Physical Layer in the subject of Computer Networks.

1. Switching techniques:
- Circuit switching: Establishes a dedicated end-to-end path between two nodes before the actual data transmission takes place. Ideal for real-time transmission of data such as voice.
- Packet switching: Data is divided into packets with destination address in each packet. Packets from multiple sources may be routed via same path and delivered out of order at destination. Used in computer networks.

2. Multiplexing: Allows multiple signals or data streams to be transferred over a common channel. Types:
- Frequency division multiplexing (FDM): Each signal is assigned a different frequency.
- Time division multiplexing (TDM): Each signal is allocated a different time slot within a fixed period.
- Wavelength division multiplexing (WDM): Each signal is assigned a different wavelength of light in fibre optic communication.

The key points to remember are the differences between circuit switching and packet switching and the various types of multiplexing techniques. Practice questions on scenarios where a particular technique is more suitable can be expected in exams.
