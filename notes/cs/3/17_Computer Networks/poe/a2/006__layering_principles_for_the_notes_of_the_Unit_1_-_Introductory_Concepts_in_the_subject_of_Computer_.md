### Layering principles for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introductory Concepts in Computer Networks

1. Layering allows division of complex problems into smaller, more manageable subproblems.
2. Each layer focuses on one aspect of the problem and interacts only with the layers immediately above and below it.
3. The layers together provide the complete functionality required to solve the overall problem.
4. Layering provides modularity which simplifies network design and implementation.
5. Changes in one layer do not affect other layers as long as the interfaces between layers remain the same. This enhances network flexibility, extensibility, and sustainability.
6. Different layers may be implemented by different vendors as long as they conform to the interface specifications. This allows for compatibility and interoperability between network elements by different vendors.

The notes cover the key concepts and role of layering in computer networks which forms the foundation for the study of topics in subsequent units. The principles of layering help understand the organization and function of the network architecture.
