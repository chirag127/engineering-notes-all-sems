### Data compression for the notes of the Unit 7 - Application Layer in the subject of Computer Networks.

1. Data compression is a technique of reducing the number of bits needed to represent data. It is used to reduce bandwidth requirements and storage space.
2. Lossless compression - The original data can be perfectly reconstructed from the compressed data. The compression ratio is relatively small. Examples: Huffman coding, Lempel-Ziv coding.
3. Lossy compression - The original data cannot be perfectly reconstructed from the compressed data. Some amount of information is lost. However, the compression ratio is higher. Examples: JPEG, MP3, MPEG.
4. JPEG is a lossy compression technique for images. It can achieve compression ratios of 1:10 to 1:20. The image quality can be varied by adjusting the compression ratio. Higher compression leads to higher loss of information and lower image quality.
5. MP3 is a lossy audio compression format. It can achieve compression ratios of 1:10 to 1:12. Higher compression ratios lead to lower audio quality. MP3 eliminates sounds that are difficult to hear and components of the audio signal that are masked by other sounds.
