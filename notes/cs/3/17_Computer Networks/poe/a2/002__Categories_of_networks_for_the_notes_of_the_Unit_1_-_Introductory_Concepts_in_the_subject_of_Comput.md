### Categories of networks for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introductory Concepts in the subject of Computer Networks

1. Local Area Networks (LANs):
- Cover a small physical area like an office, a group of buildings, or a factory.
- High data transfer speeds.
- Uses a shared communication medium or a network of cables.
- Example: Ethernet

2. Metropolitan Area Networks (MANs):
- Span an entire city.
- Uses fiber optic cables or other high-speed transport technologies.
- Examples: cable TV networks, cellular telephone networks.

3. Wide Area Networks (WANs):
- Cover a large geographical area like a country or the world.
- Uses telephone lines, satellites, and other long-distance transmission technologies.
- Usually slower than LANs and MANs.
- Examples: Internet, cellular networks.
