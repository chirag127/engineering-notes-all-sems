### Point-to-point networks

1. Point-to-point networks consist of direct connections between two nodes/devices.
2. There are no intermediate nodes involved in the communication. The two endpoints are directly connected.
3. These networks are typically used when high speed and high security are required, such as connecting two office buildings or data centers.
4. The entire capacity of the link is dedicated to the two connected nodes. There is no sharing of bandwidth with other nodes.
5. The transmission medium can be copper wire, optical fiber or wireless.
6. Examples of point-to-point networks are:
- Leased lines provided by telephone companies
- Wireless point-to-point links using directional antennas
- Fiber optic cable connecting two locations
