### Protocols and Standards

- Protocols: Rules and regulations for communication between devices. They define the format and order of messages exchanged between two or more communicating entities, as well as the actions taken on the transmission and/or receipt of a message or other event.
- Examples: HTTP, FTP, SMTP, IP, TCP, UDP, etc.
- Standards: Technical specifications that establish uniform engineering or technical criteria, methods, processes and practices. They help ensure that products from different manufacturers are compatible.
- Examples: Ethernet, Wi-Fi, USB, etc.
- Role of protocols and standards:
    - Enable different systems to communicate and interoperate.
    - Ensure flexibility and extensibility to support advancement in technology.
    - Provide common frame of reference to facilitate design, development, and testing.
- Developed by standard organizations (IEEE, IETF, ISO, etc.) through a consensus process.
- Follow a standard model or architecture: OSI model or TCP/IP model.
