### Multiplexing for the notes of the Unit 6 - Transport Layer in the subject of Computer Networks

Multiplexing is a technique used in computer networks to transmit multiple data streams simultaneously over a single communication channel. In the Transport Layer of the OSI model, Multiplexing is an important concept that plays a crucial role in ensuring the efficient and reliable transmission of data.

Here are some key points to understand Multiplexing in the Transport Layer:

- Multiplexing allows multiple applications to share a single communication channel by dividing it into smaller channels.
- There are two types of Multiplexing: Time Division Multiplexing (TDM) and Frequency Division Multiplexing (FDM).
- In TDM, the channel is divided into time slots, and each application is assigned a specific time slot for transmission. This allows each application to have exclusive access to the channel during its allocated time slot.
- In FDM, the channel is divided into frequency bands, and each application is assigned a specific frequency band for transmission. This allows each application to transmit its data on a different frequency band, which avoids interference with other applications.
- Multiplexing in the Transport Layer is achieved using the concept of Ports. Each application is assigned a unique port number, and the Transport Layer uses this port number to identify the application to which the data belongs.
- The Transport Layer also uses Multiplexing to combine multiple data streams into a single stream for transmission over the network. This is known as Multiplexing at the sender side.
- At the receiver side, the Transport Layer uses Demultiplexing to identify the individual data streams and deliver them to the appropriate application. Demultiplexing is achieved using the port number assigned to each application.

In conclusion, Multiplexing is an essential concept in the Transport Layer of computer networks. It allows multiple applications to share a single communication channel, which improves efficiency and reduces costs. Understanding the different types of Multiplexing and how it is achieved using Ports is crucial for anyone studying the Transport Layer in Computer Networks.
