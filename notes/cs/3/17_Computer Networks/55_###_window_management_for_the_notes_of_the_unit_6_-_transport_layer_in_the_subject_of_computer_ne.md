### Window management for the notes of the Unit 6 - Transport Layer in the subject of Computer Networks

Unit 6 - Transport Layer in the subject of Computer Networks will cover Window management, a technique used to manage the flow of data between devices in a computer network.

In computer networks, data is transmitted between devices in small packets. To ensure that data is transmitted efficiently, the transport layer uses window management to control the flow of data between devices. Window management involves the use of a window, which is a buffer that holds a set of data packets. The size of the window determines how many data packets can be transmitted at one time, and the transport layer uses window management to adjust the size of the window based on network conditions.

In the context of Unit 6 - Transport Layer in the subject of Computer Networks, students will learn about Window management, including how to design and implement computer networks that use this technique. This may include working with examples of Window management, analyzing the syntax and structure of Window management, and learning how to use Window management to optimize the performance of computer networks.

In Unit 6 - Transport Layer in the subject of Computer Networks, students will also learn about the benefits and limitations of using Window management, and will learn about alternative techniques for managing the flow of data between devices in a computer network, such as using flow control.
