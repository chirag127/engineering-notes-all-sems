### services for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introductory Concepts in the subject of Computer Networks

The Services section of the Unit 1 - Introductory Concepts in the subject of Computer Networks should cover the following topics:

1. Definition of Services (functions provided by the network to its users)
2. Overview of Services (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
3. Types of Services (e.g. communication services, application services, etc.)
4. Overview of Types of Services (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
5. Communication Services (e.g. email, file transfer, etc.)
6. Overview of Communication Services (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
7. Application Services (e.g. web services, database services, etc.)
8. Overview of Application Services (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
9. Quality of Service (QoS) (e.g. ensuring reliable and efficient network services)
10. Overview of Quality of Service (QoS) (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
11. Network Services in industry applications (how network services are used in various industries)
12. Further reading and resources (books, articles, online resources)

Services are a fundamental component of computer networks. Understanding the principles and techniques of services helps students to develop the skills required to design and implement effective computer networks. The hands-on practice and further reading resources will help students to develop their skills and deepen their understanding of the material.
