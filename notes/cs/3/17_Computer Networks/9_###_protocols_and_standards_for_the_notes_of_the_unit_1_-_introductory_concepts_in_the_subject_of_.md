### protocols and standards for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introductory Concepts in the subject of Computer Networks

Protocols and standards are fundamental components of computer networks, and are used to define the rules and procedures for communication between devices. The following are some of the key concepts related to protocols and standards:

1. Protocols: A protocol is a set of rules and procedures that govern communication between devices in a computer network. Examples of protocols include TCP/IP, HTTP, FTP, and SMTP.

2. Standards: A standard is a set of specifications and guidelines that are widely accepted and adopted by the industry. Examples of standards include Ethernet, Wi-Fi, and Bluetooth.

3. OSI Model: The OSI (Open Systems Interconnection) Model is a theoretical model that defines the seven layers of communication in a computer network, including the physical layer, data link layer, network layer, transport layer, session layer, presentation layer, and application layer.

4. TCP/IP: TCP/IP (Transmission Control Protocol/Internet Protocol) is a suite of protocols that are used to transmit data over the Internet.

5. HTTP: HTTP (Hypertext Transfer Protocol) is a protocol used to transfer data on the World Wide Web, and is used to request and receive web pages from a server.

6. FTP: FTP (File Transfer Protocol) is a protocol used to transfer files between computers on a network.

In this unit, you will learn about protocols and standards and the key concepts related to them, including protocols, standards, the OSI Model, TCP/IP, HTTP, and FTP.

This unit will provide a foundation for understanding the principles and practices of protocols and standards, and for exploring the various concepts and techniques used in the field of computer networks.
