### Network structure and architecture for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introductory Concepts in the subject of Computer Networks

The structure and architecture of a computer network refers to the way in which the network is organized and the technologies and protocols used to support communication between devices. The following are some of the key concepts related to network structure and architecture:

1. Network Topology: Network topology refers to the physical and logical arrangement of devices and connections in a network, such as a star, bus, or mesh topology.

2. Network Devices: Network devices are hardware components that perform specific functions in a network, such as routers, switches, and firewalls.

3. Network Protocols: Network protocols are standardized rules and procedures used to govern communication between devices in a network, such as TCP/IP and HTTP.

4. Network Layers: Network layers are abstractions used to organize the functions of a network into a hierarchical structure, such as the OSI (Open Systems Interconnection) model.

5. Network Services: Network services are functions provided by a network, such as email, file sharing, and web browsing.

6. Network Architecture: Network architecture refers to the overall design of a network, including the technologies, protocols, and devices used to support communication.

In this unit, you will learn about the structure and architecture of computer networks, including network topology, devices, protocols, layers, services, and architecture.

This unit will provide a foundation for understanding the principles and practices of computer networks and for exploring the various concepts and techniques used in the field of computer networking.
