### Network topology design for the notes of the Unit 2 - Physical Layer in the subject of Computer Networks

The Network Topology Design section of the Unit 2 - Physical Layer in the subject of Computer Networks should cover the following topics:

1. Definition of Network Topology (the arrangement of the various elements of a computer network)
2. Overview of Network Topology (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
3. Types of Network Topologies (e.g. bus, ring, star, mesh, etc.)
4. Overview of Types of Network Topologies (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
5. Network Topology Design Considerations (e.g. performance, scalability, reliability, security, etc.)
6. Overview of Network Topology Design Considerations (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
7. Network Topology Design Tools (e.g. simulation software, network mapping tools, etc.)
8. Overview of Network Topology Design Tools (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
9. Network Topology Design in industry applications (how network topology design is used in various industries)
10. Further reading and resources (books, articles, online resources)

Network Topology Design is an important component of the subject of Computer Networks. Understanding the principles and techniques of network topology design helps students to develop the skills required to design and implement effective computer networks. The hands-on practice and further reading resources will help students to develop their skills and deepen their understanding of the material.
