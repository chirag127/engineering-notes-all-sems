### Switching techniques and multiplexing for the notes of the Unit 2 - Physical Layer in the subject of Computer Networks

Unit 2 - Physical Layer in the subject of Computer Networks will cover Switching techniques and multiplexing, two important concepts in the design and implementation of computer networks.

Switching techniques are methods used to route data from one network device to another. There are several different types of switching techniques, including circuit switching, packet switching, and message switching, each of which has its own advantages and disadvantages.

Multiplexing is the process of combining multiple signals into a single signal for transmission over a shared medium. Multiplexing is used in computer networks to increase the efficiency of network bandwidth utilization.

This may include working with examples of computer networks that use switching techniques and multiplexing, analyzing the syntax and structure of computer networks, and learning how to use switching techniques and multiplexing to optimize the performance of computer networks.
