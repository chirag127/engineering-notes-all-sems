## Unit 6 - Transport Layer

The transport layer is the fourth layer of the TCP/IP model in computer networking, and is responsible for providing reliable communication between applications. The following are some of the key concepts related to the transport layer:

1. Transport Services: Transport services are the services provided by the transport layer to applications, such as reliable communication and flow control.

2. Transport Protocols: Transport protocols are the protocols used by the transport layer to provide transport services, such as TCP (Transmission Control Protocol) and UDP (User Datagram Protocol).

3. TCP: TCP is a reliable, connection-oriented transport protocol that provides flow control and error detection.

4. UDP: UDP is an unreliable, connectionless transport protocol that provides fast, low-overhead communication.

5. Segmentation: Segmentation is the process of dividing data into segments for transmission, and is performed by the transport layer.

6. Flow Control: Flow control is the process of regulating the flow of data between devices to prevent overloading of the network.

In this unit, you will learn about the transport layer in computer networking, including transport services, transport protocols, TCP, UDP, segmentation, and flow control.

This unit will provide a foundation for understanding the principles and practices of the transport layer in computer networking and for exploring the various concepts and techniques used in the field of computer networks.
