### Network performance and transmission impairments for the notes of the Unit 2 - Physical Layer in the subject of Computer Networks

Unit 2 - Physical Layer in the subject of Computer Networks will cover Network performance and transmission impairments, two important concepts related to the physical layer of computer networks.

The physical layer of a computer network is responsible for transmitting bits of data over a physical medium, such as a copper cable or optical fiber. Network performance refers to the overall performance of the network, including factors such as speed, reliability, and efficiency.

Transmission impairments refer to any degradation of the signal that occurs during transmission over the physical medium. Some common transmission impairments include attenuation, noise, and interference.

In the context of Unit 2 - Physical Layer in the subject of Computer Networks, students will learn about Network performance and transmission impairments, including how to measure and evaluate network performance and how to mitigate the effects of transmission impairments. This may include working with examples of network performance and transmission impairments, analyzing the causes and effects of these impairments, and learning how to use various techniques to improve network performance and reduce the impact of transmission impairments.

In Unit 2 - Physical Layer in the subject of Computer Networks, students will also learn about the benefits and limitations of different approaches to network performance and transmission impairments, and will learn about alternative approaches to improving network performance and reducing the impact of transmission impairments.
