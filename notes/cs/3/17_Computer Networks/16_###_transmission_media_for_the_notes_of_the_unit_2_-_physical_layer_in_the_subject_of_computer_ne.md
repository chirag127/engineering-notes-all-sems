### Transmission media for the notes of the Unit 2 - Physical Layer in the subject of Computer Networks

Unit 2 - Physical Layer in the subject of Computer Networks will cover the concept of Transmission media, the physical means by which data is transmitted across a network.

Transmission media refers to the physical means by which data is transmitted across a network, including copper wire, fiber optic cable, and wireless transmission. Different types of transmission media have different characteristics, such as bandwidth, signal quality, and cost, which affect the speed and reliability of data transmission.

In the context of Unit 2 - Physical Layer in the subject of Computer Networks, students will learn about the different types of transmission media, including copper wire, fiber optic cable, and wireless transmission. They will learn about the characteristics of each type of transmission media, including bandwidth, signal quality, and cost, and will learn how to select the appropriate type of transmission media for a given network.

In Unit 2 - Physical Layer in the subject of Computer Networks, students will also learn about the benefits and limitations of using different types of transmission media, and will learn about alternative approaches to data transmission, such as using different types of transmission media in combination.
