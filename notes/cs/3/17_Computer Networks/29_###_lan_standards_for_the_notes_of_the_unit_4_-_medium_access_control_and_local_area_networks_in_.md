### LAN standards for the notes of the Unit 4 - Medium Access Control and Local Area Networks in the subject of Computer Networks

Unit 4 - Medium Access Control and Local Area Networks in the subject of Computer Networks will cover LAN standards, a set of technical specifications and protocols used to govern the operation of Local Area Networks (LANs).

A LAN is a small network of interconnected devices that share a common communication medium, such as a copper cable or optical fiber. LANs are used to connect computers and other devices within a small geographic area, such as a home, office, or building.

LAN standards are a set of technical specifications and protocols used to govern the operation of LANs. These standards specify the physical and data link layer protocols used to transmit data over the LAN, as well as the medium access control (MAC) protocols used to manage access to the shared communication medium.

In the context of Unit 4 - Medium Access Control and Local Area Networks in the subject of Computer Networks, students will learn about LAN standards, including the most commonly used LAN standards, such as Ethernet and Wi-Fi. They will learn about the technical specifications and protocols used to govern the operation of LANs, and will learn how to design and implement LANs using these standards.

In Unit 4 - Medium Access Control and Local Area Networks in the subject of Computer Networks, students will also learn about the benefits and limitations of different LAN standards, and will learn about alternative approaches to designing and implementing LANs, such as using different types of LAN standards in combination.
