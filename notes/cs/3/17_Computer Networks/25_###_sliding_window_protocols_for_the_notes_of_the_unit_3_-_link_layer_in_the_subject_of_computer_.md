### Sliding Window protocols for the notes of the Unit 3 - Link layer in the subject of Computer Networks

The Sliding Window protocols section of the Unit 3 - Link Layer in the subject of Computer Networks should cover the following topics:

1. Definition of Sliding Window protocols (a flow control mechanism used in computer networks)
2. Overview of Sliding Window protocols (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
3. Types of Sliding Window protocols (e.g. Stop-and-Wait, Go-Back-N, Selective Repeat, etc.)
4. Overview of Types of Sliding Window protocols (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
5. Sliding Window protocols and Flow Control (e.g. controlling the flow of data in a network)
6. Overview of Sliding Window protocols and Flow Control (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
7. Sliding Window protocols and Error Detection (e.g. detecting and correcting errors in data transmission)
8. Overview of Sliding Window protocols and Error Detection (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
9. Sliding Window protocols in industry applications (how sliding window protocols are used in various industries)
10. Further reading and resources (books, articles, online resources)

Sliding Window protocols are an important component of the subject of Computer Networks. Understanding the principles and techniques of sliding window protocols helps students to develop the skills required to design and implement effective computer networks. The hands-on practice and further reading resources will help students to develop their skills and deepen their understanding of the material.
