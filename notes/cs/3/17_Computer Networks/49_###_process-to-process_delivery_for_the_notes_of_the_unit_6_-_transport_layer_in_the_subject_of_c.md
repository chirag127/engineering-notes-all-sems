### Process-to-process delivery for the notes of the Unit 6 - Transport Layer in the subject of Computer Networks

Unit 6 - Transport Layer in the subject of Computer Networks will cover the concept of Process-to-process delivery, the process of delivering data from one process to another process on a different computer.

Process-to-process delivery refers to the process of delivering data from one process to another process on a different computer. The transport layer of the OSI (Open Systems Interconnection) model is responsible for managing process-to-process delivery, ensuring that data is delivered reliably and efficiently from one process to another.
