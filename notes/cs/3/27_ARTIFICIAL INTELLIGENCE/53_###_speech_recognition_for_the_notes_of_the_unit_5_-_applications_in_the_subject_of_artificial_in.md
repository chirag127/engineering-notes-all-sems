Speech Recognition 

Speech recognition is the process of converting spoken language into text or commands that can be processed by a computer. It is a subfield of Artificial Intelligence that is concerned with the development of algorithms and systems that can recognize and transcribe spoken language.

Speech recognition is used in a variety of applications, including voice-controlled assistants, voice-activated devices, and speech-to-text software. It is also used in fields such as healthcare, education, and finance to support decision making and to provide users with relevant information.

There are two main types of speech recognition: isolated word recognition and continuous speech recognition. Isolated word recognition is used to recognize individual words, while continuous speech recognition is used to recognize speech in a continuous stream.

In conclusion, speech recognition is the process of converting spoken language into text or commands that can be processed by a computer. It is used in a variety of applications and is a subfield of Artificial Intelligence that is concerned with the development of algorithms and systems that can recognize and transcribe spoken language.
