## Unit 5 - APPLICATIONS

In this unit, you will learn about the different applications of artificial intelligence (AI) and machine learning (ML). The following topics will be covered:

1. Natural Language Processing (NLP)
   - Understanding NLP and its applications
   - Basic concepts of NLP such as tokenization, stemming, and lemmatization
   - Techniques for sentiment analysis, text classification, and language translation
   - Applications of NLP in industries such as healthcare, finance, and e-commerce

2. Computer Vision
   - Understanding computer vision and its applications
   - Basic concepts of computer vision such as image recognition, object detection, and image segmentation
   - Techniques for image classification, face recognition, and object tracking
   - Applications of computer vision in industries such as automotive, retail, and security

3. Robotics
   - Understanding robotics and its applications
   - Basic concepts of robotics such as sensors, actuators, and control systems
   - Techniques for robot perception, localization, and navigation
   - Applications of robotics in industries such as manufacturing, healthcare, and agriculture

4. Recommender Systems
   - Understanding recommender systems and their applications
   - Basic concepts of recommender systems such as collaborative filtering and content-based filtering
   - Techniques for building and evaluating recommender systems
   - Applications of recommender systems in industries such as e-commerce, entertainment, and social media

5. Speech Recognition
   - Understanding speech recognition and its applications
   - Basic concepts of speech recognition such as acoustic modeling and language modeling
   - Techniques for speech recognition using Hidden Markov Models and Deep Neural Networks
   - Applications of speech recognition in industries such as healthcare, education, and customer service

In conclusion, this unit will provide you with an understanding of the various applications of AI and ML in different industries. You will also learn the fundamental concepts and techniques used in these applications.
