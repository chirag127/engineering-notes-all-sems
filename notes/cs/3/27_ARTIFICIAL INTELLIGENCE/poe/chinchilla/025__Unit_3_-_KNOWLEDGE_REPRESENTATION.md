## Unit 3 - KNOWLEDGE REPRESENTATION

Knowledge representation is an important area in the field of Artificial Intelligence. It involves the process of encoding knowledge so that it can be used by machines to perform various tasks. In this unit, we will learn about the different techniques used for knowledge representation.

### 1. Propositional Logic

Propositional Logic is a form of symbolic logic that deals with propositions or statements that are either true or false. It is used to represent knowledge in a formal way, where a statement can be represented as a logical formula. Propositional Logic is widely used in AI for reasoning and decision-making.

### 2. First-Order Logic

First-Order Logic is an extension of Propositional Logic that deals with objects and their relationships. It is used to represent more complex knowledge, where objects can have attributes and relationships with other objects. First-Order Logic is also known as Predicate Logic and is widely used in AI for knowledge representation and reasoning.

### 3. Frames and Semantic Networks

Frames and Semantic Networks are graphical representations used for knowledge representation. Frames are used to represent objects and their attributes, while Semantic Networks are used to represent relationships between objects. Frames and Semantic Networks are used in AI for knowledge representation and reasoning.

### 4. Ontologies

Ontologies are formal representations of knowledge that define a set of concepts and the relationships between them. They are used to capture knowledge in a specific domain and are widely used in AI for knowledge representation and reasoning.

### 5. Rule-Based Systems

Rule-Based Systems are used to represent knowledge in the form of rules. These rules are used to make decisions or perform actions based on the input data. Rule-Based Systems are widely used in AI for expert systems and decision-making.

### 6. Case-Based Reasoning

Case-Based Reasoning is a knowledge representation technique that involves storing and retrieving cases from a case library. A case represents a problem, and the solution to the problem is stored in the case library. Case-Based Reasoning is widely used in AI for problem-solving and decision-making.

In conclusion, knowledge representation is an essential part of Artificial Intelligence. Different techniques are used for knowledge representation, depending on the complexity of the problem. Understanding the different techniques used for knowledge representation is important for developing intelligent systems that can perform various tasks.
