### Informed for the notes of the Unit 2 - PROBLEM SOLVING METHODS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

In this unit, we will be discussing various methods used for problem-solving in Artificial Intelligence. These methods are designed to help machines make decisions and solve complex problems in a more efficient and effective manner. Below are some of the key points you should take note of:

1. Informed search algorithms: These are search algorithms that use heuristic information to guide the search process. They are more efficient than uninformed search algorithms since they use domain-specific knowledge to guide the search. Examples of informed search algorithms include A* search and Best-first search.

2. Heuristics: These are domain-specific knowledge used by informed search algorithms to guide the search process. Heuristics are estimates of the cost of reaching the goal from a given state. The better the heuristic function, the more efficient the search algorithm.

3. Admissible heuristics: These are heuristics that never overestimate the cost of reaching the goal from a given state. They are essential in ensuring that the search algorithm finds the optimal solution.

4. Consistent heuristics: These are heuristics that satisfy the triangle inequality property. This property ensures that the heuristic estimate is consistent with the actual cost of reaching the goal.

5. Hill climbing algorithm: This is a local search algorithm that starts at a random point and moves to the neighboring state with the highest heuristic value until it reaches a local maximum. It is simple and efficient but can get stuck at local maxima.

6. Simulated annealing algorithm: This is a variant of hill climbing algorithm that allows for occasional uphill moves to avoid getting stuck at local maxima. It uses a probability distribution to decide whether to accept or reject a move to a worse state.

7. Genetic algorithms: These are search algorithms that mimic the process of natural selection to find the optimal solution. They use a population of potential solutions and apply genetic operators such as mutation and crossover to create new solutions. The fittest solutions are selected for the next generation.

8. Constraint satisfaction problems: These are problems where the solution must satisfy a set of constraints. Examples include scheduling problems and Sudoku puzzles. Constraint satisfaction algorithms use techniques such as backtracking and constraint propagation to find the solution.

By understanding these methods, you will be better equipped to solve complex problems using Artificial Intelligence. It is important to note that different problems may require different methods, and it is up to the problem solver to choose the most appropriate one.
