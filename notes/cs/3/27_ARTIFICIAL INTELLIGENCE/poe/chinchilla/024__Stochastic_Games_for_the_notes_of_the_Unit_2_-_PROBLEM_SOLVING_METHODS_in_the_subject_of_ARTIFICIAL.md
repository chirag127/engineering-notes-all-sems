### Stochastic Games

Stochastic games are a type of game in which the outcomes of actions taken by players are uncertain. In these games, the players not only have to consider the actions they take but also the possible outcomes of those actions. These games are commonly used in the field of artificial intelligence to model situations where multiple agents interact with each other in an uncertain environment.

Here are some key concepts related to stochastic games:

- **Players:** Stochastic games involve multiple players who interact with each other. Each player has a set of actions they can take, and the actions they take affect the outcomes of the game.
- **States:** A state is a particular configuration of the game, which includes the actions taken by the players and the current state of the environment. In stochastic games, the state of the game can be uncertain, and the players have to consider the possible states the game can be in.
- **Rewards:** The players in a stochastic game are motivated by rewards, which are assigned based on the outcome of the game. The rewards can be positive or negative, and the players aim to maximize their rewards.
- **Transition Function:** The transition function determines how the game moves from one state to another based on the actions taken by the players. In a stochastic game, the transition function can be uncertain, and the players have to consider the possible transitions that can occur.
- **Policy:** A policy is a strategy that determines the actions a player takes in a given state. In a stochastic game, a player's policy has to take into account the uncertainty of the game and the possible outcomes of their actions.
- **Nash Equilibrium:** A Nash equilibrium is a state of the game where no player can improve their reward by changing their strategy, given the strategies of the other players. In a stochastic game, the Nash equilibrium can be uncertain, and the players have to consider the possible equilibria that can occur.

Stochastic games are used in various applications, such as robotics, economics, and game theory. In artificial intelligence, stochastic games are used to model situations where multiple agents interact with each other in an uncertain environment. Stochastic games provide a framework for studying the behavior of agents in such situations and developing strategies for optimizing their rewards.
