# KCS

Knowledge-Centered Service (KCS) is an approach to providing technical support that emphasizes building and using knowledge resources. Some key aspects of KCS include:

1. Knowledge is separate from documents or articles. Knowledge consists of concepts and the relationships between them.
2. Knowledge is created and evolved through use. As support personnel answer questions, they capture the knowledge uncovered or created and feed it back into the knowledge base.
3. The primary deliverable is useful and useable knowledge, not documents. The knowledge is structured to be readily searched andconsumed in the course of delivering service.
4. Knowledge management is an explicit part of day-to-day work. Creating and improving the knowledge base is a natural and ongoing part of providing service.
5. The knowledge base evolves over time to expand coverage, improve accuracy and effectiveness, and keep content current.

The key benefits of a KCS approach are faster, more consistent, and higher quality service based on a continuously expanding and improving knowledge resource.

The major challenges for implementing KCS include:

- Encouraging and enabling service personnel to capture and contribute knowledge
- Developing an effective knowledge structure and content management approach
- Ongoing effort required to review, improve, and keep knowledge up-to-date
- Managing knowledge base scope and complexity to keep content findable and usable
