### Knowledge Representation for the notes of the Unit 3 - KNOWLEDGE REPRESENTATION in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

1. Knowledge Representation refers to the methods used to represent knowledge in a form that can be used by machines such as computers. It is about encoding concepts and relationships about the world in a logical form.
2. The key tasks of Knowledge Representation are:
- Ontology development: Developing a vocabulary of terms and relations.
- Formalization: Converting the knowledge into formal language.
- Reasoning: Drawing inferences and deriving new knowledge.
3. Some of the key methods of Knowledge Representation are:
- Logic-based methods: Propositions and rules. Example: Propositional and First-Order Logic.
- Frame-based methods: Representing knowledge in terms of frames. Example: Frames, Scripts.
- Semantic networks: Representing concepts and relationships in a graph structure.
- Conceptual graphs
- Neural networks and Deep Learning methods
