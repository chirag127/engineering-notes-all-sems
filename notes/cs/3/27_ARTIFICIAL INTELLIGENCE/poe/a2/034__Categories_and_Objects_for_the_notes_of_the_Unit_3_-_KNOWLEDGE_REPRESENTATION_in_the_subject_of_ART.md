### Categories and Objects for the notes of the Unit 3 - KNOWLEDGE REPRESENTATION in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS:

1. Categories:
- Classes or types of objects/entities.
- Abstract objects like actions, events, etc represented by verbs can also be categories.
- Examples: Bird, Person, Run, Eat, etc.

2. Objects:
- Instances of categories.
- Specific entities that are members of a category.
- Examples: Sparrow, John, Walking, Apple, etc.

3. IS-A Relationship:
- Also known as hyponymy or hypernymy.
- Specifies that one category is a subclass of another category.
- Examples: Sparrow IS-A Bird, John IS-A Person.

4. Part-Whole Relationship:
- Specifies that one object consists of other objects or is a component of a whole object.
- Examples: Wheel IS-PART-OF Car, Heart IS-PART-OF Body.

5. Other Relationships:
- Cause-Effect: Fire IS-CAUSED-BY Spark
- Temporal: Dawn IS-BEFORE Day
- Spatial: Above IS-ABOVE Below
- etc.
