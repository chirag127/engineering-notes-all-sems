### Definition for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

1. Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the simulation of human intelligence in machines like computers.
2. It is a branch of computer science that aims to create intelligent machines that can learn, reason, and carry out tasks like humans.
3. The main characteristics of AI are:
    - Machine Learning: The ability of a system to learn and act without being explicitly programmed.
    - Natural Language Processing: The ability of a system to analyze, understand and generate human language.
    - Computer Vision: The ability of a system to identify and process images in the same way that humans do.
    - Robotics: The design and construction of robots that can perform tasks automatically.
4. The goals of AI are:
    - Make machines smarter and more capable.
    - Automate tasks and enhance productivity.
    - Gain insights from data and make accurate predictions.
    - Create intelligent machines that can assist and augment human capabilities.
5. The applications of AI are:
    - Transportation: Self-driving cars, automated route planning.
    - Healthcare: Diagnosing diseases, robotic surgery, virtual nursing assistants.
    - Education: Personalized learning, automated grading and feedback, interactive tutoring systems.
    - Finance: Automated trading, fraud detection, personalized financial advising.
    - Business: Automated assistants, personalized recommendations, optimized scheduling and logistics.
    - Environment: Monitoring wildlife, predicting natural disasters, optimizing energy usage.
