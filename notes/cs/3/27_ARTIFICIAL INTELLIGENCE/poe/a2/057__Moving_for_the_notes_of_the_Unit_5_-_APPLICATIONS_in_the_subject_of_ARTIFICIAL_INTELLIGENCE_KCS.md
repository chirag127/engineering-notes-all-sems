### Machine Learning Applications

1. Pattern Recognition - Recognizing patterns in images, speech, text data, etc. Examples: Image classification, Speech recognition, Machine translation, etc.
2. Classification - Identifying the category an object or instance belongs to. Examples: Spam detection, Sentiment analysis, Recommendation systems, etc.
3. Regression - Estimating a quantitative value. Examples: Stock price prediction, Real estate price prediction, etc.
4. Clustering - Grouping similar data instances together. Examples: Customer segmentation, Gene sequence analysis, etc.
5. Anomaly Detection - Identifying abnormal or unusual data instances. Examples: Fraud detection, Medical diagnosis, Fault detection, etc.

### Expert Systems

1. Mimic human expertise in a limited domain.
2. Use IF-THEN rules and inference engine.
3. Apply knowledge and inferences to solve problems.
4. Examples: Medical diagnosis, Technical troubleshooting, Financial analysis, etc.

### Robotics
