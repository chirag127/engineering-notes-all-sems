### Natural Language Processing for the notes of the Unit 5 - APPLICATIONS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

1. Natural Language Processing (NLP) is a field of artificial intelligence that focuses on the interaction between computers and humans using the natural language.
2. The major tasks of NLP are:
 - Text Segmentation: Splitting text into sentences and words.
 - Part-of-speech Tagging: Tagging words with their corresponding part of speech.
 - Named Entity Recognition: Identifying and classifying named entities in the text into pre-defined categories such as persons, organizations, locations, etc.
 - Sentiment Analysis: Determining the sentiment expressed in a piece of text, whether positive, negative or neutral.
 - Relationship Extraction: Identifying relationships between entities in the text.
 - Question Answering: Answering questions posed by humans in a natural language.
3. Some applications of NLP are:
 - Machine Translation: Translating text from one human language to another.
 - Text Summarization: Producing a concise summary of a piece of text.
 - Speech Recognition: Converting speech to text.
 - Spam Detection: Identifying and filtering spam emails and messages.
 - Chatbots: Software programs that can have basic conversations with humans through text or speech.
4. The goal of NLP is to enable efficient and robust processing of human language data and to provide interfaces to systems and services through natural language. NLP techniques are bringing significant and impressive improvements to various applications incorporating AI.
