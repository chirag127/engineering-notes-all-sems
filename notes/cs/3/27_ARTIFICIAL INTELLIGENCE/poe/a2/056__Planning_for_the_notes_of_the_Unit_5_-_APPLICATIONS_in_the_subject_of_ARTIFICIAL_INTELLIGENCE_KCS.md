### Planning for the notes of the Unit 5 - APPLICATIONS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

1. Introduction to AI Applications
- Expert Systems - Applications that contain stored knowledge and infer new conclusions from the knowledge. Examples: Medical diagnosis, Technical troubleshooting, etc.
- Robotics - Design and construction of robots that can perform tasks automatically. Examples: Industrial robots, Self-driving cars, etc.
- Computer Vision - Automatic analysis and understanding of images and videos. Examples: Facial recognition, Object recognition, etc.
- Natural Language Processing - Automatic processing and understanding of human language. Examples: Machine translation, Sentiment analysis, Speech recognition, etc.

2. Search and Optimization Problems
- Problems which involve finding the best solution from a number of possible solutions.
- Examples: Travelling Salesman Problem, Job-shop Scheduling, etc. Search techniques like Hill Climbing, Beam Search, etc. can be used to solve them.

3. Machine Learning Applications
- Classification - Predicting the class/category of given data. Examples: Spam detection, Image classification, etc.
- Regression - Predicting a continuous value. Examples: Stock price prediction, etc.
- Clustering - Grouping similar data points. Examples: Customer segmentation, etc.
- Anomaly Detection - Identifying abnormal data points. Examples: Fraud detection, etc.
- Recommender Systems - Predicting users' preferences and recommending products. Examples: Product recommendations, etc.

4. Conclusion
- AI has a wide range of applications in various fields. As AI continues to advance, more applications are being developed to solve complex problems and add value to businesses and society.
