### Hardware for the notes of the Unit 5 - APPLICATIONS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

1. CPUs: Central Processing Units are the primary components that execute the instructions of a computer program. CPUs for AI applications require high processing power and speed to train complex machine learning models.

2. GPUs: Graphics Processing Units handle intensive display tasks and are more powerful than CPUs for parallel processing. They are widely used for training deep learning and neural network models. Some of the popular GPUs for AI include NVIDIA Tesla and AMD Radeon.

3. TPUs: Tensor Processing Units are specialized AI accelerators designed by Google for neural network training. They are application-specific integrated circuits (ASICs) optimized for TensorFlow operations. TPUs are more powerful and efficient than GPUs for training and inference of machine learning models.

4. FPGAs: Field-Programmable Gate Arrays are semiconductor chips that can be programmed after manufacturing to create custom digital circuits. They provide high performance and parallelism and can be optimized for specific AI workloads. However, they are more difficult to program compared to CPUs and GPUs. Some companies that manufacture FPGAs include Xilinx and Intel.
