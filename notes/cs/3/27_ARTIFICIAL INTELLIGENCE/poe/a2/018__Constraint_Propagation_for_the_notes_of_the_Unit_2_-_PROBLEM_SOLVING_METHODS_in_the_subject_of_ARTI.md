### Constraint Propagation for the notes of the Unit 2 - PROBLEM SOLVING METHODS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

1. Constraint propagation is a technique where constraints are iteratively applied to deduce new constraint deductions. 
2. It reduces the search space by detecting and removing inconsistent variable instantiations. 
3. The three main methods of constraint propagation are:
- Forward checking: Applies constraints to deduce values of other variables and detects inconsistencies.
- Arc consistency: Ensures that every value has a support for at least one neighbor. Inconsistent values are detected and removed.
- Mackworth's algorithm: Combines forward checking and arc consistency. It is more powerful but complex.
4. Constraint propagation terminates when no more deductions can be made or inconsistencies are detected. If the problem contains only unary and binary constraints, the process is guaranteed to terminate.
5. The efficiency of constraint satisfaction problems can be greatly improved by using constraint propagation as a preprocessing step to reduce the search space.
