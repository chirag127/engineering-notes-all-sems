### Heuristics for the notes of the Unit 2 - PROBLEM SOLVING METHODS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

1. Hill Climbing
- It is a simple local search technique.
- It starts with an initial solution/state and then attempts to find a better solution/state by making incremental changes to the current solution.
- It stops when no improvement is possible by changing one component.
- It may get stuck in local optima.

2. Simulated Annealing
- It is a technique inspired by the metallurgical process of annealing.
- It avoids getting stuck in local optima by allowing downhill moves with a decreasing probability.
- It has two parameters:
-- Initial Temperature: Higher temperature leads to higher probability of downhill moves.
-- Cooling Schedule: The rate at which the temperature is decreased.
- The process terminates when the temperature reaches a minimum value.

3. Genetic Algorithms
- They are inspired by natural evolution and genetics.
- They work on a population of solutions and not just a single solution.
- The solutions are represented using chromosomes which are encoded strings of values.
- They involve processes like selection, crossover, and mutation to evolve the population towards optimal solutions.
- The fitness of each solution is evaluated using a fitness function.
- They are more robust than hill climbing and simulated annealing and can avoid local optima.
