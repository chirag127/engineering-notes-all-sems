### Information Retrieval for the notes of the Unit 5 - APPLICATIONS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS.

1. Information Retrieval (IR) is the task of retrieving relevant documents from a document collection in response to a user query.
2. The key steps in an IR system are:
- Indexing: Constructing an index of the document collection. This index maps terms/phrases to the documents that contain them.
- Query Processing: Processing the user query and using the index to find relevant documents.
3. Evaluation measures for IR systems include:
- Precision: Proportion of retrieved documents that are relevant.
- Recall: Proportion of relevant documents that are retrieved.
- F1-score: Harmonic mean of precision and recall.
4. IR models include:
- Vector Space Model: Documents and queries are represented as vectors of terms. Similarity is computed based on the angle between vectors.
- Probabilistic Model: The probability of a document given a query is estimated and documents are ranked by this probability.
- Language Models: Documents are modeled as probability distributions over terms/phrases. The likelihood of a document generating a query is used for ranking.
5. Modern IR systems incorporate aspects such as:
- Term weighting: Assigning importance scores to terms in queries and documents.
- Query expansion: Including related terms in the query to retrieve more relevant documents.
- Named entity recognition: Identifying and handling named entities in queries and documents.
- Learning to rank: Using machine learning to learn a ranking function that optimizes evaluation measures.
