### Negotiation and Bargaining for the notes of the Unit 4 - SOFTWARE AGENTS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS.

- Negotiation is a dialogue between two or more parties intended to reach an agreement to exchange goods, services, or resources.
- Bargaining is a narrower type of negotiation in which the parties are trying to agree on a price or other specific terms of an exchange.
- In negotiation and bargaining, each party has a zone of possible agreement that represents the outcomes it would find acceptable. The parties seek outcomes in the overlap of their ZOPAs.
- Strategies used in negotiation and bargaining include:
-- Claiming value to enlarge your ZOPA
-- Starting with an extreme position to leave room for compromise
-- Using facts and objective criteria to support your positions
-- Building rapport and trust
-- Claiming limited resources to justify your position
-- Bluffing or threatening to gain leverage
-- Recognizing and exploiting the other party's priorities and interests
- In automated negotiation and bargaining, software agents use strategies like these to represent their users' or organizations' interests. Machine learning can help agents learn effective strategies from experience.
