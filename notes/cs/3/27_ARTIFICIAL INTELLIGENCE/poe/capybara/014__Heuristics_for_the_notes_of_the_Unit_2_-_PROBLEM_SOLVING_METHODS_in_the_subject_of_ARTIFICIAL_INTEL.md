### Heuristics for the notes of the Unit 2 - PROBLEM SOLVING METHODS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

Here are some important heuristics to keep in mind while taking notes for the Unit 2 - Problem Solving Methods in the subject of Artificial Intelligence:

- **Understand the problem:** Before attempting to solve a problem, it is important to understand the problem statement and its requirements. Make sure to read the problem statement carefully and ask questions if you have any doubts.
- **Divide and conquer:** Break down the problem into smaller sub-problems that are easier to solve. This will help you to tackle the problem in a step-by-step manner and make it more manageable.
- **Use abstraction:** Identify the important features of the problem and ignore the irrelevant details. This will help you to simplify the problem and focus on the key aspects that are necessary for solving it.
- **Look for patterns:** Try to identify any patterns or similarities between the problem and other problems that you have solved in the past. This can help you to apply the same solution or approach to the current problem.
- **Use analogies:** Draw analogies between the problem and other domains. This can help you to gain a fresh perspective on the problem and come up with new ideas or solutions.
- **Visualize the problem:** Use diagrams, flowcharts, or other visual aids to represent the problem and its solution. This can help you to understand the problem better and communicate your ideas more effectively.
- **Iterate and refine:** Solve the problem iteratively, refining your solution at each step. This will help you to improve the quality of your solution and make it more efficient.

By following these heuristics, you can take effective notes and develop a better understanding of the problem-solving methods in Artificial Intelligence.
