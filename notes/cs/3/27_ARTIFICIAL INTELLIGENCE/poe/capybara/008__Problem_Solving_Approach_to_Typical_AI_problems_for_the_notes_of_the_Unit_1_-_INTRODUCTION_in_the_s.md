### Problem Solving Approach to Typical AI Problems

In the field of Artificial Intelligence, there are a number of typical problems that can be solved using a problem-solving approach. Here are some common AI problems and how they can be approached:

1. **Search Problems:** These are problems where an AI system needs to find a solution to a problem by searching through a large number of possibilities. The problem-solving approach to search problems involves breaking down the problem into smaller sub-problems and using algorithms such as breadth-first search, depth-first search, or A* search to efficiently search through the possibilities.

2. **Constraint Satisfaction Problems:** These are problems where an AI system needs to find a solution that satisfies a set of constraints. The problem-solving approach to constraint satisfaction problems involves breaking down the problem into smaller sub-problems and using algorithms such as backtracking, forward checking, or constraint propagation to efficiently search for a solution that satisfies all the constraints.

3. **Planning Problems:** These are problems where an AI system needs to plan a sequence of actions to achieve a goal. The problem-solving approach to planning problems involves breaking down the problem into smaller sub-problems and using algorithms such as heuristic search, state-space search, or decision trees to efficiently search for a sequence of actions that will achieve the goal.

4. **Expert Systems:** These are systems that emulate the decision-making abilities of a human expert in a particular domain. The problem-solving approach to expert systems involves breaking down the problem into smaller sub-problems and using knowledge representation techniques such as rules or frames to efficiently reason about the problem domain.

5. **Machine Learning Problems:** These are problems where an AI system needs to learn from data to improve its performance on a particular task. The problem-solving approach to machine learning problems involves breaking down the problem into smaller sub-problems and using algorithms such as decision trees, neural networks, or support vector machines to efficiently learn from the data.

In conclusion, the problem-solving approach is a powerful tool for solving typical AI problems. By breaking down complex problems into smaller sub-problems and using efficient algorithms, AI systems can solve a wide range of problems in various domains.
