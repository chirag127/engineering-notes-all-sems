### Problem Solving Methods for the notes of the Unit 2 - PROBLEM SOLVING METHODS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

Problem solving is an essential part of artificial intelligence, and different methods have been developed to solve different types of problems. Here are some of the problem-solving methods used in artificial intelligence:

1. Brute Force Search: This method involves trying every possible solution until the correct one is found. This method is useful for small problems, but it can take a lot of time and resources for larger problems.

2. Heuristic Search: This method involves using a set of rules or guidelines to narrow down the search space and find a solution more quickly. This method is useful for larger problems and can save time and resources.

3. Hill Climbing: This method involves starting with an initial solution and then improving it by making small changes. This method is useful for optimization problems, but it can get stuck in local optima.

4. Genetic Algorithms: This method involves using evolutionary principles to find a solution. It starts with a population of possible solutions and then uses selection, mutation, and reproduction to generate new solutions. This method is useful for complex problems, but it can take a lot of time and resources.

5. Constraint Satisfaction: This method involves finding a solution that satisfies a set of constraints. It is useful for problems where some variables have to be set to specific values.

6. Divide and Conquer: This method involves breaking a problem down into smaller sub-problems and solving them independently. It is useful for complex problems that can be broken down into smaller parts.

7. Backtracking: This method involves trying out different solutions and undoing them if they don't work. It is useful for problems where there are many possible solutions.

In conclusion, these methods are useful for solving different types of problems in artificial intelligence. The choice of method depends on the nature of the problem and the resources available. By understanding these methods, one can become a better problem solver in the field of artificial intelligence.
