
### Events for the notes of the Unit 3 - KNOWLEDGE REPRESENTATION in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

1. The first modern computer, ENIAC, was developed in 1946.
2. In 1956, John McCarthy coined the term "artificial intelligence" at the Dartmouth Conference.
3. In 1966, the first AI chess program was created by Arthur Samuel.
4. In 1969, the first expert system, DENDRAL, was developed by Edward Feigenbaum and Bruce Buchanan.
5. In 1974, the first AI robot, Shakey, was created by the Stanford Research Institute.
6. In 1980, the first AI natural language processing system, ELIZA, was developed by Joseph Weizenbaum.
7. In 1985, the first AI machine learning system, EMYCIN, was developed by Edward Feigenbaum and Bruce Buchanan.
8. In 1997, the first AI computer vision system, Deep Blue, was developed by IBM.
9. In 2011, IBM's Watson won the Jeopardy! Challenge.
10. In 2014, Google's DeepMind AlphaGo defeated the world champion of the game Go.
