
### Alpha for the notes of the Unit 2 - PROBLEM SOLVING METHODS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

1. Problem Solving is an important part of Artificial Intelligence. It is the process of finding solutions to complex problems by using algorithms and heuristics.

2. Problem solving methods can be divided into two categories: Exact and Heuristic. Exact methods are used to solve problems by finding an exact solution, while Heuristic methods are used to find approximate solutions.

3. Exact methods include algorithms such as Breadth-first search, Depth-first search, Best-first search, and Hill-climbing. Each of these algorithms has its own strengths and weaknesses.

4. Heuristic methods include Genetic Algorithms, Simulated Annealing, and Tabu Search. These methods are used to find approximate solutions to complex problems.

5. In addition to algorithms and heuristics, problem solving methods also include search techniques such as Constraint Satisfaction and Local Search. These techniques are used to find solutions that satisfy certain constraints.

6. Problem solving methods are used in Artificial Intelligence to solve a wide range of problems, from game playing to robotics. They are also used in many other fields, such as engineering, economics, and medicine.
