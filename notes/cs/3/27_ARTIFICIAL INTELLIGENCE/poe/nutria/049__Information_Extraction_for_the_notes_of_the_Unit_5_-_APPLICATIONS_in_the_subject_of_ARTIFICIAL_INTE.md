

### Information Extraction for the notes of the Unit 5 - APPLICATIONS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

* Information extraction is the process of extracting structured information from unstructured text. It is a core component of many Artificial Intelligence (AI) applications, such as natural language processing (NLP), machine learning (ML), and knowledge engineering (KE).
* Information extraction techniques can be applied to a variety of text sources, including web pages, emails, documents, and social media posts.
* Common applications of information extraction include entity recognition and relation extraction, which are used to identify and extract entities (e.g., people, places, organizations) and relationships between them (e.g., works for, owns).
* Information extraction can also be used to extract facts from text, such as dates, numbers, and other structured data. This data can then be used to create knowledge graphs or other structured data sets.
* Information extraction is also used to extract opinions and sentiment from text, which can be used for sentiment analysis or opinion mining.
* In addition, information extraction can be used to detect and classify events, such as news events or stock market events. This can be used for event detection and forecasting.
