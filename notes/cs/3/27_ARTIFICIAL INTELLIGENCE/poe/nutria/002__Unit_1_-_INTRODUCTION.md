
## Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION

1. Introduction is the first step in understanding a topic. It provides an overview of the material that will be discussed in the following sections.
2. It is important to understand the context of the material before diving deeper into the content.
3. Introduction should provide a brief overview of the main topics that will be discussed, as well as the purpose of the material.
4. It should also provide an overview of the key concepts and terminology that will be used throughout the material.
5. Lastly, introduction should provide a clear structure of the material, such as a table of contents or a diagram.
