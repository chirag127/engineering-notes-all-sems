
### Argumentation among Agents

Argumentation among agents is a process of making decisions using a form of artificial intelligence (AI) known as argumentation. In this process, agents interact with each other to come to a consensus on a certain issue or decision. Argumentation is a powerful tool for decision-making, as it allows agents to reason and negotiate with each other in order to reach an agreement.

Argumentation among agents can be used in a variety of different contexts, including:

* Automated negotiation: Agents can use argumentation to negotiate with each other to reach an agreement on a certain issue or decision.
* Decision-making: Agents can use argumentation to make decisions based on the evidence they have collected and their own reasoning.
* Problem-solving: Agents can use argumentation to solve problems by considering different possibilities and coming to a consensus.

Argumentation among agents is an important part of artificial intelligence and is used in many different applications. It is a powerful tool for decision-making, as it allows agents to reason and negotiate with each other in order to reach an agreement.
