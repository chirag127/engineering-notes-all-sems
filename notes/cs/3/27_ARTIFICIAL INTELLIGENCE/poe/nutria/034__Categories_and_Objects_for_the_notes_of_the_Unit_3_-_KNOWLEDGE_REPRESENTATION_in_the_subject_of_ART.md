* Knowledge representation is the process of representing knowledge in a form that can be used by computers and machines to make decisions.
* Knowledge representation techniques include formal logic, frames, semantic networks, and ontologies.
* Formal logic is a way of representing knowledge in a formal language.
* Frames are a way of representing knowledge as a set of objects, attributes, and relationships.
* Semantic networks are a way of representing knowledge as a graph of nodes connected by edges.
* Ontologies are a way of representing knowledge as a set of classes, objects, and relationships.
* Knowledge representation can be used for natural language processing, automated reasoning, and machine learning.
