
### Prolog Programming for the Notes of the Unit 3 - Knowledge Representation in the Subject of Artificial Intelligence KCS

* Prolog is a logic programming language used for artificial intelligence and computational linguistics. 
* It is based on the concept of a relational database, where facts and rules about objects are stored. 
* Prolog has a declarative syntax, meaning that the programmer specifies what the program should do, not how it should be done. 
* Prolog is used for knowledge representation, which is the process of representing knowledge in a structured way. 
* Knowledge representation in Prolog involves representing facts and rules about objects as facts and rules in a relational database. 
* Prolog has a variety of built-in predicates that can be used to represent facts and rules. 
* Prolog also has a built-in query language that can be used to query the database and retrieve information. 
* Prolog programs can be written in a procedural or declarative style, depending on the programmer's preference. 
* Prolog programs can also be written using object-oriented programming techniques. 
* Prolog is often used in natural language processing and artificial intelligence applications.
