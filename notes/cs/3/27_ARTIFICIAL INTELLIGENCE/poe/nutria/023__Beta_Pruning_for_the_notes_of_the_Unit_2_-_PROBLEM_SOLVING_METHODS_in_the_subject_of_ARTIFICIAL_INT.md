
### Beta Pruning

Beta Pruning is a problem-solving technique used in Artificial Intelligence (AI). It is a type of heuristic search that is used to reduce the search space of a problem. It is used in situations where the solution is not known, and the goal is to reach a goal state.

Beta Pruning works by using a heuristic to evaluate the possible states of a problem. It then eliminates states that do not appear to be promising. This reduces the number of states that need to be considered, and thus reduces the time required to find a solution.

Beta Pruning can be used in a variety of AI applications, such as game playing, natural language processing, and robotics. It is also used in decision making and planning systems.

Beta Pruning is an effective problem-solving technique that can reduce the time required to find a solution to a problem. It is important to note, however, that it is not always the best approach, and other methods may be more effective in certain situations.
