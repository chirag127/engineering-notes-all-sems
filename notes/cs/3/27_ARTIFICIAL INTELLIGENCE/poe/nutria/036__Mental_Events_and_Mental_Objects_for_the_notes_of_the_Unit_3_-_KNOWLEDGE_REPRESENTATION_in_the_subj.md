
### Mental Events and Mental Objects 

* Mental events are events that occur in the mind, such as thoughts, feelings, and intentions. They are typically experienced as subjective and private, and are not directly observable. 
* Mental objects are representations of physical and abstract entities, such as concepts, ideas, and memories. They are stored in the mind and can be used to reason and make decisions. 
* Knowledge representation is the process of representing knowledge in a form that can be used by a computer system. It is a key component of Artificial Intelligence (AI) and is used to enable AI systems to reason, learn, and make decisions. 
* Knowledge representation is typically divided into two main categories: symbolic and sub-symbolic. Symbolic representation is based on symbols, such as words and numbers, while sub-symbolic representation is based on patterns and relationships. 
* Symbolic knowledge representation is used to represent facts, rules, and other types of declarative knowledge. It is commonly used in expert systems and rule-based systems. 
* Sub-symbolic knowledge representation is used to represent non-declarative knowledge, such as relationships, patterns, and analogies. It is commonly used in neural networks and evolutionary algorithms. 
* Knowledge representation is a key component of AI systems and is used to enable them to reason, learn, and make decisions. It is used to store and manipulate knowledge in a form that can be used by AI systems.
