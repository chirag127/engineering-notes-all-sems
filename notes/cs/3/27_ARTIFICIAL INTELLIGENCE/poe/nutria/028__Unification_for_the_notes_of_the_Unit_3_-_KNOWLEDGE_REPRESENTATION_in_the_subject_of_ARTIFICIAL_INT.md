
### Unification for the Notes of Unit 3 - Knowledge Representation in the Subject of Artificial Intelligence KCS

1. Unification is a process used in Artificial Intelligence (AI) to determine the meaning of a sentence by finding the most general form of a set of sentences.

2. Unification is a form of reasoning which allows two structures to be compared and unified if they are found to be logically equivalent.

3. Unification is used to represent knowledge in AI systems, as it allows for logical inference and deduction.

4. Unification algorithms are used to determine if two structures are equivalent, and if so, to construct a unified form of the two structures.

5. Unification algorithms are used to construct a knowledge representation language, which is used to represent facts, rules and other knowledge in an AI system.

6. Unification algorithms are used in natural language processing systems to determine the meaning of sentences, by determining the most general form of a set of sentences.
