
### Typical Intelligent Agents

1. **Expert Systems**: Expert systems are computer programs designed to solve complex problems by reasoning about knowledge, just like a human expert. They are built around a knowledge base of facts and rules, and use reasoning and search techniques to answer questions and solve problems.

2. **Natural Language Processing (NLP)**: Natural language processing is a field of computer science that focuses on enabling computers to understand and process human language. It can be used to answer questions, provide advice, and even generate complete conversations.

3. **Robotics**: Robotics is the science of designing, constructing, and operating robots. Robots are often used in applications such as manufacturing, search and rescue, and space exploration.

4. **Neural Networks**: Neural networks are a type of artificial intelligence that uses a network of interconnected nodes to process data. They are used in a variety of applications, including speech recognition and image recognition.

5. **Machine Learning**: Machine learning is a field of artificial intelligence that focuses on enabling computers to learn from data. It is used in a variety of applications, from predicting stock prices to recognizing faces.
