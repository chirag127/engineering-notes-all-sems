
## Unit 3 - KNOWLEDGE REPRESENTATION

1. Knowledge representation is the process of encoding knowledge in a form that can be used by machines. This is done by creating a representation of the information that can be understood by computers.

2. Knowledge representation is used in artificial intelligence (AI) and machine learning (ML) applications to store and manipulate data. It can also be used to create models of the world, which can then be used to make decisions and predictions.

3. Knowledge representation can be done in many different ways, such as semantic networks, frames, and ontologies. Each of these has its own advantages and disadvantages.

4. Semantic networks are a form of knowledge representation that uses nodes and links to represent knowledge. Nodes represent concepts, while links represent relationships between concepts.

5. Frames are another form of knowledge representation that uses frames to represent knowledge. A frame is a collection of facts, rules, and procedures about a particular topic.

6. Ontologies are a form of knowledge representation that uses a hierarchical structure to represent knowledge. Ontologies are useful for representing complex knowledge in a structured way.

7. Knowledge representation is an important part of AI and ML applications. It is used to store and manipulate data, create models of the world, and make decisions and predictions.
