### Negotiation and Bargaining for the notes of the Unit 4 - SOFTWARE AGENTS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

Negotiation and Bargaining:
- Negotiation and bargaining are processes used by software agents to reach agreements with other agents.
- Negotiation and bargaining are used in a variety of applications, including electronic commerce, resource allocation, and conflict resolution.
- In negotiation and bargaining, agents exchange information and make offers and counter-offers in an attempt to reach a mutually acceptable agreement.
- Negotiation and bargaining can be modeled as a game, where the outcome depends on the strategies and objectives of the agents involved.
- Negotiation and bargaining algorithms can be based on a variety of techniques, including utility theory, game theory, and decision theory.
- Understanding negotiation and bargaining is important for the design and development of software agents that can interact effectively with other agents and for the implementation of effective negotiation and bargaining algorithms.
