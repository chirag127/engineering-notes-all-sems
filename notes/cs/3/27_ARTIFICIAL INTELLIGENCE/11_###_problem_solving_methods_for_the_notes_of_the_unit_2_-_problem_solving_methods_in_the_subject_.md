### Problem solving Methods for the notes of the Unit 2 - PROBLEM SOLVING METHODS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

Problem Solving Methods:
- Problem solving methods are techniques used to solve problems in artificial intelligence.
- There are several types of problem solving methods, including:
  1. Search: involves exploring a space of possible solutions to find a solution that satisfies the problem constraints.
  2. Constraint Satisfaction Problems (CSPs): involves finding a solution that satisfies a set of constraints.
  3. Stochastic Games: involves making decisions in an environment where the outcome of each decision depends on the actions of other agents and on random events.
  4. Heuristics: involves using rules of thumb or shortcuts to find a solution that is good enough, even if it is not optimal.
  5. Optimization: involves finding the best solution from a set of possible solutions.
- The choice of problem solving method depends on the specific problem being solved and the requirements of the application.
- Understanding problem solving methods is important for the design and development of effective problem-solving algorithms and for the implementation of AI systems that can solve complex problems.
