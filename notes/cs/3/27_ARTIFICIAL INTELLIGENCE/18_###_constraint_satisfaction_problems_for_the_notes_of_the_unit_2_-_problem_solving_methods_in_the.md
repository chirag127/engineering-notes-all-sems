### Constraint Satisfaction Problems for the notes of the Unit 2 - PROBLEM SOLVING METHODS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

Constraint Satisfaction Problems (CSPs):
- Constraint Satisfaction Problems (CSPs) are problems in which the solution must satisfy a set of constraints.
- CSPs are used to model a wide range of problems, including scheduling, resource allocation, and configuration problems.
- In a CSP, the solution is represented as a set of variables, and the constraints specify the relationships between the variables.
- CSPs can be solved using a variety of methods, including backtracking, branch-and-bound, and local search.
- The choice of solution method depends on the specific problem and the requirements of the application.
- Understanding CSPs is important for the design and development of AI systems that can solve problems with constraints and for the implementation of effective constraint satisfaction algorithms.
