Searching with Partial Observations

Searching with partial observations is a problem-solving technique in Artificial Intelligence where the agent must make decisions based on incomplete or uncertain information. In searching with partial observations, the agent must make decisions based on the limited information available to it. This requires the agent to use probabilistic methods, such as Bayesian networks or Markov decision processes, to estimate the state of the environment and to make decisions based on that estimate.
