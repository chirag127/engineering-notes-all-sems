### Prolog Programming for the notes of the Unit 3 - KNOWLEDGE REPRESENTATION in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

Prolog Programming:
- Prolog is a programming language that is designed for knowledge representation and reasoning.
- Prolog is based on formal logic and is well-suited for representing and reasoning about complex relationships between objects and events.
- In Prolog, knowledge is represented as a set of rules and facts, and reasoning is performed by searching for a proof of a query using these rules and facts.
- Prolog is widely used in AI and has been applied to a variety of problems, including natural language processing, expert systems, and planning.
- Prolog is a declarative language, which means that the programmer specifies what the solution should be, rather than how to find the solution.
- Understanding Prolog is important for the design and development of AI systems that can reason about complex relationships and for the implementation of knowledge representation and reasoning algorithms.
