### Characteristics of Intelligent Agents for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

Characteristics of Intelligent Agents:
- Intelligent agents are computer systems that can perceive their environment, reason about it, and act in it.
- There are several key characteristics of intelligent agents, including:
  1. Autonomy: the ability to operate independently and make decisions without human intervention.
  2. Perception: the ability to sense and interpret information from the environment.
  3. Reasoning: the ability to use information to make decisions and solve problems.
  4. Learning: the ability to improve performance over time through experience.
  5. Social ability: the ability to interact and cooperate with other agents.
- The level of intelligence of an agent depends on the combination of these characteristics and the complexity of the tasks it can perform.
- Understanding the characteristics of intelligent agents is essential for the design and development of AI systems and for the creation of applications that can leverage AI technology.
