Definition

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a field of computer science that focuses on the development of algorithms and computer programs that can perform tasks that normally require human intelligence, such as perception, reasoning, learning, and problem solving. The goal of AI is to create systems that can perform tasks that would normally require human intelligence, such as recognizing objects, understanding natural language, and making decisions.

AI has its roots in computer science, mathematics, and psychology, and it has been influenced by other fields such as philosophy and neuroscience. AI is a rapidly growing field, and it has the potential to revolutionize many aspects of our lives, including healthcare, education, and entertainment.
