### Architecture for Intelligent Agents for the notes of the Unit 4 - SOFTWARE AGENTS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

Architecture for Intelligent Agents:
- The architecture of an intelligent agent defines its internal structure and the way it interacts with its environment.
- There are several key components in the architecture of an intelligent agent, including:
  1. Sensors: the components that gather information from the environment.
  2. Actuators: the components that perform actions in the environment.
  3. Memory: the component that stores information about the environment and the agent's history.
  4. Processor: the component that performs the reasoning and decision-making processes.
  5. Communication interface: the component that enables the agent to communicate with other agents and with the environment.
- The architecture of an intelligent agent can be designed to be centralized, where all the components are located in a single location, or decentralized, where the components are distributed across multiple locations.
- The choice of architecture depends on the specific requirements of the application and the complexity of the environment.
- Understanding the architecture of intelligent agents is important for the design and development of AI systems that can effectively interact with their environment and achieve their goals.
