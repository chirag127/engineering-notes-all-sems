### Hardware for the notes of the Unit 5 - APPLICATIONS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

Hardware for Artificial Intelligence:
- The hardware used for artificial intelligence has a significant impact on the performance and capabilities of AI systems.
- There are several types of hardware that are commonly used for AI, including: 
  1. Central Processing Units (CPUs): the traditional processors used in most computers.
  2. Graphics Processing Units (GPUs): specialized processors designed for graphics and parallel processing.
  3. Field-Programmable Gate Arrays (FPGAs): programmable chips that can be customized for specific applications.
  4. Application-Specific Integrated Circuits (ASICs): specialized chips designed for specific applications.
- The choice of hardware depends on the specific requirements of the application and the complexity of the algorithms being used.
- For example, GPU-based systems are often used for complex machine learning algorithms, while FPGAs and ASICs are often used for real-time applications.
- Understanding the hardware used for AI is important for the design and development of effective AI systems and for the optimization of performance and efficiency.
