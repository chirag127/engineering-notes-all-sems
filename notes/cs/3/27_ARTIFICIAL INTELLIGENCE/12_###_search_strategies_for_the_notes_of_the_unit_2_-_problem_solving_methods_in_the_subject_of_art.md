### Search Strategies for the notes of the Unit 2 - PROBLEM SOLVING METHODS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

Search Strategies are methods used in Artificial Intelligence to find solutions to problems by searching through a space of possible solutions.

1. Definition: Search Strategies are methods used in Artificial Intelligence to find solutions to problems by searching through a space of possible solutions. Search Strategies are used to find the best solution to a problem, based on a set of criteria.

2. Types of Search Strategies: There are several types of search strategies, including uninformed search, informed search, and heuristic search. The type of search strategy used will depend on the specific requirements of the problem and the type of information available.

3. Uninformed Search: Uninformed search is a search strategy that does not use any additional information to guide the search. This type of search is often used when the problem space is large and the information available is limited.

4. Informed Search: Informed search is a search strategy that uses additional information to guide the search. This type of search is often used when the problem space is smaller and the information available is more extensive.

5. Heuristic Search: Heuristic search is a search strategy that uses heuristics, or rules of thumb, to guide the search. This type of search is often used when the problem space is large and the information available is limited.

6. Applications: Search Strategies are used in a wide range of AI applications, including problem solving, decision making, and pathfinding. Search Strategies are also used in fields such as logistics, scheduling, and resource allocation.

In conclusion, Search Strategies are methods used in Artificial Intelligence to find solutions to problems by searching through a space of possible solutions. There are several types of search strategies, including uninformed search, informed search, and heuristic search, and the type of search strategy used will depend on the specific requirements of the problem and the type of information available. Search Strategies are used in a wide range of AI applications and are an important tool for finding solutions to problems and making decisions.
