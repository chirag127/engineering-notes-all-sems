## Unit 4 - SOFTWARE AGENTS

Unit 4: Software Agents in the subject of Artificial Intelligence KCS

Software Agents

Software agents are computer programs that are designed to perform tasks on behalf of a user or another system. They are an important aspect of Artificial Intelligence (AI) because they allow for the automation of tasks and the integration of multiple systems.

Software agents can be classified into two types: reactive agents and deliberative agents. Reactive agents respond to events in the environment, whereas deliberative agents have the ability to reason and make decisions.

Software agents are used in a variety of applications, including e-commerce, customer service, and network management. They are also used in fields such as medicine, finance, and law to support decision making and to provide users with relevant information.

One of the key advantages of software agents is their ability to operate autonomously, which allows them to perform tasks without direct human intervention. This can result in increased efficiency and reduced costs, as well as improved reliability and scalability.

In conclusion, software agents are computer programs that are designed to perform tasks on behalf of a user or another system. They can be classified into two types: reactive agents and deliberative agents. Software agents are used in a variety of applications and are an important aspect of the subject of Artificial Intelligence KCS.
