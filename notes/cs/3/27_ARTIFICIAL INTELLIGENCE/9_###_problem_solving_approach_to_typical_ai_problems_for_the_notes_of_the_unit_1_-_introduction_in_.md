### Problem Solving Approach to Typical AI problems for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

Problem Solving Approach to Typical AI problems:
- AI problems can be approached by using problem-solving methods that are specifically designed for AI.
- These methods can be divided into two main categories: search and inference.
- Search methods involve finding a solution to a problem by exploring a space of potential solutions.
- Inference methods involve using logical reasoning to make inferences about the solution to a problem.
- The choice of problem-solving method depends on the specific problem being solved and the requirements of the application.
- Common search methods include brute-force search, heuristic search, and local search.
- Common inference methods include forward chaining, backward chaining, and resolution.
- Understanding the problem-solving approach to typical AI problems is important for the design and development of AI systems that can solve real-world problems.
