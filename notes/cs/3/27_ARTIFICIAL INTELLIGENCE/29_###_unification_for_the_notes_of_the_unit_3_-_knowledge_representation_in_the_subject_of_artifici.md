### Unification for the notes of the Unit 3 - KNOWLEDGE REPRESENTATION in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

Unification:
- Unification is a process used in artificial intelligence to determine if two expressions are equivalent and to find a common representation for them.
- Unification is used in knowledge representation and reasoning, and it is a critical component of many AI systems.
- In unification, two expressions are matched against each other and variables are instantiated to make the expressions equivalent.
- Unification can be used to perform tasks such as finding common patterns in data, resolving references, and making inferences.
- Unification algorithms can be implemented using a variety of techniques, including pattern matching, substitution, and resolution.
- Understanding unification is important for the design and development of AI systems that can reason about knowledge and for the implementation of effective knowledge representation and reasoning algorithms.
