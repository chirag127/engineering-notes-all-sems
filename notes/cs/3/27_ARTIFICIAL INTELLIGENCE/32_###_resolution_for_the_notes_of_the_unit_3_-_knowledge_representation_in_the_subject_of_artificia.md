### Resolution for the notes of the Unit 3 - KNOWLEDGE REPRESENTATION in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

Resolution:
- Resolution is a method for making inferences in artificial intelligence.
- Resolution is based on the idea of resolving or combining clauses to produce new clauses that represent the solution to a problem.
- Resolution is used in automated theorem proving, where the goal is to prove the validity of a statement by deducing it from a set of axioms.
- In resolution, a statement is represented as a clause, which is a disjunction of literals.
- Resolution is performed by selecting two clauses and identifying a complementary pair of literals, one from each clause.
- The two clauses are then combined to form a new clause that represents the solution to the problem.
- Resolution is a powerful method for reasoning in artificial intelligence, but it can be computationally expensive and may not scale well to large problems.
- Understanding resolution is important for the design and development of AI systems that can perform logical inferences and for the implementation of effective automated theorem proving algorithms.
