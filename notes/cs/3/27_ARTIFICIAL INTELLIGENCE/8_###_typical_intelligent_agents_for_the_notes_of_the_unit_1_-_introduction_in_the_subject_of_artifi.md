### Typical Intelligent Agents for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

Typical Intelligent Agents:
- Intelligent agents are computer systems that can perceive their environment, reason about it, and take actions to achieve goals.
- There are several types of intelligent agents, including:
  1. Simple reflex agents: agents that take actions based on the current percept and a set of rules.
  2. Model-based agents: agents that maintain an internal model of the world and use it to reason about their environment.
  3. Goal-based agents: agents that have a set of goals and choose actions to achieve those goals.
  4. Utility-based agents: agents that make decisions based on the utility or value of different states.
  5. Learning agents: agents that can improve their performance over time by learning from experience.
- The choice of agent type depends on the specific requirements of the application and the complexity of the environment.
- Understanding the different types of intelligent agents is important for the design and development of AI systems that can effectively solve problems and achieve goals.
