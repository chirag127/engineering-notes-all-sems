### Information Retrieval for the notes of the Unit 5 - APPLICATIONS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

- Information retrieval (IR) is the process of finding and accessing relevant information from a large collection of documents, such as web pages, books, articles, etc.
- IR involves activities related to human cognition and knowledge management, such as understanding the user's information need, representing the documents and queries, ranking and filtering the results, and providing feedback and interaction
- Artificial intelligence (AI) is the branch of computer science that aims to create systems that can perform tasks that normally require human intelligence, such as reasoning, learning, planning, natural language processing, etc.
- AI methods are employed throughout the standard IR process and for novel value added services, such as personalization, summarization, question answering, recommendation, etc.
- Some examples of AI applications in IR are:

  - Pattern recognition: the ability to identify and classify patterns in data, such as images, text, speech, etc. Pattern recognition techniques can be used for document analysis, feature extraction, query expansion, etc.
  - Representation: the way of encoding and organizing the information in a suitable form for processing and storage. Representation techniques can be used for document indexing, query formulation, semantic analysis, etc.
  - Problem solving and planning: the ability to find solutions to complex and ill-defined problems, such as information need, relevance, user satisfaction, etc. Problem solving and planning techniques can be used for query optimization, result ranking, evaluation, etc.
  - Heuristics: the rules of thumb or shortcuts that can help to find good solutions in a reasonable time, without exhaustive search. Heuristics can be used for query reformulation, result filtering, feedback generation, etc.
  - Learning: the ability to improve performance and adapt to new situations based on experience and feedback. Learning techniques can be used for document classification, query refinement, result personalization, etc.
