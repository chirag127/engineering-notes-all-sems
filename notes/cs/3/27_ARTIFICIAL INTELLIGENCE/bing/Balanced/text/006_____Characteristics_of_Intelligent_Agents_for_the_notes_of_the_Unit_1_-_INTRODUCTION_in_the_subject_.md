### Characteristics of Intelligent Agents

An intelligent agent is a computer software system that can perceive its environment, act rationally, and learn from its experience. Intelligent agents can be used for various applications, such as information retrieval, decision support, automation, and collaboration. 

Some of the common characteristics of intelligent agents are:

- **Autonomy**: An intelligent agent can act independently and without direct human intervention. It can control its own actions and internal state based on its goals and preferences. 
- **Adaptivity**: An intelligent agent can learn from its experience and improve its performance over time. It can also adapt to changing situations and cope with uncertainty and incomplete information. 
- **Interaction**: An intelligent agent can communicate and cooperate with other agents, humans, and systems. It can use various forms of communication, such as natural language, gestures, or protocols. It can also exhibit social behavior, such as cooperation, coordination, and negotiation. 
- **Knowledge**: An intelligent agent can represent and reason about its environment, its own actions, and the actions of others. It can use various forms of knowledge, such as facts, rules, beliefs, preferences, and plans. It can also acquire new knowledge through observation, inference, or feedback. 
- **Goal-orientation**: An intelligent agent can act rationally and purposefully to achieve its goals and objectives. It can also balance multiple and conflicting goals and prioritize them according to its preferences. It can also generate and evaluate alternative courses of action and select the best one.
