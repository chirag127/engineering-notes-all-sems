### Negotiation and Bargaining

- Negotiation is a process of reaching an agreement between two or more parties that have conflicting interests or preferences.
- Bargaining is a subset of negotiation that focuses on the exchange of offers and counteroffers to determine the terms of the agreement.
- Negotiation and bargaining can be applied to various domains, such as business, politics, law, diplomacy, and personal relationships.
- Negotiation and bargaining can involve human-human, human-agent, or agent-agent interactions, where agents are software programs that can represent human interests or goals  .
- Artificial intelligence (AI) can play a role in negotiation and bargaining in different ways, such as:
  - Providing decision support to human negotiators, such as generating offers, evaluating outcomes, suggesting strategies, and detecting emotions .
  - Automating the negotiation process by acting as autonomous negotiators that can communicate, reason, and make decisions on behalf of humans .
  - Enhancing the negotiation outcomes by optimizing the efficiency, fairness, and satisfaction of the parties involved .
- AI-based negotiation and bargaining face several challenges, such as:
  - Modeling the preferences, beliefs, and behaviors of the other parties, which may be uncertain, incomplete, or dynamic  .
  - Dealing with complex and multi-issue negotiations, which may require trade-offs, concessions, and compromises  .
  - Handling ethical, legal, and social implications of using AI in negotiation and bargaining, such as trust, transparency, accountability, and privacy  .
