### Events

- Events are the **occurrence of things in the real world** . Anything which happens in real time are considered as the events.
- Events are an important element as they are the **initial thing to be considered** in knowledge representation.
- Events can be **described by their properties**, such as time, location, participants, causes, effects, etc.
- Events can also be **classified into types**, such as actions, states, processes, etc.
- Events can be **related to each other** by temporal, causal, or logical relations, such as before, after, during, because, etc.
- Events can be **represented by various formalisms**, such as logic, frames, scripts, scenarios, etc .
- Events can be **used to reason about the world**, such as inferring the consequences, explanations, or plans of events.
