### Problem Solving Methods

- Problem solving methods are techniques or algorithms that are used to find solutions to problems in artificial intelligence (AI).
- Problem solving methods often involve investigating potential solutions to problems through reasoning techniques, making use of polynomial and differential equations, and carrying them out and use modelling frameworks.
- A same problem may have multiple solutions, that are all accomplished using a unique algorithm. The quality of the solution depends on the efficiency, optimality, and completeness of the algorithm.
- Some of the common problem solving methods in AI are:

  - Search algorithms: These are methods that explore the state space of a problem to find the best optimal solution. Search algorithms can be classified into uninformed search and informed search, depending on whether they use any domain-specific knowledge or not . Some examples of search algorithms are breadth-first search, depth-first search, uniform-cost search, greedy search, A* search, etc.
  - Evolutionary computations: These are methods that use natural selection and genetic operators to generate and improve solutions to problems. Evolutionary computations can be applied to optimization, learning, and design problems. Some examples of evolutionary computations are genetic algorithms, genetic programming, evolutionary strategies, etc.
  - Knowledge representations: These are methods that use symbolic or sub-symbolic structures to encode and manipulate information about a problem domain. Knowledge representations can be used to reason, infer, and plan solutions to problems. Some examples of knowledge representations are propositional logic, first-order logic, semantic networks, frames, scripts, etc.
  - Machine learning: These are methods that use data and feedback to learn and improve solutions to problems. Machine learning can be divided into supervised learning, unsupervised learning, and reinforcement learning, depending on the type and amount of feedback available. Some examples of machine learning techniques are linear regression, decision trees, neural networks, k-means clustering, Q-learning, etc.

- Problem solving in AI is directly associated with the nature of humans and their activities. There are different types of problems in AI, such as:

  - Ignorable: These are problems in which solution steps can be ignored, such as sorting a list of numbers.
  - Recoverable: These are problems in which solution steps can be undone, such as playing chess.
  - Irrecoverable: These are problems in which solution steps cannot be undone, such as driving a car.

- The steps of problem solving in AI are:

  - Define the problem: This involves specifying the initial state, the goal state, and the actions or operators that can change the state of the problem.
  - Formulate the problem: This involves representing the problem in a suitable data structure, such as a graph, a tree, or a matrix.
  - Search for the solution: This involves applying a problem solving method to find a sequence of actions or operators that leads from the initial state to the goal state.
  - Execute the solution: This involves carrying out the actions or operators in the real world or in a simulated environment.
  - Evaluate the solution: This involves measuring the quality of the solution in terms of its efficiency, optimality, and completeness.

- Problem solving methods in AI are essential for building intelligent systems that can perform tasks that require human-like reasoning, learning, and decision making. Some of the applications of problem solving methods in AI are:

  - Natural language processing: This involves using problem solving methods to understand, generate, and translate natural language, such as speech and text.
  - Computer vision: This involves using problem solving methods to analyze, recognize, and manipulate images and videos.
  - Robotics: This involves using problem solving methods to plan, control, and coordinate the motions and actions of robots.
  - Game playing: This involves using problem solving methods to design and implement strategies and tactics for playing games, such as chess, checkers, and Go.
  - Expert systems: This involves using problem solving methods to encode and apply domain-specific knowledge and rules for solving problems, such as medical diagnosis, legal reasoning, and financial planning.
