### Robot

Robot is a machine that can perform tasks autonomously or semi-autonomously, often with the help of artificial intelligence. Artificial intelligence in robotics aids models in learning to perform specific tasks and makes robots more intelligent in a variety of environments. Robotics has applications in the entertainment industry, including films, video games, animation, and cartoons. Robots are also extremely useful in situations where a lot of repetition, precision, speed, or safety is required.

Some of the applications of artificial intelligence in robotics are:

- **Self-driving cars**: These are vehicles that can navigate and drive without human intervention, using sensors, cameras, maps, and deep learning algorithms. Self-driving cars can improve road safety, reduce traffic congestion, and provide mobility to people who cannot drive. Some examples of companies working on self-driving cars are Tesla, Waymo, Uber, and Cruise.
- **Autonomous drones**: These are aerial vehicles that can fly and perform tasks without human control, using computer vision, navigation, and machine learning. Autonomous drones can be used for surveillance, delivery, inspection, mapping, and entertainment. Some examples of companies working on autonomous drones are Amazon, DJI, Skydio, and Zipline.
- **Humanoid robots**: These are robots that resemble and mimic human appearance and behavior, using natural language processing, computer vision, and deep learning. Humanoid robots can interact with people and perform social and service tasks, such as reception, education, entertainment, and companionship. Some examples of companies working on humanoid robots are Hanson Robotics, SoftBank Robotics, Boston Dynamics, and Samsung.
- **Autonomous farming equipment**: These are machines that can perform agricultural tasks without human supervision, using sensors, computer vision, and machine learning. Autonomous farming equipment can improve crop yield, reduce labor costs, and optimize resource use. Some examples of companies working on autonomous farming equipment are John Deere, Blue River Technology, Harvest Automation, and FarmWise.
- **Intelligent robotics platforms**: These are software and hardware frameworks that enable developers and researchers to create and test new robotic applications, using artificial intelligence, simulation, and cloud computing. Intelligent robotics platforms can accelerate innovation and collaboration in the field of robotics. Some examples of companies working on intelligent robotics platforms are Microsoft, OpenAI, Nvidia, and Google.
