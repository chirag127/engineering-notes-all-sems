# Information Retrieval for the notes of the Unit 5 - APPLICATIONS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

- Information retrieval (IR) is the process of finding and accessing relevant information from a large collection of documents, such as web pages, books, articles, etc.
- IR involves activities related to human cognition and knowledge management, such as understanding the user's information need, representing the documents and queries, ranking and filtering the results, and providing feedback and interaction.
- Artificial intelligence (AI) is the branch of computer science that aims to create systems that can perform tasks that normally require human intelligence, such as reasoning, learning, planning, natural language processing, etc.
- AI methods are employed throughout the standard IR process and for novel value-added services, such as personalization, summarization, question answering, recommendation, etc.
- AI techniques can help to account for the intrinsic uncertainty and imprecision that characterize the subjectivity of IR, such as dealing with natural language, user preferences, relevance judgments, etc.
- Some examples of AI methods applied to IR are:
  - Pattern recognition: the process of identifying and classifying patterns in data, such as images, text, speech, etc. Pattern recognition can be used for tasks such as document classification, topic modeling, sentiment analysis, entity extraction, etc.
  - Representation: the process of encoding and organizing the information in a way that facilitates computation and reasoning. Representation can be used for tasks such as document indexing, query expansion, semantic analysis, knowledge graph construction, etc.
  - Problem solving and planning: the process of finding and executing a sequence of actions that achieve a goal. Problem solving and planning can be used for tasks such as query reformulation, result ranking, query optimization, etc.
  - Heuristics: the process of using rules of thumb or shortcuts to simplify and speed up the search process. Heuristics can be used for tasks such as query suggestion, relevance feedback, result diversification, etc.
  - Learning: the process of acquiring and improving knowledge and skills from data and experience. Learning can be used for tasks such as document clustering, query classification, result personalization, etc.
