- Artificial intelligence (AI) is the ability of a machine, program or system to learn and adapt by experience.
- AI can be classified into two types: artificial narrow intelligence (ANI) and artificial general intelligence (AGI).
  - ANI, or weak AI, is a type of AI that can only focus on one specific task or problem at a time. Examples of ANI include speech recognition, facial recognition, chess-playing programs, etc.
  - AGI, or strong AI, is the inverse of ANI. It is a type of AI that can perform any intellectual task that a human can. Examples of AGI include human-like robots, self-aware machines, etc. AGI is still a hypothetical concept and has not been achieved yet.
- AI has a multitude of applications in various domains, such as health care, finance, national security, transportation, e-commerce, etc . Some of the common applications of AI are:
  - Big data: AI can help analyze large amounts of data and extract useful insights from them. AI can also help visualize data, detect patterns, and make predictions based on data.
  - Computer vision: AI can help process and understand images and videos. AI can also help perform tasks such as face detection, object recognition, scene analysis, etc.
  - Data mining: AI can help discover hidden patterns and associations in data. AI can also help classify data, cluster data, and generate rules from data.
  - Machine learning: AI can help learn from data and improve its performance over time. AI can also help create models, algorithms, and systems that can learn from data and make decisions.
  - Natural language processing (NLP): AI can help process and understand natural language, such as speech and text. AI can also help perform tasks such as translation, summarization, sentiment analysis, etc.
  - Robotics: AI can help control and coordinate the actions of robots. AI can also help robots interact with humans and their environment, and perform tasks such as navigation, manipulation, etc.
