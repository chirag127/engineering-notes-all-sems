# Unit 5 - APPLICATIONS

## Introduction

- Artificial intelligence (AI) is the field of computer science that aims to create machines and systems that can perform tasks that normally require human intelligence and abilities.
- AI has many applications in various domains, such as natural language processing, computer vision, robotics, gaming, healthcare, education, business, and social media.
- In this unit, we will explore some of the common and emerging applications of AI, and how they are implemented using different techniques and tools.

## Natural Language Processing (NLP)

- NLP is the branch of AI that deals with the analysis, understanding, and generation of natural languages, such as English, Hindi, Chinese, etc.
- NLP has many subtasks, such as speech recognition, machine translation, sentiment analysis, text summarization, question answering, chatbots, and natural language generation.
- NLP uses various techniques, such as rule-based systems, statistical methods, deep learning, and knowledge graphs, to process and produce natural language data.
- NLP has many applications, such as voice assistants, search engines, text editors, social media analysis, e-commerce, education, and entertainment.

## Computer Vision (CV)

- CV is the branch of AI that deals with the analysis, understanding, and generation of visual data, such as images, videos, and graphics.
- CV has many subtasks, such as face detection, object recognition, scene segmentation, optical character recognition, image synthesis, video analysis, and augmented reality.
- CV uses various techniques, such as feature extraction, machine learning, deep learning, and computer graphics, to process and produce visual data.
- CV has many applications, such as biometrics, security, surveillance, medical imaging, autonomous vehicles, gaming, art, and education.

## Robotics

- Robotics is the branch of AI that deals with the design, construction, operation, and control of machines that can perform physical tasks, such as manipulation, locomotion, and interaction.
- Robotics has many subtasks, such as robot kinematics, dynamics, planning, perception, learning, and coordination.
- Robotics uses various techniques, such as sensors, actuators, controllers, algorithms, and software, to create and operate robots.
- Robotics has many applications, such as manufacturing, agriculture, mining, exploration, rescue, military, entertainment, and education.

## Gaming

- Gaming is the branch of AI that deals with the creation and analysis of games, such as board games, card games, video games, and virtual reality games.
- Gaming has many subtasks, such as game design, game mechanics, game AI, game analytics, and game development.
- Gaming uses various techniques, such as search, optimization, logic, machine learning, deep learning, and graphics, to create and analyze games.
- Gaming has many applications, such as entertainment, education, training, simulation, and research.
