# Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION

## Definition of Artificial Intelligence

- Artificial intelligence (AI) is the **simulation of human intelligence in machines** that are programmed to **learn, reason, and perceive** .
- AI is a **branch of computer science** that deals with the **creation of systems** that can **perform tasks** that normally require **human intelligence**, such as **speech recognition, decision making, problem solving, and learning** .
- AI is also the **study of how to make machines** that can **demonstrate intelligence** by **perceiving, synthesizing, and inferring information** from their **environment and data**.
