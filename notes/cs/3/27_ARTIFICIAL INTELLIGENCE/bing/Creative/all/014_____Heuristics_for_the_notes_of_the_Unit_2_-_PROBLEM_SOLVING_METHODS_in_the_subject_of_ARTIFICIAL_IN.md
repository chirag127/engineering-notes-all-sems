# Heuristics for the notes of the Unit 2 - PROBLEM SOLVING METHODS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

- Heuristics are techniques that are used to solve a problem or to make a decision, where traditional methods would be too time-consuming or impractical .
- Heuristics are often used as an approximation to an ideal solution, where the hope is that the approximate solution is good enough for the specific problem at hand.
- Heuristics are an inevitable and inseparable part of artificial intelligence, as they are a computer simulation of the human thinking process, used in situations no known algorithm can reach .
- Heuristics are generally used in conjunction with optimization algorithms to enhance the overall efficiency of the desired results .
- Heuristics can be classified into different types, such as:
  - **Constructive heuristics**: These are heuristics that build a solution incrementally, adding one element at a time, until a complete solution is obtained or a stopping criterion is met.
  - **Local search heuristics**: These are heuristics that start with an initial solution and iteratively improve it by making small changes, until a local or global optimum is reached or a stopping criterion is met.
  - **Metaheuristics**: These are heuristics that combine or adapt other heuristics to solve complex or large-scale problems, using mechanisms such as exploration, exploitation, diversification, intensification, learning, or memory.
- Heuristics can be evaluated based on different criteria, such as:
  - **Completeness**: This is the ability of a heuristic to find a solution if one exists.
  - **Optimality**: This is the ability of a heuristic to find the best solution among all possible solutions.
  - **Efficiency**: This is the ability of a heuristic to find a solution in a reasonable amount of time and space.
  - **Simplicity**: This is the ability of a heuristic to be easy to understand and implement.
  - **Robustness**: This is the ability of a heuristic to perform well in different situations and scenarios.
  - **Adaptability**: This is the ability of a heuristic to adjust to changing conditions and requirements.
