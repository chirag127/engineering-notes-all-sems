# Unit 5 - APPLICATIONS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

- This unit covers the various applications of artificial intelligence in different domains, such as natural language processing, computer vision, robotics, expert systems, and game playing.
- The main topics of this unit are:

  - Natural language processing (NLP): The branch of artificial intelligence that deals with the understanding and generation of natural languages, such as English, Hindi, or Chinese. NLP involves tasks such as speech recognition, machine translation, sentiment analysis, text summarization, question answering, and chatbots .
  - Computer vision: The branch of artificial intelligence that deals with the analysis and interpretation of visual information, such as images, videos, or scenes. Computer vision involves tasks such as face detection, object recognition, scene understanding, optical character recognition, and self-driving cars .
  - Robotics: The branch of artificial intelligence that deals with the design, construction, and control of machines that can perform physical tasks, such as manipulation, locomotion, or navigation. Robotics involves tasks such as robot planning, robot learning, robot coordination, and human-robot interaction .
  - Expert systems: The branch of artificial intelligence that deals with the development of systems that can provide expert-level knowledge and advice in specific domains, such as medicine, law, or engineering. Expert systems involve tasks such as knowledge representation, inference, explanation, and learning .
  - Game playing: The branch of artificial intelligence that deals with the development of systems that can play games that require intelligence, such as chess, checkers, or Go. Game playing involves tasks such as search, evaluation, pruning, and learning .

- The main objectives of this unit are:

  - To understand the basic concepts and techniques of natural language processing, computer vision, robotics, expert systems, and game playing.
  - To learn the applications and challenges of artificial intelligence in different domains.
  - To appreciate the current state-of-the-art and future trends of artificial intelligence in various fields.
