# Characteristics of Intelligent Agents

- An intelligent agent is a computer software system that can act autonomously and rationally in a given environment to achieve its goals  .
- Intelligent agents have the following distinguishing characteristics   :
  - **Situatedness**: An intelligent agent is situated in an environment and can perceive its state through sensors or other forms of input. It can also act on the environment through effectors or other forms of output.
  - **Autonomy**: An intelligent agent can act independently and without direct intervention from humans or other agents. It can control its own actions and internal state based on its goals and preferences.
  - **Adaptivity**: An intelligent agent can learn from its experience and improve its performance over time. It can also adapt to changing conditions and situations in the environment.
  - **Sociability**: An intelligent agent can interact with other agents, humans, or systems through communication, cooperation, or coordination. It can also exhibit social behaviors such as trust, reputation, or norms.
  - **Goal-orientedness**: An intelligent agent has one or more goals that guide its actions and decisions. It can also plan and execute strategies to achieve its goals efficiently and effectively.
  - **Knowledge-basedness**: An intelligent agent has a knowledge base that stores information about the environment, itself, and other agents. It can also reason and infer new knowledge from its existing knowledge and observations.

- Intelligent agents can be used for various applications in artificial intelligence, such as :
  - **Information access and navigation**: Intelligent agents can help users find, filter, and organize relevant information from large and complex data sources, such as the web, databases, or social media.
  - **Decision support and empowerment**: Intelligent agents can assist knowledge workers in decision-making, problem-solving, and creativity, by providing suggestions, recommendations, or feedback.
  - **Repetitive and tedious tasks**: Intelligent agents can automate and optimize tasks that are repetitive, tedious, or time-consuming, such as scheduling, booking, or ordering.
  - **Personalization and customization**: Intelligent agents can tailor their services and interactions to the preferences, needs, and interests of individual users, such as personal assistants, tutors, or coaches.
  - **Entertainment and education**: Intelligent agents can provide entertainment and education to users, such as games, simulations, or stories, by creating engaging and immersive experiences.
  - **Monitoring and surveillance**: Intelligent agents can monitor and surveil the environment, such as security, health, or traffic, by collecting, analyzing, and reporting data and events.
