## Unit 3 - KNOWLEDGE REPRESENTATION

- Knowledge representation is the process of encoding and storing information in a form that can be used by an intelligent system, such as an AI agent.
- Knowledge representation aims to capture the meaning and structure of the information, as well as the relationships and constraints among the entities and concepts involved.
- Knowledge representation also enables reasoning, inference, and problem-solving based on the available information and the goals of the system.
- Knowledge representation can be done using various methods and languages, such as logic, semantic networks, frames, production rules, ontologies, and probabilistic models.
- Some of the criteria for evaluating knowledge representation methods are expressiveness, efficiency, consistency, completeness, and naturalness.
- Some of the challenges and issues in knowledge representation are dealing with uncertainty, inconsistency, incompleteness, and ambiguity of the information, as well as the trade-off between expressiveness and tractability of the methods.
