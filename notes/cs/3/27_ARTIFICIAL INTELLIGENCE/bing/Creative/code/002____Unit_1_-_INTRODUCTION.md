# Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION

## What is Artificial Intelligence?

- Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the branch of computer science that aims to create machines and systems that can perform tasks that normally require human intelligence and abilities.
- AI can be divided into two main categories: narrow AI and general AI.
- Narrow AI refers to systems that can perform specific tasks, such as face recognition, speech recognition, natural language processing, etc.
- General AI refers to systems that can exhibit human-like intelligence and reasoning across a wide range of domains and tasks, such as common sense, creativity, problem-solving, etc. General AI is still a hypothetical and elusive goal.

## Why study Artificial Intelligence?

- AI has many applications and benefits for various fields and domains, such as medicine, education, entertainment, business, etc.
- AI can help humans to automate tedious and complex tasks, enhance productivity and efficiency, improve decision making and problem solving, and augment human capabilities and knowledge.
- AI can also pose many challenges and risks, such as ethical, social, legal, and security issues, as well as potential impacts on human values, rights, and dignity.
- AI can also raise many philosophical and scientific questions, such as the nature and limits of intelligence, the relationship between mind and body, the possibility and implications of artificial consciousness and emotions, etc.

## How to study Artificial Intelligence?

- AI is a multidisciplinary and interdisciplinary field that draws from various sources and methods, such as mathematics, logic, computer science, engineering, psychology, neuroscience, philosophy, etc.
- AI can be studied from different perspectives and levels of abstraction, such as symbolic, sub-symbolic, statistical, connectionist, evolutionary, etc.
- AI can be studied using different approaches and techniques, such as search, knowledge representation and reasoning, planning, learning, natural language processing, computer vision, robotics, etc.
- AI can be studied using different tools and platforms, such as programming languages, libraries, frameworks, software, hardware, etc.
