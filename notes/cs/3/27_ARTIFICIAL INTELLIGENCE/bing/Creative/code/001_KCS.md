# KCS

KCS stands for Knowledge-Centered Service, a methodology that aims to improve service delivery and knowledge management in service organizations. KCS has the following characteristics :

- It is a **process** that integrates the creation and maintenance of knowledge with the delivery of service.
- It is a **practice** that defines the roles, responsibilities, and skills of service agents and knowledge workers.
- It is a **performance** framework that measures the quality and effectiveness of knowledge and service.
- It is a **philosophy** that promotes a culture of collaboration, learning, and sharing.

Some of the benefits of KCS are :

- It reduces the time and effort required to resolve service requests and incidents.
- It increases the consistency and accuracy of service responses and solutions.
- It enhances the customer and employee satisfaction and loyalty.
- It fosters the innovation and improvement of service processes and products.
- It lowers the operational costs and risks of service delivery.

Some of the key concepts of KCS are :

- **Capture** knowledge in the context of service interactions, using the customer's language and perspective.
- **Structure** knowledge in a reusable and searchable format, following a standard template and style guide.
- **Reuse** knowledge to solve service issues and avoid recreating existing solutions.
- **Improve** knowledge based on feedback and usage, updating and refining the content and quality.
- **Evolve** knowledge based on demand and analytics, identifying and filling the gaps and opportunities.
