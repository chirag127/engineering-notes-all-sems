### Characteristics of Intelligent Agents

- An intelligent agent is a computer software system that can perceive its environment, act rationally, and learn from its experience  .
- Some common characteristics of intelligent agents are   :
  - **Autonomy**: The ability to act independently and self-govern without external control or intervention.
  - **Adaptivity**: The ability to adjust and improve based on feedback, experience, and changing conditions.
  - **Sociability**: The ability to interact and cooperate with other agents, humans, and systems in a meaningful and appropriate way.
  - **Situatedness**: The ability to sense and respond to the current state and context of the environment.
  - **Goal-orientation**: The ability to pursue and achieve specific objectives or outcomes.
  - **Knowledge-based**: The ability to use and manipulate symbolic representations of information and knowledge.
- An abstract description of an intelligent agent is called an abstract intelligent agent (AIA), which specifies the agent's inputs, outputs, goals, and internal states.
- An example of an AIA is the PEAS framework, which stands for Performance measure, Environment, Actuators, and Sensors. This framework defines the agent's objectives, the characteristics of the environment, the actions that the agent can perform, and the information that the agent can perceive.
