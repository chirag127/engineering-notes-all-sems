### Argumentation among Agents

- Argumentation is a mechanism for modelling the internal reasoning of an agent, as well as the interaction among rational agents .
- Argumentation can be used to represent and evaluate different types of arguments, such as deductive, inductive, abductive, defeasible, or probabilistic arguments.
- Argumentation can also be used to support various forms of dialogue, such as persuasion, negotiation, deliberation, or inquiry .
- Argumentation in multi-agent systems involves the exchange of arguments among agents, which can have different goals, beliefs, preferences, or capabilities .
- Argumentation in multi-agent systems can be studied from different perspectives, such as the syntax and semantics of argumentation frameworks, the protocols and strategies for argumentation dialogues, the algorithms and heuristics for argument generation and evaluation, and the applications and evaluation of argumentation systems  .
- Argumentation in multi-agent systems can be used for various purposes, such as resolving conflicts, reaching agreements, making decisions, explaining actions, or enhancing trust and cooperation  .
- Argumentation in multi-agent systems can also be used to model important aspects of social reasoning, such as the rights, obligations, and norms of agents, as well as the social context and consequences of their arguments.
