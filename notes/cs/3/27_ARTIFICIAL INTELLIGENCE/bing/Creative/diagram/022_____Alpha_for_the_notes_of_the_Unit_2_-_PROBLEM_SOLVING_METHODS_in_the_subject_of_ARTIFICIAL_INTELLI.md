### Alpha

- Alpha is a term used to refer to a family of artificial intelligence systems developed by DeepMind, a subsidiary of Alphabet, the parent company of Google.
- Alpha systems use deep learning and reinforcement learning to solve complex problems that require critical thinking and creativity, such as playing games, programming, and protein folding.
- Some examples of Alpha systems are:
  - AlphaGo, which defeated the world champion of the board game Go in 2016.
  - AlphaZero, which learned to master chess, shogi, and Go from scratch, without any human knowledge or guidance.
  - AlphaFold, which predicted the three-dimensional structure of proteins with unprecedented accuracy, solving a 50-year-old grand challenge in biology.
  - AlphaCode, which competed in real-world programming competitions, generating code that solved a variety of tasks.
- Alpha systems demonstrate the potential of artificial intelligence to advance scientific discovery and human knowledge.
