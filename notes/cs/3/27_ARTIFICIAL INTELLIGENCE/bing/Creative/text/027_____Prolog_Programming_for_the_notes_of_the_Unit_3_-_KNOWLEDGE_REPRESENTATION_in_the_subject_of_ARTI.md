### Prolog Programming for the notes of the Unit 3 - KNOWLEDGE REPRESENTATION in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

- Prolog is a **logic programming language** that has an important role in **artificial intelligence** .
- Unlike many other programming languages, Prolog is intended primarily as a **declarative programming language**. In Prolog, logic is expressed as **relations** (called as **facts** and **rules**) .
- Prolog is based on the **resolution principle** of logic, which allows to infer new facts from existing facts and rules using **unification** and **backtracking** .
- Prolog can be used for various applications of artificial intelligence, such as **natural language processing**, **expert systems**, **planning**, **machine learning**, and **knowledge representation and reasoning** .
- Knowledge representation and reasoning (KRR) is the field of artificial intelligence that studies how to represent and manipulate knowledge in a formal and computable way .
- KRR aims to provide methods and tools for **encoding**, **querying**, **updating**, **explaining**, and **reasoning** with knowledge in various domains and tasks .
- Prolog is one of the most popular and widely used languages for KRR, as it allows to express knowledge in a **declarative** and **logical** way, and to perform **inference** and **query answering** using its built-in mechanisms  .
- Prolog supports different **paradigms** and **extensions** of logic programming and KRR, such as **answer set programming**, **constraint logic programming**, **abductive logic programming**, **inductive logic programming**, and **description logic programming**  .
- Prolog also supports various **knowledge representation formalisms** and **reasoning methods**, such as **first-order logic**, **non-monotonic logic**, **default logic**, **modal logic**, **temporal logic**, **spatial logic**, **causal logic**, **belief revision**, **argumentation**, and **ontology languages**  .
- Prolog can be integrated with other languages and tools for KRR, such as **AILog**, **SWI-Prolog**, **XSB**, **DLV**, **Smodels**, **Clingo**, **OntoBroker**, and **Protégé**  .
