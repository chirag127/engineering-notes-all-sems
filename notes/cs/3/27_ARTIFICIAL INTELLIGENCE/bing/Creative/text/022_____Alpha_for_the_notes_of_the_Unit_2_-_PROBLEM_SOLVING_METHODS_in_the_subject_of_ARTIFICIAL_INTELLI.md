### Alpha

- Alpha is a general term for artificial intelligence systems that can learn from data and solve complex problems.
- Alpha systems are based on deep learning, a branch of machine learning that uses neural networks to model high-level abstractions in data.
- Alpha systems can perform tasks that require critical thinking, such as programming, protein folding, and mathematics.
- Some examples of Alpha systems are:
  - AlphaCode, a system developed by DeepMind that can compete in real-world programming competitions.
  - AlphaFold, a system developed by DeepMind that can accurately predict the structure of proteins, which is essential for understanding diseases and drug discovery.
  - Wolfram|Alpha, a system developed by Wolfram Research that can answer factual questions and generate practice problems for students and teachers .
- Alpha systems are not limited to specific domains or applications, but can adapt to different problems and data sources.
- Alpha systems are examples of problem-solving agents, which are rational agents that use search techniques to find solutions to specific problems.
- Problem-solving agents can use different types of search algorithms, such as uninformed search, informed search, local search, and adversarial search, depending on the characteristics of the problem and the available information.
- Problem-solving agents can also use different types of problem representations, such as state-space representation, production system representation, and logic representation, depending on the complexity and structure of the problem.
