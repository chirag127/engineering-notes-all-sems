### Information Retrieval for the notes of the Unit 5 - APPLICATIONS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

- Information retrieval (IR) is the process of finding and accessing relevant information from a large collection of documents, such as web pages, books, articles, etc.
- IR involves activities related to human cognition and knowledge management, such as understanding the user's information need, representing the documents and queries, ranking and filtering the results, and providing feedback and interaction
- Artificial intelligence (AI) is the branch of computer science that aims to create systems that can perform tasks that normally require human intelligence, such as reasoning, learning, planning, natural language processing, etc.
- AI methods are employed throughout the standard IR process and for novel value added services, such as personalization, summarization, question answering, etc.
- Some examples of AI applications in IR are:

  - Pattern recognition: the ability to identify and classify patterns in data, such as images, text, speech, etc. Pattern recognition techniques can be used for document analysis, feature extraction, text categorization, image retrieval, etc.
  - Representation: the way of encoding and organizing the information in a system, such as using vectors, graphs, ontologies, etc. Representation techniques can affect the efficiency and effectiveness of IR, as they determine how the documents and queries are compared and matched
  - Problem solving and planning: the ability to find solutions to complex and dynamic problems, such as optimizing the retrieval performance, handling uncertainty and inconsistency, adapting to changing user needs, etc. Problem solving and planning techniques can be used for query reformulation, result ranking, relevance feedback, etc.
  - Heuristics: the rules of thumb or shortcuts that can guide the search process and reduce the search space, such as using frequency, popularity, recency, etc. Heuristics can be used for query expansion, result filtering, recommendation, etc.
  - Learning: the ability to improve the system's performance and behavior based on data and feedback, such as using supervised, unsupervised, or reinforcement learning. Learning techniques can be used for document classification, query classification, user modeling, etc.

- Some challenges and limitations of AI in IR are:

  - Data quality and quantity: the availability and reliability of the data that the system uses to learn and perform, such as the accuracy, completeness, timeliness, diversity, etc. of the data
  - Evaluation and validation: the methods and metrics that the system uses to measure and improve its performance and behavior, such as the precision, recall, relevance, satisfaction, etc. of the results
  - Ethical and social issues: the implications and consequences of the system's actions and decisions, such as the privacy, security, fairness, accountability, transparency, etc. of the system
