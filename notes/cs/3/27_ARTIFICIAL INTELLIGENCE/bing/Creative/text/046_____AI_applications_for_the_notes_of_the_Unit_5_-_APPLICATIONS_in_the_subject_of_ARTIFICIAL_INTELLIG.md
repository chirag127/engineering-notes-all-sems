### AI applications

Artificial intelligence (AI) is a general purpose technology that has a multitude of applications in various domains. AI can be used to perform tasks that are difficult, tedious, or dangerous for humans, as well as to enhance human capabilities and creativity. Some of the common AI applications are:

- **Transportation**: AI can assist drivers with features like self-parking, advanced cruise control, lane keeping, collision avoidance, and traffic management. AI can also enable autonomous vehicles that can navigate complex environments without human intervention .
- **Manufacturing**: AI can help automate and optimize various aspects of manufacturing, such as quality control, predictive maintenance, supply chain management, inventory management, and product design. AI can also enable collaborative robots that can work alongside humans in non-ergonomic conditions .
- **Healthcare**: AI can help diagnose diseases, recommend treatments, monitor patients, analyze medical images, discover new drugs, and provide personalized care. AI can also enable telemedicine, chatbots, and wearable devices that can improve access and affordability of healthcare services  .
- **Finance and Banking**: AI can help detect fraud, manage risk, optimize portfolios, provide financial advice, automate trading, and enhance customer service. AI can also enable robo-advisors, chatbots, and biometric authentication that can improve security and convenience of financial transactions  .
- **Human Resources**: AI can help recruit, train, evaluate, and retain employees, as well as improve employee engagement, productivity, and well-being. AI can also enable chatbots, virtual assistants, and sentiment analysis that can improve communication and feedback within organizations .
- **Agriculture**: AI can help monitor crops, soil, weather, pests, and diseases, as well as optimize irrigation, fertilization, harvesting, and storage. AI can also enable precision agriculture, drones, and smart sensors that can improve yield and sustainability of farming  .
- **Education**: AI can help personalize learning, assess students, provide feedback, tutor, and mentor. AI can also enable adaptive learning, gamification, and virtual reality that can improve engagement and motivation of learners  .
- **E-Commerce**: AI can help analyze customer behavior, preferences, and feedback, as well as recommend products, services, and offers. AI can also enable chatbots, voice assistants, and visual search that can improve customer experience and satisfaction  .
- **Entertainment**: AI can help create, edit, and enhance content, such as music, videos, games, and art. AI can also enable content recommendation, personalization, and generation that can improve entertainment quality and diversity  .
- **Social Media**: AI can help moderate, filter, and rank content, such as posts, comments, and reviews. AI can also enable face recognition, natural language processing, and sentiment analysis that can improve social media interaction and engagement  .
