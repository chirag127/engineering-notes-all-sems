### Information Retrieval for the notes of the Unit 5 - APPLICATIONS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

- Information retrieval is a key technology for knowledge management. It deals with the search for information and the representation, storage, and organization of knowledge.
- The application of artificial intelligence is not limited to the computer field, but combined with many fields to become a new technology. In this process, artificial intelligence not only develops itself, but also brings revolutionary changes to specific fields. Information retrieval is one of the representatives.
- A simple model of an information retrieval system provides a framework for subsequent discussion of artificial intelligence concepts and their applicability in information retrieval. Concepts surveyed include pattern recognition, representation, problem-solving and planning, heuristics, and learning.
- AI applications in information retrieval include Language Models, Information Extraction, Natural Language Processing, Machine Translation, and Speech Recognition.
