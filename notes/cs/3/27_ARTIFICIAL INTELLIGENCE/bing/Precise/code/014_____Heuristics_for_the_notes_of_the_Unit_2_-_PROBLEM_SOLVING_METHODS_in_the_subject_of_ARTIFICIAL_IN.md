### Heuristics for the notes of the Unit 2 - PROBLEM SOLVING METHODS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

- Heuristics are problem-solving methods that use shortcuts to produce good-enough solutions given a limited time frame or deadline.
- Heuristics are used when the optimal solution is not necessary or when the problem is too complex to solve using traditional methods.
- Heuristics can be used to solve problems in various fields, including artificial intelligence, computer science, and psychology.
- Heuristics can be classified into two types: general heuristics and specific heuristics.
- General heuristics are problem-solving methods that can be applied to a wide range of problems, while specific heuristics are tailored to a particular problem or domain.
- Some common heuristics used in artificial intelligence include hill climbing, simulated annealing, and genetic algorithms.
- Heuristics can be used in combination with other problem-solving methods, such as search algorithms, to improve the efficiency and effectiveness of the solution.
- Heuristics can also be used to guide the search process by providing an estimate of the distance from the current state to the goal state.
- Heuristics can be learned from experience or designed by experts in the field.
- The use of heuristics can lead to suboptimal solutions, but they can also provide a good starting point for further optimization.
