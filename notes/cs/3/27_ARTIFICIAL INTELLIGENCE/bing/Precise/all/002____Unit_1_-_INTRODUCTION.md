## Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION

1. Introduction refers to the beginning or the preliminary explanation of a topic or subject.
2. It is the first section of a piece of writing, speech, or presentation, and it serves to introduce the topic to the audience.
3. The introduction should provide background information, define key terms, and explain the purpose and scope of the topic.
4. It should also capture the reader's attention and interest, and provide a clear and concise overview of the main points that will be covered in the rest of the text.
5. A well-written introduction is essential for setting the tone and providing a clear direction for the rest of the text.
