## Unit 2 - PROBLEM SOLVING METHODS

Problem solving is the process of finding a solution to a problem. There are several methods that can be used to solve problems, including:

1. **Trial and Error:** This method involves trying different solutions until the problem is solved. It is often used when there is no clear solution to the problem.

2. **Algorithm:** An algorithm is a step-by-step procedure for solving a problem. It is often used when the problem can be broken down into smaller, more manageable parts.

3. **Heuristics:** Heuristics are rules of thumb or mental shortcuts that can be used to quickly find a solution to a problem. They are often used when time is a factor and a quick solution is needed.

4. **Divide and Conquer:** This method involves breaking the problem down into smaller, more manageable parts and solving each part separately. It is often used when the problem is complex and difficult to solve as a whole.

5. **Backtracking:** Backtracking involves retracing your steps to find a solution to a problem. It is often used when the solution to the problem is not immediately obvious.

6. **Brainstorming:** Brainstorming involves generating a large number of ideas in a short amount of time. It is often used when creativity is needed to solve the problem.

These are just a few of the many problem-solving methods that can be used. The best method to use will depend on the specific problem and the circumstances in which it is being solved. It is important to choose the right method for the problem at hand in order to find the most effective solution.
