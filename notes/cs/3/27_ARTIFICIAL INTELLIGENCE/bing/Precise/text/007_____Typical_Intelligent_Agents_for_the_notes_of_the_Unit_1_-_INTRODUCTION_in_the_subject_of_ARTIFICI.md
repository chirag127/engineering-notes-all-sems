### Typical Intelligent Agents

An intelligent agent is a system that can perceive its environment, reason about it, and take actions to achieve its goals. There are several types of intelligent agents, including:

1. **Simple reflex agents**: These agents select actions based on the current percept, ignoring the rest of the percept history. They are based on condition-action rules, where the condition part matches the current percept and the action part specifies the action to be taken.

2. **Model-based reflex agents**: These agents maintain an internal model of the world, which is used to keep track of the part of the world that is not currently visible. The agent uses this model to choose actions that are based on the current percept and the model.

3. **Goal-based agents**: These agents have a goal that describes the desired state of the world. They choose actions based on the desirability of the outcomes that the actions would produce, given the current percept and the agent's knowledge of the world.

4. **Utility-based agents**: These agents have a utility function that measures the desirability of the world states. They choose actions based on the expected utility of the outcomes that the actions would produce, given the current percept and the agent's knowledge of the world.

5. **Learning agents**: These agents can improve their performance over time by learning from their experience. They have a learning element that uses feedback from the environment to improve the agent's performance.

These are some of the typical intelligent agents that can be found in the field of artificial intelligence. They can be used in a variety of applications, including robotics, gaming, and decision-making systems.
