### Prolog Programming

Prolog is a logic programming language associated with artificial intelligence and computational linguistics. It is widely used for knowledge representation and problem-solving in AI.

Here are some key points to note about Prolog programming:

1. Prolog is a declarative language, meaning that the programmer specifies the desired relationship between the inputs and outputs, rather than specifying the steps to achieve the desired result.
2. Prolog programs consist of facts and rules, which are used to represent knowledge and make inferences.
3. Prolog uses a technique called unification to match patterns and find solutions to problems.
4. Prolog has built-in support for recursion, which allows for the definition of complex relationships and problem-solving strategies.
5. Prolog is well-suited for tasks such as natural language processing, expert systems, and theorem proving.
