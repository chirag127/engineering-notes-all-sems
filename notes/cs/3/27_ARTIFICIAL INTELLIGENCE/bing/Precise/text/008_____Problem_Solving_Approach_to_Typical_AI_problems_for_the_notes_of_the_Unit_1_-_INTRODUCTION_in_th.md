### Problem Solving Approach to Typical AI problems

1. **Define the problem**: Clearly define the problem and its scope. Identify the desired outcome and the constraints that must be satisfied.
2. **Analyze the problem**: Break down the problem into smaller, more manageable sub-problems. Identify the key components and relationships between them.
3. **Develop a plan**: Develop a step-by-step plan to solve the problem. This may involve the use of algorithms, heuristics, or other problem-solving techniques.
4. **Implement the plan**: Execute the plan, making adjustments as necessary based on feedback and results.
5. **Evaluate the results**: Evaluate the results to determine if the problem has been solved satisfactorily. If not, revise the plan and repeat the process.

This approach can be applied to a wide range of AI problems, including natural language processing, computer vision, and machine learning. It is important to note that the problem-solving process is iterative and may require multiple attempts to find a satisfactory solution.
