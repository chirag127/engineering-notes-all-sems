### Search Strategies

Search strategies are methods used to solve problems in Artificial Intelligence. These methods are used to find solutions to problems by exploring the problem space in a systematic manner. There are several search strategies that can be used, including:

1. **Depth-first search**: This strategy starts at the root node and explores as far as possible along each branch before backtracking.

2. **Breadth-first search**: This strategy starts at the root node and explores all the neighboring nodes at the present depth level before moving on to the nodes at the next depth level.

3. **Uniform-cost search**: This strategy expands the node with the lowest path cost.

4. **Greedy search**: This strategy expands the node that is closest to the goal, as estimated by a heuristic function.

5. **A* search**: This strategy combines the benefits of uniform-cost search and greedy search by expanding the node with the lowest value of the sum of the path cost and the estimated cost to the goal.

These are some of the search strategies that can be used in problem solving in Artificial Intelligence. Each strategy has its own advantages and disadvantages, and the choice of strategy depends on the specific problem at hand.
