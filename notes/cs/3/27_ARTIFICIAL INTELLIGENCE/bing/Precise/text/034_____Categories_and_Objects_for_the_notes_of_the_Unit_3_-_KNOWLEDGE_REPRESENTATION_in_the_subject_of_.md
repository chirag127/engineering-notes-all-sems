### Categories and Objects

- Categories and objects are fundamental concepts in knowledge representation.
- Categories are used to group similar objects together.
- Objects are instances of categories and have properties that define them.
- Categories can have subcategories, which are more specific groupings of objects.
- Objects can belong to multiple categories.
- Categories and objects can be used to represent knowledge in a structured and organized manner.
- In artificial intelligence, categories and objects are used to represent knowledge and reason about the world.
- Categories and objects can be used to create ontologies, which are formal representations of knowledge.
- Ontologies can be used to support reasoning and decision making in artificial intelligence systems.
