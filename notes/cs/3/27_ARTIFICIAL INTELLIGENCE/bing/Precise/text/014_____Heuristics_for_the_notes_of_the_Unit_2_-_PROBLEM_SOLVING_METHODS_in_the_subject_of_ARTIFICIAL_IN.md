### Heuristics for the notes of the Unit 2 - PROBLEM SOLVING METHODS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

- Heuristics are problem-solving methods that use shortcuts to produce good-enough solutions given a limited time frame or deadline.
- Heuristics are used to speed up the process of finding a satisfactory solution, where an exhaustive search is impractical.
- Heuristics can be contrasted with algorithms, which are guaranteed to find the optimal solution, but may take longer to do so.
- Heuristics can be categorized into two types: general heuristics and domain-specific heuristics.
- General heuristics are problem-solving methods that can be applied to a wide range of problems, while domain-specific heuristics are tailored to a particular problem or domain.
- Examples of general heuristics include trial and error, hill climbing, and means-ends analysis.
- Examples of domain-specific heuristics include the A* algorithm for pathfinding and the min-max algorithm for game playing.
- Heuristics can be useful in situations where the optimal solution is not necessary, or where the cost of finding the optimal solution is too high.
- However, heuristics can also lead to suboptimal solutions or even failure to find a solution.
- It is important to carefully choose and evaluate the heuristics used in problem-solving to ensure that they are effective and efficient.
