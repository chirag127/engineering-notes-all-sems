### Unit 5 - APPLICATIONS: Language Models

A language model is a computational model that assigns probabilities to sequences of words. These models are used in various natural language processing tasks, such as speech recognition, machine translation, and text generation.

Some key points to consider when studying language models include:

1. **N-gram models**: One of the simplest and most widely used language models is the n-gram model, which assigns probabilities to sequences of words based on the frequency of their occurrence in a training corpus.

2. **Neural language models**: More recently, neural network-based language models have become popular. These models use a neural network to learn a distributed representation of words, and can capture more complex relationships between words than n-gram models.

3. **Perplexity**: A common metric for evaluating the performance of a language model is perplexity, which measures how well the model predicts a held-out test set.

4. **Applications**: Language models have a wide range of applications, including machine translation, text generation, and speech recognition. They can also be used to improve the performance of other natural language processing tasks, such as sentiment analysis and named entity recognition.
