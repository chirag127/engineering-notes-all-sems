### Unit 5 - APPLICATIONS: Moving

1. **Robotics**: AI can be used to control the movement of robots, allowing them to navigate complex environments and perform tasks that require precise movements.
2. **Autonomous vehicles**: AI is used in the development of self-driving cars, which can navigate roads and traffic without human intervention.
3. **Logistics**: AI can be used to optimize the movement of goods and materials, improving efficiency and reducing costs in industries such as transportation and warehousing.
4. **Gaming**: AI is used to control the movement of non-player characters in video games, making them more realistic and challenging for players.
5. **Prosthetics**: AI can be used to control the movement of advanced prosthetics, allowing individuals with disabilities to regain mobility and independence.
6. **Sports**: AI can be used to analyze the movement of athletes, providing insights that can improve performance and reduce the risk of injury.
