# Problem Solving Approach to Typical AI problems

## Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION

### ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

1. **Define the problem:** Clearly state the problem and its objectives. Identify the desired outcome and the constraints that must be taken into account.
2. **Gather information:** Collect relevant data and information about the problem. This may include historical data, expert opinions, and other sources of information.
3. **Generate solutions:** Use various techniques to generate potential solutions to the problem. This may include brainstorming, simulation, and optimization methods.
4. **Evaluate solutions:** Assess the potential solutions to determine their feasibility and effectiveness. This may involve testing, simulation, and cost-benefit analysis.
5. **Select the best solution:** Choose the solution that best meets the objectives and constraints of the problem.
6. **Implement the solution:** Put the selected solution into action. This may involve developing a plan, allocating resources, and executing the plan.
7. **Monitor and evaluate:** Monitor the implementation of the solution and evaluate its effectiveness. Make adjustments as necessary to ensure that the desired outcome is achieved.

These are the general steps that can be followed when approaching typical AI problems. It is important to note that the specific approach may vary depending on the nature of the problem and the available resources.
