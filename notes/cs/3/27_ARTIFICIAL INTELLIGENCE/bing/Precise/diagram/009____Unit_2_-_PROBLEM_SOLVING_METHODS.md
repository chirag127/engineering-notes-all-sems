## Unit 2 - PROBLEM SOLVING METHODS

Problem solving is the process of finding a solution to a problem. There are several methods that can be used to solve problems, including:

1. **Trial and Error:** This method involves trying different solutions until the problem is solved. It is often used when there is no clear solution to the problem.

2. **Algorithms:** An algorithm is a step-by-step procedure for solving a problem. It is often used when the problem can be broken down into smaller, more manageable parts.

3. **Heuristics:** A heuristic is a rule of thumb or a mental shortcut that can be used to solve a problem. It is often used when the problem is complex and there is no clear solution.

4. **Divide and Conquer:** This method involves breaking the problem down into smaller, more manageable parts and solving each part separately. It is often used when the problem is large and complex.

5. **Backtracking:** Backtracking involves trying different solutions and then backing up to a previous step if the solution does not work. It is often used when the problem has multiple possible solutions.

6. **Brainstorming:** Brainstorming involves generating a large number of ideas in a short period of time. It is often used when the problem requires creative thinking.

These are some of the common problem-solving methods. Each method has its own strengths and weaknesses, and the best method to use depends on the specific problem at hand. It is important to choose the right method for the problem in order to find an effective solution.
