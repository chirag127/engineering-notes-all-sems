### Hardware for the notes of the Unit 5 - APPLICATIONS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

1. **CPU (Central Processing Unit)**: The CPU is the primary component of a computer that performs most of the processing. It is responsible for executing instructions and performing calculations. AI applications require fast and powerful CPUs to perform complex calculations and data processing.

2. **GPU (Graphics Processing Unit)**: A GPU is a specialized electronic circuit designed to rapidly manipulate and alter memory to accelerate the creation of images in a frame buffer intended for output to a display device. GPUs are used in AI applications for their ability to perform parallel processing, which is useful for tasks such as image and video processing.

3. **RAM (Random Access Memory)**: RAM is a form of computer memory that can be read and changed in any order, typically used to store working data and machine code. AI applications require large amounts of RAM to store and process large datasets.

4. **Storage**: Storage devices such as hard drives and solid-state drives are used to store data and programs. AI applications require fast and large storage devices to store and retrieve large amounts of data quickly.

5. **Networking**: Networking hardware such as network interface cards, routers, and switches are used to connect computers and devices to a network. AI applications often require fast and reliable network connections to access data and communicate with other systems.

6. **Sensors and Actuators**: Sensors and actuators are used in AI applications to interact with the physical world. Sensors are used to collect data, while actuators are used to perform actions based on the data and decisions made by the AI system.

7. **Specialized Hardware**: Specialized hardware such as FPGAs (Field-Programmable Gate Arrays) and ASICs (Application-Specific Integrated Circuits) are used in some AI applications to perform specific tasks more efficiently than general-purpose hardware.
