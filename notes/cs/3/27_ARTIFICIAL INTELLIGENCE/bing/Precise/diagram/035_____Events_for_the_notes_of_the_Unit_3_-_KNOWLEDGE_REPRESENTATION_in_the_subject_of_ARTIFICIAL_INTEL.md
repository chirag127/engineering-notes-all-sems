### Unit 3 - KNOWLEDGE REPRESENTATION in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

#### Events

1. An event is a change of state or a happening that occurs at a specific time and place.
2. Events can be represented using various formalisms such as situation calculus, event calculus, and fluent calculus.
3. Situation calculus represents events as actions that cause a change in the state of the world.
4. Event calculus represents events as happening at specific time points and uses time intervals to represent the duration of events.
5. Fluent calculus extends situation calculus by introducing fluents, which are properties that can change over time.
6. These formalisms can be used to represent and reason about events in artificial intelligence systems.
7. Events can also be represented using graphical models such as Bayesian networks and influence diagrams.
8. These models can be used to represent the probabilistic relationships between events and to make predictions about future events.
