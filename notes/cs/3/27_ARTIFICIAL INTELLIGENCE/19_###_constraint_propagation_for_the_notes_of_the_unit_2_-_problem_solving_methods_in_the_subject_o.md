Constraint Propagation

Constraint Propagation is a problem-solving technique in Artificial Intelligence that is used to reduce the number of possibilities in a problem by using constraints. Constraints are conditions that must be satisfied in order for a solution to be considered valid.

In constraint propagation, the constraints in a problem are used to eliminate possibilities and narrow down the search space. This makes it easier to find a solution, as there are fewer possibilities to consider.

Constraint propagation is used in a variety of domains, including constraint satisfaction problems, scheduling problems, and optimization problems. It is also used in the development of algorithms for solving problems in artificial intelligence, such as in the optimization of neural networks.
