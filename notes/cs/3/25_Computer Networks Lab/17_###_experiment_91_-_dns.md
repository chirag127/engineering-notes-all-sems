### Experiment 9.1 - DNS

Experiment 9.1: DNS (Domain Name System):
- This experiment covers the study of the Domain Name System (DNS), a hierarchical distributed naming system for computers, services, or any resource connected to the Internet or a private network.
- Topics may include:
  - Overview of the DNS and its role in the Internet.
  - Structure and components of the DNS, including domains, zones, and name servers.
  - Resolution of domain names to IP addresses, including recursive and iterative queries.
  - Types of DNS records, including A, MX, CNAME, and NS records.
  - Configuration and management of DNS servers, including adding and updating DNS records.
  - Study of the security of the DNS, including DNS spoofing and DNS cache poisoning.
  - Applications of the DNS, including email, web browsing, and network management.
  - Analysis of real-world examples of the DNS, including domain name registrars and DNS service providers.
