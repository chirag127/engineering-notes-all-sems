## Experiment 12 - To learn handling and configuration of networking hardware like RJ-45 connector, CAT-6 cable, crimping tool, etc

Experiment 12: Handling and Configuration of Networking Hardware:
- This experiment covers the handling and configuration of networking hardware, such as RJ-45 connectors, CAT-6 cables, crimping tools, and other components.
- Topics may include:
  - Overview of networking hardware and its components.
  - Understanding the different types of cables, connectors, and tools used in networking.
  - Crimping and terminating cables, including the use of crimping tools and connectors.
  - Configuring and testing networking hardware, including switches, routers, and firewalls.
  - Troubleshooting and resolving common issues with networking hardware, such as cable problems, connectivity issues, and configuration errors.
  - Best practices for handling and configuring networking hardware, including safety, security, and performance considerations.
