### Experiment 2.2 - Study of Client – Server model

Experiment 2.2: Study of Client-Server Model:
- This experiment covers the study of the client-server model in computer networks.
- Topics may include:
  - Definition of the client-server model and its components.
  - Communication between the client and server, including request-response and data transfer.
  - Types of clients and servers, including thick and thin clients and stateful and stateless servers.
  - Advantages and disadvantages of the client-server model, including scalability, reliability, and security.
  - Applications of the client-server model, including web applications, email, and file sharing.
  - Study of the protocols used in the client-server model, including HTTP, FTP, and SMTP.
  - Analysis of real-world examples of the client-server model, including online banking, e-commerce, and social networking.
