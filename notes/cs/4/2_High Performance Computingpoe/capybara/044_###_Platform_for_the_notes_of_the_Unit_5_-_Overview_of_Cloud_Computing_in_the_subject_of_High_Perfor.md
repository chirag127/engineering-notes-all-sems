### Platform for the notes of the Unit 5 - Overview of Cloud Computing in the subject of High Performance Computing

Cloud computing is a technology that allows users to access computing resources, such as servers, storage, and applications, over the internet. It has become a popular choice for businesses and individuals as it offers several benefits such as cost savings, scalability, and flexibility. In this unit, we will be discussing the overview of cloud computing and its various platforms. Let's take a closer look at the different platforms for cloud computing.

1. Infrastructure as a Service (IaaS)
    - IaaS provides users with virtualized computing resources, such as storage, networking, and servers, over the internet.
    - Examples of IaaS platforms include Amazon Web Services (AWS), Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud Platform.

2. Platform as a Service (PaaS)
    - PaaS provides users with a platform to develop, run, and manage applications without the need for infrastructure management.
    - Examples of PaaS platforms include Heroku, Google App Engine, and Microsoft Azure.

3. Software as a Service (SaaS)
    - SaaS provides users with access to software applications over the internet without the need for installation or maintenance.
    - Examples of SaaS platforms include Salesforce, Office 365, and Dropbox.

Mnemonics and learning tricks:
- Remember IaaS as Infrastructure as a Service, which means you get virtualized computing resources.
- Remember PaaS as Platform as a Service, which means you get a platform to develop and manage applications.
- Remember SaaS as Software as a Service, which means you get access to software applications over the internet.

Advantages of cloud computing platforms:
- Cost savings as there is no need to invest in expensive hardware and infrastructure.
- Scalability as resources can be easily scaled up or down based on demand.
- Flexibility as users can access resources from anywhere with an internet connection.

Disadvantages of cloud computing platforms:
- Security concerns as data is stored on third-party servers.
- Dependence on the internet and service provider's reliability.
- Limited control over infrastructure and resources.

Examples of cloud computing platforms and their applications:
- Amazon Web Services (AWS) is used by businesses for data storage and analysis, application development, and website hosting.
- Microsoft Azure is used for creating and deploying applications, running virtual machines, and for data storage and analysis.
- Salesforce is used for customer relationship management (CRM), marketing automation, and analytics. 

In conclusion, understanding the different cloud computing platforms is essential for businesses and individuals looking to adopt cloud computing technology. Each platform has its own advantages and disadvantages, and choosing the right one depends on the specific needs and requirements of the user.
