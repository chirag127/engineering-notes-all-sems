### Resource Management and Scheduling for the notes of the Unit 3 - Overview of Cluster Computing in the subject of High Performance Computing

Resource management and scheduling are critical components of cluster computing. These processes ensure that resources are utilized efficiently and that jobs are executed in a timely manner. In this section, we will discuss resource management and scheduling in detail.

#### Resource Management

Resource management involves the allocation and management of resources in a cluster. These resources include CPU time, memory, network bandwidth, and storage. The following are some key concepts related to resource management:

- **Resource Allocation:** This involves assigning resources to users or jobs. Resource allocation can be based on a variety of factors, such as job priority, user quotas, and availability of resources.

- **Resource Monitoring:** This involves tracking the usage of resources in a cluster. Resource monitoring can be used to detect resource bottlenecks, identify underutilized resources, and optimize resource allocation.

- **Resource Reservation:** This involves reserving resources for specific jobs or users. Resource reservation can ensure that critical jobs have the resources they need and can prevent resource contention.

#### Scheduling

Scheduling involves determining the order in which jobs are executed in a cluster. The following are some key concepts related to scheduling:

- **Job Prioritization:** This involves assigning priorities to jobs based on their importance. Jobs with higher priorities are executed before jobs with lower priorities.

- **Job Dependencies:** This involves specifying dependencies between jobs. For example, a job may require the output of another job as input.

- **Fairness:** This involves ensuring that all users or jobs are treated fairly in terms of resource allocation and job execution.

#### Learning Tricks

- One way to remember the key concepts of resource management is to remember the acronym ARM, which stands for Allocation, Monitoring, and Reservation.
- To remember the key concepts of scheduling, you can use the acronym JPF, which stands for Job Prioritization, Dependencies, and Fairness.

In conclusion, resource management and scheduling are critical components of cluster computing. By efficiently managing and scheduling resources, clusters can execute jobs in a timely and efficient manner, resulting in improved performance and productivity.
