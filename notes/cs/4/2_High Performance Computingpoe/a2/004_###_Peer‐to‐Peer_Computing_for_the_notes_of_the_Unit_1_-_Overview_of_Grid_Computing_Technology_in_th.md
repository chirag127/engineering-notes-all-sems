### Peer‐to‐Peer Computing for the notes of the Unit 1 - Overview of Grid Computing Technology in the subject of High Performance Computing

Peer-to-peer (P2P) computing refers to the sharing of computer resources and services by direct exchange between systems. In a P2P network, the nodes (computers/devices) can act as both clients and servers that both consume and provide resources. P2P computing is decentralized, with all nodes having equivalent capabilities and responsibilities. This is in contrast to the traditional client-server model where some dedicated servers provide resources/services to clients.

Some key characteristics of P2P networks are:

- Decentralization: There is no central authority or centralized index/database. The nodes are self-organized.
- Resource sharing: The nodes share their resources like storage, computing, content, etc. with other nodes.
- Dynamic membership: Nodes can join and leave the network frequently. The network topology can change dynamically.
- Adaptability: The network can adapt to failures and accommodate changes. There is no single point of failure.

Advantages of P2P networks:

- Scalability: The networks can scale well to large sizes as there is no central server bottleneck. The workload is distributed across many nodes.
- Fault tolerance: The decentralized nature makes the networks fault tolerant as there is no single point of failure. The network can sustain node failures and adapt.
- Low cost: The resources are shared by many nodes, so the costs are distributed leading to potentially lower costs.

Disadvantages of P2P networks:

- Security: The decentralized and dynamic nature can make security harder to achieve. It is difficult to enforce access control and prevent malicious nodes.
- Trust: It can be difficult to establish trust between nodes and verify identities/resources in a decentralized network.
- Performance unpredictability: The performance can be variable and hard to predict as it depends on the resources and behaviors of the participating nodes which can change frequently.
