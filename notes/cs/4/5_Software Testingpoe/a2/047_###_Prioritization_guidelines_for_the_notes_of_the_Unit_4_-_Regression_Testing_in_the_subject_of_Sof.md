### Prioritization guidelines for the notes of the Unit 4 - Regression Testing in the subject of Software Testing

1. Test cases with high priority and severity:
- Test cases which failed in previous testing cycle and were marked to be re-tested.
- Test cases for core functionalities and major features of the software. Core functionalities are more prone to regression.
- Test cases with high severity bugs in previous cycle. There are chances of recurrence of severe bugs.

2. Test cases with medium priority:
- Test cases for medium risk modules or features. Medium risk features are moderately prone to regression.
- Test cases executed in previous cycle but with less number of iterations. May be bottleneck test cases or test cases with some modifications done.

3. Test cases with low priority:
- Test cases for low risk modules or features. Low risk features are less prone to regression.
- New test cases added to the test suite. New test cases are less likely to detect regression.
- Test cases not executed in the previous cycle.

Tips and Mnemonics:
- Prioritize "HFHF" - High priority, high severity first
- Core functionalities are king - test them first
- Recurrence of severe bugs is costly - test high severity cases first
- Medium risk may include medium priority
- New test cases have low chance of finding regression - keep them low priority

Advantages of prioritization:
- Effective utilization of time available for regression testing.
- Critical bugs and critical functionalities are tested first.
- Cost optimization.
