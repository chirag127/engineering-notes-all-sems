### Tailoring Software Quality Assurance Program by Reviews for the notes of the Unit 1 - Review of Software Engineering in the subject of Software Testing

The Software Quality Assurance (SQA) program can be tailored to the project needs by planning and conducting reviews at various stages of the software development life cycle.

- Requirements review: Review the requirements specification document for consistency, completeness, unambiguousness, verifiability, modifiability, and traceability. Look for requirements that are incomplete, inconsistent, redundant or unnecessary.
- Design review: Review the design document for -
-- Meeting functional and non-functional requirements.
-- Simplicity, understandability and modularity.
-- Use of suitable design techniques and principles.
-- Potential architectural flaws or weaknesses.
- Code review: Review the source code -
-- For adherence to coding standards.
-- Logic errors, incorrect algorithms or data structures.
-- Potential concurrency issues or security flaws.
-- Opportunities for code improvement and optimization.
- Test plan review: Review the test plan for -
-- Coverage of functional, non-functional and business requirements.
-- Identification of appropriate test types (unit, integration, system, acceptance, etc.).
-- Adequate test data selection and test environment setup.
-- Consistency with the overall project schedule and resource allocation.

Benefits of reviews include:
- Early detection of defects and issues.
- Opportunity to get feedback and perspectives from multiple stakeholders.
- Spreading knowledge and increasing shared understanding.
- Improving designs, requirements and code quality.
- Reducing rework and associated costs.

However, reviews need to be planned and conducted efficiently to minimize the additional time and effort spent. Mnemonics and learning tricks can be used to remember the types of reviews and their objectives. But they may not be easy to recall and apply for everyone. In such cases, it is better to understand the concepts thoroughly rather than relying on tricks.
