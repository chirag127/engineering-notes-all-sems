### Verification for the notes of the Unit 1 - Review of Software Engineering in the subject of Software Testing

- Verification is the process of evaluating software to determine whether it satisfies the specified requirements. It is done through methods like reviews, inspections, and testing.
- Reviews and inspections are performed on the various artifacts produced during software development like requirements, design, code, etc. to identify errors and faults.
- Testing is performed on the actual software product to identify defects. It involves executing the software with the intent of finding errors.
- Different types of testing like functional testing, non-functional testing, unit testing, integration testing, system testing, acceptance testing, etc. are performed to validate different aspects of the software.
- Testing is an important part of software quality assurance and helps to build quality into the software. Through testing and verification, the reliability and quality of the software can be improved.
- Some advantages of verification and testing are that errors are identified early, cost of fixing defects is reduced, customer satisfaction is increased, and risk of failure is decreased.
- Disadvantages are that verification and testing require time, effort, and cost. There is also the possibility that some defects may remain unidentified.
- Verification and testing are crucial parts of software engineering and help to ensure that the software product satisfies its requirements and is of high quality. Several techniques and methods are applied to effectively verify software.
