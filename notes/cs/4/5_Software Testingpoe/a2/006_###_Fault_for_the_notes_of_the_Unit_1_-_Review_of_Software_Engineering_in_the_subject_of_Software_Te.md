### Fault for the notes of the Unit 1 - Review of Software Engineering in the subject of Software Testing

1. Software Fault: A software fault occurs due to a mistake made in the software requirements, design or code. It leads to an incorrect behavior of the software.

2. Bug: A bug is a fault in the software that causes the software to behave unexpectedly or produce an incorrect output. Bugs are coding errors made by the programmers.

3. Failure: The deviation of the software from its expected delivery, service or result is called a failure. Failures occur due to faults in the software.

4. Error: An error is the discrepancy between the actual output or behavior of the software and the expected output or behavior. Errors occur due to faults in the software.

Advantages of finding and fixing faults:
- Increased reliability and quality of the software
- Reduced failures and customer dissatisfaction
- Reduced debugging and maintenance costs

Disadvantages of finding and fixing faults:
- Increased time required to develop and test the software
- Increased development and testing costs

Testing techniques to find faults:
- Requirements testing - Check if requirements are met
- Structural testing - Check implementation structure coverage
- Functional testing - Check software functionalities
- Performance testing - Check performance under load
- Compatibility testing - Check compatibility with other systems
- Usability testing - Check ease of use
- Regression testing - Re-test after modifications to check for re-introduced faults

Examples of faults:
- Divide by zero error
- Out of bounds array access
- Race condition leading to incorrect output
- Infinite loop leading to system freeze

Applications:
- Faults in software can lead to system crashes resulting in loss of time, money and resources.
- Thorough testing and early detection of faults leads to high quality and reliable software.
- Process improvements and fault prevention techniques help reduce the number of faults in software.
