1. Software Engineering: Software Engineering is the systematic approach to the development, operation, maintenance, and retirement of computer software. It encompasses contributions from various disciplines such as mathematics, business administration, project management, computer science, and interface design. The goal of software engineering is to produce high-quality, maintainable, and long-lasting software that meets the needs of its users.

2. Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC): SDLC is a process used by the software industry to design, develop and test high quality softwares. The SDLC aims to produce a high-quality software that meets or exceeds customer expectations, reaches completion within times and cost estimates. The common steps involved in the SDLC are: Requirements analysis, Design, Coding and debugging, Testing, Installation and deployment, Maintenance.

3. Software Testing: Software Testing is a process of evaluating the functionality of a software application with the intent of finding software bugs (errors or other defects). It involves operating the system under controlled conditions, analyzing and comparing actual outcomes to expected outcomes, and reporting any differences found. The goal of software testing is to provide information about the quality of the product or service to stakeholders. This allows them to assess risk, make decisions, and take corrective action.
