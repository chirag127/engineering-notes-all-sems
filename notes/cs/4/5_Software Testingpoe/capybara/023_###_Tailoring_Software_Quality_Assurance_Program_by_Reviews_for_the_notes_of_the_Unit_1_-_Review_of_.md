### Tailoring Software Quality Assurance Program by Reviews for the notes of the Unit 1 - Review of Software Engineering in the subject of Software Testing

Software quality assurance is a critical process that ensures the software product meets the desired quality standards. One of the most effective ways to achieve software quality assurance is through reviews. Reviews are a systematic examination of software artifacts by one or more individuals to detect and correct errors, improve quality, and ensure compliance with standards and requirements.

Tailoring software quality assurance programs by reviews is a process that enables organizations to customize their software quality assurance program to meet their specific needs. The process involves identifying the review types, review objectives, review criteria, roles and responsibilities, review process, and review tools that best suit the organization's needs.

Here are some important points to remember while tailoring software quality assurance program by reviews:

1. Identify the review types: There are different types of reviews, including walkthroughs, technical reviews, code reviews, and inspections. Each of these review types has its own strengths and weaknesses, and it's essential to identify the review types that are best suited for the organization's needs.

2. Define review objectives: The objectives of the review should be clearly defined to ensure that the review process is focused and effective. The objectives should be specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, and time-bound.

3. Define review criteria: The review criteria should be clear and well-defined to ensure that the review process is objective and consistent. The criteria should be based on the organization's quality standards, best practices, and industry standards.

4. Define roles and responsibilities: The roles and responsibilities of the review participants should be clearly defined to ensure that the review process is efficient and effective. The roles and responsibilities should be assigned based on the participants' expertise, skills, and experience.

5. Define the review process: The review process should be well-defined and documented to ensure that the review process is consistent and repeatable. The review process should include pre-review planning, review meetings, review reporting, and review follow-up.

6. Use review tools: The use of review tools can help to streamline the review process and improve its effectiveness. Review tools can include checklists, templates, defect tracking tools, and automated review tools.

Mnemonic and learning tricks:

Remember the acronym "IT DRIVES" to help you remember the key points to consider while tailoring a software quality assurance program by reviews:

- Identify the review types
- Define review objectives
- Define review criteria
- Define roles and responsibilities
- Define the review process
- Use review tools
- Ensure consistency and repeatability of the review process.
