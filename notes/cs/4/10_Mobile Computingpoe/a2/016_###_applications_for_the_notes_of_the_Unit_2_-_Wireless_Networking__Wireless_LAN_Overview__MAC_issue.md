### Applications for the notes of the Unit 2 - Wireless Networking, Wireless LAN Overview: MAC issues, IEEE 802.11, Blue Tooth, Wireless in the subject of Mobile Computing

- IEEE 802.11: Wi-Fi is the most popular wireless networking technology which is based on IEEE 802.11 standards. It operates in the 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz frequency bands with multiple modulation techniques (DSSS, HR-DSSS, OFDM). Advantages include high speed, low cost, wide availability. Disadvantages include limited range, security issues, interference. Applications include internet access, office networking, etc.
- Mnemonic: *802.11* stands for the year it was ratified - **1997**. *a/b/g/n/ac* are amendments - **a**ll **b**oys **g**o **n**ow **a**cross **c**ampus.
- Bluetooth: Bluetooth is a short-range wireless technology standard for exchanging data over short distances using radio waves in the frequency range of 2.4 to 2.485 GHz from fixed and mobile devices, and building personal area networks (PANs). Advantages include low cost, low power. Applications include wireless headphones, fitness trackers, etc.
- MAC issues: Medium Access Control (MAC) refers to the set of rules that govern how devices share a common physical transmission medium. Key issues in wireless MAC include hidden terminal problem, exposed terminal problem, capture effect, etc. Solutions include Carrier Sense Multiple Access (CSMA), Request To Send/Clear To Send (RTS/CTS), etc.
