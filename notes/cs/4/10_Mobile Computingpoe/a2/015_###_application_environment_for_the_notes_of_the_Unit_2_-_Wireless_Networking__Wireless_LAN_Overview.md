### Application environment for the notes of the Unit 2 - Wireless Networking, Wireless LAN Overview: MAC issues, IEEE 802.11, Blue Tooth, Wireless in the subject of Mobile Computing

- Wireless networking enables devices to communicate over long distances without the need for cables or wires. This allows devices to be mobile and stay connected to the network.
- The medium access control (MAC) sublayer is responsible for sharing the wireless medium between different devices and coordinating access to the network. Since the wireless medium is shared, MAC protocols are important to handle issues like collisions, interference, and varying bandwidth.
- The IEEE 802.11 standard specifies over-the-air protocols between devices and wireless access points. The most popular versions are 802.11a, b, g, and n, which specify different wireless frequencies and speeds. 802.11ax is the latest standard providing higher speeds and efficiency.
- Bluetooth is a short-range wireless technology standard for connecting nearby devices like headphones, speakers, and other peripherals. Bluetooth uses a technique called spread-spectrum frequency hopping to prevent interference. Bluetooth is commonly used to connect wireless headphones or speakers to phones.
- Wireless networking is important for mobile devices since it enables connectivity without cables. This allows devices to stay connected while moving or being used in different locations. However, wireless networking can face issues like interference, security vulnerabilities, and limited range. Proper protocols and security measures need to be in place to handle these challenges.
