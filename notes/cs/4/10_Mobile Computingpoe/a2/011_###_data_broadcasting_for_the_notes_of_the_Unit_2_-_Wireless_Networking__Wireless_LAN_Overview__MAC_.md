### Data Broadcasting for the notes of the Unit 2 - Wireless Networking, Wireless LAN Overview: MAC issues, IEEE 802.11, Blue Tooth, Wireless in the subject of Mobile Computing

- Data Broadcasting is a communication technique where information is transmitted from a single source to multiple destinations simultaneously.
- In Wireless Networking, data broadcasting is used to send information to all the devices in the network efficiently without the overhead of unicast communication where information is sent individually to each device.
- Some applications of data broadcasting in wireless networking are -
    -Sending emergency alerts to all devices in a region.
    -Streaming multimedia content to multiple devices.
    -Software/Firmware updates to multiple devices.
- The wireless technology standards like IEEE 802.11 (Wi-Fi), Bluetooth, etc. support data broadcasting through usage of special broadcast addresses and beacon signals.
- The major challenge with data broadcasting is scaling the wireless network in terms of number of devices and their distribution without significantly impacting the performance which requires usage of efficient Medium Access Control (MAC) techniques.
- Some tips to remember -
    -Data Broadcasting sends data from 1 to many (not individual unicast)
    -Common in Wireless for efficient group communication
    -Uses special addresses and signals (beacon, broadcast)
    -Scalability and performance challenges due to limited medium and collisions
    -MAC techniques help improve efficiency
