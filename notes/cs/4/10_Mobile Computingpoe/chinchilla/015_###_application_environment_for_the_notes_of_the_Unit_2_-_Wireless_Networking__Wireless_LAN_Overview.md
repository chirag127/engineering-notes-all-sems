### Application Environment for the Notes of Unit 2 - Wireless Networking, Wireless LAN Overview: MAC issues, IEEE 802.11, Blue Tooth, Wireless in the Subject of Mobile Computing

Wireless networking has become a crucial part of our daily lives, especially in the world of mobile computing. To learn about wireless LANs (Local Area Networks), we need to understand the application environment for the notes of Unit 2. This includes the MAC (Media Access Control) issues, IEEE 802.11 standards, Bluetooth technology and wireless communication.

In this section, we will discuss the following topics:

1. MAC Issues:
   - MAC layer is responsible for providing access to the shared wireless medium.
   - There are two types of MAC protocols: Distributed Coordination Function (DCF) and Point Coordination Function (PCF).
   - DCF is used in most of the wireless LANs, while PCF is optional.
   - DCF uses Carrier Sense Multiple Access with Collision Avoidance (CSMA/CA) as a medium access method.
   - CSMA/CA works by sensing the channel before transmitting and if the channel is busy, it waits for a random time before retrying to transmit.
   - However, the hidden node problem and the exposed node problem can still occur in DCF.

2. IEEE 802.11 Standards:
   - The IEEE 802.11 standards specify the physical and MAC layer specifications for wireless LANs.
   - The most commonly used standards are 802.11a, 802.11b, 802.11g, and 802.11n.
   - 802.11a and 802.11n operate at 5 GHz, while 802.11b and 802.11g operate at 2.4 GHz.
   - 802.11a has a higher data rate but shorter range, while 802.11b has a lower data rate but longer range.
   - 802.11g is backward compatible with 802.11b and has a higher data rate than 802.11b.
   - 802.11n is backward compatible with 802.11a/b/g and has a higher data rate and longer range.

3. Bluetooth Technology:
   - Bluetooth is a wireless technology that is used for short-range communication.
   - It operates at 2.4 GHz and has a range of up to 10 meters.
   - Bluetooth uses frequency hopping spread spectrum (FHSS) to avoid interference from other devices.
   - It also uses pairing to establish a secure connection between devices.
   - Bluetooth can be used for various applications, such as wireless headsets, file transfer, and wireless keyboards.

4. Wireless Communication:
   - Wireless communication is the transfer of information between devices without the use of wires.
   - It can be done using various wireless technologies, such as Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and cellular networks.
   - Wireless communication has many advantages, such as mobility, flexibility, and convenience.
   - However, it also has some disadvantages, such as limited range and bandwidth, and security issues.

Mnemonics and Learning Tricks:

1. To remember the IEEE 802.11 standards, you can use the mnemonic "ABGN" where A and N operate at 5 GHz, and B and G operate at 2.4 GHz.

2. To remember the advantages of wireless communication, you can use the mnemonic "MFC" where M stands for mobility, F for flexibility, and C for convenience.

In conclusion, understanding the application environment for wireless networking is crucial for mobile computing. By learning about MAC issues, IEEE 802.11 standards, Bluetooth technology, and wireless communication, we can build a solid foundation for further study of wireless LANs.
