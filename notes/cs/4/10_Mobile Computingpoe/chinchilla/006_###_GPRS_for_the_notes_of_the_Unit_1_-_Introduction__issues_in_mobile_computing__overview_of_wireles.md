### GPRS for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction, issues in mobile computing, overview of wireless telephony: cellular concept, GSM: in the subject of Mobile Computing

General Packet Radio Service (GPRS) is a mobile data service that provides packet-switched data transmission in the Global System for Mobile Communications (GSM) network. It is an important topic to understand in mobile computing, as it is a crucial technology for mobile internet access and data communication.

Here are some important points to understand about GPRS:

- GPRS is a wireless data communication service that allows mobile devices to transmit and receive data over the GSM network.
- It uses packet-switching technology to transmit data in small packets, which are then reassembled at the receiving end.
- GPRS offers higher data transfer rates than the traditional circuit-switched data services, which were used for voice communication.
- GPRS enables always-on internet connectivity, which means that the user does not have to establish a connection every time they want to access the internet.
- It is a relatively cost-effective way of transmitting data over the GSM network, as it is charged based on the amount of data transmitted rather than the duration of the connection.
- GPRS is widely used for mobile internet access, email, and other data communication applications on mobile devices.

Here are some advantages of using GPRS:

Here are some disadvantages of using GPRS:

- GPRS is slower than some of the newer mobile data technologies, such as 3G and 4G.
- It may not be available in all areas, as coverage depends on the availability of the GSM network.
- GPRS may not be suitable for applications that require high-speed data transfer, such as video streaming or online gaming.
