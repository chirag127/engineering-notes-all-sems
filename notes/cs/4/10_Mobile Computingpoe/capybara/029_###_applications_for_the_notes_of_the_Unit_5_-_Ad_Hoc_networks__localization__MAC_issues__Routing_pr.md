### Applications for the Notes of Unit 5 - Ad Hoc Networks, Localization, MAC Issues, Routing Protocols, Global State Routing (GSR) in Mobile Computing

Ad hoc networks have gained popularity in the field of mobile computing due to their ability to operate without a pre-existing infrastructure. In this unit, we will explore the different applications of ad hoc networks along with localization techniques, MAC issues, and routing protocols.

#### Ad Hoc Networks
- Ad hoc networks are used in emergency situations where there is no pre-existing infrastructure.
- They can also be used for military operations, disaster relief, and search and rescue missions.
- Ad hoc networks are also used in vehicular networks and wireless sensor networks.

#### Localization
- Localization is the process of determining the location of a mobile node in an ad hoc network.
- There are several techniques used for localization such as triangulation, signal strength, and time of arrival.
- Localization is important in applications such as tracking mobile nodes, navigation, and location-based services.

#### MAC Issues
- MAC issues in ad hoc networks arise due to the lack of a centralized control.
- One of the main issues is the hidden node problem, where two nodes cannot detect each other's transmissions.
- Another issue is the exposed node problem, where a node refrains from transmitting due to the interference in the transmission of another node.

#### Routing Protocols
- Routing protocols are used to establish communication between nodes in an ad hoc network.
- There are several routing protocols such as AODV, DSR, and OLSR.
- These protocols have their advantages and disadvantages and are suited for different types of networks.

#### Global State Routing (GSR)
- Global State Routing (GSR) is a routing protocol that performs better than traditional routing protocols in large-scale ad hoc networks.
- GSR is a proactive protocol that maintains a global view of the network topology.
- This protocol is suited for networks that have a high mobility rate and frequent topology changes.

In summary, ad hoc networks have a wide range of applications in mobile computing. Localization techniques, MAC issues, and routing protocols are important factors to consider while designing an ad hoc network. Global State Routing (GSR) is a protocol that outperforms traditional routing protocols in large-scale dynamic networks.
