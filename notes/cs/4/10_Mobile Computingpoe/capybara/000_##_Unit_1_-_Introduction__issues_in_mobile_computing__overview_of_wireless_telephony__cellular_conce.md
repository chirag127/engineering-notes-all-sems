## Unit 1 - Introduction, issues in mobile computing, overview of wireless telephony: cellular concept, GSM:

Mobile computing has become an integral part of our lives, and the use of mobile phones has increased significantly in recent years. This unit will provide an introduction to mobile computing, discuss the issues related to it, and give an overview of wireless telephony. The unit will also cover the cellular concept and GSM.

### Introduction:

- Mobile computing is the use of computing devices while on the move. These devices include laptops, smartphones, tablets, and wearable devices.
- Mobile computing has become popular due to its convenience and portability.
- The use of mobile computing devices has increased significantly in recent years, and it has become an integral part of our daily lives.

### Issues in mobile computing:

- Security: The security of data and privacy is a major concern in mobile computing. The risk of data breaches, hacking, and theft is high.
- Battery life: The battery life of mobile devices is a significant issue. The devices need to be charged frequently, and the battery life can be affected by various factors such as usage and temperature.
- Network connectivity: The network connectivity of mobile devices is crucial, and the devices need to be connected to a network to function properly.
- Compatibility: There are various mobile devices with different operating systems and software, which can create compatibility issues.

### Overview of wireless telephony:

- Wireless telephony is the use of wireless technology to transmit voice and data over a network.
- The wireless telephony system consists of a network of base stations, which are connected to a central switch.
- The wireless telephony system can be divided into two categories: cellular and non-cellular.
- The cellular system is the most commonly used wireless telephony system, and it is based on the cellular concept.

### Cellular concept:

- The cellular concept is based on the idea of dividing a geographical area into smaller cells, each served by a base station.
- The base stations are connected to a central switch, which routes calls to the appropriate base station.
- The advantage of the cellular concept is that it allows for more efficient use of the available frequency spectrum, and it reduces interference between different calls.
- The cellular network also provides better coverage and capacity compared to non-cellular networks.

### GSM:

- GSM stands for Global System for Mobile Communications, and it is the most widely used cellular network technology in the world.
- GSM uses a combination of time-division and frequency-division multiplexing to transmit data and voice over the network.
- GSM has several advantages, including better call quality, better security, and the ability to roam internationally.
- However, GSM also has some disadvantages, such as limited data transfer speeds and the need for specialized hardware.

Overall, this unit provides an introduction to mobile computing, discusses the issues related to it, and gives an overview of wireless telephony. The cellular concept and GSM are also covered in detail, providing a foundation for further study in this area.
