### Wireless Applications for the Notes of Unit 2 - Wireless Networking, Wireless LAN Overview: MAC Issues, IEEE 802.11, Blue Tooth, Wireless in the Subject of Mobile Computing

Wireless networking has become an integral part of our daily lives. It has brought about a revolution in the way we communicate and access information. In this section, we will discuss the various wireless applications used in mobile computing.

#### MAC Issues in Wireless LAN Overview

Wireless Local Area Networks (WLANs) use the Medium Access Control (MAC) protocol to access the wireless medium. The MAC protocol is responsible for ensuring that wireless transmissions do not interfere with each other. Some of the MAC issues that arise in WLANs are:

- Hidden Terminal Problem: This occurs when two wireless devices are within range of a third device, but not within range of each other. As a result, they may transmit simultaneously, leading to interference and data loss.
- Exposed Terminal Problem: This occurs when a wireless device refrains from transmitting because it believes another device is already transmitting. In reality, the other device is transmitting to a different device and not to the device that is refraining from transmitting.

#### IEEE 802.11

IEEE 802.11 is a set of standards that govern wireless LANs. Some of the standards that fall under IEEE 802.11 are:

- 802.11a: This standard operates at a frequency of 5GHz and provides data rates of up to 54Mbps.
- 802.11b: This standard operates at a frequency of 2.4GHz and provides data rates of up to 11Mbps.
- 802.11g: This standard operates at a frequency of 2.4GHz and provides data rates of up to 54Mbps.
- 802.11n: This standard operates at both 2.4GHz and 5GHz and provides data rates of up to 600Mbps.

#### Bluetooth

Bluetooth is a wireless technology used for short-range communication between devices. It operates at a frequency of 2.4GHz and provides data rates of up to 3Mbps. Bluetooth is commonly used for file transfers, audio streaming, and connecting peripheral devices like keyboards and mice.

#### Wireless in Mobile Computing

Wireless technology has revolutionized mobile computing. Some of the applications of wireless technology in mobile computing are:

- Wireless Internet Access: Mobile devices can access the internet through cellular networks, Wi-Fi, or satellite connections.
- Wireless File Transfer: Files can be transferred wirelessly between devices using Bluetooth or Wi-Fi Direct.
- Wireless Printing: Mobile devices can print wirelessly to compatible printers.
- Location-Based Services: Mobile devices can use wireless technology to determine their location and provide location-based services like directions and recommendations.
