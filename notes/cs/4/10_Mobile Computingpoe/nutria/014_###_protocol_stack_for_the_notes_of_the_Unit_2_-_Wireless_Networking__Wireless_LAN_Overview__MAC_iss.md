
### Protocol Stack for the Notes of the Unit 2 - Wireless Networking, Wireless LAN Overview: MAC Issues, IEEE 802.11, Bluetooth, Wireless in the Subject of Mobile Computing

* MAC Issues: The Media Access Control (MAC) layer is responsible for managing the access of the wireless medium. It defines the rules for how devices can access the wireless medium. Examples of MAC protocols include IEEE 802.11, Bluetooth, and Wireless.

* IEEE 802.11: IEEE 802.11 is a popular wireless networking protocol. It is used in wireless LANs and provides a reliable connection with a high data rate. It supports multiple access methods, such as CSMA/CA and DCF, and provides encryption and authentication mechanisms.

* Bluetooth: Bluetooth is a wireless technology that enables devices to communicate with each other over short distances. It supports point-to-point and point-to-multipoint connections and can be used to connect devices such as keyboards, mice, and headsets.

* Wireless: Wireless networks provide a way to connect devices without the need for physical cables. They use radio waves to communicate, and can be used to connect devices such as laptops, tablets, and smartphones. Wireless networks are typically used in homes, businesses, and public spaces.

* Advantages: Wireless networks provide a number of advantages over wired networks. They are easier to install, as no physical cables are required. They are also more flexible, as they can be easily moved or expanded. In addition, wireless networks are more secure, as they are not vulnerable to physical tampering.

* Disadvantages: Wireless networks also have some disadvantages. They are more susceptible to interference, as radio waves can be easily blocked or disrupted. They are also more vulnerable to security threats, as they can be easily accessed by unauthorized users.

* Applications: Wireless networks are used in a variety of applications. They are used in homes, businesses, and public spaces to provide internet access. They are also used in industrial applications, such as factory automation and machine-to-machine communication. Additionally, they are used in medical applications, such as monitoring patient health.
