
### Disconnected operations for the notes of the Unit 3 - Data management issues, data replication for mobile computers, adaptive clustering for mobile in the subject of Mobile Computing

Disconnected operations are a type of data management issue that arises when mobile computers are disconnected from the network. This can cause data replication issues, as the mobile computers may not be able to keep the data synchronized with the main database. To address this issue, adaptive clustering algorithms can be used to cluster mobile computers together, allowing them to share data even when disconnected from the network.

Advantages:

- Allows mobile computers to share data even when disconnected from the network.
- Can reduce the amount of data replication required.

Disadvantages:

- Adaptive clustering algorithms can be complex and difficult to implement.
- Clustering can cause data synchronization issues when the mobile computers are reconnected to the network.

Examples:

- An airline company uses adaptive clustering algorithms to allow its mobile computers to share passenger information even when disconnected from the network.
- A hospital uses adaptive clustering algorithms to allow its mobile computers to share patient information even when disconnected from the network.

Applications:

- Adaptive clustering algorithms can be used in any situation where mobile computers need to share data even when disconnected from the network. This includes industries such as transportation, healthcare, retail, and more.
