
## Unit 2 - Wireless Networking, Wireless LAN Overview: MAC issues, IEEE 802.11, Blue Tooth, Wireless 

Wireless networking is a type of networking that does not rely on physical wires to connect computers and other devices. It uses radio waves to transmit data over short distances. Wireless networking is used in many different applications, from home and office networks to large-scale networks such as those used in universities and businesses. 

### MAC Issues
Media Access Control (MAC) is a protocol used to regulate access to a shared medium, such as a wireless network. It is responsible for deciding which devices can access the network and when. MAC issues arise when two or more devices attempt to access the network at the same time. 

### IEEE 802.11
The IEEE 802.11 standard is a set of specifications for wireless networking. It defines the physical layer, medium access control, and logical link control of wireless networks. It is the most commonly used standard for wireless networks. 

### Bluetooth
Bluetooth is a wireless technology used to connect devices over short distances. It is used to connect phones, tablets, and other devices to each other and to computers. Bluetooth is also used to connect devices to speakers and other audio equipment. 

### Advantages of Wireless Networking
- Flexibility: Wireless networks are easy to set up and can be moved around easily. 
- Cost: Wireless networks are generally less expensive than wired networks. 
- Mobility: Wireless networks allow users to access the network from anywhere within range. 

### Disadvantages of Wireless Networking
- Security: Wireless networks are less secure than wired networks, as they are more vulnerable to interference and hacking. 
- Range: Wireless networks have a limited range, so they may not be suitable for large areas. 
- Speed: Wireless networks are generally slower than wired networks. 

### Mnemonics
- MAC: Media Access Control 
- IEEE: Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers 
- Bluetooth: Best Low-power Universal Transfer Technology
