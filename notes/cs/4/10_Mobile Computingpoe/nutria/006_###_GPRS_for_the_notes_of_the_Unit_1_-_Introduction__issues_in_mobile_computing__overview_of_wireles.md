
### GPRS for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction, issues in mobile computing, overview of wireless telephony: cellular concept, GSM: in the subject of Mobile Computing

- GPRS stands for General Packet Radio Service, which is a mobile data service available to users of GSM mobile phones. It is a packet-switched technology that allows users to access the Internet and send and receive data over the airwaves.

- GPRS is based on the same technology as GSM, but is much faster than the latter. It provides speeds of up to 114 Kbps, which is significantly faster than the 9.6 Kbps of GSM.

- GPRS can be used for a variety of applications, including web browsing, email, messaging, file transfer, and streaming media. It is also used for location-based services, such as GPS navigation.

- GPRS is a cost-effective way to access the Internet, as it does not require a dedicated connection. Instead, users are charged for the amount of data they use.

- GPRS is also more secure than GSM, as it uses encryption to protect the data being sent over the airwaves.

- GPRS is a popular choice for mobile operators, as it allows them to offer a range of services to their customers. It is also used by businesses to provide their employees with access to the Internet.
