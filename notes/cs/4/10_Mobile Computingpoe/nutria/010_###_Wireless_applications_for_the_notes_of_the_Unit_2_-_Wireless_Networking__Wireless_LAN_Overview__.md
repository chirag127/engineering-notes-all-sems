
### Wireless applications for the notes of the Unit 2 - Wireless Networking, Wireless LAN Overview: MAC issues, IEEE 802.11, Blue Tooth, Wireless in the subject of Mobile Computing

Wireless networking is a type of network technology that allows for communication over a wireless connection. This type of networking is commonly used in home and office networks, as well as in public areas such as airports and coffee shops. Wireless networking has become increasingly popular in recent years due to its convenience and ease of setup.

Wireless LAN Overview:

A wireless LAN (WLAN) is a type of local area network (LAN) that uses radio waves to provide wireless communication. The most common type of WLAN is based on the IEEE 802.11 standard. This standard defines the physical layer and media access control (MAC) layer of the network. It also defines the way in which devices can communicate with each other over the WLAN.

MAC Issues:

The MAC layer of a WLAN is responsible for controlling access to the wireless medium. It is responsible for ensuring that only one device at a time can transmit data. The MAC layer also handles the authentication process, which is used to ensure that only authorized devices can access the network.

IEEE 802.11:

IEEE 802.11 is the most common standard used for WLANs. It defines the physical layer and MAC layer of the network. It also defines the way in which devices can communicate with each other over the WLAN. It is a widely accepted standard and is used by many vendors.

Bluetooth:

Bluetooth is a wireless technology that is used for short-range communication. It is often used for connecting devices such as headsets, keyboards, and mice to computers. It is also used for connecting devices such as phones and tablets to each other.

Wireless in the subject of Mobile Computing:

Wireless technologies are becoming increasingly important in the field of mobile computing. Wireless technologies such as Wi-Fi and Bluetooth allow users to access the internet and other services while on the go. They also allow users to connect to each other and share data without the need for cables. Wireless technologies are becoming increasingly important in the field of mobile computing as they enable more efficient and convenient access to data and services.
