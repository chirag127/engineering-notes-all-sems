
### TCP over Wireless for the Notes of the Unit 2 - Wireless Networking, Wireless LAN Overview: MAC Issues, IEEE 802.11, Bluetooth, Wireless in the Subject of Mobile Computing

- Wireless networking is the use of radio waves to transfer data between two or more devices. 
- Wireless Local Area Networks (WLANs) use IEEE 802.11 standards to enable communication between two or more devices over a wireless network. 
- A Media Access Control (MAC) protocol is used to control access to the wireless network and prevent collisions between packets of data.
- IEEE 802.11 is a set of standards developed by the Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers (IEEE) for wireless LANs. It defines the physical and data link layers of the OSI model.
- Bluetooth is a short-range wireless technology that enables communication between two or more devices. It is commonly used for connecting headsets and other peripherals to computers and mobile devices.
- Wireless in the subject of mobile computing refers to the use of wireless technology to transmit data between two or more devices. It is used in mobile phones, tablets, and other portable devices to access the internet and transfer data.
- Mnemonics and learning tricks can be helpful in understanding and remembering the concepts of wireless networking. For example, the acronym "DARPA" can be used to remember the four layers of the OSI model: Data, Application, Radio, and Physical.
