
### Application Environment for the Notes of the Unit 2 - Wireless Networking, Wireless LAN Overview: MAC Issues, IEEE 802.11, Blue Tooth, Wireless in the Subject of Mobile Computing

* **MAC Issues**: Media Access Control (MAC) is a protocol used in the OSI layer to control the access of devices to the network. It is responsible for ensuring that multiple devices can access the network at the same time without interfering with each other.
* **IEEE 802.11**: IEEE 802.11 is a set of standards for wireless networking that are used to provide secure and reliable communication over a wireless network. It includes standards for physical layer, MAC layer, and data link layer.
* **Blue Tooth**: Bluetooth is a wireless technology that allows two devices to communicate with each other over short distances. It is used for a variety of applications, including data transfer, audio streaming, and more.
* **Wireless**: Wireless networking is a technology that enables communication between two or more devices without the need for a physical connection. It is used for a variety of applications, such as internet access, file sharing, and more.

Mnemonics and Learning Tricks:

* **MAC Issues**: Think of MAC as a Manager that is Accessing the Network.
* **IEEE 802.11**: Remember IEEE 802.11 as the “8-Oh-2-11”.
* **Blue Tooth**: Remember Bluetooth as the “Blue Tooth” that connects two devices.
* **Wireless**: Think of Wireless as a way to “Wire” two devices together without the need for a physical connection.
