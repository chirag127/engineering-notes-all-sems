### wireless networks for the notes of the Unit 3 - Data management issues, data replication for mobile computers, adaptive clustering for mobile in the subject of Mobile Computing
Wireless Networks:
- Wireless networks use radio waves to transmit and receive data
- Types: Wi-Fi, cellular, satellite, Bluetooth, Zigbee
- Advantages: mobility, convenience, cost-effectiveness
- Disadvantages: security risks, limited range, interference

Data Replication for Mobile Computers:
- The process of copying data between mobile devices and a central server
- Helps ensure data consistency and availability
- Can be achieved through synchronization or backup/restore methods

Adaptive Clustering for Mobile:
- A technique used in wireless networks to optimize network performance
- Involves dynamically adjusting the size and structure of clusters to accommodate changes in network conditions
- Improves network efficiency and reduces energy consumption.
