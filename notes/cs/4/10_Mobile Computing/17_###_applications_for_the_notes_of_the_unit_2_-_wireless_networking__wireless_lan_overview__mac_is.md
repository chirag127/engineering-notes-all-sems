### applications for the notes of the Unit 2 - Wireless Networking, Wireless LAN Overview: MAC issues, IEEE 802.11, Blue Tooth, Wireless in the subject of Mobile Computing
Applications for Wireless Networking: 
1. Internet Connectivity: Wi-Fi allows for high-speed internet access in homes, offices and public places.
2. Mobile Computing: Wireless networks support smartphones, laptops, and tablet devices for data transfer and internet access.
3. Wireless Printing: Wi-Fi enabled printers can be accessed from any device on the network.
4. Smart Home Automation: Wireless networks support smart home devices such as thermostats, security systems, and lighting systems.
5. Wireless Gaming: Wireless networks support online gaming and multiplayer gaming on gaming consoles.

Wireless LAN Overview: 
1. Wireless LAN (WLAN) is a local area network that uses wireless communication to connect devices.
2. WLANs use radio waves or infrared signals to transmit data between devices.
3. WLANs can be set up in homes, offices, and public spaces to provide internet access and data transfer.

MAC Issues:
1. MAC (Media Access Control) is a protocol used to control access to the network.
2. MAC issues can arise due to collisions, congestion, and errors in the MAC protocol.
3. To resolve MAC issues, techniques such as carrier sense multiple access (CSMA) and request to send (RTS) are used.

IEEE 802.11:
1. IEEE 802.11 is a set of standards for wireless local area networks (WLANs).
2. The standards define the physical and data link layers of WLANs.
3. Different versions of IEEE 802.11 standards exist, including 802.11a, 802.11b, 802.11g, 802.11n, and 802.11ac.

Bluetooth:
1. Bluetooth is a wireless technology used for short-range communication.
2. Bluetooth is used for data transfer between devices, such as smartphones, laptops, and tablets.
3. Bluetooth devices can also be used for wireless gaming, wireless printing, and smart home automation.

Wireless in Mobile Computing:
1. Wireless networks are used in mobile computing to provide internet access and data transfer for smartphones, laptops, and tablets.
2. Wireless technologies such as Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and cellular networks are used in mobile computing.
3. Wireless networks support location-based services, mobile payment systems, and other mobile applications.
