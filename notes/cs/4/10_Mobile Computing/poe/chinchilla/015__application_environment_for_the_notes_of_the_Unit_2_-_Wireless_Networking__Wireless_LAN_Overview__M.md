### Application Environment for the Notes of the Unit 2 - Wireless Networking, Wireless LAN Overview: MAC issues, IEEE 802.11, Bluetooth, Wireless in the Subject of Mobile Computing

In this unit, we will be discussing various aspects of wireless networking and wireless LANs. Specifically, we will be focusing on the following topics:

1. MAC Issues
   - The Media Access Control (MAC) layer is responsible for managing access to the wireless medium.
   - One of the main issues with the MAC layer is contention, where multiple devices try to access the medium at the same time, resulting in collisions.
   - To address this issue, various protocols have been developed, such as the Carrier Sense Multiple Access with Collision Avoidance (CSMA/CA) protocol.

2. IEEE 802.11
   - IEEE 802.11 is a set of standards for wireless LANs.
   - The standards specify the physical layer (PHY) and the MAC layer.
   - The PHY layer is responsible for transmitting and receiving data over the wireless medium.
   - The MAC layer is responsible for managing access to the medium and ensuring reliable delivery of data.

3. Bluetooth
   - Bluetooth is a wireless technology used for short-range communication between devices.
   - It operates in the 2.4 GHz frequency band and has a range of up to 10 meters.
   - Bluetooth uses a frequency-hopping spread spectrum (FHSS) technique to avoid interference from other devices.

4. Wireless in Mobile Computing
   - Wireless networking is an important aspect of mobile computing.
   - It allows mobile devices to connect to the internet and communicate with other devices.
   - However, wireless networking also presents security risks, such as eavesdropping and unauthorized access.
   - To mitigate these risks, various security protocols have been developed, such as the Wi-Fi Protected Access (WPA) and the Wi-Fi Protected Access II (WPA2) protocols.

In conclusion, this unit provides an overview of the application environment for wireless networking and wireless LANs. By the end of this unit, you should have a good understanding of the MAC issues, IEEE 802.11 standards, Bluetooth technology, and wireless networking in mobile computing.
