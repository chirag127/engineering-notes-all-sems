### Wireless Applications for the Notes of Unit 2 - Wireless Networking, Wireless LAN Overview: MAC issues, IEEE 802.11, Bluetooth, Wireless in the Subject of Mobile Computing

Wireless networking has become an essential part of modern-day communication and computing. The ability to connect to the internet and other devices without the use of cables has revolutionized the way we work, communicate and live. In this unit, we will be discussing different wireless applications and their use in various scenarios. Here are some of the key wireless applications that you need to know for your exams:

1. **Wireless Local Area Networks (WLANs):** WLANs are wireless networks that allow devices to connect to the internet or other devices within a limited area. WLANs use radio waves to transmit data between devices, and the most commonly used technology is the IEEE 802.11 standard. WLANs are widely used in homes, offices, and public places such as airports and cafes.

2. **MAC Issues in WLANs:** The Medium Access Control (MAC) layer is responsible for managing access to the wireless network. MAC issues can occur when multiple devices try to access the network simultaneously, causing collisions and reducing network performance. To avoid MAC issues, WLANs use different access protocols such as Carrier Sense Multiple Access with Collision Avoidance (CSMA/CA).

3. **IEEE 802.11 Standards:** The IEEE 802.11 standard is a set of protocols that governs WLANs. The standard defines different characteristics such as data rate, frequency band, and security protocols. Some of the commonly used IEEE 802.11 standards include 802.11a, 802.11b, 802.11g, 802.11n, and 802.11ac.

4. **Bluetooth:** Bluetooth is a wireless technology used for short-range communication between devices. Bluetooth uses radio waves to transmit data between devices, and it is commonly used in devices such as smartphones, headphones, and speakers. Bluetooth is widely used for wireless audio streaming and file transfer between devices.

5. **Wireless in Mobile Computing:** Mobile computing refers to the use of wireless devices such as smartphones, tablets, and laptops. Wireless connectivity is crucial for mobile computing, as it allows users to access the internet and other devices on the go. Mobile computing also uses different wireless technologies such as WLANs, Bluetooth, and cellular networks.

In conclusion, wireless networking and its applications have become an integral part of modern-day computing and communication. Understanding the different wireless technologies and their uses is essential for anyone working in the field of mobile computing.
