### Applications for the Notes of the Unit 2 - Wireless Networking, Wireless LAN Overview: MAC issues, IEEE 802.11, Blue Tooth, Wireless in the subject of Mobile Computing

Here are some important applications for the notes of the Unit 2 - Wireless Networking, Wireless LAN Overview: MAC issues, IEEE 802.11, Blue Tooth, Wireless in the subject of Mobile Computing:

1. Understanding Wireless LAN Overview: It is important to have an understanding of Wireless LAN Overview to develop a clear understanding of how wireless networks function. This will help students to learn about the different types of wireless networks and their characteristics.

2. MAC Issues: The understanding of MAC issues is essential for students to learn about how wireless networks manage data transmission. It includes the concepts of channel access methods, data transfer rates, and packet sizes. Understanding MAC issues will help students to identify and troubleshoot problems that may arise in wireless networks.

3. IEEE 802.11: IEEE 802.11 is a set of standards that defines the operation of wireless networks. Students need to learn about the different versions of IEEE 802.11, such as 802.11a, 802.11b, 802.11g, and 802.11n. They also need to understand the concepts of wireless LAN architecture, services, and security protocols.

4. Bluetooth: Bluetooth is a wireless technology that enables short-range communication between devices. Students need to learn about the fundamentals of Bluetooth, such as how it works, its applications, and the different versions of Bluetooth, such as Bluetooth 1.x, 2.x, and 3.x.

5. Wireless in Mobile Computing: Wireless is an essential component of Mobile Computing. Students need to learn about the different types of wireless networks used in Mobile Computing, such as WLAN, WWAN, and WPAN. They also need to understand the concepts of mobile computing architecture, services, and applications.

In conclusion, the applications mentioned above are crucial for students to learn in the Unit 2 - Wireless Networking, Wireless LAN Overview: MAC issues, IEEE 802.11, Blue Tooth, Wireless in the subject of Mobile Computing. It will provide them with a fundamental understanding of wireless networks, their components, and their applications.
