### Wireless Applications for the Notes of Unit 2 - Wireless Networking, Wireless LAN Overview: MAC Issues, IEEE 802.11, Bluetooth, Wireless in the Subject of Mobile Computing

Here are some key points regarding wireless applications in the subject of Mobile Computing:

- Wireless Networking is the practice of using radio frequencies to transmit data between devices. This can be done using a variety of technologies, including Bluetooth, WiFi, and cellular networks.
- Wireless LAN Overview is a detailed study of wireless local area networks (WLANs). This includes understanding the fundamental principles of wireless communication, the different types of WLANs, and how to secure wireless networks.
- MAC Issues refer to the challenges associated with media access control (MAC) in a wireless network. These issues can arise due to interference, congestion, or other factors that affect the transmission of data.
- IEEE 802.11 is a set of standards that govern the design and operation of wireless LANs. These standards define the protocols used for communication between devices and the radio frequencies that are used for transmitting data.
- Bluetooth is a wireless technology that is used for short-range communication between devices. It is commonly used for connecting smartphones to other devices, such as headphones, speakers, and smartwatches.
- Wireless in Mobile Computing refers to the use of wireless technologies in mobile devices, such as smartphones and tablets. This includes understanding the different types of wireless networks that can be used, the security risks associated with wireless communication, and the strategies for managing wireless networks on mobile devices.

In conclusion, understanding wireless applications is essential for anyone studying Mobile Computing. By understanding the fundamental principles of wireless communication, the different types of wireless networks, and the challenges associated with wireless communication, students can develop the skills they need to design, implement, and manage wireless networks on mobile devices.
