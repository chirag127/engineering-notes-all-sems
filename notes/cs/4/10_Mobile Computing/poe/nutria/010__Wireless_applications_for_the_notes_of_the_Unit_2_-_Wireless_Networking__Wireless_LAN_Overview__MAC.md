
### Wireless applications for the notes of the Unit 2 - Wireless Networking, Wireless LAN Overview: MAC issues, IEEE 802.11, Blue Tooth, Wireless in the subject of Mobile Computing

* Wireless Networking is the use of wireless communication networks to send and receive data. It is used to connect computers and other devices such as phones, tablets, and printers.
* Wireless LAN (WLAN) Overview is a technology that allows for the transmission of data over short distances. It is used to connect devices within a certain area, such as a home or office. WLANs use radio waves to send and receive data.
* MAC (Media Access Control) issues refer to the way in which devices on a network can access the network's resources. MAC addresses are used to identify devices on a network.
* IEEE 802.11 is a set of standards developed by the Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers (IEEE) for wireless networking. It defines the physical and MAC layers of the network, as well as the protocols used for communication.
* Bluetooth is a wireless technology that allows for the transmission of data over short distances. It is used to connect devices such as phones, tablets, and printers.
* Wireless in Mobile Computing refers to the use of wireless communication networks to access the internet and other services on mobile devices. It is used to access the internet on smartphones, tablets, and other devices.
