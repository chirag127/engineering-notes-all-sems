
### Disconnected Operations

1. Data management issues: Mobile computing often involves the need to store data on devices that may not always be connected to the network. In order to ensure the integrity of the data, disconnected operations must be supported. This involves the ability to replicate data across different devices, and to store data locally while disconnected.

2. Data replication: Data replication is the process of copying data from one device to another. This is especially important in mobile computing, where devices may be disconnected from the network for extended periods of time. Data replication ensures that data is always available, even when a device is disconnected.

3. Adaptive clustering: Adaptive clustering is the process of automatically organizing data into clusters based on certain criteria. This is useful in mobile computing, as it allows for efficient data storage and retrieval. Adaptive clustering also allows for the replication of data across multiple devices, ensuring that data is always available.
