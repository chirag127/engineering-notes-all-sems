
### Multiple Access Protocols for the Notes of the Unit 2 - Wireless Networking, Wireless LAN Overview: MAC Issues, IEEE 802.11, Blue Tooth, Wireless in the Subject of Mobile Computing

1. Wireless Networking: This refers to the use of wireless communication technologies to connect different devices, networks, and systems. It is used in a variety of applications, including home networking, business networking, and mobile computing.

2. Wireless LAN Overview: A Wireless Local Area Network (WLAN) is a type of local area network that uses radio waves to provide wireless high-speed data connections between multiple devices. It is a popular technology used in homes and offices, as it allows multiple users to access the same network without the need for cables.

3. MAC Issues: Media Access Control (MAC) is the protocol that is used to control and manage the access of multiple devices to a single network. It is responsible for the allocation of resources and the scheduling of transmissions.

4. IEEE 802.11: This is a set of standards developed by the Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers (IEEE) for wireless networking. It defines the physical and data link layers of the network, as well as the protocols used for communication between devices.

5. Blue Tooth: This is a wireless technology that is used to exchange data between two or more devices over short distances. It is used in a variety of applications, such as hands-free headsets, wireless speakers, and file sharing.

6. Wireless in Mobile Computing: Mobile computing is the use of portable computing devices, such as laptops, tablets, and smartphones, to access information and services. Wireless technologies, such as Wi-Fi and Bluetooth, are used to connect these devices to the Internet and other networks.
