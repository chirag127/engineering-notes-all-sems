
### Air Interface for the Notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction, Issues in Mobile Computing, Overview of Wireless Telephony: Cellular Concept, GSM:

1. Mobile computing is the use of portable computers and devices to access information and resources. It enables users to access information from anywhere, anytime.
2. Wireless telephony is the use of radio waves to transmit voice and data. It is used in cellular networks to provide mobile communication.
3. Cellular concept is the idea that a large geographical area is divided into smaller cells, each with its own radio transmitter and receiver.
4. GSM (Global System for Mobile Communications) is a digital cellular standard developed to provide mobile communication services. It is used in most countries around the world.
5. Air interface is the interface between the mobile station and the base station. It defines the protocol and procedures used in the communication between the two.
