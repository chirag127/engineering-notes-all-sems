
### GPRS for the Notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction, Issues in Mobile Computing, Overview of Wireless Telephony: Cellular Concept, GSM

* GPRS stands for General Packet Radio Service and is a mobile data service that is available to users of GSM and UMTS mobile phones.
* It is a packet-switched technology that allows data to be sent and received over a cellular network.
* GPRS provides data rates of up to 115 kbps, which is significantly faster than the 9.6 kbps offered by the earlier Circuit Switched Data (CSD) technology.
* GPRS is used for a variety of applications such as mobile internet access, multimedia messaging (MMS), and location-based services.
* GPRS is also used as the access network for other wireless technologies such as EDGE and HSPA.
* GPRS networks are typically operated by mobile network operators, though they can also be operated by private companies.
