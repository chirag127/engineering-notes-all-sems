### Applications for the notes of the Unit 5 - Ad Hoc networks, localization, MAC issues, Routing protocols, global state routing (GSR), in the subject of Mobile Computing

1. Ad Hoc networks:
- A collection of mobile nodes that form a temporary network without the use of any pre-existing infrastructure or centralized administration.
- Nodes act as both hosts and routers.
- Useful in emergency search-and-rescue operations, meetings or conventions where individuals want to quickly share information, and data acquisition in inhospitable terrain.

2. Localization:
- Determining the physical locations of nodes in an ad hoc network.
- Enables location-based services and routing.
- Techniques: Received signal strength indicator (RSSI), time of arrival (ToA), time difference of arrival (TDoA), and angle of arrival (AoA).

3. MAC issues:
- Hidden terminal problem: When a node is out of range of another node but both are in range of a common node, collisions can occur.
- Exposed terminal problem: A node may refrain from transmitting even when the channel is clear due to a neighboring transmission, reducing network throughput.
- Solutions: Use of RTS/CTS, scheduling, and directional antennas.
