### Applications for the notes of the Unit 2 - Wireless Networking

1. Wireless LAN Overview
    - Wireless LANs provide mobility and flexibility.
    - Uses radio waves to communicate between devices.
    - Range is limited compared to wired LANs.
    - More susceptible to interference and eavesdropping.

2. MAC issues
    - Hidden terminal problem: When two nodes are out of range of each other but can affect a third node that is in range of both. Collision can occur.
    - Exposed terminal problem: When a node refrains from transmitting due to an ongoing transmission that it can hear but is not the intended recipient of and is not impacted by. This reduces network efficiency.

3. IEEE 802.11
    - Set of standards for implementing WLANs.
    - Most popular standards are 802.11a, b, g, and n that differ in frequency, speed, and range.
    - Uses CSMA/CA (Carrier Sense Multiple Access with Collision Avoidance) to address MAC issues. Nodes check for activity and wait for a random time before transmitting to avoid collisions.

4. Bluetooth
    - Enables short-range wireless communication between devices using radio waves.
    - Mainly used to connect peripheral devices like headphones, speakers, keyboards, etc. to computers, tablets, and phones.
    - Networks are piconets consisting of one master device and up to seven active slave devices. Frequency hopping spread spectrum is used to prevent interference.

5. Wireless
    - Providing network access over a wide area using cellular networks, Wi-Fi, and other wireless technologies.
    - Enables connectivity on the move via mobile devices and has more coverage than Wi-Fi but typically slower speeds.
    - Cellular networks are the most widely used and provide data access and voice calling. Newer networks like 4G LTE offer faster speeds and lower latency.
