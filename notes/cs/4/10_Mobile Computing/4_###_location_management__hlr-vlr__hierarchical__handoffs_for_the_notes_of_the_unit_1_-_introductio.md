### location management: HLR-VLR, hierarchical, handoffs for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction, issues in mobile computing, overview of wireless telephony: cellular concept, GSM: in the subject of Mobile Computing
Location management in mobile computing refers to the process of tracking and managing the location of mobile devices in a wireless network. There are two main components in location management: Home Location Register (HLR) and Visitor Location Register (VLR). HLR stores the permanent information of a mobile device while VLR stores the temporary information of a mobile device when it moves to a new location. The hierarchical structure of location management ensures efficient and effective tracking of mobile devices. Handoff refers to the process of transferring a call from one cell to another as a mobile device moves from one location to another. Handoff is a crucial aspect of location management as it ensures seamless communication for mobile devices. Issues in mobile computing include limited battery life, limited bandwidth, and security concerns. The overview of wireless telephony includes the cellular concept and the Global System for Mobile Communications (GSM). The cellular concept refers to the division of a geographical area into small cells that are served by base stations. GSM is a standard for wireless communication and is widely used for mobile telephony.
