### TCP over wireless for the notes of the Unit 2 - Wireless Networking, Wireless LAN Overview: MAC issues, IEEE 802.11, Blue Tooth, Wireless in the subject of Mobile Computing
TCP (Transmission Control Protocol) is a common transport layer protocol used in communication over wireless networks. It provides reliable, ordered delivery of data between applications running on different devices. In wireless networks, TCP can face challenges such as high latency, low bandwidth, and frequent disconnections.

Wireless LAN (Local Area Network) Overview: 
- MAC (Media Access Control) issues: Wireless networks use a MAC layer to control access to the shared wireless medium. The MAC layer can become congested, leading to collisions and retransmissions, reducing network performance. 
- IEEE 802.11: This is a standard for wireless local area networks (WLANs). It defines specifications for the physical layer (PHY) and the MAC layer of wireless communication. 
- Bluetooth: This is a wireless technology for exchanging data over short distances. It is used for personal area networks (PANs) and is commonly used in mobile devices. 
- Wireless in Mobile Computing: Wireless technology is widely used in mobile computing devices such as smartphones, tablets, and laptops, providing users with the ability to connect to the internet and other devices while on the go.

Note: The above information is a summary of the key points related to TCP over wireless networks, including the challenges faced, the components of a wireless LAN, and the role of wireless technology in mobile computing.
