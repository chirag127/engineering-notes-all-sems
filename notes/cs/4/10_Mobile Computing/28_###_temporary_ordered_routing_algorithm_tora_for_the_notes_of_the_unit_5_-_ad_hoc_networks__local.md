### Temporary ordered routing algorithm (TORA) for the notes of the Unit 5 - Ad Hoc networks, localization, MAC issues, Routing protocols, global state routing (GSR), in the subject of Mobile Computing
Temporary Ordered Routing Algorithm (TORA) is a scalable, distributed routing protocol for ad hoc networks. It is designed to handle dynamic network topologies and provide efficient and robust routing in large, multi-hop wireless networks. TORA uses a hierarchical, directed acyclic graph (DAG) structure to maintain routing information and provide quick response to changes in network topology. The algorithm uses a combination of link-reversal and query-based techniques to maintain accurate routing information and ensure efficient routing of data packets. TORA is particularly well-suited for large, dense ad hoc networks and provides good performance in terms of routing stability, scalability, and robustness.
