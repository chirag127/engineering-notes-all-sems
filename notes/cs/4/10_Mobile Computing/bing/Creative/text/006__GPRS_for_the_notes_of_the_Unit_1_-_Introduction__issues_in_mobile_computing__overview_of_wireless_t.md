### GPRS

- GPRS stands for General Packet Radio Service .
- It is a packet oriented wireless data communication service for mobile communications on 2G and 3G cellular communication systems .
- It is non-voice, high speed packet switching technology intended for GSM networks .
- It is sometimes called a 2.5G network, as it provides data rates from 56 kilobits per second to 171.2 Kbps and uninterrupted connectivity to the internet for mobile phones and computers.
- It is a best-effort service, implying variable throughput and latency that depend on the number of other users sharing the service concurrently .
- It extends the GSM packet circuit switched data capabilities and makes the following services possible:
  - SMS messaging and broadcasting
  - "Always on" internet access
  - Multimedia messaging service (MMS)
  - Push-to-talk over cellular (PoC)
  - Instant messaging and presence—wireless village
  - Internet applications for smart devices through wireless application protocol (WAP)
  - Point-to-point (P2P) service: inter-networking with the Internet (IP)
  - Point-to-multipoint (P2M) service: point-to-multipoint multicast and point-to-multipoint group calls
- It supports the following protocols:
  - Internet Protocol (IP)
  - Point-to-Point Protocol (PPP)
  - X.25 connections
- It has the following advantages:
  - It enables faster data transmission and higher bandwidth for mobile devices
  - It reduces the cost of data services by charging per byte of data transferred rather than per minute of connection time
  - It allows simultaneous use of voice and data services on the same device
  - It supports a wide range of applications and devices, such as email, web browsing, video streaming, IoT, etc.
- It has the following disadvantages:
  - It has limited coverage and availability in some areas
  - It has lower data rates and quality of service compared to 3G and 4G technologies
  - It consumes more battery power than circuit-switched data
  - It is vulnerable to security and privacy issues, such as eavesdropping, spoofing, etc.
