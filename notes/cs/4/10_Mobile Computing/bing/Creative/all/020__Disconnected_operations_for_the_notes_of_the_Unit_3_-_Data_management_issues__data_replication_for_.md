### Disconnected operations for the notes of the Unit 3 - Data management issues, data replication for mobile computers, adaptive clustering for mobile in the subject of Mobile Computing

- Disconnected operations are a technique to handle temporary or intentional network failures or disconnections in mobile computing environments.
- Disconnected operations allow users to execute applications and access data locally on their mobile devices without relying on network connectivity or remote servers.
- Disconnected operations require mechanisms to prepare for disconnection, emulate the server functionality, and reintegrate the updates after reconnection.
- Disconnected operations can improve the availability, performance, and energy efficiency of mobile applications, as well as reduce the communication costs and network congestion.
- Disconnected operations can also pose challenges such as data consistency, conflict detection and resolution, security and privacy, and resource management.

#### Data replication for mobile computers

- Data replication is a technique to create and maintain multiple copies of data on different nodes in a distributed system.
- Data replication can support disconnected operations by allowing mobile devices to cache or hoard data locally before or during disconnection, and synchronize the updates with the remote servers after reconnection.
- Data replication can also improve the availability, performance, and fault tolerance of mobile applications, as well as balance the load and reduce the network traffic.
- Data replication can also pose challenges such as data consistency, concurrency control, update propagation, and replica management.

#### Adaptive clustering for mobile

- Adaptive clustering is a technique to organize mobile nodes into groups or clusters based on their proximity, connectivity, or other criteria.
- Adaptive clustering can support disconnected operations by allowing mobile nodes to communicate and cooperate with each other within a cluster, and exchange data and updates with other clusters or servers when possible.
- Adaptive clustering can also improve the scalability, efficiency, and reliability of mobile applications, as well as facilitate the discovery and dissemination of information and services.
- Adaptive clustering can also pose challenges such as cluster formation, maintenance, and reconfiguration, as well as inter-cluster and intra-cluster communication and coordination.
