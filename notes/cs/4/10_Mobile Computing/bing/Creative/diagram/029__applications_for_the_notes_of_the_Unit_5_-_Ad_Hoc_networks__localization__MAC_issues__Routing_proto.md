According to the search results, some of the applications of ad hoc networks are:

- Military battlefield: Ad hoc networking can enable the military to maintain an information network between the soldiers, vehicles, and headquarters without relying on fixed infrastructure .
- Personal area network (PAN): Ad hoc networking can enable short-range, local communication among devices such as laptops, smartphones, printers, etc. using technologies like Wi-Fi and Bluetooth .
- Emergency situations: Ad hoc networking can enable rescue operations, disaster relief, and emergency medical services to communicate and coordinate without depending on existing communication systems .
- Medical applications: Ad hoc networking can enable wireless sensors to monitor patients' vital signs, health conditions, and medical records without requiring wires or centralized servers .
- Environmental applications: Ad hoc networking can enable wireless sensors to collect and transmit data about weather conditions, forest fires, tsunamis, etc. without requiring wires or centralized servers .
- Vehicular ad hoc network (VANET): Ad hoc networking can enable communication among vehicles to exchange traffic information, safety warnings, entertainment, and navigation services without relying on fixed infrastructure .
- Smartphone ad hoc network (SPAN): Ad hoc networking can enable smartphones to create a wireless network using existing technologies like Wi-Fi and Bluetooth without relying on cellular or internet services.
