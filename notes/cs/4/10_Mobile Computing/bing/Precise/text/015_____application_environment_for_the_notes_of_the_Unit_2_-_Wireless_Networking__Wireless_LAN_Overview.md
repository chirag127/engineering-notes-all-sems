### Application Environment

In the context of Unit 2 - Wireless Networking, Wireless LAN Overview: MAC issues, IEEE 802.11, Blue Tooth, Wireless in the subject of Mobile Computing, the application environment refers to the software and hardware components that are required to support the execution of wireless networking applications.

Some key points to consider when discussing the application environment for wireless networking include:

1. The hardware components required to support wireless networking, such as wireless network interface cards, routers, and access points.
2. The software components required to support wireless networking, such as operating systems, drivers, and networking protocols.
3. The importance of security in the application environment, including the use of encryption and authentication protocols to protect data transmitted over wireless networks.
4. The role of standards, such as IEEE 802.11 and Bluetooth, in defining the technical specifications for wireless networking technologies and ensuring interoperability between devices.
5. The impact of the physical environment on wireless networking performance, including factors such as distance, interference, and obstacles that can affect signal strength and quality.

Overall, the application environment for wireless networking is a complex and dynamic system that requires careful consideration and planning to ensure optimal performance and security.
