## Unit 2 - Wireless Networking, Wireless LAN Overview: MAC issues, IEEE 802.11, Blue Tooth, Wireless

1. **Wireless Networking** refers to the use of wireless technology to connect devices to a network, allowing them to communicate and share data without the need for physical connections.
2. **Wireless LAN (WLAN)** is a type of wireless network that uses high-frequency radio waves to provide wireless connectivity within a limited area, such as a home, office, or campus.
3. **MAC (Media Access Control) issues** in wireless networking refer to the challenges of coordinating access to the shared wireless medium among multiple devices. This is necessary to avoid collisions and ensure efficient use of the available bandwidth.
4. **IEEE 802.11** is a set of standards for implementing wireless LANs. It defines the physical and MAC layers of the technology, and includes several variations that offer different levels of performance, range, and security.
5. **Bluetooth** is a short-range wireless technology used for personal area networking (PAN). It allows devices to communicate and share data over short distances, typically within a range of 10 meters.
6. **Wireless** refers to any type of networking or communication that does not rely on physical connections, such as cables or wires. It includes technologies such as WLAN, Bluetooth, cellular networks, and satellite communications.
