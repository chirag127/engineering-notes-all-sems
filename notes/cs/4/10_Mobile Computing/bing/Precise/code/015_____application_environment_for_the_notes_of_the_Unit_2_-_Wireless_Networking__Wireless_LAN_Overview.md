### Application Environment

In the context of Unit 2 - Wireless Networking, Wireless LAN Overview: MAC issues, IEEE 802.11, Blue Tooth, Wireless in the subject of Mobile Computing, the application environment refers to the software and hardware components that support the operation of wireless networks and their associated applications.

1. **Wireless Networks:** Wireless networks allow devices to communicate with each other without the need for physical connections. This is achieved through the use of radio waves or infrared signals to transmit data between devices.

2. **Wireless LAN Overview:** A wireless LAN (WLAN) is a type of wireless network that allows devices to connect to a local area network (LAN) through a wireless connection. WLANs are commonly used in homes, offices, and public spaces to provide internet access to mobile devices.

3. **MAC issues:** The media access control (MAC) layer is responsible for controlling access to the shared transmission medium in a wireless network. In a WLAN, the MAC layer must address issues such as interference, hidden nodes, and the efficient use of the available bandwidth.

4. **IEEE 802.11:** IEEE 802.11 is a set of standards that define the operation of WLANs. These standards specify the physical and MAC layer protocols used by WLANs to transmit data.

5. **Blue Tooth:** Bluetooth is a short-range wireless technology that allows devices to communicate with each other over distances of up to 10 meters. It is commonly used for wireless communication between mobile phones, headsets, and other portable devices.

6. **Wireless:** Wireless refers to the use of radio waves or other forms of electromagnetic radiation to transmit data between devices without the need for physical connections.
