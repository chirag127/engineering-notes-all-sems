### applications for the notes of the Unit 5 - Ad Hoc networks, localization, MAC issues, Routing protocols, global state routing (GSR), in the subject of Mobile Computing
Ad Hoc networks are self-organizing networks that do not rely on a fixed infrastructure. Applications of Ad Hoc networks include:

1. Military and emergency response communications
2. Rural and remote area networking
3. Vehicle-to-vehicle communication
4. Personal area networks

Localization is the process of determining the physical location of a device in a network. Applications of localization include:

1. GPS navigation systems
2. Location-based services in mobile devices
3. Asset tracking and management

MAC (Media Access Control) issues in mobile computing relate to the control of access to shared resources in a network. Applications of MAC protocols include:

1. Wireless LANs
2. Cellular networks
3. Wireless sensor networks

Routing protocols are used to determine the path that data should follow in a network. Applications of routing protocols include:

1. Internet Protocol (IP) networks
2. Mobile Ad Hoc networks
3. Wireless sensor networks

Global State Routing (GSR) is a type of routing protocol used in large-scale, complex networks. Applications of GSR include:

1. Large-scale wireless mesh networks
2. Wireless sensor networks
3. Military and emergency response communications.
