### Smoothing for the notes of Unit 2 - WORD LEVEL ANALYSIS in the subject of Natural Language Processing

1. Smoothing is a technique used to eliminate the problem of zero probabilities or very low probabilities in language models.

2. Smoothing is used to handle the problem of unknown words in the language model.

3. There are various smoothing techniques available, such as Laplace smoothing, Lidstone smoothing, Good-Turing smoothing, and Kneser-Ney smoothing.

4. Laplace smoothing, also known as add-one smoothing, adds one count to every word in the vocabulary to avoid zero probabilities.

5. Lidstone smoothing is an extension of Laplace smoothing that allows for a smoothing parameter other than one to be used.

6. Good-Turing smoothing is a technique that estimates the probability of unseen words by using the frequency of words that occur only once.

7. Kneser-Ney smoothing is a technique that takes into account the frequency of word n-grams in the training data to estimate the probability of unseen words.

8. Smoothing is an important technique in natural language processing as it helps in improving the accuracy of language models.

9. However, it is important to note that smoothing is not a one-size-fits-all solution and the choice of smoothing technique depends on the specific problem at hand.

10. In conclusion, smoothing is a valuable technique in natural language processing that can help to improve the accuracy of language models and handle the problem of unknown words.
