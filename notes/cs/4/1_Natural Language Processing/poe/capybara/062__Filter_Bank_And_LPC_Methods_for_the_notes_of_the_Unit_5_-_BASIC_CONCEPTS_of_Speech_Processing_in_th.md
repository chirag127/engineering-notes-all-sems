### Filter Bank And LPC Methods

In this unit, we will discuss two important methods for speech processing: Filter Bank and Linear Predictive Coding (LPC).

#### Filter Bank

Filter Bank is a method used for feature extraction in speech processing. It involves dividing the speech signal into several frequency bands and extracting features from each of these bands. Here are some key points to remember:

- Filter Bank is used to decompose the speech signal into different frequency bands.
- These frequency bands are created using a set of bandpass filters.
- The output of each filter is passed through a rectification and smoothing process.
- The resulting signals are then downsampled and used as features for further analysis.

#### Linear Predictive Coding (LPC)

LPC is another important method used for speech processing. It is a technique used to model the spectral envelope of a speech signal. Here are some key points to remember:

- LPC is used to model the spectral envelope of a speech signal.
- It involves predicting the current sample based on a linear combination of the past samples.
- The coefficients of the linear combination are known as LPC coefficients.
- These coefficients are used to synthesize the speech signal.

#### Comparison between Filter Bank and LPC

Both Filter Bank and LPC are important methods used for speech processing. Here are some key differences between the two:

- Filter Bank is used for feature extraction, while LPC is used for modeling the spectral envelope.
- Filter Bank involves dividing the speech signal into frequency bands, while LPC involves predicting the current sample based on past samples.
- Filter Bank is used for speech recognition, while LPC is used for speech compression.

Overall, both Filter Bank and LPC are important methods used for speech processing. It is important to understand the differences between them and when to use each method for a given task.
