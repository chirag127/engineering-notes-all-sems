### Markov Processes for the notes of the Unit 7 - SPEECH MODELING in the subject of Natural Language Processing

Markov Processes are an essential component of Speech Modeling in Natural Language Processing. Here are some key points to help you understand Markov Processes:

- Markov Processes are a type of stochastic process that follow the Markov Property, which states that the future state of the process depends only on the current state and not on any previous states.
- Markov Processes can be represented using a state transition matrix, which shows the probability of moving from one state to another.
- In Speech Modeling, Markov Processes are commonly used to model the probability distribution of speech sounds or phonemes.
- Hidden Markov Models (HMMs) are a type of Markov Process that are commonly used in Speech Modeling. In HMMs, the states are hidden and only the observations are visible.
- HMMs can be used to model both the acoustic and linguistic aspects of speech, making them a powerful tool in Speech Modeling.
- The Baum-Welch algorithm is a popular algorithm for training HMMs to fit a given set of observations.
- HMMs can also be used for speech recognition, where the goal is to transcribe an audio signal into a sequence of words or phonemes.
- In speech recognition, the Viterbi algorithm is commonly used to find the most likely sequence of hidden states given a sequence of observations.

By understanding Markov Processes and their applications in Speech Modeling, you can gain a deeper understanding of how speech recognition systems work and how they can be improved.
