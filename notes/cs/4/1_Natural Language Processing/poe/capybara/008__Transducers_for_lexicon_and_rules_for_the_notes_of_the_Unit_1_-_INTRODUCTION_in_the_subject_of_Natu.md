### Transducers for lexicon and rules

In Natural Language Processing, a transducer is a tool used for converting one sequence into another. The process of converting one sequence to another involves two main components: the lexicon and the rules. 

A lexicon is a collection of words and their associated meanings. It is used to recognize and generate words in a language. The lexicon is usually created by linguists and experts in the language, and is stored in a database. 

On the other hand, rules are a set of instructions that govern the behavior of a system. In the context of Natural Language Processing, rules help to define the grammar of a language. They are used to define the structure of sentences and how words can be combined to form a meaningful sentence.

Transducers for lexicon and rules are used to convert one sequence to another. They are especially useful for tasks such as part-of-speech tagging and parsing.
