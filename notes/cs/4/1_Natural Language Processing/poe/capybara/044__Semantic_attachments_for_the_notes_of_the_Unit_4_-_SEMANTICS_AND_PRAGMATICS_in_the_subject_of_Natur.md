### Semantic Attachments for the Notes of Unit 4 - SEMANTICS AND PRAGMATICS in the Subject of Natural Language Processing

In this unit, we will focus on semantic attachments, which are additional pieces of information that help us understand the meaning of a sentence. Here are some key points to keep in mind:

- Semantic attachments provide extra information about the words in a sentence, such as their tense, number, or gender. This information helps us understand the meaning of the sentence more accurately.
- There are different types of semantic attachments, including tense, aspect, modality, polarity, and definiteness. Each type of attachment provides different types of information about the sentence.
- Tense attachments indicate when an action took place, such as past, present, or future. For example, the verb "run" can be attached with the tense "ran" to indicate that the action happened in the past.
- Aspect attachments indicate how the action was performed, such as whether it was completed or ongoing. For example, the verb "run" can be attached with the aspect "had been running" to indicate that the action was ongoing in the past.
- Modality attachments indicate the speaker's attitude towards the sentence, such as whether they believe it to be true or not. For example, the sentence "I can run a marathon" can be attached with the modality "might" to indicate that the speaker is uncertain about their ability to run a marathon.
- Polarity attachments indicate whether a sentence is positive or negative. For example, the sentence "I am happy" can be attached with the polarity "not" to indicate that the speaker is not happy.
- Definiteness attachments indicate whether a noun is specific or general. For example, the noun "dog" can be attached with the definiteness "the" to indicate that the speaker is referring to a specific dog.

Overall, understanding semantic attachments is crucial for accurately interpreting the meaning of a sentence in natural language processing. By considering the different types of attachments, we can gain a more nuanced and accurate understanding of the text we are analyzing.
