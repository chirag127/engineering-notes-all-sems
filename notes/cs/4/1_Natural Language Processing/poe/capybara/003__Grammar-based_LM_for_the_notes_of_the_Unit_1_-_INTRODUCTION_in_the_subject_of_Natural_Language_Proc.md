### Grammar-based LM for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of Natural Language Processing

In the field of Natural Language Processing, Language Modeling (LM) is an important concept. It involves predicting the next word in a sentence based on the previous words. One approach to LM is the use of grammar-based models. Here are some key points about grammar-based LM:

- Grammar-based LM involves the use of formal grammars to model the structure of a language. This can be done using techniques such as Context-Free Grammar (CFG) and Probabilistic Context-Free Grammar (PCFG).
- These models can be used to generate sentences that are grammatically correct and semantically meaningful.
- One advantage of grammar-based LM is that it can handle complex sentence structures and long-range dependencies. This makes it useful for tasks such as machine translation and speech recognition.
- However, grammar-based models can be computationally expensive and may not perform as well on tasks such as text prediction and language generation compared to other approaches like neural language models.
- In addition to LM, grammar-based models can also be used for tasks such as parsing, semantic analysis, and information extraction.

In summary, grammar-based LM is an important approach to language modeling in Natural Language Processing. While it has some advantages, it also has some limitations and may not be the best approach for all tasks. Understanding the strengths and weaknesses of different LM techniques is important for developing effective NLP systems.
