### Bootstrapping methods for the notes of the Unit 4 - SEMANTICS AND PRAGMATICS in the subject of Natural Language Processing.

- Bootstrapping is the process of starting with a small amount of information and using it to acquire more knowledge.
- In natural language processing, bootstrapping methods are commonly used for semantic and pragmatic analysis.
- Bootstrapping methods for semantics involve using existing knowledge, such as lexicons, to identify word meanings and relationships.
- One popular bootstrapping method for semantics is distributional semantics, which involves analyzing the contexts in which words appear to infer their meanings.
- Another method is pattern-based bootstrapping, which uses patterns of co-occurring words to identify semantic relationships.
- Bootstrapping methods for pragmatics involve using contextual information to infer meaning, such as speaker intention and implicature.
- One popular method for pragmatic bootstrapping is the use of annotated corpora, which provide examples of how language is used in context.
- Other methods for pragmatic bootstrapping include discourse analysis and the use of conversational agents to model human communication.
- Bootstrapping methods can be useful for building language models and improving natural language processing systems, but they can also be prone to errors and require careful validation.
