### Viterbi Search

Viterbi search is a dynamic programming algorithm used in speech modeling to find the most likely sequence of hidden states in a Hidden Markov Model (HMM). It is named after its inventor, Andrew Viterbi.

#### What is Hidden Markov Model (HMM)?

- Hidden Markov Model (HMM) is a statistical model that is used to model sequences of observations.
- In HMM, the observations are assumed to be generated by a sequence of hidden states.
- HMM is widely used in speech recognition, handwriting recognition, bioinformatics, and many other applications.

#### How Viterbi Search works?

- Viterbi search algorithm is used to find the most likely sequence of hidden states in HMM.
- The algorithm works by calculating the probability of the most likely sequence of hidden states that generates the observed sequence of observations.
- The probability is calculated recursively, starting from the initial state of the HMM and moving forward through the sequence of observations.
- At each time step, the algorithm calculates the probability of each possible hidden state, given the current observation and the previous hidden state.
- The probability of each possible hidden state is then multiplied by the probability of transitioning from the previous hidden state to the current hidden state, and the maximum probability is selected.
- The selected maximum probability is stored in a table, along with a pointer to the previous hidden state that gave rise to the maximum probability.
- This process continues until the end of the observation sequence is reached.
- Finally, the most likely sequence of hidden states is obtained by tracing back through the table, starting from the maximum probability at the end of the observation sequence and following the pointers.

#### Advantages of Viterbi Search

- Viterbi search is a computationally efficient algorithm that can handle long observation sequences and large HMMs.
- It is widely used in speech recognition and other applications where the observation sequence is continuous and the number of possible hidden states is large.

#### Applications of Viterbi Search

- Viterbi search is widely used in speech recognition to find the most likely sequence of phonemes that generates the observed speech signal.
- It is also used in handwriting recognition, bioinformatics, and many other applications where the observation sequence is continuous and the number of possible hidden states is large.
