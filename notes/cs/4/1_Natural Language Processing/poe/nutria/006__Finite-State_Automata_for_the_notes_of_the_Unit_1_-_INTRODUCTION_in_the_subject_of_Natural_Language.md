
### Finite-State Automata for the Notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the Subject of Natural Language Processing

* Finite-State Automata (FSA) is a mathematical model of computation used to design and analyze algorithms for natural language processing (NLP). 
* FSA consists of a finite set of states and transitions between those states that are triggered by input symbols. 
* The input symbols are typically words, phrases, or characters. 
* A transition from one state to another is triggered when an input symbol is read. 
* The output of the FSA is determined by the final state reached after all input symbols have been processed. 
* FSA are used to model the structure of languages, such as the syntax of a sentence, to identify patterns in data, and to recognize words or phrases in text. 
* FSA can also be used to generate strings from a given language, such as a grammar. 
* FSA are an important tool for natural language processing and are used in many applications, such as speech recognition, machine translation, and text analysis.
