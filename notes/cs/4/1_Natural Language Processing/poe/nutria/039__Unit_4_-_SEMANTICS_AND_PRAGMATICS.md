
## Unit 4 - SEMANTICS AND PRAGMATICS

1. Semantics is the study of the meaning of words and sentences. It is concerned with how people interpret and understand language. 
2. Pragmatics is the study of how language is used in context. It looks at how people use language to communicate and how this affects the interpretation of what is said. 
3. Semantic rules are used to determine how words and sentences should be interpreted. These rules are based on the context in which they are used. 
4. Pragmatic rules are used to determine how language is used in a particular context. These rules are based on the speaker's intent, the listener's understanding, and the social conventions of the language. 
5. Semantics and pragmatics are closely related and are often studied together. They both help to explain how language is used and interpreted in different contexts. 
6. Semantics and pragmatics are important for language learners as they help to understand how language is used in different contexts. They also help to explain why some sentences are more appropriate in certain situations than others.
