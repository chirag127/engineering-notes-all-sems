

### Ambiguity in Syntactic Analysis

1. Ambiguity is a situation in which it is difficult to determine the intended meaning of a sentence.
2. Ambiguity can occur in natural language due to the presence of multiple valid interpretations of a sentence or phrase.
3. Syntactic analysis is the process of analyzing the structure of a sentence in order to determine its meaning.
4. Syntactic ambiguity occurs when a sentence has two or more valid syntactic interpretations.
5. Lexical ambiguity occurs when a word or phrase has multiple possible meanings.
6. Structural ambiguity occurs when a sentence contains two or more possible syntactic structures.
7. Contextual ambiguity occurs when the meaning of a phrase or sentence depends on the context in which it is used.
8. Pragmatic ambiguity occurs when the intended meaning of a phrase or sentence is unclear due to the lack of sufficient context.
