
## Unit 3 - SYNTACTIC ANALYSIS

Syntactic Analysis is an important part of language processing and natural language understanding. It involves analyzing the structure of a sentence in order to determine its meaning.

1. Syntax is the set of rules that govern the structure of a language. It is the way words are arranged to form meaningful sentences.

2. Syntactic analysis involves breaking down a sentence into its component parts and analyzing the structure of the sentence. This allows us to determine the meaning of the sentence.

3. Syntactic analysis is used to identify the parts of speech of each word in a sentence, as well as the relationships between words.

4. Syntactic analysis can be used to identify the subject, verb, and object of a sentence, as well as any modifiers or other phrases.

5. Syntactic analysis is also used to identify the grammatical structure of a sentence, such as the tense and mood.

6. Syntactic analysis is used to identify errors in a sentence, such as incorrect word order, missing words, or incorrect verb forms.

7. Syntactic analysis can also be used to identify ambiguities in a sentence, such as when two different interpretations of a sentence are possible.
