
### Spectral Distortion Using A Warped Frequency Scale

1. Spectral distortion is a phenomenon that occurs when sound waves are distorted due to a change in frequency or amplitude.
2. A warped frequency scale is a technique used to measure and analyze sound waves that have been distorted.
3. This technique is used in speech-analysis to measure the spectral characteristics of speech signals.
4. It involves the use of a non-linear frequency scale that is adjusted to match the spectral distortion of the signal.
5. This warped frequency scale is used to measure the spectral characteristics of speech signals and to identify the frequency components that are most affected by the distortion.
6. This technique is useful for speech-analysis applications such as speech recognition, speaker identification, and speech synthesis.
