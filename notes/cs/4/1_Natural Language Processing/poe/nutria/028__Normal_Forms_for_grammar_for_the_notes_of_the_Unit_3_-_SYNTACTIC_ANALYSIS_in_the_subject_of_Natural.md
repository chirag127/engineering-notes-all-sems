
### Normal Forms for Grammar

* Chomsky Normal Form (CNF) is a type of normal form used in natural language processing (NLP) to represent a sentence in the form of a set of binary trees. This form is used to simplify the process of syntactic analysis.

* Greibach Normal Form (GNF) is another type of normal form used in NLP to represent a sentence in the form of a set of binary trees. This form is used to simplify the process of syntactic analysis.

* Normal forms are used to simplify the process of syntactic analysis by providing a way to represent sentences in a more organized and structured way. This makes it easier for a computer to understand the structure of a sentence and to parse it correctly.

* Syntactic analysis is the process of analyzing a sentence to determine its meaning and structure. This is done by breaking down the sentence into its component parts and analyzing the relationship between them.

* Syntactic analysis is an important part of natural language processing because it allows computers to understand the meaning of a sentence and to generate appropriate responses.
