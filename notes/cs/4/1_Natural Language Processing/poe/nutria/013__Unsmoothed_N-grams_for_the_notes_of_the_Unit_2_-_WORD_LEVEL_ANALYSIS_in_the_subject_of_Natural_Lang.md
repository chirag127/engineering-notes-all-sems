
### Unsmoothed N-grams for the notes of the Unit 2 - WORD LEVEL ANALYSIS in the subject of Natural Language Processing

* Unsmoothed N-grams are a type of probabilistic language model that is used to predict the probability of a sequence of words.
* Unsmoothed N-grams calculate the probability of a word given the previous N words in the sequence.
* Unsmoothed N-grams assume that the probability of a word is independent of the context.
* Unsmoothed N-grams are used in natural language processing tasks such as speech recognition and machine translation.
* Unsmoothed N-grams are limited in their ability to capture long-term dependencies in language.
* Smoothing techniques can be used to improve the accuracy of Unsmoothed N-grams.
