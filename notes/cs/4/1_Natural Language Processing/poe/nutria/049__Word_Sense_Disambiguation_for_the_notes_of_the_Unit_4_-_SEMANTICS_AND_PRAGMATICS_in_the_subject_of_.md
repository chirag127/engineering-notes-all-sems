
### Word Sense Disambiguation

Word sense disambiguation (WSD) is the process of automatically determining the meaning of a word in a given context. It is an important part of Natural Language Processing (NLP) as it helps machines understand the meaning of words in a sentence.

* WSD involves assigning a sense to each occurrence of a word in a text.
* The goal of WSD is to identify the correct sense of a word in a given context.
* WSD is used in applications such as information retrieval, machine translation, and question answering.
* WSD algorithms can be supervised, unsupervised, or semi-supervised.
* Supervised WSD algorithms use labeled training data to learn how to disambiguate words.
* Unsupervised WSD algorithms use unlabeled data to identify word senses.
* Semi-supervised WSD algorithms use a combination of labeled and unlabeled data.
* WSD algorithms can use a variety of features, such as part-of-speech tags, semantic features, and syntactic features.
* WSD algorithms can be evaluated using a variety of metrics, such as accuracy and precision.
