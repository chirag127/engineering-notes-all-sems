
### Grammar-based Language Model for the Notes of Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in Natural Language Processing

1. Language Models are probabilistic models of language that are used to predict the probability of a sequence of words.
2. Grammar-based Language Models (GBLMs) are a type of language model that use a formal grammar to represent the structure of a language.
3. GBLMs are used to model the probability of a sentence given its syntactic structure.
4. GBLMs are typically composed of two components: a grammar and a probability model.
5. The grammar is a set of rules that define the structure of a language and the probability model is used to assign a probability to each sentence.
6. GBLMs can be used to generate text that follows the rules of the grammar, as well as to assign probabilities to sentences.
7. GBLMs are particularly useful for Natural Language Processing tasks such as parsing, machine translation, and text summarization.
