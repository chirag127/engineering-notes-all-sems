
### Weighted Cepstral Distances and Filtering

* Weighted Cepstral Distances (WCD) is a method of measuring the similarity between two speech signals.
* WCD measures the similarity between two speech signals by comparing the cepstral coefficients of the two signals. 
* Cepstral coefficients are obtained by taking the Fourier transform of the speech signal and then taking the inverse Fourier transform of the transformed signal.
* WCD is used in speech analysis to identify the similarity between two speech signals.
* WCD can also be used to filter out noise from a speech signal.
* Filtering is done by setting a threshold which is used to compare the cepstral coefficients of the two signals.
* If the cepstral coefficients of the two signals are above the threshold, then the signal is considered to be similar and is filtered out.
* WCD is a useful tool in speech analysis and can be used to identify similarities between two speech signals and to filter out noise.
