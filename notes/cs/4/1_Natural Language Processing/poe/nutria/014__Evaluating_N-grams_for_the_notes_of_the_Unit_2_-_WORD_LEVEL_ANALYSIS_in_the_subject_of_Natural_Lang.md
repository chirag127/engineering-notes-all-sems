
### Evaluating N-grams for the notes of the Unit 2 - WORD LEVEL ANALYSIS in the subject of Natural Language Processing

1. N-grams are sequences of n items from a given sequence of text. For example, a 3-gram (or trigram) is a sequence of three words like “the cat sat”. 
2. N-grams are commonly used in natural language processing (NLP) tasks such as language modeling, text classification, and sentiment analysis. 
3. N-grams can be used to measure the similarity between two pieces of text, or to measure the likelihood that a given sequence of words will appear in a text.
4. N-grams are also used to evaluate the performance of language models. A language model is a statistical model that assigns a probability to a given sequence of words. 
5. The performance of a language model can be evaluated by calculating the perplexity of the model. The perplexity is a measure of how well the model predicts the probability of a given sequence of words. 
6. N-grams are also used to evaluate the performance of text classification models. A text classification model is a model that assigns a label to a given piece of text. 
7. The performance of a text classification model can be evaluated by calculating the accuracy of the model. The accuracy is a measure of how well the model predicts the label of a given piece of text.
