
### Likelihood Distortions for the notes of the Unit 6 - SPEECH-ANALYSIS in the subject of Natural Language Processing

* Likelihood distortion is a problem that arises when a speech recognition system attempts to recognize speech from a large vocabulary.
* The challenge lies in the fact that the system must determine the most likely word or phrase given the input audio, but the probability of any given word or phrase is usually very low.
* To address this problem, speech recognition systems use a variety of techniques to increase the likelihood of correctly recognizing a word or phrase.
* One technique is to use a language model, which is a set of rules that assigns higher probabilities to certain words or phrases based on their frequency of occurrence in a given language.
* Another technique is to use a phonetic dictionary, which is a database of words and their associated phonemes.
* Finally, some speech recognition systems use a combination of language models and phonetic dictionaries to increase the accuracy of their predictions.
