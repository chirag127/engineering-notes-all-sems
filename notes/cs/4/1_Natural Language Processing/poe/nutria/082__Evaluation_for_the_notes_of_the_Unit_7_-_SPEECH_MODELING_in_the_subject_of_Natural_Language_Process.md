
### Evaluation for the Notes of the Unit 7 - SPEECH MODELING in the Subject of Natural Language Processing

* Evaluation of speech modeling is an important part of Natural Language Processing (NLP).
* It involves the use of metrics to measure the accuracy of a speech model.
* Common metrics used for speech modeling include word error rate (WER), perplexity, and semantic similarity.
* WER is a measure of how many words are incorrectly recognized by the model.
* Perplexity is a measure of how well the model can predict a given word given the context.
* Semantic similarity is a measure of how closely related two words are, based on their meaning.
* Evaluation of speech models can also include subjective metrics, such as user satisfaction and user experience.
* It is important to consider both objective and subjective metrics when evaluating speech models.
