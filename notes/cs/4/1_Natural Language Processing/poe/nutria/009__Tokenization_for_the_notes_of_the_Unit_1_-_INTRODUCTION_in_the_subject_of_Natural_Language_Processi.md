
### Tokenization for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of Natural Language Processing

* Tokenization is the process of breaking a sentence or text into smaller units, such as words, phrases, symbols, or other meaningful elements called tokens. 
* Tokenization helps in analyzing the structure of the sentence. It helps to identify the parts of speech, such as nouns, verbs, adjectives, etc. 
* Tokenization is a key step in natural language processing (NLP). It is used to prepare text for further processing, such as text classification, information extraction, and machine translation.
* Tokenization can be done in two ways:
    * Word tokenization: breaking the text into individual words
    * Sentence tokenization: breaking the text into individual sentences
* Tokenization is an important step for text preprocessing and can be used for a variety of tasks, such as text summarization, information extraction, and machine translation.
