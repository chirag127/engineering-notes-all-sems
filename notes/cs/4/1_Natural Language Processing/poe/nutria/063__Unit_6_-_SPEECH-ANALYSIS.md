3. Speech Analysis can be used to study the language of a particular speaker or group of speakers, to identify and analyze the language used in a particular context, or to understand the meaning of a particular speech.

5. Speech Analysis can also be used to identify and analyze the meaning of a particular utterance, to identify and analyze the context of a particular utterance, and to identify and analyze the intent of a particular utterance.
