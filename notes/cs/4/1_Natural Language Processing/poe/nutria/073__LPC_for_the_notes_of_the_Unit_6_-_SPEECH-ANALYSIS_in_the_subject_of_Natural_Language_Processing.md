
### LPC for the Notes of Unit 6 - Speech Analysis in Natural Language Processing

1. Speech Analysis is the process of analyzing spoken language to determine its meaning and purpose. 
2. LPC (Linear Predictive Coding) is a method of speech analysis that uses mathematical equations to characterize the speech signal.
3. LPC is used to analyze the spectral characteristics of speech, such as pitch, formants, and harmonics.
4. LPC is used to represent speech signals in terms of their frequency components.
5. LPC coefficients are used to represent the spectral envelope of the speech signal.
6. LPC-based speech analysis can be used to identify the speaker's gender, age, and emotion.
7. LPC-based speech analysis has applications in speech recognition, speech synthesis, and speech coding.
