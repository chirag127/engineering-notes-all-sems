
### Issues in PoS tagging for the notes of the Unit 2 - WORD LEVEL ANALYSIS in the subject of Natural Language Processing

1. One of the main issues in PoS tagging is the ambiguity of words. A single word can have multiple meanings, which can lead to different PoS tags. 
2. Another issue is the lack of context. The same word can have different PoS tags depending on the context in which it is used. 
3. PoS tagging is also affected by the complexity of the language used. Different languages have different rules for PoS tagging and it can be difficult to determine the correct PoS tag for a particular word. 
4. A further issue is the lack of resources available for PoS tagging. Different languages have different resources and it can be difficult to find the right resources for a particular language. 
5. PoS tagging is also affected by the accuracy of the algorithms used. Different algorithms can produce different results and it is important to choose the right algorithm for the task. 
6. Finally, PoS tagging is also affected by the amount of data available. The more data available, the more accurate the results will be.
