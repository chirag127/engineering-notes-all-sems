
### Speech Distortion Measures

1. **Spectral Distortion**: Spectral distortion measures are used to quantify the differences between a distorted signal and its original counterpart. These measures are typically used to assess the quality of a speech signal, and are often used to evaluate the performance of speech recognition systems.

2. **Perceptual Distortion**: Perceptual distortion measures are used to quantify the differences between a distorted signal and its original counterpart from a perceptual perspective. These measures are typically used to assess the perceived quality of a speech signal, and are often used to evaluate the performance of speech recognition systems.

3. **Cepstral Distortion**: Cepstral distortion measures are used to quantify the differences between a distorted signal and its original counterpart in the cepstral domain. These measures are typically used to assess the quality of a speech signal, and are often used to evaluate the performance of speech recognition systems.

4. **Noise Distortion**: Noise distortion measures are used to quantify the differences between a distorted signal and its original counterpart in the presence of noise. These measures are typically used to assess the quality of a speech signal, and are often used to evaluate the performance of speech recognition systems.
