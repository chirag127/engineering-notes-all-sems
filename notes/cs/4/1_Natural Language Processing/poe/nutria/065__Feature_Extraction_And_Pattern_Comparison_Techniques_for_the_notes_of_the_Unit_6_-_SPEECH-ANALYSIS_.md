
### Feature Extraction And Pattern Comparison Techniques for the Unit 6 - SPEECH-ANALYSIS in Natural Language Processing
1. Feature extraction is the process of extracting meaningful information from a given signal. This process involves extracting certain characteristics from the signal that can be used to identify or classify it.

2. Pattern comparison techniques are used to compare two or more signals to determine if they have similar characteristics. This is done by comparing the patterns of the signals and looking for similarities.

3. Speech-analysis is the process of analyzing the content of a speech. This can be done by extracting features from the speech signal, such as the frequency of certain words or phrases, and then comparing them to other speech signals.

4. Natural language processing (NLP) is the process of analyzing natural language data to gain useful insights. NLP techniques can be used to extract features from speech signals and then compare them to other speech signals.

5. Feature extraction and pattern comparison techniques can be used to classify speech signals and can be used to detect patterns in speech signals. These techniques can also be used to detect anomalies in speech signals and can be used to detect the presence of certain words or phrases.
