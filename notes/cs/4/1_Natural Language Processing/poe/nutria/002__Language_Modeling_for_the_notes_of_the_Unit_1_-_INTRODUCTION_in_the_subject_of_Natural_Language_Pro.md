
### Language Modeling for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of Natural Language Processing

* Language modeling is a technique used in natural language processing (NLP) to determine the probability of a sequence of words. 
* It is used to predict the next word in a sentence given the previous words. 
* Language models are used for many applications such as machine translation, speech recognition, and text summarization. 
* Language models are trained using large corpora of text, such as books, news articles, and webpages. 
* The most commonly used language models are n-gram models, which use the previous n words to predict the next word. 
* The n-gram model assigns a probability to each word in the sentence, based on the previous n words. 
* Other language models such as recurrent neural networks and transformers can also be used. 
* Language models can be used to generate text, by predicting the next word in a sentence based on the previous words.
