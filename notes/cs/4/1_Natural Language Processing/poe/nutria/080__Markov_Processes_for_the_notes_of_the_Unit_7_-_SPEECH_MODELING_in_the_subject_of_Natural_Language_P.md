
### Markov Processes for the Notes of Unit 7 - SPEECH MODELING in Natural Language Processing

1. Markov processes are a type of stochastic process used in natural language processing for speech modeling.
2. Markov processes are based on the assumption that the probability of a particular event depends only on the current state of the system and not on the previous history.
3. In speech modeling, Markov processes are used to model the probability of a particular word given the previous word or words.
4. Markov models can be used to generate text that is similar to a given text, by using the probability of the next word given the previous words.
5. Markov models can also be used to detect the similarity of two texts, by comparing the probability of words in each text.
6. Markov models can also be used to generate a sequence of words that best fit a given sequence of words.
7. Markov models are useful for applications such as speech recognition, natural language processing, and text generation.
