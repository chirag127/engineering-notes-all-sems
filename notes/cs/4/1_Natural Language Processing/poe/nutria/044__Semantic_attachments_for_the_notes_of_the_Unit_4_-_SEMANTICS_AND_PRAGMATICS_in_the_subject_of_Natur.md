
### Semantic Attachments for the Notes of Unit 4 - Semantics and Pragmatics in Natural Language Processing
1. Semantics is the branch of linguistics that deals with the meaning of words and phrases. It is concerned with the relationship between signs and symbols and what they represent.
2. Pragmatics is a sub-field of linguistics that studies how context contributes to meaning. It looks at how language is used in different contexts and how the meaning of utterances can change depending on the context.
3. Semantic attachments are the relationships between words and the objects they refer to. These relationships can be either literal or figurative.
4. Semantic attachments can be divided into two main categories: semantic roles and semantic relations. 
5. Semantic roles refer to the roles that words play in a sentence. Examples of semantic roles include subject, object, and verb. 
6. Semantic relations are the relationships between words in a sentence. Examples of semantic relations include synonymy, antonymy, and hyponymy. 
7. Natural language processing is the field of computer science that deals with the analysis and understanding of natural language. It is concerned with the development of algorithms and systems that can interpret and understand human language.
