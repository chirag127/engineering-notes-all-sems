
### Requirements for Representation for Unit 4 - Semantics and Pragmatics in Natural Language Processing

1. Understand the concept of word meaning and how it is related to context.
2. Recognize the role of syntax in determining sentence meaning.
3. Explain the difference between semantics and pragmatics.
4. Explain how language is used to convey meaning in a variety of contexts.
5. Describe the different types of semantic relationships between words.
6. Explain how the meaning of a sentence is affected by its context.
7. Describe how the meaning of a sentence can change depending on its intonation.
8. Identify and describe the different types of pragmatic meaning.
9. Explain the concept of presupposition and how it affects meaning.
10. Explain how language can be used to express politeness and politeness strategies.
