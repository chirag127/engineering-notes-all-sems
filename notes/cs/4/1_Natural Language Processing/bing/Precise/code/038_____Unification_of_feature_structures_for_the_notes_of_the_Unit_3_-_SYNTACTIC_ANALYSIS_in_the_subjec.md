### Unification of Feature Structures

- Unification is a process of combining two or more feature structures into a single structure.
- It is used in syntactic analysis to combine the features of different linguistic units, such as words or phrases, to form a complete representation of a sentence.
- Unification is based on the principle of feature compatibility, which means that the values of corresponding features in the structures being unified must be compatible.
- If the values of corresponding features are not compatible, unification fails and the structures cannot be combined.
- Unification can be used to enforce grammatical constraints, such as agreement between the subject and verb of a sentence.
- It is a powerful tool for representing and manipulating linguistic information in natural language processing.
