### HMMs for the notes of the Unit 7 - SPEECH MODELING in the subject of Natural Language Processing

- Hidden Markov Models (HMMs) are a statistical tool used for modeling generative sequences.
- HMMs are used in speech recognition to model the sequence of speech sounds in spoken language.
- An HMM is defined by a set of states, a set of observations, and the probabilities of transitioning between states and emitting observations.
- The Viterbi algorithm is used to find the most likely sequence of states given a sequence of observations.
- The Baum-Welch algorithm is used to estimate the parameters of an HMM given a set of observed sequences.
- HMMs can be used to model both discrete and continuous observations.
- In speech recognition, HMMs are often used to model phonemes, the basic units of sound in a language.
- HMMs can also be used to model other aspects of speech, such as prosody and intonation.
- HMMs have been widely used in speech recognition systems, and have been successful in achieving high recognition accuracy.
