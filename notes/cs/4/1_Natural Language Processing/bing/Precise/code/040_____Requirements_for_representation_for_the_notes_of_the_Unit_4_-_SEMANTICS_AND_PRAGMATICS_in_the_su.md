### Requirements for representation for the notes of the Unit 4 - SEMANTICS AND PRAGMATICS in the subject of Natural Language Processing

1. The representation must be able to capture the meaning of words, phrases, and sentences in a given language.
2. It must be able to represent the relationships between different linguistic units, such as synonymy, antonymy, and hyponymy.
3. It must be able to represent the context in which language is used, including the speaker, the listener, the time and place of the utterance, and the shared knowledge between the speaker and the listener.
4. It must be able to represent the pragmatic aspects of language use, such as implicature, presupposition, and speech acts.
5. It must be able to handle ambiguity and vagueness in language.
6. It must be able to represent the structure of sentences, including their syntactic and semantic roles.
7. It must be able to represent the compositional nature of meaning, where the meaning of a sentence is derived from the meanings of its parts and the way they are combined.
8. It must be able to represent the dynamic nature of language, where the meaning of an utterance can change depending on the context in which it is used.
9. It must be able to represent the inferential nature of language, where the meaning of an utterance is not always explicitly stated but must be inferred from the context.
10. It must be able to represent the cultural and social aspects of language use, such as politeness, formality, and register.
