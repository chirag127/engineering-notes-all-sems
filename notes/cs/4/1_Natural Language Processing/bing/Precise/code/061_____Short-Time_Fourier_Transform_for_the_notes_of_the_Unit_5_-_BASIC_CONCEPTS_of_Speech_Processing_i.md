### Short-Time Fourier Transform

- The short-time Fourier transform (STFT) is a powerful general-purpose tool for audio signal processing .
- It is the most commonly used domain for speech and audio processing .
- It defines a particularly useful class of time-frequency distributions which specify complex amplitude versus time and frequency for any signal .
- STFT is a natural extension of Fourier transform in addressing signal non-stationarity by applying windows for segmented analysis .
