### Markov Processes

Markov processes are used in the field of Natural Language Processing (NLP) for speech modeling. A Markov model is a stochastic model that models temporal or sequential data, i.e., data that is ordered. It provides a way to model the dependencies of current information with previous information. It is composed of states, a transition scheme between states, and the emission of outputs (discrete or continuous) .

Markov analysis is used in NLP to generate a sequence of words that form a complete sentence using a Markov chain, or to perform named-entity recognition and tagging parts of speech using a hidden Markov model . Markov models are also used in machine learning .

Language models are the backbone of NLP and Markov models, specifically n-grams, are closely tied to the concept . Some NLP tasks that use language modeling include transcription, language identification, and audio document retrieval .

In speech recognition, hidden Markov models (HMMs) are used to model units of speech such as phones or words, which can then be concatenated into larger units .
