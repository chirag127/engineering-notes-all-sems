## Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION

1. Introduction refers to the beginning or the preliminary explanation of a topic or subject.
2. It provides the reader with the necessary background information and context to understand the topic.
3. An introduction should be clear, concise, and engaging to capture the reader's attention.
4. It should also provide a brief overview of the main points that will be discussed in the text.
5. The introduction is an important part of any text, as it sets the tone and provides the foundation for the rest of the content.
6. A well-written introduction can help the reader to better understand and engage with the topic at hand.
