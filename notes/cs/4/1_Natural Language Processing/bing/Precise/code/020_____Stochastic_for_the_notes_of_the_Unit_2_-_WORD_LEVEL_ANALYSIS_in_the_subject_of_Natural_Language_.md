### Stochastic

Stochastic refers to a randomly determined process. In the context of Natural Language Processing (NLP), stochastic methods are used to model the probability of a sequence of words in a language.

Here are some key points to consider when studying stochastic methods in NLP:

1. Stochastic models are used to predict the likelihood of a word sequence, given a set of observed data.
2. These models are based on probability theory and can be used to analyze and generate text.
3. One common stochastic method used in NLP is the n-gram model, which predicts the probability of a word given the previous n-1 words in a sequence.
4. Another common method is the Hidden Markov Model (HMM), which is used to model sequential data and can be applied to speech recognition and part-of-speech tagging.
5. Stochastic methods can also be used in machine translation, where they are used to predict the most likely translation of a given text.

In summary, stochastic methods are an important tool in NLP, allowing us to model and predict language in a probabilistic manner. These methods can be applied to a wide range of tasks, including text generation, speech recognition, and machine translation.
