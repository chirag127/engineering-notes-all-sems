### English Morphology for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of Natural Language Processing

- Morphology is the study of the internal structure of words and the rules for forming words from their subparts, called morphemes.
- Morphemes are the smallest units of meaning in a language. They can be either free or bound.
- Free morphemes can stand alone as words, while bound morphemes must be attached to other morphemes to form words.
- English morphology includes inflectional and derivational processes.
- Inflectional processes create different grammatical forms of a word, such as plural or past tense forms.
- Derivational processes create new words by adding affixes to existing words or combining two or more words.
- English has a relatively simple inflectional system, with only eight inflectional suffixes.
- Derivational processes in English are more complex and can involve the addition of prefixes, suffixes, or both.
- Morphological analysis is the process of breaking down a word into its constituent morphemes to determine its meaning and grammatical function.
- Morphological analysis is an important component of natural language processing, as it helps computers understand and generate human language.
