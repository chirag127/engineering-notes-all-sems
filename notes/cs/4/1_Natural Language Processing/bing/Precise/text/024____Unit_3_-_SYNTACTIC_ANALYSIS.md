## Unit 3 - SYNTACTIC ANALYSIS

Syntactic analysis, also known as parsing, is the process of analyzing a string of symbols, either in natural language or in computer languages, according to the rules of a formal grammar.

1. The goal of syntactic analysis is to determine the structure of the input sentence and to check its grammatical correctness.
2. Syntactic analysis is usually performed by a parser, which can be either top-down or bottom-up.
3. Top-down parsing starts with the highest level of the parse tree and works its way down, while bottom-up parsing starts with the lowest level and works its way up.
4. Common parsing algorithms include the Earley parser, the CYK parser, and the LL and LR parsers.
5. Syntactic analysis is an important step in natural language processing and is used in applications such as machine translation, speech recognition, and text-to-speech conversion.
