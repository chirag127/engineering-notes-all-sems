## Unit 2 - WORD LEVEL ANALYSIS

Word level analysis is the process of breaking down a text into its individual words and examining their meanings, usage, and relationships with other words in the text. This type of analysis is important for understanding the meaning of a text and for identifying patterns and trends in language use.

Some key concepts in word level analysis include:

1. **Morphology:** The study of the structure and formation of words, including the rules for combining morphemes (the smallest units of meaning) to form words.

2. **Lexicology:** The study of the vocabulary of a language, including the meanings, origins, and usage of words.

3. **Semantics:** The study of meaning in language, including the relationships between words and the concepts they represent.

4. **Syntax:** The study of the rules governing the arrangement of words in sentences.

5. **Pragmatics:** The study of how context and situation influence the meaning and use of language.

Word level analysis can be used for a variety of purposes, including language teaching, linguistic research, and natural language processing. It is an essential tool for understanding how language works and for developing effective communication skills.
