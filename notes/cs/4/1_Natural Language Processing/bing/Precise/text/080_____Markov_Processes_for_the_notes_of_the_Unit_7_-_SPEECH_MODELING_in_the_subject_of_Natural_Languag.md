### Markov Processes

Markov Processes are used in the field of Natural Language Processing (NLP) for speech modeling. They provide a way to model the dependencies of current information with previous information. Markov analysis is used to generate a sequence of words that form a complete sentence, or a hidden Markov model can be used for named-entity recognition and tagging parts of speech .

- Markov models are composed of states, transition scheme between states, and emission of outputs (discrete or continuous) .
- Markov analysis is also used in machine learning .
- Markov chain is the purest Markov model .
- Applications of Markov modeling include modeling languages, image processing, bioinformatics, speech recognition, and modeling computer hardware and software systems .
- Language models are the backbone of NLP .
- N-grams are tied very closely to the concept of Markov models .
- Hidden Markov Models (HMMs) can be used for continuous speech recognition .
- HMMs can be used to model some unit of speech (phone, word) and concatenate units into larger units .

Markov Processes are a powerful tool for speech modeling in NLP and have a wide range of applications. They provide a way to model temporal or sequential data and can be used to generate natural language sentences, recognize named entities, and tag parts of speech. They are also used in machine learning and have applications in other fields such as image processing and bioinformatics.
