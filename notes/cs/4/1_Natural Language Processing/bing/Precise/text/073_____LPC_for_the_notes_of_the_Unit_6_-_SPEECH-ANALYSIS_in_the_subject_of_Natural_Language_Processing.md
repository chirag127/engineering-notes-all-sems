### LPC for the notes of the Unit 6 - SPEECH-ANALYSIS in the subject of Natural Language Processing

- LPC stands for Linear Predictive Coding.
- It is a tool used for representing the spectral envelope of a digital speech signal in compressed form.
- LPC is based on the source-filter model of speech production.
- The basic idea behind LPC is to minimize the sum of the squared differences between the original speech signal and the estimated signal.
- The coefficients of the LPC filter are determined by solving a set of linear equations using the Levinson-Durbin algorithm.
- LPC is widely used in speech coding and speech synthesis.
- It is also used in speech recognition to extract features from the speech signal.
- LPC can be used to estimate the formants of a speech signal, which are important for speech recognition and speech synthesis.
