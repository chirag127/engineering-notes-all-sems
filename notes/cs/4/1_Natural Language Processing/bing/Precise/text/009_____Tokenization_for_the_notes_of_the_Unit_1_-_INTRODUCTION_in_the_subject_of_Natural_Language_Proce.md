### Tokenization

Tokenization is the process of breaking down text into smaller units called tokens. These tokens can be words, phrases, or even sentences. In the context of Natural Language Processing, tokenization is an essential step in preparing text data for further analysis.

Here are some key points to remember about tokenization:

1. Tokenization is a fundamental step in many natural language processing tasks, such as text classification, sentiment analysis, and machine translation.
2. The choice of tokenization method can have a significant impact on the performance of the NLP model.
3. There are several methods for tokenization, including whitespace-based, rule-based, and statistical methods.
4. The choice of tokenization method depends on the specific task and the nature of the text data.
5. Tokenization can be performed at different levels of granularity, such as character-level, word-level, or sentence-level.
6. Tokenization is not always a straightforward process, and can be complicated by factors such as the presence of multi-word expressions, contractions, and punctuation.
