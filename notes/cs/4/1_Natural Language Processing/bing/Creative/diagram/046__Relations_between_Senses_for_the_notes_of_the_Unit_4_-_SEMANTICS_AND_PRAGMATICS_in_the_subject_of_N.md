Relations between senses are the semantic or meaning relations that hold between words or expressions of a language. They can be classified into two main types: paradigmatic and syntagmatic. Paradigmatic relations are those that hold between words that can substitute for each other in a given context, such as synonyms, antonyms, hyponyms, etc. Syntagmatic relations are those that hold between words that can combine with each other in a given context, such as selectional restrictions, collocations, etc.
