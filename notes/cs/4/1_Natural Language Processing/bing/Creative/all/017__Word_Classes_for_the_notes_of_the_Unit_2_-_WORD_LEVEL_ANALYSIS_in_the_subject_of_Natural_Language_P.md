### Word Classes for the notes of the Unit 2 - WORD LEVEL ANALYSIS in the subject of Natural Language Processing

- Word classes, also known as parts-of-speech, are categories of words that share similar syntactic and morphological properties, such as word order, inflection, and function.
- Word classes are useful for natural language processing because they provide information about a word and its neighbors, such as pronunciation, meaning, and possible grammatical structures.
- Word classes can be divided into major classes, such as nouns, verbs, adjectives, and adverbs, and minor classes, such as pronouns, prepositions, conjunctions, and interjections.
- Word classes can also be further subdivided into finer distinctions, such as singular and plural nouns, transitive and intransitive verbs, comparative and superlative adjectives, and so on.
- Word classes can be assigned to words automatically by using algorithms that rely on lexical, morphological, and contextual cues, such as word endings, prefixes, suffixes, and surrounding words.
- Word classes can be represented by symbols or tags, such as NN for singular noun, VB for base form verb, JJ for adjective, and RB for adverb. Different tagsets may have different numbers and types of tags, depending on the level of detail and the language being analyzed.
- Word classes can also be represented by vectors or embeddings, which are numerical representations of words that capture their semantic and syntactic similarities and differences. Word embeddings can be learned from large corpora of text using neural networks or other machine learning techniques.

Some possible mnemonics and learning tricks for word classes are:

- Nouns are names of people, places, things, or ideas. A trick to remember nouns is to think of the word "noun" as an acronym for "name of unique noun".
- Verbs are actions or states of being. A trick to remember verbs is to think of the word "verb" as an acronym for "very energetic, really busy".
- Adjectives are words that describe nouns. A trick to remember adjectives is to think of the word "adjective" as an acronym for "adds details, justifies, enhances, clarifies, tells, identifies, varies, explains".
- Adverbs are words that modify verbs, adjectives, or other adverbs. A trick to remember adverbs is to think of the word "adverb" as an acronym for "adds, describes, varies, emphasizes, relates, boosts".
