### Acoustic Phonetics for the notes of the Unit 5 - BASIC CONCEPTS of Speech Processing in the subject of Natural Language Processing

- Acoustic phonetics is the study of the acoustic characteristics of speech, including an analysis and description of speech in terms of its physical properties, such as frequency, intensity, and duration .
- Acoustic phonetics is an instrumental science that depends on ways to store, replicate, visualize, and analyze the speech signal. Acoustic phonetics is also a cumulative science in which older research continues to be influential .
- Acoustic phonetics investigates time domain features such as the mean squared amplitude of a waveform, its duration, its fundamental frequency, or frequency domain features such as the frequency spectrum, or even combined spectrotemporal features and the relationship of these properties to other branches of phonetics (e.g. articulatory or auditory phonetics), and to abstract linguistic concepts such as phonemes, phrases, or utterances.
- Acoustic phonetics was greatly enhanced in the late 19th century by the invention of the Edison phonograph, which allowed the speech signal to be recorded and then later processed and analyzed. By replaying the same speech signal from the phonograph several times, filtering it each time with a different band-pass filter, a spectrogram of the speech utterance could be built up.
- A spectrogram is a visual representation of the spectrum of frequencies of a signal as it varies with time. A spectrogram shows the frequency components of a speech sound along the vertical axis, the time along the horizontal axis, and the intensity of each frequency component as a shade of gray or color.
- A spectrogram can reveal important information about the speech signal, such as the formants, the harmonics, the noise, the voice quality, the prosody, etc. A formant is a concentration of acoustic energy around a particular frequency in the speech wave. A harmonic is a component frequency of the sound that is an integer multiple of the fundamental frequency. Noise is a random variation of amplitude in the speech signal. Voice quality is the characteristic sound of a speaker's voice, influenced by factors such as the shape and size of the vocal tract, the tension of the vocal folds, the degree of glottal closure, etc. Prosody is the variation of pitch, loudness, and duration in speech, which conveys information about the structure, meaning, and attitude of the utterance.
- A mnemonic to remember the difference between formants and harmonics is: **Formants are Frequency, Harmonics are Height**. This means that formants are related to the frequency of the speech sound, while harmonics are related to the height of the waveform. Another mnemonic is: **Formants are Filters, Harmonics are Hums**. This means that formants are the result of filtering the speech sound by the vocal tract, while harmonics are the result of humming the speech sound by the vocal folds.
- Acoustic phonetics can be applied to various fields of natural language processing, such as speech recognition, speech synthesis, speech enhancement, speech segmentation, speech analysis, speech coding, speech compression, speech translation, speech emotion recognition, speaker identification, speaker verification, speaker diarization, etc.
