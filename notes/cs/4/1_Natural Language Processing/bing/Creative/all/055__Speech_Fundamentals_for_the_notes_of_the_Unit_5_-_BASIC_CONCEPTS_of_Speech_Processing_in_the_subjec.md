### Speech Fundamentals for the notes of the Unit 5 - BASIC CONCEPTS of Speech Processing in the subject of Natural Language Processing

- Speech processing is the study of how humans produce, perceive, and understand speech, and how computers can analyze, synthesize, and recognize speech signals.
- Speech processing is an interdisciplinary field that involves linguistics, acoustics, signal processing, artificial intelligence, and machine learning.
- Speech processing has many applications, such as speech recognition, speech synthesis, speech enhancement, speech coding, speech translation, speech emotion recognition, speaker identification, and speech forensics.
- Speech processing can be divided into three main stages: speech analysis, speech representation, and speech processing.
  - Speech analysis is the process of extracting features and information from speech signals, such as pitch, intensity, duration, formants, spectral envelope, etc.
  - Speech representation is the process of transforming speech signals into a suitable format for further processing, such as waveform, spectrogram, cepstrum, linear prediction coefficients, mel-frequency cepstral coefficients, etc.
  - Speech processing is the process of manipulating, modifying, or generating speech signals, such as filtering, enhancement, compression, synthesis, recognition, etc.
- Speech processing can be performed in different domains, such as time domain, frequency domain, cepstral domain, etc. Each domain has its own advantages and disadvantages, and different techniques and algorithms are used for each domain.
- Speech processing can be performed using different methods, such as parametric, non-parametric, statistical, neural, etc. Each method has its own assumptions, limitations, and performance, and different methods are suitable for different tasks and applications.
- Speech processing can be evaluated using different criteria, such as accuracy, intelligibility, naturalness, quality, robustness, etc. Each criterion measures a different aspect of speech processing, and different criteria are relevant for different tasks and applications.

Some mnemonics and learning tricks for speech fundamentals are:

- To remember the three main stages of speech processing, use the acronym ARP: Analysis, Representation, Processing.
- To remember the different domains of speech processing, use the acronym TFC: Time, Frequency, Cepstral.
- To remember the different methods of speech processing, use the acronym PNSN: Parametric, Non-parametric, Statistical, Neural.
- To remember the different criteria of speech processing, use the acronym AINQR: Accuracy, Intelligibility, Naturalness, Quality, Robustness.
