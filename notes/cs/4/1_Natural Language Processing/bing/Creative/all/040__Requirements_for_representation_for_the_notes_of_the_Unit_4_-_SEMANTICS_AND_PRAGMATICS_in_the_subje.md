### Requirements for representation for the notes of the Unit 4 - SEMANTICS AND PRAGMATICS in the subject of Natural Language Processing

- Representation is a crucial component of natural language processing (NLP), as it determines how much useful information can be extracted from raw data for further classification or prediction .
- Representation learning is a branch of machine learning that aims to learn informative representations of objects from raw data automatically, without relying on manual feature engineering .
- Representation learning can improve NLP by making machine learning algorithms more flexible and desirable while handling large-scale and noisy unstructured data, such as speech, images, videos, time series, and texts .
- Representation learning can take part in various important topics of NLP, such as word embeddings, language models, syntactic parsing, semantic analysis, sentiment analysis, machine translation, question answering, and text generation .
- Representation learning can also address some challenges that are still not well addressed by conventional representation schemes, such as sparsity, ambiguity, polysemy, and compositionality .
- Representation learning typically adopts distributed representation, which represents each object with a low-dimensional real-valued dense vector, also known as an embedding .
- Distributed representation is able to represent data in a more compact and smoothing way, and hence is more robust to address the sparsity issue in large-scale data .
- Distributed representation can also capture semantic and syntactic similarities and relations among objects, such as words, phrases, sentences, and documents .
- Distributed representation can be learned by various methods, such as neural networks, matrix factorization, probabilistic models, and autoencoders .
- Representation learning can be divided into two subtasks: natural language understanding (NLU) and natural language generation (NLG).
- NLU is the process of mapping the given input in natural language into useful representations, such as logical forms, semantic graphs, or feature vectors.
- NLU involves analyzing different aspects of the language, such as morphology, syntax, semantics, pragmatics, and discourse.
- NLG is the process of producing meaningful phrases and sentences in the form of natural language from some internal representation, such as a knowledge base, a database, or a vector.
- NLG involves planning the content, structure, and style of the output, as well as realizing the linguistic expressions.
- Representation learning can also be applied to the domain of requirements engineering, which is the process of defining and maintaining a comprehensive, valid, and accurate body of requirements for a system.
- Representation learning can assist in the requirements decomposition process, which is the process of breaking down a set of requirements into smaller and more manageable units.
- Representation learning can use modern language processing tools to scan and tag a set of requirements, and offer support to systems engineers in their task of defining and maintaining a comprehensive, valid, and accurate body of requirements.
- Representation learning can also integrate NLP with ontologies and textual requirements, which can help to extract, organize, and validate the relevant concepts and relations from a requirements specification.
