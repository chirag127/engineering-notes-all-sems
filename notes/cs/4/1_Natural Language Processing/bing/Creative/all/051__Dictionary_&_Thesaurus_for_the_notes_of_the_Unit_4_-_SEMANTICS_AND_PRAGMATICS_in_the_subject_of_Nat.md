### Dictionary & Thesaurus for the notes of the Unit 4 - SEMANTICS AND PRAGMATICS in the subject of Natural Language Processing

- A dictionary is a collection of words and their meanings, often with additional information such as pronunciation, usage, synonyms, antonyms, etc.
- A thesaurus is a specialized dictionary that stores synonyms and antonyms of selected words in a language. Thus, a thesaurus is a resource that groups words according to similarity (Kilgarriff, 2003).
- Both dictionaries and thesauruses are useful for natural language processing (NLP), which is the application of machine learning algorithms to the analysis, understanding, and manipulation of written or spoken examples of human language.
- Some of the applications of dictionaries and thesauruses in NLP are:

  - Word sense disambiguation: This is the task of determining the correct meaning of a word in a given context, when the word has multiple possible meanings. For example, the word "bark" can mean the outer covering of a tree or the sound made by a dog. Dictionaries and thesauruses can provide clues to the correct sense based on the definition, synonyms, antonyms, and usage examples of the word.
  - Lexicography: This is the art and science of compiling and editing dictionaries. Much of the modern lexicography is corpus-based, which means that it relies on large collections of texts to extract information about words and their usage. Dictionaries and thesauruses can be used to provide textual indicators for word senses, such as collocations, frequency, register, etc.
  - Text mining and information extraction: These are the tasks of extracting useful information from large amounts of text data, such as documents, web pages, social media posts, etc. Dictionaries and thesauruses can help to identify and label words that are relevant for a specific domain, topic, or task. For example, a text mining system should be able to distinguish between a coal "mine" and a land "mine", based on their definitions, synonyms, and antonyms.

- Some of the challenges and limitations of dictionaries and thesauruses in NLP are:

  - Coverage: Dictionaries and thesauruses may not contain all the words and senses that are used in natural language, especially for new, rare, or domain-specific words. Moreover, different dictionaries and thesauruses may have different criteria for selecting and defining words, which may lead to inconsistencies and discrepancies.
  - Ambiguity: Dictionaries and thesauruses may not provide clear and unambiguous definitions and classifications of words and senses, which may make it difficult for NLP systems to interpret and use them. For example, a thesaurus may group words based on their similarity, but similarity can be subjective and context-dependent. Also, a dictionary may define a word based on its most common or general sense, but that may not be the sense that is intended or relevant in a specific context.
  - Quality: Dictionaries and thesauruses may contain errors, inaccuracies, or outdated information, which may affect their reliability and usefulness for NLP tasks. For example, a dictionary may have spelling or grammatical mistakes, a thesaurus may have missing or incorrect synonyms or antonyms, or both may have obsolete or inappropriate words or senses.

- Some of the possible mnemonics and learning tricks for dictionaries and thesauruses in NLP are:

  - Dictionaries Define, Thesauruses Synonymize: This can help to remember the main difference between dictionaries and thesauruses, that dictionaries provide definitions and thesauruses provide synonyms (and antonyms).
  - Word Sense Disambiguation: WSD: This can help to remember the acronym and the meaning of word sense disambiguation, which is one of the main applications of dictionaries and thesauruses in NLP.
  - Lexicography: Lexi-Corpus: This can help to remember that lexicography is based on corpus data, which are large collections of texts that provide information about words and their usage.
  - Text Mining: Mine Words: This can help to remember that text mining is the task of extracting useful information from text data, and that dictionaries and thesauruses can help to identify and label words that are relevant for a specific domain, topic, or task.
