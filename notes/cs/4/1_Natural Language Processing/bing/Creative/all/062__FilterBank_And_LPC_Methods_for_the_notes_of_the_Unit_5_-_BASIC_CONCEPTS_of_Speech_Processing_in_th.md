### Filter Bank And LPC Methods for the notes of the Unit 5 - BASIC CONCEPTS of Speech Processing in the subject of Natural Language Processing

- Filter Bank and LPC Methods are two techniques for feature extraction in speech recognition systems. Feature extraction is the process of converting the speech waveform into a parametric representation that captures the relevant information for recognition. 
- Filter Bank Methods are based on the idea of dividing the frequency spectrum of the speech signal into several sub-bands, and computing the energy or power of each sub-band. The sub-bands are usually chosen to match the human perception of sound, such as the mel-scale or the bark-scale. The most common filter bank method is the Mel-Frequency Cepstral Coefficients (MFCC), which applies a discrete cosine transform (DCT) to the logarithm of the filter bank energies to obtain a compact and decorrelated representation.  
- LPC Methods are based on the idea of modeling the speech signal as a linear combination of past samples, using a linear predictor. The linear predictor coefficients can be used to estimate the spectral envelope of the speech signal, which is then converted into a cepstral representation by applying a logarithm and a DCT. The most common LPC method is the Linear Predictive Cepstral Coefficients (LPCC), which uses the autocorrelation method to obtain the linear predictor coefficients.  
- The advantages of Filter Bank Methods are that they are more robust to noise and channel distortions, and that they can capture the spectral shape and dynamics of the speech signal. The disadvantages are that they require more computation and memory, and that they may lose some information due to the logarithmic compression.  
- The advantages of LPC Methods are that they are more efficient and compact, and that they can capture the vocal tract characteristics of the speech signal. The disadvantages are that they are more sensitive to noise and pitch variations, and that they may introduce some artifacts due to the linear prediction model.  
- A possible mnemonic to remember the difference between Filter Bank and LPC Methods is: **F**ilter **B**ank = **F**requency **B**ands, **L**PC = **L**inear **P**rediction. 
- A possible learning trick to understand the concept of cepstral coefficients is to think of them as the spectrum of the spectrum, or the coefficients of a Fourier series that approximates the logarithm of the spectrum.
