## Unit 6 - SPEECH-ANALYSIS

- Speech analysis is the process of extracting relevant information from the speech signal, such as the speaker's identity, emotions, intentions, and linguistic content .
- Speech analysis can be used for various purposes, such as quality assurance, customer service, security, forensics, education, and entertainment .
- Speech analysis involves several steps, such as speech segmentation, feature extraction, classification, and interpretation.
- Speech segmentation is the process of dividing the speech signal into smaller units, such as words, syllables, or phonemes.
- Feature extraction is the process of transforming the speech signal into a more compact and meaningful representation, such as spectral, temporal, or prosodic features.
- Classification is the process of assigning labels to the speech segments or features, such as speaker identity, emotion, language, or topic.
- Interpretation is the process of deriving higher-level information from the speech segments or features, such as the speaker's attitude, intention, or personality.
- Speech analysis can be performed on recorded or live speech, using speech recognition software or human listeners .
- Speech analysis can be influenced by various factors, such as the speech production mechanism, the speech transmission channel, the background noise, and the audience expectations .
- Speech analysis can be improved by using multiple sources of information, such as visual cues, contextual knowledge, or feedback mechanisms .
