### Features for the notes of the Unit 6 - SPEECH-ANALYSIS in the subject of Natural Language Processing

- Speech analysis is the task of processing and understanding human speech by computers.
- Speech analysis involves two main components: speech recognition and natural language processing.
- Speech recognition is the task of converting voice data into text data, while natural language processing is the task of analyzing the meaning and intent of the text data.
- Speech analysis can be used for various applications, such as voice-operated systems, speech-to-text transcription, speech synthesis, dialogue systems, sentiment analysis, and speech pathology.
- Speech analysis faces many challenges, such as the variability and ambiguity of human language, the noise and distortion of speech signals, the diversity of accents and dialects, and the complexity of linguistic and pragmatic rules.
- Speech analysis requires various techniques and tools, such as acoustic models, language models, grammars, parsers, lexicons, ontologies, and machine learning algorithms.
- Speech analysis can be evaluated by different metrics, such as accuracy, precision, recall, F1-score, word error rate, and mean opinion score.
