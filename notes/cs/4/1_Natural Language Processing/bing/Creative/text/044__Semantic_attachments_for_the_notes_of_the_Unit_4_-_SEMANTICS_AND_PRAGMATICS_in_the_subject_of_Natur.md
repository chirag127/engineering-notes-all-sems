### Semantic attachments for the notes of the Unit 4 - SEMANTICS AND PRAGMATICS in the subject of Natural Language Processing

- Semantics is the study of meaning in natural language, and pragmatics is the study of meaning in context.
- Semantic analysis is a subfield of natural language processing that helps machines to recognize and interpret the context and emotions of any text sample.
- Semantic analysis can also help to extract valuable data from unstructured information, such as customer reviews, social media posts, or news articles.
- Semantic grammar is a type of grammar that uses semantic categories, such as PERSON, COMPANY, DATE, or GEO, instead of linguistic categories, such as noun, verb, or adjective.
- Semantic grammar can help to resolve linguistic ambiguity, which is when a sentence can have more than one meaning depending on the context .
- Semantic attachments are the rules or functions that map the semantic categories to their corresponding meanings or representations in a knowledge base or a logic system.
- Semantic attachments can be used to perform various tasks, such as question answering, information extraction, or dialogue management.
- Semantic attachments can be implemented in different ways, such as using lambda calculus, first-order logic, or ontologies.
