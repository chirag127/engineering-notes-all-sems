### Cepstral Distances for the notes of the Unit 6 - SPEECH-ANALYSIS in the subject of Natural Language Processing

- Cepstral distance is a measure of the similarity between two speech frames based on their cepstral coefficients.
- Cepstral coefficients are obtained by applying the inverse discrete Fourier transform (IDFT) to the logarithm of the magnitude spectrum of a speech frame.
- Cepstral distance can be used for various purposes in speech analysis, such as endpoint detection, isolated word recognition, and emotional speech recognition  .
- Cepstral distance can also be used for deconvolving the speech signal into its source and filter components, by applying a liftering operation in the cepstral domain .
- Cepstral distance can be combined with other features, such as speech energy, to improve the performance of speech classification tasks .
- Cepstral distance is related to the concept of cepstral peak prominence (CPP), which is a measure of the overall level of noise or dysphonia in the speech signal. CPP can be used for clinical voice evaluation and estimation of auditory-perceptual ratings of dysphonia severity.
