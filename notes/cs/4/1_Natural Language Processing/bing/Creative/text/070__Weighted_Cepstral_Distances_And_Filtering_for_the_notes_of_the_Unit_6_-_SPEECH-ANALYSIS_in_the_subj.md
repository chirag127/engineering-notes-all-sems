### Weighted Cepstral Distances And Filtering

- The cepstrum is defined as the inverse DFT of the log magnitude of the DFT of a signal .
- The cepstrum can be used to separate the source and filter components of a speech signal by treating the log spectrum as a waveform and applying a liftering operation.
- The cepstrum can also be used to measure the similarity or distance between two signals, such as for speaker identification or speech recognition .
- A weighted cepstral distance is a distance measure that assigns different weights to different cepstral coefficients, based on some criteria such as statistical variance or perceptual relevance .
- A weighted cepstral filter is a filter that modifies the cepstral coefficients of a signal according to some weighting function, such as to compensate for the spectral tilt or to enhance the perceptual quality of the signal .
- A weighted cepstral filter can be derived from the cepstral difference of two representations of the signal, such as the linear prediction cepstrum and the immittance spectral pairs pseudo-cepstrum.
