### Four Levels of Federation for the notes of the Unit 5 - Cloud Technologies And Advancements Hadoop in the subject of Cloud Computing

1. Data Federation: Combines data from multiple data sources and presents it as a single source. Enables querying and managing data from multiple databases through a single interface.
Advantages: Provides a unified view of data, hides complexities of multiple data sources, increases performance.

2. Computational Federation: Combines computing resources from multiple systems to solve a problem. Workload is distributed across systems to improve performance, throughput, and resource utilization.
Advantages: Harness disparate computing resources, improved performance and scalability, fault tolerance.

3. Knowledge Federation: Combines knowledge and intelligence from multiple AI systems to solve complex problems. Systems work together by sharing and integrating knowledge to provide better solutions than individual systems.
Advantages: Leverages diverse knowledge and skills, solves complex problems, provides explainability.

4. Service Federation: Combines services from multiple service providers to create new value-added services. Service requests are routed to appropriate providers based on functional requirements, quality of service needs, and provider capabilities.
Advantages: Utilizes specialized services, increased availability and reliability, lower costs.

Mnemonics:
DCFK - Data, Computation, Knowledge, Service Federation

Advantages of Federation: Unified access, improved performance, scalability, utilization, complex problem solving, lower costs.

Disadvantages: Complex to implement, heterogeneous systems integration, semantic interoperability, fault diagnosis and recovery.

Applications: E-commerce, weather forecasting, robotic systems, smart cities.
