### Federated Services and Applications for the notes of the Unit 5 - Cloud Technologies And Advancements Hadoop in the subject of Cloud Computing

In cloud computing, federated services and applications refer to the integration of various cloud services and applications from multiple providers to create a seamless and unified experience for the end-users. Federated services and applications have become an essential part of cloud computing as it allows users to access resources from different cloud providers without any hassle.

Here are some of the important points to understand about federated services and applications in cloud computing:

- Federated services and applications enable users to access resources from different cloud providers without the need to create multiple accounts and manage them separately.
- The federated services and applications architecture consists of a federation identity provider (FIdP) and service providers (SPs). The FIdP is responsible for authenticating and authorizing users, while the SPs are responsible for providing access to their services.
- One of the benefits of federated services and applications is that it allows for better resource utilization. Since the resources are shared across multiple cloud providers, it ensures that the resources are being utilized to their maximum capacity.
- Federated services and applications also provide better reliability and availability. Since the resources are distributed across multiple cloud providers, it ensures that if one provider goes down, the services and applications can still be accessed from other providers.
- One of the challenges of federated services and applications is the complexity involved in managing the integration between different cloud providers. It requires a high level of coordination and standardization between the providers to ensure interoperability and seamless integration.
- There are various standards and protocols used for federated services and applications, such as SAML, OAuth, OpenID Connect, and WS-Federation. These standards ensure that different cloud providers can communicate and exchange information securely and seamlessly.
- Hadoop is a popular framework for processing large datasets in distributed computing environments. It is also widely used in cloud computing environments for big data processing. Hadoop supports federated services and applications through its support for various authentication and authorization protocols, such as Kerberos and LDAP.

Mnemonic: 
Here's a simple mnemonic to remember the benefits of federated services and applications in cloud computing - RAB (Reliability, Availability, Better resource utilization).

Learning Trick: 
To learn the various standards and protocols used for federated services and applications, you can use the acronym SOWW (SAML, OAuth, OpenID Connect, and WS-Federation).
