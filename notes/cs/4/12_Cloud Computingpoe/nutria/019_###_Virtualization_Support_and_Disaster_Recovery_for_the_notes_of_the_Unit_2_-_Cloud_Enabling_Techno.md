
### Virtualization Support and Disaster Recovery for the notes of the Unit 2 - Cloud Enabling Technologies Service Oriented Architecture in the subject of Cloud Computing

Virtualization is the process of creating a virtual version of a physical computer, server, storage device, or network resources. It allows organizations to reduce their overall IT costs by consolidating hardware and software resources and providing more efficient use of resources. Disaster recovery is the process of restoring data and systems that have been lost due to a disaster. 

Virtualization and disaster recovery are two important aspects of cloud computing. Virtualization enables organizations to create a virtual version of their physical environment, while disaster recovery provides the ability to recover lost data and systems. 

Mnemonics and Learning Tricks: 

* VDR: Virtualization, Disaster Recovery 
* VDRM: Virtualization, Disaster Recovery, Management 
* VDRMS: Virtualization, Disaster Recovery, Management, Security 

Advantages of Virtualization and Disaster Recovery: 

* Cost savings: Virtualization and disaster recovery can reduce costs by consolidating hardware and software resources and providing more efficient use of resources. 
* Improved efficiency: Virtualization and disaster recovery can improve efficiency by allowing organizations to quickly provision and manage resources. 
* Increased security: Virtualization and disaster recovery can increase security by providing a secure environment for data storage and access. 
* Increased flexibility: Virtualization and disaster recovery can provide organizations with the flexibility to quickly scale up or down their resources according to their needs. 

Disadvantages of Virtualization and Disaster Recovery: 

* Complexity: Virtualization and disaster recovery can be complex to implement and manage. 
* Limited scalability: Virtualization and disaster recovery can be limited in their scalability, as organizations may not be able to quickly scale up their resources to meet their needs. 
* Increased risk: Virtualization and disaster recovery can increase risk due to the potential for data loss or corruption. 

Applications of Virtualization and Disaster Recovery: 

* Cloud computing: Virtualization and disaster recovery are essential components of cloud computing. 
* Business continuity: Virtualization and disaster recovery can help organizations ensure business continuity in the event of a disaster. 
* Data storage and backup: Virtualization and disaster recovery can be used to store and back up data in a secure environment. 
* Infrastructure management: Virtualization and disaster recovery can be used to manage and maintain an organization's infrastructure.
