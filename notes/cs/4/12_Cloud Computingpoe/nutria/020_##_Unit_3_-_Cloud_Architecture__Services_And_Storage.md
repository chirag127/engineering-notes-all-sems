

## Unit 3 - Cloud Architecture, Services And Storage

### Mnemonics and Learning Tricks

Here are some helpful mnemonics and learning tricks to help you remember the key concepts related to cloud architecture, services and storage:

* **S**torage: **S**tore your data in the cloud.
* **A**rchitecture: **A**rchitect your cloud environment for optimal performance.
* **C**loud: **C**hoose the right cloud provider for your needs.
* **S**ervices: **S**elect the right cloud services for your applications.

### Overview

Cloud architecture, services, and storage are the three main components of any cloud computing system. Cloud architecture refers to the overall design and structure of the system, including the hardware, software, and networks that make up the system. Cloud services are the applications and services that are available on the cloud, such as virtual machines, databases, and storage. Cloud storage is the storage of data on the cloud, such as files, images, and videos.

### Cloud Architecture

Cloud architecture is the design and structure of the cloud computing system. It is responsible for the hardware, software, and networks that make up the system. The architecture is designed to provide scalability, reliability, and high performance. It is also designed to be secure, with access control and data encryption.

### Cloud Services

Cloud services are the applications and services that are available on the cloud. These include virtual machines, databases, storage, and other services. These services can be used to host websites, run applications, store data, and more.

### Cloud Storage

Cloud storage is the storage of data on the cloud, such as files, images, and videos. It is a convenient and cost-effective way to store data, as it is not necessary to purchase and maintain physical storage devices. Cloud storage is also secure, with access control and data encryption.
