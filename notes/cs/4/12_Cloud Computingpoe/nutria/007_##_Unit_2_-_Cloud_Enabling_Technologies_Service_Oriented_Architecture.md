
## Unit 2 - Cloud Enabling Technologies Service Oriented Architecture

Service Oriented Architecture (SOA) is an architectural style that enables organizations to deliver services to customers more efficiently and effectively. SOA is based on the concept of services, which are self-contained, autonomous, and loosely coupled components that can be accessed and used by other components and services. SOA makes it possible to create applications that can be dynamically composed from a variety of services.

The key characteristics of SOA are: 

* **Autonomy**: Services are autonomous and are responsible for their own behavior.
* **Loose Coupling**: Services are loosely coupled, meaning that they do not have a direct dependency on each other.
* **Reusability**: Services are reusable, which means that they can be used in multiple contexts.
* **Composability**: Services can be composed to create new applications.

One of the main advantages of SOA is that it allows organizations to quickly develop, deploy, and manage applications. It also makes it possible to create applications that are more flexible and scalable. Additionally, SOA makes it easier to maintain applications, as changes can be made to individual services without affecting the entire application.

Mnemonic to remember SOA: 

* **A**utonomy 
* **L**oosely **C**oupled
