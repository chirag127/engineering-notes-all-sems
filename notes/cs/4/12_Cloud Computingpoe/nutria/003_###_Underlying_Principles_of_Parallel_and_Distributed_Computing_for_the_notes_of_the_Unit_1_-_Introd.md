
### Underlying Principles of Parallel and Distributed Computing for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction To Cloud Computing in the subject of Cloud Computing

- Parallel computing is the use of multiple processors to solve a single problem or to process multiple tasks simultaneously.
- Distributed computing is the process of breaking down a problem or task into smaller tasks and distributing them across multiple computers connected over a network.
- Parallel computing and distributed computing share many of the same principles, such as the use of multiple processors and the division of a problem into smaller tasks.
- The main difference between the two is that in parallel computing, the tasks are all executed on the same computer, while in distributed computing the tasks are executed on different computers.
- In order to effectively utilize parallel and distributed computing, it is important to understand the underlying principles, such as data partitioning, synchronization, communication, scheduling, and fault tolerance.
- Data partitioning is the process of dividing a problem into smaller tasks and assigning each task to a different processor.
- Synchronization is the process of ensuring that all tasks are completed in the same order.
- Communication is the process of exchanging data between processors.
- Scheduling is the process of determining which tasks should be executed on which processors.
- Fault tolerance is the ability of a system to continue to function in the event of a failure.

These principles are essential for understanding how parallel and distributed computing works. By understanding and utilizing these principles, it is possible to create efficient and powerful computing systems.
