### IaaS for the Notes of Unit 3 - Cloud Architecture, Services And Storage in the Subject of Cloud Computing

Infrastructure as a Service (IaaS) is a cloud computing model that provides users with virtualized computing resources over the internet. In this model, the cloud service provider manages the physical infrastructure, including servers, storage, and networking, while users have control over the operating systems, applications, and middleware.

#### Key Characteristics of IaaS

- Scalability: IaaS allows users to scale up or down their computing resources depending on their business needs. This ability to dynamically adjust resources makes IaaS a cost-effective solution for businesses.
- On-demand self-service: Users can provision and manage computing resources on their own without the intervention of the cloud service provider.
- Resource pooling: IaaS providers pool computing resources to serve multiple users, allowing for greater efficiency and cost savings.
- Pay-per-use: Users only pay for the resources they use, making IaaS a flexible and cost-effective solution.

#### Advantages of IaaS

- Scalability: IaaS allows businesses to quickly scale up or down their computing resources as needed, making it an ideal solution for businesses with fluctuating demand.
- Cost savings: IaaS eliminates the need for businesses to invest in and maintain their own physical infrastructure, resulting in cost savings.
- Geographic flexibility: IaaS providers offer a global network of data centers, allowing businesses to deploy applications and services in multiple regions to better serve their customers.
- Disaster recovery: IaaS providers offer robust disaster recovery solutions, ensuring that businesses can quickly recover from any unforeseen disruptions.

#### Disadvantages of IaaS

- Security: As with any cloud computing model, security is a concern. Businesses must ensure that their data is secure and that the IaaS provider has adequate security measures in place.
- Technical expertise: IaaS requires technical expertise to manage and configure computing resources, making it less accessible for businesses without dedicated IT staff.
- Dependence on the provider: Businesses using IaaS are dependent on the provider for the availability and performance of their computing resources.

#### Examples of IaaS Providers

- Amazon Web Services (AWS)
- Microsoft Azure
- Google Cloud Platform
- IBM Cloud

#### Learning Tricks and Mnemonics

- "IaaS, we provide the Infrastructure, you provide the Applications and Systems." - This helps to remember the key characteristic of IaaS, which is that the provider manages the physical infrastructure while the user has control over the operating systems and applications.
- "IaaS is like renting a house, you have control over the furnishings and decor, but the landlord is responsible for maintaining the structure." - This helps to remember that the provider is responsible for managing the physical infrastructure, while the user has control over the operating systems and applications.
