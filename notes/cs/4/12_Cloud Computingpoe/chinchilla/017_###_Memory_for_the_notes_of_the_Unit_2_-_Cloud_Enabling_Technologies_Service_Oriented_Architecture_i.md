### Memory for the notes of the Unit 2 - Cloud Enabling Technologies Service Oriented Architecture in the subject of Cloud Computing

Service Oriented Architecture (SOA) is an architectural style that enables different applications to communicate with each other by providing and consuming services. It is a technology that is widely used in the field of Cloud Computing. Here are some tips and tricks to help you remember the key concepts related to SOA in Cloud Computing:

1. Remember the key components of SOA:
   - Service provider: provides the service to other applications.
   - Service consumer: consumes the service provided by the service provider.
   - Service registry: stores the information about the available services.
   - Service broker: helps in the discovery and composition of services.

2. Mnemonic to remember the benefits of SOA:
   - "ABCDEF" - Agility, Business alignment, Cost savings, Discovery, Elimination of redundancy, and Flexibility.

3. Remember the different types of services in SOA:
   - Business service: provides a business function such as inventory management.
   - Infrastructure service: provides infrastructure support such as authentication and security.
   - Application service: provides application-specific functionality such as billing.

4. Remember the advantages of using SOA in Cloud Computing:
   - Reusability of services.
   - Flexibility and scalability.
   - Interoperability between different applications.
   - Reduced development time and cost.

5. Remember the disadvantages of using SOA in Cloud Computing:
   - Increased complexity due to the need for standards and protocols.
   - Dependency on service providers.
   - Security concerns related to the sharing of services.

In summary, SOA is a crucial technology that enables different applications to communicate with each other in Cloud Computing. Remembering the key components, types of services, benefits, advantages, and disadvantages of SOA will help you better understand this technology and its application in Cloud Computing.
