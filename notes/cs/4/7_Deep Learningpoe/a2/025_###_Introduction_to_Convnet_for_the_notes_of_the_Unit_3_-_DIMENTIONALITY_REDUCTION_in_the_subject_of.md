### Introduction to Convnet for the notes of the Unit 3 - DIMENTIONALITY REDUCTION in the subject of Deep Learning

Convnets or Convolutional Neural Networks are a type of feed-forward artificial neural network primarily used for analyzing visual imagery. They are inspired by biological processes in the visual cortex and are based on the mathematical operation of convolution.

Some key points about Convnets are:

- They use a variation of multilayer perceptrons designed to use minimal preprocessing.
- They have convolutional layers that apply convolutional filters/kernels to the input data to detect spatial patterns.
- They have pooling layers that downsample the data to reduce dimensionality and capture dominant features.
- They have fully connected layers similar to regular neural networks that classify the features.
- They are very effective for image classification tasks and have achieved state-of-the-art results in the field.

**Advantages**:

- Requires minimal preprocessing as they can handle raw pixel data.
- Invariant to translation as they capture spatial patterns and dominant features.
- Hierarchy of features are automatically learned from data.

**Disadvantages**:

- Require large amounts of data to learn features.
- Can be computationally expensive to train due to multiple layers and parameters.
- May not work well on small datasets or datasets with less variation.

**Example**: Identifying objects, scenes or actions in images.

**Application**: Image classification, object detection, semantic segmentation, etc.

- "Convolution preserves the spatial relationship" to remember they capture spatial patterns
- "Pooling makes fools of details, focuses on stronger features" to remember the role of pooling layers
- "Hierarchy of learned features" to remember features are automatically learned from raw data
