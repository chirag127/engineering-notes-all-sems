### Computational & Artificial Neuroscience for the notes of the Unit 4 - OPTIMIZATION AND GENERALIZATION in the subject of Deep Learning

- Computational neuroscience is the study of neural systems' biological processes & computations using mathematical models & simulations. It combines neuroscience, computer science, physiology, physics, mathematics, and psychology to understand the nature of cognition.
- Artificial Neural Networks are inspired by biological neural networks in the brain that are capable of machine learning & pattern recognition. They are made up of interconnected nodes that must be optimized & generalized to learn complex patterns in data.
- Optimization in ANNs involves finding optimal weights & biases to minimize the loss function & make accurate predictions. This is done using gradient descent & its variants like Adam or RMSprop.
- Generalization is the ability of a model to perform well on new unseen data. This is achieved by regularization techniques like weight decay, dropout, etc. that reduce overfitting & make the model more robust.
- Mnemonics:
- " descendent gradient makes optimal ascent" - Gradient descent optimizes weights
- "dropout kicks out overfit troops" - Dropout tackles overfitting

- Advantages: Mimics biological learning, can solve complex problems, adaptive
- Disadvantages: Black-box, time-consuming training, prone to overfitting
- Applications: Image recognition, NLP, forecasting, etc.
