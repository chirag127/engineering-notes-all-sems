### Deep Vs Shallow Networks

Deep Networks have multiple hidden layers of parameters between the input and output layers whereas Shallow networks have only 1-2 hidden layers.

* Deep Networks can learn complex patterns in data and are more powerful in modeling complex relationships whereas Shallow networks can only learn simple patterns and are limited in their modeling capacity.
* Deep Networks require large amounts of data to train the many parameters whereas Shallow networks can train with relatively smaller data as they have fewer parameters.
* Deep Networks take more time to train due to many layers and parameters whereas Shallow networks take less time to train due to fewer parameters and layers.
* Examples of Deep Networks are CNNs, RNNs, LSTMs etc. Examples of Shallow networks are linear & logistic regression, single layer perceptrons etc.

Below are some Mnemonics and tips to remember the difference:

* Think Deep as in Depth i.e many layers and Shallow as shallow depth i.e few layers.
* Deep dive into data and Shallow touch the data.
* Time taken analogy: Deep like diving deep into ocean takes time, Shallow like paddling in shore takes less time.
* Complexity analogy: Deep like complex machinery with many parts, Shallow like simple machinery with few parts.

Advantages of Deep Networks:

* Can learn complex patterns
* Achieve higher accuracy
* Used in complex tasks like Computer Vision and NLP

Disadvantages of Deep Networks:

* Require large data
* Take more time to train
* Can lead to overfitting

Advantages of Shallow Networks:

* Require less data
* Train faster
* Less prone to overfitting

Disadvantages of Shallow Networks:

* Limited modeling capacity
* Achieve lower accuracy for complex tasks
