### Design of Business Process Services for the notes of the Unit 4 - Service-Oriented Analysis and Design in the subject of Service Oriented Architecture

Design of Business Process Services for Unit 4:

1. Identify business processes: Determine the core processes that drive the business and support its goals.
2. Model business processes: Represent the processes using a standard modeling language such as BPMN or UML.
3. Define service interfaces: Specify the inputs, outputs, and operations of each process as a service.
4. Design service orchestration: Determine the order and flow of services to achieve the desired business outcome.
5. Consider security and governance: Ensure that the services are secure and adhere to regulatory requirements.
6. Test and validate: Verify that the services meet the business requirements and function as expected.
7. Deploy and monitor: Deploy the services to production and monitor their performance and usage.
