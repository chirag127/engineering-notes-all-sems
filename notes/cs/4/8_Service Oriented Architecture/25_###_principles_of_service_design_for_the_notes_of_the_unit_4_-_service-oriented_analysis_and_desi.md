### Principles of Service Design for the notes of the Unit 4 - Service-Oriented Analysis and Design in the subject of Service Oriented Architecture

Service Design Principles:
1. Align with Business Goals: Services should be designed to meet business goals and objectives.
2. Focus on User Needs: Services should be designed to meet the needs of the end-users.
3. Be Reusable: Services should be designed to be reusable across different business processes.
4. Be Loosely Coupled: Services should be designed to have minimal dependencies on other services, to ensure flexibility and scalability.
5. Ensure Quality of Service: Services should be designed to meet agreed upon quality of service levels, such as reliability, availability, and performance.
6. Embrace Standards: Services should be designed to use industry standards where appropriate to ensure interoperability and ease of integration.
7. Be Secure: Services should be designed to ensure the security of sensitive information and transactions.
8. Be Scalable: Services should be designed to handle increasing demand and be able to scale up or down as needed.
