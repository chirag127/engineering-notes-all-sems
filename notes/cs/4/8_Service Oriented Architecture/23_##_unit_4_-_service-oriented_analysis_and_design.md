## Unit 4 - Service-Oriented Analysis and Design

Unit 4 - Service-Oriented Analysis and Design focuses on the development of software systems using a service-oriented architecture (SOA). This unit covers the following key topics:

1. Overview of Service-Oriented Architecture (SOA)
2. Service-Oriented Analysis (SOA)
3. Service-Oriented Design (SOD)
4. Design patterns for service-oriented systems
5. Service composition and orchestration
6. Service contracts and governance
7. Testing and deployment of service-oriented systems.

SOA is a software design approach that enables the creation of loosely coupled, reusable services that can be combined to form complex systems. SOA analysis involves identifying the services required to support the business processes and defining the interfaces and behaviors of these services.

SOD focuses on defining the technical implementation of the services, including the use of design patterns, service composition, and orchestration. Service contracts and governance ensure that services are consistently implemented and managed throughout the lifecycle of the system.

Testing and deployment of service-oriented systems involves verifying that the services work as expected and ensuring that they can be deployed and integrated into the overall system.
