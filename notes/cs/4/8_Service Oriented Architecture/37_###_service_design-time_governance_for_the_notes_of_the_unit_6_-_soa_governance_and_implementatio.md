### Service Design-time Governance for the notes of the Unit 6 - SOA Governance and Implementation in the subject of Service Oriented Architecture
Service Design-Time Governance is a set of policies, standards and procedures that govern the design and development of services in a Service Oriented Architecture (SOA) environment. It ensures that services are designed and developed in a consistent, reliable, secure and scalable manner. The following are the key aspects of Service Design-Time Governance:

1. Service Design Standards: Define the standards for designing and developing services, such as naming conventions, data format, security, error handling, and performance.

2. Service Design Review: Ensure that services are designed and developed in compliance with the standards and best practices.

3. Service Design Approval: Approve the design of services before they are implemented.

4. Service Design Documentation: Document the design of services, including the service contract, data model, security, and performance requirements.

5. Service Design Testing: Test the design of services to ensure that they meet the requirements and standards.

6. Service Design Monitoring: Monitor the design of services to ensure that they are being developed in compliance with the standards and best practices.

7. Service Design Enforcement: Enforce the standards and best practices for designing and developing services.
