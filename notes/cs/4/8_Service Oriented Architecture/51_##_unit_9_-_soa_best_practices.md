## Unit 9 - SOA Best Practices

Unit 9 - SOA Best Practices covers the following topics:
1. Design principles for Service Oriented Architecture (SOA), including loose coupling, separation of concerns, and modularity.
2. Best practices for designing and implementing services, including versioning, security, and error handling.
3. Best practices for deploying and managing services, including monitoring, testing, and continuous integration/continuous deployment (CI/CD).
4. Best practices for integrating services, including data integration, event-driven architecture, and API management.
5. Best practices for governance, including service lifecycle management, service registry and repository, and service level agreements (SLAs).

The unit covers the principles and practices needed to design, implement, deploy, and manage services in a Service Oriented Architecture (SOA). It covers best practices for designing and implementing services, deploying and managing services, integrating services, and governing services. The goal of the unit is to provide students with the knowledge and skills needed to build scalable, reliable, and secure service-based applications.
