## Unit 10 - EA and SOA for Business and IT Alignment

Unit 10 - Enterprise Architecture (EA) and Service Oriented Architecture (SOA) for Business and IT Alignment covers the following topics:
1. Understanding the role of Enterprise Architecture (EA) in aligning business and IT strategies.
2. Introduction to Service Oriented Architecture (SOA) and its role in aligning business and IT strategies.
3. The benefits of aligning business and IT strategies, including increased efficiency, innovation, and competitiveness.
4. Best practices for aligning business and IT strategies, including the use of EA and SOA frameworks and methods.
5. The role of governance in ensuring the alignment of business and IT strategies, including the use of policies, standards, and procedures.

The unit covers the concepts, tools, and techniques needed to align business and IT strategies, and to ensure that technology investments support the overall goals and objectives of the organization. It provides an overview of the benefits of aligning business and IT strategies, and the best practices for doing so, including the use of EA and SOA frameworks and methods.
