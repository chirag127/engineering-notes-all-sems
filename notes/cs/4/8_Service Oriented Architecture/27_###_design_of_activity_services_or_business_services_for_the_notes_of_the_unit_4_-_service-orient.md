### Design of Activity Services (or Business Services) for the notes of the Unit 4 - Service-Oriented Analysis and Design in the subject of Service Oriented Architecture

Design of Activity Services (or Business Services) is a crucial aspect of Service-Oriented Architecture. It involves defining the activities that a service performs and the inputs and outputs of those activities. The following are the key steps in designing activity services:

1. Identify business activities: Determine the business activities that the service needs to perform.

2. Define inputs and outputs: Specify the inputs and outputs of each business activity.

3. Determine service boundaries: Decide on the boundaries of the service, ensuring that it is cohesive and loosely coupled.

4. Define service contract: Define the service contract, including the operations and data types that the service supports.

5. Design message exchange patterns: Determine the message exchange patterns between the service and its clients.

6. Consider security and reliability: Ensure that the service is secure and reliable, and that it meets the necessary non-functional requirements.

7. Validate design: Validate the design with stakeholders, including business analysts, developers, and architects.
