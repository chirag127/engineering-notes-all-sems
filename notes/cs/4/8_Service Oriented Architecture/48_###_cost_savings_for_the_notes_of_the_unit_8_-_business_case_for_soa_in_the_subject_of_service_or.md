### Cost Savings for the notes of the Unit 8 - Business Case for SOA in the subject of Service Oriented Architecture
Cost savings is a key benefit of Service Oriented Architecture (SOA). By breaking down applications into smaller, reusable services, organizations can reduce the cost of development and maintenance.

1. Reuse of services: Reusing existing services in new projects can reduce development time and costs.

2. Improved efficiency: SOA enables better collaboration between IT and business units, leading to faster and more efficient processes.

3. Better alignment with business goals: SOA helps align IT with business goals, enabling IT to better support the business and reduce costs.

4. Reduced complexity: SOA reduces the complexity of systems, making them easier to maintain and reducing maintenance costs.

5. Improved scalability: SOA enables organizations to scale their systems more easily, reducing the cost of adding new capabilities.

6. Increased agility: SOA enables organizations to respond more quickly to changing business needs, reducing the cost of change.
