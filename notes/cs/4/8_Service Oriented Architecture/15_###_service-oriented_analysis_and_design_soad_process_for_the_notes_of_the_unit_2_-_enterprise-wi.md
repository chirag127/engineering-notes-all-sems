### Service-oriented Analysis and Design (SOAD) Process for the notes of the Unit 2 - Enterprise-Wide SOA in the subject of Service Oriented Architecture

Service-oriented Analysis and Design (SOAD) is a process for designing and building software systems using service-oriented architecture (SOA). The process involves the following steps:

1. Identify business requirements: Determine what the system should do to meet the needs of the business.

2. Define services: Break down the business requirements into a set of independent services that can be developed, tested, and deployed separately.

3. Design services: Create a detailed design for each service, including its interface, data model, and behavior.

4. Implement services: Build the services according to the design.

5. Test services: Verify that each service meets its requirements and works as intended.

6. Deploy services: Deploy the services to a production environment.

7. Monitor and maintain services: Monitor the performance of the services and make any necessary changes to keep them running smoothly.

8. Continuously improve services: Continuously evaluate and improve the services to meet changing business requirements and improve their performance.
