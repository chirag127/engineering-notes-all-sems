
## Unit 4 - Service-Oriented Analysis and Design

* Service-oriented analysis and design (SOAD) is an approach to software development that focuses on creating services that are loosely coupled, reusable, and platform independent.
* SOAD is based on the idea of creating services that can be used by different applications, and that can be changed or updated without affecting the applications that use them.
* SOAD involves the use of service-oriented architectures (SOAs) which are collections of services that can be used by applications.
* SOAD also involves the use of service-oriented design patterns, which are reusable solutions to common design problems.
* SOAD is often used in conjunction with other software development approaches such as object-oriented analysis and design (OOAD) and component-based development (CBD).
* SOAD is an iterative process, meaning that it involves the continuous refinement and improvement of services.
