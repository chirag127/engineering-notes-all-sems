
### Strategic Architecture Governance

* Strategic architecture governance is a process of ensuring that the architecture of a system or organization is aligned with the organization's strategic objectives.
* It is a key element of service-oriented architecture (SOA) governance, which is a set of processes and procedures that ensure the effective implementation, maintenance, and use of SOA.
* Strategic architecture governance involves the definition of architecture principles, standards, and guidelines that ensure that the architecture of the system or organization is in line with the organization's strategic objectives.
* It also involves the monitoring and enforcement of these principles, standards, and guidelines.
* It is important to note that strategic architecture governance is not the same as technical architecture governance. Technical architecture governance focuses on the technical aspects of the architecture, such as the design, implementation, and maintenance of the architecture.
* Strategic architecture governance, on the other hand, focuses on the strategic aspects of the architecture, such as the alignment of the architecture with the organization's strategic objectives.
* Strategic architecture governance is closely related to enterprise architecture governance, which is the process of ensuring that the architecture of the enterprise is aligned with the organization's strategic objectives.
