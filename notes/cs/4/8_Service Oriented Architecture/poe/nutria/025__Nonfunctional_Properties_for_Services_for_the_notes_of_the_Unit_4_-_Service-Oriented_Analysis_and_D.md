

### Nonfunctional Properties for Services

1. Scalability: The ability of a service to be able to handle an increased workload without significant changes to its architecture or code.
2. Availability: The ability of a service to remain available and accessible to its users.
3. Reliability: The ability of a service to consistently perform its intended function.
4. Security: The ability of a service to protect its data and resources from unauthorized access.
5. Performance: The ability of a service to respond to requests in a timely manner.
6. Quality of Service: The ability of a service to provide a consistent level of service across multiple users.
7. Maintainability: The ability of a service to be easily maintained and updated.
8. Testability: The ability of a service to be tested for correctness and performance.
