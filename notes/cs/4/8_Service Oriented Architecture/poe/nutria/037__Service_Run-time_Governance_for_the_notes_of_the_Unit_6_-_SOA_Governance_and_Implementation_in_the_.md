
### Service Run-time Governance

1. Service Oriented Architecture (SOA) is an architectural style that enables the creation of flexible, loosely coupled, and interoperable services.
2. SOA Governance is the process of ensuring that all services within an SOA are designed, deployed, and managed in a consistent and compliant manner.
3. Service Run-time Governance is the process of ensuring that all services within an SOA are running in a consistent and compliant manner.
4. Service Run-time Governance involves the monitoring and management of service performance, availability, and security.
5. Service Run-time Governance includes the processes for detecting and resolving service outages and for ensuring that service performance meets the expected service level agreements.
6. Service Run-time Governance also includes the processes for ensuring that the service is compliant with the relevant service-level agreements and policies.
7. Service Run-time Governance is essential for ensuring that services are running in a secure, reliable, and compliant manner.
