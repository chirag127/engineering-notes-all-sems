
### Stakeholder Objectives for the Unit 8 - Business Case for SOA

1. Identify the stakeholders involved in the development of a Service Oriented Architecture (SOA) and their objectives.
2. Analyze the business case for SOA and the value it provides to the stakeholders.
3. Understand the challenges and risks associated with SOA and how to mitigate them.
4. Analyze the cost and benefit of SOA and the impact it has on the stakeholders.
5. Understand the importance of aligning the stakeholders’ objectives with the SOA implementation.
6. Learn how to measure the success of the SOA implementation.
