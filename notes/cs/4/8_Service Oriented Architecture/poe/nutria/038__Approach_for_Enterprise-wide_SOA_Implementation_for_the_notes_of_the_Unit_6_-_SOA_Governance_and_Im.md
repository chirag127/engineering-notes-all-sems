
### Approach for Enterprise-wide SOA Implementation

1. Establish a governance framework: This framework should address the roles and responsibilities of stakeholders, provide guidance on the design and development of services, and ensure compliance with standards and regulations.

2. Define the service architecture: This should include an inventory of existing services, the development of new services, and the integration of existing and new services into an overall service-oriented architecture.

3. Design the service framework: This should include the definition of service contracts, the development of service-level agreements, and the development of service-level objectives.

4. Implement the service framework: This should include the deployment of services, the testing of services, and the monitoring of service performance.

5. Monitor and manage the service framework: This should include the monitoring of service performance, the identification of service issues, and the resolution of service issues.
