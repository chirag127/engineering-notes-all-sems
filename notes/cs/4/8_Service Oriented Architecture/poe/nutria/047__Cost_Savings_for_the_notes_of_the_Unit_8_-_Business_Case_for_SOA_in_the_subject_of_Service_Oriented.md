
### Cost Savings for the notes of the Unit 8 - Business Case for SOA in the subject of Service Oriented Architecture

1. Service Oriented Architecture (SOA) is an approach to software development that enables organizations to create applications and services more quickly and efficiently. 
2. SOA provides a cost-effective way to develop applications and services, as it reduces the need for costly custom development. 
3. SOA also reduces the cost of maintenance and upgrades, as changes can be made in a single place, rather than having to make changes across multiple applications. 
4. SOA also enables organizations to take advantage of existing applications and services, allowing them to reduce costs by reusing existing code and components. 
5. Finally, SOA provides the ability to scale applications and services quickly, reducing the need for costly hardware upgrades.
