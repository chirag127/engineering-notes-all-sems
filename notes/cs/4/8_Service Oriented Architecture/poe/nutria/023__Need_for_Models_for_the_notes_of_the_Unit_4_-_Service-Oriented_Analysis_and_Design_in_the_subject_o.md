
### Need for Models for the notes of the Unit 4 - Service-Oriented Analysis and Design in the subject of Service Oriented Architecture

* Service-Oriented Analysis and Design (SOAD) is a methodology for designing and developing distributed applications.
* SOAD is based on the concept of service-orientation, which is an approach for designing and developing distributed applications that are loosely coupled, highly cohesive, and maintainable.
* SOAD models are used to represent the functionality of a distributed application.
* The models are used to identify the services that are required to support the application's functionality, and to define the interactions between the services.
* SOAD models can be used to identify the required services and their interactions, as well as to identify any potential conflicts or redundancies between the services.
* SOAD models can also be used to identify potential areas of improvement in the design of the application.
* SOAD models should be designed to be flexible and extensible, so that they can be easily adapted to changes in the application's requirements.
