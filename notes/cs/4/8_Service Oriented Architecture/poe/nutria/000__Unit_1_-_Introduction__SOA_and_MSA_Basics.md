
## Unit 1 - Introduction: SOA and MSA Basics

* Service-Oriented Architecture (SOA) is an architectural style that defines the use of services to support the development of applications and systems. 
* SOA is based on the concept of service-orientation, which is a design principle that enables greater flexibility and scalability in the development of applications and systems. 
* SOA enables the development of applications and systems that are loosely coupled, meaning that they are not tightly dependent on one another and can be changed or replaced without affecting the entire system. 
* Microservices Architecture (MSA) is an architectural style that is based on the concept of service-orientation and provides a way to structure applications and systems into small, independent services. 
* MSA encourages the development of applications and systems that are highly scalable, resilient, and modular. 
* MSA enables developers to quickly develop and deploy applications and systems that are composed of small, independent services.
