
## Unit 7 - Big Data and SOA

1. Big Data refers to datasets that are too large and complex to be processed using traditional computing techniques. It is often characterized by its volume, velocity, variety and veracity.

2. Service-oriented architecture (SOA) is an architectural style that enables the creation of loosely coupled, highly interoperable services. It is based on the principles of service-orientation, which emphasize the use of services for the construction of applications.

3. SOA provides a way to manage and integrate large, complex datasets. It enables organizations to use a common set of services and components to access, process and analyze data from multiple sources.

4. Big Data and SOA can be used together to create powerful data-driven applications. By combining the scalability and flexibility of SOA with the power of Big Data, organizations can gain insights into their data and make better decisions.
