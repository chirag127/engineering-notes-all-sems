
### EA and SOA for Business and It Alignment

* Enterprise Architecture (EA) is a structured approach to understanding, designing, planning, and managing an organization's IT infrastructure. 
* EA involves the alignment of IT infrastructure with the organization's business objectives and strategy, and the integration of systems and applications across the organization.
* Service Oriented Architecture (SOA) is an architectural style that enables organizations to deliver services to their customers in a more efficient and effective manner.
* SOA enables organizations to integrate their applications and systems in a more efficient and effective manner, by providing a framework for the design, implementation, and deployment of services.
* EA and SOA are closely related, as both involve the alignment of IT infrastructure with the organization's business objectives and strategy.
* EA and SOA can be used together to create a comprehensive IT infrastructure that is aligned with the organization's business objectives and strategy.
* By leveraging the benefits of EA and SOA, organizations can create an IT infrastructure that is more efficient, cost-effective, and reliable.
