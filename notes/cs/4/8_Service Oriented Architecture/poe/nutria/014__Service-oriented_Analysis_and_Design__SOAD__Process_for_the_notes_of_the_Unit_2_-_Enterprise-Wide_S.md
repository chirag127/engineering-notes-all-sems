
### Service-oriented Analysis and Design (SOAD) Process

1. The SOAD process is a methodology used for designing and developing services for enterprise-wide Service Oriented Architecture (SOA).

2. It involves a series of steps that must be followed in order to ensure that the services are designed and implemented correctly.

3. The process begins with an analysis of the current system and its requirements. This analysis helps to identify the services that need to be developed and the features that each service should have.

4. The next step is to design the services. This includes designing the service interface and the data model.

5. After the design is complete, the services are implemented and deployed. This includes setting up the necessary infrastructure and deploying the services to the appropriate environment.

6. Finally, the services are tested and monitored to ensure that they are functioning correctly and meeting the requirements.
