### Considerations for Service-oriented Applications for the notes of the Unit 3 - Service-Oriented Applications in the subject of Service Oriented Architecture
Considerations for Service-Oriented Applications:
1. Scalability: ability to handle increased demand.
2. Availability: ensuring the service is accessible when needed.
3. Interoperability: compatibility with different systems and technologies.
4. Security: protection of sensitive data and prevention of unauthorized access.
5. Reliability: ability to perform as expected and recover from failures.
6. Maintainability: ease of updating and fixing issues.
7. Reusability: ability to use services in multiple applications.
8. Flexibility: adaptability to changing requirements and technologies.
9. Performance: efficient processing of requests and quick response times.
10. Monitoring and management: ability to monitor and manage the service effectively.
