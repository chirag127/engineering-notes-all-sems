### Big Data and its characteristics for the notes of the Unit 7 - Big Data and SOA in the subject of Service Oriented Architecture

Big Data refers to large and complex datasets that cannot be processed by traditional data processing systems. Characteristics of Big Data include:

1. Volume: The large amount of data generated by various sources.

2. Variety: The different types of data, such as structured, unstructured, and semi-structured.

3. Velocity: The speed at which data is generated and processed.

4. Veracity: The quality and accuracy of data.

5. Value: The potential insights and business value that can be derived from the data.

Big Data requires new technologies and approaches for processing, analyzing, and storing data, such as Hadoop, NoSQL databases, and cloud computing. It has significant implications for businesses, governments, and society, providing opportunities for innovation, improved decision-making, and increased efficiency.
