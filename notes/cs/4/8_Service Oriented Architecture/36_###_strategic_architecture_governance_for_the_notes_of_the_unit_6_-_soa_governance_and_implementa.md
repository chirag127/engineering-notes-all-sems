### Strategic Architecture Governance for the notes of the Unit 6 - SOA Governance and Implementation in the subject of Service Oriented Architecture

Strategic Architecture Governance is a framework that provides guidance, standards, and processes for the management of service-oriented architecture (SOA) projects. It ensures that the architecture aligns with the organization's overall business strategy, meets quality standards, and is effectively implemented. Key components of SOA governance include:

1. Architecture governance board: responsible for overseeing the development and implementation of the SOA.

2. Architecture repository: central repository for storing SOA artifacts and information.

3. Architecture development method: a systematic approach to developing the SOA.

4. Architecture review and approval process: a process for reviewing and approving SOA artifacts.

5. Architecture standards and guidelines: standards and guidelines for the development and implementation of the SOA.

6. Architecture performance management: a process for monitoring and managing the performance of the SOA.

7. Architecture change management: a process for managing changes to the SOA.

Implementing SOA governance requires collaboration between the architecture governance board, business stakeholders, and technical teams. It is important to ensure that the governance framework is flexible enough to accommodate changes in the organization's business strategy and technology landscape.
