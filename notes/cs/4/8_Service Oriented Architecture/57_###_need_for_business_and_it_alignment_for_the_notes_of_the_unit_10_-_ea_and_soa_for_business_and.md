### Need for Business and It Alignment for the notes of the Unit 10 - EA and SOA for Business and IT Alignment in the subject of Service Oriented Architecture

The need for Business and IT Alignment in Service Oriented Architecture (SOA) arises from the fact that IT and business goals and objectives are often misaligned, leading to conflicting priorities and ineffective use of technology. Business and IT alignment is the process of aligning IT and business goals and objectives, so that IT can support and enhance business goals.

SOA provides a framework for aligning IT and business goals by enabling organizations to develop flexible and scalable applications that can respond quickly to changing business requirements. By aligning IT and business goals, organizations can ensure that IT investments are aligned with business goals, leading to improved efficiency, productivity, and competitiveness.

In order to achieve business and IT alignment, organizations need to adopt a strategic approach to SOA that involves close collaboration between business and IT leaders. This includes involving business leaders in the development of SOA strategies, as well as regularly reviewing and adjusting these strategies to ensure that they continue to support business goals.
