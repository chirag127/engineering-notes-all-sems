### Conceptual Model of SOA for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction: SOA and MSA Basics in the subject of Service Oriented Architecture

A Conceptual Model of SOA is a representation of the fundamental elements and relationships of a Service Oriented Architecture. It includes the following concepts:

1. Services: Self-contained, modular units of functionality that can be composed to form larger applications.

2. Contracts: Define the interface and behavior of a service and the expectations for communication between a service provider and consumer.

3. Service Registry: A centralized repository for discovering, publishing, and managing the availability of services.

4. Service Oriented Messaging: The communication mechanism between services, including message formats and protocols.

5. Service Composition: The process of combining multiple services to form a new, composite service.

6. Service Choreography: The coordination and orchestration of services to achieve a specific business process.

7. Service Governance: The policies, processes, and tools used to manage the lifecycle of services, including security, reliability, and performance.

8. Service Consumers: Entities that use services to achieve their goals.

9. Service Providers: Entities that offer services to others.
