### Service Run-time Governance for the notes of the Unit 6 - SOA Governance and Implementation in the subject of Service Oriented Architecture

Service Run-time Governance refers to the management and monitoring of services during their execution in a SOA environment. It includes enforcing policies, ensuring compliance and monitoring performance. Key components of Service Run-time Governance include:

1. Policy Enforcement: Defining and enforcing policies for security, reliability, and performance.

2. Compliance Management: Monitoring services for compliance with policies and regulatory requirements.

3. Performance Management: Monitoring service performance and addressing issues to ensure optimal performance.

4. Service Registry and Repository: Maintaining a centralized repository of service information, including policies, contracts, and metadata.

5. Service Monitoring and Management: Monitoring service behavior and managing service instances to ensure they are meeting expectations.

Effective Service Run-time Governance is critical to ensuring the reliability and performance of services in a SOA environment. It helps to ensure that services are secure, compliant and performing optimally, which in turn supports the overall success of the SOA implementation.
