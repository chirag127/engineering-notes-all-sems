### Patterns for SOA for the notes of the Unit 3 - Service-Oriented Applications in the subject of Service Oriented Architecture

The following are common patterns for Service Oriented Architecture (SOA):
1. Service Contract: Defines the interface and parameters for a service.
2. Service Provider: Implements the service contract.
3. Service Consumer: Uses the service by calling its interface.
4. Service Registry: A centralized repository for service discovery.
5. Service Composition: Combines multiple services to form a higher-level service.
6. Service Choreography: Coordination of services to achieve a specific goal.
7. Service Orchestration: Centralized management of service execution.
8. Service Data: Management of data used by services.
9. Service Security: Ensuring the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of services.
10. Service Monitoring: Monitoring the performance and availability of services.
