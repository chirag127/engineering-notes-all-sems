### Design of Activity Services (or Business Services) for the notes of the Unit 4 - Service-Oriented Analysis and Design in the subject of Service Oriented Architecture

- Activity services (or business services) are services that encapsulate a business process or a part of it, and provide a high-level functionality to the service consumers.
- Activity services are usually composed of other services, such as entity services, utility services, or task services, that perform specific operations on data or resources.
- Activity services are designed to be reusable, loosely coupled, and aligned with the business goals and requirements.
- Activity services are often modeled using business process modeling notation (BPMN), which is a graphical representation of the flow of activities, events, gateways, and roles involved in a business process.
- Activity services can be implemented using various technologies, such as web services, RESTful services, or microservices, depending on the architectural style and the non-functional requirements of the service-oriented solution.
- Activity services can be exposed to the service consumers using service contracts, which define the interface, the quality of service, and the policies of the service.
- Activity services can be registered and discovered using service repositories, which store the metadata and the descriptions of the services.
- Activity services can be monitored and managed using service governance, which ensures the compliance, security, and performance of the services.
