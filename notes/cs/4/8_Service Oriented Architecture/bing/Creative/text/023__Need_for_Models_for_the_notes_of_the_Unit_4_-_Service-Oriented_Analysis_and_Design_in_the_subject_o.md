### Need for Models

- Models are representations of reality that help us to understand, communicate, and design complex systems.
- Models can be used for different purposes, such as analysis, design, documentation, simulation, testing, and implementation.
- Models can be expressed in different forms, such as diagrams, tables, equations, code, or natural language.
- Models can have different levels of abstraction, such as conceptual, logical, or physical.
- Models can have different perspectives, such as structural, behavioral, or functional.
- Models can have different scopes, such as enterprise, domain, or service.
- Models can have different standards, such as UML, BPMN, or SOAML.

- In service-oriented analysis and design (SOAD), models are essential tools to capture the requirements, architecture, and design of service-oriented systems.
- Models help to identify, specify, and compose services that are reusable, interoperable, and aligned with the business goals and processes.
- Models help to ensure the quality, consistency, and maintainability of service-oriented systems.
- Models help to facilitate the communication and collaboration among the stakeholders, such as business analysts, service providers, service consumers, and service brokers.
- Models help to support the service lifecycle, such as discovery, analysis, design, development, deployment, and evolution.
