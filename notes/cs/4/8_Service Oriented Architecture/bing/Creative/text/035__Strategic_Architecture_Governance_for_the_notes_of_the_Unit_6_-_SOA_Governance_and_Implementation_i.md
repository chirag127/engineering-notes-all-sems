### Strategic Architecture Governance for SOA

- Strategic Architecture Governance is the process of defining, aligning, and enforcing the SOA strategy, goals, and objectives at the enterprise level .
- SOA strategy provides the direction, vision, and roadmap for the SOA program, based on the business and IT drivers, and the current and desired state of SOA maturity .
- SOA goals are the specific and measurable outcomes that the SOA program aims to achieve, such as increased agility, reuse, interoperability, and quality .
- SOA objectives are the concrete and actionable steps that the SOA program takes to achieve the SOA goals, such as identifying and prioritizing SOA opportunities, developing and publishing SOA standards and best practices, and establishing and monitoring SOA metrics and KPIs .
- Strategic Architecture Governance involves the following activities:
  - Establishing the SOA governance guiding principles, which are the high-level statements that provide the rationale and direction for the SOA governance program.
  - Defining the SOA governance roles and responsibilities, which are the stakeholders and their accountabilities and authorities in the SOA governance program.
  - Developing the SOA governance processes, which are the workflows and procedures that govern the SOA lifecycle, from service identification to service retirement.
  - Creating the SOA governance artifacts, which are the documents and models that capture the SOA governance decisions and outputs, such as the SOA reference architecture, the SOA policy, the SOA contract, and the SOA registry/repository.
  - Implementing the SOA governance technology, which are the tools and platforms that support the SOA governance processes and artifacts, such as the SOA governance dashboard, the SOA governance automation, and the SOA governance testing.
  - Aligning the SOA governance with the business, IT, and enterprise architecture governance, which are the overarching governance frameworks that ensure the SOA governance is consistent and compliant with the organization's strategy, standards, and regulations .
- Strategic Architecture Governance for SOA is essential for ensuring the success and sustainability of the SOA program, as it provides the clarity, consistency, and control over the SOA initiatives and outcomes .
