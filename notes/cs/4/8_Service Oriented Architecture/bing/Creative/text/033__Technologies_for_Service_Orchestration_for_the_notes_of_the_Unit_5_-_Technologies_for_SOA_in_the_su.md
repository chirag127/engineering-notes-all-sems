### Technologies for Service Orchestration

- Service orchestration is the process of designing, creating, delivering, and monitoring service offerings in an automated way.
- Service orchestration is a key enabler for service providers that want to have a platform oriented operating model and deliver value beyond connectivity.
- Service orchestration can be achieved through a variety of IT automation tools, including service orchestration and automation platforms (SOAPs), workload automation solutions (WLA), and enterprise job scheduling platforms.
- Service orchestration platforms include several technologies that have overlapping capabilities, such as extensibility, low-code automation, and centralized monitoring.
- Service orchestration helps service providers to cope with the diverse and distributed requirements of 5G use cases, such as cloud environments, workload placement, and SLA management.
- Service orchestration also helps service providers to unify their technologies and overcome the challenges of legacy networks, such as silos, complexity, and inefficiency.
- Service orchestration is essential for service providers that want to leverage the opportunities of 5G and cloud computing, and to be more agile, innovative, and attractive in the ecosystem.
