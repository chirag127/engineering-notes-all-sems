### Service Design-time Governance for the notes of the Unit 6 - SOA Governance and Implementation in the subject of Service Oriented Architecture

- Service design-time governance refers to the technology and processes that support the design and implementation of service policies.
- Service policies are the rules and constraints that define how a service should behave, such as security, quality of service, availability, and so on.
- Service design-time governance aims to ensure that services are designed and developed in a consistent, reusable, and compliant manner, following the best practices and standards of service-oriented architecture (SOA).
- Service design-time governance covers the following aspects of the service lifecycle:
  - Identifying business processes that can benefit from service orientation and provide value to the organization.
  - Modeling and designing services that are aligned with the business needs and goals, and that adhere to the service contract and policy specifications.
  - Developing and testing services that implement the service logic and functionality, and that comply with the service policies.
  - Publishing and registering services in a service repository or registry, where they can be discovered and reused by other service consumers or composers.
  - Managing and monitoring the service development projects and artifacts, and ensuring that they follow the governance processes and standards.
- Service design-time governance requires the collaboration of various roles and stakeholders, such as business analysts, service architects, service developers, service testers, service managers, and service governance boards.
- Service design-time governance also requires the use of various tools and technologies, such as service modeling tools, service development environments, service testing tools, service repositories or registries, and service governance platforms.
- Service design-time governance is important for achieving the benefits of SOA, such as business agility, reuse, interoperability, and compliance.
- Service design-time governance is complementary to service run-time governance, which refers to the technology and processes that support the enforcement and monitoring of service policies during service execution .
