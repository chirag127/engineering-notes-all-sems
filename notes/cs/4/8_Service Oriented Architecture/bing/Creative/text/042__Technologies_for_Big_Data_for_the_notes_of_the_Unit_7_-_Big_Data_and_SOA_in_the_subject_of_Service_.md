### Technologies for Big Data

Big data is a term that describes large, hard-to-manage volumes of data – both structured and unstructured – that inundate businesses on a day-to-day basis. Big data can be analyzed for insights that improve decisions and give confidence for making strategic business moves.

Big data technologies can be categorized into four main types: data storage, data mining, data analytics, and data visualization. Each of these is associated with certain tools, and you’ll want to choose the right tool for your business needs depending on the type of big data technology required.

- Data storage: Big data technology that deals with data storage has the capability to fetch, store, and manage big data. Data storage technologies include data lakes, Hadoop, and cloud storage services. Data storage technologies enable organizations to collect data from a variety of sources, such as transactions, smart devices, industrial equipment, social media, and more.
- Data mining: Data mining extracts the useful patterns and trends from the raw data. Data mining technologies include SQL, NoSQL, and graph databases, as well as data integration and cleansing tools. Data mining technologies enable organizations to link, match, cleanse, and transform data across systems, and to discover hidden relationships, hierarchies, and multiple data linkages .
- Data analytics: In big data analytics, technologies are used to clean and transform data into information that can be used to drive business decisions. Data analytics technologies include data warehousing, data marts, online analytical processing (OLAP), and business intelligence (BI) tools. Data analytics technologies enable organizations to perform various types of analysis, such as descriptive, diagnostic, predictive, and prescriptive, and to apply advanced techniques, such as machine learning, deep learning, computer vision, and IoT .
- Data visualization: Data visualization is the process of presenting data in graphical or pictorial forms, such as charts, graphs, maps, and dashboards. Data visualization technologies include data visualization tools, such as Tableau, Power BI, and QlikView, and data storytelling tools, such as SAS Visual Analytics and Domo. Data visualization technologies enable organizations to communicate data insights effectively, and to make data-driven stories more engaging and persuasive.

Big data technologies are constantly evolving and improving, and new tools and techniques are emerging every year. Organizations that want to leverage big data for their competitive advantage need to stay updated on the latest trends and developments in the field, and to choose the right technologies that suit their business goals and challenges.
