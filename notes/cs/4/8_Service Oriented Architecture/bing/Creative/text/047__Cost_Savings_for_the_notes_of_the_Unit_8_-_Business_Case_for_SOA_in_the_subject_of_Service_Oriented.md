### Cost Savings for the notes of the Unit 8 - Business Case for SOA in the subject of Service Oriented Architecture

- One of the main benefits of SOA is its potential to reduce costs by eliminating redundant and inefficient systems and processes across the organization.
- SOA enables cost savings by:
  - Consolidating silos of application functionality and data that may have been duplicated or inconsistent across different departments or business units.
  - Reducing the number of software licenses and servers required to support the business operations, which also lowers the maintenance and operational costs.
  - Increasing the reuse and interoperability of existing services and components, which reduces the development time and effort for new applications or enhancements.
  - Improving the flexibility and adaptability of the IT architecture, which allows the organization to respond faster and more effectively to changing business needs and opportunities.
  - Providing a platform for innovation and differentiation, which can increase the revenue and customer satisfaction of the organization.
- According to a report by McKinsey, successful IT integrations can deliver 10 to 15 percent cost savings, and SOA can support these integrations by providing a flexible and standardized architecture.
- The Society of Actuaries (SOA) has developed a health care cost model that can help users estimate future health care insurance costs, which can also be a source of cost savings for the organization.
- The SOA also offers dues assistance to members residing in countries that have a low per capita income, which can help them afford the membership fees and access the benefits of the SOA.
