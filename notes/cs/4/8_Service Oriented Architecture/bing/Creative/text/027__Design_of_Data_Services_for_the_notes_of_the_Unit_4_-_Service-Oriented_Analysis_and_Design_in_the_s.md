### Design of Data Services

- Data services are reusable components of functionality that provide access to data sources and perform data operations, such as data integration, data quality, data profiling, data transformation, data enrichment, data masking, data governance, etc.
- Data services can be exposed as service interfaces that can be invoked by other applications or services via standard protocols, such as SOAP, REST, or JMS.
- Data services can support service-oriented architecture (SOA) by enabling data integration and data management across heterogeneous and distributed data sources, and by ensuring data quality and consistency throughout the data lifecycle.
- Data services can also support business intelligence and analytics by providing trusted and timely data to support decision making and reporting.
- Data services can be designed using a data-centric SOA approach, which involves the following steps:
  - Identify the data sources and data entities that are relevant to the business domain and the business processes.
  - Define the data contracts and data models that specify the structure, semantics, and quality of the data entities and their relationships.
  - Design the data services that implement the data contracts and data models, and provide the data operations and data logic that are required by the business processes and the data consumers.
  - Implement the data services using the appropriate technologies and tools, such as data integration platforms, data quality tools, data governance frameworks, etc.
  - Publish the data services in a service registry or a service catalog, and document the data contracts and data models using standard metadata formats, such as XML Schema, WSDL, or JSON Schema.
  - Govern the data services throughout their lifecycle, and monitor and measure their performance, availability, usage, and quality.
  - Discover and reuse the data services for new or existing applications or services, and compose them into higher-level data processes or data flows.
