### Dimensions of SOA

- SOA stands for Service-Oriented Architecture, which is a computer engineering approach that offers an architectural advancement towards enterprise systems.
- SOA describes a standard method for requesting services from distributed components and managing the results or outcomes.
- SOA provides a translation and management layer within the cloud architecture that removes the barrier for cloud clients obtaining desired services.
- SOA provides access to reusable Web services over a TCP/IP network, which makes this an important topic to cloud computing going forward.

- SOA has many benefits, such as:

  - Language Neutral Integration: SOA allows systems to offer and invoke services through a common mechanism, regardless of the developing language used.
  - Component Reuse: SOA enables the reuse of application components that are offered as services by the rest of the organization.
  - Organizational Agility: SOA defines building blocks of capabilities provided by software and offers some service(s) that meet some organizational requirement; which can be recombined and integrated rapidly.
  - Leveraging Existing System: SOA allows the identification of elements or functions of existing applications and makes them available to the organization or enterprise.

- SOA also has some risks, such as:

  - Dependence on the network: SOA relies on the availability and performance of the network to deliver the services.
  - Provider cost: SOA may incur additional costs from the service providers, such as licensing fees, subscription fees, or usage fees.
  - Enterprise standards: SOA may require the adoption of enterprise-wide standards and policies for service development, deployment, and governance.
  - Agility: SOA may introduce complexity and overhead in the service design and management, which may affect the agility and responsiveness of the system .

- SOA architecture is viewed as five horizontal layers. These are:

  - Consumer Interface Layer: These are GUI-based apps for end users accessing the applications.
  - Business Process Layer: These are business-use cases in terms of application.
  - Services Layer: These are whole-enterprise, in service inventory.
  - Service Component Layer: These are used to build the services, such as functional and technical libraries.
  - Operational Systems Layer: It contains the data model.

- SOA governance is the process of establishing and enforcing the principles, policies, standards, and best practices for service development, deployment, and management. SOA governance ensures the alignment of the services with the business goals and objectives, and the quality and performance of the services.
