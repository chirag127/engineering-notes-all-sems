### Service Run-time Governance

- Service run-time governance is the process of monitoring and controlling the behavior and performance of services and service compositions during their execution.
- Service run-time governance involves the following aspects:
  - Service availability: ensuring that services are accessible and responsive to requests from service consumers.
  - Service reliability: ensuring that services deliver consistent and correct results to service consumers.
  - Service security: ensuring that services are protected from unauthorized access and malicious attacks, and that service consumers are authenticated and authorized to use services.
  - Service scalability: ensuring that services can handle increasing or varying workloads without compromising quality of service.
  - Service compliance: ensuring that services adhere to predefined policies, standards, and regulations, and that service consumers respect the service contracts and service level agreements.
  - Service auditing: collecting and analyzing data about the usage and performance of services and service compositions, and generating reports and alerts for service providers and service consumers.
- Service run-time governance requires the use of various tools and mechanisms, such as:
  - Service registry and repository: a centralized database that stores information about the available services, their metadata, policies, contracts, and dependencies.
  - Service broker: a component that mediates the communication between service consumers and service providers, and enforces the policies and contracts associated with services.
  - Service monitor: a component that collects and analyzes data about the status and performance of services and service compositions, and generates reports and alerts for service providers and service consumers.
  - Service manager: a component that controls the lifecycle and configuration of services and service compositions, and handles the deployment, activation, deactivation, and update of services.
  - Service adapter: a component that enables the interoperability and integration of services and service compositions, and handles the transformation, routing, and mediation of service messages.
