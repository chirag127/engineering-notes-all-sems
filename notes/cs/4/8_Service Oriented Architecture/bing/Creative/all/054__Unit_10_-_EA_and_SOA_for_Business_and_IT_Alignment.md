## Unit 10 - EA and SOA for Business and IT Alignment

- EA (Enterprise Architecture) is a framework that covers all the dimensions of IT architecture for the enterprise, such as business, data, application, and technology architectures.
- SOA (Service Oriented Architecture) is an architectural strategy that uses the concept of "Services" as the underlying business-IT alignment entity. Services are self-contained, reusable, and loosely coupled components that provide functionality to support business processes.
- EA and SOA share a similar goal: to bridge the gap between business and IT through business-aligned services. EA provides the holistic view of the enterprise and its strategic direction, while SOA provides the implementation approach and the governance mechanism for the services.
- EA and SOA can benefit from each other in the following ways :
  - EA can help SOA to define the business architecture, which includes the business processes, services, and capabilities that are needed to support the business goals and objectives.
  - EA can help SOA to identify the existing and potential service consumers and providers, and to establish the service contracts and policies that govern the service interactions.
  - EA can help SOA to align the application and technology architectures with the business architecture, and to ensure the interoperability, security, and scalability of the services.
  - SOA can help EA to realize the business architecture through the implementation of services that are aligned with the business needs and expectations.
  - SOA can help EA to monitor and measure the performance and value of the services, and to provide feedback for continuous improvement and optimization.
  - SOA can help EA to adapt to the changing business and IT environment, and to enable agility and flexibility in the enterprise.

- A possible mnemonic to remember the relationship between EA and SOA is: **E**nterprise **A**rchitecture defines the **S**trategy, **O**rientation, and **A**ccountability of the services.
