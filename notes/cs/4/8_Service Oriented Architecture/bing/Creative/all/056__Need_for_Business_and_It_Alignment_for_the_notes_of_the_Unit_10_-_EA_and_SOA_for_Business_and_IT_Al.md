### Need for Business and IT Alignment for the notes of the Unit 10 - EA and SOA for Business and IT Alignment in the subject of Service Oriented Architecture

- Business and IT alignment is the process of ensuring that the technology objectives and strategies of an organization are aligned with its business goals and vision.
- Business and IT alignment is vital for market impact and growth, as it enables the organization to respond quickly and effectively to changing business needs and opportunities, and to leverage technology as a source of competitive advantage and innovation .
- Business and IT alignment can also improve operational efficiency, productivity, customer satisfaction, and employee engagement, as well as reduce costs, risks, and redundancies  .
- Business and IT alignment can be achieved by fostering a culture of collaboration and communication between IT and business teams, and by empowering business users to create and consume connected experiences using no-code or low-code tools .
- Business and IT alignment can also be facilitated by using frameworks and methodologies such as enterprise architecture (EA) and service-oriented architecture (SOA) that help to define, design, and deliver technology solutions that support the business vision and strategy .
- EA is a holistic approach to managing the complexity and change of an organization's technology landscape, by aligning its business, information, application, and infrastructure domains with its goals and principles.
- SOA is a design paradigm that promotes the creation and reuse of loosely coupled, interoperable, and standards-based services that encapsulate business functionality and can be orchestrated to deliver business processes and outcomes.
- EA and SOA can help to achieve business and IT alignment by providing a common language and vision for both IT and business stakeholders, by enabling the integration and automation of business processes across different systems and platforms, and by facilitating the adaptation and innovation of technology solutions in response to changing business needs and opportunities.

Mnemonics and learning tricks:

- To remember the benefits of business and IT alignment, use the acronym **PICER**:

  - **P**roductivity
  - **I**nnovation
  - **C**ustomer satisfaction
  - **E**mployee engagement
  - **R**educed costs and risks

- To remember the key elements of EA, use the acronym **BIAI**:

  - **B**usiness domain
  - **I**nformation domain
  - **A**pplication domain
  - **I**nfrastructure domain

- To remember the key characteristics of SOA, use the acronym **LIS**:

  - **L**oosely coupled
  - **I**nteroperable
  - **S**tandards-based
