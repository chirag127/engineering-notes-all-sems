### SOA Governance – Best Practices for the notes of the Unit 9 - SOA Best Practices in the subject of Service Oriented Architecture

SOA governance is the process of defining, implementing, and enforcing policies, guidelines, and best practices for managing the lifecycle of services and their interactions. SOA governance aims to ensure that your services are consistent, reliable, secure, and compliant with your business and technical requirements.

Some of the best practices for SOA governance are :

- Get buy-in from management. It is important to convey the necessity and benefits of governance within your organization, starting at the top. You should explain how governance can help achieve the strategic goals of SOA, such as agility, reuse, alignment, and quality.
- Choose a champion. Your SOA governance team needs a leader who can drive the vision, strategy, and execution of governance. The champion should have the authority, credibility, and influence to communicate and enforce governance policies across the organization.
- Start small, then evolve. You should not try to implement governance for all services and processes at once, but rather focus on the most critical and valuable ones. You should also establish a feedback loop to monitor and measure the effectiveness of governance, and adjust and improve it as needed.
- Avoid "death by governance". You should not impose too many rules and restrictions on your service developers and consumers, but rather balance the trade-offs between control and flexibility. You should also provide incentives and rewards for compliance and reuse, and avoid penalties and punishments for non-compliance.
- Communicate that "governance is there to help". You should not view governance as a burden or a barrier, but rather as a facilitator and an enabler of SOA success. You should also educate and train your stakeholders on the value and benefits of governance, and provide them with the tools and guidance they need to follow it.

A possible mnemonic to remember these best practices is:

**B**uy-in from management
**C**hampion
**S**tart small
**A**void death
**C**ommunicate help

You can use the acronym **BCSAC** to recall them.

Some additional points to note about SOA governance are:

- SOA governance should be aligned with your enterprise governance and IT governance frameworks, and leverage existing standards, processes, and tools where possible.
- SOA governance should cover the entire service lifecycle, from design and development, to testing and deployment, to monitoring and maintenance, to retirement and decommissioning.
- SOA governance should address both the technical and the business aspects of services, such as functionality, quality, performance, security, availability, reliability, scalability, interoperability, compliance, cost, value, ownership, and accountability.
- SOA governance should involve all the relevant stakeholders, such as service providers, service consumers, service brokers, service managers, service architects, service analysts, service developers, service testers, service administrators, service auditors, and service owners.
- SOA governance should use a combination of formal and informal mechanisms, such as policies, standards, guidelines, best practices, contracts, agreements, registries, repositories, catalogs, dashboards, reports, audits, reviews, feedback, and metrics.
