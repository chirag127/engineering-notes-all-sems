## Unit 6 - SOA Governance and Implementation

SOA governance is the process of establishing and enforcing policies, standards, and best practices for designing, developing, and managing service-oriented architectures (SOAs). SOA governance ensures that the services and processes in an SOA are aligned with the business goals and objectives, and that they are consistent, reliable, secure, and reusable.

SOA implementation is the process of creating, deploying, and integrating services and processes in an SOA. SOA implementation involves using various technologies, tools, and methodologies to design, develop, test, and monitor the services and processes, and to enable interoperability and communication among them.

Some of the topics covered in this unit are:

- SOA governance framework: A model that defines the roles, responsibilities, processes, and artifacts for governing an SOA. A SOA governance framework typically consists of four layers: strategic, tactical, operational, and technical.
- SOA governance lifecycle: A set of activities and tasks that are performed throughout the SOA development and management phases. The SOA governance lifecycle includes planning, defining, enabling, monitoring, and controlling the SOA governance policies, standards, and best practices.
- SOA governance tools: Software applications that support the SOA governance activities and tasks. SOA governance tools include registries, repositories, policy engines, service level agreement (SLA) monitors, and governance dashboards.
- SOA implementation challenges: Some of the common difficulties and issues that arise when implementing an SOA. SOA implementation challenges include complexity, heterogeneity, scalability, security, performance, testing, and change management.
- SOA implementation strategies: Some of the approaches and techniques that can be used to overcome the SOA implementation challenges and to ensure the success of an SOA. SOA implementation strategies include service identification, service design, service development, service deployment, service integration, service orchestration, service composition, service mediation, service monitoring, and service management.

Some of the mnemonics and learning tricks for this unit are:

- To remember the four layers of the SOA governance framework, use the acronym STOP: Strategic, Tactical, Operational, and Technical.
- To remember the five phases of the SOA governance lifecycle, use the acronym PDEMC: Planning, Defining, Enabling, Monitoring, and Controlling.
- To remember some of the SOA governance tools, use the acronym RAPSM: Registries, Repositories, Policy engines, SLA monitors, and governance dashboards.
- To remember some of the SOA implementation challenges, use the acronym CHaSPeT: Complexity, Heterogeneity, Scalability, Performance, Security, and Testing.
- To remember some of the SOA implementation strategies, use the acronym IDDIOSCM: Identification, Design, Development, Integration, Orchestration, Composition, Mediation, and Monitoring.
