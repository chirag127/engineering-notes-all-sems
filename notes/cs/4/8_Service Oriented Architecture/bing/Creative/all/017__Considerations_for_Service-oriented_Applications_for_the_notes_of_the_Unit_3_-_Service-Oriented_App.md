### Considerations for Service-oriented Applications for the notes of the Unit 3 - Service-Oriented Applications in the subject of Service Oriented Architecture

- Service-oriented applications are software applications that are composed of services that communicate and coordinate with each other using standard protocols and interfaces .
- Service-oriented applications aim to achieve high reusability, interoperability, agility, and scalability by leveraging the service-oriented architecture (SOA) principles and patterns  .
- Some of the considerations for designing and developing service-oriented applications are:

  - Align the services with the business value and strategic goals, rather than the technical strategy or specific projects.
  - Use basic interoperability standards and protocols, such as SOAP, REST, JSON, or XML, to enable service communication and integration .
  - Design services as self-contained, discrete, and loosely coupled units of functionality that can be accessed and updated independently  .
  - Identify and expose common and reusable functionalities as shared services, rather than implementing them for specific purposes  .
  - Use an enterprise service bus (ESB) as a centralized component that performs integration, routing, transformation, and orchestration of services .
  - Standardize the service data models and contracts to avoid unnecessary data transformation and improve interoperability.
  - Apply continuous improvement and governance to the service lifecycle, from design to deployment to maintenance  .
  - Evaluate the trade-offs between service granularity, performance, security, and complexity .

- A possible mnemonic to remember these considerations is **BUDS REST**:

  - **B**usiness value and strategic goals
  - **U**niversal interoperability standards and protocols
  - **D**iscrete and loosely coupled services
  - **S**hared and reusable services
  - **R**egistry and governance of services
  - **E**nterprise service bus
  - **S**tandardized data models and contracts
  - **T**rade-offs evaluation
