### Need for Models for the notes of the Unit 4 - Service-Oriented Analysis and Design in the subject of Service Oriented Architecture

- Service-Oriented Architecture (SOA) is an architectural style that aims to achieve business agility by aligning the IT systems with the business processes and goals.
- Service-Oriented Analysis and Design (SOAD) is a software engineering approach that applies the principles and practices of service-orientation to the analysis and design phases of the software development lifecycle.
- Models are abstractions or representations of the real-world entities and concepts that are relevant for the problem domain and the solution domain of a software system.
- Models are needed for SOAD for the following reasons:
  - Models help to communicate and document the requirements, design decisions, and architectural aspects of a SOA-based system in a clear and consistent way.
  - Models help to identify, specify, and reuse the services and microservices that provide the functionality and quality attributes of a SOA-based system .
  - Models help to analyze and evaluate the feasibility, suitability, and impact of the services and microservices that are proposed or implemented in a SOA-based system .
  - Models help to facilitate the collaboration and coordination among the different stakeholders and roles involved in the SOA development process, such as business analysts, service designers, service developers, service consumers, and service providers .
- Some of the models that are commonly used for SOAD are:
  - Business process models: These models capture the business goals, activities, flows, rules, and roles that define the business logic and behavior of a SOA-based system.
  - Service models: These models describe the functional and non-functional characteristics of the services and microservices that are exposed and consumed by a SOA-based system .
  - Service contract models: These models define the interface, protocol, and policy specifications of the services and microservices that are offered and requested by a SOA-based system .
  - Service composition models: These models represent the orchestration, choreography, and coordination of the services and microservices that are involved in a SOA-based system .
  - Service architecture models: These models depict the structure, relationships, dependencies, and interactions of the services and microservices that constitute a SOA-based system .
- A possible mnemonic to remember the types of models for SOAD is: **B**usiness **P**rocess, **S**ervice, **S**ervice **C**ontract, **S**ervice **C**omposition, **S**ervice **A**rchitecture, or **BPSSCSA**.
