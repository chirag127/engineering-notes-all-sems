### Strategic Architecture Governance for the notes of the Unit 6 - SOA Governance and Implementation in the subject of Service Oriented Architecture

- Strategic Architecture Governance is the process of defining, aligning, and enforcing the SOA strategy, goals, and objectives at the enterprise level .
- SOA strategy is the compass that guides the SOA governance activities and provides the direction, scope, and roadmap for the SOA program and initiatives.
- SOA strategy must be aligned with the business strategy, the IT strategy, and the enterprise architecture principles and standards .
- SOA goals are the desired outcomes and benefits of the SOA program and initiatives, such as increased agility, reuse, interoperability, and quality.
- SOA objectives are the measurable and specific targets and milestones that support the achievement of the SOA goals.
- Strategic Architecture Governance involves the following activities :
  - Establishing the SOA governance guiding principles that assist in the prioritization and decision-making for the design, deployment, and execution of the SOA governance regimen.
  - Defining the SOA governance roles and responsibilities that assign the authority and accountability for the SOA governance activities and artifacts.
  - Developing the SOA governance processes that specify the tasks, inputs, outputs, and controls for the SOA governance activities and artifacts.
  - Creating the SOA governance artifacts that document the SOA governance activities and artifacts, such as the SOA strategy, the SOA reference architecture, the SOA policies, the SOA contracts, and the SOA metadata.
  - Implementing the SOA governance technology that supports the SOA governance activities and artifacts, such as the SOA registry, the SOA repository, the SOA monitoring, and the SOA testing tools.
  - Monitoring and measuring the SOA governance performance and compliance that evaluate the effectiveness and efficiency of the SOA governance activities and artifacts, such as the SOA metrics, the SOA audits, and the SOA feedback mechanisms.

- A possible mnemonic to remember the six activities of Strategic Architecture Governance is **EDCIMP** (Establish, Define, Create, Implement, Monitor, and Perform).
- A possible learning trick to remember the difference between SOA goals and objectives is that goals are **general** and objectives are **specific**.
