### Technologies for Service Orchestration for the notes of the Unit 5 - Technologies for SOA in the subject of Service Oriented Architecture

- Service orchestration is the process of designing, creating, delivering, and monitoring service offerings in an automated way .
- Service orchestration is a key enabler for service providers that want to have a platform oriented operating model in order to be more active towards other players in the ecosystem, such as application developers and providers.
- Service orchestration helps service providers to deliver value beyond connectivity and be recognized as an innovative and attractive business partner.
- Service orchestration can be achieved through a variety of IT automation tools, including service orchestration and automation platforms (SOAPs), workload automation solutions (WLA), and enterprise job scheduling platforms.
- Service orchestration platforms include several technologies that have overlapping capabilities, such as extensibility, low-code automation, and centralized monitoring.
- Service orchestration solutions must be capable of VNF/CNF orchestration on multi-clouds, smart workload placement and SLA management and assurance to meet the diverse requirements of 5G use cases.
- Service orchestration is part of an overall effort to unify the technologies of service providers, especially in the context of cloud computing and 5G .
- Service orchestration is the execution of the operational and functional processes involved in designing, creating, and delivering an end-to-end service.
- Service orchestration is different from service choreography, which is a decentralized approach where services interact with each other without a central coordinator.

A possible mnemonic to remember the main points of service orchestration is:

**SOAP** - Service Orchestration and Automation Platforms
**WLA** - Workload Automation solutions
**EJS** - Enterprise Job Scheduling platforms
**VNF/CNF** - Virtual Network Functions/Cloud Native Functions
**SLA** - Service Level Agreement

SOAP, WLA, EJS, VNF/CNF, SLA

These are some of the technologies and concepts involved in service orchestration.
