The principles are explained as follows:

- User-Centered: Services should be designed based on the needs and expectations of the users, not the internal needs of the business.
- Co-Creative: Services should be designed and delivered in collaboration with all relevant stakeholders, including users, customers, employees, and partners.
- Sequencing: Services should be visualized as a sequence of interrelated actions and interactions that form the user journey, from pre-service to post-service.
- Evidencing: Services should be made tangible and visible through visual aids, such as pictures, graphs, and images, that help communicate the value and quality of the service.
- Holistic: Services should be designed to deliver a unified and efficient system, rather than component-by-component, which can lead to poor overall service performance.
- Iterative: Services should be developed as a minimum viable service (MVS) and then deployed, tested, and improved based on user feedback and data.
