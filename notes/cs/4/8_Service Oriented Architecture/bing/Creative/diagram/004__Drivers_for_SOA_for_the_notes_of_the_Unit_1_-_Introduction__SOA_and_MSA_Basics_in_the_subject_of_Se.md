According to the web search results, the drivers for SOA are the enterprise business drivers that influence the design and implementation of a service-oriented architecture. Some of the common drivers for SOA are:

- Competition: The need to compete in the market and offer better products and services to customers.
- Strategy: The vision and goals of the organization and how they align with the business processes and capabilities.
- Regulatory forces: The compliance and governance requirements that affect the business operations and data management.
- Market forces: The demand and supply of the products and services and the customer expectations and feedback.
