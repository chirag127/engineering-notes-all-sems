Service Design-time Governance is a process that aims to ensure that the services developed and delivered by an organization are aligned with the needs and expectations of the stakeholders, such as customers, employees, partners, regulators, etc. It involves defining the goals, principles, standards, policies, and roles for service design, as well as monitoring and evaluating the performance and quality of the services.
