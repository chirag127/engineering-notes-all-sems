According to the web search results, the dimensions of SOA are the following:

- Consumer interface layer: This is the layer where the end users access the applications through graphical user interfaces (GUIs).
- Business process layer: This is the layer where the business use cases are defined in terms of applications.
- Services layer: This is the layer where the whole-enterprise services are offered and stored in a service inventory.
- Service component layer: This is the layer where the services are built using functional and technical libraries.
- Operational systems layer: This is the layer where the data model is defined and stored.
