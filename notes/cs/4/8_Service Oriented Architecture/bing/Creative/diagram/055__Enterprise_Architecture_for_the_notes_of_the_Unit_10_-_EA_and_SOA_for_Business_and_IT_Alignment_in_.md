The diagram illustrates the basic architecture of a service-oriented enterprise, where the business, data, application, and technology layers are aligned and integrated through services. The business layer defines the business services, processes, requirements, and principles that guide the enterprise. The data layer defines the data model, dictionary, requirements, and principles that govern the data management and quality. The application layer defines the application services, interfaces, requirements, and principles that enable the functionality and interoperability of the applications. The technology layer defines the technology services, standards, requirements, and principles that support the infrastructure and security of the enterprise. The services in each layer are designed to be reusable, modular, and loosely coupled, following the SOA principles. The services also communicate and collaborate with each other through standardized protocols and formats, such as SOAP, REST, XML, and JSON. The enterprise architecture provides a holistic and consistent view of the enterprise and helps to align the business and IT goals and strategies.
