Service orchestration is the process of designing, creating, delivering, and monitoring service offerings in an automated way. It is a key enabler for service providers that want to have a platform oriented operating model in order to be more active towards other players in the ecosystem, such as application developers and providers . Service orchestration helps service providers to deliver value beyond connectivity and be recognized as an innovative and attractive business partner.

The service orchestration platform consists of several components that perform different functions:

- Service Catalog: This component provides a list of available services that can be ordered by customers or other applications. It also defines the service attributes, parameters, and dependencies.
