One possible approach for enterprise-wide SOA implementation is to use an enterprise service bus (ESB) as an architectural pattern that enables integration and communication between different applications and services. An ESB provides a common platform for service discovery, routing, transformation, mediation, orchestration, and security. An ESB can also leverage existing legacy systems and expose them as services through standard interfaces and protocols. An ESB can facilitate the development of loosely coupled, coarse-grained, and asynchronous services that are reusable and interoperable across the enterprise.
