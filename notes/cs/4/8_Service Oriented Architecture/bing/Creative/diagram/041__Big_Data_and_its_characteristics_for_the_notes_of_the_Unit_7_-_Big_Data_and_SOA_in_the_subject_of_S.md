Big Data is a term used to describe the massive volumes of data that organizations generate daily. But big data is more than just a large volume of data. The five characteristics of big data are- volume, value, variety, velocity, and veracity.

Volume: Volume is the size of a dataset processed and stored in the Big Data System and is known to be its most important and prominent feature.

Value: Value is the potential benefit that can be derived from analyzing and processing big data. It is the ultimate goal of big data analytics to extract meaningful insights and actionable information from the data.

Variety: Variety is the diversity of data types and sources that big data encompasses. It includes structured, unstructured, and semi-structured data from various domains such as text, audio, video, images, social media, sensors, etc.

Velocity: Velocity is the speed at which data is generated, collected, and processed in the big data system. It reflects the real-time or near-real-time nature of big data applications that require fast and timely analysis and decision making.

Veracity: Veracity is the quality and reliability of data in the big data system. It refers to the accuracy, completeness, consistency, and trustworthiness of the data sources and the data itself.
