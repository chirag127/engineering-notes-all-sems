The diagram shows how the three layers of EA (business, application, and infrastructure) are aligned with the concept of services in SOA. Services are the means of communication and integration between the layers, and they are defined by contracts that specify the inputs, outputs, and quality of service. Services are also aligned with the business needs and goals, and they are designed to be reusable, interoperable, and loosely coupled. The diagram also shows how the layers are composed of different elements, such as processes, components, nodes, roles, functions, and artifacts, that support the execution and delivery of services.
