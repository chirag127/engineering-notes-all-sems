Business process design is a method used to create a new workflow from scratch. It involves discovering, modeling, analyzing, measuring, improving and optimizing business strategy and processes. It can help organizations streamline their operations, increase efficiency and reduce costs.

One of the possible ways to design a business process is to use the Business Process Model and Notation (BPMN) standard, which is a graphical representation of business processes using symbols and diagrams. BPMN can help communicate the logic and flow of a business process in a clear and consistent way.
