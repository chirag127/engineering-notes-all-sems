## Unit 6 - SOA Governance and Implementation

SOA governance is a set of processes used for activities related to exercising control over services in a service-oriented architecture (SOA). SOA governance helps to ensure that the SOA implementation aligns with the business and IT goals, follows the agreed principles and standards, and delivers the expected benefits and value.
