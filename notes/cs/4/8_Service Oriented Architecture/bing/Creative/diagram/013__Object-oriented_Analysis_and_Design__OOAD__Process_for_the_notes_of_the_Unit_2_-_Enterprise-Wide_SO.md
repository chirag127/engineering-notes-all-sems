According to the web search results, Object-oriented Analysis and Design (OOAD) is a technical approach for analyzing and designing an application, system, or business by applying object-oriented programming, as well as using visual modeling throughout the software development process to guide stakeholder communication and product quality.

The OOAD process consists of four main activities: identification of objects, classification of objects, specification of objects, and implementation of objects. These activities can be performed iteratively and incrementally, following a methodology such as the Rational Unified Process (RUP) or the Unified Modeling Language (UML).
