Service orientation is the ability and desire to anticipate, recognize and meet others’ needs, sometimes even before those needs are articulated. Service-oriented people focus on providing satisfaction and making themselves available to others. They actively seek ways to help others and are sensitive to others’ feelings and distress.

Service orientation can be applied in daily life in various contexts, such as personal, professional, social, and global. For example, in personal life, service orientation can mean being attentive and supportive to one's family and friends, offering help when needed, and showing empathy and compassion. In professional life, service orientation can mean being customer-focused, delivering high-quality products or services, and exceeding expectations. In social life, service orientation can mean being involved in community activities, volunteering for causes, and respecting diversity and inclusion. In global life, service orientation can mean being aware of and contributing to the common good, addressing social and environmental issues, and promoting peace and justice.
