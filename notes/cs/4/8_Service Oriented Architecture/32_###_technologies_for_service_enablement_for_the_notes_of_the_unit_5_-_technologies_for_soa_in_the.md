### Technologies for Service Enablement for the notes of the Unit 5 - Technologies for SOA in the subject of Service Oriented Architecture

Service enablement technologies for SOA:
1. Web Services (XML, SOAP, WSDL, UDDI)
2. RESTful Services
3. ESB (Enterprise Service Bus)
4. API Management
5. Microservices Architecture
6. Service Virtualization
7. Service Registry and Repository
8. Service Discovery and Orchestration.

Note: These technologies are used to facilitate communication between services in a SOA environment, ensure interoperability and security, manage and reuse services, and improve overall service performance and reliability.
