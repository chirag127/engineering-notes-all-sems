### Design of Data Services for the notes of the Unit 4 - Service-Oriented Analysis and Design in the subject of Service Oriented Architecture

Design of Data Services in Unit 4 focuses on the analysis and design of services that provide access to data. The following key concepts are covered:
1. Service Contract: Defining the interface and behavior of the data service.
2. Data Model: Defining the structure of the data being served by the service.
3. Data Access: Techniques for accessing data, such as querying, filtering, and sorting.
4. Data Management: Strategies for managing data, such as caching, versioning, and security.
5. Service Composition: Techniques for combining multiple data services into a single, integrated solution.
6. Performance: Strategies for optimizing the performance of data services, such as caching and indexing.
7. Scalability: Techniques for scaling data services to meet the needs of a growing user base.
