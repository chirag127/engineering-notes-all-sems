### Cause Effect Graphing Technique

The cause effect graphing technique is a popular method used in functional testing to identify the relationship between inputs and outputs of a system. This technique helps in generating test cases that are effective in identifying defects and ensuring the functionality of the system. 

Here are some important points to understand about the cause effect graphing technique:

- The technique involves creating a graphical representation of the relationship between inputs and outputs of a system. This is done by creating a cause-effect graph, which shows the cause and effect relationship between the inputs and outputs of the system.
- The cause-effect graph is created by identifying the inputs and outputs of the system, and then identifying the factors that affect the inputs and the outputs. These factors are then used to create the cause-effect graph.
- The cause-effect graph is used to generate test cases that cover all possible combinations of inputs and outputs of the system. This helps in identifying defects and ensuring the functionality of the system.
- The technique is useful in identifying complex dependencies between inputs and outputs of the system. It helps in reducing the number of test cases required to test the system, thereby saving time and effort.
- The technique is also useful in identifying errors and defects that may not be easily identified through other testing methods.
- The cause-effect graphing technique is commonly used in black-box testing, and is particularly useful in testing software applications.

In conclusion, the cause effect graphing technique is an important method used in functional testing to identify the relationship between inputs and outputs of a system. It is an effective method for generating test cases and identifying defects in software applications.
