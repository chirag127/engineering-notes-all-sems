### Decision Table Based Testing

- Decision table based testing is a black-box testing technique.
- It is used to test the system's functionality and logic.
- The test inputs and expected outputs are specified in a tabular format called a decision table.
- It is effective in testing complex logical conditions and combinations of conditions.
- The conditions are listed in columns and the actions or expected outcomes are listed in rows.
- Each cell in the table specifies an input condition and the expected outcome.
- It can be used to test both manual and automated test cases.
- Decision tables are easy to understand and are a compact way to represent a large number of test cases.
- They provide a structural view of the combinations of inputs and outputs.
- It supports effective test coverage and prevents errors.
- However, for a large number of variables, the table can become very big and complex to design and maintain.
