### Verification for the notes of the Unit 1 - Review of Software Engineering in the subject of Software Testing.

1. Software Engineering is the study and application of engineering to the design, development and maintenance of software.

2. The software engineering process includes analysis, design, implementation/coding, testing, and maintenance.

3. The analysis phase defines the problem to be solved and identifies software requirements. The design phase develops solutions to meet the requirements. The implementation phase translates the design into code. The testing phase validates that the software meets requirements. The maintenance phase makes necessary changes to software after delivery to correct faults, to improve performance or other attributes, or to adapt to a changed environment.

4. Reviews and walkthroughs are verification methods that evaluate intermediate and final software products to find and remove defects and problems early. Inspections are a type of review involving a more formal process. Testing is also used to verify software correctness and quality.

5. Metrics are measurements that provide quantitative assessments of software products and processes. Code metrics measure attributes of the code itself. Product metrics measure the functionality, reliability, performance, and quality of the software product. Process metrics measure the effectiveness and efficiency of the software development and maintenance processes.
