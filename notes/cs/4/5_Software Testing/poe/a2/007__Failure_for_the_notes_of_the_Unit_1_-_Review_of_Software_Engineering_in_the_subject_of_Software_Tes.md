### Failure for the notes of the Unit 1 - Review of Software Engineering in the subject of Software Testing

1. Software Failure: The software is said to have failed when it does not fulfill the requirements or expectations. The following are the types of software failures:
- Functional Failure: The software is unable to perform the functions it is designed for. Eg: Calculation errors, inability to save or retrieve data etc.
- Usability Failure: The software is difficult to use, learn or understand. Eg: Complex interfaces, lack of user guidance etc.
- Performance Failure: The software does not meet the performance requirements. Eg: Slow processing speed, inability to handle required load etc.
- Security Failure: The software has vulnerabilities that can be exploited resulting in loss of data, resources or functionality.

2. Causes of Software Failure: The potential causes for software failure include:
- Incorrect requirements: The requirements do not accurately reflect the needs of the end users.
- Design errors: The software architecture or components are not designed correctly leading to issues.
- Coding bugs: The programming code contains logical flaws or does not implement the design correctly.
- Software complexity: The software may be too complex making it difficult to understand, test and maintain.
- Time constraints: Tight deadlines can lead to compromises in quality.
- Human errors: Mistakes made during software development due to lack of experience or knowledge.
- Software-hardware incompatibility: The software is not compatible with the hardware it runs on.

3. Impact of Software Failure: Software failures can have significant negative impacts such as:
- Monetary losses: Software glitches can lead to financial losses for organizations and individuals.
- Loss of life or property: Critical software failures can lead to loss of life, damage to property or equipment.
- Loss of credibility: Software failures can damage the credibility and reputation of companies and developers.
- Legal issues: Poor quality software can lead to legal disputes and lawsuits.
