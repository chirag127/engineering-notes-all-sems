### Identification of Independent Paths for the notes of the Unit 3 - Structural Testing in the subject of Software Testing

1. Identify all the possible paths through the program. This can be done by analyzing the control flow graph of the program.
2. Eliminate the infeasible paths. These are the paths that cannot be executed due to the logical contradictions or variable dependencies.
3. The remaining paths are the feasible independent paths.
4. Classification of the independent paths:
- Basis path: A path that goes through the entire procedure without any loops.
- Prime path: A path that cannot be expressed as a concatenation of two or more paths.
- Error-processing path: A path involving error-processing logic.
5. Select a subset of the independent paths for testing. This can be done randomly or using criteria such as:
- Maximum coverage of code.
- Include prime, basis and error paths.
- Distinctive test cases.
- Complexity and frequency of execution of paths.
