
### SRS Verification for the notes of the Unit 1 - Review of Software Engineering in the subject of Software Testing

- Software Requirements Specification (SRS) Verification is a process used to ensure that the requirements specified in the SRS are accurate, complete and consistent.
- The verification process includes a review of the SRS document to identify any discrepancies or ambiguities.
- The verification process also includes a review of the system design, architecture and code to ensure that the requirements are correctly implemented.
- The verification process should be conducted by an independent team of software engineers who have the necessary expertise and experience to review the SRS document.
- The verification process should be documented and all changes should be recorded.
- The verification process should be conducted at each stage of the software development life cycle to ensure that the requirements are met.
- The verification process should include testing of the system to ensure that it meets the specified requirements.
- The verification process should include a review of the system documentation to ensure that it is accurate and up-to-date.
