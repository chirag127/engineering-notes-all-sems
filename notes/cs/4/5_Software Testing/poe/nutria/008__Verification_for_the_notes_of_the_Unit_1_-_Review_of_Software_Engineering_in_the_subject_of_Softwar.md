
### Verification for the Notes of the Unit 1 - Review of Software Engineering in the Subject of Software Testing

1. Verification is the process of evaluating a system or component to determine whether the products of a given development phase satisfy the conditions imposed at the start of that phase.
2. Verification is the process of determining whether the software meets the requirements that have been specified.
3. Verification is done through the use of tests, inspections and reviews.
4. Verification includes reviews of requirements, design documents, code, test plans and test results.
5. The purpose of verification is to ensure that the software meets the requirements and works as intended.
6. Verification is an important part of software engineering and software testing, as it ensures that the software is of high quality and meets the customer’s expectations.
7. Verification is a continuous process and is done throughout the software development life cycle.
8. The verification process should be documented and all results should be recorded.
