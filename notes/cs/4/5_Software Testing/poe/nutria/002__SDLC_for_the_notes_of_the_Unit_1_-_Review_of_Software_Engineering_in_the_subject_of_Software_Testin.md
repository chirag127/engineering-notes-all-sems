
### SDLC for the Notes of Unit 1 - Review of Software Engineering in the Subject of Software Testing

1. Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC) is a process used to develop software applications and products. It is a structured approach to software development that involves planning, designing, coding, testing, and deploying the software.

2. The SDLC process consists of six stages: planning, analysis, design, implementation, testing, and maintenance. Each stage is important in the development of a successful software product.

3. Planning is the first stage in the SDLC process. It involves gathering requirements, creating a timeline, and setting goals.

4. Analysis is the second stage in the SDLC process. It involves analyzing the requirements and creating a detailed design.

5. Design is the third stage in the SDLC process. It involves designing the architecture and user interface of the software product.

6. Implementation is the fourth stage in the SDLC process. It involves coding and testing the software product.

7. Testing is the fifth stage in the SDLC process. It involves testing the software product to ensure that it meets the requirements.

8. Maintenance is the sixth and final stage in the SDLC process. It involves monitoring and maintaining the software product to ensure that it continues to meet the requirements.
