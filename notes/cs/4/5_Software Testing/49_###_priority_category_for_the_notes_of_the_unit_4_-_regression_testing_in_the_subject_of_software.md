### Priority category for the notes of the Unit 4 - Regression Testing in the subject of Software Testing
Priority categories for regression testing in software testing are used to determine the order in which tests should be executed. The priority categories are typically assigned based on the impact that a change in the software would have on the functionality of the system. The following are the common priority categories for regression testing:

1. High priority: Tests that are critical to the functioning of the system and must be executed first.

2. Medium priority: Tests that are important but not critical to the functioning of the system.

3. Low priority: Tests that are less important and can be executed after the high and medium priority tests have been completed.

The priority categories help to ensure that the most critical tests are executed first, and that the less important tests are executed last. This helps to minimize the risk of introducing new bugs into the system, and to ensure that the system remains functional after changes are made.
