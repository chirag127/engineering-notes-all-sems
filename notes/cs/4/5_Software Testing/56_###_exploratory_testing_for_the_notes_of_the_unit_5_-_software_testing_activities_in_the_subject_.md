### Exploratory Testing for the notes of the Unit 5 - Software Testing Activities in the subject of Software Testing

Exploratory Testing is an approach to software testing where the tester actively explores the application under test while simultaneously designing and executing test cases. It is an informal, ad-hoc method of testing that is performed without a predetermined test plan or script. The main focus is on uncovering as many defects as possible in a short amount of time. Exploratory Testing is often used in conjunction with other testing methods to provide a comprehensive testing approach. It is most effective when performed by an experienced tester who has a thorough understanding of the application and its requirements. This approach is particularly useful in situations where time is limited or the requirements are unclear or subject to change.
