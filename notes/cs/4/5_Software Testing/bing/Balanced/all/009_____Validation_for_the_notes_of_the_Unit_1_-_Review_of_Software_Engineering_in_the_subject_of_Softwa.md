# Validation for the notes of the Unit 1 - Review of Software Engineering in the subject of Software Testing

- Validation is the process of checking whether the software product meets the user requirements and expectations.
- Validation is also known as verification or quality assurance, as it ensures that the software product is fit for its intended purpose and satisfies the customer needs.
- Validation can be performed at different stages of the software development life cycle, such as requirements analysis, design, coding, testing, and deployment.
- Validation can involve various techniques and methods, such as reviews, inspections, walkthroughs, audits, testing, prototyping, simulation, and user acceptance testing.
- Validation can be done by different stakeholders, such as developers, testers, customers, users, managers, and quality assurance teams.
- Validation can have various benefits, such as improving the quality of the software product, reducing the defects and errors, increasing the customer satisfaction and confidence, and saving the time and cost of rework and maintenance.
