### SDLC for the notes of the Unit 1 - Review of Software Engineering in the subject of Software Testing

- SDLC stands for Software Development Life Cycle, which is a process used by the software industry to design, develop and test high-quality software products .
- The SDLC aims to produce software that meets or exceeds customer expectations, reaches completion within time and cost estimates, and is well-tested and ready for production use .
- The SDLC involves six phases as follows :
  - Requirement analysis: This phase involves gathering input from all stakeholders, including customers, salespeople, industry experts, and programmers, to identify the current problems and the desired features of the new software .
  - Planning: This phase involves determining the cost and resources required for implementing the analyzed requirements, as well as defining the scope, schedule, and deliverables of the project .
  - Software design: This phase involves creating the architectural design of the software, such as the user interface, database, modules, and components, based on the requirements and the planning .
  - Software development: This phase involves writing the code for the software, following the design specifications and coding standards, and using various tools and technologies .
  - Testing: This phase involves verifying and validating the software, using various types of testing methods, such as unit testing, integration testing, system testing, and acceptance testing, to ensure that the software meets the quality standards and the customer needs  .
  - Deployment: This phase involves delivering the software to the customer, either as a complete product or as a part of a larger system, and ensuring that the software works as expected in the real environment .
- The SDLC is a repetitive methodology, which means that each phase can be revisited and revised as needed, based on the feedback and the changes in the requirements .
- The SDLC can follow different models and methodologies, such as the waterfall model, the spiral model, the agile model, etc., depending on the nature and the complexity of the software project .
- The SDLC is important because it helps to control the development process, manage the project budget, define the roles and responsibilities of the team members, develop a clear working strategy, reduce the cost and the risk of each stage, and improve the quality and the efficiency of the software product .
