### Verification for the notes of the Unit 1 - Review of Software Engineering in the subject of Software Testing

- Verification is the process of checking whether the software meets the specifications and requirements.
- Verification can be done by various methods, such as reviews, inspections, walkthroughs, audits, and testing.
- Verification aims to ensure that the software conforms to the standards, guidelines, and regulations.
- Verification can be done at different stages of the software development life cycle, such as planning, analysis, design, coding, testing, and maintenance.
- Verification can be classified into two types: static verification and dynamic verification.
- Static verification is the process of checking the software without executing it, such as by reviewing the documents, code, and design.
- Dynamic verification is the process of checking the software by executing it, such as by testing the functionality, performance, and reliability.
- Verification can help to detect and prevent defects, errors, and faults in the software, and improve its quality and reliability.
