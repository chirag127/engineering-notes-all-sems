### Failure

- A failure is an observable deviation from the expected behavior of a system or a component.
- A failure occurs when a system or a component does not meet its requirements or specifications.
- A failure can be caused by one or more faults (defects or errors) in the system or the component.
- A failure can have different consequences depending on the context and the severity of the deviation.
- A failure can be classified into different types, such as:
  - Functional failure: the system or the component does not perform the intended function or service.
  - Non-functional failure: the system or the component does not meet the desired quality attributes, such as performance, reliability, usability, security, etc.
  - Hardware failure: the system or the component fails due to a physical or electrical malfunction of the hardware.
  - Software failure: the system or the component fails due to a logical or semantic error in the software.
  - Human failure: the system or the component fails due to a mistake or a negligence of a human user or operator.
  - Environmental failure: the system or the component fails due to an external factor or a condition that affects its operation, such as temperature, humidity, power supply, network, etc.
- A failure can be detected by various means, such as:
  - Testing: the system or the component is subjected to a set of inputs or scenarios to check its behavior and output against the expected results.
  - Monitoring: the system or the component is observed and measured during its operation to identify any anomalies or deviations from the normal behavior.
  - Reporting: the system or the component provides feedback or logs to indicate its status or any problems that occur during its operation.
  - Debugging: the system or the component is analyzed and inspected to locate and identify the cause of the failure.
- A failure can be prevented or corrected by various actions, such as:
  - Design: the system or the component is designed with high quality standards and best practices to avoid or minimize the occurrence of faults and failures.
  - Verification: the system or the component is checked and reviewed to ensure that it meets its requirements and specifications before its deployment or operation.
  - Validation: the system or the component is evaluated and confirmed to ensure that it satisfies the needs and expectations of the stakeholders and the users.
  - Maintenance: the system or the component is updated and improved to fix any faults or failures that are detected or reported, and to enhance its functionality and quality.
