### Configuration Audits

- Configuration audits are formal reviews of the software products and processes to ensure that they conform to the established standards, specifications, and plans.
- Configuration audits can be classified into two types: functional configuration audit (FCA) and physical configuration audit (PCA).
- Functional configuration audit (FCA) verifies that the software product meets the functional requirements and performance specifications.
- Physical configuration audit (PCA) verifies that the software product conforms to the physical design and documentation standards.
- Configuration audits are usually conducted by an independent team of auditors who check the software products and processes against the configuration management plan, the configuration baseline, and the configuration items.
- Configuration audits help to ensure the quality, consistency, and integrity of the software products and processes, and to identify and resolve any discrepancies or defects.
