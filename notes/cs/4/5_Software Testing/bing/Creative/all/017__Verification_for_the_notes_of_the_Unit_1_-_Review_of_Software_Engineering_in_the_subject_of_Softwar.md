### Verification for the notes of the Unit 1 - Review of Software Engineering in the subject of Software Testing

- Verification is the process of checking that a software achieves its goal without any bugs. It is the process to ensure whether the product that is developed is right or not. It verifies whether the developed product fulfills the requirements that we have.
- Verification is also known as static testing, as it does not involve executing the software. It involves activities such as inspections, reviews, walkthroughs, and desk-checking.
- Verification can be done at any stage of the software development life cycle, but it is usually done before validation.
- Verification can be done by using formal methods, such as mathematical proofs, or informal methods, such as checklists, standards, and guidelines.
- Verification can help to detect errors, inconsistencies, ambiguities, and incompleteness in the software artifacts, such as requirements, design, code, and documentation.
- Verification can also help to improve the quality, reliability, and maintainability of the software product.
- Verification can be classified into four types, according to the IEEE Standard 1012-2016:
  - Software reviews: A process or meeting during which a software product is examined by a project personnel, managers, users, customers, user representatives, or other interested parties for comment or approval.
  - Software audits: A process of independent examination of a software product or process to assess compliance with specifications, standards, contractual agreements, or other criteria.
  - Software inspections: A formal evaluation technique in which software requirements, design, or code are examined in detail by a person or a group other than the author to detect faults, violations of development standards, and other problems.
  - Software analysis: A process of evaluating software to determine its correctness, consistency, completeness, accuracy, performance, security, usability, testability, maintainability, portability, and other attributes.
