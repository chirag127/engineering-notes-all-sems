### SDLC for the notes of the Unit 1 - Review of Software Engineering in the subject of Software Testing

- SDLC stands for Software Development Life Cycle, which is a process used by the software industry to design, develop and test high-quality software products .
- The SDLC aims to produce software that meets or exceeds customer expectations, reaches completion within time and cost estimates, and is well-tested and ready for production use .
- The SDLC involves six phases as follows :
  - Requirement analysis: This phase involves gathering input from all stakeholders, including customers, salespeople, industry experts, and programmers, to identify the current problems and the desired features of the new software .
  - Planning: This phase involves determining the cost and resources required for implementing the analyzed requirements, as well as defining the scope, schedule, and deliverables of the project .
  - Software design: This phase involves creating the architectural design of the software, such as the user interface, database, modules, and components, based on the requirements and the planning .
  - Software development: This phase involves writing the code for the software, following the design specifications and coding standards, and using tools and frameworks to facilitate the development process .
  - Testing: This phase involves verifying and validating the software, using various types of testing methods, such as unit testing, integration testing, system testing, and acceptance testing, to ensure that the software meets the quality standards and the customer needs  .
  - Deployment: This phase involves delivering the software to the customer, installing it in the production environment, and providing support and maintenance services .
- The SDLC is a repetitive methodology, which means that each phase can be revisited and revised based on the feedback and the changes in the requirements .
- There are different models and methodologies of the SDLC, such as the waterfall model, the spiral model, the agile model, and the DevOps model, which follow different approaches and principles for software development .
- The SDLC has many benefits, such as :
  - Improving the quality and reliability of the software
  - Reducing the cost and time of software development
  - Enhancing the communication and collaboration among the stakeholders
  - Managing the project risks and expectations
  - Following the best practices and standards of software engineering

Some mnemonics and learning tricks for the SDLC are:

- To remember the six phases of the SDLC, you can use the acronym **RAPiD Testing** (Requirement analysis, Planning, Software design, Software development, Testing, Deployment).
- To remember the different models and methodologies of the SDLC, you can use the acronym **WASD** (Waterfall, Agile, Spiral, DevOps).
- To remember the benefits of the SDLC, you can use the acronym **QRC** (Quality, Reliability, Cost).
