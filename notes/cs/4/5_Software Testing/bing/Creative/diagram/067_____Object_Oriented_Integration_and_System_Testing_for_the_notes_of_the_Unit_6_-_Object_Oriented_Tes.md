### Object Oriented Integration and System Testing

- Object oriented integration and system testing are the two levels of testing that follow unit testing in object oriented systems.
- Object oriented integration testing is the process of testing the interactions and interfaces between the classes and objects that form the subsystems or components of the system .
- Object oriented system testing is the process of testing the functionality and performance of the entire system as a whole, including the hardware and software components .
- Some of the challenges and techniques of object oriented integration and system testing are:

  - Testing the inheritance hierarchy: The tester needs to ensure that the subclasses inherit the correct attributes and methods from the superclasses, and that the overriding and overloading mechanisms work as expected .
  - Testing the polymorphism: The tester needs to verify that the objects can behave differently depending on their types and contexts, and that the dynamic binding and dispatching mechanisms work correctly .
  - Testing the encapsulation: The tester needs to check that the data and methods of the objects are properly hidden and protected from unauthorized access or modification, and that the public interfaces are consistent and reliable .
  - Testing the collaboration: The tester needs to examine the communication and coordination between the objects, and ensure that the messages are sent and received correctly, and that the contracts and protocols are followed .
  - Testing the reuse: The tester needs to evaluate the quality and suitability of the reused classes and components, and ensure that they are compatible and adaptable to the new system .
  - Testing the concurrency: The tester needs to detect and prevent any potential conflicts or deadlocks that may arise from the simultaneous execution of multiple objects or threads .
  - Testing the scalability: The tester needs to measure and improve the ability of the system to handle increasing workload and demand, and ensure that the system does not degrade or fail under stress .
  - Testing the reliability: The tester needs to assess and enhance the dependability and availability of the system, and ensure that the system can recover from errors and faults .
  - Testing the usability: The tester needs to ensure that the system is easy to learn and use, and that it meets the user's expectations and requirements .
  - Testing the security: The tester needs to ensure that the system is protected from unauthorized access or attack, and that the data and resources are confidential and secure .

- Some of the tools and methods that can be used for object oriented integration and system testing are:

  - Test drivers and stubs: These are dummy classes or objects that simulate the behavior of the real classes or objects that are not yet available or ready for testing. They can be used to isolate and test the individual components or subsystems of the system .
  - Test frameworks and libraries: These are reusable software components that provide common functionalities and services for testing, such as test case generation, execution, reporting, and verification. They can be used to automate and standardize the testing process and reduce the testing effort .
  - Test patterns and guidelines: These are general solutions and best practices for common testing problems and challenges. They can be used to improve the quality and effectiveness of the testing design and implementation .
  - Test metrics and models: These are quantitative measures and methods that can be used to evaluate and improve the testing process and outcome, such as test coverage, test adequacy, test efficiency, and test reliability .
  - Test tools and environments: These are software or hardware devices that can be used to support and facilitate the testing process, such as test generators, analyzers, debuggers, monitors, and simulators .
