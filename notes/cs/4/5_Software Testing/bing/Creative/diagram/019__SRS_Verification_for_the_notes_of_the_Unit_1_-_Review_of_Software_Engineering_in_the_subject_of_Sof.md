SRS Verification is the process of reviewing the Software Requirements Specification (SRS) document to ensure that it is complete, consistent, unambiguous, verifiable, traceable, modifiable, prioritized, testable, relevant, human-readable, and aligned with business goals . SRS Verification can be done by using various methods, such as peer reviews, walkthroughs, inspections, etc. SRS Verification can help to improve the quality of the SRS document and the software system that is being developed.

The SRS Document Preparation step involves creating the SRS document based on the elicited and analyzed requirements for the software system. The SRS document should follow the quality characteristics mentioned above .

The Inspection Planning step involves selecting the inspection team, which consists of a moderator, an author, a reader, and one or more reviewers. The moderator is responsible for organizing and conducting the inspection. The author is the person who wrote the SRS document. The reader is the person who reads the SRS document aloud during the inspection meeting. The reviewers are the persons who check the SRS document for errors, defects, and improvement suggestions. The inspection team should also define the inspection scope, objectives, criteria, and checklist.

The Inspection Meeting step involves conducting the inspection meeting, where the reader reads the SRS document aloud and the reviewers point out any errors, defects, or improvement suggestions. The moderator records the issues and facilitates the discussion. The inspection team should also decide whether the SRS document is acceptable or needs rework.

The Inspection Report step involves preparing and distributing the inspection report, which summarizes the inspection results, such as the number and types of errors, defects, and improvement suggestions, the inspection effort and duration, and the inspection effectiveness and efficiency. The inspection report should also include the action items for the author to revise the SRS document based on the inspection feedback.

SRS Verification is an important activity in software engineering, as it can help to ensure that the SRS document is clear, complete, and accurate, and that the software system meets the needs of the business and its users. SRS Verification can also help to reduce the cost and time of software development, as it can prevent or detect errors and defects early in the software development process. SRS Verification can be done using various methods, such as peer reviews, walkthroughs, inspections, etc, depending on the size, complexity, and criticality of the software system and the SRS document. SRS Verification can be done iteratively and incrementally, as the SRS document evolves and changes during the software development process. SRS Verification can also be done in conjunction with other software engineering activities, such as SRS Validation, Software Design, Software Testing, etc, to ensure the quality and consistency of the software system and its artifacts.
