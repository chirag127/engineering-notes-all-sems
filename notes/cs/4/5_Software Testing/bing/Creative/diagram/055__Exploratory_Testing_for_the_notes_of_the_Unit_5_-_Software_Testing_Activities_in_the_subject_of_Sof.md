According to the search results, exploratory testing is an approach to software testing that is often described as simultaneous learning, test design, and execution. It focuses on discovery and relies on the guidance of the individual tester to uncover defects that are not easily covered in the scope of other tests . Exploratory testing is widely used in agile models and is all about discovery, investigation, and learning. It emphasizes personal freedom and responsibility of the individual tester .
