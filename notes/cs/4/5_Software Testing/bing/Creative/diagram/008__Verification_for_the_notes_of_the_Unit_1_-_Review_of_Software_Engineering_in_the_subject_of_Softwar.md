Verification for the notes of the Unit 1 - Review of Software Engineering in the subject of Software Testing is the process of checking that the software meets the specifications and standards that were established at the beginning of the development phase. Verification is a static testing technique that involves reviews, walk-throughs, and inspections of the software documents, plans, requirements, and specifications  . Verification aims to ensure that the software is well-engineered, consistent, complete, and correct.
