### Walkthrough for the notes of the Unit 1 - Review of Software Engineering in the subject of Software Testing

A walkthrough is a type of software review that is conducted by the author of the software product or document, along with other interested parties, to find and resolve errors and defects in the software during the early stages of the software development life cycle (SDLC) . A walkthrough is an informal and non-formal process, meaning that it does not follow any predefined rules or procedures, and it does not involve any formal authority or moderator . A walkthrough is an open-ended discussion, where the participants can ask questions and make comments about the software product or document, and the author can explain and clarify the information presented . A walkthrough is also a learning opportunity, where the participants can gain a better understanding of the software product or document, and the author can receive feedback and suggestions for improvement .
