A testing suite is a collection of test cases that are designed to verify the functionality, quality and performance of a software product or system. A testing suite can include different types of tests, such as unit tests, integration tests, regression tests, performance tests, etc. A testing suite can also be automated or manual, depending on the tools and methods used to execute the test cases.
