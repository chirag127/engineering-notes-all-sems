The diagram shows the six stages of the SDLC: requirement analysis, planning, design, development, testing, and deployment. Each stage has a specific purpose and output that feeds into the next stage. The diagram also shows the feedback loops between the stages, indicating that the SDLC is an iterative and adaptive process. The last three stages: documentation, evaluation, and maintenance, are often considered as post-development activities that ensure the quality and usability of the software product.
