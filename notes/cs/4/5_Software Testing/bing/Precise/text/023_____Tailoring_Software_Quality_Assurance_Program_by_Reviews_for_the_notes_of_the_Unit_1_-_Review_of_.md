### Tailoring Software Quality Assurance Program by Reviews

1. Software Quality Assurance (SQA) is a process that ensures that developed software meets and complies with defined or standardized quality specifications.
2. SQA is an ongoing process within the software development life cycle (SDLC) that routinely checks the developed software to ensure it meets desired quality measures.
3. One way to tailor an SQA program is by conducting reviews at various stages of the SDLC.
4. Reviews can be conducted on requirements, design, code, test cases, and other software development artifacts.
5. Reviews can help identify defects early in the development process, reducing the cost and effort required to fix them later on.
6. Reviews can also help ensure that the software meets the needs of the customer and other stakeholders.
7. Different types of reviews can be conducted, including peer reviews, walkthroughs, and inspections.
8. The type of review chosen should be based on the specific needs and goals of the SQA program.
9. Reviews should be conducted by a team of individuals with the appropriate knowledge and expertise to evaluate the software development artifacts.
10. The review process should be well-defined and documented to ensure consistency and effectiveness.
