### Error for the notes of the Unit 1 - Review of Software Engineering in the subject of Software Testing

- An error is a human action that produces an incorrect or unexpected result.
- Errors can occur at any stage of the software development process, including requirements gathering, design, coding, testing, and maintenance.
- Common causes of errors include miscommunication, misunderstanding, lack of knowledge or experience, and mistakes in judgment.
- Errors can result in defects, which are flaws in the software that cause it to behave in unintended ways.
- The goal of software testing is to identify and correct errors before the software is released to the public.
- Techniques for preventing errors include clear communication, thorough documentation, and following established software development processes and standards.
- Techniques for detecting errors include code reviews, testing, and static and dynamic analysis.
- Once errors are detected, they must be corrected through debugging and retesting.
- The cost of fixing errors increases as the software development process progresses, so it is important to detect and correct errors as early as possible.
