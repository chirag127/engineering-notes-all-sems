### Walkthrough for the notes of the Unit 1 - Review of Software Engineering in the subject of Software Testing

1. Software Engineering is the application of engineering principles to the development of software.
2. The goal of software engineering is to produce high-quality software that meets the needs of its users within a predictable schedule and budget.
3. The software development process includes activities such as requirements analysis, design, coding, testing, and maintenance.
4. Software engineering principles include modularity, abstraction, information hiding, and separation of concerns.
5. Software testing is an important part of the software development process, as it helps to ensure that the software meets its requirements and is free of defects.
6. There are many different approaches to software testing, including unit testing, integration testing, system testing, and acceptance testing.
7. Software testing can be performed manually or with the use of automated tools.
8. The choice of testing approach and tools depends on the specific needs of the project and the software being developed.
9. Continuous testing and integration can help to catch defects early in the development process, reducing the cost and time required to fix them.
10. The use of software engineering principles and practices can help to improve the quality of software and reduce the risk of defects.
