# Verification Methods for the notes of the Unit 1 - Review of Software Engineering in the subject of Software Testing

Verification is the process of evaluating the artifacts of a software development process to ensure that they meet the specified requirements. There are several methods that can be used for verification, including:

1. **Inspections**: This method involves a team of people reviewing the artifacts to identify any defects or issues. This can be done through formal meetings or informal walkthroughs.

2. **Peer Reviews**: This method involves having a peer review the artifacts to provide feedback and identify any issues. This can be done through pair programming or code reviews.

3. **Walkthroughs**: This method involves the author of the artifact presenting it to a group of people to get feedback and identify any issues.

4. **Technical Reviews**: This method involves a technical expert reviewing the artifacts to ensure that they meet the technical standards and requirements.

5. **Testing**: This method involves executing the software to identify any defects or issues. This can be done through unit testing, integration testing, system testing, and acceptance testing.

These methods can be used individually or in combination to ensure that the software development process is producing high-quality artifacts that meet the specified requirements. It is important to choose the appropriate verification method for each artifact to ensure that it is thoroughly reviewed and any issues are identified and addressed.
