# Verification for the notes of the Unit 1 - Review of Software Engineering in the subject of Software Testing

Verification is the process of evaluating the products of a development phase to ensure that they meet the specified requirements. It is a static process, meaning that it does not involve executing the code. Verification is carried out through reviews, walkthroughs, and inspections.

Here are some key points to remember about verification in the context of software engineering:

1. Verification is concerned with ensuring that the product is being built according to the requirements and design specifications.
2. Verification activities are carried out throughout the software development process, not just at the end.
3. Verification helps to catch defects early in the development process, reducing the cost and time required to fix them.
4. Verification is carried out by a team of people, including developers, testers, and other stakeholders.
5. Verification techniques include reviews, walkthroughs, and inspections. These techniques involve examining the code, design, and other artifacts to ensure that they meet the specified requirements.
6. Verification is a proactive process, meaning that it is carried out before testing to prevent defects from being introduced into the code.
