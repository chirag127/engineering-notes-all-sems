# Verification for the notes of the Unit 1 - Review of Software Engineering in the subject of Software Testing

Verification is the process of evaluating the artifacts of a software development process to ensure that they meet the specified requirements. It is a static process, meaning that it does not involve executing the code. Instead, it involves reviewing and inspecting the documents, design, code, and other artifacts to ensure that they are correct, complete, and consistent.

Here are some key points to remember about verification in the context of software engineering:

1. Verification is a quality assurance activity that aims to ensure that the software product meets the specified requirements.
2. It is a static process, meaning that it does not involve executing the code.
3. Verification activities include reviewing and inspecting documents, design, code, and other artifacts.
4. The goal of verification is to identify and fix errors and defects early in the development process, before they become more costly to fix.
5. Verification is an important part of the software development process and should be performed throughout the development lifecycle.
