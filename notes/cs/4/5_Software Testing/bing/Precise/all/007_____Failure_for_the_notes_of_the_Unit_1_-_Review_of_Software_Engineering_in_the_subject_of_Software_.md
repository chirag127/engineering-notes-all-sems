# Unit 1 - Review of Software Engineering: Failure

- Failure is an event that occurs when the delivered service of a software system deviates from its correct service.
- Failure is the inability of a system or component to perform its required functions within specified performance requirements.
- Failure can be caused by faults or errors in the software, hardware, or other components of the system.
- Failure can also be caused by external factors such as environmental conditions or human error.
- Failure can have serious consequences, including loss of data, loss of revenue, and damage to reputation.
- Failure can be prevented or mitigated through various techniques, including testing, fault tolerance, and redundancy.
- Failure analysis is the process of determining the cause of a failure and developing a plan to prevent similar failures in the future.
- Failure analysis is an important part of software engineering and is essential for improving the reliability and quality of software systems.
