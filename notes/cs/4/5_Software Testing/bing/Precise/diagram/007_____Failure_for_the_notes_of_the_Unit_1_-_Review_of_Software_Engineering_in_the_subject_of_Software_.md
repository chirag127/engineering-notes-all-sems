### Unit 1 - Review of Software Engineering: Failure

- Failure is an event that occurs when the delivered service deviates from the correct service.
- A system may fail either because it does not comply with the specification or because the specification did not adequately describe its function.
- Failure can be caused by faults in the system, which are defects introduced during the development process.
- Faults can be introduced at any stage of the development process, including requirements specification, design, coding, testing, and maintenance.
- Failure can also be caused by human error, such as incorrect data entry or misconfiguration of the system.
- Failure can have serious consequences, including loss of data, loss of revenue, and damage to reputation.
- To prevent failure, it is important to follow best practices in software engineering, including requirements analysis, design, coding, testing, and maintenance.
- It is also important to have a robust process for identifying and correcting faults, including code reviews, testing, and debugging.
- In some cases, it may be necessary to use formal methods to verify the correctness of the system and prevent failure.
