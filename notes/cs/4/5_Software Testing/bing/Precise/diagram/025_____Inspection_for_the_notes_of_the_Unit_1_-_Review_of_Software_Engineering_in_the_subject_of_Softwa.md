### Inspection

Inspection is a type of peer review process in software engineering. It is a formal, systematic, and rigorous approach to evaluating software artifacts and identifying defects. The main goal of inspection is to improve the quality of software products by detecting and correcting defects early in the development process.

Some key points to note about inspection are:

1. Inspection is a formal process that involves a team of reviewers who follow a well-defined process to evaluate software artifacts.
2. The inspection process typically involves several stages, including planning, overview, preparation, inspection meeting, and rework.
3. During the inspection meeting, the reviewers systematically examine the software artifact and identify defects.
4. The defects identified during the inspection are documented and tracked to ensure that they are corrected.
5. Inspection is an effective technique for improving software quality and can help to reduce the cost and time required for software development.

In the context of Unit 1 - Review of Software Engineering in the subject of Software Testing, inspection is an important technique for ensuring the quality of software products. By detecting and correcting defects early in the development process, inspection can help to reduce the cost and time required for software testing and maintenance.
