### SDLC for the notes of the Unit 1 - Review of Software Engineering in the subject of Software Testing

SDLC, or Software Development Life Cycle, is a process used by the software industry to design, develop, and test high-quality software. The SDLC aims to produce high-quality software that meets or exceeds customer expectations, reaches completion within times and cost estimates, and works effectively and efficiently in the current and planned information technology infrastructure.

The SDLC process includes several stages, including:

1. **Planning:** This stage involves determining the scope of the project, identifying potential risks, and developing a project plan.
2. **Analysis:** During this stage, the requirements for the software are gathered and analyzed.
3. **Design:** In this stage, the software's architecture is designed, and the software's user interface is created.
4. **Implementation:** This stage involves the actual coding and development of the software.
5. **Testing:** During this stage, the software is tested to ensure that it meets the requirements and is free of defects.
6. **Deployment:** In this stage, the software is released to the public.
7. **Maintenance:** This stage involves ongoing support for the software, including bug fixes and updates.

Each stage of the SDLC is important and plays a crucial role in the development of high-quality software. By following the SDLC process, software developers can ensure that their software is well-designed, thoroughly tested, and ready for release to the public.
