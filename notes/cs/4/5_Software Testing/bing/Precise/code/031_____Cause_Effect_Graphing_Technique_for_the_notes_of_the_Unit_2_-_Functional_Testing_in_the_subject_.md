### Cause Effect Graphing Technique

Cause Effect Graphing Technique is a black box testing technique used in functional testing. It is a graphical representation of inputs and the associated outputs effects which can be used to design test cases. Here are some key points to note about this technique:

1. Cause Effect Graphing is also known as Fishbone diagram or Ishikawa diagram.
2. It is used to identify all possible root causes of a problem or an effect.
3. The causes are usually grouped into major categories to identify the sources of variation.
4. The major categories often include people, methods, machines, materials, measurements, and environment.
5. The technique is used to identify the relationships between the different causes and the effect.
6. It helps in identifying the areas where data can be collected for further analysis.
7. The technique is useful in identifying the most likely causes of a problem and in developing a plan to address them.

This technique can be a useful tool in the functional testing process, helping to identify potential causes of problems and to design test cases to address them. It is a visual and structured approach to problem-solving, making it easier to understand and communicate the relationships between different factors.
