### Tailoring Software Quality Assurance Program by Reviews

Software Quality Assurance (SQA) is a process that ensures that developed software meets and complies with defined or standardized quality specifications. SQA is an ongoing process within the software development life cycle (SDLC) that routinely checks the developed software to ensure it meets desired quality measures.

One way to tailor an SQA program is by conducting reviews. Reviews are a critical component of the SQA process and can help to improve the quality of software by identifying and addressing issues early in the development process. Some key points to consider when tailoring an SQA program by reviews include:

1. **Defining review objectives:** Clearly define the objectives of the review process, such as identifying defects, improving software design, or ensuring compliance with standards.

2. **Selecting appropriate review techniques:** Choose review techniques that are appropriate for the software being developed and the objectives of the review process. Common review techniques include walkthroughs, technical reviews, and inspections.

3. **Training reviewers:** Ensure that reviewers are properly trained in the review techniques being used and are familiar with the software being reviewed.

4. **Conducting reviews at appropriate times:** Schedule reviews at appropriate times during the development process, such as after the completion of a design or coding phase.

5. **Documenting review results:** Document the results of the review process, including any issues identified and actions taken to address them.

By tailoring an SQA program by reviews, organizations can improve the quality of their software and ensure that it meets the desired quality specifications. This can help to reduce the risk of software defects and improve the overall reliability and performance of the software.
