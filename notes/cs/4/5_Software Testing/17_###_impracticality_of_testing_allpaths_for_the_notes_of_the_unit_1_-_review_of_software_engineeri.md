### Impracticality of Testing AllPaths for the notes of the Unit 1 - Review of Software Engineering in the subject of Software Testing
Testing all possible paths in a software system is impractical due to the following reasons:
- The number of paths increases exponentially with the size of the system
- The cost of testing all paths is prohibitively high
- The time required to test all paths is infeasible
- It is impossible to test all paths in real-world systems with limited resources
- The likelihood of finding new defects decreases as more paths are tested
- It is more efficient to focus on high-risk paths and use risk-based testing strategies

Note: This information is relevant to Unit 1 - Review of Software Engineering in the subject of Software Testing.
