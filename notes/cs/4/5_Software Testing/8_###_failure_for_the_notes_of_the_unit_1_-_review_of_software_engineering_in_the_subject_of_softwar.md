### Failure for the notes of the Unit 1 - Review of Software Engineering in the subject of Software Testing
Failure refers to the inability of a system or component to perform its intended function, within specified performance criteria. It can occur due to various reasons such as design flaws, bugs, human error, environmental factors, etc. 

In software testing, failure is the outcome of executing a test case that detects a defect in the software. The goal of testing is to identify as many failures as possible before the software is released to the customers. 

There are two types of failures: 
1. Functional failure: when the software does not meet the specified requirements. 
2. Non-functional failure: when the software does not meet the specified quality criteria such as performance, security, usability, etc. 

It is important to understand the root cause of the failure and take corrective actions to prevent it from happening again in the future. This helps to improve the quality of the software and increase customer satisfaction.
