### Tailoring Software Quality Assurance Program by Reviews for the notes of the Unit 1 - Review of Software Engineering in the subject of Software Testing
Tailoring Software Quality Assurance (SQA) program by Reviews involves designing and implementing a set of processes and activities aimed at ensuring the quality of software products. Reviews are a key component of SQA, as they provide an opportunity to identify and address defects early in the software development process. The following are the steps in tailoring an SQA program by reviews: 

1. Define the scope of the review process by establishing what will be reviewed, who will participate, and how the review will be conducted. 

2. Identify the objectives of the review process, including what the review should achieve and what metrics will be used to measure success. 

3. Select the appropriate review type, such as walkthroughs, inspections, or peer reviews. 

4. Develop a review plan, including the schedule, roles and responsibilities, and review criteria. 

5. Conduct the review, following the plan and using the review criteria to identify and address defects. 

6. Document the results of the review, including any defects identified and how they were addressed. 

7. Continuously improve the review process by using feedback from participants and the results of the review to identify areas for improvement. 

By tailoring the SQA program by reviews, organizations can improve the quality of their software products and reduce the risk of defects being introduced later in the development process.
