### Fault for the notes of the Unit 1 - Review of Software Engineering in the subject of Software Testing
Fault:
- A fault is a defect or error in a software system that causes it to behave in an unintended way.
- Faults can be the result of human error, design flaws, or environmental factors.
- Faults can cause software systems to produce incorrect results, crash, or become unavailable.
- Detecting and correcting faults is an important aspect of software testing.
- Faults can be classified into various categories, such as syntax errors, semantic errors, logical errors, and more.
