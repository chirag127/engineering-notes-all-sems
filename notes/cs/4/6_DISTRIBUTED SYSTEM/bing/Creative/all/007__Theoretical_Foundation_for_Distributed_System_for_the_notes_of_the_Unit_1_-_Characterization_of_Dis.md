### Theoretical Foundation for Distributed System for the notes of the Unit 1 - Characterization of Distributed Systems in the subject of DISTRIBUTED SYSTEM

- A distributed system is a system whose components are located on different networked computers, which communicate and coordinate their actions by passing messages to one another. 
- The components of a distributed system may have a common goal, such as solving a large computational problem, or may have individual needs, such as sharing resources or providing communication services. 
- Distributed systems face several challenges, such as maintaining concurrency of components, overcoming the lack of a global clock, and managing the independent failure of components. 
- The theoretical foundation for distributed system aims to understand the fundamental limits and possibilities of distributed computing, and to design efficient and reliable algorithms and protocols for distributed systems. 
- Some of the topics that are covered in the theoretical foundation for distributed system are:
  - Limitations of distributed systems, such as impossibility results, lower bounds, and trade-offs. 
  - Models of distributed systems, such as synchronous and asynchronous, message passing and shared memory, deterministic and randomized, fault-tolerant and Byzantine. 
  - Logical clocks and causality, such as Lamport's and vector clocks, and their applications in ordering events and detecting dependencies.  
  - Distributed algorithms and protocols, such as leader election, consensus, broadcast, multicast, distributed mutual exclusion, distributed snapshot, distributed termination detection, distributed shared memory, distributed file systems, distributed transactions, distributed commit, distributed deadlock detection, distributed garbage collection, distributed graph algorithms, distributed machine learning, distributed optimization, distributed cryptography, distributed blockchain, and distributed consensus.  
- A possible mnemonic to remember the topics in the theoretical foundation for distributed system is: LIMoLoC DiAP (Limitations, Models, Logical clocks, Distributed algorithms and protocols).
