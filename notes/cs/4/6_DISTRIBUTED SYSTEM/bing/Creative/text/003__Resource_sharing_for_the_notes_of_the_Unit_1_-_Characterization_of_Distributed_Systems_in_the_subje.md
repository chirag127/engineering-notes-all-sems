### Resource sharing for the notes of the Unit 1 - Characterization of Distributed Systems in the subject of DISTRIBUTED SYSTEM

- Resource sharing means that the existing resources in a distributed system can be accessed or remotely accessed across multiple computers in the system.
- Resources can be hardware (such as disks and printers), software (such as files, windows and data objects) or data.
- Resource sharing can be achieved in different ways, such as:
  - Data migration: the process of transferring data from one location to another in the system. Data is brought to the location of computation, that needs to access it .
  - Computation migration: the process of transferring computation rather than data across the system. This is useful when the data is too large or complex to be transferred. The computation is performed at the location of data and the result is sent back to the requester .
  - Load balancing: the process of spreading the load across the distributed system in order to optimize resource utilization and performance .
- Resource sharing can improve the scalability, availability, fault tolerance and transparency of distributed systems.
