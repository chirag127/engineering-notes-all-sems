### The Web Challenges for the notes of the Unit 1 - Characterization of Distributed Systems in the subject of DISTRIBUTED SYSTEM

- A distributed system is a system in which hardware or software components located at networked computers communicate and coordinate their actions only by passing messages .
- Distributed systems are characterized by the following fundamental properties :
  - Concurrency: components can execute in parallel and interact with each other.
  - No global clock: there is no single notion of the correct time across the system.
  - Independent failures: components can fail or crash without affecting the whole system.
- The main motivation for building and using distributed systems is to share resources, such as hardware, software, data, and services .
- The web is an example of a distributed system that enables resource sharing and communication among millions of users and servers .
- The web challenges for distributed systems are the following :
  - Scalability: the ability to handle increasing load and users without degrading performance or functionality.
  - Heterogeneity: the ability to deal with different types of devices, platforms, protocols, and formats.
  - Security: the ability to protect the system and its resources from unauthorized access, modification, or damage.
  - Failure handling: the ability to cope with partial failures and recover from errors.
  - Transparency: the ability to hide the complexity and diversity of the system from the users and applications.
  - Quality of service: the ability to provide guarantees on the availability, reliability, and performance of the system and its resources.
