### Hash for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Blockchain in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

1. Blockchain is a decentralized, distributed and public digital ledger that underlies cryptocurrencies like Bitcoin.
2. Blockchain is a continuously growing list of records called blocks which are linked together chronologically, cryptographically and secured using cryptography.
3. Each block contains a timestamp and a link to the previous block in the chain.
4. By design, blockchain is resistant to modification of the data. So the data in it is open, permanent, and verifiable.
5. Blockchain uses a peer-to-peer network and consensus algorithm to maintain the ledger. This makes the records more transparent, secure, and robust.
6. The key properties of blockchain are decentralization, immutability, transparency, and consensus.
7. The key components of a blockchain are blocks, transactions, nodes, and consensus algorithm.
8. The key advantages of blockchain are decentralization, security, transparency, and immutability.
9. The key disadvantages of blockchain are scalability, interoperability, energy consumption, and anonymity.
10. Some applications of blockchain are cryptocurrencies, smart contracts, finance, supply chain management, healthcare, etc.
