## Unit 6 - Use case 1

* Use case 1: Purchasing a product

This use case describes the steps involved when a customer purchases a product from an e-commerce website:

1. Customer browses the products on the e-commerce website and selects a product they wish to purchase.
2. Customer adds the selected product to their cart and proceeds to checkout.
3. Customer enters their personal details like name, address, payment details, etc. to place the order.
4. The e-commerce website verifies the customer details and payment information. If everything is valid, the order is placed.
5. The e-commerce website sends an order confirmation email to the customer with the order details.
6. The warehouse/fulfillment team processes the order and ships the product to the customer's address.
7. Once the product is delivered, the order is marked as completed.

**Advantages:** Allow customers to easily purchase products from the comfort of their homes. Increased convenience and a wide range of products to choose from for customers.
**Disadvantages:** Risk of frauds and scams. Delayed or missing deliveries can lead to customer dissatisfaction.

**Examples:** Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, etc.
**Applications:** E-commerce websites and mobile apps.

**Mnemonics:**
Purchasing a Product = Select -> Add to Cart -> Checkout -> Payment -> Confirmation -> Ship -> Deliver
