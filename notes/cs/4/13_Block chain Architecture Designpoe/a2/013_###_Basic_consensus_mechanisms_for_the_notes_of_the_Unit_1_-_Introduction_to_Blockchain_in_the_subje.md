### Basic consensus mechanisms for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Blockchain in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

The blockchain consensus mechanisms ensure that the nodes in a blockchain network agree on the validity of transactions and the order in which they occur. The most common consensus mechanisms are:

1. Proof-of-Work (PoW): In PoW, miners solve complex cryptographic puzzles to verify and record transactions on the blockchain. The first miner to solve the puzzle adds the transaction record to the blockchain and claims the reward. The proof-of-work system makes the blockchain secure, robust, and tamper-resistant. However, PoW consumes a lot of computational power and electricity.

Mnemonic: The one who works harder and solves the puzzle first, wins the reward.

2. Proof-of-Stake (PoS): In PoS, validators stake their own cryptocurrencies to verify and record transactions. The validator that stakes the most cryptocurrency has the highest chance of verification and adding the next block to the blockchain. PoS requires less resources than PoW but favors the wealthy.

Mnemonic: The one who stakes the most, earns the most.

3. Delegated Proof-of-Stake (DPoS): In DPoS, stakeholders elect witnesses (delegates) to verify and record transactions. Witnesses are rewarded for adding verified blocks to the blockchain. DPoS makes blockchains scalable but at the risk of centralization as the voting process may favor stakeholder coalitions.

Mnemonic: Let the elected do the work.
