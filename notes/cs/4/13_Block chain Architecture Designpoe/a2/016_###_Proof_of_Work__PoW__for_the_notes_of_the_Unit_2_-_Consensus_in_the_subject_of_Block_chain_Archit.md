### Proof of Work (PoW) for the notes of the Unit 2 - Consensus in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

Proof of Work (PoW) is a consensus algorithm used in blockchain networks to determine who has the right to produce the next block and earn the block reward. It is a competitive process where miners compete to solve a complex computational math problem (cryptographic puzzle). The first miner to solve the problem and validate the new block gets to place it on the blockchain and claim the block reward.

**How it works:**

1. Miners are constantly trying to guess the solution to the complex cryptographic problem.
2. Once a solution is found, it is broadcast to the network.
3. Other miners verify the solution is correct.
4. If verified, the block is added to the blockchain and the miner is rewarded.
5. The process then repeats to find the next block in the chain.

**Advantages:**

- Prevents spam attacks and Sybil attacks on the network by making it computationally expensive to produce blocks.
- Decentralized - anyone with computing power can participate.
- Simple to implement and understand.

**Disadvantages:**

- Inefficient use of electricity and computing resources.
- Vulnerable to mining centralization where larger miners gain more power and control.
- Security could be compromised if one miner gained 51% of the network hash rate.

**Mnemonics:**

- "computationally intensive puzzle solving"
- "the race is on to solve the puzzle"
- "mining farm vs mining decentralization"

**Applications:** Bitcoin, Ethereum (for now), Bitcoin Cash, Litecoin, etc.
