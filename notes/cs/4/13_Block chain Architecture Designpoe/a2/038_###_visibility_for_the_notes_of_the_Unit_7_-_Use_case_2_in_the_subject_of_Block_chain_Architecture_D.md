### Visibility for the notes of the Unit 7 - Use case 2 in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

The visibility controls in blockchain architecture design for use case 2 involves the following points:

1. Public blockchain: The blockchain is open and transparent. Anyone can view the data and transactions. There is no access control. Examples: Bitcoin, Ethereum
2. Permissioned blockchain: Only authorized nodes can participate in the consensus and maintain the blockchain. The data is visible to participating nodes. Examples: Hyperledger Fabric, R3 Corda
3. Private blockchain: The blockchain is restricted to specific organizations or entities. Only authorized parties can view the data and transactions. Examples: MultiChain, Quorum

Advantages of public blockchain: Decentralization, transparency, security through cryptography

Disadvantages of public blockchain: Scalability issues, privacy concerns

Advantages of permissioned/private blockchain: Scalability, privacy, flexibility

Disadvantages of permissioned/private blockchain: Less decentralized, single point of failure

Based on the use case requirements, the appropriate visibility type can be chosen for the blockchain architecture. For example, a supply chain management use case may use a permissioned blockchain to enable scalability and privacy between organizations. A cryptocurrency use case will use a public blockchain to enable decentralization and transparency.

Mnemonics:

Public: Open to all, transparent
Permissioned: Authorized parties only, visible to participants
Private: Restricted to specific entities, visible to authorized parties
