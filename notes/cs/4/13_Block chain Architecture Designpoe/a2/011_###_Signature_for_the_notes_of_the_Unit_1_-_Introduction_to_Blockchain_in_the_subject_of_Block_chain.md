### Signature for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Blockchain in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

1. Blockchain is a distributed ledger of transactions maintained by a network of computers. The ledger is append-only, so new records are added to the end of the ledger.
2. The ledger is decentralized and consensus is achieved through a proof-of-work system or proof-of-stake system. This makes the ledger tamper-resistant and immutable.
3. Blockchain uses cryptography to ensure the integrity of the data and to control read/write access. The data in the ledger is cryptographically signed and verified.
4. A blockchain is a chain of blocks where each block contains a timestamp and a link to the previous block. By design, blockchains are resistant to modification of the data.
5. Bitcoin is the first and most widely used blockchain application. Blockchain has many other applications such as smart contracts, supply chain tracking, medical records, etc.

**Advantages:** Immutability, decentralization, transparency, security, efficiency.
**Disadvantages:** Scalability, interoperability, energy consumption.

**Examples:** Bitcoin, Ethereum, Hyperledger, Corda, etc.
**Applications:** Cryptocurrencies, smart contracts, supply chain, healthcare, etc.

Here are some mnemonics and learning tricks to remember the key points:

1. DLT - Distributed Ledger Technology
2. A-B-C - Append-only, Blockchain, Cryptography
3. TIMB - Tamper-resistant, Immutable, Modiification-resistant
4. PS or POW - Proof-of-Stake or Proof-of-Work
