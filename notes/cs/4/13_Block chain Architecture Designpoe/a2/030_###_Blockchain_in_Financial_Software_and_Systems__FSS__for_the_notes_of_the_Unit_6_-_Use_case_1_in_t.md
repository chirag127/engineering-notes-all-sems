### Blockchain in Financial Software and Systems (FSS) for the notes of the Unit 6 - Use case 1 in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

- Blockchain technology can be implemented in financial software and systems to provide immutable, transparent and secure transactions. Some of the key ways in which blockchain can be leveraged in FSS are:
- Smart contracts: Blockchain enables smart contracts which are self-executing contracts with the terms of the agreement between buyer and seller being directly written into lines of code. Smart contracts can be used to automate and secure financial transactions, loans, crowdfunding, etc. without the need for intermediaries.
- Cross-border payments: Blockchain can make international money transfers faster, cheaper and more transparent. Cryptocurrencies can be used to transfer funds across borders without the need for correspondent banks and waiting periods.
- Securities trading: Blockchain can be used to trade stocks, bonds, and other financial instruments in a peer-to-peer manner without the need for a central clearing authority. This can reduce settlement times and costs.
- Digital identity: Blockchain can be used to create and verify digital identities for users. This can help in Know-Your-Customer (KYC) checks, bank accounts, etc. and prevent identity fraud. Digital identities on blockchain are decentralized and not controlled by any single organization.
- Pros: Security, immutability, transparency, disintermediation, faster and cheaper transactions.
- Cons: Scalability issues, lack of widespread adoption, volatility of cryptocurrencies, regulations unclear.
- Examples: Hyperledger, R3 Corda, SETL, etc.
- Applications: Cross-border payments, funds transfers, lending, digital identities, trading, etc.
