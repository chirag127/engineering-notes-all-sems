## Unit 8 - Use case 3

Use case 3 deals with modeling a basic e-commerce system. The main actors involved are:

- Customer: Browsing products, placing orders
- Store owner: Managing products, orders, and customers
- System: Fulfilling orders, updating product listings, sending notifications

Main steps in the use case:

1. Customer browses products on the store and adds desired products to the cart
2. Customer proceeds to checkout and enters personal details and payment info
3. Store owner receives the order and processes the payment
4. If payment is successful, store owner prepares the order and ships the products
5. Customer receives the order and can leave reviews/ratings for products and store

Advantages:

- Allows customers to conveniently shop and purchase products online
- Provides store owners an additional sales channel to reach more customers
- Standardized process with defined steps, easy to understand and implement

Disadvantages:

- Requires significant investment to build and maintain the e-commerce system
- Risk of fraud or unsuccessful payments, requiring verification mechanisms
- Reliance on third-party payment gateways and shipping services
