### Public Distribution System Social Welfare Systems for the notes of Unit 8 - Use case 3 in Block chain Architecture Design

- A public distribution system is a system established by the government to distribute essential commodities to the poor sections of the society at subsidized rates.
- Blockchain can be used to build a robust, transparent and accountable public distribution system. Some of the key benefits are:
- The entire supply chain from procurement to distribution of commodities can be tracked in real-time. This reduces leakage and corruption.
- Smart contracts can be used to automate the distribution process based on eligibility criteria. This reduces manual interference and increases efficiency.
- The distributed and immutable nature of blockchain ensures high data integrity. The records cannot be tampered with or manipulated.
- Payments can be made digitally using cryptocurrencies or stablecoins, thereby reducing use of physical cash and increasing security and transparency of transactions.
- However, there are some challenges to overcome:
- Technological literacy and access to technology among beneficiaries may be limited. Extra efforts may be required to educate and onboard them.
- Interoperability with existing systems will need to be built to leverage existing infrastructure.
- Scalability of blockchain networks can be an issue due to the volume of transactions and data. Appropriate technical solutions will need to be adopted.
- Local regulations will need to be complied with. Government support and partnerships will be crucial to adopt blockchain in public distribution systems.
