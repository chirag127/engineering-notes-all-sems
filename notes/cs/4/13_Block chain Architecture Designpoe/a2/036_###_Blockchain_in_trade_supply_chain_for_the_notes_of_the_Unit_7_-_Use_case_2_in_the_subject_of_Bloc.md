### Blockchain in trade/supply chain for the notes of the Unit 7 - Use case 2 in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

- Blockchain can bring transparency and immutability to the trade and supply chain process. The transactions can be tracked in a tamper-proof, permanent and transparent manner. This can help in tracking the provenance of goods and detecting counterfeits.
- The supply chain process involves multiple stakeholders like suppliers, manufacturers, distributors, retailers, etc. Blockchain can provide a shared, trusted and consistent view of the supply chain data to all the stakeholders. This can reduce delays, disputes and frauds.
- Smart contracts can automate the supply chain processes like payment releases, delivery triggers, etc. This can make the supply chain more efficient, accurate and fast.
- However, blockchain may not scale to handle the huge volumes of data and transactions in the supply chain. The stakeholders need to agree on a common data format and standards. There are also privacy concerns with making the entire supply chain data accessible to all.
- Some examples of blockchain in supply chain are IBM Food Trust, Walmart food safety collaboration, diamond tracking, etc. Blockchain promises significant benefits to the trade and supply chain domain if the challenges can be addressed.
