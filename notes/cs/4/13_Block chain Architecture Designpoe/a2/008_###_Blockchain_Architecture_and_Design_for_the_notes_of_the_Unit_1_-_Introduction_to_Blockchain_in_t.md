## Blockchain Architecture

- Blockchain is a distributed ledger that maintains a continuously growing list of records called blocks.
- Each block contains a timestamp and a link to the previous block.
- By design, blockchain is resistant to modification of the data. So the data in it is tamper-evident and tamper-resistant.
- The blockchain architecture consists of the following main components:

1. Network Participants - The nodes in the peer-to-peer network that maintain the blockchain.
2. Blockchain - The distributed ledger containing the blocks of transactions in a linear, chronological order.
3. Consensus Algorithm - The algorithm used by the network participants to agree on the valid blocks to be added to the blockchain. Some popular consensus algorithms are Proof-of-Work, Proof-of-Stake, etc.
4. Wallet - The software used to send and receive digital currencies and monitor blockchain transactions.
5. Miners - Special network participants that verify and record transactions to the blockchain. They are important in blockchain networks that use Proof-of-Work consensus.

## Blockchain Design

- The blockchain design involves deciding on various factors such as:

1. Choice of consensus algorithm - The choice affects scalability, security, decentralization, etc.
2. Choice of data structure - The commonly used options are Merkle trees and hash lists.
3. Choice of peer-to-peer network - The type of network used affects performance, security, etc. Some options are gossip networks, DHTs, etc.
4. Choice of blockchain data storage - The data can be stored in a file, database or other data structure. The choice affects performance, security, etc.
5. Choice of blockchain client - The client software is needed to connect to the blockchain network and perform functions. Options include full clients, light clients, etc.

- The design choices can be optimized based on the requirements and use cases of the blockchain application.
