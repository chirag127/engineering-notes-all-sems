### Visibility for the Notes of the Unit 7 - Use Case 2 in the Subject of Blockchain Architecture Design

When it comes to blockchain architecture design, visibility plays a crucial role in ensuring the security and transparency of the system. Here are some important points to keep in mind regarding visibility in the context of use case 2:

1. Blockchain nodes: Each node in the blockchain network can have varying levels of visibility depending on the permissions given to them. Nodes can be classified as full nodes, light nodes, and super nodes, with full nodes having complete visibility of the blockchain data and transactions.

2. Smart contracts: Smart contracts can be used to enforce visibility rules within the blockchain network. For example, a smart contract can be designed to restrict access to certain data or transactions only to authorized nodes.

3. Privacy concerns: While transparency is a key feature of blockchain, there may be certain instances where privacy concerns need to be taken into account. In such cases, techniques like zero-knowledge proofs can be used to provide selective transparency while maintaining the privacy of sensitive data.

4. Auditing: Auditing is an important aspect of visibility in blockchain architecture design. Auditing can help ensure that the blockchain network is functioning as intended and that there are no security breaches or malicious activities happening within the system.

5. Consensus mechanisms: The consensus mechanism used in the blockchain network can also have an impact on visibility. For example, proof-of-work (PoW) mechanisms provide a high level of visibility as all nodes are required to solve complex mathematical problems to validate transactions. On the other hand, proof-of-stake (PoS) mechanisms may provide less visibility as only a select group of nodes are responsible for validating transactions.

Mnemonic: Remember the 5 points above as "B-SPAC" - Blockchain nodes, Smart contracts, Privacy concerns, Auditing, Consensus mechanisms.

Overall, visibility is a crucial aspect of blockchain architecture design and should be carefully considered when designing and implementing blockchain solutions.
