### Design goals for the notes of the Unit 3 - Permissioned Blockchains in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

Permissioned blockchains are private blockchains where the access to the network is restricted to a group of participants who are known to each other. These blockchains are used in various industries such as finance, healthcare, supply chain management, and many more. The following design goals should be kept in mind while taking notes on permissioned blockchains in the subject of Blockchain Architecture Design:

1. **Scalability**: Permissioned blockchains should be able to handle a large number of transactions per second. The notes should include the techniques used to achieve scalability, such as sharding, sidechains, and off-chain transactions.

2. **Security**: Security is a crucial aspect of permissioned blockchains since they are used to store sensitive information. The notes should include the security mechanisms used in permissioned blockchains, such as digital signatures, consensus algorithms, and access control.

3. **Privacy**: Permissioned blockchains should ensure the privacy of the participants. The notes should include the techniques used to achieve privacy, such as encryption, zero-knowledge proofs, and ring signatures.

4. **Interoperability**: Permissioned blockchains should be able to communicate with other blockchains and traditional systems. The notes should include the methods used to achieve interoperability, such as atomic swaps, cross-chain transactions, and middleware.

5. **Governance**: Permissioned blockchains should have a well-defined governance model to ensure that the network is managed effectively. The notes should include the different governance models used in permissioned blockchains, such as federated, consortium, and hybrid models.

6. **Usability**: Permissioned blockchains should be easy to use and accessible to non-technical users. The notes should include the techniques used to improve usability, such as user-friendly interfaces, smart contracts, and automation.

Mnemonic: "SS PIGU" - Scalability, Security, Privacy, Interoperability, Governance, Usability 

Remembering this mnemonic can help students to recall the design goals of permissioned blockchains easily during exams.
