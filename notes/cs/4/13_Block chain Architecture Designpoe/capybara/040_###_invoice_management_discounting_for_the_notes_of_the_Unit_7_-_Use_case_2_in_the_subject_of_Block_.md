### Invoice Management Discounting for the notes of the Unit 7 - Use case 2 in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

Invoice management discounting is the process of financing invoices before the scheduled payment date. In this process, a third party, called a factor, purchases invoices from a company at a discounted rate, providing the company with immediate cash flow. This process can be facilitated using blockchain technology to make the process more efficient and secure.

Here are some key points to help you understand invoice management discounting:

1. Factors: Factors are third-party companies that purchase invoices from businesses at a discounted rate. Factors provide businesses with immediate cash flow, while they wait for their customers to pay their invoices.

2. Invoice discounting: Invoice discounting is a form of invoice financing where a business sells its unpaid invoices to a factor at a discounted rate to receive immediate cash flow. The factor then collects the full amount from the customers.

3. Blockchain technology: Blockchain technology can be used to create a secure and transparent system for invoice management discounting. The use of smart contracts on the blockchain allows for automated and secure transactions between the parties involved.

4. Advantages of using blockchain technology for invoice management discounting:
   - Increased efficiency in processing invoices and payments
   - Improved security and transparency
   - Reduced risk of fraud and errors
   - Increased trust between parties involved in the transaction

5. Mnemonic: Remember the acronym FID or Fidelity. Fidelity is the quality of being faithful or loyal to something. Just like how factors are loyal to purchase invoices at a discounted rate to provide immediate cash flow to the business.

6. Example: A business sells goods to a customer and generates an invoice for $10,000. The customer agrees to pay the invoice in 90 days. The business needs immediate cash flow and decides to sell the invoice to a factor at a discounted rate of 5%. The factor provides the business with $9,500 and collects the full amount from the customer after 90 days.

In conclusion, invoice management discounting is a process that can help businesses with immediate cash flow. The use of blockchain technology can make the process more efficient, secure, and transparent. Remember the mnemonic FID or Fidelity to remember the concept.
