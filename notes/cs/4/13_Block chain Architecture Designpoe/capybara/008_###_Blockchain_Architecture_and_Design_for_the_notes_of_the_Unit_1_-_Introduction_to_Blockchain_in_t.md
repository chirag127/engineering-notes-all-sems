### Blockchain Architecture and Design for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Blockchain in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

Blockchain architecture and design are crucial elements of the blockchain technology that plays a vital role in maintaining the security, transparency, and immutability of the blockchain network. In this unit, we will understand the basics of blockchain architecture and design, which includes the following:

1. **Consensus Mechanism**: Consensus mechanism is the fundamental aspect of blockchain architecture that helps in achieving trust and reliability in the blockchain network. It is a protocol or a mechanism that ensures that all the nodes in the network agree on the same version of the blockchain ledger. Some of the popular consensus mechanisms are Proof of Work (PoW), Proof of Stake (PoS), and Delegated Proof of Stake (DPoS).

2. **Blockchain Network**: A blockchain network is a peer-to-peer network where all the participants or nodes in the network interact with each other to validate and add new blocks to the blockchain. A blockchain network can be of two types - Public and Private. In a public blockchain network, anyone can join and participate in the network, whereas in a private blockchain network, only authorized participants can join and participate.

3. **Blockchain Data Structure**: Data structure is the way in which data is stored and organized in the blockchain network. The blockchain data structure is a linked list of blocks, where each block contains a set of transactions, a timestamp, and a reference to the previous block. The reference to the previous block is what makes the blockchain immutable and tamper-proof.

4. **Smart Contracts**: Smart contracts are self-executing contracts that run on the blockchain network. They are computer programs that automatically execute the terms of the contract when certain conditions are met. Smart contracts are an essential component of blockchain architecture as they automate the execution of contracts and eliminate the need for intermediaries.

5. **Cryptographic Hash Functions**: Cryptographic hash functions are mathematical functions that are used to secure the data in the blockchain network. They take an input data and produce a fixed-size output called hash, which is unique for each input data. Hash functions ensure the integrity and authenticity of the data in the blockchain network.

In conclusion, blockchain architecture and design are critical elements that ensure the security, transparency, and immutability of the blockchain network. Understanding the basics of blockchain architecture and design is essential for blockchain developers and enthusiasts who want to build blockchain-based applications and solutions.
