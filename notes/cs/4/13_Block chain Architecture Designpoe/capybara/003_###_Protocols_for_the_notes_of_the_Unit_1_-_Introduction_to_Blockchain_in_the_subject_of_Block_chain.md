### Protocols for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Blockchain in the subject of Blockchain Architecture Design

Blockchain technology is built upon various protocols that facilitate the secure transfer of data and assets between different parties. Understanding these protocols is essential for anyone looking to work with blockchain technology. In this section, we will cover some of the most important protocols used in blockchain architecture design.

1. **Proof-of-Work (PoW)**: PoW is a consensus mechanism used in blockchain networks to validate transactions and create new blocks. In this protocol, users known as miners compete to solve complex mathematical problems, and the first miner to solve the problem is rewarded with a new block and a certain amount of cryptocurrency. PoW is used in popular blockchain networks like Bitcoin and Ethereum.

2. **Proof-of-Stake (PoS)**: PoS is another consensus mechanism used in blockchain networks that is more energy-efficient than PoW. In this protocol, validators are chosen to create new blocks based on the amount of cryptocurrency they hold. Validators lock up their cryptocurrency as collateral, and if they validate a fraudulent transaction, they lose their stake. PoS is used in blockchain networks like Cardano and Tezos.

3. **Delegated Proof-of-Stake (DPoS)**: DPoS is a variant of PoS that uses a smaller group of validators to create new blocks. In DPoS, token holders can vote for delegates to represent them in the validation process. The delegates with the most votes are selected to create new blocks. DPoS is used in blockchain networks like EOS and BitShares.

4. **Federated Byzantine Agreement (FBA)**: FBA is a consensus mechanism used in private blockchain networks. In this protocol, a group of trusted nodes known as a quorum must agree on transactions before they are confirmed. FBA is used in blockchain networks like Stellar.

5. **Directed Acyclic Graph (DAG)**: DAG is a protocol used in blockchain networks like IOTA that allows for faster and more scalable transactions. In DAG, transactions are validated by referencing previous transactions, creating a graph-like structure. This allows for more parallel processing of transactions and eliminates the need for miners.

Mnemonic: P for PoW, P for PoS, D for DPoS, F for FBA, and G for DAG.

Learning Trick: Remember the acronyms and the full form of each protocol. Try to associate each protocol with a well-known blockchain network that uses it. For example, PoW is used in Bitcoin and Ethereum, PoS is used in Cardano and Tezos, DPoS is used in EOS and BitShares, FBA is used in Stellar, and DAG is used in IOTA. This will help you remember the different protocols and their applications.
