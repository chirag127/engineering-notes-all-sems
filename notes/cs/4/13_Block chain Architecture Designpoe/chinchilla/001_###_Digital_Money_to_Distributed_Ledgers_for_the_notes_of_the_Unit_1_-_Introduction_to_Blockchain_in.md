### Digital Money to Distributed Ledgers for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Blockchain in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

The advent of digital money has led to the need for secure and transparent methods of recording and transferring financial transactions. The blockchain technology, with its distributed ledger architecture, has emerged as a powerful solution to this problem. In this unit, we will explore the journey from digital money to distributed ledgers and understand the role of blockchain in this evolution.

#### Evolution of Digital Money

1. Digital Currency: Digital currency refers to electronic money that can be used to make purchases and payments online. Examples include Bitcoin, Litecoin, and Ripple.

2. Centralized Digital Currency: Centralized digital currencies are issued and managed by a central authority, such as a government or a financial institution. Examples include PayPal, Venmo, and Alipay.

3. Decentralized Digital Currency: Decentralized digital currencies are not issued or controlled by any central authority. Instead, they rely on a distributed network of users to validate transactions. Examples include Bitcoin, Ethereum, and Litecoin.

#### Distributed Ledgers

1. A distributed ledger is a database that is spread across a network of nodes. Each node has a copy of the database and the same set of rules for updating it.

2. The distributed ledger system is highly secure and transparent, as each transaction is verified and recorded by multiple nodes on the network.

3. Distributed ledgers are used in a variety of applications, including financial transactions, supply chain management, and voting systems.

#### Blockchain Technology

1. Blockchain technology is a type of distributed ledger that uses cryptographic techniques to create a secure and tamper-proof record of transactions.

2. A blockchain consists of a series of blocks, each of which contains a set of transactions. Each block is linked to the previous block, creating a chain of blocks.

3. The blockchain is maintained by a network of nodes, each of which has a copy of the entire blockchain. Transactions are verified and added to the blockchain through a process called mining.

4. Blockchain technology has several advantages, including security, transparency, and immutability. It has the potential to revolutionize the way we conduct financial transactions and other types of online interactions.

Mnemonics and Learning Tricks:

- To remember the evolution of digital currency, use the acronym CDC, which stands for Centralized, Decentralized, and Digital Currency.
- To remember the components of blockchain technology, use the acronym BMT, which stands for Blocks, Mining, and Transactions.
