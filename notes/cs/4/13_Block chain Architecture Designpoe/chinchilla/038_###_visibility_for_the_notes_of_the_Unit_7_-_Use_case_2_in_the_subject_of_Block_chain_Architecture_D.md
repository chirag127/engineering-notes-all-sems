1. Data Transparency: One of the primary benefits of blockchain technology is its ability to provide transparency in data management. In Use Case 2, visibility ensures that all data is visible to all parties involved in the transaction. This transparency ensures that the data is accurate, tamper-proof, and easily auditable.

2. Asset Tracking: In Use Case 2, blockchain technology is used to track the movement of assets. This tool provides visibility into the transaction history and ensures that all parties can track the movement of assets.

They are used in Use Case 2 to automate the execution of transactions. Visibility ensures that all parties can view the code of the smart contract, ensuring that it is secure and that all parties understand the terms of the contract.
