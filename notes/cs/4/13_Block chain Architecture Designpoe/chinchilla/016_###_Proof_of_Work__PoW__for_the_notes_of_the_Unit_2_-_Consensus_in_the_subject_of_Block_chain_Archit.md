### Proof of Work (PoW) for the notes of the Unit 2 - Consensus in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

Proof of Work (PoW) is a consensus mechanism used by many blockchain networks to validate transactions and create new blocks on the chain. In this mechanism, miners compete with each other to solve a complex mathematical puzzle, and the first miner to solve the puzzle is rewarded with new coins and the right to add the next block to the chain.

Here are some important points to remember about Proof of Work (PoW):

1. The puzzle that miners need to solve in PoW is called a hash function. The hash function takes an input and produces a fixed-length output, called a hash. The puzzle for miners is to find an input that produces a hash that meets a specific criterion, such as having a certain number of leading zeros.

2. The difficulty of the puzzle is adjusted based on the total computational power of the network. This means that as more miners join the network, the puzzle becomes harder to solve, and vice versa.

3. Once a miner solves the puzzle, they broadcast the solution to the rest of the network. Other nodes on the network then verify the solution and, if it is correct, they add the new block to their copy of the blockchain.

4. The process of solving the puzzle and adding the new block to the chain is called mining. Miners are incentivized to mine by receiving new coins as a reward for their efforts.

5. One of the main advantages of PoW is that it is very secure. Because the puzzle is difficult to solve, an attacker would need to control a majority of the network's computational power in order to successfully attack the network.

6. However, one of the main disadvantages of PoW is that it is very energy-intensive. The computational power required to solve the puzzle and mine new blocks consumes a lot of electricity, which can be a significant environmental concern.

7. Mnemonic: Think of PoW as a race to solve a puzzle. The first miner to solve the puzzle wins the race and gets to add the next block to the chain. The difficulty of the puzzle is adjusted based on the number of racers, and the winner gets a prize for their efforts.

8. Learning trick: To remember the basics of PoW, try memorizing the acronym "SOLVE". S stands for "secure", O stands for "output", L stands for "ledger", V stands for "verify", and E stands for "energy-intensive".
