### Requirements for the consensus protocols for the notes of the Unit 2 - Consensus in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

Consensus protocols are an essential component of blockchain technology. They are responsible for ensuring that all nodes on the blockchain network agree on the current state of the ledger. Without consensus, the blockchain would be vulnerable to attacks and would not be able to function as a secure and decentralized system. In this unit, we will discuss the various requirements for consensus protocols in the context of blockchain architecture design.

#### 1. Byzantine fault tolerance

Byzantine fault tolerance refers to the ability of a consensus protocol to function correctly even when some nodes on the network are malfunctioning or intentionally trying to disrupt the network. A consensus protocol must be able to tolerate these types of faults, also known as Byzantine failures, in order to maintain the integrity and security of the blockchain.

#### 2. Decentralization

Decentralization is a key feature of blockchain technology. In order to maintain a decentralized network, the consensus protocol must be designed in such a way that no single entity or group can control the decision-making process. This helps to prevent censorship and ensures that the network is truly decentralized.

#### 3. Scalability

As the number of users on a blockchain network increases, so does the amount of data being processed by the network. A consensus protocol must be scalable in order to handle large volumes of transactions and maintain fast transaction speeds even as the network grows.

#### 4. Security

Security is crucial for any blockchain network. The consensus protocol must be designed to prevent attacks such as double-spending and Sybil attacks. It should also be resistant to attacks from malicious nodes or groups attempting to disrupt the network.

#### 5. Energy efficiency

Some consensus protocols, such as Proof of Work (PoW), require a significant amount of energy to operate. This can be a barrier to entry for smaller nodes on the network and can lead to environmental concerns. Consensus protocols should be designed with energy efficiency in mind, in order to reduce the environmental impact of blockchain technology.

#### Learning tricks

One helpful mnemonic for remembering the requirements for consensus protocols is "BDSSE," which stands for Byzantine fault tolerance, decentralization, scalability, security, and energy efficiency. Another way to remember these requirements is to think of them as the "pillars" of a strong and secure blockchain network.
