## Unit 3 - Permissioned Blockchains

Permissioned blockchains, also known as private or enterprise blockchains, are a type of blockchain network where access to the network and participation in the consensus process is restricted to a certain group of nodes or users. In this unit, we will dive deeper into permissioned blockchains, their characteristics, advantages, and use cases.

### Characteristics of Permissioned Blockchains

- Restricted access: Permissioned blockchains restrict access to the network and participation in the consensus process to a certain group of nodes or users. This allows for more control over the network and can increase its security.
- Centralized control: Permissioned blockchains are often controlled by a central authority or a group of authorities. This allows for more efficient decision-making and can increase the speed of transaction processing.
- Consensus mechanisms: Permissioned blockchains can use different consensus mechanisms, such as proof of authority (PoA) or Byzantine fault tolerance (BFT), to validate transactions and maintain the integrity of the network.
- Data privacy: Permissioned blockchains can implement different levels of data privacy, such as pseudonymity or anonymity, depending on the needs of the network and its participants.

### Advantages of Permissioned Blockchains

- Increased security: Permissioned blockchains can provide increased security due to their restricted access and centralized control. This can make them more suitable for applications that require high levels of security, such as financial transactions.
- Faster transaction processing: Permissioned blockchains can process transactions faster than permissionless blockchains due to their centralized control and consensus mechanisms.
- Better scalability: Permissioned blockchains can scale better than permissionless blockchains due to their centralized control and ability to implement more efficient consensus mechanisms.

### Use Cases of Permissioned Blockchains

- Financial services: Permissioned blockchains can be used in the financial industry to facilitate faster and more secure transactions, reduce fraud, and increase transparency.
- Supply chain management: Permissioned blockchains can be used in supply chain management to improve transparency, reduce fraud, and increase efficiency.
- Healthcare: Permissioned blockchains can be used in the healthcare industry to improve data privacy, reduce fraud, and increase transparency.
- Government: Permissioned blockchains can be used by governments to increase transparency, reduce fraud, and improve efficiency in areas such as voting, identity management, and public record keeping.

### Learning Tricks

- Mnemonic: "Permissioned blockchains are like a gated community where only a certain group of people can enter and participate in community decisions."
- Visualize a gated community where only residents and authorized personnel can enter and participate in community decisions. This can help you remember the idea of restricted access and centralized control in permissioned blockchains.
