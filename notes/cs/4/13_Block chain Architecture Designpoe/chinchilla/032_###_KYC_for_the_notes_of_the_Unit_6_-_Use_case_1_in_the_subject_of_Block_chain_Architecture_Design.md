### KYC for the notes of the Unit 6 - Use case 1 in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

KYC stands for Know Your Customer, which is a mandatory process in the financial industry for verifying the identity of a customer. In the context of blockchain architecture design, KYC plays an important role in ensuring the security and integrity of the system. Here are some important points to remember about KYC in blockchain:

- KYC is a process of verifying the identity of a customer, which typically involves collecting and verifying personal information such as name, address, date of birth, and government-issued ID.
- KYC is important in blockchain architecture design because it helps to prevent fraud, money laundering, and other illegal activities by ensuring that only legitimate users are allowed to participate in the system.
- One of the key benefits of using blockchain for KYC is that it can provide a decentralized and secure way to store and manage customer information. This can help to prevent data breaches and other security risks that are common with traditional centralized systems.
- There are several different approaches to implementing KYC in blockchain architecture design, including using smart contracts, decentralized identity solutions, and third-party verification services.
