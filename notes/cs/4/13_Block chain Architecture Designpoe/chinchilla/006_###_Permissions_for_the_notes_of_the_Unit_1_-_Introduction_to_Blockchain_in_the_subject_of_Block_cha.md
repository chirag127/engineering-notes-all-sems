### Permissions for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Blockchain in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

In the field of Blockchain Architecture Design, it is important to understand the concept of permissions when it comes to taking notes for Unit 1 - Introduction to Blockchain. Permissions are the rules that govern who can access, modify, or delete data in a Blockchain network. In this context, notes refer to the written records of lectures or discussions related to the topic.

Here are some important points to keep in mind while taking notes for Unit 1 - Introduction to Blockchain in the subject of Blockchain Architecture Design:

1. Always seek permission from the instructor or the institution before taking notes. Some institutions have strict policies regarding the use of notes or recordings from lectures.

2. If the instructor permits, take clear and concise notes that capture the key concepts and ideas discussed during the lecture or discussion.

3. Avoid sharing notes with individuals who do not have permission to access them. This includes classmates who were absent during the lecture or individuals outside of the institution.

4. Be careful not to record sensitive information that could compromise the security or privacy of the Blockchain network.

5. If the instructor prohibits note-taking, pay close attention to the lecture and try to remember the key concepts and ideas discussed.

Mnemonic: PANTS

Learning trick: Always ask for permission, take clear and concise notes, never share notes with unauthorized individuals, be mindful of sensitive information, and pay close attention if note-taking is prohibited.

In conclusion, understanding permissions is crucial when taking notes for Unit 1 - Introduction to Blockchain in the subject of Blockchain Architecture Design. By following the guidelines and seeking permission from the instructor, students can effectively capture and retain the important concepts and ideas related to Blockchain technology.
