### Design goals for the notes of the Unit 3 - Permissioned Blockchains in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

Permissioned blockchains, also known as private or enterprise blockchains, are a type of blockchain where access to the network is restricted to a group of known participants. These blockchains are designed for use within organizations or consortia where trust and privacy are of utmost importance.

When studying permissioned blockchains in the subject of blockchain architecture design, it is important to keep in mind the following design goals:

1. **Scalability:** Permissioned blockchains must be able to handle large amounts of data and transactions, while maintaining high performance and low latency. This requires careful consideration of the consensus algorithm, network architecture, and data storage mechanisms.

2. **Security:** Permissioned blockchains must be highly secure to prevent unauthorized access, tampering, or theft. This requires implementing robust authentication and authorization mechanisms, as well as encryption and digital signatures to protect the data on the blockchain.

3. **Privacy:** Permissioned blockchains must ensure the privacy of the participants and their data. This requires designing the network architecture and data storage mechanisms in such a way that only authorized participants can access the data, and ensuring that sensitive data is not stored in plain text.

4. **Interoperability:** Permissioned blockchains must be able to interoperate with other blockchain networks and systems. This requires designing the blockchain with open standards and protocols, as well as ensuring compatibility with other systems and networks.

5. **Governance:** Permissioned blockchains must have a clear and transparent governance model to ensure that decisions about the network are made in a fair and democratic manner. This requires designing the blockchain with clearly defined rules and procedures for decision-making, as well as mechanisms for resolving disputes and conflicts.

Mnemonic: "SSPIG" - Scalability, Security, Privacy, Interoperability, Governance.

By keeping these design goals in mind, students studying permissioned blockchains in the subject of blockchain architecture design will have a solid understanding of the key considerations involved in designing and implementing these types of blockchain networks.
