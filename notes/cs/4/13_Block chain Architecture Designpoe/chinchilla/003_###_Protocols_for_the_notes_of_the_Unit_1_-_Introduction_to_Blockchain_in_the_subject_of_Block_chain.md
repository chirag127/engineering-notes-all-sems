### Protocols for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Blockchain in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

In the field of Blockchain Architecture Design, there are several protocols that are essential to understand. These protocols define the rules and guidelines that govern the operations of a blockchain network. Here are the key protocols to take note of:

1. Proof of Work (PoW)
   - PoW is a consensus mechanism used in blockchain networks to validate transactions and create new blocks.
   - Miners compete to solve complex mathematical puzzles, with the first miner to solve the puzzle being rewarded with new cryptocurrency.
   - Mnemonic: "Proof of Work = Puzzle solving competition for miners."

2. Proof of Stake (PoS)
   - PoS is another consensus mechanism used in blockchain networks.
   - Instead of solving puzzles, validators are chosen based on the amount of cryptocurrency they hold and are willing to 'stake' as collateral.
   - Validators are rewarded with transaction fees and newly minted cryptocurrency.
   - Mnemonic: "Proof of Stake = The more you hold, the more you validate."

3. Byzantine Fault Tolerance (BFT)
   - BFT is a fault-tolerant consensus mechanism used in blockchain networks.
   - It allows nodes to reach consensus even if some nodes are faulty or malicious.
   - Mnemonic: "Byzantine Fault Tolerance = Trusting the network even with bad actors."

4. Directed Acyclic Graph (DAG)
   - DAG is a data structure used in some blockchain networks.
   - Instead of using a linear chain of blocks, DAGs use a graph structure where each transaction can be confirmed by multiple previous transactions.
   - Mnemonic: "Directed Acyclic Graph = Transactions confirming each other in a web-like structure."

5. Interledger Protocol (ILP)
   - ILP is a protocol used for transferring value across different ledgers and networks.
   - It allows for interoperability between different blockchain networks and traditional financial systems.
   - Mnemonic: "Interledger Protocol = Connecting different ledgers like a bridge."

Understanding these protocols is crucial for designing and implementing secure and efficient blockchain networks. It is important to note that different blockchain networks may use one or more of these protocols in combination with other protocols as well.
