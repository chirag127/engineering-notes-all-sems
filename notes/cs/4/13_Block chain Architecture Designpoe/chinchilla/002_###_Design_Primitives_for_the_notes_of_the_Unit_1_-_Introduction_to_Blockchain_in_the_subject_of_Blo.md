### Design Primitives for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Blockchain in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

Blockchain technology has taken the world by storm and is revolutionizing various industries like finance, healthcare, supply chain management, and more. To understand the architecture and design of a blockchain system, it is essential to know the basic building blocks or design primitives that make up a blockchain. The design primitives of blockchain are:

1. **Blocks:** The block is the fundamental unit of a blockchain system. It contains a list of transactions that are verified and added to the blockchain. Each block is linked to the previous block in the chain, creating a chain of blocks. The first block in the chain is called the genesis block.

2. **Transactions:** A transaction is a record of an exchange of value between two parties on the blockchain network. Each transaction contains information such as the sender's address, the recipient's address, and the amount of value being transferred.

3. **Consensus Mechanism:** Consensus is the process by which blockchain nodes come to an agreement on the state of the blockchain. It ensures that all nodes agree on the same version of the blockchain. Different blockchain networks use different consensus mechanisms like Proof of Work (PoW), Proof of Stake (PoS), and Delegated Proof of Stake (DPoS).

4. **Cryptographic Hashing:** Hashing is a process of converting an input message of any size into a fixed-size output. Cryptographic hashing is used in blockchain to ensure the integrity of data. Each block in the blockchain contains a hash of the previous block, which ensures the immutability of the blockchain.

5. **Smart Contracts:** Smart contracts are self-executing contracts with the terms of the agreement between buyer and seller being directly written into lines of code. They are stored on the blockchain and automatically executed when certain conditions are met. Smart contracts eliminate the need for intermediaries, making transactions more efficient and cost-effective.

Knowing these design primitives is essential for understanding the architecture and design of a blockchain system. By understanding the role of each primitive, you can gain a deeper insight into how a blockchain system works and how it can be optimized for various use cases.

#### Learning Tricks:

- Mnemonic for the design primitives of blockchain could be "BTC CS" where BTC stands for Bitcoin and CS stands for Consensus Mechanism, Cryptographic Hashing, and Smart Contracts.
- Another learning trick could be to associate each primitive with a color or symbol. For example, Blocks can be associated with the color green, Transactions with the color blue, Consensus Mechanism with the color red, Cryptographic Hashing with the symbol of a lock, and Smart Contracts with the symbol of a contract. This way, you can create a visual representation of the blockchain design primitives to aid in memorization.
