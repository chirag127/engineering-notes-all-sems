
## Unit 5 - Hyperledger Fabric (B)

Hyperledger Fabric (B) is a permissioned blockchain platform that offers a modular architecture with a delineation of roles between the nodes in the infrastructure, execution of smart contracts, and configurable consensus and membership services. It is designed to support pluggable implementations of different components and accommodate the complexity and intricacies that exist across the economic ecosystem.

Mnemonics and Learning Tricks:
* **F**abric: A **F**lexible and **F**ast blockchain platform
* **B**lockchain: A **B**etter way to build distributed applications

Advantages:
* Highly secure and reliable
* Easy to deploy and maintain
* Flexible and extensible architecture
* Supports pluggable implementations of different components
* Supports configurable consensus and membership services

Disadvantages:
* Limited scalability
* Lack of privacy
* High cost of development

Applications:
* Supply chain management
* Digital identity
* Healthcare
* Banking and finance
* Insurance
* Government
* IoT
