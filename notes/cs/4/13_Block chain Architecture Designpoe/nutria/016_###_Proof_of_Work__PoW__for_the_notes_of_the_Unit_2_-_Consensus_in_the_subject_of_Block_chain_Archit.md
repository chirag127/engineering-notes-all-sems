

### Proof of Work (PoW) for the notes of the Unit 2 - Consensus in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

- Proof of Work (PoW) is a consensus mechanism used in blockchain networks to verify transactions and prevent double-spending.
- PoW requires miners to solve complex mathematical puzzles in order to add a new block to the blockchain.
- The miner who solves the puzzle first is rewarded with a certain amount of cryptocurrency.
- PoW is a secure and reliable way to verify transactions as it requires a lot of computational power to solve the puzzle.
- PoW is also used to prevent Sybil attacks, as it requires miners to prove their identity in order to participate in the consensus process.
- PoW is an energy-intensive process, as it requires a lot of electricity to power the computers that are solving the puzzle.
- PoW can also be vulnerable to 51% attacks, as miners with a majority of the network's computing power can take control of the network.
- To improve the efficiency of PoW, some blockchains have implemented hybrid consensus mechanisms that combine PoW with other consensus algorithms such as Proof of Stake (PoS).
