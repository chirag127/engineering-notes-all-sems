
### Blockchain Architecture and Design for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Blockchain

Blockchain Architecture and Design is a key element of the subject of Block Chain Architecture Design. It is an important concept that is used to create secure and immutable distributed ledgers.

The following are the main components of a blockchain architecture and design:

- **Nodes**: Nodes are computers that take part in the blockchain network. They are responsible for verifying and validating transactions, and for storing and maintaining the blockchain ledger.

- **Mining**: Mining is the process of verifying and validating transactions and adding them to the blockchain ledger. It is done by miners, who are rewarded with cryptocurrency for their work.

- **Consensus Protocols**: Consensus protocols are used to ensure that all nodes in the network agree on the state of the blockchain. Popular consensus protocols include Proof-of-Work (PoW) and Proof-of-Stake (PoS).

- **Smart Contracts**: Smart contracts are programs that are stored on the blockchain and are used to automate certain processes. They are written in a programming language such as Solidity and can be used to create decentralized applications (dApps).

- **Cryptography**: Cryptography is used to secure the blockchain network. It is used to encrypt data, verify transactions, and prevent tampering with the blockchain ledger.

- **Mnemonics and Learning Tricks**: Mnemonics and learning tricks are useful tools for remembering key concepts related to blockchain architecture and design. For example, the mnemonic "PoW PoS POW POW" can be used to remember the four main components of a blockchain architecture and design: Nodes, Mining, Consensus Protocols, and Smart Contracts.
