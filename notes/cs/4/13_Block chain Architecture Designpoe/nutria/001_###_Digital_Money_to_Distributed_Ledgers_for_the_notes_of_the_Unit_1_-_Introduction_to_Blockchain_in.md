
### Digital Money to Distributed Ledgers for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Blockchain in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

Digital Money to Distributed Ledgers is a concept that has been around for quite some time, but has only recently gained traction due to the rise of blockchain technology. It is a form of digital money that is decentralized, meaning that it is not controlled by any one entity or government. This allows for a more secure and transparent way of transferring funds, as well as providing a platform for smart contracts and other applications.

The main difference between digital money and distributed ledgers is that with digital money, funds are stored in a central server, while distributed ledgers are decentralized and stored in a distributed manner across multiple computers. This means that the risk of a single point of failure is much lower, as the data is not stored in one place, and is more secure.

Digital money and distributed ledgers are also more efficient than traditional banking systems. Transactions are faster, more secure, and more cost-effective. This is because they do not require a third-party intermediary to process the transactions, and there is no need for a bank to hold funds.
