
### Basic Crypto Primitives for the Notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Blockchain in the Subject of Blockchain Architecture Design

Crypto primitives are fundamental building blocks of cryptography, which are used to secure data and protect it from unauthorized access. This section will provide an overview of the most commonly used crypto primitives and their applications in blockchain technology.

1. **Hashing**: Hashing is a one-way cryptographic function which takes an input of any size and produces a fixed-length output. It is used to create digital signatures, verify the integrity of data, and detect tampering.

2. **Symmetric Cryptography**: Symmetric cryptography is a type of cryptography where the same key is used to encrypt and decrypt data. It is used to securely exchange data between two or more parties. 

3. **Asymmetric Cryptography**: Asymmetric cryptography is a type of cryptography where a pair of keys is used to encrypt and decrypt data. It is used to securely exchange data between two or more parties without the need to share a secret key.

4. **Digital Signatures**: Digital signatures are used to authenticate the origin of a message and verify its integrity. It is a type of asymmetric cryptography which uses a pair of keys to sign and verify data.

5. **Cryptographic Protocols**: Cryptographic protocols are used to securely exchange data between two or more parties. They use a combination of cryptographic techniques such as hashing, symmetric cryptography, and asymmetric cryptography to ensure data security.

6. **Zero-Knowledge Proofs**: Zero-knowledge proofs are used to prove the validity of a statement without revealing any additional information. They are used to ensure privacy in blockchain transactions.
