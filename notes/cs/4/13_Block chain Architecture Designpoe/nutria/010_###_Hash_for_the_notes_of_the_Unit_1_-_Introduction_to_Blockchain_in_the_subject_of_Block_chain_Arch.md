
### Hash for the Notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Blockchain in the Subject of Block chain Architecture Design

- **Hashing** is a cryptographic technique used to create a unique digital fingerprint of a data set. It is used to create a secure and immutable record of transactions within a blockchain. 
- **Merkle Trees**, also known as hash trees, are data structures used to efficiently store and verify large amounts of data in a blockchain. They are constructed by hashing pairs of data (or transactions) together, and then hashing the result again with another pair of data. 
- **Merkle Root** is the topmost node in a Merkle tree. It is a single hash that is the result of hashing together all of the hashes in the tree. The Merkle root is used to verify that all of the transactions in the tree are valid. 
- **Proof of Work** is a consensus algorithm used to secure a blockchain. It requires miners to solve a cryptographic puzzle in order to add a new block to the chain. 
- **Proof of Stake** is an alternative to the proof of work consensus algorithm. It requires miners to hold a certain amount of coins in order to be eligible to mine a block. 
- **Consensus Mechanisms** are algorithms used to ensure that all nodes in a blockchain network reach consensus on the state of the ledger. Popular consensus mechanisms include proof of work, proof of stake, and delegated proof of stake. 
- **Nodes** are computers that are connected to a blockchain network. They are responsible for verifying transactions and adding new blocks to the chain. 
- **Smart Contracts** are programs that are stored on a blockchain and can be used to automate the execution of certain tasks. They are used to facilitate the exchange of digital assets, such as cryptocurrency, without the need for a third party. 
- **Decentralized Applications (DApps)** are applications that are built on top of a blockchain. They are typically open source and are used to facilitate the exchange of digital assets or to provide services that are not possible with traditional applications.
