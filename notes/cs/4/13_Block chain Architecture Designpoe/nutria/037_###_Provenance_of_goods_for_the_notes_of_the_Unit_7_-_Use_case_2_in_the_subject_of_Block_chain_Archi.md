

### Provenance of Goods for the Notes of the Unit 7 - Use Case 2 in the Subject of Block Chain Architecture Design

* Provenance of goods is a process that is used to track the origin and movements of goods throughout the supply chain. 
* It is based on the principles of blockchain technology, which provides an immutable, secure and transparent record of all the transactions related to the goods.
* Provenance of goods helps to ensure that the goods are genuine and of the highest quality. It also helps to reduce counterfeiting and fraud in the supply chain.
* The process involves the creation of a digital ledger that records all the transactions related to the goods. This ledger is distributed among all the participants in the supply chain.
* The ledger contains information about the origin of the goods, the date of manufacture, the date of delivery, and any other relevant information.
* The ledger is updated in real-time, and all the participants in the supply chain can view the ledger and verify the authenticity of the goods.
* Provenance of goods helps to improve the efficiency of the supply chain by providing a secure and transparent record of all the transactions related to the goods.
* It also helps to reduce costs by eliminating the need for manual verification of the goods.
* Provenance of goods can be used in a variety of industries, including food and beverage, pharmaceuticals, and electronics.
