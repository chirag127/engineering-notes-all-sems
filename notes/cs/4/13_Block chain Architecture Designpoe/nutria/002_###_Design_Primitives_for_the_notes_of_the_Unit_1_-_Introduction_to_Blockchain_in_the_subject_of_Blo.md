
### Design Primitives for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Blockchain in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

Design primitives are the building blocks of blockchain architecture. They are the fundamental components that make up the underlying structure of a blockchain system and provide the basis for the development of applications. 

The main design primitives for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Blockchain in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design are:

* **Consensus Mechanism**: A consensus mechanism is a set of rules that govern how transactions are verified and added to the blockchain. Examples of consensus mechanisms include Proof of Work (PoW), Proof of Stake (PoS), and Delegated Proof of Stake (DPoS).

* **Cryptography**: Cryptography is the practice of using mathematical algorithms to secure data. It is used in blockchain to secure data and ensure that only authorized users can access it.

* **Smart Contracts**: Smart contracts are self-executing contracts that are written in code and stored on the blockchain. They allow users to exchange assets without the need for a third-party intermediary.

* **Distributed Ledger Technology (DLT)**: DLT is a type of technology that stores and records data across multiple computers. It is used in blockchain to ensure that all nodes in the network have access to the same data.

* **Mining**: Mining is the process of verifying and recording transactions on the blockchain. It is done by miners, who are rewarded for their work with cryptocurrency.

* **Scalability**: Scalability is the ability of a blockchain network to handle an increasing number of transactions. It is an important factor when considering the use of blockchain technology for commercial applications.

* **Privacy**: Privacy is an important aspect of blockchain technology. It allows users to keep their data secure and private.

* **Interoperability**: Interoperability is the ability of different blockchain networks to communicate with each other. This allows users to move their assets between different blockchains.

* **Governance**: Governance is the process of managing a blockchain network. It involves setting rules, making decisions, and ensuring that all participants follow the rules.
