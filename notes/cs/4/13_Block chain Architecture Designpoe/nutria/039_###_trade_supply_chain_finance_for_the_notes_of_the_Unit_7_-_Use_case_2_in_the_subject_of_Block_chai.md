
### Trade/Supply Chain Finance for the Notes of the Unit 7 - Use Case 2 in the Subject of Block Chain Architecture Design

* Trade/Supply Chain Finance (SCF) is a financial instrument that allows companies to obtain financing for their trade activities, such as purchasing raw materials, manufacturing products, and selling them to customers.
* SCF is based on the concept of leveraging the trust between buyers and suppliers in a supply chain to obtain financing.
* SCF involves the use of blockchain technology to secure and validate the transactions between buyers and suppliers.
* The main advantage of SCF is that it allows companies to obtain financing without having to rely on traditional banking sources.
* SCF also allows companies to reduce their costs by eliminating the need for intermediaries and other third parties.
* Additionally, SCF provides a secure and transparent environment for conducting transactions, which helps to reduce fraud and increase trust between buyers and suppliers.
* SCF also enables companies to streamline their supply chain operations by providing real-time visibility into the status of their transactions.
* Finally, SCF can be used to facilitate cross-border transactions, which can help companies expand their operations into new markets.
