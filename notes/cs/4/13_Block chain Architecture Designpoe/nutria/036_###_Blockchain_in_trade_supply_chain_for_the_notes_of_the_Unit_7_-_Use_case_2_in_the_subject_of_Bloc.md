
### Blockchain in trade/supply chain for the notes of the Unit 7 - Use case 2 in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

Blockchain technology has the potential to revolutionize the trade and supply chain industries. It is a distributed ledger technology that records and stores all transactions that take place within a network. The technology is secure, immutable, and can be used to create an immutable record of all transactions that take place within a network.

The use of blockchain in trade and supply chain can provide a number of benefits, such as:

- Increased transparency and traceability: Since the ledger is distributed and immutable, it can provide an accurate and up-to-date record of all transactions that take place within a network. This allows for greater transparency and traceability of goods and services, as well as the ability to track the origin and destination of goods.

- Improved security: Blockchain technology is secure and immutable, making it difficult for malicious actors to tamper with the data stored on the ledger. This can help to reduce the risk of fraud and cyber-attacks, as well as ensuring the accuracy of data stored on the ledger.

- Reduced costs: The use of blockchain technology can reduce the cost of transactions, as it eliminates the need for third-party intermediaries and reduces the time taken to complete transactions.

- Improved efficiency: Blockchain technology can help to streamline and automate the process of trade and supply chain management, as it eliminates the need for manual processes and reduces the time taken to complete transactions.
