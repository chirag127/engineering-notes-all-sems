
### Basic Consensus Mechanisms for the Notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Blockchain in the Subject of Block Chain Architecture Design

* **Proof of Work (PoW):** PoW is a consensus algorithm that requires the miners to solve a complex mathematical puzzle in order to validate a transaction and add it to the blockchain. The miners are rewarded for their efforts with a certain amount of cryptocurrency. The difficulty of the puzzle is adjusted in order to maintain a steady rate of block production.

* **Proof of Stake (PoS):** PoS is a consensus algorithm that requires miners to stake a certain amount of cryptocurrency in order to validate transactions and add them to the blockchain. The miners are rewarded for their efforts with a certain amount of cryptocurrency. The reward is proportional to the amount of cryptocurrency staked.

* **Delegated Proof of Stake (DPoS):** DPoS is a consensus algorithm that allows users to delegate their voting power to a delegate. The delegate is responsible for validating transactions and adding them to the blockchain. The delegate is rewarded for their efforts with a certain amount of cryptocurrency.

* **Byzantine Fault Tolerance (BFT):** BFT is a consensus algorithm that requires a certain number of nodes in a network to agree on a transaction before it is added to the blockchain. This algorithm is used to ensure the validity of transactions and to prevent double-spending.

* **Mnemonics and Learning Tricks:**
  * PoW = Proof of Work = Mining
  * PoS = Proof of Stake = Staking
  * DPoS = Delegated Proof of Stake = Delegated Staking
  * BFT = Byzantine Fault Tolerance = Agreement
