
### Requirements for the consensus protocols for the notes of the Unit 2 - Consensus in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

* Consensus protocols are algorithms used to ensure that all participants in a distributed system agree on a single state.
* The most common consensus protocols are Proof of Work (PoW), Proof of Stake (PoS), and Byzantine Fault Tolerance (BFT).
* PoW requires miners to solve a difficult mathematical puzzle in order to add a new block to the blockchain.
* PoS requires users to stake their coins in order to participate in the consensus process.
* BFT is a consensus protocol that is resilient to faults and can tolerate up to one-third of the participants being malicious.
* Other consensus protocols include Paxos, Raft, and Tendermint.
* All consensus protocols have their own advantages and disadvantages, and the best protocol for a given use case depends on the requirements of the system.
