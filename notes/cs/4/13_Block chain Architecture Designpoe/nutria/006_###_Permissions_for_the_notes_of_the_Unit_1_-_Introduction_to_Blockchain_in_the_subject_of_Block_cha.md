
### Permissions for the Notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Blockchain in the Subject of Block Chain Architecture Design

Permissions in blockchain networks are important for ensuring that users are able to access the resources they need, while also preventing unauthorized users from accessing sensitive data. Blockchain networks are designed to provide users with access to their data and resources in a secure, private, and trustless manner. 

The following are some of the key concepts related to permissions in blockchain networks:

1. **Access Control:** Access control is the process of controlling access to a resource or system. In blockchain networks, access control is used to ensure that only authorized users can access sensitive data or resources.

2. **Identity Management:** Identity management is the process of managing an individual's digital identity. In blockchain networks, identity management is used to ensure that users can securely access the network and their resources.

3. **Permissioned Ledgers:** Permissioned ledgers are ledgers that are only accessible to authorized users. In blockchain networks, permissioned ledgers are used to ensure that only authorized users can access the network and its resources.

4. **Cryptographic Keys:** Cryptographic keys are used to authenticate users and provide access to resources. In blockchain networks, cryptographic keys are used to ensure that only authorized users can access the network and its resources.

5. **Smart Contracts:** Smart contracts are computer programs that are used to automate transactions and enforce rules. In blockchain networks, smart contracts are used to ensure that transactions are executed in a secure and trustless manner.

By understanding these key concepts, users will be able to better understand the permissions system in blockchain networks and ensure that their data and resources are secure.
