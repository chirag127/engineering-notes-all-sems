
### Design goals for the notes of the Unit 3 - Permissioned Blockchains in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

1. **Scalability**: Permissioned blockchains should be able to scale to meet the needs of large numbers of users and transactions.
2. **Security**: Permissioned blockchains should be secure and robust against malicious actors and attacks.
3. **Privacy**: Permissioned blockchains should provide privacy for its users and transactions.
4. **Consensus**: Permissioned blockchains should enable consensus among its users to ensure the validity of transactions and the accuracy of the ledger.
5. **Interoperability**: Permissioned blockchains should enable interoperability between different blockchains to enable the transfer of data between them.
6. **Flexibility**: Permissioned blockchains should be flexible to accommodate different types of users, applications, and use cases.
7. **Auditability**: Permissioned blockchains should be auditable to ensure that the ledger is accurate and up to date.
8. **Regulatory Compliance**: Permissioned blockchains should be compliant with applicable laws and regulations.
9. **Usability**: Permissioned blockchains should be user-friendly and easy to use.
