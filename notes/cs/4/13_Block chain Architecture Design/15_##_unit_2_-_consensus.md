## Unit 2 - Consensus

Consensus refers to the process by which all participants in a decentralized network agree on a single version of a shared digital history. In blockchain technology, consensus algorithms are used to validate transactions and add them to the blockchain. The most common consensus algorithms are Proof of Work (PoW) and Proof of Stake (PoS). PoW requires nodes to perform a computational task to validate transactions and add blocks to the blockchain, while PoS requires nodes to hold a certain amount of cryptocurrency to validate transactions and add blocks. Other consensus algorithms include Delegated Proof of Stake (DPoS) and Byzantine Fault Tolerance (BFT). The choice of consensus algorithm depends on the specific requirements of the network, such as security, scalability, and energy efficiency.
