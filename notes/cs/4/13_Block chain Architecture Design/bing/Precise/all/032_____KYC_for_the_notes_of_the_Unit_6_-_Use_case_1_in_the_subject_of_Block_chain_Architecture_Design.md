# KYC (Know Your Customer) - Unit 6, Use Case 1: Blockchain Architecture Design

KYC is the process of verifying the identity of customers and assessing their potential risks for money laundering or financing of terrorism. It is a regulatory requirement for financial institutions and other regulated companies.

1. KYC involves collecting and verifying customer identification information, such as name, date of birth, address, and identification numbers.
2. The process also includes monitoring customer transactions and behavior to identify any suspicious activity.
3. KYC is important for preventing financial crimes and ensuring compliance with anti-money laundering (AML) and counter-terrorist financing (CTF) regulations.
4. Blockchain technology can be used to improve the KYC process by providing a secure and transparent platform for storing and sharing customer information.
5. This can reduce the time and cost of the KYC process for both customers and financial institutions, while also improving the accuracy and reliability of the information.
6. Blockchain-based KYC solutions can also help to prevent identity theft and fraud by providing a tamper-proof record of customer information.
