### Signature for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Blockchain in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

1. Blockchain is a distributed database that maintains a continuously growing list of records called blocks.
2. Each block contains a timestamp and a link to a previous block, forming a chain of blocks.
3. The data in a block cannot be altered retroactively without the alteration of all subsequent blocks and the consensus of the network.
4. Blockchain technology was first introduced in 2008 with the release of the Bitcoin whitepaper by an individual or group using the pseudonym Satoshi Nakamoto.
5. Blockchain technology has since been used for a wide range of applications, including cryptocurrencies, smart contracts, and supply chain management.
6. The key features of blockchain technology include decentralization, immutability, and transparency.
7. Decentralization refers to the distribution of power and decision-making among the participants in the network, rather than being controlled by a central authority.
8. Immutability refers to the inability to change or delete data once it has been recorded on the blockchain.
9. Transparency refers to the ability for all participants in the network to view and verify the transactions recorded on the blockchain.
