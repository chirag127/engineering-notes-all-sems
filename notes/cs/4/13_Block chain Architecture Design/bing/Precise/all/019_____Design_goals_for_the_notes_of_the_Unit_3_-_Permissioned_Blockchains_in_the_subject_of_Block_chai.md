# Unit 3 - Permissioned Blockchains

### Design Goals

1. **Security:** Permissioned blockchains must be designed to ensure the security of the data and transactions stored on the blockchain. This includes measures to prevent unauthorized access, tampering, and fraud.

2. **Privacy:** Permissioned blockchains must be designed to protect the privacy of the users and their data. This includes measures to ensure that sensitive information is not disclosed to unauthorized parties.

3. **Scalability:** Permissioned blockchains must be designed to scale to accommodate a large number of users and transactions. This includes measures to ensure that the blockchain can handle high volumes of data and transactions without slowing down or becoming congested.

4. **Efficiency:** Permissioned blockchains must be designed to be efficient in terms of the resources required to operate the blockchain. This includes measures to minimize the computational power, storage, and bandwidth required to operate the blockchain.

5. **Interoperability:** Permissioned blockchains must be designed to be interoperable with other systems and technologies. This includes measures to ensure that the blockchain can easily integrate with other systems and technologies, such as other blockchains, databases, and APIs.

6. **Governance:** Permissioned blockchains must be designed with a clear governance model that defines the roles and responsibilities of the various parties involved in the operation of the blockchain. This includes measures to ensure that the blockchain is operated in a transparent and accountable manner.

7. **Compliance:** Permissioned blockchains must be designed to comply with relevant laws and regulations. This includes measures to ensure that the blockchain complies with data protection, anti-money laundering, and other relevant regulations.
