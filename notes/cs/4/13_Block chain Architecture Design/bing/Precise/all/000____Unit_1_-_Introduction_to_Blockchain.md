## Unit 1 - Introduction to Blockchain

1. **Definition:** Blockchain is a distributed database that maintains a continuously growing list of records called blocks, which are linked and secured using cryptography.
2. **History:** The concept of blockchain was first introduced in 2008 by an individual or group of individuals using the pseudonym Satoshi Nakamoto, as part of the original Bitcoin whitepaper.
3. **Key Features:** Some of the key features of blockchain technology include decentralization, immutability, and transparency.
4. **Applications:** Blockchain technology has a wide range of applications, including but not limited to, cryptocurrency, supply chain management, voting systems, and identity management.
5. **Benefits:** The benefits of using blockchain technology include increased security, reduced costs, and improved efficiency.
