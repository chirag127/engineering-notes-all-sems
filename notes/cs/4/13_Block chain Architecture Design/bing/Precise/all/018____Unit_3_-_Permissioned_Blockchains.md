## Unit 3 - Permissioned Blockchains

1. **Definition:** A permissioned blockchain is a type of blockchain where access to the network is restricted to a pre-approved set of participants. 
2. **Access Control:** In a permissioned blockchain, the network administrator has the ability to control who can participate in the network, and what actions they are allowed to perform. 
3. **Use Cases:** Permissioned blockchains are often used in enterprise or business settings, where there is a need for greater control over the network and its participants. 
4. **Examples:** Some examples of permissioned blockchains include Hyperledger Fabric, Corda, and Quorum. 
5. **Advantages:** Permissioned blockchains offer several advantages over public blockchains, including faster transaction speeds, greater scalability, and improved privacy. 
6. **Disadvantages:** One potential disadvantage of permissioned blockchains is that they may be less decentralized than public blockchains, as control is concentrated in the hands of a smaller group of participants.
