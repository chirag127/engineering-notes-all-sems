### Basic consensus mechanisms

A consensus mechanism is any method used to achieve agreement, trust, and security across a decentralized computer network. In the context of blockchains and cryptocurrencies, proof-of-work (PoW) and proof-of-stake (PoS) are two of the most prevalent consensus mechanisms .

1. **Proof-of-Work (PoW)**: This consensus mechanism was invented by the creator of Bitcoin, Satoshi Nakamoto. It involves solving complex mathematical problems to validate transactions and create new blocks. This process is known as mining and requires a significant amount of computing power.

2. **Proof-of-Stake (PoS)**: This consensus mechanism is an alternative to PoW and is used by cryptocurrencies such as Ethereum. Instead of using computing power to validate transactions, PoS uses the amount of cryptocurrency held by a user as a means of determining their ability to create new blocks. This process is known as staking and requires users to hold a certain amount of cryptocurrency in their wallet.

Consensus mechanisms play an essential part in securing information by encrypting it and using automated group verification . They are the protocols, algorithms, or other computer systems that allow cryptocurrencies to work .
