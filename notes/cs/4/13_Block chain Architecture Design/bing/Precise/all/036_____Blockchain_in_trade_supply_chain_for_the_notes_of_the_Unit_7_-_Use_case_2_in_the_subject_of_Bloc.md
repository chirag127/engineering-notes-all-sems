# Blockchain in Trade/Supply Chain

Blockchain technology has the potential to revolutionize the trade and supply chain industry by improving transparency, traceability, and reducing administrative costs. Here are some ways in which blockchain can be used in trade and supply chain:

1. **Supply Chain Transparency and Traceability**: Using blockchain in the supply chain can help participants record price, date, location, quality, certification, and other relevant information to more effectively manage the supply chain. This can improve supply chain transparency and traceability.

2. **Improved Efficiency**: Blockchain relies on a shared network infrastructure, which can improve efficiency in the supply chain. Companies using blockchain technology can expect to see several key benefits, including improved efficiency.

3. **Decentralized Ledger**: The core technology of the blockchain is the decentralized ledger, which records and protects transaction data shared among multiple parties. This can help in changing and improving supply chains.

4. **No Middle Man**: Blockchain in supply chain can help make partners of businesses agree upon and share important pieces of information without involving a middle man to check on everything. Blockchains synchronizes transactions and data along with the whole network, and all the participants verify the calculations and works of the others.

5. **Increased Trust**: Blockchain provides transparency and increases trust with multi-party visibility of digital events across your supply chain. At a time of uncertainty, companies are looking to emerging technologies to improve data quality, integrity, and visibility.

These are some of the ways in which blockchain technology can be used in trade and supply chain to improve efficiency, transparency, and trust. Blockchain carries the potential to change the world, from finance to supply chain management.
