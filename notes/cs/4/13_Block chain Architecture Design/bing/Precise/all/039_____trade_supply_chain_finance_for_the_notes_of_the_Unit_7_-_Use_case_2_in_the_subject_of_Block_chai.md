# Unit 7 - Use Case 2: Trade/Supply Chain Finance

Trade finance refers to the financial instruments and products that are used by companies to facilitate international trade and commerce. Supply chain finance, on the other hand, is a set of technology-based business and financing processes that link the various parties in a transaction, including the buyer, seller, and financing institution, to lower financing costs and improve business efficiency.

Here are some key points to consider when studying trade/supply chain finance in the context of blockchain architecture design:

1. Blockchain technology can provide a secure and transparent platform for the exchange of trade documents and financing information between parties in a trade finance transaction.

2. Smart contracts can be used to automate the execution of trade finance agreements, reducing the need for intermediaries and lowering transaction costs.

3. Blockchain-based platforms can provide real-time visibility into the status of trade finance transactions, improving the ability of companies to manage their working capital and cash flow.

4. The use of blockchain technology in trade finance can help to reduce fraud and increase trust between parties, as all transactions are recorded on a tamper-proof ledger.

5. Blockchain-based trade finance solutions can provide greater access to financing for small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs), which often face challenges in obtaining trade finance from traditional financial institutions.

6. The use of blockchain technology in supply chain finance can help to improve the efficiency of the supply chain by providing real-time visibility into the movement of goods and the status of payments.

7. Blockchain-based supply chain finance solutions can also help to reduce the risk of disputes between parties, as all transactions are recorded on a tamper-proof ledger.

Overall, the use of blockchain technology in trade and supply chain finance has the potential to significantly improve the efficiency and transparency of these processes, while also reducing costs and increasing access to financing for SMEs. It is an important use case to consider when studying blockchain architecture design.
