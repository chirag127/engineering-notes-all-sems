### Unit 7 - Use Case 2: Trade/Supply Chain Finance

1. Trade finance refers to the financing of international trade, including the provision of credit, insurance, and guarantees to facilitate the movement of goods and services between buyers and sellers.
2. Supply chain finance, on the other hand, refers to the use of financial instruments, practices, and technologies to optimize the management of working capital and liquidity tied up in supply chain processes.
3. Blockchain technology can be used to improve the efficiency and transparency of trade and supply chain finance by providing a secure and immutable record of transactions and enabling real-time tracking of goods and payments.
4. Some potential benefits of using blockchain in trade and supply chain finance include reduced costs, faster processing times, and improved risk management.
5. Several initiatives are underway to explore the use of blockchain in trade and supply chain finance, including projects by major banks, shipping companies, and technology firms.
