## Unit 7 - Use case 2

- Use case 2 is a description of a system's behavior as it responds to a request from one of its stakeholders.
- The request is known as a use case, and the system's behavior is captured in a sequence of interactions between the system and its actors.
- Actors are entities that interact with the system, such as users or other systems.
- A use case diagram is a visual representation of the relationships between the use cases and actors in a system.
- Use case 2 is typically used to capture functional requirements and to define the scope of a system.
- It is important to note that use case 2 is not a complete specification of the system's behavior, but rather a high-level view of the system's intended functionality.
- Use case 2 is an effective tool for communicating with stakeholders and for ensuring that the system meets their needs.
- It is also useful for identifying potential problems and for verifying that the system is complete and consistent.
