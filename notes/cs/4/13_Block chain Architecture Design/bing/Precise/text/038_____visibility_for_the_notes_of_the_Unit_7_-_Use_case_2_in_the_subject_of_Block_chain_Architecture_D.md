### Visibility for the notes of the Unit 7 - Use case 2 in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

1. Visibility refers to the ability to view and track transactions and data on a blockchain network.
2. In the context of blockchain architecture design, visibility is an important consideration for use case 2 of unit 7.
3. The level of visibility required will depend on the specific use case and the needs of the parties involved.
4. For example, in a supply chain use case, visibility may be important for tracking the movement of goods and ensuring transparency and accountability.
5. On the other hand, in a financial use case, privacy may be more important and visibility may need to be restricted to certain parties.
6. The design of the blockchain architecture should take into account the desired level of visibility and provide appropriate mechanisms for controlling access to data.
7. This can be achieved through the use of permissioned blockchains, where access to data is controlled by a central authority, or through the use of privacy-enhancing technologies such as zero-knowledge proofs.
8. In summary, visibility is an important consideration in the design of blockchain architecture and should be carefully balanced with the need for privacy and security.
