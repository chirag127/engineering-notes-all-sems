### Design goals for the notes of the Unit 3 - Permissioned Blockchains in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

1. To provide a comprehensive understanding of the concept of permissioned blockchains and their role in the blockchain ecosystem.
2. To explain the key differences between permissioned and permissionless blockchains and their respective use cases.
3. To explore the various types of permissioned blockchains and their underlying consensus mechanisms.
4. To discuss the benefits and limitations of using permissioned blockchains in various industries and applications.
5. To provide practical examples and case studies of permissioned blockchain implementations in real-world scenarios.
6. To equip students with the knowledge and skills to design and implement permissioned blockchain solutions in their own projects.
