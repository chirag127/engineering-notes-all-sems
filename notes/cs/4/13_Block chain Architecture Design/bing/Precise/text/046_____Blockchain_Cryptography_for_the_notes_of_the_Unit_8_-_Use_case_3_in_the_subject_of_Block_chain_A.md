### Blockchain Cryptography

- Cryptography is a method of securing data from unauthorized access. In the blockchain, cryptography is used to secure transactions taking place between two nodes in a blockchain network.
- A blockchain is a distributed ledger with growing lists of records (blocks) that are securely linked together via cryptographic hashes.
- Each block contains a cryptographic hash of the previous block, a timestamp, and transaction data (generally represented as a Merkle tree, where data nodes are represented by leaves).
- Blockchain security methods include the use of public-key cryptography.
- Cryptography and hashing are two main concepts in a blockchain.
