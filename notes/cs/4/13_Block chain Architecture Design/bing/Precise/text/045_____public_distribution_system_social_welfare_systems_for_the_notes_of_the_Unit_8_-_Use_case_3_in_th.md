### Public Distribution System and Social Welfare Systems

1. Public distribution system is one of the key areas in which blockchain plays a major role .
2. Blockchain is an emerging technology which is applied in all real-world transactions to provide security and transparency .
3. An automated public food distribution system can be developed using blockchain technology and the Solidity language .
4. A blockchain-based system can be used to record all transactions and log details of these events .
5. Blockchain’s influence in the public sector has the potential to bring security, efficiency, and speed to a wide range of services and processes .
