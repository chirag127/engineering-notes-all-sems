### Digital Money to Distributed Ledgers

1. Digital currencies retain the functions of money, including being a store of value, a unit of account, and a medium of exchange.
2. A distinct feature of digital currency is its functioning distributed ledger, which allows it to function in a decentralized payment system.
3. One example of a digital currency that uses a distributed ledger is Bitcoin.
4. Distributed ledger technology (DLT) has evolved to provide other purposes such as record management and process automation.
5. A distributed ledger is a type of database that keeps multiple copies of information in different locations and can update consistently.
6. The global value of central bank digital currencies (CBDCs) is expected to grow dramatically from $100 million today to $213 billion by 2030.
7. DLT could fundamentally change the financial sector, making it more efficient, resilient, and reliable.
8. DLT has the potential to address persistent challenges in the financial sector and change the roles of financial sector stakeholders.
