### Unit 3 - Permissioned Blockchains

#### Design Goals for Permissioned Blockchains

1. **Controlled Access:** Permissioned blockchains are designed to allow only authorized participants to join the network and perform transactions. This is achieved through the use of access control mechanisms such as digital signatures and certificates.

2. **Privacy:** Permissioned blockchains aim to provide privacy to their participants by allowing them to keep their identities and transaction details confidential. This is achieved through the use of cryptographic techniques such as zero-knowledge proofs and confidential transactions.

3. **Scalability:** Permissioned blockchains are designed to be scalable, meaning they can handle a large number of transactions and participants without sacrificing performance. This is achieved through the use of efficient consensus algorithms and sharding techniques.

4. **Interoperability:** Permissioned blockchains aim to be interoperable, meaning they can communicate and exchange data with other blockchains and external systems. This is achieved through the use of standardized protocols and APIs.

5. **Compliance:** Permissioned blockchains are designed to comply with regulatory requirements and industry standards. This is achieved through the use of built-in compliance mechanisms and regular audits.

6. **Security:** Permissioned blockchains aim to provide a high level of security to their participants by protecting against malicious attacks and unauthorized access. This is achieved through the use of advanced cryptographic techniques and robust network architecture.
