### Protocols for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Blockchain in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

1. Blockchain is a distributed database that maintains a continuously growing list of records called blocks.
2. Each block contains a timestamp and a link to a previous block.
3. Once recorded, the data in any given block cannot be altered retroactively without the alteration of all subsequent blocks.
4. Blockchain technology is often associated with the cryptocurrency, Bitcoin, but it has many other applications.
5. Blockchain protocols are designed to be secure, transparent, and tamper-proof.
6. Some common blockchain protocols include Bitcoin, Ethereum, and Ripple.
7. These protocols use cryptographic algorithms to ensure the security and integrity of the data stored on the blockchain.
8. Blockchain technology has the potential to revolutionize many industries by enabling secure, decentralized, and transparent record-keeping.
