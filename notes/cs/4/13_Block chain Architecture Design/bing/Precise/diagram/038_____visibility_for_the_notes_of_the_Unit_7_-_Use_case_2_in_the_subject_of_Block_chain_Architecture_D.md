### Visibility for the notes of the Unit 7 - Use case 2 in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

- Visibility refers to the ability to view and track transactions and data on a blockchain.
- In a public blockchain, all transactions and data are visible to anyone with access to the network.
- In a private blockchain, visibility can be restricted to specific participants or groups.
- Visibility can be controlled through the use of permissions and access controls.
- In the context of Use Case 2, visibility is important for ensuring transparency and accountability in the system.
- By allowing all participants to view and track transactions, visibility can help prevent fraud and other malicious activities.
- Visibility can also facilitate auditing and regulatory compliance.
- In designing the blockchain architecture, it is important to consider the appropriate level of visibility for the specific use case and the needs of the participants.
