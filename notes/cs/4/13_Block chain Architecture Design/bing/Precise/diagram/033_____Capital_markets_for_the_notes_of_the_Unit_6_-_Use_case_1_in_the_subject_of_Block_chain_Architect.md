### Capital Markets and Blockchain Architecture Design

- Blockchain technology has the potential to improve the efficiency of various capital markets.
- In the coming years, there will likely be increased blockchain scalability and improvements in the UI/UX design of blockchain-based products.
- Blockchain technology has fundamentally changed the way financial institutions are exchanging value and building market infrastructure.
- Distributed ledger technology (DLT), of which blockchain is the most common architecture, has moved from the margins to mainstream adoption.
- In the capital markets, DLT has been regarded as a disruptive threat to existing players, a solution to inefficiencies arising from legacy technologies and operations, and a solution in search of a problem.
- Blockchain offers a new approach to data management and sharing that is being proposed as a solution to many of the inefficiencies afflicting the industry.
