## Unit 1 - Introduction to Blockchain

1. **Definition:** Blockchain is a distributed database that maintains a continuously growing list of records, called blocks, which are linked and secured using cryptography.
2. **History:** The concept of blockchain was first introduced in 2008 by an individual or group of individuals using the pseudonym Satoshi Nakamoto, as part of the original Bitcoin whitepaper.
3. **Key Features:**
    - **Decentralization:** Blockchain technology allows for the distribution of digital information without the need for a central authority.
    - **Transparency:** All participants in a blockchain network have access to the same information, making it transparent and auditable.
    - **Immutability:** Once data is recorded on a blockchain, it is nearly impossible to alter or delete, ensuring the integrity of the information.
    - **Security:** Blockchain uses cryptographic algorithms to secure the data, making it difficult for unauthorized parties to access or manipulate the information.
4. **Applications:** Blockchain technology has numerous applications, including but not limited to: cryptocurrency, supply chain management, voting systems, and identity management.
