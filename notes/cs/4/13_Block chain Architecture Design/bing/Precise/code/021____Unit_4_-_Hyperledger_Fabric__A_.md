## Unit 4 - Hyperledger Fabric (A)

Hyperledger Fabric is a blockchain framework implementation and one of the Hyperledger projects hosted by The Linux Foundation. It is designed to be a foundation for developing applications or solutions with a modular architecture. Some key features of Hyperledger Fabric include:

1. **Modular architecture:** Hyperledger Fabric has a modular and extensible architecture, allowing components such as consensus and membership services to be plug-and-play.

2. **Chaincode functionality:** Chaincode is the term used for smart contracts in Hyperledger Fabric. It allows for the development of custom business logic that can be invoked by transactions on the network.

3. **Privacy and confidentiality:** Hyperledger Fabric allows for the creation of private channels, where only the parties involved in a specific transaction can see the details of that transaction.

4. **Scalability and performance:** Hyperledger Fabric is designed to scale and support high transaction throughput, making it suitable for enterprise use cases.

Hyperledger Fabric is a popular choice for building enterprise blockchain solutions due to its modular architecture, support for custom business logic, and privacy and confidentiality features. It is used in a variety of industries, including finance, supply chain, and healthcare.
