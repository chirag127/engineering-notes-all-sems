### Digital Identity - Unit 8, Use Case 3: Blockchain Architecture Design

Digital identity refers to the online representation of an individual, organization, or entity. It encompasses all the information and attributes associated with that entity in the digital world. Digital identity can include personal information such as name, age, and address, as well as online behavior, preferences, and interactions.

Some key points to consider when discussing digital identity in the context of blockchain architecture design are:

1. Blockchain technology can provide a secure and decentralized way to store and manage digital identities.
2. By using blockchain, individuals can have more control over their personal information and how it is shared.
3. Blockchain-based digital identity solutions can help reduce the risk of identity theft and fraud.
4. Digital identity on the blockchain can be used for various use cases, such as identity verification, access control, and secure data sharing.
5. Blockchain can enable self-sovereign identity, where individuals have full control over their digital identity and can choose what information to share and with whom.
