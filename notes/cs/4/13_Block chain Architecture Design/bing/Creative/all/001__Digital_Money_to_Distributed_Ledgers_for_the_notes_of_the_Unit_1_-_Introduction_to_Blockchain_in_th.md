### Digital Money to Distributed Ledgers for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Blockchain in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

- Digital money is a form of electronic currency that can be transferred peer-to-peer without the need for intermediaries such as banks or clearinghouses.
- Distributed ledgers are databases that are shared, replicated, and synchronized among the members of a decentralized network, without a central authority or mediator.
- Blockchain is one type of distributed ledger that organizes data into blocks, which are chained together in an append-only mode, and secured by cryptographic signatures.
- Blockchain is the technology that underlies Bitcoin, the first and most well-known digital currency, as well as other cryptocurrencies and applications.
- Blockchain and distributed ledger technology (DLT) have the potential to transform various sectors and industries, such as finance, trade, supply chain, energy, and government, by enabling more efficient, resilient, and reliable transactions and record-keeping.
- Blockchain and DLT also pose various challenges and risks, such as consumer protection, financial integrity, scalability, environmental impact, legal, regulatory, and technological issues, that need to be addressed and resolved.

Some possible mnemonics and learning tricks for this topic are:

- To remember the three functions of money, think of SUM: Store of value, Unit of account, Medium of exchange.
- To remember the difference between blockchain and distributed ledger, think of BAD: Blockchain is A type of Distributed ledger.
- To remember the four main components of a blockchain, think of LANT: Ledger, Algorithm, Network, and Transactions.
