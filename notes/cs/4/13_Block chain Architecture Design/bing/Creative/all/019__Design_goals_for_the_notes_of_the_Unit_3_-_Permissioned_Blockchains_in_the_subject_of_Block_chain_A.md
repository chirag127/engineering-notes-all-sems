### Design goals for the notes of the Unit 3 - Permissioned Blockchains in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

- A permissioned blockchain is a secure distributed ledger maintained by a number of trusted validation nodes that can grant or revoke access to participants. 
- The design goals for permissioned blockchains are:
  - To achieve consensus among the validation nodes in the presence of faults or attacks. 
  - To provide transparency and accountability for the transactions and the state of the ledger. 
  - To ensure data security and privacy for the participants and the ledger. 
  - To optimize the performance and scalability of the system. 
  - To support the business objectives and requirements of the organization or the network. 
- Some of the design issues and trade-offs for permissioned blockchains are:
  - The choice of the consensus protocol and the number and selection of the validation nodes. 
  - The size and frequency of the blocks and the window size of block creation time. 
  - The level of access and control for different types of participants and the governance model. 
  - The data structure and format of the ledger and the transactions. 
  - The integration and interoperability with other systems and platforms.
