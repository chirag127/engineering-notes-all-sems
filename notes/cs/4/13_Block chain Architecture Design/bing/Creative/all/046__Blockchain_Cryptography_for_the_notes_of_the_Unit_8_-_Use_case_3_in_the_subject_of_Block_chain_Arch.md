### Blockchain Cryptography for the notes of the Unit 8 - Use case 3 in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

- Blockchain cryptography is the use of digital encryption technologies to secure transactions and data on the blockchain network.
- Blockchain cryptography relies on two main concepts: cryptography and hashing.
- Cryptography is the process of converting plain text into cipher text and vice versa, using mathematical algorithms and keys.
- Hashing is the process of generating a fixed-length output from any input, using a one-way mathematical function.
- Cryptography and hashing are used in different layers of the blockchain, such as the application layer and the consensus layer.
- Some of the common types of cryptography used in blockchain are as follows :

  - Symmetric-key cryptography: This type of cryptography uses the same key for encryption and decryption. It is fast and efficient, but it requires a secure way of sharing the key among the parties. An example of symmetric-key cryptography is the Advanced Encryption Standard (AES).
  - Asymmetric-key cryptography: This type of cryptography uses a pair of keys: a public key and a private key. The public key can be shared with anyone, while the private key is kept secret by the owner. The public key can be used to encrypt a message, and the private key can be used to decrypt it. Alternatively, the private key can be used to sign a message, and the public key can be used to verify it. This ensures confidentiality, integrity, and authenticity of the messages. An example of asymmetric-key cryptography is the Elliptic Curve Digital Signature Algorithm (ECDSA).
  - Hash-based cryptography: This type of cryptography uses hashing functions to generate keys, signatures, and proofs. It is resistant to quantum attacks, as it does not rely on mathematical problems that can be solved by quantum computers. An example of hash-based cryptography is the Lamport signature scheme.

- Some of the common use cases of cryptography in blockchain are as follows  :

  - Encryption and decryption of messages: Cryptography is used to encrypt messages between nodes in the blockchain network, ensuring confidentiality and privacy. For example, in Bitcoin, nodes use public-key cryptography to encrypt and decrypt transactions.
  - Authentication and verification of identities: Cryptography is used to authenticate identities and verify credentials on the blockchain network, preventing identity theft and fraud. For example, in Ethereum, nodes use public-key cryptography to generate addresses and sign transactions.
  - Generation and validation of signatures: Cryptography is used to generate and validate signatures on the blockchain network, ensuring integrity and non-repudiation. For example, in Hyperledger Fabric, nodes use public-key cryptography to sign and endorse transactions.
  - Creation and linking of blocks: Cryptography is used to create and link blocks on the blockchain network, ensuring immutability and traceability. For example, in Bitcoin, nodes use hashing to generate block identifiers and link them in a chain.
  - Consensus and validation of transactions: Cryptography is used to achieve consensus and validate transactions on the blockchain network, ensuring reliability and security. For example, in Bitcoin, nodes use hashing and proof-of-work to reach agreement and confirm transactions.

- Some of the advantages of cryptography in blockchain are as follows :

  - It enhances the security and resilience of the blockchain network, as it eliminates the single point of failure and protects against attacks, corruption, and downtime.
  - It enables the transparency and accountability of the blockchain network, as it allows anyone to verify the transactions and data on the blockchain.
  - It facilitates the efficiency and scalability of the blockchain network, as it reduces the need for intermediaries and central authorities, and enables faster and cheaper transactions.

- Some of the disadvantages of cryptography in blockchain are as follows:

  - It introduces the complexity and cost of the blockchain network, as it requires sophisticated algorithms and keys, and consumes a lot of computational resources and energy.
  - It poses the challenges and risks of the blockchain network, as it depends on the design and implementation of the cryptography, and may be vulnerable to human errors, biases, subversions, and malicious attacks.
