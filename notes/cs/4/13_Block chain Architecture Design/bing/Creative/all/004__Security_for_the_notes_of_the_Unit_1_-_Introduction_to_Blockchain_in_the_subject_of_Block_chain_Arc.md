### Security for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Blockchain in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

- Blockchain security is a comprehensive risk management system for a blockchain network, using cybersecurity frameworks, assurance services and best practices to reduce risks against attacks and fraud.
- Blockchain technology produces a structure of data with inherent security qualities. It's based on principles of cryptography, decentralization and consensus, which ensure trust in transactions.
- Cryptography is the main security system of the blockchain. It allows the storing and exchanging of data in a format hidden from third parties and only the person who has the technological permission to unravel the meaning of the message can receive and decode the message sent to them.
- Blockchain networks can differ in who can participate and who has access to the data. Networks are typically labeled as either public or private, which describes who is allowed to participate, and permissioned or permissionless, which describes how participants gain access to the network.
- Public blockchain networks typically allow anyone to join and for participants to remain anonymous. A public blockchain uses internet-connected computers to validate transactions and achieve consensus. Bitcoin is probably the most well-known example of a public blockchain, and it achieves consensus through "bitcoin mining."
- Private blockchains use identity to confirm membership and access privileges and typically only permit known organizations to join. Together, the organizations form a private, members-only "business network." A private blockchain in a permissioned network achieves consensus through a process called "selective endorsement," where known users verify the transactions.
- Blockchain security differs by blockchain types. Public and permissionless networks can achieve greater decentralization and distribution, but they also face more challenges in terms of scalability, privacy, and governance. Private and permissioned networks can be tightly controlled and preferable for compliance and regulatory reasons, but they also require more identity and access controls and trust among the participants.
- Blockchain security also depends on the design and implementation of the blockchain solution or network. Some of the factors that affect blockchain security are: the choice of consensus mechanism, the use of smart contracts, the integration with external systems, the management of cryptographic keys, the protection of user data, and the monitoring and auditing of network activities .
- Blockchain security is not only a technical issue, but also a social and economic one. Blockchain security requires the collaboration and coordination of multiple stakeholders, such as developers, users, regulators, and service providers. Blockchain security also involves the trade-offs and incentives of different actors, such as miners, validators, and attackers .
- Blockchain security is an evolving and dynamic field, as new threats and solutions emerge constantly. Blockchain security requires continuous learning and adaptation, as well as the adoption of standards and best practices. Blockchain security also benefits from the research and innovation of the scientific community, as well as the feedback and participation of the industry and the public .

Mnemonics and learning tricks:

- To remember the three principles of blockchain security, use the acronym CDC: Cryptography, Decentralization, Consensus.
- To remember the four types of blockchain networks, use the acronym PPPP: Public, Private, Permissioned, Permissionless.
- To remember some of the factors that affect blockchain security, use the acronym SCREAM: Smart contracts, Consensus mechanism, External systems, Access controls, Monitoring and auditing.
