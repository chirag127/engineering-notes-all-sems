### Permissions for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Blockchain in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

- A permissioned blockchain is a type of blockchain that is not open to everyone, but only to authorized users who have been granted specific roles and permissions by the network administrators  .
- A permissioned blockchain can be seen as a hybrid of a public blockchain and a private blockchain, as it combines some features of both.
- A public blockchain is a blockchain that anyone can join and participate in, without needing any permission or identification. A public blockchain is decentralized and transparent, but also consumes a lot of energy and resources.
- A private blockchain is a blockchain that is restricted to a selected group of users, usually within a single organization or entity. A private blockchain is centralized and efficient, but also lacks transparency and accountability.
- A permissioned blockchain can offer the following advantages over a public or a private blockchain  :
  - It can provide a higher level of security and privacy, as only authorized users can access and verify the transactions on the network.
  - It can allow for more scalability and performance, as the network does not have to process every transaction from every user, but only those that are relevant to the participants.
  - It can enable more flexibility and customization, as the network can be configured to suit the specific needs and requirements of the use case and the stakeholders.
  - It can facilitate more compliance and governance, as the network can enforce the rules and regulations that apply to the domain and the jurisdiction of the blockchain.
- A permissioned blockchain can be used for various applications that require security, identity, and role definition within the network, such as  :
  - Supply chain management, where different parties can track and verify the provenance and quality of the goods and services along the value chain.
  - Healthcare, where patients, providers, and insurers can share and access medical records and claims in a secure and efficient manner.
  - Banking and finance, where financial institutions and regulators can process and monitor transactions and assets in a transparent and compliant way.
  - Government and public services, where citizens, agencies, and organizations can access and provide public information and resources in a trustworthy and convenient way.

- A possible mnemonic to remember the advantages of a permissioned blockchain is **SPFC** (Security, Performance, Flexibility, Compliance).
- A possible mnemonic to remember some of the applications of a permissioned blockchain is **SHBG** (Supply chain, Healthcare, Banking, Government).
