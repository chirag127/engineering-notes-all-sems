### Public distribution system social welfare systems for the notes of the Unit 8 - Use case 3 in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

- Public distribution system (PDS) is a system where the government creates a supply chain to reach towards the public such as ration card is used by public to gain the glossary form government.
- Blockchain is an emerging technology which is applied in all real-world transactions to proved security and transparency.
- Blockchain can be used to implement PDS to completely eliminate unauthorized activities during the distribution process.
- The system for PDS consists of a network having the central government, Food Corporation of India (FCI), state government, district, fair price shops and customers, all acting as nodes of a blockchain network and contains records of all transactions that have taken place between any two parties during the distribution chain.
- Blockchain’s influence in the public sector will be mostly behind the scenes. But the technology has the potential to bring security, efficiency, and speed to a wide range of services and processes.
- A blockchain-based PDS can use smart contracts to automate the distribution process and ensure the accountability and traceability of the food grains.
- Smart contracts are self-executing contracts that are written in code and stored on the blockchain. They can enforce the rules and terms of the agreement between the parties without the need for intermediaries or third parties.
- Solidity is a contract-oriented language framework that has most of the features and functionalities required to carry out any kind of transactions between the parties in a blockchain network.
- Solidity can be used to develop a blockchain-based PDS that can record all transactions and log details of these events.
- A blockchain-based PDS can also use biometric authentication to verify the identity of the beneficiaries and prevent fraud and leakage.
- A blockchain-based PDS can also provide transparency and accountability to the public by allowing them to access the information about the food grain stocks, allocation, distribution, and consumption.
- A blockchain-based PDS can also reduce the operational costs and inefficiencies by eliminating the need for manual paperwork, intermediaries, and duplication of data.

#### Mnemonics and learning tricks

- To remember the benefits of blockchain in PDS, use the acronym **SET**:

  - **S**ecurity: Blockchain can prevent unauthorized activities and fraud by using cryptography and consensus mechanisms.
  - **E**fficiency: Blockchain can automate the distribution process and reduce the operational costs and inefficiencies by using smart contracts and eliminating intermediaries.
  - **T**ransparency: Blockchain can provide transparency and accountability to the public by allowing them to access the information about the food grain stocks, allocation, distribution, and consumption.

- To remember the components of a blockchain-based PDS, use the acronym **FANCY**:

  - **F**ood Corporation of India (FCI): The central agency that procures and stores the food grains from the farmers and allocates them to the states.
  - **A**uthentication: The process of verifying the identity of the beneficiaries using biometric or other methods.
  - **N**odes: The participants in the blockchain network that store and validate the transactions and data.
  - **C**ontracts: The smart contracts that execute the rules and terms of the distribution process and ensure the accountability and traceability of the food grains.
  - **Y**ou: The customers or beneficiaries who receive the food grains from the fair price shops using their ration cards.
