KYC stands for Know Your Customer, which is a process by which banks obtain information about the identity and address of the customers. It is a regulatory requirement to prevent money laundering, fraud, and other illegal activities. Blockchain is a distributed ledger technology that can store and verify transactions in a secure and transparent way. Blockchain can be used to improve the efficiency and accuracy of the KYC process by eliminating the redundancy, manual intervention, and uncertainty involved in the traditional KYC system. A blockchain-based KYC architecture can enable the customers to undergo the KYC procedure only once and share their verified identity with multiple banks and service providers.
