## Unit 7 - Use case 2

- Use case 2 is about designing a chatbot that can help customers book flight tickets online.
- The chatbot should be able to perform the following tasks:
  - Greet the customer and ask for their name and preferred language.
  - Ask the customer for their travel details, such as origin, destination, date, time, number of passengers, and class of service.
  - Validate the customer's input and provide feedback if there are any errors or missing information.
  - Search for available flights that match the customer's criteria and present them in a table format with the following columns: flight number, airline, departure time, arrival time, duration, and price.
  - Allow the customer to select a flight from the table or ask for more options if they are not satisfied.
  - Confirm the customer's selection and ask for their payment details, such as credit card number, expiration date, and security code.
  - Process the payment and issue a confirmation number and a ticket to the customer via email.
  - Thank the customer for using the chatbot and ask for their feedback.
- The chatbot should be able to handle the following scenarios:
  - The customer wants to change or cancel their booking.
  - The customer has a special request, such as a wheelchair, a meal preference, or a seat preference.
  - The customer wants to check the status of their flight or the weather at their destination.
  - The customer wants to chat with a human agent if they have a problem or a complaint.
  - The customer wants to end the conversation at any point.
