The diagram shows three parties involved in the supply chain: the supplier, the manufacturer and the retailer. Each party has a web application that allows them to interact with the blockchain network and record transactions related to the goods they produce, process or sell. Each party also has a blockchain node that validates and encrypts the transactions and stores them in blocks. The blocks are linked in chronological order and form a distributed ledger that serves as a single source of truth for the provenance of the goods. The ledger is shared by all the parties and provides full visibility and traceability of the goods-related data and events. The ledger also ensures the authenticity and origin of the goods and prevents fraud and counterfeiting. The ledger can also be used to resolve claim disputes or provide proof of ownership.
