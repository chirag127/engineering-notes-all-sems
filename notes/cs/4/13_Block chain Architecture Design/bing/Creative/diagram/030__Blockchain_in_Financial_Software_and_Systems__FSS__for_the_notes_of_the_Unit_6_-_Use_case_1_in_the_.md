The diagram shows how different participants in the stock market, such as issuers, buyers and sellers, can use blockchain to record and verify their transactions. Each participant has a copy of the stock ledger, which is a digital representation of the ownership and transfer of stocks. The stock ledger is updated by the blockchain network, which is a decentralized system of nodes that validate and store transactions using cryptography and consensus algorithms. The blockchain network ensures that the stock ledger is consistent, transparent and secure across all participants, and eliminates the need for intermediaries or central authorities. The blockchain network can also provide other benefits, such as faster settlement, lower costs, and enhanced compliance.
