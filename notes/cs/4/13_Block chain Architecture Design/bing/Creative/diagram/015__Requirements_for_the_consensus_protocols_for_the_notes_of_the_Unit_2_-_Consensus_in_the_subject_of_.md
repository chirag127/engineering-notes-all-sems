The requirements for the consensus protocols for the nodes of the Unit 2 - Consensus in the subject of Blockchain Architecture Design are:

- The nodes must agree on the state of the blockchain and the validity of the transactions
- The nodes must prevent a single entity from taking control of the network or distorting the truth
- The nodes must allow users to trust the network without a centralized authority
- The nodes must be able to handle malicious activities such as double spending attacks or 51% attacks
- The nodes must be able to scale and perform efficiently
