The diagram shows how users can interact with each other through digital wallets that store digital currencies. The digital wallets are connected to nodes that record and synchronize transactions in their respective electronic ledgers. The nodes are also connected to validators that verify and validate the transactions and update the ledgers accordingly. The ledgers are distributed across multiple locations and are consistent with each other. The system allows peer-to-peer transfer of value without the need for a central authority or intermediary.
