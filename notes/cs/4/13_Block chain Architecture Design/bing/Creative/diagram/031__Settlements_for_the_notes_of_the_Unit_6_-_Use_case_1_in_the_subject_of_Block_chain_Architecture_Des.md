According to the course material from NPTEL, the use case 1 for unit 6 is about token securitization for real estate transactions using blockchain technology. The use case involves the following steps:

- A property owner wants to sell a fraction of their property to multiple investors and issues tokens that represent the ownership rights of the property.
- The tokens are created and managed by a smart contract on a blockchain platform, such as Hyperledger Fabric or Ethereum.
- The smart contract defines the rules and conditions for the token issuance, transfer, and redemption, as well as the distribution of dividends and profits from the property.
- The investors can buy and sell the tokens on a secondary market, such as a decentralized exchange, or redeem them for the underlying property at a predefined price and time.
- The smart contract also records the history and provenance of the property, such as the title deeds, maintenance records, and valuation reports, and provides transparency and trust to the investors.
