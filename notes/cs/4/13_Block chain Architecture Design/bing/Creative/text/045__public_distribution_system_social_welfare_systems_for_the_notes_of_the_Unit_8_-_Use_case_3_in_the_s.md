### Public Distribution System Social Welfare Systems

- Public distribution system (PDS) is a system where the government creates a supply chain to reach towards the public, such as ration card is used by public to gain the glossary from government.
- Blockchain is an emerging technology that can provide security, transparency, and efficiency to the PDS by recording all transactions and events in a distributed ledger that is shared among all participants  .
- Blockchain can also prevent unauthorized activities, corruption, and leakage of food grains during the distribution process by using smart contracts, cryptography, and consensus mechanisms .
- Solidity is a contract-oriented programming language that can be used to develop smart contracts for the blockchain-based PDS . Smart contracts are self-executing agreements that can enforce the rules and conditions of the PDS without any intermediaries or third parties.
- A blockchain-based PDS can consist of a network of nodes that represent the central government, Food Corporation of India (FCI), state government, district, fair price shops, and customers. Each node can have a unique identity and a digital wallet that can store and transfer food tokens or coupons .
- The central government can allocate food grains to the FCI, which can store them in warehouses and distribute them to the state governments according to their quota. The state governments can then allocate food grains to the districts, which can further distribute them to the fair price shops. The customers can use their ration cards and biometric authentication to buy food grains from the fair price shops using food tokens or coupons .
- All the transactions and events in the PDS can be recorded on the blockchain ledger, which can be verified and validated by all the nodes using consensus algorithms. The blockchain ledger can provide a single source of truth and a traceable history of the PDS, which can improve accountability, auditability, and trust among the participants  .
- Blockchain can also enable real-time monitoring and feedback of the PDS, which can help in identifying and resolving issues, improving efficiency, and reducing wastage. Blockchain can also enable data sharing and analysis, which can help in improving decision making and planning for the PDS .
- Blockchain can also provide benefits such as cost reduction, time saving, and user empowerment for the PDS. Blockchain can reduce the operational and administrative costs of the PDS by eliminating intermediaries, paper work, and duplication. Blockchain can also save time by speeding up the transactions and processes of the PDS. Blockchain can also empower the users by giving them more control and choice over their food entitlements and preferences .
