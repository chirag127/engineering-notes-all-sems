### Blockchain in Financial Software and Systems (FSS)

- Blockchain is a digital collection of transactions that are tracked and recorded in a decentralized network, using advanced cryptography to ensure security and trust .
- Blockchain has many applications and benefits for the financial services industry, such as:
  - Reducing the cost and friction of transactions and trades by eliminating intermediaries, streamlining processes and enabling faster and cheaper settlements  .
  - Creating and managing digital assets that represent financial instruments, such as securities, bonds, derivatives, tokens and stablecoins, with more liquidity and transparency .
  - Enhancing the security and resilience of the financial system by preventing fraud, tampering, hacking and cyberattacks, and enabling traceability and auditability of transactions  .
  - Improving the inclusivity and accessibility of financial services by reaching underserved markets, enabling peer-to-peer transactions and lowering barriers to entry .
- Blockchain also poses some challenges and risks for the financial services industry, such as:
  - Regulatory uncertainty and complexity, as different jurisdictions have different rules and standards for blockchain and cryptoassets, and some aspects of the technology are still not well-defined or understood .
  - Technical scalability and interoperability, as different blockchain platforms and protocols may have different features, limitations and compatibility issues, and may not be able to handle the volume and speed of transactions required by the financial industry  .
  - Cultural and organizational change, as blockchain requires a shift in mindset and behavior from centralized and hierarchical systems to decentralized and collaborative ones, and may entail new roles, skills and governance models  .
- Blockchain is a rapidly evolving and innovative technology that has the potential to transform the financial services industry, but also requires careful and collaborative development and adoption by the public and private sectors .
