## Unit 8 - Use case 3

- Use case 3 is about designing and implementing a chatbot that can answer questions about a specific domain, such as travel, banking, or health.
- The main steps involved in use case 3 are:
  - Define the scope and purpose of the chatbot, such as the target audience, the domain knowledge, and the expected functionalities.
  - Collect and analyze data from various sources, such as existing FAQs, customer feedback, web pages, or domain experts, to identify the common intents and entities in the domain.
  - Design the dialog flow and the natural language understanding (NLU) model for the chatbot, using tools such as Microsoft Bot Framework, Dialogflow, or Rasa.
  - Train and test the chatbot using sample dialogs, user feedback, and metrics such as accuracy, recall, precision, and F1-score.
  - Deploy and monitor the chatbot on the desired platform, such as web, mobile, or voice, and integrate it with other services or APIs if needed.
  - Evaluate and improve the chatbot based on user feedback, analytics, and error analysis, and update the dialog flow and the NLU model accordingly.
- Some of the benefits of use case 3 are:
  - It can provide 24/7 customer service and support, reducing the need for human agents and increasing customer satisfaction and loyalty.
  - It can handle multiple queries and requests simultaneously, improving the efficiency and scalability of the service.
  - It can offer personalized and context-aware responses, enhancing the user experience and engagement.
  - It can leverage the domain knowledge and data to provide relevant and accurate information, solving the user's problems and fulfilling their needs.
- Some of the challenges of use case 3 are:
  - It requires a large and diverse data set to train and test the chatbot, which may not be easily available or accessible.
  - It involves complex and dynamic dialog flows, which may require advanced NLU and natural language generation (NLG) techniques and tools.
  - It depends on the user's input and behavior, which may be unpredictable, ambiguous, or noisy, and may require error handling and fallback strategies.
  - It needs to adapt to the changing user needs and expectations, which may require continuous evaluation and improvement of the chatbot.
