### Trade/Supply Chain Finance

- Trade finance is the process of financing international trade transactions, such as the exchange of goods and services between importers and exporters.
- Supply chain finance is the optimization of cash flows and working capital across the supply chain, such as the payment terms and financing options for buyers and suppliers.
- Blockchain is a distributed ledger technology that enables secure and transparent transactions among multiple parties without intermediaries.
- Blockchain can transform trade and supply chain finance by:
  - Streamlining and automating processes, such as issuing and verifying credits, guarantees, invoices, and payments.
  - Reducing costs, risks, and errors associated with manual and paper-based operations.
  - Enhancing efficiency, visibility, and trust among all participants in the trade ecosystem, such as banks, buyers, sellers, insurers, and regulators.
  - Enabling access to new markets, products, and customers, especially for small and medium enterprises (SMEs) that face challenges in obtaining trade finance.
  - Creating new business models and revenue opportunities for trade finance providers and platforms.
- Some examples of blockchain-based trade and supply chain finance solutions are:
  - we.trade: A digital trade finance network that connects 15 major European banks and their SME clients, offering standardized and simplified trading options and reducing transaction risks.
  - TradeLens: A container logistics platform that leverages blockchain to share data and documents across the supply chain, improving transparency, efficiency, and security.
  - Zeeve: A blockchain platform that helps businesses streamline and automate supply chain finance processes, saving money on operations and facilitating trust and continuity between parties in transactions.
  - Phemex: A cryptocurrency exchange that offers blockchain-based trade finance solutions, such as trade credit insurance, invoice factoring, and supply chain financing.
  - Deloitte: A consulting firm that helps clients implement blockchain solutions for supply chain transparency and traceability, such as tracking the provenance, quality, and certification of products.
