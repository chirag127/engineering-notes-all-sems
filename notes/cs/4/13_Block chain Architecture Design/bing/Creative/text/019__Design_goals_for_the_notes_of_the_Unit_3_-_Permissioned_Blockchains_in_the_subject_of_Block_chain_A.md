### Design goals for the notes of the Unit 3 - Permissioned Blockchains in the subject of Blockchain Architecture Design

- The notes should provide a clear and concise overview of the main concepts, features, and challenges of permissioned blockchains, which are blockchains that require participants to be authorized by a central authority or a consortium of authorities.
- The notes should explain the differences and trade-offs between permissioned and permissionless blockchains, such as the level of trust, scalability, security, privacy, and governance.
- The notes should introduce and compare some of the most popular and widely used permissioned blockchain platforms, such as Hyperledger Fabric, Corda, Quorum, and Besu, and highlight their strengths and weaknesses.
- The notes should illustrate the use cases and applications of permissioned blockchains in various domains, such as finance, supply chain, healthcare, and government, and discuss the benefits and challenges of adopting them.
- The notes should include diagrams, tables, and code snippets to help the students understand the technical aspects and implementation details of permissioned blockchains.
- The notes should also include exercises, quizzes, and case studies to test the students' knowledge and skills, and to encourage them to apply what they have learned to real-world scenarios.
