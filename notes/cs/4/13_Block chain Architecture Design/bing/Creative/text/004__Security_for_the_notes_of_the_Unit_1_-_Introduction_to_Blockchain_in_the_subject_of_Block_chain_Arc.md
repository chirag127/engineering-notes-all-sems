### Security

- Security is a critical aspect of blockchain technology, as it ensures the integrity, availability, and confidentiality of the data stored and exchanged on the distributed ledger.
- Blockchain security relies on several principles and mechanisms, such as cryptography, decentralization, consensus, and identity management, to protect the network from attacks and fraud.
- Cryptography is the main security system of the blockchain. It allows the storing and exchanging of data in a format hidden from third parties and only the person who has the technological permission to unravel the meaning of the message can receive and decode the message sent to them.
- Decentralization is the principle of distributing the data and the control over the network among multiple nodes or participants, rather than relying on a single central authority. This reduces the risk of a single point of failure, corruption, or manipulation, and increases the resilience and availability of the network.
- Consensus is the mechanism of achieving agreement among the nodes or participants on the validity and order of the transactions or data on the blockchain. Consensus ensures that the data is consistent and synchronized across the network, and that only valid transactions are added to the ledger.
- Identity management is the process of verifying and controlling the access and permissions of the nodes or participants on the blockchain. Identity management can vary depending on the type and design of the blockchain, such as public or private, permissioned or permissionless. Identity management can also involve the use of tokens, which are digital representations of value or rights on the blockchain.
- Blockchain security can differ by blockchain types, as different types of blockchains have different trade-offs and challenges in terms of security, scalability, and performance. For example, public and permissionless blockchains, such as Bitcoin, are more open and decentralized, but also more vulnerable to attacks, such as 51% attack, double-spending, or Sybil attack. Private and permissioned blockchains, such as Hyperledger Fabric, are more closed and controlled, but also more efficient and compliant, as they can use selective endorsement and identity verification to achieve consensus and security.
- Blockchain security is a comprehensive risk management system for a blockchain network, using cybersecurity frameworks, assurance services, and best practices to reduce risks against attacks and fraud. Blockchain security also requires the collaboration and coordination of the stakeholders, such as developers, users, regulators, and auditors, to ensure the security and trustworthiness of the blockchain applications and services.
