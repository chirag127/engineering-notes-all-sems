### Capital markets

Capital markets are the markets where securities such as stocks, bonds, derivatives and other financial instruments are issued, traded and settled. Capital markets facilitate the flow of funds from savers to borrowers, and provide liquidity, price discovery and risk management for the participants.

Blockchain technology has the potential to transform the capital markets industry by enabling faster, cheaper, more transparent and more secure transactions, as well as reducing intermediaries, operational risks and systemic risks. Blockchain technology can also enable new forms of securities, such as programmable digital assets, fractionalized ownership and tokenized assets, that can increase the accessibility, diversity and efficiency of the markets.

Some of the use cases of blockchain in capital markets are:

- Issuance: Blockchain can streamline the issuance process of securities by digitizing the assets, encoding the terms and conditions, automating the KYC/AML checks, and providing a single interface for investors and issuers. Blockchain can also enable new types of securities, such as security tokens, that can offer more flexibility, customization and compliance than traditional securities.
- Sales and trading: Blockchain can enable peer-to-peer trading of any asset on a verifiable ledger, without the need for intermediaries or clearing houses. Blockchain can also facilitate faster and more transparent settlement and clearing, reducing the counterparty and default risks, as well as the capital and liquidity requirements. Blockchain can also enable new trading venues, such as decentralized exchanges, that can offer more choice, competition and innovation for the traders.
- Collateral management: Blockchain can improve the efficiency and security of collateral management by providing a shared and immutable record of the collateral positions, obligations and movements across the market participants. Blockchain can also enable real-time collateral optimization, allocation and substitution, as well as automated margin calls and collateral transfers, reducing the operational costs and risks.
- Exchanges: Blockchain can enhance the performance and resilience of exchanges by eliminating the single point of failure and enabling decentralized utilities. Blockchain can also enable new types of exchanges, such as prediction markets, that can offer more information, incentives and participation for the market participants.
- Clearing and settlement: Blockchain can reduce the time, cost and complexity of clearing and settlement by enabling near-instantaneous and final settlement of transactions, without the need for intermediaries or reconciliation. Blockchain can also enable atomic swaps, that can allow the simultaneous exchange of different assets across different blockchains, reducing the settlement risk and enhancing the interoperability.
- Stablecoins: Blockchain can enable the creation and use of stablecoins, that are digital tokens that are pegged to a fiat currency or a basket of assets, and can offer the benefits of both cryptocurrencies and fiat currencies, such as low volatility, high liquidity, global reach and programmability. Stablecoins can facilitate the payment and settlement of transactions on the blockchain, as well as enable new use cases, such as cross-border remittances, decentralized lending and DeFi.
- Post-trade services and infrastructure: Blockchain can improve the post-trade services and infrastructure by providing a single source of truth for the ownership, rights and obligations of the securities, as well as enabling automated and smart contract-based execution of corporate actions, dividends, coupons, tax reporting and regulatory compliance. Blockchain can also enable new post-trade services, such as tokenization platforms, that can allow the creation, issuance and management of digital assets on the blockchain.
- Asset servicing: Blockchain can enhance the asset servicing by providing a transparent and auditable record of the asset performance, valuation and risk, as well as enabling automated and smart contract-based execution of asset servicing events, such as interest payments, principal repayments, defaults, workouts and liquidations. Blockchain can also enable new asset servicing models, such as asset-backed tokens, that can offer more liquidity, fractionalization and customization of the assets.
- Mutual fund administration: Blockchain can streamline the mutual fund administration by providing a shared and immutable record of the fund NAV, holdings, transactions and fees, as well as enabling automated and smart contract-based execution of fund administration activities, such as subscription, redemption, transfer, distribution and reporting. Blockchain can also enable new mutual fund models, such as tokenized funds, that can offer more transparency, accessibility and efficiency for the fund managers and investors.
- Custody: Blockchain can improve the custody of securities by providing a secure and decentralized way of storing and transferring the digital assets, without the need for intermediaries or custodians. Blockchain can also enable self-custody, that can allow the investors to have full control and ownership of their assets, as well as multi-sig custody, that can allow the investors to share the control and ownership of their assets with trusted parties.
