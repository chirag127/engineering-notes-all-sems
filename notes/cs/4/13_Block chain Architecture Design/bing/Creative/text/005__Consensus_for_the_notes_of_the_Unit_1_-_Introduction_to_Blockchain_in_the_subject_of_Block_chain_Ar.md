### Consensus for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Blockchain in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

- Consensus is a dynamic way of reaching agreement in a group of distributed nodes in a blockchain network, without the need for a central authority or intermediary.
- Consensus mechanisms are the programs or algorithms that enable the nodes to achieve consensus on the state of the ledger, the validity of transactions, and the addition of new blocks.
- Consensus mechanisms are essential for ensuring the security, reliability, and trustworthiness of the blockchain system, as they prevent data tampering, double-spending, and malicious attacks.
- Consensus mechanisms can be classified into two broad categories: proof-based and voting-based.
- Proof-based consensus mechanisms require the nodes to perform some work or stake some resources to participate in the consensus process. Examples of proof-based mechanisms are Proof-of-Work (PoW) and Proof-of-Stake (PoS).
- PoW is the consensus mechanism used by Bitcoin and other cryptocurrencies, where the nodes compete to solve a cryptographic puzzle and earn the right to add a new block to the chain. PoW is secure but consumes a lot of energy and resources.
- PoS is the consensus mechanism that Ethereum and other cryptocurrencies are planning to adopt, where the nodes stake some amount of coins to validate transactions and create new blocks. PoS is more energy-efficient and scalable than PoW, but has some challenges such as the "nothing-at-stake" problem and the centralization of power.
- Voting-based consensus mechanisms rely on the nodes to communicate and vote on the state of the ledger, the validity of transactions, and the addition of new blocks. Examples of voting-based mechanisms are Byzantine Fault Tolerance (BFT) and Delegated Proof-of-Stake (DPoS).
- BFT is the consensus mechanism that ensures that the nodes can reach agreement even if some of them are faulty or malicious. BFT can tolerate up to one-third of the nodes being faulty or malicious, and can achieve fast and final consensus. BFT is used by some blockchain platforms such as Hyperledger Fabric and Stellar.
- DPoS is the consensus mechanism that allows the nodes to delegate their voting power to a set of representatives or validators, who are responsible for validating transactions and creating new blocks. DPoS is more efficient and democratic than PoS, but also has some drawbacks such as the risk of collusion and corruption among the validators. DPoS is used by some blockchain platforms such as EOS and Tron.
