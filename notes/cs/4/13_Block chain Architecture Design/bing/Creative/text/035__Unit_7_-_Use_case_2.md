## Unit 7 - Use case 2

- Use case 2 is about designing and implementing a chatbot that can answer questions about a company's products and services.
- The chatbot should be able to:
  - Understand the user's intent and context from natural language input.
  - Provide relevant and accurate information from a knowledge base or a web search.
  - Handle multiple turns of conversation and maintain the dialogue state.
  - Handle errors and exceptions gracefully and recover from misunderstandings.
  - Generate natural and engaging responses that reflect the company's brand and tone of voice.
- The steps involved in developing use case 2 are:
  - Define the scope and goals of the chatbot and identify the target audience and use cases.
  - Collect and analyze data from existing sources such as customer feedback, FAQs, web pages, etc.
  - Design the dialogue flow and the user interface of the chatbot using tools such as flowcharts, wireframes, mockups, etc.
  - Implement the chatbot logic using a framework or a platform such as Microsoft Bot Framework, Google Dialogflow, Amazon Lex, etc.
  - Test and evaluate the chatbot using metrics such as accuracy, usability, satisfaction, etc.
  - Deploy and monitor the chatbot and collect feedback for improvement.
