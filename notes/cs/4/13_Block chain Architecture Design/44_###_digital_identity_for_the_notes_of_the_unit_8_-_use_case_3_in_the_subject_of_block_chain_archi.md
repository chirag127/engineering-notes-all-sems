### Digital identity for the notes of the Unit 8 - Use case 3 in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

Digital Identity:
1. Decentralized: Based on block chain technology, not controlled by a single entity.
2. Self-sovereign: User controls and manages their own identity information.
3. Verifiable: Identity information is cryptographically secure and can be verified.
4. Portable: User can take their digital identity with them across different platforms and services.
5. Privacy-preserving: Personal information is protected and only shared with consent.
