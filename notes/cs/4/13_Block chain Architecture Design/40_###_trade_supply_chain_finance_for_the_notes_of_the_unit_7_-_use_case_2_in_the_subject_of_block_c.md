### trade/supply chain finance for the notes of the Unit 7 - Use case 2 in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

Trade/supply chain finance refers to the financing of domestic and international trade transactions. In the context of blockchain architecture design, it involves the use of blockchain technology to improve the efficiency and security of trade finance transactions. The benefits of using blockchain in trade finance include reducing the risk of fraud, increasing transparency, and streamlining the financing process. This can be achieved through the use of smart contracts, which automate the process of verifying and executing trade finance transactions. Additionally, the use of blockchain can also improve the accuracy and speed of record-keeping, making it easier for all parties involved in the supply chain to access and verify information.
