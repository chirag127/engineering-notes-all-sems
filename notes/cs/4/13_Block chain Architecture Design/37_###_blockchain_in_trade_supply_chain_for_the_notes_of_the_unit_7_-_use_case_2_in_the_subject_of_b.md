### Blockchain in trade/supply chain for the notes of the Unit 7 - Use case 2 in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

Blockchain in trade/supply chain refers to the use of blockchain technology to improve the efficiency and transparency of supply chain processes. The key features of blockchain, such as immutability, decentralization, and secure data sharing, make it an ideal solution for supply chain management.

In a supply chain, various parties such as suppliers, manufacturers, distributors, and customers are involved in the movement of goods from the source to the final destination. Blockchain can help reduce the complexity and inefficiencies in the supply chain by providing a shared, secure, and transparent ledger of all transactions.

One of the main benefits of blockchain in the supply chain is increased transparency. By having a shared ledger of all transactions, all parties involved in the supply chain can have access to the same information and can track the movement of goods in real-time. This can help to reduce the risk of fraud and increase trust between parties.

Another benefit of blockchain in the supply chain is improved efficiency. Blockchain can automate many manual processes, such as the tracking of goods, the reconciliation of accounts, and the settlement of payments. This can help to reduce the time and costs involved in the supply chain.

Finally, blockchain can also help to increase the security of the supply chain. By having a secure and decentralized ledger of all transactions, it is difficult for malicious actors to manipulate the data. This can help to reduce the risk of cyber attacks and other security threats in the supply chain.

Overall, blockchain has the potential to revolutionize the supply chain by providing increased transparency, efficiency, and security.
