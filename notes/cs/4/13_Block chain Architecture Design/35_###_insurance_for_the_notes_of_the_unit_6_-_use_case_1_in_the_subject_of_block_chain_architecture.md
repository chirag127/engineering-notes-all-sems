### Insurance for the notes of the Unit 6 - Use case 1 in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

Insurance:
1. Decentralized: Utilizing smart contracts to automate claims processing, reducing costs and increasing efficiency. 
2. Tamper-proof: Immutable ledger ensures accurate record-keeping and reduces fraud.
3. Transparent: All parties have access to the same information, improving trust and reducing disputes. 
4. Automated: Smart contracts eliminate manual processes, reducing errors and improving speed.
5. Cost-effective: Reduced intermediaries and automation lead to lower costs for insurers and policyholders.
