### Design Primitives for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Blockchain in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

Design Primitives:

Design primitives are the building blocks used to design and implement blockchain systems. They are the fundamental components that make up a blockchain and determine its properties and behavior.

The main design primitives in blockchain systems include:

1. Consensus mechanism: This is the mechanism used to reach agreement on the state of the blockchain among the participants in the network. Common consensus mechanisms include proof-of-work (PoW), proof-of-stake (PoS), and delegated proof-of-stake (DPoS).

2. Ledger: This is the database that stores the state of the blockchain, including the transactions and the current state of the network.

3. Cryptographic hashes: These are mathematical functions used to secure the integrity of the blockchain by creating a unique identifier for each block in the chain.

4. Digital signatures: These are used to secure the authenticity of transactions by allowing participants to sign transactions with their private key, proving that they have authorized the transaction.

5. Smart contracts: These are self-executing contracts with the terms of the agreement between buyer and seller being directly written into lines of code.

6. Tokenization: This is the process of converting assets, such as currency or property, into digital tokens that can be traded and managed on a blockchain.

In conclusion, design primitives are the building blocks used to design and implement blockchain systems, including consensus mechanisms, ledger, cryptographic hashes, digital signatures, smart contracts, and tokenization. These design primitives determine the properties and behavior of a blockchain and are essential to its function and security.
