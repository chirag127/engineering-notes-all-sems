### Hashchain to Blockchain for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Blockchain in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

Hashchain:
- Hashchain is a simple type of blockchain that uses hashes to secure transactions and maintain the integrity of the chain.
- Each block in a hashchain contains a hash of the previous block, a timestamp, and the data of the transaction.
- A hashchain is a linear sequence of blocks, and once a block is added to the chain, its data cannot be altered.

Blockchain:
- Blockchain is a decentralized digital ledger that records transactions across a network of computers.
- Each block in a blockchain contains a cryptographic hash of the previous block, a timestamp, and the data of the transaction.
- Blockchains are secure by design and are resistant to modification of the data.
- They are used in various applications, including cryptocurrencies, supply chain management, and voting systems.
- The most well-known blockchain is the Bitcoin blockchain.
