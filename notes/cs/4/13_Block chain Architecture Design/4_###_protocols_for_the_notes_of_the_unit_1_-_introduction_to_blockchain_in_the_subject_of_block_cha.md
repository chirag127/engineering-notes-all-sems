### Protocols for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Blockchain in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

Protocols for Unit 1 Introduction to Blockchain:
1. Proof of Work (PoW): A consensus mechanism that requires nodes to perform a computational task to validate transactions.
2. Proof of Stake (PoS): A consensus mechanism where nodes are chosen to validate transactions based on the amount of stake they hold.
3. Delegated Proof of Stake (DPoS): A consensus mechanism where nodes delegate their voting power to a select few to validate transactions.
4. Byzantine Fault Tolerance (BFT): A consensus mechanism that ensures the network remains operational even if some nodes are faulty.
5. Consensus Algorithm: A set of rules used to validate transactions and maintain the integrity of the blockchain.

Note: These protocols are used to secure and validate transactions in a blockchain network. The choice of protocol depends on the specific requirements of the blockchain application.
