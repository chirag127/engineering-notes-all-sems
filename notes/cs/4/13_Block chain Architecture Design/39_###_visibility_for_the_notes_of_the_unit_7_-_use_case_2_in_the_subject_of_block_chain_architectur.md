### visibility for the notes of the Unit 7 - Use case 2 in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

Visibility in blockchain refers to the ability to see and track the flow of transactions and data within the blockchain network. This is achieved through the use of transparent and secure ledger systems, which allow all participants in the network to see and verify transactions.

1. Transparency: The transparency of blockchain networks allows for full visibility into the flow of transactions, enabling all participants to see and verify the accuracy of the data.

2. Traceability: The traceability of blockchain transactions allows for the tracking of assets and data as they move through the network, enabling participants to see where they came from and where they are going.

3. Audibility: The audibility of blockchain transactions allows for the monitoring and auditing of the network, enabling participants to detect and prevent fraudulent activity.

4. Security: The security of blockchain networks ensures that transactions are protected from tampering and unauthorized access, enabling participants to trust the accuracy and integrity of the data.

Visibility is a key aspect of blockchain architecture design, as it enables organizations to ensure the accuracy, integrity, and security of the data and transactions within the network. This is particularly important in industries such as finance, healthcare, and supply chain management, where the accuracy and security of data is critical.
