### Basic consensus mechanisms for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Blockchain in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

Consensus mechanisms are the methods used by blockchain networks to reach agreement on the state of the ledger and validate transactions. There are several basic consensus mechanisms used in blockchain technology:

1. Proof of Work (PoW): In PoW, nodes compete to solve a cryptographic puzzle, and the first node to solve the puzzle is allowed to add the next block to the blockchain. PoW is used by the original Bitcoin blockchain.

2. Proof of Stake (PoS): In PoS, nodes are selected to validate transactions and add blocks to the blockchain based on the number of tokens they hold and are willing to “stake” as collateral. PoS is more energy-efficient than PoW.

3. Delegated Proof of Stake (DPoS): In DPoS, token holders vote to elect a limited number of nodes, called witnesses or delegates, to validate transactions and add blocks to the blockchain. DPoS is used by the EOS blockchain.

4. Practical Byzantine Fault Tolerance (PBFT): In PBFT, nodes communicate with each other to reach consensus on the state of the ledger, even in the presence of malicious nodes. PBFT is used by the Hyperledger blockchain.

5. Proof of Elapsed Time (PoET): In PoET, nodes wait for a random amount of time before adding the next block to the blockchain, reducing the competition for block validation. PoET is used by the Sawtooth blockchain.

Advantages of consensus mechanisms include:

1. Decentralization: Consensus mechanisms allow for the decentralization of decision-making in the blockchain network, reducing the risk of a single point of failure.

2. Security: Consensus mechanisms help to secure the blockchain network by ensuring that all nodes agree on the state of the ledger and that transactions are validated in a secure and transparent manner.

Disadvantages of consensus mechanisms include:

1. Complexity: Consensus mechanisms can be complex and difficult to understand, making it challenging for users to participate in the network.

2. Scalability: Consensus mechanisms can limit the scalability of the blockchain network, as the number of transactions that can be processed per second is limited by the consensus mechanism being used.

In conclusion, consensus mechanisms are the methods used by blockchain networks to reach agreement on the state of the ledger, and validate transactions. There are several basic consensus mechanisms, including Proof of Work, Proof of Stake, Delegated Proof of Stake, Practical Byzantine Fault Tolerance, and Proof of Elapsed Time, each with its own advantages and disadvantages.
