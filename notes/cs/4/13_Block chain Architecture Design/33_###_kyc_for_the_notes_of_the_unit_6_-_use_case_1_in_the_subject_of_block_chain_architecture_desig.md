### KYC for the notes of the Unit 6 - Use case 1 in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

KYC (Know Your Customer) is a process of verifying the identity of clients in a financial institution. In the context of blockchain architecture design, it is used to ensure that users are who they claim to be, and to prevent illegal activities such as money laundering and financing of terrorism. This can be achieved through the collection and verification of personal information, such as name, address, and government-issued identification number. The information is then stored on the blockchain, allowing for secure and transparent access by authorized parties. KYC can also help to improve the customer experience by reducing the time and effort required to onboard new clients.
