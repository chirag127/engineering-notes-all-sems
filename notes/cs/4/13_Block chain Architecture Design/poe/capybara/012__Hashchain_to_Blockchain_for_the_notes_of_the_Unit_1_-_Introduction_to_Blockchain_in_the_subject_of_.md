### Hashchain to Blockchain

In this unit, we will cover the evolution of Hashchain to Blockchain. Blockchain is a distributed database that stores a continuously growing list of records called blocks. These blocks are linked and secured using cryptography. Here are some key points to keep in mind:

- Hashchains were originally used to create digital signatures for documents. They are a sequence of hash values that are linked together in a specific order.
- In 2008, Satoshi Nakamoto introduced the concept of blockchain in the Bitcoin whitepaper. The first blockchain was created as a public ledger for Bitcoin transactions.
- Blockchain is a decentralized system where all nodes have a copy of the ledger. This makes it difficult for any single party to manipulate the data.
- Blocks in a blockchain are created through a process called mining. Miners compete to solve a complex mathematical problem, and the first one to solve it gets to create the next block.
- Once a block is created, it is added to the blockchain and cannot be modified without the consensus of the network. This makes blockchain an immutable ledger.
- There are two types of blockchains: public and private. Public blockchains are open to anyone, while private blockchains are restricted to a specific group of participants.
- Blockchain has the potential to revolutionize many industries, including finance, healthcare, and supply chain management. Its decentralized nature and immutability make it a secure and transparent way to store and transfer data.

In summary, the evolution of Hashchain to Blockchain has led to the creation of a secure and transparent system for storing and transferring data. Understanding the basics of blockchain is essential for anyone interested in the field of blockchain architecture design.
