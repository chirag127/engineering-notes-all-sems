### Design goals for the notes of the Unit 3 - Permissioned Blockchains in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

The following are the design goals for the notes of the Unit 3 - Permissioned Blockchains in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design:

- Provide a comprehensive overview of permissioned blockchains, including their characteristics, advantages, and limitations.
- Describe the different types of permissioned blockchains and their use cases, such as consortium blockchains, private blockchains, and hybrid blockchains.
- Explain the consensus mechanisms used in permissioned blockchains, such as Proof of Authority (PoA), Proof of Stake (PoS), and Practical Byzantine Fault Tolerance (PBFT).
- Discuss the role of smart contracts and their implementation in permissioned blockchains.
- Address the security and privacy concerns related to permissioned blockchains, including access control, data confidentiality, and network vulnerabilities.
- Provide case studies and examples of permissioned blockchains in various industries, such as finance, healthcare, and supply chain management.
- Emphasize the importance of governance models and regulatory compliance in permissioned blockchains.
- Highlight the challenges and opportunities of integrating permissioned blockchains with other emerging technologies, such as Internet of Things (IoT) and Artificial Intelligence (AI).
- Include relevant diagrams, charts, and illustrations to support the content and enhance the understanding of the reader.
- Provide a list of references and further readings for those interested in exploring the topic in more depth.
