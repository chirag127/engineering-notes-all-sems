
### Design Primitives for the Notes of Unit 1 - Introduction to Blockchain

* Blockchain is a distributed ledger technology that enables the secure transfer of data and assets between participants in a network. 
* Blockchain is composed of three main components: blocks, transactions, and consensus mechanisms.
* Blocks are the basic unit of the blockchain, containing data and a cryptographic hash of the previous block.
* Transactions are the means by which data is stored and transferred on the blockchain.
* Consensus mechanisms are algorithms that enable participants to agree on the state of the blockchain.
* Smart contracts are computer programs that are stored on the blockchain and can be used to automatically execute transactions.
* Cryptocurrencies are digital assets that are used as a medium of exchange on the blockchain.
* Cryptography is the science of creating and using cryptographic algorithms to protect data and assets on the blockchain.
* Mining is the process of verifying and adding blocks to the blockchain.
