
### Visibility for the Notes of the Unit 7 - Use Case 2 in the Subject of Block Chain Architecture Design

1. Block chain architecture is a type of distributed ledger technology (DLT) that is used to store, validate, and secure data across a network of computers.
2. Visibility is an important aspect of block chain architecture design, as it allows users to view and verify the data stored on the network.
3. Block chain networks are typically composed of nodes, which are computers that store and process data.
4. Each node stores a copy of the entire ledger, which is updated in real-time when new data is added or existing data is changed.
5. This ensures that all nodes have a consistent view of the ledger and that any changes are visible to all participants in the network.
6. The visibility of the ledger also ensures that all transactions are valid and secure, as any invalid or unauthorized transactions can be easily identified and rejected.
7. In addition to providing visibility, block chain networks also provide users with a high degree of privacy and security, as all data is encrypted and stored on the nodes.
8. This ensures that only authorized users have access to the data, and that any unauthorized access attempts can be easily detected and blocked.
9. Block chain architecture design also includes consensus protocols, which are used to ensure that all nodes agree on the state of the ledger.
10. These protocols are used to validate transactions and ensure that all nodes have the same view of the ledger.
