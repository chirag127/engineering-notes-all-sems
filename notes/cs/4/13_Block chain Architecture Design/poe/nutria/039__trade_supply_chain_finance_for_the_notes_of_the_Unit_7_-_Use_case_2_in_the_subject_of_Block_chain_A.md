
### Trade/Supply Chain Finance

* Trade finance is a type of financial service that involves providing capital to companies that are engaged in international trade.
* It is used to finance the purchase of goods and services in order to facilitate cross-border transactions.
* Supply chain finance is a type of trade finance that involves providing capital to companies in the supply chain.
* It is used to finance the purchase of goods and services in order to facilitate the flow of goods and services throughout the supply chain.
* Blockchain technology can be used to facilitate trade finance and supply chain finance by providing a secure, transparent, and immutable record of transactions.
* Blockchain technology can also be used to facilitate the transfer of funds between parties, reducing the risk of fraud and increasing the efficiency of the transaction.
* Smart contracts can be used to automate the process of trade finance and supply chain finance, reducing the need for manual intervention and reducing the cost of transactions.
