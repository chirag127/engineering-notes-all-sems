
## Unit 2 - Consensus

1. Consensus is a concept that is used in many different fields, including computer science, mathematics, economics, and sociology. It refers to the agreement of a group of people on a particular issue or decision.

2. In computer science, consensus is the process of reaching agreement among distributed processes or systems. It is used to ensure that all processes or systems have the same view of the data.

3. In mathematics, consensus is the process of reaching agreement among a group of mathematicians on a particular proof or theorem.

4. In economics, consensus is the process of reaching agreement among a group of economists on a particular economic policy or course of action.

5. In sociology, consensus is the process of reaching agreement among a group of people on a particular issue or opinion.

6. Consensus is often used to reach agreement in situations where there is no clear majority opinion or when there is disagreement among the members of a group. It is also used to ensure that all members of a group have the same view of a particular issue or decision.
