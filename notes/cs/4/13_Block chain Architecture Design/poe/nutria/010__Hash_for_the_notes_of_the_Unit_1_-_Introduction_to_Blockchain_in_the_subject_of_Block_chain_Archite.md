

### Hash for the Notes of Unit 1 - Introduction to Blockchain in the Subject of Blockchain Architecture Design

1. A hash is a mathematical algorithm that is used to create a unique identifier for a set of data.
2. It is used to ensure that data is not tampered with and is secure.
3. In blockchain technology, a hash is used to generate a unique identifier for each block of data.
4. Each block includes a hash of the previous block, creating a chain of data that is secure and tamper-proof.
5. Hashes are also used to generate cryptographic keys that are used to secure transactions on the blockchain.
6. Hashing algorithms are one of the core components of blockchain technology, and are essential to the security and reliability of the system.
