
### Blockchain in Trade/Supply Chain

* Blockchain technology can be used in trade and supply chain operations to improve transparency, efficiency, and security.
* Blockchain technology can help reduce costs by streamlining processes and reducing paperwork.
* Smart contracts can be used to automate certain processes, such as payments and shipping.
* Blockchain-based supply chain tracking systems can provide real-time visibility of products from source to destination.
* Blockchain technology can be used to authenticate products, ensuring that only genuine products are sold.
* Blockchain technology can also be used to verify the provenance of goods and services, helping to reduce counterfeiting.
* Blockchain-based systems can also help to reduce fraud and enable more secure transactions.
