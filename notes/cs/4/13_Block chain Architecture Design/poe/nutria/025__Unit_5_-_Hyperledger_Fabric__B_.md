
## Unit 5 - Hyperledger Fabric (B)

1. Hyperledger Fabric is an open source blockchain platform that enables businesses to develop applications and solutions with a modular architecture.
2. It is designed to support pluggable implementations of various components, and accommodate the complexity and intricacies that exist within the consensus and membership services.
3. It is built on a distributed ledger technology that is secured using a consensus protocol.
4. It is designed to provide a framework for enterprise-grade applications that require high levels of security, privacy, and scalability.
5. It is a permissioned blockchain platform, meaning it requires users to be authenticated and authorized to access the network.
6. It supports smart contracts written in the popular programming languages such as Go, Java, and Node.js.
7. It provides a secure, efficient, and scalable platform for developing distributed ledger applications.
8. It provides a secure and reliable environment for data sharing and transactions.
9. It is designed to be modular and extensible, allowing developers to customize the platform to meet their specific needs.
10. It supports a wide range of consensus algorithms, such as Kafka, Raft, and Solo, to ensure the highest levels of security, privacy, and scalability.
