
### Consensus for the Notes of Unit 1 - Introduction to Blockchain in Blockchain Architecture Design

1. Consensus is an important concept in blockchain technology as it is used to reach agreement among distributed nodes in a blockchain network. 
2. The consensus protocol is used to validate transactions, prevent double-spending, and ensure the integrity of the blockchain.
3. There are several different consensus protocols used by different blockchain networks, each with its own advantages and disadvantages. 
4. The most commonly used consensus protocols are Proof-of-Work (PoW), Proof-of-Stake (PoS), and Delegated Proof-of-Stake (DPoS). 
5. PoW is the most widely used consensus protocol, as it is used by Bitcoin and other major cryptocurrencies. 
6. PoS is an alternative consensus protocol that is gaining popularity due to its energy efficiency and scalability. 
7. DPoS is a variant of PoS that is used by some blockchain networks to increase transaction throughput. 
8. Consensus protocols are an important part of blockchain architecture design, as they are used to ensure the security and integrity of the blockchain network.
