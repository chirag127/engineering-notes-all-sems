### Blockchain in trade/supply chain for the notes of the Unit 7 - Use case 2 in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

Blockchain technology has the potential to revolutionize the way businesses operate, particularly in supply chain management. The use of blockchain in trade/supply chain can provide several benefits such as increased transparency, security, efficiency, and cost-effectiveness. In this unit, we will explore the application of blockchain in trade/supply chain and its use case.

Here are some key points to understand the use case of blockchain in trade/supply chain:

1. Transparency: Blockchain technology enables transparency in the supply chain by providing real-time visibility into the movement of goods and transactions. Every transaction is recorded on a blockchain, creating an immutable and transparent record that can be accessed by all parties involved in the supply chain.

2. Security: Blockchain technology provides enhanced security for supply chain transactions by using cryptographic algorithms to secure the data. This makes it virtually impossible for anyone to tamper with the data, ensuring that the supply chain transactions are secure and trustworthy.

3. Efficiency: Blockchain technology can streamline the supply chain process by reducing the need for intermediaries and paperwork. This can help reduce costs and increase efficiency, as transactions can be completed faster and with fewer errors.

4. Cost-effectiveness: By using blockchain technology, businesses can reduce costs associated with intermediaries, paperwork, and other inefficiencies in the supply chain. This can help increase profitability and reduce the cost of goods for consumers.

5. Use cases: Blockchain technology is being used in several supply chain use cases, such as tracking the origin of goods, verifying the authenticity of products, and reducing the risk of fraud and counterfeiting. Blockchain can also be used for supply chain financing, allowing businesses to access financing based on the value of their assets in the supply chain.

In conclusion, the use of blockchain technology in trade/supply chain management has the potential to transform the way businesses operate. By providing increased transparency, security, efficiency, and cost-effectiveness, blockchain can help businesses streamline their supply chain operations and increase profitability. As such, it is important for businesses to explore the potential of blockchain and consider its implementation in their supply chain processes.
