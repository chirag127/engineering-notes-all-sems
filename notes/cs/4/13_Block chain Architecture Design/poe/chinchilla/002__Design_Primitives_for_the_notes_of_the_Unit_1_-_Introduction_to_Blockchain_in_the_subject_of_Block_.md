### Design Primitives for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Blockchain in the subject of Blockchain Architecture Design

In the world of blockchain, design primitives are the basic building blocks that make up the system. They are the fundamental elements that are used to create more complex systems and applications. In this unit, we will be discussing the design primitives that are used in the architecture of blockchain systems.

Here are the design primitives for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Blockchain:

1. **Cryptographic Hash Functions:** These are one-way functions that take an input and output a fixed-size string of characters. They are used to ensure data integrity and security in blockchain systems.

2. **Digital Signatures:** These are used to verify the authenticity of transactions in blockchain systems. They are created using private keys and can only be verified using the corresponding public key.

3. **Peer-to-Peer Networking:** This is the networking architecture used in blockchain systems. It allows nodes to communicate directly with each other without the need for a centralized intermediary.

4. **Consensus Mechanisms:** These are used to ensure that all nodes in a blockchain network agree on the state of the system. There are several consensus mechanisms used in blockchain systems, including Proof of Work (PoW), Proof of Stake (PoS), and Delegated Proof of Stake (DPoS).

5. **Smart Contracts:** These are self-executing contracts that are programmed to automatically enforce the terms of an agreement. They are used in blockchain systems to automate processes and ensure that transactions are executed as intended.

6. **Distributed Ledgers:** These are the databases used in blockchain systems. They are decentralized and distributed across all nodes in the network, allowing for transparency and immutability.

7. **Tokens:** These are digital representations of assets that are used in blockchain systems. They can be used to represent anything from currency to property rights.

Understanding these design primitives is essential for anyone looking to design and build blockchain systems. By mastering these building blocks, you can create more complex and sophisticated blockchain applications that are secure, transparent, and efficient.
