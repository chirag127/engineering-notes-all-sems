## Unit 7 - Use case 2

In this unit, we will discuss use case 2, which involves the following points:

- Use case 2 involves a scenario where a customer wants to purchase a product from an online store.
- The customer visits the online store and browses through the available products.
- The customer selects a product they want to purchase and adds it to their cart.
- The customer proceeds to the checkout page to complete the purchase.
- At the checkout page, the customer enters their shipping and billing information.
- The customer selects a payment method and enters their payment details.
- The customer reviews the order summary and confirms the purchase.
- The online store processes the payment and sends a confirmation email to the customer.
- The online store updates the inventory and shipping information for the purchased product.
- The purchased product is shipped to the customer's address.
- The customer receives the product and confirms its quality and condition.
- The customer leaves a review of the purchased product and their experience with the online store.

To ensure a smooth and successful use case 2, the following considerations should be taken into account:

- The online store should have a user-friendly and intuitive interface to facilitate the customer's browsing and selection of products.
- The checkout page should have clear instructions and prompts to guide the customer through the purchasing process.
- The online store should offer multiple secure and convenient payment methods to cater to the customer's preferences.
- The online store should provide timely and accurate updates on the order status and shipping information.
- The purchased product should be of high quality and meet the customer's expectations.
- The online store should encourage and facilitate customer feedback and reviews to improve their products and services.

Overall, use case 2 is a common and essential scenario in the e-commerce industry, and its success depends on the online store's ability to provide a seamless and satisfying customer experience.
