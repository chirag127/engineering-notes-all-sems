### Visibility for the Notes of Unit 7 - Use Case 2 in the Subject of Blockchain Architecture Design

In the subject of blockchain architecture design, understanding the use cases is crucial to know how blockchain technology can be applied in various industries. Unit 7 focuses on Use Case 2, which involves using blockchain technology for supply chain management. In this unit, the concept of visibility plays a significant role in ensuring the success of the blockchain solution. Here are some key points to understand visibility in blockchain supply chain management:

1. Transparency: Blockchain technology provides complete transparency in the supply chain process. Every transaction recorded on the blockchain is immutable and can be accessed by all participants in the network. This enhances visibility in the supply chain, enabling businesses to track their products from the source to the end consumer.

2. Traceability: Blockchain technology enables traceability in the supply chain by recording every transaction on the blockchain. This feature allows businesses to track the movement of goods throughout the supply chain, making it easier to identify the source of any issue or defect in the product.

3. Accountability: Blockchain technology promotes accountability in the supply chain by providing a tamper-proof record of all transactions. This helps to prevent fraudulent activities and ensures that all participants in the network are held accountable for their actions.

4. Real-time monitoring: Blockchain technology enables real-time monitoring of the supply chain process, providing businesses with up-to-date information on the movement of goods. This feature enhances visibility in the supply chain, making it easier for businesses to identify and address any issues that may arise.

5. Improved efficiency: The use of blockchain technology in supply chain management can lead to improved efficiency in the supply chain process. By providing complete visibility and traceability, businesses can optimize their supply chain operations, reducing costs and improving customer satisfaction.

In conclusion, visibility is a critical factor in the success of blockchain solutions in supply chain management. By providing transparency, traceability, accountability, real-time monitoring, and improved efficiency, blockchain technology can revolutionize the way businesses manage their supply chains. Understanding these concepts is essential for anyone looking to implement blockchain solutions in the supply chain industry.
