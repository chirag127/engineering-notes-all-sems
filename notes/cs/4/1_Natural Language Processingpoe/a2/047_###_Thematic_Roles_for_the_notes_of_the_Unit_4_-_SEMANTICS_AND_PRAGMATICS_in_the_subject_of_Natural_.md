### Thematic Roles for the notes of the Unit 4 - SEMANTICS AND PRAGMATICS in the subject of Natural Language Processing

Thematic roles are used to classify the semantic relationship between a verb and its arguments. They are used to indicate the role that a noun phrase plays with respect to the action or state described by the verb.

Some commonly used thematic roles are:

- Agent: The entity that performs the action of the verb. Eg: John kicked the ball. (John is the agent)
- Patient: The entity that is affected by the action of the verb. Eg: The ball was kicked by John. (The ball is the patient)
- Instrument: The entity used to perform the action of the verb. Eg: John kicked the ball with his foot. (his foot is the instrument)
- Beneficiary: The entity that benefits from the action of the verb. Eg: John kicked the ball for his team. (his team is the beneficiary)
- Location: The place where the action occurs. Eg: John kicked the ball in the garden. (the garden is the location)

**Mnemonics:**

- Agent does the action (who does it?)
- Patient receives the action (who gets it?)
- Instrument is used to do it (with what is it done?)
- Beneficiary benefits from it (who profits?)
- Location is where it occurs (where does it happen?)

**Advantages:**

- Help in semantic analysis and disambiguation of sentences.
- Useful for various NLP tasks like Information Extraction, Question Answering, Machine Translation, etc.
- Provide a structured way to represent the participants in a sentence and their roles.

**Disadvantages:**

- Not all verbs can have all thematic roles.
- Thematic role assignment can be ambiguous in some cases.
- Different theories propose varying sets of thematic roles.
