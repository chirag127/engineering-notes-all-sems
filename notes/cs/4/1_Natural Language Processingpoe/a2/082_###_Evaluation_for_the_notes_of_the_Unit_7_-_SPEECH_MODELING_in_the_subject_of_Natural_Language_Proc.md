### Evaluation for the notes of the Unit 7 - SPEECH MODELING in the subject of Natural Language Processing

- Speech modeling is a challenging task as speech signals are continuous and vary with speakers, speaking styles, emotions, etc. Some evaluation metrics to evaluate the performance of speech modeling techniques are:
- Word Error Rate (WER): It is the percentage of words that are incorrectly recognized. It is calculated as (S+D+I)/N where S is the number of substitutions, D is the number of deletions, I is the number of insertions, and N is the total number of words. Lower WER indicates better performance.
- Sentence Error Rate (SER): It is the percentage of sentences that have one or more errors. It is calculated as E/N where E is the number of sentences with one or more errors and N is the total number of sentences. Lower SER indicates better performance.
- Mean Opinion Score (MOS): It is a subjective measure of the quality/intelligibility of the synthesized/recognized speech. Human evaluators rate the speech on a scale of 1 (bad) to 5 (excellent). Higher MOS indicates better performance.

Some useful mnemonics/learning tricks for speech modeling evaluation metrics:
- WER: Think of word error 'rate' as the 'wrong' words per total words.
- SER: Sentence error 'rate' - sentences with 'errors' per total sentences.
- MOS: Think of 'mean opinion score' as an 'average rating' given by human evaluators.

The evaluation metrics help in comparing different speech modeling approaches and improving the techniques to achieve lower error rates and higher quality speech synthesis/recognition. The metrics can be used for both isolated words as well as continuous speech. Detailed experiments and analyses of results are required to make conclusive judgments about the approaches.
