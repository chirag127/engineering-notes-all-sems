## Unit 6 - SPEECH-ANALYSIS

- Speech analysis is the study of speech sounds to understand the patterns of sounds in language. It focuses on the acoustic characteristics of speech sounds and how they are produced.
- Some key concepts in speech analysis are:
- Phonetics: The study of speech sounds. It focuses on the physical properties of sounds like the place and manner of articulation.
- Phonology: The study of sound patterns in language. It focuses on how sounds are organized and used in a language to convey meaning.
- Acoustic phonetics: The study of the physical properties of speech sounds like frequency, amplitude, and duration.
- Articulatory phonetics: The study of how speech sounds are produced by the vocal organs like the lips, tongue, jaw, and larynx.

**Mnemonics:**
- **Phonetics** focuses on the **physics** of speech, **Phonology** focuses on the sound **patterns**.
- To remember acoustic properties: **Frequency** is about high/low pitch, **Amplitude** is about loud/soft, **Duration** is about long/short sounds.

**Learning Tips:**
- Understand the places and manners of articulation using diagrams of the vocal organs producing speech sounds.
- Listen to speech sounds and try to analyze their acoustic properties to understand the concepts better.
- Study the sound patterns and rules in your native language to understand phonological concepts.
- Practice transcribing speech sounds using the IPA to apply the concepts.
