### Word Classes for the notes of the Unit 2 - WORD LEVEL ANALYSIS in the subject of Natural Language Processing

The major word classes in English are:

1. Nouns: Words that denote a person, place, thing, idea, or concept.
- Mnemonic: Nouns name people, places, things.
- Examples: cat, Alice, Delhi, book, courage.

2. Verbs: Words that denote actions or states of being.
- Mnemonic: Verbs are action words.
- Examples: run, is, has, were, will be.

3. Adjectives: Words that modify or describe nouns.
- Mnemonic: Adjectives add extra information about nouns.
- Examples: big, small, red, messy, carefully.

4. Adverbs: Words that modify or describe verbs, adjectives, or other adverbs.
- Mnemonic: Adverbs add extra information about verbs, adjectives, or other adverbs.
- Examples: quickly, very, extremely, happily.

5. Pronouns: Words that are used in place of nouns to avoid repetition.
- Mnemonic: Pronouns take the place of nouns.
- Examples: I, you, he, she, it, we, you, they.

6. Prepositions: Words that indicate location or direction.
- Mnemonic: Prepositions indicate position.
- Examples: in, on, under, beside, near, from, to, with.

7. Conjunctions: Words that join words, phrases, or clauses.
- Mnemonic: Conjunctions conjoin.
- Examples: and, but, or, so, because, although.

The word classes are important to identify in NLP as the category of a word determines how it functions in a sentence and affects the semantic interpretation. Classifying words into their correct categories allows NLP systems to handle them appropriately.
