### WSD using Supervised for the notes of the Unit 4 - SEMANTICS AND PRAGMATICS in the subject of Natural Language Processing

- Supervised WSD uses machine learning algorithms that learn the correct senses of words from labeled training data.
- The algorithm is trained on a dataset of words along with their correct senses and uses this to classify the senses of new words.
- Advantages: Often achieves high accuracy, adaptable to different domains.
- Disadvantages: Requires large amounts of manually sense-annotated data which is expensive and time-consuming to create.
- Examples: Naive Bayes, Decision Trees, Neural Networks, Support Vector Machines.
- Applications: Improving machine translation, question answering systems, information retrieval.
- Mnemonics: Supervised sense comes from labeled data teaching. More data means more learning and more accuracy.
