### Mathematical And Perceptual for the notes of the Unit 6 - SPEECH-ANALYSIS in the subject of Natural Language Processing

* Speech signal can be studied from a mathematical and perceptual perspective:
** Mathematically, speech can be modeled as a time-varying signal that can be analyzed using tools from signal processing and transducer theory.
** Perceptually, speech can be studied as a means of encoding linguistic information that can be decoded by the auditory system.

* Mathematical techniques for speech analysis include:
** The Fourier transform - to analyze the frequency content of speech signals.
** Linear predictive coding - to model the spectral envelope of speech.
** Filter bank analysis - to decompose speech into frequency bands.

* Perceptual techniques include:
** Studying the perception of speech sounds and speech features like formant frequencies or speech rate.
** Relating mathematical parameters to perceptual measures of speech quality or intelligibility.

* Some advantages of mathematical modeling are:
** Speech analysis and synthesis can be automated using algorithms.
** Insights into the nature of the speech signal and underlying physical mechanisms can be obtained.
** Mathematical models can be used as front-ends to speech recognition and coding systems.

* Some advantages of perceptual modeling are:
** How humans encode and decode speech can be better understood.
** The perceptual salience of speech features can be quantified, which is important for compression and speech synthesis.
** The limitations of human auditory perception can be taken into account in the design of speech systems.

* A combination of mathematical and perceptual techniques is commonly used in speech processing applications to maximize accuracy and effectiveness. Mathematical techniques can focus on what can be computed, while perceptual techniques can focus on what is meaningful or useful to encode and convey.
