### Review Of Digital Signal Processing Concepts for the notes of the Unit 5 - BASIC CONCEPTS of Speech Processing in the subject of Natural Language Processing

- A digital signal is a signal that is represented by a sequence of numbers. Digital signals are used to represent information digitally using discrete values.
- Sampling is the process of converting a continuous analog signal into a discrete digital signal. The sampling rate refers to the number of samples taken per second and must be at least twice the highest frequency component of the signal as per the Nyquist sampling theorem.
- Quantization is the process of approximating the sampled signal to the nearest value in a finite set of values. The number of quantization levels determines the precision of the digital signal.
- Coding is the process of representing the quantized samples in a digital format. The most common coding techniques are pulse code modulation (PCM) and differential PCM (DPCM).
- Some advantages of digital signals are:
    - Ease of processing, storage, and transmission
    - Robustness to noise
    - Flexibility to modify and manipulate the signal
- Some disadvantages of digital signals are:
    - Require analog to digital and digital to analog conversions which can be complex
    - Limited bandwidth due to the Nyquist sampling rate
- Applications of digital signal processing include audio/video recording, transmission, and compression, biomedical signal processing, speech/image processing, radar/sonar, etc.

 Mnemonics and learning tricks:
- For sampling rate: Nyquist rate = 2 x highest frequency
- For quantization: More levels => higher precision but more complex processing
