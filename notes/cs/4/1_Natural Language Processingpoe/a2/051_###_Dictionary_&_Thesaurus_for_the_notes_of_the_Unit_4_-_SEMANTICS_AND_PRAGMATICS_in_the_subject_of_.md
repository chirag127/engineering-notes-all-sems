### Dictionary & Thesaurus for the notes of the Unit 4 - SEMANTICS AND PRAGMATICS in the subject of Natural Language Processing

- Dictionary: A dictionary is a linguistic resource that lists words in a language and provides their meanings. Dictionaries are essential for understanding the meaning of words and resolving lexical ambiguity. Some kinds of dictionaries provide pronunciation, etymology, inflected forms of words, etc. along with definitions.
- Thesaurus: A thesaurus is a reference work that lists words grouped together according to similarity of meaning. It provides synonyms and sometimes antonyms for words. Thesauri are useful for finding alternate ways of expressing ideas and for avoiding repetition in language. They are often used by writers to enrich their writing.

Mnemonics/Learning tricks:
- DIC reminds of 'definition' for Dictionary
- THE reminds of 'synonyms' for Thesaurus

Advantages:
- Help understand meaning of unfamiliar words
- Resolve lexical ambiguity
- Find alternate expressions
- Enrich language

Disadvantages:
- May include obsolete or informal words
- Synonyms may not always convey exact same sense
- Rely on human expertise so prone to errors or bias

Examples:
- Oxford English Dictionary
- Roget's Thesaurus

Applications:
- Natural Language Processing - understand word meanings, resolve ambiguity
- Information Retrieval - expand queries using synonyms
- Text summarization - choose diverse vocabulary
- Machine Translation - choose appropriate words in target language
