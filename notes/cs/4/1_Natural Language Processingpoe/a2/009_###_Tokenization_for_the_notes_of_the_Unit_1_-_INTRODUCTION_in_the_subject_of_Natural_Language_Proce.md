### Tokenization

Tokenization is the process of breaking a stream of text into words, phrases, symbols, or other meaningful elements called tokens. It is a preprocessing step for NLP tasks.

Some key points about Tokenization:

- It converts the input text into a sequence of tokens.
- Tokens can be words, phrases, symbols, numbers, etc. Depending on the requirements of the NLP task.
- It is an essential step as the subsequent processing steps work on the output tokens.
- The tokens must be segmented correctly for the NLP model to work accurately.
- There are broadly two types of Tokenization - Word Tokenization and Sentence Tokenization.
- Word Tokenization breaks the input into words.
- Sentence Tokenization breaks the input into sentences.

Mnemonics/Learning tricks:

- Think of Tokenization as breaking a sentence into 'tokens' (like breaking a bar of chocolate into small pieces).
- The better the Tokenization, the better the downstream NLP tasks perform. So focus on properly segmenting the input.

Advantages:

- It is a crucial pre-processing step for NLP as the subsequent steps work on the tokens.
- It enables the NLP models to understand the input data and extract features.

Disadvantages:

- Incorrect Tokenization can lead to errors in downstream NLP tasks.
- Tokenization of informal language texts containing slang, abbreviations, emojis, etc. can be challenging.

Applications:

- Machine Translation
- Part-of-Speech Tagging
- Named Entity Recognition
- Sentiment Analysis
- Summarization
- Question Answering Systems
