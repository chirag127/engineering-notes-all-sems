### Features for the notes of the Unit 6 - SPEECH-ANALYSIS in the subject of Natural Language Processing

1. Speech Recognition - Convert speech signal into text
- Uses acoustic and language models
- Uses Statistical approaches like HMM, DNN, LSTM, etc.
- Challenges: Accents, Noise, Speech Variations
- Examples: Siri, Alexa, Google Assistant

2. Speaker Recognition - Identify the speaker who uttered a speech signal
- Uses voice characteristics like pitch, formants, vocal tract length, etc.
- Uses Gaussian Mixture Models, i-vectors, etc.
- Applications: Forensics, Security Systems

3. Speech Synthesis - Convert text into speech
- Uses concatenative and statistical approaches
- Challenges: Producing natural sounding speech
- Examples: Screen readers, Automated announcements

* Some Mnemonics:
- For Speech Recognition: Listen Carefully, Understand Casually
- For Speaker Recognition: Speak Naturally, Get Identified Accurately
- For Speech Synthesis: Provide Text, Get Speech

* Advantages: Accessible interfaces, hands-free interactions
* Disadvantages: Error prone, often lack context
* Applications: Virtual assistants, assisted technologies, forensics, etc.
