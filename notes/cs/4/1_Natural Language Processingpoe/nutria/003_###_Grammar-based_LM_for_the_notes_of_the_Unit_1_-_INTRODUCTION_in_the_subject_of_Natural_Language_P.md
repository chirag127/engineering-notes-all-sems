
### Grammar-based LM for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of Natural Language Processing

Grammar-based Language Models (LMs) are a type of language model used in Natural Language Processing (NLP). They are used to generate text that follows the rules of a given grammar. Grammar-based LMs are used to generate natural-sounding text, as well as to parse and understand the meaning of text.

The main components of a grammar-based LM are:

- A grammar: A set of rules that define the structure of valid sentences.
- A lexicon: A collection of words and phrases used to generate text.
- A model: A mathematical representation of the grammar and lexicon that is used to generate text.

Grammar-based LMs are used to generate text that follows the rules of a given grammar. They can also be used to parse and understand the meaning of text. For example, they can be used to generate natural-sounding sentences from a given grammar, or to identify the meaning of a sentence by parsing it according to the grammar.

Grammar-based LMs are powerful tools for understanding and generating natural language. They are used in a variety of applications, such as speech recognition, natural language understanding, and machine translation.
