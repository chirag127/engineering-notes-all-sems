
### Normal Forms for Grammar for the Notes of the Unit 3 - Syntactic Analysis in the Subject of Natural Language Processing

Normal Forms are a set of rules used to simplify the structure of a sentence or phrase. They are important for Natural Language Processing (NLP) because they help computers to better understand the structure of the language and make it easier to parse.

1. Chomsky Normal Form (CNF): This form is used to represent a sentence or phrase as a set of binary trees. It is the most commonly used form for NLP, as it is easy to parse and understand.

2. Greibach Normal Form (GNF): This form is used to represent a sentence or phrase as a set of binary trees in which each node is labeled with a non-terminal. It is more difficult to parse than CNF, but is more efficient in terms of memory usage.

3. Backus-Naur Form (BNF): This form is used to represent a sentence or phrase as a set of rules. It is more difficult to parse than CNF and GNF, but is very efficient in terms of memory usage.

4. Extended Backus-Naur Form (EBNF): This form is an extension of BNF that adds additional rules to make it easier to parse.

Mnemonics:

- CNF: Chomsky Normal Form
- GNF: Greibach Normal Form
- BNF: Backus-Naur Form
- EBNF: Extended Backus-Naur Form

Advantages:

- Normal forms make it easier for computers to parse and understand sentences and phrases.
- They are more efficient in terms of memory usage.
- They can help to reduce the complexity of a sentence or phrase.

Disadvantages:

- Normal forms can be difficult to parse.
- They can be difficult to understand.
- They can be difficult to debug.

Examples:

- CNF: “The cat ate the mouse.”
- GNF: “The cat that was hungry ate the mouse.”
- BNF: “The cat <subject> ate <object> the mouse.”
- EBNF: “The cat <subject> (ate <object> the mouse | chased the mouse).”

Applications:

- Normal forms are used in NLP to help computers better understand the structure of the language.
- They can also be used in compilers to simplify the process of parsing a sentence or phrase.
- They can be used in natural language generation to generate sentences and phrases from a given set of rules.
