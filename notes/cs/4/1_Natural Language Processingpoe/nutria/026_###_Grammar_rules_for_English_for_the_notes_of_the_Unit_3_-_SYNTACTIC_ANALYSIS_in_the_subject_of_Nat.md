
### Grammar rules for English for the notes of the Unit 3 - SYNTACTIC ANALYSIS in the subject of Natural Language Processing

* Syntactic analysis is the process of analyzing the structure of a sentence to determine its syntactic relationships. It is the process of breaking down a sentence into its component parts and identifying the relationship between them.
* Syntactic analysis can be used to identify the parts of speech in a sentence, determine the subject and predicate of a sentence, and identify the relationship between the different parts of a sentence.
* The main components of syntactic analysis are:
  * Lexical Analysis: This is the process of analyzing the words in a sentence to determine the meaning of each word.
* Syntactic Analysis: This is the process of analyzing the structure of a sentence to determine its syntactic relationships. It involves breaking down a sentence into its component parts and identifying the relationship between them.
* Semantic Analysis: This is the process of analyzing the meaning of a sentence to determine its meaning.
* Pragmatic Analysis: This is the process of analyzing the context of a sentence to determine its intended meaning.

In addition to these components, there are several mnemonics and learning tricks that can be used to help remember the different components of syntactic analysis. For example, the acronym "L.S.P.S." can be used to help remember the components of syntactic analysis: Lexical, Syntactic, Semantic, and Pragmatic. Additionally, the phrase "The Cat Sat On The Mat" can be used as a mnemonic to remember the order of the components: Lexical, Syntactic, Semantic, and Pragmatic.
