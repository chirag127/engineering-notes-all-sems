
### Word Senses for the notes of the Unit 4 - SEMANTICS AND PRAGMATICS in the subject of Natural Language Processing
Word senses refer to the different meanings of a single word. In the field of Natural Language Processing (NLP), word senses are useful for understanding the context of a sentence, as well as for accurately interpreting the sentiment of a sentence.

Word senses are often used to differentiate between homonyms, which are words that have the same spelling and pronunciation but different meanings. For example, the word “bank” can mean either a financial institution or the edge of a river.

Word senses are also used to distinguish between polysemous words, which are words with multiple related meanings. For example, the word “run” can mean to move quickly, to operate, or to organize an event.

In NLP, word senses are typically represented using a lexicon, which is a database of words and their associated senses. Lexicons are used to assign a sense to each word in a sentence, which allows NLP applications to interpret the meaning of the sentence more accurately.

Word senses can also be used to detect sentiment in a sentence. For example, if the sentence contains a word with a positive sense, then the sentiment of the sentence is likely to be positive. Similarly, if the sentence contains a word with a negative sense, then the sentiment of the sentence is likely to be negative.

Finally, word senses can be used to identify relationships between words in a sentence. For example, if two words have the same sense, then they are likely to be related. This can be useful for tasks such as question answering and summarization.
