

## Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION

In this unit, we will discuss the fundamentals of computing, including the various components of a computer system, the principles of computer operation, and the different types of software.

**Components of a Computer System**

A computer system is made up of four main components:

* **Hardware**: The physical parts of the computer, such as the processor, memory, and other components.
* **Software**: The instructions and data that tell the computer what to do.
* **Input Devices**: Devices that allow you to enter data into the computer, such as a keyboard or mouse.
* **Output Devices**: Devices that allow the computer to present data to you, such as a monitor or printer.

**Principles of Computer Operation**

The basic principles of computer operation include:

* **Input**: Data is entered into the computer through an input device.
* **Processing**: The data is processed by the computer's processor.
* **Output**: The processed data is presented to the user through an output device.
* **Storage**: Data can be stored on a hard drive or other storage device for later use.

**Types of Software**

There are two main types of software:

* **System Software**: Software that is used to control and manage the hardware and other software on a computer. Examples include the operating system, device drivers, and utility programs. 
* **Application Software**: Software that is used to perform specific tasks, such as word processing, spreadsheets, or games.
