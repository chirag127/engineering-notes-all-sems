
## Unit 3 - SYNTACTIC ANALYSIS

Syntactic Analysis is a branch of linguistics that studies the structure of sentences and the rules for forming them. It is also known as sentence analysis, and is used to understand how language works and how to communicate effectively in different contexts.

### Definition

Syntactic Analysis is the process of analyzing the structure of sentences to determine the meaning of the words and phrases contained within them. This is done by examining the syntax of the sentence, which includes the order of words and the grammatical relationships between them.

### Components

Syntactic Analysis consists of three main components:

1. **Lexical Analysis**: This involves identifying the individual words and phrases in a sentence and determining their meaning.
2. **Syntactic Rules**: These are the rules that govern the order of words in a sentence and the grammatical relationships between them.
3. **Semantic Analysis**: This involves determining the meaning of the sentence as a whole.

### Process

The process of Syntactic Analysis involves the following steps:

1. **Identify the Sentence Structure**: The first step is to identify the structure of the sentence. This includes determining the subject, verb, and object, as well as any other parts of speech that are present.

2. **Analyze the Syntactic Rules**: The next step is to analyze the syntactic rules that govern the sentence. This involves looking at the order of words and the grammatical relationships between them.

3. **Determine the Meaning**: The final step is to determine the meaning of the sentence as a whole. This involves looking at the context of the sentence and the words used.

### Mnemonics and Learning Tricks

Mnemonics can be a helpful tool for remembering the components of Syntactic Analysis. One such mnemonic is:

**S**ubject, **V**erb, **O**bject, **S**yntactic **R**ules, **S**emantic **A**nalysis.

This mnemonic can be used to remember the components of Syntactic Analysis in the correct order.
