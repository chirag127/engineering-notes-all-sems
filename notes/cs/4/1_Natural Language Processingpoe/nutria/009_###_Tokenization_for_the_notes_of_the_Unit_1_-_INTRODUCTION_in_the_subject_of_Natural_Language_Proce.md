
### Tokenization for the Notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the Subject of Natural Language Processing

Tokenization is the process of breaking a stream of text up into words, phrases, symbols, or other meaningful elements called tokens. Tokenization is an essential step in natural language processing (NLP) as it helps in understanding the context of a sentence by breaking it into its component parts. 

Tokenization can be done in several ways. The simplest approach is to split the text into individual words, also known as word tokenization. Other approaches include breaking the text into sentences, phrases, or even individual characters.
