
## Unit 2 - WORD LEVEL ANALYSIS

Word Level Analysis is a process of analyzing the words of a text to identify the various components of a language. It is used in natural language processing (NLP) to understand the meaning of a text. Word Level Analysis includes techniques such as tokenization, stemming, lemmatization, part-of-speech tagging, and other methods.

### Tokenization
Tokenization is the process of breaking down a text into individual words or tokens. It is the first step of any NLP task, as it helps to identify the words of a text. Tokenization can be done using regular expressions, or using methods such as the NLTK library.

### Stemming
Stemming is the process of reducing a word to its root form. This is done by removing suffixes, prefixes, and other affixes. It helps to reduce the size of the vocabulary used in the text and to identify the common words used in the text.

### Lemmatization
Lemmatization is the process of reducing a word to its base form. It is similar to stemming, but it is more accurate as it takes into account the context of the word. Lemmatization is used to identify the different forms of the same word, such as verb tenses, plurals, etc.

### Part-of-Speech Tagging
Part-of-Speech (POS) tagging is the process of assigning a tag to each word in a text. This helps to identify the type of each word, such as nouns, verbs, adjectives, etc. It is used to understand the structure of a sentence, and to identify the relationships between words.

### Mnemonics
Mnemonics are memory aids that can help to remember the various components of a language. For example, the mnemonic “Nouns, Verbs, Adjectives, Adverbs” can be used to remember the four parts of speech. Mnemonics can be helpful to remember the various components of a language.

### Learning Tricks
Learning tricks are techniques that can help to understand and remember the components of a language. For example, using flashcards to remember words or using diagrams to understand the structure of a sentence can be helpful to learn and understand the components of a language.
