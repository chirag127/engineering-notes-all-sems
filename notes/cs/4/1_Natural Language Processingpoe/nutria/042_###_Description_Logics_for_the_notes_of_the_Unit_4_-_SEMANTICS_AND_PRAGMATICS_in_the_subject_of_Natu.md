
### Description Logics for the notes of the Unit 4 - SEMANTICS AND PRAGMATICS in the subject of Natural Language Processing

Description logics (DLs) are a family of knowledge representation languages used to represent the meaning of concepts and relationships in a knowledge domain. DLs are used in many applications, including automated reasoning, natural language processing, and artificial intelligence.

DLs are based on a formal logic and can be used to represent knowledge in a way that is both declarative and computable. DLs are used to represent and reason about the meaning of concepts and relationships in a knowledge domain. They are also used to represent and reason about the meaning of natural language sentences.

The main components of a DL are:

* A set of symbols to represent concepts, relationships, and sentences.
* A set of axioms, or rules, to describe the meaning of the symbols.
* A set of inference rules to infer new knowledge from the axioms.

DLs are used in many applications, including natural language processing, automated reasoning, and artificial intelligence. They are also used to represent and reason about the meaning of natural language sentences.
