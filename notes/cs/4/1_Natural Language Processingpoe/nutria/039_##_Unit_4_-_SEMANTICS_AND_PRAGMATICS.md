

## Unit 4 - SEMANTICS AND PRAGMATICS

Semantics and pragmatics are two distinct branches of linguistics that study the meaning of words and phrases in a language.

**Semantics** is the study of the literal meaning of words and phrases. It focuses on the relationship between words, the structure of sentences, and the context in which they are used. Semantics is concerned with the meaning of words and phrases in isolation, as well as their meaning in relation to each other.

**Pragmatics** is the study of the meaning of words and phrases in their context. It focuses on how the context in which words are used affects their meaning. Pragmatics also looks at how the speaker's intent and the listener's interpretation of the words can affect the meaning of the words.

The two branches of linguistics are closely related, as they both study the meaning of words and phrases. However, the focus of each branch is different. Semantics looks at the literal meaning of words and phrases, while pragmatics looks at how the context in which words are used affects their meaning.

Mnemonics and learning tricks for Semantics and Pragmatics:

- **S**emantics is the **S**tudy of the literal meaning of words and phrases.
- **P**ragmatics is the **P**ractice of how the context in which words are used affects their meaning.
- **S**emantics looks at the **S**tructure of sentences and the context in which they are used.
- **P**ragmatics looks at how the **P**eaker's intent and the listener's interpretation of the words can affect the meaning of the words.
