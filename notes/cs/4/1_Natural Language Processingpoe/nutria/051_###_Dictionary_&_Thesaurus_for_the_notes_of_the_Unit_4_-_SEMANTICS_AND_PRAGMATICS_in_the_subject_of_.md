
### Dictionary & Thesaurus for the notes of the Unit 4 - SEMANTICS AND PRAGMATICS in the subject of Natural Language Processing

A dictionary and thesaurus are two important tools used in Natural Language Processing (NLP). A dictionary is a collection of words and their definitions, while a thesaurus is a collection of words and their synonyms. Both of these tools are used to help identify and understand the meanings of words in a given text.

Dictionaries are used to look up the meaning of words, and to help identify the correct spelling of words. They can also be used to identify the root of a word, and to identify the part of speech associated with a word.

Thesauri are used to look up the synonyms of words. This can be useful for finding alternative words to use in a sentence, or for finding related words that can be used to describe a concept.

Both dictionaries and thesauri can be used to identify the meanings of words in a given text, as well as to identify the correct spelling of words. They can also be used to identify the root of a word, and to identify the part of speech associated with a word.

When using a dictionary or thesaurus, it is important to remember that the definitions of words may vary depending on the context in which they are used. It is also important to remember that the synonyms provided by a thesaurus may not always be the best fit for a given context.
