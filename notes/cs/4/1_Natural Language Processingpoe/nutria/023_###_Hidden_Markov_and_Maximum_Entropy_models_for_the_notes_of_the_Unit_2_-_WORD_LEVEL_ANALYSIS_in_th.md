
### Hidden Markov and Maximum Entropy models for the notes of the Unit 2 - WORD LEVEL ANALYSIS in the subject of Natural Language Processing

- **Hidden Markov Model (HMM):** HMM is a probabilistic model used to predict the probability of a sequence of observations or hidden states. It is a Markov Chain model, which means that the probability of the next state is only dependent on the current state. The model consists of a set of states, and the probability of transitioning from one state to another is determined by the transition probabilities.

- **Maximum Entropy Model (MEM):** MEM is a probabilistic model used to estimate the probability of a sequence of observations or hidden states. It is a Bayesian model, which means that the probability of the next state is determined by the prior knowledge of the data. The model consists of a set of parameters, and the probability of transitioning from one state to another is determined by the prior knowledge of the data.

- **Advantages of HMM and MEM:**
    - HMM and MEM are both powerful models for predicting the probability of a sequence of observations or hidden states.
    - HMM and MEM are both able to capture complex patterns in data.
    - HMM and MEM are both able to learn from data.
    - HMM and MEM are both able to generalize to unseen data.

- **Disadvantages of HMM and MEM:**
    - HMM and MEM can be computationally expensive.
    - HMM and MEM can be difficult to interpret.
    - HMM and MEM can be difficult to train.
    - HMM and MEM can be prone to overfitting.

    - HMM stands for Hidden Markov Model.
    - MEM stands for Maximum Entropy Model.
