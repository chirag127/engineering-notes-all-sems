
### Treebanks for the Notes of the Unit 3 - Syntactic Analysis in the Subject of Natural Language Processing

* Treebanks are a type of text corpus that contain syntactic annotations. They are used to help create and evaluate natural language processing (NLP) systems.
* Treebanks are composed of sentences that are annotated with part-of-speech tags, syntactic structures, and other linguistic information.
* Treebanks can be used to train machine learning models to identify the syntactic structure of sentences.
* Treebanks can also be used to evaluate the accuracy of existing NLP systems.
* Treebanks can be created manually or automatically. Manual creation requires a linguist to annotate each sentence with the desired information. Automated creation requires the use of algorithms to automatically annotate sentences.
* Treebanks are important for the development of NLP systems, as they provide the data needed to train and evaluate the systems.
* Mnemonic: Treebanks are like a bank of trees, providing the data and structure needed to understand language.
