
### Word Classes for the notes of the Unit 2 - WORD LEVEL ANALYSIS in the subject of Natural Language Processing

Word classes are the categories of words used to describe the function of a particular word in a sentence. In Natural Language Processing (NLP), word classes are used to determine the meaning and context of a sentence.

##### Mnemonics and Learning Tricks

One way to remember the different word classes is to use the acronym **P.A.R.T.Y**. This stands for:

* **P**ronouns
* **A**djectives
* **R**elative Clauses
* **T**ransitive Verbs
* **Y**es/No Questions

Another way to remember the different word classes is to use the acronym **N.O.U.N.S**. This stands for:

* **N**ouns
* **O**bjects
* **U**ncountable Nouns
* **N**egative Adverbs
* **S**ubjects

#### Advantages

Using word classes to determine the meaning and context of a sentence has several advantages. It helps to reduce ambiguity in language and make it easier to understand. It also helps to make it easier to translate languages, as the word classes can be used as a guide to translate a sentence into another language.

#### Disadvantages

The main disadvantage of using word classes is that it can be difficult to determine the exact meaning of a sentence. This is because some words can belong to multiple word classes, and it can be difficult to determine which one is the most appropriate. It can also be difficult to determine the exact context of a sentence, as some words can have multiple meanings depending on the context.

#### Examples

Here are some examples of how word classes can be used to determine the meaning and context of a sentence:

* **Pronouns**: She went to the store.
* **Adjectives**: The red car was speeding down the street.
* **Relative Clauses**: The book that I bought was on sale.
* **Transitive Verbs**: She ate the sandwich.
* **Yes/No Questions**: Did she go to the store?
* **Nouns**: The dog barked loudly.
* **Objects**: She threw the ball.
* **Uncountable Nouns**: The water was cold.
* **Negative Adverbs**: She never went to the store.
* **Subjects**: The cat meowed.
