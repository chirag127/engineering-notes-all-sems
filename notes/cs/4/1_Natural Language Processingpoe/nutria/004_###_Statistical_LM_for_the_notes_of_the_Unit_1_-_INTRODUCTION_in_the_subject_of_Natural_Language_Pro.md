
### Statistical LM for the Notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the Subject of Natural Language Processing

* Statistical Language Models (SLMs) are probabilistic models used to predict the likelihood of a sequence of words. 
* SLMs are widely used in Natural Language Processing (NLP) tasks like speech recognition, machine translation, text summarization, and question-answering.
* SLMs are typically built using a corpus of text, which is then used to create a probability distribution over all possible word sequences. 
* The probability of a given sequence is then calculated by multiplying the probabilities of each word in the sequence.
* SLMs are used to calculate the probability of a sentence or phrase by summing the probabilities of all possible word sequences that could form that sentence or phrase.
* SLMs can also be used to generate new sentences or phrases by selecting the most probable word sequences.
* SLMs can be further improved by using techniques like smoothing and backoff, which help to reduce the data sparsity problem.
* Mnemonics and learning tricks for SLMs include:
  * **S**tatistical - SLMs are probabilistic models
  * **L**anguage - SLMs are used for NLP tasks
  * **M**odels - SLMs are used to predict the likelihood of a sequence of words
  * **P**robability - The probability of a given sequence is calculated by multiplying the probabilities of each word in the sequence
