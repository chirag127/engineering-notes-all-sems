

### Interpolation and Backoff for the notes of the Unit 2 - WORD LEVEL ANALYSIS in the subject of Natural Language Processing

Interpolation and backoff are two important concepts in Natural Language Processing (NLP). They are used to improve the accuracy of language models, which are used to generate text or speech.

**Interpolation** is a technique used to combine the probabilities of two or more language models. This allows the model to make predictions based on a combination of the models, which can result in more accurate predictions.

**Backoff** is a technique used to reduce the probability of rare words. This is done by replacing rare words with more common words. This helps to improve the accuracy of the language model by reducing the probability of rare words, which tend to be less accurate than more common words.

Mnemonics and learning tricks to help remember Interpolation and Backoff:

- **I**nterpolation combines **P**robabilities of multiple language models
- **B**ackoff reduces the probability of **R**are words
