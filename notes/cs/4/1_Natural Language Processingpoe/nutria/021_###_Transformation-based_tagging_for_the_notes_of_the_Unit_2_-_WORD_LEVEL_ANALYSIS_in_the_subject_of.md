
### Transformation-based tagging for the notes of the Unit 2 - WORD LEVEL ANALYSIS in the subject of Natural Language Processing

Transformation-based tagging is a method of natural language processing (NLP) used to assign parts of speech to words in a sentence. It is based on the idea of transforming a sentence into a form that can be easily understood by a computer. The process involves analyzing the sentence for its grammatical structure and then applying a set of rules to determine the part of speech for each word.

The main advantage of transformation-based tagging is that it is relatively fast and accurate. It is also able to handle complex sentences, which is not possible with other methods of NLP. Additionally, it is capable of dealing with ambiguity, which is often present in natural language.

Mnemonics and Learning Tricks: 
The acronym “TBT” can be used to remember transformation-based tagging. It stands for “Transform, Breakdown, Tag.” This acronym can be used to remember the three main steps of the process: transforming the sentence into a form that can be understood by a computer, breaking down the sentence into its components, and then tagging each word with its part of speech. Additionally, it can be helpful to think of transformation-based tagging as a process of “transforming” a sentence into a form that can be understood by a computer.
