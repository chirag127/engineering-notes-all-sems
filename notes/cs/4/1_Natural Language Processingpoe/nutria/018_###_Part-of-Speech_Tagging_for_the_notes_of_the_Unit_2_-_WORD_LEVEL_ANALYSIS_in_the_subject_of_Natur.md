
### Part-of-Speech Tagging for the notes of the Unit 2 - WORD LEVEL ANALYSIS in the subject of Natural Language Processing

Part-of-Speech (POS) tagging is a process of assigning a part-of-speech label to each word in a given sentence. It is one of the core tasks of Natural Language Processing (NLP). In this unit, we will discuss the various techniques used for POS tagging, their advantages and disadvantages, and the applications of POS tagging. 

##### Techniques

There are two main techniques used for POS tagging:

1. Rule-based Tagging: This technique relies on a set of manually crafted rules to assign part-of-speech labels to words. The rules are based on the context of the sentence, the morphology of the words, and the syntactic structures of the sentences.

2. Statistical Tagging: This technique uses machine learning algorithms to assign part-of-speech labels to words. It relies on large annotated corpora to train the model. The model is then used to tag unseen sentences.

##### Advantages

1. Rule-based tagging is relatively easy to implement and can be used to tag a wide variety of languages.

2. Statistical tagging is more accurate than rule-based tagging and can be used to tag a wide variety of languages.

3. POS tagging can be used to identify the syntactic structure of a sentence, which can be used for further tasks such as parsing and machine translation.

4. POS tagging can also be used for information retrieval and question answering.

##### Disadvantages

1. Rule-based tagging is prone to errors due to the complexity of natural language.

2. Statistical tagging is computationally expensive and requires large annotated corpora to train the model.

3. POS tagging is a difficult task and can be affected by the context of a sentence.

##### Applications

1. POS tagging can be used for information retrieval and question answering.

2. POS tagging can be used to identify the syntactic structure of a sentence, which can be used for further tasks such as parsing and machine translation.

3. POS tagging can also be used for text summarization and text classification.
