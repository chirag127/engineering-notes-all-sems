
### Evaluating N-grams for the notes of the Unit 2 - WORD LEVEL ANALYSIS in the subject of Natural Language Processing

N-grams are sequences of words used to analyze text. They are commonly used in natural language processing (NLP) to identify patterns in text and to measure the similarity of two texts. N-grams can also be used to evaluate the quality of a text, such as its readability or its accuracy.

An N-gram is a sequence of N words. A 1-gram (or unigram) is a single word, a 2-gram (or bigram) is two words, a 3-gram (or trigram) is three words, and so on. N-grams can be used to measure the similarity of two texts by counting the number of times the same N-grams appear in both texts. 

N-grams can also be used to measure the readability of a text. For example, a text with a lot of long words and complex sentence structures may be harder to read than a text with shorter words and simpler sentence structures. 

N-grams can also be used to measure the accuracy of a text. For example, a text that contains a lot of spelling mistakes may be more difficult to understand than a text with fewer mistakes.

In summary, N-grams can be used to evaluate the quality of a text, such as its readability or accuracy. They can also be used to measure the similarity of two texts. Mnemonics and learning tricks can be helpful in understanding and remembering N-grams.
