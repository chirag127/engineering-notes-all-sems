### Statistical LM for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of Natural Language Processing

In the field of natural language processing, language models play a crucial role in understanding and generating human language. A statistical language model (SLM) is a type of language model that uses statistical methods to predict the probability of a sequence of words in a language. It is a mathematical model that tries to capture the underlying distribution of words in a language.

SLMs are trained on a corpus of text data, which is used to estimate the probabilities of words and sequences of words. The most common type of SLM is the n-gram model, which estimates the probability of a word based on the previous n-1 words. For example, a bigram model would estimate the probability of a word given the previous word.

There are several advantages of using SLMs, including:

- SLMs can be trained on large amounts of data, which allows them to capture the nuances and complexities of human language.
- SLMs can be used for a wide range of natural language processing tasks, including speech recognition, machine translation, and text classification.
- SLMs can be easily adapted to different languages and domains, as long as there is enough training data available.

However, there are also some disadvantages of using SLMs, including:

- SLMs can suffer from the problem of data sparsity, which occurs when there are not enough training examples for rare words or sequences of words.
- SLMs can be biased towards the training data, which can lead to errors and inaccuracies.
- SLMs may not capture the semantic meaning of words, which can lead to incorrect predictions.

To overcome these challenges, researchers have developed more advanced types of SLMs, such as neural language models, which use deep learning techniques to model the complex relationships between words in a language.

- SLMs use statistics to predict the probability of a sequence of words in a language.
- N-gram models estimate the probability of a word based on the previous n-1 words.

- Advanced SLMs, such as neural language models, use deep learning techniques to overcome these challenges.
