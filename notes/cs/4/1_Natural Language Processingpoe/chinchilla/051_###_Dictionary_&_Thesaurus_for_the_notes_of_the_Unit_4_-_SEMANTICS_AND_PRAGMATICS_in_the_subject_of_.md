### Dictionary & Thesaurus for the notes of the Unit 4 - SEMANTICS AND PRAGMATICS in the subject of Natural Language Processing

In natural language processing, dictionaries and thesauri are two essential resources for understanding the meaning of words and their relationships with other words. In this unit, we will cover the following topics related to dictionaries and thesauri:

1. **Dictionary**
- A dictionary is a resource that provides definitions and other information about words.
- It is used to look up the meaning of a word and to understand how it is used in a sentence.
- Dictionaries can be monolingual (in one language) or bilingual (in two languages).
- Some of the popular dictionaries used in natural language processing include WordNet, Oxford English Dictionary, and Merriam-Webster Dictionary.

2. **Thesaurus**
- A thesaurus is a resource that provides synonyms (words with similar meanings) and antonyms (words with opposite meanings) for a given word.
- It is used to expand vocabulary and to find alternative words to use in a sentence.
- Thesauri can be monolingual or bilingual.
- Some of the popular thesauri used in natural language processing include Roget's Thesaurus, WordNet, and Oxford Thesaurus of English.

#### Mnemonics and learning tricks

- To remember the difference between a dictionary and a thesaurus, think of a dictionary as a "definer" and a thesaurus as a "synonym finder".
- To remember some of the popular dictionaries and thesauri used in natural language processing, use acronyms such as WNO (WordNet and Oxford), MWM (Merriam-Webster and Roget's Thesaurus), or OTE (Oxford Thesaurus of English).
