### Speech Fundamentals for the notes of the Unit 5 - BASIC CONCEPTS of Speech Processing in the subject of Natural Language Processing

Speech is a complex process that involves the production, transmission, and perception of sound waves. Speech processing is the study of how computers can analyze and understand human speech. In this unit, we will explore the basic concepts of speech processing and the fundamentals of speech.

#### Phonetics

Phonetics is the study of the sounds of human speech. The sounds of speech are produced by the movement of air through the vocal tract, which includes the lungs, trachea, larynx, pharynx, and mouth. There are two types of sounds in speech: vowels and consonants.

- Vowels: Vowels are produced when air flows freely through the mouth. There are five basic vowels: A, E, I, O, and U. The position of the tongue, lips, and jaw determines the sound of the vowel.
- Consonants: Consonants are produced when the airflow is obstructed in some way. There are three types of consonants: voiced, voiceless, and nasal. Voiced consonants are produced when the vocal cords vibrate, while voiceless consonants are produced when they do not. Nasal consonants are produced when air flows through the nasal cavity.

#### Spectrogram

A spectrogram is a visual representation of the frequency content of a sound wave over time. It is a useful tool for analyzing speech because it can show how different sounds are produced in the vocal tract. In a spectrogram, time is represented on the horizontal axis, while frequency is represented on the vertical axis. The intensity of the sound is indicated by the color or shading of the spectrogram.

#### Formants

Formants are the resonant frequencies of the vocal tract. They are determined by the shape and size of the vocal tract and are responsible for the distinctive sound of different vowels. Formants can be identified in a spectrogram as regions of high intensity that correspond to the resonant frequencies of the vocal tract.

#### Prosody

Prosody refers to the rhythm, intonation, and stress patterns of speech. It is an important aspect of speech processing because it can convey information about the mood, intention, and emphasis of a speaker. Prosody can be analyzed using techniques such as pitch tracking, which identifies changes in the fundamental frequency of the voice, and duration analysis, which measures the length of different segments of speech.

#### Learning Tricks and Mnemonics

- To remember the five basic vowels, use the mnemonic "AEIOU" (A-E-I-O-U).
- To remember the difference between voiced and voiceless consonants, think of the vocal cords as "vibrating" for voiced consonants and "still" for voiceless consonants.
- To remember the three types of consonants, use the mnemonic "VAN" (Voiced, Voiceless, Nasal).

In conclusion, speech processing is a complex field that requires a deep understanding of speech fundamentals such as phonetics, spectrograms, formants, and prosody. By mastering these concepts and using learning tricks and mnemonics, you can improve your ability to analyze and understand human speech.
