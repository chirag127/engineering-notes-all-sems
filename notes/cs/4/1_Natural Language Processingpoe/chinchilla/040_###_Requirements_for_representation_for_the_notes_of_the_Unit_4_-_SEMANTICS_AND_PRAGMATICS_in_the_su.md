### Requirements for representation for the notes of the Unit 4 - SEMANTICS AND PRAGMATICS in the subject of Natural Language Processing

In the field of Natural Language Processing, Semantics and Pragmatics are two important subfields that deal with the meaning and interpretation of language. To effectively represent semantics and pragmatics in computational systems, there are certain requirements that need to be fulfilled. Here are some of the requirements for representation for the notes of the Unit 4 - SEMANTICS AND PRAGMATICS in the subject of Natural Language Processing:

1. Formality: The representation of semantics and pragmatics needs to be formal and precise to enable computational systems to understand the meaning of language. This requires the use of logic-based formalisms and ontologies.

2. Comprehensiveness: The representation should be comprehensive enough to cover all the possible meanings of a sentence. It should be able to handle ambiguity and resolve it by considering the context of the sentence.

3. Expressivity: The representation should be expressive enough to capture the nuances of language. It should be able to handle negation, tense, aspect, modality, and other linguistic phenomena.

4. Robustness: The representation should be robust enough to handle variations in language use, including dialects, slang, and idiosyncratic language.

5. Efficiency: The representation should be efficient enough to enable computational systems to process it quickly and accurately.

6. Interoperability: The representation should be interoperable with other systems and languages to enable seamless communication between different computational systems.

- To remember the requirement of formal representation, think of the word "formal" as meaning "precise and structured".
- To remember the requirement of comprehensive representation, think of the word "comprehensive" as meaning "covering all possible meanings".
- To remember the requirement of expressive representation, think of the word "expressive" as meaning "able to capture nuances".
- To remember the requirement of robust representation, think of the word "robust" as meaning "able to handle variations".
- To remember the requirement of efficient representation, think of the word "efficient" as meaning "quick and accurate".
- To remember the requirement of interoperable representation, think of the word "interoperable" as meaning "compatible with other systems".
