## Unit 2 - WORD LEVEL ANALYSIS

Word level analysis is a crucial aspect of natural language processing (NLP) that involves the identification and extraction of meaningful information from a given text at the word level. In this unit, we will cover several important concepts and techniques related to word level analysis, including:

1. Tokenization: 
Tokenization is the process of breaking a text into individual words or tokens. It is a critical step in NLP as most of the downstream tasks rely on word-level information. There are various tokenization techniques available such as whitespace tokenization, sentence tokenization, and wordpiece tokenization.

2. Stemming: 
Stemming is the process of reducing a word to its base or root form, by removing any suffixes or prefixes. This technique is used to reduce the number of unique words in a text corpus and to group words with similar meanings.

3. Lemmatization: 
Lemmatization is similar to stemming, but instead of just removing affixes, it transforms the word to its base form based on its part of speech. This technique is more accurate than stemming but is computationally expensive.

4. Part of Speech (POS) Tagging: 
POS tagging is the process of labeling each word in a text corpus with its corresponding part of speech, such as noun, verb, adjective, etc. This information is used in various NLP tasks such as named entity recognition, sentiment analysis, and machine translation.

5. Named Entity Recognition (NER): 
NER is the process of identifying and classifying named entities in a text corpus, such as people, organizations, locations, and dates. This technique is used in various NLP applications such as question answering, sentiment analysis, and information retrieval.

6. Word Embeddings: 
Word embeddings are a type of representation of words in a high-dimensional vector space. They are used to capture the semantic and syntactic relationships between words and are widely used in various NLP tasks such as text classification, sentiment analysis, and machine translation.

Mnemonics and Learning Tricks:

- For remembering the difference between stemming and lemmatization, you can think of "stemming" as cutting off the branches (suffixes) of a word to get to the stem, while "lemmatization" is like finding the root of a word by digging deeper and understanding its part of speech.

- To remember the different parts of speech, you can use the acronym "FANBOYS" which stands for conjunctions (for, and, nor, but, or, yet, so), or "WHALES" which stands for pronouns (who, whom, whose, which, what, where, when, why, how, that).

In conclusion, word level analysis is an essential aspect of NLP and involves several techniques such as tokenization, stemming, lemmatization, POS tagging, NER, and word embeddings. Understanding these techniques and their applications can help in developing more accurate and efficient NLP models.
