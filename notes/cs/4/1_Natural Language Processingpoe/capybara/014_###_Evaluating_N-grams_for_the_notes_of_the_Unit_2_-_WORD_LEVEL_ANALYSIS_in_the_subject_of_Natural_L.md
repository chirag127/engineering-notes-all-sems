### Evaluating N-grams for the notes of the Unit 2 - WORD LEVEL ANALYSIS in the subject of Natural Language Processing

N-grams are a commonly used technique in Natural Language Processing (NLP) for analyzing the frequency of word combinations in text data. In this topic, we will discuss how to evaluate N-grams and their importance in Word Level Analysis.

#### What are N-grams?

N-grams are contiguous sequences of n items, where the items can be words, letters, or any other unit of text. For example, in the sentence "I love to eat pizza", the bigrams (2-grams) would be "I love", "love to", "to eat", and "eat pizza", while the trigrams (3-grams) would be "I love to", "love to eat", and "to eat pizza". N-grams are used to identify frequent patterns of words in text data.

#### Why are N-grams important in Word Level Analysis?

N-grams are important in Word Level Analysis because they can help us identify common phrases and language patterns in text data. By identifying these patterns, we can better understand the meaning and context of the text. For example, if we are analyzing customer reviews of a product, we can use N-grams to identify common phrases that customers use to describe the product, such as "easy to use" or "durable construction".

#### How do we evaluate N-grams?

There are several techniques for evaluating N-grams, including:

1. Frequency Count: This involves counting the number of times each N-gram appears in the text data. This can help us identify the most common N-grams and their frequency of occurrence.

2. Mutual Information: This measures the degree of association between two words in an N-gram. It helps us identify which words are most likely to appear together in the text data.

3. Pointwise Mutual Information: This is similar to Mutual Information, but it takes into account the frequency of individual words in addition to the N-gram frequency. This can help us identify which N-grams are most significant in the text data.

#### Mnemonic

One helpful mnemonic for remembering the different techniques for evaluating N-grams is "FMP" - Frequency Count, Mutual Information, and Pointwise Mutual Information.

#### Advantages of N-grams

1. They can help us identify common language patterns in text data.

2. They can be used to build language models for NLP tasks such as machine translation and speech recognition.

3. They are computationally efficient and can be processed quickly on large datasets.

#### Disadvantages of N-grams

1. They can be sensitive to noise and outliers in the text data.

2. They may not capture the full meaning and context of the text.

#### Conclusion

N-grams are an important technique in Word Level Analysis and can help us identify common language patterns in text data. By evaluating N-grams using techniques such as frequency count, mutual information, and pointwise mutual information, we can better understand the meaning and context of the text.
