### Mathematical And Perceptual for the notes of the Unit 6 - SPEECH-ANALYSIS

Speech analysis is the process of studying and understanding the various characteristics of speech signals. It involves the use of mathematical and perceptual methods to analyze speech signals and extract useful information from them. In this unit, we will discuss the mathematical and perceptual aspects of speech analysis.

#### Mathematical aspects

1. Fourier analysis: Fourier analysis is a mathematical technique used to decompose a complex signal into its constituent frequency components. It is used in speech analysis to analyze the frequency content of speech signals.

2. Linear predictive coding (LPC): LPC is a method used to model the spectral envelope of speech signals. It is used to compress speech signals for transmission and storage.

3. Mel-frequency cepstral coefficients (MFCCs): MFCCs are a feature extraction technique used in speech recognition. They are derived from the short-time Fourier transform of speech signals and are used to represent the spectral envelope of the speech signal.

#### Perceptual aspects

1. Auditory perception: Auditory perception refers to the way in which the human ear processes sound. It is important in speech analysis as it helps us understand how humans perceive and interpret speech signals.

2. Speech perception: Speech perception refers to the way in which humans interpret speech signals. It involves the use of various cues such as pitch, intensity, and duration to understand the meaning of speech.

3. Phonetics: Phonetics is the study of the physical properties of speech sounds. It is important in speech analysis as it helps us understand how speech sounds are produced and perceived.

#### Mnemonics and learning tricks

1. To remember the different mathematical techniques used in speech analysis, you can use the mnemonic "FLM". FLM stands for Fourier analysis, Linear predictive coding, and Mel-frequency cepstral coefficients.

2. To remember the different perceptual aspects of speech analysis, you can use the mnemonic "ASP". ASP stands for Auditory perception, Speech perception, and Phonetics.

In conclusion, understanding the mathematical and perceptual aspects of speech analysis is crucial in Natural Language Processing. By utilizing these techniques, we can analyze speech signals and extract useful information from them. Remembering the above-mentioned mnemonics and learning tricks can help you recall the different techniques used in speech analysis.
