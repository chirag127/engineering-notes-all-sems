## Unit 6 - SPEECH-ANALYSIS

Speech analysis is a crucial component of communication science, which is concerned with understanding the structure of human speech and how it is produced, perceived, and interpreted. In this unit, we will study the various aspects of speech analysis, including its techniques, applications, and limitations.

### Techniques of Speech Analysis

The following are some of the techniques used in speech analysis:

1. Spectrogram Analysis: It is a graphical representation of the speech signal that shows the frequency content of the signal over time. It helps in identifying the various speech sounds and their parameters.

2. Pitch Analysis: It is the measurement of the fundamental frequency of the speech signal, which determines the pitch of the speaker's voice.

3. Formant Analysis: It is the measurement of the resonant frequencies of the vocal tract, which determine the quality of the speech sounds.

4. Prosodic Analysis: It is the study of the rhythm, intonation, and stress patterns of the speech signal, which convey the speaker's emotions and attitudes.

### Applications of Speech Analysis

Speech analysis has various applications in different fields, such as:

1. Forensic Analysis: It is used to analyze recorded speech samples for identifying and comparing the voiceprints of suspects and witnesses.

2. Speech Recognition: It is used in speech-to-text software, which converts spoken language into written text.

3. Speech Therapy: It is used to diagnose and treat speech disorders, such as stuttering, lisping, and voice disorders.

### Limitations of Speech Analysis

Speech analysis also has some limitations, such as:

1. Complexity: Speech analysis is a complex process that requires expertise in various fields, such as linguistics, acoustics, and signal processing.

2. Accuracy: The accuracy of speech analysis depends on the quality of the speech signal, which can be affected by environmental factors, such as background noise and speaker's accent.

3. Privacy Concerns: The use of speech analysis for surveillance and identification purposes raises privacy concerns, as it involves the collection and analysis of personal data.

### Mnemonics and Learning Tricks

One of the mnemonics to remember the techniques of speech analysis is the acronym SPFP, which stands for Spectrogram Analysis, Pitch Analysis, Formant Analysis, and Prosodic Analysis. This can help students to remember the different techniques and their applications in speech analysis.

In conclusion, speech analysis is an essential component of communication science, which helps in understanding the structure and meaning of human speech. By studying the techniques and applications of speech analysis, students can gain a deeper insight into the complexity of human language and communication.
