## Unit 7 - SPEECH MODELING

Speech modeling is the process of creating mathematical models that describe the characteristics of human speech. It is an essential component of speech processing, speech recognition, and speech synthesis. In this unit, we will learn about various techniques for speech modeling.

### 1. Acoustic Modeling

Acoustic modeling is the process of creating a mathematical representation of the relationship between speech signals and the corresponding linguistic units, such as phonemes, words, and sentences. It is one of the most important components of speech recognition systems. The main techniques used in acoustic modeling are Hidden Markov Models (HMMs) and Gaussian Mixture Models (GMMs).

### 2. Language Modeling

Language modeling is the process of creating a statistical model that predicts the probability of a word or a sequence of words in a given sentence. It is used in speech recognition systems to improve the accuracy of speech recognition by incorporating knowledge about the language being spoken. The main techniques used in language modeling are n-gram models and neural network models.

### 3. Articulatory Modeling

Articulatory modeling is the process of creating a mathematical model that describes the movements of the articulators (tongue, lips, jaw, etc.) during speech production. It is used in speech synthesis to generate speech that sounds natural and human-like. The main techniques used in articulatory modeling are the Task-Dynamic Model and the Articulatory Phonology Model.

### 4. Prosodic Modeling

Prosodic modeling is the process of creating a mathematical model that describes the variations in pitch, loudness, and duration of speech. It is used in speech synthesis to generate speech that conveys the appropriate emotional and linguistic meaning. The main techniques used in prosodic modeling are the F0 contour model and the prosodic hierarchy model.
