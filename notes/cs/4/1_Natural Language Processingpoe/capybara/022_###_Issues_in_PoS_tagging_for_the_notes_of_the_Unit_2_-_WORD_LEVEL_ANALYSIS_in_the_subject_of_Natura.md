### Issues in PoS tagging for the notes of the Unit 2 - WORD LEVEL ANALYSIS in the subject of Natural Language Processing

Part of the task of natural language processing is to identify the parts of speech (PoS) of words in a given text. This process is called PoS tagging. However, there are several issues that come with PoS tagging that one must be aware of. Here are some of them:

1. Ambiguity: There are words that can be classified into different PoS depending on their context. For instance, the word "bank" can be a noun or a verb depending on the context. This ambiguity can pose a challenge to PoS tagging.

2. Out of vocabulary words: There are words that may not be included in the corpus used for PoS tagging. This means that they may not have a tag assigned to them, which can be problematic in determining their proper PoS.

3. Typos and misspellings: Errors in spelling can lead to incorrect PoS tagging. This is because the tagger may not be able to recognize the word due to its misspelling.

4. Rare words: PoS taggers are trained on corpora that are based on common words. This means that rare words may not have enough examples for the tagger to properly identify their PoS.

5. Word order: The order of words in a sentence can also affect their PoS. For example, in the sentence "I saw the man with the telescope," the word "with" can be either a preposition or a conjunction depending on whether it modifies "man" or "telescope."

Mnemonics and Learning Tricks:

1. To help with ambiguity, it can be useful to look at the surrounding words to determine the proper PoS of a word. For example, in the sentence "I saw the bank," the word "saw" is a past tense verb, which suggests that "bank" is a noun.

2. For out of vocabulary words, it can be helpful to use a morphological analyzer, which can break down the word into its component parts to determine its PoS.

3. To avoid errors due to misspellings, one can use a spell checker before running the PoS tagger.

4. For rare words, it may be necessary to manually assign their PoS based on their context.

5. To determine the proper PoS based on word order, it can be helpful to look at the entire sentence and identify the subject, verb, and object. This can help in identifying the function of each word in the sentence.

By being aware of these issues and using these mnemonics and learning tricks, one can improve the accuracy of PoS tagging in natural language processing.
