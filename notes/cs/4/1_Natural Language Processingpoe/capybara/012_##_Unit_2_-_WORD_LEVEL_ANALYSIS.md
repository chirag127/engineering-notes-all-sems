## Unit 2 - WORD LEVEL ANALYSIS

Word level analysis involves examining the individual words in a text to understand their meanings, relationships, and patterns. It is a crucial element in the study of language and is essential for tasks such as text classification, information retrieval, and machine translation. In this unit, we will explore various techniques used for word level analysis.

### 1. Tokenization
Tokenization is the process of breaking down a text into individual words, also known as tokens. It is the first step in any natural language processing task. There are various tokenization techniques, including whitespace tokenization, regular expression tokenization, and rule-based tokenization.

### 2. Stemming and Lemmatization
Stemming and lemmatization are techniques used to reduce words to their base forms. Stemming involves removing the suffixes from words to obtain their root forms. It is a simple and fast process but may result in incorrect stemming, as it does not consider the grammatical context. Lemmatization, on the other hand, uses a dictionary to obtain the base form of a word, taking into account the grammatical context. Although lemmatization is more accurate than stemming, it is a more complex and time-consuming process.

### 3. Part-of-Speech Tagging
Part-of-speech tagging is the process of assigning a part of speech, such as noun, verb, adjective, etc., to each word in a text. This helps to identify the grammatical structure of the text and is useful for various natural language processing tasks, such as text-to-speech conversion and machine translation.

### 4. Named Entity Recognition
Named entity recognition is the process of identifying and classifying named entities, such as people, organizations, and locations, in a text. This is useful for various applications, such as information extraction, question answering, and text summarization.

### 5. Word Sense Disambiguation
Word sense disambiguation is the process of identifying the correct meaning of a word in a given context. It is a challenging task, as words can have multiple meanings, and the correct meaning depends on the context in which the word is used. Word sense disambiguation is useful for various natural language processing tasks, such as machine translation and information retrieval.

### 6. Mnemonics and Learning Tricks
There are various mnemonics and learning tricks that can be helpful for remembering the techniques used for word level analysis. For example, to remember the difference between stemming and lemmatization, you can think of them as "cutting off" and "looking up" the roots of words, respectively. To remember the parts of speech, you can use the acronym "FANBOYS" for coordinating conjunctions (for, and, nor, but, or, yet, so) and "SWABIs" for subordinating conjunctions (since, when, although, because, if).

In conclusion, word level analysis is an essential component of natural language processing, and the techniques used for word level analysis are crucial for various applications. By understanding these techniques and using mnemonics and learning tricks, we can improve our understanding and retention of this important topic.
