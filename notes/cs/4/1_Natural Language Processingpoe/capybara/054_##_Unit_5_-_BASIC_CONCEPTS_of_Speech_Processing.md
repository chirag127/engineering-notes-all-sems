## Unit 5 - BASIC CONCEPTS of Speech Processing

Speech processing is an essential area of research in the field of computer science and engineering. It involves the development of algorithms and techniques to analyze and understand spoken language. This unit will introduce you to the basic concepts of speech processing, including speech signals, speech analysis, and speech synthesis. 

### Speech Signals

Speech signals are the sounds produced by a human voice. These signals can be analyzed to extract useful information about the spoken language. Some of the basic concepts related to speech signals are:

- **Sampling and quantization**: Speech signals are continuous in nature, but they need to be converted into a digital format for processing. This conversion involves sampling the signal at regular intervals and quantizing each sample to a discrete value.

- **Frequency analysis**: Speech signals can be decomposed into their constituent frequencies using Fourier analysis. This allows us to identify the different components of the signal, such as the fundamental frequency and formants.

- **Time-domain analysis**: Speech signals can also be analyzed in the time-domain, using techniques such as waveform analysis and autocorrelation. 

### Speech Analysis

Speech analysis involves the extraction of useful information from speech signals. Some of the basic concepts related to speech analysis are:

- **Spectral analysis**: Spectral analysis involves analyzing the frequency components of a speech signal. This allows us to identify important features of the signal, such as the presence of certain phonemes or the overall pitch of the speaker's voice.

- **Formant analysis**: Formants are the resonant frequencies of the vocal tract that shape the sound of different vowels. Formant analysis involves identifying the frequencies of these resonances in a speech signal.

- **Feature extraction**: Feature extraction involves identifying the most important features of a speech signal for a particular application. For example, in speech recognition, features such as the presence of certain phonemes or the overall pitch of the speaker's voice may be important.

### Speech Synthesis

Speech synthesis involves generating speech signals from text or other input. Some of the basic concepts related to speech synthesis are:

- **Text-to-speech synthesis**: Text-to-speech synthesis involves converting written text into spoken words. This can be done using techniques such as rule-based synthesis or statistical synthesis.

- **Articulatory synthesis**: Articulatory synthesis involves modeling the movements of the vocal tract to generate speech sounds. This can be a more natural and flexible approach than text-to-speech synthesis.

- **Concatenative synthesis**: Concatenative synthesis involves combining small units of recorded speech to create a larger speech signal. This can be a more natural-sounding approach than other forms of synthesis.

### Mnemonics and Learning Tricks

- To remember the basic concepts related to speech signals, remember the acronym SF-TD, which stands for Spectral analysis, Frequency analysis, Time-domain analysis.
- To remember the different forms of speech synthesis, remember the acronym TAC, which stands for Text-to-speech synthesis, Articulatory synthesis, Concatenative synthesis.
