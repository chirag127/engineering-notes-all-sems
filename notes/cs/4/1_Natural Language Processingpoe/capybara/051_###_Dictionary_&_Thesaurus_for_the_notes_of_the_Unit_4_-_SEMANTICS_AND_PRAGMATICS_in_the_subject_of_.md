### Dictionary & Thesaurus for the notes of the Unit 4 - SEMANTICS AND PRAGMATICS in the subject of Natural Language Processing

In the field of Natural Language Processing, understanding the meanings of words is crucial. Two important tools for this purpose are dictionaries and thesauri. Here are some important points to keep in mind about these tools:

#### Dictionary
- A dictionary is a collection of words and their meanings.
- It can be used to look up the meanings of words that one is unfamiliar with.
- Dictionaries are often organized alphabetically to make it easy to find the desired word.
- In addition to definitions, dictionaries may also provide examples of how the word is used in context, synonyms, antonyms, and pronunciation guides.
- Some popular dictionaries include Oxford English Dictionary, Merriam-Webster, and Collins English Dictionary.

#### Thesaurus
- A thesaurus is a collection of words and their synonyms.
- It can be used to find alternative words with similar meanings.
- Thesauri are often organized alphabetically or by categories such as nouns, verbs, and adjectives.
- In addition to synonyms, some thesauri may also provide antonyms, related words, and examples of how the word is used in context.
- Some popular thesauri include Roget's Thesaurus, Oxford Thesaurus of English, and Merriam-Webster's Thesaurus.

Mnemonics and learning tricks:
- To remember the difference between a dictionary and a thesaurus, think of it this way: a dictionary tells you what a word means, while a thesaurus tells you what other words mean the same thing.
- To remember the pronunciation of a word, try breaking it down into smaller sounds and practicing saying them. Alternatively, use a website or app that provides audio pronunciations.
- To remember new words and their meanings, try using flashcards or creating a word journal where you write down new words and their definitions.

In conclusion, dictionaries and thesauri are important tools for understanding the meanings of words in Natural Language Processing. By familiarizing oneself with these tools and using mnemonic devices, one can improve their vocabulary and communication skills.
