### Semantic Attachments for the Notes of the Unit 4 - Semantics and Pragmatics in the Subject of Natural Language Processing

In natural language processing, semantics and pragmatics are two important areas of study. Semantics deals with the meaning of words and sentences, whereas pragmatics deals with the use of language in context. Semantic attachments are a way to represent the meaning of a sentence in a structured format. Here are some important points to keep in mind when studying semantic attachments:

1. Semantic attachments are used to represent the meaning of a sentence in a structured format.
2. They consist of a set of nodes and edges that represent the relationships between words in a sentence.
3. The nodes in a semantic attachment represent the words in a sentence, while the edges represent the relationships between these words.
4. There are different types of edges in a semantic attachment, such as the subject-predicate edge, the modifier edge, and the argument edge.
5. The subject-predicate edge represents the relationship between the subject and the predicate in a sentence.
6. The modifier edge represents the relationship between a word and its modifier in a sentence.
7. The argument edge represents the relationship between a verb and its direct or indirect object in a sentence.
8. Semantic attachments can be used for various natural language processing tasks, such as information extraction, question answering, and machine translation.
9. There are different types of semantic attachments, such as the Abstract Meaning Representation (AMR) and the Universal Conceptual Cognitive Annotation (UCCA).
