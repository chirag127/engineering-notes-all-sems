### Word Classes for the notes of the Unit 2 - WORD LEVEL ANALYSIS in the subject of Natural Language Processing

In Natural Language Processing, it is essential to understand the different types of words and their classes. Knowing the word classes helps us to analyze and understand the language better. Here are the various word classes: 

1. Nouns: Nouns are words that represent people, places, things, or ideas. They can be concrete, such as a chair or a table, or abstract, such as love and happiness. Mnemonic: Nouns are the names of things.

2. Verbs: Verbs are words that represent actions or states of being. They can be active, such as run and jump, or passive, such as be and exist. Mnemonic: Verbs are the action words.

3. Adjectives: Adjectives are words that describe or modify nouns. They can be used to indicate size, color, shape, or other qualities. Mnemonic: Adjectives are the words that add flavor to the nouns.

4. Adverbs: Adverbs are words that modify verbs, adjectives, and other adverbs. They provide information about the manner, time, place, degree, or frequency of an action or event. Mnemonic: Adverbs are the words that answer the how, when, where, how much, and how often questions.

5. Pronouns: Pronouns are words that replace nouns or noun phrases. They can be used to avoid repetition or to refer to something previously mentioned. Mnemonic: Pronouns are the words that take the place of nouns.

6. Prepositions: Prepositions are words that show the relationship between a noun or pronoun and other words in a sentence. They are used to indicate time, place, direction, or manner. Mnemonic: Prepositions are the words that show the position of something.

7. Conjunctions: Conjunctions are words that connect words, phrases, or clauses. They can be used to join two ideas or to show contrast or addition. Mnemonic: Conjunctions are the words that connect the dots.

8. Interjections: Interjections are words that express strong feelings or emotions. They are usually set apart from the rest of the sentence by an exclamation mark or a comma. Mnemonic: Interjections are the words that show excitement or emotion.

Learning these word classes and their functions will help you to understand the grammar and syntax of a sentence. It will also assist you in developing algorithms for natural language processing tasks such as text classification, sentiment analysis, and machine translation.
