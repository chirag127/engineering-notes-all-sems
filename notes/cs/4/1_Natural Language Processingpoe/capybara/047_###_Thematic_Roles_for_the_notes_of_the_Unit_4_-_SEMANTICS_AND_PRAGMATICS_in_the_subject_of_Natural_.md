### Thematic Roles for the notes of the Unit 4 - SEMANTICS AND PRAGMATICS in the subject of Natural Language Processing

Thematic roles, also known as semantic roles, are the roles that nouns and pronouns play in a sentence. They are important in Natural Language Processing as they help in understanding the meaning of a sentence. In this unit, we will explore the different thematic roles and their importance in understanding the meaning of a sentence.

Here are the different thematic roles:

1. Agent: The agent is the entity that performs the action in a sentence. For example, in the sentence "John kicked the ball," John is the agent.

2. Patient: The patient is the entity that undergoes the action in a sentence. For example, in the sentence "John kicked the ball," the ball is the patient.

3. Theme: The theme is the entity that is being talked about in a sentence. For example, in the sentence "John likes pizza," pizza is the theme.

4. Experiencer: The experiencer is the entity that perceives or experiences something in a sentence. For example, in the sentence "John saw the movie," John is the experiencer.

5. Instrument: The instrument is the entity that is used to perform an action in a sentence. For example, in the sentence "John cut the paper with scissors," scissors are the instrument.

6. Location: The location is the place where an action occurs in a sentence. For example, in the sentence "John is at the park," the park is the location.

7. Source: The source is the entity from where an action originates in a sentence. For example, in the sentence "John came from the store," the store is the source.

8. Goal: The goal is the entity where an action is directed towards in a sentence. For example, in the sentence "John went to the store," the store is the goal.

Mnemonics and learning tricks:

- Remember the acronym APPELIGOS to remember the different thematic roles: Agent, Patient, Theme, Experiencer, Instrument, Location, Goal, Source.
- Another trick is to think of a sentence and identify the different thematic roles. For example, "John gave Mary a book." In this sentence, John is the agent, Mary is the goal, and book is the patient.

Thematic roles are important in Natural Language Processing as they help in understanding the meaning of a sentence. By identifying the different thematic roles, we can analyze the relationships between entities in a sentence and understand the semantic meaning of the sentence.
