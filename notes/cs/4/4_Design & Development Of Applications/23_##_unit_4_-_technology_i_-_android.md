## Unit 4 - Technology I - Android

Unit 4 of the subject "Design & Development of Applications" focuses on the technology used for developing mobile applications on the Android platform. The unit covers the basics of the Android operating system, including its architecture, components, and APIs.

The unit will introduce students to the Android software development kit (SDK) and the Android Studio development environment, and will cover the basics of Android programming, including the Java programming language, Android user interface design, and the Android activity lifecycle.

Additionally, the unit will cover the basics of Android application development, including the creation of user interfaces, the use of intents and services, and the implementation of common features such as data storage and network access.

In conclusion, Unit 4 of the subject "Design & Development of Applications" provides a comprehensive introduction to the technology and tools used for developing mobile applications on the Android platform. Understanding the material covered in Unit 4 is essential for students who want to develop Android applications and become proficient in mobile application development.
