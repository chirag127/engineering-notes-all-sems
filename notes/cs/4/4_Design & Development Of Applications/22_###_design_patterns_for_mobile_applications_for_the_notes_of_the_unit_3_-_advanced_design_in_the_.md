### Design patterns for mobile applications for the notes of the Unit 3 - Advanced Design in the subject of Design & Development Of Applications

Design patterns for mobile applications refer to reusable solutions for common design problems in mobile application development. Design patterns provide a structured approach to designing and developing mobile applications, helping to ensure consistency and improve the overall user experience.

In Unit 3 of the subject "Design & Development of Applications", the focus is on advanced design techniques and methodologies for designing complex and sophisticated applications. Design patterns for mobile applications play a critical role in ensuring that mobile applications are well-designed, efficient, and user-friendly.

There are several common design patterns for mobile applications, including the Model-View-Controller (MVC) pattern, the Model-View-ViewModel (MVVM) pattern, and the Model-View-Presenter (MVP) pattern. Each design pattern has its own strengths and weaknesses, and the choice of design pattern will depend on the specific requirements of the mobile application.

In conclusion, design patterns for mobile applications provide a structured approach to designing and developing mobile applications, helping to ensure consistency and improve the overall user experience. Understanding design patterns for mobile applications is important for designing effective and sophisticated applications in the subject of Design & Development of Applications.
