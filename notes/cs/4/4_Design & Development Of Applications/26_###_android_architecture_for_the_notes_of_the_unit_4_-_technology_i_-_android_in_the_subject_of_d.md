### Android architecture for the notes of the Unit 4 - Technology I - Android in the subject of Design & Development Of Applications

The Android architecture refers to the overall design and structure of the Android operating system, including its components, APIs, and the relationships between them.

In Unit 4 of the subject "Design & Development of Applications", the focus is on the technology used for developing mobile applications on the Android platform. Understanding the Android architecture is important for developing effective and efficient Android applications.

The Android architecture is based on a layered approach, with the application layer at the top and the Linux kernel at the bottom. The application layer includes the Android framework, which provides a set of high-level APIs for developing Android applications. The Android framework is built on top of the Android runtime, which is responsible for executing Android applications.

The Android architecture also includes several other components, such as the Android libraries, the Android system services, and the Android native libraries. These components work together to provide a comprehensive and flexible platform for developing Android applications.

In conclusion, the Android architecture refers to the overall design and structure of the Android operating system, including its components, APIs, and the relationships between them.
