### Using Google Maps, GPS and Wi-Fi for the notes of the Unit 4 - TECHNOLOGY I – ANDROID in the subject of Design & Development Of Applications
Google Maps is a mapping service provided by Google that allows users to view maps, get directions, and see real-time traffic updates. GPS (Global Positioning System) is a technology used to determine the location of a device, such as a smartphone, based on satellite signals. Wi-Fi is a wireless networking technology that allows devices to connect to the internet.

When using Google Maps on an Android device, the GPS and Wi-Fi are used to accurately determine the user's location. This allows the app to provide turn-by-turn directions, real-time traffic updates, and other location-based services.

Additionally, using Wi-Fi can help save on data usage and improve the accuracy of location tracking, as it provides a more stable and reliable connection compared to cellular data.

Overall, the combination of Google Maps, GPS, and Wi-Fi provides a powerful tool for navigation and location-based services on Android devices.
