### Establishing the development environment for the notes of the Unit 4 - Technology I - Android in the subject of Design & Development Of Applications

Establishing the development environment for Android application development involves installing and setting up the necessary software and tools for developing and testing Android applications.

In Unit 4 of the subject "Design & Development of Applications", the focus is on the technology used for developing mobile applications on the Android platform. Establishing the development environment is a crucial step in the Android application development process, as it allows developers to create, test, and debug their applications.

The development environment typically includes the Android software development kit (SDK), the Android Studio development environment, and the Java Development Kit (JDK). Developers will need to install and configure these tools on their development machine in order to start developing Android applications.

In conclusion, establishing the development environment for Android application development involves installing and setting up the necessary software and tools for developing and testing Android applications. Understanding how to establish the development environment is important for students who want to develop Android applications and become proficient in mobile application development in the subject of Design & Development of Applications.
