## Unit 5 - Technology II -iOS

Unit 5 of the subject "Design & Development of Applications" focuses on the technology used for developing mobile applications on the iOS platform. This unit provides a comprehensive introduction to the iOS operating system, including its architecture, components, and APIs, and covers the basics of iOS programming and application development.

The unit will cover topics such as establishing the development environment for iOS application development, including installing and setting up the necessary software and tools such as Xcode and the iOS software development kit (SDK). The unit will also cover the iOS architecture, including its components such as the iOS framework, the iOS runtime, and the iOS libraries.

The unit will also cover topics such as creating and manipulating user interfaces, including views and view controllers, and handling user events such as button clicks and touch events. The unit will also cover persisting data using Core Data, a powerful and flexible data management framework provided by iOS.

The unit will also cover topics such as integrating with server-side applications, including exchanging data between an iOS application and a server-side application over a network, and using location-based services and features, including accessing GPS and Wi-Fi data.

In conclusion, Unit 5 of the subject "Design & Development of Applications" focuses on the technology used for developing mobile applications on the iOS platform and provides a comprehensive introduction to the iOS operating system, including its architecture, components, and APIs, and the basics of iOS programming and application development.
