### Establishing the development environment for the notes of the Unit 4 - TECHNOLOGY I – ANDROID in the subject of Design & Development Of Applications
To establish the development environment for Unit 4 - TECHNOLOGY I – ANDROID in the subject of Design & Development Of Applications, you need the following:
1. Android Studio: an integrated development environment (IDE) for building Android apps.
2. Java Development Kit (JDK): required to run Android Studio.
3. Android SDK: a set of tools and APIs for building Android apps.
4. A device or emulator to test your app on.

Steps:
1. Install Android Studio and JDK.
2. Start Android Studio and set up the Android SDK.
3. Create a new project in Android Studio.
4. Choose an emulator or connect an Android device to test your app on.
5. Start coding and developing your app.
