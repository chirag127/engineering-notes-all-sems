### Requirements gathering and validation for mobile applications for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of Design & Development Of Applications
Requirements gathering and validation are critical steps in the mobile application development process.

1. Requirements gathering: This involves identifying and documenting the functional and non-functional requirements of the mobile application. This includes understanding the user's needs and expectations, as well as the business requirements.

2. Requirements validation: This involves verifying that the requirements gathered are complete, accurate, and feasible. This is done by reviewing the requirements with stakeholders and testing the requirements through prototypes or mockups.

It is important to validate the requirements before starting the development process as it helps to avoid costly rework and ensures that the final product meets the needs of the users and the business.
