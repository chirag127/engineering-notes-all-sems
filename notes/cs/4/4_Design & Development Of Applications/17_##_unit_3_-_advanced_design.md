## Unit 3 - ADVANCED DESIGN
Unit 3 of the course "Advanced Design" covers the following topics:
1. Design thinking and problem solving
2. User-centered design
3. Design patterns and components
4. Design systems and style guides
5. Interaction design and prototyping
6. Responsive design and accessibility
7. Design for mobile and emerging technologies

The unit focuses on advanced techniques and principles for designing digital products and services. Students learn how to apply design thinking to complex problems and create user-centered solutions. They also learn how to use design patterns, components, and systems to create consistent and scalable designs. Additionally, they learn about interaction design, prototyping, and responsive design, and how to design for mobile and emerging technologies. The unit emphasizes hands-on learning through design projects and critiques.
