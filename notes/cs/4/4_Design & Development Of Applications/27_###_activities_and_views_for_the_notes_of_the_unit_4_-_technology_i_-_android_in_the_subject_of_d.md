### Activities and views for the notes of the Unit 4 - Technology I - Android in the subject of Design & Development Of Applications

Activities and views are two core components of the Android framework for developing mobile applications.

In Unit 4 of the subject "Design & Development of Applications", the focus is on the technology used for developing mobile applications on the Android platform. Understanding activities and views is important for developing effective and efficient Android applications.

An activity in Android represents a single screen in an application, with a user interface defined by a layout file and behavior defined by the activity's code. A view is a basic building block of an Android user interface, representing a widget or layout.

Activities and views work together to provide the user interface for an Android application. A layout file defines the structure and appearance of the user interface, while the activity's code defines the behavior and interaction with the user.

In conclusion, activities and views are two core components of the Android framework for developing mobile applications. Understanding activities and views is important for developing effective and efficient Android applications in the subject of Design & Development of Applications.
