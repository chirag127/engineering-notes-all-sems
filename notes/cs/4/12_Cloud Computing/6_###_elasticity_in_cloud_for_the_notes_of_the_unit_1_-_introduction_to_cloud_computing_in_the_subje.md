### Elasticity in Cloud for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction To Cloud Computing in the subject of Cloud Computing

Elasticity in Cloud refers to the ability of a cloud computing system to automatically scale its resources to meet changing demands. This allows organizations to quickly and easily allocate more or less computing power, storage, and other resources as needed, without having to make significant investments in new hardware. Elasticity is a key characteristic of cloud computing and one of the main benefits of using the cloud.

Elasticity can be achieved in several ways, including:

1. Auto-scaling: Automatically adjusting the number of virtual machines or containers based on demand.

2. Load balancing: Distributing workloads across multiple servers to ensure that resources are used efficiently and that no single server is overburdened.

3. Dynamic resource allocation: Allocating additional resources, such as storage and computing power, as needed.

4. On-demand provisioning: Provisioning new resources as soon as they are needed, without having to wait for approval or allocate new hardware.

Elasticity enables organizations to respond quickly to changing demands and to scale their resources up or down as needed, providing a level of flexibility and cost-effectiveness that is not possible with traditional computing systems.
