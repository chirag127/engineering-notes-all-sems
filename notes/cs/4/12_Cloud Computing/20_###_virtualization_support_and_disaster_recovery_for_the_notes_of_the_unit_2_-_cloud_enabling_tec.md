### Virtualization Support and Disaster Recovery for the notes of the Unit 2 - Cloud Enabling Technologies Service Oriented Architecture in the subject of Cloud Computing

Virtualization Support and Disaster Recovery are important components of cloud-based Service Oriented Architecture (SOA). 

1. Virtualization Support: Virtualization allows multiple virtual machines to run on a single physical host, improving resource utilization and enabling the deployment of cloud services. Virtualization also enables the creation of isolated environments for testing and development.

2. Disaster Recovery: Disaster recovery refers to the processes and technologies used to ensure that critical systems and data can be restored after a disaster. Disaster recovery solutions in the cloud typically involve replicating data and systems to a secondary location, where they can be quickly restored in the event of a failure.

3. Benefits: Virtualization support and disaster recovery in cloud computing can improve system availability and reduce downtime, helping to ensure that critical services are always available to users. They also help to reduce the costs associated with hardware and software maintenance, as well as the costs of managing and maintaining disaster recovery solutions.

Virtualization support and disaster recovery are key components of a robust and secure cloud-based SOA, and are essential for ensuring the reliability and availability of cloud services.
