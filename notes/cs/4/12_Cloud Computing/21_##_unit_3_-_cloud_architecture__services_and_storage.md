## Unit 3 - Cloud Architecture, Services And Storage

Cloud Architecture refers to the design of a cloud computing system, including the hardware, software, network, and storage components. Cloud Services are the applications and services made available to users over the internet. Examples include SaaS (Software as a Service), PaaS (Platform as a Service), and IaaS (Infrastructure as a Service). Cloud Storage is a service that allows users to store, access, and manage their data and files over the internet.
