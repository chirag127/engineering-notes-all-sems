### Underlying Principles of Parallel and Distributed Computing for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction To Cloud Computing in the subject of Cloud Computing

Underlying Principles of Parallel and Distributed Computing:

1. Concurrency: The ability to execute multiple tasks simultaneously to increase efficiency and speed.

2. Load balancing: The distribution of workloads across multiple processing units to ensure that no single unit is overburdened.

3. Fault tolerance: The ability of a system to continue functioning even if one or more components fail.

4. Scalability: The ability to increase or decrease the number of processing units as needed to handle changes in workload.

5. Resource sharing: The ability to share resources, such as memory and storage, among multiple processing units.

6. Communication: The ability of processing units to communicate with each other to exchange data and coordinate their actions.

7. Synchronization: The coordination of actions among processing units to ensure that they are working together in a consistent and predictable manner.

These principles are fundamental to the design and operation of parallel and distributed computing systems, including cloud computing. They form the basis for the development of efficient and reliable cloud computing solutions.
