### Tools and Mechanisms for Service Oriented Architecture

- Service Oriented Architecture (SOA) is an architectural style that enables the creation and integration of loosely coupled, self-contained, and interoperable services  .
- Services are software components that provide functionality through well-defined interfaces and contracts, and can be accessed and composed over a network  .
- SOA supports scenarios for application integration, data integration, and service orchestration style automation of business processes or workflows.
- SOA also enables the reuse of existing services to create new and diverse applications, and facilitates the maintenance and evolution of service-oriented systems  .
- Some of the tools and mechanisms that are essential for implementing and managing SOA are:

  - Service Registry: A service registry is a repository that stores information about the available services, such as their names, descriptions, locations, interfaces, and policies . A service registry enables service discovery, which is the process of finding and selecting the appropriate services for a given task or application .
  - Service Bus: A service bus is a middleware component that provides communication and integration capabilities between services and applications . A service bus enables service invocation, which is the process of sending and receiving messages to and from services . A service bus also supports service mediation, which is the process of transforming, routing, and enriching messages according to predefined rules and policies .
  - Service Repository: A service repository is a repository that stores artifacts related to the design, development, testing, deployment, and governance of services, such as service models, schemas, contracts, policies, and documentation . A service repository enables service lifecycle management, which is the process of planning, creating, testing, deploying, monitoring, and evolving services .
  - Service Composer: A service composer is a tool that allows the creation and modification of service compositions, which are applications or processes that invoke and coordinate multiple services to achieve a specific goal . A service composer enables service orchestration, which is the process of defining and executing the control and data flow among services .
  - Service Monitor: A service monitor is a tool that collects and analyzes data about the performance, availability, reliability, and quality of services and service compositions . A service monitor enables service management, which is the process of ensuring that services and service compositions meet the expected service level agreements (SLAs) and quality of service (QoS) requirements .
  - Service Adapter: A service adapter is a tool that enables the integration of legacy or non-service-oriented applications with service-oriented systems . A service adapter enables service enablement, which is the process of exposing the functionality of existing applications as services .
