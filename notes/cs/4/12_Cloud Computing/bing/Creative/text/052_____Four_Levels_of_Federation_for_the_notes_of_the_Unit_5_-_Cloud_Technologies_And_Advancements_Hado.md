### Four Levels of Federation for the notes of the Unit 5 - Cloud Technologies And Advancements Hadoop in the subject of Cloud Computing

- Federation is a concept that allows multiple independent systems to work together as a single logical system, without losing their autonomy or identity.
- Federation can be applied to different aspects of cloud computing, such as storage, computation, networking, and services.
- According to , there are four levels of federation in the cloud:

  - Level 1: Federation of Resources
    - This level involves the sharing of physical or virtual resources among different cloud providers or users, such as CPU, memory, disk, network, etc.
    - This level can improve the utilization, scalability, and availability of resources, as well as reduce the cost and environmental impact of cloud computing.
    - An example of this level is HDFS Federation, which allows multiple NameNodes to manage different namespaces in a single HDFS cluster    .
  - Level 2: Federation of Services
    - This level involves the integration of different cloud services, such as storage, computation, database, analytics, etc., to provide a unified and consistent interface to the users or applications.
    - This level can enhance the functionality, performance, and reliability of cloud services, as well as enable interoperability and portability among different cloud platforms.
    - An example of this level is MapReduce, which is a programming model and framework for processing large-scale data sets in parallel on a cluster of machines .
  - Level 3: Federation of Applications
    - This level involves the composition of different cloud applications, such as web, mobile, social, etc., to provide a richer and more personalized user experience.
    - This level can increase the value, diversity, and innovation of cloud applications, as well as facilitate collaboration and communication among different users or groups.
    - An example of this level is Google App Engine, which is a platform for developing and hosting web applications on Google's infrastructure .
  - Level 4: Federation of Clouds
    - This level involves the federation of different cloud providers, such as public, private, hybrid, community, etc., to form a cloud of clouds or a meta-cloud.
    - This level can achieve the ultimate goal of cloud computing, which is to provide ubiquitous, on-demand, and elastic access to a pool of shared and configurable resources and services.
    - An example of this level is OpenStack, which is an open source software platform for building and managing cloud computing environments .

- The future of federation in the cloud is to achieve higher levels of integration, coordination, and collaboration among different cloud entities, while preserving their autonomy, identity, and diversity.
