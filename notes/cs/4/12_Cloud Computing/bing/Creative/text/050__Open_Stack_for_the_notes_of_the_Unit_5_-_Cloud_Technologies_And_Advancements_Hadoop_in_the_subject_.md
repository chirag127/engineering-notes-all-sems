### Open Stack

- Open Stack is a free, open source cloud computing platform that provides infrastructure-as-a-service (IaaS) for both public and private clouds.
- Open Stack consists of interrelated components that control diverse, multi-vendor hardware pools of processing, storage, and networking resources throughout a data center.
- Users can manage Open Stack either through a web-based dashboard, through command-line tools, or through RESTful web services.
- Open Stack also provides additional components for orchestration, fault management, service management, and other services to ensure high availability of user applications.
- Open Stack is developed by the community and supported by various organizations and vendors.
- Open Stack is widely deployed and trusted by many industries and use cases, such as gaming, retail, telecom, and research.
