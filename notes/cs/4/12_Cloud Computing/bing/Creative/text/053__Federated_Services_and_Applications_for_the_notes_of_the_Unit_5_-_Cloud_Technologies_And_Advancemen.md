### Federated Services and Applications

- Federated services and applications are those that can run on multiple cloud environments that are interconnected and interoperable, forming a federated cloud.
- Federated cloud, also known as multi-cloud or cloud federation, is the deployment and management of several external and internal cloud computing services to match business needs .
- Federated cloud integrates heterogeneous cloud environments such as private, community, and public clouds into scalable computing platforms .
- Federated cloud enables users to create one virtual computing environment from multiple clouds to support evolving needs.
- Federated cloud is based on a common standard that allows different cloud providers to communicate and coordinate with each other .
- Federated cloud has several benefits, such as:
  - Reducing the consumption of energy and the cost of providers due to dynamic scalability .
  - Increasing the reliability and availability of cloud services by avoiding vendor lock-in and single point of failure .
  - Connecting various cloud service providers globally and allowing them to buy and sell services on demand .
  - Providing easy scaling up and down of resources and services according to the user's requirements .
  - Supporting diverse and specialized applications and services that may not be available in a single cloud environment .
  - Enhancing the security and privacy of cloud data by distributing it across multiple clouds and applying different policies and mechanisms .
  - Facilitating the collaboration and cooperation among different cloud users and providers for joint projects and research .
- Federated cloud also has some challenges, such as:
  - Developing and maintaining a common standard and protocol for cloud interoperability and federation .
  - Managing and monitoring the performance, quality, and compliance of federated cloud services and resources .
  - Resolving the conflicts and inconsistencies among different cloud policies and regulations .
  - Ensuring the trust and transparency among different cloud providers and users .
  - Balancing the trade-offs between the benefits and risks of federated cloud .
