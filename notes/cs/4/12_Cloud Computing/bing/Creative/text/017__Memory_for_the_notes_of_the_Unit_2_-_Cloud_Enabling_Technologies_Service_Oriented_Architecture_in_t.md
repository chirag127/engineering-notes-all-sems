### Memory for the notes of the Unit 2 - Cloud Enabling Technologies Service Oriented Architecture in the subject of Cloud Computing

- Service Oriented Architecture (SOA) is a design paradigm that enables the creation of loosely coupled, interoperable, and reusable services that can be composed to form business processes or applications.
- SOA is based on the principles of abstraction, standardization, reusability, composability, loose coupling, and discoverability.
- SOA services are self-contained, self-describing, and stateless units of functionality that expose their capabilities through standardized interfaces and protocols.
- SOA services can be classified into three types: business services, application services, and infrastructure services.
- Business services are high-level services that represent the core business functions or processes of an organization, such as order processing, customer management, or inventory management.
- Application services are low-level services that provide specific functionality or logic, such as authentication, encryption, or data access.
- Infrastructure services are foundational services that support the operation and management of other services, such as logging, monitoring, or load balancing.
- SOA services can be discovered and invoked by service consumers, which are applications or other services that need to use the functionality provided by the service providers.
- SOA services communicate with each other using standardized message formats and protocols, such as XML, SOAP, REST, or JSON.
- SOA services can be orchestrated or choreographed to form complex business processes or applications, using technologies such as BPEL, BPMN, or WS-CDL.
- SOA enables cloud computing by providing a flexible, scalable, and adaptable architecture that supports the delivery of services over the internet, on-demand, and with pay-per-use pricing models.
- SOA also enables cloud computing by facilitating the integration and interoperability of heterogeneous and distributed systems, applications, and data sources, using common standards and protocols.
- SOA benefits cloud computing by improving the reusability, composability, and loose coupling of services, which reduces the development and maintenance costs, and increases the agility and responsiveness of the cloud environment.
