### On-demand Provisioning

- On-demand provisioning is a feature of cloud computing that allows users to access computing resources as and when they need them, without having to pre-allocate or reserve them in advance.
- On-demand provisioning enables users to scale up or down their resources dynamically, depending on their workload and demand, and pay only for what they use.
- On-demand provisioning also reduces the complexity and overhead of managing and maintaining physical servers, as the cloud service provider takes care of provisioning, deprovisioning, patching, and updating the resources.
- On-demand provisioning can be applied to various types of cloud resources, such as compute, storage, network, database, and applications.
- On-demand provisioning can be performed by the user through a self-service portal, an API, or a command-line interface, or by the cloud service provider through automation and orchestration tools.
- On-demand provisioning can benefit users by improving their agility, flexibility, efficiency, and cost-effectiveness, as well as enabling them to experiment and innovate faster.
- On-demand provisioning can also benefit cloud service providers by optimizing their resource utilization, reducing their operational costs, and enhancing their customer satisfaction and retention.
