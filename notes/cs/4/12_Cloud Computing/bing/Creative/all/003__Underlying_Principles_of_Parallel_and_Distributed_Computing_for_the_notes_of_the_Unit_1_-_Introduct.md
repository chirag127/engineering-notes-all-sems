### Underlying Principles of Parallel and Distributed Computing for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction To Cloud Computing in the subject of Cloud Computing

- Parallel and distributed computing are models of computation that allow multiple tasks to be executed simultaneously, either on a single computer with multiple processors (parallel computing) or on multiple computers connected by a network (distributed computing)  .
- Parallel and distributed computing can improve the performance, scalability, and reliability of complex applications that require large amounts of data and computation  .
- Parallel and distributed computing build on fundamental systems concepts, such as concurrency, mutual exclusion, consistency, memory models, communication, synchronization, load balancing, fault tolerance, and security    .
- Concurrency refers to the execution of more than one procedure at the same time, either truly simultaneously (as on a multiprocessor) or in an unpredictably interleaved order .
- Mutual exclusion refers to the property that no two concurrent processes can access a shared resource at the same time .
- Consistency refers to the property that the state or memory of a system is coherent and valid across all the processes or processors involved .
- Memory models refer to the abstract representations of how memory is organized, accessed, and updated in a parallel or distributed system .
- Communication refers to the exchange of data or messages among processes or processors in a parallel or distributed system .
- Synchronization refers to the coordination of processes or processors in a parallel or distributed system to ensure that they operate in a consistent and orderly manner .
- Load balancing refers to the distribution of work among processes or processors in a parallel or distributed system to optimize the utilization of resources and minimize the execution time .
- Fault tolerance refers to the ability of a parallel or distributed system to continue functioning correctly in the presence of failures or errors .
- Security refers to the protection of a parallel or distributed system from unauthorized access, modification, or damage .
