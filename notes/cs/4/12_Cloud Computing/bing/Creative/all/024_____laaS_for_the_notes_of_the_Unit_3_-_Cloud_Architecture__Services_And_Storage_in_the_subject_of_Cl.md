# IaaS

- IaaS stands for **Infrastructure as a Service**  .
- It is a **cloud computing service model** that offers **on-demand infrastructure resources**, such as **compute, storage, networking, and virtualization**, to businesses and individuals via the cloud .
- IaaS allows users to **rent access** to cloud infrastructure resources as **individual services** from a cloud service provider (CSP), instead of buying and managing them on-premises or in collocated data centers .
- IaaS is one of the four types of cloud services, along with **software as a service (SaaS)**, **platform as a service (PaaS)**, and **serverless**.
- IaaS provides users with **flexibility, scalability, reliability, and cost-efficiency** in managing their infrastructure needs, as they can **pay only for what they use**, **scale up or down** as needed, **access resources from anywhere**, and **avoid hardware maintenance and upgrade costs**  .
- Some examples of IaaS providers are **Amazon Web Services (AWS)**, **Microsoft Azure**, **Google Cloud Platform (GCP)**, and **Oracle Cloud**  .
- Some common use cases of IaaS are **hosting websites and applications**, **testing and development**, **data analysis and processing**, **backup and disaster recovery**, and **high-performance computing**  .
