### Elasticity in Cloud for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction To Cloud Computing in the subject of Cloud Computing

- Elasticity in cloud computing is the ability for the system to adapt to workload changes by provisioning and de-provisioning resources in an autonomic manner, such that at each point in time the available resources match the current demand as closely as possible .
- Elasticity is a defining characteristic that differentiates cloud computing from previously proposed computing paradigms, such as grid computing.
- Elasticity in cloud computing allows the organization to adjust its storage requirements in terms of capacity and processing with respect to operational requirements.
- Elasticity in cloud computing also allows the organization to scale computer processing, memory, and storage capacity to meet changing demands.
- Elasticity in cloud computing aims at matching the amount of resource allocated to a service with the amount of resource it actually requires, avoiding over- or under-provisioning.
- Elasticity in cloud computing can be automatic, without need to perform capacity planning in advance of the occasion, or it can be a manual process where the organization is notified they are running low on resources and can then decide to add or reduce capacity when needed.
- Elasticity in cloud computing can be associated with horizontal scaling (scale-out) architecture, where more or less instances of the same service are deployed or removed, or with vertical scaling (scale-up) architecture, where the capacity of each instance is increased or decreased.
- Elasticity in cloud computing can be achieved at different levels of control, such as infrastructure level, platform level, or application level.
- Elasticity in cloud computing can bring benefits such as savings in infrastructure costs, human capital, and overall IT costs, as well as improved performance, availability, and responsiveness of the service .
- Elasticity in cloud computing can also pose challenges such as resources provisioning time, monitoring elastic applications, elasticity requirements, and multiple levels of control.

#### Mnemonics and learning tricks

- To remember the definition of elasticity in cloud computing, you can use the acronym **APAD** (Adapt to workload changes by Provisioning and De-provisioning resources in an Autonomic manner to match the current Demand).
- To remember the difference between horizontal scaling and vertical scaling, you can use the analogy of **adding more cars or upgrading the engine** of a single car to increase the speed.
- To remember the benefits of elasticity in cloud computing, you can use the acronym **SPAR** (Savings in costs, Performance improvement, Availability enhancement, Responsiveness increase).
- To remember the challenges of elasticity in cloud computing, you can use the acronym **RME** (Resources provisioning time, Monitoring elastic applications, Elasticity requirements).
