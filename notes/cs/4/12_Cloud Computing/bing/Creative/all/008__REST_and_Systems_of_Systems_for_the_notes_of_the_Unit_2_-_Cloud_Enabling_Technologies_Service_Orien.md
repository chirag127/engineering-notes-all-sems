### REST and Systems of Systems for the notes of the Unit 2 - Cloud Enabling Technologies Service Oriented Architecture in the subject of Cloud Computing

- REST stands for Representational State Transfer, which is an architectural style for designing web services that are based on the uniform and stateless transfer of resources .
- RESTful web services use HTTP methods (such as GET, POST, PUT, DELETE) to perform operations on resources, which are identified by URIs .
- RESTful web services are scalable, interoperable, and loosely coupled, which makes them suitable for cloud computing environments .
- Systems of Systems (SoS) are complex systems that consist of multiple independent and heterogeneous subsystems that collaborate to achieve a common goal .
- SoS have emergent properties that are not present in the individual subsystems, such as adaptability, resilience, and self-organization .
- SoS can be classified into four types: directed, acknowledged, collaborative, and virtual .
- Directed SoS have a central authority that controls the subsystems and imposes a common objective .
- Acknowledged SoS have a central authority that coordinates the subsystems, but the subsystems retain some operational and managerial independence .
- Collaborative SoS have no central authority, but the subsystems voluntarily cooperate to achieve a common objective .
- Virtual SoS have no central authority and no predefined objective, but the subsystems interact dynamically to respond to a situation .
- SoS are relevant for cloud computing because they can provide a holistic view of the cloud as a system that integrates various services, platforms, and infrastructures .
- SoS can also help to address some of the challenges of cloud computing, such as security, reliability, performance, and governance .

#### Mnemonics and learning tricks

- To remember the four types of SoS, you can use the acronym D.A.C.V. (Directed, Acknowledged, Collaborative, Virtual) .
- To remember the HTTP methods for RESTful web services, you can use the acronym G.P.P.D. (GET, POST, PUT, DELETE) .
- To remember the characteristics of RESTful web services, you can use the acronym S.I.L. (Scalable, Interoperable, Loosely coupled) .
