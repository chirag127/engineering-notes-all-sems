# SaaS

- SaaS stands for **Software as a Service**   .
- It is a software delivery and licensing model in which software is accessed online via a subscription, rather than bought and installed on individual computers .
- SaaS allows users to connect to and use cloud-based apps over the Internet .
- SaaS provides a complete software solution that users purchase on a pay-as-you-go basis from a cloud service provider .
- SaaS eliminates the need for users to install and run software on their own devices, and reduces the cost and complexity of software maintenance, support, and upgrades  .
- SaaS offers many benefits to users, such as scalability, accessibility, compatibility, security, and performance  .
- SaaS also benefits the software providers, as they can reach a wider market, reduce development and distribution costs, and increase customer satisfaction and retention  .
- Some common examples of SaaS are email, calendaring, and office tools (such as Microsoft Office 365), CRM (such as Salesforce), ERP (such as SAP), and e-commerce platforms (such as Shopify)  .
