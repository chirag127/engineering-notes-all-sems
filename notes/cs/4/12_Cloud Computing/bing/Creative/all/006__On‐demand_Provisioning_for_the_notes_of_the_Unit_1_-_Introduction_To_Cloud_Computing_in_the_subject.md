### On‐demand Provisioning for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction To Cloud Computing in the subject of Cloud Computing

- On-demand provisioning is a feature of cloud computing that allows users to access computing resources as needed, without having to purchase, configure, or manage them in advance .
- On-demand provisioning enables users to scale up or down their resources according to their changing needs, and only pay for what they use .
- On-demand provisioning can be applied to different types of cloud computing service models, such as infrastructure as a service (IaaS), platform as a service (PaaS), and software as a service (SaaS).
- On-demand provisioning can also be applied to different types of cloud computing deployment models, such as public cloud, private cloud, and hybrid cloud.
- On-demand provisioning can benefit users by providing them with flexibility, agility, efficiency, and cost-effectiveness .
- On-demand provisioning can also benefit cloud service providers by allowing them to optimize their resource utilization and allocation, and reduce their operational costs .
- On-demand provisioning can be implemented by using various methods and technologies, such as virtualization, automation, orchestration, self-service portals, and APIs  .
- On-demand provisioning can also involve various challenges and risks, such as security, privacy, compliance, performance, availability, and reliability .

#### Mnemonics and learning tricks

- To remember the benefits of on-demand provisioning, you can use the acronym **FACE**:
  - **F**lexibility: users can adjust their resources according to their needs
  - **A**gility: users can respond quickly to changing demands and opportunities
  - **C**ost-effectiveness: users can save money by paying only for what they use
  - **E**fficiency: users can avoid wasting resources and time
- To remember the challenges and risks of on-demand provisioning, you can use the acronym **SPACER**:
  - **S**ecurity: users need to protect their data and systems from unauthorized access and attacks
  - **P**rivacy: users need to ensure their data and systems are not exposed or leaked to unwanted parties
  - **C**ompliance: users need to follow the laws and regulations that apply to their data and systems
  - **A**vailability: users need to ensure their resources are accessible and functional at all times
  - **R**eliability: users need to ensure their resources are consistent and dependable
  - **E**xperience: users need to ensure their resources meet their expectations and requirements
