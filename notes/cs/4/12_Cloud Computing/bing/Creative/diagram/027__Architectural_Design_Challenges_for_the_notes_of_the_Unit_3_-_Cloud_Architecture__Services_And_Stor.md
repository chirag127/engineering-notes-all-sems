According to the search results, some of the architectural design challenges for the notes of the Unit 3 - Cloud Architecture, Services and Storage in the subject of Cloud Computing are:

- Scalability: How to design a cloud system that can handle increasing or decreasing demand without compromising performance, availability, or cost-efficiency.
- Virtualization: How to use virtual machines or containers to abstract the underlying hardware and enable dynamic provisioning, isolation, and portability of cloud services.
- Efficiency: How to optimize the utilization of cloud resources and minimize the operational overhead and energy consumption.
- Reliability: How to ensure that the cloud system can tolerate failures, recover from errors, and provide consistent and accurate results.
- Security: How to protect the cloud system from unauthorized access, data breaches, malicious attacks, and compliance violations.
