I/O devices are hardware components that allow a human operator or other systems to interface with a computer. They can deliver data (output) to and receive data from a computer (input). Some common I/O devices are keyboard, mouse, joystick, light pen, monitor, printer, scanner, speaker, microphone, etc.

In cloud computing, I/O devices can be used to access cloud resources, such as applications, data, and services, over the internet. They can also be used to collect and process data at the edge of the network, where the devices are located, to reduce latency and bandwidth consumption. This is known as edge computing.
