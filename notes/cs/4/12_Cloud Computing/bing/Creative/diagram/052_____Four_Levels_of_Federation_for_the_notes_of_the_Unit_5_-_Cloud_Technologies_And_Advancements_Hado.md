### Four Levels of Federation for Hadoop

- Federation is the process of integrating multiple independent systems or services into a single logical unit, while preserving their autonomy and heterogeneity.
- Federation can be applied to different aspects of cloud computing, such as infrastructure, platform, data, and applications.
- Federation can provide benefits such as scalability, availability, fault-tolerance, load balancing, interoperability, and resource optimization.
- According to , there are four levels of federation in the cloud:

  - **Level 1: Federation of Infrastructure Services**: This level involves the integration of multiple physical or virtual resources, such as servers, storage, and networks, into a unified pool that can be dynamically allocated and managed. Examples of this level are HDFS Federation   and OpenStack Federation.
  - **Level 2: Federation of Platform Services**: This level involves the integration of multiple platform services, such as databases, middleware, and frameworks, into a unified environment that can support the development and deployment of applications. Examples of this level are Google App Engine Federation and Hadoop Federation.
  - **Level 3: Federation of Data Services**: This level involves the integration of multiple data sources, such as files, databases, and streams, into a unified view that can be accessed and processed by applications. Examples of this level are MapReduce Federation and Hadoop YARN Federation.
  - **Level 4: Federation of Application Services**: This level involves the integration of multiple application services, such as web services, APIs, and workflows, into a unified composition that can provide higher-level functionality and value. Examples of this level are Service-Oriented Architecture (SOA) Federation and Hadoop Oozie Federation.

- The four levels of federation are not mutually exclusive, and can be combined to achieve different objectives and requirements. For example, Hadoop Federation can span across levels 2, 3, and 4, as it involves the integration of platform, data, and application services.
