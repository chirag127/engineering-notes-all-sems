## Unit 4 - Resource Management And Security In Cloud

Resource management and security in cloud are two important aspects of cloud computing that aim to optimize the performance, availability, and protection of cloud resources and data.

### Resource Management in Cloud

Resource management in cloud refers to the process of allocating, monitoring, and controlling the cloud resources, such as compute, storage, network, and applications, to meet the service level objectives and requirements of the cloud users and providers. Resource management in cloud involves the following tasks:

- **Resource provisioning**: The process of creating, configuring, and deploying the cloud resources on demand or in advance, based on the user requests or policies. Resource provisioning can be done manually or automatically, and can be static or dynamic. Static provisioning means the resources are fixed and predetermined, while dynamic provisioning means the resources can be adjusted and scaled according to the workload changes and demands.
- **Resource scheduling**: The process of assigning the cloud resources to the tasks or jobs that need to be executed, based on the priority, deadline, cost, and quality of service. Resource scheduling can be done centrally or distributedly, and can be deterministic or stochastic. Deterministic scheduling means the resource allocation is based on the known information and parameters, while stochastic scheduling means the resource allocation is based on the probabilistic models and predictions.
- **Resource monitoring**: The process of collecting, analyzing, and reporting the data and metrics related to the cloud resources, such as the utilization, availability, performance, and cost. Resource monitoring can be done actively or passively, and can be real-time or periodic. Active monitoring means the resources are probed or tested by the monitoring agents or tools, while passive monitoring means the resources are observed or measured by the monitoring sensors or logs. Real-time monitoring means the data and metrics are updated and displayed continuously, while periodic monitoring means the data and metrics are updated and displayed at regular intervals.
- **Resource optimization**: The process of improving the efficiency, effectiveness, and quality of the cloud resources, by applying various techniques and methods, such as load balancing, resource pooling, resource consolidation, resource migration, resource replication, and resource scaling. Resource optimization can be done reactively or proactively, and can be single-objective or multi-objective. Reactive optimization means the resources are adjusted and adapted after the workload changes and demands, while proactive optimization means the resources are adjusted and adapted before the workload changes and demands. Single-objective optimization means the resources are optimized for one goal or criterion, while multi-objective optimization means the resources are optimized for multiple goals or criteria.

### Security in Cloud

Security in cloud refers to the process of protecting the cloud resources and data from unauthorized access, use, modification, disclosure, or destruction, by applying various policies, standards, and technologies. Security in cloud involves the following aspects:

- **Security of the cloud**: The process of ensuring the security of the cloud infrastructure, platform, and software, that are provided and managed by the cloud providers. Security of the cloud includes the following elements:
  - **Physical security**: The process of securing the physical locations and devices of the cloud providers, such as the data centers, servers, routers, switches, and firewalls, from theft, damage, or sabotage, by using locks, alarms, cameras, guards, and biometrics.
  - **Network security**: The process of securing the network connections and communications of the cloud providers, such as the internet, intranet, VPN, and LAN, from interception, interruption, or alteration, by using encryption, authentication, authorization, firewall, and IDS/IPS.
  - **System security**: The process of securing the system components and configurations of the cloud providers, such as the operating systems, hypervisors, databases, and applications, from exploitation, infection, or corruption, by using patches, updates, antivirus, antimalware, and backup.
