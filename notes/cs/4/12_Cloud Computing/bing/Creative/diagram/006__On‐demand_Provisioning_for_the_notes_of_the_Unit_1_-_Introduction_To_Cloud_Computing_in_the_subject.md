On-demand provisioning is a feature of cloud computing that allows users to access computing resources as they need them, rather than having to reserve or allocate them in advance. On-demand provisioning enables users to scale up or down their resources according to their changing needs, and pay only for what they use. On-demand provisioning also reduces the complexity and cost of managing and maintaining the resources, as the cloud service provider takes care of the provisioning process.
