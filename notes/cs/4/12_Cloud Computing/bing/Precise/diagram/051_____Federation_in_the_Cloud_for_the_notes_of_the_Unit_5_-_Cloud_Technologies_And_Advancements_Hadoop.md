### Federation in the Cloud

Federation in the cloud refers to the association of small divisions to a single group for performing a common task. A federated cloud is formed by connecting the cloud environment of several cloud providers using a common standard. This helps the provider to easily scale up the resource to match business needs .

A federated cloud is a seamless environment formed by connecting the cloud environment of two or more cloud service providers using a common standard. It integrates heterogeneous cloud environments such as community cloud, public cloud, and private cloud in order to scale up the resources and services for the users .

The implementation and management of several internal and external cloud computing services to meet business demands is known as cloud federation, sometimes known as federated cloud. A global cloud system combines community, private, and public clouds into scalable computing platforms .

Some technologies that aid the cloud federation and cloud services are OpenNebula, a cloud computing platform for managing heterogeneous distributed data center infrastructures, and Aneka coordinator, a proposition of the Aneka services and Aneka peer components .

Federation with Azure AD or O365 enables users to authenticate using on-premises credentials and access all resources in the cloud. As a result, it becomes important to have a highly available AD FS infrastructure to ensure access to resources both on-premises and in the cloud .
