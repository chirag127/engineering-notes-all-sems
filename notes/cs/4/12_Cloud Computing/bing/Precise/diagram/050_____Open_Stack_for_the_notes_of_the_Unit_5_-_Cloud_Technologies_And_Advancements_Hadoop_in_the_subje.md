### OpenStack

OpenStack is an open-source cloud operating system that controls large pools of compute, storage, and networking resources throughout a datacenter. These resources are managed and provisioned through APIs with common authentication mechanisms .

- OpenStack is open-source and free software .
- It is mostly deployed as infrastructure-as-a-service (IaaS) in both public and private clouds where virtual servers and other resources are made available to users .
- The tools that comprise the OpenStack platform, called "projects," handle the core cloud-computing services of compute, networking, storage, identity, and image services .
- OpenStack is developed by the community and is one of the three most active open source projects in the world .
