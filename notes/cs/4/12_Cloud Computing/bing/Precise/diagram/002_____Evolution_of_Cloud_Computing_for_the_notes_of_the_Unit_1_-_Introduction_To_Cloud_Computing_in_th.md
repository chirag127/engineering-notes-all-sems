### Evolution of Cloud Computing

Cloud computing is the delivery of many, dynamically scalable, virtualized resources as a service through the Internet. The evolution of cloud computing can be divided into three basic phases:

1. **The Idea Phase:** This phase began in the early 1960s with the emergence of utility and grid computing and lasted until the pre-internet bubble era. Joseph Carl Robnett Licklider was the founder of cloud computing .

2. **Virtualization meets the Internet:** Virtualization for PC-based systems started in earnest. As the Internet grew in popularity, companies began to see the potential for delivering computing resources over the Internet .

3. **The Cloud is Born:** The most recent development of cloud computing has evolved from Web 2.0 technology, which caters to web applications that facilitate participatory information sharing, interoperability, and user-centered design. Examples of Web 2.0 include wikis, blogs, social networking, and video sharing .

New technologies have emerged recently as a result of the growth of cloud computing. Some of these include containers, serverless computing, and cloud security .
