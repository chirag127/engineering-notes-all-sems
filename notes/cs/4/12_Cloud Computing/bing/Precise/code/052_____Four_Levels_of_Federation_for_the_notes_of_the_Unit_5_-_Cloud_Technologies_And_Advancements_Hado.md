### Four Levels of Federation

Federation in the cloud refers to the implementation and management of several internal and external cloud computing services to meet business demands. It is sometimes known as federated cloud. A global cloud system combines community, private, and public clouds into scalable computing platforms.

The federated cloud focuses on the implementation and usability of elements in the federated cloud architecture. The four centrics of the federated cloud are customer, business, provider, service. The federated cloud architecture and mechanism are designed prioritizing the customer.
