### Definition of Cloud

- Cloud refers to a large pool of easily usable and accessible virtualized resources (such as computing power, storage, applications, and services).
- These resources can be rapidly provisioned and released with minimal management effort or service provider interaction.
- Cloud computing is a type of computing that relies on sharing computing resources rather than having local servers or personal devices to handle applications.
- The primary advantage of cloud computing is increased efficiency, as it allows organizations to consume resources only as needed and scale up or down quickly.
- Key characteristics of cloud computing include on-demand self-service, broad access, resource pooling, elasticity, and measured service.
- The most common cloud computing service models are Infrastructure as a Service (IaaS), Platform as a Service (PaaS), and Software as a Service (SaaS).
- The key benefits of cloud computing include agility, scalability, economics, and automatic updates. However, cloud computing also brings some risks and challenges, such as security concerns, vendor lock-in, and bandwidth requirements.
