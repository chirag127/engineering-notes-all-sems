### On‐demand Provisioning for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction To Cloud Computing in the subject of Cloud Computing

1. On-demand provisioning: This is one of the key characteristics of cloud computing. It refers to the ability to provision computing resources like servers and networking components dynamically as and when required by the users. The resources are provided as a service and can be scaled up or down as needed. This eliminates the need for upfront investment in infrastructure and resources that may later be under-utilized.

2. Scalability: The computing resources in cloud can be scaled up or down as per the requirements of the users. This is useful for applications with variable demand. The resources can be scaled up during peak demand and scaled down during low demand to reduce costs. The scalability is achieved through on-demand provisioning.

3. Self-service: The users can provision and manage the computing resources as per their needs through a self-service portal or interface. The cloud users do not require the involvement of the cloud provider for resource provisioning and can do it themselves as per their requirements. This increases the agility and convenience.

4. Pay-as-you-go: The cloud users pay for the resources as per their usage. There is no upfront cost or fixed cost for the resources. The users pay for what they use and can increase or decrease their usage as needed. This pay-as-you-go model makes cloud computing very cost effective as the users do not end up paying for underutilized resources.

The above points cover the key aspects of on-demand provisioning in cloud computing. On-demand provisioning along with scalability and self-service makes cloud computing a flexible and agile model for computing resources. The pay-as-you-go model in addition makes it a very cost effective solution.
