
### IAM for the Notes of Unit 4 - Resource Management and Security in Cloud Computing

1. Identity and Access Management (IAM) is a system used to manage access to cloud services and resources. It provides a secure way of controlling access to cloud services and resources, allowing users to access only the resources they are authorized to use.

2. IAM enables organizations to control who can access their cloud resources and how they can access them. It also enables organizations to control who can administer the cloud resources, as well as the level of access they can have.

3. IAM also allows organizations to set up roles and permissions for users and groups, allowing them to control who can access the cloud resources.
