
### Global Exchange of Cloud Resources

1. Cloud resource exchange (CRE) is a concept that enables organizations to share resources in the cloud. 
2. CRE enables organizations to access resources from different cloud providers, such as software, hardware and services, without having to purchase them from a single provider. 
3. It also allows organizations to access resources from different parts of the world, allowing them to access new markets and services. 
4. CRE also allows cloud providers to access resources from different parts of the world, allowing them to offer more services to their customers. 
5. CRE is a cost-effective solution for organizations that need to access resources from different parts of the world. 
6. CRE also allows organizations to access resources from different cloud providers, allowing them to access new services, features and technologies. 
7. Security is an important consideration when it comes to CRE. Organizations must ensure that the resources they are sharing are secure and that they are not exposing their data to unauthorized access. 
8. Organizations must also ensure that the resources they are sharing are compliant with applicable laws and regulations. 
9. Organizations must also ensure that the resources they are sharing are in compliance with the terms and conditions of the cloud provider. 
10. Organizations must also ensure that they are not violating any intellectual property rights of the cloud provider.
