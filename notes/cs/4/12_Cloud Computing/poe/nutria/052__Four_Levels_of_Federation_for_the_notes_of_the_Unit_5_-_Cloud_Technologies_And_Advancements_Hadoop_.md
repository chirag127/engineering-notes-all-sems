
### Four Levels of Federation
1. **Single Federation**: This is the most basic type of federation, and it is the foundation of the other three types. In this type, all the nodes of the federation are connected to the same network and share resources. 

2. **Multi-Federation**: In this type, the nodes of the federation are connected to multiple networks, which allows them to access different types of resources. This type of federation is used when there is a need for multiple types of resources and services.

3. **Hybrid Federation**: This type combines the features of both single and multi-federation. This allows the nodes of the federation to access different types of resources from different networks.

4. **Distributed Federation**: This type of federation is used when the nodes of the federation are spread across different networks. This type of federation is used when the resources and services need to be shared across multiple networks.
