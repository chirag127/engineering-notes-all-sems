## Unit 4 - Resource Management And Security In Cloud

Resource management and security in the cloud refers to the processes and technologies used to ensure the efficient use of cloud computing resources while maintaining the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of data.

Key components of resource management in the cloud include:
- Capacity planning: determining the amount of resources needed to meet demand
- Load balancing: distributing workloads across multiple resources to ensure optimal performance
- Auto-scaling: adjusting the number of resources in response to changes in demand

Security in the cloud includes:
- Access control: managing who has access to cloud resources and data
- Encryption: protecting data in transit and at rest
- Incident response: having a plan in place to respond to security incidents
- Compliance: ensuring that the use of cloud resources aligns with industry regulations and standards.
