### PaaS for the notes of the Unit 3 - Cloud Architecture, Services And Storage in the subject of Cloud Computing

PaaS (Platform as a Service) is a cloud computing model that provides a platform and infrastructure for developers to build, run, and manage web applications and services without having to worry about the underlying infrastructure.

Features:
1. Infrastructure management
2. Development environment
3. Deployment and hosting
4. Scalability and availability
5. Monitoring and maintenance

Benefits:
1. Reduced IT costs
2. Increased collaboration and productivity
3. Improved scalability and reliability
4. Faster time to market

Examples: Google App Engine, Heroku, AWS Elastic Beanstalk.
