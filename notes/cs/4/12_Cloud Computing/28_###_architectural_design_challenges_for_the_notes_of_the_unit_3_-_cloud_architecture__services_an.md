### Architectural Design Challenges for the notes of the Unit 3 - Cloud Architecture, Services And Storage in the subject of Cloud Computing

1. Scalability: The ability to handle increased demand and growth of data and users.
2. Performance: Ensuring fast and reliable access to data and services.
3. Security: Protecting data and systems from unauthorized access and threats.
4. Availability: Ensuring high uptime and availability of services.
5. Cost-effectiveness: Balancing the cost of infrastructure and services with the benefits they provide.
6. Interoperability: Ensuring compatibility and integration with existing systems and technologies.
7. Compliance: Adhering to regulations and standards for data privacy and security.
