### On‐demand Provisioning for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction To Cloud Computing in the subject of Cloud Computing

On-demand Provisioning refers to the ability to quickly and automatically allocate computing resources such as computing power, storage, and network bandwidth as needed. This is a key feature of cloud computing that enables users to scale their resources up or down as needed, without having to make a large upfront investment in hardware and software.

On-demand provisioning is achieved through the use of virtualization technology, which enables multiple virtual machines to run on a single physical machine. This enables cloud providers to dynamically allocate resources to meet the changing needs of their customers.

Benefits of on-demand provisioning include:
1. Flexibility: The ability to quickly and easily scale resources up or down as needed.
2. Cost savings: The ability to pay only for the resources that are actually used, reducing the cost of IT infrastructure.
3. Increased efficiency: The ability to allocate resources more efficiently, reducing waste and improving utilization.
4. Improved agility: The ability to respond quickly to changing business needs, improving competitiveness and innovation.

On-demand provisioning is a key characteristic of cloud computing and is a major factor in its increasing popularity and adoption.
