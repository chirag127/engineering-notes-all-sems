### Resource Provisioning Methods for the notes of the Unit 4 - Resource Management And Security In Cloud in the subject of Cloud Computing

Resource Provisioning Methods:
1. On-demand: Resources are provisioned as needed and billed based on usage.
2. Reservations: Advance purchase of resources with a discount for long-term commitment.
3. Spot instances: Bidding for unused resources with the possibility of interruption.
4. Dedicated Hosts: Physical server dedicated for exclusive use.
5. Auto-scaling: Automatically provision and de-provision resources based on demand.
