
### Digital sensors for the notes of the Unit 2 - Hardware for IoT in the subject of Internet of Things

Digital sensors are electronic devices used to measure physical characteristics such as temperature, pressure, humidity, and light. They are used to collect data from the environment and transmit it to a computer or other device. Digital sensors are essential components of the Internet of Things (IoT) and are used to monitor and control the environment.

Mnemonics:

- **T**emperature
- **P**ressure
- **H**umidity
- **L**ight

Advantages of Digital Sensors:

- High accuracy
- Low power consumption
- Easy to install and maintain
- Low cost
- Wide range of applications

Disadvantages of Digital Sensors:

- Susceptible to noise and interference
- Limited range
- Limited resolution

Examples of Digital Sensors:

- Temperature sensors
- Pressure sensors
- Humidity sensors
- Light sensors
- Motion sensors

Applications of Digital Sensors:

- Automotive
- Home automation
- Industrial automation
- Medical
- Environmental monitoring
