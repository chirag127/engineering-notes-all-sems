

### Survey routing protocols for the notes of the Unit 4 - Network & Communication aspects in IoT in the subject of Internet of Things

Survey routing protocols are used to share information between nodes in a network. These protocols are used to collect information about the network topology and the nodes in the network. They allow nodes to discover other nodes and determine the best route for data to travel.

Survey routing protocols are used in Internet of Things (IoT) networks to enable communication between nodes. They are used to share information about the network topology, the nodes in the network, and the best routes for data to travel.

Mnemonics and learning tricks: 

* **R**outing **I**nformation **P**rotocol (RIP): Uses distance-vector routing algorithms to determine the best route for data to travel.
* **O**pen **S**hortest **P**ath **F**irst (OSPF): Uses link-state routing algorithms to determine the best route for data to travel.
* **B**order **G**ateway **P**rotocol (BGP): Used to route traffic between autonomous systems.
* **D**ynamic **H**ost **C**onfiguration **P**rotocol (DHCP): Used to assign IP addresses to nodes in a network.

Advantages:

* Survey routing protocols allow nodes to discover other nodes and determine the best route for data to travel.
* They reduce the amount of data that needs to be sent across the network, as only the necessary information is shared.
* They allow for efficient and reliable communication between nodes.

Disadvantages:

* Survey routing protocols can be complex and difficult to configure.
* They can be vulnerable to malicious attacks, as they rely on the accuracy of the information shared.
* They can be resource-intensive, as they require a lot of data to be sent across the network.
