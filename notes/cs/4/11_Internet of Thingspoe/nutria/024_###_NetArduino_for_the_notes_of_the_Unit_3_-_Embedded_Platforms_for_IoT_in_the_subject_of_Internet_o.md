
### NetArduino for the notes of the Unit 3 - Embedded Platforms for IoT in the subject of Internet of Things

NetArduino is an open-source, low-cost, and low-power embedded platform that is designed for the Internet of Things (IoT). It is based on the Arduino hardware platform and is programmed using the Arduino IDE. NetArduino is designed to be used in a variety of applications, such as home automation, robotics, and smart cities.

#### Advantages of NetArduino
- Low cost: The NetArduino platform is relatively inexpensive compared to other embedded platforms, making it an attractive choice for hobbyists and professionals alike.
- Low power consumption: NetArduino is designed to be energy-efficient, making it ideal for battery-powered applications.
- Open source: NetArduino is open-source, which means that anyone can modify and improve the platform.
- Easy to use: NetArduino is designed to be user-friendly, making it easy to get started with embedded programming.

#### Disadvantages of NetArduino
- Limited memory: NetArduino has limited memory, which can limit the complexity of applications that can be built on the platform.
- Limited connectivity: NetArduino has limited connectivity options, which can limit the types of devices and networks that can be used with the platform.
- Limited support: NetArduino has limited support from the community, which can make it difficult to find help with issues or questions.
