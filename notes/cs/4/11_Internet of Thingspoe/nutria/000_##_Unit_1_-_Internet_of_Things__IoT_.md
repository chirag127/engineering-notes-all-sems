
## Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT)

IoT is a network of physical devices, vehicles, home appliances, and other items embedded with electronics, software, sensors, actuators, and network connectivity which enable these objects to connect and exchange data.

1. **What is IoT?**

IoT is a network of physical devices, vehicles, home appliances, and other items embedded with electronics, software, sensors, actuators, and network connectivity which enable these objects to connect and exchange data.

2. **Benefits of IoT**

IoT can provide many benefits, including improved efficiency, greater convenience, and cost savings. IoT can also help to improve safety and security, and make it easier to monitor and control devices remotely.

3. **Applications of IoT**

IoT is being used in a variety of applications, such as smart homes, connected cars, health monitoring, industrial automation, and smart cities.

4. **Security and Privacy in IoT**

Security and privacy are key concerns for IoT due to the potential for unauthorized access to data and devices. It is important to ensure that IoT devices are secure, and that data is protected.

5. **Mnemonics and Learning Tricks**

To remember the key components of IoT, you can use the mnemonic "E-S-A-N-C":

- **E**lectronics
- **S**oftware
- **A**ctuators
- **N**etwork
- **C**onnectivity
