
## Unit 4 - Network & Communication aspects in IoT

Network and communication aspects in the context of the Internet of Things (IoT) refer to the physical and logical connections that enable the various devices and systems that make up an IoT network to communicate with each other. This includes:

- **Network Protocols:** Network protocols such as TCP/IP, IPv6, and Zigbee are used to facilitate communication between IoT devices.
- **Network Topology:** Network topologies such as mesh and star networks can be used to optimize communication between devices.
- **Data Transmission:** Data transmission methods such as Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, and cellular networks are used to send data between devices.
- **Data Security:** Security measures such as encryption, authentication, and access control are used to protect data from unauthorized access.
- **Data Storage:** Storage solutions such as cloud storage and edge computing are used to store data and enable access to it.

- **Data Storage:** To remember the two most popular data storage solutions used in IoT, remember the phrase "Cloud and Edge".
