

### Wireless Sensor Networks for the Notes of the Unit 2 - Hardware for IoT in the Subject of Internet of Things 

* Wireless Sensor Networks (WSNs) are networks of small, low power devices that can detect, measure, and communicate physical or environmental conditions. 
* They are composed of a large number of nodes that are connected by wireless links and can be used to collect data from the environment and transmit it to a central location. 
* WSNs are used for a variety of applications, including environmental monitoring, industrial automation, healthcare monitoring, and home automation. 
* Advantages of WSNs include low cost, low power consumption, and scalability. 
* Disadvantages of WSNs include limited range, limited bandwidth, and limited processing power. 
* A good mnemonic for remembering WSNs is “LOWPOWER”: Low Cost, Operational Range, Wireless Connectivity, Processing Power, Energy Consumption, Reliability. 
* Examples of WSNs include smart home systems, industrial automation systems, and medical monitoring systems. 
* Applications of WSNs include monitoring air quality, monitoring water quality, and tracking wildlife.
