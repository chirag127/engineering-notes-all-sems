
### Sensors for the notes of the Unit 2 - Hardware for IoT in the subject of Internet of Things

Sensors are devices used to measure physical phenomena, such as temperature, pressure, humidity, light, etc. They are essential components in the Internet of Things (IoT) as they are used to collect data to be sent to a remote server.

The types of sensors used in IoT include:

- Temperature Sensors: These are used to measure temperature and can be used in applications such as climate control and monitoring.
- Pressure Sensors: These are used to measure pressure and can be used in applications such as water level monitoring and air pressure monitoring.
- Humidity Sensors: These are used to measure humidity and can be used in applications such as air quality monitoring.
- Light Sensors: These are used to measure light intensity and can be used in applications such as lighting control and monitoring.
- Motion Sensors: These are used to detect motion and can be used in applications such as security systems and monitoring.

- **T**emperature **P**ressure **H**umidity **L**ight **M**otion (TPHLM) 
- **S**ensors **A**re **M**onitoring **E**verything (SAME)
