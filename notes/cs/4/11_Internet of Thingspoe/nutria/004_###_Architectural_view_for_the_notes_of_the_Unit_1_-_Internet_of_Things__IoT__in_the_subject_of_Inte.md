
### Architectural view for the notes of the Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT) in the subject of Internet of Things

* The Internet of Things (IoT) is a network of physical objects that are connected to the internet and can communicate with each other. These objects can be anything from a smart thermostat to a self-driving car.

* The architectural view of the IoT consists of three layers: the physical layer, the communication layer, and the application layer.

* The physical layer consists of the physical objects that make up the IoT, such as sensors, actuators, and other devices. These objects are connected to the internet via a network of wires, radio frequencies, and other communication mediums.

* The communication layer consists of the protocols used to communicate between the objects in the physical layer. These protocols include Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, Zigbee, and other communication protocols.

* The application layer consists of the applications that are used to control and manage the objects in the physical layer. These applications can be web-based, mobile-based, or even embedded in the physical objects themselves.

* The IoT also has a data layer, which is responsible for collecting and storing data from the physical objects. This data can be used to analyze and make decisions on how to operate the physical objects.

* Mnemonics and learning tricks for the Architectural view for the notes of the Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT) in the subject of Internet of Things:
  * P- Physical layer
  * C- Communication layer
  * A- Application layer
  * D- Data layer
