
### Technology Behind IoT for the Notes of the Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT) in the Subject of Internet of Things

1. The Internet of Things (IoT) is a network of physical devices, vehicles, home appliances and other items embedded with electronics, software, sensors, actuators, and network connectivity which enables these objects to collect and exchange data. 
2. IoT technology allows objects to be sensed and controlled remotely across existing network infrastructure, creating opportunities for more direct integration between the physical world and computer-based systems, and resulting in improved efficiency, accuracy and economic benefit. 
3. IoT technology is based on four main components: sensors, actuators, communication networks and data processing. 
4. Sensors are used to detect and measure physical parameters in the environment. Examples include temperature, humidity, light, motion, and pressure sensors. 
5. Actuators are used to control and manipulate physical parameters in the environment. Examples include motors, relays, and valves. 
6. Communication networks allow the sensors and actuators to communicate with each other and with other computers. Examples include Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, cellular, and satellite networks. 
7. Data processing is used to process the data collected from the sensors and actuators. Examples include data mining, machine learning, and artificial intelligence algorithms. 
8. Mnemonics:
    * **I** = Internet
    * **O** = of 
    * **T** = Things 
9. Learning Tricks: 
    * Think of IoT as a giant network of connected 'things' that can collect and exchange data. 
    * Think of sensors as the eyes and ears of the IoT, collecting data from the environment. 
    * Think of actuators as the hands and feet of the IoT, controlling and manipulating physical parameters in the environment. 
    * Think of communication networks as the highways of the IoT, connecting the sensors and actuators to each other and to other computers. 
    * Think of data processing as the brains of the IoT, processing the data collected from the sensors and actuators.
