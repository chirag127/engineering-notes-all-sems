
### M2M Communication for the notes of the Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT) in the subject of Internet of Things

M2M communication, also known as Machine-to-Machine communication, is the communication between two or more machines or devices. It is a form of communication that does not require human intervention. M2M communication is used in a variety of applications in the Internet of Things (IoT).

M2M communication is based on the following technologies:

- Radio Frequency Identification (RFID): RFID is a technology that uses radio waves to identify and track objects. RFID tags are used to identify and track objects in a variety of applications, such as inventory control and asset tracking.

- Wi-Fi: Wi-Fi is a wireless networking technology that enables devices to communicate with each other over a wireless network. Wi-Fi is used in a variety of applications, such as home automation and smart home systems.

- Bluetooth: Bluetooth is a wireless technology that enables devices to communicate with each other over a short-range radio frequency. Bluetooth is used in a variety of applications, such as wireless headsets, speakers, and fitness trackers.

- Zigbee: Zigbee is a wireless communication protocol that enables devices to communicate with each other over a short-range radio frequency. Zigbee is used in a variety of applications, such as home automation and smart home systems.

- Near Field Communication (NFC): NFC is a wireless communication protocol that enables devices to communicate with each other over a short-range radio frequency. NFC is used in a variety of applications, such as contactless payment systems and digital keys.

M2M communication is used in a variety of applications in the Internet of Things (IoT), such as home automation and smart home systems, asset tracking, inventory control, digital keys, contactless payment systems, and wireless headsets.

M2M communication is beneficial because it enables devices to communicate with each other without human intervention. Additionally, M2M communication is secure and reliable, and it is energy efficient.
