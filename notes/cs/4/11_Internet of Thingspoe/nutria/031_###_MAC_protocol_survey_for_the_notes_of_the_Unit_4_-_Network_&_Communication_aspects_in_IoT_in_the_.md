
### MAC Protocol Survey for the Notes of the Unit 4 - Network & Communication Aspects in IoT in the Subject of Internet of Things

- The Medium Access Control (MAC) protocol is an important part of the Internet of Things (IoT). It is responsible for the coordination of communication between devices on the same network. 
- The MAC protocol is used to provide access to shared medium, such as a wireless network, and to control the flow of data. 
- The MAC protocol is responsible for ensuring that data is transmitted in an orderly manner, and that no two devices are sending data at the same time. 
- The MAC protocol also provides a mechanism for devices to detect collisions, and retransmit data if necessary. 
- The MAC protocol is divided into two main categories: contention-based protocols and reservation-based protocols. 
- Contention-based protocols, such as the Carrier Sense Multiple Access (CSMA) protocol, allow devices to access the medium in an arbitrary order. 
- Reservation-based protocols, such as the Time Division Multiple Access (TDMA) protocol, allow devices to access the medium in a predetermined order. 
- The MAC protocol is also responsible for providing a mechanism for devices to detect and respond to errors. 
- The MAC protocol is designed to be efficient, reliable, and secure. 
- Mnemonics for the MAC protocol include:
    - CSMA: Carrier Sense Multiple Access 
    - TDMA: Time Division Multiple Access 
    - CSMA/CD: Carrier Sense Multiple Access with Collision Detection 
    - CSMA/CA: Carrier Sense Multiple Access with Collision Avoidance

    - Visualize the data flow in terms of a highway with cars on it. 
    - Think of the MAC protocol as a traffic cop, controlling the flow of data. 
    - Remember that the goal of the MAC protocol is to ensure that data is transmitted in an orderly manner, and that no two devices are sending data at the same time.
