
### Beagle Bone for the notes of the Unit 3 - Embedded Platforms for IoT in the subject of Internet of Things

The BeagleBone is a low-cost, single-board computer designed for use in embedded systems, such as those found in the Internet of Things (IoT). It is an open source hardware platform based on the ARM Cortex-A8 processor, which runs Linux or Android. It is a powerful and versatile platform that can be used for a variety of applications, including robotics, home automation, and other embedded systems.

The BeagleBone is a powerful and versatile platform for embedded systems. It is a small, low-cost, single-board computer that runs Linux or Android. It has a powerful ARM Cortex-A8 processor, 512MB of RAM, 4GB of on-board flash storage, and a microSD card slot. It also has multiple I/O ports, including Ethernet, USB, HDMI, and CAN bus.
