
### Additions in Arduino for the Notes of the Unit 5 - Programming the Arduino in the Subject of Internet of Things

- Arduino is an open-source electronics platform based on easy-to-use hardware and software. It is intended for anyone making interactive projects.
- Arduino boards are able to read inputs - such as light on a sensor, a finger on a button, or a Twitter message - and turn it into an output - activating a motor, turning on an LED, publishing something online.
- Unit 5 of Programming the Arduino focuses on working with the Internet of Things (IoT). IoT is a system of interrelated computing devices, mechanical and digital machines, objects, animals or people that are provided with unique identifiers and the ability to transfer data over a network without requiring human-to-human or human-to-computer interaction.
- In this unit, students will learn how to connect their Arduino boards to the internet and use them to control things remotely. They will also learn how to use the Arduino IDE to write sketches that will enable their projects to interact with the internet.
- Mnemonics and learning tricks for this unit include:
  - **I**nternet of **T**hings: Think of the internet as a giant web of connected devices.
  - **S**ketch: Think of a sketch as a set of instructions that tell the Arduino board what to do.
  - **W**ireless: Remember that the Arduino board can be connected to the internet wirelessly.
  - **F**unctions: Think of functions as blocks of code that can be reused in different places in a sketch.
  - **P**ins: Remember that pins are used to connect components to the Arduino board.
  - **V**ariables: Variables are used to store data that can be used in a sketch.
