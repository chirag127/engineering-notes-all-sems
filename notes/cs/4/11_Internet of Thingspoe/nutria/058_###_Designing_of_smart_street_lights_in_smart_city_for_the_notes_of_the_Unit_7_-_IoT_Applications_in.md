
### Designing of Smart Street Lights in Smart City for the Notes of the Unit 7 - IoT Applications in the Subject of Internet of Things

1. Smart street lights are an important part of the Internet of Things (IoT) and are used to control and monitor street lighting in cities and towns.
2. Smart street lights are connected to a network and are able to communicate with each other and with other devices in the network.
3. Smart street lights can be used to detect motion, measure temperature and humidity, and detect presence of people.
4. Smart street lights are energy efficient and can be programmed to turn on and off according to the time of day and the amount of light needed.
5. Smart street lights can also be used to provide information about traffic, air quality, and other environmental conditions.
6. Smart street lights can be used to alert emergency services in case of an emergency.
7. Smart street lights can also be used to provide Wi-Fi access and other services.
8. Mnemonics and learning tricks for designing smart street lights can include:
    - **S**ensors, **T**raffic, **E**nergy, **E**mergency, **T**emperature, **L**ighting, **I**nformation, **G**eneral **H**ealth, and **T**elecommunications.
9. Advantages of smart street lights include: energy efficiency, cost savings, improved safety, and better air quality.
10. Disadvantages of smart street lights include: potential privacy concerns, potential security risks, and potential interference with other radio signals.
11. Examples of applications of smart street lights include: traffic management, emergency response, and air quality monitoring.
