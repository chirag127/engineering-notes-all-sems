
### Embedded computing basics for the notes of the Unit 3 - Embedded Platforms for IoT in the subject of Internet of Things

Embedded computing is a type of computing that is used to control physical devices. It is used in a wide range of applications, including industrial automation, robotics, and the Internet of Things (IoT).

The following are some of the basic concepts related to embedded computing:

- **Microcontrollers:** Microcontrollers are small, low-power, single-chip computers that are designed to control physical devices. They are often used in embedded systems, as they are inexpensive and require minimal power.

- **Real-time operating systems (RTOS):** RTOSs are operating systems that are designed to respond to events in real-time. They are often used in embedded systems, as they can be tailored to meet the specific requirements of the system.

- **Embedded programming languages:** Embedded programming languages are languages specifically designed for embedded systems. Examples include C, C++, and Assembly.

- **Networking:** Networking is the process of connecting two or more devices together. This is often done using a variety of protocols, such as Ethernet, Wi-Fi, and Bluetooth.

- **Sensors:** Sensors are devices used to measure physical properties, such as temperature, humidity, and light. They are often used in embedded systems to monitor the environment.

- **Mnemonic learning tricks:** Mnemonic learning tricks are techniques used to help remember information. Examples include acronyms, rhymes, and stories.

- **Debugging:** Debugging is the process of finding and fixing errors in a program. Debugging is an important part of embedded programming, as it can help ensure that the system is working correctly.
