
## Unit 7 - IoT Applications

- **Internet of Things (IoT)** is a network of physical objects such as devices, vehicles, buildings and other items embedded with electronics, software, sensors, and network connectivity which enable these objects to collect and exchange data.
- **IoT Applications** are the applications that use the data collected by the IoT devices to provide useful insights and enable automation.
- **Types of IoT Applications**: 
  - **Connected Home**: Connected home applications use IoT devices to enable users to monitor and control their home environment remotely. 
  - **Smart Cities**: Smart cities use IoT devices to monitor and control the city infrastructure, such as traffic lights, public transportation, and water and energy consumption.
  - **Industrial Automation**: Industrial automation applications use IoT devices to monitor and control industrial processes, such as manufacturing, assembly, and logistics.
  - **Healthcare**: Healthcare applications use IoT devices to monitor and track patient health, such as monitoring vital signs and tracking medication adherence.
  - **Agriculture**: Agriculture applications use IoT devices to monitor and control agricultural processes, such as crop and soil monitoring and livestock tracking.
- **Benefits of IoT Applications**: 
  - Increased efficiency and productivity
  - Improved customer experience
  - Reduced costs
  - Improved safety and security
  - Real-time monitoring and control
  - Improved decision-making
- **Mnemonics and Learning Tricks**: 
  - IoT stands for "Internet of Things"
  - IoT applications can be used to monitor and control a variety of devices and processes.
  - IoT applications can provide increased efficiency, improved customer experience, reduced costs, improved safety and security, real-time monitoring and control, and improved decision-making.
