
### Vision for the notes of the Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT) in the subject of Internet of Things

- The Internet of Things (IoT) is a network of physical objects, such as machines, devices, sensors, and other items embedded with electronics, software, actuators, and network connectivity that enable these objects to collect and exchange data.
- IoT enables the connection of physical objects to the internet, allowing them to be monitored and controlled remotely.
- IoT can be used to monitor and control physical objects such as vehicles, buildings, and other environments. 
- IoT can also be used to create smart homes, smart cities, and smart factories. 
- IoT can improve efficiency, reduce costs, and provide better customer service.
- IoT can also be used to improve safety, security, and privacy.

- IoT can help to reduce energy consumption and emissions, as well as to improve sustainability.
