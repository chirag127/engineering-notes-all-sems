
### Intel Galileo Boards

Intel Galileo boards are a type of embedded platform used in the Internet of Things (IoT). The board also includes a microcontroller, which runs at 80 MHz, and a range of other features including built-in Wi-Fi and Bluetooth connectivity, an Ethernet port, and a USB port.

The board is designed to be used in a variety of applications, including home automation, robotics, and industrial automation. It is also suitable for use in educational settings, as it provides a platform for teaching students about embedded systems and programming.

Intel Galileo boards provide a range of features that make them ideal for use in IoT applications. They are designed to be energy efficient, as they use less power than other embedded platforms. They also offer high performance, as they are capable of handling multiple tasks simultaneously. Additionally, the board’s built-in Wi-Fi and Bluetooth connectivity make it easy to connect to other devices and the internet.
