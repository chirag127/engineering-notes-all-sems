Mnemonics and learning tricks can be helpful when it comes to coding for the Arduino in the subject of Internet of Things. Here are a few to consider:

* "I See Digital": This mnemonic helps to remember the order of the digital I/O pins on the Arduino: Input, Output, Digital.

* "AIO": This mnemonic helps to remember the order of the analog I/O pins on the Arduino: Analog, Input, Output.

* "SOS": This mnemonic helps to remember the order of the serial communication pins on the Arduino: Serial, Output, Serial.
