
### IoT/M2M Systems Layers and Design Standardization for the Notes of the Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT) in the Subject of Internet of Things

1. **Application Layer**: This layer is responsible for the applications that are used to interact with the IoT devices. It includes protocols such as MQTT, CoAP, and XMPP. It also includes cloud services and web services for data collection, analysis, and storage.

2. **Network Layer**: This layer is responsible for providing connectivity between the IoT devices and the applications. It includes protocols such as 6LoWPAN, Zigbee, and Bluetooth. It also includes routing protocols such as RIP and OSPF.

3. **Data Link Layer**: This layer is responsible for providing reliable communication between the IoT devices and the applications. It includes protocols such as IEEE 802.15.4 and Wi-Fi.

4. **Physical Layer**: This layer is responsible for providing physical connections between the IoT devices and the applications. It includes protocols such as Ethernet, USB, and Serial.

5. **Security Layer**: This layer is responsible for providing security to the IoT devices and the applications. It includes protocols such as TLS, SSL, and IPSec. It also includes encryption algorithms such as AES and SHA.

6. **Design Standardization**: Design standardization is important for the development of IoT systems. It includes standards such as ISO/IEC 9001, ISO/IEC 27001, and ISO/IEC 15408. It also includes frameworks such as NIST 800-53 and NIST 800-82.
