
### Communication Technologies for the Notes of the Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT) in the Subject of Internet of Things

1. **Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE):** BLE is a wireless communication technology used to enable short-range communication between two or more devices. It is used to connect IoT devices with each other and to enable communication between them. It is based on the IEEE 802.15.4 protocol and is a low-power, low-cost, and low-data rate technology. It is used for applications such as home automation, medical devices, and wearables.

2. **Wi-Fi:** Wi-Fi is a wireless communication technology used to enable high-speed communication between two or more devices. It is based on the IEEE 802.11 protocol and is used to connect devices to the Internet. It is used for applications such as streaming media, gaming, and home automation.

3. **Zigbee:** Zigbee is a wireless communication technology used to enable short-range communication between two or more devices. It is based on the IEEE 802.15.4 protocol and is a low-power, low-cost, and low-data rate technology. It is used for applications such as home automation, medical devices, and wearables.

4. **Near Field Communication (NFC):** NFC is a wireless communication technology used to enable short-range communication between two or more devices. It is based on the ISO/IEC 18092 protocol and is a low-power, low-cost, and low-data rate technology. It is used for applications such as contactless payments, access control, and home automation.

5. **Radio Frequency Identification (RFID):** RFID is a wireless communication technology used to enable short-range communication between two or more devices. It is based on the ISO/IEC 18000-2 protocol and is a low-power, low-cost, and low-data rate technology. It is used for applications such as asset tracking and inventory management. 

6. **LoRaWAN:** LoRaWAN is a wireless communication technology used to enable long-range communication between two or more devices. It is based on the LoRa protocol and is a low-power, low-cost, and low-data rate technology. It is used for applications such as asset tracking and smart city applications. 

7. **Sigfox:** Sigfox is a wireless communication technology used to enable long-range communication between two or more devices. It is based on the Sigfox protocol and is a low-power, low-cost, and low-data rate technology. It is used for applications such as asset tracking and smart city applications.
