
### City Automation for the notes of the Unit 7 - IoT Applications in the subject of Internet of Things

* City automation is a process that uses technology to automate certain tasks in cities and urban areas. This technology includes sensors, software, and other tools that allow cities to monitor and control their environment.
* City automation is used to improve the efficiency of city operations, reduce costs, and improve the quality of life for citizens.
* It can also be used to manage traffic, reduce pollution, and improve public safety.
* City automation can be used to improve energy efficiency by monitoring and controlling energy consumption in buildings and other areas.
* It can also be used to monitor and control air quality, water quality, and waste management.
* City automation can be used to improve communication between citizens and their local government. This can be done through the use of mobile apps, social media, and other digital channels.
* City automation can also be used to improve public transportation, such as buses and trains, as well as to manage traffic congestion.
* Finally, city automation can be used to improve the quality of life for citizens by providing access to information and services, such as health care and education.
