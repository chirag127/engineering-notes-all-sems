
### IoT Examples for the notes of the Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT) in the subject of Internet of Things

- The Internet of Things (IoT) is a network of physical objects, such as devices, vehicles, buildings, and other items embedded with electronics, software, sensors, and network connectivity. 
- These objects collect and exchange data with each other, making it possible to monitor, control, and automate processes remotely. 
- Examples of IoT applications include home automation, smart cities, connected cars, and industrial automation. 
- IoT devices are connected to the internet and can communicate with each other and with other devices and systems. 
- IoT devices can be used to monitor and control various aspects of a home or business, such as temperature, lighting, security, and energy usage. 
- IoT devices can also be used to monitor and control industrial processes, such as manufacturing, inventory management, and supply chain management. 
- IoT devices can also be used to track and monitor the location of people and objects, such as automobiles and shipping containers. 
- In addition, IoT devices can be used to collect data about the environment, such as air quality, water quality, and soil quality. 
- IoT devices can also be used to provide entertainment, such as streaming music and videos, and to provide information, such as news and weather.
