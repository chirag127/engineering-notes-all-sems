
### E-health for the notes of the Unit 7 - IoT Applications in the subject of Internet of Things

E-health, also known as Electronic Health, is a term that refers to the use of digital technologies to improve the quality of healthcare delivery, reduce costs, and increase patient access to healthcare services. It involves the use of the Internet, mobile phones, wearable devices, and other digital technologies to provide healthcare services.

E-health is an integral part of the Internet of Things (IoT). IoT devices, such as medical sensors and wearables, can be used to monitor and track health conditions, improve patient care, and provide real-time feedback. E-health also enables remote monitoring and diagnosis of health conditions, allowing doctors to monitor their patients remotely.

In addition, e-health can be used to improve the quality of healthcare delivery. For example, e-health can be used to improve patient communication, reduce paperwork, and streamline the delivery of healthcare services. By streamlining the delivery of healthcare services, e-health can help reduce the cost of healthcare delivery.

E-health can also be used to improve patient access to healthcare services. For example, e-health can be used to provide online consultations and remote diagnosis of health conditions. This can help to reduce the time and cost associated with traditional healthcare visits.

Finally, e-health can be used to improve the accuracy of healthcare delivery. By using digital technologies to track and monitor health conditions, e-health can help to reduce errors and improve the accuracy of healthcare delivery.
