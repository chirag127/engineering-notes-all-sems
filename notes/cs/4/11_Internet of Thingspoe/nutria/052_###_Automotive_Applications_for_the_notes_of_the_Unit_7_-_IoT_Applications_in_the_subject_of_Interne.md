
### Automotive Applications for the Notes of the Unit 7 - IoT Applications in the Subject of Internet of Things

* Automotive applications of Internet of Things (IoT) enable vehicles to communicate with other vehicles, roadside infrastructure, and the cloud. 
* Automotive IoT applications can be used to improve safety, reduce emissions, and increase convenience. 
* Automotive IoT applications can provide drivers with real-time information about traffic conditions, road hazards, and other drivers’ behavior. 
* Automotive IoT applications can also be used to monitor vehicle performance and diagnose problems before they become serious. 
* Automotive IoT applications can be used to provide drivers with navigation assistance and to automatically adjust the vehicle’s settings for optimal performance. 
* Automotive IoT applications can also be used to provide drivers with access to entertainment and other services, such as music streaming and online shopping. 
* Automotive IoT applications can be used to improve the efficiency of fleet management and to reduce the cost of vehicle maintenance. 
* Automotive IoT applications can also be used to enable remote monitoring and control of vehicles, and to provide drivers with real-time updates on their location and status. 
* Automotive IoT applications can be used to improve the security of vehicles by providing real-time alerts and notifications in the event of a theft or other security breach. 
* Automotive IoT applications can also be used to provide drivers with access to emergency services in the event of an accident or other emergency.
