
### Overview of IOT supported Hardware platforms such as Arduino for the notes of the Unit 3 - Embedded Platforms for IoT in the subject of Internet of Things

* Internet of Things (IoT) is a network of connected physical objects that can communicate with each other and the environment around them. These objects, known as “things”, can be anything from simple sensors to complex machines. 
* Embedded platforms for IoT are hardware devices that are specifically designed for use in an IoT network. These devices are often based on microcontrollers, which are small computers that can be programmed to perform specific tasks. Examples of embedded platforms for IoT include Arduino, Raspberry Pi, and BeagleBone. 
* Arduino is a popular open-source platform for developing connected devices. It is based on the AVR microcontroller and can be programmed using the Arduino programming language. Arduino is used in a wide variety of projects, including robotics, automation, and home automation.
* Raspberry Pi is a single-board computer that is based on the ARM processor. It is used in a variety of projects, including robotics, home automation, and media centers. 
* BeagleBone is a single-board computer based on the ARM processor. It is used in a variety of projects, including robotics, home automation, and media centers.

Advantages of using embedded platforms for IoT:

* Low cost: Embedded platforms for IoT are typically much more affordable than traditional computers. 
* Low power consumption: Embedded platforms for IoT are designed to use very little power, making them ideal for use in battery-powered applications. 
* Easy to program: Embedded platforms for IoT are typically easy to program, making them ideal for prototyping and experimenting with new ideas. 
* Versatility: Embedded platforms for IoT can be used in a variety of projects, from simple sensors to complex machines.

Disadvantages of using embedded platforms for IoT:

* Limited processing power: Embedded platforms for IoT are typically limited in terms of processing power, making them unsuitable for applications that require intensive computing. 
* Limited memory: Embedded platforms for IoT typically have limited amounts of memory, making them unsuitable for applications that require large amounts of data storage. 
* Lack of support: Embedded platforms for IoT are typically not supported by mainstream operating systems, making them difficult to use in some applications. 
* Security: Embedded platforms for IoT are often vulnerable to security threats, making them unsuitable for applications that require a high level of security.
