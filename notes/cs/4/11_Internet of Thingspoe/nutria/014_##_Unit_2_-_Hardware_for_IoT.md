
## Unit 2 - Hardware for IoT

This unit covers the hardware components needed to build an Internet of Things (IoT) system. It will explain the different types of hardware components, their roles, and how they interact with each other.

### Types of Hardware Components

There are three main types of hardware components used in IoT systems: sensors, controllers, and actuators.

#### Sensors

Sensors are used to collect data from the environment. They can measure temperature, pressure, light intensity, and more. The data collected by sensors is then sent to a controller.

#### Controllers

Controllers are used to process the data collected by sensors. They can be used to make decisions based on the data, such as turning on a light when the temperature drops below a certain level.

#### Actuators

Actuators are used to control physical devices. They can be used to turn on lights, open doors, and more.

### Mnemonics and Learning Tricks

To help remember the different types of hardware components, try using the following mnemonic:

**S**ensors **C**ollect **A**nd **C**ontrol **A**ctuators.

This mnemonic can be used to remember the order of the components: Sensors, Controllers, and Actuators.
