
### Wireless Medium Access Issues for the Notes of the Unit 4 - Network & Communication Aspects in IoT in the Subject of Internet of Things

* Wireless medium access is a key component of the Internet of Things (IoT) as it allows devices to communicate with each other over a wireless network. 
* The most common wireless medium access protocols used in IoT are:
    * Wi-Fi
    * Bluetooth
    * Zigbee
    * Z-Wave
    * LoRaWAN
* Each of these protocols has its own advantages and disadvantages, and it is important to understand their features in order to choose the best one for a particular application. 
* Wi-Fi is the most widely used protocol, as it provides high-speed data transmission and is widely available. However, it has limited range and requires a lot of power to transmit. 
* Bluetooth is a low-power, low-cost protocol that is ideal for short-range communication. It is commonly used for connecting devices in a home automation system. 
* Zigbee is a low-power, low-cost protocol that is optimized for low data rate applications. It is commonly used for connecting devices in a smart home or industrial setting. 
* Z-Wave is a low-power, low-cost protocol that is optimized for low data rate applications. It is commonly used for connecting devices in a home automation system. 
* LoRaWAN is a low-power, low-cost protocol that is optimized for low data rate applications. It is commonly used for connecting devices in a large-scale IoT network. 
* In order to remember the different protocols, it can be helpful to use the mnemonic “WBLZL” (Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Zigbee, Z-Wave, and LoRaWAN).
