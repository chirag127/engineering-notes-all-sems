
## Unit 3 - Embedded Platforms for IoT

Embedded platforms for IoT are a type of computer system that can be used to enable communication between connected devices. They are designed to be small and low-power, with the ability to connect to a variety of different networks and protocols. Examples of embedded platforms for IoT include:

* Arduino: Arduino boards are open-source microcontroller-based systems that are used to control various physical devices. Arduino boards can be programmed to interact with sensors and other devices to control and monitor them.

* Raspberry Pi: The Raspberry Pi is a single-board computer that is designed to be used as a platform for embedded systems. It is capable of running a variety of operating systems, and can be used to control and monitor a variety of different devices.

* BeagleBone: BeagleBone is an open-source single-board computer that is designed to be used as an embedded system. It is capable of running a variety of operating systems, and can be used to control and monitor a variety of different devices.

Mnemonics and Learning Tricks:

* Arduino: A-R-D-U-I-N-O - A stands for Automation, R stands for Robotics, D stands for Data, U stands for Usability, I stands for Interactivity, N stands for Networking, and O stands for Open Source. 

* Raspberry Pi: R-A-S-P-I - R stands for Robust, A stands for Affordable, S stands for Small, P stands for Powerful, and I stands for Innovative. 

* BeagleBone: B-E-A-G-L-E-B-O-N-E - B stands for Board, E stands for Embedded, A stands for Applications, G stands for GSM, L stands for Low-Power, E stands for Expansion, B stands for Bus, O stands for Open Source, N stands for Networking, and E stands for Environment.
