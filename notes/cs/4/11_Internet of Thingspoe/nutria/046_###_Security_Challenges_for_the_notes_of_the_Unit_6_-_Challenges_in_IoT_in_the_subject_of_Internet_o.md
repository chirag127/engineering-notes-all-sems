
### Security Challenges for the notes of the Unit 6 - Challenges in IoT in the subject of Internet of Things

- Security is a major challenge for Internet of Things (IoT) systems due to the high number of connected devices, the complexity of the underlying networks and the potential for malicious actors to exploit vulnerabilities. 
- IoT devices are often exposed to the public internet, making them vulnerable to attacks such as distributed denial-of-service (DDoS), man-in-the-middle (MITM), data tampering, and other malicious activities.
- Furthermore, the lack of security standards and protocols for IoT devices makes it difficult to secure them.
- Additionally, the lack of device authentication and authorization can lead to unauthorized access to devices and networks.
- The use of weak passwords and insecure communication protocols can also lead to data breaches and other security issues.
- Mnemonics and learning tricks for the security challenges in IoT can be useful to remember the security principles and best practices.
- Some examples of mnemonics and learning tricks include:
  - "Secure the device, secure the network, secure the data" 
  - "Think before you link" 
  - "Secure the basics first"
- Security best practices for IoT include:
  - Using strong passwords and two-factor authentication
  - Encrypting communication between devices and networks
  - Using secure protocols such as TLS/SSL
  - Implementing access control measures such as role-based access control (RBAC)
  - Regularly patching and updating devices
  - Monitoring for potential threats and suspicious activities
  - Implementing security policies and procedures
