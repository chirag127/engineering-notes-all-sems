### Sensors for the notes of the Unit 2 - Hardware for IoT in the subject of Internet of Things

The following are the common sensors used in IoT devices:

1. Temperature Sensor:
- Measures ambient temperature and converts it into an electrical signal.
- Common types: Thermistor, Resistance Temperature Detector (RTD), Thermocouple.
- Applications: Smart thermostats, fire detection, refrigeration systems, etc.
- Mnemonic: *Thermistors and RTDs have Resistance that varies with Temperature.*

2. Humidity Sensor:
- Measures amount of moisture in air and converts it into an electrical signal.
- Common types: Capacitive, Resistive, Thermal.
- Applications: Smart air conditioning, greenhouse monitoring, etc.

3. Light Sensor:
- Measures intensity of light in the environment and converts it into an electrical signal.
- Common types: Photoresistor, Photodiode, Phototransistor.
- Applications: Smart lighting, device display brightness control, photography, etc.
- Mnemonic: *More light means more current in Photoresistors and less resistance.*

4. Motion Sensor:
- Detects physical movement in the environment and converts it into an electrical signal.
- Common types: PIR sensor, Ultrasonic sensor, Radar sensor, etc.
- Applications: Security systems, automatic doors, gesture recognition, etc.
