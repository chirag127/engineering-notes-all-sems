### IoT Examples for the notes of the Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT)

1. Smart Home: IoT devices like sensors, smart switches, thermostats, etc. are deployed in a home to automate things and enable smart living. For e.g. - smart switches can automate lights, smart thermostats can automate temperature, smart locks can automate doors, etc.

Mnemonic: Think of a fully automated home with IoT devices as 'Smart Home'

2. Smart City: IoT devices are deployed at a city level to enable efficient management of a city. For e.g. - sensors can monitor traffic and optimize traffic flow, sensors can monitor waste bins and optimize waste collection routes, sensors can monitor environmental parameters like pollution, etc.

Mnemonic: Think of a futuristic city with automation using IoT devices as 'Smart City'

3. Smart Healthcare: IoT devices can be used to enable remote health monitoring and smart healthcare. For e.g. - fitness bands can monitor health parameters like steps taken, heart rate, etc. and alert if abnormal, medical sensors can monitor health of patients in hospitals and alert doctors/nurses of critical conditions, etc.

Mnemonic: Think of wireless health monitoring using IoT devices as 'Smart Healthcare'
