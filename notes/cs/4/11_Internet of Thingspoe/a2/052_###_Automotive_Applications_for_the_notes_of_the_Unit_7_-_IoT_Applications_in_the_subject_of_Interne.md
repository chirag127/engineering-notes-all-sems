### Automotive Applications for the notes of the Unit 7 - IoT Applications in the subject of Internet of Things

- Connected vehicles: Vehicles are being equipped with IoT devices and sensors which collect data about the vehicle's health, location, nearby vehicles, traffic conditions, etc. and send it to the cloud. This data can be used to provide various services like emergency alerts, preventive maintenance, traffic monitoring, etc.
- Intelligent transportation systems: The data collected from connected vehicles and IoT sensors on roads can be used to monitor traffic conditions and optimize traffic flow. This can reduce congestion and provide users with accurate ETAs and rerouting options.
- Smart parking: IoT sensors can be used to detect available parking spots and send the data to the cloud. Drivers can use mobile apps to find available parking spots quickly reducing time and frustration.
- Usage-based insurance: Insurance companies can use the data from IoT devices in vehicles to determine premiums based on actual vehicle usage and driving behavior. Safe drivers and those who drive less can get lower premiums.

Advantages:
- Increased safety, security and convenience.
- Better traffic management and reduced congestion.
- Preventive maintenance and lowered costs.
- Personalized services and insurance.

Disadvantages:
- Privacy concerns with large amount of data being collected and shared.
- Possibility of data breaches and hacking.
- Cost of implementing IoT devices and infrastructure.

Examples: Connected cars by Tesla, Cadillac, etc. Intelligent transportation systems by Siemens, Huawei, etc. Smart parking solutions by Streetline, ParkMe, etc.

Applications: The automotive industry, transportation authorities, insurance companies, etc. can leverage IoT to provide innovative services to customers and improve operations.

 Mnemonics:
CVS: Connected Vehicles, Smart parking, Usage-based insurance
ITI: Intelligent Transportation Systems
