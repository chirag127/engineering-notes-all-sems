### Smart cards for the notes of the Unit 7 - IoT Applications in the subject of Internet of Things

- Smart cards are credit card sized cards with embedded integrated circuits that can process data. They are used for authentication, identification, data storage and application processing.
- In IoT, smart cards are used to securely store cryptographic keys and credentials for device authentication and access control. They provide an additional layer of security as the keys are stored in a tamper resistant hardware.
- Some advantages of using smart cards in IoT:
   - Secure storage of keys and credentials
   - Tamper resistance prevents unauthorized access to credentials
   - Flexible and can be used for multiple purposes like authentication, payment, etc.
   - Portable and easy to use
- However, some disadvantages are:
   - Requires a smart card reader to access the data which adds to the cost
   - Limited storage capacity
   - Reliability depends on the quality of the smart card which can vary between manufacturers
- Some applications of smart cards in IoT:
   - Authenticating IoT devices to the network
   - Enabling secure payments and transactions
   - Storing user profiles and permissions
   - Enabling access control to devices and data
- Overall, smart cards provide an secure element for key management and access control in IoT deployments though they have some limitations with storage capacity and reliance on card quality. They can be used in combination with other security measures for enhanced protection of IoT systems.
