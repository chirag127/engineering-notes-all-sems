### Ease of designing and affordability for the notes of the Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT) in the subject of Internet of Things

- IoT systems are easy to design and implement as they use existing internet infrastructure and protocols. This makes them affordable to set up as minimal additional infrastructure is required.
- IoT devices are inexpensive to procure as they use inexpensive sensors and processors. This makes IoT solutions affordable to implement for applications at a large scale.
- Open source hardware and software platforms are available for IoT which cut down the cost of development and implementation. This increases the affordability of IoT solutions.
- Cloud platforms provide inexpensive storage and computing resources for IoT applications. This reduces the cost of deploying and scaling IoT solutions.
- DIY kits are available for learning and building IoT projects. This makes it easy and affordable for students and hobbyists to learn and experiment with IoT.
