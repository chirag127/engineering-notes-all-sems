## Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT)

The Internet of Things (IoT) refers to the interconnection of everyday physical objects and appliances via the internet. These interconnected devices are equipped with sensors, software, and networking capabilities that allow them to send and receive data. The IoT allows devices to be sensed and controlled remotely across existing network infrastructure, creating opportunities for integration of the physical world into computer-based systems, resulting in improved efficiency, accuracy and economic benefit.

Some key characteristics of the IoT:

- Connected: IoT devices are interconnected via the internet
- Smart: IoT devices have sensing, data exchange, decision making and acting capabilities that make them "smart"
- Ubiquitous: IoT devices are embedded in various physical objects and there is increasing convergence of the digital and physical worlds
- Data-driven: The power of the IoT lies in the data generated by sensors and exchanged between connected devices

Examples of IoT applications:

- Smart homes: IoT devices can monitor and control things like lights, temperature, appliances, security systems, etc.
- Wearable devices: Devices like fitness trackers and smart watches track health metrics and other variables.
- Smart cities: IoT enables applications like smart parking, traffic management, environmental monitoring, etc. in cities.
- Industrial internet: IoT enables automation, predictive maintenance and optimization in manufacturing and other industrial contexts.
- Connected vehicles: Cars and vehicles are being equipped with internet connectivity and sensors to enable applications like assisted driving and fleet management.

Advantages of IoT:

- Increased efficiency and automation
- Better data-driven decisions and predictions
- Improved customer experiences
- Growth of smart cities and smart homes

Disadvantages of IoT:

- Security and privacy concerns due to large amounts of data generated and exchanged
- Interoperability challenges as many companies develop their own incompatible IoT platforms and technologies
- Expense of deployment and maintenance of IoT solutions
- Reliance on network infrastructure and connectivity
