### Designing of smart street lights in smart city for the notes of the Unit 7 - IoT Applications in the subject of Internet of Things

- Smart street lights are IoT enabled street lights that can automatically adjust their brightness based on traffic and weather conditions.
- They have motion sensors and light sensors to detect vehicle and pedestrian movements on the road as well as ambient light conditions.
- Based on the sensor data, the smart street lights can dynamically adjust their brightness. For e.g. full brightness when traffic is heavy, dim light when no traffic and turn off when no motion is detected to save energy.
- The smart street lights can be connected to a central system to monitor their working, detect any faults and control the lights.
- The key components of a smart street light system are:

1. Smart street light - having LED bulb, motion sensor, light sensor and wireless communication module.
2. Gateway - to connect the smart street lights and centralize the data.
3. Cloud platform - to monitor, control and manage the smart street light system.

- Advantages: Energy efficient, smart management, predictive maintenance, cost savings.
- Disadvantages: Initial high costs, security and hacking risks as it's an IoT system.
- Applications: Smart cities, roads, parking areas, etc.
- To remember: Think of smart street lights as IoT devices on roads that can automatically adjust brightness based on traffic and light conditions to save energy.
