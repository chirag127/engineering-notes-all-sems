### Tablets for the notes of the Unit 7 - IoT Applications in the subject of Internet of Things

- Smart Homes: IoT devices like sensors, smart switches, smart thermostats, etc. are used to automate homes. They can monitor parameters like temperature, occupancy, energy usage, etc. and control connected devices accordingly. For e.g. a smart thermostat can automatically adjust temperature based on occupancy and preferences.
- Smart Cities: IoT sensors and devices are used for applications like smart lighting, smart parking, water management, waste management, etc. in cities. They help in monitoring and optimizing city infrastructure and services. For e.g. sensors can be used to monitor availability of parking spots and guide drivers to available spots.
- Smart Agriculture: IoT sensors and devices are used to monitor soil conditions, crop conditions, livestock conditions, etc. in agriculture. They help in precision agriculture by tracking parameters to ensure optimal crop growth and livestock health. For e.g. soil moisture sensors can help in efficient irrigation and nutrient level sensors can help in providing required fertilizers.
- Smart Logistics: IoT devices can be used to track location and conditions of goods and equipment in logistics. They enable applications like asset tracking, fleet management, cold chain monitoring, etc. which help in efficient monitoring and management of logistics. For e.g. GPS devices can track location of vehicles and temperature sensors can monitor cold storage conditions.
- Smart Industries: IoT sensors and devices are used for applications like predictive maintenance, process optimization, workplace safety, etc. in industries. They help in monitoring machines, detecting anomalies, and optimizing processes to reduce costs and downtime. For e.g. vibration sensors can detect anomalous vibrations in machines to enable predictive maintenance.
