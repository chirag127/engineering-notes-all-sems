### Communication technologies for the notes of the Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT) in the subject of Internet of Things

The communication technologies used in Internet of Things (IoT) are:

1. Wi-Fi:
- Wi-Fi is a wireless networking technology that allows devices to communicate over wireless local area networks (WLANs)
- Wi-Fi enables IoT devices to connect to the Internet and communicate with other devices
- Pros: Widely used, easy to set up. Cons: Limited range, security issues
- Examples: Wi-Fi enabled smart home devices, Wi-Fi routers

2. Bluetooth:
- Bluetooth is a wireless technology standard for exchanging data over short distances (using UHF radio waves) from fixed and mobile devices, and building personal area networks (PANs)
- Bluetooth is used to create wireless connection between IoT devices and allow them to communicate with each other
- Pros: Low power, low cost. Cons: Limited range, slow speed
- Examples: Bluetooth headphones, fitness trackers, Bluetooth enabled smart home devices

3. NFC:
- NFC stands for Near Field Communication. It is a set of short-range wireless technologies, typically requiring a distance of 4cm or less to establish a connection
- NFC allows two devices brought close together to establish radio communication and transfer data
- Pros: Very short range, secure. Cons: Limited use cases
- Examples: Contactless payments, data sharing between two NFC enabled devices

4. Cellular:
- Cellular networks like 3G and 4G LTE provide wireless data connections that can be used by IoT devices to connect to the Internet
- IoT devices can be equipped with cellular modems to access the cellular network and transmit data
- Pros: Wide coverage, fast speed. Cons: Higher power requirements, higher cost
- Examples: Connected cars, smart meters, asset tracking devices
