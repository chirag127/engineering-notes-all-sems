### E-health for the notes of the Unit 7 - IoT Applications in the subject of Internet of Things

- E-health refers to the utilization of information and communication technologies (ICT) for health services and information.
- IoT enables advanced e-health systems with smart sensors, wearable devices and remote monitoring.
- Some applications of IoT in e-health:
-- Remote monitoring of patients: IoT devices like wearable sensors can constantly monitor patients and transmit data to doctors enabling remote monitoring. This reduces hospital visits and enables early detection of complications.
-- Smart pills: Pills with embedded sensors can be taken by patients to monitor adherence to medication. The sensors send data to smartphones/doctors informing if the pill was taken, dosage, etc. helping in monitoring medication schedules.
-- Smart beds: Beds with sensors can detect patient movement, heart rate, respiration, etc. and alert doctors to changes in health conditions.
-- Connected ambulances: Ambulances can be equipped with IoT devices to send patient health data to hospitals enabling doctors to start treatment plans even before the patient arrives. This critical data can save lives in emergency cases.

Advantages:
- Continuous remote monitoring enabling early detection of health issues.
- Reduction in hospital visits and costs.
- Accurate data to enable personalized health plans.

Disadvantages:
- Risk of data breaches compromising private patient data.
- Inaccurate data from sensors could lead to wrong diagnosis or treatment.
- Reliance on technology could reduce human contact impacting patient experience.
