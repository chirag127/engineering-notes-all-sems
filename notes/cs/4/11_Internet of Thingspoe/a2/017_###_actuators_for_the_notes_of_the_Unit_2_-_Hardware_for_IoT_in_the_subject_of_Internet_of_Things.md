### Actuators for IoT Hardware

Actuators are components that convert electrical signals into mechanical movements. They are used to perform physical actions based on the data received from sensors. Some common actuators used in IoT devices are:

- Motors: Motors are used to produce rotational or linear motion. They are used to control devices such as fans, pumps, etc. The most common types are DC motors and servomotors.
- Solenoids: Solenoids are electromagnets that convert electrical energy into linear motion. They are used to control devices such as locks, valves, etc. Based on the application, solenoids can be AC or DC powered.
- Relays: Relays are electro-mechanical switches that are used to control high voltage and current devices using low voltage signals. They allow IoT devices to control appliances in homes and industries.
- Piezoelectric actuators: Piezoelectric actuators produce motion by applying voltage to piezoelectric materials that change their shape based on the applied electric field. They are used in precise motion control applications such as robotic arms, printers, etc.

Some mnemonics or learning tricks for remembering actuators are:

- Motors are for motion
- Solenoids push and pull
- Relays are for high power control
- Piezos are for precision

The choice of actuators depends on the application requirements such as type of motion, force, precision, power requirements, etc. Actuators enable IoT devices to interact with the physical world and control appliances and machines.
