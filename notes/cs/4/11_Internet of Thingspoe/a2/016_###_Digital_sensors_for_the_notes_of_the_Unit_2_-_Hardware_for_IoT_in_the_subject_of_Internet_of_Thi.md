### Digital sensors for the notes of the Unit 2 - Hardware for IoT

- Digital sensors produce a digital output in the form of 0s and 1s which can be easily interpreted by microcontrollers and microprocessors. Some of the commonly used digital sensors are:

- Touch sensors: They detect touch or proximity and output a digital signal. They are used in touchscreen displays, proximity sensors, etc.
- Limit switches: They output a digital signal depending on the position of an object. They are used for position sensing in motors, valves, etc.
- Reed switches: They contain two ferromagnetic contacts that are either closed or open based on the presence of an external magnetic field. They are used in magnetic sensing applications.

Advantages:

- Digital output is easy to interface with digital components.
- Less sensitive to noise.

Disadvantages:

- Limited precision. The output is only 0s and 1s so the sensor can only sense discrete states. Continuous values cannot be measured directly.

**Mnemonics:**

- Think of digital sensors as switches (on/off)
- Touch, limit, reed - three common types of digital sensors

**Examples and applications:**

- Touchscreen displays use touch sensors to sense touch input.
- Proximity sensors use touch sensors to detect nearby objects.
- Position sensing in motors and valves use limit switches.
- Security systems use reed switches to detect open/close states of doors and windows.
