### Architectural view for the notes of the Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT) in the subject of Internet of Things

The architectural view of IoT can be represented in the following layers:

1. Perception layer: This layer consists of sensors and actuators which convert physical parameters into electrical signals and vice-versa. For example, temperature sensors, motion sensors, etc.

2. Network layer: This layer consists of communication networks like Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Zigbee, etc. which are used to connect devices to the internet.

3. Middle layer: This layer consists of gateways that connect the devices in the perception layer to the internet. The gateways have processing capabilities and help in translating between different communication technologies.

4. Application layer: This layer consists of software applications that make use of the data collected from sensors to provide various services to the end-users. For example, smart home applications.

**Mnemonics:**

Perception - Sensors
Network - Wi-Fi
Middle - Gateway
Application - Services

**Advantages:** IoT enables automation, remote monitoring, and smart applications that improve efficiency and quality of life.

**Disadvantages:** Security and privacy concerns, interoperability issues, and high costs.

**Applications:** Smart homes, smart cities, industrial automation, healthcare, transportation, etc.
