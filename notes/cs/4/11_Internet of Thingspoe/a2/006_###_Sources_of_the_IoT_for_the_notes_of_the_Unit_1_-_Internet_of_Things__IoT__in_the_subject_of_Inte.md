### Sources of the IoT

The sources of the Internet of Things (IoT) are the devices and everyday objects that are being connected to the internet and equipped with sensors, processors, and networking capabilities. The major sources of the IoT are:

1. Smart Devices: Smart devices like smartphones, smart watches, smart speakers, etc. have sensors, networking, and computing capabilities which can connect to the internet and exchange data. They are one of the major sources of the IoT as many IoT applications are built on top of smart devices.

2. Smart Home Devices: Devices like smart lights, smart locks, smart thermostats, etc. used in smart homes have connectivity and can be monitored and controlled over the internet. They contribute to the growth of the IoT by connecting more and more home appliances and devices to the internet.

3. Industrial Machines: Industrial machines and equipment are being equipped with sensors and networking capabilities to monitor and optimize their performance. This industrial IoT connects various equipment and systems in industries to the internet to enable automation and data exchange.

4. Vehicles: Modern vehicles have numerous sensors and computing systems which can connect to the internet. This allows vehicular connectivity and communication forming an important part of the IoT. The data from vehicles can be used for various applications like traffic management, emergency handling, etc.

5. Wearable Devices: Wearable devices like fitness trackers, smart watches, etc. have sensors to track health parameters and activities. They connect to the internet and exchange data contributing to the IoT and enabling applications like fitness tracking, health monitoring, etc.

Thus, the major sources of the Internet of Things are smart devices, smart home devices, industrial machines, vehicles, and wearable devices which are being connected to the internet and equipped with networking, computing, and sensing capabilities. The data exchanged between these devices enable IoT applications and services.
