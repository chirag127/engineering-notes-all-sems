## Unit 4 - Network & Communication aspects in IoT

### Overview
- IoT devices are physically distributed and connected over a network, hence network connectivity and communication are crucial aspects of IoT.
- The IoT ecosystem consists of sensors, actuators, gateways, and cloud servers that are interconnected through wireless or wired networks.
- Choosing the right networking technology and communication protocols is important based on application requirements such as range, speed, power, cost, etc.

### Network Technologies
- Wireless networks: Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Zigbee, LoRaWAN, NB-IoT, 5G
- Wired networks: Ethernet
- Selection depends on range, speed, power, cost requirements

**Wi-Fi**: Wide range, high speed, more power; suitable for home and office IoT.
**Bluetooth**: Short range, low power; suitable for personal area networks and devices like headphones, speakers, etc.
**Zigbee**: Low power, low data rate, low cost; suitable for home automation and industrial IoT.
**LoRaWAN**: Long range, low power; suitable for smart cities and agriculture IoT.
**NB-IoT, 5G**: High speed and capacity, low latency; suitable for massive IoT and critical applications.

### Communication Protocols
- Rules for exchange of data between IoT devices and servers
- Examples: HTTP, MQTT, CoAP, AMQP, WebSockets
- Selection depends on application requirements like message types, sync/async, scalability, etc.

**HTTP**: For accessing web services over the Internet
**MQTT**: Lightweight pub/sub protocol suitable for IoT
**CoAP**: Web transfer protocol for constrained devices/networks
**AMQP**: Messaging protocol for ensuring reliable and secure message exchange
**WebSockets**: For full-duplex, bidirectional communication over a single TCP connection
