## Unit 2 - Hardware for IoT

1.  Microcontrollers: Microcontrollers are integrated circuits that contain a processor core, memory, and programmable input/output peripherals. They are used to control devices and systems in IoT projects. Some popular microcontrollers used in IoT projects are:
    -   Arduino boards like Arduino Uno, Arduino Mega, etc.
    -   Raspberry Pi
    -   ESP8266 and ESP32
2.  Sensors: Sensors are devices that detect and respond to some type of input from the physical environment. They are used to sense the parameters of the surrounding and provide corresponding electrical output signals. Some commonly used sensors in IoT projects are:
    -   Temperature sensors like DHT11, DHT22, LM35, etc.
    -   Humidity sensors like DHT11, DHT22
    -   Light sensors like LDR, photoresistors
    -   PIR sensors to detect motion
    -   Ultrasonic sensors to measure distance
    -   Touch sensors
    -   Accelerometer and gyroscope to sense motion and orientation
3.  Actuators: Actuators are devices that convert an electrical signal into physical movement. They are used to perform actions in response to the input received from sensors. Some common actuators used in IoT projects are:
    -   Relays to control high voltage devices
    -   Motors like servo motors and stepper motors
    -   LCD and LED displays
    -   Buzzers
4.  Communication Protocols: Communication protocols are required to establish communication between various components of an IoT system. Some commonly used protocols are:
    -   Wi-Fi and Ethernet for internet connectivity
    -   MQTT and HTTP for device-to-device and device-to-server communication
    -   I2C and SPI for short distance communication between sensors, microcontrollers, and actuators
