### Sensor deployment & Node discovery for the notes of the Unit 4 - Network & Communication aspects in IoT in the subject of Internet of Things

**Sensor deployment:**

- Sensors are deployed in the physical environment to sense and collect data.
- Sensors can be classified into pressure sensors, temperature sensors, humidity sensors, motion sensors, image sensors, etc. based on the physical quantity they sense.
- Sensors are connected to the IoT devices like microcontrollers, routers, gateways, etc. to transmit the collected data.
- The placement of the sensors is very crucial for accurate data collection. The sensors should be placed in the appropriate location to sense the desired physical quantity.
- For example, a temperature sensor should be placed away from heat sources to get the accurate temperature of the surroundings.

**Node discovery:**

- In IoT, the devices like sensors, microcontrollers, routers, etc. are referred to as nodes.
- For communication between the nodes, the nodes should be able to discover each other.
- The process of a node finding the availability and addresses of other nodes on the network is called node discovery.
- There are various node discovery protocols like mDNS, DNS-SD, etc. which can be used for node discovery in IoT.
- The nodes can broadcast their presence to other nodes or can use a directory service to register and discover other nodes.
- Node discovery is a crucial process for establishing end-to-end connectivity between the nodes in an IoT system.

**Mnemonics:**

- For sensor deployment: Place the 'right' sensor in the 'right' place
- For node discovery: Nodes 'shout' or 'register' to 'find' others

**Advantages:** Accurate sensing and efficient communication between the nodes.
**Disadvantages:** Additional overhead for sensor placement and discovery process.
**Applications:** Smart homes, smart cities, industrial automation, etc.
