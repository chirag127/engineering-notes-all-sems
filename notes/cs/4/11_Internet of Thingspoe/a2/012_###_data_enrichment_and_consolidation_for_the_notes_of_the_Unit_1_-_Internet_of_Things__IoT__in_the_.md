### Data Enrichment and Consolidation for Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT)

#### Mnemonics and Learning Tricks:
- Remember **AAA** for data in IoT: **A**cquire, **A**nalyze, **A**ct
- Think of IoT devices as data collectors and data consolidators. They acquire data, analyze patterns and take actions.

#### Points:
1. IoT devices acquire data from sensors and inputs. This raw data needs to be enriched for analysis by adding contextual information, location tags, timestamps, etc.
2. The enriched data is then consolidated from many IoT devices and sensors. This consolidated data provides information about patterns, trends and insights.
3. The consolidated data is analyzed to derive useful information and knowledge which can enable automation, optimization and new business models.
4. Data security and privacy is important in IoT as personal and sensitive data is processed and analyzed. Strict controls and precautions must be taken to keep the data safe and secure.

#### Examples:
- Sensors in a factory acquire temperature and humidity data. This data is enriched with location and time stamps. The consolidated data from all sensors can help analyze optimal conditions and enable automatic controls.
- Wearable devices acquire health parameters of individuals. The data is enriched with personal details and consolidated to analyze health trends and optimize medical advice and care.

#### Advantages:
- Deeper insights and patterns can be derived from enriched and consolidated data which enables better decision making and automation.
- Wider coverage is achieved by consolidating data from multiple IoT devices and locations.

#### Disadvantages:
- Enrichment and consolidation of huge amounts of IoT data can be complex, time-consuming and resource intensive.
- Data security and privacy threats are increased due to accumulation and processing of personal and sensitive data from IoT devices.
