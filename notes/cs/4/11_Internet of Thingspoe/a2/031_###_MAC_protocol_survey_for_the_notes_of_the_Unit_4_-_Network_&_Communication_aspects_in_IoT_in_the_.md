### MAC protocol survey for the notes of the Unit 4 - Network & Communication aspects in IoT in the subject of Internet of Things

- **CSMA/CD (Collision Detection)**: Devices detect collisions and wait random time before retransmitting. Used in Ethernet.
- **CSMA/CA (Collision Avoidance)**: Devices use random backoff to avoid collisions. Used in Wi-Fi (802.11).
- **Token Passing**: A token is passed between devices. Only the device holding the token can transmit. Used in Token Ring and FDDI networks.
- **TDMA (Time Division Multiple Access)**: Each device is allocated a different time slot within the channel. Used in 2G digital cellular systems.
- **FDMA (Frequency Division Multiple Access)**: Each device is allocated a different frequency channel. Used in 1G analog cellular systems.
- **CDMA (Code Division Multiple Access)**: Devices are allocated different coded sequences to modulate their signals. Collision avoidance is inherent. Used in 3G and 4G digital cellular systems.
