### Wireless Medium access issues for the notes of the Unit 4 - Network & Communication aspects in IoT in the subject of Internet of Things

The following are the major wireless medium access issues in IoT:

1. Bandwidth constraints: The wireless medium has limited bandwidth. As the number of IoT devices increase, it leads to congestion and bandwidth constraints. This can lead to increased latency, packet loss, and reduced throughput.
2. Interference: There can be interference from other wireless devices and networks operating in the same frequency band. This can corrupt the data being transmitted and lead to retransmissions and reduced performance.
3. Hidden terminal problem: When two nodes are out of range of each other but can affect a third node, it leads to the hidden terminal problem. This can lead to collisions and packet loss.
4. Exposed node problem: When a node is prevented from transmitting even when the channel is idle, it leads to the exposed node problem. This leads to underutilization of the channel and reduced performance.
5. Multipath fading: The transmitted signals can take multiple paths to reach the receiver in a wireless medium. This can lead to multipath fading where the signals destructively interfere and lead to loss of data.

To address these issues, the following techniques can be used:

- Channel hopping: Switching between different frequency channels to avoid interference and congestion.
- Transmission power control: Adjusting the transmission power to the minimum required level. This reduces interference with other nodes while conserving energy.
- Collision avoidance: Using techniques like CSMA/CA to avoid collisions and packet loss.
- Directional antennas: Using directional antennas to focus the transmission in a particular direction. This reduces interference and the hidden terminal problem.
- Retransmissions: Using retransmission mechanisms to recover from packet loss due to errors or collisions. However, this leads to increased latency and reduced throughput.
