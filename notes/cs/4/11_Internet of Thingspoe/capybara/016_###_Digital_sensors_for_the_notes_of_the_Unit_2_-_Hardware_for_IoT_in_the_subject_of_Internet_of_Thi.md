### Digital Sensors for the Notes of the Unit 2 - Hardware for IoT in the Subject of Internet of Things

Digital sensors are devices that are used to detect and measure physical phenomena, such as temperature, humidity, light, and sound, and convert them into digital signals that can be processed by a computer or microcontroller. In the context of the Internet of Things (IoT), digital sensors play a crucial role in collecting and transmitting data from the physical world to the digital world.

Here are some important digital sensors that you should know about for the Unit 2 - Hardware for IoT in the subject of Internet of Things:

1. Temperature Sensors: These sensors are used to measure the temperature of the environment and convert it into a digital signal that can be read by a microcontroller. Some common types of temperature sensors include thermistors, thermocouples, and digital temperature sensors. A mnemonic to remember the types of temperature sensors could be "Three T's - Thermistors, Thermocouples, and Temperature Sensors".

2. Humidity Sensors: These sensors are used to measure the amount of moisture in the air and convert it into a digital signal. They are commonly used in applications such as weather monitoring, HVAC systems, and agriculture. One mnemonic to remember the use of humidity sensors could be "Happier Humans with Humidity Sensors".

3. Light Sensors: Also known as photoelectric sensors, these sensors are used to measure the intensity of light in an environment. They are commonly used in applications such as security systems, automatic lighting controls, and photography. A mnemonic to remember the uses of light sensors could be "Lighting up security, lighting up the world - with Light Sensors".

4. Proximity Sensors: These sensors are used to detect the presence of objects in close proximity to the sensor. They are commonly used in applications such as touchless faucets, automatic doors, and mobile devices. A mnemonic to remember the uses of proximity sensors could be "Proximity sensors - Bringing touchless technology to your fingertips".

5. Accelerometers: These sensors are used to measure acceleration, tilt, and vibration. They are commonly used in applications such as gaming controllers, fitness trackers, and navigation systems. A mnemonic to remember the uses of accelerometers could be "Accelerometers - Tracking your movements, one step at a time".

Overall, digital sensors are an essential component of IoT hardware and play a crucial role in collecting and transmitting data from the physical world to the digital world. Understanding the different types of digital sensors and their uses can help in designing and developing effective IoT solutions.
