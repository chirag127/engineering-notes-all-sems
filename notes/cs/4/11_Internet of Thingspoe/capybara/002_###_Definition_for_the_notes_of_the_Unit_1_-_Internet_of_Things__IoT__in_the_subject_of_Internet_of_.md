### Definition for the notes of the Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT) in the subject of Internet of Things

The Internet of Things (IoT) is a system of connected physical objects or devices that are embedded with sensors, software, and network connectivity, enabling them to collect and exchange data with each other and with other Internet-enabled devices.

Here are some key points to remember about IoT:

- IoT devices can range from simple sensors to complex machines, and can be found in a variety of settings such as homes, offices, factories, and cities.

- The data collected by IoT devices can be used for a variety of purposes, including monitoring and controlling systems, improving efficiency, and providing insights for decision-making.

- IoT systems often rely on cloud computing and data analytics to process and analyze the large amounts of data generated by the devices.

- Security and privacy are important considerations when designing and implementing IoT systems, as the devices can potentially collect sensitive information and be vulnerable to hacking and cyberattacks.

Mnemonics and learning tricks:

- Remember the acronym "IoT" by associating it with "Internet of Things".
- Think about how IoT devices are like "smart" objects that can communicate with each other and with us.
- Visualize a network of connected devices, with data flowing between them like a web.
