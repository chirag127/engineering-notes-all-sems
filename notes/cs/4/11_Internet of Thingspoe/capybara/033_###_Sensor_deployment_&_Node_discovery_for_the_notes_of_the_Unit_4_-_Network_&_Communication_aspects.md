### Sensor Deployment & Node Discovery

In IoT, sensor deployment and node discovery plays a crucial role in establishing communication between devices. Sensing devices are deployed to collect data and transmit it to other devices for further processing. Here are some key points to keep in mind regarding sensor deployment and node discovery:

1. Sensor Deployment
- Sensors are deployed in strategic locations to collect data and transmit it to other devices.
- The choice of sensor type depends on the nature of the data to be collected.
- Sensor deployment should be done in a way that ensures maximum coverage with minimum redundancy.
- The deployment should also consider factors like power consumption, communication range, and maintenance requirements.

2. Node Discovery
- Node discovery is the process of identifying and establishing communication with other nodes in the network.
- This is done through a process called address resolution, where the address of the node is resolved to its physical location.
- Various protocols like Address Resolution Protocol (ARP) and Neighbor Discovery Protocol (NDP) are used for node discovery.
- In wireless networks, nodes may use the Radio Frequency Identification (RFID) technology for discovery.

Mnemonics and Learning Tricks:
- For sensor deployment, remember the acronym SPAM (Strategic Placement for Achieving Maximum coverage).
- For node discovery, remember the acronym ADD (Address Discovery and Detection).

Overall, sensor deployment and node discovery are critical aspects of IoT network communication. Proper planning and implementation of these processes can lead to more efficient and effective communication between devices, ultimately improving the overall functionality of the IoT system.
