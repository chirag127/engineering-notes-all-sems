### Participatory Sensing Technology for the Notes of Unit 2 - Hardware for IoT in the Subject of Internet of Things

Participatory Sensing Technology is a type of technology that enables people to collect data about their environment using their smartphones and other mobile devices. It is a type of crowdsourcing that involves the participation of a large number of people to collect data for a specific purpose.

#### How Participatory Sensing Technology Works

Participatory Sensing Technology works by using the sensors in mobile devices to collect data about the environment. The data collected can include information about the temperature, humidity, air quality, noise level, and other environmental factors.

The data collected is then sent to a server where it is analyzed and processed. The processed data is then used to create maps and other visualizations that can be used to understand and address environmental issues.

#### Advantages of Participatory Sensing Technology

- It is a cost-effective way to collect data about the environment.
- It can be used to collect data in areas that are difficult to access.
- It allows for real-time monitoring of environmental conditions.
- It can be used to engage citizens in environmental monitoring and decision-making.

#### Applications of Participatory Sensing Technology

- Air quality monitoring
- Noise pollution monitoring
- Traffic monitoring
- Water quality monitoring
- Disaster management

#### Learning Trick

One way to remember the concept of Participatory Sensing Technology is to think of it as a way for people to participate in sensing their environment. Another way is to remember the acronym PST, which stands for Participatory Sensing Technology.
