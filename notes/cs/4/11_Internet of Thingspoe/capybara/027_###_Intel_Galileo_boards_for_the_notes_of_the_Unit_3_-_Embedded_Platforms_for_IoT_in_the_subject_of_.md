### Intel Galileo boards for the notes of the Unit 3 - Embedded Platforms for IoT in the subject of Internet of Things

The Intel Galileo board is an embedded platform designed for the Internet of Things (IoT) applications. It is a single-board computer that is based on the Intel Quark microcontroller. It is widely used in various IoT applications, such as home automation, robotics, and wearable devices.

Here are some important features and benefits of the Intel Galileo board:

- The board has an Intel Quark SoC X1000 microcontroller, which is a 32-bit, single-core, single-thread, Pentium-class processor, with a clock speed of up to 400 MHz.
- It also has 512 KB of embedded SRAM and 8 MB of NOR flash memory, which can be used to store the firmware and application code.
- The board has various interfaces, such as Ethernet, USB, UART, SPI, I2C, and GPIO, which can be used to connect to various sensors, actuators, and other devices.
- The board is compatible with various programming languages, such as C, C++, Python, and JavaScript, which makes it easy to develop applications.
- It also has a built-in Arduino-compatible shield connector, which allows it to be used with various Arduino shields and libraries.
- The board supports various operating systems, such as Linux, Windows, and RTOS, which provides flexibility in choosing the right OS for the application.
- The Galileo board is very cost-effective, which makes it a popular choice for IoT applications.

Mnemonics and Learning Tricks:

- Mnemonic: "GALI-LEO" - This can be used to remember the name of the board. "GALI" stands for "Galileo," and "LEO" stands for "Low Energy Object." This can help to remember that the board is designed for low-power IoT applications.
- Learning Trick: "Think of it as a mini-computer" - This can help to remember that the Galileo board is a single-board computer that can be used for various IoT applications.
