## Unit 2 - Hardware for IoT

The hardware used for IoT devices is an essential aspect of the development of IoT systems. In this unit, we will discuss the hardware components used in the development of IoT devices, their characteristics, and their functionality. 

Here are some important points that you must know about the hardware used in IoT devices:

1. **Microcontroller:** A microcontroller is a small computer on a single integrated circuit chip. It contains a CPU, memory, and input/output peripherals. It is the heart of an IoT device and controls its functions.

2. **Sensors:** Sensors are used to capture data from the environment. They convert physical parameters, such as temperature, humidity, or motion, into electrical signals that can be processed by the microcontroller.

3. **Actuators:** Actuators are used to perform actions based on the data received from sensors. For example, a motor can be used to adjust the temperature of a room based on the temperature data received from a sensor.

4. **Connectivity:** IoT devices need to be connected to other devices or networks to exchange data. Connectivity options include Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, cellular, and RFID.

5. **Power source:** IoT devices can be powered by batteries, solar panels, or by using energy harvesting techniques.

Mnemonics and Learning Tricks:

1. Remember the acronym "SMACP" to recall the five essential hardware components used in IoT devices - Sensors, Microcontroller, Actuators, Connectivity, and Power source.

2. Visualize the hardware components of an IoT device as a flowchart, with the microcontroller at the center, and sensors, actuators, connectivity, and power source connected to it in a circular pattern.

In conclusion, understanding the hardware components used in IoT devices is crucial for building efficient and effective IoT systems. Remembering the key hardware components and their functions can be made easier with the use of mnemonics and visual aids.
