### Tablets for the Notes of the Unit 7 - IoT Applications in the Subject of Internet of Things

Tablets have become an indispensable tool for students and professionals alike. They can be an excellent tool to take notes, especially during lectures or meetings. In the context of the Internet of Things (IoT), tablets can be used as a way to access, collect and analyze data from connected devices.

Here are some tips and tricks to make the most out of your tablet for taking notes in the Unit 7 - IoT Applications of the Internet of Things:

1. Get a stylus: A stylus can make a huge difference when taking notes on a tablet. It can make your handwriting look more natural and legible, and it can also make it easier to draw diagrams or sketches.

2. Use note-taking apps: There are many note-taking apps available for tablets, such as Evernote, OneNote, and Google Keep. These apps can help you organize your notes by subject and tag them for easy searching.

3. Use mnemonics: Mnemonics can be helpful to remember complex concepts. For example, the acronym "SMART" can be used to remember the characteristics of a good IoT application: Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, and Time-bound.

4. Draw diagrams: Diagrams can be a powerful way to visualize concepts and relationships. Tablets can be used to draw diagrams quickly and easily. For example, a flowchart can be used to illustrate the steps of an IoT application.

5. Use tables: Tables can be useful to organize data and compare different options. Tablets can be used to create tables on the fly. For example, a table can be used to compare different IoT platforms based on their features and pricing.

Overall, tablets can be a great tool for taking notes in the Unit 7 - IoT Applications of the Internet of Things. By using a stylus, note-taking apps, mnemonics, diagrams, and tables, you can create organized and effective notes that will help you study and prepare for exams.
