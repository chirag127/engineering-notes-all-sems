### Overview of IOT supported Hardware platforms such as Arduino for the notes of the Unit 3 - Embedded Platforms for IoT in the subject of Internet of Things

IOT devices are becoming increasingly popular in today's world. They are being used in various industries such as healthcare, manufacturing, transportation, and agriculture. To develop IOT devices, hardware platforms are required. Arduino is one such hardware platform that is widely used in the development of IOT devices.

Here are the details of the overview of IOT supported Hardware platforms such as Arduino for the notes of the Unit 3 - Embedded Platforms for IoT in the subject of Internet of Things:

1. Hardware platforms for IOT:

    1.1. Hardware platforms are essential for the development of IOT devices.

    1.2. IOT devices require hardware platforms that are small, affordable, and easy to use.

    1.3. Examples of IOT hardware platforms include Arduino, Raspberry Pi, BeagleBone, and Intel Edison.

2. What is Arduino?

    2.1. Arduino is an open-source hardware platform that is used in the development of IOT devices.

    2.2. It consists of a microcontroller, programming environment, and various input/output pins.

    2.3. Arduino boards come in various sizes and shapes, making them versatile and suitable for various applications.

3. Advantages of using Arduino for IOT:

    3.1. Easy to use: Arduino is easy to use, and even beginners can quickly learn how to program it.

    3.2. Open-source: Arduino is open-source, meaning that its hardware and software designs are freely available, making it easy to modify and customize.

    3.3. Affordable: Arduino boards are affordable and readily available, making them an excellent choice for hobbyists and developers working on a budget.

    3.4. Large community: Arduino has a large community of developers who share their knowledge, ideas, and projects, making it easy to get help and support.

4. Disadvantages of using Arduino for IOT:

    4.1. Limited processing power: Arduino boards have limited processing power, making them unsuitable for complex applications.

    4.2. Limited memory: Arduino boards have limited memory, making it challenging to store large amounts of data.

    4.3. Limited connectivity: Arduino boards have limited connectivity options, making it challenging to connect to the internet or other devices.

5. Examples of IOT devices developed using Arduino:

    5.1. Smart home devices: Arduino is used in the development of smart home devices such as smart thermostats, smart lighting systems, and smart locks.

    5.2. Wearables: Arduino is used in the development of wearable devices such as fitness trackers and smartwatches.

    5.3. Environmental monitoring: Arduino is used in the development of environmental monitoring systems such as air quality monitors and weather stations.

6. Mnemonics and learning tricks:

    6.1. To remember the advantages of using Arduino for IOT, you can use the acronym EALA, which stands for Easy to use, Affordable, Large community, and Open-source.

    6.2. To remember the disadvantages of using Arduino for IOT, you can use the acronym LCL, which stands for Limited processing power, Limited memory, and Limited connectivity.

In conclusion, Arduino is a popular hardware platform used in the development of IOT devices. It is easy to use, affordable, and has a large community of developers. However, it has limitations in processing power, memory, and connectivity.
