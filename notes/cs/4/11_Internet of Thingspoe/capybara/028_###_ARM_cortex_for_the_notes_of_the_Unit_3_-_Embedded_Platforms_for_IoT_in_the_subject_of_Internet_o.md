#### Introduction to ARM Cortex

- ARM Cortex is a family of microcontrollers and microprocessors designed by ARM Holdings.
- These processors are based on the ARM architecture and are widely used in embedded systems and IoT devices.
- The ARM Cortex processors are known for their low power consumption, high performance, and easy-to-use architecture.
- The Cortex processors are available in various configurations, from simple microcontrollers to high-performance microprocessors.

#### Architecture of ARM Cortex

- The ARM Cortex processors have a simple and easy-to-use architecture.
- The Cortex processors are based on the RISC (Reduced Instruction Set Computing) architecture, which means that they have a small number of instructions that execute quickly.
- The Cortex processors have a 32-bit instruction set, which allows them to execute instructions quickly and efficiently.
- The Cortex processors also have a pipelined architecture, which means that they can execute multiple instructions simultaneously, further improving their performance.
