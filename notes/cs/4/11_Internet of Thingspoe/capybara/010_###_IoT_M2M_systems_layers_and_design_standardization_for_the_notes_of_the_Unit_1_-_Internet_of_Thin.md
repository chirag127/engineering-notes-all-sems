### IoT/M2M Systems Layers and Design Standardization

The Internet of Things (IoT) is a network of connected devices that can communicate with each other and share data. It involves various layers and design standardization to ensure smooth communication between devices. Here are the different layers and design standardization in IoT/M2M systems:

1. Application Layer: This layer is responsible for the communication between the end-user and the IoT device. It includes applications such as smart home automation, remote healthcare, and industrial automation. The data generated from this layer is sent to the next layer for processing.

2. Network Layer: This layer handles the communication between the IoT devices and the network. It includes protocols such as Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, and Zigbee. The network layer ensures that the data is transmitted securely and reliably.

3. Data Transport Layer: This layer is responsible for the transmission of data between the network layer and the IoT device. It includes protocols such as MQTT and CoAP. The data transport layer ensures that the data is transmitted efficiently and without errors.

4. Device Layer: This layer includes the IoT devices such as sensors, actuators, and gateways. The device layer collects data from the sensors, processes it, and sends it to the network layer for transmission.

Design Standardization: To ensure smooth communication between the different layers and devices, there are various design standardization in IoT/M2M systems. Some of them are:

1. IoT Architecture: It provides a common framework for the design and implementation of IoT systems. It includes protocols, data models, and security features.

2. IoT Security: Security is a major concern in IoT systems. There are various design standardization for IoT security, such as authentication, encryption, and access control.

3. IoT Interoperability: Interoperability refers to the ability of different IoT devices to communicate with each other. There are various design standardization for IoT interoperability, such as standard protocols and data models.

Mnemonics and Learning Tricks:

1. Remember the layers of IoT/M2M systems by using the mnemonic "ANDD" - Application, Network, Data Transport, and Device.

2. You can remember the different design standardization by using the mnemonic "ASIAN" - Architecture, Security, Interoperability, and Access Control.

In conclusion, understanding the layers and design standardization in IoT/M2M systems is crucial for the development and implementation of IoT systems. By using the above-mentioned mnemonics and learning tricks, you can easily remember and recall the different layers and design standardization for your exams.
