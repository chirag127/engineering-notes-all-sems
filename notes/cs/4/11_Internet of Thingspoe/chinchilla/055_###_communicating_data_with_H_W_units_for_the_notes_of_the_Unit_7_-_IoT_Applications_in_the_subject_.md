### Communicating Data with H/W Units for the Notes of Unit 7 - IoT Applications in the Subject of Internet of Things

In the Internet of Things (IoT), hardware devices or sensors are used to collect data from the physical world. This data needs to be communicated to other devices or systems for processing and analysis. In this unit, we will explore the various methods of communicating data with hardware (H/W) units in IoT applications.

#### 1. Serial Communication

Serial communication is a method of transmitting data one bit at a time over a single communication channel. It is commonly used in IoT applications to communicate with sensors and other hardware devices. The two common types of serial communication protocols are:

- Universal Asynchronous Receiver/Transmitter (UART): Used for point-to-point communication between two devices.

- Inter-Integrated Circuit (I2C): Used for communication between multiple devices using a shared bus.

#### 2. Wireless Communication

Wireless communication is another method of communicating data with H/W units in IoT applications. It eliminates the need for physical cables and is commonly used in applications where data needs to be transmitted over long distances. The two common types of wireless communication protocols are:

- Wi-Fi: Used for high-speed data transmission over short distances.

- Bluetooth: Used for low-power data transmission over short distances.

#### 3. MQTT Protocol

The Message Queuing Telemetry Transport (MQTT) protocol is a lightweight messaging protocol designed for IoT applications. It is used to send and receive messages between devices in a publish-subscribe model. In this model, devices can publish messages to a topic, and other devices can subscribe to that topic to receive the messages.

#### 4. CoAP Protocol

The Constrained Application Protocol (CoAP) is a lightweight protocol designed for IoT applications. It is used to easily communicate with low-power and low-memory devices. It uses the RESTful architecture and is designed to work with HTTP for seamless integration with the web.

#### Mnemonics and Learning Tricks

- To remember the two types of serial communication protocols, use the mnemonic "UI2C" where "U" stands for UART and "I2C" stands for Inter-Integrated Circuit.

- To remember the difference between Wi-Fi and Bluetooth, use the mnemonic "Wi-Fi for speed, Bluetooth for low power."
