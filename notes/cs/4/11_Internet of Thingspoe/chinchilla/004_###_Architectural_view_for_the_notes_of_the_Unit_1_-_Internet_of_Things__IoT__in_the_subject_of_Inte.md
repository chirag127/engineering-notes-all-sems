### Architectural view for the notes of the Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT) in the subject of Internet of Things

The Internet of Things (IoT) is a system of connected devices, sensors, and other objects that are able to communicate and exchange data with each other over the internet. To better understand the functioning of this system, it is important to have a clear understanding of its architectural view. 

The architectural view of IoT comprises of the following layers:

1. **Perception Layer**: This layer includes all the sensors, devices, and other objects that are used to collect data about the physical environment. This layer is responsible for capturing and transmitting data to the next layer of the architecture.

2. **Network Layer**: This layer is responsible for transmitting the data collected from the perception layer to the cloud or the central server. This layer includes different types of networks such as Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, RFID, Zigbee, and more.

3. **Middleware Layer**: This layer plays a crucial role in the IoT architecture as it provides the necessary infrastructure for integrating different devices and applications in the system. It also enables communication between the devices and processes data collected from the perception layer.

4. **Application Layer**: This layer is responsible for providing the end-user applications that enable the user to interact with the IoT system. Applications can be designed for different purposes such as home automation, healthcare, transportation, and more.

5. **Business Layer**: This layer deals with the business aspects of IoT such as data analytics, security, and privacy. It is responsible for analyzing the data collected from the perception layer and providing insights that can help businesses make informed decisions.

Mnemonics and learning tricks for remembering these layers of IoT architecture:

- "PAN MB" - Perception, Network, Middleware, Application, Business
- "Please Note My Awesome Business" - Perception, Network, Middleware, Application, Business

Advantages of IoT Architecture:

- Enables connectivity and communication between devices
- Provides real-time data and insights that can help businesses make informed decisions
- Increases efficiency and reduces costs by automating processes
- Improves safety and security by providing remote monitoring and control of devices

Disadvantages of IoT Architecture:

- Security concerns such as unauthorized access and data breaches
- Dependency on internet connectivity for functioning of devices
- Lack of standardization and interoperability between devices from different manufacturers

Examples of IoT Architecture:

- Smart homes that use sensors and devices to automate tasks such as lighting, temperature control, and security monitoring
- Wearables such as fitness trackers and smartwatches that collect and transmit data to mobile apps for analysis
- Industrial IoT (IIoT) that use sensors and devices to monitor and control manufacturing processes for increased efficiency and reduced downtime.

In conclusion, understanding the architectural view of IoT is crucial for designing and implementing IoT systems. The layers of IoT architecture work together to enable communication, data collection, and analysis, and provide end-user applications that make IoT systems user-friendly and efficient.
