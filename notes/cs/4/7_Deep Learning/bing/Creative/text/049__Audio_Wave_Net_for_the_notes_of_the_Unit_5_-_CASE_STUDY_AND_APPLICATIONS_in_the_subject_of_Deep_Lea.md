### Audio Wave Net

- Audio Wave Net is a deep generative model of raw audio waveforms that can produce natural-sounding speech and music .
- It is based on a fully convolutional neural network that predicts the next audio sample given the previous ones .
- It uses dilated causal convolutions to increase the receptive field and capture long-range dependencies in the audio signal .
- It also uses gated activation units, residual connections, and skip connections to improve the gradient flow and the modelling capacity of the network .
- It can be conditioned on additional inputs, such as text or speaker identity, to generate speech or music with desired characteristics .
- It can learn from raw audio data without relying on any intermediate representations or vocoders .
- It can generate high-fidelity audio samples at 24 kHz with 16-bit resolution .
- It can model any kind of audio, including speech, music, and environmental sounds .
- It can reduce the gap between human and computer-generated speech by over 50% compared to previous methods .
- It can create novel and realistic audio samples that are not present in the training data .
