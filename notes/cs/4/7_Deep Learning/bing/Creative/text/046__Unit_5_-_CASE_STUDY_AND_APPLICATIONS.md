## Unit 5 - CASE STUDY AND APPLICATIONS

In this unit, we will explore some case studies and applications of artificial intelligence in various domains and scenarios. We will learn about the challenges, benefits, and limitations of AI systems in real-world contexts. We will also discuss some ethical, social, and legal implications of AI development and deployment.

Some of the topics that we will cover in this unit are:

- AI for healthcare: how AI can assist in diagnosis, treatment, prevention, and research of various diseases and conditions.
- AI for education: how AI can enhance learning outcomes, personalize instruction, and provide feedback and assessment for students and teachers.
- AI for entertainment: how AI can create and analyze content, such as music, movies, games, and art, and generate novel and engaging experiences for users.
- AI for business: how AI can optimize processes, improve decision making, and provide customer service and support for various industries and sectors.
- AI for social good: how AI can address global challenges, such as poverty, hunger, climate change, and human rights, and promote social justice and inclusion.

Each topic will include some examples of AI systems and projects that illustrate the current state-of-the-art and the future potential of AI in that domain. We will also examine some of the technical, ethical, and social issues and questions that arise from the use and impact of AI in that domain.
