## Unit 5 - CASE STUDY AND APPLICATIONS

- In this unit, you will learn how to apply the concepts and techniques of data science to real-world problems and scenarios.
- You will also learn how to communicate your findings and recommendations to different audiences and stakeholders.
- You will explore various case studies and applications of data science in different domains, such as business, health, education, social media, and more.
- You will also work on a capstone project that will allow you to showcase your data science skills and knowledge on a topic of your choice.
- The objectives of this unit are:

  - To understand the process and challenges of data science in practice.
  - To apply data science methods and tools to analyze and solve real-world problems.
  - To communicate data science results and insights effectively and persuasively.
  - To demonstrate your data science competency and creativity through a capstone project.

- The topics covered in this unit are:

  - Case study 1: Data-driven decision making in business
  - Case study 2: Predicting and preventing hospital readmissions
  - Case study 3: Analyzing student performance and learning outcomes
  - Case study 4: Mining social media data for sentiment analysis and recommendation systems
  - Capstone project: Data science project proposal and report
