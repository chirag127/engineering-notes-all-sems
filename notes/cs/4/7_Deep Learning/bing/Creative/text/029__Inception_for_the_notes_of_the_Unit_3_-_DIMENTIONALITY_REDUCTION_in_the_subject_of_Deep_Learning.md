### Inception

- Inception is a deep learning model based on convolutional neural networks (CNNs) that was introduced by Google researchers in 2014.
- Inception aims to solve the problem of computational expense and overfitting in CNNs by using multiple kernel filter sizes within the same layer, rather than stacking them sequentially.
- Inception modules are the building blocks of the Inception model. They consist of parallel branches of convolutions and pooling operations that are concatenated and sent to the next layer.
- Inception modules allow for more efficient computation and deeper networks by reducing the dimensionality of the input with 1x1 convolutions before applying larger convolutions.
- Inception modules also increase the representational power of the network by capturing features at different scales and levels of abstraction.
- The original Inception model, also known as GoogLeNet or Inception v1, achieved state-of-the-art performance on the ImageNet classification challenge in 2014.
- Since then, several variants of the Inception model have been proposed, such as Inception v2, Inception v3, and Inception v4, which improve the architecture and performance of the model  .
