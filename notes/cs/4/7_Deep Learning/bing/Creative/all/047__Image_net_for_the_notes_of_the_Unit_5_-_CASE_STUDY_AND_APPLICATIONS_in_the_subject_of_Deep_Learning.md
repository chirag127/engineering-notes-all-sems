### Image net for the notes of the Unit 5 - CASE STUDY AND APPLICATIONS in the subject of Deep Learning

- ImageNet is a large dataset of annotated photographs intended for computer vision research.
- It contains more than 14 million images of over 22000 categories (synsets) that are manually labeled by humans .
- It is used for object recognition and image classification tasks, as well as for providing critical training and benchmarking data for computer vision algorithms.
- ImageNet Large Scale Visual Recognition Challenge (ILSVRC) is an annual competition that uses subsets from the ImageNet dataset and is designed to foster the development and benchmarking of state-of-the-art algorithms.
- The ILSVRC tasks have led to milestone model architectures and techniques in the intersection of computer vision and deep learning, such as convolutional neural networks (CNNs), residual networks (ResNets), inception networks, etc.
- ImageNet can be accessed from the Kaggle ImageNet Localization Challenge, but it is a very large dataset of size more than 150GB.
- Training a machine learning model from scratch using ImageNet is a very time-consuming task, and can take days or even weeks.
- A better alternative is to use pre-trained ImageNet models in transfer learning applications, where the models are fine-tuned or adapted to specific tasks or domains.
- Some of the pre-trained ImageNet models are:
  - VGG16 and VGG19: CNN architectures with 16 and 19 layers respectively, used for image recognition and face recognition.
  - ResNet50: A residual network with 50 layers, used for image classification and object detection.
  - Inception v3: A CNN architecture with multiple branches and filters, used for image recognition and classification.
  - Xception: A CNN architecture with depthwise separable convolutions, used for image classification and segmentation.
- ImageNet makes transfer learning possible, as it allows to train a model once and use it for different tasks or domains, without requiring a large amount of labeled data for each task.
- ImageNet is a useful resource for learning and applying deep learning for computer vision, as it exposes the students to real-world problems, challenges, and solutions.
- ImageNet also teaches the students how to apply theory in practice and how to induce theory from practice, as well as how to perform critical analysis, judgment, decision-making, and action.
