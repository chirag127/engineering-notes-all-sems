Bioinformatics is an interdisciplinary field that applies computational methods to analyze biological data, such as DNA, RNA, protein sequences, structures, functions, interactions, and networks. Deep learning is a branch of machine learning that uses multiple layers of artificial neural networks to learn from large and complex data, and has achieved remarkable success in various domains, such as computer vision, natural language processing, speech recognition, and so on. Deep learning has also been widely applied to bioinformatics problems, such as sequence analysis, gene expression regulation, protein structure prediction, molecular design, drug discovery, biomedical image processing, and systems biology.
