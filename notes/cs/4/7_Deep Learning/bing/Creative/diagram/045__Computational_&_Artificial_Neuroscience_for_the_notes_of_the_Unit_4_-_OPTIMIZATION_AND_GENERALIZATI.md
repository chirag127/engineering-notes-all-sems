Computational and artificial neuroscience is a field of study that seeks to understand how the brain works by using mathematical models, simulations, and computer simulations. It is an interdisciplinary field that involves expertise in biology, physics, mathematics, computer science, and engineering . One of the main applications of computational neuroscience in artificial intelligence is in the development of neural networks, which are computational models that are inspired by the structure and function of the brain.

Optimization and generalization are two important aspects of deep learning, which is a branch of artificial intelligence that uses neural networks to learn from data. Optimization refers to the process of finding the best set of parameters for a neural network that minimizes the loss function, which measures the discrepancy between the network's predictions and the true labels. Generalization refers to the ability of a neural network to perform well on new and unseen data, not just on the training data .
