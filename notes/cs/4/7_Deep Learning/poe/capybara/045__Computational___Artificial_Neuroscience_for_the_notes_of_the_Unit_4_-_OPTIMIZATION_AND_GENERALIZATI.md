### Computational & Artificial Neuroscience for the notes of the Unit 4 - OPTIMIZATION AND GENERALIZATION in the subject of Deep Learning

In this unit, we will explore the intersection of computational science and neuroscience, as well as its application in deep learning. Here are some key concepts to keep in mind:

- Computational Neuroscience is a field of study that seeks to explain how the brain processes information and how this can be represented mathematically.

- Artificial Neural Networks (ANNs) are models inspired by the structure and function of the brain that are used in deep learning to solve complex problems.

- A popular algorithm used in ANN training is Stochastic Gradient Descent (SGD), which involves iteratively adjusting the weights of the network to minimize the loss function.

- Regularization techniques can be used to prevent overfitting, a common problem in deep learning where the model performs well on training data but poorly on new data.

- Dropout regularization involves randomly dropping out neurons during training to encourage the network to learn more robust features.

- Early stopping is another regularization technique that involves stopping training when the performance on a validation set stops improving.

- Generalization refers to how well a model performs on new, unseen data. This is an important measure of a model's ability to learn and make predictions.

- Cross-validation is a technique used to estimate a model's generalization performance by dividing the data into multiple subsets and training the model on different combinations of these subsets.

- Hyperparameter tuning involves adjusting the settings of the model, such as learning rate and number of hidden layers, to improve its performance.

- Transfer learning is a technique where a pre-trained model is used as a starting point for a new task, allowing the model to leverage the knowledge it has gained from previous tasks.

By understanding the principles of Computational and Artificial Neuroscience, we can develop more effective deep learning models that are better able to generalize and solve complex problems.
