## Unit 5 - CASE STUDY AND APPLICATIONS

In this unit, we will explore various case studies and applications of the concepts we have learned throughout the course. The following points will be covered:

- **Case studies:** We will examine real-world examples of how data science has been used to solve complex problems. These case studies will cover a range of industries, including healthcare, finance, marketing, and more. Through these examples, we will gain a better understanding of how data science can be applied in different contexts.

- **Predictive modeling:** We will dive deeper into predictive modeling and explore how it can be used to make accurate predictions about future events. We will learn about different types of models, such as linear regression, logistic regression, and decision trees, and how they can be used to make predictions in various situations.

- **Natural language processing:** We will explore how natural language processing (NLP) can be used to analyze text data. We will learn about techniques such as sentiment analysis, topic modeling, and named entity recognition, and how they can be used to extract insights from unstructured text data.

- **Computer vision:** We will examine how computer vision can be used to analyze visual data, such as images and videos. We will learn about techniques such as object detection, image classification, and facial recognition, and how they can be used to extract insights from visual data.

- **Big data:** We will explore how data science can be used to analyze large datasets, often referred to as big data. We will learn about techniques such as Hadoop and MapReduce, and how they can be used to process and analyze large amounts of data.

By the end of this unit, you will have a deeper understanding of how data science can be applied in various contexts and industries. You will have learned about a range of techniques and tools, and will be better equipped to tackle complex data science problems.
