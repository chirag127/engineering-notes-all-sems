
### Stochastic Gradient Descent for Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in Deep Learning

* Stochastic Gradient Descent (SGD) is an iterative optimization algorithm used to minimize a given cost function. 
* SGD works by taking small steps in the direction of the negative gradient of the cost function. 
* The steps taken in SGD are usually much smaller than those taken in standard gradient descent. 
* SGD is widely used in deep learning because it is an efficient way to train large neural networks. 
* SGD is an important concept in deep learning because it allows us to train deep neural networks quickly and efficiently. 
* SGD works by randomly selecting a subset of the training data and then computing the gradient of the cost function with respect to the parameters of the model. 
* SGD is an iterative algorithm, meaning that it will take multiple steps to reach the minimum of the cost function. 
* SGD is a powerful algorithm that can be used to optimize a wide variety of machine learning models.
