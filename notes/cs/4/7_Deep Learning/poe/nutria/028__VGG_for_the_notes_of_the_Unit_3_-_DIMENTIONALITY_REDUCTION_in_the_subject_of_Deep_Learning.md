
### VGG for the Notes of the Unit 3 - Dimensionality Reduction in the Subject of Deep Learning

1. VGG stands for Visual Geometry Group, a research group at Oxford University. 
2. VGG is a deep learning model that is used for image classification and object recognition. 
3. It was developed by Karen Simonyan and Andrew Zisserman in 2014. 
4. VGG networks are composed of convolutional layers, pooling layers, and fully connected layers. 
5. The convolutional layers are used for feature extraction, while the fully connected layers are used for classification. 
6. The pooling layers are used to reduce the dimensionality of the input data. 
7. VGG networks are trained on the ImageNet dataset, which contains millions of images. 
8. The VGG networks are used in many applications, including image classification, object detection, and object tracking.
