
### Recurrent Networks

* Recurrent networks are a type of artificial neural network that are designed to recognize patterns in sequential data.
* They are used in applications such as natural language processing, speech recognition, and time-series prediction.
* Recurrent networks have a memory component which allows them to remember previous inputs and use this information to make decisions.
* Recurrent networks can be divided into two types: feed-forward and feedback.
* Feed-forward recurrent networks are used for tasks such as classification, while feedback networks are used for tasks such as sequence generation.
* Recurrent networks can be trained using backpropagation through time (BPTT) or other optimization algorithms such as stochastic gradient descent (SGD).
* Regularization techniques such as dropout and weight decay can be used to improve the generalization performance of recurrent networks.
