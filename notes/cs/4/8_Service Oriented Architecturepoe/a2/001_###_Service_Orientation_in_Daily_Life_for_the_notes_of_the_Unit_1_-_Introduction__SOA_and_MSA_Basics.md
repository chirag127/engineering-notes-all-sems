### Service Orientation in Daily Life

- Services are all around us in daily life:
    - Electricity service: We get electricity supply at home which powers various home appliances. The electricity generation companies provide the electricity service which is consumed by users.
    - Water service: We get water supply at home for various purposes. The water supply department provides the water service which is consumed by users.
    - Transport service: We hire taxi/cab services or use public transport services to travel from one place to another. The transport companies provide the transport services which are utilized by commuters.
    - Food service: We go to restaurants or order food from food delivery apps to get food. The restaurants and food delivery companies provide the food services which are consumed by users.
- All these real-life services share some characteristics of software services in SOA:
    - Provided by a service provider and consumed by service consumers.
    - Have a defined interface to access the service.
    - Have defined service levels and quality.
    - Might compose other services to provide the end service.
    - Might reuse the same services for different purposes.
    - Might have different providers for the same type of service with different characteristics.
- Thus, the core concepts of services, service-orientation, service composition, and service reuse which we study in SOA have relatable analogies in our daily life service interactions. This helps in understanding the SOA concepts more easily by mapping to familiar scenarios.
