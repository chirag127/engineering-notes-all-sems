### Concepts for the notes of the Unit 7 - Big Data and SOA in the subject of Service Oriented Architecture

1. Big Data - Big data refers to extremely large data sets that may be analyzed computationally to reveal patterns, trends, and associations, especially relating to human behavior and interactions. Some characteristics of big data are:
- Volume: Amount of data is huge.
- Velocity: Data is streaming in at an incredible rate.
- Variety: Data is available in many formats like structured, unstructured, text, multimedia, etc.
- Veracity: Degree of uncertainty in data is high.

Mnemonic: Remember 'VVV' or '3Vs' to recall the key characteristics of Big Data - Volume, Velocity and Variety.

2. Hadoop - Hadoop is an open-source software framework used for distributed storage and processing of big data sets using the MapReduce programming model. It is designed to scale up from single servers to thousands of machines, each offering local computation and storage.
Advantages:
- Scalable and cost effective.
- Fault tolerant.
- Flexible.

3. MapReduce - It is a programming model for processing large data sets with a parallel, distributed algorithm on a cluster. It mainly consists of two tasks:
- Map: Divides the input into smaller sub-problems.
- Reduce: Combines the answers to all the sub-problems.

4. SOA - Service-Oriented Architecture is a style of software design where applications are built as a set of independent services that can be accessed without knowledge of their internal implementation. Services communicate with each other by passing data from one service to another, or by coordinating an activity between two or more services.
Characteristics:
- Loose coupling: Services are loosely coupled such that they have minimal dependencies on each other.
- Abstraction: The internal complexities of services are hidden behind interfaces.
- Reusability: Services can be reused to create new applications.
- Autonomy: Services can be independently deployed and managed.

Advantages:
- Increased interoperability.
- Reduced cost.
- Flexibility.

Disadvantages:
- Complexity.
- Difficulty in debugging and testing.
- Versioning problems.
