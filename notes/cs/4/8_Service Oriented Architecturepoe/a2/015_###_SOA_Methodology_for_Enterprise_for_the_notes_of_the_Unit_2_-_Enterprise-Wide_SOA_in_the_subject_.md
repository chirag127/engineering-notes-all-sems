### SOA Methodology for Enterprise

- SOA methodology provides a structured approach to design, develop and deploy Service Oriented Architectures for enterprise wide usage.
- It consists of the following key steps:

1. Identify Business Processes and Services: The key business processes and services needed to be exposed as reusable services are identified in this step. The services are identified based on business requirements and functional areas.
2. Define Interfaces for Services: The interfaces for the services are defined in a neutral and technology independent way. The services are defined based on the functionality they provide rather than the underlying technology implementation.
3. Choose Service Model: The appropriate service model like SOAP based web services, RESTful services, etc. are chosen for implementing the services based on factors like interface requirements, performance needs, security considerations, etc.
4. Implement Services: The services are implemented as per the chosen service model. The implementation can be in any programming language or platform.
5. Service Composition: The services are composed together to automate enterprise business processes. The composed services can further be reused to build higher level services or composite applications.
6. Service Publication: The services are published in a service registry or repository so that they can be discovered and reused across the enterprise.
7. Service Governance: Proper governance mechanisms are put in place to manage the services across their lifecycle to ensure high quality and reusability. The governance handles aspects like versioning, security, performance, etc.

- The key advantages of following a structured SOA methodology are increased reusability, improved flexibility, reduced development efforts and better maintainability. The methodology acts as a guiding framework for implementing SOA solutions in an efficient, consistent and reliable way across the enterprise.
