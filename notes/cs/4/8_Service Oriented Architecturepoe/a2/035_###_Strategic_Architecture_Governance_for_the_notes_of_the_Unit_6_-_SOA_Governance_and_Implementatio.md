### Strategic Architecture Governance for the notes of the Unit 6 - SOA Governance and Implementation in the subject of Service Oriented Architecture

- Strategic Architecture Governance refers to the high-level decision making process regarding the overall IT architecture of an organization. It involves establishing principles and standards for the enterprise-wide architecture including the SOA.
- The key goals of Strategic Architecture Governance are:
    - Aligning IT with business goals
    - Optimizing IT investments
    - Reducing IT risks
    - Improving IT agility
- The key steps in Strategic Architecture Governance are:
    1. Define architecture principles - Fundamental guidelines for the architecture like scalability, interoperability, etc.
    2. Define architecture standards - Technical and operational standards for the architecture like messaging standards, security standards, etc.
    3. Review and approve architecture changes - Changes to the architecture are evaluated based on the principles and standards and approved by the Architecture Review Board.
    4. Provide architecture oversight - The Architecture Review Board provides oversight on the implementation and evolution of the architecture.
- The key roles and responsibilities in Strategic Architecture Governance include:
    - CIO - Responsible for IT strategy and architecture governance
    - Enterprise Architects - Responsible for defining and evolving the architecture
    - Architecture Review Board - Responsible for reviewing and approving architecture changes
- Some tips for effective Strategic Architecture Governance:
    - Obtain senior management sponsorship and support
    - Align with business goals and key initiatives
    - Keep governance practices lightweight but rigorous
    - Foster collaborative decision making involving all stakeholders
    - Continuously communicate and educate stakeholders about the architecture
