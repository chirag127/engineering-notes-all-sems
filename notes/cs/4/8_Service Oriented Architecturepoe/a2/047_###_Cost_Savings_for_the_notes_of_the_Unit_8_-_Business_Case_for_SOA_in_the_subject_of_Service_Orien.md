### Cost Savings for the notes of the Unit 8 - Business Case for SOA in the subject of Service Oriented Architecture

- Reduced Integration costs: With SOA, services are designed to be reusable. This reduces the integration costs as the services need to be integrated only once and can be reused multiple times thereby reducing the costs of integration for every new system integration.
- Increased developer productivity: With SOA, the services are clearly defined, modular and independent. This increases the developer productivity as they can focus on one service at a time and reuse the services thereby reducing the development time.
- Reduced maintenance costs: Since the services in SOA are independent and reusable, any changes required can be made to the specific service only thereby reducing the maintenance costs and effort. The changes need not be done in all the systems that reuse the service.
- Reduced Operational costs: The operational costs are reduced in SOA as the monitoring and management is done at the service level. This is easier than monitoring and managing the entire systems. The services can be quickly identified and fixed in case of any issues thereby reducing the operational costs.

Some of the key advantages of SOA in terms of cost savings are reduced integration costs, increased developer productivity, reduced maintenance costs and reduced operational costs. The reusability of independent and modular services is a key factor that contributes to the cost savings in SOA.
