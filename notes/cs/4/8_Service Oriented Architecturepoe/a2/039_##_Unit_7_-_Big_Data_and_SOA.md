## Unit 7 - Big Data and SOA

### What is Big Data?

- Big data refers to extremely large data sets that may be analyzed computationally to reveal patterns, trends, and associations, especially relating to human behavior and interactions.
- The challenges include capture, curation, storage, search, sharing, transfer, analysis, and visualization.
- The three V's of big data: Volume, Velocity, Variety.
- Volume: huge amount of data. Velocity: data is generated and processed at high speed. Variety: different forms of data like structured, unstructured, textual, multimedia, etc.

### Characteristics of Big Data

- Volume: Amount of data is huge. Measured in PBs and Exabytes.
- Velocity: Data flows in at an incredible speed. Sometimes streaming data needs to be processed on the fly.
- Variety: Data comes in various formats like structured, semi-structured, unstructured, text, multimedia, etc.
- Veracity: Uncertainty of data. Data may be noisy, biased, incomplete or inconsistent. This makes big data processing more challenging.
- Value: Deriving value from big data is the ultimate goal. The value can provide competitive advantages and help in better decision making.

### Service-Oriented Architecture (SOA)

- SOA is a collection of services which communicate with each other. The core idea is to achieve reuse of application logic through services.
- Services are self-contained, modular applications that perform specific functions.
- Services communicate with each other through well-defined interfaces using a messaging protocol.
- The key benefits of SOA are: Modularity, Interoperability, Reusability, Maintainability and Platform Independence.
- The main components of SOA are: Services, Service registry, Service broker, Transport protocol and Service consumer.
