### Big Data and its characteristics

The characteristics of Big Data are:

1. Volume: Refers to the huge amount of data generated. The data is too large to be stored and processed by traditional data processing applications.
2. Velocity: Refers to the speed at which the data is generated and processed. The data is generated and processed at a very high speed.
3. Variety: Refers to the different types and formats of data. The data is available in different formats like structured, semi-structured and unstructured data.
4. Veracity: Refers to the uncertainty of the data. The data may contain noise, biases or abnormal values and needs to be cleaned before processing.

Mnemonics:

* Volume, Velocity, Variety, Veracity (4 V's)

Learning tricks:

* Remember the 4 V's using the phrase "Very Very Valuable Verifiable Data"
* Think of huge data as elephant (for volume), cheetah (for velocity), rainbow (for variety) and magnifying glass (for veracity)

Advantages of Big Data:

* Enables making better business decisions
* Provides insights into customer behavior and buying patterns
* Increases operational efficiency and reduces costs
* Offers competitive advantage by data monetization

Disadvantages of Big Data:

* Requirement of specialized and talented data scientists and analysts
* Additional investments required in data processing hardware and software
* Complexity in data synchronization and aggregation
* Data security and privacy concerns
* Bias in data can lead to erroneous results and decisions
