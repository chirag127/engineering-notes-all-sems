### Service Run-time Governance for the notes of the Unit 6 - SOA Governance and Implementation in the subject of Service Oriented Architecture

- Service run-time governance involves managing the execution of services at run-time. It ensures that services are operating as expected and meets non-functional requirements like performance, security, reliability, etc.
- Some of the key run-time governance capabilities are:
-- Service Level Agreement (SLA) monitoring - Monitoring key metrics like response time, throughput, availability, etc. and taking corrective actions if SLAs are violated.
-- Routing and load balancing - Directing requests to appropriate service instances based on load, geography, etc. to optimize performance.
-- Fault tolerance - Detecting and handling service failures gracefully using techniques like failover, replication, etc.
-- Security enforcement - Applying security policies and controls at run-time like authentication, authorization, encryption, etc.
-- Analytics - Collecting and analyzing metrics to gain operational insights and take proactive actions.
-- Version management - Handling multiple versions of services and switching between versions seamlessly.
-- Elastic scaling - Scaling service capacity up or down dynamically based on demand.

*Advantages:* Ensures optimal operation of services and meets business requirements.
*Disadvantages:* Additional overhead on services and governance system. Complex to implement.
*Applications:* Critical business services, services with high scalability/performance needs, etc.
