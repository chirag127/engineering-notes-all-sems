### Benefits of SOA for the notes of the Unit 8 - Business Case for SOA in the subject of Service Oriented Architecture

- **Increased business agility:** SOA allows businesses to quickly adapt to changing requirements and deliver new products/services by reusing and reconfiguring existing services. This increased flexibility and responsiveness leads to greater business agility.
- **Cost reduction:** By reusing services, SOA can help reduce redundancy and duplication of effort leading to cost savings. Maintenance costs are also lower as updates/fixes only need to be applied to a service once.
- **Abstraction:** SOA abstracts the underlying technical details of services allowing business users to focus on the business functionality being offered instead of technical implementation details. This abstraction simplifies the integration of services.
- **Interoperability:** The standardized interfaces and contracts between services foster interoperability allowing services to be integrated and composed more easily. This interoperability extends beyond technology and organizations.
- **Vendor independence:** With standardized service contracts, SOA enables you to swap out the vendor/implementation providing a service. This decreases reliance on a single vendor and avoids vendor lock-in.
- **Improved governance:** The service-oriented approach along with the usage of service registry/repositories lends itself well to improved governance of IT resources through better visibility and control of services.
