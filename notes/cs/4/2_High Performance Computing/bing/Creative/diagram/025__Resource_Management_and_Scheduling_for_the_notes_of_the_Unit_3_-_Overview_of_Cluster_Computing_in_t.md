Resource management and scheduling are critical tasks in cluster computing. They involve allocating and managing the resources of a cluster system, such as CPU, memory, disk, network, and power, to execute a set of jobs or tasks. The main objectives of resource management and scheduling are to maximize the resource utilization, minimize the processing time of jobs, and meet the quality of service and performance requirements of users and applications.
