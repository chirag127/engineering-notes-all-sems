Internet Computing is a type of distributed computing that involves the use of the Internet as a platform for sharing resources and services among different devices and networks. A typical Internet Computing diagram consists of the following components:

- Clients: These are the devices that request and consume the resources and services provided by the Internet. They can be personal computers, laptops, tablets, smartphones, or any other device that can access the web.
- Servers: These are the devices that provide and host the resources and services that are requested by the clients. They can be web servers, database servers, application servers, or any other device that can run a program and respond to requests over the network.
- Routers: These are the devices that connect different networks and direct the traffic between them. They can be physical devices or virtual devices that operate at the network layer of the Internet protocol stack.
- Switches: These are the devices that connect different devices within the same network and forward the data packets between them. They can be physical devices or virtual devices that operate at the data link layer of the Internet protocol stack.
- Links: These are the physical or logical connections that enable the data transmission between different devices and networks. They can be wired or wireless, and they can have different bandwidths and latencies.
