### Types of Cloud for the notes of the Unit 5 - Overview of Cloud Computing in the subject of High Performance Computing

Cloud computing is the delivery of computing services over the internet, such as servers, storage, databases, networking, software, analytics, and intelligence. Cloud computing can be classified into two main types: deployment models and service models.

#### Deployment models

Deployment models refer to the location and ownership of the cloud infrastructure. There are four main types of deployment models:

- **Public cloud**: The cloud infrastructure is owned and operated by a third-party cloud service provider, such as AWS, Google Cloud, or Microsoft Azure. The cloud services are delivered over the internet and are available to anyone who wants to use them. The customers share the same infrastructure and resources, which are dynamically allocated and released according to demand. Public clouds offer scalability, cost-effectiveness, and reliability, but may have less control, security, and customization than other models.
- **Private cloud**: The cloud infrastructure is exclusively used by a single organization or a group of organizations that share the same goals and policies. The cloud infrastructure can be owned, managed, and operated by the organization itself, a third-party service provider, or a combination of both. The cloud services are delivered over a private network or the internet, and are not accessible to the general public. Private clouds offer more control, security, and customization than public clouds, but may require more investment, maintenance, and expertise than other models.
- **Hybrid cloud**: The cloud infrastructure is a combination of public and private clouds, which are connected by a common technology that enables data and application portability. The hybrid cloud allows the organization to leverage the benefits of both public and private clouds, such as scalability, cost-efficiency, reliability, control, security, and flexibility. The hybrid cloud also enables the organization to address different business needs and regulatory requirements by choosing the best deployment model for each workload.
- **Multicloud**: The cloud infrastructure is a collection of multiple public and private clouds from different cloud service providers, which are not necessarily connected or integrated. The multicloud allows the organization to avoid vendor lock-in, optimize performance, reduce costs, increase reliability, and enhance security by choosing the best cloud service for each workload. The multicloud also enables the organization to leverage the innovation and diversity of different cloud service providers.

#### Service models

Service models refer to the level of abstraction and management of the cloud services. There are three main types of service models:

- **Infrastructure as a Service (IaaS)**: The cloud service provider offers on-demand access to the basic building blocks of cloud IT, such as servers, storage, networking, and virtualization. The customer can provision, configure, and manage the infrastructure resources according to their needs, and only pay for what they use. The customer is responsible for the operating systems, applications, and data that run on the infrastructure. IaaS offers scalability, flexibility, and cost-effectiveness, but may require more technical skills and security measures than other models.
- **Platform as a Service (PaaS)**: The cloud service provider offers a cloud-based platform that provides the tools and services for developing, testing, deploying, and managing applications. The customer can focus on the application logic and functionality, and does not have to worry about the underlying infrastructure, operating systems, middleware, or runtime environments. The customer only pays for the platform services that they use. PaaS offers productivity, agility, and scalability, but may have less control and portability than other models.
- **Software as a Service (SaaS)**: The cloud service provider offers a cloud-based application that is fully developed, managed, and maintained by the provider. The customer can access the application over the internet, usually through a web browser or a mobile app, and does not have to install, update, or maintain anything. The customer only pays for the application usage or subscription. SaaS offers convenience, reliability, and performance, but may have less customization and integration than other models.

Here are some possible mnemonics and learning tricks to remember the types of cloud computing:

- For the deployment models, you can use the acronym **PHM** (Public, Hybrid, Multicloud) and remember it as **PHone Me** or **PHoto My**.
