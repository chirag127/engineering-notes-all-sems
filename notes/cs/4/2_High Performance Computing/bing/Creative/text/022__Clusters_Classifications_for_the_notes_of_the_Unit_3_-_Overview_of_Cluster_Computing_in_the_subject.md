### Cluster Classifications

Cluster computing is a collection of tightly or loosely connected computers that work together as a single entity. The connected computers execute operations all together thus creating the idea of a single system. The clusters are generally connected through fast local area networks (LANs) .

Cluster computing can be classified based on different criteria, such as:

- The purpose of the cluster: High performance (HP) clusters, load-balancing clusters, and high availability (HA) clusters  .
- The structure of the cluster: Open clusters and closed clusters .
- The degree of membership of the cluster: Hard clustering and soft clustering .

#### High performance (HP) clusters

HP clusters use computer clusters and supercomputers to solve advance computational problems. They are used to performing functions that need nodes to communicate as they perform their jobs. They are designed to take benefit of the parallel processing power of several nodes .

#### Load-balancing clusters

Load-balancing clusters distribute incoming requests for resources among several nodes running similar programs or having similar content. This prevents any single node from receiving a disproportionate amount of task. This type of distribution is generally used in a web-hosting environment  .

#### High availability (HA) clusters

HA clusters are designed to maintain redundant nodes that can act as backup systems in case any failure occurs. Consistent computing services like business activities, complicated databases, customer services like e-websites and network file distribution are provided. They are designed to give uninterrupted data availability to the customers  .

#### Open clusters

Open clusters are clusters where every node has an IP address and can be accessed only through the internet or web. This type of cluster causes enhanced security concerns .

#### Closed clusters

Closed clusters are clusters where the nodes are hidden behind the gateway node, and they provide increased protection. They need fewer IP addresses and are good for computational tasks .

#### Hard clustering

Hard clustering is a type of clustering where each object belongs to a cluster or not. There is no ambiguity or uncertainty in the assignment of objects to clusters .

#### Soft clustering

Soft clustering is a type of clustering where each object belongs to each cluster to some degree. There is a measure of similarity or probability that indicates how well an object fits into a cluster .
