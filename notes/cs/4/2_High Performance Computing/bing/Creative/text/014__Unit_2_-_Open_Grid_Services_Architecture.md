## Unit 2 - Open Grid Services Architecture

- Open Grid Services Architecture (OGSA) is a service-oriented architecture for a grid computing environment for business and scientific use. 
- It was developed within the Open Grid Forum, which was called the Global Grid Forum (GGF) at the time, around 2002 to 2006. 
- OGSA describes a set of core capabilities and behaviors that address key concerns in grid systems, such as interoperability, resource sharing, optimization, quality of service, security, self-management, and information. 
- OGSA is based on several other Web service technologies, such as the Web Services Description Language (WSDL) and the Simple Object Access Protocol (SOAP), but it aims to be largely independent of transport-level handling of data. 
- OGSA has been described as a refinement of a Web services architecture, specifically designed to support grid requirements. 
- The concept of OGSA is derived from work presented in the 2002 Globus Alliance paper "The Physiology of the Grid" by Ian Foster, Carl Kesselman, Jeffrey M. Nick, and Steven Tuecke. 
- The OGSA working group within the GGF produced a document, entitled The Open Grid Services Architecture, Version 1.5 in 2006, which provides a first version of this OGSA definition. 
- The document focuses on requirements and the scope of important capabilities required to support grid systems and applications in both e-science and e-business. 
- The capabilities described are Execution Management, Data, Resource Management, Security, Self-Management, and Information. 
- The description of the capabilities is at a high-level and includes, to some extent, the interrelationships between the capabilities. 
- The OGSA framework defines a set of common behaviors and interfaces for grid services, such as service creation, lifetime management, notification, and discovery. 
- The OGSA infrastructure services provide basic functionalities for service hosting, communication, and security. 
- The OGSA execution management services provide functionalities for job submission, scheduling, monitoring, and control. 
- The OGSA data services provide functionalities for data access, transfer, replication, and integration. 
- The OGSA resource management services provide functionalities for resource allocation, reservation, and accounting. 
- The OGSA security services provide functionalities for authentication, authorization, encryption, and auditing. 
- The OGSA self-management services provide functionalities for fault tolerance, adaptation, and optimization. 
- The OGSA information services provide functionalities for collecting, storing, and querying information about grid resources and services.
