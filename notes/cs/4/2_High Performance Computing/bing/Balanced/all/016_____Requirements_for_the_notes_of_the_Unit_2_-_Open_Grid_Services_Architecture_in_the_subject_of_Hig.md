# Requirements for the notes of the Unit 2 - Open Grid Services Architecture in the subject of High Performance Computing

- The notes should cover the following topics:

  - The definition and motivation of Open Grid Services Architecture (OGSA)  
  - The core capabilities and behaviors of OGSA, such as service discovery, data access, security, notification, and fault tolerance  
  - The resource models and interfaces of OGSA, such as Grid Service, Service Data, Factory, Handle, and Reference 
  - The bindings and protocols of OGSA, such as WSDL, SOAP, and WS-Addressing 
  - The use cases and applications of OGSA, such as scientific computing, business process integration, and e-science  

- The notes should follow the markdown format and use headers, lists, tables, and code blocks as appropriate.
- The notes should include diagrams and examples to illustrate the concepts and functionalities of OGSA.
- The notes should cite the sources of information and provide the URLs of the web search results in the footnotes.
