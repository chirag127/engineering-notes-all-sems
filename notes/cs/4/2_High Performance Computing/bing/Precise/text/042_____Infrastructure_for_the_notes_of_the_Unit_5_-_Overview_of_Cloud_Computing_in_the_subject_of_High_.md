### Infrastructure for the notes of the Unit 5 - Overview of Cloud Computing in the subject of High Performance Computing

1. Cloud computing infrastructure refers to the hardware and software components required to support the delivery of cloud services to end-users.
2. These components include servers, storage devices, network devices, virtualization software, and operating systems.
3. Cloud infrastructure can be deployed in different models, including public, private, and hybrid clouds.
4. Public clouds are owned and operated by third-party service providers and offer cloud services to the general public over the internet.
5. Private clouds are owned and operated by a single organization and offer cloud services to its internal users over a private network.
6. Hybrid clouds combine the features of both public and private clouds, allowing organizations to use public cloud services for non-sensitive workloads and private cloud services for sensitive workloads.
7. Cloud infrastructure can also be deployed in different service models, including Infrastructure as a Service (IaaS), Platform as a Service (PaaS), and Software as a Service (SaaS).
8. IaaS provides users with access to virtualized computing resources, such as virtual machines, storage, and networking, over the internet.
9. PaaS provides users with a platform for developing, testing, and deploying applications without the need to manage the underlying infrastructure.
10. SaaS provides users with access to software applications over the internet, without the need to install and manage the software on their own devices.
