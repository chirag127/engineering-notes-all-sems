### High Performance Computing

Unit 1 - Overview of Grid Computing Technology

1. **Grid Computing** is a form of distributed computing that involves coordinating and sharing computing resources across multiple administrative domains.
2. It allows for the sharing of resources such as processing power, storage, and data among multiple computers and organizations.
3. Grid Computing is often used for large-scale, resource-intensive computing tasks such as scientific simulations, data analysis, and modeling.
4. It is based on the principle of collaboration and resource sharing, allowing multiple organizations to work together to solve complex problems.
5. Grid Computing technology is used in a variety of fields, including scientific research, finance, and engineering.
6. It is an important component of High Performance Computing, enabling the use of large-scale computing resources to solve complex problems.
