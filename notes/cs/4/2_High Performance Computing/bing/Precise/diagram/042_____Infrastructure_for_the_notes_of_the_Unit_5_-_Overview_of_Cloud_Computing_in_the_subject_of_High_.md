### Infrastructure for the notes of the Unit 5 - Overview of Cloud Computing in the subject of High Performance Computing

1. Cloud computing infrastructure refers to the hardware and software components required to support the delivery of cloud services to end-users.
2. These components include servers, storage devices, network equipment, virtualization software, and operating systems.
3. Cloud infrastructure can be deployed in various models, including public, private, and hybrid clouds.
4. Public cloud infrastructure is owned and operated by a third-party cloud service provider and is accessible to the general public over the internet.
5. Private cloud infrastructure is owned and operated by a single organization and is accessible only to authorized users within that organization.
6. Hybrid cloud infrastructure combines public and private cloud infrastructure, allowing organizations to take advantage of the scalability and cost-effectiveness of public cloud services while maintaining control and security over their sensitive data.
7. Cloud infrastructure is typically offered as a service, allowing organizations to pay for the resources they use on a subscription or pay-per-use basis.
8. This eliminates the need for organizations to invest in and maintain their own IT infrastructure, reducing capital expenditures and operational costs.
9. Cloud infrastructure also provides scalability, allowing organizations to quickly and easily provision additional resources as their needs change.
10. Cloud infrastructure is managed by the cloud service provider, ensuring that the underlying hardware and software components are up-to-date, secure, and reliable.
