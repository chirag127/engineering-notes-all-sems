# Introduction to Unit 2 - Open Grid Services Architecture in High Performance Computing

- Open Grid Services Architecture (OGSA) is a set of standards that define the way in which grid computing systems should be built and interact with each other.
- OGSA is based on the principles of Service-Oriented Architecture (SOA) and aims to provide a common, standard way for grid services to be defined, managed, and used.
- The goal of OGSA is to enable the creation of distributed systems that are scalable, flexible, and able to support a wide range of applications and services.
- OGSA is an important part of the larger field of High Performance Computing (HPC), which focuses on the development and use of powerful computing systems to solve complex problems.
- In this unit, we will explore the key concepts and principles of OGSA, and examine how it is used in the context of HPC.
