### Cluster Middleware and SSI for the notes of the Unit 3 - Overview of Cluster Computing in the subject of High Performance Computing
Cluster Middleware:
- Software that facilitates communication, coordination, and management of resources in a cluster computing environment.
- Examples: Rocks Cluster, OpenMPI, LSF, PBS, SLURM.

Self-Sovereign Identity (SSI):
- A decentralized digital identity model that gives individuals control over their personal data.
- Based on blockchain technology and decentralized identifiers (DIDs).
- Allows for secure and private sharing of personal information without relying on centralized authorities.
