### Platform for the notes of the Unit 5 - Overview of Cloud Computing in the subject of High Performance Computing
Unit 5 of High Performance Computing subject focuses on Overview of Cloud Computing. This unit provides an introduction to cloud computing and its various components, including infrastructure as a service (IaaS), platform as a service (PaaS), and software as a service (SaaS). The unit also covers the benefits and challenges of cloud computing, such as scalability, reliability, and security. Additionally, the unit explores the different types of cloud computing, including public, private, and hybrid clouds, and the different deployment models, including community, public, and private clouds. The unit concludes with a discussion of the future of cloud computing and its potential impact on high performance computing.
