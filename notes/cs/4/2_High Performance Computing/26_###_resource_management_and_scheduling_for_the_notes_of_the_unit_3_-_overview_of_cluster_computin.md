### Resource Management and Scheduling for the notes of the Unit 3 - Overview of Cluster Computing in the subject of High Performance Computing
Resource Management:
- Resource management involves allocating and managing resources such as CPU, memory, storage, and network bandwidth in a cluster computing environment.
- The goal is to ensure efficient use of resources and fair allocation to multiple users and applications.
- Resource management systems can be centralized or decentralized, and use various algorithms such as First-Come-First-Served, Priority-based, and Backfill.

Scheduling:
- Scheduling is the process of determining when and where to run jobs in a cluster computing environment.
- It involves assigning resources to jobs based on their requirements, and ensuring that all jobs are executed efficiently and fairly.
- Scheduling algorithms can be static or dynamic, and can use various criteria such as job priority, deadline, and resource availability.
