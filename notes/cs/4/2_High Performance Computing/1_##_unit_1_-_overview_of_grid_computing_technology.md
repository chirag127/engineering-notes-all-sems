## Unit 1 - Overview of Grid Computing Technology
Grid computing is a form of distributed computing that enables the sharing of large-scale computing resources, such as processing power, memory, and storage, across multiple computers or networks. The goal of grid computing is to provide a virtualized and seamless computing environment that can be used to solve complex problems. The technology is used in a variety of fields, including scientific research, business, and government. Grid computing is often used to tackle problems that are too large for a single computer to handle, such as simulations, data analysis, and large-scale data storage. The technology is also used to provide high-performance computing resources to remote users, such as researchers in different locations. To achieve this, grid computing systems typically use a middleware layer that manages the distribution of tasks and the allocation of resources. This layer also provides security and data management services, such as data encryption and backup. Overall, grid computing technology is an important tool for solving complex problems and enabling the sharing of computing resources in a secure and efficient manner.
