### Platform for the notes of the Unit 5 - Overview of Cloud Computing in the subject of High Performance Computing

Cloud computing has become an integral part of modern computing, and it is essential to have a basic understanding of the topic. To help you in your studies, here are some platforms that you can use to take and organize your notes on the overview of cloud computing.

1. **Google Docs:** Google Docs is a popular platform for creating and sharing notes. It is a free web-based application that allows you to create and edit documents, spreadsheets, and presentations online. The platform also allows you to share your notes with others, making it a great tool for group work and collaboration.

2. **OneNote:** OneNote is a digital notebook that allows you to take notes and organize them in a structured way. It is available on multiple platforms, including Windows, Mac, iOS, and Android. OneNote allows you to create different notebooks for different topics, making it easy to keep your notes organized.

3. **Evernote:** Evernote is a popular note-taking platform that allows you to take notes, create to-do lists, and save web pages. It is available on multiple platforms, including Windows, Mac, iOS, and Android. Evernote also allows you to share your notes with others, making it a great tool for group work and collaboration.

4. **Trello:** Trello is a project management platform that allows you to create boards, lists, and cards to organize your notes. It is available on multiple platforms, including Windows, Mac, iOS, and Android. Trello allows you to collaborate with others, making it a great tool for group work.

5. **Notion:** Notion is an all-in-one workspace that allows you to take notes, create to-do lists, and save web pages. It is available on multiple platforms, including Windows, Mac, iOS, and Android. Notion is a great tool for organizing your notes, as it allows you to create different pages for different topics and link them together.

In conclusion, taking and organizing notes is an important part of studying cloud computing. These platforms can help you stay organized and make it easier to study and collaborate with others.
