### Applications and Architectures of High Performance Grids

- High throughput computing: Used for batch processing of large numbers of similar tasks that do not require low latency. Eg. Protein folding, drug discovery, seismic analysis, etc.
- High performance computing: Used for parallel processing of computationally intensive complex tasks that require low latency. Eg. Weather forecasting, computational fluid dynamics, quantum chemistry, etc.
- Data intensive computing: Used for applications that require analysis of massive data sets. Eg. Bioinformatics, fusion simulation, etc.

Architectures:

- Public resource grids: Heterogeneous systems connected over wide-area networks. Eg. Planetlab, EGEE, TeraGrid, etc.
- Private grids: Homogeneous clusters connected over local-area networks.
- Hybrid grids: Combination of public and private grids.

The grid infrastructure consists of grid middleware to enable resource sharing, scheduling, monitoring, security, data management, etc. across distributed and heterogeneous resources.
