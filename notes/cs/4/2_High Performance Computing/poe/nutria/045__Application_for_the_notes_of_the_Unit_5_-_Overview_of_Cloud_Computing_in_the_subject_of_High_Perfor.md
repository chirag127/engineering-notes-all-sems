

### Application for the notes of the Unit 5 - Overview of Cloud Computing in the subject of High Performance Computing

1. Cloud computing is a model for enabling convenient, on-demand network access to a shared pool of configurable computing resources (e.g., networks, servers, storage, applications, and services). 
2. It is a way of delivering computing services over the internet in the form of Infrastructure as a Service (IaaS), Platform as a Service (PaaS), and Software as a Service (SaaS). 
3. IaaS provides access to virtualized hardware such as storage, networking, and servers, while PaaS provides a platform for developers to create and deploy applications. 
4. SaaS provides access to applications over the internet. 
5. Cloud computing provides businesses with the ability to scale their computing resources quickly and easily, allowing them to respond quickly to changing market conditions. 
6. Cloud computing also offers businesses the opportunity to reduce their capital expenditure (CAPEX) and operating expenditure (OPEX). 
7. Cloud computing also offers advantages such as improved security, reliability, and flexibility. 
8. Cloud computing also offers businesses the opportunity to reduce their carbon footprint by using fewer physical servers and reducing energy consumption.
