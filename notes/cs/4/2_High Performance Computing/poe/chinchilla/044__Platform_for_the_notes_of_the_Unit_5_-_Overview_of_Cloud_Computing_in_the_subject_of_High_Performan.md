### Platform for the notes of the Unit 5 - Overview of Cloud Computing in the subject of High Performance Computing

Cloud computing has become an integral part of the modern IT landscape, with more and more organizations adopting cloud-based solutions to meet their computing needs. In this unit, we will explore the fundamentals of cloud computing and understand how it works.

To help you with your studies, we recommend the following platforms for taking notes and keeping track of your progress:

1. Google Docs: Google Docs is a cloud-based word processing platform that allows you to create and edit documents online. You can use it to take notes, organize your thoughts, and collaborate with others in real-time.

2. Evernote: Evernote is a note-taking app that lets you capture and organize your thoughts in a variety of formats, including text, images, and audio recordings. You can also use it to create to-do lists, set reminders, and share notes with others.

3. OneNote: OneNote is a digital notebook that allows you to capture and organize your ideas across multiple devices. You can use it to take notes, draw diagrams, and create to-do lists, among other things.

4. Trello: Trello is a project management tool that uses boards, lists, and cards to help you organize and prioritize your tasks. You can use it to track your progress, collaborate with others, and manage your workload.

5. Microsoft Teams: Microsoft Teams is a collaboration platform that allows you to chat, share files, and collaborate with others in real-time. You can use it to communicate with your classmates, share notes, and work on group projects.

In summary, there are several platforms available to help you with your studies in cloud computing. Whether you prefer to take notes in a word processor, a note-taking app, or a digital notebook, there is a platform that can meet your needs. Additionally, project management tools and collaboration platforms can help you stay organized and work effectively with your classmates.
