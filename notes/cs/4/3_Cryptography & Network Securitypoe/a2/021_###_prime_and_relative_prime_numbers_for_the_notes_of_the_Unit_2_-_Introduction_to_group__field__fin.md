### Prime and Relative Prime Numbers

- A prime number is a positive integer greater than 1 whose only factors are 1 and itself. For example, 2, 3, 5, 7, 11, 13, 17, etc. are prime numbers.
- Two numbers are said to be relatively prime if their greatest common divisor is 1. For example, 8 and 15 are not relatively prime because GCD(8, 15) = 1. But, 7 and 15 are relatively prime because GCD(7, 15) = 1.
- Knowing prime numbers and finding relative prime numbers are important in number theory and have applications in cryptography like RSA algorithm.
- Some mnemonics to remember first few prime numbers - "Inchworm: 2, 3, 5, 7, Eleven is heaven". Some tricks to find if a number is prime - Trial division, Wilson's theorem, Fermat primality test, etc.
