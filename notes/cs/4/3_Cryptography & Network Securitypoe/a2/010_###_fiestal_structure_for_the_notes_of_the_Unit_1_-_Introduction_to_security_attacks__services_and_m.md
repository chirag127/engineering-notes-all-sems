    -   Transposition ciphers: Rail fence, simple columnar transposition, Vernam cipher.

-   Steganography: Hiding information in images, audio, videos. Least significant bit insertion.
-   Stream ciphers: Key is used to generate a keystream which is XORed with plaintext to get cipher text. Difficult to implement securely.
-   Block ciphers: Encrypt data in blocks of fixed size. Principles of confusion and diffusion by Shannon.
-   Fiestel structure: Substitution-permutation network, Feistel function, Round function.
-   DES: Data Encryption Standard, strength, differential cryptanalysis.
-   Block cipher modes of operation: ECB, CBC, CFB, OFB, CTR.
