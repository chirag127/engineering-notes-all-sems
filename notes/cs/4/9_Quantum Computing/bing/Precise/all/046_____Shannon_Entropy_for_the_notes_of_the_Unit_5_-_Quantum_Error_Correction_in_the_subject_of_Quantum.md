# Shannon Entropy for the notes of the Unit 5 - Quantum Error Correction in the subject of Quantum Computing

- Shannon entropy is a concept that builds from classical information theory.
- It is used in quantum error correction mechanisms to correct bit-flip, phase-flip, and mixed errors that can typically occur in quantum circuits.
- The entropy is a function of the logarithm of the number of states of a system.
- This logarithm is equal to the number of bits required to uniquely identify the state of the system.
- In other words, it is the number of bits required to label a state.
- Shared entanglement can provide a simpler and more fundamental theory of quantum error correction.
