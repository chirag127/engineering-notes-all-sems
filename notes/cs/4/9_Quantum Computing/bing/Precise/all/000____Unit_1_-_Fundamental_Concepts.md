## Unit 1 - Fundamental Concepts

1. **Data** refers to raw, unorganized facts that need to be processed. Data can be something simple and seemingly random and useless until it is organized.
2. **Information** is data that has been processed, organized, structured or presented in a given context to make it useful.
3. **Knowledge** is the understanding and awareness gained through the analysis of information.
4. **Wisdom** is the ability to make sound judgments and decisions based on knowledge and experience.
5. **Processing** refers to the transformation of data into information through the use of various techniques such as sorting, filtering, and summarizing.
6. **Storage** refers to the retention of data and information for future use.
7. **Retrieval** refers to the process of accessing stored data and information.
8. **Communication** refers to the exchange of data, information, and knowledge between individuals or systems.
9. **Input** refers to the process of entering data into a computer system.
10. **Output** refers to the process of presenting information to the user in a readable or understandable format.
