# Entanglement as a physical resource

Entanglement is a physical resource that can be used to simplify the theory of quantum error correction. Here are some key points to note:

1. Entanglement shared between the encoder and decoder can simplify the theory of quantum error correction  .
2. Entanglement-assisted quantum codes do not require the dual-containing constraint necessary for standard quantum error-correcting codes   .
3. This allows us to “quantize” all of classical linear coding theory   .
4. Shared entanglement provides a simpler and more fundamental theory of quantum error correction .
5. Repetitive quantum error correction can protect entanglement .
6. The coherence time of the purified entangled logical qubit (ELQ) via error detection is improved by 45 compared with the unprotected ELQ and exceeds that of the entangled physical qubits .
7. Entanglement generated by quantum error correction circuits in trapped-ion processors can be certified .
8. This approach yields a significant increase in noise tolerance while being efficient .
