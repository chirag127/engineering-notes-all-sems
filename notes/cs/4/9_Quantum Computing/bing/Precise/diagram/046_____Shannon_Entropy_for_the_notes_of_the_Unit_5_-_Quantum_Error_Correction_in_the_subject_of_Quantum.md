### Shannon Entropy for the notes of the Unit 5 - Quantum Error Correction in the subject of Quantum Computing

1. Shannon's entropy is a concept that builds from classical information theory and is used in quantum error correction mechanisms.
2. It is used in correcting bit-flip, phase-flip, and mixed errors that can typically occur in quantum circuits .
3. The entropy is a function of the logarithm of the number of states of a system .
4. This logarithm is equal to the number of bits required to uniquely identify the state of the system .
5. Shared entanglement provides a simpler and more fundamental theory of quantum error correction .
6. The theory of quantum error-correcting codes (QECCs) was established as the primary tool for fighting decoherence in quantum computers and quantum communication systems .
