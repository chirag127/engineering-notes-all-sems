### Optical Photon Quantum Computer

- Optical Photon Quantum Computer is a paradigm of quantum computation that allows universal quantum computation. It is also known as Linear Optical Quantum Computing or Linear Optics Quantum Computation.
- This type of quantum computer uses photons as information carriers.
- It mainly uses linear optical elements or optical instruments to process quantum information.
- Photon detectors and quantum memories are used to detect and store quantum information.
- The classic approach to photonic quantum computing, linear optical quantum computing, relies on qubits each based on a single photon.
- This strategy manipulates photons with mirrors, beam splitters, and phase shifters.
- Single photon detectors are then used to help read the results of what these devices have done.
- In 2001, all-optical quantum computing became feasible with the discovery that scalable quantum computing is possible using only single-photon sources, linear optical elements, and single-photon detectors.
- Although it was in principle scalable, the massive resource overhead made the scheme practically daunting.
