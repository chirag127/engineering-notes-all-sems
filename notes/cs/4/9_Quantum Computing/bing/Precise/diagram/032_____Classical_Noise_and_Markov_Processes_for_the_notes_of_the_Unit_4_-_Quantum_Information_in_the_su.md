### Classical Noise and Markov Processes

- Noise in classical systems can be described using the theory of stochastic processes.
- Markov processes are often used in the analysis of multi-stage processes.
- A Markov process is a type of stochastic process where the probability of each event depends only on the state attained in the previous event.
- The theory of classical Markov processes is well known in the probability community.
- A classical Markov semigroup of transition operators can be generated from a classical Markov process.
- Given a Markov semigroup, a Markov process can be constructed based on the Markov semigroup using the Kolmogorov consistency theorem.
- Processes that cannot produce coherence and destroy any coherence that is fed in form a strict subset of processes that appear classical when sequentially probed in the computational basis.
