### Examples of Quantum noise and Quantum Operations

Quantum noise is due to the apparently discrete nature of the small quantum constituents such as electrons, as well as the discrete nature of quantum effects, such as photocurrents . Quantum noise is similar to classical noise theory and will not always return an asymmetric spectral density .

Quantum operations describe not only unitary time evolution or symmetry transformations of isolated systems, but also the effects of measurement and transient interactions with an environment . In the context of quantum computation, a quantum operation is called a quantum channel .

One example of quantum noise is quantum decoherence, or noise as it is commonly called . Another example of quantum noise is the behavior of a special quantum system that produces a type of noise that cannot be directly composed using the predefined noise channels. In this case, it is possible to define noise channels with a list of Kraus operators .

An example of a quantum operation is a unitary transformation on the input state of a closed quantum system . Another example is a measurement operation on the input state of an open quantum system .
