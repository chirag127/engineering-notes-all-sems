## Unit 1 - Fundamental Concepts

1. **Data** refers to raw, unorganized facts that need to be processed. Data can be something simple and seemingly random and useless until it is organized.

2. **Information** is data that has been processed, organized, structured or presented in a given context to make it useful.

3. **Knowledge** is the understanding and awareness of information. It is the body of facts, principles, information and skills acquired through experience or education.

4. **Wisdom** is the ability to think and act using knowledge, experience, understanding, common sense and insight.

5. **Processing** refers to the transformation of data into information. This can be done through various methods such as sorting, filtering, analyzing and summarizing.

6. **Storage** refers to the retention of data and information for future use. This can be done through various methods such as saving to a hard drive, uploading to the cloud or writing to a physical medium such as a CD or USB drive.

7. **Retrieval** refers to the process of accessing stored data and information. This can be done through various methods such as searching, querying or browsing.

8. **Communication** refers to the exchange of data, information, knowledge and wisdom between individuals or systems. This can be done through various methods such as speaking, writing, signaling or transmitting.

9. **Representation** refers to the way in which data, information, knowledge and wisdom are presented or expressed. This can be done through various methods such as text, images, audio or video.

10. **Computation** refers to the use of mathematical and logical operations to process data and information. This can be done through various methods such as arithmetic, algebra or calculus.
