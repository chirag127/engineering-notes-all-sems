## Unit 2 - Quantum Computation

Quantum computation is a field of study that focuses on the development of computer-based technologies centered around the principles of quantum theory. Quantum theory explains the nature and behavior of energy and matter on the quantum (atomic and subatomic) level.

Some key points to consider when studying quantum computation are:

1. Quantum computers use quantum bits, or qubits, which can exist in multiple states simultaneously, unlike classical bits that can only exist in one of two states (0 or 1).
2. Quantum algorithms can solve certain problems faster than classical algorithms due to the ability of qubits to exist in multiple states simultaneously.
3. Quantum computers can perform certain calculations much faster than classical computers due to the phenomenon of quantum entanglement.
4. Quantum error correction is necessary to protect quantum information from errors due to decoherence and other sources of noise.
5. Quantum cryptography allows for secure communication based on the principles of quantum mechanics.

These are just a few of the many topics that are covered in the study of quantum computation. It is a rapidly evolving field with many exciting developments and potential applications.
