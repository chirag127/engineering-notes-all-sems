### Basic properties of Entropy for the notes of the Unit 5 - Quantum Error Correction in the subject of Quantum Computing

1. Entropy is a measure of the disorder or randomness of a system.
2. The second law of thermodynamics states that the entropy of an isolated system will always increase over time.
3. The change in entropy is equal to the heat transferred divided by the temperature of the system.
4. Entropy is a state function, meaning that its value depends only on the current state of the system and not on the path taken to reach that state.
5. The entropy of a system can be calculated using the Boltzmann formula, which relates the entropy to the number of microstates available to the system.
6. In quantum mechanics, the von Neumann entropy is used to measure the entropy of a quantum system.
7. The von Neumann entropy is defined as the negative of the trace of the density matrix of the system multiplied by the logarithm of the density matrix.
8. The concept of entropy is important in the study of quantum error correction, as it can be used to quantify the amount of information that is lost due to errors in the system.
