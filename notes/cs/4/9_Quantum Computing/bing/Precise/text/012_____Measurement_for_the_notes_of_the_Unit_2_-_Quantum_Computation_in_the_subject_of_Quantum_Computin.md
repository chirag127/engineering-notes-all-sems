### Measurement

Measurement is an important concept in quantum computation. Here are some key points to remember:

1. In quantum mechanics, measurement is the process of observing a physical quantity of a quantum system.
2. Measurement causes the system to collapse into one of its eigenstates, with a probability determined by the state's wave function.
3. The result of a measurement is one of the eigenvalues of the observable being measured.
4. The act of measurement changes the state of the system, and this is known as the collapse of the wave function.
5. In quantum computation, measurement is used to extract information from a quantum system, and is an essential part of many quantum algorithms.
6. Measurement can be performed on individual qubits or on multiple qubits simultaneously.
7. The outcome of a measurement is probabilistic, and the probabilities are determined by the state of the system before the measurement.
8. Repeated measurements on the same system will generally produce different results, due to the probabilistic nature of quantum mechanics.

These are some of the key points to remember about measurement in the context of quantum computation. It is an essential concept to understand for anyone studying quantum computing.
