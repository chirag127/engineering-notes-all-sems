### Entropy and Information

Entropy is a central concept in both classical and quantum information theory. It measures the uncertainty and the information content in the state of a physical system .

- **Classical Information Theory**: This theory is reviewed and then generalized into quantum information theory .
- **Shannon and Von Neumann Entropy**: Both of these entropies are discussed, making the connection to the compressibility of a message stream and the generalization of compressibility in a quantum system .
- **Application of Von Neumann Entropy**: The application of Von Neumann entropy in entanglement of formation for both pure and mixed bipartite quantum states is considered .
