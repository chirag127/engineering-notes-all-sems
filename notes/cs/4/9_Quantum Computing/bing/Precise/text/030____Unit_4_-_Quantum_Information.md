## Unit 4 - Quantum Information

1. Quantum information is the information of the state of a quantum system. It is the basic entity of study in quantum information theory, and can be manipulated using quantum information processing techniques.
2. Quantum information refers to both the technical definition in terms of Von Neumann entropy and the general computational term.
3. Quantum information, like classical information, can be processed using digital computers, transmitted from one location to another, manipulated with algorithms, and analyzed with computer science and mathematics.
4. Just like the basic unit of classical information is the bit, quantum information deals with qubits.
5. Quantum information science aims to explore the nature of information at the quantum level, a world in which bits can be both zero and one at the same time and perfect copying is impossible.
6. At the practical level, quantum information powers forms of secure communication that are provably impossible in a “classical” world.
