### Stabilizer codes for the notes of the Unit 5 - Quantum Error Correction in the subject of Quantum Computing

- Stabilizer codes are a subclass of quantum error-correcting codes that use a group-theoretical structure to protect quantum states from local noisy errors  .
- Stabilizer codes append ancilla qubits to the qubits that we want to protect, and use a unitary encoding circuit to rotate the global state into a subspace of a larger Hilbert space .
- The subspace is highly entangled and invariant under the action of a subgroup of the Pauli group, called the stabilizer group. The stabilizer group generates the code space and specifies the error syndrome measurement  .
- Stabilizer codes can be constructed from classical binary or quaternary codes, as long as they satisfy the dual-containing or self-orthogonality constraint. This means that the code space and its dual space have trivial intersection .
- Stabilizer codes can be encoded and decoded using quantum circuits composed of Clifford gates, which preserve the stabilizer group. They can also be manipulated using fault-tolerant quantum computation techniques  .
- Stabilizer codes have many advantages, such as:
  - They are easy to describe and analyze using the stabilizer formalism .
  - They can correct a large number of errors, depending on the code parameters .
  - They can be concatenated to achieve arbitrarily low error rates, as long as the basic error rate is below some threshold value .
  - They can be used for entanglement-assisted quantum communication, which improves the error correction capability compared to codes that do not use preshared entanglement .
- Stabilizer codes also have some disadvantages, such as:
  - They are not optimal for all types of quantum channels, and may be outperformed by other quantum codes .
  - They are limited by the no-cloning theorem, which prevents them from correcting arbitrary errors .
  - They require a large number of ancilla qubits and quantum gates, which may increase the complexity and resource consumption of the quantum system  .
