Qubits are the basic units of quantum information, analogous to bits in classical computing. They can exist in superposition of two states, usually denoted as |0> and |1>. Quantum gates are operations that manipulate one or more qubits, such as the NOT, CNOT, or Hadamard gates. Quantum measurements are the process of extracting classical information from qubits, which usually collapses their quantum state to a definite value.
