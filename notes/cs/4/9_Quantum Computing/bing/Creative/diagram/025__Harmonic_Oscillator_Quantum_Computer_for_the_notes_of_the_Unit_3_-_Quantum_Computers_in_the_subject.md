A harmonic oscillator quantum computer is a hypothetical device that would use quantum harmonic oscillators as qubits. A quantum harmonic oscillator is the quantum-mechanical analog of the classical harmonic oscillator, which is a particle subject to a restoring force that is proportional to the displacement of the particle. A quantum harmonic oscillator can exist in discrete energy levels, and can be manipulated by applying external fields or coupling to other oscillators. A harmonic oscillator quantum computer would exploit these properties to perform quantum logic operations and quantum algorithms.
