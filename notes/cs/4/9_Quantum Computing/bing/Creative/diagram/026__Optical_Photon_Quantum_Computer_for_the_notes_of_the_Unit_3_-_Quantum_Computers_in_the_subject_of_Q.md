According to Wikipedia, Optical Photon Quantum Computing is a paradigm of quantum computation that uses photons as information carriers, mainly uses linear optical elements to process quantum information, and uses photon detectors and quantum memories to detect and store quantum information.

One possible architecture for Optical Photon Quantum Computing is based on the following components:

- A laser source that generates coherent light
- A beam splitter that splits the light into two paths
- A pair of optical switches that control the routing of the light
- An optical cavity that contains a single atom that can interact with the light
- A pair of photon detectors that measure the state of the light

The basic idea is to use the laser to manipulate the atom, which can then modify the state of the photons via quantum teleportation. The atom can be reset and reused for many quantum gates, eliminating the need to build multiple distinct physical gates.
