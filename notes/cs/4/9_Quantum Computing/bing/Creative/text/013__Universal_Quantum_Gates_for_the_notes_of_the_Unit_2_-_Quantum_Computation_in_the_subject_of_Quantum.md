### Universal Quantum Gates

- Quantum gates are the basic building blocks of quantum circuits, like classical logic gates are for conventional digital circuits. 
- Quantum gates are unitary operators, and are described as unitary matrices relative to some basis. 
- A set of universal quantum gates is any set of gates to which any operation possible on a quantum computer can be reduced.
