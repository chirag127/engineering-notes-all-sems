## Unit 1 - Fundamental Concepts
Fundamental Concepts Unit 1 covers the basic building blocks of computer science, including algorithms, data structures, and programming languages. It introduces students to the concepts of computational thinking, problem-solving, and the basics of programming. Topics include the study of algorithms, their design and analysis, and the use of data structures such as arrays, linked lists, trees, and graphs. The unit also covers the basics of programming languages, including syntax, semantics, and grammar.
