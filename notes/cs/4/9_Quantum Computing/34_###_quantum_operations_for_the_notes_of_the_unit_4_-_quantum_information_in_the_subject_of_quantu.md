### Quantum Operations for the notes of the Unit 4 - Quantum Information in the subject of Quantum Computing
Quantum operations are mathematical operations applied on quantum states to produce a desired output. They are unitary transformations that preserve the norm of the state vector. Examples of quantum operations include:

1. Quantum Gates: such as X, Y, Z, Hadamard, CNOT, etc.

2. Measurements: including projective, POVM, and weak measurements.

3. State preparation: including preparation of pure and mixed states.

4. Quantum channels: including depolarizing, amplitude damping, and phase damping channels.

5. Quantum error correction: including repetition, Shor, and Steane codes.

Quantum operations are the building blocks for quantum algorithms and protocols, and their understanding is crucial for the development of quantum information processing.
