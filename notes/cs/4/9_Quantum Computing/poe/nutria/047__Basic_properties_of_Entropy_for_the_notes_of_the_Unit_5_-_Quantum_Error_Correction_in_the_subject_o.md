
### Basic Properties of Entropy

1. Entropy is a measure of the amount of disorder or uncertainty in a system.
2. In the context of quantum computing, entropy is used to measure the amount of information that is stored in a quantum system.
3. Entropy is a function of the probability of each state in the system. The higher the probability of a state, the lower the entropy.
4. Entropy is related to the number of possible states a system can have. A system with more possible states has higher entropy.
5. Entropy is also related to the number of qubits in a system. The more qubits, the higher the entropy.
6. Entropy is also related to the amount of noise in a system. The more noise, the higher the entropy.
7. Entropy can be used to measure the amount of information that is stored in a quantum system. This can be used to measure the efficiency of a quantum error correction code.
8. Entropy can also be used to measure the amount of energy that is stored in a system. This can be used to measure the amount of energy that is needed to perform a quantum computation.
