
### Measurement for the Notes of Unit 2 - Quantum Computation in Quantum Computing

1. Measurement is an essential part of quantum computing, as it is the only way to observe the state of a quantum system.
2. Measurement in quantum computing is a probabilistic process, meaning that the outcome of a measurement is not always certain.
3. Measurement in quantum computing is a non-destructive process, meaning that the state of the system is not changed by the measurement.
4. Measurement in quantum computing is a process that can be repeated multiple times, meaning that the same measurement can be performed on a system multiple times.
5. Measurement in quantum computing is a process that is not reversible, meaning that the outcome of a measurement cannot be undone.
6. Measurement in quantum computing is a process that can be used to determine the state of a quantum system, meaning that the outcome of a measurement can be used to infer information about the state of the system.
7. Measurement in quantum computing is a process that can be used to manipulate the state of a quantum system, meaning that the outcome of a measurement can be used to change the state of the system.
