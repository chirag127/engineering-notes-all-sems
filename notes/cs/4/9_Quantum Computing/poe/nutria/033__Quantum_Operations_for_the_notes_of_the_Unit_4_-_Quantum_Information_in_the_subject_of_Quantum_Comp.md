
### Quantum Operations 

1. Unitary Operations: Unitary operations are operations that preserve the norm of a quantum state. They are represented by unitary matrices and can be used to create arbitrary quantum states. Examples of unitary operations include Hadamard gates, CNOT gates, and Toffoli gates. 

2. Measurement Operations: Measurement operations are operations that measure the state of a quantum system. These operations are represented by a set of projectors and are used to determine the state of a quantum system. Examples of measurement operations include Pauli measurements, Stern-Gerlach measurements, and von Neumann measurements. 

3. Control Operations: Control operations are operations that control the flow of information in a quantum system. These operations are represented by a set of control matrices and are used to manipulate the state of a quantum system. Examples of control operations include phase gates, swap gates, and entangling gates. 

4. Decoherence Operations: Decoherence operations are operations that reduce the coherence of a quantum system. These operations are represented by a set of decoherence matrices and are used to reduce the coherence of a quantum system. Examples of decoherence operations include amplitude damping, dephasing, and thermalization.
