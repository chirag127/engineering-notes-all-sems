
### Quantum Information for the notes of the Unit 1 - Fundamental Concepts in the subject of Quantum Computing

1. Quantum information is the information that is encoded in the quantum states of a physical system. It is a branch of quantum physics and quantum computing that studies how to store, manipulate, and measure information using quantum systems. 

2. Quantum information is different from classical information in many ways. It can exist in a superposition of states, meaning that it can exist in multiple states at the same time. It can also be entangled with other quantum systems, meaning that the two systems can share information without any physical contact. 

3. Quantum information can be used to create powerful algorithms for solving complex problems. For example, quantum computers can solve certain problems much faster than classical computers, and can even solve problems that are impossible for classical computers. 

4. Quantum information is also used in cryptography, as it is much harder to eavesdrop on a quantum communication than a classical one. 

5. Quantum information can also be used to create secure communication networks, as it is much harder to hack into a quantum network than a classical one. 

6. Finally, quantum information can be used to create new technologies, such as quantum computers, quantum sensors, and quantum cryptography.
