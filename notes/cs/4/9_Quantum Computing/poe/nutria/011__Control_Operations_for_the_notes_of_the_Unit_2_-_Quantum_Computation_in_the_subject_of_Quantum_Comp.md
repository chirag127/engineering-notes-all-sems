
### Control Operations for Unit 2 - Quantum Computation

1. **Quantum Control** is the process of manipulating the quantum state of a system in order to achieve a desired outcome. This is done using a combination of classical and quantum operations. 
2. **Classical Control** is the process of manipulating the classical state of a system in order to achieve a desired outcome. This is done using a combination of classical operations.
3. **Quantum Gates** are the fundamental building blocks of quantum computation. They are used to manipulate the quantum state of a system and to control the flow of information.
4. **Quantum Circuits** are the structures used to represent quantum computations. They consist of a set of quantum gates connected in a specific way.
5. **Quantum Measurement** is the process of measuring the state of a quantum system. This is done by performing a measurement on the system and obtaining a result.
6. **Quantum Error Correction** is the process of correcting errors that occur during the computation. This is done by using redundant information to detect and correct errors.
7. **Quantum Algorithms** are algorithms that are designed to take advantage of the unique properties of quantum systems. These algorithms can be used to solve problems that are not efficiently solvable using classical algorithms.
