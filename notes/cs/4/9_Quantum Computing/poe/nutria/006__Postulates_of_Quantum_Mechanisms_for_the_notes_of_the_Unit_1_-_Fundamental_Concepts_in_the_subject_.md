
### Postulates of Quantum Mechanisms

1. Wave-Particle Duality: The wave-particle duality states that all matter exhibits both wave-like and particle-like properties. This means that particles can be described as waves, and vice versa. 

2. Heisenberg Uncertainty Principle: This principle states that it is impossible to simultaneously measure both the position and momentum of a particle with absolute precision. This means that the more precisely one of these properties is known, the less precisely the other can be known. 

3. Pauli Exclusion Principle: This principle states that no two electrons in an atom can have the same set of quantum numbers. This means that electrons must occupy different energy levels, and therefore different orbitals, within an atom. 

4. Superposition Principle: This principle states that a system can exist in multiple states at the same time. This means that a system can be in multiple places at once, or have multiple values of momentum or energy at once. 

5. Entanglement: This is a phenomenon in which two particles become linked together so that a change in the state of one particle will cause a change in the state of the other particle, regardless of the distance between them. This means that two particles can be connected, even if they are separated by large distances.
