
### Classical Noise and Markov Processes

* Classical noise is a type of random noise that is created by external sources and is characterized by a broad spectrum of frequencies.
* Markov processes are stochastic processes in which the future state of a system depends only on its current state.
* In quantum computing, noise can be used to model the effects of decoherence on quantum systems.
* Markov processes can be used to model the dynamics of quantum systems, as well as to simulate the effects of noise on quantum systems.
* Noise can be used to create randomness in a quantum system, which can be used to generate random numbers or to create randomness for cryptographic protocols.
* Noise can also be used to model the effects of environmental noise on the performance of quantum algorithms.
