
### Quantum Algorithms for Unit 2 - Quantum Computation

1. Shor's Algorithm: Shor's algorithm is a quantum algorithm for factoring large integers and finding discrete logarithms. It was developed by Peter Shor in 1994 and is the most efficient known algorithm for these tasks.

2. Grover's Algorithm: Grover's algorithm is a quantum algorithm for searching an unsorted database. It was developed by Lov Grover in 1996 and is the most efficient known algorithm for this task.

3. Deutsch-Jozsa Algorithm: The Deutsch-Jozsa algorithm is a quantum algorithm for determining whether a Boolean function is constant or balanced. It was developed by David Deutsch and Richard Jozsa in 1992 and is the most efficient known algorithm for this task.

4. Quantum Fourier Transform: The quantum Fourier transform is a quantum algorithm for computing the discrete Fourier transform of a function. It was developed by Peter Shor in 1994 and is the most efficient known algorithm for this task.

5. Quantum Phase Estimation Algorithm: The quantum phase estimation algorithm is a quantum algorithm for estimating the eigenvalues of a unitary operator. It was developed by Lov Grover and Peter Shor in 1996 and is the most efficient known algorithm for this task.
