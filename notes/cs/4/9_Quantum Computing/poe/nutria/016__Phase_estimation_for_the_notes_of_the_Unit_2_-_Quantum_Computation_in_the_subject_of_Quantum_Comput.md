
### Phase Estimation for Quantum Computation

* Phase estimation is a quantum algorithm that can be used to estimate the eigenvalues of a given unitary matrix. 
* The basic idea behind the algorithm is to use a quantum circuit to prepare a superposition of the eigenstates of the matrix. 
* Then, by measuring the output of the circuit, we can obtain an estimate of the eigenvalues. 
* This algorithm has been used to solve a variety of problems in quantum computing, including quantum phase estimation, quantum simulation, and quantum optimization. 
* Phase estimation can also be used to estimate the energy of a quantum system, which is important for applications such as quantum chemistry. 
* In addition, the algorithm can be used to estimate the time evolution of a quantum system, which is useful for studying the dynamics of quantum systems.
