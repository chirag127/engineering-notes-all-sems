
### Introduction to Unit 5 - Quantum Error Correction

1. Quantum error correction is a technique used to protect quantum information from errors caused by external noise or decoherence.

2. It uses encoding, error detection, and error correction to ensure that the quantum state remains intact.

3. Quantum error correction codes are based on classical error correction codes and use additional qubits to encode the quantum information.

4. Quantum error correction codes can be used to protect quantum information from errors caused by noise and decoherence.

5. Quantum error correction codes can also be used to reduce the amount of time needed to perform a computation.

6. Quantum error correction codes can be used to increase the accuracy of a quantum computation by reducing the probability of errors.
