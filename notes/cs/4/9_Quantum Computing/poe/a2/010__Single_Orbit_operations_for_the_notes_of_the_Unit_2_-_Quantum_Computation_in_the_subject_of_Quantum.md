### Single Orbit operations for the notes of the Unit 2 - Quantum Computation

1. Hadamard Gate: It puts a qubit into an equal superposition of |0> and |1> states. It is a single-qubit gate and is denoted by H.

2. Phase Shift Gate: It introduces a phase difference between the |0> and |1> states of a qubit. It is a single-qubit gate and is denoted by S.

3. Pauli Gates: They are a set of single-qubit gates that include I, X, Y and Z gates.
- I gate: It is the identity gate and does nothing to the qubit state.
- X gate: It flips the state of the qubit. |0> -> |1> and |1> -> |0>
- Y gate: It introduces a pi/2 phase shift between the |0> and |1> states.
- Z gate: It flips the sign of the |1> state. |0> -> |0> and |1> -> -|1>

4. CNOT Gate: It is a two-qubit gate that flips the state of the target qubit if the control qubit is |1> and does nothing if the control qubit is |0>. It is used to create entanglement between two qubits.

5. Toffoli Gate: It is a three-qubit gate that flips the state of the target qubit if both the control qubits are |1> and does nothing otherwise. It is a universal quantum gate and can be used to implement any quantum operation.
