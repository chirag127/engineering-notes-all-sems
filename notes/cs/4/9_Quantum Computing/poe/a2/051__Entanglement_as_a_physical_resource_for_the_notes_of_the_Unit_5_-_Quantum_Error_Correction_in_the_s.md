### Entanglement as a physical resource for the notes of the Unit 5 - Quantum Error Correction in the subject of Quantum Computing

1. Entanglement is a quantum mechanical phenomenon in which the quantum states of two or more objects have to be described with reference to each other, even though the individual objects may be spatially separated. This leads to correlations between observable physical properties of the systems.
2. For example, if two particles are entangled, then measuring a property of one particle instantly affects the other, no matter if they are separated by a large distance.
3. Entanglement is a necessary ingredient for many proposed quantum information processing tasks, such as quantum teleportation, superdense coding, and quantum key distribution.
4. The no-cloning theorem shows that quantum information cannot be copied. However, quantum information can be moved from one system to another. This transfer can be done using entanglement as a physical resource.
5. The quantum systems used for information processing tasks are prone to decoherence and other errors. To build practical quantum computers and quantum communication systems, quantum error correction is required to detect and correct these errors. Entanglement is a key resource for most quantum error correction schemes.
6. In summary, entanglement is a fundamental physical resource in quantum information science. It is required for many quantum information processing tasks as well as for quantum error correction to build practical quantum technologies.
