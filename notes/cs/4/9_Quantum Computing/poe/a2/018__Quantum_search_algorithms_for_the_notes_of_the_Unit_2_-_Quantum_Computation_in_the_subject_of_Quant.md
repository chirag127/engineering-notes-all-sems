### Quantum search algorithms

**Grover's algorithm**:

- Takes square root of N queries to find a marked item in an unstructured database of N items.
- Uses quantum mechanics to amplify the probability amplitude of the marked state.
- Needs an oracle to identify the marked state.
- Can be used for unstructured search, optimization, machine learning, etc.

**Quantum approximate optimization algorithm (QAOA)**:

- Used to find approximate solutions to combinatorial optimization problems.
- Uses a sequence of quantum operations (mixing unitaries) interleaved with measurements to find a good approximate solution.
- The number of mixing unitaries controls the trade-off between solution quality and runtime.
- Shows quantum speedup over classical algorithms for some problems like MaxCut.

**Quantum singular value transformation (QSVT)**:

- Uses quantum phase estimation to estimate the singular values of a matrix.
- The number of qubits controls the precision of the estimation.
- Can be used to accelerate the solution of linear systems and other algorithms that depend on singular values.
- Shows a quantum speedup when the condition number of the matrix is very large.
