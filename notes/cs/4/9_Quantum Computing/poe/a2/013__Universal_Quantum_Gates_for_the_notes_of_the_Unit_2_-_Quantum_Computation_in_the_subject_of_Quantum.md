### Universal Quantum Gates for the notes of the Unit 2 - Quantum Computation in the subject of Quantum Computing:

1. Hadamard Gate: It is a single-qubit gate that puts a qubit into an equal superposition of states. It is represented by H.
2. Phase Shift Gate: It is a single-qubit gate that introduces a phase shift in the qubit state. It is represented by S.
3. CNOT Gate: It is a two-qubit gate that flips the target qubit if and only if the control qubit is 1. It is also called as the Controlled-NOT gate.
4. Toffoli Gate: It is a three-qubit gate that flips the target qubit if and only if both the control qubits are 1. It is a universal quantum gate and can implement any Boolean function.

These gates can be combined to implement any quantum algorithm. A universal set of quantum gates is a minimum set of gates that can be used to construct any quantum circuit. The CNOT and Hadamard gates form a universal set. The Toffoli gate can also act as a universal quantum gate.
