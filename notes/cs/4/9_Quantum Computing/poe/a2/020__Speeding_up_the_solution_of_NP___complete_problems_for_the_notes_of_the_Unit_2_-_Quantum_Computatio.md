### Speeding up the solution of NP – complete problems

1. NP-complete problems: Problems whose solutions cannot be verified in polynomial time. Ex: Travelling salesman problem, Boolean satisfiability problem, etc.
2. Quantum computers can speed up the solution of NP-complete problems using quantum algorithms like Quantum Approximate Optimization Algorithm (QAOA) and Quantum Adiabatic Optimization (QAO).
3. In QAOA, the solution is approximated by preparing a quantum state that encodes candidate solutions and applying alternate sequences of quantum gate and driving Hamiltonian. The final measurement gives an approximate solution.
4. In QAO, the system is allowed to evolve gradually under a time-dependent Hamiltonian. The ground state of the final Hamiltonian encodes the solution. The adiabatic theorem ensures that if the evolution is sufficiently slow, the system remains in the ground state.
5. Though quantum algorithms do not provide guaranteed solutions, they can provide candidate solutions that can be further refined using classical algorithms to obtain the exact solution. This results in a speed up compared to solely using classical algorithms.
6. The resource requirements of these quantum algorithms are also quite low in terms of qubits and quantum gates, making them more feasible for near-term quantum computers.
