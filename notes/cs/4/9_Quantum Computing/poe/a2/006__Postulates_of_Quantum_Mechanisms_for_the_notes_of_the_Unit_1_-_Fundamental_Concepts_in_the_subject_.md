### Postulates of Quantum Mechanisms

1. Quantum superposition: A quantum system can exist in a superposition of multiple states until it is measured. For example, a qubit can be in a superposition of states "0" and "1" at once.
2. Quantum Entanglement: Quantum particles can be "entangled" so their properties are dependent on each other, even if they are separated by a large distance. An action on one system has an instantaneous effect on the other.
3. Measurement causes collapse: The act of measuring a quantum system forces it to collapse from a set of probabilities to a single state. So we can never know exactly what state a quantum system is in, we can only calculate the probability of different outcomes.
4. Probabilistic outcomes: Quantum mechanics deals with probabilities. We can't predict with certainty the outcome of a measurement or observation. We can only calculate the probability of different outcomes.
5. Wave-particle duality: Matter and light exhibit both particle-like and wave-like characteristics. This wave-particle duality is a key concept in quantum mechanics.
