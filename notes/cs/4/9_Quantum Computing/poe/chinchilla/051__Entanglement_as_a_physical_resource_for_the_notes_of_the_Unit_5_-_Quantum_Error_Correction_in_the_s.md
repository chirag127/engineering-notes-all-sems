#### Entanglement

Entanglement is a fundamental concept in Quantum Mechanics that refers to the correlation between two or more quantum systems. When two or more quantum systems are entangled, their state becomes intrinsically linked, regardless of the distance between them. Entanglement is a non-classical phenomenon that cannot be explained by classical physics. 

#### Entanglement as a Resource for Quantum Error Correction

In Quantum Error Correction, entanglement is used as a resource to protect quantum information from errors. The basic idea behind Quantum Error Correction is to encode quantum information into a larger quantum system, which is then protected from errors using entanglement.
