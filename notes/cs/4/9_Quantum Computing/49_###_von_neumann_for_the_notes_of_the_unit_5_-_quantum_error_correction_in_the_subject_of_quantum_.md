### Von Neumann for the notes of the Unit 5 - Quantum Error Correction in the subject of Quantum Computing
John von Neumann was a Hungarian-American mathematician and physicist who made significant contributions to the fields of quantum mechanics and computer science. He is best known for his work on the mathematical foundations of quantum mechanics and his development of the concept of a stored-program computer. Von Neumann's work laid the foundations for quantum error correction, which is a crucial component of quantum computing. He introduced the concept of a quantum logic gate and showed that quantum systems could be described using linear algebra. Von Neumann also wrote extensively on the theory of self-replicating automata and their potential applications in computer science. He died in 1957 at the age of 53.
