### Applications for the notes of the Unit 2 - Quantum Computation in the subject of Quantum Computing
Applications for the notes of the Unit 2 - Quantum Computation in the subject of Quantum Computing include:

1. Cryptography: Quantum computers can be used to crack traditional encryption methods.

2. Drug Discovery: Quantum computing can be used to simulate chemical reactions and predict the properties of potential new drugs.

3. Supply Chain Optimization: Quantum computing can be used to optimize the logistics of supply chain management.

4. Financial Modeling: Quantum computing can be used to simulate financial markets and make predictions about market trends.

5. Machine Learning: Quantum computing can be used to speed up training of machine learning algorithms.
