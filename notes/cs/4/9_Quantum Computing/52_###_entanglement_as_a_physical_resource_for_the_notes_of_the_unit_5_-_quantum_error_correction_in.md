### Entanglement as a physical resource for the notes of the Unit 5 - Quantum Error Correction in the subject of Quantum Computing
Entanglement is a quantum phenomenon where two or more particles become correlated in a way that the state of one particle cannot be described independently of the other(s). It is considered a physical resource for quantum computing because it enables quantum information to be transmitted and processed in a way that is secure and robust against errors.

In quantum error correction, entanglement is used to detect and correct errors that occur during quantum computation. This is achieved by encoding the quantum information into multiple entangled particles, so that if one particle experiences an error, the state of the other particles can be used to reconstruct the original state.

Entanglement also plays a key role in quantum teleportation, where quantum information can be transmitted from one location to another without physically moving the information-carrying particle.

Overall, entanglement is a crucial resource for quantum computing, enabling quantum information to be transmitted, processed, and protected against errors.
