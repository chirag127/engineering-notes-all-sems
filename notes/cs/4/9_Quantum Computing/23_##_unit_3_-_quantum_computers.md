## Unit 3 - Quantum Computers
Unit 3 - Quantum Computers:
Quantum computers are a type of computer that uses quantum mechanics to store and process information. Unlike classical computers, which use bits to store information, quantum computers use quantum bits, or qubits. Qubits can be in multiple states at once, which allows quantum computers to perform certain calculations much faster than classical computers. Quantum computers are still in their infancy and are mainly used for research purposes, but they have the potential to revolutionize many fields, including cryptography, drug discovery, and machine learning. However, developing practical quantum computers is a challenging task, as they are highly sensitive to their environment and require specialized hardware and software to operate.
