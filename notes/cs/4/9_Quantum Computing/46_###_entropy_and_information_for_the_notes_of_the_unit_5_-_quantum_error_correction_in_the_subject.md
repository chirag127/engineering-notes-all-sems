### Entropy and information for the notes of the Unit 5 - Quantum Error Correction in the subject of Quantum Computing
Entropy:
- A measure of the amount of uncertainty or randomness in a system.
- In quantum computing, entropy is used to quantify the amount of quantum information in a quantum state.

Information:
- The amount of data or knowledge about a system.
- In quantum computing, information is stored in quantum bits (qubits) and can be manipulated through quantum operations.

Quantum Error Correction:
- A technique used to protect quantum information from errors due to noise and other sources of decoherence.
- This is achieved by encoding the information into multiple qubits and detecting and correcting errors through quantum operations.
