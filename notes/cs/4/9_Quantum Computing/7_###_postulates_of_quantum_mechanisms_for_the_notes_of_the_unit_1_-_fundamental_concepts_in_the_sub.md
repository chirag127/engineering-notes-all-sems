### Postulates of Quantum Mechanisms for the notes of the Unit 1 - Fundamental Concepts in the subject of Quantum Computing
The postulates of quantum mechanics include:

1. State vector: The state of a quantum system is described by a state vector in a complex Hilbert space.

2. Observables: Physical quantities, such as position and momentum, are represented by operators in the Hilbert space.

3. Time evolution: The state vector evolves in time according to the Schrödinger equation.

4. Measurement: Measurement of an observable results in a definite outcome, with probabilities given by the Born rule.

5. Superposition: A quantum system can exist in multiple states simultaneously, known as superposition.

6. Entanglement: Two or more quantum systems can be correlated, known as entanglement, leading to non-local effects.

7. Wave-particle duality: Particles exhibit both wave-like and particle-like behavior.

These postulates form the foundation of quantum mechanics and are essential for understanding quantum computing.
