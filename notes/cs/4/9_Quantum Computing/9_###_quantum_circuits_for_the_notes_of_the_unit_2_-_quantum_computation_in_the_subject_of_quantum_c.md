### Quantum Circuits for the notes of the Unit 2 - Quantum Computation in the subject of Quantum Computing
Quantum Circuits:

- A quantum circuit is a mathematical model for quantum computation, consisting of quantum gates and quantum wires.

- Quantum gates are unitary operations that can be applied to quantum bits (qubits) to manipulate their state.

- Quantum wires are used to connect quantum gates and transmit quantum information between them.

- Quantum circuits can be used to perform various quantum algorithms, such as Grover's algorithm and Shor's algorithm.

- Quantum circuits are represented diagrammatically, with quantum gates represented by boxes and quantum wires represented by lines connecting the boxes.

- The behavior of a quantum circuit can be simulated using classical computers, but its full power can only be realized on a real quantum computer.

- The design of quantum circuits is an active area of research, as scientists and engineers work to develop more efficient and effective circuit designs for quantum computation.
