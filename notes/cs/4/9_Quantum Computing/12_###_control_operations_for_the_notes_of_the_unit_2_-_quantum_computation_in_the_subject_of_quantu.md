### Control Operations for the notes of the Unit 2 - Quantum Computation in the subject of Quantum Computing
Control operations in quantum computing are used to manipulate the state of a quantum system. They allow for the implementation of conditional logic in quantum algorithms and the creation of quantum circuits with multiple inputs and outputs. 

1. Controlled NOT (CNOT) gate: This gate operates on two qubits, with one qubit serving as the control qubit and the other as the target qubit. If the control qubit is in the state "1", the target qubit's state will be flipped. 

2. Controlled Z (CZ) gate: This gate operates on two qubits and implements a phase flip on the target qubit if the control qubit is in the state "1". 

3. Controlled phase shift (CPHASE) gate: This gate operates on two qubits and implements a phase shift on the target qubit if the control qubit is in the state "1". 

4. Toffoli gate: This gate operates on three qubits and implements a controlled NOT operation on the target qubit if both control qubits are in the state "1". 

These gates form the building blocks for more complex quantum algorithms and circuits.
