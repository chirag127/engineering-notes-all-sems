## Unit 2 - Introduction to Cryptography

Cryptography is an essential part of modern-day communication and security. This unit covers the following topics:

1. Group: A group is a set of elements that follows certain mathematical rules. In cryptography, groups are used to encrypt and decrypt messages.

2. Field: A field is a set of elements with two binary operations, addition and multiplication. Fields are used to perform mathematical operations in cryptography.

3. Finite Field of the form GF(p): A finite field is a field with a finite number of elements. GF(p) is a finite field with p elements.

4. Modular Arithmetic: Modular arithmetic is a system of arithmetic for integers, where numbers "wrap around" after a certain value. It is used in cryptography to perform mathematical operations on large numbers.

5. Prime and Relative Prime Numbers: Prime numbers are numbers that are only divisible by 1 and themselves. Relative prime numbers are numbers that share no common factors except 1. They are used in cryptography to generate keys.

6. Extended Euclidean Algorithm: The Extended Euclidean Algorithm is used to find the greatest common divisor of two numbers. It is used in cryptography to generate keys.

7. Advanced Encryption Standard (AES) encryption and decryption: AES is a symmetric encryption algorithm used to encrypt and decrypt messages. It is widely used in modern-day communication.

8. Fermat’s and Euler’s theorem: Fermat’s theorem states that if p is a prime number and a is any integer, then a^p - a is divisible by p. Euler’s theorem is a generalization of Fermat’s theorem. They are used in cryptography to generate keys.

9. Primarily Testing: Primarily testing is the process of determining whether a number is prime or composite. It is used in cryptography to generate keys.

10. Chinese Remainder theorem: The Chinese Remainder theorem is used to solve a system of linear congruences. It is used in cryptography to generate keys.

11. Discrete Logarithmic Problem: The Discrete Logarithmic Problem is the problem of finding the exponent of a given number in a given group. It is used in cryptography to generate keys.

12. Principals of public key crypto systems: Public Key Cryptography is a cryptographic system that uses two keys, a public key and a private key. It is widely used in modern-day communication.

13. RSA algorithm: RSA is a public key cryptography algorithm used to encrypt and decrypt messages. It is widely used in modern-day communication.

14. Security of RSA: The security of RSA depends on the difficulty of factoring large numbers. It is widely used in modern-day communication.
