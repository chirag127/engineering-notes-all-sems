### Chinese Remainder Theorem

The Chinese Remainder Theorem is a mathematical concept that is widely used in cryptography. It is used to solve a system of congruences using modular arithmetic. The theorem states that if we have a system of congruences with coprime moduli, then there exists a unique solution that satisfies all congruences simultaneously.

The theorem can be stated as follows:

Suppose we have a system of n congruences of the form:

$x \equiv a_1 \pmod{m_1}$

$x \equiv a_2 \pmod{m_2}$

$x \equiv a_3 \pmod{m_3}$

$x \equiv a_n \pmod{m_n}$

where $m_i$ are pairwise coprime. Then there exists a unique solution $x$ modulo $M = m_1m_2...m_n$ that satisfies all the congruences.
