### Advanced Encryption Standard (AES) encryption and decryption, Fermat’s and Euler’s theorem

In the field of Cryptography & Network Security, the Advanced Encryption Standard (AES) is a widely used encryption algorithm for secure communication. Here are some key points about AES encryption and decryption:

- AES is a symmetric key encryption method that uses the same key for both encryption and decryption.
- It uses a block cipher with a fixed block size of 128 bits and a key size of 128, 192, or 256 bits.
- AES works by applying a series of mathematical operations to the plaintext, known as rounds, to transform it into ciphertext.
- The number of rounds depends on the key size: 10 rounds for 128-bit keys, 12 rounds for 192-bit keys, and 14 rounds for 256-bit keys.
- To decrypt the ciphertext, the same key is applied in reverse order to undo the rounds and convert the ciphertext back to plaintext.

Fermat’s and Euler’s theorem are important mathematical concepts that are used in the field of Cryptography & Network Security. Here are some key points about these theorems:

- Fermat’s theorem states that if p is a prime number and a is any positive integer that is not divisible by p, then a^(p-1) is congruent to 1 (mod p).
- Euler’s theorem is a generalization of Fermat’s theorem that applies to all positive integers a and p that are relatively prime. It states that a^phi(p) is congruent to 1 (mod p), where phi(p) is Euler’s totient function, which gives the number of positive integers less than p that are relatively prime to p.

These theorems are used in various cryptographic algorithms, including RSA encryption and decryption.

In addition to AES and the theorems mentioned above, here are some other important topics in the subject of Cryptography & Network Security:

- Modular arithmetic, which is used in many cryptographic algorithms, involves performing arithmetic operations with integers modulo a given number.
- Prime and relative prime numbers are important concepts in number theory that are used in many cryptographic algorithms.
- The Extended Euclidean Algorithm is a method for finding the greatest common divisor of two integers and also for solving linear Diophantine equations.
- Primarily testing is a method for determining whether a given number is prime.
- The Chinese Remainder Theorem is a method for solving systems of linear congruences.
- The Discrete Logarithmic Problem involves finding the logarithm of a given number in a finite field, which is used in many cryptographic algorithms.
- Public key cryptography involves using two different keys for encryption and decryption, and is commonly used for secure communication. The RSA algorithm is a widely used public key encryption algorithm.
- The security of RSA depends on the difficulty of factoring large composite numbers, which is currently believed to be a hard computational problem.
