- A group is a set of elements with a binary operation that satisfies four properties: closure, associativity, identity and inverse .
- A group is called abelian or commutative if it also satisfies the commutativity property, that is, the order of the operands does not matter .
- A group is called cyclic if it can be generated by a single element, called a generator .
- A group is called finite if it has a finite number of elements, otherwise it is called infinite .
- A group is called simple if it has no proper normal subgroups, that is, subgroups that are invariant under conjugation .
- A group is called symmetric if it consists of all the permutations of a given set .
- A group is called dihedral if it consists of all the symmetries of a regular polygon .
- A group is called braid if it consists of all the ways of braiding a given number of strands.
- A field is a set of elements with two binary operations, usually called addition and multiplication, that satisfy the properties of a commutative group under each operation, and also satisfy the distributive property .
- A field is called finite if it has a finite number of elements, otherwise it is called infinite .
- A field is called prime if it has no proper subfields, that is, subfields that are not equal to the field itself or the trivial field containing only the zero element .
- A field is called algebraically closed if every polynomial with coefficients in the field has a root in the field .
- A field is called Galois if it is a finite extension of a prime field, that is, a field that can be obtained by adding a finite number of elements to a prime field .
- A finite field of the form GF(p) is a field with p elements, where p is a prime number .
- A finite field of the form GF(p^n) is a field with p^n elements, where p is a prime number and n is a positive integer .
- Modular arithmetic is a system of arithmetic where the numbers are taken modulo a fixed positive integer, called the modulus .
- Modular arithmetic can be used to define addition and multiplication operations on the set of integers modulo the modulus, which form a commutative ring .
- If the modulus is a prime number, then the integers modulo the modulus form a field, which is isomorphic to GF(p) .
- A prime number is a positive integer that has exactly two positive divisors, namely 1 and itself .
- A relative prime number is a positive integer that has no common positive divisors with another given positive integer, except 1 .
- The Extended Euclidean Algorithm is an algorithm that computes the greatest common divisor of two given positive integers, and also finds two integers that satisfy the linear Diophantine equation .
- The Extended Euclidean Algorithm can be used to find the multiplicative inverse of an element in a finite field, if it exists .
- The Advanced Encryption Standard (AES) is a symmetric-key block cipher that encrypts and decrypts data in blocks of 128 bits, using a secret key of 128, 192 or 256 bits .
