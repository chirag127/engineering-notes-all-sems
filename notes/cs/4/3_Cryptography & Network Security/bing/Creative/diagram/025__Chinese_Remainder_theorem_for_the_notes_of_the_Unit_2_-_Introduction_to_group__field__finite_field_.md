The Chinese remainder theorem is a theorem that gives a unique solution to a system of congruences with coprime moduli. It can be used to compute with large integers by breaking them down into smaller ones. The theorem was first stated by the Chinese mathematician Sun Zi in the 3rd century CE, and later generalized by Qin Jiushao in the 13th century CE.
