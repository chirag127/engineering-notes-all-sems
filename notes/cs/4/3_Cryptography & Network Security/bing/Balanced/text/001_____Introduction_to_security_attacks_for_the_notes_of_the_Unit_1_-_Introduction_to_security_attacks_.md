### Introduction to security attacks

- Security attacks are any actions that compromise the security of information owned by an organization or an individual.
- Security attacks can be classified into two categories: passive attacks and active attacks.
- Passive attacks are those that do not affect the normal functioning of the system, but only attempt to access or monitor the information. Examples of passive attacks are eavesdropping, traffic analysis, and sniffing.
- Active attacks are those that alter or disrupt the normal functioning of the system, or create false information. Examples of active attacks are modification, deletion, insertion, replay, denial of service, and masquerade.
- Security attacks can also be categorized based on the source of the attack: insider attacks and outsider attacks.
- Insider attacks are those that are launched by authorized users of the system, who abuse their privileges or exploit their knowledge of the system. Examples of insider attacks are disgruntled employees, malicious insiders, or compromised accounts.
- Outsider attacks are those that are launched by unauthorized users of the system, who exploit the vulnerabilities or weaknesses of the system. Examples of outsider attacks are hackers, crackers, or cybercriminals.
