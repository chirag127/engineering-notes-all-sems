## Unit 4 - Network & Communication aspects in IoT

Unit 4 - Network & Communication aspects in IoT refers to the various technologies and protocols used to enable communication between devices in the Internet of Things (IoT). The communication network in IoT is responsible for transmitting data between devices, as well as coordinating and controlling the devices.

1. Wireless communication technologies: IoT devices use a variety of wireless communication technologies, including Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Zigbee, and cellular networks. 

2. Network protocols: IoT devices use a variety of network protocols, including TCP/IP, MQTT, CoAP, and LwM2M. These protocols are used to transmit data between devices, as well as to control and coordinate the devices.

3. Security: IoT devices are vulnerable to security threats, such as hacking and data theft. To address these threats, IoT devices use a variety of security technologies, including encryption, authentication, and access control.

4. Interoperability: IoT devices from different manufacturers may use different communication technologies and protocols, which can make it difficult for the devices to communicate with each other. To address this issue, IoT devices use standardization and interoperability technologies, such as the AllSeen Alliance and the Open Interconnect Consortium.

In conclusion, Unit 4 - Network & Communication aspects in IoT refers to the various technologies and protocols used to enable communication between devices in the Internet of Things (IoT). IoT devices use a variety of wireless communication technologies, network protocols, security technologies, and standardization and interoperability technologies to ensure reliable and secure communication between devices.
