### communicating data with H/W units for the notes of the Unit 7 - IoT Applications in the subject of Internet of Things

1. Protocols: IoT devices use various communication protocols such as MQTT, CoAP, HTTP, etc.
2. Devices: IoT devices can communicate with each other using Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Zigbee, etc.
3. Data Collection: IoT devices collect and transmit data to a central repository.
4. Data Analysis: The collected data is analyzed to extract insights and make decisions.
5. Data Visualization: The analyzed data is presented in a visual format for easy interpretation.
6. Data Storage: The collected and analyzed data is stored for future use.
7. Data Sharing: The collected and analyzed data can be shared with other devices and systems.
