### Participatory Sensing Technology for the Notes of the Unit 2 - Hardware for IoT in the Subject of Internet of Things

- Participatory sensing is an approach to data collection and interpretation in which individuals, acting alone or in groups, use their personal mobile devices and web services to systematically explore interesting aspects of their worlds ranging from health to culture.
- Participatory sensing is a type of citizen science practice enabled by mobile phones and IoT sensors; i.e. distributed data collection by volunteer participants.
- Participatory sensing is a collaborative framework of sensing devices coordinated by computing and networking components. In this regard, participatory sensing involves task allocators, participant nodes, reputation and reliability mechanisms, incentive schemes, and data aggregation and analysis methods.
- Participatory sensing differs from opportunistic sensing mainly in the user participation. In participatory sensing, the users manually determine how, when, what, and where to share the sensed data. In opportunistic sensing, the data collection stage is automated without user participation.
- Participatory sensing can be used to monitor the different conditions of a variety of cities, as well as the collective behavior of people connected to the Internet in (almost) real time. For example, participatory sensing can be used to measure air quality, traffic congestion, noise pollution, urban accessibility, etc.
- Participatory sensing can also be used to support personal and community goals, such as fitness, wellness, social networking, education, etc.
- Participatory sensing can provide unprecedented opportunities to access sensing data on a global scale. This large amount of data can be used to improve the processes of decision making regarding different entities (e.g., individuals, groups, services, and applications).
- Participatory sensing can also pose some challenges and risks, such as privacy, security, data quality, user motivation, scalability, interoperability, etc.

#### Mnemonics and Learning Tricks

- To remember the definition of participatory sensing, you can use the acronym PAST: **P**ersonal mobile devices and web services, **A**cting alone or in groups, **S**ystematically explore interesting aspects, **T**heir worlds ranging from health to culture.
- To remember the difference between participatory sensing and opportunistic sensing, you can use the rhyme: **Participatory sensing is voluntary, opportunistic sensing is not. Participatory sensing is manual, opportunistic sensing is automatic.**
- To remember some of the applications of participatory sensing, you can use the word CUTE: **C**ity conditions, **U**rban accessibility, **T**raffic congestion, **E**nvironmental quality.
- To remember some of the challenges and risks of participatory sensing, you can use the word PUDS: **P**rivacy, **U**ser motivation, **D**ata quality, **S**ecurity.
