# Designing of smart street lights in smart city

- Smart street lights are LED luminaires that can be remotely controlled, monitored, and managed by a central system or a dashboard.
- Smart street lights can save energy, reduce maintenance costs, improve safety, and provide a platform for other smart city applications.
- Smart street lights can be integrated with sensors, cameras, wireless communication modules, and IoT network platforms to enable data collection and transmission.
- Smart street lights can be designed using the following steps:

  - Define the objectives and requirements of the smart street lighting project, such as energy savings, lighting quality, network coverage, data analytics, etc.
  - Select the appropriate LED luminaires, sensors, and communication modules that meet the technical specifications and standards of the project.
  - Choose the IoT network platform that can support the communication, management, and security of the smart street lighting system, such as Itron, Silicon Labs, etc.
  - Install and commission the smart street lighting system, ensuring the proper wiring, mounting, testing, and configuration of the devices and the network.
  - Operate and maintain the smart street lighting system, using the dashboard or the central system to monitor, control, and optimize the performance and functionality of the devices and the network.
  - Evaluate and improve the smart street lighting system, using the data and feedback collected from the system to assess the benefits, challenges, and opportunities of the project.
