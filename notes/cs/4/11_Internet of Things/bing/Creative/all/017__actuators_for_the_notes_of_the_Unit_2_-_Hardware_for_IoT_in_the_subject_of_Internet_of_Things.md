### Actuators for the notes of the Unit 2 - Hardware for IoT in the subject of Internet of Things

- An actuator is a device that converts energy into motion. It does this by taking an electrical signal and combining it with an energy source.
- In an IoT system, the actuator can act on data collected by sensors to create an outcome as determined by the chosen settings of the user.
- Actuators are the workhorses of the Internet of Things and enable the automation or remote control of a wide range of processes in sectors that include consumer, industrial, healthcare, and transportation.

- The control system acts upon an environment through the actuator. It requires a source of energy and a control signal. When it receives a control signal, it converts the source of energy to a mechanical operation.
- On the basis of which form of energy it uses, it has different types given below:

  - Hydraulic Actuators – A hydraulic actuator uses hydraulic power to perform a mechanical operation. They are actuated by a cylinder or fluid motor. The mechanical motion is converted to rotary, linear, or oscillatory motion, according to the need of the IoT device.
    - Advantages: Hydraulic actuators can produce a large magnitude of force and high speed. Used in welding, clamping, etc. Used for lowering or raising the vehicles in car transport carriers.
    - Disadvantages: Hydraulic fluid leaks can cause efficiency loss and issues of cleaning. It is expensive. It requires noise reduction equipment, heat exchangers, and high maintenance systems.
  - Pneumatic Actuators – A pneumatic actuator uses energy formed by vacuum or compressed air at high pressure to convert into either linear or rotary motion.
    - Advantages: They are a low-cost option and are used at extreme temperatures where using air is a safer option than chemicals. They need low maintenance, are durable, and have a long operational life. It is very quick in starting and stopping the motion.
    - Disadvantages: Loss of pressure can make it less efficient. The air compressor should be running continuously. Air can be polluted, and it needs maintenance.
  - Electrical Actuators – An electric actuator uses electrical energy, is usually actuated by a motor that converts electrical energy into mechanical torque.
    - Advantages: It has many applications in various industries as it can automate industrial valves. It produces less noise and is safe to use since there are no fluid leakages.
    - Disadvantages: It requires a power supply and can be affected by power outages. It can generate heat and electromagnetic interference.
  - Thermal or Magnetic Actuators – A thermal or magnetic actuator uses thermal or magnetic energy to produce motion. They are often used for micro-actuation in MEMS (Micro-Electro-Mechanical Systems) devices.
    - Advantages: They are small, lightweight, and have low power consumption. They can operate in harsh environments and have high precision and accuracy.
    - Disadvantages: They have limited force and displacement range. They can be affected by temperature and magnetic field variations.

- A mnemonic to remember the types of actuators is **H**elp **P**eople **E**at **T**acos **M**ore, where each letter stands for the first letter of each type of actuator: **H**ydraulic, **P**neumatic, **E**lectrical, **T**hermal or **M**agnetic.
- Some examples of actuators in IoT are  :

  - Smart locks that use electric motors to lock or unlock doors remotely.
  - Smart thermostats that use thermal actuators to adjust the temperature of the room based on the user's preferences or schedule.
  - Smart blinds that use magnetic actuators to open or close the blinds based on the light level or time
