# Netduino

Netduino is an open-source electronics prototyping platform based on the .NET Micro Framework. It uses the ARM Cortex-M 32-bit RISC ARM processor core as a 32-bit ARM-microcontroller. The Netduino boards (except the discontinued Mini and Go models) are designed to be pin-compatible with most Arduino shields.

Some features of Netduino are:

- It supports C# and Visual Basic languages, and can be programmed using Visual Studio or Visual Studio for Mac .
- It has a rich development environment, with debugging, breakpoints, and step-by-step execution .
- It has built-in Ethernet and microSD support on some models, and can communicate with other devices using serial, SPI, I2C, and PWM protocols .
- It has a large community of developers and enthusiasts, and a library of drivers and libraries called Netduino.Foundation .

Some applications of Netduino are:

- IoT projects, such as smart home automation, environmental monitoring, and remote control .
- Robotics, such as servo motors, sensors, and LED displays .
- Education, such as learning C#, electronics, and microcontroller programming .
