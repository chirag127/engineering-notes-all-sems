### Technology behind IoT

The technology behind IoT is the combination of hardware, software, and communication protocols that enable the interconnection and data exchange between various devices, machines, objects, and people over the internet. The technology behind IoT can be divided into four basic layers:

- **Device layer**: This layer consists of the physical devices that have sensors, actuators, or embedded systems that can collect, process, and transmit data. Examples of devices in this layer are smart phones, wearables, smart meters, cameras, RFID tags, etc. The devices in this layer can use different types of processors, such as CPUs, MCUs, GPUs, security chips, or FPGAs, depending on their functionality and performance requirements.
- **Connectivity layer**: This layer provides the network infrastructure and protocols that enable the communication between the devices and the cloud or other devices. The connectivity layer can use various wireless or wired technologies, such as Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Zigbee, Z-Wave, LoRa, 5G, Ethernet, etc. Some of the emerging technologies in this layer are eSIM, mesh networks, Wi-Fi 6, and Wi-Fi Direct .
- **Cloud layer**: This layer provides the storage, computing, and analytics capabilities that can process and manage the data generated by the devices. The cloud layer can use different platforms and services, such as AWS, Azure, Google Cloud, IBM Cloud, etc. The cloud layer can also use edge computing, which is the processing of data closer to the source, such as in edge gateways or edge devices, to reduce latency and bandwidth consumption .
- **Application layer**: This layer provides the user interface and the business logic that can deliver value and insights from the data. The application layer can use different software tools and frameworks, such as web, mobile, or desktop applications, dashboards, APIs, etc. The application layer can also use artificial intelligence, machine learning, or big data techniques to enable smart decision making and automation .

Some of the mnemonics and learning tricks for the technology behind IoT are:

- To remember the four layers of IoT technology, use the acronym **DCCA** (Device, Connectivity, Cloud, Application).
- To remember some of the wireless technologies used in IoT connectivity, use the phrase **Wise Bees Love Zucchini** (Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, LoRa, Zigbee).
- To remember some of the processors used in IoT devices, use the word **SCUFF** (Security chips, CPUs, MCUs, FPGAs, GPUs).
