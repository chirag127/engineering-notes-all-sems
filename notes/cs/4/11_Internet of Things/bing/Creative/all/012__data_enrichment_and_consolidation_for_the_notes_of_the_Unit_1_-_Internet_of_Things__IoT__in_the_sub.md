### Data enrichment and consolidation for the notes of the Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT) in the subject of Internet of Things

- Data enrichment is the process of merging and adding either first-party or third-party data to a dataset you’re already working with.
- Data enrichment allows you to get more meaning than raw data might otherwise provide, and empowers you to answer a bigger set of questions and provide deeper insights.
- Data enrichment also enhances the customer experience, since enriched data helps your business anticipate a customer’s needs and stay relevant.
- Data consolidation is the process of combining data from multiple sources into a single, consistent dataset.
- Data consolidation helps you to eliminate data silos, reduce data redundancy, improve data quality, and simplify data analysis.
- Data consolidation also enables you to create a single source of truth for your data, which can improve data governance and compliance.

- In the context of IoT, data enrichment and consolidation are important for integrating IoT data with non-IoT data and writing the data to the blockchain.
- Blockchain is a distributed ledger technology that enables secure and transparent transactions among multiple parties.
- Blockchain can improve data visibility, data security, and data analysis options for IoT data.
- For example, in a supply chain scenario where temperature-sensitive goods are shipped across the country, an IoT device might track container temperature but not the ID of the shipment. If the shipment exceeds the permitted temperature, you might need to look up multiple records to identify the shipment ID that corresponds to the sensor reading. This searching wastes valuable time, decreases visibility across multiple parties, and increases the risk for data loss.
- With data enrichment and consolidation, you can merge the IoT data (such as temperature and location) with the non-IoT data (such as shipment ID and shipper name) based on a correlation parameter (such as the device ID) and store them in a blockchain.
- By correlating the data this way, you can match and merge separate data sets and create an enriched asset in accordance with a contract.
- The enriched asset is stored in the blockchain, which ensures data integrity, immutability, and traceability.
- The blockchain also sends an enriched IoT event to a supply chain application as a notification of the success of the transaction and includes the transaction data.
- This way, you can improve data visibility, data security, and data analysis options for IoT data.

- There are different types of data enrichment that can be applied to IoT data, such as behavioral, demographic, and geographic.
- Behavioral data enrichment adds customer behavioral patterns to their user profile, such as their areas of interest and their journey leading up to their purchase decision.
- Behavioral data enrichment helps you to determine the effectiveness of advertising campaigns and justify marketing budgets.
- Demographic data enrichment adds customer demographic information to their user profile, such as their age, gender, income, and education.
- Demographic data enrichment helps you to target messaging to specific demographic groups and ensure advertisements and messaging are relatable to the customer.
- Geographic data enrichment adds customer geographic information to their user profile, such as their country, time zone, and city.
- Geographic data enrichment helps you to target messaging to different geographic groups and ensure users see content that is relatable to their location.

- Data enrichment and consolidation can be done in real time or in batch mode, depending on the use case and the data sources.
- Real-time data enrichment and consolidation involves processing data as soon as it is generated or received, and updating the data sets and the blockchain accordingly.
- Real-time data enrichment and consolidation is useful for scenarios where timely and accurate data is critical, such as fraud detection, anomaly detection, and event-driven marketing.
- Batch data enrichment and consolidation involves processing data at regular intervals, such as daily, weekly, or monthly, and updating the data sets and the blockchain accordingly.
- Batch data enrichment and consolidation is useful for scenarios where data volume is high and data latency is acceptable, such as historical analysis, reporting, and forecasting.
