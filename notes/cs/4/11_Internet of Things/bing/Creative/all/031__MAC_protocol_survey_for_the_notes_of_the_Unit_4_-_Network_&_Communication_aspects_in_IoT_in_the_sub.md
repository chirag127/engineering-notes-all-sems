### MAC protocol survey for the notes of the Unit 4 - Network & Communication aspects in IoT in the subject of Internet of Things

- MAC (Medium Access Control) protocol is the first protocol layer above the physical layer in ad hoc networks. The primary task of any MAC protocol is to control the access of the nodes to shared medium.
- MAC protocols can be classified into different categories based on various criteria, such as contention-based or contention-free, reservation-based or reservation-less, synchronous or asynchronous, sender-initiated or receiver-initiated, single-channel or multi-channel, etc .
- MAC protocols for IoT (Internet of Things) need to consider the characteristics and requirements of IoT applications, such as coverage area, scalability, transmission data rate, and applicability.
- MAC protocols for IoT can be grouped into two main categories: short-range and long-range.
- Short-range MAC protocols are designed for applications that require low power consumption, low data rate, and short distance communication. Some examples of short-range MAC protocols are RFID, NFC, Bluetooth, Bluetooth Low Energy, IEEE 802.15.4, Wireless-HART, Z-Wave, Weightless, and IEEE 802.11 a/b/g/n/ah.
- Long-range MAC protocols are designed for applications that require high coverage, high scalability, and low cost. Some examples of long-range MAC protocols are NB-IoT, LTE CAT-0, LTE CAT-M, LTE CAT-N, LoRa, and SigFox.
- A comparative study of short-range and long-range MAC protocols can be done based on the following parameters: frequency band, modulation scheme, channel access method, data rate, packet size, latency, power consumption, network topology, security, and interoperability.
- Some open research issues on MAC protocols for IoT are: how to achieve energy efficiency and reliability in heterogeneous and dynamic IoT scenarios, how to support mobility and handover in IoT networks, how to deal with interference and congestion in IoT channels, how to provide quality of service and prioritization for IoT traffic, how to ensure security and privacy for IoT data, and how to integrate different MAC protocols for IoT applications  .
