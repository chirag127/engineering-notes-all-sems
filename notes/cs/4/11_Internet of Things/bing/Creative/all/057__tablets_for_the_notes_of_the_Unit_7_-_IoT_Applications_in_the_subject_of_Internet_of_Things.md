### Tablets for the notes of the Unit 7 - IoT Applications in the subject of Internet of Things

- Tablets are portable devices that can be used for various IoT applications, such as data visualization, sensor communication, remote control, and diagnostics.
- Tablets can provide a better user interface and user experience than traditional HMI devices, especially for data-intensive and complex IoT applications.
- Tablets can also offer additional functionalities, such as accessing online manuals, video conferencing, report generation, and cloud integration.
- Tablets can be customized to meet the specific requirements of different IoT applications, such as wired external interfaces, sensor network connectivity, cellular standards, and operating systems.
- Tablets can also be integrated with other IoT devices, such as smart pill dispensers, safety controllers, and HMI communicators, to enable secure and efficient data transfer and control  .

Some advantages of using tablets for IoT applications are:

- Tablets can offer a larger and higher-resolution display than HMI devices, which can improve the readability and clarity of data and graphics.
- Tablets can support touch-screen and gesture-based inputs, which can enhance the interactivity and usability of IoT applications.
- Tablets can run various applications and software, which can increase the functionality and versatility of IoT applications.
- Tablets can connect to the internet and cloud services, which can enable data storage, analysis, and sharing, as well as remote access and control of IoT devices.
- Tablets can leverage the built-in sensors, cameras, microphones, and speakers, which can provide additional data and feedback for IoT applications.

Some disadvantages of using tablets for IoT applications are:

- Tablets may not be suitable for harsh industrial environments, such as high temperature, humidity, dust, vibration, and noise.
- Tablets may have limited battery life and power consumption, which can affect the reliability and availability of IoT applications.
- Tablets may have security and privacy issues, such as data breaches, malware, and unauthorized access, which can compromise the integrity and confidentiality of IoT data and devices.
- Tablets may have compatibility and interoperability issues, such as different standards, protocols, and formats, which can hinder the communication and integration of IoT devices and systems.
- Tablets may have legal and ethical issues, such as data ownership, liability, and regulation, which can affect the accountability and responsibility of IoT applications.
