### IoT Examples for the notes of the Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT) in the subject of Internet of Things

The Internet of Things (IoT) is a network of physical devices that can transfer data to one another without human intervention. IoT devices can include anything with a sensor assigned a unique identifier (UID). The primary goal of the IoT is to create self-reporting devices that can communicate with each other (and users) in real time.

Some of the IoT examples across various domains are:

- **Connected cars**: IoT devices can enable features such as over-the-air (OTA) software updates, GPS tracking, crash detection, driver performance monitoring, and remote access through mobile apps . Some of the companies that provide connected car solutions are Airbiquity, Zubie, and Tesla.
- **Smart appliances**: IoT devices can make home appliances more intelligent, efficient, and convenient. For example, smart refrigerators can monitor food items and suggest recipes, smart ovens can adjust cooking time and temperature, and smart washers can optimize water and detergent usage. Some of the companies that offer smart appliances are LG Electronics, Samsung, and Whirlpool.
- **Connected security systems**: IoT devices can enhance home and business security by providing features such as remote monitoring, motion detection, facial recognition, and emergency alerts. Some of the companies that provide connected security systems are Ring, Nest, and SimpliSafe.
- **Smart agriculture equipment**: IoT devices can improve agricultural productivity and sustainability by providing features such as soil and crop monitoring, irrigation control, pest and disease detection, and livestock tracking. Some of the companies that provide smart agriculture equipment are John Deere, CropX, and The Yield.
- **Connected retail**: IoT devices can transform the retail industry by providing features such as inventory management, customer behavior analysis, personalized recommendations, and contactless payments. Some of the companies that provide connected retail solutions are Amazon, Walmart, and Zebra Technologies.
- **Connected healthcare monitors**: IoT devices can improve healthcare outcomes and efficiency by providing features such as remote patient monitoring, vital signs tracking, medication adherence, and chronic disease management. Some of the companies that provide connected healthcare monitors are Fitbit, Philips, and Medtronic.
- **Connected manufacturing equipment**: IoT devices can optimize manufacturing processes and operations by providing features such as predictive maintenance, quality control, energy management, and supply chain visibility. Some of the companies that provide connected manufacturing equipment are Siemens, GE, and Bosch.
- **Connected cities**: IoT devices can make cities smarter and safer by providing features such as traffic management, waste management, air quality monitoring, and public safety. Some of the examples of connected cities are Barcelona, Singapore, and Amsterdam.
