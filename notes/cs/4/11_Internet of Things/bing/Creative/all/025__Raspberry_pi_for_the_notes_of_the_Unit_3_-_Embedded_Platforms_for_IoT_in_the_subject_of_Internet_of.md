### Raspberry pi for the notes of the Unit 3 - Embedded Platforms for IoT in the subject of Internet of Things

- Raspberry Pi is a small, low-cost, and versatile computer that can be used for various IoT projects.
- Raspberry Pi has a powerful CPU, wireless LAN, Bluetooth, and a 40-pin GPIO (General Purpose Input/Output) connector for interfacing with external sensors and devices.
- Raspberry Pi can run various operating systems, such as Linux, Windows, and Android, and supports many programming languages, such as Python, C, Java, and Node.js.
- Raspberry Pi can be connected to the internet and cloud services, such as Ubidots, to collect, store, analyze, and visualize data from IoT sensors and devices .
- Raspberry Pi can also be used to control and actuate IoT devices, such as LEDs, motors, relays, and cameras, using its GPIO pins or external modules .
- Raspberry Pi can be used for various IoT applications, such as smart home, smart agriculture, smart city, industrial automation, robotics, and gaming  .

Some mnemonics and learning tricks for Raspberry Pi for IoT are:

- Remember the acronym **R**eally **P**owerful **I**oT to recall the main features of Raspberry Pi: Really (small, low-cost, and versatile), Powerful (CPU, wireless, Bluetooth, and GPIO), and IoT (internet and cloud connectivity, data collection and analysis, and device control and actuation).
- Remember the rhyme **Pi**e in the **sky** to recall that Raspberry Pi can be connected to the cloud and use cloud services for IoT projects.
- Remember the phrase **Pi**n it to **win** it to recall that Raspberry Pi can use its GPIO pins or external modules to interface with various IoT sensors and devices.
