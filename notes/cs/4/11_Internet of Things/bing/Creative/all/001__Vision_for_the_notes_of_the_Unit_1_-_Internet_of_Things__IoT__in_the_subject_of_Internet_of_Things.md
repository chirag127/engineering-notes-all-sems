### Vision for the notes of the Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT) in the subject of Internet of Things

- The vision of the Internet of Things (IoT) is to extend the Internet to the physical world, where everyday objects can communicate, interact and exchange data with each other and with Internet services .
- The IoT vision can be seen from two perspectives: Internet-centric and thing-centric.
  - Internet-centric: The focus is on the Internet services that collect, process and present data from the "things" (devices, sensors, actuators, etc.).
  - Thing-centric: The focus is on the smart devices that have sensing, computing, communication and actuation capabilities, and can operate autonomously or collaboratively.
- The IoT vision is driven by several factors, such as :
  - The availability and affordability of wireless technologies, such as RFID, NFC, Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, etc.
  - The advancement and miniaturization of sensor and actuator technologies, such as MEMS, NEMS, etc.
  - The development and standardization of communication protocols, such as IPv6, 6LoWPAN, CoAP, MQTT, etc.
  - The emergence and adoption of cloud computing, big data analytics, machine learning, artificial intelligence, etc.
  - The increasing demand and expectation for smart applications and services, such as smart home, smart city, smart health, smart agriculture, etc.
- The IoT vision poses several challenges, such as  :
  - Scalability: How to handle the massive number and diversity of devices and data in the IoT network?
  - Interoperability: How to ensure the seamless communication and integration of devices and services from different vendors and domains?
  - Security: How to protect the devices and data from unauthorized access, modification and misuse?
  - Privacy: How to respect the users' preferences and rights regarding the collection and use of their personal data?
  - Reliability: How to ensure the availability and quality of service of the devices and applications in the IoT network?
  - Energy efficiency: How to optimize the power consumption and battery life of the devices in the IoT network?
  - Ethics: How to balance the benefits and risks of the IoT applications and services for the society and the environment?

- A possible mnemonic to remember the IoT vision and challenges is:

  - **I**nternet of **T**hings: **I**nternet-centric and **T**hing-centric
  - **S**calability, **I**nteroperability, **S**ecurity, **P**rivacy, **R**eliability, **E**nergy efficiency, **E**thics: **S**ix **I**ssues and **S**ix **P**roblems to **R**esolve for **E**verything
