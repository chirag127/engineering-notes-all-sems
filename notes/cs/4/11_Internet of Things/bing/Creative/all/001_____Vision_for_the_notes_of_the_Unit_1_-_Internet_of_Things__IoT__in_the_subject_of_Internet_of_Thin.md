# Vision for the Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT)

- The vision for the Unit 1 is to introduce the concept, definition, characteristics, and applications of the Internet of Things (IoT).
- The Unit 1 aims to provide an overview of the IoT architecture, components, protocols, and standards, as well as the challenges and opportunities of the IoT.
- The Unit 1 also intends to explore the current and future trends of the IoT, such as smart cities, smart homes, smart health, smart agriculture, and smart industry.
- The Unit 1 expects the students to be able to:
  - Define and explain the Internet of Things (IoT) and its characteristics.
  - Identify and describe the main components and layers of the IoT architecture.
  - Compare and contrast the various protocols and standards used in the IoT.
  - Analyze and evaluate the benefits and challenges of the IoT for different domains and applications.
  - Recognize and discuss the current and future trends of the IoT and their implications for society and environment.
