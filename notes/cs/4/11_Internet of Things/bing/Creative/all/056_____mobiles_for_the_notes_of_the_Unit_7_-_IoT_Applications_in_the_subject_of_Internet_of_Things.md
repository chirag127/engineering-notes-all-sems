# Mobiles for the notes of the Unit 7 - IoT Applications in the subject of Internet of Things

- Mobiles are devices that can communicate wirelessly with other devices or networks, such as smartphones, tablets, laptops, etc.
- Mobiles can act as both IoT devices and IoT applications, depending on their roles and functions in the IoT system.
- As IoT devices, mobiles can collect, process, and transmit data from various sensors, such as GPS, camera, microphone, accelerometer, etc. Mobiles can also receive commands and feedback from other devices or applications, such as smart home systems, wearable devices, etc.
- As IoT applications, mobiles can provide user interfaces for interacting with IoT devices, such as sending commands, receiving data, configuring settings, etc. Mobiles can also provide data analysis, visualization, and decision support for IoT systems, such as monitoring, alerting, reporting, etc.
- Mobile IoT applications can be classified into two categories: native and web-based. Native applications are developed for specific mobile platforms, such as Android, iOS, etc. Web-based applications are accessed through web browsers, such as Chrome, Safari, etc.
- Mobile IoT applications can have various use cases and benefits, such as:
  - Electric bikes tracking: Mobile IoT applications can help users locate, unlock, and pay for shared electric bikes, as well as monitor their battery levels, speed, and distance. Mobile IoT applications can also help city councils manage the electric bikes fleet, prevent theft, reduce accidents, and optimize maintenance.
  - Smoke and gas detectors: Mobile IoT applications can alert users and authorities in case of fire or gas leakage, as well as provide real-time data on the air quality, temperature, and humidity. Mobile IoT applications can also enable remote control and configuration of the detectors, as well as battery level monitoring.
  - Asset tracking: Mobile IoT applications can help users track and locate their assets, such as luggage, vehicles, pets, etc. Mobile IoT applications can also provide data on the asset's condition, movement, and status, as well as enable remote locking and unlocking.
  - IoT device management: Mobile IoT applications can help users connect, configure, and control their IoT devices, such as smart home appliances, wearable devices, etc. Mobile IoT applications can also provide data visualization, analysis, and feedback for the IoT devices, as well as enable firmware updates and troubleshooting .
  - IoT data collection and analysis: Mobile IoT applications can help users collect and analyze data from various IoT devices, such as environmental sensors, health monitors, etc. Mobile IoT applications can also provide data visualization, reporting, and decision support for the IoT systems, such as weather forecasting, health management, etc .
