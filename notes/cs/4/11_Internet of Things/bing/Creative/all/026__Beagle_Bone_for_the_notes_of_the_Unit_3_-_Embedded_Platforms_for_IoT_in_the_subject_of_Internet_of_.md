### Beagle Bone for the notes of the Unit 3 - Embedded Platforms for IoT in the subject of Internet of Things

- Beagle Bone is a family of open-source, low-cost, single-board computers that run Linux and can be used for IoT projects.
- Beagle Bone boards have a 1 GHz ARM Cortex-A8 processor, 512 MB or 1 GB of RAM, 4 GB of eMMC flash storage, a microSD card slot, a microHDMI port, a 10/100 or Gigabit Ethernet port, a USB 2.0 host port, a USB 2.0 client port, and a 2x46 pin expansion header.
- Beagle Bone boards also have two 200 MHz programmable real-time units (PRUs), an ARM Cortex-M3, and a SGX PowerVR graphics accelerator for offloading tasks from the main CPU.
- Beagle Bone boards support various interfaces such as LCD, UART, ADC, I2C, SPI, PWM, and more through the expansion header.
- Beagle Bone boards can be powered by a miniUSB port, a DC jack, or the expansion header.
- Beagle Bone Black Wireless is a variant of Beagle Bone Black that replaces the Ethernet port with onboard 802.11 b/g/n 2.4 GHz WiFi and Bluetooth 4.1 plus BLE.
- Beagle Bone boards can run various Linux distributions such as Debian, Ubuntu, Android, and more, as well as other operating systems such as FreeBSD, QNX, and Windows Embedded Compact 7.
- Beagle Bone boards can be programmed using various languages such as C, C++, Python, Java, JavaScript, and more, as well as frameworks such as Node.js, ROS, OpenCV, and more.
- Beagle Bone boards can be used for various IoT applications such as home automation, robotics, industrial control, education, and more.
- Beagle Bone boards can also run K3s, a lightweight Kubernetes distribution, to enable containerized applications and edge computing on IoT devices.

Some possible mnemonics and learning tricks for Beagle Bone are:

- Beagle Bone boards have **B**lack, **B**lue, **B**lack Wireless, and **B**eagleBone AI variants, which can be remembered as **B**4.
- Beagle Bone boards have **2**00 MHz PRUs, **2**x46 pin expansion header, and **2** USB ports, which can be remembered as **2**3.
- Beagle Bone boards have **4** GB of eMMC flash storage, **4** user configurable LEDs, and **4** main features: processor, memory, network, and video/audio, which can be remembered as **4**3.
- Beagle Bone boards have **7** analog pins, **65** digital pins, and **512** MB or **1** GB of RAM, which can be remembered as **7**-**65**-**512**-**1**.
