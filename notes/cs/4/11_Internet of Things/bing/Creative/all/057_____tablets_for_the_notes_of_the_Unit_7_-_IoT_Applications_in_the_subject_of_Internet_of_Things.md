# Tablets for the notes of the Unit 7 - IoT Applications

- IoT applications are the use cases of IoT devices and systems that provide value to users, businesses, or society.
- IoT devices are physical objects that are connected to the internet and can sense, communicate, and act on data.
- IoT systems are the networks, platforms, and software that enable the collection, processing, and analysis of data from IoT devices.
- Tablets are portable computing devices that have touchscreens, wireless connectivity, and various sensors and cameras.
- Tablets can be used for various IoT applications, such as:
  - Smart pill dispensers: Tablets can be paired with IoT-enabled pill dispensers that monitor and remind patients to take their medications, and send alerts to caregivers or doctors in case of non-adherence or overdose.
  - Remote monitoring and control: Tablets can be used to access and visualize data from IoT devices and systems, such as sensors, cameras, or actuators, and to send commands or adjust settings remotely.
  - Data collection and analysis: Tablets can be used to collect and analyze data from IoT devices and systems, such as environmental, health, or industrial data, and to generate insights or reports.
  - Touchless transactions: Tablets can be used to enable contactless payment, data entry, or authentication, using technologies such as NFC, QR codes, or biometrics.
  - Customized solutions: Tablets can be designed and configured to meet specific IoT use cases, such as ruggedness, security, performance, or battery life .
