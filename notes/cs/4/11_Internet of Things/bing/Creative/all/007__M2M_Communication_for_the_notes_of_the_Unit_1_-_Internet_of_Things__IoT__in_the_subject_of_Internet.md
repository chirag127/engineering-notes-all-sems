### M2M Communication for the notes of the Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT) in the subject of Internet of Things

- M2M stands for Machine to Machine communication and it describes the interaction of billions of devices and machines that are connected to the internet and to each other.
- M2M enables networked devices to exchange information and perform actions without the manual assistance of humans. Artificial intelligence (AI) and machine learning (ML) facilitate the communication between systems, allowing them to make their own autonomous choices.
- M2M technology was first adopted in manufacturing and industrial settings, where other technologies, such as SCADA and remote monitoring, helped remotely manage and control data from equipment. M2M has since found applications in other sectors, such as healthcare, business and insurance.
- M2M is also the foundation for the Internet of Things (IoT), which is a network of physical devices, vehicles, home appliances, and other items embedded with electronics, software, sensors, actuators, and connectivity that enables these objects to connect and exchange data.
- The main components of an M2M system include sensors, RFID, a Wi-Fi or cellular communications link, and autonomic computing software programmed to help a network device interpret data and make decisions. These M2M applications translate the data, which can trigger preprogrammed, automated actions.
- One of the most well-known types of machine-to-machine communication is telemetry, which has been used since the early part of the last century to transmit operational data. Telemetry uses telephone lines, radio waves, or the internet to transmit performance measurements gathered from monitoring instruments in remote locations.
- The main benefits of M2M include: reduced costs by minimizing equipment maintenance and downtime; boosted revenue by revealing new business opportunities for servicing products in the field; and improved customer service by proactively monitoring and servicing equipment before it fails or only when it is needed.
- Some examples of M2M applications in everyday life are: smart homes, smart meters, smart alarms, wearable devices, vending machines, asset tracking, predictive maintenance, and product development .
- Some examples of M2M applications in business are: smart asset tracking, smart grid, smart manufacturing, smart logistics, smart healthcare, smart agriculture, and smart cities  .

A possible mnemonic to remember the main components of an M2M system is: **S**ensors, **R**FID, **W**i-Fi or cellular, and **A**utonomic software, or **S**R**W**A.

Some possible learning tricks for M2M communication are:

- To understand the difference between M2M and IoT, think of M2M as a general concept that covers any technology that enables networked devices to communicate and act without human intervention, while IoT is a specific subset of technologies that use M2M to create a network of smart objects that can interact with each other and the environment .
- To remember some of the benefits of M2M, think of the three R's: **R**educe costs, **R**evenue boost, and **R**eliability improvement.
- To remember some of the examples of M2M applications, think of the different domains or sectors that can benefit from M2M, such as home, energy, transportation, health, business, agriculture, and urban  .
