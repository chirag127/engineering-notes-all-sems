### Data aggregation and dissemination for the notes of the Unit 4 - Network & Communication aspects in IoT in the subject of Internet of Things

- Data aggregation and dissemination are two fundamental services in Internet of Things (IoT) networks .
- Data aggregation is the process of collecting data from multiple sources, such as sensors, devices, or gateways, and processing it to extract useful information or insights.
- Data dissemination is the process of transmitting data from a source, such as a base station, an access point, or a sink node, to multiple destinations, such as nodes, devices, or users, in the IoT network .
- Data aggregation and dissemination can support many important functions in IoT networks, such as time synchronization, software updating, routing path formation, neighbor discovery, data analysis, and decision making.
- Data aggregation and dissemination can also improve the efficiency, reliability, and scalability of IoT networks by reducing the data redundancy, latency, and bandwidth consumption.
- Data aggregation and dissemination can be challenging in IoT networks due to the heterogeneity, dynamism, and resource constraints of the network components .
- Data aggregation and dissemination can be performed at different levels of the IoT network, such as the cloud, the fog, or the edge.
- Data aggregation and dissemination can benefit from various techniques, such as machine learning, network coding, cross-technology communication, and constructive interference  .

#### Mnemonics and learning tricks

- To remember the five characteristics of IoT data processing, use the acronym **V**elocity, **V**olume, **V**ariety, **V**ariability, and **V**eracity, or **5Vs**.
- To remember the difference between data aggregation and data dissemination, use the following phrases:
  - Data aggregation is **from many to one**, data dissemination is **from one to many**.
  - Data aggregation is **collecting and processing**, data dissemination is **transmitting and receiving**.
  - Data aggregation is **information extraction**, data dissemination is **information distribution**.
