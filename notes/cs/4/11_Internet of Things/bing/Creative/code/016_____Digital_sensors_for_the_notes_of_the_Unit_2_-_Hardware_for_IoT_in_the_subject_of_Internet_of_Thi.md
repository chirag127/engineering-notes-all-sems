### Digital sensors for the notes of the Unit 2 - Hardware for IoT in the subject of Internet of Things

- Digital sensors are pieces of hardware that detect changes in an environment and collect data in a digital format .
- Digital sensors are connected to a network and share data with the network, enabling the Internet of Things (IoT) applications .
- Digital sensors can be classified based on the type of data they measure, such as temperature, pressure, motion, light, sound, etc .
- Digital sensors can also be classified based on the type of communication protocol they use, such as Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, Zigbee, LoRa, etc .
- Digital sensors have advantages over analog sensors, such as higher accuracy, lower noise, easier integration, and lower power consumption .
- Digital sensors have challenges, such as security, privacy, interoperability, scalability, and reliability .
- Digital sensors are used in various IoT applications, such as consumer devices, industry 4.0, smart cities, healthcare, agriculture, etc  .
