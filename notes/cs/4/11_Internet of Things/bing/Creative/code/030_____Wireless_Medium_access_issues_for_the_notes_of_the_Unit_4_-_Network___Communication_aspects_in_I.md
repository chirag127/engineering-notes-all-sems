### Wireless Medium Access Issues for the Notes of the Unit 4 - Network & Communication Aspects in IoT in the Subject of Internet of Things

- Wireless medium access issues refer to the challenges and problems that arise when multiple devices share the same wireless channel for data transmission in the Internet of Things (IoT) environment.
- Wireless medium access control (MAC) protocols are responsible for coordinating the access among the IoT devices and ensuring efficient, reliable, and fair data delivery  .
- Some of the wireless medium access issues in IoT are:
  - Interference: Interference occurs when multiple devices transmit signals at the same time or in the same frequency band, causing distortion or corruption of the received signals . Interference can degrade the network performance, increase the packet loss rate, and reduce the energy efficiency of the IoT devices.
  - Hidden terminal problem: Hidden terminal problem occurs when two devices that are out of the range of each other try to communicate with a common receiver, resulting in collisions and retransmissions . Hidden terminal problem can reduce the network throughput and increase the delay and energy consumption of the IoT devices.
  - Exposed terminal problem: Exposed terminal problem occurs when a device that is in the range of a transmitter and a receiver refrains from transmitting to avoid interfering with the ongoing transmission, even though it could have transmitted without causing any interference . Exposed terminal problem can reduce the network utilization and increase the idle time of the IoT devices.
  - Near-far problem: Near-far problem occurs when a device that is closer to the receiver transmits with a higher power than a device that is farther away, causing the latter device to be drowned out by the former device . Near-far problem can cause unfairness and starvation among the IoT devices.
  - Scalability: Scalability refers to the ability of the network to accommodate a large number of IoT devices without compromising the network performance . Scalability is a challenge for wireless medium access control protocols, as they have to deal with the increased contention, congestion, and collision among the IoT devices.
