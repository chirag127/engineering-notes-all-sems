### Smart Cards for the Notes of the Unit 7 - IoT Applications in the Subject of Internet of Things

- A smart card is a physical device that contains a microprocessor or a microcontroller that can store and process data, communicate with other devices, and perform cryptographic operations .
- Smart cards can be used to enhance the security and functionality of IoT devices and applications by providing authentication, encryption, key management, and data storage .
- Some of the IoT applications that can benefit from smart cards are:
  - Smartwatches and fitness trackers: Smart cards can enable secure and convenient payment, identity verification, and health data management for wearable devices.
  - Healthcare monitors and sensors: Smart cards can provide secure and reliable communication and data transfer between medical devices and healthcare providers, as well as protect the privacy and integrity of patient information .
  - Smart home and building automation: Smart cards can enable access control, energy management, and remote monitoring for smart devices and systems in homes and buildings.
  - Smart transportation and logistics: Smart cards can facilitate ticketing, toll collection, vehicle identification, and fleet management for smart mobility and transportation solutions.
