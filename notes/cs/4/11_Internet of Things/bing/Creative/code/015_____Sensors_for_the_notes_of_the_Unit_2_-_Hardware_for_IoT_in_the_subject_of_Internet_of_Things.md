### Sensors for the notes of the Unit 2 - Hardware for IoT in the subject of Internet of Things

- Sensors are devices that detect and measure physical phenomena, such as temperature, pressure, motion, light, sound, etc.
- Sensors are essential components of IoT systems, as they collect the data from the physical world and send it to the cloud or other devices for processing, analysis, and action.
- Sensors can be classified into different types based on their function, output, technology, or application domain.
- Some of the common types of sensors for IoT are:

  - Temperature sensors: These sensors measure the amount of heat generated from an area or an object. They are widely used in IoT applications such as smart home, smart agriculture, smart health, etc. Examples of temperature sensors are thermocouples, thermistors, resistance temperature detectors (RTDs), etc. 
  - Proximity sensors: These sensors detect the presence or absence of objects near the sensor without physical contact. They are useful for IoT applications such as security, automation, robotics, etc. Examples of proximity sensors are ultrasonic sensors, infrared sensors, capacitive sensors, etc. 
  - Pressure sensors: These sensors detect changes in a gas or liquid. They are used for IoT applications such as weather monitoring, industrial control, automotive, etc. Examples of pressure sensors are piezoresistive sensors, capacitive sensors, optical sensors, etc. 
  - Water quality sensors: These sensors measure parameters such as pH, dissolved oxygen, turbidity, conductivity, etc. of water. They are used for IoT applications such as environmental monitoring, water management, agriculture, etc. Examples of water quality sensors are electrochemical sensors, optical sensors, biosensors, etc. 
  - Chemical and gas sensors: These sensors measure the concentration of specific chemicals or gases in the air or liquid. They are used for IoT applications such as air quality monitoring, industrial safety, health care, etc. Examples of chemical and gas sensors are electrochemical sensors, metal oxide sensors, optical sensors, etc. 
  - Infrared sensors: These sensors detect the infrared radiation emitted or reflected by objects. They are used for IoT applications such as motion detection, thermal imaging, remote sensing, etc. Examples of infrared sensors are pyroelectric sensors, thermopile sensors, bolometer sensors, etc. 
  - Smoke sensors: These sensors detect the presence of smoke or fire in the environment. They are used for IoT applications such as fire safety, security, etc. Examples of smoke sensors are ionization sensors, photoelectric sensors, etc. 
  - Motion sensors: These sensors detect the movement or position of objects or people. They are used for IoT applications such as security, automation, gaming, etc. Examples of motion sensors are accelerometers, gyroscopes, magnetometers, etc. 
  - Light sensors: These sensors measure the intensity or wavelength of light. They are used for IoT applications such as ambient lighting, color detection, optical communication, etc. Examples of light sensors are photodiodes, phototransistors, photovoltaic cells, etc. 
  - Sound sensors: These sensors measure the amplitude or frequency of sound waves. They are used for IoT applications such as voice recognition, noise detection, acoustic sensing, etc. Examples of sound sensors are microphones, piezoelectric sensors, etc. 
  - Humidity sensors: These sensors measure the amount of water vapor in the air or other gases. They are used for IoT applications such as weather monitoring, agriculture, health care, etc. Examples of humidity sensors are capacitive sensors, resistive sensors, etc. 
  - Biomedical sensors: These sensors measure physiological parameters such as heart rate, blood pressure, glucose level, etc. of living beings. They are used for IoT applications such as health care, fitness, wellness, etc. Examples of biomedical sensors are electrocardiogram (ECG) sensors, blood pressure sensors, glucose sensors, etc. 
  - Accelerometers: These sensors measure acceleration, which is the change in speed, direction, and intensity of movement. They are used for IoT applications such as motion detection, gesture recognition, navigation, etc. Examples of accelerometers are piezoelectric sensors, capacitive sensors, etc.
