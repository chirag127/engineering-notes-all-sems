### Beagle Bone

Beagle Bone is a series of low-cost, open-source, single-board computers (SBCs) that run Linux and can be used for various embedded applications, such as robotics, 3D printing, home automation, and Internet of Things (IoT). 

Some of the features and benefits of Beagle Bone are:

- It has a small size (about the size of a credit card) and a low power consumption (about 2W).
- It has a high-performance processor (ARM Cortex-A8 or ARM Cortex-A15) that can run up to 1 GHz and support various operating systems, such as Android, Ubuntu, Debian, and Angstrom.  
- It has plenty of input/output (I/O) interfaces, such as USB, Ethernet, HDMI, audio, serial, and GPIO, that can be used to connect various sensors, actuators, displays, and peripherals. 
- It has a rich ecosystem of add-on boards, called capes, that can extend the functionality of the Beagle Bone, such as adding wireless connectivity, LCD screens, cameras, motor drivers, and more. 
- It has a large and active community of developers, hobbyists, and educators, who provide support, documentation, tutorials, and projects for the Beagle Bone. 

Some of the examples of Beagle Bone products are:

- BeagleBone Black: The most popular and widely used Beagle Bone product, which has a 1 GHz ARM Cortex-A8 processor, 512 MB of RAM, 4 GB of eMMC flash storage, and a 3D graphics accelerator. It costs about $60.00. 
- BeagleBone Green: A variant of the BeagleBone Black, which has two Grove connectors for easy plug-and-play of sensors and actuators. It costs about $70.00. 
- BeagleBone Blue: A Beagle Bone product designed for robotics, which has a built-in battery management, a 9-axis inertial measurement unit (IMU), a barometer, a magnetometer, four DC motor drivers, eight servo outputs, and four quadrature encoder inputs. It costs about $80.00.
- BeagleBone AI: A Beagle Bone product designed for artificial intelligence (AI) and machine learning (ML), which has a 1.5 GHz ARM Cortex-A15 processor, 1 GB of RAM, 16 GB of eMMC flash storage, and a dual-core PowerVR SGX544 GPU. It also has a Texas Instruments C66x digital signal processor (DSP), four embedded vision engines (EVEs), and two dual-core programmable real-time units (PRUs). It costs about $100.00. 
- PocketBeagle: A tiny and low-cost Beagle Bone product, which has a 1 GHz ARM Cortex-A8 processor, 512 MB of RAM, and a microSD card slot. It costs about $25.00.
