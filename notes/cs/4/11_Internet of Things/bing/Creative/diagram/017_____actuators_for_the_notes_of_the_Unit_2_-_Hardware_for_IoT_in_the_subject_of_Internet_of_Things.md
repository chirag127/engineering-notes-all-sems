### Actuators for IoT

- An actuator is a device that converts energy into motion. It does this by taking an electrical signal and combining it with an energy source.
- In IoT, actuators enable a physical action based on data that originates with one or more sensors. The conversion of sensor data to activity follows this sequence:
  - Sensors detect an event in the physical environment.
  - The sensor data is transmitted to a gateway or cloud platform via a communication network.
  - The gateway or cloud platform processes the sensor data and applies the logic or rules defined by the user or application.
  - The gateway or cloud platform sends a command to the actuator to perform a specific action.
  - The actuator receives the command and executes the action using its energy source.
- Some examples of actuators in IoT are :
  - Servo Motors: A servomotor is a rotary actuator or linear actuator that allows for precise control of angular or linear position, velocity and acceleration. Servo motors are commonly used in robotics, automation, CNC machines, etc.
  - Stepper Motors: Stepper motors are DC motors that move in discrete steps. They can be controlled by pulses of current and can achieve high torque and precision. Stepper motors are often used in printers, scanners, cameras, etc.
  - DC Motors (Continuous Rotation Motors): DC motors are two wire (power & ground), continuous rotation motors. They can vary their speed and direction by changing the polarity and voltage of the current. DC motors are widely used in toys, fans, pumps, etc.
  - Linear Actuator: A linear actuator is a device that converts rotational motion into linear motion. It can be driven by a motor, a hydraulic system, a pneumatic system, etc. Linear actuators are used in medical devices, industrial machines, etc.
  - Soft Actuators: Soft actuators are made of flexible materials that can deform or change shape when stimulated by an external force. They can be actuated by thermal, magnetic, electric, light, or chemical energy. Soft actuators are used in wearable devices, soft robotics, etc.
- Actuators are essential components of IoT systems that enable interaction with the physical world. They can perform various tasks such as opening or closing a valve, adjusting the temperature, turning on or off a light, etc. Actuators can also work in conjunction with other actuators or sensors to create complex and dynamic behaviors.
