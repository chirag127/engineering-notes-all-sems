The Internet of Things (IoT) is a network of physical objects embedded with sensors and actuators that communicate with computing systems via wired or wireless networks, allowing the physical world to be digitally monitored or even controlled. The vision of IoT is to enable seamless ubiquitous connectivity among things and human beings, and to provide intelligent computing services for various applications and domains. There are different perspectives on the IoT vision, such as Internet-centric, thing-centric, or semantic-centric.
