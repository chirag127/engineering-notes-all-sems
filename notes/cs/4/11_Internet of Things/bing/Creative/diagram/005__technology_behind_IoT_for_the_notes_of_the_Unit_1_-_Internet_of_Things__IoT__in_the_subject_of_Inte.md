The IoT gateway acts as a bridge between the IoT devices and the IoT platform, which provides data storage, analysis, security, transfer and application integration. The IoT platform enables the IoT applications to access and use the data from the IoT devices and provide services and solutions for different domains and scenarios. The IoT connectivity, analytics and applications are the three main layers of the IoT technology stack that enable the IoT system to work.
