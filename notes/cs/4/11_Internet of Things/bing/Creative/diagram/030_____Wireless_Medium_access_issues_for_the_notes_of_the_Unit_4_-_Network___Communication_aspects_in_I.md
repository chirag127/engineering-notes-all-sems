### Wireless Medium Access Issues for the Notes of the Unit 4 - Network & Communication Aspects in IoT in the Subject of Internet of Things

- Wireless medium access issues refer to the challenges and problems that arise when multiple IoT devices share the same wireless channel for data transmission and reception.
- Wireless medium access issues can affect the network performance, reliability, energy efficiency, and latency of IoT applications  .
- Some of the wireless medium access issues in IoT are:
  - Interference: Interference occurs when the signals from different IoT devices or other wireless sources overlap and cause distortion or corruption of the data . Interference can reduce the signal-to-noise ratio, increase the packet error rate, and decrease the network throughput. Interference can be caused by external sources, such as WLAN, Bluetooth, microwave ovens, or other IoT devices operating in the same frequency band .
  - Hidden terminal problem: Hidden terminal problem occurs when two or more IoT devices are within the transmission range of a common receiver, but not within the transmission range of each other . This can lead to collisions and packet loss, as the devices are unaware of each other's transmissions and may transmit at the same time .
  - Exposed terminal problem: Exposed terminal problem occurs when two or more IoT devices are within the transmission range of each other, but not within the transmission range of their intended receivers . This can lead to unnecessary waiting and channel wastage, as the devices may refrain from transmitting when they sense the channel is busy, even though their transmissions will not interfere with each other's receivers .
  - Near-far problem: Near-far problem occurs when two or more IoT devices have different distances from a common receiver, and the signal strength of the closer device is much higher than the signal strength of the farther device . This can lead to unfairness and starvation, as the closer device may dominate the channel and prevent the farther device from accessing the channel .
  - Scalability: Scalability refers to the ability of the network to accommodate the increasing number of IoT devices and data traffic without degrading the network performance and quality of service . Scalability is a challenge for wireless medium access, as the network may face congestion, collision, delay, and energy consumption issues when the number of IoT devices and data traffic grow exponentially .
- To address the wireless medium access issues in IoT, various medium access control (MAC) protocols have been proposed and developed  . MAC protocols are responsible for coordinating and regulating the access of IoT devices to the shared wireless channel  . MAC protocols can be classified into different categories, such as contention-based, reservation-based, hybrid, cognitive, and cooperative  . Each category has its own advantages and disadvantages, and may be suitable for different IoT scenarios and applications  .
