## Unit 7 - IoT Applications

- IoT applications are the use cases of IoT technology that provide various benefits to different industries, organizations and individuals.
- IoT applications can be classified into several categories, such as:
  - Transportation and mobility
  - Energy and utilities
  - Environment and agriculture
  - Buildings and homes
  - Cities and communities
  - Supply chain and logistics
  - Industrial, commercial and agricultural management
  - Healthcare and wellness
- Some examples of IoT applications in each category are:

  - Transportation and mobility: Connected vehicles, traffic management, smart parking, fleet management, etc.
  - Energy and utilities: Smart grids, smart meters, demand response, renewable energy integration, etc.
  - Environment and agriculture: Air quality monitoring, water quality monitoring, soil moisture monitoring, precision farming, etc.
  - Buildings and homes: Smart lighting, smart thermostats, smart locks, smart security, smart appliances, etc.
  - Cities and communities: Smart street lights, smart waste management, smart public safety, smart governance, etc.
  - Supply chain and logistics: Asset tracking, inventory management, warehouse automation, cold chain monitoring, etc.
  - Industrial, commercial and agricultural management: Predictive maintenance, remote monitoring, process optimization, quality control, etc.
  - Healthcare and wellness: Wearable devices, telemedicine, remote patient monitoring, medication adherence, etc.

- The benefits of IoT applications include:
  - Improving efficiency and productivity
  - Reducing costs and waste
  - Enhancing safety and security
  - Increasing customer satisfaction and loyalty
  - Creating new business opportunities and revenue streams
  - Solving social and environmental challenges
