### Architectural view

- The architectural view of IoT is a way of describing the components, relationships, and functions of an IoT system.
- There are different models and frameworks for the architectural view of IoT, but a common one is the three-layer architecture, which consists of the perception layer, the network layer, and the application layer.
- The perception layer is the lowest layer of the IoT system, which is responsible for sensing, collecting, and processing data from the physical world. It includes devices such as sensors, actuators, RFID tags, cameras, etc.
- The network layer is the middle layer of the IoT system, which is responsible for transmitting, storing, and processing data from the perception layer to the application layer. It includes technologies such as wireless networks, cloud computing, edge computing, etc.
- The application layer is the highest layer of the IoT system, which is responsible for providing services and applications to the users and consumers of the IoT system. It includes domains such as smart home, smart city, smart health, smart agriculture, etc.
