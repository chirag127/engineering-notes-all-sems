## Unit 3 - Embedded Platforms for IoT

An embedded platform for IoT is a combination of hardware and software that enables IoT devices to perform specific tasks, such as sensing, processing, communicating, and acting. Embedded platforms for IoT can vary in terms of complexity, functionality, power consumption, and cost, depending on the application requirements and the device capabilities.
