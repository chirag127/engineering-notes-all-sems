The Conceptual Framework for the notes of the Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT) in the subject of Internet of Things is a way of describing the basic components and processes of IoT systems. According to , the Conceptual Framework can be expressed as:

Gather + Consolidate + Connect + Collect + Assemble + Manage and Analyse = Internet of Things with connectivity to cloud services.

The Sensor Layer is responsible for gathering data from the physical world and sending commands to actuators. The Network Layer is responsible for consolidating and transmitting the data from the sensors to the cloud or other devices. The Application Layer is responsible for collecting, assembling, managing and analysing the data from the network layer and providing services and user interfaces for the IoT applications.
