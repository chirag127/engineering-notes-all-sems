### Data enrichment and consolidation for the notes of the Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT) in the subject of Internet of Things

- Data enrichment is the process of enhancing, refining, or otherwise improving raw data by adding or integrating other data sources.
- Data consolidation is the process of combining data from multiple sources into a single, consistent, and integrated view.
- Data enrichment and consolidation are important for IoT applications because they can improve data visibility, data security, and data analysis options.
- For example, in a supply chain scenario, IoT data from sensors and devices can be enriched with non-IoT data from databases, contracts, or other sources to create a more complete and accurate picture of the assets, transactions, and events .
- To enrich and consolidate IoT data, the following steps can be performed :
  - Define a correlation parameter that can link IoT data and non-IoT data, such as the IoT device ID, the asset ID, or the transaction ID.
  - Use a contract, a Node-RED flow, or another method to match IoT data and non-IoT data based on the correlation parameter.
  - Merge the matched IoT data and non-IoT data to create an enriched asset in accordance with a contract or a predefined schema.
  - Store the enriched asset in the blockchain, a database, or another storage system.
  - Send an enriched IoT event to an IoT application or a third-party service as a notification of the success of the transaction and include the transaction data.
- The benefits of enriching and consolidating IoT data include :
  - Increased data quality, accuracy, and completeness
  - Enhanced data security and traceability through blockchain
  - Improved data visibility and accessibility across the IoT network
  - Advanced data analysis and insights through machine learning, artificial intelligence, or other methods
  - Personalized data segmentation and targeting for advertisements and recommendations
