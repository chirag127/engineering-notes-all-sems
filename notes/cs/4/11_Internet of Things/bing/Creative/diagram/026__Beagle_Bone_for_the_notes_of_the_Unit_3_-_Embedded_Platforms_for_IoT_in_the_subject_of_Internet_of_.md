The BeagleBone Black is a low-cost, open-source, single-board computer that can run Linux and other operating systems. It has a 1GHz ARM Cortex-A8 processor, 512MB of DDR3 RAM, 4GB of eMMC flash storage, and various interfaces such as USB, Ethernet, HDMI, and GPIO pins.
