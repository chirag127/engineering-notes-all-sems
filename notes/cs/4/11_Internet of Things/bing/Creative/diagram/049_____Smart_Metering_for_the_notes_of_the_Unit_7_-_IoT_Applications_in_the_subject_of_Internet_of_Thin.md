### Smart Metering

- Smart metering is the monitoring of resource consumption, such as energy, water, gas, etc., through modern metering devices connected to the Internet via IoT technology.
- Smart metering devices, also known as smart meters, can record and communicate energy consumption data to utilities for monitoring and other use cases, such as billing and demand response.
- Smart metering can provide in-depth analysis of resource usage, efficiency, and patterns, enabling a more streamlined and sustainable approach to resource management.
- Smart metering can also benefit end-users by allowing them to monitor their own consumption, receive accurate and timely bills, and participate in dynamic pricing schemes.
- Smart metering is one of the most significant IoT initiatives worldwide, as it can help utilities reduce operational costs, improve customer service, and optimize grid performance.
- Smart metering IoT devices typically use low-power wide-area network (LPWAN) connectivity and embedded SIM (eSIM) technology to ensure reliable, secure, and cost-effective data transmission.
- Smart metering IoT devices can also integrate with other IoT applications, such as smart lighting, smart parking, smart waste management, etc., to create smart city solutions.
