### M2M Communication

- M2M stands for Machine to Machine communication, which refers to the automated exchange of data between machines, devices, or systems without human intervention.
- M2M is the foundation for IoT (Internet of Things), which is a network of physical objects embedded with sensors, software, and connectivity that enable data collection and analysis.
- M2M communication can occur over various types of networks, such as cellular, wired, wireless, or satellite.
- M2M communication enables applications such as remote monitoring, asset tracking, smart metering, industrial automation, healthcare, and smart cities.
- M2M communication can improve efficiency, productivity, safety, and customer satisfaction by providing real-time information and control.
