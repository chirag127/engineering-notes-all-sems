Sensor deployment and node discovery are important aspects of IoT networks, as they determine the topology, coverage, connectivity, and energy efficiency of the system. Sensor deployment can be either deterministic or random, depending on the application and environment. Node discovery is the process of identifying and locating the sensor nodes in the network, which can be done using various techniques such as broadcasting, clustering, or localization algorithms.
