The diagram illustrates the basic architecture of a typical IoT system, consisting of three main components: IoT devices, IoT gateway, and IoT platform. Each component has its own features and challenges that affect the ease of designing and affordability of the IoT system.

The IoT devices are the physical objects that collect data from the environment or perform actions based on commands. They include sensors, actuators, controllers, and RFID or Mote devices. The ease of designing and affordability of IoT devices depend on factors such as low cost, low power, availability, and embedded software.

The IoT gateway is the intermediary device that connects the IoT devices to the IoT platform. It performs functions such as data link, adaptation, security, and connectivity. The ease of designing and affordability of IoT gateway depend on factors such as ease of use, standardization, compatibility, and configurability.

The IoT platform is the cloud-based service that provides the functionality and intelligence for the IoT system. It performs functions such as usability, affordance, integration, and analytics. The ease of designing and affordability of IoT platform depend on factors such as open source, scalability, reliability, and security.
