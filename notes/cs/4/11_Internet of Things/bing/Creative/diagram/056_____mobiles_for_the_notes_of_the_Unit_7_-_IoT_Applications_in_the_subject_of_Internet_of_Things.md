### Mobiles for the notes of the Unit 7 - IoT Applications in the subject of Internet of Things

- Mobiles are devices that can communicate wirelessly with other devices or networks, such as smartphones, tablets, laptops, etc.
- Mobiles can act as both IoT devices and IoT applications, depending on their role and functionality in the IoT system.
- As IoT devices, mobiles can collect, process, and transmit data from their sensors or other connected devices, such as cameras, GPS, accelerometers, etc.
- As IoT applications, mobiles can provide a user interface to interact with IoT devices or systems, such as sending commands, receiving notifications, configuring settings, analyzing data, etc.
- Mobile IoT applications are software programs that run on mobile devices and enable the use of IoT technologies and services.
- Mobile IoT applications can be classified into two categories: consumer and enterprise.
- Consumer mobile IoT applications are designed for personal or household use, such as smart home, health and fitness, entertainment, etc.
- Enterprise mobile IoT applications are designed for business or industrial use, such as asset tracking, predictive maintenance, supply chain management, etc.
- Some examples of mobile IoT applications are:

  - Electric bikes tracking: A mobile app that allows users to locate, unlock, and pay for shared electric bikes using NB-IoT technology.
  - Smoke and gas detectors: A mobile app that monitors the status and alerts of smoke and gas detectors installed in buildings using NB-IoT technology.
  - Asset tracking: A mobile app that tracks the location and condition of assets, such as vehicles, containers, or equipment, using LTE-M or NB-IoT technology.
  - IoT PnP: A mobile app that connects to Azure IoT Central and simulates an IoT device, sending telemetry data and receiving commands using QR codes.

- Mobile IoT applications have several benefits, such as:

  - Enhancing user experience and convenience by providing remote control and access to IoT devices or systems.
  - Improving efficiency and productivity by enabling real-time data collection and analysis, automation, and optimization of IoT processes or operations.
  - Reducing costs and risks by facilitating predictive maintenance, asset management, and safety monitoring of IoT devices or systems.
  - Increasing innovation and competitiveness by creating new business models, services, and opportunities based on IoT technologies and data.
