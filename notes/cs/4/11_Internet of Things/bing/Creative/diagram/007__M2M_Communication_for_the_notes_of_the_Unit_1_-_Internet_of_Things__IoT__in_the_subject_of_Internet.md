M2M communication stands for Machine to Machine communication and is a technology that enables devices to exchange data without human intervention. M2M communication is a key component of IoT, which is the network of physical devices, vehicles, home appliances and other items embedded with electronics, software, sensors, actuators and connectivity that enables these objects to connect and exchange data .
