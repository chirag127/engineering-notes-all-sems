### IoT/M2M systems layers and design standardization

- IoT/M2M systems are composed of interconnected devices and applications that communicate and exchange data over the internet or other networks.
- IoT/M2M systems require standardization to ensure interoperability, security, scalability, and efficiency of the solutions and services.
- Standardization can be done at different layers of the IoT/M2M systems, such as the radio layer, the network layer, the service layer, and the application layer.
- The radio layer deals with the physical and medium access control (MAC) aspects of the wireless communication between devices and gateways. Some of the standards in this layer are LTE-M, NB-IoT, EC-GSM-IoT, LoRaWAN, Sigfox, etc.
- The network layer deals with the routing, addressing, and transport protocols for the data transmission over the internet or other networks. Some of the standards in this layer are IPv6, 6LoWPAN, CoAP, MQTT, HTTP, etc.
- The service layer deals with the common functionalities and interfaces for the IoT/M2M applications, such as device management, data management, security, discovery, etc. Some of the standards in this layer are oneM2M, OMA LwM2M, OCF, etc.
- The application layer deals with the specific logic and semantics of the IoT/M2M applications, such as data models, ontologies, APIs, etc. Some of the standards in this layer are SAREF, NGSI-LD, WoT, etc.
