### Designing of smart street lights in smart city

- Smart street lights are street lights that can adjust their brightness, color temperature, and operation according to various factors, such as weather, traffic, time, and events.
- Smart street lights can save energy, reduce costs, improve safety, and support various smart city applications, such as traffic management, environmental monitoring, and public security.
- Smart street lights can be connected to a central management system via wireless communication technologies, such as cellular, ZigBee, or LoRaWAN, which enable remote control, monitoring, and data collection.
- Smart street lights can also be equipped with sensors, cameras, and other devices that can collect and transmit data about the surrounding environment, such as temperature, humidity, air quality, noise, traffic flow, and pedestrian activity.
- Smart street lights can use the data collected from the sensors and cameras to adjust their lighting parameters, such as brightness, color temperature, and flashing patterns, to optimize the lighting performance and user experience.
- Smart street lights can also use the data collected from the sensors and cameras to provide information and services to the city authorities, citizens, and businesses, such as traffic alerts, weather forecasts, parking availability, and emergency notifications.
- Smart street lights can be integrated with other smart city infrastructure, such as smart grids, smart buildings, and smart transportation, to create a more efficient, sustainable, and livable urban environment.
