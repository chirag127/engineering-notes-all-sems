## Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT)

- The Internet of Things (IoT) is a network of physical objects that are embedded with sensors, actuators, and communication devices that can interact with each other and with other devices or systems over the internet.
- The IoT enables various applications and services that can improve the quality of life, efficiency, productivity, and sustainability of different domains, such as smart homes, smart cities, smart health, smart agriculture, smart industry, and smart environment.
- The IoT also poses various challenges and issues, such as security, privacy, interoperability, scalability, reliability, and energy consumption, that need to be addressed by researchers and practitioners.
- The IoT can be classified into different types based on the characteristics of the devices, the communication technologies, the architectures, the protocols, the applications, and the services involved.
- Some of the common types of IoT are:

  - Consumer IoT: This type of IoT focuses on providing smart and personalized services to individual users or households, such as smart appliances, smart wearables, smart entertainment, smart security, and smart assistants.
  - Industrial IoT: This type of IoT focuses on improving the efficiency, productivity, quality, and safety of industrial processes, such as manufacturing, logistics, mining, and energy.
  - Enterprise IoT: This type of IoT focuses on enhancing the performance, management, and optimization of business operations, such as asset tracking, inventory control, supply chain management, and customer relationship management.
  - Infrastructure IoT: This type of IoT focuses on monitoring and controlling the physical infrastructure, such as transportation, water, electricity, gas, and waste management.
  - Environmental IoT: This type of IoT focuses on sensing and analyzing the environmental conditions, such as air quality, weather, soil, and water, and providing information and alerts for decision making and action.
  - Social IoT: This type of IoT focuses on enabling social interactions and collaborations among devices, users, and communities, such as social networking, crowdsourcing, and gamification.
