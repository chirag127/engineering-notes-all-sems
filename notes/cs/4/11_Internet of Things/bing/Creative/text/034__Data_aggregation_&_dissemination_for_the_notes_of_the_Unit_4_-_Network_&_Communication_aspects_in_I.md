### Data aggregation and dissemination for the notes of the Unit 4 - Network and Communication aspects in IoT in the subject of Internet of Things

- Data aggregation and dissemination are two fundamental services in Internet of Things (IoT) networks.
- Data aggregation is the process of collecting data from multiple sources, such as sensors, devices, or applications, and transforming it into a unified format for further analysis or processing.
- Data dissemination is the process of distributing data to multiple destinations, such as users, applications, or other devices, in a timely and efficient manner.
- Data aggregation and dissemination can support various functions and applications in IoT networks, such as time synchronization, software updating, routing path formation, neighbor discovery, data analytics, and decision making.
- Data aggregation and dissemination can also improve the performance and efficiency of IoT networks, such as reducing network traffic, saving energy, enhancing reliability, and ensuring security.
- Data aggregation and dissemination can be challenging in IoT networks due to the heterogeneity, scalability, dynamics, and resource constraints of the network devices and the data sources.
- Data aggregation and dissemination can be performed at different levels of the IoT network, such as the edge, the fog, or the cloud, depending on the requirements and capabilities of the network devices and the data sources.
- Data aggregation and dissemination can involve different techniques and protocols, such as cross-technology communication (CTC), network coding, constructive interference, data compression, data fusion, data filtering, data scheduling, and data routing.
