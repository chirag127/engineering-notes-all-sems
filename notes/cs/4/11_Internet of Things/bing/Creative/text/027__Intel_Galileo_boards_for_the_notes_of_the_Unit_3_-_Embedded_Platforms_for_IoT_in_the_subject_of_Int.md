### Intel Galileo boards

- Intel Galileo boards are Arduino-certified development boards based on Intel x86 architecture and are designed for the maker and education communities.
- Intel released two versions of Galileo, referred to as Gen 1 and Gen 2, which have some differences in features and specifications.
- Galileo boards are based on the Intel® Quark SoC X1000 Application Processor, a 32-bit Intel Pentium-class system on a chip with a clock speed of 400 MHz  .
- Galileo boards have 256 MB of DDR3 memory, 8 MB of NOR flash memory, and a microSD card slot for additional storage .
- Galileo boards support a wide range of I/O interfaces, such as USB, Ethernet, UART, SPI, I2C, PWM, and analog inputs  .
- Galileo boards are compatible with the Arduino software development environment and can use Arduino shields designed for the Uno R3  .
- Galileo boards also have some PC industry standard I/O ports and features, such as a mini-PCI Express slot, a 10/100 Mb Ethernet port, a RS-232 serial port, and a JTAG header for debugging .
- Galileo boards can run Linux-based operating systems, such as Yocto Project, and can also support other operating systems, such as Windows and RTOS .
- Galileo boards can be used for various Internet of Things (IoT) applications, such as smart home, wearable, robotics, automation, and education.
