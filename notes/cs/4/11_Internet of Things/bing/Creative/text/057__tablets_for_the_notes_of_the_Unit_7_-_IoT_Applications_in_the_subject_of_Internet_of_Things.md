### Tablets for the notes of the Unit 7 - IoT Applications in the subject of Internet of Things

- Tablets are portable devices that can run various applications and connect to the internet via Wi-Fi or cellular networks.
- Tablets can be used as IoT devices or as interfaces to access and control other IoT devices and applications.
- Tablets can provide various benefits for IoT applications, such as:
  - Enhanced visualization and interaction with data-intensive IoT applications, such as smart home, health monitoring, industrial automation, etc. 
  - Improved mobility and convenience for users who can access IoT applications from anywhere and anytime. 
  - Increased functionality and versatility for users who can use tablets for multiple purposes, such as viewing drawings and manuals, videoconferencing, reporting, etc. 
  - Reduced cost and complexity for IoT developers who can leverage existing tablet platforms and software to create IoT applications. 
- Tablets can also pose some challenges and limitations for IoT applications, such as:
  - Security and privacy risks for users who may expose their personal or sensitive data to unauthorized access or malicious attacks. 
  - Durability and reliability issues for tablets that may not withstand harsh industrial or environmental conditions. 
  - Compatibility and interoperability problems for tablets that may not support certain IoT protocols, standards, or devices. 
  - Battery life and performance constraints for tablets that may not handle high-volume or real-time IoT data processing.
