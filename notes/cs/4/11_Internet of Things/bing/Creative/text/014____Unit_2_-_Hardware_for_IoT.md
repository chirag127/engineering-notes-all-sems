## Unit 2 - Hardware for IoT

- Hardware for IoT refers to the physical devices and components that enable the connectivity, communication, and functionality of IoT applications.
- Hardware for IoT can be classified into four main categories: sensors, microcontrollers, communication modules, and other IoT hardware.
- Sensors are the most critical hardware in IoT applications and are used to gather information from the surroundings. These systems are made up of power management modules, RF, energy and sensing modules .
- Microcontrollers are devices in a single integrated circuit devoted to executing a single task and running an application. They are responsible for processing the data collected by the sensors and sending it to the communication modules.
- Communication modules are the hardware that manage the wireless or wired transmission of data between the IoT devices and the cloud or other devices. They can use various protocols and technologies such as WiFi, Bluetooth, ZigBee, cellular, LoRa, etc .
- Other IoT hardware are the devices that provide additional functionality or support to the IoT applications, such as actuators, displays, cameras, batteries, etc .
- Some examples of IoT hardware platforms and prototyping kits are Raspberry Pi, Arduino, ESP32, Particle, BeagleBone, etc. These platforms provide a combination of hardware and software components that can be used to develop and test IoT applications.
