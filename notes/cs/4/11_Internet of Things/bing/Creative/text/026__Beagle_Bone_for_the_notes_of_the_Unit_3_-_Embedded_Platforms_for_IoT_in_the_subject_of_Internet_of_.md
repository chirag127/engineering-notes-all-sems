### BeagleBone

- BeagleBone is a family of low-cost, open-source, Linux-based computers that can be used for various applications such as IoT, robotics, gaming, AI, etc.     
- BeagleBone boards are small, credit-card-sized devices that can run different operating systems such as Debian, Android, Ubuntu, etc. and can be connected to the Internet and other peripherals.     
- BeagleBone boards are powered by ARM processors that provide high-performance, low-power, and flexible functionality. They also have co-processors for real-time tasks, graphics, and floating-point operations.   
- BeagleBone boards have 512MB or 1GB of RAM, 4GB or 16GB of on-board flash storage, microSD card slot, USB ports, Ethernet port, HDMI port, and 2x 46 pin headers for connecting various sensors and actuators.   
- BeagleBone boards can be extended with cape plug-in boards that add extra features such as LCD display, camera, motor control, wireless communication, etc.   
- BeagleBone boards are supported by a large and active community of developers, hobbyists, and educators who provide software, documentation, tutorials, projects, and support.
