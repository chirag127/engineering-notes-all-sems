### Automotive Applications

The Internet of Things (IoT) is a network of connected devices that can communicate, collect, and exchange data over the internet. IoT has many applications in various industries, including the automotive industry. IoT can enhance the performance, safety, efficiency, and user experience of vehicles and transportation systems. Some of the applications of IoT in the automotive industry are:

- **Fleet management**: IoT can help fleet operators and owners monitor the location, speed, fuel consumption, and maintenance status of their vehicles in real time. This can help optimize routes, reduce costs, improve safety, and increase customer satisfaction. IoT can also enable remote diagnostics and updates of the vehicles, as well as driver behavior management and training.
- **Vehicle tracking**: IoT can help drivers and owners track their vehicles using GPS and cellular networks. This can help prevent theft, recover stolen vehicles, and provide insurance benefits. IoT can also help drivers find parking spaces, pay tolls, and access traffic information .
- **Connected cars**: IoT can enable cars to communicate with each other, with the infrastructure, and with the cloud. This can enable features such as adaptive cruise control, lane departure warning, collision avoidance, and autonomous driving. IoT can also enhance the in-vehicle infotainment and telematics systems, such as navigation, entertainment, voice control, and emergency services  .
- **Automotive maintenance system**: IoT can help drivers and owners monitor the health and performance of their vehicles, and alert them of any potential issues or faults. IoT can also enable predictive maintenance, which can prevent breakdowns, extend the lifespan, and reduce the repair costs of the vehicles. IoT can also help manufacturers and dealers improve their quality management and customer service   .
- **Autonomous vehicles**: IoT can enable vehicles to drive themselves without human intervention, using sensors, cameras, radar, lidar, and artificial intelligence. Autonomous vehicles can offer benefits such as improved safety, reduced congestion, lower emissions, and increased mobility and convenience. IoT can also enable vehicle-to-grid integration, which can allow autonomous vehicles to store and share energy with the power grid  .

IoT is changing the face of the automotive industry, and creating new opportunities and challenges for the stakeholders. IoT can help improve the design, performance, and cost of the vehicles, while ensuring quality control and customer satisfaction. IoT can also create new business models and revenue streams, such as subscription-based services, data monetization, and mobility-as-a-service. However, IoT also poses some risks and barriers, such as cybersecurity, privacy, regulation, standardization, and interoperability  . Therefore, the automotive industry needs to adopt IoT strategically and collaboratively, and leverage its potential to create value and competitive advantage.
