### Definition

The Internet of Things (IoT) is the concept of connecting any device (so long as it has an on/off switch) to the Internet and to other connected devices. The IoT is a giant network of connected things and people – all of which collect and share data about the way they are used and about the environment around them.

The IoT enables devices and objects with built-in sensors, processing ability, software and other technologies to communicate and exchange data with other devices and systems over the Internet or other communications networks.

The IoT can also be defined as the integration of people, processes and technology with connectable devices and sensors to enable remote monitoring, status, manipulation and evaluation of trends of such devices.

Some examples of IoT devices are smart microwaves, self-driving cars, wearable fitness devices, connected footballs, smart thermostats and appliances, etc.
