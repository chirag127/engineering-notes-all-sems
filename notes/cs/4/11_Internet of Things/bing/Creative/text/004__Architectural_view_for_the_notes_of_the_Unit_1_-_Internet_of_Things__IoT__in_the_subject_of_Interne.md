### Architectural view for the notes of the Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT) in the subject of Internet of Things

- The architectural view of IoT is a way of describing the components, relationships, and functions of an IoT system.
- There are different architectural views for IoT, depending on the level of abstraction, the perspective, and the purpose of the analysis.
- Some of the common architectural views for IoT are:

  - **Device-centric view**: This view focuses on the physical devices that are connected to the IoT network, such as sensors, actuators, gateways, and controllers. It describes the characteristics, capabilities, and interfaces of the devices, as well as their communication protocols and standards.
  - **Data-centric view**: This view focuses on the data that is generated, collected, processed, and exchanged by the IoT devices and applications. It describes the data models, formats, quality, security, and analytics of the IoT data, as well as the data flows and storage options.
  - **Service-centric view**: This view focuses on the services that are provided by the IoT system to the users and other systems. It describes the functionality, quality, and availability of the IoT services, as well as the service discovery, composition, and orchestration mechanisms.
  - **Application-centric view**: This view focuses on the applications that are built on top of the IoT services and data. It describes the user requirements, scenarios, and interactions of the IoT applications, as well as the application logic, design, and development tools.
  - **Business-centric view**: This view focuses on the business value and impact of the IoT system. It describes the business goals, models, and strategies of the IoT system, as well as the business processes, roles, and stakeholders involved.

- These architectural views are not mutually exclusive, but rather complementary and interrelated. They can be used to analyze different aspects and dimensions of an IoT system, and to identify the challenges and opportunities for IoT development and deployment.
