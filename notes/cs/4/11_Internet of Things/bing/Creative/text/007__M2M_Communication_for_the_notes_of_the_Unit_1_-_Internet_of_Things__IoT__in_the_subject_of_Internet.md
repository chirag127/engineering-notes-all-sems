### M2M Communication for the notes of the Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT) in the subject of Internet of Things

- M2M stands for Machine-to-Machine communication, which refers to the exchange of data and information between devices without human intervention.
- M2M communication is a key component of the Internet of Things (IoT), which enables the interconnection and interaction of physical and virtual objects over the internet.
- M2M communication can be classified into two types: wired and wireless. Wired M2M communication uses cables, wires, or optical fibers to connect devices, while wireless M2M communication uses radio waves, infrared, or other electromagnetic signals to transmit data.
- M2M communication can be used for various applications, such as smart metering, remote monitoring, asset tracking, security, healthcare, agriculture, and industrial automation.
- M2M communication has several benefits, such as improving efficiency, reducing costs, enhancing safety, and creating new business opportunities.
- M2M communication also faces some challenges, such as interoperability, scalability, security, privacy, and reliability.
