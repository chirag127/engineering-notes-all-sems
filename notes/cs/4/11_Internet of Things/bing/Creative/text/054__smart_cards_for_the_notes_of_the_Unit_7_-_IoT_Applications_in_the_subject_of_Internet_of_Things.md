### Smart Cards

Smart cards are small portable devices that contain a microprocessor, some memory, and some applications. They are usually the size of a credit card and can communicate with a card reader or a mobile phone using contact or contactless technology. Smart cards are widely used in various industries and applications, such as payment, identity, mobile telephony, healthcare, and Internet of Things (IoT).

Some of the features and benefits of smart cards are:

- They can securely store and process data on the card, without relying on external systems or networks.
- They can authenticate the cardholder and the parties who want to access the card using methods such as PIN code or biometrics.
- They can protect the data and the communications with encryption and digital signatures.
- They can support multiple applications on the same card, such as payment, loyalty, transit, etc.
- They can enhance the user experience and convenience with contactless and mobile technologies.

Some of the use cases of smart cards in IoT are:

- Smart cards can be embedded into IoT devices as secure elements, which can authenticate the devices and the applications, and protect the data and the communications with encryption and key management.
- Smart cards can be used to authenticate the users and the administrators of IoT devices, and provide access control and authorization.
- Smart cards can be used to store and manage the credentials and certificates of IoT devices, and enable secure updates and provisioning.
- Smart cards can be used to enable secure payment and billing for IoT services, such as smart metering, smart parking, smart vending, etc.
