### Vision for the notes of the Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT) in the subject of Internet of Things

- Define the concept of Internet of Things (IoT) and its characteristics, such as connectivity, heterogeneity, scalability, interoperability, and intelligence.
- Explain the benefits and challenges of IoT, such as improved efficiency, productivity, convenience, security, privacy, and complexity.
- Describe the architecture and components of IoT, such as sensors, actuators, gateways, cloud, fog, edge, and applications.
- Identify the key technologies and protocols that enable IoT, such as wireless communication, networking, data management, security, and analytics.
- Provide examples and use cases of IoT in various domains, such as smart home, smart city, smart health, smart agriculture, smart industry, and smart environment.
- Discuss the current trends and future directions of IoT, such as artificial intelligence, machine learning, blockchain, 5G, and quantum computing.
