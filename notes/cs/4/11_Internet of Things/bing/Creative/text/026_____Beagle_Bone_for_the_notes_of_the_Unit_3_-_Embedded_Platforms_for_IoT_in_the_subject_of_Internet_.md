### Beagle Bone

- Beagle Bone is a series of low-cost, open-source, single-board Linux computers created by Texas Instruments .
- Beagle Bone can run various operating systems, such as Android, Ubuntu, Debian, and Angstrom .
- Beagle Bone has a 1GHz ARM Cortex-A8 processor, 512MB of DDR3 RAM, 4GB of eMMC flash storage, and a 3D graphics accelerator .
- Beagle Bone has many input/output (I/O) interfaces, such as USB, Ethernet, HDMI, microSD, serial, and GPIO .
- Beagle Bone can be extended with cape plug-in boards that add additional functionality, such as sensors, displays, cameras, and wireless connectivity .
- Beagle Bone is suitable for building embedded projects that require real-time analysis, such as 3D printers, arcade games, robots, and smart home devices .
- Beagle Bone has several variants, such as Beagle Bone Black, Beagle Bone Green, Beagle Bone Blue, Beagle Bone AI, and PocketBeagle .
- Beagle Bone Black is the most popular and widely used variant, which costs around $60 and has a larger community and support .
- Beagle Bone Green is a modified version of Beagle Bone Black, which replaces the HDMI port with two Grove connectors for easy sensor integration.
- Beagle Bone Blue is a robotics-focused variant, which has a built-in IMU, motor drivers, and battery management.
- Beagle Bone AI is a high-performance variant, which has a dual-core ARM Cortex-A15 processor, 1GB of RAM, 16GB of eMMC flash storage, and a neural processing unit for machine learning applications.
- PocketBeagle is a miniaturized variant, which has a 1GHz ARM Cortex-A8 processor, 512MB of RAM, and a microSD slot, but no eMMC flash storage or Ethernet port.
