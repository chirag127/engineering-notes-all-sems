### E-health

- E-health is a term that refers to the use of electronic processes and communication, such as computers, the Internet, and mobile devices, to facilitate health improvement and health care services  .
- E-health can include various applications, such as telemedicine, telehealth, m-health, e-learning, e-prescription, e-referral, e-records, e-surveillance, e-public health, and e-research .
- E-health can provide benefits such as improving access, quality, efficiency, and equity of health care, enhancing patient empowerment and engagement, reducing costs and errors, and supporting health promotion and disease prevention  .
- E-health can also pose challenges such as ethical, legal, and social issues, data security and privacy, digital divide and health literacy, technical interoperability and standards, and evaluation and evidence of effectiveness  .
- E-health is an emerging and evolving field that requires collaboration and coordination among various stakeholders, such as health care providers, patients, policy makers, researchers, and industry  .
