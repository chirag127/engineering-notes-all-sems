### Embedded computing basics for the notes of the Unit 3 - Embedded Platforms for IoT in the subject of Internet of Things

- Embedded systems are electronic devices that contain a microcontroller or a microprocessor that can perform a specific function or task .
- Embedded systems are often used to create IoT devices, which are devices that can connect to the internet and communicate with other devices or services .
- Embedded systems have some common characteristics, such as  :
  - Power source: Embedded systems need a source of power to operate, such as batteries, utility power, or power from the host system.
  - Time base: Embedded systems need a way to measure time and synchronize events, such as a clock or a timer.
  - Digital processing: Embedded systems need a processor or a microcontroller to execute instructions and process data, such as an ARM Cortex-M4 processor.
  - Memory: Embedded systems need memory to store data and instructions, such as ROM, RAM, flash, or EEPROM.
  - Software and firmware: Embedded systems need software or firmware to control the functionality and behavior of the device, such as C, Python, or Arduino.
  - Specialized circuitry: Embedded systems may need additional hardware components to perform specific tasks, such as sensors, actuators, LEDs, or buttons.
  - Input from the environment: Embedded systems may need to receive data or signals from the external world, such as temperature, light, sound, or motion.
  - Output to the environment: Embedded systems may need to send data or signals to the external world, such as display, sound, motion, or communication.
- Embedded systems have some common challenges, such as  :
  - Resource constraints: Embedded systems may have limited power, memory, processing speed, or bandwidth, which require efficient and optimized design and programming.
  - Real-time requirements: Embedded systems may have to perform their functions within a specific time frame or deadline, which require accurate and reliable timing and synchronization.
  - Security and privacy: Embedded systems may have to protect the data and communication from unauthorized access or tampering, which require encryption, authentication, or verification.
  - Testing and debugging: Embedded systems may be difficult to test and debug, especially when they are deployed in remote or inaccessible locations, which require tools and techniques such as simulators, emulators, or debuggers.
