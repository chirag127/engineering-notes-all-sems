## Unit 7 - IoT Applications

- IoT applications are the use cases of IoT technology that provide value to various domains and industries by enabling connectivity, data exchange, and automation of physical objects and systems.
- IoT applications can be classified into different categories based on the type of devices, networks, and users involved, such as consumer IoT, industrial IoT, smart cities, smart agriculture, smart healthcare, smart transportation, etc.
- Some examples of IoT applications are:

  - Remote monitoring: IoT devices can monitor the location, performance, condition, or environmental factors of various assets, such as machinery, vehicles, livestock, etc., and send the data to a cloud platform for analysis and action. This can help reduce service costs, improve operational efficiency, enhance customer experience, and increase security and safety.  
  - Predictive maintenance: IoT devices can use machine learning to analyze the data collected from sensors and predict the outcomes and failures of the assets, such as machines, equipment, pipelines, etc. This can help service providers to intervene before the problems become critical and avoid downtime, waste, and customer dissatisfaction. 
  - Smart lighting: IoT devices can control the lighting systems of buildings, streets, or homes based on the occupancy, time, weather, or user preferences. This can help reduce energy consumption, lower carbon emissions, and improve the ambiance and comfort of the users.  
  - Smart agriculture: IoT devices can monitor the soil moisture, temperature, humidity, rainfall, pest infestation, crop growth, etc., and provide real-time feedback and recommendations to the farmers. This can help optimize the irrigation, fertilization, pest control, and harvesting processes, and increase the crop yield and quality.  
  - Smart healthcare: IoT devices can measure the vital signs, blood pressure, glucose level, heart rate, etc., of the patients and send the data to the doctors or caregivers for remote diagnosis and treatment. This can help improve the health outcomes, reduce the healthcare costs, and enhance the patient satisfaction and engagement.
