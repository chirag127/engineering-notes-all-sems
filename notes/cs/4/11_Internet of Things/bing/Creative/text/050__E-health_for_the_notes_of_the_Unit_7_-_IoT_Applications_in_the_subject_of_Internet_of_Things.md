### E-health for the notes of the Unit 7 - IoT Applications in the subject of Internet of Things

- E-health is the application of digital technology to healthcare practice, such as using the Internet, IoT devices, mobile apps, cloud computing, big data analytics, etc. to provide health-related services and information .
- E-health has many benefits, such as improving access, quality, efficiency, and affordability of healthcare, enhancing patient engagement and satisfaction, supporting preventive and personalized care, and reducing errors and costs .
- E-health also faces many challenges, such as ensuring interoperability, security, privacy, ethics, and reliability of the systems and data, overcoming technical and regulatory barriers, and addressing the digital divide and health literacy issues .
- IoT is a key enabler of e-health, as it allows the interconnection of smart devices and sensors that can collect, transmit, and process health data in real time, and provide feedback and interventions to the users and healthcare providers .
- IoT applications in e-health can be classified into three categories: wearable, implantable, and ambient.
  - Wearable devices are those that can be worn on the body, such as smart bands, watches, glasses, clothing, etc. They can monitor various physiological parameters, such as heart rate, blood pressure, temperature, activity, etc., and provide alerts, reminders, and recommendations to the users .
  - Implantable devices are those that can be inserted into the body, such as pacemakers, insulin pumps, cochlear implants, etc. They can perform specific functions, such as stimulating, regulating, or delivering drugs to the organs or tissues, and communicate with external devices or networks .
  - Ambient devices are those that can be embedded in the environment, such as smart home appliances, cameras, speakers, etc. They can sense the presence, location, and behavior of the users, and provide assistance, guidance, and entertainment to them .
- Some examples of IoT applications in e-health are:
  - Remote patient monitoring, which allows the patients to stay at home and receive care from the healthcare providers via the Internet and IoT devices, such as blood glucose meters, blood pressure cuffs, pulse oximeters, etc. This can reduce hospital visits, costs, and risks of infections, and improve patient comfort and adherence .
  - Telemedicine, which allows the patients to consult with the healthcare providers via the Internet and IoT devices, such as video conferencing, chatbots, etc. This can increase access, convenience, and timeliness of healthcare, and reduce travel and waiting time .
  - Smart medication management, which allows the patients to receive reminders, instructions, and feedback on their medication intake via the Internet and IoT devices, such as smart pill bottles, dispensers, patches, etc. This can improve medication adherence, safety, and effectiveness, and reduce errors and adverse events .
  - Smart health coaching, which allows the patients to receive personalized and interactive guidance and support on their health and wellness goals via the Internet and IoT devices, such as mobile apps, wearable sensors, etc. This can improve health behaviors, outcomes, and satisfaction, and reduce chronic diseases and complications .
  - Smart emergency response, which allows the patients to receive immediate and appropriate assistance in case of emergencies, such as falls, accidents, strokes, etc., via the Internet and IoT devices, such as wearable sensors, panic buttons, etc. This can save lives, reduce damages, and improve recovery .
