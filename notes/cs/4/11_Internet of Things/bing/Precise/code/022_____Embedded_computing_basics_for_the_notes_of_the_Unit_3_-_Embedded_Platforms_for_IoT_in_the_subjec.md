### Embedded Computing Basics

Embedded computing refers to the use of computer systems within other devices or products. These systems are designed to perform specific tasks and are often integrated into larger systems to provide specific functionality. Here are some key points to consider when studying embedded computing basics for the Unit 3 - Embedded Platforms for IoT in the subject of Internet of Things:

1. **Embedded systems** are computer systems that are integrated into other devices or products. They are designed to perform specific tasks and are often used to control, monitor, or process data within the larger system.

2. **Microcontrollers** are small, low-power computers that are often used in embedded systems. They typically have a limited amount of memory and processing power, but are designed to be highly efficient and reliable.

3. **Real-time operating systems (RTOS)** are operating systems designed for use in embedded systems. They are designed to provide predictable and reliable performance, with features such as real-time scheduling and interrupt handling.

4. **Embedded software** is software that is designed to run on embedded systems. It is typically written in low-level languages such as C or assembly, and is optimized for performance and efficiency.

5. **Embedded hardware** refers to the physical components of an embedded system, including microcontrollers, sensors, actuators, and other electronic components.

6. **Embedded platforms** are pre-built hardware and software systems that provide a foundation for developing embedded systems. They often include a microcontroller, memory, input/output interfaces, and other components, as well as a software development environment.

7. **Internet of Things (IoT)** refers to the network of physical devices, vehicles, home appliances, and other items embedded with electronics, software, sensors, actuators, and connectivity which enables these objects to connect and exchange data.

These are some of the key concepts to consider when studying embedded computing basics for the Unit 3 - Embedded Platforms for IoT in the subject of Internet of Things. It is important to have a solid understanding of these concepts in order to effectively design and develop embedded systems for IoT applications.
