### Overview of IoT Supported Hardware Platforms such as Arduino

#### Unit 3 - Embedded Platforms for IoT in the Subject of Internet of Things

1. **Introduction:** IoT (Internet of Things) refers to the interconnection of physical devices, vehicles, buildings, and other items embedded with electronics, software, sensors, and network connectivity that enable these objects to collect and exchange data.

2. **IoT Hardware Platforms:** There are several hardware platforms available that support the development of IoT applications. These platforms provide the necessary hardware and software components to build and deploy IoT solutions.

3. **Arduino:** One of the most popular IoT hardware platforms is Arduino. Arduino is an open-source electronics platform based on easy-to-use hardware and software. It is designed to make electronics more accessible to artists, designers, hobbyists, and anyone interested in creating interactive objects or environments.

4. **Arduino Boards:** Arduino boards are microcontroller-based development boards that come in a variety of shapes and sizes. Some of the most popular Arduino boards include the Arduino Uno, Arduino Mega, and Arduino Nano.

5. **Arduino Shields:** Arduino shields are modular circuit boards that can be stacked on top of an Arduino board to extend its capabilities. There are many different types of shields available, including shields for wireless communication, motor control, and sensors.

6. **Arduino Software:** The Arduino software, also known as the Arduino IDE (Integrated Development Environment), is a program that runs on your computer and allows you to write, compile, and upload code to your Arduino board. The Arduino IDE is available for Windows, Mac OS X, and Linux.

7. **Conclusion:** Arduino is a versatile and easy-to-use platform for developing IoT applications. With its wide range of hardware and software options, it is an excellent choice for anyone looking to get started with IoT development.
