### IoT/M2M systems layers and design standardization

- The oneM2M architecture divides IoT functions into three major domains: the application layer, the services layer, and the network layer .
- The application layer is given major attention in the oneM2M architecture, with a focus on connectivity between devices and their applications .
- The structures of communication protocols for digital M2M and IoT systems are conceptually broken into abstract layers, with the most common models having three, four, five, or seven layers .
- The main ETSI IoT standardization activities are conducted at the radio layer in 3GPP (LTE-M, NB-IoT, and EC-GSM-IoT) and at the service layer in oneM2M .
- A wide range of technologies work together to connect things in the Internet of Things (IoT), and ETSI is involved in standardizing many of these technologies, including Smart Machine-to-Machine (M2M) communications .
- The Open Geospatial Consortium (OGC), an International Industry Consortium, has also suggested open standards for sensors’ discovery, capabilities, quality, and other aspects with support to geographical information web support .
