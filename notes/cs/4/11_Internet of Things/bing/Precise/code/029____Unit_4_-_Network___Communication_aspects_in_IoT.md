## Unit 4 - Network & Communication aspects in IoT

1. **Introduction to IoT Networking:** IoT devices are connected to the internet and communicate with each other using various networking technologies. These technologies can be wired or wireless and are chosen based on the requirements of the IoT system.

2. **Wireless Networking Technologies:** Some of the most commonly used wireless networking technologies in IoT include Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Zigbee, and cellular networks such as 4G and 5G. These technologies have different ranges, data rates, and power requirements, making them suitable for different IoT applications.

3. **Wired Networking Technologies:** Wired networking technologies such as Ethernet are also used in IoT systems. These technologies provide high data rates and reliable connections, making them suitable for applications that require large amounts of data transfer.

4. **Network Topologies:** IoT systems can use different network topologies such as star, mesh, and tree topologies. The choice of topology depends on the requirements of the IoT system, such as the number of devices, the range of communication, and the need for redundancy.

5. **Communication Protocols:** IoT devices use various communication protocols to exchange data. Some of the most commonly used protocols in IoT include MQTT, CoAP, and HTTP. These protocols have different features and are chosen based on the requirements of the IoT system.

6. **Security in IoT Networking:** Security is a major concern in IoT networking. IoT devices can be vulnerable to attacks, and it is important to use secure communication protocols and encryption to protect the data transmitted between devices.

7. **Cloud Computing and IoT:** Cloud computing plays a major role in IoT systems. IoT devices can send data to the cloud for storage and processing, and the cloud can be used to control and manage IoT devices remotely.

8. **Edge Computing and IoT:** Edge computing is a new trend in IoT where data processing is performed at the edge of the network, close to the IoT devices. This can reduce the amount of data that needs to be transmitted to the cloud and can improve the performance of the IoT system.

9. **Conclusion:** Networking and communication are essential aspects of IoT systems. The choice of networking technology, topology, and communication protocol depends on the requirements of the IoT system. Security and the use of cloud and edge computing are also important considerations in IoT networking.
