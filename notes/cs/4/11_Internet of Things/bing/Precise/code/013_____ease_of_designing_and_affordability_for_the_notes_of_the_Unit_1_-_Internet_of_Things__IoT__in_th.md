### Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT): Ease of Designing and Affordability

1. The Internet of Things (IoT) refers to the interconnection of physical devices, vehicles, buildings, and other items embedded with electronics, software, sensors, and network connectivity, which enables these objects to collect and exchange data.
2. The ease of designing IoT devices has increased significantly in recent years due to the availability of low-cost hardware and software development tools.
3. Many IoT devices can be built using off-the-shelf components, such as microcontrollers, sensors, and wireless modules, which can be easily integrated into custom designs.
4. The cost of these components has also decreased significantly, making it more affordable for individuals and small businesses to develop their own IoT devices.
5. In addition, there are many open-source software libraries and development platforms available that can be used to develop IoT applications, reducing the need for specialized knowledge and expertise.
6. The affordability of IoT devices has also been improved by the availability of low-cost cloud services for data storage and processing, which can be used to support IoT applications.
7. Overall, the ease of designing and affordability of IoT devices has made it possible for a wider range of individuals and organizations to develop and deploy IoT solutions, leading to increased innovation and growth in the IoT industry.
