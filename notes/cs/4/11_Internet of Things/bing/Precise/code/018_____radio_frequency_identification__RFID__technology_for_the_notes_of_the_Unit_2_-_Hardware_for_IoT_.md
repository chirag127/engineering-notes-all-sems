### Radio Frequency Identification (RFID) Technology

Radio Frequency Identification (RFID) technology is a wireless communication technology that uses radio waves to identify and track objects. It is commonly used in the Internet of Things (IoT) for hardware identification and tracking.

Here are some key points to note about RFID technology:

1. RFID technology uses radio waves to transmit data between a reader and a tag attached to an object.
2. The tag contains a microchip that stores data and an antenna that receives and transmits signals.
3. There are two main types of RFID tags: passive and active. Passive tags do not have their own power source and rely on the reader to power them, while active tags have their own battery and can transmit data over longer distances.
4. RFID technology can be used for a wide range of applications, including inventory management, asset tracking, and supply chain management.
5. RFID technology is often used in combination with other technologies, such as sensors and wireless communication, to provide more comprehensive tracking and monitoring solutions.
