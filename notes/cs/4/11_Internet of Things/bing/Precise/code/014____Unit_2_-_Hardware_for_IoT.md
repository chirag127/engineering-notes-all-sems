## Unit 2 - Hardware for IoT

1. **Introduction:** The Internet of Things (IoT) is a network of interconnected devices that can collect and exchange data. The hardware components of an IoT system include sensors, actuators, microcontrollers, and communication modules.

2. **Sensors:** Sensors are used to collect data from the environment. They can measure physical quantities such as temperature, humidity, light, and motion. There are many types of sensors available, including temperature sensors, humidity sensors, light sensors, and motion sensors.

3. **Actuators:** Actuators are used to control physical systems. They can convert electrical signals into mechanical motion. Examples of actuators include motors, solenoids, and relays.

4. **Microcontrollers:** Microcontrollers are small computers that can be programmed to perform specific tasks. They are used to control the sensors and actuators in an IoT system. Examples of microcontrollers include the Arduino and the Raspberry Pi.

5. **Communication Modules:** Communication modules are used to transmit data between the devices in an IoT system. They can use various communication protocols such as Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and cellular networks.

6. **Conclusion:** The hardware components of an IoT system are essential for collecting and exchanging data. They include sensors, actuators, microcontrollers, and communication modules. These components work together to enable the IoT system to interact with the physical world.
