### Conceptual Framework for the notes of the Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT) in the subject of Internet of Things

1. **Introduction to IoT:** The Internet of Things (IoT) is a network of interconnected devices that can communicate with each other and exchange data over the internet.
2. **IoT Architecture:** The IoT architecture consists of four main layers: the device layer, the communication layer, the information layer, and the application layer.
3. **IoT Devices:** IoT devices are physical objects that are equipped with sensors, actuators, and communication capabilities to collect and exchange data.
4. **IoT Communication Protocols:** IoT devices communicate with each other and with the cloud using various communication protocols such as MQTT, CoAP, and HTTP.
5. **IoT Data Management:** IoT data management involves the collection, storage, and analysis of data generated by IoT devices.
6. **IoT Security:** IoT security is concerned with protecting IoT devices and the data they generate from unauthorized access and manipulation.
7. **IoT Applications:** IoT has numerous applications in various domains such as smart homes, smart cities, healthcare, and agriculture.
