## Unit 2 - Hardware for IoT

1. **Sensors**: Sensors are the fundamental building blocks of IoT. They are used to collect data from the environment and convert it into a digital format that can be processed by a computer. Some common types of sensors used in IoT include temperature sensors, humidity sensors, light sensors, and motion sensors.

2. **Microcontrollers**: Microcontrollers are small, low-power computers that are used to control the operation of IoT devices. They are responsible for reading data from sensors, processing it, and sending it to the cloud or other devices.

3. **Communication Modules**: Communication modules are used to transmit data between IoT devices and the cloud. Some common communication technologies used in IoT include Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and cellular networks.

4. **Power Sources**: IoT devices need a power source to operate. This can be in the form of a battery, a solar panel, or a connection to the mains power supply.

5. **Actuators**: Actuators are used to control the physical environment. They can be used to turn on lights, open doors, or control the temperature of a room. Some common types of actuators used in IoT include motors, solenoids, and relays.

6. **Gateways**: Gateways are used to connect IoT devices to the cloud. They are responsible for aggregating data from multiple devices, processing it, and sending it to the cloud for further analysis.

7. **Cloud Infrastructure**: The cloud infrastructure is used to store, process, and analyze data generated by IoT devices. It can also be used to remotely control and manage IoT devices.

These are some of the key hardware components used in IoT systems. They work together to collect data from the environment, process it, and take action based on the insights generated.
