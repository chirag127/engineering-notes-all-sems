### Raspberry Pi for the notes of the Unit 3 - Embedded Platforms for IoT in the subject of Internet of Things

- Raspberry Pi is a series of small single-board computers (SBCs) developed by the non-profit Raspberry Pi Foundation as a platform to promote computer-science education in schools and developing countries.
- Raspberry Pi 3 is the third generation of Raspberry Pi and it packs quite a formidable punch in its credit card-sized package.
- In addition to the standard features of the Raspberry Pi (such as four USB 2.0 ports and built-in Ethernet), it has: A 1.2GHz 64-bit quad-core ARMv8 CPU, 802.11n Wireless LAN, Bluetooth 4.1 Low Energy (BLE).
- The Raspberry Pi has proven an excellent alternative to programmable logic controllers, a standard in many industries including automation and manufacturing. It is relatively inexpensive and efficient at carrying out programmed tasks through its GPIO interface.
