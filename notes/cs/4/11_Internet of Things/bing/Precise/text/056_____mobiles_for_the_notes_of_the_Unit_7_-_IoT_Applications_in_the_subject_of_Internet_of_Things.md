### Unit 7 - IoT Applications: Mobiles

1. Mobile devices, such as smartphones and tablets, are an integral part of the Internet of Things (IoT) ecosystem.
2. Mobile devices can act as both sensors and controllers in IoT systems.
3. Mobile devices can collect data from their built-in sensors, such as GPS, accelerometer, and camera, and send this data to IoT platforms for processing and analysis.
4. Mobile devices can also receive data and commands from IoT platforms and act on this information, such as displaying notifications or controlling smart home devices.
5. Mobile applications can be developed to interface with IoT devices and systems, providing a user-friendly way to interact with and control IoT devices.
6. Mobile devices can also act as gateways, connecting IoT devices to the internet and allowing them to communicate with each other and with cloud-based services.
7. The widespread adoption of mobile devices and their increasing capabilities make them a key component in the growth and development of the IoT.
