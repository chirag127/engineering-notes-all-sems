### Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT): Technology behind IoT

- IoT works through a combination of wireless networking technology, physical devices, advanced data analytics and cloud computing.
- A group of physical devices is wired or wirelessly linked to each other and/or a central area.
- IoT devices connect to the internet via GSM cellular networks, Wi-Fi, or ethernet.
- The Internet of Things is the concept of connecting any device (so long as it has an on/off switch) to the Internet and to other connected devices.
- The IoT is a giant network of connected things and people – all of which collect and share data about the way they are used and about the environment around them.
- Six IoT technologies are now classified as fairly mature or mainstream: CPUs, MCUs, GPUs, security chips, FPGA, and edge gateways.
- Four IoT technologies are close to maturity: eSIM, mesh networks, 5G, and Wi-Fi 6.
- Edge computing refers to the technology used to make smart devices do more than just send or receive data to their IoT platform. It increases the computing power at the edges of an IoT network, reducing communication latency and improving response time.
