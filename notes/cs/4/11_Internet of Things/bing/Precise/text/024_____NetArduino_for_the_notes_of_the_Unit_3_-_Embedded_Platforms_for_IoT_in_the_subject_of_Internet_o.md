### Netduino for IoT

- Netduino is an open-source electronics platform that uses the .NET Micro Framework.
- It is based on a 32-bit ARM microcontroller and is compatible with most Arduino shields.
- Netduino has 5 different models: Netduino, Netduino Plus, Netduino Plus 2, Netduino Mini, and Netduino Go.
- The first platform chosen to build a Starter Kit is Netduino Plus 2, which runs at 168 MHz with 1 MB Flash and 192 KB RAM.
- Wilderness Labs acquired Netduino in 2017 and used it to help prototype their next-generation .NET efforts on embedded systems.
- Netduino.Foundation was created to make it plug-and-play with sensors.
- Netduino is a powerful platform for IoT development, with software library modules developed specifically for IoT, such as IR codecs or sensor/motor drivers.
