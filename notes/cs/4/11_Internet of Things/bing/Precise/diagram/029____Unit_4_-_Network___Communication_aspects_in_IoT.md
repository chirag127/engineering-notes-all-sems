## Unit 4 - Network & Communication aspects in IoT

1. **Introduction to IoT Networking:** IoT devices are connected to the internet and communicate with other devices and systems through various networking technologies.

2. **Wireless Communication Technologies:** There are several wireless communication technologies used in IoT, including Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Zigbee, Z-Wave, and cellular (e.g. 3G, 4G, 5G).

3. **Wired Communication Technologies:** Wired communication technologies such as Ethernet and Powerline Communication (PLC) are also used in IoT.

4. **Network Protocols:** IoT devices use various network protocols to communicate with each other and with the cloud, including HTTP, MQTT, CoAP, and AMQP.

5. **Network Security:** Network security is a critical aspect of IoT, as devices are often connected to the internet and can be vulnerable to cyber attacks. Security measures such as encryption, authentication, and firewalls are used to protect IoT devices and networks.

6. **Cloud Computing and IoT:** Cloud computing plays a significant role in IoT, as it provides the infrastructure for storing and processing large amounts of data generated by IoT devices.

7. **Edge Computing and IoT:** Edge computing is a technology that allows data processing to be performed closer to the source of the data, reducing the need for data to be transmitted to the cloud. This can improve the performance and efficiency of IoT systems.

8. **IoT Communication Standards:** There are several standards and organizations that govern the communication and interoperability of IoT devices, including the Open Connectivity Foundation (OCF), the Industrial Internet Consortium (IIC), and the Zigbee Alliance.
