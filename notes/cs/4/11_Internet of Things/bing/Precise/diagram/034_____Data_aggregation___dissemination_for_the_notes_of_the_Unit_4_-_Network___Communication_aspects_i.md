### Data aggregation & dissemination for the notes of the Unit 4 - Network & Communication aspects in IoT in the subject of Internet of Things

- Data aggregation is the process of collecting and combining data from multiple sources to provide a more comprehensive view of the data.
- In the context of IoT, data aggregation is used to collect data from multiple sensors and devices to provide a more complete picture of the environment or system being monitored.
- Data dissemination is the process of distributing or transmitting data to multiple recipients or locations.
- In the context of IoT, data dissemination is used to transmit data from sensors and devices to a central location for processing and analysis, or to other devices for further action.
- Data aggregation and dissemination are important aspects of the network and communication aspects of IoT, as they enable the efficient collection and transmission of data from multiple sources.
- Effective data aggregation and dissemination can improve the performance and reliability of IoT systems, and enable more advanced analysis and decision-making.
