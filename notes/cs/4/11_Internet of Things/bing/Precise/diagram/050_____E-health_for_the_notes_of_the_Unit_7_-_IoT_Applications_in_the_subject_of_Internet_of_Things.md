# E-health

E-health refers to the use of electronic means and technologies to deliver health-related services and information. The Internet of Things (IoT) has been applied for various e-Health applications to improve diagnostic tools, such as magnetic resonance imaging, and epigenetic and neuropsychological tests .

Some of the applications of IoT in e-health include:
- **Wearable technology**: Smart bands, fitness trackers, and smartwatches are common IoT applications in the market. These devices can be useful for people to monitor their health .
- **Remote physiological monitoring**: IoT devices in the form of wearables like fitness bands and other wirelessly connected devices like blood pressure and heart rate monitoring cuffs, glucometer, etc. give patients access to personalized attention .
- **Improving physician care**: IoT-Health has the potential to create a pervasive environment for monitoring patient health and safety as well as improving how physicians deliver care .

IoT technologies used in e-health include intelligent sensing technologies, cloud and edge computing, and big data analytics . The integration of IoT, fog, and cloud infrastructure is also a focus of research in this area .
