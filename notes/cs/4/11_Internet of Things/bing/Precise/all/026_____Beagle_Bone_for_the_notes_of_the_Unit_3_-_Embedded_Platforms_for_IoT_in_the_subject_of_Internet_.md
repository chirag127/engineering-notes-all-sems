# BeagleBone for IoT

- The BeagleBone Black board is the newest member of the BeagleBoard family .
- It can boot Linux in under 10 seconds and get started on development in less than 5 minutes with just a single USB cable .
- This board is very useful in developing IoT applications .
- It is widely used in automation, IoT, and robotics at the development level .
- The BeagleBone Black is just like the simple BeagleBone but it is faster and has some extra features which make it preferable .
- Most engineers and developers like to use it as a server and hub for small networks .
- The Beagle family revs as high as 1GHz with flexible peripheral interfaces and a proven ecosystem of feature-rich "Cape" plug-in boards .
- The Bosch IoT Suite provides developers with the gateway software development tools and board support packages to build and run IoT applications on top of BeagleBone and connect devices via the gateway .
