# Wireless Medium Access Issues

Wireless communication is the transfer of information or power between two or more points that are not connected by an electrical conductor. When it comes to communication using a wireless medium there is always a concern about the interference due to other present wireless communication technologies.

Some of the important issues which are observed are:

1. **Half Duplex operation**: Half-duplex transmission means when the sender and receiver both are capable of sharing data but one at a time.
2. **Time-varying channel**: The channel through which the communication is done varies with time.
3. **Burst channel errors**: These are errors that occur in bursts, i.e., a group of errors occurring together.

The **Medium Access Control (MAC)** protocol is critical in the IoT since it coordinates data transport among various IoT devices. However, to achieve high network throughput, low energy consumption, and low latency, various issues at the MAC layer must be overcome.
