### Data aggregation & dissemination for the notes of the Unit 4 - Network & Communication aspects in IoT in the subject of Internet of Things

- Data dissemination and aggregation are two fundamental services in Internet of Things (IoT) networks.
- In data dissemination, a bulk of data is transmitted from a base station (e.g, an AP in the WiFi network or a sink node in the ZigBee network) to multiple nodes in the IoT network.
- Research in IoT ensures that collecting, processing and distributing the data needs to be improved to carryout data aggregation, processing and dissemination tasks of IoT data management.
- Data Processing focuses on the characteristics Velocity, Volume, Variety, Variability, and Veracity.
- IoT has many issues in terms of data management, processing, data governance, security, storage, and metadata management.
- Various strategies and techniques are used to provide better solutions, which provide data to the end-user with usability, reliability, security, and scalability.
