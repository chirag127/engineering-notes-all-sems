# Embedded Computing Basics

Embedded computing refers to the use of computer systems and software to perform dedicated functions within larger systems. These systems are often integrated into other devices or products, and are designed to operate with minimal human intervention.

Here are some key points to consider when studying embedded computing basics for Unit 3 - Embedded Platforms for IoT in the subject of Internet of Things:

1. **Embedded systems** are computer systems that are integrated into other devices or products, and are designed to perform specific tasks. These systems often have limited processing power, memory, and storage, and are optimized for their specific tasks.

2. **Microcontrollers** are small, low-cost computers that are used in embedded systems. They typically have limited processing power, memory, and storage, and are designed to interface with sensors, actuators, and other components.

3. **Real-time operating systems (RTOS)** are operating systems designed for use in embedded systems. They are optimized for fast, predictable response times, and are often used in applications where timing is critical.

4. **Programming languages** commonly used in embedded systems include C, C++, and assembly language. These languages are chosen for their efficiency and low-level control over the hardware.

5. **Hardware and software design** are important considerations when developing embedded systems. The hardware must be designed to meet the requirements of the application, while the software must be optimized for the specific hardware and the tasks it will perform.

6. **Power consumption** is a critical consideration in many embedded systems, particularly those that are battery-powered. Techniques such as power management and low-power design can be used to minimize power consumption and extend battery life.

7. **Connectivity** is an important consideration in many embedded systems, particularly those used in IoT applications. Embedded systems may use a variety of wired and wireless communication technologies to connect to other devices and systems.

These are some of the key points to consider when studying embedded computing basics for Unit 3 - Embedded Platforms for IoT in the subject of Internet of Things. It is important to have a solid understanding of these concepts in order to effectively design and develop embedded systems for IoT applications.
