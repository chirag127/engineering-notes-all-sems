### Design Principles for Connected Devices

1. **Interoperability**: Connected devices should be able to communicate and work with other devices, regardless of the manufacturer or operating system.
2. **Security**: Connected devices should have strong security measures in place to protect user data and prevent unauthorized access.
3. **Reliability**: Connected devices should be reliable and function consistently, with minimal downtime or errors.
4. **User control**: Users should have control over their connected devices, including the ability to customize settings and manage their data.
5. **Privacy**: Connected devices should respect user privacy and provide transparency about data collection and usage.
6. **Ease of use**: Connected devices should be easy to set up and use, with intuitive interfaces and clear instructions.
7. **Scalability**: Connected devices should be able to scale to accommodate growth and changing needs.
8. **Sustainability**: Connected devices should be designed with sustainability in mind, including energy efficiency and responsible disposal.
