# Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT)

### Ease of Designing and Affordability

1. The Internet of Things (IoT) is a network of interconnected devices that can communicate with each other and share data.
2. The design of IoT devices has become easier due to the availability of low-cost hardware and software platforms.
3. The cost of IoT devices has decreased, making them more affordable for consumers and businesses.
4. The use of open-source software and hardware platforms has also contributed to the ease of designing and affordability of IoT devices.
5. The availability of cloud-based services has made it easier to store and process data from IoT devices, reducing the need for expensive on-premises infrastructure.
6. The development of wireless communication technologies, such as Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and cellular networks, has made it easier to connect IoT devices to the internet.
7. The use of standardized protocols and interfaces has made it easier to integrate IoT devices with other systems and applications.
8. The growth of the IoT ecosystem, including the availability of development tools, libraries, and support communities, has made it easier for developers to design and build IoT devices and applications.
