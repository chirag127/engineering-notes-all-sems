### Participatory Sensing Technology

1. Participatory Sensing is an approach to data collection and interpretation in which individuals, acting alone or in groups, use their personal mobile devices and web services to systematically explore interesting aspects of their worlds ranging from health to culture .
2. Participatory sensing will task deployed mobile devices to form interactive, participatory sensor networks that enable public and professional users to gather, analyze and share local knowledge .
3. Microphones and imagers on-board the mobile handsets can record environmental data now, while in the future other sensors will be integrated or connect wirelessly .
4. Cell tower localization, GPS and other technologies can provide location and time-synchronization data .
5. Wireless radios and onboard processing enable human interaction with both local data processing and remote servers .
6. With these capabilities in mind, new network architecture is described that enhances data credibility, quality, privacy and ‘shareability’, encouraging participation at personal, social and urban scales .
7. In participatory sensing, individuals are explicitly and actively involved in the sensing process, and choose to perform some critical operations (e.g., operating the sensors, performing certain tasks, etc.) to meet application requirements .
