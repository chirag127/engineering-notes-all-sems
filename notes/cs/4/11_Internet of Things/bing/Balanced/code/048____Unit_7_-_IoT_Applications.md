## Unit 7 - IoT Applications

- IoT applications are the use cases of the Internet of Things, which is the network of physical objects embedded with sensors, software, and other technologies that enable data collection and communication.
- IoT applications can bring value to various domains, such as industry, commerce, agriculture, healthcare, and smart cities.
- Some examples of IoT applications are:

  - Smart lighting systems that can adjust the brightness and color of the lights based on the time of day, occupancy, and ambient conditions, reducing energy consumption and improving comfort .
  - Smart parking systems that can monitor the availability of parking spaces and guide drivers to the nearest vacant spot, increasing efficiency and revenue .
  - Smart agriculture systems that can monitor the soil moisture, temperature, humidity, and crop health, and control the irrigation, fertilization, and pest management, optimizing the yield and quality of the crops .
  - Smart health systems that can measure the vital signs, activity levels, and medication adherence of patients, and alert the caregivers in case of emergencies, improving the quality and accessibility of care .
  - Smart city systems that can measure the air quality, traffic, noise, and waste management, and provide real-time information and feedback to the citizens and authorities, enhancing the livability and sustainability of the urban environment .
