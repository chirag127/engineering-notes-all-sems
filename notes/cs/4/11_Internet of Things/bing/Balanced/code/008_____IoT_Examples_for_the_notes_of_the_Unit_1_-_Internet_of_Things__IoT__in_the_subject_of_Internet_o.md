### IoT Examples

The Internet of Things (IoT) is the network of physical objects that are embedded with sensors, software, and other technologies to connect and exchange data with other devices and systems over the internet. IoT has many applications in various domains, such as smart homes, smart cities, smart agriculture, smart healthcare, smart manufacturing, smart retail, and smart transportation. Here are some examples of IoT devices and systems in each domain:

- Smart homes: Smart homes are residences that use IoT devices to automate and control various aspects of the home, such as lighting, temperature, security, entertainment, and appliances. Some examples of smart home devices are smart thermostats, smart speakers, smart locks, smart cameras, smart doorbells, smart plugs, smart lights, and smart TVs.
- Smart cities: Smart cities are urban areas that use IoT devices and systems to improve the efficiency and quality of life of the citizens, such as traffic management, waste management, energy management, environmental monitoring, public safety, and public services. Some examples of smart city devices and systems are smart street lights, smart parking meters, smart trash cans, smart water meters, smart air quality sensors, smart CCTV cameras, and smart emergency response systems.
- Smart agriculture: Smart agriculture is the application of IoT devices and systems to enhance the productivity and sustainability of farming, such as crop monitoring, soil monitoring, irrigation management, pest control, livestock management, and precision agriculture. Some examples of smart agriculture devices and systems are smart drones, smart sprinklers, smart soil sensors, smart weather stations, smart pest traps, smart collars, and smart tractors.
- Smart healthcare: Smart healthcare is the use of IoT devices and systems to improve the quality and accessibility of healthcare services, such as remote patient monitoring, telemedicine, chronic disease management, medication adherence, fitness tracking, and elderly care. Some examples of smart healthcare devices and systems are smart watches, smart bands, smart scales, smart pill bottles, smart inhalers, smart glucose meters, and smart blood pressure monitors.
- Smart manufacturing: Smart manufacturing is the integration of IoT devices and systems into the manufacturing process, such as production monitoring, quality control, inventory management, predictive maintenance, energy management, and supply chain management. Some examples of smart manufacturing devices and systems are smart sensors, smart cameras, smart robots, smart machines, smart RFID tags, smart meters, and smart dashboards.
- Smart retail: Smart retail is the adoption of IoT devices and systems to enhance the customer experience and the business performance of the retail sector, such as personalized recommendations, inventory management, payment processing, customer loyalty, and store analytics. Some examples of smart retail devices and systems are smart shelves, smart mirrors, smart carts, smart kiosks, smart beacons, smart cards, and smart cameras.
- Smart transportation: Smart transportation is the application of IoT devices and systems to improve the safety, efficiency, and convenience of transportation, such as traffic management, parking management, vehicle tracking, navigation, collision avoidance, and autonomous driving. Some examples of smart transportation devices and systems are smart traffic lights, smart parking sensors, smart GPS devices, smart dashcams, smart cars, smart buses, and smart bikes.
