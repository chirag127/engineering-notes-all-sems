### E-health

E-health is a term that refers to the use of electronic processes and communication technologies to support health and health-related fields. E-health can include various applications, such as:

- Telehealth: the delivery of health care services and information remotely, using devices such as computers, tablets, smartphones, etc. Telehealth can enable access to health care for people who live in rural or remote areas, or who have mobility or transportation issues. Telehealth can also reduce costs and improve efficiency and quality of care. Examples of telehealth services are telemedicine, teleconsultation, telemonitoring, tele-education, etc.
- Electronic health records (EHRs): the digital storage and exchange of health information among health care providers, patients, and other authorized parties. EHRs can facilitate the coordination and continuity of care, improve patient safety and quality of care, and support clinical decision making and research. EHRs can also enable patients to access and manage their own health information and communicate with their health care providers online.
- Mobile health (m-health): the use of mobile devices and wireless technologies to support health and wellness. M-health can include applications such as health apps, wearable devices, sensors, text messages, etc. M-health can enable self-management of chronic conditions, health promotion and prevention, disease surveillance and outbreak response, and emergency preparedness and response.
- Health information systems (HIS): the systems and processes that collect, store, analyze, and disseminate health-related data and information. HIS can support health planning, management, evaluation, and research. HIS can also enable the generation and use of health indicators, statistics, and evidence for policy making and practice improvement.
- E-learning: the use of electronic media and information and communication technologies to support education and training in health and health-related fields. E-learning can include online courses, webinars, podcasts, simulations, games, etc. E-learning can enhance the accessibility, flexibility, and quality of health education and training for health professionals and the general public.
