## Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT)

- The Internet of Things (IoT) is the **interconnection** of computing devices embedded in everyday objects, enabling them to **send and receive data** over the Internet or other communications networks .
- The IoT allows information to be **exchanged** between objects and devices, as well as **monitored**, **manipulated** and **analyzed** by people, processes and technology.
- The IoT can be used for various purposes, such as **smart homes**, **smart cities**, **smart agriculture**, **smart health**, **smart industry**, **smart transportation**, **smart energy**, **smart environment** and more.
- The IoT can provide **benefits** such as improved efficiency, convenience, safety, security, quality, productivity, innovation and sustainability.
- The IoT can also pose **challenges** such as privacy, security, interoperability, scalability, reliability, complexity, regulation and ethics.
- The IoT consists of **four main components**: devices, networks, platforms and applications.
  - **Devices** are the physical objects that have sensors, actuators, processors, software and communication capabilities to interact with the IoT.
  - **Networks** are the communication infrastructures that connect the devices to each other and to the Internet, using wired or wireless technologies such as Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Zigbee, LoRaWAN, 5G, etc.
  - **Platforms** are the software systems that provide services and functionalities for the IoT, such as data collection, storage, processing, analysis, visualization, management, security, etc.
  - **Applications** are the software programs that use the IoT data and services to provide solutions and value for specific domains and users.
