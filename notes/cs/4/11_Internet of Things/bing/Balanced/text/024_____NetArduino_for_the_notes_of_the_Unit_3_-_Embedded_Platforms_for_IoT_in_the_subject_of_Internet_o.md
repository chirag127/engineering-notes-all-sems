### Netduino for IoT

- Netduino is an open-source electronics platform that uses the.NET Micro Framework to run C# code on a 32-bit ARM microcontroller  .
- Netduino Plus 2 is one of the models of Netduino that has 1 MB Flash and 192 KB RAM, and supports Ethernet, microSD, and 6 PWM channels .
- Netduino Plus 2 can be programmed using Visual Studio or Visual Studio Code, and can communicate with other devices using serial, I2C, SPI, or TCP/IP protocols .
- Netduino Plus 2 can be used for various IoT applications, such as home automation, sensor networks, robotics, and web services .
- Netduino Plus 2 is compatible with most of the Arduino shields, which are add-on boards that extend the functionality of the base board  .
- Netduino Plus 2 can also use Netduino.Foundation, a library that provides drivers and helpers for many sensors, displays, motors, and other components.
