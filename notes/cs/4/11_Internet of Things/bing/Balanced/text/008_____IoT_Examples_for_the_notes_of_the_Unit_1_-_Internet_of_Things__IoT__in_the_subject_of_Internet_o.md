### IoT Examples

The Internet of Things (IoT) is the network of physical objects that can collect, process, exchange and utilize data interrelatedly, via the internet or other communications networks. IoT has many different applications in various domains, such as transportation, home automation, healthcare, agriculture, retail, manufacturing and smart cities. Here are some examples of IoT devices and systems that illustrate the potential and diversity of IoT:

- **Connected cars**: Cars that can communicate with other vehicles, infrastructure, cloud services and drivers through sensors, cameras, GPS, onboard computers and wireless technologies. Connected cars can offer features such as navigation, entertainment, safety, diagnostics, maintenance and remote control. Some examples of connected car companies are Tesla, Ford, Toyota and BMW.
- **Smart appliances**: Appliances that can be controlled and monitored remotely via smartphones, tablets or voice assistants. Smart appliances can also learn from user preferences, optimize energy consumption, perform self-diagnosis and update firmware. Some examples of smart appliances are refrigerators, ovens, washers, dryers, coffee makers and air conditioners from brands like Samsung, LG, Whirlpool and GE.
- **Connected security systems**: Security systems that can detect and prevent intrusions, fire, smoke, gas leaks and other hazards through sensors, cameras, alarms, locks and sirens. Connected security systems can also send alerts and notifications to users and authorities, stream live video feeds, enable remote access and control, and integrate with other smart devices. Some examples of connected security systems are Ring, Nest, SimpliSafe and ADT.
- **Smart agriculture equipment**: Equipment that can monitor and control various aspects of crop production, such as soil moisture, temperature, humidity, irrigation, fertilization, pest control and harvesting. Smart agriculture equipment can also collect and analyze data to optimize yield, quality and efficiency. Some examples of smart agriculture equipment are drones, sensors, robots, sprinklers and tractors from companies like John Deere, DeLaval, CropX and PrecisionHawk.
- **Connected retail**: Retail that can enhance customer experience, engagement and loyalty through personalized offers, recommendations, coupons, rewards and feedback. Connected retail can also improve inventory management, supply chain optimization, theft prevention and staff productivity. Some examples of connected retail are smart shelves, digital signage, RFID tags, beacons and mobile apps from retailers like Amazon, Walmart, Target and Starbucks.
- **Connected healthcare monitors**: Monitors that can measure and track various health parameters, such as heart rate, blood pressure, glucose level, oxygen saturation, sleep quality and activity level. Connected healthcare monitors can also transmit data to healthcare providers, caregivers and users, provide alerts and reminders, and support diagnosis and treatment. Some examples of connected healthcare monitors are smart watches, fitness trackers, blood pressure monitors, glucose meters and pulse oximeters from brands like Apple, Fitbit, Omron, iHealth and Masimo.
- **Connected manufacturing equipment**: Equipment that can automate and optimize various aspects of industrial production, such as quality control, maintenance, safety, energy efficiency and waste reduction. Connected manufacturing equipment can also enable real-time monitoring, remote control, predictive analytics and machine learning. Some examples of connected manufacturing equipment are robots, sensors, PLCs, SCADA systems and MES systems from companies like Siemens, ABB, Rockwell Automation and GE.
- **Connected cities**: Cities that can improve the quality of life, sustainability and resilience of urban areas through smart management of infrastructure, services, resources and environment. Connected cities can also enable citizen participation, transparency, innovation and social inclusion. Some examples of connected cities are smart lighting, smart parking, smart waste management, smart traffic management and smart public safety from cities like Barcelona, Singapore, Amsterdam and New York.
