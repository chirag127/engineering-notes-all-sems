### Architectural view for the notes of the Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT) in the subject of Internet of Things

- The architectural view of IoT is a way of describing the structure and behavior of an IoT system, which consists of various components that communicate and interact with each other to achieve a common goal.
- The architectural view of IoT can help to understand the requirements, design, implementation, and evaluation of an IoT system, as well as to identify the challenges and opportunities for IoT applications and services.
- There is no standard or universal architecture for IoT, as different IoT systems may have different functionalities, domains, and requirements. However, some common architectural layers can be identified and described, based on the existing literature and practice of IoT.
- The following are some of the common architectural layers of IoT, along with their functions and examples:

  - **Perception layer**: This is the lowest layer of IoT, which deals with the sensing and identification of physical objects and phenomena. It consists of various sensors, actuators, RFID tags, cameras, and other devices that can collect data from the environment and perform actions on it. The perception layer is responsible for providing the raw data and information for the IoT system.
  - **Network layer**: This is the layer that connects the perception layer with the upper layers of IoT, and enables the transmission and processing of data and information. It consists of various communication technologies, protocols, and standards that can support the data exchange and interoperability among different devices and components of IoT. The network layer is responsible for providing the connectivity and reliability for the IoT system.
  - **Middleware layer**: This is the layer that bridges the gap between the network layer and the application layer of IoT, and provides the common services and functions that are needed by the IoT applications and users. It consists of various software platforms, frameworks, and tools that can support the data management, storage, analysis, security, privacy, and semantic interoperability of IoT. The middleware layer is responsible for providing the functionality and scalability for the IoT system.
  - **Application layer**: This is the highest layer of IoT, which deals with the delivery and consumption of the IoT services and solutions. It consists of various applications, systems, and users that can utilize the data and information provided by the lower layers of IoT, and create value and benefits for different domains and scenarios. The application layer is responsible for providing the usability and intelligence for the IoT system.
