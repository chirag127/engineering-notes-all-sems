### M2M Communication

- M2M stands for Machine to Machine communication, which refers to the automated exchange of data between machines, devices, or systems without human intervention.
- M2M is the foundation for IoT, which is the Internet of Things, a network of physical objects embedded with sensors, software, and connectivity that enable them to collect, process, and share data.
- M2M communication can occur over various types of networks, such as cellular, wired, wireless, or satellite, depending on the application and the environment.
- M2M communication enables various benefits, such as remote monitoring and control, automation, efficiency, safety, and cost savings.
- M2M communication can be applied to various domains, such as smart homes, smart cities, smart grids, smart agriculture, smart health, smart transportation, smart manufacturing, and smart retail.
