## Unit 7 - IoT Applications

- IoT applications are the use cases of the Internet of Things, which is the network of physical objects embedded with sensors, software, and other technologies that enable data exchange and communication with other devices and systems over the internet.
- IoT applications can bring a lot of value to various domains, such as industry, commerce, agriculture, healthcare, and smart cities, by improving efficiency, productivity, safety, and quality of life.
- Some examples of IoT applications are:

  - Smart lighting systems that can adjust the brightness and color of the lights based on the time of day, occupancy, and user preferences, reducing energy consumption and enhancing comfort .
  - Smart parking systems that can monitor the availability of parking spaces and guide drivers to the nearest vacant spot, increasing profits and reducing traffic congestion .
  - Smart agriculture systems that can collect data on soil moisture, temperature, humidity, and crop health, and use it to optimize irrigation, fertilization, and pest control, improving crop yield and quality .
  - Smart health systems that can enable remote monitoring and emergency notification of patients' vital signs, such as blood pressure, heart rate, and glucose levels, using wearable devices or implants, improving health outcomes and reducing healthcare costs .
  - Smart city systems that can measure air quality, radiation levels, noise pollution, and traffic flow, and use it to inform citizens, authorities, and service providers, improving environmental and public safety .
