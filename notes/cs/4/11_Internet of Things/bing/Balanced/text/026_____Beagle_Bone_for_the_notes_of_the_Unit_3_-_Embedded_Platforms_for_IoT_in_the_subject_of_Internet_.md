### Beagle Bone

- Beagle Bone is a credit-card-sized Linux computer that connects to the Internet and runs software such as Android and Ubuntu.
- Beagle Bone is based on the Texas Instruments AM335x 720MHz ARM® processor, which provides plenty of I/O and processing power for real-time analysis.
- Beagle Bone can be complemented with cape plug-in boards to augment functionality, such as adding sensors, displays, motors, etc.
- Beagle Bone is an affordable and open-source platform for makers and developers who want to build projects like 3D-printers, arcade games, robots, etc.
- Beagle Bone has several variants, such as Beagle Bone Black, Beagle Bone Green, Beagle Bone AI, etc, which differ in features, price, and availability .
- Beagle Bone supports various programming languages, such as C, Python, JavaScript, etc, and can be programmed using various IDEs, such as Cloud9, Arduino, etc.
- Beagle Bone has a large and active community of users and developers who provide support, tutorials, and resources for learning and troubleshooting.
