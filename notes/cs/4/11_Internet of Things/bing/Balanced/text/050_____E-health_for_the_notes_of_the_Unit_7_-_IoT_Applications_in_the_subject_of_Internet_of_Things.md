### E-health

- E-health is a term that refers to the use of electronic processes and communication, such as computers, the Internet, and mobile devices, to facilitate health improvement and health care services  .
- E-health can include various applications, such as telehealth, telemedicine, m-health, e-learning, e-prescribing, e-referrals, e-records, e-surveillance, and e-research .
- E-health can provide benefits such as improved access, quality, efficiency, and cost-effectiveness of health care, as well as enhanced patient empowerment, engagement, and satisfaction  .
- E-health can also pose challenges such as ethical, legal, social, and technical issues, such as privacy, security, interoperability, standardization, regulation, and evaluation  .
- E-health is an emerging and evolving field that requires multidisciplinary collaboration, innovation, and research to address the needs and expectations of various stakeholders, such as patients, health professionals, health organizations, and health systems  .
