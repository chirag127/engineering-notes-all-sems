### E-health

E-health is a term that refers to the use of information and communication technologies (ICT) to support health and health-related fields. E-health can include various applications, such as:

- Telehealth: the delivery of health care services and information remotely, using technologies such as computers, mobile devices, video conferencing, and wearable sensors. Telehealth can enable access to health care for people in rural or remote areas, reduce travel costs and time, and improve the quality and efficiency of health care delivery. Telehealth can also support self-management of chronic conditions, patient education, and health promotion.   
- Electronic health records (EHRs): the digital storage and exchange of health information among health care providers, patients, and other authorized parties. EHRs can improve the continuity, accuracy, and security of health information, facilitate clinical decision making and coordination of care, and enable data analysis and research. EHRs can also empower patients to access and control their own health information and participate in their own care. 
- Mobile health (mHealth): the use of mobile devices, such as smartphones and tablets, to deliver health care services and information. mHealth can include applications, such as health-related apps, text messages, and voice calls, that can provide health education, reminders, alerts, monitoring, diagnosis, treatment, and feedback. mHealth can also enable real-time data collection and transmission, and support communication and collaboration among health care providers and patients. mHealth can enhance the accessibility, affordability, and quality of health care, especially for underserved populations and low-resource settings. 
- Health information systems (HIS): the systems and processes that collect, store, manage, and analyze health data and information. HIS can support various functions, such as health administration, planning, financing, regulation, evaluation, and research. HIS can also enable the generation and dissemination of health information products, such as reports, dashboards, and indicators, that can inform health policy and practice. HIS can improve the availability, reliability, and usability of health information, and facilitate evidence-based decision making and action. 

E-health can offer various benefits, such as:

- Improving the access, quality, and efficiency of health care services and information
- Reducing the costs and barriers of health care delivery and utilization
- Enhancing the communication and collaboration among health care providers and patients
- Empowering patients to participate in their own care and health management
- Supporting health promotion, prevention, and education
- Enabling data analysis, research, and innovation
- Informing health policy and practice

E-health can also pose various challenges, such as:

- Ensuring the privacy, security, and confidentiality of health information
- Addressing the ethical, legal, and social implications of e-health
- Developing and maintaining the technical infrastructure and standards for e-health
- Ensuring the interoperability and compatibility of e-health systems and applications
- Evaluating the effectiveness and impact of e-health interventions and outcomes
- Building the capacity and skills of health care providers and users for e-health
- Engaging and involving the stakeholders and beneficiaries of e-health
- Addressing the digital divide and health inequities in e-health access and use
