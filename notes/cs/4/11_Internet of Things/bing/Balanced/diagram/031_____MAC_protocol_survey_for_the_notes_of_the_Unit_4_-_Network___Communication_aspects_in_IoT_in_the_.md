### MAC protocol survey for the notes of the Unit 4 - Network & Communication aspects in IoT in the subject of Internet of Things

- MAC (Medium Access Control) protocol is the first protocol layer above the physical layer in wireless decentralized networks in IoT.
- MAC protocol aims to coordinate access among IoT devices to the shared wireless medium and avoid collisions and interference.
- MAC protocol can be classified into two main categories: contention-based and contention-free.
- Contention-based MAC protocol allows devices to compete for the channel access without reservation or scheduling, but it may cause collisions and waste of bandwidth.
- Contention-free MAC protocol assigns channel access to devices based on reservation or scheduling, but it may incur overhead and delay.
- IEEE 802.11ah, also known as WiFi HaLow, is a scalable solution for medium-range communication in IoT that leverages various innovative MAC features, such as restricted access window, target wake time, and group addressed transmission .
- IEEE 802.11ah supports both contention-based and contention-free MAC modes, and it can adapt to different traffic patterns and network scenarios .
- IEEE 802.11ah also supports mobility management and power saving mechanisms for IoT devices .
- Other MAC protocols for IoT include Bluetooth Low Energy, ZigBee, LoRaWAN, and NB-IoT, which have different characteristics and trade-offs in terms of coverage, data rate, energy efficiency, and scalability.
- MAC protocols for IoT are still evolving and facing new challenges, such as heterogeneity, security, reliability, and quality of service .
