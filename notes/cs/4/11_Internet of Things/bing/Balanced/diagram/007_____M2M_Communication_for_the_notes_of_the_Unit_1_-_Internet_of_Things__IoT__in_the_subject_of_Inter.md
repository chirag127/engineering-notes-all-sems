### M2M Communication

- M2M stands for Machine to Machine communication, which is the exchange of data between two or more devices without human intervention.
- M2M is a key component of the Internet of Things (IoT), which is the network of physical objects embedded with sensors, software, and connectivity that enable them to collect, process, and share data.
- M2M communication can be achieved through various methods, such as serial connection, powerline connection, or wireless communication.
- M2M communication enables various applications and benefits, such as remote monitoring, automation, smart metering, asset tracking, security, and health care.
- M2M communication faces some challenges, such as interoperability, scalability, security, and privacy.
