### IoT/M2M systems layers and design standardization for the notes of the Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT) in the subject of Internet of Things

IoT/M2M systems are composed of multiple layers, including:

1. Physical layer: This layer includes the physical devices and sensors that collect data and communicate with other devices in the network.

2. Network layer: This layer includes the communication protocols and technologies used to transmit data between devices in the network.

3. Data layer: This layer includes the data management and storage systems used to store and process data collected by the devices in the network.

4. Application layer: This layer includes the applications and services that use the data collected by the devices in the network.

Design standardization is important for IoT/M2M systems, as it helps to ensure interoperability and compatibility between different devices and systems in the network. Some of the key design standards for IoT/M2M systems include:

1. Communication protocols: Standards such as MQTT, CoAP, and AMQP are used to ensure interoperability between devices in the network.

2. Data formats: Standards such as JSON and XML are used to ensure that data can be easily shared and processed between different devices and systems.

3. Security standards: Standards such as TLS and SSL are used to ensure the security of data transmitted between devices in the network.

In conclusion, IoT/M2M systems are composed of multiple layers, including the physical layer, network layer, data layer, and application layer. Design standardization is important for IoT/M2M systems, as it helps to ensure interoperability and compatibility between different devices and systems in the network. Key design standards for IoT/M2M systems include communication protocols, data formats, and security standards.
