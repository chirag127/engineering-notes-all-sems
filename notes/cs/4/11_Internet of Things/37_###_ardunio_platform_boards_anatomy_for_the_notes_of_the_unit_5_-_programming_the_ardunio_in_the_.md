### Ardunio Platform Boards Anatomy for the notes of the Unit 5 - Programming the Ardunio in the subject of Internet of Things

Arduino boards have the following key components:

1. Microcontroller: the central processing unit that runs the programmed instructions.
2. Input/Output (I/O) pins: used to interact with other devices and sensors.
3. USB port: used for programming and power supply.
4. Voltage regulator: regulates the voltage from the USB or external power supply to the correct level for the microcontroller.
5. Crystal oscillator: sets the clock speed for the microcontroller.
6. Flash memory: stores the program code.
7. Reset button: used to reset the microcontroller.
8. Power LED: indicates the board is powered.
9. TX/RX LEDs: indicate serial communication activity.

Additionally, some boards have additional components such as:

1. EEPROM memory: used to store data that persist even after the board is powered off.
2. R3 headers: used to connect shields, which are expansion boards that add functionality to the Arduino.
3. DC power jack: used to connect an external power supply.
