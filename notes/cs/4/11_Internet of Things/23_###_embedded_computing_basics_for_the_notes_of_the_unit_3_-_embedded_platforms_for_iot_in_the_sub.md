### Embedded computing basics for the notes of the Unit 3 - Embedded Platforms for IoT in the subject of Internet of Things

Embedded computing refers to the use of computer systems integrated into other devices and products, serving a specific function or set of functions. 

1. Types of embedded systems: Standalone, Networked, Real-time, Mobile. 
2. Characteristics: Limited resources (memory, processing power), specific function, real-time constraints.
3. Key components: Microcontroller/Microprocessor, Memory, Input/Output interfaces.
4. Development process: Requirements gathering, Design, Implementation, Testing, Deployment.
5. Applications: Consumer electronics, Automotive, Healthcare, Industrial control, Robotics.
6. Operating Systems: Embedded Linux, RTOS (Real-Time Operating Systems), FreeRTOS.
7. Programming languages: C, C++, Assembly.

Note: This is a basic overview of embedded computing, and the subject can be much more complex and detailed.
