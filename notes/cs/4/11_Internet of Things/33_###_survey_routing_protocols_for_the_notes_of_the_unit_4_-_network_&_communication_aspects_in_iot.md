### Survey routing protocols for the notes of the Unit 4 - Network & Communication aspects in IoT in the subject of Internet of Things

Survey routing protocols are a class of routing protocols specifically designed for use in IoT networks. These protocols are used to gather information about the network topology and to determine the best routes for data transmission. 

Advantages of survey routing protocols include:

1. Efficient network discovery: Survey routing protocols allow for efficient discovery of the network topology, reducing the time required to establish a route.

2. Dynamic network adaptation: Survey routing protocols allow the network to adapt dynamically to changes in the network topology, ensuring that data is transmitted over the best available route.

3. Reduced overhead: Survey routing protocols have low overhead, as they only transmit information about the network topology, rather than the data itself.

Disadvantages of survey routing protocols include:

1. Limited scalability: Survey routing protocols may not be suitable for large IoT networks, as the amount of information transmitted by the protocol can become overwhelming.

2. Complexity: Survey routing protocols can be complex to implement, requiring specialized hardware and software.

In conclusion, survey routing protocols are a class of routing protocols specifically designed for use in IoT networks. These protocols are used to gather information about the network topology and to determine the best routes for data transmission. Survey routing protocols have advantages, including efficient network discovery, dynamic network adaptation, and reduced overhead, but also have disadvantages, including limited scalability and complexity.
