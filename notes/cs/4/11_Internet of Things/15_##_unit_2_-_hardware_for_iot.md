## Unit 2 - Hardware for IoT

Unit 2 - Hardware for IoT covers the various types of hardware components used in Internet of Things (IoT) devices. The main components include:
1. Sensors: detect and measure physical parameters like temperature, light, and motion. 
2. Actuators: control physical devices such as motors, valves, and pumps.
3. Microcontrollers: process and store data from sensors, and control actuators.
4. Network interfaces: provide connectivity to the internet and other devices.
5. Power management: ensure reliable and efficient power supply to the device.
6. Enclosures: protect and secure the hardware components.

Each component has its own specifications, such as accuracy, reliability, and power consumption, that must be considered when selecting hardware for an IoT device. Additionally, the hardware must be compatible with the software and communication protocols used in the device.
