### Raspberry pi for the notes of the Unit 3 - Embedded Platforms for IoT in the subject of Internet of Things

Raspberry Pi is a small, low-cost, single-board computer designed to promote the teaching of basic computer science in schools and developing countries. It was developed by the Raspberry Pi Foundation in the UK in 2012.

Features of Raspberry Pi:

1. Hardware: Raspberry Pi has a Broadcom BCM2837B0, Cortex-A53 (ARMv8) 64-bit SoC @ 1.4GHz, 1GB LPDDR4-3200 SDRAM (2GB, 4GB, or 8GB versions also available), 2.4GHz and 5GHz IEEE 802.11ac wireless, Bluetooth 5.0, BLE, Gigabit Ethernet, 2 USB 3.0 ports, 2 USB 2.0 ports, and 40 GPIO pins.

2. Operating System: Raspberry Pi runs on a variety of operating systems, including Raspberry Pi OS (previously known as Raspbian), a Debian-based Linux distribution, and several other Linux distributions.

3. Applications: Raspberry Pi can be used for a variety of applications, including home media centers, game consoles, desktop computers, and educational tools. It is also commonly used as a platform for Internet of Things (IoT) projects, due to its low cost and small form factor.

Advantages of Raspberry Pi:

1. Cost: Raspberry Pi is a low-cost computer, making it accessible to a wider range of users.

2. Size: Raspberry Pi is small and compact, making it ideal for use in embedded systems and IoT projects.

3. Community: Raspberry Pi has a large and active community, providing support and resources for users.

Disadvantages of Raspberry Pi:

1. Performance: Raspberry Pi is not as powerful as other computers, making it unsuitable for demanding applications.

2. Limited hardware: Raspberry Pi has limited hardware compared to other computers, which can limit its capabilities.

In conclusion, Raspberry Pi is a small, low-cost, single-board computer designed to promote the teaching of basic computer science. It runs on a variety of operating systems and can be used for a variety of applications, including IoT projects. Raspberry Pi has advantages, including cost, size, and community, but also has disadvantages, including limited performance and hardware.
