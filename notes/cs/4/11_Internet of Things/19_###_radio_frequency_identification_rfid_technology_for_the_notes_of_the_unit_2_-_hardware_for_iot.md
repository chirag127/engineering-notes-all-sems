### radio frequency identification (RFID) technology for the notes of the Unit 2 - Hardware for IoT in the subject of Internet of Things

Radio Frequency Identification (RFID) is a wireless technology used for identifying and tracking objects using radio waves. It consists of three components: a reader, an antenna, and a tag. The reader sends out a radio frequency signal, which is received by the antenna and transmitted to the tag. The tag responds with its unique identifier, which is then transmitted back to the reader and processed to identify the object. RFID is widely used in various applications such as inventory management, supply chain management, and security systems. It offers benefits such as real-time tracking, improved accuracy, and reduced costs compared to traditional identification methods. However, RFID technology also has some limitations, such as privacy concerns, limited range, and interference from other RF signals.
