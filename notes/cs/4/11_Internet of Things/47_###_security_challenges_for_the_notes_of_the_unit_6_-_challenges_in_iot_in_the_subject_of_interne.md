### Security Challenges for the notes of the Unit 6 - Challenges in IoT in the subject of Internet of Things

1. Device security: IoT devices often lack strong security measures, making them vulnerable to hacking and malware attacks.

2. Network security: IoT devices communicate through networks, which can be vulnerable to cyber attacks.

3. Data privacy: IoT devices generate and store large amounts of personal data, which can be vulnerable to unauthorized access and misuse.

4. Interoperability: IoT devices from different manufacturers may not be compatible, leading to security vulnerabilities.

5. Lack of standardization: IoT devices may use different protocols, making it difficult to secure the entire system.

6. Limited resources: IoT devices often have limited processing power and memory, making it difficult to implement strong security measures.

7. Complexity: IoT systems can be complex, making it difficult to identify and address security vulnerabilities.
