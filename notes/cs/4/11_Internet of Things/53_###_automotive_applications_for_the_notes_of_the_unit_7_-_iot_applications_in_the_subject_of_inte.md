### Automotive Applications for the notes of the Unit 7 - IoT Applications in the subject of Internet of Things

Automotive Applications refer to the use of IoT technology in the automotive industry. This includes areas such as vehicle tracking, predictive maintenance, infotainment, and autonomous vehicles. IoT devices are used to collect data from vehicles, which is then used to improve various aspects of the driving experience. For example, predictive maintenance uses data from sensors to predict when a vehicle may need maintenance, reducing the risk of breakdowns and improving vehicle reliability. Infotainment systems use IoT technology to provide drivers and passengers with access to entertainment and information while on the road. Autonomous vehicles use IoT technology to gather data from sensors and make decisions about driving, potentially reducing the risk of accidents and improving overall road safety.
