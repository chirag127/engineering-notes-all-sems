### Actuators for the notes of the Unit 2 - Hardware for IoT

1. Actuators: Actuators are devices that convert an electrical signal into mechanical action. They are used to perform physical actions based on the data received from sensors. Some common examples of actuators are:
- Motors: Motors are used to generate motion. They convert electrical energy into kinetic energy. DC motors and Stepper motors are commonly used in IoT devices.
- Solenoids: Solenoids convert electrical energy into linear motion. They contain coiled wires that generate a magnetic field when electric current passes through them. The magnetic field is used to move an object in a linear path.
- Relays: Relays are electrically operated switches that are used to control high voltage devices using low voltage signals. They allow a low power signal to control a high power device.
2. Working: Actuators receive input signals from a microcontroller or other control system which then controls the actuator to perform a physical action like moving a motor, operating a switch, etc. The type of signal (analog/digital), operating voltage, maximum load capacity, operating speed, etc. are considered while selecting an appropriate actuator for an application.
3. Applications: Actuators find applications in various IoT devices to perform physical actions based on the data received from sensors. Some examples are:
- Opening and closing valves in smart irrigation systems.
- Locking and unlocking doors in smart home security systems.
- Moving motors in robotics and automation systems.
- Operating switches and relays in various industrial control systems.
