## Unit 7 - IoT Applications

1. Smart Homes - IoT devices can automate homes by controlling lights, thermostats, appliances, and other devices. Motion sensors can detect presence and adjust settings accordingly. Smart locks allow keyless entry. Home devices can be monitored and controlled remotely via mobile apps.

2. Smart Cities - IoT can optimize and integrate city infrastructure and services. Applications include smart parking, traffic management, environmental monitoring, utility monitoring, and security. Sensors can track availability of parking spots, monitor traffic and pollution, detect leaks or outages, and monitor security. Collected data can enable city officials to make informed decisions.

3. Healthcare - IoT enables continuous monitoring of patients and medical assets. Devices can track patients' vital signs, monitor blood glucose or other medical conditions, and alert doctors to changes or emergencies. IoT can track the location and status of medical equipment and ensure proper maintenance. Wearable or implantable sensors transmit data to healthcare providers to enable preventive and proactive treatment.

4. Manufacturing - IoT streamlines the manufacturing process via machine monitoring and control. Sensors track the performance and status of manufacturing equipment and environments. The data is analyzed to maximize uptime and optimize performance. IoT devices also track the location and status of work-in-process inventory and can automate many tasks. This results in cost savings, reduced waste, and higher quality products.

5. Transportation - IoT enables smart transportation systems. Applications include fleet management, smart vehicles, and intelligent transportation systems. Fleet vehicles can be tracked in real-time, and data from vehicles can be monitored for predictive maintenance. Vehicles are being outfitted with internet connectivity and sensors to provide navigation assistance, remote diagnostics, and a range of smart features. Intelligent transportation systems use data from sensors and cameras to monitor traffic and road conditions and dynamically control traffic flow.
