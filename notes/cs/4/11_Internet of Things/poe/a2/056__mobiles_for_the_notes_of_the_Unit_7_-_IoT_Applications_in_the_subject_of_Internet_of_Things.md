### Mobiles for IoT Applications

- Smartphones can act as IoT devices and hubs to control other IoT devices. They have computing capabilities, connectivity and a user interface which makes them suitable for IoT applications.
- Some uses of mobiles in IoT:
    - Monitoring and controlling smart home devices like lights, thermostats, etc. from the mobile.
    - Receiving notifications from IoT sensors about security, environment, etc.
    - Mobile payments and transactions using NFC.
    - Mobile health applications to track health metrics using wearable sensors.
    - Mobile as a gateway to access and control devices through the cloud.
    - Beacon technology using Bluetooth to provide location-based services and customized user experiences.
    - Mobile apps to monitor and control IoT devices like vehicles, pumps, etc. in industrial environments.
- Challenges:
    - Variety of mobile platforms and operating systems.
    - Security and privacy concerns with mobile data and IoT control.
    - Short battery life of mobiles can limit their usage for certain IoT applications.
    - Seamless connectivity between mobiles and IoT devices is required which can be difficult to achieve.
