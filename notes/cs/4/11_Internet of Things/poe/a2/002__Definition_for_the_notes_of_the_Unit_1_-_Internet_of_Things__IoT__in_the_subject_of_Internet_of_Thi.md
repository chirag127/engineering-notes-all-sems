### Definition for the notes of the Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT)

1. Internet of Things (IoT) refers to the interconnection of everyday physical objects and appliances via the internet. These devices are equipped with sensors, processors, and networking capabilities that allow them to send and receive data.
2. IoT allows everyday objects to be sensed and controlled remotely across existing network infrastructure, creating opportunities for integration of the physical world into computer-based systems, resulting in improved efficiency, accuracy and economic benefit.
3. IoT is a huge network of connected things and people - all of which collect and share data about the way they are used and about the environment around them.
4. The key components of the IoT are: sensors, connectivity, data storage and analytics. Sensors are used to detect changes in the environment. Connectivity is needed to enable the flow of data between devices. Data storage and analytics are used to process the data collected and generate useful insights.
5. The potential benefits of IoT include increased efficiency, improved accuracy, economic benefit, and improved customer experiences. However, IoT also brings security and privacy risks and challenges that must be addressed.
