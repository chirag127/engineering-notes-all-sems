### MAC protocol survey for the notes of the Unit 4 - Network & Communication aspects in IoT in the subject of Internet of Things.

1. ALOHA:
- Pure Aloha: Nodes transmit packets whenever they have data to send. If collision occurs, nodes retransmit after a random time. Low throughput.
- Slotted Aloha: Time is divided into slots. Nodes can only transmit at slot boundaries. If collision occurs, nodes retransmit in next slot. Higher throughput than pure Aloha.

2. Carrier Sense Multiple Access (CSMA):
- Nodes sense the medium before transmitting. If idle, they transmit. If busy, they wait for a random time and sense again.
- Example: CSMA/CD used in Ethernet. Colliding nodes detect collision and retransmit after a random wait.

3. Time Division Multiple Access (TDMA):
- Time is divided into slots assigned to each node. Nodes only transmit in their allocated slots. Coordinated allocation of slots to nodes required. Higher throughput and efficiency but requires synchronization among nodes.

4. Frequency Division Multiple Access (FDMA):
- The available bandwidth is divided into frequency bands assigned to nodes. Nodes only transmit in their allocated frequency bands. Frequency allocation to nodes must be coordinated. Susceptible to interference from adjacent frequency bands.
