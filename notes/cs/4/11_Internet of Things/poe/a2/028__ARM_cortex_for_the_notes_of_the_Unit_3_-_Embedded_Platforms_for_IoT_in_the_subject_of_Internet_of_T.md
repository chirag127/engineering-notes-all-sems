### ARM Cortex

- ARM Cortex is a family of 32-bit RISC ARM processor cores designed by ARM Holdings.
- Cortex cores are designed as microprocessor solutions, and have been shipped in tens of billions of devices.
- The ARM Cortex series of cores include Cortex-M, Cortex-R and Cortex-A.
- Cortex-M cores are designed for microcontroller use, and are the most common cores in low-cost and low-power IoT devices. Cortex-R cores are designed for hard real-time tasks. Cortex-A cores are designed for high-performance applications such as smartphones and tablets.
- Key features of Cortex cores include:
    - 32-bit ARM instruction set architecture
    - Two operating modes: ARM mode (32-bit) and Thumb mode (16-bit instructions) allowing improved code density.
    - Memory Protection Unit providing memory protection and memory management.
    - Advanced bus interfaces and co-processors to offload common tasks from the main CPU.
    - Scalable performance and scalable power usage.
- Cortex cores are designed for synthesisation as fabless semiconductor intellectual property cores, as well as implementation in ASICs or ASSPs.
- Leading semiconductor companies that have Cortex cores in many of their designs include:
    - Qualcomm
    - Samsung
    - Nvidia
    - Broadcom
