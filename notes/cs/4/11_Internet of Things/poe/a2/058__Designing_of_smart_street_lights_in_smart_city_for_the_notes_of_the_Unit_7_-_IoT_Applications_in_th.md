### Designing of smart street lights in smart city

1. Smart street lights are the street lights integrated with sensors and wireless technologies which can automatically adjust the intensity of light based on the traffic and pedestrian movement.
2. The sensors used can be motion sensors, daylight sensors and occupancy sensors. The motion and occupancy sensors detect the traffic and pedestrian movement to adjust the intensity of light. The daylight sensors detect the ambient light to adjust the intensity of the street lights.
3. The data from the sensors can be sent to the microcontroller which can control the intensity of the LED street lights. The wireless technologies like Wi-Fi, Zigbee or LoRaWAN can be used to send the sensor data and also to control the street lights.
4. The smart street lights save a lot of energy by adjusting the light intensities based on the requirement. The dimming of lights can be done gradually depending on the sensor inputs. The status of the street lights can also be monitored remotely.
5. The smart street lights along with other smart city solutions like smart parking, smart garbage management etc. make a city smart and sustainable. The data from the sensors can provide useful insights into the traffic and pedestrian patterns which can be used to improve other services.
