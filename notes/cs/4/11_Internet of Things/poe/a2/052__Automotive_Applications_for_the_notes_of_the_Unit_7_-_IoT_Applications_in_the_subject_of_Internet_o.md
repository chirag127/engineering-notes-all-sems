### Automotive Applications

- Smart cars with WiFi and sensors: New cars are being equipped with WiFi and various sensors to collect data about the car's status and surroundings. This data can be used to provide various smart features and self-driving capabilities.
- Predictive maintenance: Sensors can detect potential issues with the car like low tire pressure or malfunctioning components. This data can be used to alert the driver or auto-schedule maintenance to address issues proactively before they become problems.
- Improved navigation: Data from sensors and cameras can improve navigation by detecting traffic, road hazards or accidents in real-time and rerouting the driver.
- Smart parking: Sensors can detect available parking spots and navigate the driver to the optimal spot. Payment can also be automated via connected systems.
- Road monitoring: Data from numerous connected cars on the road can be aggregated to monitor traffic, road conditions, accidents and other metrics on a city-wide scale. This data can optimize traffic flow and redirect drivers.
- Infotainment: Systems within the car can provide a range of information and entertainment options, all powered by internet connectivity and AI. Options include streaming music, news, weather alerts, points of interest and more.
