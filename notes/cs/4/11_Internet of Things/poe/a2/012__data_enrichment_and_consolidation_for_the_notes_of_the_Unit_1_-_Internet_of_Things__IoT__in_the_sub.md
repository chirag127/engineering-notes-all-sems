### Data Enrichment and Consolidation for Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT) Notes

1. Data collected from IoT devices is raw and sparse. It needs to be enriched and consolidated to derive meaningful insights.
2. Data enrichment techniques like imputation are used to fill in missing values or provide more context to the data. Statistical techniques or machine learning models can be used for imputation.
3. Data consolidation involves combining data from multiple IoT devices or sources. This could be combining data from sensors monitoring the same metrics in the same location or combining data from different types of sensors to gain a more holistic view.
4. The consolidated data is then stored for analysis. This could be storing the raw consolidated data or storing aggregates of the data for faster analysis and to reduce storage costs.
5. Time-series databases are commonly used to store IoT data as they are optimized for storing and retrieving time-stamped data. This makes them suitable for storing the massive volumes of time-series data generated by IoT devices.

The above points cover the key steps involved in enriching and consolidating the data collected from IoT devices to enable analysis and gain insights. The enriched and consolidated data can then be used to build various analytics capabilities for IoT applications.
