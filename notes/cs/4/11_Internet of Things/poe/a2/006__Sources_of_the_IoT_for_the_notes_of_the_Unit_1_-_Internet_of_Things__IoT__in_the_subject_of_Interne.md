### Sources of the IoT for the notes of the Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT) in the subject of Internet of Things

1. Sensors: Sensors are devices that can detect and measure physical parameters from the real world such as temperature, humidity, pressure, light, motion etc. and convert them into electrical signals which can be measured and recorded. IoT devices use various sensors to collect data from the physical world.

2. Microcontrollers: Microcontrollers are embedded systems that contain memory, programmable input/output pins and a processor core. They are used to control the sensors and actuators in IoT devices and relay the data from sensors to the gateways/cloud. They run on embedded firmware and have limited computing capability.

3. Connectivity options: IoT devices need to be connected to the internet to relay the data from the physical world and allow remote access. This requires various connectivity options such as Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, RFID, NFC, cellular data, Ethernet, etc. The choice of connectivity option depends on factors like range, bandwidth, power requirements, cost, etc.

4. Processing and storage: The data collected by sensors needs to be processed, stored and analyzed either at the edge devices or in the cloud. IoT devices use onboard processing and storage capabilities or rely on cloud computing services to derive insights and enable automation from the collected data.

5. Actuators: Actuators are devices that can convert an electrical signal into motion or physical change. IoT uses actuators to enable automation by performing physical actions based on analyzed data from sensors. Various actuators used in IoT include motors, LEDs, displays etc.

6. User interfaces: User interfaces are required to monitor, control and interact with IoT devices. This can include interfaces on the IoT devices themselves or mobile/web apps to access IoT devices and data from the cloud. The user interfaces enable human-machine interaction with the IoT systems.
