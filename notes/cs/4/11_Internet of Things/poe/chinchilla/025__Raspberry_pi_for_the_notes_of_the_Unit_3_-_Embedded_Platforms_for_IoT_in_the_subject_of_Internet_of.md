### Raspberry Pi for the notes of the Unit 3 - Embedded Platforms for IoT in the subject of Internet of Things

Raspberry Pi is a single-board computer that has gained a lot of popularity in the IoT industry due to its affordability, versatility, and compatibility with various operating systems. In this section, we will discuss the features and capabilities of Raspberry Pi in the context of embedded platforms for IoT.

Here are some key points to keep in mind:

- Raspberry Pi is a low-cost, credit-card-sized computer that can be used in a variety of IoT applications, including home automation, robotics, and industrial IoT.

- Raspberry Pi is powered by an ARM processor (usually ARM Cortex-A53 or ARM Cortex-A72) and comes with a range of built-in connectivity options, including Wi-Fi, Ethernet, and Bluetooth.

- Raspberry Pi runs on a variety of operating systems, including Raspbian (a Debian-based OS developed specifically for Raspberry Pi), Ubuntu, and Windows 10 IoT Core.

- Raspberry Pi can be programmed using a variety of programming languages, including Python, C, C++, and Java.

- Raspberry Pi has a range of GPIO (General Purpose Input/Output) pins that can be used for interfacing with sensors, actuators, and other hardware components.

- Raspberry Pi has a range of accessories and add-ons that can be used to extend its capabilities, including cameras, displays, and motor controllers.

- Raspberry Pi has a large and active community of developers and enthusiasts who share knowledge, resources, and projects through online forums and communities.

In summary, Raspberry Pi is a powerful and versatile embedded platform for IoT that offers a wide range of features and capabilities at an affordable price point. Its compatibility with various operating systems and programming languages, as well as its built-in connectivity options and GPIO pins, make it an ideal choice for a variety of IoT applications.
