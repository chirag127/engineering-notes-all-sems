## Unit 4 - Network & Communication aspects in IoT

In this unit, we will learn about the network and communication aspects of IoT. The following points will be covered:

- **Introduction to IoT networks**: We will start by understanding the basics of IoT networks, including their types and the protocols used for communication.

- **Wireless communication technologies**: We will then move on to discussing the various wireless communication technologies used in IoT networks, such as Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Zigbee, and LoRaWAN.

- **Network topologies**: We will learn about the different network topologies used in IoT, including star, mesh, and tree topologies.

- **IoT gateways**: We will discuss the role of IoT gateways in connecting IoT devices to the internet and the cloud.

- **Security in IoT networks**: We will cover the various security measures that should be implemented in IoT networks to protect against cyber attacks.

- **IoT data communication**: We will learn about the different ways in which data is transmitted in IoT networks, including MQTT, CoAP, and HTTP.

- **IoT cloud communication**: We will discuss the role of cloud computing in IoT networks and how data is transmitted between IoT devices and the cloud.

- **IoT communication standards**: We will cover the various standards and protocols used in IoT communication, including 6LoWPAN, Thread, and AllJoyn.

- **IoT communication challenges**: We will conclude by discussing the challenges faced in IoT communication, such as low power consumption and limited bandwidth.

By the end of this unit, you will have a thorough understanding of the network and communication aspects of IoT, enabling you to design and implement robust IoT networks.
