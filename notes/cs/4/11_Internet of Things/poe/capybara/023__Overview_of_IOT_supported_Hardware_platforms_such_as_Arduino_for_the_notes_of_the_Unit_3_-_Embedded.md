### Overview of IOT supported Hardware platforms such as Arduino

In the world of the Internet of Things, hardware platforms play a crucial role in enabling the deployment of connected devices. The purpose of this overview is to provide an understanding of some of the most popular hardware platforms used in the development of IoT applications, with a focus on the Arduino platform.

Here are some key points to keep in mind:

- IoT hardware platforms are designed to enable the creation of connected devices that can communicate with each other and with the internet.
- Arduino is a popular open-source hardware platform that is widely used in the development of IoT applications due to its ease of use and affordability.
- Arduino boards come in a variety of sizes and configurations, from the small and simple Arduino Uno to the more powerful Arduino Mega.
- Arduino boards can be programmed using a variety of programming languages, including C++, which is the most common language used in the development of IoT applications.
- In addition to Arduino, there are a number of other popular IoT hardware platforms, including Raspberry Pi, BeagleBone, and Intel Edison.
- Each of these platforms has its own strengths and weaknesses, and the choice of platform will depend on the specific requirements of the IoT application being developed.

Overall, IoT hardware platforms are an essential component of the development of connected devices, and Arduino is a popular choice due to its ease of use, affordability, and versatility. However, it is important to keep in mind that there are a number of other platforms available, and the choice of platform will depend on the specific needs of the project at hand.
