### NetArduino

NetArduino is an embedded platform that is commonly used for Internet of Things (IoT) applications. It is based on the popular Arduino platform, but with the added benefit of built-in Ethernet connectivity.

Here are some important points to know about NetArduino:

- NetArduino is a microcontroller board that is based on the Atmel SAM3X8E ARM Cortex-M3 CPU.
- It has 54 digital input/output pins, 12 analog inputs, 4 UARTs (hardware serial ports), a 84 MHz clock, an Ethernet PHY (physical layer transceiver), and a USB port.
- The built-in Ethernet connectivity allows NetArduino to communicate with other devices on a network and to connect to the internet.
- NetArduino can be programmed using the Arduino Integrated Development Environment (IDE), which is a free software tool that makes it easy to write and upload code to the board.
- There are many libraries and examples available for NetArduino, which can be used to quickly build IoT applications.
- NetArduino is compatible with a wide range of shields (add-on boards) that can be used to extend its functionality.
- NetArduino is a popular choice for IoT applications because of its ease of use and flexibility.

In summary, NetArduino is an embedded platform that is well-suited for IoT applications. It is based on the Arduino platform and has built-in Ethernet connectivity, making it easy to communicate with other devices on a network and to connect to the internet. Its ease of use and flexibility make it a popular choice for IoT applications.
