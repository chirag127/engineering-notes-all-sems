### Raspberry pi for the notes of the Unit 3 - Embedded Platforms for IoT in the subject of Internet of Things

Raspberry Pi is a low-cost, credit-card sized computer that plugs into a computer monitor or TV, and uses a standard keyboard and mouse. It is a popular choice for IoT development due to its affordability, versatility and ease of use. Here are some important notes on Raspberry Pi for Unit 3 of the Embedded Platforms for IoT course:

- Raspberry Pi is a single-board computer that runs on Linux-based operating systems such as Raspbian, Debian and Ubuntu.
- It has a Broadcom BCM2835 system on a chip (SoC) which includes an ARM-based processor, GPU, and RAM.
- Raspberry Pi supports various programming languages such as Python, C, C++, Java, Scratch, and Node.js.
- It has GPIO pins that allow it to interface with sensors, actuators and other components in IoT projects.
- Raspberry Pi can be used for a wide range of IoT applications such as home automation, robotics, weather stations, media centers, and more.
- It supports various communication protocols such as Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Ethernet, and USB.
- Raspberry Pi has a strong community of developers and enthusiasts who share knowledge and resources through online forums, blogs, and social media.
- There are several models of Raspberry Pi available, each with different specifications and capabilities. Some of the popular models are Raspberry Pi 4, Raspberry Pi 3, and Raspberry Pi Zero.

In conclusion, Raspberry Pi is a powerful and versatile platform for IoT development that offers a low-cost, easy-to-use solution for building innovative projects. Its popularity and community support make it an ideal choice for beginners and experts alike.
