
### Actuators for the Notes of Unit 2 - Hardware for IoT in the Subject of Internet of Things

* Actuators are devices used to convert energy into motion. They are used to control the motion of mechanical systems and are used in a wide range of applications, from robotics to industrial automation.
* Actuators can be divided into two main types: linear and rotary. Linear actuators move in a straight line, while rotary actuators move in a circular motion.
* Linear actuators are used in applications such as robotics, automotive, and industrial automation. Examples of linear actuators include pneumatic cylinders, hydraulic cylinders, and electric linear actuators.
* Rotary actuators are used in applications such as robotics, automotive, and industrial automation. Examples of rotary actuators include servo motors, stepper motors, and DC motors.
* In the Internet of Things (IoT) context, actuators are used to control the physical environment. For example, an IoT-enabled device can be used to control the temperature of a room, the speed of a fan, or the position of a robotic arm.
