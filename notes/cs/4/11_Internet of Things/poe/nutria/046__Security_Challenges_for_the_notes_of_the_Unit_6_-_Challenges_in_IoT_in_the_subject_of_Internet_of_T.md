
### Security Challenges for the Notes of Unit 6 - Challenges in IoT

1. Lack of Security Standards: The Internet of Things is a relatively new technology, and as such, there are no established security standards. This means that devices are often vulnerable to attack, as they lack the necessary security measures.

2. Weak Authentication: Many IoT devices use weak authentication methods, such as passwords or simple PINs. These methods are easy to guess and can be easily compromised.

3. Unpatched Software: Many IoT devices run outdated software, which can contain vulnerabilities that can be exploited by attackers.

4. Poorly Secured Network Connections: Many IoT devices are connected to networks that are not properly secured, making them vulnerable to attack.

5. Unencrypted Data: Many IoT devices transmit data in an unencrypted format, making it easy for attackers to intercept and modify.

6. Lack of Accountability: IoT devices are often connected to multiple networks, making it difficult to determine who is responsible for any security breaches.
