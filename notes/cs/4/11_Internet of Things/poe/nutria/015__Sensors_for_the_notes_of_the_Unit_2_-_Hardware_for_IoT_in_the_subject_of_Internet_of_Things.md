
### Sensors for the Notes of Unit 2 - Hardware for IoT in the Subject of Internet of Things

* Sensors are a key component of any Internet of Things (IoT) system. They are responsible for collecting data from the environment and providing feedback to the user.
* There are many different types of sensors available, including temperature, humidity, light, motion, pressure, and more. Each type of sensor has its own unique capabilities and limitations.
* It is important to select the right type of sensor for the application. Factors to consider include accuracy, power consumption, cost, and environmental conditions.
* Sensors can be connected to the IoT system either directly or through a gateway. Direct connections are often more reliable, but require more power and may be more expensive.
* Sensor data can be used to trigger events, such as sending an alert when a temperature threshold is exceeded. It can also be used to control devices, such as turning on a light when motion is detected.
* Sensor networks can be used to collect data from multiple sources and provide a more comprehensive view of the environment. This can be used to improve decision-making and automate processes.
