
### Intel Galileo Boards

Intel Galileo boards are embedded platforms for the Internet of Things (IoT). They are designed to provide a wide range of features, including:

* High-performance, low-power processor based on the Intel Quark family of microcontrollers
* Support for a variety of operating systems, including Linux and Windows
* Support for multiple communication protocols, such as Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and Ethernet
* Support for a wide range of sensors and actuators
* Support for programming and debugging tools
* Support for real-time operating systems (RTOS)
* Ability to connect to the cloud for data storage and analysis
