
### Overview of IOT Supported Hardware Platforms

This section provides an overview of IOT supported hardware platforms, such as Arduino, for the notes of Unit 3 - Embedded Platforms for IoT in the subject of Internet of Things.

* Arduino is an open source hardware platform for creating interactive objects that can sense and control physical devices. It is based on a microcontroller board and a development environment that is used to write and upload code to the board.

* Arduino can be used to create a wide range of projects, from simple LED displays to complex robots. It can be used for home automation, data logging, and more.

* Arduino is compatible with a variety of different components, such as sensors, motors, and displays. It is also compatible with a variety of programming languages, such as C/C++, Java, and Python.

* Arduino can be used to create a variety of Internet of Things (IoT) applications, such as home automation, data logging, and more. It is also used to create projects that are connected to the Internet, such as web servers and web-enabled devices.

* Arduino is a popular choice for hobbyists and makers, as it is easy to use and has a wide range of support and tutorials available online.
