
## Unit 4 - Network & Communication aspects in IoT

* Network & Communication aspects in IoT are essential for the successful implementation of IoT applications.
* Communication protocols such as MQTT, CoAP, AMQP, and XMPP are used to enable communication between IoT devices.
* Networking technologies such as Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, ZigBee, and LoRa are used to enable communication between different IoT devices.
* Security protocols such as TLS/SSL, IPSec, and SSH are used to protect the integrity of IoT data.
* Network topologies such as star, mesh, and hybrid are used to establish communication between IoT devices.
* Networking standards such as IEEE 802.15.4, IEEE 802.11, and IEEE 802.3 are used to ensure interoperability between different IoT devices.
