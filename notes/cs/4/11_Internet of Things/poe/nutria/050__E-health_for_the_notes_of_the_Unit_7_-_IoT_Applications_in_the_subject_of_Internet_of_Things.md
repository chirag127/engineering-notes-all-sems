
### E-health for the notes of the Unit 7 - IoT Applications in the subject of Internet of Things

1. E-health is the use of technology to enable access to healthcare information and services.
2. E-health technologies can include telehealth, telemedicine, and virtual health.
3. Telehealth is the use of technology to enable remote healthcare, such as video conferencing.
4. Telemedicine is the use of technology to enable remote diagnosis and treatment of medical conditions.
5. Virtual health is the use of technology to enable remote monitoring and management of health data.
6. The Internet of Things (IoT) is a network of devices, sensors, and other connected objects that can communicate with each other and exchange data.
7. IoT is being used to enable the development of e-health solutions, such as remote patient monitoring, automated drug delivery, and wearable medical devices.
8. E-health solutions enabled by IoT can help improve the quality of healthcare and reduce costs.
