
### IoT/M2M Systems Layers and Design Standardization

1. The Internet of Things (IoT) is a network of physical objects, such as devices, sensors, and vehicles, that are connected to the internet and can communicate with each other.

2. Machine-to-Machine (M2M) systems are used to connect machines and devices to each other, allowing them to communicate and exchange data.

3. IoT and M2M systems are based on a layered architecture, which consists of five layers: the physical layer, the network layer, the data layer, the application layer, and the service layer.

4. The physical layer is the hardware of the system, such as the sensors, devices, and communication networks. 

5. The network layer is responsible for communication between the physical devices and the data layer.

6. The data layer is responsible for storing, processing, and analyzing the data collected from the physical layer.

7. The application layer is responsible for providing applications and services that can be used to interact with the system.

8. The service layer is responsible for providing the necessary infrastructure and services to enable the applications to interact with the system.

9. Design standardization is the process of creating a standard set of rules and processes for the design and development of IoT and M2M systems. This ensures that the system is built to the same standards and specifications, and that it is compatible with other systems.
