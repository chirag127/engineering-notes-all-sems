
### Wireless Medium Access Issues for the Notes of the Unit 4 - Network & Communication Aspects in IoT in the Subject of Internet of Things

1. Wireless medium access protocols are used to control how multiple devices access a shared wireless medium.
2. The two most common medium access protocols are Carrier Sense Multiple Access with Collision Detection (CSMA/CD) and Carrier Sense Multiple Access with Collision Avoidance (CSMA/CA).
3. CSMA/CD is used in Ethernet networks and is based on the concept of collisions. Devices that detect a collision on the medium will stop transmitting and wait a random amount of time before attempting to retransmit.
4. CSMA/CA is used in wireless networks and is based on the concept of contention. Devices that detect that the medium is busy will wait a random amount of time before attempting to transmit.
5. Wireless networks use multiple techniques to reduce the chances of collisions and contention, such as spread spectrum technologies, frequency hopping, and power control.
6. Wireless networks also use multiple techniques to improve the quality of the signal, such as antenna diversity, beamforming, and frequency reuse.
