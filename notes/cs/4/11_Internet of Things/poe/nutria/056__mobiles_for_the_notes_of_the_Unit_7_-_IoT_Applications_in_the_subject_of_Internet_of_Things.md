

### Mobiles for the Notes of the Unit 7 - IoT Applications in the Subject of Internet of Things

1. Mobile devices are increasingly used as a gateway to the Internet of Things (IoT).
2. Mobile devices are equipped with a variety of sensors and communication technologies that can be used to interact with the physical world.
3. Mobile devices are able to access a variety of services and data sources to provide a more contextualized user experience.
4. Mobile applications can use the data gathered from the device's sensors to trigger automated processes in the physical world.
5. Mobile devices can also be used to control and manage connected devices in the IoT.
6. Mobile devices can be used to monitor and analyze data from connected devices in the IoT.
7. Mobile devices can be used to securely access and interact with cloud-based services and applications.
