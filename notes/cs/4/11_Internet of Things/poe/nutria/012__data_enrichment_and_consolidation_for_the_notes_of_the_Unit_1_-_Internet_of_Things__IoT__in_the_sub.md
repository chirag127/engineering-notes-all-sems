
### Data Enrichment and Consolidation for the Notes of the Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT) in the Subject of Internet of Things

1. Data enrichment is the process of gathering more data and adding it to existing data sets. This can be done manually or with the help of automation and machine learning algorithms.

2. Data consolidation is the process of combining multiple data sets into one unified data set. This can help to improve the accuracy and completeness of the data and make it easier to analyze.

3. The Internet of Things (IoT) is a network of physical objects, such as sensors and devices, that are connected to the internet and can communicate with each other and with other networks.

4. IoT data can be used to enrich existing data sets and provide valuable insights into how the physical world works.

5. IoT data can also be used to consolidate multiple data sets into one unified data set. This can help to improve the accuracy and completeness of the data and make it easier to analyze.
