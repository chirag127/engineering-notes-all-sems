
## Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT)

1. The Internet of Things (IoT) is a term used to describe the connection of physical objects to the internet.

2. IoT devices are able to collect and share data with other devices, enabling them to interact with each other and with humans in real-time.

3. IoT devices can be used in a variety of applications, including home automation, healthcare, smart cities, and industrial automation.

4. IoT devices are typically connected to the internet via Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, or other wireless technologies.

5. IoT devices are often equipped with sensors that can detect and measure environmental conditions such as temperature, humidity, and light.

6. IoT devices are often connected to cloud-based platforms that enable data storage, analysis, and visualization.

7. Security is a major concern when it comes to IoT, as the devices can be vulnerable to hacking and other malicious activities.

8. The development of IoT technology is currently in its early stages, and there is potential for further expansion in the future.
