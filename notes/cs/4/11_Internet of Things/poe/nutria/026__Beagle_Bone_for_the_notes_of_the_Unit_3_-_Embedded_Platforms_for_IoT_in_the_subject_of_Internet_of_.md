
### Beagle Bone for the Notes of Unit 3 - Embedded Platforms for IoT

1. The BeagleBone is a single board computer designed to be used as an embedded platform for the Internet of Things (IoT). It is based on an ARM Cortex-A8 processor, and runs a version of the Linux operating system.

2. The BeagleBone is designed to be a low-cost, low-power device, making it ideal for use in a variety of applications such as home automation, robotics, and industrial control.

3. It is also capable of running a variety of programming languages, such as Python, JavaScript, and C/C++.

4. The BeagleBone is a powerful platform for developing IoT applications, and can be used to create a wide variety of projects. It is also compatible with a number of open source hardware and software projects, making it easy to integrate with existing systems.
