
### NetArduino for the Notes of Unit 3 - Embedded Platforms for IoT in the Subject of Internet of Things

1. NetArduino is an open source hardware and software platform that enables developers to create Internet of Things (IoT) devices.
2. It combines a 32-bit ARM Cortex-M3 processor with built-in Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and Ethernet connectivity.
3. It also features a range of additional hardware features, including an accelerometer, gyroscope, temperature sensor, and a range of digital and analog ports.
4. The software platform is based on the Arduino programming language and development environment, which makes it easy to create and deploy IoT applications.
5. NetArduino also supports a range of third-party libraries and frameworks, such as Node.js, which makes it easy to connect to other IoT devices and services.
6. Additionally, NetArduino is designed to be highly secure, with built-in encryption and authentication protocols that protect against malicious attacks.
