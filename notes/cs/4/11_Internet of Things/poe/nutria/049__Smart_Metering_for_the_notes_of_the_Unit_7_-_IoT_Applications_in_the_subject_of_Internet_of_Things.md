
### Smart Metering for the Notes of Unit 7 - IoT Applications in the Subject of Internet of Things

- Smart metering is a technology used to measure and monitor the consumption of energy, water, or gas in a home or business.
- Smart meters provide real-time data on energy consumption, allowing users to better manage their usage and reduce costs.
- Smart meters are connected to the internet, allowing for remote monitoring and control of energy usage.
- Smart meters can be used to detect energy-saving opportunities, such as identifying energy-inefficient appliances or energy-saving settings.
- Smart meters are becoming more common in homes and businesses, as they offer greater control over energy usage and can help reduce energy costs.
- Smart meters can also be used to detect energy theft or tampering, as the data can be monitored remotely.
- Smart meters can be used to provide real-time energy usage data to energy providers, allowing for more accurate billing and improved customer service.
