
### M2M Communication for the Notes of the Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT)

* Machine-to-machine (M2M) communication is the process of connecting two or more machines to enable them to share information and data.
* M2M communication is used in a variety of contexts, including industrial automation, smart homes, healthcare, and transportation.
* M2M communication is enabled by the Internet of Things (IoT), which is a network of physical objects (devices) connected to the internet and equipped with sensors, software, and other technologies that enable them to collect and exchange data.
* M2M communication is based on the concept of edge computing, which is the process of collecting and processing data at the edge of the network, rather than in the cloud.
* M2M communication is used to monitor and control devices in the IoT, as well as to enable remote access and control.
* M2M communication is also used to enable predictive maintenance, which helps to reduce downtime and improve efficiency.
