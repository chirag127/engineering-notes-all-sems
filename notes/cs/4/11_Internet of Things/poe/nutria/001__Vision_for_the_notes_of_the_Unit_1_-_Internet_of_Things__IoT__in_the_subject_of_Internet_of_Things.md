
### Vision for the Notes of the Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT)

1. The Internet of Things (IoT) is the network of physical objects that are embedded with sensors, software, and other technologies for the purpose of connecting and exchanging data with other devices and systems over the internet.

2. IoT enables the connection of physical objects to the digital world, allowing them to interact with each other, as well as with humans, in order to collect and exchange data.

3. IoT devices can be used to monitor and control various aspects of the environment, such as temperature, humidity, and lighting, as well as to monitor the performance of machines and other equipment.

4. IoT applications can be used to automate processes, such as in smart homes, smart factories, and smart cities.

5. IoT can also be used to improve the efficiency of businesses, by providing real-time data and analytics.

6. IoT security is a major concern, as the devices are often vulnerable to cyber-attacks. Therefore, it is important to ensure that the devices are secure and that all data is protected.
