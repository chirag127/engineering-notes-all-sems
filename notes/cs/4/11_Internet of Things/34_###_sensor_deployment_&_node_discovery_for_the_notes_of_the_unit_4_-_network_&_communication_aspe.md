### Sensor deployment & Node discovery for the notes of the Unit 4 - Network & Communication aspects in IoT in the subject of Internet of Things

Sensor deployment and node discovery are important aspects of Internet of Things (IoT) networks and communication.

Sensor deployment refers to the process of installing and configuring sensors in an IoT network. This process involves determining the optimal location for sensors, ensuring that sensors are properly connected to the network, and configuring sensors to collect and transmit data.

Node discovery refers to the process of locating and identifying nodes (devices) in an IoT network. This process is important for enabling communication between nodes and for managing the network as a whole. Node discovery can be performed using various protocols, such as Zigbee, Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE), and Wi-Fi Direct.

In conclusion, sensor deployment and node discovery are important aspects of IoT networks and communication. Sensor deployment involves installing and configuring sensors in an IoT network, while node discovery involves locating and identifying nodes in the network. Effective sensor deployment and node discovery are essential for ensuring the functionality and performance of IoT networks.
