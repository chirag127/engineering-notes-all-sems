### Data aggregation & dissemination for the notes of the Unit 4 - Network & Communication aspects in IoT in the subject of Internet of Things

Data aggregation refers to the process of collecting data from multiple sources and combining it into a single, unified view. In IoT, data from various devices is aggregated to provide a comprehensive view of the network and its components. This data can be used to monitor performance, detect anomalies, and make decisions.

Dissemination refers to the distribution of the aggregated data to relevant stakeholders. In IoT, this can include sending data to cloud-based servers for analysis, or directly to end-users for display on mobile devices. The goal of dissemination is to make the data accessible and usable for decision making.

In the context of Network & Communication aspects in IoT, data aggregation and dissemination play a crucial role in ensuring the efficient and effective functioning of the network. By aggregating data from various sources, network administrators can gain a comprehensive understanding of network performance and identify areas for improvement. Disseminating this data to relevant stakeholders enables them to make informed decisions and take appropriate actions to optimize the network.
