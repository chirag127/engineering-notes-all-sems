### Sensors for the notes of the Unit 2 - Hardware for IoT in the subject of Internet of Things

Sensors are devices used to detect and measure physical phenomena, such as temperature, light, motion, and pressure. In the context of the Internet of Things (IoT), sensors are used to collect data from the physical world and transmit it to the IoT network for further processing and analysis.

There are many types of sensors used in IoT, including:

1. Temperature sensors: These measure temperature and are used in applications such as climate control and temperature monitoring.

2. Light sensors: These measure light levels and are used in applications such as lighting control and ambient light sensing.

3. Motion sensors: These detect motion and are used in applications such as security systems and activity tracking.

4. Pressure sensors: These measure pressure and are used in applications such as weather monitoring and tire pressure monitoring.

5. Environmental sensors: These measure various environmental parameters, such as humidity, air quality, and atmospheric pressure, and are used in applications such as weather monitoring and air quality control.

Sensors play a critical role in IoT by collecting data from the physical world and transmitting it to the IoT network. This data can then be used to control devices, monitor conditions, and make decisions based on the data collected.

In conclusion, sensors are devices used to detect and measure physical phenomena, such as temperature, light, motion, and pressure. In the context of IoT, sensors are used to collect data from the physical world and transmit it to the IoT network for further processing and analysis. There are many types of sensors used in IoT, including temperature sensors, light sensors, motion sensors, pressure sensors, and environmental sensors.
