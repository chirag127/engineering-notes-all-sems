### E-health for the notes of the Unit 7 - IoT Applications in the subject of Internet of Things

E-health refers to the use of digital technologies to improve health and healthcare. It encompasses a wide range of applications, including telemedicine, mHealth, electronic health records, and wearable devices for monitoring health and wellness. The goal of e-health is to improve access to quality healthcare, reduce costs, and enhance patient outcomes. IoT plays a crucial role in e-health by enabling the creation of connected health systems that can collect, process, and analyze data from various sources. This data can be used to improve decision making, monitor patient health, and provide remote care. Some examples of IoT applications in e-health include wearable devices for monitoring vital signs, smart pills for tracking medication adherence, and connected medical devices for remote patient monitoring. Overall, e-health has the potential to revolutionize healthcare by improving outcomes, reducing costs, and increasing access to quality care.
