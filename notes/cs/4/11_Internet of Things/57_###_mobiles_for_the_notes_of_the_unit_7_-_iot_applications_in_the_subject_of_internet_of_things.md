### mobiles for the notes of the Unit 7 - IoT Applications in the subject of Internet of Things

Mobiles play a crucial role in IoT applications. They provide a convenient interface for accessing and controlling IoT devices and services. Mobile applications can be used to:
1. Monitor and control smart home devices such as lights, temperature, security systems, etc.
2. Track fitness and health using wearable devices and mobile apps.
3. Manage and control industrial processes remotely.
4. Monitor and control energy usage in smart homes and buildings.
5. Connect and control connected vehicles and transportation systems.
6. Access and control smart city services such as traffic management, waste management, etc.
7. Provide real-time monitoring and control of various IoT devices and systems.
