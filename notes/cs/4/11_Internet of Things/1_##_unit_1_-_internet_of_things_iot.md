## Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT)

Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT) covers the following topics:
1. Definition and history of IoT
2. IoT architecture and components
3. IoT network protocols and technologies
4. IoT devices and sensors
5. IoT data management and analytics
6. IoT applications and use cases
7. IoT security and privacy

The unit provides an overview of the Internet of Things (IoT), including its definition, history, architecture, components, network protocols, devices, data management, applications, security, and privacy. The goal of the unit is to provide students with a comprehensive understanding of IoT, including its key concepts, technologies, and applications, and to prepare them to design, develop, and implement IoT solutions.
