### IoT Examples for the notes of the Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT) in the subject of Internet of Things

IoT Examples:
1. Smart Home: Automation of lights, temperature, security systems, etc.
2. Wearables: Fitness trackers, smartwatches, etc.
3. Smart Healthcare: Remote patient monitoring, wearable medical devices, etc.
4. Industrial IoT: Predictive maintenance, supply chain optimization, etc.
5. Smart Agriculture: Precision farming, livestock monitoring, etc.
6. Connected Cars: Telematics, infotainment, autonomous driving, etc.
7. Smart Energy: Smart grid, renewable energy management, etc.
