### Other challenges for the notes of the Unit 6 - Challenges in IoT in the subject of Internet of Things

1. Interoperability: Devices from different manufacturers may not be able to communicate with each other.
2. Security: IoT devices are vulnerable to hacking and cyber attacks.
3. Privacy: Personal data is at risk of being collected and used without consent.
4. Scalability: Managing a large number of connected devices can be challenging.
5. Reliability: IoT devices may fail or malfunction, causing disruption.
6. Power consumption: IoT devices often have limited battery life and require frequent charging.
7. Network congestion: A large number of connected devices may overload the network.
