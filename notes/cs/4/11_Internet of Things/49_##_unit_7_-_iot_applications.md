## Unit 7 - IoT Applications

IoT (Internet of Things) Applications refer to the use of connected devices to collect, process, and exchange data. These devices are embedded with sensors, software, and connectivity, enabling them to interact with the physical world and each other.

-Smart homes: Automation of lighting, heating, cooling, and security systems.
-Industrial IoT: Monitoring and control of industrial processes, predictive maintenance, and process optimization.
-Healthcare: Wearable devices for monitoring vital signs, telemedicine, and remote patient monitoring.
-Agriculture: Precision farming, soil and crop monitoring, and livestock tracking.
-Transportation: Connected vehicles, traffic management, and supply chain optimization.
-Smart cities: Energy management, waste management, and public safety.

These are some of the key areas where IoT applications are being used to improve efficiency, reduce costs, and enhance the quality of life.
