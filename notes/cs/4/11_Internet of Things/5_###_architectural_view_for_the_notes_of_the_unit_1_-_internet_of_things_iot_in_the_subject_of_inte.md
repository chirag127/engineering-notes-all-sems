### Architectural view for the notes of the Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT) in the subject of Internet of Things

The architectural view of Internet of Things (IoT) refers to the overall structure and design of the system, including the components, relationships, and interactions between these components.

The main components of an IoT architecture are:

1. Devices: The physical devices that make up the IoT, such as sensors, actuators, and embedded systems.

2. Network: The communication infrastructure that connects the devices, including wired and wireless networks.

3. Platform: The software and hardware infrastructure that provides the necessary support for the IoT devices, such as device management and data storage.

4. Applications: The software applications that use the data generated by the IoT devices to provide value to the end-users.

The relationships between these components are critical to the success of an IoT architecture. The devices must be able to communicate with the network, the network must be able to support the devices, and the platform must be able to manage the devices and provide the necessary support for the applications.

In conclusion, the architectural view of IoT refers to the overall structure and design of the system, including the components, relationships, and interactions between these components. The main components of an IoT architecture are devices, network, platform, and applications, and the relationships between these components are critical to the success of an IoT architecture.
