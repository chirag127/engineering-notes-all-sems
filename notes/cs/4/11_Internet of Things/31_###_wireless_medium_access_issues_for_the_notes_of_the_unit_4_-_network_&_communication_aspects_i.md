### Wireless Medium access issues for the notes of the Unit 4 - Network & Communication aspects in IoT in the subject of Internet of Things

Wireless Medium Access Issues refer to the challenges in managing access to the wireless communication channel in IoT networks. The main challenges in managing access to the wireless medium in IoT networks include:

1. Interference: Interference from other devices can disrupt communication and reduce the reliability of the network.

2. Limited Bandwidth: IoT networks often have limited bandwidth, making it difficult to support a large number of devices.

3. Energy Efficiency: IoT devices are often battery-powered and must conserve energy to maintain a long battery life.

4. Reliability: IoT networks must be reliable to ensure that critical applications, such as medical devices and industrial control systems, can operate effectively.

To address these challenges, various Wireless Medium Access Control (MAC) protocols have been developed, including:

1. Carrier Sense Multiple Access (CSMA): This protocol allows devices to sense the presence of other devices before transmitting, reducing the likelihood of interference.

2. Time Division Multiple Access (TDMA): This protocol divides the communication channel into time slots, allowing each device to transmit in its own time slot, reducing the likelihood of interference.

3. Duty-Cycled MAC: This protocol reduces the energy consumption of IoT devices by allowing them to enter a low-power mode when not transmitting.

In conclusion, Wireless Medium Access Issues refer to the challenges in managing access to the wireless communication channel in IoT networks. These challenges include interference, limited bandwidth, energy efficiency, and reliability. To address these challenges, various Wireless Medium Access Control (MAC) protocols have been developed, including Carrier Sense Multiple Access (CSMA), Time Division Multiple Access (TDMA), and Duty-Cycled MAC.
