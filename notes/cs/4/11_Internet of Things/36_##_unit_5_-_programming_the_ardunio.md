## Unit 5 - Programming the Ardunio

Unit 5 - Programming the Arduino covers the following topics:
1. Introduction to the Arduino platform and its components, including the Arduino board, sensors, and actuators.
2. Overview of the Arduino Integrated Development Environment (IDE) and how to use it to write, upload, and debug Arduino programs.
3. Basic programming concepts, including data types, variables, control structures, functions, and arrays.
4. Interfacing with sensors and actuators, including reading data from sensors, controlling actuators, and using analog and digital inputs and outputs.
5. Advanced programming concepts, such as event-driven programming, serial communication, and data storage.

The unit provides a comprehensive introduction to programming the Arduino platform, including the Arduino board, sensors, and actuators. It covers the basics of programming, including data types, variables, control structures, functions, and arrays, as well as more advanced concepts such as event-driven programming, serial communication, and data storage. The goal of the unit is to provide students with the knowledge and skills needed to build their own Arduino-based projects, including simple robots and Internet of Things (IoT) devices.
