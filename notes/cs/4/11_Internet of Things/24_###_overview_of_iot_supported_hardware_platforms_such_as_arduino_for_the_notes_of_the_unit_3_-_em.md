### Overview of IOT supported Hardware platforms such as Arduino for the notes of the Unit 3 - Embedded Platforms for IoT in the subject of Internet of Things

The Internet of Things (IoT) is a network of physical devices, vehicles, homes, and other items embedded with electronics, software, sensors, and network connectivity, enabling these objects to collect and exchange data. To support the development of IoT applications, various hardware platforms have been developed, including the Arduino platform.

Arduino is an open-source platform for building electronics projects. It consists of a microcontroller board and a software development environment that allows users to write and upload code to the board. Arduino is widely used in IoT projects due to its simplicity, affordability, and versatility.

Advantages of the Arduino platform for IoT include:

1. Easy to use: Arduino is designed to be user-friendly, with a simple programming language and a large community of developers who share their projects and knowledge.

2. Affordable: Arduino boards are relatively inexpensive, making it accessible for hobbyists and students to experiment with IoT projects.

3. Versatile: Arduino boards can be used to control a wide range of devices, including sensors, actuators, and displays, making it a popular choice for IoT projects.

4. Open-source: Arduino is an open-source platform, which means that users have access to the source code and can modify it to suit their needs.

Disadvantages of the Arduino platform for IoT include:

1. Limited processing power: Arduino boards have limited processing power and memory, which can limit their ability to handle complex IoT applications.

2. Security concerns: Arduino boards do not have built-in security features, making them vulnerable to hacking and other security threats.

In conclusion, the Arduino platform is a popular choice for IoT projects due to its simplicity, affordability, and versatility. Arduino consists of a microcontroller board and a software development environment that allows users to write and upload code to the board. While the Arduino platform has advantages such as being easy to use and versatile, it also has limitations such as limited processing power and security concerns.
