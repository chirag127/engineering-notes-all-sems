Mnemonics and Learning Tricks:

- Remember the four fundamental properties of distributed systems using the acronym ACID. ACID stands for Atomicity, Consistency, Isolation, and Durability.
- To remember the difference between tightly coupled and loosely coupled systems, think of a tightly coiled spring (tightly coupled) and a loosely coiled spring (loosely coupled).
- To remember the difference between synchronous and asynchronous communication, think of synchronous communication as a phone call (both parties are present and talking in real-time), and asynchronous communication as sending a text message (the recipient can respond at their convenience).
