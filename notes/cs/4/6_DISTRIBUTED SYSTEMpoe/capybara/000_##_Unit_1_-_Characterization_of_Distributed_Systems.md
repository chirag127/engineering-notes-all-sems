## Unit 1 - Characterization of Distributed Systems

Distributed systems are a set of computers that work together and coordinate their actions to appear as a single coherent system. In this unit, we will learn about the different characteristics of distributed systems that make them unique.

### 1.1 Scalability
Scalability is the ability of a distributed system to handle increasing workloads by adding more resources. It is an essential characteristic of distributed systems and is achieved through horizontal and vertical scaling.

### 1.2 Transparency
Transparency is the ability of a distributed system to hide its internal workings from the user. There are different types of transparency, including:
- Access transparency: allows users to access remote resources as if they were local.
- Location transparency: hides the location of resources from users.
- Concurrency transparency: allows users to access shared resources without interference.
- Failure transparency: hides the effect of failures from users.

### 1.3 Availability
Availability refers to the ability of a distributed system to provide its services continuously, even in the presence of failures. It is achieved through redundancy and fault tolerance mechanisms.

### 1.4 Reliability
Reliability refers to the ability of a distributed system to perform its functions correctly, even in the presence of failures. It is achieved through error detection and recovery mechanisms.

### 1.5 Consistency
Consistency refers to the property that all nodes in a distributed system see the same data at the same time. It is achieved through synchronization mechanisms and protocols.

### 1.6 Security
Security refers to the ability of a distributed system to protect its resources from unauthorized access, modification, or destruction. It is achieved through authentication, authorization, and encryption mechanisms.

### 1.7 Learning Tricks
To remember the different characteristics of distributed systems, you can use the mnemonic "STRACCS." Each letter corresponds to one of the characteristics:
- S: Scalability
- T: Transparency
- R: Reliability
- A: Availability
- C: Consistency
- C: Concurrency
- S: Security

Overall, understanding the different characteristics of distributed systems is essential for designing and implementing reliable, scalable, and secure distributed applications.
