### Information Retrieval for the notes of the Unit 5 - Applications in the subject of Artificial Intelligence
Information Retrieval (IR) is the process of finding relevant information from a large collection of data. IR systems are used for various applications such as web search, document retrieval, and question answering. IR techniques include indexing, querying, and ranking. Indexing involves preprocessing data to create an index that makes searching faster. Querying involves searching the index for relevant data based on user-specified criteria. Ranking involves scoring and ordering the results based on relevance. IR algorithms such as Boolean Model, Vector Space Model, and Probabilistic Model are used to rank results. IR systems can be evaluated based on precision, recall, and F1-score.
