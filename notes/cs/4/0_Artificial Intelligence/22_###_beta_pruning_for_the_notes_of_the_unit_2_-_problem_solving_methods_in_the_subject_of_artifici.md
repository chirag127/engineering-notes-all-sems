### Beta Pruning for the notes of the Unit 2 - Problem Solving Methods in the subject of Artificial Intelligence
Beta pruning is a search algorithm optimization technique used in Artificial Intelligence to reduce the number of nodes that need to be evaluated in a minimax tree. It operates by keeping track of a lower and upper bound on the minimax value of each node and pruning branches that cannot possibly contain the optimal solution. The lower bound is the minimum possible value that a node can have, while the upper bound is the maximum possible value. If the lower bound is greater than or equal to the upper bound of the parent node, the branch can be pruned. This optimization significantly reduces the number of nodes that need to be evaluated and improves the efficiency of the algorithm.
