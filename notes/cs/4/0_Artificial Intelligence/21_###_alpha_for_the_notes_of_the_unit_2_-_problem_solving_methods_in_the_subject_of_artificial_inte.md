### Alpha for the notes of the Unit 2 - Problem Solving Methods in the subject of Artificial Intelligence
Alpha:
- Alpha is a type of artificial intelligence algorithm used in reinforcement learning.
- It is designed to learn from experience and improve its decision-making over time.
- Alpha uses a deep neural network to process information and make predictions.
- AlphaGo, a famous example, used Alpha to beat the world champion in the game of Go.
- Alpha is also used in various other applications, including game playing, recommendation systems, and autonomous vehicles.
- Alpha algorithms require a large amount of data and computing power to train effectively.
