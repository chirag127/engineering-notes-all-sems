### Game Playing for the notes of the Unit 2 - Problem Solving Methods in the subject of Artificial Intelligence
Game playing is a subfield of artificial intelligence concerned with creating computer programs that can play games. The goal of game playing AI is to develop algorithms and techniques that enable computers to make decisions and take actions in games, such as chess, checkers, and tic-tac-toe.

There are two main approaches to game playing AI: rule-based systems and decision-making systems. Rule-based systems rely on a set of pre-defined rules to determine the best move, while decision-making systems use techniques such as search algorithms, decision trees, and Monte Carlo tree search to evaluate different moves and choose the best one.

The most important factor in game playing AI is the evaluation function, which assigns a score to each possible move based on its potential impact on the game. This score is used to guide the search for the best move.

Another important aspect of game playing AI is the ability to learn from experience. Reinforcement learning algorithms allow the AI to learn from past games and improve its performance over time.

Overall, game playing AI is a challenging and exciting area of research that combines elements of computer science, mathematics, and psychology. It has important applications in fields such as education, entertainment, and military simulation.
