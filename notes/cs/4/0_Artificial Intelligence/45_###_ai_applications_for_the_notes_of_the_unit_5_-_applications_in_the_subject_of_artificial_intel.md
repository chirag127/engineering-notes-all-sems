### AI applications for the notes of the Unit 5 - Applications in the subject of Artificial Intelligence
AI applications can be broadly categorized into: 
1. Natural Language Processing (NLP): for language-based tasks such as sentiment analysis, language translation, and text classification.
2. Computer Vision: for image and video analysis tasks such as object detection, image classification, and facial recognition.
3. Robotics: for tasks such as autonomous navigation, object manipulation, and decision making in dynamic environments.
4. Recommender Systems: for personalized product or content recommendations based on user behavior data.
5. Predictive Analytics: for tasks such as predictive maintenance, fraud detection, and stock market predictions.
6. Gaming: for tasks such as game AI, game design, and game development.
7. Healthcare: for tasks such as medical diagnosis, drug discovery, and personalized treatment plans.
