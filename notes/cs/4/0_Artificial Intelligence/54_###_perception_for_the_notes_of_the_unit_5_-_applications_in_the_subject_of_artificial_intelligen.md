### Perception for the notes of the Unit 5 - Applications in the subject of Artificial Intelligence
Perception refers to the process of acquiring, interpreting, selecting, and organizing sensory information to form a meaningful and coherent experience of the world. It involves receiving data from the environment through sensory input (e.g. vision, hearing, touch), and using that information to build a mental representation of the world. In AI, perception is used in various applications, such as:
1. Computer Vision: to analyze and understand visual information from images and videos.
2. Robotics: for object recognition, motion detection, and environment mapping.
3. Virtual Reality: to create immersive experiences by perceiving and responding to user's actions.
4. Autonomous Systems: for perception-based decision making, such as self-driving cars.
