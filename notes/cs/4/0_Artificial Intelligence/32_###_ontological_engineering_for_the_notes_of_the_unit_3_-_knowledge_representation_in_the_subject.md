### Ontological Engineering for the notes of the Unit 3 - Knowledge Representation in the subject of Artificial Intelligence
Ontological Engineering is the process of creating, maintaining and evolving ontologies, which are formal representations of a shared understanding of a domain. It involves defining concepts and relationships among them, creating a hierarchy of classes and properties, and specifying constraints and axioms. The goal of ontological engineering is to create a common understanding of a domain, which can be used for knowledge sharing and reuse, and for building intelligent systems. It is a crucial aspect of knowledge representation in Artificial Intelligence and helps in creating a shared understanding of the world for AI systems to operate on.
