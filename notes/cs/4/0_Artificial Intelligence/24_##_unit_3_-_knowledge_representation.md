## Unit 3 - Knowledge Representation
Knowledge representation is the process of encoding information in a way that a computer system can understand and manipulate. Unit 3 focuses on the study of methods and techniques for representing knowledge in a structured and symbolic form, making it accessible to computer systems. This includes topics such as ontologies, semantic networks, frames, rules, and logic. The goal is to enable computers to perform tasks such as reasoning, problem-solving, and decision-making based on this knowledge. The unit also covers the use of knowledge representation in artificial intelligence and the semantic web.
