### Forward Chaining for the notes of the Unit 3 - Knowledge Representation in the subject of Artificial Intelligence
Forward Chaining is a reasoning method in Artificial Intelligence that starts with known facts and applies inference rules to derive new conclusions. It is also known as data-driven reasoning or bottom-up reasoning. In Forward Chaining, the system starts with a set of facts and applies production rules to deduce new facts until a goal is reached or no more deductions can be made. The process continues until a conclusion is reached or the system determines that no further deductions can be made. It is used in expert systems and decision support systems. Forward Chaining is suitable for problems with many rules and few facts, and it is efficient in finding all possible solutions. However, it may not terminate if there are cyclic rules or if the knowledge base is incomplete.
