### Problem solving Methods for the notes of the Unit 2 - Problem Solving Methods in the subject of Artificial Intelligence
1. Brute Force: Try all possible solutions to find the best one.
2. Heuristics: Use an educated guess to find a good solution quickly.
3. Divide and Conquer: Break down the problem into smaller subproblems and solve each one.
4. Dynamic Programming: Store and reuse solutions to subproblems to solve the main problem.
5. Greedy Algorithms: Make the locally optimal choice at each step to find a global solution.
6. Backtracking: Try potential solutions and undo them if they don't work.
7. Tree and Graph Search: Explore all possible paths in a tree or graph to find a solution.
8. Evolutionary Algorithms: Use principles of natural selection to find a solution.
