### Unification for the notes of the Unit 3 - Knowledge Representation in the subject of Artificial Intelligence
Unification is a process in AI that combines two expressions into a single, more general expression. It is used in symbolic reasoning and knowledge representation to find a common solution or explanation for two or more expressions. Unification algorithms work by comparing the terms in the expressions and finding a substitution that makes them equivalent. The substitution is then applied to the expressions to produce the unified expression. Unification is a key component of many AI systems, including expert systems, natural language processing, and theorem proving.
