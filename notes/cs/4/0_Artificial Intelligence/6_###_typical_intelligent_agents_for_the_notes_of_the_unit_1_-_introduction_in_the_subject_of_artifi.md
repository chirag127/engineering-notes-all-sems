### Typical Intelligent Agents for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction in the subject of Artificial Intelligence
"Typical Intelligent Agents" is a subtopic within the "Unit 1 - Introduction" in the subject of Artificial Intelligence. It covers the different types of agents that can exhibit intelligent behavior. These agents can be physical entities such as robots, or purely software-based, such as recommendation systems. The unit may also discuss the characteristics of intelligent agents, such as perception, reasoning, and action. The aim of this subtopic is to provide students with a clear understanding of what constitutes an intelligent agent, and how they can be designed and implemented in real-world applications.
