### Definition for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction in the subject of Artificial Intelligence
Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a branch of computer science concerned with creating machines that can perform tasks that typically require human intelligence, such as visual perception, speech recognition, decision-making, and language translation. 

Unit 1 - Introduction:
- Defines AI and its history
- Discusses different types of AI, including narrow AI and general AI
- Explores the potential benefits and challenges of AI 
- Introduces the ethical considerations surrounding AI development and use 

Note: The Unit 1 - Introduction provides a comprehensive overview of Artificial Intelligence, including its definition, history, and various applications. It also highlights the potential benefits and challenges of AI, as well as the ethical considerations that must be taken into account when developing and using AI technology.
