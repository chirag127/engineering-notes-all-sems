### Intrusion Detection Systems for the notes of the Unit 2 - Confidentiality Policies in the subject of COMPUTER SYSTEM SECURITY

Unit 2 of the Computer System Security course focuses on Confidentiality Policies, including the aspect of Intrusion Detection Systems (IDS).

Intrusion Detection Systems (IDS) are security technologies that are designed to detect unauthorized access to computer systems and networks. IDS systems use a variety of techniques to detect potential security threats, including analyzing network traffic, monitoring system logs, and analyzing system behavior.

There are two main types of IDS systems: network-based IDS (NIDS) and host-based IDS (HID). Network-based IDS systems monitor network traffic to detect potential security threats, while host-based IDS systems monitor the behavior of individual systems to detect security threats.

In the context of the Computer System Security course, students in Unit 2 may learn about Intrusion Detection Systems as part of the Confidentiality Policies unit. This may include working with examples of different types of IDS systems, learning about the impact of IDS systems on the confidentiality of sensitive information, and learning strategies for implementing effective IDS systems.

IDS systems are security technologies that are designed to detect unauthorized access to computer systems and networks, and are available in two main types: network-based IDS (NID) and host-based IDS (HID). Students will learn about IDS systems through interactive activities and hands-on experiences, and will learn strategies for implementing effective IDS systems.
