### Introduction for the notes of the Unit 1 - Computer System Security Introduction in the subject of COMPUTER SYSTEM SECURITY

1. Computer System Security is the protection of computer systems and networks from theft, damage, and unauthorized access.
2. It involves implementing measures to prevent, detect, and respond to security threats.
3. Security threats can come from various sources, including hackers, viruses, and malicious software.
4. Computer System Security is important for protecting sensitive information, such as personal data and financial information.
5. It is also important for ensuring the availability and integrity of computer systems and networks.
6. There are various methods and technologies used to secure computer systems, including firewalls, encryption, and access controls.
7. It is important for individuals and organizations to be aware of the risks and to take appropriate measures to protect their computer systems and networks.
