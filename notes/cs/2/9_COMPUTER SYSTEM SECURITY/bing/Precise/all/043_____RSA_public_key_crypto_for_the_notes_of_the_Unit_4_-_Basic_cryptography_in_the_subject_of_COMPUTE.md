### RSA Public Key Crypto

RSA is a public key cryptographic algorithm named after its inventors Ron Rivest, Adi Shamir, and Leonard Adleman. It is widely used for secure data transmission. Here are some key points to remember about RSA public key cryptography:

1. RSA is an asymmetric cryptographic algorithm, meaning it uses two keys: a public key and a private key.
2. The public key is used to encrypt data, while the private key is used to decrypt data.
3. The security of RSA relies on the difficulty of factoring large prime numbers.
4. The keys for RSA are generated by selecting two large prime numbers and computing their product, which is called the modulus.
5. The public key consists of the modulus and a public exponent, while the private key consists of the modulus and a private exponent.
6. The public and private exponents are chosen such that they are inverses of each other modulo the totient of the modulus.
7. RSA can be used for both encryption and digital signatures.
8. RSA is widely used in secure communication protocols such as SSL/TLS and SSH.
