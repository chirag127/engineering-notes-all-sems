## Unit 1 - Computer System Security Introduction

1. **Computer System Security** refers to the protection of computer systems and networks from theft, damage, and unauthorized access.
2. It is important to ensure the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of data and resources.
3. **Threats** to computer system security can come from various sources, including hackers, viruses, and malicious software.
4. **Preventive measures** include the use of firewalls, antivirus software, and strong passwords.
5. **Encryption** is a technique used to protect sensitive data by converting it into an unreadable format.
6. **Access control** is the process of granting or denying access to resources based on a user's identity and authorization level.
7. **Backup and recovery** procedures are important to ensure that data can be restored in the event of a system failure or security breach.
8. **Security policies** and **training** are essential to ensure that employees understand and follow best practices for computer system security.
