### The Marketplace for vulnerabilities

- The marketplace for vulnerabilities refers to the buying and selling of information about security vulnerabilities in software and systems.
- Vulnerability management is an important aspect of maintaining the security of systems and applications. Many companies offer vulnerability management services and tools to help organizations identify and remediate vulnerabilities in their systems.
- Some major companies in the security and vulnerability management market include Strobes Security, IBM, McAfee, Broadcom Inc, and Qualys Inc .
- The global Security and Vulnerability Management Market size is projected to grow from USD 13.8 billion in 2021 to USD 18.7 billion by 2026 .
- The security and vulnerability management market has been growing rapidly in recent years, driven by the increasing frequency and sophistication of cyber attacks .
- Many cloud service providers, such as Amazon Web Services (AWS), offer security solutions and tools through their marketplaces to help customers maintain a secure environment .
- Government agencies, such as the Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA), also maintain catalogs of known exploited vulnerabilities to help organizations stay informed and take appropriate action .
