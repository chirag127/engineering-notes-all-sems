### Email security

Email security is the practice of protecting email accounts and communications from unauthorized access, loss, or compromise. Email is often used to spread malware, spam, and phishing attacks, which can compromise the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of email data and systems. Email security also involves ensuring the authenticity and non-repudiation of email messages, which means verifying the identity of the sender and the integrity of the message content.

Some of the topics related to email security are:

- **Email encryption**: The process of encoding email messages and attachments with cryptographic algorithms to prevent unauthorized access or modification. Email encryption can be done at different levels, such as end-to-end encryption, transport layer encryption, or message-level encryption. Email encryption can protect the privacy and security of email data in transit and at rest.
- **Email authentication**: The process of verifying the identity and legitimacy of the sender and the recipient of an email message. Email authentication can be done with various protocols and mechanisms, such as Sender Policy Framework (SPF), DomainKeys Identified Mail (DKIM), Domain-based Message Authentication, Reporting and Conformance (DMARC), and Secure/Multipurpose Internet Mail Extensions (S/MIME). Email authentication can prevent spoofing, phishing, and impersonation attacks.
- **Email filtering**: The process of screening and sorting incoming and outgoing email messages based on predefined criteria, such as sender, recipient, subject, content, or attachments. Email filtering can be done with various tools and techniques, such as spam filters, antivirus software, content analysis, and machine learning. Email filtering can reduce the volume and impact of unwanted or malicious email messages, such as spam, malware, and phishing.
- **Email archiving**: The process of storing and preserving email messages and attachments for future reference, compliance, or legal purposes. Email archiving can be done with various methods and technologies, such as cloud storage, backup systems, or specialized software. Email archiving can enhance the availability and accessibility of email data, as well as the accountability and auditability of email activities.
