### Summary of weaknesses of internet security

- Internet security is the protection of information systems and networks from cyberattacks, unauthorized access, data theft, and other threats.
- Weaknesses of internet security can compromise the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of data and resources, and expose users to various risks and damages.
- Some of the common weaknesses of internet security are:

  - **Lack of a high-level strategy**: Many businesses, especially new and small ones, simply lack a high-level strategy for their cybersecurity needs. They do not have a clear vision, goals, policies, or standards for securing their systems and data. They also do not allocate sufficient resources, personnel, or training for cybersecurity. This makes them vulnerable to cyberattacks and compliance issues.
  - **Unsecured networks**: If the network is not secured, it is trivially easy for nefarious parties to gain access to the system. Unsecured networks may not have encryption, authentication, firewall, or antivirus software to protect the data in transit and at rest. They may also have open ports, weak passwords, or default settings that can be exploited by hackers .
  - **Unsecured communication channels**: If the business is using unsecured communication channels, such as email, instant messaging, or social media, to share sensitive or confidential information, they are exposing themselves to data breaches, phishing, spoofing, and malware attacks. Unsecured communication channels may not have encryption, digital signatures, or verification mechanisms to ensure the identity and integrity of the sender and receiver .
  - **Unknown bugs**: Unknown bugs are flaws or errors in the software or hardware that can cause unexpected or undesired behavior, such as crashes, freezes, data loss, or security breaches. Unknown bugs may be present in the operating system, applications, web browsers, plugins, or devices that the user is using. They may also be introduced by updates, patches, or new features that are not properly tested or verified .
  - **Outdated systems**: Outdated systems are those that are not updated or maintained regularly to fix known bugs, vulnerabilities, or compatibility issues. Outdated systems may have security holes that can be exploited by hackers, or performance issues that can affect the user experience. Outdated systems may also not support the latest security features, standards, or protocols that are required for optimal protection .
  - **Lack of monitoring**: Lack of monitoring is the failure to track, analyze, or report the activities, events, or incidents that occur on the system or network. Lack of monitoring can prevent the detection, prevention, or response to cyberattacks, anomalies, or threats. Lack of monitoring can also hinder the identification, assessment, or mitigation of risks, vulnerabilities, or impacts .
  - **IoT and multiple connection points**: IoT (Internet of Things) is the network of physical objects, such as devices, sensors, or appliances, that are connected to the internet and can communicate or exchange data. Multiple connection points are the various ways that the user can access the system or network, such as computers, smartphones, tablets, or wireless networks. IoT and multiple connection points can increase the complexity, diversity, and scale of the system or network, and create more entry points for hackers to attack. IoT and multiple connection points can also introduce new or unknown risks, vulnerabilities, or challenges for security .
  - **Untrained employees**: Untrained employees are those who do not have the knowledge, skills, or awareness to follow the best practices, policies, or procedures for cybersecurity. Untrained employees may not know how to protect their passwords, devices, or data, or how to recognize, avoid, or report phishing, malware, or other cyberattacks. Untrained employees may also unintentionally or deliberately violate the security rules, regulations, or standards that apply to their organization, industry, or sector .

- These weaknesses of internet security can have serious consequences for the users, such as:

  - **Data loss or theft**: Data loss or theft is the unauthorized or accidental deletion, alteration, or disclosure of data, such as personal, financial, or business information. Data loss or theft can result from cyberattacks, such as ransomware, spyware, or data breaches, or from human errors, such as accidental deletion, formatting, or corruption.
