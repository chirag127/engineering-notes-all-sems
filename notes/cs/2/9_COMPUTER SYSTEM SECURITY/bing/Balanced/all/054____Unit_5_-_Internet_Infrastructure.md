## Unit 5 - Internet Infrastructure

- Internet infrastructure is the physical hardware, transmission media, and software used to interconnect computers and users on the Internet.
- Internet infrastructure is responsible for hosting, storing, processing, and serving the information that makes up websites, applications, and content.
- Internet infrastructure is a network of networks, composed of different types and levels of networks that connect in various ways.
- The main components of Internet infrastructure are:
  - End devices: These are the devices that users interact with, such as computers, smartphones, tablets, etc. End devices can send and receive data over the Internet using various protocols and applications.
  - Network devices: These are the devices that connect end devices to each other and to other networks, such as routers, switches, access points, firewalls, etc. Network devices can perform various functions, such as routing, switching, filtering, encrypting, etc .
  - Transmission media: These are the physical or wireless means of transmitting data between network devices, such as cables, fibers, radio waves, etc. Transmission media can have different characteristics, such as bandwidth, latency, noise, etc.
  - Network services: These are the software programs that provide various functionalities and resources to end devices and network devices, such as DNS, DHCP, FTP, HTTP, etc. Network services can run on dedicated servers or on cloud platforms.
- Internet infrastructure is governed by various standards, protocols, and organizations that ensure its interoperability, security, and reliability.
- Internet infrastructure is constantly evolving and expanding to meet the growing demands and challenges of users, applications, and content.
