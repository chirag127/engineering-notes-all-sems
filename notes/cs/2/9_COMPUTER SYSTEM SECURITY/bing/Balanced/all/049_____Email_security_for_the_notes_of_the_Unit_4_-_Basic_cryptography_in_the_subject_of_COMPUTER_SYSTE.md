# Email security

Email security is the practice of protecting email accounts and communications from unauthorized access, loss, or compromise. Email is often used to spread malware, spam, and phishing attacks, which can compromise the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of email data and systems. Email security also involves establishing policies and using tools to prevent, detect, and respond to these threats.

Some of the topics related to email security are:

- **Email encryption**: Email encryption is the process of transforming plain text email messages into ciphertext that can only be read by authorized parties. Email encryption can be done at different levels, such as end-to-end encryption, transport layer security (TLS), or secure/multipurpose internet mail extensions (S/MIME). Email encryption can help protect the privacy and authenticity of email messages and attachments.
- **Email authentication**: Email authentication is the process of verifying the identity and legitimacy of the sender and the recipient of an email message. Email authentication can help prevent spoofing, phishing, and impersonation attacks, which can trick users into revealing sensitive information or downloading malicious attachments. Email authentication can be done using different protocols, such as sender policy framework (SPF), domain keys identified mail (DKIM), or domain-based message authentication, reporting, and conformance (DMARC).
- **Email filtering**: Email filtering is the process of screening incoming and outgoing email messages based on predefined criteria, such as sender, recipient, subject, content, or attachments. Email filtering can help block unwanted or malicious emails, such as spam, phishing, or malware, from reaching the user's inbox or the organization's network. Email filtering can be done using different techniques, such as blacklists, whitelists, heuristics, or machine learning.
- **Email archiving**: Email archiving is the process of storing email messages and attachments in a secure and searchable repository for future reference. Email archiving can help comply with legal and regulatory requirements, preserve organizational knowledge, and facilitate e-discovery and audit processes. Email archiving can be done using different methods, such as on-premise, cloud-based, or hybrid solutions.
