# Summary of weaknesses of internet security

Internet security is the protection of information systems and networks from unauthorized access, use, modification, or destruction. Internet security is essential for ensuring the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of data and services on the internet. However, internet security faces many challenges and weaknesses that can compromise its effectiveness and expose users and organizations to various cyber threats. Some of the common weaknesses of internet security are:

- **Lack of a high-level strategy**: Many businesses, especially new and small ones, simply lack a high-level strategy for their cybersecurity needs. They do not have a clear vision, goals, policies, or standards for securing their information systems and networks. They also do not allocate sufficient resources, personnel, or training for cybersecurity. This makes them vulnerable to cyberattacks and breaches that can damage their reputation, operations, and finances.

- **Unsecured networks**: If the network is not secured, it is trivially easy for nefarious parties to gain access to the system. Unsecured networks can result from weak or default passwords, open ports, unencrypted data, outdated or missing patches, or misconfigured firewalls or routers. Unsecured networks can allow attackers to intercept, modify, or steal data, launch denial-of-service attacks, or install malware or ransomware on the system .

- **Unsecured communication channels**: If the communication channels are not secured, they can be exploited by cybercriminals to eavesdrop, spoof, or tamper with the messages. Unsecured communication channels can result from the use of plain text, weak encryption, or untrusted certificates. Unsecured communication channels can expose sensitive or confidential information, such as personal details, passwords, credit card numbers, or trade secrets, to unauthorized parties .

- **Unknown bugs**: Unknown bugs are flaws or errors in the software or hardware that can cause unexpected or undesirable behavior. Unknown bugs can be exploited by cybercriminals to bypass security measures, access restricted areas, execute arbitrary code, or crash the system. Unknown bugs can be introduced by human mistakes, design flaws, or compatibility issues. Unknown bugs can be hard to detect and fix, especially if they are hidden, complex, or rare .

- **Outdated systems**: Outdated systems are those that are no longer supported, maintained, or updated by the vendors or developers. Outdated systems can have known or unknown vulnerabilities that can be exploited by cybercriminals to compromise the system. Outdated systems can also have compatibility or performance issues with newer technologies or standards. Outdated systems can pose a serious risk to internet security, as they can expose the system to malware, data breaches, or cyberattacks .

- **Lack of monitoring**: Lack of monitoring is the absence or insufficiency of tools or processes to track, analyze, or report the activities or events on the system or network. Lack of monitoring can prevent the detection, prevention, or response to cyber threats or incidents. Lack of monitoring can also hinder the identification, investigation, or remediation of security issues or vulnerabilities. Lack of monitoring can leave the system or network blind to potential or ongoing cyberattacks or breaches .

- **IoT and multiple connection points**: IoT (Internet of Things) refers to the network of physical devices, such as sensors, cameras, appliances, or vehicles, that are connected to the internet and can communicate or exchange data. Multiple connection points refer to the various ways or devices that users or organizations can access the internet, such as computers, smartphones, tablets, or routers. IoT and multiple connection points can increase the complexity and diversity of the internet infrastructure, as well as the number and variety of cyber threats and vulnerabilities. IoT and multiple connection points can also increase the difficulty and cost of securing and managing the internet security .

- **Untrained employees**: Untrained employees are those who do not have the necessary knowledge, skills, or awareness to protect themselves or the organization from cyber threats or incidents. Untrained employees can be the weakest link in the internet security, as they can fall victim to phishing, social engineering, or malware attacks, or unintentionally expose or compromise the system or network. Untrained employees can also violate or ignore the security policies or best practices, or fail to report or respond to security issues or incidents. Untrained employees can pose a significant risk to internet security, as they can facilitate or enable cyberattacks or breaches .
