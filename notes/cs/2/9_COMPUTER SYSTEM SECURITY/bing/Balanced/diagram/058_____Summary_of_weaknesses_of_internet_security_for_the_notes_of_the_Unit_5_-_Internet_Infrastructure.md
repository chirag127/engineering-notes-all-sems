### Summary of weaknesses of internet security

- Internet security is the protection of computer systems and networks from cyberattacks, such as hacking, malware, phishing, identity theft, etc.
- Weaknesses of internet security are the factors that make it easier for cybercriminals to exploit the vulnerabilities of the systems and networks and compromise the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of the data and services.
- Some of the common weaknesses of internet security are:

  - **Lack of a high-level strategy**: Many businesses, especially new and small ones, simply lack a high-level strategy for their cybersecurity needs. They do not have a clear vision, goals, policies, standards, or procedures for securing their systems and networks. They also do not have a dedicated team or budget for cybersecurity. This makes them vulnerable to cyberattacks and unable to respond effectively in case of a breach.
  - **Unsecured networks**: If the network is not secured, it is trivially easy for nefarious parties to gain access to the system. Unsecured networks may have weak or default passwords, open ports, unencrypted traffic, or outdated protocols. These allow cybercriminals to intercept, modify, or inject malicious data or commands into the network, or to launch denial-of-service attacks .
  - **Unsecured communication channels**: If the business is using unsecured communication channels, such as email, instant messaging, social media, or cloud services, to transmit or store sensitive data, it is exposing itself to cyberattacks. Cybercriminals can use phishing, spoofing, or malware to trick the users into revealing their credentials, downloading malicious attachments, or clicking on malicious links. They can also use man-in-the-middle attacks to eavesdrop or alter the communication .
  - **Unknown bugs**: Unknown bugs are the flaws or errors in the software or hardware that can cause unexpected or undesired behavior. They can be exploited by cybercriminals to bypass security measures, execute arbitrary code, escalate privileges, or access restricted data. Unknown bugs are hard to detect and fix, and may remain undiscovered for a long time .
  - **Outdated systems**: Outdated systems are the systems that are not updated with the latest security patches, updates, or versions. They may have known vulnerabilities that have been fixed in the newer versions, but are still exposed in the older ones. Cybercriminals can use these vulnerabilities to launch attacks against the outdated systems, such as ransomware, botnets, or zero-day exploits .
  - **Lack of monitoring**: Lack of monitoring is the absence or insufficiency of tools or processes to monitor the system and network activity, performance, or security. Without proper monitoring, the business may not be aware of the potential or actual cyberattacks, or the indicators of compromise. This may delay or prevent the detection, analysis, or response to the cyberattacks, and increase the damage or loss .
  - **IoT and multiple connection points**: IoT and multiple connection points are the devices or systems that are connected to the internet or the network, such as smartphones, tablets, laptops, smart TVs, smart appliances, sensors, cameras, etc. They increase the convenience and functionality of the users, but also increase the attack surface and complexity of the network. They may have weak or no security features, or may be compromised by malware or hackers. They can be used as entry points or vectors for cyberattacks, or as part of a botnet to launch distributed attacks .
  - **Untrained employees**: Untrained employees are the employees who are not aware of or trained on the best practices, policies, or procedures for cybersecurity. They may not know how to protect their devices, data, or accounts, or how to recognize or report suspicious or malicious activities. They may also fall victim to social engineering, phishing, or insider threats. They are the weakest link in the cybersecurity chain, and the most common cause of human error or negligence .
