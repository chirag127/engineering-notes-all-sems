## Unit 5 - Internet Infrastructure

- Internet infrastructure is the physical hardware, transmission media, and software used to interconnect computers and users on the Internet.
- Internet infrastructure is responsible for hosting, storing, processing, and serving the information that makes up websites, applications, and content.
- Internet infrastructure is a network of networks, composed of different types and levels of networks that connect in various ways.
- The main components of Internet infrastructure are:
  - End devices: These are the computers, smartphones, tablets, and other devices that users access the Internet with.
  - Local area networks (LANs): These are the networks that connect end devices within a limited area, such as a home, office, or school.
  - Wide area networks (WANs): These are the networks that connect LANs across larger distances, such as cities, countries, or continents.
  - Internet service providers (ISPs): These are the companies that provide access to the Internet for end users and other networks.
  - Internet backbone: This is the network of high-speed and high-capacity links that connect the major ISPs and exchange points.
  - Internet exchange points (IXPs): These are the locations where different ISPs and networks exchange traffic and data.
  - Domain name system (DNS): This is the system that translates human-readable domain names (such as www.example.com) into numerical IP addresses (such as 192.168.1.1) that identify the location of the servers that host the websites and applications.
  - Web servers: These are the computers that store and deliver the web pages and other content that users request through their browsers.
  - Web browsers: These are the applications that users use to access and view the web pages and other content on the Internet.
- Internet infrastructure is constantly evolving and expanding to meet the growing demand and needs of users and businesses.
- Internet infrastructure faces various challenges and opportunities, such as security, privacy, reliability, scalability, affordability, accessibility, and innovation.
