### Other issues in access control for computer system security

Access control is the process of granting or denying access to resources based on the identity and attributes of users or groups. Access control systems aim to protect the confidentiality, integrity and availability of resources from unauthorized or malicious actors. However, access control systems can also face some issues that can compromise their effectiveness or security. Some of these issues are:

- **Not secure enough**: Access control systems can have various vulnerabilities that can be exploited by attackers, such as weak passwords, default settings, misconfigurations, outdated software, network exposure, physical access, etc. These vulnerabilities can allow attackers to bypass, tamper with, or disable the access control mechanisms and gain unauthorized access to resources .
- **Keycards lack true authorization**: Keycards are a common method of authentication in access control systems, but they have some limitations and drawbacks. Keycards can be lost, stolen, duplicated, or shared, which can result in unauthorized access or identity theft. Keycards also do not verify the identity of the user, but only the possession of the card, which can be insufficient for some scenarios that require higher levels of assurance.
- **Improper setup**: Access control systems require proper setup and configuration to function correctly and securely. This includes defining the access control policy, assigning the roles and permissions, configuring the devices and software, testing the system, and updating the system regularly. Improper setup can lead to errors, inconsistencies, conflicts, or loopholes in the access control system, which can affect its performance or security .
- **Lack of management**: Access control systems also require ongoing management and maintenance to ensure their effectiveness and security. This includes monitoring the system, auditing the logs, reviewing the policy, enforcing the compliance, detecting and responding to incidents, and resolving the issues. Lack of management can lead to outdated, inaccurate, or incomplete information, which can affect the access control decisions or cause security breaches .
