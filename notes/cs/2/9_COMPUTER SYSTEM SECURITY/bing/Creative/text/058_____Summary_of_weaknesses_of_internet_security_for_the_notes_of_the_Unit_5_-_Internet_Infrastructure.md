### Summary of weaknesses of internet security

- Internet security is the protection of information systems and networks from cyberattacks, unauthorized access, data theft, and other threats.
- Weaknesses of internet security are the factors that make the information systems and networks vulnerable to cyberattacks and compromise their confidentiality, integrity, and availability.
- Some of the common weaknesses of internet security are:

  - **Lack of a high-level strategy**: Many businesses, especially new and small ones, simply lack a high-level strategy for their cybersecurity needs. They do not have a clear vision, goals, policies, and procedures for securing their information systems and networks. They also do not allocate sufficient resources, personnel, and training for cybersecurity. This makes them easy targets for cybercriminals who can exploit their lack of awareness, preparedness, and defense.

  - **Unsecured networks**: If the network is not secured, it is trivially easy for nefarious parties to gain access to the system. Unsecured networks do not have proper encryption, authentication, firewall, and antivirus measures to protect the data and devices connected to them. They also do not monitor and update the network security regularly to prevent and detect cyberattacks. This exposes the network to various threats such as malware, ransomware, phishing, denial-of-service, man-in-the-middle, and brute-force attacks .

  - **Unsecured communication channels**: If the communication channels are not secured, they can be intercepted, modified, or disrupted by cybercriminals. Unsecured communication channels do not use encryption, digital signatures, or certificates to ensure the privacy, authenticity, and integrity of the messages and data exchanged. They also do not verify the identity and trustworthiness of the senders and receivers of the communication. This allows cybercriminals to eavesdrop, spoof, tamper, or hijack the communication and steal or manipulate the information .

  - **Unknown bugs**: Unknown bugs are the flaws or errors in the software or hardware that can cause unexpected or undesirable behavior or results. Unknown bugs can be exploited by cybercriminals to bypass the security measures, gain unauthorized access, execute malicious code, or cause damage to the system. Unknown bugs can be introduced by the developers, vendors, or users of the software or hardware, and can be difficult to detect and fix. They can also be intentionally planted by cybercriminals as backdoors or trojans to compromise the system .

  - **Outdated systems**: Outdated systems are the software or hardware that are no longer supported, maintained, or updated by the developers or vendors. Outdated systems are vulnerable to cyberattacks because they do not have the latest security patches, features, or improvements to address the emerging threats and risks. They also do not comply with the current standards, regulations, or best practices for cybersecurity. Outdated systems can be exploited by cybercriminals to exploit the known or unknown vulnerabilities, or to take advantage of the compatibility or interoperability issues with the newer systems .

  - **Lack of monitoring**: Lack of monitoring is the absence or inadequacy of the processes and tools to observe, analyze, and report the activities and events on the information systems and networks. Lack of monitoring prevents the detection, prevention, and response to cyberattacks, as well as the identification, assessment, and mitigation of the risks and impacts. Lack of monitoring also hinders the accountability, auditability, and compliance of the information systems and networks. Lack of monitoring can be caused by the lack of resources, skills, or awareness of the importance and benefits of monitoring .

  - **IoT and multiple connection points**: IoT (Internet of Things) and multiple connection points are the devices and systems that are connected to the internet and can communicate with each other. IoT and multiple connection points increase the functionality, convenience, and efficiency of the information systems and networks, but also increase the complexity, diversity, and exposure of the security challenges. IoT and multiple connection points can introduce new vulnerabilities, threats, and risks to the information systems and networks, as well as increase the attack surface and vectors for cybercriminals. IoT and multiple connection points can also be difficult to secure, manage, and monitor, due to the lack of standards, protocols, or regulations for them .
