### Summary of weaknesses of internet security for the notes of the Unit 5 - Internet Infrastructure in the subject of COMPUTER SYSTEM SECURITY

- Internet security is the protection of information systems and networks from cyberattacks, unauthorized access, data theft, and other threats.
- Weaknesses of internet security are the factors that make information systems and networks vulnerable to cyberattacks and compromise their confidentiality, integrity, and availability.
- Some of the common weaknesses of internet security are:

  - **Lack of a high-level strategy**: Many businesses, especially new and small ones, simply lack a high-level strategy for their cybersecurity needs. They do not have a clear vision, goals, policies, and procedures for securing their information systems and networks. They also do not allocate sufficient resources, personnel, and training for cybersecurity. This makes them easy targets for cybercriminals who can exploit their lack of awareness, preparedness, and defense.
  - **Unsecured networks**: If the network is not secured, it is trivially easy for nefarious parties to gain access to the system. Unsecured networks do not have proper encryption, authentication, firewall, and antivirus measures to protect the data and devices connected to them. They also do not monitor and update their network security regularly to prevent and detect intrusions. This exposes the network to various attacks such as eavesdropping, spoofing, man-in-the-middle, denial-of-service, and ransomware .
  - **Unsecured communication channels**: If the business is using unsecured communication channels, such as email, instant messaging, social media, or cloud services, to transmit sensitive or confidential information, they are risking their data being intercepted, altered, or stolen by cybercriminals. Unsecured communication channels do not have adequate encryption, authentication, and verification mechanisms to ensure the privacy and integrity of the data and the identity of the sender and receiver. They also do not have proper backup and recovery plans in case of data loss or breach.
  - **Unknown bugs**: Unknown bugs are flaws or errors in the software or hardware of the information system or network that can be exploited by cybercriminals to compromise the system. Unknown bugs can be caused by poor design, development, testing, or maintenance of the software or hardware. They can also be introduced by malicious insiders or third-party vendors who have access to the system. Unknown bugs can allow cybercriminals to bypass security controls, execute malicious code, access or modify data, or take over the system .
  - **Outdated systems**: Outdated systems are information systems or networks that are running on old or obsolete software or hardware that are no longer supported or updated by the vendors or developers. Outdated systems are vulnerable to cyberattacks because they do not have the latest security patches, fixes, or features that can protect them from new or emerging threats. They also have compatibility issues with newer systems or devices that can cause errors, malfunctions, or disruptions .
  - **Lack of monitoring**: Lack of monitoring is the failure to track, analyze, and report the activities and events that occur on the information system or network. Lack of monitoring prevents the business from detecting and responding to cyberattacks, anomalies, or incidents that can compromise the system. It also prevents the business from identifying and improving the performance, efficiency, and security of the system. Lack of monitoring can be caused by insufficient tools, skills, or resources for monitoring, or by negligence or complacency of the staff or management.
  - **IoT and multiple connection points**: IoT (Internet of Things) and multiple connection points are the devices and systems that are connected to the internet or network, such as smartphones, tablets, laptops, cameras, sensors, smart appliances, etc. IoT and multiple connection points increase the complexity and diversity of the information system or network, and also increase the potential entry points for cyberattacks. IoT and multiple connection points can be compromised by cybercriminals if they are not secured, updated, or controlled properly. They can also pose risks to the system if they are lost, stolen, or misused by unauthorized users .
