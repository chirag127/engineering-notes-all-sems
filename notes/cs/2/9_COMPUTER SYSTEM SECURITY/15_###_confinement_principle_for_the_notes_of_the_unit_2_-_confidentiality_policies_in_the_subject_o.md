### Confinement Principle for the notes of the Unit 2 - Confidentiality Policies in the subject of COMPUTER SYSTEM SECURITY

The Confinement Principle is a fundamental security principle that is applied in computer systems security. It states that a system should be designed in such a way that it restricts the actions of a user or process to only those actions that are necessary for the completion of its intended task.

The Confinement Principle is critical for ensuring the security and privacy of sensitive information and systems, as it helps to prevent unauthorized access, manipulation, or theft of information. By restricting the actions of users and processes to only those that are necessary, the Confinement Principle helps to minimize the risk of security breaches and to ensure the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of information.

In this unit, we will study the Confinement Principle and its application in computer systems security. We will examine the strategies for implementing the Confinement Principle, and explore the use of various security technologies, such as access control, firewalls, and encryption, to enforce the Confinement Principle. This will provide a foundation for understanding the importance of the Confinement Principle in computer systems security, and for exploring the various applications of computer systems security in the workplace.
