## Unit 2 - Confidentiality Policies

Unit 2 of the Computer System Security course focuses on Confidentiality Policies.

Confidentiality policies refer to the rules and regulations that govern the protection of sensitive or confidential information. These policies are designed to ensure that sensitive information is protected from unauthorized access, theft, or damage, and to ensure that the information is used only for the purposes for which it was intended.

In the context of the Computer System Security course, students in Unit 2 will learn about the different types of confidentiality policies, including data protection policies, privacy policies, and security policies. They will also learn about the importance of confidentiality policies in protecting sensitive information, and will learn about the different types of information that may be considered confidential, such as personal information, financial information, and intellectual property.

In this unit, students will learn about the different types of confidentiality policies and the importance of implementing these policies to protect sensitive information. They will also learn about the different types of information that may be considered confidential, and will learn about the different types of security measures and techniques that are used to protect confidential information, including encryption, access controls, and firewalls.

In summary, Unit 2 of the Computer System Security course focuses on Confidentiality Policies, including the different types of confidentiality policies, the importance of confidentiality policies in protecting sensitive information, and the different types of security measures and techniques that are used to protect confidential information. Students will learn about the different types of information that may be considered confidential, and will learn about the importance of implementing confidentiality policies to protect sensitive information.
