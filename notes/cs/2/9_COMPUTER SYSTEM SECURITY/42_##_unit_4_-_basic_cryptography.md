## Unit 4 - Basic cryptography

Unit 4 of the Computer System Security course focuses on Basic Cryptography.

Cryptography is the practice of secure communication in the presence of third parties, known as adversaries. It is used to protect sensitive information from unauthorized access, theft, or damage.

In the context of the Computer System Security course, students in Unit 4 will learn about the basics of cryptography, including the different types of encryption algorithms, key management, and digital signatures. They will also learn about the use of cryptography in securing communications, such as email and instant messaging, and in protecting sensitive information, such as financial transactions and personal data.

In this unit, students will learn about the history of cryptography, the different types of encryption algorithms, and the importance of key management in cryptography. They will also learn about the use of cryptography in securing communications and protecting sensitive information, and will learn about the different types of digital signatures and their use in authentication and non-repudiation.

In summary, Unit 4 of the Computer System Security course focuses on Basic Cryptography, including the different types of encryption algorithms, key management, and digital signatures. Students will learn about the use of cryptography in securing communications and protecting sensitive information, and will learn about the different types of digital signatures and their use in authentication and non-repudiation.
