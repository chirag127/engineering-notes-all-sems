### Email security for the notes of the Unit 4 - Basic cryptography in the subject of COMPUTER SYSTEM SECURITY

Email security is a critical aspect of computer system security, as email is one of the most widely used forms of communication and is often used to transmit sensitive information. In order to ensure the security of email, it is important to implement robust security measures, such as:

1. Encryption: Encryption is used to protect the confidentiality of information as it is transmitted over a network.

2. Digital signatures: Digital signatures are used to verify the authenticity of email messages and to prevent tampering.

3. Spam filtering: Spam filtering is used to prevent unwanted or malicious email from reaching the user's inbox.

4. Anti-virus software: Anti-virus software is used to detect and prevent the spread of malware, such as viruses and Trojans, through email.

In this unit, we will study the basics of email security, including encryption, digital signatures, spam filtering, and anti-virus software. We will examine the strategies for implementing these security measures, and explore the use of email security technologies in various applications, such as secure architecture design, isolation, and leasing. This will provide a foundation for understanding the principles and practices of computer system security, and for exploring the various security measures used to protect computer systems.
