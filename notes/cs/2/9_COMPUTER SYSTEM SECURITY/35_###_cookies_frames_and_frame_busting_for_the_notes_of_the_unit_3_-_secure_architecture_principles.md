### Cookies frames and frame busting for the notes of the Unit 3 - Secure architecture principles isolation and leas in the subject of COMPUTER SYSTEM SECURITY

Cookies, frames, and frame busting are related concepts in web security and architecture. They play a role in ensuring the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of information on the web.

1. Cookies: Cookies are small text files that are stored on a user's computer by a website. They are used to store information about the user's preferences, such as login credentials, and to track the user's activity on the website.

2. Frames: Frames are a feature of web pages that allow multiple pages to be displayed within a single page. They can be used to display ads, navigation menus, or other content on a web page.

3. Frame Busting: Frame busting is a technique that is used to prevent a web page from being displayed within a frame. This is done to prevent malicious websites from displaying content within a frame that could be used to steal sensitive information or to launch phishing attacks.

In this unit, we will study the concepts of cookies, frames, and frame busting, and their role in web security and architecture. We will examine the strategies for securing cookies and frames, and explore the use of frame busting techniques to prevent malicious activity on the web. This will provide a foundation for understanding the principles and practices of web security and architecture, and for exploring the various applications of web security in the workplace.
