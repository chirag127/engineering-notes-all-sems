### More on confinement techniques for the notes of the Unit 2 - Confidentiality Policies in the subject of COMPUTER SYSTEM SECURITY

Unit 2 of the Computer System Security course focuses on Confidentiality Policies, including the aspect of confinement techniques.

Confinement techniques refer to the methods used to restrict access to sensitive information and systems, and to prevent unauthorized access, use, or disclosure of sensitive information. Confinement techniques are an important aspect of computer system security and are used to ensure the confidentiality of sensitive information.

There are several types of confinement techniques, including physical security measures, such as locks and security cameras, as well as logical security measures, such as access control systems, firewalls, and encryption.

In the context of the Computer System Security course, students in Unit 2 may learn about confinement techniques as part of the Confidentiality Policies unit. This may include working with examples of different confinement techniques, learning about the impact of confinement techniques on the confidentiality of sensitive information, and learning strategies for implementing effective confinement techniques.
