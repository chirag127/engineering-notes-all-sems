### Basic security problems for the notes of the Unit 5 - Internet Infrastructure in the subject of COMPUTER SYSTEM SECURITY

The basic security problems in internet infrastructure refer to the various security challenges faced by organizations and individuals in securing their online presence and protecting their information. In the Unit 5 - Internet Infrastructure in the subject of COMPUTER SYSTEM SECURITY, students learn about the internet infrastructure and the various security challenges faced by organizations and individuals in securing their online presence and protecting their information. The notes for this unit should include the following topics:

1. Definition of basic security problems in internet infrastructure (security challenges faced by organizations and individuals in securing their online presence and protecting their information)
2. Overview of basic security problems (types, causes, consequences)
3. Characteristics of effective security measures (prevention, detection, response)
4. Types of security threats in internet infrastructure (malware, phishing, DDoS attacks)
5. Techniques for improving security in internet infrastructure (firewalls, intrusion detection, encryption)
6. The role of government and industry in regulating security in internet infrastructure (legislation, certification, standards)
7. Applications of security measures in different fields (commerce, finance, healthcare)
8. Limitations of security measures in internet infrastructure (complexity of systems, cultural differences)
9. Techniques for overcoming limitations (awareness, education, security audits)
10. Further reading and resources (books, journals, online resources)

Basic security problems in internet infrastructure are a critical concern for organizations and individuals in securing their online presence and protecting their information. Understanding the principles and techniques of security measures in internet infrastructure helps students to develop the skills required to secure their online presence and protect their information effectively and efficiently in a variety of contexts.
