## Unit 5 - Internet Infrastructure

Unit 5 of the subject Computer System Security will focus on the Internet infrastructure, which refers to the underlying components and technologies that make up the Internet. This includes the physical and logical components, such as routers, switches, servers, and protocols, that are used to connect computers and devices to the Internet and to enable communication and data exchange.

Some of the key topics that will be covered in Unit 5 include:

1. Network protocols: Network protocols are the rules and standards that govern communication and data exchange over the Internet. Examples of network protocols include TCP/IP, HTTP, and DNS.

2. Routing: Routing is the process of directing network traffic from one network to another, and it is an essential component of the Internet infrastructure.

3. Switching: Switching is the process of forwarding network traffic from one network to another, and it is an essential component of the Internet infrastructure.

4. Servers: Servers are computers that provide services to other computers and devices, and they play a crucial role in the Internet infrastructure.

In this unit, we will study the Internet infrastructure and its various components, including network protocols, routing, switching, and servers. We will examine the strategies for designing and implementing secure Internet infrastructure, and explore the use of security technologies, such as firewalls, intrusion detection systems, and access control systems, to secure the Internet infrastructure. This will provide a foundation for understanding the principles and practices of computer system security, and for exploring the various security measures used to protect computer systems.
