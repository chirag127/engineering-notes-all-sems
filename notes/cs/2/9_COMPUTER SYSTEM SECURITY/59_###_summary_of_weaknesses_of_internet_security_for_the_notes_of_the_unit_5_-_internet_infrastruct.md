### Summary of weaknesses of internet security for the notes of the Unit 5 - Internet Infrastructure in the subject of COMPUTER SYSTEM SECURITY

Internet security refers to the measures taken to protect the privacy and security of information transmitted over the internet. In the Unit 5 - Internet Infrastructure in the subject of COMPUTER SYSTEM SECURITY, students learn about the infrastructure of the internet and the various threats and vulnerabilities associated with the internet. The notes for this unit should include the following topics:

1. Definition of internet security (measures taken to protect the privacy and security of information transmitted over the internet)
2. Overview of internet security (types of threats, security measures, security policies)
3. Summary of weaknesses of internet security (lack of encryption, weak passwords, social engineering)
4. Types of internet security threats (malware, phishing, DDoS attacks)
5. Techniques for improving internet security (encryption, firewalls, antivirus software)
6. The role of internet infrastructure in internet security (network design, routing, domain name resolution)
7. Applications of internet security in different fields (e-commerce, finance, healthcare)
8. Limitations of internet security (complexity of systems, lack of awareness)
9. Techniques for overcoming limitations (education, training, security audits)
10. Further reading and resources (books, journals, online resources)

The weaknesses of internet security pose a significant threat to the privacy and security of information transmitted over the internet. Understanding the infrastructure of the internet and the various threats and vulnerabilities associated with the internet helps students to develop the skills required to secure information transmitted over the internet effectively and efficiently.
