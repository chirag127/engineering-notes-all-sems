### Summary of weaknesses of internet security for the notes of the Unit 5 - Internet Infrastructure in the subject of COMPUTER SYSTEM SECURITY

1. Open architecture: The Internet has an open architecture which allows free flow of information. This openness makes it vulnerable to attacks and cybercrimes. There are no controls on who can access the network or what information can be exchanged.

2. Anonymity: It is easy to stay anonymous on the Internet. This anonymity makes it difficult to trace the origin of attacks or hold people accountable for their actions. Criminals take advantage of this to conduct frauds, scams and other cybercrimes without being identified.

3. Decentralized control: The Internet does not have any centralized control. This makes it difficult to implement and enforce security controls or measures across the network. The decentralized nature and lack of universal policies or standards on security pose challenges to ensuring cybersecurity.

4. Malware and viruses: The Internet enables quick spread of malware, viruses, worms and other malicious software. Once a malware enters the network, it can spread rapidly to systems across the globe. This can have damaging consequences like stealing data, gaining unauthorized access and disabling systems.

5. Data security: The Internet makes it easy to access and exchange data. However, this also means that data security is difficult to achieve. Data can be easily stolen or hacked due to vulnerabilities in networks, servers or individual systems. Lack of awareness about secure data practices among users also contributes to data security problems on the Internet.
