### Public key distribution for the notes of the Unit 4 - Basic cryptography in the subject of COMPUTER SYSTEM SECURITY

In the world of computer system security, cryptography plays a significant role in ensuring data confidentiality and secure communication. The primary purpose of cryptography is to convert plaintext into ciphertext to protect the data from unauthorized access. Public key cryptography, also known as asymmetric cryptography, is a widely used technique that involves the use of a pair of keys, namely public and private keys, to secure the data.

In this unit, we will focus on the public key distribution for securing the notes of Basic cryptography. Here are some key points to understand the concept of public key distribution:

1. Public key distribution is the process of securely distributing the public keys to the intended recipients.

2. One of the widely used methods for public key distribution is the Public Key Infrastructure (PKI), which involves the use of digital certificates.

3. A digital certificate is a digital document that contains the public key and some additional information about the owner of the key, such as name, email address, and organization.

4. The digital certificate is issued by a trusted third-party, known as a Certificate Authority (CA), to ensure the authenticity of the public key.

5. The receiver can verify the authenticity of the digital certificate by checking the CA's signature on the certificate.

6. Once the authenticity of the digital certificate is established, the receiver can access the public key and use it for secure communication with the sender.

7. Another method for public key distribution is the use of a web of trust, in which users can authenticate the keys of other users based on their level of trust.

8. In a web of trust, a user can sign the public key of another user to indicate that they trust the authenticity of the key.

9. The more signatures a public key has, the more trusted it is in the web of trust.

10. However, the web of trust requires a considerable level of trust among the users, and it may not be suitable for large organizations or enterprises.

In conclusion, public key distribution is a crucial aspect of securing the notes of Basic cryptography. The use of digital certificates through PKI is the most widely used method for public key distribution. A web of trust is another method for public key distribution, but it requires a high level of trust among the users. It is essential to understand these concepts thoroughly to ensure secure communication and data confidentiality.
