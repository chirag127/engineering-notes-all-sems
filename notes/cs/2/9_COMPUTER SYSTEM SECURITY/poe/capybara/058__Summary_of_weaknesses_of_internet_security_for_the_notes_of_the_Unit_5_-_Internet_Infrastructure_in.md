### Summary of weaknesses of internet security for the notes of the Unit 5 - Internet Infrastructure in the subject of COMPUTER SYSTEM SECURITY

The internet has revolutionized the way we communicate, work, and live our lives. However, with the increase in internet usage, the risks associated with online activities have also increased. Below are some weaknesses of internet security:

- **Vulnerabilities in software and hardware:** The software and hardware used in internet infrastructure are not always secure. Hackers can exploit vulnerabilities in these systems to gain unauthorized access to sensitive information.

- **Phishing attacks:** Phishing is a type of social engineering attack used to trick people into giving away sensitive information such as passwords, credit card numbers, and other personal information. Phishing attacks are becoming more sophisticated, making them harder to detect.

- **Weak passwords:** Weak passwords are a common weakness in internet security. Many people use simple and easy-to-guess passwords, making it easier for hackers to gain access to their accounts.

- **Malware:** Malware is a type of software designed to harm or exploit computers and networks. Malware can spread through emails, downloads, and other online activities, compromising the security of computer systems and networks.

- **Insider threats:** Insider threats are security risks that come from within an organization. Employees, contractors, and other insiders with access to sensitive information can compromise internet security by intentionally or unintentionally sharing confidential information.

- **Lack of encryption:** Encryption is the process of converting data into a format that can only be read by authorized users. Lack of encryption can lead to data breaches and unauthorized access to sensitive information.

In conclusion, internet security is a complex and constantly evolving field. It is important to stay vigilant and take steps to protect against the weaknesses in internet security to safeguard sensitive information and prevent cyber attacks.
