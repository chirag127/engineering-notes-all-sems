## Unit 3 - Secure architecture principles isolation and leas

In this unit, we will learn about the secure architecture principles of isolation and least privilege. These principles are essential in ensuring the security of systems and networks. 

### Isolation

Isolation is the principle of separating different components of a system or network to reduce the risk of a security breach. This can be achieved through the following methods:

- **Network Segmentation**: This involves dividing a network into smaller subnetworks, each with its own security controls. This ensures that a security breach in one subnetwork does not affect the others.
- **Virtualization**: This involves creating virtual machines or containers to isolate applications and services from each other. This reduces the risk of a security breach spreading to other applications and services.
- **Data Separation**: This involves separating sensitive data from non-sensitive data to reduce the risk of unauthorized access or theft.

### Least Privilege

Least privilege is the principle of giving users and processes only the minimum access required to perform their tasks. This reduces the risk of a security breach by limiting the damage that can be caused by a compromised user or process. The following are some ways to implement least privilege:

- **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)**: This involves assigning roles to users based on their job functions and giving them access only to the resources necessary for their roles.
- **Permission-Based Access Control**: This involves granting permissions to users based on specific actions they need to perform. For example, a user may be granted read-only access to a file, but not write access.
- **Application Sandboxing**: This involves running an application in a restricted environment with limited access to system resources. This reduces the risk of a security breach by limiting the damage that can be caused by a compromised application.

In conclusion, the principles of isolation and least privilege are crucial in ensuring the security of systems and networks. By implementing these principles, organizations can reduce the risk of a security breach and protect their sensitive data and assets.
