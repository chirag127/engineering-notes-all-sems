
### Basic security problems for the notes of the Unit 5 - Internet Infrastructure in the subject of COMPUTER SYSTEM SECURITY

1. Data interception: Data sent over the Internet is vulnerable to interception by malicious actors, who can access and modify the data without the knowledge of the sender or receiver.

2. Unauthorized access: Unauthorized access to data stored in computers connected to the Internet can be achieved through various methods, such as using stolen credentials or exploiting security weaknesses.

3. Denial of service: Denial of service attacks can be used to disrupt communication between computers connected to the Internet, making it difficult or impossible to access certain services.

4. Malicious software: Malicious software, such as viruses, can be spread over the Internet, causing damage to computers and data stored on them.

5. Phishing: Phishing attacks can be used to trick users into revealing confidential information, such as passwords or credit card numbers.

6. Spoofing: Spoofing attacks can be used to disguise the identity of a sender, making it difficult to determine the source of an attack.
