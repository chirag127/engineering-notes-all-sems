
### Confinement Principle

1. The confinement principle states that a system should be designed so that a user can access only the information and resources that they are authorized to access.
2. This principle is important in computer security because it helps to prevent users from accessing confidential information or resources that they do not have permission to access.
3. The principle is also known as the principle of least privilege, which states that users should have the least amount of privileges necessary to perform their tasks.
4. The principle of least privilege is important because it helps to minimize the damage that can be done by malicious users, as they will not be able to access resources or information that they are not authorized to access.
5. Confinement can be enforced through the use of access control systems, which are used to control who can access what resources.
6. Access control systems can be based on user authentication, which requires users to provide credentials such as passwords, or can be based on authorization, which requires users to be granted specific privileges to access certain resources.
7. Confinement is also enforced through the use of encryption, which is used to protect confidential information from unauthorized access.
8. The use of encryption ensures that only those who have the correct encryption key can access the encrypted information.
