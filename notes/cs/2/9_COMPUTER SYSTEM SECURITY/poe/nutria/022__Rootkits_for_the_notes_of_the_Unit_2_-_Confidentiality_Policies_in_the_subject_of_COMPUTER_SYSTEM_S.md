
### Rootkits for the notes of the Unit 2 - Confidentiality Policies in the subject of COMPUTER SYSTEM SECURITY

* A rootkit is a type of malicious software that is designed to gain access to a computer system without the user's knowledge. 
* Rootkits are often used to hide other malicious software, such as viruses, spyware, and Trojans, from being detected by anti-virus programs. 
* Rootkits can also be used to gain access to confidential data, such as passwords, financial information, and other sensitive information. 
* Rootkits are often difficult to detect because they are designed to be stealthy, and they can be installed in a variety of ways, including through email attachments, malicious websites, and malicious software downloads. 
* It is important to protect your computer system from rootkits by using an up-to-date anti-virus program and by avoiding downloading unknown files or clicking on links in emails. 
* Additionally, it is important to ensure that all confidential data is stored securely and that access to it is restricted to authorized personnel only.
