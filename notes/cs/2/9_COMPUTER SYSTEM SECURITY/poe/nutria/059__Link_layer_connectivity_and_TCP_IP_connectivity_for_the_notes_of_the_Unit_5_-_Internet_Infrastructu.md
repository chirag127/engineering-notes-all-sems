
### Link Layer Connectivity 
- Link layer connectivity is the process of connecting two devices together on the same network.
- It is responsible for the physical connection between two devices, such as Ethernet cables, Wi-Fi, or Bluetooth.
- It is also responsible for data transmission between two devices, such as packet switching and error checking.
- Link layer protocols include Ethernet, ARP, and PPP.

### TCP/IP Connectivity
- The Transmission Control Protocol (TCP) and Internet Protocol (IP) are two of the main protocols used in the Internet Protocol Suite (TCP/IP).
- TCP/IP is responsible for the connection between two devices on the Internet.
- It is responsible for data transmission between two devices, such as packet switching, error checking, and addressing.
- TCP/IP protocols include IP, TCP, UDP, ICMP, and IGMP.
