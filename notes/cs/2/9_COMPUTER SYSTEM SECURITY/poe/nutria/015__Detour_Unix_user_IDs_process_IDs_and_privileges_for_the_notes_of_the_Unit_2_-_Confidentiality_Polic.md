
### Detour Unix user IDs, Process IDs and Privileges

1. **User IDs** are the identifiers used to differentiate between users on a Unix system. They are associated with a user's account and are used to control access to the system and its resources.

2. **Process IDs** are the identifiers used to differentiate between processes running on a Unix system. They are associated with a process and are used to control access to the system and its resources.

3. **Privileges** are the rights and permissions granted to users and processes on a Unix system. Privileges are used to control access to the system and its resources.

4. Unix systems use a combination of user IDs, process IDs and privileges to control access to the system and its resources. This is known as **access control**.

5. Access control is an important part of maintaining the confidentiality of data on a Unix system. It is important to ensure that only authorized users and processes have access to sensitive data.
