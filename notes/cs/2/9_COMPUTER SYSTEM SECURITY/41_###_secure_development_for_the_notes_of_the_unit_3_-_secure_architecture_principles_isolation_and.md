### Secure development for the notes of the Unit 3 - Secure architecture principles isolation and leas in the subject of COMPUTER SYSTEM SECURITY

Secure development is a critical aspect of computer system security, as it involves the process of designing, developing, and testing software and systems in a secure manner. Secure development helps to minimize the risk of security breaches and to ensure the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of information.

Secure development involves the following steps:

1. Requirements gathering and analysis: The first step in secure development is to gather and analyze the requirements for the software or system, and to identify any potential security risks or vulnerabilities.

2. Design and architecture: The next step in secure development is to design and develop the software or system in a secure manner, taking into account any security risks or vulnerabilities that were identified in the requirements gathering and analysis phase.

3. Implementation and testing: The final step in secure development is to implement and test the software or system, and to ensure that it meets the security requirements and standards.

In this unit, we will study the principles and practices of secure development, and we will examine the strategies for designing, developing, and testing software and systems in a secure manner. This will provide a foundation for understanding the importance of secure development in computer system security, and for exploring the various applications of security in the workplace.
