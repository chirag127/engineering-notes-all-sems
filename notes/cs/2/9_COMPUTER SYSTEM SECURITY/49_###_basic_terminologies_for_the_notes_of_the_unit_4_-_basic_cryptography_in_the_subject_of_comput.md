### Basic terminologies for the notes of the Unit 4 - Basic cryptography in the subject of COMPUTER SYSTEM SECURITY

Cryptography is the study of techniques for secure communication in the presence of third parties. In the Unit 4 - Basic cryptography in the subject of COMPUTER SYSTEM SECURITY, students learn about the basic concepts and techniques of cryptography and how to apply these concepts in different contexts. The notes for this unit should include the following topics:

1. Definition of cryptography (the study of techniques for secure communication in the presence of third parties)
2. Overview of cryptography (types, methods, purposes)
3. Basic terminologies in cryptography (plaintext, ciphertext, encryption, decryption)
4. Types of cryptography (symmetric, asymmetric, hash functions)
5. Techniques for implementing cryptography (substitution, transposition, stream ciphers)
6. The role of cryptography in computer system security (confidentiality, integrity, authentication)
7. Applications of cryptography in different fields (e-commerce, finance, healthcare)
8. Limitations of cryptography (complexity of systems, performance overhead)
9. Techniques for overcoming limitations (optimization, awareness, education)
10. Further reading and resources (books, journals, online resources)

Cryptography is an important aspect of computer system security and is essential for secure communication in the presence of third parties. Understanding the basic concepts and techniques of cryptography helps students to develop the skills required to secure communication effectively and efficiently in a variety of contexts.
