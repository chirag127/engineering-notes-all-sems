### Error 404 digital Hacking in India part 2 chase for the notes of the Unit 2 - Confidentiality Policies in the subject of COMPUTER SYSTEM SECURITY

Error 404, digital hacking, and the chase for confidentiality policies are all related to the protection of sensitive information in computer systems. In the Unit 2 - Confidentiality Policies in the subject of COMPUTER SYSTEM SECURITY, students learn about the principles and techniques of protecting sensitive information in computer systems, including the importance of confidentiality policies and the challenges of securing sensitive information in the digital age. The notes for this unit should include the following topics:

1. Definition of Error 404 (a common error message displayed when a requested web page cannot be found)
2. Definition of digital hacking (unauthorized access to or manipulation of computer systems, networks, or data)
3. Overview of confidentiality policies (types, purposes, audiences)
4. Characteristics of effective confidentiality policies (clarity, enforceability, adaptability)
5. Techniques for improving confidentiality policies (risk assessment, incident response, user education)
6. The impact of digital hacking on confidentiality policies (data theft, system disruption, financial loss)
7. The role of government and industry in regulating digital hacking (legislation, certification, standards)
8. Techniques for improving security in the face of digital hacking (encryption, firewalls, access controls)
9. Applications of confidentiality policies in different fields (commerce, finance, healthcare)
10. Further reading and resources (books, journals, online resources)
