### Defense against Control Hijacking - Platform Defenses for the notes of the Unit 1 - Computer System Security Introduction in the subject of COMPUTER SYSTEM SECURITY

Control hijacking refers to the unauthorized takeover of control over a computer system or network. To defend against control hijacking, platform defenses are implemented to prevent unauthorized access and ensure the security of the system. In the Unit 1 - Computer System Security Introduction in the subject of COMPUTER SYSTEM SECURITY, students learn about the basic concepts of computer system security and the various types of security threats. The notes for this unit should include the following topics:

1. Definition of control hijacking (unauthorized takeover of control over a computer system or network)
2. Overview of control hijacking (types, methods, consequences)
3. Platform defenses against control hijacking (access control, authentication, encryption)
4. Techniques for implementing platform defenses (firewalls, intrusion detection systems, network security)
5. Guidelines for effective platform defenses (regular updates, monitoring, testing)
6. Applications of platform defenses in different fields (banking, healthcare, government)
7. Limitations of platform defenses (complexity of systems, cultural differences)
8. Techniques for overcoming limitations (contextual information, visual aids)
9. Comparison of platform defenses with other security measures (software security, data security)
10. Further reading and resources (books, journals, online resources)

Defense against control hijacking is critical for ensuring the security of computer systems and networks. Understanding the principles and techniques of platform defenses helps students to develop the skills required to secure computer systems effectively and efficiently in a variety of contexts.
