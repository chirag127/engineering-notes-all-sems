### Magnetic Disk

- A magnetic disk is a type of secondary memory that consists of a flat disc with a magnetic coating that stores data .
- It is used to store various programs and files that are not needed by the computer when it is running .
- The magnetic coating can be polarized in one direction or the opposite direction to represent binary data (1 or 0) .
- The disk is divided into circular tracks and sectors that can be accessed by a read/write head that moves over the disk surface.
- The disk rotates at a high speed to allow fast data transfer between the disk and the main memory.
- The advantages of magnetic disk are:
  - It can store large amounts of data at a low cost per unit .
  - It can be easily updated and modified by rewriting the data on the disk .
  - It can be randomly accessed, meaning any data can be retrieved without reading the entire disk .
- The disadvantages of magnetic disk are:
  - It is slower than the main memory, as it requires mechanical movement of the disk and the head .
  - It is prone to physical damage and data loss due to dust, heat, shock, or magnetic interference .
  - It is not portable, as it is fixed inside the computer or requires external power and connection .
