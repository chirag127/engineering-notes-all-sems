## Unit 5 - Input / Output

Unit 5 - Input/Output in the subject of Computer Organization and Architecture covers the design and implementation of input/output (I/O) systems in computer systems. The I/O system is responsible for transferring data between the computer and external devices, such as keyboards, mice, displays, and storage devices.

In the notes for Unit 5, students will learn about the different types of I/O systems, including parallel I/O, serial I/O, and direct memory access (DMA) I/O. They will also learn about the different types of I/O devices, including input devices, output devices, and storage devices, and how they are connected to the computer.

The notes may also cover the implementation of I/O systems, including the use of I/O controllers, I/O interfaces, and I/O protocols to manage the transfer of data between the computer and external devices.

The notes may also cover the performance of I/O systems, including the speed and accuracy of data transfer, and how to optimize the performance of I/O systems for different applications.

Overall, the topic of I/O systems is an important aspect of the study of computer organization and architecture, and is likely to be covered in detail in the notes for Unit 5 - Input/Output in the subject of Computer Organization and Architecture. By learning about I/O systems, students will be equipped with the knowledge and skills they need to understand the design and implementation of I/O systems, and how they affect the performance of computer systems.
