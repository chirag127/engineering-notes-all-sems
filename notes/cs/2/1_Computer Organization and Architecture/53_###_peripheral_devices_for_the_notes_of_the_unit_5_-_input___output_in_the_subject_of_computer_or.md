### Peripheral devices for the notes of the Unit 5 - Input / Output in the subject of Computer Organization and Architecture

"Peripheral Devices" is a topic covered in Unit 5 - Input/Output in the subject of Computer Organization and Architecture.

Peripheral devices are components that are connected to a computer system to expand its capabilities. Peripheral devices can be used for input, output, or storage. Some common types of peripheral devices include:

1. Input Devices: Input devices, such as keyboards and mice, are used to enter data into the computer system.

2. Output Devices: Output devices, such as monitors and printers, are used to display or print data generated by the computer system.

3. Storage Devices: Storage devices, such as hard disk drives and flash drives, are used to store data and programs on the computer system.

In Unit 5 - Input/Output, students will learn about the design and implementation of peripheral devices, including the different types of peripheral devices, and the interfaces used to connect peripheral devices to the computer system.

This knowledge and understanding is important for students pursuing careers in computer science and engineering, as it provides the foundation for more advanced topics in the field of computer organization and architecture, such as computer architecture, computer networks, and computer systems design.
