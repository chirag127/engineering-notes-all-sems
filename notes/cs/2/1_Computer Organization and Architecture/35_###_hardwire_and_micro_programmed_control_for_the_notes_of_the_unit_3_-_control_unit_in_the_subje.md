### Hardwire and micro programmed control for the notes of the Unit 3 - Control Unit in the subject of Computer Organization and Architecture

"Hardwired and Microprogrammed Control" is a topic covered in Unit 3 - Control Unit in the subject of Computer Organization and Architecture.

1. Hardwired Control: Hardwired control is a type of control unit design where the control signals are generated by dedicated circuits and gates. This type of control is characterized by its simplicity and speed, as the control signals are generated directly by the hardware.

2. Microprogrammed Control: Microprogrammed control is a type of control unit design where the control signals are generated by a microprogram stored in a control memory. This type of control is characterized by its flexibility and programmability, as the control signals can be easily modified by changing the microprogram.

In Unit 3 - Control Unit, students will learn about the design and implementation of hardwired and microprogrammed control units, including the advantages and disadvantages of each type of control, and the trade-offs between speed, flexibility, and programmability.

This knowledge and understanding is important for students pursuing careers in computer science and engineering, as it provides the foundation for more advanced topics in the field of computer organization and architecture, such as computer architecture, computer networks, and computer systems design.
