

### Programmed I/O for the notes of the Unit 5 - Input / Output in the subject of Computer Organization and Architecture

* Programmed I/O is a type of input/output (I/O) operation in which the processor initiates the data transfer. It is the most basic form of I/O, where the processor must be involved in the data transfer process. 
* Programmed I/O requires the processor to perform a series of instructions to complete the data transfer. The processor must first send a command to the I/O device, then wait for the device to respond. 
* The processor must then read or write the data from or to the device, and finally check for any errors that may have occurred during the data transfer. 
* Programmed I/O is commonly used for slow devices such as keyboards, printers and modems. 
* Programmed I/O can also be used for faster devices such as hard disks, but it is not the most efficient way to transfer data.
