
### I/O Ports

* I/O ports are physical connection points on a computer which allow communication between the computer and its peripherals. 
* I/O ports can be used to connect a variety of different devices such as keyboards, mice, scanners, printers, and external storage. 
* The most common types of I/O ports are USB, FireWire, and serial ports.
* USB ports are the most common type of I/O port and are used to connect a variety of different devices. 
* FireWire ports are used to connect high-speed devices such as digital cameras and external hard drives. 
* Serial ports are used to connect legacy devices such as modems and printers. 
* I/O ports can also be used to connect external displays such as monitors and projectors.
