
### Fetch and Execute

The fetch and execute cycle is a core concept of computer organization and architecture. It is the process by which a computer retrieves and executes instructions stored in memory. 

The cycle consists of four steps:
1. Fetch - The processor retrieves an instruction from memory.
2. Decode - The instruction is decoded and the necessary data is retrieved.
3. Execute - The processor performs the operation specified by the instruction.
4. Store - The result of the operation is stored in memory.

The fetch and execute cycle is repeated continuously until the program is finished. This cycle is the basis for all computer operations and is the foundation of computer architecture.
