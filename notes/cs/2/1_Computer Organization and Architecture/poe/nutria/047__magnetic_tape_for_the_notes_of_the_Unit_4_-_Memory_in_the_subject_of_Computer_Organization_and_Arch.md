
### Magnetic Tape

Magnetic tape is a storage medium used in computers for long-term data storage. It consists of a thin strip of metal coated with a magnetic material, and is used in a variety of applications, including recording audio and video, computer data storage, and archiving.

Advantages of Magnetic Tape:
- Low cost per unit of storage
- High capacity
- High data transfer rates
- Durability

Disadvantages of Magnetic Tape:
- Limited data access speed
- Susceptible to physical damage
- Prone to magnetic interference
- Requires special equipment to read and write data
