
### Buses

A bus is a communication system that transfers data between components inside a computer, or between computers. Buses can be parallel, meaning that they carry multiple bits of data simultaneously, or serial, meaning that they carry one bit at a time.

* Address Bus: The address bus is used to send the memory address of the data or instruction that the processor needs.

* Data Bus: The data bus is used to send the data or instruction itself.

* Control Bus: The control bus is used to control the flow of data between the processor and memory, as well as between the processor and other components. It can also be used to send signals that indicate when a processor is ready to receive data.

* Expansion Bus: The expansion bus is used to connect additional components, such as video cards, sound cards, and network cards, to the computer.
