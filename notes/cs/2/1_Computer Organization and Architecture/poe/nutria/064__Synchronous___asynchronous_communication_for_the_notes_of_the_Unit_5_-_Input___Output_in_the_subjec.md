
### Synchronous & asynchronous communication

* Synchronous communication is a type of communication in which two or more devices send and receive data simultaneously. This means that all devices must be connected and working at the same time for the communication to take place.

* Asynchronous communication is a type of communication in which two or more devices send and receive data at different times. This means that the devices don't have to be connected or working at the same time for the communication to take place.

* Both synchronous and asynchronous communication are used in computer systems. Synchronous communication is used for real-time applications, such as streaming video or audio, while asynchronous communication is used for applications that don't require real-time data, such as email.

* In computer architecture, synchronous and asynchronous communication are used for input/output (I/O) operations. Synchronous I/O operations are used for devices that require data to be sent and received simultaneously, such as keyboards and mice. Asynchronous I/O operations are used for devices that don't require data to be sent and received simultaneously, such as hard drives and printers.
