
### Peripheral devices for the notes of the Unit 5 - Input / Output in the subject of Computer Organization and Architecture

* Input devices are hardware components that allow users to enter data and commands into a computer. Examples of input devices include keyboards, mice, touchscreens, microphones, scanners, and cameras.
* Output devices are hardware components that allow a computer to communicate with the user. Examples of output devices include monitors, projectors, speakers, and printers.
* Storage devices are hardware components that allow a computer to store data and programs. Examples of storage devices include hard drives, solid state drives, optical drives, and USB flash drives.
* Network devices are hardware components that allow a computer to connect to other computers and to the internet. Examples of network devices include routers, switches, and modems.
