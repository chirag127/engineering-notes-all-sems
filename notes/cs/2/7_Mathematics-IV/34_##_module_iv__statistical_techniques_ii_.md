## Module IV: Statistical Techniques II:

Module IV: Statistical Techniques II:
- This module covers advanced statistical methods for data analysis.
- Topics include: Hypothesis Testing, Analysis of Variance (ANOVA), Regression Analysis, Non-parametric Tests, etc.

Hypothesis Testing:
- A statistical method to test the validity of a claim or hypothesis about a population based on a sample.
- Steps include: Formulating the null and alternative hypotheses, selecting a test statistic, determining the decision rule, and interpreting the results.

Analysis of Variance (ANOVA):
- A method to compare the means of two or more groups to determine if they are significantly different.
- Types: One-way ANOVA, Two-way ANOVA, etc.

Regression Analysis:
- A method to model the relationship between a dependent variable and one or more independent variables.
- Types: Simple Linear Regression, Multiple Linear Regression, Logistic Regression, etc.

Non-parametric Tests:
- Statistical tests that do not assume a specific distribution for the data.
- Examples: Wilcoxon Rank Sum Test, Kruskal-Wallis Test, etc.
