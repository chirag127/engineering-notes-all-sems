### Binomial for the notes of the Module IV: Statistical Techniques II: in the subject of Mathematics-IV KCS

Module IV of the Mathematics-IV KCS course covers the topic of Statistical Techniques II, including the Binomial distribution.

The Binomial distribution is a discrete probability distribution that models the number of successes in a fixed number of independent trials, where each trial has only two possible outcomes, such as success or failure. The Binomial distribution is characterized by two parameters: the number of trials, n, and the probability of success in each trial, p.

In the context of the Mathematics-IV KCS course, students will learn about the Binomial distribution as part of Module IV. This may include working with examples of the Binomial distribution, calculating probabilities and cumulative probabilities, and analyzing the behavior of the Binomial distribution for different values of n and p.
