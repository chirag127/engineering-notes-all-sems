KCS

KCS (Knowledge-Centered Support) is a customer service methodology that focuses on creating, managing and sharing knowledge as the primary means of resolving customer inquiries. The goal of KCS is to provide a consistent and efficient customer experience by leveraging knowledge from previous interactions to resolve customer inquiries faster and more accurately.

KCS is based on several key principles, including:

1. Knowledge as the primary means of resolving customer inquiries: In KCS, knowledge is treated as the primary means of resolving customer inquiries, rather than relying on individual expertise or trial-and-error. 

2. Knowledge creation and management: KCS emphasizes the creation and management of knowledge as a critical component of customer service. This includes capturing and organizing knowledge from customer interactions, and making it easily accessible to customer service agents.

3. Collaboration and continuous improvement: KCS encourages collaboration between customer service agents and other stakeholders, such as product teams, to continuously improve the quality and relevance of knowledge.

4. Measuring success: KCS uses metrics to measure the effectiveness of knowledge-centered support, such as the number of customer inquiries resolved using knowledge, the time to resolution, and customer satisfaction.

KCS is typically implemented as a customer service platform, which provides tools for creating, managing, and sharing knowledge, as well as metrics to measure the effectiveness of knowledge-centered support.

In conclusion, KCS is a customer service methodology that focuses on creating, managing and sharing knowledge as the primary means of resolving customer inquiries. It provides a consistent and efficient customer experience by leveraging knowledge from previous interactions, and encourages collaboration and continuous improvement.
