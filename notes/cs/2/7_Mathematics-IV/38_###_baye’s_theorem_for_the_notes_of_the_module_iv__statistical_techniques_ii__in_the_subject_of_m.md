### Baye’s theorem for the notes of the Module IV: Statistical Techniques II: in the subject of Mathematics-IV KCS

Module IV of the Mathematics-IV KCS course covers the topic of Statistical Techniques II. One of the topics in this module is Baye’s theorem.

Baye’s theorem is a mathematical formula that relates the probability of an event to the prior probability of the event and the likelihood of the event given some evidence. Baye’s theorem is used in Bayesian statistics to update the probabilities of events based on new evidence.

Baye’s theorem is expressed as:

P(A|B) = (P(B|A) * P(A)) / P(B)

where P(A|B) is the posterior probability of event A given evidence B, P(B|A) is the likelihood of evidence B given event A, P(A) is the prior probability of event A, and P(B) is the prior probability of evidence B.
