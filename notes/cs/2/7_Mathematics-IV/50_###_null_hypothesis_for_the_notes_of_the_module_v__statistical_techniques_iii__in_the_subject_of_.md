### Null hypothesis for the notes of the Module V: Statistical Techniques III: in the subject of Mathematics-IV KCS

Module V of the Mathematics-IV KCS course covers the topic of Statistical Techniques III, including hypothesis testing. The null hypothesis is a fundamental concept in hypothesis testing.

The null hypothesis is a statement about a population parameter that is tested using statistical methods. The null hypothesis is typically the default or initial assumption that is made about a population parameter, and is assumed to be true until proven otherwise.

In hypothesis testing, the goal is to determine whether the null hypothesis can be rejected based on the evidence provided by a sample of data. If the evidence provided by the sample supports the null hypothesis, then the null hypothesis is not rejected. If the evidence provided by the sample does not support the null hypothesis, then the null hypothesis is rejected.

In the context of the Mathematics-IV KCS course, students will learn about the null hypothesis as part of Module V. This may include working with examples of hypothesis testing, formulating null hypotheses, and testing null hypotheses using statistical methods.
