### Measures of central tendency for the notes of the Module III: Statistical Techniques I: in the subject of Mathematics-IV KCS

The Module III: Statistical Techniques I covers the following topic:

1. Measures of Central Tendency:
Measures of central tendency are statistical measures that describe the center of a set of data. There are three main measures of central tendency: mean, median, and mode.

2. Mean:
The mean is the sum of all the data points divided by the number of data points. It is the most commonly used measure of central tendency.

3. Median:
The median is the middle value of a set of data when the data is arranged in numerical order. If the number of data points is odd, the median is the middle value. If the number of data points is even, the median is the average of the two middle values.

4. Mode:
The mode is the value that appears most frequently in a set of data. A set of data can have one mode, multiple modes, or no mode.

It is important to note that the mean, median, and mode are not always the same, and the choice of measure of central tendency depends on the distribution of the data and the purpose of the analysis.
