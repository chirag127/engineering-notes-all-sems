## Module V: Statistical Techniques III:

Module V: Statistical Techniques III:

- Topics covered in this module include regression analysis, correlation, and hypothesis testing.

Regression Analysis:
- A statistical method used to model the relationship between a dependent variable and one or more independent variables.
- Linear and non-linear regression are two common types.
- Regression analysis is used to make predictions and understand the impact of independent variables on the dependent variable.

Correlation:
- A statistical measure of the relationship between two variables.
- Correlation can be positive, negative, or zero.
- Correlation is used to understand the relationship between variables and to make predictions.

Hypothesis Testing:
- A statistical method used to test a claim or hypothesis about a population parameter.
- Hypothesis testing involves formulating null and alternative hypotheses, selecting a sample, and making decisions based on the sample data.
- Common hypothesis tests include t-tests, ANOVA, and chi-squared tests.

- This module also covers advanced topics such as multiple regression, logistic regression, and time series analysis.
