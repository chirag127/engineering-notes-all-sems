# Linear and Non Linear Partial Equations of first order for the notes of the Module I: Partial Differential Equations in the subject of Mathematics-IV KCS

1. A partial differential equation (PDE) is an equation involving partial derivatives of an unknown function of several independent variables.
2. A first-order PDE is an equation of the form F(x, y, u, u_x, u_y) = 0, where u_x and u_y are the first partial derivatives of u with respect to x and y, respectively.
3. A PDE is said to be linear if it is linear in the unknown function u and its partial derivatives. Otherwise, it is called non-linear.
4. The general form of a linear first-order PDE is a(x, y)u_x + b(x, y)u_y + c(x, y)u = f(x, y).
5. The method of characteristics can be used to solve linear first-order PDEs.
6. Non-linear first-order PDEs can be more challenging to solve and may require advanced techniques such as the method of characteristics, separation of variables, or numerical methods.
