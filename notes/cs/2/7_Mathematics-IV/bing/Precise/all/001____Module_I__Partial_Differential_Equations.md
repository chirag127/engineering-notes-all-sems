## Module I: Partial Differential Equations

1. **Definition:** A partial differential equation (PDE) is a mathematical equation that involves two or more independent variables, an unknown function (dependent on those variables), and partial derivatives of the unknown function with respect to the independent variables.

2. **Classification:** PDEs can be classified into several types, including linear and nonlinear, homogeneous and non-homogeneous, and first-order and higher-order.

3. **Solving PDEs:** There are several methods for solving PDEs, including separation of variables, method of characteristics, and numerical methods.

4. **Applications:** PDEs have numerous applications in various fields, including physics, engineering, and finance. Some common examples include the heat equation, wave equation, and Black-Scholes equation.

5. **Further Study:** For a more in-depth study of PDEs, one can refer to textbooks such as "Partial Differential Equations" by Lawrence C. Evans or "An Introduction to Partial Differential Equations" by Michael Renardy and Robert C. Rogers.
