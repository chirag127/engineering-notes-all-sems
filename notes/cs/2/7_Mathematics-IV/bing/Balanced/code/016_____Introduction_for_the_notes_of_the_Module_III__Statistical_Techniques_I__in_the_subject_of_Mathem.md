- In this module, we will learn about some basic concepts and methods of statistics, which are useful for analyzing and interpreting data.
- Statistics is the science of collecting, organizing, summarizing, and drawing conclusions from data. It can also be used to make predictions and decisions based on data.
- There are two main branches of statistics: descriptive statistics and inferential statistics.
- Descriptive statistics deals with summarizing and displaying data using numerical measures, tables, graphs, and charts. It helps us to understand the main features and patterns of the data.
- Inferential statistics deals with drawing conclusions and making generalizations about a population based on a sample of data. It helps us to test hypotheses and estimate parameters using probability theory and statistical tests.
- Some of the topics we will cover in this module are:

  - Measures of central tendency, such as mean, median, and mode, which describe the center or typical value of a data set.
  - Measures of dispersion, such as range, variance, and standard deviation, which describe the spread or variability of a data set.
  - Measures of skewness and kurtosis, which describe the shape or symmetry of a data distribution.
  - Probability distributions, such as binomial, Poisson, normal, and t-distributions, which model the behavior of random variables and events.
  - Sampling techniques, such as simple random sampling, stratified sampling, and cluster sampling, which are used to select a representative subset of a population for data collection.
  - Sampling distributions, such as the sampling distribution of the mean and the sampling distribution of the proportion, which describe the distribution of a statistic computed from a sample.
  - Point estimation and interval estimation, which are methods of estimating an unknown population parameter using a sample statistic and a margin of error.
  - Hypothesis testing, which is a method of testing a claim or a statement about a population parameter using a sample statistic and a significance level.
  - Correlation and regression, which are methods of measuring and modeling the relationship between two or more variables.
