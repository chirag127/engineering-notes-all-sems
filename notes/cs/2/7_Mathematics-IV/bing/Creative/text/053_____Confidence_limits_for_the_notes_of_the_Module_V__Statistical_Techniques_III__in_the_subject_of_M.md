### Confidence limits

- Confidence limits are a pair of numbers that specify the upper and lower bounds of a confidence interval.
- A confidence interval is a range of values that contains the true parameter of a population with a given probability, based on a sample statistic .
- The probability of the confidence interval is called the confidence level, and is usually expressed as a percentage, such as 95% or 99% .
- The confidence level indicates how often the confidence interval would contain the true parameter if the same sampling procedure was repeated many times .
- The confidence limits depend on the sample size, the sample variability, the confidence level, and the sampling distribution of the statistic .
- The confidence limits can be calculated using formulas, tables, or software, depending on the type of statistic and the distribution involved .
- The confidence limits can be used to assess the precision, accuracy, and reliability of an estimate, as well as to test hypotheses and make inferences about the population .
