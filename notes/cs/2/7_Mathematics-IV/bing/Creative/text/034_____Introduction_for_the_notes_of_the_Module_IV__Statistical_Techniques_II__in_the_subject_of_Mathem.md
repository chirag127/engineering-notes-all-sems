### Introduction for the notes of the Module IV: Statistical Techniques II in the subject of Mathematics-IV KCS

- In this module, you will learn about some advanced statistical techniques that are useful for data analysis and inference.
- You will learn how to perform hypothesis testing, which is a method of making decisions based on data and a predefined criterion.
- You will also learn how to use analysis of variance (ANOVA), which is a technique for comparing the means of several groups of data.
- Additionally, you will learn how to apply regression analysis, which is a technique for modeling the relationship between a dependent variable and one or more independent variables.
- Finally, you will learn how to use correlation analysis, which is a technique for measuring the strength and direction of the linear association between two variables.
- These techniques will help you to understand the patterns and trends in your data, and to draw meaningful conclusions from them.
