## Module III: Statistical Techniques I:

Module III of the course on Statistical Techniques I focuses on the basics of statistical analysis, including descriptive statistics, probability distributions, and hypothesis testing.

In this module, students will learn about:

1. Descriptive Statistics: This covers the use of summary statistics, such as mean, median, and standard deviation, to describe and summarize data.

2. Probability Distributions: This covers the concept of probability distributions, including normal, binomial, and Poisson distributions, and their properties.

3. Hypothesis Testing: This covers the process of testing statistical hypotheses, including the formulation of null and alternative hypotheses, the calculation of test statistics, and the interpretation of p-values.

In the context of the course, students will learn about statistical techniques through interactive activities, lectures, and hands-on experiences. This may include working with simulations, conducting data analysis, and interpreting the results of statistical tests.

In summary, Module III of the course on Statistical Techniques I focuses on the basics of statistical analysis, including descriptive statistics, probability distributions, and hypothesis testing. Students will learn about these concepts through interactive activities, lectures, and hands-on experiences.
