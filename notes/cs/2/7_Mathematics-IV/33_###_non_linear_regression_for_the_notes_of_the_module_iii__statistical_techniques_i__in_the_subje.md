### non linear regression for the notes of the Module III: Statistical Techniques I: in the subject of Mathematics-IV KCS

Non-linear regression is a statistical technique used to model the relationship between a dependent variable and one or more independent variables. Unlike linear regression, which models the relationship as a straight line, non-linear regression models the relationship as a non-linear function.

The non-linear regression model is specified by a functional form, which is a mathematical equation that describes the relationship between the dependent variable and the independent variables. The functional form is typically chosen based on prior knowledge of the system being modeled, or by trial and error.

Once the functional form is chosen, the parameters of the model are estimated using numerical optimization techniques, such as maximum likelihood estimation or gradient descent. The estimated parameters are then used to make predictions about the dependent variable based on the independent variables.

Non-linear regression is used in a variety of applications, including the modeling of complex physical systems, the analysis of biological data, and the prediction of financial outcomes. It is particularly useful when the relationship between the dependent variable and the independent variables is not well described by a linear model.

In this module, we will study the concept of non-linear regression, including the specification of the functional form and the estimation of the parameters. We will also examine the algorithms used to perform non-linear regression, and explore the applications of non-linear regression in various fields. This will provide a foundation for understanding the use of statistical techniques for modeling complex relationships, and for exploring the various applications of statistical techniques in the field of mathematics and science.
