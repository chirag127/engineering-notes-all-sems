### Statistical Quality Control (SQC) for the notes of the Module V: Statistical Techniques III: in the subject of Mathematics-IV KCS

Module V of the Mathematics-IV KCS course covers the topic of Statistical Techniques III, including statistical quality control (SQC).

Statistical quality control (SQC) is a method used to monitor and control the quality of a process or product. SQC uses statistical methods to monitor the process or product and detect any changes or deviations from the desired quality level.

SQC includes several techniques, including control charts, process capability analysis, and acceptance sampling. Control charts are used to monitor the process and detect changes in the process over time. Process capability analysis is used to determine whether a process is capable of producing products that meet the desired quality level. Acceptance sampling is used to determine whether a batch of products meets the desired quality level.

In the context of the Mathematics-IV KCS course, students will learn about statistical quality control (SQC) as part of Module V. This may include working with examples of SQC, using SQC techniques to monitor and control quality, and interpreting the results of SQC analyses.
