One common method of solving linear PDEs of higher order with constant coefficients is the method of characteristic lines. In this method, the solution is expressed as a linear combination of exponential functions, and the coefficients are determined by imposing initial and boundary conditions.

Another method of solving linear PDEs of higher order with constant coefficients is the method of eigenfunction expansions, in which the solution is expressed as a linear combination of eigenfunctions of the linear operator associated with the PDE. The coefficients are determined by imposing initial and boundary conditions.

A third method of solving linear PDEs of higher order with constant coefficients is the method of Green's functions, in which the solution is expressed as an integral involving the Green's function of the linear operator associated with the PDE. The Green's function is determined by solving a related homogeneous PDE with appropriate boundary conditions.
