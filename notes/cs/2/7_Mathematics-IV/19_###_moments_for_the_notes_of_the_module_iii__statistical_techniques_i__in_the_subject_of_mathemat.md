### Moments for the notes of the Module III: Statistical Techniques I: in the subject of Mathematics-IV KCS

Moments:
- Moments are measures of the shape of a distribution.
- Types of Moments: Raw Moments, Central Moments, Standardized Moments, Skewness, Kurtosis.
- Raw Moments: Measure of the distribution about the origin.
- Central Moments: Measure of the distribution about the mean.
- Standardized Moments: Central moments divided by standard deviation.
- Skewness: Measure of asymmetry in a distribution.
- Kurtosis: Measure of peakedness or flatness of a distribution.

Module III: Statistical Techniques I:
- Covers various statistical techniques used in data analysis.
- Topics may include: Descriptive Statistics, Probability Distributions, Sampling, Estimation, Hypothesis Testing, ANOVA, and Regression Analysis.
- Descriptive Statistics: Summarizing and describing data using measures such as mean, median, and standard deviation.
- Probability Distributions: Models for the probability of occurrence of different values in a dataset.
- Sampling: Selecting a subset of data from a larger population to make inferences about the population.
- Estimation: Determining the value of a population parameter from a sample.
- Hypothesis Testing: Testing claims about a population based on sample data.
- ANOVA: Analysis of variance, comparing means of multiple groups.
- Regression Analysis: Modeling the relationship between a dependent variable and one or more independent variables.
