### Equations of Transmission lines for the notes of the Module II: Applications of Partial Differential Equations: in the subject of Mathematics-IV KCS

Transmission lines are an important component of electrical power systems, used to transmit electrical energy over long distances. The behavior of transmission lines can be modeled using partial differential equations (PDEs), which describe the distribution of voltage and current along the line.

The most common PDE used to model transmission lines is the telegrapher's equation, which describes the relationship between voltage and current in an electrical circuit. This equation is a first-order linear PDE that can be solved using various numerical methods, such as finite difference or finite element methods.

Another important equation used to model transmission lines is the wave equation, which describes the propagation of electromagnetic waves along the line. This equation is a second-order linear PDE that can be solved using techniques such as the method of characteristics or the method of eigenfunction expansions.

In this module, we will study the equations of transmission lines, including the telegrapher's equation and the wave equation. We will examine the numerical methods used to solve these equations, and explore the applications of these equations in the analysis and design of electrical power systems. This will provide a foundation for understanding the use of mathematical techniques for modeling and analyzing the behavior of transmission lines, and for exploring the various applications of PDEs in the field of electrical engineering.
