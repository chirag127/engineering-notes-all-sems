### Addition and multiplication law of probability for the notes of the Module IV: Statistical Techniques II: in the subject of Mathematics-IV KCS

Module IV of the Mathematics-IV KCS course covers the topic of Statistical Techniques II, including the laws of probability. The two main laws of probability are the addition law and the multiplication law.

The addition law of probability states that the probability of the union of two or more mutually exclusive events is equal to the sum of their individual probabilities. Mathematically, this is expressed as:

P(A or B) = P(A) + P(B)

The multiplication law of probability states that the probability of two events occurring simultaneously is equal to the product of their individual probabilities. Mathematically, this is expressed as:

P(A and B) = P(A) * P(B)
