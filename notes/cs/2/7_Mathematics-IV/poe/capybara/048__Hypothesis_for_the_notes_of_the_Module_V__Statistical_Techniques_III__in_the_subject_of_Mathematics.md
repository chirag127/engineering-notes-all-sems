### Hypothesis for the notes of the Module V: Statistical Techniques III: in the subject of Mathematics-IV KCS

Hypothesis testing is a fundamental concept in statistical analysis. It helps in making decisions based on the available data. In the context of the module V of Statistical Techniques III, we will explore various hypothesis testing methods. Here are the hypothesis that we will learn about:

1. **Null Hypothesis (H0):** This hypothesis states that there is no significant difference between the sample data and the population data. It is denoted by H0. 

2. **Alternative Hypothesis (Ha):** This hypothesis states that there is a significant difference between the sample data and the population data. It is denoted by Ha. 

3. **One-tailed Hypothesis:** This hypothesis is used when we want to test a directional hypothesis. It is also called a directional hypothesis. 

4. **Two-tailed Hypothesis:** This hypothesis is used when we want to test a non-directional hypothesis. It is also called a non-directional hypothesis. 

5. **Type I Error (α-error):** This error occurs when we reject the null hypothesis when it is true. It is also called a false positive. 

6. **Type II Error (β-error):** This error occurs when we fail to reject the null hypothesis when it is false. It is also called a false negative. 

7. **Level of Significance (α):** It is the probability of making a Type I Error. It is usually set at 0.05 or 0.01. 

8. **P-value:** It is the probability of obtaining a sample statistic as extreme or more extreme than the observed value, assuming that the null hypothesis is true. 

9. **Critical Region:** It is the region in which the observed test statistic falls under the rejection of the null hypothesis. 

10. **Test Statistic:** It is the value calculated from the sample data that is used to test the hypothesis. 

In conclusion, the above hypothesis will enable us to understand various aspects of hypothesis testing. It will help us in making informed decisions based on the available data.
