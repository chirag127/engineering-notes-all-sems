### Addition and Multiplication Law of Probability

Probability is an important concept in statistics and mathematics. It is used to measure the likelihood of an event occurring. In this module, we will discuss the addition and multiplication law of probability.

#### Addition Law of Probability

The addition law of probability is used to find the probability of the occurrence of one or more events. It states that the probability of the union of two or more events is the sum of their individual probabilities minus the probability of their intersection.

The formula for the addition law of probability is:

P(A or B) = P(A) + P(B) - P(A and B)

where P(A) and P(B) are the probabilities of events A and B, respectively, and P(A and B) is the probability of their intersection.

#### Multiplication Law of Probability

The multiplication law of probability is used to find the probability of the occurrence of two or more independent events. It states that the probability of the intersection of two or more independent events is the product of their individual probabilities.

The formula for the multiplication law of probability is:

P(A and B) = P(A) × P(B)

where P(A) and P(B) are the probabilities of events A and B, respectively.

#### Examples:

1. A bag contains 4 red balls and 6 blue balls. What is the probability of picking a red ball or a blue ball?

Solution:

P(red or blue) = P(red) + P(blue) - P(red and blue)

P(red or blue) = 4/10 + 6/10 - 0

P(red or blue) = 1

Therefore, the probability of picking a red ball or a blue ball is 1.

2. A coin is tossed twice. What is the probability of getting two heads?

Solution:

P(heads on first toss) = 1/2

P(heads on second toss) = 1/2

P(two heads) = P(heads on first toss) × P(heads on second toss)

P(two heads) = 1/2 × 1/2

P(two heads) = 1/4

Therefore, the probability of getting two heads is 1/4.

In conclusion, the addition and multiplication law of probability are important concepts in statistics and mathematics. They are used to find the probability of the occurrence of one or more events and two or more independent events, respectively.
