### Poisson Distribution

Poisson Distribution is a statistical distribution that is used to model the number of times an event occurs in a given time period. It is a discrete probability distribution that is often used in situations where events occur randomly and independently over a period of time.

#### Characteristics of Poisson Distribution
- The events occur randomly and independently over time.
- The probability of an event occurring is the same for any given time interval.
- The events are rare, meaning that the probability of two or more events occurring at the same time is negligible.
- The expected number of events in a given time period is constant.

#### Formula for Poisson Distribution
The formula for Poisson Distribution is given as:

P(X = x) = (e^(-λ) * λ^x) / x!

where, 
- x is the number of events that occur in a given time period.
- λ is the expected number of events that occur in that time period.
- e is the mathematical constant e ≈ 2.71828.

#### Mean and Variance of Poisson Distribution
The mean and variance of a Poisson Distribution are given by:

Mean = λ
Variance = λ

#### Applications of Poisson Distribution
Poisson Distribution is widely used in various fields like:
- Insurance: To model the number of claims that may occur in a given time period.
- Telecommunications: To model the number of phone calls arriving at a call center.
- Manufacturing: To model the number of defects in a product.
- Biology: To model the number of mutations in a DNA sequence.

#### Limitations of Poisson Distribution
- It assumes that the events occur randomly and independently over time, which may not always be the case.
- It is only applicable to rare events, meaning that the probability of two or more events occurring at the same time is negligible.
- It cannot be used to model events that occur continuously over time.
