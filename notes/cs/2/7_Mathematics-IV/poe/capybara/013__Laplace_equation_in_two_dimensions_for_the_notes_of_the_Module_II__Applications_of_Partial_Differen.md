### Laplace equation in two dimensions

The Laplace equation in two dimensions is a partial differential equation that describes the steady-state temperature distribution in a two-dimensional region. It is given by:

```
∇²u(x,y) = 0
```

where `∇²` is the Laplacian operator, `u(x,y)` is the temperature at point `(x,y)` in the region.

#### Properties of Laplace equation

- The Laplace equation is a second-order partial differential equation.
- It is a linear equation, which means that if `u₁` and `u₂` are solutions to the Laplace equation, then `au₁ + bu₂` is also a solution, where `a` and `b` are constants.
- The Laplace equation is homogeneous, which means that if `u` is a solution, then `ku` is also a solution, where `k` is a constant.
- The Laplace equation is elliptic, which means that it has a unique solution for given boundary conditions.

#### Solution of Laplace equation

The Laplace equation can be solved using a variety of methods, including:

- Separation of variables
- Fourier series
- Complex analysis

The solution to the Laplace equation is known as the harmonic function, which satisfies the Laplace equation.
