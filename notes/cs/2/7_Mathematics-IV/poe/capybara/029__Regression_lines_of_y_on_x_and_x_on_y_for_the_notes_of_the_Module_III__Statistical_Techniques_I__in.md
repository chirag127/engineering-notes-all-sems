### Regression lines of y on x and x on y for the notes of the Module III: Statistical Techniques I: in the subject of Mathematics-IV KCS

In the field of statistics, regression analysis is a widely-used technique for studying the relationship between two variables. The regression line is a line that best fits the data points in a scatter plot, and it can be used to make predictions about the value of one variable based on the value of another variable.

In this module, we will be discussing the regression lines of y on x and x on y. Here are some important points to keep in mind:

#### Regression line of y on x:
- The regression line of y on x is a line that best fits the data points when y is the dependent variable and x is the independent variable.
- This line is found by minimizing the sum of the squared distances between the data points and the line.
- The equation of the regression line of y on x is given by y = a + bx, where a is the y-intercept and b is the slope of the line.
- The slope of the line (b) represents the change in y for every unit change in x. A positive slope indicates a positive relationship between the variables, while a negative slope indicates a negative relationship.

#### Regression line of x on y:
- The regression line of x on y is a line that best fits the data points when x is the dependent variable and y is the independent variable.
- This line is found by minimizing the sum of the squared distances between the data points and the line.
- The equation of the regression line of x on y is given by x = c + dy, where c is the x-intercept and d is the slope of the line.
- The slope of the line (d) represents the change in x for every unit change in y. A positive slope indicates a positive relationship between the variables, while a negative slope indicates a negative relationship.

It is important to note that the regression line does not necessarily imply causation between the variables. It only shows the relationship between them and can be used for making predictions.

In conclusion, the regression lines of y on x and x on y are important tools for analyzing the relationship between two variables. They can be used to make predictions and are found by minimizing the sum of the squared distances between the data points and the line. It is important to understand the equations and interpretation of the slope when working with regression lines.
