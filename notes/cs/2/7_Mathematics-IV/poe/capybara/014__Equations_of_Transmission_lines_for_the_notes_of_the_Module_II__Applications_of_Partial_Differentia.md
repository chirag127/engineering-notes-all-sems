### Equations of Transmission lines

Transmission lines are used to transmit electrical signals from one point to another. There are two types of transmission lines: distributed and lumped. In this module, we will focus on distributed transmission lines.

#### Governing equations

The governing equations for transmission lines are the Telegrapher's equations. These equations describe the propagation of electromagnetic waves along a transmission line. The Telegrapher's equations are given by:

$$\frac{\partial V}{\partial z}=-L\frac{\partial I}{\partial t}-R I$$
$$\frac{\partial I}{\partial z}=-C\frac{\partial V}{\partial t}-G V$$

where V is the voltage, I is the current, L is the inductance per unit length, C is the capacitance per unit length, R is the resistance per unit length, G is the conductance per unit length, and z is the distance along the transmission line.

#### Wave equation

The Telegrapher's equations can be combined to give the wave equation:

$$\frac{\partial^2 V}{\partial z^2}=(LC)\frac{\partial^2 V}{\partial t^2}+(LG+RC)\frac{\partial V}{\partial t}+RGV$$

This equation describes the propagation of electromagnetic waves along a transmission line. The wave equation can be solved using various techniques such as the method of characteristics, separation of variables, and Fourier analysis.

#### Boundary conditions

Boundary conditions are required to solve the wave equation. The two most common boundary conditions are:

1. The voltage and current are continuous at the boundary between two transmission lines.
2. The voltage and current are zero at the end of the transmission line.

#### Applications

Transmission lines are used in various applications such as:

1. Telecommunications: Transmission lines are used to transmit signals in telephone networks, internet networks, and cable television networks.
2. Power transmission: Transmission lines are used to transmit electrical power from power plants to homes and businesses.
3. Antennas: Transmission lines are used to connect antennas to radios and televisions.
