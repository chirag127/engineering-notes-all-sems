### Introduction

In this module, we will be diving deeper into statistical techniques and exploring more advanced methods of analyzing data. Here are some key points to keep in mind:

- Statistical techniques are used to analyze and interpret data in order to make informed decisions.
- In this module, we will be focusing on two main areas: regression analysis and hypothesis testing.
- Regression analysis is a statistical method used to examine the relationship between one dependent variable and one or more independent variables. It is commonly used in fields such as economics, finance, and social sciences.
- Hypothesis testing is a statistical method used to determine whether a hypothesis about a population parameter is supported by the sample data. It is a critical tool in scientific research and decision-making.
- In order to understand these techniques, it is important to have a strong foundation in basic statistical concepts such as probability, random variables, and sampling distributions.
- We will also be using software such as R or Python to perform statistical analyses and create visualizations of the data.
- It is important to approach statistical analysis with a critical and skeptical mindset, and to always consider the limitations and assumptions of the methods being used.
- By the end of this module, you will have a deeper understanding of statistical techniques and be able to apply them to real-world data analysis problems.
