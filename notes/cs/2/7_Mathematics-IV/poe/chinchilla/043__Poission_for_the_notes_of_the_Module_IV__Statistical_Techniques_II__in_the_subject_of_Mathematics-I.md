The Poisson distribution is a discrete probability distribution that is used to model the number of events that occur in a fixed interval of time or space. It is named after French mathematician Siméon Denis Poisson, who first introduced it in the early 19th century.

Here are some key points to remember about the Poisson distribution:

- The Poisson distribution is used to model the number of events that occur in a fixed interval of time or space, where the events occur randomly and independently of each other.
- The Poisson distribution has a single parameter, usually denoted by λ (lambda), which represents the average number of events that occur in the interval. The Poisson distribution assumes that the probability of an event occurring is proportional to the length of the interval, and that the events occur independently of each other.
- The probability mass function (PMF) of the Poisson distribution is given by P(X = k) = (λ^k * e^(-λ))/k!, where X is the random variable representing the number of events, k is a non-negative integer, λ is the parameter representing the average number of events, and e is the mathematical constant approximately equal to 2.718.
- The mean and variance of the Poisson distribution are both equal to λ. This means that if the average number of events in a fixed interval is λ, then the expected value and variance of the number of events are both λ.
- The Poisson distribution is often used to model rare events, such as accidents, defects, or failures. It can also be used to model the number of customers arriving at a store or the number of phone calls received by a call center in a fixed interval of time.
- The Poisson distribution can be approximated by the normal distribution when the parameter λ is large. This is known as the Poisson approximation to the normal distribution. The approximation is good when λ is greater than or equal to 10.
- The Poisson distribution has many applications in different fields, such as biology, physics, finance, and engineering. It is an important tool for analyzing and understanding random events that occur in a fixed interval of time or space.

In summary, the Poisson distribution is a useful tool for modeling the number of events that occur in a fixed interval of time or space. It is a simple yet powerful distribution that has many applications in different fields.
