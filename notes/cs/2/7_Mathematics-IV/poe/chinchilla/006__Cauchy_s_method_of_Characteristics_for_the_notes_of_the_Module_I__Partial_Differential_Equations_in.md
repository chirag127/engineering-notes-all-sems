1. **Introduction to Cauchy's Method of Characteristics**

   - Cauchy's method of characteristics is a technique used to solve first-order partial differential equations.
   - The method involves the use of characteristic curves, which are curves along which the solution to the partial differential equation is constant.

   - By combining the solutions along the characteristic curves, we can obtain the general solution to the partial differential equation.
