

## Module III: Statistical Techniques I:

In this module, you will learn about various statistical techniques that are used to analyze and interpret data. These techniques are essential for understanding the relationships between different variables and making informed decisions based on the data.

Here are some of the key points to keep in mind when studying statistical techniques:

- Statistical techniques are used to analyze data and identify patterns and relationships between variables.
- Descriptive statistics are used to summarize and describe the characteristics of a dataset, while inferential statistics are used to make predictions and generalizations based on a sample of data.
- Measures of central tendency, such as the mean, median, and mode, are used to describe the average value of a dataset.
- Measures of dispersion, such as the range, variance, and standard deviation, are used to describe the spread of data around the mean.
- Probability distributions, such as the normal distribution, are used to model the probability of different outcomes in a dataset.
- Hypothesis testing is used to determine whether there is a significant difference between two or more groups or variables.
- Correlation and regression analysis are used to determine the strength and direction of the relationship between two or more variables.

Remember to practice applying these techniques to real-world datasets and problems to develop a deeper understanding of how they work and how they can be used to make informed decisions.
