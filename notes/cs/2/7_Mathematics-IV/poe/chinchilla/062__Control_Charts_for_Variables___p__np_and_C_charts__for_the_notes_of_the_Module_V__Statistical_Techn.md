### Control Charts for Variables (p, np, and C charts)

A control chart is a statistical tool that helps in monitoring a process and detecting any changes in the process. The Control charts for variables are used to monitor the quality of a product or process when the data collected is in the form of continuous measurements.

In this module, we will discuss three types of control charts for variables: p charts, np charts, and C charts.

#### P charts
- P charts are used when the data is in the form of proportions or percentages.
- The P chart is used to monitor the proportion of defective items in a sample.
- The chart is constructed by plotting the proportion of defective items in a sample on the y-axis and the sample number on the x-axis.
- The upper and lower control limits are calculated based on the sample size and the desired level of control.
- Any point that falls outside the control limits indicates that the process is out of control.

#### Np charts
- Np charts are used when the data is in the form of counts.
- The Np chart is used to monitor the number of defective items in a sample.
- The chart is constructed by plotting the number of defective items in a sample on the y-axis and the sample number on the x-axis.
- The upper and lower control limits are calculated based on the sample size and the desired level of control.
- Any point that falls outside the control limits indicates that the process is out of control.

#### C charts
- C charts are used when the data is in the form of counts.
- The C chart is used to monitor the number of defects in a sample.
- The chart is constructed by plotting the number of defects in a sample on the y-axis and the sample number on the x-axis.
- The upper and lower control limits are calculated based on the sample size and the desired level of control.
- Any point that falls outside the control limits indicates that the process is out of control.

In conclusion, Control Charts for Variables are essential tools in monitoring the quality of a product or process. The type of chart used depends on the type of data collected. By using these charts, any changes in the process can be detected early, and appropriate action can be taken to maintain the quality of the product or process.
