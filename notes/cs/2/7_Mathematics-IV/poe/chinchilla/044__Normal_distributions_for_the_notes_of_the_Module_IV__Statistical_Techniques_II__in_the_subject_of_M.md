

### Normal Distributions for the Notes of the Module IV: Statistical Techniques II: in the Subject of Mathematics-IV KCS

Normal distributions are an essential part of statistics and are widely used to analyze and interpret data. In this module, we will learn about normal distributions and their properties. Here are some important points to keep in mind:

- A normal distribution is a continuous probability distribution that has a bell-shaped curve.
- The bell-shaped curve is symmetrical, meaning that the mean, median, and mode are all equal.
- The mean, denoted by μ, represents the center of the distribution.
- The standard deviation, denoted by σ, measures the spread of the distribution.
- The area under the curve is equal to 1, meaning that the total probability of all possible outcomes is 1.
- The empirical rule, also known as the 68-95-99.7 rule, states that for a normal distribution, approximately 68% of the data falls within one standard deviation of the mean, 95% within two standard deviations, and 99.7% within three standard deviations.
- The normal distribution is widely used in statistical inference, including hypothesis testing and confidence interval estimation.

To summarize, the normal distribution is an important concept in statistics that helps us to understand and analyze data. By understanding its properties, we can better interpret and draw conclusions from our data.
