### Probability mass function and Probability density function

In statistical analysis, probability plays a significant role in determining the likelihood of an event occurring. Probability mass function (PMF) and probability density function (PDF) are two essential concepts that help in understanding the probability distribution of a random variable. In this module, we will learn about the PMF and PDF and their applications in statistical analysis.

#### Probability Mass Function (PMF)

A probability mass function (PMF) is a function that gives the probability of a discrete random variable taking on a specific value. It is denoted by P(X = x), where X is the random variable and x is the specific value that it can take. The PMF satisfies the following properties:

- The PMF of a random variable X is defined for all possible values of X.
- The sum of the probabilities of all possible values of X is equal to 1.
- The PMF is non-negative for all possible values of X.

#### Probability Density Function (PDF)

A probability density function (PDF) is a function that gives the probability density of a continuous random variable at a particular point. It is denoted by f(x), where x is the point in the domain of X. The PDF satisfies the following properties:

- The PDF of a random variable X is defined for all possible values of X.
- The area under the PDF curve between any two points a and b gives the probability of X lying between a and b.
- The PDF is non-negative for all possible values of X.

#### Applications

PMF and PDF are used in various statistical applications, some of which are:

- In hypothesis testing, PMF and PDF help in determining the likelihood of a hypothesis being true or false.
- In Bayesian analysis, PMF and PDF help in updating the probability of an event occurring based on prior knowledge and new evidence.
- In machine learning, PMF and PDF help in building models that can predict the probability of an event occurring.

#### Conclusion

Probability mass function and probability density function are important concepts in statistical analysis. They help in understanding the probability distribution of a random variable, which is essential in various statistical applications. By understanding PMF and PDF, one can develop a deeper understanding of statistical analysis and make informed decisions based on data.
