### Statistical Quality Control (SQC)

- SQC refers to the application of statistical techniques to control the quality of a product or process.
- It is used to maintain the quality of a product or process by monitoring and regulating variance from standards or target values.
- The main objectives of SQC are:
- To produce products/services of uniform quality.
- To reduce production of defectives or off-specification products.
- To reduce cost by avoiding unnecessary inspection and quality costs.
- The three main tools of SQC are:
- Control charts: Used to monitor the variance of a process/product over time from target values. Alerts to undesirable variations to take corrective actions.
- Acceptance sampling: Used to accept/reject a batch of products based on inspecting a sample from the batch. Avoids 100% inspection.
- Attribute sampling: Used for attributes like defects/non-defects. Proportions are estimated and compared to acceptance/rejection criteria.
- Variables sampling: Used for variables measured on a continuous scale. Mean/variance is estimated and compared to acceptance/rejection criteria.
