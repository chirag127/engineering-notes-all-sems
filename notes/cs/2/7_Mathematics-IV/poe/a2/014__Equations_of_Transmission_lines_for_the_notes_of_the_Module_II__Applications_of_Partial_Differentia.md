### Equations of Transmission lines for the notes of the Module II: Applications of Partial Differential Equations: in the subject of Mathematics-IV KCS.

1. Telegrapher's equations: The telegrapher's equations describe the voltage and current on a transmission line as a function of position and time. They are a pair of first-order partial differential equations.

2. Solution of telegrapher's equations: The solution of telegrapher's equations can be obtained by methods of characteristics, Fourier transform and Laplace transform. The solution describes the propagation of voltage and current waves along the transmission line.

3. Input impedance: The input impedance of a transmission line depends on the load impedance and length of the line. It controls the amount of reflection from the load. The transmission line is matched when its input impedance equals the source impedance.

4. Reflection and transmission coefficients: The reflection and transmission coefficients can be calculated from the input impedance. They describe the magnitude and phase of reflected and transmitted waves due to a mismatch.

5. Power flow: The power flow through a transmission line depends on the reflection coefficient and transmission coefficient. The power factor and efficiency are determined from the powers of the incident, reflected and transmitted waves.
