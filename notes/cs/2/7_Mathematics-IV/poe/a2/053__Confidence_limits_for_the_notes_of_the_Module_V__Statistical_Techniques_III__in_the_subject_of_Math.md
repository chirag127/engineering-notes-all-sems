### Confidence limits for the notes of the Module V: Statistical Techniques III: in the subject of Mathematics-IV KCS.

1. Confidence interval: An interval estimate of a population parameter. It is calculated from a given set of sample data.
2. Confidence level (CL): The probability that the confidence interval covers the true population parameter. It is usually 95% or 99%.
3. Margin of error (ME): The amount of possible sampling error in a confidence interval. It depends on the sample size and confidence level.
4. Procedure to construct a confidence interval:
- Choose a confidence level (CL), say 95%
- Choose a sample and calculate the sample statistic, say sample mean
- Determine the critical value (CV) corresponding to the chosen CL and sample size
- Calculate the margin of error (ME)
- Construct the confidence interval: Sample statistic ± ME
