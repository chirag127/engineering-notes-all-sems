### Curve Fitting for the notes of the Module III: Statistical Techniques I: in the subject of Mathematics-IV KCS.

1. Curve fitting is a statistical technique to determine the "best fit" curve/line/surface that fits a given set of data points. It involves minimizing the sum of squared residuals (difference between actual and predicted values) to get the desired curve.
2. The curve can be linear, quadratic, cubic, exponential, logarithmic, power, trigonometric, etc. The choice of curve depends upon the nature of the relationship between variables.
3. The least squares method is commonly used to determine the best fit curve. It requires finding the parameter values which minimize the sum of squared errors/residuals.
4. Residual analysis and R-squared value are used to check the goodness of fit. A good fit would have small random residual values and a high R-squared value close to 1.
5. Applications: Curve fitting is used in data analysis, functions approximation, forecasting, machine learning, computer vision, signal processing, etc. It is a useful technique to analyze data and find underlying patterns.
