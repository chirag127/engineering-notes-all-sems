### Baye's Theorem

* Baye's theorem is a way of updating the probabilities of an event based on new information or evidence.
* It uses conditional probability to determine the probability of an event A given that event B has occurred.
* The formula for Baye's theorem is: P(A|B) = [P(B|A) x P(A)] / P(B)
* Where:
    * P(A|B) is the posterior probability of A given B
    * P(B|A) is the likelihood of B given A
    * P(A) is the prior probability of A
    * P(B) is the prior probability of B
* Baye's theorem is useful in medical testing, spam filtering, and recommendation systems.
* It allows you to update the probability of a hypothesis (such as a patient having a disease) in light of new evidence (such as a medical test result).
