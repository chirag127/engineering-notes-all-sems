### Regression lines of y on x and x on y for the notes of the Module III: Statistical Techniques I: in the subject of Mathematics-IV KCS.

1. Regression line of y on x:
- When x is independent variable and y is dependent variable, the line which fits the given data points in such a way that the sum of squares of deviations is minimum is called as regression line of y on x.
- The equation of regression line is y = a + bx where 'a' is y-intercept and 'b' is slope of the line.
- The slope 'b' indicates the rate of change in y w.r.t change in x.

2. Regression line of x on y:
- When y is independent variable and x is dependent variable, the line which fits the given data points in such a way that the sum of squares of deviations is minimum is called as regression line of x on y.
- The equation of regression line is x = a + by where 'a' is x-intercept and 'b' is slope of the line.
- The slope 'b' indicates the rate of change in x w.r.t change in y.

3. Differences between regression lines of y on x and x on y:
- In regression line of y on x, x is independent variable and in regression line of x on y, y is independent variable.
- The equations of regression lines are of different forms. In y on x, the equation is y = a + bx while in x on y, the equation is x = a + by.
- The slopes have different interpretations. In y on x, slope indicates rate of change in y w.r.t x while in x on y, slope indicates rate of change in x w.r.t y.
