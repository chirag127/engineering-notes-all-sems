# KCS

Knowledge-Centered Service

1. KCS is a methodology to provide consistent and high quality support by leveraging knowledge.
2. It focuses on creating, organizing and providing knowledge to users.
3. The key components of KCS are:
 - Knowledge base: A collection of knowledge articles.
 - Knowledge workers: People responsible for managing the knowledge base.
 - Knowledge processes: Processes to create, update, organize and distribute knowledge.
4. The benefits of KCS are:
 - Increased speed of response.
 - Improved accuracy and consistency.
 - Reduced repetitive work.
 - Easier onboarding of new team members.
 - Knowledge retention even when employees leave.
5. To implement KCS, the first step is to identify common questions and solutions and document them as knowledge articles. Then establish knowledge processes and teams to build the knowledge base and keep it updated. Eventually, the goal is to have users self-serve most of their queries from the knowledge base.
