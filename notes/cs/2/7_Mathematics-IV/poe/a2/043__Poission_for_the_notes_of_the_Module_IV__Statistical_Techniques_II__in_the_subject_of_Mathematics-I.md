### Poisson for the notes of the Module IV: Statistical Techniques II: in the subject of Mathematics-IV KCS.

1. Poisson distribution is a discrete probability distribution that describes the random occurrence of events.
2. It models the number of times an event occurs in a fixed period of time or space if these events occur with a known average rate and independently of the time since the last event.
3. The Poisson distribution is characterized by a single parameter lambda (λ) which is the expected number of occurrences during the given time interval.
4. The probability mass function of a Poisson random variable X is:
P(X=x) = (e^-λ)*(λ^x)/x! for x = 0, 1, 2, ...
where e is the base of the natural logarithms.
5. Mean and variance of Poisson distribution:
Mean = Variance = λ
So the Poisson distribution has equal mean and variance when λ is the parameter.
6. Poisson distribution is often used to model the number of:
- Customers arriving at a store in a given time interval.
- Calls arriving at a call center in a given time interval.
- Vehicles passing through a toll plaza in a given time interval.
- Errors in a piece of electronic equipment.
- Occurrence of an event in a given space area like number of craters on the moon or number of trees in a given area.
