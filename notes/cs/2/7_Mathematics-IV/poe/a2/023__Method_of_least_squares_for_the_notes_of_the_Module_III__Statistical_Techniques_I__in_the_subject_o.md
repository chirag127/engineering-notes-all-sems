### Method of least squares for the notes of the Module III: Statistical Techniques I: in the subject of Mathematics-IV KCS.

1. The method of least squares is a standard approach to estimating the unknown parameters in a linear regression model.
2. The least squares approach minimizes the sum of squared vertical deviations from the data points to the regression line.
3. The steps to obtain the least squares estimate are:
- Form the normal equations by taking the partial derivatives of the error function with respect to the parameters and setting them equal to zero.
- Solve the normal equations to obtain the parameter estimates.
4. The least squares estimates have desirable properties:
- They are unbiased, meaning that on average they equal the true population parameters.
- They have minimum variance, meaning they have the smallest possible error of all unbiased estimates.
5. The least squares estimates provide the "best fit" line in the sense of minimizing the sum of squared errors, but this does not necessarily imply that the line fits well or that it will predict data accurately. The goodness of fit and predictive accuracy need to be assessed using additional metrics and residual analysis.
