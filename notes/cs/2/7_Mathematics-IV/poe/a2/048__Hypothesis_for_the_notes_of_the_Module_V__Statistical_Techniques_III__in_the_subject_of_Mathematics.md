### Hypothesis for the notes of the Module V: Statistical Techniques III: in the subject of Mathematics-IV KCS.

1. A hypothesis is an assumption or declaration that is made about a population parameter. It is an educated guess about the value of a population parameter.
2. A hypothesis can be of two types:
- Null hypothesis (H0): It is a statement of no difference or no effect. It is assumed to be true until it can be rejected.
- Alternative hypothesis (H1): It is a statement that contradicts the null hypothesis. It reflects the expectations or predictions that the researcher has.
3. A hypothesis test is a statistical method used to make decisions about a population parameter. It uses sample data to evaluate whether to reject or fail to reject the null hypothesis.
4. The steps involved in hypothesis testing are:
- State the null hypothesis and alternative hypothesis
- Select the significance level ()
- Collect the sample and calculate the test statistic
- Determine the rejection region and critical value
- Compare the test statistic with the critical value and draw conclusions
5. The types of hypothesis tests are:
- One-tailed test: Used when the alternative hypothesis specifies the direction of difference
- Two-tailed test: Used when the alternative hypothesis does not specify the direction of difference
