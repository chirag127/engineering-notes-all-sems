### Control Charts for variables ( X and R Charts)

- Control charts are used to monitor the quality of a process. They help in detecting the unusual variations in the process at an early stage so that corrective actions can be taken.
- Control charts for variables are used to monitor variables data like weight, temperature, length, etc. Two commonly used control charts for variables data are:
- X-bar chart: Used to monitor the mean/average of the process. The subgroup means are plotted on the chart. Upper and lower control limits are marked based on the average and standard deviation of the sample means. If a point falls outside the control limits, it indicates that the process average has shifted.
- R chart: Used to monitor the range of the data. The ranges of the subgroups are plotted on the chart. Upper and lower control limits are marked based on the average and standard deviation of the ranges. If a point falls outside the control limits, it indicates that the process variability has changed.
- Advantages: Simple to construct and easy to interpret. Help in quick detection of process changes so that corrections can be made.
- Limitations: Require large sample sizes to be effective. Sensitive to sample size changes. Assumptions of normality and consistency may get violated in practice.
