### Correlation and Rank correlation for the notes of the Module III: Statistical Techniques I: in the subject of Mathematics-IV KCS.

1. Correlation: Correlation is a statistical technique which is used to measure the strength and direction of a linear relationship between two quantitative variables.

- It is denoted by r and its value lies between +1 and -1.
- A positive value of r indicates a positive correlation i.e. variables increase or decrease together.
- A negative value of r indicates a negative correlation i.e. variables increase and decrease in opposite directions.
- A value of r = 0 indicates no correlation between the variables.

2. Steps to calculate correlation:

- Plot the scatter plot and check the pattern of points.
- Calculate the sum of squares of differences from the mean (SSD) for both variables (x and y).
- Calculate the covariance of x and y.
- Calculate the correlation coefficient: r = Covariance(x,y) / (SSDx * SSDy)^(1/2)

3. Properties of correlation coefficient:

- -1 ≤ r ≤ 1
- r = 0 indicates no correlation
- The sign of r indicates direction of correlation i.e. positive or negative
- r^2 is the proportion of variance in y that can be explained by x or vice versa
- Correlation does not imply causation
