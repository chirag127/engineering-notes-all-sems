## Module I: Partial Differential Equations

1. Introduction
- Partial differential equations (PDEs) are equations that involve the derivatives of a function with respect to two or more variables.
- They are used to model complex phenomena in engineering, science, and mathematics.
- PDEs find applications in mechanics, fluid dynamics, electromagnetism, diffusion, quantum mechanics, finance, and more.

2. First-order PDEs
- The general form of a first-order PDE in two variables (x and y) is: f(x,y,u,u_x,u_y) = 0
- Common examples:
    - u_x + u_y = 0 (the wave equation)
    - u_x = f(x,y) (transport equation)
- Methods to solve first-order PDEs: separation of variables, integrating factors, and exact equations.

3. Second-order linear PDEs
- The general form is: a(x,y)u_{xx} + b(x,y)u_{yy} + c(x,y)u_x + d(x,y)u_y + e(x,y)u = f(x,y)
- Important examples: the heat equation, the wave equation, and the Laplace equation.
- Solution methods: separation of variables, Fourier series, and other transforms.
