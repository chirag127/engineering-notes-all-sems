### Fitting of straight lines for the notes of the Module III: Statistical Techniques I: in the subject of Mathematics-IV KCS.

1. The straight line can be represented in the form y = mx + c, where m is the slope and c is the intercept.
2. The least square method is used to fit a straight line to a given set of data points.
3. The regression line is the straight line that best fits the data points. It minimizes the sum of squares of errors/deviations.
4. The regression line passes through the point (mean x, mean y). The slope (m) and intercept (c) can be calculated as:
m = (Σ(x - mean x)(y - mean y)) / (Σ(x - mean x)2)
c = mean y - m * mean x
5. The coefficient of determination (R2) measures the goodness of fit and is calculated as:
R2 = (Explained variation) / (Total variation)
R2 values range from 0 to 1. A higher value indicates better fit.
6. The residual is the vertical distance between the data point and regression line. The mean of residuals is zero if the regression line fits the data well.
