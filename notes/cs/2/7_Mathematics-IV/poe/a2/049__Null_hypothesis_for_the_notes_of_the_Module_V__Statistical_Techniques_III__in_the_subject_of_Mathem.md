### Null hypothesis for the notes of the Module V: Statistical Techniques III: in the subject of Mathematics-IV KCS.

1. The null hypothesis (H0) is a hypothesis which the researcher tries to disprove, contradict or reject. It is a statement of no effect or no difference. The null hypothesis states that there is no relationship between the two variables or the variables are equal.
2. The null hypothesis is depicted by H0. The alternate hypothesis is H1 which is the hypothesis the researcher is trying to prove.
3. While testing a hypothesis, the researcher follows the process:
- Set up a null hypothesis (H0) and an alternate hypothesis (H1)
- Collect sample data and calculate the test statistic
- Determine the p-value and compare it to the significance level (α)
- If p-value ≤ α, reject H0, else fail to reject H0
4. Example: To check if a coin is fair (equal probability of heads and tails), the null hypothesis would be:
H0: The coin is fair (p(heads) = p(tails) = 0.5)
H1: The coin is biased (p(heads) ≠ p(tails))
The test statistic would be calculated from the sample data of coin flips and p-value determined. If p-value is less than or equal to the significance level (say 0.05), H0 is rejected, else not rejected.
