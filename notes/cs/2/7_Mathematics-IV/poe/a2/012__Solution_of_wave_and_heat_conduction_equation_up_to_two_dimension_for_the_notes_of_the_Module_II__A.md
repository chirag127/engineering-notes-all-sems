### Solution of wave and heat conduction equation up to two dimension for the notes of the Module II: Applications of Partial Differential Equations: in the subject of Mathematics-IV KCS.

1. **Wave equation in 1D:**
- The wave equation in 1D is $\frac{\\partial^2u}{\\partial t^2} = c^2 \\frac{\\partial^2u}{\\partial x^2}$
- The solution is $u(x,t) = f(x \\pm ct)$ where $f$ is an arbitrary differentiable function.
- The initial conditions are $u(x,0) = f_0(x)$ and $\\frac{\\partial u}{\\partial t}(x,0) = f_1(x)$
- The solution satisfying initial conditions is $u(x,t) = f_0(x \\pm ct) + f_1(x)t$

2. **Wave equation in 2D:**
- The wave equation in 2D is $\frac{\\partial^2u}{\\partial t^2} = c^2(\\frac{\\partial^2u}{\\partial x^2} + \\frac{\\partial^2u}{\\partial y^2})$
- The solution is $u(x,y,t) = f(x \\pm ct, y \\pm ct)$ where $f$ is an arbitrary differentiable function.
- The initial conditions are $u(x,y,0) = f_0(x,y)$ and $\\frac{\\partial u}{\\partial t}(x,y,0) = f_1(x,y)$
- The solution satisfying initial conditions is $u(x,y,t) = f_0(x \\pm ct, y \\pm ct) + f_1(x,y)t$
