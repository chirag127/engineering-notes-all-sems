## Module II: Applications of Partial Differential Equations:

1. Heat Equation:
- Used to model heat diffusion.
- Examples: Cooling of a rod, heating of a plate, etc.
- Solution gives temperature distribution.

2. Wave Equation:
- Models wave propagation.
- Examples: Sound waves, light waves, elastic waves, etc.
- Solution gives displacement, velocity or pressure.

3. Laplace's Equation:
- Models stationary systems in equilibrium.
- Examples: Electric potential, gravitational potential, fluid flow, etc.
- Solution gives scalar quantity distribution.

4. diffusion Equation:
- Models diffusion or dispersion of particles/quantities.
- Examples: Spread of pollutants, spread of populations, etc.
- Solution gives concentration distribution.
