### Laplace equation in two dimensions

1. Laplace equation in two dimensions is ∂2u/∂x2 + ∂2u/∂y2 = 0.
2. It is a second order partial differential equation which describes many physical phenomena like equilibrium temperature distribution, electrostatic potential and fluid flow.
3. Dirichlet boundary condition: If the values of the function u are specified on the boundary of the region, then it is called Dirichlet boundary condition. It is of the form u = g on ∂Ω where g is a given function.
4. Neumann boundary condition: If the normal derivative of the function u is specified on the boundary of the region, then it is called Neumann boundary condition. It is of the form ∂u/∂n = h on ∂Ω where h is a given function.
5. Solution of Laplace equation: The solution of Laplace equation with Dirichlet boundary conditions can be obtained using separation of variables technique. The solution is of the form u(x, y) = X(x)Y(y) where X and Y satisfy ordinary differential equations.
