### Exponential curves for the notes of the Module III: Statistical Techniques I: in the subject of Mathematics-IV KCS.

1. Exponential growth: When a quantity increases at a rate proportional to its current value, it is said to show exponential growth. The growth rate is expressed as a percentage known as the growth factor.
2. Exponential decay: When a quantity decreases at a rate proportional to its current value, it is said to show exponential decay. The decay rate is expressed as a percentage known as the decay factor.
3. Exponential models: Exponential models are commonly used to model situations showing exponential growth and decay. The general form of an exponential model is y = ab^x, where a is the initial amount and b is the growth/decay factor.
4. Applications: Exponential models are used in various applications such as:
- Modeling population growth.
- Modeling spread of diseases.
- Modeling decay of radioactive elements.
- Modeling charging and discharging of capacitors.
