### Linear and Non Linear Partial Equations of first order for the notes of the Module I: Partial Differential Equations in the subject of Mathematics-IV KCS

Module I of the Mathematics-IV KCS course covers the topic of Partial Differential Equations (PDEs). PDEs are mathematical equations that describe the behavior of a function of multiple variables, and are used to model a wide range of physical and engineering phenomena.

Linear and Non-Linear Partial Differential Equations of first order are two types of Partial Differential Equations.

Linear Partial Differential Equations of first order are PDEs that are linear with respect to the unknown function and its derivatives. These equations can be solved using a variety of techniques, including characteristic curves and the method of characteristics.

Non-Linear Partial Differential Equations of first order are PDEs that are non-linear with respect to the unknown function and its derivatives. These equations are typically more difficult to solve than linear PDEs, and require more advanced techniques, such as numerical methods or approximation techniques.
