## Module I: Partial Differential Equations

Module I of the course on Partial Differential Equations (PDEs) is an introduction to the study of PDEs and their applications. PDEs are mathematical equations that describe the behavior of functions of multiple variables, and are used to model a wide range of physical and engineering phenomena, such as heat transfer, fluid flow, and electromagnetic fields.

In this module, students will learn about the basic concepts and techniques used to solve PDEs, including:

1. Classification of PDEs: This covers the different types of PDEs, such as elliptic, parabolic, and hyperbolic PDEs, and their properties.

2. Separation of Variables: This covers the technique of separating the variables in a PDE and solving for each variable separately.

3. Method of Characteristics: This covers the technique of transforming a PDE into a set of ordinary differential equations (ODEs) that can be solved using standard ODE methods.

4. Finite Difference Methods: This covers numerical methods for solving PDEs, including finite difference methods and finite element methods.

In the context of the course, students will learn about PDEs through interactive activities, lectures, and hands-on experiences. This may include working with simulations, writing code to solve PDEs, and analyzing the behavior of PDEs in response to different inputs.

In summary, Module I of the course on Partial Differential Equations (PDEs) is an introduction to the study of PDEs and their applications. The module covers the basic concepts and techniques used to solve PDEs, including classification of PDEs, separation of variables, method of characteristics, and finite difference methods. Students will learn about PDEs through interactive activities, lectures, and hands-on experiences.
