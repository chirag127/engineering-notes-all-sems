### Correlation and Rank correlation for the notes of the Module III: Statistical Techniques I: in the subject of Mathematics-IV KCS

Module III of the Mathematics-IV KCS course covers the topic of Statistical Techniques I. One of the topics in this module is Correlation and Rank Correlation.

Correlation is a statistical measure that quantifies the linear relationship between two variables. Correlation is expressed as a value between -1 and 1, where a value of -1 indicates a perfect negative linear relationship, a value of 1 indicates a perfect positive linear relationship, and a value of 0 indicates no linear relationship.

Rank Correlation, also known as Spearman's rank correlation, is a non-parametric measure of the strength of association between two variables. Rank correlation measures the relationship between two variables by comparing the ranks of the values in each variable, rather than the actual values.

In the context of the Mathematics-IV KCS course, students will learn about Correlation and Rank Correlation as part of Module III. This may include working with examples of correlation and rank correlation, calculating correlation and rank correlation coefficients, and interpreting the results of correlation and rank correlation analyses.

Correlation is a statistical measure that quantifies the linear relationship between two variables, while Rank Correlation is a non-parametric measure of the strength of association between two variables.
