### Random variables (Discrete and Continuous Random variable) for the notes of the Module IV: Statistical Techniques II: in the subject of Mathematics-IV KCS

Module IV of the Mathematics-IV KCS course covers the topic of Statistical Techniques II, including the concept of random variables.

Random variables are variables that can take on different values based on chance or probability. There are two types of random variables: discrete random variables and continuous random variables.

Discrete random variables are random variables that can take on a finite or countably infinite number of values. Examples of discrete random variables include the number of heads in a coin flip, the number of cars passing through a street in a given time period, and the number of students in a class.

Continuous random variables are random variables that can take on any value within a specified range. Examples of continuous random variables include the height of an individual, the weight of an object, and the time it takes to complete a task.

In the context of the Mathematics-IV KCS course, students will learn about random variables, including discrete random variables and continuous random variables, as part of Module IV. This may include working with examples of random variables, calculating probabilities for random variables, and analyzing the behavior of random variables.

In summary, Module IV of the Mathematics-IV KCS course covers the topic of Statistical Techniques II, and includes a discussion of random variables. Random variables are variables that can take on different values based on chance or probability, and can be either discrete or continuous. Students will learn about random variables through interactive activities and hands-on experiences.
