## Unit 5 - Iterators & Recursion: Recursive Fibonacci , Tower Of Hanoi

Unit 5 of the Python Programming course focuses on Iterators and Recursion, including the topics of Recursive Fibonacci and Tower of Hanoi.

Recursive Fibonacci is a sequence of numbers where each number is the sum of the two preceding numbers. The Fibonacci sequence can be generated using a recursive algorithm, which is a type of algorithm that calls itself to solve a problem.

The Tower of Hanoi is a mathematical puzzle that involves moving a stack of disks from one peg to another, with the constraint that you can only move one disk at a time, and you cannot place a larger disk on top of a smaller disk. The Tower of Hanoi can be solved using a recursive algorithm.

In the context of the Python Programming course, students in Unit 5 will learn about iterators and recursion, including the topics of Recursive Fibonacci and Tower of Hanoi. They will learn how to use recursive algorithms to solve problems, and will have hands-on experiences working with recursive algorithms in Python.

In summary, Unit 5 of the Python Programming course focuses on Iterators and Recursion, including the topics of Recursive Fibonacci and Tower of Hanoi. Students will learn about recursive algorithms and will have hands-on experiences working with recursive algorithms in Python.
