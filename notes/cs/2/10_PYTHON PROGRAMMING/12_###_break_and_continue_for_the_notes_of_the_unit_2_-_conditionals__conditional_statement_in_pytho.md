### Break and Continue for the notes of the Unit 2 - Conditionals: Conditional statement in Python (if-else statement, its working and execution), Nested-if statement and Elif statement in Python, Expression Evaluation & Float Representation. in the subject of PYTHON PROGRAMMING

Break and Continue are two important control statements in Python that are used to control the flow of execution in a program.

1. Break: The break statement is used to break out of a loop prematurely. When the break statement is executed, the loop is terminated, and the program continues with the next statement after the loop.

2. Continue: The continue statement is used to skip the current iteration of a loop and move on to the next iteration. When the continue statement is executed, the current iteration is skipped, and the program continues with the next iteration of the loop.
