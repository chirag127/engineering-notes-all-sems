### Modules : Introduction , Importing Modules , for the notes of the Unit 4 - Sieve of Eratosthenes: generate prime numbers with the help of an algorithm given by the Greek Mathematician named Eratosthenes, whose algorithm is known as Sieve of Eratosthenes. in the subject of PYTHON PROGRAMMING

2. Overview of modules (reusability, encapsulation, and separation of concerns)
3. Techniques for importing modules in Python (import statement, from statement, and alias)
4. Overview of the Sieve of Eratosthenes algorithm (generating prime numbers using a mathematical technique)
5. Hands-on practice with programming exercises (implementing the Sieve of Eratosthenes algorithm, testing and debugging, and documenting code)
6. Further reading and resources (books, online tutorials, forums)

The Modules: Introduction, Importing Modules section of this unit should provide students with a solid understanding of modules in Python and how to import and use them in their programming projects. The hands-on practice and further reading resources will help students to develop their programming skills and deepen their understanding of the material.
