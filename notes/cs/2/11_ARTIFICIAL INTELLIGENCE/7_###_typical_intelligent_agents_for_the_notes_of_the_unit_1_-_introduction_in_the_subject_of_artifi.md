### Typical Intelligent Agents for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

Typical Intelligent Agents:
- Intelligent agents are computer systems that are designed to perform tasks that would normally require human intelligence, such as perception, reasoning, and decision making.
- There are several types of intelligent agents, including: 
  1. Simple reflex agents: respond to the current percept and do not consider the history of past percepts.
  2. Model-based agents: maintain an internal model of the world and use it to make decisions.
  3. Goal-based agents: have a set of goals and use them to make decisions.
  4. Utility-based agents: make decisions based on the expected utility of each action.
  5. Learning agents: use experience to improve their performance over time.
- Intelligent agents can be used in a variety of applications, including robotics, recommendation systems, and autonomous vehicles.
- The design of intelligent agents is a complex process that involves several key components, including:
  1. Sensors: used to gather information about the environment.
  2. Actuators: used to perform actions in the environment.
  3. A knowledge base: used to store information about the world.
  4. Inference engine: used to make decisions based on the information gathered by the sensors and stored in the knowledge base.
- Understanding the characteristics and types of intelligent agents is essential for the design and development of artificial intelligence systems.
