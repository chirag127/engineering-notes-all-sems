### Planning for the notes of the Unit 5 - APPLICATIONS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

Planning is a critical aspect of Artificial Intelligence that involves finding a sequence of actions to achieve a goal.

1. Definition: Planning is the process of finding a sequence of actions that can be taken to achieve a goal. Planning is used in AI systems to make decisions and perform tasks.

2. Types of Planning: There are several types of planning, including classical planning, probabilistic planning, and reactive planning. The type of planning used will depend on the specific requirements of the AI system and the type of task being performed.

3. Classical Planning: Classical planning involves finding a sequence of actions that can be taken to achieve a goal in a deterministic environment. This type of planning is well-suited for tasks where the outcome of each action is known with certainty.

4. Probabilistic Planning: Probabilistic planning involves finding a sequence of actions that can be taken to achieve a goal in an uncertain environment. This type of planning takes into account the uncertainty of the environment and the potential for actions to have different outcomes.

5. Reactive Planning: Reactive planning involves finding a sequence of actions in real-time, based on the current state of the environment. This type of planning is well-suited for tasks where the environment is changing rapidly and the AI system needs to respond quickly.

6. Applications: Planning is used in a wide range of AI applications, including robotics, expert systems, and autonomous agents. Planning is also used in fields such as logistics, scheduling, and resource allocation.

In conclusion, planning is a critical aspect of AI that involves finding a sequence of actions to achieve a goal. There are several types of planning, including classical planning, probabilistic planning, and reactive planning, and the type of planning used will depend on the specific requirements of the AI system and the type of task being performed. Planning is used in a wide range of AI applications and is an important tool for decision making and task performance.
