### Characteristics of Intelligent Agents for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

Characteristics of Intelligent Agents:
- Intelligent agents are computer systems that can perform tasks autonomously and make decisions based on information from their environment.
- There are several key characteristics that define intelligent agents, including:
1. Autonomy: Intelligent agents are capable of operating independently and making decisions without human intervention.
2. Perception: Intelligent agents can perceive their environment and gather information to support decision-making.
3. Reasoning: Intelligent agents can reason and make decisions based on the information they have gathered.
4. Learning: Intelligent agents can learn from their experiences and improve their performance over time.
5. Adaptability: Intelligent agents can adapt to changing conditions and modify their behavior as needed.
6. Interaction: Intelligent agents can interact with their environment and other agents to achieve their goals.
- These characteristics are essential for intelligent agents to be able to perform complex tasks and operate effectively in real-world environments.
- Understanding the characteristics of intelligent agents is important for the development of AI systems and for the design of applications that can leverage AI technology.
