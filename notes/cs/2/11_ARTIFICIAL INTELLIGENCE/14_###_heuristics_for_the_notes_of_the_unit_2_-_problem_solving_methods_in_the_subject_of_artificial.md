### Heuristics for the notes of the Unit 2 - PROBLEM SOLVING METHODS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

Heuristics:
- Heuristics are problem-solving strategies or "rules of thumb" that can be used to quickly find a solution to a problem.
- Heuristics are often used in artificial intelligence and decision-making, where the goal is to find a solution in a limited amount of time.
- Common heuristics include simplification, working backwards, and using analogies.
- Simplification involves breaking down a complex problem into smaller, more manageable parts.
- Working backwards involves starting from the desired outcome and working backwards to find the steps necessary to achieve it.
- Using analogies involves finding a similar problem that has already been solved and using the solution to the analogous problem as a starting point for solving the current problem.
- Heuristics can be useful for quickly finding a solution, but they can also lead to biases and errors, so it is important to use them in combination with other problem-solving strategies.
- Understanding heuristics is important for developing effective problem-solving skills and for understanding the limitations and potential biases of these strategies.
