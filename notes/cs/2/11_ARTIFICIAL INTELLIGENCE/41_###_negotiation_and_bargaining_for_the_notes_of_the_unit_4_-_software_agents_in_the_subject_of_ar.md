Negotiation and Bargaining

Negotiation and bargaining are processes in which two or more agents work together to reach an agreement or a decision. These processes are an important aspect of software agents in Artificial Intelligence because they allow agents to interact and make decisions in a cooperative manner.

In negotiation and bargaining, agents work together to find a mutually acceptable solution to a problem or to reach an agreement on a decision. This requires the agents to communicate and exchange information, to make offers and counter-offers, and to evaluate the proposals of other agents.

There are several approaches to negotiation and bargaining, including distributive bargaining, integrative bargaining, and cooperative bargaining. Distributive bargaining is a process in which the agents work together to divide a fixed resource, such as money or time. Integrative bargaining is a process in which the agents work together to find a solution that benefits both parties. Cooperative bargaining is a process in which the agents work together to find a solution that benefits all parties.

In conclusion, negotiation and bargaining are processes in which two or more agents work together to reach an agreement or a decision. These processes are an important aspect of software agents in Artificial Intelligence because they allow agents to interact and make decisions in a cooperative manner.
