Constraint Propagation

Constraint Propagation is a problem-solving method used in Artificial Intelligence (AI) to reduce the search space for a solution to a problem. The method involves the use of constraints, which are conditions that must be satisfied for a solution to be considered valid.

Constraint Propagation is used in a variety of AI applications, including constraint satisfaction problems, scheduling problems, and optimization problems. The method involves the use of a set of constraints to eliminate possibilities and reduce the search space for a solution.

The constraints are used to propagate information throughout the search space, and they are used to eliminate possibilities that are not consistent with the constraints. The process of constraint propagation continues until a solution is found or until it is determined that no solution exists.
