## Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION

Unit 1 - Introduction is a foundational unit that provides an overview of the subject matter and provides the necessary background information for the rest of the course.

1. Overview: The introduction unit provides an overview of the subject matter, including the scope, goals, and objectives of the course. This unit sets the stage for the rest of the course by providing a broad understanding of the topic and the context in which it will be studied.

2. Background Information: The introduction unit provides background information on the subject matter, including the history, development, and current state of knowledge in the field. This information is essential for understanding the context in which the subject matter will be studied and the significance of the topic.

3. Key Concepts: The introduction unit introduces key concepts and terminology that will be used throughout the course. Understanding these concepts and terms is essential for following the rest of the course and for engaging in further study of the subject matter.

4. Learning Outcomes: The introduction unit outlines the learning outcomes of the course, including the skills and knowledge that students will gain through their study. This information provides a roadmap for the rest of the course and helps students to focus their learning and understand the purpose of the course.

5. Assessment: The introduction unit may also provide information on assessment, including the types of assessments that will be used, the criteria for evaluation, and the weighting of assessments. This information is important for students to understand how their learning will be evaluated and to plan their study accordingly.

In conclusion, the introduction unit is a crucial component of any course, providing an overview of the subject matter and setting the stage for the rest of the course. Understanding the key concepts, learning outcomes, and assessment criteria is essential for students to engage in meaningful learning and to succeed in the course.
