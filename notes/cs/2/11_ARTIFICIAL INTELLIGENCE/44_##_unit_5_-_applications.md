## Unit 5 - APPLICATIONS

Applications of Artificial Intelligence:
- Artificial Intelligence (AI) has a wide range of applications in various fields, including:
  1. Robotics: AI is used to develop autonomous robots that can perform tasks, such as manufacturing, inspection, and maintenance.
  2. Healthcare: AI is used to develop systems that can diagnose diseases, predict patient outcomes, and support medical decision-making.
  3. Finance: AI is used to develop systems that can analyze financial data, make investment decisions, and detect fraud.
  4. Transportation: AI is used to develop autonomous vehicles and systems that can optimize transportation networks.
  5. Customer Service: AI is used to develop chatbots and virtual assistants that can provide customer support.
  6. Natural Language Processing: AI is used to develop systems that can understand and generate human language.
  7. Computer Vision: AI is used to develop systems that can recognize and interpret visual information.
- AI has the potential to revolutionize many industries and improve the quality of life for people around the world.
- Understanding the applications of AI is important for the development of new technologies and for the design of systems that can leverage AI to solve real-world problems.
