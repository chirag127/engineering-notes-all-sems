## Unit 4 - SOFTWARE AGENTS

Software Agents:
- Software agents are computer programs that can perform tasks autonomously and interact with other agents or systems.
- Software agents are designed to operate in complex and dynamic environments, where they can make decisions based on information from their environment and other agents.
- Software agents can be used in a variety of applications, including recommendation systems, e-commerce, and customer service.
- There are several key characteristics of software agents, including:
  1. Autonomy: the ability to operate independently and make decisions without human intervention.
  2. Communication: the ability to exchange information with other agents and systems.
  3. Adaptability: the ability to modify their behavior in response to changes in the environment.
  4. Intelligence: the ability to reason and make decisions based on information from their environment.
- Software agents can be designed using a variety of programming languages and tools, including Java, Python, and Agent-Oriented Programming (AOP).
- Understanding software agents is important for the design and development of intelligent systems and for the creation of applications that can leverage AI technology.
