## Unit 2 - PROBLEM SOLVING METHODS

Unit 2: Problem Solving Methods

Problem solving is a critical skill that is essential for success in many areas of life. It involves the ability to identify problems, gather information, evaluate options, and make decisions.

There are several methods that can be used to solve problems, including the following:

1. Brainstorming: This is a group problem-solving technique that involves generating a large number of ideas in a short amount of time. Brainstorming is often used to generate creative solutions to problems.

2. Root cause analysis: This method involves identifying the underlying cause of a problem by breaking it down into smaller parts and examining each part in detail.

3. Decision tree analysis: This method involves creating a visual representation of the problem and the options for solving it, in order to evaluate the potential outcomes of each option and make a decision.

4. SWOT analysis: This method involves evaluating the strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats associated with a problem in order to make a decision.

5. Pareto analysis: This method involves prioritizing problems based on their impact and frequency, in order to focus on the most important issues.

6. Flowcharting: This method involves creating a visual representation of the steps involved in a process, in order to identify potential problems and improve efficiency.

7. Fishbone diagram: This method involves creating a visual representation of the causes of a problem, in order to identify the root cause and find a solution.

In conclusion, problem solving is a critical skill that is essential for success in many areas of life. There are several methods that can be used to solve problems, including brainstorming, root cause analysis, decision tree analysis, SWOT analysis, Pareto analysis, flowcharting, and fishbone diagram. Understanding these methods and learning how to apply them is an important aspect of the subject of Problem Solving Methods.
