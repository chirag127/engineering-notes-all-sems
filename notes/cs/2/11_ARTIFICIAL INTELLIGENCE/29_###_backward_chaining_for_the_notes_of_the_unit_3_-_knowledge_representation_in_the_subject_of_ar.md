### Backward Chaining for the notes of the Unit 3 - KNOWLEDGE REPRESENTATION in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

Backward Chaining:
- Backward chaining is a reasoning method used in artificial intelligence to solve problems by working backwards from the goal to the current state.
- Backward chaining starts with the desired goal and searches for a set of rules or premises that can be used to achieve the goal.
- The process of backward chaining involves matching the goal with the conclusion of each rule in the knowledge base and using the premises of the matching rule to generate new subgoals.
- The process continues until a set of subgoals is generated that can be achieved using the current state of the system.
- Backward chaining is useful for solving problems in which the goal is well-defined but the path to the goal is not known.
- Backward chaining can be used in combination with other reasoning methods, such as forward chaining, to solve complex problems.
- Understanding backward chaining is important for the design and development of knowledge-based systems and for the implementation of effective problem-solving algorithms in artificial intelligence.
