### Search Strategies for the notes of the Unit 2 - PROBLEM SOLVING METHODS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

Search Strategies:
- Search strategies are methods used to find a solution to a problem in a systematic and efficient manner.
- Search strategies are used in artificial intelligence to solve problems such as finding the shortest path between two points, or finding a solution to a complex optimization problem.
- There are several types of search strategies, including uninformed search, informed search, and heuristic search.
- Uninformed search strategies, such as breadth-first search and depth-first search, do not use any prior knowledge about the problem or its solution.
- Informed search strategies, such as best-first search and A* search, use prior knowledge about the problem and its solution to guide the search.
- Heuristic search strategies use heuristics, or rules of thumb, to guide the search.
- The choice of search strategy depends on the specific problem being solved and the available information about the problem and its solution.
- Understanding search strategies is essential for solving problems in artificial intelligence and for the development of effective problem-solving algorithms.
