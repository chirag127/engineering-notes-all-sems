Stochastic Games:
- Stochastic games are a type of game theory that involve randomness and uncertainty.
- In a stochastic game, players take turns making decisions, and the outcome of each decision depends on the actions of other players and on random events.
- Stochastic games are used to model real-world situations where the outcome of an action depends on the actions of other agents and on unpredictable events.
- There are several types of stochastic games, including two-player games, multi-player games, and repeated games.
- The solution to a stochastic game is often represented by a strategy, which is a plan of action for each possible situation.
- The solution to a stochastic game can be found using various methods, including value iteration, policy iteration, and linear programming.
- Stochastic games have been used in a variety of applications, including decision making in economics, finance, and operations research.
- Understanding stochastic games is important for developing effective decision-making algorithms and for modeling real-world situations where uncertainty and randomness play a role.
