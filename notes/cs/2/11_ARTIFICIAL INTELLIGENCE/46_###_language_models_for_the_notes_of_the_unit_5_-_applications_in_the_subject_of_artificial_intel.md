Language Models: 
- Language models are computer systems that are trained to generate text that is similar to human language.
- Language models are used in a variety of applications, including machine translation, text generation, and dialogue systems.
- There are several types of language models, including statistical language models and neural language models.
- Statistical language models use statistical techniques, such as n-grams and Markov models, to generate text.
- Neural language models use artificial neural networks to generate text, and they have become increasingly popular in recent years due to their ability to generate high-quality text.
- Language models can be trained on large datasets of text, and they can be fine-tuned for specific applications.
- The quality of a language model is often measured by its ability to generate text that is similar to human language and by its ability to generate text that is coherent and consistent.
- Understanding language models is important for the development of AI systems that can generate human-like text and for the design of applications that use natural language processing.
