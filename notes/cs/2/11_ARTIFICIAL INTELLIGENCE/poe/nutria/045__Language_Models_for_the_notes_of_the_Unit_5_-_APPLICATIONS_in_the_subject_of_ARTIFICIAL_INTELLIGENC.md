
### Language Models 

- Language models are statistical models used to predict the probability of a sequence of words. 
- They are used in many natural language processing tasks such as machine translation, speech recognition, and text summarization. 
- Language models are typically trained on large datasets of text, such as news articles or books. 
- The goal is to learn the probability distribution of words given a context. 
- For example, a language model might be used to predict the likelihood of a given word appearing after another word or phrase. 
- Language models can also be used to generate text, such as in chatbots or automated writing. 
- The most commonly used language models are n-gram models, which predict the probability of a given word based on the preceding n words. 
- Other language models such as recurrent neural networks and transformers have been developed to improve the accuracy of language modeling.
