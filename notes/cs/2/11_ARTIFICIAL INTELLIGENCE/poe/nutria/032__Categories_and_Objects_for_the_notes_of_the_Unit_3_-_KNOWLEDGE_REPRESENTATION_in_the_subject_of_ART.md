
### Categories and Objects for the Notes of Unit 3 - Knowledge Representation in the Subject of Artificial Intelligence KCS

1. Knowledge Representation: Knowledge representation is the process of representing knowledge in a formal language that can be understood by computers. It involves expressing facts, rules, and other information in a way that is meaningful to a computer system and can be used to solve problems.

2. Categories: Categories are ways of organizing information into meaningful groups. They are used to classify objects and concepts into sets. Categories can be used to represent knowledge in a formal language, such as in a knowledge base.

3. Objects: Objects are the entities that are represented in a knowledge base. They can represent physical objects, such as chairs, or abstract concepts, such as numbers. Objects can be related to each other and can have properties associated with them.

4. Rules: Rules are statements that define how objects and categories are related. They are used to express relationships between objects and categories, and can be used to infer new knowledge.

5. Reasoning: Reasoning is the process of using rules to infer new knowledge from existing knowledge. It is used to answer questions and solve problems.
