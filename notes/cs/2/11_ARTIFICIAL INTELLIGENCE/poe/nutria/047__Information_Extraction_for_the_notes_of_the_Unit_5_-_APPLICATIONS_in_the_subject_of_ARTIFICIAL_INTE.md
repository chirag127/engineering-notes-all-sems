
### Information Extraction for the notes of the Unit 5 - APPLICATIONS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

1. Information extraction (IE) is a sub-field of artificial intelligence that deals with the extraction of useful information from unstructured or semi-structured sources.
2. IE systems are used to automatically extract structured information from natural language texts, such as emails, web pages, and other documents.
3. IE systems can be used to extract information from large volumes of data, such as for text mining or for knowledge discovery.
4. IE systems can also be used to extract specific types of information, such as product prices, customer reviews, or contact information.
5. IE systems are used in a variety of applications, such as customer service, medical records, financial services, and legal analytics.
6. IE systems can also be used to extract information from images and videos.
7. IE systems can be used to automate tasks such as document summarization, question answering, and entity extraction.
8. IE systems can also be used to identify relationships between entities, such as people and organizations.
