
## Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION

1. Introduction is the first unit of any course or study material. It is the part of the course which introduces the student to the topic and sets the foundation for the rest of the course.

2. In this unit, the student will be exposed to the basics of the topic and will be given an overview of the course. This will help the student to understand the context and the importance of the course.

3. The student should also be aware of the objectives of the course, the learning outcomes, and the assessment criteria. This will help the student to prepare for the course and to ensure that they are on the right track.

4. It is important to note that the introduction should be comprehensive and should provide the student with enough information to understand the course. The introduction should also provide the student with the necessary resources and references to help them understand the topic better.
