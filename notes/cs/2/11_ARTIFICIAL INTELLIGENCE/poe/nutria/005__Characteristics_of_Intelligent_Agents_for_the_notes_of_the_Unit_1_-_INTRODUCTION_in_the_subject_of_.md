
### Characteristics of Intelligent Agents

1. Autonomy: Intelligent agents are capable of independent decision-making and action.
2. Reactivity: Intelligent agents are able to perceive their environment and respond to changes in it in a timely manner.
3. Proactivity: Intelligent agents are able to take the initiative and act on their own, rather than just reacting to external stimuli.
4. Social Ability: Intelligent agents are able to interact with other agents and humans in a meaningful way.
5. Learning: Intelligent agents are able to use experience to improve their performance in the future.
6. Adaptability: Intelligent agents are able to adapt to changing conditions in their environment.
