
## Unit 2 - PROBLEM SOLVING METHODS

1. Problem solving is a process of identifying and solving a problem. It involves a series of steps that help to identify the problem, define the problem, analyze the problem, develop solutions, and implement the solution.

2. The most common problem-solving methods include trial and error, brainstorming, root cause analysis, and the scientific method.

3. Trial and error is a method that involves trying different solutions until the desired result is achieved.

4. Brainstorming is a process of generating ideas and solutions by a group of people.

5. Root cause analysis is a method of problem solving that involves identifying the root cause of a problem and taking steps to address it.

6. The scientific method is a systematic process of collecting data, analyzing data, and coming up with conclusions.

7. Other problem-solving methods include decision-making, problem-solving models, and problem-solving techniques.

8. Decision-making is a process of making choices based on available data and information.

9. Problem-solving models are frameworks that help to identify and solve problems.

10. Problem-solving techniques are specific strategies and methods used to solve problems.
