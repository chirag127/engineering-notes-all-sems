
### Search Strategies for the Notes of the Unit 2 - PROBLEM SOLVING METHODS in the Subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

1. Uninformed Search Strategies: These search strategies do not use any additional information about the problem. Examples include Breadth-first search, Depth-first search, Iterative deepening search, and Uniform-cost search.

2. Informed Search Strategies: These search strategies use additional information about the problem to guide the search. Examples include Greedy search, A* search, and Recursive best-first search.

3. Heuristic Search Strategies: These search strategies use heuristics to guide the search. Examples include Hill-climbing, Simulated annealing, and Genetic algorithms.

4. Adversarial Search Strategies: These search strategies are used in two-player games, such as chess. Examples include Minimax, Alpha-beta pruning, and Monte Carlo Tree Search.
