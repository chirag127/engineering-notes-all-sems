
### Hardware for the notes of the Unit 5 - APPLICATIONS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

1. Hardware for Artificial Intelligence (AI) includes physical components such as processors, memory, storage, and networks. 
2. Processor: A processor is the brain of a computer and is responsible for executing instructions. AI algorithms require a lot of processing power, so modern AI applications often use specialized processors like GPUs and TPUs. 
3. Memory: Memory is used to store data and instructions. AI applications require large amounts of memory, so modern AI applications often use specialized memory components like HBM and HMC. 
4. Storage: Storage is used to store large amounts of data. AI applications often require large amounts of data, so modern AI applications often use specialized storage components like NVMe and SSDs. 
5. Networks: Networks are used to connect computers and devices. AI applications often require large amounts of data to be transferred quickly, so modern AI applications often use specialized networks like Ethernet and InfiniBand.
