
### Resolution for the notes of the Unit 3 - KNOWLEDGE REPRESENTATION in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

1. Knowledge Representation is the process of representing knowledge in a way that can be understood and used by AI systems. 
2. Representing knowledge in a structured format allows AI systems to process information more efficiently and accurately. 
3. Knowledge Representation techniques include: 
    - Semantic networks: Representing knowledge using nodes and arcs. 
    - Frames: Representing knowledge using slots and values. 
    - Production rules: Representing knowledge using if-then rules. 
    - Ontologies: Representing knowledge using hierarchies of concepts. 
4. Knowledge Representation is essential for AI systems to perform tasks such as natural language processing, image recognition, and decision-making. 
5. AI systems use knowledge representation to convert data into a form that can be used for reasoning and decision-making. 
6. Knowledge Representation is also important for AI systems to be able to learn from experience and adapt to changing environments.
