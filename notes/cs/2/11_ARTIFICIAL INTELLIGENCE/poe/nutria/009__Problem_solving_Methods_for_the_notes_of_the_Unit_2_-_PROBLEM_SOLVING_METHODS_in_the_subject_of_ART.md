
### Problem Solving Methods for the notes of the Unit 2 - PROBLEM SOLVING METHODS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

1. Brute Force: Brute force is a problem solving method in which all possible solutions are tried until the correct solution is found. This method is simple and effective, but it can be time consuming and inefficient when the number of possible solutions is very large.

2. Heuristics: Heuristics are problem solving methods that use experience and intuition to arrive at a solution. They are often used when an optimal solution cannot be found, or when the search space is too large to explore exhaustively.

3. Divide and Conquer: Divide and conquer is a problem solving method in which a problem is divided into smaller sub-problems, which are then solved individually. This method is useful for problems that can be broken down into independent components.

4. Backtracking: Backtracking is a problem solving method in which a solution is found by exploring all possible paths. This method is useful for problems that have multiple solutions, or when the search space is too large to explore exhaustively.

5. Greedy Algorithms: Greedy algorithms are problem solving methods that involve making decisions based on the current state of the problem. This method is useful for problems that can be solved by making a series of local decisions.

6. Dynamic Programming: Dynamic programming is a problem solving method that involves breaking down a problem into sub-problems, and then finding an optimal solution by combining the solutions to the sub-problems. This method is useful for problems where the optimal solution can be found by combining the solutions to smaller sub-problems.
