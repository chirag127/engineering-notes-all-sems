
### Argumentation among Agents

Argumentation among software agents is a process in which agents attempt to reach a consensus on a given issue. It is a form of distributed problem solving, in which multiple agents can interact and negotiate with each other in order to reach an agreement. Argumentation among agents is an important topic in the field of artificial intelligence, as it allows agents to make decisions based on the information they have available. 

Argumentation among agents can be used in a variety of contexts, such as in multi-agent systems, where agents interact to reach a collective goal, or in distributed decision making, where agents must make decisions that are beneficial for the entire system. In the context of artificial intelligence, argumentation among agents is used to facilitate the decision making process. 

Argumentation among agents is based on the idea that agents can use their knowledge and experience to reach a consensus on a given issue. This process involves agents exchanging information, making arguments, and engaging in a dialogue in order to reach an agreement. Argumentation among agents is also used to evaluate the quality of decisions made by agents. 

Argumentation among agents can be used in a variety of applications, such as in automated negotiation, where agents negotiate with each other to reach a mutually beneficial agreement, or in multi-agent systems, where agents cooperate to achieve a common goal. Argumentation among agents can also be used in distributed decision making, where agents must make decisions that are beneficial for the entire system.
