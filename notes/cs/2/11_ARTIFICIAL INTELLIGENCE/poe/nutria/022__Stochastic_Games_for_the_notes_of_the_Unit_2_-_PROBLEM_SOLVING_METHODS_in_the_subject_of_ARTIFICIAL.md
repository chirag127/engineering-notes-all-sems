
### Stochastic Games 

Stochastic games are a type of game where the players have incomplete information about the game. They are used to model situations in which the players have to make decisions based on uncertain information.

In a stochastic game, the players take turns making moves, and the outcome of the game is determined by the combination of the players' moves. The players cannot be certain of the outcome of the game until it is over.

The players in a stochastic game can be either cooperative or non-cooperative. In a cooperative game, the players work together to achieve a common goal. In a non-cooperative game, the players are competing against each other to achieve their individual goals.

Stochastic games can be used to model a wide range of real-world situations, such as economic markets, political decisions, and military strategies. They are also used in the study of artificial intelligence, as they provide a way to model complex decision-making processes.
