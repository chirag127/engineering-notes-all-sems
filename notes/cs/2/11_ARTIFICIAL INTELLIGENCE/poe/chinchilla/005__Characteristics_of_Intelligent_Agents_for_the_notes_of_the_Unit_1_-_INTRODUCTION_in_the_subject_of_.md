### Characteristics of Intelligent Agents

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is concerned with the development of intelligent agents that can perform tasks autonomously without human intervention. An intelligent agent is a computer program that can perceive its environment, reason about it, and act accordingly to achieve its goals. Here are the characteristics of intelligent agents:

1. Autonomy: Intelligent agents are autonomous, meaning they can operate independently without human intervention. They can perceive their environment, make decisions, and take actions based on their goals.

2. Reactive: Intelligent agents are reactive, meaning they can perceive their environment and respond to changes in it. They can react to changes in their environment, such as new information or events.

3. Proactive: Intelligent agents are proactive, meaning they can take the initiative to achieve their goals. They can take actions on their own without being prompted by external stimuli.

4. Goal-oriented: Intelligent agents are goal-oriented, meaning they have a specific goal or objective that they are designed to achieve. They can reason about their goals and take actions to achieve them.

5. Adaptive: Intelligent agents are adaptive, meaning they can learn from their experiences and improve their performance over time. They can adjust their behavior based on feedback from their environment and improve their performance.

6. Communicative: Intelligent agents are communicative, meaning they can interact with other agents or humans. They can exchange information, collaborate, and coordinate their actions to achieve their goals.

7. Rational: Intelligent agents are rational, meaning they make decisions based on their goals and the available information. They can reason about their goals and take actions to achieve them in the most efficient and effective way possible.

In conclusion, intelligent agents are computer programs that can perceive their environment, reason about it, and act accordingly to achieve their goals. They are autonomous, reactive, proactive, goal-oriented, adaptive, communicative, and rational. These characteristics make them suitable for a wide range of applications, including robotics, game playing, natural language processing, and decision-making systems.
