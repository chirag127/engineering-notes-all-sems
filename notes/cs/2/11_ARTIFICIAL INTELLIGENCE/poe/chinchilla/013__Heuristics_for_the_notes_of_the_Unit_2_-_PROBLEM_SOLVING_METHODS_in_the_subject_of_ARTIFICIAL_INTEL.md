### Heuristics for the notes of the Unit 2 - PROBLEM SOLVING METHODS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

In the field of Artificial Intelligence, problem-solving is one of the most critical tasks. To solve a problem, one needs to have a structured approach and a set of heuristics to guide them. Here are some heuristics that can be helpful in the notes of the Unit 2 - Problem Solving Methods in the subject of Artificial Intelligence KCS:

1. **Understand the problem:** Read and analyze the problem statement carefully. Identify the problem's constraints, objectives, and requirements before attempting to solve it.

2. **Divide and conquer:** Break down the problem into smaller sub-problems or modules. Tackle each module independently, and then combine the solutions to solve the larger problem.

3. **Use abstraction:** Abstract away the unnecessary details of the problem, and focus on the essential parts. This will help simplify the problem and make it easier to solve.

4. **Look for patterns:** Look for patterns or similarities between the problem and previously solved problems. This can help you identify a suitable approach or algorithm to solve the problem.

5. **Use prior knowledge:** Use your prior knowledge and experience to identify relevant concepts, techniques, and algorithms that can be applied to solve the problem.

6. **Iterate:** Iterate through the solution process, testing and refining your solution until it meets the requirements of the problem.

7. **Evaluate the solution:** Evaluate your solution against the problem requirements and constraints. Identify any weaknesses in the solution, and look for ways to improve it.

8. **Document the solution:** Document your solution, including the approach, algorithm, and any relevant code or diagrams. This will help you and others understand and reproduce the solution in the future.

By following these heuristics, you can approach problem-solving in a structured and efficient manner. These strategies will help you break down complex problems, identify suitable approaches, and evaluate your solutions effectively. Good luck with your studies!
