## Unit 2 - PROBLEM SOLVING METHODS

In this unit, we will be discussing various problem-solving methods that can be used to tackle complex problems. These problem-solving methods are widely used in various fields such as engineering, computer science, mathematics, and business. By the end of this unit, you will have a good understanding of different approaches to problem-solving and be able to apply them to real-world problems.

Here are some of the problem-solving methods that we will cover in this unit:

1. **Trial and Error Method:** This method involves trying different solutions until the correct one is found. It is often used in situations where there are limited resources or time.

2. **Root Cause Analysis:** This method involves identifying the underlying causes of a problem, rather than just treating the symptoms. By addressing the root cause of a problem, it is possible to prevent the problem from occurring again in the future.

3. **Brainstorming:** This method involves generating a large number of ideas, without worrying about their feasibility or practicality. This approach can help to identify new and innovative solutions to problems.

4. **Fishbone Diagram:** This method involves creating a visual representation of the different factors that contribute to a problem. By analyzing these factors, it is possible to identify the root cause of the problem.

5. **Six Sigma:** This method involves using statistical analysis to identify and eliminate defects in a process. It is often used in manufacturing and production processes.

6. **Pareto Analysis:** This method involves identifying the most significant factors contributing to a problem. By focusing on these factors, it is possible to address the root cause of the problem and improve overall performance.

7. **SWOT Analysis:** This method involves analyzing the strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats of a project or organization. By identifying these factors, it is possible to develop strategies to improve performance and achieve goals.

In conclusion, problem-solving is an essential skill that is required in almost every field. By learning these different problem-solving methods, you will be able to approach complex problems with confidence and find innovative solutions that can lead to improved performance and success.
