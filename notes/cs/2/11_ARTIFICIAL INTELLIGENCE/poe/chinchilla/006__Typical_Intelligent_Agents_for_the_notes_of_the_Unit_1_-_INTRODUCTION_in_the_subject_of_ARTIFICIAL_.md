### Typical Intelligent Agents

Intelligent agents are computer programs that are capable of performing tasks autonomously without human intervention. These agents are designed to interact with their environment and make decisions based on the information they receive. There are different types of intelligent agents, each with its own characteristics and capabilities. In this section, we will discuss some of the typical intelligent agents.

1. Simple reflex agents
- These agents are the simplest type of intelligent agents.
- They operate based on a set of pre-defined rules and make decisions based on the current state of the environment.
- They do not have any internal state or memory.

2. Model-based reflex agents
- These agents have an internal model of the environment.
- They use this model to make decisions based on the current state of the environment and their goals.
- They can handle situations where the environment is not fully observable.

3. Goal-based agents
- These agents have a goal or set of goals that they are trying to achieve.
- They use their internal model of the environment to determine the best course of action to achieve their goals.
- They can handle situations where the environment is not fully observable or where there are multiple possible actions to take.

4. Utility-based agents
- These agents make decisions based on a utility function.
- The utility function assigns a value to each possible action that the agent can take.
- The agent chooses the action that maximizes its expected utility.

5. Learning agents
- These agents are capable of learning from their interactions with the environment.
- They can improve their decision-making abilities over time by adjusting their internal model of the environment.
- They can handle situations where the environment is complex and unpredictable.

In conclusion, there are different types of intelligent agents, each with its own characteristics and capabilities. The choice of the type of agent to use depends on the requirements of the specific application. Understanding the different types of agents is essential for developing effective intelligent systems.
