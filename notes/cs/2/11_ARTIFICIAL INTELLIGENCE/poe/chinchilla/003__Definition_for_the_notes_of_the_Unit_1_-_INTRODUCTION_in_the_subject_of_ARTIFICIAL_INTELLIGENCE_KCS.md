### Definition for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a rapidly evolving field that has the potential to revolutionize the way we live and work. It involves the development of intelligent machines that can perform tasks that typically require human intelligence, such as visual perception, speech recognition, decision-making, and language translation.

Here are some key definitions and concepts to keep in mind when studying AI:

1. Artificial Intelligence: It refers to the ability of machines to perform cognitive tasks that are typically associated with humans, such as perception, reasoning, and decision-making.

2. Machine Learning: It is a subset of AI that involves the development of algorithms and statistical models that enable machines to learn from data and improve their performance over time.

3. Deep Learning: It is a type of machine learning that involves the use of artificial neural networks to learn and improve performance on a specific task.

4. Neural Networks: They are a set of algorithms that are designed to recognize patterns in data. They are modeled after the structure and function of the human brain.

5. Natural Language Processing (NLP): It is a branch of AI that focuses on the interaction between computers and human language. It involves the development of algorithms that can understand and generate natural language.

6. Robotics: It is the branch of AI that deals with the design, construction, and operation of robots that can perform tasks autonomously or in collaboration with humans.

7. Expert Systems: They are computer programs that are designed to mimic the decision-making abilities of a human expert in a particular field.

8. Computer Vision: It is a field of study that focuses on enabling machines to interpret and understand visual information from the world around them.

9. Intelligent Agents: They are software programs that can perform specific tasks on behalf of a human user, such as scheduling appointments or making recommendations.

In conclusion, AI is a complex and rapidly evolving field that encompasses a wide range of technologies and applications. By understanding the key concepts and definitions outlined in this unit, you will be better equipped to navigate this exciting and dynamic field.
