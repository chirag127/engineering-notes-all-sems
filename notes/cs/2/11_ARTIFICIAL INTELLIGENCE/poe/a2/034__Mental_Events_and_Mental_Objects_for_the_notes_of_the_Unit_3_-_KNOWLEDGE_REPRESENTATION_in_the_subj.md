### Mental Events and Mental Objects

- Mental events refer to our internal experiences like perceptions, thoughts, memories, beliefs, intentions, emotions, etc. Our mind is full of such mental events happening all the time.
- Mental objects refer to the objects/entities that our mental events are about. For example, when we perceive or think about a chair, 'chair' is the mental object.
- Representing mental events and mental objects is a key challenge in building AI systems that can reason about the mental states of humans. We need ways to formally represent concepts, ideas, beliefs, goals, etc. in a computer.
- Some approaches for representing mental objects are:
  - Formal Concept Analysis: Hierarchies of objects and their properties
  - Frames: Representations of stereotypical situations and objects with slots for properties
  - Semantic networks: Graph structures representing relations between concepts
  - Scripts: Schematic knowledge about typical sequences of events
- Representing mental events is even more challenging and currently not very well understood. Some possibilities include:
  - Propositional attitudes: Beliefs, desires, intentions as propositions
  - Qualia: Subjective experiences and qualities of sensations
  - Emotions: Valence, arousal, expressions
