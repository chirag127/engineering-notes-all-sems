### Unification for the notes of the Unit 3 - KNOWLEDGE REPRESENTATION in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

1. Knowledge Representation - It is a way of representing knowledge in a formal manner which can be understood by computers. It represents knowledge about the world so that it can be used to solve complex problems.
2. Why is Knowledge Representation Required? - In AI, the ultimate goal is to create intelligent machines that can solve problems, take decisions and learn from experience. To achieve this, the machines must have knowledge about the world which can be used to reason about the problems. Hence, knowledge representation is important to store and reason about the knowledge.
3. Key Tasks of Knowledge Representation - The key tasks of knowledge representation are:
- Organizing knowledge: The knowledge must be organized in a logical and structured format.
- Simplifying Complex Concepts: Complex real-world concepts must be simplified while representing them.
- Sharing Knowledge: The represented knowledge must be such that it can be shared between multiple agents or systems.
- Reasoning: The representation must allow effective and efficient reasoning. The machines must be able to derive new conclusions from the available knowledge.
4. Requirements of a Good Knowledge Representation - The key requirements of a good knowledge representation are:
- Expressiveness: It should be expressive enough to represent a wide variety of knowledge.
- Precision: The representation should be precise and unambiguous.
- Consistency: The knowledge should be consistent, i.e. there should not be any contradictory statements.
- Inference: It should be capable of enabling automated inference, i.e. deriving new conclusions from the available knowledge.
- Adaptability: The representation should be adaptable to accommodate changes in the knowledge.
