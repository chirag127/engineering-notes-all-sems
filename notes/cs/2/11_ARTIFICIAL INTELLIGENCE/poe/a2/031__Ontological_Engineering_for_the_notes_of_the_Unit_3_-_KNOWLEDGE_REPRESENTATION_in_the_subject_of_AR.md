### Ontological Engineering for the notes of the Unit 3 - KNOWLEDGE REPRESENTATION in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

1. Ontology is a formal representation of knowledge as a set of concepts within a domain and the relationships between those concepts.
2. Ontologies are used to reason about the entities in that domain and may be used to deduce new knowledge.
3. Ontology engineering is the process of actually building ontologies. It is a multidisciplinary field involving areas such as artificial intelligence, databases, software engineering, knowledge engineering, linguistics, and philosophy.
4. The key activities in ontology engineering are:
- Determining the domain and scope of the ontology
- Considering reusing and borrowing existing ontologies
- Enumerating important terms in the ontology
- Defining the classes and the class hierarchy
- Defining the properties of classes and relationships between classes
- Creating instances
- Providing documentation for the ontology
- Evaluating the ontology
5. Some of the challenging issues in ontology engineering are:
- Dealing with changing domains and contexts
- Combining multiple ontologies (ontology mapping)
- Handling inconsistencies and errors
- Evaluating the quality of ontologies
- Methods and tools to support the ontology engineering process
