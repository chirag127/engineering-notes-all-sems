### Problem solving Methods for the notes of the Unit 2 - PROBLEM SOLVING METHODS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

1. Trial and Error
- A random approach where solutions are tried and checked until the correct one is found
- Not efficient for complex problems but can be useful for small, discrete problems

2. Hill Climbing
- A local search approach where neighbouring solutions are explored and the best one is chosen as the next candidate solution
- Can get stuck in local optima and not find the global optimal solution

3. Beam Search
- Explores a fixed number of candidate solutions (the beam) at each step instead of just one as in Hill Climbing
- Can avoid getting stuck in local optima but still not guaranteed to find the global optimal solution

4. Genetic Algorithms
- Inspired by natural evolution and selection
- A population of candidate solutions is bred over multiple generations using processes like mutation and crossover to produce better solutions
- Useful for problems where the solution space is large and complex
