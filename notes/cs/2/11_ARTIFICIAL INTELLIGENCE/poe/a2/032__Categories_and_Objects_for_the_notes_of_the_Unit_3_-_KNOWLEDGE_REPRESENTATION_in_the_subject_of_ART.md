### Categories and Objects for the notes of the Unit 3 - KNOWLEDGE REPRESENTATION in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

1. Categories
- Classes or types of objects. e.g. Color, Shape, Animal, etc.
- In knowledge representation, categories are represented using concepts.
- A category can have subcategories. e.g. Animal can have subcategories like Mammals, Reptiles, Birds, etc.

2. Objects
- Specific instances of a category. e.g. Red, Circle, Dog, etc.
- In knowledge representation, objects are represented using instances.
- Each object belongs to one or more categories. e.g. Red belongs to Color category, Circle belongs to Shape category and Dog belongs to Animal category.

3. Taxonomy
- Hierarchical structure of categories.
- Shows genus-species relationships between categories.
- Helps in classifying objects under appropriate categories.
- e.g. The taxonomy of Dog can be: Kingdom -> Animalia -> Phylum -> Chordata -> Class -> Mammalia -> Order -> Carnivora -> Family -> Canidae -> Genus -> Canis -> Species -> C. familiaris
