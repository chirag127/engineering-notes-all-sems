### Information Extraction for the notes of the Unit 5 - APPLICATIONS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

1. Information Extraction is the task of automatically extracting structured information from unstructured and/or semi-structured machine-readable documents.
2. The key steps in Information Extraction are:
- Text Segmentation - Dividing the text into sentences and paragraphs.
- Named Entity Recognition - Identifying and classifying named entities into pre-defined categories like person names, locations, organizations, etc.
- Coreference Resolution - Resolving different expressions that refer to the same entity.
- Relationship Extraction - Identifying relationships between entities or events.
- Event Extraction - Identifying events, their participants and attributes from the text.
3. The applications of Information Extraction include:
- Automated summarization of documents and news articles.
- Filling out of web forms in an automated manner.
- Maintaining and updating databases.
- Question Answering systems.
- Sentiment Analysis.
- Trend Analysis.
