### Characteristics of Intelligent Agents

1. Perceptual ability: The ability to use input sensors to detect changes in the environment and recognize patterns. The inputs could be visual, textual, auditory, etc.
2. Memory: The ability to store representations of input patterns and logical conclusions.
3. Learning: The ability to improve performance over time based on experience. This could be improving the accuracy of pattern matching or improving the effectiveness of problem-solving strategies.
4. Natural language processing: The ability to communicate with humans in human language, both written and spoken.
5. Reasoning: The logical ability to solve problems, make inferences, draw conclusions, and make decisions. This could include abilities like classification, prediction, planning, and problem solving.
6. Problem solving: The ability to formulate problems, evaluate possible solutions, and take actions towards a goal. This may involve searching large possibility spaces, recognizing and avoiding dead ends, making subgoals, and learning from both successes and failures.
7. Creativity: The ability to think "outside the box" and generate unique and valuable ideas or artifacts. This is one of the most challenging capabilities to develop in AI systems.
8. Social skills: The ability to recognize emotions, understand cultural norms, and interact politely and helpfully. This includes abilities like emotional intelligence, empathy, politeness, and teamwork.
