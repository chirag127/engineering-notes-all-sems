### Knowledge Representation for the notes of the Unit 3 - KNOWLEDGE REPRESENTATION in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

1. Knowledge Representation deals with representing knowledge in a computer-understandable manner. It is required to reason about knowledge.
2. Some key KR methods are:
- Propositional Logic: Uses propositions/ sentences and logical connectives like AND, OR, NOT to represent knowledge. Limited expressiveness.
- First Order Logic (FOL): Uses variables, quantifiers, functions, predicates to represent knowledge. More expressive than Propositional logic.
- Frames: Represents knowledge in terms of objects, properties of objects, and relations between objects.
- Rules: If-Then rules to represent knowledge. Can be used to infer new facts.
- Scripts: Represent stereotypical sequences of events. Help in understanding stories, etc.
- Conceptual Graphs: Graphical way to represent knowledge. Nodes represent concepts and links represent relations.
3. The choice of KR method depends on the domain and task. There is no single best method. Need to choose a suitable, computational methodology.
4. Sound and Complete KR methods are desirable as they do not derive incorrect conclusions and can derive all correct conclusions respectively. However, most KR methods are not sound and complete.
