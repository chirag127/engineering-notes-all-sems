### Events for the notes of the Unit 3 - KNOWLEDGE REPRESENTATION in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

1. Knowledge Representation - Representing knowledge in a format that can be used by a computer is known as Knowledge Representation. It is required to store the knowledge in a computer and to use it for various AI tasks like reasoning, problem solving, planning, learning, etc.

2. Reasons for Knowledge Representation - There are three main reasons for Knowledge Representation:

- To share knowledge: It enables knowledge to be shared between people, computers and other machines.
- To be accessible to automated reasoning: The represented knowledge can be used by automated reasoning systems to deduce new conclusions, answer questions, solve problems, etc.
- To be persistent: The represented knowledge can be stored and used even after the creator of the knowledge is not available. It persists beyond the lifetime of its creator.

3. Requirements of Knowledge Representation - There are certain requirements or characteristics that a good knowledge representation scheme must satisfy:

- Correctness: The represented knowledge must be correct and consistent.
- Clarity: The representation must be clear and unambiguous.
- Completeness: All the available knowledge must be represented.
- conciseness: It must be concise and not repetitive.
- Coherence: The represented ideas must be logically coherent.
- Computability: The representation scheme must be computable. The knowledge must be represented in a format that can be processed by a computer.

4. Issues in Knowledge Representation - There are a number of issues and challenges in Knowledge Representation:

- Incompleteness: It is difficult to represent all the available knowledge.
- Inconsistency: The knowledge from multiple sources can be inconsistent. It is difficult to deal with inconsistent knowledge.
- Vagueness: The real world knowledge is often vague and uncertain. It is difficult to represent vague and uncertain knowledge.
- Knowledge Acquisition Bottleneck: Acquiring knowledge and representing it in a computer- understandable form is difficult.
- Volume of Knowledge: The volume of knowledge is very huge. It is difficult to represent this vast amount of knowledge.
- Dynamics of Knowledge: Knowledge is not static. It keeps changing over time. It is difficult to efficiently represent the dynamics of knowledge.
