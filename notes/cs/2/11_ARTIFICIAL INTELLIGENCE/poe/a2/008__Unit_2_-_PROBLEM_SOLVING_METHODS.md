## Unit 2 - PROBLEM SOLVING METHODS

1. Brainstorming
- Generating multiple solutions without criticism
- Freewheeling generation of ideas
- Facilitates creativity
- Builds on ideas of others

2. Flowchart
- Graphical representation of steps/logic
- Connects steps in a process and shows sequence
- Aids in identification of issues and solutions
- Easy to understand

3. Trial-and-Error
- Practical, hands-on approach
- Trying various solutions till right one is found
- Useful when solutions are easy to try and evaluate
- Time-consuming if many solutions need to be evaluated

4. Cause and Effect Analysis
- Identifying all possible causes for a problem
- Analyzing the effects of each cause
- Helps determine root cause and understand the problem thoroughly
- May not be suitable for complex problems with many inter-related causes
