### Typical Intelligent Agents

- Expert Systems: Expert systems are AI systems that contain encoded knowledge and inference procedures to solve problems in a specific domain that normally requires human expertise. They are designed to perform at the level of a human expert. The knowledge is represented in the form of if-then rules.
- Fuzzy Systems: Fuzzy systems are built on fuzzy logic and are capable of reasoning based on vague or uncertain information. They make decisions based on degrees of truth rather than the usual true or false. They are useful in cases where precise computations are not possible.
- Neural Networks: Neural networks are inspired by the human brain and are made up of interconnected nodes that must be trained on large amounts of data to detect patterns and learn how to perform a task. They are useful for tasks like pattern recognition, classification, prediction, etc.
- Robotics: Robotics combines AI with mechanical engineering to create intelligent agents that can perform physical tasks. Robots are able to perform actions in the real world and often use techniques like computer vision and fuzzy systems to sense and interact with their environment.
- Speech Recognition Systems: Speech recognition systems convert speech into text and allow individuals to interact with computers and devices through voice. They use techniques like hidden Markov models and deep neural networks to learn patterns in speech and match new input to the correct text output.
