### Unification

Unification is a process in automated reasoning and logic programming that attempts to identify two expressions with each other by finding a substitution that makes them equal. It is used in the field of artificial intelligence, particularly in the area of knowledge representation.

5. Unification is used to solve equations and to match patterns in logic programming.
