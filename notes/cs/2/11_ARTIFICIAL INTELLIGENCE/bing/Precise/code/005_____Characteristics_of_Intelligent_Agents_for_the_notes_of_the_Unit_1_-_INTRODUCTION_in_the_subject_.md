### Characteristics of Intelligent Agents

An intelligent agent is a system that can perceive its environment, reason about it, and take actions to achieve its goals. Here are some characteristics of intelligent agents:

1. **Autonomy**: Intelligent agents are capable of operating without human intervention. They can make decisions and take actions on their own.

2. **Adaptability**: Intelligent agents can learn from their experiences and adapt their behavior accordingly. They can improve their performance over time.

3. **Goal-oriented**: Intelligent agents have specific goals that they strive to achieve. They take actions that are aimed at achieving their goals.

4. **Reactivity**: Intelligent agents can perceive their environment and respond to changes in it. They can react to new situations in a timely manner.

5. **Proactivity**: Intelligent agents can take the initiative and act on their own. They do not simply react to their environment, but can also act in anticipation of future events.

6. **Social ability**: Intelligent agents can interact with other agents and humans. They can communicate, cooperate, and coordinate their actions with others.
