### Typical Intelligent Agents

An intelligent agent is a system that can perceive its environment, reason about it, and take actions to achieve its goals. There are several types of intelligent agents, including:

1. **Simple reflex agents**: These agents select actions based on the current percept, ignoring the rest of the percept history. They operate using condition-action rules, also known as if-then rules.

2. **Model-based reflex agents**: These agents maintain an internal model of the world, which they use to choose actions. They use their percept history to update their model of the world and select actions based on this model.

3. **Goal-based agents**: These agents choose actions based on their goals. They use their percept history and their internal model of the world to determine which actions will bring them closer to achieving their goals.

4. **Utility-based agents**: These agents choose actions based on their utility function, which assigns a value to each possible state of the world. They use their percept history and their internal model of the world to determine which actions will maximize their utility.

5. **Learning agents**: These agents can learn from their experiences and improve their performance over time. They use their percept history and their internal model of the world to learn and update their decision-making process.

These are some of the typical intelligent agents that can be found in the field of artificial intelligence. Each type of agent has its strengths and weaknesses, and the choice of agent depends on the specific problem at hand.
