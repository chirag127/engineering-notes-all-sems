## Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION

1. Introduction refers to the beginning or the opening of a topic, subject or a concept.
2. It is the first section of a piece of writing, speech or presentation.
3. The purpose of an introduction is to provide background information, define key terms and concepts, and engage the reader's interest.
4. An effective introduction should be clear, concise, and well-organized.
5. It should provide a roadmap for the rest of the content, outlining the main points and the direction of the argument or discussion.
6. In academic writing, the introduction is typically followed by a literature review, which provides an overview of the existing research on the topic.
7. The introduction is an important part of any piece of writing, as it sets the tone and provides the context for the rest of the content.
