### Unification

Unification is a process in automated reasoning and logic programming that is used to find a substitution that makes two terms identical. It is used in the field of Artificial Intelligence, specifically in the area of Knowledge Representation, as a way to represent and manipulate knowledge.

Here are some key points to remember about unification:

1. Unification is a process that finds a substitution to make two terms identical.
2. It is used in automated reasoning and logic programming.
3. Unification is used in the field of Artificial Intelligence, specifically in the area of Knowledge Representation.
4. It is a way to represent and manipulate knowledge.
