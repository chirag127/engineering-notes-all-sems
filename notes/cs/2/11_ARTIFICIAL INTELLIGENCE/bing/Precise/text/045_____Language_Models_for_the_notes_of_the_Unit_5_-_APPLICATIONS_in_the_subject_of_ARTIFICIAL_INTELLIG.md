### Language Models

Language models are a type of statistical model used in natural language processing to predict the likelihood of a sequence of words. They are used in a variety of applications, including speech recognition, machine translation, and text generation.

Some key points to consider when studying language models include:

1. Language models are trained on large amounts of text data to learn the patterns and relationships between words.
2. They can be used to generate text by predicting the next word in a sequence based on the previous words.
3. There are different types of language models, including n-gram models and neural network-based models.
4. N-gram models use a fixed-length sequence of words to predict the next word, while neural network-based models can take into account longer-range dependencies between words.
5. Language models can be fine-tuned for specific tasks, such as text classification or sentiment analysis.
