### Informed Search Methods

Informed search in AI is a type of search algorithm that uses additional information to guide the search process, allowing for more efficient problem-solving compared to uninformed search algorithms. This information can be in the form of heuristics, estimates of cost, or other relevant data to prioritize which states to expand and explore .

In Artificial Intelligence, search techniques are universal problem-solving methods. Rational agents or problem-solving agents in AI mostly use these search strategies or algorithms to solve a specific problem and provide the best result. Problem-solving agents are goal-based agents and use atomic representation .

In summary, informed search methods in AI use additional information to guide the search process and are used by rational or problem-solving agents to solve specific problems and provide the best result. These methods are more efficient than uninformed search algorithms and can help prioritize which states to expand and explore.
