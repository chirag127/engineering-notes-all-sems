# Forward Chaining

Forward chaining is a method of reasoning in artificial intelligence where the inference engine starts with the available data and uses inference rules to extract more data until a goal is reached. It is a data-driven approach, also known as bottom-up reasoning, where the system starts with the facts and works its way up to the conclusion.

Here are some key points to remember about forward chaining:

1. Forward chaining is a data-driven approach to reasoning.
2. It starts with the available data and uses inference rules to extract more data.
3. The process continues until a goal is reached.
4. It is also known as bottom-up reasoning.
5. Forward chaining is commonly used in rule-based systems and expert systems.
