## Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION

1. Introduction is the first section of any written work.
2. It provides the reader with an overview of the topic and sets the context for the rest of the work.
3. The introduction should be clear, concise, and engaging, and should provide the reader with a reason to continue reading.
4. It should also include a thesis statement, which is a sentence that summarizes the main argument or point of the work.
5. The introduction is an important part of any written work, as it sets the tone for the rest of the work and helps the reader understand the purpose and direction of the work.
