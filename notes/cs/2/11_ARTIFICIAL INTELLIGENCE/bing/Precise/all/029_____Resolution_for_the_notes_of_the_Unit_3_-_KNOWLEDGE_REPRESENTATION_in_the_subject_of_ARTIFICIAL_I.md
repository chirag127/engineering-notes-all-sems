# Unit 3 - KNOWLEDGE REPRESENTATION in ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE

### Resolution

- Resolution is an inference rule used in both Propositional and First-order Predicate Logic in different ways .
- It is used for proving the satisfiability of a sentence .
- The key idea for the resolution method is to use the knowledge base and negated goal to obtain a null clause (which indicates contradiction)  .
- Resolution method is also called Proof by Refutation  .
- Since the knowledge base itself is consistent, the contradiction must be introduced by a negated goal  .
