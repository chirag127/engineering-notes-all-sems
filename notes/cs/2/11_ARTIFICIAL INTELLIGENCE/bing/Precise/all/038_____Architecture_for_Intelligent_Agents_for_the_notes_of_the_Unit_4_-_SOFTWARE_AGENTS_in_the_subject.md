### Architecture for Intelligent Agents

#### Unit 4 - SOFTWARE AGENTS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

1. An intelligent agent is a system that can perceive its environment, reason about it, and take actions to achieve its goals.
2. The architecture of an intelligent agent refers to the underlying structure and organization of the system.
3. There are several common architectures for intelligent agents, including reactive, deliberative, hybrid, and layered architectures.
4. Reactive architectures are based on the idea that an agent should respond directly to changes in its environment without any internal reasoning or planning.
5. Deliberative architectures, on the other hand, involve more complex internal reasoning and planning processes to determine the best course of action.
6. Hybrid architectures combine elements of both reactive and deliberative architectures to balance the strengths and weaknesses of each approach.
7. Layered architectures organize the agent's reasoning and decision-making processes into a hierarchy of layers, with each layer responsible for a different level of abstraction or complexity.
8. The choice of architecture for an intelligent agent depends on the specific requirements and goals of the system, as well as the characteristics of the environment in which it operates.
