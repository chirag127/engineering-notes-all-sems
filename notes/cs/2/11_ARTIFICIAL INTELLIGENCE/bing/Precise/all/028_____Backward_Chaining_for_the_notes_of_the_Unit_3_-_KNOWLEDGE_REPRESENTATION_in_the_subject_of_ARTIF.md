### Backward Chaining

Backward chaining is a method of reasoning used in artificial intelligence and is commonly used in expert systems and rule-based systems. It is a form of inferencing that starts with the goal or conclusion and works backward to determine what facts must be true in order to reach that goal.

Here are some key points to remember about backward chaining:

1. Backward chaining is used to prove a goal by working backward from the goal to the facts.
2. It is a form of inferencing that starts with the goal or conclusion and works backward to determine what facts must be true in order to reach that goal.
3. Backward chaining is commonly used in expert systems and rule-based systems.
4. It is a powerful tool for problem-solving and decision-making.
5. Backward chaining can be used to solve complex problems by breaking them down into smaller, more manageable sub-problems.
