### Forward Chaining

Forward chaining is a popular inference method used in artificial intelligence and expert systems. It is a data-driven approach that starts with the available data and uses inference rules to extract more data until a goal is reached.

Here are some key points to remember about forward chaining:

1. Forward chaining is a bottom-up approach, starting with the available data and working towards the goal.
2. It is a data-driven approach, meaning that the inference process is driven by the available data.
3. Forward chaining uses inference rules to derive new facts from the available data.
4. The process continues until a goal is reached or no new facts can be derived.
5. Forward chaining is commonly used in rule-based expert systems, where the rules are used to derive new facts from the available data.

In summary, forward chaining is a powerful inference method used in artificial intelligence and expert systems to derive new facts from available data using inference rules. It is a bottom-up, data-driven approach that continues until a goal is reached or no new facts can be derived.
