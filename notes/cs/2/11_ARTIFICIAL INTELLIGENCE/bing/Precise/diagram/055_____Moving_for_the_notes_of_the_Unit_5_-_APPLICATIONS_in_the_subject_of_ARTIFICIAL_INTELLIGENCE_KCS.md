### Moving - Unit 5: APPLICATIONS - ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

1. Moving is the ability of an AI system to navigate and interact with its environment.
2. This can be achieved through various means such as locomotion, manipulation, and perception.
3. Locomotion refers to the ability of an AI system to move from one place to another. This can be achieved through various means such as walking, rolling, or flying.
4. Manipulation refers to the ability of an AI system to interact with objects in its environment. This can be achieved through various means such as grasping, pushing, or pulling.
5. Perception refers to the ability of an AI system to sense and interpret its environment. This can be achieved through various means such as vision, hearing, or touch.
6. AI systems can use these abilities to perform tasks such as navigation, search and rescue, and transportation.
7. Moving is an important application of AI as it allows AI systems to interact with the physical world and perform tasks that would otherwise be difficult or impossible for humans to do.
