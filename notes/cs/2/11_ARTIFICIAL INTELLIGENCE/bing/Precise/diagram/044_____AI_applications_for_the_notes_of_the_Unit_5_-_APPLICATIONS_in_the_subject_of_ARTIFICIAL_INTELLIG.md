3. **Transportation:** AI is used in transportation for route optimization, traffic management, and autonomous vehicles. AI can analyze large amounts of transportation data to identify patterns and make predictions, improving transportation efficiency and safety.

4. **Retail:** AI is used in retail for inventory management, personalized marketing, and customer service. AI can analyze large amounts of retail data to identify patterns and make predictions, improving retail operations and customer experience.

5. **Manufacturing:** AI is used in manufacturing for quality control, predictive maintenance, and supply chain management. AI can analyze large amounts of manufacturing data to identify patterns and make predictions, improving manufacturing efficiency and productivity.

6. **Education:** AI is used in education for personalized learning, grading, and student assessment. AI can analyze large amounts of educational data to identify patterns and make predictions, improving student outcomes and teacher effectiveness.

7. **Entertainment:** AI is used in entertainment for content recommendation, game development, and music composition. AI can analyze large amounts of entertainment data to identify patterns and make predictions, improving entertainment experiences.
