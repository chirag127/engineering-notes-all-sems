### Unification

Unification is a process in automated reasoning and logic programming that is used to find a substitution that makes two terms identical. It is a fundamental concept in the field of knowledge representation in artificial intelligence.

Here are some key points to remember about unification:

1. Unification is used to find a substitution that makes two terms identical.
2. It is a fundamental concept in the field of knowledge representation in artificial intelligence.
3. Unification is used in automated reasoning and logic programming.
4. The process of unification involves finding a substitution that makes two terms identical.
5. Unification is an important concept in the field of artificial intelligence and is used to represent knowledge in a structured and organized manner.
