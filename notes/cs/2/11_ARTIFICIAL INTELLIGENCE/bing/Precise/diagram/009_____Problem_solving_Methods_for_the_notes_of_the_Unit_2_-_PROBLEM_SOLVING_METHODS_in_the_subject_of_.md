### Unit 2 - PROBLEM SOLVING METHODS

#### Problem Solving Methods

Problem-solving methods are strategies and techniques used to find solutions to problems. In the field of artificial intelligence, problem-solving methods are used to develop intelligent systems that can solve complex problems. Some of the common problem-solving methods used in artificial intelligence are:

1. **Search and Optimization:** This method involves searching through a large set of possible solutions to find the best one. Optimization techniques are used to improve the efficiency of the search process.

2. **Logical Reasoning:** This method involves using logical rules to deduce new information from given facts. Logical reasoning is used to solve problems that can be represented as a set of logical statements.

3. **Probabilistic Reasoning:** This method involves using probability theory to represent and reason about uncertain information. Probabilistic reasoning is used to solve problems that involve uncertainty and incomplete information.

4. **Machine Learning:** This method involves using algorithms to learn from data and make predictions or decisions. Machine learning is used to solve problems that require the system to adapt and improve its performance over time.

5. **Expert Systems:** This method involves using knowledge-based systems that mimic the decision-making abilities of a human expert. Expert systems are used to solve problems that require specialized knowledge and expertise.

These are some of the common problem-solving methods used in artificial intelligence. Each method has its strengths and limitations, and the choice of method depends on the specific problem at hand. It is important to carefully analyze the problem and choose the most appropriate method for solving it.
