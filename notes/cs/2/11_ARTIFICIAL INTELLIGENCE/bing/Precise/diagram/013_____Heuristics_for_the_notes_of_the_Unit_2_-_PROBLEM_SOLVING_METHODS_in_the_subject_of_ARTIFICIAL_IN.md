### Heuristics for the notes of the Unit 2 - PROBLEM SOLVING METHODS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

Heuristics are problem-solving methods that use shortcuts to produce good-enough solutions given a limited time frame or deadline. Here are some key points to remember about heuristics:

1. Heuristics are often used when a problem is too complex or time-consuming to solve using an exact algorithm.
2. Heuristics can be thought of as "rules of thumb" or educated guesses that help guide the search for a solution.
3. Heuristics can be used to reduce the search space and make the problem more manageable.
4. Heuristics can be used in combination with other problem-solving methods, such as search algorithms or constraint satisfaction techniques.
5. Heuristics can be domain-specific, meaning that they are tailored to a particular problem or type of problem.
6. Heuristics can be learned through experience or through the use of machine learning techniques.
7. Heuristics are not guaranteed to find the optimal solution, but they can often find a good-enough solution in a reasonable amount of time.
8. Heuristics can be evaluated based on their effectiveness, efficiency, and ease of use.
