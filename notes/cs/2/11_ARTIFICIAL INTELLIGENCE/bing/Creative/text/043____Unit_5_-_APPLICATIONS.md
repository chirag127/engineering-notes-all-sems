## Unit 5 - APPLICATIONS

- In this unit, we will learn about some of the applications of artificial intelligence in various domains and industries.
- We will explore how AI can be used to solve problems, enhance productivity, improve customer experience, and create new opportunities.
- We will also discuss some of the challenges and limitations of AI, as well as the ethical and social implications of its use.
- The topics covered in this unit are:

  - AI for Healthcare: How AI can help diagnose diseases, provide personalized treatment, monitor patients, and assist medical professionals.
  - AI for Education: How AI can support learning, teaching, assessment, and feedback in different educational settings and levels.
  - AI for Business: How AI can optimize business processes, enhance decision making, automate tasks, and provide insights and recommendations.
  - AI for Entertainment: How AI can create and enhance content, such as music, art, games, and movies, and provide interactive and immersive experiences.
  - AI for Social Good: How AI can address global challenges, such as poverty, climate change, disaster relief, and human rights, and promote social justice and inclusion.
