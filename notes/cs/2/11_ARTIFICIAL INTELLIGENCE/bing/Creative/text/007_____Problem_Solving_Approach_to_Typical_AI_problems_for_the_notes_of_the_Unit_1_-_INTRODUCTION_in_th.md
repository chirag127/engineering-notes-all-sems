### Problem Solving Approach to Typical AI problems

- A typical AI problem is one that involves some form of intelligence, such as reasoning, planning, learning, perception, natural language processing, etc.
- A problem solving approach to AI is a method of finding solutions to such problems by applying some general principles and techniques of AI, such as search, knowledge representation, inference, etc.
- A problem solving approach to AI consists of the following steps:

  - **Problem formulation**: This step involves defining the problem precisely, specifying the initial state, the goal state, and the actions or operators that can change the state.
  - **Problem representation**: This step involves choosing a suitable way of encoding the problem, such as using logic, graphs, trees, etc. The representation should capture the essential features of the problem and facilitate the search for solutions.
  - **Search strategy**: This step involves selecting a method of exploring the space of possible solutions, such as using depth-first search, breadth-first search, heuristic search, etc. The search strategy should be efficient and effective in finding solutions.
  - **Solution evaluation**: This step involves checking whether the solution found by the search strategy satisfies the goal state and the constraints of the problem. The solution should also be optimal or near-optimal in terms of some criteria, such as time, cost, quality, etc.
  - **Solution execution**: This step involves implementing the solution in the real world, such as using a robot, a computer program, a natural language system, etc. The solution should be robust and adaptable to changing situations and feedback.
