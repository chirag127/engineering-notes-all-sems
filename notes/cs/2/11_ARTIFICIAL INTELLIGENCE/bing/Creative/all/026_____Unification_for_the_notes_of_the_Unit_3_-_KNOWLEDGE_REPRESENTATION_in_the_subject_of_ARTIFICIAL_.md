# Unification

- Unification is a process of finding a substitution that makes two terms or expressions identical.
- Unification is used in logic programming, automated theorem proving, and term rewriting systems.
- Unification can be seen as a generalization of pattern matching, where variables can occur on both sides of the equation.
- Unification can be performed on different kinds of terms, such as first-order terms, higher-order terms, lambda terms, etc.
- Unification can be classified into different types, such as syntactic unification, semantic unification, higher-order unification, etc.
- Unification can be implemented using different algorithms, such as Robinson's algorithm, Martelli-Montanari algorithm, Huet's algorithm, etc.
- Unification has various applications, such as query answering, resolution, type inference, natural language processing, etc.
