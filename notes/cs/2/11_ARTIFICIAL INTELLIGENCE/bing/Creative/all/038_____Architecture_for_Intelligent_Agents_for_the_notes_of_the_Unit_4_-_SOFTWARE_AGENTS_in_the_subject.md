# Architecture for Intelligent Agents

- An intelligent agent is a system that perceives its environment via sensors and acts rationally upon that environment with its effectors.
- The architecture of an agent describes its modules and capabilities, and how these operate together.
- The agent architecture determines how the agent function is implemented in the agent program.
- The agent function is a mathematical function that maps any given percept sequence to an action.
- The agent program is an algorithm that runs on the agent's architecture and implements the agent function.
- Agent = Architecture + Agent Program

## Types of Agent Architectures

- There are three primary types of agent architectures in AI:

  - Reactive agents: Reactive agents are the simplest type of AI agent. They act only on the basis of the current percept, without any memory or reasoning. They are fast and robust, but lack flexibility and adaptability. Examples of reactive agents are thermostats, obstacle-avoiding robots, and tic-tac-toe players.
  - Deliberative agents: Deliberative agents are more complex than reactive agents. They have an internal model of the world, which they update based on their percepts. They use reasoning and planning to decide what actions to take, based on their goals and beliefs. They are more flexible and adaptable, but also slower and more prone to errors. Examples of deliberative agents are chess players, route planners, and natural language translators.
  - Interactive agents: Interactive agents are the most advanced type of AI agent. They interact with other agents, including humans, in a social and cooperative way. They have a model of themselves, their environment, and other agents. They use communication, negotiation, and learning to achieve their goals and improve their performance. They are more intelligent and sociable, but also more complex and challenging to design. Examples of interactive agents are personal assistants, chatbots, and social robots.

## Applications of Intelligent Agents

- Intelligent agents have a wide range of applications in various domains, such as:

  - E-commerce: Intelligent agents can help customers find products, compare prices, negotiate deals, and make transactions online. They can also help sellers advertise, manage inventory, and provide customer service.
  - Education: Intelligent agents can act as tutors, mentors, or peers for learners, providing feedback, guidance, and motivation. They can also help teachers design, deliver, and evaluate courses.
  - Entertainment: Intelligent agents can create immersive and interactive experiences for users, such as games, movies, and virtual reality. They can also generate content, such as stories, music, and art.
  - Health: Intelligent agents can assist patients, doctors, and nurses in diagnosis, treatment, and monitoring of health conditions. They can also provide health information, advice, and support.
  - Security: Intelligent agents can protect systems, networks, and data from cyberattacks, malware, and unauthorized access. They can also detect, prevent, and respond to physical threats, such as fire, theft, and terrorism.
