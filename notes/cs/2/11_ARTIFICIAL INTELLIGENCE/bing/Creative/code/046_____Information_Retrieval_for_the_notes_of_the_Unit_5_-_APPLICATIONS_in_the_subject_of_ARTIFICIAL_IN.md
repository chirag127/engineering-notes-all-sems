### Information Retrieval for the notes of the Unit 5 - APPLICATIONS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

- Information retrieval (IR) is the process of finding relevant information from a large collection of documents, such as web pages, books, articles, etc., based on a user's query.
- Artificial intelligence (AI) is the branch of computer science that aims to create systems that can perform tasks that normally require human intelligence, such as reasoning, learning, planning, natural language processing, etc..
- AI methods are employed throughout the standard IR process and for novel value added services, such as personalization, recommendation, summarization, question answering, etc..
- The standard IR process consists of the following steps :
  - Document collection: The IR system gathers the documents that are relevant to the domain of interest, such as web crawling, database indexing, etc.
  - Document preprocessing: The IR system transforms the documents into a suitable representation for retrieval, such as tokenization, stemming, stopword removal, etc.
  - Query processing: The IR system interprets the user's query and converts it into a suitable representation for retrieval, such as natural language processing, query expansion, etc.
  - Document ranking: The IR system assigns a score to each document based on its relevance to the query, such as vector space model, probabilistic model, language model, etc.
  - Document retrieval: The IR system returns a ranked list of documents to the user, such as pagination, snippet generation, etc.
  - User feedback: The IR system collects the user's feedback on the retrieved documents and uses it to improve the retrieval performance, such as relevance feedback, clickthrough data, etc.
- AI methods can be applied to each of the above steps to enhance the IR system's capabilities, such as   :
  - Document collection: AI methods can help to discover new sources of information, such as social media, blogs, etc., and to filter out irrelevant or low-quality documents, such as spam, duplicates, etc.
  - Document preprocessing: AI methods can help to extract meaningful features from the documents, such as named entities, topics, sentiments, etc., and to reduce the dimensionality of the document representation, such as latent semantic analysis, latent Dirichlet allocation, etc.
  - Query processing: AI methods can help to understand the user's information need, such as query classification, query reformulation, etc., and to generate natural language queries from other modalities, such as speech, image, etc.
  - Document ranking: AI methods can help to model the relevance of documents to queries, such as learning to rank, neural networks, etc., and to incorporate various factors that affect the relevance, such as user preferences, context, etc.
  - Document retrieval: AI methods can help to present the retrieved documents in a user-friendly way, such as visualization, summarization, etc., and to provide interactive and conversational interfaces, such as chatbots, voice assistants, etc.
  - User feedback: AI methods can help to learn from the user's feedback, such as reinforcement learning, active learning, etc., and to personalize the retrieval results, such as collaborative filtering, content-based filtering, etc.
