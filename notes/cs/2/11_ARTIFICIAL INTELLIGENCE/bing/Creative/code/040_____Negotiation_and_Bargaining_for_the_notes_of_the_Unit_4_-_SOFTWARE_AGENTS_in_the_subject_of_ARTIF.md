### Negotiation and Bargaining for the notes of the Unit 4 - SOFTWARE AGENTS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

- Negotiation and bargaining are processes of reaching an agreement between two or more parties that have conflicting interests or preferences.
- Negotiation and bargaining can be applied to various domains, such as business, politics, law, diplomacy, and personal relationships.
- Negotiation and bargaining can involve human-human, human-agent, or agent-agent interactions, where agents are software entities that can act autonomously on behalf of humans or other agents  .
- Negotiation and bargaining can be classified into different types, such as distributive, integrative, cooperative, competitive, single-issue, multi-issue, one-shot, repeated, bilateral, or multilateral.
- Negotiation and bargaining can be modeled using different approaches, such as game theory, heuristic search, argumentation, dialogue, machine learning, or artificial neural networks  .
- Negotiation and bargaining can be supported or enhanced by artificial intelligence (AI) techniques, such as information retrieval, natural language processing, decision making, planning, reasoning, or learning    .
- Negotiation and bargaining can benefit from AI by reducing human cognitive biases, improving human negotiators' effectiveness, automating and optimizing negotiation outcomes, and facilitating cross-cultural and complex negotiations    .
- Negotiation and bargaining can also pose challenges and risks for AI, such as ethical, social, legal, and technical issues, such as fairness, transparency, accountability, privacy, security, and trust  .
