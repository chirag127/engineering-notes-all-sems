### Information Retrieval for the notes of the Unit 5 - APPLICATIONS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

- Information retrieval (IR) is a process of finding and accessing relevant information from a large collection of documents, such as web pages, books, articles, etc.
- IR systems can benefit from the application of artificial intelligence (AI) techniques to account for the intrinsic uncertainty and imprecision that characterize the subjectivity of this task
- AI methods are employed throughout the standard IR process and for novel value added services
- Some of the applications of AI in IR are:

  - Pattern recognition: AI techniques can be used to identify and classify patterns in the documents, such as topics, genres, sentiments, entities, etc. 
  - Representation: AI techniques can be used to represent the documents and the queries in a suitable format, such as vectors, graphs, ontologies, etc. 
  - Problem solving and planning: AI techniques can be used to formulate and execute strategies for retrieving relevant information, such as ranking, filtering, summarizing, etc. 
  - Heuristics: AI techniques can be used to devise and apply rules of thumb or shortcuts for improving the efficiency and effectiveness of IR, such as relevance feedback, query expansion, etc. 
  - Learning: AI techniques can be used to learn from the data and the user behavior and adapt the IR system accordingly, such as personalization, recommendation, etc. 

- Some of the examples of AI-based IR systems are:

  - Google: Google uses AI techniques such as natural language processing, machine learning, and deep learning to provide relevant and personalized search results, suggestions, and advertisements
  - Watson: Watson is an AI system developed by IBM that can answer natural language questions using a combination of knowledge representation, reasoning, and learning techniques
  - Alexa: Alexa is a virtual assistant developed by Amazon that can perform various tasks and services based on voice commands, using AI techniques such as speech recognition, natural language understanding, and dialog management
