# Unit 5 - APPLICATIONS

## Robot

A robot is a machine that can perform tasks autonomously or semi-autonomously, often with the help of artificial intelligence. AI can enable robots to sense, plan, learn, communicate, and act in complex and dynamic environments.

Some of the applications of AI in robotics are:

- **Industrial robotics**: AI can help robots to perform various tasks in manufacturing, such as assembly, welding, painting, quality inspection, and packaging. AI can also help robots to adapt to changing conditions, optimize production, and collaborate with humans .
- **Service robotics**: AI can help robots to provide various services to humans, such as customer service, health care, education, entertainment, and security. AI can enable robots to interact with humans using natural language, gestures, and emotions, and to personalize their services according to the user's preferences and needs .
- **Autonomous robotics**: AI can help robots to operate independently in unstructured and uncertain environments, such as self-driving cars, drones, underwater vehicles, and space exploration. AI can enable robots to perceive, navigate, map, and manipulate their surroundings, and to coordinate with other robots or agents .
- **Research robotics**: AI can help robots to advance the scientific understanding of various domains, such as biology, physics, chemistry, and psychology. AI can enable robots to conduct experiments, collect and analyze data, generate hypotheses, and discover new knowledge .
