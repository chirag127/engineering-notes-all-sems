## Unit 5 - APPLICATIONS

- This unit covers some of the common applications of artificial intelligence in various domains, such as natural language processing, computer vision, robotics, gaming, and decision making.
- The objectives of this unit are to:
  - Understand the basic concepts and techniques of natural language processing, such as tokenization, parsing, sentiment analysis, and machine translation.
  - Learn how computer vision enables machines to perceive and understand visual information, such as images, videos, and faces.
  - Explore the challenges and opportunities of robotics, such as navigation, manipulation, coordination, and human-robot interaction.
  - Discover how artificial intelligence can enhance gaming, such as creating intelligent agents, generating content, and designing adaptive gameplay.
  - Analyze how artificial intelligence can support decision making, such as optimization, planning, scheduling, and recommendation systems.
- The topics covered in this unit are:
  - Natural language processing
  - Computer vision
  - Robotics
  - Gaming
  - Decision making
