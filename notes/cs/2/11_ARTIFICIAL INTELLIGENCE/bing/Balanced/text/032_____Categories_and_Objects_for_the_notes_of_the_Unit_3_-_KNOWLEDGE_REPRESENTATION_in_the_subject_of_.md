### Categories and Objects

- A category is a group of entities that share some common properties or characteristics. For example, the category of animals includes all living beings that can move, breathe, and reproduce.
- An object is a specific instance of a category. For example, a dog is an object that belongs to the category of animals. An object has a unique identity and a set of attributes that describe its state and behavior.
