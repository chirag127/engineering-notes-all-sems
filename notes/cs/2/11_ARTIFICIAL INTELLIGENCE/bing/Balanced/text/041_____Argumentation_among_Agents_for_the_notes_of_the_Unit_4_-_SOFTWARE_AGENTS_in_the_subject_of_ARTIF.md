### Argumentation among Agents

- Argumentation is a process of reasoning and communication that involves the exchange and evaluation of arguments, where an argument is a claim supported by some evidence or reasons.
- Argumentation can be used by artificial agents to achieve various goals, such as persuasion, negotiation, deliberation, explanation, and learning.
- Argumentation can also be used to model and analyze human reasoning and dialogue, as well as to design and implement intelligent systems that can interact with humans or other agents in a rational and cooperative way.
- Argumentation in artificial intelligence is a multidisciplinary field that draws on theories and methods from logic, philosophy, linguistics, psychology, law, and computer science.
- Some of the main topics and challenges in argumentation in artificial intelligence are:

  - How to represent and reason with arguments, taking into account their structure, content, context, and uncertainty.
  - How to define and compute the acceptability or strength of arguments, based on different criteria and semantics.
  - How to design and implement argumentation protocols and systems that can support various types of interaction and dialogue among agents, such as inquiry, debate, negotiation, and deliberation.
  - How to evaluate and compare the performance and behavior of argumentation-based agents and systems, using empirical methods and benchmarks.
  - How to apply argumentation techniques and tools to various domains and applications, such as law, medicine, education, e-government, e-commerce, and social media.
