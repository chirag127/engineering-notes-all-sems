### Typical Intelligent Agents

- An intelligent agent (IA) is an entity that makes a decision, that enables artificial intelligence to be put into action.
- It can also be described as a software entity that conducts operations in the place of users or programs after sensing the environment.
- It uses actuators to initiate action in that environment.
- Intelligent agents in AI are autonomous entities that act upon an environment using sensors and actuators to achieve their goals.
- In addition, intelligent agents may learn from the environment to achieve those goals.
- Driverless cars and the Siri virtual assistant are examples of intelligent agents in AI.
- Intelligent agents in artificial intelligence are closely related to agents in economics, and versions of the intelligent agent paradigm are studied in cognitive science, ethics, the philosophy of practical reason, as well as in many interdisciplinary socio-cognitive modeling and computer social simulations.
- There are different types of intelligent agents in AI, such as:
  - Simple reflex agents: These agents act based on the current state of the environment, without considering the consequences of their actions or the history of the environment. They are the simplest and most limited type of agents.
  - Model-based reflex agents: These agents maintain an internal model of the environment, which they update based on their observations and actions. They use this model to choose the best action for the current situation, taking into account the possible outcomes of their actions.
  - Goal-based agents: These agents have a specific goal or set of goals that they want to achieve, and they act based on the expected utility of their actions. They use a model of the environment and a planning algorithm to find the optimal sequence of actions that will lead them to their goal.
  - Utility-based agents: These agents have a utility function that measures how desirable a state of the environment is for them, and they act based on the expected utility of their actions. They use a model of the environment and a decision-making algorithm to find the optimal action that will maximize their utility.
  - Learning agents: These agents have the ability to learn from their experience and improve their performance over time. They use a learning component that updates their knowledge or parameters based on their feedback, and a performance component that uses the learned knowledge or parameters to choose the best action.
