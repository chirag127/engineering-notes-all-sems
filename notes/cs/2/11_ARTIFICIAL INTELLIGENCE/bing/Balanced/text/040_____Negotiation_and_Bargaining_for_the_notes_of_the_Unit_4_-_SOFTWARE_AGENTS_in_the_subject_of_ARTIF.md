### Negotiation and Bargaining

- Negotiation is a process of reaching an agreement between two or more parties that have conflicting interests or preferences .
- Bargaining is a subset of negotiation that involves making offers and counteroffers until a deal is reached or rejected.
- Negotiation and bargaining can be applied to various domains, such as business, politics, law, diplomacy, and personal relationships .
- Negotiation and bargaining can be performed by humans, software agents, or a combination of both  .
- Software agents are autonomous programs that can act on behalf of humans or other agents in a negotiation or bargaining scenario  .
- Software agents can have different roles, such as negotiators, mediators, advisors, or facilitators  .
- Software agents can have different capabilities, such as communication, reasoning, learning, planning, and decision making  .
- Software agents can have different strategies, such as cooperative, competitive, or mixed-motive  .
- Software agents can have different objectives, such as maximizing their own utility, satisfying their preferences, or reaching a Pareto-optimal outcome  .
- Software agents can face different challenges, such as uncertainty, complexity, incompleteness, or inconsistency of information, as well as ethical, social, and legal issues  .
- Software agents can benefit from artificial intelligence techniques, such as machine learning, natural language processing, game theory, and multi-agent systems, to improve their negotiation and bargaining performance  .
- Software agents can also leverage human intelligence and emotions, such as empathy, trust, fairness, and rapport, to enhance their negotiation and bargaining outcomes  .
