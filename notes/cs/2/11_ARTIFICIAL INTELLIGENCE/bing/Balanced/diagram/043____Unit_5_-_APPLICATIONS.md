## Unit 5 - APPLICATIONS

- This unit covers some of the practical applications of artificial intelligence in various domains and industries.
- The topics include:
  - Natural language processing: how AI can understand, generate, and translate natural language texts and speech.
  - Computer vision: how AI can recognize, analyze, and manipulate images and videos.
  - Robotics: how AI can control and coordinate the actions of physical machines and devices.
  - Expert systems: how AI can encode and reason with human knowledge and expertise.
  - Machine learning: how AI can learn from data and improve its performance over time.
  - Artificial neural networks: how AI can model complex nonlinear functions and processes using interconnected nodes and layers.
  - Deep learning: how AI can use multiple layers of artificial neural networks to achieve state-of-the-art results in various tasks.
  - Reinforcement learning: how AI can learn from its own actions and rewards in dynamic and uncertain environments.
  - Game playing: how AI can play and win complex and strategic games against human or computer opponents.
  - Artificial creativity: how AI can generate novel and original outputs in various domains such as art, music, and literature.
  - Ethical and social issues: how AI can affect human values, rights, and responsibilities in various contexts and scenarios.
