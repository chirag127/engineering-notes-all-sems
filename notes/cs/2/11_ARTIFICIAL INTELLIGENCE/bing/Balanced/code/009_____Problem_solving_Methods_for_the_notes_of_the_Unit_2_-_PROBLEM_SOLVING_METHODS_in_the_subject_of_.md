### Problem solving Methods

- Problem solving methods are general strategies or techniques that can be applied to different types of problems in artificial intelligence.
- Problem solving methods can be classified into two main categories: **search-based methods** and **knowledge-based methods**.
- Search-based methods are methods that explore a large space of possible solutions or actions, and try to find the best one according to some criteria or objective function. Examples of search-based methods are:
  - **Blind search methods**: These methods do not use any domain-specific knowledge or heuristics, and rely on brute-force exploration of the search space. Examples of blind search methods are breadth-first search, depth-first search, and uniform-cost search.
  - **Heuristic search methods**: These methods use some domain-specific knowledge or heuristics to guide the search process and reduce the search space. Examples of heuristic search methods are best-first search, greedy search, A* search, and hill-climbing search.
  - **Optimization methods**: These methods try to find the optimal solution or action that maximizes or minimizes some objective function. Examples of optimization methods are genetic algorithms, simulated annealing, and gradient descent.
- Knowledge-based methods are methods that use some domain-specific knowledge or rules to solve problems or perform actions. Examples of knowledge-based methods are:
  - **Logic-based methods**: These methods use logic as a formal language to represent and reason about the problem domain and the solution. Examples of logic-based methods are propositional logic, first-order logic, and resolution.
  - **Rule-based methods**: These methods use rules as a way to represent and apply the knowledge or expertise of the problem domain. Examples of rule-based methods are production systems, expert systems, and inference engines.
  - **Frame-based methods**: These methods use frames as a way to represent and organize the knowledge or concepts of the problem domain. Examples of frame-based methods are semantic networks, conceptual graphs, and ontologies.
