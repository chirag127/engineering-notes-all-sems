### Quizzes for the notes of the Unit 3 - Technical Presentation in the subject of Technical Communication

Quizzes are a common assessment tool used in the Unit 3 - Technical Presentation of the subject of Technical Communication. They are used to evaluate a student's understanding of the material covered in class and to provide feedback on the student's progress.

Quizzes can take various forms, including:
1. Multiple choice: The student selects the correct answer from a list of options.
2. True/false: The student indicates whether a statement is true or false.
3. Short answer: The student provides a brief written response to a question.
4. Matching: The student matches terms or concepts with their definitions.
5. Fill-in-the-blank: The student provides a word or phrase to complete a sentence or statement.

Quizzes can be administered in a variety of ways, including:
1. In-class: The quiz is given during a class session and completed on paper or electronically.
2. Online: The quiz is posted online and completed by the student at a time convenient for them.
3. Homework: The quiz is assigned as homework and completed outside of class.

Quizzes are an effective way to assess a student's understanding of the material and provide feedback on their progress. They can also be used to identify areas where the student needs additional support or clarification. It is important to design quizzes that are fair, reliable, and valid measures of the student's understanding of the material.
