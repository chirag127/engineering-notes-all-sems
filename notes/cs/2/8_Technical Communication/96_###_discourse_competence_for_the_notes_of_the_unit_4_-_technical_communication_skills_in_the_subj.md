### Discourse competence for the notes of the Unit 4 - Technical Communication Skills in the subject of Technical Communication

Unit 4 of the Technical Communication course focuses on Technical Communication Skills, including the aspect of discourse competence.

Discourse competence refers to the ability to effectively communicate information and ideas in a specific context or situation. This includes the ability to understand and use the conventions and norms of a particular discipline or field.

In technical communication, discourse competence is critical for ensuring that information is communicated effectively and accurately. Technical writers must be familiar with the conventions and norms of their field, and must be able to use language and terminology appropriately.

In the context of the Technical Communication course, students in Unit 4 will learn about discourse competence as part of the Technical Communication Skills unit. This may include working with examples of discourse competence in technical communication, analyzing the impact of discourse competence on the effectiveness of technical communication, and learning strategies for developing discourse competence.
