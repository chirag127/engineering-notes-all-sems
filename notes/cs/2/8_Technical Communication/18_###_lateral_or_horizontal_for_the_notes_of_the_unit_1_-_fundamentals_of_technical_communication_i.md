### Lateral or Horizontal for the notes of the Unit 1 - Fundamentals of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication

Lateral or horizontal communication refers to the exchange of information between individuals or groups at the same organizational level. 

In the context of technical communication, lateral communication is important because it allows individuals or groups to share information and collaborate on projects. This can lead to more efficient and effective communication, as well as improved collaboration and problem-solving.

In the context of the Technical Communication course, students may learn about lateral communication as part of Unit 1, the Fundamentals of Technical Communication. This may include understanding the importance of lateral communication in the workplace, learning about different forms of lateral communication, and practicing lateral communication skills.
