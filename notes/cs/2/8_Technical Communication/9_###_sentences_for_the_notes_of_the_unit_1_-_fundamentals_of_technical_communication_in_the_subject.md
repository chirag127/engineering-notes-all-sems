### sentences for the notes of the Unit 1 - Fundamentals of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication

Sentences are the basic building blocks of written and spoken language. They are used to express a complete thought or idea, and they are composed of a subject and a predicate.

In technical communication, sentences are used to convey information and instructions in a clear and concise manner. Technical writers and communicators must be able to write effective sentences that are easy to understand and follow, and that accurately convey the intended message.

There are several types of sentences in technical communication, including simple sentences, compound sentences, complex sentences, and compound-complex sentences. Simple sentences contain a single independent clause, while compound sentences contain two or more independent clauses joined by a conjunction. Complex sentences contain an independent clause and one or more dependent clauses, while compound-complex sentences contain two or more independent clauses and one or more dependent clauses.

In this unit, we will study the fundamentals of technical communication, including the structure and function of sentences. We will also examine the use of different types of sentences in technical communication, and explore the strategies for writing effective sentences. This will provide a foundation for understanding the use of language in technical communication, and for exploring the various applications of technical communication in various fields.
