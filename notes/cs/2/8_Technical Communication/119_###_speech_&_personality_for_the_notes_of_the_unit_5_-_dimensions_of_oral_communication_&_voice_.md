### Speech & personality for the notes of the Unit 5 - Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics in the subject of Technical Communication

Speech and personality are two important dimensions of oral communication that can have a significant impact on the effectiveness of a message. In the Unit 5 - Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics in the subject of Technical Communication, students learn about the principles and techniques of effective oral communication and how to apply these principles in different contexts. The notes for this unit should include the following topics:

1. Definition of speech and personality in oral communication (the way a speaker communicates and the impact of personality on communication)
2. Overview of speech and personality in oral communication (types of messages, audience, purpose)
3. Characteristics of effective speech (clarity, conciseness, accuracy)
4. Techniques for improving speech (breathing, pronunciation, articulation)
5. The impact of personality on oral communication (confidence, charisma, sincerity)
6. Techniques for improving personality in oral communication (body language, tone of voice, eye contact)
7. Applications of speech and personality in different fields (design, engineering, science)
8. Limitations of speech and personality in oral communication (complexity of information, cultural differences)
9. Techniques for overcoming limitations (contextual information, visual aids)
10. Further reading and resources (books, journals, online resources)

Speech and personality are important dimensions of oral communication that can have a significant impact on the effectiveness of a message. Understanding the principles and techniques of speech and personality helps students to develop the skills required to communicate technical information effectively and efficiently in a variety of contexts.
