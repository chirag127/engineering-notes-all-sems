## Unit 1 - Fundamentals of Technical Communication

Unit 1 of Fundamentals of Technical Communication covers the basics of technical writing and communication. It introduces the principles and practices of effective technical communication, including audience analysis, purpose, and tone.

The unit covers various types of technical documents, including memos, reports, instructions, and proposals, and discusses the characteristics and features of each type of document. It also covers the process of writing technical documents, including pre-writing, drafting, and revising.

In addition, the unit covers the basics of technical style and tone, including the use of technical vocabulary, clear and concise writing, and the avoidance of ambiguity. The unit also covers the basics of document design, including the use of headings, subheadings, and visual aids to enhance the readability and effectiveness of technical documents.

The unit also covers the basics of ethical considerations in technical communication, including the use of accurate and credible sources, the proper citation of sources, and the avoidance of plagiarism.

In this unit, students will learn the skills and techniques necessary to effectively communicate technical information to a variety of audiences. This will provide a foundation for understanding the principles and practices of technical communication, and for exploring the various applications of technical communication in the workplace.
