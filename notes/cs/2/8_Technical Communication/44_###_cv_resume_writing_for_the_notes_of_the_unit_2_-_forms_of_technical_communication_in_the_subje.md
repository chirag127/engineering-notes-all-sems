### C.V./Resume writing for the notes of the Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication

Unit 2 of the Technical Communication course focuses on Forms of Technical Communication, including C.V./Resume writing.

A C.V. (Curriculum Vitae) or Resume is a document that provides a summary of a person's education, work experience, and skills. It is used to apply for jobs and to demonstrate a person's qualifications and experience to potential employers.

Effective C.V./Resume writing requires a clear and concise expression of the person's qualifications, experience, and skills. It is important to tailor the C.V./Resume to the specific job and industry, and to highlight the most relevant information for the reader.

In the context of the Technical Communication course, students in Unit 2 may learn about C.V./Resume writing as part of the Forms of Technical Communication. This may include working with examples of C.V./Resumes, analyzing the impact of C.V./Resume writing on the job search process, and learning strategies for writing effective C.V./Resumes.

In summary, Unit 2 of the Technical Communication course focuses on Forms of Technical Communication, and may include a discussion of C.V./Resume writing. A C.V./Resume is a document that provides a summary of a person's education, work experience, and skills, and is used to apply for jobs. Students will learn about C.V./Resume writing through interactive activities and hands-on experiences, and will learn strategies for writing effective C.V./Resumes.
