### Etiquette for the notes of the Unit 5 - Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics in the subject of Technical Communication

Etiquette refers to the set of conventions and customs that govern behavior in a particular social context. In the context of oral communication, etiquette refers to the conventions and customs that govern behavior during presentations and public speaking.

In technical communication, it is important to understand and follow the conventions and customs of oral communication, including:

1. Professionalism: Technical communicators must be professional in their appearance, behavior, and language, and they must be able to maintain a professional demeanor during presentations and public speaking.

2. Respect: Technical communicators must be respectful of their audience, and they must be able to effectively engage the audience and build rapport.

3. Timeliness: Technical communicators must be timely in their presentations and public speaking, and they must be able to effectively manage their time and stay within the allotted time for their presentation.

4. Confidence: Technical communicators must be confident in their presentations and public speaking, and they must be able to effectively convey their message and engage the audience.

In this unit, we will study the etiquette of oral communication, including the conventions and customs that govern behavior during presentations and public speaking. We will also examine the strategies for effectively following the conventions and customs of oral communication, and explore the use of etiquette in various applications, such as technical writing, technical editing, and technical illustration. This will provide a foundation for understanding the importance of etiquette in technical communication, and for exploring the various applications of technical communication in the workplace.
