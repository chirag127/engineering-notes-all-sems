### style for the notes of the Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication

Style refers to the way in which language is used to communicate a message. In technical communication, style is an important aspect of effective communication, as it affects the clarity and understanding of the message.

Technical writing style is characterized by clear, concise, and precise language. Technical writers use specific technical vocabulary and terminology, and they avoid the use of unnecessary words and jargon. Technical writing style is also objective and neutral, and it avoids the use of personal opinions and subjective language.

In addition to the use of clear and concise language, technical writing style also includes the use of visual aids, such as headings, subheadings, and tables, to help organize and present information. Technical writing style also includes the use of proper grammar, punctuation, and sentence structure, and it adheres to a consistent format and structure.

In this unit, we will study the style of technical communication, including the use of clear and concise language, the use of visual aids, and the conventions of technical writing. We will also examine the use of style in various forms of technical communication, and explore the strategies for writing in an effective and professional style. This will provide a foundation for understanding the importance of style in technical communication, and for exploring the various applications of technical communication in the workplace.
