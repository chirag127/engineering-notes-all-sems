### Strategic competence for the notes of the Unit 4 - Technical Communication Skills in the subject of Technical Communication

Strategic competence refers to the ability to use language effectively and appropriately in a given context. It involves understanding the purpose and audience of a communication and choosing the most appropriate language, tone, and format to achieve that purpose.

There are several components of strategic competence, including:
1. Contextual Awareness: Understanding the context in which communication is taking place, including the purpose, audience, and situation.
2. Discourse Analysis: Understanding the structure and organization of a text and how it is used to achieve a specific purpose.
3. Genre Awareness: Understanding the conventions and expectations of different types of texts, such as reports, emails, and presentations.
4. Rhetorical Awareness: Understanding the strategies and techniques used to persuade, inform, or entertain an audience.
5. Cultural Awareness: Understanding the impact of culture on communication and how to communicate effectively across cultural boundaries.

To develop strategic competence, it is important to practice using language in a variety of contexts and to reflect on the choices made when communicating. This can involve analyzing texts, practicing writing and speaking in different genres, and considering the impact of culture on communication.

In conclusion, strategic competence is a critical aspect of technical communication and is essential for success in a variety of professional settings. By understanding the context and purpose of communication, choosing the appropriate language, tone, and format, and reflecting on one's own communication practices, individuals can develop the skills necessary to communicate effectively and achieve their goals.
