### Definition for the notes of the Unit 1 - Fundamentals of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication

A definition is a statement that explains the meaning of a word or concept. In technical communication, definitions are used to clarify terms and concepts that are central to the topic being discussed.

Definitions can be given in several forms, including formal definitions, operational definitions, and contextual definitions. Formal definitions are precise and concise statements that provide a clear and accurate explanation of a term or concept. Operational definitions describe how a term or concept is used in a specific context, and they are often used in scientific and technical fields. Contextual definitions provide the meaning of a term or concept in relation to other terms or concepts, and they are often used in writing and speaking.

In technical communication, definitions are used to ensure that the intended meaning of terms and concepts is clear and consistent. This is particularly important in technical writing, where precise and accurate communication is essential for effective communication and understanding.

In this unit, we will study the fundamentals of technical communication, including the use of definitions. We will also examine the different forms of definitions, and explore the strategies for writing effective definitions. This will provide a foundation for understanding the use of language in technical communication, and for exploring the various applications of technical communication in various fields.
