### interpersonal Communication for the notes of the Unit 3 - Technical Presentation in the subject of Technical Communication

Unit 3 of the Technical Communication course focuses on Technical Presentation, including the aspect of interpersonal communication.

Interpersonal communication refers to the process of exchanging information, ideas, and feelings between two or more people. It is an important aspect of technical presentation, as it helps to establish rapport and engage the audience.

In technical presentations, interpersonal communication can be achieved through the use of eye contact, body language, tone of voice, and other nonverbal cues. Technical presenters must also be aware of the audience's reactions to their presentation and adjust their communication accordingly.

In the context of the Technical Communication course, students in Unit 3 will learn about interpersonal communication as part of the Technical Presentation unit. This may include working with examples of interpersonal communication in technical presentations, analyzing the impact of interpersonal communication on the effectiveness of technical presentations, and learning strategies for using interpersonal communication in technical presentations.
