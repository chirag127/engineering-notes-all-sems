### Vowel sounds for the notes of the Unit 5 - Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics in the subject of Technical Communication

Unit 5 of the Technical Communication course focuses on Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics, including the aspect of vowel sounds.

Vowel sounds are the sounds produced by the vocal cords when the air flow is not obstructed. In oral communication, vowel sounds are important for the clarity and intelligibility of speech.

There are several factors that can affect vowel sounds, including the position of the tongue, the shape of the mouth, and the tension in the vocal cords. In order to produce clear and intelligible vowel sounds, speakers must be able to control these factors and produce vowel sounds that are consistent and well-articulated.

In the context of the Technical Communication course, students in Unit 5 will learn about vowel sounds as part of the Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics unit. This may include working with examples of different vowel sounds, learning about the factors that affect vowel sounds, and practicing producing clear and intelligible vowel sounds.
