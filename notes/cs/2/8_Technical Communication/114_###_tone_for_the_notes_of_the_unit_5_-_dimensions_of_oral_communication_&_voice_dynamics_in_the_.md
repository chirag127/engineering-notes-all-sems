### Tone for the notes of the Unit 5 - Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics in the subject of Technical Communication

Tone refers to the attitude or feeling conveyed in a technical communication, including oral communication. In the Unit 5 - Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics in the subject of Technical Communication, students learn about the principles and techniques of effective oral communication and how to apply these principles in different contexts. The notes for this unit should include the following topics:

1. Definition of tone in oral communication (attitude or feeling conveyed in speech)
2. Overview of tone in oral communication (types, purposes, audiences)
3. Characteristics of effective tone in oral communication (clarity, conciseness, accuracy)
4. Techniques for developing tone in oral communication (body language, vocal variety, emphasis)
5. Guidelines for effective tone in oral communication (organization, tone, format)
6. Techniques for improving tone in technical speaking (clear delivery, concise language, visual aids)
7. Applications of tone in different fields (presentations, interviews, negotiations)
8. Limitations of tone in oral communication (cultural differences, language barriers)
9. Techniques for overcoming limitations (contextual information, visual aids)
10. Further reading and resources (books, journals, online resources)

Tone is an important aspect of oral communication and helps to convey the attitude or feeling of the speaker effectively. Understanding the principles and techniques of tone helps students to develop the skills required to communicate technical information accurately and efficiently in a variety of contexts.
