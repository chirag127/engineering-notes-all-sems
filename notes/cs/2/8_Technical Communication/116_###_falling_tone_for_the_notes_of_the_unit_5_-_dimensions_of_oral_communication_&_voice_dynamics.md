### Falling Tone for the notes of the Unit 5 - Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics in the subject of Technical Communication

Falling tone, also known as a descending tone or a falling intonation, is a type of voice inflection that is characterized by a decrease in pitch at the end of a sentence or phrase. In oral communication, the falling tone is often used to convey a sense of finality or to indicate the end of a thought or statement.

The falling tone is an important aspect of voice dynamics, as it can greatly impact the effectiveness of oral communication. The use of a falling tone can help to convey the meaning and intent of the speaker, and it can also help to engage the audience and to maintain their attention.

In technical communication, the falling tone can be used to emphasize key points, to signal the end of a thought or statement, or to convey a sense of finality. Technical communicators must be able to effectively use the falling tone to enhance the clarity and effectiveness of their message, and to ensure that their message is effectively received and understood by the audience.
