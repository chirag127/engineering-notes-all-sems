### Distinction between General and Technical Communication for the notes of the Unit 1 - Fundamentals of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication

Unit 1 of the Technical Communication course covers the Fundamentals of Technical Communication, including the distinction between general and technical communication.

General communication refers to everyday forms of communication, such as conversation, writing, and media. It is designed to convey information, opinions, and ideas to a general audience.

Technical communication, on the other hand, is a specialized form of communication that is used to convey technical information to a specific audience. Technical communication is used in fields such as science, engineering, medicine, and technology, and is designed to inform, instruct, or persuade a technical audience.

In the context of the Technical Communication course, students will learn about the distinction between general and technical communication as part of Unit 1. This may include working with examples of general and technical communication, comparing and contrasting the two forms of communication, and understanding the unique features and requirements of technical communication.

In summary, Unit 1 of the Technical Communication course covers the Fundamentals of Technical Communication, and includes a discussion of the distinction between general and technical communication. General communication is a form of communication used to convey information, opinions, and ideas to a general audience, while technical communication is a specialized form of communication used to convey technical information to a specific audience. Students will learn about the distinction between general and technical communication through interactive activities and hands-on experiences.
