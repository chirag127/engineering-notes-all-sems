### Impersonal for the notes of the Unit 3 - Technical Presentation in the subject of Technical Communication

Impersonal:
- Impersonal refers to a style of writing or speaking that is objective and avoids the use of personal pronouns (e.g. "I", "you").
- Impersonal style is often used in technical writing to maintain objectivity and professionalism.

Unit 3: Technical Presentation:
- Covers the skills and techniques required for effective technical presentations.
- Topics may include: Audience Analysis, Content Development, Presentation Design, Delivery Techniques, Visual Aids, etc.
- Audience Analysis: Understanding the needs, interests, and backgrounds of the audience to tailor the presentation.
- Content Development: Organizing information and developing clear and concise messages.
- Presentation Design: Creating an effective structure and format for the presentation.
- Delivery Techniques: Using body language, voice, and other techniques to engage the audience and deliver the message effectively.
- Visual Aids: Using graphics, slides, and other visual aids to support the presentation and enhance understanding.
