### Competence for the notes of the Unit 5 - Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics in the subject of Technical Communication

Unit 5 of the Technical Communication course focuses on Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics, including the aspect of competence.

Competence in oral communication refers to the ability to effectively communicate information and ideas through speech. This includes the ability to articulate ideas clearly and concisely, as well as the ability to engage the audience and respond to questions.

In technical communication, competence in oral communication is important for delivering presentations and participating in meetings and other types of interactions. Technical professionals must be able to effectively communicate technical information to their target audience, and must also be able to respond to questions and engage in discussion.

In the context of the Technical Communication course, students in Unit 5 will learn about competence in oral communication as part of the Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics unit. This may include working with examples of effective and ineffective oral communication, learning about the key elements of effective oral communication, and practicing oral communication skills through interactive activities.

Competence in oral communication refers to the ability to effectively communicate information and ideas through speech, and is important for delivering presentations and participating in meetings and other interactions. Students will learn about competence in oral communication through interactive activities and hands-on experiences, and will learn how to effectively communicate technical information to their target audience.
