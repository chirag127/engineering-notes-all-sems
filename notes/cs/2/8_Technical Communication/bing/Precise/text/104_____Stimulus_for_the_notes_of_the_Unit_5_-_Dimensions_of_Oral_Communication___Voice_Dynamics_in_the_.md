### Stimulus

Stimulus is an important concept in the study of oral communication and voice dynamics. Here are some key points to consider:

1. A stimulus is any event or situation that evokes a response.
2. In oral communication, stimuli can include verbal cues, such as the words spoken by a speaker, as well as nonverbal cues, such as tone of voice, facial expressions, and body language.
3. The way a speaker responds to a stimulus can affect the effectiveness of their communication.
4. Understanding the stimuli that affect communication can help speakers to improve their communication skills and better connect with their audience.
5. Stimuli can vary depending on the context of the communication, such as the setting, the relationship between the speaker and the audience, and the purpose of the communication.
6. Effective speakers are able to adapt their communication style to respond to different stimuli and achieve their communication goals.

These are some key points to consider when studying the role of stimulus in oral communication and voice dynamics. It is important to understand how stimuli can affect communication in order to improve one's communication skills and effectively connect with an audience.
