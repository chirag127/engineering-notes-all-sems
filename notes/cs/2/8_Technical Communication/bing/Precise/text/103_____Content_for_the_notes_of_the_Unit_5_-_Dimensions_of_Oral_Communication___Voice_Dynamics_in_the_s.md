### Content for the notes of the Unit 5 - Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics in the subject of Technical Communication

1. Oral communication refers to the spoken interaction between two or more individuals.
2. The dimensions of oral communication include the speaker, the message, the channel, the listener, the feedback, and the context.
3. Voice dynamics refers to the variations in pitch, volume, and rate of speech that can affect the delivery of a message.
4. Effective use of voice dynamics can enhance the clarity and impact of a message.
5. Techniques for improving voice dynamics include practicing proper breathing, articulation, and vocal variety.
6. It is important to consider the audience and the context when using voice dynamics in oral communication.
