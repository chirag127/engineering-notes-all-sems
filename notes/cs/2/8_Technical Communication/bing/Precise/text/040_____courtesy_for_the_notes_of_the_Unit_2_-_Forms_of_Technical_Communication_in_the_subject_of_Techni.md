### Courtesy

Courtesy is an important aspect of technical communication. It involves being polite, respectful, and considerate in your interactions with others. Here are some points to keep in mind when practicing courtesy in technical communication:

1. Use polite language and avoid using offensive or confrontational words or phrases.
2. Show respect for the opinions and ideas of others, even if you do not agree with them.
3. Be considerate of the needs and feelings of others, and try to accommodate them when possible.
4. Use proper titles and forms of address when communicating with others.
5. Avoid interrupting others when they are speaking, and listen attentively to what they have to say.
6. Respond promptly and courteously to requests for information or assistance.
7. Take responsibility for your actions and apologize if you have made a mistake or caused offense.

By practicing courtesy in your technical communication, you can help to foster positive relationships and create a more productive and collaborative working environment.
