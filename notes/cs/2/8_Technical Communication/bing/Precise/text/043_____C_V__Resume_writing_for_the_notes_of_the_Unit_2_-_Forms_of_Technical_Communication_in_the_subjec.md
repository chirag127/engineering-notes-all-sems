### C.V./Resume writing for the notes of the Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication

A Curriculum Vitae (C.V.) or a resume is a document that summarizes a person's educational and professional history, skills, and achievements. It is used to apply for jobs and is often the first point of contact between a job seeker and an employer.

Here are some points to consider when writing a C.V. or resume:

1. **Format**: Choose a clear and easy-to-read format for your C.V. or resume. Use headings, bullet points, and white space to make the document visually appealing and easy to scan.

2. **Length**: A C.V. is typically longer than a resume and can be two or more pages, while a resume is usually one or two pages. However, the length of the document may vary depending on the industry and the amount of experience the job seeker has.

3. **Content**: Include information about your education, work experience, skills, and achievements. Be sure to highlight any relevant experience and accomplishments that demonstrate your ability to perform the job you are applying for.

4. **Tailor your C.V. or resume**: Customize your C.V. or resume to the specific job you are applying for. Use keywords from the job description and highlight the skills and experience that are most relevant to the position.

5. **Proofread**: Make sure to carefully proofread your C.V. or resume to avoid any errors or typos. A well-written and error-free document can make a positive impression on the employer.

In summary, a C.V. or resume is an important document that summarizes a person's educational and professional history. When writing a C.V. or resume, it is important to choose a clear format, include relevant information, tailor the document to the specific job, and proofread carefully.
