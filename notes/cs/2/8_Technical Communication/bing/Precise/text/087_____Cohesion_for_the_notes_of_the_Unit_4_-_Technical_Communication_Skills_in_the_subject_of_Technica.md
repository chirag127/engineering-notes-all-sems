### Cohesion

Cohesion refers to the grammatical and lexical relationships between the different elements of a text. It is the property that creates a sense of unity and coherence in a piece of writing, making it easier for the reader to follow the writer's train of thought. Here are some key points to remember about cohesion in technical communication:

1. Cohesion can be achieved through the use of various linguistic devices, such as pronouns, conjunctions, and transitional phrases, which help to link sentences and paragraphs together.

2. Repetition of key words and phrases can also help to create cohesion, by reinforcing the main ideas and themes of the text.

3. Cohesion is closely related to coherence, which refers to the logical and consistent organization of ideas in a text. A cohesive text is not necessarily coherent, and vice versa.

4. In technical communication, it is important to strike a balance between cohesion and clarity. Overuse of cohesive devices can make a text difficult to read, while a lack of cohesion can make it difficult to follow the writer's train of thought.

5. Effective use of headings, subheadings, and formatting can also help to create a sense of cohesion in a technical document, by visually organizing the information and guiding the reader through the text.

6. In summary, cohesion is an important aspect of effective technical communication, as it helps to create a sense of unity and coherence in a text, making it easier for the reader to follow the writer's train of thought. It can be achieved through the use of various linguistic devices, as well as through effective organization and formatting. However, it is important to strike a balance between cohesion and clarity, to ensure that the text is both easy to read and easy to follow.
