### Rising Tone

- A rising tone is a type of intonation pattern in speech where the pitch of the voice increases over time.
- It is commonly used to indicate a question, uncertainty, or politeness.
- In the context of oral communication, using a rising tone can convey a sense of openness and willingness to engage in conversation.
- It can also be used to express interest or surprise.
- In some languages, a rising tone can change the meaning of a word or phrase.
- Voice dynamics, including the use of rising tone, can greatly impact the effectiveness of communication.
- It is important to be aware of and practice using different intonation patterns to improve communication skills.
