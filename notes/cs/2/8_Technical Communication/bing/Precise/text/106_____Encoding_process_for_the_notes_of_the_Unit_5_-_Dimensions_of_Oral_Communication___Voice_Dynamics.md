### Encoding process for the notes of the Unit 5 - Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics in the subject of Technical Communication

1. Encoding is the process of converting information into a form that can be transmitted and understood by the receiver.
2. In oral communication, the speaker encodes their message using verbal and nonverbal cues such as tone of voice, body language, and facial expressions.
3. The encoding process is influenced by the speaker's communication skills, their knowledge of the subject, and their ability to organize their thoughts and ideas.
4. Effective encoding requires the speaker to consider the audience and tailor their message to the audience's needs and level of understanding.
5. In the context of Unit 5 - Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics, encoding involves using vocal techniques such as pitch, volume, and inflection to convey meaning and emotion.
6. The speaker must also be aware of the cultural and social norms of the audience and adjust their communication style accordingly.
7. Effective encoding is essential for successful oral communication and can help to ensure that the message is received and understood as intended.
