### Interjections

Interjections are words or phrases used to express strong feelings or emotions. They are often used to convey surprise, excitement, or other strong reactions. Here are some key points to remember about interjections:

1. Interjections are typically used at the beginning of a sentence and are followed by an exclamation point or a comma.
2. They can be used to express a wide range of emotions, including happiness, anger, surprise, and disappointment.
3. Some common interjections include "wow," "ouch," "oh no," and "yay."
4. Interjections can be used in both formal and informal settings, but they are more common in informal speech.
5. In technical communication, interjections can be used to grab the audience's attention and convey a sense of urgency or importance.

These are some of the key points to remember about interjections in the context of technical communication. They can be a useful tool for engaging your audience and conveying strong emotions in your presentations.
