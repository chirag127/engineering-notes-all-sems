### Etiquette for the notes of the Unit 5 - Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics in the subject of Technical Communication

1. **Be prepared**: Know your topic and audience well before delivering your speech or presentation.
2. **Be clear and concise**: Speak clearly and concisely to effectively communicate your message.
3. **Use appropriate body language**: Use gestures, facial expressions, and eye contact to engage your audience and convey your message.
4. **Use appropriate tone and volume**: Speak in a tone and volume that is appropriate for the setting and audience.
5. **Be respectful**: Show respect for your audience by being polite and courteous.
6. **Listen actively**: Listen actively to your audience and respond appropriately to their feedback and questions.
7. **Use visual aids effectively**: Use visual aids to enhance your message and make it more engaging for your audience.
8. **Be confident**: Speak confidently to convey your message effectively and engage your audience.
9. **Practice good time management**: Manage your time effectively to ensure that you cover all of your points within the allotted time.
10. **Be adaptable**: Be prepared to adapt your presentation or speech to the needs and feedback of your audience.
