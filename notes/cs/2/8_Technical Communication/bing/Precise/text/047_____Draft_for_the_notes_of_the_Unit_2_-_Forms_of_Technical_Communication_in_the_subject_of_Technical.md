### Draft for the notes of the Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication

1. **Technical Reports:** Technical reports are documents that present and analyze scientific or technical data and information. They are often used to communicate the results of research, experiments, or other investigations.

2. **User Manuals:** User manuals are documents that provide instructions on how to use a product or service. They are often written for non-technical users and are designed to be easy to understand and follow.

3. **Proposals:** Proposals are documents that present a plan or idea for consideration. They are often used to request funding or support for a project or initiative.

4. **Specifications:** Specifications are documents that describe the requirements or characteristics of a product or service. They are often used to define the standards that must be met in order to ensure quality and consistency.

5. **White Papers:** White papers are documents that present information or arguments on a specific topic. They are often used to inform or persuade decision-makers or other stakeholders.

6. **Presentations:** Presentations are visual and oral forms of communication that are used to convey information or ideas to an audience. They often include slides, graphics, and other visual aids to help illustrate the points being made.

7. **Emails:** Emails are electronic messages that are used to communicate information or ideas. They are often used for quick, informal communication and can be sent to one or more recipients.

8. **Memos:** Memos are short, written messages that are used to communicate information or ideas within an organization. They are often used for internal communication and are typically more formal than emails.

9. **Technical Articles:** Technical articles are written pieces that present and analyze technical information or ideas. They are often published in trade journals or other industry publications and are intended for a professional audience.

10. **Websites:** Websites are collections of web pages that present information or ideas. They are often used to provide information about a company, product, or service and can include text, images, videos, and other multimedia content.
