### Definition for the notes of the Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication

Technical communication is a type of communication that conveys technical information to a specific audience for a specific purpose. It is a multidisciplinary field that involves the creation, design, and dissemination of technical content in various forms and formats.

Some common forms of technical communication include:

1. User manuals and guides: These provide instructions and information on how to use a product or service.
2. Technical reports: These are detailed documents that present research, analysis, or findings on a specific topic.
3. White papers: These are authoritative reports that provide information on a specific topic and are often used to inform or persuade decision-makers.
4. Proposals: These are documents that present a plan or idea to a potential client or customer.
5. Technical specifications: These are detailed descriptions of the features and requirements of a product or service.
6. Online help: This is a form of technical communication that provides assistance to users of a software application or website.

These are just a few examples of the many forms of technical communication. The goal of technical communication is to effectively convey technical information to a specific audience in a clear and concise manner.
