### Emphasis

- Emphasis is the technique of making certain information stand out in a piece of text.
- It is used to draw the reader's attention to specific words or phrases.
- Emphasis can be achieved through various means, such as font size, bold or italic text, underlining, or color.
- In technical communication, emphasis is often used to highlight important information, such as warnings or instructions.
- Care should be taken not to overuse emphasis, as it can make the text difficult to read and reduce its effectiveness.
- Emphasis should be used consistently throughout a document to avoid confusion.
- The use of emphasis should be guided by the principles of clarity and concision, and should always serve to enhance the reader's understanding of the text.
