### Completeness for the notes of the Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication

- Completeness is an essential aspect of technical communication.
- It refers to the inclusion of all necessary information in a document or message.
- A complete document or message should provide the reader with all the information they need to understand the topic or carry out a task.
- Incomplete information can lead to confusion, misunderstandings, and errors.
- To ensure completeness, the writer should consider the audience's needs and expectations, and include all relevant information.
- The writer should also organize the information in a logical and coherent manner, and use clear and concise language to convey the information.
- In some cases, it may be necessary to include additional resources, such as diagrams, tables, or references, to provide a complete understanding of the topic.
- It is important to review and revise the document or message to ensure that all necessary information has been included and that the information is presented in a clear and effective manner.
