### Syllables for the notes of the Unit 5 - Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics in the subject of Technical Communication

- A syllable is a unit of sound that forms a word or part of a word.
- Syllables are important in oral communication because they help to break down words into smaller, more manageable units of sound.
- Syllables can affect the rhythm, stress, and intonation of speech.
- In English, syllables can be divided into two main types: open and closed.
- Open syllables end in a vowel sound, while closed syllables end in a consonant sound.
- The number of syllables in a word can affect its pronunciation and stress pattern.
- Syllable division can also affect the spelling of certain words.
- Understanding syllables and their role in oral communication can help improve pronunciation, clarity, and overall effectiveness in speaking.
