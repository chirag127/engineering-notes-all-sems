### Grammatical for the notes of the Unit 4 - Technical Communication Skills in the subject of Technical Communication

- Grammar is the set of rules that govern the structure of sentences and the use of words in a language.
- Proper grammar is essential for clear and effective communication.
- Common grammatical errors include subject-verb agreement, pronoun-antecedent agreement, and incorrect use of punctuation.
- It is important to proofread and edit your writing to ensure that it is grammatically correct.
- There are many resources available to help improve your grammar, including grammar handbooks, online tutorials, and writing centers.
- Practicing and studying grammar can help improve your writing skills and make you a more effective communicator.
