### Conciseness for the notes of the Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication

- Conciseness refers to the use of the fewest and most effective words possible to convey a message.
- Concise writing is clear, direct, and to the point.
- Concise writing avoids unnecessary repetition, filler words, and overly complex language.
- Concise writing is important in technical communication because it helps the reader understand the information quickly and easily.
- To write concisely, focus on the main message, use active voice, and choose precise words.
- Avoid using jargon or technical terms unless necessary for the audience.
- Edit and revise your writing to remove any unnecessary words or phrases.
- Concise writing is not only more effective, but also more professional and easier to read.
