### Response for the notes of the Unit 5 - Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics in the subject of Technical Communication

1. Oral communication refers to the spoken interaction between two or more individuals.
2. It is an essential part of everyday life and is used in various contexts such as personal, social, and professional.
3. The dimensions of oral communication include the speaker, the message, the channel, the listener, and the feedback.
4. Voice dynamics refers to the variations in pitch, volume, and tone of voice that convey emotions and attitudes.
5. Effective use of voice dynamics can enhance the delivery of a message and make it more engaging for the listener.
6. In the context of technical communication, oral communication and voice dynamics play a crucial role in conveying technical information in a clear and concise manner.
7. It is important for technical communicators to develop strong oral communication skills and to be able to effectively use voice dynamics to engage their audience.
