### Vowel Sounds

Vowel sounds are an important aspect of oral communication and voice dynamics. Here are some key points to remember when studying vowel sounds for Unit 5 - Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics in the subject of Technical Communication:

1. Vowels are speech sounds produced by the relatively free passage of air through the mouth and throat.
2. In English, there are five vowel letters: A, E, I, O, and U. However, there are many more vowel sounds, as each vowel letter can represent multiple sounds.
3. Vowel sounds can be classified as either monophthongs or diphthongs. Monophthongs are vowel sounds that have a single, unchanging sound, while diphthongs are vowel sounds that consist of two distinct vowel sounds pronounced in close succession.
4. The quality of a vowel sound is determined by the position of the tongue and the shape of the lips when the sound is produced.
5. Vowel sounds can also be classified as either long or short, depending on the duration of the sound.
6. In oral communication, vowel sounds play a crucial role in conveying meaning and emotion. Proper pronunciation and enunciation of vowel sounds can greatly enhance the clarity and effectiveness of communication.
