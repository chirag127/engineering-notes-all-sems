### Interjections

Interjections are words or phrases used to express strong feelings or emotions. They are often used to convey surprise, excitement, or other strong reactions. Here are some key points to remember about interjections:

1. Interjections are typically used at the beginning of a sentence and are followed by an exclamation point or a comma.
2. They can be used to express a wide range of emotions, including happiness, anger, surprise, and disappointment.
3. Interjections are not grammatically connected to the rest of the sentence and can often stand alone.
4. Some common interjections include "Wow!", "Ouch!", "Oh no!", and "Yay!".
5. Interjections can also be used to attract attention or to fill a pause in conversation, such as "Um" or "Ahem".
6. In technical communication, interjections should be used sparingly and only when appropriate to convey a specific emotion or reaction.
