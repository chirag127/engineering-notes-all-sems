### Cohesion

Cohesion refers to the way in which the different parts of a text are connected and work together to create a coherent whole. It is an important aspect of effective technical communication, as it helps to ensure that the information presented is clear, well-organized, and easy to follow.

Some key points to consider when striving for cohesion in technical communication include:

1. Use of transitional words and phrases: These help to guide the reader through the text and show the relationships between different ideas.

2. Consistent use of terminology: Using the same terms consistently throughout a text can help to avoid confusion and improve clarity.

3. Clear organization: Organizing information in a logical and clear manner can help to improve the flow of the text and make it easier for the reader to follow.

4. Use of headings and subheadings: These can help to break up the text into manageable sections and provide a clear structure for the reader to follow.

5. Use of visual aids: Diagrams, tables, and other visual aids can help to illustrate complex ideas and make the information more accessible to the reader.

By paying attention to these and other aspects of cohesion, technical communicators can create effective and engaging content that is easy for their audience to understand and follow.
