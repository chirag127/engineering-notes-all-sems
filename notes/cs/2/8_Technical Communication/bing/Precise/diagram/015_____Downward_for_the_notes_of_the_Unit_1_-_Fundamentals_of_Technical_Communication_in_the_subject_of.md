### Unit 1 - Fundamentals of Technical Communication

#### Downward Communication

- Downward communication refers to the flow of information from higher levels of an organization to lower levels.
- It is typically used by managers to convey information, instructions, and expectations to employees.
- Examples of downward communication include company policies, procedures, and goals, as well as performance feedback and job instructions.
- Effective downward communication is essential for ensuring that employees understand their roles and responsibilities, and for fostering a sense of alignment and engagement within the organization.
- Some common methods of downward communication include meetings, emails, memos, and reports.
- To ensure that downward communication is effective, it is important for managers to communicate clearly and concisely, and to provide opportunities for feedback and clarification.
