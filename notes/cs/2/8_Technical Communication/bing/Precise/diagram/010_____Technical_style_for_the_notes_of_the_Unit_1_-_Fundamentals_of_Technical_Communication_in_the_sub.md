### Technical style for the notes of the Unit 1 - Fundamentals of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication

1. **Formality**: Technical writing should be formal and professional in tone. Avoid using colloquial language, slang, or contractions.
2. **Clarity**: The language used in technical writing should be clear and concise. Avoid using complex sentences or jargon that may confuse the reader.
3. **Accuracy**: Technical writing should be accurate and precise. Ensure that all facts, figures, and data are correct and up-to-date.
4. **Objectivity**: Technical writing should be objective and unbiased. Avoid expressing personal opinions or making subjective statements.
5. **Organization**: Technical writing should be well-organized and structured. Use headings, subheadings, and bullet points to break up the text and make it easier to read and understand.
6. **Visuals**: Technical writing often includes diagrams, charts, and other visual aids to help convey information. Ensure that all visuals are clear, relevant, and properly labeled.
7. **Consistency**: Technical writing should be consistent in terms of formatting, style, and language. Use the same font, spacing, and formatting throughout the document, and ensure that all terms and abbreviations are used consistently.
8. **Conciseness**: Technical writing should be concise and to the point. Avoid using unnecessary words or repeating information.
