### Distinction between General and Technical Communication

General communication and technical communication are two distinct forms of communication. Here are some key differences between the two:

1. **Purpose:** The purpose of general communication is to convey information, ideas, and opinions, while the purpose of technical communication is to convey technical information and instructions.

2. **Audience:** General communication is intended for a broad audience, while technical communication is intended for a specific audience, such as engineers, scientists, or technicians.

3. **Style:** General communication is often informal and conversational, while technical communication is formal and precise.

4. **Format:** General communication can take many forms, such as spoken, written, or visual, while technical communication often follows specific formats, such as user manuals, technical reports, or instructional videos.

5. **Content:** General communication covers a wide range of topics, while technical communication focuses on technical subjects, such as science, technology, engineering, or mathematics.

In summary, general communication and technical communication differ in their purpose, audience, style, format, and content. Technical communication is a specialized form of communication that conveys technical information and instructions to a specific audience.
