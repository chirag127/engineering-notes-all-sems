### Interpersonal Communication

Interpersonal communication is the process of exchanging information, ideas, and feelings between two or more people. This type of communication can take many forms, both verbal and nonverbal. Some common forms of interpersonal communication include speaking, writing, gestures, and body language.

Here are some key points to remember about interpersonal communication:

1. Interpersonal communication is a two-way process. Both parties must be actively involved in the exchange of information.
2. Effective interpersonal communication requires active listening. This means paying attention to what the other person is saying and responding appropriately.
3. Nonverbal communication, such as body language and tone of voice, can convey just as much meaning as the words being spoken.
4. Interpersonal communication can be influenced by many factors, including the relationship between the parties, the context of the communication, and the individual communication styles of the parties involved.
5. Effective interpersonal communication is essential for building and maintaining relationships, both personal and professional.
