### Strategies for the notes of the Unit 3 - Technical Presentation in the subject of Technical Communication

1. **Understand the purpose of the presentation**: Before taking notes, it is important to understand the purpose of the presentation. This will help you focus on the most important information and take more effective notes.

2. **Identify the main points**: During the presentation, try to identify the main points that the speaker is making. These points should be the focus of your notes.

3. **Use abbreviations and symbols**: To take notes quickly, use abbreviations and symbols to represent common words and phrases.

4. **Organize your notes**: Organize your notes in a way that makes sense to you. This could mean using headings, bullet points, or numbering to group related information together.

5. **Review and revise your notes**: After the presentation, review your notes and make any necessary revisions. This will help you retain the information and ensure that your notes are accurate and complete.

6. **Summarize the information**: Summarize the information in your notes in your own words. This will help you better understand and remember the information.

7. **Use visual aids**: If the presentation includes visual aids, such as slides or diagrams, incorporate these into your notes. This will help you better understand and remember the information.

8. **Ask questions**: If you have any questions or need clarification on any points, don't hesitate to ask the speaker. This will help you better understand the information and take more effective notes.
