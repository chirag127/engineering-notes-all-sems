### Emphasis

Emphasis is a principle of technical communication that refers to the highlighting of important information in a text. This can be achieved through various means, including:

1. **Typographical emphasis**: This includes the use of bold, italic, and underlined text to draw attention to specific words or phrases.

2. **Visual emphasis**: This includes the use of color, graphics, and other visual elements to highlight important information.

3. **Structural emphasis**: This includes the use of headings, subheadings, and bullet points to organize information and make it easier to find.

4. **Positional emphasis**: This includes placing important information at the beginning or end of a sentence, paragraph, or document, where it is more likely to be noticed.

Effective use of emphasis can help readers quickly identify and understand the most important information in a text. It is an essential tool for technical communicators, who must often convey complex information in a clear and concise manner.
