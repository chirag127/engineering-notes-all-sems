### Unit 1 - Fundamentals of Technical Communication: Reading & Comprehension

1. **Reading** is the process of decoding written symbols to extract meaning from them.
2. **Comprehension** is the ability to understand and interpret the meaning of written text.
3. Effective reading and comprehension skills are essential for successful communication in technical fields.
4. To improve reading and comprehension skills, one can practice active reading techniques such as previewing, questioning, and summarizing.
5. Previewing involves skimming the text to get an overview of its content and structure.
6. Questioning involves asking questions about the text to clarify understanding and engage with the material.
7. Summarizing involves condensing the main ideas of the text into a brief summary to aid retention and understanding.
8. It is also helpful to expand one's vocabulary and knowledge of technical terminology to improve reading and comprehension in technical fields.
9. In technical communication, it is important to read and comprehend technical documents such as manuals, reports, and specifications accurately and efficiently.
10. Effective reading and comprehension skills can also aid in the critical evaluation of technical information and the ability to communicate technical concepts to others.
