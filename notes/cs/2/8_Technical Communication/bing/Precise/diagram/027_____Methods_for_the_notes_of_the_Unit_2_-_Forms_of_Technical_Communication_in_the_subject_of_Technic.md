### Methods for the notes of the Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication

1. **Written Communication:** This includes traditional forms such as letters, memos, and reports, as well as modern forms such as emails, texts, and social media posts.
2. **Oral Communication:** This includes face-to-face conversations, telephone calls, video conferences, and presentations.
3. **Visual Communication:** This includes diagrams, charts, graphs, photographs, and videos.
4. **Nonverbal Communication:** This includes body language, facial expressions, and tone of voice.

Each of these forms of communication has its own strengths and weaknesses, and the most effective communicators are able to use a combination of these methods to convey their message. It is important to consider the audience, the purpose of the communication, and the context when choosing the most appropriate form of communication.
