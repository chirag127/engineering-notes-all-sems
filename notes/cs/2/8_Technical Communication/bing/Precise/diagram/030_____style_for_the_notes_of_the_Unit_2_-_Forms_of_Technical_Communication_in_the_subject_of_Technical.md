### Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication

1. **Reports:** Reports are written documents that present information in an organized format for a specific audience and purpose. They can be used to convey progress, results, or recommendations.

2. **Proposals:** Proposals are written documents that present a plan or idea to a specific audience in order to persuade them to take a certain course of action. They can be used to request funding, support, or approval for a project.

3. **Manuals:** Manuals are written documents that provide instructions or guidelines on how to perform a specific task or use a particular product. They can be used to help users understand how to operate, maintain, or troubleshoot a product.

4. **Emails:** Emails are electronic messages that are used to communicate information quickly and efficiently. They can be used to convey information, ask questions, or make requests.

5. **Memos:** Memos are brief written documents that are used to communicate information within an organization. They can be used to convey updates, announcements, or decisions.

6. **Presentations:** Presentations are visual and oral forms of communication that are used to convey information to an audience. They can be used to inform, persuade, or motivate.

7. **Websites:** Websites are collections of web pages that are used to convey information to a wide audience. They can be used to provide information about a company, product, or service.

8. **Social Media:** Social media platforms are used to communicate and share information with a wide audience. They can be used to promote a product, service, or event, or to engage with customers or followers.

9. **Video and Audio:** Video and audio recordings are used to convey information in a visual or auditory format. They can be used to demonstrate a product, provide training, or share information.

These are some of the common forms of technical communication. Each form has its own unique characteristics and can be used for different purposes. It is important to choose the appropriate form of communication for the specific audience and purpose.
