### Combination of Expression

Combination of expression refers to the use of multiple modes of communication to convey a message. In technical communication, it is important to use a combination of expression to effectively communicate complex information. Some common modes of expression used in technical communication include:

1. **Text:** Written language is a powerful tool for conveying information. It is precise, clear, and can be easily referenced.

2. **Visuals:** Visuals such as diagrams, charts, and graphs can help to convey complex information in an easily understandable format.

3. **Audio:** Audio can be used to convey information through speech, music, or sound effects.

4. **Video:** Video combines visual and audio elements to create a dynamic and engaging mode of communication.

5. **Interactive media:** Interactive media such as simulations, animations, and games can be used to engage the audience and help them to understand complex concepts.

Using a combination of these modes of expression can help to create effective technical communication that is clear, engaging, and easily understood. It is important to carefully consider the audience and the information being conveyed when choosing which modes of expression to use.
