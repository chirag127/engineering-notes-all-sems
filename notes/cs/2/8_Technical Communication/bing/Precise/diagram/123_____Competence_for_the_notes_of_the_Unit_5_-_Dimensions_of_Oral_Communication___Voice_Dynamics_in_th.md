### Competence

Competence is one of the key dimensions of oral communication and voice dynamics in technical communication. It refers to the ability to effectively communicate and convey information in a clear, concise, and accurate manner. Some key points to consider when discussing competence in oral communication include:

1. **Knowledge and understanding**: Competence in oral communication requires a thorough understanding of the subject matter being discussed. This includes having a strong grasp of technical terms, concepts, and ideas.

2. **Clarity and concision**: Effective oral communication requires the ability to convey information in a clear and concise manner. This means avoiding jargon, using simple language, and getting straight to the point.

3. **Accuracy**: Competence in oral communication also involves ensuring that the information being conveyed is accurate and up-to-date. This requires careful research and fact-checking.

4. **Confidence**: Competence in oral communication also involves projecting confidence when speaking. This can be achieved through good posture, eye contact, and a clear, steady voice.

5. **Adaptability**: Competent oral communicators are able to adapt their communication style to suit different audiences and situations. This may involve adjusting the tone, language, and level of detail used.

In summary, competence in oral communication involves a combination of knowledge, clarity, accuracy, confidence, and adaptability. By developing these skills, individuals can become more effective communicators in technical settings.
