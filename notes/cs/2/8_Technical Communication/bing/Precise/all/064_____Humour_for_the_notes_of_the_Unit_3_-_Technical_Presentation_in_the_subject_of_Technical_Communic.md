# Humour

Humour is the quality of being amusing or comic, especially as expressed in literature or speech. It is a tool that can be used to make an audience laugh or feel amused. Humour can be used in technical presentations to:

1. **Engage the audience:** Humour can help to grab the audience's attention and keep them engaged throughout the presentation.

2. **Make the presentation memorable:** Using humour can make the presentation more memorable for the audience, as they are more likely to remember something that made them laugh.

3. **Create a positive atmosphere:** Humour can help to create a positive atmosphere in the room, making the audience more receptive to the information being presented.

4. **Break up complex information:** Humour can be used to break up complex information, making it easier for the audience to understand and retain.

5. **Build rapport with the audience:** Using humour can help the presenter to build rapport with the audience, making them more likely to trust and listen to the presenter.

It is important to note that humour should be used appropriately and in moderation. The presenter should ensure that the humour is relevant to the topic and appropriate for the audience. Overuse of humour can detract from the presentation and make it less effective.
