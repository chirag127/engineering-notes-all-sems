# Emphasis

Emphasis is a technique used in technical communication to draw the reader's attention to specific information. It is an important tool for effective communication, as it helps the reader to focus on the most important points. Here are some ways to create emphasis in technical communication:

1. **Use headings and subheadings**: Headings and subheadings help to organize the information and make it easier for the reader to find the information they are looking for.

2. **Use formatting**: Bold, italic, and underlined text can be used to draw the reader's attention to specific words or phrases.

3. **Use lists**: Lists, whether ordered or unordered, can help to break up the text and make it easier to read. They also help to draw the reader's attention to specific points.

4. **Use graphics**: Graphics, such as charts, diagrams, and images, can help to illustrate a point and make it easier for the reader to understand.

5. **Use white space**: White space can help to break up the text and make it easier to read. It can also be used to draw the reader's attention to specific points.

6. **Use color**: Color can be used to highlight specific information and make it stand out from the rest of the text.

7. **Use contrast**: Contrast, such as using a different font or font size, can help to draw the reader's attention to specific information.

In summary, emphasis is an important tool in technical communication, and there are many ways to create emphasis, including the use of headings, formatting, lists, graphics, white space, color, and contrast. These techniques can be used individually or in combination to help the reader focus on the most important information.
