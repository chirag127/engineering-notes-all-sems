# Strategies for the notes of the Unit 3 - Technical Presentation in the subject of Technical Communication

1. **Understand the purpose of the presentation**: Before taking notes, it is important to understand the purpose of the presentation. This will help you to focus on the most important information and to organize your notes in a way that will be useful for you later.

2. **Identify the main points**: As you listen to the presentation, try to identify the main points that the speaker is making. These are the key ideas that you will want to remember and that will help you to understand the overall message of the presentation.

3. **Use abbreviations and symbols**: To take notes quickly, you can use abbreviations and symbols to represent common words and ideas. For example, you might use "+" to represent "and" or "w/" to represent "with."

4. **Organize your notes**: As you take notes, try to organize them in a way that makes sense to you. You might use headings, bullet points, or numbering to group related information together.

5. **Review and revise your notes**: After the presentation, take some time to review and revise your notes. This will help you to remember the information better and to identify any gaps or misunderstandings in your understanding.

6. **Use visual aids**: If the presentation includes visual aids, such as slides or diagrams, you can use these to help you take notes. You might copy the most important information from the visual aids into your notes, or you might use the visual aids as a reference when you are reviewing your notes later.

7. **Ask questions**: If you have any questions or if anything is unclear, don't be afraid to ask the speaker for clarification. This will help you to understand the information better and to take more accurate notes.
