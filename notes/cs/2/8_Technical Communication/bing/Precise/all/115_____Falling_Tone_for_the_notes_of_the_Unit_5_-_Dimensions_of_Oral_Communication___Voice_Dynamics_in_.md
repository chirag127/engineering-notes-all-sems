### Falling Tone

- Falling tone is a type of intonation pattern in speech.
- It is characterized by a downward pitch movement at the end of an utterance.
- Falling tone is commonly used to indicate the end of a statement or to convey finality.
- It can also be used to express certainty, assertiveness, or confidence.
- In English, falling tone is often used in declarative sentences, commands, and wh-questions.
- Falling tone can also be used to convey politeness or formality in certain contexts.
- In contrast, rising tone is often used to indicate a question or uncertainty.
- The use of falling tone can vary across languages and dialects.
- Proper use of falling tone can enhance the clarity and effectiveness of oral communication.
