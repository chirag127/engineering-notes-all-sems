### Completeness

Completeness is an important aspect of technical communication. It refers to the inclusion of all necessary information in a document or message. Here are some points to consider when ensuring completeness in technical communication:

1. Identify the purpose of the communication and the audience's needs. This will help determine what information is necessary to include.
2. Include all relevant facts, data, and details. This will help the audience understand the message and make informed decisions.
3. Use clear and concise language to convey the information. Avoid using jargon or technical terms that the audience may not understand.
4. Organize the information in a logical and easy-to-follow manner. This will help the audience find the information they need quickly.
5. Review the document or message to ensure that all necessary information has been included. If something is missing, add it in.

By following these steps, you can ensure that your technical communication is complete and effectively conveys the necessary information to the audience.
