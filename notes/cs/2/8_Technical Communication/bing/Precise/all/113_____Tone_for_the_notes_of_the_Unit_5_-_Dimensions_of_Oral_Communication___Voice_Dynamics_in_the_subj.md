# Unit 5 - Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics

### Tone

- Tone refers to the attitude or emotion conveyed through the speaker's voice.
- It is an important aspect of oral communication as it can greatly affect the listener's perception of the message being conveyed.
- Tone can be conveyed through various vocal elements such as pitch, volume, and inflection.
- A speaker's tone can convey emotions such as happiness, anger, sarcasm, or sincerity.
- It is important for a speaker to be aware of their tone and to use it effectively to convey their intended message.
- In technical communication, a formal and professional tone is often used to convey information in a clear and concise manner.
- However, depending on the context and audience, a speaker may choose to use a more casual or conversational tone to establish rapport with the listener.
- Effective use of tone can enhance the delivery of a message and improve the overall effectiveness of oral communication.
