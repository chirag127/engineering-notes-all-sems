# Encoding process for the notes of the Unit 5 - Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics in the subject of Technical Communication

1. Encoding is the process of converting information into a code or format that can be transmitted and understood by a receiver.
2. In oral communication, encoding involves the speaker converting their thoughts and ideas into spoken words and nonverbal cues.
3. The speaker must consider the audience and the context in which the message is being delivered in order to effectively encode their message.
4. Factors such as tone of voice, body language, and facial expressions can all impact the encoding process and the effectiveness of the message being delivered.
5. The encoding process is an essential component of effective oral communication and is critical to ensuring that the intended message is accurately conveyed to the audience.
