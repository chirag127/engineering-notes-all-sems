### Interjections

Interjections are words or phrases used to express strong feeling or sudden emotion. They are included in a sentence, usually at the beginning, to express a sentiment such as surprise, disgust, joy, excitement, or enthusiasm.

Here are some points to remember about interjections:

1. Interjections are usually one word or a short phrase and are often followed by an exclamation point.
2. They do not have a grammatical function in the sentence and do not affect the syntax.
3. Interjections can be used to convey a wide range of emotions and can be used in both formal and informal situations.
4. Some common interjections include "Oh!", "Wow!", "Ouch!", "Hey!", "Hooray!", and "Alas!".
5. Interjections can also be used to attract attention or to fill a pause in conversation, such as "Um", "Er", or "Ah".

In summary, interjections are used to express strong emotions or feelings and can add flavor and personality to writing and speech. They should be used sparingly in formal writing but can be used more freely in informal situations.
