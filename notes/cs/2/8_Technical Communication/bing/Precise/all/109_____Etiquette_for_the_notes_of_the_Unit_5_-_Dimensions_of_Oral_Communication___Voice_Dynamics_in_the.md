# Etiquette for the notes of the Unit 5 - Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics in the subject of Technical Communication

1. **Be prepared**: Before delivering any oral communication, it is important to be well-prepared with the content and the delivery method.
2. **Be clear and concise**: Oral communication should be clear and to the point. Avoid using complex language or jargon that may confuse the audience.
3. **Use appropriate tone and body language**: The tone of voice and body language play a crucial role in oral communication. Use a tone that is appropriate for the situation and audience, and use body language to reinforce the message.
4. **Listen actively**: Effective oral communication involves not only speaking but also listening. Listen actively to the audience and respond appropriately to their feedback and questions.
5. **Be respectful**: Show respect to the audience by using polite language and avoiding any offensive or inappropriate remarks.
6. **Use visual aids**: Visual aids can help to reinforce the message and make the communication more engaging. Use visual aids such as slides, charts, or diagrams to support the oral communication.
7. **Practice**: Practice delivering the oral communication to improve the delivery and build confidence.

These are some of the key points to keep in mind when preparing for and delivering oral communication in the context of technical communication. It is important to follow these etiquette guidelines to ensure effective and professional communication.
