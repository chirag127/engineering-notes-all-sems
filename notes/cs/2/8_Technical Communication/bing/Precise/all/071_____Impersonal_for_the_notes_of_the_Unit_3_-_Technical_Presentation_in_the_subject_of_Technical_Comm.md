# Impersonal

Impersonal writing is a style of writing that is objective and unbiased. It is often used in technical, scientific, and academic writing. Here are some key points to remember when writing in an impersonal style:

1. Use the third person point of view: Instead of using first person pronouns like "I" or "we," use third person pronouns like "he," "she," "it," or "they."
2. Avoid using contractions: Contractions like "don't" or "can't" are informal and should be avoided in impersonal writing. Instead, use the full form of the word, such as "do not" or "cannot."
3. Use passive voice: Passive voice can help to remove the focus from the writer or speaker and place it on the subject or action. For example, instead of saying "I conducted the experiment," you could say "The experiment was conducted."
4. Avoid using emotive language: Emotive language is language that is intended to evoke an emotional response from the reader. In impersonal writing, it is important to avoid using language that could be seen as biased or subjective.
5. Use precise and specific language: Impersonal writing should be clear and concise. Use specific and precise language to convey your message.

By following these guidelines, you can write in an impersonal style that is appropriate for technical, scientific, and academic writing. This style of writing can help to convey information in a clear and objective manner.
