### Combination of Expression

Combination of expression refers to the use of multiple modes of communication to convey a message. These modes can include verbal, nonverbal, written, and visual communication. In technical communication, it is important to effectively combine these modes to ensure that the intended message is clearly understood by the audience.

1. **Verbal communication** involves the use of spoken words to convey a message. This can include face-to-face conversations, telephone calls, and video conferences.

2. **Nonverbal communication** includes body language, facial expressions, and gestures. These cues can provide additional information and context to the spoken message.

3. **Written communication** involves the use of written words to convey a message. This can include emails, reports, and technical documentation.

4. **Visual communication** includes the use of images, diagrams, and videos to convey information. These visual aids can help to clarify complex concepts and make the information more accessible to the audience.

In technical communication, it is important to effectively combine these modes of expression to ensure that the intended message is clearly understood by the audience. This can involve using visual aids to support verbal explanations, providing written documentation to supplement verbal instructions, and using nonverbal cues to reinforce the spoken message. By effectively combining these modes of expression, technical communicators can ensure that their message is effectively conveyed to their audience.
