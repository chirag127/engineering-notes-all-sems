### Types of Technical Communication

Technical communication is a broad field that includes various types of documents and communication methods. Here are some common forms of technical communication:

1. **User manuals:** These documents provide instructions on how to use a product or service. They are often written for non-technical users and include step-by-step instructions, diagrams, and illustrations.

2. **Technical reports:** Technical reports are used to communicate the results of research, experiments, or other technical work. They are often written for a technical audience and include detailed information, data, and analysis.

3. **Specifications:** Specifications are detailed descriptions of the requirements for a product, service, or system. They are often used in the design and development process to ensure that the final product meets the desired standards.

4. **Proposals:** Proposals are documents that present a plan or idea for consideration. They are often used to request funding or support for a project and include detailed information about the proposed work, its benefits, and its costs.

5. **White papers:** White papers are documents that present information or arguments on a specific topic. They are often used to educate readers or to advocate for a particular position.

6. **Online help:** Online help is a form of technical communication that provides assistance to users of a software application or website. It often includes step-by-step instructions, frequently asked questions, and troubleshooting information.

These are just a few examples of the many forms of technical communication. The specific types of documents and communication methods used will vary depending on the needs of the audience and the goals of the communication.
