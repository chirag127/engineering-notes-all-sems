### Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication

1. **Reports:** Reports are written documents that present information in an organized format for a specific audience and purpose. They can be used to convey technical information, research findings, or recommendations.

2. **Proposals:** Proposals are written documents that outline a plan or idea and request approval or funding from a decision-maker. They are often used in business and government settings to present new ideas or projects.

3. **Manuals:** Manuals are written documents that provide instructions or guidelines on how to use a product or perform a task. They are often used in technical fields to provide detailed information on how to operate or maintain equipment.

4. **Emails:** Emails are electronic messages that are used to communicate information quickly and efficiently. They are often used in business and professional settings to exchange information or make requests.

5. **Memos:** Memos are short, informal written documents that are used to communicate information within an organization. They are often used to convey updates, announcements, or decisions.

6. **Presentations:** Presentations are visual and oral forms of communication that are used to convey information to an audience. They often include slides, graphics, and other visual aids to help convey the information.

7. **Websites:** Websites are collections of web pages that provide information on a particular topic or organization. They are often used to provide information to a wide audience and can include text, images, videos, and other multimedia content.

8. **Social Media:** Social media platforms are online tools that allow users to create and share content and interact with others. They are often used by businesses and organizations to engage with their audience and promote their products or services.
