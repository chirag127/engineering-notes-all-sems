### Cohesion

Cohesion refers to the way in which the different parts of a piece of writing are connected. It is an important aspect of technical communication, as it helps to ensure that the reader can easily follow the flow of ideas and information.

Here are some key points to consider when working to improve the cohesion of a piece of writing:

1. Use transitional words and phrases: Transitional words and phrases, such as "however," "in addition," and "on the other hand," can help to guide the reader from one idea to the next.

2. Repeat key words and phrases: Repeating key words and phrases can help to reinforce the connections between different parts of the text.

3. Use parallel structure: Using parallel structure, in which similar ideas are expressed using similar grammatical constructions, can help to create a sense of coherence and flow.

4. Use headings and subheadings: Headings and subheadings can help to organize the text and make it easier for the reader to follow.

5. Use consistent formatting: Consistent formatting, such as the use of bullet points or numbered lists, can help to visually reinforce the connections between different parts of the text.

By paying attention to these and other aspects of cohesion, it is possible to create technical communication that is clear, well-organized, and easy to follow.
