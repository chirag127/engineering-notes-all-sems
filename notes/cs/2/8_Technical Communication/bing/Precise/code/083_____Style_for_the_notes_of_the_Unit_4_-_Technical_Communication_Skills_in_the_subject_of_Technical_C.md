### Style for the notes of the Unit 4 - Technical Communication Skills in the subject of Technical Communication

1. **Clarity:** The language used should be clear and concise. Avoid using jargon or technical terms that the reader may not understand.
2. **Conciseness:** The content should be brief and to the point. Avoid using unnecessary words or phrases.
3. **Accuracy:** The information presented should be accurate and verifiable. Double-check all facts and figures before including them in the notes.
4. **Organization:** The content should be well-organized and structured in a logical manner. Use headings, subheadings, and bullet points to break up the text and make it easier to read.
5. **Tone:** The tone of the notes should be formal and professional. Avoid using colloquial language or slang.
6. **Format:** The notes should be formatted in a consistent and easy-to-read manner. Use a clear font and appropriate spacing to improve readability.
7. **Visuals:** Include relevant visuals, such as diagrams or charts, to help illustrate key points and concepts.
8. **References:** Cite all sources used in the notes to give credit where credit is due and to allow the reader to verify the information presented.
