### Thesis/Project writing for the notes of the Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication

- Thesis or project writing is a form of technical communication that involves presenting research or project work in a structured and formal manner.
- The purpose of thesis or project writing is to communicate the results of research or project work to a specific audience, such as academic peers, supervisors, or industry professionals.
- The structure of a thesis or project report typically includes an abstract, introduction, literature review, methodology, results, discussion, conclusion, and references.
- The abstract provides a brief summary of the research or project, including the research question, methodology, and key findings.
- The introduction provides background information on the topic and outlines the research question or project objectives.
- The literature review provides a critical analysis of existing research on the topic and identifies gaps in the literature that the current research or project aims to address.
- The methodology section describes the research design, data collection, and data analysis methods used in the study or project.
- The results section presents the findings of the research or project, including any data, graphs, or tables.
- The discussion section interprets the results and discusses their implications for the research question or project objectives.
- The conclusion summarizes the key findings of the research or project and provides recommendations for future research or project work.
- The references section lists all sources cited in the report, using a consistent citation style.
- It is important to follow the guidelines and formatting requirements of the institution or organization for which the thesis or project report is being written.
- Effective thesis or project writing requires careful planning, organization, and attention to detail. It is important to present the research or project work in a clear, concise, and professional manner.
