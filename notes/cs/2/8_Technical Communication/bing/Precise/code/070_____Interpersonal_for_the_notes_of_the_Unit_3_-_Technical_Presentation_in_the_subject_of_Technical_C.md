### Interpersonal

Interpersonal refers to the interactions and relationships between individuals. In the context of technical communication, interpersonal skills are essential for effective communication and collaboration. Here are some key points to consider when discussing interpersonal skills in technical presentations:

1. **Active Listening**: Active listening involves paying attention to what the speaker is saying, and providing feedback to show that you understand. This can help to build trust and rapport between the speaker and the audience.

2. **Nonverbal Communication**: Nonverbal communication includes body language, facial expressions, and tone of voice. These cues can convey important information about the speaker's emotions and intentions, and can help to reinforce the message being conveyed.

3. **Conflict Resolution**: Conflict is a natural part of any relationship, and it is important to have the skills to resolve conflicts in a constructive manner. This can involve finding common ground, and working towards a solution that is mutually beneficial.

4. **Empathy**: Empathy involves understanding and sharing the feelings of others. This can help to build stronger relationships, and can facilitate more effective communication.

5. **Assertiveness**: Assertiveness involves expressing your thoughts and feelings in a clear and direct manner, while also respecting the rights and opinions of others. This can help to ensure that your message is heard and understood, and can help to prevent misunderstandings.

These are just a few of the key interpersonal skills that are important for effective technical presentations. By developing and utilizing these skills, you can improve your ability to communicate and collaborate with others.
