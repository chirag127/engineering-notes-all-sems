### Syllables for the notes of the Unit 5 - Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics in the subject of Technical Communication

- A syllable is a unit of sound that forms a word or part of a word.
- It is typically made up of a vowel sound or a combination of vowel and consonant sounds.
- Syllables play an important role in oral communication as they help in the pronunciation of words and in the rhythm and flow of speech.
- In English, syllables can be divided into two types: stressed and unstressed.
- Stressed syllables are pronounced with more emphasis and are usually longer in duration than unstressed syllables.
- The correct use of syllable stress can greatly improve the clarity and effectiveness of oral communication.
- Syllable division rules can help in the correct pronunciation of words and in the identification of stressed and unstressed syllables.
- These rules include dividing a word between two consonants, dividing a word before a single consonant that is followed by a vowel, and dividing a word after a vowel that is followed by a single consonant.
- Practicing syllable division and stress can improve one's oral communication skills and voice dynamics.
