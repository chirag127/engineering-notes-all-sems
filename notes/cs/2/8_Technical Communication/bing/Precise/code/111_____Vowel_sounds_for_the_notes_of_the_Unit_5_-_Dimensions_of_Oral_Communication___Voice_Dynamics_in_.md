### Vowel sounds for the notes of the Unit 5 - Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics in the subject of Technical Communication

1. Vowels are speech sounds produced by the relatively free passage of air through the mouth, usually forming the nucleus of a syllable.
2. There are five vowel letters in the English alphabet: A, E, I, O, and U.
3. The sounds of vowels are determined by the position of the tongue and the shape of the lips when the sound is produced.
4. Vowels can be classified into two categories: monophthongs and diphthongs.
5. Monophthongs are vowel sounds that have a single, unchanging sound quality, while diphthongs are vowel sounds that consist of a glide from one vowel sound to another.
6. In English, there are 12 monophthongs and 8 diphthongs.
7. The quality of vowel sounds can also be affected by the surrounding consonants, a phenomenon known as coarticulation.
8. Proper use of vowel sounds is important for clear and effective communication, as mispronunciation of vowel sounds can lead to misunderstandings.
9. Practice and training can help improve the accuracy and clarity of vowel sound production.
