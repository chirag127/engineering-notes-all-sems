### Reading & Comprehension

Reading and comprehension are fundamental skills in technical communication. Here are some key points to consider when studying this topic:

1. **Active reading:** This involves engaging with the text, asking questions, and making connections to better understand the material.

2. **Vocabulary:** Understanding technical terms and jargon is essential for comprehending technical texts.

3. **Text structure:** Technical texts often have a specific structure, such as an introduction, methods, results, and conclusion. Understanding this structure can help with comprehension.

4. **Visual aids:** Technical texts often include diagrams, charts, and graphs to help convey information. Being able to interpret these visual aids is important for comprehension.

5. **Summarizing:** Being able to summarize the main points of a technical text is a useful skill for comprehension and retention of information.

6. **Critical thinking:** Evaluating the information presented in a technical text, considering its relevance and reliability, is an important aspect of comprehension.

In summary, reading and comprehension are essential skills in technical communication, and there are several strategies that can be employed to improve these skills, including active reading, understanding vocabulary and text structure, interpreting visual aids, summarizing, and critical thinking. These skills can be developed and improved with practice.
