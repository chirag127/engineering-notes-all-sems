### Methods for the notes of the Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication

1. **Written Communication:** This includes traditional forms such as letters, memos, and reports, as well as electronic forms such as email, instant messaging, and text messaging.
2. **Oral Communication:** This includes face-to-face conversations, telephone calls, video conferences, and presentations.
3. **Visual Communication:** This includes diagrams, charts, graphs, photographs, and videos.
4. **Nonverbal Communication:** This includes body language, facial expressions, and tone of voice.

Each form of communication has its own advantages and disadvantages, and the most effective communicators are able to use a combination of these forms to convey their message effectively. It is important to consider the audience, the purpose, and the context when choosing the most appropriate form of communication.
