### Interjections

Interjections are words or phrases used to express strong emotion or surprise. They are typically used in spoken language and informal writing and are often set apart from a sentence by an exclamation point or by a comma when the feeling is not as strong.

Here are some points to remember about interjections:

1. Interjections are used to express a wide range of emotions, including happiness, excitement, anger, surprise, and disgust.
2. They can be a single word, such as "Wow!" or "Ouch!" or a phrase, such as "Oh no!" or "Good grief!"
3. Interjections are often used at the beginning of a sentence, but they can also be used in the middle or at the end.
4. They do not have a grammatical connection to the rest of the sentence and can often be removed without changing the meaning of the sentence.
5. In formal writing, interjections should be used sparingly and only when appropriate to convey a strong emotion.
