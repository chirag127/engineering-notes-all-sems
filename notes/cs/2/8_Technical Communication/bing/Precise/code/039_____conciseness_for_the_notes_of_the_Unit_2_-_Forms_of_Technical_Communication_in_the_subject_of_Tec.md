### Conciseness for the notes of the Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication

- Conciseness refers to the use of the fewest and most effective words possible to convey a message.
- Concise writing is clear, direct, and avoids unnecessary repetition or elaboration.
- Conciseness is important in technical communication because it helps the reader to quickly understand the information being presented.
- To achieve conciseness, one should:
  - Use active voice instead of passive voice.
  - Avoid redundancy and repetition.
  - Use precise and specific language.
  - Eliminate unnecessary words and phrases.
  - Use lists and tables to present information in a clear and organized manner.
- Concise writing is not only more effective, but it also saves time and space, making it easier for the reader to access and understand the information being presented.
