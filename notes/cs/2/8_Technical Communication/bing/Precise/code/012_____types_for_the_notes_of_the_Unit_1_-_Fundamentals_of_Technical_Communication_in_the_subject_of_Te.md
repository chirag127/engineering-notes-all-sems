### Unit 1 - Fundamentals of Technical Communication

#### Types of Technical Communication

1. **Instructional Materials:** These include user manuals, tutorials, and other materials that provide step-by-step instructions on how to use a product or perform a task.

2. **Technical Reports:** These are detailed documents that present research findings, data analysis, and other technical information.

3. **Proposals:** These are documents that present a plan or idea for a project, product, or service, and are often used to persuade others to support or fund the proposal.

4. **Specifications:** These are detailed descriptions of the requirements and standards for a product, service, or system.

5. **White Papers:** These are authoritative reports that provide information on a specific topic, often used to present research findings or to provide a solution to a problem.

6. **Technical Manuals:** These are comprehensive guides that provide detailed information on how to use, maintain, and repair a product or system.

7. **Emails and Memos:** These are short, informal documents used to communicate information within an organization.

8. **Presentations:** These are visual and oral presentations of technical information, often used to explain complex concepts or to persuade others to support a proposal or idea.

9. **Websites and Online Help:** These are online resources that provide technical information and support to users.

10. **Technical Illustrations and Graphics:** These are visual representations of technical information, such as diagrams, charts, and graphs, used to clarify and explain complex concepts.
