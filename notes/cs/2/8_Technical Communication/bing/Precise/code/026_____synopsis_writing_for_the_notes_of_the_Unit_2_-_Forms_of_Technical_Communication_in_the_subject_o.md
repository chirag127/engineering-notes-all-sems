### Synopsis Writing for the Notes of the Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication in the Subject of Technical Communication

1. A synopsis is a brief summary of the main points of a written work.
2. It is used to provide an overview of the content and to help the reader quickly understand the main ideas.
3. In the context of technical communication, a synopsis can be used to summarize the key points of a technical document or report.
4. To write a synopsis, one should read the original work carefully and identify the main ideas and key points.
5. The synopsis should be concise and to the point, avoiding unnecessary details or repetition.
6. It is important to use clear and precise language when writing a synopsis, as it is intended to convey information quickly and efficiently.
7. A well-written synopsis can be a valuable tool for readers, helping them to quickly understand the content of a technical document or report.
