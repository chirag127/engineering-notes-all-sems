### Humour for the notes of the Unit 3 - Technical Presentation in the subject of Technical Communication

1. Humour is the quality of being amusing or comic, especially as expressed in literature or speech.
2. Humour can be used as a tool in technical presentations to engage the audience and make the presentation more memorable.
3. Humour can be used to lighten the mood and make complex or dry topics more accessible.
4. Humour can be used to build rapport with the audience and create a positive atmosphere.
5. Humour should be used appropriately and in moderation, as overuse or inappropriate use can detract from the presentation.
6. Humour should be relevant to the topic and audience, and should not be offensive or insensitive.
7. Humour can be incorporated into a technical presentation through the use of anecdotes, jokes, cartoons, or humorous visuals.
8. The use of humour in a technical presentation should be carefully planned and rehearsed to ensure its effectiveness.
9. Humour can be a powerful tool in technical communication, but it should be used with care and consideration.
