### Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication

1. **Technical Reports:** These are detailed documents that convey technical information to a specific audience. They are often used to communicate the results of research, experiments, or other technical work.

2. **User Manuals:** These are instructional documents that provide information on how to use a product or service. They are often written for non-technical users and include step-by-step instructions and illustrations.

3. **Proposals:** These are documents that present a plan or idea to a specific audience. They are often used to persuade the audience to take a specific action, such as funding a project or approving a new product.

4. **Technical Specifications:** These are detailed documents that describe the technical requirements of a product or service. They are often used by engineers and other technical professionals to ensure that a product meets certain standards.

5. **White Papers:** These are authoritative reports that provide information on a specific topic. They are often used to educate readers about a complex issue or to present a solution to a problem.

6. **Presentations:** These are visual and oral presentations that convey technical information to an audience. They often include slides, charts, and other visual aids to help the audience understand the information.

7. **Emails and Memos:** These are short, written communications that are used to convey technical information within an organization. They are often used to provide updates, make requests, or share information with colleagues.

8. **Technical Blogs and Articles:** These are online publications that provide technical information to a wider audience. They are often written by experts in a specific field and are used to share knowledge and insights with others.
