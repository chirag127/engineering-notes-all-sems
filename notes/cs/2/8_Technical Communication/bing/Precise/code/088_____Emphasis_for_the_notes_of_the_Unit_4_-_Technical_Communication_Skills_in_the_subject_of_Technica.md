### Emphasis

Emphasis is a principle of technical communication that refers to the use of various techniques to draw the reader's attention to specific information. Emphasis can be achieved through several means, including:

1. **Typography**: Bold, italic, and underlined text can be used to emphasize specific words or phrases. Font size and color can also be used to draw attention to important information.

2. **Layout**: The placement of information on the page can also be used to emphasize specific content. For example, placing important information at the top of the page or in the center of the page can draw the reader's attention.

3. **Visuals**: The use of visuals such as images, graphs, and charts can also be used to emphasize specific information. Visuals can be used to illustrate important concepts or to highlight key data.

4. **Lists**: The use of lists, such as bulleted or numbered lists, can also be used to emphasize specific information. Lists can be used to present information in a clear and organized manner, making it easier for the reader to understand and remember.

In technical communication, it is important to use emphasis effectively to ensure that the reader can easily identify and understand the most important information. Emphasis should be used sparingly and consistently to avoid overwhelming or confusing the reader.
