### Exposition narration for the notes of the Unit 4 - Technical Communication Skills in the subject of Technical Communication

- Exposition narration is a type of writing that explains or informs.
- It is used to convey information and knowledge in a clear and concise manner.
- In technical communication, exposition narration is often used to describe processes, procedures, and concepts.
- The goal of exposition narration is to provide the reader with a clear understanding of the topic at hand.
- To achieve this, the writer must use clear and concise language, and organize the information in a logical and coherent manner.
- Examples of exposition narration in technical communication include user manuals, technical reports, and instructional materials.
- Effective exposition narration requires careful planning and organization, as well as a thorough understanding of the subject matter.
- In summary, exposition narration is an essential tool for technical communicators, as it allows them to convey complex information in a clear and accessible manner.
