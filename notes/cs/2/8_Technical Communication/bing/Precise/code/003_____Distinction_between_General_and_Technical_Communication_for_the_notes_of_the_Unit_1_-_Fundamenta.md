### Distinction between General and Technical Communication

General communication and technical communication are two distinct forms of communication. Here are some key differences between the two:

1. **Purpose:** The purpose of general communication is to convey information, ideas, and opinions to a broad audience, while the purpose of technical communication is to convey technical information to a specific audience.

2. **Audience:** General communication is intended for a general audience, while technical communication is intended for a specific audience with a technical background or knowledge.

3. **Language:** General communication uses everyday language, while technical communication uses technical language and terminology specific to the field.

4. **Format:** General communication can take many forms, including written, verbal, and visual, while technical communication is often presented in a structured format, such as a user manual or technical report.

5. **Clarity:** General communication may use figurative language, metaphors, and other literary devices, while technical communication strives for clarity and precision in conveying information.

These are some of the key distinctions between general and technical communication. Understanding these differences can help in effectively communicating technical information to a specific audience.
