### Etiquette for the notes of the Unit 5 - Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics in the subject of Technical Communication

1. **Be prepared**: Know your topic and audience, and have a clear understanding of the purpose of your communication.
2. **Be clear and concise**: Use simple language and avoid jargon. Keep your message focused and to the point.
3. **Be respectful**: Show respect for your audience by using appropriate language and tone, and by being attentive to their needs and concerns.
4. **Be engaging**: Use vocal variety, body language, and visual aids to keep your audience engaged and interested.
5. **Be confident**: Speak with confidence and conviction to convey your message effectively.
6. **Be adaptable**: Be prepared to adjust your communication style to suit the needs of your audience and the situation.
7. **Be receptive**: Be open to feedback and willing to adjust your approach as needed.
8. **Be professional**: Maintain a professional demeanor and adhere to the norms of professional communication.
