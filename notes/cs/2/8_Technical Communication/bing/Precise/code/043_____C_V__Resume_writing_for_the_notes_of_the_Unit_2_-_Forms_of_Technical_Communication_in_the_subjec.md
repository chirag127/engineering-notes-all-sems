### C.V./Resume writing for the notes of the Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication

A C.V. (Curriculum Vitae) or resume is a document that summarizes your education, work experience, skills, and achievements. It is used to apply for jobs and is often the first point of contact between you and a potential employer. Here are some points to consider when writing a C.V. or resume:

1. **Format**: Choose a clear and easy-to-read format. Use headings, bullet points, and white space to make the document easy to scan.
2. **Length**: Keep your C.V. or resume concise. One to two pages is usually sufficient.
3. **Contact Information**: Include your name, address, phone number, and email address at the top of the document.
4. **Education**: List your educational qualifications in reverse chronological order, starting with the most recent. Include the name of the institution, the degree or qualification obtained, and the dates of attendance.
5. **Work Experience**: List your work experience in reverse chronological order, starting with the most recent. Include the name of the employer, your job title, the dates of employment, and a brief description of your responsibilities and achievements.
6. **Skills**: Include any relevant skills, such as language proficiency, computer skills, or technical skills.
7. **Achievements**: Highlight any notable achievements, such as awards, publications, or successful projects.
8. **References**: It is not necessary to include references on your C.V. or resume. You can provide them upon request.

Remember to tailor your C.V. or resume to the specific job you are applying for. Use keywords from the job description and highlight your relevant skills and experience. Proofread your document carefully to ensure that it is free of errors. A well-written C.V. or resume can help you stand out from other applicants and increase your chances of being invited for an interview.
