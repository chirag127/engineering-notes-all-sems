### Methods of Presentation

Unit 3 - Technical Presentation in the subject of Technical Communication

1. **Oral Presentation:** This method involves delivering information verbally, usually in front of an audience. It can be accompanied by visual aids such as slides or videos to enhance the presentation.

2. **Written Presentation:** This method involves presenting information in a written format, such as a report or a paper. It can be accompanied by visual aids such as graphs or charts to enhance the presentation.

3. **Multimedia Presentation:** This method involves presenting information using a combination of different media, such as text, images, audio, and video. It can be delivered through various platforms, such as a website or a video.

4. **Poster Presentation:** This method involves presenting information on a poster, usually at a conference or a trade show. It can be accompanied by a short oral presentation to explain the poster.

5. **Demonstration:** This method involves showing how something works or how to do something, usually through a live demonstration or a video.

Each method has its own advantages and disadvantages, and the choice of method depends on the purpose of the presentation, the audience, and the presenter's skills and preferences. It is important to choose the most effective method for the specific situation to ensure that the information is communicated effectively.
