### Definition for the notes of the Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication

Technical communication is a type of communication that conveys technical information to a specific audience for a specific purpose. It is a form of communication that is used in various fields such as science, engineering, and technology.

Some common forms of technical communication include:

1. **User manuals:** These are documents that provide instructions on how to use a product or service.
2. **Technical reports:** These are documents that present technical information in a structured format.
3. **White papers:** These are documents that provide detailed information on a specific topic, often used to inform or persuade.
4. **Proposals:** These are documents that present a plan or idea for consideration or approval.
5. **Specifications:** These are documents that provide detailed information on the requirements or characteristics of a product or service.
6. **Presentations:** These are oral or visual presentations of technical information, often used to inform or persuade.

These are just a few examples of the many forms of technical communication. The specific form used will depend on the audience, the purpose, and the type of information being conveyed.
