### Interpersonal Skills

Interpersonal skills are soft skills that involve the ability to communicate and build relationships with others. They are often considered innate personality traits or habits, but they can also be learned and improved. Interpersonal skills are important for technical presentations because they help you to:

- Understand your audience and their needs
- Deliver your message clearly and confidently
- Engage your listeners and keep their attention
- Handle questions and feedback effectively
- Collaborate with your team members and other presenters

Some examples of interpersonal skills that are useful for technical presentations are:

- Active listening: This means listening to others with the purpose of gathering information and engaging with the speaker. Active listening shows that you respect and value the other person's input and feedback. It also helps you to avoid misunderstandings and clarify any doubts or confusion.
- Dependability: This means being reliable and trustworthy in fulfilling your responsibilities and commitments. Dependability shows that you are professional and respectful of your audience and your team. It also helps you to avoid delays and errors in your presentation.
- Empathy: This means being able to understand and share the feelings and perspectives of others. Empathy helps you to connect with your audience and tailor your presentation to their interests and expectations. It also helps you to handle any emotional or sensitive issues that may arise during your presentation.
- Leadership: This means being able to guide, motivate and inspire others to achieve a common goal. Leadership helps you to coordinate and manage your team and your presentation effectively. It also helps you to establish your credibility and authority as a presenter.
- Teamwork: This means being able to work well with others and cooperate towards a shared objective. Teamwork helps you to leverage the strengths and skills of your team members and create a cohesive and harmonious presentation. It also helps you to resolve any conflicts or challenges that may occur during your presentation.
