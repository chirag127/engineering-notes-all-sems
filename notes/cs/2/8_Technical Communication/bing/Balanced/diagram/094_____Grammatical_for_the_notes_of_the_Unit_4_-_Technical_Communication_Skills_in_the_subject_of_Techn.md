### Grammatical

- Grammatical means relating to grammar or conforming to the rules of grammar.
- Grammar is the system and structure of a language, including the sounds, words, sentences, and punctuation.
- Grammatical meaning is the part of meaning that varies from one inflectional form to another (as from plays to played to playing).
- Grammatical analysis is the process of identifying and describing the grammatical elements and structures of a sentence or text.
- Grammatical sentences are well formed, correct, acceptable, and idiomatic according to the norms of a language .
- Grammatical rules are the principles and conventions that govern how words and phrases are combined and arranged in a language.
- Grammatical errors are mistakes or deviations from the standard or accepted usage of a language.
- Grammatical categories are the classes or groups of words or forms that share certain grammatical features or functions, such as number, gender, case, tense, mood, voice, etc..
- Grammatical functions are the roles or relations that words or phrases have in a sentence or clause, such as subject, object, predicate, modifier, etc..
