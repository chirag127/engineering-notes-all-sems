# Emotion

- Emotion is a **natural instinctive state of mind** that arises from one's **circumstances, mood, or relationships with others** .
- Emotion is often **accompanied by a physical reaction**, such as flushing, sweating, trembling, or crying .
- Emotion is **subjectively experienced** as a **strong feeling** that is usually **directed toward a specific object**.
- Emotion is **associated with thoughts, feelings, behavioral responses, and a degree of pleasure or displeasure**.
- Emotion is **different from reasoning or knowledge**, although they can influence each other .
- Emotion is **responsive to significant internal and external events** that affect one's well-being .
- Emotion is **part of the affective aspect of consciousness**.
