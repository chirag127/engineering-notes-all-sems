# Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication

## Introduction
- Technical communication is the process of conveying information in a clear, concise, and accurate manner to a specific audience for a specific purpose.
- Technical communication can take various forms, such as reports, proposals, manuals, memos, letters, emails, presentations, websites, blogs, podcasts, etc.
- Technical communication can be classified into two broad categories: internal and external communication.

## Internal Communication
- Internal communication is the exchange of information within an organization or a team.
- Internal communication can have various purposes, such as informing, instructing, persuading, motivating, coordinating, collaborating, etc.
- Internal communication can use various media, such as oral, written, visual, or electronic.
- Some examples of internal communication are:

  - Reports: documents that provide information, analysis, or recommendations on a specific topic or problem.
  - Proposals: documents that offer a plan or a solution to a problem or a need.
  - Manuals: documents that provide instructions or guidelines on how to use a product, a service, a system, or a process.
  - Memos: short documents that communicate information, requests, reminders, or feedback within an organization or a team.
  - Letters: formal documents that communicate information, requests, or feedback to a specific person or group within or outside an organization.
  - Emails: electronic messages that communicate information, requests, or feedback to one or more recipients within or outside an organization.
  - Presentations: oral or visual communication that deliver information, analysis, or recommendations to an audience.

## External Communication
- External communication is the exchange of information between an organization and its external stakeholders, such as customers, clients, suppliers, investors, regulators, media, etc.
- External communication can have various purposes, such as informing, promoting, persuading, educating, entertaining, etc.
- External communication can use various media, such as oral, written, visual, or electronic.
- Some examples of external communication are:

  - Websites: online platforms that provide information, services, or products to the public or a specific audience.
  - Blogs: online journals that provide personal or professional opinions, insights, or experiences on a specific topic or field.
  - Podcasts: audio or video files that provide information, entertainment, or education on a specific topic or field.
  - Brochures: printed or electronic documents that provide information, features, or benefits of a product, a service, or an organization.
  - Flyers: printed or electronic documents that provide information, announcements, or invitations for an event, a program, or an activity.
  - Newsletters: printed or electronic documents that provide news, updates, or information about an organization, a product, a service, or a field.
  - Press releases: documents that provide information, announcements, or statements to the media or the public about an organization, a product, a service, or an event.
  - Social media: online platforms that enable users to create and share content, opinions, or information with others.
