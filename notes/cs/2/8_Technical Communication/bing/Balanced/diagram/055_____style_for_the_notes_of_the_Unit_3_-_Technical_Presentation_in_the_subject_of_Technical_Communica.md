### Style for the notes of the Unit 3 - Technical Presentation

- Style is the way you express your ideas and information in a technical presentation.
- Style affects the clarity, credibility, and impact of your presentation.
- Style includes the following elements:
  - Language: the choice of words, sentences, and tone that you use to communicate with your audience.
  - Visuals: the use of images, graphs, charts, diagrams, and other visual aids to support your message and enhance your presentation.
  - Delivery: the way you speak, gesture, move, and interact with your audience during your presentation.
- To achieve an effective style for your technical presentation, you should follow these guidelines:
  - Use clear, concise, and precise language that is appropriate for your audience, purpose, and context.
  - Avoid jargon, slang, and unnecessary technical terms that may confuse or alienate your audience.
  - Use active voice, positive words, and parallel structure to create a strong and coherent message.
  - Use visuals that are relevant, accurate, and easy to understand and interpret by your audience.
  - Use visuals that complement and reinforce your verbal message, not distract or contradict it.
  - Use visuals that are consistent, simple, and attractive in design and layout.
  - Use delivery techniques that enhance your credibility, confidence, and rapport with your audience.
  - Use delivery techniques that suit your personality, voice, and presentation style.
  - Use delivery techniques that match your audience's expectations, preferences, and culture.
  - Practice your presentation before delivering it to your audience.
