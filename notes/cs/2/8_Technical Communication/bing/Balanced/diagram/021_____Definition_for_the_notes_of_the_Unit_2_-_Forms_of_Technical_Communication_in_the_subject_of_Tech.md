### Definition for the notes of the Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication

- Technical communication is the process of conveying information and instructions to a specific audience for a specific purpose.
- Forms of technical communication are the different ways of presenting technical information and instructions to the audience, such as written, oral, visual, or multimedia.
- Some examples of forms of technical communication are:

  - Written: reports, manuals, proposals, memos, emails, letters, etc.
  - Oral: presentations, lectures, speeches, interviews, meetings, etc.
  - Visual: graphs, charts, diagrams, maps, illustrations, photos, videos, etc.
  - Multimedia: websites, podcasts, webinars, animations, simulations, etc.

- The choice of the form of technical communication depends on the purpose, audience, context, and medium of the communication.
