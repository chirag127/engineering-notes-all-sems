### Considerateness for the notes of the Unit 5 - Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics in the subject of Technical Communication

- Considerateness is one of the seven C's of effective communication, along with clarity, conciseness, completeness, correctness, concreteness, and courtesy .
- Considerateness means showing respect and empathy for the audience, their needs, interests, and feelings  .
- Considerateness also means using positive and polite language, avoiding negative or offensive words, and emphasizing on the "you" approach rather than the "I" or "we" approach .
- Considerateness is important for oral communication because it helps to build rapport, trust, and goodwill with the audience, and to avoid misunderstandings, conflicts, or resentment  .
- Some strategies to show considerateness in oral communication are  :
  - Use appropriate nonverbal cues, such as eye contact, facial expressions, gestures, and posture, to convey interest, respect, and attentiveness.
  - Adapt your message to the audience's level of knowledge, background, and expectations, and avoid jargon, technical terms, or acronyms that may confuse them.
  - Use examples, stories, or analogies that relate to the audience's experiences, values, or goals, and that illustrate your main points.
  - Use questions, feedback, or interactive activities to engage the audience, check their understanding, and address their concerns or objections.
  - Use humor, compliments, or appreciation to create a positive and friendly tone, but avoid sarcasm, criticism, or insults that may offend or hurt the audience.
  - Use transitions, summaries, or signposts to help the audience follow the structure and flow of your message, and to highlight the key takeaways or action steps.
