# Tone
- Tone is the attitude or emotion that the speaker conveys through the use of voice and words.
- Tone can affect the meaning and impact of the message, as well as the relationship between the speaker and the listener.
- Tone can be influenced by various factors, such as the purpose, context, audience, mood, and personality of the speaker.
- Tone can be classified into different types, such as formal, informal, friendly, hostile, polite, rude, confident, hesitant, etc.
- Tone can be modified by changing the pitch, volume, speed, and quality of the voice, as well as the choice of words, phrases, and expressions.
- Tone can be analyzed by paying attention to the verbal and non-verbal cues of the speaker, such as the facial expressions, gestures, posture, eye contact, etc.
- Tone can be improved by practicing the following skills:
  - Identify the purpose and audience of the communication and choose an appropriate tone accordingly.
  - Use positive and respectful language and avoid negative and offensive words.
  - Vary the voice modulation and intonation to convey the intended meaning and emotion.
  - Use feedback and self-evaluation to assess the effectiveness of the tone and make necessary adjustments.
