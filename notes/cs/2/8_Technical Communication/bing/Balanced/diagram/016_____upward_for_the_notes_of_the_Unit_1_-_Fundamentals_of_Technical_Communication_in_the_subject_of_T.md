### Upward Communication

Upward communication is the process by which lower-level company employees can directly communicate with upper management to provide feedback, complaints or suggestions regarding the day-to-day operations of the company. Upward communication is increasing in popularity among organizations to encourage a participative work culture.

Some of the main points to note about upward communication are:

- Upward communication is participative because it encourages lower-level employees to voice their opinions. In contrast, downward communication is directive in nature as it allows upper management to instruct employees on particular company matters.
- Upward communication can improve employee morale, motivation, and engagement as it shows that the management values their input and respects their views. It can also foster a sense of trust and loyalty between employees and managers.
- Upward communication can enhance the quality of decision-making and problem-solving as it provides managers with valuable information and feedback from the front-line workers who are directly involved in the work processes. It can also help managers identify and address any issues or challenges that may arise in the organization.
- Upward communication can facilitate organizational change and innovation as it allows employees to share their ideas and suggestions for improvement and development. It can also help managers communicate the vision and goals of the organization and gain the support and commitment of the employees.

Some of the examples of upward communication are:

- Employee surveys and questionnaires that allow employees to express their opinions and feedback on various aspects of the organization.
- Suggestion boxes or online platforms that enable employees to submit their ideas and recommendations for improvement and innovation.
- Performance appraisals and reviews that involve employees and managers discussing the strengths and weaknesses of the employees and setting goals and expectations for the future.
- Meetings and forums that provide opportunities for employees and managers to exchange information and views on various topics and issues.
- Reports and memos that communicate the progress and results of the work done by the employees to the managers.

Some of the advantages of upward communication are:

- It can improve the communication flow and transparency in the organization as it reduces the barriers and gaps between the different levels of the hierarchy.
- It can enhance the organizational culture and climate as it creates a more democratic and participative environment where employees feel valued and empowered.
- It can increase the productivity and efficiency of the organization as it allows managers to make more informed and effective decisions and actions based on the input and feedback from the employees.
- It can foster the learning and development of the employees and managers as it provides opportunities for feedback, coaching, and mentoring.

Some of the disadvantages of upward communication are:

- It can create information overload and confusion for the managers as they may receive too much or conflicting information and feedback from the employees.
- It can cause resentment and frustration among the employees if their input and feedback are ignored or rejected by the managers.
- It can increase the risk of distortion and misinterpretation of the information and feedback as it passes through different levels and channels of the organization.
- It can challenge the authority and credibility of the managers as they may face criticism or opposition from the employees.
