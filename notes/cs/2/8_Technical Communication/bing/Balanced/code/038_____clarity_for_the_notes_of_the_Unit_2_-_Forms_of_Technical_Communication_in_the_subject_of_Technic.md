# Clarity for the notes of the Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication

- Technical communication is the method of producing and sharing ideas and information in organizations, especially about technical or specialized topics, such as computer applications, medical procedures, or environmental regulations .
- Technical communication also involves using technology to create or distribute that communication, such as web pages, help files, or social media sites.
- Technical communication also provides instructions about how to do something, regardless of how technical the task is or even if technology is used to create or distribute that communication.
- Technical communication can have different purposes, such as defining, instructing, informing, or persuading.
- Technical communication can take different forms, such as business letters, memos, reports, notices, proposals, websites, blogs, and more .
- Technical communication should be clear, concise, accurate, and appropriate for the intended audience and context.
