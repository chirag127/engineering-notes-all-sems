# Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication

## Introduction
- Technical communication is the process of conveying information and instructions to a specific audience for a specific purpose.
- Technical communication can take various forms, such as written, oral, visual, or digital.
- Technical communication can be classified into two main categories: internal and external.

## Internal Technical Communication
- Internal technical communication is the communication that takes place within an organization or a team.
- Internal technical communication can have different purposes, such as:
  - Sharing information and knowledge
  - Coordinating tasks and projects
  - Solving problems and making decisions
  - Providing feedback and evaluation
  - Building rapport and trust
- Internal technical communication can take various forms, such as:
  - Memos and reports
  - Emails and instant messages
  - Meetings and presentations
  - Manuals and guidelines
  - Wikis and blogs

## External Technical Communication
- External technical communication is the communication that takes place between an organization or a team and its external stakeholders, such as customers, clients, partners, suppliers, regulators, or the public.
- External technical communication can have different purposes, such as:
  - Promoting products and services
  - Persuading and influencing
  - Educating and instructing
  - Informing and updating
  - Entertaining and engaging
- External technical communication can take various forms, such as:
  - Brochures and flyers
  - Websites and social media
  - Newsletters and magazines
  - Proposals and contracts
  - Manuals and tutorials

## Conclusion
- Technical communication is a vital skill for professionals in any field or industry.
- Technical communication can be adapted to different audiences, purposes, and contexts.
- Technical communication can be improved by following the principles of clarity, accuracy, conciseness, coherence, and ethics.
