### Method for the notes of the Unit 4 - Technical Communication Skills in the subject of Technical Communication

- Unit 4 covers the following topics: oral communication, written communication, visual communication, and non-verbal communication.
- Oral communication refers to the use of speech to convey information, ideas, opinions, or emotions. It includes aspects such as pronunciation, intonation, stress, accent, fluency, and clarity.
- Written communication refers to the use of written symbols to convey information, ideas, opinions, or emotions. It includes aspects such as grammar, vocabulary, spelling, punctuation, style, and format.
- Visual communication refers to the use of images, diagrams, charts, graphs, maps, or other visual elements to convey information, ideas, opinions, or emotions. It includes aspects such as design, layout, color, contrast, and clarity.
- Non-verbal communication refers to the use of body language, facial expressions, gestures, eye contact, or other physical cues to convey information, ideas, opinions, or emotions. It includes aspects such as posture, movement, distance, and appearance.
