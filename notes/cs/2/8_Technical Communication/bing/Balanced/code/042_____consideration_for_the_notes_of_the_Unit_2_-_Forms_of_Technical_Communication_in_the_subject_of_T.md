### Considerations for the notes of the Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication

- Unit 2 covers the various forms of technical communication, such as reports, proposals, memos, letters, emails, presentations, and websites.
- The notes should include the following points for each form of technical communication:
  - The definition and purpose of the form
  - The typical audience and context of the form
  - The general structure and format of the form
  - The style and tone of the form
  - The common types and examples of the form
  - The advantages and disadvantages of the form
  - The best practices and tips for the form
- The notes should also include some exercises or activities to practice the skills and knowledge of each form of technical communication.
- The notes should be clear, concise, accurate, and organized, using headings, subheadings, bullet points, tables, charts, diagrams, and other visual aids as appropriate.
- The notes should cite the sources of information and provide references using a consistent citation style.
