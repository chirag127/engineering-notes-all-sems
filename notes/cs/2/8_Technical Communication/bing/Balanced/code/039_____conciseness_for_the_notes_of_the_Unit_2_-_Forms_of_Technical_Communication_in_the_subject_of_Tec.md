### Conciseness
- Conciseness is the quality of using only the words that are necessary to convey a clear and accurate message.
- Conciseness is important for effective technical communication because it helps the readers to understand the information quickly and easily, and avoids wasting their time and attention.
- Conciseness can be achieved by following some general principles, such as:
  - Eliminating unnecessary words, such as redundancies, fillers, modifiers, and qualifiers.
  - Using specific and concrete words, rather than vague and abstract ones.
  - Using active voice, rather than passive voice, when possible and appropriate.
  - Using parallel structure, which means using the same grammatical form for items in a list or series.
  - Using lists, tables, graphs, or diagrams, when they can present the information more clearly and concisely than text.
  - Revising and editing the text to remove any errors, inconsistencies, or ambiguities.
