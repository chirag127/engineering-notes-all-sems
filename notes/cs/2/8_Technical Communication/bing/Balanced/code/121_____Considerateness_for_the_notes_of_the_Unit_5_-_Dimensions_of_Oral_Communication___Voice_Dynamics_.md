### Considerateness for the notes of the Unit 5 - Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics in the subject of Technical Communication

- Considerateness is one of the seven C's of effective communication, along with clarity, conciseness, completeness, correctness, concreteness, and courtesy .
- Considerateness means showing respect and empathy for the audience, understanding their needs and expectations, and using a positive and polite tone of voice  .
- Considerateness is important for oral communication because it helps to build rapport and trust with the listeners, stimulate their interest and attention, and avoid misunderstandings and conflicts .
- Some features of considerate oral communication are  :
  - Emphasizing on "you" approach, which means focusing on the benefits and interests of the audience, rather than the speaker's own agenda or ego.
  - Empathizing with the audience and exhibiting interest in them, which means listening actively, asking relevant questions, acknowledging their feelings and opinions, and providing feedback.
  - Showing optimism and positivity towards the audience, which means emphasizing on "what is possible" rather than "what is impossible", using words that convey enthusiasm, appreciation, gratitude, and encouragement, and avoiding words that convey negativity, criticism, blame, or sarcasm.
  - Adapting to the audience's level of understanding, background, and culture, which means using simple and clear language, avoiding jargon and technical terms, providing examples and illustrations, and respecting their values and beliefs.
  - Being honest and sincere with the audience, which means telling the truth, admitting mistakes, giving credit where due, and avoiding exaggeration, manipulation, or deception.
- Some examples of considerate oral communication are  :
  - "Thank you for your time and attention. I hope you found this presentation useful and informative. If you have any questions or feedback, please feel free to ask me."
  - "I understand your frustration and disappointment. I apologize for the inconvenience caused by the delay in the delivery. We are working hard to resolve the issue as soon as possible. Please let me know if there is anything I can do to assist you."
  - "You have done a great job on this project. I appreciate your hard work and dedication. You have shown creativity and initiative in solving the problems. I am proud of you and your team."
  - "I would like to explain the main features of our new product. It is designed to meet your needs and expectations. It is easy to use, reliable, and affordable. It will help you save time, money, and energy. Let me show you how it works."
  - "I respect your opinion and perspective. I agree with some of your points, but I disagree with others. I would like to share my views and reasons with you. I hope we can have a constructive and respectful dialogue."
