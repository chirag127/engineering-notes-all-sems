### Content for the notes of the Unit 4 - Technical Communication Skills in the subject of Technical Communication

- Technical communication is a practice-oriented field that emphasizes detailed, cogent, and informative writing, and often requires skills in analysis, research, and design, as well as knowledge of editing and publication processes .
- Technical communication often involves communicating expert, technical, or highly complex information to a non-expert or more general audience. Technical communication often works to convince an audience to take some specific action. Technical communication might define, instruct, inform, or persuade.
- Technical skills are the specialized knowledge and expertise required to perform specific tasks and use specific tools and programs in real world situations. Diverse technical skills are required in just about every field and industry, from IT and business administration to health care and education.
- Technical communication skills are the abilities to use technical skills effectively in various communication contexts, such as writing, speaking, listening, and presenting. Technical communication skills enable one to communicate technical information clearly, concisely, accurately, and ethically to various audiences and purposes.
- Some examples of technical communication skills are:
  - Writing technical reports, manuals, proposals, memos, emails, etc.
  - Creating technical diagrams, charts, graphs, tables, etc.
  - Giving technical presentations, demonstrations, or instructions.
  - Collaborating with other technical professionals or stakeholders.
  - Evaluating and revising technical documents or products.
  - Using appropriate style, tone, format, and language for technical communication.
  - Adhering to ethical principles and standards for technical communication.
- Technical communication skills are essential for any technical professional who wants to succeed in their field and career. Technical communication skills can help one to:
  - Communicate technical information effectively to various audiences and purposes.
  - Enhance one's credibility and professionalism as a technical expert.
  - Solve technical problems and make informed decisions.
  - Improve one's productivity and efficiency as a technical worker.
  - Advance one's career and professional development.
