### Method for the notes of the Unit 3 - Technical Presentation in the subject of Technical Communication

- Technical presentation is a form of oral communication that involves delivering information, ideas, or arguments to an audience using visual aids, such as slides, graphs, charts, or videos.
- Technical presentation requires careful planning, preparation, and practice to achieve the desired objectives and outcomes.
- The method for the notes of the technical presentation consists of the following steps:

  - **Step 1: Analyze the purpose and audience of the presentation.** This step involves identifying the main goal, message, and scope of the presentation, as well as the characteristics, expectations, and needs of the audience. This will help to tailor the content, tone, and style of the presentation to suit the audience and the occasion.
  - **Step 2: Research and organize the content of the presentation.** This step involves gathering relevant and reliable information from various sources, such as books, journals, websites, or experts, and organizing it into a logical and coherent structure. The structure of the presentation should include an introduction, a body, and a conclusion, as well as transitions and signposts to guide the audience through the presentation. The content of the presentation should be clear, concise, and accurate, and should support the main message and goal of the presentation.
  - **Step 3: Design and prepare the visual aids of the presentation.** This step involves creating and selecting appropriate visual aids, such as slides, graphs, charts, or videos, to enhance the delivery and comprehension of the presentation. The visual aids should be relevant, simple, consistent, and attractive, and should complement the verbal content of the presentation. The visual aids should also be tested and checked for errors, compatibility, and readability before the presentation.
  - **Step 4: Write and rehearse the notes of the presentation.** This step involves writing and practicing the notes of the presentation, which are the key points, examples, explanations, or cues that the presenter will use during the presentation. The notes of the presentation should be brief, legible, and easy to follow, and should not be read verbatim from the slides or the paper. The notes of the presentation should also be rehearsed several times, preferably in front of a mirror, a recorder, or a friend, to improve the confidence, fluency, and delivery of the presentation.
  - **Step 5: Deliver and evaluate the presentation.** This step involves presenting the technical presentation to the audience, using the notes, the visual aids, and the verbal and non-verbal skills of the presenter. The presenter should also monitor the feedback and reactions of the audience, and adjust the presentation accordingly. After the presentation, the presenter should evaluate the strengths and weaknesses of the presentation, and seek feedback from the audience, the peers, or the instructor, to improve the future presentations.
