### Pronunciation for the notes of the Unit 5 - Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics in the subject of Technical Communication

- Pronunciation is the way a word or a language is spoken, or the manner in which someone utters a word.
- Pronunciation is one of the dimensions of oral communication that affects the clarity, accuracy and effectiveness of the message.
- Pronunciation involves the correct articulation of sounds, syllables, stress, intonation and rhythm of speech.
- Some factors that influence pronunciation are:
  - The native language of the speaker and the influence of its sound system and rules on the target language.
  - The exposure and practice of the speaker with the target language and its varieties and accents.
  - The awareness and knowledge of the speaker about the phonetic symbols and rules of the target language.
  - The purpose and context of the communication and the expectations and preferences of the audience.
- Some strategies to improve pronunciation are:
  - Listening to authentic and varied sources of the target language, such as podcasts, videos, songs, speeches, etc. and imitating the speakers' pronunciation.
  - Recording and analyzing one's own speech and comparing it with the standard or desired pronunciation.
  - Using dictionaries, online tools, apps or software that provide the phonetic transcription and audio pronunciation of words.
  - Practicing tongue twisters, minimal pairs, rhymes, chants and other exercises that focus on specific sounds, syllables, stress, intonation and rhythm.
  - Seeking feedback and guidance from native or proficient speakers, teachers, tutors or peers and correcting the errors and mistakes.
