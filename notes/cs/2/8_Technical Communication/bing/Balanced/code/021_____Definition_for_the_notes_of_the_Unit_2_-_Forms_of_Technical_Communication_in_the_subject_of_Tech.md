### Definition for the notes of the Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication

- Technical communication is the process of conveying information and instructions to a specific audience for a specific purpose.
- Forms of technical communication are the different ways of presenting technical information and instructions to the audience, such as documents, graphics, videos, audio, etc.
- Some common forms of technical communication are:

  - Technical reports: These are written documents that provide detailed information and analysis on a specific topic, such as research findings, project outcomes, feasibility studies, etc.
  - Technical manuals: These are written documents that provide instructions and guidance on how to use, operate, maintain, or repair a product, system, or service.
  - Technical proposals: These are written documents that propose a solution to a problem or a plan for a project, and explain the benefits, costs, and feasibility of the proposed solution or plan.
  - Technical memos: These are short written documents that communicate information or instructions within an organization, such as updates, announcements, requests, feedback, etc.
  - Technical presentations: These are oral or visual presentations that deliver technical information or instructions to an audience, such as lectures, demonstrations, webinars, etc.
  - Technical posters: These are visual displays that summarize technical information or instructions in a concise and attractive way, such as charts, diagrams, graphs, etc.
  - Technical websites: These are online platforms that provide technical information or instructions to the audience, such as blogs, wikis, podcasts, etc.
