### Lateral or Horizontal Communication

- Lateral or horizontal communication is communication between departments, teams, and individuals of the same level within an organization    .
- Examples of lateral or horizontal communication are sending a memo to a colleague in finance, emailing your department, or catching up with a teammate over a coffee .
- The purpose of lateral or horizontal communication is to coordinate activities, share information, solve problems, resolve conflicts, and build relationships among peers  .
- The benefits of lateral or horizontal communication are improved collaboration, innovation, efficiency, morale, and customer satisfaction   .
- The challenges of lateral or horizontal communication are lack of clarity, consistency, feedback, trust, and alignment   .
- The strategies to improve lateral or horizontal communication are setting clear goals and expectations, using appropriate channels and tools, providing regular feedback, fostering a culture of respect and trust, and aligning with the organizational vision and values   .
