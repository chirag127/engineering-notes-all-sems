### Nuances for the notes of the Unit 4 - Technical Communication Skills in the subject of Technical Communication

- Unit 4 covers the topic of interview, which is an interaction between two or more persons for a specific purpose, in which the interviewer asks the interviewee specific questions in order to assess his/her suitability for recruitment, admission, or promotion  .
- The notes of Unit 4 include the following subtopics:
  - Types of interviews, such as structured, unstructured, panel, group, stress, telephonic, and video interviews  .
  - Preparation for interviews, such as researching the organization, reviewing the resume, anticipating questions, rehearsing answers, dressing appropriately, and arriving on time  .
  - Conducting interviews, such as greeting the interviewer, making eye contact, listening attentively, answering confidently, asking relevant questions, and thanking the interviewer  .
  - Common interview questions, such as personal, educational, professional, behavioral, situational, and technical questions  .
  - Common interview mistakes, such as being late, unprepared, nervous, dishonest, arrogant, rude, or negative  .
- The notes of Unit 4 also provide some tips and examples for writing effective resumes and cover letters, which are essential documents for applying for jobs or higher studies  .
- The notes of Unit 4 aim to help students develop their technical communication skills, which are the abilities to communicate effectively in a professional context, using appropriate language, tone, format, and media  .
