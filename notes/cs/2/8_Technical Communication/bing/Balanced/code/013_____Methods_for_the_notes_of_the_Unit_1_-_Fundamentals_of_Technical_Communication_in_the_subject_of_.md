### Methods for the notes of the Unit 1 - Fundamentals of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication

- Technical communication is the method of producing and sharing ideas and information in organizations, especially about technical or specialized topics, using various modes and media .
- Technical communication can have different purposes, such as defining, instructing, informing, or persuading an audience to take some specific action.
- Technical communication can cover various domains, such as the soft and hard sciences, high technology, consumer electronics, business processes and practices, etc.
- Technical communication can involve different types of deliverables, such as online help, user manuals, technical manuals, white papers, specifications, process and procedure manuals, industrial videos, reference cards, data sheets, journal articles, patents, training, business papers, and technical reports.
- Technical communication can require different skills, such as research, analysis, design, writing, editing, testing, and evaluation.
- Technical communication can benefit from different tools, such as word processors, graphics software, presentation software, web authoring software, content management systems, etc.
- Technical communication can follow different standards, such as style guides, formatting guidelines, accessibility requirements, ethical principles, etc.
- Technical communication can face different challenges, such as audience analysis, cultural differences, legal issues, technical complexity, etc.
