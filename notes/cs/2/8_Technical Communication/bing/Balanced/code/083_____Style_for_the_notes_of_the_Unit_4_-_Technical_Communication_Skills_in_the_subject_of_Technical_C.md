# Unit 4 - Technical Communication Skills

## Objectives
- To understand the importance and principles of effective technical communication
- To learn the skills and strategies for oral and written communication in technical contexts
- To practice the techniques of planning, organizing, drafting, revising, and editing technical documents
- To apply the principles of design, layout, and visual aids to enhance the readability and usability of technical documents
- To develop the skills of teamwork, collaboration, and presentation in technical communication

## Topics
- Technical communication: definition, characteristics, and purposes
- Technical communication process: planning, writing, and revising
- Technical communication genres: reports, proposals, instructions, manuals, and others
- Technical communication style: clarity, conciseness, coherence, and correctness
- Technical communication design: format, layout, typography, and graphics
- Technical communication ethics: honesty, accuracy, and responsibility

## Key Points
- Technical communication is the exchange of information that helps people interact with technology, solve problems, and accomplish tasks.
- Technical communication is different from general communication in terms of its audience, purpose, context, and format.
- Technical communication requires a systematic process of planning, writing, and revising, which involves analyzing the situation, gathering and organizing information, drafting the document, and editing and proofreading the final version.
- Technical communication can take various forms and genres, such as reports, proposals, instructions, manuals, and others, depending on the communication goals and needs of the audience.
- Technical communication should follow the principles of style, such as clarity, conciseness, coherence, and correctness, to ensure the effectiveness and professionalism of the message.
- Technical communication should also follow the principles of design, such as format, layout, typography, and graphics, to enhance the readability and usability of the document.
- Technical communication should adhere to the ethical standards of honesty, accuracy, and responsibility, to respect the rights and interests of the audience, the organization, and the society.
