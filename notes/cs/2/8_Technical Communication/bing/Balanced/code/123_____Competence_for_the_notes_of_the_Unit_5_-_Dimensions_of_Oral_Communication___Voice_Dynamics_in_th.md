### Competence for the notes of the Unit 5 - Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics in the subject of Technical Communication

- Competence is the ability to perform a task effectively and efficiently.
- Communication competence is how well a person can communicate their thoughts and ideas to others using verbal and nonverbal skills.
- Oral communication competence is the specific subset of communication competence that involves speaking and listening skills.
- Oral communication competence is important for professional purposes, such as presenting information, persuading an audience, collaborating with others, and building relationships.
- Oral communication competence can be developed by practicing the following aspects:
  - Mindfulness: being aware of the communication situation and the goals, needs, and expectations of oneself and others.
  - Self-monitoring: adjusting one's communication behavior based on the feedback and reactions of others.
  - Adaptability: changing one's communication style and strategies to suit different contexts and audiences.
  - Empathy: understanding and sharing the feelings and perspectives of others.
  - Cognitive complexity: being able to analyze and interpret multiple aspects of a communication situation.
  - Ethics: following the principles of honesty, respect, fairness, and responsibility in communication.
- Oral communication competence also depends on the mastery of the linguistic components of a language, such as vocabulary, grammar, pronunciation, and intonation.
- Oral communication competence also requires the knowledge and application of the cultural norms, values, and practices of the communication context.
- Oral communication competence can be assessed by using various criteria, such as clarity, coherence, organization, accuracy, relevance, appropriateness, and effectiveness.
