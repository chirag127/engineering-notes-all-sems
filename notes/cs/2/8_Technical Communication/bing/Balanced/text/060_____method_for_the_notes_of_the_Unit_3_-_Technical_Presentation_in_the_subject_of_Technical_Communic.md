### Method for the notes of the Unit 3 - Technical Presentation in the subject of Technical Communication

- A technical presentation is a form of oral communication that conveys technical information to a specific audience, such as engineers, managers, customers, or the general public.
- A technical presentation has three main components: the content, the delivery, and the visual aids.
- The content of a technical presentation should be clear, concise, accurate, and relevant to the audience's needs and interests. It should also have a logical structure, such as introduction, body, and conclusion, and use appropriate transitions and signposts to guide the audience through the main points.
- The delivery of a technical presentation should be confident, engaging, and respectful. The speaker should use a clear and audible voice, vary the tone and pace, avoid filler words and jargon, and maintain eye contact and body language that convey enthusiasm and professionalism. The speaker should also practice the presentation beforehand, anticipate possible questions and objections, and handle them politely and effectively.
- The visual aids of a technical presentation should be simple, attractive, and consistent. They should support and complement the verbal message, not distract or overwhelm it. The speaker should use graphs, charts, diagrams, images, or videos that illustrate the key concepts, data, or results, and avoid too much text, clutter, or animation. The speaker should also check the equipment and the compatibility of the visual aids before the presentation, and use pointers, laser pens, or gestures to highlight the important parts.
