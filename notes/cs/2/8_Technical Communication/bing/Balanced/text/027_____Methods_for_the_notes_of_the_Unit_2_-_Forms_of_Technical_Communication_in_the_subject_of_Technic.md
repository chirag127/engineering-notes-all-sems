### Methods for the notes of the Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication

- Technical communication is the process of conveying information and instructions to a specific audience for a specific purpose .
- Technical communication can take various forms, such as reports, manuals, proposals, memos, presentations, etc .
- Technical communication requires the use of different methods and skills, such as visual representation, verbal and written skills, obtaining and using information, and the use of information and communications technology .
- Some of the methods for the notes of the unit 2 are:

  - The 7 Cs of effective business writing: clear, concise, complete, correct, coherent, courteous, and convincing. These principles help to ensure that the message is understood and acted upon by the audience.
  - The Rhetorical Triangle: logos, ethos, and pathos. These elements help to establish the credibility, logic, and emotion of the message and the communicator.
  - The Document Design: layout, typography, color, graphics, etc. These aspects help to enhance the readability, attractiveness, and usability of the document.
  - The Visual Aids: tables, charts, diagrams, images, etc . These tools help to illustrate, summarize, and clarify the information and data in the document.
  - The Oral Presentation: delivery, voice, body language, slides, etc . These factors help to engage, persuade, and inform the audience in a face-to-face or online setting.
