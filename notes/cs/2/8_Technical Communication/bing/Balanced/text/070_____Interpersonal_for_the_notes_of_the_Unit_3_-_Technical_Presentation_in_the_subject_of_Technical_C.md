### Interpersonal Skills for Technical Presentation

Interpersonal skills are the skills required to effectively communicate, interact, and work with individuals and groups. They are essential for delivering a successful technical presentation, as they help to convey the message clearly, engage the audience, and build rapport and trust. Some of the key interpersonal skills for technical presentation are:

- **Communication skills**: These include verbal and non-verbal skills, such as speaking, writing, listening, and using body language. Communication skills help to express the technical information in a simple, concise, and accurate way, and to adapt the language and tone to the audience and the context. Communication skills also involve asking questions, giving feedback, and summarizing and paraphrasing what others have said.
- **Empathy skills**: These are the skills that enable one to understand and share the feelings, thoughts, and perspectives of others. Empathy skills help to show respect and appreciation for the audience, and to anticipate their needs, expectations, and concerns. Empathy skills also help to avoid or resolve conflicts, and to handle criticism and objections gracefully.
- **Active listening skills**: These are the skills that involve paying full attention to what others are saying, and showing interest and curiosity. Active listening skills help to comprehend the technical information better, and to identify the main points and the gaps. Active listening skills also help to encourage the audience to participate and to ask questions, and to demonstrate that their opinions and feedback are valued.
- **Presentation skills**: These are the skills that involve planning, preparing, and delivering a technical presentation in an effective and engaging way. Presentation skills help to organize the technical information in a logical and coherent structure, and to use appropriate visual aids and examples to support the message. Presentation skills also help to manage the time, the space, and the audience, and to handle questions and comments confidently.
