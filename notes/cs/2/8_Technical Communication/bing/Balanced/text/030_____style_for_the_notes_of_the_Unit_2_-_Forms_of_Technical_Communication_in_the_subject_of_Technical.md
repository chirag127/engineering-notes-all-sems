### Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication

- Technical communication is the process of conveying information and instructions to a specific audience for a specific purpose.
- Technical communication can take various forms, such as written, oral, visual, or digital.
- Some common forms of technical communication are:

  - **Reports**: Documents that present facts, analysis, and recommendations on a topic or a problem. Reports can be formal or informal, analytical or informational, technical or non-technical, etc. Examples of reports are feasibility studies, progress reports, lab reports, etc.
  - **Proposals**: Documents that offer a plan or a solution to a problem or a need. Proposals can be solicited or unsolicited, internal or external, academic or professional, etc. Examples of proposals are research proposals, project proposals, grant proposals, etc.
  - **Instructions**: Documents that explain how to do something or how something works. Instructions can be procedural or descriptive, step-by-step or overview, simple or complex, etc. Examples of instructions are user manuals, tutorials, recipes, etc.
  - **Correspondence**: Documents that communicate with a specific person or a group of people. Correspondence can be formal or informal, personal or professional, internal or external, etc. Examples of correspondence are letters, memos, emails, etc.
  - **Presentations**: Documents that deliver information and ideas to an audience in an oral or a visual format. Presentations can be informative or persuasive, individual or group, live or recorded, etc. Examples of presentations are speeches, slideshows, podcasts, etc.
  - **Web pages**: Documents that provide information and interaction on the internet. Web pages can be static or dynamic, text-based or multimedia, informative or entertaining, etc. Examples of web pages are blogs, wikis, social media, etc.

- Each form of technical communication has its own purpose, audience, structure, style, and design. Technical communicators should choose the appropriate form for their communication situation and follow the guidelines and conventions for that form.
