### Individual conferencing for the notes of the Unit 3 - Technical Presentation in the subject of Technical Communication

- Individual conferencing is a process of meeting with the instructor one-on-one to discuss the progress and feedback of the technical presentation assignment.
- The purpose of individual conferencing is to help the students improve their presentation skills, clarify any doubts or questions, and receive constructive criticism and suggestions from the instructor.
- The benefits of individual conferencing are:
  - It allows the students to receive personalized and specific feedback that is tailored to their strengths and weaknesses.
  - It enables the students to ask questions and clarify any confusion or misunderstanding about the assignment or the presentation topic.
  - It provides the students with an opportunity to practice their presentation skills and receive immediate feedback and guidance from the instructor.
  - It helps the students to build rapport and trust with the instructor and gain confidence and motivation for the presentation.
- The steps of individual conferencing are:
  - Before the conference, the students should prepare a draft of their presentation outline, slides, and notes, and review the assignment guidelines and rubric.
  - During the conference, the students should present their draft to the instructor, listen to the feedback and suggestions, ask questions and clarify any doubts, and take notes of the key points and action items.
  - After the conference, the students should revise their presentation based on the feedback and suggestions, and practice their presentation skills until they are confident and ready for the final presentation.
