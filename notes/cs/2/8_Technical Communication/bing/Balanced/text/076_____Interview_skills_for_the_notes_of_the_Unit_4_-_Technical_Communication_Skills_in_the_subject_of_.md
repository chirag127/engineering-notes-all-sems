### Interview skills for the notes of the Unit 4 - Technical Communication Skills

- Interview skills are the abilities and techniques that help you prepare for, perform well in, and follow up on a job interview.
- Technical communication skills are the abilities and methods that help you communicate effectively and efficiently with technical audiences, such as engineers, developers, designers, or scientists.
- Interview skills and technical communication skills are closely related, as they both involve presenting yourself and your work in a clear, concise, and professional manner.
- Some of the interview skills that are relevant for technical communication are:

  - **Researching the company and the role.** Before the interview, you should learn as much as you can about the company's mission, vision, values, products, services, culture, and goals. You should also understand the requirements and expectations of the role you are applying for, and how your skills and experience match them. This will help you tailor your answers and show your interest and enthusiasm for the job.
  - **Preparing your portfolio and samples.** As a technical communicator, you should have a portfolio that showcases your work and demonstrates your skills and abilities. You should also prepare some samples of your technical documents, such as user manuals, guides, tutorials, reports, or articles. You should be able to explain the purpose, audience, process, and outcome of each sample, and how you applied the principles and best practices of technical communication.
  - **Practicing your communication skills.** Communication skills are essential for any job interview, but especially for technical communication roles. You should practice your verbal and nonverbal communication skills, such as speaking clearly and confidently, using appropriate language and tone, listening actively and attentively, asking and answering questions, and using body language and eye contact. You should also practice your oral presentation skills, such as using visual aids, organizing your content, and engaging your audience.
  - **Solving technical problems and challenges.** In a technical interview, it is not uncommon for interviewers to present brain teasers, numerical reasoning problems, or technical assessment problems that test your ability to create solutions and solve problems. You should be able to demonstrate your analytical, logical, and creative thinking skills, as well as your technical knowledge and expertise. You should also be able to explain your approach and reasoning, and justify your decisions and choices.
  - **Collaborating with others and handling feedback.** Technical communicators often work with a variety of people and projects. Employers ask this question to learn more about your interpersonal skills. Use your answer to show that you can collaborate with others and solve problems. Explain how you use your communication skills to help you overcome challenges. You should also be ready to handle feedback, both positive and negative, from your interviewers, and show that you are open to learning and improving.
  - **Following up and expressing gratitude.** After the interview, you should send a thank-you note to the interviewers, expressing your appreciation for their time and interest, and reiterating your enthusiasm and fit for the role. You should also highlight any key points or achievements that you discussed during the interview, and address any questions or concerns that they may have. This will help you leave a positive and lasting impression, and show your professionalism and courtesy.
