### Speaking with a purpose

- Speaking with a purpose means having a clear and specific goal for your oral communication, such as informing, instructing, persuading, or entertaining your audience .
- Speaking with a purpose also means adapting your message to suit the needs, expectations, and background of your audience, as well as the context and occasion of your speech .
- Speaking with a purpose requires planning, organizing, and delivering your speech effectively, using appropriate language, tone, voice, and nonverbal cues.
- Speaking with a purpose can help you achieve various outcomes, such as:
  - Sharing technical or specialized information with a non-expert or general audience 
  - Convincing your audience to take some specific action or adopt a certain viewpoint 
  - Building rapport and trust with your audience and enhancing your credibility
  - Engaging and entertaining your audience and making a lasting impression
- A useful framework for speaking with a purpose is PAM (Purpose, Audience, and Message), which helps you define your objective, analyze your audience, and craft your message accordingly.
