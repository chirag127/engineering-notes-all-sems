### 7 Cs of effective business writing

Business writing is a form of technical communication that aims to convey information, persuade readers, or achieve a specific goal in a clear, concise, and professional manner. To achieve effective business writing, one should follow the 7 Cs, which are:

- **Clarity**: The message should be clear and easy to understand. Avoid ambiguity, jargon, and unnecessary words. Use simple and direct language, and organize the information logically.
- **Conciseness**: The message should be brief and to the point. Avoid repetition, redundancy, and irrelevant details. Use short and simple sentences, and omit needless modifiers.
- **Completeness**: The message should include all the necessary information and details. Avoid leaving out important facts, figures, or evidence. Provide enough background and context, and answer the 5 Ws and 1 H (who, what, when, where, why, and how).
- **Correctness**: The message should be accurate and free of errors. Avoid factual, grammatical, spelling, or punctuation mistakes. Use appropriate tone, style, and format, and follow the rules and conventions of the language.
- **Courtesy**: The message should be polite and respectful. Avoid offensive, rude, or insensitive language. Use positive and constructive words, and show appreciation and empathy for the reader.
- **Consideration**: The message should be tailored to the audience and purpose. Avoid making assumptions, generalizations, or stereotypes. Use the appropriate level of formality, and address the reader's needs, interests, and expectations.
- **Concreteness**: The message should be specific and precise. Avoid vague, abstract, or general statements. Use concrete and factual examples, and support the claims with evidence and data.
