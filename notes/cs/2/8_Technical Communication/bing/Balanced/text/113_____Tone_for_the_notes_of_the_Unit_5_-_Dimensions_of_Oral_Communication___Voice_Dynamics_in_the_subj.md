### Tone for the notes of the Unit 5 - Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics in the subject of Technical Communication

- Tone is the attitude or emotion that the speaker conveys through their voice and words.
- Tone can affect the meaning and impact of the message, as well as the relationship between the speaker and the listener.
- Tone can be influenced by various factors, such as the purpose, context, audience, mood, and personality of the speaker.
- Tone can be classified into different types, such as formal, informal, friendly, hostile, polite, rude, confident, hesitant, etc.
- Tone can be modified by using different vocal elements, such as pitch, volume, rate, pause, stress, and intonation.
- Tone can also be enhanced by using non-verbal cues, such as facial expressions, gestures, eye contact, and posture.
- Tone can be adjusted according to the situation and the feedback from the listener, to achieve the desired effect and outcome of the communication.
- Tone can be evaluated by listening to the speaker's voice and words, and observing their body language and reactions.
- Tone can be improved by practicing and developing vocal skills, as well as being aware of the audience's needs and expectations.
