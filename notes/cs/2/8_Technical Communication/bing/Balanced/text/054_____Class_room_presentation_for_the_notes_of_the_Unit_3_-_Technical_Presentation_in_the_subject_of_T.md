### Class room presentation for the notes of the Unit 3 - Technical Presentation in the subject of Technical Communication

- A class room presentation is a form of oral communication that involves delivering information, ideas, or arguments to an audience of students and teachers.
- A class room presentation can have various purposes, such as informing, persuading, demonstrating, or entertaining.
- A class room presentation can be based on a written report, a research project, a case study, a group work, or a personal experience.
- A class room presentation requires careful planning, preparation, and practice to achieve the desired outcomes and to overcome the challenges of speaking in public.
- A class room presentation consists of three main parts: introduction, body, and conclusion.
- The introduction should capture the attention of the audience, establish the topic and purpose of the presentation, and preview the main points or outline.
- The body should develop the main points or arguments with supporting evidence, examples, or data. The body should also address the possible questions or objections from the audience and provide counterarguments or solutions.
- The conclusion should summarize the main points or arguments, restate the purpose of the presentation, and provide a call to action or a recommendation for the audience.
- A class room presentation should follow the principles of effective technical communication, such as clarity, conciseness, coherence, accuracy, and appropriateness.
- A class room presentation should also use various verbal and nonverbal techniques to enhance the delivery and the impact of the message, such as voice, eye contact, gestures, posture, facial expressions, visual aids, and transitions.
