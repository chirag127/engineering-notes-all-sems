### Strategies for the notes of the Unit 3 - Technical Presentation in the subject of Technical Communication

- Notes are brief records of the main points, ideas, or facts of a presentation.
- Notes can help the presenter to recall the structure, content, and delivery of the presentation.
- Notes can also help the audience to follow the presentation and retain the information.
- There are different types of notes, such as outlines, cue cards, mind maps, and slides.
- The choice of notes depends on the purpose, audience, and context of the presentation, as well as the personal preference of the presenter.
- Some general strategies for preparing and using notes are:

  - Use keywords, phrases, or symbols instead of full sentences or paragraphs.
  - Use bullet points, numbers, or indentation to organize the notes logically and hierarchically.
  - Use colors, fonts, or highlighting to emphasize the main points or transitions.
  - Use diagrams, charts, graphs, or images to illustrate the data or concepts.
  - Use abbreviations, acronyms, or shorthand to save space and time.
  - Use consistent and clear formatting and notation throughout the notes.
  - Review and revise the notes before the presentation to ensure accuracy and completeness.
  - Practice the presentation with the notes to ensure fluency and confidence.
  - Keep the notes visible, accessible, and readable during the presentation.
  - Refer to the notes briefly and occasionally, but do not read from them or rely on them too much.
  - Adapt the notes to the feedback, questions, or comments from the audience during the presentation.
