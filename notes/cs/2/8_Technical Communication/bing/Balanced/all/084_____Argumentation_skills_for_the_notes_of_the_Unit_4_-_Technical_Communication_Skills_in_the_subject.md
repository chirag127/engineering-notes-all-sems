# Argumentation skills for the notes of the Unit 4 - Technical Communication Skills in the subject of Technical Communication

- Argumentation skills are the ability to present and support a claim or a point of view using logical reasoning and evidence.
- Argumentation skills are important for technical communication because they help to persuade, inform, or convince the audience of the validity, relevance, or significance of a technical problem, solution, or recommendation.
- Argumentation skills involve the following steps:
  - Identify the purpose and the audience of the argument.
  - Formulate a clear and specific thesis statement that expresses the main claim or position of the argument.
  - Provide supporting arguments that explain and justify the thesis statement using facts, examples, statistics, expert opinions, or other types of evidence.
  - Anticipate and address possible counterarguments or objections that the audience might have and refute them with counter-evidence or concessions.
  - Organize the argument in a logical and coherent structure that follows the principles of clarity, conciseness, and completeness.
  - Use appropriate language, tone, and style that suit the purpose, audience, and context of the argument.
  - Revise and edit the argument for accuracy, consistency, and correctness of grammar, spelling, and punctuation.
- Argumentation skills can be applied to various types of technical communication, such as reports, proposals, memos, letters, emails, presentations, or articles.
