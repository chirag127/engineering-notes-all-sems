# Socio-linguistic competence

- Socio-linguistic competence is the ability to use and respond to language appropriately, given the setting, the topic, and the relationships among the people communicating.
- Socio-linguistic competence involves understanding the social and cultural context of language use, such as the norms, values, beliefs, and behavioural patterns of a culture.
- Socio-linguistic competence also includes knowing which words and phrases fit a given situation and audience, and how to achieve the desired effect with language.
- Socio-linguistic competence is different from linguistic competence, which is the system of unconscious knowledge that one knows when they know a language.
- Socio-linguistic competence is important for effective communication, especially in cross-cultural settings, where different rules and expectations may apply.
- Socio-linguistic competence can be developed by exposure to various forms and styles of language, by observing and analyzing how language is used in different contexts, and by practicing and receiving feedback on one's own language use.
