# Analysis for the notes of the Unit 4 - Technical Communication Skills in the subject of Technical Communication

- Unit 4 of the subject of Technical Communication covers the topic of **Technical Communication Skills**, which are the abilities to communicate effectively in various technical contexts and situations.
- The unit consists of four subtopics: **Interview**, **Group Discussion**, **Presentation**, and **Technical Writing**.
- The following are the main points of each subtopic:

  - **Interview**: It is an interaction between two or more persons for a specific purpose, in which the interviewer asks the interviewee specific questions in order to assess his/her suitability for recruitment, admission, or promotion  . The person who answers the questions of an interview is called an interviewee or a candidate . The person who asks the questions of an interview is called an interviewer or a panelist . The interview can be classified into different types, such as structured, unstructured, behavioral, situational, stress, panel, group, telephonic, video, and online  . The interview requires the interviewee to prepare well, dress appropriately, arrive on time, greet the interviewer, listen carefully, answer confidently, ask relevant questions, and thank the interviewer  .

  - **Group Discussion**: It is a technique of communication in which a small group of people exchange their ideas, opinions, and arguments on a given topic or problem in a face-to-face situation  . The group discussion can be used for various purposes, such as selection, problem-solving, decision-making, brainstorming, and learning  . The group discussion can be categorized into different types, such as topical, case-based, abstract, and role-play  . The group discussion requires the participants to follow certain rules, such as be polite, respect others, speak clearly, listen actively, contribute positively, and summarize effectively  .

  - **Presentation**: It is a form of oral communication in which a speaker delivers a message to an audience with the help of visual aids, such as slides, charts, graphs, images, and videos  . The presentation can be used for various purposes, such as informing, persuading, educating, entertaining, and motivating  . The presentation can be classified into different types, such as informative, persuasive, demonstrative, and narrative  . The presentation requires the speaker to plan well, design effectively, practice thoroughly, deliver confidently, and handle questions skillfully  .

  - **Technical Writing**: It is a type of written communication that conveys technical information to a specific audience for a specific purpose, such as instruction, explanation, description, or persuasion  . The technical writing can be used for various purposes, such as manuals, reports, proposals, memos, letters, emails, and blogs  . The technical writing can be characterized by certain features, such as clarity, accuracy, conciseness, coherence, objectivity, and professionalism  . The technical writing requires the writer to follow certain steps, such as analyze the audience, define the purpose, collect the information, organize the content, draft the document, revise the document, and format the document  .

- The unit aims to help the students to develop and enhance their technical communication skills, which are essential for their academic and professional success. The unit also provides the students with various examples, exercises, and tips to practice and improve their technical communication skills. The unit is based on the syllabus and guidelines of the Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University (AKTU).
