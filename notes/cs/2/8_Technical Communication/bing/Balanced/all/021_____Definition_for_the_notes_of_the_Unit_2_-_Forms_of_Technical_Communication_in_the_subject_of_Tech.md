# Definition for the notes of the Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication

- Technical communication is the process of conveying information and instructions to a specific audience for a specific purpose.
- Forms of technical communication are the different ways of presenting technical information and instructions to the audience, such as reports, manuals, proposals, memos, etc.
- The main forms of technical communication are:

  - **Reports**: Documents that provide factual information and analysis on a topic, such as progress reports, feasibility reports, research reports, etc.
  - **Manuals**: Documents that provide instructions and guidance on how to use a product, service, or system, such as user manuals, installation manuals, troubleshooting manuals, etc.
  - **Proposals**: Documents that present a plan or an idea for a project, product, or service, and persuade the audience to accept it, such as business proposals, grant proposals, project proposals, etc.
  - **Memos**: Documents that communicate brief messages or information within an organization, such as policy memos, meeting memos, announcement memos, etc.
  - **Letters**: Documents that communicate formal messages or information to external parties, such as customers, clients, suppliers, etc., such as complaint letters, inquiry letters, recommendation letters, etc.
  - **Emails**: Documents that communicate informal messages or information to internal or external parties, such as colleagues, friends, family, etc., such as status emails, feedback emails, thank-you emails, etc.
  - **Presentations**: Documents that present information and ideas orally or visually to an audience, such as slides, posters, videos, etc.
  - **Websites**: Documents that present information and ideas online to a global audience, such as blogs, wikis, podcasts, etc.
