# Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication

- Technical communication is the process of conveying information and instructions to a specific audience for a specific purpose.
- Technical communication can take various forms, such as written, oral, visual, or digital.
- Some common forms of technical communication are:

  - **Reports**: Documents that present facts, analysis, and recommendations on a topic or a problem. Reports can be formal or informal, internal or external, analytical or informational, etc.
  - **Proposals**: Documents that offer a plan or a solution to a problem or a need. Proposals can be solicited or unsolicited, internal or external, academic or professional, etc.
  - **Manuals**: Documents that provide instructions or guidance on how to use a product, a service, a system, or a process. Manuals can be user manuals, technical manuals, reference manuals, etc.
  - **Presentations**: Oral or visual communication that delivers information or ideas to an audience. Presentations can be informative or persuasive, formal or informal, individual or group, etc.
  - **Websites**: Digital platforms that provide information or services to users. Websites can be informational or interactive, personal or professional, static or dynamic, etc.
  - **Emails**: Electronic messages that communicate information or requests to one or more recipients. Emails can be formal or informal, internal or external, brief or detailed, etc.
  - **Memos**: Short documents that communicate information or requests within an organization. Memos can be formal or informal, internal or external, brief or detailed, etc.
  - **Letters**: Written documents that communicate information or requests to one or more recipients outside an organization. Letters can be formal or informal, internal or external, brief or detailed, etc.

- Each form of technical communication has its own purpose, audience, structure, style, and format.
- Technical communicators should choose the appropriate form of communication based on the context and the goal of the communication.
