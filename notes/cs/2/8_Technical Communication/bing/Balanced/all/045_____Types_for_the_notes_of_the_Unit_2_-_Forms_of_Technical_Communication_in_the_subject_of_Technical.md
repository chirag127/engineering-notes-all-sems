# Types for the notes of the Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication

- Forms of Technical Communication are the different ways of conveying technical information to a specific audience for a specific purpose.
- Some common forms of technical communication are:

  - **Technical reports**: These are documents that present the results of research, analysis, or evaluation of a technical problem or project. They usually have a formal structure, such as an abstract, introduction, methods, results, discussion, conclusion, and references. They may also include figures, tables, graphs, charts, and appendices to support the main text. Technical reports are often written for experts, managers, or clients who need to understand the details and implications of the technical work.

  - **Technical proposals**: These are documents that propose a solution to a technical problem or a plan for a technical project. They usually have a persuasive tone, as they aim to convince the reader to accept the proposed idea, method, or product. They may also have a formal structure, such as a cover letter, executive summary, introduction, background, objectives, methods, schedule, budget, qualifications, and references. They may also include figures, tables, graphs, charts, and appendices to support the main text. Technical proposals are often written for potential sponsors, funders, or clients who need to evaluate the feasibility and benefits of the proposed work.

  - **Technical manuals**: These are documents that provide instructions or guidance on how to use, operate, maintain, or repair a technical product, system, or process. They usually have a descriptive and instructional tone, as they aim to explain the features, functions, and procedures of the technical subject. They may also have a modular structure, such as a table of contents, introduction, overview, chapters, sections, sub-sections, steps, tips, warnings, glossary, index, and appendices. They may also include figures, diagrams, screenshots, icons, and symbols to illustrate the main text. Technical manuals are often written for users, customers, or technicians who need to learn or perform the technical tasks.

  - **Technical presentations**: These are oral or visual forms of communication that present technical information to a specific audience for a specific purpose. They usually have a clear and concise tone, as they aim to deliver the main points and key messages of the technical work. They may also have a logical structure, such as an introduction, body, and conclusion. They may also use slides, posters, videos, animations, or other visual aids to support the main speech. Technical presentations are often given for peers, colleagues, supervisors, or customers who need to understand or appreciate the technical work.
