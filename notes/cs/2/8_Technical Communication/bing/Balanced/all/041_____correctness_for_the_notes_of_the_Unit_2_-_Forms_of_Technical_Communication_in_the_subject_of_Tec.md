# Correctness for the notes of the Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication

- Correctness is one of the essential qualities of effective technical communication.
- Correctness means that the information presented is accurate, complete, and free of errors, such as grammatical, spelling, punctuation, or factual mistakes.
- Correctness also implies that the tone, style, and format of the communication are appropriate for the purpose, audience, and context.
- Correctness can enhance the credibility, clarity, and persuasiveness of the communication, while incorrectness can undermine them and cause confusion, misunderstanding, or mistrust.
- Some of the factors that affect the correctness of technical communication are:

  - Accuracy: The information should be based on reliable sources, verified by evidence, and updated as needed. The information should not be distorted, exaggerated, or omitted.
  - Completeness: The information should cover all the relevant aspects of the topic, answer the questions or needs of the audience, and provide sufficient details and examples. The information should not be too vague, general, or superficial.
  - Consistency: The information should be coherent, logical, and organized. The information should follow the same conventions, standards, and terminology throughout the communication. The information should not be contradictory, redundant, or irrelevant.
  - Conformity: The information should adhere to the rules and principles of grammar, spelling, punctuation, and syntax. The information should also follow the guidelines and specifications of the document type, format, and style. The information should not contain errors, typos, or deviations that affect the readability and professionalism of the communication.
  - Courtesy: The information should be respectful, polite, and considerate of the audience. The information should avoid offensive, biased, or inappropriate language, tone, or expressions. The information should also acknowledge the sources, opinions, or contributions of others.
