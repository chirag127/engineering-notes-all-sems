# Analysis & Findings for the notes of the Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication

- Technical communication is a form of communication based on the writing and sharing of technical information with a specialized target audience.
- Technical communication can have various purposes, such as defining, instructing, informing, or persuading.
- Technical communication can take various forms, such as letters, emails, instructions, reports, proposals, websites, blogs, manuals, online help, reference information, consumer literature, etc  .
- Technical communication requires certain skills, such as clarity, accuracy, conciseness, coherence, organization, design, and ethics.
- Technical communication can have various benefits, such as improving productivity, quality, safety, customer satisfaction, and professional reputation.
