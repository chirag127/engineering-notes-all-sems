# Technical Proposal for the notes of the Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication

- A technical proposal is a document that outlines a project plan, its objectives, methods, expected outcomes, and budget.
- A technical proposal is usually written for a specific audience, such as a client, a funding agency, or a supervisor.
- A technical proposal should be clear, concise, and persuasive, and should follow a logical structure and format.
- A technical proposal typically consists of the following sections:

  - **Title page**: This section includes the title of the proposal, the name and affiliation of the author(s), the date of submission, and the name and contact information of the recipient(s).
  - **Abstract**: This section provides a brief summary of the proposal, highlighting the main points, such as the problem statement, the objectives, the methods, the expected outcomes, and the significance of the project.
  - **Table of contents**: This section lists the headings and subheadings of the proposal, along with the page numbers.
  - **Introduction**: This section introduces the topic and the background of the project, defines the problem or the need that the project aims to address, states the objectives and the scope of the project, and provides an overview of the proposal.
  - **Literature review**: This section reviews the existing literature and research related to the project, identifies the gaps and limitations in the current knowledge, and establishes the relevance and originality of the project.
  - **Methodology**: This section describes the methods and procedures that will be used to carry out the project, such as the research design, the data collection and analysis techniques, the instruments and materials, the timeline, and the ethical considerations.
  - **Results and discussion**: This section presents and interprets the expected results and outcomes of the project, and discusses their implications and limitations.
  - **Conclusion and recommendations**: This section summarizes the main findings and contributions of the project, and provides recommendations for future research or action.
  - **References**: This section lists the sources that are cited in the proposal, following a consistent citation style.
  - **Appendices**: This section includes any additional or supplementary information that supports the proposal, such as tables, figures, charts, graphs, maps, questionnaires, etc.
