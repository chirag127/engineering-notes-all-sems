# Style for the notes of the Unit 4 - Technical Communication Skills in the subject of Technical Communication

- Style is the way a writer expresses ideas in a document. It includes the choice of words, sentences, and paragraphs, as well as the tone, voice, and perspective of the writer.
- Style is important for technical communication because it affects the clarity, accuracy, and effectiveness of the message. A good style can help the reader understand the purpose, content, and organization of the document, as well as the credibility and professionalism of the writer.
- Some general principles of style for technical communication are:

  - Use plain and precise language. Avoid jargon, slang, idioms, euphemisms, and vague or ambiguous terms. Use technical terms only when necessary and define them clearly for the audience. Use words that are familiar, concrete, and specific to convey the meaning clearly and accurately.
  - Use active voice and strong verbs. Active voice shows who or what performs the action in a sentence, while passive voice shows who or what receives the action. Active voice is usually more direct, clear, and concise than passive voice. Strong verbs express the action clearly and forcefully, while weak verbs rely on modifiers or auxiliary verbs. Use active voice and strong verbs to emphasize the agent and the action in a sentence, and to avoid wordiness and confusion.
  - Use parallel structure and consistent format. Parallel structure means using the same grammatical form for items in a list, a series, or a comparison. Consistent format means using the same font, size, color, alignment, spacing, and punctuation for headings, subheadings, lists, tables, figures, and other elements in a document. Use parallel structure and consistent format to show the relationship and hierarchy of the information, and to make the document easy to read and follow.
  - Use positive and courteous tone. Positive tone means using words and phrases that express confidence, optimism, and respect. Courteous tone means using words and phrases that show politeness, consideration, and appreciation. Use positive and courteous tone to establish rapport with the audience, to convey the message in a friendly and professional manner, and to avoid negative or offensive expressions.
