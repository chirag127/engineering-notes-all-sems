# Individual conferencing for the notes of the Unit 3 - Technical Presentation in the subject of Technical Communication

- Individual conferencing is a process of meeting one-on-one with a teacher or a peer to discuss and improve a technical presentation.
- The purpose of individual conferencing is to provide feedback, guidance, and support to the presenter, as well as to identify and address any issues or challenges that may arise during the preparation or delivery of the presentation.
- The benefits of individual conferencing include:
  - Enhancing the quality and effectiveness of the presentation by incorporating the suggestions and insights of the reviewer.
  - Developing the presenter's skills and confidence in technical communication by receiving constructive criticism and encouragement.
  - Strengthening the relationship and collaboration between the presenter and the reviewer by establishing trust and mutual respect.
- The steps of individual conferencing are:
  - Before the conference, the presenter should prepare a draft or an outline of the presentation, as well as a list of questions or concerns that they want to discuss with the reviewer.
  - During the conference, the presenter should share their presentation and explain their goals, audience, and main points. The reviewer should listen attentively and ask clarifying questions if needed. The reviewer should then provide feedback on the strengths and weaknesses of the presentation, as well as suggestions for improvement. The presenter should listen respectfully and take notes of the feedback. The presenter and the reviewer should also discuss and resolve any issues or challenges that the presenter may face in delivering the presentation.
  - After the conference, the presenter should revise and refine their presentation based on the feedback received. The presenter should also thank the reviewer for their time and input. The presenter and the reviewer may schedule a follow-up conference if necessary.
