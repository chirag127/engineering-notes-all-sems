### Methods of Presentation

- A method of presentation is a way of communicating information to an audience, such as speech, visuals, storytelling, etc.
- Different methods of presentation have different advantages and disadvantages, depending on the purpose, audience, and context of the presentation.
- Some common methods of presentation are:

  - **Speech**: This is the most common method of presentation, where the speaker uses verbal language to convey the message. Speech can be enhanced by using vocal variety, humor, humility, and avoiding technical jargon .
  - **Visuals**: These are graphical elements that support the speech, such as charts, diagrams, images, videos, etc. Visuals can help explain technical information and processes, capture attention, and increase retention .
  - **Storytelling**: This is a method of presentation that uses narrative techniques to engage the audience emotionally and intellectually. Storytelling can help illustrate technical concepts, provide examples, and create interest.
  - **Impact**: This is a method of presentation that focuses on the benefits and outcomes of the technical information, rather than the details and features. Impact can help persuade the audience, motivate action, and demonstrate relevance .

- A good presentation should use a combination of methods, depending on the goal, audience, and context of the presentation. A good presentation should also:

  - Have a clear and specific purpose
  - Have a logical and coherent structure
  - Have a strong introduction and conclusion
  - Use appropriate language and tone 
  - Use reliable and credible sources
  - Use effective delivery skills, such as eye contact, gestures, posture, etc. 
  - Use feedback and evaluation to improve
