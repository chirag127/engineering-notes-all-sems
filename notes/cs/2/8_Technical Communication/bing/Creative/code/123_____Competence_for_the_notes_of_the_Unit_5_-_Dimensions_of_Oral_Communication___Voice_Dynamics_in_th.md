# Competence for the notes of the Unit 5 - Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics in the subject of Technical Communication

- Competence is the ability to perform a task effectively and efficiently.
- Communication competence is how well a person can communicate their thoughts and ideas to others.
- Oral communication competence is the ability to use spoken language for professional purposes, such as persuading, informing, or entertaining an audience.
- Oral communication competence involves the following dimensions:
  - Cognitive dimension: the knowledge and understanding of the topic, the audience, the context, and the communication goals.
  - Behavioral dimension: the skills and strategies to use verbal and nonverbal cues, such as tone, gestures, eye contact, and feedback.
  - Affective dimension: the attitudes and emotions that influence the communication process, such as confidence, interest, and empathy.
- Oral communication competence also requires the following elements:
  - Language proficiency: the mastery of the linguistic components, such as vocabulary, grammar, pronunciation, and fluency.
  - Communicative competence: the ability to use language appropriately and effectively in various situations, taking into account the cultural norms, beliefs, and practices of the interlocutors.
  - Critical thinking: the ability to analyze, evaluate, and synthesize information from various sources, and to present logical and coherent arguments.
  - Metacognitive skills: the ability to monitor and regulate one's own communication process, and to reflect on and improve one's performance.
- Oral communication competence can be developed by becoming a more mindful communicator and a higher self-monitor, which means paying attention to one's own and others' communication behaviors, and adjusting them according to the feedback and the situation.
- Oral communication competence can be assessed by using various methods, such as rubrics, portfolios, peer evaluations, self-evaluations, and oral presentations.
- Oral communication competence can benefit a person in various ways, such as enhancing their academic performance, career prospects, interpersonal relationships, and personal growth.
