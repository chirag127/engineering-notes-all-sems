### Methods for the notes of the Unit 1 - Fundamentals of Technical Communication

- The notes should cover the following topics and subtopics as per the syllabus:

  - Technical Communication: Features; Distinction between General and Technical Communication; Language as a tool of Communication; Dimensions of Communication: Reading & comprehension; Technical writing: sentences; Paragraph; Technical style: Definition, types & Methods; The flow of Technical Communication.
  - Approaches/Requisites of Technical Communication: Brevity; Clarity; Coherence; Cohesion; Completeness; Conciseness; Correctness; Courtesy; Emphasis; Objectivity; Organization; Precision; Relevance; Simplicity; Tone; Visual Aids.
  - Barriers to Communication: Physical; Semantic; Psychological; Cross-cultural; Ethical; Overcoming barriers to communication.

- The notes should follow the guidelines for effective technical writing:

  - Identify the purpose and audience of the communication.
  - Use clear, concise, and accurate language that is appropriate for the context and the audience.
  - Organize the information logically and coherently, using headings, subheadings, lists, tables, graphs, etc. as needed.
  - Provide sufficient details, examples, and evidence to support the main points and arguments.
  - Use consistent and correct grammar, spelling, punctuation, and formatting.
  - Cite the sources of information and acknowledge the contributions of others.
  - Revise and edit the communication for clarity, completeness, and correctness.

- The notes should include examples, illustrations, and exercises to help the students understand and apply the concepts and skills of technical communication .
- The notes should be written in a simple, direct, and objective tone, avoiding jargon, slang, bias, and ambiguity .
- The notes should be formatted according to the standards and conventions of technical communication, such as using headings, margins, fonts, spacing, alignment, etc. .
