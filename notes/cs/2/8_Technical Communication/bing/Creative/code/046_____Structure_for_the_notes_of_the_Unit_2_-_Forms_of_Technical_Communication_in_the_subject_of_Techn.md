# Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication

## Introduction
- Technical communication is the process of conveying information and instructions to a specific audience for a specific purpose.
- Technical communication can take various forms, such as reports, manuals, proposals, memos, emails, presentations, websites, blogs, podcasts, etc.
- Technical communication can be classified into two main categories: internal and external communication.

## Internal Communication
- Internal communication is the exchange of information and instructions within an organization or a team.
- Internal communication can have various purposes, such as informing, instructing, persuading, coordinating, motivating, etc.
- Internal communication can use various forms, such as memos, emails, newsletters, reports, manuals, etc.
- Internal communication should be clear, concise, accurate, consistent, and respectful.

## External Communication
- External communication is the exchange of information and instructions with people outside the organization or the team, such as customers, clients, suppliers, partners, regulators, etc.
- External communication can have various purposes, such as informing, instructing, persuading, promoting, negotiating, etc.
- External communication can use various forms, such as proposals, letters, brochures, websites, blogs, podcasts, etc.
- External communication should be clear, concise, accurate, consistent, and courteous.

## Factors Affecting the Choice of Forms of Technical Communication
- The choice of forms of technical communication depends on various factors, such as:
  - The purpose of the communication
  - The audience of the communication
  - The context of the communication
  - The medium of the communication
  - The resources and constraints of the communication
- The choice of forms of technical communication should be appropriate, effective, and ethical.
