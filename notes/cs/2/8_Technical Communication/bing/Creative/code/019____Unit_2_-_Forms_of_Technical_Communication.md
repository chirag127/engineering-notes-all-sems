## Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication

Technical communication is the process of conveying information and instructions to a specific audience for a specific purpose. Technical communication can take various forms, such as:

- **Written communication**: This includes documents, reports, manuals, proposals, memos, emails, letters, etc. that use text, graphics, tables, charts, and other elements to communicate technical information. Written communication is usually formal, clear, concise, and accurate.
- **Oral communication**: This includes presentations, speeches, lectures, interviews, meetings, etc. that use spoken words, gestures, visuals, and other aids to communicate technical information. Oral communication is usually interactive, dynamic, and persuasive.
- **Visual communication**: This includes diagrams, drawings, photographs, videos, animations, graphs, maps, etc. that use images, symbols, colors, and other elements to communicate technical information. Visual communication is usually attractive, informative, and complementary to other forms of communication.
- **Digital communication**: This includes websites, blogs, podcasts, social media, wikis, etc. that use electronic media, software, and networks to communicate technical information. Digital communication is usually interactive, accessible, and adaptable to different devices and platforms.
