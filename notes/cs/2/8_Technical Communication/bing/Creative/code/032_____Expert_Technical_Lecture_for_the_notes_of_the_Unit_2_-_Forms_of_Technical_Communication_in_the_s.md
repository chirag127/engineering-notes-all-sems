## Introduction

Technical communication is the process of conveying information and instructions to a specific audience for a specific purpose. Technical communication can take various forms, such as written, oral, visual, or digital. In this lecture, we will discuss some of the common forms of technical communication and their characteristics, advantages, and disadvantages.

## Written Technical Communication

Written technical communication is the most widely used form of technical communication. It involves creating documents that communicate technical information in a clear, concise, and accurate manner. Some examples of written technical communication are:

- Reports: Reports are documents that present the results of an investigation, analysis, or evaluation of a problem, situation, or phenomenon. Reports can be classified into different types, such as feasibility reports, progress reports, lab reports, research reports, etc. Reports usually have a standard structure, such as title page, abstract, introduction, body, conclusion, recommendations, references, and appendices.
- Manuals: Manuals are documents that provide instructions or guidance on how to use, operate, maintain, or repair a product, system, or service. Manuals can be classified into different types, such as user manuals, installation manuals, maintenance manuals, troubleshooting manuals, etc. Manuals usually have a modular structure, such as cover page, table of contents, introduction, overview, procedures, warnings, tips, glossary, index, etc.
- Proposals: Proposals are documents that propose a solution to a problem, a plan for a project, or a request for funding or approval. Proposals can be classified into different types, such as solicited proposals, unsolicited proposals, internal proposals, external proposals, etc. Proposals usually have a persuasive structure, such as title page, executive summary, introduction, background, objectives, methods, benefits, costs, schedule, qualifications, references, etc.
- Memos: Memos are documents that communicate brief messages or information within an organization. Memos can be classified into different types, such as informational memos, directive memos, request memos, response memos, etc. Memos usually have a simple structure, such as heading, introduction, body, conclusion, and signature.

Some of the advantages of written technical communication are:

- It provides a permanent record of the information and can be referred to later.
- It can reach a large and diverse audience across time and space.
- It can convey complex and detailed information with precision and clarity.
- It can be edited, revised, and formatted to enhance the quality and appearance of the document.

Some of the disadvantages of written technical communication are:

- It can be time-consuming and costly to produce and distribute.
- It can be misunderstood or misinterpreted by the readers due to lack of feedback or context.
- It can be outdated or obsolete due to changes in technology or circumstances.
- It can be lost, damaged, or stolen.
