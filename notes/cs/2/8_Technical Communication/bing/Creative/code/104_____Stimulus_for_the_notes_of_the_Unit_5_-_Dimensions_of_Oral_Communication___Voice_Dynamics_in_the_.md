### Stimulus for the notes of the Unit 5 - Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics in the subject of Technical Communication

- The purpose of this unit is to provide an overview of the various aspects of oral communication and voice dynamics that are essential for effective technical communication.
- Oral communication refers to the process of conveying information, ideas, opinions, or emotions through spoken words, gestures, and non-verbal cues.
- Voice dynamics refers to the modulation of one's voice to suit the context, purpose, and audience of the communication.
- Some of the dimensions of oral communication and voice dynamics that are covered in this unit are:

  - The advantages and disadvantages of oral communication over written communication.
  - The types and modes of oral communication, such as formal and informal, interpersonal and group, synchronous and asynchronous, etc.
  - The principles and techniques of oral communication, such as clarity, brevity, coherence, relevance, etc.
  - The factors affecting oral communication, such as physical, psychological, social, cultural, and environmental factors.
  - The barriers and challenges of oral communication, such as noise, distraction, misunderstanding, bias, etc.
  - The strategies and skills for overcoming the barriers and challenges of oral communication, such as active listening, feedback, paraphrasing, questioning, etc.
  - The elements and characteristics of voice dynamics, such as pitch, volume, rate, tone, etc.
  - The importance and benefits of voice dynamics for oral communication, such as enhancing the message, expressing emotions, creating rapport, etc.
  - The tips and exercises for improving one's voice dynamics, such as breathing, articulation, pronunciation, etc.

- The unit also provides some examples and activities to illustrate and practice the concepts and skills of oral communication and voice dynamics.
