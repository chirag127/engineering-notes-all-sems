### Distinction between General and Technical Communication

General communication and technical communication are two types of communication that differ in their purpose, style, audience, and content. Here are some of the main distinctions between them:

- **Purpose**: General communication is usually aimed at conveying information, opinions, emotions, or social interactions. Technical communication is usually aimed at instructing, persuading, or informing about a specific topic or task.
- **Style**: General communication is often informal, subjective, and flexible. Technical communication is often formal, objective, and precise.
- **Audience**: General communication is not always intended for a specific audience or context. Technical communication is always tailored to the needs, expectations, and background of a specific audience and context.
- **Content**: General communication may contain any type of message, such as personal, literary, or artistic. Technical communication may contain only technical messages, such as scientific, engineering, or business. Technical communication also makes use of technical terms, graphics, and formats to convey the message clearly and effectively    .
