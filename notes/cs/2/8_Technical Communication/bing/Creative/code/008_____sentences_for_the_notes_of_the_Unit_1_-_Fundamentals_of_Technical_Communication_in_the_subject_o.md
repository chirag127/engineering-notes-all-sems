### Sentences for the notes of the Unit 1 - Fundamentals of Technical Communication

- Technical communication is the process of conveying information in a clear, concise, and accurate manner to a specific audience for a specific purpose .
- Technical communication differs from general communication in terms of its features, such as accuracy, clarity, conciseness, coherence, objectivity, and usability .
- Language is a tool of communication that enables the exchange of ideas, information, and emotions among people .
- Dimensions of communication include reading, writing, listening, speaking, and non-verbal communication .
- Reading and comprehension are essential skills for technical communication, as they help to understand the purpose, context, and content of a text .
- Technical writing is the creation of documents that provide instructions, explanations, descriptions, or reports on technical or specialized topics .
- Sentences are the basic units of technical writing, and they should be clear, concise, coherent, and correct .
- Paragraphs are groups of sentences that develop a main idea, and they should have a topic sentence, supporting sentences, and a concluding sentence .
- Technical style is the way of presenting information in a technical document, and it should be consistent, appropriate, and effective .
- Definition is a statement that explains the meaning of a term or concept, and it can be classified into formal, informal, extended, and operational definitions .
- Types of definitions include synonyms, antonyms, examples, etymology, and classification .
- Methods of definitions include genus and difference, function, structure, and process .
- The flow of communication is the direction and sequence of information exchange among the sender, receiver, and channel of communication .
- Types of communication flow include downward, upward, horizontal, diagonal, and circular .
- Barriers to communication are factors that hinder the effectiveness of communication, such as noise, distortion, ambiguity, jargon, and cultural differences .
