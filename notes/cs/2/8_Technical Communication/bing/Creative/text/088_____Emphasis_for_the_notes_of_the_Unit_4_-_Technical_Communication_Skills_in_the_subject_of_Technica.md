### Emphasis

- Emphasis is the technique of highlighting the important or main points in a text or speech.
- Emphasis can be achieved by using various methods, such as:
  - Position: placing the key information at the beginning or end of a sentence, paragraph, or document.
  - Repetition: repeating the same word, phrase, or idea for emphasis or clarity.
  - Contrast: using opposite or contrasting words or ideas to create emphasis or interest.
  - Typography: using different fonts, sizes, colors, or styles to draw attention to certain words or phrases.
  - Punctuation: using marks such as commas, dashes, colons, or exclamation points to separate or highlight important information.
  - Figures of speech: using rhetorical devices such as metaphors, similes, hyperbole, or irony to create emphasis or effect.
- Emphasis is important for effective technical communication because it helps the reader or listener to:
  - Identify the main purpose or message of the text or speech.
  - Understand the structure and organization of the text or speech.
  - Follow the logic and reasoning of the text or speech.
  - Remember the key points or details of the text or speech.
  - Engage with the text or speech and respond to it.
