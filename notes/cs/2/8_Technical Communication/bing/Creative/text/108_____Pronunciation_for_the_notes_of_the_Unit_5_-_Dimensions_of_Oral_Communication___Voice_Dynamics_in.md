### Pronunciation for the notes of the Unit 5 - Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics in the subject of Technical Communication

- Pronunciation is the way a word or a language is spoken, or the manner in which someone utters a word.
- Pronunciation is one of the dimensions of oral communication that affects the clarity, accuracy and effectiveness of the message.
- Pronunciation involves the correct articulation of sounds, syllables, stress, intonation and rhythm of speech.
- Some factors that influence pronunciation are:
  - The native language of the speaker and the influence of its sound system and rules.
  - The exposure to the target language and the variety of accents and dialects.
  - The level of education and training in the target language and the awareness of the pronunciation rules and norms.
  - The purpose and context of communication and the expectations of the audience and the situation.
  - The personal preferences and habits of the speaker and the degree of confidence and motivation.
- Some strategies to improve pronunciation are:
  - Listening to authentic and diverse sources of the target language and imitating the sounds and patterns of native speakers.
  - Practicing the pronunciation of individual sounds, words, phrases and sentences with the help of dictionaries, phonetic symbols, audio recordings and feedback tools.
  - Learning the rules and patterns of stress, intonation and rhythm and applying them to different types of speech and discourse.
  - Reading aloud texts of various genres and styles and paying attention to the pronunciation features and variations.
  - Recording and evaluating one's own speech and identifying the areas of improvement and correction.
  - Seeking feedback and guidance from experts, teachers and peers and working on the errors and difficulties.
