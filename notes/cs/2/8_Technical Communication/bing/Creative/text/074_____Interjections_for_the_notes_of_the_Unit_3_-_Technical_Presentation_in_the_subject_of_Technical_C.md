### Interjections

- Interjections are words or phrases that express emotions or feelings.
- Interjections are usually followed by an exclamation mark (!) or a comma (,).
- Interjections can be used to show surprise, joy, anger, disappointment, agreement, hesitation, etc.
- Interjections can be used at the beginning, middle, or end of a sentence.
- Interjections can be used to attract attention, to interrupt, to agree or disagree, to fill pauses, etc.
- Interjections can be classified into four types: primary, secondary, response, and filler.

#### Primary interjections
- Primary interjections are words that have no other function than to express emotions or feelings.
- Examples: Wow! Ouch! Yuck! Hooray! Ugh! Oops!

#### Secondary interjections
- Secondary interjections are words that have other functions in the language, but can also be used as interjections to express emotions or feelings.
- Examples: Great! Hello! Goodbye! Cheers! Congratulations!

#### Response interjections
- Response interjections are words or phrases that are used to respond to a question, a statement, or a situation.
- Examples: Yes! No! Maybe! Sure! Of course! Absolutely!

#### Filler interjections
- Filler interjections are words or phrases that are used to fill pauses, to show hesitation, to indicate thinking, or to avoid silence.
- Examples: Um... Er... Well... You know... I mean... Like...
