### Etiquette for the notes of the Unit 5 - Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics in the subject of Technical Communication

- Etiquette is the set of rules and conventions that govern the behavior and conduct of individuals or groups in a given social or professional context.
- Etiquette for the notes of the Unit 5 is the set of guidelines and principles that help the students to prepare, organize, and present their notes effectively and efficiently.
- Some of the etiquette for the notes of the Unit 5 are:

  - Use clear and concise language that is appropriate for the audience and the purpose of the notes.
  - Use headings, subheadings, bullet points, and numbering to structure the notes and highlight the main points and subpoints.
  - Use diagrams, charts, graphs, tables, and images to illustrate and support the information and data in the notes.
  - Use consistent and accurate formatting, style, and citation for the notes and the sources of information.
  - Use proper grammar, spelling, punctuation, and capitalization for the notes and avoid errors and ambiguities.
  - Use respectful and polite tone and language for the notes and avoid offensive, biased, or inappropriate expressions.
  - Use feedback and revision to improve the quality and clarity of the notes and correct any mistakes or gaps.
