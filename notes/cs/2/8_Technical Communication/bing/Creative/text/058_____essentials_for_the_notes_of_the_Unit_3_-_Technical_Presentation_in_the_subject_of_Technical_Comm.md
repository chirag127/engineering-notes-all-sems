### Essentials for the notes of the Unit 3 - Technical Presentation in the subject of Technical Communication

- A technical presentation is a form of oral communication that conveys technical information to a specific audience, such as engineers, scientists, managers, or customers.
- A technical presentation has three main objectives: to inform, to persuade, and to instruct.
- A technical presentation consists of three main parts: introduction, body, and conclusion.
- The introduction should capture the attention of the audience, establish the purpose and scope of the presentation, and preview the main points.
- The body should present the technical information in a clear, logical, and coherent manner, using appropriate visual aids, examples, and evidence.
- The conclusion should summarize the main points, restate the purpose and significance of the presentation, and provide a call to action or recommendation.
- A technical presentation should follow the principles of effective communication, such as clarity, conciseness, accuracy, relevance, and coherence.
- A technical presentation should also consider the needs and expectations of the audience, such as their background, interests, level of understanding, and attitude.
- A technical presentation should be prepared, practiced, and delivered with confidence, professionalism, and enthusiasm.
