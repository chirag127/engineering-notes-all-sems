### Methods for the notes of the Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication

- Technical communication is the method of producing and sharing ideas and information in organizations.
- Technical communication can have different purposes, such as defining, instructing, informing, or persuading.
- Technical communication can use different modes and methods, such as written communication, oral communication, video, infographic/images, etc..
- Technical communication can cover different domains, such as the soft and hard sciences, high technology, consumer electronics, business processes and practices, etc..
- Technical communication can create different products (deliverables) for different media and audiences, such as online help, user manuals, technical manuals, white papers, specifications, process and procedure manuals, industrial videos, reference cards, data sheets, journal articles, patents, training, business papers, and technical reports.
- Technical communication can involve different skills and competencies, such as researching, writing, editing, designing, testing, evaluating, and collaborating.
- Technical communication can follow different standards and guidelines, such as style guides, ethical codes, legal regulations, and accessibility requirements.
