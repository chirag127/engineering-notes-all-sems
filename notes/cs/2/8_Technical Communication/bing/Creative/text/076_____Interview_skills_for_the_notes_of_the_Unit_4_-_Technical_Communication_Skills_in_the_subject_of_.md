### Interview skills for the notes of the Unit 4 - Technical Communication Skills in the subject of Technical Communication

- Interview skills are the abilities and techniques that help you prepare for, conduct, and follow up on a job interview.
- Interview skills are important for technical communicators because they need to demonstrate their knowledge, skills, and abilities in various technical domains, as well as their communication and interpersonal skills.
- Some of the interview skills that technical communicators should have are:

  - **Research skills**: Before the interview, you should research the company, the role, and the interviewer. You should also review your resume, portfolio, and work samples, and prepare relevant examples and stories to showcase your achievements and skills.
  - **Oral communication skills**: During the interview, you should communicate clearly, concisely, and confidently. You should use appropriate language, tone, and body language, and avoid jargon, slang, and filler words. You should also listen actively, ask relevant questions, and show interest and enthusiasm.
  - **Problem-solving skills**: In a technical interview, you may be asked to solve technical problems, such as brain teasers, numerical reasoning, or coding challenges. You should approach these problems logically, explain your thought process, and demonstrate your technical skills and knowledge.
  - **Collaboration skills**: Technical communicators often work with different people and projects, such as subject matter experts, developers, designers, and users. You should show that you can work well with others, adapt to different situations, and handle feedback and criticism.
  - **Follow-up skills**: After the interview, you should send a thank-you note to the interviewer, expressing your appreciation and interest in the role. You should also follow up on the status of your application, and respond to any further requests or questions from the employer.
