### Tone for the notes of the Unit 5 - Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics in the subject of Technical Communication

- Tone is the attitude or emotion that the speaker conveys through their voice and words.
- Tone can affect the meaning and impact of the message, as well as the listener's perception of the speaker and the topic.
- Tone can be influenced by various factors, such as the speaker's mood, intention, purpose, audience, context, and culture.
- Tone can be expressed through various vocal elements, such as pitch, volume, rate, pause, stress, and intonation.
- Tone can also be conveyed through non-verbal cues, such as facial expressions, gestures, posture, and eye contact.
- Tone can be classified into different types, such as formal, informal, friendly, hostile, polite, rude, confident, hesitant, enthusiastic, bored, etc.
- Tone can be adjusted according to the situation and the listener's needs and expectations, by using appropriate words, phrases, and vocal elements.
- Tone can be evaluated by listening to the speaker's voice and words, and observing their non-verbal cues, as well as by considering the feedback from the listener and the outcome of the communication.
