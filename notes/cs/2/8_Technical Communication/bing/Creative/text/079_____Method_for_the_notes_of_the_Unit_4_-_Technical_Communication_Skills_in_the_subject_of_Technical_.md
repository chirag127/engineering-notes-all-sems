### Method for the notes of the Unit 4 - Technical Communication Skills in the subject of Technical Communication

- Unit 4 covers the following topics: oral communication, written communication, visual communication, and non-verbal communication.
- Oral communication refers to the use of speech to convey information, ideas, opinions, or emotions. It includes aspects such as pronunciation, tone, pitch, volume, accent, and fluency.
- Written communication refers to the use of text to convey information, ideas, opinions, or emotions. It includes aspects such as grammar, spelling, punctuation, vocabulary, style, and format.
- Visual communication refers to the use of images, diagrams, charts, graphs, maps, or symbols to convey information, ideas, opinions, or emotions. It includes aspects such as clarity, accuracy, relevance, and aesthetics.
- Non-verbal communication refers to the use of body language, facial expressions, gestures, eye contact, or posture to convey information, ideas, opinions, or emotions. It includes aspects such as consistency, appropriateness, and cultural sensitivity.
- To make effective notes for Unit 4, one should follow these steps:
  - Identify the main points and sub-points of each topic and write them down in a logical order.
  - Use bullet points, numbers, headings, or keywords to organize the notes and make them easy to read and remember.
  - Use abbreviations, symbols, or diagrams to save time and space and to highlight important information.
  - Use examples, illustrations, or comparisons to explain or clarify complex concepts or terms.
  - Use colors, fonts, or highlighting to emphasize key points or to differentiate between different types of information.
  - Review the notes regularly and revise them if necessary to ensure accuracy and completeness.
