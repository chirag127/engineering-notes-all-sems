### Forms for the notes of the Unit 3 - Technical Presentation in the subject of Technical Communication

- Notes are brief records of information that help the speaker to deliver a presentation effectively.
- Notes can be written in different forms, such as outlines, cue cards, mind maps, or slides.
- Outlines are linear structures that show the main points and subpoints of a presentation in a hierarchical order. They can be written in full sentences or keywords.
- Cue cards are small cards that contain key words or phrases that remind the speaker of the main ideas or transitions. They can be numbered or color-coded to indicate the sequence of the presentation.
- Mind maps are graphical representations of the main topic and its related subtopics, using symbols, colors, images, or keywords. They can help the speaker to visualize the connections and associations between different aspects of the presentation.
- Slides are visual aids that display the main points, data, diagrams, or images that support the presentation. They can be created using software tools such as PowerPoint, Google Slides, or Prezi. They can also include animations, transitions, or sound effects to enhance the presentation.
