### Public Speaking for the notes of the Unit 3 - Technical Presentation in the subject of Technical Communication

- A technical presentation is a type of public speaking that aims to inform, educate or persuade an audience about a specific topic, usually related to science, engineering, technology or business.
- Technical presentations require careful preparation, clear structure, engaging delivery and effective use of visual aids to communicate complex or technical information in a clear and concise way.
- Some tips for delivering a successful technical presentation are:

  - Know your subject matter. You should be confident and knowledgeable about the topic you are presenting, and be able to answer questions or clarify doubts from the audience. You should also avoid reading from your slides or notes, and instead use them as cues or reminders.
  - Know your audience. You should tailor your presentation to the level of understanding, interest and expectations of your audience. You should also use appropriate language, terminology and examples that suit your audience's background and needs.
  - Configure your IDE (Integrated Development Environment). If you are presenting a software or coding project, you should make sure that your IDE is set up properly and that your code runs smoothly and error-free. You should also test your code beforehand and have a backup plan in case of technical issues.
  - Minimize distractions. You should eliminate any potential sources of distraction or interruption, such as notifications, sounds, pop-ups or background noise. You should also check your internet connection, microphone, camera and other equipment before your presentation.
  - Get the right equipment. You should use a high-quality microphone, camera, headset and speaker to ensure that your voice and image are clear and audible. You should also use a reliable and compatible software or platform to deliver your presentation, such as Zoom, PowerPoint, Google Slides or Prezi.
  - Rehearse in advance. You should practice your presentation several times, preferably in front of a mirror, a friend, a colleague or a mentor. You should also time yourself and adjust your content and pace accordingly. You should also seek feedback and improve your presentation based on the suggestions.
  - Finally, enjoy the experience. You should try to relax and have fun while presenting your technical project. You should also show enthusiasm, passion and curiosity about your topic, and engage with your audience through eye contact, gestures, questions and stories.

- By following these tips, you can deliver a technical presentation that is informative, persuasive and memorable.
