### Style for the notes of the Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication

- Style is the way a writer uses language to communicate ideas to the readers.
- Style can be influenced by the purpose, audience, tone, and format of the technical communication.
- Style can be evaluated by four criteria: clarity, conciseness, coherence, and correctness.
- Clarity is the quality of being clear, precise, and easy to understand. Clarity can be achieved by using simple words, active voice, concrete nouns, and specific details.
- Conciseness is the quality of being brief, direct, and to the point. Conciseness can be achieved by eliminating unnecessary words, avoiding repetition, and using parallel structure.
- Coherence is the quality of being logical, consistent, and well-organized. Coherence can be achieved by using transitions, headings, lists, and other devices to connect ideas and guide the readers.
- Correctness is the quality of being accurate, appropriate, and free of errors. Correctness can be achieved by following the rules of grammar, spelling, punctuation, and citation, and by adapting to the conventions of the field and the document type.
