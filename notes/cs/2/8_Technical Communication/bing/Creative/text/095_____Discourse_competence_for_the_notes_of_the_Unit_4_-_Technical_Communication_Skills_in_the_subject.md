### Discourse competence for the notes of the Unit 4 - Technical Communication Skills in the subject of Technical Communication

- Discourse competence is the ability to understand and express oneself in a given language, combining grammatical forms and meaning to achieve a linked text (oral or written), in different communication situations .
- Discourse competence involves four aspects: cohesion, coherence, structure and genre.
  - Cohesion refers to the use of linguistic devices, such as pronouns, conjunctions, and reference words, to connect sentences and paragraphs in a text.
  - Coherence refers to the logical and meaningful relationship between sentences and paragraphs in a text, based on the topic, the purpose, and the audience.
  - Structure refers to the organization and layout of a text, such as the introduction, the body, and the conclusion, as well as the headings, subheadings, and bullet points.
  - Genre refers to the type and style of a text, such as a report, a letter, a memo, or a presentation, which follows certain conventions and expectations of the communication situation.
- Discourse competence is essential for effective technical communication, as it helps to create clear, concise, and coherent texts that convey information and persuade the audience .
- Discourse competence can be developed by practicing different types of texts, analyzing the audience and the purpose, using appropriate language and tone, and following the guidelines and standards of the discourse community .
