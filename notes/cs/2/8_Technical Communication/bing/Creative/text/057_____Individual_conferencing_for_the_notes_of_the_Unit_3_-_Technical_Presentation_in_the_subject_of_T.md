### Individual conferencing for the notes of the Unit 3 - Technical Presentation in the subject of Technical Communication

- Individual conferencing is a process of meeting with the instructor one-on-one to discuss the progress and feedback of the technical presentation assignment.
- The purpose of individual conferencing is to help the students improve their presentation skills, content, organization, delivery, and visual aids.
- The benefits of individual conferencing are:
  - It provides personalized and specific guidance and suggestions for each student.
  - It allows the students to ask questions and clarify doubts about the assignment and the evaluation criteria.
  - It helps the students to identify their strengths and weaknesses and set goals for improvement.
  - It builds rapport and trust between the instructor and the students.
  - It motivates the students to work on their presentation and prepare well for the final delivery.
- The steps of individual conferencing are:
  - Before the conference, the students should prepare a draft of their presentation outline, slides, and notes, and submit them to the instructor for review.
  - During the conference, the instructor and the student should discuss the following aspects of the presentation:
    - The topic, purpose, audience, and context of the presentation.
    - The main points, supporting details, and transitions of the presentation.
    - The structure, organization, and coherence of the presentation.
    - The language, tone, style, and clarity of the presentation.
    - The delivery, voice, eye contact, gestures, and posture of the presentation.
    - The visual aids, design, layout, color, font, and images of the presentation.
  - After the conference, the students should revise their presentation based on the feedback and suggestions from the instructor, and practice their presentation before the final delivery.
