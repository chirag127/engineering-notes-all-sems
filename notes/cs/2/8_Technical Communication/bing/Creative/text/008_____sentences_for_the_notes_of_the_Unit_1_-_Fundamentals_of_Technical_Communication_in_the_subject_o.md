### Sentences for the notes of the Unit 1 - Fundamentals of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication

- Technical communication is the method of producing and sharing ideas and information in organizations.
- Technical communication products include letters, emails, instructions, status reports, project proposals, websites, blogs, product descriptions, user manuals, white papers, journal articles, and government documents .
- The goal of technical communication is to create easily comprehensible information for a target specific audience.
- Technical communication requires clarity, accuracy, conciseness, coherence, and appropriateness of language, tone, and format.
- Technical communication skills are essential for professionals in various fields, such as engineering, science, technology, business, and health.
- Technical communication skills involve understanding the target audience, gathering technical knowledge, conducting background research, adopting communication strategies, and using writing tools .
- Technical communication skills can help professionals communicate effectively, persuade and inform others, solve problems, collaborate with teams, and advance their careers .
