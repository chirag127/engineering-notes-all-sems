### Critical thinking

- Critical thinking is the **objective analysis and evaluation** of an issue in order to form a **judgment** .
- Critical thinking is a **widely accepted educational goal** that involves **careful thinking directed to a goal**.
- Critical thinking is a **skill** that can be **developed** and **improved** through **practice** and **feedback**.
- Critical thinking is **important** for **technical communication** because it helps to:
  - **Identify** and **clarify** the **purpose**, **audience**, and **context** of the communication.
  - **Gather**, **organize**, and **evaluate** relevant and reliable **information** and **evidence**.
  - **Apply** appropriate **criteria** and **standards** to **assess** the **quality** and **validity** of the communication.
  - **Create** and **present** clear, concise, and coherent **arguments** and **explanations** that **support** the communication **goal**.
  - **Anticipate** and **address** potential **objections**, **questions**, and **alternatives** from the audience.
  - **Reflect** on and **revise** the communication based on **feedback** and **self-assessment**.
