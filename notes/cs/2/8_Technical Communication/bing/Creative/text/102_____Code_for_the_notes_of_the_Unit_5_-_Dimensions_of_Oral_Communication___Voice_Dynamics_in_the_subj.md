### Code for the notes of the Unit 5 - Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics in the subject of Technical Communication

- Dimensions of oral communication are the aspects that affect the effectiveness and quality of spoken messages, such as the purpose, audience, context, content, structure, style, tone, and delivery of the speech .
- Voice dynamics are the variations and modulations of the voice that convey meaning, emotion, and attitude, such as pitch, volume, rate, pause, stress, and intonation .
- Some of the topics covered in this unit are  :
  - The importance and benefits of oral communication in technical and professional settings, such as building rapport, conveying complex ideas, persuading and influencing others, and receiving immediate feedback.
  - The principles and strategies of effective oral communication, such as clarity, conciseness, coherence, confidence, courtesy, and credibility.
  - The types and formats of oral communication, such as formal and informal, one-way and two-way, interpersonal and group, and verbal and non-verbal.
  - The steps and guidelines for preparing and delivering oral presentations, such as analyzing the purpose, audience, and context, researching and organizing the content, designing and using visual aids, rehearsing and practicing the speech, and managing the time, space, and audience interaction.
  - The techniques and skills for improving voice dynamics, such as breathing, articulation, pronunciation, projection, modulation, and expression.
  - The challenges and barriers of oral communication, such as anxiety, distraction, noise, misunderstanding, and cultural differences, and the ways to overcome them, such as relaxation, preparation, adaptation, and feedback.
