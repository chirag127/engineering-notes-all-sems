### Competence in Oral Communication

- Competence in oral communication is the ability to communicate effectively and appropriately with others using spoken language.
- Competence in oral communication involves four main components: knowledge, skills, motivation, and context.
- Knowledge refers to the understanding of the rules, norms, and conventions of oral communication, such as grammar, vocabulary, pronunciation, nonverbal cues, and cultural expectations.
- Skills refer to the ability to apply the knowledge of oral communication in various situations, such as listening, speaking, questioning, clarifying, persuading, and adapting.
- Motivation refers to the willingness and desire to communicate orally, as well as the confidence and attitude towards oral communication.
- Context refers to the factors that influence oral communication, such as the purpose, audience, setting, channel, and feedback.
- Competence in oral communication can be developed through practice, feedback, reflection, and self-monitoring.
- Competence in oral communication can benefit individuals in various aspects of life, such as academic, professional, personal, and social.
- Competence in oral communication can help individuals to achieve their goals, express their ideas, build relationships, and influence others.
