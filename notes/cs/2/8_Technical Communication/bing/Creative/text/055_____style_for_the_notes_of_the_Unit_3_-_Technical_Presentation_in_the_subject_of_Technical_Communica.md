### Style for the notes of the Unit 3 - Technical Presentation in the subject of Technical Communication

- Style is the way a speaker or writer chooses to express ideas, using language, tone, and voice.
- Style can affect the clarity, persuasiveness, and credibility of a technical presentation.
- Style can also influence the audience's perception of the speaker or writer, and their attitude towards the topic.
- Some general guidelines for effective style in technical presentations are:

  - Use clear and concise language, avoiding jargon, slang, and unnecessary words.
  - Use appropriate level of formality, depending on the purpose, context, and audience of the presentation.
  - Use active voice, rather than passive voice, to emphasize the agent and action of a sentence.
  - Use parallel structure, to create balance and coherence among similar elements in a sentence or a list.
  - Use transitions, to connect and organize the main points and subpoints of the presentation.
  - Use examples, analogies, and visual aids, to illustrate and support the key ideas and concepts of the presentation.
  - Use positive and respectful tone, to establish rapport and trust with the audience, and to avoid offending or alienating them.
  - Use personal pronouns, such as "I", "we", and "you", to create a sense of involvement and engagement with the audience.
  - Use rhetorical devices, such as repetition, contrast, and questions, to emphasize and reinforce the main message and purpose of the presentation.
  - Use humor, anecdotes, and stories, to capture the audience's attention and interest, and to make the presentation more memorable and enjoyable.
