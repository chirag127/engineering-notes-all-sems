# Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication

## Introduction
- Technical communication is the process of conveying information in a clear, concise, and accurate manner to a specific audience for a specific purpose.
- Technical communication can take various forms, such as reports, proposals, manuals, instructions, presentations, memos, emails, etc.
- Technical communication can be classified into two categories: internal and external.
  - Internal communication is the exchange of information within an organization, such as between employees, managers, departments, etc.
  - External communication is the exchange of information with people outside the organization, such as customers, clients, suppliers, regulators, etc.

## Objectives
- To understand the characteristics and purposes of different forms of technical communication.
- To identify the appropriate form of technical communication for a given situation and audience.
- To apply the principles of effective technical communication to create various forms of technical documents.

## Forms of Technical Communication
- The following are some of the common forms of technical communication, along with their definitions, characteristics, purposes, and examples:

### Reports
- A report is a formal document that provides information, analysis, or recommendations on a specific topic or problem.
- A report usually follows a standard structure, such as title page, abstract, introduction, body, conclusion, recommendations, references, and appendices.
- A report can have different types, such as feasibility report, progress report, technical report, research report, etc.
- A report can have different purposes, such as to inform, persuade, evaluate, propose, etc.
- An example of a report is a feasibility report on a new project, which provides information on the background, objectives, methods, results, and recommendations of the project.

### Proposals
- A proposal is a document that offers a solution, a plan, or a course of action to a specific problem or need.
- A proposal usually follows a similar structure as a report, but with more emphasis on the benefits, costs, and feasibility of the proposed solution.
- A proposal can have different types, such as solicited proposal, unsolicited proposal, internal proposal, external proposal, etc.
- A proposal can have different purposes, such as to persuade, request, suggest, etc.
- An example of a proposal is a proposal for a new product, which provides information on the problem, the solution, the benefits, the costs, and the implementation plan of the product.

### Manuals
- A manual is a document that provides instructions, guidelines, or procedures on how to use, operate, maintain, or repair a product, system, or service.
- A manual usually follows a logical sequence of steps, with clear headings, subheadings, lists, diagrams, tables, etc.
- A manual can have different types, such as user manual, installation manual, maintenance manual, troubleshooting manual, etc.
- A manual can have different purposes, such as to instruct, guide, explain, etc.
- An example of a manual is a user manual for a software application, which provides information on how to install, run, configure, and use the application.

### Instructions
- Instructions are a set of directions or commands that tell someone how to do something or achieve a specific goal.
- Instructions usually follow a chronological order of steps, with imperative verbs, numbered lists, bullet points, etc.
- Instructions can have different types, such as procedural instructions, process instructions, directional instructions, etc.
- Instructions can have different purposes, such as to teach, demonstrate, direct, etc.
- An example of instructions is a recipe for a dish, which provides information on the ingredients, equipment, steps, and tips for making the dish.

### Presentations
- A presentation is a form of oral communication that delivers information, ideas, or arguments to an audience, usually with the aid of visual aids, such as slides, charts, graphs, etc.
- A presentation usually follows a clear structure, such as introduction, body, conclusion, and questions and answers.
- A presentation can have different types, such as informative presentation, persuasive presentation, technical presentation, etc.
- A presentation can have different purposes, such as to inform, persuade, explain, etc.
- An example of a presentation is a sales pitch for a new product, which provides information on the problem, the solution, the benefits, and the call to action of the product.
