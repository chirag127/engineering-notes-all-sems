# C.V./Resume writing

A C.V. (curriculum vitae) or a resume is a document that summarizes your skills, education, work experience and achievements. It is used to apply for jobs and showcase your suitability for the role. A C.V. or a resume can also help you to network with potential employers and create a positive impression.

Here are some tips on how to write an effective C.V. or resume:

- **Tailor your C.V. or resume to every job you apply for.** Research the job description and the company and use keywords and power words that match their requirements. Highlight your relevant skills and achievements and show how you can add value to the role.
- **Show your passion.** Use a professional summary or an objective statement to introduce yourself and explain your motivation for applying. Demonstrate your enthusiasm and interest in the field and the employer. Avoid generic statements and clichés and use specific examples to back up your claims.
- **Be specific and show your impact.** Use bullet points and action verbs to describe your work experience and achievements. Provide proof and use hard numbers to quantify your results and accomplishments. For example, instead of saying "managed a team of 10", say "led a team of 10 to increase sales by 25% in six months".
- **Be confident.** Use positive language and avoid words that undermine your credibility, such as "maybe", "hopefully", "somewhat" or "a little". Emphasize your strengths and skills and showcase your achievements and awards. Avoid lying or exaggerating on your C.V. or resume, as this can damage your reputation and trustworthiness.
- **Proofread.** Check your C.V. or resume for spelling, grammar, punctuation and formatting errors. Use a consistent and clear font, such as Calibri, Georgia, Open Sans or Cambria, and a font size of 10 to 12 points. Use headings, subheadings, bold and italic text to organize your information and make it easy to read. Use white space and margins to create a balanced and professional layout. Ask someone else to review your C.V. or resume and give you feedback.
- **Use a professional email address.** Your email address should be simple and appropriate, preferably consisting of your name and a common domain, such as Gmail or Outlook. Avoid using email addresses that are too casual, funny, outdated or personal, such as "cutiepie123@gmail.com" or "ilovecats@hotmail.com".
- **Keep it concise and relevant.** Your C.V. or resume should be no longer than two pages, unless you are applying for an academic or research position, in which case you may need a longer C.V. Include only the information that is relevant to the job and the employer, and avoid adding unnecessary details or personal information, such as your age, marital status, hobbies or political views.
