# Techniques for the notes of the Unit 3 - Technical Presentation in the subject of Technical Communication

- A technical presentation is a form of oral communication that conveys technical information to a specific audience, such as engineers, managers, customers, or the general public.
- A technical presentation requires careful planning, preparation, and practice to achieve its purpose and objectives.
- Some of the techniques for the notes of the technical presentation are:

  - **Identify the purpose and audience of the presentation.** The purpose is the main goal or message that the presenter wants to convey to the audience. The audience is the group of people who will listen to the presentation and who may have different levels of technical knowledge, interest, and expectations. The presenter should tailor the content, language, and style of the presentation to suit the purpose and audience.
  - **Organize the content of the presentation.** The content is the information that the presenter wants to share with the audience. The presenter should organize the content into a clear and logical structure, such as introduction, body, and conclusion. The introduction should capture the attention of the audience, provide the background and context of the topic, and state the main points or thesis of the presentation. The body should develop the main points or arguments with supporting evidence, examples, and explanations. The conclusion should summarize the main points or findings, restate the thesis, and provide a call to action or recommendation.
  - **Prepare the notes of the presentation.** The notes are the written or visual aids that the presenter uses to guide the delivery of the presentation. The notes should be concise, clear, and consistent with the content and structure of the presentation. The notes should include the key words, phrases, or sentences that the presenter wants to emphasize or remember, as well as the transitions, cues, or signals that the presenter wants to use to connect the different parts of the presentation. The notes should also include the references, citations, or sources of the information that the presenter uses in the presentation.
  - **Use the notes effectively during the presentation.** The notes are not meant to be read word for word, but to serve as a reminder or a prompt for the presenter. The presenter should use the notes sparingly and discreetly, and avoid relying on them too much. The presenter should maintain eye contact with the audience, speak clearly and confidently, and use appropriate gestures, expressions, and voice modulation. The presenter should also monitor the time, pace, and feedback of the presentation, and adjust the notes accordingly.
