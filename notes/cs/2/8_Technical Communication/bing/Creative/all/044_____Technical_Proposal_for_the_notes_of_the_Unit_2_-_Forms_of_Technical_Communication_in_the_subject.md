# Technical Proposal for the notes of the Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication

- A technical proposal is a document that outlines a project plan, its objectives, methods, expected outcomes, and budget.
- A technical proposal is usually written for a specific audience, such as a client, a funding agency, or a supervisor, who will evaluate the feasibility and worthiness of the proposed project.
- A technical proposal should follow a clear and logical structure, such as:

  - Introduction: This section provides the background and context of the project, the problem statement, the purpose and scope of the proposal, and the main research questions or hypotheses.
  - Literature Review: This section reviews the existing literature and research related to the project topic, and identifies the gaps or limitations that the project aims to address.
  - Methodology: This section describes the methods and procedures that will be used to conduct the project, such as the research design, data collection and analysis, ethical considerations, and limitations.
  - Results and Discussion: This section presents and interprets the findings of the project, and discusses their implications and significance for the research questions or hypotheses, the literature, and the practice.
  - Conclusion and Recommendations: This section summarizes the main points and contributions of the project, and provides recommendations for future research or action.
  - References: This section lists the sources that were cited in the proposal, following a consistent citation style.
  - Appendices: This section includes any additional or supplementary information that supports the proposal, such as tables, figures, charts, graphs, surveys, questionnaires, etc.

- A technical proposal should use clear, concise, and accurate language, and avoid jargon, slang, or vague terms.
- A technical proposal should also use appropriate visual aids, such as graphs, charts, tables, or diagrams, to illustrate and enhance the information in the text.
- A technical proposal should be proofread and edited for grammar, spelling, punctuation, and formatting errors, and checked for plagiarism and citation accuracy.
