# Analysis & Findings

- Analysis and findings are the results of a technical investigation or research that are presented in a clear and logical manner.
- Analysis and findings can be presented in different forms of technical communication, such as reports, articles, presentations, posters, etc.
- Analysis and findings should be based on reliable data and evidence, and should address the purpose, scope, and objectives of the technical investigation or research.
- Analysis and findings should also include the following elements:

  - A summary or overview of the main points and conclusions of the analysis and findings.
  - A description of the methods, procedures, tools, and techniques used to collect, process, and analyze the data and evidence.
  - A discussion of the results and outcomes of the analysis and findings, highlighting the key findings, implications, limitations, and recommendations.
  - A comparison and contrast of the analysis and findings with the existing literature, theories, models, or frameworks in the relevant field or discipline.
  - A reflection and evaluation of the strengths, weaknesses, challenges, and opportunities of the analysis and findings, as well as the implications for future research or practice.
  - A list of references and sources that support the analysis and findings, following the appropriate citation and formatting style.
  - A list of appendices, tables, figures, graphs, charts, or other visual aids that supplement the analysis and findings, if necessary.

- Analysis and findings should be written in a clear, concise, coherent, and consistent manner, using appropriate language, tone, style, and format for the intended audience, purpose, and context of the technical communication.
- Analysis and findings should also be edited and proofread for accuracy, completeness, correctness, and clarity, and should follow the ethical principles and standards of the technical communication field or discipline.
