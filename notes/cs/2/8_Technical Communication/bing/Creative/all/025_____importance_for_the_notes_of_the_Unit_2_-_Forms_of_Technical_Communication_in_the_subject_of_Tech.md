# Importance of Notes for Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication

- Notes are a way of recording and summarizing information from various sources, such as books, articles, lectures, or websites.
- Notes help technical communicators to:
  - Organize and outline their ideas before writing the initial draft
  - Distinguish between facts and opinions, and avoid falsifying or misrepresenting information
  - Document their sources and avoid plagiarism
  - Review and revise their work for clarity, concision, and accessibility
- Notes can take different forms, such as quotations, paraphrases, and summaries, depending on the purpose and context of the communication
- Notes should follow the principles of technical communication, such as:
  - Responding to a specific rhetorical situation and audience needs
  - Using appropriate language, tone, and style
  - Using visual aids, such as tables, graphs, or diagrams, to enhance the message
  - Following the conventions of the genre and format
