# Individual conferencing for the notes of the Unit 3 - Technical Presentation in the subject of Technical Communication

- Individual conferencing is a process of meeting one-on-one with a teacher or a peer to discuss and improve a technical presentation.
- The purpose of individual conferencing is to provide feedback, guidance, and support to the presenter, as well as to identify and address any issues or challenges that may arise during the preparation or delivery of the presentation.
- The benefits of individual conferencing include:
  - Enhancing the quality and effectiveness of the presentation by incorporating constructive criticism and suggestions from the conference partner.
  - Developing the presenter's communication skills and confidence by practicing and rehearsing the presentation in a safe and supportive environment.
  - Strengthening the relationship and rapport between the presenter and the conference partner by establishing trust and mutual respect.
  - Encouraging the presenter to reflect on their own strengths and weaknesses and to set realistic and achievable goals for improvement.
- The steps of individual conferencing are:
  - Before the conference, the presenter should prepare a draft or an outline of their presentation, as well as a list of questions or concerns that they want to address during the conference.
  - During the conference, the presenter should share their presentation with the conference partner and solicit feedback on various aspects of the presentation, such as the content, structure, style, delivery, and visual aids. The conference partner should listen attentively, ask clarifying questions, offer constructive criticism and suggestions, and praise the presenter's efforts and achievements.
  - After the conference, the presenter should review the feedback and suggestions from the conference partner and revise their presentation accordingly. The presenter should also thank the conference partner for their time and input and follow up with them if they have any further questions or concerns.
