# Clarity for the notes of the Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication

- Technical communication is the method of producing and sharing ideas and information in organizations.
- Technical communication can have different purposes, such as defining, instructing, informing, or persuading.
- Technical communication can deal with technical or specialized topics, such as computer applications, medical procedures, or environmental regulations .
- Technical communication can use technology to create or distribute the communication, such as web pages, help files, or social media sites .
- Technical communication can provide instructions about how to do something, regardless of how technical the task is .
- Technical communication can take different forms, such as business letters, memos, reports, notices, proposals, websites, and blogs .
- Technical communication can target different audiences, such as customers, employees, managers, experts, or the general public .
- Technical communication should follow some general principles, such as clarity, accuracy, conciseness, coherence, and ethics .
