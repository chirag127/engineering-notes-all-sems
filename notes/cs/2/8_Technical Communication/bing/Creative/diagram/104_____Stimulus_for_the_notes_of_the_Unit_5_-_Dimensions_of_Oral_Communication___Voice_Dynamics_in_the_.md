### Stimulus for the notes of the Unit 5 - Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics in the subject of Technical Communication

- A stimulus is an idea or a concept that is activated by the sender in the communication process .
- A stimulus can be based on personal experiences, observations, opinions, feelings, or facts.
- A stimulus can be expressed in various forms, such as words, gestures, sounds, images, or symbols.
- A stimulus can be influenced by the sender's background, context, purpose, and audience.
- A stimulus can be attention-getting in a productive or distracting way.
- A stimulus can trigger different responses from the receiver, depending on their perception, interpretation, and feedback.
- A stimulus can be enhanced or hindered by the channel, medium, and noise in the communication process.
- A stimulus can be used to initiate, maintain, or end a conversation.
- A stimulus can be used to inform, persuade, entertain, or instruct the receiver.
- A stimulus can be used to create rapport, empathy, or trust with the receiver.
- A stimulus can be used to express emotions, attitudes, or opinions.
- A stimulus can be used to clarify, confirm, or correct misunderstandings.
- A stimulus can be used to ask questions, answer questions, or request feedback.
- A stimulus can be used to motivate, inspire, or challenge the receiver.
- A stimulus can be used to negotiate, resolve, or avoid conflicts.
