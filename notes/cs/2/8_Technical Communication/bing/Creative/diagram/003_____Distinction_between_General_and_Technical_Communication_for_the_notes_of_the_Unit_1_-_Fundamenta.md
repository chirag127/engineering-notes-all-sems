### Distinction between General and Technical Communication

- General communication is **informal** in style and approach, while technical communication is **mostly formal** in style and approach  .
- General communication **does not follow** a set pattern, while technical communication **follows** a set pattern of organization, such as introduction, body, and conclusion.
- General communication is **mostly oral**, while technical communication is **both oral and written**.
- General communication is **not always** for a specific audience, while technical communication is **always** for a specific audience with a specific purpose .
- General communication **does not use** technical terms or graphics, while technical communication **uses** technical terms or graphics to convey complex or specialized information .
