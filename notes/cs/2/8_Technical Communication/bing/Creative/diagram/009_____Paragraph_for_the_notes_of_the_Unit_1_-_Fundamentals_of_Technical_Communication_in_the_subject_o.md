### Paragraph for the notes of the Unit 1 - Fundamentals of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication

- A paragraph is a group of sentences that develops one main idea.
- A paragraph has three parts: a topic sentence, supporting sentences, and a concluding sentence.
- A topic sentence introduces the main idea of the paragraph and usually comes at the beginning.
- Supporting sentences provide details, examples, facts, or evidence that support the topic sentence.
- A concluding sentence summarizes the main idea of the paragraph and often restates the topic sentence in different words.
- A paragraph should be coherent, cohesive, concise, and clear.
- Coherence means that the sentences are logically connected and easy to follow.
- Cohesion means that the sentences are linked by transitional words or phrases, pronouns, or repetition of key words.
- Conciseness means that the sentences are free of unnecessary words or redundancy.
- Clarity means that the sentences are precise, accurate, and appropriate for the audience and purpose.
- A paragraph should also have unity, which means that all the sentences relate to the same main idea and do not introduce irrelevant or contradictory information.
