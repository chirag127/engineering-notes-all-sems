### Quizzes for the notes of the Unit 3 - Technical Presentation in the subject of Technical Communication

1. What are the three main types of technical presentations according to the notes?
    - A) Informative, persuasive, and instructional
    - B) Informal, formal, and interactive
    - C) Oral, written, and visual
    - D) Technical, non-technical, and mixed
    - Answer: A

2. What are the four steps of the presentation process according to the notes?
    - A) Planning, preparing, practicing, and presenting
    - B) Analyzing, designing, developing, and delivering
    - C) Researching, organizing, rehearsing, and performing
    - D) All of the above
    - Answer: D

3. What are some of the factors to consider when analyzing the audience for a technical presentation according to the notes?
    - A) Their background, expectations, and needs
    - B) Their size, location, and diversity
    - C) Their interest, attitude, and knowledge
    - D) All of the above
    - Answer: D

4. What are some of the elements to include in the introduction of a technical presentation according to the notes?
    - A) A hook, a thesis statement, and a preview of the main points
    - B) A greeting, a title, and a summary of the main points
    - C) A question, a problem statement, and a solution
    - D) A joke, a story, and a call to action
    - Answer: A

5. What are some of the guidelines for designing effective slides for a technical presentation according to the notes?
    - A) Use a consistent and clear layout, font, and color scheme
    - B) Use bullet points, images, and graphs to support the verbal message
    - C) Use animations, transitions, and sound effects to enhance the visual appeal
    - D) A and B
    - Answer: D
