### Concreteness

- Concreteness is an aspect of communication that means being **specific, definite, and vivid** rather than vague and general.
- A concrete communication uses **specific facts and figures** and avoids ambiguity and confusion .
- Concreteness is one of the six Cs of effective communication, along with clarity, conciseness, correctness, coherence, and courtesy  .
- Concreteness helps the audience to **understand and interpret** the message accurately and in the way the sender intended .
- Concreteness can be achieved by using **concrete language**, which refers to tangible and observable things, such as names, dates, numbers, examples, and evidence.
- Concreteness can also be enhanced by using **visual aids**, such as graphs, charts, tables, diagrams, and images, to illustrate the message and make it more appealing and comprehensible .
- Concreteness is especially important in technical communication, as it helps to convey complex and technical information in a clear and precise way .
