- Technical communication is the process of conveying information and instructions to a specific audience for a specific purpose, such as solving a problem, completing a task, or making a decision.
- Technical communication skills are essential for engineers, scientists, technicians, and other professionals who need to communicate effectively with their colleagues, clients, customers, and users.
- Technical communication skills include the ability to:
  - Analyze the purpose, audience, and context of the communication situation.
  - Plan, organize, and structure the content and format of the document or presentation.
  - Use clear, concise, accurate, and appropriate language and visuals.
  - Follow the conventions and standards of the genre, discipline, and organization.
  - Revise, edit, and proofread the document or presentation for clarity, correctness, and completeness.
  - Evaluate the effectiveness and usability of the document or presentation and seek feedback for improvement.
- Technical communication skills can be improved by:
  - Reading and analyzing examples of good technical communication in various fields and genres.
  - Practicing writing and presenting different types of technical documents and presentations for different audiences and purposes.
  - Seeking feedback from peers, instructors, and experts on the strengths and weaknesses of your technical communication.
  - Using online tools and resources to check grammar, spelling, style, and readability of your technical communication.
  - Reviewing and applying the principles and guidelines of technical communication.
