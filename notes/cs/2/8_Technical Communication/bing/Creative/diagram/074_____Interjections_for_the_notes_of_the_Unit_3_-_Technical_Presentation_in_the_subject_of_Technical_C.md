### Interjections

- Interjections are words or phrases that express emotions or feelings.
- Interjections are usually followed by an exclamation mark (!) or a comma (,).
- Interjections can be used to show surprise, joy, anger, disappointment, agreement, etc.
- Interjections can be placed at the beginning, middle, or end of a sentence.
- Interjections can be used to capture the attention of the audience, to emphasize a point, or to add some humor or personality to a presentation.
- Some examples of interjections are:

  - Wow! That's amazing!
  - Ouch! That must have hurt!
  - Well, I don't agree with that.
  - Oops! I made a mistake.
  - Hooray! We won the game!
  - Ugh! I hate this traffic!
  - Hey, listen to me!
  - Oh, I see what you mean.
  - Yikes! That's scary!
  - Phew! That was close!
