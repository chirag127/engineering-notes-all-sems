### Competence for the notes of the Unit 5 - Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics in the subject of Technical Communication

- Competence in oral communication is the ability to clearly transmit ideas and information orally in a way that is appropriate to the topic, purpose, and audience.
- Oral communication competence includes the following dimensions    :
  - Linguistic competence: the knowledge and use of the linguistic components of a language, such as vocabulary, grammar, pronunciation, and discourse.
  - Pragmatic competence: the ability to use language effectively and appropriately in different situations and contexts, such as formal and informal, academic and professional, and social and cultural.
  - Strategic competence: the ability to use various verbal and nonverbal strategies to overcome communication difficulties, such as clarifying, paraphrasing, repeating, and gesturing.
  - Interactional competence: the ability to initiate, maintain, and end a conversation, as well as to respond to feedback, take turns, and cooperate with others.
  - Critical competence: the ability to analyze, evaluate, and synthesize information from various sources, as well as to express one's own opinions, arguments, and perspectives.
  - Intercultural competence: the ability to communicate respectfully and effectively with people from diverse backgrounds, cultures, and perspectives, as well as to recognize and avoid stereotypes, biases, and prejudices.
- Oral communication competence can be developed through various activities, such as  :
  - Listening to and watching different types of oral texts, such as speeches, presentations, interviews, podcasts, and videos.
  - Practicing and performing different types of oral tasks, such as dialogues, debates, discussions, role-plays, and simulations.
  - Receiving and providing constructive feedback on one's own and others' oral performance, such as content, organization, delivery, and language.
  - Reflecting on and improving one's own oral communication skills, such as strengths, weaknesses, goals, and strategies.
- Oral communication competence is important for various purposes, such as  :
  - Academic success: oral communication skills are essential for participating in class discussions, delivering oral presentations, and collaborating with peers and instructors.
  - Professional development: oral communication skills are required for communicating with colleagues, clients, and employers, as well as for presenting one's work, ideas, and achievements.
  - Personal growth: oral communication skills are beneficial for developing one's confidence, identity, and relationships, as well as for expressing one's emotions, values, and beliefs.
