### Forms of Technical Communication

- Technical communication is the method of producing and sharing ideas and information in organizations, especially about technical or specialized topics, such as computer applications, medical procedures, or environmental regulations.
- Technical communication can also involve using technology to create or distribute the communication, such as web pages, help files, or social media sites.
- Technical communication can also provide instructions about how to do something, regardless of how technical the task is or even if technology is used to create or distribute that communication.
- Technical communication can have different purposes, such as defining, instructing, informing, or persuading.
- Technical communication can take different forms, such as business letters, memos, reports, notices, proposals, websites, blogs, and more .
- Technical communication can target different audiences, such as internal or external, expert or novice, general or specific, and so on.
- Technical communication can follow different standards, such as style, format, tone, design, and ethics, depending on the context, purpose, and audience of the communication.
