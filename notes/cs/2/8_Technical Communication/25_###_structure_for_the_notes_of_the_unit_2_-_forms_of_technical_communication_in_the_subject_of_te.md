### structure for the notes of the Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication

Structure is a key element of technical communication, as it helps to organize and present information in a clear and logical manner. The structure of a technical document is determined by its purpose, audience, and content, and it should be designed to meet the needs of the reader.

In technical communication, there are several common structures that are used in different types of documents, including memos, reports, instructions, and proposals. Each type of document has a specific structure that is designed to meet its specific purpose and audience.

For example, a memo typically has a simple structure that includes a header, an introduction, a body, and a conclusion. A report typically has a more complex structure that includes an executive summary, an introduction, a methodology, a results section, a discussion section, and a conclusion.

The structure of a technical document should be designed to guide the reader through the information, and to help the reader understand and retain the information. Effective structure involves the use of headings, subheadings, and visual aids, and it should be designed to enhance the readability and effectiveness of the document.

In this unit, we will study the forms of technical communication, including the structure of different types of documents. We will also examine the strategies for designing effective structures, and explore the use of structure in various applications, such as technical writing, technical editing, and technical illustration. This will provide a foundation for understanding the principles and practices of technical communication, and for exploring the various applications of technical communication in the workplace.
