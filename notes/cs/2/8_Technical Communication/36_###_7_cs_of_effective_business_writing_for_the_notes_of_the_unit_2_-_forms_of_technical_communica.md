### 7 Cs of effective business writing for the notes of the Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication

Unit 2 of the Technical Communication course covers Forms of Technical Communication, including the 7 Cs of effective business writing.

The 7 Cs of effective business writing are a set of principles that can be used to improve the clarity, conciseness, and effectiveness of business writing. The 7 Cs are:

1. Clarity: Ensure that your writing is clear and easy to understand.

2. Conciseness: Keep your writing short and to the point.

3. Completeness: Make sure that your writing covers all of the relevant information.

4. Consideration: Think about the needs and perspectives of your audience.

5. Concreteness: Use specific examples and details to support your writing.

6. Correctness: Ensure that your writing is accurate and free of errors.

7. Coherence: Make sure that your writing is well-organized and easy to follow.

In the context of the Technical Communication course, students in Unit 2 will learn about the 7 Cs of effective business writing as part of the Forms of Technical Communication. This may include working with examples of business writing, learning how to apply the 7 Cs to business writing, and practicing writing business documents.
