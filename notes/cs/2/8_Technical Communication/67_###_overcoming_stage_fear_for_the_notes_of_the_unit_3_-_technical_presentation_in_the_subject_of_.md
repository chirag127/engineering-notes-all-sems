### Overcoming Stage Fear for the notes of the Unit 3 - Technical Presentation in the subject of Technical Communication

Overcoming Stage Fear:
- Stage fear, also known as performance anxiety, is a common fear among speakers.
- Causes of stage fear include fear of judgment, lack of preparation, and past negative experiences.
- To overcome stage fear, speakers can:
  - Prepare thoroughly and practice their presentation.
  - Focus on their message, not themselves.
  - Use positive self-talk and visualization techniques.
  - Address past negative experiences and reframe them.
  - Seek support from friends, family, or a therapist.
