### courtesy for the notes of the Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication

Unit 2 of the Technical Communication course covers Forms of Technical Communication, including the aspect of courtesy.

In technical communication, courtesy refers to the use of polite and respectful language and tone in writing and other forms of communication. This includes avoiding language that could be perceived as rude, condescending, or dismissive.

The use of courtesy in technical communication is important because it helps to build trust and credibility with the audience. When technical information is presented in a polite and respectful manner, the audience is more likely to be engaged and receptive to the information being presented.

In the context of the Technical Communication course, students in Unit 2 will learn about the importance of courtesy in technical communication as part of the Forms of Technical Communication. This may include working with examples of courteous and non-courteous language, analyzing the impact of language on the audience, and practicing writing in a courteous and respectful manner.

In summary, Unit 2 of the Technical Communication course covers Forms of Technical Communication, and includes a discussion of the aspect of courtesy. In technical communication, courtesy refers to the use of polite and respectful language and tone, and is important because it helps to build trust and credibility with the audience. Students will learn about the importance of courtesy in technical communication through interactive activities and hands-on experiences.
