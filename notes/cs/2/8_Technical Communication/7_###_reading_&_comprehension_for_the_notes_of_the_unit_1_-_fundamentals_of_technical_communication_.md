### Reading & comprehension for the notes of the Unit 1 - Fundamentals of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication

Reading and comprehension are fundamental skills in technical communication, as they are essential to understanding and interpreting technical information. In the Unit 1 - Fundamentals of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication, students learn about the basics of reading and comprehension and how to apply these skills in technical contexts. The notes for this unit should include the following topics:

1. Definition of reading and comprehension and their importance in technical communication
2. Overview of reading strategies (previewing, skimming, scanning, intensive reading)
3. Techniques for improving reading speed and comprehension (speed-reading, active reading, note-taking)
4. Types of technical texts (manuals, reports, specifications, data sheets)
5. Key elements of technical texts (headings, subheadings, graphics, tables, bullet points)
6. Techniques for understanding technical texts (identifying main ideas, recognizing patterns, summarizing)
7. Applications of reading and comprehension in technical communication (design, engineering, science)
8. Limitations of reading and comprehension (language barriers, cultural differences, technical jargon)
9. Techniques for overcoming limitations (dictionary use, glossaries, translation software)
10. Further reading and resources (books, journals, online resources)

Reading and comprehension are essential skills in technical communication and provide a foundation for understanding and interpreting technical information. Understanding these skills and how to apply them in technical contexts helps students to develop the skills required to communicate effectively in technical fields and to make informed decisions based on technical information.
