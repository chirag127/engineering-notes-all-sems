### Draft for the notes of the Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication

A draft is an early version of a document that is used to organize and refine the content before it is finalized. In technical communication, drafts are an important part of the writing process, as they provide a framework for the final document and allow for revisions and improvements.

Drafts can be used to organize and present information in a clear and logical manner, and they can be used to test the structure and content of the document. Drafts can also be used to identify areas that need improvement, and to make changes to the document before it is finalized.

In technical communication, drafts can be used to create a variety of documents, including reports, memos, instructions, and proposals. Drafts are typically written in a rough and informal manner, and they are used to test and refine the content and structure of the document.
