### Methods for the notes of the Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication

Methods refer to the techniques and strategies used to create and deliver technical communication. In technical communication, there are several methods that can be used to create and deliver effective messages, including written communication, oral communication, and visual communication.

Written communication includes methods such as reports, memos, instructions, and proposals. Written communication is often used to provide detailed and comprehensive information, and it is often used to communicate technical information to a wide audience.

Oral communication includes methods such as presentations, meetings, and teleconferencing. Oral communication is often used to provide information and instructions in real-time, and it is often used to communicate technical information to a small or focused audience.

Visual communication includes methods such as diagrams, charts, and graphs. Visual communication is often used to provide information that is easy to understand and to convey complex information in a clear and concise manner.

In this unit, we will study the forms of technical communication, including written communication, oral communication, and visual communication. We will also examine the methods used in each form of communication, and explore the strategies for creating and delivering effective messages. This will provide a foundation for understanding the principles and practices of technical communication, and for exploring the various applications of technical communication in the workplace.
