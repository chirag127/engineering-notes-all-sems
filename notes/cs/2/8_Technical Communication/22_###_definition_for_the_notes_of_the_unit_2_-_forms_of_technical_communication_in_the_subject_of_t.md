### Definition for the notes of the Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication

The Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication covers the various forms of technical communication and their applications. The notes for this unit should include the following topics:

1. Definition of technical communication (conveying technical information to a specific audience)
2. Overview of forms of technical communication (writing, speaking, visual communication)
3. Technical writing (types of technical documents, structure, style)
4. Technical speaking (presentations, oral reports, public speaking)
5. Visual communication (illustrations, diagrams, graphs, multimedia)
6. Multimedia technical communication (animation, video, audio)
7. Web-based technical communication (websites, online help, e-learning)
8. Mobile technical communication (mobile apps, mobile websites)
9. Collaborative technical communication (conferencing, wikis, blogs)
10. Further reading and resources (books, journals, online resources)

Technical communication takes many forms and is used in a variety of fields, including engineering, science, and technology. Understanding the forms of technical communication helps students to develop the skills required to communicate technical information effectively and to make informed decisions about the best methods for conveying information to a specific audience.
