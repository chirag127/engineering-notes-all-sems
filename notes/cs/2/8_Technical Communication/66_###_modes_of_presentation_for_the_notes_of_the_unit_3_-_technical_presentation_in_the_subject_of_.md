### Modes of Presentation for the notes of the Unit 3 - Technical Presentation in the subject of Technical Communication

The mode of presentation refers to the method or format used to deliver technical information to an audience. In technical communication, there are several modes of presentation, including:

1. Oral presentations: Oral presentations are delivered in person, and they involve the use of spoken language to convey technical information. Oral presentations can be delivered in a variety of settings, including classrooms, conference rooms, and online platforms.

2. Slideshow presentations: Slideshow presentations are delivered using visual aids, such as slides, to convey technical information. Slideshow presentations can be delivered in person or online, and they are often used to support oral presentations.

3. Demonstrations: Demonstrations involve the use of physical objects or equipment to demonstrate technical concepts or procedures. Demonstrations are often used in hands-on training sessions, and they are designed to engage the audience and to provide a tangible representation of the information.

4. Video presentations: Video presentations are delivered using video technology, and they can be used to convey technical information in a variety of settings. Video presentations can be used to deliver presentations in person, and they can also be used to deliver presentations online.

In this unit, we will study the modes of presentation used in technical communication, including oral presentations, slideshow presentations, demonstrations, and video presentations. We will also examine the use of each mode in various applications, and explore the strategies for creating and delivering effective presentations in each mode. This will provide a foundation for understanding the principles and practices of technical communication, and for exploring the various applications of technical communication in the workplace.
