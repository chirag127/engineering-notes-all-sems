Reports are used to present information and findings in a structured and organized manner, and they are typically longer and more formal. Instructions are used to provide step-by-step guidance for performing a task, and they are typically clear and concise. Proposals are used to present a plan or solution to a problem, and they are typically persuasive and convincing.
