### Public Speaking for the notes of the Unit 3 - Technical Presentation in the subject of Technical Communication

Unit 3 of the Technical Communication course focuses on Technical Presentation, including the aspect of public speaking.

Public speaking is the act of presenting information to an audience in a structured and organized manner. In technical communication, public speaking is used to present technical information to a non-technical or technical audience.

Effective public speaking requires clear and concise language, effective use of visual aids, and the ability to engage and respond to the audience. Technical presenters must also be able to adapt their presentation style to the needs and preferences of their audience.

In the context of the Technical Communication course, students in Unit 3 will learn about public speaking as part of the Technical Presentation. This may include working with examples of effective and ineffective public speaking, learning about the elements of effective public speaking, and practicing public speaking through interactive activities.

In summary, Unit 3 of the Technical Communication course focuses on Technical Presentation, and includes a discussion of public speaking. Public speaking is the act of presenting information to an audience in a structured and organized manner, and is an important aspect of technical communication. Students will learn about public speaking through interactive activities and hands-on experiences, and will learn strategies for effective public speaking.
