## Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication

Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication covers the various forms of technical communication and their applications in different fields. The notes for this unit should include the following topics:

1. Overview of forms of technical communication (written, oral, visual)
2. Written technical communication (letters, memos, reports, proposals)
3. Oral technical communication (presentations, oral reports, teleconferencing)
4. Visual technical communication (charts, graphs, diagrams, illustrations)
5. Multimedia technical communication (animation, video, audio)
6. Applications of forms of technical communication (design, engineering, science)
7. Choosing the appropriate form of communication (audience, purpose, context)
8. Writing and designing effective technical documents (structure, style, format, layout)
9. Preparing and delivering effective presentations (organization, delivery, visual aids)
10. Further reading and resources (books, journals, online resources)

Understanding the various forms of technical communication and their applications is an important part of the study of technical communication. This unit provides students with the skills required to choose the appropriate form of communication for a specific audience and purpose, and to create effective technical documents and presentations.
