### Grammatical for the notes of the Unit 4 - Technical Communication Skills in the subject of Technical Communication

In technical communication, it is essential to use proper grammar to convey information accurately and effectively. Here are some grammatical rules to keep in mind when writing technical documents:

1. Use active voice: Using active voice in technical writing makes the information more clear and concise. It also helps in emphasizing the subject of the sentence.

2. Avoid using passive voice: Passive voice makes the sentence less clear and precise. It also makes the information less engaging to the reader.

3. Use proper punctuation: Proper punctuation helps in conveying the meaning of the sentence accurately. It also helps in making the document more professional.

4. Use correct verb tenses: Using the correct verb tense helps in conveying the right time frame of the events described in the document. It also helps in making the document more accurate and credible.

5. Use appropriate sentence structure: Using appropriate sentence structure helps in making the document more readable and understandable. It also helps in conveying the information in a logical and organized manner.

6. Use correct subject-verb agreement: Using correct subject-verb agreement helps in making the sentence grammatically correct. It also helps in conveying the meaning of the sentence accurately.

7. Avoid using slang or jargon: Using slang or jargon in technical documents can confuse the reader and make the document less professional.

By following these grammatical rules, technical communicators can create effective and professional documents that accurately convey information to the reader.
