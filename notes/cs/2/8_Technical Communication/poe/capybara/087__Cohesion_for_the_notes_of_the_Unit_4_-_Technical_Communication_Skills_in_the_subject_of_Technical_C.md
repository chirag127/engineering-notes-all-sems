### Cohesion

Cohesion is an important aspect of technical communication. It refers to the degree to which the parts of a text are connected and work together to convey a single, unified message. Cohesion is essential in technical communication as it ensures that the information being communicated is clear, concise, and easy to understand.

Here are some ways to achieve cohesion in technical communication:

- Use transitional words and phrases to link ideas and sentences together. Examples of transitional words include "however," "moreover," and "therefore."

- Use parallel structure to make sentences easier to read and understand. Parallel structure involves using the same grammatical structure for similar ideas within a sentence or paragraph.

- Use consistent terminology throughout the text. This ensures that the reader does not become confused by different terms being used to refer to the same concept.

- Use headings and subheadings to organize the text and make it easier to read. Headings should be informative and reflect the content of the section they introduce.

- Use cohesive devices such as pronouns, repetition, and synonyms to tie ideas together. Cohesive devices help to create links between sentences and paragraphs and make the text easier to follow.

- Use active voice rather than passive voice. Active voice makes the text more direct and easier to understand.

By using these techniques, technical communicators can ensure that their documents are cohesive and easy to understand, which is essential when communicating complex technical information.
