### Essentials for the Notes of the Unit 3 - Technical Presentation in the Subject of Technical Communication

When it comes to creating effective technical presentations, there are certain essentials that should be included in your notes. Here are some key points to keep in mind:

- **Purpose of the presentation**: Clearly define the purpose of your presentation in your notes. This will help you stay focused and ensure that your presentation stays on track.

- **Target audience**: Identify your target audience and tailor your presentation to their needs and interests. Make sure your notes include information about your audience, such as their level of technical expertise and their expectations for the presentation.

- **Content**: Your notes should include a clear outline of the content of your presentation. This should include the main points you want to make, any supporting data or examples, and any visuals or other materials you plan to use.

- **Visual aids**: Visual aids can be a powerful tool for engaging your audience and conveying complex information. Your notes should include information about the visual aids you plan to use, such as slides, diagrams, or videos.

- **Delivery**: The way you deliver your presentation is just as important as the content itself. Your notes should include information about your delivery style, such as your tone of voice, body language, and pacing. You should also practice your delivery before the actual presentation to ensure that you are comfortable with your style.

- **Timing**: Timing is crucial in a technical presentation. Your notes should include a clear timeline for your presentation, including how long you plan to spend on each section and any breaks or Q&A sessions.

- **Feedback**: Finally, your notes should include a plan for gathering feedback from your audience. This can help you improve your presentation skills for future presentations and ensure that your audience is satisfied with the information you have provided.

By keeping these essentials in mind when creating your notes for a technical presentation, you can improve your chances of delivering an effective and engaging presentation that meets the needs of your audience.
