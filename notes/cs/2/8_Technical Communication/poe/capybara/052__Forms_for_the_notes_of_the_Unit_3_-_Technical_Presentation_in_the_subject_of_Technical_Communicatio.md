### Forms for the notes of the Unit 3 - Technical Presentation in the subject of Technical Communication

When taking notes during a technical presentation, it is important to have a structured and organized format. Here are some forms that can be used to take effective notes:

1. **Outline form:** This form involves creating headings and subheadings to organize the information presented. This is a great option for presentations that follow a clear structure or outline.

2. **Cornell Notes form:** This form involves dividing the page into three sections: a main section for notes, a side section for key points or questions, and a summary section at the bottom. This form is useful for retaining information and studying later on.

3. **Mind map form:** This form involves creating a visual representation of the information presented. This is a great option for presentations that involve complex or interconnected concepts.

4. **Chart or table form:** This form involves organizing the information into a chart or table format. This is a great option for presentations that involve statistics or comparisons.

Regardless of the form used, it is important to include the following information in your notes:

- The date and title of the presentation
- The name of the presenter(s)
- Key terms and definitions
- Examples and case studies
- Main points and conclusions
- Questions or areas that require further research or clarification

By using a structured form and including all necessary information, you can create effective notes that will help you retain and apply the information presented in the technical presentation.
