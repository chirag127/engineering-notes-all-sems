### Strategies for the notes of the Unit 3 - Technical Presentation in the subject of Technical Communication:

1. **Active Listening:** During the technical presentation, focus on active listening to ensure you capture all the essential points. Maintain eye contact with the presenter, nod your head, and take notes to help you stay engaged.

2. **Organize Your Notes:** After the presentation, organize your notes by creating an outline that highlights the essential points presented. This ensures that you have a clear understanding of the topic and can easily refer to your notes when needed.

3. **Use Short Sentences:** When taking notes, use short sentences and bullet points to capture the essential information. This helps you to stay organized and to understand the key points of the presentation.

4. **Highlight Key Information:** Highlight key information in your notes to ensure that you can quickly refer to it when needed. This could be done using different colors or underlining the essential points in your notes.

5. **Stay Focused:** During the presentation, avoid distractions and stay focused on the topic being presented. This helps you to capture all the essential points and ensures that your notes are accurate.

6. **Review Your Notes:** After the presentation, review your notes to ensure that you have captured all the essential points accurately. This helps you to reinforce your understanding of the topic and to clarify any doubts that you may have.

7. **Use Visual Aids:** If the presenter uses visual aids such as slides or diagrams, be sure to take notes on these as well. This helps you to understand the topic better and to remember the key points of the presentation.

8. **Ask Questions:** If you have any questions or doubts during the presentation, be sure to ask them. This helps you to clarify any doubts you may have and to better understand the topic being presented.

9. **Summarize the Presentation:** Finally, summarize the presentation in a few sentences to ensure that you have a clear understanding of the topic. This also helps you to remember the key points of the presentation and to refer to them when needed.
