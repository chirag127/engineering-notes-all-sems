### Style for the Notes of the Unit 4 - Technical Communication Skills in the Subject of Technical Communication

When taking notes on technical communication skills, it is important to maintain a formal and professional tone throughout. Here are some guidelines to follow for note-taking:

- Use clear and concise language: Avoid using slang or jargon that may not be familiar to everyone. Use simple and straightforward language that is easy to understand.

- Be objective: It is important to remain neutral when taking notes. Avoid injecting personal opinions or biases into your notes.

- Use active voice: When taking notes, use active voice instead of passive voice. This helps to make your notes more concise and clear.

- Organize your notes: Group related information together and use headings and subheadings to organize your notes. This helps to make it easier to review and study your notes later on.

- Use abbreviations carefully: While abbreviations can help to save time and space, they can also be confusing if not used correctly. Only use abbreviations that are commonly accepted within the field.

- Take note of key concepts: Make sure to note down the main ideas and concepts covered in class. This will help you to better understand the material and prepare for exams.

- Use diagrams and drawings: Sometimes, it can be helpful to use diagrams or drawings to illustrate complex ideas. This can also help to make your notes more engaging and memorable.

By following these guidelines, you can create effective and professional notes on technical communication skills. These notes will be a valuable resource for studying and reviewing the material covered in class.
