### Features for the notes of the Unit 1 - Fundamentals of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication

- Clarity: The notes should be clear and concise to ensure easy understanding of the content. Technical communication is all about conveying complex information in a simple and clear manner. The notes should follow this principle.

- Structure: The notes should be structured logically, with headings and subheadings to guide the reader through the content. This will improve readability and make it easier for the reader to follow the flow of information.

- Accuracy: Technical communication requires accuracy in the information presented. The notes should be factually correct and free from errors to ensure that the reader has access to reliable information.

- Objectivity: The notes should be objective and free from personal bias or opinions. This ensures that the reader can form their own opinions based on the information presented.

- Consistency: The notes should be consistent in the use of terminology, formatting, and language. This helps to avoid confusion and ensures that the reader can easily navigate the notes.

- Use of visuals: Visual aids such as diagrams, charts, and graphs can be used to enhance the understanding of complex information. The notes should include these visuals wherever appropriate.

- References: The notes should include references to any external sources used in the content. This helps to establish credibility and allows the reader to access further information if needed.

- Accessibility: The notes should be accessible to all readers, including those with disabilities. This can be achieved through the use of accessible formatting and language.

- Review: The notes should be reviewed and updated regularly to ensure that the information presented is up-to-date and accurate. This also helps to improve the quality of the notes over time.
