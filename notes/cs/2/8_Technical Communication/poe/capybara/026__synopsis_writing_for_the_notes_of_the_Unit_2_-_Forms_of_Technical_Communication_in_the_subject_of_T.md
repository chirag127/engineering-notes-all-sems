### Synopsis Writing for the Notes of the Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication in the Subject of Technical Communication

Technical communication is the process of conveying technical information to the audience in a clear and concise manner. One of the essential forms of technical communication is synopsis writing. A synopsis is a brief summary of a larger work, such as a technical report, research paper, or proposal. It is an important document that provides an overview of the main points and key findings of the work. Here are some guidelines for writing a synopsis:

1. Start with a clear and concise statement of the purpose of the work. This should be a one or two-sentence summary that captures the main objective of the work.

2. Provide a brief overview of the methodology used in the work. This should include a description of the research design, data collection methods, and data analysis techniques.

3. Summarize the key findings of the work. This should be a brief description of the main results, conclusions, and recommendations.

4. Highlight the significance of the work. This should include a discussion of how the work contributes to the field, its potential impact, and any practical applications.

5. Use clear and concise language. Avoid technical jargon and complex terminology that may be difficult for the audience to understand.

6. Keep the synopsis brief. A good rule of thumb is to aim for a length of one to two pages, depending on the length and complexity of the original work.

In conclusion, synopsis writing is an important form of technical communication that requires a clear and concise summary of the main points of a larger work. By following these guidelines, you can create an effective synopsis that communicates the key findings and significance of your work to the audience.
