### Definition for the notes of the Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication

Technical communication is the process of conveying technical information to a specific audience for a specific purpose. It is a broad term that encompasses various forms of communication, including written, oral, and visual communication. In this unit, we will focus on the different forms of technical communication. Below are the definitions of the various forms of technical communication:

1. Reports: Reports are formal documents that present information in a structured format. They are usually written for a specific audience and purpose, such as to inform a company's management about the performance of a product or to provide technical information to a client.

2. Proposals: Proposals are formal documents that propose a solution to a problem or suggest a course of action. They are often used in business settings to propose new projects or initiatives.

3. Instructions: Instructions are documents that provide step-by-step guidance on how to perform a task or use a product. They are often used in technical settings to guide users or employees on how to operate machinery or software.

4. Manuals: Manuals are comprehensive documents that provide detailed information about a product, process, or system. They are often used in technical settings to guide users or employees on how to operate machinery or software.

5. Presentations: Presentations are visual aids that are used to convey information to an audience. They are often used in business settings to present new products or services or to provide updates on ongoing projects.

6. Emails: Emails are written messages that are sent electronically. They are often used in business settings to communicate with colleagues or clients and to provide updates on projects or tasks.

In conclusion, technical communication is an essential part of many industries and professions. Understanding the different forms of technical communication is crucial for effectively communicating technical information to a specific audience for a specific purpose.
