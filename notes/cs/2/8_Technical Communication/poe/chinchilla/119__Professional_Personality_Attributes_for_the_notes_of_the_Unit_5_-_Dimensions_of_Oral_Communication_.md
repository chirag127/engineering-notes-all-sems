### Professional Personality Attributes

In the field of technical communication, oral communication and voice dynamics play a crucial role in conveying information effectively. However, it is not just about the words we speak or the tone of our voice. Our professional personality attributes can greatly impact how we are perceived by others and can influence the success of our communication efforts. Here are some key professional personality attributes to keep in mind:

- **Confidence:** Confidence is essential in any form of communication. It helps to establish credibility and can make the listener feel more at ease. Speak clearly and with conviction, and avoid using filler words such as "um" or "like" which can indicate uncertainty.

- **Empathy:** Empathy is the ability to understand and share the feelings of others. When communicating, it is important to take into account the perspective of the listener and tailor your message accordingly. This can involve using language that is easily understood, or adjusting your tone to match the situation.

- **Flexibility:** Being flexible in your communication style can help you adapt to different situations and audiences. This might involve using different language or terminology depending on the context, or adjusting your tone and body language to match the mood of the conversation.

- **Professionalism:** Professionalism is about presenting yourself in a manner that is appropriate for the situation. This might involve dressing appropriately for a meeting, using formal language in a professional setting, or maintaining a respectful tone when communicating with others.

- **Active listening:** Active listening involves paying close attention to what the other person is saying, and responding appropriately. This might involve asking questions to clarify a point, or paraphrasing what the other person has said to show that you understand.

- **Positivity:** A positive attitude can go a long way in establishing a good rapport with others. This might involve using positive language, focusing on solutions rather than problems, and maintaining a friendly demeanor.

- **Adaptability:** Being adaptable in your communication style can help you navigate unexpected situations and challenges. This might involve adjusting your message or tone depending on the feedback you receive, or being open to new ideas and perspectives.

By cultivating these professional personality attributes, you can improve your oral communication and voice dynamics, and become a more effective communicator overall.
