## Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication

1.  Memos - Used for internal communication within an organization. They are brief and to the point. They follow a standard structure including a header, opening, discussion, and closing.
2.  Letters - Can be formal or informal. Formal letters follow a standard structure and are used for official communication. Informal letters have a friendly, conversational tone.
3.  Proposals - Present a concept or idea to convince the reader to take a particular course of action. They include an introduction, problem statement, objectives/goals, methods, resources, timeline, and budget.
4.  Reports - Communicate information or results of an analysis or experiment. They have a standard structure including an abstract, introduction, methodology, results, discussion, and conclusion section. Data and graphics are often included to aid understanding.
5.  Resumes - Summarize one's education, skills, and experience. They are concise and focused on qualifications relevant to a specific position or career field. A cover letter is often sent with a resume to express interest in a position and highlight key experiences that match what the employer is seeking.
