### Clarity for the notes of the Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication

1. Technical Memos
- Used for internal communication within an organization.
- Usually less formal than reports.
- Provide relevant facts and data.

2. Reports
- Used to provide analysis or share results of a project or study.
- Very structured with sections for specific types of information.
- Abstract or executive summary, introduction, methods, results, discussion, conclusions, recommendations.

3. Proposals
- Present plans to solve a problem or pursue a project.
- Persuade the audience to accept the proposed solution or project.
- Include statement of the problem, objectives, methods, budget, timeline, and qualifications.

4. Procedures
- Explain step-by-step how to complete a task or process.
- Clear, directive language using active voice.
- Organize chronologically, by steps, or by goals.

5. Instructions
- More specific and limited than procedures.
- Explain how to assemble, install, operate, or perform a limited task.
- Clear, concise language. Organize sequentially by steps.
