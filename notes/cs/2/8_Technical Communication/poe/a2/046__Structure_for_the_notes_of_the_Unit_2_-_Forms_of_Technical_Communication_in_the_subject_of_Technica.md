### Structure for the notes of the Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication

1. Memo: Used for communicating official information within an organization. It is brief and concise.
- Includes: To, From, Subject, Date.
- Uses: Status updates, announcements, procedures.

2. Report: Used to convey specific information or present research findings.
- Includes: Title page, Abstract or Executive Summary, Table of Contents, Introduction, Methods, Results, Discussion, Conclusions, References.
- Uses: Project updates, data analysis, problem solutions.

3. Proposal: Used to persuade the reader to accept an idea or plan of action.
- Includes: Title, Summary, Problem Statement, Objectives, Methods, Resources, Budget, Qualifications.
- Uses: Project funding, new process implementation, research funding.

4. Instructions: Used to provide steps required for completing a task or process.
- Includes: Materials list, sequential steps, diagrams/images, cautions/warnings.
- Uses: Assembly, software installation, machine operation, experimental procedures.

5. Letter: Used for external correspondence to convey a message or request information.
- Includes: Letter format with header and closing.
- Uses: Business correspondence, community outreach, customer service.
