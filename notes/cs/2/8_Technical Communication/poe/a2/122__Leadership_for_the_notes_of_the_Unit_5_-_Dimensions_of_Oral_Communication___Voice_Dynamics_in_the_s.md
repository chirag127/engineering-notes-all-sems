### Leadership for the notes of the Unit 5 - Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics in the subject of Technical Communication

1. Leaders communicate a vision: Leaders have a clear vision and communicate that to others. They share their ideas, goals and the path to achieve them with their team. This helps in aligning the team in one direction.
2. Leaders listen: Leaders are good listeners. They listen to their team members and understand their concerns or issues. This helps them in making better decisions and keeping the team motivated.
3. Leaders motivate and inspire: Leaders motivate and inspire their team by recognizing their efforts and achievements. They encourage the team during difficulties and help them see the bigger picture. This boosts the team's morale, productivity and loyalty.
4. Leaders provide feedback: Leaders provide both positive and constructive feedback to their team members. This helps the team members understand their strengths and weaknesses and work on their improvement. This leads to their professional development and improved performance.
5. Leaders delegate: Leaders delegate responsibilities and trust their team members to get the work done. This makes the team members feel valued and gives them opportunities to develop their skills. Delegation also reduces the workload of leaders and helps them focus on other important tasks.

The above points cover the key leadership skills for effective oral communication and to create the desired impact and dynamics. Leaders can communicate the vision, listen to understand, motivate to inspire, provide feedback and delegate to empower their team members. Practicing these skills can make one a good leader and strong oral communicator.
