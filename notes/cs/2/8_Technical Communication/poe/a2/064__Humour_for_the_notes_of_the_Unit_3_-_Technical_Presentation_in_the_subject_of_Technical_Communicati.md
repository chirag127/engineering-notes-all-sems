### Humour for the notes of the Unit 3 - Technical Presentation in the subject of Technical Communication

1. Use of humour judiciously: Using humour in technical presentations needs to be done very carefully. It should be used only when appropriate and when it genuinely adds value to the presentation. Forcing humour where it does not fit can be off-putting for the audience.
2. Keep it relevant: Any humour used should be directly related to the topic of the presentation. Forcing in unrelated jokes or one-liners just for the sake of humour can be seen as filler content and distracting. The humour should tie back to the core message or lessons of the presentation.
3. Keep it appropriate: The type of humour used should be appropriate for the audience and occasion. What is appropriate for friends may not be so for colleagues or managers. The presenter needs to understand the audience well and determine what kind of humour can be suitably used. Offensive or inappropriate jokes should be avoided.
4. Keep it light: The humour used should be light-hearted, good-natured and not cross any lines. It should not get too personal or make anyone uncomfortable. The idea is to induce a gentle chuckle and not make people cringe.
5. Practice and deliver appropriately: Any humour, even if well-written on paper may not land well if the delivery is off. The presenter needs to practice the humorous parts and deliver them with the right tone and style to get the intended effect on the audience. A misdelivery can lead to an awkward pause or even offend the audience.
