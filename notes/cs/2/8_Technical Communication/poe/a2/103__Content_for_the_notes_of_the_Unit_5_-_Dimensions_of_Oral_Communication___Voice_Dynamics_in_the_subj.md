### Content for the notes of the Unit 5 - Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics in the subject of Technical Communication

1. Dimensions of Oral Communication
- Content: What is being communicated? The actual message or information being shared.
- Context: The conditions or circumstances surrounding the communication. Includes factors like the physical setting, the relationships between the speakers, and the expectations of the speakers and listeners.
- Process: How the communication is being sent and received. Includes things like the medium used to communicate and how the speakers are listening, interpreting messages, and providing feedback.

2. Voice Dynamics
- Volume: The level of loudness or softness of a speaker's voice. A speaker can vary volume for emphasis or to capture attention.
- Rate: The speed at which words are spoken. A faster rate can convey excitement or urgency while a slower rate can suggest seriousness or importance.
- Pitch: The highness or lowness of a speaker's voice. Pitch is varied to provide intonation and emotional cues.
- Pauses: Brief periods of silence that provide timing, allow listeners to process information, and draw attention to important points. Effective use of pauses impacts the overall flow and rhythm of a speech.
