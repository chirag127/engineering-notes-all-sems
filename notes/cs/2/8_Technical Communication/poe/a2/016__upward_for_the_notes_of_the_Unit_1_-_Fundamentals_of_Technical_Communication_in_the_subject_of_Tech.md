### What is Technical Communication?

- Technical Communication is a broad field that focuses on the effective transmission of information about technical topics through various communication mediums including text, video, and speech.
- The goal of Technical Communication is to convey complex information to users in a clear, concise, and understandable manner.
- Technical Communication encompasses the following types of communication:
    - User manuals
    - Technical reports
    - Proposals
    - Journal articles
    - Presentations
    - Online help systems
    - Software documentation

### Why is Technical Communication important?

- Technical Communication minimizes misunderstandings and prevents errors or hazards that could result from miscommunication about technical topics.
- Clear communication about technical information enables users to safely and effectively use products, systems, or services as intended.
- In many cases, clear technical writing can prevent financial loss, legal issues, or even physical harm. For these reasons, strong technical communication skills are highly valuable across industries.
