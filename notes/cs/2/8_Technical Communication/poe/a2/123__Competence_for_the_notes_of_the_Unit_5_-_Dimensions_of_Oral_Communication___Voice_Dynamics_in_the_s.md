### Competence for the notes of the Unit 5 - Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics in the subject of Technical Communication

1. Dimensions of Oral Communication
- Content: What you say - accuracy, completeness, coherence, conciseness
- Process: How you say it - pace, pauses, filler words
- Delivery: bodily - eye contact, gestures, posture; vocal - tone, volume, rate

2. Achieving Clarity in Speech
- Organize your thoughts before speaking
- Use simple words and short sentences
- Provide context and explain technical terms
- Highlight key phrases and important points

3. Elements of Effective Delivery
- Vocal variety: change pitch, pace, pause, volume
- Enunciate clearly: articulate words, open mouth
- Project confidence: make eye contact, use hand gestures, stand/sit upright

4. Voice Dynamics
- Pitch: high/low; varied = engaging; monotone = boring
- Pace: fast/slow; varied = interesting; rushing = confusing
- Pause: short/long; use for effect; show confidence; avoid filler words ("um", "like")
- Volume: loud/soft; varied = emphasis; too soft/loud = frustrating

5. Practice and Prepare
- Know your material and purpose
- Practice out loud
- Record yourself and identify areas to improve
- Prepare notes/script as a backup
