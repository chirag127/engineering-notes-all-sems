### Solution of communication problems with verbal and non verbal means for the notes of the Unit 4 - Technical Communication Skills in the subject of Technical Communication.

1. Clarify misunderstandings:
- Ask questions to verify understanding.
- Paraphrase and summarize to confirm.
- Repeat information or requests if needed.

2. Be an engaged listener:
- Give full attention to the speaker.
- Maintain eye contact, nod and use affirming words like 'uh-huh' to show interest.
- Avoid distractions and multitasking.
- Provide appropriate verbal and non-verbal feedback.

3. Be respectful:
- Use polite words and tone.
- Avoid insults, personal attacks and sarcasm.
- Respect opinions and values that differ from your own.
- Avoid insensitive or inappropriate remarks.

4. Be open and honest:
- Share information freely and truthfully.
- Admit mistakes and limitations.
- Avoid deceit, dishonesty and withholding of relevant information.

5. Choose the appropriate communication channel:
- Consider urgency and importance of the message.
- Determine the best way to reach the intended audience.
- Ensure the channel is accessible and acceptable to all parties.
- Select the channel that will be the most effective and appropriate.
