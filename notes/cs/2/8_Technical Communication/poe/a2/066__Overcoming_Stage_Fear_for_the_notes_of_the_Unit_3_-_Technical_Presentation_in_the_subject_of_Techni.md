### Overcoming Stage Fear

1. Prepare thoroughly: Being well versed with the topic and content boosts confidence. Practice the presentation multiple times in front of mirrors or friends and family. Knowing the content inside out alleviates stage fear.

2. Focus on the message: Remember why you are presenting. Focus on the value of the information for the audience rather than yourself. This shifts the attention away from self doubt and nervousness and towards the ultimate goal of sharing knowledge.

3. Visualize success: Picture yourself giving a smooth and flawless presentation. This helps in conditioning the mind to see oneself succeeding and being at ease while presenting. Positive visualization is a powerful technique to calm nerves.

4. Start strong: Begin with a firm voice and confidence. The way you start has a big impact on how the rest of the presentation goes. Even if feeling nervous on the inside, displaying strength at the outset can help gain control over the nerves.

5. Make eye contact: Engage the audience by making eye contact. This connects you to the listeners and helps in receiving their positive affirmation which boosts confidence. Looking at friendly and nod ding faces can encourage a presenter and relax them.

6. Pause and breathe: If at any point you feel nervous or panicked, pause, take a deep breath and then continue. Deep breathing is very calming for the body and mind. Such pauses also give the audience time to reflect on a point and gives the presenter an opportunity to recollect themselves.

7. Smile: Having a smile on your face, even if forced, triggers positive feelings in the body and mind. The audience also connects more with a presenter who appears comfortable and happy rather than nervous. A genuine smile after a presentation also signals achievement to the self.
