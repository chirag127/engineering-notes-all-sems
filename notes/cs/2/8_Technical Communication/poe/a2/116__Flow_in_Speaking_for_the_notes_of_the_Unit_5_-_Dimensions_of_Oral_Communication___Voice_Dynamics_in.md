### Flow in Speaking

1. Start smoothly: Begin speaking with confidence and composure. Take a few deep breaths and start in a composed manner.

2. Maintain pace: Speak at an appropriate pace which is neither too fast nor too slow. A moderate pace allows the audience to absorb the information being presented and also allows the speaker to speak effectively.

3. Emphasize important points: Use techniques like pausing, repetition or changing the tone to highlight key points or important portions of the speech. This helps the audience understand the structure and key takeaways from the speech.

4. Use transitions: Include transition words or phrases to link ideas and make the speech flow in a smooth manner. This provides coherence and continuity to the speech. Examples of transition words are: firstly, moreover, therefore, consequently, etc.

5. End strongly: End the speech by effectively summarizing the main points and finishing with a key message or call to action. This leaves a lasting impact on the audience. Some ways to end strongly are: with a quote, with a rhetorical question, or with a key summary statement.

6. Practice: Continuous practice is essential to being an effective speaker. Practice the speech multiple times in front of a mirror or to friends and family to get comfortable with the content and flow, and to reduce anxiety. With regular practice, speaking will become more natural and flawless.
