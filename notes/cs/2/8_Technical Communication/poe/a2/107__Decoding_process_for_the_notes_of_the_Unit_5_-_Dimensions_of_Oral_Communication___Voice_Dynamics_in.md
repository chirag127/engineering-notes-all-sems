### Decoding process for the notes of the Unit 5 - Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics in the subject of Technical Communication.

1. Oral Communication:
- Verbal communication through speech
- Face to face exchange of information
- Tone, pitch, pace, pauses, accent play an important role
- Feedback is immediate

2. Dimensions of Oral Communication:
- Content: Ideas, facts, thoughts being communicated
- Context: Setting, audience, purpose, occasion, relationship between speakers
- Process: How the message is encoded and decoded including tone, pace, gestures, etc.

3. Voice Dynamics:
- Volume: Loudness or softness of the voice
- Rate: Speed of speaking - slow, moderate, fast
- Pitch: Highness or lowness of the voice
- Pauses: Brief stops or breaks while speaking
- Inflection: Rising and falling of the pitch to add meaning
- Rhetorical Devices: Techniques to make speech effective like repetition, contrast, etc.

The above points cover the key topics to study and remember for the given unit of the Technical Communication course.
