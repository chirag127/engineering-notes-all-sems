### Description for the notes of the Unit 4 - Technical Communication Skills in the subject of Technical Communication.

1. What is Technical Communication?
- Technical communication is a broad field that involves effectively communicating technical information to others.
- It focuses on the clear, concise, and consistent communication of specialized and technical information.
- Examples include user manuals, reports, proposals, instruction sets, etc.

2. Importance of Technical Communication
- It increases productivity and reduces costs by minimizing errors and maximizing understanding.
- It fosters innovation as new ideas and technologies can be shared and discussed.
- It enhances safety by ensuring proper understanding of warnings, specifications, and instructions.
- It enables efficient problem-solving. Technical writers can identify gaps in processes or products and convey solutions.

3. Attributes of Good Technical Communication
- Accuracy: Information is correct, updated, and verified.
- Clarity: Easy to understand, jargon minimized, complex concepts simplified.
- Conciseness: Information is presented in few words while retaining clarity.
- Consistency: Use of standard templates/styles for different mediums and consistent tone, terminology, format, etc.
- Objectivity: Personal biases or opinions do not affect the communication. Facts and evidence are presented to support points.
