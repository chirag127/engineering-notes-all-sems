### Group Discussion for the notes of the Unit 4 - Technical Communication Skills in the subject of Technical Communication

1. Choose a appropriate topic for discussion: Choose a topic which is relevant to the subject and allows multiple perspectives and opinions to emerge. A controversial topic that allows debate is preferable.

2. Form groups: Form groups of 4-6 members. Heterogeneous groups with mix of gender and capabilities are preferred. This leads to diverse perspectives.

3. Assign roles: Assign roles like moderator, timekeeper, notetaker, speaker identifier to group members. This ensures orderly discussion.

4. Present problem: The moderator presents the problem or topic for discussion to the group. The topic can be provided by the instructor or chosen by the group.

5. Gather perspectives: The group members share their perspectives and opinions on the topic. They can build on others' opinions and have a constructive debate.

6. Synthesize and conclude: The group synthesizes the discussion and arrives at a conclusion or solution. The notetaker can summarize the key points discussed.

7. Share outcomes: Each group shares their conclusions or solutions with the entire class. This leads to a wider exchange of ideas and learning.
