### Audience Participation for the notes of the Unit 3 - Technical Presentation in the subject of Technical Communication

1.  Asking questions: The presenter should encourage the audience to ask questions during and after the presentation. This helps in clarifying doubts, engaging the audience and receiving feedback. Questions can be asked in between sections or at the end of the presentation. The presenter should be prepared to answer the questions.

2.  Pausing: The presenter can pause in between sections or slides and ask the audience their opinions or experiences related to the topic. This makes the presentation more interactive and helps the presenter understand the audience's perspective better. The pauses should be brief and the presenter should then relate the responses back to the presentation topic.

3.  Quizzing: The presenter can include quizzes or polls in the presentation to check the audience's understanding of the topic. This makes the presentation more engaging and helps identify topics that need more elaboration. The quizzes can be scored or unscored. The results can be displayed and discussed with the audience.

4.  Discussions: After the presentation, the presenter can conduct focused discussions on specific topics or open the floor to general discussions. Discussions help to clarify misunderstandings, gain different perspectives and receive constructive feedback from the audience. The discussion time can be limited to a few minutes to avoid going off-track. The key points from the discussions can be summarized at the end.
