### Speech & Personality

- Speech is a reflection of one's personality. The way a person speaks conveys their traits and characteristics.
- Speech features like rate, pause, pitch, volume, articulation, etc. are closely linked with personality. For example:
- Fast speech rate can indicate urgency or impatience. Slow rate can indicate carefulness or hesitance.
- Frequent pauses can indicate being thoughtful or nervous. Infrequent pauses can indicate being confident or ill-prepared.
- High pitch can indicate excitement or anxiety. Low pitch can indicate relaxation or boredom.
- Loud volume can indicate assertiveness or aggression. Soft volume can indicate shyness or tiredness.
- Clear articulation can indicate confidence or emphasis. Slurred or mumbled speech can indicate lack of preparation or low energy.
- Using the appropriate speech features as per the context and audience can make a good impression and convey the right message. Being aware of one's dominant speech patterns and making conscious efforts to adapt them when needed is important for effective communication.
