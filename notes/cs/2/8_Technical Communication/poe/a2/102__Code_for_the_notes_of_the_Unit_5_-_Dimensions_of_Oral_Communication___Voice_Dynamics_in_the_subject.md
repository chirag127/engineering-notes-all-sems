### Code for the notes of the Unit 5 - Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics in the subject of Technical Communication

1. Oral Communication
- Oral communication refers to the spoken interaction between two or more individuals. It includes face-to-face conversations, speech, telephonic conversations, video conferencing, meetings, etc.
- The key dimensions of oral communication are:
-- Content: The actual message that is being conveyed. It could be ideas, thoughts, feelings, or information.
-- Context: The situation and conditions in which the communication takes place. It includes the place, time, culture, and relationship between the communicators.
-- Process: How the message is transmitted and exchanged between the communicators. It refers to the style, tone, verbal cues, listening, and feedback aspects of the communication.

2. Voice Dynamics
- Voice dynamics refers to the systematic study of the qualities and characteristics of a person's voice.
- The key aspects of voice dynamics are:
-- Pitch: The highness or lowness of a sound. A high pitch indicates excitement or question while a low pitch indicates calmness or emphasis.
-- Volume: The loudness or softness of the voice. A loud volume grabs attention while a soft volume depicts politeness or intimacy.
-- Pace: The speed of speaking. A fast pace shows urgency or enthusiasm while a slow pace shows seriousness or caution.
-- Pausing: Intentional breaks in speech. Pausing is used to give emphasis or allows listeners to reflect on the information.
-- Inflection: The rise and fall of the voice to express emotions or attitudes. Inflection varies based on the intent and context.
