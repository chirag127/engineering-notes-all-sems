### Analysis & Findings for the notes of the Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication

1. Memos: Memos are documents used for internal communication in an organization. They are brief and concise. They get straight to the point and follow a standard structure. They are a quick and informal way of communicating information or requesting action.

2. Proposals: Proposals are documents that offer a solution to a problem or courses of action to achieve a goal. They are persuasive documents that need to demonstrate the benefits of a proposed solution or action. Proposals should be structured in a logical manner and backed by evidence and data.

3. Reports: Reports are documents that present factual information or results of an analysis. They should be straightforward, objective, clear, and concise. Reports follow standard formats with sections including an introduction, methods, results, discussion, and conclusion. Data and evidence are essential components of reports.

4. Presentations: Presentations are oral modes of communication, often supported by visuals like slides. They are a way to convey information to an audience in person. Effective presentations are clear, concise, and logical. Visuals, delivery, and pacing are important aspects of presentations. Presentations can be an opportunity to persuade or engage an audience.
