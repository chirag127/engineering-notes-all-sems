### Technical research Paper writing for the notes of the Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication.

1. Select a focused research topic: Choose a specific and focused topic that can be explored in depth within the required length of the research paper. The topic should not be too broad or too narrow. Some examples could be:
- Effects of technology on communication
- Trends in remote working and collaboration tools
- Data security and privacy practices

2. Review existing research: Review scholarly articles, research reports, and books on your chosen topic. This helps you get an overview of the current state of research and identify any gaps that your work could help address. Note key themes, insights, and unanswered questions that could be explored further.

3. Develop a research question: Formulate 1 or 2 specific research questions your paper will explore. Effective questions should yield meaningful analysis and insights, not just basic information. They help give your paper focus and direction.

4. Outline your paper: Create an outline that includes an introduction, methods (if applicable), results (if applicable), discussion, and conclusion section. The outline gives structure to your paper and helps you stay organized as you research and write. Flesh out each of the sections with details and subsections as needed.

5. Research and write: Conduct additional research to gather evidence and information to support the points in your outline. Write the first draft of your paper, following the standard structure and including in-text citations and a reference list of all sources used. Edit and revise as needed.

6. Proofread and format: Carefully proofread your final draft and correct any errors or typos. Format your paper according to the required style guide (e.g. APA style or MLA style). Get feedback from others if possible and make final improvements.
