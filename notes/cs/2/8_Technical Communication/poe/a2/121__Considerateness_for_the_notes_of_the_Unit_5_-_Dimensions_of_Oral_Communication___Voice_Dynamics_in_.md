### Considerateness for the notes of the Unit 5 - Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics in the subject of Technical Communication

1. Dimensions of Oral Communication
- Content: Having relevant and useful information. Choosing appropriate language and level of detail for the audience.
- Delivery: Using an enthusiastic and engaging tone of voice. Varying speech to keep audience engaged. Using pause and silence effectively.
- Interpersonal Effectiveness: Appropriately acknowledging audience and their needs. Adopting an open and friendly demeanor.

2. Voice Dynamics
- Pitch: The highness or lowness of a sound. Varied pitch helps to convey emphasis and engage the audience.
- Volume: The loudness or softness of a voice. Varied volume highlights important points and keeps audience attentive.
- Rate: The speed of speaking. An appropriate rate allows audience to comprehend the information. Slow down for complex ideas or when audience may be taking notes.
- Pause: Brief silence within a sentence or between sentences. Used to give audience time to absorb information and for speaker to organize thoughts.

The content aims to highlight key considerations for effective oral communication and techniques to dynamically engage an audience. Understanding the elements of vocal variety and purposefully incorporating them serves to strengthen delivery and interpersonal effectiveness.
