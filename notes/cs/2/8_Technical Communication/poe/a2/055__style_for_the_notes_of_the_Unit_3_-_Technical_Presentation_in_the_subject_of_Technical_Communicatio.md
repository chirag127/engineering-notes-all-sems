### Style for the notes of Unit 3 - Technical Presentation

1. Prepare the presentation content:
- Decide the key topics and points to be covered in the presentation.
- Structure the content in a logical flow.
- Include diagrams, illustrations or examples wherever required to explain the concepts.
- Limit the amount of text on each slide and use minimal bullet points.
- Rehearse the presentation multiple times to get comfortable with the flow and time management.

2. Choose an appropriate theme:
- Select a theme that is minimal, professional and complements the presentation topic.
- Use a large, easy to read font like Arial or Calibri.
- Limit the use of colors and use them consistently across slides.
- Apply consistent formatting for titles, headings and bullet points.

3. Design impactful slides:
- Each slide should contain one key message or point.
- Use minimal text and more visuals like graphics, charts, diagrams or images.
- Follow the standard slide layout with titles and footers.
- Apply the theme's colors and fonts consistently.
- Ensure the content is easy to read even from the back of the room.

4. Prepare to present:
- Know the content thoroughly. Practice the presentation multiple times.
- Arrive early and check the presentation equipment before the talk.
- Greet the audience, smile and make eye contact.
- Speak slowly and clearly. Vary your tone to keep the audience engaged.
- Highlight key points and use hand gestures appropriately to emphasize important content.
- Leave time for questions and discussions.
