### Interpersonal

- Eye contact: Make eye contact with the audience while presenting to connect with them and appear confident. Do not stare or make people uncomfortable.
- Body language: Use appropriate hand gestures to emphasize important points. Stand/move around naturally and do not appear stiff. Maintain an open and engaging posture.
- Tone: Vary your tone to keep the audience engaged. Speak clearly and with energy/enthusiasm to keep the presentation interesting while maintaining a professional demeanor.
- Pauses: Include occasional pauses for effect. Pauses allow important points to sink in and give the audience time to reflect or take notes. They also prevent the presentation from sounding like a constant stream of talking.
- Questions: Anticipate common questions and address them in the presentation. Leave time at the end for the audience to ask questions. Listen carefully and respond appropriately.
- Dress: Dress in professional, formal business attire. Your appearance affects your credibility and the audience's engagement.

Additional tips:
- Practice your presentation out loud, ideally in front of others. Hearing yourself speak the words makes a significant difference compared to just reading silently.
- Know your material inside and out. If you are well versed in the topic, you will sound more confident when presenting and answering questions.
- Be authentic and let your personality shine through. An engaging presentation is genuine and connects the topic to you as the speaker.
- Have fun! If you are enjoying the presentation process, your enthusiasm will show through to the audience.
