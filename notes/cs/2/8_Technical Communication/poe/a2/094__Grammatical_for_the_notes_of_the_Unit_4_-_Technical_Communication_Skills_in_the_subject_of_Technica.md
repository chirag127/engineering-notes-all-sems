### Grammatical for the notes of the Unit 4 - Technical Communication Skills

1. Subject-Verb Agreement: The verb must agree with the subject in number (singular or plural). Example: The students (subject) were (verb) attentive.

2. Consistency: Maintain consistency in tone, formatting, punctuation, capitalization, etc. throughout the document. Example: Use a formal tone and active voice consistently across the document.

3. Parallelism: Use parallel structure for list items, headings, and phrases. Example: To install the software, (1) download the installer, (2) run the installer, and (3) follow the on-screen prompts.

4. Concision: Remove unnecessary words and be concise. Example: Instead of saying "the reason is because," say "because."

5. Variety: Use a variety of sentence structures and lengths for an engaging writing style. Vary sentence openings to avoid repetition. Example: Mix simple, compound, and complex sentences. Open sentences in different ways (with phrases or dependent/independent clauses).

6. Transition Words: Use transition words or phrases to connect sentences and sections. They enhance flow and readability. Example: Therefore, Consequently, Additionally, etc.

7. Active Voice: Use active voice as much as possible for a strong, compelling writing style. In active voice, the subject performs the action. Example: I completed the work (active) vs The work was completed by me (passive).

8. Clarity: Be clear and straightforward. Avoid unnecessary complexity, ambiguity, and obscurity. Example: Precise language, simple words, minimal jargon.
