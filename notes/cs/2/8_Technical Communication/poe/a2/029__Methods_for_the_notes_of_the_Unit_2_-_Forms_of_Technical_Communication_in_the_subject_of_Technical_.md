### Methods for the notes of the Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication

1. Emails
- Used for quick communication
- Informal in tone
- Includes a subject line, salutation, message body, and closing
- Should be concise and to the point

2. Memos
- Used for internal communication in an organization
- Semi-formal in tone
- Includes a header with To, From, Date, and Subject
- Uses a direct writing style with clear and concise language

3. Letters
- Used for external communication
- Formal in tone based on the recipient
- Includes a header with sender and recipient information
- Uses a direct yet polite and professional writing style

4. Reports
- Used to convey technical information or the results of an analysis or study
- Varies in formality based on audience and purpose
- Includes an abstract or executive summary, introduction, methods, results, discussion, and conclusion sections
- Uses a structured writing style with technical language and data/evidence to support key points

5. Proposals
- Used to propose a solution to a problem or suggest an idea or course of action
- Varies in formality based on audience and purpose
- Includes sections such as introduction, problem statement, objectives, methods, evaluation, budget, timeline, and conclusion
- Uses a persuasive writing style with a clear explanation of the issue and compelling arguments to convince the reader to take action
