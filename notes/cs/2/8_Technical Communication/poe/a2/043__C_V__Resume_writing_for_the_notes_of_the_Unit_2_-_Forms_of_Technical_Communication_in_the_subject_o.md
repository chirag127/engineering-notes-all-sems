### C.V./Resume writing for the notes of the Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication

1. A resume is a brief, written summary of your qualifications, skills, and accomplishments. It is a persuasive document meant to demonstrate your value as a candidate for a job.
2. Aim for one page if you have less than 10 years of experience, or 2 pages if you have more experience.
3. Focus on relevant work experience and education. Include the companies you worked for, dates employed, job titles, and bullet points highlighting your key accomplishments and qualifications.
4. List your education along with relevant coursework, projects, or awards. Include your GPA if it was above a 3.0.
5. Include relevant skills such as languages, software, machinery, etc. Add a separate skills section or include key skills in your experience descriptions.
6. Include volunteering or leadership experience if relevant to the job. These show your interests and character.
7. Check carefully for any spelling or grammar errors. Have a friend or family member review your resume as well.
8. You can modify your resume for different positions you apply to. Tailor your resume to the specific job requirements.
9. Keep your resume concise and focused. Avoid including personal details or information not related to the job goal.
