### Dimensions of Communication

1. Verbal Communication: Communication through spoken words. Includes face-to-face conversations, telephone calls, voicemails, etc.
2. Nonverbal Communication: Communication without words. Includes body language, facial expressions, tone of voice, etc.
3. Written Communication: Communication through written words. Includes letters, emails, reports, etc.
4. Visual Communication: Communication through visual aids. Includes presentations, infographics, illustrations, etc.

These different dimensions of communication are used together in technical communication to effectively convey a message to the intended audience. Choosing the appropriate dimensions and combining them strategically helps in efficient communication.
