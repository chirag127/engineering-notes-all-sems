### Strategies for the notes of the Unit 3 - Technical Presentation in the subject of Technical Communication.

1. Prepare the presentation content:
- Understand the topic and the objective of the presentation thoroughly.
- Research the topic and gather relevant data and information.
- Structure the content in a logical flow with an introduction, body and conclusion.
- Include examples and stories to make the presentation engaging.
- Use simple and easy to understand language.
- Include visual aids like images, graphs, charts, videos, etc. to enhance understanding.

2. Prepare the presentation design:
- Choose an attractive and minimal theme for the slides.
- Use large, easy to read fonts with minimal text on each slide.
- Include blank spaces and avoid clutter.
- Use a neat slide layout and be consistent across slides.
- Include themes, colors and effects to make the presentation visually compelling.

3. Prepare to present:
- Practice the presentation multiple times in front of a mirror or to friends and family.
- Learn the content and timings thoroughly to speak confidently.
- Start with a smile and enthusiastic tone to grab attention.
- Use hand gestures to engage the audience but do not overdo them.
- Make eye contact with the audience and pause after asking questions.
- End on a high note to leave a lasting impact.
