### Draft for the notes of the Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication

1.  Reports
    - Formal documents written to convey specific information or present findings and recommendations.
    - Usually include an introduction, a body with relevant facts and figures, and a conclusion with recommendations.
    - Common types: Progress reports, feasibility reports, compliance reports, etc.
2.  Proposals
    - Formal offers to undertake a project or task.
    - Convince the reader to accept your proposal by highlighting the need, your solution, and a project timeline and budget.
    - Common types: Business proposals, project proposals, research proposals, etc.
3.  Instructions
    - Explain how to do something in a sequence of steps.
    - Present information clearly and concisely. Use illustrations and examples when needed.
    - Common types: Assembly instructions, operation manuals, recipes, etc.
4.  Presentations
    - Verbal communication of information to a live audience.
    - Combine visual aids like slides, handouts, and props with a clear speech or lecture to engage the audience.
    - Types: PowerPoint presentations, lectures, speeches, etc.
