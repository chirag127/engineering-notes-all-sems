### Forms of Technical Communication

1.  Manuals: Step-by-step instructions to perform a task, operate a machine or system. Includes illustrations and screenshots.Written in a simple and clear style.
2.  Reports: Presentation of findings from an investigation or study. Includes an introduction, background, methods, results, discussion, and conclusion sections. Data and evidence are analyzed and presented in a logical flow.
3.  Proposals: Requests for resources or approvals to address an identified need. Includes a description of the issue, goals, objectives, methods, budget, timeline, and evaluation plans. Written persuasively to convince the reader of the merits and feasibility.
4.  Emails/Memos: Short textual communication for a specific purpose. Should have a clear subject line, opening, content, and closing. Keep concise while maintaining politeness and clarity.
5.  Presentations: Oral delivery of information, often supported by visual slides, handouts, or props. Structure content logically, vary tone, and use design elements to effectively convey key messages to an audience. Practice extensively beforehand.
