### The flow of Communication for the notes of the Unit 1 - Fundamentals of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication

1. Sender: The person who generates and sends the message.
2. Encoding: The process of converting thoughts into a communicable form (written, oral, visual, etc.).
3. Message: The communication that is sent (written document, speech, video, etc.).
4. Medium: The means through which the message is delivered (email, video call, letter, etc.).
5. Decoding: The process of interpreting the message by the receiver.
6. Receiver: The person who receives the message.
7. Feedback: The receiver's response to the message. This feedback continues the communication process.

The flow of communication is essential to ensure that the intended message is received and understood by the intended audience. An effective technical communicator must be aware of and manage each component of the communication process to achieve desired goals.
