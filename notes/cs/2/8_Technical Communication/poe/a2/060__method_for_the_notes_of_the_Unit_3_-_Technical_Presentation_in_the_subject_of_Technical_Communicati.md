### Method for the notes of the Unit 3 - Technical Presentation in the subject of Technical Communication:

- Explain what is Technical Presentation.
- Discuss the importance of Technical Presentation in professional world.
- List down the essential skills required for Technical Presentation like subject knowledge, communication skills, time management, etc.

2. Preparation for Technical Presentation
- Choose an appropriate topic which you are comfortable with and have good knowledge of.
- Do thorough research on the topic and prepare the content.
- Structure the content in a logical flow with headings and subheadings.
- Prepare slides or other presentation aids if required.
- Practice the presentation several times to boost confidence and manage time.

3. Elements of a good Technical Presentation
- Have a clear theme or message.
- Start with an outline of the structure.
- Use simple and easy to understand language.
- Include graphs, diagrams, images or videos if suitable to make the presentation more engaging and interesting.
- Highlight key points and important takeaways.
- Keep the presentation concise and within the specified time limit.

4. Tips for effective Technical Presentation delivery
- Dress formally and be well-groomed.
- Have good body language like eye contact, open posture, hand gestures, etc.
- Speak clearly and with confidence.
- Use pauses and variations in tone to keep the audience engaged.
- Be open to questions and discussions.
