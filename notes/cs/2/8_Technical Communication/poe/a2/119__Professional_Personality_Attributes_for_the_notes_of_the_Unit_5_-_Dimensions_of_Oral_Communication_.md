### Professional Personality Attributes

1. Confidence: Having confidence in one's own skills and abilities. Speaking with confidence can help win the trust and respect of others.
2. Positivity: Having an optimistic outlook and displaying enthusiasm. A positive attitude can help engage audiences and bring energy to a talk.
3. Humility: Showing humility and recognizing one's own weaknesses or limitations. Being humble helps appear credible and relatable to audiences.
4. Passion: Demonstrating passion or excitement for one's subject matter or work. Speaking with passion can help draw audiences in and keep their interest.
5. Sense of humor: Incorporating humor and wit appropriately can help connect with audiences and make information more engaging, memorable, and shareable. However, humor should be used judiciously based on audience and context.
6. Authenticity: Coming across as genuine, sincere, and authentic. Audiences will appreciate a speaker who is being their true self.

The points describe professional personality attributes relevant for oral communication to appear credible and engaging. The attributes can help win trust, draw interest, connect with audiences, and make information memorable. However, appropriateness based on context should be considered, especially for humor.
