 Here are the notes on Speaking with a purpose for Unit 5 - Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics in Technical Communication:

### Speaking with a purpose

1. Inform: Sharing information, educating the audience on a topic. For example, a technical presentation informing users about a new product.
2. Persuade: Convincing the audience to take a particular action or accept a specific view or idea. For example, persuading management to invest in a new initiative.
3. Entertain: Amusing, interesting and engaging the audience. For example, a motivational speech to inspire and entertain employees.
4. Build goodwill: Creating a positive impression and strengthening relationships. For example, a thank you speech to show appreciation to business partners.

To achieve the purpose effectively:

- Clearly state the purpose at the beginning
- Structure the content logically to support the purpose
- Use an appropriate tone, style and language for the purpose
- Provide sufficient evidence and examples to substantiate points
- Call the audience to action if persuading or inspiring them

### Dimensions of Oral Communication

1. Physical: Volume, pace, pitch, pauses, etc.
2. Visual: Facial expressions, hand gestures, body language, appearance, etc.
3. Verbal: Word choice, grammar, articulation, pronunciation, etc.

Using the dimensions effectively:

- Project confidence through erect posture and firm voice
- Engage the audience using varied tone and facial/hand expressions
- Speak clearly and with appropriate pace/pauses for comprehension
- Use simple and concise language
- Ensure visual aids are clear and aid understanding
