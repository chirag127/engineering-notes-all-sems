
### Technical Report for the notes of the Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication

1. Technical reports are written documents that provide detailed information about a particular topic or subject. They are typically used to communicate technical information to other specialists or to the general public. 
2. Technical reports can be used for a variety of purposes, including but not limited to: 
    * Describing the results of an experiment or research project 
    * Explaining the design and operation of a system 
    * Evaluating the performance of a product or process 
    * Summarizing the findings of a survey or study 
3. Technical reports typically include the following components: 
    * Introduction 
    * Background information 
    * Methodology 
    * Results 
    * Discussion 
    * Conclusion 
4. Technical reports are often used to communicate technical information to other specialists or to the general public. They can also be used to communicate the results of research and experiments, as well as to evaluate the performance of products and processes. 
5. Technical reports should be written in a clear and concise manner, and should be organized in a logical manner. They should also include relevant diagrams, charts, and tables, as well as references to the sources of information used.
