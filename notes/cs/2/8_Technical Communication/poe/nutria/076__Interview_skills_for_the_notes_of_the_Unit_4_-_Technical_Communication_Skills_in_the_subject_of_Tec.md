
### Interview Skills

1. Research the company and the position you are applying for. Being knowledgeable about the company and the role you are applying for will help you to answer questions more confidently and make a good impression.

2. Prepare answers to common interview questions. Think about the most common questions asked in interviews and prepare answers that showcase your skills and experience.

3. Practice your answers. It can be helpful to practice your answers with a friend or family member. This will help you to become more comfortable with the questions and will help you to think on your feet during the interview.

4. Dress appropriately. Make sure to dress in a way that is appropriate for the job you are applying for.

5. Be confident. Even if you are feeling nervous, try to remain confident and remember that you are well-prepared for the interview.

6. Ask questions. Asking questions during the interview shows that you are engaged and interested in the role.

7. Follow up. After the interview, make sure to follow up with a thank you email or letter. This will show your interest in the role and will help you to stand out from other applicants.
