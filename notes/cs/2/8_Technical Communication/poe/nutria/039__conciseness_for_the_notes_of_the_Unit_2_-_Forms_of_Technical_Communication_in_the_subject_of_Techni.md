
### Conciseness

* Conciseness is a key element of effective technical communication. 
* Technical writers should strive to communicate their message in the fewest words possible. 
* Concise writing is direct and to the point, avoiding unnecessary words and phrases.
* Concise writing is also free of jargon and technical terms, unless they are necessary to convey the message.
* Concise writing should be easy to understand and follow, with clear and concise sentences. 
* Concise writing should also be organized in a logical order, with the most important information first. 
* Finally, concise writing should be free of errors in grammar, spelling, punctuation, and formatting.
