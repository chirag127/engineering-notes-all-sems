
### Dimensions of Communication

1. Verbal Communication: Verbal communication involves the use of spoken language to convey messages. It is the most common and direct form of communication.

2. Nonverbal Communication: Nonverbal communication involves the use of body language, gestures, facial expressions, eye contact, and other physical signs to convey messages. It is an important form of communication and can be used to supplement verbal communication.

3. Written Communication: Written communication involves the use of written words to convey messages. It is a formal form of communication and is often used in technical communication.

4. Visual Communication: Visual communication involves the use of images, diagrams, and other visuals to convey messages. It is an effective way to communicate complex information quickly and effectively.
