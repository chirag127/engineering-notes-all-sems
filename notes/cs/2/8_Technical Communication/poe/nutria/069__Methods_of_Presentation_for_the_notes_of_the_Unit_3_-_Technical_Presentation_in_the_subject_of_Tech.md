
### Methods of Presentation for the notes of the Unit 3 - Technical Presentation in the subject of Technical Communication

1. Oral Presentation: This is the most common form of presentation, in which the presenter speaks to the audience, often accompanied by visual aids such as slides, videos, or other media.
2. Written Presentation: Written presentations are typically used to present information in a more permanent form. This can be done through reports, brochures, articles, or other written documents.
3. Visual Presentation: Visual presentations are used to communicate information through visuals such as charts, diagrams, photographs, videos, or other visual media.
4. Multimedia Presentation: Multimedia presentations are often used to combine multiple types of media, such as text, audio, video, and images, to create a more engaging presentation.
5. Digital Presentation: Digital presentations are presentations that are presented through digital media, such as websites, social media, or other online platforms.
