

## Unit 1 - Fundamentals of Technical Communication

1. Technical communication is the process of sharing information among people who have a common interest. It is used to convey complex information in a clear, concise and easily understandable manner. 
2. Technical communication is often used in a professional setting, such as in engineering, software development, and product design. 
3. It is also used in the classroom to teach students about a particular subject. 
4. Technical communication involves the use of specialized language, diagrams, charts, and other visuals to convey information. 
5. Good technical communication requires an understanding of the audience, the purpose of the communication, and the context of the communication. 
6. It also requires clear, concise, and accurate writing that is tailored to the audience. 
7. Technical communication is used to explain complex concepts, convey instructions, and provide feedback. 
8. It can also be used to create documents, such as manuals, reports, and presentations. 
9. Technical communicators must be able to effectively communicate their message to the audience and ensure that the message is understood.
