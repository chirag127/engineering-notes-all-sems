
### Analysis & Findings for the notes of the Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication

1. Technical communication is the process of conveying information about a product, service or process to a target audience. It is used to inform, instruct, and persuade.
2. Technical communication is used in a variety of contexts, including business, engineering, medicine, law, and science.
3. Technical communication is a specialized form of communication that requires knowledge of the subject matter as well as the ability to communicate effectively.
4. Technical communication is used to convey complex information in a clear and concise manner.
5. Technical communication requires the use of specialized language, visuals, and other media to effectively convey the message.
6. Technical communication is used to create documents such as user manuals, instruction guides, and reference materials.
7. Technical communication is also used to create reports, proposals, and presentations.
8. Technical communication should be accurate, clear, concise, and well-organized.
9. Technical communication should also be tailored to the target audience and written in a style that is appropriate for the situation.
