
### Audience Analysis for the Notes of Unit 3 - Technical Presentation in Technical Communication

1. Audience analysis is the process of identifying and understanding the characteristics of a group of people who will be the intended audience for a technical presentation. 
2. It is important to consider the audience's needs, interests, and background knowledge when preparing a technical presentation. 
3. It is also important to consider the audience's demographic characteristics, such as age, gender, educational level, and cultural background.
4. Audience analysis can help the presenter to tailor the technical presentation to the audience's needs and interests. 
5. It can also help the presenter to anticipate the audience's questions and objections. 
6. Audience analysis can also be used to determine the most effective communication strategies to use in a technical presentation. 
7. For example, a presenter can use visual aids, such as diagrams and charts, to illustrate complex concepts for a technical audience. 
8. Audience analysis can also help the presenter to determine the best delivery style for a technical presentation. 
9. For example, a presenter can use a conversational style to engage a more informal audience, or a formal style to engage a more formal audience. 
10. Audience analysis can also help the presenter to determine the most appropriate language to use in a technical presentation. 
11. For example, a presenter can use technical terms and jargon to engage a technical audience, or use simpler language to engage a more general audience.
