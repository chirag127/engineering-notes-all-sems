
### Individual conferencing for the notes of the Unit 3 - Technical Presentation in the subject of Technical Communication

* Individual conferencing is a method of providing feedback on a student's work. It is a one-on-one conversation between a student and a teacher or professor. 
* During individual conferencing, the student will present their work and the teacher will provide constructive feedback. 
* The goal of individual conferencing is to help the student improve their work. It is an opportunity for the student to get personalized feedback and to ask questions. 
* During the individual conferencing session, the teacher will provide feedback on the student's work. The teacher may suggest changes or provide tips on how to improve the work. 
* The student should take notes during the session so that they can refer back to the feedback later. It is also important for the student to ask questions during the session in order to better understand the feedback.
