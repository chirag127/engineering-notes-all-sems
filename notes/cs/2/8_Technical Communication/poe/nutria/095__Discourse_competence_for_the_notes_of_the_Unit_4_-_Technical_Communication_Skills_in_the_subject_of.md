
### Discourse competence for the notes of the Unit 4 - Technical Communication Skills in the subject of Technical Communication

1. Discourse competence is the ability to communicate effectively in a variety of contexts. It involves the use of language and other forms of communication to convey ideas, thoughts and feelings.

2. Technical communication is the use of language and other forms of communication to convey technical information. It involves the use of specialized vocabulary, writing techniques and formatting to ensure that the intended message is accurately conveyed.

3. Discourse competence in technical communication requires an understanding of the language and its conventions. This includes an understanding of the structure of the language, its grammar, its spelling and its punctuation.

4. In addition, discourse competence in technical communication requires an understanding of the conventions of technical writing. This includes an understanding of the purpose of the document, the audience for whom it is intended, the format of the document and the conventions of technical writing.

5. Discourse competence in technical communication also requires an understanding of the conventions of communication. This includes an understanding of the different forms of communication, the different methods of communication and the different conventions of communication.

6. Finally, discourse competence in technical communication requires an understanding of the conventions of communication in a variety of contexts. This includes an understanding of the conventions of communication in different cultures, different countries and different industries.
