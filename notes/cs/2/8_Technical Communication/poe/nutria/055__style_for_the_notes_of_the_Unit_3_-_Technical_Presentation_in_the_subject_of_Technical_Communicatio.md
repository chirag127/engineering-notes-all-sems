
### Unit 3 - Technical Presentation in Technical Communication

1. Technical presentations are used to communicate complex information in an organized and concise manner.
2. Presentations should be tailored to the audience and should include visuals, such as charts and diagrams, to help explain the material.
3. Presenters should practice their delivery and use techniques, such as eye contact and body language, to engage the audience.
4. Presentations should be structured in a logical order and should include an introduction, body, and conclusion.
5. Presenters should be prepared to answer questions from the audience and should have a backup plan in case of technical difficulties.
6. Presenters should have a clear goal in mind and should strive to communicate the information in an effective manner.
