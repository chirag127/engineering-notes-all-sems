
### Code for the notes of the Unit 5 - Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics in the subject of Technical Communication

1. Oral communication is a form of communication that is conducted through spoken words. It is an important tool for conveying information, expressing ideas and feelings, and establishing relationships with others.

2. Voice dynamics is the study of how our voices are used to convey messages. It involves understanding the different qualities of our voices, such as volume, pitch, speed, and tone.

3. Technical communication is the process of conveying technical information in a clear and concise manner. It involves the use of language, visuals, and other media to effectively communicate technical information.

4. In technical communication, voice dynamics are important for conveying the information in a clear and understandable way. Voice dynamics can be used to emphasize key points, to draw attention to important information, and to make the information easier to understand.

5. Good voice dynamics can help to create an engaging and effective presentation. It is important to use the right tone and volume when speaking, as well as to ensure that the words are clear and easily understood.

6. It is also important to use appropriate pauses and inflection to keep the listener's attention and to emphasize important points. Additionally, speaking at a consistent volume and pace helps to keep the listener's attention and to ensure that the information is understood.
