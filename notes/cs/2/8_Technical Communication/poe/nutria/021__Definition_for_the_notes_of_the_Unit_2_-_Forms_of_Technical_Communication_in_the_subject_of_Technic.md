
### Definition for the Notes of the Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication

* Technical Communication: Technical Communication is a form of communication that focuses on conveying technical information to a target audience. It includes a wide range of topics, such as engineering, computer programming, and software development.

* Technical Writing: Technical Writing is the practice of writing documents that explain how to use or understand a product or service. It is a form of technical communication that is used to explain complex topics in an easy-to-understand manner.

* Technical Documentation: Technical Documentation is a form of technical communication that provides information about a product or service. It is usually written in a format that is easy to understand and includes diagrams, illustrations, and other visuals to help explain the topic.

* Technical Presentations: Technical Presentations are a form of technical communication that involves presenting technical information to an audience. It is used to educate and inform the audience about a specific topic.

* Technical Reports: Technical Reports are a form of technical communication that provide detailed information about a topic. They are usually written in a formal format and include data, analysis, and conclusions.
