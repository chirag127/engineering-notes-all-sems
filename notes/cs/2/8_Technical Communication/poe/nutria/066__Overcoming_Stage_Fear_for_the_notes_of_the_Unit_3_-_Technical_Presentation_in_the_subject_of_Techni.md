
### Overcoming Stage Fear

1. Understand the source of fear: Fear of public speaking often stems from a fear of being judged or perceived as inadequate. It is important to recognize that this fear is often irrational and unfounded.

2. Practice: Practicing your presentation in front of a mirror, or with friends and family, is a great way to gain confidence in your ability to deliver a successful presentation.

3. Prepare: Preparation is key to overcoming fear. Familiarise yourself with the content of your presentation, the audience, and the venue.

4. Visualize success: Visualizing yourself delivering a successful presentation can help to reduce your anxiety and boost your confidence.

5. Take deep breaths: Taking deep breaths can help to calm your nerves and reduce stress.

6. Focus on the audience: Focusing on the audience and connecting with them can help to reduce your fear.

7. Have a positive attitude: Having a positive attitude and believing in yourself can help to reduce your fear.

8. Use humour: Humour can be a great way to engage with an audience and reduce your fear.

9. Don’t be too hard on yourself: It is important to remember that mistakes happen and that it is OK to make mistakes.

10. Relax: Relaxing and taking time to enjoy the experience can help to reduce your fear.
