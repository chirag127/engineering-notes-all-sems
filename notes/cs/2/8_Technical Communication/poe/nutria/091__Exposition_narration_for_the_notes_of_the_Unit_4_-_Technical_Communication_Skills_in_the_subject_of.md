
### Exposition Narration for the Notes of the Unit 4 - Technical Communication Skills in the Subject of Technical Communication

1. Exposition narration is a type of writing that is used to explain a concept, process, or idea in a clear and concise manner. 
2. It is often used in technical writing to explain complex concepts or processes in an easy-to-understand way.
3. Exposition narration should be written in a way that is both informative and engaging.
4. It should be written in a clear and logical order, with each point leading logically to the next.
5. The language used should be appropriate for the audience and the purpose of the writing.
6. It should be written in a way that is accessible to all readers, regardless of their level of technical knowledge.
7. To ensure that the exposition narration is effective, it is important to use examples and visuals to illustrate the points being made.
8. It is also important to use appropriate formatting and structure, such as headings, bullet points, and tables.
9. Finally, it is important to ensure that the exposition narration is concise and to the point, avoiding unnecessary details and explanations.
