

## Unit 4 - Technical Communication Skills

1. Technical communication is the process of exchanging information between two or more people in order to achieve a desired outcome. It is an essential skill in the workplace, as it allows people to communicate effectively and efficiently.

2. Technical communication involves the use of written, verbal, visual, and digital communication methods. Written communication includes emails, memos, reports, and other documents. Verbal communication includes face-to-face conversations, phone conversations, and presentations. Visual communication includes diagrams, charts, and photographs. Digital communication includes websites, social media, and other digital platforms.

3. Technical communication requires an understanding of the audience, purpose, and context of the communication. Knowing the audience helps to tailor the message to the appropriate level of detail. Understanding the purpose allows the communicator to focus the message on the desired outcome. Considering the context helps to ensure that the message is appropriate for the situation.

4. Technical communication also requires an understanding of the language used in the communication. Technical language should be used when appropriate, but it should also be used in a way that is clear and understandable to the audience.

5. Good technical communication also requires the ability to listen. Listening actively and carefully helps to ensure that the message is understood and that any questions are answered.

6. Finally, good technical communication requires the ability to use feedback. Feedback from the audience can help to refine the message and ensure that it is understood.
