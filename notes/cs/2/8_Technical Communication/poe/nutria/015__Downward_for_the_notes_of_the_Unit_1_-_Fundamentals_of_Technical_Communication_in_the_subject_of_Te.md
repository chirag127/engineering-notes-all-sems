
### Downward for the notes of the Unit 1 - Fundamentals of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication

1. Technical communication is the process of sharing information and knowledge among people within an organization or with external stakeholders. It is the exchange of information, ideas, and opinions among individuals in order to reach a common goal. 

2. Technical communication can take many forms, including written documents, presentations, videos, and other media. It is important to understand the audience, purpose, and context of the communication in order to effectively convey the message. 

3. Technical communication should be clear, concise, and organized. It should also be free of jargon and technical terms that may be difficult for the audience to understand. 

4. Technical communication should be tailored to the audience. This includes considering age, education level, language, and cultural background. 

5. Technical communication should be accurate, objective, and unbiased. It should also use appropriate language and tone. 

6. Technical communication should be designed to be accessible and understandable to all audiences. This includes using visuals and other media to support the message. 

7. Technical communication should be reviewed for accuracy and clarity before it is shared with the audience. This includes having other individuals review the document or presentation for any potential errors.
