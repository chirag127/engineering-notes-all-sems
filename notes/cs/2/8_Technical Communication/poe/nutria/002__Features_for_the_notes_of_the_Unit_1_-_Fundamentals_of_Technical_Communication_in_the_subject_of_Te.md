
### Features for the Notes of the Unit 1 - Fundamentals of Technical Communication in the Subject of Technical Communication

1. Understanding of technical communication principles: Technical communication involves the use of specialized language and concepts to convey information to a specific audience.
2. Ability to use the appropriate style and format for the audience: Technical communication must be tailored to the needs of the audience.
3. Knowledge of the various tools used in technical communication: Technical communication often involves the use of various tools, such as diagrams, flowcharts, and tables.
4. Understanding of the importance of accuracy and precision: Technical communication must be accurate and precise in order to be effective.
5. Ability to use appropriate visual aids: Visual aids can be used to enhance the understanding of technical topics.
6. Understanding of the importance of clarity: Technical communication must be clear and concise in order to be effective.
7. Ability to use appropriate technical language: Technical language must be used appropriately in order to be understood by the intended audience.
8. Understanding of the importance of ethics in technical communication: Ethical considerations must be taken into account when creating technical documents.
