
### Interjections for the Notes of Unit 3 - Technical Presentation in the Subject of Technical Communication

* Interjections are words or phrases that express emotion or surprise. They are often used to add emphasis to a statement or to express a feeling.
* Interjections can be used to show excitement, surprise, or enthusiasm. For example, "Wow!" or "Yay!"
* Interjections can also be used to show annoyance, disbelief, or disapproval. For example, "Ugh!" or "Oh no!"
* Interjections can be used to add emphasis to a statement or to emphasize a point. For example, "Seriously?" or "Absolutely!"
* Interjections can also be used to show politeness or respect. For example, "Please" or "Thank you".
* Interjections can be used to show agreement or understanding. For example, "Ah" or "I see".
* Interjections can also be used to show a lack of understanding or confusion. For example, "Huh?" or "What?".
