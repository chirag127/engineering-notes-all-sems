
### Public Speaking for the Notes of Unit 3 - Technical Presentation in Technical Communication

1. Public speaking is an important skill for many professions, including technical communication.
2. Speak clearly and confidently in order to convey the message effectively.
3. Use appropriate body language and gestures to emphasize points and maintain audience attention.
4. Make sure to practice the presentation beforehand to ensure accuracy and clarity.
5. Use visual aids, such as slides or diagrams, to help illustrate points and keep the audience engaged.
6. Encourage audience participation by asking questions and allowing time for discussion.
7. Respond to questions and comments from the audience in a polite and professional manner.
8. Be prepared for any technical issues that may arise and have a backup plan in place.
9. Conclude the presentation by summarizing the main points and thanking the audience for their attention.
