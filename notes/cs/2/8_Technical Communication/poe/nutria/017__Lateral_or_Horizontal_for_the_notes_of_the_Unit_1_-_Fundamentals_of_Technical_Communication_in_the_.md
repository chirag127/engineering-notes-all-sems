
### Lateral or Horizontal for the notes of the Unit 1 - Fundamentals of Technical Communication

1. Lateral communication is the exchange of information between two or more people who are at the same hierarchical level in an organization.
2. Horizontal communication is the exchange of information between two or more people who are at different hierarchical levels in an organization.
3. Lateral communication is often used to share ideas, build relationships, and collaborate on projects.
4. Horizontal communication is often used to provide information, give feedback, and coordinate activities.
5. Both lateral and horizontal communication are essential for effective communication in any organization.
6. It is important to remember that communication should flow both ways, up and down the hierarchy.
7. Effective communication within an organization is essential for its success.
