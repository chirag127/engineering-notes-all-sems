
### Unit 1 - Fundamentals of Technical Communication

1. Technical communication is the process of conveying information in a clear and concise manner to an audience of experts or non-experts.

2. Technical communication is used in a variety of contexts and industries, including engineering, software development, medicine, and business.

3. Technical communication involves the use of specialized language, diagrams, visuals, and other tools to effectively communicate complex information.

4. Technical communicators must be knowledgeable about the subject matter they are communicating and must be able to tailor their message to the audience they are addressing.

5. Technical communicators must also be aware of the legal and ethical implications of their work, particularly when dealing with sensitive or confidential information.
