
### Interpersonal for the Notes of Unit 3 - Technical Presentation in Technical Communication

1. Interpersonal communication is the process of exchanging information between two or more people. It is an essential part of any successful business and can help to build relationships, foster understanding, and create a positive work environment.

2. When giving a technical presentation, it is important to be aware of the interpersonal aspects of communication. This includes nonverbal communication such as body language, facial expressions, and eye contact. It is also important to consider the tone of voice and the words used.

3. It is important to be aware of the audience's needs and expectations. This will help to ensure that the presentation is tailored to the audience's level of understanding and that the message is clear and concise.

4. It is also important to consider the audience's cultural background and to be respectful of any differences in opinion. This will help to foster understanding and create a positive atmosphere.

5. Finally, it is important to be confident and to project a positive attitude. This will help to create an atmosphere of trust and understanding, which will make the presentation more successful.
