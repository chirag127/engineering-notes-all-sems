
### Solution of communication problems with verbal and non verbal means

1. Verbal communication involves the use of words and language to convey a message. It can be used in both written and spoken form.

2. Non-verbal communication involves the use of gestures, body language, facial expressions, and other visual cues to convey a message.

3. Effective communication requires the use of both verbal and non-verbal communication to ensure that the message is properly understood by the receiver.

4. It is important to be aware of cultural differences and to take into account the context of the communication when determining the most appropriate form of communication.

5. It is also important to be aware of the tone and volume of one's voice when communicating verbally, as this can have a significant impact on the way in which the message is received.

6. In some cases, it may be necessary to use a combination of verbal and non-verbal communication to ensure that the message is properly understood.
