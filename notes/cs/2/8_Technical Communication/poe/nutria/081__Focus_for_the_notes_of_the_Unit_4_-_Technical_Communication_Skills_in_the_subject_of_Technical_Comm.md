

### Focus for the notes of the Unit 4 - Technical Communication Skills in the subject of Technical Communication

1. Understand the purpose of technical communication and the different types of technical communication.
2. Learn the different stages of the technical communication process and how to develop the technical communication materials.
3. Learn the principles of technical writing, including clarity, conciseness, accuracy, and organization.
4. Understand the importance of audience analysis and how to tailor your message to the appropriate audience.
5. Learn the basics of visual design, including the use of images, tables, and graphs.
6. Develop the skills to create effective technical documents, including instructions, reports, proposals, and presentations.
7. Learn how to use technology to create and deliver technical communication materials.
8. Understand the ethical considerations of technical communication.
