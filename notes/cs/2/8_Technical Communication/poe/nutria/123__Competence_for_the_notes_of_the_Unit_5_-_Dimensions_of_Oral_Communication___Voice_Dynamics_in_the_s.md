
### Competence for the notes of the Unit 5 - Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics in the subject of Technical Communication

1. Oral communication is the exchange of information between two or more people using verbal and nonverbal cues. It allows people to express their ideas, feelings, and opinions in a clear and concise manner.

2. Voice dynamics refer to the use of vocal inflection, volume, and pace to convey meaning in speech. It is an important tool for effective communication, as it can help to emphasize certain points and create a more engaging experience for the listener.

3. Listening is an important part of oral communication, as it allows the speaker to understand the other person’s point of view and respond accordingly. It is also important to be aware of nonverbal cues, such as body language, facial expressions, and eye contact, which can provide additional information about the speaker’s message.

4. It is important to be aware of cultural differences when engaging in oral communication, as different cultures have different expectations and norms when it comes to communication. It is important to be aware of these differences in order to ensure that the message is communicated effectively.

5. Voice dynamics can also be used to create a more engaging experience for the listener. For example, using a variety of vocal inflection, volume, and pace can help to emphasize certain points and make the message more memorable.

6. It is important to be aware of the audience when engaging in oral communication, as different audiences may have different expectations and needs. It is important to tailor the message to the audience in order to ensure that it is received in the way that it was intended.
