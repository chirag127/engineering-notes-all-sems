
### Objective for the Notes of the Unit 4 - Technical Communication Skills in the Subject of Technical Communication

1. Develop an understanding of the fundamentals of technical communication, including the purpose, audience, and context of technical documents.
2. Learn to develop and use effective technical communication strategies.
3. Understand the principles of effective document design, including typography, layout, and visuals.
4. Learn to use different types of technical communication, such as instructions, reports, proposals, and presentations.
5. Develop the skills to write and edit technical documents for accuracy and clarity.
6. Learn to use different types of media, such as graphics, audio, and video, to enhance technical documents.
7. Develop the skills to evaluate and revise technical documents.
8. Understand the importance of ethical and professional communication.
