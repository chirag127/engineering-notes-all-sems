
### Rising Tone for the Notes of the Unit 5 - Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics in the Subject of Technical Communication

1. Rising tone is an important aspect of oral communication, as it helps to convey emotion and emphasis in a conversation.
2. Rising tone is used to express enthusiasm, excitement, or surprise. It can also be used to ask questions or to show agreement.
3. In order to use rising tone effectively, the speaker must be conscious of their vocal pitch and use it to their advantage.
4. The speaker must also be aware of the context in which they are speaking, as the same rising tone can have different meanings in different situations.
5. When speaking in a professional setting, such as in a technical presentation, rising tone should be used sparingly. It can be used to emphasize certain points, but should not be used to make an entire presentation sound overly enthusiastic.
6. In order to practice using rising tone effectively, it can be helpful to record oneself speaking and then listen back to the recording to identify areas where rising tone could be used to emphasize certain points.
