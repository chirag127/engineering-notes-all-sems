
### Decoding process for the notes of the Unit 5 - Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics in the subject of Technical Communication

1. Oral communication is the process of exchanging information between two or more people through verbal or non-verbal means.
2. Voice dynamics is the study of how the human voice is used to communicate effectively. It includes the use of pitch, volume, and other vocal characteristics to convey meaning.
3. Technical communication is the process of conveying technical information in a clear and concise manner. This includes the use of diagrams, charts, and other visuals to explain complex concepts.
4. The dimensions of oral communication and voice dynamics include:
    * Intonation: the way words are spoken, including the use of pitch, volume, and other vocal characteristics.
    * Clarity: the ability to be understood by the listener.
    * Emphasis: the use of emphasis to draw attention to certain words or phrases.
    * Pronunciation: the correct pronunciation of words.
    * Fluency: the ability to speak without hesitation or errors.
    * Articulation: the ability to use words and phrases in a way that is easily understood by the listener.
    * Delivery: the manner in which the speaker presents the message.
5. In order to effectively communicate, it is important to be aware of these dimensions and use them to your advantage. With practice, you can learn to use your voice to convey meaning and be understood by your listeners.
