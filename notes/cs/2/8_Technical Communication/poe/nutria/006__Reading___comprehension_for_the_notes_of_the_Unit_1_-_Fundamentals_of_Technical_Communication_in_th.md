
### Reading & Comprehension for the Notes of the Unit 1 - Fundamentals of Technical Communication

1. Reading is an important part of technical communication. It involves understanding the meaning of a text, as well as the context in which it was written.

2. In order to comprehend a text, one must first analyze it. This involves breaking it down into its component parts and examining each part for its meaning.

3. It is also important to consider the purpose of the text. Is it meant to inform, persuade, or entertain?

4. In addition, the reader must consider the audience for the text. Who is the intended audience? What do they need to know?

5. Finally, the reader must be aware of any cultural context in which the text was written. This includes understanding the language, customs, and values of the culture in which the text was written.

6. Once a text has been read and analyzed, it is important to evaluate it. This involves considering the writer’s purpose and the reader’s reaction. Did the writer accomplish their goal? Did the reader understand the text?

7. Finally, it is important to remember that reading and comprehension are ongoing processes. As one reads more, one’s understanding of a text will grow.
