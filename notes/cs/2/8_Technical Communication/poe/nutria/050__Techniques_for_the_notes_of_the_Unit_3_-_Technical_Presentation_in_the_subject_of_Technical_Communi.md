

### Techniques for the Notes of Unit 3 - Technical Presentation in Technical Communication

1. Know your audience: Before beginning your presentation, take the time to understand who your audience is and what their interests and needs are. This will help you tailor your presentation to their needs and ensure that your message is well received.

2. Structure your presentation: It's important to structure your presentation in a logical way that flows from point to point. Begin with an introduction, then move on to the main points of your presentation, and finally, conclude with a summary and/or call to action.

3. Use visuals: Visuals can be a great way to engage your audience and keep them interested in your presentation. Use charts, graphs, pictures, and videos to illustrate your points and make your presentation more engaging.

4. Practice: Before giving your presentation, make sure to practice it multiple times. This will help you become more comfortable with the material and ensure that you are able to deliver your presentation with confidence.

5. Speak clearly: Make sure to speak clearly and at a moderate pace. Avoid using jargon and technical terms that your audience may not understand.

6. Be prepared for questions: After your presentation, it is likely that your audience will have questions. Be prepared to answer them and provide additional information as needed.
