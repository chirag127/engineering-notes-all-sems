
### Definition for the notes of the Unit 1 - Fundamentals of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication

* Technical Communication: The use of language, visuals, and digital media to communicate information in a clear, concise, and effective manner. 
* Technical Writing: The practice of creating technical documents such as user manuals, instructional materials, and online help systems.
* Technical Editing: The process of reviewing, revising, and formatting documents to ensure accuracy and clarity.
* Technical Design: The process of designing documents that are visually appealing and easy to use.
* Technical Documentation: The practice of creating and maintaining documents that explain how a product or system works.
* Usability Testing: The process of testing the usability of a product or system by observing how users interact with it.
* Technical Support: The practice of providing technical assistance to users who are having difficulty using a product or system.
