
### Interpersonal Communication for the Notes of the Unit 3 - Technical Presentation in the Subject of Technical Communication

1. Interpersonal communication is a form of communication that involves two or more people in a face-to-face interaction. 
2. The purpose of interpersonal communication is to share information, build relationships, and foster understanding between two or more individuals. 
3. The key components of interpersonal communication are verbal and non-verbal communication, listening, and understanding. 
4. Verbal communication involves the use of words, both written and spoken, to convey information. 
5. Non-verbal communication includes body language, facial expressions, and gestures. 
6. Listening is an important part of communication, as it allows individuals to understand the messages being sent by others. 
7. Understanding involves interpreting the messages being sent and responding appropriately. 
8. In a technical presentation, interpersonal communication is used to convey complex information in a clear and concise manner. 
9. Effective interpersonal communication can help build trust and foster collaboration between individuals. 
10. Good interpersonal communication skills are essential for successful technical presentations.
