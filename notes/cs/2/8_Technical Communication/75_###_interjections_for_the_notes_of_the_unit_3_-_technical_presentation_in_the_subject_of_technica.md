### Interjections for the notes of the Unit 3 - Technical Presentation in the subject of Technical Communication

Interjections:
- Interjections are words or phrases used to express strong emotion or surprise.
- Examples: Wow!, Oh!, Oops!, etc.
- Interjections can be used to add emphasis or to show the speaker's attitude towards the topic.

Unit 3: Technical Presentation:
- This unit covers the skills and techniques needed for effective technical presentations.
- Topics may include: Audience Analysis, Presentation Planning, Visual Aids, Delivery Techniques, etc.
- Audience Analysis: Understanding the needs and expectations of the audience.
- Presentation Planning: Organizing the content and structure of the presentation.
- Visual Aids: Using graphics, images, and other visual elements to enhance the presentation.
- Delivery Techniques: Effective techniques for speaking, body language, and voice control.
- This unit may also cover the use of technology in presentations, such as slide software and multimedia tools.
