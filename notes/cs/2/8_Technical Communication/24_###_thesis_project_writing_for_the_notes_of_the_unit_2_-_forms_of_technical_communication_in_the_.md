### Thesis/Project writing for the notes of the Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication

Thesis/project writing is a form of technical communication that involves the preparation of a written document that presents the results of a research project or investigation. In the Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication, students learn about the process of writing a thesis or project and the skills required to communicate technical information effectively. The notes for this unit should include the following topics:

1. Definition of thesis/project writing and its purpose (presenting the results of a research project or investigation)
2. Overview of the thesis/project writing process (topic selection, research, writing, revision)
3. Characteristics of effective thesis/project writing (clarity, conciseness, accuracy)
4. Thesis/project structure (introduction, literature review, methodology, results, discussion, conclusion)
5. Techniques for conducting research (literature search, data collection, data analysis)
6. Writing techniques for thesis/project writing (organizing information, writing clear and concise sentences, using appropriate technical terminology)
7. Ethics in thesis/project writing (plagiarism, bias, confidentiality)
8. Document design (formatting, layout, typography)
9. Peer review and revision (providing feedback, making revisions)
10. Further reading and resources (books, journals, online resources)

Thesis/project writing is an important form of technical communication and provides students with the opportunity to apply the skills they have learned in the subject of Technical Communication to a real-world project. Understanding the process of thesis/project writing and the skills required to communicate technical information effectively helps students to develop the skills required to succeed in their future careers.
