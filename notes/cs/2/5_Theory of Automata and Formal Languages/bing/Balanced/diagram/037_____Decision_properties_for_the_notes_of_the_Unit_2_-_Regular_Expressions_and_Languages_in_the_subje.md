### Decision Properties for the Notes of the Unit 2 - Regular Expressions and Languages

- Decision properties are questions that can be answered with a yes or no for a given language or a class of languages.
- For regular languages, there are several decision properties that are decidable, meaning that there exists an algorithm that can answer them in finite time.
- Some of the decision properties of regular languages are:

  - Emptiness: Given a regular expression or a finite automaton, is the language generated by it empty?
  - Non-emptiness: Given a regular expression or a finite automaton, is the language generated by it non-empty?
  - Finiteness: Given a regular expression or a finite automaton, is the language generated by it finite?
  - Infiniteness: Given a regular expression or a finite automaton, is the language generated by it infinite?
  - Membership: Given a regular expression or a finite automaton and a string, does the string belong to the language generated by it?
  - Equality: Given two regular expressions or two finite automata, do they generate the same language?
  - Subset: Given two regular expressions or two finite automata, is the language generated by one a subset of the language generated by the other?
  - Disjointness: Given two regular expressions or two finite automata, are the languages generated by them disjoint, meaning that they have no common strings?
  - Complement: Given a regular expression or a finite automaton, what is the regular expression or the finite automaton that generates the complement of the language generated by it?
  - Intersection: Given two regular expressions or two finite automata, what is the regular expression or the finite automaton that generates the intersection of the languages generated by them?
  - Union: Given two regular expressions or two finite automata, what is the regular expression or the finite automaton that generates the union of the languages generated by them?
  - Concatenation: Given two regular expressions or two finite automata, what is the regular expression or the finite automaton that generates the concatenation of the languages generated by them?
  - Kleene Closure: Given a regular expression or a finite automaton, what is the regular expression or the finite automaton that generates the Kleene closure of the language generated by it?

- These decision properties can be solved using various methods, such as converting regular expressions to finite automata or vice versa, applying closure properties of regular languages, using algorithms such as the subset construction, the minimization algorithm, or the pumping lemma for regular languages.
