### Context Free Grammars for Pushdown Automata

- A context-free grammar (CFG) is a set of rewriting rules that can be used to generate or reproduce patterns/strings recursively.
- A context-free language (CFL) is the set of all strings that can be generated by a CFG.
- A pushdown automaton (PDA) is a finite-state machine with an added two-way storage tape, called the pushdown store or stack.
- A PDA can accept a CFL by either emptying its stack or reaching a final state.
- There is an equivalence between CFGs and PDAs, meaning that for every CFG, there is a PDA that accepts the same language, and vice versa .
- The conversion from a CFG to a PDA is based on the idea of simulating the derivation or sequence of production rules that results in a given string.
- The conversion from a PDA to a CFG is based on the idea of capturing the configurations or states of the PDA and the contents of the stack in the production rules.
