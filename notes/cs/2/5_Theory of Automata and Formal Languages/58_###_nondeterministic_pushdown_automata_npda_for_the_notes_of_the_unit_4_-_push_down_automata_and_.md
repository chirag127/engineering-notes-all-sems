### Nondeterministic Pushdown Automata (NPDA) for the notes of the Unit 4 - Push Down Automata and Properties of Context Free Languages in the subject of Theory of Automata and Formal Languages

Unit 4 of the Theory of Automata and Formal Languages course covers the topics of Push Down Automata and Properties of Context Free Languages. Push Down Automata are abstract machines that are used to recognize context-free languages, which are a type of formal language.

Nondeterministic Pushdown Automata (NPDA) is a type of pushdown automaton that can transition between states based on multiple possible inputs. Unlike deterministic pushdown automata, which can only transition based on a single input, NPDA can transition based on multiple inputs, allowing for more flexible and powerful recognition of context-free languages.

NPDA are defined by a set of states, inputs, outputs, and transitions between states. The transitions between states are determined by the input symbol and the symbol at the top of the stack. NPDA can also push and pop symbols from the stack, allowing for more complex recognition of context-free languages.
