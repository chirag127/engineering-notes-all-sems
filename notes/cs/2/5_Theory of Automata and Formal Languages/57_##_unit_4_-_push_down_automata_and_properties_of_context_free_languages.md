## Unit 4 - Push Down Automata and Properties of Context Free Languages

Unit 4 - Push Down Automata and Properties of Context Free Languages is a unit in the subject of Theory of Automata and Formal Languages that focuses on the study of push down automata (PDA) and context free languages (CFL).

Push down automata are a type of automaton that use a stack to store and process information. PDAs are more powerful than finite automata, and can recognize a larger class of languages, including context free languages.

Context free languages are languages that can be described by context free grammars (CFGs), and can be recognized by push down automata. CFLs are characterized by a limited set of rules, and are used to generate strings in a straightforward and predictable manner.

In this unit, we will study the concepts of push down automata and context free languages, and examine the algorithms used to recognize and process these languages. We will also study the properties of push down automata and context free languages, and examine the trade-offs involved in using different algorithms. This will provide a foundation for understanding the design and implementation of algorithms for processing push down automata and context free languages, and for exploring the various applications of PDAs and CFLs in the field of automata theory and formal languages.
