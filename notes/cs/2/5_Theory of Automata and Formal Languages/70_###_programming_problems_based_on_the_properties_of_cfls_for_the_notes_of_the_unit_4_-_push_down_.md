### Programming problems based on the properties of CFLs for the notes of the Unit 4 - Push Down Automata and Properties of Context Free Languages in the subject of Theory of Automata and Formal Languages

Unit 4 - Push Down Automata and Properties of Context Free Languages in the subject of Theory of Automata and Formal Languages focuses on the study of pushdown automata and the properties of context-free languages. In this unit, students learn about the concepts and algorithms involved in the design and implementation of pushdown automata and how they are used to recognize and manipulate context-free languages.

In this unit, students can expect to encounter programming problems based on the properties of context-free languages. These problems can include:

1. Implementing a pushdown automaton to recognize strings in a context-free language
2. Determining the context-free language generated by a given grammar
3. Proving the non-context-free nature of a language using the pumping lemma
4. Converting a context-free grammar to a pushdown automaton
5. Implementing a parser for a context-free language using a pushdown automaton

These programming problems provide an opportunity for students to apply their knowledge of pushdown automata and the properties of context-free languages to practical, real-world problems. They help students to understand the role of pushdown automata in the recognition and manipulation of context-free languages and to develop their skills in algorithm design and implementation.
