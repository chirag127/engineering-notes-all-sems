Two stack Pushdown Automata (2SPDA) is a type of Pushdown Automaton (PDA) that uses two stacks instead of one. A PDA is a type of automaton that can recognize context-free languages, and is characterized by a stack that can be used to store symbols during the processing of a string.

In a 2SPDA, the first stack is used to store symbols that represent the current state of the automaton, while the second stack is used to store symbols that represent the input string. The two stacks are used together to process the string, and the sequence of operations performed on the stacks determines the behavior of the automaton.

Two stack PDAs are more powerful than standard PDAs, as they allow for the manipulation of multiple stacks, and provide a more flexible way to process strings. They are also used in the implementation of algorithms for processing context-free languages, and in the construction of algorithms for parsing and processing strings.
