### Decision properties for the notes of the Unit 2 - Regular Expressions and Languages in the subject of Theory of Automata and Formal Languages

Decision properties are a set of properties that determine the computational complexity of algorithms for recognizing and processing formal languages. These properties are used to classify the languages into different complexity classes, and to determine the efficiency of algorithms for processing these languages.

The most important decision properties are the time and space complexity of algorithms, which determine the amount of time and memory required to process a language. Other decision properties include the number of states in the minimal automaton for the language, the number of transitions in the automaton, and the size of the grammar for the language.

In this unit, we will study the decision properties of formal languages, and examine the algorithms used to determine these properties. We will also study the trade-offs involved in using different algorithms, and examine the impact of these properties on the computational complexity of algorithms for processing formal languages. This will provide a foundation for understanding the design and implementation of algorithms for processing formal languages, and for exploring the various applications of decision properties in the field of automata theory and formal languages.
