### Finite Automata with output for the notes of the Unit 1 - Basic Concepts and Automata Theory in the subject of Theory of Automata and Formal Languages

A finite automata with output (also known as a Mealy machine) is a finite state machine that associates an output with each transition in addition to the next state. It is a type of finite automata that is used to model the behavior of systems that produce outputs based on their inputs.

In the Unit 1 - Basic Concepts and Automata Theory of the subject of Theory of Automata and Formal Languages, students learn about the basic concepts and algorithms involved in automata theory and formal languages. The notes for this unit should include the following topics:

1. Definition of finite automata with output (Mealy machine) and its purpose (modeling systems that produce outputs)
2. Overview of finite automata with output (input, output, transition, state)
3. Types of finite automata with output (deterministic, non-deterministic)
4. Finite automata with output representation (state transition table, state transition diagram)
5. Finite automata with output implementation (hardware, software)
6. Comparison of finite automata with output and finite automata (input, output, transition, state)

Finite automata with output is an important concept in the study of Automata Theory and Formal Languages. It provides a foundation for understanding the concepts and algorithms involved in modeling systems that produce outputs based on their inputs and helps students to understand the role of finite automata with output in automata theory and formal languages.
