### Introduction to Theory of Computation for the notes of the Unit 1 - Basic Concepts and Automata Theory in the subject of Theory of Automata and Formal Languages

The Theory of Computation is a branch of theoretical computer science that studies the limits of computation. It is concerned with the study of algorithms and their computational complexity, and with the study of formal languages and their relationship to automata.

The theory of computation is divided into two main branches: Automata Theory and Formal Languages. Automata Theory studies the design and behavior of abstract machines, known as automata, that are used to model and solve computational problems. Formal Languages studies the structure and properties of formal languages, which are used to describe the syntax and semantics of programming languages, and to specify the requirements of software systems.

In this unit, we will study the basic concepts of the theory of computation, including finite automata, regular expressions, context-free grammars, and Turing machines. We will also examine the relationship between automata and formal languages, and study the concepts of decidability and undecidability. This will provide a foundation for understanding the design and analysis of algorithms, and for exploring the limits of computation and the foundations of computer science.
