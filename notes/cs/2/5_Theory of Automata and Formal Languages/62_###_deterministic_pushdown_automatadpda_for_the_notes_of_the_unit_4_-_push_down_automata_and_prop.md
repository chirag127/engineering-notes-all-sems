### Deterministic Pushdown Automata(DPDA) for the notes of the Unit 4 - Push Down Automata and Properties of Context Free Languages in the subject of Theory of Automata and Formal Languages

Unit 4 of the Theory of Automata and Formal Languages course covers the topics of Pushdown Automata and Properties of Context Free Languages. Pushdown Automata are a type of finite automaton that have the ability to store and manipulate data on a stack.

Deterministic Pushdown Automata (DPDA) are a type of pushdown automaton that have a deterministic behavior, meaning that there is a unique next state for each combination of current state and input symbol. DPDA are used to recognize context-free languages, which are a type of formal language that can be described by context-free grammars.
