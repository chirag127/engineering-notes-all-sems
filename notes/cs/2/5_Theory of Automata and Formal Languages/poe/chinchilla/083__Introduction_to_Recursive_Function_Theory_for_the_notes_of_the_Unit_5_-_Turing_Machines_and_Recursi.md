### Introduction to Recursive Function Theory

Recursive function theory is a branch of computer science that deals with the study of recursive functions and their properties. It is closely related to computability theory and the theory of Turing machines.

In this unit, we will cover the following topics:

1. Definition of Recursive Functions
    - Recursive functions are a class of functions that can be defined by a finite set of rules or equations.
    - They are also known as computable functions because they can be computed by a Turing machine.

2. Properties of Recursive Functions
    - Recursive functions are closed under composition, recursion, and primitive recursion.
    - They are also closed under the operations of addition, multiplication, and exponentiation.

3. Church-Turing Thesis
    - The Church-Turing thesis states that any function that can be computed by an algorithm can be computed by a Turing machine.
    - This thesis is the basis of the theory of computability and the study of Turing machines.

4. Recursive Function Hierarchies
    - Recursive function hierarchies are a way of classifying recursive functions based on their computational power.
    - The hierarchy includes the classes of primitive recursive functions, recursive functions, and recursively enumerable functions.

5. Applications of Recursive Function Theory
    - Recursive function theory has applications in various areas of computer science, including programming language design, compiler construction, and artificial intelligence.

In summary, recursive function theory is an important area of study in computer science that provides a foundation for the theory of computability and the study of Turing machines. Understanding the properties and hierarchies of recursive functions is essential for designing efficient algorithms and solving computational problems.
