### Computability

Computability is a fundamental concept in the study of automata theory and formal languages. It refers to the ability to determine whether a problem can be solved by a computer or not. In this section, we will discuss the basic concepts of computability.

1. **Turing Machines**

Turing Machines are theoretical models of computation that can simulate any algorithmic process. They consist of a tape divided into cells, a read/write head that moves along the tape, and a finite control that determines the machine's behavior. The Turing Machine can read the symbol on the tape, write a new symbol, and move the head to the left or right.

2. **Halting Problem**

The halting problem is an unsolvable problem in computer science. It asks whether a given program will halt or run indefinitely for a given input. Alan Turing proved that there is no general algorithm that can solve the halting problem for all programs and inputs.

3. **Computable Functions**

A function f(x) is said to be computable if there exists a Turing Machine that can compute f(x) for all x in its domain. Computable functions are also called recursive functions or effectively calculable functions.

4. **Undecidable Problems**

Undecidable problems are problems that cannot be solved by any algorithmic process. The halting problem is an example of an undecidable problem. Other examples include the Post Correspondence Problem and the Entscheidungsproblem.

5. **Church-Turing Thesis**

The Church-Turing thesis is an important theory in computability. It states that any function that can be computed by an algorithm can also be computed by a Turing Machine. This thesis is widely accepted in computer science and mathematics.

In conclusion, computability is a crucial concept in the study of automata theory and formal languages. It provides the foundation for understanding the limits of computation and the theory of algorithms. Understanding the concepts of Turing Machines, halting problem, computable functions, undecidable problems, and Church-Turing thesis is essential for any student of computer science.
