### Closure properties of Regular Languages

1. Union: If L1 and L2 are regular languages, then L1 U L2 is also a regular language.

2. Concatenation: If L1 and L2 are regular languages, then L1 L2 is also a regular language.

3. Kleene star: If L is a regular language, then L* is also a regular language.

4. Complement: If L is a regular language, then (Σ* - L) is also a regular language.

The above four properties are called closure properties since starting from a regular language, applying any number of these properties, we get another regular language.

Hence, the set of all regular languages is closed under union, concatenation, Kleene star and complement. This makes regular languages easier to handle and analyze.

The above points cover the key closure properties of regular languages. The properties help in deriving new regular languages from given regular languages and aid in proving languages to be regular. They play an important role in the study and understanding of regular languages.
