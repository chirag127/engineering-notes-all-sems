### Two stack Pushdown Automata

Notes for Unit 4 - Push Down Automata and Properties of Context Free Languages

- A two stack PDA has two stacks - input stack and auxiliary stack.
- The input is read from the input stack and pushed to the auxiliary stack.
- The transitions are based on the symbol on the top of the auxiliary stack and the input symbol.
- The acceptance is done by emptying the input stack and reaching a final state.
- A two stack PDA is more powerful than a single stack PDA as it can remember more than one previous transition.
- The language accepted by a two stack PDA is a subset of context free languages.
- The conversion of a context free grammar to a two stack PDA is more straightforward compared to single stack PDA.
- The languages accepted by two stack PDA lie strictly between the languages accepted by single stack PDA and Turing machines.
