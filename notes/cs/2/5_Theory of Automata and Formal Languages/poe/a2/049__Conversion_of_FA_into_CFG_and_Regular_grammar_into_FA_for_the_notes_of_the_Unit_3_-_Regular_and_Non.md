### Conversion of FA into CFG and Regular grammar into FA for the notes of the Unit 3 - Regular and Non-Regular Grammars in the subject of Theory of Automata and Formal Languages

- A Finite Automaton (FA) can be converted into a Context Free Grammar (CFG) by following steps:
- Consider the states of FA as the variables of CFG.
- Consider the initial state of FA as the start variable of CFG.
- Consider the final states of FA as the terminals of CFG.
- Consider the transitions of FA as the production rules of CFG. The productions are of the form A -> aB/a where A,B are states variables and a is the input symbol.

- A Regular Grammar can be converted into a Finite Automaton (FA) by:
- Taking the start symbol of Regular Grammar as the initial state of FA.
- Taking the terminals of Regular Grammar as the final states of FA.
- Creating transitions between the states according to the productions of Regular Grammar. The transitions are labelled with the symbols that are replaced in the productions.

The topics FA, CFG and Regular Grammar are related to each other and can be inter-converted using the above steps for creating study notes and understanding the concepts in depth.
