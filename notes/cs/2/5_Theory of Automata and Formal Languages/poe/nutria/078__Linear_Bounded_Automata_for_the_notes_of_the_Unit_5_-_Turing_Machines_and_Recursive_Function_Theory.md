
### Linear Bounded Automata

* Linear Bounded Automata (LBA) is a type of automaton that is used to recognize certain types of languages.
* It is a finite state machine with an additional restriction that the number of states is bounded by a linear function of the length of the input.
* It is an important concept in the field of theoretical computer science, as it can be used to solve problems related to language recognition and context-free grammar.
* LBA can be used to recognize languages that are not context-free, such as the language of palindromes.
* LBA is used in the study of recursive function theory, as it can be used to recognize languages that are Turing-recognizable.
* LBA is also used in the study of recursive languages, as it can be used to recognize languages that are recursively enumerable.
