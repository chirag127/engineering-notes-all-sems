
### Closure Properties of Regular Languages

1. **Closure under Union**: The union of two regular languages is also a regular language.

2. **Closure under Concatenation**: The concatenation of two regular languages is also a regular language.

3. **Closure under Kleene Star**: The Kleene star of a regular language is also a regular language.

4. **Closure under Complementation**: The complement of a regular language is also a regular language.
