

### Introduction to Theory of Computation 

1. Theory of Computation is a branch of Computer Science that deals with the study of the capabilities and limitations of computers. 

2. Automata Theory is a subfield of Theory of Computation that deals with the study of abstract machines and their properties. 

3. Automata Theory is used to describe the behavior of computing machines and to design computer programs. 

4. Automata Theory is closely related to Formal Languages, which are used to describe the syntax and structure of computer programs. 

5. Automata Theory is used to design and build efficient algorithms for solving problems and to analyze the complexity of algorithms. 

6. Automata Theory is used to design and implement digital circuits and computer architectures.
