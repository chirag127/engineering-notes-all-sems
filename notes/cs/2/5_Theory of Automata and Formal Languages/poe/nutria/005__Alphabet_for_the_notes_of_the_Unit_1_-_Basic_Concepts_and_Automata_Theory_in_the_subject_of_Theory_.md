
### Alphabet for the notes of the Unit 1 - Basic Concepts and Automata Theory in the subject of Theory of Automata and Formal Languages

* An alphabet is a finite, non-empty set of symbols.
* Alphanumeric symbols are used to create strings.
* Strings are made up of symbols from an alphabet.
* Formal languages are made up of strings from an alphabet.
* Automata Theory is the study of abstract machines and how they process strings from an alphabet.
* Automata Theory is used to model, design and analyze computing systems.
* Automata Theory is used to study the properties of languages and to determine if a language is regular or context-free.
