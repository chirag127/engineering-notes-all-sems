
### Deterministic Context Free Languages (DCFL)

* A DCFL is a type of context free language (CFL) that can be recognized by a deterministic pushdown automaton (DPDA).
* DPDA is a type of pushdown automaton (PDA) that is able to recognize a CFL without any nondeterminism.
* DPDA has two components: the finite control and the stack. The finite control reads the input and the stack stores the information about the current state.
* DPDA can recognize a DCFL in linear time.
* The properties of DCFLs are similar to those of CFLs, including closure under union, intersection, concatenation, Kleene star, and homomorphism.
* DCFLs are not closed under complement, reversal, or intersection with regular languages.
