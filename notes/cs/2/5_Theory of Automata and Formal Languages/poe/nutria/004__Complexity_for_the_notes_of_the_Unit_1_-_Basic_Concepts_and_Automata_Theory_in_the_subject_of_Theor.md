
### Complexity for the Notes of the Unit 1 - Basic Concepts and Automata Theory

1. Complexity is a measure of the difficulty of a problem, usually expressed in terms of time and/or space required to solve the problem.
2. Automata theory is the study of abstract machines and their properties. It is concerned with the design, analysis, and implementation of algorithms that can be implemented on a computer.
3. Automata theory provides a mathematical model for understanding and analyzing the behavior of systems.
4. Automata theory is used to study the behavior of finite state machines, Turing machines, and other types of abstract machines.
5. Automata theory is also used to study the behavior of formal languages and their associated grammars.
6. Automata theory is used to study the properties of systems such as regular languages, context-free languages, and pushdown automata.
7. Automata theory is also used to study the properties of computational models such as Turing machines, cellular automata, and quantum computers.
8. Automata theory is used to study the properties of algorithms and their complexity.
