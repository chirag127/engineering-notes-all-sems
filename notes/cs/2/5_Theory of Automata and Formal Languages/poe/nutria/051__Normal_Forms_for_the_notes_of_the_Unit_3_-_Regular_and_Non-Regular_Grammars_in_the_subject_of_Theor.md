
### Normal Forms for the Notes of the Unit 3 - Regular and Non-Regular Grammars in the Subject of Theory of Automata and Formal Languages

* Normal forms provide a way of representing a grammar in a simpler form. 
* There are three normal forms for regular grammars: Chomsky Normal Form (CNF), Greibach Normal Form (GNF) and Right Linear Form (RLF). 
* Chomsky Normal Form (CNF) requires that all productions have the form A → BC or A → a, where A, B and C are non-terminals and a is a terminal. 
* Greibach Normal Form (GNF) requires that all productions have the form A → aB, where A is a non-terminal and a and B are terminals. 
* Right Linear Form (RLF) requires that all productions have the form A → aB, where A and B are non-terminals and a is a terminal. 
* Non-regular grammars do not have normal forms. Instead, they are represented by a set of productions.
