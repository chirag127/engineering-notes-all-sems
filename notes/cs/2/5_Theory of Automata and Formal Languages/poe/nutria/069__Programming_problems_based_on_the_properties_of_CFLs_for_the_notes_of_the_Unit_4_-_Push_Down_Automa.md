
### Programming Problems Based on the Properties of CFLs

1. Construct a context-free grammar that generates the language consisting of all strings of a's and b's, with at least one a and an even number of b's.
2. Construct a context-free grammar that generates the language consisting of all strings of a's and b's, with at least one b and an even number of a's.
3. Construct a context-free grammar that generates the language consisting of all strings of a's and b's, with the same number of a's and b's.
4. Construct a context-free grammar that generates the language consisting of all strings of a's and b's, with the same number of a's and b's and at least one b.
5. Construct a context-free grammar that generates the language consisting of all strings of a's and b's, with at least one a and an odd number of b's.
6. Construct a context-free grammar that generates the language consisting of all strings of a's and b's, with at least one b and an odd number of a's.
7. Construct a context-free grammar that generates the language consisting of all strings of a's and b's, with at least two a's and an even number of b's.
8. Construct a context-free grammar that generates the language consisting of all strings of a's and b's, with at least two b's and an even number of a's.
9. Construct a context-free grammar that generates the language consisting of all strings of a's and b's, with at least two a's and an odd number of b's.
10. Construct a context-free grammar that generates the language consisting of all strings of a's and b's, with at least two b's and an odd number of a's.
