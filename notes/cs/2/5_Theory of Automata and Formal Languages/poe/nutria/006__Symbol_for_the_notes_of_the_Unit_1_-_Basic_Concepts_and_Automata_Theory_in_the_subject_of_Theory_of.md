
### Unit 1 - Basic Concepts and Automata Theory

* Automata Theory is the study of abstract machines and how they can be used to solve problems.
* A symbol is an abstract representation of a concept or idea. Symbols are used to represent entities in the world of automata theory.
* A formal language is a set of symbols and rules for combining them to form strings of symbols.
* An automaton is a machine that can be used to recognize and process strings of symbols.
* Finite state machines are automata that can be used to recognize patterns in strings of symbols.
* Pushdown automata are automata that can be used to recognize context-free languages.
* Turing machines are automata that can be used to recognize any computable language.
* Regular expressions are a notation for describing strings of symbols. They can be used to represent languages accepted by finite state machines.
* Grammars are a notation for describing languages. They can be used to represent languages accepted by pushdown automata and Turing machines.
