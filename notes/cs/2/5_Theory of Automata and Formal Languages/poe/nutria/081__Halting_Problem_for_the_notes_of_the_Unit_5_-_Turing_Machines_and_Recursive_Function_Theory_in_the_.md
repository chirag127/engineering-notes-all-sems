

### Halting Problem

The Halting Problem is a classic problem in the field of Theory of Automata and Formal Languages. It is related to Unit 5 - Turing Machines and Recursive Function Theory.

The problem is to determine whether a given Turing Machine will halt, given a particular input. This is an undecidable problem, meaning that there is no algorithm that can always determine whether a Turing Machine will halt or not.

The Halting Problem is important because it is a fundamental limitation of computability. It shows that not all problems can be solved with a Turing Machine, and that there are some problems that are inherently unsolvable.

This is an important concept to understand when studying Theory of Automata and Formal Languages, and it is essential to understand the limitations of computability.
