
## Unit 5 - Turing Machines and Recursive Function Theory

* Turing machines are abstract models of computation that can be used to simulate any algorithm or program. They are composed of a finite-state machine and a tape that can store data. Turing machines can be used to solve problems that are otherwise difficult to solve using conventional algorithms.

* Recursive function theory is a branch of mathematics that studies the properties of recursive functions. Recursive functions are functions that can be defined in terms of themselves. They are used in computer science to solve problems that cannot be solved using conventional algorithms.

* Turing machines and recursive function theory are closely related. Turing machines can be used to simulate recursive functions, and recursive functions can be used to solve problems that cannot be solved using conventional algorithms.

* Turing machines and recursive function theory have many applications. They are used in cryptography, artificial intelligence, and computer science. They are also used in the study of computability and complexity theory.
