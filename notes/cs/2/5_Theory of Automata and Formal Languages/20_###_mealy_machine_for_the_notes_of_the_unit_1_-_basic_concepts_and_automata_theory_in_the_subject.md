### Mealy Machine for the notes of the Unit 1 - Basic Concepts and Automata Theory in the subject of Theory of Automata and Formal Languages

Unit 1 of the Theory of Automata and Formal Languages course covers the topics of Basic Concepts and Automata Theory. Automata Theory is the study of abstract machines and their behavior.

A Mealy machine is a type of finite state machine that is used to model the behavior of systems. Mealy machines are defined by a set of states, inputs, outputs, and transitions between states.

In a Mealy machine, the output of the machine is dependent on both the current state of the machine and the current input. This is in contrast to a Moore machine, in which the output is dependent only on the current state of the machine.

Mealy machines are commonly used to model the behavior of systems that have complex interactions between inputs and outputs. For example, Mealy machines can be used to model the behavior of digital circuits, communication protocols, and control systems.

In the context of the Theory of Automata and Formal Languages course, students will learn about Mealy machines as a type of finite state machine. This may include working with simulations of Mealy machines, writing code to implement Mealy machines, and analyzing the behavior of Mealy machines in response to different inputs.
