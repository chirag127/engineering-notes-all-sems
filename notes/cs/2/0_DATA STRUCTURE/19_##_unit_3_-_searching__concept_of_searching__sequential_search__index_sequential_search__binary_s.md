## Unit 3 - Searching: Concept of Searching, Sequential search, Index Sequential Search, Binary Search. Concept of Hashing & Collision resolution Techniques used in Hashing. Sorting: Insertion Sort, Selection, Bubble Sort, Quick Sort, Merge Sort, Heap Sort and Radix Sort.

"Unit 3 - Searching" covers the fundamental concepts and techniques used in searching and sorting algorithms. The goal of this unit is to provide students with a comprehensive understanding of searching and sorting algorithms, and to develop their skills in designing and implementing these algorithms.

The following are some of the key topics that may be covered in Unit 3 - Searching:

1. Concept of Searching: This section covers the basic concepts of searching, including the definition of searching, and the purpose of searching algorithms.

2. Sequential Search: This section covers the sequential search algorithm, including its implementation, and its time and space complexity.

3. Index Sequential Search: This section covers the index sequential search algorithm, including its implementation, and its time and space complexity.

4. Binary Search: This section covers the binary search algorithm, including its implementation, and its time and space complexity.

5. Concept of Hashing & Collision Resolution Techniques: This section covers the concept of hashing, including the definition, purpose, and implementation of hashing algorithms, and the techniques used to resolve collisions in hashing algorithms.

6. Sorting: This section covers the basic concepts of sorting, including the definition of sorting, and the purpose of sorting algorithms.

7. Insertion Sort, Selection, Bubble Sort: This section covers the insertion sort, selection sort, and bubble sort algorithms, including their implementation, and their time and space complexity.

8. Quick Sort, Merge Sort, Heap Sort, Radix Sort: This section covers the quick sort, merge sort, heap sort, and radix sort algorithms, including their implementation, and their time and space complexity.

The goal of Unit 3 - Searching is to provide students with a comprehensive understanding of searching and sorting algorithms, including their definition, purpose, implementation, and time and space complexity. This knowledge and understanding is important for students pursuing careers in computer science, as it provides the foundation for more advanced topics in the field.
