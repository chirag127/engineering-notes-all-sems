## Unit 1 - Introduction: Basic Terminology, Elementary Data Organization, Built in Data Types in C. Algorithm, Efficiency of an Algorithm, Time and Space Complexity, Asymptotic notations: Big Oh, Big Theta and Big Omega, Time-Space trade-off. Abstract Data Types (ADT)

"Unit 1 - Introduction" provides an overview of the basic concepts and terminology used in the study of computer science and algorithms. This unit is designed to provide students with a solid foundation in the fundamental concepts and techniques used in the field, and to prepare them for more advanced topics in computer science.

The following are some of the key topics that may be covered in Unit 1 - Introduction:

1. Basic Terminology: This section covers the basic terminology and concepts used in computer science, including data structures, algorithms, and computational complexity.

2. Elementary Data Organization: This section covers the basic techniques used to organize data in computer systems, including arrays, linked lists, and trees.

3. Built in Data Types in C: This section covers the built-in data types in the C programming language, including integers, floating-point numbers, and characters.

4. Algorithm: This section covers the definition and purpose of algorithms, and the techniques used to design and analyze algorithms.

5. Efficiency of an Algorithm: This section covers the importance of efficiency in algorithms, and the techniques used to measure the efficiency of algorithms, including time and space complexity.

6. Time and Space Complexity: This section covers the concepts of time and space complexity, and the techniques used to measure the efficiency of algorithms in terms of time and space.

7. Asymptotic Notations: This section covers the concepts of asymptotic notations, including Big Oh, Big Theta, and Big Omega, and the techniques used to describe the efficiency of algorithms in terms of these notations.

8. Time-Space Trade-off: This section covers the concept of the time-space trade-off, and the techniques used to balance the trade-off between time and space in algorithms.

9. Abstract Data Types (ADT): This section covers the concept of abstract data types, and the techniques used to design and implement abstract data types in computer systems.

The goal of Unit 1 - Introduction is to provide students with a comprehensive understanding of the basic concepts and terminology used in computer science and algorithms, and to prepare them for more advanced topics in the field. This knowledge and understanding is essential for students pursuing careers in computer science, as it provides the foundation for more advanced topics in the field.
