Time and Space Complexity are important concepts in the field of data structure and algorithms. The time complexity of an algorithm refers to the amount of time it takes for the algorithm to complete its execution. The space complexity of an algorithm refers to the amount of memory space required by the algorithm during its execution.

In the notes for Unit 1, students will learn about the different methods used to measure the time and space complexity of algorithms, including asymptotic notations such as Big Oh, Big Theta, and Big Omega. They will also learn about the trade-off between time and space complexity, and how to choose the best algorithm for a particular problem based on the time and space complexity of the algorithm.
