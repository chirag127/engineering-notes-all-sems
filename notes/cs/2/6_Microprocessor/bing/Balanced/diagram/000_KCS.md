# KCS

KCS stands for Knowledge-Centered Service, which is a methodology that aims to improve service delivery and customer satisfaction by creating and maintaining documentation as part of the support process .

Some of the main features and benefits of KCS are:

- It is based on the principle of "capture, structure, reuse, and improve" knowledge, which means that support agents document their solutions and learnings as they solve customer issues, and then share them with others through a knowledge base.
- It enables faster and more consistent resolution of customer issues, as well as reduced costs and increased productivity for the service organization, by leveraging existing knowledge and avoiding duplication of work.
- It fosters a culture of collaboration, learning, and innovation among support agents, as they are encouraged to contribute, review, and update knowledge articles, and to provide feedback and recognition to their peers.
- It supports continuous improvement of the service quality and the knowledge base, by measuring and analyzing the usage, value, and effectiveness of knowledge articles, and by identifying and addressing knowledge gaps and opportunities.

KCS is not to be confused with KCS, which is also the abbreviation for Kansas City Southern, a railroad company that operates in the United States and Mexico .
