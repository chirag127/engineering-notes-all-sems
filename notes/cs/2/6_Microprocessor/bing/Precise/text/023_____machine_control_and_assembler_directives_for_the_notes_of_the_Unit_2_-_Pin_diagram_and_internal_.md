### Machine Control and Assembler Directives

Machine control and assembler directives are an important part of the instruction set of the 8085 microprocessor. These instructions are used to control the operation of the machine and to provide information to the assembler.

1. **Machine Control Instructions:** These instructions are used to control the operation of the machine. They include instructions such as HLT (Halt), NOP (No Operation), EI (Enable Interrupts), and DI (Disable Interrupts).

2. **Assembler Directives:** These are instructions that are used to provide information to the assembler. They are not executed by the microprocessor, but are used by the assembler to generate the machine code. Some common assembler directives include ORG (Origin), EQU (Equates), and END (End of Program).

It is important to understand the use of these instructions and directives when studying the 8085 microprocessor and its instruction set. They play a crucial role in the operation of the machine and in the assembly of programs.
