### ALU

The ALU, or Arithmetic Logic Unit, is a fundamental component of the 8085 microprocessor. It is responsible for performing arithmetic and logical operations on data. The ALU is part of the internal architecture of the 8085 microprocessor, along with registers, control and status, interrupt and machine cycle.

- The ALU performs arithmetic operations such as addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division.
- It also performs logical operations such as AND, OR, XOR, and NOT.
- The ALU receives its inputs from the registers and performs the specified operation.
- The result of the operation is stored back in the registers or memory.
- The ALU also sets the status flags based on the result of the operation.
- The status flags are used by the control unit to make decisions and control the flow of the program.

The ALU is an essential component of the 8085 microprocessor and plays a crucial role in the execution of instructions. It is important to have a good understanding of the ALU and its operations when studying the 8085 microprocessor.
