### Instruction Formats and Classification

Unit 2 of the subject Microprocessor KCS covers the pin diagram and internal architecture of the 8085 microprocessor, including its registers, ALU, control and status, interrupt, and machine cycle. It also covers the instruction sets, addressing modes, instruction formats, and instruction classification.

Instruction classification is the categorization of instructions based on their function. The instruction set of the 8085 microprocessor can be classified into the following categories:

1. **Data transfer instructions:** These instructions are used to transfer data between registers, memory, and I/O devices.
2. **Arithmetic operations:** These instructions perform arithmetic operations such as addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division.
3. **Logical operations:** These instructions perform logical operations such as AND, OR, XOR, and NOT.
4. **Branching operations:** These instructions are used to alter the sequence of program execution by transferring control to a different memory location.
5. **Machine control instructions:** These instructions are used to control the operation of the microprocessor, such as enabling or disabling interrupts, halting the microprocessor, or resetting it.
6. **Assembler directives:** These are not instructions, but rather directives for the assembler. They are used to define data, reserve memory, and specify the starting address of the program.

It is important to understand the different instruction formats and classifications in order to effectively program the 8085 microprocessor.
