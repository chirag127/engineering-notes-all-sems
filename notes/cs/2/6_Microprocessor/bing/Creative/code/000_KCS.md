# KCS

KCS stands for Knowledge-Centered Service, which is a methodology for creating and maintaining knowledge as part of the service delivery process. KCS aims to improve the quality and efficiency of service organizations by capturing, structuring, and reusing the knowledge that is generated during service interactions. Some of the benefits of KCS are:

- Faster resolution of customer issues
- Reduced workload for service agents
- Increased customer satisfaction and loyalty
- Enhanced collaboration and learning among service teams
- Continuous improvement of knowledge base and service processes

The KCS methodology consists of four main practices:

- Capture: Service agents capture the customer's context and issue, as well as their own actions and outcomes, in the form of a knowledge article. The article is written in the customer's language and follows a standard structure and format.
- Structure: Service agents structure the knowledge article using metadata, such as keywords, categories, and links, to make it easy to find and reuse. The article is also validated for accuracy, completeness, and relevance by the agent or a peer.
- Reuse: Service agents reuse existing knowledge articles to solve customer issues, or to create new articles based on existing ones. The article is updated with any new information or feedback from the customer or the agent.
- Improve: Service agents improve the quality and usefulness of the knowledge base by flagging, rating, and reviewing the articles. The article is also monitored for usage, performance, and impact, and is revised or retired as needed.

KCS is based on a set of principles and core concepts that guide the implementation and adoption of the methodology. Some of the key principles are:

- Demand-driven: Knowledge is created and maintained based on the actual needs and requests of the customers and the service agents, not on assumptions or predictions.
- Trust: Service agents are empowered and trusted to create and modify knowledge articles, without excessive oversight or approval. The quality of the articles is ensured by a collaborative and self-correcting process.
- Evolve: Knowledge is dynamic and constantly changing, and so is the KCS methodology. The KCS practices and processes are adapted and improved based on feedback, data, and experience.

KCS is a widely recognized and adopted best practice for service organizations, and is supported by the Consortium for Service Innovation, a non-profit organization that promotes innovation and excellence in service. KCS is also aligned with other service frameworks, such as ITIL and ITSM, and can be integrated with various service tools and platforms.
