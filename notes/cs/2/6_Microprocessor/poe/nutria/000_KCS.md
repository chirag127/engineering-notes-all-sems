
# Knowledge Centered Support (KCS)

KCS is a methodology for providing effective support to customers. It is based on the concept of knowledge sharing, which involves capturing and sharing knowledge to improve the customer experience and reduce costs.

KCS focuses on the capture and reuse of knowledge in order to provide the best possible customer experience. It is based on the idea that knowledge should be captured, organized, and shared in order to be effective.

KCS involves the following steps:

1. Capture: Capturing knowledge in the form of articles, documents, and other content.
2. Organize: Organizing the captured knowledge into a structure that makes it easy to find and use.
3. Share: Sharing the knowledge with customers and other stakeholders.
4. Measure: Measuring the effectiveness of the knowledge sharing process.

KCS has been shown to reduce customer support costs and improve customer satisfaction. It is an effective way to provide knowledge-based customer support.
