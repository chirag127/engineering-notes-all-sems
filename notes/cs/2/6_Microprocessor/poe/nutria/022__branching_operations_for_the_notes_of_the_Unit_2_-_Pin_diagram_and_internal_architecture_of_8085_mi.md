- **Interrupt**: This is a signal that is sent to the microprocessor to indicate that an event has occurred. The microprocessor can then take the appropriate action.

- **Machine Cycle**: This is the sequence of steps that the microprocessor takes when executing an instruction. The machine cycle consists of the fetch, decode, execute, and writeback stages.
