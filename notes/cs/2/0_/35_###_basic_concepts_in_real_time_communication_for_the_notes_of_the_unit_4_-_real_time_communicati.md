### Basic Concepts in Real time Communication for the notes of the Unit 4 - Real Time Communication in the subject of Real Time System

1. Real-time communication: refers to the exchange of data between two or more parties in real-time with low latency.

2. Latency: refers to the delay between the transmission and receipt of data.

3. Protocols: set of rules that govern the communication between two or more parties. Examples: TCP, UDP, WebRTC.

4. WebRTC: open-source framework for real-time communication on the web.

5. Streams: continuous flow of data.

6. Signaling: process of establishing, maintaining, modifying and ending real-time communication sessions.

7. Peer-to-peer: direct communication between two parties without the need for a central server.

8. Server-based: communication between two parties through a central server.

9. Synchronous communication: both parties are active during the communication.

10. Asynchronous communication: one party sends a message and the other party receives it at a later time.
