### Processors and Resources for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction of Real Time System in the subject of Real Time System

Processors:
- Central Processing Unit (CPU) that performs computation and control functions.
- Microcontrollers with integrated CPU, memory, and peripherals.

Resources:
- Physical resources such as memory, I/O devices, and storage.
- Logical resources such as semaphores, message queues, and shared memory.
- Time and computational resources such as CPU time and processing power.
