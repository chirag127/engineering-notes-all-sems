### Precedence Constraints and Data Dependency for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction of Real Time System in the subject of Real Time System

"Precedence Constraints and Data Dependency" are key concepts in the field of Real Time Systems. These concepts are often covered in Unit 1 - Introduction of Real Time System in the subject of Real Time System.

1. Precedence Constraints: Precedence constraints refer to the relationships between tasks in a real-time system, where the completion of one task is dependent on the completion of another task. These constraints are used to ensure that tasks are completed in the correct order and that the system operates correctly.

2. Data Dependency: Data dependency refers to the relationship between tasks in a real-time system, where the completion of one task is dependent on the availability of data from another task. This dependency is used to ensure that data is available when it is needed, and that tasks are completed in the correct order.

The goal of studying precedence constraints and data dependency is to develop an understanding of the relationships between tasks in a real-time system, and to gain the skills and knowledge needed to design and implement real-time systems that are efficient, reliable, and meet the needs of the system's stakeholders.

By understanding these concepts, students will be able to design real-time systems that are able to meet the demands of real-time applications, including real-time control systems, real-time data acquisition systems, and real-time communication systems.
