In the context of Unit 1 - Introduction to Real Time Systems, the definition of a real-time system is important to understand the characteristics, requirements, and design of these systems.

1. Characteristics: Real-Time Systems have several key characteristics, including real-time constraints, predictability, and determinism. Real-time constraints specify the maximum time within which the system must respond to an event, while predictability and determinism ensure that the system behaves in a consistent and predictable manner.

2. Requirements: Real-Time Systems have specific requirements, including performance, reliability, and availability. Performance refers to the ability of the system to meet its real-time constraints, reliability refers to the ability of the system to operate without failure, and availability refers to the ability of the system to be accessible and ready for use when needed.

3. Design: The design of a Real-Time System is influenced by its requirements and characteristics. The design process typically involves defining the system requirements, choosing the appropriate hardware and software components, and verifying the system's performance and reliability.
