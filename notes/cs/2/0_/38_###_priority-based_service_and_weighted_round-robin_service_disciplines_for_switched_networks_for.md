### Priority-Based Service and Weighted Round-Robin Service Disciplines for Switched Networks for the notes of the Unit 4 - Real Time Communication in the subject of Real Time System

Priority-Based Service Discipline:
- Prioritizes network traffic based on the priority level assigned to each packet.
- High priority packets are transmitted before low priority packets.
- Can be used in real-time communication systems to ensure timely delivery of critical data.

Weighted Round-Robin Service Discipline:
- Distributes network bandwidth among multiple traffic flows based on a weight assigned to each flow.
- Higher weighted flows receive more bandwidth compared to lower weighted flows.
- Can be used to allocate bandwidth resources fairly among multiple applications in real-time communication systems.
