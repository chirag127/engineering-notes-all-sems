### Clock Driven Approach for the notes of the Unit 2 - Real Time Scheduling in the subject of Real Time System

Clock Driven Approach is a scheduling algorithm in real-time systems where tasks are executed based on a fixed clock rate. The clock rate is determined by the system designer and is used to schedule tasks in a predictable and deterministic manner. This approach is useful for real-time systems where deadlines must be met, such as control systems, embedded systems, and multimedia systems.

In the Clock Driven Approach, tasks are assigned a priority based on their deadline and are executed in the order of their priority. The clock rate determines the maximum amount of time a task can take to execute. If a task takes longer than its assigned time, it is considered to have missed its deadline and the system may take corrective action.

Advantages of the Clock Driven Approach include its simplicity, predictability, and deterministic behavior. It is also easy to implement and debug. However, it may not be suitable for systems with a large number of tasks or systems with complex task dependencies.

In conclusion, the Clock Driven Approach is a useful scheduling algorithm for real-time systems where deadlines must be met. It is simple, predictable, and deterministic, but may not be suitable for all real-time systems.
