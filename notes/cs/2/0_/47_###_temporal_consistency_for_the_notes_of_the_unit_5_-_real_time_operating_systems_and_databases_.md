### Temporal Consistency for the notes of the Unit 5 - Real Time Operating Systems and Databases in the subject of Real Time System

Temporal Consistency refers to the consistency of data over time. In real-time systems, it is important to ensure that the data is consistent and up-to-date, even in the presence of failures or changes in the system. This is especially important in databases, where data is stored and accessed by multiple applications. Temporal consistency can be achieved through techniques such as transactions, versioning, and time-stamping. Transactions ensure that changes to the data are made atomically and are either fully committed or fully rolled back. Versioning allows multiple versions of the data to be stored and accessed, providing a history of changes. Time-stamping allows the data to be linked to a specific point in time, providing a clear record of when the data was last updated. By ensuring temporal consistency, real-time systems can provide accurate and reliable data, even in dynamic and changing environments.
