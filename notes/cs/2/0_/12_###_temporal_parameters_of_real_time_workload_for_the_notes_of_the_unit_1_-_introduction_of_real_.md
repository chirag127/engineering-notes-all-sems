### Temporal Parameters of Real Time Workload for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction of Real Time System in the subject of Real Time System

Temporal Parameters of Real Time Workload for the Notes of Unit 1 - Introduction of Real Time System in the subject of Real Time System:

Real-time systems are computer systems that are designed to respond to events within a specified time constraint. Temporal parameters are the time-based characteristics of real-time workloads, and they are critical to the design and performance of real-time systems.

1. Response Time: Response time is the time between the initiation of a request and the start of the system's response. In real-time systems, response time is a critical temporal parameter, as it determines the system's ability to meet its real-time requirements.

2. Latency: Latency is the time between the initiation of a request and the completion of the system's response. In real-time systems, latency is also a critical temporal parameter, as it determines the overall performance of the system.

3. Deadline: Deadline is the time by which a system must complete its response to a request. In real-time systems, deadlines are used to ensure that the system meets its real-time requirements.

4. Period: Period is the time between the initiation of two consecutive requests. In real-time systems, period is used to determine the frequency of requests, and it is a critical temporal parameter for determining the system's ability to meet its real-time requirements.

5. Jitter: Jitter is the variation in the time between the initiation of two consecutive requests. In real-time systems, jitter is a critical temporal parameter, as it can impact the system's ability to meet its real-time requirements.

In conclusion, temporal parameters are the time-based characteristics of real-time workloads, and they are critical to the design and performance of real-time systems. Understanding the response time, latency, deadline, period, and jitter of real-time workloads is essential for designing and implementing real-time systems that meet their real-time requirements.
