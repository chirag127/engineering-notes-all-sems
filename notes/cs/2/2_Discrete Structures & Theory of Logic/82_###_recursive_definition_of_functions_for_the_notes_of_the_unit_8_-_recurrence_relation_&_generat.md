### Recursive definition of functions for the notes of the Unit 8 - Recurrence Relation & Generating function in the subject of Discrete Structures & Theory of Logic

A recursive definition of a function is a definition that defines a function in terms of itself. In other words, a function is defined by calling itself with a smaller input. Recursive definitions are used to solve problems that can be broken down into smaller sub-problems, and they are a powerful tool in computer science and mathematics.

Unit 8 of Discrete Structures & Theory of Logic focuses on the study of recurrence relations and generating functions. The notes for this unit should include the following topics:

1. Definition of a recursive function and its components (base case, recursive case)
2. Recurrence relations (linear homogeneous, linear non-homogeneous, non-linear)
3. Solving recurrence relations (substitution method, characteristic equation method, generating functions)
4. Generating functions (definition, properties, manipulation, application)
5. Applications of recursive functions (combinatorics, dynamic programming, divide-and-conquer algorithms)

It is important to understand the concepts and properties of recursive functions in order to apply them in various fields and solve problems related to discrete structures and the theory of logic.
