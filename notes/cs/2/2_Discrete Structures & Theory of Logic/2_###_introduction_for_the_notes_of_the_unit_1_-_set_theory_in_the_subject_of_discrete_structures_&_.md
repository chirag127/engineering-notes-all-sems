### Introduction for the notes of the Unit 1 - Set Theory in the subject of Discrete Structures & Theory of Logic

"Introduction" is a topic covered in Unit 1 - Set Theory in the subject of Discrete Structures & Theory of Logic.

Set theory is the mathematical study of sets, which are collections of objects. Sets are used to model mathematical objects, such as numbers, functions, and relations, and to study their properties and relationships.

In Unit 1 - Set Theory, students will learn the basics of set theory, including the definition of sets, the operations that can be performed on sets, and the different types of sets, such as finite and infinite sets, and empty and non-empty sets.

This knowledge and understanding is important for students pursuing careers in computer science and mathematics, as set theory provides the foundation for more advanced topics in these fields, such as algorithms, data structures, and mathematical logic.
