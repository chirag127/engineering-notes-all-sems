### Permutation and Symmetric groups for the notes of the Unit 2 - Algebraic Structures in the subject of Discrete Structures & Theory of Logic

"Permutation and Symmetric Groups" is a topic covered in Unit 2 - Algebraic Structures in the subject of Discrete Structures & Theory of Logic.

Permutation is the rearrangement of elements in a set. In mathematics, permutations are used to study the properties and relationships of sets and their elements.

A symmetric group is a group of permutations that preserve the structure of a set. Symmetric groups are used to study the symmetries of sets and their elements, and to classify and analyze the symmetries of objects in mathematics and physics.

In Unit 2 - Algebraic Structures, students will learn about permutation and symmetric groups, including the definition and properties of permutations, the definition and properties of symmetric groups, and the methods used to study and manipulate permutations and symmetric groups.

This knowledge and understanding is important for students pursuing careers in mathematics, computer science, and related fields, as it provides the foundation for more advanced topics in discrete mathematics and theoretical computer science, such as group theory, abstract algebra, and Galois theory.
