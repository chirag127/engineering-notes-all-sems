### Algebraic manipulation of Boolean expressions for the notes of the Unit 3 - Lattices in the subject of Discrete Structures & Theory of Logic

1. Boolean expressions: Expressions constructed using binary variables and logical operators like AND, OR, NOT.
2. Types of expressions:
 - Conjunctive normal form (CNF): AND of ORs of literals
 - Disjunctive normal form (DNF): OR of ANDs of literals
 - Sum of products (SOP) form: OR of ANDs of variables
 - Product of sums (POS) form: AND of ORs of variables
3. Conversion between forms:
 - CNF => DNF: De Morgan's laws
 - SOP => POS: Dual of a Boolean function
 - SOP => CNF: Distribution law
 - POS => SOP: Dual of a Boolean function
4. Simplification of expressions:
 - Idempotent laws: A + A = A, A.A = A
 - Absorption laws: A + A.B = A, A.B + B = A
 - Complement laws: A + ~A = 1, A.~A = 0
 - etc.
5. Minimization of Boolean expressions using Karnaugh maps.
