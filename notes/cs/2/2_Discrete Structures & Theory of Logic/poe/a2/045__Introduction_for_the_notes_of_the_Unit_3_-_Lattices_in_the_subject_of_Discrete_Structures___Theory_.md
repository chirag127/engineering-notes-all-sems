### Introduction for the notes of the Unit 3 - Lattices

1. A lattice is a partially ordered set in which any two elements have a supremum (least upper bound) and an infimum (greatest lower bound).
2. Lattices are useful in many areas of mathematics and computer science, including order theory, abstract algebra, logic, and theoretical computer science.
3. The study of lattices began in the late 19th century with works of people including Ernst Schroder, Ernst Steinitz, Garrett Birkhoff, and others.
4. The concepts of lattices are useful in many areas like abstract algebra to define certain structures such as modular lattices, posets, and algebraic lattices. In logic, lattices are used to represent logical relationships. In theoretical computer science, they are used in domain theory to represent denotational semantics of programming languages.
5. In this unit, we will study the formal definition of lattices, different types of lattices, and their properties and applications in various fields. We will cover topics including modular lattices, distributive lattices, algebraic lattices, and logical lattices. We will understand how the concepts of lattices are used to solve problems in various disciplines.
