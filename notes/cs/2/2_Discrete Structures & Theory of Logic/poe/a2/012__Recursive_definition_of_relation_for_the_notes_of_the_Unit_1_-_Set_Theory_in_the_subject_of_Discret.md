### Recursive definition of relation for the notes of the Unit 1 - Set Theory in the subject of Discrete Structures & Theory of Logic

1. A recursive definition is a definition that refers to itself to define a concept.
2. A relation R on a set A is recursively defined if it is defined in terms of itself.
3. For example, the relation "is an ancestor of" on a set of people is recursively defined:
- x is an ancestor of x
- If y is an ancestor of z, then y's parents are also ancestors of z
4. The recursive definition must also include one or more base cases that stop the recursion.
5. Another example: The relation "less than" on natural numbers is recursively defined:
- 0 is less than any natural number
- If n < m, then n + 1 is less than m + 1
