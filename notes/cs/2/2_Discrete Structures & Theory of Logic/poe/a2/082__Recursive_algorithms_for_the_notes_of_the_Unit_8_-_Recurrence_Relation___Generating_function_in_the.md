### Recursive algorithms for the notes of the Unit 8 - Recurrence Relation & Generating function in the subject of Discrete Structures & Theory of Logic

1. A recursive algorithm is an algorithm which calls itself repeatedly to solve a problem.
2. A recurrence relation is a mathematical equation that defines a sequence recursively.
3. A generating function is a formal power series that encodes a sequence, and is used to find properties of the sequence.
4. To solve a recurrence relation, try expressing the solution in terms of the closed-form expression for the base case and recursive calls.
5. For a linear recurrence relation with constant coefficients, you can find a closed-form expression using the characteristic equation.
6. Generating functions allow you to convert between the sequence representation and coefficient representation of a sequence, and are useful for analyzing sequences.
