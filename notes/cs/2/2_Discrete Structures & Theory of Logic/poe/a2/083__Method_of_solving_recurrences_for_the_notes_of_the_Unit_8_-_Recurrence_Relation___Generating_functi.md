### Method of solving recurrences for the notes of the Unit 8 - Recurrence Relation & Generating function in the subject of Discrete Structures & Theory of Logic.

1. Substitution Method:
- Guess a solution
- Plug it into the recurrence & solve for constants
- Use the initial conditions to evaluate constants

2. Iteration Method:
- Start with the initial conditions
- Iterate the recurrence to get successive terms

3. Homogeneous Linear Recurrence with Constant Coefficients:
- Guess a solution of the form an = c1rn-1 + c2rn-2 + ... + ckrk
- Plug into the recurrence & solve for constants ci
- Use the initial conditions to evaluate constants

4. Nonhomogeneous Recurrence:
- Guess a particular solution for the nonhomogeneous part
- Reduce to a homogeneous recurrence & solve
- Use superposition to get the general solution
