### Operations on relations for the notes of the Unit 1 - Set Theory in the subject of Discrete Structures & Theory of Logic

1. Union of relations:
- The union of two relations R and S over the same set A is the relation that includes all pairs that are either in R or in S or in both.
- It is denoted by R ∪ S.

2. Intersection of relations:
- The intersection of two relations R and S over the same set A is the relation that includes all pairs that are common to both R and S.
- It is denoted by R ∩ S.

3. Difference of relations:
- The difference of two relations R and S over the same set A is the relation that includes all pairs that belong to R but not to S.
- It is denoted by R − S.

4. Composition of relations:
- The composition of two relations R and S over sets A and B respectively is a relation over A and C defined by (xRy) ⇒ (zR(ySz)) for all x in A, y in B and z in C.
- It is denoted by S ◦ R or R;S.
