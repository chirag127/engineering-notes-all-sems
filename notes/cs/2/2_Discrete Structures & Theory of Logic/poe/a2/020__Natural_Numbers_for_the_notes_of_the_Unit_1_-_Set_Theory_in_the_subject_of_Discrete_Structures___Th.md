### Natural Numbers

1. The natural numbers are the positive integers starting from 1: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, and so on.
2. The natural numbers are used to count objects and measure quantities.
3. The natural numbers are infinite in number. There is no largest natural number.
4. The natural numbers are used as indices to identify positions in ordered lists or sequences.
5. The natural numbers form the most basic example of an ordered sequence of numbers in mathematics.
6. The fundamental properties of the natural numbers are:

- Each natural number has a unique successor, except 1.
- No two natural numbers have the same successor.
- 1 is the first natural number.
- The natural numbers are closed under the operation of taking successors.
