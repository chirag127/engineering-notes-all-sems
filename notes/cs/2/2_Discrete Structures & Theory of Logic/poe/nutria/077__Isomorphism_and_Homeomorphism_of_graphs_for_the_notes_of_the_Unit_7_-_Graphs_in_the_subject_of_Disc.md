
### Isomorphism and Homeomorphism of Graphs

* Isomorphism is an equivalence relation between two graphs, G and H, which states that there exists a bijective mapping between the vertices of G and the vertices of H such that the edges of G and the edges of H are in the same order.
* Homeomorphism is a stronger form of isomorphism, which states that the two graphs are not only equivalent but also have the same structure.
* In graph theory, isomorphism and homeomorphism are used to classify graphs according to their structure. 
* Isomorphism is used to determine whether two graphs are equivalent, while homeomorphism is used to determine whether two graphs are structurally equivalent.
* Isomorphism and homeomorphism are important concepts in graph theory, as they can be used to determine the structure of a graph and to identify patterns in graph data.
