
### Variants of Induction for the notes of the Unit 1 - Set Theory in the subject of Discrete Structures & Theory of Logic

* Mathematical induction is a method of proof used to prove statements involving natural numbers. It is a form of direct proof, and is sometimes called complete induction. 
* Strong induction is a method of proof used to prove statements involving natural numbers. It is a form of indirect proof, and is sometimes called complete induction. 
* Transfinite induction is a method of proof used to prove statements involving sets of numbers that are not necessarily finite. It is a form of direct proof, and is sometimes called complete induction. 
* Well-ordering principle is a method of proof used to prove statements involving sets of numbers that are not necessarily finite. It is a form of indirect proof, and is sometimes called complete induction. 
* Principle of mathematical induction is a method of proof used to prove statements involving sets of numbers that are not necessarily finite. It is a form of indirect proof, and is sometimes called complete induction.
