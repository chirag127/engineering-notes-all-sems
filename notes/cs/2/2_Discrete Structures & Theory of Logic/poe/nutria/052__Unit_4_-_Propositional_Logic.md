
## Unit 4 - Propositional Logic

1. Propositional logic is a branch of logic that deals with the study of propositions and their relationships.
2. A proposition is a statement that is either true or false.
3. Propositional logic is used to analyze the validity of arguments and to determine the truth value of a statement.
4. In propositional logic, the basic building blocks are called atomic propositions. 
5. Atomic propositions are statements that cannot be broken down into simpler components. 
6. Propositional logic uses logical connectives to combine two or more atomic propositions.
7. The most common logical connectives are AND, OR, NOT, IMPLIES, and IFF. 
8. Propositional logic is used to prove the validity of arguments and to determine whether a given statement is true or false.
9. Propositional logic can also be used to solve logical puzzles and to analyze the structure of arguments.
