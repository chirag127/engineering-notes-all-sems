

### Subgroups and Order for the Notes of the Unit 2 - Algebraic Structures in the Subject of Discrete Structures & Theory of Logic

1. Subgroups: A subgroup is a subset of a group that is itself a group with respect to the same operation. In other words, a subgroup is a group contained within another group.
2. Order: The order of a group is the number of elements in the group. A group of order two is called a cyclic group, and a group of order three is called an alternating group.
3. Closure: Closure is the property that all elements in the group remain in the group after the operation is applied.
4. Associativity: Associativity is the property that the order in which the operation is applied does not affect the result of the operation.
5. Identity Element: An identity element is an element in the group that leaves all other elements unchanged when the operation is applied.
6. Inverse Element: An inverse element is an element in the group that, when combined with another element, produces the identity element.
7. Lagrange's Theorem: Lagrange's theorem states that the order of a subgroup of a finite group must divide the order of the group.
8. Cauchy's Theorem: Cauchy's theorem states that every finite group contains an element of order two.
