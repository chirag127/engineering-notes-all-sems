
### Definition and Elementary Properties of Rings and Fields

* A **ring** is a set of elements, typically denoted by $R$, with two binary operations, usually called **addition** and **multiplication**, denoted by $+$ and $\cdot$ respectively, that satisfy certain properties. 
* The properties of a ring include closure, associativity, distributivity, and the existence of an additive and multiplicative identity. 
* A **field** is a ring with additional properties, such as the existence of multiplicative inverses, that allow for the division of elements in the field. 
* Rings and fields are examples of **algebraic structures**, which are sets with operations that satisfy certain properties. 
* Rings and fields are important in mathematics, as they are used to define algebraic equations and to study the properties of equations. 
* In particular, rings and fields are used to study the properties of polynomials, which are equations with multiple variables. 
* The study of rings and fields is known as **abstract algebra**, and is an important part of the study of mathematics.
