
### Logic Gates

Logic gates are the building blocks of digital circuits, allowing for the manipulation of binary data. They are used to create logic functions that can be used to control the flow of information in a circuit.

* **AND Gate**: The AND gate takes two inputs, A and B, and produces an output of 1 if both A and B are 1, otherwise it produces an output of 0.

* **OR Gate**: The OR gate takes two inputs, A and B, and produces an output of 1 if either A or B is 1, otherwise it produces an output of 0.

* **NOT Gate**: The NOT gate takes one input, A, and produces an output of 0 if A is 1, otherwise it produces an output of 1.

* **NAND Gate**: The NAND gate takes two inputs, A and B, and produces an output of 0 if both A and B are 1, otherwise it produces an output of 1.

* **NOR Gate**: The NOR gate takes two inputs, A and B, and produces an output of 0 if either A or B is 1, otherwise it produces an output of 1.

* **XOR Gate**: The XOR gate takes two inputs, A and B, and produces an output of 1 if A and B are different, otherwise it produces an output of 0.

* **XNOR Gate**: The XNOR gate takes two inputs, A and B, and produces an output of 1 if A and B are the same, otherwise it produces an output of 0.
