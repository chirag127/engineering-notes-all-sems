
### Proposition for the Notes of Unit 4 - Propositional Logic in Discrete Structures & Theory of Logic

1. Propositional logic is a branch of logic that deals with the study of propositions and their truth values.
2. A proposition is a statement that is either true or false.
3. Propositional logic studies the ways in which propositions can be combined and manipulated to form more complex statements.
4. Propositional logic can be used to determine the validity of arguments, as well as to construct proofs.
5. Propositional logic makes use of logical connectives, such as "and", "or", "not", "if-then" and "if and only if".
6. Propositional logic can also be used to represent knowledge in a formal way, by using symbols to represent facts and relationships between facts.
7. Propositional logic can be used to reason about problems in many different fields, including computer science, mathematics, philosophy, and artificial intelligence.
