
## Unit 3 - Lattices

* A lattice is a mathematical structure that consists of points, lines, and planes. It is a three-dimensional array of points that are connected by lines.
* Lattices can be used to represent the structure of crystals, molecules, and other physical objects.
* Lattices can also be used to describe the structure of abstract objects, such as groups, rings, and fields.
* A lattice is composed of an array of points. Each point can be connected to other points by lines.
* The number of lines connecting each point is called the coordination number.
* The coordination number of a lattice is determined by the number of points that are connected to each other.
* The lattice can be divided into unit cells, which are the smallest repeating unit in the lattice.
* Each unit cell contains a certain number of points, and the arrangement of these points determines the structure of the lattice.
* Lattices can be classified according to their symmetry, which is the arrangement of points in the lattice.
* The most common types of lattices are cubic, hexagonal, and tetragonal.
* Lattices can also be classified according to their Bravais lattice, which is the arrangement of points in the lattice.
* The Bravais lattice is determined by the number of points in the unit cell and the arrangement of these points.
* The Bravais lattice is the basis for the study of crystallography, which is the study of the structure of crystals.
