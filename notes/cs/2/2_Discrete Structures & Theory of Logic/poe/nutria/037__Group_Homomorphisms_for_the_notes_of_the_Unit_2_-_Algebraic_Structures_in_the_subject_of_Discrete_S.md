2. A homomorphism is a structure-preserving map between two algebraic objects.

5. An example of a homomorphism of groups is an isomorphism, which is a bijective homomorphism.

7. A homomorphism of groups can also be an automorphism, which is an isomorphism from a group to itself. 
8. A homomorphism of groups can also be an epimorphism, which is a surjective homomorphism. 
9. The kernel of a homomorphism of groups is the set of elements that map to the identity element of the target group. 
10. The image of a homomorphism of groups is the set of elements in the target group that are the images of elements in the source group.
