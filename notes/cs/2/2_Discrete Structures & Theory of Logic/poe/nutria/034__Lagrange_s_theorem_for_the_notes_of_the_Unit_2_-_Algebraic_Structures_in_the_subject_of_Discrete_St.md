
### Lagrange's Theorem

* Lagrange's theorem states that, for any finite group G, the size of the subgroup H of G is a divisor of the size of the group G.
* This theorem is named after Joseph-Louis Lagrange, an 18th century mathematician.
* In order to prove Lagrange's theorem, we need to use the concept of cosets. A coset is a subset of elements of a group G which can be obtained by multiplying any element of the group G by a fixed element of the subgroup H.
* The size of the coset is equal to the size of the subgroup H. This implies that the size of the subgroup H divides the size of the group G.
* Lagrange's theorem can be used to prove the existence of subgroups in a group. It can also be used to prove the uniqueness of the subgroups in a group.
