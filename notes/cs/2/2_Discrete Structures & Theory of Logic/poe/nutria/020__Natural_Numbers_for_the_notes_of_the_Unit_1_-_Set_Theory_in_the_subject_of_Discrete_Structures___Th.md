
### Natural Numbers for the notes of the Unit 1 - Set Theory in the subject of Discrete Structures & Theory of Logic

1. Natural numbers are a subset of the integers and are represented as a set of numbers that start from 1 and extend to infinity. 
2. The set of natural numbers can be represented using the notation N = {1, 2, 3, 4, ...}. 
3. Natural numbers are commonly used in counting and measuring. 
4. The set of natural numbers is closed under addition and multiplication, meaning that if two natural numbers are added or multiplied, the result is also a natural number. 
5. Natural numbers can also be used to define sets in set theory. 
6. For example, the set of even numbers can be defined as the set of natural numbers that are divisible by two. 
7. Natural numbers can also be used to define functions. 
8. For example, the function f(x) = x2 is defined for all natural numbers x. 
9. Natural numbers are also used in the study of discrete structures and theory of logic. 
10. For example, natural numbers can be used to represent states in finite state machines and to define algorithms.
