
## Unit 6 - Trees

1. Trees are an important part of the natural environment, providing habitats for wildlife and offering many benefits to humans. 
2. Trees are woody perennial plants, meaning they have a woody stem and live for more than two years. 
3. Trees are classified into two main groups: conifers and broadleaf trees. 
4. Conifers are evergreen trees that produce cones and needles, while broadleaf trees are deciduous and produce leaves. 
5. Trees are essential for the environment, providing habitats for animals, regulating air quality, and helping to reduce global warming by absorbing carbon dioxide. 
6. Trees also provide humans with many benefits, such as food, shelter, wood, and oxygen. 
7. Tree roots help to stabilize soil and prevent erosion, while branches and leaves provide shade and reduce the amount of sunlight that reaches the ground. 
8. Trees also provide food in the form of nuts, fruits, and sap. 
9. Trees are an important part of any landscape and can be used to create a beautiful and inviting space. 
10. Proper care and maintenance of trees is essential for their health and longevity.  This includes pruning, fertilizing, and watering.
