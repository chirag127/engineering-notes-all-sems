
## Unit 8 - Recurrence Relation & Generating function

1. **Recurrence Relation**: A recurrence relation is an equation that describes a sequence of numbers in terms of the preceding terms. It is a mathematical expression that allows us to define a sequence in terms of itself.

2. **Generating Function**: A generating function is a formal power series whose coefficients are related to the sequence of numbers being studied. Generating functions can be used to solve recurrence relations.

3. **Solving Recurrence Relations**: To solve a recurrence relation, we must first identify the pattern of the recurrence relation. Once the pattern is identified, we can use the generating function to solve for the nth term of the sequence.

4. **Applications of Recurrence Relations and Generating Functions**: Recurrence relations and generating functions can be used to solve problems in various fields such as computer science, engineering, economics, and biology. They can be used to model and solve problems involving sequences, probability, and optimization.
