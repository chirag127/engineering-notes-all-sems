
### Definition for the Notes of Unit 2 - Algebraic Structures in the Subject of Discrete Structures & Theory of Logic

1. Algebraic Structures are mathematical systems that consist of one or more sets, along with operations and relations between them. 
2. Examples of algebraic structures include groups, rings, fields, and lattices. 
3. A group is a set of elements with a binary operation that satisfies the following conditions: associativity, identity element, and inverse element. 
4. A ring is a set with two binary operations that satisfy certain conditions, such as associativity and distributivity. 
5. A field is a set with two binary operations, addition and multiplication, that satisfy certain conditions, such as associativity and distributivity. 
6. A lattice is a partially ordered set in which each two elements have a greatest lower bound and a least upper bound. 
7. Discrete Structures & Theory of Logic is the study of the properties of discrete structures and the logical relationships between them. 
8. Discrete Structures & Theory of Logic includes topics such as set theory, graph theory, Boolean algebra, and formal logic.
