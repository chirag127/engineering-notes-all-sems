
### Algebraic Manipulation of Boolean Expressions 

1. Boolean expressions are mathematical expressions that contain Boolean variables and operators. 
2. Boolean variables are either true or false and can be represented by the symbols 1 and 0, respectively. 
3. Boolean operators are AND, OR, NOT, XOR, NAND, NOR, and XNOR. 
4. Algebraic manipulation of Boolean expressions involves using the rules of Boolean algebra to simplify and manipulate expressions.
5. The basic rules of Boolean algebra are De Morgan's laws, absorption laws, and distributive laws. 
6. De Morgan's laws state that the negation of a conjunction is the disjunction of the negations, and the negation of a disjunction is the conjunction of the negations. 
7. The absorption laws state that the conjunction of a variable with its negation is always false, and the disjunction of a variable with its negation is always true. 
8. The distributive laws state that the conjunction of a variable and a disjunction is the same as the disjunction of the conjunctions of the variable and each term of the disjunction, and the disjunction of a variable and a conjunction is the same as the conjunction of the disjunctions of the variable and each term of the conjunction. 
9. Algebraic manipulation of Boolean expressions can be used to simplify expressions, to find equivalent expressions, and to analyze the behavior of digital circuits.
