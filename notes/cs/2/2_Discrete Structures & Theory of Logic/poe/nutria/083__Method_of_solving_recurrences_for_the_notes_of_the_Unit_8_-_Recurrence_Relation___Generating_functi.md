
### Method of Solving Recurrences

1. **Recurrence Relation:** A recurrence relation is an equation that describes a sequence of values in terms of the values that precede them.

2. **Generating Function:** A generating function is a formal power series that is used to solve a recurrence relation.

3. **Homogeneous Recurrences:** Homogeneous recurrences are recurrences that are of the form a_n = a_(n-1) + a_(n-2) + ... + a_(n-k). These recurrences can be solved using generating functions.

4. **Non-Homogeneous Recurrences:** Non-homogeneous recurrences are recurrences that are of the form a_n = a_(n-1) + a_(n-2) + ... + a_(n-k) + b_n. These recurrences can be solved using the method of undetermined coefficients.

5. **Linear Differential Equations:** Linear differential equations are equations of the form a_n' = a_(n-1)' + a_(n-2)' + ... + a_(n-k)'. These equations can be solved using the method of variation of parameters.
