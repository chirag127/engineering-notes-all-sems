### Method of solving recurrences for the notes of the Unit 8 - Recurrence Relation & Generating function in the subject of Discrete Structures & Theory of Logic

A recurrence relation is a mathematical equation that describes the relationship between consecutive terms in a sequence. Recurrence relations are commonly used to model various types of discrete systems, such as counting problems, dynamic programming, and random walks.

The method of solving recurrences involves finding a closed-form expression for the terms of a sequence, given its initial conditions and a recurrence relation. There are several methods for solving recurrences, including substitution, iteration, and generating functions.

The substitution method involves finding a closed-form expression for the terms of a sequence by substituting the recurrence relation into itself. The iteration method involves finding a closed-form expression for the terms of a sequence by iterating the recurrence relation.

Generating functions are a powerful tool for solving recurrences, as they allow us to convert a recurrence relation into a polynomial equation. Generating functions are particularly useful for solving linear recurrences, as they provide a simple and efficient method for finding the closed-form expression for the terms of a sequence.
