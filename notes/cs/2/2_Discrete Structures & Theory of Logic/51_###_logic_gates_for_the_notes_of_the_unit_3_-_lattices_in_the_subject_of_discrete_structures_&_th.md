### Logic gates for the notes of the Unit 3 - Lattices in the subject of Discrete Structures & Theory of Logic

Logic gates are electronic components that perform logical operations on one or more inputs and produce a single output. They are the basic building blocks of digital circuits, and are used to implement Boolean functions. There are several types of logic gates, including:

1. AND gate: The output of an AND gate is 1 if and only if all inputs are 1.

2. OR gate: The output of an OR gate is 1 if and only if at least one input is 1.

3. NOT gate: The output of a NOT gate is the negation of its input.

4. NAND gate: The output of a NAND gate is the negation of the output of an AND gate.

5. NOR gate: The output of a NOR gate is the negation of the output of an OR gate.

6. XOR gate: The output of an XOR gate is 1 if and only if exactly one input is 1.

7. XNOR gate: The output of an XNOR gate is the negation of the output of an XOR gate.

Logic gates are used in a variety of applications, including computer circuits, digital logic design, and control systems. They are also used in the design of complex digital systems, such as microprocessors, memory devices, and communication systems.
