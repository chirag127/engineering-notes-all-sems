## Unit 8 - Recurrence Relation & Generating function

Unit 8 of Discrete Structures & Theory of Logic focuses on the study of Recurrence Relations and Generating Functions.

A recurrence relation is an equation that defines a sequence of numbers in terms of one or more previous terms of the sequence. It is used to model various types of problems in computer science, mathematics, and engineering.

A generating function is a mathematical tool that is used to represent sequences of numbers as a single function. It provides a convenient way to manipulate sequences and to solve problems related to sequences.

The notes for Unit 8 should include the following topics:

1. Definition of a recurrence relation and its components (initial conditions, recurrence relation)
2. Solution of recurrence relations (homogeneous, non-homogeneous, characteristic equation)
3. Generating functions (definition, power series representation, generating function of a sequence)
4. Properties of generating functions (multiplication, differentiation, integration)
5. Applications of recurrence relations and generating functions (counting problems, solving differential equations, generating functions of combinatorial objects)

It is important to understand the concepts and properties of recurrence relations and generating functions in order to apply them in various fields and solve problems related to discrete structures and the theory of logic.
