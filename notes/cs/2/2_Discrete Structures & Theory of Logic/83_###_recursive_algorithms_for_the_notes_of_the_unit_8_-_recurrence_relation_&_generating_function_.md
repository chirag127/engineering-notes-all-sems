### Recursive algorithms for the notes of the Unit 8 - Recurrence Relation & Generating function in the subject of Discrete Structures & Theory of Logic

Recursive algorithms are a type of algorithm that solve problems by breaking them down into smaller sub-problems and solving those sub-problems recursively. In other words, a recursive algorithm is an algorithm that solves a problem by calling itself on smaller instances of the same problem.

Unit 8 of Discrete Structures & Theory of Logic focuses on the study of recurrence relations and generating functions. The notes for this unit should include the following topics:

1. Definition of recursive algorithms and their components (base case, recursive case)
2. Examples of recursive algorithms (factorial, Fibonacci sequence, binary search)
3. Properties of recursive algorithms (time complexity, space complexity, tail recursion)
4. Recurrence relation and its components (homogeneous and non-homogeneous recurrence relation)
5. Generating function and its components (ordinary generating function, exponential generating function)
6. Applications of recursive algorithms and generating functions (combinatorics, number theory, discrete mathematics)

Recursive algorithms and generating functions are important in the study of discrete structures and the theory of logic because they provide a way to solve problems in a concise and elegant manner. Understanding the concepts and properties of recursive algorithms and generating functions is essential for solving problems related to recurrence relations and for studying more advanced topics in computer science.
