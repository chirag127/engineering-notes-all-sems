# Real Time System

Real time systems are computer systems that must meet strict timing constraints in order to perform their intended function. These systems are designed to respond to events in a timely manner and provide a predictable response time. Some examples of real-time systems are air traffic control systems, automotive control systems, and medical monitoring systems.

Here are some key points to understand about real time systems:

- Real-time systems are used in applications where timing is critical to the success of the system.
- These systems have a set of requirements that must be met in order for the system to function correctly.
- Real-time systems are classified into hard real-time systems and soft real-time systems. Hard real-time systems have strict timing requirements that must be met, while soft real-time systems have more relaxed timing requirements.
- The timing requirements of real-time systems are typically measured in terms of response time, deadline, and jitter.
- Response time is the time it takes for a system to respond to an event.
- Deadline is the time by which a system must respond to an event.
- Jitter is the variation in response time.
- Real-time systems typically use specialized hardware and software to meet their timing requirements. For example, real-time operating systems (RTOS) are designed to provide deterministic response times.
- Real-time systems have a number of challenges that must be addressed, including scheduling, synchronization, and resource management.
- Real-time systems are becoming increasingly important in modern society, as they are used in a wide variety of applications, including aerospace, automotive, medical, and industrial control systems.
- Real-time systems are an important area of study for computer science and engineering students, as they require a deep understanding of computer architecture, operating systems, and software engineering principles.

In summary, real-time systems are an important and challenging area of computer science and engineering that are used in a wide variety of applications. Understanding the key concepts and techniques used in real-time systems is essential for anyone working in this field.
