
### Processors and Resources for the Notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction of Real Time System in the Subject of Real Time System

1. Processors: A processor is a central processing unit (CPU) that is responsible for executing instructions and managing the resources of a computer system. It is the main component of a computer system and is responsible for performing operations and executing instructions.

2. Resources: Resources are the physical and logical components of a computer system that are used to perform tasks. Examples of resources include memory, storage, and peripherals.

3. Real Time Systems: Real-time systems are computer systems that must respond to external events within a certain amount of time. These systems must be able to process data quickly and accurately in order to meet the required deadlines. Examples of real-time systems include embedded systems, process control systems, and robotics.

4. Scheduling Algorithms: Scheduling algorithms are used to determine the order in which processes should be executed. These algorithms take into account the amount of time required to execute each process and the availability of resources. Examples of scheduling algorithms include preemptive scheduling, non-preemptive scheduling, and priority scheduling.
