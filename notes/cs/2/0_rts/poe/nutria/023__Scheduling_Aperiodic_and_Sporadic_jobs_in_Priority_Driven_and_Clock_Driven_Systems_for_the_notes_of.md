
### Scheduling Aperiodic and Sporadic jobs in Priority Driven and Clock Driven Systems

1. Aperiodic jobs are those which arrive at unpredictable times and have varying service times. Examples of aperiodic jobs include user input, interruptions, and network messages. 
2. Sporadic jobs are those which have a predictable arrival time but an unpredictable service time. Examples of sporadic jobs include audio and video streaming. 
3. Priority driven systems prioritize jobs based on their priority values. The job with the highest priority value is serviced first. 
4. Clock driven systems use a clock to determine when a job should be serviced. The clock is set to a certain frequency and each job is serviced at that frequency. 
5. In priority driven systems, aperiodic jobs are serviced first and sporadic jobs are serviced after. 
6. In clock driven systems, both aperiodic and sporadic jobs are serviced at the same frequency.
