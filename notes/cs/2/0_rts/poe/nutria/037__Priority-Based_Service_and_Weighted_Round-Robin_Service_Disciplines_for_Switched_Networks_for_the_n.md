
### Priority-Based Service and Weighted Round-Robin Service Disciplines for Switched Networks

1. Priority-based service is a scheduling discipline used to allocate resources in switched networks. It ensures that higher priority traffic is given precedence over lower priority traffic.

2. Weighted round-robin (WRR) is a scheduling discipline used to allocate resources in switched networks. It ensures that each traffic flow is allocated a fair share of the resources.

3. In WRR, the total bandwidth of the network is divided into equal parts, and each traffic flow is allocated a portion of the bandwidth.

4. Priority-based service and WRR are two important scheduling disciplines used in real-time communication systems.

5. Priority-based service is useful for allocating resources to time-sensitive traffic, such as voice or video.

6. WRR is useful for allocating resources to non-time-sensitive traffic, such as email or web browsing.

7. Both scheduling disciplines can be used to ensure that all traffic flows are given a fair share of the resources.
