
### Typical Real Time Applications

1. Automotive systems: Automotive systems such as cruise control, engine control, and anti-lock brake systems are all examples of real-time applications.
2. Industrial Process Control: Process control systems are used to monitor and control industrial processes such as chemical plants, oil refineries, and paper mills.
3. Avionics: Avionics systems are used in aircrafts and spacecrafts to monitor and control the aircrafts.
4. Robotics: Robotics systems are used to control and monitor robots.
5. Embedded Systems: Embedded systems are used in a wide variety of applications such as consumer electronics, medical devices, and industrial automation.
6. Network Control: Network control systems are used to monitor and control networks such as LANs, WANs, and the Internet.
7. Video and Audio Processing: Video and audio processing systems are used to process and manipulate video and audio signals.
8. Telecommunications: Telecommunications systems are used to transmit and receive data and voice signals over a network.
9. Medical Systems: Medical systems are used to monitor and control medical devices and equipment.
10. Security Systems: Security systems are used to monitor and control access to buildings and other areas.
