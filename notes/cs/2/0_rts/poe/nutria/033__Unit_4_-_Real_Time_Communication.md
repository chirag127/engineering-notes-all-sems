
## Unit 4 - Real Time Communication

1. Real-time communication (RTC) is a type of communication that occurs in real-time, meaning that the participants in the conversation are able to interact with each other in the moment.

2. RTC is used in a variety of contexts, from online chat rooms and video conferencing to voice and video calls.

3. Many RTC technologies are based on the Internet Protocol (IP), which allows for the transmission of data over networks.

4. RTC technologies have become increasingly popular in recent years, as they allow for more efficient communication and collaboration between individuals and groups.

5. RTC is used in a variety of contexts, from online gaming and social networking to business and education.

6. In order to ensure the quality of RTC, it is important to use reliable networks and equipment, as well as appropriate protocols.

7. Additionally, it is important to ensure that the necessary security measures are in place to protect the privacy of users.
