
## Unit 2 - Real Time Scheduling

1. Real-time scheduling is the process of assigning tasks to resources in a timely manner in order to ensure that deadlines are met.
2. Real-time scheduling algorithms are used to manage the timing of tasks in order to guarantee the completion of the tasks within a given time frame.
3. Real-time scheduling algorithms are used in a variety of applications, such as embedded systems, robotics, computer networks, and multimedia systems.
4. The two main types of real-time scheduling algorithms are static and dynamic.
5. Static scheduling algorithms are used when the tasks and resources are known in advance and do not change over time.
6. Dynamic scheduling algorithms are used when the tasks and resources can change over time.
7. The two main criteria used to evaluate real-time scheduling algorithms are schedulability and response time.
8. Schedulability is the ability of the scheduling algorithm to guarantee that all tasks can be completed within the given time frame.
9. Response time is the time it takes for a task to be completed from the time it is submitted to the scheduling algorithm.
10. Real-time scheduling algorithms are used to ensure the timely completion of tasks and to guarantee that the tasks are completed within the given time frame.
