
### Non-preemptive Critical Sections

1. Non-preemptive critical sections are sections of code that are executed without interruption or preemption.
2. In a real-time system, non-preemptive critical sections are used to ensure that critical tasks are completed without interruption.
3. Non-preemptive sections are used to protect shared resources from concurrent access.
4. Non-preemptive sections are used to ensure that tasks are executed in the correct order.
5. Non-preemptive sections are used to guarantee that tasks are completed within a certain time frame.
6. Non-preemptive sections are used to ensure that tasks are not starved of resources.
7. Non-preemptive sections are used to guarantee the integrity of data.
8. Non-preemptive sections are used to guarantee the consistency of data.
9. Non-preemptive sections are used to protect critical sections of code from being interrupted.
10. Non-preemptive sections are used to prevent deadlock situations from occurring.
