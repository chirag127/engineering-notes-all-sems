
### Hard Real Time Systems 

1. Real Time Systems are systems that must guarantee the completion of specific tasks within a specific time frame. 
2. Hard Real Time Systems must guarantee the completion of specific tasks within a specific time frame, or else the system will fail.
3. Hard Real Time Systems are used in mission-critical applications, such as aerospace, defense, and medical systems.
4. Hard Real Time Systems have strict deadlines that must be met, and any delay in task completion can lead to catastrophic results.
5. Hard Real Time Systems require special hardware and software architectures in order to meet the deadlines.
6. Hard Real Time Systems must be designed, implemented, and tested with extreme care in order to ensure their reliability.
