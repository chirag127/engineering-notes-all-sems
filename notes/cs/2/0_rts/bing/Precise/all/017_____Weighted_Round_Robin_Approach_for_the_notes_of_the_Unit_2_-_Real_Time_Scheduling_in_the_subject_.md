### Weighted Round Robin Approach

Weighted Round Robin (WRR) is a scheduling algorithm used in real-time systems. It is an extension of the Round Robin algorithm, where each task is assigned a weight that determines the length of its time slice. The time slice of each task is proportional to its weight.

Here are the key points to remember about the Weighted Round Robin approach:

1. In WRR, tasks are assigned a weight, which determines the length of their time slice.
2. The time slice of each task is proportional to its weight.
3. Tasks with higher weights are given longer time slices, and therefore have a higher priority.
4. WRR is a fair scheduling algorithm, as it ensures that all tasks are given a chance to execute.
5. WRR is suitable for real-time systems where tasks have different priorities and execution times.

In summary, the Weighted Round Robin approach is a scheduling algorithm used in real-time systems, where tasks are assigned a weight that determines the length of their time slice. This approach ensures fairness and is suitable for systems where tasks have different priorities and execution times.
