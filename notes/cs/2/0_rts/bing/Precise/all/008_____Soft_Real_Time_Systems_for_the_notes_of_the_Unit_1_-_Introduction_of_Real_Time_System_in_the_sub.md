### Soft Real Time Systems

Soft real-time systems are systems where the completion of a task after its deadline may still be useful, but may result in degraded system performance. In other words, the system can tolerate occasional missed deadlines.

Some characteristics of soft real-time systems include:

1. The system is designed to meet the timing requirements of the majority of its tasks, but not all.
2. The system can continue to function even if some deadlines are missed.
3. The consequences of missing a deadline are not catastrophic, but may result in reduced system performance or quality of service.
4. The system may employ techniques such as dynamic scheduling and resource allocation to improve its ability to meet deadlines.

Examples of soft real-time systems include multimedia systems, online gaming, and some control systems.

It is important to note that the distinction between hard and soft real-time systems is not always clear-cut, and some systems may have both hard and soft real-time requirements. In such cases, the system must be designed to meet the hard real-time requirements, while also attempting to meet the soft real-time requirements as much as possible.
