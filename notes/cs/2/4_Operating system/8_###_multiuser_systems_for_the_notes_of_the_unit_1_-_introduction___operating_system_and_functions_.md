### Multiuser Systems for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction : Operating system and functions in the subject of Operating system

Multiuser systems are computer systems that allow multiple users to access and use the same computer resources simultaneously. These systems are designed to provide an environment where multiple users can work on the same computer, each with their own isolated environment and resources.

The operating system plays a crucial role in managing and coordinating the activities of multiple users in a multiuser system. It is responsible for allocating and managing the computer's resources, such as memory, CPU time, and disk space, and ensuring that each user has access to the resources they need.

Multiuser systems can be classified into two types: time-sharing systems and distributed systems.

In time-sharing systems, multiple users share the same computer, and the operating system allocates resources dynamically to each user based on their needs. This allows multiple users to work on the same computer simultaneously, each with their own isolated environment.

In distributed systems, multiple computers are connected together to form a network, and each computer acts as a separate node in the network. The operating system in a distributed system is responsible for managing the communication between the nodes, and for coordinating the distribution of resources and tasks among the nodes.

In this unit, we will study the concepts and principles of multiuser systems, and examine the role of the operating system in managing and coordinating the activities of multiple users. We will also study the different types of multiuser systems, and examine the challenges and issues involved in implementing and managing these systems.
