### Sleeping Barber Problem for the notes of the Unit 2 - Concurrent Processes in the subject of Operating system

The Sleeping Barber Problem is a classic problem in the study of concurrent processes. It is used to illustrate the concepts of synchronization, deadlocks, and inter-process communication.

The problem is set in a barbershop where customers arrive to get their hair cut. The barber is either cutting hair or sleeping. If the barber is sleeping and a customer arrives, the barber wakes up and cuts the customer's hair. If the barber is cutting hair and a customer arrives, the customer takes a seat in the waiting room. If there are no available seats in the waiting room, the customer leaves.

The Sleeping Barber Problem is used to demonstrate the importance of synchronization between processes and the need to coordinate access to shared resources. In the context of the barbershop, the shared resource is the barber's chair, which must be protected from concurrent access by multiple customers.

In the Operating System course, students will learn about the Sleeping Barber Problem as an example of a concurrent process problem. They will study the problem in detail and learn about the different approaches to solving it, including semaphores and monitors.
