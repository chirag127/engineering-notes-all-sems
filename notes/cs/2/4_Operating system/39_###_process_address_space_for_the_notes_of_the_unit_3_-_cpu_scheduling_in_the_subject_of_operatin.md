### Process address space for the notes of the Unit 3 - CPU Scheduling in the subject of Operating system

Process address space is a region of memory that is assigned to a process in a computer system. The address space of a process is used to store the code, data, and stack of the process.

In the Unit 3 - CPU Scheduling of the subject of Operating System, students learn about the concepts and algorithms involved in CPU scheduling and process management. The notes for this unit should include the following topics:

1. Definition of process address space and its purpose (memory allocation for processes)
2. Overview of process address space in computer systems (virtual memory, paging)
3. Structure of process address space (code, data, stack)
4. Protection of process address space (memory protection, access control)
5. Sharing of process address space (shared libraries, interprocess communication)
6. Comparison of process address space in different operating systems (Windows, Linux, Unix)

Process address space is an important part of learning CPU scheduling and process management. It provides a foundation for understanding the concepts and algorithms involved in memory management and helps students to understand the role of operating systems in managing the memory of concurrent processes.
