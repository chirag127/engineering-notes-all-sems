
### Mutual Exclusion for the Notes of Unit 2 - Concurrent Processes in Operating System

* Mutual exclusion is a technique used to ensure that only one process at a time can access a shared resource.
* To implement mutual exclusion, processes must use synchronization techniques, such as semaphores, monitors, or message passing. 
* Mutual exclusion is important in order to prevent race conditions, which can lead to unpredictable results and system instability.
* Mutual exclusion can be implemented using hardware or software solutions. Hardware solutions are typically faster, but require more complex hardware. Software solutions are typically less expensive, but require more complex software. 
* Mutual exclusion is used in a variety of operating system processes, such as resource allocation and scheduling. It is also used to ensure that processes do not interfere with each other when accessing shared resources.
