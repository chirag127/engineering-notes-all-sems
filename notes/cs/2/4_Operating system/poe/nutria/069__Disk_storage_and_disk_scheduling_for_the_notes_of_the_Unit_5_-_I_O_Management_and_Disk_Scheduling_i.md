
### Disk Storage and Disk Scheduling

* Disk storage is a form of computer data storage that stores information on a physical disk. It is typically used to store large amounts of data that can be accessed quickly. 

* Disk scheduling is the process of determining which requests for disk access should be serviced first. It is important for the efficient operation of the computer system, as it allows the disk to be used more efficiently. 

* The most common type of disk scheduling algorithms are First Come First Serve (FCFS), Shortest Seek Time First (SSTF), and SCAN. 

* FCFS is the simplest algorithm, as it services requests in the order that they are received. 

* SSTF is more efficient than FCFS, as it services requests based on the amount of time it will take to move the disk arm to the requested sector. 

* SCAN is the most efficient algorithm, as it services requests in a single direction, and then reverses direction when it reaches the end of the disk.
