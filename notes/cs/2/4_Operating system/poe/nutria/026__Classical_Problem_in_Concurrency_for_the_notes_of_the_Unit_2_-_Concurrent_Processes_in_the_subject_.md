

### Classical Problems in Concurrency

1. **Mutual Exclusion:** This is a situation in which two or more processes are attempting to access a shared resource, such as a file or a printer, at the same time. This can lead to data corruption or deadlock.

2. **Deadlock:** This is a situation in which two or more processes are waiting for each other to finish, creating a cycle that prevents any of the processes from progressing.

3. **Starvation:** This is a situation in which one or more processes are prevented from accessing a shared resource due to the actions of other processes.

4. **Race Condition:** This is a situation in which the result of a computation depends on the order in which processes are scheduled to access a shared resource.

5. **Livelock:** This is a situation in which two or more processes are constantly attempting to access a shared resource without making any progress.
