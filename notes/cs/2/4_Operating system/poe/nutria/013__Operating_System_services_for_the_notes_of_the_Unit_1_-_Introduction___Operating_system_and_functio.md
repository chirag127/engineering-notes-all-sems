
### Operating System Services

1. **Process Management**: Process management is an important function of an operating system. It involves the creation, scheduling, and termination of processes, as well as the sharing of resources between them.
2. **Memory Management**: Memory management is the process of allocating and managing memory in a computer system. It includes the allocation of physical memory, virtual memory, and other resources.
3. **File Management**: File management is the process of organizing, storing, and retrieving data from a computer system. It includes creating, deleting, and managing files and directories.
4. **Device Management**: Device management is the process of managing and controlling the use of hardware devices in a computer system. It involves the installation, configuration, and maintenance of devices such as printers, scanners, and other peripherals.
5. **Network Management**: Network management is the process of managing and controlling the use of a computer network. It involves the configuration, monitoring, and maintenance of network components such as routers, switches, and other devices.
6. **Security Management**: Security management is the process of protecting a computer system from unauthorized access. It involves the implementation of security policies, the monitoring of network traffic, and the enforcement of security measures.
