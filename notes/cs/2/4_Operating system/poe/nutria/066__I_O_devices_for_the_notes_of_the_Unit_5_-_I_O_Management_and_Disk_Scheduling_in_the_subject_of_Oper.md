
### I/O Devices

* Input devices are used to enter data into a computer. Examples include keyboards, mice, touchscreens, scanners, and digital cameras.
* Output devices are used to display the results of a computer's processing. Examples include monitors, printers, and speakers.
* Storage devices are used to store data and programs. Examples include hard disks, SSDs, optical disks, and flash drives.
* Network devices are used to connect computers and other devices to a network. Examples include routers, switches, and modems.
* Peripheral devices are used to provide additional functionality to a computer. Examples include game controllers, webcams, and microphones.
* I/O management is the process of controlling the flow of data between the CPU and I/O devices.
* Disk scheduling is the process of deciding which requests for disk access should be serviced first. Common algorithms include First Come First Serve, Shortest Seek Time First, and SCAN.
