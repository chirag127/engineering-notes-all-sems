
### Scheduling Concepts for the notes of the Unit 3 - CPU Scheduling in the subject of Operating System

1. CPU scheduling is the process of deciding which process in a multi-process system should be allocated to the processor at any given time.
2. The goal of CPU scheduling is to optimize the system's performance and provide a fair share of the processor to each process.
3. CPU scheduling algorithms can be divided into two categories: preemptive and non-preemptive.
4. Preemptive scheduling algorithms are based on the concept of priority. The scheduler selects the process with the highest priority for execution.
5. Non-preemptive scheduling algorithms are based on the concept of fairness. The scheduler selects the process with the shortest remaining time for execution.
