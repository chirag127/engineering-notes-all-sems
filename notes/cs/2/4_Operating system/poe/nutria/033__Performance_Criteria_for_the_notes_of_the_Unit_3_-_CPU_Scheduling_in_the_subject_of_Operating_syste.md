
### Performance Criteria for the Notes of Unit 3 - CPU Scheduling in Operating System
1. Understand the basic concepts of CPU scheduling and its components such as processes, threads, and scheduling algorithms.
2. Identify the different types of CPU scheduling algorithms such as first-come first-served (FCFS), shortest job first (SJF), and round-robin (RR).
3. Analyze the performance of CPU scheduling algorithms in terms of throughput, turnaround time, and waiting time.
4. Compare and contrast the different algorithms and their performance.
5. Explain the concept of priority scheduling and its associated algorithms.
6. Identify the different types of priority scheduling algorithms such as static and dynamic priority scheduling.
7. Analyze the performance of priority scheduling algorithms in terms of throughput, turnaround time, and waiting time.
8. Explain the concept of multi-level queue scheduling and its associated algorithms.
9. Understand the process of implementing and managing CPU scheduling in an operating system.
10. Identify the different types of CPU scheduling algorithms used in different operating systems.
