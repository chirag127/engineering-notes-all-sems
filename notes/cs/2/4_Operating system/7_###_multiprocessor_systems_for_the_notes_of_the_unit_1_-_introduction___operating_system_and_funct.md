### Multiprocessor Systems for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction : Operating system and functions in the subject of Operating system

A multiprocessor system is a computer system that consists of multiple processors working together to perform tasks. In this type of system, multiple processors share the same memory and I/O resources and work together to complete tasks.

In the Unit 1 - Introduction : Operating system and functions of the subject of Operating system, students learn about the basic concepts of operating systems and their functions. The notes for this unit should include the following topics:

1. Definition of a multiprocessor system and its components (processors, memory, I/O)
2. Types of multiprocessor systems (symmetric, asymmetric, distributed)
3. Advantages and disadvantages of multiprocessor systems (performance, cost, reliability)
4. Operating system support for multiprocessor systems (scheduling, synchronization, memory management)
5. Examples of multiprocessor systems (servers, supercomputers, workstations)
6. Comparison with single-processor systems (performance, cost, reliability)

Multiprocessor systems are an important part of computer systems and play a crucial role in the field of operating systems. Understanding the concepts and algorithms involved in multiprocessor systems is essential for solving problems related to operating systems, computer architecture, and computer engineering.
