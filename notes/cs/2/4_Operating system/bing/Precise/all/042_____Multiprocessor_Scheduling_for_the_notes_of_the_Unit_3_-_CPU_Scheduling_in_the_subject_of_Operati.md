### Multiprocessor Scheduling

Multiprocessor scheduling, also known as multiple processor scheduling, focuses on designing the system's scheduling function, which consists of more than one processor. In multiprocessor scheduling, multiple CPUs share the workload (load sharing), allowing multiple tasks to operate concurrently .

A multiprocessor is a system that has more than one processor but shares the same memory, bus, and input/output devices. The scheduling process of a multiprocessor is more complex than that of a single processor system .

Some key points to remember about multiprocessor scheduling are:

- Multiple CPUs share the load (load sharing) in multiprocessor scheduling so that various processes run simultaneously .
- A multiprocessor is a system that has more than one processor but shares the same memory, bus, and input/output devices .
- The scheduling process of a multiprocessor is more complex than that of a single processor system .
