### Time sharing for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction : Operating system and functions in the subject of Operating system

Time sharing is a method of computer resource management that allows multiple users to share a single computer system simultaneously. It works by dividing the computer's processing time into small slices, called time slices, and allocating each time slice to a different user. This allows multiple users to access the computer system at the same time and perform their own tasks.

In the Unit 1 - Introduction: Operating System and Functions, students learn about the basic concepts and functions of operating systems. The notes for this unit should include the following topics:

1. Definition of time sharing and its advantages (resource sharing, increased efficiency)
2. Overview of time sharing in computer systems (time slices, multitasking)
3. Comparison with other resource allocation methods (batch processing, multiprogramming)
4. Implementation of time sharing in operating systems (scheduling algorithms, context switching)
5. Limitations of time sharing (response time, security)
6. Evolution of time sharing in computer systems (mainframe computers, personal computers)

Time sharing is an important part of learning operating systems. It provides a foundation for understanding the concepts and algorithms involved in computer resource management and helps students to understand the role of operating systems in managing computer resources.
