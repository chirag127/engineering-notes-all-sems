### Software Crisis

- Software crisis is a term used to describe the difficulty of writing useful and efficient computer programs in the required time.
- Software crisis was due to the rapid increases in computer power and the complexity of the problems that could not be tackled with the existing software development methods.
- Some of the causes of software crisis are:
  - Poor project management and planning
  - Lack of skilled developers and programmers
  - Under-specified or changing requirements
  - Low quality and reliability of software products
  - High cost and maintenance of software systems
  - Inefficient and outdated software development processes
- Some of the solutions to software crisis are:
  - Adopting agile and iterative software development methodologies
  - Using modern tools and technologies to automate and streamline software development tasks
  - Applying software engineering principles and best practices to ensure quality and efficiency
  - Improving communication and collaboration among stakeholders and team members
  - Focusing on solving the most important and interesting problems that add value to the users and society
- A possible mnemonic to remember the causes of software crisis is **PULCH** (Poor management, Under-specified requirements, Low quality, Costly maintenance, High complexity).
- A possible mnemonic to remember the solutions to software crisis is **AUFIC** (Agile methods, Updated tools, Focused problems, Improved communication, Quality engineering).
