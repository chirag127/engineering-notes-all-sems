### ISO 9000 Models in SRS

- ISO 9000 is a set of international standards on quality management and quality assurance developed to help organizations effectively document the quality system elements needed to maintain an efficient quality system.
- ISO 9000 is not specific to any one industry and can be applied to organizations of any size. It can help a company satisfy its customers, meet regulatory requirements, and achieve continual improvement.
- ISO 9000 is a series, or family, of quality management standards, while ISO 9001 is a standard within the family. The ISO 9000 family of standards also contains an individual standard named ISO 9000, which lays out the fundamentals and vocabulary for quality management systems (QMS) .
- ISO 9000 series of standards are based on seven quality management principles that senior management can apply to promote organizational improvement. These are:
  - Customer focus: Understand the needs of existing and future customers, align organizational objectives with customer needs and expectations, and meet or exceed customer requirements.
  - Leadership: Establish a vision and direction for the organization, create and maintain an internal environment that encourages people to achieve the organization's objectives, and provide the resources and support needed for the QMS.
  - Engagement of people: Recognize, empower, and involve people at all levels as essential contributors to the QMS, enhance their competence and satisfaction, and promote collaboration and innovation.
  - Process approach: Manage activities and resources as processes, identify interrelated processes and their inputs and outputs, measure and control process performance, and improve processes based on data and analysis.
  - Improvement: Adopt a culture of improvement, align improvement activities with strategic objectives, implement improvement projects using a systematic approach, and learn from past successes and failures.
  - Evidence-based decision making: Base decisions on the analysis and evaluation of data and information, ensure the availability and reliability of data and information, use appropriate methods and tools for analysis and evaluation, and communicate and monitor the results and actions of decisions.
  - Relationship management: Identify and select relevant external providers and partners, establish and maintain mutually beneficial relationships, agree on clear and consistent expectations, and monitor and review performance and feedback.
- ISO 9000 series of standards also contains guidelines for the application of ISO 9001 to the development, supply, installation, and maintenance of computer software. This is known as ISO 9000-3:1997. It provides guidance on how to interpret and implement the requirements of ISO 9001 in the context of software engineering, such as planning, design, testing, delivery, and maintenance.
- ISO 9000 series of standards can benefit software engineering in various ways, such as :
  - Improving the documentation of organizational processes, making them more transparent, consistent, and traceable.
  - Enhancing the quality of software products and services, reducing defects, errors, and failures, and increasing customer satisfaction and loyalty.
  - Increasing the efficiency and effectiveness of software development and maintenance, reducing wastage, rework, and costs, and optimizing the use of resources and time.
  - Facilitating the compliance with legal and regulatory requirements, such as safety, security, and privacy, and minimizing the risks of litigation and penalties.
  - Promoting a culture of continuous improvement, innovation, and learning, and fostering a positive image and reputation for the organization.
