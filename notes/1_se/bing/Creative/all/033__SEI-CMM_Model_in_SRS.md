### SEI-CMM Model in SRS

- SEI-CMM stands for Software Engineering Institute - Capability Maturity Model.
- It is a methodology used to develop and refine an organization's software development process.
- It describes a five-level evolutionary path of increasingly organized and systematically more mature processes.
- It does not specify a particular process or sequence, but provides a reference for comparing actual software engineering practices against the essential elements of good software engineering.
- It was developed and promoted by the Software Engineering Institute (SEI), a research and development center sponsored by the U.S. Department of Defense (DOD) and now part of Carnegie Mellon University.
- It is similar to ISO 9001, one of the ISO 9000 series of standards specified by the International Organization for Standardization, but more explicit in defining the means for continuous process improvement.
- It is now superseded by the CMM Integration (CMMI), which is an evolution of CMM that integrates various disciplines of software engineering, such as systems engineering, software engineering, and integrated product and process development.
- SRS stands for Software Requirements Specification, which is a document that describes the requirements and specifications of a software system or product.
- SRS is one of the key process areas in the SEI-CMM model, as it defines the scope, functionality, quality, and constraints of the software system or product.
- SRS is also one of the key deliverables in the software development life cycle, as it serves as the basis for design, development, testing, and maintenance of the software system or product.
- SRS should be clear, complete, consistent, traceable, verifiable, and modifiable, according to the SEI-CMM model.

Some possible mnemonics and learning tricks for the SEI-CMM model in SRS are:

- To remember the five levels of maturity, use the acronym IRDMO: Initial, Repeatable, Defined, Managed, Optimizing.
- To remember the key process areas for each level of maturity, use the following phrases:

  - Level 2: Repeatable - Requirements Management, Software Project Planning, Software Project Tracking and Oversight, Software Subcontract Management, Software Quality Assurance, Software Configuration Management.
    - Phrase: Repeat after me: Requirements, Planning, Tracking, Subcontracting, Assurance, Configuration.
  - Level 3: Defined - Organization Process Focus, Organization Process Definition, Training Program, Integrated Software Management, Software Product Engineering, Intergroup Coordination, Peer Reviews.
    - Phrase: Define your organization: Focus, Definition, Training, Integration, Engineering, Coordination, Reviews.
  - Level 4: Managed - Quantitative Process Management, Software Quality Management.
    - Phrase: Manage your process: Quantify, Quality.
  - Level 5: Optimizing - Defect Prevention, Technology Change Management, Process Change Management.
    - Phrase: Optimize your performance: Prevent, Change, Change.

- To remember the characteristics of a good SRS, use the acronym C2TVCM: Clear, Complete, Consistent, Traceable, Verifiable, Modifiable.
