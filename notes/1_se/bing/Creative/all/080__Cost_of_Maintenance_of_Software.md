### Cost of Maintenance of Software

- Software maintenance is the process of modifying, updating, enhancing, or fixing software after its deployment to ensure its functionality, performance, security, and usability.
- Software maintenance costs are the expenses incurred by software owners or users to keep the software operational and up-to-date throughout its life cycle.
- Software maintenance costs can vary depending on several factors, such as:
  - The type and complexity of the software and its underlying infrastructure.
  - The number and frequency of software changes or updates required.
  - The number and type of software users and their feedback or requests.
  - The availability and quality of software documentation and source code.
  - The composition and expertise of the software maintenance team and their sourcing model (in-house or outsourced).
  - The type and scope of software maintenance activities (corrective, adaptive, preventive, or perfective).
  - The time coverage and service level agreement (SLA) of the software maintenance service provider.
- According to some estimates, software maintenance costs can range from $5,000 to $50,000+ per month, or 15-20% of the initial development costs per year . In total, software maintenance costs can make up to 90% of the software total cost of ownership (TCO).
- Software maintenance costs can be reduced by following some best practices, such as:
  - Adopting a modular and scalable software architecture that allows for easy changes and updates.
  - Using standard and well-documented coding practices and tools that facilitate software maintenance and testing.
  - Implementing preventive and perfective maintenance activities regularly to avoid software deterioration and enhance software quality.
  - Collecting and analyzing user feedback and software performance metrics to identify and prioritize software maintenance needs and issues.
  - Choosing a reliable and experienced software maintenance service provider that can offer cost-effective and high-quality solutions.
