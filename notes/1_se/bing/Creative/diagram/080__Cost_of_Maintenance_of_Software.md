The cost of maintenance of software is the ongoing expense of keeping the software operational, secure, and up-to-date. It includes activities such as fixing errors, adapting to changes in the environment, preventing deterioration, and enhancing performance and usability. The cost of maintenance of software depends on various factors, such as the type and complexity of the software, the number of users, the availability and quality of the documentation, the team composition and seniority, and the scope and frequency of the maintenance activities.
