A walk through (walkthrough) is a type of static testing technique that involves a presentation of the code or document by the author to a group of peers, who then give their feedback and suggestions. The purpose of a walk through is to improve the quality of the product, find defects, and resolve issues. A walk through is usually informal and does not follow a strict procedure or checklist. The participants of a walk through are the author, the moderator, the scribe, the reviewers, and sometimes the manager. The walk through is a collaborative and constructive process that helps the author improve their work and find defects early in the development cycle.
