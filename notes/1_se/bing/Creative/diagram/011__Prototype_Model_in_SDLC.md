The prototype model in SDLC is a methodology that involves developing a preliminary version of the software or system, testing it with the users or customers, and refining it based on their feedback until a satisfactory prototype is achieved. The prototype model is useful when the requirements are not fully known or there is a need for constant updates based on user satisfaction. It can be considered as a trial-and-error method that involves the developers and the users.
