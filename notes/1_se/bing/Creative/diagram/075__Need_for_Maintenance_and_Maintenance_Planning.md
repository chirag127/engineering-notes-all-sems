The need for maintenance and maintenance planning is to ensure the reliability, safety, and productivity of the assets and equipment used for operation. Maintenance planning is the process of deciding which assets and equipment need maintenance, what combination of inspections and tasks they require, and how often all this needs to happen. Maintenance scheduling is the process of deciding when to do the work and who should perform it. An effective maintenance plan should include the following elements:

- The scope and purpose of the maintenance plan
- The team and skills required for the maintenance tasks
- The work instructions and procedures for the maintenance tasks
- The schedule and priority of the maintenance tasks
- The spare parts and tools needed for the maintenance tasks
- The contractors and vendors involved in the maintenance tasks
