Resource Allocation Models (RAIM) in software project management are methods or frameworks for allocating resources to project tasks in order to achieve project objectives. Resources can be material, equipment, financial, or human resources. Resource allocation involves identifying the right resources, their skills, capacity, availability, and utilization, and assigning them to the right tasks at the right time.
