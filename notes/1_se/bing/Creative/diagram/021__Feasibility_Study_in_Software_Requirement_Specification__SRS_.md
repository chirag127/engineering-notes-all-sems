A feasibility study is a phase in the software development lifecycle (SDLC) that evaluates the technical, economic, legal, operational, and schedule aspects of a project to determine its viability and potential risks. A feasibility study helps to identify the best solution among the available alternatives and to estimate the resources, time, and cost required to implement it. A feasibility study is usually conducted after the requirement analysis phase, where the software requirements specification (SRS) document is created. The SRS document describes what the software will do and how it will be expected to perform, as well as the functionality the product needs to fulfill the needs of all stakeholders.
