## Unit 4 - Software Testing

Software testing is the process of validating that a piece of software meets its business and technical requirements. Software testing can be performed at different levels and stages of a software development life cycle, such as unit testing, integration testing, system testing, acceptance testing, etc. Software testing can also follow different approaches and methodologies, such as waterfall, agile, scrum, v-model, etc.

One way to illustrate the software testing process is to use a flowchart, which is a diagram that shows the sequence of steps and decisions involved in a process. A flowchart can help software testers to plan, execute, and report the testing activities, as well as to communicate and collaborate with other stakeholders.

A software testing process flowchart typically consists of the following elements:

- Ovals: For start and stop points of the process
- Rectangles: For tasks or actions performed in the process
- Diamonds: For decisions or branching points in the process
- Arrows: For the direction and flow of the process
