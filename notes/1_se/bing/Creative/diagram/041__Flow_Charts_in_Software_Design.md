A flowchart is a graphical representation of an algorithm or a process that uses symbols and connectors to show the flow of information and actions. Flowcharts are useful for designing, analyzing, documenting, and managing software systems. There are different types of flowcharts, such as system flowcharts, data flowcharts, program flowcharts, and more, depending on the purpose and level of detail.

To draw a flowchart, you need to identify the steps, decisions, inputs, and outputs of the process, and use standard symbols to represent them. You also need to use connectors to show the direction and sequence of the flow. You can use tools like Microsoft Visio, Lucidchart, diagrams.net, or Textografo to create flowcharts online or offline, or you can use ASCII characters to draw simple flowcharts in plain text.
