The Requirement Engineering Process in SRS is the process of eliciting, analyzing, specifying, validating, and managing the software requirements for a system. It aims to establish a clear and complete understanding of what the system should do and how it should perform. The Requirement Engineering Process in SRS typically consists of the following steps:

- Feasibility study: This step involves determining whether the proposed system is feasible, desirable, and cost-effective for the customer and the organization. It also identifies the main objectives, scope, and constraints of the system.
- Requirements elicitation: This step involves gathering the software requirements from various sources, such as stakeholders, users, documents, existing systems, and domain experts. It also involves resolving any conflicts or ambiguities among the requirements.
- Requirements analysis: This step involves analyzing the software requirements to ensure that they are clear, consistent, complete, correct, and testable. It also involves prioritizing, organizing, and modeling the requirements using various techniques, such as use cases, scenarios, data flow diagrams, etc.
- Requirements specification: This step involves documenting the software requirements in a formal and structured way, using a standard notation or language, such as IEEE 830-1998. It also involves defining the functional and non-functional requirements, as well as the system qualities, constraints, and assumptions.
- Requirements validation: This step involves verifying that the software requirements meet the customer's needs and expectations, and that they conform to the quality standards and regulations. It also involves reviewing, inspecting, and testing the requirements with the stakeholders, users, and experts.
- Requirements management: This step involves controlling and tracking the changes to the software requirements throughout the system development lifecycle. It also involves maintaining the traceability, consistency, and completeness of the requirements.
