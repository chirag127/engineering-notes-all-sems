Schedule/Duration of Maintenance in software project management is the process of planning and executing the activities that are required to keep the software product in a functional and reliable state. Maintenance can include corrective, adaptive, perfective, and preventive actions that address errors, changes, enhancements, and risks in the software system. The schedule and duration of maintenance depend on various factors, such as the complexity of the software, the availability of resources, the frequency of updates, the user feedback, and the contractual obligations.
