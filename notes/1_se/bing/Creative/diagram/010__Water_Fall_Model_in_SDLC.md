The waterfall model in SDLC is a linear, sequential approach to software development that divides the project into different phases. Each phase must be completed before the next phase can begin and there is no overlapping in the phases. The phases are:

- Requirement gathering and analysis: All possible requirements of the system to be developed are captured and documented.
- System design: The requirement specifications are studied and the system design is prepared. This specifies the hardware and system requirements and defines the overall system architecture.
- Implementation: The system is developed in small units, which are tested for their functionality.
- Integration and testing: All the units are integrated into a system and tested for any faults and failures.
- Deployment of system: The product is deployed in the customer environment or released into the market.
- Maintenance: The product is maintained to fix any issues or enhance its features.
