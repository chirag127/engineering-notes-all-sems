#### Compliance with Design and Coding Standards (Coding Standards) Static testing strategy

- Compliance with design and coding standards is a static testing technique that involves checking the software artifacts against predefined rules, guidelines, and best practices.
- The purpose of this technique is to ensure consistency, readability, maintainability, and quality of the software products.
- The benefits of this technique are:
  - It can detect defects and deviations from the standards early in the development process, reducing the cost and effort of fixing them later.
  - It can improve the communication and collaboration among the developers, reviewers, and testers, as they share a common understanding of the expected standards.
  - It can facilitate the reuse and integration of the software components, as they follow the same conventions and formats.
  - It can enhance the security and reliability of the software products, as they adhere to the established principles and practices.
- The steps of this technique are:
  - Define the design and coding standards for the software project, based on the requirements, specifications, and architecture of the system.
  - Review the design and coding standards with the stakeholders, and obtain their approval and feedback.
  - Communicate the design and coding standards to the developers, and provide them with the necessary tools and resources to follow them.
  - Check the software artifacts against the design and coding standards, using manual or automated methods, such as inspections, reviews, walkthroughs, checklists, or static analysis tools.
  - Report and document the results of the compliance checks, and identify the non-compliant items and the corrective actions needed.
  - Verify and validate the compliance of the software artifacts, and ensure that the standards are met and the defects are resolved.
