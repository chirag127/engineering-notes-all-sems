#### Preventive Maintenance (PM) of Software

- Preventive maintenance (PM) of software is the process of improving or correcting software defects before they cause failures or affect the performance of the system.
- PM of software can be classified into two types: perfective maintenance and adaptive maintenance.
- Perfective maintenance is the process of enhancing the functionality, usability, or efficiency of software by adding new features, improving the user interface, or optimizing the code.
- Adaptive maintenance is the process of modifying the software to cope with changes in the environment, such as new hardware, operating systems, standards, or user requirements.
- PM of software can provide several benefits, such as:
  - Reducing the risk of software failures and errors
  - Increasing the reliability and availability of the system
  - Extending the lifespan and usability of the software
  - Improving the customer satisfaction and loyalty
  - Reducing the cost and effort of corrective maintenance
- PM of software can also pose some challenges, such as:
  - Requiring a clear and updated documentation of the software
  - Requiring a systematic and rigorous testing and quality assurance process
  - Requiring a skilled and experienced development team
  - Requiring a careful planning and prioritization of the maintenance tasks
  - Requiring a constant monitoring and evaluation of the software performance and feedback
