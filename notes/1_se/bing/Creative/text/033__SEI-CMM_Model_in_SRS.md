### SEI-CMM Model in SRS

- SEI-CMM stands for Software Engineering Institute - Capability Maturity Model.
- It is a methodology used to develop and refine an organization's software development process.
- It describes a five-level evolutionary path of increasingly organized and systematically more mature processes.
- It was developed and promoted by the SEI, a research and development center sponsored by the U.S. Department of Defense and now part of Carnegie Mellon University.
- It is based on the principle that the quality of a software product is largely determined by the quality of the process used to develop and maintain it.
- It provides a reference for comparing actual software engineering practices against the essential elements of good software engineering.
- It does not specify a particular process or sequence, but rather a set of best practices that can be tailored to the specific needs and goals of each organization.
- It also provides a framework for continuous process improvement and a way to measure the progress and maturity of the software development process.
- It is similar to ISO 9001, one of the ISO 9000 series of standards specified by the International Organization for Standardization, but more explicit and detailed in defining the means to achieve good software quality.
- It has been widely adopted by many organizations in various domains and industries, and has been proven to improve software quality, productivity, customer satisfaction, and business performance.
- It has evolved into CMMI (Capability Maturity Model Integration), which is a more comprehensive and integrated model that covers not only software engineering, but also systems engineering, hardware engineering, and other disciplines.
