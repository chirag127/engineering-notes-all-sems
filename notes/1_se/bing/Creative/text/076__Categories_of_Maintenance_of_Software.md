### Categories of Maintenance of Software

Software maintenance is the process of modifying and updating software after it has been delivered to the end user. Software maintenance can be classified into four categories:

- **Corrective maintenance**: This is the process of fixing errors or bugs in the software that are discovered after the software is deployed. Corrective maintenance can be reactive (responding to reported problems) or proactive (anticipating and preventing potential problems).
- **Adaptive maintenance**: This is the process of adapting the software to changes in the environment, such as new hardware, operating systems, standards, or user requirements. Adaptive maintenance can involve adding new features, modifying existing features, or removing obsolete features.
- **Perfective maintenance**: This is the process of improving the quality or performance of the software, such as enhancing the usability, efficiency, reliability, or security of the software. Perfective maintenance can involve refactoring, optimizing, or redesigning the software.
- **Preventive maintenance**: This is the process of preventing future problems or reducing the complexity of the software, such as removing dead code, updating documentation, or improving the maintainability of the software. Preventive maintenance can involve reengineering, restructuring, or reorganizing the software.
