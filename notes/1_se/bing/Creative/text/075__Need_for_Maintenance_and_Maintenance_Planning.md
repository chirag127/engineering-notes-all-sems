### Need for Maintenance and Maintenance Planning

- Maintenance is the process of keeping a system or equipment in a good working condition by performing regular inspections, repairs, replacements, or overhauls.
- Maintenance is necessary to ensure the reliability, availability, safety, and efficiency of a system or equipment.
- Maintenance can also improve the performance, quality, and productivity of a system or equipment, as well as reduce the operating costs, environmental impacts, and risks of failures or breakdowns.
- Maintenance planning is the process of determining the maintenance activities, resources, schedules, and procedures for a system or equipment.
- Maintenance planning is necessary to optimize the maintenance process and achieve the desired maintenance objectives and outcomes.
- Maintenance planning can also help to coordinate the maintenance activities with the operational activities, minimize the downtime and disruptions, maximize the utilization and lifespan of the system or equipment, and comply with the regulatory and contractual requirements.
