#### Design Structure Charts in Software Design

- A design structure chart (DSC) is a chart that shows the breakdown of a system into its lowest manageable parts. 
- It is a top-down modular design tool that helps in structured programming and software engineering. 
- It uses boxes to represent modules or functions, and lines to represent the connections or relationships between them. 
- It visualizes the hierarchy, complexity, and size of the system, and the data and control flow among the modules. 
- It aids in dividing and conquering a large software problem, by recursively breaking it down into smaller and simpler parts. 
- It serves as a blueprint and a communication tool for the software designers and the client. 
- It helps in identifying the configuration items, the internal interfaces, and the impact of changes.
