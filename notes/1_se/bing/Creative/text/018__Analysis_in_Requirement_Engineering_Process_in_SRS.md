### Analysis in Requirement Engineering Process in SRS

- Requirement engineering is the process of eliciting, analyzing, specifying, and validating the software requirements from the client, users, and stakeholders  .
- Software requirements specification (SRS) is a document that describes the software's functionality, features, constraints, and quality attributes in a clear, concise, and consistent manner  .
- Analysis is the stage of requirement engineering where the elicited requirements are examined for completeness, correctness, consistency, feasibility, and testability .
- The main activities of analysis are :
  - Identifying and modeling the system objects, resources, and behaviors.
  - Specifying the data interfaces, control interfaces, and user interfaces of the software.
  - Specifying the error handling, potential modifications, and validation plans for the software.
  - Documenting the rationale, priority, and risk of each requirement.
  - Ignoring the implementation details and focusing on the customer needs and expectations.
- The main benefits of analysis are :
  - It helps to clarify and refine the software requirements and eliminate any ambiguity or inconsistency.
  - It helps to communicate the software requirements to the developers, testers, and maintainers of the software.
  - It helps to estimate the cost, time, and resources needed for the software development.
  - It helps to ensure that the software meets the quality standards and satisfies the customer needs.
