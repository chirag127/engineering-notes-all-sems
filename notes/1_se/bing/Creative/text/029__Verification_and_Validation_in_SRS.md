### Verification and Validation in SRS

- Verification and validation are two important processes in software engineering that aim to ensure the quality and correctness of software requirements.
- Verification is the process of checking that the software requirements are consistent, complete, and conform to the standards and specifications. It answers the question: "Are we building the product right?"
- Validation is the process of checking that the software requirements meet the customer's needs and expectations. It answers the question: "Are we building the right product?"
- Verification and validation can be done using various techniques, such as inspection, walkthrough, formal verification, model-based verification, prototyping, black-box testing, acceptance testing, user feedback, traceability, and agile methodologies.
- Verification and validation should be done continuously throughout the development of requirements at every level and as part of baseline activities and reviewed during the System Requirements Reviews (SRR).
- Verification and validation can help to avoid errors, inconsistencies, ambiguities, and incompleteness in the software requirements, and to ensure that the software meets the customer's requirements and satisfaction.
