### Basic Concept of Software Design

- Software design is the process of defining the architecture, components, interfaces, and other characteristics of a software system before its implementation.
- Software design can be seen as a problem-solving activity that involves applying principles, concepts, and techniques to transform a set of requirements into a solution that satisfies them.
- Software design can be performed at different levels of abstraction, such as conceptual, logical, and physical. Each level provides a different perspective and detail of the system.
- Software design can be influenced by various factors, such as the type, size, and complexity of the system, the quality attributes and constraints, the development methodology and tools, and the stakeholder needs and expectations.
- Software design can be evaluated and improved using various methods, such as design reviews, inspections, testing, metrics, and patterns. The goal is to ensure that the design meets the requirements and adheres to the standards and best practices.
