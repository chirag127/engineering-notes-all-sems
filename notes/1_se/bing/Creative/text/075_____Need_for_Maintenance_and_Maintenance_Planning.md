### Need for Maintenance and Maintenance Planning

- Maintenance is the process of keeping the equipment and facilities in good working condition to ensure their reliability, availability, and efficiency.
- Maintenance is essential for the following reasons:
  - To prevent breakdowns and failures that may cause production losses, safety hazards, or environmental damage.
  - To extend the useful life of the equipment and facilities and reduce the need for replacement or overhaul.
  - To optimize the performance and quality of the products or services delivered by the equipment and facilities.
  - To reduce the operating costs and energy consumption of the equipment and facilities.
  - To comply with the legal and regulatory requirements and standards for the equipment and facilities.
- Maintenance planning is the process of determining the maintenance activities, resources, and schedules required to achieve the maintenance objectives and policies.
- Maintenance planning is important for the following reasons:
  - To ensure that the maintenance activities are performed in a timely, efficient, and effective manner.
  - To coordinate the maintenance activities with the production or service schedules and minimize the disruption or downtime.
  - To allocate the maintenance resources (such as manpower, materials, tools, and equipment) optimally and avoid wastage or shortage.
  - To monitor and control the maintenance performance and quality and identify the areas for improvement or corrective actions.
  - To document and record the maintenance activities and results for future reference and analysis.
