### Software as an Evolutionary Entity

- Software as an evolutionary entity is a concept that refers to the process of creating software and then upgrading it on a regular basis for different purposes, such as adding new features, removing obsolete functionality, fixing bugs, adapting to changes in the environment, or improving performance  .
- Software evolution is important because organizations invest large amounts of money in their software and are completely dependent on this software. Software also co-evolves with its domains, such as the user requirements, the business processes, the hardware platforms, the operating systems, the programming languages, and the software engineering methods .
- Software evolution is driven by various factors, such as feedback from the results of the behavior under execution of the software, as perceived by various stakeholders, advances and growth in the user organizations, and adaptation to changes in the external world, both independent and as a result of installation and use of the software .
- Software evolution is challenging because it involves dealing with complex, dynamic, and uncertain systems that are difficult to specify, design, implement, test, and maintain . Software evolution also requires managing the trade-offs between the costs and benefits of changing the software, the risks and opportunities of introducing new functionality, the quality and reliability of the software, and the satisfaction and expectations of the users .
- Software evolution can be studied from different perspectives, such as the software life cycle, the software process, the software product, the software architecture, the software metrics, the software quality, the software maintenance, the software reuse, the software configuration management, the software testing, the software verification and validation, the software evolution laws, the software evolution models, the software evolution patterns, the software evolution tools, and the software evolution strategies   .
