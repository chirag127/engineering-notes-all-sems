#### Test Drivers and Test Stubs software testing strategy

Test Drivers and Test Stubs are two software testing strategies that are used to test the interaction between different software components. These strategies are commonly used in integration testing, where the goal is to test how well different components of a software system work together.

- **Test Drivers** are used to simulate the behavior of a component that calls another component. They are used to test the interaction between the calling component and the called component. Test Drivers are typically used when the calling component is not yet available or is not yet fully functional.

- **Test Stubs** are used to simulate the behavior of a component that is called by another component. They are used to test the interaction between the called component and the calling component. Test Stubs are typically used when the called component is not yet available or is not yet fully functional.

Advantages of using Test Drivers and Test Stubs:
- They allow for early testing of the interaction between different components, even if some components are not yet available or fully functional.
- They can help to isolate defects in specific components, making it easier to identify and fix problems.
- They can help to reduce the risk of integration problems later on in the development process.

Disadvantages of using Test Drivers and Test Stubs:
- They require additional effort to develop and maintain.
- They may not accurately simulate the behavior of the real components, which can lead to incorrect test results.

In summary, Test Drivers and Test Stubs are useful software testing strategies for testing the interaction between different components of a software system. They can help to identify and fix problems early on in the development process, reducing the risk of integration problems later on. However, they do require additional effort to develop and maintain, and may not always accurately simulate the behavior of the real components.
