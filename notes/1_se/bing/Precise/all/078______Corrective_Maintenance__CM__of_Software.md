#### Corrective Maintenance (CM) of Software

Corrective Maintenance (CM) of software refers to the process of fixing errors or defects in software after it has been delivered to the user. This type of maintenance is reactive in nature and is performed when a problem or issue is identified in the software. Here are some key points to remember about Corrective Maintenance (CM) of software:

1. Corrective Maintenance is performed to fix errors or defects in software that have been identified by the user or through testing.
2. This type of maintenance is reactive in nature and is performed after the software has been delivered to the user.
3. Corrective Maintenance can be time-consuming and costly, as it requires identifying the root cause of the problem and implementing a solution.
4. It is important to have a well-defined process for reporting and tracking issues in software to ensure that Corrective Maintenance is performed in a timely and efficient manner.
5. Corrective Maintenance can be performed through various methods, including patching, updating, or replacing the affected software component.
6. A good mnemonic to remember the key aspects of Corrective Maintenance is "FIRE": Fixing Identified errors Reactively and Efficiently.

In summary, Corrective Maintenance (CM) of software is an important process for ensuring the continued functionality and reliability of software. It involves fixing errors or defects in software after they have been identified and can be performed through various methods. Having a well-defined process for reporting and tracking issues is crucial for performing Corrective Maintenance in a timely and efficient manner. Remember the mnemonic "FIRE" to help you remember the key aspects of Corrective Maintenance.
