### Review and Management of User Needs in Requirement Engineering Process in SRS

1. Requirement engineering is the process of defining, documenting, and maintaining requirements in the engineering design process.
2. The review and management of user needs is an important part of the requirement engineering process in the development of a software requirements specification (SRS).
3. The SRS is a document that describes the requirements of a software system, including functional and non-functional requirements.
4. The review and management of user needs involves gathering and analyzing information from users and stakeholders to determine their needs and expectations for the software system.
5. This information is used to develop a set of requirements that accurately reflects the needs of the users and stakeholders.
6. The review and management of user needs also involves validating the requirements to ensure that they are complete, consistent, and feasible.
7. The requirements are then prioritized and used to guide the development of the software system.
8. The review and management of user needs is an ongoing process throughout the development of the software system, as user needs and expectations may change over time.
9. Effective review and management of user needs can help to ensure that the software system meets the needs of its users and stakeholders, and can help to reduce the risk of project failure.
