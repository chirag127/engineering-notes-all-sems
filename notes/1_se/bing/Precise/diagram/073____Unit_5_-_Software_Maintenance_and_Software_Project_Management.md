This diagram shows the relationship between software maintenance and project management. Software maintenance is an ongoing process that is necessary to keep the software up to date and functioning properly. Project management, on the other hand, involves planning, executing, monitoring, and controlling a project to ensure its successful completion. The two are interconnected, as software maintenance can inform project planning and execution, while project management can help ensure that maintenance tasks are completed on time and within budget.
