#### Walk Through (Walkthrough) Static testing strategy

Walk Through, also known as Walkthrough, is a static testing strategy that involves a thorough review of the software code or document. Here are some key points to remember about Walk Through Static testing strategy:

- Walk Through is a manual testing technique that involves a group of people reviewing the code or document to identify defects, errors, and potential issues.
- The Walk Through technique is often used during the early stages of software development, such as during the requirement gathering and design phases.
- The main goal of Walk Through is to identify and correct defects early in the software development life cycle to save time and cost in the long run.
- Walk Through sessions are typically led by a moderator who guides the group through the review process and helps to keep the discussion on track.
- The Walk Through process involves a step-by-step review of the code or document, with each reviewer looking for different types of errors and issues.
- The reviewers may use checklists or other tools to help them identify potential issues and ensure that nothing is overlooked.
- Walk Through can be time-consuming and require a significant amount of effort from the participants, but it is a valuable technique for identifying defects early in the software development process.
- Walk Through sessions can be conducted in person or remotely, using tools such as screen sharing and video conferencing.
- The Walk Through technique can be used for a variety of software development artifacts, including requirements documents, design documents, code, and test plans.

Overall, Walk Through is a valuable static testing strategy that can help to identify defects early in the software development process. By using this technique, software development teams can save time and cost by addressing issues early on, before they become more difficult and expensive to fix.
