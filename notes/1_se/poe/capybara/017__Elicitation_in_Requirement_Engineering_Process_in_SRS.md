### Elicitation in Requirement Engineering Process in SRS

Elicitation is a crucial step in the Requirement Engineering Process in Software Requirements Specification (SRS). It is the process of gathering information from stakeholders and users to identify their needs and requirements. Below are some essential points to understand the elicitation process in SRS:

1. Elicitation is the initial step in the Requirement Engineering Process. It involves identifying stakeholders, understanding their needs, and gathering requirements.

2. The main objective of elicitation is to identify the requirements accurately and comprehensively. It involves identifying the stakeholders, their expectations, and their needs.

3. Different elicitation techniques are used to gather information from stakeholders. Some of the commonly used techniques are interviews, surveys, focus groups, and brainstorming.

4. It is essential to involve all the stakeholders in the elicitation process to ensure that all requirements are identified. The stakeholders may include end-users, customers, developers, and other people who may be impacted by the system.

5. The elicitation process must be conducted systematically to ensure that all requirements are identified. It involves analyzing the gathered data and verifying the requirements with the stakeholders.

6. The results of the elicitation process must be documented in the SRS document. The document must clearly define the requirements, their priorities, and any constraints that may impact the development of the system.

7. It is essential to validate the elicited requirements with the stakeholders to ensure that they are accurate, complete, and feasible.

8. The elicitation process may need to be repeated if new requirements are identified or if there are changes in the stakeholders' needs.

9. The success of the elicitation process depends on the involvement of all stakeholders, the selection of appropriate elicitation techniques, and the documentation of requirements in the SRS document.

In conclusion, Elicitation is a crucial step in the Requirement Engineering Process in SRS. It involves identifying stakeholders, understanding their needs, and gathering requirements. The elicitation process must be conducted systematically, involving all stakeholders, using appropriate techniques, and documenting the requirements accurately in the SRS document.
