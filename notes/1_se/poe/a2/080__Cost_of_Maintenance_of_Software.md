### Cost of Maintenance of Software

1. Fixing bugs and errors: After the software is developed and deployed, there may be some bugs or errors found in the software. The cost involved in fixing those bugs and errors is known as maintenance cost. This is a recurring cost and can be high if there are many bugs or if they are complex to fix.
2. Updating software: As the technology changes, the software needs to be updated to incorporate those changes and add new features. The cost involved in updating the software as per the changing requirements and technology is also a part of maintenance cost.
3. Improving software performance: The maintenance cost is also involved in improving the performance of the software by optimizing the code and other aspects. Optimization may be required to handle increasing load or to make the software work more efficiently.
4. Documentation: Proper documentation of the software is important for its maintenance. The maintenance cost includes the cost of developing and updating documentation as and when required.
5. Training: The maintenance cost also includes the cost of training new personnel in the maintenance and use of the software. As personnel change in a project, training cost would be incurred continuously.
