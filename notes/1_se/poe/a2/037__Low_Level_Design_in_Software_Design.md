### Low Level Design in Software Design

1. Low level design focuses on the concrete components/modules of the system and their interactions. It defines the internal structure and organization of the system.
2. The main goals of low level design are:
- Reduce complexity and improve understandability.
- Increase the robustness and flexibility of the system.
- Facilitate division of work and parallel development.
3. Some tips for effective low level design:
- Identify functional components and allocate responsibilities to them.
- Define clean interfaces between components.
- Focus on flexibility and extensibility.
- Keep components loosely coupled and highly cohesive.
- Use architectural patterns as applicable to solve common problems.
- Evaluate alternatives and take important design decisions.
4. The inputs for low level design are:
- High level design document.
- Requirement specifications.
- Estimates of sizes/complexities of different components.
- Risks and assumptions.
5. The outputs of low level design are:
- Component diagrams depicting the interaction between components.
- Interface specifications for the components.
- Notes/documents describing the key logic/algorithms in the components.
