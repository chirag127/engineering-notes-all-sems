### ISO 9000 Models in SRS

1. The ISO 9000 family of standards relates to quality management systems and are designed to help organizations ensure that they meet the needs of customers and other stakeholders.
2. The ISO 9001 standard is the most well-known standard in the ISO 9000 family and sets out the requirements for a quality management system.
3. ISO 9001:2015 is the current version of the ISO 9001 standard and has several models including:
- The Plan-Do-Check-Act (PDCA) model which emphasizes the continuous improvement of processes.
- The process approach model which focuses on the inputs and outputs of processes and evaluating risk and opportunities.
- The risk-based thinking model which requires organizations to determine the risks and opportunities that could affect the ability to meet customer requirements.
4. Following ISO 9001 guidelines and implementing the models can help an organization to meet customer requirements, address risks, and continually improve to provide value to stakeholders. The models provide a systematic approach to managing an organization and meeting quality objectives.
