#### Corrective Maintenance (CM) of Software

- CM refers to reactive modifications made to software to fix defects, errors or bugs.
- It is performed after software is deployed to correct faults and improve performance.
- CM is unplanned maintenance and consumes a significant portion of maintenance budget and effort.
- CM process involves detecting, diagnosing and fixing bugs or errors.
- Steps involved in CM:
    - Problem analysis - Identifying and locating the cause of problem.
    - Program correction - Fixing the software code to remove the defect.
    - Retesting - Testing the corrected software to ensure issue is resolved.
    - Release - Releasing the updated software.
- Effective practices for CM:
    - Track bugs/issues - Maintain log of problems and action taken.
    - Use debugging tools - Facilitate detection and localization of bugs.
    - Automate testing - Increase coverage of retesting process.
    - Document changes - Maintain change logs to track modifications.
- Benefits of effective CM:
    - Higher reliability and quality of software.
    - Lower cost of maintenance in the long run.
    - Improved user satisfaction.
    - Compliance with requirements.
