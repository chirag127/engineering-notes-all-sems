### Top-Down and Bottom-Up Testing Strategies in Software Testing

1. Top-Down Testing Strategy:
- Involves starting testing from the module at highest level and proceeding downwards to the lowest level module.
- Focusses on important functionality first and stubs are used for modules that are yet to be developed.
- Helps in early detection of defects and interfaces issues.
- Easy to execute as top level functionality is known.

2. Bottom-Up Testing Strategy:
- Starts testing from lowest level modules and proceed upwards to the highest level module.
- Focuses on testing each module thoroughly.
- Defects in lower level modules can affect high level modules leading to cascading effects.
- May be time-consuming to execute as functionality of individual modules needs to be known in detail.

Both strategies are useful and often used in combination based on the software architecture and time constraints. The testing strategy needs to be selected based on the system/application under test. A mix of both top-down and bottom-up testing strategies leads to more effective software testing.
