### Estimation of Various Parameters such as Cost and Time in software project management

1. Estimation of project cost:
- Expert opinion: Experts estimate cost based on their experience of similar projects. Less accurate but quick.
- Algorithmic cost modeling: Based on project size and complexity, cost is calculated using statistical techniques. More accurate but time-consuming.
- Bottom-up estimation: Cost of individual tasks is estimated and summed up. Most accurate but very time-consuming.

2. Estimation of project schedule:
- Same techniques as cost estimation: Expert opinion, algorithmic modeling, bottom-up estimation.
- PERT (Program Evaluation and Review Technique): Estimates 3 time durations - optimistic, most likely, and pessimistic. Uses weighted average to get expected time.
- Gantt charts: Bars represent tasks and their deadlines. Useful for planning and monitoring project schedule.

3. Estimation of project risks:
- Expert judgment: Experts predict possible risks and impacts.
- Checklist: Pre-defined list of risks is checked.
- Risk breakdown structure: Risks are categorized hierarchically.
- Risk register: Includes all identified risks, their attributes and mitigation plans.
