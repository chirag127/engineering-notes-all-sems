### Analysis in Requirement Engineering Process in SRS

1. Requirement analysis: In this step, the requirements are gathered from the stakeholders and analyzed for completeness and consistency. The requirements are prioritized based on importance and feasibility. Ambiguous, incomplete and inconsistent requirements are identified and clarified.
2. Requirement specification: The requirements are organized and specified in a standard template. The requirements are unambiguously stated in a technology-independent way. The requirements are verifiable and testable.
3. Feasibility analysis: The specified requirements are analyzed for feasibility in terms of costs, time and technology constraints. Infeasible requirements are identified and options to handle them are evaluated. The feasible requirements are approved to proceed to the next stage.
4. Requirement validation: The specified requirements are validated by demonstrating their correctness and appropriateness. The requirements are checked for consistency, completeness, unambiguousness, verifiability, modifiability, and traceability. User acceptance testing is performed to validate the requirements.
