
### Elicitation in Requirement Engineering Process in SRS

1. Requirement Elicitation is the process of gathering information about the requirements of a system from stakeholders, users, and other sources. 
2. It is the first step in the requirement engineering process, and it involves identifying and understanding the stakeholders' needs and expectations. 
3. Requirements can be gathered through interviews, surveys, workshops, questionnaires, and other techniques. 
4. The purpose of requirement elicitation is to ensure that the requirements are well-defined, complete, consistent, and correct. 
5. During the process, the stakeholders should be consulted to ensure that their requirements are accurately captured and that the system meets their needs. 
6. The requirement elicitation process should be iterative, as requirements may change over time. 
7. The output of the requirement elicitation process is a set of requirements that will be used to design, develop, and test the system.
