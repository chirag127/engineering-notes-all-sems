
### Software Quality Frameworks (SQF) in SRS

1. SQF is a set of processes and procedures used to ensure the quality of software products. 
2. It is based on the ISO/IEC 9126 standard, which defines six quality characteristics: functionality, reliability, usability, efficiency, maintainability, and portability. 
3. SQF can be used to define the requirements for software development projects and to measure the quality of the resulting product. 
4. SQF is composed of three main components: quality assurance, quality control, and quality management. 
5. Quality assurance focuses on preventing defects by ensuring that processes are followed and that the necessary resources are available. 
6. Quality control focuses on detecting and correcting defects in the software. 
7. Quality management focuses on setting goals, monitoring progress, and making adjustments to ensure that the software meets the customer's requirements. 
8. SQF is an important part of the software development process and can help ensure that software products meet the customer's expectations.
