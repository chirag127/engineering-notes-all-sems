
### Software Quality Assurance (SQA) in SRS

1. Software Quality Assurance (SQA) is a process used to ensure that software products meet the quality standards set by the organization.
2. SQA activities include planning, designing, implementing, monitoring, and evaluating software quality assurance processes.
3. SQA processes are typically integrated into the software development life cycle and are used to identify and address software quality issues at each stage of the development process.
4. SQA activities should be tailored to the specific needs of the organization and should be based on established standards, procedures, and best practices.
5. SQA processes should include activities such as reviews, inspections, and tests that are designed to identify and address software quality issues.
6. SQA activities should be documented and reported on a regular basis to ensure that software quality is maintained throughout the development process.
