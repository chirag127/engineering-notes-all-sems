
### Software Quality Attributes

1. Reliability: The ability of a system to perform its intended functions correctly and consistently over a period of time.
2. Usability: The ability of a system to be used by a user to achieve their desired tasks effectively and efficiently.
3. Performance: The ability of a system to meet its performance requirements, such as response time, throughput, and resource utilization.
4. Security: The ability of a system to protect its resources from unauthorized access and malicious activities.
5. Maintainability: The ability of a system to be modified and maintained over its life cycle.
6. Portability: The ability of a system to be moved from one environment to another with minimal effort.
7. Scalability: The ability of a system to be scaled up or down to meet changing demands.
8. Testability: The ability of a system to be tested effectively and efficiently.
