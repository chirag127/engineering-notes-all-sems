
### ISO 9000 Models in SRS

1. ISO 9000 is a family of standards related to quality management and quality assurance. It is designed to help organizations ensure that their products and services meet customer and regulatory requirements.

2. The ISO 9000 models are divided into three categories: Quality Management System (QMS), Quality Assurance (QA), and Quality Control (QC).

3. The QMS focuses on the overall management of quality in an organization. It includes the development of policies, procedures, and standards for quality management.

4. The QA is focused on the process of ensuring that the product or service meets the specified requirements. This includes the review of processes, plans, and procedures to ensure that they are effective and efficient.

5. The QC focuses on the process of ensuring that the product or service meets the specified requirements. This includes the review of processes, plans, and procedures to ensure that the product or service meets the customer's requirements.

6. The Software Requirements Specification (SRS) is a document that describes the software requirements for a system. It includes the functional and non-functional requirements of the system, as well as the user interface requirements. The SRS is used to define the scope of the software project, and it is used to guide the development process.
