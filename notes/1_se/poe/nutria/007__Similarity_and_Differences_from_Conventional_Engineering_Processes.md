

### Similarity and Differences from Conventional Engineering Processes

* Both conventional engineering processes and modern engineering processes involve the use of mathematics and science to solve problems and create products.
* Conventional engineering processes involve the use of manual labor, such as drawing plans, constructing prototypes, and assembling components. In modern engineering processes, computers and automated machines are used to perform these tasks.
* Conventional engineering processes can take a long time to complete, since each step must be carefully planned and executed. Modern engineering processes are often faster due to the use of computer-aided design (CAD) and computer-aided manufacturing (CAM) software.
* Conventional engineering processes often require a great deal of trial and error, while modern engineering processes can be more efficient due to the use of computer simulations and virtual prototyping.
* Conventional engineering processes are often limited to a single product or service, while modern engineering processes can be used to create multiple products or services at once.
* Conventional engineering processes often require a great deal of physical materials, while modern engineering processes can use digital materials and components.
