
### Decision Tables in Software Requirement Specification (SRS)

1. Decision tables are a structured representation of a set of conditions and corresponding actions.
2. They are used to specify the behavior of a system when certain conditions are met.
3. Decision tables are used to specify the behavior of a system for a given set of conditions.
4. Decision tables are used to capture the logic of complex systems and represent it in a tabular format.
5. Decision tables are composed of two parts: conditions and actions.
6. Conditions are the inputs to the system and the actions are the outputs.
7. Each row in a decision table represents a set of conditions and an associated action.
8. The conditions in a decision table can be expressed in terms of Boolean logic.
9. The conditions in a decision table can also be expressed using natural language.
10. Decision tables are used to capture the logic of complex systems and represent it in a tabular format.
