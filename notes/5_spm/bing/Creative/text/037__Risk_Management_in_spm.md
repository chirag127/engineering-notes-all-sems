### Risk Management in SPM

Risk management is the process of identifying, assessing, prioritizing, managing and controlling project risks. It is an essential part of software project management (SPM) that aims to increase the chances of project success and reduce the impact of negative events or conditions.

Some of the main goals of risk management in SPM are:

- Understanding whether a project is worth taking
- Refining the budget for the project
- Aligning the project strategy and work with the business objectives
- Empowering teams to drive business outcomes
- Responding quickly and effectively to changes and uncertainties
- Terminating the project as planned within scope, quality, budget and time

Some of the main steps of risk management in SPM are:

- Define risk: Establish the risk management standards, procedures, roles and responsibilities for the project
- Identify risk: Collect and document the specific project risks, their root causes, categories, probabilities and impacts
- Analyze risk: Perform qualitative and/or quantitative analysis of the risks to prioritize them and determine their expected value
- Plan risk: Develop risk response strategies and contingency plans for the most critical risks
- Monitor and control risk: Track and update the risk status, implement the risk response plans, and evaluate their effectiveness

Some of the main techniques and tools of risk management in SPM are:

- Meetings: Involve the relevant stakeholders and experts in the risk management process
- Document analysis: Review the project documents and records to identify potential risks
- Risk breakdown structures, checklists, templates: Use predefined categories and sources of risks to facilitate the identification and analysis process
- Analogy: Use historical data and experience from similar projects to estimate the risk probabilities and impacts
- Boehm's top ten causes for project failures: Use a list of the most common causes of project failures in software development to identify the risks applicable to the project at hand 
- Security risk management (SRM): Use a system-wide managerial tool to analyze and manage safety and security risks to personnel, assets and operations
