### Stress in spm

- Stress is a state of mental or emotional strain or tension resulting from adverse or demanding circumstances.
- Stress can affect one's performance, health, well-being, and family life.
- Stress can be caused by various factors, such as unrealistic deadlines, lack of resources, interpersonal conflicts, project environment, and customer expectations.
- Stress can be managed by using various techniques, such as detachment, conflict resolution, prioritization, relaxation, and support.
- Detachment is the ability to mentally distance oneself from a stressful situation and focus on something more pleasant or positive.
- Conflict resolution is the skill of resolving disputes or disagreements in a constructive and respectful way, without escalating the tension or hostility.
- Prioritization is the process of ranking tasks or activities according to their importance and urgency, and allocating time and resources accordingly.
- Relaxation is the practice of reducing physical and mental stress by engaging in activities that calm the body and mind, such as breathing exercises, meditation, yoga, music, or hobbies.
- Support is the act of seeking or providing emotional, social, or practical help from or to others who are facing similar challenges or difficulties.
