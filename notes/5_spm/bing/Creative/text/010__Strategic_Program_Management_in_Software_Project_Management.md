### Strategic Program Management in Software Project Management

- Strategic program management is the process of managing programs mapped to business objectives that improve organizational performance.
- A program is a collection of related projects that contribute to a larger strategic goal .
- A program manager oversees and coordinates the various projects and other strategic initiatives throughout an organization .
- A program manager also helps to drive organizational change by helping with agile transformations, including helping to implement DevOps practices and principles.
- A program manager can bring agile and DevOps to life for teams across large programs or individual projects by tailoring programs to the specific requirements and opportunities of the business.
- A strategic program management office (PMO) is a component of the organization that helps achieve a competitive advantage and reduce operating costs by increasing the project management maturity and linking the projects to the strategic plans.
- A strategic PMO model consists of the following components:
  - Project management assessment: benchmarks the current project management maturity level and identifies the areas needing improvement.
  - Project management methods and standards: develops and implements project management processes, procedures, best practices, and methodologies covering the nine knowledge areas of the PMBOK® Guide.
  - Project management training: provides project management education and certification to the project managers and team members.
  - Project management tools: selects and supports the project management software and hardware tools.
  - Project management support: provides project management consulting, mentoring, and auditing services.
  - Project management reporting: collects, analyzes, and communicates project performance data and metrics.
  - Project portfolio management: aligns the projects with the strategic objectives and priorities, and optimizes the allocation of resources and risks.
- Strategic project management is the process of thinking about the projects in light of their connection to the strategic plan.
- Strategic project management ensures that the mix of projects is appropriate and sufficient to deliver the strategic objectives, and that the projects are managed professionally and efficiently.
