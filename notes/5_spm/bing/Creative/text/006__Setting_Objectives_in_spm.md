### Setting Objectives in SPM

- Setting objectives in SPM means defining the desired outcomes of a project or a portfolio of projects that align with the business strategy.
- Setting objectives helps to clarify the expectations, focus, motivation, and engagement of the project team and stakeholders.
- Setting objectives also helps to measure the progress and success of the project or portfolio, and to adjust the strategy and resources as needed.
- The steps to setting objectives in SPM are:

  1. Identify the business goals and priorities that the project or portfolio supports.
  2. Break down the goals into specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, and time-bound (SMART) objectives.
  3. Assign roles and responsibilities for each objective, and communicate them clearly to the project team and stakeholders.
  4. Establish the criteria and indicators for evaluating the performance and impact of each objective.
  5. Monitor and track the progress and results of each objective, and report them regularly to the project team and stakeholders.
  6. Review and analyze the feedback and data on each objective, and identify the strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats (SWOT) of the project or portfolio.
  7. Adjust the objectives, strategy, and resources as needed, based on the SWOT analysis and the changing business environment.

- Setting objectives in SPM is an iterative and dynamic process that requires constant communication, collaboration, and alignment among the project team and stakeholders. It also requires flexibility and adaptability to respond to the changing needs and expectations of the business.
