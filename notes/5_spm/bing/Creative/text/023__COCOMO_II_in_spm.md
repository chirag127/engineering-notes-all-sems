### COCOMO II in SPM

COCOMO II is a software cost estimation model that was developed at the University of Southern California as an update to the original COCOMO model published by Dr. Barry Boehm in 1981. COCOMO II aims to address the software development practices in the 1990s and 2000s, such as non-sequential and rapid-development process models, reuse-driven approaches, object-oriented approaches, software process maturity effects, and quality estimation.

COCOMO II consists of three sub-models that can be applied at different stages of the software development life cycle:

- Application Composition Model: This model is used for estimating the effort and schedule of prototyping or end-user programming using application generators or composition aids. This model is based on counting the number of function points or object points and adjusting them for reuse and complexity .
- Early Design Model: This model is used for estimating the effort and schedule of the software project in the early design stage, when only the size and the major characteristics of the system are known. This model is based on calculating the nominal effort using a regression formula and then adjusting it for 7 cost drivers that affect the productivity .
- Post-Architecture Model: This model is used for estimating the effort and schedule of the software project after the detailed architecture and design are completed. This model is based on calculating the nominal effort using a regression formula and then adjusting it for 17 cost drivers that affect the productivity .

COCOMO II also provides a method for estimating the software size in terms of lines of code or function points, and a method for adjusting the size for reuse or re-engineering. COCOMO II also incorporates the effects of software economies and diseconomies of scale, and the effects of software process maturity on the effort and schedule estimation.

COCOMO II is a widely used and well-tested software cost estimation model that can provide accurate and reliable estimates for different types of software projects and development practices. COCOMO II can also be calibrated and tailored to fit the specific needs and characteristics of an organization or a project .
