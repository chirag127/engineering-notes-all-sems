### Cost Schedules in SPM

- Cost schedules are detailed plans that show the estimated costs of a project over its duration.
- Cost schedules help project managers to monitor and control the project budget, track the actual costs versus the planned costs, and identify any cost variances or risks.
- Cost schedules are usually based on the work breakdown structure (WBS) of the project, which defines the scope and deliverables of the project in terms of tasks, activities, and milestones.
- Cost schedules can be created using various methods, such as bottom-up, top-down, parametric, or analogous estimating, depending on the level of detail and accuracy required.
- Cost schedules can be presented in different formats, such as tables, charts, graphs, or histograms, to show the distribution of costs over time, resources, or categories.
- Cost schedules are dynamic and should be updated regularly to reflect the changes in the project scope, schedule, or resources, as well as the actual costs incurred.
- Cost schedules are essential for effective project management, as they provide the basis for measuring the project performance, evaluating the project value, and making informed decisions.
