### Effort and Cost Estimation Techniques in spm

- Effort and cost estimation are important aspects of software project management (SPM) that help in planning, budgeting, and controlling a software project.
- Effort estimation is the process of predicting the amount of human resources and time required to complete a software project, while cost estimation is the process of estimating the monetary expenses involved in the project.
- There are various techniques for effort and cost estimation, which can be classified into three categories: algorithmic, non-algorithmic, and hybrid.
- Algorithmic techniques use mathematical formulas or models to calculate the effort and cost based on some input parameters, such as the size, complexity, or functionality of the software. Examples of algorithmic techniques are COCOMO, Function Point Analysis, and SLIM.
- Non-algorithmic techniques rely on expert judgment, analogy, or historical data to estimate the effort and cost. Examples of non-algorithmic techniques are Delphi method, Wideband Delphi, and Work Breakdown Structure.
- Hybrid techniques combine the features of both algorithmic and non-algorithmic techniques to improve the accuracy and reliability of the estimates. Examples of hybrid techniques are COCOMO II, COSMIC, and SCOPE.
- The choice of the estimation technique depends on various factors, such as the availability of data, the type and size of the project, the level of detail required, and the experience and skill of the estimators.
