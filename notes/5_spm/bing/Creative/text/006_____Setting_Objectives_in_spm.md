### Setting Objectives in SPM

- Setting objectives in SPM means defining the desired outcomes of a project or a portfolio that align with the business goals and strategy.
- Setting objectives in SPM helps to:
  - Give clarity of expectations to employees.
  - Focus, motivate and engage staff.
  - Plan, deliver and track value across different methodologies.
  - Ensure strategic direction is clearly understood and followed throughout the organization.
- Setting objectives in SPM involves the following steps:
  - Identify the business objectives and strategy that the project or portfolio supports.
  - Define the scope, deliverables, benefits, and success criteria of the project or portfolio.
  - Break down the objectives into measurable and achievable milestones and tasks.
  - Assign roles and responsibilities to the project team and stakeholders.
  - Communicate the objectives to the project team and stakeholders and get their feedback and buy-in.
  - Monitor and review the progress and performance of the project or portfolio against the objectives.
  - Adjust the objectives as needed based on changing circumstances or feedback.
