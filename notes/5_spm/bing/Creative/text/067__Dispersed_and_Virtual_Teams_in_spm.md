### Dispersed and Virtual Teams in SPM

- Dispersed and virtual teams are teams that have members who are not located in the same place, but work together through online communication.
- Dispersed and virtual teams can span organizational or geographic boundaries, and leverage diversity in people's backgrounds, attitudes, and capabilities .
- Dispersed and virtual teams can have advantages such as:
  - Better task accomplishment by selecting individuals with better skill sets.
  - More efficiency and creativity in completing successful projects.
  - More flexibility in work hours and locations.
  - Access to the best talent across the world.
- Dispersed and virtual teams can also have challenges such as:
  - Communication problems, cultural misunderstandings, or disagreements about when and where to meet .
  - Difficulty in establishing trust, cohesion, and social presence among team members .
  - Difficulty in finding and developing effective leaders .
  - Persistence with failing projects for longer than co-located teams.
- Dispersed and virtual teams require good communication, coordination, and collaboration skills, as well as appropriate technologies and tools to support their work  .
- Dispersed and virtual teams are becoming more common and relevant in the current and future work environment, especially due to the Covid-19 pandemic and the rise of remote work  .
