### Organizational Behavior in SPM

Organizational behavior (OB) is the systematic study and application of knowledge about how individuals and groups act within the organizations where they work. OB matters for your career, and successful companies tend to employ effective OB practices .

Some of the topics that OB covers are:

- Motivation: How can managers motivate employees to perform well and achieve their goals?
- Communication: How can managers communicate effectively with different audiences, such as subordinates, peers, superiors, customers, and stakeholders?
- Managing groups and teams: How can managers create and manage diverse, cohesive, and productive groups and teams?
- Conflict resolution: How can managers resolve conflicts among employees, between employees and managers, or between the organization and its environment?
- Power and politics: How can managers use power and influence to achieve organizational objectives, and how can they deal with political behavior in the workplace?
- Making decisions: How can managers make rational, ethical, and creative decisions in complex and uncertain situations?
- Leadership: How can managers inspire, guide, and support employees to achieve a shared vision and mission?
- Organizational culture: How can managers create and maintain a positive, innovative, and ethical organizational culture?
- Organizational change: How can managers initiate, implement, and sustain organizational change in response to internal and external challenges and opportunities?

To learn more about OB, you will need to develop critical thinking skills, such as asking tough questions, evaluating the answers you receive, and acting in an effective and ethical manner regardless of situational characteristics. You will also need to be a lifelong learner, as OB is a dynamic and evolving field that requires constant updating and adaptation. This book will provide you with the vocabulary, framework, and tools necessary for you to diagnose situations, apply OB concepts and theories, and improve your OB skills and competencies.
