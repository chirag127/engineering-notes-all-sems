### Health and Safety in SPM

- SPM stands for Sahaviriya Plate Mill, a public company in Thailand that produces steel plates for various industries.
- SPM is committed to ensuring the health and safety of its employees, contractors, customers, and communities, as well as protecting the environment from any adverse impacts of its operations.
- SPM has adopted the ISO 45001 standard for occupational health and safety management, which provides a framework for identifying, assessing, and controlling risks, and improving performance and compliance.
- SPM has also implemented the TIS 18001 standard, which is a Thai national standard for occupational health and safety management, based on the OSHA PSM (Process Safety Management) standard of the United States.
- SPM has established a health and safety policy, objectives, and targets, and has allocated sufficient resources and responsibilities for implementing and maintaining the health and safety management system.
- SPM has conducted a hazard identification and risk assessment (HIRA) for all its activities, processes, and products, and has developed appropriate controls and preventive measures to eliminate or minimize the risks.
- SPM has provided training and awareness programs for all its employees and contractors, to ensure they have the necessary competence, skills, and knowledge to perform their tasks safely and effectively.
- SPM has established a communication system to ensure the timely and accurate exchange of information and feedback on health and safety issues, both internally and externally.
- SPM has documented and maintained all the relevant information and records related to its health and safety management system, and has ensured their accessibility, legibility, and confidentiality.
- SPM has developed and implemented operational procedures and work instructions for all its critical and hazardous activities, and has ensured their compliance and effectiveness through regular monitoring and inspection.
- SPM has prepared and tested emergency plans and response procedures for various scenarios, such as fire, explosion, spill, leak, or injury, and has ensured the availability and readiness of the necessary equipment and personnel.
- SPM has measured and evaluated its health and safety performance and compliance, using various indicators and criteria, such as incident rates, audit results, legal requirements, and customer feedback.
- SPM has conducted internal and external audits of its health and safety management system, to verify its conformity and effectiveness, and to identify any gaps or opportunities for improvement.
- SPM has reviewed and analyzed the results of its health and safety performance and audits, and has taken corrective and preventive actions to address any non-conformities, incidents, or complaints, and to prevent their recurrence.
- SPM has continuously improved its health and safety management system, by setting and reviewing its policy, objectives, and targets, and by implementing the best practices and innovations in the industry.
