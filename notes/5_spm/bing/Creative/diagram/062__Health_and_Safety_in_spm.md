Health and Safety in spm is a topic that covers the standards, regulations, and practices that aim to prevent or reduce the risk of injury, illness, or environmental harm in the pharmaceutical manufacturing industry. Spm stands for speciality pharmaceutical manufacturing, which involves the production of drugs that have specific therapeutic effects or target specific diseases or conditions.

One of the main sources of guidance for health and safety in spm is the Process Safety Management (PSM) standard issued by the Occupational Safety and Health Administration (OSHA). The PSM standard applies to any facility that handles, stores, or processes highly hazardous chemicals, such as flammable, explosive, toxic, or reactive substances. The PSM standard requires the facility to implement a comprehensive management system that covers 14 elements, such as:

- Process safety information
- Process hazard analysis
- Operating procedures
- Training
- Mechanical integrity
- Management of change
- Pre-startup safety review
- Emergency planning and response
- Incident investigation
- Compliance audits
- Employee participation
- Contractor management
- Hot work permit
- Trade secrets
