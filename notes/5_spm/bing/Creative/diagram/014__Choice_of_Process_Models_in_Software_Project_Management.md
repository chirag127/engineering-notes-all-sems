A software process model is an abstract representation of the development process that specifies the stages and order of the activities involved in designing, implementing, and testing a software system. There are many kinds of software process models, such as waterfall, agile, iterative, spiral, etc. The choice of a process model depends on various factors, such as project requirements, size, complexity, cost of delay, and stakeholder expectations.
