The diagram shows that the decisions at each level are interrelated and influence each other. The strategic decisions guide the tactical decisions, which in turn guide the operational decisions. The decisions at each level also depend on the input from various sources, such as data, research, stakeholders, and cognitive biases. The decision making process in SPM is not linear or static, but rather iterative and adaptive, as the SPMs need to monitor and adjust their decisions based on the feedback and changing conditions of the project.
