The Oldham-Hackman Job Characteristic Model in spm is a normative approach to job enrichment that specifies five core job dimensions that will lead to critical psychological states in the individual employee . These dimensions are skill variety, task identity, task significance, autonomy, and feedback. The model also suggests that these psychological states will result in positive outcomes for the individual and the organization, such as high motivation, high-quality performance, high job satisfaction, low absenteeism, and low labor turnover .
