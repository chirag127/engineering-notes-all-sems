The basics of software estimation in SPM are the following steps:

1. Estimate the size of the software product, either in lines of code (LOC) or function points (FP) or other units of measure.
2. Estimate the effort required to develop the software product, either in person-months or person-hours.
3. Estimate the schedule of the software project, either in calendar months or weeks.
4. Estimate the cost of the software project, either in dollars or local currency.

There are different methods and techniques to perform each step of software estimation, such as analogy, expert judgment, algorithmic models, parametric models, etc . The choice of the method depends on the availability of historical data, the complexity of the software product, the accuracy required, and the experience of the estimators .

Software estimation is a challenging and important activity in software project management, as it affects the planning and control of the project, the quality and reliability of the software product, and the satisfaction of the customers and stakeholders . Software estimation is also subject to many uncertainties and risks, such as changing requirements, unforeseen difficulties, human factors, etc . Therefore, software estimation should be done carefully, iteratively, and transparently, and should be updated regularly as the project progresses  .
