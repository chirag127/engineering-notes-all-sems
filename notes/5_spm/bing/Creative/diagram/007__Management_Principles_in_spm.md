Management Principles in spm are the guidelines and best practices for managing software projects effectively and efficiently. According to the website of SmartOrg, a company that provides software and consulting services for strategic portfolio management, there are six principles of strategic portfolio management:

- Define and communicate a clear strategy and vision
- Align projects and resources with strategic objectives
- Evaluate and compare projects objectively and consistently
- Balance risk and return across the portfolio
- Foster an inclusive and collaborative process
- Communicate and learn from results
