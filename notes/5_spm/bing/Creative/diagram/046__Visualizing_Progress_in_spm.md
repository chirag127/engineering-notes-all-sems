Visualizing Progress in SPM is a technique for tracking and presenting the data about the project's progress and performance. It can help the manager to make informed decisions based on the data and to adjust the instruction or intervention if needed. One of the methods for visualizing progress in SPM is using a graph that shows the student's or the project's data points, the goal line, and the trend line. The graph can provide a visual representation of how well the student or the project is progressing and whether they are in need of an instructional change to prompt better learning or performance. The graph can also show the effectiveness of the instructional changes by comparing the trend line slope with the goal line slope.
