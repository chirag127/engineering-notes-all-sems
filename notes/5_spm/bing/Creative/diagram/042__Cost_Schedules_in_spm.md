Cost schedules in software project management (SPM) are used to show the weekly or monthly costs over the life of the project. They help to estimate the total cost of the project and to monitor the actual cost vs. the planned cost. They also help to allocate the budget and resources efficiently.

One way to draw a cost schedule in ASCII is to use a table format, where each row represents a week or a month, and each column represents a cost category, such as staff, overheads, usage, etc. The table can also show the cumulative cost and the cost variance for each period.
