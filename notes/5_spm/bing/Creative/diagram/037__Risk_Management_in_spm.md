Risk management is a process of identifying, analyzing, planning, and controlling the risks that may affect the project objectives. Risk management is one of the core components of strategic portfolio management (SPM), which is a framework for aligning investments and work with business strategy and outcomes.

Risk identification is the process of collecting and describing the specific project risks that may influence the project objectives. It is an iterative process that involves various techniques, such as meetings, document analysis, risk breakdown structures, checklists, templates, and analogy. The output of this process is a risk register, which documents the characteristics of each risk.

Risk analysis is the process of assessing the probability and impact of each identified risk. It can be qualitative or quantitative, depending on the level of detail and accuracy required. Qualitative risk analysis involves ranking the risks based on their likelihood and severity, using scales or matrices. Quantitative risk analysis involves estimating the numerical values of the probability and impact of each risk, using statistical methods or tools. The output of this process is a prioritized list of risks, which indicates their relative importance and urgency.

Risk planning is the process of developing strategies and actions to avoid, reduce, transfer, or accept the risks that may affect the project objectives. It involves selecting the most appropriate risk response for each risk, based on the results of the risk analysis. Risk planning also involves allocating resources, assigning responsibilities, and establishing contingency plans for the risks that cannot be eliminated or reduced. The output of this process is a risk management plan, which documents the risk responses and the risk management procedures.

Risk control is the process of monitoring and updating the risks throughout the project lifecycle. It involves tracking the status and performance of the risks and the risk responses, identifying new or changed risks, and evaluating the effectiveness and efficiency of the risk management process. Risk control also involves communicating and reporting the risk information to the relevant stakeholders, and taking corrective actions when needed. The output of this process is a risk report, which provides an overview of the current risk situation and the risk management activities.
