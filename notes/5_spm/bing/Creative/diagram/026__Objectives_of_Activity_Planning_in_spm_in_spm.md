The objectives of activity planning in software project management are to:

- Define the activities and tasks that need to be performed to achieve the project goals
- Estimate the duration, effort, and resources required for each activity and task
- Sequence and schedule the activities and tasks according to their dependencies and constraints
- Identify the critical path and the slack time for each activity and task
- Monitor and control the progress and performance of the activities and tasks
- Adjust the plan as needed to cope with changes and risks
