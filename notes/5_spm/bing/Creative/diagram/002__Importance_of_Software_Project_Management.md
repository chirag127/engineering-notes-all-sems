Software project management is the practice of planning and delivering software development projects within variables such as time, quality, cost, and scope. It follows the end-to-end software development lifecycle, which encompasses many steps from gathering requirements to developing and testing to releasing the product and, finally, ongoing maintenance.

Software project management is important for many reasons, such as:

- It ensures there is a clear focus and objective for the project, aligned with the strategic goals of the business.
- It helps to manage the budget, schedule, resources, and risks of the project, and to optimize them for the best outcome .
- It provides leadership and direction to the project team, and fosters collaboration and communication among stakeholders .
- It ensures proper expectations are set and met around what can be delivered, by when, and for how much.
- It ensures the quality of the product is consistent and meets the requirements and standards of the customer .
- It enables automation and efficiency in the project process, using tools and frameworks that support the software development lifecycle .
