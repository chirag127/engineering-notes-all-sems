Stress in spm can refer to different things, depending on the context. It could mean:

- Stress in project management, which is the experience of pressure, anxiety, or discomfort caused by various factors related to managing a project, such as unrealistic deadlines, lack of resources, conflicts, or expectations .
- Stress in Sijil Pelajaran Malaysia (SPM), which is the national examination for secondary school students in Malaysia, and is considered to be very important for their future education and career prospects. Students may feel stressed due to the high stakes, the difficulty of the exam, the competition, or the parental and societal pressure.
- Stress in specialized pro-resolving mediators (SPMs), which are molecules derived from omega-3 fatty acids that help to resolve inflammation and promote tissue healing. Stress can impair the production and function of SPMs, leading to chronic inflammation and various diseases.
