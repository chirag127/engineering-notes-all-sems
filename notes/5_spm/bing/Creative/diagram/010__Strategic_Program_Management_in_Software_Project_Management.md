Strategic Program Management in Software Project Management is a process of managing multiple related software projects that are aligned with the organization's strategic goals and objectives . Program management helps to improve organizational performance by sharing project resources, costs, and activities, and by driving organizational change and innovation  . Program management also involves strategy and planning, as well as monitoring and controlling the various projects and their interdependencies .
