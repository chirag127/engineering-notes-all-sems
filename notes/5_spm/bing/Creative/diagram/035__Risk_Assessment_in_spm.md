Risk assessment in spm is the process of identifying, analyzing, and managing the risks associated with subsea pipelines due to installation and operation of single point mooring (SPM) systems. SPM systems are offshore loading terminals that allow tankers to moor and transfer oil or gas from subsea pipelines. SPM systems pose various threats to subsea pipelines, such as dropped anchor, dragged anchor, ship sinking, collision, fatigue, corrosion, etc.
