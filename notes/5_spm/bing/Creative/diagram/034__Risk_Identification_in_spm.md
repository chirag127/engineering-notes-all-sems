Risk identification is the process of discovering and documenting the risks that may affect a project, its feasibility, or its management plan. It is the first step in the risk management process, which also includes risk analysis, risk response planning, risk monitoring and control, and risk communication.

There are various techniques for risk identification, such as brainstorming, checklists, document analysis, root cause analysis, assumptions analysis, and interviews. These techniques help to identify the sources, causes, and effects of potential risks, as well as their probability and impact.
