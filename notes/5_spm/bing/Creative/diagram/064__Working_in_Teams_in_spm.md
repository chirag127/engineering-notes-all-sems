Working in Teams in spm is a concept that refers to the process of collaborating with other people to achieve a common goal in software project management. Working in teams can have many benefits, such as:

- Improving communication and coordination among team members
- Enhancing creativity and problem-solving skills
- Sharing knowledge and expertise
- Increasing motivation and commitment
- Reducing errors and risks
- Delivering better quality and value to customers

However, working in teams also poses some challenges, such as:

- Managing conflicts and disagreements
- Balancing individual and group interests
- Dealing with diversity and cultural differences
- Establishing roles and responsibilities
- Setting clear and realistic expectations
- Evaluating and rewarding performance

Therefore, working in teams requires careful planning, leadership, and communication skills. A team should have a clear vision, mission, and objectives, as well as a well-defined structure, process, and culture. A team should also have a balanced mix of different personalities, skills, and roles, such as:

- The chair: the person who leads the team and facilitates meetings
- The plant: the person who generates ideas and solutions
- The monitor-evaluator: the person who evaluates and selects the best options
- The shaper: the person who directs the team's attention to the important issues
- The team worker: the person who creates a positive and supportive environment
- The resource investigator: the person who finds and obtains resources and information
- The completer-finisher: the person who completes and delivers tasks
- The company worker: the person who is a good team player and undertakes any necessary tasks
