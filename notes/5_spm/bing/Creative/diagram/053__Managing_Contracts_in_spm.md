Managing Contracts in spm is a process of creating and managing contractual agreements with third party service providers, who deliver services to the agency's clients. The main reasons for entering into contracts are to monitor service quality, provider performance, and service delivery costs. The contract lifecycle consists of the following stages: issuing, activating, amending, renewing, and terminating contracts. A resource manager oversees the contract management process and works with the providers and provider groups.
