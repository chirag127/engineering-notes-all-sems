Resource allocation in SPM (software project management) is the process of assigning and scheduling available resources in the most effective and economical way possible. Resources include staff, budgets, materials, and equipment needed for the successful completion of a project. Resource allocation helps to plan ahead, monitor progress, and adjust resources as needed.
