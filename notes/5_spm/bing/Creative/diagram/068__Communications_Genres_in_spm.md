Communications genres are the types of communication that are used in software project management. They include:

- **Formal communication**: This is the communication that follows a predefined format, structure, and protocol. It is usually written and documented, and it serves as a record of the project activities, decisions, and outcomes. Formal communication includes reports, plans, specifications, contracts, reviews, audits, and minutes of meetings.
- **Informal communication**: This is the communication that is spontaneous, flexible, and casual. It is usually oral and interactive, and it facilitates collaboration, feedback, and problem-solving. Informal communication includes conversations, discussions, brainstorming, phone calls, emails, and chats.
- **Technical communication**: This is the communication that involves the use of specialized terminology, symbols, diagrams, and formats. It is usually written and graphical, and it conveys the technical aspects of the project, such as requirements, design, implementation, testing, and maintenance. Technical communication includes models, diagrams, code, test cases, and documentation.
