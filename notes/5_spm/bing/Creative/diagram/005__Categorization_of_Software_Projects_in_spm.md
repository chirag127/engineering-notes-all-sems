Categorization of software projects in software project management (SPM) is a way of grouping software projects based on some common characteristics or criteria. There are different ways of categorizing software projects, such as by development model, platform, scope, user interaction, maturity level, open-source software participation, delivery method, type, technology, size, and scale of operations, ownership and control, implementation, and purpose. Different categories may have different implications for the planning, execution, monitoring, and control of software projects.
