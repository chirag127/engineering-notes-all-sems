Setting objectives in SPM is the process of defining and aligning the goals of the project portfolio with the business strategy. It involves identifying, prioritizing, and scheduling the right work that supports the strategic initiatives and delivers value to the organization. Setting objectives also helps to clarify the expectations, focus, and motivation of the project teams and stakeholders.
