A communication plan is an outline of how important information will be communicated to stakeholders throughout the project. It also determines who will be receiving the communication, how those people will receive it, when they’ll receive it, and how often they should expect to receive that information.

A communication plan typically includes the following elements:

- Communication objectives: The goals of the communication plan, such as increasing awareness, changing behavior, or providing feedback.
- Communication channels: The tools and methods used to deliver the messages, such as email, chat, video conference, phone, etc.
- Communication frequency: The schedule and timing of the messages, such as daily, weekly, monthly, etc.
- Communication roles and responsibilities: The people involved in the communication process, such as the sender, receiver, approver, etc.
- Communication stakeholders: The groups or individuals who have an interest or influence in the project, such as the project team, sponsors, customers, etc.
- Communication messages: The content and format of the messages, such as status updates, reports, presentations, etc.
