Leadership in SPM is the process of guiding and influencing a team towards achieving the strategic goals of an organization. Leadership in SPM involves various roles and responsibilities, such as planning, organizing, communicating, motivating, problem-solving, and decision-making. Leadership in SPM also requires various skills, such as vision, creativity, adaptability, integrity, and emotional intelligence.
