A dispersed team is a group of people who work together on a common project but are located in different places, such as different cities, countries, or continents. A virtual team is a type of dispersed team that relies on information and communication technologies to collaborate across distance and time. Dispersed and virtual teams can offer many advantages, such as increased diversity, flexibility, and efficiency, but they also face many challenges, such as communication difficulties, coordination problems, and reduced trust.
