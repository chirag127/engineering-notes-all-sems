Organizational behavior (OB) is the study of how people behave and interact in organizations. It draws on concepts and theories from various disciplines, such as psychology, sociology, anthropology, and social psychology. OB helps us understand the factors that influence individual and group behavior, such as personality, perception, motivation, emotions, communication, leadership, power, culture, and ethics. OB also helps us design and manage organizations that are effective, ethical, and satisfying for the people who work in them.
