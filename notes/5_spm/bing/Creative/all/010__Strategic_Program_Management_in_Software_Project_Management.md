### Strategic Program Management in Software Project Management

- Strategic program management is the process of managing programs mapped to business objectives that improve organizational performance.
- A program is a collection of related projects that share a common goal and deliver value to the organization .
- A program manager is the person who oversees and coordinates the various projects and other strategic initiatives throughout the organization.
- A program manager also helps to drive organizational change by helping with agile transformations, including helping to implement DevOps practices and principles.
- Program management is different from project management, which is the process of leading a single, focused piece of work with a specific scope and defined output .
- A project manager is the person who manages the work of a team to achieve the goals and deliverables of a project .
- Program management entails the following components:
  - Project management assessment: This is the process of benchmarking the current project management maturity level to industry standards and best practices and identifying the areas needing improvement.
  - Project management methods and standards: This is the process of developing and implementing project management processes and procedures covering the nine knowledge areas of the PMBOK® Guide, as well as identifying and institutionalizing best practices and methodologies.
  - Project management training and mentoring: This is the process of providing project management education and coaching to project managers and team members to enhance their skills and competencies.
  - Project management tools and techniques: This is the process of selecting and using appropriate project management software and tools to facilitate project planning, execution, monitoring, and control.
  - Project management reporting and metrics: This is the process of collecting and analyzing project data and information to measure project performance and progress, as well as communicating the results to stakeholders.
  - Project portfolio management: This is the process of selecting, prioritizing, and managing the projects and programs that align with the strategic goals and objectives of the organization.
- The benefits of program management include :
  - Improved alignment of projects and programs with the organizational strategy and vision .
  - Increased efficiency and effectiveness of project and program delivery by sharing resources, costs, and activities .
  - Enhanced collaboration and communication among project and program stakeholders and team members .
  - Reduced risks and uncertainties by identifying and managing interdependencies and dependencies among projects and programs .
  - Increased value and benefits realization by ensuring that the outputs and outcomes of projects and programs meet the needs and expectations of the customers and the organization .
- A possible mnemonic to remember the components of program management is **APART**:
  - **A**ssessment: Benchmarking the project management maturity level and identifying improvement areas.
  - **P**rocesses and procedures: Developing and implementing project management methods and standards.
  - **A**dvancement: Providing project management training and mentoring.
  - **R**esources: Selecting and using project management tools and techniques.
  - **T**racking and reporting: Collecting and analyzing project management data and information, and communicating the results.
