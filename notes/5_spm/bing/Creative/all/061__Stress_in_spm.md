### Stress in spm

- Stress in spm can refer to either stress in project management or stress in Sijil Pelajaran Malaysia (SPM), a national examination taken by students in Malaysia.
- In this response, I will focus on stress in project management, as it seems more relevant to the user's request.
- Stress in project management is the experience of physical, mental, or emotional strain or tension caused by the demands and challenges of managing a project.
- Stress can have negative impacts on the project manager's performance, health, well-being, and family life, as well as the project outcomes and stakeholder satisfaction.
- Some common sources of stress in project management are:
  - Unrealistic timeline and expectations
  - Lack of resources and control
  - Conflict and communication issues
  - Uncertainty and ambiguity
  - High responsibility and accountability
- Some techniques for managing stress in project management are:
  - Detach or dissociate: mentally distance yourself from the stressful situation and focus on something more positive or relaxing .
  - Plan and prioritize: set clear and realistic goals, break down tasks into manageable steps, and focus on the most important and urgent ones .
  - Delegate and collaborate: share the workload and responsibility with your team members, seek help and support from others, and communicate effectively .
  - Balance and recharge: maintain a healthy lifestyle, take breaks and rest, engage in hobbies and leisure activities, and spend time with family and friends .
  - Learn and grow: view challenges as opportunities, seek feedback and improvement, and develop your skills and competencies .
- A mnemonic to remember these techniques is **DPDBL** (pronounced as dip-dibble), which stands for **D**etach, **P**lan, **D**elegate, **B**alance, and **L**earn.
