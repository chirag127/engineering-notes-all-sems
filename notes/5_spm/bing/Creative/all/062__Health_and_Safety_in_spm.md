### Health and Safety in spm

- SPM stands for **S**ahaviriya **P**late **M**ill, a company that produces steel plates in Thailand.
- SPM has been awarded for its occupational health and safety compliant to TIS 18001 standards, which are based on the international standards of OHSAS 18001.
- TIS 18001 standards aim to provide a framework for managing the prevention of work-related injuries, illnesses, and fatalities.
- Some of the benefits of implementing TIS 18001 standards are:
  - Reducing the risks of accidents and incidents
  - Enhancing the reputation and image of the company
  - Improving the morale and motivation of the employees
  - Increasing the efficiency and productivity of the operations
  - Saving costs and resources by avoiding legal penalties and compensation claims
- Some of the requirements of TIS 18001 standards are:
  - Establishing a health and safety policy and objectives
  - Identifying the hazards and assessing the risks
  - Implementing the necessary controls and measures
  - Providing the appropriate training and communication
  - Monitoring and measuring the performance and effectiveness
  - Reviewing and improving the system continuously
- Some of the examples of health and safety practices in SPM are:
  - Quickly cleaning up all spills
  - Maintaining a tidy work area
  - Frequently washing hands
  - Wearing a laboratory coat and other personal protective equipment (PPE)
  - Labeling containers correctly
  - Keeping entryways clear
  - Using safe and well-maintained equipment
  - Following the standard operating procedures (SOPs)
  - Reporting and investigating any incidents or near misses
  - Conducting regular audits and inspections
- A mnemonic to remember the benefits of TIS 18001 standards is **RISE IS**:
  - **R**educe risks
  - **I**mprove image
  - **S**trengthen morale
  - **E**nhance efficiency
  - **I**ncrease savings
  - **S**upport improvement
