### Dispersed and Virtual Teams in SPM

- Dispersed and virtual teams are groups of people who work on common projects by making collaborative efforts to achieve shared goals and objectives, without being physically co-located.
- Dispersed and virtual teams can be spatially separated (from “across the hall” to “scattered worldwide”), temporally separated (spanning different time zones), configurationally uneven (for example, five members in one location and two in another) and culturally diverse .
- Dispersed and virtual teams can offer several benefits, such as:
  - Flexibility and convenience for team members to manage their work and personal lives
  - Access to the best and lowest-cost global talent and expertise
  - Reduced real estate and travel costs for the organization
  - Increased diversity and creativity in problem-solving and innovation
- Dispersed and virtual teams can also face several challenges, such as:
  - Difficulties in communication and coordination due to distance, time, and technology barriers
  - Reduced trust and social cohesion among team members who rarely or never meet face-to-face
  - Increased inability to establish a common ground and shared understanding of the project goals, roles, and processes
  - Increased risk of conflicts and misunderstandings due to cultural differences and lack of nonverbal cues
- To manage dispersed and virtual teams effectively, the following practices are recommended :
  - Select the right team members who have good communication skills, high emotional intelligence, an ability to work independently, and the resilience to recover from snafus
  - Establish the right leadership style that is supportive, empowering, and inclusive, and that fosters a sense of team identity and belonging
  - Create the right touchpoints that enable frequent and timely communication, feedback, and recognition, and that facilitate social interaction and relationship-building
  - Use the right technology that suits the needs and preferences of the team, and that supports collaboration, information-sharing, and decision-making
- A possible mnemonic to remember the four must-haves for dispersed and virtual teams is **S.E.C.T.**: **S**elect, **E**stablish, **C**reate, and **U**se.
- A possible learning trick to understand the benefits and challenges of dispersed and virtual teams is to use a SWOT analysis framework, where **S** stands for **Strengths**, **W** stands for **Weaknesses**, **O** stands for **Opportunities**, and **T** stands for **Threats**. For example:

| SWOT | Dispersed and Virtual Teams |
| --- | --- |
| Strengths | Flexibility, access to talent, cost savings, diversity |
| Weaknesses | Communication, trust, common ground, conflict |
| Opportunities | Innovation, learning, customer satisfaction, market expansion |
| Threats | Competition, coordination, culture, technology |
