## Unit - 5 - Staffing in Software Projects for the notes of the course Software Project Management in spm

Staffing in software projects refers to the process of identifying, hiring, and managing the human resources needed to complete a software project successfully. Staffing is a critical aspect of software project management, as it affects the quality, cost, and schedule of the project. Staffing also influences the motivation, performance, and satisfaction of the project team members.

Some of the topics that are covered in this unit are:

- Staffing planning: This is the process of determining the roles, responsibilities, skills, and number of team members required for the project. Staffing planning also involves creating a staffing calendar, a job specification, and a staffing acquisition strategy. Staffing planning helps to align the project objectives with the available human resources and to avoid overstaffing or understaffing the project.  
- Staffing acquisition: This is the process of finding, selecting, and hiring the best candidates for the project roles. Staffing acquisition can be done internally (from within the organization) or externally (from outside the organization). Staffing acquisition can also involve outsourcing, offshoring, or nearshoring some or all of the project activities to third-party vendors. Staffing acquisition should be done in a timely, cost-effective, and fair manner.  
- Staffing management: This is the process of leading, motivating, training, and developing the project team members. Staffing management also involves monitoring, evaluating, and rewarding the team members' performance, as well as resolving any conflicts or issues that may arise among them. Staffing management aims to create a positive and productive work environment for the project team and to ensure their commitment and retention.  

Some of the mnemonics and learning tricks for this unit are:

- To remember the steps of staffing planning, use the acronym PACE: Pipeline, Calendar, Job specs, and Acquisition strategy. 
- To remember the sources of staffing acquisition, use the acronym IOON: Internal, Outsourcing, Offshoring, and Nearshoring. 
- To remember the aspects of staffing management, use the acronym LEMUR: Leading, Evaluating, Motivating, Upgrading, and Retaining.
