### Managing Interactive Processes in spm

- Interactive processes are those that involve direct communication and coordination between the software project manager (spm) and the stakeholders, such as customers, users, developers, testers, etc.
- Interactive processes are essential for ensuring that the project meets the expectations and needs of the stakeholders, and that any issues or changes are addressed promptly and effectively.
- Interactive processes can be classified into four categories: initiating, planning, executing, and closing.
- Initiating processes are those that define and authorize the project or a project phase. They include activities such as identifying the project scope, objectives, deliverables, stakeholders, and sponsor; developing the project charter; and obtaining formal approval to start the project.
- Planning processes are those that establish the project plan and the project management plan. They include activities such as defining and prioritizing the requirements, creating the work breakdown structure, estimating the resources, time, and cost, developing the schedule, budget, and quality plan, identifying and analyzing the risks, and planning the communication, procurement, and stakeholder engagement strategies.
- Executing processes are those that implement the project plan and produce the project deliverables. They include activities such as directing and managing the project work, performing quality assurance, acquiring and developing the project team, managing the communication, procurement, and stakeholder engagement, and implementing the approved changes and corrective actions.
- Closing processes are those that finalize and close the project or a project phase. They include activities such as verifying and validating the deliverables, completing and documenting the lessons learned, releasing the resources, archiving the project records, and obtaining formal acceptance and sign-off from the stakeholders.

- Some mnemonics and learning tricks for the managing interactive processes in spm are:

  - To remember the four categories of interactive processes, use the acronym **IPEC** (Initiating, Planning, Executing, Closing).
  - To remember the initiating processes, use the phrase **I Do Stakeholder Sponsor Charter** (Identify scope, objectives, deliverables, stakeholders, and sponsor; Develop project charter; Obtain formal approval).
  - To remember the planning processes, use the phrase **D WERQ RIP CS** (Define and prioritize requirements; Work breakdown structure; Estimate resources, time, and cost; Schedule, budget, and quality plan; Risk identification and analysis; Plan communication, procurement, and stakeholder engagement strategies).
  - To remember the executing processes, use the phrase **DAP MIME** (Direct and manage project work; Perform quality assurance; Acquire and develop project team; Manage communication, procurement, and stakeholder engagement; Implement changes and corrective actions).
  - To remember the closing processes, use the phrase **VVC RAL AS** (Verify and validate deliverables; Complete and document lessons learned; Release resources; Archive project records; Obtain formal acceptance and sign-off).
