### Cost Schedules in SPM

- Cost schedules are detailed plans that show the estimated costs of a software project over time.
- Cost schedules are derived from the work breakdown structure (WBS), the effort and duration estimates, and the resource allocation.
- Cost schedules are used to monitor and control the project budget, track the actual costs vs. planned costs, and identify any cost variances or issues.
- Cost schedules can be presented in different formats, such as tables, charts, graphs, or histograms.
- Cost schedules can be updated and revised throughout the project life cycle, as more information becomes available or changes occur.
- Cost schedules are part of the cost management plan, which outlines the processes and tools for estimating, budgeting, and controlling costs in a software project.
