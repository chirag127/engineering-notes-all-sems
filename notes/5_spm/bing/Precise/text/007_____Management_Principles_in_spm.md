### Management Principles in SPM

1. **Planning:** This principle involves setting goals and objectives for the organization and developing strategies to achieve them.
2. **Organizing:** This principle involves arranging resources and tasks to achieve the goals and objectives set in the planning stage.
3. **Leading:** This principle involves directing and motivating employees to achieve the goals and objectives set in the planning stage.
4. **Controlling:** This principle involves monitoring the progress of the organization towards achieving its goals and objectives and making necessary adjustments to ensure success.

These principles are essential for effective management in any organization, including those in the field of software project management (SPM). By applying these principles, managers can ensure that their projects are completed on time, within budget, and to the desired level of quality.
