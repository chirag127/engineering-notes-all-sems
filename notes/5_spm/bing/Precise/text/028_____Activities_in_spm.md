### Activities in SPM

SPM, or Statistical Parametric Mapping, is a technique used in neuroimaging data analysis. Here are some activities that can be performed using SPM:

1. **Preprocessing**: This includes steps such as realignment, normalization, and smoothing of the neuroimaging data to prepare it for further analysis.
2. **Statistical Analysis**: SPM can be used to perform statistical analysis on the preprocessed data, such as t-tests and ANOVA, to identify significant differences or effects.
3. **Modeling**: SPM allows for the creation of statistical models to explain the observed data, such as the General Linear Model (GLM).
4. **Visualization**: SPM provides tools for visualizing the results of the analysis, such as statistical maps and overlays on anatomical images.
5. **Inference**: SPM can be used to make inferences about the underlying neural activity based on the results of the analysis.
