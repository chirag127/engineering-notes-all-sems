### Objectives of Activity Planning in SPM

Activity planning is an essential part of software project management. The main objectives of activity planning are to:

1. Ensure that the appropriate resources will be available precisely when required.
2. Avoid different activities competing for the same resources at the same time.
3. Produce a detailed schedule showing which staff carry out each activity.
4. Produce a detailed plan against which actual achievement may be measured.
5. Produce a timed cash flow forecast.
6. Provide a framework that enables the manager to make reasonable estimates of resources, cost, and schedule.
7. Assess the feasibility of the project within required timescales and resource constraints.

Activity planning is an ongoing effort throughout the life of the project and is essential for effective project management. It helps to ensure that the project is completed on time, within budget, and to the required quality standards.
