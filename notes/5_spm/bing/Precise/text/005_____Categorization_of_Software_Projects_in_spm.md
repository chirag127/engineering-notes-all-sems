### Categorization of Software Projects in SPM

Software Project Management (SPM) is a sub-field of Project Management in which software projects are planned, implemented, monitored, and controlled. Software projects can be categorized based on various factors such as the development model, platform, scope, user interaction, maturity level, open-source software participation, and delivery method .

1. **By Development Model**: Software projects can be categorized based on the development model used, such as Agile, Waterfall, Iterative, and Incremental .
2. **By Platform**: Software projects can also be categorized based on the platform they support, such as Desktop, Mobile, Web-based, and Embedded .
3. **By Scope**: The scope of a software project can also be used as a categorization factor, with projects being classified as Small, Medium, or Large .
4. **By User Interaction**: Software projects can also be categorized based on the type of user interaction they support, such as Batch or Interactive .
5. **By Maturity Level**: The maturity level of a software project can also be used as a categorization factor, with projects being classified as New, Legacy, or Mature .
6. **By Open-Source Software Participation**: Software projects can also be categorized based on their level of participation in open-source software, such as None, Partial, or Full .
7. **By Delivery Method**: The delivery method of a software project can also be used as a categorization factor, with projects being classified as Internal, Outsource, Hosted, or Cloud-Based .

These are some of the ways in which software projects can be categorized in SPM. Different categorization methods may be used depending on the specific needs and requirements of the project and the organization.
