### Sequencing and Scheduling in SPM

Sequencing and scheduling are two important techniques used in project management to organize and plan the tasks, jobs, or activities of a project. 

- **Sequencing** is the process of determining the order in which activities must be completed. It involves arranging the tasks in a logical sequence, where the next task is started once the previous one is completed .
- **Scheduling**, on the other hand, is the process of assigning time to accomplish different tasks. It involves creating a schedule that clearly indicates when each of the project's activities is planned to occur and what resources it will need  .

SPM (Sequencing and Scheduling Activities) is a process that helps to plan, coordinate, and execute complex projects. It enables project managers to schedule tasks, assign resources, and track progress in a way that minimizes risk and maximizes efficiency .

The ultimate goal of scheduling is to create a realistic project schedule that provides a basis for monitoring project progress for the time dimension of the project .
