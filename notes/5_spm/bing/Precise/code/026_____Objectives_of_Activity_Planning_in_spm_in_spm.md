### Objectives of Activity Planning in SPM

Activity planning is a crucial step in the Software Project Management (SPM) process. The main objectives of activity planning in SPM are:

1. **To identify and define project activities**: Activity planning involves breaking down the project into smaller, manageable tasks or activities. This helps in better understanding the scope of the project and the work involved.

2. **To determine the sequence of activities**: Once the activities have been identified, the next step is to determine the order in which they need to be performed. This is important for ensuring that the project progresses smoothly and efficiently.

3. **To estimate the duration of activities**: Activity planning also involves estimating the time required to complete each activity. This information is used to develop a realistic project schedule.

4. **To allocate resources**: Activity planning helps in identifying the resources (such as personnel, equipment, and materials) required for each activity. This information is used to allocate resources effectively and efficiently.

5. **To develop a project schedule**: The information gathered during activity planning is used to develop a project schedule. This schedule outlines the start and end dates for each activity and helps in tracking the progress of the project.

In summary, activity planning is an essential step in the SPM process that helps in identifying and defining project activities, determining their sequence, estimating their duration, allocating resources, and developing a project schedule. It is important for ensuring that the project is completed on time, within budget, and to the desired level of quality.
