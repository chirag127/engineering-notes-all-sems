### Collection of Data in SPM

1. **Primary Data:** Primary data is the data collected by the investigator himself for the first time. It is original in character and is generated by surveys, observations, experiments, and questionnaires.

2. **Secondary Data:** Secondary data is the data that has already been collected by someone else and is available for use. It is not original in character and is obtained from published or unpublished sources.

3. **Methods of Collecting Primary Data:**
    - **Direct Personal Investigation:** The investigator collects the data personally by visiting the respondents.
    - **Indirect Oral Investigation:** The investigator collects the data by asking questions from the persons who are supposed to have knowledge of the subject.
    - **Information through Correspondents:** The investigator appoints local agents or correspondents in different places to collect the data.
    - **Mailed Questionnaire Method:** The investigator sends the questionnaire by mail to the respondents with a request to fill it up and return it.
    - **Schedule Method:** The investigator or his representative fills up the schedule on the basis of the information collected from the respondents.

4. **Sources of Secondary Data:**
    - **Published Sources:** Published sources include books, newspapers, magazines, journals, and government publications.
    - **Unpublished Sources:** Unpublished sources include private diaries, letters, and unpublished biographies.
    - **Electronic Sources:** Electronic sources include the internet, television, and radio.
