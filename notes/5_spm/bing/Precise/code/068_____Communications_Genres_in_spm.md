### Communications Genres in SPM

1. **Emails**: Emails are a common form of communication in SPM. They are used to send messages, updates, and documents to team members, stakeholders, and clients.
2. **Meetings**: Meetings are used to discuss project progress, issues, and plans. They can be held in person, over the phone, or via video conference.
3. **Reports**: Reports are used to document project progress, issues, and plans. They can be shared with team members, stakeholders, and clients.
4. **Presentations**: Presentations are used to share project information with team members, stakeholders, and clients. They can be delivered in person, over the phone, or via video conference.
5. **Instant Messaging**: Instant messaging is used for quick, informal communication between team members. It can be used to ask questions, share updates, and coordinate tasks.
6. **Project Management Software**: Project management software is used to track project progress, assign tasks, and share information with team members. It can also be used to communicate with team members, stakeholders, and clients.
