### Stress in SPM

Stress is a common issue faced by students preparing for the Sijil Pelajaran Malaysia (SPM) examination. Here are some points to consider:

1. **Causes of stress:** Common causes of stress among SPM students include academic pressure, high expectations from parents and teachers, and fear of failure.

2. **Effects of stress:** Prolonged stress can have negative effects on a student's physical and mental health, including headaches, fatigue, anxiety, and depression.

3. **Managing stress:** There are several ways to manage stress, including regular exercise, getting enough sleep, eating a healthy diet, and practicing relaxation techniques such as deep breathing or meditation.

4. **Seeking help:** If stress becomes overwhelming, it is important for students to seek help from a trusted adult, such as a parent, teacher, or counselor.

5. **Preparation is key:** Proper preparation and time management can help reduce stress levels. Students should create a study schedule and stick to it, breaking up study sessions into manageable chunks and taking regular breaks.

Remember, stress is a normal part of life, and everyone experiences it from time to time. The key is to learn how to manage it effectively. With the right tools and support, students can successfully navigate the challenges of the SPM examination.
