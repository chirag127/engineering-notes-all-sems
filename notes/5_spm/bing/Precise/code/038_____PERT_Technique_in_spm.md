### PERT Technique in SPM

1. PERT (Program Evaluation and Review Technique) is a project management tool used to schedule, organize, and coordinate tasks within a project.
2. It is a statistical tool that represents a project as a network diagram made up of nodes (tasks) and arrows (dependencies between tasks).
3. PERT is used to estimate the minimum time required to complete a project by calculating the critical path, which is the longest sequence of tasks that must be completed on time for the project to meet its deadline.
4. PERT is particularly useful for complex projects with many interdependent tasks and uncertain task durations.
5. PERT was developed in the 1950s by the US Navy for the Polaris missile program.
6. PERT is often used in conjunction with the Critical Path Method (CPM), which is another project management tool that focuses on identifying the critical path of a project.
7. PERT is a useful tool for project managers as it helps them to identify potential delays and bottlenecks in the project, and to develop contingency plans to mitigate these risks.
8. PERT is not a perfect tool, and its accuracy depends on the accuracy of the estimates of task durations and dependencies. It is important for project managers to regularly review and update the PERT chart as the project progresses.
