### Framework for Management and Control in SPM

1. **Strategic Planning:** This involves setting long-term goals and objectives for the organization and developing strategies to achieve them. It also involves allocating resources and setting priorities to support the implementation of the strategies.

2. **Performance Measurement:** This involves establishing performance indicators and targets to measure the progress of the organization towards achieving its goals and objectives. It also involves collecting and analyzing data to assess the performance of the organization.

3. **Management Control:** This involves establishing policies, procedures, and systems to ensure that the organization operates effectively and efficiently. It also involves monitoring the performance of the organization and taking corrective action when necessary.

4. **Reporting and Communication:** This involves communicating the performance of the organization to internal and external stakeholders. It also involves providing information to support decision-making and to ensure accountability.

5. **Continuous Improvement:** This involves regularly reviewing and evaluating the performance of the organization and identifying opportunities for improvement. It also involves implementing changes to improve the effectiveness and efficiency of the organization.

These are the key components of a framework for management and control in SPM. By implementing these components, an organization can effectively manage and control its operations to achieve its goals and objectives.
