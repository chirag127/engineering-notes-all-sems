### Management Principles in SPM

1. **Planning**: This principle involves setting goals and objectives for the organization and developing strategies to achieve them. It is important to have a clear understanding of the organization's mission and vision, as well as its strengths and weaknesses, in order to develop effective plans.

2. **Organizing**: This principle involves arranging resources, including personnel, finances, and materials, in a way that will help the organization achieve its goals. This includes creating a structure for the organization, assigning roles and responsibilities, and establishing procedures for communication and decision-making.

3. **Leading**: This principle involves directing and motivating employees to achieve the organization's goals. This includes setting clear expectations, providing feedback and recognition, and creating a positive work environment.

4. **Controlling**: This principle involves monitoring the organization's performance and taking corrective action when necessary. This includes setting performance standards, measuring actual performance, and making changes to improve performance.

These principles are essential for effective management in any organization, including those in the field of SPM. By applying these principles, managers can help their organizations achieve their goals and operate efficiently.
