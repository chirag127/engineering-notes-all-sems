### Categorization of Software Projects in SPM

Software project management (SPM) involves the categorization of software projects into different types based on their characteristics. This categorization helps project managers to better understand the nature of the project and to apply appropriate management techniques. Some common categories of software projects include:

1. **Internal projects:** These are projects that are initiated and executed within an organization for its own use. They are usually aimed at improving the internal processes and operations of the organization.

2. **Contract projects:** These are projects that are initiated by an external client who contracts the software development organization to develop a software product or system. The client specifies the requirements and the development organization delivers the product according to the contract.

3. **Commercial projects:** These are projects that are initiated by the software development organization with the aim of developing a software product or system for sale in the market. The development organization invests its own resources in the project and hopes to make a profit from the sale of the product.

4. **Open-source projects:** These are projects where the source code of the software product or system is made available to the public for free. The development of the software is usually done collaboratively by a community of developers who contribute their time and expertise to the project.

Each of these categories of software projects has its own unique characteristics and challenges, and project managers need to be aware of these in order to effectively manage the project. For example, contract projects may require more formal documentation and communication with the client, while open-source projects may require more collaboration and community management. Understanding the category of the project can help project managers to apply the appropriate management techniques and to achieve project success.
