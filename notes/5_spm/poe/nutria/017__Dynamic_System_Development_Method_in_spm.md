
### Dynamic System Development Method in SPM

1. Dynamic System Development Method (DSDM) is an agile project delivery framework that focuses on the delivery of products in a highly collaborative and fast-paced environment.

2. DSDM is based on the principles of iterative development, where the product is incrementally developed in small cycles of development.

3. DSDM focuses on delivering the most important features of the product first and then iteratively adding more features as the project progresses.

4. The main goal of DSDM is to reduce the risk of project failure by delivering a working product as quickly as possible.

5. DSDM is also focused on delivering a high quality product that meets the customer's needs.

6. The DSDM process consists of eight phases: pre-project, feasibility study, business study, functional model iteration, design and build iteration, testing, implementation, and post-implementation review.

7. The DSDM process also includes a number of roles and responsibilities, such as the Project Manager, Business Analyst, Technical Architect, and Tester.

8. DSDM also emphasizes the importance of communication and collaboration between team members in order to ensure that the project is successful.
