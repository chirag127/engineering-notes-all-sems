
### Critical Path (CRM) Method in SPM

1. The Critical Path (CRM) Method is a project management tool used to plan and control the duration of a project.

2. It is based on the concept of critical path, which is the longest path in a project network diagram, and is used to determine the minimum completion time of the project.

3. The CRM method uses a network diagram to represent the activities, resources, and dependencies of a project.

4. Each activity is represented by a node, and the arrows represent the dependencies between the activities.

5. The length of an activity is determined by its duration and the amount of resources required to complete it.

6. The critical path is the sequence of activities that have the longest total duration, and the total duration of the project is equal to the duration of the critical path.

7. The CRM method can be used to identify the critical activities, estimate the duration of the project, and plan and control the resources required to complete the project.
