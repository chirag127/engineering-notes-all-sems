
### Methodologies in Software Project Management
* Agile: Agile is a project management methodology that focuses on delivering projects in small increments. It emphasizes an iterative approach, with frequent feedback and adaptation to changing requirements.
* Waterfall: The Waterfall methodology is a traditional approach to project management, where projects are broken down into distinct phases. It is a linear approach, with each phase building on the previous one.
* Scrum: Scrum is an iterative project management methodology that focuses on delivering projects in small increments. It emphasizes collaboration between the stakeholders, with frequent feedback and adaptation to changing requirements.
* Kanban: Kanban is a project management methodology that emphasizes visualizing the workflow and limiting work in progress. It is a flexible approach that can be used for both traditional and agile projects.
* Lean: Lean is a project management methodology that focuses on eliminating waste and improving efficiency. It emphasizes continuous improvement and the use of data to drive decisions.
