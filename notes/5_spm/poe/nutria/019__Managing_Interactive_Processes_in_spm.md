
### Managing Interactive Processes in SPM

1. Interactive processes are processes that require user input or interaction in order to complete the process.
2. An example of an interactive process is a web form, which requires users to fill in information and submit it in order to complete the process.
3. Interactive processes can be divided into two categories: synchronous and asynchronous.
4. Synchronous processes require the user to be present and actively interacting with the system in order to complete the process.
5. Asynchronous processes do not require the user to be present and can be completed without any user interaction.
6. In order to manage interactive processes, it is important to understand the different types of processes and how they interact with each other.
7. It is also important to understand the user experience of interacting with the system and how to optimize the process for maximum efficiency.
8. In order to design and implement effective interactive processes, it is important to consider the user's needs and goals, as well as the system's capabilities.
9. It is also important to consider the security and privacy implications of interactive processes, as they can be vulnerable to malicious actors.
10. Finally, it is important to ensure that the process is accessible to all users, regardless of their abilities or disabilities.
