
### Health and Safety in SPM

1. Safety is the primary concern in any workplace. Every workplace should have a safety plan that outlines the necessary steps to ensure the safety of everyone in the workplace.

2. Personal protective equipment (PPE) should be provided to all employees in order to protect them from potential hazards. PPE should include items such as safety glasses, gloves, and hard hats.

3. All employees should be trained on the proper use of PPE and other safety equipment. Training should also include instruction on how to recognize and avoid potential hazards.

4. All employees should be aware of the safety regulations and procedures in place in the workplace. This includes knowing the location of fire extinguishers, emergency exits, and other safety equipment.

5. All employees should be encouraged to report any potential hazards they may encounter. This includes reporting any potential safety violations or unsafe working conditions.

6. All employees should be encouraged to practice good safety habits, such as wearing PPE, following safety protocols, and reporting any potential hazards.

7. Regular safety inspections should be conducted to ensure that the workplace is free of any potential hazards.

8. First aid kits should be easily accessible in the workplace in case of an emergency.

9. All employees should be aware of the emergency procedures in place in the workplace. This includes knowing the location of the nearest hospital and how to contact emergency services.

10. All employees should be encouraged to take regular breaks and get adequate rest. This will help to reduce the risk of fatigue-related accidents.
