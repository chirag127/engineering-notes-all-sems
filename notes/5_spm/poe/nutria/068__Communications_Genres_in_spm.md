
### Communications Genres in SPM

1. **Interpersonal Communication**: This type of communication occurs between two or more people and often involves verbal, non-verbal, and written communication. It is used to establish relationships, share ideas, and build trust.

2. **Group Communication**: This type of communication occurs between two or more people in a group setting, such as a meeting, seminar, or class. Group communication involves sharing ideas and working together to reach a common goal.

3. **Organizational Communication**: This type of communication occurs between two or more people in an organization. It is used to coordinate activities, share information, and build relationships.

4. **Mass Communication**: This type of communication occurs between a sender and a large audience. It is used to reach a large number of people and is typically used in marketing, advertising, and public relations.

5. **Media Communication**: This type of communication occurs between a sender and a receiver through the use of media, such as television, radio, newspapers, and the internet. It is used to inform, educate, and entertain.
