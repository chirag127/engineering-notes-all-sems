
### Management Control in SPM

1. Management control is a process used by organizations to ensure that their strategies are being implemented and that their goals are being achieved.

2. It includes setting objectives, measuring performance, and taking corrective action when necessary.

3. The main purpose of management control is to ensure that the organization is running efficiently and effectively.

4. Management control systems are designed to monitor and measure the performance of individuals, teams, departments, and the entire organization.

5. They involve a combination of financial and non-financial measures to assess the performance of the organization.

6. Management control systems can be used to identify and address any areas of underperformance, as well as to reward good performance.

7. They can also be used to identify opportunities for improvement and to ensure that the organization is meeting its objectives.

8. Management control systems are essential for any organization, as they help ensure that the organization is achieving its goals and objectives.
