
## Unit - 3 - Activity Planning and Risk Management

Activity planning is a critical part of software project management. It involves the identification of tasks and activities that must be completed in order to achieve project objectives. Proper activity planning can help to reduce the risk of project failure by ensuring that tasks are completed on time and within budget.

- Identify the project objectives and scope.
- Break the project down into smaller tasks and activities.
- Estimate the time and resources required for each task.
- Establish dependencies between tasks.
- Create a timeline for completion of the project.
- Monitor progress and adjust the timeline as needed.
- Identify potential risks and develop strategies to mitigate them.
- Establish a communication plan to ensure that stakeholders are kept informed of progress.
- Manage changes to the project plan.
- Evaluate the project upon completion.
