
### Leadership in SPM

1. SPM stands for Strategic Performance Management and is the process of setting objectives, measuring progress towards those objectives, and taking corrective action to ensure the objectives are met.

2. Leadership in SPM is essential for successful implementation and execution of the process. Leaders must be able to motivate their teams, develop a plan to achieve the objectives, and provide guidance and feedback throughout the process.

3. Leaders must be able to communicate effectively with their teams and stakeholders in order to ensure that everyone is working towards the same objectives. They must also be able to identify potential risks and develop strategies to mitigate them.

4. Leaders must be able to make tough decisions when necessary and be willing to take responsibility for their actions. They must also be able to delegate tasks to their teams and provide support and guidance when needed.

5. Leaders must also be able to use data and analytics to make informed decisions and to measure the success of their strategies. They must be able to identify areas of improvement and take corrective action when necessary.
