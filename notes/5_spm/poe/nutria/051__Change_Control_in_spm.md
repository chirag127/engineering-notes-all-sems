
### Change Control in SPM

* Change control is a process used to manage and document changes in a project or system.
* It is important to have an effective change control system in place to ensure that any changes made to the system are tracked, documented and approved by the relevant stakeholders.
* Change control involves identifying, assessing, authorizing, implementing and documenting changes.
* The change control process should be tailored to the specific project or system, taking into account the complexity and scope of the change.
* A change control system should include a change request form that outlines the details of the proposed change, including the impact on the system, the cost of the change, the timeline for implementation and the stakeholders involved.
* The change request form should be reviewed by the relevant stakeholders and approved before any changes are made.
* Once the change has been approved, the change control process should include monitoring and tracking the changes to ensure they are implemented correctly and on schedule.
* Change control is an important part of systems engineering and project management, and should be included in any system or project plan.
