### Communications Genres in SPM

In SPM (Standard Progressive Matrices), communication genres refer to the various forms of communication used to convey information. Understanding different communication genres is essential for effective communication.

Here are the types of communication genres that one should know:

- **Narrative**: Narrative is a genre of communication that tells a story. It can be either fictional or non-fictional. A narrative often includes characters, setting, plot, and conflict.

- **Expository**: Expository communication is a genre that explains or informs. It is used when the writer wants to give information or explain something. It can be found in textbooks, manuals, and encyclopedias.

- **Persuasive**: Persuasive communication is a type of communication that aims to persuade the audience to take a specific action or believe in a particular idea. It is often used in advertising, speeches, and political campaigns.

- **Descriptive**: Descriptive communication is a genre that describes a person, place, or thing in detail. It is often used in literature, poetry, and travel writing.

- **Argumentative**: Argumentative communication is a genre that involves presenting evidence to support a particular position or idea. It is often used in debate, legal writing, and academic writing.

- **Instructional**: Instructional communication is a genre that gives directions or explains how to do something. It can be found in manuals, recipes, and how-to guides.

- **Informative**: Informative communication is a genre that provides useful and valuable information. It can be found in news articles, documentaries, and educational videos.

- **Entertainment**: Entertainment communication is a genre that is designed to entertain the audience. It includes genres such as movies, music, and television shows.

By understanding these different genres of communication, you can effectively communicate in different situations and contexts. Each genre has its unique features and characteristics that make it suitable for specific purposes.
