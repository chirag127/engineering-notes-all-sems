## Unit - 1 - Project Evaluation and Project Planning

1. Project Identification
- Identify potential projects through business needs, market demands, technological advances, or legal requirements.
- Evaluate proposed projects based on strategic alignment, costs, benefits, and risks to select projects that provide the greatest value to the organization.

2. Project Charter
- Document the business case for the project including costs, benefits, scope, objectives, and risks.
- Obtain sponsor's approval and authorization to commit resources to the project.

3. Project Planning
- Develop a detailed project plan that includes tasks, resource requirements, costs, and schedule.
- Sequence tasks properly considering dependencies and resource constraints.
- Estimate task durations and resource requirements and determine critical path to complete project on schedule.
- Prepare schedules and budgets and obtain sponsor approval.
- Plan for risks and quality assurance.

4. Project Monitoring and Control
- Track progress, costs, and risks and take corrective actions as needed to ensure project objectives are met.
- Obtain necessary approvals before making changes to plans.
- Accept or reject deliverables, provide feedback, and take actions to resolve issues.
