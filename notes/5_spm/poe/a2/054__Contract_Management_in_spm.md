### Contract Management in spm

1. Define requirements: Clearly define requirements and scope of work. Any ambiguity can lead to disputes later.
2. Choose the right type of contract: Choose an appropriate type of contract - fixed price, cost plus, time and materials, etc. based on nature of work and risk involved.
3. Define deliverables: Clearly list down all deliverables and milestones. Leave no room for misunderstanding.
4. Specify terms and conditions: Include standard terms and conditions to allocate risks and responsibilities. Customize based on the project as needed.
5. Get necessary approvals: Get necessary approvals from stakeholders before signing the contract.
6. Formalize the agreement: Formally sign the contract with the chosen vendor/supplier to make it a legally valid document.
7. Manage and control: Proactively manage and control the contract to ensure adherence to terms and conditions as well as dealing with any issues or changes.
8. Close the contract: Formalize contract closure and settle any pending payments or issues. Archive the contract documentation for future reference.
