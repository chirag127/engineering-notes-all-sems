### Framework for Management and Control in spm

1. Planning: Deciding in advance what to do, how to do it, when to do it and who will do it. It bridges the gap from where we are to where we want to be.
2. Organizing: Arranging and structuring work to accomplish the plan. It involves distributing and allocating work, coordinating resources and delegating authority.
3. Leading: Influencing people to work willingly and enthusiastically to achieve goals. It involves motivation, communication, guidance, etc.
4. Controlling: Monitoring performance and taking corrective action as needed. It involves measuring progress, comparing with objectives and taking necessary steps to ensure desired results.
