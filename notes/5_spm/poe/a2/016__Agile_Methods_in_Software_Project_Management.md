### Agile Methods in Software Project Management

1. Agile methods refer to a group of software development methods based on iterative and incremental development, where requirements and solutions evolve through collaboration between self-organizing, cross-functional teams.
2. The Agile Manifesto outlines four core values:

- Individuals and interactions over processes and tools
- Working software over comprehensive documentation
- Customer collaboration over contract negotiation
- Responding to change over following a plan

3. Some of the popular Agile methods are:

- Scrum: Focuses on teamwork and uses an iterative, incremental approach. Work is done in sprints (timeboxed periods, usually 2 weeks) and the requirements can change. Daily stand-up meetings and sprint planning meetings are held.
- Extreme Programming (XP): Focuses on programming best practices and incorporates frequent feedback, continuous testing, and continuous integration. Releases are made early and often.
- Lean Software Development: Focuses on delivering customer value by reducing wastage and increasing speed and quality. It incorporates principles like "eliminate waste" and "build integrity in".
- Kanban: Focuses on visualizing work and limiting work-in-progress to identify and remove bottlenecks. The workflow is represented on a Kanban board with columns representing different stages of work.

4. The benefits of Agile methods include:

- Early and continuous delivery of valuable software
- Ability to adapt to changing requirements
- Increased visibility into project status
- Better team morale and productivity
- Higher quality software
- Closer alignment with customer needs
