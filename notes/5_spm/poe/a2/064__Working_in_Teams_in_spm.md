### Working in Teams in spm

1. Define clear goals and objectives: Having a clear set of goals and objectives that the team is working towards is crucial for team success. Define the key tasks and metrics to track progress. Communicating these goals and metrics to the entire team ensures everyone is on the same page about what needs to be achieved.

2. Determine roles and responsibilities: With the goals defined, determine and assign key roles and responsibilities to team members that play to each individual's strengths. Define decision making processes and procedures for resolving conflicts. Clear roles and processes reduce confusion and duplication of work.

3. Foster open communication: For teams to work effectively, there must be open communication amongst team members. Provide opportunities and avenues for team members to ask questions and raise concerns. Promote a culture of transparency and constructive feedback. Open communication leads to a better understanding of issues and faster resolution of problems.

4. Focus on accountability: While teams work together to achieve goals, individual team members must be accountable for their responsibilities. Track progress against defined metrics and goals and identify any obstacles or blockers. Follow up with team members to ensure tasks are completed on time and to the expected quality standards. Accountability improves the reliability and delivery of work.

5. Evaluate and improve: Regularly evaluate how the team is working together and how to improve. Collect feedback from team members and analyze team dynamics and performance. Identify opportunities for improvement which could include expanding skills through training, refining processes, or reallocating team member roles. Continual reflection and improvement sustains high team performance.
