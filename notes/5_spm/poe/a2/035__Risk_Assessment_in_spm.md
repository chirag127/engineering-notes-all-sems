### Risk Assessment in Software Project Management

1. Risk identification: Identifying potential risks that may affect the project objectives like budget overrun, delays in schedule, lack of resources etc. Brainstorming sessions, interviews, workshops can be conducted to identify risks.
2. Risk analysis: Analyzing the impact and likelihood of the identified risks. Risks are analyzed in terms of impact on cost, time and scope. Both quantitative and qualitative techniques can be used for risk analysis.
3. Risk response planning: Planning responses to the identified risks. For high impact and high likelihood risks, responses like risk avoidance, risk transference, risk mitigation, risk acceptance are planned.
4. Risk monitoring and control: Continuously monitoring risks and implementing risk response plans. As the project progresses, new risks may emerge or existing risks may change. Risk monitoring helps identify and respond to these changes.

The above points cover the key steps involved in risk assessment and management in software project management. A formal risk management process helps identify and mitigate threats to project objectives, thereby increasing chances of project success.
