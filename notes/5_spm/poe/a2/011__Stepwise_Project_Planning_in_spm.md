### Stepwise Project Planning in spm

1. Identify the project goal and objectives
- Clearly define the scope and requirements of the project
- Determine key deliverables and success criteria

2. Estimate budget and resources
- Estimate costs of human resources, equipment, and other materials
- Determine resource availability and allocate resources accordingly
- Account for contingency and risk mitigation costs

3. Create a work breakdown structure
- Break down the project into smaller, manageable tasks
- Group related tasks into phases and components
- Assign responsibilities and deadlines for each task

4. Prepare a schedule
- Determine dependencies between tasks and sequence them accordingly
- Assign durations for each task based on estimates
- Include buffers for risks and delays
- Track progress and update schedule as required

5. Risk management
- Identify potential risks that may impact the project
- Assess the likelihood and impact of each risk
- Plan risk mitigation measures to minimize impact
- continuously monitor for risks and update risk management plans

6. Execute the project
- Carry out the work as per schedule
- Evaluate progress, analyze variances, and take corrective action
- Report on key performance indicators and report problems
- Get approvals and handovers as required between project phases

7. Close the project
- Obtain final signoff and acceptance of the project deliverables
- Document the project and lessons learned
- Release resources and do a final audit of costs and schedule
- Recognize and reward project team members
