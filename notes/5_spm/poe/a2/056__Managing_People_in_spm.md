### Managing People in spm

1. Clearly define expectations: It is important to clearly communicate expectations to team members. Set specific, measurable, achievable, relevant and time-bound objectives. This will eliminate confusion and ensure everyone is working towards the same goals.

2. Provide necessary resources: For people to perform their jobs well, they need to have the necessary resources and tools required. As a manager, it is your responsibility to allocate required resources such as equipment, funds, etc. to your team members. Lack of resources can hinder productivity and growth.

3. Offer feedback and recognition: Regular feedback and recognition can boost team morale and motivation. Provide both positive and constructive feedback to your team members and recognize good work and achievements. This will encourage the right behaviors and better results.

4. Resolve issues: As a manager, you need to address any issues or conflicts proactively. Hold regular meetings to understand challenges and resolve problems. Foster an environment where team members feel comfortable communicating openly about any issues they are facing.

5. Train and develop: Continuous training and development is important for growth and progress. As a manager, you should conduct regular training sessions to enhance the skills and knowledge of your team members. This will enable them to perform better and prepare for more senior roles in the organization.

6. Lead by example: The best way to earn your team's respect and motivate them is by leading by example. Practice the behaviors and ways of working that you want to see from your team members. Model integrity, accountability, communication, and other such leadership traits in your own work.
