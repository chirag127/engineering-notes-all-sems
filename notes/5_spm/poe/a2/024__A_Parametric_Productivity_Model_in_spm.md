### A Parametric Productivity Model in spm

1. Define productivity as output/input ratio: Productivity (P) = Output (O) / Input (I)
2. Identify major outputs and inputs:
- Outputs: Products, services, satisfied customers, profit, growth, etc.
- Inputs: Time, money, resources, people, etc.
3. Select key outputs and inputs to monitor: Focus on 3-5 major outputs and 3-5 major inputs that substantially impact productivity.
4. Set target levels for outputs and inputs: Establish realistic targets, goals or objectives for the key outputs and inputs.
5. Measure actual levels of outputs and inputs: Regularly measure actual performance and calculate the output/input ratios.
6. Analyze gaps between actual and target levels: Identify gaps or discrepancies between actual and target levels of the output/input ratios.
7. Take action to address gaps: Implement strategies and actions to address the gaps and improve the output/input ratios.
8. Repeat and adjust: Continually monitor, measure and take action to optimize the productivity of the system. Adjust targets and actions as needed.

The above points describe a basic parametric model for managing and optimizing productivity in a system. The key is to regularly monitor and measure actual output/input ratios, compare with targets, analyze gaps and take action to close the gaps. The specific outputs, inputs, targets and actions would depend on the particular system and organization.
