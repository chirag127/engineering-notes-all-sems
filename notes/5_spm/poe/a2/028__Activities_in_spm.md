### Activities in SPM

1. **Revision:** Regular revision of all topics and subjects is essential. Make short notes and revise them frequently. Solve previous year question papers and sample papers to get an idea of the question paper pattern and types of questions.
2. **Practice:** Practice as many problems and questions as possible. This will help in strengthening the concepts and smoothening the process of applying the concepts to solve problems. Practicing regularly improves speed and accuracy.
3. **Discussions:** Have discussions with teachers and classmates on difficult topics and concepts. This helps in getting a better understanding of the topics and different ways to approach problems. Discussing with others also adds different perspectives to a topic which makes learning more effective.
4. **Positivity:** Having a positive and confident mindset goes a long way in dealing with exams. Do not get overwhelmed by the vast syllabus or difficult topics. With consistent efforts and hard work, students can achieve success in the exam. Taking breaks in between long study sessions and engaging in recreational activities helps in relaxation and rejuvenation.
5. **Sleep:** Having adequate sleep and rest is essential for concentration, focus and application of concepts. Lack of sleep can lead to fatigue, lack of comprehension and unintended errors. Hence, students must get at least 7-8 hours of quality sleep every day to achieve their full potential in the exam.
