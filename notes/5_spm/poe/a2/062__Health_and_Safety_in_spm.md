### Health and Safety in spm

1. Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)
- Wear appropriate PPE like safety goggles, dust masks, safety shoes, ear plugs, etc. depending on the activity.
- Ensure PPE fits properly and is not damaged. Ill-fitting or damaged PPE will not provide adequate protection.

2. Safe Working Practices
- Follow all safety procedures and instructions provided.
- Ensure work area is clear of clutter and all cords/equipment are properly secured to avoid tripping/falling hazards.
- Use equipment properly and wear appropriate safety mechanisms like blade guards and wheel chocks.
- Report any damaged equipment or safety issues to the relevant personnel immediately.

3. Emergency Procedures
- Familiarize yourself with emergency exits, alarm points, fire extinguishers, first aid kits, and evacuation procedures.
- Know how to respond to potential emergencies like fires, chemical spills, medical emergencies, etc.
- Practice emergency drills and remain calm if an actual emergency occurs. Get to safety and alert others if necessary.

4. Housekeeping
- Work area should be tidy and tools/equipment should be properly stored when not in use.
- Spills should be cleaned up immediately to avoid slips and other hazards.
- Report any damage or safety issues with the work environment to the relevant personnel.
