### Objectives of Activity Planning in spm

1. Identify the goals and objectives: The primary objective of activity planning is to identify the goals and objectives of the project or program. The planner needs to determine the expected outcomes and impacts to achieve through the activity.

2. Determine resource requirements: The activity planner determines the kind of resources - financial, human, technical, and material - required to implement the planned activities. Resource estimation is done at the planning stage to ensure availability of necessary resources for successful execution of the activities.

3. Establish activity schedule: The planner determines the schedule and timelines for the activities. This helps in implementing the activities as per the schedule to achieve the goals and objectives in a timely manner. A well-designed schedule provides a structured plan for task execution.

4. Assign responsibilities: The activity planner assigns responsibilities to appropriate personnel to execute the planned activities effectively. Responsibilities are assigned based on the skills and expertise of the employees or team members. This ensures accountability for all the tasks and activities.

5. Foresee challenges: A good activity planner foresees potential challenges or obstacles that may impact the successful execution of the planned activities. By anticipating challenges in advance, the planner can develop strategies or alternatives to address the challenges and minimize risks or disruptions to activity implementation.

6. Allocate budget: The activity planner allocates necessary budget for the planned activities. The budget needs to be adequate and aligned with the resource requirements and timelines for the activities. Proper budget allocation at the planning stage maximizes the efficient and effective use of funds to achieve the project goals and objectives.
