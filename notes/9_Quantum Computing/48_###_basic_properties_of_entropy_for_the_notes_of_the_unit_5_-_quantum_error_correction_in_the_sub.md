### Basic properties of Entropy for the notes of the Unit 5 - Quantum Error Correction in the subject of Quantum Computing
1. Entropy is a measure of the amount of uncertainty or randomness in a system. 
2. It is defined as the negative of the sum of the probabilities of each possible outcome multiplied by the logarithm of those probabilities. 
3. Higher entropy means greater uncertainty, while lower entropy indicates more order or predictability. 
4. In quantum computing, entropy is used to quantify the amount of quantum information in a quantum state. 
5. The entropy of a quantum state can never be negative and is always bounded above by the logarithm of the dimension of the state space. 
6. The entropy of a quantum state can be used to determine the amount of quantum information that can be transmitted or stored in that state.
