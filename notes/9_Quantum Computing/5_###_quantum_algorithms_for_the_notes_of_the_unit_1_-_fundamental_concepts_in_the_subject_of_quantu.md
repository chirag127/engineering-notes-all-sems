### Quantum Algorithms for the notes of the Unit 1 - Fundamental Concepts in the subject of Quantum Computing
Quantum Algorithms:
- Quantum algorithms use the properties of quantum mechanics to solve problems faster than classical algorithms.
- Examples: Shor's algorithm for prime factorization, Grover's algorithm for database search, and Simon's algorithm for period finding.
- Advantages: exploitation of quantum parallelism and interference.
- Limitations: susceptible to quantum noise and require specialized hardware.

Unit 1 - Fundamental Concepts in Quantum Computing:
- Quantum bits (qubits) and quantum states.
- Superposition and entanglement.
- Quantum gates and quantum circuits.
- Measurement and collapse of the wave function.
- No-cloning theorem and quantum teleportation.
- Bloch sphere representation of qubits.
