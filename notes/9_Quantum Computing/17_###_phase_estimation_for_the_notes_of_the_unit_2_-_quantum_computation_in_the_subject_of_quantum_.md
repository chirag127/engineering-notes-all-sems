### Phase estimation for the notes of the Unit 2 - Quantum Computation in the subject of Quantum Computing
Phase estimation is a quantum algorithm used to estimate the eigenvalues of a unitary operator. It is a key component in several quantum algorithms, including Shor's algorithm for factoring integers and simulating quantum systems. The basic idea of the phase estimation algorithm is to use the quantum Fourier transform to map the eigenvalues of a unitary operator to the amplitudes of a quantum state. This allows us to obtain an estimate of the eigenvalues by measuring the quantum state. The accuracy of the estimate depends on the number of qubits used and the number of measurements performed. The phase estimation algorithm is an important tool for understanding the behavior of quantum systems and has a wide range of applications in quantum computing and quantum information processing.
