### Universal Quantum Gates for the notes of the Unit 2 - Quantum Computation in the subject of Quantum Computing
Universal quantum gates are the building blocks of quantum circuits. They are basic operations that can be combined to perform more complex quantum algorithms. The main universal quantum gates are:

1. Hadamard (H) gate: This gate is used to put a qubit into a superposition state.

2. Pauli X (X) gate: This gate flips the state of a qubit from 0 to 1 or vice versa.

3. Pauli Y (Y) gate: This gate rotates the state of a qubit around the Y-axis in the Bloch sphere.

4. Pauli Z (Z) gate: This gate rotates the state of a qubit around the Z-axis in the Bloch sphere.

5. CNOT (Controlled NOT) gate: This gate is a two-qubit gate that flips the state of the target qubit if the control qubit is in the state 1.

6. CZ (Controlled Z) gate: This gate is a two-qubit gate that applies a Z gate to the target qubit if the control qubit is in the state 1.

These gates can be combined to perform more complex operations and are the foundation of quantum algorithms.
