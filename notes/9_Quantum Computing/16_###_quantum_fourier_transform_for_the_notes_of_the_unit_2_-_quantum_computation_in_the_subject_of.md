### Quantum Fourier transform for the notes of the Unit 2 - Quantum Computation in the subject of Quantum Computing
Quantum Fourier Transform (QFT) is a linear transformation used in quantum computing to perform the discrete Fourier transform (DFT) of a quantum state. It is a key component of many quantum algorithms, including Shor's algorithm for factoring integers and Grover's algorithm for searching an unsorted database. QFT maps the quantum state of a system to its frequency space representation. The output of QFT is a superposition of all possible frequencies of the input state, allowing for efficient quantum algorithms. The QFT can be implemented using quantum gates, such as Hadamard and controlled-phase gates. The complexity of QFT is O(n^2), where n is the number of qubits in the input state.
