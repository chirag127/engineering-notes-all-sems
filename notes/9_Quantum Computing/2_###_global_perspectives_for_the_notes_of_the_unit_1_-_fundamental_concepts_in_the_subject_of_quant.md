### Global Perspectives for the notes of the Unit 1 - Fundamental Concepts in the subject of Quantum Computing
Quantum computing is a new and rapidly evolving field that has the potential to revolutionize many areas of science and technology. The fundamental concepts of quantum computing include:

1. Quantum bits (qubits) - the basic building blocks of quantum computing, which can exist in multiple states at the same time.

2. Superposition - the ability of qubits to exist in multiple states at the same time, which makes quantum computing much more powerful than classical computing.

3. Entanglement - a quantum phenomenon where the state of one qubit is dependent on the state of another qubit, even when they are separated by large distances.

4. Interference - the ability of quantum systems to interfere with each other, which can be used to solve problems that are difficult or impossible to solve using classical computing.

5. Quantum algorithms - algorithms that are specifically designed to take advantage of the unique properties of quantum computing, such as superposition and entanglement.

6. Quantum error correction - techniques used to correct errors that occur in quantum computing, which are a result of the delicate nature of quantum systems.

7. Quantum simulation - the use of quantum computing to simulate quantum systems, which can provide insights into the behavior of quantum systems that are difficult or impossible to observe using classical computing.

Overall, the field of quantum computing is still in its early stages, but it has the potential to have a major impact on many areas of science and technology in the future.
