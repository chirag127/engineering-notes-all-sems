## Unit 5 - Quantum Error Correction
Quantum Error Correction (QEC) is a technique used to protect quantum information from errors caused by various sources such as noise in quantum systems. It works by encoding the quantum information into a larger number of physical qubits, or quantum bits, which are more robust to errors. The encoded information can then be recovered even if some of the physical qubits become corrupted. QEC is crucial for building large-scale quantum computers, which will be able to perform certain tasks much faster than classical computers. Some common QEC codes include the Shor code, Steane code, and the surface code.
