### Single Orbit operations for the notes of the Unit 2 - Quantum Computation in the subject of Quantum Computing
Single orbit operations in quantum computation refers to the manipulation of individual quantum states or qubits in a quantum system. These operations are performed using quantum gates, which are the basic building blocks of quantum circuits. The goal of single orbit operations is to control the state of a qubit, allowing it to be in either a 0 or 1 state. This is done by applying a unitary transformation to the qubit, which changes its state but preserves its overall quantum properties. Some common single orbit operations include the NOT gate, the Hadamard gate, and the rotation gates. These operations are crucial for performing quantum algorithms and for implementing quantum error correction techniques.
