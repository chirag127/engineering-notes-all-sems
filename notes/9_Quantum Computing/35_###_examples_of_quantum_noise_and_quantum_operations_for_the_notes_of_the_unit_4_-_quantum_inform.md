### Examples of Quantum noise and Quantum Operations for the notes of the Unit 4 - Quantum Information in the subject of Quantum Computing
Quantum Noise:
1. Amplitude Damping: loss of energy from a quantum system to its environment
2. Dephasing: loss of coherence between quantum states
3. Phase Fluctuations: random fluctuations in the phase of a quantum system
4. Thermal Noise: random fluctuations in energy due to temperature

Quantum Operations:
1. Quantum Gates: unitary operations that manipulate quantum states
2. Measurement: process of extracting information from a quantum system
3. State Preparation: process of preparing a quantum system in a specific state
4. Quantum Channel: a physical process that transforms quantum states.
