## Unit 4 - Quantum Information
Quantum Information is a field of study that deals with the application of quantum mechanics to the processing, communication, and storage of information. It encompasses topics such as quantum cryptography, quantum computing, and quantum communication. In quantum computing, quantum bits (qubits) are used instead of classical bits to perform calculations in a more efficient manner. In quantum communication, quantum states are used to transmit information securely. In quantum cryptography, the principles of quantum mechanics are used to create secure communication channels. Overall, quantum information provides new ways of processing and transmitting information that are not possible with classical information processing techniques.
