### Strong Sub Additivity for the notes of the Unit 5 - Quantum Error Correction in the subject of Quantum Computing
Strong Subadditivity (SSA) is a mathematical theorem in quantum information theory that states that the entropy of a composite quantum system cannot be greater than the sum of the entropies of its individual parts. This theorem provides a fundamental limit on the amount of information that can be transmitted through a quantum channel and is a key concept in the field of quantum error correction. SSA is used to determine the maximum amount of information that can be transmitted through a noisy quantum channel, and to design quantum error correction codes that can correct errors introduced by the channel. SSA is also used to study the properties of quantum entanglement and quantum correlations, and to understand the relationship between entropy and information in quantum systems.
