
### History of Internet

- The Internet is a global system of interconnected computer networks that use the Internet Protocol Suite (TCP/IP) to link billions of devices worldwide. 
- The history of the Internet began in the 1960s when the United States Department of Defense funded research into packet switching and ARPANET. 
- The first message was sent over ARPANET in 1969.
- In the early 1970s, the first commercial dial-up networks were created, allowing users to connect to the Internet.
- In the 1980s, the Internet Protocol Suite (TCP/IP) was standardized, allowing computers to communicate over the Internet.
- In the 1990s, the World Wide Web was developed, allowing users to access information on the Internet. 
- In the 2000s, the development of broadband and mobile networks enabled users to access the Internet from anywhere. 
- In the 2010s, the development of social media and cloud computing allowed users to share and store data on the Internet.
