### Connecting to the Internet

1. **Internet Service Provider (ISP):** To connect to the internet, you need to subscribe to an Internet Service Provider (ISP). ISPs provide internet access to customers through various technologies such as broadband, DSL, or dial-up.

2. **Modem:** A modem is a device that connects your computer or home network to the internet. It converts the digital signals from your computer into analog signals that can be transmitted over the telephone or cable lines, and vice versa.

3. **Router:** A router is a device that connects multiple devices to the internet. It directs internet traffic between your home network and the internet. Some routers also have built-in modems, which means you only need one device to connect to the internet.

4. **Ethernet cable or Wi-Fi:** To connect your computer or device to the internet, you can use an Ethernet cable or connect wirelessly through Wi-Fi. An Ethernet cable provides a wired connection between your device and the router, while Wi-Fi provides a wireless connection.

5. **Web browser:** Once you are connected to the internet, you can access the World Wide Web using a web browser. A web browser is a software application that allows you to view and interact with websites.
