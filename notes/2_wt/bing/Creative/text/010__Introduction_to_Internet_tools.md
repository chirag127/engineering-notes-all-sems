### Introduction to Internet tools

- Internet tools are electronic instruments based on Internet technology with which parties can interact.
- Internet tools can be used for various purposes, such as communication, information, commerce, entertainment, education, and collaboration.
- Some examples of Internet tools are:
  - Intranet: a private network within an organization that uses Internet protocols and standards.
  - Extranet: a network that connects an organization with its external partners, such as suppliers, customers, or collaborators, using Internet protocols and standards.
  - Web page: a document that can be accessed and viewed on the World Wide Web, a system of interlinked hypertext documents on the Internet.
  - E-mail: a system of sending and receiving messages electronically over a computer network.
  - Videoconference: a system of transmitting and receiving video and audio signals over a computer network, allowing people in different locations to communicate face-to-face.
  - Discussion groups: online forums where people can post messages and reply to others on a specific topic or interest.
- Internet tools can be accessed by various devices, such as computers, smartphones, tablets, or smart TVs, that are connected to the Internet, a system architecture that has revolutionized communications and methods of commerce by allowing various computer networks around the world to interconnect.
- Internet tools can provide many benefits, such as:
  - Enhancing productivity and efficiency by facilitating data sharing, coordination, and automation.
  - Expanding access and reach by overcoming geographical and temporal barriers.
  - Increasing innovation and creativity by enabling new forms of expression, interaction, and collaboration.
  - Improving learning and education by offering diverse and rich sources of information, knowledge, and skills.
  - Empowering individuals and communities by providing opportunities for participation, engagement, and empowerment.
