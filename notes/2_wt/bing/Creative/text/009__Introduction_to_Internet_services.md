### Introduction to Internet services

- Internet services are the applications or functions that allow users to access, exchange, or create information over the Internet, which is a global network of interconnected computers.
- Internet services can be classified into four main categories: communication, information, collaboration, and commerce.
- Communication services enable users to interact with other people or groups through various modes, such as email, chat, voice, video, social media, etc. Examples of communication services are Gmail, Skype, WhatsApp, Facebook, Twitter, etc.
- Information services provide users with access to a vast amount of data, such as text, graphics, sound, software, etc. that can be searched, downloaded, or uploaded. Examples of information services are Google, Wikipedia, YouTube, Dropbox, etc.
- Collaboration services allow users to work together on common projects or tasks, such as creating documents, sharing files, editing photos, etc. Examples of collaboration services are Google Docs, Google Drive, Slack, Zoom, etc.
- Commerce services enable users to buy or sell goods or services online, such as products, tickets, subscriptions, etc. Examples of commerce services are Amazon, eBay, Netflix, Spotify, etc.
