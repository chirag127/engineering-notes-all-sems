Web development strategies are the methods and techniques used in the planning, design, development, deployment and management of web applications and systems. The aim of web development strategies is to ensure that these applications and systems are aligned with the business goals of the organisation, and that they meet the needs of the users.

Some of the best web development strategies to know are :

- Use relevant images and terms that describe the content and purpose of the website.
- Include social media sharing links to increase the visibility and reach of the website.
- Adopt calls to action (CTAs) that engage the visitors and guide them to the main services or products offered by the website.
- Use precise images that appeal to the target audience and convey the message of the website.
- Optimize the website for speed, performance, security and accessibility.
- Use responsive design that adapts to different devices and screen sizes.
- Use web analytics tools to measure and improve the website's performance and user experience.
