There are several tools and websites that can help you draw ASCII diagrams, such as Asciio, DrawIt, Asciiflow, JavE, and Graph::Easy. These tools allow you to create boxes, lines, arrows, and other shapes using ASCII characters. You can also export your diagrams to text or HTML format.

To draw a detailed ASCII diagram for connecting to the internet, you need to consider the components and connections involved in the process. For example, you may have a device, such as a computer or a smartphone, that connects to a router or a modem, which in turn connects to an internet service provider (ISP), which then connects to the internet backbone, which consists of various networks and servers around the world.
