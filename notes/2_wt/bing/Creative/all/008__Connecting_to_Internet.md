### Connecting to Internet

- Connecting to the internet is the process of establishing a communication link between a device and a network that provides access to various online services and resources.
- There are different types of internet connections, such as wireless broadband, ethernet, and dial-up, each with its own advantages and disadvantages.
- Wireless broadband, also known as Wi-Fi, is the most common and convenient way of connecting to the internet, especially for mobile devices. It uses radio waves to transmit data between a device and a router or a hotspot. To connect to a wireless network, you need to:
  - Turn on the Wi-Fi feature on your device. You can usually find it in the network settings or the quick settings menu.
  - Select the network name (SSID) that you want to connect to. You may need to enter a password or a security key if the network is protected.
  - Wait for the connection to be established. You should see a Wi-Fi icon on your device's status bar or notification area.
- Ethernet is a wired connection that uses a cable to connect a device to a router or a modem. It is faster and more reliable than wireless broadband, but it is less flexible and portable. To connect to an ethernet network, you need to:
  - Plug one end of an ethernet cable into the ethernet port on your device. It is usually located on the back or the side of the device and has a shape similar to a phone jack.
  - Plug the other end of the cable into the router or the modem. It may have a label that says "LAN" or "Internet".
  - Wait for the connection to be established. You should see an ethernet icon on your device's status bar or notification area.
- Dial-up is an outdated connection that uses a phone line to connect a device to an internet service provider (ISP). It is slow and noisy, and it prevents you from using the phone line for other purposes. To connect to a dial-up network, you need to:
  - Ensure that you have a dial-up modem installed on your device. It is usually an internal or external device that connects to the phone jack on your device or the wall.
  - Connect the modem to the phone jack using a phone cable. You may need to use a splitter if you have only one phone jack in your location.
  - Configure the dial-up settings on your device. You may need to enter the phone number, the username, and the password provided by your ISP.
  - Dial the ISP's number using the modem. You should hear some beeping and buzzing sounds as the modem negotiates the connection.
  - Wait for the connection to be established. You should see a dial-up icon on your device's status bar or notification area.

- A mnemonic to remember the types of internet connections is: **WED** (Wireless, Ethernet, Dial-up).
- A learning trick to understand the difference between wireless and ethernet connections is: **Wireless is like a radio, ethernet is like a cable**.
