### Jain Literature in Pali, Prakrit, and Sanskrit

Jain literature is vast and diverse, with texts written in various languages such as Pali, Prakrit, and Sanskrit. These texts offer valuable insights into the Jain philosophy, beliefs, and practices. Here are some key points to keep in mind while studying Jain literature in Pali, Prakrit, and Sanskrit:

1. Pali Literature:
   - Pali is a language closely related to the Prakrit languages and is used extensively in Buddhist literature.
   - Jain texts written in Pali include the Agamas and the Tattvartha Sutra.
   - The Agamas are a collection of Jain scriptures that contain the teachings of the Jain Tirthankaras (spiritual leaders).
   - The Tattvartha Sutra is a key Jain text that provides a comprehensive overview of the Jain philosophy.

2. Prakrit Literature:
   - Prakrit is a group of languages that were widely used in ancient India.
   - Jain literature in Prakrit includes the canonical texts like the Jain Agamas, the commentaries, and the exegeses.
   - The Jain Agamas are considered the most authoritative texts in Jainism, and they contain the teachings of the Tirthankaras.
   - The commentaries and exegeses on the Agamas provide a detailed explanation of the Jain doctrines and practices.

3. Sanskrit Literature:
   - Sanskrit is an ancient language of India and is widely used in Hindu and Buddhist literature.
   - Jain literature in Sanskrit includes texts like the Puranas, the Upangas, and the Mulasutras.
   - The Puranas are Jain texts that provide a detailed account of the lives of the Tirthankaras and their teachings.
   - The Upangas are a group of texts that provide further elaboration on the Jain doctrines and practices.
   - The Mulasutras are considered the foundation texts of Jainism and provide a detailed explanation of the Jain philosophy.

Mnemonic: To remember the three languages used in Jain literature, one can use the acronym PPS, where P stands for Pali, P stands for Prakrit, and S stands for Sanskrit.

In conclusion, studying Jain literature in Pali, Prakrit, and Sanskrit is essential for understanding the Jain philosophy and its rich cultural heritage. By delving into these texts, one can gain valuable insights into the Jain principles and practices that have guided the lives of millions of people for centuries.
