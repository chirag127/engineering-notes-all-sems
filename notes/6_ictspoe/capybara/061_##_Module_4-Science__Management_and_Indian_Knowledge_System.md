## Module 4-Science, Management and Indian Knowledge System

This module focuses on the importance of science and management in the Indian knowledge system. The module covers the following topics:

1. Science in the Indian knowledge system: The module discusses the contributions of ancient Indian scientists and their impact on modern-day science. Students will learn about the contributions of Aryabhata, Varahamihira, and Bhaskaracharya to mathematics and astronomy. They will also learn about the ancient Indian practice of Ayurveda and its relevance in modern medicine.

2. Management in the Indian knowledge system: The module covers the concept of management in ancient India and how it has evolved over time. Students will learn about the principles of management as outlined in ancient Indian texts such as the Arthashastra and the Manusmriti. The module also covers the concept of leadership and the qualities required to be an effective leader.

3. Indian knowledge system in modern times: The module discusses the relevance of the Indian knowledge system in modern times. Students will learn about how the principles of management and science in ancient India can be applied in modern-day businesses and organizations. The module also covers the role of Indian knowledge systems in sustainable development.

Mnemonics and learning tricks:

1. Remember the contributions of ancient Indian scientists by using the mnemonic "AVB." This stands for Aryabhata, Varahamihira, and Bhaskaracharya.

2. To remember the principles of management outlined in the Arthashastra, use the acronym "PACE." This stands for Planning, Administration, Coordination, and Evaluation.

In conclusion, this module provides students with an understanding of the importance of science and management in the Indian knowledge system. By learning about the contributions of ancient Indian scientists and the principles of management outlined in ancient Indian texts, students can apply this knowledge in modern-day businesses and organizations.
