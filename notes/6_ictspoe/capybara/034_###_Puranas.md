### Puranas

Puranas are ancient Hindu texts that are considered to be a part of the Hindu canon. The word "Purana" means "old, ancient" in Sanskrit. There are 18 Puranas, which are divided into three categories - Sattva, Rajas, and Tamas. Here are some important points to remember about the Puranas:

1. The Puranas were written between the 3rd and 10th centuries CE, and were primarily composed in Sanskrit.

2. They contain stories and legends about the Hindu gods, goddesses, and saints, as well as information on various rituals and practices.

3. The 18 Puranas are classified into three categories based on their attributes. The Sattva Puranas include Vishnu Purana, Bhagavata Purana, and Garuda Purana. The Rajas Puranas include Brahma Purana, Padma Purana, and Vishnu Purana. The Tamas Puranas include Shiva Purana, Markandeya Purana, and Skanda Purana.

4. The Puranas are considered to be a significant part of Hindu theology and philosophy, and are often used as a source of reference by scholars and practitioners.

5. The Puranas also contain genealogies of kings and dynasties, as well as descriptions of geography, astronomy, and cosmology.

Mnemonic trick: A simple mnemonic trick to remember the names of the 18 Puranas is "Vayu Brought Harivamsha, Vishnu and Bhagavata; Brahma Presented Padma, Vaishnava and Markandeya; Shiva Gave Matsya, Kurma and Linga; Skanda Gave Agni, Bhavishya and Brahmavaivarta; and Finally, Narada Gave the Devi-Bhagavata."

7. The Puranas have had a significant impact on Indian culture and society, and have been the subject of numerous artistic and literary works over the centuries.

In conclusion, the Puranas are a fascinating and important part of Hindu mythology and philosophy, and provide valuable insights into the cultural and religious traditions of India.
