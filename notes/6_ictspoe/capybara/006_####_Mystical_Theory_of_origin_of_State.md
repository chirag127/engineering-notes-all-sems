#### Mystical Theory of origin of State

The Mystical Theory of origin of State is one of the oldest theories that explains the origin of the State. This theory is based on the belief that the State has a divine origin, and that it is created by a higher power.

Following are the key points to understand the Mystical Theory of origin of State:

- The Mystical Theory of origin of State believes that the State is not created by humans, but rather by a divine power or God.
- According to this theory, the ruler of the State is not a representative of the people, but rather a representative of God.
- The ruler of the State is seen as a divine figure who is chosen by God to lead the people.
- The people are expected to obey the ruler and follow his commands, as they are seen as the commands of God.
- This theory is based on the belief that the State is necessary for the well-being of the people, and that God has created the State for this purpose.
- This theory was widely accepted in ancient times, and many rulers used it to justify their authority and power.

Advantages of the Mystical Theory:

- This theory provides a strong justification for the authority of the ruler, as he is seen as a representative of God.
- This theory can provide a sense of unity and purpose among the people, as they are expected to follow the commands of the divine ruler.

Disadvantages of the Mystical Theory:

- This theory can be used by rulers to justify their oppressive and unjust actions, as they can claim that they are carrying out the will of God.
- This theory can also lead to a lack of accountability and transparency, as the ruler is seen as above the law.

Examples of the Mystical Theory:

- The Pharaohs of ancient Egypt used the Mystical Theory to justify their absolute authority and power.
- The Chinese Emperor was also seen as a divine figure who was chosen by God to rule over the people.

Applications of the Mystical Theory today:

- The Mystical Theory is not commonly accepted in modern times, as most modern societies have secular governments that are not based on religious beliefs.
