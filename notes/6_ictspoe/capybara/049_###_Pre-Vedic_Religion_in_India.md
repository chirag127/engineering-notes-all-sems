### Pre-Vedic Religion in India

Pre-Vedic religion in India refers to the religious practices and beliefs that existed in India before the arrival of the Vedic civilization. These religions were primarily animistic and worshiped natural phenomena such as trees, animals, and rivers. Here are some key points about Pre-Vedic religion in India:

1. Animism: Pre-Vedic religion in India was primarily animistic, meaning that they believed in the existence of spirits in trees, animals, and other natural phenomena.

2. Worship of Nature: Pre-Vedic religion worshipped natural phenomena such as trees, rivers, and animals. They believed that these natural elements had spiritual powers and could bring good fortune or misfortune.

3. Fire Worship: Fire was also an important element in Pre-Vedic religion. Fire was seen as a symbol of purity and was used in various rituals and ceremonies.

4. Sacrifice: Sacrifice was an important part of Pre-Vedic religion. Animals such as cows and horses were sacrificed to appease the gods and bring good fortune.

5. Lack of Caste System: Pre-Vedic religion did not have a caste system. People were free to practice their own religion and worship their own gods.
