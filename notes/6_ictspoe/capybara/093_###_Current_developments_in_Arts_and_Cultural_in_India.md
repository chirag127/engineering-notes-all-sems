### Current developments in Arts and Cultural in India

India has a rich cultural heritage that dates back several centuries. The country is known for its diversity, and this is reflected in its art and cultural traditions. India has always been a hub for art and culture and has contributed significantly to the global art scene. Here are some current developments in arts and culture in India:

1. Digitalization of Art
In recent years, there has been a significant shift towards digital art. Many artists are exploring digital mediums such as video, animation, and virtual reality to create new and innovative works of art. This has opened up new avenues for artists and has also made art more accessible to the public.

2. Revival of Traditional Art Forms
India has a rich tradition of art forms such as painting, sculpture, dance, and music. However, many of these art forms were on the verge of extinction. In recent years, there has been a renewed interest in these art forms, and efforts are being made to preserve and promote them. This has led to the revival of many traditional art forms.

3. International Collaborations
India has been collaborating with other countries to promote art and cultural exchange. This has led to the creation of new works of art that blend different cultural traditions. It has also provided opportunities for artists to showcase their work on a global platform.

4. Art Festivals
India is known for its vibrant and colorful festivals, and many of these festivals have a cultural component. In recent years, there has been an increase in the number of art festivals across the country. These festivals provide a platform for artists to showcase their work and interact with the public.

5. Street Art
Street art has become increasingly popular in India in recent years. Many cities across the country have seen an increase in the number of murals and graffiti. This has provided a platform for artists to express themselves and has also added to the vibrancy of the urban landscape.

Mnemonic: 
DRIAS - Digitalization, Revival, International collaborations, Art festivals, Street art.
