#### Theories of origin of State in India

There are various theories of origin of State in India. Some of the significant theories are as follows:

1. The Divine Theory: According to this theory, the State was established through the divine will of God. The king was considered as the representative of God on earth. The theory is also known as the ‘Right of Conquest’ theory.

2. The Social Contract Theory: This theory was proposed by Jean-Jacques Rousseau. According to this theory, the State was formed through a social contract between the people and the ruler. The people surrendered their rights to the ruler in exchange for protection and security.

3. The Evolutionary Theory: According to this theory, the State was not created in a single moment. It evolved over time. The family was the first unit of society. Later, families joined together to form clans, and clans joined together to form tribes. Finally, tribes united to form a State.

4. The Force Theory: According to this theory, the State emerged through the use of force. The stronger group conquered the weaker group and established a State.
