### Harappan Technologies in India and Ancient India

The Harappan civilization, also known as the Indus Valley civilization, was one of the earliest civilizations in the world, and it flourished in the northwestern part of the Indian subcontinent between 2600 BCE and 1900 BCE. The Harappan people were known for their advanced technology and sophisticated urban planning. Here are some of the significant technologies and advancements that were developed during the Harappan civilization:

1. Urban Planning: The Harappan civilization was the first to develop a sophisticated urban planning system. The cities were laid out in a grid pattern, with streets and buildings arranged in a systematic manner. The cities were also equipped with a drainage system, which was ahead of its time.

2. Agriculture: The Harappan civilization was one of the first to develop an irrigation system for agriculture. They used canals to divert water from rivers and distribute it to their fields. They also developed a variety of crops, including wheat, barley, and cotton.

3. Writing System: The Harappan civilization had a writing system that is still not fully deciphered. The script was written from right to left, and it is believed to have been used for administrative purposes.

4. Metallurgy: The Harappan people were skilled metallurgists and made use of copper, bronze, and gold. They also had a highly developed system of weights and measures, which suggests that they had a sophisticated trade network.

5. Pottery: The Harappan civilization was known for its pottery, which was made using a wheel. They produced a variety of pottery, including storage jars, cooking pots, and drinking vessels.

Apart from the Harappan civilization, ancient India also made significant contributions to technology and science. Here are some of the advancements that were developed during ancient India:

1. Mathematics: Ancient India made significant contributions to mathematics, including the development of the decimal system and the invention of zero. The decimal system is used worldwide today.

2. Astronomy: Ancient India was one of the first civilizations to develop a sophisticated system of astronomy. They used their knowledge to develop calendars and make accurate predictions about celestial events.

3. Medicine: Ancient India developed a sophisticated system of medicine, which was based on the principles of Ayurveda. Ayurveda is still practiced in India today and is known for its holistic approach to health and wellness.

4. Architecture: Ancient India was known for its impressive architecture, which included the construction of massive temples and palaces. The most famous example of ancient Indian architecture is the Taj Mahal.
