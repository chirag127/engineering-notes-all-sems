#### Contract Theory of origin of State

The contract theory of the origin of the state is a political theory that explains how the state came into being. According to this theory, the state is a result of a social contract between individuals in a society. This theory was first proposed by philosophers like John Locke, Thomas Hobbes, and Jean-Jacques Rousseau.

Here are some key points to understand the Contract Theory of origin of State:

1. The state is not a natural entity: The contract theory suggests that the state is not a natural entity but a result of a conscious agreement among individuals in a society. 

2. State is created for the common good: According to this theory, the state is created to promote the common good of individuals in a society. 

3. Individuals surrender some rights to the state: In order to promote the common good, individuals in a society surrender some of their natural rights to the state. 

4. The state has a limited role: The contract theory suggests that the state has a limited role in the lives of individuals in a society. Its primary role is to protect the rights of individuals and maintain social order. 

5. The consent of the governed: The contract theory suggests that the state can exist only if individuals in a society consent to be governed by it.
