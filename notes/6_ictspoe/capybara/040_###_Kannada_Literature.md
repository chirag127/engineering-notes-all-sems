### Kannada Literature

Kannada literature is one of the oldest and richest literatures in India. It is the literature of the Kannada-speaking people and is primarily written in the Kannada language. Kannada literature has a long and rich literary history, spanning over a thousand years, with numerous literary works in various genres.

#### History

The history of Kannada literature can be traced back to the 9th century CE. The earliest known literary work in Kannada is the Kavirajamarga, written by the Rashtrakuta king Amoghavarsha I in the 9th century CE. The Kavirajamarga is a treatise on rhetoric and poetics and is considered the earliest literary work in Kannada.

Kannada literature can be broadly divided into two periods: the ancient or classical period and the medieval or modern period. The ancient period, which lasted from the 9th to the 13th century, produced some of the finest literary works in Kannada, including the Vachana literature, the works of Pampa, Ranna, and Jainendra Kumar, and the works of the Hoysala poets.

The medieval period, which lasted from the 14th to the 19th century, saw a decline in the quality of Kannada literature. However, it also produced some important works, including the works of Sarvajna, the Haridasa literature, and the works of Kanakadasa.

#### Genres

Kannada literature has a rich tradition of various literary genres, including poetry, prose, drama, and folk literature. Some of the major genres in Kannada literature are:

- Vachana literature: This is a form of devotional poetry composed by the Shaiva saints in the 12th century CE. The Vachanas are written in a simple and direct style and deal with themes such as devotion, morality, and social reform.

- Epics: Kannada literature has several epics, including the Pampa Bharata, the Kumaravyasa Bharata, and the Harishchandra Kavya. These epics are written in a grand style and deal with mythological and historical themes.

- Yakshagana: Yakshagana is a form of folk theatre that originated in Karnataka. It combines dance, music, and drama and is usually based on mythological or historical stories.

- Novels: Kannada literature has a rich tradition of novels, starting from the early 20th century. Some of the major Kannada novelists are Kuvempu, Shivaram Karanth, and S.L. Bhyrappa.
