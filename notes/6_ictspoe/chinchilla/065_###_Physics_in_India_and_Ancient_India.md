### Physics in India and Ancient India

Physics, as a subject, has been studied and researched for centuries in India. Ancient Indian scientists and philosophers made significant contributions to the field of physics, which were based on observation and experimentation. Some of the major contributions to the field of physics made by ancient Indian scientists are as follows:

1. Law of Gravitation: The law of gravitation was discovered by the Indian scientist, Bhaskaracharya, in the 12th century. He proposed that objects are attracted to each other with a force that is proportional to their masses and inversely proportional to the distance between them.

2. Optics: Indian scientists made significant contributions to the field of optics. The Indian mathematician and astronomer, Aryabhata, discovered that light travels in straight lines. He also proposed that the speed of light is constant.

3. Mathematics: Mathematics was an important tool in the study of physics in ancient India. Indian mathematicians developed the concept of zero, which is now an integral part of the modern number system. They also developed the decimal system.

4. Astronomy: Astronomy was an important area of study in ancient India. Indian astronomers made significant contributions to the field of astronomy, including the discovery of the solar and lunar eclipses.

5. Medicine: Indian scientists also made significant contributions to the field of medicine. The Indian physician, Sushruta, developed surgical techniques that are still used today.
