### Sangama Literature in India

Sangama literature is a collection of poems and hymns composed in Kannada language during the 15th and 16th centuries in the Vijayanagara Empire. This literature is named after the Sangama dynasty of the Vijayanagara Empire, which ruled from 1336 to 1485.

Some of the important features of Sangama literature are:

1. Language: Sangama literature is composed in Kannada language, which was the official language of the Vijayanagara Empire.

2. Themes: The literature covers a wide range of themes, including devotion, love, war, and politics.

3. Style: The style of Sangama literature is characterized by its simplicity and clarity. The poems are easy to understand and are meant to be recited or sung.

4. Poetry: The literature is mainly composed of poetry, which is divided into two categories: shatpadi (six-lined verses) and tripadi (three-lined verses).

Some of the famous poets of Sangama literature are:

1. Kumara Vyasa: Kumara Vyasa was a prominent poet and scholar who is best known for his epic poem, the "Karnataka Bharata Kathamanjari." This poem is a retelling of the Mahabharata in Kannada language and is considered one of the greatest works of Kannada literature.

2. Timmanna Kavi: Timmanna Kavi was a court poet of the Vijayanagara Empire who is known for his work, the "Pampa Ramayana." This is a retelling of the Ramayana in Kannada language and is considered one of the most important works of Sangama literature.

3. Chamarasa: Chamarasa was a poet and philosopher who is known for his work, the "Prabhulingaleele." This is a collection of poems that deal with the philosophy of the Lingayat sect of Hinduism.
