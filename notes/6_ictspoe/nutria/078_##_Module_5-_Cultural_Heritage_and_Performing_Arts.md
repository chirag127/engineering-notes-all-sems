
## Module 5- Cultural Heritage and Performing Arts

- Cultural heritage and performing arts are the artistic expressions of a culture, passed down through generations. 
- They include traditional music, dance, literature, visual arts, and other forms of creative expression. 
- These expressions are important to a culture’s identity, and can help to preserve cultural knowledge and traditions. 
- They also provide a way for people to connect with their past, and to share their culture with others. 
- Cultural heritage and performing arts can also provide a source of income and employment for those involved in their production and performance.
- Cultural heritage and performing arts can take many forms, from traditional folk music and dance, to modern art forms such as hip hop and graffiti.
