
### Medicine in India and Ancient India

- Medicine in India has a long and storied history, with many of the earliest written records of medical practices coming from the ancient Indian subcontinent.
- Ancient Indian medicine was based on a holistic approach, with an emphasis on preventive care and the use of natural remedies.
- Ayurveda was the primary system of medicine in ancient India. It is a holistic system of healing that combines physical, mental, and spiritual components to treat illness and maintain health.
- Ayurvedic medicine was based on the belief that the body is made up of three humors: wind, bile, and phlegm. Each of these humors had to be kept in balance in order for a person to stay healthy.
- Herbal remedies and diet were used to restore the balance of the humors.
- Ayurveda also used massage, yoga, and meditation for healing.
- Charaka, a physician from ancient India, wrote the Charaka Samhita, which is one of the oldest and most influential texts on Ayurvedic medicine.
- Ancient Indian medicine also included surgery. Sushruta, another physician from ancient India, wrote the Sushruta Samhita, which is a detailed text on surgical techniques.
- Ancient Indian medicine also included the use of metals and minerals in the treatment of various illnesses.
- Indian medicine was also influenced by Buddhist and Jain philosophies, which emphasized non-violence and the use of natural remedies.
- While modern medicine has advanced significantly since the time of ancient India, many of the principles of Ayurveda are still practiced today.
