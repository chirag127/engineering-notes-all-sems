
### Six System Indian Philosophy
- The Six System Indian Philosophy is a set of schools of thought that originated in ancient India. It includes the Samkhya, Yoga, Nyaya, Vaisheshika, Mimamsa and Vedanta systems.
- Samkhya is a dualistic system that focuses on the separation of consciousness and matter. It emphasizes the importance of purusha (spirit) and prakriti (matter) in understanding the universe.
- Yoga is a system of spiritual practice that focuses on meditation, breath control, and physical postures. It is meant to bring the practitioner closer to a state of union with the divine.
- Nyaya is a school of thought that focuses on logical reasoning and the pursuit of truth. It is associated with the development of the Nyaya Sutras, a set of texts that outline the basic principles of logic.
- Vaisheshika is a system of thought that focuses on the nature of reality. It is based on the concept of atomism, which states that all things are composed of atoms.
- Mimamsa is a school of thought that focuses on the interpretation of the Vedas, the ancient scriptures of Hinduism. It is concerned with the ritual performance of Vedic sacrifices and the correct interpretation of Vedic texts.
- Vedanta is a system of thought that focuses on the nature of Brahman, the ultimate reality. It is based on the Upanishads, a set of texts that are part of the Vedas.
