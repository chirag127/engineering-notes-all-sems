
## Module 4-Science, Management and Indian Knowledge System

* Science: Science is the systematic study of the structure and behaviour of the physical and natural world through observation and experiment. It involves the study of different fields such as physics, chemistry, biology, astronomy, and earth science.

* Management: Management is the process of planning, organizing, leading, and controlling the activities of an organization to achieve its goals. It involves the use of various techniques and tools to ensure that the organization's objectives are met.

* Indian Knowledge System: Indian Knowledge System is a system of knowledge that has been developed over centuries and is based on the ancient Indian texts and philosophies. It includes various disciplines such as Vedic knowledge, Ayurveda, Yoga, Jyotish, and Vastu. It is a holistic system of knowledge that focuses on understanding the interconnectedness between the physical, mental, and spiritual aspects of life.

* Mnemonics and Learning Tricks: Mnemonics are techniques that help to improve memory by associating information with mental images, stories, or rhymes. They can be used to remember complex concepts, formulas, and facts. Learning tricks are methods that can be used to help students learn more efficiently. Examples of learning tricks include using diagrams and visual aids, breaking down complex topics into smaller chunks, and using mnemonics.
