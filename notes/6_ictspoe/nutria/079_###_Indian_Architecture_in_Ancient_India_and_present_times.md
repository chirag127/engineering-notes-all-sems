
### Indian Architecture in Ancient India and Present Times

1. Ancient Indian architecture is characterized by its advanced engineering and construction techniques, as well as its unique style of art and design.
2. Ancient Indian architecture includes the iconic Taj Mahal, the Ellora Caves, and the Ajanta Caves.
3. The most prominent feature of ancient Indian architecture is the use of stone and brick to create structures. This was done using a technique known as "vaastu shastra," which was based on the principles of balance and harmony.
4. Ancient Indian architecture also featured intricate carvings and sculptures, which were used to decorate the buildings.
5. In present-day India, the use of modern materials such as steel, concrete, and glass has become increasingly popular. However, traditional techniques such as vaastu shastra are still used in many parts of the country.
6. Modern Indian architecture is characterized by its use of bold colors, geometric shapes, and modern materials.
7. One of the most iconic examples of modern Indian architecture is the Lotus Temple in New Delhi. This unique structure was designed by the famous architect, Fariburz Sahba.
8. Other examples of modern Indian architecture include the Baha’i House of Worship in New Delhi and the Jawahar Kala Kendra in Jaipur.
9. Indian architecture has been influenced by many different cultures, including the Mughals, the British, and the Portuguese.
10. Mnemonic for Indian Architecture: TEEAJ (Taj Mahal, Ellora Caves, Ajanta Caves, Modern Indian Architecture, Lotus Temple, Baha’i House of Worship, Jawahar Kala Kendra).
