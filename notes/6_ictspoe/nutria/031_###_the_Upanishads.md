
### The Upanishads
The Upanishads are a collection of ancient Indian texts, composed between 800 BCE and 200 CE. They form the core of the Hindu philosophical tradition and are known for their profound spiritual teachings.

The Upanishads are composed of conversations between sages and their students, and are divided into two categories: those that discuss the nature of Brahman (the ultimate reality), and those that discuss the nature of Atman (the individual soul).

The Upanishads are traditionally studied in order to gain insight into the nature of reality and the human condition. They provide a framework for understanding the relationship between the material and spiritual realms, and how one can live a life of purpose and fulfillment.
