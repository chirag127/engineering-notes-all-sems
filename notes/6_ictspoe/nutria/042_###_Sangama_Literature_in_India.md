
### Sangama Literature in India

Sangama literature is a term used to refer to the works of literature written in India between the 11th and 14th centuries. It is considered to be the period of transition between the classical and modern literature of India. The literature of this period was composed in Sanskrit and Prakrit, and was heavily influenced by the religious, social and political changes of the times.

The Sangama period saw the emergence of several important literary works, including the Ramayana and Mahabharata, two of the most important Hindu epics. Other works include the plays of Bhasa, the poetry of Kalidasa, the works of Bhartrihari and the devotional literature of the Alvars and the Nayanars.

The Sangama period also saw the emergence of several important philosophical works, such as the Vedanta Sutras, the Upanishads, the Bhagavad Gita and the Brahma Sutras. These works provided the basis for later philosophical and religious movements, such as Advaita Vedanta and Shaivism.
