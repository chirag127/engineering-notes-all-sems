
### Puranas

* The Puranas are a body of ancient Hindu texts that are traditionally attributed to the sage Vyasa. 
* They are among the oldest and most important religious texts of Hinduism, and are believed to have been written between 400 BCE and 300 CE. 
* The Puranas contain stories, myths and legends about the gods, goddesses and heroes of Hinduism, as well as instructions on rituals, ethics and philosophy. 
* They also provide information on the history and geography of India, and are believed to have been composed by various sages over the centuries. 
* The Puranas are divided into five categories, known as the Mahapuranas, the Upapuranas, the Sthala Puranas, the Brahmanas and the Bhagavata Purana. 
* Each of these categories contains several individual texts, all of which are traditionally attributed to Vyasa. 
* The Puranas are generally divided into two sections: the Purva-Khanda, which deals with creation and the gods, and the Uttara-Khanda, which deals with the lives of the gods and their descendants. 
* The Puranas are an important source of information on the history and culture of ancient India, and have been widely studied by scholars. 
* They are also an important source of inspiration for many Hindu devotional practices and rituals. 
* A common mnemonic device to remember the five categories of the Puranas is “MUSPB”, which stands for Mahapuranas, Upapuranas, Sthala Puranas, Brahmanas, and Bhagavata Purana. 
* Another mnemonic device to remember the five categories of the Puranas is “MAUBS”, which stands for Mahapuranas, Upapuranas, Agamas, Brahmanas, and Sthala Puranas.
