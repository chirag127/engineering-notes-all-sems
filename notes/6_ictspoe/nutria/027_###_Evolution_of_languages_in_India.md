
### Evolution of languages in India

- India is home to a large number of languages, with more than 1,500 languages and dialects spoken in the country. 
- The history of language evolution in India can be traced back to the ancient Indus Valley civilization, which used a form of proto-Dravidian language. 
- Sanskrit is the oldest known language in India, and one of the oldest in the world. It was the language of the Vedic period, and is still used in religious ceremonies and literature today. 
- Over time, the language evolved and developed into a variety of different languages, including Hindi, Urdu, Gujarati, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Marathi and Malayalam. 
- The English language was introduced to India in the 18th century during the British colonial period. 
- Today, English is one of the official languages of India, and is widely used in business, education and government.
- In addition to the languages mentioned above, there are also several minority languages spoken in India, such as Punjabi, Sindhi, Nepali and Assamese.
- To help remember the languages, a mnemonic device could be used, such as: "HUG BTMKM - Hindi, Urdu, Gujarati, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Marathi, Malayalam".
