

### Chemistry in India and Ancient India

* Indian contributions to the field of chemistry can be traced back to ancient times. The Vedas, the Upanishads, and the Arthashastra all mention the use of various chemical processes.
* In the medieval period, Indian chemists made significant contributions to the field of alchemy, particularly in the development of mercury-based medicines.
* During the Mughal period, Indian chemists developed a method of distillation known as the “mild” process, which was used to produce essential oils and perfumes.
* In the 18th century, Indian scientists began to make advances in the study of organic chemistry, particularly in the area of dyeing.
* In modern times, Indian chemists have made significant contributions to the fields of inorganic, organic, and physical chemistry.
* Indian chemists have developed a variety of new compounds, such as polymers, dyes, and catalysts, which have been used in a range of industries, including pharmaceuticals, food processing, and electronics.
* Indian chemists have also made important contributions to the field of nanotechnology, particularly in the development of nanomaterials.
* In addition, Indian chemists have been at the forefront of developing green chemistry, which seeks to reduce the environmental impact of chemical processes.
