
#### Marriage in Society in Ancient India

Marriage in ancient India was a complex social system that included various rules and regulations. It was a sacred institution, and was seen as a way to ensure the continuation of the family line. Marriage was also seen as a way to strengthen the bond between families, and to create alliances.

Marriage was an important part of ancient Indian society, and was seen as a way to ensure the continuation of the family line. Marriage was also seen as a way to strengthen the bond between families, and to create alliances.

In ancient India, marriages were arranged by the families of the bride and groom. The families would consult with astrologers to determine the best time for the wedding. Once the date was set, the bride and groom would exchange garlands of flowers, signifying the union of two families.

In ancient India, marriages were usually monogamous. Divorce was not common, but it was allowed in certain cases. The bride's family would usually provide a dowry to the groom's family, which was seen as a sign of respect and gratitude.
