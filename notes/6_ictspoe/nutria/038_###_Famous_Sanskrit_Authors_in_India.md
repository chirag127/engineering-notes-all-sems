
### Famous Sanskrit Authors in India

* Kalidasa: Kalidasa is one of the most renowned Sanskrit poets and dramatists. He is believed to have lived in the 4th or 5th century CE and is considered to be the greatest poet and dramatist in Sanskrit literature. He is known for his plays such as Abhijnana Shakuntala and Vikramorvashiya and for his epic poem, the Raghuvamsa.

* Valmiki: Valmiki is the author of the great Hindu epic, the Ramayana. He is believed to have lived in the 8th century BCE and is revered as the first poet of Sanskrit literature. He is credited with inventing the shloka, a type of poetic metre used in Sanskrit literature.

* Vyasa: Vyasa is the author of the Mahabharata, the great Hindu epic. He is believed to have lived in the 4th century BCE and is considered to be one of the seven great sages or Rishis in Hinduism. He is also credited with compiling the Vedas and the Upanishads.

* Bhasa: Bhasa is a Sanskrit playwright who is believed to have lived in the 2nd century BCE. He is credited with writing thirteen plays, including Pratima Nataka, Abhiseka Nataka, Dutavakya and Madhyamavyayoga.

* Bhartrihari: Bhartrihari is a Sanskrit poet who is believed to have lived in the 7th century CE. He is credited with writing three great works of Sanskrit literature: the Satakas, the Vairagya Satakas and the Shringara Satakas.

* Ksemendra: Ksemendra is a Sanskrit poet and dramatist who is believed to have lived in the 11th century CE. He is credited with writing several plays, including the Avimaraka and the Kalavikagnimitra. He is also known for his poetic works such as the Subhashita Ratna Malika and the Kavya Mala.

* Magha: Magha is a Sanskrit poet and dramatist who is believed to have lived in the 7th century CE. He is credited with writing the great epic, the Shishupala Vadha. He is also known for his plays, such as the Pratima Nataka and the Karnabhara.

* Bana: Bana is a Sanskrit poet and dramatist who is believed to have lived in the 7th century CE. He is credited with writing several plays, including the Kadambari and the Harshacharita. He is also known for his poetic works, such as the Harivamsa and the Kavyadarsha.

* Dandin: Dandin is a Sanskrit poet and dramatist who is believed to have lived in the 7th century CE. He is credited with writing several plays, including the Dashakumaracharita and the Kavyadarsha. He is also known for his poetic works, such as the Dasakumaracharita and the Kavyalankara.
