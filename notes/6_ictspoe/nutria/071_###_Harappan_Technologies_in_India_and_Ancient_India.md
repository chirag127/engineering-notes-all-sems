
### Harappan Technologies in India and Ancient India

* The Harappan civilization of India is one of the oldest and most advanced civilizations in the world. It flourished in the Indus Valley from around 3300 BCE to 1500 BCE.

* Harappans had a sophisticated system of town planning, which included roads, drainage systems, and well-planned cities. They also had a system of weights and measures, and used a script to communicate.

* Harappans were skilled in metalworking and used copper and bronze to make tools, weapons, and jewelry. They also crafted seals and jewelry out of precious stones like lapis lazuli and turquoise.

* Harappans were also skilled in pottery, and they used a variety of techniques to create beautiful pieces. They also created terracotta figurines, which are now considered to be some of the earliest examples of sculpture in India.

* Harappans were also skilled in agriculture, and they developed a system of irrigation that allowed them to grow a variety of crops. They also domesticated animals and used them for transport and food.

* Harappans were also skilled in trade and commerce, and they traded with Mesopotamia, Egypt, and the Persian Gulf. They used a system of barter and also used a variety of coins.

* Harappans were also skilled in mathematics and used a decimal system to calculate weights and measures. They also used a form of geometry to construct buildings and other structures.

* Harappans also used a variety of religious practices, including worship of the Mother Goddess, and some scholars believe that the earliest forms of Hinduism and Buddhism may have originated in the Harappan civilization.
