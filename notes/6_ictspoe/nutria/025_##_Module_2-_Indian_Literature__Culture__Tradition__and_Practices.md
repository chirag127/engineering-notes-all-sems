
## Module 2- Indian Literature, Culture, Tradition, and Practices

* Indian literature can be broadly divided into two main categories: classical and modern. Classical literature includes the works of ancient authors such as the Vedas, the Ramayana, and the Mahabharata. Modern literature includes works of the 20th and 21st centuries, such as the works of Rabindranath Tagore, Mahasweta Devi, and Arundhati Roy.
* Indian culture is a blend of diverse religions, languages, customs, and traditions. It is a rich and vibrant culture that has been shaped by its long history of invasions, migrations, and settlements. Indian culture is characterized by its strong family values, its respect for elders, and its emphasis on hospitality and generosity.
* Indian traditions are based on the teachings of the Vedas, the ancient Hindu scriptures. These traditions include the practice of yoga, meditation, and Ayurveda. They also include the practice of Hinduism, Buddhism, and Jainism.
* Indian practices are based on the belief that the universe is composed of five elements: earth, water, fire, air, and ether. These five elements are believed to interact with each other in a harmonious way. The practice of yoga, meditation, and Ayurveda are all based on this belief.
* Mnemonic: ILCTP (Indian Literature, Culture, Tradition, Practices)
