

#### Contract Theory of origin of State

- Contract Theory of origin of State is a theory which states that the origin of the state is a result of a contract between the citizens and the government. 
- This contract is made voluntarily and is based on mutual trust and agreement between both the parties. 
- The main idea behind this theory is that the state has the authority to make laws and enforce them only because people have consented to it. 
- The contract theory is based on the idea of social contract which was first proposed by the philosopher Thomas Hobbes. 
- According to this theory, the citizens give up some of their rights and freedoms in exchange for the protection and security provided by the state. 
- The contract theory is further divided into two types, namely the tacit contract theory and the explicit contract theory. 
- The tacit contract theory states that the citizens are assumed to have agreed to the terms of the state without any explicit agreement. 
- On the other hand, the explicit contract theory states that the citizens have agreed to the terms of the state through some form of explicit agreement. 
- The contract theory of origin of state is an important concept in political philosophy as it helps to explain the origin and purpose of the state. 
- It also helps to explain the relationship between the citizens and the state, and how the state is able to maintain its authority and power. 
- Mnemonic: COOS - Contract Origin of State.
