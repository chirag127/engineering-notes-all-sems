
### Northern Indian Languages

* Northern Indian languages are a family of languages spoken primarily in the northern part of India, including Hindi, Punjabi, Urdu, and Sanskrit.
* Hindi is the most widely spoken language in this family, with over 500 million speakers. It is the official language of India and is one of the 22 scheduled languages of the Indian Constitution.
* Punjabi is the second most widely spoken language in the family, with over 100 million speakers. It is the official language of the Indian state of Punjab and is one of the 22 scheduled languages of the Indian Constitution.
* Urdu is the third most widely spoken language in this family, with over 50 million speakers. It is the official language of Pakistan and is one of the 22 scheduled languages of the Indian Constitution.
* Sanskrit is the fourth most widely spoken language in this family, with over 30 million speakers. It is one of the oldest languages in the world and is the liturgical language of Hinduism and Buddhism.
