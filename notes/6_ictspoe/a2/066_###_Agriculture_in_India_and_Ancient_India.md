### Agriculture in India and Ancient India

- Agriculture has been practiced in India since ancient times. Some important points about agriculture in ancient India are:
- The Indus Valley civilization was an agrarian society and grew crops like wheat, barley, lentils, etc. using irrigation.
- The Vedic period saw expansion of agriculture and introduction of iron ploughs and axes. Crops like rice, millet, wheat, lentils, pulses, etc. were cultivated.
- The Mauryan empire under Ashoka further promoted irrigation, deforestation and production of crops like sugarcane.
- The Gupta empire emphasized on agricultural production and gave tax concessions to farmers. Canals were built and wells were dug for irrigation. Crops like wheat, rice, millets, barley, pulses, oilseeds, sugarcane, etc. were grown.
- Advantages: Increased food production and surplus for trade; Stable economic base; Growth of civilization and urbanization.
- Mnemonics: Think of the crops as 'VIBGYOR' - Wheat, Rice, Millets, Barley, Gram, Peas - to remember the variety of crops grown.
- The farming techniques and crops grown in ancient India are still prevalent today with some modifications. Thus, it forms an important part of Indian history and culture.
