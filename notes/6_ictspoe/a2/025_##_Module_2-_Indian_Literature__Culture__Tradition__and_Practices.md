## Module 2- Indian Literature, Culture, Tradition, and Practices

- Indian literature includes some of the oldest and longest epics in the world like Ramayana and Mahabharata which portray the Indian culture, values, and traditions.
- Sanskrit literature is considered as one of the richest literatures in the world. Kalidasa's works like Abhijnanashakuntalam and Kumarasambhavam are considered as gems of Sanskrit poetry.
- Tamil literature is one of the oldest literatures in India. Thirukkural by Thiruvalluvar is one of the most important works in Tamil literature which deals with ethics and morality.
- Folk literature includes ballads, folk tales, proverbs, and riddles which were transmitted orally. They reflect the simple way of life of common people.
- Indian traditional art forms include classical dances like Bharatanatyam, Kathakali, Odissi, Manipuri, etc and classical music like Carnatic and Hindustani music which are based on intricate rhythmic patterns and melodies.
- Indian handicrafts and paintings exhibit a variety of styles, forms, and themes across different regions reflecting the cultural diversity of India. For ex: Madhubani paintings, Warli paintings, Tanjore paintings, etc.
- Traditional festivals in India like Diwali, Holi, Onam, Pongal, etc are celebrated to express joy and are an important way of preserving Indian culture and bringing people together. They are associated with mythological stories and agriculture harvest.
- Traditional Indian dress and ornaments for women like saree, churidar, bindi, bangles, mehendi, etc are deeply embedded in Indian culture. Men traditionally wear dhoti, kurta, turban, etc depending on the region.
- Traditional Indian cuisine uses a variety of spices and is quite popular across the world due to its unique taste and aroma. Some popular dishes are curries, biryanis, kebabs, naan, idli, dosa, etc.
- Yoga and meditation are ancient Indian practices that promote both physical and mental well-being with the help of breathing techniques and bodily postures. They are popular all over the world due to their therapeutic benefits.
