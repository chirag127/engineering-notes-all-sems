### Famous Sanskrit Authors in India

Some of the most famous Sanskrit authors in India and their major works are:

- Kalidasa: He is considered as one of the greatest Sanskrit poets. His most famous works are Abhijnanashakuntala, Kumarasambhava, Meghaduta, and Raghuvamsha.
- Bharavi: He was a renowned Sanskrit poet of the 7th century. His most famous work is Kiratarjuniya which tells the story of Arjuna's battle with Shiva.
- Magha: He was one of the most celebrated Sanskrit poets of the early 7th century. His most famous epic poem is Sisupalavadha which describes the story of Krishna slaying Sisupala.
- Bhartrihari: He was a renowned Sanskrit poet of the 6th century. His Neeti Shatakam, Shringara Shatakam, and Vairagya Shatakam are among the most famous works in Sanskrit. They contain insightful verses on wisdom, love, and renunciation respectively.

Some mnemonics and learning tricks for these famous Sanskrit authors are:

- Kalidasa: K (first letter of Kalidasa) stands for King as Kalidasa was the court poet of King Vikramaditya.
- Bharavi: Think of Bharat (India) and Vi (as in Victory) to remember Bharavi. His work Kiratarjuniya tells the story of Arjuna's victory.
- Magha: The name Magha (meaning month in Sanskrit) indicates the early 7th century as his period. His Sisupalavadha describes Krishna's victory.
- Bhartrihari: 'Bhartri' sounds similar to 'Bharti' meaning 'intellect' and 'Hari' referring to Lord Vishnu. His works contain intellectual verses including those on renunciation (Vishnu being a renunciate).

The above points and mnemonics can help in easily remembering the famous Sanskrit authors and their major works.
