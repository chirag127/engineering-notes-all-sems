### Painting as Cultural Heritage of India

Indian painting has a long tradition and history in Indian art. It dates back to pre-historic times, where paintings have been discovered on the ancient cave walls.

- Cave paintings: The cave paintings at Bhimbetka rock shelters in Madhya Pradesh are estimated to be around 30,000 years old. These paintings depict scenes from the lives of the people who lived during that age, such as hunting, dancing, and animals.
- Miniature paintings: Miniature paintings flourished during the Mughal era. These highly intricate and colourful paintings were made using grains of precious stones and gold or silver leaf. Mughal miniatures depicted court scenes, grand events, and other aristocratic deeds. Tanjore paintings from Tamil Nadu and Pichhwai paintings from Rajasthan are also examples of famous miniature paintings.
- Madhubani paintings: Madhubani paintings are a style of folk paintings done by the women of Madhubani district in Bihar. These paintings are made using natural dyes and pigments, and they depict Hindu mythological themes or scenes from nature.
- Kerala murals: Kerala murals are a distinguishing feature of the traditional art form of Kerala. These frescoes adorn the walls of Kerala temples and palaces. They often depict stories from Indian epics and mythologies.

Some key characteristics of Indian paintings include:

- Use of natural colours from vegetables, minerals, indigo, etc.
- Religious and mythological themes depicting Indian culture and beliefs.
- An emphasis on detail and intricate patterns.
- The paintings have a flat two-dimensional style without shadows.
- Symbology, such as certain colours representing specific moods or symbols. For example, blue depicts Krishna as it was his favourite colour.

The rich painting tradition of India has endured for centuries and continues to thrive and evolve today. It represents the cultural heritage of India with deep historical roots as well as influences from different periods. Studying Indian paintings provides insights into Indian history, culture, and traditions.
