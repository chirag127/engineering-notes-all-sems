### Martial Arts Traditions of India

India has a long history of martial arts traditions. Some of the major martial art forms that originated in India are:

1. Kalaripayattu - Originated in Kerala. Focuses on agility, coordination and application of pressure points. Uses weapons like spear, sword, shield, sticks.
Mnemonic: Kalaripayattu - Kerala's ancient martial art using spears and swords

2. Silambam - Originated in Tamil Nadu. Uses a long bamboo staff known as silambam. Focuses on quick movements and agility.
Mnemonic: Silambam - Tamil Nadu's stick martial art

3. Gatka - Originated in Punjab. Uses wooden sticks, daggers and swords. Focuses on sparring and simulating battlefield techniques. Associated with the Sikh martial tradition.
Mnemonic: Gatka - Sikh stick and sword fighting martial art from Punjab

4. Thang-Ta - Originated in Manipur. Uses swords, spears and sticks. Includes both armed and unarmed techniques. Focuses on fluidity of movements and speed.
Mnemonic: Thang-Ta - Manipur's flexible martial art using swords and spears

Advantages of Indian martial arts:
- Focus on agility, speed, coordination and technique instead of sheer strength
- Use of weapons and unarmed techniques
- Include meditative practices to cultivate awareness and control

Applications:
- Self defense
- Physical fitness
- Mental discipline
