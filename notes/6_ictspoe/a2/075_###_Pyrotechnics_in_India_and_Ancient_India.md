### Pyrotechnics in India and Ancient India

- Pyrotechnics or the art of creating fireworks and other applications using combustible materials dates back to ancient India. The knowledge of gunpowder and its applications in pyrotechnics and fireworks originated in ancient India.
- The earliest references to the use of pyrotechnics are found in the Vedas. The use of firecrackers during religious rituals and celebrations was prevalent. The knowledge was later transmitted to China and other parts of the world.
- The Rasaratna Samuchchaya, an important alchemical treatise by Virasena, describes the preparation of gunpowder and its use in pyrotechnics. It includes recipes for making light emitting fireworks, golden rain, sparks, crackers etc. It describes the proportions of charcoal, sulphur and nitre to be used.
- The Agni Purana too describes the ingredients and methods of preparation of various pyrotechnics including rockets, light balls and others. The Manasollasa, an encyclopedia from the 12th century also contains descriptions of recipes and instructions for creating fireworks.
- Pyrotechnics was widely used in festivals, weddings and other celebrations in ancient India. The use of fireworks was an integral part of many religious rituals as well as royal ceremonies and processions to create a spectacle and celebrate special occasions.
- The knowledge of pyrotechnics spread from India to the Middle East and eventually to Europe. The variety of pyrotechnics and fireworks in ancient India was unmatched and formed the basis for further advancements in pyrotechnics around the world.
