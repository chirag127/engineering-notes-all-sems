### Physics in India and Ancient India

- Ancient Indian mathematics and physics contributed to concepts like infinity and zero, floating point numbers, geometry theorems and trigonometry, and ideas resembling modern calculus.
- The Indian astronomer Aryabhata formulated ideas for the rotation and revolution of the Earth and the elliptical orbits of planet, and described gravitation 1,000 years before Newton.
- Indian philosophers theorized about the atom from the 6th century BCE. The Nyaya and Vaisheshika schools developed probabilistic theories of atomism.
- The 1st century CE text, Yoga Sutras, described principles of physics including topics like inertia, transformation of energy, and different types of motions.
- In medieval India, rudimentary forms of trigonometry were developed. Bhaskara, Brahmagupta, and others described the use of trigonometry in calculations for astronomy.
- The Kerala School of Astronomy and Mathematics proposed a heliocentric model of gravitation in the 16th century.
- Key learning tricks: Remember Aryabhata for rotation and revolution of Earth, and elliptical orbits; Nyaya and Vaisheshika schools for early Indian atomism theories; Yoga Sutras for principles of physics like inertia and energy transformation; Medieval Indian scholars for early trigonometry in astronomy calculations.
