### Malayalam Literature

- Malayalam literature refers to literature in the Malayalam language from the region of Kerala in South India. Malayalam literature begins from the 9th century CE with the Kulashekhara Alwar.

- The earliest extant literary works in Malayalam are the Vazhappalli Inscriptions (ca. 830 CE), Odia Inscription (850 CE) and Tharisapally Copper Plate (10th century). The oldest literary work in Malayalam (around the 12th century) is the Ramacharitam, an epic poem written by Cheeraman in the form of Kathaprasangam.

- Major Medieval period poets and literary works in Malayalam:
- Rama Panikkar - adapted parts of Ramayana into Malayalam poetry in the 14th century.
- Rama Variayar - wrote Vadakkan Pattukal, tales of Northern warriors in the 15th century. 
- Neelalohitam and Charitaratnadarpana - two of the earliest Sanskrit works on Malabar by Uddanda Varman and Madhava Panikkar in the 16th century.

- Major authors and literary works from 1800-1947:
- Kumaran Asan - significant contributions to Malayalam poetry and Vedanta. Famous works: Veena Poovu, Duravastha.
- Ulloor S. Parameswara Iyer - initiated modernism in Malayalam poetry. Famous works: Poomukhappadiyile Mana, Sahityamanjari.
- Thoppil Bhasi - pioneered modernism in Malayalam prose. Famous works: Muthassi, Kootukar.
- Vaikom Muhammad Basheer - most influential Malayalam fiction writer. Famous works: Balyakalasakhi, Pathummayude Manam.
