### The Ramayana

The Ramayana is an ancient Indian epic poem which narrates the struggle of the divine prince Rama to rescue his wife Sita from the demon king Ravana. It is one of the two major Sanskrit epics of ancient India, the other being the Mahabharata.

* The Ramayana consists of 24,000 verses in seven books (kandas) and 500 cantos (sargas).
* It is attributed to the Hindu sage Valmiki and dated to around 500 BCE to 100 BCE.
* The Ramayana is one of the largest ancient epics in world literature. It has had a profound impact on art and culture in South and Southeast Asia.

**Main Characters:**

* Rama: The protagonist of the epic, the seventh avatar of the god Vishnu.
* Sita: The wife of Rama and the daughter of Janaka. She is abducted by Ravana.
* Ravana: The demon king of Lanka who abducts Sita.
* Hanuman: The monkey king and devoted follower of Rama who helps find Sita.

**Key Events:**

1. King Dasharatha of Ayodhya has three wives and four sons: Rama, Bharata, Lakshmana and Shatrughna.
2. Rama is destined to become king, but his stepmother Kaikeyi exiles him to the forest for 14 years. Sita and Lakshmana accompany him.
3. The demon king Ravana of Lanka abducts Sita. Rama meets the monkey king Sugreeva who helps him.
4. Hanuman locates Sita in Lanka and gives Rama's message to her. Rama allies with Sugreeva and builds a monkey army.
5. After a lengthy battle, Rama defeats and kills Ravana. He rescues Sita and returns to Ayodhya where he is crowned king.

**Mnemonics and Learning Tricks:**

* Rama, Sita, Lakshmana, Hanuman can be remembered as "Rama's sila hum" which means "Rama's stone pillar/rock".
* The 7 kandas can be remembered as "Pa-V-Ya-K-La-Ar-U" (Balakanda, Ayodhya Kanda, Aranya Kanda, Kishkindha Kanda, Sundara Kanda, Yuddha Kanda, Uttara Kanda).
