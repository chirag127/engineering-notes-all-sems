### Northern Indian languages

The major languages spoken in Northern India are:

- Hindi: Officially the main language of the Indian government and the most widely spoken. Uses Devanagari script.
Mnemonic: "Himalayas are In India, Naturally Divine" (first letter of each word forms Hindi)

- Punjabi: Spoken in Punjab state, uses Gurmukhi script.
Mnemonic: "Punjabis Generally Use Good Milk Products" (first letter of each word forms Punjabi)

- Kashmiri: Spoken mainly in Jammu and Kashmir, uses Devanagari script.
- Nepali: Spoken in neighboring Nepal, as well as parts of Northern India near Nepal border, uses Devanagari script.

Advantages of learning Northern Indian languages:
- Understand Indian culture, poetry, literature, and films better
- Easier to travel and communicate in Northern India
- Career opportunities in government, translation, interpreting, and teaching

Disadvantages:
- Languages are complex with different scripts, expressions, and levels of formality
- Not widely spoken outside of India, so less useful for international communication

Applications:
- Government work in Northern India
- Study of Indian history, culture, and arts
- Translation and interpreting
- Teaching Indian languages
