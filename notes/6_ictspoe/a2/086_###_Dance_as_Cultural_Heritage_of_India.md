### Dance as Cultural Heritage of India

Dance is an integral part of Indian culture and heritage. Indian classical dance forms represent a rich cultural heritage and are a unique manifestation of the country's artistic traditions. Some of the major classical dance forms of India are:

1. Bharatanatyam: Originated in Tamil Nadu, Bharatanatyam is a traditional dance form characterized by the postures, purity of form, precision of movement and expression of spiritual ideas. It represents the culmination of the ancient Sanskrit text 'Natya Shastra'. Key aspects are nritta (pure dance), nritya (solo expressive dance) and natya (dramatic dance).

2. Kathak: Kathak is one of the major forms of Indian classical dance. It originated in Northern India, and evolved from a story telling tradition in which bards called 'kathakas' vocalized narrative poetry to musical accompaniment in temples. Today, Kathak includes elements such as intricate rhythmic footwork, pirouettes, and expressive hand gestures. Key features are chakkars (rapid spins), tatkar (percussive footwork), and laykari (rhythmic footwork).

3. Kuchipudi: Kuchipudi is another classical Indian dance form having its origin in the state of Andhra Pradesh. It involves a lot of complex rhythmic patterns and quick rhythmic feet movements. The dance is accompanied by Carnatic music and the dancer has to be adept in both nritta (pure dance) as well as natya (dramatic art). Kuchipudi also uses speech and song for dialogues as part of its repertoire.
