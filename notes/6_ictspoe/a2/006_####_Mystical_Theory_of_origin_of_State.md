#### Mystical Theory of origin of State

- According to this theory, state originated from divine or supernatural sanction. The proponents of this theory believed that state was created by God or some superhuman power to maintain law and order and realize an eternal divine purpose.
- Examples: Divine right theory of monarchy which emphasizes that the authority of the monarch is derived from God. The religious sanctity accorded to the institution of kingship in ancient societies like Egypt, China, etc.
- Mnemonics: King rules by Gods wish (to remember divine right theory of origin of state)
- Advantages: This theory strengthens the authority of the state as people believe that disobedience to the state means disobedience to God. It helps control people through religious faith and fear.
- Disadvantages: This theory is not based on reason or evidence. It relies on faith and religious beliefs. In modern times, this theory has been discarded as it rules out popular sovereignty and limits scope for democracy and accountable governance.
- Applications: This theory was used in ancient and medieval societies to reinforce the divine and unquestionable authority of monarchs. In modern times, this theory has no takers and is just of historical interest.
