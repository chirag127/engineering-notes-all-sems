### Indian’s Cultural Contribution to the World

- Zero: Indians invented the concept of zero and the decimal system. The decimal number system makes counting and arithmetic calculations easier. The invention of zero was a major breakthrough in mathematics.
- Chess: The popular game of chess originated in India in the 6th century CE. Originally called 'Chaturanga', the Sanskrit name, it included dice and moved to Persia where it became 'Shatranj' and then to Europe. The current rules were established in Spain and Italy in the 15th century.
- Yoga: Yoga incorporates physical, mental, and spiritual practices and originated in ancient India. There are references to yoga in Indian scriptures like the Bhagavad Gita and the Yoga Sutras of Patanjali. Yoga gained popularity in the West starting in the 20th century. Regular yoga practice has many health benefits including increased flexibility and strength.
- Ayurveda: Ayurveda is a system of traditional medicine that originated in India. It aims to preserve health and wellness using a holistic approach, and it uses various herbs and mineral or metal substances. Ayurveda considers the body, mind, and spirit in the diagnosis and treatment of an individual. It is one of the world's oldest and most comprehensive systems of medicine.
- Fibonacci Numbers: The Fibonacci sequence was first described by Virasena in the 8th century, earlier than Leonardo Fibonacci's description in the 13th century. The sequence appears often in Indian mathematics in connection with Sanskrit poetry and prosody. The Fibonacci sequence has many applications in mathematics, art, design, and computer algorithms.
