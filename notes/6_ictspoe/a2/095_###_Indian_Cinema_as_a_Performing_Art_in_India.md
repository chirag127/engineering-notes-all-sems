### Indian Cinema as a Performing Art in India

Indian cinema, especially Bollywood films, are a major part of Indian culture and arts. Indian films are known for their musical song and dance numbers, as well as their melodramatic storylines. Indian cinema has a wide following not just in India, but across the world. Some key points about Indian cinema as a performing art:

- Acting styles in Indian cinema are often dramatic and theatrical, with exaggerated emotions and expressions. Dancing and singing are interwoven into the films, with songs often picturized as elaborate musical numbers. The dances are choreographed featuring many dancers and are integral to Indian films.
- The music of Indian films is very popular, with songs and instrumental pieces an important component. Composers like A.R. Rahman have produced very popular and melodic film soundtracks. The playback singers who dub for the actors are also celebrities, like Lata Mangeshkar.
- Indian films are lengthy, often 2-3 hours long, and are 'masala films' with a mix of action, comedy, romance, drama, and music. They tend to be highly entertaining and escapist, with good triumphing over evil. The plots invoke emotions and family values.
- Some of the most successful and popular actors in Indian cinema are Amitabh Bachchan, Shah Rukh Khan, Deepika Padukone, and Aishwarya Rai Bachchan. Famous directors include Satyajit Ray, Raj Kapoor, and Mani Ratnam. Indian cinema is the largest film producer in the world.
- Indian films have recognizable tropes like the inclusion of gods and goddesses in song sequences, quick edits, use of colour, and last minute rescues and revelations. The cinema is deeply tied to Indian culture and traditions. It provides catharsis and entertainment for the masses. Overall, Indian cinema uses dance, music, and drama as a combined audio-visual performing art form.
