## Module 4-Science, Management and Indian Knowledge System

This module covers the following topics:

- Indian contributions to science and technology including mathematics, astronomy, metallurgy, agriculture, medicine, etc.
- Management practices and principles from ancient Indian texts like Kautilya's Arthashastra. Key principles of management like division of work, planning, organisation, leadership, control, etc. were enunciated by Indian thinkers.
- The Indian Knowledge System or the indigenous system of knowledge ranging from material sciences to natural and social sciences. It encompassed logic, epistemology, metaphysics, ethics, aesthetics, philosophy of science, education, statecraft, and varied other domains of learning.

Key points:

- Indian mathematicians made pioneering contributions to arithmetic, algebra, geometry, and trigonometry. The decimal system, negative numbers, zero, and calculus were contributed by Indians.
- In astronomy, Indians proposed heliocentric theories, calculated the circumference of the earth, and made advances in trigonometry applied to spheres.
- Metallurgy was highly advanced with production of high quality steel. Many temples and iron pillars still stand as proof of the advancement of this technology.
- Agricultural practices were scientific including crop rotation, agricultural productivity forecasting, pest control, and productivity enhancement techniques.
- Sushruta and Charaka were pioneers of medicine and surgery. Many surgical procedures and techniques in biology and physiology were described in detail.
- Kautilya's Arthashastra describes principles of management, economics, politics, and military strategy. It talks about organizational structure, leadership, control mechanisms, etc.
- The Indian Knowledge System covers a wide range of topics using logic, inference, and validation through experiments. It emphasizes the unity of material and spiritual aspects of life.

The key learning tricks and mnemonics are:

- For mathematics contributions: "Brahmagupta-Bhaskara" to remember mathematicians, " wheels of numerals" for decimal system.
- For astronomy: "Aryabhata" to remember astronomer, "spherical trigonometry" for advances.
- For metallurgy: "Iron pillar" to remember advancement.
- For agriculture: "Crop rotation" and "productivity enhancement" to remember practices.
- For medicine: "Sushruta-Charaka" to remember physicians, "surgery" and "biology" for advances.
- For management: "Arthashastra" to remember text, "organizational structure" and "control mechanisms" for principles.
