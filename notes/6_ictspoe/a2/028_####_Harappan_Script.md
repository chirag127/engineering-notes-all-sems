#### Harappan Script

The Harappan script is the script used by the Indus Valley Civilization, though it has not yet been deciphered. The following points summarize key facts about the Harappan script:

- The Harappan script was written from right to left using boustrophedon style (meaning alternating directions in each line).
- It had about 400 distinct symbols, though it's unclear if each symbol represented a word, syllable, or letter.
- The script has been found on seal impressions, copper tablets, ceramic pots, and other artifacts.
- The brevity of inscriptions and small number of tablets discovered has made decipherment challenging. Some historians believe it may be logo-syllabic, using symbols for words and syllables.
- Despite attempts at decipherment, the Harappan script remains undeciphered to date. Deciphering it could reveal further insights into Harappan language and culture.
