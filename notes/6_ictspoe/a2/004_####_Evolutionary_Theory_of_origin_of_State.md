#### Evolutionary Theory of origin of State

According to the Evolutionary or Historical theory, the State is a product of historical development and evolution. The State originated from the primitive tribal society as a result of the following evolutionary processes:

1. Growth of population and territory: With the growth of population and the expansion of territory, personal contacts broke down. So administration became necessary to hold people and territories together. This led to the emergence of the State.

2. Division of labour: As society progressed, division of labour occurred. Different classes and occupations emerged. This led to the creation of laws and authority to regulate production and exchange. This necessitated the existence of the State.

3. Conflicts: There were constant conflicts between tribes and groups over territory and resources. The stronger groups conquered and subjugated the weaker ones. The conquerors erected a system of administration to control the conquered groups. Thus the State came into being.

4. Private property: With the growth of agriculture and the concept of private property, disputes arose over the control of property and means of production. To protect private property and to resolve disputes, the authority of the State was required. Thus the institution of the State emerged.

## Advantages:
- Explains the origin of the State as a natural outcome of social evolution and growth.
- Highlights the role of important factors like population growth, division of labour, conflicts and private property in the development of the State.

## Disadvantages:
- Difficult to fix the exact point of origin of the State.
- Fails to explain the origin of the initial primitive tribal society.
- Gives undue importance to factors like conflicts and ignores factors like cooperation.
