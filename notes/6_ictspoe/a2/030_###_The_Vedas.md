### The Vedas

The Vedas are the oldest scriptures of Hinduism and are considered to be the foundation of all other scriptures. There are four Vedas:

1. Rig Veda: Contains hymns and mantras praising the various deities. It is the oldest Veda and contains some of the earliest religious ideas of the Indus Valley civilization.

2. Yajur Veda: Contains sacrificial formulas to be used by priests during rituals. It contains prose passages as well as verses from the Rig Veda.

3. Sama Veda: Consists of melodies meant to be sung by priests during rituals using hymns from the Rig Veda. It focuses on the musical chanting of hymns.

4. Atharva Veda: Contains hymns and spells meant to achieve worldly goals like acquiring wealth or begetting sons, as well as hymns with philosophical ideas. It is the youngest of the four Vedas.

**Mnemonics**: To remember the four Vedas, remember - "Regrettably, Your Athlete Seems Ill" where the first letter of each word represents a Veda in order: Rig, Yajur, Sama, Atharva.

The Vedas are some of the most sacred texts in Hinduism and are considered to have been revealed to ancient sages, not composed by them. They are written in Vedic Sanskrit and contain some of the earliest forms of religious poetry and ideas from the Indus Valley civilization around 1500 BCE. Though the Vedas are considered by Hindus as eternal truths, they provide insight into the beliefs and practices of ancient Indo-Aryan peoples.
