#### Mystical Theory of origin of State

- The mystical theory of origin of state is a theory that claims that the state is a divine creation and that the ruler is chosen by God or gods to govern the people.
- The theory is also known as the theory of divine right of kings or the theory of divine origin of state.
- The theory is based on the belief that the state is not a human institution, but a sacred and supernatural entity that transcends human reason and will.
- The theory is often used to justify the absolute and unquestionable authority of the ruler, who is seen as the representative or the embodiment of God or gods on earth.
- The theory is supported by various religious texts, traditions, and rituals that affirm the divine origin and sanction of the state and the ruler.
- The theory is one of the oldest and most widespread theories of state origin, and it has been adopted by various civilizations and cultures throughout history, such as ancient Egypt, Mesopotamia, China, India, Israel, Rome, and medieval Europe.
- The theory is criticized by many modern thinkers and scholars, who argue that the state is a human and social construct, and that the ruler is not divinely appointed, but elected or selected by the people or by other human factors.
- The theory is also challenged by the concepts of democracy, human rights, secularism, and rationalism, which emphasize the role of human agency, choice, and consent in the formation and functioning of the state.
