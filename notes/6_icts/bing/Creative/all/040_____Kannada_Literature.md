# Kannada Literature

Kannada literature is the body of written works in the Kannada language, a member of the Dravidian family spoken mainly in the Indian state of Karnataka and written in the Kannada script. It is one of the oldest and richest literatures in India, dating back to the 6th century AD. Some of the main features of Kannada literature are:

- It covers a wide range of topics, such as grammar, philosophy, prosody, rhetoric, chronicles, biography, history, drama, cuisine, dictionaries and encyclopedias.
- It has produced many eminent poets, writers, scholars and thinkers, such as Pampa, Ranna, Harihara, Basavanna, Akka Mahadevi, Kumara Vyasa, Purandara Dasa, Kanaka Dasa, Sarvajna, Kuvempu, Bendre, Shivaram Karanth, U.R. Ananthamurthy and Girish Karnad .
- It has developed various forms and genres, such as champu, vachana, tripadi, ragale, shatpadi, kavya, prabandha, navodaya, navya, bandaya, dalita and vachamarga .
- It has been influenced by and has influenced other languages and literatures, such as Sanskrit, Prakrit, Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam, Marathi, Urdu, English and Hindi .
- It has been recognized and honored by various awards and institutions, such as the Jnanpith Award, the Sahitya Akademi Award, the Karnataka Sahitya Academy, the Kannada Sahitya Parishat and the Kannada University .

Kannada literature is a rich and vibrant expression of the culture, history and identity of the Kannada people and their land. It is a source of pride and inspiration for millions of Kannada speakers and readers across the world. It is also a valuable contribution to the diversity and beauty of the world literature.
