# Malayalam Literature

Malayalam literature is the literature written in Malayalam, a South-Dravidian language spoken in the Indian state of Kerala. It has a rich and diverse history that spans over a thousand years. Some of the main features and periods of Malayalam literature are:

- The earliest known literary work in Malayalam is **Ramacharitam**, a poem composed in the late 12th or early 13th century by Cheeraman, a Chera king. It narrates the story of Rama in a simple and elegant style.
- The next phase of Malayalam literature is marked by the emergence of **Manipravalam**, a hybrid style that combines Malayalam and Sanskrit words and grammar. This style was mainly used for writing erotic and devotional poetry by poets such as Cherusseri Namboothiri, Thunchaththu Ezhuthachan, and Poonthanam .
- The 16th and 17th centuries saw the development of **Pattu**, a popular song literature that used native meters and folk elements. Some of the famous Pattu works are **Krishnagatha** by Cherusseri, **Ramakathapattu** by Kadungathur Narayanan, and **Kannassaramayanam** by Ramapurathu Warrier .
- The 18th and 19th centuries witnessed the rise of **modern prose** in Malayalam, influenced by Western education and literature. The first travelogue in any Indian language, **Varthamanappusthakam**, was written by Paremmakkal Thoma Kathanar in 1785. The first novel in Malayalam, **Indulekha**, was written by O. Chandu Menon in 1889. The first short story in Malayalam, **Vasanavikriti**, was written by K. C. Kesava Pillai in 1891 .
- The 20th and 21st centuries have seen the flourishing of various genres and movements in Malayalam literature, such as **romanticism**, **realism**, **modernism**, **postmodernism**, **feminism**, **Dalit literature**, **environmental literature**, and **diaspora literature**. Some of the prominent writers of this period are Kumaran Asan, Vallathol Narayana Menon, Ulloor S. Parameswara Iyer, G. Sankara Kurup, Thakazhi Sivasankara Pillai, M. T. Vasudevan Nair, O. V. Vijayan, Kamala Surayya, M. Mukundan, K. Satchidanandan, Arundhati Roy, Benyamin, and K. R. Meera .

Malayalam literature is a rich and vibrant expression of the culture, history, and identity of the Malayali people. It reflects their diversity, creativity, and resilience in the face of various challenges and changes. It is also a source of inspiration, education, and entertainment for readers across the world.
