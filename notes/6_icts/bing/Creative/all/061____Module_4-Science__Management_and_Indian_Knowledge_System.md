# Module 4-Science, Management and Indian Knowledge System

This module aims to introduce the students to the rich and diverse Indian Knowledge System (IKS) and its relevance and applications in various fields of science and management. The module will cover the following topics:

- What is IKS and why is it important to study it?
- The sources and methods of IKS, such as Vedas, Upanishads, Puranas, Itihasas, Shastras, etc.
- The contributions of IKS to mathematics, astronomy, medicine, metallurgy, architecture, etc.
- The principles and practices of IKS in management, such as ethics, leadership, governance, innovation, etc.
- The challenges and opportunities of integrating IKS with modern science and management.

Some of the learning outcomes of this module are:

- To appreciate the diversity and depth of IKS and its relevance to contemporary issues.
- To develop a critical and analytical perspective on IKS and its comparison with other knowledge systems.
- To apply the insights and methods of IKS to solve problems and create value in science and management domains.
- To foster a sense of pride and responsibility towards the preservation and dissemination of IKS.
