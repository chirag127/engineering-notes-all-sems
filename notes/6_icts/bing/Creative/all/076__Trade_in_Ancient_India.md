### Trade in Ancient India

- Trade and commerce were important aspects of ancient Indian civilization. India had a rich and diverse economy that included agriculture, handicrafts, mining, metallurgy, and maritime activities.
- India was well connected with other regions of the world through land and sea routes. India traded with Mesopotamia, Egypt, Persia, Greece, Rome, China, Southeast Asia, and Africa. India exported spices, textiles, gems, ivory, pearls, metals, and other products, and imported gold, silver, wine, silk, horses, and other goods.
- Trade and commerce were regulated by the state as well as by guilds and associations of merchants and artisans. The state collected taxes and duties, maintained roads and ports, issued coins and weights, and enforced laws and contracts. The guilds and associations provided quality control, standardization, training, and welfare for their members. They also acted as banks and credit institutions, and sometimes as diplomatic agents.
- Trade and commerce stimulated the development of urban centers, cultural exchange, scientific and technological innovation, and religious diversity in ancient India. Some of the famous cities that emerged as centers of trade and commerce were Harappa, Mohenjo-daro, Lothal, Taxila, Ujjain, Pataliputra, Kanchipuram, Madurai, and Muziris. Trade and commerce also facilitated the spread of Indian culture, art, literature, and religion to other parts of the world, especially through Buddhism and Hinduism. Trade and commerce also influenced the development of mathematics, astronomy, medicine, metallurgy, and navigation in ancient India.

#### Mnemonics and learning tricks

- To remember the main products that India exported, use the acronym **STIG PM** (Spices, Textiles, Ivory, Gems, Pearls, Metals).
- To remember the main products that India imported, use the acronym **G SWISH** (Gold, Silver, Wine, Silk, Horses).
- To remember the main regions that India traded with, use the acronym **MEG RC SAFA** (Mesopotamia, Egypt, Greece, Rome, China, Southeast Asia, Africa).
- To remember the main cities that emerged as centers of trade and commerce, use the acronym **HULL TUP KAMM** (Harappa, Ujjain, Lothal, Taxila, Pataliputra, Kanchipuram, Madurai, Muziris).
