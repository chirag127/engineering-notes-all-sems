# Political Ideals in Ancient India and their relevance in modern times

- Ancient India had a rich tradition of political thought and practice that spanned from the Vedic period to the medieval period.
- Some of the political ideals that emerged from this tradition are:

  - Democracy: The Rig Veda, the oldest of the four Vedas, extolled the concept of **Samjnana**, which means the collective consciousness or the national mind of the people. The Rig Veda also mentioned the **Sabha** and the **Samiti**, which were the assemblies of the people that participated in the governance and decision-making of the society. Democracy in ancient India was not limited to the political sphere, but also extended to the social and economic spheres, where people enjoyed freedom of speech, expression, occupation, and religion.
  - Dharma: The term **Dharma** means the cosmic law or the moral order that governs the universe and the human society. Dharma was the basis of the political philosophy and the legal system of ancient India. Dharma prescribed the duties and responsibilities of the king and the subjects, as well as the rights and privileges of the different classes and groups of the society. Dharma also emphasized the values of justice, truth, non-violence, compassion, and welfare of all beings .
  - Artha: The term **Artha** means the material wealth or the economic prosperity that is essential for the well-being of the society. Artha was one of the four goals of human life, along with Dharma, Kama, and Moksha. Artha was also the subject of the **Arthashastra**, the treatise on statecraft and political economy written by Kautilya, the prime minister of the Mauryan emperor Chandragupta. The Arthashastra laid down the principles and policies of governance, administration, diplomacy, war, taxation, trade, and welfare .
  - Rajadharma: The term **Rajadharma** means the duty or the ideal of kingship. Rajadharma was derived from the concept of Dharma, and it defined the role and the qualities of the king as the protector and the promoter of the society. Rajadharma also prescribed the code of conduct and the ethical standards for the king, such as being virtuous, benevolent, impartial, generous, courageous, and wise. Rajadharma also emphasized the importance of the king's advisors, ministers, and counselors, who were expected to be loyal, competent, and honest .

- The relevance of these political ideals in modern times are:

  - Democracy: Democracy is widely regarded as the best form of government that ensures the participation and representation of the people in the political process. Democracy also fosters the values of equality, liberty, fraternity, and human rights, which are essential for the dignity and development of the human society. Democracy in modern times can learn from the ancient Indian model of democracy, which was more inclusive, holistic, and decentralized than the contemporary models.
  - Dharma: Dharma is a universal and timeless concept that can guide the human society towards a harmonious and sustainable existence. Dharma can inspire the modern society to adopt a moral and ethical approach to the political, social, and environmental issues that plague the world today. Dharma can also help the modern society to balance the material and spiritual aspects of life, and to respect the diversity and interdependence of all forms of life .
  - Artha: Artha is a pragmatic and realistic concept that recognizes the importance of the economic dimension of the human society. Artha can inform the modern society to pursue the economic development and prosperity that is conducive to the welfare and happiness of the people, and not at the cost of the environment and the future generations. Artha can also encourage the modern society to adopt a rational and scientific approach to the management and utilization of the natural and human resources .
  - Rajadharma: Rajadharma is a noble and idealistic concept that sets the standards and expectations for the political leadership and the governance of the society. Rajadharma can inspire the modern society to elect and support the leaders who are capable, responsible, and accountable to the people, and who can uphold the values of justice, peace, and democracy.
