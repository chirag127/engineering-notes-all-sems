### Political Ideals in Ancient India and their relevance in modern times

- Ancient India had a rich tradition of political thought and practice that spanned from the Vedic period to the medieval period.
- The political ideals of ancient India were based on the concepts of Dharma, Rta, Rajadharma, Dandaniti, Arthashastra, and Lokasangraha.
- Dharma is the cosmic law that governs the universe and the moral duty of every individual. Rta is the natural order that sustains the harmony of the cosmos and the society. Rajadharma is the duty of the king to uphold Dharma and Rta by ruling with justice, benevolence, and wisdom. Dandaniti is the science of politics and administration that deals with the use of power, policy, and law. Arthashastra is the art of statecraft and economics that aims at the material prosperity and security of the state. Lokasangraha is the welfare of the people and the world, which is the ultimate goal of the state and the king.
- The political ideals of ancient India were expressed and developed in various sources, such as the Vedas, the Upanishads, the Epics, the Dharmashastras, the Puranas, the Buddhist and Jain texts, and the works of Kautilya, Kamandaka, Sukra, and others.
- The political ideals of ancient India were not static or monolithic, but dynamic and diverse. They reflected the changing historical, social, and cultural contexts of India. They also accommodated different views and perspectives, such as the orthodox and the heterodox, the monarchical and the republican, the centralized and the decentralized, the ideal and the pragmatic, and so on.
- The political ideals of ancient India have relevance and significance for modern times, as they offer insights and lessons on various aspects of politics and governance, such as the role and responsibility of the ruler, the rights and duties of the citizens, the balance and integration of power and morality, the importance and challenges of welfare and development, the need and methods of diplomacy and defense, the value and diversity of culture and civilization, and so on.
- The political ideals of ancient India can also inspire and inform the contemporary debates and discourses on democracy, human rights, secularism, pluralism, federalism, nationalism, globalization, and other issues that affect the modern world.
