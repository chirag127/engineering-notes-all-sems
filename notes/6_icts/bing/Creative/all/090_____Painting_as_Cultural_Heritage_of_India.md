# Painting as Cultural Heritage of India

- Painting is one of the oldest and most diverse forms of art in India, dating back to prehistoric times.
- Painting reflects the cultural, religious, and political aspects of Indian society, as well as the aesthetic and creative expressions of the artists.
- Painting is also a medium of preserving and transmitting the intangible cultural heritage of India, such as songs, dances, stories, rituals, and beliefs.
- Some of the major types of Indian paintings are:

  - **Madhubani painting**: A folk art form from Bihar, characterized by intricate geometric patterns, bright colors, and symbolic motifs depicting nature, deities, and social events. Madhubani painting is traditionally done by women on walls and floors using natural dyes and brushes made of bamboo and cotton.
  - **Warli painting**: A tribal art form from Maharashtra, characterized by simple and monochromatic linear figures depicting the daily life, culture, and beliefs of the Warli people. Warli painting is usually done on mud walls using white rice paste .
  - **Mural painting**: A large-scale painting done on walls, ceilings, or other architectural surfaces, depicting religious, historical, or mythological scenes. Mural painting is one of the oldest forms of Indian art, found in ancient caves, temples, palaces, and monuments. Some of the famous examples of mural painting are Ajanta caves, Ellora caves, Brihadeshwara temple, and Hampi .
  - **Miniature painting**: A small-scale painting done on paper, cloth, ivory, or other materials, using fine brushes and rich colors. Miniature painting flourished in India from the 10th to the 19th century, under the patronage of various rulers and courts. Some of the prominent styles of miniature painting are Pala, Mughal, Rajasthani, Pahari, and Deccan .
  - **Modern painting**: A broad term that encompasses the various styles and movements of painting that emerged in India in the 19th and 20th centuries, influenced by Western art, nationalism, social reform, and individual expression. Some of the notable modern painters are Raja Ravi Varma, Abanindranath Tagore, Nandalal Bose, Amrita Sher-Gil, M.F. Husain, and S.H. Raza .

- Painting is a valuable and vibrant part of the Indian art and culture, that showcases the diversity, creativity, and heritage of the country.
