### Northern Indian languages

- Northern Indian languages are mainly the languages that belong to the Indo-Aryan branch of the Indo-European language family.  
- They are spoken by about 78% of the population of India, mostly in the northern, central and western regions of the country. 
- Some of the major Northern Indian languages are Hindi, Urdu, Punjabi, Kashmiri, Sindhi, Gujarati, Marathi, Rajasthani, Bhojpuri, Maithili and Nepali.  
- Hindi is the most widely spoken language in Northern India and the official language of the Indian Union.  It is written in the Devanagari script and has many regional dialects and varieties. 
- Urdu is the official language of Jammu and Kashmir and one of the official languages of Delhi.  It is written in the Perso-Arabic script and has a rich literary tradition influenced by Persian and Arabic. 
- Punjabi is the official language of Punjab and one of the official languages of Delhi and Chandigarh.  It is written in the Gurmukhi script and has a strong cultural and religious identity associated with Sikhism. 
- Kashmiri is the official language of Jammu and Kashmir and one of the official languages of Ladakh.  It is written in the Perso-Arabic script or the Devanagari script and has a distinctive phonology and grammar. 
- Sindhi is the official language of Sindh province in Pakistan and one of the official languages of India.  It is written in the Perso-Arabic script or the Devanagari script and has a rich vocabulary influenced by Sanskrit, Persian and Arabic. 
- Gujarati is the official language of Gujarat and one of the official languages of Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu.  It is written in the Gujarati script and has a prominent literary and commercial history. 
- Marathi is the official language of Maharashtra and one of the official languages of Goa and Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu.  It is written in the Devanagari script and has a long and influential literary tradition. 
- Rajasthani is a group of languages and dialects spoken in Rajasthan and neighbouring states.  It is written in the Devanagari script and has a rich folk and oral culture. 
- Bhojpuri is a language spoken in Bihar, Uttar Pradesh, Jharkhand and parts of Nepal.  It is written in the Devanagari script and has a popular cinema and music industry. 
- Maithili is a language spoken in Bihar, Jharkhand and parts of Nepal.  It is written in the Devanagari script or the Mithilakshar script and has a classical literary heritage. 
- Nepali is the official language of Nepal and one of the official languages of Sikkim and Darjeeling district in West Bengal.  It is written in the Devanagari script and has a close affinity with Hindi and other Northern Indian languages.
