### Indian’s Cultural Contribution to the World

India is a country of rich and diverse culture, with a long and ancient history. India has made many contributions to the world in various fields, such as mathematics, science, medicine, architecture, art, language, literature, religion, philosophy, and more. Here are some of the major contributions of India to the world:

- **Mathematics**: India is the birthplace of many mathematical concepts and discoveries, such as the decimal system, the concept of zero, the place value system, algebra, trigonometry, geometry, calculus, and more. India also invented the numerals that are used in most of the world today, known as the Hindu-Arabic numerals. Some of the famous Indian mathematicians include Aryabhata, Brahmagupta, Bhaskara, Madhava, Ramanujan, and more.

- **Architecture**: India has a rich and diverse architectural heritage, with influences from various regions, religions, and periods. India is home to many magnificent monuments, temples, palaces, forts, mosques, churches, and more, that showcase the artistic and engineering skills of the Indian people. Some of the examples of Indian architecture are the Taj Mahal, the Qutub Minar, the Khajuraho temples, the Hampi ruins, the Ajanta and Ellora caves, the Konark Sun Temple, and more.

- **Textiles**: India is one of the oldest and largest producers of textiles in the world, with a history of more than 5000 years. India is known for its cotton, silk, wool, and jute fabrics, as well as its various techniques of dyeing, printing, weaving, embroidering, and embellishing. India has also influenced the fashion and textile industry of many countries, such as China, Japan, Persia, Europe, and more, with its exports of fabrics, garments, and accessories. Some of the famous Indian textiles are muslin, chintz, calico, brocade, jamdani, bandhani, ikat, patola, kantha, and more.

- **Medicine**: India is the origin of many systems of medicine, such as Ayurveda, Siddha, Unani, and Yoga, that are based on the holistic approach of healing the body, mind, and spirit. India also made many discoveries and inventions in the field of medicine, such as surgery, anesthesia, plastic surgery, cataract surgery, vaccination, dentistry, and more. Some of the famous Indian physicians and surgeons include Charaka, Sushruta, Nagarjuna, Vagbhata, and more.

- **Language**: India is a multilingual country, with more than 120 languages and 1600 dialects spoken by its people. India is also the source of many languages that belong to the Indo-European and Dravidian language families, such as Sanskrit, Hindi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, and more. India has also influenced the languages of many other countries, such as English, Persian, Arabic, Chinese, Japanese, and more, with its words, grammar, literature, and scripts. Some of the famous Indian languages are Sanskrit, Pali, Prakrit, Tamil, Hindi, Urdu, and more.

- **Literature**: India has a rich and diverse literary tradition, with works in various languages, genres, and forms. India is the home of many epic poems, such as the Ramayana, the Mahabharata, and the Jataka tales, that have influenced the literature and culture of many countries, such as Southeast Asia, China, Japan, and more. India is also the home of many classical and modern poets, writers, and scholars, such as Kalidasa, Valmiki, Vyasa, Bhartrihari, Amir Khusrau, Kabir, Mirabai, Rabindranath Tagore, R.K. Narayan, and more.
