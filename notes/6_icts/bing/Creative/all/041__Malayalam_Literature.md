### Malayalam Literature

- Malayalam literature is the literature written in Malayalam, a South-Dravidian language spoken in the Indian state of Kerala and some union territories. 
- Malayalam literature has a rich and diverse history, spanning from the 12th century CE to the present day. It has been influenced by various sources, such as Sanskrit, Tamil, Arabi Malayalam, English, and other regional languages.  
- Malayalam literature can be broadly divided into four periods: early, medieval, modern, and contemporary. Each period has its own distinctive features, genres, styles, and themes.  

#### Early Period (12th to 15th century CE)

- The earliest extant work of Malayalam literature is Ramacharitam, an epic poem based on the Ramayana, written in a mixture of Malayalam and Tamil.  
- The Niranam poets, a family of poets from central Travancore, were the pioneers of Malayalam poetry. They translated Sanskrit works like the Ramayana and the Bhagavad Gita into Malayalam.  
- Manipravalam, a literary dialect that blended Malayalam and Sanskrit, emerged as a popular medium of expression. It was used to compose erotic poetry, message poems, and campus (mixed prose and verse works).  
- The Līlātilakam, a treatise on Malayalam grammar and poetics, was written in Sanskrit in the 14th century. It described the rules and conventions of Manipravalam literature.  
- The Unnunili Sandesam, a message poem modeled on Kalidasa's Meghaduta, is considered the finest work of this period. It describes the journey of a messenger from the Malabar coast to the court of the king of Travancore.  

#### Medieval Period (16th to 18th century CE)

- This period witnessed the rise of devotional literature in Malayalam, influenced by the Bhakti movement and the arrival of Christianity and Islam.  
- Thunchaththu Ezhuthachan, known as the father of modern Malayalam literature, wrote the Adhyatma Ramayana and the Mahabharata in Malayalam. He also standardized the Malayalam alphabet and introduced the kilipattu (parrot song) genre.  
- Cherusseri Namboothiri, another prominent poet, wrote the Krishnagatha, a poem on the life of Krishna, in a simple and elegant style. He also wrote the first Malayalam biography, the Bhashakautaliyam.  
- Poonthanam Nambudiri, a devotee of Krishna, wrote the Jnanappana, a collection of devotional songs, in a colloquial dialect. He also wrote the Njanarachana, a commentary on the Bhagavad Gita.  
- Melpathur Narayana Bhattathiri, a scholar and poet, wrote the Narayaniyam, a condensed version of the Bhagavata Purana, in Sanskritized Malayalam. He also wrote the Prakriyasarvasvam, a work on Sanskrit grammar.  
- Kunchan Nambiar, the founder of the Ottan Thullal (a satirical dance drama), wrote many humorous and witty poems in Malayalam. He also wrote the Thullal Ramayanam, a parody of the Ramayana.  
- Arabi Malayalam, a dialect that mixed Malayalam and Arabic, developed as a medium of religious and secular literature among the Muslims of Kerala. It produced works like the Muhyadheen Mala, a poem on the life of a Sufi saint, and the Mappila Songs, folk songs on various themes.  

#### Modern Period (19th to 20th century CE)

- This period saw the emergence of prose, novel, drama, short story, essay, and journalism in Malayalam
