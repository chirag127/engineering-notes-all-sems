#### Challenges faced by Women in Society in Ancient India

- Women in ancient India enjoyed a high status in society during the early Vedic period, around 1500 BC, when they could participate in war, gymnastics, public activities, decision-making, and education . They also had a role in teaching and spreading the Vedic knowledge, and some of them were respected philosophers.
- Women's status began to decline in the later Vedic period, when they lost their economic and political rights, and were subjected to the patriarchal norms of the Manusmriti texts, which prohibited widow remarriage, inheritance, and education for women . They were also considered as impure and inferior to men, and had to follow strict rules of conduct and obedience .
- Women's condition further deteriorated during the medieval period, from 500 AD to 1500 AD, when they faced various forms of oppression and violence, such as child marriage, sati, purdah, polygamy, and rape . They were also denied access to education, property, and religious rights, and were confined to the domestic sphere.
- Women's empowerment in India has been a long and uneven struggle, which gained momentum during the British occupation, when some social reforms were introduced to improve the status of women. However, even in the modern period, women face many challenges and discrimination in terms of gender equality, education, employment, health, safety, and political participation .

Some possible mnemonics and learning tricks for the challenges faced by women in ancient India are:

- To remember the four periods of women's status in ancient India, use the acronym **VELM** (Vedic, Later Vedic, Medieval, Modern).
- To remember the five forms of oppression and violence faced by women in the medieval period, use the acronym **CSPRP** (Child marriage, Sati, Purdah, Polygamy, Rape).
- To remember the six areas of challenges and discrimination faced by women in the modern period, use the acronym **GEEHSP** (Gender equality, Education, Employment, Health, Safety, Political participation).
