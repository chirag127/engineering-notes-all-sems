## Module 1- Society State and Polity in India

This module covers the following topics:

- The origin and evolution of the state in ancient India, and the different theories that explain it, such as evolutionary theory, force theory, mystical theory and contract theory.
- The stages of state formation in ancient India, and the characteristics of different types of states, such as tribal, monarchical, republican and imperial.
- The concept and role of kingship, council of ministers and administration in ancient India, and the sources of political authority and legitimacy, such as dharma, varna, ashrama and raja dharma.
- The political ideals and values in ancient India, and the influence of various schools of thought, such as Brahmanism, Buddhism, Jainism, Charvaka and Kautilya, on the political philosophy and ethics of ancient India.
- The conditions and challenges of the welfare state in ancient India, and the measures taken by the rulers and the society to ensure social justice, economic development, security and cultural diversity.

Some of the possible mnemonics and learning tricks for this module are:

- To remember the four theories of the origin of the state, use the acronym **EFMC** (Evolutionary, Force, Mystical, Contract).
- To remember the four stages of state formation in ancient India, use the acronym **TRIM** (Tribal, Republican, Imperial, Monarchical).
- To remember the four sources of political authority and legitimacy in ancient India, use the acronym **DVAR** (Dharma, Varna, Ashrama, Raja dharma).
- To remember the five schools of thought that influenced the political ideals and values in ancient India, use the acronym **B-BACK** (Brahmanism, Buddhism, Jainism, Charvaka, Kautilya).
