# North Indian Literature

- North Indian literature refers to the literature produced in the languages of northern India, such as Hindi, Urdu, Punjabi, Kashmiri, Rajasthani, etc.
- North Indian literature is influenced by the ancient Indian background, which includes two Sanskrit epic poems, the Mahabharata and Ramayana, as well as the Bhagavata-purana and the other Puranas.
- North Indian literature also reflects the cultural and religious diversity of the region, as well as the historical and political events that shaped its history, such as the Islamic invasions, the Mughal empire, the British colonial rule, the Partition, and the Independence movement.
- Some of the prominent genres of North Indian literature are poetry, drama, novel, short story, essay, biography, autobiography, and travelogue.
- Some of the notable authors of North Indian literature are Kabir, Tulsidas, Mirza Ghalib, Premchand, Rabindranath Tagore, Munshi Premchand, Saadat Hasan Manto, Amrita Pritam, Khushwant Singh, Mahadevi Varma, Harivansh Rai Bachchan, etc.
- North Indian literature also includes the literature of the Northeastern states of India, such as Assam, Meghalaya, Manipur, Mizoram, Nagaland, Sikkim, and Tripura, which have rich cultural and ethnolinguistic diversity.
- The literature of the Northeastern states is representative of varied histories, languages, socio-cultural and religious practices, as well as the issues of identity, marginalization, insurgency, and development.
- Some of the genres of the literature of the Northeastern states are folk literature, oral literature, modern literature, and tribal literature.
- Some of the authors of the literature of the Northeastern states are Birendra Kumar Bhattacharya, Mamang Dai, Temsula Ao, Easterine Kire, Mitra Phukan, Arupa Patangia Kalita, etc.
- North Indian literature is also related to the literature of the Native Americans, who are the indigenous peoples of the Americas, especially the North American cultures (from the Eskimos to the Indians along the Mexican border).
- The literature of the Native Americans is based on their mythology, which can be divided into three major cultural regions: North American cultures, Central and South American urban cultures, and Caribbean and South American hunting-and-gathering and farming cultures.
- Some of the genres of the literature of the Native Americans are oral narratives, songs, chants, prayers, legends, myths, folktales, and historical accounts.
- Some of the authors of the literature of the Native Americans are N. Scott Momaday, Leslie Marmon Silko, Louise Erdrich, Sherman Alexie, Joy Harjo, etc.
