### Vedic Religion in India

- Vedic religion, also called Vedism, was the religion of the ancient Indo-European-speaking peoples who entered India about 1500 BCE from the region of present-day Iran.
- It takes its name from the collections of sacred texts known as the Vedas, which are the oldest stratum of religious activity in India for which there exist written materials.
- It was one of the major traditions that shaped Hinduism, the dominant religion of India and the third largest religion in the world.
- The Vedic religion consisted of various beliefs and practices, such as:
  - The worship of a pantheon of deities, mostly personifications of natural forces, such as Indra (thunder), Agni (fire), Surya (sun), Varuna (water), and Soma (a sacred plant).
  - The performance of elaborate rituals and sacrifices, especially the fire sacrifice (yajna), which involved the offering of oblations to the gods through the mediation of priests.
  - The recitation and preservation of the Vedic texts, which were composed and handed down orally over a period of about 10 centuries, from about the 15th to the 5th century BCE.
  - The development of various schools of thought and philosophy, such as the Upanishads, which explored the nature of the self, the cosmos, and the ultimate reality (Brahman).
  - The emergence of social and religious divisions, such as the four varnas (classes) of Brahmins (priests), Kshatriyas (warriors), Vaishyas (merchants), and Shudras (servants), and the concept of karma (action and its consequences).
- The Vedic religion gradually gave way to classical Hinduism, which incorporated elements from other traditions, such as Buddhism, Jainism, and local cults.
- Some aspects of the Vedic religion, however, continue to be observed within the framework of modern Hinduism, such as the reverence for the Vedas, the practice of certain rituals, and the worship of some Vedic gods.

#### Mnemonics and learning tricks

- To remember the four Vedas, use the acronym RYAS: Rigveda, Yajurveda, Samaveda, and Atharvaveda.
- To remember the four varnas, use the acronym BKVS: Brahmins, Kshatriyas, Vaishyas, and Shudras.
- To remember the main Vedic gods, use the acronym IAVAS: Indra, Agni, Varuna, Surya, and Soma.
