# The Ramayana

The Ramayana is a Hindu epic that narrates the life and adventures of Rama, the prince of Kosala, and his wife Sita. The Ramayana has the following main points:

- Rama is the son of King Dasaratha of Kosala, who performs a sacrifice to obtain a son. He is given four sons by the gods: Rama, Bharata, Lakshmana, and Shatrughna. Rama is the eldest and the most virtuous of the four  .
- Rama wins the hand of Sita, the daughter of King Janaka of Mithila, by breaking the bow of Shiva, a feat that no one else could accomplish. Sita is the incarnation of Lakshmi, the goddess of wealth and prosperity  .
- Rama is chosen by Dasaratha to be his successor, but his stepmother Kaikeyi, influenced by her maid Manthara, asks Dasaratha to exile Rama for fourteen years and make Bharata the king instead. Dasaratha reluctantly agrees, and Rama leaves for the forest with Sita and Lakshmana, his loyal brother. Bharata refuses to accept the throne and goes to the forest to persuade Rama to return, but Rama insists on fulfilling his father's promise. Bharata takes Rama's sandals and places them on the throne as a symbol of Rama's authority   .
- Rama, Sita, and Lakshmana wander in the forest and meet many sages, friends, and foes. They encounter the demoness Surpanakha, who falls in love with Rama and tries to seduce him. Rama rejects her, and Lakshmana cuts off her nose and ears. Surpanakha complains to her brother Ravana, the king of Lanka and the most powerful demon in the world. Ravana decides to take revenge by abducting Sita. He sends his uncle Maricha, who disguises himself as a golden deer, to lure Rama and Lakshmana away from Sita. Rama chases the deer and kills it, but not before Maricha imitates Rama's voice and cries for help. Sita hears the cry and urges Lakshmana to go and help Rama. Lakshmana reluctantly leaves, drawing a protective circle around Sita and warning her not to cross it. Ravana then appears as a mendicant and asks Sita for alms. Sita crosses the circle to give him food, and Ravana grabs her and flies away in his chariot. Sita drops her jewels along the way, hoping that Rama will find them. Rama and Lakshmana return and realize that Sita has been kidnapped. They begin their search for Sita   .
- Rama and Lakshmana meet Hanuman, the monkey king, and his army of monkeys and bears. Hanuman is the son of the wind god and a devotee of Rama. He helps Rama and Lakshmana to find Sita, who is imprisoned in Lanka. Hanuman flies across the ocean and reaches Lanka, where he finds Sita in a grove. He gives her Rama's ring as a token of his identity and assures her that Rama will come to rescue her. He also sets fire to Lanka with his tail and returns to Rama with the news of Sita's whereabouts   .
- Rama and his allies build a bridge across the ocean and reach Lanka. They fight a fierce battle with Ravana and his army of demons. Rama kills many of Ravana's generals and brothers, including Kumbhakarna, who is a giant and a formidable warrior. Rama finally confronts Ravana and slays him with a divine arrow. He frees Sita and crowns Vibhishana, Ravana's brother who defected to Rama's side, as the new king of Lanka   .
