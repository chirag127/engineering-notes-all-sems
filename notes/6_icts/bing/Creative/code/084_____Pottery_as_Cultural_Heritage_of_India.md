### Pottery as Cultural Heritage of India

- Pottery is one of the most ancient and enduring forms of artistic expression in India. It reflects the cultural diversity, creativity and history of the Indian people.
- Pottery in India can be traced back to the early settlements of Lahuradewa and Mehargarh, dating back to the 7th millennium BCE . The Indus Valley Civilization, which flourished between 2600 and 1900 BCE, was one of the first cultures to use pottery for various purposes, such as storage, cooking, ritual and trade .
- Pottery in India has evolved over time, influenced by various factors, such as geography, climate, religion, trade and invasions. Different regions of India have developed their own distinctive styles, techniques and motifs of pottery, such as terracotta, glazed, painted, blue pottery, black pottery and red pottery .
- Pottery in India is not only a functional craft, but also a form of artistic expression and cultural identity. Pottery reflects the beliefs, values, traditions and aesthetics of the Indian people. Pottery is used for various purposes, such as decoration, worship, festivals, gifts and souvenirs .
- Pottery in India is also a source of livelihood for many artisans, who practice this craft with skill and passion. Pottery is a part of the intangible cultural heritage of India, which needs to be preserved and promoted for future generations.
