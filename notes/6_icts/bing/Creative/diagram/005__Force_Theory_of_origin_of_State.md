The Force Theory of origin of State is a theory that explains the emergence of the State as a result of the conquest and domination of the weak by the strong. According to this theory, the State is the product of human aggression and violence, and its authority is based on the principle of might is right. The theory implies that the State is not a mutually beneficial institution, but a tool of exploitation and oppression of the lower classes by the upper classes.

The diagram shows how the strong tribe conquers the weak tribe and establishes its rule over them. The conqueror becomes the ruler and the conquered becomes the ruled. The ruler then forms a State, which is a political community that has a defined territory and a government. The State exercises its authority over the territory and the government administers the laws and policies of the State. The State also maintains and extends its power within and without by using or threatening force against the ruled and other States. The Force Theory of origin of State assumes that the State is the result of war and domination, and that its legitimacy and purpose are derived from the power of the ruler.
