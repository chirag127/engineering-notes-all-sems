### Pottery as Cultural Heritage of India

- Pottery is one of the most ancient and tangible forms of Indian art, dating back to the prehistoric times .
- Pottery reflects the cultural diversity and richness of India, as different regions have their own styles, techniques, motifs and materials of pottery making .
- Pottery also plays an important role in studying the history, culture and society of India, as it provides clues about the trade, technology, religion, economy and lifestyle of various periods and civilizations .
- Some of the major types of pottery in India are:

  - **Terracotta pottery**: This is the most common and basic form of pottery, made from clay and baked in the sun or kiln. It is usually red, brown or orange in color and has a coarse texture. It is used for making pots, figurines, toys, lamps, tiles and other decorative items .
  - **Glazed pottery**: This is a type of pottery that has a shiny and smooth surface, created by applying a layer of glass-like substance over the clay. It is usually white, blue or green in color and has a fine texture. It is used for making tableware, vases, jars, tiles and other ornamental items .
  - **Paper mache pottery**: This is a type of pottery that is made from paper pulp mixed with glue, clay and other materials. It is usually painted with bright colors and has a light and delicate texture. It is used for making masks, dolls, boxes, trays and other artistic items .
  - **Blue pottery**: This is a type of glazed pottery that is made from quartz and other minerals, instead of clay. It is usually blue, turquoise or white in color and has a glossy and smooth texture. It is used for making bowls, plates, cups, tiles and other elegant items .
  - **Black pottery**: This is a type of pottery that is made from clay mixed with iron and other metals. It is usually black, brown or gray in color and has a metallic and shiny texture. It is used for making pots, pitchers, lamps, urns and other ethnic items .

- Pottery is a living craft tradition of India that is still practiced extensively by many artisans and communities. It is also an eco-friendly and sustainable form of art that uses natural and locally available materials.
- Pottery is a cultural heritage of India that showcases the creativity, skill and aesthetic sense of the Indian people. It is a source of pride, identity and livelihood for many Indians. It is also a medium of expression, communication and education for the Indian society .
