### Pottery as Cultural Heritage of India

Pottery is one of the oldest and most enduring forms of artistic expression in India. It reflects the rich and diverse cultural heritage of the Indian subcontinent, from the ancient Indus Valley Civilisation to the present day. Pottery is not only a utilitarian craft, but also a medium of creativity, symbolism and identity.
