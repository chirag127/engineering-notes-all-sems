Purusārtha is a key concept in Hinduism that refers to the four proper goals or aims of a human life. They are:

- Dharma: righteousness, moral values, duty, law, virtue, and right way of living.
- Artha: prosperity, economic values, wealth, and well-being.
- Kama: pleasure, love, psychological values, and enjoyment.
- Moksha: liberation, spiritual values, and self-actualization.

These four goals are not mutually exclusive, but rather interdependent and complementary. They are meant to guide the individual and the society towards an ideal world that is harmonious, balanced, and fulfilling. However, they also involve a hierarchy of preference, where Dharma is considered more important than Artha or Kama, and Moksha is considered the ultimate goal of human life.
