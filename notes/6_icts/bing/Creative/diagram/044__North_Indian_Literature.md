### North Indian Literature

North Indian Literature is a broad term that encompasses the literary traditions of the various languages and cultures of the northern part of the Indian subcontinent. It includes the ancient Sanskrit literature, the medieval literature of the Indo-Aryan languages, the modern literature of the Hindi-Urdu continuum, and the literature of other regional languages such as Punjabi, Gujarati, Marathi, Bengali, Kashmiri, and Nepali. 

The following ascii diagram shows a simplified overview of the major branches and genres of North Indian Literature, based on the information from the web search results. It is not exhaustive or comprehensive, but rather a general representation of the diversity and richness of the literary heritage of North India.
