The Evolutionary Theory of origin of State is a theory that explains the state as a product of historical and social evolution. It rejects the idea that the state is a divine creation, a social contract, a patriarchal or matriarchal expansion, or a result of force. Instead, it argues that the state emerged gradually from the development of kinship, religion, property, defence, force, and political consciousness among human groups  .

The diagram above illustrates the basic stages of the evolution of the state according to this theory. The state originated from the family, which expanded into clans and tribes based on kinship ties. Religion, property, and defence were some of the factors that influenced the formation of larger and more complex political units. Force was also used to conquer and unify different groups under a common authority. Political consciousness was the final factor that led to the emergence of the state as a distinct and sovereign entity.
