Urdu literature is the literature written in the Urdu language, which is mostly spoken in Pakistan and northern India. It is influenced by the cultural and religious traditions of Islam, as well as the Persian and Sanskrit languages. Urdu literature is mainly known for its poetry, especially the ghazal and nazm forms, but it also includes other genres such as short stories, novels, and essays.
