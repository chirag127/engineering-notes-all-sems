### Urdu Literature in India

- Urdu literature is the literature written in the Urdu language, which is spoken by the Muslims of Pakistan and northern India .
- Urdu literature is influenced by the Persian, Arabic, and Turkish languages, as well as the local languages of the Indian subcontinent, such as Hindi, Punjabi, Sindhi, and Gujarati.
- Urdu literature is mainly composed of poetry, especially the verse forms of the ghazal and the nazm, which are lyrical expressions of love, longing, and mysticism. Some of the most famous Urdu poets are Mirza Ghalib, Mir Taqi Mir, Allama Iqbal, Faiz Ahmed Faiz, and Ahmad Faraz.
- Urdu literature also includes prose genres, such as the short story, the novel, the essay, and the biography. Some of the prominent Urdu prose writers are Saadat Hasan Manto, Ismat Chughtai, Qurratulain Hyder, Khwaja Ahmad Abbas, and Munshi Premchand.
- Urdu literature has a rich and diverse history, spanning from the medieval period to the modern era, and reflecting the social, political, and cultural changes in the Indian subcontinent. Urdu literature has also contributed to the development of other literary traditions, such as Hindi literature, Bengali literature, and Pakistani literature.
