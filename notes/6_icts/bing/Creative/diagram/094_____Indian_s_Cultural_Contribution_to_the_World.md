### Indian's Cultural Contribution to the World

India is one of the oldest and most diverse civilizations in the world. It has a rich and varied cultural heritage that has influenced many aspects of human development. Some of the major contributions of India to the world are:

- **Mathematics**: India is the birthplace of the decimal system, the concept of zero, the use of negative numbers, algebra, trigonometry, geometry, calculus, and many other branches of mathematics. India also developed the concept of infinity, the value of pi, the Fibonacci sequence, and the binary system .
- **Architecture**: India has a remarkable architectural legacy that spans from ancient temples, palaces, forts, and monuments to modern skyscrapers, bridges, and dams. India is known for its distinctive styles of architecture, such as the Indo-Islamic, the Mughal, the Rajput, the Dravidian, the Buddhist, the Jain, and the Sikh. Some of the world-famous architectural wonders of India are the Taj Mahal, the Qutub Minar, the Khajuraho temples, the Hampi ruins, the Ajanta and Ellora caves, and the Golden Temple .
- **Textiles**: India is one of the largest producers and exporters of textiles in the world. India has a long history of textile manufacturing and innovation, dating back to the Indus Valley Civilization. India is the origin of cotton, silk, muslin, chintz, calico, and many other fabrics that are widely used today. India is also known for its exquisite embroidery, weaving, dyeing, printing, and painting techniques, such as the Kalamkari, the Bandhani, the Ikat, the Batik, and the Tie and Dye .
- **Medicine**: India is the home of Ayurveda, one of the oldest systems of medicine in the world. Ayurveda is based on the principles of balance, harmony, and natural healing. India also developed the concepts of surgery, anesthesia, plastic surgery, dentistry, and vaccination. India is credited with the invention of the surgical instruments, the sutures, the cataract surgery, the rhinoplasty, the extraction of teeth, and the inoculation against smallpox .
- **Language**: India is a multilingual nation, with 22 official languages and hundreds of regional languages and dialects. India is the source of many languages that belong to the Indo-European and the Dravidian families, such as Sanskrit, Hindi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, and Malayalam. India also influenced the development of many other languages, such as English, Persian, Arabic, and Southeast Asian languages. India is the origin of many words, such as guru, yoga, karma, nirvana, mantra, avatar, and jungle .
- **Art**: India has a rich and diverse artistic tradition that encompasses various forms of expression, such as painting, sculpture, literature, music, dance, and theatre. India is known for its vibrant and colorful art forms, such as the Madhubani, the Warli, the Pattachitra, the Tanjore, and the Mysore paintings, the stone, metal, and wood sculptures, the epics, the poetry, the novels, and the folktales, the classical, folk, and modern music, the Bharatanatyam, the Kathak, the Odissi, and the Bhangra dances, and the Ramleela, the Yakshagana, the Kathakali, and the Nautanki theatre .
- **Religion**: India is the birthplace of four major religions of the world: Hinduism, Buddhism, Jainism, and Sikhism. India is also the land of many saints, sages, gurus, and mystics, who have spread their teachings and philosophies across the globe. India is a place of pilgrimage and worship for millions of people, who visit the sacred sites, such as the Varanasi, the Bodh Gaya, the Amritsar, and the Mecca Masjid. India is also a place of religious diversity and tolerance, where people of different faiths coexist and celebrate their festivals, such as the Diwali, the Holi, the Eid, and the Christmas .
