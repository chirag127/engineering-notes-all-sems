The Six Systems of Indian Philosophy are Nyaya, Vaisheshika, Samkhya, Yoga, Purva Mimamsa and Vedanta or Uttara Mimamsa. Each of these systems differs in one way or the other in terms of its concepts, phenomena, laws and dogmas.
