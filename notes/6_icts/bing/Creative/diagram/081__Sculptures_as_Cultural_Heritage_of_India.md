Sculptures are one of the oldest and most enduring forms of artistic expression in India. They reflect the rich cultural and religious diversity of the Indian subcontinent, as well as the influences of various historical periods and regions. Sculptures in India can be made of stone, metal, terracotta, wood, ivory, or other materials, and can depict human, animal, or divine figures, as well as abstract or geometric shapes.
