Puranas are a vast genre of Indian literature that contain stories, legends, myths, genealogies, cosmology, theology, and philosophy related to Hinduism and Jainism. They are considered as Smritis, or secondary scriptures, and are composed in Sanskrit and other Indian languages. There are 18 major Puranas and 18 minor Puranas, each focusing on a different aspect of Hinduism or Jainism.
