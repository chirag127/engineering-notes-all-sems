The Upanishads are late Vedic texts that deal with the philosophical and mystical aspects of Hinduism. They explore the nature of the self (atman) and its relation to the supreme reality (brahman). They also contain various teachings on ethics, meditation, cosmology, and metaphysics. The Upanishads are considered the essence of the Vedas and the source of Vedanta, one of the six schools of Hindu philosophy.
