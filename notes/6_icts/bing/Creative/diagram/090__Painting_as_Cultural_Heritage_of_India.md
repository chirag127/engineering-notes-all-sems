Painting as Cultural Heritage of India

Painting is one of the oldest and most diverse forms of Indian art. It reflects the rich cultural and religious heritage of India, as well as the influences of various historical periods and regions. Painting in India can be broadly classified into two categories: mural painting and miniature painting. Mural painting refers to the art of painting on walls, ceilings, or other large surfaces, usually in temples, palaces, or caves. Miniature painting refers to the art of painting on small surfaces, such as paper, cloth, ivory, or palm leaf, usually for personal or courtly use.
