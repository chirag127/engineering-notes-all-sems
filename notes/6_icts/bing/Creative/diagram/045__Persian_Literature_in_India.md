### Persian Literature in India

Persian literature in India refers to the literary works produced by Persian-speaking writers and poets in the Indian subcontinent, especially during the Delhi Sultanate and the Mughal Empire. Persian was the official language and the language of culture and literature in these empires, and it influenced the development of many regional languages, such as Urdu, Hindi, Punjabi, Kashmiri, Bengali and Deccani. Persian literature in India also reflects the political, social, religious and cultural aspects of the Indo-Persian civilization that emerged from the interaction of Persian and Indian elements.
