Kannada literature is the literature written in the Kannada language, a member of the Dravidian family spoken mainly in the Indian state of Karnataka. It has a history of more than one and a half millennia, and has produced many notable works in various genres, such as poetry, prose, drama, and grammar. Kannada literature can be broadly divided into three linguistic phases: Old (450–1200 CE), Middle (1200–1700 CE) and Modern (1700–present); and three religious categories: Jain, Lingayatism and Vaishnava.
