The contract theory of origin of state is a theory that explains how the state emerged from a voluntary agreement among individuals who lived in a state of nature, without any political authority or law. The theory was developed by philosophers such as Thomas Hobbes, John Locke and Jean-Jacques Rousseau, who had different views on the nature of the state of nature, the terms of the contract and the role of the state.
