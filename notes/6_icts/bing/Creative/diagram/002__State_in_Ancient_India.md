According to the search results, there were three main types of states in ancient India: monarchy, republic, and oligarchy. Monarchy was the most prevalent type of state, where a king or a queen ruled over a territory. Republic was a type of state where the people elected their representatives or leaders, who formed a council or an assembly. Oligarchy was a type of state where a few powerful people or families controlled the affairs of the state.
