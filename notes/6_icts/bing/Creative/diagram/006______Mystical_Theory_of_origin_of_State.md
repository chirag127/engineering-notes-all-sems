#### Mystical Theory of Origin of State

- The mystical theory of origin of state is also known as the **divine origin theory** or the **theory of divine right of kings** .
- The exponents of this theory believe that the state did not come into being by any effort of man, but by the **will of God** .
- According to this theory, God created the state and chose some people to rule over others as his representatives .
- The rulers were therefore **sacred** and **infallible**, and their authority was **absolute** and **unquestionable** .
- The subjects had to obey the rulers as a **religious duty**, and any resistance or rebellion was a **sin** .
- This theory was used to justify the **monarchical** and **theocratic** forms of government in ancient and medieval times .
- Some examples of this theory are the **Egyptian pharaohs**, the **Chinese emperors**, the **Roman caesars**, the **Islamic caliphs**, the **Christian popes** and the **European kings** .
- This theory has been **criticized** and **rejected** by modern political thinkers for being **unscientific**, **irrational**, **undemocratic** and **oppressive** .
