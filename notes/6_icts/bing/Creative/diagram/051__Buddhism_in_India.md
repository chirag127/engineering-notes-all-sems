Buddhism is a religion or philosophy that originated in India in the 5th century BCE by Siddhartha Gautama, also known as the Buddha. Buddhists believe that human life is suffering and that the way to end suffering is to follow the teachings of the Buddha, which are based on the Four Noble Truths and the Noble Eightfold Path. Buddhists also believe in karma, rebirth, and nirvana, which is the ultimate state of liberation from the cycle of suffering. Buddhism has many branches and traditions, such as Theravada, Mahayana, and Vajrayana, and has spread to many countries in Asia and beyond.
