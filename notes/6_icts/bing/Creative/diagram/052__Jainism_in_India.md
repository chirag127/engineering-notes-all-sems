Jainism is one of the oldest religions of India, with a history of more than 2500 years. It teaches the principle of nonviolence and respect for all living beings. Jains follow the teachings of 24 tirthankaras, or spiritual leaders, the last of whom was Mahavira. Jains are a minority in India, but they have contributed significantly to the culture, art, literature, and social welfare of the country.
