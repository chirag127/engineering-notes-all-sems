Brahmi script is an ancient writing system of South Asia that appeared as a fully developed script in the third century BCE. It is the ancestor of many modern scripts of South and Southeast Asia, such as Devanagari, Tamil, Sinhala, Thai, and Khmer. Brahmi is an abugida, which means that it uses diacritical marks to indicate vowels attached to consonant symbols. Brahmi script is also mentioned in the ancient texts of Hinduism, Jainism, and Buddhism.
