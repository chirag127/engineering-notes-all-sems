Engineering and Architecture as Cultural Heritage of India
---
India has a rich and diverse architectural heritage that reflects its history, culture, and geography. India's architecture is influenced by various factors, such as:

- The religious beliefs and practices of different communities, such as Hinduism, Buddhism, Jainism, Islam, Christianity, Sikhism, and Zoroastrianism.
- The political and economic conditions of different periods, such as the Mauryan Empire, the Gupta Empire, the Delhi Sultanate, the Mughal Empire, the Maratha Empire, the British Raj, and the post-independence era.
- The climatic and environmental conditions of different regions, such as the Himalayas, the Thar Desert, the Gangetic Plain, the Deccan Plateau, and the coastal areas.
- The artistic and aesthetic preferences of different rulers, patrons, and builders, such as Ashoka, Chandragupta II, Qutub-ud-din Aibak, Shah Jahan, Shivaji, Tipu Sultan, and Lutyens.
