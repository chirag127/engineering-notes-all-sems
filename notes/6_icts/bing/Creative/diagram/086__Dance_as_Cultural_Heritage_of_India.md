Dance is an ancient and celebrated cultural tradition in India. It reflects the diversity and richness of the Indian culture and history. There are two main types of dance in India: classical and folk. Classical dances are based on ancient texts and scriptures, and are performed with elaborate costumes, makeup, and gestures. Folk dances are more spontaneous and expressive, and are performed by various communities and regions across the country. Both types of dance have a religious and spiritual significance, and often depict stories from the Hindu epics and mythologies.

Each dance form has its own unique features, such as:

- Bharatanatyam is a dance of Tamil Nadu in southern India. It traces its origins back to the Natyashastra, an ancient treatise on theatre written by the mythic priest Bharata. It is characterized by bent legs, rhythmic feet, and expressive hand gestures.
- Kathakali is a dance of Kerala in southwestern India. It is a highly stylized dance drama, with heavy makeup and colorful costumes. It depicts stories from the Hindu epics and mythologies, such as the Ramayana and the Mahabharata.
- Kathak is a dance of northern India. It is a dance of love, with intricate footwork and graceful movements. It originated from the kathakars, or storytellers, who used to recite mythological tales in rhythm with music and dance.
- Bhangra is a dance of Punjab in northwestern India. It is a lively and energetic dance, performed during the harvest festival of Baisakhi. It involves jumping, kicking, and swinging of arms, accompanied by drums and folk songs.
- Garba is a dance of Gujarat in western India. It is a circular dance, performed by women during the nine nights of Navratri, a festival dedicated to the goddess Durga. It involves clapping and spinning, with colorful costumes and accessories.
- Lavani is a dance of Maharashtra in western India. It is a sensual and seductive dance, performed by women in saris and jewelry. It involves fast-paced movements and suggestive lyrics, often dealing with social issues and love affairs.

These are some of the examples of the dance as cultural heritage of India. There are many more forms of dance that showcase the diversity and beauty of the Indian culture. Dance is a way of expressing emotions, beliefs, and values, and also a way of preserving and celebrating the traditions and history of India.
