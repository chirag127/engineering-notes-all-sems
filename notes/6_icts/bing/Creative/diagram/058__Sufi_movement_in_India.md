The Sufi movement in India was a socio-religious movement that started in the 13th century and spread to different regions of the subcontinent. The Sufis were Muslim mystics who sought to find the truth of divine love and knowledge through direct personal experience of God. They followed different orders or silsilas, each with its own spiritual leader or pir. The Sufis also interacted with the Hindu and Sikh traditions and influenced the development of the Bhakti movement. The Sufis were known for their poetry, music, dance, and service to humanity.
