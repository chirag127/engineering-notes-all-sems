There are many famous Sanskrit authors in India who have contributed to the rich and diverse literature of the ancient language. Some of them are:

- Valmiki, who is regarded as the first poet and the author of the epic Ramayana .
- Vyasa, who is credited with composing the Mahabharata, the longest epic poem in the world, and the Vedas, the sacred scriptures of Hinduism .
- Kalidasa, who is considered as the greatest poet and dramatist of classical Sanskrit literature. He wrote three plays, two epics and several lyrical poems, such as Abhijnanasakuntalam, Raghuvamsa, Meghaduta and Kumarasambhava  .
- Bhartrihari, who is known for his three collections of verses on love, renunciation and wisdom, called Sringara, Vairagya and Niti Satakas .
- Bhasa, who is one of the earliest and most prolific Sanskrit dramatists, whose thirteen plays were rediscovered in the 20th century. He wrote on various themes, such as the Mahabharata, the Ramayana, the Puranas and the Buddhist legends .
- Magha, who is famous for his epic poem Sisupalavadha, which narrates the story of the slaying of Sisupala by Krishna at the Rajasuya sacrifice .
- Jayadeva, who is celebrated for his lyrical poem Gita Govinda, which depicts the love of Krishna and Radha in twelve cantos .
- Shankaracharya, who is revered as one of the greatest philosophers and teachers of Hinduism. He wrote many commentaries on the Vedas, the Upanishads and the Brahma Sutras, as well as original works, such as Vivekachudamani and Saundaryalahari .
