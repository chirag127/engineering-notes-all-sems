### Kautilya’s Arthashastra

- Kautilya’s Arthashastra is an ancient Indian treatise on statecraft, political science, economic policy and military strategy .
- It is attributed to Kautilya, also known as Chanakya, Vishnugupta, or the Prime Minister of the Mauryan Empire   .
- It is considered to be one of the earliest and most influential texts on politics and governance in India .
- It covers various topics such as the role and duties of the king, the structure and administration of the state, the principles of diplomacy and war, the laws and justice system, the taxation and revenue system, the trade and commerce system, the welfare and development of the people, and the ethics and morality of the ruler and the subjects  .
- It is based on a realistic and pragmatic approach to politics, economics and society, and reflects a deep understanding of human nature, psychology and behavior  .
- It advocates peace over war, but also advises the ruler to be prepared and vigilant for any external or internal threats, and to use a network of spies and informers for intelligence and security  .
- It is divided into 15 books, 150 chapters and 180 topics, and contains about 6000 verses in Sanskrit  .
- It was rediscovered in the 20th century by a scholar named R. Shamasastry, who published the first English translation in 1915  .
- It has influenced many political leaders and thinkers in India and abroad, and has been compared to the works of Machiavelli, Hobbes, Sun Tzu and Aristotle  .
