#### Mystical Theory of origin of State

- The mystical theory of origin of state is also known as the **divine origin theory** or the **theory of divine right of kings** .
- According to this theory, the state is created by **God** and the ruler is his **agent** or **representative** on earth .
- The ruler derives his authority from **God** and is responsible to **God** alone for his actions .
- The subjects have to obey the ruler as a **religious duty** and any resistance to his authority is a **sin** .
- The ruler is **above the law** and no subject has any right to question his authority or his actions .
- This theory was prevalent in the ancient and medieval times, when religion and politics were combined in the person of the ruler .
- Examples of this theory are found in the **Old Testament** of the Bible, the **Manusmriti** of ancient India, the **Papacy** of medieval Europe, the **Caliphate** of the Muslim world, and the **Dalai Lama** of Tibet .
- This theory is based on **faith** and **tradition** and does not have any **scientific** or **rational** basis .
- This theory has been **criticized** and **rejected** by modern thinkers, who argue that the state is a **human** institution and the ruler is a **servant** of the people, not a **master** .
