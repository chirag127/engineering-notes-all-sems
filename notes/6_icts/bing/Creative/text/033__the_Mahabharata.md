### The Mahabharata

- The Mahabharata is an ancient Sanskrit epic poem that tells the story of the kingdom of Kurus and the war between two groups of cousins, the Kauravas and the Pandavas, for the throne of Hastinapura     .
- The Mahabharata is regarded as both a historical account of Hinduism's birth and a code of ethics for the faithful .
- The Mahabharata consists of almost 100,000 couplets, divided into 18 sections, plus a supplement titled Harivamsha. It is about seven times the length of the Iliad and the Odyssey combined.
- The authorship of the Mahabharata is traditionally ascribed to the sage Vyasa, who appears in the work as the grandfather of the Kauravas and the Pandavas. The date and the historical occurrence of the war are much debated.
- The main plot of the Mahabharata is as follows    :
  - The blindness of Dhritarashtra, the elder of two princes, causes him to be passed over in favour of his brother Pandu as king on their father's death.
  - A curse prevents Pandu from fathering children, and his wife Kunti asks the gods to father children in Pandu's name. As a result, the god Dharma fathers Yudhishthira, the Wind fathers Bhima, Indra fathers Arjuna, and the Ashvins father Nakula and Sahadeva. These five sons are known as the Pandavas. Pandu's second wife, Madri, also gives birth to two sons, but she and Pandu die soon after.
  - Dhritarashtra has 100 sons, known as the Kauravas, who are all demons. The eldest son, Duryodhana, leads the other 99 brothers and has a seething hatred for the Pandavas.
  - The enmity and jealousy between the cousins forces the Pandavas to leave the kingdom when their father dies. During their exile, they jointly marry Draupadi, who is born out of a sacrificial fire, and meet their cousin Krishna, who remains their friend and companion thereafter.
  - The Pandavas return to the kingdom, but they are again exiled to the forest for 13 years, when Yudhishthira loses everything in a game of dice with Duryodhana. Duryodhana cheats in the game with the help of his uncle Shakuni, and humiliates Draupadi by trying to disrobe her in public.
  - During their exile, the Pandavas study with Brahmins and train in warfare. Arjuna is granted a celestial weapon by the god Indra. Draupadi is kidnapped by a king named Jayadratha, but she is rescued by the Pandavas. Bhima kills a demon named Hidimba and marries his sister Hidimbi, who gives birth to a son named Ghatotkacha. Arjuna also marries a princess named Subhadra, who is Krishna's sister, and they have a son named Abhimanyu.
  - In the 13th year, the Pandavas disguise themselves and take refuge in the king Virata's palace. They have various adventures there, such as Arjuna helping Virata's son win back cattle from thieves, and Bhima killing a wrestler named Kichaka, who tries to molest Draupadi. The Pandavas manage to complete their exile without being detected by the Kauravas.
  - After the exile, the Pandavas ask for their kingdom back, but Duryodhana refuses to give it up. A war is inevitable, and both sides prepare for it. Krishna advises Yudhishthira to ask for a peaceful transfer of power first, but Duryodhana denies it. Krishna offers to help either side, but only as a charioteer or a soldier. The Pandavas choose Krishna as their charioteer, and the Kauravas get his army.
