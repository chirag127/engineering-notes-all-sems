### Theatre as Cultural Heritage of India

- Theatre is a form of art that combines music, dance, dialogue, and spectacle to create a live performance that entertains and educates the audience.
- Theatre in India has a long and rich history that dates back to the Vedic period, when religious rituals and hymns were performed as a form of worship and communication with the gods.
- The earliest text on theatre in India is the Natya Shastra, written by Bharata Muni around 500 BC. It is a comprehensive treatise that covers various aspects of theatre, such as aesthetics, dramaturgy, stagecraft, music, dance, and acting .
- Theatre in India has evolved through three main periods: the classical period, the traditional period, and the modern period.
  - The classical period (from 500 BC to 1000 AD) was marked by the development of Sanskrit drama, which followed the rules and conventions of the Natya Shastra. The most famous playwrights of this period were Kalidasa, Bhasa, Shudraka, and Vishakhadatta. Their plays were based on epics, legends, history, and social issues, and were performed in royal courts and temples.
  - The traditional period (from 1000 AD to 1800 AD) was characterized by the emergence and growth of various regional forms of theatre, such as Kutiyattam, Kathakali, Yakshagana, Bhavai, Nautanki, Tamasha, Jatra, and Ramlila. These forms were influenced by local cultures, languages, religions, and folk traditions, and were performed in rural and urban settings for the masses. They used music, dance, masks, costumes, and props to create colorful and lively spectacles  .
  - The modern period (from 1800 AD to present) was initiated by the introduction of Western theatre and influences, such as realism, naturalism, social drama, and experimental theatre. The colonial rule, the nationalist movement, the independence struggle, and the post-independence issues shaped the themes and styles of modern Indian theatre. Some of the prominent playwrights and directors of this period are Rabindranath Tagore, Mohan Rakesh, Vijay Tendulkar, Girish Karnad, Badal Sircar, Habib Tanvir, and Satyadev Dubey .
- Theatre in India is a cultural heritage that reflects the diversity, creativity, and identity of the Indian people. It is a medium of expression, education, and entertainment that has survived and adapted to the changing times and contexts. It is a living tradition that continues to evolve and innovate, and to contribute to the global theatre scene .
