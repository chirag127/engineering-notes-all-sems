## Module 1- Society State and Polity in India

- This module aims to provide an overview of the historical and contemporary aspects of society, state and polity in India.
- Society refers to the network of social relationships, norms, values, beliefs, and practices that shape the collective life of a group of people.
- State refers to the political organization of a territory, with a government, laws, institutions, and administrative structures that exercise authority and maintain order.
- Polity refers to the form and structure of the state, as well as the principles and practices of governance, democracy, and citizenship.
- India is a diverse and complex society, with multiple religions, languages, ethnicities, castes, and regions, each with its own history and culture.
- India is also a federal and parliamentary democracy, with a constitution that guarantees fundamental rights and freedoms to its citizens, and a multi-party system that reflects the pluralism of its society.
- India's society, state and polity have evolved over time, influenced by various factors such as colonialism, nationalism, social movements, economic development, globalization, and regionalism.
- Some of the key topics that this module will cover are:

  - The historical origins and development of Indian society, state and polity, from ancient to modern times.
  - The diversity and unity of Indian society, and the challenges and opportunities of managing social diversity and inequality.
  - The constitutional and institutional framework of Indian state and polity, and the role and functioning of various organs and agencies of government.
  - The political culture and behavior of Indian citizens, and the issues and trends of electoral politics, party system, and social movements.
  - The major debates and controversies in Indian society, state and polity, such as secularism, federalism, nationalism, regionalism, and development.
