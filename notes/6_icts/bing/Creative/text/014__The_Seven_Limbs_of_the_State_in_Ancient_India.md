### The Seven Limbs of the State in Ancient India

- The Seven Limbs of the State (Saptanga) is a political concept that was formulated by Kautilya in his treatise Arthashastra  .
- It refers to the seven essential organs or elements of a state that are necessary for its existence and functioning .
- The seven limbs are:

  - King (Svamin): The king is the supreme authority and the head of the state. He is responsible for the peace, justice and stability of the state. He is independent and has the power to appoint and dismiss his ministers and officials .
  - Amatya (Minister or Bureaucrat): The amatya is the second in command and the chief adviser of the king. He assists the king in administration, policy-making and diplomacy. He is selected on the basis of merit and loyalty .
  - Janapada (Territory or Population): The janapada is the land and the people of the state. It is the source of revenue and manpower for the state. It is protected by the king and his army from external and internal threats .
  - Durga (Fort or Capital): The durga is the fortified city or the capital of the state. It is the center of political, economic and cultural activities. It is the seat of the king and his court. It is also the place of refuge and defense in times of war .
  - Kosa (Treasure or Finance): The kosa is the wealth and the resources of the state. It is accumulated by taxation, trade and conquest. It is used for the maintenance of the state, the welfare of the people and the expansion of the state .
  - Danda (Coercive Authority or Army): The danda is the force and the power of the state. It is the army and the police of the state. It is used for enforcing the law, maintaining the order and defending the state from enemies .
  - Mitra (Ally or Friend): The mitra is the external support and the partner of the state. It is another state or a group of states that have a friendly and cooperative relationship with the state. It is used for mutual benefit, assistance and protection .

- According to Kautilya, the state is like a living organism that depends on the proper functioning of its limbs. If any of the limbs is weak or defective, the state will suffer and decline. Therefore, the king should strive to strengthen and balance all the limbs of the state .
