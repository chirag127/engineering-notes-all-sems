### The Ramayana

- The Ramayana is a Hindu epic poem that narrates the life and adventures of Rama, the prince of Kosala and an incarnation of the god Vishnu .
- The main theme of the Ramayana is the triumph of good over evil, as Rama battles and defeats the demon king Ravana, who kidnaps his wife Sita .
- The Ramayana consists of seven books or kandas, each with varying numbers of chapters or sargas .
- The Ramayana is attributed to the sage Valmiki, who is believed to have composed it in Sanskrit between the 5th and 4th centuries BCE .
- The Ramayana has been translated, adapted, and retold in various languages and cultures, and has influenced literature, art, music, and film .

The following is a brief summary of the main events of the Ramayana:

- Book One: Rama is born as the eldest son of King Dasaratha of Kosala, who is childless and performs a sacrifice to obtain sons. Rama is accompanied by his three brothers, Bharata, Lakshmana, and Shatrughna, who are also sons of Dasaratha by his three wives. Rama is recognized as a virtuous and heroic prince, and he marries Sita, the daughter of King Janaka of Mithila, after breaking the bow of Shiva in a contest. Rama is chosen by his father to be the heir to the throne, but his stepmother Kaikeyi, influenced by her maid Manthara, demands that Dasaratha banish Rama to the forest for 14 years and crown Bharata instead. Dasaratha reluctantly agrees, and Rama leaves with Sita and Lakshmana, who insist on accompanying him. Bharata refuses to accept the throne and goes to the forest to persuade Rama to return, but Rama refuses to disobey his father's command. Bharata takes Rama's sandals and places them on the throne as a symbol of Rama's authority, and vows to rule as Rama's regent until he returns  .
- Book Two: Rama, Sita, and Lakshmana wander in the forest and meet various sages and ascetics, who welcome them and offer them advice and protection. They also encounter and slay many demons and monsters, who are the allies of Ravana, the king of Lanka and the leader of the demons. Ravana hears of Sita's beauty and decides to abduct her, with the help of his uncle Maricha, who disguises himself as a golden deer and lures Rama and Lakshmana away from Sita. Ravana then appears as a mendicant and kidnaps Sita, while Rama and Lakshmana are searching for the deer. Sita resists Ravana's advances and remains faithful to Rama, while Ravana tries to persuade her to marry him. Rama and Lakshmana are devastated by Sita's abduction and search for her, aided by a vulture named Jatayu, who dies after fighting with Ravana. They also meet a monkey named Hanuman, who is the son of the wind god and the minister of Sugriva, the king of the monkeys. Hanuman becomes Rama's loyal devotee and helps him to form an alliance with Sugriva, who promises to assist Rama in finding and rescuing Sita  .
- Book Three: Rama helps Sugriva to regain his kingdom from his brother Vali, who had usurped the throne and taken Sugriva's wife. Sugriva then sends his monkey army to search for Sita in all directions, with Hanuman as the leader of the southward group. Hanuman and his companions reach the southern coast and discover that Sita is in Lanka, across the ocean. Hanuman leaps over the ocean and reaches Lanka, where he finds Sita in a grove, guarded by demonesses. He gives her Rama's ring as a token and assures her that Rama will come to rescue her.
