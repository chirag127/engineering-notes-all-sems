### Malayalam Literature

- Malayalam literature is the literature written in Malayalam, a South-Dravidian language spoken in the Indian state of Kerala.
- The earliest extant literary work in Malayalam is Ramacharitam, a narrative poem based on the Ramayana, composed in the late 12th or early 13th century .
- Malayalam literature has a rich and diverse history, influenced by Sanskrit, Tamil, Arabic, Portuguese, English and other languages .
- Malayalam literature can be broadly divided into four periods: classical, medieval, modern and contemporary.
- The classical period (13th to 16th century) witnessed the emergence of various genres such as Manipravalam, Champu, Sandesa Kavya, Kathakali and Thullal . Some of the prominent writers of this period are Ezhuthachan, Kunchan Nambiar, Melpathur Narayana Bhattathiri and Unnayi Warrier .
- The medieval period (17th to 19th century) saw the development of prose, novel, drama, biography, travelogue and journalism in Malayalam literature . Some of the notable writers of this period are Thunchaththu Ramanujan Ezhuthachan, Kottarathil Sankunni, Kerala Varma Valiya Koil Thampuran and C. V. Raman Pillai .
- The modern period (20th century) was marked by the emergence of social realism, romanticism, progressive movement, modernism, postmodernism and other literary trends in Malayalam literature . Some of the influential writers of this period are Kumaran Asan, Vallathol Narayana Menon, Ulloor S. Parameswara Iyer, G. Sankara Kurup, Thakazhi Sivasankara Pillai, Vaikom Muhammad Basheer, M. T. Vasudevan Nair, O. V. Vijayan and Kamala Surayya .
- The contemporary period (21st century) is characterized by the diversity and experimentation of Malayalam literature, reflecting the changes in society, culture, politics and technology . Some of the prominent writers of this period are M. Mukundan, Sethu, K. R. Meera, Benyamin, Anand, N. S. Madhavan and K. Satchidanandan .
- Malayalam literature has also produced many acclaimed poets, critics, translators, editors, publishers and scholars who have contributed to the enrichment and recognition of the language and literature  .
