### Drama as Cultural Heritage of India

- Drama is a form of performing arts, where stories are enacted by the utilisation of dialogue, music, gesture, dance, and sound.
- Drama is one of the oldest and most diverse forms of cultural expression in India, dating back to the ancient Vedic period .
- Drama in India has been influenced by various religious, social, and linguistic traditions, as well as by regional and historical factors .
- Drama in India can be broadly classified into two categories: classical and folk.
- Classical drama in India is based on the Sanskrit theatre, which is the earliest existing form of drama and theatre in the world . Sanskrit theatre was codified by Bharata Muni in his treatise Natyashastra, which is considered the fifth Veda by Hindus. Sanskrit theatre follows the principles of rasa (aesthetic emotion), dhvani (suggestion), alamkara (ornamentation), and abhinaya (acting). Some of the famous Sanskrit playwrights are Kalidasa, Bhasa, Sudraka, Vishakhadatta, and Harsha .
- Folk drama in India is a diverse and vibrant form of theatre, which reflects the local cultures, languages, and traditions of various regions and communities in India. Folk drama is usually performed in open-air venues, such as village squares, courtyards, or temples, and involves the participation of the audience. Folk drama incorporates elements of music, dance, mime, puppetry, masks, and costumes, and often deals with themes of social satire, myth, legend, and history. Some of the popular forms of folk drama in India are Nautanki, Tamasha, Jatra, Bhavai, Yakshagana, and Therukoothu.
- Drama in India has also been influenced by the colonial and post-colonial periods, which introduced new genres, styles, and themes to the Indian theatre scene. Some of the notable modern Indian playwrights are Rabindranath Tagore, Mohan Rakesh, Vijay Tendulkar, Girish Karnad, Badal Sircar, and Mahesh Dattani.
- Drama in India is a rich and diverse cultural heritage, which showcases the creativity, diversity, and dynamism of the Indian people and their history. Drama in India is also a medium of social commentary, cultural critique, and artistic expression, which reflects the contemporary issues and challenges faced by the Indian society .
