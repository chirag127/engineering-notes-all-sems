### Hindi Literature

Hindi literature is the literature written in the Hindi language, which includes various dialects and scripts. Hindi literature has a long and rich history, dating back to the 11th century. Some of the main features and topics of Hindi literature are:

- Hindi literature is divided into four periods: Adikal (the early period), Bhaktikal (the devotional period), Ritikal (the scholastic period) and Adhunik (the modern period).
- Hindi literature is influenced by Sanskrit, Prakrit, Pali, Persian, Arabic, Urdu and English languages and literatures.
- Hindi literature reflects the religious, social, cultural and political aspects of India, such as Hinduism, Islam, Buddhism, Jainism, Sikhism, nationalism, colonialism, feminism, etc.
- Hindi literature has various genres and forms, such as poetry, prose, drama, novel, short story, essay, biography, autobiography, travelogue, etc.
- Hindi literature has produced many eminent writers and works, such as Kabir, Tulsidas, Surdas, Mirabai, Premchand, Jaishankar Prasad, Mahadevi Varma, Harivansh Rai Bachchan, Munshi Premchand, Bhisham Sahni, Nirmal Verma, etc.
