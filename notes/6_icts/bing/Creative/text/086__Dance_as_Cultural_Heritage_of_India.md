### Dance as Cultural Heritage of India

Dance is an ancient and celebrated cultural tradition in India. It reflects the diversity and richness of Indian culture, history and mythology. Dance in India can be classified into two broad categories: classical and folk. Classical dances are based on ancient texts and codified rules, while folk dances are spontaneous and expressive, often performed during festivals and celebrations. Here are some of the main features of dance as cultural heritage of India:

- Classical dances of India are derived from the Natyashastra, an ancient treatise on theatre written by the sage Bharata. It describes the theory and practice of dance, drama and music, and lays down the principles of aesthetics, expression and performance. There are eight recognized classical dance forms in India: Bharatanatyam, Kathak, Kathakali, Kuchipudi, Manipuri, Mohiniyattam, Odissi and Sattriya. Each dance form has its own style, technique, repertoire and regional origin. Classical dances are usually performed on stage, with elaborate costumes, makeup, music and props. They depict stories from Hindu epics, legends and scriptures, using gestures, facial expressions and body movements. Classical dances are considered as a form of worship and devotion, as well as a medium of artistic expression.   

- Folk dances of India are diverse and varied, reflecting the local traditions, customs and beliefs of different regions and communities. They are often performed in groups, with simple costumes and accessories, and accompanied by folk songs and instruments. Folk dances are lively, energetic and joyful, and convey the emotions and sentiments of the people. They are usually associated with specific occasions, such as harvest, weddings, seasons, festivals and rituals. Some of the popular folk dances of India are Bhangra, Garba, Dandiya, Ghoomar, Bihu, Lavani, Chhau, Kalbelia and Dumhal. Folk dances are an integral part of the rural and tribal culture of India, and a source of entertainment and social bonding.  

- Dance in India is not only a cultural heritage, but also a living and evolving art form. It has been influenced by various historical, social and political factors, such as invasions, migrations, colonialism, nationalism, globalization and modernization. Dance in India has also been influenced by other art forms, such as cinema, theatre, literature and music. Dance in India has also influenced and inspired other dance forms across the world, such as ballet, contemporary, jazz and hip-hop. Dance in India is a dynamic and creative expression of the human spirit, and a testimony of the diversity and unity of India.
