### Mathematics in India and Ancient India

- Mathematics or 'Ganita' was the 'Science of Calculations' in ancient India.
- Mathematics was considered as one of the highest sciences and the head of all Vedas and shastras.
- The origin of ancient mathematics can be traced to the Indus valley civilization, which had a sophisticated system of weights and measures, geometry, and decimal notation.
- The classical period of Indian mathematics (400 CE to 1200 CE) saw important contributions by scholars like Aryabhata, Brahmagupta, Bhaskara II, and Varāhamihira .
- Some of the achievements of Indian mathematics include:
  - The concept of zero and its use as a placeholder and a number .
  - The decimal place-value system and the use of symbols for digits .
  - The development of algebra, including quadratic equations, indeterminate equations, and the concept of negative numbers .
  - The development of trigonometry, including the sine, cosine, and tangent functions, and the use of the Pythagorean theorem .
  - The development of arithmetic, including fractions, square roots, and the concept of infinity .
  - The development of geometry, including the approximation of pi, the area of a circle, and the volume of a sphere .
  - The development of astronomy, including the calculation of the solar and lunar eclipses, the heliocentric model of the solar system, and the sidereal year .
- Indian mathematics influenced the mathematics of other civilizations, such as the Arabs, the Greeks, the Chinese, and the Europeans  .
