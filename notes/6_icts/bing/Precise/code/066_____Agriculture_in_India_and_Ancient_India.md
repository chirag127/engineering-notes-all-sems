### Agriculture in India and Ancient India

- Agriculture in India has a long history, with evidence of domestication of plants and animals dating back to 9000 BC .
- The Mehrgarh period sites, which date back to around 8000-6000 BC, provide evidence of early agricultural practices in India .
- In ancient India, agriculture was closely associated with religious customs and traditions .
- The Vedic people were skilled in cultivation and succeeded in agriculture .
- In South India, a wide range of crops were cultivated, including rice, sugarcane, millets, black pepper, various grains, coconuts, beans, cotton, plantain, tamarind, and sandalwood .
- Agriculture in the Indian subcontinent was the largest producer of wheat and grain .
- Implements and techniques were developed for agriculture, leading to settled life .
- The Ganges River valley became an increasingly developed agricultural region after about 3000 BP .
