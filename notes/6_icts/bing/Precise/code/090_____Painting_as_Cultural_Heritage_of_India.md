### Painting as Cultural Heritage of India

- Indian art is among the oldest in the world, with archaeologists discovering paintings that were created over 70,000 years ago .
- Indian paintings hold a special place in history and continue to influence artists to this day .
- Traditional art in India depicted cultural, religious, and political developments through clever metaphors .
- Indian art forms, including paintings, are a part of the country's rich culture and heritage .
- Beautiful bursts of color with vivid patterns and symbolic images capture Indian culture beautifully on the canvas .
- There are many different types of Indian paintings, including Madhubani, Warli, and Murals, each with its own unique style and history .
- Indian paintings have a rich history and diversity, with the earliest known Indian paintings dating back to pre-historic times, such as those found in places like Bhimbetka rock shelters in Madhya Pradesh .
- India has one of the world’s largest collections of songs, music, dance, theatre, folk traditions, performing arts, rites and rituals, paintings and writings that are known as the ‘Intangible Cultural Heritage’ (ICH) of humanity .
