#### Evolutionary Theory of origin of State
The Evolutionary Theory of the origin of the state is one of the several theories that attempt to explain how states or governments came into existence. According to this theory, the state evolved from the family, which gradually grew in size and complexity as population increased and societies became more organized.

1. The theory suggests that the earliest form of human organization was the family, which was headed by the father or the eldest male member.
2. As families grew in size and number, they formed clans or tribes, which were larger kinship groups.
3. As population increased and societies became more complex, the need for a more formal system of governance arose.
4. The theory proposes that the state emerged as a natural progression from the family and clan/tribe system, as a means of providing for the common defense and welfare of the people.
5. The state, according to this theory, evolved gradually over time, as societies became more complex and the need for a more formal system of governance became apparent.

This theory is based on the idea that the state is a natural and necessary part of human society, and that it evolved gradually over time as societies became more complex. It is one of several theories that attempt to explain the origin of the state, and is often contrasted with other theories such as the Divine Right Theory, the Force Theory, and the Social Contract Theory.
