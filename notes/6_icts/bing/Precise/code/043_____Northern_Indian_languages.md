### Northern Indian languages
- Northern India is home to several Indo-Aryan languages.
- The Hindustani lingua franca, which includes both Hindi and Urdu, is native to Northern India.
- There is also an extensive range of western and eastern Hindi dialects.
- Bihari languages, including Bhojpuri, Magadhi, and Maithili, are also spoken in the region.
- Punjabi, Pahari languages, and Kashmiri are other examples of Northern Indian languages.
- The historical evolution of these languages is not well defined, and there is no consensus on when the modern North Indian languages emerged.
- These languages have assimilated various aboriginal instances   .
