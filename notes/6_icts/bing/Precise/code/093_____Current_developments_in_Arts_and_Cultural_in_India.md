### Current developments in Arts and Cultural in India

- India has a rich cultural heritage and is home to 37 world heritage sites, two thriving film industries, and a diversely vibrant cultural heritage.
- Several cities in India are actively working towards creating a unique cultural identity for themselves.
- Three cities in India, Thrissur and Nilambur in Kerala and Warangal in Telangana, have recently joined the UNESCO Global Network of Learning Cities.
- The Kiran Nadar Museum of Art is developing a new institution, the Museum of Art and Photography will open in Bangalore, the Gujral Foundation is commissioning a public pavilion series in Delhi, and the Kochi Biennale will have its fifth edition this December.
- The Ministry of Culture, Government of India, provides financial assistance for cultural activities in performing arts, allied cultural activities, and for the promotion of art and culture.
- India has nominated Hoysala Temples for inscription in the World Heritage List and has recently got back a Goat-Headed Yogini Statue.
