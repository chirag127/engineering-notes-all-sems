### Jain Literature in Pali,Prakrit And Sanskrit

- Jain literature refers to the literature of the Jain religion. It is a vast and ancient literary tradition, which was initially transmitted orally .
- The oldest surviving material is contained in the canonical Jain Agamas, which are written in Ardhamagadhi, a Prakrit (Middle-Indo Aryan) language .
- Early Jain literature is mostly written in Prakrit language. Prakrit language was a form of Sanskrit language .
- Pali language was a form of Prakrit language which was used in Magadha .
- Pali and Prakrit were the first languages to emerge from Sanskrit. Pali was used for the dissemination of Buddhistic ideas, whereas Prakrit was used for the dissemination of Jain doctrines .
- The majority of Buddhist literature is written in Pali, while Jain literature is written in Prakrit .
