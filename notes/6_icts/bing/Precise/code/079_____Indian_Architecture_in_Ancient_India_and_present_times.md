### Indian Architecture in Ancient India and present times

Indian architecture is rooted in the history, culture, and religion of India. Among several architectural styles and traditions, the best-known include the many varieties of Hindu temple architecture and Indo-Islamic architecture, especially Rajput architecture, Mughal architecture, South Indian architecture and Indo-Saracenic architecture .

The Mauryan period is considered as the beginning of the classical period of Indian architecture. Nagara and Dravidian architectural styles developed in the early medieval period with the rise of Hindu revivalism and predominant role of Hindu temple architecture in the Indian Subcontinent .

In the 20th century, the growth in the economy and population created a high demand for housing. Globalisation and a migratory population led to a shift in Indian architecture from a holistic point of view .

Some examples of ancient Indian architecture include the Ajanta caves, the Humayun Tomb, and the grandeur of the Vijayanagara Empire- Hampi .
