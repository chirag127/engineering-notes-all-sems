#### Mystical Theory of origin of State

The mystical theory of the origin of the state is one of the oldest theories of state formation. According to this theory, the state is a divine institution, created by God or the gods, and its rulers are divinely ordained to govern. The theory holds that the state is not a human creation, but rather a supernatural entity that exists to fulfill a divine purpose.

Some key points of the mystical theory of the origin of the state are:

1. The state is a divine institution, created by God or the gods.
2. The rulers of the state are divinely ordained to govern.
3. The state is not a human creation, but rather a supernatural entity.
4. The state exists to fulfill a divine purpose.

This theory has been influential in many societies throughout history, particularly in those with strong religious traditions. It has been used to justify the rule of monarchs and other leaders, who were seen as having a divine right to govern. However, the theory has also been criticized for its lack of empirical evidence and for its potential to justify authoritarian rule.
