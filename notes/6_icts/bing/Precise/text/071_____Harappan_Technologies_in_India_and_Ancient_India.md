### Harappan Technologies in India and Ancient India

- The Indus River Valley Civilization, also known as the Harappan Civilization, extended from modern-day northeast Afghanistan to Pakistan and northwest India and existed from 3300-1300 BCE.
- Important innovations of this civilization include standardized weights and measures, seal carving, and metallurgy with copper, bronze, lead, and tin.
- The Harappan civilization is referred to as a Bronze Age civilization. Customarily, unalloyed copper was used for manufacturing artifacts and rarely tin was mixed with copper to make bronze.
- Harappan tools and weapons were simple in form. They comprised of flat-axes, chisels, arrowheads, spearheads, knives, saws, razors, and fish-hooks.
- The Harappans of Dholavira developed an efficient system for conservation, harvesting, and storage of water, which speaks eloquently about their advanced hydraulic engineering.
