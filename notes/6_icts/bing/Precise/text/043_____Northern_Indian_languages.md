### Northern Indian languages

- Northern India is home to several Indo-Aryan languages.
- The Hindustani lingua franca, which includes both Hindi and Urdu, is native to Northern India.
- There is also an extensive range of western and eastern Hindi dialects.
- Bihari languages, such as Bhojpuri, Magadhi, and Maithili, are also spoken in the region.
- Other languages spoken in Northern India include Punjabi, Pahari languages, and Kashmiri.
- The exact time when these modern North Indian languages emerged is not known.
- Each state in North India has its own official language or languages, including Hindi, Urdu, Punjabi, Kashmiri, Dogri, and English.
