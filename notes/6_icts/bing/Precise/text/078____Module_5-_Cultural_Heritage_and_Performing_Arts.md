## Module 5- Cultural Heritage and Performing Arts

Cultural heritage refers to the legacy of physical artifacts and intangible attributes of a group or society that are inherited from past generations, maintained in the present, and bestowed for the benefit of future generations. Cultural heritage includes tangible culture (such as buildings, monuments, landscapes, books, works of art, and artifacts), intangible culture (such as folklore, traditions, language, and knowledge), and natural heritage (including culturally significant landscapes, and biodiversity).

Performing arts are a form of art in which artists use their voices, bodies, or inanimate objects to convey artistic expression. It is different from visual arts, which is when artists use paint, canvas, or various materials to create physical or static art objects. Performing arts include a range of disciplines which are performed in front of a live audience, including theatre, music, dance, opera, and circus arts.

Some key points to remember about cultural heritage and performing arts are:

1. Cultural heritage is the legacy of physical artifacts and intangible attributes of a group or society.
2. Cultural heritage includes tangible culture, intangible culture, and natural heritage.
3. Performing arts are a form of art in which artists use their voices, bodies, or inanimate objects to convey artistic expression.
4. Performing arts include a range of disciplines which are performed in front of a live audience.
5. Cultural heritage and performing arts are important for preserving and sharing the history and traditions of a group or society.
