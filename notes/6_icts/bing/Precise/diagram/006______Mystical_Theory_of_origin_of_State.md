#### Mystical Theory of origin of State

The mystical theory of the origin of the state is one of the oldest theories that explains the formation of the state. According to this theory, the state is of divine origin and is created by God or gods. The ruler is considered to be the representative of God on earth and is responsible for maintaining order and justice in society. Here are some key points about this theory:

1. The state is considered to be of divine origin and is created by God or gods.
2. The ruler is considered to be the representative of God on earth.
3. The ruler is responsible for maintaining order and justice in society.
4. The theory is based on the belief in the divine right of kings, where the monarch is considered to be chosen by God to rule.
5. This theory was prevalent in ancient civilizations such as Egypt, Mesopotamia, and China, where the rulers were considered to be divine or semi-divine.
6. The theory has been criticized for being unscientific and for justifying the absolute power of the ruler.
