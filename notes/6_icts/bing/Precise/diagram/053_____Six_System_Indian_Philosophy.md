### Six System Indian Philosophy

The Six Systems of Indian Philosophy are:

1. **Nyaya**: Founded by Akshapada Gautama, it is a school that deals with logic and epistemology.
2. **Vaisheshika**: Popularly understood as the atomistic school of Indian Philosophy.
3. **Samkhya**: An extremely complex school of philosophy that goes deep into the understanding of the creator and the creation.
4. **Yoga**: A school of philosophy that focuses on the practice of meditation and self-discipline to attain spiritual insight and tranquility.
5. **Purva Mimamsa**: A school of philosophy that focuses on the interpretation of the Vedas.
6. **Uttara Mimamsa or Vedanta**: A school of philosophy that focuses on the study of the Upanishads.

Each of these systems differs in one way or the other in terms of its concepts, phenomena, laws, and dogmas.
