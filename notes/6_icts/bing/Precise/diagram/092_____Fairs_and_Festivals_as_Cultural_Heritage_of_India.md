### Fairs and Festivals as Cultural Heritage of India

India is a land of vibrant celebrations and is known for its numerous fairs and festivals. These celebrations showcase the culture and life of India and are celebrated with high spirit in every town. The flow of festivals continues throughout the year in India.

Some of the most famous fairs and festivals of India include:
- Modhera Dance Festival of Gujarat, also called the Uttarardh Mahotsavaor, is a cultural celebration inside the premises of Modhera Sun Temple.
- Pongal of South India is the harvest festival of the land.
- Konark Dance Festival of Odisha.
- Jaisalmer Desert Festival.
- Goa Carnival.
- Nehru Trophy Boat Race of Kerala.
- Pushkar Mela.
- Bikaner Camel Festival.

India has one of the world’s largest collections of songs, music, dance, theatre, folk traditions, performing arts, rites and rituals, paintings and writings that are known as the ‘Intangible Cultural Heritage’ (ICH) of humanity.

These fairs and festivals are held according to the lunar calendar and are extremely popular among the locals as well as the tourists. They provide a glimpse into the rich cultural heritage of India and are a must-visit for anyone interested in experiencing the diversity of this country.
