## Module 2- Indian Literature, Culture, Tradition, and Practices

1. Indian literature is one of the oldest in the world, with a rich tradition of poetry, drama, and prose in various languages.
2. Indian culture is diverse and encompasses a wide range of customs, beliefs, and practices, influenced by the country's history, geography, and religions.
3. Indian traditions include festivals, rituals, and ceremonies that are celebrated throughout the year, often with great enthusiasm and community participation.
4. Indian practices include a variety of social, religious, and cultural activities, such as yoga, meditation, and Ayurveda, which have gained popularity worldwide.
