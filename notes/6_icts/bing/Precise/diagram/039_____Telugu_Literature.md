### Telugu Literature

Telugu literature is the body of works written in the Telugu language. It consists of various forms of literature, including but not limited to:

- Poems
- Short stories
- Novels
- Plays
- Song lyrics

There is evidence to suggest that Telugu literature dates back to at least the middle of the first millennium. The first extant works, however, are from the 11th century when the Mahabharata was first translated to Telugu .

The literature is mainly poetry and secular and religious epics, with the śataka (“century” of verses) as a very popular form.

Telugu is a Dravidian language spoken in an area north of Madras, India, and running inland to Bellary. It is the official language of the states of Andhra Pradesh and Telangana and has more than 75 million speakers in the early 21st century. The first written materials in the language date from 575 CE .
