### Northern Indian languages

- Northern India is home to several Indo-Aryan languages.
- The Hindustani lingua franca, which includes both Hindi and Urdu, is native to Northern India.
- There is also an extensive range of western and eastern Hindi dialects.
- Bihari languages, such as Bhojpuri, Magadhi, and Maithili, are also spoken in Northern India.
- Other languages spoken in the region include Punjabi, Pahari languages, and Kashmiri.
- There is no consensus on when the modern North Indian languages, such as western and eastern Hindi dialects, Punjabi, Sindhi, Pahari, and Kashmiri languages, and Hindustani, emerged.
- The official languages in one or more of the states and union territories located in North India are Hindi, Urdu, Punjabi, Kashmiri, Dogri, and English.
