# Agriculture in India and Ancient India

- Agriculture has a long history in India, with the domestication of plants and animals reported in the subcontinent by 9000 BC .
- The excavation of the Mehrgarh period sites, around 8000-6000 BC, provides evidence of early Indian agriculture .
- In ancient India, agriculture was associated with religious customs and traditions, particularly among the Vedic people who were skilled in cultivation .
- A wide range of crops were cultivated in ancient India, including rice, sugarcane, millets, black pepper, various grains, coconuts, beans, cotton, plantain, tamarind, and sandalwood .
- Agriculture in the Ganges River valley became increasingly developed after about 3000 BP .
- Indian subcontinent agriculture was the largest producer of wheat and grain .
- Settled life soon followed with implements and techniques being developed for agriculture .
