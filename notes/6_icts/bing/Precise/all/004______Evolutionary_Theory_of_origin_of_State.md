#### Evolutionary Theory of origin of State

The Evolutionary Theory of the origin of the state is one of the several theories that attempt to explain how states or governments came into existence. According to this theory, the state evolved from the family, which gradually grew in size and complexity, eventually forming a state.

1. The theory suggests that the earliest form of human society was the family, which was based on blood ties and the natural bond between parents and children.
2. As the family grew in size, it became necessary to establish rules and regulations to govern the behavior of its members. This led to the formation of clans or tribes, which were larger groups of related families.
3. Over time, these clans or tribes began to engage in trade and commerce with one another, leading to the development of more complex social and economic relationships.
4. As these relationships grew more complex, it became necessary to establish a system of governance to regulate them. This led to the formation of the state, which was a political organization with the power to enforce laws and maintain order.
5. The state, according to the evolutionary theory, emerged as a natural progression from the family, through the clan or tribe, to a more complex political organization.

This theory is based on the idea that human society has evolved over time, and that the state is a natural result of this evolution. It is important to note that this is just one of several theories about the origin of the state, and that there is no definitive answer to this question. Different scholars and theorists have proposed different explanations for the emergence of the state, and the debate continues to this day.
