#### Mystical Theory of origin of State

The mystical theory of the origin of the state is one of the oldest theories that explains the origin of the state. According to this theory, the state is a divine institution that was created by God or gods. The rulers of the state were considered to be representatives of the divine power on earth and were therefore given the right to rule by divine authority.

Some key points of the mystical theory of the origin of the state are:

1. The state is a divine institution created by God or gods.
2. The rulers of the state are representatives of the divine power on earth.
3. The right to rule is given to the rulers by divine authority.
4. The state is considered to be a necessary part of the divine plan for the world.

This theory was prevalent in ancient civilizations such as Egypt, where the Pharaoh was considered to be a god-king, and in medieval Europe, where the divine right of kings was a widely accepted concept. However, this theory has largely been discredited in modern times, as it is based on religious beliefs rather than empirical evidence.
