### Harappan Technologies in India and Ancient India

The Harappan Civilization, also known as the Indus River Valley Civilization, existed from 3300-1300 BCE and extended from modern-day northeast Afghanistan to Pakistan and northwest India. This civilization was known for its important innovations and technological advancements, some of which are listed below:

1. **Standardized weights and measures**: The Harappans developed a system of standardized weights and measures, which helped in trade and commerce.

2. **Seal carving**: The Harappans were skilled in seal carving, which was used for marking goods and for identification.

3. **Metallurgy**: The Harappans were skilled in metallurgy and worked with metals such as copper, bronze, lead, and tin. They developed new techniques in metallurgy and were considered a Bronze Age society.

4. **Handicraft**: The Harappans were skilled in handicraft and used semi-precious gemstones such as carnelian to create intricate designs.

5. **Hydraulic structures**: The Harappans were advanced in the field of hydrology and water resources management. They constructed sophisticated hydraulic structures and had systems for wastewater disposal and treatment.

These are just some of the technological advancements of the Harappan Civilization. They were a highly advanced society and their innovations and technologies had a lasting impact on the world.
