### Urdu Literature in India

- Urdu literature refers to the writings in the Urdu language of the Muslims of Pakistan and northern India.
- It is written in the Perso-Arabic script.
- With a few major exceptions, the literature is the work of Muslim writers who take their themes from the life of the Indian subcontinent.
- Urdu literature is a vast field of immense knowledge and rich literary works by famous poets and writers.
- Though Hindi and Urdu have the same root, they have developed and evolved on distinct lines.
- Urdu literature tends to be dominated by poetry, especially the verse forms of the ghazal and nazm.
- It has expanded into other styles of writing, including that of the short story, or afsana.
