### Six System Indian Philosophy

The Six Systems of Indian Philosophy are philosophical traditions that originated in the Indian subcontinent. These systems are known as Vaishesika, Nyaya, Samkhya, Yoga, Purva Mimansa, and Vedanta or Uttara Mimansa.

1. **Nyaya** - Founded by Akshapada Gautama, this school deals with logic and epistemology. It is known for its analysis of logic and its means of knowledge known as inference .
2. **Vaisheshika** - This is the atomistic school of Indian Philosophy.
3. **Samkhya** - This is an extremely complex school of philosophy that goes deep into the understanding of the creator and the creation.
4. **Yoga** - This system is closely related to the Samkhya philosophy.
5. **Purva Mimamsa** - This system focuses on the interpretation of the Vedas.
6. **Vedanta or Uttara Mimamsa** - This system is the conclusion of the Vedas and applies to the Upanishads, which were elaborations of the Vedas, and to the school that arose out of the study of the Upanishads.

Each of these systems differs in one way or the other in terms of its concepts, phenomena, laws, and dogmas.
