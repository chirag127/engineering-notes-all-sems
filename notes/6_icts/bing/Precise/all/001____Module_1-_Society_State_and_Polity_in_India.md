## Module 1- Society State and Polity in India

1. **Society** refers to a group of people who share a common culture, language, and institutions.
2. **State** refers to a political entity that has sovereignty over a defined territory and population.
3. **Polity** refers to the form of government or political organization of a state.
4. India is a **federal parliamentary democratic republic**.
5. The **President of India** is the head of state, while the **Prime Minister** is the head of government.
6. India has a **bicameral parliament** consisting of the **Rajya Sabha** (Council of States) and the **Lok Sabha** (House of the People).
7. The **Indian Constitution** is the supreme law of the land and outlines the powers and duties of the government and the rights and freedoms of citizens.
8. India has a **multi-party system** with several national and regional political parties.
9. The **judiciary** is independent and has the power of judicial review to ensure that laws and government actions are in compliance with the Constitution.
10. India has a diverse society with many different languages, religions, and cultures.
