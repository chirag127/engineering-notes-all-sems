# Trade in Ancient India

- Trade was an important source of commerce in ancient India.
- Ancient Mesopotamian texts mention Magan and Meluhha, which are thought to refer to India generally or, perhaps, Mohenjo-Daro specifically.
- Artifacts from the Indus Valley region have been found at sites in Mesopotamia, though their precise point of origin in India is not always clear.
- Much of the significant data for long-distance and maritime trade across South Asia in the ancient period comes from archaeological sources.
- Textual sources also provide interesting and significant information about material culture and trade in the ancient world.
