## Module 5- Cultural Heritage and Performing Arts

- Cultural heritage is the legacy of physical and intangible attributes of a group or society that are inherited from past generations, maintained in the present and bestowed for the benefit of future generations.
- Cultural heritage can be tangible, such as buildings, monuments, landscapes, books, works of art and artefacts, or intangible, such as oral traditions, languages, rituals, values, customs and skills.
- Performing arts are forms of creative expression that are performed in front of an audience, such as music, dance, theatre, opera, circus and puppetry.
- Performing arts are part of the intangible cultural heritage of many cultures and play a vital role in preserving and transmitting their history, identity, values and beliefs.
- Performing arts can also be a source of social cohesion, cultural diversity, artistic innovation and intercultural dialogue.
- Some examples of performing arts that are recognized by UNESCO as intangible cultural heritage are:

  - Urtiin Duu, traditional folk long song from Mongolia, which is sung in a wide vocal range and accompanied by a horse-head fiddle.
  - Wayang puppet theatre from Indonesia, which uses intricately carved and painted puppets to narrate stories from Hindu epics or local folklore.
  - Flamenco, an artistic expression fusing song, dance and music from Spain, which reflects the diverse cultures and influences that have shaped the Andalusian region.
  - Kabuki, a classical Japanese theatre form that combines drama, music, dance and elaborate costumes and make-up to portray historical events or moral conflicts.
  - Tango, a social dance and musical genre from Argentina and Uruguay, which expresses the identity, feelings and aspirations of the urban lower classes.

- To learn more about cultural heritage and performing arts, you can:

  - Explore the UNESCO website on intangible cultural heritage, which provides information, videos and photos of various performing arts from around the world.
  - Visit museums, theatres, festivals and cultural events that showcase the performing arts of different cultures and regions.
  - Participate in workshops, classes or clubs that teach or practice performing arts, such as singing, dancing, acting or playing an instrument.
  - Read books, articles, journals or blogs that discuss the history, theory, criticism or appreciation of performing arts.
  - Watch documentaries, films, shows or online videos that feature or analyze performing arts.
