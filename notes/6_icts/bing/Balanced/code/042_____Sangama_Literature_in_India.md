### Sangama Literature in India

- Sangama literature is the earliest known literature in the Tamil language, composed between the 1st and 4th centuries CE by poets belonging to three literary academies (sangams) in Madurai, India .
- Sangama literature consists of 18 works, including eight anthologies of poems (Ettuthokai) and ten long poems (Pattuppattu), as well as commentaries and grammatical treatises.
- Sangama literature is broadly classified into two categories: akam (inner) and puram (outer). Akam poetry deals with themes of love, eroticism, and emotions, while puram poetry deals with themes of heroism, war, and public life.
- Sangama literature reflects the social, cultural, political, and religious aspects of ancient Tamil society, as well as the natural beauty and diversity of the Tamil land. It also showcases the poetic skills, imagery, and metaphors of the sangam poets.
- Sangama literature is considered to be a valuable source of information and inspiration for Tamil culture, language, and literature, as well as for the history of South India and Sri Lanka . It is also admired for its aesthetic and artistic qualities by scholars and readers across the world.
