### Dance as Cultural Heritage of India

- Dance is an ancient and celebrated cultural tradition in India that reflects the diversity, history and beliefs of its people .
- Dance in India can be classified into two main categories: classical and folk .
- Classical dance forms are based on the ancient texts of Natya Shastra and Abhinaya Darpana, which prescribe the rules of movement, expression, music and costumes.
- The Sangeet Natak Academy recognises eight classical dance forms in India, namely, Bharatnatyam, Kathak, Kuchipudi, Kathakali, Odissi, Mohiniyattam, Manipuri and Satriya.
- Each classical dance form has its own distinctive style, origin, theme and repertoire, but they all share some common elements such as the use of mudras (hand gestures), abhinaya (facial expressions), nritya (pure dance) and natya (dramatic dance).
- Folk dance forms are more regional and spontaneous, reflecting the local customs, festivals, traditions and lifestyles of the people.
- Folk dances are usually performed in groups, with simple costumes and props, and often accompanied by folk songs and music.
- Some of the popular folk dances of India are Bhangra, Garba, Dandiya, Bihu, Lavani, Chhau, Ghoomar, Kalbelia and Dumhal.
- Dance in India is not only a form of entertainment, but also a way of expressing devotion, celebrating life, preserving culture and promoting social harmony  .
