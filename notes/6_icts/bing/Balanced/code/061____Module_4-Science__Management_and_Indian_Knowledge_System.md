## Module 4-Science, Management and Indian Knowledge System

This module covers the following topics:

- Astronomy in India: The development of astronomy in India from the Vedic period to the modern era, the contributions of Indian astronomers such as Aryabhata, Varahamihira, Brahmagupta, Bhaskara, and others, the concepts of sidereal and tropical zodiacs, the calculation of eclipses, the heliocentric and geocentric models, the astronomical instruments such as the gnomon, the armillary sphere, the astrolabe, and the observatories built by Jai Singh II.
- Chemistry in India: The origin and evolution of chemistry in India, the classification of substances into five elements (panchabhuta), the concepts of alchemy and metallurgy, the production of various metals, alloys, minerals, dyes, perfumes, medicines, and cosmetics, the contributions of Indian chemists such as Nagarjuna, Kanada, Charaka, Sushruta, and others, the techniques of distillation, calcination, sublimation, and extraction.
- Mathematics in India: The history and development of mathematics in India from the Indus Valley Civilization to the colonial period, the contributions of Indian mathematicians such as Baudhayana, Pingala, Panini, Katyayana, Apastamba, Shulba Sutras, Sulba Sutras, Jaina mathematics, Buddhist mathematics, Aryabhata, Brahmagupta, Bhaskara, Mahavira, Madhava, Nilakantha, and others, the concepts of zero, decimal system, place value system, fractions, algebra, geometry, trigonometry, calculus, combinatorics, and number theory.
- Physics in India: The origin and development of physics in India, the philosophical foundations of physics in the schools of Samkhya, Nyaya, Vaisheshika, and Vedanta, the concepts of atomism, causation, motion, force, gravity, sound, light, heat, electricity, and magnetism, the contributions of Indian physicists such as Kanada, Gautama, Udayana, Pakudha Katyayana, Patanjali, Bharata, Varahamihira, Aryabhata, Brahmagupta, Bhaskara, and others, the devices and experiments such as the pinhole camera, the water clock, the balance, the thermometer, the electroscope, and the compass.
- Agriculture in India: The history and evolution of agriculture in India from the Neolithic period to the present day, the diversity of crops, soils, climates, and irrigation systems, the concepts of crop rotation, fertilization, pest control, and animal husbandry, the contributions of Indian agriculturists such as Kashyapa, Varahamihira, Surapala, Amarakosha, and others, the agricultural practices and innovations such as the plough, the seed drill, the transplantation, the terrace farming, the organic farming, and the green revolution.
- Medicine in India: The origin and development of medicine in India from the ancient times to the modern era, the classification of medicine into Ayurveda, Siddha, Unani, and Homeopathy, the concepts of health, disease, diagnosis, treatment, prevention, and hygiene, the contributions of Indian physicians such as Charaka, Sushruta, Vagbhata, Nagarjuna, Madhava, and others, the branches and specialties of medicine such as surgery, anatomy, physiology, pharmacology, pathology, ophthalmology, dentistry, and psychiatry, the medical texts and treatises such as the Charaka Samhita, the Sushruta Samhita, the Ashtanga Hridaya, the Rasashastra, and the Yoga Sutras.
- Metallurgy in India: The history and advancement of metallurgy in India from the Bronze Age to the modern times, the production and use of various metals and alloys such as copper, bronze, iron, steel, zinc, tin, lead, gold, silver, and brass, the concepts of smelting, forging, casting, welding, tempering, and corrosion, the contributions of Indian metallurgists such as Nagarjuna, Dhundiraja, Raghunatha, and others, the metallurgical artifacts and monuments such as the iron pillar of Delhi, the bronze statue of Nataraja, the wootz steel, and the zinc distillation apparatus.
