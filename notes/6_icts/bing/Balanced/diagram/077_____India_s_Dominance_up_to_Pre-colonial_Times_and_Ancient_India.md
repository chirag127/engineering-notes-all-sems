### India’s Dominance up to Pre-colonial Times and Ancient India

- India is one of the oldest civilizations in the world, dating back to 2500 BC, when the Indus Valley cities were flourishing .
- India witnessed the rise and fall of many empires, such as the Mauryans, Guptas, Kushans, Cholas, Delhi Sultanate, Vijayanagara, Mughals, Marathas, and Sikhs .
- India was a major cultural, religious, and economic center, influencing and being influenced by other regions of Asia, Europe, and Africa .
- India had a rich and diverse heritage of art, literature, architecture, philosophy, science, mathematics, and medicine .
- India was also a hub of trade and commerce, connecting the Indian Ocean, the Arabian Sea, and the Bay of Bengal with the rest of the world .
- India's dominance was challenged by the arrival of European powers, especially the British, who gradually established their colonial rule over most of the subcontinent by the 19th century .
- India's independence movement, led by various leaders and organizations, culminated in the partition of India and Pakistan in 1947, ending the British colonial era.
