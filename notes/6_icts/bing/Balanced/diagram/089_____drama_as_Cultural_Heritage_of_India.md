### Drama as Cultural Heritage of India

- Drama is a form of performing arts, where stories are enacted by the utilisation of dialogue, music, gesture, dance, and sound.
- Drama is one of the oldest and richest forms of cultural expression in India, dating back to the ancient Vedic period .
- Drama in India has been influenced by various religious, social, and linguistic traditions, such as Hinduism, Buddhism, Jainism, Islam, and regional languages.
- Drama in India has also been shaped by various classical theatre traditions, such as Sanskrit theatre, folk theatre, and Urdu theatre  .
- Sanskrit theatre is the earliest existing form of drama and theatre in India, attributed to the sage Bharata, who wrote the Natyashastra, a treatise on dramaturgy .
- Folk theatre is a diverse and vibrant form of drama that reflects the local cultures, customs, and beliefs of different regions and communities in India.
- Urdu theatre is a form of drama that emerged from the courtly culture of the Mughal Empire, and later developed into the Parsi theatre, which influenced modern theatre, particularly in Hindi, Marathi, and Bengali languages.
- Drama in India is a cultural heritage that showcases the diversity, creativity, and spirituality of the Indian people, and also contributes to the social and educational development of the society.
