### Current developments in Arts and Culture in India

- India has a rich and diverse cultural heritage that includes various forms of art, music, dance, theatre, literature, architecture, and festivals.
- India is also home to 37 world heritage sites, two major film industries, and many regional and folk traditions that reflect the diversity and pluralism of the country.
- In recent years, India has witnessed some new trends and developments in the field of arts and culture, such as:

  - The emergence of contemporary Indian art that explores themes such as identity, globalization, social issues, and environmental concerns. Some of the prominent contemporary artists include Anish Kapoor, Subodh Gupta, Bharti Kher, Nalini Malani, and Jitish Kallat.
  - The inclusion of three Indian cities, Thrissur and Nilambur in Kerala and Warangal in Telangana, in the UNESCO Global Network of Learning Cities. These cities have demonstrated their commitment to promoting lifelong learning, social inclusion, and cultural diversity through various initiatives and programs.
  - The nomination of Hoysala Temples in Karnataka for inscription in the World Heritage List. These temples are examples of the Hoysala style of architecture that flourished between the 11th and 14th centuries and are known for their intricate carvings and sculptures.
  - The repatriation of a 10th-century Yogini statue from the US to India. The statue, depicting a female deity with a goat head, was stolen from a temple in Bihar and smuggled to the US. It was returned to India after a legal battle and is now displayed at the National Museum in New Delhi.
  - The celebration of Sydney WorldPride, a flagship event that brings together the LGBT community and its allies from around the world. India participated in the event with a delegation of artists, activists, and performers who showcased the diversity and vibrancy of the Indian queer culture.
