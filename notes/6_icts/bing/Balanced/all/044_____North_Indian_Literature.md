# North Indian Literature

- North Indian literature refers to the literature produced in the northern regions of India, which include the states of Delhi, Uttar Pradesh, Uttarakhand, Himachal Pradesh, Jammu and Kashmir, Punjab, Haryana, and Rajasthan.
- North Indian literature is influenced by the languages, cultures, religions, and histories of the diverse people who inhabit the region. Some of the major languages of North Indian literature are Hindi, Urdu, Punjabi, Kashmiri, Rajasthani, and Pahari.
- North Indian literature has a rich and varied tradition that spans from ancient times to the present day. Some of the notable genres and forms of North Indian literature are:
  - Epics and Puranas: These are ancient religious texts that narrate the stories of gods, goddesses, heroes, and sages. Some of the famous epics and Puranas of North India are the Ramayana, the Mahabharata, the Bhagavata Purana, and the Vishnu Purana.
  - Poetry: Poetry is one of the most popular and expressive forms of North Indian literature. Poetry can be classified into various types, such as devotional, romantic, lyrical, patriotic, satirical, and modern. Some of the renowned poets of North India are Kabir, Tulsidas, Mirabai, Surdas, Amir Khusrau, Mir Taqi Mir, Ghalib, Iqbal, Tagore, Premchand, and Harivansh Rai Bachchan.
  - Drama: Drama is another important form of North Indian literature that combines dialogue, music, dance, and spectacle. Drama can be divided into classical and folk forms. Some of the classical forms of North Indian drama are Natak, Prakarana, and Rasaka. Some of the folk forms of North Indian drama are Nautanki, Ramleela, Raslila, and Bhavai.
  - Prose: Prose is the form of North Indian literature that uses ordinary language and sentences to convey meaning. Prose can be categorized into fiction and non-fiction. Fiction includes novels, short stories, and fables. Non-fiction includes essays, biographies, memoirs, and travelogues. Some of the prominent prose writers of North India are Premchand, Munshi Premchand, Rajendra Yadav, Bhisham Sahni, Khushwant Singh, Ruskin Bond, and Vikram Seth.
  - Modern literature: Modern literature is the form of North Indian literature that emerged in the 20th and 21st centuries and reflects the social, political, and cultural changes of the times. Modern literature can be influenced by various movements, such as realism, romanticism, modernism, postmodernism, and feminism. Some of the notable modern writers of North India are Manto, Ismat Chughtai, Kamleshwar, Nirmal Verma, Mahasweta Devi, Arundhati Roy, and Amitav Ghosh.
