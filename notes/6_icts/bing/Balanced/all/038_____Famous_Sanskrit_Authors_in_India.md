# Famous Sanskrit Authors in India

Sanskrit is one of the oldest and most influential languages in India. It is the language of the Vedas, the Upanishads, the Mahabharata, the Ramayana, and many other classical texts. Sanskrit literature is rich and diverse, spanning various genres and periods. Here are some of the famous Sanskrit authors in India:

- **Kalidasa**: He is considered to be the greatest Sanskrit poet and dramatist, who lived in the 4th or 5th century CE. He wrote two epic poems, Kumārasambhava and Raghuvaṃśa, which depict the lives of the gods and kings. He also wrote three plays, Mālavikāgnimitram, Abhijñānaśākuntalam, and Vikramorvaśīyam, which are known for their romantic and lyrical style. He is admired for his mastery of language, imagery, and emotion .
- **Bharavi**: He was a 6th century CE poet who wrote the Kirātārjunīya, an epic poem that narrates the encounter between Arjuna and Shiva in the form of a hunter. The poem is known for its complex and ornate style, as well as its philosophical and moral themes.
- **Bhartṛhari**: He was a 5th century CE poet and philosopher who wrote three collections of verses, the Śatakatraya, on the topics of love, politics, and renunciation. He is also attributed with the Vākyapadīya, a treatise on grammar and linguistics.
- **Bhāsa**: He was a 3rd or 4th century BCE playwright who wrote thirteen plays, mostly based on the Mahabharata and the Ramayana. His plays are notable for their realism, humor, and innovation. He is regarded as one of the earliest and most original Sanskrit dramatists.
- **Bhatta Narayana**: He was a 7th century CE poet who wrote the Venīsaṃhāra, a play that depicts the abduction and rescue of Draupadi by the Pandavas. The play is praised for its dramatic and emotional intensity, as well as its poetic beauty.
- **Banabhatta**: He was a 7th century CE poet and prose writer who served as the court poet of King Harsha. He wrote the Harṣacarita, a biography of Harsha, and the Kādambarī, a romantic novel that tells the story of a love affair between a prince and a princess. He is known for his elegant and elaborate style, as well as his use of fantasy and imagination.
- **Vidyadhar Shastri**: He was a 20th century poet who wrote two epic poems, Sārasvatīkaṇṭhābharaṇam and Rāmāyaṇamañjarī, as well as several shorter poems, plays, and songs of praise. He received the Jnanpith Award, India's highest literary award, in 2009. He is recognized for his contribution to the revival and modernization of Sanskrit literature.
