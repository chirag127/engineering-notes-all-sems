### Seals as Cultural Heritage of India

Seals have been an integral part of Indian culture for centuries. These seals have played a significant role in shaping the history, art and cultural heritage of India. Here are some of the points highlighting the importance of seals as cultural heritage of India:

- Seals were used as a means of identification and authentication in ancient times. They were used to mark ownership of goods, documents and property. These seals were made of various materials such as clay, stone, ivory and metal.

- The Harappan civilization, one of the earliest civilizations in the world, used seals extensively. These seals were made of steatite and had intricate designs and inscriptions on them. They were used for a variety of purposes such as trade, religion and administrative purposes.

- The seals found in the Harappan civilization are an important source of information about the society, culture and beliefs of that time. The inscriptions on these seals are in an ancient script that is yet to be fully deciphered.

- Seals from the Mauryan and Gupta periods have also been found. These seals were made of various materials such as copper and gold. They had images of animals, humans and deities on them. These seals provide valuable insights into the art and iconography of that time.

- Seals were also used in the medieval period by various dynasties such as the Mughals and the Rajputs. These seals were made of precious materials such as gold and silver and had intricate designs and inscriptions on them.

- Indian seals have also been found in various other countries such as Iraq, Bahrain and Oman. These seals were used for trade purposes and provide evidence of the extensive trade networks that existed in ancient times.

- Today, seals continue to be an important part of Indian culture. They are used for various purposes such as religious ceremonies, weddings and other auspicious occasions. Seals also continue to be used for administrative purposes such as stamping of documents.

In conclusion, seals are an important part of India's cultural heritage. They have played a significant role in shaping the history, art and culture of India. The intricate designs and inscriptions on these seals provide valuable insights into the society, beliefs and culture of ancient India.
