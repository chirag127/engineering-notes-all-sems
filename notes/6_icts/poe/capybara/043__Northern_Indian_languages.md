### Northern Indian languages

The Northern Indian languages are a group of Indo-Aryan languages spoken in the northern part of India. Here are some key points about these languages:

- The Northern Indian languages include Hindi, Punjabi, Bengali, Gujarati, Marathi, and many other regional languages.
- Hindi is the most widely spoken language in the Northern Indian region and is the official language of India. It is spoken by over 41% of the Indian population.
- Punjabi is the second most widely spoken language in the Northern Indian region and is primarily spoken in the state of Punjab. It is also spoken in parts of Pakistan and around the world by the Punjabi diaspora.
- Bengali is primarily spoken in the state of West Bengal and is also the official language of Bangladesh. It is the fifth most widely spoken language in the world, with over 250 million speakers.
- Gujarati is primarily spoken in the state of Gujarat and is also spoken in parts of Pakistan and around the world by the Gujarati diaspora. It is the 26th most widely spoken language in the world, with over 46 million speakers.
- Marathi is primarily spoken in the state of Maharashtra and is also spoken in parts of India, Sri Lanka, and Israel. It is the 19th most widely spoken language in the world, with over 83 million speakers.
- The Northern Indian languages have a rich literary tradition, with many important works of literature dating back several centuries.
- These languages are written in the Devanagari script, which is shared by several other Indian languages as well.
- Learning any of these languages can be a valuable asset for anyone looking to work or travel in Northern India.
