
### The Ramayana

* The Ramayana is an ancient Indian epic poem, composed by the sage Valmiki, and is believed to be the first work of literature in Sanskrit. 
* It is one of the two great epics of India, the other being the Mahabharata.
* It is the story of Rama, an incarnation of the Hindu god Vishnu, and his quest to rescue his wife Sita from the demon king Ravana.
* The Ramayana is divided into seven books, each containing several chapters. It is composed of 24,000 verses and is written in anustubh meter.
* The Ramayana is an important source of information about the culture and society of ancient India. It is also a source of inspiration for many poets and authors, and is an important part of Hindu mythology and religion.
* The Ramayana is believed to have been composed between the 5th and 4th centuries BCE. It is one of the most influential works of literature in the world, and is still widely read and performed today.
