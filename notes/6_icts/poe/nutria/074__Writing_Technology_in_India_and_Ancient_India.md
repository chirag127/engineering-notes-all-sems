
### Writing Technology in India and Ancient India

* Writing technology in India dates back to the Indus Valley Civilization, which flourished in the region around 3,000 BCE. Writing was done on clay tablets, which were inscribed with symbols representing words and ideas. 
* The ancient Indians developed an advanced form of writing called Brahmi script, which was used to write Sanskrit and other languages. This script is the basis for many modern Indian scripts, including Devanagari, which is used to write Hindi and other languages. 
* Ancient Indians also invented a system of writing called Kharosthi, which was used in the northwest part of the subcontinent. This system was used to write Gandhari, an ancient language related to Sanskrit. 
* In the ancient period, writing was used to record religious texts, laws, and other important documents. It was also used to record scientific and mathematical knowledge, including the famous Surya Siddhanta, an astronomical treatise written in the 4th century BCE.
* In the modern period, writing technology has advanced significantly. Computers and other digital devices are now used to write, store, and share information. In addition, the Internet has revolutionized the way people communicate, allowing them to share ideas and information with people all over the world.
