
### Buddhist Literature in Pali,Prakrit And Sanskrit

* Pali literature includes the Tipitaka (the three baskets of Buddhist teachings) and the commentaries. It also includes the Jataka tales, the Therigatha (verses of female elders), the Milinda Panha (questions of King Milinda), and the Abhidhamma Pitaka.

* Prakrit literature includes the Buddhist Jatakas and the Avadanas. It also includes the Buddhist scriptures written in the Prakrit language, such as the Mahavastu, the Lalitavistara, the Mahaparinibbana Sutta, and the Mahasamghika Vinaya.

* Sanskrit literature includes the Mahayana sutras, such as the Lotus Sutra and the Prajnaparamita Sutras. It also includes the Abhidharma texts, such as the Abhidharmakosa and the Madhyamaka texts.
