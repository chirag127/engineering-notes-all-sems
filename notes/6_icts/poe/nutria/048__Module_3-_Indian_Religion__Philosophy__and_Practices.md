
## Module 3- Indian Religion, Philosophy, and Practices

1. Hinduism is the oldest and major religion in India, and is based on the Vedic texts, a collection of ancient religious texts. It is believed to have been founded around 1500 BCE.

2. Jainism is an ancient religion that originated in India, and is based on the teachings of Mahavira. It is believed to have been founded around 500 BCE.

3. Buddhism is a religion based on the teachings of the Buddha, and is believed to have been founded around 500 BCE. It is practiced in India, as well as other countries in Asia.

4. Sikhism is a religion based on the teachings of Guru Nanak, and is believed to have been founded around 1500 CE. It is practiced in India, as well as other countries in Asia.

5. Zoroastrianism is an ancient religion that originated in Persia, and is believed to have been founded around 1000 BCE. It is practiced in India, as well as other countries in the Middle East.

6. Indian philosophy is a rich tradition of thought that has its roots in the Vedas. It includes various schools of thought, such as Samkhya, Yoga, Nyaya, Vaisheshika, and Mimamsa.

7. Ayurveda is an ancient system of medicine that originated in India, and is based on the principles of balance and harmony. It is believed to have been founded around 1000 BCE.

8. Indian culture is an ancient and diverse culture, with influences from various religions and philosophies. It includes a variety of traditions, such as music, art, dance, and literature.
