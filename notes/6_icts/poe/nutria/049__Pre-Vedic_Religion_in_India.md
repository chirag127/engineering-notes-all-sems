
### Pre-Vedic Religion in India

- Pre-Vedic religion in India was the dominant religious practice in the region prior to the introduction of Vedic religion.
- It is believed to have been an amalgamation of various indigenous traditions and beliefs that were prevalent in the region.
- Pre-Vedic religion was polytheistic and animistic, with many gods and goddesses being worshipped.
- The religion was largely oral and was passed down through generations by word of mouth.
- Rituals and ceremonies were conducted to honor the gods and goddesses and to ask for their blessings.
- Sacrifices were also made to please the gods and goddesses.
- The most important gods and goddesses were Indra, Varuna, Agni, and Ushas.
- Belief in the afterlife was also an important part of pre-Vedic religion, with the belief that the soul would be reincarnated after death.
