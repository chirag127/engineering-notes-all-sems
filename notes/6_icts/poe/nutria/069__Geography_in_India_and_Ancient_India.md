
### Geography in India and Ancient India

- India is the seventh largest country in the world, with a total area of 3.287 million square kilometers.
- India is bordered by the Arabian Sea, the Indian Ocean, and Bay of Bengal to the south, and by Pakistan, China, Nepal, Bhutan, and Bangladesh to the north and east.
- India has a diverse geography, ranging from the snow-capped Himalayas in the north to the tropical rainforests of the south.
- Ancient India was home to the Indus Valley Civilization, one of the earliest civilizations in the world.
- The Indus Valley Civilization was centered in the northwestern region of the Indian subcontinent, and was the most advanced civilization of its time.
- Ancient India was also home to the Mauryan Empire, the Gupta Empire, and the Mughal Empire.
- Ancient Indian geography was characterized by the Himalayas, the Indo-Gangetic Plain, the Thar Desert, and the Deccan Plateau.
- India is home to a variety of climates, including tropical, temperate, and alpine.
- India is home to a variety of flora and fauna, including tigers, elephants, and rhinoceroses.
