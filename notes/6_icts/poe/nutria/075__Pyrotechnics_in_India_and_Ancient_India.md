
### Pyrotechnics in India and Ancient India

Pyrotechnics have been used in India since ancient times. In ancient India, fireworks were used in religious ceremonies and festivals. 

* Fireworks were used to celebrate weddings and special occasions. 
* Fireworks were also used in warfare to create a distraction or to frighten the enemy. 
* Fireworks were also used in traditional theatrical performances. 
* Fireworks were used to create a spectacle of light and sound. 

In modern India, pyrotechnics are widely used for public displays and celebrations. Fireworks are used to celebrate national holidays, festivals, and other significant events. Fireworks are also used in private celebrations such as weddings, birthdays, and anniversaries. 

* Fireworks are used to create a visual and auditory display. 
* Fireworks are used to create a sense of excitement and joy. 
* Fireworks are also used to create a sense of awe and wonder. 
* Fireworks are also used to create a sense of patriotism and national pride. 

Pyrotechnics have been used in India for centuries and continue to be used for public and private celebrations. Fireworks are used to create a spectacle of light and sound, and to create a sense of joy and excitement.
