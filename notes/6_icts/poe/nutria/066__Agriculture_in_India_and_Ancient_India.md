
### Agriculture in India and Ancient India

1. Agriculture has been a major part of India's economy since ancient times. 
2. India is the second largest producer of food in the world, and the majority of its population is involved in agriculture. 
3. Ancient India had an advanced system of agriculture, which included irrigation systems, crop rotation, and animal husbandry. 
4. The Indus Valley Civilization (3000-1500 BC) was one of the earliest civilizations in the world and had a sophisticated agricultural system. 
5. Ancient Indian farmers used tools such as ploughs and sickles to cultivate their crops. 
6. Ancient Indian farmers also practiced crop rotation and used manure and natural fertilizers to improve soil fertility. 
7. India is the largest producer of rice, wheat, pulses, tea, and jute in the world. 
8. India is also a major producer of spices, such as turmeric, cardamom, and chilli. 
9. India is the world's largest producer of milk, with the majority of it produced by small-scale farmers. 
10. The Indian government has implemented various policies and schemes to improve agricultural productivity, such as the National Food Security Mission and the Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana.
