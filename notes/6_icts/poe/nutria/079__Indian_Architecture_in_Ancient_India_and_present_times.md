
### Indian Architecture in Ancient India and Present Times

* Ancient Indian architecture was highly influenced by religious and cultural beliefs. Examples include the Taj Mahal, the Sun Temple of Konark, and the Ellora Caves.
* Ancient Indian architecture was characterized by the use of various materials such as stone, brick, and wood.
* Ancient Indian architecture also made use of various building techniques such as post and lintel, corbelling, and trabeated structures.
* Ancient Indian architecture was also characterized by the use of various decorative elements such as sculptures, friezes, and reliefs.
* In modern times, Indian architecture has seen significant changes in terms of materials, techniques, and decorative elements.
* Modern Indian architecture is characterized by the use of steel, concrete, and glass.
* Modern Indian architecture also makes use of various modern building techniques such as cantilevering, cantilevered columns, and trusses.
* Modern Indian architecture also makes use of various modern decorative elements such as neon lighting, LED lighting, and digital displays.
* Indian architecture in modern times has also seen the emergence of various new styles such as modernism, post-modernism, and neo-traditionalism.
