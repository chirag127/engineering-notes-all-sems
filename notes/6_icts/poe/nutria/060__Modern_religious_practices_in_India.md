
### Modern Religious Practices in India

1. Hinduism is the major religion in India, with approximately 80% of the population adhering to it.

2. Hinduism is often referred to as a way of life, rather than a religion, and it is highly diverse, with many different sects and traditions.

3. Other religions in India include Islam (14%), Christianity (2.3%), Sikhism (1.7%), Buddhism (0.7%), Jainism (0.4%) and other faiths (0.6%).

4. Hinduism has been around for thousands of years and has a strong presence in many aspects of Indian culture, including art, literature, music, and dance.

5. The primary modern religious practices of Hinduism include daily prayer, meditation, and the observance of festivals and rituals.

6. Hindus also practice yoga, which is an ancient physical, mental, and spiritual practice.

7. In Islam, the five pillars of faith are Shahadah (declaration of faith), Salat (prayer), Zakat (charity), Sawm (fasting), and Hajj (pilgrimage to Mecca).

8. Muslims practice the five daily prayers and other rituals such as the recitation of the Quran, fasting during the month of Ramadan, and performing Hajj.

9. Christians in India practice the teachings of Jesus Christ, including attending weekly services, participating in communion, and following the Ten Commandments.

10. Buddhists practice meditation, chanting, and the observance of festivals and rituals. They also follow the teachings of the Four Noble Truths and the Noble Eightfold Path.
