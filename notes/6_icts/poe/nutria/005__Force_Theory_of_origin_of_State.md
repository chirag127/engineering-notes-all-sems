

#### Force Theory of origin of State

1. The Force Theory of origin of State is a theory which states that the state was formed through the use of force, or the threat of force.

2. This theory is one of the oldest theories in political science and has been around since the time of Thomas Hobbes.

3. The main idea of the theory is that in order to form a state, a group of individuals must agree to give up some of their freedoms in exchange for protection from external threats.

4. The theory claims that the state is formed when individuals agree to give up some of their freedoms in exchange for protection from external threats.

5. The state is then maintained by the use of force, or the threat of force, to ensure that citizens abide by the laws of the state.

6. This theory is seen as a way to explain the origin of the state, but it is also seen as a way to explain the power dynamics of the state.

7. This theory has been used to explain the power of the state and how it is used to maintain order and control citizens.

8. Critics of this theory argue that it does not take into account the role of consent in the formation of the state and that it ignores the fact that states are formed through mutual agreement and not just through the use of force.
