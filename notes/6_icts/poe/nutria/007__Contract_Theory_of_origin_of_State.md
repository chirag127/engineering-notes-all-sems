
#### Contract Theory of origin of State

1. The Contract Theory of origin of State is a theory of political philosophy which states that the origin of the state is based on a contract between the governors and the governed. 
2. The theory was first proposed by Thomas Hobbes in his book Leviathan, where he argued that the state of nature was a state of war, and that people had to enter into a social contract in order to escape this state.
3. According to this theory, the state is formed when people agree to give up some of their rights in exchange for the protection of their lives and property.
4. The main idea behind this theory is that the state is formed through a voluntary agreement between the rulers and the ruled.
5. The Contract Theory of origin of State has been used to explain the origin of many modern states.
6. It has been argued that the Contract Theory of origin of State is a valid explanation for the origin of the modern state and that it is still relevant today.
