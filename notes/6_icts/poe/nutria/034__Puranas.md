
### Puranas

1. Puranas are a genre of Hindu religious texts which are believed to be written by ancient sages.

2. They are generally considered to be part of the Vedic literature, and are believed to have been composed in Sanskrit between 400 BCE and 400 CE.

3. The Puranas contain stories about the creation of the universe, the life and deeds of gods and goddesses, and the genealogy of kings.

4. They also contain accounts of the great wars between gods and demons, as well as stories of the Avataras (incarnations) of Vishnu.

5. The Puranas are divided into eighteen major texts, which are further divided into several sub-texts.

6. The most famous of these are the Bhagavata Purana, the Vishnu Purana, the Shiva Purana, and the Matsya Purana.

7. The Puranas are an important source of Hindu mythology, and are often used in Hindu religious ceremonies and rituals.
