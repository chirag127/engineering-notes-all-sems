
### Water Management in India and Ancient India

* In India, water management has been a top priority since ancient times. The earliest recorded water management practices date back to the Indus Valley Civilization, which developed sophisticated irrigation systems and water storage techniques.
* In recent years, India has made significant progress in water management through the implementation of various policies, such as the National Water Policy, the National Water Framework Law, and the National Water Mission. These policies have helped to increase the availability of clean water and reduce water pollution.
* Ancient India had an advanced system of water management, which included the construction of dams, canals, and tanks. These structures were used to store and distribute water for irrigation and other purposes.
* In modern India, the government has implemented a number of water management initiatives, such as the National Water Grid, the National Water Quality Monitoring Programme, and the National River Conservation Plan. These initiatives have helped to improve water quality and reduce water-related diseases.
* In addition, India has also implemented various water conservation measures, such as rainwater harvesting and watershed management. These measures have helped to reduce water wastage and improve water availability.
* The government has also taken steps to ensure that water is used efficiently, such as through the implementation of water pricing and metering systems. These measures have helped to reduce water wastage and improve water availability.
