
### Heterodox Sects in India

1. Heterodox sects in India are those which reject the authority of the Vedas and the Brahmanic priesthood. These sects include Jainism, Buddhism, and Ajivikas. 

2. Jainism is an ancient Indian religion that teaches non-violence and respect for all life. It is based on the teachings of Mahavira, who lived in the 6th century BCE.

3. Buddhism is a religion and philosophy based on the teachings of the Buddha, Siddhartha Gautama, who lived in the 5th century BCE. It teaches that suffering can be ended by following the Noble Eightfold Path.

4. Ajivikas were a heterodox sect that believed in predestination and denied the existence of an immortal soul. They were founded by Makkhali Gosala in the 6th century BCE.

5. Other sects include the Charvakas, who were materialists who denied the existence of gods, and the Pasupatas, who were dualists who believed in the existence of two gods.

6. Heterodox sects were opposed by the Brahmanic religion, which led to religious persecution and the decline of these sects in India.
