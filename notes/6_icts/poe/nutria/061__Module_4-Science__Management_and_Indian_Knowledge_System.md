
## Module 4-Science, Management and Indian Knowledge System

1. Science: Science is a systematic way of acquiring knowledge about the physical world through observation and experimentation. It involves the study of various fields such as mathematics, physics, chemistry, biology, engineering, and other related disciplines.

2. Management: Management is the process of organizing, planning, leading, and controlling resources in order to achieve specific goals. It involves the use of various tools and techniques to ensure that goals are met in an efficient and effective manner.

3. Indian Knowledge System: Indian Knowledge System is an ancient system of knowledge that has been passed down from generation to generation. It includes various aspects such as philosophy, religion, science, art, and literature. It has been developed over centuries and is based on the principles of harmony, balance, and interdependence.
