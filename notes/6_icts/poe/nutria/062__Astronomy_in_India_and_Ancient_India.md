
### Astronomy in India and Ancient India

* Astronomy has been practiced in India since ancient times, with references to it in Vedic literature and the Upanishads.
* Ancient Indian astronomers made significant contributions to the field, such as the calculation of the sidereal rotation period of the Earth, which is the time it takes for the Earth to rotate once around its axis.
* Ancient Indian astronomers were also the first to accurately calculate the length of the year, and the position of the planets in the night sky.
* During the medieval period, Indian astronomers developed several new astronomical instruments, including the astrolabe and the celestial globe.
* In the modern era, Indian astronomers have made significant contributions to the field of astrophysics, including the discovery of several new stars and planets.
* Indian astronomers have also played an important role in the development of space exploration, with Indian scientists and engineers playing a major role in the development of the Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO).
