
### Theatre as Cultural Heritage of India

1. Theatre has been a part of Indian culture for centuries, with evidence of theatrical performances dating back to the ancient Vedic period.

2. Indian theatre is a diverse and vibrant form of art that is influenced by the country's rich cultural heritage. Traditional Indian theatre includes folk theatre, classical theatre, and modern theatre.

3. Folk theatre is an integral part of Indian culture and is often performed at festivals and other special occasions. It is often performed in the open air and is characterised by its use of music, dance, and mime.

4. Classical theatre is the most popular form of theatre in India. It is based on the Sanskrit language and has been performed for centuries. It is characterised by its use of elaborate costumes, music, and dance.

5. Modern theatre in India is a relatively recent phenomenon. It is characterised by its use of contemporary themes and its focus on social issues.

6. Theatre in India is an important part of the country's cultural heritage. It is a form of art that is enjoyed by people of all ages and backgrounds, and it is a vital part of Indian culture.
