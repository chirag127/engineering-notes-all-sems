
### Urdu Literature in India

* Urdu literature is a rich and varied tradition of literature in the Urdu language, which is mainly spoken in Pakistan and India.
* Urdu literature has a long history, dating back to the 12th century. It has been influenced by many different cultures, including Persian, Arabic, and Hindi.
* The earliest form of Urdu literature was poetry, which was written in a style known as ghazal. This form of poetry has been popular for centuries, and is still popular today.
* Urdu literature also includes prose works such as novels, short stories, and essays. These works often focus on topics such as politics, religion, and social issues.
* Many famous writers have written in Urdu, including Faiz Ahmed Faiz, Mirza Ghalib, and Muhammad Iqbal.
* Urdu literature has also been an important part of the culture of India, and has been widely read and appreciated.
* Urdu literature has been translated into many languages, including English, French, German, and Spanish.
