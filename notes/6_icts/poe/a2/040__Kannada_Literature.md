### Kannada Literature

1. Kannada literature has a history of over a thousand years. The earliest extant literature dates from the 9th century CE.
2. The Kannada literature can be broadly divided into: Jain literature, Vaishnava literature and Virashaiva literature. The earliest existing record of Kannada poetry are the Kadambari by Banabhatta and the Chhinnabhiseka Charitamaha Purana by Pampa.
3. During the late 10th century, Kannada literature flourished under the patronage of Rashtrakuta dynasty. This classical literature was court-centric and elite in nature and included writing in genres such as prose, poetry, rhetoric, and grammar.
4. Several important literary works were written during the Western Ganga Dynasty rule in the 10th century. The earliest Kannada writing is attested in the Halmidi Inscription of 450 CE. The Kannada poetic literature flourished in multiple genres from aristocratic literature to devotional literature between the 9th and 12th centuries.
5. Dasa sahitya is a special form of literature that flourished in the Bhakti period of the 16th and 17th centuries. Kumara Vyasa and Gadugina Bharata by Naranappa are considered the magnum opus in Kannada literature. Modern Kannada literature includes M. Govinda Pai, Kuvempu, U. R. Ananthamurthy and Girish Karnad.
