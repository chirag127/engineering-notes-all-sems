### Famous Sanskrit Authors in India

1. Kalidasa: Kalidasa was one of the greatest Sanskrit authors who lived in the 4th-5th century CE. His works include Abhijnanashakuntala, Kumarsambhava, Raghuvamsha, and Meghaduta. His writings are considered as poetic and literary masterpieces.

2. Valmiki: Valmiki is the author of the Ramayana, one of the two major Sanskrit epics of ancient India. He is referred to as the 'adikavi', the first poet in Sanskrit literature. The Ramayana tells the story of Rama and Sita, and their battle against the demon king Ravana.

3. Vatsyayana: Vatsyayana was the author of the Kama Sutra, an ancient Indian Hindu text on human sexual pleasure. The Kama Sutra is considered as one of the oldest surviving manuals of erotic love.

4. Panini: Panini was an ancient Sanskrit grammarian who lived in the 4th century BCE. He formulated the 3995 rules of Sanskrit morphology, syntax, and semantics in his text 'Ashtadhyayi' that established the foundation for Sanskrit grammar.

5. Bharavi: Bharavi was a 6th-century Sanskrit author who wrote the epic poem Kiratarjuniya telling the story of Arjuna's battle with Shiva in the form of a mountain tribal. His writings are notable for their rich descriptions and bold metaphors.
