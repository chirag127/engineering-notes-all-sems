### The Upanishads

1. The Upanishads are ancient Hindu scriptures that contain some of the central philosophical concepts and ideas of Hinduism.
2. They are considered as part of the Vedas, the primary scriptures of Hinduism. There are over 200 Upanishads but only 13 are considered as major ones.
3. The Upanishads primarily discuss metaphysical questions about the existence of Brahman, Atman and the relationship between the two. Brahman is the eternal, infinite, omnipresent spiritual source of the universe. Atman refers to the eternal essence of the individual.
4. The Upanishads teach that Brahman and Atman are essentially the same - "Thou art That". This is the ultimate truth and the aim is to realize this unity through meditation and spiritual reflection. This is known as achieving Moksha or liberation from the cycle of birth and death.
5. Some of the core ideas in the Upanishads are karma, samsara, maya, meditation and yoga. The Upanishads have had a huge influence on Hindu philosophy and also on Buddhism, Jainism and other Indian philosophies. They contain some of the earliest mentions of yoga and meditation as paths to liberation.
6. The Upanishads are some of the most influential spiritual and philosophical works in Indian history and have inspired other religions including Buddhism. They provide a glimpse into the spiritual and philosophical debates in ancient India.
