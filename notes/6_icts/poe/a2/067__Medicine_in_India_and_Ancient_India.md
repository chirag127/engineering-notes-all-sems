### Medicine in India and Ancient India

1. Ancient Indian medicine, Ayurveda, has a long history of around 3,500 years. The earliest references to health practices and medical procedures are found in the Vedas which are the ancient scriptures of India.

2. Ayurveda system is based on the idea of balance in bodily systems and uses a comprehensive holistic approach. It emphasises on the prevention of disease and promotion of good health.

3. Sushruta Samhita and Charaka Samhita are two of the foundational texts of Ayurveda which describe 1,120 illnesses, 700 medicinal plants, 64 preparations from mineral sources and 57 preparations based on animal sources.

4. Surgical procedures were also practised in ancient India. Sushruta documented various surgical procedures and classified surgeries into eight categories. He contributed to the study of anatomy and human embryology.

5. Ancient Indians were pioneers in plastic surgery too. The technique of rhinoplasty or reconstructing the nose was developed in ancient India.

6. Scientific advances in other fields like mathematics and metallurgy also contributed to developments in medicine. For example, the idea of zero and the decimal system allowed precise representation of quantities of substances used for treatment. Metallurgical procedures led to creation of fine instruments for surgical procedures.

7. While ancient Indian medicine made notable contributions, it also had limitations as there was no understanding of bacteria and viruses and how they cause diseases. With increased understanding of diseases and modern scientific advances, today we have a wide range of treatment options using modern and traditional medicine practices.
