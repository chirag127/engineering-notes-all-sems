### Engineering and Architecture as Cultural Heritage of India

1. Ancient Indian civilizations like Indus Valley civilization, Vedic period civilization etc. had developed advanced engineering and architectural skills. Their expertise can be seen in the construction of planned cities, drainage systems, brick structures etc.
2. Major examples of engineering feats are the temples of South India like Tanjore temple, Lepakshi temple etc. They were constructed using heavy rocks and displayed excellent masonry skills. The Kailasanathar temple at Ellora was carved out of a single rock.
3. The iconic Qutub Minar and Red Fort display the excellence of Indo-Islamic architecture. The use of arches, vaults and calligraphy are unique features of this style. The Taj Mahal is recognized as one of the most beautiful buildings in the world and combines elements from Islamic, Persian, Turkish and Indian architectural styles.
4. The stepwells of Gujarat like Rani ki vav display unique architectural style to store water and beat the heat. They show excellent engineering skills to construct underwater structures and galleries.
5. The palaces of Rajasthan like City Palace, Udaipur and Hawa Mahal display a blend of Rajput and Mughal architectural styles. They show the prowess of craftsmen in constructing large edifices with delicate carvings and jharokhas.

The examples show that India has a rich cultural heritage of engineering and architecture which represents the skills, knowledge and creativity of artisans and craftsmen over centuries. They need to be preserved as national monuments and studied widely.
