### Urdu Literature in India

1. Urdu language developed in northern India around Delhi in the 12th-13th centuries. It incorporated vocabulary from Persian, Turkish, Arabic, Punjabi, and other languages.
2. Urdu poetry flourished particularly under the Mughal Empire. Renowned poets like Amir Khusraw, Mir Taqi Mir, Ghalib, and Iqbal wrote ghazals, nazms, masnavi, and rubai in Urdu. Ghalib is considered the greatest of the Urdu poets.
3. Urdu prose saw development in the 19th-20th centuries with the writings of novelists and short story writers like Premchand, Saadat Hasan Manto, Qurratulain Hyder, and Ismat Chughtai who depicted the societal changes of the time.
4. After the partition of India and Pakistan in 1947, a large number of Urdu speakers migrated to Pakistan. As a result, the center of Urdu literature also shifted to Pakistan where it continues to flourish today.
5. In India, Urdu literature is still being produced, especially in regions like Uttar Pradesh and Delhi which have a sizeable Urdu speaking population. Notable contemporary Indian Urdu writers include Munawwar Rana, Haider Qureshi, and Zehra Nigah.
