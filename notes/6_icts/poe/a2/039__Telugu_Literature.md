### Telugu Literature

1. Telugu literature refers to literature in the Telugu language. It has a history of over a thousand years. The earliest inscriptions in Telugu are found on the walls of Guntupalle and Chilakaluripet mandals in Krishna district dating back to 575 CE, while the first literary writings were composed by Thikkana, Errana, Yerrapragada and Nannaya during the 11th and 12th centuries.
2. Ancient Telugu literature includes Telugu Mahabharata, Telugu Ramayana, Telugu Bhagavatam etc. Written in genres like padyam (verse), satakam (collection of verses), vilasam, kavya, and natika (drama). The South Indian Shaiva saint cum philosopher Potuluri Veera Brahman illustrated the principle of religious tolerance and spread bhakti literature in simple Telugu.
3. The Vijayanagara Empire and its luminaries like Tenali Ramakrishna and Dhoyi enriched literature during the 14th-16th centuries. Krishnadeva Raya's Amuktamalyada is a famous Telugu poem describing the pangs of separation of lovers and comparing them with the pain of a devotee separated from God.
4. Post-Vijayanagara Telugu Literature includes Annamayya's devotional poems, Tallapaka Annamacharya regarded as the Pada-kavita Pitamaha in Telugu, Vemana- a popular Telugu philosopher and Bahinabai- a 17th century Marathi saint-poet who wrote in Telugu.
