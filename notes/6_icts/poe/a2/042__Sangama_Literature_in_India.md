### Sangama Literature in India

1. Sangama literature refers to the body of writings in Tamil produced during the Sangam period, roughly from 300 BCE to 300 CE. The literature deals with emotional and material topics and had 2,381 known poems in it.

2. The poems in Sangam literature were composed by 473 poets and cover various themes like love, heroism, ethics, and philosophies on topics such as governance and the uncertainty of life. They provide a window into the cultural and societal practices prevalent in ancient Tamilakam.

3. The Sangam literature consists of eight anthologies, categorized as Ettuttokai and Pattuppāṭṭu, each dealing with a different theme. The compositions invoke imagery from nature, use complex metaphors and subtly convey deeper themes through seemingly simple verses. They are believed to have laid the foundation for Classical Tamil poetry and literature.

4. The available Sangam literature is believed to be just a small fraction of the original body of works as much of the compositions were lost over time. The poems that survive today provide historians insights into the history, culture, and society of ancient Tamilakam, encompassing the regions of present-day Tamil Nadu, Kerala, and southern Karnataka. Their literary qualities and timeless themes have also led to their enduring appeal.
