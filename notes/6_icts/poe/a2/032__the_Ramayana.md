### The Ramayana

1. The Ramayana is an ancient Indian epic poem which narrates the struggle of the divine prince Rama to rescue his wife Sita from the demon king Ravana.
2. Rama is the prince of Ayodhya and the eldest son of King Dasharatha. He is an avatar of the god Vishnu.
3. Dasharatha has three queens - Kausalya, Kaikeyi and Sumitra. Rama is the son of Kausalya. Kaikeyi has two sons - Bharata and Lakshmana. Sumitra has two sons - Lakshmana and Shatrughna.
4. King Dasharatha decides to crown Rama as the prince regent. However, Kaikeyi demands that her son Bharata be made the king and that Rama be exiled to the forest for 14 years. Dasharatha reluctantly sends Rama to the forest. Lakshmana accompanies Rama.
5. In the forest, Rama's wife Sita is abducted by the demon king Ravana. Rama then wages a war against Ravana with the help of the monkey king Sugreeva and his general Hanuman.
6. After a series of battles, Rama defeats and kills Ravana. He rescues Sita and returns to Ayodhya. He is crowned as the king of Ayodhya, ushering in a golden age of prosperity and righteousness.
