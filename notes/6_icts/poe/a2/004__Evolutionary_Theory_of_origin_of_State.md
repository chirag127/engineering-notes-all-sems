#### Evolutionary Theory of origin of State

1. According to Evolutionary theorists, state is a product of gradual historical development. It arose out of the primitive tribal organizations of ancient societies.
2. As societies evolved, the institutions and practices of government also evolved in a gradual manner. There was no social contract or deliberate creation of state.
3. The main causes of evolution of state were:
- Growth in population and settlement: As population grew, simple tribal organizations were inadequate to provide security, settle disputes, and maintain law and order. This necessitated the emergence of more complex institutions of government.
- Increased economic interaction: Growth of economic activities and trade led to increased conflicts and disputes that could not be managed by informal institutions of tribe. This necessitated creation of formal institutions of state to regulate such interactions.
- Growth in social stratification: Inequality grew in societies and ruling class emerged. The elite class organized themselves and institutionalized their domination, creating state to perpetuate their power.
- Growth of warfare: As societies faced threats from external invasion and internal rebellions, informal tribal militias were replaced by organized professional armies. This increased the power of chieftains and rulers, contributing to evolution of state.

The evolutionary theory thus sees origin of state as a result of natural growth of societies and not as a deliberate creation to fulfill any social contract. The weaknesses and inefficacies of tribal organizations led to gradual development of state.
