### Physics in India and Ancient India

1. Ancient Indian sciences like mathematics, astronomy, metallurgy, and hydrology gave rise to many concepts in physics. Many theories and discoveries in physics were prevalent in ancient India:
- Concept of zero and place value system in mathematics enabled advances in arithmetic and algebra.
- Notions of gravity, relativity of time, and spherical Earth were proposed in ancient Indian astronomy.
- Metallurgical practices led to production of high-quality steel and creation of the Iron Pillar of Delhi, which exhibits properties of corrosion resistance.
- Water clocks and other devices were invented for timekeeping and measuring intervals.

2. Ancient Indian philosophers proposed ideas resembling modern physics concepts:
- Notion of atomism by Kanada and others, proposing that matter is composed of infinitesimal particles.
- Theory of light by Gautama, stating that light travels in a straight line and gets reflected on striking surfaces.
- Proposal by Brahmagupta that Earth is spherical and attracted toward the center.

3. Such discoveries highlight the scientific temper in ancient India and its applications toward understanding the natural world via principles of physics, even though the modern scientific method was yet to be formulated. The confluence of philosophy, mathematics, and spirituality in ancient India led to distinctive ideas in physics that were precursors to modern concepts.
