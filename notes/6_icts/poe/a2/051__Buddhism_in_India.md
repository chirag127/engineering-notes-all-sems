### Buddhism in India

1. Buddhism was founded in ancient India by Siddhartha Gautama, who is known as the Buddha, in the 6th century BCE.
2. The Buddha was an ascetic who meditated on the nature of life and taught the Four Noble Truths, the Eightfold Path, and the concepts of karma and rebirth.
3. The Buddha's teachings spread rapidly across ancient India, gaining royal patronage. The Mauryan emperor Ashoka promoted Buddhism and helped it spread beyond India to other Asian countries.
4. The Buddha's teachings were recorded in the sutras, and different branches or schools of Buddhism developed with distinct philosophies and practices but sharing common beliefs.
5. Buddhism influenced the culture and traditions of ancient India, seen in art, architecture, and literature. Many ancient Buddhist monasteries and temples were built, some surviving today as UNESCO World Heritage Sites.
6. Buddhism declined in India starting around the 12th century due to the expansion of Hinduism and later the arrival of Islam, but is still practiced in various forms today, especially in Himalayan regions and Southeast Asia.
