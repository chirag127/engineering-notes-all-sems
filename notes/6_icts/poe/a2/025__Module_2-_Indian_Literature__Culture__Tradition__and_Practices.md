## Module 2- Indian Literature, Culture, Tradition, and Practices

- Indian literature includes religious texts like Vedas, Ramayana, Mahabharata; Sanskrit plays by Kalidasa; Tamil Sangam literature; Bhakti movement poetry.
- Indian culture is one of the oldest and unique cultures of the world. It includes festivals like Diwali, Holi; classical dances like Bharatnatyam; Indian cuisine like biryani, pani puri; traditional dresses like saree, dhoti; among others.
- Indian tradition includes religions like Hinduism, Buddhism, Jainism; yoga and meditation; joint families; respect for elders; farming and agriculture festivals; handicrafts.
- Indian practices include namaste as a greeting; applying tilak; rangoli designs in front of the house; prayers and aarti in temples and at home.
