## Module 5- Cultural Heritage and Performing Arts

I. Cultural Heritage

1. Cultural heritage refers to the legacy of physical artifacts and intangible attributes of a group or society that are inherited from past generations.
2. This includes monuments, groups of buildings, and sites which are of historical, cultural, or other form of significance.
3. Preserving cultural heritage is important as it gives a sense of identity to communities and future generations. It represents tradition and history of a place.

II. Performing Arts

1. Performing arts include dance, music, opera, theatre, and other art forms where artists perform in front of an audience.
2. Performing arts are a form of cultural heritage as they represent the artistic traditions of a community. They are passed down through generations and evolve over time.
3. Different countries and regions have their own unique styles of performing arts. They are studied to understand the culture, customs, and history of that society.
4. Performing arts are a means of expression and entertainment. They boost creativity and also have economic value through performances and tourism.
