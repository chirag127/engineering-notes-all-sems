#### IBM Big Data strategy

1. IBM focuses on providing solutions for big data problems rather than just tools and technologies. It offers end-to-end solutions for data scientists and business users to solve key business problems using big data.

2. IBM's big data platform is Hadoop-based and built around IBM BigInsights along with IBM Streams for real-time analytics. It offers security, governance and management capabilities and integrates with analytics tools like SPSS and data warehouses.

3. IBM emphasizes gaining business value from big data via cognitive capabilities. It leverages machine learning, natural language processing and its Watson technology to derive insights and predictions from data. The insights can be embedded into business processes and applications.

4. IBM's big data strategy involves partnering with various technology companies to provide integrated solutions. It has partnerships with Cloudera, Hortonworks, MongoDB, DataStax, etc. to offer customers a variety of data fabric choices. It also works with analytics and business application partners.

5. According to IBM, the key to effective big data strategies is around data governance and data science skills. It offers various training programs, certifications and communities to enable organizations build the right skills and best practices around big data.
