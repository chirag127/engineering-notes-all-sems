### IBM Big Data strategy

1. Focus on cloud - IBM is focused on providing big data solutions on the cloud. It acquired various companies like Cleversafe, Compose, etc. to strengthen its cloud offerings for big data storage and analytics.
2. Invest in analytics - IBM made significant investments in analytics by acquiring companies like SPSS, i2 and Vivisimo. It integrated their capabilities into its analytics and AI platforms like Watson to provide advanced analytics and AI solutions for big data.
3. Open source contributions - IBM contributes to open source big data projects like Apache Hadoop, Spark, Kafka, etc. It uses these open source technologies in its big data platform and also contributes to their development. This helps IBM incorporate the latest innovations from open source community into its solutions.
4. Partnerships - IBM also partners with big data leaders like Hortonworks to enhance its big data solutions. These partnerships help IBM extend its reach and provide more powerful solutions to its clients.
5. Industry solutions - IBM has developed industry specific solutions for big data problems in various sectors like banking, healthcare, telecommunications, etc. These solutions are tailored to the specific requirements of industries to address their key challenges.

The above points cover the key highlights of IBM's big data strategy focusing on cloud, analytics, open source and partnerships to develop innovative solutions for various industries. The strategy aims to establish IBM as a leading player in the big data market.
