
### Drivers for Big Data

1. Cost Reduction: Big Data technologies enable businesses to reduce costs by efficiently utilizing and managing data.

2. Increased Revenue: Big Data technologies can help businesses identify patterns and trends in customer behavior, allowing them to better target their marketing efforts and increase sales.

3. Improved Decision Making: Big Data technologies provide businesses with access to vast amounts of data, which can be used to make more informed decisions.

4. Improved Customer Experience: Big Data technologies allow businesses to better understand and anticipate customer needs, leading to improved customer service and satisfaction.

5. Improved Efficiency: Big Data technologies enable businesses to automate processes, resulting in improved efficiency and cost savings.
