
## Unit 1 - Introduction to Big Data

1. Big Data is a term used to describe the large volume of data that is generated by businesses, organizations, and individuals in the digital world. 
2. It is data that is too large or complex to be managed and analyzed using traditional methods. 
3. Big Data is characterized by its volume, variety, velocity, and veracity. 
4. Volume refers to the sheer amount of data that is collected. Variety refers to the different types of data that can be collected, such as structured, semi-structured, and unstructured data. 
5. Velocity refers to the speed at which data is generated and needs to be analyzed. Veracity refers to the accuracy and reliability of the data. 
6. Big Data is used to gain insights into customer behavior, market trends, and other areas of business. 
7. It can be used to identify patterns, trends, and correlations in data, as well as to make predictions about future events. 
8. Big Data is also used to improve operational efficiency and to make better decisions. 
9. Companies use Big Data to improve customer experience, optimize processes, and to gain a competitive advantage. 
10. Big Data can be collected from a variety of sources, including web and social media, sensors, and mobile devices. 
11. It is important to ensure that the data is collected, stored, and analyzed in a secure and ethical manner.
