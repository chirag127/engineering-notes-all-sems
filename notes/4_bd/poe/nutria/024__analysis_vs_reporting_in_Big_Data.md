
### Analysis vs Reporting in Big Data

1. Analysis is the process of exploring and understanding data in order to identify patterns, trends, and relationships. It involves the use of statistical techniques and tools to uncover insights from large datasets.

2. Reporting is the process of presenting the findings from an analysis in a meaningful way. It involves the use of charts, graphs, tables, and other visualizations to communicate the findings to stakeholders.

3. Analysis is used to identify patterns and trends in data, whereas reporting is used to communicate the findings of an analysis.

4. Analysis is usually done by data scientists or analysts, whereas reporting is typically done by business users.

5. Analysis is a more technical process, whereas reporting is more of a business process.

6. Analysis requires a deep understanding of the data, whereas reporting requires a basic understanding of the data and the ability to create meaningful visualizations.
