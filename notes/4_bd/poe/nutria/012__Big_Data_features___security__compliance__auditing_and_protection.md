
### Big Data features – security, compliance, auditing and protection

1. Security: Big Data security is a critical part of any organization’s data management strategy. Security measures are designed to protect data from unauthorized access and use, and to ensure that data is securely stored and transmitted. Security measures may include encryption, authentication, access control, data masking, and data loss prevention.

2. Compliance: Organizations must comply with various laws and regulations when managing and processing data. Compliance measures are designed to ensure that data is managed in accordance with applicable laws and regulations. Compliance measures may include data classification, data retention, and data privacy.

3. Auditing: Auditing is an important part of any data management strategy. Auditing measures are designed to ensure that data is accurately recorded and reported. Auditing measures may include data quality checks, data integrity checks, and other data validation processes.

4. Protection: Data protection measures are designed to protect data from unauthorized access, use, and disclosure. Protection measures may include data encryption, data masking, data access control, and data loss prevention.
