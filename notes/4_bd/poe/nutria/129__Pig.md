
### Pig

- Pigs are omnivorous animals, meaning they eat both plants and animals.
- Pigs have an excellent sense of smell and can be trained to detect certain odors.
- Pigs have four toes on each foot and walk on the tips of their toes.
- Pigs have a four-chambered stomach which helps them to digest their food more efficiently.
- Pigs have a social hierarchy and will compete for dominance within a group.
- Pigs can reach speeds of up to 11 mph and can swim for long distances.
- Pigs have a highly developed sense of touch and can recognize objects by touch.
- Pigs are intelligent animals and can be trained to perform various tasks.
