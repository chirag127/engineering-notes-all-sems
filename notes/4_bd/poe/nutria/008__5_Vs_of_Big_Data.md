
### 5 Vs of Big Data
1. Volume: The amount of data generated and stored by organizations is growing exponentially. Big data is characterized by its large volume, which requires special storage and computing capabilities to process.

2. Variety: Big data includes structured data, such as customer records in a database, as well as unstructured data, such as emails, videos, and social media posts.

3. Velocity: Big data is generated at a rapid rate, and organizations must be able to process it quickly to gain insights and make decisions.

4. Veracity: Big data can be noisy, inconsistent, and incomplete. Organizations must be able to identify and clean up data to ensure accuracy.

5. Value: Big data can be used to uncover insights and make better decisions. Organizations must identify the data that is most valuable to them and focus on collecting and analyzing it.
