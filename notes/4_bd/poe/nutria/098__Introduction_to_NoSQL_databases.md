
#### Introduction to NoSQL databases

* NoSQL stands for "Not Only SQL" and is an alternative to traditional relational databases. 
* NoSQL databases are designed to provide scalability, availability, and performance for large datasets. 
* NoSQL databases are also known as "non-relational" or "distributed" databases. 
* NoSQL databases are characterized by their lack of a fixed schema, which allows them to store data in a more flexible and dynamic way. 
* NoSQL databases are often used for applications that require large datasets with high availability and scalability. 
* NoSQL databases typically use a key-value store, document store, graph database, or column-oriented store to store data. 
* NoSQL databases are often used in distributed systems, as they can scale horizontally across multiple servers and provide high availability. 
* NoSQL databases are typically more cost-effective than traditional relational databases, as they can be deployed on commodity hardware. 
* NoSQL databases can also be used to store data that does not fit well into a relational model, such as unstructured data or data from web applications.
