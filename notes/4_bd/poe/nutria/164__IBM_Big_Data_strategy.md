

#### IBM Big Data Strategy

1. IBM's big data strategy is focused on leveraging data to help organizations make better decisions and improve their operations.

2. IBM offers a wide range of products and services to help organizations manage and analyze their data, including cloud-based solutions, data warehouses, and analytics tools.

3. IBM has developed a comprehensive set of tools and methods to help organizations extract insights from their data. These include machine learning, natural language processing, and predictive analytics.

4. IBM's big data strategy also includes initiatives to help organizations gain insights from unstructured data, such as social media, web traffic, and IoT data.

5. IBM has also developed a number of open source projects to help organizations manage and analyze their data more effectively. These include Apache Spark, Hadoop, and IBM Watson.

6. IBM's big data strategy is focused on helping organizations use their data to gain insights, improve their operations, and make better decisions.
