
### Big Data Characteristics
1. Volume: Big data is characterized by the large amount of data generated by businesses, organizations, and individuals.
2. Variety: Big data comes in many different forms, such as structured, unstructured, and semi-structured data.
3. Velocity: Big data is generated at an extremely fast rate, and it must be processed quickly in order to be useful.
4. Veracity: The quality of the data is important, as it must be accurate and reliable in order to be useful.
5. Value: Big data must be analyzed in order to extract actionable insights that can be used to inform decisions.
