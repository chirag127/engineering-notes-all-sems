### 5 Vs of Big Data

The 5 Vs of Big Data refer to the five key characteristics that define Big Data: Volume, Velocity, Variety, Veracity, and Value.

1. **Volume**: This refers to the vast amount of data that is generated every second. With the rise of the Internet of Things (IoT) and social media, the amount of data being generated is growing at an exponential rate.

2. **Velocity**: This refers to the speed at which data is generated and processed. The faster the data is generated and processed, the more valuable it is.

3. **Variety**: This refers to the different types of data that are generated. Data can come in many different formats, including structured, semi-structured, and unstructured data.

4. **Veracity**: This refers to the accuracy and reliability of the data. Data must be accurate and reliable in order to be useful.

5. **Value**: This refers to the value that can be derived from the data. Data must be analyzed and processed in order to extract valuable insights.

A mnemonic to remember the 5 Vs of Big Data is "Vast Volume, Varied Variety, Velocity, Veracity, and Value."
