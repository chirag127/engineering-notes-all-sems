# Zookeeper

A zookeeper is a professional who is responsible for the care and management of animals in a zoo. Some of the key responsibilities of a zookeeper include:

1. Feeding and providing water to the animals.
2. Cleaning and maintaining the animals' enclosures.
3. Observing and monitoring the animals' behavior and health.
4. Administering medication and providing medical care as needed.
5. Assisting with breeding programs and the raising of young animals.
6. Providing enrichment activities to stimulate the animals' physical and mental well-being.
7. Educating visitors about the animals and their conservation.

Zookeepers typically have a background in animal science, biology, or a related field, and may have completed specialized training in zoo animal management. They must have a strong understanding of animal behavior and the ability to work safely with a variety of species. Zookeepers must also be able to communicate effectively with colleagues, veterinarians, and the public.
