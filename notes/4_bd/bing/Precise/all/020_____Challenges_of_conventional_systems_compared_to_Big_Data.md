### Challenges of Conventional Systems Compared to Big Data

1. **Scalability**: Conventional systems are designed to handle a limited amount of data. As the volume of data grows, these systems struggle to scale up to meet the demand. Big Data systems, on the other hand, are designed to scale horizontally, allowing them to handle large volumes of data with ease.

2. **Data Variety**: Conventional systems are designed to handle structured data, such as data stored in relational databases. Big Data systems, on the other hand, are designed to handle a wide variety of data types, including structured, semi-structured, and unstructured data.

3. **Real-time Processing**: Conventional systems are not designed to handle real-time data processing. Big Data systems, on the other hand, are designed to process data in real-time, allowing for faster decision making and more timely insights.

4. **Cost**: Conventional systems can be expensive to scale up as the volume of data grows. Big Data systems, on the other hand, are designed to be cost-effective, allowing organizations to store and process large volumes of data at a lower cost.

5. **Flexibility**: Conventional systems are rigid in their design, making it difficult to adapt to changing data requirements. Big Data systems, on the other hand, are designed to be flexible, allowing organizations to easily adapt to changing data requirements.

These are some of the key challenges that conventional systems face when compared to Big Data systems. Big Data systems are designed to overcome these challenges, allowing organizations to effectively manage and analyze large volumes of data.
