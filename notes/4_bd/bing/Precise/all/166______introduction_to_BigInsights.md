#### Introduction to BigInsights

BigInsights is an IBM product designed to help firms analyze the increasing volume, velocity, and veracity of data of their interest . It is important to note that BigInsights does not replace a relational database management system (DBMS) or a traditional data warehouse .

BigInsights is designed to help firms analyze a diverse range of data, including data that is loosely structured or largely unstructured. Various types of text data fall into this category .

In summary, BigInsights is a tool that can complement other software you may already have, to help you analyze and make sense of the large amounts of data that your organization may be dealing with.
