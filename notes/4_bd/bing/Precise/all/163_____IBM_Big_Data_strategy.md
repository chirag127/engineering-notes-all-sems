### IBM Big Data strategy

- IBM, a US-based computer hardware and software manufacturer, had implemented a Big Data strategy.
- The company offered solutions to store, manage, and analyze the huge amounts of data generated daily.
- IBM equipped large and small companies to make informed business decisions.
- Smarter Planet was a corporate initiative of IBM, which sought to highlight how government and business leaders were capturing the potential of smarter systems to achieve economic and sustainable growth and societal progress.
- IBM has a six-step process to design a data strategy: 
  1. Understand your business objectives.
  2. Assess your current state.
  3. Map out data strategy framework.
  4. Define your data’s target.
- IBM recommends giving data assets and accelerators top priority, developing a process and culture around data that enables true standardization, re-use, portability, speed to action and risk reduction across the end-to-end data lifecycle.
