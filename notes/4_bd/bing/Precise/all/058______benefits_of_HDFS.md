#### Benefits of HDFS

Hadoop Distributed File System (HDFS) is an open-source subproject within Hadoop that offers several core benefits when dealing with big data:

1. **Fault tolerance**: HDFS has been designed to detect faults and automatically recover quickly, ensuring continuity and reliability .
2. **Speed**: Because of its cluster architecture, it can maintain 2 GB of data per second .
3. **Access to more types of data**: Specifically, streaming data .
4. **Compatibility and portability**: HDFS is designed to be portable across multiple hardware platforms and to be compatible with a variety of underlying operating systems .
5. **Scalability**: HDFS provides high aggregate data bandwidth and can scale to hundreds of nodes in a single cluster .
6. **Data locality**: HDFS stores data on the compute nodes, reducing the distance between the data and the computation, and thus improving performance .
7. **Cost-effectiveness**: The DataNodes that store the data rely on inexpensive off-the-shelf hardware, which cuts storage costs. Also, because HDFS is open source, there's no licensing fee .
8. **Large data set storage**: HDFS can store large amounts of data .

These benefits make HDFS a powerful tool for dealing with big data. It is fast, reliable, and cost-effective, making it an ideal choice for many organizations.
