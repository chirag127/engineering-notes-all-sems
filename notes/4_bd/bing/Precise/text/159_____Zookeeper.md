### Zookeeper

- A zookeeper is a professional who is responsible for the care and management of animals in a zoo.
- Zookeepers are responsible for the daily care of the animals, including feeding, cleaning, and providing enrichment.
- They also monitor the health and behavior of the animals and work with veterinarians to provide medical care when needed.
- Zookeepers may also be involved in breeding programs, research, and conservation efforts.
- To become a zookeeper, one typically needs a degree in a related field such as biology or animal science, as well as experience working with animals.
- The job can be physically demanding and may involve working outdoors in all weather conditions.
- Zookeepers must also have strong communication skills, as they often interact with the public and provide educational programs.
- The role of a zookeeper is crucial in ensuring the well-being of the animals in their care and in promoting conservation and education.
