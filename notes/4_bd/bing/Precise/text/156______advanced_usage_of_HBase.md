#### Advanced Usage of HBase

HBase is a distributed, scalable, big data store that is used for random, real-time read/write access to large datasets. It is used in various fields and industries, including healthcare, e-commerce, and sports, among others.

- In the healthcare sector, HBase is used for storing genome sequences and disease history of people or a particular area .
- In the field of e-commerce, HBase is used for storing logs about customer search history and it also performs analytics and target advertisement for better business insights .
- In sports, HBase is used to store match details and the history of each match .

HBase has two fundamental key structures: the row key and the column key. Both can be used to convey meaning, by either the data they store, or by exploiting their sorting order. These keys can be used to solve commonly found problems when designing storage solutions .

It is important to note that HBase is not a traditional database system and does not have features such as typed columns, transactions, advanced query languages, or secondary indexes. It is best used when there is a need to store huge volumes of data and high scalability is required .
