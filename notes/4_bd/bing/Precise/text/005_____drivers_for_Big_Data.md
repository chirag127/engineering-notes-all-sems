### Drivers for Big Data

1. **Volume**: The amount of data being generated and stored is growing at an exponential rate, driven by the increasing use of digital devices and the Internet of Things (IoT).
2. **Velocity**: The speed at which data is being generated and processed is also increasing, driven by the need for real-time analysis and decision-making.
3. **Variety**: Data is being generated in a wide variety of formats, including structured, semi-structured, and unstructured data, driven by the increasing use of social media, mobile devices, and other digital platforms.
4. **Veracity**: The accuracy and reliability of data is becoming increasingly important, driven by the need for data-driven decision-making and the potential consequences of inaccurate or unreliable data.
5. **Value**: The potential value that can be derived from big data is driving its adoption, as organizations seek to gain insights and make data-driven decisions to improve their operations and competitiveness.
