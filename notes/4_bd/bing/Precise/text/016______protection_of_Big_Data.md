#### Protection of Big Data

Big data includes big privacy concerns. To safeguard big data and ensure it can be used for analytics, you need to create a framework for privacy protection that can handle the volume, velocity, variety, and value of big data as it is moved between environments, processed, analyzed, and shared.

The key to protecting the privacy of your big data while still optimizing its value is ongoing review of four critical data management activities :
1. **Data collection**
2. **Retention and archiving**
3. **Data use**, including use in testing, DevOps, and other data masking scenarios
4. **Creating and updating disclosure policies and practices**

Big data security’s mission is clear enough: keep out on unauthorized users and intrusions with firewalls, strong user authentication, end-user training, and intrusion protection systems (IPS) and intrusion detection systems (IDS). In case someone does gain access, encrypt your data in transit and at rest.
