#### How Zookeeper helps in monitoring a cluster

- Zookeeper helps maintain configuration information, naming, and group services for distributed applications .
- It implements different protocols on the cluster so that the application should not implement on their own .
- Zookeeper provides a single coherent view of multiple machines .
- As long as a majority of hosts are up, the service will be available .
- Zookeeper monitoring helps make sure the total node count inside the ZooKeeper tree is consistent .
- Zookeeper is one of the best cluster coordination systems that make use of the most durable synchronization methods to maintain the cluster's nodes' flawless connectivity .
- Running a Prometheus monitoring service is the easiest way to ingest and record ZooKeeper's metrics .
- There are several top ZooKeeper monitoring tools available, including Sematext, Prometheus & Grafana, ManageEngine, Site24x7, OpsView, Instana, SignalFx, Datadog, and AppDynamics .
