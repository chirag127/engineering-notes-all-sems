Auditing of big data is the process of examining and evaluating the quality, reliability, and integrity of data collected and analyzed by organizations for various purposes. Auditing of big data can help identify risks, errors, fraud, and inefficiencies in the data lifecycle, and provide assurance and recommendations for improvement. Auditing of big data can also help ensure compliance with laws, regulations, and standards related to data protection, privacy, and security.
