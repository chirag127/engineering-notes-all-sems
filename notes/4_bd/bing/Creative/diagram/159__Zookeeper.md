Zookeeper is a distributed coordination service that provides configuration information, naming, synchronization, and group services for distributed applications. Zookeeper has a simple client-server architecture, where clients are nodes (machines) that use the service and servers are nodes that provide the service. Zookeeper also has a hierarchical namespace of nodes, called znodes, that store data and have permissions.
