Big data is a term that describes large, hard-to-manage volumes of data – both structured and unstructured – that inundate businesses on a day-to-day basis. Big data can be analyzed for insights that improve decisions and give confidence for making strategic business moves.

Big data is important because it allows businesses to see the whole picture, making better-informed decisions that will enable them to operate more efficiently with less risk. It provides businesses with information about their customers and how they interact with the company, which leads to:

- Cost savings
- Time savings
- Understanding the market conditions
- Social media listening
- Boosting customer acquisition and retention
- Solving advertisers' problems and offering marketing insights
- Driving innovations and product development
- Improving customer service and satisfaction
- Enhancing security and fraud detection
- Optimizing operational performance and quality
- Increasing competitiveness and profitability
- Supporting research and development
- Enabling new business models and opportunities
- Empowering employees and stakeholders
- Creating social and environmental benefits
