The history of big data innovation can be traced back to ancient times, when humans used tally sticks, abacuses, libraries and mechanical computers to store and process data. However, the term "big data" was coined only in 2005 by Roger Mougalas from O'Reilly Media, to refer to a large set of data that is almost impossible to manage and process using traditional business intelligence tools .
