IBM Big Data strategy is a set of solutions and services that help organizations store, manage, and analyze large and diverse data sets from various sources. IBM Big Data strategy aims to enable data-driven decision making, innovation, and competitive advantage.

IBM Cloud Pak for Data is a unified data and AI platform that provides a range of data services, such as data integration, data governance, data quality, data catalog, data virtualization, data science, and data analytics. IBM Cloud Pak for Data runs on Red Hat OpenShift Container Platform and can be deployed on any cloud or on-premises environment.

IBM Cloud Pak for Business Automation is a platform that provides automation services, such as business process management, document processing, content management, decision management, and robotic process automation. IBM Cloud Pak for Business Automation leverages AI and data from IBM Cloud Pak for Data to optimize and automate business workflows and decisions.

IBM Big Data strategy helps organizations to:

- Collect and integrate data from various sources, such as structured, semi-structured, and unstructured data, from different sources, and in different sizes from terabytes to zettabytes.
- Manage and govern data with security, privacy, and compliance policies and standards.
- Analyze and explore data with advanced analytic techniques, such as machine learning, natural language processing, and computer vision.
- Act on data insights with real-time and predictive models that help improve business outcomes and customer experiences.
- Innovate and create new products and services with data and AI capabilities.
