#### Data ingest with Flume and Sqoop in HDFS

Flume and Sqoop are two tools that can be used to ingest data from different sources into HDFS. Flume is mainly used for streaming data, such as logs, events, or messages, while Sqoop is mainly used for bulk data, such as relational databases, data warehouses, or structured datastores.
