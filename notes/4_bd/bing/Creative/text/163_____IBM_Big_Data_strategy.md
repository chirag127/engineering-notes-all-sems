### IBM Big Data strategy

- IBM Big Data strategy is a corporate initiative that aims to provide solutions for storing, managing, and analyzing the large volumes of data generated daily by various sources, such as social media, sensors, mobile devices, etc.
- IBM Big Data strategy is part of its Smarter Planet vision, which seeks to leverage the potential of smarter systems to achieve economic and sustainable growth and societal progress  .
- IBM Big Data strategy consists of four key components: Big Data platform, Big Data services, Big Data solutions, and Big Data ecosystem .
  - Big Data platform: IBM offers a comprehensive and integrated platform that supports various types of data, such as structured, unstructured, and semi-structured, and various sources, such as cloud, on-premise, or hybrid. The platform includes products such as IBM InfoSphere BigInsights, IBM InfoSphere Streams, IBM PureData System for Analytics, IBM PureData System for Hadoop, IBM PureData System for Operational Analytics, IBM DB2 with BLU Acceleration, IBM Cognos, IBM SPSS, and IBM Watson  .
  - Big Data services: IBM provides a range of services to help clients design, implement, and optimize their Big Data strategy, such as consulting, implementation, integration, migration, support, education, and training. The services cover various domains, such as analytics, data management, data governance, data quality, data security, data privacy, and data monetization  .
  - Big Data solutions: IBM delivers industry-specific and cross-industry solutions that address the business challenges and opportunities of Big Data, such as customer analytics, fraud detection, risk management, operational efficiency, innovation, and social media analytics. The solutions are based on the IBM Big Data platform and leverage the IBM Big Data services and ecosystem  .
  - Big Data ecosystem: IBM collaborates with a network of partners, such as technology providers, solution providers, academic institutions, research organizations, and industry associations, to enhance the value and adoption of Big Data solutions. The ecosystem includes partners such as Cloudera, Hortonworks, MapR, SAS, SAP, Oracle, Microsoft, Teradata, Accenture, Deloitte, PwC, and MIT   .
- IBM Big Data strategy aims to help clients gain insights from their data, improve their decision making, enhance their performance, and create competitive advantage. IBM Big Data strategy also supports the development of cognitive computing and artificial intelligence, which are the next frontiers of Big Data analytics   .
