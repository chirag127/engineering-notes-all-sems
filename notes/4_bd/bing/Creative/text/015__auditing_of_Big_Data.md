#### Auditing of Big Data

- Big data refers to data sets that are large, fast and diverse, and that can provide valuable insights for organizations to innovate and improve their performance .
- Auditing big data involves assessing the risks, controls and governance of big data programs, as well as the quality, reliability and usefulness of the data and analytics outputs  .
- Auditing big data requires auditors to have a good understanding of the big data concepts, components, technologies and tools, as well as the business objectives and success criteria of the big data program .
- Auditing big data also requires auditors to adopt new audit methodologies and techniques, such as data-driven auditing, continuous auditing, and audit analytics, to cope with the volume, velocity and variety of big data  .
- Auditing big data poses many challenges for auditors, such as data quality, data security, data privacy, data ethics, data governance, data integration, data validation, data interpretation, and data visualization   .
- Auditing big data can provide many benefits for auditors, such as enhancing audit efficiency, effectiveness, coverage, and value, as well as improving audit communication, reporting, and assurance    .
