### IBM Big Data strategy

IBM Big Data strategy is the company's approach to provide solutions for storing, managing and analyzing large and diverse data sets that include structured, semi-structured and unstructured data from various sources and in different sizes. IBM Big Data strategy aims to help organizations gain new insights, make better and faster decisions, and create new products and services based on data.

Some of the key aspects of IBM Big Data strategy are:

- IBM leverages open source software such as Apache Hadoop, Apache Spark and the Hadoop ecosystem as cost-effective, flexible and scalable data processing and storage tools that can handle the volume, velocity and variety of data being generated today.
- IBM partners with Cloudera, a leading provider of enterprise-grade, secure and governed data lake solutions based on Hadoop, to offer a unified platform that combines Cloudera's distribution with IBM's product ecosystem.
- IBM offers a hybrid SQL-on-Hadoop engine called IBM Db2 Big SQL that enables low latency, high performance and a single database connection for disparate data sources, such as relational databases, NoSQL databases, object stores and Hadoop clusters.
- IBM provides a real-time data replication solution called IBM Big Replicate for Hadoop that minimizes downtime and ensures data consistency across different Hadoop distributions, on-premises and cloud data storage sites.
- IBM enables data scientists and analysts to build and train AI and machine learning models, and prepare and analyze big data, all in a flexible hybrid cloud environment using IBM Watson Studio, a collaborative platform that integrates data and AI tools.
- IBM aligns its Big Data strategy with its Smarter Planet initiative, which seeks to highlight how government and business leaders can use smarter systems to achieve economic and sustainable growth and societal progress.
