#### Pig - Introduction to PIG

- Pigs are mammals of the family Suidae, which includes eight genera and 16 species of wild and domestic pigs.
- Pigs are also known as hogs or swine. Male pigs are called boars and female pigs are called sows .
- Pigs are omnivorous animals that can eat a variety of foods, such as leaves, roots, fruits, rodents, reptiles, and corn .
- Pigs have a thick skin with sparse bristles and a long snout that is used for digging and smelling . They have sharp tusks that help them fight and defend themselves.
- Pigs are one of the most intelligent domesticated animals and can learn various tasks and tricks. They have poor eyesight but a great sense of smell.
- Pigs are found and raised all over the world, and provide valuable products to humans, such as pork, lard, leather, glue, fertilizer, and medicine .
- Pigs can be classified into three types based on their use and characteristics: lard pigs, bacon pigs, and pork pigs. Lard pigs are large and fatty, bacon pigs are smaller and leaner, and pork pigs are the smallest and produce tender meat.
- Pigs can weigh between 300 and 700 pounds (140 and 300 kilograms) when fully grown, and sometimes much more. They can live up to 25 years in the wild.
- Pigs reproduce by mating and giving birth to a litter of young called piglets or shoats . A sow can have up to 12 piglets at a time, and they usually nurse for three to five weeks. Piglets can double their weight in one week.
