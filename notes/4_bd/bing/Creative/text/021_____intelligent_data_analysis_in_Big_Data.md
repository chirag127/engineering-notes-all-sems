### Intelligent Data Analysis in Big Data

- Intelligent Data Analysis (IDA) is one of the most important approaches in the field of data mining, which attracts great concerns from the researchers.
- IDA aims to extract useful knowledge and insights from large and complex datasets, using various techniques such as data preparation, data visualization, predictive modeling, and machine learning .
- Big Data refers to the massive volume, variety, velocity, and veracity of data that exceeds the capacity of traditional data processing systems.
- Big Data Analytics (BDA) is the application of advanced analytic techniques, including data mining, statistical analysis, and predictive modeling on big datasets as new business intelligence practice .
- BDA uses computational intelligence techniques to transform raw data into valuable information and actionable insights for decision making.
- AI and BDA work together to enhance the efficiency and effectiveness of data analysis, by automating and simplifying the complex and time-consuming tasks involved in IDA.
- AI and BDA also enable users to work with, manipulate, and surface actionable insights faster from large, complex datasets, by identifying patterns, relationships, anomalies, and trends in the data .
- AI and BDA can provide various benefits for various domains and applications, such as healthcare, education, finance, marketing, security, and social media, by improving the quality, accuracy, and reliability of data analysis and decision making  .
