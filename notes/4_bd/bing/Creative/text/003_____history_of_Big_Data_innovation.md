### History of Big Data innovation

- Big Data is the term used to describe the large and complex datasets that are generated from various sources and require advanced techniques and technologies to store, process, and analyze.
- The concept of Big Data is not new, as humans have been collecting and analyzing data for centuries. However, the term Big Data was coined in the late 1990s by John Mashey, a computer scientist and executive at Silicon Graphics, who used it to describe the challenges and opportunities of data-intensive computing.
- The emergence of Big Data innovation was driven by several factors, such as:
  - The exponential growth of data from the internet, social media, mobile devices, sensors, and other digital sources.
  - The development of new technologies and tools, such as cloud computing, distributed systems, parallel processing, machine learning, and artificial intelligence, that enabled the storage, processing, and analysis of Big Data at scale and speed.
  - The recognition of the value and potential of Big Data for various domains and applications, such as business, science, health, education, government, and society.
- Some of the milestones and achievements of Big Data innovation are:
  - In 2003, Google published a paper on MapReduce, a programming model and framework for processing large-scale data sets using a cluster of computers.
  - In 2004, Google published a paper on Bigtable, a distributed storage system for managing structured data at petabyte scale.
  - In 2006, Amazon launched its Elastic Compute Cloud (EC2) and Simple Storage Service (S3), which provided cloud computing and storage services for Big Data applications.
  - In 2008, Yahoo! released Hadoop, an open-source software platform that implemented the MapReduce and distributed file system concepts for Big Data processing.
  - In 2009, IBM launched the Watson project, a cognitive computing system that used natural language processing and machine learning to answer questions from large and diverse data sources.
  - In 2010, Facebook launched the Open Compute Project, an initiative to share the designs and best practices of its data center infrastructure for Big Data processing.
  - In 2012, the Obama administration announced the Big Data Research and Development Initiative, a multi-agency effort to invest in Big Data research and innovation for national priorities.
  - In 2014, Alibaba processed 278 million orders and $9.3 billion in sales on its Singles' Day online shopping event, using its Big Data platform and analytics.
  - In 2016, AlphaGo, a computer program developed by Google's DeepMind, defeated Lee Sedol, a world champion of Go, a complex board game, using deep neural networks and reinforcement learning trained on Big Data.
  - In 2019, the Event Horizon Telescope project captured the first image of a black hole, using a global network of telescopes and Big Data processing techniques.
