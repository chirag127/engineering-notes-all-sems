#### Introduction to Infosphere

- Infosphere is a neologism composed of information and sphere. The word refers to an environment, like a biosphere, that is populated by informational entities called inforgs.
- Infosphere can also be defined as the whole system of services and documents, encoded in any semiotic and physical media, whose contents include any sort of data, information and knowledge.
- Infosphere is not limited to purely online environments, but can also include physical, biological, social and cultural aspects of information.
- Infosphere is a metaphysical realm of information, data, knowledge and communication, that can be accessed and manipulated by inforgs.
- Infosphere is a key concept in the philosophy of information, which studies the nature, value and role of information in various domains.
- Infosphere is also a term used by IBM to refer to its suite of products and services for data integration, data quality, data governance and master data management  .
