#### IBM Big Data strategy

IBM Big Data strategy is the company's approach to offer solutions to store, manage, and analyze the huge amounts of data generated daily and equip large and small companies to make informed business decisions. IBM Big Data strategy is based on the following principles:

- Leveraging open source software such as Apache Hadoop, Apache Spark and the entire Hadoop ecosystem as cost-effective, flexible data processing and storage tools designed to handle the volume, velocity and variety of data being generated today.
- Providing hybrid cloud solutions that enable data integration, governance, security and analytics across on-premises and cloud data sources, as well as across different Hadoop distributions and vendors.
- Offering AI and machine learning capabilities that help build and train models, and prepare and analyze big data, all in a flexible hybrid cloud environment.
- Partnering with other industry leaders such as Cloudera to drive advanced analytics with an enterprise-grade, secure, governed, open source-based data lake.
- Aligning data strategy with business strategy and objectives, and helping customers understand and solve their specific data challenges and use cases.

Some of the key benefits of IBM Big Data strategy are:

- Accessing a large volume and variety of data sources and gaining new insights and actions.
- Starting small and scaling to handle data from historical records and in real-time.
- Saving costs in storing and analyzing large amounts of data with flexible data processing and storage tools.
- Discovering patterns and insights that help improve business efficiency and innovation.
- Gauging customer needs and potential risks and creating new products and services.
