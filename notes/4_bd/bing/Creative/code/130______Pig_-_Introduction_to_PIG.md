#### Pig - Introduction to PIG

- A pig is a domestic or wild animal that belongs to the family Suidae, along with other species such as the warthog and the babirusa .
- Pigs are omnivorous mammals that have a stout body, short legs, thick skin, and a long snout .
- Pigs have four toes on each foot, but only use the middle two for walking .
- Pigs are highly intelligent, social, and adaptable animals that can learn from each other and communicate through vocalizations and body language.
- Pigs are raised by humans for various purposes, such as food (pork, bacon, ham, etc.), leather, glue, fertilizer, and medicine .
- Pigs can also be kept as pets, especially the smaller breeds such as the pot-bellied pig and the miniature pig.
- Pigs have a long history of association with humans, dating back to at least 7000 BC in China and 6000 BC in Europe.
- Pigs have been featured in many cultures and religions, such as the Chinese zodiac, the Greek myth of the Caledonian boar, the Hindu deity Varaha, and the Jewish and Islamic dietary laws.
