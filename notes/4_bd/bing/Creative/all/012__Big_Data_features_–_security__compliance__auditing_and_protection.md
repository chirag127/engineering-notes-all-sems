### Big Data features – security, compliance, auditing and protection

- Big data security refers to the measures and tools used to protect big data from unauthorized access, modification, or deletion. Big data security is important to ensure the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of big data.
- Big data compliance refers to the adherence to the laws, regulations, standards, and policies that govern the collection, processing, storage, and use of big data. Big data compliance is important to ensure the legality, ethics, and accountability of big data.
- Big data auditing refers to the process of examining and verifying the big data systems, processes, and activities to ensure that they are operating correctly, efficiently, and securely. Big data auditing is important to ensure the quality, performance, and reliability of big data.
- Big data protection refers to the strategies and techniques used to safeguard big data from accidental or intentional loss, damage, or corruption. Big data protection is important to ensure the availability, recoverability, and resilience of big data.

Some of the common challenges and solutions for big data features are:

- Challenge: Big data is often distributed across multiple locations, platforms, and formats, making it difficult to apply consistent and comprehensive security policies and controls.
  - Solution: Big data security frameworks and standards, such as Apache Sentry, Apache Ranger, and Kerberos, can help to define and enforce security policies and controls across big data environments. These frameworks and standards can also support encryption, authentication, authorization, and auditing of big data.
- Challenge: Big data is subject to various and evolving compliance requirements, such as the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA), and the Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard (PCI DSS), depending on the type, source, and use of the data.
  - Solution: Big data compliance tools and services, such as AWS Compliance Center, Google Cloud Compliance, and Microsoft Azure Compliance, can help to assess and manage the compliance status and risks of big data. These tools and services can also provide guidance, best practices, and resources for achieving and maintaining compliance.
- Challenge: Big data is complex and dynamic, making it difficult to monitor and audit its systems, processes, and activities. Big data also generates large volumes of audit logs and records, which can be challenging to store, analyze, and report.
  - Solution: Big data auditing tools and platforms, such as Apache Eagle, Apache Flume, and Splunk, can help to collect, process, and visualize big data audit logs and records. These tools and platforms can also provide alerts, dashboards, and reports for big data auditing.
- Challenge: Big data is valuable and vulnerable, making it a target for cyberattacks, natural disasters, human errors, and hardware failures. Big data also requires high availability and scalability, which can increase the risk of data loss, damage, or corruption.
  - Solution: Big data protection techniques and solutions, such as backup, replication, disaster recovery, and fault tolerance, can help to prevent and recover from big data loss, damage, or corruption. These techniques and solutions can also ensure the availability and scalability of big data.
