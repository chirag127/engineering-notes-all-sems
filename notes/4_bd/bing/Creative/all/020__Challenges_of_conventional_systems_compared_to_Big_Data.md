### Challenges of conventional systems compared to Big Data

- Conventional systems are designed to handle structured and homogeneous data, such as relational databases, spreadsheets, and text files. Big Data, on the other hand, is characterized by the 5 Vs: volume, velocity, variety, veracity, and value. This means that Big Data is large, fast, diverse, uncertain, and potentially useful. Conventional systems are not able to cope with these challenges without significant modifications or extensions.
- Some of the specific challenges of conventional systems compared to Big Data are:

  - Scalability: Conventional systems have limited storage and processing capacity, which makes them unable to handle the massive volume and velocity of Big Data. Big Data requires distributed and parallel systems that can scale horizontally by adding more nodes or vertically by increasing the resources of each node. Big Data also requires techniques such as compression, sampling, and aggregation to reduce the data size and complexity.
  - Heterogeneity: Conventional systems assume that the data is structured and follows a predefined schema, which makes them unable to handle the variety and veracity of Big Data. Big Data can be unstructured, semi-structured, or structured, and can come from different sources and formats, such as text, images, audio, video, social media, sensors, etc. Big Data also can be noisy, incomplete, inconsistent, or inaccurate, which affects its quality and reliability. Big Data requires techniques such as integration, transformation, cleaning, and enrichment to handle the data heterogeneity and uncertainty.
  - Analysis: Conventional systems rely on predefined queries and reports to analyze the data, which makes them unable to handle the value and complexity of Big Data. Big Data can provide new insights and opportunities for decision making, innovation, and discovery, but it also poses new challenges for data analysis, such as high dimensionality, sparsity, nonlinearity, and dynamics. Big Data requires techniques such as machine learning, data mining, statistics, visualization, and natural language processing to extract meaningful and actionable information from the data.
