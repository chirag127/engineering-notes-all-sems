#### IBM Big Data strategy

- IBM Big Data strategy is the company's approach to offer solutions to store, manage, and analyze the huge amounts of data generated daily and equip large and small companies to make informed business decisions   .
- IBM Big Data strategy is part of its Smarter Planet initiative, which seeks to highlight how government and business leaders can capture the potential of smarter systems to achieve economic and sustainable growth and societal progress.
- IBM Big Data strategy consists of six steps:
  - Understand your business objectives: Connect your data strategy with the business strategy and get buy-in from stakeholders and senior leadership.
  - Assess your current state: Unpack pain points to reveal blockers and gaps in your data capabilities and infrastructure.
  - Map out data strategy framework: Define your data's target state, vision, and roadmap, and prioritize use cases that align with business goals and deliver value.
  - Design your data architecture: Choose the right data platform and tools that can handle the volume, velocity, and variety of your data sources and use cases.
  - Implement your data strategy: Execute your data strategy with agile and iterative methods, and measure and communicate your progress and outcomes.
  - Govern and evolve your data strategy: Establish data governance and quality standards, policies, and processes, and continuously monitor and improve your data strategy based on feedback and changing needs.
- IBM Big Data strategy leverages open source software such as Apache Hadoop, Apache Spark, and the entire Hadoop ecosystem as cost-effective, flexible data processing and storage tools designed to handle the volume of data being generated today.
- IBM Big Data strategy also incorporates artificial intelligence (AI) and machine learning (ML) capabilities to enable advanced analytics and predictive modelling that can help organizations gain new insights and take action.
- IBM Big Data strategy offers various solutions and products to support different aspects of data management and analytics, such as IBM and Cloudera big data solutions, IBM Db2 Big SQL, IBM Big Replicate for Hadoop, and IBM Watson Studio.
- IBM Big Data strategy helps organizations to access a large volume of data and analyze a large variety of sources of data to gain new insights and take action, start small and scale to handle data from historical records and in real-time, save costs in storing and analyzing large amounts of data, discover patterns and insights that help them do business more efficiently, gauge customer needs and potential risks, and create new products and services.
