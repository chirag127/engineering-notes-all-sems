### Drivers for Big Data

- Drivers for Big Data are the factors that motivate the need for collecting, storing, processing, and analyzing large and complex datasets.
- Some of the drivers for Big Data are:

  - **Data explosion**: The amount of data generated by various sources such as social media, sensors, mobile devices, e-commerce, etc. is growing exponentially. According to IDC, the global data volume will reach 175 zettabytes by 2025, up from 33 zettabytes in 2018. This data explosion poses challenges for traditional data management systems that cannot handle such volumes, variety, velocity, and veracity of data.
  - **Business intelligence**: The ability to gain insights from data and make informed decisions is crucial for businesses to gain competitive advantage, improve customer satisfaction, optimize operations, and reduce costs. Big Data analytics can help businesses to discover hidden patterns, trends, correlations, and anomalies in data, and derive actionable insights that can improve business performance and outcomes.
  - **Scientific discovery**: The advancement of science and technology depends on the ability to collect, analyze, and interpret large and complex datasets from various domains such as astronomy, biology, physics, chemistry, etc. Big Data can enable scientists to conduct experiments, simulations, and observations at unprecedented scales and resolutions, and discover new phenomena, laws, and principles that can advance scientific knowledge and innovation.
  - **Social good**: The application of Big Data can also benefit society and humanity in various ways, such as improving public health, education, security, environment, etc. Big Data can help to monitor, predict, and prevent diseases, epidemics, and pandemics, enhance learning outcomes and access to education, detect and prevent crimes and terrorism, reduce carbon emissions and waste, and promote social justice and equality.
