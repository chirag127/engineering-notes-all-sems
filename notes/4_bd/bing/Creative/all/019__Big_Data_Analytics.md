### Big Data Analytics

- Big data analytics is the use of advanced analytic techniques against very large, diverse big data sets that include structured, semi-structured and unstructured data, from different sources, and in different sizes from terabytes to zettabytes.
- Big data analytics can help businesses discover market trends, insights, and patterns that can improve their decision-making, processes, systems, and profitability.
- Big data analytics can also help businesses understand customer needs, identify potential risks, create new products and services, and optimize their operations.
- Big data analytics uses the four data analysis methods: descriptive, diagnostic, predictive, and prescriptive.
- Big data analytics relies on technologies such as business intelligence (BI) tools, data warehouses, data lakes, cloud computing, machine learning, and artificial intelligence to collect, process, and analyze the data .
- Big data analytics can be applied to various industries such as health care, education, finance, retail, manufacturing, and more .
- Big data is characterized by the five V's: volume, velocity, variety, variability, and value.
  - Volume: the amount of data generated and stored
  - Velocity: the speed at which data is created and processed
  - Variety: the diversity of data types and sources
  - Variability: the inconsistency and unpredictability of data quality and meaning
  - Value: the usefulness and relevance of data for business outcomes
- A mnemonic to remember the five V's of big data is: **V**ery **V**ast **V**ariety of **V**olatile **V**aluable data.
