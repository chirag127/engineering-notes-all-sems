### Big Data applications

Big data applications are software systems that use advanced analytic techniques to process and analyze large, diverse and complex data sets from various sources and in different formats. Big data applications can help companies to make better business decisions, optimize operations, enhance customer experience, create new products and services, and improve security and compliance.

Some of the common big data applications are:

- **Marketing**: Big data applications can help marketers to forecast customer behavior, preferences, and product strategies by analyzing data from various channels, such as social media, web, mobile, and video. Big data applications can also help marketers to measure the effectiveness of their campaigns, optimize their budget allocation, and personalize their messages to different segments of customers. Some examples of big data applications in marketing are Centerfield, 3Q/DEPT, and DISQO.

- **Transportation**: Big data applications can help transportation providers and users to improve navigation, traffic management, and safety by analyzing data from GPS, sensors, devices, and weather stations. Big data applications can also help transportation companies to optimize their routes, schedules, and fuel consumption, as well as to monitor their vehicles and drivers. Some examples of big data applications in transportation are Uber, Waze, and INRIX.

- **Government and public administration**: Big data applications can help government agencies and public organizations to track and manage data related to tax, defense, public health, education, and social welfare. Big data applications can also help government entities to enhance their transparency, accountability, and citizen engagement by providing open data platforms, dashboards, and analytics tools. Some examples of big data applications in government and public administration are IRS, CDC, and Data.gov.

- **Business**: Big data applications can help businesses to streamline their management operations, optimize their costs, and increase their productivity and profitability by analyzing data from their internal and external sources, such as ERP, CRM, SCM, and market data. Big data applications can also help businesses to identify new opportunities, improve customer satisfaction, and gain competitive advantage. Some examples of big data applications in business are IBM, SAP, and Oracle.

- **Healthcare**: Big data applications can help healthcare providers and patients to access and share medical records, accelerate diagnosis and treatment, and enhance the quality and efficiency of care by analyzing data from electronic health records, medical devices, wearables, and genomics. Big data applications can also help healthcare researchers and innovators to discover new drugs, therapies, and devices, as well as to monitor and prevent diseases and outbreaks. Some examples of big data applications in healthcare are Cerner, Philips, and Flatiron Health.

- **Cybersecurity**: Big data applications can help cybersecurity professionals and organizations to detect and prevent system vulnerabilities, cyberattacks, and data breaches by analyzing data from network logs, firewalls, antivirus, and malware. Big data applications can also help cybersecurity experts and agencies to investigate and respond to cyber incidents, as well as to enhance their security policies and standards. Some examples of big data applications in cybersecurity are IBM, McAfee, and FireEye.
