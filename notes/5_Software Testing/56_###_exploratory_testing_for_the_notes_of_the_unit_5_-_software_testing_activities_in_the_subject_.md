### Exploratory Testing for the notes of the Unit 5 - Software Testing Activities in the subject of Software Testing
Exploratory testing is a type of software testing that is performed without a pre-determined test plan or script. The goal of exploratory testing is to discover and uncover new and unknown issues in the software through hands-on testing. This type of testing is performed by testers with a deep understanding of the software and its intended use, and is often used to validate assumptions and identify areas for improvement. Exploratory testing is highly interactive and allows the tester to explore the software in an ad-hoc manner, making it an effective method for finding unexpected issues. It is best suited for early stages of software development, where the requirements and design are still evolving, and for complex software systems where a more structured approach is not feasible.
