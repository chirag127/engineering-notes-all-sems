### Prioritization guidelines for the notes of the Unit 4 - Regression Testing in the subject of Software Testing
1. Understand the purpose and objectives of regression testing.
2. Identify the areas of the software that have changed since the last test.
3. Evaluate the impact of the changes on the existing functionality.
4. Prioritize tests based on criticality of the affected areas and potential risks.
5. Use automated testing tools to improve efficiency and consistency.
6. Reuse existing test cases where possible.
7. Continuously monitor and update the regression test suite.
