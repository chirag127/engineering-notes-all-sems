### SDLC for the notes of the Unit 1 - Review of Software Engineering in the subject of Software Testing
The Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC) is a systematic approach to software development that outlines the stages involved in the creation of a software product from conception to deployment. The stages typically include:

1. Requirements gathering and analysis: Understanding the needs and requirements of the stakeholders.

2. Design: Creating a blueprint for the software, including its architecture and user interface.

3. Implementation: Writing the code and developing the software.

4. Testing: Verifying that the software meets the requirements and is free of defects.

5. Deployment: Installing and delivering the software to the end-users.

6. Maintenance: Providing ongoing support and updating the software as needed.

Each stage of the SDLC is critical to the success of the software development process, and it is important to follow a structured approach to ensure that the software is developed efficiently and effectively.
