### Error for the notes of the Unit 1 - Review of Software Engineering in the subject of Software Testing
Error is a deviation from expected or desired outcome in software development. It can be caused by mistakes in coding, design, requirements, or other factors. Error handling is an important aspect of software testing, as it helps identify and correct these deviations to improve the quality of the software. In software testing, errors can be detected through various techniques such as unit testing, integration testing, and system testing. Effective error handling can improve the reliability and usability of software, reducing the risk of potential failures and improving user experience.
