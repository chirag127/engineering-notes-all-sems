### Inspection for the notes of the Unit 1 - Review of Software Engineering in the subject of Software Testing
Inspection is a formal review process where code, documents, or other work products are examined by peers to find defects and improve quality. It involves several participants including the author, inspector, and a moderator. The goal is to identify and correct errors, improve clarity, and ensure the work product meets the standards and requirements. Inspections can be applied to various stages in the software development life cycle, including design, code, and documentation. They are a cost-effective way to improve software quality and reduce rework. However, they require discipline and a well-defined process to be effective.
