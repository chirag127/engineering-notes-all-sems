### Risk Analysis for the notes of the Unit 4 - Regression Testing in the subject of Software Testing
Risk Analysis:

- Identify potential risks associated with software changes (e.g. regression bugs)
- Assess the impact of these risks on the software's functionality and performance
- Prioritize the tests to be performed based on the level of risk
- Choose appropriate regression testing techniques (e.g. selective, full, or regression test automation)
- Continuously monitor and review the results of regression testing to identify any new risks and adjust the testing process accordingly.
