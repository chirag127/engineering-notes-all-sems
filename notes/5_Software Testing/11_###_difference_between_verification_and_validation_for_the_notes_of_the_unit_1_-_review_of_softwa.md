### Difference Between Verification and Validation for the notes of the Unit 1 - Review of Software Engineering in the subject of Software Testing
Verification and Validation are two key concepts in software testing.
Verification is the process of evaluating a system or its component(s) with the intent to determine whether the developed product meets specified requirements. It is a process of checking whether the product is designed and developed as per the customer's requirements.
Validation is the process of evaluating a system or its component(s) during or at the end of the development process to determine whether it satisfies specified requirements. It is a process of evaluating the software during or at the end of the development process to determine whether it is fit for its intended use.
In summary, Verification is focused on the design and development of the software, while Validation focuses on the software's functionality and performance.
