### Validation for the notes of the Unit 1 - Review of Software Engineering in the subject of Software Testing
Validation is the process of evaluating a system or its component(s) with the intent to determine whether it satisfies the specified requirements. In software testing, validation is used to determine whether the software product meets the customer's needs and expectations. This is achieved by comparing the actual results of the software product with the expected results. Validation can be performed at various stages of the software development life cycle, including during the requirements gathering, design, coding, and testing phases. It is important to validate software to ensure that it is functional and meets the desired quality standards.
