## Unit 1 - Review of Software Engineering
Software Engineering is the study and application of engineering to the design, development, and maintenance of software. Unit 1 covers the basics of software engineering and its various concepts, including:

1. Definition and history of software engineering
2. Software development life cycle models
3. Software requirements analysis and specification
4. Software design and architecture
5. Software testing and quality assurance
6. Software maintenance and evolution
7. Software project management

The unit also covers the software development process and the various phases involved, including planning, analysis, design, implementation, testing, and maintenance. Additionally, the unit covers the various software development methodologies and models, such as Waterfall, Agile, and DevOps. The unit concludes with a discussion of software project management, including project planning, risk management, and project estimation.
