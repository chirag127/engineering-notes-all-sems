### Configuration Audits for the notes of the Unit 1 - Review of Software Engineering in the subject of Software Testing
Configuration Audit:
- A process to review and verify the configuration management practices and artifacts of a software project
- Objective is to ensure that the project follows established standards and procedures
- Evaluates the completeness, consistency, and accuracy of the configuration management information
- Includes review of version control systems, change management, and release management processes
- Helps identify areas for improvement and reduce risk of configuration errors during development and maintenance.
