### Verification for the notes of the Unit 1 - Review of Software Engineering in the subject of Software Testing
Verification is the process of evaluating a system or its component(s) with the intent to find whether it satisfies the specified requirements or not. In the context of Unit 1 - Review of Software Engineering in the subject of Software Testing, verification is used to ensure that the software meets the specified requirements before it is released to the customer. The verification process involves a series of activities such as inspections, walkthroughs, and reviews to identify and correct defects in the software. The goal of verification is to find and correct errors early in the development process, which helps to reduce the cost of fixing defects later in the development cycle. It is an important step in the software development life cycle as it helps to improve the quality of the software and reduce the risk of failure.
