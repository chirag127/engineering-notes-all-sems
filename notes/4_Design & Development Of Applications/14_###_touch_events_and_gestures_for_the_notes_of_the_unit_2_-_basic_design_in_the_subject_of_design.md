### touch events and gestures for the notes of the Unit 2 - BASIC DESIGN in the subject of Design & Development Of Applications
Touch events and gestures are interactions between a user and a touch-enabled device, such as a smartphone or tablet. They allow users to interact with applications in a more natural and intuitive way.

1. Touch events:
- Touch start: triggered when a finger touches the screen.
- Touch move: triggered when a finger moves across the screen.
- Touch end: triggered when a finger is lifted from the screen.
- Touch cancel: triggered when a touch event is interrupted, such as by a phone call.

2. Gestures:
- Tap: a quick touch and release of the screen.
- Double tap: two quick taps in succession.
- Swipe: a quick swipe across the screen in a specific direction.
- Pinch: a two-finger gesture used to zoom in or out.
- Long press: holding a finger down on the screen for a set amount of time.
