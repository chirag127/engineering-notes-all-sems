### iOS features for the notes of the Unit 5 - TECHNOLOGY II –iOS in the subject of Design & Development Of Applications
iOS Notes for Unit 5 - TECHNOLOGY II - iOS:
1. User-friendly interface: iOS has a simple, intuitive interface that is easy to use.
2. Multi-touch gestures: iOS supports multi-touch gestures, such as pinch-to-zoom and swipe-to-delete.
3. Siri: Siri is a virtual assistant that can perform tasks, answer questions, and provide recommendations.
4. iMessage: iMessage is an instant messaging service that allows users to send text messages, photos, and videos.
5. Face ID/Touch ID: Face ID and Touch ID are biometric authentication methods that allow users to unlock their devices using facial recognition or fingerprint scanning.
6. AirDrop: AirDrop is a feature that allows users to quickly and easily transfer files between Apple devices.
7. App Store: The App Store is a platform that allows users to download and install applications.
8. iCloud: iCloud is a cloud storage service that allows users to store and access their data from multiple devices.
9. Continuity: Continuity is a feature that allows users to seamlessly switch between Apple devices and continue their work.
10. Family Sharing: Family Sharing allows multiple family members to share purchases, subscriptions, and more.
