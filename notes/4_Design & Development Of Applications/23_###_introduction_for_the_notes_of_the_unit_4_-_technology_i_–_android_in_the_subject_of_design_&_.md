### Introduction for the notes of the Unit 4 - TECHNOLOGY I – ANDROID in the subject of Design & Development Of Applications
Introduction to Unit 4 - Technology I - Android:
- Android is a mobile operating system based on Linux
- Developed by Google
- Open source platform
- Used for developing apps for smartphones and tablets
- Java programming language used to write apps
- Popular due to its versatility and large app store (Google Play)
- Covers basic concepts such as activities, intents, and services.
