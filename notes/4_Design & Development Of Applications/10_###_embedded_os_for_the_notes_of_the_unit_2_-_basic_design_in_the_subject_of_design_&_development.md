### Embedded OS for the notes of the Unit 2 - BASIC DESIGN in the subject of Design & Development Of Applications
Embedded OS:
-A type of operating system designed to run on embedded systems, small, low-power devices with limited resources.
-Examples: FreeRTOS, EmbOS, uC/OS-III, and ChibiOS.
-Key features: Real-time performance, low memory footprint, and efficient power management.
-Uses: Internet of Things (IoT) devices, consumer electronics, industrial automation, and medical devices.
-Benefits: Improved reliability, security, and ease of development.
-Challenges: Limited resources and strict real-time requirements.
