### Publishing and delivery of mobile applications for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of Design & Development Of Applications
Publishing and delivery of mobile applications involves the following steps:
1. Finalizing app design and development
2. Testing the app for bugs and compatibility
3. Submitting the app to app stores (Google Play Store, Apple App Store)
4. Approval from the app store
5. Publishing the app for download
6. Updating the app with bug fixes and new features
7. Marketing the app to reach potential users

Delivery of the app involves making it available to the end-users through various channels such as app stores, direct download links, or enterprise distribution.

It is important to follow the guidelines set by app stores to ensure the app is approved and meets the quality standards. Regular updates and maintenance are crucial to maintain the app's performance and user satisfaction.

In conclusion, publishing and delivery of mobile applications require careful planning and execution to ensure a smooth and successful launch.
