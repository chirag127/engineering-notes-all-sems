### Packaging and deployment for the notes of the Unit 4 - TECHNOLOGY I – ANDROID in the subject of Design & Development Of Applications
Packaging and deployment for Android apps involves the following steps:

1. Compiling source code into APK (Android Package) file.
2. Signing the APK with a digital signature.
3. Aligning the APK using zipalign tool.
4. Installing the APK on an emulator or device.
5. Publishing the app to Google Play Store or other app stores. 

It is important to properly package and deploy an Android app to ensure its security and compatibility with different devices. The APK file contains all the necessary resources and code to run the app, and the digital signature provides authenticity and integrity of the app. Zipalign optimizes the APK to improve its performance on devices. Finally, publishing the app to app stores makes it easily accessible to users.
