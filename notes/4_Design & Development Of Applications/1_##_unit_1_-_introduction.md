## Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION
Unit 1 is an introduction to a subject or course. It provides an overview of what will be covered in the subsequent units and sets the context for the rest of the course. The introduction unit typically includes the following topics:
1. Purpose of the course
2. Overview of course content
3. Learning objectives
4. Prerequisites
5. Course structure
6. Assessment methods
7. Instructor information

The introduction unit is crucial as it helps students understand what they will learn and what is expected of them. It also helps students determine whether the course is a good fit for their needs and interests.
