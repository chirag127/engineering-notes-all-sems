## Unit 5 - TECHNOLOGY II –iOS
iOS is a mobile operating system developed by Apple Inc. for its iPhone, iPad, and iPod Touch devices. It is built on a Unix-based foundation and is known for its user-friendly interface, stability, and security. iOS is designed to provide a seamless user experience with its intuitive interface, built-in apps, and integration with other Apple devices. The latest version of iOS is iOS 14, which was released in 2020. It includes features such as a new home screen design, App Library, picture-in-picture mode, and improved privacy controls. Overall, iOS is a powerful and user-friendly operating system that is widely used by millions of people around the world.
