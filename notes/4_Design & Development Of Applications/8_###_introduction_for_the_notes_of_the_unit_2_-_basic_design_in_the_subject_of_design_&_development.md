### Introduction for the notes of the Unit 2 - BASIC DESIGN in the subject of Design & Development Of Applications
Unit 2 of Design & Development of Applications focuses on Basic Design. It covers the fundamental principles and techniques used in the design of applications, including user-centered design, prototyping, and testing. The unit introduces key concepts such as usability, accessibility, and user experience, and explores how these concepts can be applied in the design process. The aim of this unit is to provide students with a solid foundation in the basics of design and to equip them with the skills and knowledge necessary to create effective and user-friendly applications.
