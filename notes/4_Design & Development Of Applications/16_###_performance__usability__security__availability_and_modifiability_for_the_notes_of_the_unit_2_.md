### performance, usability, security, availability and modifiability for the notes of the Unit 2 - BASIC DESIGN in the subject of Design & Development Of Applications
Performance: 
- The notes for Unit 2 should be optimized for quick access and retrieval. 
- The layout should be clean and organized to improve readability. 

Usability: 
- The notes should be easy to navigate and understand, with clear headings and subheadings. 
- The language used should be simple and straightforward. 

Security: 
- The notes should be protected against unauthorized access and modification. 
- Encryption should be used to secure sensitive information. 

Availability: 
- The notes should be accessible from multiple devices and platforms. 
- The notes should be stored on a secure and reliable server. 

Modifiability: 
- The notes should be easily updated and modified as needed. 
- The design should allow for the addition of new information and the removal of outdated information.
