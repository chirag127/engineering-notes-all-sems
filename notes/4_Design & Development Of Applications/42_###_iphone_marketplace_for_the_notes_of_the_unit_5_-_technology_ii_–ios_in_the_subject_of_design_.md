### iPhone marketplace for the notes of the Unit 5 - TECHNOLOGY II –iOS in the subject of Design & Development Of Applications
The iPhone marketplace, also known as the App Store, is a digital distribution platform for iOS apps. It was launched in 2008 and is exclusively available on Apple devices, including the iPhone, iPad, and iPod Touch. The App Store offers a wide range of apps, including games, productivity tools, social media, and more. Developers can submit their apps for review and once approved, they can be downloaded by users from the App Store. The App Store uses a revenue-sharing model, where developers receive a percentage of the sales from their app. The App Store has become a major source of revenue for Apple and has been instrumental in the success of the iPhone. It has also created a thriving ecosystem for developers and has helped to drive innovation in mobile app development.
