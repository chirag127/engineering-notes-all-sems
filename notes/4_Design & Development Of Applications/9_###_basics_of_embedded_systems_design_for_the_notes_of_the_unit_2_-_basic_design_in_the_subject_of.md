### Basics of embedded systems design for the notes of the Unit 2 - BASIC DESIGN in the subject of Design & Development Of Applications
Embedded Systems Design:
- Definition: A computer system integrated into a device to perform specific functions.
- Key elements: Processor, memory, input/output interfaces, and software.
- Design process: Requirements gathering, hardware and software design, implementation, testing, and maintenance.
- Real-time operating systems: Used to manage time-critical tasks and provide predictable behavior.
- Interrupts: Mechanism to temporarily stop ongoing tasks and handle high priority events.
- Memory management: Allocating and managing memory to ensure efficient use of resources.
- Debugging techniques: Debugging hardware and software, using in-circuit emulators and trace tools.
- Power management: Minimizing power consumption to extend battery life.
- Security: Protecting the device and its data from unauthorized access.
