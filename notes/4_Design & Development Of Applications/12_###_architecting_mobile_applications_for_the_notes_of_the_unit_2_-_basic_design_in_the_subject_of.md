### Architecting mobile applications for the notes of the Unit 2 - BASIC DESIGN in the subject of Design & Development Of Applications
1. Design for Mobile: Understanding the unique constraints and opportunities of mobile devices, such as screen size and input methods.

2. User-Centered Design: Focusing on the needs and goals of the end-user to create a compelling and usable experience.

3. Platform Guidelines: Adhering to the design guidelines of each platform (iOS, Android, etc.) to ensure consistency and a native look and feel.

4. Navigation & Information Architecture: Organizing content and functionality in a way that is intuitive and easy to use.

5. Interaction Design: Creating interactions that are natural and intuitive, such as gestures and touch-based inputs.

6. Responsive Design: Ensuring that the app adapts to different screen sizes and orientations, providing a consistent experience across devices.

7. Accessibility: Making the app usable by people with disabilities, such as those who are blind or have low vision.

8. Performance: Optimizing the app to ensure fast and responsive performance, even on low-end devices.

9. Security: Protecting sensitive data and ensuring the app is secure from malicious attacks.

10. Scalability: Designing the app to handle increasing amounts of data and user traffic as it grows.
