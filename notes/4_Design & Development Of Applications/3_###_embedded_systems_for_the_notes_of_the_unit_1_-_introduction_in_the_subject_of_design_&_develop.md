### Embedded systems for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of Design & Development Of Applications
Embedded systems are computer systems that are integrated into other devices or products to perform specific functions. They are designed to operate in real-time and are often used in products that require control, data processing, and communication capabilities. Key features of embedded systems include:

- Real-time operation: The system must respond to events in real-time and complete tasks within specified time constraints.

- Limited resources: Embedded systems typically have limited processing power, memory, and storage compared to traditional computers.

- Specialized hardware: Embedded systems often use specialized hardware components such as microcontrollers, sensors, and actuators to perform specific tasks.

- Integration with other systems: Embedded systems often communicate with other systems and devices to exchange data and control operations.

Applications of embedded systems include:

- Consumer electronics: Examples include smartphones, digital cameras, and home appliances.

- Automotive systems: Examples include engine control systems, navigation systems, and infotainment systems.

- Industrial control systems: Examples include process control systems, robotics, and machine control systems.

- Medical devices: Examples include pacemakers, insulin pumps, and patient monitoring systems.

Embedded systems play a critical role in many industries and are essential for the operation of many products and devices.
