### Market and business drivers for mobile applications for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of Design & Development Of Applications
1. Increased Mobile Usage: The rise in mobile device usage has led to an increase in demand for mobile applications. 

2. Improved Technology: Advances in technology have made it easier to develop and distribute mobile applications. 

3. Growing E-Commerce: The growth of e-commerce has led to an increase in the need for mobile applications that support online shopping. 

4. Increased Adoption of Smartphones: The widespread adoption of smartphones has created a large market for mobile applications. 

5. Changing Consumer Behavior: Consumers are increasingly relying on their mobile devices for everyday tasks, driving demand for mobile applications that make these tasks easier. 

6. Growing Demand for On-the-Go Services: The need for information and services while on-the-go has driven the growth of mobile applications. 

7. Increased Competition: The growing number of businesses entering the mobile application market has increased competition and driven innovation. 

8. Growing Business Needs: Companies are increasingly relying on mobile applications to improve their operations, increase productivity, and reach new customers.
