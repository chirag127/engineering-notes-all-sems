The IoT Based Heart Monitoring System typically includes an ECG sensor that is integrated into a wearable device, such as a smartwatch or chest strap. The ECG sensor is used to detect electrical signals produced by the heart, and to transmit this data to a central monitoring system.

The central monitoring system is used to process and analyze the data collected by the ECG sensor, and to provide real-time insights into heart activity, such as heart rate, rhythm, and electrical activity. The system may also be integrated with other health monitoring devices, such as activity trackers and blood pressure monitors, to provide a more comprehensive view of overall health.
