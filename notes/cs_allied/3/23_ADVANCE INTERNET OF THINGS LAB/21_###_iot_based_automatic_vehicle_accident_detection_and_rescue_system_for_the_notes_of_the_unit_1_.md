3. Emergency response system: An emergency response system, such as an ambulance or fire truck, is dispatched to the location of the accident in response to the data transmitted from the sensors.

4. User interface: A user interface, such as a smartphone or tablet, is used to access the data transmitted from the sensors and to receive notifications of accidents and emergency responses.
