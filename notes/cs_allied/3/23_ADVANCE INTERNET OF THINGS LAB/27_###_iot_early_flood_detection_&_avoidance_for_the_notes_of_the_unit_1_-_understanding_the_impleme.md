IoT Early Flood Detection & Avoidance is a system that is designed to detect the early signs of a flood and to provide real-time alerts to help prevent damage and loss of life. The system is part of the Internet of Things (IoT) and uses a network of sensors, wireless communication technologies, and data analytics to monitor and predict the risk of a flood.

The IoT Early Flood Detection & Avoidance system typically includes sensors for monitoring water levels, rainfall, and other environmental conditions that are related to the risk of a flood. The data collected by the sensors is transmitted to a central monitoring system, where it is analyzed using data analytics techniques to predict the risk of a flood.

In the event of a potential flood, the central monitoring system generates real-time alerts that are sent to relevant authorities and stakeholders, such as emergency responders, local residents, and businesses. The alerts may include information about the location and severity of the potential flood, as well as instructions for avoiding or mitigating the flood.
