
### Mountain Climber Health & GPS Tracker

* Mountain climbers face a range of potential health risks, including hypothermia, exhaustion, dehydration and altitude sickness.
* A GPS tracker can help climbers identify their location, track their progress and provide an alert if they enter a hazardous area.
* It can also be used to track the climber's pulse rate, oxygen levels and other vital signs, and to provide an early warning if the climber is in danger.
* IOT technology can be used to monitor the climber's health and provide real-time feedback to the climber and their support team.
* By using IOT technology, climbers can be provided with the information they need to make informed decisions about their safety and the safety of their team.
