
### IOT Contactless Covid Testing Booth Automation

1. The implementation of Internet of Things (IoT) in contactless Covid testing booths is a great way to increase safety and efficiency in the testing process. 
2. IoT technology can be used to automate the entire testing process, from check-in to results. 
3. IoT devices can be used to track the movement of people in and out of the booth, as well as to monitor the temperature of people entering the booth. 
4. IoT devices can also be used to collect data from the Covid test in order to provide accurate and timely results. 
5. The use of IoT in contactless Covid testing booths can help reduce the risk of infection and provide more accurate results.
