
### IOT based Smart Energy Meter Monitoring with Theft Detection

1. Smart energy meters are digital devices that measure and record energy consumption in real time.

2. The main purpose of smart energy meters is to accurately measure, monitor, and analyze energy usage.

3. Smart energy meters can also detect energy theft, which is when someone illegally obtains electricity without paying for it.

4. Smart energy meters use a variety of technologies, such as wireless communication, sensors, and microcontrollers, to measure and record energy consumption.

5. Smart energy meters can provide detailed information about energy consumption, such as the time of day when energy is being used and the amount of energy being used.

6. Smart energy meters can help identify energy saving opportunities and can be used to detect energy theft.

7. Smart energy meters can also be used to identify potential problems with the energy system, such as faulty wiring or overloaded circuits.

8. In order to ensure accuracy, smart energy meters must be calibrated regularly.
