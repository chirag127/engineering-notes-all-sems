
### IOT Smart Parking Using RFID
This topic focuses on the problem analysis and designing a solution for the subject of ADVANCE INTERNET OF THINGS LAB (Unit 3).

1. Problem Analysis:
    * Identify the problem that needs to be solved.
    * Analyze the problem in terms of its scope, complexity and impact.
    * Identify the stakeholders and their interests.
    * Analyze the requirements that must be met to solve the problem.

2. Designing a Solution:
    * Generate a list of possible solutions.
    * Evaluate the solutions in terms of their feasibility and cost.
    * Select the most suitable solution.
    * Design the solution in detail.
    * Develop a plan for implementing the solution.
