
### Contactless IOT Doorbell

* Contactless IOT Doorbells are a type of doorbell that utilizes the Internet of Things (IoT) to provide a secure and convenient way to identify visitors at the door. 
* By using a combination of facial recognition, voice recognition, and other biometric identification techniques, contactless IOT doorbells are able to identify visitors and allow entry into a home or business. 
* The technology behind contactless IOT doorbells is similar to that of other IoT devices, such as smart thermostats and home security systems. 
* In order to make use of contactless IOT doorbells, users must first install the necessary hardware and software. This includes a camera, microphone, and other necessary components. 
* Once the system is installed, users can configure the settings to their preferences. This includes setting up facial recognition, voice recognition, and other biometric identification techniques. 
* Contactless IOT doorbells are becoming increasingly popular as they provide a secure and convenient way to identify visitors at the door. 
* They are also becoming more affordable, making them a great option for those who want to increase the security of their home or business.
