
## Unit 1 - Understanding the Implementation of IOT

1. **Internet of Things (IoT)** is a network of physical objects, such as sensors, devices, and appliances, that are connected to the internet and can collect and exchange data. 
2. IoT enables objects to be sensed and controlled remotely across existing network infrastructure, creating opportunities for more direct integration of the physical world into computer-based systems, and resulting in improved efficiency, accuracy and economic benefit. 
3. IoT devices can be programmed to act on data they collect, or to send data to other devices or cloud-based services. 
4. IoT devices can be connected to the internet through various communication protocols, such as Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, RFID, NFC, and cellular networks. 
5. IoT applications can be used to automate processes, monitor and control devices, and collect data for analytics. 
6. Security is a major concern when it comes to IoT, as IoT devices can be vulnerable to cyber-attacks. 
7. To secure IoT devices, organizations should implement strong authentication, encryption, and access control measures.
