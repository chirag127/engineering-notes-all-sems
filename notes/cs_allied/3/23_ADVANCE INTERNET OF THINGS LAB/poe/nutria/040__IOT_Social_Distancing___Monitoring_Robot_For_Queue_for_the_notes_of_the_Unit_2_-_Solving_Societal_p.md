
### IOT Social Distancing & Monitoring Robot For Queue

The Internet of Things (IoT) can be used to solve societal problems, such as social distancing and monitoring queues. By leveraging the power of connected devices, IoT can be used to create a smart system that can detect people in a queue, monitor their distance from one another, and alert them if they are too close.

Here are some of the benefits of using an IoT-based social distancing and monitoring robot for queues:

1. Automated Monitoring: The robot can detect people in a queue and monitor their distance from one another without any manual intervention.

2. Accurate and Real-time Data: The robot can provide accurate and real-time data about the distance between people in the queue, which can be used to ensure social distancing.

3. Improved Efficiency: The robot can help to reduce the time taken to monitor queues, thus improving the efficiency of the system.
