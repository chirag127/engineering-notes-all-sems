
### IOT Based Heart Monitoring System Using ECG 

1. The Internet of Things (IoT) is an interconnected network of physical objects, such as sensors, devices, and machines, which are connected to the internet and are able to collect, transfer, and exchange data. 
2. IoT-based heart monitoring systems use electrocardiography (ECG) to measure the electrical activity of the heart.
3. The ECG signal is recorded by electrodes placed on the patient’s chest and is monitored by a computer. 
4. The data is then analyzed to detect any abnormalities in the heart rhythm.
5. The data can be used to identify heart diseases, such as arrhythmias, and to diagnose and monitor the condition of the patient. 
6. IoT-based heart monitoring systems can be used to monitor the patient’s heart rate, blood pressure, and other vital signs in real-time.
7. The data can be used to provide medical professionals with information about the patient’s health and to provide timely interventions. 
8. IoT-based heart monitoring systems can also be used to monitor the patient’s activity levels and to provide personalized health advice. 
9. The systems can be used to alert medical professionals in case of an emergency and to provide remote monitoring of the patient’s condition.
