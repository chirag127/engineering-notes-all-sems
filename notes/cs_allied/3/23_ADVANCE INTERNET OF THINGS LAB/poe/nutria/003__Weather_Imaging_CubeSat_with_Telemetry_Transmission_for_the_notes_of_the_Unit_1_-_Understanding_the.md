
### Weather Imaging CubeSat with Telemetry Transmission

1. CubeSats are miniature satellites that are used for a variety of applications, including weather imaging and telemetry transmission.
2. CubeSats are typically designed to be small and lightweight, allowing them to be launched into space relatively cheaply.
3. CubeSats are equipped with various sensors to collect data related to weather, such as temperature, humidity, and pressure.
4. The data collected by the CubeSat is then transmitted to a ground station via telemetry, allowing for real-time monitoring of the weather conditions.
5. CubeSats can also be used to monitor other environmental conditions, such as air quality, water levels, and soil moisture.
6. CubeSats are a cost-effective way of obtaining real-time data on weather and other environmental conditions, making them ideal for use in the field of Advance Internet of Things.
