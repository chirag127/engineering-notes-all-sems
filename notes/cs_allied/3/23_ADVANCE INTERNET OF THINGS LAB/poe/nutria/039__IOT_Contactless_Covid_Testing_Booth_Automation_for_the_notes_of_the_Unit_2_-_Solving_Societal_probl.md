
### IOT Contactless Covid Testing Booth Automation

In this unit, we will discuss the use of Internet of Things (IoT) in solving societal problems, with a focus on contactless Covid testing booth automation. 

1. Automated Covid Testing: Automated Covid testing booths can be used to reduce human contact and contact with potentially infected surfaces. These booths can be equipped with sensors, cameras, and other IoT devices to detect and analyze the presence of Covid-19 in a person.

2. Automated Temperature Checks: Automated temperature checks can be used to detect fever, which is a common symptom of Covid-19. These booths can be equipped with thermal cameras and other IoT devices to detect and analyze the body temperature of a person.

3. Automated Contact Tracing: Automated contact tracing can be used to trace and identify people who have come in contact with Covid-19 infected individuals. These booths can be equipped with RFID readers, Bluetooth, and other IoT devices to detect and analyze the contact of a person with other people.

4. Automated Sanitization: Automated sanitization can be used to reduce the spread of Covid-19. These booths can be equipped with automated sanitization systems, such as UV-C light, ozone, and other IoT devices to sanitize the surfaces of the booth.

By using IoT devices in contactless Covid testing booths, we can reduce the risk of Covid-19 transmission and help to stop its spread.
