
### IOT based Manhole Detection and Monitoring System

1. Manhole detection and monitoring systems are an important part of the Internet of Things (IoT).

2. These systems are used to detect and monitor the conditions of manholes in order to prevent accidents and ensure safety.

3. They use various sensors and communication technologies to detect and monitor the conditions of manholes.

4. The sensors used in these systems can detect a variety of parameters such as temperature, humidity, pressure, and gas levels.

5. The communication technologies used include Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, LoRa, and Zigbee.

6. These systems can be used to detect and monitor manholes in real-time and can alert the authorities in case of any emergency.

7. The data collected by these systems can be analyzed to improve the maintenance and safety of manholes.
