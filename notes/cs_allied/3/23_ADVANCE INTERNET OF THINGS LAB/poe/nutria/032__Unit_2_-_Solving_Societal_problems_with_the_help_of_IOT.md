
## Unit 2 - Solving Societal Problems with the Help of IOT

1. The Internet of Things (IoT) is a network of physical devices, vehicles, home appliances, and other items embedded with electronics, software, sensors, actuators, and connectivity which enables these objects to connect and exchange data.

2. IoT has the potential to revolutionize the way we live and work, and can be used to solve many of society's problems.

3. One of the most important applications of IoT is in healthcare. IoT-enabled devices can be used to monitor patients' vital signs and provide early warning for medical emergencies.

4. IoT can also be used to improve public safety. Smart sensors can be used to detect fires, floods, and other natural disasters, and alert authorities in real-time.

5. IoT can also be used to improve energy efficiency. Smart meters can be used to monitor energy consumption, and intelligent thermostats can be used to adjust the temperature in buildings to optimize energy usage.

6. IoT can also be used to improve transportation. Smart traffic systems can be used to monitor and control traffic flows, and smart parking systems can help drivers find available parking spots.

7. IoT can also be used to improve food production. Smart sensors can be used to monitor soil and water conditions, and intelligent irrigation systems can be used to optimize water usage.

8. IoT has the potential to revolutionize the way we live and work, and can be used to solve many of society's problems.
