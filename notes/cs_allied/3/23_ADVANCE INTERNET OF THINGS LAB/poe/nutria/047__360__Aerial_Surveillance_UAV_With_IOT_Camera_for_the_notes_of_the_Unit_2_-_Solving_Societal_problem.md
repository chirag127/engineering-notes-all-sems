
### 360° Aerial Surveillance UAV With IOT Camera

* Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs) are becoming increasingly popular in several fields, such as surveillance, agriculture, and search and rescue. 
* UAVs equipped with Internet of Things (IoT) cameras can provide 360° aerial surveillance, which can be used for a variety of applications such as monitoring large areas for security purposes, observing wildlife in their natural habitats, and inspecting infrastructure for damage. 
* IoT cameras can be used to detect and track objects in real-time, providing a comprehensive view of the environment. 
* UAVs can also be used to monitor traffic, detect hazardous materials, and search for lost persons. 
* Additionally, UAVs equipped with IoT cameras can be used to monitor natural disasters such as floods, fires, and earthquakes. 
* UAVs are becoming an important tool for solving societal problems, and this technology is becoming increasingly accessible.
