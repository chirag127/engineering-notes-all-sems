
### IOT Water Pollution Monitor RC Boat

This unit is about understanding the implementation of IOT in the subject of ADVANCE INTERNET OF THINGS LAB. 

1. IOT Water Pollution Monitor RC Boat is a robotic boat used to measure and monitor water pollution. 
2. The boat is equipped with sensors to measure the pH, temperature, dissolved oxygen, and turbidity of the water. 
3. The boat is also equipped with a camera to take pictures of the water surface and to detect any visible signs of pollution. 
4. The data collected by the sensors is sent to a computer for analysis and to generate a report. 
5. The report is then used to determine the level of water pollution and to take necessary action. 
6. The boat is powered by solar energy and can be programmed to move autonomously. 
7. The boat is also equipped with a GPS system to track its location. 
8. The IOT Water Pollution Monitor RC Boat can be used for a variety of applications such as monitoring water quality in rivers, lakes, and oceans.
