
### Raspberry Pi Air and Noise Pollution Monitoring System Over IOT

1. The Internet of Things (IoT) is a network of physical objects, such as sensors, connected to the Internet. 
2. These objects can collect data from their environment and send it to a central server, allowing us to monitor and control them remotely. 
3. Raspberry Pi is a low-cost, single-board computer that can be used to create IoT projects. 
4. Air and noise pollution monitoring systems are IoT projects that measure the air and noise quality in a given area. 
5. They use sensors to collect data and send it to a central server for analysis. 
6. The data can be used to create reports and alert the authorities if the air or noise pollution levels exceed a certain threshold. 
7. An IoT air and noise pollution monitoring system can help us understand the local environment and take steps to improve it.
