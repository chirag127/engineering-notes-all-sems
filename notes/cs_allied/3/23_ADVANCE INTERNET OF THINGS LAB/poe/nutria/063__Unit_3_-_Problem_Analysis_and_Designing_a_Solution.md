
## Unit 3 - Problem Analysis and Designing a Solution

1. Problem analysis is the process of understanding the problem and identifying the root cause of the problem. It involves gathering information about the problem, analyzing the data, and developing a solution to the problem.

2. Designing a solution requires understanding the problem and its context, and then designing a solution that solves the problem in an efficient and effective way. This process involves determining the goals of the solution, identifying the stakeholders involved, and designing a solution that meets the needs of all stakeholders.

3. The process of problem analysis and designing a solution can be divided into three stages: analysis, design, and implementation.

4. In the analysis stage, the problem is identified and the root cause is determined. This involves understanding the problem, gathering data, and analyzing the data.

5. In the design stage, the goals of the solution are determined and the solution is designed. This involves determining the stakeholders involved, understanding their needs, and designing a solution that meets those needs.

6. In the implementation stage, the solution is implemented. This involves testing the solution and ensuring that it meets the goals and satisfies the needs of all stakeholders.

7. Problem analysis and designing a solution is a critical skill for any software engineer or business analyst. It requires understanding the problem, gathering data, analyzing the data, and designing a solution that meets the needs of all stakeholders.
