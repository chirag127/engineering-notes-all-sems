

### IOT Based Coal Mine Safety Monitoring and Alerting System

1. IOT (Internet of Things) is a network of physical objects that are connected to the internet and can collect and exchange data. 
2. IOT can be used to monitor and control industrial systems, such as coal mine safety systems. 
3. Coal mines have a unique set of safety requirements due to the hazardous environment, and IOT can be used to monitor and alert workers of potential dangers. 
4. IOT based coal mine safety monitoring and alerting systems use sensors to detect hazardous gases, vibration, temperature, and other environmental factors. 
5. The data collected by the sensors is sent to a central monitoring system which can alert workers of potential dangers. 
6. IOT systems can also be used to monitor the movement of workers and equipment in the mine, and provide real-time updates on the location of personnel. 
7. IOT based coal mine safety systems can also be used to detect and prevent accidents, and to provide emergency response teams with real-time data. 
8. IOT can also be used to automate processes in the coal mine, such as ventilation and lighting control, to improve safety and efficiency.
