
### IOT Garbage Segregator & Bin Level Indicator

This technology is a combination of an Internet of Things (IoT) enabled garbage segregator and a bin level indicator. It is designed to help solve societal problems related to waste management and disposal.

**Garbage Segregator:** The garbage segregator is an IoT enabled device that is used to separate different types of waste materials. It is equipped with sensors that detect the type of waste material and then segregate it according to pre-defined categories. This helps to ensure that the waste is disposed of in the most efficient way.

**Bin Level Indicator:** The bin level indicator is an IoT enabled device that is used to measure the level of waste material in a bin. It is equipped with sensors that detect the level of waste material in the bin and then sends a notification to the user when the bin is full. This helps to ensure that the waste is disposed of in a timely manner.

**Benefits:**
- Improves waste management efficiency
- Reduces the amount of waste that is sent to landfills
- Reduces the amount of time spent on waste disposal
- Helps to reduce environmental pollution
