### IOT Temperature & Mask Scan Entry System

This system uses IOT devices to scan the temperature and mask of individuals entering a space. It helps in contactless monitoring of health conditions and ensures safety protocols are followed.

- Temperature sensor: An infrared temperature sensor is used to measure the forehead temperature of individuals. If the temperature is above the set threshold, it indicates a fever and the entry is denied.
- Mask detection: A camera is used to detect if the individual is wearing a mask. If no mask is detected, the entry is denied. Computer vision and deep learning techniques are used for accurate mask detection.
- Entry control: Based on the temperature scan and mask detection results, the entry is either allowed or denied. If all conditions are met, the turnstile or door is unlocked to allow entry. Else, an alarm is triggered to indicate denied entry.
- Data logging: The temperature and mask detection results of all individuals are logged along with time stamps. This data can be used to monitor trends and take necessary actions.
- Notification: If an individual with high temperature or no mask is detected, notifications are sent to the appropriate personnel to take action.

The IOT temperature and mask scan entry system enhances safety and security using contactless monitoring and logging. It can be deployed at entrances of offices, airports, hospitals, etc. to minimize health risks in public spaces. Using IOT and deep learning, it provides an efficient entry control mechanism during the ongoing pandemic.
