### Contactless IOT Doorbell

1. Problem Statement: Existing doorbells require physical contact which can spread germs during the ongoing pandemic. A contactless doorbell can solve this problem.

2. Components Required:
- Microcontroller board (like Arduino)
- Ultrasonic sensor to detect visitors
- Buzzer or speaker to produce doorbell sound
- WiFi module to connect the doorbell to home WiFi
- Power supply for the components

3. Working: The ultrasonic sensor will continuously monitor the area in front of the door to detect any visitors. When a visitor is detected within a specified range, the microcontroller will trigger the buzzer to produce doorbell sound. It will also send a notification to the homeowner's device via the WiFi network. The homeowner can then unlock the door using a smart lock or talk to the visitor via an intercom system.

4. Advantages:
- Contactless - prevents spreading of germs
- Remotely monitor door from anywhere in the home
- Get notifications on owner's devices immediately when a visitor arrives

5. Applications: Can be installed at the entrances of homes, offices, hospitals, etc. to enable contactless doorbell functionality.
