### IOT Weather Station Airship

- An IOT weather station airship floats in the air to measure weather parameters like temperature, humidity, air pressure, wind speed, etc. and sends the data to the ground station.
- The airship has sensors to measure the weather parameters which are connected to a microcontroller.
- The microcontroller processes the data from the sensors and sends it to the ground station using wireless communication technologies like Wi-Fi, GSM, etc.
- The ground station has an internet connection to upload the weather data to the cloud.
- The weather data on the cloud can be accessed by meteorological departments, farmers, etc. to get live weather updates and take decisions accordingly.
- The airship is made of a lightweight material filled with a gas like helium to stay afloat in the air for longer durations.
- The IOT weather station airship is useful to get weather measurements from locations where it is difficult to place weather stations on the ground. It can fly at different altitudes to get weather data at various heights.
- The limitations are that it depends on winds and cannot stay stationary at a particular location. It has to keep floating in the direction of winds. The airship has to be brought down periodically for maintenance.
