### Wearable Computer With Temperature Distance Sensors

- Wearable computers are electronic devices that are worn on the body, usually as accessories or implants. They are used for applications as varied as health monitoring, virtual reality, and data collection.
- Temperature sensors can detect the body temperature of the wearer and alert in case of fever or high temperature. This can be useful for health monitoring applications to detect illnesses.
- Distance sensors can detect the proximity of the wearer to other objects or people. This can be used to alert the wearer to maintain social distancing or monitor close interactions for contact tracing applications.
- The data from such wearable sensors can be collected and sent to an IoT platform to derive insights and trigger alerts or actions. This enables solving societal problems by monitoring health, controlling spread of diseases, and other applications.
- The components required would be sensors to detect temperature and distance, processing unit to collect and transmit the data, and connectivity options to send the data to an IoT platform. The exact technical components would depend on the specific use case and requirements.
- Wearable computers with such sensors can enable contactless monitoring of individuals and aid in tackling medical and public health issues. However, data privacy and security considerations are important when collecting and using personal data from such devices. Appropriate safeguards must be in place to protect user information.
