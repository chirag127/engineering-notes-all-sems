### IOT Circuit Breaker Project

- An IOT based circuit breaker can be designed which can monitor the electricity consumption of household appliances and can cut off the power supply in case of overload or short circuit to prevent damage to appliances and wiring.
- The IOT circuit breaker will have current sensors to measure the current drawn by the appliances. It will have a microcontroller to process the sensor data and detect abnormal current draws.
- In case of overload or short circuit, the microcontroller will trigger a relay to cut off the power supply. It can also send alerts to the user via internet through inbuilt wifi/GSM modules.
- The threshold for overload and short circuit can be configured by the user based on the capacity of wiring and appliances. The IOT circuit breaker can provide an additional layer of safety for electricity consumption.
- The IOT circuit breaker can be expanded to monitor the electricity usage of individual appliances and provide analytics on energy consumption and cost which can help in efficient usage of electricity.
