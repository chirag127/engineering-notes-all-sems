### IOT Social Distancing & Monitoring Robot For Queue

**For the notes of the Unit 1 - Understanding the implementation of IOT in the subject of ADVANCE INTERNET OF THINGS LAB.**

1. The robot is designed to monitor social distancing in queues and ensure people maintain a minimum distance of 1.5 meters from each other.
2. It has ultrasonic sensors to detect proximity of people and cameras to monitor the queue.
3. If it detects people not maintaining social distancing, it alerts them with audio warnings and alerts officials monitoring the queue.
4. The robot can autonomously navigate to monitor the entire queue length and breadth.
5. The data from sensors and cameras is collected and processed on an on-board Raspberry Pi system which controls the robot navigation and warning systems.
6. The IOT system allows monitoring of multiple such robots at different locations from a central control system. Alerts can be monitored and queues can be managed efficiently while ensuring safety protocols.
7. The robot is an application of IOT and embedded systems for a social purpose of maintaining safety and security during the ongoing pandemic.
8. It demonstrates the use of sensors, cameras, robotics and warning systems integrated into an IOT system.
