### IOT Prison Break Monitoring & Alerting System

1. Sensors are placed on the prison walls and fences to monitor any intrusions or damages. These sensors detect vibrations, cuts or breakages and alert the security team.
2. CCTV cameras with motion detection capabilities are installed to monitor the prisoners and detect any suspicious movements or gatherings. If a large number of prisoners are found to be moving together or if any prisoner is moving in unauthorized areas, alerts are raised.
3. Biometric sensors and RFID tags are used to track the location and movements of individual prisoners. If a prisoner is found to be outside the authorized location or zone, alerts are triggered.
4. Gas sensors detect outbreak of fires or release of toxic gases and raise emergency alerts for evacuation and protective actions.
5. All the alerts are monitored 24/7 in a centralized security control room. Action is taken immediately based on the type and severity of the alert. The security team can also monitor all the sensors and cameras directly for manual oversight.
6. The IOT system helps reduce the possibility of prisoners breaking out of the prison by continuous monitoring and triggering timely alerts. It enhances the security and safety of the prison.

The above points cover the key aspects of an IOT based prison break monitoring and alerting system. The IOT devices and sensors monitor the critical parameters and trigger alerts to the security team to enable quick actions. The continuous monitoring improves security and reduces breakout attempts.
