### IOT Mining Tracking & Worker Safety Helmet

- IOT Mining Helmet is used to track the location and health parameters of miners working underground. It is equipped with various sensors and connectivity modules.
- The helmet has sensors to detect hazardous gases like carbon monoxide, methane, etc. and alerts the mining control room if the gas concentration increases above the safe level.
- It has physiological sensors to monitor health parameters like pulse, temperature, respiration rate of the miner and sends an alert to the control room if any parameter goes out of the safe range.
- The helmet has GPS and GPRS modules to continuously track the location of each miner and update the control room regarding their location. This can help in rescuing miners in case of any mishap or emergency.
- The data from various sensors of the helmet can be used to identify potential risks and ensure safer working conditions for the miners. The physiological data can also be used to monitor the health of miners in the long run.
- The implementation of IOT in mining safety helmets is a good example of how IOT can be utilized to monitor assets, workers, and work conditions to increase safety and efficiency. The data collected can be leveraged to gain useful business insights and take corrective actions.
