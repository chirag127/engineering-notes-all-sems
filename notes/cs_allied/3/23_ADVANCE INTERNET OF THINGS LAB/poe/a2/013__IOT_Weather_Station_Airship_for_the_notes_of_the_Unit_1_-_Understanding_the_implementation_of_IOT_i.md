### IOT Weather Station Airship for the notes of the Unit 1 - Understanding the implementation of IOT in the subject of ADVANCE INTERNET OF THINGS LAB

1. An IOT weather station airship refers to a blimp or balloon equipped with sensors and communication modules to measure weather parameters at high altitudes and transmit the data to the ground station.
2. The key components of an IOT weather station airship include:
    - Gas filled balloon or blimp to provide lift and make the airship airborne. Helium or hydrogen gas is used for filling the balloon.
    - Sensor array to measure weather parameters like temperature, humidity, air pressure, wind speed, wind direction, etc.
    - Microcontroller and communication modules to collect and transmit the sensor data.
    - Power supply like batteries to power the electronic components.
3. The advantages of using an IOT weather station airship are:
    - It can reach high altitudes and measure weather parameters that are not easily accessible from ground stations.
    - It is more affordable than weather satellites and weather balloons.
    - It can be navigated to measure weather parameters at specific locations.
4. The challenges involved in implementing an IOT weather station airship are:
    - Reliability of sensors and communication modules at high altitudes.
    - Power management and ensuring continuous power supply for extended duration flights.
    - Navigating and controlling the airship to specific locations.
    - Retrieving the airship and sensor data after flights.
5. IOT weather station airships have the potential to supplement traditional weather monitoring techniques and provide localized high altitude weather monitoring at affordable costs. They can be useful for weather forecasting and studying atmospheric phenomena.
