### IOT Based Heart Monitoring System Using ECG for the notes of the Unit 2 - Solving Societal problems with the help of IOT in the subject of ADVANCE INTERNET OF THINGS LAB

1. Introduction
- ECG or Electrocardiogram is a diagnostic tool used to access the electrical activity of the heart.
- IOT based ECG system allows remote and continuous monitoring of heart activity.
- Such a system can be used to monitor heart patients and alert doctors in case of abnormality.

2. Components
- ECG electrodes: To capture heart's electrical activity
- ECG circuit: To amplify and filter signals
- Microcontroller: To digitize and process signals
- Wireless transmitter: To send data to IOT platform
- IOT platform: To receive and display data and alert doctors

3. Working
- ECG electrodes are placed on patient's chest to capture electrical signals
- Signals are sent to ECG circuit to amplify and filter noises
- Microcontroller converts analog signals to digital and processes them
- Processed signals are sent wirelessly to IOT platform
- IOT platform analyzes data and alerts doctors in case of abnormal patterns

4. Applications
- Remote monitoring of heart patients
- Early detection of heart abnormalities
- Continuous monitoring allowing immediate medical assistance
- Tracking heart health trends over time

5. Conclusion
- IOT based ECG system allows convenient and continuous monitoring of heart activity.
- It can help in early detection of heart conditions and provide timely medical assistance thereby improving patient outcomes.
- Such systems have wide applications in remote patient monitoring and telemedicine.
