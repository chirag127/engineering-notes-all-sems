## Unit 2 - Solving Societal problems with the help of IOT

1. Smart Cities - IOT can help in making smart cities which can efficiently manage and optimize city functions like:
- Traffic management - Sensors can monitor traffic and smart signals can control traffic flow.
- Waste management - Sensors can monitor waste bins and alert authorities for collection when full.
- Energy management - Sensors can monitor and optimize energy usage of street lights, buildings, etc.
- Water management - Sensors can monitor water leakages and usage to prevent wastage.

2. Healthcare - IOT can help improve healthcare through:
- Remote monitoring of patients - Sensors can monitor patients and alert doctors to changes.
- Smart wearable devices - Devices can monitor health in real-time and track things like heart rate, oxygen levels, etc.
- Automated preventive care - IOT can enable robotic systems to provide automated preventive care.

3. Agriculture - IOT can help improve agriculture through:
- Soil monitoring - Sensors can monitor soil conditions like moisture, pH, nutrient levels, etc. and help optimize farm management.
- Livestock monitoring - Sensors can monitor livestock conditions and alert farmers to any health or behavioral changes.
- Smart irrigation - IOT can enable automated irrigation systems which provide the optimum amount of water required.
