### IOT Early Flood Detection & Avoidance

1. IOT sensors can be used to detect rising water levels in rivers or lakes and alert relevant authorities.
2. Water level sensors can be placed at regular intervals along the banks of rivers or lakes. They detect the water level and send the data to a central server.
3. The server monitors the data from all the sensors and detects an abnormal or rapid rise in water levels.
4. Once a dangerous rise in water levels is detected, the server sends out alerts to the relevant local authorities like disaster management authorities.
5. The authorities can then evacuate people living in low-lying areas and take other flood avoidance actions to reduce loss of life and property.
6. The IOT based system provides early detection of floods which can save many lives as authorities get more time to react and evacuate people compared to traditional flood detection systems.
7. The system can be expanded to include more parameters like rainfall, inflow, outflow, etc. to get a more comprehensive view of the flood situation. More data can lead to even earlier detection of floods.

The above content summarizes how IOT sensors and a central server can be used to detect rising flood waters early and alert authorities to take action, thereby preventing loss of life and property during floods.
