### IOT based Intelligent Gas Leakage Detector Using Arduino

- An IOT based gas leakage detection system is designed to detect the leakage of harmful gases like LPG, Natural gas, etc. and alert the user through the internet.
- The system consists of gas sensors to detect the gas leakage, a microcontroller (here Arduino) to control the system and a WiFi module to send alerts through the internet.
- The gas sensors detect the gases in the surrounding and the analog values from the sensors are sent to the Arduino.
- The Arduino is programmed to check if the sensed values cross the threshold limit (indicating gas leakage). If a leakage is detected, the Arduino sends an alert message to the user through the WiFi module.
- The user can be alerted through SMS, Email or an app notification depending on the system design.
- This system can be placed in residential or commercial areas for monitoring gas leakages and ensuring safety. It is an ideal example of solving societal problems through IOT concepts.
- The system's operation can be periodically monitored on a dashboard through the internet. Alerts are logged for analysis and reference.
- The system is low cost, easy to deploy and helps in remote monitoring of gas leakages. It significantly reduces the risks associated with gas leakages by providing an early warning system.
