### IOT based Three Phase Power Failure Monitoring with SMS Alerts

- IOT sensors are used to sense the voltage and current of three phases of electricity supply.
- The sensed values are sent to the microcontroller which calculates power and checks for power failure in any of the three phases.
- In case of power failure in any phase, the microcontroller sends an SMS alert to the concerned person through GSM module.
- The hardware involves current sensors, voltage sensors, microcontroller, GSM module and power supply.
- The software involves programming the microcontroller to calculate power, check for failure and send SMS.
- This project helps in real time monitoring of three phase power supply and getting alerts in case of power failure for taking corrective actions.
- It has applications in critical power backup systems to ensure continuous power supply.
