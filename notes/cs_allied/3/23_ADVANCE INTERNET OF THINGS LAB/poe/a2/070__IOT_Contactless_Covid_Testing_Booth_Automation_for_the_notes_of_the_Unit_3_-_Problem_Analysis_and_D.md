### IOT Contactless Covid Testing Booth Automation

- The project involves designing an IoT based contactless Covid-19 testing booth.
- The booth will have sensors to detect a person entering and exiting. It will sanitize the booth after every exit.
- A contactless temperature sensor will check the body temperature of the person entering. If the temperature is above the threshold, the person will be denied entry.
- A pulse oximeter sensor will check the SpO2 levels of the person. If it is below the threshold, the person will be denied entry.
- A COVID-19 testing kit will collect the sample from the person via a contactless mechanism. The sample will then be tested for COVID-19.
- The test results will be displayed on an LCD screen outside the booth. If positive, an alarm will alert the medical staff.
- The automation will minimize human intervention and risk of virus transmission to the medical staff. It provides an efficient and timely COVID testing solution.
- The core components would be sensors, motors, LCD display, Raspberry Pi controller, and testing kit. Programming will be done to coordinate the components and implement the automation.
