### IOT based Manhole Detection and Monitoring System

1. Introduction
- Manholes are underground openings to access sewers and utility tunnels.
- They pose a risk of accidents to pedestrians due to open or uncovered manholes.
- An IOT based system can detect and monitor manhole covers to alert authorities about any open or faulty covers to avoid accidents.

2. Components
- Sensors: Detect the state of manhole covers, whether open or closed. Can be ultrasonic, IR or pressure sensors.
- Microcontroller: Receives data from sensors and processes it.
- Communication module: Sends data from microcontroller to cloud/servers. Can use WiFi, GPRS, NB-IoT.
- Cloud/Server: Stores and analyses data, monitors manhole covers in an area, sends alerts if any cover is open or faulty.

3. Working
- Sensors sense the state of manhole covers continuously.
- Microcontroller receives and processes the data from sensors.
- Communication module sends the data to cloud/server.
- Cloud/server monitors data from all manhole covers, detects any open or faulty covers and sends alerts to responsible authorities to take action.

4. Conclusion
- An IOT based manhole detection and monitoring system can help detect uncovered or faulty manhole covers and alert authorities to avoid accidents and injuries to pedestrians.
- It makes the monitoring process efficient and continuous unlike manual inspection.
- The system can be scaled to monitor manholes across a city or region.
