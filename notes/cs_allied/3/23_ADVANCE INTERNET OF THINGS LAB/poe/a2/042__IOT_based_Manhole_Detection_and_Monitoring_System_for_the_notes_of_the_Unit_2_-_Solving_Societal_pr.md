### IOT based Manhole Detection and Monitoring System

- Manholes are underground openings to access sewer lines or other municipal services.
- Due to aging infrastructure or heavy usage, manholes can deteriorate or shift position over time leading to potential hazards on roads.
- IOT can be utilized to detect and monitor the status of manholes:
- Sensors can be placed inside manholes to detect level, temperature, gases, etc. and alert authorities to any issues.
- CCTV cameras can monitor for leaks, cracks or other structural damage.
- Tilt sensors can detect if manholes have shifted from their original position.
- All sensor data can be collected and monitored in a central dashboard to take necessary actions to ensure road safety and efficient municipal services.
- With real-time monitoring, issues can be detected early and dealt with proactively avoiding dangerous road situations or service outages.
- IOT enables smart cities to effectively manage their infrastructure through connected sensor networks and data analytics.
