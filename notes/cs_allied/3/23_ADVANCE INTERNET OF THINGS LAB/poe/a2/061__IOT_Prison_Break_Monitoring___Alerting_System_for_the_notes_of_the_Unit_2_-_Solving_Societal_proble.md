### IOT Prison Break Monitoring & Alerting System

1. Prison breaks are serious issues that need to be prevented. An IOT based system can help monitor prisons and alert officials in case of a breakout.
2. Sensors can be placed on prison walls and fences to detect any damage or breaches. Motion sensors can be used to detect any unauthorized movement.
3. Cameras can be installed across the prison and monitored 24/7 by officials. Any suspicious activity can be detected and acted upon immediately.
4. Prisoners can be tracked using RFID tags or wrist bands. If any prisoner moves out of authorized areas or tries to remove the tag, officials can be alerted.
5. All alerts can be sent to officials on their mobile devices so that immediate action can be taken. The IOT system must have fail-safes and backups in place to avoid system downtime.
6. With numerous sensors and monitoring systems in place, the chance of prisoners breaking out can be minimized. Any attempts can be detected and curtailed early with the help of such an IOT based alerting system.
