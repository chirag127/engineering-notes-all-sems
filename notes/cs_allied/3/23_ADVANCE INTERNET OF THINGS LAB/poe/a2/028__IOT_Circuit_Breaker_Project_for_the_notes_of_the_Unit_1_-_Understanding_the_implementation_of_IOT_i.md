### IOT Circuit Breaker Project for the notes of the Unit 1 - Understanding the implementation of IOT in the subject of ADVANCE INTERNET OF THINGS LAB.

1. Introduction to IOT Circuit Breaker
- An IOT Circuit Breaker is a device that can make or break an electric circuit under a set of conditions.
- It is integrated with sensors and wireless connectivity to monitor parameters like current, voltage, temperature, etc. and can trip the circuit in case of abnormal conditions to prevent damage.
- The IOT Circuit Breaker can be accessed and controlled remotely through the internet.

2. Components of IOT Circuit Breaker
- Microcontroller: To control the circuit breaker and process sensor data
- Sensors: To sense parameters like current, voltage, temperature, etc.
- Wireless Module: To connect to the internet and enable remote access
- Relay: To trip the circuit in case of abnormal conditions detected by the sensors
- Power Supply: To power the microcontroller and other components

3. Working of IOT Circuit Breaker
- The sensors continuously monitor the electric parameters and send data to the microcontroller
- The microcontroller processes the data and checks for permissible range
- If any abnormal value is detected, the microcontroller operates the relay to trip the circuit
- The IOT Circuit Breaker can be accessed remotely to monitor status and modify conditions
- The circuit breaker state can be controlled remotely as well in some implementations

4. Applications of IOT Circuit Breaker
- Overload and short-circuit protection for home appliances and devices
- Temperature sensing and protection for devices
- Remote monitoring and control of electric devices
- Smart electric panels with remote accessibility
