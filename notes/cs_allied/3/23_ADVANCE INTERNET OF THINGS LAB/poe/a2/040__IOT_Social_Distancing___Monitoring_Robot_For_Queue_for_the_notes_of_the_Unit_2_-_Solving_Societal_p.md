### IOT Social Distancing & Monitoring Robot For Queue

- Due to the COVID-19 pandemic, maintaining social distancing in public places like shops, hospitals, banks, etc. has become very important.
- An IOT based social distancing monitoring robot can be deployed in such places to monitor queue and ensure that proper social distancing is maintained.
- The robot will have ultrasonic sensors to detect the distance between people standing in a queue.
- If the distance between two people is less than the recommended social distancing gap, the robot will alert them by flashing lights or playing an audio message to warn them and increase the gap.
- The robot can also track people not wearing masks and alert the authorities.
- Data about social distancing violations can be collected and analyzed to take necessary actions.
- Such an IOT based robot can reduce human intervention and minimize the risk of virus spread through monitoring.
