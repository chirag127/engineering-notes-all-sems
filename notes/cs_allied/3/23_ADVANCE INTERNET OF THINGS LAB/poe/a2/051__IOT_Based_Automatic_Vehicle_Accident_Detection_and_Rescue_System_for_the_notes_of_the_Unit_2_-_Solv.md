### IOT Based Automatic Vehicle Accident Detection and Rescue System

- Vehicle accidents are one of the major causes of fatalities and severe injuries across the world. An IOT based automatic vehicle accident detection and rescue system can help detect accidents quickly and alert emergency services for faster rescue operations.
- The system consists of sensors, a microcontroller and communication modules installed in the vehicle. The sensors detect sudden changes in velocity or trajectory of the vehicle which indicates an accident.
- The microcontroller processes the sensor data and determines if an accident has occurred. In case of an accident, it sends an alert message to emergency services with the location of the vehicle using the communication modules like GSM, GPS, WiFi, etc.
- Emergency services can then dispatch medical help to the accident site as soon as possible and save lives by providing quick medical attention to the injured. The faster the emergency response, the higher the chances of survival and recovery of accident victims.
- The IOT based system removes the need for human intervention to alert emergency services in the event of a crash. This can significantly reduce response times and improve outcomes especially in the case of severe, life-threatening injuries. The system can thus leverage IOT to solve an important societal problem and save lives.
