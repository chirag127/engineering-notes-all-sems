### IOT Covid Patient Health Monitor in Quarantine for the notes of the Unit 1 - Understanding the implementation of IOT in the subject of ADVANCE INTERNET OF THINGS LAB.

- Patients in home quarantine can be monitored remotely using IOT devices and sensors.
- Body temperature, heart rate, oxygen saturation levels and other health parameters can be continuously monitored using wireless sensors.
- The health data is sent to a central monitoring system which alerts doctors/healthcare workers if any of the parameters go beyond safe levels.
- This allows patients to be monitored 24/7 without requiring physical presence of healthcare workers and minimizes risk of infection to them.
- The patients can be provided with an emergency alert button to notify doctors in case of any deterioration in health or emergency.
- Location tracking mechanisms can also be used to ensure patients remain in quarantine.
- Data security and privacy must be ensured through necessary technical and administrative controls.
- IOT enabled remote health monitoring can be useful for monitoring not just Covid-19 patients but also other patients with chronic conditions who require regular monitoring.
