### Contactless IOT Doorbell

- A contactless IoT doorbell is a smart doorbell that enables visitors to ring the bell without touching the physical button. It uses motion sensors to detect people approaching the door and rings the bell automatically.
- The key components of a contactless IoT doorbell are:
-- Motion sensor: To detect movement near the doorbell
-- Microcontroller: To receive signals from the motion sensor and trigger the doorbell
-- Doorbell: The electrical doorbell that is triggered by the microcontroller
-- Power supply: To provide power to the electronic components
- The motion sensor detects visitors approaching the door and sends a signal to the microcontroller. The microcontroller processes the signal and rings the doorbell. This allows visitors to announce their presence without touching the doorbell, enabling touch-free operation that is important during the COVID-19 pandemic.
- The IOT capabilities allow the doorbell to be connected to home WiFi and smartphone apps. This enables the homeowner to receive notifications when the doorbell is rung and monitor the entrance remotely. The IOT connectivity makes the doorbell 'smart' and more useful than a regular doorbell.
- The implementation of a contactless IOT doorbell requires electronic components, IOT modules, and programming the microcontroller to respond to signals from the motion sensor and trigger the doorbell. It demonstrates the application of IOT and automation technologies to improve simple home devices.
