### Wearable Computer With Temperature Distance Sensors

1. Introduction
- A wearable computer is a computing device that is worn on the body. It has sensors to detect various parameters and is connected to the Internet.
- In this project, we will design a wearable computer that has temperature and distance sensors. It will detect the temperature of the surrounding environment and the distance of nearby objects.

2. Components
- Microcontroller: Arduino Nano
- Temperature sensor: LM35 temperature sensor
- Distance sensor: Ultrasonic distance sensor (HC-SR04)
- Display: LCD display
- Power supply: Battery

3. Working
- The temperature sensor will detect the ambient temperature and send the analog voltage output to the Arduino Nano.
- The Arduino Nano will convert the analog voltage to digital temperature values and display it on the LCD display.
- The distance sensor will send out ultrasonic pulses and detect the time taken for echo return to calculate the distance of nearby objects. This will also be displayed on the LCD display.
- The wearable device will be powered by a battery. The readings can be logged and sent to the Internet via Wi-Fi for monitoring and analysis.

4. Applications
- The temperature sensor can be used to monitor body temperature or environmental temperature.
- The distance sensor can be used as an obstacle avoidance system or to measure dimensions of objects.
- The data can be sent to the Internet for remote patient monitoring or tracking physical parameters in smart homes or industries.
