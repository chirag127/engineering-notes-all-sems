### IOT based Three Phase Power Failure Monitoring with SMS Alerts

In today's world, the demand for electricity has increased drastically. Three-phase power is used to power large motors and other heavy loads. However, power outages in three-phase systems can cause significant damage to the equipment. Therefore, monitoring three-phase power is critical. Here's how IOT can help monitor three-phase power and provide SMS alerts in case of a failure.

- The system consists of three voltage sensors, one for each phase, connected to an IOT device. The sensors continuously monitor the voltage and send the data to the IOT device.
- The IOT device transmits the data to the cloud platform, where it is analyzed by the software for any abnormalities in the power levels.
- In case of a power failure, the software detects the drop in voltage and sends an SMS alert to the designated mobile number. The alert contains information about which phase has failed.
- The system can also be configured to send alerts to multiple numbers or email addresses, ensuring that someone is always informed in case of a power failure.
- The IOT device can also be configured to send alerts if the voltage levels go above or below a certain threshold, indicating a potential problem before a power failure occurs.
- The system can be used in residential, commercial, or industrial settings, providing a reliable and efficient way to monitor three-phase power and prevent equipment damage.

In conclusion, IOT-based three-phase power failure monitoring with SMS alerts is an excellent solution to prevent equipment damage caused by power outages. It provides a reliable, efficient, and cost-effective way to monitor three-phase power and ensure that someone is always notified in case of a failure.
