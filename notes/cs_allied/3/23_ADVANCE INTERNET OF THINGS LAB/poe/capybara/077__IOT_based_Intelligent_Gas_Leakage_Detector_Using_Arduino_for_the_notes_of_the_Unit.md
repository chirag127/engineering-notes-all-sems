### IOT based Intelligent Gas Leakage Detector Using Arduino

- An IOT based intelligent gas leakage detector is a device that detects the presence of gas in the surrounding air and sends an alert to the user via the internet.
- This device is based on Arduino technology, which is an open-source platform used for building electronics projects.
- The gas detector consists of sensors that detect the gas and a microcontroller (Arduino) that processes the data from the sensors and sends an alert to the user.
- The sensors used in the gas detector can detect different types of gases, such as LPG, methane, propane, and carbon monoxide.
- The gas detector is connected to the internet using Wi-Fi or Ethernet, which enables the user to receive alerts on their smartphone or computer.
- The device can be programmed to send alerts via email, SMS, or push notification.
- The gas detector can also be connected to a home automation system, which can automatically shut off the gas supply in case of a leak.
- The gas detector is easy to install and can be placed in various locations, such as the kitchen, garage, or basement.
- The device is powered by a USB cable or battery, which makes it portable and easy to use.
- An IOT based intelligent gas leakage detector is a reliable and cost-effective solution for detecting gas leaks and preventing potential hazards.
