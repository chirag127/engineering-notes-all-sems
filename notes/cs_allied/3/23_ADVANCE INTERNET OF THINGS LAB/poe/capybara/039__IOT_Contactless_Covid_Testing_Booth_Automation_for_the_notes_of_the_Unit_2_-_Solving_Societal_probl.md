### IOT Contactless Covid Testing Booth Automation

In the midst of the Covid-19 pandemic, contactless testing has become a crucial tool in the fight against the virus. The development of IoT-enabled contactless testing booths has been a significant step forward in this direction. Here are some key points about IOT Contactless Covid Testing Booth Automation:

- The IOT-enabled contactless testing booths are equipped with sensors and cameras that can detect the presence of a person in the booth. They also have temperature sensors that can measure the body temperature of the person.
- The booth is designed in a way that the person can enter and exit without touching any surfaces. There is a foot-operated sanitization dispenser at the entrance of the booth, which dispenses sanitizer when a person steps on it.
- The person enters the booth and stands on a designated spot. The camera and sensors detect the person's presence, and the testing process begins automatically.
- The testing process involves the collection of a sample using a swab. The swab is inserted into a container, which is then automatically sealed and transported to the laboratory for testing.
- The booth is equipped with a display screen that provides information on the testing process and the results. The results are usually available within 24-48 hours.
- The IOT-enabled contactless testing booths are designed to be operated remotely, which means that they can be monitored and controlled from a central location.
- The use of IOT-enabled contactless testing booths has several advantages over traditional testing methods. It reduces the risk of infection transmission, saves time and resources, and provides accurate and timely results.
- The development of IOT-enabled contactless testing booths is a significant step forward in the fight against the Covid-19 pandemic. It is an excellent example of how technology can be used to solve societal problems.

In conclusion, IOT Contactless Covid Testing Booth Automation is a crucial tool in the fight against the Covid-19 pandemic. Its development is a significant step forward in the use of technology to solve societal problems.
