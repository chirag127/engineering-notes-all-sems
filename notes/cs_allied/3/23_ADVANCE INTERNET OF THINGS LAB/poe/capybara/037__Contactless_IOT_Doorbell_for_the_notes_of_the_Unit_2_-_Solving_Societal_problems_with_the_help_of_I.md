### Contactless IOT Doorbell

In the era of the Covid-19 pandemic, the need for contactless technology has risen significantly. The Contactless IOT Doorbell is an innovative solution that addresses the issue of contactless entry into homes and buildings. Let's explore the features of this doorbell.

- The Contactless IOT Doorbell is equipped with an infrared sensor that detects the presence of a person at the doorstep. This sensor is activated when someone approaches the door, and the doorbell rings automatically.

- The doorbell has a camera that captures the image of the person standing at the door. This image is then sent to the homeowner's smartphone or tablet, allowing them to see who is at the door without having to open it.

- The doorbell also has a microphone and a speaker that enable two-way communication between the homeowner and the visitor. This feature eliminates the need for face-to-face contact, making it ideal for situations where social distancing is necessary.

- The Contactless IOT Doorbell is connected to the internet, allowing the homeowner to control it from anywhere in the world. This feature is particularly useful for people who travel frequently or those who want to monitor their home remotely.

- The doorbell is easy to install and can be connected to the existing wiring of the home. It is compatible with most smartphones and tablets, making it accessible to a wide range of users.

In conclusion, the Contactless IOT Doorbell is an excellent solution for contactless entry into homes and buildings. Its innovative features make it an ideal choice for people who value safety and convenience.
