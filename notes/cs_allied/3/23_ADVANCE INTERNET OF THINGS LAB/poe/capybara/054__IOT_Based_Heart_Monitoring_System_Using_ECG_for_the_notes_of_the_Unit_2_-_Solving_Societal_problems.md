### IOT Based Heart Monitoring System Using ECG

- An IOT-based heart monitoring system is a revolutionary technology that can help to monitor a patient's heart condition remotely.
- This system uses ECG (Electrocardiogram) to record the electrical activity of the heart and transmit it to the healthcare provider.
- The system consists of a wearable device that is attached to the patient's chest, which records the ECG signals and sends them to the cloud using the internet.
- The healthcare provider can access the patient's ECG data from anywhere and anytime, which helps in early detection of any abnormalities in the patient's heart condition.
- The system also has an alert system that sends an alarm to the healthcare provider if the patient's heart rate or rhythm is abnormal.
- This technology is especially helpful for patients who have a history of heart diseases or those who are at risk of developing heart problems.
- With the help of this system, doctors can monitor their patients' heart condition more closely and provide timely treatment if necessary.
- This technology also reduces the need for frequent visits to the hospital, which saves patients time and money.
- The system is easy to use, and patients can wear it comfortably without any inconvenience.
- In conclusion, an IOT-based heart monitoring system using ECG is an excellent example of how technology can help solve societal problems. This technology can improve the quality of life for patients with heart diseases and reduce healthcare costs.
