### IOT Social Distancing & Monitoring Robot For Queue

In today's world, social distancing has become a crucial aspect to prevent the spread of contagious diseases. The IOT social distancing and monitoring robot for queues is an innovative solution that enables people to maintain social distancing while waiting in queues or standing in crowded places.

Here are some key points about the IOT social distancing and monitoring robot for queues:

- The robot is equipped with sensors that can detect the distance between people in a queue. It can alert people if they are standing too close to each other, reminding them to maintain social distancing.
- The robot can also monitor the number of people in a queue and provide real-time updates on the length of the queue. This helps people to plan their time and avoid crowded places.
- The robot can be controlled remotely through a mobile application, making it easy to deploy in different locations.
- The robot has a built-in camera that can capture images and videos of the queue. This can be useful for monitoring the queue and identifying any issues or incidents.
- The robot is designed to be cost-effective and easy to maintain, making it a feasible solution for public places like airports, hospitals, and shopping malls.

In conclusion, the IOT social distancing and monitoring robot for queues is an innovative solution that can help prevent the spread of contagious diseases by enforcing social distancing in public places. Its advanced features and cost-effectiveness make it a viable option for public places, ensuring the safety and well-being of people.
