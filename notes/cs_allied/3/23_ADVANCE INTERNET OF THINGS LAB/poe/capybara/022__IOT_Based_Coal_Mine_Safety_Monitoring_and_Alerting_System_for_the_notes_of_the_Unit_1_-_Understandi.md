### IOT Based Coal Mine Safety Monitoring and Alerting System

In the coal mining industry, there are a number of risks involved in the mining process. One of the major risks is the occurrence of accidents due to the release of harmful gases, fire, or other hazardous situations. To address these risks, an IoT-based coal mine safety monitoring and alerting system can be implemented. Here are the key points of this system:

- The system uses IoT sensors that are installed in the coal mines to monitor the surroundings and collect data related to temperature, humidity, gas concentration, and other parameters.

- The data collected by the sensors is transmitted to a central control system using a wireless network. This data is then analyzed in real-time to identify any hazardous situations.

- In case the sensors detect any hazardous situation, such as the presence of toxic gases or high temperature, an alert is sent to the central control system. The system then triggers an alarm or sends a message to the relevant authorities to take necessary action.

- The system also includes wearable IoT devices that are worn by the miners. These devices monitor the vital signs of the miners, such as heart rate, body temperature, and oxygen saturation, and transmit this data to the central control system.

- In case the wearable devices detect any abnormality in the vital signs of the miners, an alert is sent to the central control system. The system then triggers an alarm or sends a message to the relevant authorities to take necessary action.

- The system also includes a dashboard that displays real-time data related to the mining operations and the safety of the miners. This dashboard can be accessed by the mining company or the relevant authorities to monitor the operations and take necessary action.

Overall, an IoT-based coal mine safety monitoring and alerting system can help improve the safety of the miners and reduce the risks involved in the mining process.
