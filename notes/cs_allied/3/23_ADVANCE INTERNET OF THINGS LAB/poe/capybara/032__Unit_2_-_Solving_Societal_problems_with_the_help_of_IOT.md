## Unit 2 - Solving Societal problems with the help of IOT

In this unit, we will learn about how the Internet of Things (IoT) can be used to solve societal problems. Here are some key points to remember:

- IoT refers to the network of physical devices, vehicles, home appliances, and other items embedded with electronics, software, sensors, and connectivity that enables these objects to connect and exchange data.
- IoT can be used to solve societal problems such as traffic congestion, energy waste, and air pollution by creating smart cities.
- Smart cities use IoT devices such as sensors, cameras, and smart traffic lights to collect data and optimize city services.
- IoT can also be used to improve healthcare by creating wearable devices that monitor patients' health in real-time and alert healthcare providers if there are any problems.
- IoT can be used to improve agriculture by creating smart farming systems that use sensors to monitor soil conditions, weather patterns, and crop health, allowing farmers to optimize their crop yields.
- IoT can also be used to improve disaster response by creating early warning systems that use sensors to detect earthquakes, floods, and other natural disasters, allowing for quicker and more effective response times.
- However, there are also challenges associated with using IoT to solve societal problems, such as data privacy concerns and the need for robust cybersecurity measures to protect against hacking and cyberattacks.
- It is important for policymakers and industry leaders to work together to develop guidelines and regulations to ensure that IoT is used in a safe and ethical manner for the benefit of society.
