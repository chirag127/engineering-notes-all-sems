### IOT based Three Phase Power Failure Monitoring with SMS Alerts

In today's world, power outages can have a significant impact on businesses and households. Power failures can cause a loss of productivity, damage to equipment, and even safety hazards. Therefore, it is essential to monitor power failures and take necessary actions to minimize the impact of such failures.

Internet of Things (IOT) provides an excellent solution for monitoring power failures. Here is a brief overview of how IOT can be used for Three Phase Power Failure Monitoring with SMS Alerts:

1. Hardware Requirements: 
   - Arduino Uno board
   - Three-phase voltage sensor
   - Three-phase current sensor
   - GSM module
   - Relay module
   - Buzzer
   - LED indicators

2. Circuit Design:
   - Connect the three-phase voltage sensor and current sensors to the Arduino board.
   - Connect the GSM module to the Arduino board.
   - Connect the relay module, buzzer, and LED indicators to the Arduino board.
   
3. Software Requirements:
   - Arduino IDE
   - SIM800L library

4. Programming the Arduino board:
   - Use the Arduino IDE to program the board to read the voltage and current values continuously.
   - Use the GSM module to send SMS alerts to pre-defined phone numbers in case of power failure.
   - Use the relay module to turn off/on the power supply to the equipment in case of a power failure.
   - Use the buzzer and LED indicators to provide audio and visual alerts in case of power failure.

5. Advantages of IOT based Three Phase Power Failure Monitoring with SMS Alerts:
   - Real-time monitoring of power supply.
   - Immediate alerts in case of power failure.
   - Remote control of power supply.
   - Improved safety and reduced equipment damage.
   - Improved productivity and reduced downtime.

In conclusion, IOT based Three Phase Power Failure Monitoring with SMS Alerts is an excellent solution to monitor power failures and minimize their impact. With the use of sensors, GSM modules, and Arduino boards, it is possible to monitor power supply in real-time and take necessary actions in case of power failures. This technology is highly beneficial for businesses and households that rely heavily on electricity.
