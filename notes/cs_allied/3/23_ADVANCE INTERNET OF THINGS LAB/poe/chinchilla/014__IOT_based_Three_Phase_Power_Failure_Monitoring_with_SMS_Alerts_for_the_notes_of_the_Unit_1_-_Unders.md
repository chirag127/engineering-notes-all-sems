### IOT based Three Phase Power Failure Monitoring with SMS Alerts

In this section, we will discuss the implementation of IoT in monitoring three-phase power failures and sending SMS alerts. The IoT-based solution provides real-time monitoring and notification of power failures, which can help in preventing damage to expensive electrical equipment and avoid production losses.

#### Components

The following are the components required for the implementation of the IoT-based three-phase power failure monitoring system:

- Three-phase power supply
- IoT device (such as Raspberry Pi or Arduino)
- Current transformer sensors
- GSM module
- Power relay

#### Working

The current transformer sensors are connected to the IoT device, which continuously measures the current flowing through the sensors. If there is a power failure in any of the three phases, the current will drop to zero, and the IoT device will detect it.

Once the power failure is detected, the IoT device sends an SMS alert to the designated phone number using the GSM module. Additionally, the IoT device can also trigger the power relay to switch off the electrical equipment connected to the three-phase power supply.

#### Advantages

The IoT-based three-phase power failure monitoring system offers several advantages, including:

- Real-time monitoring of power failures
- Immediate notification of power failures through SMS alerts
- Automatic switch-off of electrical equipment to prevent damage
- Improved safety of expensive electrical equipment
- Reduced production losses due to power failures

#### Conclusion

In conclusion, the implementation of IoT in monitoring three-phase power failures and sending SMS alerts provides a reliable and efficient solution for preventing damage to electrical equipment and avoiding production losses. The IoT-based system offers real-time monitoring and notification of power failures, enabling quick action to be taken to prevent damage and losses.
