### Contactless IOT Doorbell

In this section, we will discuss the implementation of an IOT-based contactless doorbell system. With the advancement of technology, contactless systems have gained popularity due to the COVID-19 pandemic. This system eliminates the need for physical contact with the doorbell, thereby reducing the risk of infection. Let's dive into the details of this system:

#### Components:

The following are the components required for the implementation of this system:

- Raspberry Pi
- Ultrasound Sensor
- LED light
- Buzzer
- Power Supply
- Wi-Fi module

#### Working:

The working of this system involves the following steps:

1. The ultrasound sensor detects any movement in front of the door.
2. The Raspberry Pi receives the data from the sensor and triggers the LED light and buzzer to indicate the presence of a visitor.
3. The Raspberry Pi also sends notifications to the owner's mobile phone through the Wi-Fi module to inform them about the visitor.
4. The owner can then check the live feed from the camera and communicate with the visitor through the intercom system.
5. The door can be remotely unlocked by the owner through the mobile app.

#### Advantages:

The following are the advantages of the contactless IOT doorbell system:

- Reduces the risk of infection by eliminating physical contact.
- Provides enhanced security by allowing remote access to the door.
- Enables communication with the visitor through the intercom system.
- Provides live feed of the visitor through the camera.
- Allows remote unlocking of the door through the mobile app.

#### Limitations:

The following are the limitations of the contactless IOT doorbell system:

- Requires a stable internet connection for proper functioning.
- May not be suitable for areas with poor internet connectivity.
- The system may malfunction due to technical issues, leading to false notifications.

#### Conclusion:

In conclusion, the contactless IOT doorbell system is an innovative solution that provides enhanced security and reduces the risk of infection. This system can be implemented in residential as well as commercial areas. However, it is essential to consider the limitations of the system before implementing it.
