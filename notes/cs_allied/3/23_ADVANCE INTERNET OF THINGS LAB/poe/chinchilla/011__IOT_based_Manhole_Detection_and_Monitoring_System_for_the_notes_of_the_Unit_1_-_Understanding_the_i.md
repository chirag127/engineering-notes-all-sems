### IOT based Manhole Detection and Monitoring System

The Internet of Things (IoT) is revolutionizing the way we live and work. It enables us to connect and manage a wide range of devices and applications remotely. One such application is the IOT based Manhole Detection and Monitoring System. In this system, a network of sensors is installed in the manhole covers to detect the presence of water or any other material that can cause blockages in the sewage system. Here are some key features and benefits of this system:

- The system uses ultrasonic sensors, which are non-contact sensors that can detect the level of water or any other material in the manhole.
- The sensors send data to a central server over a wireless network, which can be accessed by authorized personnel using a web-based interface or mobile application.
- The system can send real-time alerts to the concerned authorities in case of any blockages or overflow in the manhole, enabling them to take immediate action to avoid any damage or health hazards.
- The system also provides historical data that can be used for predictive maintenance and planning for future upgrades and maintenance.
- The system can help in reducing the maintenance cost and time, as the authorities can monitor the manholes remotely and take action only when required, rather than inspecting them regularly.
- The system can also enhance the safety of the workers, as they can avoid entering the manholes for inspection and maintenance.

In conclusion, the IOT based Manhole Detection and Monitoring System is a highly efficient and cost-effective solution for monitoring and managing the sewage system. It can help in reducing the maintenance cost and time, improving the safety of the workers, and avoiding any damage or health hazards caused by blockages or overflow in the manholes.
