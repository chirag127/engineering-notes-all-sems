### IOT Contactless Covid Testing Booth Automation

In the wake of the COVID-19 pandemic, the need for contactless testing has become paramount. The IoT technology has enabled the development of contactless COVID testing booths. Here are some points that explain the implementation of IoT in contactless COVID testing booth automation:

1. **Sensors**: IoT sensors are used to detect the presence of a person, and the temperature of their body. These sensors can detect temperature, humidity, and other environmental conditions.

2. **RFID Tags**: IoT-based RFIDs are used to track the movement of people in the testing booth. These tags help in keeping a record of the time a person spends inside the booth.

3. **Automatic Doors**: The testing booth is equipped with automatic doors that open and close based on the movement of the person. This eliminates the need for physical contact with the door handle.

4. **QR Code**: A QR code is provided to the person for testing. They need to scan the code to get their test results.

5. **Automatic Sanitizer Dispenser**: The testing booth is equipped with an automatic sanitizer dispenser that dispenses sanitizer when a person enters and exits the booth.

6. **IoT-based Camera**: An IoT-based camera is installed inside the testing booth to monitor the process. The camera helps in keeping a record of the person's temperature and the time they spend inside the booth.

7. **Cloud-based Data Storage**: All the data collected from the sensors, RFID tags, and camera are stored in the cloud. This data can be accessed remotely by authorized personnel.

8. **Alerts**: If a person's temperature is above the normal range or if they test positive for COVID-19, an alert is sent to the concerned authorities.

9. **Remote Control**: The testing booth can be controlled remotely using an IoT-based application. This helps in monitoring the process and making any necessary changes.

In conclusion, the implementation of IoT in contactless COVID testing booths has revolutionized the testing process. It has made the process safer and more efficient.
