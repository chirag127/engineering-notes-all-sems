### IOT based Three Phase Power Failure Monitoring with SMS Alerts

In today's world, electricity has become a basic necessity of life. Any power outage can cause inconvenience or even losses for businesses. Therefore, it is essential to monitor power supply continuously to avoid any disruptions. Internet of Things (IoT) based Three Phase Power Failure Monitoring with SMS Alerts is a solution that provides real-time monitoring of power supply and alerts the user in case of any power failure. This technology can be used in various industries such as manufacturing, healthcare, and agriculture.

Here are some key points about IoT based Three Phase Power Failure Monitoring with SMS Alerts:

1. The system consists of sensors, microcontrollers, and a GSM module that work together to monitor power supply and send alerts.

2. The sensors are placed on the three-phase power supply lines to detect the presence or absence of power.

3. The microcontroller is responsible for collecting data from the sensors and sending it to the cloud platform.

4. The cloud platform stores the data and analyzes it to detect any power failure. If a power failure is detected, the system sends an alert to the user's mobile phone via SMS.

5. The user can also access the data in real-time through a web application or mobile app.

6. This technology can be integrated with other systems such as generators or UPS to ensure continuous power supply in case of any power failure.

7. IoT based Three Phase Power Failure Monitoring with SMS Alerts can help businesses to minimize downtime, reduce losses, and improve productivity.

In conclusion, IoT based Three Phase Power Failure Monitoring with SMS Alerts is a valuable solution for businesses that rely heavily on power supply. With real-time monitoring and alerts, users can quickly respond to any power failure and minimize the impact on their operations.
