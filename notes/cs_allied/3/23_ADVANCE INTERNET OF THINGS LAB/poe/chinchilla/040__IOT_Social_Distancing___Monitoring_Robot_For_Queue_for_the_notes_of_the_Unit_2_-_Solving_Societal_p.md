### IOT Social Distancing & Monitoring Robot For Queue

In the current scenario of the COVID-19 pandemic, maintaining social distancing has become a necessity to prevent the spread of the virus. To ensure the safety of people in public places, an IoT-based social distancing and monitoring robot for queues can be used. This robot can monitor the distance between people and alert them if they are violating the social distancing norms. Here are some of the features of this robot:

- The robot is equipped with sensors that can measure the distance between people and detect if they are maintaining the recommended distance.
- The robot can move around in the queue and monitor the distance between people.
- If the distance between two people is less than the recommended distance, the robot can alert them using a speaker or a display.
- The robot can also notify the authorities if a violation of social distancing norms is detected.
- The robot can be programmed to keep track of the number of people in the queue and provide an estimated waiting time to the people.
- The robot can also be used to distribute masks and sanitizers to the people in the queue.
- The robot can be controlled remotely using a smartphone or a web application.
- The robot can be equipped with a camera to monitor the queue and detect any suspicious activity.

The IoT-based social distancing and monitoring robot for queues can be used in various public places such as hospitals, government offices, banks, supermarkets, and other places where people gather in large numbers. This robot can help in maintaining social distancing and preventing the spread of the virus.
