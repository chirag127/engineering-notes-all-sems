### Contactless IOT Doorbell for the notes of the Unit 2 - Solving Societal problems with the help of IOT in the subject of ADVANCE INTERNET OF THINGS LAB

The Contactless IoT Doorbell is a device that is designed to provide a contactless solution for answering the door. The device is part of the Internet of Things (IoT) and uses wireless communication technologies, such as Wi-Fi or Bluetooth, to connect to a central monitoring system.

The Contactless IoT Doorbell typically consists of a doorbell button and a camera that are integrated into a single device. The doorbell button is used to initiate a doorbell ring, and the camera is used to provide a live video feed of the person at the door. The data from the doorbell and camera is transmitted to a central monitoring system, where it is processed and analyzed to provide real-time information about who is at the door.

The central monitoring system is typically connected to a mobile device, such as a smartphone or tablet, which can be used to view the live video feed and to remotely answer the door. This allows the homeowner to answer the door without having to physically be at the door, and provides a contactless solution for answering the door in the context of the COVID-19 pandemic and other health and safety concerns.

This may include understanding the components and architecture of the system, as well as the data processing and analysis techniques that are used to extract insights from the data collected by the system.
