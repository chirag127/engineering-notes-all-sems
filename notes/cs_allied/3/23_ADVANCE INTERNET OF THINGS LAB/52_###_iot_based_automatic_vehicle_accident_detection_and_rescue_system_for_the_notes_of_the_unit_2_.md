### IOT Based Automatic Vehicle Accident Detection and Rescue System for the notes of the Unit 2 - Solving Societal problems with the help of IOT in the subject of ADVANCE INTERNET OF THINGS LAB

The IoT Based Automatic Vehicle Accident Detection and Rescue System is a system designed to detect and respond to vehicle accidents in real-time. The system is part of the Internet of Things (IoT) and uses a combination of sensors, wireless communication technologies, and data analytics to monitor and respond to accidents.

The IoT Based Automatic Vehicle Accident Detection and Rescue System typically includes sensors, such as accelerometers and GPS, that are integrated into a vehicle. The sensors are used to detect the occurrence of an accident, such as a sudden change in acceleration or a collision, and to transmit data about the accident to a central monitoring system.

The central monitoring system is used to process and analyze the data collected by the sensors, and to generate real-time alerts that are sent to relevant authorities and stakeholders, such as emergency responders, local residents, and businesses. The alerts may include information about the location and severity of the accident, as well as instructions for responding to the emergency.
