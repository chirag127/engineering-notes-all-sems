### IOT Water Pollution Monitor RC Boat

- An IOT water pollution monitor RC boat is a remote-controlled device that can measure and transmit water quality parameters to an online server using internet of things (IOT) technology  .
- The main components of the device are:
  - A boat chassis with a motorized propeller system and a battery pack.
  - An Arduino microcontroller board that controls the boat movement and the sensors.
  - A wireless module that connects the Arduino to the internet via Wi-Fi or cellular network.
  - A GPS module that provides the location of the boat.
  - Various sensors that measure water quality parameters such as pH, temperature, turbidity, dissolved oxygen, etc.
  - An LCD display that shows the sensor readings and the boat status.
  - An RC remote that allows the user to steer the boat and switch between manual and automatic modes.
- The device can be used to monitor water pollution in various scenarios such as lakes, rivers, ponds, reservoirs, etc. It can help to identify the sources and causes of water pollution, and to take appropriate actions to prevent or reduce it.
- The device can also be integrated with a web or mobile application that displays the sensor data in real-time, and provides graphical and statistical analysis of the water quality trends. The application can also alert the user or the authorities in case of any abnormal or critical situations .
- The device can be further improved by adding more sensors, increasing the battery life, enhancing the wireless communication, and implementing machine learning algorithms to detect and classify water pollution patterns .
