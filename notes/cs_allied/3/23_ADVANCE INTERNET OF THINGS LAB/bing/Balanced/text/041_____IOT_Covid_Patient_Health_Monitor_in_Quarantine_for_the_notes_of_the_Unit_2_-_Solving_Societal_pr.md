### IOT Covid Patient Health Monitor in Quarantine

- IOT Covid Patient Health Monitor in Quarantine is a system that uses internet of things (IOT) devices to measure and transmit the vital signs of covid-19 patients who are isolated at home or in a facility.
- The system aims to reduce the risk of infection for health workers and other patients, as well as to provide real-time data for remote monitoring and intervention by medical professionals.
- The system consists of three main components: sensors, gateway, and cloud.
  - Sensors are wearable or non-invasive devices that measure the body temperature, pulse rate, blood pressure, and oxygen saturation of the patients. Examples of sensors are thermometers, pulse oximeters, blood pressure cuffs, and smart watches.
  - Gateway is a device that collects the data from the sensors and sends it to the cloud via Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, or cellular network. Examples of gateway devices are smartphones, tablets, or laptops.
  - Cloud is a platform that stores, processes, and analyzes the data from the gateway devices. It also provides a user interface for the medical staff and the patients to access and visualize the data. Examples of cloud platforms are Google Cloud, Amazon Web Services, or Microsoft Azure.
- The system can provide several benefits for the covid-19 pandemic response, such as:
  - Improving the quality of care and the outcomes of the patients by enabling timely detection and intervention of critical conditions.
  - Reducing the burden and the exposure of the health workers by minimizing the need for physical visits and contact with the patients.
  - Optimizing the use of the health resources and facilities by prioritizing the patients who need more attention and care.
  - Enhancing the compliance and the satisfaction of the patients by providing them with feedback and guidance on their health status and self-care.
