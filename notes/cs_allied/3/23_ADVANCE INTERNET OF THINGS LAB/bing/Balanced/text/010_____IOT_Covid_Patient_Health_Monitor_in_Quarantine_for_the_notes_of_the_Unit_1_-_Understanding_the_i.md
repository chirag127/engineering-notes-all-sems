### IOT Covid Patient Health Monitor in Quarantine

- IOT Covid Patient Health Monitor in Quarantine is a system that uses internet of things (IOT) devices to measure and transmit the vital signs of covid-19 patients who are isolated at home or in a facility.
- The system aims to reduce the risk of infection for health workers and other patients, as well as to provide real-time data for remote monitoring and intervention by medical professionals.
- The system consists of three main components: sensors, gateway, and cloud.
  - Sensors are wearable or non-invasive devices that measure the body temperature, pulse rate, blood pressure, and oxygen saturation of the patients. Examples of sensors are thermometers, pulse oximeters, blood pressure monitors, and smart watches.
  - Gateway is a device that collects the data from the sensors and sends it to the cloud via a wireless network. Examples of gateway are smartphones, tablets, or laptops.
  - Cloud is a platform that stores, processes, and analyzes the data from the gateway and provides a dashboard for the medical staff to access and visualize the data. Examples of cloud are web servers, databases, or cloud services.
- The system can provide several benefits for the covid-19 patients and the health care system, such as:
  - Improving the quality of care by enabling timely detection of abnormal signs and symptoms, and providing alerts and recommendations for the patients and the medical staff.
  - Reducing the cost of care by avoiding unnecessary hospitalizations, visits, and tests, and optimizing the use of resources and personnel.
  - Enhancing the patient satisfaction and compliance by providing convenience, comfort, and privacy for the patients, and reducing the anxiety and stress caused by the pandemic.
  - Supporting the public health response by providing data for epidemiological studies, contact tracing, and policy making.
