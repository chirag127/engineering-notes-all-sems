### IOT Circuit Breaker Project

- The IOT Circuit Breaker Project is a system that provides a password-based circuit breaker system using IOT .
- The system aims to solve the problem of fatal accidents that happen with line men due to electric shocks, which are a result of miscoordination or miscommunication between line men and substations .
- The system uses a wifi module paired with Atmega328p microcontroller locally to connect to the internet .
- The system allows the user to control the electrical loads remotely through a web interface, where the user can enter the password and switch on or off the circuit breaker .
- The system also sends the status of the circuit breaker to the user via email or SMS, and alerts the user if there is any unauthorized access or tampering with the circuit breaker .
- The system can be implemented for industrial and commercial customers, where wireless solutions can offer best-in-class RF performance and extend wireless connectivity across harsh environments.
- The system can also be integrated with other IoT devices and sensors, such as smart water monitoring, smart irrigation system, smart building, home automation, etc. to create a smart and efficient environment.
