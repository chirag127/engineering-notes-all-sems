### IOT based Smart Energy Meter Monitoring with Theft Detection

- IOT based Smart Energy Meter Monitoring with Theft Detection is a system that uses Internet of Things (IoT) technology to monitor the energy consumption and detect the power theft in real time     .
- The system consists of smart energy meters installed at the consumer end and the distribution end, which can communicate with each other and a central server through wireless or wired network    .
- The smart energy meters can measure various parameters such as voltage, current, power, energy, power factor, etc. and send them to the server periodically or on demand    .
- The server can store, process, analyze and display the data received from the smart energy meters using various techniques such as machine learning, statistical regression, multimodel forecasting, etc. to detect any abnormality or discrepancy in the energy consumption or generation  .
- The server can also send alerts or notifications to the concerned authorities or consumers in case of any power theft or malfunctioning of the smart energy meters    .
- The system can also provide various features such as remote control, billing, tariff, load management, demand response, etc. to improve the efficiency and reliability of the power distribution system   .
- The system can reduce the energy loss, wastage and theft, and increase the revenue, customer satisfaction and environmental sustainability     .
