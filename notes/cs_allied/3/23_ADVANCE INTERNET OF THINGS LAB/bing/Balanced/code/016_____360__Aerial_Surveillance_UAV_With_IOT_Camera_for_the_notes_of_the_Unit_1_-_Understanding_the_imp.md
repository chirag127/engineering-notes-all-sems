### 360° Aerial Surveillance UAV With IOT Camera

- Aerial surveillance is the key to security and military based operations. It provides real time information on enemy movements which plays a key role in precision strikes  .
- Aerial surveillance can be performed by using unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) or drones, which are remotely controlled or autonomous aircraft that can carry cameras, sensors, and other payloads .
- A 360° aerial surveillance UAV with IOT camera is a drone that can capture and stream 360-degree video from a spherical camera mounted on the drone   .
- IOT stands for Internet of Things, which is a network of physical devices, sensors, and software that can communicate and exchange data over the internet .
- A 360° aerial surveillance UAV with IOT camera can provide a panoramic view of the surroundings, which can be useful for monitoring large areas, detecting threats, and enhancing situational awareness   .
- A 360° aerial surveillance UAV with IOT camera can also be integrated with other IOT devices and platforms, such as cloud computing, edge computing, and artificial intelligence, to enable data processing, storage, and analysis .
- A 360° aerial surveillance UAV with IOT camera can be used for various applications, such as military operations, border security, disaster management, crowd surveillance, and environmental monitoring    .
- A 360° aerial surveillance UAV with IOT camera can be designed and implemented by using various components, such as a drone frame, a flight controller, a battery, a motor, a propeller, a transmitter, a receiver, a spherical camera, and an IOT module  .
- A 360° aerial surveillance UAV with IOT camera can be controlled and monitored by using a smartphone, a tablet, or a computer, which can receive and display the 360-degree video stream from the drone  .
- A 360° aerial surveillance UAV with IOT camera can face some challenges, such as limited battery life, wireless interference, privacy and security issues, and regulatory and ethical concerns  .
