### Contactless IOT Doorbell

- A contactless IOT doorbell is a device that allows the user to ring the doorbell without touching it physically, using a gesture or a voice command.
- The device consists of the following components:
  - A microcontroller board, such as Arduino or Raspberry Pi, that acts as the brain of the device and controls the input and output signals.
  - A sensor, such as an ultrasonic sensor or a microphone, that detects the presence or the sound of the user and triggers the doorbell.
  - A speaker or a buzzer, that produces the sound of the doorbell when activated by the sensor.
  - A wireless module, such as Wi-Fi or Bluetooth, that connects the device to the internet and allows the user to receive notifications on their smartphone or other devices when someone rings the doorbell.
  - A power source, such as a battery or a solar panel, that supplies the required voltage and current to the device.
- The device works as follows:
  - The sensor continuously monitors the environment for any gesture or sound from the user.
  - When the sensor detects a valid gesture or sound, it sends a signal to the microcontroller board, which then activates the speaker or the buzzer to produce the sound of the doorbell.
  - The microcontroller board also sends a signal to the wireless module, which then communicates with the internet and sends a notification to the user's smartphone or other devices, informing them that someone has rung the doorbell.
  - The user can then view the notification and decide whether to answer the door or not.
- The benefits of a contactless IOT doorbell are:
  - It reduces the risk of spreading germs or viruses by avoiding physical contact with the doorbell.
  - It enhances the convenience and accessibility of the user, as they can ring the doorbell with a simple gesture or a voice command, without having to reach for the doorbell or press any button.
  - It improves the security and privacy of the user, as they can remotely monitor who is at the door and decide whether to open it or not, without revealing their presence or identity.
  - It enables the user to customize the sound and the notification of the doorbell, according to their preferences and needs.
