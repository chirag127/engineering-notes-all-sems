# IOT Mining Tracking & Worker Safety Helmet

- IOT or the internet of things is a technology that enables us to control hardware devices through the internet.
- Mining is one of the most dangerous jobs in the world, as miners face various hazards such as gas, fire, explosion, collapse, etc.
- IOT Mining Tracking & Worker Safety Helmet is a system that aims to improve the safety and efficiency of miners by using sensors, microcontrollers, and wireless communication   .
- The system consists of two main components: the helmet node and the tracker node   .
- The helmet node is worn by the miner and contains sensors to measure temperature, humidity, gas, etc. It also has an RF transmitter to send the sensor data and the miner's ID to the tracker node   .
- The tracker node is installed at the mining site and receives the data from the helmet nodes. It also has an RF receiver to locate the position of the miners. It uses an ATmega microcontroller to process the data and send it to a web server over the internet   .
- The web server displays the data on a dashboard that can be accessed by the mining authorities or the rescue team. The dashboard shows the real-time status and location of the miners, as well as the environmental conditions of the mining site   .
- The system can alert the authorities or the rescue team in case of any emergency or abnormal situation, such as high temperature, low oxygen, gas leakage, fire, etc   .
- The system is a cost-effective, practical, eco-friendly, and reliable way to protect the workers working in the mining field .
