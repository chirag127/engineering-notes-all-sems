### IOT Prison Break Monitoring & Alerting System

- The system is designed to prevent and detect prison breaks by tracking the location and activities of the inmates using radio frequency (RF) technology and Internet of Things (IoT) platform.
- The system consists of the following components:
  - RF trackers: These are small devices attached to each inmate that transmit a unique code wirelessly to the central monitoring unit. The RF trackers can also detect the movement and proximity of the inmates.
  - Central monitoring unit: This is a microcontroller-based circuit that receives the signals from the RF trackers and scans through the data of all the inmates. The central monitoring unit can also send commands to the RF trackers to activate alarms or shocks if needed.
  - IoT portal: This is an online platform that displays the status and location of each inmate on a map and alerts the authorities in case of any prison break or anomaly. The IoT portal can also provide historical data and analytics of the inmates' behavior and patterns.
- The system works as follows:
  - The RF trackers continuously send their codes and location data to the central monitoring unit via RF signals.
  - The central monitoring unit compares the received data with the predefined data of the inmates and their validated locations. If the data matches, the system continues to monitor the inmates. If the data does not match, the system triggers an alert and sends the details of the inmate and the location to the IoT portal.
  - The IoT portal receives the alert and displays the information on the map and the dashboard. The IoT portal also sounds an alarm and notifies the authorities via email or SMS. The authorities can then take appropriate action to stop the prison break and capture the inmate.
- The system has the following advantages:
  - It enhances the security and safety of the prison and the society by preventing and detecting prison breaks in real time.
  - It reduces the cost and manpower required for manual monitoring and surveillance of the inmates.
  - It provides accurate and reliable data and analytics of the inmates' location and activities that can help in understanding and improving their rehabilitation and reintegration.
