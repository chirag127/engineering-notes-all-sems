### IOT based Manhole Detection and Monitoring System

- A drainage monitoring system plays a significant role in keeping towns and cities healthy and clean.
- Most of the manholes are open without any observation that cause accidents. In India, many cities adopted emptying underground system because it is vital.
- Manhole monitoring is also important to prevent contamination of fresh water due to problems in sewage drainage system and urban floods due to water blockage.
- The proposed system is a low cost, low maintenance, IoT based real time system that alerts the managing station through message when any manhole crosses its threshold values.
- The system consists of sensors, Arduino, GSM and GPS modules, and a cloud server  .
- The sensors are used to detect the water level, gas level, and manhole cover status in the manhole  .
- The Arduino is used to process the sensor data and send it to the cloud server via GSM and GPS modules  .
- The cloud server is used to store and analyze the data and send alerts to the authorities and the public via SMS or email  .
- The system can also display the location and status of the manholes on a web or mobile application.
- The system can reduce the death risk of manual scavengers who clean the underground drainage and also benefit the public. It can also improve the manhole management and maintenance in smart cities .
