### Weather Imaging CubeSat with Telemetry Transmission

- A CubeSat is a type of miniaturized satellite that has a standard size of 10x10x10 cm and a mass of up to 1.33 kg. CubeSats can be deployed in low Earth orbit for various applications, such as communication, GPS, remote sensing, and space weather monitoring  .
- A weather imaging CubeSat is a CubeSat that carries a camera or a radiometer to capture images or measurements of the Earth's atmosphere, clouds, and precipitation. The data can be used for weather prediction and forecasting systems  .
- A telemetry transmission CubeSat is a CubeSat that transmits the data collected by the weather imaging CubeSat to a ground station or a relay satellite. The transmission can be done using radio frequency, optical, or laser communication .
- A weather imaging CubeSat with telemetry transmission is a CubeSat that combines both functions of weather imaging and data transmission. It can provide real-time or near-real-time information about the weather conditions and phenomena on Earth .
- Some examples of weather imaging CubeSats with telemetry transmission are:
  - CubIXSS: A CubeSat that measures the solar irradiance and the ionospheric electron density using a miniature X-ray spectrometer and a radio beacon.
  - SunCET: A CubeSat that monitors the coronal mass ejections and the solar energetic particles using a coronagraph and a particle detector.
  - TEMPEST-D: A CubeSat that observes the temporal evolution of clouds and precipitation using a five-frequency millimeter-wave imaging radiometer.
