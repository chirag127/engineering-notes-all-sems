### IOT based Manhole Detection and Monitoring System

- IOT based Manhole Detection and Monitoring System is a project that aims to prevent accidents and contamination caused by open or blocked manholes in urban areas    .
- The system uses sensors such as water level sensor, gas sensor, ultrasonic sensor, and temperature sensor to detect the status of the manholes and send alerts to the authorities via GSM and GPS modules   .
- The system also uses a camera to capture the images of the manholes and upload them to a cloud server for further analysis and visualization .
- The system is low cost, low maintenance, and real time, and can improve the health and safety of the public and the manual scavengers who clean the underground drainage  .
- The system is an example of how IOT can be used to solve societal problems by creating a smart and efficient drainage monitoring system    .
