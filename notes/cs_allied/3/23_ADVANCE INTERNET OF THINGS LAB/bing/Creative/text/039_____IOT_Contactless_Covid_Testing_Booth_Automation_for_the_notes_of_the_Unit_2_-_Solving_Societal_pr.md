### IOT Contactless Covid Testing Booth Automation

- IOT contactless covid testing booth automation is a system that aims to reduce the risk of virus transmission and human errors in the covid testing process.
- The system uses RFID technology to monitor the person's details, such as name, contact number, and address, and store them in a database.
- The system also uses a microcontroller to control the testing booth, which consists of a swab collection mechanism, a sample container, and a disinfection system.
- The system can perform the swab collection automatically, without the need for human intervention, and send the sample to the laboratory for testing.
- The system can also disinfect the booth after each test, using UV light or spray, to ensure hygiene and safety.
- The system can communicate with the person and the laboratory using GSM modem, which can send SMS messages or voice calls.
- The system can also use MATLAB to analyze the test results and generate reports, which can be accessed through a web portal or a mobile app.
- The system can provide instant and accurate covid testing, and reduce the workload and exposure of the health workers.
