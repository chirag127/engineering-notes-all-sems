### IOT Water Pollution Monitor RC Boat

- IOT Water Pollution Monitor RC Boat is a project that aims to measure and transmit water quality data using an RC boat equipped with sensors and an IOT module  .
- The project involves the following steps:
  - Designing and assembling the RC boat with a motorized propeller system, a battery, an Arduino board, an ESP8266 Wi-Fi module, a pH sensor, a turbidity sensor, a temperature sensor, and an LCD display  .
  - Programming the Arduino board to read the sensor values, display them on the LCD, and send them to an IOT server using the ESP8266 module  .
  - Creating an IOT server using a platform such as ThingSpeak or Blynk to receive, store, and visualize the water quality data from the RC boat  .
  - Testing the RC boat in a water body and controlling it with a remote to collect and transmit the water quality data to the IOT server  .
- The project can help to monitor the water pollution levels in real-time and alert the authorities or the public about the water quality conditions  .
- The project can also be extended to include other sensors such as dissolved oxygen, conductivity, or salinity, and to use a GPS module to track the location of the RC boat .
