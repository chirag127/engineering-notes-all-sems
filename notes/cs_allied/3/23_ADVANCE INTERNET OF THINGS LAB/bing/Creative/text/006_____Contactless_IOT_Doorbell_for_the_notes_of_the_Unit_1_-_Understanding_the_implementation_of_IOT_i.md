### Contactless IOT Doorbell

- A contactless IOT doorbell is a device that allows visitors to ring a bell without touching it, and notifies the owner of the house through a smartphone app or a web interface.
- The device uses a proximity sensor, such as an ultrasonic sensor or an infrared sensor, to detect the presence of a visitor near the doorbell.
- The device also uses a microcontroller, such as an Arduino or a Raspberry Pi, to process the sensor data and send a signal to the cloud platform, such as AWS or Firebase, via Wi-Fi or Bluetooth.
- The cloud platform then sends a notification to the owner's smartphone or web browser, along with a snapshot or a video of the visitor, captured by a camera attached to the device.
- The owner can then view the visitor's image or video, and decide whether to answer the door or not, or send a message to the visitor through the app or the web interface.
- The device can also be integrated with other smart home devices, such as smart locks, smart lights, or smart speakers, to enable more features and functionalities, such as unlocking the door remotely, turning on the lights, or playing a greeting message.
