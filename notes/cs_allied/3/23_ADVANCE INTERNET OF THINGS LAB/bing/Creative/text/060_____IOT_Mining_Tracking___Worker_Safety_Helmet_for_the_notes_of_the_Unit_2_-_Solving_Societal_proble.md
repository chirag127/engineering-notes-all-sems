### IOT Mining Tracking & Worker Safety Helmet

- IOT Mining Tracking & Worker Safety Helmet is a system that aims to improve the safety and efficiency of workers in the mining industry by using the Internet of Things (IoT) technology .
- The system consists of two main components: a smart helmet and a tracker circuit .
- The smart helmet is worn by the workers and is equipped with sensors that monitor the environmental conditions such as temperature, humidity, gas, etc. and the health status of the workers such as heart rate, blood pressure, etc. .
- The smart helmet also has a radio frequency (RF) module that transmits the sensor data and the worker's identity to the tracker circuit .
- The tracker circuit is installed at the mining site and is connected to the Internet. It receives the data from the smart helmets and sends it to a cloud server for further analysis and visualization .
- The tracker circuit also helps to locate the workers in the mining site by using the RF signals from the smart helmets .
- The system allows the mining managers and supervisors to monitor the workers' safety and productivity in real time and to take appropriate actions in case of any emergency or accident .
- The system also provides alerts and notifications to the workers and the managers in case of any hazardous events or situations .
- The system is a cost-effective, practical, eco-friendly, and reliable way to protect the workers working in the mining field .
