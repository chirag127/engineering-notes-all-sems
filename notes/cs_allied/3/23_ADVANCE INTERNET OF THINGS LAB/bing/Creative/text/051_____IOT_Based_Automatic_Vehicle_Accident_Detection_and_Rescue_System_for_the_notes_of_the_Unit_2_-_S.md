### IOT Based Automatic Vehicle Accident Detection and Rescue System

- An IOT based automatic vehicle accident detection and rescue system is a system that detects accidents and communicates information to rescue teams via SMS, web applications, or Android mobile applications  .
- The system uses sensors such as vibration sensors, GPS modules, GSM modules, and microcontrollers to detect the occurrence, location, and severity of accidents  .
- The system can also classify the type of accident based on the sensor data and use sensor fusion techniques to improve the accuracy and reliability of the detection.
- The system can reduce the response time and save lives by alerting the rescue teams and providing them with the necessary information to reach the accident site and provide medical assistance   .
- The system can also prevent theft and vandalism of the vehicle by sending alerts to the owner and the police.
- The system is an example of how IOT can solve societal problems by enhancing road safety and reducing fatalities in smart cities .
