### IOT Covid Patient Health Monitor in Quarantine

- IOT Covid Patient Health Monitor in Quarantine is a system that uses internet of things (IOT) devices and sensors to measure and transmit the vital signs of covid-19 patients who are isolated at home or in designated facilities.
- The system aims to reduce the risk of infection for health workers and other patients, as well as to provide real-time data and alerts for timely intervention and treatment.
- The system consists of the following components     :
  - A wearable device that is attached to the patient's body and measures the body temperature, pulse rate, and oxygen saturation level using a temperature sensor, a pulse sensor, and an oximeter sensor respectively.
  - A microcontroller that processes the sensor data and sends it to a cloud server using a wireless communication module such as Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, or cellular network.
  - A cloud server that stores and analyzes the sensor data and provides a web interface for accessing and visualizing the data by authorized users such as doctors, nurses, and family members.
  - A mobile application that allows the users to monitor the patient's condition and receive notifications and alerts in case of any abnormality or emergency.
- The system has the following advantages     :
  - It enables remote and continuous monitoring of multiple covid-19 patients without the need for frequent visits or physical contact by health workers.
  - It reduces the burden on the health infrastructure and the demand for hospital beds and medical equipment by allowing the patients to stay at home or in less crowded facilities.
  - It improves the quality of care and the outcomes of the patients by providing accurate and timely information and feedback to the health workers and the patients themselves.
  - It facilitates the detection and prevention of complications and deterioration of the patient's condition by alerting the users of any abnormal or critical values of the vital signs.
  - It supports the research and development of new treatments and vaccines by providing large-scale and reliable data on the covid-19 patients and their response to different interventions.
