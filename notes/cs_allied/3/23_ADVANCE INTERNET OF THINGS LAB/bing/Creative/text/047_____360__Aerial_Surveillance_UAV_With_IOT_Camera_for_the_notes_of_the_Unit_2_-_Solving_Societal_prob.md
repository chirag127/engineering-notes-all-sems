### 360° Aerial Surveillance UAV With IOT Camera

- Aerial surveillance is the key to security and military based operations. It provides real time information on enemy movements which plays a key role in precision strikes  .
- Aerial surveillance can also be used for crowd monitoring, disaster management, wildlife conservation, and other applications that require a wide-angle view of the ground.
- A 360° aerial surveillance UAV is a drone that can capture and stream a spherical video of its surroundings using a 360-degree camera mounted on it   .
- A 360-degree camera is a device that can capture images or videos from all directions simultaneously using multiple lenses and sensors.
- An IOT camera is a camera that can connect to the internet and transmit data to a cloud server or a remote device  .
- A 360° aerial surveillance UAV with IOT camera can provide a comprehensive and immersive view of the ground situation to the users, who can access the video feed from any device with internet connection  .
- A 360° aerial surveillance UAV with IOT camera can also perform image processing and analysis on the cloud, such as face recognition, object detection, and anomaly detection.
- A 360° aerial surveillance UAV with IOT camera can be controlled remotely using a smartphone app or a web interface, or can operate autonomously using GPS and sensors  .
- A 360° aerial surveillance UAV with IOT camera can be deployed in various scenarios, such as border security, urban surveillance, event management, and environmental monitoring   .
- A 360° aerial surveillance UAV with IOT camera can offer several advantages, such as low cost, high mobility, wide coverage, and real-time feedback   .
- A 360° aerial surveillance UAV with IOT camera can also face some challenges, such as battery life, network connectivity, data security, and privacy issues   .
