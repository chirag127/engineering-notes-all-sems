  - A microcontroller unit (such as Arduino) that controls the boat movement and the sensors

  - A wireless module (such as Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, GSM, etc.) that sends the sensor data to an online server or a mobile app
  - A power supply unit (such as battery, solar panel, etc.) that provides the required voltage and current to the device
