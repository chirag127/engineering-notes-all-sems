### IOT Garbage Segregator & Bin Level Indicator

- IOT Garbage Segregator & Bin Level Indicator is a system that uses sensors, microcontrollers and internet connectivity to automate the process of garbage segregation and level monitoring.
- The system consists of multiple smart dustbins that are placed at different locations in a city or a campus. Each dustbin has a sensor that detects the type of waste (dry or wet) and a sensor that measures the level of garbage in the bin.
- The dustbins are connected to a microcontroller that sends the data to an IOT platform over the internet. The IOT platform displays the bin level data and the waste type data on a web dashboard or a mobile app.
- The system can alert the authorities or the waste management service when the bins are full or when a specific type of waste needs to be collected. This can help in optimizing the waste collection routes and reducing the fuel consumption and greenhouse gas emissions.
- The system can also help in improving the hygiene and sanitation of the city or the campus by preventing the overflow of garbage and the spread of diseases. It can also encourage the citizens or the students to segregate their waste properly and reduce the environmental impact of waste disposal.
