### IOT Social Distancing & Monitoring Robot For Queue

- The aim of this project is to design a robot that can monitor and enforce social distancing in queues, such as in banks, malls, schools, etc.   
- The robot uses a 4-wheel drive system and a line following principle to move along with the queue and detect the distance between individuals using ultrasonic sensors.  
- The robot also has a camera and a speaker to capture and display the images of the violators and warn them to maintain a safe distance.   
- The robot is connected to the internet using a Wi-Fi module and can send the data to a cloud server for further analysis and visualization.   
- The robot can also alert the authorities or the management of the premises in case of repeated or serious violations.  
- The robot can help prevent the spread of Covid-19 and other infectious diseases by ensuring that people follow the social distancing guidelines and reduce the risk of exposure.
