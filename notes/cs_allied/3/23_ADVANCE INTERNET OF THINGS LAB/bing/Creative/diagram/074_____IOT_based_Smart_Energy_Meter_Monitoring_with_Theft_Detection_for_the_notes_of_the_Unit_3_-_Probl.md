### IOT based Smart Energy Meter Monitoring with Theft Detection

- IOT based Smart Energy Meter Monitoring with Theft Detection is a system that aims to reduce the energy crisis and the power theft by using smart meters and Internet of Things (IoT) technology.
- The system consists of smart meters installed at the consumer end and the distribution end, which can measure and transmit the energy consumption data to a cloud server via wireless communication.
- The cloud server can analyze the data and detect any abnormality or discrepancy between the consumer and the distribution end data, which may indicate a power theft or a faulty meter.
- The system can also monitor the real-time energy consumption and provide feedback to the consumers and the utility providers through a web or mobile application, which can help them to optimize their energy usage and billing.
- The system can also alert the authorities in case of any power theft or meter tampering, and provide the location and the identity of the culprit.
- The system can improve the efficiency and reliability of the power distribution network, and reduce the losses and costs due to power theft and wastage.
