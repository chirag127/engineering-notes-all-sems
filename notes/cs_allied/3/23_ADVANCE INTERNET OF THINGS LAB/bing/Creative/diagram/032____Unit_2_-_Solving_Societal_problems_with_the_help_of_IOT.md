## Unit 2 - Solving Societal problems with the help of IOT

- IOT stands for Internet of Things, which refers to the network of physical devices, sensors, actuators, and software that can collect, process, and exchange data over the internet.
- IOT can help solve various societal problems by enabling smart solutions that can improve efficiency, safety, convenience, and quality of life for people and communities.
- Some examples of societal problems that can be solved with the help of IOT are:

  - **Smart cities**: IOT can help create smart cities that can optimize urban planning, transportation, energy, water, waste management, public safety, and governance. For instance, IOT can enable smart traffic lights that can adjust to real-time traffic conditions, smart parking systems that can guide drivers to available spaces, smart grids that can balance energy supply and demand, smart water meters that can detect leaks and conserve water, smart waste bins that can notify when they are full, smart cameras that can monitor crime and emergencies, and smart platforms that can facilitate citizen participation and feedback.
  - **Smart health**: IOT can help improve health and well-being by enabling smart health devices and systems that can monitor, diagnose, treat, and prevent diseases and disorders. For example, IOT can enable smart wearables that can track vital signs, activity, and sleep, smart implants that can deliver drugs or stimulate nerves, smart sensors that can detect falls or seizures, smart pills that can transmit data from inside the body, smart telemedicine that can connect patients and doctors remotely, and smart hospitals that can optimize patient care and workflow.
  - **Smart agriculture**: IOT can help enhance agricultural productivity and sustainability by enabling smart farming devices and systems that can monitor, control, and optimize crop and livestock production. For instance, IOT can enable smart irrigation that can adjust to soil moisture and weather conditions, smart pest control that can detect and repel pests, smart fertilization that can deliver nutrients based on plant needs, smart harvesting that can automate and optimize crop collection, smart livestock tracking that can monitor animal health and location, and smart food traceability that can ensure food safety and quality.
  - **Smart environment**: IOT can help protect and conserve the environment by enabling smart environmental devices and systems that can monitor, analyze, and mitigate environmental issues. For example, IOT can enable smart air quality sensors that can measure and report air pollution levels, smart weather stations that can forecast and warn about extreme weather events, smart fire detectors that can alert and respond to wildfires, smart flood sensors that can measure and prevent water damage, smart wildlife trackers that can protect endangered species, and smart energy efficiency devices that can reduce greenhouse gas emissions and save costs.
