### IOT Covid Patient Health Monitor in Quarantine

- **Problem statement**: With the increasing number of COVID-19 cases, it is becoming difficult to keep a track on the health conditions of many quarantined patients. Doctors need to regularly monitor patient health, but there are increasing number of patients for the doctors to monitor, and the doctors are at risk of infection just for monitoring purpose.
- **Proposed solution**: To solve this problem, an IOT-based patient health monitor in quarantine can be designed and implemented, which allows for remotely monitoring of multiple covid patients over the internet. The system monitors patient heartbeat, temperature and blood pressure using a heartbeat sensor, temperature sensor and BP Sensor respectively. The data is then sent to a cloud platform, where it can be accessed by the doctors and medical staff through a web or mobile application. The system can also alert the doctors if any of the parameters are abnormal or critical.
- **Design steps**:
  - **Step 1**: Identify the hardware and software components required for the system, such as sensors, microcontroller, Wi-Fi module, cloud platform, web or mobile application, etc.
  - **Step 2**: Connect the sensors to the microcontroller and the Wi-Fi module, and program the microcontroller to read the sensor data and send it to the cloud platform using Wi-Fi.
  - **Step 3**: Create an account on a cloud platform, such as ThingSpeak, Firebase, AWS, etc., and configure the channel or database to receive and store the sensor data from the Wi-Fi module.
  - **Step 4**: Develop a web or mobile application that can access the cloud platform and display the sensor data in a graphical or tabular format. The application should also have features such as login, registration, patient selection, notification, etc.
  - **Step 5**: Test the system by attaching the sensors to a patient and checking the data transmission and display on the cloud platform and the application. Verify the accuracy and reliability of the system and make any necessary adjustments or improvements.
- **Advantages**:
  - The system can reduce the workload and risk of infection for the doctors and medical staff, as they can monitor multiple patients remotely and efficiently.
  - The system can provide real-time and continuous health monitoring for the patients, and alert the doctors in case of any emergency or abnormality.
  - The system can give relief to the overcrowded health infrastructure and prevent the medical practitioner from unnecessary exposure.
  - The system can also help the patients to measure health parameters on their own and keep track of their recovery progress.
- **Challenges**:
  - The system may face issues such as network connectivity, data security, sensor accuracy, power consumption, etc., which need to be addressed and resolved.
  - The system may not be able to replace the physical examination and diagnosis of the doctors, and may require human intervention and supervision in some cases.
  - The system may not be affordable or accessible for all the patients, especially in rural or remote areas, and may require proper training and guidance for the users.
