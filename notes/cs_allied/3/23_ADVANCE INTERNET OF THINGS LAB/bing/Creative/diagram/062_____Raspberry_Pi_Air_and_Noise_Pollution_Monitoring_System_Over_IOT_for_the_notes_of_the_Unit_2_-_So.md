### Raspberry Pi Air and Noise Pollution Monitoring System Over IOT

- This system is an IOT-based method that uses Raspberry Pi to monitor and check the air quality index and the sound pollution of a region  .
- The system consists of two main modules: the air quality index monitoring module and the sound intensity detection module  .
- The air quality index monitoring module uses sensors to measure the levels of carbon dioxide, methane, and dust particles in the air .
- The sound intensity detection module uses a microphone to measure the noise level in decibels .
- The data collected from the sensors is continuously fed to the Raspberry Pi controller, which processes and displays it on an LCD screen .
- The Raspberry Pi also sends the data to a cloud server using Wi-Fi communication, where it can be accessed and analyzed by the users .
- The system can alert the users of any anomalies or violations of the air quality and noise standards using email or SMS notifications.
- The system can help in solving the societal problems of air and noise pollution by providing real-time and accurate data, raising awareness, and enabling preventive actions .
