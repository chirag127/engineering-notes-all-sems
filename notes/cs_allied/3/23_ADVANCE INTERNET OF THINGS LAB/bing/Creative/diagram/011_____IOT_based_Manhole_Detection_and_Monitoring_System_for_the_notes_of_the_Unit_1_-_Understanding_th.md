### IOT based Manhole Detection and Monitoring System

- IOT based Manhole Detection and Monitoring System is a project that aims to improve the safety and efficiency of the drainage system in urban areas by using sensors, GSM, GPS and cloud computing    .
- The main objectives of this project are:
  - To detect and alert the authorities about the open or broken manholes that can cause accidents or injuries to the pedestrians or vehicles   .
  - To monitor and measure the water level and flow rate in the drainage pipes and manholes to prevent blockage, overflow or contamination of fresh water    .
  - To reduce the manual intervention and risk of the workers who clean the underground drainage system  .
  - To provide real-time data and analysis of the drainage system to the managing station via cloud computing and web application    .
- The main components of this project are:
  - Sensors: Various sensors are used to measure the water level, flow rate, temperature, humidity, gas and pressure in the drainage pipes and manholes    . Some of the sensors used are ultrasonic sensor, water flow sensor, DHT11 sensor, MQ2 sensor and pressure sensor  .
  - Arduino: Arduino is a microcontroller board that is used to process the data from the sensors and send it to the GSM and GPS modules   .
  - GSM and GPS modules: GSM and GPS modules are used to send the location and alert messages to the authorities via SMS or call in case of any emergency or abnormal situation   .
  - Cloud computing: Cloud computing is used to store and analyze the data from the sensors and provide a web application for the managing station to monitor and control the drainage system    . Some of the cloud platforms used are Firebase, ThingSpeak and AWS   .
- The main advantages of this project are:
  - It improves the safety and efficiency of the drainage system by detecting and preventing accidents, blockage, overflow and contamination    .
  - It reduces the manual intervention and risk of the workers who clean the underground drainage system  .
  - It provides real-time data and analysis of the drainage system to the managing station via cloud computing and web application    .
  - It is low cost, low maintenance and scalable    .
