### IOT based Manhole Detection and Monitoring System

- A drainage monitoring system plays a significant role in keeping towns and cities healthy and clean.
- Most of the manholes are open without any observation that cause accidents and contamination of fresh water .
- Manual scavengers who clean the underground drainage face health and safety risks.
- IOT based manhole detection and monitoring system aims to solve these problems by using sensors, GSM, GPS and Arduino modules   .
- The system can detect the water level, gas level, temperature and pressure inside the manhole and send alerts to the authorities via SMS or web server   .
- The system can also track the location of the manhole and the status of the manhole cover using GPS and RFID tags .
- The system can prevent accidents, urban floods, water contamination and manual scavenging by providing real-time data and warnings  .
- The system is low cost, low maintenance, scalable and energy efficient .
