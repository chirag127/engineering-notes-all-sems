### IOT Based Coal Mine Safety Monitoring and Alerting System

- The main objective of this system is to monitor and detect various hazards inside a coal mine, such as gas leakage, fire, earthquake, and water level, and alert the workers and authorities in case of emergency    .
- The system consists of two modules: a transmitter module and a receiver module.
- The transmitter module is installed inside the coal mine and has sensors for temperature, smoke, and methane gas . The transmitter module also has an LCD display and an RF transmitter.
- The receiver module is located outside the coal mine and has an RF receiver, an LCD display, a buzzer, and an IoT module. The receiver module receives the data from the transmitter module and displays it on the LCD screen. The receiver module also sends the data to the cloud through the IoT module, where it can be accessed and analyzed by the authorities  .
- The system uses a low power communication protocol such as LoRa to transmit the data from the sensors to the central IoT gateway.
- The system can detect abnormal conditions such as high temperature, high smoke level, high methane level, earthquake, and water level, and trigger an alarm on the receiver module and the cloud   .
- The system can also send SMS alerts to the workers and authorities using a GSM module .
- The system can improve the safety and security of the coal mine workers and reduce the risk of accidents and disasters    .
