### IOT Based Heart Monitoring System Using ECG

- IOT Based Heart Monitoring System Using ECG is an application of Internet of Things (IoT) in medical science that aims to provide remote and real-time diagnosis of heart diseases using electrocardiogram (ECG) signals  .
- ECG is a graphical representation of the electrical activity of the heart that can reveal various aspects of the heart's function and health .
- The system consists of three main components: an ECG acquisition device, an IoT platform, and a web or mobile application  .
- The ECG acquisition device is a wearable sensor that can measure and record the ECG signals from the patient's chest using electrodes . The device can be based on Arduino microcontroller or NodeMCU ESP8266 module .
- The IoT platform is a cloud-based service that can receive, store, process, and analyze the ECG data from the device using wireless communication protocols such as Wi-Fi or Bluetooth  . The platform can be based on AWS cloud, Ubidots, or other IoT services  .
- The web or mobile application is a user interface that can display the ECG waveform, heart rate, respiration rate, and other parameters derived from the ECG data to the patient or the doctor   . The application can also provide alerts, notifications, and recommendations based on the analysis of the ECG data  .
- The system can provide several benefits such as: 
  - Improving the accessibility and affordability of health care services for patients living in remote or rural areas .
  - Enhancing the quality and accuracy of diagnosis and treatment of heart diseases by providing continuous and real-time monitoring of the heart's condition  .
  - Reducing the risk of complications and mortality due to delayed or missed diagnosis of heart diseases .
  - Increasing the patient's awareness and involvement in their own health management  .
