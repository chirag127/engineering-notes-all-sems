# Weather Imaging CubeSat with Telemetry Transmission

- A CubeSat is a type of miniaturized satellite that has a standard size of 10x10x10 cm and a mass of up to 1.33 kg. CubeSats can be deployed in low Earth orbit for various applications, such as communication, GPS, remote sensing, and space weather monitoring  .
- A weather imaging CubeSat is a CubeSat that carries a camera or a radiometer to capture images or measurements of the Earth's atmosphere, clouds, and precipitation. The data can be used for weather prediction and forecasting systems  .
- A telemetry transmission CubeSat is a CubeSat that transmits the data collected by the weather imaging CubeSat back to the ground station using a radio or a laser link. The transmission can be done in real time or in batches, depending on the available bandwidth and power .
- A weather imaging CubeSat with telemetry transmission is a CubeSat that combines both functions of weather imaging and data transmission. It can provide timely and accurate information about the weather conditions and phenomena in different regions of the Earth .
- Some examples of weather imaging CubeSats with telemetry transmission are:
  - CubIXSS: A CubeSat that measures the solar irradiance and the X-ray spectrum of the Sun to study the effects of solar flares on the Earth's ionosphere.
  - SunCET: A CubeSat that monitors the coronal mass ejections and the solar wind to understand the impact of space weather on the Earth's magnetosphere.
  - DYNAGLO: A CubeSat that observes the global lightning activity and the atmospheric gravity waves to improve the modeling and forecasting of severe storms.
  - WindCube: A CubeSat that uses a Doppler lidar to measure the wind speed and direction in the troposphere and the lower stratosphere to enhance the weather and climate prediction.
  - TEMPEST-D: A CubeSat that uses a five-frequency millimeter-wave radiometer to observe the temporal evolution of clouds and precipitation processes.
