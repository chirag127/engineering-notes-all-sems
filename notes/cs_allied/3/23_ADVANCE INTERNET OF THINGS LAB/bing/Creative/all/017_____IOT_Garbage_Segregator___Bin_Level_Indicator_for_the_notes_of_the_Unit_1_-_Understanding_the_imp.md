# IOT Garbage Segregator & Bin Level Indicator

- IOT Garbage Segregator & Bin Level Indicator is a system that automates the process of garbage segregation and level monitoring using Internet of Things (IOT) technology.
- The system consists of multiple smart dustbins that are equipped with sensors and actuators to detect the type and level of garbage in each bin.
- The system uses a microcontroller to control the segregation mechanism and to communicate with an IOT platform over the internet.
- The system can segregate the garbage into different categories, such as organic, plastic, metal, paper, etc., based on the weight, color, or shape of the garbage.
- The system can also measure the level of garbage in each bin and send the data to the IOT platform, which displays the bin level data over the internet.
- The system can alert the authorities or the waste management service when the garbage bins need to be emptied, thus reducing the overflow of garbage and improving the efficiency of waste collection and disposal.
- The system can help to counter the garbage crisis and to promote environmental sustainability by reducing the landfill waste and increasing the recycling rate.
