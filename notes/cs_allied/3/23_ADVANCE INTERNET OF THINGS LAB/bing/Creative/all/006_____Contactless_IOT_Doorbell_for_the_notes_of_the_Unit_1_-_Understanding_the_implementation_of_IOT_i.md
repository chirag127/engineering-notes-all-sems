# Contactless IOT Doorbell

- A contactless IOT doorbell is a device that uses internet of things (IOT) technology to alert the house owner about the arrival of a visitor without requiring physical contact.
- A contactless IOT doorbell can also perform other functions, such as scanning the temperature of the visitor, recognizing the face of the visitor, capturing the image of the visitor, and sending alerts to the house owner through a mobile app or a web interface.
- A contactless IOT doorbell can enhance the security and safety of the household, especially during the Covid-19 pandemic, by reducing the risk of transmission and detecting potential symptoms of the disease.
- A contactless IOT doorbell typically consists of the following components:
  - A microcontroller, such as NodeMCU or Raspberry Pi, that acts as the brain of the device and controls the communication and processing of data.
  - A non-contact infrared temperature sensor, such as MLX90614, that measures the temperature of the visitor from a distance and sends the data to the microcontroller.
  - A camera module, such as Pi Camera or ESP32-CAM, that captures the image of the visitor and sends it to the microcontroller for face recognition or storage.
  - A speaker or a buzzer, that produces a sound or a voice message to notify the visitor and the house owner about the status of the device.
  - A wireless module, such as Wi-Fi or Bluetooth, that connects the device to the internet and enables the data transmission and reception between the device and the mobile app or the web interface.
  - A power supply, such as a battery or a solar panel, that provides the necessary voltage and current to the device.
  - A casing or a frame, that encloses the device and protects it from environmental factors.
- A contactless IOT doorbell can be implemented using various software tools and platforms, such as:
  - Blynk, a cloud-based IOT platform that allows the creation of mobile apps and web interfaces for controlling and monitoring IOT devices.
  - Firebase, an online database service that stores and retrieves the data from the IOT devices and provides real-time updates and notifications.
  - OpenCV, an open source computer vision library that performs face recognition and image processing tasks.
  - Google Assistant, a virtual assistant that provides voice user interface and natural language processing capabilities.
