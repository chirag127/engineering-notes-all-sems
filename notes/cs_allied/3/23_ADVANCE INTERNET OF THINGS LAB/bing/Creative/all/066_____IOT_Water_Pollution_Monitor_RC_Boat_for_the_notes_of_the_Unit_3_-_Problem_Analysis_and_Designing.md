# IOT Water Pollution Monitor RC Boat

- IOT Water Pollution Monitor RC Boat is a project that aims to measure and transmit water quality data using an RC boat equipped with sensors and an IOT module  .
- The project is remote-operated and controlled by an RC remote using which it can be maneuvered accordingly, a motorized propeller system to provide the forward and backward motion, and a rudder system to provide the left and right motion.
- The boat carries relevant sensors to measure the water quality parameters such as pH, turbidity, temperature, dissolved oxygen, etc. and transmit them wirelessly to a remote control terminal or an IOT server online  .
- The project can help to monitor the water pollution levels in real-time and alert the authorities or the public about the water quality status and the need for remedial actions  .
- The project can also be used for educational purposes, research purposes, or recreational purposes as it can provide a fun and interactive way to learn about water pollution and its effects .

## Problem Analysis and Designing a Solution

- The problem that the project aims to solve is the lack of efficient and convenient methods to monitor the water pollution levels in different water bodies such as lakes, rivers, ponds, etc. that are affected by various sources of contamination such as industrial waste, agricultural runoff, sewage, etc.  .
- The existing methods of water quality monitoring are either manual, which involves collecting water samples and testing them in laboratories, or fixed, which involves installing sensors and devices at specific locations in the water body. Both of these methods have limitations such as high cost, low coverage, low frequency, low accuracy, etc.  .
- The proposed solution is to use an RC boat that can move freely and flexibly in the water body and collect and transmit water quality data in real-time using sensors and an IOT module. This solution can overcome the limitations of the existing methods and provide advantages such as low cost, high coverage, high frequency, high accuracy, etc.    .
- The design of the solution involves the following steps:
  - Selecting the appropriate RC boat model and modifying it to fit the sensors and the IOT module  .
  - Selecting the appropriate sensors and the IOT module that can measure and transmit the water quality parameters    .
  - Connecting the sensors and the IOT module to the RC boat and the RC remote using wires and wireless communication protocols    .
  - Programming the IOT module to collect the sensor data and send it to the remote control terminal or the IOT server online using a web or mobile application    .
  - Testing and debugging the RC boat and the IOT system to ensure the proper functioning and accuracy of the solution    .
