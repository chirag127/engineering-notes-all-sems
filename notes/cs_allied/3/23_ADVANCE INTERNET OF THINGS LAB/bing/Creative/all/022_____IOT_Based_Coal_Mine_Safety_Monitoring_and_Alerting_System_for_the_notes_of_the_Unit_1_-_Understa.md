# IOT Based Coal Mine Safety Monitoring and Alerting System

- IOT based coal mine safety monitoring and alerting system is a project that aims to improve the safety and security of coal miners and detect the hazards inside a coal mine  .
- The system consists of sensors, an IoT gateway, an LCD screen, an RF transmitter, and a cloud platform  .
- The sensors are installed in the transmitter module, which is carried by the coal miners or placed in strategic locations inside the mine  .
- The sensors can measure various parameters such as temperature, smoke, methane, water level, fire, and earthquake   .
- The sensor data is transmitted to the IoT gateway using a low power communication protocol such as LoRa or Zigbee.
- The IoT gateway can analyze the data and display it on the LCD screen . It can also send the data to the cloud platform using the internet  .
- The cloud platform can store, process, and visualize the data, and provide alerts and notifications to the authorized users such as mine managers, rescue teams, and authorities .
- The system can help to monitor the mine environment, detect the abnormal situations, and alert the relevant stakeholders in real time   .
- The system can also help to reduce the accidents, casualties, and losses caused by the mine hazards, and improve the efficiency and productivity of the mining operations   .
