# IOT Temperature & Mask Scan Entry System

## Introduction

- IOT Temperature & Mask Scan Entry System is a device that uses Internet of Things (IoT) technology to monitor and control the access of people to indoor spaces based on their body temperature and mask wearing status.
- The device aims to prevent the spread of COVID-19 and other infectious diseases by detecting fever and mask compliance, and denying entry to those who do not meet the criteria.
- The device consists of a contactless temperature scanner, a camera, a display screen, a gate-like structure, and a Raspberry Pi system that processes the data and communicates with a cloud server.
- The device can be placed in or in front of buildings, such as universities, offices, malls, airports, etc., to ensure the safety and health of the occupants and visitors.

## Working Principle

- The device works as follows:
  - A person approaches the device and stands in front of the camera and the temperature scanner.
  - The camera captures the image of the person and uses a machine learning algorithm to detect if the person is wearing a mask or not.
  - The temperature scanner measures the body temperature of the person using infrared radiation and displays it on the screen.
  - The Raspberry Pi system compares the temperature and mask status of the person with the predefined thresholds and sends a signal to the gate-like structure to allow or deny entry.
  - The device also sends the data of the person, such as the image, temperature, mask status, time, and date, to a cloud server for further analysis and storage.
  - The device can be configured to send alerts or notifications to the authorities or the person in case of high temperature or mask violation.

## Advantages

- The device has the following advantages:
  - It is contactless and reduces the risk of cross-contamination and human error.
  - It is fast and accurate and can scan multiple people simultaneously.
  - It is flexible and mobile and can be placed in different locations and environments.
  - It is easy to use and maintain and does not require special training or skills.
  - It is cost-effective and energy-efficient and can be powered by batteries or solar panels.
  - It is scalable and adaptable and can be integrated with other systems and devices, such as facial recognition, biometric authentication, attendance management, etc.
