# Wearable Computer With Temperature Distance Sensors

- A wearable computer is a device that can be worn on the body and can perform various computing tasks.
- A wearable computer with temperature distance sensors is a device that can measure the temperature and distance of objects or people in a contactless manner.
- A wearable computer with temperature distance sensors can be used for various applications, such as:
  - Health monitoring: The device can monitor the body temperature of the wearer or other people and alert them of any abnormality or fever.
  - Social distancing: The device can measure the distance between the wearer and other people and warn them of any violation of the recommended distance.
  - Environment sensing: The device can measure the temperature and distance of various objects or surfaces in the surroundings and provide useful information or feedback.
- A wearable computer with temperature distance sensors can be developed using a Raspberry Pi controller with a battery, touch screen display, lidar sensor, temperature sensor all together mounted in a compact way over wrist strap .
  - The Raspberry Pi controller is a small and low-cost computer that can run various operating systems and programs.
  - The battery is a power source that can provide electricity to the device for a certain period of time.
  - The touch screen display is a user interface that can show various information and allow the user to interact with the device by touching the screen.
  - The lidar sensor is a device that can measure the distance of objects or people by emitting laser pulses and detecting the reflected signals.
  - The temperature sensor is a device that can measure the temperature of objects or people by detecting the infrared radiation emitted by them.
- A wearable computer with temperature distance sensors can be programmed using various languages and frameworks, such as Python, C++, Arduino, etc.
  - The program can control the sensors and the display, process the data, and perform the desired functions.
  - The program can also communicate with other devices or networks using wireless technologies, such as Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, etc.
- A wearable computer with temperature distance sensors can be improved by using different or additional sensors, such as:
  - Graphene-based sensors: Graphene is a material that has high electrical conductivity and sensitivity, and can be used to make thin and flexible sensors.
  - Masimo Radius T: Masimo Radius T is a wearable, wireless sensor that continuously measures a patient’s body temperature and can be integrated with other Masimo devices.
