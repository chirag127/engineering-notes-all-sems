# IOT based Smart Energy Meter Monitoring with Theft Detection

- IOT based Smart Energy Meter Monitoring with Theft Detection is a system that aims to reduce the energy crisis and power theft by properly monitoring the energy consumption and avoiding energy wastage.
- The system uses Arduino as the main controller and ESP8266 as the Wi-Fi module to connect the smart energy meters to the internet.
- The system divides the area of electricity consumers into sections and installs an IOT based energy meter for each section to monitor the parameters such as voltage, current, power, and energy.
- The system also provides a master unit at the distribution pole site to monitor the data from all the sections and compare it with the data from the distribution end smart meter.
- The system uses statistical regression method to detect the power theft by continuously monitoring the consumer and distribution end smart meters data and identifying any abnormality or discrepancy.
- The system alerts the authorities and the consumers about the power theft and the energy consumption through an android application and a web portal .
- The system also provides features such as remote switching, tariff calculation, billing, and payment through the android application and the web portal .
- The system can improve the efficiency, reliability, and transparency of the power distribution system and reduce the losses due to power theft and wastage.
