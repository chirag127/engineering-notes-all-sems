### Wearable Computer With Temperature Distance Sensors

A wearable computer with temperature distance sensors is a device that can be worn on the body and is equipped with sensors that can measure temperature and distance. These sensors can be used for a variety of purposes, such as monitoring and controlling furnaces, oil level sensors, intake air temperature sensors, and automotive sensors in industries.

One example of a wearable computer with temperature distance sensors is a device developed using a Raspberry Pi controller with a battery, touch screen display, lidar sensor, and temperature sensor all together mounted in a compact way over a wrist strap . This makes a smart, easy to carry wearable computer.

The wearable computer provides the following features:
- Ability to get the power of a personal computer right on your hand.
- Touch display for easy interaction.
- Internet connectivity on Smartwatch.
- Ability to google search from anywhere.
- Contactless temperature sensing anywhere .
- Contactless and Accurate distance measurement using Lidar .

This device can be used for a variety of purposes and can provide the user with the power of a personal computer right on their hand. The touch display allows for easy interaction and the internet connectivity allows the user to search for information from anywhere. The contactless temperature sensing and distance measurement using Lidar provide additional functionality to the device.
