### IOT based Three Phase Power Failure Monitoring with SMS Alerts

- IOT based Three Phase Power Failure Monitoring with SMS Alerts is a system that monitors the power supply of three-phase systems and sends SMS alerts in case of power failure.
- The system uses an IOT module to connect to the internet and send SMS alerts to the user's mobile phone.
- The system can be used in various industries and households to monitor the power supply and prevent damage to equipment due to power failure.
- The system can also be used to monitor the power supply of remote locations and send alerts to the concerned authorities in case of power failure.
- The system can be easily integrated with existing power supply systems and can be customized according to the user's requirements.
- The system can help in reducing the downtime of equipment and can save costs associated with power failure.
- The system can also help in improving the safety of the equipment and the people working with the equipment.
