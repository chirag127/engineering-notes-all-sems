### IOT Contactless Covid Testing Booth Automation

Unit 2 - Solving Societal problems with the help of IOT in the subject of ADVANCE INTERNET OF THINGS LAB

1. **Introduction:** The COVID-19 pandemic has brought about the need for innovative solutions to tackle the spread of the virus. One such solution is the use of IOT contactless COVID testing booths.
2. **How it works:** These booths use various sensors and IOT devices to automate the testing process. This includes temperature sensors, cameras, and other devices to collect data and perform the test without the need for human contact.
3. **Benefits:** The use of IOT contactless COVID testing booths has several benefits. It reduces the risk of transmission of the virus, as there is no need for physical contact between the tester and the person being tested. It also speeds up the testing process, as the automation allows for faster and more efficient testing.
4. **Challenges:** There are also challenges associated with the use of IOT contactless COVID testing booths. These include the need for reliable and accurate sensors, as well as the need for robust data management and analysis systems to ensure that the data collected is used effectively.
5. **Conclusion:** IOT contactless COVID testing booths are an innovative solution to the challenges posed by the COVID-19 pandemic. They offer a safe and efficient way to test for the virus, and have the potential to play a key role in controlling its spread. However, there are also challenges that need to be addressed in order to fully realize their potential.
