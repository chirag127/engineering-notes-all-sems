### 360° Aerial Surveillance UAV With IOT Camera

Unit 2 - Solving Societal problems with the help of IOT in the subject of ADVANCE INTERNET OF THINGS LAB

1. A 360° Aerial Surveillance UAV with IOT Camera is a type of unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) that is equipped with an Internet of Things (IoT) camera.
2. This type of UAV is capable of providing 360-degree aerial surveillance, allowing for a comprehensive view of the area being monitored.
3. The IoT camera enables the UAV to transmit real-time data and images to a remote location, allowing for real-time monitoring and analysis.
4. This technology can be used to solve societal problems by providing enhanced surveillance and monitoring capabilities in areas such as public safety, disaster response, and environmental monitoring.
5. For example, in the case of a natural disaster, a 360° Aerial Surveillance UAV with IOT Camera could be deployed to provide real-time images and data to first responders, allowing them to more effectively assess the situation and respond accordingly.
6. Similarly, this technology could be used to monitor environmental conditions, such as air and water quality, to identify and address potential issues before they become major problems.
7. Overall, the use of 360° Aerial Surveillance UAVs with IOT Cameras has the potential to greatly enhance our ability to address societal problems through the use of advanced technology.
