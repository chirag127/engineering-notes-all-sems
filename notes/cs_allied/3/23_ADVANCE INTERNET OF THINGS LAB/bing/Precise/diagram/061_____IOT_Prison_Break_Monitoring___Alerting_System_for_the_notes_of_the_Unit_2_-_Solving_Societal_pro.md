### IOT Prison Break Monitoring & Alerting System

- The Internet of Things (IoT) is a network of interconnected devices that can communicate with each other and share data.
- In the context of a prison, IoT technology can be used to monitor and alert authorities in the event of a prison break.
- Sensors and cameras can be placed throughout the prison to detect any unusual activity or movement.
- If a prison break is detected, an alert can be sent to the authorities in real-time, allowing them to respond quickly and prevent the escape.
- This system can also be used to monitor the behavior of prisoners and detect any potential security threats.
- By using IoT technology, prisons can improve their security measures and prevent prison breaks.
- This is an example of how IoT can be used to solve societal problems and improve public safety.
