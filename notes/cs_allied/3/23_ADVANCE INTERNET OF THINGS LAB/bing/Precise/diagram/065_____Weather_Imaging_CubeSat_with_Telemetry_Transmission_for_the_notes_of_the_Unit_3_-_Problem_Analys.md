### Weather Imaging CubeSat with Telemetry Transmission

#### Unit 3 - Problem Analysis and Designing a Solution

- A CubeSat is a type of miniaturized satellite for space research that is made up of multiples of 10×10×10 cm cubic units.
- CubeSats have become increasingly popular in recent years due to their low cost and ease of deployment.
- One potential application of CubeSats is for weather imaging and telemetry transmission.
- This involves using the CubeSat to capture images of weather patterns and transmit the data back to Earth for analysis.
- The design of such a CubeSat would need to take into account the requirements for imaging and telemetry transmission, as well as the constraints of the CubeSat form factor.
- The design process would involve analyzing the problem, identifying the requirements, and designing a solution that meets those requirements within the constraints of the CubeSat form factor.
- This could involve selecting appropriate sensors and communication systems, as well as designing the CubeSat structure and power systems to support the required functionality.
- The end result would be a CubeSat capable of capturing and transmitting weather data, providing valuable information for weather forecasting and analysis.
