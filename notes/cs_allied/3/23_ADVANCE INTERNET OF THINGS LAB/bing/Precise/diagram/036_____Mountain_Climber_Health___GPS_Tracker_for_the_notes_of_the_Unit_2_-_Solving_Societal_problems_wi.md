### Mountain Climber Health & GPS Tracker

Unit 2 - Solving Societal problems with the help of IOT in the subject of ADVANCE INTERNET OF THINGS LAB

- Mountain climbing is a physically demanding activity that requires a high level of fitness and endurance.
- A Mountain Climber Health & GPS Tracker can be a useful tool for climbers to monitor their health and track their location during a climb.
- The device can track the climber's heart rate, oxygen saturation, and other vital signs to ensure that they are in good health and not overexerting themselves.
- The GPS tracking feature can help climbers navigate their route and also provide their location to rescue teams in case of an emergency.
- The device can also be connected to a smartphone app to provide real-time data and alerts to the climber and their support team.
- The use of IOT technology in the Mountain Climber Health & GPS Tracker can help improve the safety and well-being of climbers, and also provide valuable data for research and development in the field of mountain climbing.
