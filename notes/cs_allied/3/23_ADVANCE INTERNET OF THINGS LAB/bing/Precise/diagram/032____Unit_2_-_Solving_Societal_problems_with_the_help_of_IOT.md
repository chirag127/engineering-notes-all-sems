## Unit 2 - Solving Societal problems with the help of IOT

1. **Introduction:** The Internet of Things (IoT) is a network of interconnected devices that can collect and exchange data. This technology has the potential to solve many societal problems by providing real-time information and enabling efficient decision-making.

2. **Smart Cities:** IoT can be used to create smart cities, where sensors and devices are used to monitor and manage traffic, energy consumption, waste management, and air quality. This can lead to reduced pollution, improved public health, and more efficient use of resources.

3. **Healthcare:** IoT can also be used to improve healthcare by enabling remote monitoring of patients, tracking medication adherence, and providing real-time information to healthcare providers. This can lead to improved patient outcomes and reduced healthcare costs.

4. **Agriculture:** IoT can be used to improve agriculture by enabling precision farming, where sensors are used to monitor soil moisture, temperature, and nutrient levels. This can lead to improved crop yields, reduced water usage, and more efficient use of fertilizers.

5. **Disaster Response:** IoT can be used to improve disaster response by providing real-time information about the location and severity of disasters. This can enable faster and more effective response, reducing the impact of disasters on communities.

6. **Conclusion:** IoT has the potential to solve many societal problems by providing real-time information and enabling efficient decision-making. By leveraging this technology, we can create smarter cities, improve healthcare, increase agricultural productivity, and respond more effectively to disasters.
