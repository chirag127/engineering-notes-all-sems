### IOT based Smart Energy Meter Monitoring with Theft Detection

IOT based Smart Energy Meter Monitoring with Theft Detection is a system that uses the Internet of Things (IoT) technology to monitor energy consumption and detect theft in real-time. This system is designed to solve societal problems by providing a more efficient and secure way to monitor energy usage.

1. **Smart Energy Meter**: The system uses a smart energy meter to measure the energy consumption of a household or building. The meter is connected to the internet and sends data to a central server for analysis.

2. **Real-time Monitoring**: The system allows for real-time monitoring of energy consumption. This means that the user can see how much energy is being used at any given time. This can help the user to identify areas where energy is being wasted and take steps to reduce consumption.

3. **Theft Detection**: The system is designed to detect theft of energy. If the system detects an unusual pattern of energy consumption, it will send an alert to the user. The user can then investigate the cause of the unusual energy usage.

4. **Benefits**: The benefits of using an IOT based Smart Energy Meter Monitoring with Theft Detection system include increased efficiency, reduced energy consumption, and improved security. By monitoring energy usage in real-time, the user can identify areas where energy is being wasted and take steps to reduce consumption. The system also provides an additional layer of security by detecting theft of energy.
