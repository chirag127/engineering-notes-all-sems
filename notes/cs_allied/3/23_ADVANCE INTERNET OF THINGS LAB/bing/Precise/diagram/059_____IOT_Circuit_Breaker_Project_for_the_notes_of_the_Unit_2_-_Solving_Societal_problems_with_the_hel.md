### IOT Circuit Breaker Project

The IOT Circuit Breaker Project is a part of Unit 2 - Solving Societal problems with the help of IOT in the subject of ADVANCE INTERNET OF THINGS LAB. The project aims to develop a circuit breaker that can be controlled and monitored remotely using the Internet of Things (IOT) technology.

1. **Overview:** The IOT Circuit Breaker Project involves the development of a circuit breaker that can be controlled and monitored remotely using IOT technology. This allows for remote control of the circuit breaker, as well as real-time monitoring of its status and performance.

2. **Benefits:** The use of IOT technology in the circuit breaker provides several benefits, including increased safety, improved efficiency, and reduced maintenance costs. By allowing for remote control and monitoring, the IOT Circuit Breaker can help prevent accidents and improve the overall performance of the electrical system.

3. **Applications:** The IOT Circuit Breaker can be used in a variety of applications, including residential, commercial, and industrial settings. It can be used to control and monitor the electrical systems in homes, offices, factories, and other buildings.

4. **Implementation:** The implementation of the IOT Circuit Breaker Project involves the development of both hardware and software components. The hardware components include the circuit breaker itself, as well as sensors and communication modules. The software components include the control and monitoring software, as well as the user interface.

5. **Challenges:** The development of the IOT Circuit Breaker Project presents several challenges, including the need for reliable and secure communication, the integration of the hardware and software components, and the development of user-friendly interfaces.

Overall, the IOT Circuit Breaker Project is an important and innovative project that has the potential to improve the safety and efficiency of electrical systems, while also reducing maintenance costs. It is a valuable addition to the study of IOT and its applications in solving societal problems.
