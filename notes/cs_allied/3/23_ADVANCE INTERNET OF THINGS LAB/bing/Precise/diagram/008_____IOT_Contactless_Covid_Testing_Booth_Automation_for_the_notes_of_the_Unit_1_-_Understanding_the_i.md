### IOT Contactless Covid Testing Booth Automation

Unit 1 - Understanding the implementation of IOT in the subject of ADVANCE INTERNET OF THINGS LAB

1. IOT or Internet of Things refers to the interconnection of physical devices, vehicles, buildings, and other items embedded with electronics, software, sensors, and network connectivity that enables these objects to collect and exchange data.
2. Contactless Covid Testing Booth Automation is an application of IOT that aims to automate the process of Covid-19 testing in a safe and efficient manner.
3. The implementation of IOT in this context involves the use of various sensors and devices to collect data, such as temperature, heart rate, and respiratory rate, from individuals being tested.
4. This data is then transmitted to a central system for analysis and interpretation, allowing for the rapid identification of potential Covid-19 cases.
5. The use of IOT in this context can help to reduce the risk of transmission of the virus, as well as improve the efficiency and accuracy of the testing process.
6. Some of the key components of an IOT Contactless Covid Testing Booth may include temperature sensors, heart rate monitors, respiratory rate sensors, and a central processing unit for data analysis and interpretation.
7. The implementation of IOT in this context can also involve the use of machine learning algorithms to improve the accuracy of the testing process and to identify potential cases more quickly.
8. Overall, the use of IOT in the context of Contactless Covid Testing Booth Automation represents a promising approach to improving public health and safety during the ongoing Covid-19 pandemic.
