### Contactless IOT Doorbell

A contactless IOT doorbell is a device that allows visitors to ring the doorbell without physically touching it. This can be achieved through the use of sensors, such as infrared or proximity sensors, that detect the presence of a person near the doorbell and trigger the ringing mechanism.

The implementation of IOT in a contactless doorbell can provide several benefits, including:

1. Improved hygiene: By eliminating the need for physical contact, the spread of germs and viruses can be reduced.
2. Enhanced accessibility: For individuals with mobility or dexterity challenges, a contactless doorbell can be easier to use.
3. Increased convenience: With the integration of IOT, the doorbell can be connected to other smart devices, such as a smartphone or a smart speaker, allowing the homeowner to receive notifications or even communicate with the visitor remotely.
4. Customization: The use of IOT technology allows for the customization of the doorbell's behavior, such as changing the ringtone or adjusting the sensitivity of the sensors.

In summary, a contactless IOT doorbell is a practical and convenient solution that leverages the power of IOT technology to improve the user experience. It is a relevant topic for the study of the implementation of IOT in the subject of ADVANCE INTERNET OF THINGS LAB, Unit 1.
