### IOT Water Pollution Monitor RC Boat

Unit 3 - Problem Analysis and Designing a Solution

ADVANCE INTERNET OF THINGS LAB

- An IOT Water Pollution Monitor RC Boat is a remote-controlled boat equipped with sensors and communication technology to monitor water pollution levels in bodies of water.
- The boat can be controlled remotely and can navigate to different areas of the water body to collect data on pollution levels.
- The sensors on the boat can measure various parameters such as pH, dissolved oxygen, temperature, and conductivity to determine the water quality.
- The data collected by the sensors is transmitted in real-time to a remote server using IOT technology.
- The data can be analyzed and visualized to provide insights into the water pollution levels and to identify areas that require attention.
- The use of an RC boat allows for efficient and cost-effective monitoring of water pollution levels in large bodies of water.
- The boat can be designed to be autonomous, allowing it to navigate and collect data without human intervention.
- The use of IOT technology enables real-time monitoring and data analysis, providing timely information for decision-making and action.
