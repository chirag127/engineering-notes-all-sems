### IOT Temperature & Mask Scan Entry System

Unit 1 - Understanding the implementation of IOT in the subject of ADVANCE INTERNET OF THINGS LAB

1. The IOT Temperature & Mask Scan Entry System is an application of the Internet of Things (IOT) technology in the field of public health and safety.
2. This system uses sensors and cameras to detect the body temperature and mask-wearing status of individuals entering a building or public space.
3. The system can be programmed to allow or deny entry based on the readings from the sensors and cameras.
4. The system can also be connected to a central database to keep track of the number of people entering and exiting the building, as well as their temperature and mask-wearing status.
5. The implementation of this system can help prevent the spread of infectious diseases by ensuring that individuals with a fever or without a mask are not allowed to enter the building.
6. The system can be integrated with other IOT devices, such as smart locks and alarms, to provide a comprehensive solution for building security and public health.
7. The use of IOT technology in this system allows for real-time monitoring and data collection, which can be used for further analysis and decision-making.
