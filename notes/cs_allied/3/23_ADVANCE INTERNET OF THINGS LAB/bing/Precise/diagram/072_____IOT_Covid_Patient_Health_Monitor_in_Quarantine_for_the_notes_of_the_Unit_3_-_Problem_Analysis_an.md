### IOT Covid Patient Health Monitor in Quarantine

Unit 3 - Problem Analysis and Designing a Solution

ADVANCE INTERNET OF THINGS LAB

1. **Introduction:** The COVID-19 pandemic has brought about the need for remote monitoring of patients in quarantine. An IoT-based health monitoring system can provide a solution to this problem by allowing healthcare professionals to monitor the vital signs of patients remotely.

2. **Problem Analysis:** The main challenge in designing an IoT-based health monitoring system for COVID-19 patients in quarantine is to ensure the accuracy and reliability of the data collected. The system must be able to collect data on vital signs such as body temperature, heart rate, and oxygen saturation levels, and transmit this data to healthcare professionals in real-time.

3. **Designing a Solution:** The solution to this problem involves the use of sensors and IoT devices to collect and transmit data on the vital signs of patients. The system must be designed to be user-friendly and easy to use, with clear instructions for patients on how to use the devices. The data collected must be transmitted securely to healthcare professionals, who can then monitor the patients' health and provide appropriate care.

4. **Conclusion:** An IoT-based health monitoring system can provide an effective solution for remote monitoring of COVID-19 patients in quarantine. By collecting and transmitting data on vital signs in real-time, healthcare professionals can monitor the health of patients and provide appropriate care, even from a distance. This can help to improve patient outcomes and reduce the burden on healthcare systems.
