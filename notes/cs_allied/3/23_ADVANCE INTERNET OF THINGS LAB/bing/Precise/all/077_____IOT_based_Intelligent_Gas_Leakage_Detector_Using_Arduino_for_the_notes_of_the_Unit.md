# IOT based Intelligent Gas Leakage Detector Using Arduino

- An IOT based gas leakage detector using Arduino is a device that helps in determining gas leakage in the surroundings and sends data to an IOT module .
- The device gets connected to WIFI and the minimum and maximum parameters can be set accordingly .
- Such IOT and Arduino based gas leakage detector systems can be installed in homes, hotels, and LPG gas storage areas .
- The LPG gas detector system senses the LPG gas using an MQ5 gas sensor .
- This device continuously monitors the level of LPG gas present in the air .
- A wide range of MQ sensors is available in the market that can measure Methane, Butane, LPG, smoke, Alcohol, Ethanol, Carbon Monoxide, Hydrogen Gas, Ammonia, etc .
- These sensors are widely used in Smoke Detection, Gas Detection, and Air Quality Monitoring systems .
- Liquified Petroleum Gas (LPG) is commonly used in households, industries, and other places as it is very convenient, affordable, eco-friendly, and less hazardous than its other alternatives .
