# IOT Temperature & Mask Scan Entry System

## Unit 1 - Understanding the implementation of IOT

1. An IOT Temperature & Mask Scan Entry System is a system that uses Internet of Things (IOT) technology to monitor and control the entry of individuals into a building or facility.
2. The system typically includes sensors that can detect the body temperature of an individual and whether or not they are wearing a mask.
3. If the individual's temperature is within a normal range and they are wearing a mask, the system can grant them access to the building or facility.
4. If the individual's temperature is above a certain threshold or they are not wearing a mask, the system can deny them access and alert the appropriate personnel.
5. The system can be connected to the internet and can send data to a central server for monitoring and analysis.
6. The use of IOT technology allows for real-time monitoring and control of the entry system, as well as the ability to remotely update the system's parameters and settings.
7. This type of system can be useful in preventing the spread of infectious diseases and ensuring the safety and health of individuals within the building or facility.
