# IOT Water Pollution Monitor RC Boat

Unit 3 - Problem Analysis and Designing a Solution

ADVANCE INTERNET OF THINGS LAB

- An IOT Water Pollution Monitor RC Boat is a remote-controlled boat equipped with sensors and communication technology to monitor water pollution levels in bodies of water.
- The boat can be controlled remotely and can navigate to different locations to collect data on water quality.
- The sensors on the boat can measure various parameters such as pH, temperature, dissolved oxygen, and turbidity, among others.
- The data collected by the sensors is transmitted in real-time to a remote server or cloud platform for analysis and visualization.
- The boat can be designed to be powered by renewable energy sources such as solar panels, making it an environmentally friendly solution.
- The use of an IOT Water Pollution Monitor RC Boat can help in the early detection of water pollution and can aid in the formulation of effective measures to address the problem.
- The design of the boat can be customized to meet the specific requirements of the body of water being monitored and the data being collected.
- The boat can be equipped with additional features such as cameras and GPS to enhance its capabilities.
