# IOT Water Pollution Monitor RC Boat

The IOT Water Pollution Monitor RC Boat is a device that can be used to monitor water pollution levels in bodies of water such as lakes, rivers, and oceans. This device is part of the Unit 2 - Solving Societal problems with the help of IOT in the subject of ADVANCE INTERNET OF THINGS LAB.

Some key features of the IOT Water Pollution Monitor RC Boat include:

1. The ability to remotely control the boat using a smartphone or other device.
2. The use of sensors to measure various water quality parameters such as pH, dissolved oxygen, and temperature.
3. The ability to transmit data in real-time to a central server for analysis and monitoring.
4. The use of GPS to track the location of the boat and the data collected.

The IOT Water Pollution Monitor RC Boat can be used to help solve societal problems by providing accurate and timely data on water pollution levels. This data can be used by government agencies, environmental organizations, and other stakeholders to make informed decisions about water management and pollution control.

Overall, the IOT Water Pollution Monitor RC Boat is an innovative and useful tool for monitoring water pollution levels and helping to solve societal problems related to water quality. It is an important part of the ADVANCE INTERNET OF THINGS LAB curriculum and provides students with hands-on experience in using IOT technology to address real-world challenges.
