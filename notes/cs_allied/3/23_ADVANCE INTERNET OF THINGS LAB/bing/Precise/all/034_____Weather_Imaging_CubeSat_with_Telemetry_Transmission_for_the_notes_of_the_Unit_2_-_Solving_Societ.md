# Weather Imaging CubeSat with Telemetry Transmission

- CubeSats are small, low-cost satellites that can be used for a variety of purposes, including weather imaging.
- Weather imaging CubeSats can be equipped with cameras and other sensors to capture images and data of weather patterns and phenomena.
- These CubeSats can transmit the collected data back to Earth using telemetry, allowing scientists and meteorologists to analyze the data and make more accurate weather predictions.
- The use of CubeSats for weather imaging can help solve societal problems by providing more accurate and timely weather information, which can be used for disaster preparedness and response, agriculture, and other applications.
- The Internet of Things (IoT) can play a role in this by enabling the collection, transmission, and analysis of data from CubeSats and other weather sensors.
- By integrating CubeSats and IoT technologies, it is possible to create a more comprehensive and effective system for weather monitoring and prediction.
