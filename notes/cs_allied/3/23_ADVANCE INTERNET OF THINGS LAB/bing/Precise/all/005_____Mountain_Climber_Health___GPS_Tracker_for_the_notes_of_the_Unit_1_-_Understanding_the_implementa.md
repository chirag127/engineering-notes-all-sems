# Mountain Climber Health & GPS Tracker

Unit 1 - Understanding the implementation of IOT in the subject of ADVANCE INTERNET OF THINGS LAB

- Mountain climbers face several health risks while climbing, including altitude sickness, hypothermia, and dehydration.
- A health and GPS tracker can help monitor the climber's vital signs and location in real-time.
- The device can be connected to the internet using IoT technology, allowing for remote monitoring by a support team.
- The support team can track the climber's progress, monitor their health, and provide assistance if needed.
- The device can also provide the climber with information about their surroundings, such as weather conditions and potential hazards.
- This technology can improve the safety and success of mountain climbing expeditions.
