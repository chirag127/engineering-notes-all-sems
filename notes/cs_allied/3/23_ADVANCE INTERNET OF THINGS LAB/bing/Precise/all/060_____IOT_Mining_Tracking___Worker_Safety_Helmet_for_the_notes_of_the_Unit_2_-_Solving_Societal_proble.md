# IOT Mining Tracking & Worker Safety Helmet

- IOT Mining Tracking & Worker Safety Helmet is a system that is designed to protect workers in the mining industry using a microcontroller-based circuit on the worker helmet.
- The helmet is integrated with an RF-based tracking system which, in coordination with the tracker RF systems, helps provide data over IOT.
- The system makes use of an ATmega microcontroller-based RF tracker circuitry to receive the data transmitted by worker helmet nodes.
- This helps map the current location of workers through the entire mining site.
- The system is a cost-effective, practical, eco-friendly, and safest way to protect the workers working in the mining field.
- The main objective of this research is to design a smart helmet system for mining industry application which would be monitored by the system on the grounds of hazardous events such as temperature, humidity, gas, etc.
