# IOT based Three Phase Power Failure Monitoring with SMS Alerts

## Unit 2 - Solving Societal problems with the help of IOT in the subject of ADVANCE INTERNET OF THINGS LAB

- IOT based Three Phase Power Failure Monitoring with SMS Alerts is a system that monitors the power supply of three-phase systems and sends SMS alerts in case of power failure.
- The system uses IOT technology to monitor the power supply and send alerts to the user's mobile phone.
- The system can be used in various applications such as industrial plants, hospitals, and data centers where continuous power supply is critical.
- The system can help prevent damage to equipment and loss of data due to power failure.
- The system can also help reduce downtime and increase productivity by alerting the user to power failure in real-time.
- The system can be easily integrated with existing power monitoring systems and can be customized to meet the specific needs of the user.
- The system is easy to install and operate and can be accessed remotely using a web interface or mobile app.
- The system is cost-effective and can help save money by reducing the need for manual monitoring and reducing the risk of equipment damage due to power failure.
