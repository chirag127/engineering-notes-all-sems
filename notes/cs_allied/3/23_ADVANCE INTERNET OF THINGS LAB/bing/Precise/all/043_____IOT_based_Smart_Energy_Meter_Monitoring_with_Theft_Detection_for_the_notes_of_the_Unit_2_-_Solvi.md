### IOT based Smart Energy Meter Monitoring with Theft Detection

IOT based Smart Energy Meter Monitoring with Theft Detection is a system that uses the Internet of Things (IoT) technology to monitor energy consumption and detect theft in real-time. This system is designed to solve societal problems related to energy consumption and theft.

1. **Smart Energy Meter**: The system uses a smart energy meter to monitor energy consumption in real-time. The smart energy meter is connected to the internet and sends data to a cloud server for analysis.

2. **Real-time Monitoring**: The system allows for real-time monitoring of energy consumption. This enables users to track their energy usage and make informed decisions about their energy consumption.

3. **Theft Detection**: The system is designed to detect theft of energy. It does this by comparing the energy consumption data from the smart energy meter with the expected energy consumption. If there is a discrepancy, the system will send an alert to the user.

4. **Cloud Server**: The system uses a cloud server to store and analyze the data from the smart energy meter. The cloud server is responsible for detecting theft and sending alerts to the user.

5. **Benefits**: The benefits of using an IOT based Smart Energy Meter Monitoring with Theft Detection system include increased energy efficiency, reduced energy costs, and improved detection of energy theft.

This system is an example of how IoT technology can be used to solve societal problems. By using IoT technology, we can improve energy efficiency, reduce costs, and detect theft in real-time. This is just one of the many ways that IoT technology can be used to improve our lives and solve societal problems.
