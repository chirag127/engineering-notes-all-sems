# Contactless IOT Doorbell

Unit 3 - Problem Analysis and Designing a Solution

Subject: ADVANCE INTERNET OF THINGS LAB

- A contactless IOT doorbell is a device that allows visitors to ring the doorbell without physically touching it.
- This can be achieved through the use of sensors, such as infrared or proximity sensors, that detect the presence of a person near the doorbell.
- When the sensor is triggered, the doorbell sends a signal to the homeowner's smartphone or other device, alerting them of the visitor.
- The homeowner can then communicate with the visitor through a built-in speaker and microphone, or through a video call.
- This type of doorbell can be useful in reducing the spread of germs and viruses, as it eliminates the need for physical contact.
- It can also provide added convenience and security, as the homeowner can see and communicate with the visitor without having to open the door.
- To design a contactless IOT doorbell, one must consider the type of sensor to use, the communication method between the doorbell and the homeowner's device, and the power source for the doorbell.
- The design should also take into account the user experience, ensuring that the doorbell is easy to use and provides clear and reliable communication.
