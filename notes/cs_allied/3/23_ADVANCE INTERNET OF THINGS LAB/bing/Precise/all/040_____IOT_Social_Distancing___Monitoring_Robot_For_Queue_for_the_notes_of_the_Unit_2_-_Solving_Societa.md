# IOT Social Distancing & Monitoring Robot For Queue

- The IOT Social Distancing & Monitoring Robot For Queue is a system designed to limit the spread of Covid by measuring the distance between individuals in queues.
- This system is essential for places such as banks, government offices, shopping malls, schools, and theatres, where long queues can last for hours every day.
- The robot consists of a 4 wheel design system used to drive the robotic vehicle.
- It makes use of a line following principle to constantly move along with the queue and monitor for social distancing violations.
- Social distancing is of key importance during the current pandemic. It helps limit the spread of covid by observing distance between disease spreading individuals.
- This bot will help us to follow social distancing perfectly.
