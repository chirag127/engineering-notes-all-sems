# IOT Social Distancing & Monitoring Robot For Queue

- The IOT Social Distancing & Monitoring Robot For Queue is designed to prevent the spread of COVID-19 by ensuring that social distancing is followed in queues .
- This system is essential for places where long queues are common, such as banks, government offices, shopping malls, schools, and theaters .
- The robot consists of a 4-wheel design system used to drive the robotic vehicle and makes use of a line following principle to constantly move along with the queue and monitor for social distancing violations .
- Social distancing is of key importance during the current pandemic as it helps limit the spread of COVID-19 by observing distance between individuals  .
