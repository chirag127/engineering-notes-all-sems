# 360° Aerial Surveillance UAV With IOT Camera

Unit 1 - Understanding the implementation of IOT

In the subject of ADVANCE INTERNET OF THINGS LAB, one of the topics covered is the implementation of IOT in aerial surveillance using UAVs (Unmanned Aerial Vehicles) equipped with IOT cameras.

- A 360° aerial surveillance UAV with an IOT camera is a type of drone that is capable of capturing high-resolution images and videos from all angles while in flight.
- The IOT camera is connected to the internet, allowing for real-time transmission of data to a remote location for monitoring and analysis.
- This technology is particularly useful in applications such as border security, disaster management, and wildlife monitoring, where real-time information is critical for decision-making.
- The use of UAVs for aerial surveillance reduces the need for human intervention, increasing efficiency and reducing the risk of injury or loss of life.
- The implementation of IOT in aerial surveillance using UAVs is an example of how the internet of things is revolutionizing the way we collect and process data.
