# ADVANCE INTERNET OF THINGS LAB

The Advance Internet of Things (IoT) Lab is a course that focuses on the design, development, and implementation of IoT systems. The course covers the following topics:

1. Introduction to IoT: This topic covers the basics of IoT, including its definition, architecture, and applications.

2. IoT Protocols: This topic covers the various communication protocols used in IoT systems, including MQTT, CoAP, and HTTP.

3. IoT Devices: This topic covers the various types of IoT devices, including sensors, actuators, and gateways.

4. IoT Platforms: This topic covers the various IoT platforms available for developing and deploying IoT systems, including AWS IoT, Azure IoT, and Google Cloud IoT.

5. IoT Security: This topic covers the various security concerns and challenges associated with IoT systems, including data privacy, device security, and network security.

6. IoT Applications: This topic covers the various applications of IoT, including smart homes, smart cities, and industrial IoT.

7. IoT Project: This topic covers the development of an IoT project, including the design, implementation, and testing of an IoT system.

The Advance IoT Lab provides students with hands-on experience in developing and deploying IoT systems. Students will work on projects that involve the design and implementation of IoT systems, using various IoT devices, protocols, and platforms. The course is designed to provide students with a comprehensive understanding of IoT and its applications.
