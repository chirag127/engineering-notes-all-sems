# IOT Circuit Breaker Project

The IOT Circuit Breaker Project is a part of Unit 2 - Solving Societal problems with the help of IOT in the subject of ADVANCE INTERNET OF THINGS LAB. The project aims to use the Internet of Things (IoT) technology to improve the safety and efficiency of electrical systems.

1. **Overview:** The IOT Circuit Breaker is a device that can monitor and control the flow of electricity in a circuit. It can detect and prevent overloading, short circuits, and other electrical faults. The device can be remotely controlled and monitored using a smartphone or computer.

2. **Benefits:** The IOT Circuit Breaker can improve the safety of electrical systems by preventing electrical fires and other hazards. It can also improve the efficiency of electrical systems by reducing energy consumption and preventing power outages.

3. **Implementation:** The IOT Circuit Breaker can be implemented using a microcontroller, sensors, and a communication module. The microcontroller is responsible for monitoring and controlling the flow of electricity. The sensors can detect changes in the electrical current, voltage, and other parameters. The communication module allows the device to communicate with a smartphone or computer.

4. **Challenges:** There are several challenges that need to be addressed when implementing the IOT Circuit Breaker. These include ensuring the reliability and security of the device, as well as ensuring that it is easy to use and maintain.

5. **Conclusion:** The IOT Circuit Breaker is a promising technology that can improve the safety and efficiency of electrical systems. However, there are several challenges that need to be addressed in order to successfully implement the technology. By addressing these challenges, the IOT Circuit Breaker has the potential to make a significant impact on society.
