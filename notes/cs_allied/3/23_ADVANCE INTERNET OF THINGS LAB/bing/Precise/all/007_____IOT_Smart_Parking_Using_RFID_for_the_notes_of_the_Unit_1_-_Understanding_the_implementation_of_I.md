# IOT Smart Parking Using RFID

## Unit 1 - Understanding the implementation of IOT

### Introduction
- The Internet of Things (IoT) is a network of interconnected devices that can communicate with each other and share data.
- Smart parking is one of the many applications of IoT technology.
- RFID (Radio Frequency Identification) is a technology that can be used to implement smart parking solutions.

### How it works
- RFID tags are attached to vehicles and RFID readers are installed at the entrance and exit of the parking lot.
- When a vehicle enters the parking lot, the RFID reader reads the tag and sends the information to the central system.
- The central system then assigns a parking spot to the vehicle and guides the driver to the spot using a display or a mobile app.
- When the vehicle leaves the parking lot, the RFID reader reads the tag again and the central system calculates the parking fee and processes the payment.

### Advantages
- Smart parking using RFID can reduce the time it takes to find a parking spot and improve the overall parking experience.
- It can also reduce traffic congestion and air pollution caused by vehicles driving around looking for a parking spot.
- The system can also provide real-time information about the availability of parking spots, which can be useful for drivers and parking lot operators.

### Conclusion
- IoT technology, combined with RFID, can be used to implement smart parking solutions that can improve the parking experience, reduce traffic congestion, and provide real-time information about parking availability.
