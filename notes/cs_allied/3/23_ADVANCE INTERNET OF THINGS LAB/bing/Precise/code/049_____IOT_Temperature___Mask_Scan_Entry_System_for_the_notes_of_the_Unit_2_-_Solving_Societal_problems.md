### IOT Temperature & Mask Scan Entry System

The IOT Temperature & Mask Scan Entry System is a solution that can help solve societal problems with the help of IOT. This system is part of the Unit 2 - Solving Societal problems with the help of IOT in the subject of ADVANCE INTERNET OF THINGS LAB.

1. The system uses IOT technology to scan the temperature and mask-wearing status of individuals entering a building or facility.
2. The system can be set up at the entrance of a building or facility to ensure that individuals entering the building have a normal body temperature and are wearing a mask.
3. The system can help prevent the spread of infectious diseases by denying entry to individuals who have a fever or are not wearing a mask.
4. The system can be connected to a central database to keep track of the temperature and mask-wearing status of individuals entering the building.
5. The system can be integrated with other IOT devices, such as smart locks, to automatically lock or unlock doors based on the temperature and mask-wearing status of individuals.

This system is an example of how IOT technology can be used to solve societal problems and improve public health. By using IOT technology, we can create smart and connected systems that can help prevent the spread of infectious diseases and improve the overall health and well-being of our communities.
