### 360° Aerial Surveillance UAV With IOT Camera

Unit 1 - Understanding the implementation of IOT

- A 360° Aerial Surveillance UAV with IOT Camera is a type of Unmanned Aerial Vehicle (UAV) that is equipped with an Internet of Things (IOT) camera.
- This type of UAV is designed to provide 360-degree aerial surveillance, allowing for a comprehensive view of the area being monitored.
- The IOT camera on the UAV is connected to the internet, allowing for real-time transmission of data and images.
- This technology can be used for a variety of applications, including security and surveillance, disaster response, and environmental monitoring.
- The implementation of IOT in this type of UAV allows for remote control and monitoring, as well as the ability to collect and analyze data in real-time.
- This technology is an example of how the Internet of Things can be applied to enhance the capabilities of existing systems and improve their effectiveness.
