### IOT Mining Tracking & Worker Safety Helmet

1. The IOT Mining Tracking & Worker Safety Helmet is a system designed to improve the safety of workers in the mining industry.
2. The helmet is integrated with an RF-based tracking system, which, in coordination with the tracker RF systems, helps provide data over IOT.
3. The system makes use of an ATmega microcontroller-based RF tracker circuitry to receive the data transmitted by worker helmet nodes.
4. This helps map the current location of workers throughout the entire mining site.
5. The proposed system is a cost-effective, practical, eco-friendly, and safe way to protect workers in the mining field.
6. The main objective of this research is to design a smart helmet system for mining industry application, which would be monitored by the system on the grounds of hazardous events such as temperature, humidity, gas, etc.
