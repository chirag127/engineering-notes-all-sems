### Contactless IOT Doorbell

A contactless IOT doorbell is a device that allows visitors to alert the occupants of a building or residence that they are at the door without physically touching the doorbell. This can be achieved through various technologies such as motion sensors, proximity sensors, or voice recognition.

Some benefits of a contactless IOT doorbell include:

1. Improved hygiene: By eliminating the need for physical contact, the spread of germs and viruses can be reduced.
2. Accessibility: For individuals with mobility or dexterity issues, a contactless doorbell can be easier to use than a traditional doorbell.
3. Convenience: With the integration of IOT technology, the doorbell can be connected to other smart devices, allowing for remote monitoring and control.
4. Security: The use of sensors and cameras can provide an added layer of security, allowing occupants to see and communicate with visitors before opening the door.

In the context of solving societal problems with the help of IOT, a contactless IOT doorbell can contribute to public health efforts by reducing the spread of illness. Additionally, it can improve accessibility and convenience for individuals with disabilities or mobility issues.
