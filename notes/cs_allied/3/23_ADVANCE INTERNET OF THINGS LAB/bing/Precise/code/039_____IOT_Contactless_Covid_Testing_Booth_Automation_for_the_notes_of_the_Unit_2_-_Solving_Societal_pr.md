### IOT Contactless Covid Testing Booth Automation

Unit 2 - Solving Societal problems with the help of IOT in the subject of ADVANCE INTERNET OF THINGS LAB

1. The IOT Contactless Covid Testing Booth Automation is a solution that uses the Internet of Things (IoT) technology to automate the process of Covid-19 testing in a contactless manner.
2. This solution aims to reduce the risk of transmission of the virus by minimizing human contact during the testing process.
3. The testing booth is equipped with sensors and devices that can collect samples, such as saliva or nasal swabs, without the need for a healthcare worker to be present.
4. The collected samples are then automatically sent to a laboratory for analysis, and the results are communicated back to the individual being tested.
5. This solution can help increase the efficiency and safety of Covid-19 testing, and can be deployed in various settings such as airports, hospitals, and public spaces.
6. The use of IoT technology in this solution allows for real-time monitoring and data collection, which can help improve the overall effectiveness of the testing process.
7. The automation of the testing process can also help reduce the workload of healthcare workers, allowing them to focus on other critical tasks.
8. Overall, the IOT Contactless Covid Testing Booth Automation is an innovative solution that leverages the power of IoT technology to address a pressing societal problem.
