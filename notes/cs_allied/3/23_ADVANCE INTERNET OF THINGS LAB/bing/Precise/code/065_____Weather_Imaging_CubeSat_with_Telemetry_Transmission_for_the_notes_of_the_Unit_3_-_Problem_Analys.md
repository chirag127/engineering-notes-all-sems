### Weather Imaging CubeSat with Telemetry Transmission

- CubeSats are small satellites that are used for space research and exploration.
- They are typically 10x10x10 cm in size and weigh around 1-1.33 kg.
- CubeSats can be used for a variety of purposes, including weather imaging and telemetry transmission.
- Weather imaging CubeSats are equipped with cameras and sensors that can capture images and data of weather patterns and phenomena.
- This data can be transmitted back to Earth using telemetry transmission, allowing scientists and researchers to analyze and study the data.
- The use of CubeSats for weather imaging and telemetry transmission can provide valuable information for weather forecasting and climate research.
- CubeSats are relatively low-cost and can be launched into orbit in large numbers, providing a cost-effective way to gather weather data from space.
- The design of a weather imaging CubeSat with telemetry transmission involves several steps, including problem analysis, requirements specification, and system design.
- In the problem analysis phase, the goals and objectives of the CubeSat are defined and the requirements for the CubeSat are identified.
- In the requirements specification phase, the specific requirements for the CubeSat are defined in detail, including the technical specifications for the cameras, sensors, and telemetry transmission system.
- In the system design phase, the overall design of the CubeSat is developed, including the selection of components and the layout of the CubeSat.
- The design of a weather imaging CubeSat with telemetry transmission requires careful consideration of factors such as power requirements, data storage and transmission, and the environmental conditions in space.
