### Mountain Climber Health & GPS Tracker

Unit 2 - Solving Societal problems with the help of IOT in the subject of ADVANCE INTERNET OF THINGS LAB

- Mountain climbers face many challenges while climbing, including harsh weather conditions, altitude sickness, and the risk of getting lost.
- A Mountain Climber Health & GPS Tracker can help address these challenges by providing real-time information about the climber's location, health, and the surrounding environment.
- The device can be equipped with sensors to monitor the climber's heart rate, oxygen levels, and body temperature, and can send alerts if any of these readings fall outside of a safe range.
- The GPS tracker can help the climber navigate and can also send their location to rescue teams in case of an emergency.
- By leveraging the Internet of Things (IoT), the device can also provide real-time weather updates and warnings, helping the climber make informed decisions about their climb.
- Overall, a Mountain Climber Health & GPS Tracker can help improve the safety and success of mountain climbing expeditions.
