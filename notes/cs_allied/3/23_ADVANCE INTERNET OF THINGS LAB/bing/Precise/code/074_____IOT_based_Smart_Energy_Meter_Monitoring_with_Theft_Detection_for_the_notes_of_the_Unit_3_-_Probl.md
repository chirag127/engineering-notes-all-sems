### IOT based Smart Energy Meter Monitoring with Theft Detection

- An IOT based Smart Energy Meter is a device that measures the amount of electrical energy consumed by a residence, business, or an electrically powered device.
- The device is connected to the internet and can send data to a remote server for monitoring and analysis.
- The device can also detect any abnormal usage patterns that may indicate energy theft.
- The device can be programmed to send alerts to the user or the energy provider in case of any abnormal usage or theft detection.
- The device can also be used to monitor energy consumption in real-time and provide the user with insights on how to reduce their energy consumption and save money on their energy bills.
- The device can be integrated with other smart home devices to provide a complete home energy management solution.
