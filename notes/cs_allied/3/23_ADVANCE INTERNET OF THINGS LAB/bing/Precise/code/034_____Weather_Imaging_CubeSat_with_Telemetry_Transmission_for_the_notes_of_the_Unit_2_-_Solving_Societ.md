### Weather Imaging CubeSat with Telemetry Transmission

- CubeSats are small satellites that are used for space research and exploration.
- They are typically 10x10x10 cm in size and weigh around 1-1.33 kg.
- CubeSats can be used for a variety of purposes, including weather imaging and telemetry transmission.
- Weather imaging CubeSats can capture images of the Earth's atmosphere and weather patterns.
- These images can be used to improve weather forecasting and to monitor climate change.
- Telemetry transmission refers to the transmission of data from the CubeSat to a ground station.
- This data can include information about the CubeSat's position, temperature, and other operational parameters.
- The combination of weather imaging and telemetry transmission in a CubeSat can provide valuable information for solving societal problems related to weather and climate.
- For example, accurate weather forecasting can help farmers plan their crops, and monitoring climate change can inform policy decisions related to the environment.
- The use of CubeSats for weather imaging and telemetry transmission is an example of how the Internet of Things (IoT) can be used to solve societal problems.
