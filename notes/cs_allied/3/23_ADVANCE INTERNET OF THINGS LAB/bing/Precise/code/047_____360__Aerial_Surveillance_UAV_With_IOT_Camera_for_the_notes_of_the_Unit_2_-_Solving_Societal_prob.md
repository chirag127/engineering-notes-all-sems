### 360° Aerial Surveillance UAV With IOT Camera

- A 360° Aerial Surveillance UAV with IOT Camera is a type of Unmanned Aerial Vehicle (UAV) that is equipped with an Internet of Things (IOT) camera.
- This type of UAV is capable of providing 360-degree aerial surveillance, allowing for a comprehensive view of the area being monitored.
- The IOT camera on the UAV is connected to the internet, allowing for real-time transmission of data and images.
- This technology can be used for a variety of purposes, including monitoring traffic, tracking wildlife, and providing security for large events or public spaces.
- The use of IOT technology in UAVs allows for more efficient and effective surveillance, as the data collected can be analyzed and acted upon in real-time.
- This technology has the potential to solve societal problems by providing a cost-effective and efficient means of monitoring and addressing issues in real-time.
