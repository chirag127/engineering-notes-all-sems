### IOT Water Pollution Monitor RC Boat

Unit 3 - Problem Analysis and Designing a Solution

ADVANCE INTERNET OF THINGS LAB

1. The IOT Water Pollution Monitor RC Boat is a device that can be used to monitor water pollution levels in bodies of water such as lakes, rivers, and oceans.
2. The device is equipped with sensors that can detect various pollutants such as chemicals, heavy metals, and organic matter.
3. The data collected by the sensors is transmitted to a central server using IOT technology, where it can be analyzed and used to make informed decisions about water management.
4. The RC boat is controlled remotely, allowing it to navigate to different areas of the body of water to collect data.
5. The design of the IOT Water Pollution Monitor RC Boat involves selecting the appropriate sensors, designing the boat to house the sensors and electronics, and integrating the IOT technology to transmit the data.
6. The problem analysis involves identifying the specific pollutants that need to be monitored, the requirements for data collection and transmission, and the environmental conditions in which the device will operate.
7. The solution design involves selecting the appropriate hardware and software components, designing the system architecture, and testing the device to ensure it meets the requirements.
8. The IOT Water Pollution Monitor RC Boat is an effective tool for monitoring water pollution levels and can help to improve water management and protect the environment.
