### IOT based Three Phase Power Failure Monitoring with SMS Alerts

- IOT based Three Phase Power Failure Monitoring with SMS Alerts is a system that monitors the power supply of three-phase systems and sends SMS alerts in case of power failure.
- The system uses an IOT module to connect to the internet and send SMS alerts to the user's mobile phone.
- The system can be used in industrial, commercial, and residential settings to monitor the power supply and ensure that critical equipment and appliances are not affected by power failures.
- The system can be configured to send SMS alerts to multiple users, allowing for quick response and resolution of power failures.
- The system can also be used to monitor the power supply of remote locations, allowing for quick response and resolution of power failures in areas that are difficult to access.
- The system can be integrated with other IOT devices and systems to provide a comprehensive solution for power failure monitoring and management.
- The system can be customized to meet the specific needs of the user, allowing for flexibility and scalability.
