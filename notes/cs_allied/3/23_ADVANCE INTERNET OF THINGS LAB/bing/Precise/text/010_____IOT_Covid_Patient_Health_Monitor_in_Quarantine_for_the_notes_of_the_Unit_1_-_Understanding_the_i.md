### IOT Covid Patient Health Monitor in Quarantine

- The Internet of Things (IoT) has been used to monitor the health of Covid patients in quarantine.
- IoT devices such as smart thermometers, pulse oximeters, and blood pressure monitors can be used to remotely monitor the vital signs of patients.
- This allows healthcare professionals to keep track of the patient's health without having to physically visit them, reducing the risk of infection.
- The data collected by the IoT devices can be transmitted to a central server, where it can be analyzed and used to make decisions about the patient's care.
- This technology can also be used to monitor the health of patients in isolation at home, allowing them to receive medical attention without having to leave their house.
- The use of IoT in healthcare has the potential to improve patient outcomes and reduce the burden on healthcare systems.
