### IOT Social Distancing & Monitoring Robot For Queue

- The IOT Social Distancing & Monitoring Robot is a device that helps maintain social distancing in queues.
- It uses sensors to detect the distance between individuals and alerts them if they are too close to each other.
- The robot can also monitor the length of the queue and provide real-time updates to individuals waiting in line.
- This technology can be used in various settings such as supermarkets, banks, and hospitals to ensure that social distancing guidelines are being followed.
- The use of IOT technology allows for remote monitoring and control of the robot, making it easier to manage and maintain.
- The implementation of this technology can help reduce the spread of infectious diseases and improve public health.
- This is an example of how IOT technology can be used to solve societal problems and improve the quality of life for individuals.
