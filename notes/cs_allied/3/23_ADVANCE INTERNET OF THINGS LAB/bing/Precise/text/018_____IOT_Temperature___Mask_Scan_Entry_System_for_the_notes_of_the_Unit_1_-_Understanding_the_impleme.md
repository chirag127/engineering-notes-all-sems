### IOT Temperature & Mask Scan Entry System

The IOT Temperature & Mask Scan Entry System is an implementation of the Internet of Things (IOT) technology that is used to monitor and control the entry of individuals into a building or a specific area. This system is designed to enhance safety measures by scanning the temperature and checking if the individual is wearing a mask before allowing entry.

1. **Temperature Scanning**: The system uses a temperature sensor to scan the body temperature of the individual. If the temperature is above the normal range, the system will trigger an alarm and deny entry to the individual.

2. **Mask Detection**: The system uses a camera and image recognition technology to detect if the individual is wearing a mask. If the individual is not wearing a mask, the system will trigger an alarm and deny entry to the individual.

3. **Entry Control**: The system is connected to the entry control mechanism, such as a door or a gate, and can control the opening and closing of the entry point based on the results of the temperature and mask scan.

4. **Data Collection and Analysis**: The system collects data on the temperature and mask-wearing status of individuals and can analyze this data to identify trends and patterns. This information can be used to improve safety measures and make informed decisions.

The IOT Temperature & Mask Scan Entry System is an effective way to enhance safety measures and prevent the spread of infectious diseases. It is easy to implement and can be customized to meet the specific needs of different organizations.
