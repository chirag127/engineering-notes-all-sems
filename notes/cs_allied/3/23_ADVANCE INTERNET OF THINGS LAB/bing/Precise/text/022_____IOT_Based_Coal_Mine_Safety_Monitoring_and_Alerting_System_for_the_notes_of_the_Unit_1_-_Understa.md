### IOT Based Coal Mine Safety Monitoring and Alerting System

- The Internet of Things (IoT) is a network of interconnected devices that can collect and exchange data.
- In the context of coal mining, IoT can be used to improve safety by monitoring various parameters such as air quality, temperature, and humidity.
- Sensors can be placed throughout the mine to collect data on these parameters in real-time.
- This data can then be transmitted to a central monitoring system, where it can be analyzed to detect any potential safety hazards.
- If a hazard is detected, an alert can be sent to the appropriate personnel, allowing them to take action to prevent accidents or injuries.
- This system can also be used to monitor the health and location of miners, providing an additional layer of safety.
- Overall, the implementation of an IoT-based coal mine safety monitoring and alerting system can greatly improve the safety of coal mining operations.
