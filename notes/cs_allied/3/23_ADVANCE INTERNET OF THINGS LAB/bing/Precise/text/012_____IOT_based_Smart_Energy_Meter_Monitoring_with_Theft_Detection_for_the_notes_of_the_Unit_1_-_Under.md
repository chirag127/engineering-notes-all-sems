### IOT based Smart Energy Meter Monitoring with Theft Detection

Unit 1 - Understanding the implementation of IOT in the subject of ADVANCE INTERNET OF THINGS LAB

1. An IOT based smart energy meter is a device that measures the consumption of electrical energy and communicates the information to the electricity supplier for monitoring and billing purposes.
2. The device is equipped with sensors that can detect any abnormal behavior in the energy consumption pattern, such as sudden spikes or drops, which may indicate energy theft.
3. The device can also monitor the quality of the power supply and send alerts to the user or the electricity supplier in case of any issues.
4. The device can be accessed and controlled remotely through a web or mobile application, allowing the user to monitor their energy consumption in real-time and make informed decisions about their energy usage.
5. The implementation of IOT in smart energy meters can help reduce energy theft, improve the efficiency of the energy distribution system, and provide a better user experience for the consumers.
