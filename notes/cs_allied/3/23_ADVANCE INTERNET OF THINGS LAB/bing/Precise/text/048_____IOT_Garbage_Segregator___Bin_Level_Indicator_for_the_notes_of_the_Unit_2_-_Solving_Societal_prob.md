### IOT Garbage Segregator & Bin Level Indicator

The IOT Garbage Segregator & Bin Level Indicator is a system that uses the Internet of Things (IoT) technology to solve societal problems related to waste management. This system is part of the Unit 2 - Solving Societal problems with the help of IOT in the subject of ADVANCE INTERNET OF THINGS LAB.

The system has two main components:

1. **Garbage Segregator:** This component is responsible for separating different types of waste, such as organic, recyclable, and non-recyclable, using sensors and actuators. This segregation is important for efficient waste management and recycling.

2. **Bin Level Indicator:** This component is responsible for monitoring the level of waste in the bin and sending notifications to the waste management authorities when the bin is full. This helps to ensure timely collection of waste and prevents overflow.

The system is designed to be easy to use and maintain, and can be installed in public places such as parks, streets, and residential areas. It can help to improve the cleanliness and hygiene of the environment, and reduce the negative impact of waste on the environment.

Overall, the IOT Garbage Segregator & Bin Level Indicator is an innovative solution that uses IoT technology to address the important issue of waste management in society. It is a practical and effective way to improve the quality of life for people and protect the environment.
