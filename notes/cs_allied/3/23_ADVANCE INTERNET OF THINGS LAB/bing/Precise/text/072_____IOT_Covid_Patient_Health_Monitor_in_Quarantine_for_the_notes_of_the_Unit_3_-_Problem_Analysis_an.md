### IOT Covid Patient Health Monitor in Quarantine

Unit 3 - Problem Analysis and Designing a Solution in the subject of ADVANCE INTERNET OF THINGS LAB

1. **Introduction:** The COVID-19 pandemic has brought about a need for remote monitoring of patients in quarantine. An IoT-based health monitoring system can provide a solution for this problem.
2. **Problem Analysis:** Patients in quarantine need to be monitored for symptoms such as fever, cough, and difficulty breathing. Manual monitoring by healthcare workers can be time-consuming and put them at risk of infection.
3. **Designing a Solution:** An IoT-based health monitoring system can be designed to remotely monitor the vital signs of patients in quarantine. This can include sensors to measure body temperature, heart rate, and oxygen saturation levels.
4. **Implementation:** The system can be implemented using wearable devices or sensors placed in the patient's room. The data collected by the sensors can be transmitted to a central server for analysis and monitoring by healthcare professionals.
5. **Benefits:** The use of an IoT-based health monitoring system can reduce the workload of healthcare workers and minimize their risk of infection. It can also provide real-time data on the patient's condition, allowing for timely intervention if necessary.
6. **Conclusion:** An IoT-based health monitoring system can provide an effective solution for remote monitoring of patients in quarantine during the COVID-19 pandemic. It can improve patient care and safety while reducing the burden on healthcare workers.
