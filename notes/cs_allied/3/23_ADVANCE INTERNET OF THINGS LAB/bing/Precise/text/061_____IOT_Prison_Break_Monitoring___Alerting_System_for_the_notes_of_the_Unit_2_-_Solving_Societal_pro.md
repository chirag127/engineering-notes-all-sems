### IOT Prison Break Monitoring & Alerting System

- The IOT Prison Break Monitoring & Alerting System is a system that uses Internet of Things (IoT) technology to monitor and alert authorities of prison breaks.
- The system can be implemented using various sensors and devices, such as motion sensors, cameras, and alarms, which are connected to a central control system.
- The sensors and devices can be placed strategically throughout the prison to detect any unusual activity or movement.
- If a prison break is detected, the system can automatically alert the authorities and provide real-time information to help them respond quickly and effectively.
- The system can also be integrated with other security systems, such as biometric identification and access control, to enhance the overall security of the prison.
- The use of IoT technology in prison security can help prevent prison breaks and improve the safety of both prisoners and prison staff.
- This system is an example of how IoT technology can be used to solve societal problems and improve public safety.
