### IOT based Anti-theft Flooring System using Raspberry Pi

- The IOT based Anti-theft Flooring System using Raspberry Pi is a system designed to secure and guard a house in the absence of the owner .
- The system monitors the entire floor for movement and tracks even a single step anywhere on the floor .
- The user is alerted through mail over IOT when movement is detected .
- The system uses secure flooring tiles connected with IOT .
- When the system is turned on, any movement inside the house is detected and the information is passed over IOT .
- When a thief enters the house and steps on the floor, the movement is immediately sensed by the sensor which passes on the signal to the Raspberry Pi controller .
- The controller processes the signal to determine if it is valid and then moves the camera to the area where movement was detected .
- The camera then transmits the image over the Internet for the homeowner to check .
