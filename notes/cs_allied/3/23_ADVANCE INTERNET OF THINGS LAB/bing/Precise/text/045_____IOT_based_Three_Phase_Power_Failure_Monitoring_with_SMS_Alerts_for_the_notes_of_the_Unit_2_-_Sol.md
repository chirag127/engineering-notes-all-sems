### IOT based Three Phase Power Failure Monitoring with SMS Alerts

- IOT based Three Phase Power Failure Monitoring with SMS Alerts is a system that monitors the power supply of three-phase systems and sends SMS alerts in case of power failure.
- The system uses an IOT module to connect to the internet and send SMS alerts to the user's mobile phone.
- The system can be used in various applications such as industrial plants, commercial buildings, and residential homes to monitor the power supply and prevent damage to equipment due to power failure.
- The system can be configured to send SMS alerts to multiple users, allowing for quick response in case of power failure.
- The system can also be used to monitor the power supply of remote locations, allowing for quick response in case of power failure.
- The system can be integrated with other IOT devices to provide a comprehensive solution for power failure monitoring and management.

This system is an example of how IOT can be used to solve societal problems by providing real-time monitoring and alerts for critical systems. It can help prevent damage to equipment and ensure the safety of people and property. It is a useful tool for managing power supply in various applications.
