### IOT based Manhole Detection and Monitoring System

An IOT based Manhole Detection and Monitoring System is a system that uses the Internet of Things (IoT) technology to detect and monitor manholes in a city. This system can help solve societal problems by improving the safety and sanitation of the city.

1. The system uses sensors to detect the presence of manholes and their status (open or closed).
2. The data collected by the sensors is transmitted to a central server using wireless communication.
3. The server processes the data and generates alerts if a manhole is detected to be open.
4. The alerts are sent to the relevant authorities, who can take appropriate action to close the manhole and prevent accidents.
5. The system can also monitor the level of gases and other pollutants in the manholes and generate alerts if the levels exceed safe limits.
6. This can help improve the sanitation of the city by enabling timely maintenance and cleaning of the manholes.

This system is an example of how IoT technology can be used to solve societal problems and improve the quality of life in cities. It is a part of the Unit 2 - Solving Societal problems with the help of IOT in the subject of ADVANCE INTERNET OF THINGS LAB.
