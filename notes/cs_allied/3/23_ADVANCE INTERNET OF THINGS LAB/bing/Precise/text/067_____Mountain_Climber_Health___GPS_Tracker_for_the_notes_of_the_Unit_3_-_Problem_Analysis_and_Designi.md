### Mountain Climber Health & GPS Tracker

Unit 3 - Problem Analysis and Designing a Solution in the subject of ADVANCE INTERNET OF THINGS LAB

- Mountain climbers face a number of health risks while climbing, including altitude sickness, hypothermia, and dehydration.
- A health and GPS tracker can help monitor the climber's vital signs and location in real-time, providing valuable information to the climber and their support team.
- The device can be designed to measure heart rate, oxygen saturation, body temperature, and other vital signs.
- The GPS tracker can provide the climber's location, altitude, and speed, allowing the support team to track their progress and provide assistance if needed.
- The device can also be equipped with an emergency button, allowing the climber to send a distress signal in case of an emergency.
- The data collected by the device can be transmitted to the support team in real-time, allowing them to monitor the climber's health and progress and provide assistance if needed.
- The device can be designed to be lightweight, durable, and easy to use, making it an essential tool for mountain climbers.
- In summary, a mountain climber health and GPS tracker can help improve the safety and well-being of mountain climbers by providing real-time monitoring of their vital signs and location. It is an important tool for problem analysis and designing a solution in the field of advanced Internet of Things.
