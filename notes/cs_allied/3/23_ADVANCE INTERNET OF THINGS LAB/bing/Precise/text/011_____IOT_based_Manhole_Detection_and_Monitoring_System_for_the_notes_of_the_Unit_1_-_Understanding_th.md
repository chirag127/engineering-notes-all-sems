### IOT based Manhole Detection and Monitoring System

- An IOT based Manhole Detection and Monitoring System is a system that uses the Internet of Things (IOT) technology to detect and monitor manholes in real-time.
- The system consists of sensors that are placed inside the manholes to detect any changes in the environment, such as the level of water or gas.
- The data collected by the sensors is transmitted to a central server using wireless communication.
- The server processes the data and generates alerts if any abnormal conditions are detected.
- The system can also be used to monitor the maintenance of the manholes and to schedule maintenance activities.
- The implementation of such a system can help to prevent accidents and improve the safety of the public.
- The use of IOT technology in this system allows for real-time monitoring and quick response to any abnormal conditions.
- The system can be integrated with other systems, such as traffic management systems, to provide a comprehensive solution for the management of urban infrastructure.
