### Weather Imaging CubeSat with Telemetry Transmission

- Satellites are launched in orbit for a variety of purposes including communication, GPS, Weather imaging and similar applications.
- Weather imaging satellites are used to transmit data about weather parameters that can be used for prediction and forecasting systems.
- A demo weather imaging and parameter transmission satellite can be developed to transmit weather data for analysis back to the earth.
- NASA has selected four CubeSats for Space Weather Tech Development, including CubIXSS, SunCET, DYNAGLO, and WindCube.
- The Temporal Experiment for Storms and Tropical Systems Demonstration (TEMPEST-D) CubeSat has demonstrated a new five-frequency, millimeter-wave imaging radiometer to observe the temporal evolution of clouds and precipitation processes.
