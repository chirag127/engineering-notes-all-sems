### Blogs and online communities

- A blog is an online journal or informational website that offers regularly updated content (blog post) about a topic.
- A blog post is written in an informal or conversational style and presents information in reverse chronological order.
- Blogs are among the major platforms on which online communities form.
- An online community is a group of people with common interests, and who use the Internet tools and spaces (e.g., blogs, websites, and email) to communicate, create something together, and pursue common interests over time.
- Online communities have their own set of guidelines and needs, like online community engagement, moderation, and management.
- Online communities can benefit from web semantics, which is the study of the meaning and structure of data on the web, to enhance their content, searchability, and interoperability.
- Web semantics can help online communities to:
  - Organize and classify their content using metadata, ontologies, and vocabularies.
  - Improve their search engine optimization (SEO) and visibility on the web.
  - Enable data integration and sharing across different platforms and applications.
  - Provide richer and more personalized user experiences.
- Some examples of online communities that use web semantics are:
  - Wikipedia, which uses the Semantic MediaWiki extension to annotate and query its content.
  - Stack Overflow, which uses tags and categories to structure and filter its questions and answers.
  - Facebook, which uses the Open Graph protocol to embed metadata in its web pages and enable social sharing.
