### Evaluation through analysis for the notes of the Unit 5 - Evaluation of Social Network Analysis

- Social network analysis (SNA) is a tool used in modern sociology to identify the links between individuals in various social systems.
- SNA can also be used in monitoring and evaluation in order to probe deeper into the power of the social network and how it can be used to measure program outcomes and impact.
- SNA analyses the behavior of the individual at the micro level, the pattern of relationships (network structure) at the macro level, and the interactions between the two.
- SNA helps the understanding of phenomena that emerge from the interaction of individuals or institutions and is useful to study complex adaptive systems .
- SNA can be used to map the influence and the exchange of information among network actors and to assess their adaptive capacity.
- SNA can also be used to evaluate the effectiveness of network interventions, such as network formation, network strengthening, network governance, and network learning.
- SNA can provide quantitative and visual data on the network characteristics, such as network size, density, centrality, cohesion, diversity, and reciprocity.
- SNA can also reveal the types and quality of relationships within the network, such as trust, collaboration, communication, and knowledge sharing.
- SNA can help to identify the strengths and weaknesses of the network, the gaps and opportunities for improvement, the key actors and influencers, and the potential outcomes and impacts of the network.
- SNA can be used in different phases of complex healthcare intervention research, such as problem identification, intervention design, implementation, and evaluation.
- SNA can help to understand the context and the stakeholders of the intervention, the mechanisms and processes of the intervention, the outcomes and impacts of the intervention, and the feedback and learning from the intervention.
- SNA can also help to compare and contrast different network interventions and to assess their cost-effectiveness and sustainability.
