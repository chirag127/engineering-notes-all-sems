# Ontological representation of social relationships

- Ontology is a formal and explicit specification of a shared conceptualization of a domain of interest .
- Ontological representation of social relationships is a method of describing and modeling the types, properties, and patterns of social interactions among individuals or groups in a social network  .
- Ontological representation of social relationships can help to:
  - Support the automated integration of social information on a semantic basis .
  - Capture established concepts in social network analysis, such as centrality, density, cohesion, etc. .
  - Understand the social structure and its effects on individual and collective behavior .
- Ontological representation of social relationships can be done at different levels of abstraction and granularity, depending on the purpose and context of the analysis .
- One example of ontological representation of social relationships is the Friend of a Friend (FOAF) ontology, which is a popular method in applications such as the Semantic Web .
  - FOAF describes social relationships as RDF statements with a subject and an object of type foaf:Person and a predicate that is a subproperty of the foaf:knows relationship .
  - FOAF allows for the expression of various kinds of social relationships, such as acquaintance, colleague, friend, family, etc. .
  - FOAF also allows for the description of other aspects of a person, such as name, gender, interests, etc. .
- Another example of ontological representation of social relationships is the social relationship representation model proposed by , which consists of four layers:
  - Layer 1 is the fundamental, where each social network application has its relationship data represented by ontology and conceptual graph .
  - Layer 2 is the integration, where relevant social relationship datasets or user concerns are integrated into a united representation form based on the context .
  - Layer 3 is the analysis, where social network analysis methods are applied to the integrated social relationship data to extract useful information and knowledge .
  - Layer 4 is the visualization, where the results of the analysis are presented in an intuitive and interactive way to the users .
