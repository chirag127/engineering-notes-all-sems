# Web-based networks for the notes of the Unit 2 - Web Semantics in Social Network Applications in the subject of SOCIAL NETWORK ANALYTICS

- Web-based networks are networks that use the web as a platform for connecting people and organizations, and for sharing and distributing information.
- Web semantics is the study of the meaning and structure of web data, such as web pages, hyperlinks, tags, and social media posts.
- Social network applications are applications that use web-based networks to enable social interactions, communication, collaboration, and information exchange among users.
- Social network analytics is the field of data analytics that uses web semantics and network science to understand and analyze the patterns, dynamics, and outcomes of social network applications.

Some of the topics covered in this unit are:

- The concepts and principles of web semantics, such as ontologies, RDF, SPARQL, and Linked Data.
- The methods and tools for extracting, representing, querying, and reasoning with web semantic data.
- The types and characteristics of web-based networks, such as social media networks, web hyperlink networks, and folksonomies.
- The techniques and algorithms for social network analysis, such as centrality, community detection, influence, and diffusion.
- The applications and challenges of social network analytics, such as social media marketing, online reputation management, recommendation systems, and fake news detection.
