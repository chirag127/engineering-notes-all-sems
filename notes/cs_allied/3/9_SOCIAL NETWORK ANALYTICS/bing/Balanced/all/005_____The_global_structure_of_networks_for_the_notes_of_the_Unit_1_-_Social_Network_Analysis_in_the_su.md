# The global structure of networks

- Social network analysis (SNA) is a general study of the social network using network and graph theory concepts  .
- A social network is a set of actors (nodes) and the relationships (edges) between them  .
- SNA aims to understand the behavior of individuals, the pattern of relationships, and the interactions between the two levels .
- SNA can be applied to various domains, such as health, business, education, politics, and culture   .
- SNA can reveal the structure of a social network, such as its size, density, centrality, clustering, and community detection    .
- The structure of a social network can influence and be influenced by various factors, such as information diffusion, social influence, innovation, collaboration, and trust     .
- SNA can also compare the structure of different social networks, such as across time, space, or groups    .
- SNA can use various methods and tools, such as surveys, interviews, observations, experiments, simulations, and software     .
