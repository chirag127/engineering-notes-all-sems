# GraphUtil for the notes of the Unit 4 - Developing Social-Semantic Applications

- GraphUtil is a generic term for any tool or library that can help with creating, manipulating, analyzing, and visualizing graphs or networks.
- Graphs or networks are data structures that consist of nodes (also called vertices or entities) and edges (also called links or relationships) that connect the nodes.
- Graphs can be used to model various types of social phenomena, such as social networks, information diffusion, community detection, influence analysis, and more.
- Graph analysis techniques can be divided into two categories: structural and semantic.
  - Structural analysis focuses on the shape and topology of the graph, such as the degree, centrality, clustering, and distance of the nodes and edges.
  - Semantic analysis focuses on the meaning and content of the nodes and edges, such as the attributes, labels, types, and weights of the nodes and edges.
- Graph analysis techniques can also be divided into two levels: micro and macro.
  - Micro analysis focuses on the properties and behaviors of individual nodes and edges, such as the role, status, and activity of the nodes and edges.
  - Macro analysis focuses on the properties and behaviors of the whole graph or subgraphs, such as the size, density, modularity, and dynamics of the graph or subgraphs.
- Graph analysis techniques can be applied to various types of graphs, such as directed or undirected, weighted or unweighted, simple or complex, static or dynamic, and homogeneous or heterogeneous graphs.
- Graph analysis techniques can be implemented using various tools and libraries, such as Python NetworkX, Gephi, Graphviz, igraph, and more.
- Graph analysis techniques can be used for various purposes, such as exploring, describing, explaining, predicting, and optimizing the social phenomena modeled by the graphs.
