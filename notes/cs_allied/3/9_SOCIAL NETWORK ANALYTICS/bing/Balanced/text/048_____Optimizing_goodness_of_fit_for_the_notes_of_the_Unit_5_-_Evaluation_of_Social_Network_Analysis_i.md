### Optimizing goodness of fit for the notes of the Unit 5 - Evaluation of Social Network Analysis

- Goodness of fit is a measure of how well the observed data correspond to the fitted or assumed model.
- In social network analysis, goodness of fit can be used to compare different models of network formation or evolution, such as exponential random graph models (ERGMs), stochastic actor-oriented models (SAOMs), or latent space models (LSMs).
- Goodness of fit can also be used to assess the validity and reliability of network measures, such as centrality, density, clustering, or homophily.
- Some common methods to evaluate goodness of fit are:

  - Visual inspection of the fitted curve or model against the observed data, using plots such as scatter plots, histograms, or Q-Q plots.
  - Statistical tests, such as chi-square test, Kolmogorov-Smirnov test, or likelihood ratio test, that compare the observed and expected frequencies or probabilities of network properties or outcomes.
  - Information criteria, such as Akaike information criterion (AIC), Bayesian information criterion (BIC), or deviance information criterion (DIC), that balance the fit and complexity of the model, and allow for model selection and comparison.
  - Diagnostic statistics, such as residuals, leverage, influence, or Cook's distance, that identify outliers, influential observations, or sources of misfit in the data or model.

- To optimize goodness of fit, one should:

  - Choose an appropriate model that reflects the underlying network processes and assumptions, and that can capture the salient features and patterns of the data.
  - Estimate the model parameters using suitable methods, such as maximum likelihood estimation (MLE), Markov chain Monte Carlo (MCMC), or generalized method of moments (GMM), that ensure consistency, efficiency, and robustness of the estimates.
  - Check the model fit using multiple methods and criteria, and compare the results with alternative models or benchmarks, such as null models, random graphs, or empirical networks.
  - Refine the model by adding, removing, or modifying the model terms, variables, or constraints, and re-estimate and re-evaluate the model fit until a satisfactory level of fit is achieved.
