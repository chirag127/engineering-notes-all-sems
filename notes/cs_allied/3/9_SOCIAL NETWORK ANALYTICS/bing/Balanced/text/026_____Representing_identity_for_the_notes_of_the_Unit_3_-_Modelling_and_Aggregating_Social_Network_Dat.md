### Representing identity for the notes of the Unit 3 - Modelling and Aggregating Social Network Data in the subject of SOCIAL NETWORK ANALYTICS

- Identity is the set of attributes and characteristics that define a person or an entity in a social network.
- Identity can be represented by nodes in a graph, where each node has a unique identifier and a set of attributes that describe its features.
- Identity can also be inferred from the relationships and interactions between nodes, such as the type, frequency, and strength of the links.
- Identity can be influenced by the social context and the network structure, such as the position, role, and centrality of the nodes.
- Identity can be dynamic and evolving over time, as nodes can change their attributes and relationships in response to external events or internal motivations.
- Identity can be analyzed using various methods and techniques, such as network visualization, network metrics, network models, and network algorithms.
- Identity can be used for various applications and purposes, such as understanding user behavior, detecting communities, predicting outcomes, and recommending actions.
