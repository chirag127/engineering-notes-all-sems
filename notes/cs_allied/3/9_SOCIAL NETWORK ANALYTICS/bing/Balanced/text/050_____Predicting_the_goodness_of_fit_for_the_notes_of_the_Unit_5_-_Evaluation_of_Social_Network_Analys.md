### Predicting the goodness of fit for the notes of the Unit 5 - Evaluation of Social Network Analysis

- Social network analysis (SNA) is a method of studying the patterns and structures of relationships between individuals or groups in a network.
- Evaluation of SNA involves assessing how well a given model or hypothesis fits the observed network data, and how well it explains the network phenomena of interest.
- Goodness of fit (GOF) is a measure of how closely the observed network data match the expected network data under a certain model or hypothesis.
- GOF can be tested using various statistical methods, such as chi-square tests, likelihood ratio tests, or bootstrap methods.
- GOF can be assessed for different aspects of the network structure, such as degree distribution, clustering, reciprocity, transitivity, homophily, or centrality.
- GOF can help to compare different models or hypotheses, to select the best one, or to identify the limitations or assumptions of a model or hypothesis.
- GOF can also help to diagnose potential problems or errors in the network data collection or analysis, such as missing data, measurement error, or sampling bias.
