### Blogs and online communities

- Blogs are online journals or informational websites that offer regularly updated content (blog posts) about a topic.
- Blogs are written in an informal or conversational style and present information in reverse chronological order.
- Blogs can be run by an individual, group, or corporation.
- Blogs are among the major platforms on which online communities form.
- Online communities are groups of people with a shared interest or purpose who use the internet to communicate with each other.
- Online communities can use various tools and platforms, such as forums, message boards, social media, wikis, podcasts, etc.
- Online communities provide more robust information resources, allowing users to browse files, videos, and blogs, then interpret them with other community members or experts through two-way conversations.
- Online communities can have their own set of guidelines and needs, such as online community engagement, moderation, and management.
- Blogging communities are collections of individual blogs tied together by a larger common value or theme.
- Blogging communities can have conversations on the individual blogs, between the blogs, on a common message board, or across other blogs in the blogosphere.
- Blogging communities can help bloggers to network, share ideas, learn from each other, and increase their visibility and reach.
