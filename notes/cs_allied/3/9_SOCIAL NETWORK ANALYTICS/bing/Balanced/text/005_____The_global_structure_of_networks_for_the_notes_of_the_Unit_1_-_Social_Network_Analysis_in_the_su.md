### The global structure of networks

- A social network is a set of actors (such as individuals, groups, organizations, or other entities) and the relationships (such as friendship, collaboration, communication, or influence) among them .
- Social network analysis (SNA) is the study of the structure and dynamics of social networks, using methods from graph theory, statistics, sociology, and psychology .
- The global structure of a network refers to the overall pattern of relationships among the actors, such as the density, centralization, clustering, and heterogeneity of the network .
- The global structure of a network can reveal important properties and functions of the network, such as the diffusion of information, the emergence of norms, the formation of groups, and the distribution of power and resources .
- The global structure of a network can be measured and compared using various metrics and models, such as the degree distribution, the average path length, the diameter, the modularity, the assortativity, and the small-world and scale-free properties .
- The global structure of a network can also be influenced by various factors, such as the size, composition, and history of the network, the type and strength of the relationships, and the external environment and context of the network .
