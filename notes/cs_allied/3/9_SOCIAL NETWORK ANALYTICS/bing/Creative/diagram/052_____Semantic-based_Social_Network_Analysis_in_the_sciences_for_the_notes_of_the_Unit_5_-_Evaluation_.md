### Semantic-based Social Network Analysis

- Semantic-based Social Network Analysis (SNA) is a method that combines semantic analysis and network analysis to study social data and contents.
- Semantic analysis is the process of extracting and representing the meaning and structure of social data and contents, such as text, images, videos, relation types, and quantitative or qualitative data  .
- Network analysis is the process of identifying and measuring the patterns and structures of social networks, such as nodes, links, clusters, communities, centrality, and diffusion  .
- Semantic-based SNA aims to provide a more comprehensive and accurate understanding of social phenomena and behavior by integrating the semantic and network perspectives .
- Semantic-based SNA can be applied to various domains and scenarios, such as social media, online communities, e-commerce, e-learning, e-government, and e-health .
- Semantic-based SNA faces some challenges and opportunities, such as:
  - How to acquire and represent spatio-temporal data for social network search?
  - How to model and analyze cross-media big data in social networks ?
  - How to use deep semantic learning to enhance social network analysis?
  - How to design and implement indexing and ranking mechanisms for social network search?
  - How to define and evaluate hybrid approaches that combine semantic and network analysis ?
