# SOCIAL NETWORK ANALYTICS

- Social network analytics is the application of social network analysis (SNA) to social media data.
- SNA is a process of quantitative and qualitative analysis of a social network, which measures and maps the flow of relationships and relationship changes between knowledge-possessing entities .
- Social media data consists of user-generated content and interactions on various platforms, such as Facebook, Twitter, Instagram, YouTube, etc.
- Social network analytics can be used for various purposes, such as:
  - Understanding the structure, dynamics, and influence of social networks.
  - Identifying key actors, communities, and topics in social networks.
  - Measuring the sentiment, opinion, and emotion of social media users.
  - Detecting trends, patterns, and anomalies in social media data.
  - Evaluating the effectiveness and impact of social media campaigns and strategies.
- Social network analytics involves the following steps:
  - Data collection: Gathering and storing social media data from various sources and platforms.
  - Data preprocessing: Cleaning, filtering, and transforming social media data into a suitable format for analysis.
  - Data analysis: Applying SNA methods and techniques to social media data, such as network visualization, centrality measures, community detection, sentiment analysis, topic modeling, etc.
  - Data interpretation: Extracting insights and knowledge from the results of data analysis, and communicating them to stakeholders and decision-makers.
