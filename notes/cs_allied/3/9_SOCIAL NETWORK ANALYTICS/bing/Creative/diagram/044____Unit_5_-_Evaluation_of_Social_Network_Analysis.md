## Unit 5 - Evaluation of Social Network Analysis

- Social network analysis (SNA) is the process of investigating social structures through the use of network and graph theories.
- SNA can be used to study various phenomena, such as communication, collaboration, influence, trust, power, diffusion, and innovation, among social actors.
- SNA can be applied to different domains, such as sociology, psychology, anthropology, economics, politics, education, health, and computer science.
- SNA can be performed at different levels of analysis, such as individual, group, organization, community, or society.
- SNA can be conducted using different methods, such as surveys, interviews, observations, experiments, or online data collection.
- SNA can be visualized using different tools, such as graphs, matrices, maps, or dashboards.
- SNA can be evaluated using different criteria, such as validity, reliability, generalizability, usability, and impact.

### Evaluation of SNA

- Evaluation of SNA is the process of assessing the quality, usefulness, and effectiveness of SNA for a specific purpose, context, and audience.
- Evaluation of SNA can be done by different stakeholders, such as researchers, practitioners, clients, users, or funders.
- Evaluation of SNA can be done at different stages, such as before, during, or after the SNA project.
- Evaluation of SNA can be done using different methods, such as feedback, reviews, ratings, tests, or metrics.
- Evaluation of SNA can be done using different standards, such as criteria, indicators, benchmarks, or best practices.

### Challenges of Evaluating SNA

- Evaluating SNA can be challenging due to the following reasons:
  - SNA is a complex and multidisciplinary field that requires a comprehensive and integrative approach to evaluation.
  - SNA is a dynamic and evolving field that requires a flexible and adaptive approach to evaluation.
  - SNA is a context-dependent and audience-specific field that requires a tailored and customized approach to evaluation.
  - SNA is a value-laden and ethical field that requires a transparent and responsible approach to evaluation.
