### Web-based networks for the notes of the Unit 2 - Web Semantics in Social Network Applications in the subject of SOCIAL NETWORK ANALYTICS

- Web-based networks are networks that are hosted on the web and connect people and organizations through various online platforms and channels.
- Web semantics is the study of the meaning and structure of the data and information on the web, such as web pages, hyperlinks, tags, metadata, etc.
- Social network applications are applications that use web-based networks to enable social interactions, communication, collaboration, and information sharing among users.
- Social network analytics is the field of data analytics that uses web semantics and network science to understand and analyze the patterns, dynamics, and outcomes of social network applications.
- Some of the topics covered in this unit are:

  - Web-based social network models: These are mathematical and computational models that represent the structure and properties of web-based networks, such as degree distribution, clustering coefficient, network diameter, etc.
  - Web-based social network analysis: These are methods and techniques that measure and visualize the characteristics and behaviors of web-based networks, such as centrality, community detection, network evolution, etc.
  - Web-based social network mining: These are approaches and algorithms that extract and discover useful and actionable knowledge from web-based networks, such as link prediction, recommendation systems, sentiment analysis, etc.
  - Web-based social network applications: These are examples and case studies of how web-based networks can be used for various purposes and domains, such as e-commerce, education, health, politics, etc.
