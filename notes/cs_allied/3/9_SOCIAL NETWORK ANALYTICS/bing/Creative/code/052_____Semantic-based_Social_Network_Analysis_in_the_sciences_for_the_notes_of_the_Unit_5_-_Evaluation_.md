### Semantic-based Social Network Analysis in the sciences

- Semantic-based Social Network Analysis (SNA) is a method of analyzing social data and contents using semantic techniques, such as natural language processing, ontology engineering, and machine learning .
- Semantic-based SNA aims to extract sense and meaning from social data and contents, such as text, relation types, and quantitative or qualitative data related to social content.
- Semantic-based SNA can help to organize, identify patterns and structures, and understand the meanings of social data and contents, especially in the context of big data and cross-media data .
- Semantic-based SNA can be applied to various domains and disciplines, such as sociology, psychology, communication, education, health, politics, and business .
- Semantic-based SNA can provide new insights and perspectives for social network research, such as discovering latent communities, detecting influential actors, predicting behaviors, and recommending actions .
- Semantic-based SNA can also benefit from the integration of other methods and approaches, such as graph theory, network science, social simulation, and artificial intelligence .
- Semantic-based SNA faces some challenges and limitations, such as the quality and validity of social data and contents, the scalability and efficiency of semantic techniques, and the ethical and legal issues of social data and contents .
