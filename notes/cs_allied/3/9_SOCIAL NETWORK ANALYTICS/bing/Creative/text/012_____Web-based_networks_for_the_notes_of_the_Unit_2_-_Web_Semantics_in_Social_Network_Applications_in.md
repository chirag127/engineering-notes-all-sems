### Web-based networks for the notes of the Unit 2 - Web Semantics in Social Network Applications in the subject of SOCIAL NETWORK ANALYTICS

- Web-based networks are networks that are hosted on the web and connect people and organizations through various online platforms and channels.
- Web semantics is the study of the meaning and structure of the data and information on the web, such as web pages, hyperlinks, tags, hashtags, keywords, etc.
- Social network applications are applications that use web-based networks to enable social interactions, communication, collaboration, and information sharing among users and groups.
- Social network analytics is the field of data analytics that uses web semantics and network science to understand and analyze the patterns, dynamics, and outcomes of social network applications.
- Some of the topics and concepts covered in this unit are:

  - Web-based social network models: These are mathematical and computational models that represent the structure and properties of web-based networks, such as degree distribution, clustering coefficient, diameter, centrality, modularity, etc.
  - Web-based social network analysis: These are methods and techniques that use web-based social network models to measure, visualize, and interpret the characteristics and behaviors of web-based networks, such as network density, network cohesion, network diversity, network influence, network diffusion, etc.
  - Web-based social network mining: These are methods and techniques that use web-based social network analysis to discover and extract useful and actionable insights and knowledge from web-based networks, such as community detection, link prediction, recommendation systems, sentiment analysis, topic modeling, etc.
  - Web-based social network applications: These are examples and case studies of how web-based social network mining can be applied to various domains and scenarios, such as online marketing, online education, online health, online politics, online entertainment, etc.

- Some of the tools and resources that can be used for web-based social network analytics are:

  - Netlytic: A cloud-based social network analyzer that can automatically summarize textual data and discover communication networks from publicly accessible social media posts.
  - Hootsuite Analytics: A social media analytics tool that can track metrics from all major networks in one dashboard, such as reach, engagement, sentiment, conversions, etc.
  - IBM Watson Analytics for Social Media: A social media analytics tool that can use natural language processing and machine learning to understand and visualize social media data, such as trends, topics, opinions, emotions, etc.
