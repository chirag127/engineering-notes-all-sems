### Determining Equality

In the context of Unit 3 - Modelling and Aggregating Social Network Data in the subject of SOCIAL NETWORK ANALYTICS, determining equality refers to the process of identifying whether two or more social network data objects are equivalent. This can be done through various methods, including:

1. **Attribute comparison:** This method involves comparing the attributes of the data objects to determine if they are equal. For example, if two nodes in a social network have the same attributes (e.g., age, gender, location), they may be considered equal.

2. **Structural comparison:** This method involves comparing the structure of the data objects to determine if they are equal. For example, if two nodes in a social network have the same connections to other nodes, they may be considered equal.

3. **Behavioral comparison:** This method involves comparing the behavior of the data objects to determine if they are equal. For example, if two nodes in a social network exhibit the same patterns of interaction with other nodes, they may be considered equal.

It is important to note that the method used to determine equality may vary depending on the specific context and goals of the analysis. Additionally, multiple methods may be used in combination to provide a more comprehensive assessment of equality.
