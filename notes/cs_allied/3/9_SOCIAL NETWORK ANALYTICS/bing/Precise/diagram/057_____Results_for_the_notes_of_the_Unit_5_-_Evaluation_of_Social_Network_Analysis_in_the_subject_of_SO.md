### Results for the notes of the Unit 5 - Evaluation of Social Network Analysis in the subject of SOCIAL NETWORK ANALYTICS

1. Social Network Analysis (SNA) is an approach for examining the relationships among individuals, groups, organizations or other entities within a specified network through the use of networks and graph theory .
2. SNA can be used in evaluations when the quality, frequency, and nature of interaction among partners and stakeholders are seen as key to the achievement of outcomes .
3. The main questions SNA can help answer are how individuals, groups, or other entities within a specified network are connected .
4. SNA is a quantitative approach to measure the strength and types of relationships within a network. It examines how different players relate to each other and assesses the characteristics of those relationships .
5. An SNA focuses on network structure, rather than the individual characteristics of the partners .
