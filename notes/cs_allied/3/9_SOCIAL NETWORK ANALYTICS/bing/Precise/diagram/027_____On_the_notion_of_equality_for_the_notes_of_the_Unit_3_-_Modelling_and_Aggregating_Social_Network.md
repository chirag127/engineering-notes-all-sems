### On the notion of equality for the notes of the Unit 3 - Modelling and Aggregating Social Network Data in the subject of SOCIAL NETWORK ANALYTICS

1. Equality is a concept that refers to the state of being equal, or having the same rights, opportunities, and status as others.
2. In the context of social network analytics, equality can refer to the equal distribution of resources, opportunities, and information among members of a network.
3. One way to measure equality in a network is to analyze the distribution of centrality measures, such as degree centrality, betweenness centrality, and eigenvector centrality.
4. Another way to measure equality is to analyze the distribution of resources, such as the distribution of wealth or power, among members of a network.
5. Inequality in a network can lead to negative outcomes, such as reduced social cohesion and increased conflict.
6. Promoting equality in a network can lead to positive outcomes, such as increased cooperation and collaboration among members.
7. There are various methods and techniques that can be used to promote equality in a network, such as redistributive policies and affirmative action programs.
8. It is important to note that the notion of equality is complex and can vary depending on the context and the perspective of the observer. Therefore, it is important to carefully define and operationalize the concept of equality when analyzing social network data.
