# Unit 5 - Evaluation of Social Network Analysis
## Semantic-based Social Network Analysis in the sciences

- Semantic network analysis is particularly useful today with the increasing volumes of text-based information available. It is one of the developing, cutting-edge methods to organize, identify patterns and structures, and understand the meanings of our information society .
- In social studies of science, research fields have been considered as self-organized communities, also referred to as invisible colleges.
- Recently smaller groups with shared research interests have been addressed as virtual teams.
- Semantic Network Analysis in Social Sciences introduces the fundamentals of semantic network analysis and its applications in the social sciences.
