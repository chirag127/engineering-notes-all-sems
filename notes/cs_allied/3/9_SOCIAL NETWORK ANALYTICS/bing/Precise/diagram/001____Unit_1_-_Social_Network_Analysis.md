## Unit 1 - Social Network Analysis

Social Network Analysis (SNA) is a research technique that focuses on identifying and comparing the relationships within and between individuals, groups, and organizations.

1. **Concepts and Measures**: SNA involves the use of various concepts and measures to analyze social networks, including centrality, density, and clustering coefficient.
2. **Data Collection**: Data for SNA can be collected through various methods, including surveys, interviews, and observation.
3. **Visualization**: SNA often involves the use of visualization tools to represent social networks graphically, making it easier to identify patterns and relationships.
4. **Applications**: SNA has a wide range of applications, including in fields such as sociology, anthropology, and business.
5. **Limitations**: Like any research technique, SNA has its limitations, including the potential for incomplete or inaccurate data and the difficulty of establishing causality.
