### Electronic sources for network analysis

Electronic sources for network analysis are essential for the study of social network applications. These sources provide data and information that can be used to analyze and understand the structure and behavior of social networks. Some of the electronic sources for network analysis include:

1. **Social media platforms:** Social media platforms such as Facebook, Twitter, and LinkedIn provide a wealth of data on social interactions and connections. These platforms can be used to collect data on user behavior, network structure, and communication patterns.

2. **Online forums and discussion boards:** Online forums and discussion boards are another source of data for network analysis. These platforms provide a space for users to discuss and share information on a wide range of topics. The data collected from these platforms can be used to analyze the structure and behavior of online communities.

3. **Blogs and news websites:** Blogs and news websites provide a rich source of data on public opinion and social trends. By analyzing the content and comments on these websites, researchers can gain insights into the attitudes and opinions of the public on a wide range of issues.

4. **Academic databases:** Academic databases such as JSTOR and Google Scholar provide access to a wide range of scholarly articles and research papers on social network analysis. These databases can be used to find the latest research and developments in the field.

These are just a few examples of the electronic sources that can be used for network analysis in the study of social network applications. By using these sources, researchers can collect and analyze data to better understand the structure and behavior of social networks.
