# Representation for the notes of the Unit 5 - Evaluation of Social Network Analysis in the subject of SOCIAL NETWORK ANALYTICS

1. Evaluation of Social Network Analysis (SNA) involves assessing the effectiveness and impact of SNA techniques and methods.
2. Evaluation can be done through various methods such as surveys, interviews, and case studies.
3. It is important to evaluate SNA to ensure that the techniques and methods used are appropriate and effective for the specific context and goals.
4. Evaluation can also help identify areas for improvement and guide future research and development in the field of SNA.
5. Some common metrics used in the evaluation of SNA include network density, centrality measures, and clustering coefficients.
6. It is important to consider both quantitative and qualitative measures when evaluating SNA.
7. Evaluation of SNA can provide valuable insights into the dynamics and structure of social networks, and can inform decision-making and policy development.
