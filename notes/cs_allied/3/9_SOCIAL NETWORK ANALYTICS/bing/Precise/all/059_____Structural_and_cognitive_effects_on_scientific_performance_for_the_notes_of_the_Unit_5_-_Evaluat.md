### Structural and cognitive effects on scientific performance

Unit 5 - Evaluation of Social Network Analysis in the subject of SOCIAL NETWORK ANALYTICS

1. Structural effects refer to the impact of the network structure on the performance of scientists. This includes the position of the scientist in the network, the density of the network, and the presence of structural holes.
2. Cognitive effects refer to the impact of the cognitive characteristics of scientists on their performance. This includes their level of expertise, their ability to learn and adapt, and their creativity.
3. Both structural and cognitive effects can influence the scientific performance of individuals and groups.
4. The position of a scientist in the network can affect their access to information and resources, which can impact their performance.
5. The density of the network can affect the flow of information and the level of collaboration, which can also impact performance.
6. Structural holes, or gaps in the network, can provide opportunities for innovation and the introduction of new ideas.
7. The level of expertise of a scientist can affect their ability to understand and contribute to their field.
8. The ability to learn and adapt is important for scientists to stay current in their field and to continue to make contributions.
9. Creativity is important for the generation of new ideas and the advancement of scientific knowledge.
10. Social network analysis can be used to evaluate the structural and cognitive effects on scientific performance.
