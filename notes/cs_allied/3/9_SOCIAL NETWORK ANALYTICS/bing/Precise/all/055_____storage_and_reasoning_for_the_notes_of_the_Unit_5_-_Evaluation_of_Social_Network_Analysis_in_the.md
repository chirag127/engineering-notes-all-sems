# Storage and Reasoning

## Unit 5 - Evaluation of Social Network Analysis

### Social Network Analytics

- Social network analysis (SNA) is the process of investigating social structures through the use of network and graph theories.
- It characterizes networked structures in terms of nodes (individual actors, people, or things within the network) and the ties, edges, or links (relationships or interactions) that connect them.
- SNA can be used to evaluate the effectiveness of social networks by analyzing the relationships between individuals or groups within the network.
- This can provide valuable insights into the dynamics of the network, including the flow of information, the distribution of power, and the identification of key influencers.
- SNA can also be used to identify potential areas for improvement within the network, such as increasing connectivity or reducing bottlenecks.
- The storage and reasoning of data in SNA is critical for the accurate evaluation of the network.
- Data storage refers to the methods and technologies used to store and manage the data collected during the analysis.
- This can include the use of databases, data warehouses, and data lakes to store and organize the data.
- Reasoning refers to the process of drawing conclusions and making decisions based on the data.
- This can involve the use of statistical methods, machine learning algorithms, and other analytical techniques to identify patterns and relationships within the data.
- The effective storage and reasoning of data is essential for the accurate evaluation of social networks and the identification of potential areas for improvement.
