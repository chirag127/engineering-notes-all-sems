### Representation for the notes of the Unit 5 - Evaluation of Social Network Analysis in the subject of SOCIAL NETWORK ANALYTICS

1. Social Network Analysis (SNA) is an approach for examining the relationships among individuals, groups, organizations or other entities within a specified network through the use of networks and graph theory .
2. SNA helps the understanding of phenomena that emerge from the interaction of individuals or institutions .
3. SNA is a powerful method that centralizes individuals and their relations, in that the effect of the individual on the social network can be uncovered and the network of individual groups can be evaluated holistically .
4. SNA shows the structural gaps and social capital in institutions, and focuses managers' attention on critical informal networks .
5. Social network analysis in general studies the behavior of the individual at the micro level, the pattern of relationships (network structure) at the macro level, and the interactions between the two .
6. Social networks are both the cause of and the result of individual behavior .
7. An SNA is a quantitative approach to measure the strength and types of relationships within a network .
8. It examines how different players relate to each other and assesses the characteristics of those relationships .
9. An SNA focuses on network structure, rather than the individual characteristics of the partners .
10. SNA is a powerful technique to study how social relationships within a network are established and evolve .
11. SNA is used in various phases of complex healthcare intervention research and has value for developing and evaluating complex healthcare interventions .
