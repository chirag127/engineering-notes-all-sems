# SOCIAL NETWORK ANALYTICS

Social Network Analysis (SNA) is the process of investigating social structures through the use of networks and graph theory. It characterizes networked structures in terms of nodes (individual actors, people, or things within the network) and the ties, edges, or links (relationships or interactions) that connect them .

- SNA has emerged as a key technique in modern sociology and has gained significant popularity in various fields such as anthropology, biology, demography, communication studies, economics, geography, history, information science, organizational studies, political science, public health, social psychology, development studies, sociolinguistics, and computer science .

- Management consultants use SNA to map their business relations and further investigate their mutual relationships .

- Social media analytics helps companies address customer experiences and use them to spot trends related to offerings and brands, understand conversations, derive customer sentiment towards products and services, and gauge response to social media and other marketing efforts .

- SNA techniques can also be applied to networks outside of the societal realm .
