## Unit 3 - Modelling and Aggregating Social Network Data

1. **Introduction to Social Network Data:** Social network data refers to the information that is generated through the interactions and relationships between individuals or entities within a social network. This data can be used to model and analyze the structure and behavior of the network.

2. **Data Collection:** Social network data can be collected through various methods such as surveys, observation, and data mining. The choice of method depends on the research question and the availability of data.

3. **Data Representation:** Social network data can be represented using graphs, where nodes represent individuals or entities and edges represent relationships or interactions between them. Graphs can be directed or undirected, weighted or unweighted, depending on the nature of the relationships.

4. **Data Aggregation:** Social network data can be aggregated to provide a summary of the network structure and behavior. Common aggregation methods include calculating network-level metrics such as density, centrality, and clustering coefficient, as well as community detection algorithms to identify groups of closely connected nodes.

5. **Data Analysis:** Social network data can be analyzed using various methods such as network visualization, statistical modeling, and machine learning. The choice of method depends on the research question and the nature of the data.

6. **Applications:** Social network data has numerous applications in fields such as sociology, marketing, and epidemiology. It can be used to study the spread of information, the formation of social groups, and the influence of social networks on behavior.
