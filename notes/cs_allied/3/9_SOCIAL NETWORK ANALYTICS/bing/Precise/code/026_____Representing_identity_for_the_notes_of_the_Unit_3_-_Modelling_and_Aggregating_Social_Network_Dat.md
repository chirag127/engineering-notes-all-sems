### Representing identity for the notes of the Unit 3 - Modelling and Aggregating Social Network Data in the subject of SOCIAL NETWORK ANALYTICS

- Identity representation is a crucial aspect of social network analysis.
- It refers to the way individuals or entities are represented within the network.
- Identity can be represented using various attributes such as name, age, gender, location, occupation, interests, etc.
- These attributes can be used to create a profile for each individual or entity in the network.
- The choice of attributes used to represent identity can have a significant impact on the analysis of the network.
- For example, if the analysis focuses on the professional relationships within the network, occupation and work-related attributes may be more relevant than personal attributes such as age or gender.
- Identity representation can also be influenced by the data collection methods used. For example, if data is collected through surveys, the attributes used to represent identity may be limited by the questions asked in the survey.
- In summary, representing identity is an important step in social network analysis and the choice of attributes used can have a significant impact on the results of the analysis.
