### Network Analysis

Network analysis is a method used to study the relationships between entities in a network. It is commonly used in the field of social network analysis to study the interactions between individuals, groups, and organizations.

In Unit 1 of the subject of Social Network Analytics, the focus is on Social Network Analysis. Here are some key points to consider:

1. Social Network Analysis (SNA) is the process of investigating social structures through the use of network and graph theories.
2. SNA is used to study the relationships between individuals, groups, and organizations.
3. SNA can be used to identify key players, subgroups, and the overall structure of a network.
4. SNA can also be used to study the flow of information, resources, and influence within a network.
5. SNA can be applied to a wide range of fields, including sociology, anthropology, psychology, and business.

These are some of the key points to consider when studying Network Analysis in the context of Social Network Analytics. It is important to have a strong understanding of these concepts in order to effectively analyze and understand social networks.
