### Determining Equality for the Notes of the Unit 3 - Modelling and Aggregating Social Network Data in the Subject of Social Network Analytics

1. Equality in social network data can be determined by comparing the attributes and relationships of the nodes in the network.
2. One way to determine equality is to use graph isomorphism, which checks if two graphs are structurally the same, regardless of the labels of the nodes.
3. Another way to determine equality is to use node similarity measures, such as Jaccard similarity or cosine similarity, to compare the attributes and relationships of the nodes.
4. Aggregation methods, such as clustering or community detection, can also be used to group similar nodes together and determine equality within the groups.
5. It is important to consider the context and purpose of the analysis when determining equality in social network data, as different methods may be more appropriate for different situations.
