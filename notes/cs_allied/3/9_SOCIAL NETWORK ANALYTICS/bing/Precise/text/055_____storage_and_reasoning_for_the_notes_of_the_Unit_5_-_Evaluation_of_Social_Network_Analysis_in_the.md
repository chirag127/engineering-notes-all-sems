### Storage and Reasoning for the Notes of the Unit 5 - Evaluation of Social Network Analysis in the Subject of Social Network Analytics

1. Storage refers to the process of saving and organizing data in a way that it can be easily accessed and retrieved for future use.
2. In the context of Social Network Analysis, storage is important for keeping track of the data collected from social networks, such as user interactions, relationships, and attributes.
3. Reasoning refers to the process of drawing conclusions and making decisions based on the available data and information.
4. In Social Network Analysis, reasoning is used to analyze the data collected from social networks and derive insights about the relationships and interactions between users.
5. Evaluation of Social Network Analysis involves assessing the effectiveness and accuracy of the methods and techniques used in the analysis.
6. This can be done by comparing the results of the analysis with known information or by using statistical methods to measure the accuracy of the analysis.
7. Proper storage and reasoning are essential for the successful evaluation of Social Network Analysis, as they ensure that the data is organized and analyzed in a rigorous and systematic manner.
