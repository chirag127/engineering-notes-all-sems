### Representation for the notes of the Unit 5 - Evaluation of Social Network Analysis in the subject of SOCIAL NETWORK ANALYTICS

1. Social Network Analysis (SNA) is a method for studying social structures through the use of network and graph theories.
2. It involves the mapping and measuring of relationships and flows between people, groups, organizations, computers, or other information/knowledge processing entities.
3. Evaluation of SNA involves assessing the effectiveness and efficiency of the methods and techniques used in the analysis.
4. This can include evaluating the accuracy and validity of the data collected, the appropriateness of the analytical methods used, and the usefulness and relevance of the results obtained.
5. Evaluation can also involve comparing the results of SNA with other methods of analysis or with established theories and models.
6. It is important to conduct evaluation in order to ensure that SNA is being used effectively and to identify areas for improvement and further development.
7. There are several approaches to evaluating SNA, including statistical methods, qualitative methods, and comparative methods.
8. The choice of evaluation method will depend on the specific goals and objectives of the analysis, as well as the resources and expertise available.
