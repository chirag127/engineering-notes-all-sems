### The Global Structure of Networks

1. The global structure of a network refers to the overall organization and patterns of connections between nodes in the network.
2. One way to analyze the global structure of a network is to examine its degree distribution, which shows the frequency of nodes with a given number of connections.
3. Another way to analyze the global structure of a network is to examine its clustering coefficient, which measures the extent to which nodes in the network tend to cluster together.
4. The global structure of a network can also be analyzed by examining its average path length, which measures the average number of steps it takes to get from one node to another in the network.
5. The global structure of a network can also be influenced by the presence of communities or groups of nodes that are more densely connected to each other than to the rest of the network.
6. The global structure of a network can also be influenced by the presence of hubs or highly connected nodes that play a central role in the network.
7. The global structure of a network can have important implications for the spread of information, the resilience of the network to disruptions, and the ability of the network to facilitate coordination and cooperation among its members.
