### Structural and cognitive effects on scientific performance for the notes of the Unit 5 - Evaluation of Social Network Analysis in the subject of SOCIAL NETWORK ANALYTICS

The structure and cognition of a scientific network can have a significant impact on its performance and productivity. Structural effects refer to the arrangement and organization of the network, including its size, density, and centrality measures. Cognitive effects refer to the knowledge, skills, and attitudes of the individuals within the network.

The following are some of the key structural effects on scientific performance:

1. Network size: A larger network can provide access to a wider range of expertise and resources, but can also lead to increased competition and decreased collaboration.

2. Network density: A dense network can facilitate information exchange and collaboration, but can also lead to increased competition and decreased innovation.

3. Centrality measures: High levels of centrality, such as high betweenness or closeness centrality, can indicate a high level of influence within the network, but can also lead to decreased collaboration and increased competition.

The following are some of the key cognitive effects on scientific performance:

1. Knowledge: Individuals with a high level of knowledge and expertise can contribute to the advancement of the field, but can also lead to increased competition and decreased collaboration.

2. Skills: Individuals with high levels of technical skills can contribute to the advancement of the field, but can also lead to increased competition and decreased collaboration.

3. Attitudes: Positive attitudes towards collaboration and sharing can lead to increased collaboration and innovation, while negative attitudes can lead to decreased collaboration and competition.

Overall, the structure and cognition of a scientific network can have a significant impact on its performance and productivity. Understanding these effects is important for the effective evaluation and management of scientific networks.
