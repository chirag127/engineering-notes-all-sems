### Evaluation of web-based social network extraction for the notes of the Unit 5 - Evaluation of Social Network Analysis in the subject of SOCIAL NETWORK ANALYTICS

Evaluation of web-based social network extraction refers to the process of assessing the accuracy and effectiveness of algorithms and tools used to extract social network information from the web. This is an important step in the field of social network analysis, as it helps to ensure that the extracted data is accurate and reliable.

There are several methods for evaluating web-based social network extraction, including:

1. Ground truth: This involves comparing the extracted data to a known and verified set of data to assess its accuracy.

2. Precision and recall: These metrics measure the accuracy of the extracted data, with precision indicating the proportion of correct data, and recall indicating the proportion of data that has been correctly extracted.

3. F-measure: This is a composite metric that combines precision and recall, and provides a single measure of the accuracy of the extracted data.

4. User study: This involves evaluating the extracted data with the help of human participants, allowing for a more subjective assessment of the accuracy and usefulness of the extracted data.

In general, the choice of evaluation method will depend on the specific requirements of the extraction task, and the resources available for the evaluation. However, it is important to use a systematic and rigorous evaluation process to ensure that the extracted data is accurate and reliable, and can be used to support meaningful social network analysis.
