### Development of Social Network Analysis for the Notes of Unit 1 - Social Network Analysis in the Subject of Social Network Analytics

Social Network Analysis (SNA) is a powerful technique used to study social structures and relationships among individuals or organizations. The development of SNA has been a gradual process over the years, with contributions from various fields such as mathematics, sociology, anthropology, and computer science. In this unit, we will explore the development of SNA and its applications in social network analytics.

Here are some key points about the development of social network analysis:

1. The origins of social network analysis can be traced back to the work of Jacob Moreno in the 1930s. Moreno developed the sociogram, a visual representation of social relationships, and used it to study communication patterns in groups.

2. In the 1950s and 1960s, sociologists such as Harrison White and Mark Granovetter developed mathematical models to analyze social networks. These models focused on the patterns of ties between individuals and the implications of those patterns for social behavior.

3. In the 1970s, the field of anthropology began to adopt SNA methods to study kinship networks and other social structures. Anthropologists such as John Barnes and Elizabeth Bott used SNA to explore the connections between individuals, groups, and institutions.

4. The development of computer technology in the 1980s and 1990s made it possible to analyze larger and more complex networks. Social network analysis software such as UCINET and Pajek were developed during this time, making it easier for researchers to analyze and visualize social networks.

5. Today, social network analysis is used in a wide range of fields, including sociology, anthropology, psychology, business, and marketing. It is used to study everything from online social networks to the spread of diseases through social contacts.

In conclusion, the development of social network analysis has been a gradual process over the years, with contributions from various fields. Today, SNA is a powerful tool used to study social structures and relationships, and its applications are widespread. Understanding the development of SNA is crucial to understanding its potential as a research tool in social network analytics.
