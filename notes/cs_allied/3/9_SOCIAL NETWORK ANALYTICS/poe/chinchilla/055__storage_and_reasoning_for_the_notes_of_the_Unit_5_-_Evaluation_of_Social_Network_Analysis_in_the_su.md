### Storage and Reasoning for the Notes of Unit 5 - Evaluation of Social Network Analysis in the Subject of Social Network Analytics

In this unit, we will discuss the evaluation of Social Network Analysis (SNA) and how it can be used to analyze social networks. Here are some important points to keep in mind when taking notes on this topic:

1. Evaluation of SNA is necessary because it helps researchers to assess the quality of a network analysis and the validity of its conclusions.

2. The two main types of evaluation are internal and external. Internal evaluation focuses on the quality of the data and the analysis, while external evaluation focuses on the relevance and usefulness of the analysis to the research question.

3. Internal evaluation can be done through visual inspection of the network, statistical analysis, and sensitivity analysis. Visual inspection involves looking at the network graph to identify patterns and outliers. Statistical analysis involves testing for statistical significance and correlation. Sensitivity analysis involves testing the robustness of the results by changing the parameters or assumptions of the analysis.

4. External evaluation can be done through expert review, peer review, and stakeholder review. Expert review involves seeking feedback from experts in the field. Peer review involves seeking feedback from peers who have expertise in the area of research. Stakeholder review involves seeking feedback from stakeholders who may be affected by the research.

5. Storage of SNA data is important because it allows researchers to keep track of the data and analysis over time. There are several options for storage, including spreadsheets, databases, and specialized software. It is important to choose a storage option that is appropriate for the size and complexity of the data.

6. Reasoning with SNA data involves interpreting the results and drawing conclusions based on the analysis. This can be done through visual inspection of the network, statistical analysis, and qualitative analysis. Qualitative analysis involves interpreting the results in the context of the research question and using them to answer the research question.

In conclusion, evaluation of SNA is an important part of social network analysis, and it involves both internal and external evaluation. Storage of SNA data is also important, and there are several options available. Reasoning with SNA data involves interpreting the results and drawing conclusions based on the analysis.
