### Representing identity for the notes of the Unit 3 - Modelling and Aggregating Social Network Data in the subject of SOCIAL NETWORK ANALYTICS:

1. Representing identity: How individuals present themselves in a social network. This could be through usernames, profile pictures, bios, etc.
2. Digital identity: The data trails and digital footprint we leave through online interactions and behaviors. This could include information we explicitly provide through profiles as well as data implicitly collected through our usage patterns and behaviors.
3. Impression management: The process of controlling information about yourself to influence how others perceive you. This is common on social networks where people carefully curate profiles and posts to craft a desired image.
4. Self-presentation: How we choose to portray ourselves to others through communication. This could include emphasizing certain aspects of our identity over others depending on the context and audience.
