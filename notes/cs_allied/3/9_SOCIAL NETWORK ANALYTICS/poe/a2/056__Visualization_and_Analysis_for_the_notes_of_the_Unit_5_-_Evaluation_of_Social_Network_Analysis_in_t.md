### Visualization and Analysis for the notes of the Unit 5 - Evaluation of Social Network Analysis in the subject of SOCIAL NETWORK ANALYTICS

1. Visualization techniques for social networks:
- Node-link diagrams: Nodes representing actors and links representing relationships.
- Matrix representations: Adjacency matrix and sociomatrix representing connections between actors.
- Centrality measures: Degree centrality, betweenness centrality, closeness centrality, etc. to find important nodes.
- Community detection algorithms: To find densely connected groups or clusters in the network. Eg. Girvan-Newman algorithm.

2. Analysis of social networks:
- Degree distribution: The distribution of the number of connections of each node. Eg. Scale-free networks follow power-law degree distribution.
- Small-world phenomenon: Most nodes are reachable from every other node in a small number of steps.
- Structural holes: Gaps between dense clusters that can be exploited by brokers to get benefits.
- Modeling and simulation of social networks.

3. Applications of social network analysis:
- Understanding spread of information, innovations, epidemics, etc.
- Finding influential people or key players in a network.
- Recommendation systems.
- Detecting communities and analyzing group structures.
- Studying organization structure and relationships.
