### Development of Social network analysis for the notes of the Unit 1 - Social Network Analysis in the subject of SOCIAL NETWORK ANALYTICS.

1. The study of social networks is not new and has its origins in the writings of thinkers like Georg Simmel who analyzed social interactions and relationships.
2. However, the systematic study of social networks only emerged as a separate field of enquiry in the 1930s with the ethnographic study of rural communities.
3. The keydevelopment that enabled the modern study of social networks was the emergence of computing technologies that allowed large network datasets to be stored and analyzed.
4. In the 1950s, anthropologist John Barnes began to analyze systems of kinship and marriage as networks. Also, the sociologist J.A. Barnes began to analyze social structure using network analysis.
5. In the 1970s, Mark Granovetter studied the strength of weak ties and the concept of structural holes, which highlighted the importance of bridging ties in networks.
6. In the 1980s, interest in social network analysis burgeoned with the publication of multiple important papers and books formalizing network ideas and bringing new mathematical techniques.
7. The development of personal computers and new network analysis software in the 1990s enabled much larger network datasets to be analyzed, giving rise to many new insights into social structure and dynamics.
8. The 21st century has seen the continued growth of interest in social network analysis with the availability of vast new datasets, such as digitally-mediated networks, and ever more sophisticated computational techniques to analyze them.
