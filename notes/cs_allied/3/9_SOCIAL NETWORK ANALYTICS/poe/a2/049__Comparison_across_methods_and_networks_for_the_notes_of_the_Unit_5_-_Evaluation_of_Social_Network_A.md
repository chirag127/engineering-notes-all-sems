### Comparison across methods and networks for the notes of the Unit 5 - Evaluation of Social Network Analysis in the subject of SOCIAL NETWORK ANALYTICS.

1. Centrality measures: Degree, Betweenness, Closeness, Eigenvector
- Degree centrality: Number of direct connections a node has. Indicates popularity/activity.
- Betweenness centrality: Number of shortest paths going through a node. Indicates control over information flow.
- Closeness centrality: Average distance of a node to all other nodes. Indicates reach/influence.
- Eigenvector centrality: Importance of nodes that a given node is connected to. Indicates power/prestige.

2. Clustering coefficients: Local vs Global
- Local: Fraction of neighbors of a node that are connected to each other. Indicates density of local network.
- Global: Average of local clustering coefficients of all nodes. Indicates density of overall network.

3. Distance: Geodesic distance
- Shortest path between two nodes. Indicates closeness/separation between nodes.

4. Components: Strongly vs Weakly connected
- Strongly connected: Each node is reachable from every other node.
- Weakly connected: Not all nodes are reachable from each other, but network is still connected.
- Unconnected: Network splits into two or more disconnected components.

5. Structural holes: Bridges and brokers
- Bridges: Nodes that connect otherwise disconnected parts of the network.
- Brokers: Nodes that connect clusters/groups and control flow of information between them.
