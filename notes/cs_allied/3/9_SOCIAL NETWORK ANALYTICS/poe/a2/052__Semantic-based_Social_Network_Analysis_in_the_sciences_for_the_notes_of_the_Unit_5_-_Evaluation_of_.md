### Semantic-based Social Network Analysis in the sciences

- Semantic social network analysis incorporates semantic data such as keywords, categories, and concepts into the analysis of social networks.
- It allows researchers to analyze the content and themes of communications, and how these relate to network structure.
- For example, semantic social network analysis can reveal clusters of individuals who frequently discuss similar topics, or influential individuals who discuss a diverse range of topics.
- In the sciences, semantic social network analysis has been used to map scientific disciplines and understand the flow of ideas between them. For example, researchers have analyzed the keywords and abstracts of published papers to uncover emergent research fronts and identify boundary-spanning individuals and institutions.
- More broadly, semantic social network analysis provides a means of evaluating the content and themes that underlie a social network, revealing why the network is structured as it is and how this structure shapes the flow and exchange of information. It thus offers a valuable approach for analyzing science and innovation networks.
