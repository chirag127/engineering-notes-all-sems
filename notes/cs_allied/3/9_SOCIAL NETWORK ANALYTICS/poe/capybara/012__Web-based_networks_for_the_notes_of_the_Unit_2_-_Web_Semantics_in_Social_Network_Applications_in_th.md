### Web-based networks for the notes of the Unit 2 - Web Semantics in Social Network Applications in the subject of SOCIAL NETWORK ANALYTICS

In this unit, we will learn about web-based networks and their importance in social network applications. Here are some key points to remember:

- Web-based networks are networks that are formed through web-based interactions between individuals. These interactions can be through social media platforms, forums, blogs, and other online platforms.

- Web-based networks can be analyzed using social network analysis techniques. These techniques help in understanding the structure and dynamics of the network, identifying key players or influencers, and predicting the behavior of the network.

- Web-based networks can be used for a variety of purposes, such as marketing, advertising, political campaigning, and social activism. Companies and organizations can use web-based networks to reach out to their customers or target audience, while individuals can use these networks to connect with like-minded people and share their opinions or ideas.

- Semantic web technologies, such as RDF and OWL, can be used to represent and share data on the web-based networks. These technologies help in making the data more meaningful and useful, and enable interoperability between different web-based networks.

- Web-based networks also raise important ethical and privacy concerns. It is important to safeguard the personal information of individuals and ensure that their rights are protected in the online world.

- Overall, web-based networks play a crucial role in the modern society, and their study is essential for understanding the dynamics of social interactions on the web.
