### Reasoning with instance equality for the notes of the Unit 3 - Modelling and Aggregating Social Network Data in the subject of SOCIAL NETWORK ANALYTICS

The Unit 3 - Modelling and Aggregating Social Network Data in the subject of Social Network Analytics focuses on the use of instance equality reasoning to model and aggregate data in social networks. Instance equality reasoning involves the comparison of instances or objects in a network to determine if they are equivalent or not. This is important in social network analysis because it allows for the aggregation of data from multiple sources, such as online profiles, to create a comprehensive view of a person or entity. This unit will cover the various methods and techniques used for instance equality reasoning, including string matching, entity resolution, and probabilistic methods, among others. The unit will also examine the challenges and issues associated with instance equality reasoning, such as data quality, scalability, and privacy, among others. The goal of this unit is to provide students with a comprehensive understanding of the concepts, techniques, and tools associated with instance equality reasoning, and to equip them with the skills needed to design, implement, and evaluate social network analytics systems that make use of these techniques.
