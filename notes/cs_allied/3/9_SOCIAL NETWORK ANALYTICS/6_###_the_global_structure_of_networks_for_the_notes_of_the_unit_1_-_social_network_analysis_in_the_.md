### The global structure of networks for the notes of the Unit 1 - Social Network Analysis in the subject of SOCIAL NETWORK ANALYTICS

The global structure of networks refers to the overall pattern of connections and relationships between entities in a network. In the context of social network analysis, this refers to the pattern of connections and relationships between individuals in a social network.

The global structure of networks can be analyzed using a variety of measures, including:
1. Density: the proportion of possible connections that actually exist in the network.
2. Clustering coefficient: the degree to which entities in a network tend to cluster together, forming tightly connected groups.
3. Path length: the average number of steps required to move from one entity to another in the network.
4. Degree distribution: the distribution of the number of connections for each entity in the network.

The global structure of networks can have a significant impact on the behavior and dynamics of the network, and can be used to make predictions about the behavior of the network in the future.

In summary, The global structure of networks refers to the overall pattern of connections and relationships between entities in a network, with a focus on social network analysis in the context of individuals in a social network. It can be analyzed using measures such as density, clustering coefficient, path length, and degree distribution, and can have a significant impact on the behavior and dynamics of the network.
