### Electronic discussion networks for the notes of the Unit 2 - Web Semantics in Social Network Applications in the subject of SOCIAL NETWORK ANALYTICS

Electronic discussion networks, also known as online discussion forums, are web-based platforms for discussion and exchange of information. They enable individuals to participate in discussions on various topics, share information, and connect with others who have similar interests.

Electronic discussion networks have the following features:
1. User registration: users must register to participate in the discussion network, providing a unique identifier and personal information.
2. Topic-based discussion: discussions are organized around specific topics, allowing users to participate in discussions that are relevant to their interests.
3. Posting and commenting: users can post messages and comments on the discussion network, and other users can respond to these posts.
4. Search and filtering: users can search for specific topics and posts, and filter the results based on various criteria, such as date, author, and popularity.

Electronic discussion networks play an important role in social network analysis, as they provide a rich source of data on the opinions, interests, and behaviors of individuals in a network.

In summary, Electronic discussion networks are web-based platforms for discussion and exchange of information, with features such as user registration, topic-based discussion, posting and commenting, and search and filtering. They play an important role in social network analysis, providing a rich source of data on the opinions, interests, and behaviors of individuals in a network.
