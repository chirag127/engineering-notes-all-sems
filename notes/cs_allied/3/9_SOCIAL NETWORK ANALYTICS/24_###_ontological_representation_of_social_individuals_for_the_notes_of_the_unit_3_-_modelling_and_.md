### Ontological representation of social individuals for the notes of the Unit 3 - Modelling and Aggregating Social Network Data in the subject of SOCIAL NETWORK ANALYTICS

Ontological representation of social individuals involves using ontologies to define concepts and relationships in the domain of social networks. An ontology for social individuals would define concepts such as person, organization, location, and event, and the relationships between these concepts, such as works-for, lives-in, and attends.

The key features of an ontology for social individuals include:
1. Concept definitions: definitions of the concepts and entities in the domain, such as person, organization, location, and event.
2. Relationships: definitions of the relationships between concepts, such as works-for, lives-in, and attends.
3. Axioms: logical statements that describe the relationships between concepts and provide additional information about the domain.

Ontological representation of social individuals enables machines to understand the meaning of data in social networks, and to perform tasks such as data integration, information retrieval, and knowledge representation.
