## Unit 1 - Social Network Analysis

Unit 1 - Social Network Analysis covers the concepts and techniques used to analyze social networks. Social network analysis is the study of the relationships and connections between individuals in a social network.

The following are key aspects of social network analysis:

1. Social Networks: Social networks are networks of individuals who are connected by relationships or interactions. Social networks can be analyzed to understand the relationships and connections between individuals.

2. Network Structure: Network structure refers to the pattern of relationships and connections between individuals in a social network. Network structure can be analyzed to understand the relationships and connections between individuals.

3. Centrality Measures: Centrality measures are metrics that are used to quantify the importance of individuals in a social network. Centrality measures can be used to identify key individuals in a social network, and to understand the relationships and connections between individuals.

4. Community Detection: Community detection is the process of identifying groups of individuals in a social network who are more closely connected to each other than to individuals outside of the group. Community detection can be used to understand the relationships and connections between individuals in a social network.

5. Network Evolution: Network evolution refers to the changes in the relationships and connections between individuals in a social network over time. Network evolution can be analyzed to understand the relationships and connections between individuals in a social network.

By understanding these concepts, organizations can effectively analyze social networks to understand the relationships and connections between individuals. Unit 1 - Social Network Analysis covers the use of social network analysis, including network structure, centrality measures, community detection, and network evolution, in the context of social networks.
