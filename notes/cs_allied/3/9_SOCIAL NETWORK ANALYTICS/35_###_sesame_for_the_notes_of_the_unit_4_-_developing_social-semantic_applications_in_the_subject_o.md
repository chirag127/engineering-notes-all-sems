One of the key features of Sesame is its support for SPARQL, the standard query language for RDF data. SPARQL enables users to perform complex queries on RDF data, including filtering, aggregation, and data transformation operations. Sesame is widely used in a variety of applications, including semantic web applications, linked data applications, and knowledge management systems. It is also used in a range of research projects, including natural language processing, information retrieval, and social network analysis.
