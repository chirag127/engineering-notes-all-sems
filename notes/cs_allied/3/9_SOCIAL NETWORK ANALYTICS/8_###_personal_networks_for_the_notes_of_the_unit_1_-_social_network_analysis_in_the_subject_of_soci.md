### Personal networks for the notes of the Unit 1 - Social Network Analysis in the subject of SOCIAL NETWORK ANALYTICS

Unit 1 - Social Network Analysis covers the concept of personal networks, which are networks of individuals that are connected by personal relationships. Personal networks are a subset of social networks, and are often analyzed to understand the relationships and connections between individuals.

The following are key aspects of personal networks:

1. Personal Relationships: Personal relationships are relationships between individuals that are based on personal connections, such as family, friends, and colleagues. Personal relationships are the foundation of personal networks.

2. Network Structure: Network structure refers to the pattern of relationships and connections between individuals in a personal network. Network structure can be analyzed to understand the relationships and connections between individuals in a personal network.

3. Centrality Measures: Centrality measures are metrics that are used to quantify the importance of individuals in a personal network. Centrality measures can be used to identify key individuals in a personal network, and to understand the relationships and connections between individuals.

4. Network Evolution: Network evolution refers to the changes in the relationships and connections between individuals in a personal network over time. Network evolution can be analyzed to understand the relationships and connections between individuals in a personal network.

5. Personal Network Analysis: Personal network analysis is the study of personal networks, including the relationships and connections between individuals, the structure of the network, and the evolution of the network over time.

By understanding these concepts, organizations can effectively analyze personal networks to understand the relationships and connections between individuals. Unit 1 - Social Network Analysis covers the use of personal network analysis, including personal relationships, network structure, centrality measures, network evolution, and personal network analysis, in the context of social network analytics.
