### Development of Social network analysis for the notes of the Unit 1 - Social Network Analysis in the subject of SOCIAL NETWORK ANALYTICS

Social network analysis (SNA) is a field of study that focuses on the analysis of social networks, including the structure and dynamics of relationships between individuals, organizations, and other entities. The development of SNA has been driven by the growing importance of social networks in many areas, including business, politics, and society.

The development of SNA can be traced back to the early 20th century, when sociologists and anthropologists first began to study social networks. In the 1950s and 1960s, the development of network analysis techniques, such as graph theory and social network analysis, provided new tools for the analysis of social networks.

In the 1990s and 2000s, the growth of the Internet and the development of social media platforms, such as Facebook and Twitter, led to a new era of SNA, as researchers began to study online social networks and the impact of social networks on society.

Today, SNA is a growing field of study, with applications in many areas, including marketing, politics, and public health. SNA is used to study the structure and dynamics of social networks, to identify key influencers and opinion leaders, to understand the spread of information and ideas, and to measure the impact of social networks on society.
