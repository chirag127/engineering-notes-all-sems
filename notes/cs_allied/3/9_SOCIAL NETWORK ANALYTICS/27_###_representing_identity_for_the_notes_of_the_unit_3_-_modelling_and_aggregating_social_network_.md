### Representing identity for the notes of the Unit 3 - Modelling and Aggregating Social Network Data in the subject of SOCIAL NETWORK ANALYTICS

Unit 3 - Modelling and Aggregating Social Network Data covers the concept of representing identity, which is the process of modeling and representing the identity of entities in a social network.

In the context of social network analytics, representing identity is important for understanding the relationships and interactions between entities in a social network.

The following are key aspects of representing identity in social network analytics:

1. Entity Representation: Entity representation is the process of modeling and representing the identity of entities in a social network. Entity representation can be performed using various techniques, such as node-based representation and edge-based representation.

2. Identity Attributes: Identity attributes are characteristics that describe the identity of entities in a social network. Identity attributes can include demographic information, such as age and gender, and can also include information about relationships and interactions in the network.

3. Social Network Structure: The structure of a social network, including the relationships and interactions between entities, can provide information about the identity of entities in the network.

4. Representation Learning: Representation learning is the process of learning a representation of entities in a social network that can be used for analysis. Representation learning can be performed using various techniques, such as deep learning and unsupervised learning.

5. Graph Embedding: Graph embedding is the process of mapping entities in a social network to a low-dimensional vector space, where the relationships and interactions between entities can be analyzed.

By understanding these concepts, organizations can effectively use representation to model and represent the identity of entities in a social network, and to understand the relationships and interactions between entities in the network. Unit 3 - Modelling and Aggregating Social Network Data covers the use of representation, including entity representation, identity attributes, social network structure, representation learning, and graph embedding, in the context of social network analytics.
