### Web-based networks for the notes of the Unit 2 - Web Semantics in Social Network Applications in the subject of SOCIAL NETWORK ANALYTICS

Web-based networks are online networks that are built on the World Wide Web. They are used to connect people, organizations, and other entities through a common platform. Social network applications are a type of web-based network that are designed for social interaction and communication.

These networks are built on the principles of web semantics, which is the study of meaning and structure in online content. Web semantics provides a standardized way of representing and exchanging information on the web, which makes it possible to build web-based networks that can be easily understood and used by a wide range of users.

Social network applications use web semantics to represent relationships between people, organizations, and other entities. They also use web semantics to represent the content that is shared on the network, such as posts, comments, and photos.

One of the key benefits of web-based networks is that they allow users to connect with others from anywhere in the world. They also provide a platform for communication and collaboration, which can be useful for both personal and professional purposes.

Another benefit of web-based networks is that they allow for the collection and analysis of large amounts of data. This data can be used to gain insights into social behavior, relationships, and communication patterns. This type of analysis is known as social network analysis and is used to study the structure and behavior of social networks.

Overall, web-based networks are an important part of the modern internet and play a significant role in social interaction and communication. They provide a platform for social network applications, which are used by millions of people around the world to connect, communicate, and collaborate.
