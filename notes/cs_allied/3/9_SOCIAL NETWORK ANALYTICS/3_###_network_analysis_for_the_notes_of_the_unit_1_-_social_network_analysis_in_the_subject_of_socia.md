### Network analysis for the notes of the Unit 1 - Social Network Analysis in the subject of SOCIAL NETWORK ANALYTICS

Network analysis is a field of study that focuses on the analysis of relationships between entities in a network. In the context of social network analysis, this refers to the study of relationships between individuals in a social network.

Network analysis can be used to study a variety of topics in social network analysis, including:
1. Centrality: the study of the importance of individuals in a network, based on measures such as degree centrality, betweenness centrality, and eigenvector centrality.
2. Community detection: the study of the structure of communities in a network, and the identification of individuals who play a key role in connecting communities.
3. Link prediction: the study of predicting future relationships between individuals in a network.
4. Influence analysis: the study of the spread of information and influence within a network.

Network analysis can be performed using a variety of techniques, including graph theory, machine learning, and network visualization.

In summary, Network analysis is a field of study that focuses on the analysis of relationships between entities in a network, with a focus on social network analysis in the context of individuals in a social network. It can be used to study centrality, community detection, link prediction, and influence analysis, and can be performed using a variety of techniques, including graph theory, machine learning, and network visualization.
