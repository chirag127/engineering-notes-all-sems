### Descriptive analysis for the notes of the Unit 5 - Evaluation of Social Network Analysis in the subject of SOCIAL NETWORK ANALYTICS

Unit 5 of Evaluation of Social Network Analysis in the subject of Social Network Analytics focuses on the concept of Descriptive Analysis. Descriptive Analysis is a type of data analysis that is used to summarize and describe the main features of a dataset. The goal of this unit is to provide students with a comprehensive understanding of the methods and techniques used for performing descriptive analysis in social network analysis.

The following topics may be covered in this unit:

1. Introduction to Descriptive Analysis: An overview of the concept of descriptive analysis and its importance in social network analysis.

2. Measures of Central Tendency: An introduction to the different measures of central tendency, including mean, median, and mode, among others.

3. Measures of Dispersion: An introduction to the different measures of dispersion, including range, variance, and standard deviation, among others.

4. Visualization Techniques: An overview of the different visualization techniques used for descriptive analysis, including histograms, box plots, and scatter plots, among others.

5. Network Metrics: An introduction to the different network metrics used for descriptive analysis in social network analysis, including degree centrality, betweenness centrality, and closeness centrality, among others.

In conclusion, Unit 5 of Evaluation of Social Network Analysis in the subject of Social Network Analytics provides a comprehensive overview of Descriptive Analysis, including its methods, techniques, and applications. This knowledge is essential for summarizing and describing the main features of a dataset in social network analysis, and for developing accurate and effective social network analysis models.
