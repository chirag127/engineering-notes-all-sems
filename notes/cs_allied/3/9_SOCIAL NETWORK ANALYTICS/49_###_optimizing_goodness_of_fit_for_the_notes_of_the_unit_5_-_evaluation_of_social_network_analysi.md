### Optimizing goodness of fit for the notes of the Unit 5 - Evaluation of Social Network Analysis in the subject of SOCIAL NETWORK ANALYTICS

Optimizing goodness of fit refers to the process of improving the accuracy of a model by adjusting its parameters to better match the data. The goal of optimizing goodness of fit is to achieve the best possible fit between the model and the data, while minimizing the error between the model and the data.

There are several methods for optimizing goodness of fit, including:

1. Maximum likelihood estimation: This involves finding the parameters of the model that maximize the likelihood of the data given the model.

2. Least squares estimation: This involves finding the parameters of the model that minimize the sum of the squared differences between the model and the data.

3. Bayesian estimation: This involves using Bayesian methods to estimate the parameters of the model, taking into account prior knowledge about the parameters and the data.

The choice of method for optimizing goodness of fit depends on the specific requirements of the analysis and the type of data being analyzed. For example, if the data is noisy, then a method that can handle noise, such as Bayesian estimation, may be more appropriate.

Overall, optimizing goodness of fit is an important aspect of social network analysis, as it allows for the development of more accurate and reliable models, and can help to identify areas for improvement.
