# SOCIAL NETWORK ANALYTICS

Social Network Analytics is a field of study that involves analyzing and interpreting data from social networks, such as Facebook, Twitter, and LinkedIn. The goal of social network analytics is to understand patterns and relationships in social networks, and to use this information to make informed decisions.

The following are key aspects of social network analytics:

1. Network Structure: Network structure refers to the relationships between individuals in a social network. Network structure can be analyzed to understand patterns and relationships in social networks.

2. Node Centrality: Node centrality refers to the importance of a node in a network. Node centrality can be used to identify key individuals in a social network, and to understand their role in the network.

3. Link Analysis: Link analysis is the process of analyzing the relationships between individuals in a social network. Link analysis can be used to understand patterns and relationships in social networks.

4. Sentiment Analysis: Sentiment analysis is the process of analyzing the sentiment of messages in a social network. Sentiment analysis can be used to understand the attitudes and opinions of individuals in a social network.

5. Community Detection: Community detection is the process of identifying groups of individuals in a social network who have similar interests and behaviors. Community detection can be used to understand patterns and relationships in social networks.

By understanding these concepts, organizations can effectively use social network analytics to understand patterns and relationships in social networks, and to make informed decisions.
