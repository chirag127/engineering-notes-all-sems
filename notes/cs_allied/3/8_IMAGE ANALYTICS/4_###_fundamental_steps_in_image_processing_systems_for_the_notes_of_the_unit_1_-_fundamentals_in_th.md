### Fundamental steps in Image Processing Systems for the notes of the Unit 1 - Fundamentals in the subject of IMAGE ANALYTICS

Image processing systems typically involve several fundamental steps, including:

1. Image acquisition: the process of obtaining an image, either by capturing it using a camera or scanner, or by loading it from a digital file.

2. Image pre-processing: the process of preparing the image for further processing, which may involve tasks such as noise reduction, contrast enhancement, and color correction.

3. Image analysis: the process of analyzing the image to extract information, which may involve tasks such as segmentation, feature extraction, and object recognition.

4. Image interpretation: the process of making sense of the information extracted from the image, which may involve tasks such as classification and pattern recognition.

5. Image visualization: the process of presenting the results of the image analysis in a meaningful way, which may involve tasks such as image rendering and data visualization.

In summary, image processing systems typically involve several fundamental steps, including image acquisition, pre-processing, analysis, interpretation, and visualization. These steps are essential for effective and efficient image analysis and interpretation.
