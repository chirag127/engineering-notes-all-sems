### Color Fundamentals for the notes of the Unit 1 - Fundamentals in the subject of IMAGE ANALYTICS

Color Fundamentals are the basic principles that govern the representation and manipulation of color in digital images.

Color can be represented in a number of ways, including RGB (Red, Green, and Blue), HSL (Hue, Saturation, and Lightness), and CMYK (Cyan, Magenta, Yellow, and Key).

The RGB color model represents color as a combination of red, green, and blue light, and is used in most digital displays, including computer monitors and televisions.

The HSL color model represents color as a combination of hue, saturation, and lightness, and is used in image processing and color correction.

The CMYK color model represents color as a combination of cyan, magenta, yellow, and key, and is used in printing.

In summary, Color Fundamentals are the basic principles that govern the representation and manipulation of color in digital images. Color can be represented in a number of ways, including RGB, HSL, and CMYK, each of which is used in different applications.
