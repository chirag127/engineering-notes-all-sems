### Image Acquisition for the notes of the Unit 1 - Fundamentals in the subject of IMAGE ANALYTICS

Image Acquisition refers to the process of obtaining digital images from various sources. This process can involve capturing images using digital cameras, scanning physical images, or downloading images from the internet.

The key factors to consider in image acquisition include:
1. Image quality: the quality of the image, including resolution, color depth, and noise.
2. Image format: the format of the image, including JPEG, PNG, TIFF, and others.
3. Image size: the size of the image, including dimensions and file size.
4. Image acquisition devices: the devices used to acquire the images, including digital cameras, scanners, and others.
5. Image acquisition settings: the settings used during image acquisition, including resolution, color depth, and others.

In summary, Image Acquisition refers to the process of obtaining digital images from various sources. Key factors to consider in image acquisition include image quality, format, size, acquisition devices, and acquisition settings.
