# IMAGE ANALYTICS

Image Analytics refers to the process of extracting insights and knowledge from image data. It involves the use of machine learning and computer vision techniques to analyze images and extract meaningful information from them. Image Analytics can be applied in a variety of domains, including:

1. Medical Imaging: Image Analytics can be used to analyze medical images, such as X-rays and MRI scans, to diagnose and monitor diseases.

2. Retail: Image Analytics can be used to analyze images of products and customer behavior in retail stores, to improve customer experience and increase sales.

3. Surveillance: Image Analytics can be used to analyze video footage from surveillance cameras, to detect and prevent crime.

4. Agriculture: Image Analytics can be used to analyze images of crops and soil, to improve crop yields and reduce waste.

5. Manufacturing: Image Analytics can be used to inspect products and identify defects, to improve quality control and reduce waste.

In conclusion, Image Analytics refers to the process of extracting insights and knowledge from image data. It involves the use of machine learning and computer vision techniques to analyze images and extract meaningful information from them. Image Analytics can be applied in a variety of domains, including medical imaging, retail, surveillance, agriculture, and manufacturing, and is used to improve decision making and drive business outcomes.
