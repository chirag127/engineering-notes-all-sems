### Multilayer Feedforward Neural Networks for the notes of the Unit 5 - Image Pattern Classification in the subject of IMAGE ANALYTICS

4. Performance Evaluation: An introduction to the different metrics used for evaluating the performance of Multilayer Feedforward Neural Networks, including accuracy, precision, recall, and F1 score, among others.

5. Applications of Multilayer Feedforward Neural Networks: An overview of the different applications of Multilayer Feedforward Neural Networks in image pattern classification, including object recognition, image segmentation, and face recognition, among others.

In conclusion, Unit 5 of Image Pattern Classification in the subject of Image Analytics provides a comprehensive overview of Multilayer Feedforward Neural Networks, including their theory, implementation, and applications.
