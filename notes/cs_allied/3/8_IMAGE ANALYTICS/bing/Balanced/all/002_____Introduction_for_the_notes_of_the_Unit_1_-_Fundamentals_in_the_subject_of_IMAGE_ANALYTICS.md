# Introduction for the notes of the Unit 1 - Fundamentals in the subject of IMAGE ANALYTICS

- Image analytics is the process of extracting meaningful information from digital images using computer vision, machine learning, and artificial intelligence techniques.
- Image analytics can be used for various applications, such as face recognition, medical imaging, security, surveillance, biometrics, autonomous driving, and more.
- Image analytics involves several steps, such as image acquisition, preprocessing, feature extraction, classification, segmentation, detection, recognition, and analysis.
- Image acquisition is the process of capturing an image from a camera or other source and converting it into a digital format that can be processed by a computer.
- Image preprocessing is the process of enhancing the quality of an image by removing noise, correcting illumination, adjusting contrast, resizing, cropping, and other operations.
- Feature extraction is the process of extracting relevant information from an image, such as edges, corners, shapes, colors, textures, and other descriptors that can be used for further analysis.
- Classification is the process of assigning an image or a part of an image to a predefined category, such as animal, plant, vehicle, etc.
- Segmentation is the process of dividing an image into meaningful regions or objects based on some criteria, such as color, texture, shape, etc.
- Detection is the process of locating and identifying specific objects or regions of interest in an image, such as faces, eyes, cars, pedestrians, etc.
- Recognition is the process of matching an image or a part of an image to a known entity, such as a person, a place, a logo, a barcode, etc.
- Analysis is the process of extracting higher-level information from an image, such as emotions, actions, events, relationships, etc.
