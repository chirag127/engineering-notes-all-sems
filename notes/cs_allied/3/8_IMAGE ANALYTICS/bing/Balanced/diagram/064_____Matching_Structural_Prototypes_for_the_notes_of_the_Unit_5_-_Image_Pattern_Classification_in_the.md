### Matching Structural Prototypes

- Matching structural prototypes is a technique for image pattern classification that involves comparing an unknown pattern with a set of known prototypes that represent different classes.
- A prototype is a sub-image or a graph that captures the essential features of a class .
- The matching process can be exact or inexact, depending on the degree of similarity required between the pattern and the prototype .
- Exact matching requires that the pattern and the prototype have the same size, shape, orientation, and pixel values.
- Inexact matching allows for some variations or distortions in the pattern and the prototype, such as scaling, rotation, translation, noise, or occlusion  .
- The matching score can be computed using various methods, such as correlation, distance, or similarity measures .
- The pattern is assigned to the class of the prototype with the highest matching score .
- Matching structural prototypes can be used for various applications, such as object detection, face recognition, medical imaging, and edge detection  .
- Matching structural prototypes can also handle complex patterns that require a description of their structure rather than a simple classification.
- Matching structural prototypes can be combined with other techniques, such as feature extraction, segmentation, or transformation, to improve the performance and robustness of the classification  .
