### Introduction

- Image analytics is the process of applying advanced techniques such as computer vision, machine learning, and artificial intelligence to extract meaningful information from images and videos.
- Image analytics can be used for various applications such as face recognition, object detection, medical imaging, security, entertainment, and more.
- Image analytics involves three main steps: image acquisition, image processing, and image analysis.
- Image acquisition is the process of capturing or obtaining images from different sources such as cameras, scanners, satellites, etc.
- Image processing is the process of enhancing, transforming, or manipulating images to improve their quality, appearance, or suitability for further analysis.
- Image analysis is the process of extracting features, patterns, or information from images using various techniques such as segmentation, classification, clustering, etc.
- Image analytics requires a combination of domain knowledge, mathematical skills, and programming skills to implement and apply various algorithms and methods.
- Image analytics is a rapidly evolving field that offers many challenges and opportunities for research and innovation.
