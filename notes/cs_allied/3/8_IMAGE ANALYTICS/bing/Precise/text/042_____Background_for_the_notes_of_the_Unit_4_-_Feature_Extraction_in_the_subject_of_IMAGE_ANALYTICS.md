### Background for the notes of the Unit 4 - Feature Extraction in the subject of IMAGE ANALYTICS

- Feature extraction is the process of extracting relevant and non-redundant information from raw data.
- In the context of image analytics, feature extraction involves identifying and extracting important visual characteristics from an image.
- These characteristics, or features, can include color, texture, shape, and spatial relationships.
- Feature extraction is an important step in image analysis and is used for tasks such as object recognition, image classification, and image retrieval.
- There are many techniques for feature extraction, including edge detection, corner detection, and scale-invariant feature transform (SIFT).
- Feature extraction can be performed manually or automatically using machine learning algorithms.
- The choice of features and the method of extraction can have a significant impact on the performance of image analysis tasks.
- Feature extraction is an active area of research, with ongoing efforts to develop new and more effective methods.
