### Shape Numbers

Shape numbers are used to extract shape-related information from an image. They are used in various feature extraction techniques such as:

- Moment invariants: contour moments, geometric moments, Zernike moment, radial Chebyshev moment, Legendre moment, homocentric polar-radius moment, orthogonal Fourier-Mellin moment, and pseudo Zernike moment .
- Polygon approximation shape features .
- Scale space shape features .
- Shape transform domain shape feature .
- SIFT techniques .

These techniques are used in content-based image retrieval (CBIR) to recognize and classify image shapes . For example, in one study, 4 kinds of features were used for processing, and the total number of features for each shape after PCA was equal to 80. Then MLP selected 12 features from 80 features for retrieval. The resultant retrieval accuracy was equal to 87.5%, while without utilizing the neural network, it was 79.15% .
