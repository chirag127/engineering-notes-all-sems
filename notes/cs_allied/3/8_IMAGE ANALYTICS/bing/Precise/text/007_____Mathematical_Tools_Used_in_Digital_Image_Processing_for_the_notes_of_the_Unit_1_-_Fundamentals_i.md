### Mathematical Tools Used in Digital Image Processing

1. **Linear Algebra:** Linear algebra is a branch of mathematics that deals with linear equations, matrices, and vector spaces. It is used in digital image processing for tasks such as image compression, filtering, and transformation.

2. **Calculus:** Calculus is a branch of mathematics that deals with rates of change and how things change. It is used in digital image processing for tasks such as edge detection, image enhancement, and image restoration.

3. **Probability and Statistics:** Probability and statistics are branches of mathematics that deal with uncertainty and data analysis. They are used in digital image processing for tasks such as noise reduction, image segmentation, and pattern recognition.

4. **Fourier Analysis:** Fourier analysis is a branch of mathematics that deals with the representation of functions as sums of simpler trigonometric functions. It is used in digital image processing for tasks such as image filtering, image compression, and image enhancement.

5. **Numerical Analysis:** Numerical analysis is a branch of mathematics that deals with the development and analysis of numerical methods for solving mathematical problems. It is used in digital image processing for tasks such as image interpolation, image registration, and image reconstruction.
