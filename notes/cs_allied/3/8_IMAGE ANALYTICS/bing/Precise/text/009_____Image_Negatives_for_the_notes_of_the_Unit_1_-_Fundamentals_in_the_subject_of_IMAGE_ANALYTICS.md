### Image Negatives

- An image negative is created by inverting the colors in an image.
- In a grayscale image, this means that black becomes white and white becomes black, while shades of gray are inverted as well.
- In a color image, each color channel (red, green, and blue) is inverted individually.
- The inversion of an image can be used for a variety of purposes, such as enhancing the visibility of details in dark areas of an image, or creating an artistic effect.
- In traditional film photography, a negative is an intermediate step in the process of creating a final print. The negative is a reversed version of the original image, with the colors and tones inverted.
- In digital image processing, the negative of an image can be easily created using software tools that allow for the inversion of color values.
- The concept of image negatives is an important fundamental in the subject of image analytics, as it provides a basis for understanding how image data can be manipulated and transformed.
