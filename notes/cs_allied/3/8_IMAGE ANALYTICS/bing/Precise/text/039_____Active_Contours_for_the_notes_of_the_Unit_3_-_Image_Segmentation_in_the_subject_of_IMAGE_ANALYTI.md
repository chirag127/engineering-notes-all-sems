### Active Contours

- Active Contours, also known as snakes, is an iterative region-growing image segmentation algorithm.
- The algorithm involves specifying initial curves on an image and then using the activecontour function to evolve the curves towards object boundaries .
- Active contour is a segmentation method that uses energy forces and constraints to separate the pixels of interest from a picture for further processing and analysis .
- It is defined as an active model for the segmentation process. Contours are the boundaries that define the region of interest in an image .
- Active contour is one of the active models in segmentation techniques, which makes use of the energy constraints and forces in the image for separation of the region of interest .
- It defines a separate boundary or curvature for the regions of the target object for segmentation .
- The Active Contour Model (ACM) is a standard image analysis technique with numerous variants .
- The snakes model is popular in computer vision and is widely used in applications like object tracking and shape recognition .
