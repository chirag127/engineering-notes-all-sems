### Snakes and Level Sets for the notes of the Unit 3 - Image Segmentation in the subject of IMAGE ANALYTICS

Unit 3 - Image Segmentation covers the concepts of snakes and level sets, which are techniques used for image segmentation.

Snakes and level sets are active contour models that are used to segment images into regions. Snakes and level sets are used to model the boundaries of objects in an image, and to evolve these boundaries over time to fit the objects in the image.

The following are key aspects of snakes and level sets in image segmentation:

1. Snakes: Snakes are active contour models that are used to model the boundaries of objects in an image. Snakes are initialized at a starting position and evolve over time to fit the objects in the image.

2. Level Sets: Level sets are active contour models that are used to model the boundaries of objects in an image. Level sets are initialized as a surface in the image and evolve over time to fit the objects in the image.

3. Energy Functions: Energy functions are used to define the behavior of snakes and level sets. Energy functions are used to determine the evolution of the contour models over time.

4. Image Gradients: Image gradients are used to provide information about the intensity levels in an image. Image gradients are used to calculate the energy functions used in snakes and level sets.

5. Evolution: Snakes and level sets evolve over time to fit the objects in the image. The evolution of the contour models is determined by the energy functions and image gradients.

By understanding these concepts, organizations can effectively use snakes and level sets in image segmentation to model the boundaries of objects in an image and to evolve these boundaries over time to fit the objects in the image.
