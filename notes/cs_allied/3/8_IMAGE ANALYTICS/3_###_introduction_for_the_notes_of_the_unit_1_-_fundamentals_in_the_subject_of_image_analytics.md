### Introduction for the notes of the Unit 1 - Fundamentals in the subject of IMAGE ANALYTICS

Image Analytics is the process of extracting meaningful information from digital images. It involves the use of mathematical and computational algorithms to analyze and interpret images, in order to gain insights and make decisions. Image Analytics is a multidisciplinary field that draws on computer vision, machine learning, signal processing, and pattern recognition.

The goal of Image Analytics is to extract meaningful information from images, such as identifying objects, detecting patterns, and measuring properties. Image Analytics can be applied to a wide range of applications, including medical imaging, satellite imaging, security and surveillance, and industrial inspection.

In Image Analytics, images are typically represented as arrays of pixel values, and algorithms are used to process these arrays to extract features and make decisions. The process of Image Analytics can be divided into several stages, including image acquisition, pre-processing, feature extraction, classification, and post-processing.
