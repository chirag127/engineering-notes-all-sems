### Moment Invariants for the notes of the Unit 4 - Feature Extraction in the subject of IMAGE ANALYTICS

Moment Invariants are mathematical features used in image analysis to describe the shape and structure of objects in an image. They are calculated from the moments of the image intensity values, and are designed to be invariant to transformations such as rotation, scaling, and translation.

Some commonly used moment invariants include:
1. Hu Moments: A set of seven moments that are invariant to translation, scaling, and rotation.
2. Zernike Moments: A set of orthogonal polynomials that describe the shape of an object.
3. Legendre Moments: A set of polynomials that describe the shape of an object, similar to Zernike Moments.

Moment invariants are used in a variety of image analysis tasks, such as object recognition, image matching, and pattern classification. They provide a compact and robust representation of the shape of an object, which can be used to compare and distinguish objects in an image.

Overall, moment invariants are an important tool in the field of image analytics, as they provide a way to extract meaningful features from images that are invariant to common transformations, making them useful for a wide range of image analysis tasks.
