### Introduction

In this unit, we will be discussing the fundamentals of image analytics. Image analytics is the process of extracting information from digital images using various computer vision techniques. It has a wide range of applications in fields such as healthcare, security, and entertainment.

Here are some key concepts you should be familiar with:

- **Digital Images:** Digital images are made up of pixels, which are tiny squares of color that make up an image. When we analyze images, we typically work with them in a digital format.

- **Image Processing:** Image processing involves manipulating digital images to improve their quality or extract useful information. Techniques include filtering, segmentation, and edge detection.

- **Computer Vision:** Computer vision is a field of study that focuses on enabling computers to interpret and analyze visual data from the real world. It involves techniques such as object detection, feature extraction, and image recognition.

- **Machine Learning:** Machine learning is a type of artificial intelligence that involves training algorithms to make predictions or decisions based on data. It is commonly used in image analytics for tasks such as image classification and object detection.

- **Deep Learning:** Deep learning is a subset of machine learning that involves training artificial neural networks to make more complex predictions or decisions. It is commonly used in image analytics for tasks such as image segmentation and facial recognition.

By understanding these key concepts, you will be able to better understand how image analytics works and how it can be applied in various fields. In the following sections, we will explore each of these concepts in more detail.
