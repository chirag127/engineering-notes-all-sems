### Histogram Processing for the notes of the Unit 1 - Fundamentals in the subject of IMAGE ANALYTICS

Histogram processing is a fundamental technique in image analytics that is used to enhance the visual quality of images. It involves transforming the pixel intensity values of an image in order to improve its contrast and brightness. Here are some key points to keep in mind when studying histogram processing:

- A histogram is a graphical representation of the distribution of pixel intensity values in an image. It shows how many pixels have a certain intensity value.
- Histogram equalization is a technique used to adjust the contrast of an image by redistributing its pixel intensity values. The aim is to make the histogram of the image as flat as possible. This technique can be used to make an image more visually appealing and easier to interpret.
- Histogram matching is a technique used to adjust the brightness and contrast of an image to match a reference image. This technique is used when you want to make two images look similar in terms of brightness and contrast, even if they have different pixel intensity values.
- Histogram stretching is a technique used to enhance the contrast of an image by stretching its pixel intensity values to fill the entire range of possible values. This technique can be used to bring out the details in an image that might otherwise be hidden.

In summary, histogram processing is a powerful tool for enhancing the visual quality of images. By understanding the key techniques involved in histogram processing, you can improve the quality of your image data and make it easier to interpret.
