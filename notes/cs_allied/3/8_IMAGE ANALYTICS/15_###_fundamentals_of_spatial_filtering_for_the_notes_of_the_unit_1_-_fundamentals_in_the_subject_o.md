### Fundamentals of Spatial Filtering for the notes of the Unit 1 - Fundamentals in the subject of IMAGE ANALYTICS

Unit 1 - Fundamentals of Image Analytics covers the fundamentals of spatial filtering, which is a technique used to process and analyze images. Spatial filtering involves transforming the image into a different representation, in order to highlight certain features or remove noise.

The following are key aspects of spatial filtering:

1. Convolution: Convolution is a mathematical operation used to apply a filter to an image. Convolution involves multiplying the filter with the image and summing the results to produce a new image.

2. Filters: Filters are mathematical functions used to process images. Filters can be used to sharpen, blur, or enhance images.

3. Low-pass filters: Low-pass filters are filters that remove high-frequency components from an image, resulting in a blurred image.

4. High-pass filters: High-pass filters are filters that remove low-frequency components from an image, resulting in a sharpened image.

5. Edge detection: Edge detection is the process of detecting the boundaries between objects in an image. Edge detection is performed using filters that highlight the edges in an image.

By understanding these concepts, organizations can effectively use spatial filtering to process and analyze images. Unit 1 - Fundamentals of Image Analytics covers the use of spatial filtering, including convolution, filters, low-pass filters, high-pass filters, and edge detection, in image analytics.
