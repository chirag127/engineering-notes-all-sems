### Opening and Closing for the notes of the Unit 2 - Morphological Image Processing in the subject of IMAGE ANALYTICS

Unit 2 - Morphological Image Processing covers the concepts of opening and closing, which are morphological operations used in image processing.

1. Opening: Opening is a morphological operation that involves eroding an image followed by dilation. Opening is used to remove small objects or noise from an image while preserving the overall structure of the objects in the image.

2. Closing: Closing is a morphological operation that involves dilating an image followed by erosion. Closing is used to fill in small gaps or holes in objects while preserving the overall structure of the objects in the image.

3. Structuring Element: A structuring element is a small binary image used in morphological operations. The shape of the structuring element determines the type of morphological operation performed.

4. Uses: Opening and closing are used in image processing tasks such as noise removal, object extraction, and image segmentation.

By understanding these concepts, organizations can effectively use opening and closing in morphological image processing to process and analyze images.
