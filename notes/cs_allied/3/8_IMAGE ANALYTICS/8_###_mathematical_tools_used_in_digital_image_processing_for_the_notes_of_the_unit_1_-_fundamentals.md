### Mathematical Tools Used in Digital Image Processing for the notes of the Unit 1 - Fundamentals in the subject of IMAGE ANALYTICS

Mathematical tools play a critical role in digital image processing, providing the necessary tools for analyzing, transforming, and manipulating images. Some common mathematical tools used in digital image processing include:

1. Linear Algebra: used for image transformations, such as rotations, translations, and scaling.

2. Calculus: used for image smoothing and edge detection.

3. Fourier Analysis: used for image filtering, compression, and feature extraction.

4. Probability and Statistics: used for image segmentation, classification, and pattern recognition.

5. Numerical Methods: used for solving differential equations and partial differential equations, which are used in image processing tasks such as denoising, deblurring, and inpainting.

In summary, mathematical tools play a critical role in digital image processing, providing the necessary tools for analyzing, transforming, and manipulating images. Common mathematical tools used in digital image processing include linear algebra, calculus, Fourier analysis, probability and statistics, and numerical methods.
