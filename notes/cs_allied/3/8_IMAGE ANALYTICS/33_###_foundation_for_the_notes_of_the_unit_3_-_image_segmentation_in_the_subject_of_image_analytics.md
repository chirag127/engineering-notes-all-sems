### Foundation for the notes of the Unit 3 - Image Segmentation in the subject of IMAGE ANALYTICS

Unit 3 - Image Segmentation covers the foundation of image segmentation, which is the process of dividing an image into multiple segments or regions.

The following are key aspects of image segmentation:

1. Definition: Image segmentation is the process of dividing an image into multiple segments or regions, each of which corresponds to a different object or part of the image.

2. Objectives: The objective of image segmentation is to divide an image into meaningful segments or regions, such that each segment corresponds to a different object or part of the image.

3. Techniques: Image segmentation can be performed using a variety of techniques, including thresholding, edge detection, region growing, and clustering.

4. Applications: Image segmentation is used in a wide range of applications, including object recognition, image compression, and medical imaging.

5. Challenges: Image segmentation is a challenging task due to the complexity of images and the variability of objects and parts within images.

By understanding these concepts, organizations can effectively use image segmentation to divide images into meaningful segments or regions, and to analyze and process images in a wide range of applications. Unit 3 - Image Segmentation covers the use of image segmentation, including its definition, objectives, techniques, applications, and challenges, in the context of image analytics.
