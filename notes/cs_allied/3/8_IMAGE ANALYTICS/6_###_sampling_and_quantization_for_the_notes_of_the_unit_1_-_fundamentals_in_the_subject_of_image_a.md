### Sampling and Quantization for the notes of the Unit 1 - Fundamentals in the subject of IMAGE ANALYTICS

Sampling and quantization are two important techniques used in image analytics to reduce the amount of data in an image while still preserving its essential features.

Sampling: Sampling is the process of reducing the number of pixels in an image by selecting only a subset of the pixels to represent the entire image. This reduces the size of the image and the amount of data that needs to be processed.

Quantization: Quantization is the process of reducing the number of bits used to represent the color of each pixel in an image. This reduces the amount of data in the image and the amount of storage required to store the image.

In summary, sampling and quantization are two important techniques used in image analytics to reduce the amount of data in an image while still preserving its essential features. Sampling reduces the number of pixels in an image, while quantization reduces the number of bits used to represent the color of each pixel.
