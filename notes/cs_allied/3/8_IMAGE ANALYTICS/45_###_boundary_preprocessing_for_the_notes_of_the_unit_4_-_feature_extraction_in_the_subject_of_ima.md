### Boundary Preprocessing for the notes of the Unit 4 - Feature Extraction in the subject of IMAGE ANALYTICS

Boundary Preprocessing is a technique used in image analysis to prepare the image boundary for feature extraction. The goal of boundary preprocessing is to improve the accuracy and robustness of feature detection by removing noise and enhancing the features of interest.

The key steps in boundary preprocessing are:
1. Noise reduction: The image is filtered to remove noise, such as random variations in intensity, that can interfere with feature detection.
2. Edge detection: The image is processed to detect the boundaries between regions, which are often the features of interest in image analysis.
3. Edge enhancement: The edges are enhanced to improve their visibility and to make them easier to detect. This may include techniques such as histogram equalization, gradient magnitude, and thresholding.

Examples of boundary preprocessing techniques include:
1. Median filtering
2. Gaussian smoothing
3. Canny edge detection
4. Laplacian of Gaussian (LoG) edge detection

In summary, Boundary Preprocessing is a technique used in image analysis to prepare the image boundary for feature extraction. The process includes noise reduction, edge detection, and edge enhancement, and examples of boundary preprocessing techniques include median filtering, Gaussian smoothing, Canny edge detection, and Laplacian of Gaussian (LoG) edge detection.
