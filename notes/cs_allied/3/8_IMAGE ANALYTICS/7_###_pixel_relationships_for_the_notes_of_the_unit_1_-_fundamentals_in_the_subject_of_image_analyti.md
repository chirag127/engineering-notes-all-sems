### Pixel Relationships for the notes of the Unit 1 - Fundamentals in the subject of IMAGE ANALYTICS

Pixel relationships refer to the relationships between pixels in an image. These relationships can be used to extract meaningful information from an image and are an important aspect of image analysis and understanding. There are several types of pixel relationships, including:

1. Spatial relationships: Spatial relationships refer to the relationships between pixels in terms of their position in the image. This includes relationships such as adjacency, proximity, and orientation.

2. Intensity relationships: Intensity relationships refer to the relationships between the intensity values of pixels in the image. This includes relationships such as brightness, contrast, and color.

3. Texture relationships: Texture relationships refer to the relationships between pixels in terms of their texture. This includes relationships such as roughness, smoothness, and pattern.

These pixel relationships can be used in various image analysis tasks, such as object recognition, segmentation, and classification. By analyzing the relationships between pixels in an image, algorithms can extract meaningful information and make decisions about the content of the image.

In conclusion, Pixel relationships refer to the relationships between pixels in an image and are an important aspect of image analysis and understanding. There are several types of pixel relationships, including spatial relationships, intensity relationships, and texture relationships. These relationships can be used in various image analysis tasks, such as object recognition, segmentation, and classification, to extract meaningful information from an image.
