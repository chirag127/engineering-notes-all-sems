### Pattern Classification by Prototype Matching for the notes of the Unit 5 - Image Pattern Classification in the subject of IMAGE ANALYTICS

Unit 5 of Image Pattern Classification in the subject of Image Analytics focuses on the use of prototype matching for image pattern classification. Prototype matching is a type of image classification that involves matching a test image to a set of prototypes, or reference images, to determine the class of the test image. This unit will cover the various methods and techniques used for prototype matching, including nearest neighbor, k-nearest neighbor, and decision trees, among others. The unit will also examine the challenges and issues associated with prototype matching, such as overfitting, the curse of dimensionality, and the choice of similarity metric, among others.

The unit will also cover the use of prototype matching in various applications, such as object recognition, face recognition, and scene classification, among others. The goal of this unit is to provide students with a comprehensive understanding of the concepts, techniques, and tools associated with prototype matching, and to equip them with the skills needed to design, implement, and evaluate image pattern classification systems that make use of these techniques.
