### Examples of Stream Processing for the notes of the Unit 1 - Fundamentals of Stream Processing in the subject of STREAM PROCESSING AND ANALYTICS

Examples of Stream Processing:
1. Financial trading: analyzing stock prices in real-time to make informed trading decisions.
2. Fraud detection: detecting fraudulent transactions as they occur in real-time.
3. IoT: processing and analyzing data from IoT devices in real-time to gain insights into device usage and performance.
4. Social media analysis: analyzing social media data in real-time to identify trends and sentiment.
5. Log analysis: analyzing log data in real-time to identify potential issues and monitor system performance.
6. Marketing: analyzing customer behavior in real-time to personalize marketing campaigns and improve customer engagement.
7. Healthcare: processing and analyzing patient data in real-time to improve patient outcomes and reduce costs.
