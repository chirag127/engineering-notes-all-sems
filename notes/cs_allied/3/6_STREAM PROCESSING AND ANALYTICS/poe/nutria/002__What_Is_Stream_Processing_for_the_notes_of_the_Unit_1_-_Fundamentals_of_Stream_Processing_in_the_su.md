
### What Is Stream Processing?

- Stream processing is a form of data processing that involves analyzing and manipulating data in real-time, as it is being generated or received.
- Stream processing is used in scenarios where data needs to be processed quickly and accurately, such as in financial markets, IoT applications, and online services.
- Stream processing is different from batch processing, which involves processing data in large batches, usually at periodic intervals.
- Stream processing systems can process data quickly and accurately, and can detect patterns and anomalies in data streams in real-time.
- Stream processing systems are often used in conjunction with other technologies, such as machine learning and artificial intelligence, to provide more accurate and timely insights.
