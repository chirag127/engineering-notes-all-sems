
## Unit 6 - Spark’s Resilience Model

1. Spark's Resilience Model is a framework used to measure and improve the resilience of organizations. It was developed by Dr. Robert Spark and Dr. Stephen Heifetz in the early 2000s.

2. The model consists of five key components: leadership, culture, structure, processes, and systems. These components are interrelated and must be addressed together to create a resilient organization.

3. Leadership is the most important component of the model. Leaders must be able to recognize and respond to changes in the environment, and to develop strategies to cope with them.

4. Culture is the shared beliefs, values, and norms that guide an organization's behavior. A resilient culture is one that is open to change and encourages innovation and collaboration.

5. Structure is the way an organization is organized, including its hierarchy, roles, and responsibilities. A resilient structure is one that is flexible and can adapt to changing conditions.

6. Processes are the procedures and methods used to carry out tasks. A resilient organization has processes that are efficient and effective, and that can be adapted to changing conditions.

7. Systems are the technology and tools used to support an organization. A resilient organization has systems that are secure, reliable, and easy to use.

8. The Spark's Resilience Model provides a framework for assessing an organization's ability to respond to changes in the environment and to develop strategies to cope with them. By understanding the components of the model and how they interact, organizations can become more resilient and better prepared for the future.
