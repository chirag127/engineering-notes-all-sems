# STREAM PROCESSING AND ANALYTICS

Stream processing is a technology used to process high-volume, fast-moving data streams in real-time. It involves analyzing data as it is generated or received, rather than waiting for all of the data to be stored before processing it. Analytics is the systematic examination of data or statistics to gain insights and make informed decisions. Stream processing and analytics are often used together to provide real-time insights into data as it is generated. This can be useful in a variety of applications, including financial trading, fraud detection, IoT, and social media analysis. Stream processing and analytics can also be used to identify patterns and trends in real-time, allowing organizations to respond quickly to changing conditions.
