## Unit 6 - Spark’s Resilience Model

- Spark’s resilience model is a framework that helps individuals and organizations cope with stress, adversity, and change.
- The model consists of four components: self-awareness, self-care, social awareness, and social care.
- Self-awareness is the ability to recognize and understand one’s own emotions, thoughts, values, strengths, and limitations.
- Self-care is the practice of taking care of one’s physical, mental, emotional, and spiritual well-being.
- Social awareness is the ability to empathize and communicate effectively with others, especially those who are different from oneself.
- Social care is the practice of building and maintaining supportive and positive relationships with others, and contributing to the common good.
- The model suggests that by developing and balancing these four components, one can enhance one’s resilience, which is the capacity to adapt and thrive in the face of challenges and changes.
- The model also provides a set of strategies and tools to help individuals and organizations assess and improve their resilience levels.
