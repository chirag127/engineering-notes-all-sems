### Architectural Models for the notes of the Unit 3 - Components of a Data Platform in the subject of STREAM PROCESSING AND ANALYTICS

- Data architecture is the design and management of data and its systems in an organization or a solution.
- Data architecture consists of three essential models that are combined to form a comprehensive data architecture :
  - Conceptual model: It is a high-level business model that uses entity-relationship diagrams to show the entities and their attributes, as well as the relationships among them . It defines the scope and context of the data and its meaning.
  - Logical model: It is a more detailed and technical model that represents the data in terms of structures and formats, such as tables, columns, classes, XML tags, etc . It specifies the rules and constraints for the data and its manipulation.
  - Physical model: It is the lowest-level model that describes how the data is stored, accessed, and processed in the physical devices and systems, such as databases, files, servers, networks, etc . It optimizes the performance and efficiency of the data and its systems.
- Data architecture also involves the following aspects:
  - Data pipelines: They are the processes and workflows that move, transform, and integrate data from various sources to various destinations, such as data lakes, data warehouses, data marts, etc.
  - Data streaming: It is the technique of processing and analyzing data in real-time as it is generated or received from various sources, such as sensors, web logs, social media, etc.
  - Data analytics: It is the process of applying various methods and tools to extract insights and value from data, such as descriptive, predictive, and prescriptive analytics, data mining, data visualization, etc.
  - Data governance: It is the set of policies, rules, standards, and best practices that ensure the quality, security, privacy, and compliance of data and its systems.
  - Data platforms: They are the integrated and scalable environments that support the data architecture and enable the data pipelines, data streaming, data analytics, and data governance. They can be based on cloud computing, artificial intelligence, machine learning, Kubernetes, and other technologies.
