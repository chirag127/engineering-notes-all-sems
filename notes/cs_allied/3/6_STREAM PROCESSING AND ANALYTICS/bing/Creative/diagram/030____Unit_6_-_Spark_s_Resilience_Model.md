## Unit 6 - Spark’s Resilience Model

- Resilience is the ability to cope with stress and adversity, and to bounce back from challenges and difficulties.
- Spark’s resilience model is a framework that helps individuals and organizations to assess and improve their resilience levels.
- The model consists of four dimensions: **Self**, **Situation**, **Support**, and **Strategies**.
- Each dimension has three sub-dimensions that represent different aspects of resilience.

### Self
- Self refers to the personal characteristics and attributes that influence how one perceives and responds to stress and adversity.
- The three sub-dimensions of self are:
  - **Self-awareness**: The ability to recognize and understand one’s own emotions, thoughts, strengths, and weaknesses, and how they affect one’s behavior and performance.
  - **Self-regulation**: The ability to manage and control one’s own emotions, impulses, and reactions, and to adapt to changing circumstances and demands.
  - **Self-efficacy**: The belief in one’s own capabilities and competence to achieve one’s goals and overcome challenges.

### Situation
- Situation refers to the external factors and conditions that create or contribute to stress and adversity.
- The three sub-dimensions of situation are:
  - **Situation awareness**: The ability to perceive and comprehend the relevant information and cues in the environment, and to anticipate the potential outcomes and consequences of a situation.
  - **Situation appraisal**: The ability to evaluate and interpret the significance and implications of a situation, and to identify the opportunities and threats that it presents.
  - **Situation management**: The ability to plan and execute appropriate actions and responses to a situation, and to monitor and adjust them as needed.

### Support
- Support refers to the social resources and networks that provide assistance and encouragement to cope with stress and adversity.
- The three sub-dimensions of support are:
  - **Support seeking**: The ability and willingness to seek and accept help and advice from others when facing challenges and difficulties.
  - **Support giving**: The ability and willingness to offer and provide help and advice to others who are facing challenges and difficulties.
  - **Support utilization**: The ability and effectiveness to use and benefit from the help and advice received from others.

### Strategies
- Strategies refer to the specific techniques and methods that enhance resilience and reduce stress and adversity.
- The three sub-dimensions of strategies are:
  - **Cognitive strategies**: The techniques and methods that involve the use of mental processes, such as positive thinking, reframing, problem-solving, and goal-setting, to cope with stress and adversity.
  - **Behavioral strategies**: The techniques and methods that involve the use of physical actions, such as relaxation, exercise, time management, and self-care, to cope with stress and adversity.
  - **Emotional strategies**: The techniques and methods that involve the use of emotional expressions, such as humor, gratitude, optimism, and compassion, to cope with stress and adversity.
