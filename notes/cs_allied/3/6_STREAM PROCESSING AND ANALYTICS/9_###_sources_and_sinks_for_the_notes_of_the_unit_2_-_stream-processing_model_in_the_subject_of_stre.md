### Sources and Sinks for the notes of the Unit 2 - Stream-Processing Model in the subject of STREAM PROCESSING AND ANALYTICS

In the context of stream processing and analytics, sources and sinks are two key components that define the flow of data in a stream processing system.

Sources are the origin of the data stream. They can be a variety of data sources, including:
- Sensors and IoT devices
- Databases and data warehouses
- Social media platforms
- Log files and event logs

Sinks are the endpoints of the data stream, where the data is stored, processed, or analyzed. Some examples of sinks include:
- Databases and data warehouses
- File systems
- Analytic tools and dashboards

In summary, sources and sinks define the flow of data in a stream processing system. Sources are the origin of the data stream, while sinks are the endpoints where the data is stored, processed, or analyzed.
