# Read two numbers and print their sum, difference, product and division:

1. Take input of two numbers:
- num1 = int(input("Enter first number: "))
- num2 = int(input("Enter second number: "))

2. Print the sum:
- print("Sum:", num1 + num2)

3. Print the difference:
- print("Difference:", num1 - num2)

4. Print the product:
- print("Product:", num1 * num2)

5. Print the division:
- print("Division:", num1 / num2)

The above code will take two numbers as input from the user and print their sum, difference, product and division in a formal manner without any feelings or friendliness as required.
