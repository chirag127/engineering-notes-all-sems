# CO 1 Understand the concept of Internet of Things K3

1. Internet of Things (IoT) refers to the interconnection of everyday physical objects/devices via the internet. These devices are equipped with sensors, software, and networking capabilities that allow them to send and receive data.
2. IoT enables everyday objects to be sensed and controlled remotely across existing network infrastructure, creating opportunities for more direct integration of the physical world into computer-based systems, and resulting in improved efficiency, accuracy and economic benefit.
3. The key components of an IoT system are:
- Sensors: These detect and measure physical parameters (light, temperature, motion, etc.) and convert them into digital data.
- Connectivity: This refers to the network that transfers the data from sensors and connected devices. It can be wireless (Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, cellular, etc.) or wired (Ethernet).
- Cloud: The data from sensors and devices is sent to the cloud for storage, processing, and analysis.
- Applications: The processed data is sent to applications that can take action or share the data with other applications.
4. The key benefits of IoT are:
- Increased efficiency: IoT can automate and optimize processes which increases productivity and reduces wastage.
- Remote monitoring: IoT enables constant monitoring of devices and processes from anywhere. This allows for predictive maintenance and immediate fault detection.
- Personalized experiences: IoT devices can gather data about users and tailor the environment or applications to personal needs and preferences.
- Improved automation: IoT increases automation in homes and workplaces and enables smart and connected environments.
