### The student should have hands on experience in using various sensors like temperature, humidity, smoke, light, etc. and

1. Temperature sensor: Measures the temperature and can detect variations in temperature. Used to monitor temperature-controlled environments or detect overheating components.

2. Humidity sensor: Measures the amount of moisture in the air. Important for monitoring conditions that require specific humidity levels. Can detect high humidity that could lead to mold or water damage.

3. Smoke sensor: Detects smoke or fire. Critical safety sensor used to detect fires early and trigger alarms or other actions like shutting down equipment.

4. Light sensor: Measures ambient light levels. Can be used to detect when lights should be turned on or off based on detected light level. Important for energy efficiency and automation.

The student should gain practical experience connecting the sensors to development boards and monitoring/recording the sensor data. This hands-on work will reinforce the theoretical understanding of how the different sensors work and their applications. The data from the sensors can also be used in simple projects to detect and respond to the conditions detected by the sensors.
