#### CO 1 Understand the concept of Internet of Things K3

1. Internet of Things (IoT) refers to the interconnection of everyday physical objects and appliances via the internet. These devices are equipped with sensors, software, and network connectivity that allows them to send and receive data.
2. IoT enables everyday objects to be sensed and controlled remotely across existing network infrastructure, creating opportunities for more direct integration between the physical world and computer-based systems, and resulting in improved efficiency, accuracy and economic benefit.
3. The key components of IoT are:
- Sensors: To detect and track changes in the environment. For example, motion sensors, humidity sensors, temperature sensors, etc.
- Connectivity: To enable the transfer of data over a network. For example, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, cellular, etc.
- Processors: To collect, store, and analyze the data from sensors and facilitate connectivity. For example, microcontrollers, microprocessors, etc.
- Software: To interpret the data and provide interfaces to the users. For example, IoT platforms, IoT analytics tools, etc.
4. The potential benefits of IoT include increased efficiency, improved accuracy, economic benefit, and improved customer experience. However, IoT also brings potential risks and challenges related to security, privacy, interconnectivity, and standardization that must be addressed.
