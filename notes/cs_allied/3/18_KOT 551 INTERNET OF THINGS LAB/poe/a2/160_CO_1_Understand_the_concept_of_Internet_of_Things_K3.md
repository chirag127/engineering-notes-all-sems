# CO 1 Understand the concept of Internet of Things K3

1. Internet of Things (IoT) refers to the interconnection of everyday physical objects/devices through the internet. These devices are fitted with sensors, software, and network connectivity which allows them to send and receive data.

2. The core components of an IoT system are:

- Sensors: To sense and collect data from the surrounding environment. Eg: Temperature sensor, motion sensor, etc.
- Connectivity: To establish connectivity between the devices and the internet. Eg: Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Cellular, etc.
- Cloud: To store and process the data collected by the sensors.
- Applications: To access the data and take actions. Eg: Monitoring app, automation app, etc.

3. The key benefits of IoT are:

- Efficient Monitoring: Continuous monitoring of devices, systems, and environments leading to better optimization and control.
- Automation: Devices can be automated to perform actions based on data inputs leading to time and cost savings.
- Remote Access: Devices and systems can be accessed and controlled from any location at any time.
- Improved Accuracy: Sensors provide accurate data leading to fact-based decision making.

4. The applications of IoT include smart homes, smart cities, industrial automation, smart agriculture, logistics and transportation, healthcare, etc. IoT has the potential to radically change how businesses and people interact with the physical world.
