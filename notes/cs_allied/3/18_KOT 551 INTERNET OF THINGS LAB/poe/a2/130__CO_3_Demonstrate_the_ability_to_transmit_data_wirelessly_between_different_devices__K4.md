#### CO 3 Demonstrate the ability to transmit data wirelessly between different devices. K4

1. Bluetooth: Bluetooth is a wireless technology standard for exchanging data over short distances using radio waves in the frequency range of 2.4 GHz from fixed and mobile devices, and building personal area networks. Bluetooth enables transmission of data such as audio streams between devices like headphones and mobile phones, wireless keyboards and mice connections with computers etc.

2. Wi-Fi: Wi-Fi or Wireless Fidelity is a popular wireless networking technology that allows electronic devices to connect to the internet and network in wireless local area networks or WLANs. Wi-Fi enabled devices can connect to the network and internet through wireless access points that transmit data using radio waves. Wi-Fi is faster than Bluetooth and can connect multiple devices with a wider range but has higher power requirements.

3. Infrared: Infrared or IR is a wireless technology that uses infrared light to transmit data between devices. The devices have IR transmitter and receiver that send and detect infrared light pulses to communicate. Infrared has a shorter range and slower speed than Bluetooth or Wi-Fi and requires a direct line of sight between devices. However, it has a lower power requirement suitable for remote controls.

4. Radio Frequency: Radio frequency or RF is another wireless technology that uses radio waves to transmit data between devices. The RF signals have a longer range than infrared but can be less secure and interfere with other electronics. RF technology is commonly used in garage door openers, car locks etc. The frequency range and power level can be varied for different applications.
