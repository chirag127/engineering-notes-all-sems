

# c) Get the Status of a Bulb at a Remote Place (on the LAN) Through Web

1. A local area network (LAN) is a computer network that interconnects computers in a limited area such as a home, school, or office building.
2. The LAN can be connected to the internet through a router.
3. A web-enabled device, such as a computer, tablet, or smartphone, can be used to access the LAN remotely.
4. To access the status of a bulb at a remote place through the web, a user must first connect the bulb to the LAN.
5. A user can then use a web-enabled device to access the LAN and access the status of the bulb.
6. The status of the bulb can be checked by sending a request to the bulb and receiving a response.
7. The response will indicate whether the bulb is on or off.
