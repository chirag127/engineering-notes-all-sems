
# KOT 554 INTERNET OF THINGS LAB KCS

1. Internet of Things (IoT) is a network of physical objects that are connected to the internet, enabling them to collect and exchange data.

2. IoT can be used to automate tasks, such as controlling home appliances, monitoring energy usage, or tracking inventory in warehouses.

3. IoT devices can be connected to the internet via a variety of protocols, including Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and cellular networks.

4. IoT devices are typically equipped with sensors that detect physical conditions, such as temperature, humidity, and motion.

5. IoT applications can be used to monitor and control physical objects in real time, as well as to collect and analyze data.

6. IoT applications can also be used to create smarter cities, homes, and workplaces.

7. Security is an important consideration when deploying IoT devices, as they can be vulnerable to cyber attacks.

8. To ensure the security of IoT devices, it is important to use secure protocols, such as TLS/SSL, and to use authentication and encryption.
