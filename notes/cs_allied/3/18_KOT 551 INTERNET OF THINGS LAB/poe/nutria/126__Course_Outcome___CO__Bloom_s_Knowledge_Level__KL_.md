
## Course Outcome (CO) Bloom's Knowledge Level (KL)

1. Knowledge (KL1): The learner will be able to recall and recognize facts, terms, and concepts related to the course.
2. Comprehension (KL2): The learner will be able to explain and interpret information related to the course.
3. Application (KL3): The learner will be able to use the course material to solve problems and apply it to real-world situations.
4. Analysis (KL4): The learner will be able to break down the material into its component parts and recognize relationships between them.
5. Synthesis (KL5): The learner will be able to combine the material into a new whole and create something new.
6. Evaluation (KL6): The learner will be able to assess the validity and accuracy of the material and make judgments about it.
