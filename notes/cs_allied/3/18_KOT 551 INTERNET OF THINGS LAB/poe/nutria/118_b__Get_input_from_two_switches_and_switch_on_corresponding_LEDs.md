

### b) Get input from two switches and switch on corresponding LEDs

1. Connect two switches to the microcontroller.
2. Program the microcontroller to read the input from the switches.
3. When the input from the switches is detected, the microcontroller should switch on the corresponding LEDs.
4. The microcontroller should be programmed to ensure that the LEDs stay on until the switch is turned off.
5. The microcontroller should be programmed to detect the status of the switches and turn on/off the LEDs accordingly.
