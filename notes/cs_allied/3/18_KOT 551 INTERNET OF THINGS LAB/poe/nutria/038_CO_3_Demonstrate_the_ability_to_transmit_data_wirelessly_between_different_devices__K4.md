
# CO 3 Demonstrate the ability to transmit data wirelessly between different devices. K4

1. Wireless data transmission is the process of sending data from one device to another without the use of cables or wires. 
2. Wireless data transmission is enabled by a variety of technologies, including Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, cellular networks, and satellite communication. 
3. Wireless data transmission is used in a variety of applications, such as connecting computers and other devices to the internet, streaming audio and video, and transferring files. 
4. Wireless data transmission is also used in industrial settings, such as in automated production lines, to monitor and control equipment. 
5. To ensure a secure and reliable connection, wireless data transmission requires the use of encryption protocols, such as WPA2, and authentication methods, such as passwords. 
6. Additionally, wireless data transmission is subject to interference from other devices and environmental factors, such as walls and other obstructions.
