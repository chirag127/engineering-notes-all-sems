

# The Student and Sensors

1. Temperature Sensors - Temperature sensors measure the temperature of an environment or object. They can be used in a variety of applications, such as controlling the temperature of a room or monitoring the temperature of a machine.

2. Humidity Sensors - Humidity sensors measure the humidity of an environment or object. They can be used to monitor the humidity of a room or to measure the moisture content of a material.

3. Smoke Sensors - Smoke sensors detect the presence of smoke in an environment. They are typically used in fire detection systems, but can also be used to detect smoke from other sources.

4. Light Sensors - Light sensors measure the amount of light in an environment. They can be used to measure the amount of sunlight in a room, or to detect the presence of artificial light.

5. Other Sensors - There are many other types of sensors that can be used to measure a variety of different things. These include sensors for measuring pressure, vibration, sound, and more.
