
#### Input

- Input is the process of gathering data and information from a variety of sources in order to process it and produce useful output.
- Input can be in the form of raw data, such as numbers and text, or it can be in the form of a command from a user. 
- The data and information can be gathered from a variety of sources, including manual input from a user, sensors, or other devices.
- The input is then processed by a computer system, which produces useful output. 
- The output can be in the form of a report, a graph, or other data that can be used to make decisions or take action. 
- Input is an essential part of any computer system, as it is the first step in the process of transforming data into useful information.
