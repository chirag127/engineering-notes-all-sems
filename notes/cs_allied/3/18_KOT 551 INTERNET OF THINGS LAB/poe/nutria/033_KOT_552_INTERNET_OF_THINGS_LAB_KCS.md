
# KOT 552 INTERNET OF THINGS LAB KCS

1. The Internet of Things (IoT) is a network of physical objects, such as devices, sensors, and actuators, that are connected to the internet and can communicate with each other. 
2. IoT enables the collection and exchange of data between connected devices, allowing for the automation of processes, improved efficiency, and increased safety. 
3. IoT applications include smart homes, connected cars, and industrial automation.
4. IoT systems consist of three main components: hardware, software, and communication. 
5. Hardware components include sensors, actuators, and microcontrollers. 
6. Software components include operating systems, middleware, and applications. 
7. Communication components include networks, protocols, and gateways. 
8. Security is a major concern in IoT systems and includes authentication, authorization, and encryption.
