
# CO 3 Demonstrate the Ability to Transmit Data Wirelessly Between Different Devices. K4

1. Wireless communication is a technology that allows two or more devices to communicate without the need for physical connections. 
2. The most common type of wireless communication is Wi-Fi, which is used to connect computers, phones, and other devices to the internet. 
3. Bluetooth is another type of wireless communication that is commonly used to connect devices such as headphones, speakers, and keyboards.
4. Near Field Communication (NFC) is a type of wireless communication that is used to exchange information between two devices in close proximity.
5. Radio Frequency Identification (RFID) is a type of wireless communication that uses radio waves to identify and track objects.
6. Infrared (IR) is a type of wireless communication that is used to control devices such as TVs and remote controls.
7. Zigbee is a type of wireless communication that is used for home automation and the Internet of Things (IoT).
8. Wireless communication can be used to transmit data, audio, and video between devices.
9. Wireless communication can also be used for remote control of devices such as robots and drones.
10. Security measures such as encryption and authentication can be used to protect the data transmitted over wireless networks.
