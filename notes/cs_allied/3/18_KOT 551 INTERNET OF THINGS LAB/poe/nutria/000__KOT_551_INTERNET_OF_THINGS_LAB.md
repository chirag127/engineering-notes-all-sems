
# KOT 551 INTERNET OF THINGS LAB
1. Internet of Things (IoT) is a network of physical devices, such as sensors, actuators, and controllers, connected through the internet. 
2. IoT enables the collection and exchange of data between connected devices and applications. 
3. IoT devices are used to monitor and control physical objects, such as temperature, humidity, pressure, and more. 
4. IoT applications include smart homes, smart cities, industrial automation, and healthcare. 
5. IoT devices can be connected to the internet using various communication protocols such as Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and cellular networks. 
6. Security is a major concern when it comes to IoT, as connected devices can be vulnerable to cyber-attacks. 
7. To ensure the security of IoT devices, it is important to use strong authentication, encryption, and access control.
