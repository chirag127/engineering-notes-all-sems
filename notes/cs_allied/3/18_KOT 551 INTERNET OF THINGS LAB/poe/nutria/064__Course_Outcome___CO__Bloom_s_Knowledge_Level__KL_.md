

## Course Outcome (CO) Bloom's Knowledge Level (KL)

1. Remembering (KL 1): The ability to recall facts, terms, and concepts.
2. Understanding (KL 2): The ability to comprehend the meaning, translation, interpolation, and interpretation of instructions and problems.
3. Applying (KL 3): The ability to use learned material in new and concrete situations.
4. Analyzing (KL 4): The ability to break down material into its component parts so that its organizational structure may be understood.
5. Evaluating (KL 5): The ability to judge the value of material for a given purpose.
6. Creating (KL 6): The ability to put parts together to form a whole and to make judgements about the relative value of that whole.
