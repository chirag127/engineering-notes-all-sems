

# b) Read two numbers and print their sum, difference, product and division

1. First, define two variables, `a` and `b`, to store the two numbers.
2. To print the sum of the two numbers, use the following code: `print(a + b)`
3. To print the difference of the two numbers, use the following code: `print(a - b)`
4. To print the product of the two numbers, use the following code: `print(a * b)`
5. To print the division of the two numbers, use the following code: `print(a / b)`
