
# At the End of Course, the Student Will Be Able To:

1. Understand the fundamentals of the subject matter.
2. Apply the concepts to real-world scenarios.
3. Analyze data to form conclusions.
4. Develop strategies for solving complex problems.
5. Communicate effectively in both written and verbal form.
6. Utilize technology to enhance productivity.
7. Demonstrate the ability to work both independently and collaboratively.
