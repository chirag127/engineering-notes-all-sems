
#### CO 1 Understand the concept of Internet of Things K3

* The Internet of Things (IoT) is a network of physical objects that are connected to the internet and can collect, send, and receive data.
* These objects can range from everyday items such as home appliances, medical devices, and vehicles, to industrial equipment such as factory machines and robots.
* IoT devices are connected to the internet through a variety of communication protocols such as Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and cellular networks.
* IoT devices can be used to monitor and control physical objects in the real world. For example, a connected thermostat can be used to adjust the temperature in a home, while a connected car can be used to monitor its location and speed.
* IoT devices can also be used to collect data from the physical world. For example, a connected sensor can be used to measure the temperature in a room, or a connected camera can be used to monitor the activity in a public space.
* IoT devices can also be used to automate processes in the physical world. For example, a connected irrigation system can be used to automatically water plants in a garden, or a connected security system can be used to automatically sound an alarm when an intruder is detected.
