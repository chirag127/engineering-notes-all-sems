

#### d) Area of a Given Shape (Rectangle, Triangle and Circle)

* Rectangle: The area of a rectangle is calculated by multiplying its length and width. For example, if the length of a rectangle is 5 cm and its width is 3 cm, then the area of the rectangle is 15 cm<sup>2</sup>.

* Triangle: The area of a triangle is calculated by multiplying its base and height and dividing the result by two. For example, if the base of a triangle is 5 cm and its height is 3 cm, then the area of the triangle is 7.5 cm<sup>2</sup>.

* Circle: The area of a circle is calculated by multiplying its radius and the value of pi (π) and squaring the result. For example, if the radius of a circle is 5 cm, then the area of the circle is 78.5 cm<sup>2</sup>.
