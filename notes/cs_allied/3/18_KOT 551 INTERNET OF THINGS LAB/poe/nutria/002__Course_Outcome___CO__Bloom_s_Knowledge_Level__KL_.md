
## Course Outcome (CO) Bloom’s Knowledge Level (KL)

1. **Remembering (KL 1):** Being able to recall facts and details from memory.
2. **Understanding (KL 2):** Being able to comprehend the meaning of material.
3. **Applying (KL 3):** Being able to use acquired knowledge in a new situation.
4. **Analyzing (KL 4):** Being able to break down material into its component parts and understand how the parts relate to one another.
5. **Evaluating (KL 5):** Being able to make judgments based on criteria and standards.
6. **Creating (KL 6):** Being able to use existing knowledge to create something new.
