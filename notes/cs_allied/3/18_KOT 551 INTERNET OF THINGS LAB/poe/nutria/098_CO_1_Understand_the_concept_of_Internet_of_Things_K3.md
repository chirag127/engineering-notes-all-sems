
# CO 1 Understand the concept of Internet of Things K3

The Internet of Things (IoT) is a concept that refers to the connection of physical objects to the internet, allowing them to send and receive data. This connection enables the objects to interact with each other and with users, and to be monitored and controlled remotely. IoT devices can be used in a variety of applications, such as home automation, smart cities, healthcare, and industrial automation.

The main components of the IoT are:

* Sensors: Sensors are used to collect data from the environment, such as temperature, humidity, and pressure.

* Actuators: Actuators are used to control the environment, such as turning on lights or opening doors.

* Connectivity: Connectivity is used to send and receive data between the sensors, actuators, and users.

* Cloud Computing: Cloud computing is used to store and process the data collected from the sensors and actuators.

* Analytics: Analytics are used to analyze the data collected from the sensors and actuators.

* Security: Security is used to protect the data collected from the sensors and actuators, as well as the devices themselves.

The IoT has the potential to revolutionize how we interact with our environment and with each other, and to create new opportunities for businesses and individuals alike.
