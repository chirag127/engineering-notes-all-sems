
# KOT 553 INTERNET OF THINGS LAB KCS

This course focuses on the design and implementation of Internet of Things (IoT) systems and applications. Topics include the fundamentals of IoT, network protocols, distributed computing, sensor networks, and data analytics. The course also covers the development of IoT applications using different platforms, such as Arduino, Raspberry Pi, and Intel Edison.

1. Introduction to Internet of Things (IoT): Overview of IoT, its applications, and implications.
2. Network Protocols: Different types of networks, protocols, and communication technologies used in IoT.
3. Distributed Computing: Distributed computing models and algorithms for IoT applications.
4. Sensor Networks: Sensor networks, their design, and applications.
5. Data Analytics: Data analysis techniques for IoT applications.
6. IoT Platforms: Platforms for developing IoT applications, such as Arduino, Raspberry Pi, and Intel Edison.
7. IoT Applications: Design and development of IoT applications using different platforms.
