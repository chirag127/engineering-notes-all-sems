#### CO 1 Understand the concept of Internet of Things K3

Internet of Things (IoT) is a network of physical objects, devices, vehicles, and other items that are embedded with sensors, software, and connectivity, enabling them to collect and exchange data over the internet.

Here are some key points to help you understand the concept of IoT:

- IoT devices can be anything from smart home appliances, wearables, vehicles, industrial machines, and more. These devices communicate with each other and with the internet to provide various functionalities and services.
- The data collected by IoT devices can be used to improve efficiency, productivity, and decision-making in various industries such as healthcare, agriculture, manufacturing, and transportation.
- IoT devices are equipped with sensors that collect data such as temperature, humidity, pressure, and more. This data is then processed and transmitted to other devices or servers for analysis and decision-making.
- The connectivity of IoT devices is enabled by various wireless technologies such as Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and cellular networks. This allows them to communicate with each other and with the internet in real-time.
- IoT devices can be controlled and monitored remotely through a smartphone or computer, making them more convenient and accessible to users.
- Security is a major concern when it comes to IoT devices, as they are vulnerable to hacking and cyber attacks. Ensuring the security and privacy of IoT devices and their data is crucial for their widespread adoption and success.

Knowing the basics of IoT is essential in today's world, as it is rapidly transforming various industries and is expected to have a major impact on our daily lives. Understanding the concept of IoT will help you stay informed and prepared for the future of technology.
