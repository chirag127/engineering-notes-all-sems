## Course Outcome (CO) Bloom’s Knowledge Level (KL)

In education, Course Outcome (CO) refers to the specific skills, knowledge, and attitudes that a student is expected to acquire after completing a course. Bloom’s Knowledge Level (KL) on the other hand, refers to the classification of learning objectives according to their cognitive complexity. Bloom's Taxonomy categorizes learning objectives into six levels: Remembering, Understanding, Applying, Analyzing, Evaluating, and Creating. In this section, we will discuss how to determine the Bloom’s Knowledge Level of Course Outcomes.

### Remembering (KL 1)

- Identify and recall previously learned information, concepts, and facts related to the course.
- Memorize and recall definitions, dates, names, and other information.
- Recognize and recall events, processes, and procedures.

### Understanding (KL 2)

- Interpret and explain the meaning of information, concepts, and ideas related to the course.
- Summarize and paraphrase information in one’s own words.
- Compare and contrast different concepts and ideas.
- Explain cause and effect relationships.

### Applying (KL 3)

- Use previously learned information, concepts, and ideas to solve problems or complete tasks related to the course.
- Apply principles, theories, and concepts to real-world situations.
- Use critical thinking skills to analyze and evaluate information.

### Analyzing (KL 4)

- Break down complex information, concepts, and ideas into smaller parts to understand their relationships and how they work together.
- Identify patterns and connections between different pieces of information.
- Analyze and evaluate the effectiveness of different methods, techniques, and strategies.

### Evaluating (KL 5)

- Make judgments and form opinions about the quality, value, and effectiveness of information, concepts, and ideas related to the course.
- Evaluate the strengths and weaknesses of different arguments, theories, and perspectives.
- Apply critical thinking skills to make informed decisions and solve problems.

### Creating (KL 6)

- Use previously learned information, concepts, and ideas to create new and original products, solutions, or ideas related to the course.
- Develop and design new methods, techniques, and strategies.
- Apply critical thinking skills to generate innovative solutions to complex problems.

In conclusion, understanding the Bloom’s Knowledge Level of Course Outcomes is important for educators to design effective curriculums and assessments that align with the learning objectives of the course. By using Bloom’s Taxonomy, educators can create a learning environment that promotes critical thinking, problem-solving, and creativity.
