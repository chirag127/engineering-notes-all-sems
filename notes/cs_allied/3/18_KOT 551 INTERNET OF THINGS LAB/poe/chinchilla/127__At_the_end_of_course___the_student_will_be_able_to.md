### At the end of course, the student will be able to:

- Demonstrate a comprehensive understanding of the course material and its application in relevant contexts.
- Analyze and evaluate complex ideas, arguments, and concepts related to the course material.
- Synthesize information from various sources and present it in a clear and coherent manner.
- Apply critical thinking skills to solve problems related to the course material.
- Develop effective communication skills, both written and verbal, to express ideas and arguments clearly and persuasively.
- Understand and apply ethical principles and standards relevant to the course material.
- Work collaboratively with others to achieve common goals related to the course material.
- Develop a lifelong learning attitude and a desire to continue learning and growing in the field.
- Use technology and other resources effectively to enhance learning and stay up-to-date in the field.
- Apply knowledge and skills gained from the course to real-world situations and challenges.
