#### Note: The Instructor may add/delete/modify/tune experiments.

- Experiments are an essential part of the scientific process, allowing researchers to test hypotheses and gather data to support or refute their ideas.
- However, experiments can be complex and require careful planning and execution to ensure accurate results.
- Therefore, it is common for instructors to make changes to experiments, such as adding or deleting steps, modifying procedures, or tuning the equipment used.
- Students should be aware that these changes can significantly affect the outcome of the experiment and may require adjustments to their approach or interpretation of the results.
- It is important for students to carefully read and follow the instructions provided by the instructor and to ask questions if anything is unclear.
- In addition, students should keep detailed records of their experimental procedures and results, including any changes made by the instructor, to ensure accuracy and reproducibility.
- Finally, students should be prepared for unexpected results or deviations from the expected outcome, as this is a natural part of the experimental process and can provide valuable insights and opportunities for further investigation.
