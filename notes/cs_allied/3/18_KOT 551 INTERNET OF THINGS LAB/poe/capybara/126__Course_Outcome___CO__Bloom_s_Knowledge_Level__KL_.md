## Course Outcome (CO) Bloom’s Knowledge Level (KL)

In order to understand the course outcomes and the corresponding Bloom's knowledge level, it is important to have a clear understanding of both. The course outcomes refer to the specific learning outcomes that are expected of students upon completion of a course. Bloom's knowledge level, on the other hand, refers to the different levels of learning that students can achieve in a particular subject.

The following are some of the course outcomes and their corresponding Bloom's knowledge level:

### CO1: Understand the fundamental concepts of the subject matter.

- KL1: Remembering - Recall basic facts, terms, and concepts related to the subject matter.
- KL2: Understanding - Explain the meaning of basic concepts and principles related to the subject matter.

### CO2: Analyze and interpret data related to the subject matter.

- KL3: Applying - Use knowledge of the subject matter to solve problems and analyze data.
- KL4: Analyzing - Break down information into its component parts and understand relationships between them.

### CO3: Evaluate and synthesize information related to the subject matter.

- KL5: Evaluating - Make judgments about the value, quality, or effectiveness of information related to the subject matter.
- KL6: Creating - Use knowledge of the subject matter to generate new ideas, products, or solutions.

### CO4: Communicate effectively about the subject matter.

- KL2: Understanding - Explain the meaning of basic concepts and principles related to the subject matter.
- KL6: Creating - Use knowledge of the subject matter to create clear and effective communication materials.

By understanding the course outcomes and the corresponding Bloom's knowledge level, students can better prepare themselves for exams and assessments. It is important to focus on higher levels of learning, such as analyzing and creating, in order to achieve a deeper understanding of the subject matter.
