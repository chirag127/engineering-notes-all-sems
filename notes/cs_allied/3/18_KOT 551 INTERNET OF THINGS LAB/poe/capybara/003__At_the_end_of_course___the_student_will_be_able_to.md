### At the end of course, the student will be able to:

- Understand the fundamental principles and concepts of the subject matter, including its history, evolution, and various subfields.
- Analyze and critique scholarly literature, research, and data related to the subject matter using appropriate academic standards and methods.
- Apply theoretical frameworks and models to real-world problems and scenarios, and evaluate their effectiveness and limitations.
- Communicate effectively and persuasively in oral and written formats, using appropriate language, tone, and style for different audiences and purposes.
- Collaborate and work effectively in teams, using interpersonal and leadership skills to achieve common goals and objectives.
- Use relevant tools, technologies, and software to conduct research, analyze data, and present findings in a professional and visually appealing manner.
- Demonstrate ethical and responsible behavior, including adherence to academic integrity policies, respect for human subjects and participants, and sensitivity to cultural and social diversity.
- Develop a personal and professional development plan that reflects their interests, strengths, and career goals, and identifies opportunities for growth and learning.
