# Note: The Instructor may add/delete/modify/tune experiments

Here are some important points to keep in mind regarding the topic of modifying experiments:

- The instructor has the authority to make changes to the experiments as needed. This could include adding new experiments, deleting existing experiments, or making modifications to the experimental procedures.
- It is important for students to stay up-to-date with any changes made to the experiments. This can be done by regularly checking the course materials and communicating with the instructor.
- When modifications are made to an experiment, it is possible that the results may be affected. Therefore, it is important to carefully consider any changes before implementing them.
- Students should also be aware that modifications to experiments may require additional resources or equipment. It is important to plan accordingly in order to ensure that the experiment can be completed successfully.
- If a modification is made to an experiment, it is important to document the change in detail. This documentation should include the reason for the change, the specific modifications made, and any potential impact on the results.
- Finally, students should always follow the instructions provided by the instructor for each experiment. This will help to ensure that the experiment is completed accurately and safely.
