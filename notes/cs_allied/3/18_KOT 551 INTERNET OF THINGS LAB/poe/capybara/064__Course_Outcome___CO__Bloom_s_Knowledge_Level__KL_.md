## Course Outcome (CO) Bloom’s Knowledge Level (KL)

In education, Bloom's Taxonomy is a framework used to categorize educational goals into a hierarchy of learning objectives. The following is a breakdown of the course outcomes and their corresponding Bloom’s knowledge level:

### CO1 - Understand fundamental concepts of the subject matter

- Recall basic principles and concepts related to the subject matter.
- Explain key terms and definitions associated with the subject matter.
- Identify and describe the main components of the subject matter.

### CO2 - Analyze and interpret data

- Examine data in order to identify patterns and relationships.
- Interpret results of data analyses to draw conclusions and make recommendations.
- Evaluate data quality and identify potential sources of error or bias.

### CO3 - Apply subject matter knowledge to solve problems

- Use subject matter knowledge to identify and define problems.
- Analyze problems and develop solutions using subject matter knowledge.
- Evaluate and refine solutions to ensure effectiveness.

### CO4 - Communicate effectively

- Communicate subject matter information clearly and effectively in written or oral form.
- Use appropriate language and terminology to convey ideas.
- Tailor communication to the needs and interests of the target audience.

### CO5 - Demonstrate ethical and professional behavior

- Adhere to ethical and professional standards in all aspects of subject matter practice.
- Identify and address potential ethical and professional conflicts in subject matter practice.
- Take responsibility for personal and professional development, including ongoing learning and skill-building.
