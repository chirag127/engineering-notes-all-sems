# Area of a given shape

When calculating the area of a shape, it is important to know the appropriate values based on the shape. In this section, we will focus on three common shapes - rectangle, triangle and circle.

## Rectangle

A rectangle is a four-sided shape with opposite sides being equal in length and parallel to each other. The formula to calculate the area of a rectangle is:

```
area = length x width
```

where length and width are the two sides of the rectangle.

## Triangle

A triangle is a three-sided shape. The formula to calculate the area of a triangle is:

```
area = 1/2 x base x height
```

where base is the length of the bottom of the triangle, and height is the distance from the base to the top point of the triangle.

## Circle

A circle is a round shape with no corners or edges. The formula to calculate the area of a circle is:

```
area = π x radius^2
```

where π (pi) is a mathematical constant approximately equal to 3.14, and radius is the distance from the center of the circle to its edge.

It is important to note that when reading the appropriate values from standard, the units should be consistent throughout. For example, if the length and width of a rectangle are given in meters, the area will be in square meters. Similarly, if the radius of a circle is given in centimeters, the area will be in square centimeters.

By understanding the appropriate formulas and values, you can easily calculate the area of a given shape. Practice solving problems using these formulas to improve your understanding and prepare for exams.
