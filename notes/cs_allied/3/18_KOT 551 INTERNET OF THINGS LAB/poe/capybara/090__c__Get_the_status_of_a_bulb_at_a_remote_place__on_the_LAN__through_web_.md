#### c) Get the status of a bulb at a remote place (on the LAN) through web.

If you are looking for a way to get the status of a bulb at a remote place on the LAN through web, here are some steps you can follow:

1. Ensure that the bulb and the device from which you want to get the status are connected to the same local area network (LAN).

2. Find out the IP address of the bulb. You can usually do this by accessing the router settings and looking for the connected devices list.

3. Open a web browser on the device from which you want to get the status of the bulb and enter the IP address of the bulb in the address bar.

4. If the bulb has a web interface, it should load in the browser window. You may need to enter a username and password to access the interface.

5. Look for the status of the bulb on the interface. This could be in the form of a button or a status indicator.

6. If the bulb does not have a web interface, you may need to use a third-party app or software to get its status. Look for apps or software that are compatible with the type of bulb you have and follow their instructions.

7. If you want to automate the process of getting the status of the bulb, you can use programming languages like Python to create scripts that can interact with the bulb's web interface and retrieve its status.

Remember to always ensure the security of your LAN and the devices connected to it. Use strong passwords and keep your software and firmware up-to-date to protect against vulnerabilities.
