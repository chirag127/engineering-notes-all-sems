# Area of a Given Shape

## Introduction
In geometry, the area of a shape is the amount of space that it takes up. The area of a shape can be calculated by using different formulas depending on the shape. In this section, we will discuss how to calculate the area of three common shapes: rectangle, triangle, and circle, by reading the shape and appropriate values from the standard.

## Rectangle
- A rectangle is a four-sided shape with opposite sides that are equal in length and parallel to each other.
- The area of a rectangle can be calculated by multiplying its length and width.
- If the length of a rectangle is l and its width is w, then the area of the rectangle can be calculated using the formula: `Area = l * w`.

## Triangle
- A triangle is a three-sided shape.
- The area of a triangle can be calculated by using the formula: `Area = 1/2 * base * height`.
- The base of a triangle is the length of its bottom side, and the height of a triangle is the perpendicular distance from the base to the opposite vertex.

## Circle
- A circle is a shape with a curved boundary that is equidistant from the center.
- The area of a circle can be calculated by using the formula: `Area = π * r^2`.
- Here, `π` is a mathematical constant which is approximately 3.14, and `r` is the radius of the circle.

## Conclusion
In conclusion, the area of a shape can be calculated by using the appropriate formula, depending on the shape. For a rectangle, the area is calculated by multiplying its length and width. For a triangle, the area is calculated by using the formula: `Area = 1/2 * base * height`. For a circle, the area is calculated by using the formula: `Area = π * r^2`. By reading the shape and appropriate values from the standard, we can easily calculate the area of these common shapes.
