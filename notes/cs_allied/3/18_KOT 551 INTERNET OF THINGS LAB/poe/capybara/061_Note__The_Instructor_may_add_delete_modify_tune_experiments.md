# Note: The Instructor may add/delete/modify/tune experiments

- As a student, it is important to understand that the instructor may make changes to the experiments that you are expected to perform.

- These changes could be in the form of addition, deletion, modification, or tuning of the experiments.

- The reason for such changes could be manifold. It could be due to the availability of resources, time constraints, or the need to align the experiments with the learning objectives of the course.

- It is important to pay close attention to any announcements or updates made by the instructor regarding the experiments.

- Be sure to read the updated experiment instructions carefully and thoroughly before attempting to perform the experiment.

- If you have any doubts or questions regarding the updated instructions, do not hesitate to clarify them with the instructor.

- It is also important to be flexible and adaptable when performing experiments. Sometimes, unexpected issues may arise that could require you to modify your approach to the experiment.

- Remember to document any changes that you make to the experiment instructions or your approach to the experiment.

- This will help you to better understand the experiment and its underlying principles, and will also help you to accurately report your findings and conclusions.

- Ultimately, the goal of performing experiments is to learn and gain a deeper understanding of the subject matter. By being open to changes and being adaptable, you can make the most out of your experiments and enhance your learning experience.
