#### b) Get input from two switches and switch on corresponding LEDs

To get input from two switches and switch on corresponding LEDs, the following steps can be followed:

1. Connect the two switches to the input pins of the microcontroller.
2. Connect the two LEDs to the output pins of the microcontroller.
3. Write a program to read the input from the switches and turn on the corresponding LEDs.
4. Upload the program to the microcontroller.
5. Test the circuit by pressing the switches and observing the LEDs.

In this way, the input from the two switches can be used to control the corresponding LEDs. The program can be modified to implement different behaviors, such as turning on both LEDs when both switches are pressed, or turning off the LEDs when the switches are released. The circuit can also be expanded to include more switches and LEDs, or other components such as buzzers or motors.
