#### CO 3 Demonstrate the ability to transmit data wirelessly between different devices. K4

1. **Wireless data transmission** refers to the transfer of information between two or more devices without the use of wires or cables.
2. There are several **wireless technologies** that can be used for data transmission, including Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, cellular networks, and satellite communication.
3. **Wi-Fi** is a popular wireless technology that uses radio waves to transmit data between devices. It is commonly used for internet access and local area networking.
4. **Bluetooth** is another wireless technology that uses radio waves to transmit data between devices. It is commonly used for short-range communication, such as between a smartphone and a wireless headset.
5. **Cellular networks** use radio waves to transmit data between mobile devices and cell towers. These networks are commonly used for mobile internet access and voice communication.
6. **Satellite communication** uses satellites in orbit around the Earth to transmit data between devices. This technology is commonly used for global positioning systems (GPS) and satellite television.
7. To demonstrate the ability to transmit data wirelessly between different devices, one could set up a wireless network using Wi-Fi or Bluetooth and transfer files or stream media between devices.
8. Another way to demonstrate this ability is to use a cellular network to access the internet on a mobile device or to make a voice call.
9. It is important to understand the **strengths and limitations** of each wireless technology in order to choose the most appropriate one for a given task. For example, Wi-Fi is well-suited for high-speed data transfer over short distances, while satellite communication is better for long-distance communication in remote areas.
