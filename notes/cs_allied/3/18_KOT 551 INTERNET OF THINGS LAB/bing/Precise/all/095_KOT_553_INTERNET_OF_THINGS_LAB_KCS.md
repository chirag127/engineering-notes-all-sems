# KOT 553 INTERNET OF THINGS LAB KCS

- KOT 553 is a course on the Internet of Things (IoT) Lab offered by the KCS department.
- The course focuses on the practical implementation of IoT concepts and technologies.
- Students will learn how to design, develop, and deploy IoT systems.
- The course covers topics such as sensors, actuators, connectivity, cloud computing, and data analytics.
- Students will work on hands-on projects to apply their knowledge and skills.
- The course is designed to provide students with a strong foundation in IoT and prepare them for careers in this rapidly growing field.
