# Course Outcome (CO) and Bloom’s Knowledge Level (KL)

Course Outcome (CO) refers to the expected knowledge, skills, and attitudes that students should possess upon completion of a specific course. These outcomes are used to assess the effectiveness of the course and to ensure that the course content aligns with the overall program goals.

Bloom’s Knowledge Level (KL) is a framework for categorizing educational learning objectives into levels of complexity and specificity. The levels are: Remembering, Understanding, Applying, Analyzing, Evaluating, and Creating. These levels can be used to assess the level of understanding that students have achieved in a course.

- Bloom’s KL provides a framework for categorizing educational learning objectives.
