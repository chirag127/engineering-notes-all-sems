# CO 1 Understand the concept of Internet of Things K3

The Internet of Things (IoT) is a concept that describes the interconnectivity of everyday objects and devices through the internet or other communication networks. These devices, often referred to as "smart" devices, are equipped with sensors, processing ability, and software that allow them to collect, store, and share data with other devices and systems.

1. **Description:** IoT includes many "smart," computer-like devices that can connect with the Internet or interact via wireless networks. These "things" include phones, appliances, thermostats, lighting systems, irrigation systems, security cameras, vehicles, and even animals and cities .
2. **Data Collection and Sharing:** IoT devices can collect data, store data, or share data with each other over their connections. This data can only be accessed by other smart-things .
3. **Physical Objects:** IoT describes physical objects (or groups of such objects) with sensors, processing ability, software, and other technologies that connect and exchange data with other devices and systems .
4. **Applications:** IoT technology can be used in various applications, such as detecting popular areas in a showroom, identifying fast-selling components, and automatically aligning sales data with supply . It is also enabling smart cities and, in the future, driverless cars .
5. **Fourth Industrial Revolution:** Along with other emerging technologies such as AI, the IoT is part of the Fourth Industrial Revolution .
