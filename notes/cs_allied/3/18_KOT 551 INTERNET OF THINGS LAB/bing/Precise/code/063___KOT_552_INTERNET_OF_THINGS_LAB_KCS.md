# KOT 552 INTERNET OF THINGS LAB KCS

- Internet of Things (IoT) is a network of physical objects or "things" embedded with electronics, software, sensors, and connectivity to enable these objects to collect and exchange data.
- IoT allows objects to be sensed and controlled remotely across existing network infrastructure, creating opportunities for more direct integration of the physical world into computer-based systems, and resulting in improved efficiency, accuracy, and economic benefit.
- The IoT Lab course provides hands-on experience in designing and implementing IoT systems.
- The course covers the following topics:
  - Introduction to IoT
  - IoT Architecture
  - IoT Protocols
  - IoT Devices and Sensors
  - IoT Data Analytics
  - IoT Security
  - IoT Applications
- Students will work on projects to design and implement IoT systems using various hardware and software platforms.
- The course is designed for students with a background in computer science or a related field.
- The course is a combination of lectures, lab sessions, and project work.
- The course is graded based on lab assignments, project work, and a final exam.
