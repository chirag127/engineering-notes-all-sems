#### Note: The Instructor may add/delete/modify/tune experiments

- It is important to note that the instructor has the authority to make changes to the experiments.
- This means that the instructor may add new experiments, delete existing ones, modify the details of the experiments, or tune them to better suit the needs of the class.
- As a student, it is important to be aware of these potential changes and to be prepared to adapt to them.
- This flexibility allows the instructor to tailor the course to the specific needs and interests of the students, and to ensure that the course remains up-to-date and relevant.
- It is recommended to regularly check for updates and to communicate with the instructor to stay informed about any changes to the experiments.
