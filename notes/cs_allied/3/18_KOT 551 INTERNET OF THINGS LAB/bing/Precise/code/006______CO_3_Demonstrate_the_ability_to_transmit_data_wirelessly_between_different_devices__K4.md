#### CO 3 Demonstrate the ability to transmit data wirelessly between different devices. K4

1. Wireless data transmission is the transfer of information between two or more devices without the use of wires or cables.
2. There are several technologies that enable wireless data transmission, including Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, and cellular networks.
3. Bluetooth is a short-range wireless technology that is commonly used to connect devices such as smartphones, tablets, and laptops to peripherals such as headphones, speakers, and keyboards.
4. Wi-Fi is a wireless networking technology that uses radio waves to provide high-speed internet and network connections to devices within a local area network (LAN).
5. Cellular networks use radio waves to transmit data over long distances, allowing mobile devices to connect to the internet and make phone calls from almost anywhere.
6. To transmit data wirelessly between different devices, the devices must be equipped with the appropriate wireless technology and be within range of each other or a wireless access point.
7. The process of transmitting data wirelessly between different devices typically involves establishing a connection, sending the data, and confirming that the data has been received.
8. Wireless data transmission has many advantages, including increased mobility, flexibility, and convenience. However, it is important to ensure that wireless connections are secure to prevent unauthorized access to sensitive information.
