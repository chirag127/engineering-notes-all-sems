## Course Outcome (CO) Bloom’s Knowledge Level (KL)

- Course Outcome (CO) refers to the expected knowledge, skills, and attitudes that students should possess upon completion of a specific course.
- COs are statements that describe the measurable skills and abilities that students should be able to demonstrate as a result of completing a course.
- Bloom’s Taxonomy is a framework for categorizing educational goals and objectives into different levels of complexity and specificity.
- Bloom’s Knowledge Level (KL) is the first and most basic level of Bloom’s Taxonomy, which focuses on the recall and recognition of information.
- At the Knowledge Level, students are expected to remember and recall basic facts, concepts, and principles related to the course material.
- COs can be mapped to different levels of Bloom’s Taxonomy, including the Knowledge Level, to ensure that the course is designed to meet the desired learning outcomes.
- By aligning COs with Bloom’s Taxonomy, instructors can design assessments and activities that target the appropriate level of cognitive complexity and help students achieve the desired learning outcomes.
