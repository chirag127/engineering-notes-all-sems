### The student should have hands-on experience in using various sensors like temperature, humidity, smoke, light, etc.

- **Temperature sensor:** A temperature sensor is a device that measures the temperature of its environment and converts the input data into electronic data to record, monitor, or signal temperature changes.
- **Humidity sensor:** A humidity sensor is a device that measures the relative humidity of the air around it. It works by detecting changes in electrical resistance or capacitance caused by moisture in the air.
- **Smoke sensor:** A smoke sensor is a device that detects the presence of smoke in the air. It is commonly used in fire alarms and smoke detectors to alert people to the presence of a fire.
- **Light sensor:** A light sensor is a device that measures the amount of light in its environment. It is commonly used in automatic lighting systems to turn lights on or off based on the amount of natural light available.

Having hands-on experience with these sensors can help students understand how they work and how they can be used in various applications. This can be valuable knowledge for students pursuing careers in fields such as engineering, science, or technology.
