# Note: The Instructor may add/delete/modify/tune experiments

- This statement indicates that the instructor has the authority to make changes to the experiments being conducted.
- The instructor may add new experiments to the curriculum to enhance the learning experience.
- The instructor may also delete experiments that are no longer relevant or necessary.
- The instructor may modify or tune existing experiments to better align with the learning objectives or to improve the effectiveness of the experiment.
- This flexibility allows the instructor to tailor the experiments to the needs of the students and the course.
