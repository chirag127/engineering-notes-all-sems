# KOT 553 INTERNET OF THINGS LAB KCS

- Internet of Things (IoT) is a network of physical objects or "things" embedded with electronics, software, sensors, and connectivity to enable these objects to collect and exchange data.
- IoT allows objects to be sensed and controlled remotely across existing network infrastructure, creating opportunities for more direct integration of the physical world into computer-based systems, and resulting in improved efficiency, accuracy, and economic benefit.
- The IoT Lab course (KOT 553) provides students with hands-on experience in designing, building, and deploying IoT systems.
- The course covers topics such as IoT architecture, sensors and actuators, communication protocols, data analysis, and security.
- Students will work on projects to develop IoT solutions for real-world problems, using tools and platforms such as Arduino, Raspberry Pi, and cloud services.
- The course is designed for students with a background in computer science or a related field, and assumes prior knowledge of programming and basic electronics.
- The course is part of the KCS (Korea Computer Science) program, which offers a range of courses in computer science and related fields.
