## Course Outcome (CO) Bloom’s Knowledge Level (KL)

- Course Outcome (CO) refers to the expected knowledge, skills, and attitudes that students should possess upon completion of a specific course.
- COs are usually defined by the instructor or the institution and are used to evaluate the effectiveness of the course in achieving its intended goals.
- Bloom’s Taxonomy is a framework for categorizing educational goals and objectives into different levels of complexity and specificity.
- The Knowledge Level (KL) of Bloom’s Taxonomy refers to the lowest level of the hierarchy, where the focus is on the recall of information.
- At the Knowledge Level, students are expected to remember and recall basic facts, concepts, and principles related to the course material.
- COs can be mapped to different levels of Bloom’s Taxonomy, including the Knowledge Level, to ensure that the course is designed to facilitate the achievement of the desired learning outcomes.
- By aligning COs with Bloom’s Taxonomy, instructors can design assessments and instructional activities that target the appropriate level of cognitive complexity and promote deeper learning.
