#### CO 3 Demonstrate the ability to transmit data wirelessly between different devices. K4

1. Wireless data transmission is the transfer of information between two or more devices without the use of wires or cables.
2. There are several technologies that enable wireless data transmission, including Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, and cellular networks.
3. Bluetooth is a short-range wireless technology that allows devices to communicate with each other over distances of up to 10 meters. It is commonly used for transmitting data between smartphones, tablets, and other personal devices.
4. Wi-Fi is a wireless networking technology that uses radio waves to provide high-speed internet and network connections. It is commonly used in homes, offices, and public places to provide wireless internet access.
5. Cellular networks use radio waves to transmit data between mobile devices and cell towers. These networks are commonly used to provide mobile internet access and to enable smartphones to make calls and send text messages.
6. To transmit data wirelessly between different devices, the devices must be equipped with the appropriate wireless technology and must be within range of each other or a wireless network.
7. The process of transmitting data wirelessly involves encoding the data into a signal that can be transmitted over the airwaves, transmitting the signal, and then decoding the signal back into data at the receiving device.
8. Wireless data transmission has many advantages, including increased mobility, flexibility, and convenience. However, it can also be less secure than wired data transmission and may be subject to interference from other wireless devices or networks.
