#### CO 3 Demonstrate the ability to transmit data wirelessly between different devices. K4

1. Wireless data transmission is the transfer of information between two or more devices without the use of wires or cables.
2. There are several ways to transmit data wirelessly, including infrared, radio frequency, Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, and cellular networks.
3. Infrared technology uses light waves to transmit data between devices. It is commonly used in remote controls for televisions and other consumer electronics.
4. Radio frequency technology uses electromagnetic waves to transmit data. It is commonly used in wireless networking, satellite communication, and GPS.
5. Bluetooth technology uses short-range radio waves to transmit data between devices. It is commonly used in wireless headphones, speakers, and keyboards.
6. Wi-Fi technology uses radio waves to transmit data between devices on a local area network. It is commonly used in homes and businesses to connect devices to the internet.
7. Cellular networks use radio waves to transmit data between mobile devices and cell towers. It is commonly used in smartphones to connect to the internet and make phone calls.
8. To transmit data wirelessly between different devices, the devices must be equipped with the appropriate wireless technology and be within range of each other or a wireless network.
9. Wireless data transmission has several advantages, including increased mobility, flexibility, and convenience. However, it can also have security risks and may be subject to interference from other wireless devices.
