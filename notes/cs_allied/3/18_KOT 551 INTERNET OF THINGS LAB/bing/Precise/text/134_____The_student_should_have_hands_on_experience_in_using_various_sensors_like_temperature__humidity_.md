### The student should have hands on experience in using various sensors like temperature, humidity, smoke, light, etc.

- **Temperature Sensor**: A temperature sensor is a device that measures the temperature of its environment and converts the input data into electronic data to record, monitor, or signal temperature changes.
- **Humidity Sensor**: A humidity sensor is a device that measures the relative humidity of the air around it. It works by detecting changes in electrical resistance or capacitance caused by changes in humidity.
- **Smoke Sensor**: A smoke sensor is a device that detects the presence of smoke in the air. It typically uses an optical or ionization detection method to detect the presence of smoke particles.
- **Light Sensor**: A light sensor is a device that measures the amount of light in its environment. It typically uses a photodiode or a photoresistor to convert light into an electrical signal.

Having hands-on experience with these sensors can help students understand how they work and how they can be used in various applications. It can also help them develop practical skills in using and integrating these sensors into different systems.
