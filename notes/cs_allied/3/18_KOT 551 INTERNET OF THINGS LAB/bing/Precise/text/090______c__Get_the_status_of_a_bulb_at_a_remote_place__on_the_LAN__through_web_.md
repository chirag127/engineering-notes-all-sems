#### c) Get the status of a bulb at a remote place (on the LAN) through web.

1. To get the status of a bulb at a remote place on the LAN through the web, you need to have a device that can connect to the internet and access the LAN.
2. The bulb must be connected to a smart home system or a device that can communicate its status over the LAN.
3. You can use a web interface or an app to access the smart home system or device and check the status of the bulb.
4. The status of the bulb can be displayed as on or off, and may also include additional information such as brightness or color.
5. It is important to ensure that the smart home system or device is properly configured and connected to the LAN to accurately retrieve the status of the bulb.
