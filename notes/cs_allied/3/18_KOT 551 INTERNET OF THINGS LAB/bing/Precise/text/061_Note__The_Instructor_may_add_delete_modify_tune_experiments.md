# Note: The Instructor may add/delete/modify/tune experiments

- The instructor has the authority to make changes to the experiments.
- These changes may include adding new experiments, deleting existing experiments, modifying the details of the experiments, or tuning the parameters of the experiments.
- The purpose of these changes is to improve the learning experience for the students.
- Students should be aware that the experiments are subject to change and should be prepared to adapt to any changes made by the instructor.
- It is important for students to communicate with the instructor to stay up to date with any changes made to the experiments.
