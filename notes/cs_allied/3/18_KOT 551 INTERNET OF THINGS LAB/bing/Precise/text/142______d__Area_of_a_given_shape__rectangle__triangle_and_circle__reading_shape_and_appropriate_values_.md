#### d) Area of a given shape (rectangle, triangle and circle) reading shape and appropriate values from standard

The area of a given shape can be calculated using the appropriate formula for that shape. Here are the formulas for calculating the area of a rectangle, triangle, and circle:

1. **Rectangle**: The area of a rectangle can be calculated by multiplying its length by its width. The formula for the area of a rectangle is `A = l * w`, where `A` is the area, `l` is the length, and `w` is the width.

2. **Triangle**: The area of a triangle can be calculated using the base and height of the triangle. The formula for the area of a triangle is `A = (b * h) / 2`, where `A` is the area, `b` is the base, and `h` is the height.

3. **Circle**: The area of a circle can be calculated using its radius. The formula for the area of a circle is `A = π * r^2`, where `A` is the area, `π` is approximately equal to 3.14, and `r` is the radius.

To calculate the area of a given shape, one must first identify the shape and then use the appropriate formula to calculate its area. The values for the variables in the formula (such as length, width, base, height, or radius) must be read from a standard, such as a ruler or a protractor.
