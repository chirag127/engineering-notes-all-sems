# Note: The Instructor may add/delete/modify/tune experiments

- It is important to note that the instructor has the authority to make changes to the experiments.
- This means that the instructor may add new experiments, delete existing ones, modify the details of the experiments, or tune them to better fit the needs of the course.
- This flexibility allows the instructor to tailor the course content to the specific needs of the students and to keep the course material up-to-date and relevant.
- Students should be aware of this possibility and be prepared for changes to the course material.
- It is recommended that students regularly check for updates and communicate with the instructor to stay informed about any changes to the experiments.
