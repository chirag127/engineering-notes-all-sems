# At the end of the course, the student will be able to:

- Demonstrate a comprehensive understanding of the subject matter.
- Apply the knowledge and skills acquired during the course to real-life situations.
- Analyze and evaluate information critically and effectively.
- Communicate ideas and arguments clearly and coherently.
- Work collaboratively with others to achieve common goals.
- Demonstrate the ability to learn independently and take responsibility for their own learning.
- Develop a range of transferable skills that are valuable in the workplace and in further study.
- Reflect on their own learning and identify areas for further development.
