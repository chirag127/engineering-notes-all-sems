## Course Outcome (CO) Bloom’s Knowledge Level (KL)

- Course Outcome (CO) refers to the expected knowledge, skills, and attitudes that students should possess upon completion of a specific course or program of study.
- Bloom’s Knowledge Level (KL) is a framework for categorizing educational goals and objectives according to the level of cognitive complexity.
- The framework consists of six levels: Remembering, Understanding, Applying, Analyzing, Evaluating, and Creating.
- COs are often mapped to Bloom’s KL to ensure that the course or program of study is designed to facilitate the achievement of the desired level of cognitive complexity.
- By mapping COs to Bloom’s KL, educators can design assessments and instructional strategies that are aligned with the desired level of cognitive complexity.
- This can help to ensure that students are challenged appropriately and that they are able to achieve the desired learning outcomes.
