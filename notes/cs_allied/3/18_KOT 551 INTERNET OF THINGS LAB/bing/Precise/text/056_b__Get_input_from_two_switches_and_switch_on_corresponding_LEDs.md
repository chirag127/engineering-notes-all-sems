# Get input from two switches and switch on corresponding LEDs

1. To get input from two switches and switch on corresponding LEDs, you will need a microcontroller, two switches, two LEDs, and the necessary circuit components such as resistors and wires.
2. Connect the switches to the input pins of the microcontroller and the LEDs to the output pins.
3. Write a program for the microcontroller to read the state of the switches and turn on the corresponding LED when the switch is pressed.
4. Upload the program to the microcontroller and test the circuit by pressing the switches and observing the behavior of the LEDs.

This is a basic example of how to get input from two switches and switch on corresponding LEDs. The specific details of the circuit and program will depend on the microcontroller and components used. It is important to consult the datasheets and documentation for the specific components to ensure proper operation.
