# CO 1 Understand the concept of Internet of Things K3

The Internet of Things (IoT) is a concept that describes the interconnectivity of physical objects, or "things," through the use of sensors, software, and other technologies. These objects are able to connect to the internet or interact with other devices via wireless networks. Some examples of IoT devices include phones, appliances, thermostats, lighting systems, irrigation systems, security cameras, vehicles, and even animals and cities .

- IoT devices can collect, store, and share data with each other over their connections .
- The data collected by IoT devices can be accessed by other smart devices .
- IoT technology can be used for a variety of purposes, such as detecting popular areas in a showroom, identifying which components are selling the fastest, and automatically aligning sales data with supply .
- IoT is part of the Fourth Industrial Revolution and is enabling the development of smart cities and driverless cars .
