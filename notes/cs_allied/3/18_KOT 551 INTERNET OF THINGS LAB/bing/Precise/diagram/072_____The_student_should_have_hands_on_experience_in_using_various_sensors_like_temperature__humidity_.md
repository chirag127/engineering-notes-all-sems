### The student should have hands on experience in using various sensors like temperature, humidity, smoke, light, etc.

- **Temperature sensors** are used to measure the temperature of a given environment or object. They can be used in a variety of applications, such as monitoring the temperature of a room or a refrigerator.
- **Humidity sensors** are used to measure the amount of water vapor in the air. They can be used in applications such as weather monitoring or controlling the humidity levels in a building.
- **Smoke sensors** are used to detect the presence of smoke in the air. They are commonly used in fire alarm systems to alert people to the presence of a fire.
- **Light sensors** are used to measure the amount of light in a given environment. They can be used in applications such as controlling the brightness of a display or turning on street lights at night.

It is important for students to have hands-on experience with these sensors as it allows them to understand how they work and how they can be used in real-world applications. This can be achieved through practical experiments and projects that involve using these sensors to collect and analyze data.
