#### Note: The Instructor may add/delete/modify/tune experiments

- It is important to note that the instructor has the authority to make changes to the experiments.
- This means that the instructor may add new experiments, delete existing ones, modify the content or procedures of the experiments, or tune the experiments to better fit the needs of the class.
- As a student, it is important to be aware of these potential changes and to be prepared to adapt to them.
- This flexibility allows the instructor to tailor the course to the specific needs and interests of the students, and to ensure that the course content remains up-to-date and relevant.
- It is recommended to regularly check for updates and announcements from the instructor regarding any changes to the experiments.
