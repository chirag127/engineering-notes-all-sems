### Hands-on Experience with Sensors

It is important for students to have hands-on experience with various sensors, including but not limited to:

1. **Temperature sensors**: These sensors measure the temperature of the environment or an object. They can be used in a variety of applications, such as monitoring the temperature of a room or a refrigerator.

2. **Humidity sensors**: These sensors measure the amount of water vapor in the air. They can be used in applications such as weather monitoring or controlling the humidity in a greenhouse.

3. **Smoke sensors**: These sensors detect the presence of smoke in the air. They are commonly used in fire alarms and can help prevent fires from spreading.

4. **Light sensors**: These sensors measure the amount of light in the environment. They can be used in applications such as controlling the brightness of a display or turning on street lights at night.

By working with these sensors, students can gain practical knowledge and experience in using them in real-world applications. This can help them better understand the principles behind the sensors and how they can be used to solve problems.
