# CO 3: Demonstrate the ability to transmit data wirelessly between different devices (K4)

1. **Wireless data transmission** refers to the transfer of information between two or more devices without the use of wires or cables.
2. **Bluetooth** is a common wireless technology used for short-range data transmission between devices such as smartphones, laptops, and speakers.
3. **Wi-Fi** is another wireless technology used for data transmission over longer distances, typically within a local area network (LAN).
4. **Infrared** technology can also be used for wireless data transmission, but it requires a direct line of sight between the transmitting and receiving devices.
5. To transmit data wirelessly between different devices, the devices must be equipped with the necessary hardware and software to support the chosen wireless technology.
6. The devices must also be within range of each other and properly configured to establish a wireless connection.
7. Once a connection is established, data can be transmitted wirelessly between the devices using the chosen wireless technology.
8. Wireless data transmission can provide convenience and flexibility, but it is important to ensure that the connection is secure to prevent unauthorized access to the transmitted data.
