## Course Outcome (CO) Bloom’s Knowledge Level (KL)

Course Outcome (CO) refers to the expected knowledge, skills, and attitudes that students should possess upon completion of a specific course. COs are typically defined by the instructor and are used to assess the effectiveness of the course in achieving its intended goals.

Bloom’s Knowledge Level (KL) is a framework for categorizing educational learning objectives into levels of complexity and specificity. The levels are: Remembering, Understanding, Applying, Analyzing, Evaluating, and Creating. These levels can be used to assess the level of understanding that students have achieved in relation to the COs of a course.

- COs and KLs are important tools for instructors to use in designing and assessing the effectiveness of their courses.
- COs should be specific, measurable, and achievable, and should align with the overall goals of the course.
- KLs provide a framework for assessing the depth of understanding that students have achieved in relation to the COs.
- Instructors can use KLs to design assessments and activities that target specific levels of understanding, and to provide feedback to students on their progress towards achieving the COs.
