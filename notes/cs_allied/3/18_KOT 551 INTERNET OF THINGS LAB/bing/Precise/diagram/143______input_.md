#### Input

- Input refers to the process of entering data or information into a computer or other electronic device.
- This can be done through various input devices such as a keyboard, mouse, touch screen, microphone, or scanner.
- The data or information entered can be in the form of text, images, audio, or video.
- Input is a crucial step in the process of computing, as it allows the user to interact with the computer and provide it with the necessary data to perform tasks.
- Some common types of input include typing on a keyboard, clicking or moving a mouse, speaking into a microphone, or scanning a document.
- Input devices vary in their design and functionality, and can be chosen based on the specific needs of the user and the task at hand.
- For example, a graphic designer may use a drawing tablet as their primary input device, while a data entry clerk may rely on a keyboard and mouse.
- The input process is typically followed by processing, where the computer manipulates the data entered, and output, where the results of the processing are displayed or stored.
