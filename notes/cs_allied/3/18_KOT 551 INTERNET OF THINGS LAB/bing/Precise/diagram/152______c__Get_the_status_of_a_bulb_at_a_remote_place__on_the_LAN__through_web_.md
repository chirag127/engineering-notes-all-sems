#### c) Get the status of a bulb at a remote place (on the LAN) through web.

1. To get the status of a bulb at a remote place on the LAN through the web, a device with internet connectivity is required.
2. The device must be connected to the same LAN as the bulb.
3. The bulb must be a smart bulb that can be controlled through the internet.
4. The user must have the necessary login credentials to access the bulb's control interface.
5. The user can then log in to the bulb's control interface and check the status of the bulb.
6. The status of the bulb can include information such as whether it is on or off, its brightness level, and its color.
7. The user can also control the bulb remotely through the web interface, such as turning it on or off or changing its brightness or color.
