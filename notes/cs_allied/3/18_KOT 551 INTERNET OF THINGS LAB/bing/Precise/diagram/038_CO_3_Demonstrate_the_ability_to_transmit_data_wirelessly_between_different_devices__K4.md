# CO 3 Demonstrate the ability to transmit data wirelessly between different devices. K4

1. **Wireless data transmission** refers to the transfer of information between two or more devices without the use of wires or cables.
2. **Bluetooth** is a common wireless technology used for short-range data transmission between devices such as smartphones, laptops, and speakers.
3. **Wi-Fi** is another widely used wireless technology that allows devices to connect to the internet or a local network without the use of cables.
4. **Infrared** technology is used for short-range data transmission and is commonly found in remote controls and some older mobile phones.
5. **Near Field Communication (NFC)** is a short-range wireless technology used for contactless payments and data exchange between devices.
6. To transmit data wirelessly between devices, both devices must have compatible wireless technology and be within range of each other.
7. The process of transmitting data wirelessly between devices may vary depending on the technology used, but generally involves pairing or connecting the devices and selecting the data to be transferred.
8. Wireless data transmission has many advantages, including increased mobility, convenience, and flexibility. However, it is important to ensure that the data being transmitted is secure to prevent unauthorized access.
