## DETAILED SYLLABUS

- A detailed syllabus is a document that outlines the topics, objectives, assignments, assessments, and policies of a course.
- A detailed syllabus can help students to understand the expectations and requirements of the course, as well as to plan their study time and workload.
- A detailed syllabus can also help instructors to organize and structure their course content, as well as to communicate their teaching philosophy and methods to the students.
- A detailed syllabus typically includes the following sections:

  - Course information: This section provides basic information about the course, such as the course title, code, number, credits, prerequisites, instructor name and contact details, office hours, meeting times and locations, etc.
  - Course description: This section provides a brief overview of the course, such as the main topics, themes, goals, and outcomes of the course, as well as the relevance and importance of the course to the discipline or program.
  - Course objectives: This section lists the specific learning outcomes or competencies that the students are expected to achieve by the end of the course, as well as the alignment of these objectives with the program or institutional outcomes.
  - Course materials: This section lists the required and recommended texts, readings, resources, and materials that the students need to access or purchase for the course, as well as the availability and format of these materials (e.g., online, library, bookstore, etc.).
  - Course schedule: This section provides a detailed breakdown of the course content, activities, and assessments for each week or unit of the course, as well as the due dates and deadlines for the assignments and exams.
  - Course policies: This section outlines the rules and expectations that the students and the instructor need to follow and respect in the course, such as the attendance, participation, communication, academic integrity, grading, late submission, extension, revision, feedback, appeal, and accommodation policies.
  - Course evaluation: This section describes the methods and criteria that the instructor will use to assess the students' learning and performance in the course, such as the types, weights, and rubrics of the assignments and exams, as well as the grading scale and distribution of the course.
  - Course support: This section provides information and guidance on the available academic and non-academic support services and resources that the students can access or consult for the course, such as the tutoring, counseling, advising, disability, diversity, and wellness services.
