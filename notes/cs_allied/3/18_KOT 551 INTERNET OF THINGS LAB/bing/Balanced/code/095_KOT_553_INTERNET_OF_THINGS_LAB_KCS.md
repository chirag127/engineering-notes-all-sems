# KOT 553 INTERNET OF THINGS LAB KCS

- This is a course offered by Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University for the third year students of Computer Science and Engineering (IoT) branch.
- The course code is KOT 553 and the course name is Internet of Things Lab.
- The course aims to provide practical exposure to the students on the concepts, principles and challenges of Internet of Things (IoT).
- The course covers the following topics:

  - Introduction to IoT and its applications.
  - Hardware devices and sensors used for IoT.
  - Communication protocols and standards for IoT.
  - Cloud computing and data analytics for IoT.
  - Security and privacy issues in IoT.
  - IoT project development and deployment.

- The course has a total of 50 marks, out of which 30 marks are for internal assessment and 20 marks are for external examination.
- The internal assessment consists of 10 marks for attendance, 10 marks for lab performance and 10 marks for lab record.
- The external examination consists of a practical exam of 20 marks, where the students have to demonstrate their IoT projects.
- The course requires the students to have access to the following hardware and software tools:

  - Arduino Uno board and various sensors and actuators.
  - Raspberry Pi board and various sensors and actuators.
  - ESP8266 Wi-Fi module and NodeMCU board.
  - MQTT protocol and Mosquitto broker.
  - Python programming language and libraries.
  - AWS IoT Core and AWS Lambda services.
  - Google Firebase and Google Cloud Functions services.
  - Blynk app and Blynk server.

- The course is based on the KOHLER® Konnect platform, which is a collection of smart kitchen and bath products that use IoT technology to enhance the user experience.
- The course is taught by the faculty members of the Internet of Things Lab, which is a cutting-edge facility for IoT innovation and research at Kohler.
