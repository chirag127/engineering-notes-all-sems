# Course Outcome (CO) Bloom's Knowledge Level (KL)

- Bloom's Knowledge Level (KL) consists of six categories: Remember, Understand, Apply, Analyze, Evaluate, and Create. Each category represents a different level of complexity and depth of learning.

| Apply | Use knowledge or skills in a new or different situation. | Solve a math problem using a formula. |

| Evaluate | Make judgments or decisions based on criteria or standards. | Assess the quality of a research paper. |
| Create | Generate, plan, or produce something original or novel. | Write a poem based on a theme. |
