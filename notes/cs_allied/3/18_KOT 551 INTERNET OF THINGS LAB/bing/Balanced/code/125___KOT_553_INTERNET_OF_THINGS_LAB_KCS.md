# KOT 553 INTERNET OF THINGS LAB KCS

- This is a course offered by Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University for the third year students of Computer Science and Engineering (IoT) branch.
- The course code is KOT 553 and the course name is Internet of Things Lab.
- The course aims to provide practical exposure to the students on the concepts, principles and challenges of Internet of Things (IoT).
- The course covers the following topics:

  - Introduction to IoT and its applications
  - IoT hardware devices and sensors
  - IoT communication protocols and standards
  - IoT cloud platforms and services
  - IoT security and privacy issues
  - IoT data analytics and visualization
  - IoT project development and deployment

- The course has a total of 50 marks, out of which 30 marks are for internal assessment and 20 marks are for external examination.
- The internal assessment consists of 10 marks for attendance, 10 marks for lab performance and 10 marks for lab record.
- The external examination consists of a practical exam of 20 marks, in which the students have to demonstrate their IoT projects.
- The course requires the students to have access to the following resources:

  - IoT devices and sensors, such as Arduino, Raspberry Pi, ESP8266, etc.
  - IoT communication modules, such as Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, ZigBee, etc.
  - IoT cloud platforms, such as AWS IoT, Google Cloud IoT, IBM Watson IoT, etc.
  - IoT development tools, such as Arduino IDE, Node-RED, Python, etc.
  - IoT data analytics and visualization tools, such as Power BI, Tableau, etc.

- The course is designed to help the students to:

  - Understand the basic concepts, principles and challenges of IoT
  - Learn how to use various IoT hardware devices and sensors
  - Learn how to communicate and interact with IoT devices using different protocols and standards
  - Learn how to connect and manage IoT devices using cloud platforms and services
  - Learn how to secure and protect IoT devices and data
  - Learn how to analyze and visualize IoT data
  - Learn how to develop and deploy IoT projects for real-world scenarios

- The course is expected to enhance the students' skills and knowledge in the domain of IoT and prepare them for the future opportunities and challenges in this field.
