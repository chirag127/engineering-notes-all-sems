# Course Outcome (CO) Bloom's Knowledge Level (KL)

- Course Outcome (CO) is a statement that describes what a learner should be able to do at the end of a course or a unit of instruction.
- Bloom's Knowledge Level (KL) is a classification of cognitive skills that learners can demonstrate in different domains of learning, such as knowledge, comprehension, application, analysis, synthesis, and evaluation.
- CO and KL are related because they both help to define the learning objectives and assessment criteria for a course or a unit of instruction.
- CO and KL can be used to design and align the curriculum, instruction, and assessment of a course or a unit of instruction, ensuring that the learners achieve the desired learning outcomes.
- CO and KL can be expressed using verbs that indicate the level of cognitive skill required for the learners to perform the outcome, such as define, explain, apply, compare, create, or evaluate.
- CO and KL can be mapped to each other using a matrix or a table that shows the relationship between the outcomes and the levels of knowledge.
- CO and KL can be used to evaluate the effectiveness and quality of a course or a unit of instruction, by measuring the learners' achievement of the outcomes and the levels of knowledge.
