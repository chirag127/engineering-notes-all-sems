# KOT 551 INTERNET OF THINGS LAB

- Internet of Things (IoT) is the network of physical objects, devices, vehicles, buildings and other things that are embedded with sensors, software and other technologies for the purpose of connecting and exchanging data with other devices and systems over the internet.
- IoT Lab is a resource for students and faculty to experiment with the latest devices and datasets for research and applications in IoT.
- IoT Lab can help to answer questions such as:
  - How to collect, store, analyze and visualize data from IoT devices?
  - How to design, develop and deploy IoT applications and services?
  - How to ensure the security, privacy and reliability of IoT systems?
  - How to integrate IoT with other technologies such as cloud computing, artificial intelligence and blockchain?
- IoT Lab can provide access to various IoT devices and platforms, such as:
  - Raspberry Pi, Arduino, ESP32 and other microcontrollers
  - Sensors, actuators, cameras, GPS and other peripherals
  - MQTT, CoAP, HTTP and other communication protocols
  - AWS IoT, Google Cloud IoT, Azure IoT and other cloud services
  - Node-RED, Python, C/C++ and other programming languages
- IoT Lab can also offer guidance and support for IoT projects, such as:
  - IoT project ideas and examples
  - IoT project planning and management
  - IoT project documentation and presentation
  - IoT project evaluation and feedback
