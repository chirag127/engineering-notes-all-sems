# CO 1 Understand the concept of Internet of Things K3

- Internet of Things (IoT) is a technology that allows us to add a device to an inert object that can measure environmental parameters, generate associated data and transmit them through a communications network.
- IoT devices are embedded with sensors, software, and other technologies for the purpose of connecting and exchanging data with other devices and systems over the internet.
- IoT devices range from ordinary household objects to sophisticated industrial tools.
- IoT devices can collect and share data about the way they are used and about the environment around them.
- IoT devices can be controlled remotely or autonomously, and can interact with other devices or humans.
- IoT devices can enable new applications and services, such as smart homes, smart cities, smart health, smart agriculture, smart manufacturing, etc.
- IoT devices can also pose challenges and risks, such as security, privacy, interoperability, scalability, reliability, etc.

Some examples of IoT devices are:

- Thermostats, lights, refrigerators, and other appliances that can be controlled via smartphone or voice assistant.
- Cars, bikes, scooters, and other vehicles that can communicate with each other, the road, or the cloud.
- Wearables, such as smart watches, fitness trackers, or health monitors that can track vital signs, activity, or location.
- Plant electronic systems, roofs, or other infrastructure that can monitor environmental conditions, energy consumption, or maintenance needs.
- Industrial machines, robots, or sensors that can optimize production, quality, or safety.
