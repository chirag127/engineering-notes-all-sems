#### CO 1 Understand the concept of Internet of Things K3

- The Internet of Things (IoT) is a technology that allows us to add a device to an inert object that can measure environmental parameters, generate associated data and transmit them through a communications network.
- The IoT is a giant network of connected things and people that collect and share data about the way they are used and about the environment around them.
- The IoT devices can be anything that has an on/off switch and can be connected to the Internet or other communications networks.
- Some examples of IoT devices are:
  - Smart thermostats that can adjust the temperature based on the user's preferences and the weather conditions.
  - Smart cars that can communicate with other vehicles, traffic lights, and road signs to optimize the driving experience.
  - Smart lights that can turn on and off automatically based on the presence of people and the ambient light.
  - Smart refrigerators that can monitor the food items and order them online when they are running low.
  - Smart alarm clocks that can work on a specified day and time, and also turn on the coffee machine and open the windows.
- Some benefits of IoT are:
  - Increased efficiency and productivity by automating tasks and optimizing processes.
  - Enhanced safety and security by monitoring and alerting potential threats and hazards.
  - Improved customer satisfaction and loyalty by providing personalized and customized services and products.
  - Reduced costs and waste by saving energy and resources.
  - Enabled innovation and creativity by creating new opportunities and solutions.
