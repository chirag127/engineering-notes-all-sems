# Note: The Instructor may add/delete/modify/tune experiments

- This note is a disclaimer that the instructor has the authority and discretion to change the experiments that are planned for the course.
- The instructor may add new experiments, delete existing ones, modify the objectives, methods, or outcomes of the experiments, or tune the parameters, settings, or requirements of the experiments.
- The instructor may do so for various reasons, such as to align the experiments with the course objectives, to adapt to the availability of resources, to update the experiments with the latest developments, or to accommodate the feedback or needs of the students.
- The instructor will inform the students of any changes to the experiments in advance and provide the necessary instructions and guidance for the students to perform the experiments successfully.
- The students are expected to follow the instructor's directions and comply with the changes to the experiments. The students are also encouraged to ask questions, provide feedback, and participate actively in the experiments.
