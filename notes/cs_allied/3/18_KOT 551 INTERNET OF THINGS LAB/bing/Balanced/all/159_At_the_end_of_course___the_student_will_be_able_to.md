# At the end of course, the student will be able to

- Define the main concepts and principles of the course topic.
- Apply the learned skills and knowledge to solve problems and complete tasks related to the course topic.
- Analyze and evaluate the information and arguments presented in the course materials and sources.
- Communicate effectively and professionally using the appropriate terminology and format for the course topic.
- Demonstrate ethical and responsible behavior in relation to the course topic and its implications for society and the environment.
- Collaborate with others and work independently to achieve the course objectives and outcomes.
