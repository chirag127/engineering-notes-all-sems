# Note: The Instructor may add/delete/modify/tune experiments

- This note means that the instructor of the course has the authority and discretion to change the experiments that are part of the curriculum.
- The instructor may add new experiments that are relevant to the course objectives and learning outcomes, or delete existing experiments that are outdated or redundant.
- The instructor may also modify or tune the existing experiments to make them more suitable for the level of difficulty, the available resources, the time constraints, or the assessment criteria of the course.
- The note implies that the experiments are not fixed or final, and that the students should be prepared for possible changes or adjustments in the experimental procedures, materials, or expectations.
- The note also suggests that the instructor is open to feedback and suggestions from the students or other stakeholders regarding the experiments, and that the instructor may revise the experiments accordingly.
