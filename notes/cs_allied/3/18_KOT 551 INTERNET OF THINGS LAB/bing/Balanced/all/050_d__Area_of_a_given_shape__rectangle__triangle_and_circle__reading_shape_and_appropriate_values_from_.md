# Area of a given shape (rectangle, triangle and circle) reading shape and appropriate values from standard

- The area of a shape is the amount of space that it covers in a two-dimensional plane.
- Different shapes have different formulas for calculating their areas, depending on their properties and dimensions.
- To find the area of a given shape, we need to read the shape name and the appropriate values from the standard input, and then apply the corresponding formula.
- Here are some common shapes and their area formulas:

  - Rectangle: A rectangle is a quadrilateral with four right angles and opposite sides of equal length. To find the area of a rectangle, we need to read the length and the width from the standard input, and then multiply them. The formula is:

    `Area = length * width`

  - Triangle: A triangle is a polygon with three sides and three angles. To find the area of a triangle, we need to read the base and the height from the standard input, and then multiply them by half. The formula is:

    `Area = (base * height) / 2`

  - Circle: A circle is a shape with all points at the same distance from the center. To find the area of a circle, we need to read the radius from the standard input, and then multiply it by the constant pi and by itself. The formula is:

    `Area = pi * radius * radius`

- Here are some examples of how to find the area of a given shape:

  - Example 1: Find the area of a rectangle with length 10 cm and width 5 cm.

    - Read the shape name from the standard input: rectangle
    - Read the length from the standard input: 10
    - Read the width from the standard input: 5
    - Apply the formula: Area = length * width
    - Calculate the result: Area = 10 * 5
    - Output the result: 50 cm^2^

  - Example 2: Find the area of a triangle with base 12 cm and height 9 cm.

    - Read the shape name from the standard input: triangle
    - Read the base from the standard input: 12
    - Read the height from the standard input: 9
    - Apply the formula: Area = (base * height) / 2
    - Calculate the result: Area = (12 * 9) / 2
    - Output the result: 54 cm^2^

  - Example 3: Find the area of a circle with radius 7 cm.

    - Read the shape name from the standard input: circle
    - Read the radius from the standard input: 7
    - Apply the formula: Area = pi * radius * radius
    - Calculate the result: Area = 3.14 * 7 * 7
    - Output the result: 153.86 cm^2^
