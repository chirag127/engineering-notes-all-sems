# Area of a given shape (rectangle, triangle and circle) reading shape and appropriate values from standard

- The area of a shape is the amount of space that it covers.
- Different shapes have different formulas for calculating their areas.
- To find the area of a given shape, we need to read the shape name and the appropriate values from the standard input, such as the length, width, base, height, or radius of the shape.
- The standard input is a way of providing data to a program, usually through the keyboard or a file.
- Here are some examples of how to find the area of a rectangle, a triangle, and a circle, reading the shape and the values from the standard input.

## Area of a rectangle

- A rectangle is a four-sided shape with opposite sides equal and right angles.
- The area of a rectangle is given by the formula: `area = length * width`
- To find the area of a rectangle, we need to read the shape name, the length, and the width from the standard input.
- For example, if the standard input is:

```
rectangle
10
5
```

- Then the area of the rectangle is: `area = 10 * 5 = 50`

## Area of a triangle

- A triangle is a three-sided shape with three angles.
- The area of a triangle is given by the formula: `area = (base * height) / 2`
- To find the area of a triangle, we need to read the shape name, the base, and the height from the standard input.
- For example, if the standard input is:

```
triangle
12
8
```

- Then the area of the triangle is: `area = (12 * 8) / 2 = 48`

## Area of a circle

- A circle is a shape with a curved boundary that is equidistant from a fixed point called the center.
- The area of a circle is given by the formula: `area = pi * radius * radius`
- Pi is a constant value that is approximately equal to 3.14.
- To find the area of a circle, we need to read the shape name and the radius from the standard input.
- For example, if the standard input is:

```
circle
7
```

- Then the area of the circle is: `area = 3.14 * 7 * 7 = 153.86`
