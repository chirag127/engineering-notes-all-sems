# CO 1 Understand the concept of Internet of Things K3

- Internet of Things (IoT) is the interconnection of computing devices embedded in everyday objects, enabling them to send and receive data over the internet or other communications networks .
- IoT devices can include sensors, actuators, cameras, speakers, microphones, GPS, RFID, etc. that can collect, process, and transmit data to other devices or systems.
- IoT devices can also receive commands or instructions from other devices or systems, enabling remote monitoring, control, and automation of various tasks and processes.
- IoT devices can communicate with each other or with a central server or cloud platform, using different protocols and standards, such as Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Zigbee, MQTT, CoAP, etc.
- IoT applications can span across various domains and industries, such as smart homes, smart cities, smart agriculture, smart healthcare, smart manufacturing, smart transportation, etc.
- IoT can provide various benefits, such as improved efficiency, productivity, convenience, safety, security, quality, and sustainability, as well as new opportunities for innovation and value creation .
- IoT can also pose various challenges, such as privacy, security, interoperability, scalability, reliability, and ethics, that need to be addressed by appropriate policies, regulations, and standards .
