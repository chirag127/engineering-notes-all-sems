#### CO 1 Understand the concept of Internet of Things K3

- Internet of Things (IoT) is the interconnection of computing devices embedded in everyday objects, enabling them to send and receive data over the internet or other communications networks .
- IoT devices can include sensors, actuators, cameras, speakers, microphones, GPS, RFID, etc. that can collect, process, and transmit data to other devices or systems.
- IoT devices can also receive commands or instructions from other devices or systems, allowing for remote monitoring, control, automation, and optimization of various processes and applications .
- IoT devices can communicate with each other or with a central server or cloud platform, using different protocols and standards, such as Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Zigbee, MQTT, CoAP, etc.
- IoT devices can be grouped into networks or systems based on their functions, domains, or applications, such as smart home, smart city, smart grid, smart health, smart agriculture, etc.
- IoT devices can generate large amounts of data that can be analyzed, processed, and stored using big data, cloud computing, artificial intelligence, and machine learning techniques, to provide insights, predictions, and recommendations for various purposes .
- IoT devices can also interact with humans through user interfaces, voice assistants, chatbots, etc., to provide information, feedback, guidance, and entertainment .
- IoT devices can offer various benefits, such as improved efficiency, productivity, convenience, safety, security, quality, and sustainability, as well as new opportunities, services, and business models .
- IoT devices can also pose various challenges, such as privacy, security, interoperability, scalability, reliability, and ethical issues, that need to be addressed by proper design, regulation, and governance .
