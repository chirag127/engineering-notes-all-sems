# The topic is

- This is an incomplete sentence. It does not have a subject or a predicate.
- A subject is the person, place, thing, or idea that the sentence is about. A predicate is the part of the sentence that tells what the subject does or is.
- For example, in the sentence "The dog barked loudly", the subject is "the dog" and the predicate is "barked loudly".
- To complete the sentence, you need to add a subject and a predicate after the word "is".
- For example, you could write "The topic is nuclear fusion" or "The topic is how to write a good essay".
