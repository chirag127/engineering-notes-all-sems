# Note: The Instructor may add/delete/modify/tune experiments

- This is a statement that indicates that the instructor of a course or a laboratory has the authority and the flexibility to change the experiments that are planned or assigned to the students.
- The instructor may do this for various reasons, such as:
  - To align the experiments with the course objectives and learning outcomes.
  - To update the experiments with the latest developments and technologies in the field.
  - To accommodate the availability of resources, equipment, and materials.
  - To adjust the difficulty level and the duration of the experiments according to the students' abilities and progress.
  - To diversify the experiments and avoid repetition or redundancy.
- The statement also implies that the students should be prepared and adaptable to the changes that the instructor may introduce in the experiments.
- The students should follow the instructions and guidelines given by the instructor for each experiment and consult with the instructor if they have any questions or concerns.
