# The topic is

- The topic is a general term for what a piece of writing, speech, or conversation is about.
- The topic can be expressed in different ways, such as a word, a phrase, a question, or a theme.
- The topic helps the writer or speaker to focus on the main idea and purpose of their communication.
- The topic also helps the reader or listener to understand the context and relevance of the information presented.
- The topic can be identified by looking for clues in the title, introduction, headings, keywords, or summary of a text or speech.
- The topic can be refined or narrowed down by specifying the scope, angle, or aspect of the subject that is being discussed.
- The topic can be related to other topics by using categories, comparisons, contrasts, or connections.
