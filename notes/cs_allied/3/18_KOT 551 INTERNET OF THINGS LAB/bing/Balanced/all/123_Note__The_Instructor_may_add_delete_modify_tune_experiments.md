# Note: The Instructor may add/delete/modify/tune experiments

- This note is a disclaimer that the instructor has the authority and discretion to change the experiments that are part of the course syllabus or curriculum.
- The instructor may add new experiments, delete existing ones, modify the objectives, methods, or outcomes of the experiments, or tune the difficulty, duration, or evaluation criteria of the experiments.
- The instructor may do so for various reasons, such as to align the experiments with the course objectives, to update the experiments with the latest developments or technologies, to accommodate the availability of resources or equipment, or to enhance the learning outcomes or experience of the students.
- The instructor may inform the students of the changes in advance or at the time of conducting the experiments, depending on the nature and extent of the changes.
- The students are expected to follow the instructions of the instructor and adapt to the changes in the experiments, as they are part of the course requirements and assessment.
