# Input

- Input is the process of providing data or instructions to a computer or a device.
- Input can be done through various devices, such as keyboard, mouse, microphone, camera, scanner, etc.
- Input can also be done through different methods, such as typing, clicking, speaking, scanning, etc.
- Input can be categorized into two types: manual and automatic.
  - Manual input is when the user directly enters the data or instructions, such as typing a text or clicking a button.
  - Automatic input is when the data or instructions are entered by another device or program, such as reading a barcode or receiving an email.
- Input can be used for various purposes, such as:
  - Entering commands or queries to perform a task or get information, such as searching the web or opening a file.
  - Providing data or information to a program or a system, such as filling a form or uploading a file.
  - Communicating with other users or devices, such as sending a message or making a call.
  - Controlling or interacting with a device or a system, such as adjusting the volume or playing a game.
