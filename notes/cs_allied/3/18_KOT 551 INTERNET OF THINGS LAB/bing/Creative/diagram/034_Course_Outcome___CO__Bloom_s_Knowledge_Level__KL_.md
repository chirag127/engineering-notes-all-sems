# Course Outcome (CO) Bloom's Knowledge Level (KL)

- A course outcome (CO) is a statement that describes what students should be able to do or demonstrate after completing a course.
- A course outcome should be specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, and time-bound (SMART).
- A course outcome should align with the course objectives, content, activities, and assessments.
- A course outcome should also reflect the level of cognitive complexity or difficulty that students are expected to achieve.
- Bloom's taxonomy is a framework that classifies different types of learning objectives and outcomes according to six levels of cognitive complexity: remember, understand, apply, analyze, evaluate, and create.
- Bloom's knowledge level (KL) is a subcategory of the remember level that refers to the ability to recall or recognize factual information, such as terms, definitions, facts, or principles.
- Bloom's knowledge level can be further divided into four types: factual, conceptual, procedural, and metacognitive.
- Factual knowledge is the basic elements that students must know to be acquainted with a discipline or solve problems in it, such as terminology, symbols, or specific details.
- Conceptual knowledge is the interrelationships among the basic elements within a larger structure that enable them to function together, such as classifications, categories, principles, or theories.
- Procedural knowledge is how to do something, methods of inquiry, and criteria for using skills, algorithms, techniques, or methods, such as rules, steps, methods, or criteria.
- Metacognitive knowledge is the awareness and understanding of one's own cognition and cognitive processes, such as strategies, tasks, or contexts.
- Examples of course outcomes at the knowledge level are:

  - Define the key terms and concepts related to the course topic.
  - Identify the main components and functions of the course topic.
  - List the steps or procedures involved in the course topic.
  - Describe the strategies or methods used in the course topic.
