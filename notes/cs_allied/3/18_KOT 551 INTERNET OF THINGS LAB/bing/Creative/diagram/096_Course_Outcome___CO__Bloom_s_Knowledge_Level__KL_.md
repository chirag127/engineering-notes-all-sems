# Course Outcome (CO) Bloom's Knowledge Level (KL)

- Bloom's Knowledge Level (KL) is a classification of the cognitive domain of learning, which refers to the mental processes involved in acquiring and using knowledge.
- Bloom's Knowledge Level (KL) consists of six levels: Remember, Understand, Apply, Analyze, Evaluate, and Create. Each level represents a different degree of complexity and depth of learning.
- The levels of Bloom's Knowledge Level (KL) are hierarchical, meaning that each level builds on the previous one and requires more cognitive skills and abilities.
