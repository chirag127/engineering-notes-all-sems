# CO 1 Understand the concept of Internet of Things K3

- Internet of Things (IoT) is the interconnection of physical objects or devices that have sensors, software, and other technologies that enable them to communicate and exchange data with other devices and systems over the internet or other networks .
- IoT can be used for various purposes, such as remote monitoring, control, automation, data analysis, and decision making.
- IoT can be applied to various domains, such as smart homes, smart cities, smart agriculture, smart health, smart industry, smart transportation, and smart environment.
- IoT can provide various benefits, such as improved efficiency, convenience, safety, security, quality, and sustainability.
- IoT can also pose various challenges, such as privacy, security, interoperability, scalability, reliability, and regulation.
