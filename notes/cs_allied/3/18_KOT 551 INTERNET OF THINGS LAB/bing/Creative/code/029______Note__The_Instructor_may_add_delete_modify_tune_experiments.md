#### Note: The Instructor may add/delete/modify/tune experiments

- This note indicates that the instructor has the authority and discretion to change the experiments that are part of the course curriculum.
- The instructor may add new experiments that are relevant to the course objectives and learning outcomes, or delete existing experiments that are outdated or redundant.
- The instructor may also modify or tune the existing experiments to make them more suitable for the course level, duration, resources, and assessment criteria.
- The instructor should inform the students about any changes in the experiments well in advance, and provide clear instructions and guidelines for performing the experiments.
- The instructor should also explain the rationale and benefits of the changes, and how they align with the course goals and expectations.
- The students should follow the instructor's directions and feedback, and be prepared to perform the experiments as per the revised plan.
