#### d) Area of a given shape (rectangle, triangle and circle) reading shape and appropriate values from standard

The area of a shape is the amount of space that it covers. Different shapes have different formulas for calculating their areas. Here are some common shapes and their area formulas:

- Rectangle: A rectangle is a quadrilateral with four right angles. The area of a rectangle is the product of its length and width. If `l` is the length and `w` is the width, then the area of a rectangle is `A = l * w`.
- Triangle: A triangle is a polygon with three sides and three angles. The area of a triangle depends on the base and the height. The base is any side of the triangle and the height is the perpendicular distance from the opposite vertex to the base. If `b` is the base and `h` is the height, then the area of a triangle is `A = (1/2) * b * h`.
- Circle: A circle is a set of points that are equidistant from a fixed point called the center. The area of a circle depends on the radius, which is the distance from the center to any point on the circle. If `r` is the radius, then the area of a circle is `A = pi * r^2`, where `pi` is a constant that is approximately equal to 3.14.

To find the area of a given shape, we need to read the shape and the appropriate values from the standard input. The standard input is a way of providing data to a program from an external source, such as a keyboard or a file. Here is a possible algorithm for finding the area of a given shape:

- Step 1: Read the shape from the standard input and store it in a variable called `shape`.
- Step 2: If `shape` is "rectangle", then read the length and width from the standard input and store them in variables called `l` and `w`. Then calculate the area of the rectangle using the formula `A = l * w` and print the result.
- Step 3: If `shape` is "triangle", then read the base and height from the standard input and store them in variables called `b` and `h`. Then calculate the area of the triangle using the formula `A = (1/2) * b * h` and print the result.
- Step 4: If `shape` is "circle", then read the radius from the standard input and store it in a variable called `r`. Then calculate the area of the circle using the formula `A = pi * r^2` and print the result.
- Step 5: If `shape` is not "rectangle", "triangle" or "circle", then print an error message saying that the shape is not valid.
