### Sensors and Actuators

- Sensors: Sensors are input devices that provide input to a microcontroller in the form of physical changes like temperature, pressure, light etc. Some common types of sensors are:
	- Temperature sensors: Provide input in the form of change in temperature. Eg: LM35, DS18B20 etc.
	- Light sensors: Provide input in the form of change in light intensity. Eg: LDR, Photo transistors etc.
	- Pressure sensors: Provide input in the form of change in pressure. Eg: MPX5010DP etc.
- Actuators: Actuators are output devices that convert the electrical signals from a microcontroller into physical movements or actions. Some common types of actuators are:
	- Motors: Convert electrical signals into mechanical movements. Eg: DC motors, Servo motors etc.
	- Solenoids: Convert electrical signals into linear mechanical movements.
	- Buzzers: Convert electrical signals into sound.

The inputs from sensors are taken by the microcontroller and appropriate outputs are given to the actuators based on the programming logic. Sensors and actuators form the hardware interface components for a microcontroller based system.
