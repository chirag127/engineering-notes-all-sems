### Hardware to Hardware Communication

* Parallel Communication: Data is transmitted bit by bit simultaneously over multiple wires. Fast but requires more wires. Example: Parallel port, printer port.
* Serial Communication: Data is transmitted sequentially over a single wire one bit at a time. Requires fewer wires but is slower. Examples: UART, I2C, SPI.
* Serial Peripheral Interface (SPI):
** Full duplex, synchronous serial data transfer
** Uses a master-slave architecture with a single master
** Devices are connected in a daisy chain with separate lines for data in, data out, and clock
** Communication is faster than I2C
* Inter-Integrated Circuit (I2C):
** Uses only two wires: Serial Data (SDA) and Serial Clock (SCL)
** Devices are connected in a daisy chain and addressed using a 7-bit or 10-bit address
** Half duplex
** Typically used for communication between peripherals and microcontrollers on the same circuit board
