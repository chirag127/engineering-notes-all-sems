### Arm Devices

1. Arm Ltd. designs and licenses ARM processors as intellectual property (IP) cores for incorporation into larger system-on-chip (SoC) or system on module (SoM) designs by licensees.
2. ARM processors are widely used in smartphones, tablets and servers. They are power efficient and provide good performance.
3. The ARM architecture is RISC (Reduced Instruction Set Computing) based. RISC architecture has fewer instructions hence simpler and faster to execute.
4. The ARM instruction set includes load/store architecture and a barrel shifter allowing fast shift operations.
5. The ARM core families include:
    - ARM Cortex: Application processors used in smartphones and tablets. Example: Cortex-A53, Cortex-A72 etc.
    - ARM Mali: Graphic processing units. Example: Mali-G71, Mali-G72 etc.
    - ARM SecurCore: Processors for security and trust applications.
6. The ARM cores are highly configurable and come in a variety of configurations targeting different performance levels and price points.
7. ARM licenses its IP to semiconductor companies who design their own ARM-based chips for various applications. Examples: Qualcomm, Samsung, Nvidia, Apple etc.
8. The low power and high performance of ARM processors has resulted in their use in a vast array of embedded and mobile devices including wearables. Their use in servers is also increasing due to high performance and power efficiency.
