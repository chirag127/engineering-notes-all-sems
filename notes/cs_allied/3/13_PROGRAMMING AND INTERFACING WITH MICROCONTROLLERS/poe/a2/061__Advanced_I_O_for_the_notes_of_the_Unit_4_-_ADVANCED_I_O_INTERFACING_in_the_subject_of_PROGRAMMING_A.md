### Advanced I/O Interfacing

- Serial Peripheral Interface (SPI)
- Inter-Integrated Circuit (I2C)
- Universal Asynchronous Receiver/Transmitter (UART)

#### Serial Peripheral Interface (SPI)
- Synchronous serial data transfer protocol
- Full duplex communication - data can be sent and received simultaneously
- Uses a master-slave architecture with a single master
- Supports high speed data transfer (up to 10 Mbps)
- Uses 4 wires: Sclk (clock), Mosi (master out, slave in), Miso (master in, slave out), SS (slave select)

#### Inter-Integrated Circuit (I2C)
- Synchronous serial data transfer protocol
- Supports multiple masters and slaves with individual addresses
- Uses 2 wires: SDA (serial data) and SCL (serial clock)
- Data transfer speeds up to 400 kbps
- Good for low-speed peripheral devices like temperature sensors, RTC, etc.

#### Universal Asynchronous Receiver/Transmitter (UART)
- Asynchronous serial communication protocol
- Converts data between parallel and serial forms
- Usually has a transmit pin (TxD) and receive pin (RxD)
- Data transfer speeds up to 2 Mbps
- Widely used for serial communication with devices like GPS, Bluetooth, etc.
