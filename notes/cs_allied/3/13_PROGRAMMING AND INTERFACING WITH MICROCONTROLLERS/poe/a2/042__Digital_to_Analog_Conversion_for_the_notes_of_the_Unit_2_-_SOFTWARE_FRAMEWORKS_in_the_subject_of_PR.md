### Digital to Analog Conversion

- Digital to analog conversion is the process of converting a digital signal into an analog signal.
- It is done using a device called Digital to Analog Converter (DAC).
- A DAC converts the digital binary code into a voltage or current signal.
- The input is in the form of binary bits and the output is a continuous representation of a quantity.
- The DAC consists of a resistor network and a set of switches.
- The switches are controlled by the digital input code.
- Depending on the input code, the switches connect the resistor network such that the desired output voltage is obtained.
- Higher the number of bits in the input code, higher is the resolution of the DAC.
- This is required to get a more accurate analog output.
- DACs are used in applications where analog signals are required from a digital system, such as audio systems, data acquisition systems, etc.
- The reverse of DAC is Analog to Digital Conversion which converts analog signals to digital signals.
