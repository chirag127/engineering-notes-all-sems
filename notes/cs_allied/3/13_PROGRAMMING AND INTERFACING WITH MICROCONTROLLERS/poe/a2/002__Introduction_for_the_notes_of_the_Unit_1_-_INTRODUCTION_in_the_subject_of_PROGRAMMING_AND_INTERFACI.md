### Introduction

1. Microcontrollers are small computers on a single integrated circuit containing a processor core, memory, and programmable input/output peripherals.
2. They are used in automatically controlled products and devices, such as automobile engine control systems, implantable medical devices, remote controls, office machines, appliances, power tools, toys and other embedded systems.
3. By reducing the size and cost compared to a design that uses a separate microprocessor, memory, and input/output devices, microcontrollers make it economical to digitally control even more devices and appliances.
4. Mixed signal microcontrollers have analog-to-digital converters, pulse-width modulation outputs, etc. on the same chip as the processor and memory. This integration drastically reduces cost and the need for external components or circuitry and also dramatically increases reliability.
5. Popular microcontroller families include PIC, AVR, ARM, MSP430, 8051 etc. Each family has its own characteristics, such as what operating voltage it operates at, current draw, processing speed, what peripherals it has, price, etc.
