
### Basic Electronics for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of PROGRAMMING AND INTERFACING WITH MICROCONTROLLERS

1. Electronics is the branch of science and technology that deals with the design and application of circuits and components that use electricity as part of their operation. 
2. Basic electronics involves understanding the behavior of electrical components, such as resistors, capacitors, inductors and transistors, and how they interact with each other to form circuits. 
3. Microcontrollers are small, low-cost computers that can be programmed to perform a variety of tasks. 
4. Microcontrollers are used in a wide variety of applications, from controlling robots to running home automation systems. 
5. Programming microcontrollers involves understanding the basics of programming languages, such as C and assembly language, and how to write programs that can interact with the microcontroller’s hardware.
6. Interfacing with microcontrollers involves understanding how to connect the microcontroller to other devices, such as sensors, actuators and displays. 
7. Debugging microcontroller programs requires understanding how to use debugging tools to identify and fix programming errors.
