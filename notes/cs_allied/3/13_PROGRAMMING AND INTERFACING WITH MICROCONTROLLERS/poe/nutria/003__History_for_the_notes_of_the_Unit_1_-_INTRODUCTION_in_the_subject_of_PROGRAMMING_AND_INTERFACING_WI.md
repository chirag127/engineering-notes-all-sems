- Microcontrollers were first developed in the 1970s and were used in a variety of applications, including automotive, industrial, and consumer electronics.
- Microcontrollers are used in a wide range of applications, including home appliances, automotive systems, and industrial automation.
- Microcontrollers are typically programmed in a high-level language such as C or assembly language.
- Microcontrollers are used to control a variety of devices, including motors, sensors, and displays.
- Microcontrollers are used to interface with other devices, such as memory, analog-to-digital converters, and digital-to-analog converters.
- Microcontrollers can be programmed to perform a variety of tasks, including data acquisition, data processing, and control.
