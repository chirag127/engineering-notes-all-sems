
### Sensor Characterization for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of PROGRAMMING AND INTERFACING WITH MICROCONTROLLERS

* Sensors are devices that measure physical quantities and convert them into electrical signals. 
* Sensors can measure physical quantities such as temperature, pressure, humidity, light intensity and many others. 
* Interfacing sensors with microcontrollers requires knowledge of the sensor's electrical characteristics, such as its voltage and current requirements, as well as its output signal type. 
* Sensor characterization is the process of determining the electrical characteristics of a sensor, so that it can be interfaced with a microcontroller. 
* Sensor characterization involves measuring the sensor's sensitivity, linearity, accuracy, resolution, and other parameters. 
* To accurately characterize a sensor, it is important to use the correct measurement equipment and techniques. 
* Once a sensor has been characterized, it can be interfaced with a microcontroller, allowing the microcontroller to measure and respond to the physical quantity being measured by the sensor.
