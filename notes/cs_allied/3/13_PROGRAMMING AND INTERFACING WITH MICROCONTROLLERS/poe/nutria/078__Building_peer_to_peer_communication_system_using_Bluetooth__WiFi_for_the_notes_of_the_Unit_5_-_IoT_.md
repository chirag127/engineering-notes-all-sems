
### Building Peer to Peer Communication System Using Bluetooth & WiFi 

1. Bluetooth is a wireless technology that enables two devices to communicate with each other over short distances. It is used for a variety of applications, including streaming audio and video, transferring files, and connecting to the Internet.

2. Bluetooth works by allowing two devices to establish a secure connection and then send and receive data. This data can be in the form of audio, video, text, or other types of files.

3. WiFi is a wireless networking technology that allows two or more devices to communicate with each other over a network. It is used for a variety of applications, including streaming audio and video, transferring files, and connecting to the Internet.

4. WiFi works by allowing two or more devices to establish a secure connection and then send and receive data. This data can be in the form of audio, video, text, or other types of files.

5. IoT (Internet of Things) is the network of physical devices, vehicles, home appliances, and other items embedded with electronics, software, sensors, actuators, and network connectivity which enable these objects to connect and exchange data.

6. IoT enables devices to communicate and interact with each other over the internet, allowing users to control and monitor their devices from anywhere. This can be used to automate processes, such as turning lights on and off, as well as to monitor and control devices remotely.

7. The use of Bluetooth and WiFi in an IoT system can provide a secure, reliable, and cost-effective way to connect devices and share data. Bluetooth and WiFi can be used to create a peer-to-peer network, which allows devices to communicate directly with each other without the need for an intermediary. This can be used to reduce latency and improve the speed of communication.

8. By using Bluetooth and WiFi in an IoT system, users can create a secure and reliable network that can be used to control and monitor devices from anywhere. This can be used to automate processes, as well as to monitor and control devices remotely.
