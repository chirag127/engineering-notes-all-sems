
## Unit 4 - ADVANCED I/O INTERFACING

* I/O Interfacing is the process of connecting and communicating between two or more devices, typically between a computer and a peripheral device.
* I/O devices are typically divided into two categories: input and output devices. Input devices allow the user to send data to the computer, while output devices allow the computer to send data to the user.
* Common input devices include keyboards, mice, scanners, and touchscreens. Common output devices include monitors, printers, and speakers.
* Advanced I/O Interfacing involves connecting multiple devices together, often through a bus or network.
* Advanced I/O Interfacing can be used to connect multiple computers together, as well as to connect multiple peripherals together.
* Advanced I/O Interfacing can also be used to control and monitor external devices, such as sensors, motors, and actuators.
* Advanced I/O Interfacing is often used in robotics and automation systems to control and monitor multiple devices simultaneously.
