
### Analog to Digital Conversion 

Analog to Digital Conversion (ADC) is a process of converting analog signals into digital signals. It is used in microcontroller-based systems to measure various physical parameters such as voltage, temperature, pressure, etc.

ADC is used to convert analog signals into digital signals using a sampling rate. The sampling rate is the rate at which the analog signal is sampled, and it is determined by the frequency of the signal. The higher the sampling rate, the more accurate the digital signal will be. 

ADC is used in various applications such as medical instruments, consumer electronics, industrial automation, and automotive systems. It is also used in embedded systems to measure various physical parameters such as temperature, pressure, voltage, etc.
