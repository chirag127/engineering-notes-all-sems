
### Wired/Wireless Networking 

* Wired networks use cables to connect devices together, while wireless networks use radio waves to transmit data. 
* Wired networks are typically faster and more secure than wireless networks, but they require more setup and are more difficult to expand. 
* Wireless networks are easier to set up and maintain, and they can be used to connect devices in remote locations. However, they are less secure than wired networks and can be subject to interference from other wireless devices.
* Network protocols are responsible for managing the communication between devices on a network. Common protocols used in wired networks include Ethernet, TCP/IP, and Token Ring. Common protocols used in wireless networks include 802.11, Bluetooth, and Zigbee. 
* Network topologies are the physical layout of the network. Common topologies used in wired networks include bus, star, and ring. Common topologies used in wireless networks include ad hoc and infrastructure. 
* Network security is an important consideration when setting up a network. Common security measures include encryption, authentication, and access control.
