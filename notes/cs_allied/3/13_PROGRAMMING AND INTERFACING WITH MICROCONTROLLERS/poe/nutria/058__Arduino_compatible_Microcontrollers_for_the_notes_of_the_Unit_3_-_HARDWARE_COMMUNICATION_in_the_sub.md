
### Arduino Compatible Microcontrollers

* Arduino compatible microcontrollers are microcontrollers that are designed to be compatible with the Arduino platform. 
* These microcontrollers are typically based on the Atmel AVR microcontroller architecture, which is the same architecture used by the original Arduino boards. 
* They offer the same features as the original Arduino boards, including the ability to program the microcontroller using the Arduino IDE and to access a wide range of I/O pins. 
* They are often used in applications where the user wants to leverage the existing Arduino environment and libraries, while taking advantage of the lower cost and higher performance of a dedicated microcontroller. 
* The most popular Arduino compatible microcontrollers are the Atmel ATmega328 and the Atmel ATmega2560. 
* These microcontrollers are available in a variety of packages and offer a wide range of features, including multiple I/O pins, timers, and serial communication interfaces. 
* They are also compatible with a variety of shields, which provide additional features such as wireless communication and motor control.
