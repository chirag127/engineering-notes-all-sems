- SQLite is highly scalable and can handle large amounts of data without compromising performance. It is also highly reliable and has a proven track record of being used in mission-critical applications.
- SQLite can be easily integrated with microcontrollers and other embedded systems. It has a small memory footprint and can be compiled into the firmware of the device.
- SQLite is compatible with a wide range of programming languages, including C, C++, Java, Python, and more. This makes it easy to develop applications that use SQLite.
- SQLite is open-source and free to use, which makes it an attractive option for developers who want to keep their costs low.
