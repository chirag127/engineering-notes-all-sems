### Raspberry Pi

Raspberry Pi is a small, low-cost computer that was created with the aim of promoting the teaching of basic computer science in schools. It was developed by the Raspberry Pi Foundation and has become popular among hobbyists and professionals alike due to its flexibility and versatility.

Here are some key points to keep in mind when learning about Raspberry Pi:

- Raspberry Pi is a single-board computer that runs on a Linux-based operating system.
- It was originally designed to be used in education, but has since found a wide range of applications in fields such as home automation, robotics, and media centers.
- The most recent version, Raspberry Pi 4, features a quad-core ARM Cortex-A72 processor, up to 8GB of RAM, and support for dual 4K displays.
- The Raspberry Pi Foundation also produces a range of add-on boards called HATs (Hardware Attached on Top) that can be used to expand the capabilities of the computer.
- Programming languages such as Python and Scratch are commonly used to interact with the Raspberry Pi's GPIO (General Purpose Input/Output) pins, which allow it to interface with a wide range of sensors, motors, and other electronic components.
- The Raspberry Pi community is active and supportive, with a wealth of resources available online including tutorials, forums, and projects.

Overall, Raspberry Pi is a powerful and accessible tool for learning about programming and electronics. Whether you're a beginner or an experienced maker, there's always something new to discover with this versatile computer.
