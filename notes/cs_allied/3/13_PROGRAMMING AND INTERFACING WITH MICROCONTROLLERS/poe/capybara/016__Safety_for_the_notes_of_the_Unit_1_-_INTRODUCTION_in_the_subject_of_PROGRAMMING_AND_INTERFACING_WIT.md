### Safety

When working with microcontrollers, it is important to keep safety in mind to prevent injury and damage to equipment. Here are some safety tips to keep in mind:

- Always wear safety goggles when working with microcontrollers to protect your eyes from damage.
- Make sure to work in a well-ventilated area to prevent inhaling any fumes or particles that may be produced during the process.
- Never touch the microcontroller with wet hands or when standing on a wet surface to avoid electrical shock.
- Always turn off the power supply before connecting or disconnecting any wires to prevent short circuits.
- Keep the work area clean and organized to prevent accidents caused by tripping or falling.
- Use proper tools and equipment when working with microcontrollers to prevent damage to the components.
- Never try to modify or repair a microcontroller that is still connected to a power source.
- Always follow the manufacturer's instructions and safety guidelines when working with microcontrollers.

By following these safety tips, you can ensure a safe and efficient working environment when programming and interfacing with microcontrollers.
