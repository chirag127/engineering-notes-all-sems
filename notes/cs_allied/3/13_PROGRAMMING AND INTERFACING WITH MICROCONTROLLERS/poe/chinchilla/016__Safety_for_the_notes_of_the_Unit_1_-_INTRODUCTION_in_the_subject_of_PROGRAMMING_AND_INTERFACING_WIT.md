### Safety

When working with microcontrollers, it is important to keep safety in mind. Here are some safety tips to follow:

- Always wear proper safety gear, such as safety glasses or goggles, when working with microcontrollers.
- Never touch the pins or leads of a microcontroller while it is powered on. This can lead to electrical shock or damage to the microcontroller.
- Use caution when connecting or disconnecting wires from a microcontroller, as there may be electrical current flowing through them.
- Keep a fire extinguisher nearby in case of electrical fires.
- Do not expose microcontrollers to extreme temperatures or moisture, as this can damage them.
- Use caution when handling soldering irons or other hot tools, and always work in a well-ventilated area to avoid inhaling fumes.
- Make sure to properly ground yourself before handling microcontrollers to avoid static electricity buildup, which can damage the components.
- Follow all manufacturer guidelines and instructions when working with microcontrollers, and do not attempt to modify or alter them without proper training and knowledge.

By following these safety tips, you can help ensure a safe working environment when programming and interfacing with microcontrollers.
