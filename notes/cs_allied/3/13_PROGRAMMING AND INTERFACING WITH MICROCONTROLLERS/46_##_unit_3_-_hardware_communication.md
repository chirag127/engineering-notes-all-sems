## Unit 3 - HARDWARE COMMUNICATION

Unit 3 - Hardware Communication in a subject focuses on the different methods and techniques used to communicate between hardware devices and systems. This unit covers the basics of hardware communication, including the different types of communication protocols, the different communication interfaces, and the different communication technologies.

In this unit, students will learn about:

1. Communication protocols: Communication protocols are the rules and standards that govern the communication between devices. This includes an overview of common communication protocols, such as RS-232, I2C, and SPI.

2. Communication interfaces: Communication interfaces are the physical and electrical connections that are used to transfer data between devices. This includes an overview of common communication interfaces, such as USB, Ethernet, and Wi-Fi.

3. Communication technologies: Communication technologies are the underlying technologies that are used to transfer data between devices. This includes an overview of common communication technologies, such as infrared, Bluetooth, and Zigbee.

By understanding the different methods and techniques used in hardware communication, students can gain a deeper appreciation for the technology and its impact on embedded systems. This understanding can help students to develop the skills necessary to design, build, and program embedded systems that use hardware communication.
