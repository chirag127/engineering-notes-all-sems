### Sketch for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of PROGRAMMING AND INTERFACING WITH MICROCONTROLLERS

Sketch is a term used in the context of programming and interfacing with microcontrollers, and refers to a program or set of instructions that is written to control the behavior of a microcontroller. A sketch is typically written in a high-level programming language, such as C or Python, and is used to specify the actions that the microcontroller should take in response to inputs and events.

In the context of Unit 1 - Introduction in the subject of Programming and Interfacing with Microcontrollers, a sketch might be used to:

1. Control the behavior of a microcontroller: A sketch can be written to control the behavior of a microcontroller, such as reading inputs from sensors, processing data, and controlling outputs.

2. Demonstrate the capabilities of a microcontroller: A sketch can be used to demonstrate the capabilities of a microcontroller, such as reading inputs from sensors, processing data, and controlling outputs.

3. Provide a starting point for students: A sketch can be used as a starting point for students to learn about programming and interfacing with microcontrollers, and can be modified and extended as students gain experience and knowledge.

Sketches are an important tool for programming and interfacing with microcontrollers, as they provide a means of controlling the behavior of these devices and demonstrating their capabilities. By writing and modifying sketches, students can gain a deeper understanding of the capabilities of microcontrollers and the programming techniques used to control them.
