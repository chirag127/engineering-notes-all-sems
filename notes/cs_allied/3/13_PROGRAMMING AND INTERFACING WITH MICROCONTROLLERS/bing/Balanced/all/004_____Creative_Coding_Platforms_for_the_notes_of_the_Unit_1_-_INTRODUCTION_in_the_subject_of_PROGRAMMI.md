# Creative Coding Platforms

- Creative coding is the practice of using programming languages and techniques to create artistic expressions, such as animations, games, music, and interactive design.
- Creative coding platforms are tools or environments that enable users to write, run, and share code for creative purposes, often with visual or audio feedback.
- Some examples of creative coding platforms are:

  - **Scratch**: A free online platform that allows children of all ages to code, share, and remix their own stories, games, and animations. Scratch is translated into 50+ languages and available both online and offline.
  - **p5.js**: A JavaScript library that is based on the core principles of Processing, a popular creative coding framework. p5.js enables users to create graphics, sound, and interactivity on the web.
  - **Tynker**: A leading K-12 creative coding platform that teaches students of all ages the coding skills to design and power animations, games, music, robots and drones, smart devices, virtual worlds like Minecraft, and more. Tynker offers a variety of courses, projects, and activities for different levels and interests.
  - **OpenProcessing**: A web-based platform that allows users to create and share sketches using Processing or p5.js. OpenProcessing is a community of creative coders, educators, and designers that explore, experiment, and play.
  - **Glitch**: A collaborative programming environment that lives in your browser and deploys code as you type. Glitch lets users remix existing projects or start from scratch, and supports a variety of languages and frameworks. Glitch also integrates with image and video sharing APIs, such as Instagram and Flickr, for inspiration.
