# Creative Coding Platforms

Creative coding is a form of programming that uses code to create artistic expressions, such as animations, games, music, and interactive design. Creative coding platforms are tools or environments that enable users to write, run, and share code for creative purposes. Some of the features and benefits of creative coding platforms are:

- They often use visual or simplified languages that are easy to learn and understand, such as Scratch, p5.js, or Tynker  .
- They provide libraries or frameworks that support various media and modes of interaction, such as graphics, sound, video, sensors, and web APIs  .
- They allow users to experiment and iterate quickly, as well as to debug and test their code in real time, such as Glitch or OpenProcessing .
- They foster a community of creative coders, educators, and designers that can collaborate, inspire, and learn from each other, such as DevArt or Scratch .

Creative coding platforms can be used for various educational and artistic purposes, such as:

- Teaching and learning the fundamentals of programming, logic, and computational thinking, as well as the connections between code and creativity  .
- Exploring and expressing one's ideas, emotions, and aesthetics through code, as well as developing one's artistic voice and style  .
- Creating and sharing original or remixed works of art, such as games, animations, music, or interactive installations, that can engage and entertain audiences  .
- Solving problems and challenges, such as designing for social good, making data visualizations, or prototyping new products or services  .
