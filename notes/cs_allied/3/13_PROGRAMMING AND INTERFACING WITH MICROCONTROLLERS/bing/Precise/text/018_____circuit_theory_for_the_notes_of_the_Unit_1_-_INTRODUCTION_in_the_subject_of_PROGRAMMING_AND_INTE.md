### Circuit Theory - Unit 1: INTRODUCTION

Circuit theory is a branch of electrical engineering that deals with the analysis and design of electrical circuits. It is used to understand the behavior of circuits and to predict their performance. Some key concepts in circuit theory include:

1. **Circuit elements**: These are the basic building blocks of a circuit, such as resistors, capacitors, and inductors.
2. **Ohm's Law**: This law states that the current flowing through a conductor is directly proportional to the voltage applied across it.
3. **Kirchhoff's Laws**: These laws describe the conservation of charge and energy in a circuit.
4. **Thevenin's and Norton's Theorems**: These theorems provide methods for simplifying complex circuits.
5. **Superposition**: This principle states that the response of a linear circuit to multiple inputs is the sum of the responses to each input considered separately.

Circuit theory is an essential foundation for the study of electronics and is used in the design of many electronic devices, including microcontrollers. Understanding circuit theory is crucial for programming and interfacing with microcontrollers.
