### Wired/Wireless Networking

Wired and wireless networking are two methods of connecting devices to a network. Wired networking involves the use of physical cables to connect devices, while wireless networking uses radio waves to transmit data between devices.

#### Wired Networking
- Wired networking is typically faster and more reliable than wireless networking.
- Wired networks use Ethernet cables to connect devices to a network.
- Ethernet cables come in different categories, with Cat5e and Cat6 being the most common.
- Wired networks are more secure than wireless networks, as data is transmitted through physical cables and is less susceptible to interception.

#### Wireless Networking
- Wireless networking allows devices to connect to a network without the use of physical cables.
- Wireless networks use radio waves to transmit data between devices.
- Wireless networks are typically slower and less reliable than wired networks.
- Wireless networks are less secure than wired networks, as data is transmitted through the air and is more susceptible to interception.

#### Comparison between Wired and Wireless Networking
- Wired networks are faster, more reliable, and more secure than wireless networks.
- Wireless networks are more convenient and flexible, as devices can connect to the network without the use of physical cables.
- The choice between wired and wireless networking depends on the specific needs and requirements of the user.
