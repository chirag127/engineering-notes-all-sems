### Representing “reality” with computers

- Modeling reality inside a computer is done at several levels:
    1. Physical conversion of input signals into digital signals (as well as converting digital signals into an output signal).
    2. Encoding schemes for representing information in binary.
    3. Algorithmic modeling of physical processes .
- Computers are a universal machine of sorts. They can emulate almost any imaginable process or task .
- Data models are used to represent reality as simply as possible. One such model is the raster data model, which is widely used in applications ranging far beyond geographic information systems (GISs) .
