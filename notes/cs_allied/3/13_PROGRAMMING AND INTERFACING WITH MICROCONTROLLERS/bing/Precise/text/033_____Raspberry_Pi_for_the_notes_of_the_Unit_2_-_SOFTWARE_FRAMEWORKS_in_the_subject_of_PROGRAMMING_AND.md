### Raspberry Pi

Raspberry Pi is a series of small single-board computers developed in the United Kingdom by the Raspberry Pi Foundation. It was developed to promote the teaching of basic computer science in schools and in developing countries.

Some key points to note about Raspberry Pi are:

1. It is a low-cost computer that can be used for a variety of projects.
2. It runs on a variety of operating systems, including Raspbian, a Debian-based operating system.
3. It has a range of models with varying specifications, including the Raspberry Pi Zero, Raspberry Pi 3, and Raspberry Pi 4.
4. It has a large and active community of users and developers, with a wide range of resources and tutorials available online.
5. It can be used for a variety of projects, including media centers, game consoles, and home automation systems.
6. It supports a range of programming languages, including Python, Scratch, and C++.
7. It has a range of expansion boards, called HATs (Hardware Attached on Top), that can be used to add additional functionality.

Raspberry Pi is a versatile and powerful tool for learning about computer science and electronics, and for developing a wide range of projects. It is an important part of the software frameworks unit in the subject of programming and interfacing with microcontrollers.
