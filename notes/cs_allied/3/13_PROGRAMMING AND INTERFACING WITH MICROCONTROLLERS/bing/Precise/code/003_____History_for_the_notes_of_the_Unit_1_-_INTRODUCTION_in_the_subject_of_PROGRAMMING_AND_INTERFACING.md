### Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION: History of Programming and Interfacing with Microcontrollers

1. Microcontrollers are small computers that are embedded in various devices and systems to control their functions.
2. The first microcontrollers were developed in the 1970s, with the introduction of the Intel 4004 and the Texas Instruments TMS1000.
3. These early microcontrollers were limited in their capabilities and were primarily used for simple control tasks.
4. As technology advanced, microcontrollers became more powerful and versatile, allowing for more complex control systems and applications.
5. Programming languages and tools for microcontrollers also evolved, making it easier for developers to create and implement control systems.
6. Today, microcontrollers are used in a wide range of applications, from consumer electronics to industrial control systems, and continue to play a vital role in the development of new technologies.
