### openFrameworks

- openFrameworks is an open source C++ toolkit for creative coding .
- It is designed to assist the creative process by providing a simple and intuitive framework for experimentation .
- It works as a general purpose glue and wraps together several commonly used libraries, including: OpenGL, GLEW, GLUT, libtess2 and cairo for graphics .
- openFrameworks is a cross-platform toolkit and supports as many development environments and operating systems as possible .
- When you download openFrameworks, you can pick your platform and development environment of choice, and have projects and examples ready to learn from and play with .
- It is also available for arm boards running linux like Raspberry Pi, Beaglebone (black), Pandaboard, BeagleBoard and others .
- The master branch of openFrameworks on GitHub contains the newest, most recently updated code .
