### PIC for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of PROGRAMMING AND INTERFACING WITH MICROCONTROLLERS

- PIC stands for Peripheral Interface Controller.
- It is a family of microcontrollers developed by Microchip Technology.
- PIC microcontrollers are popular due to their ease of use, wide availability, and low cost.
- They are commonly used in embedded systems and for interfacing with various peripheral devices.
- PIC microcontrollers are available in a range of sizes and capabilities, from small 8-bit devices to larger 32-bit devices.
- They are programmed using a variety of languages, including assembly, C, and BASIC.
- PIC microcontrollers have a range of built-in peripherals, including timers, serial communication, and analog-to-digital converters.
- They are commonly used in applications such as motor control, sensor interfacing, and user interfaces.
- PIC microcontrollers are a popular choice for hobbyists and students due to their low cost and ease of use.
- There are many resources available for learning how to program and interface with PIC microcontrollers, including online tutorials, books, and development kits.
