### Sensors and Actuators

Sensors and actuators are essential components of hardware communication in the field of programming and interfacing with microcontrollers. They are used to interact with the physical world by measuring and controlling physical parameters.

#### Sensors
- A sensor is a device that detects and responds to changes in physical parameters such as temperature, pressure, light, or motion.
- Sensors convert physical parameters into electrical signals that can be processed by microcontrollers.
- Common types of sensors include temperature sensors, light sensors, motion sensors, and pressure sensors.

#### Actuators
- An actuator is a device that converts electrical signals into physical motion or action.
- Actuators are used to control physical parameters such as position, speed, or force.
- Common types of actuators include motors, solenoids, and relays.

In summary, sensors and actuators are essential components of hardware communication in the field of programming and interfacing with microcontrollers. They enable microcontrollers to interact with the physical world by measuring and controlling physical parameters. Sensors detect changes in physical parameters and convert them into electrical signals, while actuators convert electrical signals into physical motion or action.
