### Measurements for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of PROGRAMMING AND INTERFACING WITH MICROCONTROLLERS

1. Measurements are an essential part of programming and interfacing with microcontrollers.
2. Measurements can be used to determine the values of various physical quantities such as voltage, current, resistance, temperature, etc.
3. Measurements can be made using various tools and techniques such as multimeters, oscilloscopes, and sensors.
4. Accurate measurements are important for ensuring the proper functioning of microcontroller-based systems.
5. Measurements can be used to calibrate and test microcontroller-based systems to ensure that they are operating within specified parameters.
6. Measurements can also be used to diagnose and troubleshoot problems in microcontroller-based systems.
7. It is important to understand the principles of measurement and the proper use of measurement tools and techniques in order to make accurate and reliable measurements.
