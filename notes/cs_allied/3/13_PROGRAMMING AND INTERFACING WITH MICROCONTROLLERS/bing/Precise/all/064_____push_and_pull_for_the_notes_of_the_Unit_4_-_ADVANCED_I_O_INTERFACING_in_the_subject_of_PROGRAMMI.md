### Unit 4 - ADVANCED I/O INTERFACING: Push and Pull

Push and pull are two concepts in the field of advanced I/O interfacing with microcontrollers. These concepts are related to the way data is transferred between the microcontroller and external devices.

1. **Push**: In push mode, the microcontroller actively sends data to the external device. The microcontroller initiates the data transfer and controls the timing of the transfer. This mode is commonly used when the microcontroller needs to send data to a device that is ready to receive it.

2. **Pull**: In pull mode, the external device actively requests data from the microcontroller. The external device initiates the data transfer and controls the timing of the transfer. This mode is commonly used when the external device needs data from the microcontroller, but the microcontroller may not be ready to send it immediately.

Both push and pull modes have their advantages and disadvantages, and the choice of mode depends on the specific requirements of the system being designed. It is important for a programmer and system designer to understand these concepts and be able to choose the appropriate mode for their application.
