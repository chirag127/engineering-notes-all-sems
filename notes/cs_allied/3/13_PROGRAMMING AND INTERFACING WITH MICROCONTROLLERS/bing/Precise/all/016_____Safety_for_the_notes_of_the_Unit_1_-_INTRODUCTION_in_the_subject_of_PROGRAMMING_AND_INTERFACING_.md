# Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION
## Safety

- Safety is an important aspect of programming and interfacing with microcontrollers.
- Microcontrollers are electronic devices that can control other devices and systems. They are used in a wide range of applications, from simple toys to complex industrial systems.
- When working with microcontrollers, it is important to follow safety guidelines to prevent damage to the microcontroller, other devices, or injury to the user.
- Some safety guidelines to follow when working with microcontrollers include:
  - Always read the datasheet and user manual of the microcontroller and other devices before using them.
  - Use appropriate protective equipment, such as gloves and safety glasses, when working with microcontrollers and other electronic devices.
  - Ensure that the microcontroller and other devices are properly grounded to prevent electrostatic discharge (ESD) damage.
  - Do not touch the microcontroller or other electronic devices with wet hands or when standing in water.
  - Do not apply excessive voltage or current to the microcontroller or other devices, as this can cause damage or injury.
  - Always use appropriate tools and equipment when working with microcontrollers and other electronic devices.
  - Follow all local and national electrical and safety codes when working with microcontrollers and other electronic devices.
  - Always dispose of electronic waste, such as used microcontrollers and batteries, in an environmentally responsible manner.
