# Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION: History of Programming and Interfacing with Microcontrollers

- Microcontrollers are small computers that are embedded in various electronic devices to control their functions.
- The first microcontroller was developed in the 1970s by Texas Instruments.
- Microcontrollers are programmed using a variety of programming languages, including assembly, C, and C++.
- Interfacing with microcontrollers involves connecting them to other devices, such as sensors, displays, and motors, to allow them to interact with the physical world.
- Over time, microcontrollers have become smaller, more powerful, and more versatile, allowing them to be used in a wide range of applications.
