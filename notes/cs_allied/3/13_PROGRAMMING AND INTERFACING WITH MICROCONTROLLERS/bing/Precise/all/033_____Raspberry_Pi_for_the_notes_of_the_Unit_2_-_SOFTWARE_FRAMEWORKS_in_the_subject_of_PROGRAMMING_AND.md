### Unit 2 - SOFTWARE FRAMEWORKS
#### Raspberry Pi

- Raspberry Pi is a series of small single-board computers developed in the United Kingdom by the Raspberry Pi Foundation.
- The original model became popular for its low cost and small size, making it suitable for use in education, robotics, and home automation projects.
- The Raspberry Pi runs a variety of operating systems, including Raspbian, a Debian-based Linux distribution, and other Linux distributions such as Ubuntu and Arch Linux.
- The Raspberry Pi also supports other operating systems such as Windows 10 IoT Core, a version of Windows 10 designed for use on Internet of Things devices.
- The Raspberry Pi has a number of software frameworks available for use, including the WiringPi library for interfacing with the GPIO pins, the PiCamera library for interfacing with the camera module, and the Pygame library for creating games and multimedia applications.
- The Raspberry Pi also has support for a number of programming languages, including Python, C, C++, Java, and Scratch.
- The Raspberry Pi has a large and active community of developers and users, with many resources available online for learning and development.
