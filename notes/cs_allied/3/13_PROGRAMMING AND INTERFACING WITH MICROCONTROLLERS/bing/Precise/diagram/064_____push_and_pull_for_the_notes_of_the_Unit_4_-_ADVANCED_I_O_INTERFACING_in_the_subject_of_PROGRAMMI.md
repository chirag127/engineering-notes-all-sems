### Unit 4 - ADVANCED I/O INTERFACING
#### Push and Pull

- Push and pull are two types of output configurations for digital circuits.
- A push configuration, also known as a totem-pole output, actively drives the output high or low.
- A pull configuration, also known as an open-drain or open-collector output, only actively drives the output low.
- In a pull configuration, an external pull-up resistor is required to drive the output high.
- The choice between push and pull configurations depends on the specific application and requirements.
- Push configurations are generally faster and can source more current than pull configurations.
- Pull configurations are more flexible and can be used in wired-AND configurations.
