### Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION: History

1. The history of microcontrollers can be traced back to the invention of the transistor in 1947.
2. The first microprocessor, the Intel 4004, was introduced in 1971. It was a 4-bit processor designed for use in calculators.
3. In 1974, the Intel 8080, an 8-bit microprocessor, was introduced. It was designed for use in computers and became the basis for the first personal computers.
4. The first microcontroller, the Intel 8048, was introduced in 1976. It was an 8-bit device with on-chip memory and peripherals, designed for use in embedded systems.
5. Since then, microcontrollers have evolved to include more advanced features such as larger memory, faster processing speeds, and more peripherals.
6. Today, microcontrollers are used in a wide variety of applications, including consumer electronics, automotive systems, and industrial control systems.
