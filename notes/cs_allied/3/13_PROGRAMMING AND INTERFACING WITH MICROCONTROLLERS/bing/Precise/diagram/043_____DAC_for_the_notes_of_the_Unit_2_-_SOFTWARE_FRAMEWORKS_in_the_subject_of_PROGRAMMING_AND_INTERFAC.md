### DAC
DAC stands for Digital-to-Analog Converter. It is a system that converts a digital signal into an analog signal. A DAC is used in many applications where an analog signal is required, such as in audio and video systems.

Here are some key points to remember about DACs:
- A DAC converts a digital signal into an analog signal by using a reference voltage and a digital code.
- The resolution of a DAC is determined by the number of bits in the digital code.
- The accuracy of a DAC is determined by the stability of the reference voltage and the linearity of the conversion process.
- There are several types of DACs, including binary-weighted, R-2R ladder, and sigma-delta DACs.
- DACs are used in many applications, including audio and video systems, instrumentation, and control systems.
