### PIC for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of PROGRAMMING AND INTERFACING WITH MICROCONTROLLERS

- PIC stands for **Peripheral Interface Controller**.
- It is a family of microcontrollers developed by **Microchip Technology**.
- PIC microcontrollers are popular due to their ease of use, wide availability, and large user base.
- They are commonly used in embedded systems and for interfacing with various peripherals.
- PIC microcontrollers come in a variety of sizes and capabilities, ranging from small 8-bit devices to powerful 32-bit devices.
- They are known for their low power consumption, making them suitable for battery-powered applications.
- PIC microcontrollers can be programmed using a variety of languages, including assembly, C, and BASIC.
- They are commonly used in applications such as motor control, sensor interfacing, and communication.
