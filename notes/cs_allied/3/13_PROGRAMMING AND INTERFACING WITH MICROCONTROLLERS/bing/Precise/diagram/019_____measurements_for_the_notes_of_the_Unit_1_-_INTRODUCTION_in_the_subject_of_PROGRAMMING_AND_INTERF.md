### Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION: Measurements

1. Measurements are a fundamental concept in the field of programming and interfacing with microcontrollers.
2. Measurements refer to the process of determining the value of a physical quantity by comparing it with a standard unit.
3. In the context of microcontrollers, measurements are used to gather data from sensors and other input devices, which can then be processed and used to control various outputs.
4. Common units of measurement used in microcontroller programming include volts, amperes, ohms, and hertz, among others.
5. Accurate measurements are essential for the proper functioning of microcontroller-based systems, as they provide the data needed for decision-making and control.
6. Various tools and techniques are used to make measurements in microcontroller systems, including multimeters, oscilloscopes, and logic analyzers.
7. It is important to understand the principles of measurement and the limitations of the tools used in order to make accurate and reliable measurements.
