### Wired/Wireless Networking

#### Unit 4 - ADVANCED I/O INTERFACING in the subject of PROGRAMMING AND INTERFACING WITH MICROCONTROLLERS

- Wired networking involves the use of physical cables to connect devices to a network.
- Common types of wired networking include Ethernet and USB.
- Ethernet is a widely used standard for wired networking and uses a cable with an RJ-45 connector.
- USB (Universal Serial Bus) is another common type of wired networking and is used to connect peripherals to a computer.
- Wireless networking, on the other hand, uses radio waves to transmit data between devices.
- Common types of wireless networking include Wi-Fi and Bluetooth.
- Wi-Fi is a widely used standard for wireless networking and allows devices to connect to a network without the use of cables.
- Bluetooth is another common type of wireless networking and is used for short-range communication between devices.
- Both wired and wireless networking have their advantages and disadvantages.
- Wired networking is generally faster and more secure than wireless networking.
- However, wireless networking is more convenient and flexible as it does not require the use of cables.
- When choosing between wired and wireless networking, it is important to consider factors such as speed, security, and convenience.
