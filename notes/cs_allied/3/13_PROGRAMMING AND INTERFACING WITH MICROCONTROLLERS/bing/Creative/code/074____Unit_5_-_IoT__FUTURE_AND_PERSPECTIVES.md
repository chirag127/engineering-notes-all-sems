## Unit 5 - IoT, Future and Perspectives

- IoT stands for Internet of Things, which is a network of physical devices, sensors, actuators, and software that can communicate and exchange data over the internet.
- IoT has many applications in various domains, such as smart homes, smart cities, smart agriculture, smart health, smart industry, smart transportation, and smart environment.
- IoT can provide benefits such as improved efficiency, convenience, safety, security, sustainability, and quality of life.
- IoT also faces challenges such as privacy, security, interoperability, scalability, reliability, and ethical issues.
- IoT is expected to grow rapidly in the future, with more devices, data, and services being connected and integrated.
- IoT will also have an impact on the society, economy, and environment, creating new opportunities and challenges for individuals, businesses, and governments.
- IoT will require new skills, knowledge, and competencies for the workforce, as well as new policies, regulations, and standards for the governance.
- IoT will also raise new ethical, social, and legal questions, such as who owns the data, who is responsible for the actions, and who benefits from the value.
