### Raspberry Pi for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of PROGRAMMING AND INTERFACING WITH MICROCONTROLLERS

Raspberry Pi is a small, low-cost, single-board computer that was developed in the United Kingdom in 2012. It is designed to promote the teaching of computer science and electronics, and it has become a popular platform for hobbyists, educators, and makers.

Components of Raspberry Pi:

1. Processor: Raspberry Pi is equipped with a Broadcom BCM2835 system-on-a-chip (SoC), which includes an ARM1176JZF-S 700 MHz processor and a VideoCore IV GPU.

2. Memory: Raspberry Pi is equipped with either 256 MB or 512 MB of SDRAM, depending on the model.

3. Storage: Raspberry Pi is equipped with a microSD card slot, which is used to store the operating system and data.

4. Connectivity: Raspberry Pi is equipped with Ethernet, USB, HDMI, and audio outputs, as well as a 40-pin expansion header for connecting additional peripherals.

Applications of Raspberry Pi:

1. Education: Raspberry Pi is widely used in education to teach computer science and electronics, and it is used in a variety of educational programs and initiatives.

2. Hobby and Maker Projects: Raspberry Pi is popular among hobbyists and makers, who use it to build a wide range of projects, such as robots, media centers, and home automation systems.

3. Industrial and Commercial Applications: Raspberry Pi is used in a variety of industrial and commercial applications, such as digital signage, point-of-sale systems, and industrial control systems.
