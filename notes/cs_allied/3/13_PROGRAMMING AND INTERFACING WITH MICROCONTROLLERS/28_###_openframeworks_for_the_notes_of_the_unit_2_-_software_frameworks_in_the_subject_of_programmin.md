### openFrameworks for the notes of the Unit 2 - SOFTWARE FRAMEWORKS in the subject of PROGRAMMING AND INTERFACING WITH MICROCONTROLLERS

openFrameworks is an open-source software framework for creative coding. It provides a platform for developing interactive applications and visualizations, including applications for wearable computing, mixed reality, and the Internet of Things (IoT).

The following are some of the key features of openFrameworks:

1. Cross-platform: openFrameworks runs on a variety of platforms, including Windows, macOS, and Linux, making it accessible to a wide range of users.

2. Interactivity: openFrameworks provides a platform for developing interactive applications, including support for mouse, keyboard, and other input devices.

3. Graphics: openFrameworks includes a powerful graphics library, making it easy to create and display graphics, animations, and visualizations.

4. Addons: openFrameworks has a large community of developers who have created a wide range of addons, including support for specific hardware platforms and sensors, and libraries for specific tasks such as audio processing and computer vision.

5. Community support: openFrameworks has a large and active community, providing support and resources for users, including tutorials, forums, and documentation.

Overall, openFrameworks is a powerful and flexible platform for developing interactive applications and visualizations. Its ease of use, cross-platform support, and large community of developers make it an ideal tool for artists, designers, and developers working in the fields of wearable computing, mixed reality, and IoT.
