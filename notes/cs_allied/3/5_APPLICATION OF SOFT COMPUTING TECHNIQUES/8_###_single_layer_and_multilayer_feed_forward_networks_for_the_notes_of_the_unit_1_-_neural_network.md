### single layer and multilayer feed forward networks for the notes of the Unit 1 - Neural Networks-I (Introduction & Architecture) in the subject of APPLICATION OF SOFT COMPUTING TECHNIQUES

Single Layer and Multilayer Feed Forward Networks are two types of artificial neural networks.

Single Layer Feed Forward Network:
1. Consists of a single layer of artificial neurons, with each neuron processing the inputs and producing an output.
2. Used for simple linear problems, where the relationship between the inputs and outputs is linear.
3. Easy to train, but limited in their ability to model complex non-linear relationships.

Multilayer Feed Forward Network:
1. Consists of multiple layers of artificial neurons, with each layer processing the outputs of the previous layer to produce its own outputs.
2. Used for complex non-linear problems, where the relationship between the inputs and outputs is non-linear.
3. More powerful than single layer feed forward networks, but more difficult to train.
4. Can be trained using supervised learning algorithms, such as backpropagation.

In summary, single layer feed forward networks are used for simple linear problems, while multilayer feed forward networks are used for complex non-linear problems. Understanding the difference between these two types of networks is crucial for choosing the right type of network for a given problem.
