### effect of learning rule co-efficient for the notes of the Unit 2 - Neural Networks-II (Back propagation networks) in the subject of APPLICATION OF SOFT COMPUTING TECHNIQUES

The learning rate coefficient in Back Propagation Networks determines the step size at which the model updates its weights during the training process. A high learning rate results in quick convergence but may cause overshooting and oscillation, while a low learning rate may converge slowly or get stuck in a local minimum. Hence, finding an optimal learning rate is crucial for the effective training of the neural network. In Unit 2 of "Application of Soft Computing Techniques", the effect of the learning rate coefficient on the performance of the network is discussed in detail. Additionally, various techniques for finding an optimal learning rate are also introduced, such as grid search, random search, learning rate schedules, and adaptive learning rate methods. Understanding the impact of the learning rate coefficient is crucial for the successful application of Back Propagation Networks in real-world problems.
