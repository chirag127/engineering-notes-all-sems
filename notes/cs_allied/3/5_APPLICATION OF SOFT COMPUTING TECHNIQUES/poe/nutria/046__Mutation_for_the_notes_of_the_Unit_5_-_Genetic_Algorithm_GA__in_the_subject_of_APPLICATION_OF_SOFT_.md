
### Mutation for the notes of the Unit 5 - Genetic Algorithm(GA) in the subject of APPLICATION OF SOFT COMPUTING TECHNIQUES

1. Mutation is a genetic operator used to maintain genetic diversity from one generation of a population of genetic algorithm chromosomes to the next.
2. Mutation involves the random alteration of one or more gene values in a chromosome.
3. The mutation rate is a parameter of a genetic algorithm which determines the degree of randomness introduced into the population.
4. A low mutation rate can cause the genetic algorithm to converge on a local optimum, while a high mutation rate can help it escape local optima.
5. Mutation can also be used to introduce completely new solutions into the population, and thus explore new regions of the search space.
6. Mutation can be applied to all types of chromosomes, including binary, integer, and real-valued representations.
7. Mutation is often used in conjunction with other genetic operators, such as crossover and selection, to create a complete genetic algorithm.
