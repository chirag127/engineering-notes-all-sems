
### Genetic Representations for the Notes of the Unit 5 - Genetic Algorithm (GA) in the Subject of Application of Soft Computing Techniques

* A genetic algorithm is a search technique used in computing to find exact or approximate solutions to optimization and search problems. 
* Genetic algorithms are based on the principles of natural selection and genetics, and can be used to solve a wide variety of problems. 
* Genetic algorithms use a set of rules, known as a genetic representation, to encode solutions to a problem. 
* The genetic representation is a way of representing a problem in a way that can be manipulated by the genetic algorithm. 
* A genetic representation can be a binary string, a list of numbers, or a set of symbols. 
* In a genetic algorithm, a population of possible solutions is created, and each solution is represented by a unique genetic representation. 
* The genetic algorithm then uses a set of operators, such as selection, crossover, and mutation, to manipulate the population and find better solutions. 
* The genetic algorithm can also use a fitness function to evaluate the quality of the solutions and determine which solutions are better than others. 
* The genetic algorithm continues to manipulate the population until a satisfactory solution is found.
