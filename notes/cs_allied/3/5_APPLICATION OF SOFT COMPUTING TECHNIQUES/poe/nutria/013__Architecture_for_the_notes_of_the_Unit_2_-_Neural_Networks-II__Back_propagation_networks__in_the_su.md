

### Architecture for the Notes of Unit 2 - Neural Networks-II (Back Propagation Networks)

1. Neural Networks-II (Back Propagation Networks): Neural networks-II (also known as back propagation networks) are a type of artificial intelligence system that uses a layered structure of interconnected nodes to identify patterns and correlations in data.

2. Structure of Back Propagation Networks: Back propagation networks have an input layer, one or more hidden layers, and an output layer. Each layer is composed of neurons, which are connected to each other by weights.

3. Training of Back Propagation Networks: Back propagation networks are trained by adjusting the weights between the neurons in each layer. This is done by using a technique known as gradient descent, which minimizes the error between the predicted output and the actual output.

4. Applications of Back Propagation Networks: Back propagation networks can be used for a variety of tasks such as image recognition, natural language processing, and financial forecasting. They are also used in robotics and autonomous systems.
