
### Procedures of Genetic Algorithm (GA)
1. **Initialization**: The initial population is randomly generated.
2. **Fitness Function**: The fitness function is used to evaluate the fitness of each individual in the population.
3. **Selection**: The individuals with higher fitness are selected for reproduction.
4. **Crossover**: Crossover is performed on the selected individuals to generate new offspring.
5. **Mutation**: Mutation is performed on the offspring to introduce new genetic material into the population.
6. **Replacement**: The new offspring are added to the population and the least fit individuals are removed.
7. **Termination**: The algorithm terminates when a certain condition is met.
