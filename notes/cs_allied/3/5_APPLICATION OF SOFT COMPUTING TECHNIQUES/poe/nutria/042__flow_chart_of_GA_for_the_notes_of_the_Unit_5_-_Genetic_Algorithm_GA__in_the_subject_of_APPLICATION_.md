
### Flow Chart of GA for the Notes of the Unit 5 - Genetic Algorithm(GA) in the Subject of Application of Soft Computing Techniques

1. Genetic Algorithms (GA) are a type of evolutionary algorithms which are inspired by the process of natural selection.

2. GA uses a population of potential solutions to a problem and applies a selection process to choose the best solutions.

3. The selection process is based on the fitness of the solutions. The fittest solutions are more likely to be chosen for reproduction.

4. Reproduction is done by combining the best solutions to produce new, better solutions.

5. Mutation is used to introduce random changes to the solutions in order to explore new possibilities.

6. The process is repeated until a satisfactory solution is found.
