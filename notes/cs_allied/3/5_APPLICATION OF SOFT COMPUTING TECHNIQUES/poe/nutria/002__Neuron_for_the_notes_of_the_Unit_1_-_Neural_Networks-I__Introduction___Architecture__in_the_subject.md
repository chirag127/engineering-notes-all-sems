
### Neuron

Neurons are the basic building blocks of neural networks. They are responsible for processing input data and producing output. A neuron is a computational unit that takes one or more inputs, performs some operation on them and produces one or more outputs.

1. Structure: A neuron is composed of a cell body and several processes called dendrites and axon. The cell body contains the nucleus and other organelles, while the dendrites and axon are extensions of the cell body that receive and transmit signals, respectively.

2. Activation Function: The activation function of a neuron determines the output of the neuron based on the inputs it receives. Commonly used activation functions include sigmoid, tanh, and ReLU.

3. Learning: Neurons can learn to recognize patterns in data and adjust their weights accordingly. This process is called training and is essential for the development of neural networks.

4. Types: There are several types of neurons, including input neurons, output neurons, and hidden neurons. Input neurons receive data from the outside world, output neurons produce the desired output, and hidden neurons process data and pass it along to other neurons.
