### back propagation learning methods for the notes of the Unit 2 - Neural Networks-II (Back propagation networks) in the subject of APPLICATION OF SOFT COMPUTING TECHNIQUES

Backpropagation is a supervised learning algorithm used to train artificial neural networks, particularly feedforward neural networks. The goal of backpropagation is to minimize the error between the predicted output and the actual output.

There are several backpropagation learning methods, including:

1. Stochastic gradient descent (SGD): In SGD, the weights are updated after each training example, using the gradient of the error with respect to the weights.

2. Mini-batch gradient descent: In mini-batch gradient descent, the weights are updated after processing a small batch of training examples, rather than after each individual example.

3. Batch gradient descent: In batch gradient descent, the weights are updated after processing all of the training examples, using the average gradient of the error with respect to the weights.

The choice of backpropagation learning method depends on the size of the dataset, the computational resources available, and the desired accuracy and convergence rate.

In conclusion, backpropagation is a supervised learning algorithm used to train artificial neural networks. There are several backpropagation learning methods, including stochastic gradient descent, mini-batch gradient descent, and batch gradient descent, and the choice of method depends on the size of the dataset, the computational resources available, and the desired accuracy and convergence rate. Understanding backpropagation is important for designing and training effective neural networks.
