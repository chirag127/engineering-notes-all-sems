### Genetic representations for the notes of the Unit 5 - Genetic Algorithm(GA) in the subject of APPLICATION OF SOFT COMPUTING TECHNIQUES

A genetic representation is the way in which a candidate solution is encoded in a genetic algorithm (GA). The genetic representation determines how the solution is manipulated during the search process, and has a significant impact on the performance of the GA.

There are several common genetic representations, including:
1. Binary representation: solutions are encoded as binary strings, where each bit represents a decision variable.
2. Integer representation: solutions are encoded as integers, where each integer represents a decision variable.
3. Real-valued representation: solutions are encoded as real numbers, where each real number represents a decision variable.
4. Permutation representation: solutions are encoded as permutations, where each permutation represents a sequence of decision variables.

In summary, a genetic representation is the way in which a candidate solution is encoded in a genetic algorithm (GA), and determines how the solution is manipulated during the search process. Common genetic representations include binary representation, integer representation, real-valued representation, and permutation representation.
