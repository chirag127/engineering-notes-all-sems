### procedures of GA for the notes of the Unit 5 - Genetic Algorithm(GA) in the subject of APPLICATION OF SOFT COMPUTING TECHNIQUES

Genetic algorithms (GAs) are optimization algorithms based on the principles of evolution and natural selection. The basic procedure of a GA is as follows:

1. Initialization: a population of candidate solutions is randomly generated.

2. Evaluation: each candidate solution is evaluated based on a fitness function that measures its quality.

3. Selection: the best solutions are selected for reproduction based on their fitness.

4. Crossover: pairs of solutions are combined to produce offspring solutions.

5. Mutation: small random changes are introduced into the offspring solutions.

6. Repeat: the process is repeated, with the new offspring solutions forming the next generation of candidate solutions.

7. Termination: the process is terminated when a satisfactory solution is found or a stopping criterion is met.

In summary, the basic procedure of a genetic algorithm (GA) involves initializing a population of candidate solutions, evaluating each solution based on a fitness function, selecting the best solutions for reproduction, combining solutions to produce offspring, introducing small random changes, repeating the process, and terminating when a satisfactory solution is found or a stopping criterion is met.
