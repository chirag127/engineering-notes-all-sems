### working principle for the notes of the Unit 5 - Genetic Algorithm(GA) in the subject of APPLICATION OF SOFT COMPUTING TECHNIQUES

The working principle of a Genetic Algorithm (GA) is based on the principles of natural selection and genetics. It is a optimization algorithm that uses a population-based search to find the best solution to a problem.

The basic steps of a GA are as follows:
1. Initialization: a population of candidate solutions is generated randomly.
2. Evaluation: each candidate solution is evaluated based on a fitness function that measures its quality.
3. Selection: the best solutions are selected for reproduction, based on their fitness.
4. Crossover: new solutions are generated by combining the best solutions from the previous step.
5. Mutation: small random changes are introduced into the new solutions to increase diversity.
6. Repeat steps 2-5 until a satisfactory solution is found or a stopping criterion is met.

In summary, the working principle of a Genetic Algorithm (GA) is based on the principles of natural selection and genetics, and involves the steps of initialization, evaluation, selection, crossover, and mutation. The GA uses a population-based search to find the best solution to a problem, and repeats these steps until a satisfactory solution is found or a stopping criterion is met.
