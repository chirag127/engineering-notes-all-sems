### flow chart of GA for the notes of the Unit 5 - Genetic Algorithm(GA) in the subject of APPLICATION OF SOFT COMPUTING TECHNIQUES

A flow chart of a genetic algorithm (GA) is a graphical representation of the steps involved in the algorithm. The following is a general flow chart of a GA:

1. Initialization: A population of candidate solutions is randomly generated.

2. Evaluation: Each candidate solution is evaluated using a fitness function to determine its quality.

3. Selection: The best candidate solutions are selected for reproduction based on their fitness.

4. Crossover: The selected candidate solutions are combined to produce offspring.

5. Mutation: The offspring are subjected to random mutations to introduce diversity into the population.

6. Evaluation: The offspring are evaluated using the fitness function.

7. Replacement: The offspring are added to the population, and the worst candidate solutions are replaced.

8. Termination: The algorithm terminates when a satisfactory solution is found or a maximum number of generations is reached.

The flow chart of a GA is a high-level representation of the algorithm and may vary depending on the specific implementation of the GA. Unit 5 - Genetic Algorithm (GA) covers the theory and implementation of GAs, including the steps involved in the algorithm and the various techniques used for selection, crossover, and mutation.
