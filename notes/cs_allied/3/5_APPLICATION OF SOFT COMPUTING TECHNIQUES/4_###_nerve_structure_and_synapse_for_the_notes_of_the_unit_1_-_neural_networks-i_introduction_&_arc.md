### Nerve structure and synapse for the notes of the Unit 1 - Neural Networks-I (Introduction & Architecture) in the subject of APPLICATION OF SOFT COMPUTING TECHNIQUES

The nervous system is composed of nerves and neurons, which are specialized cells that transmit signals between different parts of the body. The structure of a nerve cell, or neuron, consists of a cell body, dendrites, and an axon. The cell body contains the nucleus and other organelles, while the dendrites receive signals from other neurons. The axon is a long, thin extension of the cell that transmits signals to other neurons or muscles.

A synapse is the junction between two neurons where signals are transmitted from one neuron to another. At a synapse, the axon of one neuron releases a chemical called a neurotransmitter, which diffuses across the synaptic cleft and binds to receptors on the dendrites of the next neuron. This binding triggers a change in the electrical potential of the dendrites, which leads to the initiation of a new nerve impulse in the next neuron.

In conclusion, the nervous system is composed of nerves and neurons, which are specialized cells that transmit signals between different parts of the body. The structure of a neuron consists of a cell body, dendrites, and an axon, while the synapse is the junction between two neurons where signals are transmitted. Understanding the structure and function of nerves and synapses is important for understanding how neural networks work and how they can be applied to solve various problems in the field of Soft Computing Techniques.
