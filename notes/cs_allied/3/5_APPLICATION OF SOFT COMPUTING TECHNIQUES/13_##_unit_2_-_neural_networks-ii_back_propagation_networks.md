## Unit 2 - Neural Networks-II (Back propagation networks)

Neural Networks-II (Back Propagation Networks) is a unit in the field of Artificial Neural Networks (ANNs) that focuses on the training of feedforward neural networks using the backpropagation algorithm.

Feedforward neural networks are a type of ANN that consists of an input layer, one or more hidden layers, and an output layer. The backpropagation algorithm is used to train these networks by adjusting the weights between the nodes in the network in order to minimize the error between the predicted outputs and the actual outputs. 

The backpropagation algorithm works by first calculating the error between the predicted outputs and the actual outputs, and then using this error to adjust the weights in the network. This is done by propagating the error backwards through the network, starting from the output layer and working backwards towards the input layer. The weights are adjusted in such a way as to minimize the error, and this process is repeated until the error reaches a minimum.

There are several variants of the backpropagation algorithm, including batch backpropagation, online backpropagation, and mini-batch backpropagation. The choice of which variant to use depends on the specific requirements of the application, and the size and complexity of the data being used for training.

Back Propagation Networks have been successfully applied in a wide range of applications, including pattern recognition, image classification, speech recognition, and control systems. They are widely used due to their ability to learn and generalize from examples, and their simplicity and ease of implementation.

In conclusion, Back Propagation Networks are an important unit in the field of Artificial Neural Networks, and focus on the training of feedforward neural networks using the backpropagation algorithm. The backpropagation algorithm is a powerful tool for training neural networks, and has been widely used in a wide range of applications due to its ability to learn and generalize from examples.
