### Genetic operators for the notes of the Unit 5 - Genetic Algorithm(GA) in the subject of APPLICATION OF SOFT COMPUTING TECHNIQUES

Genetic algorithms (GAs) are optimization algorithms based on the principles of natural selection and genetics. They use a set of genetic operators to manipulate the population of candidate solutions and evolve the population over multiple generations.

Unit 5 - Genetic Algorithm(GA) covers the following genetic operators:

1. Selection: Selection is the process of choosing the most fit solutions from the current population to be used for reproduction.

2. Crossover: Crossover is the process of combining two solutions to create new solutions. It is inspired by the process of sexual reproduction in biology.

3. Mutation: Mutation is the process of randomly changing the parameters of a solution. It is inspired by the process of genetic mutation in biology.

4. Elitism: Elitism is the process of preserving the best solutions from one generation to the next. This helps ensure that the best solutions are not lost during the evolution process.

5. Diversity preservation: Diversity preservation is the process of maintaining a diverse population of solutions. This helps ensure that the population does not converge to a single solution and that the GA can continue to evolve and find better solutions.

By using these genetic operators, GAs can efficiently explore the solution space and find optimal solutions to complex problems. Unit 5 - Genetic Algorithm(GA) covers the use of these genetic operators in GAs and provides a comprehensive understanding of how GAs work.
