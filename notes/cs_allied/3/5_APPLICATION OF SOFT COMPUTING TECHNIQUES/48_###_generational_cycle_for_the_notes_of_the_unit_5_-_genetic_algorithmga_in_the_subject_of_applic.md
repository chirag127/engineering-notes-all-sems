### Generational Cycle for the notes of the Unit 5 - Genetic Algorithm(GA) in the subject of APPLICATION OF SOFT COMPUTING TECHNIQUES

The Generational Cycle is a key component of the Genetic Algorithm (GA). The Generational Cycle refers to the repeated process of selection, crossover, and mutation that is performed in each generation of the GA. The Generational Cycle is repeated until a satisfactory solution is found or a stopping criteria is met.

The Generational Cycle consists of the following steps:

1. Selection: The best candidate solutions are selected for reproduction based on their fitness.

2. Crossover: The selected candidate solutions are combined to produce offspring, which are new candidate solutions.

3. Mutation: Random changes are introduced into the offspring to produce new candidate solutions.

4. Evaluation: The fitness of each candidate solution is evaluated using a fitness function.

5. Repeat: The process is repeated until a satisfactory solution is found or a stopping criteria is met.

The Generational Cycle is a key component of the GA, as it drives the evolution of the candidate solutions and allows the GA to search for better solutions over time. The choice of selection, crossover, and mutation methods, as well as the parameters used in these methods, can have a significant impact on the performance of the GA.

In conclusion, the Generational Cycle is a key component of the Genetic Algorithm (GA) and refers to the repeated process of selection, crossover, and mutation that is performed in each generation of the GA. The Generational Cycle is repeated until a satisfactory solution is found or a stopping criteria is met, and drives the evolution of the candidate solutions and allows the GA to search for better solutions over time.
