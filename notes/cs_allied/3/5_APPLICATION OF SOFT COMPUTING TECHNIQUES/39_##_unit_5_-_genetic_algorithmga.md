## Unit 5 - Genetic Algorithm(GA)

Genetic Algorithms (GA) are optimization algorithms inspired by the process of natural selection and evolution. They are used to solve optimization problems, such as finding the maximum or minimum value of a function, by simulating the process of natural selection and evolution.

GA consists of the following steps:

1. Initialization: The initial population of candidate solutions is generated randomly.

2. Evaluation: The fitness of each candidate solution is evaluated using a fitness function.

3. Selection: The best candidate solutions are selected for reproduction based on their fitness.

4. Crossover: The selected candidate solutions are combined to produce offspring, which are new candidate solutions.

5. Mutation: Random changes are introduced into the offspring to produce new candidate solutions.

6. Repeat: The process is repeated until a satisfactory solution is found or a stopping criteria is met.

GA can be used for a variety of optimization problems, including function optimization, constraint optimization, and combinatorial optimization. The performance of GA depends on several factors, including the size of the population, the selection method, the crossover method, and the mutation rate.
