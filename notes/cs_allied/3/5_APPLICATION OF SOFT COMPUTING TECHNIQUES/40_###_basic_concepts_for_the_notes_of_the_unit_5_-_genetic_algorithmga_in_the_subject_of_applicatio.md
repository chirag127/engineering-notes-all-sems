### Basic concepts for the notes of the Unit 5 - Genetic Algorithm(GA) in the subject of APPLICATION OF SOFT COMPUTING TECHNIQUES

Genetic algorithms (GAs) are optimization algorithms based on the principles of natural selection and genetics. They are used to find solutions to complex problems by evolving a population of candidate solutions over multiple generations.

Unit 5 - Genetic Algorithm(GA) covers the following basic concepts of GAs:

1. Chromosomes: A chromosome represents a candidate solution in a GA. It is a string of binary or real-valued numbers that encode the parameters of the solution.

2. Fitness function: A fitness function is a function that evaluates the quality of a candidate solution. The fitness function determines which solutions are more fit and are more likely to be selected for reproduction.

3. Selection: Selection is the process of selecting the most fit solutions from the current population to be used for reproduction.

4. Crossover: Crossover is the process of combining two solutions to create new solutions. It is inspired by the process of sexual reproduction in biology.

5. Mutation: Mutation is the process of randomly changing the parameters of a solution. It is inspired by the process of genetic mutation in biology.

6. Termination criterion: A termination criterion is a condition that determines when the GA should stop. This could be a maximum number of generations, a minimum fitness value, or some other condition.

GAs are a powerful optimization technique that can find solutions to complex problems in a wide range of fields, including engineering, finance, and biology. By following the basic concepts of GAs, organizations can use this technique to optimize their processes and make better decisions.
