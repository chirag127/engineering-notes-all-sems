### solution for the notes of the Unit 2 - Neural Networks-II (Back propagation networks) in the subject of APPLICATION OF SOFT COMPUTING TECHNIQUES

The solution for the Unit 2 - Neural Networks-II (Back Propagation Networks) in the subject of Application of Soft Computing Techniques is to use the backpropagation algorithm to train feedforward neural networks. The backpropagation algorithm is a supervised learning algorithm that is used to adjust the weights of the nodes in a feedforward neural network in order to minimize the error between the predicted outputs and the actual outputs.

The backpropagation algorithm works by first calculating the error between the predicted outputs and the actual outputs, and then using this error to adjust the weights in the network. This is done by propagating the error backwards through the network, starting from the output layer and working backwards towards the input layer. The weights are adjusted in such a way as to minimize the error, and this process is repeated until the error reaches a minimum.

There are several variants of the backpropagation algorithm, including batch backpropagation, online backpropagation, and mini-batch backpropagation. The choice of which variant to use depends on the specific requirements of the application, and the size and complexity of the data being used for training.

Backpropagation networks have been successfully applied in a wide range of applications, including pattern recognition, image classification, speech recognition, and control systems. They are widely used due to their ability to learn and generalize from examples, and their simplicity and ease of implementation.
