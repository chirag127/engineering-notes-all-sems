## Unit 1 - Neural Networks-I (Introduction & Architecture)

Neural networks are a type of machine learning model that are inspired by the structure and function of the human brain. They consist of layers of interconnected nodes, called neurons, which process and transmit information.

Unit 1 - Neural Networks-I covers the basics of neural networks, including their architecture and different types of layers. The first step in understanding neural networks is to understand the architecture, which refers to the structure and organization of the network.

A typical neural network consists of an input layer, one or more hidden layers, and an output layer. The input layer receives the input data and passes it to the hidden layers for processing. The hidden layers use activation functions to process the data and produce an output, which is then passed to the output layer. The output layer produces the final output of the network.

There are different types of layers in a neural network, including fully connected layers, convolutional layers, and recurrent layers. Each type of layer is designed to process specific types of data and perform specific types of operations.

Fully connected layers are the simplest type of layer and are used to process dense data, such as images and text. Convolutional layers are used to process image data and are designed to capture the spatial relationships between pixels. Recurrent layers are used to process sequential data, such as time series data and speech data.

In conclusion, Unit 1 - Neural Networks-I provides an introduction to the architecture and different types of layers in neural networks. Understanding these concepts is crucial for building and training effective neural networks.
