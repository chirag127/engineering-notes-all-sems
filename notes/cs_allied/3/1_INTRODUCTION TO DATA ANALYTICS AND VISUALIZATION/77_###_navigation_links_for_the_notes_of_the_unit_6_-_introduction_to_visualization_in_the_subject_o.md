### Navigation Links for the notes of the Unit 6 - Introduction to Visualization in the subject of INTRODUCTION TO DATA ANALYTICS AND VISUALIZATION

Navigation Links in Data Visualization:
- Navigation links are graphical elements used in data visualization to allow users to navigate and explore data.
- Navigation links can be used in a variety of formats, including:
  1. Hyperlinks: links that connect to other web pages or resources.
  2. Interactive visualizations: visualizations that allow users to interact with data by clicking, dragging, or hovering over elements.
  3. Drill-down visualizations: visualizations that allow users to explore data at different levels of detail by clicking on elements.
- Navigation links can be used to improve the usability and interactivity of data visualizations, making it easier for users to explore and understand data.
- Understanding navigation links is important for the design and development of effective data visualizations and for the creation of user-friendly and interactive data analytics applications.
