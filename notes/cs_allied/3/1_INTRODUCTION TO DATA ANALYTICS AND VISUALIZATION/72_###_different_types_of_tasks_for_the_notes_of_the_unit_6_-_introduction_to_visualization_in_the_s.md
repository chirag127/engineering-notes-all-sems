### Different Types of Tasks for the notes of the Unit 6 - Introduction to Visualization in the subject of INTRODUCTION TO DATA ANALYTICS AND VISUALIZATION

Different Types of Tasks in Data Visualization:
- Data visualization is the process of creating graphical representations of data to facilitate the analysis and interpretation of data.
- There are a variety of tasks that can be performed using data visualization, including:
  1. Exploration: the process of discovering patterns and relationships in data through visual inspection.
  2. Comparison: the process of comparing data across multiple dimensions or categories.
  3. Presentation: the process of presenting data to others in a clear and concise manner.
  4. Explanation: the process of explaining data and insights to others through visual representations.
- Different types of data visualization techniques are suitable for different types of tasks, and it is important to choose the appropriate technique for the task at hand.
- Understanding the different types of tasks in data visualization is important for the analysis and interpretation of data and for the development of effective data visualization solutions.
