Classification of Data in Data Analytics:
- Classification is a process of categorizing data into different groups or classes based on certain characteristics.
- Classification is a fundamental task in data analytics and is used in a variety of applications, including:
  1. Customer segmentation: dividing customers into different groups based on their characteristics and behaviors.
  2. Fraud detection: identifying fraudulent transactions based on patterns and anomalies in the data.
  3. Image classification: categorizing images into different classes based on their content.
- Classification algorithms can be supervised or unsupervised, depending on whether or not the algorithm has access to labeled data.
- Common classification algorithms include:
  1. Decision trees
  2. Naive Bayes
  3. Support Vector Machines (SVM)
  4. Neural networks
- Understanding classification is important for the analysis and interpretation of data and for the development of data-driven solutions to real-world problems.
