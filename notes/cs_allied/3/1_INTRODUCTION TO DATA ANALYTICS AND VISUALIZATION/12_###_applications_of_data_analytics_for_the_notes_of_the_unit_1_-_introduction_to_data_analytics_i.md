Unit 1: Introduction to Data Analytics

Applications of Data Analytics

Data Analytics is the process of analyzing and interpreting data to extract insights and information that can be used to make informed decisions. Data Analytics is used in a variety of applications, including business, healthcare, and government.

Business: In business, data analytics is used to analyze customer behavior, sales data, and market trends to make informed decisions about product development, marketing, and customer engagement.

Healthcare: In healthcare, data analytics is used to analyze patient data, medical records, and clinical trial data to improve patient outcomes and to develop new treatments.

Government: In government, data analytics is used to analyze data from a variety of sources, including census data, crime data, and economic data, to make informed decisions about policy and resource allocation.

In conclusion, Data Analytics is the process of analyzing and interpreting data to extract insights and information that can be used to make informed decisions.
