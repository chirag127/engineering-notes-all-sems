Mining Frequent Item Sets in Data Analytics:
- Frequent item sets are sets of items that appear frequently together in a data set.
- Mining frequent item sets is a process of identifying frequent item sets in a data set.
- Frequent item sets can be used for a variety of tasks, including market basket analysis, association rule mining, and recommendation systems.
- Frequent item sets can be mined using a variety of algorithms, including:
  1. Apriori algorithm
  2. FP-Growth algorithm
  3. Eclat algorithm
- Frequent item sets can be mined from transactional data, such as sales data or customer purchase data.
- Understanding frequent item sets and the mining of frequent item sets is important for the analysis and interpretation of data and for the development of data-driven solutions to real-world problems.
