### data preparation for the notes of the Unit 2 - Data Analytics Lifecycle in the subject of INTRODUCTION TO DATA ANALYTICS AND VISUALIZATION

Data Preparation in Data Analytics:
- Data preparation is the process of cleaning, transforming, and integrating data to make it ready for analysis.
- Data preparation is a critical step in the data analytics lifecycle, as the quality and accuracy of the results depend on the quality of the data.
- Data preparation can include the following tasks:
  1. Data cleaning: the process of removing errors, inconsistencies, and missing values from the data.
  2. Data transformation: the process of converting data into a format that is suitable for analysis.
  3. Data integration: the process of combining data from multiple sources into a single data set.
- Data preparation can be performed using a variety of tools and techniques, including data wrangling, data mapping, and data normalization.
- Understanding data preparation is important for the effective and efficient analysis of data and for the accuracy of the results.
