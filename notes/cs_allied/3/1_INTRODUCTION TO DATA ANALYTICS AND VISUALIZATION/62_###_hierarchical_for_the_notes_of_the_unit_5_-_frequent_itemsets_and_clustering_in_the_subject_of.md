Hierarchical Clustering in Data Analytics:
- Hierarchical clustering is a type of clustering algorithm that creates a hierarchical representation of data.
- Hierarchical clustering algorithms can be either agglomerative or divisive, depending on whether they start with individual data points and merge them into larger clusters or start with all data points and divide them into smaller clusters.
- Hierarchical clustering algorithms can be used for a variety of tasks, including:
  1. Customer segmentation: the grouping of customers into similar groups based on their characteristics.
  2. Image segmentation: the partitioning of an image into meaningful regions.
  3. Document clustering: the grouping of documents into similar categories based on their content.
- Hierarchical clustering algorithms can be used in combination with other techniques, such as dimension reduction and visualization, to improve the analysis and interpretation of data.
- Understanding hierarchical clustering is important for the analysis and interpretation of data and for the development of data-driven solutions to real-world problems.
