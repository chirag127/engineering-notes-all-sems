### Case studies for the notes of the Unit 4 - Mining Data Streams in the subject of INTRODUCTION TO DATA ANALYTICS AND VISUALIZATION

Case Studies in Mining Data Streams:
- Case studies are real-world examples of data analytics applications that can be used to illustrate the concepts and techniques covered in the subject "Introduction to Data Analytics and Visualization".
- Unit 4 of the subject focuses on mining data streams, which are continuous and rapidly changing data sources that require real-time processing and analysis.
- Case studies in mining data streams can include the following applications:
  1. Fraud detection: the identification of fraudulent transactions in real-time using data from multiple sources.
  2. Social network analysis: the analysis of social network data to identify patterns and relationships among users.
  3. Sensors and IoT: the analysis of data from sensors and Internet of Things (IoT) devices to monitor and control physical systems.
- Case studies provide a practical and hands-on approach to learning about data analytics and can help students to understand the real-world applications and challenges of data analytics.
- Understanding case studies is important for the development of data analytics skills and for the application of data analytics to real-world problems.
