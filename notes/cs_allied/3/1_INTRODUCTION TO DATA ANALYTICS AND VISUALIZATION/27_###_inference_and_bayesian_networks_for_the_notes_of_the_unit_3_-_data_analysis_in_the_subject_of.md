### inference and Bayesian networks for the notes of the Unit 3 - Data Analysis in the subject of INTRODUCTION TO DATA ANALYTICS AND VISUALIZATION

Inference and Bayesian Networks in Data Analytics:
- Inference is the process of drawing conclusions from data and knowledge.
- Bayesian networks are graphical models that represent probabilistic relationships between variables.
- Bayesian networks can be used for inference, as they provide a compact representation of probabilistic knowledge and can be used to perform probabilistic reasoning.
- Bayesian networks consist of nodes that represent variables and directed edges that represent probabilistic relationships between variables.
- Bayesian networks can be used for a variety of tasks, including:
  1. Causal inference: the determination of the causes and effects of variables.
  2. Predictive modeling: the prediction of future values based on past data.
  3. Diagnosis: the determination of the causes of a particular phenomenon.
- Understanding inference and Bayesian networks is important for the analysis and interpretation of data and for the development of probabilistic models for data analysis.
