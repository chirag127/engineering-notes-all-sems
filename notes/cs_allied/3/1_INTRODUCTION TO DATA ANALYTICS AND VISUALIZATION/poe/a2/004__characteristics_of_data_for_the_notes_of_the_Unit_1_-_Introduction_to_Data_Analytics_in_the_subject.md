 Here are the characteristics of data for the notes of Unit 1 - Introduction to Data Analytics:

### Volume
- The amount of data available for analysis. Data can be from a few megabytes to petabytes in size.
- Large volume of data allows deeper insights but also presents challenges in storage and processing.

### Velocity
- The speed at which data is generated and processed. Data can be static or streaming in real-time at high speed.
- High velocity requires algorithms and technology capable of processing data quickly while still being accurate.

### Variety
- The different types and formats of data. Data can be structured ( numerical, categorical), semi-structured (XML, JSON), or unstructured (text, images, videos).
- A variety of data requires using multiple techniques and tools to store, manage, and analyze the data.

### Veracity
- The accuracy, truthfulness, and reliability of the data. The degree to which the data can be trusted to be accurate and consistent.
- Data veracity is important to minimize bias or errors and derive meaningful insights. Techniques like data verification, cleaning, and uncertainty quantification can be used.

### Value
- The usefulness of the data and the insights that can be derived from it to achieve business goals. The value depends on the questions that can be answered and the decisions that can be made from the data.
- Determining the value of data and using it effectively is a key goal of data analytics. Subject matter expertise is important to understand the potential value of data.
