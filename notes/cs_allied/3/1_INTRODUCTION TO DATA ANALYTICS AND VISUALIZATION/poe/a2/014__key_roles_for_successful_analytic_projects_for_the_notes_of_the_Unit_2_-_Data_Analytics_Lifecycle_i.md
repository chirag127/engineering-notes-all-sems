 Here are the key roles for successful analytic projects:

### 1. Business Sponsor
- The business sponsor is the person who identifies the business problem or opportunity and funds the analytics project.
- They help define the business objectives and success metrics for the project.
- They are responsible for implementing the insights or recommendations from the project.

### 2. Data Owner
- The data owner is responsible for the data used in the analytics project.
- They ensure the data is accessible, accurate, and appropriate for the analytics objectives.
- They help interpret the data and understand data limitations or quality issues.

### 3. Analyst
- The analyst is responsible for the analytics work including identifying the analysis approach, acquiring and preparing the data, conducting the analysis, evaluating the results, and communicating insights and recommendations.
- Strong analytic and problem-solving skills are required.
- Knowledge of data analytics methods and tools is necessary.

### 4. Subject Matter Expert
- The subject matter expert provides domain knowledge to help define the problem, interpret the data, and evaluate the results and insights.
- They help determine whether the analysis makes sense and the recommendations are practical and impactful.
- Their knowledge is critical to a successful project.

The key roles work together as a team to ensure the analytics project is successful and the insights and recommendations lead to real business impact. Effective communication and collaboration across the roles are essential.
