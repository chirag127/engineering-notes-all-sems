### Analysis vs Reporting

- **Analysis:** The process of examining and interpreting data in order to draw conclusions or make inferences. Analysis involves breaking down data into smaller pieces to uncover patterns and insights. It seeks to answer 'why' and 'how'.
- **Reporting:** The presentation of analysis and findings. A report outlines key takeaways and insights from data analysis but does not include the deep examination and interpretation of the data that occurs during analysis. Reporting aims to inform and tell a story using data.

To summarize:

- Analysis digs deep into data to find insights and answers
- Reporting shares and presents insights and conclusions from analysis
- Analysis asks 'why' and 'how', reporting shares 'what'
- Both are important parts of working with and communicating about data
