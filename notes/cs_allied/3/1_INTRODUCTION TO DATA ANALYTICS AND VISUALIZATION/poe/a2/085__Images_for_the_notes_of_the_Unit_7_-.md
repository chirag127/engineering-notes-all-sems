### Images for the notes of the Unit 7 -

1. Images are two-dimensional representations of objects through recording of light or other electromagnetic radiation.
2. Images are recorded using optical lenses which may be the human eye or digital image sensors.
3. The characteristics of a photographic film or digital sensor determine response to color and intensity of light.
4. Color images use filters and additional color information to render a more accurate color reproduction of the scene.
5. Black and white images have varying tones of grey which correspond to varying light intensities.
6. Image format refers to the standardized encoding system for storing and transmitting the numeric representation of an image.
7. JPG or JPEG is a common format which supports lossy compression and produces images with varying levels of compression artifacts.
8. PNG is a lossless image format which produces larger file sizes but does not have compression artifacts.
9. Vector graphics use mathematical formulas to represent lines and shapes which can scale to any size without loss of quality.
10. Raster or bitmap images are composed of a grid of pixels which can become distorted or pixilated when scaling to larger sizes.
