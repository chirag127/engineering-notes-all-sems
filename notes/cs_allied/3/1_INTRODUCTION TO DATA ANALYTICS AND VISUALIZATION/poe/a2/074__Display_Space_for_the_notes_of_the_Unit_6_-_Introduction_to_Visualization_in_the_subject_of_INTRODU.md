### Display Space for the notes of the Unit 6 - Introduction to Visualization

1. Visualization is the graphical representation of information and data. It makes complex data more understandable, spot patterns and insights faster.
2. The key types of data visualization are:
- Charts: Bar chart, line chart, pie chart, etc. to show trends, comparisons, proportions, etc.
- Graphs: Node-link diagrams, hierarchy diagrams, flow diagrams, etc. to show relationships between entities.
- Maps: Thematic maps, dot density maps, choropleth maps, etc. to display geographical data.
- Infographics: Combines data visualizations, images, and text to visually represent information or knowledge.
3. The key steps in data visualization are:
- Understanding the data and requirements
- Choosing appropriate visualization types
- Designing the visualization
- Implementing the visualization
- Evaluating and refining the visualization
4. Some tools for data visualization are:
- Excel
- Tableau
- Power BI
- Datawrapper
- D3.js
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- etc.
