### Market Based Modelling

- Market based modelling is a technique to analyze customer data and find associations between products.
- It is used to identify products that are frequently bought together. These are known as frequent itemsets.
- Frequent itemsets are used to understand customer buying patterns and relationships between products.
- This can help in promotions, recommendations, personalized offerings, etc.
- Some of the key concepts in market basket analysis are:
-- Support: Percentage of transactions that contain a particular itemset.
-- Confidence: Probability that a transaction containing a particular itemset will also contain another item.
-- Lift: Ratio of confidence of two items appearing together to the individual supports of the items. A lift greater than 1 indicates a positive association.

The goal is to find all frequent itemsets that have support greater than a user-defined minimum support threshold. This can be done using the Apriori algorithm.
Once the frequent itemsets are found, clustering can be performed on the itemsets to group similar products or customers together. This provides further insights into the data.
