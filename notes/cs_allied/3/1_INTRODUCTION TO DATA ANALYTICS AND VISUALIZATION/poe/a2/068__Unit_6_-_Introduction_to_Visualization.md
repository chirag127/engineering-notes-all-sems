## Unit 6 - Introduction to Visualization

1. Data visualization refers to the presentation of data in a graphical or pictorial form. It makes data more understandable, spot patterns, and identify trends.
2. Some of the key benefits of data visualization are:
- Easy understanding: Visuals are easy to understand than raw data. People can more easily spot patterns and trends in data when presented visually.
- Universal language: Visuals act as a universal language and can be understood by people across geographies, languages, and cultures.
- Saves time: People can understand complex data or spot insights fast when presented visually rather than going through rows and columns of data.
- Improves communication: Visuals help to communicate information more effectively to both technical and non-technical stakeholders.
3. Some of the popular types of charts used for data visualization are:
- Line charts: To show trends over a period of time
- Bar charts: To compare different categories
- Pie charts: To show proportions of a whole
- Scatter plots: To show relationships between two variables
- Heatmaps: To show intensity of data in a 2D format
- Maps: To show geographical data

4. Data visualization tools: Some of the popular tools for data visualization are Tableau, Power BI, matplotlib, seaborn, etc. They provide easy drag and drop interfaces to create interactive and engaging data visualizations without coding.
