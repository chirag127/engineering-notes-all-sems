## Unit 5 - Frequent Itemsets and Clustering

1. Frequent Itemsets:
- Frequent itemsets refer to a set of items that appear together frequently in a transaction database.
- They are used to discover association rules between items.
- The support of an itemset is the percentage of transactions that contain the itemset. Frequent itemsets have support above a user-specified minimum support threshold.
- The Apriori algorithm is commonly used to mine frequent itemsets from transactional databases.

2. Clustering:
- Clustering refers to the unsupervised classification of patterns into groups.
- The goal is to group similar data points together and separate very different data points into different clusters.
- Some common clustering algorithms are:
-- K-Means: Cluster data by trying to separate samples into K clusters.
-- Hierarchical Clustering: Produce a tree-like dendrogram showing the clustering process.
-- DBSCAN: Cluster data based on density, discovering arbitrary shaped clusters.
- Clustering is often used for exploratory data analysis to find hidden patterns or grouping in data. It can be applied to datasets without labeled responses.
