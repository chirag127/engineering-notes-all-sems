### Limitations of Visualization

1. Cognitive overload: Visualization that includes too much information or complex graphics can confuse the user and make it difficult to understand the key insights. It is important to keep the visualizations simple and avoid clutter.
2. Loss of details: While visualizations help in providing a high-level overview, they may hide some details in the data that can be uncovered by analyzing raw data. Visualizations provide a selective view of the data and some aspects may get missed.
3. Misinterpretation: Visualizations are prone to misinterpretation or optical illusions. Colors, scales, and chart types can influence how information is perceived. It is important to choose appropriate visual encodings and scales to avoid misleading interpretations.
4. Preattentive attributes: While preattentive attributes help in fast perception of patterns, they have limitations too. The ability to perceive patterns is limited to a small set of visual properties like color, shape, orientation, etc. Other properties cannot be perceived preattentively.
5. Tool expertise: Visualization tools require expertise to use effectively. Not everyone will have the skills to choose the right visualization type or tweak it for optimum effectiveness. This can lead to suboptimal visualization designs if done by novices. Proper training is required to develop expertise in visualization.
