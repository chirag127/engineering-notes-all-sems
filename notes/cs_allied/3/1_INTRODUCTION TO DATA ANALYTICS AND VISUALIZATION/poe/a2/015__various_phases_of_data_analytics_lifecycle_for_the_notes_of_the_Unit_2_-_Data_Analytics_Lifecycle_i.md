### Various Phases of Data Analytics Lifecycle

1. Business Understanding: Understanding the business problem and framing the right questions to solve the problem. This phase involves discussion with stakeholders and domain experts to understand the key objectives and requirements.

2. Data Understanding: Understanding the data available which can be used to solve the business problem. This includes activities like data profiling, identifying data sources and quantifying variability in the data. The goal is to determine if the available data is sufficient and suitable to solve the problem.

3. Data Preparation: Preparing the data to be used in the modeling process. This phase includes activities like data cleaning, data integration, data transformation, etc. The goal is to get data in the required format and quality to be used in the modeling process.

4. Modeling: Applying various machine learning algorithms and techniques to train and develop models that can solve the business problem. This phase involves splitting data into training and testing sets, trying out different algorithms and models, evaluating and optimizing the performance of the models.

5. Evaluation: Evaluating the performance of the developed models and selecting the best models to deploy. Performance is evaluated based on metrics like accuracy, precision, recall, etc. The goal is to identify the most suitable models that can solve the business problem.

6. Deployment: Deploying the selected models and integrating into the business processes. The models are deployed to production and made accessible to stakeholders to solve the business problem. Monitoring and retraining cycles are set up to keep the models updated and performing optimally.
