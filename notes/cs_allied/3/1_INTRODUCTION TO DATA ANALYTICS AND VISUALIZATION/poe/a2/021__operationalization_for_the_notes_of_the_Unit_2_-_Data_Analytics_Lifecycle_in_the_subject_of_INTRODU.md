### Operationalization

1. Problem Framing - Clearly defining the problem and objectives. What question needs to be answered? What decisions need to be made?

2. Data Collection - Gathering relevant data to solve the problem. This could be from internal or external sources. Needs to be relevant, accurate, and bias-free.

3. Data Preparation - Cleaning and transforming the data into a suitable format for analysis. Fixing errors or missing values. Ensuring consistency and formatting the data appropriately.

4. Data Analysis - Applying analytical techniques to understand patterns, relationships, and insights in the data. This could include statistics, data mining, or machine learning.

5. Insights and Decisions - Interpreting the results and insights from the analysis and using them to make decisions or take actions to solve the initial problem.

6. Evaluate and Repeat - Evaluating the impact of decisions and repeating the lifecycle as needed with new data or issues. Continuously optimizing the process.
