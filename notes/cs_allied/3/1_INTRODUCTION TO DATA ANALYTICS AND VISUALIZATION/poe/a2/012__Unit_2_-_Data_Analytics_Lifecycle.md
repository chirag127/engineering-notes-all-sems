## Unit 2 - Data Analytics Lifecycle

1. Business Understanding
- Define the business problem and project objectives.
- Assess the situation and identify key questions to be answered.
- Determine metrics to evaluate relevance and accuracy of analysis.

2. Data Understanding
- Identify and evaluate relevant data sources.
- Understand data collection processes and quality.
- Examine properties and relationships within data.
- Propose methods to handle missing data, anomalies, etc.

3. Data Preparation
- Clean and prepare data for analysis.
- Handle missing values, outliers, etc.
- Merge, aggregate, transform data as needed.
- Finalize datasets for analysis.

4. Modeling
- Select an algorithm for analysis based on variables, data types, and outcomes.
- Train and evaluate the model.
- Tune model parameters to optimize performance.
- Compare multiple models to choose optimal one.

5. Evaluation
- Evaluate model performance against business objectives and metrics.
- Validate models and evaluate limitations, assumptions, and risks.
- Review results and determine next steps: retrain model, get additional data, etc.

6. Deployment
- Integrate model into production environment.
- Monitor model performance and retrain as needed.
- Review impact on key metrics and business goals.
