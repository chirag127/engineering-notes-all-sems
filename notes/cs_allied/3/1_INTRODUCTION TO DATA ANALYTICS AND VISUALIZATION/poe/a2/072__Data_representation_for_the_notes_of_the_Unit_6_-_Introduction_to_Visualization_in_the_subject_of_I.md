### Data representation for the notes of the Unit 6 - Introduction to Visualization in the subject of INTRODUCTION TO DATA ANALYTICS AND VISUALIZATION

1. Data visualization is the presentation of data in a pictorial or graphical format. It enables decision makers to see analytics presented visually, so they can grasp difficult concepts or identify new patterns.

2. There are three main ways to represent data visually:

- Charts: Charts convert the data into lines, bars, slices of pie, etc. to show comparisons or trends. Examples include line charts, bar charts, pie charts, etc.
- Infographics: Infographics are visual representations of information, data or knowledge. They present complex information quickly and clearly.
- Maps: Maps are visual representations of geographic data. They use elements like color, size, etc. to represent data tied to locations.

3. An effective data visualization should be:

- Clear: The visualization should be simple, minimal and uncluttered.
- Accurate: It should represent the data correctly without any distortion or exaggeration.
- Easy to understand: It should enable easy and quick understanding of the insights or patterns in the data.
- Interactive: It should allow the user to interact, explore and filter the data to uncover additional insights.
- Visually appealing: It should use elements like color, fonts, layout, etc. to make the insights stand out and grab attention.

4. The key steps to create a data visualization are:

1. Gather and understand the data
2. Determine the key insights and story
3. Choose an appropriate visualization type
4. Design the visualization
5. Evaluate and refine the visualization
