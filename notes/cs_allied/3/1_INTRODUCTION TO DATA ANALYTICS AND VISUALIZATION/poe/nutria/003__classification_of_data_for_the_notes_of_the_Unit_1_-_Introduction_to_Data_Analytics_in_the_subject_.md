
### Classification of Data

1. Nominal Data: Nominal data are used to identify objects or entities. They are qualitative in nature and can be represented by labels and categories. Examples of nominal data include gender, country, and product type.

2. Ordinal Data: Ordinal data are used to indicate the order of objects or entities. They are also qualitative in nature and can be represented by labels and categories. Examples of ordinal data include movie ratings, survey responses, and sports rankings.

3. Interval Data: Interval data are used to measure the magnitude of a quantity. They are quantitative in nature and can be represented by numbers. Examples of interval data include temperature, age, and distance.

4. Ratio Data: Ratio data are used to measure the magnitude of a quantity. They are quantitative in nature and can be represented by numbers. Examples of ratio data include weight, speed, and volume.
