
### Discovery for the Notes of the Unit 2 - Data Analytics Lifecycle in the Subject of INTRODUCTION TO DATA ANALYTICS AND VISUALIZATION

1. Data Discovery is the process of exploring and analyzing data to gain insights and identify patterns.
2. It is an iterative process that involves gathering data from multiple sources, exploring it, and analyzing it to uncover patterns and relationships.
3. Data Discovery can involve the use of various tools and techniques such as data mining, machine learning, and visualization.
4. It can be used to identify trends, anomalies, and correlations in data sets.
5. Data Discovery can also be used to identify potential areas of improvement in existing data sets.
6. The process of Data Discovery can be used to inform decisions and strategies in areas such as marketing, finance, and operations.
7. Data Discovery can also be used to identify potential opportunities for innovation and new product development.
