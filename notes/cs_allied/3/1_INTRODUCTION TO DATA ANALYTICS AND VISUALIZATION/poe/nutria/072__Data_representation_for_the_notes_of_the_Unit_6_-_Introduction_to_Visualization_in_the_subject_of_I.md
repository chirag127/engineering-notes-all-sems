
### Data Representation for the Notes of Unit 6 - Introduction to Visualization

1. Data visualization is the process of representing data in a graphical or pictorial form. It is used to make data easier to understand and interpret.

2. Data visualization can be used to represent data in a variety of ways, such as bar charts, line graphs, scatter plots, and pie charts.

3. Data visualization can also be used to represent data in a more complex form, such as heat maps, geographic maps, and network diagrams.

4. Data visualization can also be used to compare different sets of data and to identify patterns and trends in data.

5. Data visualization can be used to provide insights into data that would otherwise be difficult to interpret.

6. Data visualization can be used to communicate information more effectively, as it is easier to understand than raw data.

7. Data visualization can help to identify correlations between different variables, as well as to identify outliers in data.

8. Data visualization can also help to identify potential areas for further investigation or analysis.
