
### Analysis vs Reporting

Data analysis and reporting are two distinct but related activities in data analytics. 

* **Data Analysis** is the process of examining data sets in order to draw conclusions about the underlying information. It involves identifying patterns, trends, and correlations in the data in order to make decisions and predictions. 

* **Data Reporting** is the process of presenting the results of data analysis in a clear and concise manner. It involves summarizing the data, creating visualizations, and communicating the results in a way that is easy to understand and interpret.

Data analysis and reporting are essential components of data analytics and are used to inform decision-making and drive business strategy. By combining the two processes, organizations can gain valuable insights into their data and use these insights to make more informed decisions.
