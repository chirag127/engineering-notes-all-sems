
### Case Studies for the Notes of the Unit 4 - Mining Data Streams in the Subject of INTRODUCTION TO DATA ANALYTICS AND VISUALIZATION

1. **Twitter Sentiment Analysis**: This case study explores how to use data mining techniques to analyze the sentiment of tweets. By analyzing the sentiment of tweets, companies can gain valuable insight into how their customers feel about their products.

2. **Netflix Recommendation System**: This case study examines how Netflix uses data mining techniques to make movie recommendations to its users. Netflix's recommendation system uses data mining to identify patterns in user behavior and generate personalized movie recommendations.

3. **Social Network Analysis**: This case study looks at how data mining can be used to analyze social networks. By analyzing the structure and content of social networks, data miners can identify influential members of the network, detect groups and communities, and measure the spread of information.

4. **Fraud Detection**: This case study examines how data mining can be used to detect fraudulent transactions. By analyzing patterns in customer data, data miners can identify suspicious transactions and help reduce fraud.

5. **Customer Segmentation**: This case study looks at how data mining can be used to segment customers into different groups. By analyzing customer data, data miners can identify customer segments and target them with more relevant marketing messages.
