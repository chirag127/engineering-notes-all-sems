
### Rendering Time for the notes of the Unit 6 - Introduction to Visualization in the subject of INTRODUCTION TO DATA ANALYTICS AND VISUALIZATION

1. Rendering time is the amount of time it takes for a computer to produce a final image from a given set of instructions. 
2. Rendering time depends on a number of factors, including the complexity of the scene, the resolution of the output image, and the rendering software used. 
3. Rendering time can be reduced by optimizing the scene and the rendering settings, as well as by using more powerful hardware. 
4. For simple scenes, rendering time can be as short as a few seconds, while for complex scenes it can take hours or even days. 
5. To reduce rendering time, it is important to understand the different types of rendering, such as ray tracing and scanline rendering, and to use the most appropriate technique for the scene. 
6. It is also important to use efficient data structures and algorithms to reduce the time it takes to generate the final image.
