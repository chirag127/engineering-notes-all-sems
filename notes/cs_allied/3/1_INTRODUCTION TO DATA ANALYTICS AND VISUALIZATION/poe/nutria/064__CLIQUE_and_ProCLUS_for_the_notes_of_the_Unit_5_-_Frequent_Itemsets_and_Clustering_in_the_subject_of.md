
### CLIQUE and ProCLUS

* CLIQUE is an algorithm used to discover clusters of objects in a dataset. It works by finding dense regions of data points that are connected to each other. CLIQUE can be used to identify clusters of similar items in a dataset, such as customers with similar buying habits, or items that are frequently purchased together.

* Proclus is an algorithm used to determine the most frequent itemsets in a dataset. It works by finding the most frequently occurring itemsets in a dataset, such as items that are often purchased together. Proclus can be used to identify clusters of similar items in a dataset, such as customers who often purchase similar items. It can also be used to identify items that are frequently purchased together.
