Clustering is a powerful data analysis technique used to identify similar groups of data points in a dataset. * Clustering Data Streams: Data streams are data points that are continuously being generated and collected. Clustering data streams can help identify patterns and trends in the data. Clustering data streams can also help identify outliers or anomalies in the data.

* Clustering Large Datasets: Clustering large datasets can help identify patterns and trends in the data. Clustering large datasets can also help identify outliers or anomalies in the data. Clustering large datasets can also help identify data points that are similar to each other.
