
### Market Based Modelling for Unit 5 - Frequent Itemsets and Clustering

* Market-based modelling is a type of data analysis that uses market-related data to identify trends, patterns, and correlations.
* Market-based modelling can be used to identify customer preferences, predict future sales, and optimize marketing campaigns.
* Frequent itemsets are sets of items that occur frequently in a given dataset. These itemsets can be used to identify patterns and relationships in data.
* Clustering is a type of unsupervised learning technique that groups similar data points together. It is often used to segment customers into different groups based on their preferences and behaviors.
* Market-based modelling can be used to identify customer segments and target them with specific marketing campaigns.
* Market-based modelling can also be used to identify correlations between products and customer preferences, which can be used to optimize product offerings and pricing.
