
### Evolution of Analytic Scalability

1. Data Analytics is a process of collecting, organizing, analyzing, and interpreting data to gain insights for decision-making.
2. Analytic scalability is the ability to scale an analytics workload to process large amounts of data.
3. There are two main approaches to analytic scalability: distributed computing and parallel computing.
4. Distributed computing involves breaking down the data into smaller chunks and processing them on multiple computers or servers.
5. Parallel computing involves running multiple processes on the same computer or server at the same time.
6. Both approaches have their advantages and disadvantages, and the best approach will depend on the specific data analytics task.
7. In recent years, cloud computing has become a popular option for analytic scalability as it allows for easy scalability of computing resources.
8. As technology continues to evolve, the options for analytic scalability will continue to grow, allowing for more efficient and effective data analytics.
