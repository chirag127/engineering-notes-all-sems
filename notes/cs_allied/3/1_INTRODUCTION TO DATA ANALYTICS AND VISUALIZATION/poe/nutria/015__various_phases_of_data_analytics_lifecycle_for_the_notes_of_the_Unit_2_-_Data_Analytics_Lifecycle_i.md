
### Various Phases of Data Analytics Lifecycle
1. Data Collection: This is the first and most important step in the data analytics lifecycle. It involves gathering data from various sources such as databases, websites, surveys, and more. The data must be collected in a structured format to ensure accuracy and consistency.

2. Data Cleaning: This is the process of cleaning and organizing the collected data. It includes removing any duplicates, correcting errors, and filling in missing information.

3. Data Analysis: This is the process of analyzing the collected data. It involves the use of various techniques such as statistical analysis, machine learning, and data mining to uncover patterns and insights.

4. Data Visualization: This is the process of creating visual representations of the data. It is used to make the data easier to understand and interpret.

5. Data Reporting: This is the process of presenting the results of the data analysis in a meaningful way. It includes creating charts, graphs, and other visuals to help make the data easier to comprehend.
