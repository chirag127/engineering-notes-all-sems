
### Bayesian Modeling

Bayesian modeling is a statistical approach to data analysis that uses Bayesian inference to make predictions based on observed data. In Bayesian modeling, a prior distribution is used to represent prior knowledge about the parameters of a model and the data is used to update the prior distribution to a posterior distribution.

Bayesian modeling is a powerful tool for data analysis, as it allows for complex models to be constructed and updated quickly, while also providing a measure of uncertainty in the model's predictions.

Bayesian modeling is commonly used in the field of data analysis and visualization, as it provides a means of understanding the relationships between variables in a dataset and making predictions about future data points.

In the Unit 3 - Data Analysis in the subject of INTRODUCTION TO DATA ANALYTICS AND VISUALIZATION, Bayesian modeling is used to analyze and visualize data. Bayesian modeling can be used to:

- Estimate the probability of a certain outcome based on observed data
- Make predictions about future data points
- Identify patterns and relationships between variables
- Create models that can be used to make predictions
- Evaluate the performance of models
- Make decisions based on data
