
### Linear Systems Analysis & Nonlinear Dynamics

1. Linear Systems Analysis is the study of linear differential equations and their solutions. It is used to model and analyze physical, biological and financial systems. 
2. Nonlinear Dynamics is the study of nonlinear differential equations and their solutions. It is used to study complex systems such as chaotic systems, and can be used to predict the behavior of such systems. 
3. Data Analysis is the process of collecting, cleaning, transforming and analyzing data in order to gain insights and make decisions. 
4. Data Visualization is the process of transforming data into graphical representations such as charts, graphs, and maps in order to gain insights and make decisions. 
5. Machine Learning is the process of using algorithms to analyze data and make predictions. It can be used to identify patterns in data and make decisions.
