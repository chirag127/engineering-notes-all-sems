
### Characteristics of Data

1. **Accuracy**: Data should be accurate, up-to-date, and represent the real-world situation.
2. **Completeness**: All relevant data should be present and accounted for.
3. **Relevance**: Data should be relevant to the task or decision at hand.
4. **Timeliness**: Data should be available when needed.
5. **Accessibility**: Data should be easy to access and use.
6. **Consistency**: Data should be consistent in terms of format, units, and definitions.
7. **Integrity**: Data should be free from errors, duplicates, and inconsistencies.
