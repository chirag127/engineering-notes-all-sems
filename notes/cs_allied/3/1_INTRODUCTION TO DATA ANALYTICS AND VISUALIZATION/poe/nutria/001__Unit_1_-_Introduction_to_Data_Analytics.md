
## Unit 1 - Introduction to Data Analytics

1. Data analytics is the process of examining data sets in order to draw conclusions about the information they contain, increasingly with the aid of specialized systems and software.
2. Data analytics technologies and techniques are widely used in commercial industries to enable organizations to make more-informed business decisions and by scientists and researchers to verify or disprove scientific models, theories and hypotheses.
3. Data analytics has many different facets and approaches, including data mining, text analytics, predictive analytics, machine learning, and artificial intelligence.
4. Data analytics is used to identify patterns and trends in data in order to make better decisions and predictions.
5. Data analytics can be used to improve customer experience, optimize business processes, create new products and services, and gain a competitive advantage.
6. Data analytics can also be used to identify potential risks and opportunities and to monitor performance.
