
### Issues for the notes of the Unit 6 - Introduction to Visualization in the subject of INTRODUCTION TO DATA ANALYTICS AND VISUALIZATION

1. Inaccurate or incomplete data: Data used for visualization can be inaccurate or incomplete, resulting in misleading conclusions and inaccurate visualizations.

2. Over-simplification: Visualizations can simplify complex data sets and relationships, leading to misinterpretation and misunderstanding of the underlying data.

3. Too much information: Too much data can lead to confusion and overwhelm. It is important to find the right balance between visualizing too much and too little data.

4. Unclear labels: Poorly labeled axes, legends, and other elements can lead to confusion and misinterpretation.

5. Poor color choice: Choosing the wrong colors for a visualization can make it difficult to interpret the data.

6. Poor design: Poor design can lead to cluttered and confusing visualizations.

7. Unclear purpose: Visualizations should have a clear purpose and goal. Without this, it can be difficult to interpret the data.
