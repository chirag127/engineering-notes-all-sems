

### Introduction to Streams Concepts

1. Streams are continuous, unbounded data flows that are generated from various sources.
2. Streams can be classified into two types: static and dynamic. Static streams are generated from a single source, while dynamic streams are generated from multiple sources.
3. Streams are typically processed in real-time, meaning that the data is processed as it is generated.
4. Streams can be processed using a variety of algorithms, such as filtering, clustering, and classification.
5. Streams can be used for a variety of applications, such as monitoring, analysis, and prediction.
6. Stream processing can be used to identify patterns in the data and make predictions about future events.
7. The ability to process streams in real-time enables organizations to respond quickly to changes in the environment.
8. Stream processing also enables organizations to gain insights into the data that would otherwise be difficult to obtain.
