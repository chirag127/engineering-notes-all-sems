
### Sources and Nature of Data for Unit 1 - Introduction to Data Analytics

Data is the foundation of any data analytics project. Data can come from a variety of sources, including databases, surveys, interviews, and online sources.

1. **Databases**: Databases are a common source of data for data analytics projects. They are organized collections of data that are stored in a structured format. Examples of databases include relational databases, NoSQL databases, and graph databases.

2. **Surveys**: Surveys are a great source of data for data analytics projects. They provide a way to collect data from a large population in a short amount of time. Surveys can be conducted online, in-person, or by phone.

3. **Interviews**: Interviews are another common source of data for data analytics projects. They are typically conducted with a small number of individuals and provide a more in-depth understanding of the data.

4. **Online Sources**: Online sources are a great way to collect data for data analytics projects. Examples of online sources include websites, forums, and social media.
