### Different Types of Tasks

Visualization is an important aspect of data analytics that helps in understanding complex data patterns and relationships. There are different types of tasks that can be performed using visualization techniques. Some of the common tasks are:

1. **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) Visualization:** EDA visualization is used to explore the data and identify patterns, trends, and outliers. It helps in identifying missing values, data inconsistencies, and other anomalies in the data.

2. **Data Comparison Visualization:** Data comparison visualization is used to compare two or more datasets to identify similarities and differences. It is useful in identifying patterns, trends, and outliers that are common or unique to each dataset.

3. **Data Distribution Visualization:** Data distribution visualization is used to visualize the distribution of data across one or more dimensions. It helps in identifying the central tendency, dispersion, and skewness of the data.

4. **Correlation Visualization:** Correlation visualization is used to visualize the relationships between two or more variables. It helps in identifying the strength and direction of the relationship between variables.

5. **Geospatial Visualization:** Geospatial visualization is used to visualize data on a map. It helps in identifying patterns and trends in data that are related to geography.

6. **Temporal Visualization:** Temporal visualization is used to visualize data over time. It helps in identifying patterns and trends in data that are related to time.

Visualization is an important tool for data analysts and data scientists. It helps in identifying patterns, trends, and outliers in data that are not easily visible through other means. By using different types of visualization techniques, analysts can gain insights into complex data patterns and relationships, and make more informed decisions based on the data.
