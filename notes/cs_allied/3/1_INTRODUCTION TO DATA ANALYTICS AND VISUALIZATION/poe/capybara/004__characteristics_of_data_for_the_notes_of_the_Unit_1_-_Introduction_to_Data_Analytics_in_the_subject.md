### Characteristics of Data

Data is the foundation of data analytics. The following are the characteristics of data that you should be familiar with:

- **Accuracy**: Data should be accurate and free from errors. Inaccurate data can lead to incorrect analysis and results.

- **Completeness**: Data should be complete and contain all the required information. Missing data can lead to gaps in analysis and results.

- **Consistency**: Data should be consistent and follow the same format throughout. Inconsistent data can lead to confusion and errors in analysis.

- **Timeliness**: Data should be timely and up-to-date. Stale data can lead to incorrect analysis and results.

- **Relevance**: Data should be relevant to the analysis being performed. Irrelevant data can lead to incorrect analysis and results.

- **Validity**: Data should be valid and relevant to the analysis being performed. Invalid data can lead to incorrect analysis and results.

- **Uniqueness**: Data should be unique and not duplicated. Duplicated data can skew analysis and results.

- **Granularity**: Data should be at the appropriate level of detail for the analysis being performed. Granular data can lead to more accurate analysis and results.

- **Accessibility**: Data should be accessible and easy to obtain. Inaccessible data can hinder analysis and results.

- **Security**: Data should be secured and protected from unauthorized access. Unsecured data can lead to data breaches and compromised analysis.

Understanding the characteristics of data is crucial to performing accurate and meaningful data analytics.
