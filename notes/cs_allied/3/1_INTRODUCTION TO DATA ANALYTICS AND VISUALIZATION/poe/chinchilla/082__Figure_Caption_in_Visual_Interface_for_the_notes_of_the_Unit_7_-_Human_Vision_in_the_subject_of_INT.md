### Figure Caption in Visual Interface for the notes of the Unit 7 - Human Vision in the subject of INTRODUCTION TO DATA ANALYTICS AND VISUALIZATION

In data analytics and visualization, it is important to present information in a clear and concise manner. One way to do this is by including figure captions in visual interfaces. Here are some key points to keep in mind when creating figure captions:

- A figure caption is a brief description that accompanies a visual representation, such as a graph or chart.
- The caption should provide a clear and concise summary of the information presented in the visual representation.
- It should also include any relevant context or background information that helps the viewer understand the data being presented.
- The caption should be placed directly below the visual representation, and should be clearly labeled with a figure number.
- When creating a figure caption, it is important to use language that is easy to understand and avoids technical jargon as much as possible.
- The caption should also be written in a formal style, with proper grammar and punctuation.
- Avoid using emojis or other non-text elements in figure captions, as they can be distracting and may not be easily understood by all viewers.
- Finally, it is important to ensure that any external links or references are omitted from the figure caption, as they can be misleading or confusing to viewers.

In summary, figure captions are an important component of visual interfaces in data analytics and visualization. By following these guidelines and creating clear, concise, and informative captions, viewers can more easily understand and interpret the data being presented.
