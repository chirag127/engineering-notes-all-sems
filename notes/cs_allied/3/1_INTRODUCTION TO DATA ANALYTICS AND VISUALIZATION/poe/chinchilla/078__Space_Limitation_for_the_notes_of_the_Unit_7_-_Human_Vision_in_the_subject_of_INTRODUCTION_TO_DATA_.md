### Space Limitation for the notes of the Unit 7 - Human Vision in the subject of INTRODUCTION TO DATA ANALYTICS AND VISUALIZATION

When taking notes for the Unit 7 - Human Vision in the subject of Introduction to Data Analytics and Visualization, it is essential to keep in mind the space limitation. This is because taking extensive notes can make it difficult to review them later on. Therefore, it is crucial to take concise and precise notes that capture the essential information. Here are some tips to help you take effective notes within space limitations:

1. Use abbreviations: When taking notes, using abbreviations can help you save space. However, ensure that the abbreviations are clear and easy to understand. For instance, use "w/" for "with" and "w/o" for "without".

2. Use symbols: Symbols can also be used to represent words or phrases. For example, use an arrow "->" to represent "leads to" or "causes".

3. Highlight key points: Highlighting key points can help you identify important information quickly. Use highlighters or underline key phrases or words to make them stand out.

4. Use mind maps: Mind maps are a useful tool for capturing information in a concise and organized way. They allow you to link ideas and concepts, helping you to see the bigger picture. You can use software or draw them by hand.

5. Summarize information: Summarizing information can help you save space and capture the main ideas. For example, write a brief summary of a paragraph or section in your own words.

6. Use bullet points: Bullet points are an excellent way to organize information into short, concise sections. They help to break up the text and make it easier to read.

7. Use headings and subheadings: Headings and subheadings help to organize your notes and make it easier to find specific information. Use them to group related sections or topics.

In conclusion, taking notes for the Unit 7 - Human Vision in the subject of Introduction to Data Analytics and Visualization requires a balance between capturing essential information and space limitation. Using the tips listed above can help you take effective notes that are concise and easy to review.
