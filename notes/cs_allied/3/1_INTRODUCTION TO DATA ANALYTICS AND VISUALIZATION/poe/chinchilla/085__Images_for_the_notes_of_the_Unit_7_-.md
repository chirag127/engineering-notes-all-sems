### Images for the notes of the Unit 7 -

When taking notes for Unit 7, it is important to include relevant images to aid in your understanding of the subject matter. Here are some tips for incorporating images into your notes:

1. Use diagrams and charts: Visual aids such as diagrams and charts can help you better understand complex concepts. For example, if you are studying the human body, including a diagram of the circulatory system can help you understand how blood flows through the body.

2. Include photographs: Photographs can be useful in providing real-life examples of the subject matter. For example, if you are studying a historical event, including a photograph of the event can help you better understand the context and the impact it had.

3. Use symbols and icons: Symbols and icons can be used to represent ideas and concepts. For example, using a lightbulb icon to represent an idea or a dollar sign to represent money can help you quickly understand the meaning of the information.

4. Draw your own visuals: If you are a visual learner, drawing your own diagrams and charts can be a helpful way to better understand the material. This can also help you remember the information more effectively.

5. Use color: Color can be used to highlight important information or to differentiate between different ideas. For example, using red to highlight a key point or green to represent a positive outcome can help you better understand the information.

Incorporating images into your notes can be a powerful way to enhance your understanding of the material. By considering the different types of visuals that can be used, you can create a visual language that is unique to your learning style and helps you retain the information more effectively.
