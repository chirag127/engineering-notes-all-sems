### Sources and nature of data for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Data Analytics in the subject of INTRODUCTION TO DATA ANALYTICS AND VISUALIZATION

In this unit, we will be discussing the sources and nature of data that are used for data analytics. Data analytics is the process of examining large and complex data sets in order to draw conclusions about the information they contain. It is important to understand where the data comes from and how it is collected in order to properly analyze it. Here are some key points to keep in mind:

- **Data sources**: There are many different sources of data that can be used for data analytics. Some examples include:

  - **Internal data**: Data that is generated within an organization, such as sales data, customer data, or employee data.
  
  - **External data**: Data that is collected from sources outside of an organization, such as government data, social media data, or market research data.
  
  - **Sensor data**: Data that is generated by sensors, such as temperature data or GPS data.
  
  - **Web data**: Data that is collected from websites, such as web traffic data or user behavior data.
  
  - **Machine-generated data**: Data that is generated by machines, such as log files or sensor data.
  
- **Data nature**: The nature of the data that is collected can vary widely. Some important factors to consider include:

  - **Data type**: Data can be structured or unstructured, and can include text, numerical data, images, or video.
  
  - **Data quality**: The quality of the data can vary widely, and it is important to ensure that the data is accurate and reliable before using it for analysis.
  
  - **Data volume**: The volume of data that is collected can be very large, and it is important to have the right tools and techniques in place to process and analyze it effectively.
  
- **Data collection methods**: There are many different methods that can be used to collect data, including:

  - **Surveys**: Surveys can be used to collect data from a large number of people, and can be conducted in person, over the phone, or online.
  
  - **Focus groups**: Focus groups can be used to collect qualitative data from a small group of people, and are often used to gather feedback on a specific topic.
  
  - **Observational studies**: Observational studies involve observing people or events in their natural environment, and can be used to gather data on behavior or patterns.
  
  - **Experiments**: Experiments involve manipulating variables in order to observe the effect on an outcome, and can be used to test hypotheses.
  
- **Data preparation**: Before data can be analyzed, it must be cleaned and prepared. This involves tasks such as removing duplicates, filling in missing values, and transforming data into a format that can be analyzed.

Understanding the sources and nature of data is an important first step in data analytics. By understanding where the data comes from and how it is collected, you can ensure that your analysis is accurate and reliable.
