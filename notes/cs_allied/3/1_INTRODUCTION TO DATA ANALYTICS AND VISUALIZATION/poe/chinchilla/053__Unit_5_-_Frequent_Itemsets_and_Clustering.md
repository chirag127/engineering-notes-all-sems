## Unit 5 - Frequent Itemsets and Clustering

In this unit, we will cover the following topics related to frequent itemsets and clustering:

1. Introduction to frequent itemsets:
   - Defining frequent itemsets
   - Support and confidence measures
   - Identifying frequent itemsets using the Apriori algorithm

2. Association rule mining:
   - Defining association rules
   - Confidence and lift measures
   - Discovering interesting association rules

3. Introduction to clustering:
   - Defining clustering
   - Types of clustering algorithms
   - Distance measures and similarity measures

4. Hierarchical clustering:
   - Defining hierarchical clustering
   - Agglomerative and divisive clustering
   - Single linkage, complete linkage, and average linkage

5. Partitioning clustering:
   - Defining partitioning clustering
   - K-means and K-medoids algorithms
   - Advantages and disadvantages of partitioning clustering

6. Density-based clustering:
   - Defining density-based clustering
   - DBSCAN algorithm
   - Advantages and disadvantages of density-based clustering

By the end of this unit, you will have a good understanding of frequent itemsets and clustering algorithms. You will be able to identify frequent itemsets using the Apriori algorithm, discover interesting association rules, and apply different clustering algorithms to different types of data.
