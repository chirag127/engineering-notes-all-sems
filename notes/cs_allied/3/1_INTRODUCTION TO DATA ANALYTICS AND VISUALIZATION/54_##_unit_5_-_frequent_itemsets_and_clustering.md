Introduction to Frequent Itemsets and Clustering

Market basket analysis is used to understand customer behavior, and it is used to inform marketing and sales strategies. Frequent itemsets are typically implemented using algorithms such as Apriori and FP-Growth.

Clustering is used in a variety of applications, including customer segmentation, image analysis, and document classification.
