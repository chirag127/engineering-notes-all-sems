## Unit 7 - Human Vision

Unit 7 - Human Vision in Introduction to Data Analytics and Visualization:
- Unit 7 focuses on the study of human vision and its role in data visualization.
- Human vision is a complex process that involves the eyes, the brain, and the perception of visual information.
- Understanding human vision is important for the design and development of effective data visualizations that are easily understood by human users.
- Topics covered in Unit 7 may include:
  1. Visual perception: the process by which the brain interprets visual information.
  2. Color theory: the study of color and its use in data visualization.
  3. Visual encoding: the process of representing data in visual form.
  4. Visualization design principles: guidelines for effective data visualization design.
- Unit 7 may also cover the use of tools and technologies for data visualization, such as Tableau, D3.js, and ggplot2.
- Understanding human vision and its role in data visualization is important for the development of effective data analytics solutions that are easily understood and used by human users.
