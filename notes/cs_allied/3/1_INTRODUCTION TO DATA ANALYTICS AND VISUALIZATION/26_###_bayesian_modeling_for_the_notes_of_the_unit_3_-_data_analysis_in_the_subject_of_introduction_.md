### Bayesian modeling for the notes of the Unit 3 - Data Analysis in the subject of INTRODUCTION TO DATA ANALYTICS AND VISUALIZATION

Bayesian modeling is a statistical method used to model uncertainty in data analysis.

1. Definition: Bayesian modeling is a statistical method used to model uncertainty in data analysis. Bayesian modeling is based on Bayes' theorem, which is used to update the probability of an event based on new data.

2. Key Components: The key components of Bayesian modeling include the prior probability, the likelihood, and the posterior probability. The prior probability is the probability of an event before new data is collected, the likelihood is the probability of the data given the event, and the posterior probability is the probability of the event given the data.

3. Prior Probability: The prior probability is the probability of an event before new data is collected. The prior probability is used to represent prior knowledge or beliefs about the event.

4. Likelihood: The likelihood is the probability of the data given the event. The likelihood is used to represent the evidence for the event.

5. Posterior Probability: The posterior probability is the probability of the event given the data. The posterior probability is used to update the prior probability based on the new data.

6. Advantages: Bayesian modeling has several advantages, including the ability to incorporate prior knowledge or beliefs into the analysis, the ability to model uncertainty, and the ability to update the model based on new data.

7. Limitations: Despite its advantages, Bayesian modeling also has several limitations, including the difficulty of choosing the appropriate prior probability, the difficulty of ensuring the accuracy of the model, and the difficulty of ensuring the scalability of the model.

8. Applications: Bayesian modeling is used in a wide range of applications in data analysis, including model selection, parameter estimation, and hypothesis testing. Bayesian modeling is also used in fields such as machine learning, statistics, and engineering.

In conclusion, Bayesian modeling is a statistical method used to model uncertainty in data analysis. Bayesian modeling is based on Bayes' theorem and uses the prior probability, the likelihood, and the posterior probability to model uncertainty. Bayesian modeling is used in a wide range of applications in data analysis and is an important tool for modeling uncertainty in data analysis.
