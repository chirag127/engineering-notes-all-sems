### analysis of time series for the notes of the Unit 3 - Data Analysis in the subject of INTRODUCTION TO DATA ANALYTICS AND VISUALIZATION

Analysis of Time Series in Data Analytics:
- Time series analysis is a method of analyzing data that is collected over time.
- Time series analysis is used in a variety of applications, including financial analysis, trend analysis, and forecasting.
- Time series analysis can include the following tasks:
  1. Time series decomposition: the process of breaking down a time series into its components, such as trend, seasonality, and residuals.
  2. Time series smoothing: the process of removing noise from a time series to reveal underlying patterns.
  3. Time series forecasting: the process of making predictions about future values of a time series based on past data.
- Time series analysis can be performed using a variety of techniques, including moving averages, exponential smoothing, and ARIMA models.
- Understanding time series analysis is important for the analysis and interpretation of data collected over time and for the development of data-driven solutions to real-world problems.
