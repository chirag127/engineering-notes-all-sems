### key roles for successful analytic projects for the notes of the Unit 2 - Data Analytics Lifecycle in the subject of INTRODUCTION TO DATA ANALYTICS AND VISUALIZATION

Successful analytic projects require several key roles to ensure that the project is completed effectively and efficiently.

1. Project Manager: The project manager is responsible for overseeing the entire project, including defining the project scope, developing a project plan, and ensuring that the project is completed on time and within budget.

2. Data Analyst: The data analyst is responsible for collecting, cleaning, and transforming data, as well as performing exploratory data analysis and creating visualizations to communicate insights.

3. Business Subject Matter Expert: The business subject matter expert is responsible for providing domain-specific knowledge and understanding of the business problem that the project is trying to solve.

4. Data Engineer: The data engineer is responsible for designing and implementing the data infrastructure, including data storage, data processing, and data retrieval systems.

5. Data Scientist: The data scientist is responsible for developing and implementing advanced analytics models, including machine learning and statistical models, to extract insights from data.

6. Visualization Specialist: The visualization specialist is responsible for creating visualizations to communicate insights and findings to stakeholders.

7. Stakeholder: Stakeholders are individuals or groups who have a vested interest in the project and its outcome. Stakeholders can include business users, executives, and end-users.

8. Advantages: Having these key roles in place has several advantages, including the ability to effectively manage the project, the ability to effectively collect, process, and analyze data, and the ability to effectively communicate insights and findings to stakeholders.

9. Limitations: Despite its advantages, having these key roles in place also has several limitations, including the difficulty of ensuring that all roles are filled with qualified individuals, the difficulty of ensuring that all roles are aligned with the project goals, and the difficulty of ensuring that all roles are working together effectively.

10. Applications: These key roles are used in a wide range of data analytics projects, including business intelligence, customer analytics, and financial analytics.

In conclusion, successful analytic projects require several key roles, including a project manager, data analyst, business subject matter expert, data engineer, data scientist, visualization specialist, and stakeholders. Having these key roles in place has several advantages, including the ability to effectively manage the project, collect, process, and analyze data, and communicate insights and findings to stakeholders.
