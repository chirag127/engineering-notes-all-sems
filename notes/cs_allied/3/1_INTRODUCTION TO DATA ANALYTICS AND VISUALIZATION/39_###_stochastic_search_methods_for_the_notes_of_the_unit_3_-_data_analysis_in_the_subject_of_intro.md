### stochastic search methods for the notes of the Unit 3 - Data Analysis in the subject of INTRODUCTION TO DATA ANALYTICS AND VISUALIZATION

Stochastic search methods are optimization techniques used to find the global minimum or maximum of a function by randomly sampling the search space. They are used in a variety of applications, including data analysis, machine learning, and engineering.

1. Random Search: A simple method that generates random points in the search space and selects the best point based on the function value.

2. Simulated Annealing: A method that uses a probabilistic approach to escape from local minima and find the global minimum.

3. Particle Swarm Optimization: A population-based method that simulates the behavior of a swarm of particles to find the global minimum.

4. Genetic Algorithms: A method that uses principles of natural selection and genetics to evolve a population of candidate solutions towards the global minimum.

Stochastic search methods are useful in cases where the function to be optimized is complex and has multiple local minima. They are also useful when the function is noisy or when the gradient is not available.

In summary, stochastic search methods are optimization techniques that use random sampling to find the global minimum or maximum of a function. They are useful in a variety of applications and can be applied to complex or noisy functions.
