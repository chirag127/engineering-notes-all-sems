## Unit 3 - Data Analysis

Unit 3 - Data Analysis in Data Analytics:
- Unit 3 of the subject "Introduction to Data Analytics and Visualization" focuses on data analysis, which is the process of exploring, summarizing, and making inferences from data.
- Unit 3 covers the following topics:
- Descriptive statistics: the use of measures such as mean, median, and standard deviation to summarize and describe the properties of a data set.
- Inferential statistics: the use of statistical models and techniques to make inferences and predictions based on a sample of data.
- Data visualization: the use of graphical representations of data to facilitate understanding and communication of data insights.
- Data cleaning and preparation: the process of cleaning and transforming data to prepare it for analysis.
- Exploratory data analysis (EDA): the process of exploring and summarizing data to identify patterns, relationships, and anomalies.
- Understanding data analysis is important for the extraction of insights and knowledge from data and for the development of data-driven solutions to real-world problems.
