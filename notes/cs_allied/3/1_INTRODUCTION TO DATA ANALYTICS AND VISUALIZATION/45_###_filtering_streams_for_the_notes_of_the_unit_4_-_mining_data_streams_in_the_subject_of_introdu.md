Filtering Streams in Data Analytics:
- Filtering streams refers to the process of selecting and processing data from a continuous stream of data.
- Filtering streams is an important task in data analytics, as it allows data to be processed in real-time and enables the discovery of patterns and relationships in data as it is being generated.
- Filtering streams can be performed using a variety of techniques, including:
  1. Sampling: the process of selecting a subset of data from a stream for processing.
  2. Window-based processing: the process of processing data within a sliding window of time.
  3. Data reduction: the process of reducing the size of a data stream by aggregating or summarizing data.
- Filtering streams can be performed using a variety of tools and technologies, including Apache Flink, Apache Kafka, and Apache Spark Streaming.
- Understanding filtering streams is important for the processing and analysis of real-time data and for the development of data-driven solutions to real-world problems.
