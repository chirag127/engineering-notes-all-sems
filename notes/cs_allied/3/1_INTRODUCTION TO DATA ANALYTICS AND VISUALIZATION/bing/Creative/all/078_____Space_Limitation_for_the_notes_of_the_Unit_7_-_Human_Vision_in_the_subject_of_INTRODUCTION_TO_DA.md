# Space Limitation

- Space limitation refers to the constraint of the available space on a data visualization, such as a chart, a dashboard, or a report.
- Space limitation affects how much data and information can be displayed, how clearly and accurately they can be perceived, and how easily and efficiently they can be interacted with.
- Space limitation can be caused by various factors, such as:
  - The size and resolution of the display device, such as a monitor, a tablet, or a smartphone.
  - The layout and design of the data visualization, such as the choice of chart type, the number and arrangement of elements, the use of color, text, and symbols, and the inclusion of interactive features.
  - The purpose and context of the data visualization, such as the intended audience, the message or story to be conveyed, the level of detail and complexity required, and the available time and attention span of the viewers.
- Space limitation can be addressed by various strategies, such as:
  - Reducing the amount of data and information to be displayed, by filtering, aggregating, sampling, or summarizing the data, and by focusing on the most relevant and important aspects of the data.
  - Increasing the efficiency and effectiveness of the data and information display, by choosing the appropriate chart type, scale, and orientation, by using visual encoding techniques, such as color, shape, size, and position, by applying design principles, such as contrast, alignment, proximity, and hierarchy, and by adding interactive features, such as zooming, panning, filtering, and highlighting.
  - Adapting the data and information display to the display device, by using responsive design, which adjusts the layout and design of the data visualization according to the size and resolution of the device, by using multiple views, which show different aspects or levels of detail of the data in separate charts or dashboards, and by using progressive disclosure, which reveals more data and information as the viewer interacts with the data visualization.
