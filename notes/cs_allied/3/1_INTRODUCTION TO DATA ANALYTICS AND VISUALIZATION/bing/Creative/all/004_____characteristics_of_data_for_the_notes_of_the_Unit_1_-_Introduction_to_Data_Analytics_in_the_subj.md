# Characteristics of Data

- Data is any collection of facts, figures, symbols, or values that can be processed by a computer or a human.
- Data can be classified into different types based on various criteria, such as:
  - The level of measurement: nominal, ordinal, interval, or ratio.
  - The structure: structured, semi-structured, or unstructured.
  - The source: primary, secondary, or tertiary.
  - The format: text, numeric, audio, video, image, etc.
- Data can have different characteristics that affect its quality, usability, and value, such as:
  - Accuracy: the degree to which the data reflects the true or correct value of the phenomenon it represents.
  - Completeness: the degree to which the data covers all the relevant aspects of the phenomenon it represents.
  - Consistency: the degree to which the data is free of contradictions, conflicts, or errors within and across different sources or formats.
  - Timeliness: the degree to which the data is up-to-date and relevant for the current situation or decision.
  - Relevance: the degree to which the data is useful and applicable for the intended purpose or audience.
  - Accessibility: the degree to which the data is available and easy to obtain, understand, and use by the intended users.
  - Security: the degree to which the data is protected from unauthorized access, modification, or disclosure.
  - Privacy: the degree to which the data respects the rights and preferences of the individuals or entities it represents or affects.
