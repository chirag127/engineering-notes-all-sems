# Model Building for Data Analytics

- Model building is the fourth phase of the data analytics lifecycle, where the data science team develops and tests the analytical methods and models that were planned in the previous phase .
- The main objectives of this phase are to create data sets for training, testing, and production purposes, to apply the selected methods and techniques to the data, and to evaluate the performance and accuracy of the models .
- The steps involved in this phase are :
  - **Data set creation**: The data science team splits the data into three subsets: training, testing, and production. The training set is used to train the model, the testing set is used to validate the model, and the production set is used to deploy the model in the real-world scenario. The data sets should be representative, balanced, and consistent with the business problem and the data quality requirements.
  - **Model development**: The data science team applies the chosen methods and techniques to the training data set, such as regression, classification, clustering, or recommendation. The team may use different tools and frameworks, such as Python, R, SQL, or Azure Machine Learning, depending on the complexity and scale of the problem. The team may also use different algorithms and parameters, such as linear, logistic, or decision tree, to optimize the model performance and accuracy.
  - **Model testing**: The data science team evaluates the model using the testing data set, which is unseen by the model during the training process. The team uses various metrics and techniques, such as accuracy, precision, recall, F1-score, or confusion matrix, to measure the model performance and accuracy. The team may also use cross-validation, bootstrapping, or A/B testing, to reduce the overfitting or underfitting of the model. The team may also compare the results of different models and select the best one based on the business objectives and criteria.
  - **Model refinement**: The data science team iterates the model development and testing steps until the model meets the desired performance and accuracy levels. The team may also perform feature engineering, feature selection, or dimensionality reduction, to improve the model performance and accuracy. The team may also perform error analysis, sensitivity analysis, or bias-variance trade-off, to identify and address the sources of errors and uncertainties in the model.
  - **Model documentation**: The data science team documents the model, including the data sets, methods, techniques, algorithms, parameters, metrics, results, and assumptions. The team also documents the limitations, challenges, and recommendations for the model improvement and deployment. The team also communicates the model to the stakeholders and obtains their feedback and approval.
