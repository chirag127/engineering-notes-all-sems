# Sources and nature of data

- Data is any type of information that can be measured, accessed or used in any way in a given study.
- Data sources are the physical or digital locations where data is stored in a data table, data object, or some other storage format.
- Data sources can be classified into two types: statistical and non-statistical .
  - Statistical sources refer to data that is gathered for some official purposes, such as censuses, surveys, administrative records, etc .
  - Non-statistical sources refer to data that is collected for other administrative purposes or for the private sector, such as business transactions, social media, web analytics, etc .
- Data sources can also be classified into two types: primary and secondary .
  - Primary sources refer to data that is collected directly from the original source or the phenomenon of interest, such as experiments, observations, interviews, etc .
  - Secondary sources refer to data that is derived from primary sources or other secondary sources, such as books, articles, reports, etc .
- The nature of data refers to the characteristics or attributes of data, such as its type, format, quality, validity, reliability, etc.
  - Data type refers to the kind of data, such as numerical, categorical, ordinal, etc.
  - Data format refers to the way data is represented, such as text, image, audio, video, etc.
  - Data quality refers to the accuracy, completeness, consistency, timeliness, and relevance of data.
  - Data validity refers to the extent to which data measures what it is intended to measure.
  - Data reliability refers to the extent to which data can be reproduced or replicated under similar conditions.
- The sources and nature of data are important to consider in data analytics and visualization, as they affect the choice of methods, tools, and techniques for data processing, analysis, and presentation.
