## Unit 3 - Data Analysis

- Data analysis is the process of collecting, organizing, exploring, interpreting, and presenting data to answer questions, solve problems, or support decisions.
- Data analysis involves the following steps:
  - Define the question or problem: What are you trying to find out or solve with data?
  - Collect the data: How will you gather the relevant data for your question or problem?
  - Organize the data: How will you store, manage, and clean the data for analysis?
  - Explore the data: How will you summarize, visualize, and describe the data to understand its characteristics and patterns?
  - Interpret the data: How will you use statistical methods, models, or algorithms to draw conclusions or make predictions from the data?
  - Present the data: How will you communicate your findings or recommendations to your audience using tables, charts, reports, or dashboards?
- Data analysis can be performed using various tools, such as spreadsheets, databases, programming languages, software applications, or online platforms.
- Data analysis can be applied to different domains, such as business, science, education, health, sports, or social issues.
