### Display Space for the notes of the Unit 6 - Introduction to Visualization in the subject of INTRODUCTION TO DATA ANALYTICS AND VISUALIZATION

- Visualization is the process of creating and displaying graphical representations of data or information.
- Visualization can help to explore, analyze, communicate, and understand data in various contexts and domains.
- Visualization can be classified into different types based on the purpose, the data, the audience, and the technique.
- Some common types of visualization are:
  - Statistical charts: These are graphical displays of numerical data, such as bar charts, pie charts, histograms, scatter plots, etc. They can show the distribution, correlation, comparison, or composition of data.
  - Maps: These are graphical displays of spatial data, such as geographic locations, regions, routes, etc. They can show the location, distance, direction, or area of data.
  - Networks: These are graphical displays of relational data, such as nodes, links, paths, etc. They can show the structure, connectivity, hierarchy, or flow of data.
  - Diagrams: These are graphical displays of abstract or conceptual data, such as shapes, symbols, icons, etc. They can show the logic, process, classification, or organization of data.
  - Infographics: These are graphical displays of complex or multidimensional data, such as text, images, icons, charts, etc. They can show the story, context, or insight of data.
- Visualization can be created and displayed using various tools and platforms, such as software applications, web browsers, mobile devices, etc.
- Visualization can be evaluated and improved based on various criteria, such as accuracy, clarity, aesthetics, interactivity, etc.
