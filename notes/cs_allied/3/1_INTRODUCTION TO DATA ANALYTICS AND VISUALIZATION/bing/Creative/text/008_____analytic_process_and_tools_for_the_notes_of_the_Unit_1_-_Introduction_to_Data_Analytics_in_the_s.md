### Analytic Process and Tools

- Analytic process is the systematic application of methods and tools to transform data into useful information for decision making and problem solving.
- Analytic tools are software applications or platforms that enable data collection, analysis, visualization, and reporting.
- Some of the common analytic tools are:
  - Microsoft Power BI: A data visualization tool that allows users to create interactive dashboards and reports from various data sources.
  - Tableau: A business intelligence tool that enables users to explore, analyze, and share data in various formats and styles.
  - Qlik Sense: A machine learning tool that uses artificial intelligence to generate insights and recommendations from data.
  - Looker: A data platform that connects to various databases and provides a unified view of data across the organization.
- Some of the common analytic process steps are:
  - Define the problem or objective: Identify the purpose and scope of the analysis, the stakeholders involved, and the expected outcomes.
  - Collect and prepare the data: Gather the relevant data from various sources, such as databases, files, surveys, etc. and clean, transform, and integrate the data for analysis.
  - Explore and analyze the data: Use descriptive, diagnostic, predictive, or prescriptive analytics techniques to discover patterns, trends, relationships, and anomalies in the data.
  - Visualize and communicate the results: Use charts, graphs, tables, maps, etc. to present the findings and insights in a clear and compelling way to the audience.
  - Implement and monitor the actions: Apply the recommendations or solutions derived from the analysis to the real-world situation and measure the impact and effectiveness of the actions.
