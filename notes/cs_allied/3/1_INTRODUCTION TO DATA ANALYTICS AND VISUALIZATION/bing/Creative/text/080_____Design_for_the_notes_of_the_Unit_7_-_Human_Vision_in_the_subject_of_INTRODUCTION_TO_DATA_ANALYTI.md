### Unit 7 - Human Vision in Data Analytics and Visualization

- Human vision is the process of perceiving and interpreting visual information from the environment.
- Data analytics and visualization are techniques that use graphical representations of data to facilitate understanding, communication and decision making.
- Human vision and data visualization are closely related, as both rely on the principles of visual perception, cognition and communication.
- Some of the topics covered in this unit are:

  - The characteristics of human vision, such as acuity, color vision, depth perception, attention and eye movements.
  - The principles of visual encoding, such as data types, visual variables, graphical integrity and aesthetics.
  - The principles of visual analysis, such as exploratory and explanatory analysis, interaction and annotation.
  - The principles of visual communication, such as storytelling, narrative, audience and context.
  - The challenges and opportunities of data visualization, such as scalability, uncertainty, ethics and innovation.
