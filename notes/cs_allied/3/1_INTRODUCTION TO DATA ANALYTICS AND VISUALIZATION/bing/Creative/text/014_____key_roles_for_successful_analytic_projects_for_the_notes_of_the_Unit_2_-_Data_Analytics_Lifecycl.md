### Key roles for successful analytic projects

According to the Data Analytics Lifecycle, a successful analytic project requires the collaboration of different roles and skills to achieve the desired outcomes. Some of the key roles are:

- **Project sponsor**: The project sponsor is responsible for the start of the project and provides all the requirements for the project and defines the core business problem. The project sponsor also secures the necessary resources and funding for the project and approves the final deliverables. The project sponsor is usually a senior executive or a business leader who has a stake in the project's success .
- **Project manager**: The project manager ensures that key milestones and objectives are met on time and at the expected quality. The project manager also coordinates the communication and collaboration among the project team and other stakeholders, and manages the project risks and issues. The project manager is usually a certified professional who has experience in managing complex projects .
- **Business user**: A business user is someone who understands the domain area and usually benefits from the results. This person can consult and advise the project team on the context of the project, the value of the results, and how the outputs will be operationalized. The business user is usually a business analyst, a line manager, or a deep subject matter expert .
- **Business Intelligence Analyst**: A business intelligence analyst is someone who can collect, analyze, and report on the data relevant to the project. This person can also design and develop dashboards, visualizations, and reports that can communicate the insights and recommendations to the business users and other stakeholders. The business intelligence analyst is usually a skilled user of various BI tools and platforms, such as Tableau, Power BI, or Qlik .
- **Database Administrator (DBA)**: A database administrator is someone who can manage and maintain the data sources and databases that are used for the project. This person can also ensure the data quality, security, and availability, and perform data backup and recovery. The database administrator is usually a technical expert who has knowledge of various database systems and languages, such as SQL, Oracle, or MongoDB .
- **Data engineer**: A data engineer is someone who can design and build the data pipelines and architectures that can ingest, process, and store the data for the project. This person can also implement the data transformations, integrations, and validations that are needed for the project. The data engineer is usually a proficient programmer who has experience in working with various data technologies and frameworks, such as Hadoop, Spark, or Kafka .
- **Data scientist**: A data scientist is someone who can apply advanced analytical techniques and methods, such as machine learning, statistics, or optimization, to the data to generate insights and solutions for the project. This person can also evaluate and validate the analytical models and results, and communicate the findings and recommendations to the project team and other stakeholders. The data scientist is usually a highly qualified professional who has a strong background in mathematics, computer science, or a related field .

These roles are not mutually exclusive, and one person might fulfill one or more of the roles described above in a part-time or full-time way, depending on the project's scope, complexity, and maturity. The key for a successful analytics project is to have a clear definition of the roles and responsibilities, and a good alignment and collaboration among the project team and other stakeholders.
