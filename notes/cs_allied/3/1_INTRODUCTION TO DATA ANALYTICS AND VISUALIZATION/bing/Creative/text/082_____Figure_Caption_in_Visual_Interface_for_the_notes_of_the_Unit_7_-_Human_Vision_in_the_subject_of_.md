### Figure Caption in Visual Interface

- A figure caption is a text that accompanies a graphical element in a visual interface, such as a chart, a map, a diagram, or an image.
- A figure caption serves several purposes, such as:
  - Providing a title or a summary of the graphical element
  - Explaining the data source, the variables, the units, the scale, or the legend of the graphical element
  - Highlighting the main findings, trends, patterns, or insights from the graphical element
  - Providing additional information or context that is not visible or obvious from the graphical element
- A figure caption should be concise, clear, accurate, and informative. It should not repeat or contradict the information in the graphical element, but rather complement or enhance it.
- A figure caption should be placed close to the graphical element, preferably below it, and aligned with its left edge. It should use a smaller font size than the main text, but larger than the labels or annotations in the graphical element.
- A figure caption should follow a consistent format and style throughout the visual interface. It should use proper punctuation, capitalization, and grammar. It should avoid jargon, abbreviations, or acronyms that are not defined or explained in the visual interface.
- A figure caption should be tested for readability, comprehension, and usability by the intended audience of the visual interface. It should be revised or improved based on the feedback or evaluation results.
