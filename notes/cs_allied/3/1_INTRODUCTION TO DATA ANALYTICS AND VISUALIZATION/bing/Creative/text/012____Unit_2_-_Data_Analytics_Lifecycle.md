## Unit 2 - Data Analytics Lifecycle

- The data analytics lifecycle is a framework for planning and executing data analysis projects, from defining the business problem to deploying the solution.
- The data analytics lifecycle consists of six phases: discovery, data preparation, model planning, model building, communicating results, and operationalization.
- The discovery phase involves identifying the business problem, the stakeholders, the objectives, the success criteria, the resources, and the risks of the project.
- The data preparation phase involves acquiring, exploring, cleansing, integrating, and transforming the data for analysis.
- The model planning phase involves selecting the appropriate techniques, tools, and algorithms for data analysis, as well as designing and testing the data model.
- The model building phase involves applying the data model to the data, validating the results, and refining the model as needed.
- The communicating results phase involves presenting and visualizing the findings, insights, and recommendations from the data analysis, as well as documenting the process and outcomes.
- The operationalization phase involves deploying the data model into production, monitoring its performance, and maintaining and updating it as needed.
