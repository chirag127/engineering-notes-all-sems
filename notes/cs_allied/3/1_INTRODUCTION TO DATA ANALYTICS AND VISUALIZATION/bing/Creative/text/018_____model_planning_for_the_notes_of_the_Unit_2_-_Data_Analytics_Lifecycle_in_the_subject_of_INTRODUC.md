### Model Planning
- Model planning is the process of selecting and applying appropriate analytical techniques to the data to address the business problem or question.
- Model planning involves the following steps:
  - Define the analytical objectives and metrics: what are the desired outcomes and how will they be measured?
  - Identify the data requirements and sources: what data is needed and where can it be obtained?
  - Assess the data quality and availability: how reliable, complete, and relevant is the data?
  - Choose the analytical methods and tools: what techniques and software will be used to analyze the data?
  - Design the analytical workflow and architecture: how will the data be processed, transformed, and integrated?
  - Plan the model validation and evaluation: how will the results be tested and verified?
- Model planning requires a good understanding of the business context, the data characteristics, and the analytical options.
- Model planning should be aligned with the project scope, timeline, and resources.
- Model planning should be iterative and flexible, as new data and insights may emerge during the analysis.
