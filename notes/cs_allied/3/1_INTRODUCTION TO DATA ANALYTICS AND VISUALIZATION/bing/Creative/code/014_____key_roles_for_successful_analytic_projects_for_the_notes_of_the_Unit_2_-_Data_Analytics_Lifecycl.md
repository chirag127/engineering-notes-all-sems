### Key Roles for Successful Analytic Projects

According to the Data Analytics Lifecycle, a successful analytic project requires the collaboration of different roles and skills. Some of the key roles are:

- **Project sponsor**: The project sponsor is responsible for the start of the project and provides all the requirements for the project and defines the core business problem. The project sponsor also approves the budget, timeline, and scope of the project, and ensures that the project aligns with the strategic goals of the organization. The project sponsor can be a senior executive, a business owner, or a key stakeholder.  
- **Project manager**: The project manager ensures that key milestones and objectives are met on time and at the expected quality. The project manager also coordinates the communication, collaboration, and integration of the project team and other stakeholders. The project manager can be a certified professional, a business analyst, or a subject matter expert.  
- **Business user**: A business user is someone who understands the domain area and usually benefits from the results. This person can consult and advise the project team on the context of the project, the value of the results, and how the outputs will be operationalized. A business user can be a business analyst, a line manager, or a deep subject matter expert.  
- **Business Intelligence Analyst**: A business intelligence analyst is someone who can collect, analyze, and report on data from various sources, such as databases, spreadsheets, or web services. This person can also create dashboards, visualizations, and reports to communicate the insights and recommendations to the business users and other stakeholders. A business intelligence analyst can be a data analyst, a data engineer, or a data scientist.  
- **Database Administrator (DBA)**: A database administrator is someone who can design, implement, and maintain the databases that store and manage the data for the project. This person can also ensure the security, performance, and availability of the databases, and troubleshoot any issues that arise. A database administrator can be a data engineer, a data architect, or a data scientist.  
- **Data engineer**: A data engineer is someone who can build, test, and deploy the data pipelines and platforms that enable the ingestion, processing, and transformation of data from various sources and formats. This person can also implement the data quality, governance, and validation rules, and optimize the data for analysis and modeling. A data engineer can be a data architect, a data scientist, or a software engineer.  
- **Data scientist**: A data scientist is someone who can apply advanced statistical and machine learning techniques to analyze and model the data, and generate insights and predictions that address the business problem. This person can also communicate the findings and recommendations to the business users and other stakeholders, and evaluate the impact and value of the project. A data scientist can be a statistician, a machine learning engineer, or a domain expert.  

These roles are not mutually exclusive, and one person can fulfill more than one role depending on the size, scope, and complexity of the project. However, it is important to have a clear definition and assignment of the roles and responsibilities, and to ensure that the project team has the necessary skills, tools, and resources to deliver a successful analytic project.
