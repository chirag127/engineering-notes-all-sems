### Data Preparation for the Notes of the Unit 2 - Data Analytics Lifecycle

Data preparation is the process of transforming raw data into a format that is suitable for analysis. Data preparation is an essential step in the data analytics lifecycle, as it ensures the quality, accuracy, and completeness of the data. Data preparation can also help to reduce the complexity and size of the data, and to enrich it with additional information or features.

The data preparation process can vary depending on the data sources, the analytical goals, and the tools used, but it typically consists of the following steps     :

- **Data collection**: This step involves determining what data is needed, gathering it from various sources, and establishing consistent access to it. Data collection can be done manually or automatically, using different methods such as web scraping, APIs, databases, files, etc. Data collection can also involve validating the data sources, ensuring data security, and complying with data regulations.
- **Data discovery and classification**: This step involves exploring the data, understanding its structure, content, and quality, and identifying any issues or anomalies. Data discovery and classification can also involve labeling the data according to its type, format, domain, sensitivity, etc. Data discovery and classification can help to assess the suitability and relevance of the data for the analysis, and to prioritize the data preparation tasks.
- **Data cleansing**: This step involves correcting any errors, inconsistencies, or outliers in the data, and filling in any missing or incomplete data. Data cleansing can also involve standardizing the data formats, values, and units, and removing any duplicates or irrelevant data. Data cleansing can improve the accuracy and reliability of the data, and reduce the noise and bias in the analysis.
- **Data formatting**: This step involves transforming the data into a format that is compatible with the analytical tools and methods. Data formatting can involve converting the data types, encoding, or compression, and reshaping the data structure, such as pivoting, aggregating, or splitting the data. Data formatting can also involve applying any calculations, functions, or formulas to the data, such as scaling, normalizing, or deriving new variables. Data formatting can enhance the usability and efficiency of the data, and facilitate the analysis.
- **Data integration**: This step involves combining data from different sources, tables, or files into a single dataset. Data integration can involve matching, merging, or joining the data based on common keys or attributes, and resolving any conflicts or discrepancies in the data. Data integration can also involve enriching the data with additional information or features from external sources, such as geolocation, demographics, or sentiment analysis. Data integration can increase the completeness and diversity of the data, and provide a holistic view of the analysis.
- **Data analysis**: This step involves applying various analytical techniques and tools to the data, such as descriptive, predictive, or prescriptive analytics, to generate insights, patterns, or recommendations. Data analysis can involve using different methods such as statistics, machine learning, or visualization, and evaluating the results and outcomes of the analysis. Data analysis can also involve iterating the data preparation steps based on the feedback or findings of the analysis, to refine the data and improve the analysis.

Data preparation is a crucial and iterative process that can have a significant impact on the quality and value of the data analysis. Data preparation can also be a challenging and time-consuming process that requires various skills and tools. Therefore, data preparation should be planned and executed carefully, and supported by appropriate technologies and best practices.
