### Sources and nature of data

- Data is any type of information that can be measured, accessed or used in any way in a given study.
- Data can be gathered from two places: internal and external sources.
- Internal sources are those that are generated within the organization, such as sales records, customer feedback, inventory data, etc.
- External sources are those that are obtained from outside the organization, such as government reports, market surveys, social media, etc.
- The sources of data can be classified into two types: statistical and non-statistical .
- Statistical sources refer to data that is gathered for some official purposes, such as censuses, and officially administered surveys .
- Non-statistical sources refer to the collection of data for other administrative purposes or for the private sector .
- The nature of data can be classified into two types: qualitative and quantitative.
- Qualitative data is data that is descriptive and subjective, such as opinions, feelings, attitudes, etc.
- Quantitative data is data that is numerical and objective, such as measurements, counts, percentages, etc.
- The nature of data can also be classified into two types: primary and secondary.
- Primary data is data that is collected directly by the researcher for the specific purpose of the study, such as interviews, experiments, observations, etc.
- Secondary data is data that is collected by someone else for a different purpose, and is reused by the researcher for the study, such as books, journals, websites, etc.
