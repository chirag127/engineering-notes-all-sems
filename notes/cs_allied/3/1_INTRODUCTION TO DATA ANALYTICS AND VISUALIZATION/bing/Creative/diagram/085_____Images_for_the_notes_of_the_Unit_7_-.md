- Unit 7 is about **digital media** and how it is used for communication, entertainment, education, and social change.
- Digital media refers to any media that is created, stored, and transmitted using digital technologies, such as computers, smartphones, cameras, and the internet.
- Some examples of digital media are:

  - **Text**: written or typed words that can be displayed on a screen or printed on paper, such as e-books, blogs, tweets, and emails.
  - **Images**: visual representations of objects, people, places, or ideas, such as photos, drawings, icons, and emojis.
  - **Audio**: sounds that can be heard by humans or machines, such as music, podcasts, voice messages, and sound effects.
  - **Video**: moving images that can be accompanied by audio, such as movies, TV shows, YouTube videos, and video calls.
  - **Animation**: a type of video that creates the illusion of movement by displaying a series of still images in rapid succession, such as cartoons, GIFs, and video games.
  - **Interactive media**: media that allows the user to interact with the content or other users, such as websites, apps, games, and social media platforms.

- Digital media can be classified into two categories based on how they are produced and distributed:

  - **User-generated content (UGC)**: media that is created and shared by individuals or groups of users, usually for personal or social purposes, such as blogs, podcasts, YouTube videos, and social media posts.
  - **Professional content (PC)**: media that is created and distributed by organizations or companies, usually for commercial or educational purposes, such as e-books, movies, TV shows, and online courses.
