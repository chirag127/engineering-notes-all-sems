### Display Space

- The display space is the area where the visual representation of data is shown to the user.
- The display space can be physical (such as a paper, a screen, a wall, etc.) or virtual (such as a web page, a mobile app, a VR headset, etc.).
- The display space has some properties that affect the design and perception of the visualization, such as:
  - Size: the dimensions of the display space in terms of length and width.
  - Resolution: the number of pixels or dots per inch (dpi) that the display space can show.
  - Aspect ratio: the ratio of the width to the height of the display space.
  - Color depth: the number of colors or shades of gray that the display space can show.
  - Brightness: the intensity of the light emitted or reflected by the display space.
  - Contrast: the difference between the brightest and the darkest areas of the display space.
  - Viewing angle: the angle at which the user can see the display space without distortion or loss of quality.
  - Viewing distance: the distance between the user and the display space.
  - Interactivity: the degree to which the user can manipulate or interact with the display space or the visualization.
- The display space can also have some constraints or limitations that affect the design and perception of the visualization, such as:
  - Bandwidth: the amount of data that can be transferred or displayed per unit of time.
  - Memory: the amount of data that can be stored or processed by the display device or the visualization system.
  - Processing power: the speed and efficiency of the display device or the visualization system to perform calculations or operations on the data.
  - Accessibility: the availability and usability of the display space or the visualization for different types of users, such as those with visual impairments, color blindness, low literacy, etc.
  - Cost: the amount of money or resources required to acquire, maintain, or use the display space or the visualization.
- The display space can also have some affordances or opportunities that affect the design and perception of the visualization, such as:
  - Scalability: the ability of the display space or the visualization to adapt to different sizes or resolutions of data or display devices.
  - Flexibility: the ability of the display space or the visualization to accommodate different types of data or user preferences.
  - Creativity: the ability of the display space or the visualization to inspire or stimulate new ideas or insights from the data or the user.
  - Engagement: the ability of the display space or the visualization to attract or retain the attention or interest of the user.
  - Communication: the ability of the display space or the visualization to convey or facilitate the message or the meaning of the data or the user.
