### Real time sentiment analysis
- Real time sentiment analysis is a machine learning technique that automatically recognizes and extracts the sentiment in a text whenever it occurs .
- Sentiment is the subjective opinion or attitude expressed in a text, such as positive, negative, or neutral.
- Real time sentiment analysis is most commonly used to analyze brand and product mentions in live social comments and posts .
- Real time sentiment analysis can help businesses and organizations to monitor customer feedback, identify trends, improve customer service, and gain insights into their target audience .

#### Applications of real time sentiment analysis
- Some of the applications of real time sentiment analysis are :
  - Social media monitoring: analyzing the sentiment of tweets, posts, comments, reviews, etc. to measure brand reputation, customer satisfaction, and market sentiment.
  - Customer service: analyzing the sentiment of customer queries, complaints, feedback, etc. to improve response time, quality, and satisfaction.
  - Market research: analyzing the sentiment of surveys, polls, focus groups, etc. to understand customer preferences, needs, and expectations.
  - Product development: analyzing the sentiment of product reviews, ratings, feedback, etc. to identify strengths, weaknesses, and opportunities for improvement.

#### Challenges of real time sentiment analysis
- Some of the challenges of real time sentiment analysis are:
  - Data quality: ensuring that the data is relevant, accurate, and complete for the analysis.
  - Data volume: handling the large and continuous stream of data from various sources and formats.
  - Data variety: dealing with the diversity and complexity of natural language, such as slang, sarcasm, irony, emoticons, etc.
  - Data context: understanding the meaning and intention of the text in relation to the domain, topic, and situation.

#### Techniques of real time sentiment analysis
- Some of the techniques of real time sentiment analysis are :
  - Rule-based: using predefined rules and lexicons to assign sentiment scores based on the presence and frequency of keywords, phrases, and modifiers.
  - Machine learning: using supervised or unsupervised learning algorithms to train models that can classify or score the sentiment of the text based on features, such as word embeddings, n-grams, etc.
  - Deep learning: using neural networks, such as convolutional neural networks (CNNs) or recurrent neural networks (RNNs), to learn complex and non-linear patterns and features from the text for sentiment analysis.
  - Hybrid: combining rule-based and machine learning techniques to leverage the advantages of both approaches and overcome their limitations.
