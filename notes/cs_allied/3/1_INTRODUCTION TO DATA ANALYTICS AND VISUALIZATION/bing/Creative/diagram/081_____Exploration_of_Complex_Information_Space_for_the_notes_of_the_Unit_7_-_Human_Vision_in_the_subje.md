### Exploration of Complex Information Space

- Complex information spaces are information spaces that are not only large but also heterogeneous in their structure, meaning that they contain different types of data, such as text, images, audio, video, etc.
- Exploration of complex information spaces is the process of interacting with data in order to extract meaningful information, such as patterns, trends, outliers, relationships, etc.
- Exploration of complex information spaces requires presentation techniques that provide intuitive orientation aids and navigation techniques, such as:
  - Information visualizations that use graphical representations of data to convey information, such as charts, graphs, maps, etc. 
  - Information metaphors that use familiar concepts or objects to represent abstract or unfamiliar data, such as desktop, bookshelf, landscape, etc. 
  - Information filters that allow the user to select, reduce, or highlight relevant data based on certain criteria, such as keywords, categories, ratings, etc. 
  - Information overviews that provide a summary or a context of the data, such as thumbnails, outlines, tables of contents, etc. 
- Exploration of complex information spaces also requires interaction techniques that enable the user to manipulate, query, or modify the data, such as:
  - Zooming and panning that allow the user to adjust the level of detail or the focus of the data, such as magnifying, shrinking, scrolling, etc. 
  - Linking and brushing that allow the user to select or highlight data in one view and see the corresponding data in another view, such as selecting a point in a scatterplot and seeing the details in a table, etc. 
  - Querying and filtering that allow the user to specify or refine the data based on certain conditions, such as searching, sorting, grouping, etc. 
  - Annotation and bookmarking that allow the user to add or save notes or comments to the data, such as labeling, tagging, marking, etc. 
- Exploration of complex information spaces can be applied to various domains and scenarios, such as:
  - Human space exploration that involves information technology as an infrastructure technology that enables other technology and capabilities, such as vehicle health management, automated assembly, automated operations, etc.
  - Visual exploration of complex information spaces that involves cluster analysis as a technique to group data based on similarity or dissimilarity, and 3D space as a medium to display and navigate the data, such as using a 3D landscape metaphor to represent clusters of documents, etc.
