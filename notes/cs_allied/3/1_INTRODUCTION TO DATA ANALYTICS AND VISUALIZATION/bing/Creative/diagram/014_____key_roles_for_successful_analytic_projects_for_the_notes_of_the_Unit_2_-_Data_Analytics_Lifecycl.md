### Key roles for successful analytic projects

- A successful analytic project requires the collaboration of different roles with different skills and responsibilities. Some of the key roles are:

  - **Business analyst**: This role is responsible for understanding the business problem, defining the objectives and scope of the project, identifying the data sources and requirements, and communicating the results and recommendations to the stakeholders.

  - **Data engineer**: This role is responsible for collecting, cleaning, transforming, and storing the data from various sources, ensuring the data quality and integrity, and creating and maintaining the data pipelines and infrastructure.

  - **Data analyst**: This role is responsible for exploring, analyzing, and visualizing the data, applying statistical and mathematical techniques, generating insights and hypotheses, and testing and validating the assumptions and results.

  - **Data scientist**: This role is responsible for applying advanced analytical methods, such as machine learning and artificial intelligence, to the data, building and deploying predictive and prescriptive models, and evaluating and improving the model performance and accuracy.

  - **Data steward**: This role is responsible for ensuring the compliance and governance of the data, defining and enforcing the data policies and standards, and managing the data security and privacy.

  - **Data visualization specialist**: This role is responsible for designing and creating effective and engaging data visualizations, using appropriate tools and techniques, and enhancing the data storytelling and communication.

- Depending on the size and complexity of the project, these roles can be performed by one or more individuals or teams, and they may overlap or complement each other. The key is to have a clear definition and alignment of the roles and responsibilities, and to foster a collaborative and iterative approach to the project.
