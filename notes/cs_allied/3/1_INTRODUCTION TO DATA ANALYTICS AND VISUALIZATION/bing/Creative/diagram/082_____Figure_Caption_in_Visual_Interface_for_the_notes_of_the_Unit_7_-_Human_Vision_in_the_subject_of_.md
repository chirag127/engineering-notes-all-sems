### Figure Caption in Visual Interface

- A figure caption is a text that accompanies a figure in a visual interface, such as a chart, a graph, a map, or an image.
- A figure caption serves several purposes, such as:
  - Providing a title or a summary of the figure
  - Explaining the data source, the variables, the units, the scale, or the method of the figure
  - Highlighting the main findings, the trends, the patterns, or the outliers of the figure
  - Comparing or contrasting the figure with other figures or data
  - Providing additional information or context for the figure
- A figure caption should be clear, concise, accurate, and informative. It should not repeat the information that is already shown in the figure, but rather complement or supplement it.
- A figure caption should be placed near the figure, preferably below it, and aligned with the left edge of the figure. It should be formatted differently from the main text, such as using a smaller font size, a different font style, or a different color.
- A figure caption should follow a consistent style and format throughout the document or the interface. It should use proper grammar, punctuation, and capitalization. It should also use appropriate terminology and notation for the data and the figure.
- A figure caption should be numbered sequentially if there are multiple figures in the document or the interface. The number should be preceded by the word "Figure" and followed by a period. The number should also be referenced in the main text when the figure is mentioned or discussed. For example, "Figure 1. The distribution of GDP per capita in 2020."
