### Sources and nature of data

- Data is any type of information that can be measured, accessed or used in any way in a given study.
- Data sources are the physical or digital places where data is stored in a data table, data object, or some other storage format.
- Data sources can be classified into two types: statistical and non-statistical .
  - Statistical sources refer to data that is gathered for some official purposes, such as censuses, surveys, administrative records, etc .
  - Non-statistical sources refer to data that is collected for other administrative purposes or for the private sector, such as business transactions, social media, web analytics, etc .
- Data sources can also be classified into two types: primary and secondary .
  - Primary sources refer to data that is collected directly from the original source or the phenomenon under study, such as experiments, observations, interviews, etc .
  - Secondary sources refer to data that is derived from primary sources or other secondary sources, such as books, articles, reports, etc .
- The nature of data refers to the characteristics or attributes of data, such as its quality, quantity, format, type, etc.
  - The quality of data refers to how accurate, reliable, valid, and relevant the data is for the research question.
  - The quantity of data refers to how much data is available and how representative it is of the population or sample under study.
  - The format of data refers to how the data is organized, structured, and presented, such as tables, graphs, charts, etc.
  - The type of data refers to the level of measurement or the scale of data, such as nominal, ordinal, interval, or ratio.
