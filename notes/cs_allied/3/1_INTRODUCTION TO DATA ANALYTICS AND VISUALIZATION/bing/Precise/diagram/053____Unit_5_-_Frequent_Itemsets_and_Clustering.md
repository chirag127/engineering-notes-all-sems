## Unit 5 - Frequent Itemsets and Clustering

Frequent itemsets and clustering are two important concepts in data mining and machine learning.

1. **Frequent Itemsets**: Frequent itemsets refer to sets of items that frequently appear together in a dataset. These sets can be used to identify patterns and relationships between items, which can be useful for tasks such as market basket analysis and recommendation systems.

2. **Clustering**: Clustering is the process of grouping similar data points together into clusters. This can be useful for tasks such as data compression, data visualization, and anomaly detection.

Both frequent itemsets and clustering can be used to gain insights into data and to make predictions based on that data. These techniques are commonly used in fields such as marketing, finance, and healthcare.
