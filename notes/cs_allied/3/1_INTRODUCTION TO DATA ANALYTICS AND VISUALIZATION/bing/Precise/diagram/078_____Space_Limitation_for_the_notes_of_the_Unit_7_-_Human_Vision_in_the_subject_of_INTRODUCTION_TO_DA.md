### Space Limitation

1. Space limitation refers to the limited amount of space available for displaying visual information.
2. This can be a challenge when working with large datasets or complex visualizations.
3. Techniques such as filtering, aggregation, and zooming can be used to overcome space limitations.
4. Filtering involves selecting a subset of the data to display, based on certain criteria.
5. Aggregation involves combining multiple data points into a single value, such as an average or sum.
6. Zooming allows the user to focus on a specific area of the visualization, while still maintaining an overall view of the data.
7. These techniques can help to make the most of the available space, while still conveying the necessary information to the user.
