### Time Limitations

In the context of Unit 7 - Human Vision in the subject of INTRODUCTION TO DATA ANALYTICS AND VISUALIZATION, time limitations refer to the constraints on the amount of time available for visual processing and analysis of data.

1. Human visual processing is limited by the speed at which the brain can process information. This means that there is a limit to the amount of data that can be effectively analyzed and understood within a given time frame.

2. Time limitations can also arise from external factors such as deadlines or the need to make quick decisions based on the available data.

3. To overcome time limitations, it is important to prioritize the most relevant data and use effective visualization techniques to quickly convey the key insights.

4. Techniques such as pre-attentive processing, which leverages the brain's ability to quickly identify certain visual features, can also help to overcome time limitations.

5. It is also important to consider the trade-off between the time available for analysis and the level of detail and accuracy required. In some cases, it may be necessary to sacrifice some level of detail in order to meet time constraints.

6. Overall, effective data analysis and visualization requires a balance between the time available and the level of detail and accuracy required, taking into account the limitations of human visual processing.
