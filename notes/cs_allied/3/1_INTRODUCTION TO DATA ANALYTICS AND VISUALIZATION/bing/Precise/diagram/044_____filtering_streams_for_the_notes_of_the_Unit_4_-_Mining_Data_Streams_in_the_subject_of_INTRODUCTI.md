### Filtering Streams

Filtering streams is a technique used in the field of data stream mining to extract useful information from continuous, rapid data streams. It is a part of Unit 4 - Mining Data Streams in the subject of INTRODUCTION TO DATA ANALYTICS AND VISUALIZATION. Here are some key points to remember:

1. Filtering streams involves selecting only the relevant data from a stream, based on certain criteria or conditions.
2. This technique is useful for reducing the amount of data that needs to be processed and stored, as well as for improving the accuracy and efficiency of data analysis.
3. Common filtering methods include sampling, windowing, and sketching.
4. Sampling involves selecting a representative subset of the data stream for analysis.
5. Windowing involves dividing the data stream into fixed-size segments or "windows" and analyzing each window separately.
6. Sketching involves creating a compact summary or "sketch" of the data stream, which can be used for approximate analysis.
7. Filtering streams is an important step in many data stream mining applications, including anomaly detection, trend analysis, and predictive modeling.
