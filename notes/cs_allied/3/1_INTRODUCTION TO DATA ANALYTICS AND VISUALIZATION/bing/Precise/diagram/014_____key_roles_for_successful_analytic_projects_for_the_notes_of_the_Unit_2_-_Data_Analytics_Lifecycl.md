### Key Roles for Successful Analytic Projects

In order to successfully carry out an analytic project, several key roles must be fulfilled:

1. **Project Sponsor**: This individual is responsible for providing the necessary resources and support for the project. They are also responsible for ensuring that the project aligns with the organization's overall goals and objectives.

2. **Project Manager**: The project manager is responsible for overseeing the day-to-day operations of the project. They ensure that the project stays on track and that all team members are working towards the same goals.

3. **Data Scientist**: The data scientist is responsible for analyzing the data and developing models to extract insights from the data. They use their expertise in statistics, machine learning, and data visualization to help the team make data-driven decisions.

4. **Data Engineer**: The data engineer is responsible for designing, building, and maintaining the data infrastructure. They ensure that the data is properly stored, processed, and made available to the data scientist and other team members.

5. **Business Analyst**: The business analyst is responsible for understanding the business context of the project and translating the insights from the data into actionable recommendations. They work closely with the project sponsor and other stakeholders to ensure that the project delivers value to the organization.

6. **Domain Expert**: The domain expert provides subject matter expertise to the project team. They have a deep understanding of the industry or field in which the project is taking place and can provide valuable insights and guidance to the team.

These roles are essential for the success of an analytic project. Each role brings a unique set of skills and expertise to the table, and it is important for the team to work together to achieve the project's goals.
