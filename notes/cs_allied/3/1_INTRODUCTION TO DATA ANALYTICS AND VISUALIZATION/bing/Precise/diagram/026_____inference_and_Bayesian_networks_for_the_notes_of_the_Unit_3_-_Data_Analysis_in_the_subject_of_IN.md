### Inference and Bayesian Networks

#### Unit 3 - Data Analysis

##### Introduction to Data Analytics and Visualization

- **Inference** refers to the process of drawing conclusions from data, often through the use of statistical models.
- **Bayesian networks** are graphical models that represent the probabilistic relationships between variables in a system.
- Bayesian networks can be used for inference by calculating the probabilities of different outcomes given certain evidence.
- Inference in Bayesian networks is typically performed using algorithms such as variable elimination or belief propagation.
- Bayesian networks can be useful for data analysis as they provide a way to model complex relationships between variables and make predictions based on observed data.
- Bayesian networks can also be used for decision making, as they allow for the calculation of the expected utility of different actions given the available evidence.
