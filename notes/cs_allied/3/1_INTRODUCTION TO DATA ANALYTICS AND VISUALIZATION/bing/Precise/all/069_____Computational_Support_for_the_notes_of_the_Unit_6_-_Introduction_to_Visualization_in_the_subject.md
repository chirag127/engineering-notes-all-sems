# Computational Support for the notes of the Unit 6 - Introduction to Visualization in the subject of INTRODUCTION TO DATA ANALYTICS AND VISUALIZATION

1. Visualization is the graphical representation of data and information.
2. It is used to communicate data in a clear and effective manner.
3. Visualization tools and techniques can help to identify trends, patterns, and relationships in data.
4. There are various types of visualization techniques, including charts, graphs, maps, and diagrams.
5. Visualization software can be used to create interactive and dynamic visualizations.
6. Some popular visualization tools include Tableau, QlikView, and Microsoft Power BI.
7. Effective visualization requires an understanding of the data, the audience, and the message to be conveyed.
8. Visualization can be used in various fields, including business, science, and healthcare.
9. It is important to choose the right visualization technique for the data and the intended audience.
10. Visualization can help to make data-driven decisions and to communicate complex information in a clear and concise manner.
