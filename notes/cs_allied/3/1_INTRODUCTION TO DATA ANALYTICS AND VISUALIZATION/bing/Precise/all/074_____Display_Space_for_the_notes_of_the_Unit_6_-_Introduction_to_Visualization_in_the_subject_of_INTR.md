# Unit 6 - Introduction to Visualization

## Display Space

1. Display space refers to the area where visualizations are presented to the user.
2. It can be a physical space, such as a computer monitor or a printed page, or a virtual space, such as a web page or a mobile device screen.
3. The size and resolution of the display space can affect the readability and effectiveness of the visualization.
4. It is important to consider the display space when designing visualizations to ensure that the information is presented in a clear and effective manner.
5. The display space can also be used to provide additional context or information to the user, such as labels, titles, and legends.
6. The use of color, size, and position can help to effectively utilize the display space and convey information to the user.
