# Model Planning

Model planning is an important step in the Data Analytics Lifecycle, which is covered in Unit 2 of the subject "Introduction to Data Analytics and Visualization". Here are some key points to consider when planning a model:

1. **Define the business objective**: The first step in model planning is to clearly define the business objective that the model is intended to address. This will help to guide the selection of the appropriate modeling technique and the choice of variables to include in the model.

2. **Select the modeling technique**: There are many different modeling techniques to choose from, including regression, decision trees, and neural networks. The choice of technique will depend on the nature of the data and the business objective.

3. **Identify the relevant variables**: Once the modeling technique has been selected, the next step is to identify the variables that will be included in the model. These variables should be chosen based on their relevance to the business objective and their ability to improve the predictive power of the model.

4. **Prepare the data**: Before the model can be built, the data must be prepared. This may involve cleaning the data, transforming variables, and dealing with missing values.

5. **Partition the data**: The data should be partitioned into training and validation sets. The training set is used to build the model, while the validation set is used to evaluate the model's performance.

6. **Build the model**: Once the data has been prepared and partitioned, the model can be built using the selected modeling technique and the identified variables.

7. **Evaluate the model**: The final step in model planning is to evaluate the model's performance using the validation set. This will help to determine whether the model is meeting the business objective and whether any changes need to be made to improve its performance.

These are some of the key points to consider when planning a model as part of the Data Analytics Lifecycle. By following these steps, you can ensure that your model is well-designed and effective in achieving its intended business objective.
