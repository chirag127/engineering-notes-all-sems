## Unit 7 - Human Vision

1. The human eye is a complex organ that allows us to see the world around us.
2. The eye is made up of several parts, including the cornea, iris, pupil, lens, retina, and optic nerve.
3. The cornea is the clear, dome-shaped surface that covers the front of the eye. It helps to focus light onto the retina.
4. The iris is the colored part of the eye that controls the size of the pupil, which is the opening that allows light to enter the eye.
5. The lens is a transparent structure that changes shape to focus light onto the retina.
6. The retina is a layer of light-sensitive cells at the back of the eye that converts light into electrical signals.
7. These signals are then sent to the brain via the optic nerve, where they are interpreted as visual images.
8. The process of seeing involves the eye capturing light, focusing it onto the retina, and converting it into electrical signals that are sent to the brain for interpretation.
9. The human eye is capable of seeing a wide range of colors and can adapt to different lighting conditions.
10. However, the eye is not perfect and can be affected by conditions such as nearsightedness, farsightedness, and astigmatism, which can be corrected with glasses or contact lenses.
