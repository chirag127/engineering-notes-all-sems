### Unit 7 - Human Vision

Human vision is an important topic in the field of data analytics and visualization. Understanding how humans perceive and process visual information can help in designing effective visualizations. Here are some key points to consider:

1. **The human eye**: The human eye is capable of detecting a wide range of colors and brightness levels. It is sensitive to changes in brightness and color, and can adapt to different lighting conditions.

2. **Color perception**: Humans perceive color through the activation of three types of cone cells in the retina, each sensitive to a different range of wavelengths. The combination of signals from these cells allows us to perceive a wide range of colors.

3. **Visual acuity**: Visual acuity refers to the ability to resolve fine details. It is affected by factors such as the distance of the object, the level of illumination, and the contrast between the object and its background.

4. **Gestalt principles**: The Gestalt principles describe how humans perceive visual information and organize it into meaningful patterns. These principles include proximity, similarity, continuity, closure, and symmetry.

5. **Pre-attentive processing**: Pre-attentive processing refers to the initial stage of visual perception, in which the brain rapidly identifies basic features of the visual scene, such as color, shape, and orientation. This stage occurs before conscious attention is directed to specific objects or regions of the scene.

6. **Visual attention**: Visual attention refers to the process of selectively focusing on specific aspects of the visual scene while ignoring others. Attention can be directed by factors such as the salience of objects, the goals of the observer, and the context of the scene.

7. **Visual memory**: Visual memory refers to the ability to store and retrieve visual information. It plays a crucial role in tasks such as object recognition, spatial navigation, and visual problem-solving.

Understanding these aspects of human vision can help in designing effective visualizations that effectively communicate information to the viewer. By taking into account the capabilities and limitations of the human visual system, designers can create visualizations that are clear, engaging, and easy to understand.
