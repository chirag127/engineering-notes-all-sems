### Clustering High Dimensional Data

Clustering high-dimensional data refers to the cluster analysis of data with anywhere from a few dozen to many thousands of dimensions. There are four main challenges that need to be overcome when clustering high-dimensional data:

1. **Multiple dimensions are hard to think in and impossible to visualize**: Due to the exponential growth of the number of possible subspaces, it becomes increasingly difficult to think in multiple dimensions and to visualize the data.
2. **The concept of distance becomes less precise**: As the number of dimensions grows, the distance between data points becomes less precise.
3. **High-dimensional data space is huge**: The high-dimensional data space is huge and it has complex data types and attributes.
4. **Conventional distance measures can be ineffective**: Clusters in high-dimensional data are significantly small and the conventional distance measures can be ineffective.

To overcome these challenges, the high-dimensional data is often reduced to low-dimension data to make the clustering and search for clusters simpler. Some applications may require appropriate models of clusters, especially for high-dimensional data.
