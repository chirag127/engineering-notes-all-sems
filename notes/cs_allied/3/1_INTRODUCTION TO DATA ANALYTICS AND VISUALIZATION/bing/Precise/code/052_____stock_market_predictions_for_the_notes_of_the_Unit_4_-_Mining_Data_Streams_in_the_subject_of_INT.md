### Unit 4 - Mining Data Streams: Stock Market Predictions

1. Stock market data analysis needs the help of artificial intelligence and data mining techniques. The volatility of stock prices depends on gains or losses of certain companies.
2. Many people consider stock market prediction as gambling. However, it is possible to generate constructive patterns by the analysis of stock prices. Data mining techniques can be applied on past and present financial data to generate patterns and decision-making algorithms.
3. A dataset for stock market prediction can be found on websites like Kaggle. The dataset has many columns like date, open, close, high, low, and turnover, etc. High and low are the boundaries of the stock prices, where a close column gives the closing value for the end of the day. The first step is to clean the dataset.
4. Precisely predicting stocks is essential for investors to gain enormous profits. However, the volatility of the market makes this kind of prediction highly difficult. Data Mining and Machine Learning could be used to guide an investor’s decisions.
5. The main purpose of forecasting by data mining in the stock market is to discover knowledge that can assist decision-makers. It is important that companies use data mining with utmost care to improve their business by increasing revenue and reducing costs.
6. Prediction accuracy results can range from 72.73% to 86.21% when combining news polarities and historical stock prices together to predict future stock prices.
