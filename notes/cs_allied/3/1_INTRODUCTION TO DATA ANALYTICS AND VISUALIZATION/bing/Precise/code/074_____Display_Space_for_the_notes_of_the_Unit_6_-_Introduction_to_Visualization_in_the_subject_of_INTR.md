### Display Space for the notes of the Unit 6 - Introduction to Visualization in the subject of INTRODUCTION TO DATA ANALYTICS AND VISUALIZATION

1. Visualization is the graphical representation of data and information.
2. It helps to communicate information clearly and effectively through graphical means.
3. Visualization can be used to discover patterns, trends, and relationships that might be difficult to discern from raw data.
4. There are many types of visualizations, including charts, graphs, maps, and diagrams.
5. The choice of visualization depends on the type of data being represented and the intended audience.
6. Effective visualization requires careful consideration of design principles, such as the use of color, scale, and layout.
7. Visualization tools and software can help to create and customize visualizations.
8. Interactive visualizations allow users to explore and manipulate data in real-time.
9. Visualization is an important tool in data analysis and decision-making.
