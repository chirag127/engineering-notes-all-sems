### CLIQUE and ProCLUS

CLIQUE (CLustering In QUEst) and ProCLUS (PROjected CLUStering) are two algorithms used for clustering high-dimensional data in the field of data analytics and visualization.

#### CLIQUE
- CLIQUE is an algorithm that automatically identifies dense clusters in subspaces of maximum dimensionality.
- It partitions the data space into non-overlapping rectangular units and identifies the dense units.
- The dense units are then merged to form clusters.
- CLIQUE can handle noise and can identify clusters of arbitrary shapes.

#### ProCLUS
- ProCLUS is a projected clustering algorithm that finds clusters in subspaces of the data.
- It starts by selecting a set of cluster representatives and then assigns each point to its nearest representative.
- The algorithm then identifies the relevant attributes for each cluster and prunes the irrelevant ones.
- ProCLUS can handle noise and can identify clusters of arbitrary shapes.

These two algorithms are commonly used in the analysis of frequent itemsets and clustering, which is a topic covered in Unit 5 of the subject "Introduction to Data Analytics and Visualization". They are useful tools for finding patterns and relationships in large, high-dimensional datasets.
