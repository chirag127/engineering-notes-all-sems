### Limitations of Visualization

1. **Data Quality**: The quality of the data being visualized is crucial for accurate and meaningful visualizations. Poor data quality can lead to misleading or incorrect visualizations.

2. **Data Complexity**: Visualizations can become difficult to interpret when dealing with large and complex datasets. It is important to carefully select and organize the data to be visualized to avoid overwhelming the viewer.

3. **Perception and Interpretation**: The way a visualization is perceived and interpreted can vary among individuals. It is important to consider the target audience and their potential biases when designing a visualization.

4. **Design Choices**: The design choices made when creating a visualization can greatly impact its effectiveness. Poor design choices can make a visualization difficult to understand or misleading.

5. **Technical Limitations**: There may be technical limitations that restrict the types of visualizations that can be created or the amount of data that can be displayed.

6. **Misuse**: Visualizations can be misused to intentionally mislead or manipulate the viewer. It is important to use visualizations ethically and responsibly.
