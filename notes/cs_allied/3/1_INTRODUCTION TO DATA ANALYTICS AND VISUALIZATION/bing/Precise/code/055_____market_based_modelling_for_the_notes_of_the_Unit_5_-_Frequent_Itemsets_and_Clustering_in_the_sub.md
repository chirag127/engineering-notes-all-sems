### Market Based Modelling

Market based modelling is a technique used to analyze customer purchase data to identify patterns and relationships among items. This technique is commonly used in the field of market basket analysis, where the goal is to find associations between items that are frequently purchased together.

Here are some key points to remember about market based modelling:

1. Market based modelling is used to analyze customer purchase data to identify patterns and relationships among items.
2. This technique is commonly used in the field of market basket analysis.
3. The goal of market based modelling is to find associations between items that are frequently purchased together.
4. Market based modelling can help businesses to better understand their customers' purchasing behavior and make more informed decisions about product placement, promotions, and pricing.
5. There are several algorithms and techniques that can be used for market based modelling, including the Apriori algorithm and association rule mining.
