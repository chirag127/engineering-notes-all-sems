### Discovery
- The discovery phase is the first phase of the Data Analytics Lifecycle.
- It involves identifying the business problem or opportunity that can be addressed using data analytics.
- During this phase, the team will work with business stakeholders to understand their needs and goals.
- The team will also identify the data sources that will be needed to address the problem or opportunity.
- The discovery phase is critical to the success of the project, as it sets the foundation for the rest of the lifecycle.
- It is important to take the time to thoroughly understand the business problem or opportunity and to gather all the necessary data before moving on to the next phase.
