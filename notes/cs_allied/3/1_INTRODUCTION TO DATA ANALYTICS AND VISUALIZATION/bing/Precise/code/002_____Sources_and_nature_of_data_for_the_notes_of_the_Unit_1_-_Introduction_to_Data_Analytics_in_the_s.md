### Sources and nature of data for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Data Analytics in the subject of INTRODUCTION TO DATA ANALYTICS AND VISUALIZATION

1. **Data sources**: Data can be collected from various sources such as surveys, experiments, and observational studies. Data can also be obtained from secondary sources such as government agencies, private organizations, and academic institutions.

2. **Nature of data**: Data can be classified into different types based on its nature. Some common types of data include numerical, categorical, and ordinal data.

3. **Numerical data**: Numerical data is data that is represented by numbers. It can be further classified into discrete and continuous data. Discrete data is data that can only take on certain values, while continuous data can take on any value within a certain range.

4. **Categorical data**: Categorical data is data that can be divided into categories. It is also known as nominal data. Examples of categorical data include gender, race, and religion.

5. **Ordinal data**: Ordinal data is data that can be ordered or ranked. It is similar to categorical data, but the categories have a specific order. Examples of ordinal data include educational level and income level.

6. **Data quality**: The quality of data is important in data analysis. Data quality can be affected by factors such as missing data, measurement error, and data entry errors. It is important to assess the quality of data before conducting any analysis.

7. **Data preprocessing**: Data preprocessing is the process of preparing data for analysis. This may involve cleaning the data, transforming the data, and selecting relevant features. Data preprocessing is an important step in the data analysis process.
