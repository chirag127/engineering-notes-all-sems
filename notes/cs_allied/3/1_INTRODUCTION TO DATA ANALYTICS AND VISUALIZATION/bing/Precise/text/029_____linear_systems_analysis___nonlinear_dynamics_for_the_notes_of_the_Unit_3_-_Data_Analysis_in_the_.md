### Linear Systems Analysis & Nonlinear Dynamics

Unit 3 - Data Analysis in the subject of INTRODUCTION TO DATA ANALYTICS AND VISUALIZATION

1. Linear systems analysis is a mathematical technique used to analyze the behavior of linear systems. Linear systems are systems whose output is directly proportional to their input.
2. Nonlinear dynamics is the study of systems that exhibit complex and often chaotic behavior due to the presence of nonlinearity in their equations of motion.
3. Linear systems analysis is used to understand the behavior of systems that can be modeled using linear equations, while nonlinear dynamics is used to understand the behavior of systems that cannot be modeled using linear equations.
4. Linear systems analysis is often used in fields such as engineering, physics, and economics to model and analyze the behavior of systems such as electrical circuits, mechanical systems, and financial markets.
5. Nonlinear dynamics is used in fields such as biology, chemistry, and ecology to model and analyze the behavior of complex systems such as ecosystems, chemical reactions, and biological populations.
6. Linear systems analysis and nonlinear dynamics are both important tools in the field of data analysis, as they allow us to understand and predict the behavior of complex systems using mathematical models.
