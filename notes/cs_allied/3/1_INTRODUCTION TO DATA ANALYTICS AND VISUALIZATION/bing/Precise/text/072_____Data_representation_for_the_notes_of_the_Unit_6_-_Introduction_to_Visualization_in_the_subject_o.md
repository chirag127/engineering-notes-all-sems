### Data Representation

Data representation is the method used to display or present data in a meaningful way. It is an essential aspect of data visualization, which is the graphical representation of information and data. In Unit 6 - Introduction to Visualization of the subject INTRODUCTION TO DATA ANALYTICS AND VISUALIZATION, we will learn about various methods of data representation.

1. **Tables**: Tables are used to organize data in rows and columns. They are useful for presenting data in a structured manner and for comparing values.

2. **Charts and Graphs**: Charts and graphs are used to represent data visually. They can be used to show trends, relationships, and patterns in data. Some common types of charts and graphs include bar charts, pie charts, line graphs, and scatter plots.

3. **Maps**: Maps are used to represent data that has a geographical component. They can be used to show the distribution of data across different regions.

4. **Infographics**: Infographics are visual representations of data that combine text, images, and charts. They are used to present complex information in an easy-to-understand manner.

5. **Dashboards**: Dashboards are used to display key performance indicators and other important data in a single view. They are commonly used in business intelligence and data analytics to provide real-time information to decision-makers.

In conclusion, data representation is a crucial aspect of data visualization, and there are various methods available to represent data in a meaningful way. It is important to choose the appropriate method of data representation based on the type of data and the intended audience.
