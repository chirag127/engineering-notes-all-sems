### Computational Support for the notes of the Unit 6 - Introduction to Visualization in the subject of INTRODUCTION TO DATA ANALYTICS AND VISUALIZATION

1. Visualization is the graphical representation of data and information.
2. It helps to understand complex data sets by representing them in a more intuitive and accessible format.
3. There are various tools and techniques available for data visualization, including charts, graphs, maps, and infographics.
4. Effective data visualization can help to identify patterns, trends, and relationships within the data.
5. It can also facilitate communication and collaboration by making data more understandable to a wider audience.
6. Computational support for data visualization includes the use of software tools and programming languages to create, manipulate, and display visual representations of data.
7. Common tools for data visualization include spreadsheet programs, business intelligence software, and specialized visualization software.
8. Programming languages such as R and Python also have libraries and packages for data visualization.
9. The choice of tool or technique for data visualization depends on the nature of the data, the intended audience, and the desired outcome.
10. Effective data visualization requires a combination of technical skills, domain knowledge, and design expertise.
