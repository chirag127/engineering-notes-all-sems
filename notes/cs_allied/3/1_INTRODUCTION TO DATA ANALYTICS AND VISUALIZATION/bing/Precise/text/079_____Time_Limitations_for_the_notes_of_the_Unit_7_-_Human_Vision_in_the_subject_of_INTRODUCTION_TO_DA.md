### Time Limitations

1. Human vision has limitations in terms of the amount of time it takes to process visual information.
2. The human eye can only process a limited amount of information at a time, and it takes time for the brain to interpret this information.
3. This means that there is a delay between the moment when visual information is received by the eye and the moment when it is processed by the brain.
4. This delay can affect the accuracy and speed of decision-making based on visual information.
5. In the context of data analytics and visualization, it is important to consider these time limitations when designing visualizations and presenting data to users.
6. Effective visualizations should present information in a clear and concise manner, allowing users to quickly understand and interpret the data.
7. This can help to minimize the impact of the time limitations of human vision and improve the effectiveness of data analysis and decision-making.
