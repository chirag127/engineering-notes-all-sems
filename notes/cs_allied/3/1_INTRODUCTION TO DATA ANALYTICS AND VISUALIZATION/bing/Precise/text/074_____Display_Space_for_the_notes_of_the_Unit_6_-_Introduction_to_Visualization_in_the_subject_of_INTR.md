### Display Space for the notes of the Unit 6 - Introduction to Visualization in the subject of INTRODUCTION TO DATA ANALYTICS AND VISUALIZATION

1. Visualization is the graphical representation of data and information.
2. It helps to communicate complex data in an easy-to-understand manner.
3. Visualization techniques include charts, graphs, maps, and diagrams.
4. The choice of visualization technique depends on the type of data and the intended audience.
5. Effective visualization requires careful consideration of design principles, such as color, scale, and layout.
6. Visualization tools and software can help to create and customize visualizations.
7. Interactive visualizations allow users to explore and manipulate data in real-time.
8. Visualization can help to identify patterns, trends, and relationships in data.
9. It can also facilitate decision-making and problem-solving.
10. The field of data visualization is constantly evolving, with new techniques and technologies being developed.
