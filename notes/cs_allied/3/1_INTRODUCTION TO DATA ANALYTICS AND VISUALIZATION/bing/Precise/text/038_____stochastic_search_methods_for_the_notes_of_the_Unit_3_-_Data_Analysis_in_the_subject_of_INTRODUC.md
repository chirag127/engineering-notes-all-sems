### Stochastic Search Methods

Stochastic search methods are optimization methods that generate and use random variables. These methods are designed for problems with inherent random noise or deterministic problems solved by injected randomness . In stochastic problems, the random variables appear in the formulation of the optimization problem itself, which involves random objective functions or random constraints .

One of the most popular stochastic search algorithms is the genetic algorithm, which is a method that mimics the evolutionary process of natural selection .

Stochastic search methods can be combined with artificial neural networks (ANNs) in two ways. The ANN can be initially trained and validated with all available experimental data, and then the optimization method is applied to find the optimum configuration of the ANN .

Stochastic search techniques form the backbone of modern machine learning and data analysis. They are used in computer systems that extract information from huge data sets (data mining), recognize patterns, perform classification, or suggest diagnoses .
