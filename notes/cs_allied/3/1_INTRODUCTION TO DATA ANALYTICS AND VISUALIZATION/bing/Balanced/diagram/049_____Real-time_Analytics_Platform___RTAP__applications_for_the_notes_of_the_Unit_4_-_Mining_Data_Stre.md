### Real-time Analytics Platform (RTAP) applications

- A real-time analytics platform (RTAP) is a system that enables organizations to process and analyze data in real-time, without any delay or latency.
- RTAPs can help organizations gain insights, make decisions, and take actions based on the current state of their data, rather than relying on historical or batch data.
- RTAPs can be used for various applications, such as:

  - Crisis management: RTAPs can help organizations respond to emergencies, disasters, or threats by providing real-time information and alerts, and enabling coordination and collaboration among stakeholders.
  - An increased company vision: RTAPs can help organizations monitor and optimize their performance, operations, and customer satisfaction by providing real-time feedback and dashboards.
  - Quicker and less costly changes: RTAPs can help organizations test and implement changes in their products, services, or processes by providing real-time results and evaluations.
  - Better apps and website management: RTAPs can help organizations improve their user experience and engagement by providing real-time analytics on user behavior, preferences, and feedback .
  - Better resource allocation and money management: RTAPs can help organizations optimize their budget, resources, and investments by providing real-time analytics on costs, revenues, and profits.

- Some examples of real-time analytics tools and platforms are:

  - Google Cloud DataFlow: A platform that enables data streaming and processing using Python SDK and Python 3, and supports filtering and analytics of real-time data.
  - Microsoft Power BI: A suite of business analytics tools that enable data visualization, reporting, and publishing of real-time data from various sources.
  - Tableau: A business intelligence platform that enables data exploration, analysis, and visualization of real-time data from various sources.
  - Qlik Sense: A machine learning platform that enables data integration, analysis, and visualization of real-time data from various sources.
  - Looker: A data platform that enables data modeling, analysis, and visualization of real-time data from various sources.
