### Exploration of Complex Information Space

- Complex information spaces are information spaces that are not only large but also heterogeneous in their structure, meaning they contain different types of data and relationships.
- Exploration of complex information spaces is the process of interacting with data in order to extract meaningful information, such as patterns, trends, outliers, or insights .
- Exploration of complex information spaces requires presentation techniques that provide intuitive orientation aids and navigation techniques, such as information visualizations, overviews, zooming, filtering, or clustering  .
- Exploration of complex information spaces also requires information technology that supports the different aspects of the exploration process, such as increasing safety, efficiency, performance, or decreasing cost.
- Exploration of complex information spaces is relevant for human space exploration, as it can help with vehicle health management, automated assembly, automated operations, and scientific discovery .
