### Navigation Links

- Navigation links are interactive elements that allow users to move from one page or section to another in a website or application.
- Navigation links can be placed in different locations, such as the header, the sidebar, the footer, or within the content itself.
- Navigation links can have different styles, such as text, icons, buttons, tabs, menus, or breadcrumbs.
- Navigation links should be clear, consistent, and intuitive, so that users can easily find the information they are looking for.
- Navigation links should also provide feedback, such as highlighting, changing color, or showing tooltips, to indicate the current or selected page or section.
- Navigation links should follow the principles of web accessibility, such as using descriptive labels, providing alternative text for images, and ensuring keyboard and screen reader compatibility.
- Navigation links can enhance the user experience and the usability of a website or application, by reducing the cognitive load, improving the navigation efficiency, and increasing the user satisfaction.
