### Analytic Process and Tools

- Analytic process is the systematic and logical approach to identify, collect, analyze, and communicate data and information for decision making and problem solving.
- Analytic tools are the software, methods, techniques, and frameworks that support the analytic process and enable data visualization, exploration, discovery, and presentation.
- Some of the steps involved in the analytic process are:
  - Define the problem or question
  - Identify the data sources and requirements
  - Collect and prepare the data
  - Explore and analyze the data
  - Interpret and communicate the results
  - Evaluate and refine the process
- Some of the tools used for the analytic process are:
  - Process analysis and modeling tools: These tools help to document, map, and understand the current and desired states of a process, and identify the inputs, outputs, resources, and activities involved. Examples are flow charts, process maps, written procedures, and work instructions  .
  - Data analysis and visualization tools: These tools help to manipulate, transform, summarize, and display data in various forms, such as tables, charts, graphs, maps, dashboards, and reports. Examples are Microsoft Power BI, Tableau, Qlik Sense, and Looker.
  - Machine learning and artificial intelligence tools: These tools help to apply advanced algorithms and techniques to learn from data, discover patterns, make predictions, and generate insights. Examples are Python, R, TensorFlow, and IBM Watson .
  - Business intelligence and reporting tools: These tools help to integrate, store, query, and analyze data from various sources, and provide interactive and self-service capabilities to users. Examples are SQL, Oracle, SAP, and Microsoft Excel.
