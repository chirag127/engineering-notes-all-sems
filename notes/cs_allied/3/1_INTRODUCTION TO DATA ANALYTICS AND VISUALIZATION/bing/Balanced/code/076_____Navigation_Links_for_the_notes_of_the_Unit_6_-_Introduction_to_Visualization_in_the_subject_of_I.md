### Navigation Links

- Navigation links are interactive elements that allow users to navigate through a data visualization, such as a dashboard, a chart, or a map.
- Navigation links can be used to:
  - Filter or highlight data based on user selection or input.
  - Drill down or up to different levels of detail or aggregation.
  - Switch between different views or perspectives of the data.
  - Zoom in or out to focus on a specific area or region of the data.
  - Provide additional information or context for the data, such as annotations, tooltips, or pop-ups.
- Navigation links can be implemented using various techniques, such as:
  - Buttons, sliders, checkboxes, radio buttons, or drop-down menus.
  - Clickable icons, images, or text labels.
  - Brushing, linking, or cross-filtering between multiple visualizations.
  - Panning, scrolling, or dragging on the visualization.
  - Hovering, tapping, or clicking on the visualization elements.
- Navigation links should be designed to:
  - Enhance the user experience and engagement with the data visualization.
  - Support the user's tasks and goals with the data visualization.
  - Be consistent, intuitive, and easy to use and understand.
  - Avoid cluttering, distracting, or confusing the user.
  - Provide feedback and guidance to the user.
