### Model Building for Data Analytics

- Model building is the fourth phase of the data analytics lifecycle, where the data science team develops and tests the analytical methods and techniques that were planned in the previous phase .
- The main steps involved in model building are :
  - Creating data sets for training, testing, and production purposes. These data sets should be representative, balanced, and consistent with the business problem and the data quality requirements.
  - Developing and applying the analytical methods and techniques, such as regression, classification, clustering, etc. to the training data set. The choice of methods and techniques depends on the type and complexity of the data, the business objectives, and the expected outcomes.
  - Evaluating and validating the performance and accuracy of the analytical methods and techniques on the testing data set. The evaluation and validation metrics should be aligned with the business goals and expectations, and should be transparent and interpretable.
  - Iterating and refining the analytical methods and techniques based on the feedback and results from the evaluation and validation step. The iteration and refinement process should aim to improve the model performance, robustness, and generalizability, as well as to address any issues or limitations identified in the previous steps.
- The output of the model building phase is a trained and tested analytical model that can be deployed to the production environment and used to generate insights and predictions for the business problem  .
