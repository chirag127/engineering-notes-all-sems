### Need for the notes of the Unit 2 - Data Analytics Lifecycle in the subject of INTRODUCTION TO DATA ANALYTICS AND VISUALIZATION

- The Data Analytics Lifecycle is a framework that guides the process of transforming data into insights and actions.
- The Data Analytics Lifecycle consists of six phases: Discovery, Data Preparation, Model Planning, Model Building, Communicate Results, and Operationalize.
- The notes of the Unit 2 - Data Analytics Lifecycle are important for the following reasons:

  - They help to understand the objectives, requirements, and constraints of a data analytics project.
  - They provide a systematic and structured approach to design, implement, and evaluate data analytics solutions.
  - They illustrate the best practices, tools, and techniques for each phase of the data analytics lifecycle.
  - They demonstrate the applications and benefits of data analytics in various domains and contexts.
  - They prepare the students for the assessment and evaluation of the subject of INTRODUCTION TO DATA ANALYTICS AND VISUALIZATION.
