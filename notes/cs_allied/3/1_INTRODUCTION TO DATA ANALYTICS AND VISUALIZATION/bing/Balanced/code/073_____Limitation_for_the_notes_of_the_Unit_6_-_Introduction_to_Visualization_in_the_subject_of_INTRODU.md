### Limitation for the notes of the Unit 6 - Introduction to Visualization in the subject of INTRODUCTION TO DATA ANALYTICS AND VISUALIZATION

- The notes are based on the textbook "Data Visualization: A Practical Introduction" by Kieran Healy and the lecture slides by the instructor.
- The notes are not a substitute for reading the textbook or attending the lectures. They are only a summary of the main concepts and techniques covered in the unit.
- The notes do not include all the examples, exercises, or code snippets from the textbook or the lectures. They are only meant to provide an overview of the topics and help the students review the key points.
- The notes do not cover all the possible types of data visualization or the tools and software available for creating them. They are only focused on the basic principles and methods of data visualization using R and ggplot2.
- The notes do not provide any guidance on how to choose the appropriate data visualization for a given data set or research question. They are only intended to introduce the students to the variety and possibilities of data visualization.
- The notes do not address the ethical, social, or political implications of data visualization. They are only concerned with the technical and aesthetic aspects of data visualization.
