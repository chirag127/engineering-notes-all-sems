### Figure Caption in Visual Interface

- A figure caption is a text that accompanies and explains a graphical element in a visual interface, such as a chart, a map, a diagram, or an image.
- A figure caption can provide additional information, context, or interpretation for the graphical element, such as its source, its purpose, its main findings, or its limitations.
- A figure caption can also guide the user's attention to specific aspects or features of the graphical element, such as highlighting key patterns, trends, outliers, or anomalies.
- A figure caption should be concise, clear, and accurate, and avoid unnecessary or redundant details that can distract or confuse the user.
- A figure caption should be placed close to the graphical element, preferably below it, and aligned with its left edge.
- A figure caption should use a smaller font size and a different color than the main text of the visual interface, to create a visual contrast and hierarchy.
- A figure caption should follow a consistent format and style throughout the visual interface, and use appropriate punctuation and grammar.
- A figure caption should be numbered if there are multiple graphical elements in the visual interface, and referenced in the main text by their numbers.
