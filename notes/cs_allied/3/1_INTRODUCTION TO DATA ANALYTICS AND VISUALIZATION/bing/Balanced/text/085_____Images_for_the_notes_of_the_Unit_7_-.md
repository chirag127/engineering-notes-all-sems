### Images for the notes of the Unit 7

- Unit 7 is about **digital image processing**, which is the use of computer algorithms to perform image processing on digital images.
- Digital image processing involves the following steps:
  - Image acquisition: capturing an image from a source, such as a camera or a scanner, and converting it into a digital format.
  - Image enhancement: improving the quality or appearance of an image, such as contrast, brightness, sharpness, noise reduction, etc.
  - Image restoration: correcting or removing distortions or degradations in an image, such as blurring, noise, artifacts, etc.
  - Image segmentation: dividing an image into meaningful regions or objects, such as edges, contours, regions of interest, etc.
  - Image representation: describing or encoding an image using features, such as pixels, vectors, matrices, histograms, etc.
  - Image compression: reducing the size or bandwidth of an image, while preserving its quality or information, such as JPEG, PNG, GIF, etc.
  - Image recognition: identifying or classifying an image or its contents, such as face recognition, object detection, optical character recognition, etc.
- Some examples of images for the notes of the Unit 7 are:

  - An image of a camera and a scanner, to illustrate the image acquisition step.
  - An image of a low-contrast and a high-contrast image, to illustrate the image enhancement step.
  - An image of a blurred and a deblurred image, to illustrate the image restoration step.
  - An image of a segmented image, with different regions or objects marked with different colors, to illustrate the image segmentation step.
  - An image of a pixel grid, a vector, a matrix, a histogram, and a feature map, to illustrate the image representation step.
  - An image of an original image and a compressed image, with their file sizes and quality measures, to illustrate the image compression step.
  - An image of a face, an object, a character, and a scene, with their labels or categories, to illustrate the image recognition step.
