### Need for the notes of the Unit 2 - Data Analytics Lifecycle in the subject of INTRODUCTION TO DATA ANALYTICS AND VISUALIZATION

- The notes of the Unit 2 - Data Analytics Lifecycle are important for understanding the process of data analysis and the steps involved in it.
- The notes cover the following topics:
  - The definition and scope of data analytics and its applications in various domains.
  - The phases of the data analytics lifecycle, such as discovery, data preparation, model planning, model building, communication of results, and operationalization.
  - The challenges and best practices of each phase of the data analytics lifecycle, such as data quality, data integration, data exploration, data modeling, data visualization, and data storytelling.
  - The tools and techniques used for each phase of the data analytics lifecycle, such as data sources, data formats, data cleaning, data transformation, data mining, data analysis, data presentation, and data deployment.
- The notes help the students to:
  - Learn the concepts and principles of data analytics and its role in decision making and problem solving.
  - Develop the skills and competencies required for performing data analysis and creating data products.
  - Apply the data analytics lifecycle to real-world scenarios and case studies.
  - Evaluate the quality and effectiveness of data analysis and data products.
  - Communicate the insights and recommendations derived from data analysis to various stakeholders.
