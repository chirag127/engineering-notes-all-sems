### Unit 7 - Human Vision in Data Analytics and Visualization

- Human vision is the process of perceiving and interpreting visual information from the environment.
- Data analytics and visualization are techniques to transform data into graphical representations that can reveal patterns, trends, and insights.
- Human vision and data visualization are closely related, as both rely on the principles of perception, cognition, and communication.
- Some of the topics covered in this unit are:

  - The characteristics of human vision, such as color, shape, size, orientation, motion, depth, and attention.
  - The challenges and limitations of human vision, such as visual illusions, biases, and noise.
  - The design principles and guidelines for effective data visualization, such as clarity, accuracy, simplicity, and aesthetics.
  - The types and functions of data visualization, such as charts, graphs, maps, diagrams, and dashboards.
  - The evaluation methods and criteria for data visualization, such as usability, utility, and user satisfaction.
  - The applications and examples of data visualization in various domains, such as business, science, education, and social media.
