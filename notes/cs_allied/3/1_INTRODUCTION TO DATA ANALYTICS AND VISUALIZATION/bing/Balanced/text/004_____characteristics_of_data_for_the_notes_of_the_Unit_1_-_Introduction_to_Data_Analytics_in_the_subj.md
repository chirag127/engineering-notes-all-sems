### Characteristics of Data

Data is any form of information that has been gathered and organized in a meaningful format that can be processed further. Data can be used for various purposes, such as analysis, decision making, communication, and visualization. Data can be classified into different types, such as numerical, categorical, ordinal, and interval. Data can also have different levels of measurement, such as nominal, ordinal, interval, and ratio.

To understand and evaluate data, it is important to consider its characteristics, such as:

- **Accuracy**: This refers to how pure and clean the data is, and how free it is from errors, mistakes, and biases . Accurate data should reflect the true state of reality and be consistent with the source of data collection.
- **Relevance**: This refers to how well the data matches the needs and requirements of the user or the purpose of the analysis . Relevant data should be useful, meaningful, and appropriate for the context and the problem at hand.
- **Consistency**: This refers to how reliable and stable the data is across different sources, systems, and time periods . Consistent data should have the same format, structure, and values, and should not contradict or conflict with other data.
- **Completeness**: This refers to how much of the data is available and how well it covers the scope and range of the analysis. Complete data should have no missing, incomplete, or outdated values, and should represent the entire population or sample of interest.
- **Uniqueness**: This refers to how distinct and different the data is from other data, and how well it avoids duplication and redundancy . Unique data should have only one record or instance for each entity or observation, and should not have any unnecessary or irrelevant information.

These characteristics of data quality can affect the validity, reliability, and usefulness of the data analysis and visualization. Therefore, it is essential to check and improve the data quality before performing any data analytics and visualization tasks.
