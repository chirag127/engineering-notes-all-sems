# Exploration of Complex Information Space

- Complex information spaces are information spaces that are not only large but also heterogeneous in their structure, meaning they contain different types of data, such as text, images, audio, video, etc.
- Exploring complex information spaces involves interacting with data in order to extract meaningful information, such as patterns, trends, outliers, relationships, etc. 
- Exploring complex information spaces requires presentation techniques that provide intuitive orientation aids and navigation techniques, such as overviews, zooming, filtering, highlighting, etc. 
- Exploring complex information spaces also requires information visualization techniques that represent data in graphical or symbolic forms, such as charts, graphs, maps, icons, etc.  
- Exploring complex information spaces can benefit from cluster analysis, which is a technique that groups data into meaningful categories based on their similarity or dissimilarity.
- Exploring complex information spaces can also benefit from information technology, which is an infrastructure technology that enables other technology and capabilities, such as vehicle health management, automated assembly, and automated operations.
- Exploring complex information spaces can have applications in various domains, such as human space exploration, scientific discovery, business intelligence, education, entertainment, etc.
