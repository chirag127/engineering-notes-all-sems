# Discovery

Discovery is the first phase of the data analytics lifecycle, which defines the roadmap of how data is generated, collected, processed, used, and analyzed to achieve business goals . In this phase, the following tasks are performed:

- Understanding the business issue or problem that needs to be solved using data analytics .
- Defining the objectives and scope of the data analytics project .
- Identifying the stakeholders and their expectations .
- Assessing the available resources, such as data sources, tools, skills, and budget .
- Formulating the initial hypotheses or questions that guide the data analysis .

The discovery phase is crucial for setting the direction and foundation of the data analytics project. It helps to clarify the business needs, align the project goals with the stakeholder expectations, and plan the data collection and analysis strategies  . The discovery phase also helps to avoid potential pitfalls, such as data quality issues, scope creep, or unrealistic expectations  .
