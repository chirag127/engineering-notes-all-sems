## Unit 5 - Frequent Itemsets and Clustering

- This unit covers two important topics in data mining: finding frequent itemsets and clustering data points.
- Frequent itemsets are sets of items that occur together frequently in a given dataset, such as transactions, documents, or web pages. They can reveal interesting patterns and associations among the data items.
- Clustering is the process of grouping data points into clusters based on their similarity or distance. It can help discover the underlying structure and categories of the data, as well as identify outliers and anomalies.
- The main objectives of this unit are:

  - To understand the concepts and applications of frequent itemsets and association rules.
  - To learn the Apriori algorithm and its variations for finding frequent itemsets efficiently.
  - To understand the concepts and applications of clustering and cluster analysis.
  - To learn the k-means algorithm and its variations for finding clusters of data points.
  - To compare and evaluate different clustering methods and measures.

  - Frequent itemsets and association rules
    - Definition and examples of frequent itemsets and association rules
    - Motivation and applications of frequent itemsets and association rules
    - Challenges and issues in finding frequent itemsets and association rules
    - Apriori algorithm and its variations for finding frequent itemsets
    - Rule generation and evaluation methods for finding association rules
  - Clustering and cluster analysis
    - Definition and examples of clustering and cluster analysis
    - Motivation and applications of clustering and cluster analysis
    - Challenges and issues in clustering and cluster analysis
    - k-means algorithm and its variations for finding clusters
    - Hierarchical clustering and its variations for finding clusters
