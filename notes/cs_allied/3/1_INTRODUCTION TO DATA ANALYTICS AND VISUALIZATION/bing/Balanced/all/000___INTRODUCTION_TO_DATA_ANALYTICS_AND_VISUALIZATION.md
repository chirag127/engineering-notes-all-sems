# Introduction to Data Analytics and Visualization

- Data analytics is the process of collecting, organizing, analyzing, and interpreting data to gain insights and support decision-making.
- Data visualization is the presentation of data in a graphical or pictorial format, using charts, graphs, maps, and other visual elements to communicate information effectively and efficiently.
- Data analytics and visualization are interrelated and complementary skills that can help businesses and organizations to understand their data, identify patterns and trends, and communicate their findings to various audiences.
- Data analytics and visualization can be applied to various domains and industries, such as marketing, finance, healthcare, education, sports, and social media.
- Data analytics and visualization require the use of various tools and techniques, such as statistical methods, programming languages, software applications, and design principles.
- Data analytics and visualization also involve following best practices and ethical standards, such as ensuring data quality, accuracy, and validity, respecting data privacy and security, and avoiding bias and distortion.
