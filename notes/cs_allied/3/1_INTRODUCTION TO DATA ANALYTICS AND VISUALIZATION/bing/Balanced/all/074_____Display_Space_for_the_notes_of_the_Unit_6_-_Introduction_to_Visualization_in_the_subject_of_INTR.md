# Unit 6 - Introduction to Visualization

- Visualization is the process of creating and displaying graphical representations of data or information.
- Visualization can help to explore, analyze, communicate, and understand data in various domains and contexts.
- Visualization can be classified into different types based on the purpose, the data, the audience, and the technique.
- Some common types of visualization are:

  - **Charts**: graphical displays of quantitative or categorical data, such as bar charts, pie charts, line charts, scatter plots, etc.
  - **Maps**: graphical displays of spatial or geographic data, such as choropleth maps, heat maps, point maps, etc.
  - **Diagrams**: graphical displays of abstract or conceptual data, such as flowcharts, network diagrams, tree diagrams, etc.
  - **Infographics**: graphical displays of complex or multidimensional data, such as timelines, dashboards, icons, etc.
  - **Artistic visualizations**: graphical displays of aesthetic or expressive data, such as paintings, sculptures, animations, etc.

- Visualization can be created and viewed using various tools and platforms, such as software applications, web browsers, mobile devices, etc.
- Visualization can be evaluated and improved based on various criteria, such as accuracy, clarity, aesthetics, interactivity, etc.
- Visualization can be influenced by various factors, such as data quality, design principles, cognitive processes, ethical issues, etc.
