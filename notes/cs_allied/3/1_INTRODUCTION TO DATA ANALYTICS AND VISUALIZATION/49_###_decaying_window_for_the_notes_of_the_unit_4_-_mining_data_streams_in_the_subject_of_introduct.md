Decaying Window in Data Stream Mining:
- Decaying window is a technique used in data stream mining to handle the problem of data aging in data streams.
- In data stream mining, data is analyzed in real-time as it is generated, and the data is constantly changing and evolving.
- Decaying window is used to maintain a moving window of the most recent data, while discarding older data that may no longer be relevant.
- The size of the window can be adjusted based on the rate of data generation and the desired level of accuracy.
- Decaying window is used in a variety of applications, including:
  1. Fraud detection: identifying fraudulent transactions based on patterns and anomalies in the data.
  2. Anomaly detection: identifying unusual or unexpected patterns in the data.
  3. Predictive modeling: making predictions about future values based on past data.
- Understanding decaying window is important for the analysis and interpretation of data streams and for the development of data-driven solutions to real-world problems.
