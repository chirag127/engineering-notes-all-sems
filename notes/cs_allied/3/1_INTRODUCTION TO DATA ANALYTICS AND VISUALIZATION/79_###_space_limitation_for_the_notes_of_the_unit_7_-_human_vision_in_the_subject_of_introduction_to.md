Space Limitation

Space limitation refers to the concept that the human visual system has a limited capacity to process and perceive information. This means that the amount of information that can be displayed in a single visual representation is limited by the capacity of the human visual system.

For example, when displaying a large dataset, it may not be possible to display all of the data points in a single visual representation. Instead, a subset of the data may need to be displayed, or the data may need to be aggregated in some way to reduce the amount of information that needs to be processed.

The space limitation of the human visual system can also impact the design of visual representations. For example, visual representations that are too complex or that contain too much information may be difficult for the human visual system to process, and they may not effectively communicate the intended message.

In conclusion, space limitation refers to the concept that the human visual system has a limited capacity to process and perceive information. Understanding space limitation is important for effective data visualization, as it can impact the design of visual representations and the amount of information that can be displayed in a single visual representation.
