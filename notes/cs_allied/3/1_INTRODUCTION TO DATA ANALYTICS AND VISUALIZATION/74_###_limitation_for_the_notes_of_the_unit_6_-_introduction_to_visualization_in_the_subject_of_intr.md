### Limitation for the notes of the Unit 6 - Introduction to Visualization in the subject of INTRODUCTION TO DATA ANALYTICS AND VISUALIZATION

Data visualization has several limitations that impact the effectiveness and accuracy of visual representations of data.

1. Definition: Data visualization is the process of creating visual representations of data to make it easier to understand and analyze. Despite its benefits, data visualization also has several limitations that impact the effectiveness and accuracy of visual representations of data.

2. Limitations of Data: One of the main limitations of data visualization is the limitations of the data itself. This includes limitations in the quality, accuracy, and completeness of the data, which can impact the accuracy of the visual representation.

3. Limitations of Visualization Techniques: Another limitation of data visualization is the limitations of the visualization techniques themselves. This includes limitations in the ability of the visualization techniques to effectively represent complex data, limitations in the ability to effectively compare multiple variables, and limitations in the ability to effectively represent large amounts of data.

4. Limitations of the Human Perception: Another limitation of data visualization is the limitations of human perception. This includes limitations in the ability of humans to accurately perceive and interpret visual representations of data, limitations in the ability to accurately compare multiple variables, and limitations in the ability to accurately perceive patterns and trends in the data.

5. Limitations of the Viewer: Another limitation of data visualization is the limitations of the viewer. This includes limitations in the viewer’s knowledge and experience, limitations in the viewer’s ability to understand and interpret the visual representation, and limitations in the viewer’s ability to effectively use the visual representation to make decisions.

6. Limitations of Technology: Another limitation of data visualization is the limitations of technology. This includes limitations in the ability of technology to effectively represent complex data, limitations in the ability of technology to effectively handle large amounts of data, and limitations in the ability of technology to effectively visualize data in real-time.

7. Advantages: Despite its limitations, data visualization also has several advantages, including the ability to effectively represent complex data, the ability to effectively compare multiple variables, and the ability to effectively represent large amounts of data.

In conclusion, data visualization has several limitations that impact the effectiveness and accuracy of visual representations of data. These limitations include limitations of the data itself, limitations of the visualization techniques, limitations of human perception, limitations of the viewer, and limitations of technology. Despite its limitations, data visualization also has several advantages, including the ability to effectively represent complex data and compare multiple variables.
