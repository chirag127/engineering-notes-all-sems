Estimating Moments in Data Streams:
- Moments are statistical measures that describe the properties of a data set.
- In data stream mining, moments are used to summarize and describe the properties of data that are generated in real-time.
- Common moments include mean, variance, skewness, and kurtosis.
- Estimating moments in data streams is challenging due to the high volume and velocity of data, as well as the need for real-time processing.
- A variety of algorithms have been developed for estimating moments in data streams, including:
  1. Online algorithms: algorithms that incrementally update the estimates of moments as new data arrives.
  2. Sketch-based algorithms: algorithms that use random sampling and aggregation to reduce the volume of data that needs to be processed.
- Understanding the estimation of moments in data streams is important for the analysis and interpretation of real-time data and for the development of data-driven solutions to real-world problems.
