### Data representation for the notes of the Unit 6 - Introduction to Visualization in the subject of INTRODUCTION TO DATA ANALYTICS AND VISUALIZATION

Data Representation in Visualization:
- Data representation is the process of transforming data into a visual format for analysis and interpretation.
- Data representation can be performed using a variety of techniques and tools, including:
  1. Bar charts: used to represent data as bars of different heights or lengths.
  2. Line charts: used to represent data as a series of points connected by lines.
  3. Scatter plots: used to represent data as a collection of points in two-dimensional space.
  4. Heat maps: used to represent data as a matrix of colored cells.
- Data representation can be performed using a variety of tools and technologies, including Tableau, D3.js, and matplotlib.
- Understanding data representation is important for the analysis and interpretation of data and for the development of data-driven solutions to real-world problems.
