Frequent Pattern-Based Clustering Methods

Frequent pattern-based clustering methods are a type of clustering method that use frequent patterns in data to group similar data points into clusters. The goal of frequent pattern-based clustering methods is to identify patterns in data that are common to many data points, and to use these patterns to group similar data points into clusters.

Frequent pattern-based clustering methods are often used in market basket analysis, where the goal is to identify groups of items that are frequently purchased together. For example, frequent pattern-based clustering methods can be used to identify groups of customers who frequently purchase the same items, and to understand the relationships between different items.

There are several frequent pattern-based clustering methods, including association rule-based clustering, frequent itemset-based clustering, and sequential pattern-based clustering. Association rule-based clustering uses association rules, which are rules that describe the relationships between items, to group similar data points into clusters. Frequent itemset-based clustering uses frequent itemsets, which are sets of items that are frequently purchased together, to group similar data points into clusters. Sequential pattern-based clustering uses sequential patterns, which are patterns in the order in which items are purchased, to group similar data points into clusters.

In conclusion, frequent pattern-based clustering methods are a type of clustering method that use frequent patterns in data to group similar data points into clusters.
