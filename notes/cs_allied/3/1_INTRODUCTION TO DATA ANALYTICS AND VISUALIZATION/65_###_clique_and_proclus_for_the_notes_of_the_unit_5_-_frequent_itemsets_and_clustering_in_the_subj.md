- CLIQUE and Proclus are algorithms used in data analytics for the discovery of frequent item sets and clusters in transaction data.
- CLIQUE is a density-based algorithm for finding dense clusters in a data set.
- Proclus is a density-based algorithm for finding clusters in a data set that are not necessarily dense.
- CLIQUE and Proclus can be used for a variety of tasks, including:
  1. Customer segmentation: the grouping of customers into similar groups based on their purchasing behavior.
  2. Recommendation systems: the generation of product recommendations for customers based on their purchasing history.
  3. Inventory management: the optimization of inventory levels based on demand patterns and trends.
- CLIQUE and Proclus can be used in combination with other algorithms, such as association rule mining, to improve the accuracy and robustness of data analytics models.
