### Design for the notes of the Unit 7 - Human Vision in the subject of INTRODUCTION TO DATA ANALYTICS AND VISUALIZATION

Design is the process of creating visual representations of data and information to communicate insights and support decision-making.

1. Definition: Design is the process of creating visual representations of data and information to communicate insights and support decision-making. Design is an important aspect of data visualization and involves combining data, design principles, and human perception to create effective and meaningful visualizations.

2. Key Elements: The key elements of design include color, typography, composition, and hierarchy. Color is used to convey information and create visual interest, typography is used to communicate the structure and hierarchy of the data, composition is used to arrange the elements on the page, and hierarchy is used to organize the information and create a visual hierarchy.

3. Color: Color is an important element of design and is used to convey information and create visual interest. Color can be used to distinguish between different categories of data, highlight important information, and create a visual hierarchy.

4. Typography: Typography is another important element of design and is used to communicate the structure and hierarchy of the data. Typography involves the use of font size, font style, and font weight to create a visual hierarchy and communicate the structure of the data.

5. Composition: Composition is the arrangement of elements on the page and is an important aspect of design. Composition involves creating a visual balance and hierarchy, as well as guiding the viewer's eye through the visual representation.

6. Hierarchy: Hierarchy is the organization of information and is an important aspect of design. Hierarchy involves creating a visual hierarchy of information, with the most important information being emphasized and the less important information being de-emphasized.

7. Advantages: Design is an important aspect of data visualization and has several advantages, including the ability to communicate insights and support decision-making, the ability to create visual interest and engagement, and the ability to create effective and meaningful visualizations.

8. Limitations: Despite its advantages, design also has several limitations, including the difficulty of ensuring the accuracy of the visual representation, the difficulty of ensuring the scalability of the design, and the difficulty of ensuring the interpretability of the visual representation.

9. Applications: Design is used in a wide range of applications in data visualization, including business intelligence, customer analytics, and financial analytics. Design is also used in fields such as computer science, engineering, and artificial intelligence.
