### characteristics of data for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Data Analytics in the subject of INTRODUCTION TO DATA ANALYTICS AND VISUALIZATION

Characteristics of Data:
- Data can have various characteristics that describe its properties and quality.
- The characteristics of data can affect the type of analysis that can be performed, the accuracy of the results, and the interpretation of the results.
- Some of the key characteristics of data include:
  1. Variables: the data elements that describe the objects or subjects being analyzed.
  2. Types of variables: categorical, ordinal, interval, and ratio.
  3. Distribution: the pattern of values for a particular variable.
  4. Central tendency: the central value of a set of data, such as mean, median, and mode.
  5. Variability: the spread of values for a particular variable, such as range and standard deviation.
  6. Outliers: values that are significantly different from the majority of the data.
- Understanding the characteristics of data is important for the preparation and analysis of data and for the interpretation of the results.
