Unit 1 of Introduction in the subject of Natural Language Processing may cover the topic of Issues in Part-of-Speech (PoS) Tagging. PoS Tagging is the process of assigning a grammatical category to each word in a sentence, such as noun, verb, adjective, and adverb.

3. Ambiguity in PoS Tagging: An overview of the different types of ambiguity that can occur in PoS Tagging, including homonymy, polysemy, and context-dependent ambiguity, among others.

4. PoS Tagging Accuracy: An overview of the different factors that can affect PoS Tagging accuracy, including data quality, training data size, and model complexity, among others.

5. PoS Tagging and Named Entity Recognition: An overview of the relationship between PoS Tagging and Named Entity Recognition, including how PoS Tagging can be used to improve Named Entity Recognition accuracy.

6. PoS Tagging and Parsing: An overview of the relationship between PoS Tagging and Parsing, including how PoS Tagging can be used to improve Parsing accuracy.
