### English Morphology for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of NATURAL LANGUAGE PROCESSING

Unit 1 of Introduction in the subject of Natural Language Processing focuses on the topic of English Morphology. English Morphology is the study of the structure and form of words in the English language.

The following topics may be covered in this unit:

1. Introduction to English Morphology: An overview of the concept of English Morphology and its importance in Natural Language Processing.

2. Word Formation: An introduction to the different methods of word formation, including inflection, derivation, and compounding, among others.

3. Parts of Speech: An overview of the different parts of speech in the English language, including nouns, verbs, adjectives, and adverbs, among others.

4. Inflection: An introduction to inflection, which is the process of adding affixes to words to change their grammatical properties.

5. Derivation: An overview of derivation, which is the process of forming new words from existing words by adding affixes.

6. Compounding: An introduction to compounding, which is the process of forming new words by combining two or more existing words.

7. Morphological Analysis: An overview of morphological analysis, which is the process of analyzing the structure and form of words in the English language.

8. Tools and Techniques for English Morphology: An introduction to the different tools and techniques used for English Morphology, including morphological analyzers, part-of-speech taggers, and lemmatizers, among others.

In conclusion, Unit 1 of Introduction in the subject of Natural Language Processing provides a comprehensive overview of English Morphology, including its methods, techniques, and applications. This knowledge is essential for understanding the structure and form of words in the English language and for developing effective Natural Language Processing models.
