### WORD LEVEL ANALYSIS

In Natural Language Processing, Word Level Analysis is the process of breaking down a sentence into individual words and analyzing them based on their syntactic and semantic properties. Here are some important points to keep in mind when studying Word Level Analysis:

- **Tokenization:** The first step in Word Level Analysis is tokenization, which involves splitting a sentence into individual words or tokens. This is usually done using whitespace as a delimiter, but more sophisticated methods can also be used.
- **Part-of-Speech Tagging:** Once the sentence has been tokenized, the next step is to identify the part of speech of each word. This is known as Part-of-Speech (POS) tagging. For example, a word might be classified as a noun, verb, adjective, etc.
- **Morphological Analysis:** Morphological Analysis involves identifying the structure and properties of words. This can include identifying the base form of a word (e.g. "play" for "playing"), identifying prefixes and suffixes, and determining the tense, aspect, and mood of a verb.
- **Named Entity Recognition:** Named Entity Recognition (NER) is the process of identifying and classifying named entities in a sentence, such as people, organizations, and locations. This is important for many NLP tasks, such as information extraction and question answering.
- **Word Sense Disambiguation:** Word Sense Disambiguation (WSD) is the process of determining the correct meaning of a word in context. This is important because many words have multiple meanings, and the correct meaning may depend on the surrounding words and context.
- **Lexical Semantics:** Finally, Lexical Semantics involves analyzing the meaning of individual words and how they relate to each other. This includes identifying synonyms, antonyms, and other relationships between words.

By studying Word Level Analysis in Natural Language Processing, you can gain a deeper understanding of how language works and how it can be analyzed and processed by computers.
