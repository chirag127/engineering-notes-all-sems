### Unsmoothed N-grams for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of NATURAL LANGUAGE PROCESSING

When we talk about Natural Language Processing, one of the important concepts is N-grams. An N-gram is a contiguous sequence of n items from a given sample of text or speech. In this section, we will discuss the concept of Unsmoothed N-grams and their significance in NLP.

Here are some important points to understand Unsmoothed N-grams:

- Unsmoothed N-grams are the simplest form of N-grams. They count the frequency of each N-gram directly in the text without any smoothing or adjustment. 

- The probability of a word sequence in Unsmoothed N-grams is just the ratio of the number of times the sequence appears to the total number of N-grams in the text. 

- Unsmoothed N-grams suffer from the sparsity problem. When the size of N-grams increases, the number of unique N-grams in the text increases exponentially. This leads to a situation where many N-grams have a frequency of zero, which makes it difficult to estimate the probability of unseen N-grams.

- Unsmoothed N-grams are often used as a baseline in NLP tasks because they are simple and easy to compute. They are also useful for comparing the performance of other smoothing techniques.

- Unsmoothed N-grams can be used in various NLP applications, such as language modeling, speech recognition, machine translation, and text classification.

In conclusion, Unsmoothed N-grams are an important concept in Natural Language Processing. They are the simplest form of N-grams and can be used as a baseline for more sophisticated smoothing techniques. Understanding Unsmoothed N-grams is essential for anyone working with NLP.
