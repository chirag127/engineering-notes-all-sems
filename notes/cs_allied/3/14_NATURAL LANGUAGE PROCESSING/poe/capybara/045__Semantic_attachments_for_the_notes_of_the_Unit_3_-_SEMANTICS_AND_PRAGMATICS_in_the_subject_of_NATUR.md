### Semantic Attachments for the Notes of the Unit 3 - Semantics and Pragmatics in the Subject of Natural Language Processing

In the field of Natural Language Processing, semantics and pragmatics play a crucial role in understanding the meaning of language. The following are some of the semantic attachments that are used to represent the meaning of words and sentences:

1. Lexical Semantic Attachments: These are used to represent the meaning of individual words. They include:

   - WordNet: WordNet is a lexical database that groups English words into sets of synonyms called synsets. Each synset represents a distinct concept and contains a list of words that are semantically related.
   
   - FrameNet: FrameNet is a lexical database that groups words into frames, which represent a set of semantic roles and their relationships to each other. Frames provide a way to represent the meaning of a word in terms of its typical usage in language.
   
   - VerbNet: VerbNet is a lexical database that groups English verbs into classes based on their syntactic and semantic properties. Each class represents a set of verbs that share similar argument structures and semantic roles.
   
2. Syntactic Semantic Attachments: These are used to represent the meaning of sentences in terms of their syntactic structure. They include:

   - Semantic Role Labeling: Semantic Role Labeling (SRL) is the process of identifying the semantic roles played by the arguments of a sentence. These roles include agent, patient, theme, location, and time, among others.
   
   - Dependency Parsing: Dependency Parsing is the process of identifying the syntactic relationships between words in a sentence. These relationships are represented as directed edges between words in a dependency tree.
   
3. Discourse Semantic Attachments: These are used to represent the meaning of discourse in terms of its structure and coherence. They include:

   - Discourse Analysis: Discourse Analysis is the process of analyzing the structure and coherence of discourse. This includes identifying the topic, theme, and discourse markers used to connect sentences and paragraphs.
   
   - Coreference Resolution: Coreference Resolution is the process of identifying the referents of pronouns and other referring expressions in a discourse. This is important for maintaining coherence and understanding the relationships between entities in a text.
   
In conclusion, semantic attachments are important tools for representing the meaning of language in Natural Language Processing. They provide a way to capture the various aspects of meaning, from individual words to entire discourses.
