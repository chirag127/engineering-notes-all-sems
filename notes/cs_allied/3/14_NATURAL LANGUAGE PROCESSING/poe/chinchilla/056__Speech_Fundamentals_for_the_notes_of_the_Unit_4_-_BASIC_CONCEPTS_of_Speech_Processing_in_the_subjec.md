### Speech Fundamentals for the notes of the Unit 4 - BASIC CONCEPTS of Speech Processing in the subject of NATURAL LANGUAGE PROCESSING

Speech is the most common form of communication used by humans. It is the process of producing and interpreting vocal sounds that convey meaning. The study of speech processing is a crucial aspect of natural language processing, which deals with the analysis and understanding of human language.

Here are some fundamental concepts related to speech processing that are important to understand:

1. Phonetics: Phonetics is the study of the physical properties of speech sounds. It deals with the production, transmission, and perception of sounds in language. Phonetics is divided into three branches: articulatory, acoustic, and auditory.

2. Phonology: Phonology is the study of how sounds are organized and used in languages. It deals with the patterns of sounds in a language, including the rules for combining sounds and the way they change in different contexts.

3. Prosody: Prosody refers to the patterns of stress, intonation, and rhythm in speech. It is an important aspect of speech that conveys meaning beyond the individual words.

4. Speech recognition: Speech recognition is the process of converting spoken language into text or commands that a computer can understand. It involves the use of algorithms and machine learning techniques to analyze speech and extract meaning from it.

5. Speech synthesis: Speech synthesis is the process of generating speech from written text or other input. It involves the use of algorithms and techniques to produce natural-sounding speech that can be used in various applications, such as voice assistants and text-to-speech systems.

6. Speech coding: Speech coding is the process of compressing speech signals for efficient transmission or storage. It involves the use of algorithms and techniques to reduce the amount of data needed to represent speech while maintaining its quality.

7. Speech enhancement: Speech enhancement is the process of improving the quality and intelligibility of speech signals that have been degraded by noise or other factors. It involves the use of algorithms and techniques to remove or reduce unwanted noise and enhance the speech signal.

In conclusion, speech processing is a complex and multi-disciplinary field that involves the study of various aspects of speech, including phonetics, phonology, prosody, speech recognition, speech synthesis, speech coding, and speech enhancement. Understanding these fundamental concepts is essential for anyone interested in natural language processing and speech technology.
