### Review Of Digital Signal Processing Concepts for the notes of the Unit 4 - BASIC CONCEPTS of Speech Processing in the subject of NATURAL LANGUAGE PROCESSING

In this review, we will cover the fundamental concepts of Digital Signal Processing (DSP) that are essential to understanding the basics of Speech Processing. DSP is an important branch of signal processing that deals with the manipulation of signals that are represented as a sequence of numbers.

#### What is Digital Signal Processing?

Digital Signal Processing is the process of analyzing and manipulating digital signals to extract useful information from them. Digital signals are sampled and quantized versions of continuous analog signals. DSP algorithms are used to process these digital signals for various applications such as audio and image processing, speech recognition, and telecommunications.

#### Sampling and Quantization

Sampling is the process of converting a continuous-time signal into a discrete-time signal by taking samples at regular intervals. Quantization is the process of mapping each sample to a discrete value. The number of bits used to represent each sample determines the resolution of the quantization.

#### Fourier Transform

Fourier Transform is a mathematical tool used to analyze signals in the frequency domain. It decomposes a signal into its constituent frequencies. The Discrete Fourier Transform (DFT) is a digital implementation of the Fourier Transform and is used extensively in DSP.

#### Filtering

Filtering is the process of removing unwanted components from a signal. Low-pass filters allow only low frequencies to pass through while high-pass filters allow only high frequencies to pass through. Band-pass filters allow only a specific range of frequencies to pass through.

#### Time-Frequency Analysis

Time-Frequency Analysis is used to analyze signals in both the time and frequency domains. The Short-Time Fourier Transform (STFT) is a widely used time-frequency analysis tool that divides a signal into short segments and then applies the Fourier Transform to each segment.

#### Conclusion

In this review, we covered the fundamental concepts of Digital Signal Processing (DSP) that are essential to understanding Speech Processing. Sampling and quantization, Fourier Transform, filtering, and time-frequency analysis are some of the key concepts in DSP. These concepts play a crucial role in various applications such as speech recognition and telecommunications.
