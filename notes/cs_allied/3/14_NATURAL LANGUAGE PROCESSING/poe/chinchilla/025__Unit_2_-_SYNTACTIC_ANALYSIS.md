## Unit 2 - SYNTACTIC ANALYSIS

Syntactic analysis, also known as parsing, is a fundamental process in natural language processing. It is the process of analyzing the grammatical structure of a sentence to determine its meaning. Syntactic analysis involves the use of formal grammar rules to identify the relationship between words in a sentence and their roles in the sentence structure.
