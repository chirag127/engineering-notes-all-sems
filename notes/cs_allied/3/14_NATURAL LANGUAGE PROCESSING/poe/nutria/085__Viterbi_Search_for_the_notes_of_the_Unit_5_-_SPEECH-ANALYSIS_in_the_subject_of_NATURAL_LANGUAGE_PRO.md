
### Viterbi Search for the notes of the Unit 5 - SPEECH-ANALYSIS in the subject of NATURAL LANGUAGE PROCESSING

* Viterbi search is a technique used to determine the most likely sequence of states in a Hidden Markov Model (HMM).
* It is a dynamic programming algorithm that uses a trellis structure to keep track of the most likely paths through a sequence of observations.
* The algorithm begins with an initial state and then iteratively updates the probabilities of the most likely paths through the sequence of observations.
* The algorithm can be used to analyze speech signals in order to recognize words, phrases, and other patterns.
* The Viterbi search algorithm can be used to identify the most likely sequence of states in a HMM given a sequence of observations.
* The algorithm is used in many applications such as speech recognition, natural language processing, and bioinformatics.
