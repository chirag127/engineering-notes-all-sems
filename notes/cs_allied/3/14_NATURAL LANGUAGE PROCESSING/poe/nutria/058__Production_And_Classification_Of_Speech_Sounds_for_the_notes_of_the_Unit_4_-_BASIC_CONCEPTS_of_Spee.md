
### Production and Classification of Speech Sounds

- Speech sounds are produced by a combination of air being expelled from the lungs, vibration of the vocal folds, and the shape of the vocal tract. 
- Speech sounds can be classified into various categories, such as vowels, consonants, and diphthongs. 
- Vowels are produced by the shape of the vocal tract, which allows the air to flow freely. 
- Consonants are produced by the shape of the vocal tract, which constricts the air flow and creates a sound. 
- Diphthongs are combinations of two vowel sounds, and can be produced by rapidly changing the shape of the vocal tract. 
- Speech sounds can also be classified according to their manner of articulation, such as plosives, fricatives, nasals, and affricates. 
- Plosives are produced by completely blocking the air flow and then releasing it. 
- Fricatives are produced by partially blocking the air flow and creating a turbulent sound. 
- Nasals are produced by allowing the air to flow through the nose. 
- Affricates are a combination of a plosive and a fricative. 
- Speech sounds can also be classified according to their place of articulation, such as bilabial, labiodental, alveolar, palatal, velar, and glottal. 
- Bilabial sounds are produced by using both lips. 
- Labiodental sounds are produced by using the lower lip and the upper teeth. 
- Alveolar sounds are produced by using the tip of the tongue and the alveolar ridge. 
- Palatal sounds are produced by using the middle of the tongue and the hard palate. 
- Velar sounds are produced by using the back of the tongue and the soft palate. 
- Glottal sounds are produced by using the vocal folds.
