
### Minimum Edit Distance 

Minimum Edit Distance (MED) is a metric used to measure the similarity between two strings of characters. It is widely used in Natural Language Processing (NLP) to compare words or phrases in order to determine the most likely meaning. MED is based on the Levenshtein Distance algorithm, which calculates the minimum number of operations (insertion, deletion, substitution) required to transform one string into another. 

MED is typically used in NLP to compare two strings and determine the most likely meaning, based on the number of operations required to transform one string into another. This can be used to compare words or phrases in different languages, or to find the closest match to a given string. 

MED can also be used to identify typos, correct spelling errors, and detect plagiarism. By comparing two strings, MED can determine the probability that one is a copy of the other. It can also be used to measure the similarity between two documents, or to compare two versions of the same document.
