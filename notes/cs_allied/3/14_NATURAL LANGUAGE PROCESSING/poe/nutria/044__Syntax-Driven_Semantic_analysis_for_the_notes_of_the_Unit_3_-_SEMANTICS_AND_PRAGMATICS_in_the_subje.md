
### Syntax-Driven Semantic Analysis for Unit 3 - Semantics and Pragmatics in Natural Language Processing

* Syntax-driven semantic analysis is a process of understanding the meaning of a sentence by analyzing its syntactic structure.
* This process is used in natural language processing (NLP) to understand the meaning of a sentence in a given context.
* Syntactic analysis involves analyzing the structure of a sentence by identifying its components, such as nouns, verbs, adjectives, adverbs, and other parts of speech.
* Syntactic analysis is used to identify the relationships between words in a sentence and to determine the intended meaning of the sentence.
* Semantic analysis is the process of understanding the meaning of a sentence by analyzing its context and the relationships between words.
* Semantic analysis is used to identify the relationships between words and to determine the intended meaning of the sentence.
* Pragmatics is the study of how language is used in different contexts.
* Pragmatic analysis is used to identify the intended meaning of a sentence by considering its context and the relationships between words.
* Natural language processing (NLP) combines syntactic, semantic, and pragmatic analysis to understand the meaning of a sentence in a given context.
