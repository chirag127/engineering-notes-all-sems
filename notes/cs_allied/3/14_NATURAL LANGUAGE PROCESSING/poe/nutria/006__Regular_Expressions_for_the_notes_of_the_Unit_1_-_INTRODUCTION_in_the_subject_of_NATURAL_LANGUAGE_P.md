
### Regular Expressions for the Notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the Subject of Natural Language Processing

* Regular expressions are a powerful tool for pattern matching and text manipulation. 
* They are used in many programming languages, as well as in natural language processing (NLP) to identify patterns in text. 
* Regular expressions are composed of characters, which can be literal (e.g. `a`) or special characters (e.g. `*`). 
* Special characters are used to represent a range of characters, such as `[a-z]` for all lowercase letters, or `[0-9]` for all digits. 
* Regular expressions can also be used to match specific patterns, such as `\d+` to match one or more digits, or `\w+` to match one or more alphanumeric characters. 
* Regular expressions can also be used to replace text, such as replacing all digits with an asterisk (`\d` → `*`). 
* Regular expressions can be used to perform a variety of tasks, such as tokenization, part-of-speech tagging, and named entity recognition. 
* Regular expressions can also be used to search for specific patterns in text, such as finding all emails in a document.
