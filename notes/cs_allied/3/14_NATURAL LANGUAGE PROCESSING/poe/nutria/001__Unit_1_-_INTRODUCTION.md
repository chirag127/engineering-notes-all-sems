
## Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION

1. Introduction is the first step in any educational course. It provides the context and background information that will help the student understand the material that follows. 
2. It is important to have a clear understanding of the objectives of the course and the topics that will be covered. 
3. Introduction also outlines the expectations for the student, such as the timeline and assignments that will be required. 
4. It is also important to identify any prerequisites that the student may need to complete before beginning the course. 
5. Finally, introduction should provide a clear outline of the topics that the student will be expected to master by the end of the course.
