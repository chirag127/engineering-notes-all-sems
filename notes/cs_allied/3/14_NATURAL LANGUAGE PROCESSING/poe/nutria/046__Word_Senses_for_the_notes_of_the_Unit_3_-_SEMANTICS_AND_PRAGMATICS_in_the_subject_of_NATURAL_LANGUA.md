
### Word Senses

* Word senses refer to the different meanings of a word. For example, the word "bank" can mean a financial institution, the edge of a river, or the act of leaning.

* Word senses can be organized into a hierarchy. At the top level, there is a single sense for the word. Lower levels contain more specific senses.

* Semantic ambiguity occurs when a word has multiple meanings. For example, the phrase "I saw a bank" could refer to a financial institution or the edge of a river.

* Pragmatics is the study of how context influences the meaning of words. In the phrase "I saw a bank", the context can help determine which sense of the word "bank" is being used.

* Natural language processing (NLP) systems use word senses to better understand the meaning of text. For example, a system might use word senses to determine that "I saw a bank" is referring to a financial institution.
