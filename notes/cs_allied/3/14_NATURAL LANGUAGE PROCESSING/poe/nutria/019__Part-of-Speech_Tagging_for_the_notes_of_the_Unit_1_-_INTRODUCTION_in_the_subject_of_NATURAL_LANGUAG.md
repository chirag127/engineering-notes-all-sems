
### Part-of-Speech Tagging for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of NATURAL LANGUAGE PROCESSING

* Part-of-Speech (POS) tagging is an essential task in Natural Language Processing (NLP) that assigns a specific part of speech to each word in a sentence.
* POS tagging is used to identify the syntactic structure of sentences and to determine the correct meaning of words in a given context.
* The most common tagging schemes are based on the Penn Treebank tag set, which includes 36 distinct tags.
* POS tagging is used in a variety of applications, such as Machine Translation, Text Summarization, Information Extraction, and Question Answering.
* The most popular algorithms for POS tagging are Hidden Markov Models (HMMs) and Maximum Entropy Models (MEMs).
* POS tagging is an important step in the pre-processing of text-based data in NLP tasks.
