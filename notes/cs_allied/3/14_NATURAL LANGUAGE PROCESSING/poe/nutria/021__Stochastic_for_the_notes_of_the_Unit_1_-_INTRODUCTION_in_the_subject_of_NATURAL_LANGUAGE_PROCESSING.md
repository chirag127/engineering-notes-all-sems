* In NLP, stochastic processes are used to model language, such as in speech recognition and machine translation.
* Stochastic processes are useful for modeling the uncertainty that is inherent in language.
* Stochastic processes can be used to generate new sentences or words, which can then be used in language generation tasks.
* Stochastic processes can also be used to evaluate the quality of language models, such as those used in machine translation.
