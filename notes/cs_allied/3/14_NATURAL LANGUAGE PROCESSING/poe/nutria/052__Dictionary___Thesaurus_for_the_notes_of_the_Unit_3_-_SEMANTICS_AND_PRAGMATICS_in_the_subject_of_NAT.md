
### Dictionary & Thesaurus

Dictionary and thesaurus are essential tools in Natural Language Processing (NLP). They are used to identify the meaning of words, phrases, and sentences in a given context. 

**Dictionary** 

A dictionary is a collection of words and their definitions. It can provide a comprehensive description of the meanings of words, including synonyms, antonyms, and etymologies. A dictionary can be used to look up the meaning of a word, find synonyms, and learn about how a word is used in different contexts. 

**Thesaurus**

A thesaurus is a collection of words and their related concepts. It can provide a comprehensive list of synonyms and related concepts for a given word. It can also provide a list of related words and concepts for a given phrase or sentence. Thesauri are useful for understanding the nuances of language and for expanding one's vocabulary.
