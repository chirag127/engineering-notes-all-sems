
### Dependency Grammar for the Notes of Unit 2 - Syntactic Analysis in Natural Language Processing

* Dependency Grammar is a type of syntactic analysis used in Natural Language Processing (NLP) to identify the relationship between words in a sentence.
* Dependency Grammar is based on the idea that a sentence is composed of two parts: the head (the main verb) and its dependents (other words in the sentence). 
* Dependency Grammar uses a directed graph to represent the relationship between the words in a sentence. Each word is represented as a node, and the edges between the nodes represent the relationships between the words. 
* Dependency Grammar is used to identify the syntactic structure of a sentence, which is important for understanding the meaning of the sentence. 
* Dependency Grammar is also used for language generation, where it is used to generate grammatically correct sentences from a given set of words. 
* Dependency Grammar is used in a variety of NLP tasks, such as parsing, text summarization, machine translation, and question answering.
