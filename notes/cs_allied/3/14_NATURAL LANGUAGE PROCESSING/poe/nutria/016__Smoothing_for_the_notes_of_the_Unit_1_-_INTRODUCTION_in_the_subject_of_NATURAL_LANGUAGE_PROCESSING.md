
### Smoothing for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of NATURAL LANGUAGE PROCESSING

1. Smoothing is a technique used in Natural Language Processing (NLP) to reduce the variance of a model. It is used to prevent overfitting and to improve the generalization of the model.

2. Smoothing techniques can be divided into two categories: additive smoothing and interpolation smoothing. 

3. Additive smoothing involves the addition of a constant value to all the probabilities of a model. This constant value is called the smoothing parameter.

4. Interpolation smoothing involves the addition of a parameter to the probabilities of a model. This parameter is called the interpolation parameter.

5. Smoothing techniques are used to reduce the variance of a model and to improve the generalization of the model. They are also used to prevent overfitting and to improve the accuracy of a model.
