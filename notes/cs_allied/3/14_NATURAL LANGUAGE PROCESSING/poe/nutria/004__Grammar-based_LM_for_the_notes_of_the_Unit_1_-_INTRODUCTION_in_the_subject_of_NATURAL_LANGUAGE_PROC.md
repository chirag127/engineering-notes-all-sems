
### Grammar-based LM for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of NATURAL LANGUAGE PROCESSING

1. A grammar-based language model (LM) is a type of statistical language model that uses a set of grammar rules to generate a sequence of words. 
2. These models are based on the idea that a language can be described by a set of rules that govern the structure of sentences. 
3. Grammar-based LMs are used to generate sequences of words that are grammatically correct and make sense in context. 
4. They are used in natural language processing (NLP) tasks such as machine translation, question answering, and text summarization. 
5. Grammar-based LMs are trained on large datasets of text and use algorithms such as n-grams, Markov models, and neural networks to generate sequences of words. 
6. The advantage of using a grammar-based LM is that it can generate more accurate results than a non-grammar based LM, as it can take into account the structure of sentences. 
7. Additionally, grammar-based LMs can be used to generate more natural sounding sentences, as they can generate sentences with more complex structures. 
8. Finally, grammar-based LMs can be used to generate more accurate results in tasks such as machine translation, question answering, and text summarization.
