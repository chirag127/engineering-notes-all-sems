
### Speech Distortion Measures

* Speech distortion measures are used to measure the quality of a speech signal. 
* These measures are used to identify distortions in the speech signal, such as noise, reverberations, and other types of distortion. 
* Common speech distortion measures include signal-to-noise ratio (SNR), signal-to-noise-plus-distortion ratio (SNDR), and perceptual evaluation of speech quality (PESQ). 
* SNR is a measure of the ratio of the signal power to the noise power, while SNDR is a measure of the ratio of the signal power to the sum of the noise power and distortion power. 
* PESQ is a measure of the perceived quality of a speech signal. It is based on a model of the human auditory system and is used to evaluate the quality of a speech signal in terms of intelligibility, naturalness, and overall quality. 
* These measures can be used to compare different speech signals and to determine the effect of various types of distortion on the speech signal.
