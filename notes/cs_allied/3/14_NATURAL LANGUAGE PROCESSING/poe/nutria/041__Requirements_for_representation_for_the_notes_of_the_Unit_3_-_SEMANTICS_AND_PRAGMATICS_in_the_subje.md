
### Requirements for Representation for the Notes of the Unit 3 - Semantics and Pragmatics in the Subject of Natural Language Processing

1. Semantics is concerned with the meaning of words and sentences, and how language can be used to convey meaning.
2. Pragmatics is concerned with the context in which language is used, and how meaning is derived from context.
3. Representation of language in computers requires a formal representation of the meaning and context of language.
4. Representations such as frames, semantic networks, and ontologies can be used to represent language.
5. Natural language processing systems use these representations to interpret language and generate responses.
6. Syntax is concerned with the structure of language, and how words and sentences are formed.
7. Natural language processing systems use syntactic analysis to identify the structure of language and determine the meaning of words and sentences.
