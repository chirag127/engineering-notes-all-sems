
### Detecting and Correcting Spelling Errors for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of NATURAL LANGUAGE PROCESSING

* Natural language processing (NLP) is a field of computer science that focuses on the interactions between computers and human languages.
* Spell checking is an important component of NLP, as it helps to ensure that the text is grammatically correct.
* Spell checking algorithms can detect spelling errors by comparing the text against a dictionary of correct words.
* Spell checking algorithms can also suggest corrections for misspelled words, based on the context of the sentence.
* In some cases, spell checking algorithms can also detect homonyms, which are words that have the same spelling but different meanings.
* Spell checking algorithms can be used to improve the accuracy of machine translation systems and other NLP applications.
