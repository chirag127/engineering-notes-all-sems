
## Unit 2 - SYNTACTIC ANALYSIS

1. Syntactic analysis is the process of analyzing the structure of a sentence. It involves breaking down a sentence into its different components, such as subject, verb, object, adverbial, and so on. 

2. Syntactic analysis is useful for understanding how a sentence is constructed and for determining the meaning of a sentence. It can also help identify errors in a sentence and suggest improvements.

3. Syntactic analysis is typically done by identifying the parts of speech of the words in a sentence and then analyzing the structure of the sentence. For example, in the sentence “The cat chased the mouse”, the subject is “The cat”, the verb is “chased”, and the object is “the mouse”. 

4. Syntactic analysis can also be used to identify the function of a sentence. For example, a sentence can be a statement, a question, a command, or an exclamation. 

5. Syntactic analysis is often used in natural language processing (NLP) applications, such as machine translation and text summarization. It can also be used to identify the relationships between words in a sentence and to determine the sentiment of a sentence.
