
### HMMs for the Notes of the Unit 5 - SPEECH-ANALYSIS in the Subject of NATURAL LANGUAGE PROCESSING

1. Hidden Markov Models (HMMs) are a type of probabilistic model used in Natural Language Processing (NLP) to analyze speech.

2. HMMs are used to model the probability of a sequence of observations given a sequence of states.

3. The states in an HMM are hidden, meaning that they cannot be observed directly.

4. HMMs use a set of probability distributions to estimate the probability of a sequence of observations given a sequence of states.

5. HMMs are used in speech recognition, text-to-speech synthesis, and language modeling.

6. HMMs are also used in machine translation, where they are used to model the probability of a sequence of words in one language given a sequence of words in another language.

7. HMMs are also used in speech synthesis, where they are used to model the probability of a sequence of words given a sequence of phonemes.

8. HMMs are also used in speech recognition, where they are used to model the probability of a sequence of words given a sequence of acoustic features.

9. HMMs are also used in natural language understanding, where they are used to model the probability of a sequence of words given a sequence of semantic features.
