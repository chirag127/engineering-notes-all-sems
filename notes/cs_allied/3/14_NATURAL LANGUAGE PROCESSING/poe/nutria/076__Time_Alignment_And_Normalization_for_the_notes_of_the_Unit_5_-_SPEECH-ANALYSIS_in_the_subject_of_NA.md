
### Time Alignment And Normalization 

Time alignment and normalization are important components of speech analysis in natural language processing. 

Time alignment is the process of aligning two or more sequences of data, such as audio or text, in order to compare them. This is useful for tasks such as speech recognition, where the alignment of audio and text is necessary in order to identify words. 

Normalization is the process of adjusting the data to a common scale. This is useful for tasks such as speech recognition, where the normalization of audio is necessary in order to accurately identify words. 

Time alignment and normalization are important components of speech analysis in natural language processing, as they allow for the accurate comparison of data sequences and the accurate identification of words.
