
### Feature Extraction and Pattern Comparison Techniques

* Feature extraction is the process of extracting meaningful features from a given input. In speech analysis, it involves extracting the relevant characteristics of the speech signal, such as the pitch, energy, and frequency.

* Pattern comparison techniques are used to compare two or more speech signals and determine if they are similar or different. These techniques are used to identify the speaker, detect emotion, and determine the language of the speech.

* Hidden Markov Models (HMMs) are a type of pattern comparison technique used in speech analysis. HMMs are used to detect patterns in speech signals and can be used to identify the language of the speech and detect emotion.

* Support Vector Machines (SVMs) are another type of pattern comparison technique used in speech analysis. SVMs are used to classify speech signals and can be used to identify the speaker and detect emotion.

* Neural networks are a type of pattern comparison technique used in speech analysis. Neural networks are used to detect patterns in speech signals and can be used to identify the speaker and detect emotion.
