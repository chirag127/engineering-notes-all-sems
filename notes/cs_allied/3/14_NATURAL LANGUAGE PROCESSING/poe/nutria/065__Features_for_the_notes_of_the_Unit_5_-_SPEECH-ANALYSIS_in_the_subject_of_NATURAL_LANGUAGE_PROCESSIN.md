
### Features for the notes of the Unit 5 - SPEECH-ANALYSIS in the subject of NATURAL LANGUAGE PROCESSING

1. Speech analysis is the process of breaking down a spoken language into its component parts and understanding the meaning of each part.
2. Speech analysis algorithms are used to identify the words, phrases, and other units of meaning in a spoken language.
3. Speech analysis algorithms can also be used to identify the speaker's intonation, stress, and other features of their speech.
4. Speech analysis algorithms can be used to detect emotions in a speaker's voice, such as happiness, anger, or sadness.
5. Speech analysis algorithms can be used to identify the language being spoken and to detect the dialect of the speaker.
6. Speech analysis algorithms can be used to identify the gender of the speaker and to detect accents.
7. Speech analysis algorithms can be used to identify the speaker's native language.
8. Speech analysis algorithms can be used to detect and identify speech disorders, such as stuttering and dyslexia.
