## Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION

Unit 1 of Introduction is typically the first unit in a course or subject and provides an overview of the course or subject content, objectives, and structure. It may also include the following topics:

1. Overview of the course or subject: A brief description of the course or subject, including its purpose, goals, and intended audience.

2. Course or subject objectives: A list of the objectives of the course or subject, including the skills, knowledge, and competencies that students will acquire upon completion.

3. Course or subject structure: An outline of the structure of the course or subject, including the units, topics, and assessments.

4. Course or subject resources: A list of the resources available to students, including textbooks, online resources, and support services.

5. Introduction to the instructor: A brief introduction to the instructor, including their background, experience, and contact information.

In conclusion, Unit 1 of Introduction provides an overview of the course or subject and its objectives, structure, and resources. This information is essential for students to understand the content and expectations of the course or subject and to plan their learning accordingly.
