### WORD LEVEL ANALYSIS for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of NATURAL LANGUAGE PROCESSING

Word level analysis is a type of natural language processing (NLP) that focuses on the analysis of individual words and their relationships within a sentence. This includes tasks such as part-of-speech (POS) tagging, named entity recognition (NER), and word sense disambiguation.

Components of Word Level Analysis:

1. Part-of-Speech (POS) Tagging: POS tagging is the process of classifying words into their grammatical categories, such as nouns, verbs, and adjectives.

2. Named Entity Recognition (NER): NER is the process of identifying and classifying named entities, such as people, organizations, and locations, in a text.

3. Word Sense Disambiguation: Word sense disambiguation is the process of determining the correct meaning of a word in a given context.

Applications of Word Level Analysis:

1. Text Classification: Word level analysis is used in text classification, such as sentiment analysis and topic classification, to identify the meaning and sentiment of a text.

2. Information Retrieval: Word level analysis is used in information retrieval, such as search engines and recommendation systems, to improve the accuracy and relevance of search results.

3. Machine Translation: Word level analysis is used in machine translation to improve the accuracy and fluency of translations, by taking into account the meaning and context of words.

In conclusion, word level analysis is a type of natural language processing (NLP) that focuses on the analysis of individual words and their relationships within a sentence. This includes tasks such as part-of-speech (POS) tagging, named entity recognition (NER), and word sense disambiguation, and it has numerous applications in fields such as text classification, information retrieval, and machine translation.
