### Dictionary & Thesaurus for the notes of the Unit 3 - SEMANTICS AND PRAGMATICS in the subject of NATURAL LANGUAGE PROCESSING

A dictionary and thesaurus are two important tools for Natural Language Processing (NLP). A dictionary is a resource that provides definitions for words, while a thesaurus provides synonyms for words.

In NLP, dictionaries and thesauruses are used to perform various tasks, such as part-of-speech tagging, named entity recognition, and sentiment analysis. For example, part-of-speech tagging involves identifying the role that a word plays in a sentence, such as a noun, verb, adjective, or adverb. A dictionary can be used to identify the part-of-speech of a word by providing definitions and examples of how the word is used.

A thesaurus is used to identify synonyms for words, which can be useful in tasks such as text generation, machine translation, and sentiment analysis. For example, in sentiment analysis, a thesaurus can be used to identify words that have a similar meaning, which can help to improve the accuracy of the sentiment analysis.

In conclusion, dictionaries and thesauruses are important resources for NLP, as they provide information about the meaning and usage of words. By using dictionaries and thesauruses, NLP systems can perform a wide range of tasks, such as part-of-speech tagging, named entity recognition, and sentiment analysis, with greater accuracy and efficiency.
