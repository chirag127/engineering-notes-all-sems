### Finite-State Automata for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of NATURAL LANGUAGE PROCESSING

Unit 1 of Introduction in the subject of Natural Language Processing covers the topic of Finite-State Automata. Finite-State Automata are mathematical models used to represent and process strings of symbols, including natural language text.

The following topics are covered in this unit:

1. Introduction to Finite-State Automata: An overview of finite-state automata, including their definition, properties, and applications.

2. Formal languages: An introduction to formal languages, including regular languages and context-free languages, and their relationship to finite-state automata.

3. Finite-State Automata Models: An overview of the different types of finite-state automata models, including deterministic finite-state automata (DFA), nondeterministic finite-state automata (NFA), and weighted finite-state automata (WFA), among others.

4. String Processing: An overview of the different methods and techniques used for processing strings of symbols, including string matching, string substitution, and string transduction, among others.

5. Applications of Finite-State Automata: An overview of the different applications of finite-state automata in natural language processing, including lexical analysis, parsing, and text classification, among others.

In conclusion, Unit 1 of Introduction in the subject of Natural Language Processing provides a comprehensive overview of Finite-State Automata, including their models, methods, and applications. This knowledge is essential for understanding the basic concepts and techniques used in natural language processing and for developing effective natural language processing systems.
