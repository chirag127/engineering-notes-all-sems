3. Transducers for Rules: An introduction to transducers for rules, which are mathematical models that are used to transform sequences of lexical categories into sequences of grammatical structures.

4. Finite-State Transducers: An overview of finite-state transducers, which are mathematical models that are based on finite-state automata and are used to perform lexical and grammatical analysis.

5. Context-Free Grammars: An introduction to context-free grammars, which are mathematical models that define the structure of a language and are used to perform grammatical analysis.

6. Applications of Transducers: An overview of the different applications of transducers in natural language processing, including speech recognition, machine translation, and sentiment analysis, among others.
