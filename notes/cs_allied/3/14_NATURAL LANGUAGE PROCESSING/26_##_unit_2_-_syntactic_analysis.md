## Unit 2 - SYNTACTIC ANALYSIS

Unit 2 - Syntactic Analysis covers the basics of syntactic analysis, a type of natural language processing (NLP) that focuses on the analysis of the structure and relationships of words within a sentence. Syntactic analysis is used to identify the grammatical structure of a sentence, including its constituent parts (such as noun phrases and verb phrases) and the relationships between these parts.

Components of Syntactic Analysis:

1. Parsing: Parsing is the process of analyzing the structure of a sentence and identifying its constituent parts and relationships.

2. Grammar Rules: Syntactic analysis is based on a set of grammar rules that define the structure of sentences and the relationships between their constituent parts.

3. Part-of-Speech (POS) Tagging: Syntactic analysis often includes POS tagging, which is the process of classifying words into their grammatical categories, such as nouns, verbs, and adjectives.

Applications of Syntactic Analysis:

1. Text Classification: Syntactic analysis is used in text classification, such as sentiment analysis and topic classification, to identify the meaning and sentiment of a text.

2. Machine Translation: Syntactic analysis is used in machine translation to improve the accuracy and fluency of translations, by taking into account the structure and relationships of words within a sentence.

3. Text Summarization: Syntactic analysis is used in text summarization to identify the most important parts of a text and summarize it into a shorter form.

In conclusion, Unit 2 - Syntactic Analysis covers the basics of syntactic analysis, a type of natural language processing (NLP) that focuses on the analysis of the structure and relationships of words within a sentence. Syntactic analysis is used to identify the grammatical structure of a sentence, including its constituent parts and the relationships between these parts, and it has numerous applications in fields such as text classification, machine translation, and text summarization.
