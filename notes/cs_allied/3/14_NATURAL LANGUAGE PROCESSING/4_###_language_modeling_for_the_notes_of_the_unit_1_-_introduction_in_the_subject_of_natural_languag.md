### Language Modeling for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of NATURAL LANGUAGE PROCESSING

Language modeling is a critical component of Natural Language Processing (NLP), and involves the creation of statistical models that are used to predict the likelihood of sequences of words in a language. These models are used to perform various NLP tasks, such as text classification, named entity recognition, and machine translation.

There are several types of language models, including:

1. N-gram models: N-gram models are the simplest type of language model, and they predict the likelihood of a word based on the previous N-1 words in the sequence.

2. Markov models: Markov models are an extension of N-gram models, and they take into account not just the previous N-1 words, but also the context of the words in the sequence.

3. Neural language models: Neural language models are a type of deep learning model that is designed to capture the underlying structure of language. They are trained on large amounts of text data, and they are capable of capturing complex relationships between words and phrases.

Language modeling is a critical component of NLP, as it provides the foundation for many NLP tasks. By creating accurate and robust language models, NLP systems can better understand and process natural language text, and provide more accurate and meaningful results.

Overall, language modeling is a critical component of NLP, and is used to predict the likelihood of sequences of words in a language. By understanding language modeling, students can gain a deeper appreciation for the technology and its impact on NLP and other related fields.
