3. Prosodic Features: Prosodic features are used to represent the prosodic information in speech signals, such as the rhythm, stress, and intonation of speech. Pattern comparison techniques are used to determine the similarity between speech signals, and they are useful for tasks such as speaker recognition and speech recognition.
