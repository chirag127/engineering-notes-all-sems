### Statistical LM for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of NATURAL LANGUAGE PROCESSING

Unit 1 of Introduction in the subject of Natural Language Processing focuses on the concept of Statistical Language Models (LMs). Statistical LMs are mathematical models that are used to predict the probability of a sequence of words in a language.

The following topics may be covered in this unit:

1. Introduction to Statistical LMs: An overview of the concept of statistical LMs and their importance in natural language processing.

2. N-gram Models: An introduction to N-gram models, which are statistical LMs that predict the probability of a word based on the previous n-1 words in a sequence.

3. Hidden Markov Models: An overview of hidden Markov models, which are statistical LMs that model the underlying hidden states of a sequence.

4. Maximum Entropy Models: An introduction to maximum entropy models, which are statistical LMs that maximize the entropy of the model subject to constraints.

5. Neural Language Models: An overview of neural language models, which are deep learning models that use neural networks to predict the probability of a sequence of words.

6. Applications of Statistical LMs: An overview of the different applications of statistical LMs, including speech recognition, machine translation, and sentiment analysis, among others.

In conclusion, Unit 1 of Introduction in the subject of Natural Language Processing provides a comprehensive overview of the concept of Statistical Language Models (LMs), including their methods, techniques, and applications. This knowledge is essential for understanding the use of statistical LMs in natural language processing and for developing accurate and effective natural language processing models.
