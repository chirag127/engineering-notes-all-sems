Filter bank and LPC methods are two important techniques used in speech processing to analyze the spectral and temporal properties of speech signals.

Filter bank methods are used to decompose speech signals into a set of narrowband sub-bands, or filters. Each filter is designed to respond to a specific frequency range in the speech signal, and the outputs of the filters are used to represent the spectral content of the speech signal. Filter bank methods are used for a variety of tasks in speech processing, including speech coding, speech recognition, and speech synthesis.

Linear Predictive Coding (LPC) is a method used to model the spectral envelope of speech signals. LPC is based on the idea that the spectral envelope of a speech signal can be approximated by a set of linear filters. The coefficients of these filters are used to represent the spectral envelope of the speech signal, and can be used for a variety of tasks in speech processing, including speech coding, speech recognition, and speech synthesis.

Filter bank and LPC methods are complementary techniques, and are often used together in speech processing systems. For example, filter bank methods may be used to decompose speech signals into sub-bands, and LPC methods may be used to model the spectral envelope of each sub-band.
