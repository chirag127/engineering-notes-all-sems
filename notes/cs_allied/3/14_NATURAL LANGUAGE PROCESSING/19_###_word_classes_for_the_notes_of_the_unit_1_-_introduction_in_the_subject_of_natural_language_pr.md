### Word Classes for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of NATURAL LANGUAGE PROCESSING

Word classes, also known as parts of speech, are categories that are used to describe the grammatical role of words in a sentence. In natural language processing (NLP), word classes are used to analyze and understand the structure of language, and to perform tasks such as text classification and named entity recognition.

The following are some of the most common word classes in NLP:

1. Nouns: Nouns are words that refer to people, places, things, or ideas. They are often used to identify the subject of a sentence.

2. Verbs: Verbs are words that describe actions, states, or events. They are often used to identify the predicate of a sentence.

3. Adjectives: Adjectives are words that describe or modify nouns or pronouns. They are often used to provide additional information about the subject of a sentence.

4. Adverbs: Adverbs are words that describe or modify verbs, adjectives, or other adverbs. They are often used to provide additional information about the action, state, or event described by the verb.

5. Pronouns: Pronouns are words that replace nouns. They are often used to refer to people, places, things, or ideas that have already been mentioned.

6. Prepositions: Prepositions are words that show the relationship between a noun or pronoun and other elements in a sentence. They are often used to indicate location, time, or direction.

7. Conjunction: Conjunctions are words that are used to connect words, phrases, or clauses. They are often used to join two independent clauses or to provide additional information.

By understanding word classes, students can gain a deeper appreciation for the structure of language and its impact on NLP. This understanding can help students to develop the skills necessary to analyze and understand language, and to develop NLP systems and applications.
