A functional view in the context of the Unit 2 - Reference Architecture in the subject of IOT ARCHITECTURE AND PROTOCOLS refers to the representation of the functions and their relationships within the system. It provides a high-level view of the system's components and how they interact to achieve the desired outcome.

In the context of IoT, the functional view is used to identify the key components of the system and to understand how they interact to support the end-to-end process. This view is particularly useful in the early stages of system design and development, as it helps to identify the necessary building blocks and to establish the overall system architecture.

The functional view typically includes the following components:

1. Sensors and Actuators: These are the devices that collect data from the environment and perform actions based on that data. They are the "endpoints" of the IoT system and can range from simple temperature sensors to complex industrial machinery.

5. Data Visualization and Presentation: This component is responsible for presenting the data in a meaningful and useful manner. It may involve the use of dashboards, reports, or other tools to visualize and present the data to end users.

6. Control and Automation: This component is responsible for controlling the actions of the actuators based on the data collected from the sensors. It may involve the use of rule engines, decision trees, or other algorithms to automate the control of the actuators.
