
### Bluetooth Low Energy 

Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) is a wireless communication protocol designed for low power consumption and short range communication. It is used in a variety of Internet of Things (IoT) applications such as home automation, medical devices, and industrial automation. BLE is based on the Bluetooth 4.0 standard, which was released in 2010. 

BLE operates in the 2.4GHz ISM band, and uses frequency hopping spread spectrum (FHSS) to ensure reliable communication. It has a maximum data rate of 1 Mbps, and a range of up to 10 meters. BLE uses the GAP (Generic Access Profile) and GATT (Generic Attribute Profile) to define the communication between devices. 

BLE is well suited for applications that require low power consumption and short range communication. It is ideal for applications that require frequent communication, such as home automation, medical devices, and industrial automation. BLE can be used to create networks of connected devices, and is often used in conjunction with other protocols such as Wi-Fi and Zigbee.
