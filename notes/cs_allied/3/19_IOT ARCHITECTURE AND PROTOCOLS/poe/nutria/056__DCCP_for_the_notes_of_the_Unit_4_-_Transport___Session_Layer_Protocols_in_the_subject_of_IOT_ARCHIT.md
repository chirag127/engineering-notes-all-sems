
### DCCP for the notes of the Unit 4 - Transport & Session Layer Protocols in the subject of IOT ARCHITECTURE AND PROTOCOLS

* Datagram Congestion Control Protocol (DCCP) is a transport layer protocol designed to provide reliable, congestion-controlled data delivery services for applications that do not require the reliability of TCP.
* DCCP is a connection-oriented protocol that provides reliable, congestion-controlled data delivery services.
* DCCP uses a congestion control mechanism called “Congestion Control Identifier” (CCID) to identify the congestion control algorithms used by the sender and receiver.
* DCCP also supports optional features such as congestion control, reliability, and flow control.
* DCCP is suitable for applications that require reliable, congestion-controlled data delivery services, such as streaming media applications.
* DCCP is not suitable for applications that require reliable, in-order data delivery, such as web and email applications.
