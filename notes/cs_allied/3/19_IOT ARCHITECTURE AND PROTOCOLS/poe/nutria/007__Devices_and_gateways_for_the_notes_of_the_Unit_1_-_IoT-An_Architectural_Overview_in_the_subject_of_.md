
### Devices and Gateways for Unit 1 - IoT-An Architectural Overview

- Devices are the physical components of the Internet of Things (IoT) that collect and send data, such as sensors, actuators, and embedded systems.
- Gateways are the interface between these devices and the network, and are responsible for connecting the IoT devices to the Internet. They can also provide additional services, such as data storage, data processing, and security.
- IoT networks can be divided into two categories: Local Area Networks (LANs) and Wide Area Networks (WANs).
- LANs are used for connecting devices within a limited area, such as a home or office.
- WANs are used for connecting devices across larger distances, such as cities or countries.
- IoT networks use a variety of protocols, including TCP/IP, MQTT, CoAP, and Zigbee, to enable communication between devices.
- Security is an important consideration when designing an IoT system. Encryption, authentication, and authorization can all be used to protect data and ensure the security of the system.
