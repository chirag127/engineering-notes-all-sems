

### Transport Layer

1. The Transport Layer is responsible for providing reliable, in-order delivery of messages between two applications. 
2. It provides services such as error correction, flow control, and congestion control. 
3. It is the layer that is responsible for end-to-end communication between two applications. 
4. It is also responsible for data segmentation and reassembly. 
5. The Transport Layer protocols used in IoT are UDP, TCP, and SCTP. 
6. UDP (User Datagram Protocol) is a connectionless protocol that is used for applications that require low overhead and do not require reliable delivery. 
7. TCP (Transmission Control Protocol) is a connection-oriented protocol that provides reliable, in-order delivery of messages. 
8. SCTP (Stream Control Transmission Protocol) is a connection-oriented protocol that provides reliable, in-order delivery of messages and provides additional features such as multi-homing and multi-streaming. 
9. The Transport Layer also provides Quality of Service (QoS) for messages. QoS is used to guarantee message delivery and reduce latency. 
10. The Transport Layer is responsible for ensuring that messages are delivered to the intended recipient in a timely manner.
