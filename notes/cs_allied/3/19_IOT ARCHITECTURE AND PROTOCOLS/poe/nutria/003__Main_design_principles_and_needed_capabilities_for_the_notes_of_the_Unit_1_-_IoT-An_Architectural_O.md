
### Main design principles and needed capabilities for the notes of the Unit 1 - IoT-An Architectural Overview in the subject of IOT ARCHITECTURE AND PROTOCOLS

1. The Internet of Things (IoT) is a network of physical objects (devices, sensors, actuators, etc.) that are connected to the Internet and can interact with each other and with the environment.

2. IoT architectures must be designed to support scalability and flexibility, while ensuring security and privacy.

3. An IoT architecture must provide mechanisms for device identification, authentication, authorization, and access control.

4. IoT architectures must provide mechanisms for data collection, storage, analysis, and communication.

5. IoT architectures must provide mechanisms for device management and monitoring.

6. IoT architectures must provide mechanisms for dynamic configuration and reconfiguration.

7. IoT architectures must support interoperability between different devices and networks.

8. IoT architectures must support distributed computing, distributed storage, and distributed analytics.

9. IoT architectures must provide mechanisms for fault tolerance and reliability.
