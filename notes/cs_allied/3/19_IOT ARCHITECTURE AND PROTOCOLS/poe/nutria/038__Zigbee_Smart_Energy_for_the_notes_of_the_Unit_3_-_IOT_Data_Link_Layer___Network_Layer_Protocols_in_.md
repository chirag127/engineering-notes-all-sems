
### Zigbee Smart Energy

Zigbee Smart Energy is a protocol used in the Internet of Things (IoT) to facilitate communication between devices. It is based on the IEEE 802.15.4 standard and is designed for low-power, low-cost wireless networks. It is used in applications such as home automation, industrial automation, energy management, and building automation.

Zigbee Smart Energy is a two-layer protocol. The data link layer (DLL) provides the physical and media access control (MAC) functions, while the network layer (NL) provides the routing and management functions.

The DLL is responsible for setting up a wireless link between devices, managing the radio frequency (RF) channels, and providing data encryption and integrity. It also provides a mechanism for devices to discover each other and exchange data. The NL provides the network-level services, such as routing, addressing, and network management.

Zigbee Smart Energy is a reliable and secure protocol, making it ideal for use in applications such as home automation and energy management. It is also easy to configure and provides a low-cost solution for connecting IoT devices.
