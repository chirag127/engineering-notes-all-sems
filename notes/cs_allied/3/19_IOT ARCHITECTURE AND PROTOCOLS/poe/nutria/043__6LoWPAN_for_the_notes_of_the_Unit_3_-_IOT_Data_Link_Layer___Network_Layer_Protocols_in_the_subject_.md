
### 6LoWPAN

6LoWPAN is an acronym for IPv6 over Low-Power Wireless Personal Area Networks. It is a network protocol designed to enable communication between low-power, low-bandwidth devices over a wireless personal area network. 6LoWPAN is based on the IEEE 802.15.4 standard and provides a link layer and network layer protocol for communication between devices.

6LoWPAN is used for communication between Internet of Things (IoT) devices, such as sensors, actuators, and embedded systems. It is designed to be low-power, low-bandwidth, and low-cost, making it ideal for connecting devices in remote or difficult-to-reach locations. 6LoWPAN is also designed to be secure and reliable, allowing for secure communication between devices.

6LoWPAN is used in a variety of applications, including home automation, industrial control, and medical monitoring. It is also used in smart cities, where it is used to monitor traffic, air quality, and other environmental data. 6LoWPAN is also used in smart homes, where it is used to control lights, appliances, and other devices.
