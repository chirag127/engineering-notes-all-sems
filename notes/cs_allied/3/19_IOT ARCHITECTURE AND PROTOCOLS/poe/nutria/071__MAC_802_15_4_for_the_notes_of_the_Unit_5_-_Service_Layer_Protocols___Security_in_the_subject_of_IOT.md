
### MAC 802.15.4

MAC 802.15.4 is a media access control (MAC) protocol used in low-rate wireless personal area networks (LR-WPANs). It is a sub-layer of the IEEE 802.15 standard, which is part of the IEEE 802.15.4 family of standards. It is designed for applications that require low data rates, low power consumption, and low cost.

The main features of the protocol include:

- Support for star, tree, and mesh topologies.
- Support for both contention-based and contention-free access.
- Support for both unicast and multicast communication.
- Support for variable packet sizes up to 127 bytes.
- Support for up to 16 channels in the 2.4 GHz band.
- Support for adaptive frequency hopping.
- Support for both synchronous and asynchronous communication.
- Support for AES-128 encryption.
- Support for time synchronization.
