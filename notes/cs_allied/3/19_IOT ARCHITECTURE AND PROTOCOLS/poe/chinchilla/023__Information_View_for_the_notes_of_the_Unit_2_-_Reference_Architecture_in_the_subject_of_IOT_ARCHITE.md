### Information View for the notes of the Unit 2 - Reference Architecture in the subject of IOT ARCHITECTURE AND PROTOCOLS

Information view is an important aspect of reference architecture in the context of IoT architecture and protocols. It is concerned with the representation of information and data in a structured and organized manner. Here are some key points related to information view in IoT reference architecture:

- Information view is one of the four views of IoT reference architecture, the other three being business view, functional view, and deployment view.
- The main objective of information view is to provide a clear understanding of the information and data that needs to be collected, processed, and analyzed in an IoT system.
- Information view takes into consideration the different types of data that are generated by various IoT devices and sensors, such as sensor data, device metadata, and user data.
- The information view also deals with the storage and management of data in IoT systems. It defines the data models and schemas that are used to represent and organize the data in a structured and consistent way.
- The information view is closely related to the functional view, as it defines the data flows and interactions between different functional components of an IoT system.
- A well-defined information view is crucial for the successful implementation and operation of IoT systems. It helps in ensuring data integrity, security, and privacy, as well as facilitating data analysis and decision making.
- The information view can be represented using various modeling techniques, such as entity-relationship diagrams, UML diagrams, and data flow diagrams.
- The information view is an evolving aspect of IoT reference architecture, as new technologies and standards emerge and new use cases and applications are developed. Therefore, it is important to keep updating and refining the information view as the IoT ecosystem evolves.

In conclusion, information view is a critical aspect of reference architecture in the field of IoT. It provides a structured and organized representation of data and information, which is necessary for the successful implementation and operation of IoT systems. By understanding the key concepts and principles of information view, one can gain an in-depth understanding of the data aspects of IoT architecture and protocols.
