### Deployment and Operational View

In reference to the IoT architecture and protocols, the deployment and operational view refers to the way in which IoT devices are deployed, managed, and utilized in the real world. Here are some key points to keep in mind:

- Deployment refers to the process of placing IoT devices in the field where they will be used. This process involves selecting appropriate hardware, configuring the devices, and installing them in the appropriate location.

- Operational view refers to the ongoing management and use of IoT devices once they have been deployed. This involves monitoring their performance, troubleshooting issues, and making adjustments to ensure they are functioning optimally.

- When it comes to deployment, there are a number of factors to consider, including the location of the devices, the type of environment they will be operating in, and the requirements of the application they are being used for.

- One important consideration when deploying IoT devices is the network infrastructure that will be used to connect them. This may involve selecting a wireless protocol, such as Wi-Fi or Bluetooth, or using a wired connection, such as Ethernet.

- Once devices have been deployed, they must be managed and maintained to ensure they are functioning properly. This may involve monitoring their performance, detecting and troubleshooting issues, and making adjustments to optimize their performance.

- Operational view also involves the ongoing use of IoT devices in real-world applications. This may involve collecting and analyzing data from the devices, using that data to drive decision-making, and integrating the devices with other systems and applications.

- To effectively manage and utilize IoT devices, it is important to have a clear understanding of their capabilities and limitations, as well as the requirements of the applications they are being used for.

- As IoT technology continues to evolve, new deployment and operational challenges are likely to arise. However, by staying up-to-date with the latest developments in the field, organizations can ensure they are able to effectively leverage IoT devices for maximum benefit.
