### Zigbee Smart Energy

- Zigbee Smart Energy (Zigbee SE) is a protocol designed for monitoring and actively managing energy consumption at the end-user level.
- Zigbee SE can help reduce waste, energy consumption and enables utilities to monitor and manage customers’ energy use.
- Zigbee SE is based on the Zigbee protocol, which is a low-cost, low-power, wireless mesh network standard.
- Zigbee SE supports various devices, such as smart meters, in-home displays, smart appliances, thermostats, load control switches, and smart plugs.
- Zigbee SE is the world’s leading standard for interoperable products that monitor, control, inform and automate the delivery and use of energy, gas, and water.
- Zigbee SE aims to create “Green Homes” by coordinating energy usage, optimizing its generation and consumption, and reducing emissions footprint.
- Zigbee SE is an Internet Protocol-based communication protocol that uses the global 2.4GHz spectrum and regional SubGHz frequencies.
- Zigbee SE is an enhancement of the Zigbee Smart Energy version 1 specifications, which were based on the Zigbee Application Layer.
- Zigbee SE provides security, reliability, scalability, and interoperability for smart energy applications.
