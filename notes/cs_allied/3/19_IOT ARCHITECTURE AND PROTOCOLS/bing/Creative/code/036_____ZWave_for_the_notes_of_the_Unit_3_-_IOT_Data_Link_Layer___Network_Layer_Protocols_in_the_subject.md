### ZWave

ZWave is a wireless communication protocol designed for smart home and IoT devices. It operates on the low-frequency 800 to 900 MHz band, which avoids interference with the 2.4 GHz band where Wi-Fi and Bluetooth operate. ZWave supports encryption, mesh networking, low power consumption, and interoperability among different vendors. Some of the features and characteristics of ZWave are:

- ZWave was developed by Zensys, a Danish company, in 1999.
- ZWave is a proprietary protocol owned by Sigma Designs, Inc. There is an open source implementation of ZWave protocol stack called open-zwave, but it does not support security layer.
- ZWave uses a star or mesh topology, where each device can act as a repeater and relay messages to other devices. This increases the range and reliability of the network.
- ZWave supports up to 232 devices in a network, and up to four hops between the source and the destination.
- ZWave uses frequency shift keying (FSK) modulation and binary phase shift keying (BPSK) modulation for data transmission. The data rate is 9.6 kbps or 40 kbps, depending on the region and the modulation scheme.
- ZWave uses a 128-bit AES encryption to secure the communication between devices. It also supports network inclusion and exclusion, which allows adding or removing devices from the network without compromising the security.
- ZWave is compatible with a wide range of smart home and IoT devices, such as sensors, switches, dimmers, thermostats, locks, cameras, etc. It also supports different device classes, such as controllers, slaves, routing slaves, and enhanced slaves.
- ZWave is certified by the Z-Wave Alliance, a consortium of over 700 companies that promote and support the ZWave protocol. The Z-Wave Alliance also provides a logo and a certification program for ZWave products.

ZWave is one of the leading network protocols for smart home and IoT automation, due to its low power, low data rate, and high interoperability features. It is suitable for control and sensor applications that do not require high bandwidth or latency. ZWave is also a mature and stable protocol that has been in the market for over 20 years.
