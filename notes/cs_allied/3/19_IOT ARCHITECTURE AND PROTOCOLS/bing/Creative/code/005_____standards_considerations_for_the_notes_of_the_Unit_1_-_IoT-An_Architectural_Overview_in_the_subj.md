# Standards considerations for the notes of the Unit 1 - IoT-An Architectural Overview

- The notes should provide a clear and concise introduction to the concept, definition, and characteristics of the Internet of Things (IoT).
- The notes should explain the main components and layers of a basic IoT architecture, such as perception, network, cloud, and application .
- The notes should describe the different architectural views and design principles of IoT, such as functional, information, deployment, operational, and business .
- The notes should illustrate the IoT architecture with examples of real-world scenarios and use cases, such as smart home, smart city, smart agriculture, smart healthcare, etc. .
- The notes should highlight the main challenges and opportunities of IoT, such as security, privacy, interoperability, scalability, reliability, etc. .
- The notes should follow a logical and coherent structure, with clear headings, subheadings, bullet points, diagrams, tables, etc.
- The notes should cite the sources of information using proper referencing styles, such as APA, MLA, IEEE, etc.
