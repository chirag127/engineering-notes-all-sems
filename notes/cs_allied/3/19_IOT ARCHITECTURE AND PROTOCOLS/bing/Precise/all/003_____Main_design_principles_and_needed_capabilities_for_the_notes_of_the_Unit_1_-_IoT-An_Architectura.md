### Main design principles and needed capabilities for the notes of the Unit 1 - IoT-An Architectural Overview in the subject of IOT ARCHITECTURE AND PROTOCOLS

1. **Interoperability:** IoT devices and systems must be able to communicate and exchange data with each other, regardless of the manufacturer, operating system, or other factors.
2. **Scalability:** IoT systems must be able to handle a large number of connected devices and the data they generate.
3. **Security:** IoT devices and systems must be secure to protect against unauthorized access and data breaches.
4. **Reliability:** IoT systems must be reliable and able to operate without interruption.
5. **Modularity:** IoT systems should be designed in a modular way, allowing for easy upgrades and maintenance.
6. **Energy efficiency:** IoT devices should be energy efficient to reduce their impact on the environment and to extend their battery life.
7. **Data management:** IoT systems must be able to manage and process large amounts of data in real-time.
8. **User-friendly:** IoT devices and systems should be easy to use and intuitive for the end-user.
