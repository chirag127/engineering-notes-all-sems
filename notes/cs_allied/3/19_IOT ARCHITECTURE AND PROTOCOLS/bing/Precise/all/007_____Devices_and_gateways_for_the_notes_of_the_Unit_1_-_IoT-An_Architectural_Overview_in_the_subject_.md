# Devices and Gateways

## Unit 1 - IoT-An Architectural Overview

### IoT Devices
- IoT devices are the physical objects that are connected to the internet and can collect and exchange data.
- These devices can range from simple sensors to complex machines.
- They can be embedded in everyday objects such as home appliances, vehicles, and buildings.
- IoT devices can be equipped with various sensors, actuators, and communication technologies to interact with their environment and other devices.

### IoT Gateways
- IoT gateways are the intermediary devices that connect IoT devices to the internet and other networks.
- They provide a bridge between the IoT devices and the cloud, enabling data transfer and communication between the two.
- IoT gateways can perform various functions such as data aggregation, data filtering, and protocol translation.
- They can also provide security features such as data encryption and authentication to ensure the safe transfer of data.
