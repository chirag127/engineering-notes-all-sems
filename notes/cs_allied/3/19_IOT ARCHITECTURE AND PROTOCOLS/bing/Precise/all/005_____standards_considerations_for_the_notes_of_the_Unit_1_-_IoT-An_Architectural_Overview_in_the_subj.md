# Standards considerations for the notes of the Unit 1 - IoT-An Architectural Overview in the subject of IOT ARCHITECTURE AND PROTOCOLS

1. The notes should provide a clear and concise overview of the architecture of IoT systems.
2. The notes should include information on the various layers of the IoT architecture, including the perception layer, network layer, and application layer.
3. The notes should discuss the role of various protocols in IoT systems, including communication protocols, data transfer protocols, and security protocols.
4. The notes should provide information on the various standards that are relevant to IoT systems, including wireless communication standards, data transfer standards, and security standards.
5. The notes should include diagrams and illustrations to help explain the concepts discussed in the text.
6. The notes should be well-organized and easy to follow, with clear headings and subheadings to guide the reader.
7. The notes should be written in a formal and academic style, using appropriate technical terminology.
8. The notes should be thoroughly proofread to ensure accuracy and clarity.
9. The notes should include references to relevant sources and further reading materials for students who wish to learn more about the topic.
10. The notes should be regularly updated to reflect the latest developments and advancements in the field of IoT architecture and protocols.
