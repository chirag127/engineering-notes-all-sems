### MAC 802.15.4

MAC 802.15.4 is a standard that defines the operation of low-rate wireless personal area networks (LR-WPANs). It is a protocol that is used for communication in the service layer of the IoT architecture. Here are some key points about MAC 802.15.4:

- MAC 802.15.4 is designed to operate in the unlicensed frequency bands, including 2.4 GHz, 915 MHz, and 868 MHz.
- The standard specifies a maximum data rate of 250 kbps in the 2.4 GHz band.
- MAC 802.15.4 supports both star and peer-to-peer network topologies.
- The standard defines two types of devices: full-function devices (FFDs) and reduced-function devices (RFDs). FFDs can operate as coordinators or routers, while RFDs are typically used as end devices.
- MAC 802.15.4 includes security features such as encryption and authentication to protect the data transmitted over the network.
- The standard is widely used in applications such as home automation, industrial control, and medical monitoring.
