### BBF for the notes of the Unit 5 - Service Layer Protocols & Security in the subject of IOT ARCHITECTURE AND PROTOCOLS

- BBF stands for Broadband Forum, which is an organization that works on the development of broadband network standards.
- One of the projects of BBF is the Connected Home, which presents service providers with opportunities and challenges in delivering and measuring the broadband experience in homes and businesses .
- BBF has developed a standardized architecture, protocol, and information model called the User Services Platform (USP), defined in Broadband Forum TR-369, to allow the development of applications for the connected home .
- USP represents the natural evolution of the Broadband Forum’s CPE WAN Management Protocol (CWMP), commonly known as TR-069, and allows for easy mapping of the home network to control service quality and monitor threats, as well as secure control of IoT, smart home, and smart networking functions locally or from the Cloud .
