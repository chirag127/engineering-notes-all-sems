### Information View for the notes of the Unit 2 - Reference Architecture in the subject of IOT ARCHITECTURE AND PROTOCOLS

- The Information View is one of the four views of the IoT Reference Architecture.
- It focuses on the data and information aspects of the IoT system.
- It defines the data models, data flows, and data storage and management within the system.
- The Information View is concerned with how data is collected, processed, and used within the IoT system.
- It also deals with issues such as data privacy, security, and ownership.
- The Information View is closely related to the other views of the IoT Reference Architecture, particularly the Functional View and the Deployment View.
- Understanding the Information View is essential for designing and implementing effective IoT systems.
