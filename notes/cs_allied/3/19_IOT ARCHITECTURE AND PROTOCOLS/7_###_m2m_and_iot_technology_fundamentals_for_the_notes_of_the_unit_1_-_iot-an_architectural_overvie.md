### M2M and IoT Technology Fundamentals for the notes of the Unit 1 - IoT-An Architectural Overview in the subject of IOT ARCHITECTURE AND PROTOCOLS

M2M (Machine-to-Machine) and IoT (Internet of Things) technology are key components of the modern connected world. They refer to the ability of devices to communicate with each other and with other systems over the internet.

M2M technology is a subset of IoT technology and refers specifically to the communication between machines or devices. It allows devices to exchange data and perform tasks without the need for human intervention.

The IoT, on the other hand, refers to the interconnectivity of devices, including both M2M devices and other devices such as smartphones, laptops, and home appliances. The IoT encompasses a wide range of technologies and applications, including smart homes, connected cars, and industrial automation.

The foundation of M2M and IoT technology is the use of networked devices that can communicate with each other and with other systems. These devices are equipped with sensors, actuators, and other components that allow them to collect and process data, and to perform actions based on that data.

The communication between M2M and IoT devices is facilitated by various wireless communication technologies, such as Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Zigbee, and cellular networks. These technologies allow devices to communicate with each other and with other systems over long distances, and to transmit large amounts of data.

To ensure that M2M and IoT devices can communicate with each other, there are a number of protocols and standards that have been developed. These protocols and standards include MQTT, CoAP, and HTTP, and they define the rules for data transmission and communication between devices.

In conclusion, M2M and IoT technology are key components of the modern connected world, allowing devices to communicate with each other and with other systems over the internet. The foundation of these technologies is the use of networked devices that can collect and process data, and the communication between devices is facilitated by various wireless communication technologies and protocols and standards. Understanding the fundamentals of M2M and IoT technology is essential for anyone interested in the field of IoT architecture and protocols.
