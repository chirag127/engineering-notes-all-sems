### Transport Layer for the notes of the Unit 4 - Transport & Session Layer Protocols in the subject of IOT ARCHITECTURE AND PROTOCOLS

The transport layer is a layer in the Internet Protocol (IP) stack that provides reliable and efficient data transfer between applications running on different devices. The transport layer is responsible for breaking up data into smaller, manageable packets, and for reassembling the packets into the original data at the receiving end.

There are two main transport layer protocols used in the Internet Protocol (IP) network: Transmission Control Protocol (TCP) and User Datagram Protocol (UDP).

TCP is a reliable, connection-oriented transport layer protocol that provides guaranteed delivery of data. When using TCP, data is transmitted in a series of packets, and the receiving device acknowledges the receipt of each packet. If a packet is lost or corrupted, the sender will retransmit the packet until it is successfully received.

UDP is a connectionless, unreliable transport layer protocol that provides fast, efficient data transfer. Unlike TCP, UDP does not guarantee the delivery of data, and packets may be lost or delivered out of order. However, UDP is often used in real-time applications, such as streaming media, where the loss of a few packets is acceptable and the speed of data transfer is more important than reliability.

The transport layer provides important services to the applications running on a device, including reliable data transfer, flow control, and error correction. By breaking up data into smaller packets and transmitting the packets over the network, the transport layer helps to ensure that data is transmitted efficiently and reliably, even over large and complex networks.

Overall, the transport layer is an important layer in the Internet Protocol (IP) stack, and is responsible for providing reliable and efficient data transfer between applications running on different devices. Understanding the transport layer and its role in the IP network is an important aspect of the study of IoT Architecture and Protocols.
