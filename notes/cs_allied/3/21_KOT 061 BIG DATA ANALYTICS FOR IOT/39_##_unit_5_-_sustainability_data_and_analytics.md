## Unit 5 - SUSTAINABILITY DATA AND ANALYTICS

Sustainability data and analytics refers to the collection, analysis, and interpretation of data related to sustainability and environmental issues. This data is used to inform decisions and actions aimed at promoting sustainability and reducing the environmental impact of human activities.

Sustainability data and analytics covers a wide range of topics, including energy consumption, resource use, waste generation, greenhouse gas emissions, and water usage. This data is collected from a variety of sources, including sensors, monitoring systems, and reporting mechanisms.

In the context of sustainability data and analytics, it is important to understand the principles of data collection, analysis, and interpretation, and to be familiar with the tools and techniques used to analyze sustainability data. This may include understanding the types of data that are collected, the methods used to collect and store this data, and the techniques used to analyze and interpret this data.

In addition, the course may cover the use of sustainability data and analytics to inform decision-making and actions aimed at promoting sustainability and reducing the environmental impact of human activities. This may include the use of data visualization and reporting tools to communicate sustainability information to stakeholders, as well as the use of predictive analytics and simulation models to evaluate the impact of different sustainability initiatives.

Overall, sustainability data and analytics is an important aspect of promoting sustainability and reducing the environmental impact of human activities, and understanding the principles and applications of this field is an important aspect of the study of this subject.
