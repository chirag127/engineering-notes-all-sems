# KOT 061 BIG DATA ANALYTICS FOR IOT

"KOT 061 Big Data Analytics for IoT" is a course that focuses on the use of big data analytics in the Internet of Things (IoT). The course is designed to provide students with an understanding of the challenges and opportunities associated with big data analytics in the context of IoT, as well as the skills and knowledge needed to apply big data analytics techniques to IoT data.

In the course, students will learn about the architecture and components of big data analytics systems, as well as the various data sources and data types that are commonly used in IoT. Students will also learn about the different types of big data analytics techniques, including data mining, machine learning, and predictive analytics, and will explore the use of these techniques in the context of IoT.

In addition, students will learn about the challenges and opportunities associated with big data analytics in IoT, including issues related to data privacy, security, and data quality, as well as the challenges of managing and processing large volumes of IoT data.

The course will also cover the use of big data analytics tools and platforms, including Hadoop, Spark, and NoSQL databases, and will provide hands-on experience with these tools through the use of case studies and practical exercises.

Overall, "KOT 061 Big Data Analytics for IoT" is a comprehensive course that provides students with a deep understanding of the use of big data analytics in the Internet of Things, and the skills and knowledge needed to apply big data analytics techniques to IoT data.
