## Unit 1 - BIG DATA PLATFORMS FOR THE INTERNET OF THINGS

In this unit, we will explore the big data platforms used for the Internet of Things (IoT). Here are the key points to understand:

- Big data platforms are used to collect, store, and analyze large amounts of data generated by IoT devices.

- The three main types of big data platforms used for IoT are cloud-based, on-premise, and hybrid.

- Cloud-based big data platforms are hosted on remote servers and are accessible from anywhere with an internet connection. They are scalable, flexible, and cost-effective, making them a popular choice for IoT applications.

- On-premise big data platforms are installed on local servers and are managed by the organization. They offer more control and security but require a significant upfront investment.

- Hybrid big data platforms combine cloud-based and on-premise solutions to provide the benefits of both. They are ideal for organizations that need to balance cost, scalability, and control.

- The big data platforms used for IoT must be able to handle the volume, variety, and velocity of the data generated by IoT devices. They must also ensure data security, privacy, and compliance with regulations.

- Some of the popular big data platforms used for IoT include Amazon Web Services (AWS), Microsoft Azure, Google Cloud Platform, IBM Watson, and Apache Hadoop.

- These platforms offer a range of features and tools, such as data ingestion, storage, analysis, visualization, and machine learning. They also provide APIs and SDKs for developers to build custom IoT applications.

- To select the right big data platform for your IoT application, you must consider factors such as data volume, processing speed, data security, scalability, cost, and integration with other systems.

- Overall, big data platforms are essential for making sense of the vast amounts of data generated by IoT devices. They enable organizations to gain insights, improve decision-making, and create new value propositions for their customers.
