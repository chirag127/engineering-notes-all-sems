
### Necessary and sufficient condition for false authentication prevention for the notes of the Unit 2 - RFID FALSE AUTHENTICATIONS in the subject of KOT 061 BIG DATA ANALYTICS FOR IOT KCS

1. Radio Frequency Identification (RFID) systems are vulnerable to false authentication attacks.
2. The most common form of false authentication is known as the relay attack.
3. To prevent false authentication, it is necessary to have a secure authentication protocol that uses an appropriate cryptographic algorithm.
4. The authentication protocol must also provide sufficient security measures to ensure that the data being exchanged is not compromised.
5. The authentication protocol should also provide a mechanism for verifying the identity of the user.
6. Additionally, the authentication protocol should be able to detect and prevent replay attacks.
7. Finally, the authentication protocol should ensure that the data is not modified or tampered with during transmission.
