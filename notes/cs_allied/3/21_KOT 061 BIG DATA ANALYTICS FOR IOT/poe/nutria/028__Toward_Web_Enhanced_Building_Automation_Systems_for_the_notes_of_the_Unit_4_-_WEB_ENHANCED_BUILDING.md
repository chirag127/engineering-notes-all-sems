
### Toward Web Enhanced Building Automation Systems

* Building automation systems (BAS) are used to monitor and control the operations of buildings, such as temperature, lighting, and security. 
* Web enhanced building automation systems (WEBAS) are a type of BAS that uses web-based technologies to provide remote access and control of building systems. 
* WEBAS are typically based on open standards such as XML, HTML, and SOAP, and can be accessed from any web-enabled device. 
* WEBAS can be used to monitor and control a wide range of building systems, from HVAC and lighting, to security and fire alarms. 
* WEBAS can be integrated with other systems, such as energy management systems and building information systems, to provide a comprehensive view of a building's operations. 
* WEBAS can also be used to automate processes, such as scheduling and reporting, and to provide data-driven insights into building operations.
