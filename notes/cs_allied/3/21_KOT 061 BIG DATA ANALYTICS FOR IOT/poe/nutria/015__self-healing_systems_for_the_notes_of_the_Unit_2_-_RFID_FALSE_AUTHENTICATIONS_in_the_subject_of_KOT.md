
### Self-Healing Systems for Unit 2 - RFID False Authentications in KOT 061 Big Data Analytics for IoT KCS

1. Self-healing systems are a type of technology that can detect and repair errors or faults in a system without human intervention.
2. RFID false authentication occurs when an RFID reader incorrectly identifies a valid RFID tag as invalid.
3. This can be caused by a number of factors, including environmental noise, incorrect tag placement, or incorrect tag programming.
4. To prevent false authentication, RFID systems should be designed to detect and respond to errors quickly and accurately.
5. Self-healing systems can detect and respond to errors more quickly than traditional RFID systems, allowing for faster identification and resolution of false authentication errors.
6. Self-healing systems can also be used to detect and respond to other types of errors, such as incorrect tag programming or incorrect tag placement.
7. Self-healing systems are becoming increasingly important for IoT applications, as they can help ensure the accuracy and reliability of data collected from RFID tags.
