
# Knowledge Centric Service (KCS)

KCS is a service-oriented approach to knowledge management that seeks to maximize the value of an organization's knowledge by making it easily accessible, shareable, and reusable. KCS focuses on creating and maintaining knowledge in the form of reusable, documented solutions to customer problems.

KCS seeks to improve customer service by providing agents with quick access to accurate and up-to-date information, as well as reducing the time it takes to resolve customer issues.

## Benefits of KCS

1. Improved customer service: By providing agents with quick access to accurate and up-to-date information, KCS reduces the time it takes to resolve customer issues.

2. Increased efficiency: KCS helps to reduce the amount of time spent on customer service calls by providing agents with access to knowledge that can be quickly used to resolve customer issues.

3. Greater knowledge sharing: KCS encourages knowledge sharing and reuse by providing a platform for agents to share their knowledge with each other and the entire organization.

4. Increased customer satisfaction: By providing agents with quick access to accurate and up-to-date information, KCS helps to improve customer satisfaction and loyalty.

5. Reduced costs: By reducing the amount of time spent on customer service calls, KCS helps to reduce the costs associated with customer service.
