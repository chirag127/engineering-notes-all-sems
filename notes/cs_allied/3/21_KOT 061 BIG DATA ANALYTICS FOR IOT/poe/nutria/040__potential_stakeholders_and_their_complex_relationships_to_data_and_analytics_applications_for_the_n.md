
### Potential Stakeholders and Their Complex Relationships to Data and Analytics Applications

1. Businesses: Businesses can use data and analytics applications to gain insights into customer behavior, improve operational efficiency, and develop new products and services.
2. Governments: Governments use data and analytics applications to monitor and analyze economic activity, identify trends in public opinion, and improve public services.
3. Non-Profit Organizations: Non-profit organizations use data and analytics applications to track and measure their impact, understand donor behavior, and develop more effective programs.
4. Researchers: Researchers use data and analytics applications to analyze large datasets and uncover new insights.
5. Consumers: Consumers use data and analytics applications to make informed decisions about their purchases and activities.
6. Technology Companies: Technology companies use data and analytics applications to develop new products and services, optimize their operations, and identify new opportunities.
