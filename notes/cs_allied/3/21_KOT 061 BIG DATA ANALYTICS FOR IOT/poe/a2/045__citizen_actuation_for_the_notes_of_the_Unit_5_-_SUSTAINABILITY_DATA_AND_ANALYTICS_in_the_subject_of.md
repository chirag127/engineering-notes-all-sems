### Citizen Actuation

- Citizen actuation refers to engaging and motivating citizens to take action on sustainability issues in their local communities.
- Data and analytics can be used to understand citizen perceptions, priorities, and behaviors related to sustainability and then design interventions to motivate changes in actions.
- For example, data from surveys and sensors can be used to identify key barriers to and motivators for more sustainable transportation choices. This data can then inform the design of incentives, messaging, or infrastructure changes to motivate citizens to choose more sustainable transportation options.
- Another example is using data to understand energy usage patterns in a community and then designing targeted messages or comparisons to motivate energy efficiency improvements in homes and businesses.
- The effectiveness of citizen actuation approaches depends on properly understanding the local context and citizen perspectives and tailoring interventions to the priorities and motivations of the specific community and citizens. Data and analytics are crucial to gaining these necessary insights to enable impactful citizen actuation.
