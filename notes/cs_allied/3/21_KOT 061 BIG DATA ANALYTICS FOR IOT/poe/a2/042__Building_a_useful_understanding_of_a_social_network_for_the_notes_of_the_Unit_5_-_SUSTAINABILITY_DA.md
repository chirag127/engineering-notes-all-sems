### Building a useful understanding of a social network for the notes of the Unit 5 - SUSTAINABILITY DATA AND ANALYTICS in the subject of KOT 061 BIG DATA ANALYTICS FOR IOT KCS:

1. A social network is a social structure made up of a set of social actors (such as individuals or organizations), sets of dyadic ties, and other social interactions between actors.
2. The social network perspective provides a set of methods for analyzing the structure of whole social entities as well as a variety of theories explaining the patterns observed in these structures.
3. The study of these structures uses social network analysis to identify local and global patterns, locate influential entities, and examine network dynamics.
4. Social networks and the analysis of them is an inherently interdisciplinary academic field which emerged from social anthropology, sociology, statistics, and graph theory.
5. Explain the key terms: network, nodes, edges, degree, betweenness centrality, closeness centrality, eigenvector centrality, homophily, structural holes with examples.
6. Discuss how social network analysis is done using network diagrams and matrix representations. Explain the advantages and disadvantages of both.
7. Explain the principles and significance of hierarchical, core-periphery and small-world network structures. Give examples of networks exhibiting these structures.
8. Discuss the challenges of studying social networks and doing social network analysis. Explain the common mistakes and biases that can arise.
9. Explain how understanding social networks can lead to insights and applications in various domains like epidemiology, economics, and management. Give relevant examples.
