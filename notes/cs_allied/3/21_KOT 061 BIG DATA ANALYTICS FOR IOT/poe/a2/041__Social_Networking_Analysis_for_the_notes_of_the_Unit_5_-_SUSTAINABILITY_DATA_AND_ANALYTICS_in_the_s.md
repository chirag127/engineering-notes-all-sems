### Social Networking Analysis for the notes of the Unit 5 - SUSTAINABILITY DATA AND ANALYTICS in the subject of KOT 061 BIG DATA ANALYTICS FOR IOT KCS

**Point 1:** Social network analysis (SNA) is the process of investigating social structures through the use of networks and graph theory. It characterizes networked structures in terms of nodes (individual actors, people, or things within the network) and the ties, edges, or links (relationships or interactions) that connect them.

**Point 2:** SNA provides a powerful set of tools for analyzing how relationships between people, groups, and organizations form, evolve, and influence behaviors and outcomes. It helps us understand the implications of social structures and how these structures influence individuals and societies.

**Point 3:** Some of the key measures and metrics in SNA include:

- Degree centrality: Number of direct connections a node has. A higher degree means a node is more well-connected.
- Betweenness centrality: Measure of how often a node lies on the shortest path between two other nodes. A higher betweenness means a node connects disparate parts of the network.
- Closeness centrality: Measure of how close a node is to all other nodes in the network. A higher closeness means a node can reach other nodes more quickly.
- Density: Measure of how connected the overall network is. A higher density means there are more connections relative to the maximum possible number of connections.

**Point 4:** SNA has a wide range of applications in various domains including sociology, anthropology, political science, biology, computer science, and more. Some example use cases include:

- Identifying key influencers, leaders, and opinion makers in a social network.
- Understanding how information, resources, or diseases spread through a network.
- Detecting communities and subcultures in a network.
- Evaluating the resilience and robustness of a network.
- Monitoring organizational dynamics and interactions.
- Recommending connections between people or discovering hidden relationships.
