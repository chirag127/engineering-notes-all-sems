### Interoperability problem in the IoT context

1. Lack of common standards: There are no common standards for interoperability between IoT devices and platforms. Different companies have their own protocols and specifications resulting in devices and platforms which cannot communicate with each other.

2. Variety of technologies: The IoT ecosystem comprises of a variety of technologies like RFID, Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, ZigBee, etc. This heterogeneity in technologies poses challenges in interoperability.

3. Scalability issues: The huge number of connected IoT devices and platforms can pose challenges to interoperability as it is difficult to handle connections and communication between billions of devices and platforms.

4. Security concerns: The IoT systems are susceptible to various security threats and attacks which can hamper the interoperability of devices and platforms. Hence, a common security framework is required to enable secure interoperability.

5. High investment: To achieve full interoperability in IoT systems, it requires high investments to standardize or replace proprietary technologies and enable devices/platforms to communicate using common standards and protocols.

The above points discuss the major interoperability challenges in the Internet of Things context which need to be addressed to realize the full potential of IoT. Establishing common standards and protocols along with a common security framework can enhance the interoperability between the diverse devices and platforms in the IoT ecosystem.
