### Self-Healing Systems

- Self-healing systems detect and recover from faults or attacks to maintain an acceptable level of service.
- In RFID systems, self-healing can involve detecting and mitigating malicious readers and tags.
- Possible approaches:
-- Redundancy: Duplicate readers and tags; majority vote to filter out malicious ones.
-- Spatial and temporal filtering: Ignore inconsistent readings from neighboring readers or those that occur too quickly.
-- Blacklisting: Detected malicious readers/tags are blocked from the system.
-- Moving to active RFID tags: Active tags have more complex functionality and can implement challenge-response authentication to prevent spoofing.
- The specific self-healing approaches used will depend on system requirements and what types of faults/attacks need to be handled. A combination of techniques may be required for robust security.
