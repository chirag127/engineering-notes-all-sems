2. Spatial technology such as GPS, geospatial data, etc. can be integrated with IoT to understand the spatial relationships and interactions between connected devices. This can enable location-based services and spatial data analytics.
3. The location data from IoT devices can be visualized on maps to identify patterns and relationships. Heat maps can be created to identify concentration of devices in a particular area.
4. Spatial data analytics can reveal insights such as devices concentrated in a particular region may experience similar issues or requirements. This can help in predictive maintenance and targeted service delivery.
5. However, the geographical data from IoT devices can pose privacy risks if the location of devices can be tracked. Appropriate security and privacy measures need to be implemented to prevent misuse of spatial data from IoT networks.
