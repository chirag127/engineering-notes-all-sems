### Potential stakeholders and their complex relationships to data and analytics applications

- Government agencies: Governments are interested in data and analytics applications to gain insights into citizen sentiment, public policy impact, environmental sustainability, etc. However, there are significant privacy concerns with how government agencies collect and use data.
- Private companies: Many companies see the potential for data and analytics to gain business insights and competitive advantage. However, there are risks around data privacy, security, and ethical use of customer data and algorithms.
- Researchers: Researchers want access to data to advance science and address complex problems. However, there are many challenges around data access, privacy, and responsible research conduct.
- Citizens: Individuals want the benefits of data-driven innovations but have valid concerns about data privacy, security, bias, and other harms. There is no universal view on data usage among citizens.
- Open data advocates: Those advocating for open data share the potential benefits of open data but there needs to be a balance with privacy rights and responsible data sharing.
- Ethicists: Experts in ethics highlight the need to address societal implications of data usage and put appropriate guardrails and governance in place. However, there is no universal agreement on specific governance approaches.

In summary, there are many potential stakeholders interested in data and analytics applications, but each has a complex set of perspectives on the risks and rewards of data usage. Balancing these competing interests and finding alignment on responsible data practices is an ongoing challenge.
