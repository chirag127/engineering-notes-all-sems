### Emerging Technologies in Health Information Systems

- Health information systems (HIS) are systems that collect, store, process, and communicate health-related data for decision-making, planning, and management of health services and programs.
- Emerging technologies are new or evolving technologies that have the potential to transform or disrupt existing practices, processes, or paradigms in various domains, including health care.
- Some of the emerging technologies that are influencing HIS are:

  - **Connected health devices**: These are devices that can monitor, measure, or transmit health data from patients to providers, caregivers, or other stakeholders, using wireless or wired connections. Examples include wearable sensors, smart watches, blood pressure monitors, glucose meters, etc. These devices can enable remote patient monitoring, chronic disease management, and personalized care delivery.
  - **Digital therapeutics**: These are software-based interventions that can deliver evidence-based therapies or treatments for various health conditions, such as mental health, diabetes, obesity, etc. Examples include mobile apps, web platforms, chatbots, etc. These interventions can complement or replace traditional therapies, improve patient engagement and adherence, and reduce costs and side effects.
  - **Artificial intelligence (AI)**: This is the technology that can perform tasks that normally require human intelligence, such as reasoning, learning, decision-making, etc. Examples include machine learning, natural language processing, computer vision, etc. AI can enhance HIS by providing data analysis, diagnosis, prognosis, treatment recommendations, clinical decision support, etc.
  - **Telehealth**: This is the delivery of health care services and information using telecommunications technologies, such as phone, video, email, etc. Examples include teleconsultation, telemedicine, telemonitoring, etc. Telehealth can increase access to care, especially for rural and underserved populations, improve quality and efficiency of care, and reduce costs and travel time.
  - **Virtual reality (VR)**: This is the technology that can create immersive and interactive simulations of real or imagined environments, using devices such as headsets, gloves, etc. Examples include VR games, VR therapy, VR training, etc. VR can improve HIS by providing education, training, rehabilitation, pain management, distraction, etc.

- These emerging technologies can offer various benefits for HIS, such as:

  - Improving data quality, availability, and interoperability
  - Enhancing patient-centered and personalized care
  - Supporting evidence-based and data-driven decision-making
  - Reducing errors, risks, and costs
  - Increasing efficiency, productivity, and innovation

- However, these emerging technologies also pose various challenges for HIS, such as:

  - Ensuring data security, privacy, and ethics
  - Addressing technical, regulatory, and legal barriers
  - Managing data volume, complexity, and diversity
  - Evaluating effectiveness, impact, and value
  - Developing skills, competencies, and capacities

- Therefore, it is important to adopt a holistic and strategic approach to integrate these emerging technologies into HIS, considering the needs, preferences, and contexts of the stakeholders, and the opportunities, risks, and trade-offs of the technologies.
