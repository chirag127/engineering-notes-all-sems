### Intelligent Transportation Systems and Wireless Access in Vehicular Environment Technology for Developing Smart Cities

- Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITS) are the application of advanced technologies to improve the efficiency, safety, and sustainability of transportation systems in smart cities  .
- ITS involve the use of sensors, communication devices, data analytics, and artificial intelligence to collect, process, and share information among different transportation modes, infrastructure, and users .
- Wireless Access in Vehicular Environment (WAVE) is a set of standards and protocols for wireless communication between vehicles and roadside units in ITS  .
- WAVE enables Vehicle-to-Vehicle (V2V) and Vehicle-to-Infrastructure (V2I) communication, which are collectively known as Vehicle-to-Everything (V2X) communication  .
- WAVE supports various applications and services for ITS, such as traffic management, safety warning, navigation, infotainment, and environmental monitoring  .
- WAVE consists of four main components: the physical layer, the medium access control layer, the networking layer, and the security layer  .
- The physical layer defines the frequency band, modulation scheme, and data rate for wireless transmission  .
- The medium access control layer defines the channel access mechanism, frame format, and quality of service for wireless transmission  .
- The networking layer defines the addressing scheme, routing protocol, and message format for wireless transmission  .
- The security layer defines the encryption, authentication, and integrity protection for wireless transmission  .
- WAVE is based on the IEEE 802.11p standard, which is an extension of the IEEE 802.11 standard for wireless local area networks (WLANs)  .
- WAVE also follows the IEEE 1609 family of standards, which specify the security, networking, and application services for WAVE  .
- WAVE is compatible with other wireless technologies, such as cellular networks, Bluetooth, and Wi-Fi  .
- WAVE is a key technology for developing smart cities, as it can enable various benefits, such as improved traffic flow, reduced congestion, enhanced safety, lower emissions, and better user experience     .
