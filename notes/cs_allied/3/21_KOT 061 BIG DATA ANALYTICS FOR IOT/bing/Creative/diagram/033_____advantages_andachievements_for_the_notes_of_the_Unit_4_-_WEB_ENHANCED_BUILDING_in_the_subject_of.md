### Advantages and Achievements for the Notes of the Unit 4 - Web Enhanced Building in the Subject of KOT 061 Big Data Analytics for IoT KCS

- Web enhanced building is a concept that uses the Internet of Things (IoT) to collect, process, and analyze data from various sensors and devices installed in a building, such as temperature, humidity, lighting, occupancy, energy consumption, etc.
- Web enhanced building can provide many advantages for building owners, managers, and occupants, such as:
  - Improved energy efficiency and reduced carbon footprint by optimizing the use of resources and minimizing waste.
  - Enhanced security and safety by detecting and preventing potential threats and hazards, such as fire, intrusion, leakage, etc..
  - Increased comfort and productivity by adjusting the environmental conditions and services according to the preferences and needs of the occupants.
  - Reduced operational and maintenance costs by monitoring the performance and condition of the building systems and equipment and providing timely alerts and recommendations.
  - Increased value and competitiveness by offering smart and sustainable features and services that attract and retain customers and tenants.
- Web enhanced building can also enable new opportunities and innovations for big data analytics, such as:
  - Reusing IoT data across multiple parts of the organization for different purposes and goals, such as marketing, customer service, quality assurance, etc..
  - Applying advanced techniques and tools, such as machine learning, artificial intelligence, cloud computing, etc., to extract meaningful insights and patterns from the large and complex IoT data.
  - Developing intelligent and adaptive applications and solutions that can leverage the IoT data and analytics to provide real-time feedback and actions, such as smart lighting, smart HVAC, smart parking, etc..
  - Creating new business models and value propositions that can generate revenue and profit from the IoT data and analytics, such as data monetization, data sharing, data as a service, etc..
- Some examples of achievements and applications of web enhanced building are:
  - The Korea Institute of Science and Technology (KIST) has developed a smart building system that can monitor and control the indoor environment, energy consumption, and security of the building using IoT sensors and devices, cloud computing, and big data analytics.
  - The Microsoft Azure IoT platform provides a comprehensive solution for building smart buildings that can connect, monitor, and manage the IoT devices and data, and integrate with other Azure services, such as Azure Digital Twins, Azure IoT Central, Azure IoT Hub, etc..
  - The Amazon Web Services (AWS) IoT platform offers a visualization and monitoring dashboard for IoT data that can capture, transform, and load streaming data from IoT devices into AWS using Amazon Kinesis Firehose, and then visualize and analyze the data using Amazon QuickSight.
