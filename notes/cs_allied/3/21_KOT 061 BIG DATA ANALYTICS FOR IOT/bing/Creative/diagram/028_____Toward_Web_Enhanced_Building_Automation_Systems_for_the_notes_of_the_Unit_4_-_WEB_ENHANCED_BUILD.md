### Toward Web Enhanced Building Automation Systems

- Building automation systems (BAS) are systems that control and monitor the mechanical, electrical, and plumbing systems of a building, such as heating, ventilation, air conditioning, lighting, security, fire alarm, etc.
- BAS are typically proprietary and closed systems that use specific protocols and interfaces that are not compatible with the Internet or other systems.
- Web enhanced building automation systems (WEBAS) are systems that leverage the Internet and web technologies to provide more functionalities and interoperability for BAS, such as remote access, data analysis, visualization, integration, and automation.
- WEBAS can benefit from the Internet of Things (IoT) paradigms, which aim to connect physical objects to the Internet and enable machine-to-machine communication, data exchange, and service discovery.
- WEBAS can also benefit from the Semantic Web technologies, which aim to provide a common framework for data representation, annotation, and reasoning, and enable intelligent and context-aware applications.
- Some of the challenges and requirements for WEBAS are:

  - Discovery: how to find and identify the devices and services that are part of a BAS and provide relevant information about them, such as location, type, status, capabilities, etc.
  - Query: how to access and manipulate the data and services of a BAS using standard and expressive languages and protocols, such as HTTP, REST, SPARQL, etc.
  - Security: how to ensure the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of the data and services of a BAS, and prevent unauthorized access, modification, or disruption.
  - Scalability: how to handle the large number and diversity of devices and services that are part of a BAS, and cope with the dynamic and heterogeneous nature of the environment.
  - Integration: how to enable the interoperability and collaboration of different BAS and other systems, such as smart grids, smart cities, smart homes, etc.
  - Automation: how to enable the self-management and optimization of a BAS, and provide intelligent and adaptive control and decision making.

- Some of the existing solutions and frameworks for WEBAS are:

  - WebCTRL: a web-based platform that provides facility managers with software tools to control, manage, report, and analyze the performance of a BAS.
  - APOGEE: a flexible and scalable system that provides controllers, field devices, and software for a BAS, and supports various communication protocols and standards, such as BACnet, Modbus, OPC, etc.
  - BMS: a comprehensive range of controllers, field devices, and software that provide customized solutions for a BAS, and enable energy efficiency, comfort, and security.
  - BuildingsWoT: a framework that uses the Web of Things (WoT) and Semantic Web technologies to provide a uniform and interoperable access to the devices and services of a BAS, and enable discovery, query, and automation .
