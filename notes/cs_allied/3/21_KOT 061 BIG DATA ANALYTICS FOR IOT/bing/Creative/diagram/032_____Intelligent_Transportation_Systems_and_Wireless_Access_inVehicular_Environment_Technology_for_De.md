# Intelligent Transportation Systems and Wireless Access in Vehicular Environment Technology for Developing Smart Cities

## Introduction

- A smart city is a city that employs top-grade technological applications to facilitate operations, enhance quality of life, and improve sustainability.
- Intelligent transportation systems (ITS) involve the use of advanced transportation technologies, such as sensors, communication, data analysis, and automation, to improve mobility, safety, efficiency, and environmental performance of transportation systems.
- Wireless access in vehicular environment (WAVE) is a set of standards and protocols that enable wireless communication among vehicles and between vehicles and infrastructure, such as roadside units, traffic lights, and service providers.
- ITS and WAVE are enabling technologies for future smart cities, as they can support various applications and services, such as traffic management, road safety, vehicle-to-everything (V2X) communication, electric vehicle charging, and port modernization  .

## Intelligent Transportation Systems

- ITS can be classified into five main categories, according to the type of transportation mode, infrastructure, or service involved:

  - Advanced traveler information systems (ATIS): provide real-time information to travelers, such as traffic conditions, route guidance, public transit schedules, parking availability, and weather forecasts.
  - Advanced public transportation systems (APTS): improve the operation and management of public transit systems, such as buses, trains, and taxis, by using technologies such as automatic vehicle location, smart card payment, passenger counting, and dynamic scheduling.
  - Advanced traffic management systems (ATMS): optimize the flow and control of traffic, by using technologies such as traffic sensors, adaptive signal control, ramp metering, incident detection, and congestion pricing.
  - Advanced vehicle control systems (AVCS): enhance the performance and safety of vehicles, by using technologies such as adaptive cruise control, lane keeping, collision avoidance, and autonomous driving.
  - Commercial vehicle operations (CVO): improve the efficiency and safety of freight transportation, by using technologies such as electronic toll collection, weigh-in-motion, hazardous material monitoring, and fleet management.

- ITS can offer various benefits for smart cities, such as:

  - Reducing travel time, fuel consumption, greenhouse gas emissions, and accidents.
  - Increasing accessibility, reliability, comfort, and convenience for travelers.
  - Improving productivity, competitiveness, and profitability for transportation operators and service providers.
  - Enhancing social equity, public health, and quality of life for citizens.

## Wireless Access in Vehicular Environment

- WAVE is based on the IEEE 802.11p standard, which defines the physical and medium access control layers for wireless communication in the 5.9 GHz band, with a range of up to 1000 meters and a data rate of up to 27 Mbps.
- WAVE also includes the IEEE 1609 family of standards, which define the higher layers of the communication stack, such as security, networking, and application.
- WAVE supports two types of communication modes:

  - Vehicle-to-vehicle (V2V): enables direct communication among vehicles, without relying on infrastructure, to exchange safety-related information, such as speed, position, direction, and braking status.
  - Vehicle-to-infrastructure (V2I): enables communication between vehicles and infrastructure, such as roadside units, traffic lights, and service providers, to exchange traffic-related information, such as traffic conditions, signal timing, road hazards, and service availability.

- WAVE can support various applications and services for smart cities, such as :

  - Cooperative driving: enables vehicles to coordinate their actions and movements, such as platooning, lane changing, and intersection crossing, to improve traffic efficiency and safety.
  - Intelligent parking: enables vehicles to find and reserve parking spaces, and pay for parking fees, by using wireless communication with parking facilities and service providers.
  - Electric vehicle charging: enables vehicles to locate and access charging stations, and pay for electricity, by using wireless communication with charging facilities and service providers.
  - Roadside assistance: enables vehicles to request and receive assistance, such as towing, repair, or emergency services
