### Emerging Technologies in Health Information Systems

- Health information systems (HIS) are systems that collect, store, process, and communicate health-related data for decision-making and planning in healthcare delivery and management.
- Emerging technologies are technologies that are novel, innovative, and have the potential to disrupt or transform existing practices, markets, or industries.
- Some of the emerging technologies in HIS are:

  - **Artificial intelligence (AI) and machine learning (ML)**: AI and ML are technologies that enable machines to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence, such as reasoning, learning, decision-making, and natural language processing. AI and ML can be applied to HIS to improve diagnosis, treatment, prediction, personalization, and automation of healthcare processes .
  - **Big data and analytics**: Big data refers to the large, complex, and diverse datasets that are generated from various sources, such as electronic health records, wearable devices, social media, and genomic sequencing. Analytics is the process of extracting meaningful insights and patterns from big data using statistical, computational, and visualization techniques. Big data and analytics can be used to HIS to enhance quality, efficiency, safety, and outcomes of healthcare delivery and research .
  - **Telehealth and remote care**: Telehealth and remote care are technologies that enable the delivery of healthcare services and information over distance, using communication devices, such as smartphones, tablets, computers, and sensors. Telehealth and remote care can be used to HIS to increase access, convenience, affordability, and continuity of care, especially for rural, underserved, and vulnerable populations .
  - **Digital therapeutics**: Digital therapeutics are technologies that deliver evidence-based interventions to prevent, manage, or treat a medical condition, using software applications, such as mobile apps, web platforms, or wearable devices. Digital therapeutics can be used to HIS to complement or substitute traditional therapies, such as medication, counseling, or surgery, and to empower patients to self-manage their health and wellness .
  - **Virtual reality (VR)**: VR is a technology that creates an immersive and interactive simulation of a three-dimensional environment, using devices, such as headsets, gloves, or controllers. VR can be used to HIS to provide training, education, therapy, and entertainment for healthcare professionals, patients, and the general public .

- These emerging technologies in HIS have the potential to improve the quality, efficiency, accessibility, and affordability of healthcare, as well as to create new opportunities and challenges for healthcare stakeholders, such as providers, payers, regulators, researchers, and consumers. Therefore, it is important to understand, evaluate, and adopt these technologies in a responsible, ethical, and sustainable manner.
