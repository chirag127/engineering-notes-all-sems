### Citizen Actuation

- Citizen actuation is the activation of a human being as the mechanism by which a control system acts upon the environment.
- It is a way of closing the loop started by citizen sensing, which is the collection and analysis of data from citizens about events in their surrounding environment.
- Citizen actuation aims to make the citizen reports an actionable item, by sending a task to suitable occupants of an environment to complete.
- Citizen actuation can be used for smart environments, such as smart buildings, smart cities, or smart grids, to improve sustainability, efficiency, and user satisfaction.
- Citizen actuation can also be used for social good, such as disaster response, civic engagement, or community building.
- Citizen actuation requires a framework that can identify the relevant events, select the appropriate actuation channels, match the tasks with the suitable citizens, and monitor the task execution and feedback.
- Citizen actuation also requires a governance model that can ensure the quality, security, privacy, and ethics of the data and the actions involved.
- Citizen actuation can benefit from the existing enterprise automation function, which can provide the tools, training, and support for the citizen automation process.
- Citizen actuation can have positive impacts on the environment and the society, such as reducing energy consumption, increasing awareness, and fostering collaboration  .
- Citizen actuation can also face some challenges, such as user motivation, trust, reliability, and scalability  .
