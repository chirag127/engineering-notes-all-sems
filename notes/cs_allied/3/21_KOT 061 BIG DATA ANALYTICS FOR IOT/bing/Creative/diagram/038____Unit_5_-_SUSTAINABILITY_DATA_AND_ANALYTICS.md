## Unit 5 - SUSTAINABILITY DATA AND ANALYTICS

- This unit introduces the concepts and methods of sustainability data and analytics, which are essential for measuring, monitoring and improving the environmental, social and economic performance of organizations and systems.
- The unit covers the following topics:

  - The definition and scope of sustainability data and analytics, and their applications in various domains and sectors.
  - The sources and types of sustainability data, such as environmental, social and governance (ESG) data, life cycle assessment (LCA) data, carbon footprint data, etc.
  - The methods and tools for collecting, processing, analyzing and visualizing sustainability data, such as surveys, sensors, databases, software, dashboards, etc.
  - The challenges and opportunities of sustainability data and analytics, such as data quality, availability, interoperability, privacy, ethics, etc.
  - The case studies and examples of sustainability data and analytics in practice, such as corporate sustainability reporting, sustainable supply chain management, smart cities, etc.

- The learning outcomes of this unit are:

  - To understand the concepts and principles of sustainability data and analytics, and their relevance and importance for sustainability management and decision making.
  - To identify and evaluate the sources and types of sustainability data, and their advantages and limitations for different purposes and contexts.
  - To apply the methods and tools for collecting, processing, analyzing and visualizing sustainability data, and to interpret and communicate the results and insights.
  - To recognize and address the challenges and opportunities of sustainability data and analytics, and to critically reflect on their ethical and social implications.
  - To appreciate and learn from the case studies and examples of sustainability data and analytics in practice, and to explore the potential and limitations of data-driven sustainability solutions.
