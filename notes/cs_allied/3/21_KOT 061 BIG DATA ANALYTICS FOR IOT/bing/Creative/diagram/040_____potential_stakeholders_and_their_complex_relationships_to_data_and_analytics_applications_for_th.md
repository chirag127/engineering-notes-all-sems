### Potential stakeholders and their complex relationships to data and analytics applications

- Stakeholders are individuals or groups that have an interest or influence in the data and analytics applications of a business or organization .
- Stakeholders can have different roles, responsibilities, expectations, and needs regarding the data and analytics applications, and they can also have different levels of power, interest, and influence over the decision-making and outcomes  .
- Stakeholders can be classified into two main types: primary and secondary.
  - Primary stakeholders, also known as internal stakeholders, are directly associated with a business and include people like an employee, shareholder, customer, supplier or creditor.
  - Secondary stakeholders, also known as external stakeholders, are not directly associated with a business but can affect or be affected by its data and analytics applications, such as regulators, competitors, media, or community.
- Stakeholders can have complex relationships to data and analytics applications, depending on the type, purpose, and value of the data and analytics involved .
  - For example, some stakeholders may be involved in data acquisition, data analysis, data curation, data storage, or data utilization, while others may only be interested in the results or outcomes of the data and analytics applications .
  - Some stakeholders may have conflicting or competing interests or goals regarding the data and analytics applications, such as privacy, security, quality, accuracy, or profitability .
  - Some stakeholders may have different levels of access, control, or ownership over the data and analytics applications, such as data providers, data consumers, data owners, or data stewards .
- Stakeholder management is the process of identifying, analyzing, engaging, and communicating with the stakeholders to ensure their expectations and needs are met, and their interests and influence are aligned with the data and analytics objectives and strategies of the business or organization  .
  - Stakeholder management involves understanding the stakeholder's perspective, value proposition, and personal impact of the data and analytics applications, and delivering the results and insights in a clear, concise, and compelling way .
  - Stakeholder management also involves prioritizing, negotiating, and resolving any issues or conflicts that may arise among the stakeholders regarding the data and analytics applications, and ensuring their feedback and input are incorporated and valued .
