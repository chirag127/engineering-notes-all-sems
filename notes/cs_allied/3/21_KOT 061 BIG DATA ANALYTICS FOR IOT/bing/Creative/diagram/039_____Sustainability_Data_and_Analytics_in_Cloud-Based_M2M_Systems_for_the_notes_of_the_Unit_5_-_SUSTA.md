### Sustainability Data and Analytics in Cloud-Based M2M Systems

- M2M stands for machine-to-machine communication, which enables devices to exchange data and perform actions without human intervention.
- Cloud-based M2M systems provide scalable, flexible, and cost-effective solutions for managing large-scale data and processes from various sources, such as sensors, smart meters, and smart devices.
- Sustainability governance is the process of ensuring that the environmental, social, and economic impacts of an organization or a system are minimized and optimized.
- Data analytics is the process of extracting meaningful insights from data using various techniques, such as statistics, machine learning, and visualization.
- Sustainability data and analytics in cloud-based M2M systems aim to link monitored objects, monitoring data, and applications to stakeholders' needs and provide a platform for accessing and performing data analytics of their own interest.
- The main challenges of sustainability data and analytics in cloud-based M2M systems are:
  - How to identify and analyze the stakeholders and their requirements for data analytics in different domains and scenarios, such as smart buildings, smart cities, and smart grids.
  - How to link, enrich, and manage data from heterogeneous sources and formats, such as raw sensor data, metadata, and semantic annotations.
  - How to provide a platform-as-a-service (PaaS) that supports data and process integration, data storage and management, data access and security, and data analytics tools and frameworks.
  - How to evaluate the performance, scalability, and usability of the cloud-based data analytics system and the quality and usefulness of the data analytics results.
- The main contributions of the topic are:
  - A detailed analysis of stakeholders in cloud-based sustainability governance in smart buildings and their requirements for data analytics .
  - Techniques for linking, enriching, and managing data for sustainability analysis in M2M environments .
  - A data analytics system including a PaaS and an analytics framework for managing relationships between stakeholders, monitored objects, monitoring data, and applications .
  - A prototype of the data analytics system based on a real-world cloud system for facility monitoring and analytics .
