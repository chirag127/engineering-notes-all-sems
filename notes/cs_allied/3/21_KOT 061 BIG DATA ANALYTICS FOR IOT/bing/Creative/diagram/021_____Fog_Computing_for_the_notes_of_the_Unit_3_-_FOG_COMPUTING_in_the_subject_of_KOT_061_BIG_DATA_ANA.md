### Fog Computing

- Fog computing is a decentralized computing infrastructure that uses edge devices to carry out computation, storage, and communication locally and routed over the Internet backbone .
- Fog computing is also known as edge computing or fogging .
- Fog computing is intended for distributed computing where numerous devices connect to a cloud, but closer to the data source and the end users .
- Fog computing can provide low latency, high bandwidth, real-time data processing, security, and privacy for IoT applications  .
- Fog computing can also reduce the network congestion and the load on the cloud servers by filtering, aggregating, and analyzing the data locally  .
- Fog computing is not a substitute, but a complement to cloud computing. Fog computing is more energy-efficient than cloud computing .
- Fog computing follows the standards proposed by OpenFog Consortium and adopted by IEEE.
