### Advantages and Achievements for the Notes of the Unit 4 - WEB ENHANCED BUILDING in the Subject of KOT 061 BIG DATA ANALYTICS FOR IOT KCS

- Web enhanced building is a concept that uses the Internet of Things (IoT) to connect various devices and sensors in a building and collect, process, and analyze the data generated by them.
- Web enhanced building can provide various advantages such as:
  - Improved energy efficiency and sustainability by optimizing the use of resources and reducing greenhouse gas emissions.
  - Enhanced security and safety by detecting and preventing potential threats and hazards.
  - Increased comfort and productivity by adjusting the environmental conditions and providing personalized services to the occupants.
  - Reduced operational and maintenance costs by monitoring and diagnosing the performance and health of the building systems.
- Web enhanced building can also achieve various achievements such as:
  - Creating a visualization and monitoring dashboard for IoT data with Amazon Kinesis Analytics and Amazon QuickSight.
  - Developing an IoT-enabled big data analytics architecture for multimedia data processing and computation.
  - Implementing an integrated IoT big data analytics framework for the storage and analysis of real time data generated from IoT sensors deployed inside the smart building.
  - Optimizing data analytics with the Internet of Things by reusing, refining, and reporting the IoT data across multiple parts of the organization.
