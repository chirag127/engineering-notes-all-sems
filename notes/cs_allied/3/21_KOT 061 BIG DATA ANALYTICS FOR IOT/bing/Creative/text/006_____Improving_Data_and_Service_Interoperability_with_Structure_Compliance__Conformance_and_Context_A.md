### Improving Data and Service Interoperability with Structure, Compliance, Conformance and Context Awareness

- Interoperability is the ability of different systems or components to exchange and use information effectively and efficiently.
- Interoperability is essential for the Internet of Things (IoT), which involves a large number of heterogeneous devices, data sources and services that need to communicate and collaborate.
- Interoperability can be achieved at different levels, such as syntactic, semantic, pragmatic and dynamic.
- Syntactic interoperability refers to the common formats and protocols for data exchange, such as XML, JSON, HTTP, etc.
- Semantic interoperability refers to the common meanings and definitions of data and services, such as ontologies, vocabularies, schemas, etc.
- Pragmatic interoperability refers to the common goals and expectations of data and service providers and consumers, such as contracts, policies, quality of service, etc.
- Dynamic interoperability refers to the ability of data and service providers and consumers to adapt to changing situations and requirements, such as context, preferences, availability, etc.
- Interoperability can be improved by using four concepts: structure, compliance, conformance and context awareness.
- Structure refers to the organization and representation of data and services, such as resources, links, methods, etc.
- Compliance refers to the adherence of data and services to a set of rules or constraints, such as standards, specifications, formats, etc.
- Conformance refers to the compatibility of data and services with a given schema or model, such as XML Schema, JSON Schema, etc.
- Context awareness refers to the ability of data and services to capture and use relevant information about the situation and environment, such as location, time, user, device, etc.
- The chapter proposes an architectural style, Structural Services, which combines the service modelling capabilities of SOA with the flexibility of structured resources in REST.
- Structural Services aim to enhance interoperability, especially in terms of heterogeneity (variety), by using compliance and conformance (less restrictive than schema sharing) and context awareness (which increases the range of applicability).
- Structural Services use a context model that contemplates forward and backward contextual information, which can be used to improve data and service discovery, selection, composition and adaptation.
- The chapter also presents some examples and applications of Structural Services in the IoT domain, such as smart cities, smart homes, smart grids, etc.
