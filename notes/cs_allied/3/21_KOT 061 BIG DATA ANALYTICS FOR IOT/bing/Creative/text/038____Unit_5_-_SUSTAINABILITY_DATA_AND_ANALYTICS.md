## Unit 5 - SUSTAINABILITY DATA AND ANALYTICS

- This unit introduces the concepts and methods of sustainability data and analytics, which are essential for measuring, monitoring, and improving the environmental, social, and economic performance of organizations and systems.
- The unit covers the following topics:
  - The definition and scope of sustainability data and analytics, and the main sources and types of data relevant for sustainability assessment and decision making.
  - The principles and standards of sustainability reporting and disclosure, and the main frameworks and tools for collecting, managing, and communicating sustainability data and information.
  - The techniques and applications of sustainability analysis and modeling, such as life cycle assessment, carbon footprinting, material flow analysis, and system dynamics.
  - The challenges and opportunities of sustainability data and analytics in the context of big data, artificial intelligence, and digital transformation, and the ethical and social implications of data-driven sustainability solutions.
- The unit aims to provide students with the knowledge and skills to:
  - Understand the role and value of sustainability data and analytics in supporting sustainability goals and strategies.
  - Identify and evaluate the quality and reliability of sustainability data and information from various sources and stakeholders.
  - Apply appropriate methods and tools to collect, process, analyze, and visualize sustainability data and information.
  - Communicate and report sustainability data and information effectively and transparently to different audiences and purposes.
  - Critically reflect on the limitations and uncertainties of sustainability data and analytics, and the ethical and social issues related to data collection, use, and sharing.
