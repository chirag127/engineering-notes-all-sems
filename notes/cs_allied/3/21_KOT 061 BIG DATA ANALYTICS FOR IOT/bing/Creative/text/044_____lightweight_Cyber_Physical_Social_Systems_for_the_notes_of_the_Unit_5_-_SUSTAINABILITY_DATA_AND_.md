### Lightweight Cyber Physical Social Systems

- Cyber Physical Social Systems (CPSS) are systems that integrate cyber, physical and social components to provide enhanced services and user experiences.
- Cyber components include computing, communication and sensing devices that interact with physical components such as machines, buildings and vehicles.
- Social components include human users, groups and communities that interact with cyber and physical components through social networks, platforms and applications .
- Lightweight CPSS are CPSS that have low computational and communication overhead, high scalability and adaptability, and minimal impact on the physical and social environments .
- Lightweight CPSS can be applied to various domains such as facility management, smart cities, healthcare, education, entertainment and security .
- Some of the benefits of lightweight CPSS are:
  - Improved efficiency, monitoring, controllability and reliability of physical systems.
  - Enhanced user satisfaction, engagement, participation and collaboration in social systems.
  - Reduced energy consumption, resource utilization and environmental footprint of cyber systems.
  - Increased resilience, robustness, security and privacy of CPSS against malicious attacks and failures .
- Some of the challenges and opportunities of lightweight CPSS are:
  - Developing novel architectures, models, algorithms and protocols for lightweight CPSS that can handle the complexity, heterogeneity, uncertainty and dynamics of cyber, physical and social components.
  - Leveraging big data analytics, machine learning, artificial intelligence and blockchain technologies to extract useful insights, optimize performance and ensure trustworthiness of lightweight CPSS .
  - Balancing the trade-offs between functionality, quality, cost and risk of lightweight CPSS in different scenarios and contexts.
  - Addressing the ethical, legal and social implications of lightweight CPSS on human values, rights and responsibilities.
