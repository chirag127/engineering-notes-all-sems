### Adaptive Pipelined Neural Network Structure in Self-aware Internet of Things

- Self-aware Internet of Things (IoT) is a concept that aims to enable IoT devices to monitor, interpret, and adapt to their own states and environments in a dynamic and autonomous manner.
- Self-aware IoT devices can improve the reliability, performance, and efficiency of IoT systems, especially in complex and changing scenarios such as smart environments.
- Adaptive pipelined neural network (APNN) is a novel technique that applies a modified version of pipelined recurrent neural network (PRNN) to self-aware IoT devices.
- APNN consists of three main components: a sensor layer, a hidden layer, and an output layer.
- The sensor layer collects and preprocesses the data from the IoT device and its environment, such as temperature, humidity, battery level, etc.
- The hidden layer consists of multiple PRNN modules that are connected in a pipeline fashion. Each PRNN module has a recurrent feedback loop that allows it to store and update its internal state based on the previous and current inputs.
- The output layer produces the desired output or action for the IoT device, such as adjusting the power consumption, switching the mode, or sending a notification.
- APNN can adapt to the changing conditions and requirements of the IoT device and its environment by dynamically adjusting the number, size, and configuration of the PRNN modules in the hidden layer.
- APNN can also learn from the feedback and evaluation of its output and performance, and update its parameters and weights accordingly.
- APNN has been tested and evaluated in different domains, such as smart home, smart grid, and smart health . The results show that APNN can achieve high accuracy, low latency, and low energy consumption in self-aware IoT applications .
