### Big Data Platforms for the Internet of Things

- Big data platforms are software systems that enable the collection, processing, analysis, and visualization of large and complex data sets generated by the Internet of Things (IoT) devices and applications.
- IoT devices are physical objects that are embedded with sensors, actuators, and communication capabilities that allow them to interact with each other and with the environment.
- IoT applications are software programs that use the data from IoT devices to provide various services and functionalities, such as smart home, smart city, smart health, smart agriculture, etc.
- Big data platforms for the IoT can be classified into three main types: connectivity platforms, cloud platforms, and analytics platforms.
- Connectivity platforms provide communication technologies to connect physical objects with the data center (on-premise or cloud) and transmit information between them. Among popular connectivity protocols and standards for the Internet of Things are MQTT, DDS, AMQP, Bluetooth, ZigBee, WiFi, Cellular, LoRaWAN and more.
- Cloud platforms provide storage, computing, and management services for the data collected by IoT devices. They also offer scalability, reliability, security, and cost-effectiveness for the IoT applications. Some of the most popular cloud platforms for creating Internet of Things systems are Amazon Web Services (AWS), Microsoft Azure, Google Cloud Platform, Artik from Samsung Electronics, Cisco Cloud Connect, Salesforce Cloud, Watson and BlueMix from IBM, OpenStack  .
- Analytics platforms provide tools and techniques for extracting insights and value from the data generated by IoT devices and applications. They can perform various tasks, such as data cleaning, integration, transformation, aggregation, mining, visualization, and machine learning. Some of the examples of big data analytics platforms for the IoT are Nexus Integra, Apache Spark, Apache Hadoop, Apache Kafka, Apache Storm, Apache Flink, Apache NiFi, Apache Beam, etc .
- Big data platforms for the IoT are essential for enabling the development and deployment of innovative and intelligent solutions that can improve the quality of life, enhance the efficiency of operations, and create new business opportunities.
