## Unit 4 - WEB ENHANCED BUILDING

- A web enhanced building is a building that uses web technologies to improve its functionality, efficiency, and user experience.
- A web enhanced building can have different levels of web integration, from web-enhanced courses that use online platforms to supplement classroom learning, to web-enhanced building automation systems that use Internet of Things (IoT) devices to monitor and control various aspects of the building .
- Some benefits of web enhanced building are:
  - Increased accessibility and convenience for users, who can access information and services anytime and anywhere through the web.
  - Reduced operational costs and energy consumption, as web-enabled devices can optimize the performance and usage of building resources.
  - Enhanced security and safety, as web-enabled devices can detect and respond to emergencies and threats.
  - Improved user satisfaction and engagement, as web-enabled devices can provide personalized and interactive features.
- Some challenges of web enhanced building are:
  - Technical complexity and compatibility, as web-enabled devices may have different standards, protocols, and interfaces that need to be integrated and coordinated.
  - Data privacy and security, as web-enabled devices may collect and transmit sensitive information that needs to be protected from unauthorized access and misuse.
  - Ethical and social implications, as web-enabled devices may affect the behavior and well-being of users and stakeholders in various ways.
