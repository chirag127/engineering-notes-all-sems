# Improving Data and Service Interoperability with Structure, Compliance, Conformance and Context Awareness

- Interoperability is the ability of different systems, devices, or applications to exchange and use data or services without requiring special efforts from the users or developers.
- Interoperability is essential for the Internet of Things (IoT), which involves a large number of heterogeneous and distributed entities that need to communicate and cooperate to achieve common goals.
- Interoperability can be achieved at different levels, such as syntactic, semantic, or pragmatic.
- Syntactic interoperability refers to the common format and structure of the data or messages exchanged.
- Semantic interoperability refers to the common meaning and interpretation of the data or messages exchanged.
- Pragmatic interoperability refers to the common understanding and expectations of the behavior and functionality of the services or applications exchanged.
- Interoperability can be improved by using various techniques, such as:
  - Structure: using common or compatible data models, schemas, or formats to represent the data or messages exchanged .
  - Compliance: using common or compatible standards, protocols, or specifications to define the rules and constraints of the data or messages exchanged .
  - Conformance: using common or compatible methods, tools, or tests to verify and validate the compliance of the data or messages exchanged .
  - Context awareness: using common or compatible mechanisms, models, or metadata to capture and convey the relevant information about the situation, environment, or purpose of the data or messages exchanged.
- An example of an architectural style that combines these techniques is Structural Services, which integrates the service modeling capabilities of Service-Oriented Architecture (SOA) with the flexibility of structured resources in Representational State Transfer (REST).
- Structural Services use structured resources as the basic unit of data and service exchange, which can be composed, decomposed, or transformed according to the needs and preferences of the consumers and providers.
- Structural Services use compliance and conformance to ensure the syntactic and semantic interoperability of the structured resources, and context awareness to ensure the pragmatic interoperability of the structured resources.
- Structural Services can improve the interoperability of data and services in the IoT by addressing the challenges of heterogeneity, scalability, and dynamism.
