# Application of Geographical Concepts and Spatial Technology to the Internet of Things for the notes of the Unit 2 - RFID False Authentications in the subject of KOT 061 BIG DATA ANALYTICS FOR IOT KCS

- RFID (Radio Frequency Identification) is a technology that uses radio waves to identify objects or people by attaching tags to them. RFID tags contain a unique identifier and some data that can be read by RFID readers.
- RFID authentication is the process of verifying the identity and validity of RFID tags and readers. RFID authentication is important for ensuring the security and privacy of RFID systems, especially in applications such as product authentication, access control, and supply chain management .
- RFID false authentication is a situation where a legitimate tag is wrongly rejected by a reader, or an illegitimate tag is wrongly accepted by a reader. RFID false authentication can compromise the integrity and reliability of RFID systems, and lead to potential losses or damages .
- RFID false authentication can be caused by various factors, such as:
  - Tag or reader malfunction or damage
  - Tag or reader tampering or cloning
  - Tag or reader misconfiguration or mismatch
  - Tag or reader interference or collision
  - Tag or reader protocol or algorithm flaws
- RFID false authentication can be prevented or mitigated by using various techniques, such as:
  - Tag or reader quality control and maintenance
  - Tag or reader encryption and authentication protocols
  - Tag or reader mutual authentication and challenge-response mechanisms
  - Tag or reader randomization and diversification schemes
  - Tag or reader anti-collision and anti-interference methods
  - Tag or reader error detection and correction codes
