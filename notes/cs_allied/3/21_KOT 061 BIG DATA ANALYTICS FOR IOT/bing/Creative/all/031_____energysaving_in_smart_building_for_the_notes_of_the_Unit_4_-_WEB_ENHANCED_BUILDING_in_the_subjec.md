# Energy Saving in Smart Building

- Smart buildings are buildings that use advanced technologies and data analytics to optimize various aspects of building performance, such as energy efficiency, comfort, security, and sustainability.
- Energy saving is one of the main benefits and goals of smart buildings, as buildings account for about 40% of global energy consumption and 36% of greenhouse gas emissions.
- Some of the ways that smart buildings can save energy and lower their carbon footprint are    :

  - Getting a carbon footprint measurement to identify the sources and levels of energy consumption and emissions in the building and set targets for improvement.
  - Using IoT devices and sensors to monitor and control various parameters, such as temperature, humidity, lighting, occupancy, and air quality, and adjust them according to the needs and preferences of the occupants and the external conditions.
  - Doing an energy efficiency retrofit to upgrade the existing equipment and systems, such as HVAC, lighting, windows, insulation, and appliances, with more energy-efficient and smart alternatives.
  - Incorporating renewable energy sources, such as solar panels, wind turbines, or geothermal systems, to generate clean and low-cost electricity or heating/cooling for the building.
  - Managing office space to optimize the utilization and allocation of the available space and avoid wasting energy on unused or underused areas.
  - Maximizing heating and cooling efficiency by using smart thermostats, variable speed drives, heat pumps, and demand response programs to regulate the indoor temperature and reduce the peak load on the grid.
  - Recycling everything, such as paper, plastic, metal, glass, and organic waste, to reduce the amount of waste sent to landfills and the associated emissions and costs.
  - Reducing travel footprint by encouraging telecommuting, video conferencing, carpooling, or using public transportation or electric vehicles for commuting and business trips.
  - Educating and engaging the occupants and stakeholders to raise awareness and promote behavioral changes that can contribute to energy saving and sustainability in the building.
