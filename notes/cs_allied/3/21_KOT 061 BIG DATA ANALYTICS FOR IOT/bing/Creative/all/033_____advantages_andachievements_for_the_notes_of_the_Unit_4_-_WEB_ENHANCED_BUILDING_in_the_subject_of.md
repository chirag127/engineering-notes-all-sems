# Advantages and Achievements for the Notes of the Unit 4 - Web Enhanced Building in the Subject of KOT 061 Big Data Analytics for IoT KCS

- Web enhanced building is a concept that uses the Internet of Things (IoT) to collect, process, and analyze data from various sensors and devices installed in a building, such as temperature, humidity, lighting, security, occupancy, etc.
- Web enhanced building can provide many advantages for building owners, managers, and occupants, such as:
  - Improved energy efficiency and sustainability by optimizing the use of resources and reducing greenhouse gas emissions.
  - Enhanced comfort and productivity by adjusting the environmental conditions according to the preferences and needs of the occupants.
  - Increased safety and security by detecting and preventing potential hazards and intrusions.
  - Reduced operational and maintenance costs by identifying and resolving issues before they become critical.
  - Increased asset value and marketability by demonstrating the performance and benefits of the building.
- Web enhanced building can also enable new opportunities and applications for big data analytics, such as:
  - Predictive analytics: using historical and real-time data to forecast future trends and events, such as energy demand, occupancy patterns, equipment failures, etc.
  - Prescriptive analytics: using data-driven insights to recommend optimal actions and solutions, such as energy-saving strategies, maintenance schedules, emergency responses, etc.
  - Descriptive analytics: using data visualization and reporting tools to summarize and communicate the current and past status and performance of the building, such as energy consumption, environmental quality, occupant satisfaction, etc.
  - Diagnostic analytics: using data mining and machine learning techniques to identify and explain the root causes and factors of problems and anomalies, such as energy waste, comfort complaints, security breaches, etc.
- Some examples of achievements and innovations in web enhanced building are:
  - The Korea Institute of Science and Technology (KIST) developed a smart building system that uses IoT sensors and big data analytics to monitor and control the indoor environment and energy consumption of the building, achieving up to 30% energy savings and 15% improvement in occupant comfort.
  - The Microsoft Azure IoT platform provides a comprehensive solution for building smart buildings, including IoT devices, cloud services, data analytics, and artificial intelligence, enabling customers to create customized and scalable applications for various scenarios, such as energy management, space optimization, asset tracking, etc.
  - The Amazon Web Services (AWS) IoT platform offers a visualization and monitoring dashboard for IoT data, using Amazon Kinesis Firehose and Amazon QuickSight to capture, transform, and load streaming data from IoT devices, and to create interactive charts and graphs to display the data in real time.
