# GenomicsDriven Wellness Tracking and Management System (GO-WELL)

- GO-WELL is a concept of a health information system that uses genomic, clinical, environmental, and behavioral data to provide predictive and personalized care for patients .
- GO-WELL aims to engage patients in their own wellness management by providing them with feedback, recommendations, and alerts based on their genomic profile and risk factors .
- GO-WELL leverages the advances in genomics, bioinformatics, and data analytics to interpret the complex and heterogeneous data sources and generate meaningful and actionable insights for patients and clinicians .
- GO-WELL consists of four main components: data collection, data integration, data analysis, and data presentation .
  - Data collection involves gathering genomic, clinical, environmental, and behavioral data from various sources, such as electronic health records, personal health records, wearable devices, sensors, and self-reports .
  - Data integration involves harmonizing, validating, and storing the collected data in a secure and scalable data warehouse .
  - Data analysis involves applying various methods and algorithms, such as machine learning, data mining, and statistical inference, to extract relevant and useful information from the integrated data .
  - Data presentation involves visualizing and communicating the analysis results to the patients and clinicians through user-friendly and interactive interfaces, such as dashboards, reports, and alerts .
- GO-WELL is designed to be flexible, adaptable, and scalable to accommodate different types of data, users, and diseases .
- GO-WELL is illustrated with an example of prostate cancer, a common and complex disease that has both genetic and environmental influences .
  - GO-WELL collects genomic data from patients' DNA samples, clinical data from their medical records, environmental data from their geographic location and lifestyle, and behavioral data from their self-reports and wearable devices .
  - GO-WELL integrates the collected data and applies various analysis methods to identify the patients' risk factors, such as genetic variants, biomarkers, family history, age, race, diet, smoking, and physical activity .
  - GO-WELL presents the analysis results to the patients and clinicians in a personalized and comprehensive way, such as showing the patients' risk score, risk distribution, risk comparison, risk trend, and risk reduction strategies .
  - GO-WELL also provides feedback, recommendations, and alerts to the patients and clinicians, such as suggesting screening tests, preventive measures, treatment options, and follow-up plans .
- GO-WELL is expected to improve the quality and efficiency of healthcare delivery, as well as the outcomes and satisfaction of patients, by enabling early detection, prevention, and intervention of diseases .
