# Lightweight Cyber Physical Social Systems

- Cyber-physical systems (CPS) are systems that integrate computation, communication, sensing and actuation with physical processes .
- Cyber-physical-social systems (CPSS) are systems that extend CPS with social networks and human interactions  .
- Lightweight CPSS are systems that aim to reduce the complexity, cost and resource consumption of CPSS while maintaining their functionality and performance  .
- Some of the characteristics of lightweight CPSS are:
  - They use distortion-based security mechanisms to protect the data and control operations from adversaries .
  - They leverage the social network data and human feedback to improve the efficiency, monitoring, controllability and reliability of the physical systems  .
  - They employ dynamic stochastic hybrid models to capture the uncertainty and heterogeneity of the CPSS components .
  - They adopt distributed and decentralized algorithms to coordinate the CPSS actions and optimize the system objectives .
- Some of the applications of lightweight CPSS are:
  - Smart homes and buildings that can adjust the temperature, lighting and ventilation based on the occupants' preferences and activities  .
  - Smart grids that can balance the supply and demand of electricity and integrate renewable energy sources .
  - Smart transportation that can optimize the traffic flow and reduce the emissions and accidents  .
  - Smart healthcare that can monitor the patients' health and provide personalized and timely interventions  .
