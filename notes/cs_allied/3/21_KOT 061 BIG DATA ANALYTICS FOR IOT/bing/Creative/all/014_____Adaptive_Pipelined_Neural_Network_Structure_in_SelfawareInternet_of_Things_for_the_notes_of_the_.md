# Adaptive Pipelined Neural Network Structure in Self-aware Internet of Things

- Self-aware Internet of Things (IoT) is a concept that aims to enable IoT devices to monitor, interpret, and adapt to their own states and environments in a dynamic and autonomous manner.
- Self-aware IoT devices can improve the reliability, performance, and efficiency of IoT systems, especially in complex and changing scenarios such as smart environments.
- One of the main challenges of self-aware IoT is to design and implement suitable architectures and algorithms that can support the self-awareness cycle, which consists of four phases: monitoring, interpretation, resolution, and adaptation.
- Adaptive neural networks are proposed as a solution to some of these challenges; monitoring the system and environment, mapping a suitable solution and adapting the system accordingly.
- A novel application of a modified Pipelined Recurrent Neural Network (PRNN) is proposed in this chapter with experiments aimed to assess its applicability to online learning and adaptation in self-aware IoT.
- PRNN is a type of recursive neural network that can process sequential data in a pipelined manner, which reduces the computational complexity and latency.
- The modified PRNN introduces a feedback mechanism that allows the network to adjust its weights and structure based on the error signals and the environmental changes.
- The modified PRNN also incorporates a self-organizing map (SOM) layer that can cluster the input data and provide a high-level representation of the system state.
- The modified PRNN is tested on two scenarios: a smart home system and a smart grid system, both of which involve dynamic and uncertain environments.
- The results show that the modified PRNN can achieve high accuracy and low error rates in both scenarios, and can adapt to the changes in the system and environment in a timely and effective manner.
- The modified PRNN can also reduce the number of neurons and connections in the network, which improves the scalability and efficiency of the network.
- The modified PRNN is a promising technique for implementing self-aware IoT systems that can cope with the challenges of big data and complex environments.
