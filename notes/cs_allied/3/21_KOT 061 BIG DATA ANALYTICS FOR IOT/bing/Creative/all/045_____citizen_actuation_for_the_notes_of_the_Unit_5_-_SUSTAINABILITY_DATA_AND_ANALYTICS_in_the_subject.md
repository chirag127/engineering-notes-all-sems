# Citizen Actuation

- Citizen actuation is the activation of a human being as the mechanism by which a control system acts upon the environment.
- Citizen actuation is a way of closing the loop started by citizen sensing, which is the collection and analysis of data from citizens about events in their surrounding environment.
- Citizen actuation aims to make the reports from citizen sensing an actionable item, by sending a task to suitable occupants of an environment to complete .
- Citizen actuation can be used for smart environments, such as smart buildings, smart cities, or smart grids, to improve their efficiency, sustainability, and resilience.
- Citizen actuation can leverage the existing communication channels, such as social media, mobile applications, or web portals, to reach out to the citizens and motivate them to participate.
- Citizen actuation can benefit from the use of big data analytics and IoT technologies, such as sensors, actuators, cloud computing, and machine learning, to collect, process, and act on the data from citizen sensing.
- Citizen actuation can face some challenges, such as privacy, security, trust, incentives, and feedback, that need to be addressed to ensure the effectiveness and acceptance of the approach.
- Citizen actuation can have positive impacts on the social, environmental, and economic aspects of sustainability, by empowering the citizens, reducing the resource consumption, and saving the costs.
- Citizen actuation can be illustrated by an example of an experiment that showed a reduction in average energy usage of 24% by sending messages to the occupants of a building to turn off the lights or appliances when not needed.
