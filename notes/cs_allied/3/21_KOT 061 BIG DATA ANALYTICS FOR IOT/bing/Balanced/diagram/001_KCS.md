# KCS

- KCS stands for Knowledge-Centered Service, a methodology for creating and maintaining knowledge in an organization.
- KCS is based on four principles: 
  - **Integrate**: Knowledge creation and maintenance should be integrated with the workflow of solving issues and delivering services.
  - **Evolve**: Knowledge should be continuously improved and updated based on feedback and usage.
  - **Collaborate**: Knowledge should be shared and reused across the organization and with external stakeholders.
  - **Leverage**: Knowledge should be used to improve the quality and efficiency of service delivery and to enable self-service and innovation.
- KCS has two loops: the **Solve Loop** and the **Evolve Loop**.
  - The Solve Loop focuses on capturing, structuring, and reusing knowledge at the point of interaction with customers or users.
  - The Evolve Loop focuses on analyzing, improving, and governing knowledge based on demand, usage, and performance metrics.
- KCS has eight practices that guide the implementation and adoption of the methodology:
  - **Content Health**: Ensure that the knowledge base is accurate, complete, and relevant.
  - **Process Integration**: Align the knowledge processes with the service processes and the organizational goals.
  - **Performance Assessment**: Measure the impact and value of knowledge on the service outcomes and the customer satisfaction.
  - **Leadership and Communication**: Engage and support the leadership and the stakeholders in the vision and the benefits of KCS.
  - **Roles and Responsibilities**: Define and assign the roles and responsibilities for creating, maintaining, and using knowledge.
  - **Coaching and Development**: Provide feedback and guidance to the knowledge workers and the knowledge managers.
  - **Technology**: Select and configure the technology tools that support the knowledge processes and the knowledge workers.
  - **Adoption**: Plan and execute the change management and the communication strategies for adopting KCS.
