### Applying spatial relationships, functions, and models for the notes of the Unit 2 - RFID FALSE AUTHENTICATIONS in the subject of KOT 061 BIG DATA ANALYTICS FOR IOT KCS

- Spatial relationships, functions, and models are methods of data analytics that are used to analyze geographic patterns and determine the spatial relationship between physical objects or events.
- Spatial data are the common thread for data and information that are keyed or tagged based on space and time, such as location, distance, direction, and movement.
- Spatial analysis is the collection, display, and manipulation of spatial data, such as street addresses, zip codes, satellite images, and GPS coordinates, to uncover location-based insights and patterns.
- Spatial analysis can be performed using a variety of tools, such as maps, charts, graphs, and spatial statistics, to visualize, explore, and model spatial data.
- Spatial analysis can be applied to various domains and applications, such as urban planning, environmental management, transportation, health, crime, and marketing.
- RFID (Radio Frequency Identification) is a technology that uses radio waves to identify and track objects or people using tags and readers.
- RFID tags are small devices that can be attached to or embedded in objects or people, and store information such as identification, location, and status.
- RFID readers are devices that can communicate with RFID tags and read or write data to them using radio signals.
- RFID false authentication is a type of attack that occurs when an unauthorized RFID reader or tag tries to access or modify data from a legitimate RFID system, such as a smart card, a passport, or a product inventory.
- RFID false authentication can compromise the security, privacy, and integrity of RFID systems and data, and cause financial or personal losses, identity theft, or fraud.
- Big data analytics is the process of extracting valuable insights from large and complex data sets using advanced techniques and tools, such as machine learning, artificial intelligence, and cloud computing.
- Big data analytics can help improve the performance, efficiency, and innovation of various processes and applications, such as business intelligence, decision making, customer service, and product development.
- IoT (Internet of Things) is the network of physical objects or devices that are embedded with sensors, software, and connectivity, and can communicate and exchange data with other devices or systems over the internet.
- IoT can enable various applications and services, such as smart homes, smart cities, smart health, smart agriculture, and smart industry, that can enhance the quality of life, productivity, and sustainability of people and society.
- Big data analytics for IoT is the application of big data analytics techniques and tools to the data generated by IoT devices and systems, such as sensor data, social media data, and location data.
- Big data analytics for IoT can help discover patterns, trends, anomalies, and correlations in IoT data, and provide actionable insights and solutions for various IoT challenges and opportunities, such as security, privacy, reliability, efficiency, and innovation.
