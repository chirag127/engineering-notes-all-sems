### Citizen Actuation for Smart Environments

- Citizen actuation is the activation of a human being as the mechanism by which a control system acts upon the environment.
- Citizen actuation aims to close the loop started by citizen sensing, which is the collection and analysis of data from the surrounding environment by citizens.
- Citizen actuation can be used to improve the sustainability of smart environments by motivating and influencing the behavior of the occupants to reduce energy consumption, waste generation, and carbon footprint  .
- Citizen actuation can be implemented by using various methods, such as:
  - Gamification: applying game elements and mechanics to non-game contexts to increase engagement, motivation, and fun .
  - Persuasion: using communication strategies and techniques to change the attitudes or behaviors of the target audience .
  - Incentivization: offering rewards or benefits to the participants for completing certain actions or tasks .
  - Feedback: providing information or guidance to the participants about their performance or progress .
- Citizen actuation can be evaluated by using various metrics, such as:
  - Energy savings: the amount of energy saved by the participants as a result of the citizen actuation intervention.
  - User satisfaction: the degree of satisfaction or enjoyment of the participants with the citizen actuation intervention.
  - User engagement: the level of involvement or interest of the participants in the citizen actuation intervention.
  - User behavior: the changes in the habits or actions of the participants due to the citizen actuation intervention.
- Citizen actuation can face various challenges, such as:
  - Privacy: the protection of the personal data and preferences of the participants from unauthorized access or use .
  - Trust: the confidence of the participants in the reliability and validity of the citizen actuation intervention .
  - Scalability: the ability of the citizen actuation intervention to handle a large number of participants and data sources .
  - Ethics: the adherence of the citizen actuation intervention to the moral principles and values of the participants and the society .
