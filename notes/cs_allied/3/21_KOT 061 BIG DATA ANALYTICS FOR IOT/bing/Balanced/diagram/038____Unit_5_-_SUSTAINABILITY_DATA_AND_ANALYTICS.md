## Unit 5 - SUSTAINABILITY DATA AND ANALYTICS

- Sustainability data and analytics is the process of collecting, analyzing, and using data to measure and improve the environmental, social, and economic impacts of an organization or a system  .
- Sustainability data and analytics can help organizations to:
  - Identify and prioritize the most relevant sustainability issues and opportunities for their business .
  - Set and track sustainability goals and targets, and report on their progress and performance  .
  - Benchmark their sustainability performance against industry standards and best practices .
  - Engage with stakeholders and communicate their sustainability value proposition  .
  - Innovate and create new products, services, and business models that address sustainability challenges and opportunities  .
- Sustainability data and analytics can be applied to various domains and sectors, such as:
  - Energy and resource efficiency: optimizing the use of energy, water, materials, and other resources, and reducing greenhouse gas emissions and waste  .
  - Supply chain management: ensuring the sustainability of suppliers, products, and services, and minimizing the environmental and social risks and impacts along the value chain  .
  - Customer and market insights: understanding the preferences, behaviors, and needs of customers and stakeholders, and creating value propositions that align with their sustainability expectations  .
  - Sustainable development: supporting the achievement of the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) and other global and local sustainability agendas  .
- Sustainability data and analytics can leverage various sources and types of data, such as:
  - Internal data: data generated by the organization's own operations, processes, systems, and transactions  .
  - External data: data obtained from third-party sources, such as market research, industry reports, government databases, social media, and IoT sensors  .
  - Qualitative data: data that describes the attributes, characteristics, opinions, and perceptions of people, objects, or events  .
  - Quantitative data: data that measures the numerical values, quantities, frequencies, and trends of people, objects, or events  .
- Sustainability data and analytics can employ various methods and tools, such as:
  - Data collection: gathering and organizing data from various sources and formats, such as surveys, interviews, observations, documents, databases, and APIs  .
  - Data cleaning: removing, correcting, or replacing inaccurate, incomplete, or irrelevant data, and ensuring data quality and consistency  .
  - Data analysis: applying statistical, mathematical, or computational techniques to explore, summarize, visualize, and interpret data, and to generate insights and recommendations  .
  - Data modeling: creating and testing hypotheses, assumptions, or scenarios based on data, and using them to make predictions, estimations, or classifications  .
  - Data reporting: communicating and presenting data analysis and modeling results, using charts, graphs, tables, dashboards, or narratives  .
