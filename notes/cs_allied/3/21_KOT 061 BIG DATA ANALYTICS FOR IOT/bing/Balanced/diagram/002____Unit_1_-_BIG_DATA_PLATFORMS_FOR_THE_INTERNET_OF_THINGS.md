## Unit 1 - BIG DATA PLATFORMS FOR THE INTERNET OF THINGS

- The Internet of Things (IoT) is a network of physical objects that can communicate and exchange data with each other and with the cloud.
- Big Data is a term that refers to the large volume, variety, velocity, and veracity of data that is generated by IoT devices and other sources.
- Big Data platforms are software systems that enable the collection, storage, processing, analysis, and visualization of Big Data.
- Big Data platforms for the IoT have some specific challenges and requirements, such as:
  - Handling heterogeneous and unstructured data from different devices and sensors.
  - Providing scalability and elasticity to cope with the increasing number and diversity of IoT devices and data sources.
  - Ensuring security and privacy of the data and the devices.
  - Supporting real-time and near-real-time analytics and decision making.
  - Integrating with other platforms and applications, such as cloud computing, artificial intelligence, and machine learning.
- Some examples of Big Data platforms for the IoT are:
  - Amazon Web Services (AWS) IoT: A cloud-based platform that provides a range of services and tools for connecting, managing, and analyzing IoT devices and data .
  - Microsoft Azure IoT: A cloud-based platform that offers various solutions and services for building, deploying, and managing IoT applications and devices .
  - Google Cloud IoT: A cloud-based platform that enables secure and scalable IoT data ingestion, processing, storage, and analysis using Google's infrastructure and services .
  - Artik from Samsung Electronics: A platform that consists of hardware modules, software tools, cloud services, and security features for developing and deploying IoT solutions .
  - Cisco Cloud Connect: A platform that provides connectivity, security, management, and analytics for IoT devices and data across multiple clouds and networks .
  - Salesforce Cloud: A platform that leverages the capabilities of Salesforce's CRM and other cloud services to connect, monitor, and analyze IoT data and devices .
  - Watson and BlueMix from IBM: A platform that combines IBM's cognitive computing and cloud technologies to enable IoT data integration, analysis, and action .
  - OpenStack: An open-source platform that provides a set of software components for building and managing cloud-based IoT applications and services .
