### GenomicsDriven Wellness Tracking and Management System (GO-WELL)

- GO-WELL is a concept of a health information system that uses genomic variation data, clinical, environmental, and behavioural observations to provide predictive and personalized care for patients .
- GO-WELL aims to engage patients in their own health management by providing them with feedback, recommendations, and alerts based on their genomic profile, risk factors, lifestyle, and preferences .
- GO-WELL consists of four main components: a genomic data repository, a clinical data repository, a wellness tracking and management module, and a web-based user interface .
- The genomic data repository stores the genomic variation data of the patients, such as single nucleotide polymorphisms (SNPs), copy number variations (CNVs), and gene expression data .
- The clinical data repository stores the clinical data of the patients, such as medical history, diagnosis, treatment, and outcomes .
- The wellness tracking and management module analyzes the genomic and clinical data of the patients, and applies various algorithms and models to generate personalized wellness scores, risk assessments, and intervention plans .
- The web-based user interface allows the patients and the healthcare providers to access and interact with the wellness tracking and management module, and to view and update their genomic and clinical data, wellness scores, risk assessments, and intervention plans .
- GO-WELL can be applied to various diseases and conditions, such as prostate cancer, diabetes, cardiovascular diseases, and obesity .
- GO-WELL can benefit the patients by empowering them to take charge of their own health, improving their quality of life, and reducing their healthcare costs .
- GO-WELL can benefit the healthcare providers by enhancing their decision making, improving their patient outcomes, and optimizing their resource allocation .
