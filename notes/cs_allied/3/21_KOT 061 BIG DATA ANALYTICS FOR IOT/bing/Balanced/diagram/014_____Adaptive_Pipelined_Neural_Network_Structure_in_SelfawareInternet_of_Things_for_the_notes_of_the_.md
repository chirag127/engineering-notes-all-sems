### Adaptive Pipelined Neural Network Structure in Self-aware Internet of Things

- Self-aware Internet of Things (IoT) is a concept that aims to enable IoT systems to monitor, interpret, and adapt to their own state and environment in a dynamic and autonomous way.
- Self-aware IoT systems face many challenges, such as complexity, heterogeneity, scalability, mobility, and uncertainty.
- Adaptive neural networks are proposed as a solution to some of these challenges; monitoring the system and environment, mapping a suitable solution and adapting the system accordingly.
- A novel application of a modified Pipelined Recurrent Neural Network (PRNN) is proposed in this chapter with experiments aimed to assess its applicability to online learning and adaptation in self-aware IoT systems.
- PRNN is a type of recursive neural network that consists of multiple stages, each with its own input, output, and hidden layer. The output of each stage is fed as input to the next stage, forming a pipeline of information flow.
- The modified PRNN introduces two features: a feedback loop that allows the output of the last stage to influence the input of the first stage, and a self-organizing map that clusters the input data into different categories and assigns different weights to each stage according to the category.
- The feedback loop enables the PRNN to capture the temporal dependencies and dynamics of the input data, while the self-organizing map enables the PRNN to adapt to the changing patterns and characteristics of the input data.
- The experiments show that the modified PRNN can achieve better performance and accuracy than the original PRNN and other neural network models in online learning and adaptation tasks, such as predicting the temperature and humidity of a smart environment.
- The modified PRNN can also handle noisy and incomplete data, as well as cope with different levels of complexity and uncertainty in the input data.
- The modified PRNN can be applied to various domains and scenarios of self-aware IoT systems, such as smart homes, smart cities, smart health, and smart grids.
- The modified PRNN can provide a flexible and adaptive architecture for self-aware IoT systems, enabling them to learn from their own experience and environment, and to adjust their behavior and functionality accordingly.
