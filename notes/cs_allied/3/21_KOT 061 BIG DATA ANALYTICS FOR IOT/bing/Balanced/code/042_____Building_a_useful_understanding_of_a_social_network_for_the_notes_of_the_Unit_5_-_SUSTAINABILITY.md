### Building a useful understanding of a social network for the notes of the Unit 5 - SUSTAINABILITY DATA AND ANALYTICS in the subject of KOT 061 BIG DATA ANALYTICS FOR IOT KCS

- Social network analysis (SNA) is a method of studying the structure and dynamics of social relationships among actors, such as individuals, groups, organizations, or communities .
- SNA can be used to understand how social networks influence the sustainability of construction activities and the built environment, such as resource use, waste management, stakeholder engagement, innovation, and resilience .
- SNA can also be used to understand how social-ecological networks (SENs) shape the interactions and feedbacks between human and natural systems, such as ecosystem services, biodiversity, governance, and adaptation .
- To build a useful understanding of a social network, the following steps are recommended:
  - Define the research question and the scope of the network, such as the type, level, and boundary of the actors and the relationships.
  - Collect data on the actors and the relationships, using methods such as surveys, interviews, observations, or secondary sources.
  - Analyze the data using network metrics, such as density, centrality, cohesion, subgroups, and roles, to describe the network structure and identify key actors and patterns.
  - Visualize the network using graphs, maps, or matrices, to illustrate the network structure and highlight important features.
  - Interpret the results and draw implications for the research question and the network sustainability, using theories, models, or frameworks.
- SNA can provide useful insights for sustainability data and analytics, such as:
  - Identifying the sources, flows, and gaps of information and resources in the network, and how they affect the network performance and outcomes.
  - Evaluating the network governance and collaboration, and how they influence the network trust, learning, and innovation.
  - Assessing the network resilience and adaptation, and how they depend on the network diversity, redundancy, and connectivity.
  - Recommending strategies for improving the network sustainability, such as enhancing the network communication, coordination, and cooperation.
