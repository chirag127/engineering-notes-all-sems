### Intelligent Transportation Systems and Wireless Access in Vehicular Environment Technology for Developing Smart Cities

- Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITS) are the application of advanced technologies to improve the efficiency, safety, and sustainability of transportation systems in urban areas.
- ITS involve the use of sensors, communication devices, data analytics, and artificial intelligence to monitor, control, and optimize the movement of people and goods in smart cities.
- Wireless Access in Vehicular Environment (WAVE) is a set of standards and protocols for enabling wireless communication among vehicles and between vehicles and infrastructure in ITS.
- WAVE supports various applications and services for ITS, such as vehicle-to-vehicle (V2V), vehicle-to-infrastructure (V2I), and vehicle-to-everything (V2X) communication, cooperative driving, traffic management, road safety, and infotainment.
- Some of the benefits of ITS and WAVE for developing smart cities are:

  - Reduced congestion, emissions, and fuel consumption by optimizing traffic flow and routing.
  - Enhanced safety and security by preventing collisions, alerting drivers of hazards, and enabling emergency response.
  - Improved mobility and accessibility by providing real-time information, multimodal transportation options, and personalized services.
  - Increased efficiency and productivity by facilitating logistics, delivery, and public transportation operations.
  - Promoted innovation and competitiveness by fostering new business models, services, and technologies.

- Some of the challenges and issues of ITS and WAVE for developing smart cities are:

  - High cost and complexity of deploying and maintaining the infrastructure and devices for ITS and WAVE.
  - Lack of interoperability and standardization among different systems, vendors, and regions for ITS and WAVE.
  - Privacy and security risks of collecting, transmitting, and processing large amounts of sensitive data for ITS and WAVE.
  - Ethical and social implications of the impact of ITS and WAVE on human behavior, decision making, and responsibility.

- Some of the current and future trends and developments of ITS and WAVE for developing smart cities are:

  - Integration of ITS and WAVE with other smart city domains, such as energy, environment, health, and education.
  - Adoption of 5G and beyond technologies for enabling low-latency, high-bandwidth, and reliable communication for ITS and WAVE.
  - Advancement of artificial intelligence and machine learning for enabling autonomous and intelligent transportation systems and services for ITS and WAVE.
  - Involvement of stakeholders and users in the design, implementation, and evaluation of ITS and WAVE for smart cities.
