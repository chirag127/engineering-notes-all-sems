### Lightweight Cyber Physical Social Systems

- Cyber Physical Social Systems (CPSS) are systems that integrate cyber, physical and social components to provide enhanced services and user experiences.
- CPSS can be applied to various domains such as smart cities, smart homes, smart healthcare, smart transportation, smart manufacturing, etc.
- CPSS face many challenges such as security, privacy, scalability, interoperability, reliability, heterogeneity, etc.
- Lightweight CPSS are CPSS that aim to reduce the computational and communication overhead, energy consumption, and resource utilization of the system components.
- Lightweight CPSS can be achieved by using various techniques such as data compression, encryption, distortion, aggregation, filtering, etc.
- Lightweight CPSS can improve the performance, efficiency, and sustainability of the system and its applications.
- Lightweight CPSS can also enable new applications and scenarios that require low latency, high mobility, and dynamic adaptation.
