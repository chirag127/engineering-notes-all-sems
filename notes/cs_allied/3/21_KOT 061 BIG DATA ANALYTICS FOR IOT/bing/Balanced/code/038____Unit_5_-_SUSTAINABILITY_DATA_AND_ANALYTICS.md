## Unit 5 - SUSTAINABILITY DATA AND ANALYTICS

- This unit covers the concepts and methods of collecting, analyzing, and reporting sustainability data.
- Sustainability data refers to the information that measures the environmental, social, and economic impacts and performance of an organization, product, or service.
- Sustainability analytics is the process of applying data science techniques and tools to sustainability data to generate insights, support decision making, and communicate results.
- The main topics of this unit are:

  - Data sources and types: how to identify, access, and evaluate different sources and types of sustainability data, such as internal, external, primary, secondary, quantitative, and qualitative data.
  - Data collection and management: how to design, implement, and maintain data collection and management systems, such as surveys, interviews, databases, and data quality assurance and control methods.
  - Data analysis and visualization: how to apply descriptive, inferential, and predictive statistics, as well as data visualization techniques, to explore, summarize, and communicate sustainability data, such as using spreadsheets, software, and dashboards.
  - Data reporting and disclosure: how to prepare and present sustainability data reports and disclosures, such as using standards, frameworks, and indicators, such as the Global Reporting Initiative (GRI), the Sustainability Accounting Standards Board (SASB), and the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).
