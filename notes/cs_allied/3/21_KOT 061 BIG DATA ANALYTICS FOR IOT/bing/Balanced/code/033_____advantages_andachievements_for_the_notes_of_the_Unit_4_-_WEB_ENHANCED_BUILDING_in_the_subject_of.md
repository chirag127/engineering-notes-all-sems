### Advantages and Achievements for the Notes of the Unit 4 - Web Enhanced Building in the Subject of KOT 061 Big Data Analytics for IoT KCS

- Web enhanced building is a concept that uses the Internet of Things (IoT) to collect, process, and analyze data from various sensors and devices installed in a building, such as temperature, humidity, lighting, occupancy, energy consumption, etc.
- Web enhanced building can provide many advantages for building owners, managers, occupants, and the environment, such as:
  - Improving energy efficiency and reducing greenhouse gas emissions by optimizing the use of heating, cooling, ventilation, and lighting systems based on real-time data and intelligent algorithms.
  - Enhancing security and safety by detecting and preventing potential threats, such as fire, intrusion, or malfunction, and alerting the relevant authorities or personnel.
  - Increasing comfort and productivity by adjusting the indoor environment according to the preferences and needs of the occupants, such as temperature, lighting, noise, etc.
  - Providing insights and recommendations for improving the performance and maintenance of the building systems and equipment, such as identifying faults, anomalies, or inefficiencies, and suggesting corrective actions.
- Web enhanced building can also enable new applications and services for the building users, such as:
  - Smart parking, which can help drivers find and reserve available parking spaces in the building or nearby areas.
  - Smart access, which can allow authorized users to enter and exit the building using their smartphones or biometric authentication.
  - Smart meeting, which can facilitate the scheduling and management of meeting rooms and resources, such as projectors, screens, or whiteboards.
  - Smart health, which can monitor and improve the health and well-being of the occupants, such as tracking their physical activity, stress level, or air quality.
- Web enhanced building can leverage various technologies and tools for data collection, processing, and analysis, such as:
  - IoT devices, such as sensors, actuators, cameras, or smart meters, which can generate and transmit data to the cloud or edge servers.
  - Cloud computing, which can provide scalable and cost-effective storage and computing resources for processing and analyzing large volumes of data.
  - Edge computing, which can enable faster and more efficient data processing and analysis at the edge of the network, closer to the data sources, reducing the latency and bandwidth requirements.
  - Big data analytics, which can apply various techniques and methods, such as machine learning, deep learning, or stream processing, to extract valuable insights and knowledge from the data .
  - Data visualization, which can present the data and insights in an intuitive and interactive way, using charts, graphs, dashboards, or maps, to facilitate decision making and action taking.
