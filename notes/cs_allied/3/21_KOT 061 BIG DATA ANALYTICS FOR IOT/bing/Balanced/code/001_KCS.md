# KCS

KCS stands for Knowledge-Centered Service, a best practice methodology for service organizations to work more effectively with knowledge. KCS aims to improve service delivery, productivity, cost reduction, and customer satisfaction by creating and maintaining documentation as part of the support process .

Some of the main features of KCS are:

- KCS is based on the principle of "capture, structure, reuse, and improve" knowledge as a by-product of solving problems.
- KCS involves creating knowledge articles that are based on the demand and context of the customers, rather than the supply and perspective of the experts.
- KCS uses a two-tiered article quality standard: complete and correct for internal use, and usable and findable for external use.
- KCS encourages collaboration and feedback among support agents and customers to continuously improve the quality and relevance of the knowledge base.
- KCS leverages technology and tools to support the knowledge creation and maintenance process, such as search engines, content management systems, and analytics.

KCS is not to be confused with Kansas City Southern, a railroad company that operates in the US and Mexico.
