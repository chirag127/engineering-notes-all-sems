### Big Data challenges and requirements coming from different SmartCity applications

- SmartCity applications are those that use information and communication technologies (ICT) to enhance the quality of life, efficiency of urban operations and services, and competitiveness of a city .
- SmartCity applications generate and consume huge amounts of data from various sources, such as sensors, devices, vehicles, buildings, and people, in real time or near real time  .
- Big Data is a term that refers to the collection, processing, analysis, and visualization of large and complex data sets that are beyond the capabilities of traditional data management tools and methods  .
- Big Data can provide valuable insights and information to enhance SmartCity applications, such as improving traffic management, energy efficiency, public safety, health care, education, and environmental sustainability   .
- However, Big Data also poses many challenges and requirements for SmartCity applications, such as:
  - The availability of large computational and storage facilities to process streams of data produced within a smart city environment .
  - The integration and interoperability of heterogeneous data sources and formats, such as structured, unstructured, and semi-structured data, from different domains and stakeholders .
  - The security and privacy of data and information, especially when data is shared across different platforms, applications, and entities, such as the city, the citizens, the service providers, and the regulators .
  - The quality and reliability of data and information, such as the accuracy, completeness, timeliness, and consistency of data, and the validity, relevance, and usefulness of information .
  - The scalability and adaptability of data and information systems, such as the ability to handle increasing volumes, velocities, and varieties of data, and to cope with changing needs, demands, and contexts of SmartCity applications .
- Therefore, Big Data platforms for the Internet of Things (IoT) are needed to address these challenges and requirements, by providing the following functionalities :
  - Data collection and ingestion, such as the acquisition, transmission, and preprocessing of data from various sources and sensors, using IoT technologies and protocols .
  - Data storage and management, such as the organization, indexing, and retrieval of data in distributed and cloud-based systems, using NoSQL databases and data lakes .
  - Data processing and analysis, such as the extraction, transformation, and loading of data, and the application of various techniques, such as machine learning, data mining, and artificial intelligence, to discover patterns, trends, and insights from data .
  - Data visualization and presentation, such as the representation, communication, and interaction of data and information, using various methods, such as dashboards, charts, maps, and reports .
  - Data governance and security, such as the definition, implementation, and enforcement of policies, standards, and regulations for data and information, such as data ownership, access, sharing, quality, and protection  .
