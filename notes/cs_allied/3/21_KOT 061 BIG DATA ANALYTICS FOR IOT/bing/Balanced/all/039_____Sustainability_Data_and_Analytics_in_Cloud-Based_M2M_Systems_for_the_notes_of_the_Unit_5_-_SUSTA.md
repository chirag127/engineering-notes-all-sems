# Sustainability Data and Analytics in Cloud-Based M2M Systems

- M2M stands for machine-to-machine communication, which enables devices to exchange data and perform actions without human intervention.
- Cloud-based M2M systems are platforms that provide services for connecting, managing, and analyzing data from various devices and sensors in the cloud.
- Sustainability data and analytics refer to the collection, processing, and interpretation of data related to environmental, social, and economic aspects of sustainability.
- Cloud-based M2M systems can support sustainability governance by providing solutions for integrating IoT data and processes, linking monitored objects, monitoring data, and applications to stakeholders' needs, and offering tools for data analytics of different interests.
- Some of the benefits of cloud-based M2M systems for sustainability data and analytics are:
  - Scalability: cloud-based M2M systems can handle large volumes of data from multiple sources and devices, and provide elastic resources for data storage and processing.
  - Interoperability: cloud-based M2M systems can enable data exchange and integration across different domains and platforms, and support various data formats and protocols.
  - Accessibility: cloud-based M2M systems can provide easy and secure access to data and analytics for different stakeholders, such as building owners, facility managers, tenants, and regulators, through web-based interfaces and APIs.
  - Customization: cloud-based M2M systems can allow stakeholders to define their own data models, queries, and analytics, and to customize their views and dashboards according to their preferences and goals.
- Some of the challenges of cloud-based M2M systems for sustainability data and analytics are:
  - Data quality: cloud-based M2M systems need to ensure the accuracy, completeness, and timeliness of the data collected from various devices and sensors, and to deal with data errors, outliers, and missing values.
  - Data privacy: cloud-based M2M systems need to protect the confidentiality and integrity of the data and analytics, and to comply with the relevant laws and regulations regarding data collection, storage, and usage.
  - Data security: cloud-based M2M systems need to prevent unauthorized access, modification, and deletion of the data and analytics, and to detect and mitigate potential cyberattacks and threats.
  - Data governance: cloud-based M2M systems need to establish clear roles and responsibilities for data ownership, management, and sharing, and to ensure the alignment of data and analytics with the sustainability objectives and policies of the stakeholders.
