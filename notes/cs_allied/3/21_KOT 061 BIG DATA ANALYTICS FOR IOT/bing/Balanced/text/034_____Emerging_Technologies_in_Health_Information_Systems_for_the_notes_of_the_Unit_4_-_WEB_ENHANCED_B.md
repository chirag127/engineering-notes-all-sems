### Emerging Technologies in Health Information Systems

- Health information systems (HIS) are systems that collect, store, process, and communicate health-related data for decision-making and planning in health care delivery and management.
- Emerging technologies are technologies that are novel, innovative, and have the potential to disrupt or transform existing practices, markets, or industries.
- Some of the emerging technologies that are impacting HIS are:

  - Connected health devices: These are devices that can monitor, measure, or transmit health data from patients to providers, such as wearable sensors, smart watches, or implantable devices. They can enable remote patient monitoring, chronic disease management, and personalized care.
  - Digital therapeutics: These are software-based interventions that can deliver evidence-based therapies or treatments for various health conditions, such as mental health, diabetes, or addiction. They can complement or replace traditional therapies, improve patient engagement and adherence, and reduce costs and side effects.
  - Artificial intelligence: This is the technology that can perform tasks that normally require human intelligence, such as reasoning, learning, or decision-making. It can be applied to various aspects of HIS, such as diagnosis, prognosis, treatment, research, or administration. It can improve the quality, efficiency, and accessibility of health care services.
  - Telehealth: This is the delivery of health care services and information via electronic or digital means, such as phone, video, or internet. It can enable access to health care for remote or underserved populations, reduce travel and waiting time, and enhance patient-provider communication and collaboration.
  - Virtual reality: This is the technology that can create immersive and interactive simulations of real or imagined environments, using devices such as headsets, gloves, or controllers. It can be used for various purposes in HIS, such as education, training, therapy, rehabilitation, or entertainment. It can enhance the learning outcomes, motivation, and satisfaction of health care professionals and patients.

- These emerging technologies can offer various benefits for HIS, such as:

  - Improving the quality, safety, and effectiveness of health care delivery and outcomes   .
  - Enhancing the patient-centeredness, engagement, and empowerment of health care services and experiences   .
  - Increasing the efficiency, productivity, and innovation of health care processes and systems   .
  - Reducing the costs, barriers, and disparities of health care access and utilization   .

- However, these emerging technologies also pose various challenges and risks for HIS, such as:

  - Ensuring the reliability, validity, and accuracy of the data and information generated and used by these technologies   .
  - Protecting the privacy, security, and confidentiality of the data and information shared and stored by these technologies   .
  - Addressing the ethical, legal, and social implications of the use and impact of these technologies on health care providers, patients, and society   .
  - Developing the skills, competencies, and standards for the adoption and integration of these technologies into health care practice and education   .
  - Evaluating the effectiveness, efficiency, and cost-effectiveness of these technologies for health care outcomes and value   .

- Therefore, it is important to understand, assess, and monitor the emerging technologies in HIS, and to develop and implement appropriate strategies and policies to maximize their benefits and minimize their risks for health care quality and equity   .
