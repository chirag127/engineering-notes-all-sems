### Citizen Actuation

- Citizen actuation is the activation of a human being as the mechanism by which a control system acts upon the environment.
- It is a way of closing the loop started by citizen sensing, which is the collection and analysis of data from the surrounding environment by citizens using various devices.
- Citizen actuation aims to make the data collected by citizen sensing an actionable item, by sending tasks or feedback to suitable occupants of the environment to complete or respond to .
- Citizen actuation can be used for various purposes, such as improving energy efficiency, enhancing public safety, promoting social engagement, or facilitating urban planning .
- Citizen actuation requires a framework that can identify the relevant actors, the appropriate tasks, the optimal communication channels, and the expected outcomes.
- Citizen actuation also involves ethical, legal, and social issues, such as privacy, consent, trust, motivation, and incentives .
- Citizen actuation is an emerging concept that can benefit from further research and experimentation, especially in the context of smart environments and the Internet of Things  .
