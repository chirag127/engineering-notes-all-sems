## Unit 3 - FOG COMPUTING

- Fog computing is a decentralized computing infrastructure that uses edge devices to perform computation, storage, and communication locally and over the Internet backbone.
- Fog computing is also known as edge computing or fogging  .
- Fog computing aims to bring the advantages and power of the cloud closer to where data is created and acted upon, such as IoT devices .
- Fog computing is not a substitute, but a complement to cloud computing. Fog computing is more energy-efficient than cloud computing .
- Fog computing can improve latency, bandwidth, security, and privacy of data processing and transmission  .
- Fog computing can support applications that require real-time, low-latency, and location-aware processing, such as smart cities, autonomous vehicles, and healthcare  .
- Fog computing follows the standards proposed by the OpenFog Consortium, which is a consortium of industry, academia, and government members that aims to promote and advance fog computing.
