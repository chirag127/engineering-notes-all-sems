### Adaptive Pipelined Neural Network Structure in Self-aware Internet of Things

- Self-aware Internet of Things (IoT) is a concept that aims to enable IoT devices to monitor, interpret, and adapt to their own states and environments in a dynamic and autonomous manner.
- Self-aware IoT devices can improve the reliability, performance, and efficiency of IoT systems, especially in complex and changing scenarios such as smart environments.
- Adaptive Pipelined Neural Network (APNN) is a novel technique that applies a modified Pipelined Recurrent Neural Network (PRNN) to achieve self-awareness in IoT devices.
- APNN consists of three main components: a sensor layer, a pipeline layer, and an output layer.
- The sensor layer collects and preprocesses the data from the IoT device and its environment, such as temperature, humidity, battery level, etc.
- The pipeline layer consists of a series of recurrent neural networks (RNNs) that process the data in parallel and sequentially, forming a pipeline structure.
- The pipeline layer can learn the temporal and spatial patterns of the data, and can adapt its structure and parameters according to the feedback from the output layer.
- The output layer generates the desired outputs for the IoT device, such as actions, decisions, or predictions.
- The output layer also provides feedback to the pipeline layer, indicating the performance and accuracy of the APNN.
- APNN can achieve self-awareness in IoT devices by performing the following functions :
  - Monitoring: APNN can monitor the state and environment of the IoT device, and detect any anomalies or changes that may affect its functionality.
  - Interpretation: APNN can interpret the data collected by the sensor layer, and extract useful information and features that can help the IoT device to understand its situation and context.
  - Resolution: APNN can generate appropriate outputs for the IoT device, such as actions, decisions, or predictions, based on the interpretation of the data and the desired goals of the IoT device.
  - Adaptation: APNN can adapt its structure and parameters to the changing state and environment of the IoT device, and improve its performance and accuracy over time.
- APNN has been tested and evaluated in different fields, such as smart home, smart grid, and smart health, and has shown promising results in terms of self-awareness, adaptability, and scalability .
