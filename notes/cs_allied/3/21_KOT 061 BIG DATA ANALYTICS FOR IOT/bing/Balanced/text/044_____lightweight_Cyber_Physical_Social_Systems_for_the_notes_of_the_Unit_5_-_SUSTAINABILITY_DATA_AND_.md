### Lightweight Cyber Physical Social Systems

- Cyber physical social systems (CPSSs) are systems that integrate cyber, physical, and social components to provide enhanced services and functionalities.
- CPSSs can be applied to various domains, such as smart cities, smart homes, smart health, smart transportation, smart manufacturing, and smart agriculture.
- CPSSs face several challenges, such as security, privacy, scalability, interoperability, reliability, and adaptability .
- Lightweight security is a possible solution to address the security challenge of CPSSs, especially for resource-constrained devices and networks.
- Lightweight security aims to provide security services, such as confidentiality, integrity, and authentication, with minimal computational and communication overhead.
- Distortion-based lightweight security is a novel approach that leverages the inherent distortion tolerance of physical systems to achieve security with low complexity and low bandwidth consumption.
- Distortion-based lightweight security works by introducing controlled distortion to the data before transmission, and recovering the original data at the receiver by exploiting the physical system model and the distortion characteristics.
- Distortion-based lightweight security can be applied to different types of physical systems, such as linear, nonlinear, and stochastic systems.
- Distortion-based lightweight security can achieve a trade-off between security and performance, depending on the distortion level and the system dynamics.
- Distortion-based lightweight security can also provide resilience against denial-of-service attacks and replay attacks, by using dynamic distortion parameters and timestamps.
