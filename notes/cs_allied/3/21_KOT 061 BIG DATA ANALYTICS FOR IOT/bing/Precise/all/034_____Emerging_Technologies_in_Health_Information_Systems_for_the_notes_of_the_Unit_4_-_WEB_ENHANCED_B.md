# Emerging Technologies in Health Information Systems

Emerging technologies in health information systems are transforming healthcare delivery and improving patient outcomes. Here are some of the emerging technologies in this field:

1. **Connected health devices**: These devices allow for real-time monitoring of patient health and can transmit data to healthcare providers for analysis and intervention .

2. **Digital therapeutics**: These are evidence-based therapeutic interventions driven by software programs to prevent, manage, or treat a medical disorder or disease .

3. **Artificial intelligence**: AI is being used in healthcare to analyze large amounts of data, make predictions, and assist in decision-making  .

4. **Telehealth**: Telehealth allows for remote patient monitoring and virtual consultations, reducing the need for in-person visits .

5. **Virtual reality**: Virtual reality is being used in healthcare for pain management, physical therapy, and mental health treatment .

These emerging technologies are evolving rapidly and have the potential to greatly improve healthcare delivery and patient outcomes. Providers and payers should monitor these technologies while weighing digital health capabilities and delivery channels for serving patients and members .
