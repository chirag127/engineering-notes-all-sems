### Sustainability Data and Analytics in Cloud-Based M2M Systems

- This chapter contributes to the analysis of stakeholders in cloud-based sustainability governance in smart buildings and their requirements for data analytics.
- It provides techniques for linking, enriching, and managing data for sustainability analysis in M2M environments.
- It also includes a data analytics system with a Platform-as-a-Service (PaaS) and an analytics framework for managing relationships between stakeholders, monitored objects, monitoring data, and applications.
- The goal is to develop techniques for linking monitored objects, monitoring data, and applications to stakeholder’s needs and to provide a cloud-based data analytics system with a Platform-as-a-Service (PaaS) and supporting tools for stakeholders to access data and perform data analytics of their own interest.
- The techniques can be adopted by cloud-based M2M platform providers.
- To illustrate the usefulness of the work, a prototype atop a real-world cloud system for facility monitoring and analytics is presented.
