# Lightweight Cyber Physical Social Systems

- Cyber-Physical Systems (CPS) comprise of physical systems and their corresponding cyber systems that are tightly integrated at all levels and scales.
- CPS offer opportunities for improving efficiency, monitoring, controllability, and reliability of constructed facilities.
- Integrating social networks to CPSs results in a new paradigm called Cyber-Physical-Social Systems (CPSSs).
- CPSSs contain integrated cyber parts, including computing, communication, and physical parts; it uses computations and communication embedded in and interacting with physical processes to add new capabilities to physical systems.
- A CPSS is a novel paradigm of cyber-physical and cyber-social systems with a highly inhomogeneous and distributed nature integrating dynamic stochastic hybrid systems including computation, communication, sensing and actuation, and social systems.
- Their usage can be traced to homes such as smart homes.
- Distortion-Based Lightweight Security for Cyber-Physical Systems is of critical significance as it can be used to manipulate or damage the control operations by adversaries.
