# Role of Adaptive Neural Network for the Notes of the Unit 2 - RFID False Authentications in the Subject of KOT 061 Big Data Analytics for IoT KCS

- Adaptive neural networks can play a key role in the development of intelligent manufacturing by providing observer-based control for nonlinear strict-feedback cyber-physical systems subject to false data injection attacks.
- A comprehensive time-series analysis technique can be proposed and evaluated to identify and filter false-positives from the RFID data stream.
- With the development of wireless Internet of Things (IoT) devices, Radio frequency identification (RFID) has been used as a promising technology for the proliferation and communication in IoT.
- Self-adaptive RFID authentication can be used for the Internet of Things.
