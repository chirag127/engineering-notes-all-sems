# Social Networking Analysis

Social Networking Analysis (SNA) is the process of investigating social structures through the use of network and graph theories. It is a key technique in the field of sustainability data and analytics, particularly in the context of Big Data Analytics for IoT (KOT 061).

Here are some key points to consider when studying SNA for Unit 5 - Sustainability Data and Analytics:

1. SNA can be used to identify and analyze patterns of relationships between individuals, groups, and organizations.
2. It involves the use of mathematical and computational methods to model and analyze social networks.
3. SNA can provide insights into the structure and dynamics of social systems, including the identification of key actors, the distribution of power and influence, and the detection of communities and subgroups.
4. In the context of sustainability, SNA can be used to analyze the flow of information and resources within and between social systems, and to identify opportunities for intervention and change.
5. SNA is a powerful tool for understanding the complex interactions between social and ecological systems, and for developing strategies for sustainable development.
