### Necessary and sufficient condition for false authentication prevention

- A necessary and sufficient condition for false authentication prevention in RFID systems is the use of semaphore operations.
- A naive semaphore-based solution has been proposed which revises the pattern by adding semaphore operations to avoid false authentications.
- This solution has been demonstrated to be effective in preventing false authentications through experiments.
- False authentication may also arise under other situations such as multiple readers interacting with one or more tags, or one reader interacting with multiple tags.
- The use of semaphore operations can satisfy the necessary and sufficient condition for false authentication prevention, thus preventing false authentications.
