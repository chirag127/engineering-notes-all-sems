### Network Protocol- Data Dissemination for the notes of the Unit 1 - BIG DATA PLATFORMS FOR THE INTERNET OF THINGS in the subject of KOT 061 BIG DATA ANALYTICS FOR IOT KCS

1. **Internet Protocol (IP)** is a set of rules that dictates how data gets sent to the internet.
2. **IoT protocols** ensure that information from one device or sensor gets read and understood by another device, a gateway, or a service.
3. Different IoT protocols have been designed and optimized for different scenarios and usage.
4. The protocol and compatible interconnection for the simple wireless connectivity with relaxed throughput (2–250 kb/s), low range (up to 100 m), moderate latency (10–50 ms) requirements and low cost, adapted to devices previously not connected to the Internet were defined in **IEEE 802.15.4**.
5. IoT network technologies to be aware of toward the bottom of the protocol stack include **cellular, wifi, and Ethernet**, as well as more specialized solutions such as **LPWAN, Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE), ZigBee, NFC, and RFID**.
6. **NB-IoT** is becoming the standard for LPWAN networks, according to Gartner.
