### YA TRAP

YA TRAP is a technique used to prevent RFID false authentication in the context of Big Data Analytics for IoT. It is a part of the Unit 2 - RFID False Authentications in the subject of KOT 061 Big Data Analytics for IoT KCS.

- YA TRAP stands for "Yet Another TRansaction Authentication Protocol".
- It is designed to provide secure authentication for RFID systems.
- YA TRAP uses a combination of cryptographic techniques to ensure the authenticity of the data transmitted between the RFID tag and the reader.
- It helps to prevent common attacks such as replay attacks, man-in-the-middle attacks, and cloning attacks.
- YA TRAP is considered to be a robust and effective solution for preventing false authentication in RFID systems.
