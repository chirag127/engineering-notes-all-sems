### Unit 3 - FOG COMPUTING in the subject of KOT 061 BIG DATA ANALYTICS FOR IOT KCS

- Fog computing is a suitable platform for new proliferating IoT devices, services, and applications .
- The fog provides IoT data processing and storage locally at IoT devices instead of sending them to the cloud .
- In contrast to the cloud, the fog provides services with faster response and greater quality .
- Fog computing may be considered the best choice to enable the IoT to provide efficient and secure services for many IoT users .
- Fog Computing enhances Big Data Analytics capabilities in Cloud – IoT architecture for smart devices of IoT environment .
- IoT sensor produces data for big data analytics .
- Big IoT data analytics by fog computing structure is on emerging phase and requires extensive research to produce more proficient knowledge and smart decisions .
- The number of devices within Internet of Things (IoT) that are connected and available via Internet will be between 50 and 100 billion by 2020 .
