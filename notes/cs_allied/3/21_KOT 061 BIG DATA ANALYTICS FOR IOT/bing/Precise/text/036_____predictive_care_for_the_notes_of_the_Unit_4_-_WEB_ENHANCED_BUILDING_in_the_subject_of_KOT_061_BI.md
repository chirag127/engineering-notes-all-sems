### Predictive Care

Predictive care is a proactive approach to maintenance and repair of equipment and systems. It involves using data analytics and machine learning algorithms to predict when maintenance or repair is needed, and to schedule it in advance to prevent downtime or failure. Predictive care is an important aspect of web-enhanced buildings, as it can improve the efficiency and reliability of building systems.
