### Toward Web Enhanced Building Automation Systems

1. Building automation systems (BAS) are used to control and monitor various building systems, including heating, ventilation, and air conditioning (HVAC), lighting, and security.
2. The integration of web technologies into BAS can enhance their capabilities and provide additional benefits.
3. Web-enabled BAS can be accessed and controlled remotely, allowing for more efficient management and maintenance of building systems.
4. The use of web technologies can also facilitate the collection and analysis of data from building systems, providing valuable insights into building performance and energy usage.
5. This can lead to improved energy efficiency and cost savings, as well as enhanced occupant comfort and safety.
6. The development of web standards and protocols for BAS, such as BACnet and oBIX, has facilitated the integration of web technologies into building automation systems.
7. The use of web technologies in BAS is an ongoing area of research and development, with the potential for further advancements and benefits in the future.
