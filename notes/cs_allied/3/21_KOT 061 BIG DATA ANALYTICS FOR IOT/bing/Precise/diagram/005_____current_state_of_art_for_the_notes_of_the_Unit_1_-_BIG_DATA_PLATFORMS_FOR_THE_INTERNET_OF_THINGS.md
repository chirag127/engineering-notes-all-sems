### Current State of Art for the Notes of the Unit 1 - BIG DATA PLATFORMS FOR THE INTERNET OF THINGS in the Subject of KOT 061 BIG DATA ANALYTICS FOR IOT KCS

- Big Data is a term used to describe the large volume of structured and unstructured data that is generated and collected by organizations and individuals.
- The current state of the art with respect to 'Big Data' frameworks and libraries is an active area of research and development.
- Big Data System Benchmarking is a process of evaluating the performance of Big Data systems. There are several categories of benchmarks, including Map-Reduce based system benchmarking, SQL-based analytical system benchmarking, NoSQL-based database benchmarking, and Big graph system benchmarking.
- Data and Information: Data are plain facts. The word "data" is plural for "datum." Data is nothing but facts and statistics stored or free flowing over a network, generally. It's raw and unprocessed. When data are processed, organized, structured or presented in a given context so as to make it useful, it is called information.
