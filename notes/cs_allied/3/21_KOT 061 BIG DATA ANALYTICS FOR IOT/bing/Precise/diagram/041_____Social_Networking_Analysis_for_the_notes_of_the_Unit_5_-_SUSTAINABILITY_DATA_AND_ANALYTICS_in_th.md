### Social Networking Analysis

Social Networking Analysis (SNA) is the process of investigating social structures through the use of network and graph theories. It is a key technique in the field of sustainability data and analytics, particularly in the context of Big Data Analytics for IoT (KOT 061).

Some key points to consider when studying SNA in the context of Unit 5 - Sustainability Data and Analytics are:

1. SNA can be used to identify and analyze patterns of relationships between individuals, groups, and organizations.
2. It can help to uncover hidden connections and relationships that may not be immediately apparent.
3. SNA can be used to analyze the flow of information and resources within a network, and to identify key influencers and bottlenecks.
4. It can also be used to identify potential areas for intervention and improvement, in order to enhance the sustainability of the network.
5. SNA is particularly relevant in the context of IoT, as the vast amounts of data generated by connected devices can provide valuable insights into the functioning of social networks.
