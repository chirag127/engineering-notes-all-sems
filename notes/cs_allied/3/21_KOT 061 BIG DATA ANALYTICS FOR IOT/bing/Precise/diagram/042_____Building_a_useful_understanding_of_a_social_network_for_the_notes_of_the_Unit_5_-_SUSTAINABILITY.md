### Building a useful understanding of a social network

1. A social network is a structure made up of individuals or organizations, connected by one or more specific types of relationships.
2. Social networks can be used to analyze the interactions and relationships between individuals or organizations.
3. Understanding a social network can provide insights into the behavior and decision-making processes of the individuals or organizations within the network.
4. To build a useful understanding of a social network, it is important to identify the key actors and relationships within the network.
5. This can be done through the use of network analysis techniques, such as centrality measures, which can identify the most influential actors within the network.
6. It is also important to understand the context in which the social network operates, including any external factors that may influence the behavior of the individuals or organizations within the network.
7. By building a useful understanding of a social network, it is possible to identify opportunities for intervention or improvement, and to make informed decisions about how to engage with the network.
