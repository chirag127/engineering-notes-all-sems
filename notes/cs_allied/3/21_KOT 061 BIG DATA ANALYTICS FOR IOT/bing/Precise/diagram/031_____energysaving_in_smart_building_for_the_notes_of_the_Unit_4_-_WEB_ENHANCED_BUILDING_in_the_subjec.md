### Energy Saving in Smart Building

Smart buildings can improve energy efficiency by utilizing modern technologies such as smart HVAC systems, smart lighting, smart windows, and the supply of sustainable energy. This alone allows saving up to 50% of a building’s energy consumption.

1. **Smart HVAC systems**: Smart HVAC systems can help to reduce energy consumption by automatically adjusting the temperature and airflow based on the occupancy and usage of the building.

2. **Smart Lighting**: Smart lighting systems can help to reduce energy consumption by automatically adjusting the lighting based on the occupancy and usage of the building.

3. **Smart Windows**: Smart windows can save energy by supporting HVAC systems to cool and heat a smart building.

4. **Sustainable Energy Supply**: To even further advance a smart building’s energy efficiency, supplying your own electricity or heating/cooling can be very useful. Photovoltaic panels or solar water heaters can reduce the amount of energy demanded by the public network.

Smart building systems can also boost energy efficiency by tracking occupancy and space usage to power more intelligent cleaning processes. Rather than having cleaning teams follow the same rota every day, with a smart usage monitoring system you can see which spaces have been used.
