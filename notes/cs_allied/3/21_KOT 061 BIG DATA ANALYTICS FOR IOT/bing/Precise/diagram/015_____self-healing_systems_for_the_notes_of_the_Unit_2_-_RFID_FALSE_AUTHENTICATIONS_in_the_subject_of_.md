### Self-Healing Systems

Self-healing systems are designed to automatically detect and correct problems within a system. These systems can be used in a variety of applications, including IT operations and electrical distribution systems.

In the context of RFID false authentication, self-healing systems can help to mitigate the impact of interference in the electromagnetic field caused by other medical equipment, metallic objects, liquid, glass, and moist environments. These factors can affect the read rates and read accuracy of RFID readers, leading to false reads.

Self-healing systems can be either data-based or event-based. In a data-based self-healing environment, data is collected and analyzed to identify trends and patterns, similar to AIOps solutions. This information can then be used to automatically detect and correct problems within the system.

Overall, self-healing systems can provide a practical solution for maintaining the accuracy and reliability of RFID technology in challenging environments.
