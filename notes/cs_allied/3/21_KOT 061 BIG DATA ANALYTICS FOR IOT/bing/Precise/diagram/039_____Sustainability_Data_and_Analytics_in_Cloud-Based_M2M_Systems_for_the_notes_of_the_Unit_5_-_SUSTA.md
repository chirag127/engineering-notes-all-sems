### Sustainability Data and Analytics in Cloud-Based M2M Systems

- The goal is to develop techniques for linking monitored objects, monitoring data, and applications to stakeholder’s needs and to provide a cloud-based data analytics system with a Platform-as-a-Service (PaaS) and supporting tools for stakeholders to access data and perform data analytics of their own interest.

- This chapter contributes:
  1. A detailed analysis of stakeholders in cloud-based sustainability governance in smart buildings and their requirements for data analytics.
  2. Techniques for linking, enriching and managing data for sustainability analysis in M2M environments.
  3. A data analytics system including a PaaS and an analytics framework for managing relationships between stakeholders, monitored objects, monitoring data and applications.

- The techniques can be adopted by cloud-based M2M platform providers.

- A prototype is presented atop a real-world cloud system for facility monitoring and analytics.
