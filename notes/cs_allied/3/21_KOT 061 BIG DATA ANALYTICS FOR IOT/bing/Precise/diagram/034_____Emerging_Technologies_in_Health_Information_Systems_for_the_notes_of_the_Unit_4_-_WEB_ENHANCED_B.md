### Emerging Technologies in Health Information Systems

1. **Connected Health Devices**: These devices allow for remote monitoring of patients' health and can provide real-time data to healthcare providers.
2. **Digital Therapeutics**: These are software-based interventions that can help prevent, manage, or treat a medical condition.
3. **Artificial Intelligence**: AI can be used to analyze large amounts of data to improve diagnosis, treatment, and patient outcomes.
4. **Telehealth**: Telehealth allows for remote consultations and treatment, improving access to healthcare for patients in remote or underserved areas.
5. **Virtual Reality**: VR can be used for medical training, pain management, and mental health treatment.

These emerging technologies have the potential to improve healthcare delivery and outcomes for patients. Providers and payers should monitor their development and consider how they can be integrated into their services.
