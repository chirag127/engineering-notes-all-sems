### Emerging Technologies in Health Information Systems

1. **Connected Health Devices**: These devices allow for remote monitoring of patients' health and can provide real-time data to healthcare providers.
2. **Digital Therapeutics**: These are evidence-based therapeutic interventions driven by software programs to prevent, manage, or treat a medical disorder or disease.
3. **Artificial Intelligence**: AI can be used in healthcare to improve patient outcomes, reduce costs, and increase efficiency.
4. **Telehealth**: Telehealth allows for remote consultations and monitoring, reducing the need for in-person visits.
5. **Virtual Reality**: Virtual reality can be used in healthcare for training, therapy, and pain management.

These emerging technologies have the potential to transform healthcare delivery and improve patient outcomes. Providers and payers should monitor these technologies while weighing digital health capabilities and delivery channels for serving patients and members .
