## Unit 4 - WEB ENHANCED BUILDING

Web-enhanced building refers to the integration of web technologies into the design and operation of buildings. This can include a variety of features, such as:

1. **Building Automation Systems (BAS)**: These systems use web-based interfaces to allow building managers to monitor and control various building systems, such as HVAC, lighting, and security.

2. **Remote Access**: Web-enhanced buildings may allow remote access to building systems, allowing building managers to monitor and control systems from off-site locations.

3. **Energy Management**: Web-based energy management systems can provide real-time data on energy usage, allowing building managers to identify areas for improvement and implement energy-saving measures.

4. **Occupant Interaction**: Web-enhanced buildings may provide occupants with web-based interfaces for controlling building systems, such as lighting and temperature, as well as for accessing building information and services.

5. **Smart Building Technologies**: Web-enhanced buildings may incorporate smart building technologies, such as sensors and automation systems, to improve building performance and occupant comfort.

Overall, the integration of web technologies into building design and operation can provide numerous benefits, including improved energy efficiency, enhanced occupant comfort, and increased operational efficiency.
