### Necessary and sufficient condition for false authentication prevention for the notes of the Unit 2 - RFID FALSE AUTHENTICATIONS in the subject of KOT 061 BIG DATA ANALYTICS FOR IOT KCS

1. **Mutual Authentication**: Both the RFID tag and the reader must authenticate each other to prevent false authentication.
2. **Encryption**: The data transmitted between the RFID tag and the reader must be encrypted to prevent interception and tampering.
3. **Key Management**: The keys used for encryption must be properly managed and updated to prevent unauthorized access.
4. **Physical Security**: The RFID tag and the reader must be physically secured to prevent tampering or replacement.
5. **Access Control**: Access to the RFID system must be controlled and monitored to prevent unauthorized access.
6. **Auditing**: The RFID system must be regularly audited to detect and prevent false authentication.
7. **Standardization**: The RFID system must follow industry standards and best practices to ensure security and prevent false authentication.
