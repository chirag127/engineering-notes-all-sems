### Citizen Actuation

Citizen actuation refers to the involvement of citizens in the process of collecting, analyzing, and utilizing data to improve sustainability. This can be achieved through various means, including:

1. **Participatory sensing:** This involves citizens using their personal devices, such as smartphones, to collect data on various environmental factors, such as air quality, noise levels, and traffic congestion. This data can then be used to inform decision-making and improve sustainability.

2. **Crowdsourcing:** This involves citizens contributing data and information to a common platform, which can then be used to improve sustainability. For example, citizens can report instances of littering or illegal dumping, which can then be used to inform waste management strategies.

3. **Community-based monitoring:** This involves citizens working together to monitor and report on environmental factors in their local area. This can include monitoring air and water quality, tracking wildlife populations, and reporting on the impacts of climate change.

Citizen actuation can play a crucial role in improving sustainability by providing valuable data and insights that can inform decision-making. By involving citizens in the process, it can also help to increase awareness and engagement on sustainability issues.
