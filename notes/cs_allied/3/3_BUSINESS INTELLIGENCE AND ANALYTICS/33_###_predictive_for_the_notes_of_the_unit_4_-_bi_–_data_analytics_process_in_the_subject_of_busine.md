### Predictive for the notes of the Unit 4 - BI – DATA ANALYTICS PROCESS in the subject of BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE AND ANALYTICS KCS

Predictive Analytics is a process of using data, statistical algorithms, and machine learning techniques to identify the likelihood of future outcomes based on historical data. 

In the context of Business Intelligence and Analytics, Predictive Analytics is used to make predictions about future events, such as customer behavior, market trends, and operational performance. 

The steps involved in Predictive Analytics are:
1. Data collection and preparation: gathering and cleaning the data needed for the analysis.
2. Data exploration and visualization: analyzing the data to identify patterns and relationships.
3. Model selection: choosing the appropriate machine learning algorithm based on the type of problem being solved and the nature of the data.
4. Model training: using the data to train the machine learning algorithm to make predictions.
5. Model evaluation: evaluating the performance of the model using metrics such as accuracy, precision, and recall.
6. Model deployment: deploying the model in a production environment to make predictions.

Applications of Predictive Analytics in Business Intelligence and Analytics include:
1. Customer segmentation and targeting
2. Sales forecasting and demand planning
3. Fraud detection and risk management
4. Predictive maintenance and operations optimization
5. Marketing campaign optimization

It is important to note that Predictive Analytics is a subset of Data Analytics and is used to make predictions based on historical data.
