### Iris Datasets for the notes of the Unit 4 - BI – DATA ANALYTICS PROCESS in the subject of BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE AND ANALYTICS KCS

The Iris dataset is a commonly used dataset in the field of Business Intelligence (BI) and data analytics. It consists of 150 observations of iris flowers, each with four features: sepal length, sepal width, petal length, and petal width. The dataset was collected by Ronald Fisher in 1936 and is used for testing and demonstrating various machine learning algorithms.

The Iris dataset is a popular choice for data analytics and machine learning because it is small, well-understood, and easily accessible. It is often used as a benchmark for evaluating the performance of machine learning algorithms, and it is also used for educational purposes to demonstrate the basics of data analysis and machine learning.

In conclusion, the Iris dataset is a widely used dataset in the field of BI and data analytics. It provides a small and well-understood dataset that is useful for testing and demonstrating various machine learning algorithms, and it is also used for educational purposes to demonstrate the basics of data analysis and machine learning.
