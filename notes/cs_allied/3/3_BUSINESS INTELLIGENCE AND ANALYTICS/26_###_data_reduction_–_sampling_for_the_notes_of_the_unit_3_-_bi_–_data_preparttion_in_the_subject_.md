### Data Reduction – Sampling for the notes of the Unit 3 - BI – DATA PREPARTTION in the subject of BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE AND ANALYTICS KCS

Sampling is a statistical method used in data reduction to obtain a representative subset of the data. The goal of sampling is to reduce the amount of data to be processed, increase efficiency and reduce the cost of data analysis. Sampling is used in Business Intelligence and Analytics to make inferences about the population based on a smaller sample. There are different types of sampling methods including random sampling, stratified sampling, cluster sampling, and systematic sampling. The choice of sampling method depends on the research question, data distribution, and the desired level of precision. Sampling can introduce bias if not conducted properly, so it is important to understand the underlying assumptions and limitations of each method. Sampling can also be used to validate the results of data analysis, by comparing the results obtained from the sample to the results obtained from the entire data set. Overall, sampling is an important tool in Business Intelligence and Analytics, allowing analysts to make informed decisions based on a smaller, more manageable subset of data.
