### Data Modelling for the notes of the Unit 2 - BI – DATA MINING & WAREHOUSING in the subject of BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE AND ANALYTICS KCS

Data modeling is the process of creating a conceptual representation of data to be stored in a database. It involves defining the structure of data, relationships between data entities, and constraints on the data to ensure data integrity.

In the context of Business Intelligence and Analytics, data modeling is crucial for data warehousing. Data warehousing is the process of collecting, storing, and managing large amounts of data from various sources for analysis and decision making.

There are several types of data models used in data warehousing, including:

1. Conceptual Data Model: A high-level representation of data entities and relationships, independent of any specific database technology.

2. Logical Data Model: A detailed representation of data entities, attributes, and relationships, independent of any specific database technology.

3. Physical Data Model: A representation of the database design, including specific details on data storage, indexing, and access methods.

Data modeling in data warehousing also involves the use of data normalization techniques to eliminate data redundancy and improve data consistency. Normalization involves breaking down complex data structures into simpler, related structures to minimize data duplication and improve data integrity.

In conclusion, data modeling is a crucial step in the data warehousing process, providing a foundation for efficient data storage and analysis. By creating a well-designed data model, organizations can ensure that their data is accurate, consistent, and accessible for analysis and decision making.
