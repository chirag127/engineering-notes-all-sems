### Introduction to analytics process for the notes of the Unit 4 - BI – DATA ANALYTICS PROCESS in the subject of BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE AND ANALYTICS KCS

The Analytics process is a systematic approach to analyzing data to extract insights and make informed decisions. It typically involves the following steps:

1. Define the problem: Identify the business problem or decision that needs to be made and determine the data needed to solve it.

2. Collect data: Gather data from various sources, such as databases, spreadsheets, and web APIs.

3. Clean and prepare data: Clean and transform the data to ensure it is in a format suitable for analysis.

4. Explore data: Use descriptive statistics and visualizations to understand the data and identify patterns and relationships.

5. Model data: Use statistical models and algorithms to analyze the data and make predictions or draw conclusions.

6. Validate results: Verify the accuracy and reliability of the results.

7. Communicate results: Present the insights and recommendations to stakeholders in a clear and concise manner.

8. Implement and monitor: Take action based on the insights and monitor the results to ensure they are in line with expectations.

This process is iterative and may be repeated multiple times to refine the results and ensure the best possible outcome. It is important to have a clear understanding of the data and the business problem being solved, as well as the tools and techniques used in the analysis process.
