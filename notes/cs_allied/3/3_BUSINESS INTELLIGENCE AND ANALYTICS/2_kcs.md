KCS

KCS (KuCoin Shares) is a cryptocurrency token and the native token of the KuCoin exchange. It was created in 2017 and is used as a utility token for the exchange. The token has several use cases, including:

1. Trading fees discount: KCS holders receive a discount on trading fees when they use KCS to pay for their trades.

2. Dividends: KuCoin shares 90% of its trading fee revenue with KCS holders in the form of daily dividends.

3. Decision making: KCS holders can participate in decision-making processes for the exchange, such as voting on new token listings.

4. Access to exclusive features: KCS holders have access to exclusive features on the exchange, such as early access to new token sales and premium customer support.

The value of KCS is directly tied to the success of the KuCoin exchange. As the exchange grows and attracts more users, the demand for KCS is expected to increase, leading to a higher token price. Additionally, the daily dividend payouts provide a steady source of passive income for KCS holders.
