### Behavioral for the notes of the Unit 4 - BI – DATA ANALYTICS PROCESS in the subject of BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE AND ANALYTICS KCS

Behavioral Analytics is the process of analyzing and understanding human behavior and the factors that influence it. In the context of Business Intelligence and Analytics, Behavioral Analytics is used to understand customer behavior and to make informed decisions about marketing, sales, and customer service.

The steps involved in Behavioral Analytics are:
1. Data collection: gathering data on customer behavior, such as purchase history, website visits, and customer feedback.
2. Data preparation: cleaning and transforming the data to prepare it for analysis.
3. Data exploration and visualization: analyzing the data to identify patterns and relationships.
4. Model selection: choosing the appropriate machine learning algorithm based on the type of problem being solved and the nature of the data.
5. Model training: using the data to train the machine learning algorithm to make predictions.
6. Model evaluation: evaluating the performance of the model using metrics such as accuracy, precision, and recall.
7. Model deployment: deploying the model in a production environment to make predictions.

Applications of Behavioral Analytics in Business Intelligence and Analytics include:
1. Customer segmentation and targeting
2. Customer churn prediction
3. Customer lifetime value prediction
4. Customer behavior prediction
5. Personalization and recommendation

It is important to note that Behavioral Analytics is a subset of Predictive Analytics and is used to make predictions about customer behavior based on historical data.
