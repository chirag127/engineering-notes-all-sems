### Analysis Methodologies for the notes of the Unit 2 - BI – DATA MINING & WAREHOUSING in the subject of BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE AND ANALYTICS KCS

Analysis Methodologies are systematic approaches to analyzing data to extract insights and make informed decisions. In the context of Business Intelligence (BI) and data mining, there are several commonly used methodologies, including:

1. Descriptive Analytics: This methodology involves summarizing and describing the characteristics of data, such as mean, median, mode, and standard deviation. It is used to gain a general understanding of the data and to identify patterns and trends.

2. Predictive Analytics: This methodology involves using statistical models and algorithms to make predictions about future events or trends based on historical data. It is used to identify potential opportunities and risks and to make informed decisions.

3. Prescriptive Analytics: This methodology involves using mathematical models and optimization algorithms to determine the best course of action based on the data. It is used to make decisions that maximize the value of a system or process.

4. Data Mining: This methodology involves using machine learning algorithms to identify patterns and relationships in large data sets. It is used to discover hidden insights and to make predictions about future events.

5. Text Mining: This methodology involves analyzing unstructured text data to extract meaningful insights and to make informed decisions. It is used to analyze customer feedback, social media posts, and other sources of unstructured data.

6. Graph Analytics: This methodology involves analyzing relationships and connections between entities in a graph data structure. It is used to identify relationships and patterns in complex data sets, such as social networks and supply chains.

It is important to choose the appropriate analysis methodology based on the goals and objectives of the project, as well as the type and quality of data available.
