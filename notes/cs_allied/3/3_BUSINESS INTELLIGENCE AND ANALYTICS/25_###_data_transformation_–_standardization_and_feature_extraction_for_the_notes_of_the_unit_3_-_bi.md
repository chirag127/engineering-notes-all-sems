### Data Transformation – Standardization and Feature Extraction for the notes of the Unit 3 - BI – DATA PREPARTTION in the subject of BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE AND ANALYTICS KCS

Data Transformation is a crucial step in the data preparation process for Business Intelligence and Analytics. Two common techniques used for this purpose are Standardization and Feature Extraction.

Standardization is the process of transforming variables to have a mean of zero and a standard deviation of one. This is done to ensure that all variables have the same scale, which is important for many statistical algorithms. The formula for standardization is: (x - mean) / standard deviation.

Feature Extraction is the process of creating new variables from existing ones. This is done to improve the quality of the data and to make it more suitable for analysis. Feature extraction can be performed using various techniques such as principal component analysis, independent component analysis, and factor analysis.

In conclusion, data transformation is a key step in the data preparation process for Business Intelligence and Analytics. Standardization and Feature Extraction are two common techniques used for this purpose, and they play a crucial role in ensuring that the data is suitable for analysis.
