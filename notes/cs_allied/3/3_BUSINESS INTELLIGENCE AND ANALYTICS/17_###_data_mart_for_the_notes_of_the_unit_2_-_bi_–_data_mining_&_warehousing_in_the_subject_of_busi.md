### Data Mart for the notes of the Unit 2 - BI – DATA MINING & WAREHOUSING in the subject of BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE AND ANALYTICS KCS

Data Mart is a subset of a larger data warehouse that is designed to serve a specific department or business unit within an organization. It is a smaller, more focused version of a data warehouse that provides tailored information for a specific business need.

Data Marts are used in Business Intelligence and Analytics to provide quick, easy access to information for decision-making purposes. They are designed to be more accessible and user-friendly than a full-fledged data warehouse, which can be complex and difficult to navigate.

Data Marts are typically populated with data from a variety of sources, including transactional systems, operational databases, and external sources. The data is then transformed, cleansed, and integrated into the Data Mart to ensure that it is accurate and consistent.

Data Marts can be used in a variety of ways, including:

- Providing quick access to information for decision-making purposes
- Supporting specific business processes, such as sales analysis or customer relationship management
- Providing data for reporting and analysis
- Supporting data mining and predictive analytics

In order to build a Data Mart, it is important to understand the specific needs of the business unit or department that will be using it. This includes understanding the types of data that will be stored in the Data Mart, the types of queries that will be run against the data, and the types of reports that will be generated.

Once the Data Mart has been built, it is important to maintain it by regularly updating the data, monitoring performance, and making changes as necessary. This will ensure that the Data Mart continues to provide accurate, up-to-date information for decision-making purposes.

In summary, Data Marts are a valuable tool in Business Intelligence and Analytics, providing quick, easy access to information for decision-making purposes. They are designed to be more accessible and user-friendly than a full-fledged data warehouse, and can be used to support a variety of business processes and analysis.
