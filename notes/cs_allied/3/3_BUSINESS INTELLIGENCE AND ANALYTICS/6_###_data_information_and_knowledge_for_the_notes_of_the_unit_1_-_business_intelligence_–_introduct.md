### Data Information and Knowledge for the notes of the Unit 1 - BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE – INTRODUCTION in the subject of BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE AND ANALYTICS KCS

Data, information, and knowledge are closely related concepts that are critical to the field of business intelligence and analytics.

Definition:

Data: Raw facts and figures that are collected and recorded, but have not yet been processed or organized in a meaningful way.

Information: Data that has been processed and organized in a way that makes it meaningful and useful.

Knowledge: Information that has been interpreted and contextualized, and is used to make informed decisions and take action.

Properties:

1. Data is the foundation of information and knowledge.
2. Information is created by processing and organizing data in a way that makes it meaningful and useful.
3. Knowledge is created by interpreting and contextualizing information, and using it to make informed decisions and take action.
4. Data, information, and knowledge are interdependent, and the value of each increases as it moves up the hierarchy.

Applications:

1. Business intelligence and analytics rely on data, information, and knowledge to provide insights and support decision-making.
2. Data management and quality are critical to ensuring that data is accurate, relevant, and usable for business intelligence and analytics purposes.
3. Data warehousing and data mining are used to collect, store, and analyze large amounts of data to generate information and knowledge.
4. Dashboards, reports, and visualizations are used to present information and knowledge in a way that is easy to understand and use.

It is important to have a clear understanding of data, information, and knowledge in order to effectively use business intelligence and analytics to support decision-making and drive business success.
