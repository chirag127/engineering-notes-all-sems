### PCA for the notes of the Unit 3 - BI – DATA PREPARTTION in the subject of BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE AND ANALYTICS KCS

PCA (Principal Component Analysis) is a dimensionality reduction technique that transforms high dimensional data into a lower dimensional space, while retaining most of the information in the data. 

In the context of Business Intelligence and Analytics, PCA is used to identify patterns in large datasets and to reduce the number of variables in a dataset, making it easier to visualize and analyze. 

The steps involved in PCA are:
1. Data standardization: converting the variables to have a mean of 0 and a standard deviation of 1.
2. Calculation of covariance matrix: to identify the relationships between variables.
3. Calculation of eigenvectors and eigenvalues: to determine the principal components.
4. Determining the number of components to retain: based on the explained variance.
5. Transformation of the original data into the principal component space: by multiplying the data by the eigenvectors.

Applications of PCA in Business Intelligence and Analytics include:
1. Data visualization
2. Data compression
3. Outlier detection
4. Feature selection
5. Noise reduction
6. Data exploration

It is important to note that PCA is an unsupervised technique, meaning it does not use any target variable to perform the analysis.
