## Unit 3 - BI – DATA PREPARTTION

Unit 3 of Business Intelligence (BI) focuses on data preparation, a crucial step in the BI process. The goal of data preparation is to clean, transform, and organize data so that it can be analyzed and used effectively. This involves several steps including:

1. Data collection: Gathering data from various sources such as databases, spreadsheets, and APIs.

2. Data cleaning: Removing any irrelevant, inconsistent, or duplicate data.

3. Data transformation: Converting data into a format that can be easily analyzed, such as converting text to numbers.

4. Data enrichment: Adding additional information to the data, such as geographical information or demographic data.

5. Data integration: Combining data from multiple sources into a single, unified dataset.

6. Data normalization: Ensuring that data is consistent and comparable across different sources.

Data preparation is a critical step in the BI process as it lays the foundation for accurate and meaningful analysis. Poorly prepared data can lead to incorrect insights and decisions. To ensure the quality of the data, it is important to have a clear understanding of the data sources, data types, and data relationships.

Tools such as data wrangling and data blending software can be used to automate and streamline the data preparation process. However, it is important to have a good understanding of the data and the data preparation process to effectively use these tools.

In conclusion, data preparation is a key aspect of the BI process and plays a crucial role in ensuring the accuracy and usefulness of the resulting insights and decisions.
