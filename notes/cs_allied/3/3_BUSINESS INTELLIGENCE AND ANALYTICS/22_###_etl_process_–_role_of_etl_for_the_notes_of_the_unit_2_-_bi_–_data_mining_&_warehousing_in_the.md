### ETL Process – Role of ETL for the notes of the Unit 2 - BI – DATA MINING & WAREHOUSING in the subject of BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE AND ANALYTICS KCS

The ETL process is a crucial step in the data warehousing and business intelligence (BI) pipeline. It involves extracting data from various sources, transforming it into a consistent format, and loading it into a centralized data repository (i.e. data warehouse).

The role of ETL in BI and data mining is to provide a single source of truth for analysis and reporting. It allows organizations to integrate data from disparate systems into a single, unified data model. This enables organizations to gain a comprehensive view of their data, which is essential for making informed business decisions.

The ETL process typically includes the following steps:
1. Extraction: Collecting data from various sources such as databases, flat files, or APIs.
2. Transformation: Cleaning, transforming, and standardizing the data to ensure consistency and accuracy. This may involve removing duplicates, correcting errors, and transforming data into the desired format.
3. Loading: Loading the transformed data into the data warehouse.

In summary, the ETL process plays a crucial role in enabling organizations to make informed business decisions by providing a single source of truth for analysis and reporting.
