### Business Activity Monitoring for the notes of the Unit 5 - IMPLEMENTATION OF BI in the subject of BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE AND ANALYTICS KCS

Business Activity Monitoring (BAM) is a process of real-time monitoring and analysis of business processes, with the goal of identifying and addressing performance issues and opportunities for improvement. 

In the context of Business Intelligence and Analytics, BAM is used to provide real-time visibility into key business processes, allowing organizations to quickly identify and respond to performance issues and opportunities for improvement. 

The steps involved in BAM are:
1. Data collection: gathering data from various sources, such as transactional systems, log files, and sensors.
2. Data preparation: cleaning and transforming the data to prepare it for analysis.
3. Data analysis: analyzing the data to identify performance issues and opportunities for improvement.
4. Alert generation: generating alerts when performance issues or opportunities for improvement are identified.
5. Visualization: visualizing the data and alerts to provide real-time visibility into business processes.
6. Decision making: using the insights from the data and alerts to make informed decisions about business processes.

Applications of BAM in Business Intelligence and Analytics include:
1. Real-time monitoring of key business processes
2. Early detection of performance issues and opportunities for improvement
3. Improved decision making and operational efficiency
4. Improved customer satisfaction and loyalty

In conclusion, Business Activity Monitoring (BAM) is a process of real-time monitoring and analysis of business processes, with the goal of identifying and addressing performance issues and opportunities for improvement. Understanding BAM is important for improving the performance and efficiency of business processes in an organization.
