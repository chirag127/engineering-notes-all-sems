### Social Media Analytics

- Social media analytics is the process of gathering and analyzing data from social networks such as Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, or Twitter   .
- Social media analytics can support business decisions and measure the performance of actions based on those decisions through social media.
- Social media analytics is broader than metrics such as likes, follows, retweets, previews, clicks, and impressions gathered from individual channels.
- Social media analytics also differs from reporting offered by services that support marketing campaigns such as LinkedIn or Google Analytics.
- Social media analytics can benefit businesses by enabling marketers to spot trends in consumer behavior that are relevant to a business's industry and can influence the success of its products and services.
- Social media analytics can also help businesses to understand their target audience, monitor their online reputation, identify influencers and advocates, optimize their content strategy, and measure their return on investment (ROI) from social media.
- Social media analytics can be performed using various tools and techniques, such as social media monitoring or social listening, sentiment analysis, text analysis, network analysis, and visual analytics .
- Social media monitoring or social listening is the process of tracking online conversations about products, brands, or topics of interest on social media platforms .
- Sentiment analysis is the process of determining the emotional tone or attitude of the text or speech, such as positive, negative, or neutral .
- Text analysis is the process of extracting meaningful information from unstructured text data, such as keywords, topics, entities, or themes .
- Network analysis is the process of studying the structure and dynamics of social networks, such as nodes, links, clusters, or communities .
- Visual analytics is the process of using interactive visualizations to explore and communicate complex data and insights .
