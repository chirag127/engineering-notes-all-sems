## Unit 5 - IMPLEMENTATION OF BI

Business intelligence (BI) is the process of transforming data into insights that help businesses make better decisions, improve performance, and gain a competitive edge. BI involves collecting, integrating, analyzing, and presenting data from various sources, such as internal databases, external systems, web services, social media, etc. BI also includes tools and techniques for data visualization, reporting, dashboarding, data mining, predictive analytics, and more.

To implement BI successfully in an organization, the following steps are recommended  :

- Create a BI strategy that aligns with the business goals and objectives, identifies the key stakeholders and their needs, defines the scope and priorities of the BI project, and establishes the metrics and KPIs to measure the outcomes and ROI of the BI initiative.
- Appoint stakeholders and educate the staff on the benefits and value of BI, the roles and responsibilities of each team member, the best practices and standards for data quality and governance, and the expected outcomes and deliverables of the BI project.
- Build a strong BI team or outsource the BI development and maintenance to a reliable and experienced vendor. The BI team should consist of experts in data management, data analysis, data visualization, data science, and business domain knowledge. The BI team should also collaborate closely with the business users and IT staff to ensure the BI solution meets the requirements and expectations of the end-users.
- Find the best software and tools for your BI needs, such as data integration tools, data warehouse or data lake platforms, BI platforms, data visualization tools, etc. The software and tools should be scalable, secure, reliable, user-friendly, and compatible with your existing data sources and systems. You should also consider the cost, features, support, and maintenance of the software and tools before making a decision.
- Choose your data storage, environment, and platform for your BI solution, such as cloud, on-premise, or hybrid. The data storage should be able to store and process large volumes and varieties of data, such as structured, semi-structured, and unstructured data. The environment and platform should be able to support the BI functionalities, such as data integration, data analysis, data visualization, etc. You should also consider the performance, availability, security, and scalability of the data storage, environment, and platform.
- Finetune your data preparation process, which involves extracting, transforming, loading, cleansing, validating, and enriching the data from various sources into the data storage. The data preparation process should ensure the data is accurate, consistent, complete, and relevant for the BI analysis and reporting. The data preparation process should also be automated, documented, and monitored to ensure the data quality and timeliness.
- Consider more advanced solutions, such as data mining, predictive analytics, machine learning, artificial intelligence, etc. to enhance your BI capabilities and insights. These solutions can help you discover hidden patterns, trends, and relationships in the data, forecast future outcomes and scenarios, optimize business processes and decisions, and generate recommendations and actions based on the data. These solutions require more sophisticated tools, techniques, and skills, but can also provide more value and competitive advantage for your business.
- Deploy, test, and launch your BI solution to the end-users, such as managers, analysts, executives, etc. The BI solution should be easy to access, use, and understand, and should provide relevant, timely, and actionable insights for the end-users. The BI solution should also be evaluated and validated against the metrics and KPIs defined in the BI strategy, and the feedback and suggestions from the end-users should be collected and incorporated into the BI solution.
- Maintain and improve your BI solution over time, by monitoring the performance, usage, and satisfaction of the BI solution, updating the data sources and systems, adding new features and functionalities, fixing bugs and errors, and providing training and support for the end-users. The BI solution should also be aligned with the changing business needs and objectives, and the emerging trends and technologies in the BI domain.
