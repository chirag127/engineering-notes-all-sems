### Predictive Analytics

- Predictive analytics is a set of business intelligence (BI) technologies that uncovers relationships and patterns within large volumes of data that can be used to predict behavior and events.
- Predictive analytics is forward-looking, using past events to anticipate the future.
- Predictive analytics is a branch of advanced analytics that makes predictions about future outcomes using historical data combined with statistical modeling, data mining techniques and machine learning.
- Predictive analytics can help companies find patterns in data to identify risks and opportunities, improve decision making, optimize processes, enhance customer satisfaction, and increase revenue and profit .
- Predictive analytics can be applied to various domains, such as marketing, finance, healthcare, education, manufacturing, retail, and government .

#### Analytical Techniques

- Predictive analytics uses various analytical techniques to create predictive models that can generate forecasts, classifications, or recommendations .
- Some of the common analytical techniques are :
  - Machine learning: The ability of a machine to learn and then mimic human intelligence without explicit programming. Machine learning can use supervised, unsupervised, or reinforcement learning methods to find patterns and make predictions.
  - Predictive modeling: A statistical technique used to predict future behavior. Predictive modeling utilizes regression, classification, or clustering algorithms to analyze data and generate a mathematical function that represents the relationship between input and output variables.
  - Regression techniques: A type of predictive modeling that estimates the relationship between a dependent variable and one or more independent variables. Regression techniques can be linear or nonlinear, and can handle continuous or discrete data. Examples of regression techniques are linear regression, logistic regression, polynomial regression, and decision trees.
  - Classification techniques: A type of predictive modeling that assigns a label to an input based on predefined categories. Classification techniques can handle binary or multiclass problems, and can use probabilistic or deterministic approaches. Examples of classification techniques are k-nearest neighbors, support vector machines, naive Bayes, and neural networks.
  - Clustering techniques: A type of predictive modeling that groups similar inputs together based on their features. Clustering techniques can be hierarchical or partitioning, and can use distance or density measures. Examples of clustering techniques are k-means, hierarchical clustering, and DBSCAN.
  - Association rules: A type of data mining technique that discovers frequent patterns, associations, or correlations among a set of items or variables. Association rules can be used to find market basket analysis, product recommendation, or customer segmentation. Examples of association rules are Apriori, Eclat, and FP-growth.
  - Time series analysis: A type of data analysis that studies the behavior and trends of a variable over time. Time series analysis can be used to forecast future values, detect anomalies, or identify seasonality and cycles. Examples of time series analysis are ARIMA, exponential smoothing, and LSTM.

#### Predictive Analytics Process

- Predictive analytics follows a systematic process that involves the following steps :
  - Define the problem: Identify the business objective, the target variable, and the scope of the analysis.
  - Collect the data: Gather the relevant data from various sources, such as databases, files, web, or sensors.
  - Prepare the data: Clean, transform, and integrate the data to make it suitable for analysis. This may include handling missing values, outliers, duplicates, errors, or inconsistencies.
  - Explore the data: Perform descriptive and exploratory analysis to understand the characteristics, distribution, and relationships of the data. This may include using summary statistics, visualizations, or hypothesis testing.
  - Build the model: Select and apply the appropriate analytical technique to create the predictive model. This may include splitting the data into training and testing sets, choosing the parameters and hyperparameters, and evaluating the performance and accuracy of the model.
