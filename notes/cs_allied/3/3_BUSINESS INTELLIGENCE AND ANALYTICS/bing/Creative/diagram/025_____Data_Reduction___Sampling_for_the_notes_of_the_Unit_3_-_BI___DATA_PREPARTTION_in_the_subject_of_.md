### Data Reduction – Sampling

- Data reduction is a technique used in data mining to reduce the size of a dataset while still preserving the most important information.
- Data sampling is one of the methods used for data reduction, as it can reduce the large dataset into a much smaller data sample .
- Data sampling can be useful for reducing the size of a dataset while still preserving the overall trends and patterns in the data .
- There are four types of sampling data reduction methods:
  - Simple Random Sample Without Replacement (SRSWOR): This method involves selecting a subset of the data randomly without allowing duplicates. The size of the sample is fixed in advance.
  - Simple Random Sample With Replacement (SRSWR): This method involves selecting a subset of the data randomly with allowing duplicates. The size of the sample is fixed in advance.
  - Cluster Sample: This method involves dividing the data into groups or clusters based on some criteria, and then selecting one or more clusters randomly to form the sample. The size of the sample is variable depending on the number and size of the clusters selected.
  - Stratified Sample: This method involves dividing the data into groups or strata based on some criteria, and then selecting a simple random sample from each stratum to form the sample. The size of the sample is variable depending on the number and size of the strata and the sampling fraction for each stratum.
