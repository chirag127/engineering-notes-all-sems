# KCS

KCS stands for Knowledge-Centered Service, a methodology that aims to improve service delivery and customer satisfaction by creating and maintaining documentation as part of the support process .

Some of the main features and benefits of KCS are:

- It is based on the principle of "capture, structure, reuse, and improve" knowledge as a by-product of solving problems .
- It empowers support agents to create, update, and publish knowledge articles without requiring approval from a central authority .
- It enables faster and more consistent resolution of issues by reusing existing knowledge and avoiding duplication of effort .
- It improves the quality and relevance of knowledge by incorporating feedback from users and agents, and by applying analytics and metrics to measure performance and identify gaps .
- It fosters a culture of collaboration and learning among support teams, and enhances their skills and confidence .
- It reduces costs and increases efficiency by minimizing the need for escalation, rework, and training .

KCS is not to be confused with KCS, the abbreviation for Kansas City Southern, a railroad company that operates in the United States and Mexico .
