### Difference between OLAP and OLTP

- OLAP stands for Online Analytical Processing, while OLTP stands for Online Transaction Processing.
- OLAP is optimized for conducting complex data analysis for smarter decision-making, while OLTP is ideal for making simple updates, insertions and deletions in databases.
- OLAP has a multi-dimensional schema, so it can support complex queries of multiple data facts and dimensions, while OLTP has a relational schema, so it can support simple queries of single data facts and dimensions .
- OLAP processes data more slowly as compared to OLTP, because the OLAP queries often involve large numbers of records and calculations, while OLTP processes data more quickly, because the OLTP queries are simple and straightforward.
- OLAP updates and inserts data less frequently, because the OLAP data is usually historical and aggregated from various sources, while OLTP updates and inserts data more frequently, because the OLTP data is current and transactional.
- OLAP requires more storage space, because the OLAP data is denormalized and duplicated for faster access, while OLTP requires less storage space, because the OLTP data is normalized and minimized for consistency.
