## Unit 2 - BI – DATA MINING & WAREHOUSING

- **Business Intelligence (BI)** is the process of transforming data into insights that help businesses make better decisions and improve their performance .
- **Data Warehousing (DW)** is the process of collecting, integrating, and storing data from multiple sources in a centralized and consistent data store that supports data analysis, data mining, artificial intelligence, and machine learning .
- **Data Mining (DM)** is the process of discovering patterns, trends, and associations in large and complex data sets using various techniques such as classification, clustering, association, regression, and anomaly detection .
- **BI, DW, and DM** together form the backbone of data-driven decision making, as they enable organizations to run powerful analytics on huge volumes of data and generate actionable insights .
- Some of the benefits of BI, DW, and DM are:
  - They help improve data quality, governance, and security by ensuring data is accurate, consistent, and accessible .
  - They help reduce data redundancy, complexity, and cost by eliminating data silos and consolidating data in a single source of truth .
  - They help enhance data exploration, visualization, and reporting by providing user-friendly tools and interfaces that allow users to interact with data and create dashboards and reports .
  - They help support data-driven decision making by providing insights that can help optimize business processes, identify opportunities, and solve problems .
  - They help foster innovation and competitiveness by enabling organizations to leverage data as a strategic asset and gain a competitive edge in the market .
