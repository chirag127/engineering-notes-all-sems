### Selection for the notes of the Unit 3 - BI – DATA PREPARTTION in the subject of BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE AND ANALYTICS KCS

- Selection is the process of choosing a subset of data from a larger data set based on some criteria or condition.
- Selection can be used for various purposes, such as filtering out irrelevant or noisy data, reducing the size of the data set, focusing on a specific aspect or dimension of the data, or extracting a sample for further analysis.
- Selection can be performed at different levels of granularity, such as row-level, column-level, cell-level, or group-level.
- Selection can be applied to different types of data, such as structured, semi-structured, or unstructured data.
- Selection can be done manually or automatically, depending on the complexity and volume of the data and the availability of tools and techniques.
- Selection can be based on different criteria or conditions, such as value, range, pattern, frequency, similarity, dissimilarity, correlation, causation, or user preference.
- Selection can be done using different methods or techniques, such as SQL queries, regular expressions, filters, slicers, pivot tables, charts, graphs, or visual analytics tools.
- Selection can have different effects or outcomes, such as improving the quality, accuracy, relevance, or completeness of the data, enhancing the performance, efficiency, or scalability of the analysis, or revealing new insights, patterns, or trends in the data.
