### Data Information and Knowledge

- Data is a collection of raw facts, such as numbers, words, images, sounds, or videos, that can be processed by a computer  .
- Information is data that has been processed, organized, structured, or presented in a meaningful way to provide context and meaning  .
- Knowledge is information that has been applied, analyzed, synthesized, or evaluated to produce insights, understanding, or wisdom  .
- Business intelligence (BI) is the process of transforming data into information, and information into knowledge, to support data-driven decision making in organizations  .
- BI includes data analytics and business analytics, but uses them only as parts of the whole process. BI helps users draw conclusions from data analysis and present them in a visual and interactive way .
- BI combines business analytics, data mining, data visualization, data tools and infrastructure, and best practices to help organizations make more data-driven decisions.
- BI can help organizations improve their performance, efficiency, profitability, customer satisfaction, and competitive advantage  .
- Knowledge management (KM) is the process of creating, sharing, using, and managing the knowledge and information of an organization.
- KM includes the activities of BI, but also covers the human and social aspects of knowledge creation and sharing, such as culture, collaboration, communication, and learning.
- KM aims to leverage the collective knowledge and experience of an organization to enhance its innovation, creativity, and problem-solving capabilities.
- KM can help organizations improve their adaptability, agility, responsiveness, and sustainability.
