### Complex Event Processing

- Complex event processing (CEP) is the use of technology to predict high-level events likely to result from specific sets of low-level factors .
- By identifying and analyzing cause-and-effect relationships among events in real time, CEP allows personnel to take effective actions in response to specific opportunities or threats.
- CEP matches continuously incoming events against a pattern and provides insight into what is happening.
- CEP is also known as event, stream or event stream processing.
- CEP is a technology for aggregating, processing, and analyzing massive streams of data in order to gain real-time insights from events as they occur.
- CEP can be used for various applications, such as fraud detection, risk management, customer behavior analysis, business process optimization, and smart grid management.
