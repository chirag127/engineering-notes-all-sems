### Social Media Analytics

- Social media analytics is the process of gathering and analyzing data from social networks such as Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, or Twitter   .
- Social media analytics is broader than metrics such as likes, follows, retweets, previews, clicks, and impressions gathered from individual channels. It also differs from reporting offered by services that support marketing campaigns such as LinkedIn or Google Analytics.
- Social media analytics can benefit businesses by enabling marketers to spot trends in consumer behavior that are relevant to a business's industry and can influence the success of its products and services.
- Social media analytics can also help businesses to monitor their online reputation, identify influencers and advocates, measure the impact of their social media campaigns, and optimize their social media strategy .
- Social media analytics can be performed using various tools and techniques, such as social media monitoring or social listening, sentiment analysis, text analysis, network analysis, and visual analytics .
- Social media monitoring or social listening is the process of tracking online conversations about a brand, product, company, or topic across different social media platforms .
- Sentiment analysis is the process of determining the emotional tone or attitude of a text, such as a tweet, a comment, or a review, towards a subject or an entity .
- Text analysis is the process of extracting meaningful information from unstructured text data, such as keywords, topics, themes, entities, or categories .
- Network analysis is the process of studying the structure and dynamics of social networks, such as the relationships and interactions among users, groups, or communities .
- Visual analytics is the process of using graphical representations and interactive visualizations to explore, analyze, and communicate data and insights .
