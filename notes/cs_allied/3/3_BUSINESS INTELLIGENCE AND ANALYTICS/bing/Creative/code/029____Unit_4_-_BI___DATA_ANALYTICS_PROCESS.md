# Unit 4 - BI – DATA ANALYTICS PROCESS

## Learning Objectives

- To understand the steps involved in the data analytics process.
- To learn the techniques and tools for data collection, preparation, analysis, and visualization.
- To apply the data analytics process to various business scenarios and problems.

## Introduction

- Data analytics is the process of transforming raw data into meaningful insights that can support decision making and problem solving in various domains.
- Data analytics can be classified into four types: descriptive, diagnostic, predictive, and prescriptive.
- Descriptive analytics summarizes what has happened in the past using historical data.
- Diagnostic analytics explains why something has happened in the past using statistical methods and data mining techniques.
- Predictive analytics forecasts what is likely to happen in the future using machine learning and artificial intelligence techniques.
- Prescriptive analytics recommends what actions should be taken in the future using optimization and simulation techniques.
- The data analytics process consists of six steps: define the problem, collect the data, prepare the data, analyze the data, visualize the data, and communicate the results.

## Define the Problem

- The first step in the data analytics process is to define the problem or the question that needs to be answered using data.
- The problem definition should be clear, specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, and time-bound (SMART).
- The problem definition should also include the scope, objectives, assumptions, constraints, and stakeholders of the project.
- The problem definition should guide the subsequent steps of the data analytics process and help evaluate the results.

## Collect the Data

- The second step in the data analytics process is to collect the data that is relevant and sufficient to answer the problem or the question.
- The data can be collected from various sources, such as internal databases, external websites, social media, surveys, sensors, etc.
- The data can be structured, semi-structured, or unstructured, depending on the format and the level of organization of the data.
- The data can be quantitative or qualitative, depending on the type and the scale of the data.
- The data collection process should follow the principles of data quality, such as accuracy, completeness, consistency, timeliness, validity, and reliability.

## Prepare the Data

- The third step in the data analytics process is to prepare the data for analysis by cleaning, transforming, and integrating the data.
- The data preparation process involves identifying and handling missing values, outliers, errors, duplicates, and inconsistencies in the data.
- The data preparation process also involves transforming the data into a suitable format and structure for analysis, such as normalizing, scaling, encoding, aggregating, and discretizing the data.
- The data preparation process also involves integrating the data from different sources and formats into a single data set or a data warehouse, using techniques such as joining, merging, appending, and concatenating the data.
- The data preparation process should ensure that the data is ready and reliable for analysis.

## Analyze the Data

- The fourth step in the data analytics process is to analyze the data using various techniques and tools to generate insights and answer the problem or the question.
- The data analysis process involves applying descriptive, diagnostic, predictive, and prescriptive analytics techniques to the data, depending on the type and the purpose of the analysis.
- The data analysis process also involves selecting and applying appropriate statistical methods, machine learning algorithms, and artificial intelligence models to the data, depending on the characteristics and the complexity of the data.
- The data analysis process also involves evaluating and validating the results of the analysis, using techniques such as error analysis, cross-validation, and hypothesis testing.
- The data analysis process should produce accurate, relevant, and actionable insights for the problem or the question.

## Visualize the Data

- The fifth step in the data analytics process is to visualize the data and the results of the analysis using various techniques and tools to communicate the insights and the findings effectively.
- The data visualization process involves choosing and creating appropriate charts, graphs, maps, dashboards, and reports to display the data and the results of the analysis, depending on the audience and the message of the visualization.
- The data visualization process also involves applying the principles of data visualization, such as simplicity, clarity, consistency, contrast, and color, to enhance the readability and the attractiveness of the visualization.
- The data visualization process also involves interpreting and explaining the visualization, using techniques such as annotations, captions, titles, and legends, to convey the meaning and the significance of the visualization.
- The data visualization process should deliver clear, concise, and compelling visualizations for the data and the results of the analysis
