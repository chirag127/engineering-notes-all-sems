### Business Activity Monitoring

Business Activity Monitoring (BAM) is the process of monitoring business activities through business process management software. BAM provides real-time information about the status and results of various operations, processes, and transactions. BAM is used to improve the speed and effectiveness of business operations by keeping track of what is happening and making issues visible quickly. BAM also enables an enterprise to make better informed business decisions, quickly address problem areas, and re-position organizations to take full advantage of emerging opportunities  .

Some of the key concepts and components of BAM are:

- **Business events**: These are the occurrences or changes in the state of business entities or processes that are of interest to the business. Examples of business events are orders, shipments, payments, inventory levels, customer complaints, etc. Business events can be generated from various sources, such as applications, databases, sensors, devices, etc.
- **Business metrics**: These are the measures or indicators of business performance or outcomes that are derived from business events. Examples of business metrics are revenue, profit, customer satisfaction, market share, etc. Business metrics can be aggregated, filtered, calculated, or transformed to provide meaningful insights.
- **Business rules**: These are the policies or conditions that define the business logic or behavior of the business processes or entities. Examples of business rules are discounts, promotions, credit limits, service level agreements, etc. Business rules can be used to trigger actions or alerts based on business events or metrics.
- **Business dashboards**: These are the graphical user interfaces that display the business metrics and events in real-time or near real-time. Examples of business dashboards are charts, gauges, tables, maps, etc. Business dashboards can be customized, interactive, and drill-down to provide different levels of detail and analysis.
- **Business alerts**: These are the notifications or messages that inform the business users or stakeholders about the occurrence of important or exceptional business events or metrics. Examples of business alerts are emails, SMS, pop-ups, sounds, etc. Business alerts can be configured, prioritized, and escalated to ensure timely and appropriate response.
