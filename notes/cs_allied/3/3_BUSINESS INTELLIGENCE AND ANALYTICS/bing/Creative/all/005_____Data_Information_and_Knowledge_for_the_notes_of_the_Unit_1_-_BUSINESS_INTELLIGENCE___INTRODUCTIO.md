# Data, Information, and Knowledge

- Data, information, and knowledge are key concepts in business intelligence and analytics.
- Data are raw facts that are collected from various sources, such as transactions, sensors, surveys, etc. Data can be structured, semi-structured, or unstructured, depending on the format and level of organization.
- Information is data that has been processed, organized, and presented in a meaningful context, such as a report, a dashboard, a chart, etc. Information can answer questions or support decision making by providing insights and patterns.
- Knowledge is information that has been applied, interpreted, and evaluated by human experts, such as managers, analysts, or consultants. Knowledge can generate solutions, recommendations, or actions that can help improve business performance and achieve goals.
- Business intelligence (BI) is the process of transforming data into information, and information into knowledge, using various tools and techniques, such as data analytics, data mining, data visualization, data tools and infrastructure, and best practices.
- Business analytics (BA) is a subset of BI that focuses on the use of advanced statistics and predictive analytics to discover patterns and forecast future trends from data.
- Knowledge management (KM) is a broader concept that encompasses BI and BA, as well as other activities, such as capturing, storing, sharing, and applying knowledge within and across organizations.
