## Unit 2 - BI – DATA MINING & WAREHOUSING

- Data mining is the process of discovering patterns, trends, and insights from large and complex data sets using various techniques and algorithms.
- Data warehousing is the process of designing, building, and maintaining a centralized repository of integrated, consistent, and reliable data from multiple sources for business intelligence purposes.
- The main components of a data warehouse are:
  - Data sources: The original data sources that provide the raw data for the data warehouse, such as operational databases, files, web logs, etc.
  - ETL (Extract, Transform, Load): The process of extracting data from the data sources, transforming it into a suitable format and structure, and loading it into the data warehouse.
  - Data warehouse: The database that stores the transformed and integrated data in a multidimensional model, such as star schema or snowflake schema, that supports efficient querying and analysis.
  - Data marts: The subsets of data from the data warehouse that are tailored for specific business functions or users, such as sales, marketing, finance, etc.
  - OLAP (Online Analytical Processing): The tools and techniques that enable users to perform complex and interactive analysis on the data warehouse or data marts, such as slicing, dicing, drilling, pivoting, etc.
  - Data mining: The tools and techniques that enable users to discover hidden patterns, trends, and insights from the data warehouse or data marts, such as classification, clustering, association, regression, etc.
  - BI applications: The applications that provide users with the ability to access, visualize, and communicate the results of data mining and OLAP, such as dashboards, reports, charts, etc.

- The main benefits of data mining and warehousing are:
  - They enable users to gain a deeper and broader understanding of their business processes, customers, competitors, and markets.
  - They support data-driven decision making and problem solving by providing actionable insights and recommendations.
  - They improve the quality, consistency, and reliability of data by eliminating data silos, redundancies, and inconsistencies.
  - They enhance the performance, scalability, and security of data management and analysis by using specialized hardware, software, and techniques.
  - They increase the value and usability of data by transforming it into meaningful and relevant information and knowledge.
