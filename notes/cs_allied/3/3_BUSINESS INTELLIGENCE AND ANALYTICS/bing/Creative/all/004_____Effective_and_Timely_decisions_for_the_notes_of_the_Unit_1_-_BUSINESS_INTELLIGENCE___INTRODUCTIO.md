# Effective and Timely Decisions

- Effective and timely decisions are the ones that help the organization achieve its goals and objectives, and respond to the changing business environment.
- The main purpose of business intelligence systems is to provide knowledge workers with tools and methodologies that allow them to make effective and timely decisions  .
- Effective decisions are based on reliable and relevant information and knowledge, which are derived from rigorous analytical methods and techniques.
- Timely decisions are made within the appropriate time frame, considering the urgency and complexity of the situation, and the availability and quality of the data.
- Business intelligence systems enable effective and timely decisions by:
  - Collecting and integrating data from various sources, both internal and external, and transforming them into a consistent and coherent format.
  - Storing and organizing data in a data warehouse or a data lake, which serves as a single source of truth for the organization.
  - Analyzing and processing data using various methods, such as data mining, data visualization, machine learning, natural language processing, etc.
  - Presenting and communicating the results of the analysis in a clear and understandable way, using dashboards, reports, charts, graphs, etc.
  - Supporting the decision making process by providing insights, recommendations, predictions, and actions based on the analysis.
  - Monitoring and evaluating the outcomes and impacts of the decisions, and providing feedback and learning for future improvement.
