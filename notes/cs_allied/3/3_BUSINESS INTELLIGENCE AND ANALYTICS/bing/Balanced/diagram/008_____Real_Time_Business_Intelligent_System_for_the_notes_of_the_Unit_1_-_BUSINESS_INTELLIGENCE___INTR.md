### Real Time Business Intelligent System

- A real time business intelligent system is a system that delivers business intelligence or information about business operations as they occur.
- It enables access to the most relevant, up-to-the-minute data and visualizations for decision making.
- It can use different architectures and techniques to achieve real time data processing, such as:
  - Event-based: using complex event processing, event stream processing and mashup techniques to analyze events without storing them in a database.
  - Data warehouse: increasing the refresh cycle of an existing data warehouse to provide near real time data.
  - Server-less technology: using cloud-based services and platforms to provide real time data without the need for servers or infrastructure.
- It can provide various benefits for businesses, such as:
  - Faster and more accurate insights into business performance and trends.
  - Improved customer satisfaction and loyalty by providing personalized and timely services.
  - Enhanced operational efficiency and agility by optimizing processes and resources.
  - Increased competitive advantage and innovation by identifying and exploiting new opportunities.
