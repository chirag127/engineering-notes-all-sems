### Introduction to analytics process

- Analytics is the process of discovering, interpreting, and communicating significant patterns in data .
- Analytics helps us see insights and meaningful data that we might not otherwise detect.
- Analytics can be applied to different domains, such as website analytics, business analytics, social media analytics, etc.
- Analytics can also be classified into different types, such as descriptive analytics, diagnostic analytics, predictive analytics, and prescriptive analytics, based on the purpose and scope of the analysis.
- Analytics process is a systematic and iterative approach to analyze data and generate insights for decision making.
- Analytics process consists of three main elements: input, process, and output.
- Input refers to the data sources and data quality that are used for the analysis.
- Process refers to the methods and techniques that are applied to the data, such as data cleaning, data transformation, data modeling, data visualization, etc.
- Output refers to the results and recommendations that are derived from the analysis, such as reports, dashboards, alerts, actions, etc.
- Analytics process can be improved by following some best practices, such as defining the business problem, identifying the stakeholders, selecting the appropriate tools and methods, validating and verifying the results, and communicating the findings effectively.
