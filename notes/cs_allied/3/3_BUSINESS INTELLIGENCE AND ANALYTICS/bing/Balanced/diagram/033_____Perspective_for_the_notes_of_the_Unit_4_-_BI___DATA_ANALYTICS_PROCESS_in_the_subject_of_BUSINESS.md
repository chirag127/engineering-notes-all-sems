### Perspective for the notes of the Unit 4 - BI – DATA ANALYTICS PROCESS in the subject of BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE AND ANALYTICS KCS

- Perspective in data analytics refers to the process of analyzing data from various viewpoints or perspectives to gain deeper insights and make informed decisions.
- There are different types of perspectives in data analytics, such as descriptive, diagnostic, predictive, and prescriptive perspectives.
- Descriptive perspective answers the question of what happened in the past or what is happening now by summarizing and visualizing data.
- Diagnostic perspective answers the question of why something happened or is happening by exploring the causes and effects of data patterns.
- Predictive perspective answers the question of what will happen in the future by using statistical models and machine learning algorithms to forecast data trends and outcomes.
- Prescriptive perspective answers the question of what should be done to achieve a desired goal or optimize a situation by using optimization techniques and simulation models to generate and evaluate recommendations for action .
- The data analysis perspective is also a process that helps marketers progress from start to scale by targeting actionable insights v. more traditional, linear data analysis/response approaches.
- The data analysis perspective is also a tool for data-driven decision-making, which involves using data and analytics to support and improve business decisions and actions.
- The key steps in data and analytics strategic planning are to: start with the mission and goals of the organization, determine the strategic impact of data and analytics on those goals, prioritize action steps to realize business goals using data and analytics objectives, and build a data and analytics strategic roadmap.
- The data analysis perspective is an essential skill for business intelligence and analytics professionals, as it enables them to understand, interpret, and communicate data insights effectively and efficiently.
