### Data Modelling

- Data modelling is the process of creating a visual representation of either a whole information system or parts of it to communicate connections between data points and structures  .
- Data modelling is a core data management discipline that helps pinpoint information needs for different business processes.
- Data modelling is an iterative process that should be repeated and refined as business needs change.
- Data modelling is used to define and analyze data requirements needed to support the business processes within the scope of corresponding information systems in organizations.

#### Types of Data Modelling

- Data modelling has evolved alongside database management systems, with model types increasing in complexity as businesses' data storage needs have grown.
- Here are several model types:

  - **Conceptual data model:** A high-level, abstract representation of the data and their relationships, without any technical details. It is used to communicate the overall scope and objectives of the data to stakeholders .
  - **Logical data model:** A more detailed and structured representation of the data and their relationships, with attributes, data types, constraints, and business rules. It is used to validate the data requirements and design the database schema .
  - **Physical data model:** A specific and technical representation of the data and their relationships, with physical storage parameters, indexes, keys, partitions, and performance optimization. It is used to implement the database on a specific platform .

#### Benefits of Data Modelling

- Data modelling can provide several benefits for businesses, such as:

  - Improving data quality and consistency by enforcing standards and rules .
  - Enhancing data integration and interoperability by facilitating data sharing and exchange across systems and applications .
  - Increasing data security and compliance by identifying sensitive and regulated data and applying appropriate policies and controls .
  - Supporting data analysis and decision making by enabling data exploration and visualization .
  - Reducing data complexity and redundancy by eliminating data duplication and conflicts .
  - Saving time and cost by streamlining data development and maintenance .
