# Data Information and Knowledge for Business Intelligence and Analytics

- Data is the raw and unprocessed facts that are collected from various sources, such as transactions, sensors, surveys, etc. Data can be structured, semi-structured, or unstructured, depending on the format and organization of the data. Data by itself has no meaning or context, and needs to be processed and analyzed to extract value from it .
- Information is the result of processing, organizing, and interpreting data in a way that makes it useful and meaningful for a specific purpose. Information can answer questions such as who, what, when, where, and how. Information can be presented in various forms, such as tables, charts, graphs, reports, etc. Information can help users understand the current state of a situation or problem, and provide insights for decision making  .
- Knowledge is the application of information to a context, such as a domain, a problem, or a goal. Knowledge is derived from information by applying rules, logic, experience, intuition, or judgment. Knowledge can answer questions such as why, how, and what if. Knowledge can be explicit or tacit, depending on the degree of codification and transferability. Knowledge can help users solve problems, create new solutions, or improve existing ones .
- Business Intelligence (BI) is the process of collecting, analyzing, and presenting data and information to support business decision making. BI includes data analytics and business analytics, but uses them only as parts of the whole process. BI helps users draw conclusions from data analysis, and communicate them effectively to stakeholders. BI can help organizations make more data-driven decisions, and improve their performance, efficiency, and competitiveness  .
- Business Analytics (BA) is the subset of BI that focuses on descriptive and diagnostic analytics, which answers questions of where the organization has been, where it is now, and why things are the way they are now. BA uses statistical and mathematical techniques to explore, summarize, and explain data patterns and trends. BA can help organizations monitor their key performance indicators, identify their strengths and weaknesses, and discover the root causes of problems .
- Data Analytics (DA) is the subset of BI that focuses on predictive and prescriptive analytics, which answers questions of where the organization is going, and what it should do to achieve its goals. DA uses advanced statistics and machine learning to forecast future outcomes and optimize decisions. DA can help organizations anticipate opportunities and risks, and recommend the best actions to take .
