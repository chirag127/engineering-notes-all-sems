## Unit 2 - BI – DATA MINING & WAREHOUSING

- Data mining is the process of discovering patterns, trends, and insights from large and complex data sets using various techniques and algorithms.
- Data warehousing is the process of designing, building, and maintaining a centralized repository of integrated and consistent data from multiple sources for analytical purposes.
- The main objectives of data mining and warehousing are to support business intelligence (BI), which is the use of data analysis to gain insights and make informed decisions.
- The main components of a data warehouse are:
  - Data sources: the original data from various operational systems, such as databases, files, web logs, etc.
  - Extraction, transformation, and loading (ETL) tools: the software that extracts, cleans, transforms, and loads the data from the sources to the warehouse.
  - Data warehouse database: the storage system that organizes the data in a multidimensional structure, such as tables, cubes, or stars.
  - Metadata: the data that describes the structure, meaning, and quality of the data in the warehouse.
  - Data marts: the subsets of the data warehouse that are tailored for specific business functions or users, such as sales, marketing, or finance.
  - Online analytical processing (OLAP) tools: the software that enables users to perform complex and interactive analysis on the data in the warehouse, such as slicing, dicing, drilling, pivoting, etc.
  - Data mining tools: the software that applies various algorithms and techniques to discover patterns, trends, and insights from the data in the warehouse, such as classification, clustering, association, regression, etc.
  - Data visualization tools: the software that presents the results of the analysis in graphical or interactive forms, such as charts, dashboards, maps, etc.
- The main benefits of data mining and warehousing are:
  - They enable faster and easier access to high-quality and consistent data for analysis.
  - They support various types of analysis, such as descriptive, predictive, and prescriptive.
  - They help to identify hidden patterns, trends, and insights that can improve business performance and competitiveness.
  - They facilitate better decision making and problem solving based on data-driven evidence and insights.
