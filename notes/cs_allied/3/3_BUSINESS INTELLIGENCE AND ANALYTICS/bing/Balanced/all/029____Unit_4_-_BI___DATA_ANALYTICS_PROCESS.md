## Unit 4 - BI – DATA ANALYTICS PROCESS

- Data analytics is the process of transforming raw data into meaningful insights for decision making and problem solving.
- Data analytics can be classified into four types: descriptive, diagnostic, predictive, and prescriptive.
- Descriptive analytics summarizes what has happened in the past using historical data. It answers the question "What happened?" Examples of descriptive analytics are dashboards, reports, charts, and tables.
- Diagnostic analytics analyzes why something happened in the past using historical data. It answers the question "Why did it happen?" Examples of diagnostic analytics are root cause analysis, correlation analysis, and regression analysis.
- Predictive analytics forecasts what is likely to happen in the future using historical data and statistical models. It answers the question "What will happen?" Examples of predictive analytics are forecasting, classification, and clustering.
- Prescriptive analytics recommends what actions should be taken in the future to achieve a desired outcome using historical data, statistical models, and optimization techniques. It answers the question "What should we do?" Examples of prescriptive analytics are optimization, simulation, and decision analysis.
- The data analytics process consists of six steps: define the problem, collect the data, prepare the data, analyze the data, communicate the results, and act on the results.
- Define the problem: This step involves identifying the business objective, the research question, and the scope of the analysis.
- Collect the data: This step involves acquiring the relevant data from various sources, such as databases, files, web, sensors, etc.
- Prepare the data: This step involves cleaning, transforming, and integrating the data to make it suitable for analysis. It includes tasks such as data quality assessment, data cleansing, data integration, data transformation, data reduction, and data exploration.
- Analyze the data: This step involves applying appropriate analytical techniques and tools to the data to generate insights. It includes tasks such as data modeling, data mining, data visualization, and data interpretation.
- Communicate the results: This step involves presenting and explaining the insights to the stakeholders using effective communication methods and formats. It includes tasks such as report writing, dashboard creation, storytelling, and data visualization.
- Act on the results: This step involves implementing the insights into action plans and monitoring the outcomes and feedback. It includes tasks such as decision making, action planning, performance measurement, and feedback collection.
