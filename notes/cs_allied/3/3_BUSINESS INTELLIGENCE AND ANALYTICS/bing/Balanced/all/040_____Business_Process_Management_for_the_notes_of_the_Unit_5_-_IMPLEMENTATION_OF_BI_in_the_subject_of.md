# Business Process Management

- Business Process Management (BPM) is a discipline that uses various methods to discover, model, analyze, measure, improve and optimize business processes  .
- A business process is an activity or set of activities that helps accomplish an organization's goals, such as increasing profits or promoting workforce diversity.
- BPM can help organizations to align their business processes with their business strategy, improve their efficiency and effectiveness, enhance their customer satisfaction and innovation, and reduce their costs and risks   .
- BPM involves the following steps :
  - Define the business process and its objectives, scope, inputs, outputs, stakeholders, and performance indicators.
  - Discover the current state of the business process by collecting data and information from various sources, such as interviews, observations, documents, and systems.
  - Model the current state of the business process by using graphical or textual representations, such as flowcharts, diagrams, or notation languages, to capture its structure, logic, and behavior.
  - Analyze the current state of the business process by identifying its strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats, as well as its gaps, bottlenecks, redundancies, and inefficiencies.
  - Improve the current state of the business process by designing and implementing changes that address the identified issues and enhance the desired outcomes, such as simplifying, automating, standardizing, or integrating the process activities.
  - Monitor the improved state of the business process by measuring and evaluating its performance against the predefined indicators and benchmarks, as well as collecting feedback from the stakeholders and customers.
  - Optimize the improved state of the business process by continuously reviewing and refining the process based on the monitoring results and the changing business needs and environment.
- BPM can be supported by various tools and technologies, such as BPM software, business process modeling tools, business process analysis tools, business process improvement tools, business process monitoring tools, and business process optimization tools .
- BPM can also be integrated with other disciplines and methodologies, such as business intelligence, business analytics, business rules management, business process automation, business process reengineering, lean management, and agile management .
