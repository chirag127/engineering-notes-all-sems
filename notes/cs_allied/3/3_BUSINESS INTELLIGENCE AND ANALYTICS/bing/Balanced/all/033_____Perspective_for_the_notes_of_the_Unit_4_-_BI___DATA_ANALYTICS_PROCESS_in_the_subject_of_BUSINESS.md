# Perspective for the notes of the Unit 4 - BI – DATA ANALYTICS PROCESS in the subject of BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE AND ANALYTICS KCS

- Perspective in data analytics refers to the process of analyzing data from various viewpoints or perspectives to gain deeper insights and make informed decisions.
- There are different types of perspectives in data analytics, such as descriptive, diagnostic, predictive, and prescriptive analytics .
- Descriptive analytics summarizes what has happened in the past using historical data. It answers the question of "what" happened. For example, descriptive analytics can show the sales trends, customer behavior, or operational performance of a business.
- Diagnostic analytics explains why something has happened in the past using statistical techniques. It answers the question of "why" something happened. For example, diagnostic analytics can identify the root causes, correlations, or patterns of a problem or an opportunity.
- Predictive analytics forecasts what is likely to happen in the future using machine learning and data mining. It answers the question of "what if" something happens. For example, predictive analytics can estimate the demand, revenue, or risk of a business scenario.
- Prescriptive analytics recommends what is the best course of action to take in the future using optimization and simulation. It answers the question of "what should" be done. For example, prescriptive analytics can suggest the optimal price, product, or strategy for a business goal.
- The data analytics process is a systematic approach to transform raw data into meaningful and actionable insights. It involves the following steps :
  - Define the business problem or opportunity and the data analytics objectives.
  - Collect and prepare the relevant data from various sources and formats.
  - Explore and analyze the data using appropriate methods and tools.
  - Interpret and communicate the results and recommendations to the stakeholders.
  - Implement and monitor the actions and outcomes of the data analytics project.
