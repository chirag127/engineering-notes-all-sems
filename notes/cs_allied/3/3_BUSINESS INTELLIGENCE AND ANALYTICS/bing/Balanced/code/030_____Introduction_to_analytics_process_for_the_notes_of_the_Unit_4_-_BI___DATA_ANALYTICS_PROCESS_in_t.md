### Introduction to analytics process

- Analytics is the process of discovering, interpreting, and communicating significant patterns in data.
- Analytics helps us see insights and meaningful data that we might not otherwise detect.
- Analytics can be applied to various domains, such as website analytics, business analytics, social media analytics, etc.
- Analytics can be classified into four types: descriptive, predictive, prescriptive, and diagnostic.
  - Descriptive analytics: It summarizes what has happened in the past using data aggregation and data mining techniques.
  - Predictive analytics: It forecasts what might happen in the future using statistical models and machine learning algorithms.
  - Prescriptive analytics: It suggests what actions should be taken to optimize a desired outcome using optimization and simulation techniques.
  - Diagnostic analytics: It explains why something happened in the past using root cause analysis and data discovery tools.
- Business analytics is a specific application of analytics that focuses on improving business performance and decision making using data and analytical methods.
- Business analytics can be used to solve various business problems, such as customer segmentation, churn prediction, product recommendation, fraud detection, etc.
- Business analytics can also be used to support various business functions, such as marketing, finance, operations, human resources, etc.
- Business process analysis (BPA) is an approach to analyzing business operation processes using analytics techniques.
- BPA is a detailed, multi-step examination of each part of a process to identify what is working well, what needs to be improved, and how any necessary improvements can best be made.
- BPA can help to streamline workflows, reduce costs, increase efficiency, enhance customer satisfaction, and achieve business goals.
- BPA can be performed using various tools and methods, such as process mapping, process modeling, process simulation, process measurement, process improvement, etc.
