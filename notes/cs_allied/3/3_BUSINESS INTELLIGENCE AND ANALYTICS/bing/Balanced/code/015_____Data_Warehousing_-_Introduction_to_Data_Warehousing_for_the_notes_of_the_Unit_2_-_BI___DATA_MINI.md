### Data Warehousing - Introduction to Data Warehousing

- A data warehouse is a database designed to enable and support business intelligence (BI) activities, especially analytics .
- A data warehouse aggregates data from different sources into a single, central, consistent data store to support data analysis, data mining, artificial intelligence (AI), and machine learning.
- A data warehouse is solely intended to perform queries and analysis and often contains large amounts of historical data.
- A data warehouse helps users understand and enhance their organization's performance by providing them with relevant, accurate, and timely information.
- A data warehouse can also enable decision support, reporting, forecasting, and trend analysis by integrating data from various operational systems and business processes.
- A data warehouse typically follows a dimensional model, which consists of facts and dimensions. Facts are numerical measures of business events, and dimensions are descriptive attributes that provide context to the facts.
- A data warehouse can be implemented using various architectures, such as the star schema, the snowflake schema, or the data vault model. Each architecture has its own advantages and disadvantages depending on the data sources, the data quality, the data volume, and the analytical requirements.
