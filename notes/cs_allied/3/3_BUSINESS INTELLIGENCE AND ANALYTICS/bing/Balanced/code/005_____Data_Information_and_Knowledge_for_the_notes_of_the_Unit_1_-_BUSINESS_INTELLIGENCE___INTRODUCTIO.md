### Data Information and Knowledge for Business Intelligence and Analytics

- Data is the raw and unprocessed facts that are collected from various sources, such as transactions, sensors, surveys, etc. Data can be structured, semi-structured, or unstructured, depending on the format and organization of the data.
- Information is the processed and organized data that has some meaning and context. Information can be used to answer questions, solve problems, or support decisions. Information can be presented in various forms, such as tables, charts, reports, etc.
- Knowledge is the understanding and insight that is derived from information. Knowledge can be explicit or tacit, depending on the level of codification and transferability of the knowledge. Knowledge can be used to create value, innovate, or improve performance.
- Business Intelligence (BI) is the process of transforming data into information and knowledge that can help organizations make more data-driven decisions. BI involves various activities, such as data collection, data integration, data analysis, data visualization, data dissemination, and data governance. BI can provide descriptive and diagnostic analytics, which answer questions of what happened and why it happened in the past or present.
- Business Analytics (BA) is the process of applying advanced statistical and mathematical techniques to data to discover patterns, trends, and relationships that can help organizations make more predictive and prescriptive decisions. BA involves various methods, such as data mining, machine learning, optimization, simulation, etc. BA can provide predictive and prescriptive analytics, which answer questions of what will happen and what should be done in the future.
