### Effective and Timely Decisions for Business Intelligence

- Business intelligence (BI) is the process of surfacing and analyzing data in an organization to make informed business decisions.
- The main purpose of BI systems is to provide knowledge workers with tools and methodologies that allow them to make effective and timely decisions  .
- Effective decisions are those that rely on information and knowledge that are more dependable, accurate, and relevant to the problem or opportunity at hand  .
- Timely decisions are those that are made within the appropriate time frame, considering the urgency, complexity, and impact of the situation  .
- To make effective and timely decisions, BI systems need to:
  - Collect and integrate data from various sources, such as internal databases, external websites, social media, sensors, etc.
  - Organize and store data in a way that facilitates easy access, retrieval, and analysis, such as using data warehouses, data marts, data lakes, etc.
  - Analyze and transform data into meaningful insights and analytics, such as using descriptive, predictive, and prescriptive methods, data mining, machine learning, artificial intelligence, etc.
  - Report and communicate insights and analytics to the relevant stakeholders, such as using dashboards, visualizations, reports, alerts, etc.
- To develop and implement a successful BI strategy, organizations need to:
  - Assess their current BI ecosystem, including their data sources, data quality, data governance, data infrastructure, data analysis, data reporting, data culture, etc.
  - Define their BI goals and objectives, such as improving customer satisfaction, increasing revenue, reducing costs, enhancing innovation, etc.
  - Identify their BI requirements and challenges, such as data availability, data security, data integration, data analysis, data visualization, data literacy, etc.
  - Evaluate and select the best BI tools and solutions that match their needs and budget, such as cloud-based, on-premise, open-source, proprietary, etc .
  - Implement and monitor their BI projects and initiatives, such as using agile, waterfall, or hybrid methodologies, assigning roles and responsibilities, setting milestones and deliverables, measuring and evaluating outcomes, etc.
  - Continuously improve and optimize their BI processes and practices, such as using feedback, testing, experimentation, learning, etc.
