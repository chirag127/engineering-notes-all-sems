### Social Media Analytics

- Social media analytics is the process of gathering and analyzing data from social networks such as Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, or Twitter   .
- Social media analytics is broader than metrics such as likes, follows, retweets, previews, clicks, and impressions gathered from individual channels. It also differs from reporting offered by services that support marketing campaigns such as LinkedIn or Google Analytics.
- Social media analytics can benefit businesses by enabling marketers to spot trends in consumer behavior that are relevant to a business's industry and can influence the success of its products and services.
- Social media analytics can also help businesses to monitor their online reputation, identify influencers and advocates, measure the impact of their social media campaigns, and optimize their social media strategy  .
- Social media analytics can be performed using various tools and techniques, such as social media monitoring or social listening, sentiment analysis, text analysis, network analysis, and visual analytics .
- Social media monitoring or social listening is the process of tracking online conversations about products, brands, competitors, or topics of interest. It can help businesses to understand the opinions, preferences, and needs of their customers and prospects, as well as to identify potential issues or opportunities.
- Sentiment analysis is the process of determining the emotional tone or attitude of a text, such as a tweet, a comment, or a review. It can help businesses to measure the satisfaction, loyalty, and advocacy of their customers, as well as to detect and respond to negative feedback or complaints.
- Text analysis is the process of extracting meaningful information from unstructured text data, such as keywords, topics, entities, or themes. It can help businesses to discover the main themes and topics of online conversations, as well as to segment and profile their audience based on their interests, demographics, or behaviors.
- Network analysis is the process of studying the structure and dynamics of social networks, such as the relationships, interactions, and influence among users, groups, or communities. It can help businesses to identify the most influential or active users, groups, or communities in their industry or niche, as well as to understand how information, opinions, or trends spread across the network.
- Visual analytics is the process of using graphical representations and interactive visualizations to explore, analyze, and communicate data. It can help businesses to gain insights and patterns from large and complex data sets, as well as to present and share their findings in an engaging and intuitive way.
