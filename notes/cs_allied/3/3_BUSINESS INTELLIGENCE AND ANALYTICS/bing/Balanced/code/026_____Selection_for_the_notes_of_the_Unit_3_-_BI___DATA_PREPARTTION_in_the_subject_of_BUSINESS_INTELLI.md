### Selection for the notes of the Unit 3 - BI – DATA PREPARTTION in the subject of BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE AND ANALYTICS KCS

- Data preparation is the process of gathering, combining, structuring and organizing data so it can be used in business intelligence (BI), analytics and data visualization applications.
- Data preparation is a very important part of BI, as it ensures that the data is ready for analysis and provides accurate and actionable insights for intelligent business decisions .
- The steps of data preparation include :
  - Access: Identify and access various sources of business data, such as databases, files, web services, etc.
  - Ingest: Introduce the data into the analysis tools, such as BI platforms, data warehouses, data lakes, etc.
  - Cleanse: Remove or correct any errors, inconsistencies, duplicates, outliers, or missing values in the data set.
  - Format: Standardize the data types, formats, units, scales, and conventions of the data set.
  - Transform: Apply any calculations, aggregations, joins, filters, or other operations to modify or enrich the data set.
  - Integrate: Combine data from different sources or formats into a unified and consistent data set.
  - Load: Store the prepared data set in a suitable location or format for further analysis or visualization.
- Data preparation can be done manually or with the help of data preparation tools, which can automate some or all of the steps and provide features such as data profiling, data quality, data lineage, data catalog, etc.
- Data preparation can improve the efficiency, effectiveness, and quality of BI, analytics, and data visualization, as it can reduce the time and effort required for data analysis, enhance the reliability and validity of the data, and enable more comprehensive and insightful data exploration .
