### Data Mart

- A data mart is a **subset** of a data warehouse that focuses on a **particular subject area**, such as a department, a business function, or a topic    .
- A data mart is designed for use by a **specific group of users** who need to access, analyze, and understand the data relevant to their needs    .
- A data mart is a **relational database** that stores transactional data in rows and columns, which makes it easy to organize, query, and manipulate .
- A data mart contains **historical data** that can be used to identify patterns, trends, and insights for decision making .
- A data mart can be **independent** or **dependent** on a data warehouse. An independent data mart is created directly from source systems, while a dependent data mart is derived from a data warehouse .
- A data mart can also be classified as **top-down** or **bottom-up**. A top-down data mart is built after a data warehouse, while a bottom-up data mart is built before a data warehouse .
- A data mart can have different **architectures**, such as star schema, snowflake schema, or fact constellation.
- A data mart can provide **benefits** such as faster data access, improved data quality, increased user satisfaction, and reduced costs    .
- A data mart can also have some **challenges**, such as data inconsistency, data redundancy, data security, and data governance  .
