### Real Time Business Intelligent System

- A real time business intelligent system is a system that delivers business intelligence or information about business operations as they occur .
- Business intelligence is the process of collecting, analyzing, and presenting data to support decision making and improve business performance.
- A real time business intelligent system aims to provide near to zero latency and access to information whenever it is required .
- A real time business intelligent system can use different architectures and techniques to enable real time data analysis, such as:
  - Event-based: This approach uses event driven systems that may use complex event processing, event stream processing, and mashup techniques to analyze events without storing them in a database.
  - Data warehouse: This approach uses a data warehouse that is refreshed frequently or continuously to store and query data from various sources.
  - Server-less technology: This approach uses cloud-based services and platforms that can scale and process data on demand without requiring servers or infrastructure.
- A real time business intelligent system can provide various benefits for organizations, such as:
  - Faster and more accurate decision making: By providing timely and relevant information, a real time business intelligent system can help organizations make better decisions and respond to changing situations.
  - Improved customer satisfaction: By providing personalized and customized services, a real time business intelligent system can help organizations meet customer needs and expectations.
  - Enhanced operational efficiency: By optimizing processes and resources, a real time business intelligent system can help organizations reduce costs and increase productivity.
  - Competitive advantage: By gaining insights and opportunities, a real time business intelligent system can help organizations innovate and differentiate themselves from competitors.
