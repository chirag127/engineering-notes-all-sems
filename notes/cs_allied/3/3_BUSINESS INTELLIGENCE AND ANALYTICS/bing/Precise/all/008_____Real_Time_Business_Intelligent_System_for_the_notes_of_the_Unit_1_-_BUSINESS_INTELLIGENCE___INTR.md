# Real Time Business Intelligent System

Real Time Business Intelligence (RTBI) is a system that provides real-time information to decision-makers. It is a type of business intelligence that allows for the analysis of data as soon as it becomes available. This enables businesses to make decisions based on the most up-to-date information possible.

Some key features of RTBI systems include:

1. **Data Integration:** RTBI systems integrate data from multiple sources, including internal systems and external data feeds. This allows for a comprehensive view of the business and its operations.

2. **Real-Time Analytics:** RTBI systems provide real-time analytics, allowing decision-makers to analyze data as soon as it becomes available. This enables businesses to make decisions based on the most up-to-date information possible.

3. **Dashboards and Visualization:** RTBI systems often include dashboards and visualization tools that allow decision-makers to view data in a graphical format. This makes it easier to identify trends and patterns in the data.

4. **Alerts and Notifications:** RTBI systems can be configured to send alerts and notifications when certain conditions are met. This allows decision-makers to take immediate action when necessary.

RTBI systems can provide significant benefits to businesses, including improved decision-making, increased efficiency, and enhanced competitiveness. By providing real-time information to decision-makers, RTBI systems enable businesses to make informed decisions and respond quickly to changing conditions.
