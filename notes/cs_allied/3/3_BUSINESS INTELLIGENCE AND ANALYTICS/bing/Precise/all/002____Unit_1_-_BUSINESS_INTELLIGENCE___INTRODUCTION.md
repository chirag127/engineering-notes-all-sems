## Unit 1 - BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE – INTRODUCTION

Business Intelligence (BI) is a set of techniques and tools for the acquisition and transformation of raw data into meaningful and useful information for business analysis purposes. BI technologies are capable of handling large amounts of structured and sometimes unstructured data to help identify, develop and otherwise create new strategic business opportunities.

The goal of BI is to allow for the easy interpretation of these large volumes of data. Identifying new opportunities and implementing an effective strategy based on insights can provide businesses with a competitive market advantage and long-term stability.

Some of the key benefits of BI include:
- Improved decision making: By providing accurate and up-to-date information, BI can help decision-makers make more informed choices.
- Increased efficiency: BI can help automate and streamline business processes, leading to increased efficiency and productivity.
- Better understanding of customer behavior: BI can help businesses better understand their customers' needs and preferences, allowing them to tailor their products and services accordingly.
- Identification of new business opportunities: By analyzing data, BI can help businesses identify new opportunities for growth and expansion.

BI technologies include data warehouses, data mining, online analytical processing (OLAP), dashboards, and reporting tools. These technologies can be used to collect, integrate, analyze, and present business information in a way that is easy to understand and act upon.

In summary, BI is a powerful tool that can help businesses make better decisions, improve efficiency, and identify new opportunities for growth. It is an essential component of modern business strategy and is widely used by companies of all sizes and industries.
