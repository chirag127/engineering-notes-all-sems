### Introduction to analytics process

The analytics process is a series of steps that are taken to transform raw data into meaningful insights that can be used to make informed decisions. The process typically involves the following steps:

1. **Data collection:** This involves gathering data from various sources, such as databases, spreadsheets, and external data sources. The data collected should be relevant to the problem or question being addressed.

2. **Data preparation:** This involves cleaning, transforming, and organizing the data so that it is ready for analysis. This may include removing outliers, dealing with missing values, and converting data into a usable format.

3. **Data analysis:** This involves using statistical and mathematical techniques to identify patterns and relationships in the data. This may include techniques such as regression analysis, clustering, and predictive modeling.

4. **Data visualization:** This involves presenting the results of the analysis in a visual format, such as charts, graphs, and dashboards. This helps to communicate the results to others and to make the insights more easily understandable.

5. **Decision making:** This involves using the insights gained from the analysis to make informed decisions. This may involve making changes to business processes, developing new products, or identifying new opportunities.

The analytics process is an iterative one, where the results of the analysis may lead to new questions and further analysis. It is an important part of business intelligence and analytics, and can help organizations to make data-driven decisions.
