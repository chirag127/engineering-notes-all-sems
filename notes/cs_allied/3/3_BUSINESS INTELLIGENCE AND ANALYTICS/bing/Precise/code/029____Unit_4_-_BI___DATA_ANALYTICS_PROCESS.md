## Unit 4 - BI – DATA ANALYTICS PROCESS

Business Intelligence (BI) is the process of transforming raw data into meaningful and actionable information. The data analytics process is a key component of BI, which involves the collection, integration, analysis, and presentation of business information.

The data analytics process can be broken down into the following steps:

1. **Data Collection:** This involves gathering data from various sources, such as databases, spreadsheets, and external data sources. The data collected should be relevant to the business problem or question being addressed.

2. **Data Integration:** This involves combining data from multiple sources to create a unified view of the data. This step is important for ensuring that the data is consistent and accurate.

3. **Data Analysis:** This involves using statistical and mathematical techniques to identify patterns and relationships in the data. This step is crucial for generating insights and making informed decisions.

4. **Data Presentation:** This involves presenting the results of the data analysis in a clear and concise manner, using visualizations and reports. This step is important for communicating the insights and findings to decision-makers.

Overall, the data analytics process is an iterative process, where the results of the analysis are used to refine the data collection and integration steps, leading to more accurate and meaningful insights. It is a crucial component of BI, enabling businesses to make data-driven decisions and gain a competitive advantage.
