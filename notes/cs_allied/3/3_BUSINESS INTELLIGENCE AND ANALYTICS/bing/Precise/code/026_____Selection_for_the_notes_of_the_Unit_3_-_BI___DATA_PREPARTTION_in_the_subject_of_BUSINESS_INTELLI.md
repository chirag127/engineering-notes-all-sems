### Selection for the notes of the Unit 3 - BI – DATA PREPARTTION in the subject of BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE AND ANALYTICS KCS

1. Data preparation is the process of cleaning, transforming, and organizing raw data into a format suitable for analysis.
2. This process is critical for the success of any business intelligence and analytics project, as the quality of the data used for analysis directly impacts the accuracy and reliability of the insights generated.
3. Common data preparation tasks include data profiling, data cleansing, data transformation, and data integration.
4. Data profiling involves examining the data to identify potential issues such as missing or inconsistent values, and to understand the structure and relationships within the data.
5. Data cleansing involves correcting or removing inaccurate, incomplete, or irrelevant data.
6. Data transformation involves converting the data into a format suitable for analysis, such as aggregating, normalizing, or encoding the data.
7. Data integration involves combining data from multiple sources to create a unified view of the data.
8. Effective data preparation requires a thorough understanding of the data, the business context, and the analytical goals of the project.
9. Data preparation tools and techniques can help automate and streamline the data preparation process, but human expertise and judgment are still essential to ensure the quality and accuracy of the data.
