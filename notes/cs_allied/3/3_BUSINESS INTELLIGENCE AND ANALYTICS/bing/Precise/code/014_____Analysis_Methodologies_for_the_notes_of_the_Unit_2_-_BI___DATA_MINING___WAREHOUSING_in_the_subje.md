### Analysis Methodologies for the notes of the Unit 2 - BI – DATA MINING & WAREHOUSING in the subject of BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE AND ANALYTICS KCS

1. **Data Mining**: This is the process of discovering patterns and knowledge from large amounts of data. The data sources can include databases, data warehouses, the internet, and other sources.

2. **Association Rule Learning**: This is a method for discovering interesting relations between variables in large databases. It is intended to identify strong rules discovered in databases using different measures of interestingness.

3. **Cluster Analysis**: This is the task of grouping a set of objects in such a way that objects in the same group (called a cluster) are more similar to each other than to those in other groups (clusters).

4. **Classification**: This is the problem of identifying to which of a set of categories (subpopulations) a new observation belongs, on the basis of a training set of data containing observations (or instances) whose category membership is known.

5. **Regression Analysis**: This is a statistical process for estimating the relationships among variables. It includes many techniques for modeling and analyzing several variables, when the focus is on the relationship between a dependent variable and one or more independent variables.

6. **Anomaly Detection**: This is the identification of rare items, events or observations which raise suspicions by differing significantly from the majority of the data.

7. **Neural Networks**: These are a set of algorithms, modeled loosely after the human brain, that are designed to recognize patterns. They interpret sensory data through a kind of machine perception, labeling or clustering raw input.

8. **Decision Trees**: This is a decision support tool that uses a tree-like model of decisions and their possible consequences, including chance event outcomes, resource costs, and utility.

9. **Genetic Algorithms**: These are adaptive heuristic search algorithms based on the evolutionary ideas of natural selection and genetics. They are used to find approximate solutions to difficult optimization and search problems.

10. **Machine Learning**: This is a method of teaching computers to learn from data, without being explicitly programmed. It is a type of artificial intelligence that allows software applications to become more accurate in predicting outcomes without being explicitly programmed to do so.
