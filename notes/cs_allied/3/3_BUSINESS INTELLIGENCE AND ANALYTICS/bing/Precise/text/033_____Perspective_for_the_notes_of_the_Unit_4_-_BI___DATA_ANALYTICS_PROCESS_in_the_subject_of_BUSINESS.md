### Perspective for the notes of the Unit 4 - BI – DATA ANALYTICS PROCESS in the subject of BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE AND ANALYTICS KCS

1. **Perspective** refers to the way in which data is viewed and analyzed.
2. It is important to consider different perspectives when analyzing data in order to gain a more complete understanding of the information.
3. Different stakeholders may have different perspectives on the same data, and it is important to consider all of these perspectives in order to make informed decisions.
4. In the context of Business Intelligence and Analytics, perspective can refer to the way in which data is visualized, the level of detail at which it is analyzed, and the specific questions that are asked of the data.
5. Considering multiple perspectives can help to identify trends, patterns, and relationships in the data that may not be immediately apparent from a single perspective.
6. It is important to be aware of any biases or assumptions that may influence one's perspective, and to actively seek out and consider alternative perspectives in order to ensure that the analysis is as objective and comprehensive as possible.
