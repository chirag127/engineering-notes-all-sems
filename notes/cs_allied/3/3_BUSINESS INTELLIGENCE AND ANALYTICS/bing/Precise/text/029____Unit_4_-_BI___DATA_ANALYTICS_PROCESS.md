## Unit 4 - BI – DATA ANALYTICS PROCESS

1. **Business Understanding**: The first step in the data analytics process is to understand the business problem that needs to be solved. This involves identifying the key stakeholders, defining the objectives, and determining the scope of the analysis.

2. **Data Understanding**: The next step is to understand the data that is available to solve the business problem. This involves collecting, cleaning, and exploring the data to identify any patterns or relationships that may exist.

3. **Data Preparation**: Once the data is understood, it needs to be prepared for analysis. This involves transforming, integrating, and cleaning the data to ensure that it is in the right format for analysis.

4. **Modeling**: The next step is to build a model that can be used to analyze the data. This involves selecting the appropriate modeling technique, building the model, and validating it to ensure that it is accurate.

5. **Evaluation**: Once the model has been built, it needs to be evaluated to determine its effectiveness. This involves comparing the results of the model to the business objectives to determine if the model is providing the desired insights.

6. **Deployment**: The final step in the data analytics process is to deploy the model. This involves integrating the model into the business processes and using it to make data-driven decisions.
