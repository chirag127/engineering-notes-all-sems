### Architectural Representation

Architectural representation is the graphical representation of an architectural design, typically using drawings, diagrams, and models. It is an essential tool for architects, engineers, and designers to communicate their ideas and designs to clients, contractors, and other stakeholders.

1. Architectural drawings: These are technical drawings that depict the design of a building or structure. They can include floor plans, elevations, sections, and details.
2. Diagrams: These are simplified representations of a design, often used to convey specific information or to illustrate a concept or process.
3. Models: These are physical or digital representations of a design, often used to help visualize the final product and to test different design options.

Architectural representation is an important part of the design process, as it allows architects and designers to explore and communicate their ideas, and to receive feedback from clients and other stakeholders. It is also essential for the construction process, as it provides the necessary information for contractors to build the structure according to the design.
