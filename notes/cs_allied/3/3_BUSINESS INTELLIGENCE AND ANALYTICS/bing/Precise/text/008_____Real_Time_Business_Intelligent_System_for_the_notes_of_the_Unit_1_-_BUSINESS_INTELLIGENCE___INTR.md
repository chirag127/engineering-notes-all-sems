### Real Time Business Intelligent System

Real-time business intelligence (BI) is the process of delivering information and analysis in real-time or near real-time. This allows businesses to make decisions and take actions based on the most up-to-date information. Real-time BI can be achieved through a variety of methods, including:

1. **Data Warehousing:** A data warehouse is a large, centralized repository of data that is used to support business intelligence and decision-making activities. Data is extracted from various sources, transformed to fit operational needs, and loaded into the data warehouse for analysis.

2. **Data Integration:** Data integration involves combining data from different sources into a single, unified view. This can be achieved through various methods, including extract, transform, and load (ETL) processes, enterprise information integration (EII), and enterprise application integration (EAI).

3. **Data Analysis:** Data analysis involves examining, cleaning, transforming, and modeling data to discover useful information and support decision-making. This can be achieved through various methods, including data mining, statistical analysis, and predictive modeling.

4. **Dashboards and Visualization:** Dashboards and visualization tools allow users to interact with data and view it in a graphical format. This can help users to quickly identify trends, patterns, and outliers in the data.

Real-time BI can provide many benefits to businesses, including improved decision-making, increased agility, and enhanced competitiveness. However, it can also present challenges, such as the need for high-quality data, robust infrastructure, and skilled personnel. It is important for businesses to carefully evaluate their needs and capabilities before implementing a real-time BI system.
