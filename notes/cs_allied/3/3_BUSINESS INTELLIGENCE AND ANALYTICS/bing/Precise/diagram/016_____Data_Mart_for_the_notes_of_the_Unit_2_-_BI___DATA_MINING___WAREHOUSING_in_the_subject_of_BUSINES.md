### Data Mart

A data mart is a subset of a data warehouse that is designed to serve a specific business unit or function. It is a subject-oriented database that is optimized for the needs of a particular group of users. Data marts are often used to provide business users with faster and more focused access to the data they need for decision-making.

Some key points to consider when discussing data marts include:

1. Data marts are designed to serve the needs of a specific business unit or function.
2. They are subject-oriented and optimized for the needs of a particular group of users.
3. Data marts provide faster and more focused access to data for decision-making.
4. They are often used in conjunction with a larger data warehouse, but can also be used as standalone solutions.
5. Data marts can be implemented using a variety of technologies, including relational databases, multidimensional databases, and in-memory databases.
