### Online Analytical Processing (OLAP) – Tools for the notes of the Unit 2 - BI – DATA MINING & WAREHOUSING in the subject of BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE AND ANALYTICS KCS

1. OLAP is a category of software tools that allows users to analyze data from multiple dimensions, providing a multidimensional view of data.
2. OLAP tools are used for data mining and data warehousing, which are essential components of business intelligence and analytics.
3. OLAP tools enable users to perform complex calculations, trend analysis, and data modeling, allowing them to gain insights into their data and make informed decisions.
4. Some popular OLAP tools include Microsoft SQL Server Analysis Services, Oracle OLAP, IBM Cognos TM1, and SAP BusinessObjects.
5. OLAP tools are often used in conjunction with data visualization tools, such as Tableau or QlikView, to provide a graphical representation of data.
6. OLAP tools are commonly used in business intelligence applications, such as sales analysis, financial reporting, and inventory management.
7. OLAP tools can be used to analyze large volumes of data, making them useful for big data applications.
8. OLAP tools can be used to create reports, dashboards, and other visualizations, allowing users to easily share their findings with others.
