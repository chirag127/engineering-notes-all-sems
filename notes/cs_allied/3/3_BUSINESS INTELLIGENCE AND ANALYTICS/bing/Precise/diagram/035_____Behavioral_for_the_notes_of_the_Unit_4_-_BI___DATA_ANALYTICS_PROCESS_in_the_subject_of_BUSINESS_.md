### Behavioral

Behavioral analytics is a subset of business analytics that focuses on understanding how consumers behave when interacting with a particular product or service. It is used to identify patterns and trends in consumer behavior, and to develop strategies to improve customer engagement and retention.

Some key points to consider when studying behavioral analytics as part of the BI – Data Analytics Process in the subject of Business Intelligence and Analytics KCS are:

1. Behavioral analytics involves the collection and analysis of data on consumer behavior, including their actions, preferences, and interactions with a product or service.

2. This data can be collected through various means, including web analytics, social media analytics, and customer relationship management (CRM) systems.

3. The data is then analyzed using advanced statistical and machine learning techniques to identify patterns and trends in consumer behavior.

4. These insights can be used to develop targeted marketing campaigns, improve customer engagement, and increase customer retention.

5. Behavioral analytics is an important tool for businesses looking to gain a competitive advantage by better understanding their customers and their needs.

6. It is important to ensure that the data collected is accurate and representative of the target population, and that appropriate privacy and data protection measures are in place.
