### Perspective for the notes of the Unit 4 - BI – DATA ANALYTICS PROCESS in the subject of BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE AND ANALYTICS KCS

The perspective for the Unit 4 - BI - Data Analytics Process in the subject of Business Intelligence and Analytics (KCS) is focused on the importance of understanding and implementing a structured process for data analytics. This process is essential for organizations to effectively leverage the vast amounts of data they collect and turn it into actionable insights that drive business decisions.

The data analytics process typically involves the following steps:

1. Data collection: The first step in the data analytics process is to collect data from various sources, such as databases, spreadsheets, and web-based applications.

2. Data preparation: Once the data is collected, it must be cleaned, transformed, and integrated to ensure it is ready for analysis. This step involves removing any irrelevant or duplicate data, transforming data into the appropriate format, and integrating data from multiple sources.

3. Data exploration: In this step, the data is analyzed to identify patterns, relationships, and anomalies. This is typically done using various data visualization and statistical techniques.

4. Data modeling: In this step, a mathematical model is developed to represent the relationships and patterns identified in the data. This model can then be used to make predictions and test hypotheses.

5. Data evaluation: In this step, the model is evaluated to determine its accuracy and effectiveness. This involves comparing the predictions made by the model with actual results.

6. Data deployment: The final step in the data analytics process is to deploy the model and make it available for use by decision-makers. This may involve integrating the model into existing business processes or creating new applications to support decision-making.

In conclusion, the perspective for the Unit 4 - BI - Data Analytics Process in the subject of Business Intelligence and Analytics (KCS) is focused on the importance of understanding and implementing a structured process for data analytics. This process is essential for organizations to effectively leverage the vast amounts of data they collect and turn it into actionable insights that drive business decisions.
