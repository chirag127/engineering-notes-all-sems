### Effective and Timely decisions for the notes of the Unit 1 - BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE – INTRODUCTION in the subject of BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE AND ANALYTICS KCS

Effective Decision Making:
1. Identify the problem or opportunity
2. Gather and analyze data
3. Develop and evaluate alternatives
4. Choose the best alternative
5. Implement the decision
6. Monitor and review the outcome

Timely Decision Making:
1. Prioritize and set deadlines for decision making
2. Use technology to automate and streamline data collection and analysis
3. Encourage open communication and collaboration among stakeholders
4. Continuously monitor and update information to stay current
5. Stay flexible and be prepared to adjust decisions as new information becomes available.
