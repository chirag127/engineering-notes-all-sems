### Difference between OLAP and OLTP for the notes of the Unit 2 - BI – DATA MINING & WAREHOUSING in the subject of BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE AND ANALYTICS KCS

OLAP (Online Analytical Processing) and OLTP (Online Transaction Processing) are two types of database systems used in data warehousing and business intelligence.

OLAP:
1. Designed for complex, multi-dimensional analysis of large data sets
2. Supports ad-hoc querying and analysis of data
3. Uses multi-dimensional data structures, such as cubes, to enable fast data retrieval
4. Typically used for business intelligence, data mining, and decision support systems

OLTP:
1. Designed for fast transaction processing and data recording
2. Uses a relational database structure
3. Optimized for quick data retrieval and data updates
4. Typically used for day-to-day business operations and transaction processing.
