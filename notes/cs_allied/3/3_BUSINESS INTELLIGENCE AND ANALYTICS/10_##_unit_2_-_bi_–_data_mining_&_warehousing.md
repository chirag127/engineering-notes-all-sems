## Unit 2 - BI – DATA MINING & WAREHOUSING

Unit 2 of the subject "Mathematical Foundation AI, ML, and Data Science" focuses on Business Intelligence (BI) and Data Mining & Warehousing.

Business Intelligence (BI) is a set of technologies, processes, and practices used to analyze and transform data into meaningful information that can be used to support decision-making. BI enables organizations to access and analyze large amounts of data from various sources, such as databases, data warehouses, and data marts, to gain insights into their business operations and performance.

Data Mining & Warehousing are two critical components of BI. Data Mining is the process of discovering patterns and relationships in large datasets to identify meaningful information. It involves the use of algorithms and statistical models to extract insights from data. Data Warehousing is the process of storing and managing large amounts of data in a centralized repository, such as a data warehouse, to support data analysis and decision-making.

In this unit, you will learn about the different data mining techniques, such as association rule mining, clustering, and classification, and how they can be used to extract insights from data. You will also learn about data warehousing concepts, such as data integration, data cleaning, and data transformation.

Additionally, you will learn about the architecture of data warehouses and how they can be used to support data analysis and decision-making. You will also learn about the different types of data warehouses, such as operational data stores, data marts, and enterprise data warehouses, and how they can be used to support different BI needs and requirements.

In summary, Unit 2 of the subject "Mathematical Foundation AI, ML, and Data Science" provides an introduction to Business Intelligence (BI) and Data Mining & Warehousing, and covers the key concepts and techniques used to support data analysis and decision-making in organizations.
