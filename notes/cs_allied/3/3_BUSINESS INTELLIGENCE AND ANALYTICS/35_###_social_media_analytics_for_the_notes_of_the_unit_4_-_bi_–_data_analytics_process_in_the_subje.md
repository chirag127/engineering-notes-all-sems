### Social Media Analytics for the notes of the Unit 4 - BI – DATA ANALYTICS PROCESS in the subject of BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE AND ANALYTICS KCS

Social media analytics is the process of collecting, analyzing, and interpreting data from social media platforms to gain insights and inform business decisions. Social media analytics can be used to track and measure the impact of social media marketing campaigns, monitor brand reputation, and gain insights into consumer behavior and preferences.

Examples of social media analytics techniques include:

1. Sentiment analysis: The process of determining the emotional tone of a piece of text, such as a social media post, to determine whether it is positive, negative, or neutral.

2. Influencer analysis: The process of identifying individuals who have a large following on social media and who can influence the opinions and actions of others.

3. Trend analysis: The process of identifying and tracking trends and patterns in social media data, such as the popularity of certain topics or hashtags.

4. Engagement analysis: The process of measuring the level of engagement with a brand or product on social media, such as the number of likes, comments, and shares.

5. Demographic analysis: The process of analyzing social media data to gain insights into the demographics of a brand’s audience, such as age, gender, and location.

In conclusion, social media analytics is a crucial aspect of business intelligence and analytics, providing valuable insights into consumer behavior and preferences, and helping to inform business decisions. Understanding social media analytics is important for businesses looking to effectively leverage social media platforms to reach and engage with their target audience.
