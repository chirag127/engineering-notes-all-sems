### Data Transformation – Standardization and Feature Extraction

Data transformation is a crucial step in data preparation for business intelligence (BI) and analytics. It involves converting raw data into a format that is suitable for analysis. Two important techniques used in data transformation are standardization and feature extraction.

#### Standardization

Standardization is the process of transforming data so that it has a mean of zero and a standard deviation of one. This technique is useful when working with data that have different scales or units of measurement. Standardization ensures that each variable has the same weight in the analysis, making it easier to compare and interpret the results.

The steps for standardization are as follows:

1. Calculate the mean and standard deviation of the data.
2. Subtract the mean from each value in the data.
3. Divide each value by the standard deviation.

#### Feature Extraction

Feature extraction is the process of identifying and extracting important information from raw data. It involves selecting a subset of the original variables or creating new variables based on patterns or relationships in the data. Feature extraction is useful when working with large datasets or when there are many variables that may not all be relevant to the analysis.

The steps for feature extraction are as follows:

1. Identify the variables that are relevant to the analysis.
2. Use statistical techniques such as principal component analysis (PCA) or factor analysis to identify patterns or relationships in the data.
3. Create new variables based on the patterns or relationships identified in step 2.

#### Conclusion

Data transformation is an important step in data preparation for BI and analytics. Standardization and feature extraction are two techniques that can help to ensure that the data is suitable for analysis. By standardizing the data, each variable has the same weight in the analysis, making it easier to compare and interpret the results. Feature extraction allows for the identification of important information in large datasets or when there are many variables that may not all be relevant to the analysis.
