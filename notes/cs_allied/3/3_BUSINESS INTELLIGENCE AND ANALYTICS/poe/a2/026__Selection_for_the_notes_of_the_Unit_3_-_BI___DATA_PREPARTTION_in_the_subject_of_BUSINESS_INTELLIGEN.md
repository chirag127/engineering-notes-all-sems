### Selection for the notes of the Unit 3 - BI – DATA PREPARTTION in the subject of BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE AND ANALYTICS KCS

1. Data Cleaning:
- Removing duplicate records
- Handling missing values
- Consistency checks (name formats, zip codes, etc.)
- Standardization (units, formats, etc.)
- Outlier treatment

2. Data Integration:
- Combining data from multiple sources
- Resolving inconsistencies across data sources
- Matching records that refer to the same entity
- Data transformation to match a common schema

3. Data Transformation:
- Data type conversion (e.g. strings to dates)
- Data formatting (e.g. date formats)
- Deriving calculated fields
- Aggregation (roll-up, drill-down)
- Sorting and ordering

4. Data Reduction:
- Data compression
- Sampling
- Aggregation
- Dimensionality reduction

The above points cover the key aspects of data preparation required before any analysis or modeling can be performed on the data. The prepared data needs to be of high quality and in the appropriate format as per the subsequent requirements.
