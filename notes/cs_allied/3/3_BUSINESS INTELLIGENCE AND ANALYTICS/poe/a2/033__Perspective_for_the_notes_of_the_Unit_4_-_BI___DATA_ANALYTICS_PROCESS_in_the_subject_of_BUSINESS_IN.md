### Perspective for the notes of the Unit 4 - BI – DATA ANALYTICS PROCESS in the subject of BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE AND ANALYTICS KCS

1. Data Analytics Process
- Business Understanding: Understanding the business problem and requirements. Identifying the business objectives and constraints.
- Data Understanding: Understanding the data sources and data quality. Exploring the data to identify patterns and insights.
- Data Preparation: Cleaning and transforming the data.Handling missing values and inconsistent data.
- Modeling: Applying various machine learning algorithms to train and evaluate models.
- Evaluation: Evaluating the performance of the models and selecting the best model.
- Deployment: Deploying the best model and monitoring its performance.

2. Data Mining Process
- Selection: Selecting the data mining task and data mining tools.
- Pre-processing: Preparing the data by cleaning and transforming it.
- Data Mining: Applying data mining algorithms to extract patterns and insights.
- Post-processing: Interpreting and evaluating the results. Applying the results to the business problem.

3. Types of Data Mining Tasks
- Classification: Predicting the category/class of a data instance. Ex: Predicting if a customer will churn.
- Regression: Predicting a continuous value. Ex: Predicting the revenue.
- Clustering: Grouping similar data instances. Ex: Segmenting customers with similar characteristics.
- Association Rule Learning: Finding relationships between variables. Ex: Finding products frequently bought together.
- Anomaly Detection: Identifying unusual patterns in the data. Ex: Detecting fraudulent transactions.
