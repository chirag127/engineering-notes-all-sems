### Effective and Timely decisions for the notes of the Unit 1 - BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE – INTRODUCTION in the subject of BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE AND ANALYTICS KCS.

- Business Intelligence is the process of analyzing data and turning it into useful information that helps businesses make informed decisions.
- The goal of Business Intelligence is to allow organizations to measure, monitor and manage the key metrics and KPIs for their business and make data-driven decisions based on actual insights instead of gut feelings or instincts.
- Timely and accurate information is essential for effective decision making in an organization. BI tools help businesses get the right information to the right people at the right time.
- Some of the key decisions that can be made efficiently using BI include:
-- Identifying new opportunities and growth areas
-- Optimizing internal business processes
-- Reducing costs and inefficiencies
-- Monitoring progress and performance against goals
-- Gaining a competitive advantage by responding faster to market changes
- The components of a typical BI solution are:
-- Data sources - internal and external databases with data
-- Data warehouse - combines and stores data from multiple sources
-- BI tools - for data analysis, reporting, visualization, etc.
-- Users - employees, managers, executives accessing the information
