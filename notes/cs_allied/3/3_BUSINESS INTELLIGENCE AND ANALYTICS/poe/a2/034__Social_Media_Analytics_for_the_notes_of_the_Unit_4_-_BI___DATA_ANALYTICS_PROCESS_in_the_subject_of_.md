### Social Media Analytics

- Social media analytics is the process of gathering data from social media sites and analyzing that data to make business decisions.
- Some of the common social media sites are Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, LinkedIn, etc.
- The data available on these sites include likes, comments, shares, followers, posts, etc. which can be analyzed to understand user sentiment, engagement, reach, influencers, etc.
- The analysis of social media data helps businesses to understand their audience better, identify key influencers, optimize their social media marketing campaigns, launch new products, etc.
- Some of the common metrics used in social media analytics are:
-- Impressions: Number of times posts were displayed.
-- Reach: Number of unique users who saw the post.
-- Engagement: Likes, comments, shares, etc. on posts.
-- Sentiment: Analyzing user opinions as positive, negative or neutral.
-- Influencers: Identifying users with most reach or engagement.
- There are many tools available to extract and analyze data from social media like Hootsuite, Sprout Social, AgoraPulse, etc.
- Social media analytics requires data extraction, data cleaning, data analysis, and data visualization to derive meaningful insights and decisions.
