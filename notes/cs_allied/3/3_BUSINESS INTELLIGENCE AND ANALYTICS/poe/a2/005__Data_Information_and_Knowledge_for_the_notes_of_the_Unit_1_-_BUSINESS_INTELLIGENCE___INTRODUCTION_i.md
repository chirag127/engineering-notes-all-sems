### Data, Information, Knowledge

- **Data** refers to raw facts and figures collected or generated from various sources. Data is unstructured and unprocessed. For e.g. Sales numbers, stock prices, employee records, etc.
- **Information** is data that has been organized, structured, and processed in a way that provides context and meaning. Information allows us to make decisions and draw insights. For e.g. Sales reports, charts, graphs, etc.
- **Knowledge** is information combined with experience, context, interpretation, and reflection. It is about understanding and applying information and data to address problems or make decisions. For e.g. Understanding sales trends and patterns.

To summarize:

Data -> Information -> Knowledge

Raw facts -> Organized and processed data -> Understanding and application

The goal of Business Intelligence is to convert data into information and information into knowledge to drive better business decisions.
