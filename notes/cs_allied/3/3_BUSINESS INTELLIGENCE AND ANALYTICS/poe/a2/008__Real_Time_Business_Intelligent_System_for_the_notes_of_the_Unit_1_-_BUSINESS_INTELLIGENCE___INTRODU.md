### Real Time Business Intelligent System

1. Business Intelligence (BI) is the process of converting raw data into meaningful information that can be used to make informed business decisions.

2. Real-time BI is a system that delivers information on operational performance within minutes or seconds after an event. This real-time data delivery enables businesses to react faster to business events.

3. The key components of a Real-Time BI system are:

    - Data sources: Data can come from operational systems, databases, sensors, etc. The data must be delivered in real-time to the BI system.
    - ETL processes: The data is extracted from sources, transformed and loaded into a data warehouse/lake for further processing. These ETL processes must function with minimal latency to enable real-time analytics.
    - Analytical models: Analytical models are applied on the data to get insights and metrics. These models need to function efficiently to generate outputs from the data in real-time.
    - Data visualization: The insights from the analytical models are visualized through dashboards or reports. The visualizations must refresh at a rapid pace to reflect the real-time nature of the data and metrics.

4. The benefits of a Real-Time BI system are:

    - Fast decision making: Getting data in real-time enables businesses to detect opportunities and issues quickly and make time-critical decisions.
    - Operational efficiency: Real-time visibility into key metrics can help identify inefficiencies and take corrective actions promptly.
    - Better customer experience: Understanding customer behavior and reactions in real-time can enable better engagement and personalized experiences.
