### Architectural Representation for the notes of the Unit 1 - BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE – INTRODUCTION in the subject of BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE AND ANALYTICS KCS.

- BI architecture consists of multiple layers and components for collecting and analysing data and representing information.
- The major layers of a typical BI architecture are:
- Data sources layer - Consists of internal and external data sources.
- Data integration layer - Extracts, transforms and loads data from sources into a data warehouse.
- Data warehouse layer - Integrated, subject-oriented, time-variant and non-volatile collection of data.
- Business analytics layer - BI tools and applications for reporting, online analytical processing, data mining, etc.
- Interface layer - User interfaces to interact with the BI system and visualize information.
- The components of a BI system are:
- ETL tools - Extract, transform and load data.
- Metadata repository - Stores metadata about the data sources and data warehouse.
- OLAP server - Enables multidimensional analysis of data.
- Reporting and visualization tools - Used to create reports and dashboards.
- Query and analysis tools - Used to analyse data and find insights.
- The major benefits of implementing a BI system are:
- Provide an integrated view of data from multiple sources.
- Enable data-driven decision making through analysis and reporting.
- Identify opportunities and trends by exploring data.
- Present data in a visually insightful manner through reports and dashboards.
