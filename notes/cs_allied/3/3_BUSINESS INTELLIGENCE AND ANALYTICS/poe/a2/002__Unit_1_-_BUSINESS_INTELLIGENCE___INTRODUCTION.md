## Unit 1 - BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE – INTRODUCTION

1. Business Intelligence (BI) is the process of collecting, storing, and analyzing data to help in business decision-making. The ultimate goal of BI is to help companies make smarter business decisions by providing meaningful and actionable insights.
2. The key steps in the BI process are:
- Data Collection: Collecting data from various sources like databases, data warehouses, external sources, etc.
- Data Storage: Storing the collected data in a centralized data repository called a data warehouse.
- Data Analysis: Analyzing the data using data mining and data visualization techniques to discover useful information and insights.
- Action: Taking action or making decisions based on the insights gained from the analysis.
3. The key technologies used in BI are:
- ETL (Extract, Transform, Load): For collecting and storing data from multiple sources.
- Data Warehousing: For storing integrated data.
- Data Mining: For analyzing data and discovering patterns.
- Data Visualization: For data representation in a graphical format.
4. The key benefits of BI are:
- Helps in better decision making through data insights.
- Identifies new opportunities and ways to improve operational efficiencies.
- Increases organizational profitability and competitiveness.
- Provides a holistic view of the business.
- Enables data-driven culture in the organization.
