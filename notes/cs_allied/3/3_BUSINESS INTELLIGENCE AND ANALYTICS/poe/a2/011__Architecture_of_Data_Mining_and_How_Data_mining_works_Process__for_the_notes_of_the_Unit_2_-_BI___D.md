### Architecture of Data Mining and How Data mining works(Process)

1. Data Collection: The first step is to collect the data from various sources like databases, data warehouses, etc. The data can be structured, semi-structured or unstructured.

2. Data Preparation: The collected data is then prepared for mining by following methods:
- Data Cleaning: Removing noise and inconsistent data.
- Data Integration: Integrating data from multiple sources.
- Data Transformation: Converting the data into appropriate formats and Data Reduction techniques like aggregation and filtering.
- Data discretization and normalization: Converting continuous data into intervals for analysis.

3. Data Mining: Appropriate data mining techniques are applied on the prepared data to extract knowledge and patterns. Some of the popular techniques are:
- Classification: To predict a class or category.
- Regression: To predict a value.
- Clustering: To find hidden patterns or clusters in data.
- Association Rule Learning: To find relationships between variables.
- Anomaly Detection: To detect anomalies in data.

4. Pattern Evaluation: The extracted patterns are evaluated for their interestingness and usefulness. The criteria for evaluation include:
- Comprehensibility: How easy is it to understand the pattern?
- Interestingness: How useful or novel is the pattern?
- Actionability: Can the knowledge be acted upon?
- Certainty: How confident can we be in the extracted pattern?

5. Knowledge Presentation: The evaluated, interesting patterns are represented in an understandable format to the user. The presentation can be in the form of graphs, charts, rules, stories, etc.
