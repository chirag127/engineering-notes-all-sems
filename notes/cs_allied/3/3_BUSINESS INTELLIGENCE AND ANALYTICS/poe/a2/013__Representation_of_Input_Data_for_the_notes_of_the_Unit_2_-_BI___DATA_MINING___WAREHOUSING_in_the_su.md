### Representation of Input Data for the notes of the Unit 2 - BI – DATA MINING & WAREHOUSING in the subject of BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE AND ANALYTICS KCS.

-   Raw data: The data collected directly from various source systems without any alteration or processing is known as raw data. Raw data is messy, inconsistent and not suitable for analysis.
-   Processed data: The raw data is then cleaned, integrated and transformed into a suitable format for analysis. This processed data is structured and consistent which is suitable for analysis.
-   Information: The processed data is then analyzed and interpreted to derive insight and meaning from it. This derived insight or meaning from the data is known as information.
-   Knowledge: The information is then combined with experiences and expertise to make decisions and judgements. This combination of information, experiences and expertise is known as knowledge.

The key steps involved in the flow of data to knowledge are:

1. Collection of raw data
2. Data processing
3. Data analysis
4. Information derivation
5. Knowledge formation

The representation of data at each step can be in various forms like numbers, characters, images, audio, video, etc. The appropriate form of representation is chosen based on the nature and purpose of the data. The processed data is typically represented in a structured format like tables, CSV files, etc. suitable for analysis.
