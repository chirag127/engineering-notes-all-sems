### Predictive for the notes of the Unit 4 - BI – DATA ANALYTICS PROCESS in the subject of BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE AND ANALYTICS KCS

1. Data Gathering: Collecting data from various sources like databases, data warehouses, spreadsheets, etc. Required data is identified and extracted.
2. Data Preparation: The collected data is prepared for analysis by cleaning, transforming and formatting it. Errors and inconsistencies are handled.
3. Data Modeling: The prepared data is analyzed and modeled to identify patterns and relationships useful for business decisions. Statistical models and data mining techniques are applied.
4. Data Analysis: The data models are evaluated and analyzed to derive business insights and information like trends, clusters, anomalies, correlations, etc.
5. Interpretation: The analysis results are interpreted to understand the implications and significance of the insights in business context. Decisions and actions are determined.
6. Implementation: The decisions and actions are implemented to achieve business objectives and add value.

The process is iterative and continuous to keep the analysis updated with latest data and business requirements. Data analysis enables fact-based decisions and strategic planning for businesses.
