## Unit 1 - BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE – INTRODUCTION

Business intelligence refers to the processes, technologies, and tools that organizations use to collect, analyze, and present data that can be used to make informed business decisions. Here are some key points to keep in mind when studying business intelligence:

- Business intelligence involves collecting data from various sources, such as internal databases, social media, and customer feedback.
- The collected data is often stored in a data warehouse, which is a central repository of data that can be accessed and analyzed by the organization.
- Business intelligence tools, such as dashboards and scorecards, are used to visualize and present the data in a way that is easy to understand.
- Data analysis techniques, such as data mining and predictive analytics, can be used to identify patterns and trends in the data, which can be used to make informed business decisions.
- Business intelligence can be used to help organizations improve their operations, increase efficiency, and identify new opportunities for growth.
- Business intelligence is not just for large organizations - small and medium-sized businesses can also benefit from the insights and information provided by business intelligence tools and techniques.

In summary, business intelligence is a critical component of modern business operations, and it provides organizations with the insights and information they need to make informed decisions and stay ahead of the competition.
