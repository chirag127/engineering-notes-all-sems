### Perspective for the notes of the Unit 4 - BI – DATA ANALYTICS PROCESS in the subject of BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE AND ANALYTICS KCS

- The BI - Data Analytics Process is a crucial aspect in the field of Business Intelligence and Analytics.
- The process involves various steps that need to be followed to analyze and interpret data to make better business decisions.
- The first step in the process is to identify the business problem that needs to be solved through data analysis.
- The next step is to collect relevant data from various sources such as databases, spreadsheets, and other data sources.
- Once the data is collected, the next step is to clean and preprocess it to remove any errors or inconsistencies in the data.
- The data is then analyzed using various techniques such as statistical analysis, machine learning, and data visualization to identify patterns and trends in the data.
- The insights gained from the analysis are then used to make informed business decisions that can help organizations achieve their goals.
- It is important to note that the BI - Data Analytics Process is an iterative process, which means that the steps need to be repeated until the desired results are achieved.
- Effective communication and collaboration among team members are crucial for the success of the BI - Data Analytics Process.
- To ensure the accuracy and reliability of the analysis, it is important to use appropriate tools and techniques that are relevant to the business problem being solved.
- The BI - Data Analytics Process is a continuous process that requires organizations to keep up with the latest trends and technologies in the field of Business Intelligence and Analytics.
