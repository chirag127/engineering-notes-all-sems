### Introduction - History and Evolution

- Business Intelligence (BI) refers to the technologies, applications, and practices for the collection, integration, analysis, and presentation of business information.
- BI has evolved over the years from simple reporting tools to sophisticated analytical systems that can provide real-time insights into business operations.
- In the early days of BI, reports were generated manually using spreadsheets and databases, and the process was time-consuming and error-prone.
- With the advent of data warehousing, the process of collecting and storing business data became more efficient, and BI tools became more automated.
- The 1990s saw the emergence of online analytical processing (OLAP) tools that allowed users to analyze data from multiple perspectives and dimensions.
- In the 2000s, BI tools became more user-friendly, with the introduction of dashboards, scorecards, and visualization tools.
- The rise of big data in the 2010s led to the development of new BI tools that could handle large volumes of data in real-time.
- Today, BI is an essential part of business operations, with organizations relying on it to make data-driven decisions and gain a competitive edge in the market.
