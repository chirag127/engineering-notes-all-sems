### Types of Analytical Techniques in BI – Descriptive

Business Intelligence (BI) is a process that involves the collection, integration, analysis, and presentation of business information. BI aims to support decision-making and improve the overall performance of an organization. One of the major components of BI is analytics, which refers to the application of statistical and mathematical techniques to extract insights from data. There are different types of analytical techniques in BI, and in this section, we will discuss the descriptive techniques.

Descriptive analytics is a technique that provides a summary of past events or data. It answers the question of what happened in the past and provides insights into trends, patterns, and outliers. The following are the types of descriptive techniques in BI:

1. Reporting: Reporting is a basic form of descriptive analytics that involves collecting and presenting data in a structured format. Reporting provides information on key performance indicators (KPIs) and metrics, such as sales, revenue, and profit. Reports can be generated on a regular basis, such as daily, weekly, or monthly.

2. Data visualization: Data visualization is the graphical representation of data. It provides a visual summary of data that is easy to understand and interpret. Visualization tools, such as charts, graphs, and maps, can be used to display data in a meaningful way.

3. Drill-down analysis: Drill-down analysis is a technique that allows users to explore data in more detail by drilling down from summary to detailed data. For example, a user can start with a report that shows sales by region and then drill down to sales by product, or even by customer.

4. Dashboards: Dashboards are a collection of visualizations and reports that provide an overview of key performance indicators (KPIs) and metrics. They are designed to be easy to read and provide a quick snapshot of performance.

5. Data mining: Data mining is a technique that involves discovering patterns and relationships in data. It is used to identify hidden insights and trends that are not visible through simple reporting or visualization.

In conclusion, descriptive analytics provides a summary of past events or data and helps to identify trends, patterns, and outliers. The types of descriptive techniques in BI include reporting, data visualization, drill-down analysis, dashboards, and data mining. These techniques help organizations to make informed decisions based on historical data.
