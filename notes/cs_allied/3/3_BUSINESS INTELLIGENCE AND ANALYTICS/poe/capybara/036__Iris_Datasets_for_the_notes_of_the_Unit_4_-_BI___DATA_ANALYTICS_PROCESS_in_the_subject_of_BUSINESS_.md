### Iris Datasets

Iris datasets are commonly used in the field of data analytics and machine learning. The iris dataset contains information about the flowers and their respective species. It is a multivariate dataset with four variables: sepal length, sepal width, petal length, and petal width. 

Here are some important points to keep in mind about the iris datasets:

- The dataset contains a total of 150 records, with 50 records for each of the three species of iris flowers: setosa, versicolor, and virginica.
- Each record contains measurements for the four variables: sepal length, sepal width, petal length, and petal width.
- The dataset is often used for classification problems, where the goal is to predict the species of the iris flower based on its measurements.
- The iris dataset is a popular dataset for beginners to learn data analytics and machine learning techniques.
- It is a well-studied dataset and has been used in many research studies.
