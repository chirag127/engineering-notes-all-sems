
### Data Information and Knowledge for the notes of the Unit 1 - BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE – INTRODUCTION in the subject of BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE AND ANALYTICS KCS

1. Business Intelligence (BI) is the process of collecting, organizing, and analyzing data to gain insights and make informed decisions.

2. BI tools are used to analyze data from multiple sources, including internal databases, external sources, and other data warehouses.

3. BI solutions can be used to identify trends, uncover hidden patterns, and provide predictive analytics.

4. BI solutions can be used to improve customer experience, optimize operations, and measure performance.

5. BI can be used to improve decision making by providing insights into customer behavior, market trends, and operational efficiency.

6. BI can be used to identify opportunities for growth and innovation.

7. BI can be used to monitor and analyze competitor performance.

8. BI solutions can be used to improve the accuracy of forecasting and budgeting.
