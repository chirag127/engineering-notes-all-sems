
### Types of Analytical Techniques in BI –Descriptive 

1. Descriptive Analytics: This type of analytics is used to summarize the data and provide insights into the data. It helps to identify patterns, trends, and relationships in the data. It is used to gain a better understanding of the data and to identify areas of improvement.

2. Predictive Analytics: This type of analytics is used to forecast future outcomes based on current and historical data. It helps to identify and quantify risks and opportunities in the data.

3. Prescriptive Analytics: This type of analytics is used to recommend actions to take based on the data. It helps to identify and quantify the best course of action to take in order to achieve a desired outcome.

4. Cognitive Analytics: This type of analytics is used to identify insights and patterns in unstructured data. It helps to identify and quantify insights that may not be obvious or easily derived from structured data.
