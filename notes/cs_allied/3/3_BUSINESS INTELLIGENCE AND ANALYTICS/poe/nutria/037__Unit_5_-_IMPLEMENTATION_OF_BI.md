
## Unit 5 - IMPLEMENTATION OF BI

1. Business Intelligence (BI) is a technology-driven process used to analyze data and present actionable information to help organizations make better decisions. 
2. BI tools are used to identify trends, spot opportunities, and measure performance.
3. BI implementation involves the use of data warehousing, data mining, and analytics to gain insights from data.
4. Data warehousing is the process of collecting, storing, and organizing data from multiple sources.
5. Data mining is the process of analyzing large datasets to uncover patterns, correlations, and trends.
6. Analytics is the process of interpreting data to gain insights and inform decision-making.
7. BI implementation requires careful planning and the selection of the right tools and technologies.
8. Organizations must consider their specific needs, budget, and resources when selecting BI tools.
9. BI implementation also requires the integration of data sources, the development of dashboards and reports, and the training of staff to use the tools.
10. A successful BI implementation requires ongoing maintenance and monitoring to ensure that the data is accurate and up-to-date.
