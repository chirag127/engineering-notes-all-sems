
### Architectural Representation for the notes of the Unit 1 - BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE – INTRODUCTION in the subject of BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE AND ANALYTICS KCS

1. Business Intelligence (BI) is a technology-driven process used to analyze data and provide actionable insights to help organizations make informed business decisions.

2. BI systems are composed of databases, data warehouses, data marts, and analytics tools. The data warehouses store large amounts of historical data and are used to generate reports and analyze trends. Data marts are smaller and more specialized databases used to store specific types of data.

3. Analytics tools are used to analyze the data and provide insights. These tools include predictive analytics, descriptive analytics, and prescriptive analytics.

4. BI systems are used to generate reports, identify trends, and make predictions. They can also be used to monitor and measure performance, identify areas of improvement, and optimize processes.

5. BI systems can be deployed on-premises or in the cloud. On-premises systems are more secure and provide more control, but they require more maintenance and are more expensive. Cloud-based systems are more cost-effective and easier to maintain, but they lack the same level of control and security.
