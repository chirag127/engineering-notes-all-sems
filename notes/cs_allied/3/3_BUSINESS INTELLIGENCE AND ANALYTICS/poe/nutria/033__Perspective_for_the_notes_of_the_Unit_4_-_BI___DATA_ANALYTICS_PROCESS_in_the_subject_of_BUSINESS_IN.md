
### Perspective for the Notes of the Unit 4 - BI – DATA ANALYTICS PROCESS in the Subject of BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE AND ANALYTICS KCS

1. Business Intelligence (BI) and analytics are two closely related concepts. BI is the process of collecting and analyzing data to gain insights into business operations, while analytics is the application of data-driven insights to improve business performance.

2. Data analytics is the process of collecting, organizing, and analyzing data to gain insights and make better decisions. It involves the use of data-driven techniques such as predictive analytics, machine learning, and artificial intelligence to uncover patterns and trends in data.

3. The BI process consists of four main stages: data collection, data cleaning and preparation, data analysis, and data visualization. The data collected is then used to create reports and visualizations that can help businesses make informed decisions.

4. Data analysis is the process of uncovering patterns, trends, and insights from data. This can be done through a variety of techniques such as statistical analysis, machine learning, and artificial intelligence.

5. Data visualization is the process of creating visual representations of data. This can help businesses gain insights into their data and make better decisions.

6. Business Intelligence and analytics are essential for any business that wants to gain insights into their data and make informed decisions. By leveraging the power of data, businesses can improve their operations, increase efficiency, and gain a competitive edge.
