
### Data Mart for the notes of the Unit 2 - BI – DATA MINING & WAREHOUSING in the subject of BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE AND ANALYTICS KCS

- Data Marts are specialized databases that contain a subset of the data from an enterprise data warehouse. 
- Data Marts are created to focus on specific business areas, such as sales, finance, or marketing. 
- Data Marts are typically smaller than an enterprise data warehouse, and are used to provide more focused analysis and reporting. 
- Data Marts are often used to provide a “single version of the truth” for a particular business area, instead of relying on multiple data sources. 
- Data Mining is the process of analyzing large datasets to discover patterns and trends. 
- Data Mining can be used to identify relationships between different data points, and can be used to make predictions about future events. 
- Data Mining techniques include clustering, classification, and regression. 
- Data Warehousing is the process of storing large amounts of data in a centralized repository. 
- Data Warehousing is used to provide a single source of truth for an organization, and can be used to provide a complete view of an organization’s data. 
- Data Warehousing can be used to provide historical data, as well as real-time data.
