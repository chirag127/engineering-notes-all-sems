
### Introduction - History and Evolution
Business intelligence (BI) is the practice of leveraging data and analytics to gain insight and make better decisions. BI has been around for centuries, with its roots in the early days of data analysis. The term “business intelligence” was first used in the late 1950s to describe the use of data to inform decisions.

Over the years, BI has evolved to become an essential tool for businesses of all sizes. It has become increasingly sophisticated and automated, with the use of advanced analytics and machine learning.

Today, BI is used to inform decisions across a wide range of industries, from finance to healthcare to retail. It helps organizations gain insights into their customers, competitors, and markets, and provides them with the data they need to make informed decisions.

BI is also used to identify trends, predict outcomes, and optimize processes. By leveraging data and analytics, businesses can gain a competitive edge and improve their bottom line.
