
### Difference between OLAP and OLTP

OLAP (Online Analytical Processing) and OLTP (Online Transaction Processing) are two database systems used to store and manage data.

**OLAP**

- OLAP is used for data analysis and reporting.
- It is used to store large amounts of historical data.
- It is used to support decision-making and data mining.
- It is used for data warehousing, online analytical processing and multidimensional analysis.
- It is used for complex queries and analysis of data.

**OLTP**

- OLTP is used for data entry and record keeping.
- It is used to store current data.
- It is used for online transaction processing.
- It is used to support day-to-day operations.
- It is used for basic queries and data manipulation.
