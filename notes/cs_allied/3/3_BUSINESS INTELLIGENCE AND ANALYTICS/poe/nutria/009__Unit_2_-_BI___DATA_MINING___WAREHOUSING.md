
## Unit 2 - BI – DATA MINING & WAREHOUSING

* Business Intelligence (BI) is the process of collecting, analyzing, and presenting data to make better business decisions.
* Data Mining is the process of extracting useful information from large datasets. It involves finding patterns and relationships in the data that can be used to make predictions.
* Data Warehousing is the process of storing and organizing data in a way that makes it easier to access and analyze. Data warehouses are used to store large amounts of data that can be used for reporting and analysis.
* BI tools are used to create dashboards and reports that provide insights into the data. These tools are used to visualize data and create reports that can be used to make better decisions.
* BI systems can be used to identify trends and patterns in the data that can help identify opportunities and risks. They can also be used to analyze customer behavior and identify areas of improvement.
