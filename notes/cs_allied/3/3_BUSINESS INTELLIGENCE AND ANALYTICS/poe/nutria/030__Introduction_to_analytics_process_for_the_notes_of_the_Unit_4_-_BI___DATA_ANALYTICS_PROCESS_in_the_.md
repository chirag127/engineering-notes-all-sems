
### Introduction to Analytics Process

1. Business Intelligence and Analytics (KCS) is a field that involves the use of technology, data, and analytics to gain insight into business operations and performance.
2. Data analytics is the process of collecting, organizing, analyzing, and interpreting data to uncover trends and patterns that can be used to make business decisions.
3. The data analytics process typically consists of four stages: data collection, data preparation, data analysis, and data visualization.
4. Data collection involves gathering data from various sources and storing it in a format that can be analyzed.
5. Data preparation involves cleaning, formatting, and transforming the data to make it easier to analyze.
6. Data analysis involves applying various statistical and machine learning techniques to uncover patterns and insights from the data.
7. Data visualization involves creating visual representations of the data to make it easier to understand and interpret.
8. The data analytics process can be used to gain insights into customer behavior, product performance, market trends, and more.
