
### Data Modelling for the notes of the Unit 2 - BI – DATA MINING & WAREHOUSING in the subject of BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE AND ANALYTICS KCS

1. Data modelling is a process of creating a data model for the data to be stored in a database. It involves analyzing the data requirements and defining the relationships between different data elements.

2. Data mining is the process of discovering patterns and correlations in large datasets to extract meaningful information. It involves using various techniques such as classification, clustering, association, and regression to identify patterns and trends in data.

3. Data warehousing is the process of collecting, storing, and managing large amounts of data from multiple sources. It involves creating a data warehouse, which is a repository of data from multiple sources.

4. Business intelligence (BI) is the process of collecting, analyzing, and presenting data to support decision making. It involves using various tools and techniques such as data mining, data warehousing, and analytics to identify trends, relationships, and patterns in data.

5. Analytics is the process of analyzing data to gain insights and make better decisions. It involves using various tools and techniques such as regression analysis, predictive analytics, and machine learning to identify patterns and relationships in data.
