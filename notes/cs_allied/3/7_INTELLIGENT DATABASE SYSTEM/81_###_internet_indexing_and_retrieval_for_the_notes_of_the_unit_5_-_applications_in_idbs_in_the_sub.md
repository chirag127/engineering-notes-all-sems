### Internet indexing and retrieval for the notes of the Unit 5 - Applications in IDBS in the subject of INTELLIGENT DATABASE SYSTEM

Internet indexing and retrieval is the process of organizing and searching the vast amount of information available on the Internet. This includes text, images, videos, and other types of content. The goal of internet indexing and retrieval is to provide users with quick and accurate access to the information they are seeking.

There are several approaches to internet indexing and retrieval, including:
1. Web crawling: The process of automatically discovering and indexing web pages by following links from one page to another.
2. Text indexing: The process of analyzing text to create an index of the words and phrases it contains, making it easier to search for specific information.
3. Image indexing: The process of analyzing images to create an index of their content, making it easier to search for specific images.
4. Video indexing: The process of analyzing videos to create an index of their content, making it easier to search for specific videos.

In the context of intelligent database systems, internet indexing and retrieval is a critical component of many applications, such as search engines, recommendation systems, and data mining. By organizing and indexing the vast amount of information available on the Internet, these systems can provide users with quick and accurate access to the information they are seeking.
