### Issues for the notes of the Unit 2 - Semantic Data Models in the subject of INTELLIGENT DATABASE SYSTEM

The domain of Unit 2 - Semantic Data Models in the subject of Intelligent Database Systems focuses on the use of semantic data models to represent and manage data in a more meaningful and intelligent way. The unit will explore the various types of semantic data models, including ontologies, conceptual data models, and semantic networks, among others. The unit will also examine the challenges and issues associated with the use of semantic data models, such as data integration, data interoperability, and the maintenance of data consistency and coherence. Additionally, the unit will cover the use of semantic data models in various applications, such as knowledge management, natural language processing, and expert systems, among others. The goal of this unit is to provide students with a comprehensive understanding of the concepts, techniques, and tools associated with semantic data models, and to equip them with the skills needed to design, implement, and evaluate intelligent database systems that make use of these models.
