### Updates in deductive databases for the notes of the Unit 3 - Knowledge-Based Systems- AI Ccontext in the subject of INTELLIGENT DATABASE SYSTEM

Updates in deductive databases are changes made to the knowledge represented in a deductive database. Deductive databases use a combination of rules and facts to represent knowledge, and updates can include adding new facts, modifying existing facts, or removing facts.

Updates in deductive databases can be performed in a number of ways, including:
1. Rule-based updates: updates are performed by adding, modifying, or removing rules in the database.
2. Fact-based updates: updates are performed by adding, modifying, or removing facts in the database.
3. Query-based updates: updates are performed by executing queries that modify the state of the database.

Updates in deductive databases can have a significant impact on the knowledge represented in the database, and must be performed carefully to ensure that the consistency and integrity of the knowledge is maintained.

In summary, Updates in deductive databases are changes made to the knowledge represented in a deductive database. Updates can be performed through rule-based updates, fact-based updates, or query-based updates, and must be performed carefully to ensure the consistency and integrity of the knowledge is maintained.
