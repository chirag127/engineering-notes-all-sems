### Comparison with other data models for the notes of the Unit 2 - Semantic Data Models in the subject of INTELLIGENT DATABASE SYSTEM

The domain of the Unit 2 - Semantic Data Models in the subject of Intelligent Database Systems focuses on the comparison of different data models used in databases, with a specific emphasis on semantic data models. A semantic data model is a type of data model that explicitly defines the meaning of data elements and the relationships between them. This unit will compare semantic data models with other data models, such as relational, network, and hierarchical data models, and will discuss the advantages and disadvantages of each. The unit will also cover topics such as ontologies, semantic networks, and RDF, as well as the use of semantic data models in intelligent database systems. The goal of this unit is to provide students with a comprehensive understanding of the different data models used in databases, and to equip them with the knowledge and skills needed to design and implement intelligent database systems that use semantic data models.
