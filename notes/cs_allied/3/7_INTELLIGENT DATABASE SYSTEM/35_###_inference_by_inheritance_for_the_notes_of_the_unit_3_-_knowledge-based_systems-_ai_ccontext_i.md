### Inference by inheritance for the notes of the Unit 3 - Knowledge-Based Systems- AI Ccontext in the subject of INTELLIGENT DATABASE SYSTEM

Inference by inheritance is a method of reasoning in knowledge-based systems where new knowledge is derived from existing knowledge by inheritance. In this method, knowledge is represented in the form of a hierarchy, with more general knowledge at the top of the hierarchy and more specific knowledge at the bottom.

In inference by inheritance, new knowledge is derived by inheriting the properties of existing knowledge and adding additional information to make it more specific. For example, if a knowledge base contains information about dogs, a new knowledge about a specific breed of dog can be derived by inheriting the properties of the general knowledge about dogs and adding information about the specific breed.

Inference by inheritance can be used in several ways in knowledge-based systems, including:

1. Knowledge representation: Inference by inheritance can be used to represent knowledge in a hierarchical form, making it easier to manage and organize large amounts of knowledge.

2. Reasoning: Inference by inheritance can be used to reason about new knowledge by deriving it from existing knowledge, making it possible to answer questions and make decisions based on the knowledge in the system.

3. Problem solving: Inference by inheritance can be used to solve problems by combining existing knowledge to form new knowledge that can be used to solve the problem.

In conclusion, Inference by inheritance is a method of reasoning in knowledge-based systems where new knowledge is derived from existing knowledge by inheritance. This method can be used for knowledge representation, reasoning, and problem solving, and makes it possible to manage and organize large amounts of knowledge, answer questions and make decisions based on the knowledge in the system, and solve problems by combining existing knowledge.
