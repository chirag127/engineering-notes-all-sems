### Motivation for the notes of the Unit 5 - Applications in IDBS in the subject of INTELLIGENT DATABASE SYSTEM

Unit 5 - Applications in IDBS covers the concept of motivation, which is the driving force behind the development and use of Intelligent Database Systems (IDBS).

Motivation is important in IDBS because it provides a reason for organizations to use IDBS, and it drives the development and use of IDBS.

The following are key aspects of motivation in IDBS:

1. Business Needs: Business needs are a major source of motivation for the development and use of IDBS. Business needs drive the development and use of IDBS because organizations need to manage and analyze data to make informed decisions.

2. Technological Advancements: Technological advancements are another source of motivation for the development and use of IDBS. Technological advancements drive the development and use of IDBS because new technologies make it possible to manage and analyze data in new and innovative ways.

3. Data Growth: Data growth is a major source of motivation for the development and use of IDBS. Data growth drives the development and use of IDBS because organizations need to manage and analyze increasing amounts of data.

4. Competitive Advantage: Competitive advantage is another source of motivation for the development and use of IDBS. Competitive advantage drives the development and use of IDBS because organizations need to use data to gain a competitive edge.

By understanding these concepts, organizations can effectively understand the motivation for the development and use of IDBS, and can effectively use IDBS to meet their business needs and gain a competitive advantage. Unit 5 - Applications in IDBS covers the motivation for the development and use of IDBS, including business needs, technological advancements, data growth, and competitive advantage, in the context of intelligent database systems.
