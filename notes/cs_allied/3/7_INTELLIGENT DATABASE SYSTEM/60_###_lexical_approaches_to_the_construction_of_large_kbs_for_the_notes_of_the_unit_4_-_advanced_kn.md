### Lexical approaches to the construction of large KBs for the notes of the Unit 4 - Advanced Knowledge-Based Systems in the subject of INTELLIGENT DATABASE SYSTEM

Lexical approaches to the construction of large knowledge bases (KBs) involve using natural language text as the source of information to build the KB. The following are some of the key concepts involved in this process:

1. Named entity recognition (NER): The process of identifying named entities such as people, organizations, and locations in text.

2. Relation extraction: The process of identifying relationships between named entities in text, such as "person X works for organization Y."

3. Word sense disambiguation (WSD): The process of determining the correct sense of a word in context, as words can have multiple meanings.

4. Coreference resolution: The process of identifying when two or more mentions in text refer to the same entity.

5. Ontology learning: The process of automatically constructing an ontology from text, which can be used to represent the relationships between entities in the KB.

These lexical approaches are often used in combination to build large KBs, such as those used in question answering and information retrieval systems. The resulting KBs can be used to support advanced knowledge-based systems, such as those that can answer complex questions or make inferences based on the information they contain.
