## Unit 3 - Knowledge-Based Systems- AI Ccontext

Unit 3 - Knowledge-Based Systems covers the context and basic concepts of knowledge-based systems in the field of artificial intelligence (AI). The following are key aspects of knowledge-based systems:

1. Knowledge Representation: Knowledge representation is the process of representing knowledge in a computer-understandable form. This is a critical aspect of knowledge-based systems, as it allows knowledge to be stored, manipulated, and used by the system.

2. Expert Systems: Expert systems are knowledge-based systems that are designed to mimic the decision-making abilities of a human expert in a specific domain. Expert systems use knowledge representation and reasoning techniques to solve problems and make decisions.

3. Rule-Based Systems: Rule-based systems are knowledge-based systems that use a set of rules to represent knowledge and make decisions. Rule-based systems are often used in expert systems and other knowledge-based systems.

4. Natural Language Processing: Natural language processing (NLP) is a subfield of AI that focuses on the processing and understanding of human language. NLP is an important aspect of knowledge-based systems, as it allows knowledge-based systems to interact with humans in a more natural way.

5. Machine Learning: Machine learning is a subfield of AI that focuses on the development of algorithms that can learn from data. Machine learning is an important aspect of knowledge-based systems, as it allows knowledge-based systems to improve their performance over time.

By understanding these basic concepts, organizations can effectively use knowledge-based systems to solve problems and make decisions in a variety of domains. Unit 3 - Knowledge-Based Systems covers the use of knowledge-based systems, including expert systems, rule-based systems, NLP, and machine learning, in the context of AI.
