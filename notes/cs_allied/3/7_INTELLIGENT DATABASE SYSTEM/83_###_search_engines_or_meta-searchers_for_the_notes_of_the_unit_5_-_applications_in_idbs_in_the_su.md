### Search engines or meta-searchers for the notes of the Unit 5 - Applications in IDBS in the subject of INTELLIGENT DATABASE SYSTEM

Search engines or meta-searchers are tools used to search and retrieve information from the web. They work by indexing web pages and making them searchable based on keywords and other metadata. There are two main types of search engines:

1. Web search engines: These search engines are designed to search for information on the web, such as websites, images, videos, and news articles. Examples include Google, Bing, and Yahoo.

2. Meta-searchers: These search engines are designed to search multiple search engines and databases simultaneously, providing a consolidated view of the search results. Examples include Dogpile and Metacrawler.

In the context of intelligent database systems, search engines and meta-searchers can be used to search and retrieve information from databases, as well as from the web. They provide a convenient way for users to find information, and can be customized to search specific databases or data sources.

Overall, search engines and meta-searchers are important tools for information retrieval and management, and play a crucial role in the field of intelligent database systems.
