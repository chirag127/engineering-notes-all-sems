### Conclusion for the notes of the Unit 3 - Knowledge-Based Systems- AI Context in the subject of INTELLIGENT DATABASE SYSTEM

- Knowledge-based systems are a type of artificial intelligence that use a knowledge base to solve problems.
- These systems are designed to mimic the problem-solving abilities of human experts in a specific domain.
- The knowledge base contains facts and rules that represent the expertise of the domain.
- Inference engines are used to apply the rules and draw conclusions from the facts in the knowledge base.
- Knowledge-based systems can be used in a variety of applications, including decision support, diagnosis, and planning.
- These systems have the potential to improve the efficiency and effectiveness of decision-making processes.
- However, the development of knowledge-based systems can be challenging, as it requires the acquisition and representation of expert knowledge.
- Additionally, the maintenance of the knowledge base can be time-consuming, as it must be updated to reflect changes in the domain.
