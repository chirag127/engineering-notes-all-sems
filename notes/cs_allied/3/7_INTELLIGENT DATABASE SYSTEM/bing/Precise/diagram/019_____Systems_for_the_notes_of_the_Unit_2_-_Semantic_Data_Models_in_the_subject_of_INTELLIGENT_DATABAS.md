### Systems for the notes of the Unit 2 - Semantic Data Models in the subject of INTELLIGENT DATABASE SYSTEM

1. Semantic data models are used to represent the meaning of data within the context of its interrelationships with other data.
2. These models are used to improve the understanding of the data and to facilitate communication between the users and the developers of the system.
3. Semantic data models are based on concepts such as entities, attributes, and relationships.
4. Entities represent real-world objects or concepts, attributes represent the properties of these entities, and relationships represent the associations between entities.
5. Semantic data models are used in the design of databases, where they provide a high-level view of the data and its organization.
6. These models are also used in the development of knowledge-based systems, where they provide a formal representation of the knowledge contained in the system.
7. There are several types of semantic data models, including the Entity-Relationship model, the Object-Oriented model, and the Semantic Object model.
8. Each of these models has its own strengths and weaknesses, and the choice of model depends on the specific requirements of the system being developed.
9. Semantic data models are an important tool for the design and development of intelligent database systems, as they provide a clear and concise representation of the data and its relationships.
