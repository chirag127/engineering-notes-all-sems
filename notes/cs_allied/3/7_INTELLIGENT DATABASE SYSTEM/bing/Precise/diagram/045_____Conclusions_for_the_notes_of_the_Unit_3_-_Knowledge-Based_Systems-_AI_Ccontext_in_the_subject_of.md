### Conclusions for the notes of the Unit 3 - Knowledge-Based Systems- AI Context in the subject of INTELLIGENT DATABASE SYSTEM

1. Knowledge-based systems are computer programs that use artificial intelligence to solve problems within a specialized domain that ordinarily requires human expertise.
2. These systems are designed to mimic the decision-making abilities of a human expert in a particular field.
3. They are used in a variety of applications, including medical diagnosis, financial planning, and legal decision-making.
4. Knowledge-based systems rely on a knowledge base, which is a collection of facts, rules, and heuristics about the domain in question.
5. The system uses this knowledge, along with inference mechanisms, to draw conclusions and make decisions.
6. Knowledge-based systems have the potential to improve the efficiency and accuracy of decision-making in many fields.
7. However, they also have limitations, including the difficulty of acquiring and representing knowledge, and the potential for errors and biases in the system's reasoning.
8. Ongoing research in the field of artificial intelligence is aimed at addressing these challenges and improving the capabilities of knowledge-based systems.
