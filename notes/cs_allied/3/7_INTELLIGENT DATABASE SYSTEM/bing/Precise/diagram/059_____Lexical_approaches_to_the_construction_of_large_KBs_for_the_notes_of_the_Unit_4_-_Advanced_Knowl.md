### Lexical approaches to the construction of large KBs

Lexical approaches to the construction of large knowledge bases (KBs) involve the use of natural language processing techniques to extract information from large amounts of text data. These approaches can be used to automatically construct large-scale KBs for use in advanced knowledge-based systems.

Some key points to consider when using lexical approaches to construct large KBs include:

1. The use of natural language processing techniques to extract information from text data.
2. The ability to automatically construct large-scale KBs from the extracted information.
3. The importance of using high-quality text data as the source for information extraction.
4. The need for effective disambiguation and entity resolution techniques to accurately identify and link entities within the KB.
5. The potential for the use of machine learning techniques to improve the accuracy and efficiency of the information extraction process.

Overall, lexical approaches to the construction of large KBs offer a powerful and scalable way to automatically construct advanced knowledge-based systems from large amounts of text data. However, the success of these approaches depends on the quality of the text data used as the source for information extraction, as well as the effectiveness of the natural language processing, disambiguation, and entity resolution techniques employed.
