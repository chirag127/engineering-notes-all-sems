### General characteristics of IDBSs

An Intelligent Database System (IDBS) is a database system that combines the capabilities of a traditional database management system (DBMS) with artificial intelligence techniques to provide enhanced functionality and performance. Some of the general characteristics of IDBSs are:

1. **Data modeling and representation**: IDBSs support advanced data modeling and representation techniques, such as object-oriented, semantic, and deductive data models, to represent complex data and relationships.

2. **Inference and reasoning**: IDBSs incorporate inference and reasoning mechanisms, such as rule-based systems, to derive new information from the data stored in the database.

3. **Knowledge representation**: IDBSs support the representation and manipulation of knowledge, in addition to data, using techniques such as frames, semantic networks, and ontologies.

4. **Natural language processing**: IDBSs provide natural language interfaces to enable users to interact with the database using natural language queries and commands.

5. **Machine learning**: IDBSs incorporate machine learning techniques to learn from the data and improve their performance over time.

6. **Intelligent query processing**: IDBSs support intelligent query processing, which involves the use of advanced query optimization techniques and the ability to handle imprecise and incomplete queries.

7. **User modeling**: IDBSs maintain user models to understand the needs and preferences of individual users and provide personalized responses.

These are some of the general characteristics of IDBSs that distinguish them from traditional DBMSs and enable them to provide enhanced functionality and performance.
