### The 'tight coupling' approach for the notes of the Unit 4 - Advanced Knowledge-Based Systems in the subject of INTELLIGENT DATABASE SYSTEM

- The 'tight coupling' approach refers to the integration of knowledge-based systems and databases.
- In this approach, the knowledge-based system and the database are closely integrated and work together to provide intelligent data processing.
- This approach is different from the 'loose coupling' approach, where the knowledge-based system and the database are separate entities that communicate with each other.
- The tight coupling approach has several advantages, including improved performance, increased consistency, and easier maintenance.
- However, it also has some disadvantages, such as increased complexity and the need for specialized knowledge to implement and maintain the system.
- In summary, the tight coupling approach is a powerful tool for integrating knowledge-based systems and databases, but it requires careful consideration and planning to implement effectively.
