### Conclusions for the notes of the Unit 5 - Applications in IDBS in the subject of INTELLIGENT DATABASE SYSTEM

1. Intelligent Database Systems (IDBS) are designed to manage large and complex data sets by incorporating artificial intelligence techniques.
2. IDBS have various applications in different fields such as finance, healthcare, and marketing.
3. IDBS can be used for data analysis, data mining, and decision support.
4. IDBS can improve the efficiency and effectiveness of data management and analysis.
5. IDBS can help organizations make better decisions by providing accurate and timely information.
6. IDBS can also help organizations to identify patterns and trends in their data, which can be used to improve their operations and strategies.
7. IDBS can be integrated with other systems to provide a comprehensive solution for data management and analysis.
8. The use of IDBS is becoming increasingly popular due to the growing need for intelligent data management in various industries.
