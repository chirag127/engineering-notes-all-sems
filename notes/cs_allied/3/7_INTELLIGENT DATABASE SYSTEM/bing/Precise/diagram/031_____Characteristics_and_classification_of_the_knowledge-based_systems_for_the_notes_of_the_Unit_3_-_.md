### Characteristics and classification of the knowledge-based systems

Knowledge-based systems (KBS) are computer programs that reason and use a knowledge base to solve complex problems. The following are the characteristics of knowledge-based systems:

1. **Expertise:** KBS possess a high level of expertise in a particular domain. They can solve complex problems that require specialized knowledge and experience.

2. **Symbolic reasoning:** KBS use symbolic representations of knowledge and perform logical reasoning to draw conclusions.

3. **Explanation:** KBS can provide explanations for their reasoning and justify their conclusions.

4. **Uncertainty management:** KBS can handle uncertain and incomplete information and make decisions based on the available evidence.

5. **Adaptability:** KBS can learn from experience and improve their performance over time.

Knowledge-based systems can be classified into two main categories:

1. **Rule-based systems:** These systems use a set of rules to represent knowledge and perform reasoning. The rules are typically in the form of IF-THEN statements.

2. **Frame-based systems:** These systems use a network of frames to represent knowledge. A frame is a data structure that represents a concept or object and contains attributes and values that describe its properties.

In the context of AI, knowledge-based systems are used to solve complex problems that require specialized knowledge and expertise. They are commonly used in expert systems, decision support systems, and intelligent tutoring systems.
