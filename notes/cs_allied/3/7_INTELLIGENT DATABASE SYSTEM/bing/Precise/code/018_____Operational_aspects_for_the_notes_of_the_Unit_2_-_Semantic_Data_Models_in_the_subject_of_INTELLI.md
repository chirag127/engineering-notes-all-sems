### Operational aspects for the notes of the Unit 2 - Semantic Data Models in the subject of INTELLIGENT DATABASE SYSTEM

1. **Semantic Data Models** provide a higher level of abstraction for data modeling, which focuses on the meaning of the data within the context of its interrelationships and attributes.
2. These models are used to represent the data in a way that is closer to the way humans perceive and understand the data.
3. The main components of a semantic data model are **entities**, **attributes**, and **relationships**.
4. Entities represent real-world objects or concepts, and are defined by their attributes.
5. Attributes are characteristics or properties of an entity, and can have different data types such as text, numbers, or dates.
6. Relationships represent the associations between entities, and can be one-to-one, one-to-many, or many-to-many.
7. Semantic data models are often used in the design of **intelligent databases**, which are databases that can understand and interpret the meaning of the data they store.
8. These databases use advanced techniques such as **natural language processing**, **machine learning**, and **artificial intelligence** to provide more sophisticated data retrieval and analysis capabilities.
9. Some examples of intelligent databases include **knowledge-based systems**, **expert systems**, and **decision support systems**.
10. The use of semantic data models and intelligent databases can improve the efficiency and effectiveness of data management and analysis, and can provide valuable insights and decision-making support for businesses and organizations.
