### Lexical approaches to the construction of large KBs

Lexical approaches to the construction of large knowledge bases (KBs) for advanced knowledge-based systems involve the use of natural language processing techniques to extract information from large corpora of text. These techniques can be used to automatically build and update KBs, making it possible to create and maintain large-scale knowledge bases.

Some key points to consider when using lexical approaches to construct large KBs include:

1. **Selection of text corpora**: The selection of text corpora is an important step in the construction of large KBs using lexical approaches. The corpora should be relevant to the domain of the KB and should contain a large amount of high-quality text.

2. **Preprocessing of text**: Preprocessing of text is necessary to prepare the text for analysis. This may involve tokenization, part-of-speech tagging, and other techniques to identify and extract relevant information from the text.

3. **Information extraction**: Information extraction techniques are used to identify and extract relevant information from the text. This may involve the use of named entity recognition, relation extraction, and other techniques to identify and extract relevant facts and relationships from the text.

4. **Knowledge representation**: The extracted information must be represented in a form that can be used by the knowledge-based system. This may involve the use of ontologies, frames, or other knowledge representation formalisms to represent the extracted information in a structured and machine-readable form.

5. **Updating the KB**: The KB must be updated regularly to incorporate new information extracted from the text corpora. This may involve the use of machine learning techniques to automatically update the KB based on new information extracted from the text.

Overall, lexical approaches to the construction of large KBs provide a powerful and scalable way to automatically build and maintain large-scale knowledge bases for advanced knowledge-based systems. These approaches can be used to extract and represent a wide range of information from large corpora of text, making it possible to create and maintain large and up-to-date KBs.
