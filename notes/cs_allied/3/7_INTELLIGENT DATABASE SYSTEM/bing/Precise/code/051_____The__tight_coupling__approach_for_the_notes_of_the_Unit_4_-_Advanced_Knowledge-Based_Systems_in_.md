### The 'tight coupling' approach for the notes of the Unit 4 - Advanced Knowledge-Based Systems in the subject of INTELLIGENT DATABASE SYSTEM

- The 'tight coupling' approach refers to the integration of knowledge-based systems and database systems in a way that allows for seamless interaction between the two.
- This approach involves the incorporation of knowledge representation and reasoning capabilities into the database system itself.
- This allows for the use of advanced knowledge-based techniques, such as rule-based systems and expert systems, to be applied directly to the data stored in the database.
- The tight coupling approach can provide several benefits, including improved data consistency and integrity, increased efficiency in data processing, and enhanced decision-making capabilities.
- However, implementing a tight coupling approach can also present challenges, such as the need for specialized knowledge and expertise in both knowledge-based systems and database systems.
- Overall, the tight coupling approach represents a powerful tool for integrating advanced knowledge-based techniques into database systems, allowing for more intelligent and effective data management.
