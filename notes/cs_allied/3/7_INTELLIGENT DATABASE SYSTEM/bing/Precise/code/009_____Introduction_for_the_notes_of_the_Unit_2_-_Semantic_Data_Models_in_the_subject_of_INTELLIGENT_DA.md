### Introduction for the notes of the Unit 2 - Semantic Data Models in the subject of INTELLIGENT DATABASE SYSTEM

1. Semantic data models are high-level data models that capture the meaning of data within the context of its interrelationships with other data.
2. They provide a way to represent data in a more abstract and conceptual manner, allowing for a more natural and intuitive understanding of the data.
3. Semantic data models are used in the design of databases, where they serve as a blueprint for the organization and structure of the data.
4. They are also used in the development of software applications, where they provide a way to model the data used by the application.
5. Semantic data models are typically represented using graphical notations, such as entity-relationship diagrams or object-role modeling diagrams.
6. They are often used in conjunction with other data modeling techniques, such as logical and physical data models, to provide a complete and comprehensive view of the data and its relationships.
7. Semantic data models are an important tool for database designers and developers, as they provide a way to capture the meaning and structure of the data in a way that is easy to understand and work with.
