### Motivation for the notes of the Unit 5 - Applications in IDBS in the subject of INTELLIGENT DATABASE SYSTEM

1. Intelligent Database Systems (IDBS) are designed to manage large and complex data sets by incorporating artificial intelligence and machine learning techniques.
2. The motivation for studying the applications of IDBS is to understand how these systems can be used to improve data management and analysis in various domains.
3. IDBS can be applied in a wide range of fields, including finance, healthcare, marketing, and transportation, among others.
4. By studying the applications of IDBS, students can gain a deeper understanding of how these systems can be used to solve real-world problems and improve decision-making processes.
5. Additionally, understanding the applications of IDBS can help students develop the skills and knowledge necessary to design and implement intelligent database systems in their future careers.
