## Unit 3 - Knowledge-Based Systems- AI Context

1. Knowledge-based systems are computer programs that use artificial intelligence to solve problems within a specialized domain that ordinarily requires human expertise.
2. These systems are designed to mimic the problem-solving abilities of human experts in a particular field.
3. Knowledge-based systems are made up of two main components: a knowledge base and an inference engine.
4. The knowledge base contains the domain-specific knowledge and facts that the system uses to solve problems.
5. The inference engine is the part of the system that applies logical rules to the knowledge base to deduce new information and draw conclusions.
6. Knowledge-based systems can be used in a wide range of applications, including medical diagnosis, financial planning, and legal decision-making.
7. These systems can provide expert-level advice and recommendations to users, helping them make informed decisions.
8. Knowledge-based systems can also be used to automate complex decision-making processes, reducing the need for human intervention.
9. However, the effectiveness of a knowledge-based system depends on the quality and completeness of the knowledge base, as well as the accuracy of the inference engine.
10. It is important to regularly update and maintain the knowledge base to ensure that the system remains accurate and up-to-date.
