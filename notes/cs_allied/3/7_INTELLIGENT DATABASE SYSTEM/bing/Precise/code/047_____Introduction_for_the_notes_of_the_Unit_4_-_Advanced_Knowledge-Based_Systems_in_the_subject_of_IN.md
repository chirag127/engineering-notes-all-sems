### Introduction for the notes of the Unit 4 - Advanced Knowledge-Based Systems in the subject of INTELLIGENT DATABASE SYSTEM

- Advanced Knowledge-Based Systems (AKBS) are computer programs that use artificial intelligence techniques to solve complex problems.
- These systems are designed to mimic human reasoning and decision-making processes.
- AKBS can be used in a variety of applications, including expert systems, decision support systems, and intelligent tutoring systems.
- In the context of intelligent databases, AKBS can be used to improve the efficiency and effectiveness of data retrieval and analysis.
- Some of the key features of AKBS include the ability to learn from experience, the ability to reason and make decisions based on incomplete or uncertain information, and the ability to explain their reasoning and decisions.
- In this unit, we will explore the principles and techniques used in the design and implementation of AKBS, as well as their applications in the field of intelligent databases.
