### Issues for the notes of the Unit 2 - Semantic Data Models in the subject of INTELLIGENT DATABASE SYSTEM

- Semantic data models are high-level conceptual data models that include semantic information to describe the meaning and relationships of data .
- Semantic data models can be used to capture more of the meaning of an application environment than conventional data models, such as hierarchical, network, or relational .
- Semantic data models can also enable the expression and exchange of information that is not easily represented in conventional data models, such as rules, constraints, and inference .
- Semantic data models can be classified into three main types: entity-relationship models, functional models, and object-oriented models.
- Entity-relationship models use entities, attributes, and relationships to represent data and their semantics. Entities are objects or concepts that can be identified and distinguished. Attributes are properties or characteristics of entities. Relationships are associations or connections between entities.
- Functional models use functions, domains, and mappings to represent data and their semantics. Functions are operations or transformations that produce outputs from inputs. Domains are sets of values that can be used as inputs or outputs of functions. Mappings are rules or expressions that define how functions map inputs to outputs.
- Object-oriented models use objects, classes, and methods to represent data and their semantics. Objects are instances of classes that have attributes and behaviors. Classes are categories or types of objects that share common attributes and behaviors. Methods are procedures or actions that can be performed by or on objects.
- Some of the issues or challenges of semantic data models are:
  - How to design and implement semantic data models that are consistent, complete, and correct.
  - How to ensure the interoperability and integration of semantic data models across different systems and platforms .
  - How to query and manipulate semantic data models efficiently and effectively .
  - How to maintain and evolve semantic data models over time and in response to changing requirements .
  - How to evaluate and compare the quality and performance of semantic data models .
