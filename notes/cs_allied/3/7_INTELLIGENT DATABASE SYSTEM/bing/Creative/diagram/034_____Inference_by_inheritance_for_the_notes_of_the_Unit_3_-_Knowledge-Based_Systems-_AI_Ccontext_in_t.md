### Inference by inheritance

- Inference by inheritance is a technique of reasoning that uses the hierarchical structure of classes and subclasses to derive new facts from existing facts.
- Inference by inheritance is based on the principle of **generalization** and **specialization**. Generalization is the process of grouping similar entities into a common class, while specialization is the process of creating subclasses from a superclass by adding more specific features.
- Inference by inheritance can be applied to both **is-a** and **has-a** relationships. Is-a relationships are based on the concept of **inheritance**, where a subclass inherits all the properties and methods of its superclass. Has-a relationships are based on the concept of **composition**, where a class has another class as one of its attributes.
- Inference by inheritance can be implemented using different types of **knowledge representation** systems, such as **semantic networks**, **frames**, and **ontologies**. Semantic networks are graphs that use nodes to represent concepts and links to represent relationships. Frames are data structures that use slots to store attributes and values of a concept. Ontologies are formal specifications of the concepts, relationships, and constraints in a domain .
- Inference by inheritance can be used to perform different types of **reasoning** tasks, such as **classification**, **subsumption**, **matching**, and **query answering**. Classification is the process of assigning an entity to a class based on its features. Subsumption is the process of checking if a class is a subclass of another class. Matching is the process of finding the most similar class to an entity based on its features. Query answering is the process of retrieving relevant information from a knowledge base based on a query .
- Inference by inheritance can also be combined with other types of inference techniques, such as **rule-based inference**, **probabilistic inference**, and **neural network inference**. Rule-based inference uses conditional statements to derive new facts from existing facts. Probabilistic inference uses statistical methods to assign probabilities to facts and hypotheses. Neural network inference uses artificial neural networks to learn patterns and associations from data  .
