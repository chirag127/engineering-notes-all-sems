### Architectural solutions for the notes of the Unit 4 - Advanced Knowledge-Based Systems in the subject of INTELLIGENT DATABASE SYSTEM

- Advanced knowledge-based systems (AKBS) are software systems that use artificial intelligence techniques to perform complex tasks that require human expertise and reasoning.
- AKBS can be classified into two types: expert systems and intelligent agents.
- Expert systems are AKBS that emulate the problem-solving skills of a human expert in a specific domain, such as medical diagnosis, financial planning, or engineering design.
- Intelligent agents are AKBS that act autonomously and proactively in dynamic and uncertain environments, such as e-commerce, robotics, or gaming.
- Architectural solutions for AKBS are the design decisions and principles that guide the development and maintenance of AKBS, such as the choice of knowledge representation, inference mechanism, communication protocol, and adaptation strategy.
- Architectural solutions for AKBS can be influenced by various factors, such as the requirements, the domain, the stakeholders, the technology, and the environment.
- Architectural solutions for AKBS can be documented and managed using architectural knowledge management (AKM) tools, which aim to codify and maintain the architectural knowledge of a software system in a form that can be easily accessed by different stakeholders.
- Architectural solutions for AKBS can be evaluated and improved using various methods, such as architectural patterns, metrics, reviews, and testing.
- Architectural solutions for AKBS can benefit from the use of cloud computing platforms, such as AWS, which provide scalable, secure, and cost-effective services for hosting, deploying, and managing AKBS.
- Architectural solutions for AKBS can also benefit from the use of self-adaptive systems, which are systems that can monitor, analyze, plan, and execute changes to their own behavior and structure in response to changes in the environment.
