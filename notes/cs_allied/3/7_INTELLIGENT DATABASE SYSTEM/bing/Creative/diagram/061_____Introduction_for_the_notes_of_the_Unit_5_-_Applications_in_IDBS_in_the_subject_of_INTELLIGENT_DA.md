### Introduction

- Intelligent Database Systems (IDBS) are database systems that integrate artificial intelligence techniques, such as machine learning, natural language processing, knowledge representation and reasoning, to enhance the functionality and performance of traditional database systems.
- IDBS can provide various benefits, such as:
  - Improving the quality and efficiency of data management, analysis, and retrieval.
  - Supporting complex and flexible queries, such as natural language queries, fuzzy queries, and multimedia queries.
  - Enabling intelligent data mining and knowledge discovery, such as classification, clustering, association rule mining, and anomaly detection.
  - Facilitating decision making and problem solving, such as recommendation systems, expert systems, and planning systems.
  - Enhancing the security and privacy of data, such as encryption, access control, and anonymization.
- IDBS can be applied to various domains and scenarios, such as:
  - E-commerce and web services, such as product recommendation, customer segmentation, and sentiment analysis.
  - Healthcare and biomedical informatics, such as diagnosis, prognosis, treatment, and drug discovery.
  - Education and learning analytics, such as adaptive learning, personalized feedback, and student modeling.
  - Social media and network analysis, such as community detection, influence analysis, and opinion mining.
  - Smart cities and IoT, such as traffic management, energy optimization, and environmental monitoring.
