
## Unit 4 - Advanced Knowledge-Based Systems

1. Knowledge-based systems (KBS) are computer systems that are designed to use knowledge to solve complex problems.

2. KBS typically consist of a knowledge base, a set of rules, and an inference engine. The knowledge base contains the data and facts about the problem domain, while the rules are used to make inferences and draw conclusions.

3. KBS can be used to solve a wide range of problems, including decision-making, problem-solving, and natural language processing.

4. KBS are often used in applications where there is a large amount of data and complex rules, such as medical diagnosis and financial analysis.

5. KBS can be used to automate processes and reduce the need for human intervention.

6. KBS can also be used to identify patterns and trends in data, and to make predictions about future events.

7. KBS can be used to improve customer service, as they can provide personalized recommendations and automated responses.

8. KBS can also be used to improve the accuracy and efficiency of decision-making processes.
