

### The Resolution Principle for the Notes of the Unit 3 - Knowledge-Based Systems- AI Context in the Subject of INTELLIGENT DATABASE SYSTEM

1. The resolution principle is a problem-solving strategy used in artificial intelligence (AI) and knowledge-based systems. It is used to determine the best solution to a given problem.

2. The resolution principle works by breaking down a problem into smaller sub-problems and then solving them one at a time. This process is called "resolution".

3. The resolution principle is a form of logical reasoning and is based on the idea of "proof by contradiction". This means that a problem can be solved by assuming the opposite of what needs to be proven and then showing that it leads to a contradiction.

4. The resolution principle is used in many AI and knowledge-based systems, including expert systems, natural language processing, and automated reasoning.

5. The resolution principle is a powerful tool for problem-solving and can be used to solve complex problems. However, it is not always the best approach and can be computationally expensive.
