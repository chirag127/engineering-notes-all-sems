
### Basic concepts for the notes of the Unit 3 - Knowledge-Based Systems- AI Context in the subject of INTELLIGENT DATABASE SYSTEM

1. **Knowledge-Based Systems:** Knowledge-Based Systems (KBSs) are computer systems that are designed to mimic the behavior of a human expert in a particular domain. They are composed of a knowledge base, which stores the information about the domain, and an inference engine, which uses the knowledge base to make decisions.

2. **AI Context:** AI Context is the application of AI techniques to a particular problem or domain. AI Contexts can range from natural language processing to robotics and machine learning.

3. **Expert Systems:** Expert Systems are computer systems that use AI techniques to solve complex problems or tasks. They are composed of a knowledge base, which stores information about the domain, and an inference engine, which uses the knowledge base to make decisions.

4. **Knowledge Representation:** Knowledge Representation is the process of encoding knowledge into a form that can be used by a computer system. Common forms of knowledge representation include frames, semantic networks, and ontologies.

5. **Ontologies:** Ontologies are formal representations of knowledge. They are composed of concepts, relationships, and rules. They are used to represent the knowledge of a particular domain in a structured and consistent way.

6. **Explanation-Based Learning:** Explanation-Based Learning (EBL) is a type of machine learning that uses explanations of past decisions or actions to improve future decisions or actions. It is often used to improve the accuracy of AI systems.

7. **Case-Based Reasoning:** Case-Based Reasoning (CBR) is a type of AI technique that is used to solve problems by drawing on past experience. It is based on the idea that similar problems have similar solutions.
