
### Research Prototypes for the Notes of the Unit 3 - Knowledge-Based Systems- AI Context in the Subject of INTELLIGENT DATABASE SYSTEM

1. Knowledge-based systems (KBS) are a type of artificial intelligence (AI) system that uses a knowledge base of facts and rules to solve problems.

2. KBSs are designed to represent and reason with the knowledge of a particular domain, such as medical diagnosis, legal reasoning, or engineering design.

3. KBSs can be used for a variety of tasks, including problem solving, planning, and natural language processing.

4. KBSs are composed of two main components: a knowledge base, which stores the knowledge, and a reasoning engine, which uses the knowledge to solve problems.

5. Research prototypes are KBSs that are developed to investigate the feasibility of a particular application or to evaluate the performance of a particular algorithm.

6. Research prototypes are typically developed using existing KBS software tools, such as CLIPS or Prolog, or by developing a custom KBS application.

7. Research prototypes are often used to test and evaluate the performance of an AI system in a particular domain, such as medical diagnosis or natural language processing.

8. Research prototypes can also be used to explore the potential of a particular AI technique in a specific domain, such as machine learning or neural networks.
