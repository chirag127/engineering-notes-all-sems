## Unit 3 - Knowledge-Based Systems- AI Context

In this unit, we will be discussing knowledge-based systems and their role in the larger context of Artificial Intelligence. Here are the key points to keep in mind:

1. Knowledge-based systems are a type of AI that uses a knowledge base to make decisions.
2. The knowledge base is a database of information that the system uses to reason and draw conclusions.
3. Knowledge-based systems are used in a wide variety of applications, including medical diagnosis, financial analysis, and customer service.
4. The main advantage of knowledge-based systems is their ability to handle complex, uncertain, and incomplete information.
5. The development of knowledge-based systems requires expertise in both AI and the domain of the problem being solved.
6. The knowledge base can be created manually or automatically, depending on the complexity of the problem and the availability of data.
7. Knowledge-based systems can be rule-based or case-based, depending on the type of reasoning used.
8. Rule-based systems use a set of if-then rules to make decisions, while case-based systems use historical cases to make decisions.
9. Knowledge-based systems can also use a combination of rule-based and case-based reasoning.
10. In addition to the knowledge base, knowledge-based systems may also include a user interface, an inference engine, and a knowledge acquisition system.

In conclusion, knowledge-based systems are an important part of the larger field of AI, and they have many practical applications. By understanding the principles of knowledge-based systems, we can develop more effective and efficient AI systems that can help solve complex problems in various domains.
