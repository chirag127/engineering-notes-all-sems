### Proposals for the notes of the Unit 5 - Applications in IDBS in the subject of INTELLIGENT DATABASE SYSTEM

- **Big Data Analytics**: Explain how IDBS is used to analyze and process large volumes of data, known as big data. Discuss the challenges of big data analytics, such as data integration, data quality, and scalability.

- **Applications of IDBS**: Provide examples of how IDBS is used in various industries, such as healthcare, finance, and retail. Discuss the benefits of using IDBS, such as improved decision-making, increased efficiency, and better customer service.
