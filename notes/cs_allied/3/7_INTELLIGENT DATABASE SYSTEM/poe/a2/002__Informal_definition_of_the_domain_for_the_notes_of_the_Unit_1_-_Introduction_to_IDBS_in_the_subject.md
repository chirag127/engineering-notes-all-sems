1. Intelligent Database System (IDBS) is a database system that has some intelligence in it and is capable of some smart activities like learning from experience, drawing inferences, explaining its predictions and decisions, handling imperfect and incomplete information, adapting to dynamic and unpredictable environments etc.
2. IDBS incorporates techniques like machine learning, natural language processing, fuzzy logic, expert systems etc. to provide intelligent behavior.
3. IDBS can understand complex queries posed in natural language and provide appropriate responses and results. It can make predictions and decisions based on historical data and patterns.
4. The key capabilities of IDBS are:
- Understanding natural language queries and commands
- Learning and predicting based on data
- Drawing inferences and decision making even with incomplete data
- Providing explanations and answers in natural language
- Adapting to changing environments and requirements
