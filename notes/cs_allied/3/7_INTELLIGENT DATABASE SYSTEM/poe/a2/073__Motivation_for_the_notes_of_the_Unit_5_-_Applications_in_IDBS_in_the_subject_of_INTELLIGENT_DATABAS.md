### Motivation for the notes of the Unit 5 - Applications in IDBS in the subject of INTELLIGENT DATABASE SYSTEM.

1. To understand the applications of Intelligent Database Systems (IDBS) in various domains.
2. To learn about the implementation of IDBS techniques in real-world scenarios.
3. To explore the benefits and limitations of applying IDBS to applications.
4. To comprehend how IDBS can enhance database systems and make them more efficient and effective.
5. To grasp the concepts of machine learning and data mining techniques used in IDBS applications.

The notes aim to provide an overview of how IDBS can be utilized in applications such as:

- Recommender Systems: To provide personalized recommendations to users based on their profiles and past behaviors.
- Fraud Detection: To identify anomalous transactions and suspicious patterns in data.
- Sentiment Analysis: To analyze opinions and sentiments expressed in user reviews and social media.
- Image Processing: To categorize and retrieve images based on their visual features.
- Text Mining: To extract insights from text data using techniques like classification, clustering, summarization, etc.

The applications of IDBS come with various benefits as well as limitations which will also be discussed in the notes. The notes will be formal and not contain any feelings, friendliness or emojis as required.
