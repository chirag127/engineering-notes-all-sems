### Research prototypes for the notes of the Unit 3 - Knowledge-Based Systems- AI Ccontext in the subject of INTELLIGENT DATABASE SYSTEM

1. Expert Systems: Expert systems are AI applications that contain encoded knowledge and decision-making skills of human experts. They consist of a knowledge base and an inference engine. The knowledge base contains domain knowledge and rules, while the inference engine applies the rules to the knowledge base to deduce new conclusions.

2. Fuzzy Systems: Fuzzy systems can handle imprecise or uncertain knowledge. They are based on fuzzy set theory, which allows partial membership in a set, rather than the crisp membership of conventional sets. Fuzzy systems have been applied to problems such as control, pattern recognition, decision making, and forecasting.

3. Genetic Algorithms: Genetic algorithms are search techniques inspired by natural evolution. They operate on a population of candidate solutions to a problem, selecting fitter solutions and generating new candidate solutions through crossover and mutation. Genetic algorithms are often used to optimize complex problems with large search spaces.

4. Machine Learning: Machine learning is a field of AI that focuses on the development of computer systems that can change when exposed to new data. Machine learning algorithms build a mathematical model based on sample data, known as "training data", in order to make predictions or decisions without being explicitly programmed to perform the task. Machine learning algorithms are often categorized as supervised learning, unsupervised learning, or reinforcement learning.
