### Basic concepts for the notes of the Unit 5 - Applications in IDBS in the subject of INTELLIGENT DATABASE SYSTEM

1. Expert Systems
- Expert systems are computer programs that contain stored knowledge and inferencing capabilities that provide expert advice in a specific domain.
- They are designed to solve problems in a narrow area that normally requires human expertise.
- The knowledge is represented in the form of rules and constraints.
- The inferencing engine uses the rules and constraints to deduce new conclusions from known facts.

2. Decision Support Systems
- Decision Support Systems (DSS) are interactive computer-based systems that help decision makers solve unstructured problems.
- A DSS aids the process of decision making, but it does not necessarily make the decision for the user.
- DSS are primarily based on databases and models.
- They use data mining, statistical analysis, and other techniques to retrieve relevant information for decision making.

3. Database Applications
- There are several applications of database such as:
--Banking: For maintaining accounts, loans, deposits, etc.
--Airline reservations: For booking and scheduling flights and maintaining passenger records.
--University registrations: For maintaining student and course records.
--Sales and inventory: For maintaining sales and purchase records and managing inventory.
- Database applications are designed to store and manipulate data for various business and organizational activities.
