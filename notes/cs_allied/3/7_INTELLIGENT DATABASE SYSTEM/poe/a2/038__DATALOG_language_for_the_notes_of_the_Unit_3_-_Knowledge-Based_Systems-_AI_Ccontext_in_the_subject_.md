### DATALOG language

- DATALOG is a declarative logic programming language used to represent knowledge bases.
- It is a subset of Prolog and has a simple syntax.
- In DATALOG, facts and rules are represented in the form of relationships/predicates.
- Facts are of the form 'predicate(argument1, argument2, ...)' which state that the relationship holds between the arguments.
- Rules are of the form 'head :- body' where head is a predicate and body is a conjunction of atomic formulas (facts and predicates). It means that if body is true, then head must also be true.
- Variables begin with capital letters and constants begin with lowercase letters.
- DATALOG programs can be executed using a backward chaining algorithm to infer new facts.
- Some advantages of DATALOG are simplicity, understandability, and efficient implementations and query evaluations.
- However, it has some limitations like inability to express recursion and inability to handle negation efficiently.

The above notes cover the key points about DATALOG language which is used to represent knowledge in knowledge-based systems. The syntax, elements, execution, advantages, and limitations of DATALOG have been discussed.
