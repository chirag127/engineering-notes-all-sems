### The 'tight coupling' approach for the notes of the Unit 4 - Advanced Knowledge-Based Systems in the subject of INTELLIGENT DATABASE SYSTEM.

1. Tightly coupled systems: In these systems, the inference engine and the knowledge base are integrated tightly. The knowledge base is represented using data structures and algorithms suited for efficient retrievals and inferences.

2. The inference procedures and the knowledge representation formalisms are designed together to exploit the efficiencies possible due to the tight integration. Typically, specialized search and retrieval procedures and data structures are used for efficient inferencing.

3. Examples of tightly coupled systems are rule-based expert systems using RETE networks, logic-based systems using binary resolution, and frame-based systems using data structures tailored for efficient frame retrievals.

4. The advantage of tight coupling is efficiency due to customized data structures and algorithms. The disadvantage is that the systems are harder to build and less flexible. Modifications to the knowledge base may require modifications to the inference engine and vice versa.

5. Examples of tightly coupled systems are OPS5, KEE and ART. OPS5 uses a RETE network to implement efficient rule-based inferences. KEE and ART use frames and frame-based methods for efficient inferences.
