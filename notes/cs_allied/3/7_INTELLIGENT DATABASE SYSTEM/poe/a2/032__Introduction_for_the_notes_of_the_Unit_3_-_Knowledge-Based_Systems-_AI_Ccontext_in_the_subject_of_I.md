### Introduction for the notes of the Unit 3 - Knowledge-Based Systems- AI Ccontext in the subject of INTELLIGENT DATABASE SYSTEM:

1. Knowledge-Based Systems: Systems that use knowledge, encoded as facts and rules, to solve problems in a specific domain. They contain a knowledge base and an inference engine.

2. Knowledge Base: A structured representation of knowledge as facts and rules in a specific domain. It is a collection of declarative statements about a domain which are accepted as true.

3. Inference Engine: The software component that interprets the knowledge in the knowledge base and draws conclusions from it. It matches facts and patterns of facts against the rules in the knowledge base to reach conclusions, make inferences, and solve problems.

4. Expert Systems: A type of knowledge-based system that contains expertise and skills of human experts in a specific domain so that the system can provide expert-level decisions or perform tasks like a human expert. They are designed to solve complex, specialized problems in a narrow domain.

5. AI Context: The field of artificial intelligence that focuses on designing systems that can mimic human intelligence. AI systems are powered by algorithms and machine learning techniques that allow them to learn from data, recognize patterns, infer knowledge, and perform human-like tasks such as decision making, speech recognition, language translation, etc.
