### Conclusion for the notes of the Unit 3 - Knowledge-Based Systems- AI Ccontext in the subject of INTELLIGENT DATABASE SYSTEM

1. Knowledge-based systems are computer systems that are built on expert knowledge and artificial intelligence techniques.
2. They contain a knowledge base that represents expert knowledge in a reusable form.
3. The knowledge base is constructed by knowledge engineers in consultation with domain experts.
4. The inference engine uses the knowledge in the knowledge base and infers new conclusions from it.
5. The components of a knowledge-based system are the knowledge base, the inference engine, the user interface, and the explanation module.
6. The characteristics of knowledge-based systems are narrow domains, dependence on knowledge, and expensive development.
7. The advantages of knowledge-based systems are providing expertise, reducing time, and enhancing productivity.
8. The applications of knowledge-based systems are in medical diagnosis, technical troubleshooting, financial analysis, and education.
9. The limitations of knowledge-based systems are that the expertise is limited, knowledge acquisition is difficult, and performance can be inefficient.
