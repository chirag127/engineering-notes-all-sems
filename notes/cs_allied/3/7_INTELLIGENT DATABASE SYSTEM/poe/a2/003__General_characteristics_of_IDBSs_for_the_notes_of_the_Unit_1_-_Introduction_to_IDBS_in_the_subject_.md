### General characteristics of IDBSs for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to IDBS in the subject of INTELLIGENT DATABASE SYSTEM

1. IDBSs have inference capabilities which allow them to derive new facts from the available data. They can make inferences, generalizations, and even learn from experience.
2. IDBSs have explanation facilities which provide explanations or reasons for their inferences and decisions. They can explain how they derived a particular inference or why they made a particular decision.
3. IDBSs have learning abilities which allow them to modify their knowledge structures based on new data or results. They can adapt to changes in the domain or improve their performance over time based on experience.
4. IDBSs have knowledge representation structures to represent knowledge. The knowledge is represented in the form of rules, frames, semantic networks, conceptual graphs, etc.
5. IDBSs have a mechanism to acquire knowledge. The knowledge can be provided by domain experts or can be learned by the system from data using machine learning techniques.
