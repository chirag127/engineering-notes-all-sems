### The CYC project for the notes of the Unit 4 - Advanced Knowledge-Based Systems in the subject of INTELLIGENT DATABASE SYSTEM.

1. The CYC project is an AI knowledge base project started in 1984 by Douglas Lenat.
2. The goal of CYC project is to encode common sense knowledge into machine-readable format to enable AI systems to make inferences and decisions like humans.
3. The knowledge in CYC is represented in the form of CycL - a formal language designed for the project.
4. The knowledge in CYC includes facts, rules of thumb, and heuristics about the world that most people know but rarely state explicitly. For example, knowing that things fall downwards, liquids take the shape of their containers, etc.
5. The sheer scale of world knowledge to be encoded makes the CYC project challenging and time-taking. Still, millions of assertions have been encoded in the knowledge base which can be upgraded continuously.
6. The encoded knowledge in CYC can be used by various AI applications to draw inferences, answer questions, and take decisions in an intelligent manner. The knowledge can be customized and extended as per the requirements.
7. The CYC project demonstrates the importance of world knowledge in achieving human-level intelligence and the difficulties in encoding such common sense knowledge in a machine-usable format.
