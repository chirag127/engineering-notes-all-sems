### Basic concepts for the notes of the Unit 3 - Knowledge-Based Systems- AI Ccontext in the subject of INTELLIGENT DATABASE SYSTEM

1. Knowledge-Based Systems: Systems that use artificial intelligence techniques to solve problems that normally require human expertise. They contain a knowledge base and an inference engine.

2. Knowledge Base: A repository of knowledge used by a knowledge-based system. It contains facts and rules about a specific domain. The knowledge is represented in a manner suitable for processing by the inference engine.

3. Inference Engine: The component of a knowledge-based system that applies the knowledge in the knowledge base to solve given problems. It draws conclusions from the knowledge base in a logical and automated manner.

4. Expert Systems: A type of knowledge-based system that contains knowledge and heuristics of human experts. They are designed to solve complex problems in a specific domain. They can perform at the level of a human expert or even exceed human expertise.

5. Fuzzy Systems: A type of knowledge-based system that can reason on uncertain or imprecise information. They employ fuzzy logic which represents degrees of truth rather than crisp true/false values. They are suitable for problems that involve imprecision or vagueness.
