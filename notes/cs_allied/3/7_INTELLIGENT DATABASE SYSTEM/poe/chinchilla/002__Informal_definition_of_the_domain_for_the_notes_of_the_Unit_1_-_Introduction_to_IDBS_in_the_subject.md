### Informal Definition of the Domain for the Notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to IDBS in the Subject of Intelligent Database System

In this unit, we will be introducing the concept of Intelligent Database Systems (IDBS) and how they differ from traditional database systems. Here are some key points to keep in mind:

- IDBS are a type of database system that uses artificial intelligence (AI) techniques to improve the efficiency and effectiveness of data management.
- They are designed to handle large amounts of complex data and provide intelligent insights and predictions based on that data.
- IDBS can be used in a variety of domains, including healthcare, finance, and e-commerce, among others.
- They can help organizations make better-informed decisions by providing real-time analysis of data and identifying patterns and trends.
- IDBS can also be used for predictive modeling, which involves using data to predict future outcomes or trends.
- These systems can employ various AI techniques such as machine learning, natural language processing, and computer vision to extract insights from data.
- IDBS can also handle unstructured data, such as text and images, and use AI to extract meaning from that data.
- Some common examples of IDBS include recommendation systems, fraud detection systems, and predictive maintenance systems.
- IDBS have the potential to revolutionize the way organizations store and use data, leading to improved decision-making and increased efficiency.

Remember to stay focused and engaged throughout this unit as we delve deeper into the world of Intelligent Database Systems.
