## Unit 3 - Knowledge-Based Systems- AI Context

In this unit, we will discuss knowledge-based systems and their role in the context of artificial intelligence (AI). Knowledge-based systems are a type of AI system that uses knowledge to perform tasks that would normally require human intelligence. These systems are designed to reason and make decisions based on the knowledge they have been provided or have acquired through experience.

Here are the main topics we will cover in this unit:

1. Introduction to Knowledge-Based Systems:
    * Definition of knowledge-based systems
    * Components of knowledge-based systems
    * Advantages and disadvantages of knowledge-based systems

2. Knowledge Representation:
    * Types of knowledge representation
    * Semantic networks
    * Frames
    * Rules
    * Ontologies

3. Reasoning:
    * Types of reasoning
    * Forward chaining
    * Backward chaining
    * Fuzzy logic
    * Bayesian networks

4. Expert Systems:
    * Definition of expert systems
    * Components of expert systems
    * Advantages and disadvantages of expert systems
    * Applications of expert systems

5. Natural Language Processing:
    * Definition of natural language processing
    * Components of natural language processing
    * Applications of natural language processing

6. Case-Based Reasoning:
    * Definition of case-based reasoning
    * Components of case-based reasoning
    * Advantages and disadvantages of case-based reasoning
    * Applications of case-based reasoning

7. Knowledge Acquisition:
    * Definition of knowledge acquisition
    * Knowledge acquisition techniques
    * Knowledge engineering

In summary, knowledge-based systems are an important subset of AI that uses knowledge to perform tasks that would normally require human intelligence. This unit covers the main components of knowledge-based systems, including knowledge representation, reasoning, expert systems, natural language processing, case-based reasoning, and knowledge acquisition. By the end of this unit, you should have a solid understanding of the concepts and applications of knowledge-based systems in the context of AI.
