### Introduction

Knowledge-Based Systems (KBS) are a type of Artificial Intelligence (AI) system that uses knowledge representation to solve complex problems. In the context of Intelligent Database Systems, KBS are used to extract knowledge from databases and use this knowledge to provide intelligent decision-making support. In this unit, we will explore the various aspects of KBS and their applications in the context of Intelligent Database Systems.

The following are the key topics that we will cover in this unit:

1. Knowledge Representation: We will explore the different methods of knowledge representation and how they can be used to model knowledge in databases. We will also discuss the advantages and disadvantages of different knowledge representation techniques.

2. Inference Engines: Inference engines are the heart of KBS. They provide a mechanism for reasoning and making decisions based on the knowledge stored in the database. We will discuss the different types of inference engines and how they can be used to make intelligent decisions.

3. Expert Systems: Expert systems are a type of KBS that use a knowledge base and inference engine to provide expert advice on a specific problem domain. We will explore the architecture of expert systems and how they can be used to solve complex problems.

4. Rule-Based Systems: Rule-based systems are a type of KBS that use a set of rules to make decisions. We will discuss the different types of rules and how they can be used to model knowledge in databases.

5. Case-Based Reasoning: Case-based reasoning is a type of KBS that uses past experiences to make decisions. We will explore the different components of case-based reasoning and how they can be used to solve complex problems.

6. Ontologies: Ontologies are a formal representation of knowledge that can be used to model complex domains. We will explore the different types of ontologies and how they can be used to model knowledge in databases.

In conclusion, the study of Knowledge-Based Systems in the context of Intelligent Database Systems is critical for providing intelligent decision-making support. By understanding the various aspects of KBS, we can model knowledge in databases and use this knowledge to solve complex problems. In this unit, we will explore the different techniques and architectures of KBS and their applications in the context of Intelligent Database Systems.
