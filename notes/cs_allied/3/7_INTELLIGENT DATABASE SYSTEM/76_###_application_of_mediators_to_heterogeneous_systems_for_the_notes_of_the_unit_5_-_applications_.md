### Application of mediators to heterogeneous systems for the notes of the Unit 5 - Applications in IDBS in the subject of INTELLIGENT DATABASE SYSTEM

The domain of Unit 5 - Applications in IDBS in the subject of Intelligent Database Systems focuses on the application of mediators to heterogeneous systems. Heterogeneous systems are systems that use different data models, data formats, and data sources, making it difficult to integrate and exchange data between them. A mediator is a software component that mediates between these systems, providing a unified view of the data and enabling data integration and exchange. 

The unit will cover the use of mediators in various applications, such as data warehousing, data integration, and data exchange, among others. The unit will also examine the various types of mediators, including data mediators, semantic mediators, and process mediators, among others. The unit will also cover the use of mediators in intelligent database systems, including the use of AI techniques to enhance the functionality and performance of mediators.

The goal of this unit is to provide students with a comprehensive understanding of the concepts, techniques, and tools associated with mediators, and to equip them with the skills needed to design, implement, and evaluate intelligent database systems that make use of these components.
