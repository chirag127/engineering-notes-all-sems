## Unit 4 - Advanced Knowledge-Based Systems

The Unit 4 - Advanced Knowledge-Based Systems focuses on more advanced concepts and techniques in the field of knowledge-based systems. Topics covered in this unit include:

1. Machine learning: the use of algorithms and statistical models to learn from data and make predictions or decisions.

2. Natural language processing: the use of algorithms and techniques to process and understand natural language text.

3. Knowledge discovery: the process of discovering new knowledge from data using techniques such as data mining and knowledge discovery.

4. Case-based reasoning: a type of reasoning that uses past experiences, or cases, to solve new problems.

5. Evolutionary algorithms: a type of algorithm that uses principles of evolution, such as natural selection and mutation, to solve problems.

In summary, Unit 4 - Advanced Knowledge-Based Systems covers advanced concepts and techniques in the field of knowledge-based systems, including machine learning, natural language processing, knowledge discovery, case-based reasoning, and evolutionary algorithms. These topics provide a deeper understanding of the capabilities and limitations of knowledge-based systems and their applications in various domains.
