### Proposals for the notes of the Unit 5 - Applications in IDBS in the subject of INTELLIGENT DATABASE SYSTEM

The domain of Unit 5 - Applications in IDBS in the subject of Intelligent Database Systems focuses on the various proposals and approaches for the applications of intelligent database systems. This unit will cover the latest research and development in the field of intelligent database systems, including topics such as data warehousing, data integration, and data exchange, among others. The unit will examine the various proposals for the use of intelligent database systems in real-world applications, such as e-commerce, healthcare, and financial services, among others.

The unit will also explore the use of AI techniques, such as machine learning, natural language processing, and expert systems, among others, to enhance the functionality and performance of intelligent database systems. The goal of this unit is to provide students with a comprehensive understanding of the latest developments and trends in the field of intelligent database systems, and to equip them with the knowledge and skills needed to design and implement intelligent database systems that meet the needs of real-world applications.
