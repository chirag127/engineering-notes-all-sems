### Introduction for the notes of the Unit 3 - Knowledge-Based Systems- AI Ccontext in the subject of INTELLIGENT DATABASE SYSTEM

Unit 3 - Knowledge-Based Systems- AI Context covers the introduction to knowledge-based systems and their role in the context of artificial intelligence. Knowledge-based systems are computer systems that use knowledge to solve problems and make decisions.

The following are key aspects of knowledge-based systems in the AI context:

1. Knowledge Representation: Knowledge representation is the process of representing knowledge in a form that can be processed by a computer system. This includes the use of formal languages and ontologies to represent knowledge.

2. Expert Systems: Expert systems are knowledge-based systems that mimic the decision-making abilities of a human expert in a particular domain. Expert systems use knowledge representation and reasoning techniques to solve problems and make decisions.

3. Natural Language Processing (NLP): Natural language processing (NLP) is a field of AI that deals with the processing of human language. NLP is used in knowledge-based systems to process and understand natural language inputs.

4. Reasoning: Reasoning is the process of using knowledge to solve problems and make decisions. Reasoning is a critical component of knowledge-based systems, as it allows the system to use its knowledge to solve problems and make decisions.

By understanding the introduction to knowledge-based systems, organizations can effectively use these systems to solve problems and make decisions in the context of artificial intelligence. Unit 3 - Knowledge-Based Systems- AI Context covers the use of knowledge-based systems, including knowledge representation, expert systems, NLP, and reasoning, in the context of artificial intelligence.
