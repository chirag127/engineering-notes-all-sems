### Temporal databases for the notes of the Unit 5 - Applications in IDBS in the subject of INTELLIGENT DATABASE SYSTEM

Temporal databases are databases that store time-based data, such as historical data, events, and changes over time. They are designed to capture and manage the evolution of data over time, and provide a way to track changes and analyze trends.

The main features of temporal databases include:

1. Time-based data: Temporal databases store data that is associated with a specific time or period of time.

2. Historical data: Temporal databases provide a way to store and manage historical data, so that it can be used for analysis and reporting.

3. Time-based queries: Temporal databases support time-based queries, so that data can be retrieved based on specific time periods or events.

4. Temporal data models: Temporal databases use specialized data models that are designed to capture and manage time-based data.

5. Versioning: Temporal databases provide a way to track changes to data over time, so that previous versions can be retrieved and analyzed.

Temporal databases are used in a variety of applications, including financial, healthcare, and retail. They are particularly useful for applications that require the tracking of changes over time, such as stock prices, patient health records, or sales data.

By using temporal databases, organizations can gain a deeper understanding of their data and make more informed decisions based on historical trends and patterns. This can help organizations to improve their operations, reduce costs, and drive innovation.
