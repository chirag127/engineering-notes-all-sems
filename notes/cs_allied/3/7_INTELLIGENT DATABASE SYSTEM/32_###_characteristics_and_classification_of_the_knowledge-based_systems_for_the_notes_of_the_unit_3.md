### Characteristics and classification of the knowledge-based systems for the notes of the Unit 3 - Knowledge-Based Systems- AI Ccontext in the subject of INTELLIGENT DATABASE SYSTEM

Unit 3 - Knowledge-Based Systems in the context of AI covers the characteristics and classification of knowledge-based systems. The following are key aspects of knowledge-based systems:

1. Characteristics: Knowledge-based systems have several key characteristics, including the use of a knowledge base, the use of inference rules, and the ability to make decisions based on the knowledge in the system.

2. Knowledge Base: A knowledge base is a collection of information that is used by a knowledge-based system to make decisions. The knowledge base can include facts, rules, and heuristics.

3. Inference Rules: Inference rules are used by knowledge-based systems to make decisions. These rules specify how the system should use the knowledge in the knowledge base to make decisions.

4. Decision Making: Knowledge-based systems are designed to make decisions based on the knowledge in the system. They use inference rules to determine the best course of action based on the knowledge in the knowledge base.

5. Classification: Knowledge-based systems can be classified based on the type of knowledge they use, including rule-based systems, expert systems, and case-based systems.

By understanding the characteristics and classification of knowledge-based systems, organizations can effectively use these systems to support decision making and problem solving.
