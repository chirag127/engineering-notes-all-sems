### Common Approaches to Real Time Scheduling for the notes of the Unit 2 - Real Time Scheduling in the subject of Real Time System

Common Approaches to Real-Time Scheduling:
- Real-time scheduling is the process of allocating resources to tasks in a real-time system to meet their timing constraints.
- There are several common approaches to real-time scheduling, including:
  1. Fixed Priority Scheduling: tasks are assigned a priority and are executed in order of priority.
  2. Earliest Deadline First (EDF): tasks are executed in order of their deadline.
  3. Rate Monotonic Scheduling (RMS): tasks are assigned a period and are executed in order of their period.
  4. Least Slack Time (LST): tasks are executed in order of the amount of time remaining until their deadline.
  5. Hybrid Scheduling: a combination of multiple scheduling algorithms is used to allocate resources to tasks.
- The choice of scheduling approach depends on the specific requirements of the system and the nature of the workload.
- Understanding the common approaches to real-time scheduling is important for the design and development of real-time systems that can meet their timing constraints and for the implementation of effective scheduling algorithms.
