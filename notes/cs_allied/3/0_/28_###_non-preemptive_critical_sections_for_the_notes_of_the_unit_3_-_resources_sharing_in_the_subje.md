### Non-preemptive Critical Sections for the notes of the Unit 3 - Resources Sharing in the subject of Real Time System

Non-Preemptive Critical Sections in Real-Time Systems:
- In real-time systems, critical sections are regions of code that access shared resources and must be protected from concurrent access.
- Non-preemptive critical sections are critical sections that cannot be interrupted by other tasks.
- Non-preemptive critical sections are used to ensure that shared resources are accessed in a predictable and consistent manner.
- Non-preemptive critical sections can be protected using various synchronization mechanisms, such as semaphores, monitors, and message passing.
- Non-preemptive critical sections can lead to deadlocks, where tasks are blocked and cannot proceed, and to priority inversion, where lower priority tasks block the execution of higher priority tasks.
- Understanding non-preemptive critical sections is important for the design and development of real-time systems that can share resources effectively and for the implementation of effective synchronization mechanisms.
