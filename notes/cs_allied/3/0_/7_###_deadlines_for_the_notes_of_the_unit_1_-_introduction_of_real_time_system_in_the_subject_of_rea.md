### Deadlines for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction of Real Time System in the subject of Real Time System

Deadlines in Real-Time Systems:
- Deadlines are an important concept in real-time systems, where the timing of events is critical.
- In real-time systems, deadlines are used to specify the maximum amount of time that a task can take to complete.
- Deadlines are used to ensure that the system can meet its performance requirements, such as response time, accuracy, and reliability.
- Deadlines can be specified in absolute terms, relative to the start of the task, or relative to the completion of other tasks.
- Deadlines can be hard or soft, depending on the consequences of missing the deadline.
- Hard deadlines are deadlines that must be met, or the system will fail.
- Soft deadlines are deadlines that can be missed, but with a reduction in quality or performance.
- Understanding deadlines is important for the design and development of real-time systems that can meet their performance requirements and for the implementation of effective scheduling algorithms.
