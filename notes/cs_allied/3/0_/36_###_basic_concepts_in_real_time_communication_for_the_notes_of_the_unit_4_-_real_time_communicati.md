### Basic Concepts in Real time Communication for the notes of the Unit 4 - Real Time Communication in the subject of Real Time System

Basic Concepts in Real-Time Communication:
- Real-time communication is a critical component of real-time systems, where the timing of events is critical.
- Real-time communication provides the means for tasks in a real-time system to exchange data and coordinate their behavior.
- The basic concepts in real-time communication include:
  1. Message passing: the exchange of data between tasks using messages.
  2. Communication protocols: the rules and procedures used for communication between tasks.
  3. Synchronization: the coordination of the behavior of tasks to ensure that they work together effectively.
  4. Quality of Service (QoS): the characteristics of communication that determine its reliability and performance.
- Real-time communication is used in a variety of applications, including control systems, multimedia systems, and embedded systems.
- Understanding the basic concepts in real-time communication is important for the design and development of real-time systems that can exchange data effectively and meet their timing constraints.
