### Priority Driven Approach for the notes of the Unit 2 - Real Time Scheduling in the subject of Real Time System

Priority Driven Approach in Real-Time Scheduling:
- Priority driven approach is a method for scheduling tasks in real-time systems, where the priority of tasks is used to determine the order in which they are executed.
- In a priority driven approach, tasks are assigned a priority, and the task with the highest priority is executed first.
- The priority of tasks can be assigned based on various criteria, such as their deadlines, the importance of their results, or the criticality of their execution.
- Priority driven approach can be implemented using various algorithms, such as rate-monotonic scheduling, earliest deadline first scheduling, and least laxity first scheduling.
- Priority driven approach is a popular method for real-time scheduling due to its simplicity and its ability to handle hard real-time constraints.
- However, priority driven approach can also lead to priority inversion, where a lower priority task blocks the execution of a higher priority task.
- Understanding the priority driven approach is important for the design and development of real-time systems that can meet their performance requirements and for the implementation of effective scheduling algorithms.
