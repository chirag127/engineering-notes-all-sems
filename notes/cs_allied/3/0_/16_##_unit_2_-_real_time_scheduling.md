## Unit 2 - Real Time Scheduling

Unit 2 - Real-Time Scheduling:
- Unit 2 of the subject "Real-Time Systems" focuses on the design and implementation of real-time scheduling algorithms.
- Real-time scheduling is the process of allocating resources to tasks in a real-time system to meet their timing constraints.
- Unit 2 covers the following topics:
  1. Real-time scheduling algorithms, including rate-monotonic scheduling, earliest-deadline-first scheduling, and least-laxity-first scheduling.
  2. Scheduling analysis, including response-time analysis, schedulability analysis, and performance evaluation.
  3. Scheduling in practice, including scheduling in multi-processor systems, scheduling in distributed systems, and scheduling in embedded systems.
- Understanding real-time scheduling is important for the design and development of real-time systems that can meet their performance requirements and for the implementation of effective real-time scheduling algorithms.
