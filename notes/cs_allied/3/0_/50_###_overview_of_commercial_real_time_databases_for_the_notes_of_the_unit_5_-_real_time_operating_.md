### Overview of Commercial Real Time databases for the notes of the Unit 5 - Real Time Operating Systems and Databases in the subject of Real Time System

Overview of Commercial Real-Time Databases:
- Real-time databases are databases that support real-time data processing and are designed for use in real-time systems.
- Commercial real-time databases are databases that are commercially available and are used in real-world applications.
- Commercial real-time databases are designed to meet the performance and reliability requirements of real-time systems and to handle large amounts of data.
- Commercial real-time databases include features such as real-time data processing, high availability, scalability, and fault tolerance.
- Commercial real-time databases are used in a variety of applications, including control systems, financial systems, and embedded systems.
- Examples of commercial real-time databases include Oracle Real-Time Database, Microsoft SQL Server, and IBM DB2.
- Understanding the overview of commercial real-time databases is important for the design and development of real-time systems that can effectively manage and process data.
