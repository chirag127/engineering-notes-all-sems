Temporal Parameters of Real-time Workload:
- Temporal parameters are the time constraints that define the behavior of real-time workloads in a real-time system.
- The main temporal parameters of real-time workloads are:
  1. Deadline: the time by which a task must complete its execution.
  2. Period: the time between two consecutive releases of a task.
  3. Execution Time: the time required for a task to complete its execution.
  4. Response Time: the time between the release of a task and its completion.
- These parameters are used to define the behavior of real-time workloads and to ensure that the system meets its timing constraints.
- Understanding the temporal parameters of real-time workloads is important for the design and development of real-time systems that can meet their timing constraints and provide predictable performance.
- The temporal parameters of real-time workloads must be carefully managed to ensure that the system can meet its timing constraints and provide predictable performance.
