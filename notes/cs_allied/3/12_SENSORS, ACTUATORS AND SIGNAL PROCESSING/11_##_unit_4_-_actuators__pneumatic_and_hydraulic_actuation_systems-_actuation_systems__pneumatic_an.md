## Unit 4 - Actuators: Pneumatic and Hydraulic Actuation Systems- Actuation systems, Pneumatic and hydraulic systems, Directional Control valves, Pressure control valves, Cylinders, Servo and proportional control valves, Process control valves, Rotary actuators.

Actuators are devices that convert an input signal into a physical motion. They are an essential component of control systems and are used in a wide range of applications, including industrial process control, robotics, and automation.

Pneumatic and hydraulic actuation systems are two of the most common types of actuation systems. Pneumatic systems use compressed air to generate motion, while hydraulic systems use pressurized fluid to generate motion.

The following are some of the key components of pneumatic and hydraulic actuation systems:

1. Directional control valves: These valves control the direction of fluid flow in a hydraulic system, or the direction of air flow in a pneumatic system.

2. Pressure control valves: These valves control the pressure of fluid or air in a system.

3. Cylinders: These are the components that generate motion in a hydraulic or pneumatic system, by converting fluid or air pressure into linear motion.

4. Servo and proportional control valves: These valves are used to control the speed and position of a cylinder in a hydraulic or pneumatic system.

5. Process control valves: These valves are used to control the flow of fluid or air in a process control system.

6. Rotary actuators: These are actuators that generate rotary motion, and are used in applications where linear motion is not sufficient.

In conclusion, actuators play a critical role in control systems and are used in a wide range of applications. Pneumatic and hydraulic actuation systems are two of the most common types of actuation systems, and are used to convert an input signal into a physical motion.
