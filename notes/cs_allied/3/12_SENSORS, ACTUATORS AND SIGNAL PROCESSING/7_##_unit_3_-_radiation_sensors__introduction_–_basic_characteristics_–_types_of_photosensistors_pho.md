## Unit 3 - Radiation Sensors: Introduction – Basic Characteristics – Types of Photosensistors/Photo detectors– X-ray and Nuclear Radiation Sensors– Fiber Optic Sensors

Unit 3 of Radiation Sensors focuses on the use of radiation sensors in various applications. Radiation sensors are devices that detect and measure different types of radiation, including light, X-rays, and nuclear radiation.

The following topics are covered in this unit:

1. Introduction to Radiation Sensors: An overview of radiation sensors, including their working principle, applications, and limitations.

2. Basic Characteristics of Radiation Sensors: An introduction to the basic characteristics of radiation sensors, including sensitivity, linearity, and response time.

3. Types of Photosensors/Photo detectors: An overview of the different types of photosensors and photo detectors, including photodiodes, phototransistors, and photoresistors, among others.

4. X-ray and Nuclear Radiation Sensors: An introduction to the use of X-ray and nuclear radiation sensors in various applications, including medical imaging, security, and environmental monitoring.

5. Fiber Optic Sensors: An introduction to the use of fiber optic sensors in various applications, including temperature sensing, strain sensing, and pressure sensing, among others.

In conclusion, Unit 3 of Radiation Sensors provides a comprehensive overview of the use of radiation sensors in various applications. This knowledge is essential for engineers and technologists who want to design and implement effective radiation sensing systems for a wide range of applications.
