# KCS

KCS stands for Knowledge-Centered Service, a methodology that aims to improve service delivery and knowledge management in service organizations. Here are some key points about KCS:

- KCS is based on the principle that knowledge is a by-product of solving problems and that the knowledge should be captured, structured, and reused as part of the service process .
- KCS involves creating and maintaining knowledge articles that document the issues, causes, and solutions of the problems that customers, employees, or systems encounter .
- KCS encourages service agents to use the existing knowledge articles to solve problems, and to update or create new articles if the knowledge is missing or incomplete .
- KCS benefits service organizations by reducing costs, increasing productivity, improving customer satisfaction, and enhancing collaboration and innovation .
- KCS is not a tool or a software, but a set of practices and processes that can be implemented with various tools and platforms .
- KCS has four phases of adoption: adoption, proficiency, leverage, and leadership. Each phase has different goals, activities, and measures of success.
- KCS is supported by the Consortium for Service Innovation, a non-profit organization that develops and promotes best practices for service and support.
