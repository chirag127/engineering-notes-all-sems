# KCS (Knowledge-Centered Service)

KCS is a methodology designed to integrate knowledge creation and maintenance into the problem-solving process. It is a set of practices and techniques focused on knowledge as a key asset of the support organization. The main goals of KCS are to:

1. Improve the efficiency and effectiveness of the support organization by reducing the time to resolve issues.
2. Increase customer satisfaction by providing accurate and timely information.
3. Improve the quality of the knowledge base by capturing, structuring, and reusing knowledge.
4. Encourage collaboration and knowledge sharing among support staff.

KCS is based on the principle that knowledge should be captured and shared as close to the source as possible. This means that support staff are encouraged to create and update knowledge articles as part of their daily work. The knowledge base is then used to provide consistent and accurate information to customers.

KCS is not a one-size-fits-all solution and must be adapted to the specific needs and culture of the organization. It requires a commitment to continuous improvement and a willingness to change processes and behaviors.
