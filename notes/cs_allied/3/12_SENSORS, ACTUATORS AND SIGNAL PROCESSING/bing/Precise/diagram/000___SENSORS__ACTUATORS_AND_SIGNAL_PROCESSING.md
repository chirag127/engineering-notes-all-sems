# SENSORS, ACTUATORS AND SIGNAL PROCESSING

Sensors, actuators, and signal processing are three essential components of any control system. They work together to gather information, process it, and act on it to achieve a desired outcome.

1. **Sensors** are devices that detect and measure physical properties such as temperature, pressure, or light. They convert these properties into electrical signals that can be processed by a computer or control system.

2. **Actuators** are devices that convert electrical signals into physical actions. They can be used to control the movement of a machine, the flow of a fluid, or the position of a valve.

3. **Signal processing** is the process of analyzing and manipulating the signals generated by sensors. It can be used to filter out noise, extract useful information, or control the behavior of a system.

Together, these three components form the basis of many control systems, from simple thermostats to complex industrial processes. By gathering information about the environment, processing it, and acting on it, they enable systems to respond to changing conditions and achieve their desired outcomes.
