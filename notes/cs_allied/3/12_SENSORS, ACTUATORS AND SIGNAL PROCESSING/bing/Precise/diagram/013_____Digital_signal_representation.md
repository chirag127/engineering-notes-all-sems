### Digital Signal Representation

Digital signal representation refers to the use of discrete numerical values to represent information. This is in contrast to analog signals, which use continuous values to represent information.

1. Digital signals are typically represented using binary code, where each bit can have a value of either 0 or 1.
2. The use of digital signals allows for more efficient and accurate transmission and processing of information.
3. Digital signals are less susceptible to noise and interference than analog signals.
4. Digital signal processing techniques can be used to manipulate and analyze digital signals in various ways.
5. Digital signal representation is widely used in modern communication systems, computer systems, and other electronic devices.
