# Unit 2 - Thermal Sensors

## Introduction
Thermal sensors are devices that measure temperature or temperature gradients. They are used in a wide range of applications, including industrial processes, environmental monitoring, and medical diagnostics.

## Gas Thermometric Sensors
Gas thermometric sensors measure temperature by detecting changes in the pressure or volume of a gas. These sensors are commonly used in industrial and laboratory settings.

## Thermal Expansion Type Thermometric Sensors
Thermal expansion type thermometric sensors measure temperature by detecting changes in the physical dimensions of a material. These sensors are commonly used in thermostats and temperature gauges.

## Acoustic Temperature Sensor
Acoustic temperature sensors measure temperature by detecting changes in the speed of sound in a medium. These sensors are commonly used in industrial and laboratory settings.

## Dielectric Constant and Refractive Index Thermo-sensors
Dielectric constant and refractive index thermo-sensors measure temperature by detecting changes in the dielectric constant or refractive index of a material. These sensors are commonly used in industrial and laboratory settings.

## Helium Low Temperature Thermometer
Helium low temperature thermometers measure temperature by detecting changes in the properties of helium gas. These sensors are commonly used in cryogenic applications.

## Nuclear Thermometer
Nuclear thermometers measure temperature by detecting changes in the properties of atomic nuclei. These sensors are commonly used in research and laboratory settings.

## Magnetic Thermometer
Magnetic thermometers measure temperature by detecting changes in the magnetic properties of a material. These sensors are commonly used in research and laboratory settings.

## Resistance Change Type Thermometric Sensors
Resistance change type thermometric sensors measure temperature by detecting changes in the electrical resistance of a material. These sensors are commonly used in industrial and laboratory settings.

## Thermo-EMF Sensors
Thermo-EMF sensors measure temperature by detecting changes in the electromotive force (EMF) generated by a thermocouple. These sensors are commonly used in industrial and laboratory settings.

## Junction Semiconductor Types
Junction semiconductor types measure temperature by detecting changes in the properties of semiconductor junctions. These sensors are commonly used in electronic devices.

## Thermal Radiation Sensors
Thermal radiation sensors measure temperature by detecting the thermal radiation emitted by an object. These sensors are commonly used in industrial and laboratory settings.

## Quartz Crystal Thermo-electric Sensors
Quartz crystal thermo-electric sensors measure temperature by detecting changes in the properties of a quartz crystal. These sensors are commonly used in precision temperature measurement applications.

## NQR Thermometry
NQR thermometry measures temperature by detecting changes in the nuclear quadrupole resonance (NQR) of a material. These sensors are commonly used in research and laboratory settings.

## Spectroscopic Thermometry
Spectroscopic thermometry measures temperature by detecting changes in the spectral properties of a material. These sensors are commonly used in research and laboratory settings.

## Noise Thermometry
Noise thermometry measures temperature by detecting changes in the noise generated by a material. These sensors are commonly used in research and laboratory settings.

## Heat Flux Sensors
Heat flux sensors measure temperature by detecting the heat flux between two materials. These sensors are commonly used in industrial and laboratory settings.
