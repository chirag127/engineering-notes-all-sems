# Mechanical Actuation Systems

- Mechanical actuation systems are used in countless applications where automated control is needed, such as manufacturing, robotics, aerospace and defense.
- Mechanical actuation systems use energy to move or change the position of an object, usually by converting one kind of motion into another .
- Mechanical actuation systems are controlled by some type of electronic controller, which activates the actuator when it receives a signal from an input device.

## Types of motion

- There are two main types of motion in mechanical actuation systems: linear and rotary.
- Linear motion is the movement of an object along a straight line, such as a piston or a slider.
- Rotary motion is the movement of an object around a fixed point, such as a wheel or a shaft.
- Linear and rotary motions can be combined or converted using various mechanisms, such as gears, cams, belts, chains, etc.

## Kinematic chains

- A kinematic chain is a sequence of rigid bodies connected by joints that allow relative motion between them.
- A kinematic chain can be classified as open or closed, depending on whether the first and last bodies are connected or not.
- A kinematic chain can also be classified as serial or parallel, depending on whether the bodies are arranged in a single line or in multiple branches.
- A kinematic chain can be used to transmit motion and force between different parts of a mechanical actuation system, such as a robot arm or a vehicle suspension.

## Cams

- A cam is a rotating or sliding piece that has a curved or irregular shape, which pushes or pulls a follower along a predefined path.
- A cam can be used to convert rotary motion into linear or oscillating motion, or to vary the speed or direction of motion.
- A cam can also be used to control the timing or sequence of events in a mechanical actuation system, such as a valve or a switch.
- A cam can have different profiles, such as circular, elliptical, sinusoidal, etc., depending on the desired motion of the follower.

## Gears

- A gear is a rotating wheel that has teeth or cogs on its rim, which mesh with another gear or a rack to transmit motion and torque.
- A gear can be used to change the speed, direction, or magnitude of motion in a mechanical actuation system, such as a motor or a gearbox.
- A gear can also be used to synchronize or coordinate the motion of multiple parts in a mechanical actuation system, such as a clock or a differential.
- A gear can have different shapes, such as spur, helical, bevel, worm, etc., depending on the angle and orientation of the teeth.

## Ratchet and pawl

- A ratchet and pawl is a mechanism that consists of a toothed wheel and a spring-loaded lever, which allows motion in one direction only.
- A ratchet and pawl can be used to prevent backdriving or reverse motion in a mechanical actuation system, such as a winch or a jack.
- A ratchet and pawl can also be used to increment or count the motion of a part in a mechanical actuation system, such as a meter or a counter.
- A ratchet and pawl can have different configurations, such as linear, rotary, or bidirectional, depending on the direction and mode of operation.

## Belt and chain drives

- A belt and chain drive is a mechanism that consists of a flexible loop of material or a series of linked segments, which wraps around two or more pulleys or sprockets to transmit motion and force.
- A belt and chain drive can be used to transfer motion and torque between distant or parallel shafts in a mechanical actuation system, such as a conveyor or a bicycle.
- A belt and chain drive can also be used to adjust the speed or tension of motion in a mechanical actuation system, such as a fan or a sewing machine.
- A belt and chain drive can have different types, such as flat, V, timing, or roller, depending on the shape and material of the loop or the segments.
