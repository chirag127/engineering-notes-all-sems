# KCS

## What is KCS?

- KCS stands for Knowledge-Centered Support.
- It is a methodology to provide consistent technical support through reusable, organized knowledge base.
- The knowledge base contains solutions to common problems which can be searched and accessed by support agents to resolve customer issues.
- This reduces repetitive work and provides fast solutions leading to improved customer satisfaction.

## Key principles of KCS

1. Capture knowledge - Technical solutions and insights from support interactions are captured and added to the knowledge base.
2. Structure knowledge - The knowledge base is well-organized with proper categories and search capabilities to easily retrieve relevant content.
3. Reuse knowledge - Support agents first search the knowledge base for solutions and reuse the content to resolve customer issues instead of recreating the same solutions.
4. Improve knowledge - The knowledge base is continuously improved based on feedback and as new solutions are added. Outdated content is removed.
5. Align with business - The knowledge and solutions align with business goals and the overall technical strategy.

## Benefits of KCS

- Increased speed of issue resolution leading to higher customer satisfaction
- Reduced repetitive work for support agents enabling them to handle more issues
- Consistent and accurate solutions leading to greater efficiency and productivity
- Scalable system to manage growth - new solutions and agents can be onboarded easily
- Living and evolving knowledge base keeping content updated with continuous learning
