### Sensors Applications: Introduction

- Sensors are used in various applications to detect and measure physical and chemical properties and convert them into electrical signals which can be measured and recorded.
- Some of the major applications of sensors are:

#### On-board Automobile Sensors (Automotive Sensors)

- Measure vehicle speed, wheel speed, engine speed, fluid levels, etc.
- Examples: Speed sensor, throttle position sensor, coolant temperature sensor, oxygen sensor, etc.

#### Home Appliance Sensors

- Measure temperature, motion, touch, humidity, etc. in home appliances like refrigerators, air conditioners, washing machines, etc.
- Examples: Temperature sensor, motion sensor, touch sensor, humidity sensor, etc.

#### Aerospace Sensors

- Measure acceleration, pressure, temperature, force, rotation, etc. in aircraft and spacecraft.
- Examples: Accelerometer, pressure sensor, temperature sensor, force sensor, rotation sensor, etc.

#### Sensors for Manufacturing

- Measure force, pressure, temperature, level, flow, etc. in manufacturing for process control and quality control.
- Examples: Force sensor, pressure sensor, temperature sensor, level sensor, flow sensor, etc.

#### Sensors for Environmental Monitoring

- Measure temperature, humidity, air quality, water quality, soil conditions, etc. for environmental monitoring applications.
- Examples: Temperature sensor, humidity sensor, air quality sensor, water quality sensor, soil moisture sensor, etc.
