## Unit 1 - Sensors / Transducers: Principles Classification, Parameters, Characteristics, Environmental Parameters (EP), Characterization.

1. Sensors / Transducers: Principles
- A sensor is a device that detects and responds to some type of input from the physical environment.
- The output is in the form of an electrical or optical signal.
- The input is called a stimulus and the output is called a response.
- The input-output relationship in a sensor can be linear or non-linear.
- Sensors have a certain range in which they are effective and outside this range they do not respond or respond inaccurately.

2. Sensors / Transducers: Classification
- Classification can be based on:
-- Physical effect used (e.g. piezoelectric, photoelectric, capacitive, inductive, etc.)
-- Transduction mechanism (e.g. thermistors are thermal, strain gauges are resistive)
-- Nature of the measured quantity (e.g. temperature sensors, pressure sensors, position sensors, etc.)
