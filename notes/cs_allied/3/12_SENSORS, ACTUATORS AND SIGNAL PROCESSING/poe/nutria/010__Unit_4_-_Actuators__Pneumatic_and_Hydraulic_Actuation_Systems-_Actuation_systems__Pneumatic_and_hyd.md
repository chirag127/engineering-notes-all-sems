
## Unit 4 - Actuators: Pneumatic and Hydraulic Actuation Systems

Actuation systems are used to control the motion of a machine or device. They can be powered by either pneumatic or hydraulic power.

### Pneumatic Actuation Systems

Pneumatic actuation systems use compressed air to power the motion of a machine or device. This type of system is used in applications where speed and accuracy are important. 

The main components of a pneumatic actuation system include a compressor, directional control valves, pressure control valves, cylinders, and rotary actuators.

#### Directional Control Valves

Directional control valves are used to control the direction of flow in a pneumatic system. They are typically actuated by a lever, push button, or solenoid.

#### Pressure Control Valves

Pressure control valves regulate the pressure of the air in a pneumatic system. They are typically actuated by a lever, push button, or solenoid.

#### Cylinders

Cylinders are used to convert the pressure of the air into linear motion. They can be single acting or double acting.

#### Servo and Proportional Control Valves

Servo and proportional control valves are used to regulate the flow rate of the air in a pneumatic system. They are typically actuated by a lever, push button, or solenoid.

#### Process Control Valves

Process control valves are used to regulate the flow rate of the air in a pneumatic system. They are typically actuated by a lever, push button, or solenoid.

### Hydraulic Actuation Systems

Hydraulic actuation systems use pressurized fluid to power the motion of a machine or device. This type of system is used in applications where speed and accuracy are important. 

The main components of a hydraulic actuation system include a pump, directional control valves, pressure control valves, cylinders, and rotary actuators.

#### Directional Control Valves

Directional control valves are used to control the direction of flow in a hydraulic system. They are typically actuated by a lever, push button, or solenoid.

#### Pressure Control Valves

Pressure control valves regulate the pressure of the fluid in a hydraulic system. They are typically actuated by a lever, push button, or solenoid.

#### Cylinders

Cylinders are used to convert the pressure of the fluid into linear motion. They can be single acting or double acting.

#### Servo and Proportional Control Valves

Servo and proportional control valves are used to regulate the flow rate of the fluid in a hydraulic system. They are typically actuated by a lever, push button, or solenoid.

#### Process Control Valves

Process control valves are used to regulate the flow rate of the fluid in a hydraulic system. They are typically actuated by a lever, push button, or solenoid.

#### Rotary Actuators

Rotary actuators are used to convert the pressure of the fluid into rotary motion. They are typically actuated by a lever, push button, or solenoid.
