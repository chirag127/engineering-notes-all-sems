

## Unit 3 - Radiation Sensors: Introduction

Radiation sensors are devices used to detect and measure radiation. They are used in a wide range of applications, such as medical imaging, nuclear power plant monitoring, and space exploration. Radiation sensors are typically made up of two components: a detector and a readout device.

### Basic Characteristics

Radiation sensors measure the amount of radiation present in a given area. The sensitivity of a radiation sensor is determined by its ability to detect a certain amount of radiation. The accuracy of a radiation sensor is determined by its ability to measure the radiation accurately.

### Types of Photosensors/Photo Detectors

Photosensors are radiation sensors that are sensitive to light. They are used in a variety of applications, such as night vision, surveillance, and environmental monitoring. Photosensors are typically made up of a photosensitive material, such as silicon, and a readout device.

### X-ray and Nuclear Radiation Sensors

X-ray and nuclear radiation sensors are used to detect and measure X-ray and nuclear radiation. These sensors are typically made up of a scintillator, such as a crystal or a gas, and a readout device.

### Fiber Optic Sensors

Fiber optic sensors are radiation sensors that are sensitive to light. They are used in a variety of applications, such as medical imaging, environmental monitoring, and space exploration. Fiber optic sensors are typically made up of a photosensitive material, such as a polymer or a glass, and a readout device.
