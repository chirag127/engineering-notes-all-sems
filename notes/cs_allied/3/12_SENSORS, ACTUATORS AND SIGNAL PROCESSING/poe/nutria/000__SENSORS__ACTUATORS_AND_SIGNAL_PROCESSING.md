
# SENSORS, ACTUATORS AND SIGNAL PROCESSING

* Sensors are devices used to measure physical characteristics such as temperature, pressure, light, and sound. They convert the physical characteristics into electrical signals which can be used to control other devices.

* Actuators are devices that convert electrical signals into mechanical motion. Common examples of actuators are motors, solenoids, and relays.

* Signal processing is the process of manipulating signals to extract useful information. It can involve filtering, amplifying, or modifying signals in some way. It is used in many fields, such as medical imaging, audio processing, and communications.
