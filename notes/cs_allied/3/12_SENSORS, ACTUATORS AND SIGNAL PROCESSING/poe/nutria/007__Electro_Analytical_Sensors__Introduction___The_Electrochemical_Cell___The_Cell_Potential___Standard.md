

### Electro Analytical Sensors: Introduction

- Electro analytical sensors are devices used to measure electrical potentials in chemical systems.
- They are used for a variety of purposes, including the measurement of pH, redox potentials, and the concentration of ions in solution.
- An electrochemical cell is composed of two electrodes, an anode and a cathode, which are separated by an electrolyte.
- The cell potential is the difference in electrical potential between the two electrodes.

### The Cell Potential

- The cell potential is the difference in electrical potential between the two electrodes.
- It is determined by the concentration of the ions in the electrolyte and the redox potential of the electrodes.
- The standard hydrogen electrode (SHE) is a reference electrode used to measure the cell potential.

### Standard Hydrogen Electrode (SHE)

- The standard hydrogen electrode (SHE) is a reference electrode used to measure the cell potential.
- It consists of a platinum wire immersed in a solution of hydrogen ions and has a potential of zero volts.
- The SHE is used as a reference point for measuring other cell potentials.

### Liquid Junction and Other Potentials

- The liquid junction potential is the difference in electrical potential between two solutions separated by a semipermeable membrane.
- This potential is due to the differences in the ionic concentration of the two solutions.
- Other potentials, such as the Nernst potential and the Galvani potential, are also used to measure the cell potential.

### Polarization

- Polarization is the tendency of a cell to lose its potential due to the flow of current.
- It occurs when the cell is subjected to a current, causing a decrease in the cell potential.
- Polarization can be minimized by increasing the cell area or reducing the current.

### Concentration Polarization

- Concentration polarization is the tendency of a cell to lose its potential due to the accumulation of ions at the electrodes.
- It occurs when the concentration of ions in the electrolyte is not uniform.
- It can be minimized by increasing the flow rate of the electrolyte or reducing the current.

### Reference Electrodes

- Reference electrodes are used to measure the cell potential.
- They are usually composed of a metal wire immersed in a solution of known ionic concentration.
- Reference electrodes are used to calibrate other electrodes and measure the cell potential.

### Sensor Electrodes

- Sensor electrodes are used to measure the cell potential in a variety of applications.
- They are usually composed of a metal wire or a semiconductor material, such as a metal oxide.
- Sensor electrodes are used to measure the pH, redox potential, and concentration of ions in solution.

### Electro Ceramics in Gas Media

- Electro ceramics are used to measure the cell potential in gas media.
- They are composed of a metal oxide, such as zirconia, which is used to measure the cell potential.
- Electro ceramics are used to measure the pH, redox potential, and concentration of gases in the atmosphere.
