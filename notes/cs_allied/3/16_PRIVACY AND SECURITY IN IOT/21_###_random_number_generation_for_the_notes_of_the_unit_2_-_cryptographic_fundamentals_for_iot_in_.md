The security of the encryption and decryption process depends on the quality of the random numbers used to generate the keys. There are a number of methods for generating random numbers, including hardware-based methods, software-based methods, and hybrid methods that use a combination of hardware and software. Hardware-based methods, such as true random number generators (TRNGs), are considered to be the most secure as they use physical processes, such as radioactive decay or thermal noise, to generate random numbers.

Software-based methods, such as pseudorandom number generators (PRNGs), use algorithms to generate random numbers. Although these methods are less secure than hardware-based methods, they are more practical for use in IoT devices, as they are less expensive and require less power.

In conclusion, random number generation is a critical component of cryptography in IoT, as it is used to generate keys for encrypting and decrypting data.
