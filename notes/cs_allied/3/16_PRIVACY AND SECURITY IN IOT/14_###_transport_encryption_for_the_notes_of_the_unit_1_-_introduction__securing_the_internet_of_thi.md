### Transport Encryption for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION: SECURING THE INTERNET OF THINGS in the subject of PRIVACY AND SECURITY IN IOT

Transport encryption is a security technique that is used to protect data as it is transmitted over a network. It involves encrypting data before it is transmitted and then decrypting it after it is received, making it more difficult for attackers to intercept and read the data.

In the context of the Unit 1 - Introduction: Securing the Internet of Things in the subject of Privacy and Security in IoT, transport encryption is particularly important, as it helps to ensure the privacy and security of sensitive information as it is transmitted between IoT devices and systems.

There are several types of transport encryption, including:

1. SSL/TLS: This is a widely used encryption protocol that is used to secure web traffic and other types of network traffic.

2. IPSec: This is a protocol that is used to secure IP traffic, and it is widely used in VPNs and other types of secure networks.

3. SSH: This is a protocol that is used to secure remote access and file transfers, and it is widely used in secure shell (SSH) connections.

To ensure the privacy and security of sensitive information as it is transmitted between IoT devices and systems, organizations should implement transport encryption and use secure communication protocols, such as SSL/TLS or IPSec.

Overall, transport encryption is a critical component of privacy and security in IoT, and it is important for organizations to understand the importance of secure communication protocols and to implement transport encryption to protect sensitive information as it is transmitted over networks. By understanding transport encryption, students can gain a deeper appreciation for the technology and its impact on privacy and security in IoT.
