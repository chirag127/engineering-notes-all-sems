### Threats to Access Control, Privacy, and Availability for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION: SECURING THE INTERNET OF THINGS in the subject of PRIVACY AND SECURITY IN IOT

The Internet of Things (IoT) involves the collection, storage, and exchange of large amounts of personal and sensitive information, making it critical to ensure the privacy, security, and availability of IoT devices and systems. However, IoT devices and systems are vulnerable to a variety of threats that can compromise access control, privacy, and availability.

Threats to Access Control:

1. Unauthorized Access: Unauthorized access is a threat to access control, as it allows unauthorized individuals to access sensitive information and systems.

2. Malicious Insiders: Malicious insiders, such as employees or contractors, can compromise access control by exploiting their access to sensitive information and systems.

3. Hacking: Hacking is a threat to access control, as it allows unauthorized individuals to gain access to sensitive information and systems.

Threats to Privacy:

1. Data Breaches: Data breaches are a threat to privacy, as they can result in the exposure of sensitive personal and financial information.

2. Identity Theft: Identity theft is a threat to privacy, as it can result in unauthorized access to sensitive information and financial accounts.

3. Surveillance: Surveillance is a threat to privacy, as it can result in the collection and use of sensitive personal information for malicious purposes.

Threats to Availability:

1. DDoS Attacks: DDoS attacks are a threat to availability, as they can result in the disruption of critical systems and services.

2. Malware: Malware is a threat to availability, as it can result in the compromise of critical systems and data.

3. Physical Attacks: Physical attacks are a threat to availability, as they can result in the destruction of critical systems and data.

In conclusion, the Internet of Things (IoT) involves the collection, storage, and exchange of large amounts of personal and sensitive information, making it critical to ensure the privacy, security, and availability of IoT devices and systems. IoT devices and systems are vulnerable to a variety of threats that can compromise access control, privacy, and availability, including unauthorized access, malicious insiders, hacking, data breaches, identity theft, surveillance, DDoS attacks, malware, and physical attacks.
