### Encryption and Decryption for the notes of the Unit 2 - CRYPTOGRAPHIC FUNDAMENTALS FOR IOT in the subject of PRIVACY AND SECURITY IN IOT

In the world of IoT, security is of utmost importance. Encryption and decryption are two essential techniques that can be used to ensure data security. Encryption is the process of converting plaintext into ciphertext, making it unreadable by anyone except those who have the key to decrypt it. Decryption, on the other hand, is the process of converting ciphertext back to plaintext using the key.

Here are some important points to keep in mind when it comes to encryption and decryption for IoT:

1. **Types of Encryption:** There are two types of encryption techniques - symmetric and asymmetric. In symmetric encryption, the same key is used for both encryption and decryption, while in asymmetric encryption, different keys are used for encryption and decryption.

2. **Key Exchange:** In asymmetric encryption, the process of exchanging keys securely is crucial. Public key cryptography is used to exchange keys securely between two parties. This involves the use of a public key and a private key. The public key is used for encryption, while the private key is used for decryption.

3. **Encryption Algorithms:** There are various encryption algorithms available that can be used for IoT. Some of the most popular ones include AES, RSA, and DES.

4. **Strength of Encryption:** The strength of encryption is determined by the key size. A larger key size means a stronger encryption. The key size should be chosen based on the level of security required.

5. **Decryption Process:** Decryption is the reverse process of encryption. The ciphertext is decrypted using the same key that was used for encryption. The decrypted text should be the same as the original plaintext.

6. **Encryption for IoT Applications:** Encryption is crucial for IoT applications where sensitive data is transmitted over the internet. It ensures that the data cannot be intercepted and read by unauthorized parties.

7. **Challenges with Encryption:** Encryption can be challenging for IoT because of the limited resources available on IoT devices. Encryption algorithms that are too complex can slow down the device's performance. It's important to choose an encryption algorithm that is suitable for the device's capabilities.

In conclusion, encryption and decryption are essential techniques for ensuring data security in IoT. It's important to choose the right encryption algorithm and key size to ensure the level of security required. As IoT devices become more prevalent, it's essential to ensure that they are secure and protected against potential threats.
