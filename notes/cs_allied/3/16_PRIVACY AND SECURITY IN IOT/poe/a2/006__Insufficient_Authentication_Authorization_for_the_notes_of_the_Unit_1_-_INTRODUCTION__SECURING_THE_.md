### Insufficient Authentication/Authorization

- Many IoT devices have weak or default passwords that are easy to guess. This allows unauthorized access to the devices.
- Some IoT devices do not have any authentication mechanism, allowing anyone to access the data and functionality.
- Even when authentication is present, authorization controls are often lacking. Anyone who authenticates can access all data and functionality, rather than being restricted to specific data and functions they should access.
- Without strong authentication and authorization, IoT devices can be compromised to spy on users, launch attacks on other systems, or manipulate devices in harmful ways.
- Recommendations:
  - Require strong, unique passwords or credentials for IoT devices.
  - Implement two-factor or multi-factor authentication when possible.
  - Implement strong authorization controls to restrict access to specific data and functionality.
  - Ensure credentials are securely stored and transmitted.
