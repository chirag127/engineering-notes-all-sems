### IoT Node Authentication

- IoT devices are heterogeneous and resource-constrained. Standard authentication mechanisms like passwords may not be feasible.
- Need lightweight authentication mechanisms for IoT nodes. Some options:
- Pre-shared keys: Devices and gateways share a secret key. Used for authentication and encryption. Scalability and key distribution issues.
- Certificates: Require public key infrastructure and certificate management. Resource-intensive for IoT nodes.
- Device-specific passwords: Generated passwords for IoT devices based on device properties like MAC address. Vulnerable to spoofing attacks.
- Physically unclonable functions (PUFs): Leverage unique physical properties of IoT devices to generate device-specific cryptographic keys. More efficient but hardware-dependent.
- Biometrics: Leverage physical characteristics of users or devices for authentication. Pose usability and security challenges for resource-constrained IoT nodes.
