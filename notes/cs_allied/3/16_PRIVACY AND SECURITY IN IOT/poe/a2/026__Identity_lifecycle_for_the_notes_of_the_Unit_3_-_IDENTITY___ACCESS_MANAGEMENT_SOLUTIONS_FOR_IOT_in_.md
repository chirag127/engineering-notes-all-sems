### Identity lifecycle for the notes of the Unit 3 - IDENTITY & ACCESS MANAGEMENT SOLUTIONS FOR IOT

1. Enrollment: The process of registering a new device or user and issuing credentials. During enrollment, an identity is established for the device or user along with the credentials they will use to authenticate.
2. Authentication: The process of verifying the identity of a device or user based on the credentials they present. Successful authentication allows access to protected resources.
3. Authorization: The process of determining what resources a device or user is allowed to access after they have been authenticated. Authorization is based on the access control rules and policies which map identities to the resources they can access.
4. Account linking: The process of associating multiple identities or credentials for the same device, user, or entity. This allows them to be recognized as the same identity using different methods. For example, linking a username and password to a certificate or biometric template.
5. Revocation: The process of invalidating credentials or removing identity relationships. Revocation ensures that identities and credentials that are no longer valid can no longer be used to access resources. Reasons for revocation include compromised credentials, deleted users, or decommissioned devices.

The above points cover the key steps in the identity lifecycle for IoT devices and users. Implementing a complete identity lifecycle allows IoT solutions to securely manage access to resources. Proper identity management is critical for privacy and security.
