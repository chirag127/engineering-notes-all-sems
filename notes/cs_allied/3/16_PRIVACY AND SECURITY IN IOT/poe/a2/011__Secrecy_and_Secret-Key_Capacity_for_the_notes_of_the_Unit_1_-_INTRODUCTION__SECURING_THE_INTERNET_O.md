### Secrecy and Secret-Key Capacity

- Secrecy refers to keeping information hidden from unintended recipients.
- Secret-key cryptography uses a shared key to encrypt and decrypt information. The key must be kept secret to maintain secrecy.
- The secret-key capacity refers to the amount of information that can be encrypted using a secret key before it needs to be changed. If too much information is encrypted with the same key, it becomes easier to crack the key and decrypt the information.
- In IoT devices with limited computing power and memory, secret keys must be relatively small. This limits the secret-key capacity and requires keys to be rotated frequently to maintain strong secrecy.
- Key rotation processes must be carefully designed and implemented to avoid vulnerabilities that could compromise secrecy.
