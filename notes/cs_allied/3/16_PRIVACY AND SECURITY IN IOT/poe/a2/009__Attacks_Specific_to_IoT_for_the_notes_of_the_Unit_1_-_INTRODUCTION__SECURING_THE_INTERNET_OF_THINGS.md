### Attacks Specific to IoT

1.Unauthorized Access: IoT devices often have weak authentication mechanisms which can be exploited to gain unauthorized access. Hackers can access the device and steal sensitive data or take control of the device.

2.Denial of Service (DoS): IoT devices are often not robustly designed and can be overwhelmed by floods of traffic. This can disrupt the functioning of the device and the network. DoS attacks on IoT devices can have physical consequences if the devices are critical infrastructure components.

3.Ransomware: Malware can infect IoT devices and encrypt their data and functions. Hackers can then demand ransom payments to decrypt the devices and restore functionality. This can have damaging consequences if the devices are part of critical infrastructure.

4.Eavesdropping: The data transmitted between IoT devices can often be eavesdropped on due to lack of encryption. This can compromise personal information and privacy. Sensitive data should always be encrypted when transmitting between IoT devices.

5.Botnets: IoT devices with vulnerabilities can be compromised and added to botnets to conduct DDoS attacks or other malicious activities at a large scale. The huge number of IoT devices present a major risk in this regard. IoT devices should be securely configured and patched to mitigate this risk.
