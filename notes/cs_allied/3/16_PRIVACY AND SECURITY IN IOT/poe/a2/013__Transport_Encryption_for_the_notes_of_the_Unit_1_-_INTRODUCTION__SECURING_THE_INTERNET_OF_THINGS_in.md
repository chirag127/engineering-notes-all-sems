### Transport Encryption

Transport encryption is used to encrypt the data being transmitted between two endpoints. This protects the data from eavesdropping and man-in-the-middle attacks.

Some key points about transport encryption:

- It operates on the transport layer (layer 4) of the OSI model.
- It encrypts the entire data packet being transmitted.
- It uses encryption algorithms like AES and RSA to encrypt the data.
- It uses protocols like TLS and DTLS to handle the encryption and decryption of data.
- It requires the endpoints to have pre-shared keys or certificates to establish secure communication channels.
- It protects the data only during transit but not at rest (when stored). Additional mechanisms like filesystem encryption must be used to protect data at rest.

Transport encryption is critical for securing IoT data transmission and ensuring privacy of sensitive information. Protocols like TLS must be used with strong ciphers and keys to make brute-force and other attacks infeasible.
