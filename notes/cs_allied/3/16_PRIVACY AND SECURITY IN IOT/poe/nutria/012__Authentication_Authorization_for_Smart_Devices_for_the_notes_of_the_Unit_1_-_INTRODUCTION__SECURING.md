
### Authentication/Authorization for Smart Devices

1. Authentication is the process of verifying the identity of a user or device before granting access to a network or system.
2. Authorization is the process of granting or denying access to a network or system based on the user or device's identity.
3. Authentication and authorization are essential components of any secure system, and are especially important for smart devices connected to the Internet of Things (IoT).
4. Authentication can be implemented using a variety of methods, such as passwords, biometrics, or two-factor authentication.
5. Authorization is typically done by assigning roles and permissions to users or devices.
6. To ensure security, authentication and authorization must be implemented correctly and regularly monitored.
