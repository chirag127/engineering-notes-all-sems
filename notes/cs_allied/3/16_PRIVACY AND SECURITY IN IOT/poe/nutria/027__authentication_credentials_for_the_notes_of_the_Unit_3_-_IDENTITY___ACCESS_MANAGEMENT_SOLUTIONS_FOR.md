
### Authentication Credentials for Identity & Access Management Solutions for IoT

1. Authentication credentials are the means by which a user or device is identified and granted access to a system or network.
2. Authentication credentials can be static, such as passwords, or dynamic, such as biometric data.
3. The use of authentication credentials is essential for ensuring the security of IoT networks and systems.
4. Authentication credentials should be unique and difficult to guess.
5. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is recommended for IoT systems, as it adds an extra layer of security.
6. MFA requires multiple authentication methods, such as a password and a biometric scan.
7. Authentication credentials should be changed regularly to prevent unauthorized access.
8. Access control lists (ACLs) can be used to restrict access to certain parts of an IoT system.
9. Access control lists should be regularly reviewed and updated to ensure that only authorized users have access to the system.
10. Policies and procedures should be in place to ensure that authentication credentials are securely stored and managed.
