
### Concerns in Data Dissemination for the Notes of the Unit 4 - Privacy Preservation and Trust Models for IoT in the Subject of Privacy and Security in IoT

1. Data privacy and security are essential components of IoT systems that must be addressed.
2. The data collected by IoT devices can be used for malicious purposes, such as identity theft or data manipulation.
3. Unauthorized access to data collected by IoT devices can lead to data leakage and abuse of personal information.
4. Data collected by IoT devices is often stored in the cloud, which increases the risk of data breaches.
5. The use of encryption and authentication can help protect data collected by IoT devices from unauthorized access.
6. Privacy preserving models, such as differential privacy, can be used to protect the data collected by IoT devices from being used for malicious purposes.
7. Trust models can be used to ensure that data collected by IoT devices is used in an appropriate manner.
8. Data collected by IoT devices should be stored securely and only accessed when necessary.
9. Appropriate measures should be taken to ensure that the data collected by IoT devices is not misused or abused.
