
### Cryptographic Controls Built into IoT Messaging and Communication Protocols

1. Cryptography is the practice of using algorithms to encrypt and decrypt data. It is used to protect data from unauthorized access and to ensure the integrity of the data.

2. Cryptographic controls are used to secure IoT messaging and communication protocols. These controls can include authentication, data encryption, digital signatures, and key management.

3. Authentication is used to ensure that only authorized users can access the data. This can be achieved through the use of passwords, biometrics, or other forms of authentication.

4. Data encryption is used to protect data from unauthorized access. It involves transforming the data into a form that is unreadable by unauthorized users.

5. Digital signatures are used to verify the identity of the sender and the integrity of the message. They are created using a cryptographic key and can be used to authenticate the sender and verify the message.

6. Key management is used to manage the keys used for encryption and decryption. It involves the secure storage, distribution, and use of the keys.

7. Cryptographic controls are essential for ensuring the security of IoT messaging and communication protocols. They help to protect the data from unauthorized access and ensure the integrity of the data.
