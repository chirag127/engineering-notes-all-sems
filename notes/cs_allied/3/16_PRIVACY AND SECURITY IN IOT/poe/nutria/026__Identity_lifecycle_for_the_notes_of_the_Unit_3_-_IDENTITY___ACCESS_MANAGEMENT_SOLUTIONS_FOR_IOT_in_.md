

### Identity Lifecycle for the Notes of the Unit 3 - IDENTITY & ACCESS MANAGEMENT SOLUTIONS FOR IOT in the Subject of PRIVACY AND SECURITY IN IOT

1. Identity lifecycle is the process of creating, managing, and revoking user identities within an organization. 
2. It is a key part of an organization's security strategy and is essential for protecting digital assets. 
3. The identity lifecycle consists of four main stages: creation, authentication, authorization, and revocation. 
4. Creation involves creating a unique identity for each user and assigning an authentication mechanism to establish their identity. 
5. Authentication is the process of verifying the identity of the user. 
6. Authorization is the process of granting access to resources based on the user's identity. 
7. Finally, the revocation stage involves revoking access to resources when the user no longer needs them. 
8. Identity and access management solutions for IoT can help organizations manage identity lifecycles by providing automated identity management and access control. 
9. These solutions can help organizations create, authenticate, authorize, and revoke user identities, as well as monitor user activity to ensure that users are only accessing resources that they are authorized to access.
