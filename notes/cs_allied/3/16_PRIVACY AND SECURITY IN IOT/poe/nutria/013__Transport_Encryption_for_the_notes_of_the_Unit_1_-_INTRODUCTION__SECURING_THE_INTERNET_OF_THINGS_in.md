
### Transport Encryption

- Transport encryption is a type of encryption used to protect data in transit. It is used to secure communication between two or more parties over a network.

- It is used to protect data from being intercepted by malicious actors, such as hackers, and to ensure that the data remains confidential.

- Transport encryption is used in a variety of applications, including web browsing, email, messaging, and file transfers.

- Transport encryption is implemented using a variety of protocols, such as Secure Sockets Layer (SSL) and Transport Layer Security (TLS).

- The use of transport encryption is essential for protecting the privacy and security of data in the Internet of Things (IoT). It ensures that data is not intercepted by malicious actors and remains confidential.
