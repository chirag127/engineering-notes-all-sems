

### Vulnerabilities for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION: SECURING THE INTERNET OF THINGS in the subject of PRIVACY AND SECURITY IN IOT

1. Unencrypted communication: Unencrypted communication between devices can lead to interception and manipulation of data, which can lead to serious security threats.

2. Lack of authentication: Without proper authentication, it is difficult to verify the identity of the device that is sending data. This can lead to malicious actors sending false or malicious data.

3. Insecure APIs: APIs that are not properly secured can allow for access to sensitive data or control of devices by malicious actors.

4. Insufficient logging: Without proper logging of activities, it is difficult to detect malicious activity or identify the source of a security breach.

5. Unpatched software: Outdated or unpatched software can contain known vulnerabilities that can be exploited by malicious actors.

6. Poor physical security: Poor physical security can lead to the theft of devices, which can lead to data breaches or malicious control of devices.
