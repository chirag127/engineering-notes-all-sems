
### Hashes for the Notes of the Unit 2 - Cryptographic Fundamentals for IoT in the Subject of Privacy and Security in IoT

1. Hashes are one-way cryptographic functions that take an input of any length and produce a fixed-length output.
2. Hashes are used to verify the integrity of data and to detect any changes made to the data.
3. Hashes are also used to store passwords in a secure manner, by storing the hash of the password instead of the actual password.
4. Hashes are typically used in conjunction with digital signatures, to ensure that the data has not been tampered with.
5. Hashes are also used in IoT to ensure the authenticity of data being transmitted across networks.
6. Popular hashing algorithms used in IoT include SHA-256, SHA-512, and MD5.
7. It is important to note that hashes are not reversible and therefore cannot be used to decrypt data.
