

### Authorization with Publish / Subscribe schemes

* Publish/subscribe schemes are a type of authorization method used in identity and access management (IAM) solutions for IoT.
* This type of authorization allows data to be published to a system and then subscribed to by other users or devices.
* This allows data to be shared securely and efficiently, while also allowing access to be easily managed and controlled.
* In a publish/subscribe scheme, the publisher is responsible for authorizing access to the data, while the subscriber is responsible for ensuring that the data is only used for its intended purpose.
* Publish/subscribe schemes are used in many different types of IAM solutions, including authentication, authorization, and access control.
* They can also be used to provide secure communication between devices, as well as secure data storage and retrieval.
