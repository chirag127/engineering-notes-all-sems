### Encryption and Decryption for the notes of the Unit 2 - CRYPTOGRAPHIC FUNDAMENTALS FOR IOT in the subject of PRIVACY AND SECURITY IN IOT

In the world of IoT, security and privacy are crucial aspects that need to be taken into consideration. Encryption and decryption are essential techniques used to ensure data security and privacy in IoT networks. Here are some important points to understand about encryption and decryption:

- Encryption is the process of converting plain text into cipher text, which is unreadable without the proper decryption key.
- Decryption is the process of converting cipher text back into plain text using the decryption key. 
- There are two types of encryption: symmetric and asymmetric.
- Symmetric encryption uses the same key for both encryption and decryption. This means that the sender and the receiver must have access to the same key.
- Asymmetric encryption, on the other hand, uses two keys: a public key for encryption and a private key for decryption. The public key can be shared with anyone, while the private key must be kept secret.
- Some commonly used encryption algorithms in IoT include Advanced Encryption Standard (AES), Rivest-Shamir-Adleman (RSA), and Elliptic Curve Cryptography (ECC).
- To ensure data security, it is important to use a strong encryption algorithm and to keep the encryption key secure.
- Decryption is the reverse process of encryption, and it requires the proper decryption key to convert the cipher text back into plain text.
- In IoT networks, encryption and decryption are used to secure data transmission between devices, secure data storage, and ensure the privacy of user data.
- However, encryption and decryption can also have a performance impact on IoT devices due to the computational power required to perform these operations. As such, it is important to balance security and performance considerations when implementing encryption and decryption in IoT networks.

Overall, encryption and decryption are important techniques used to ensure data security and privacy in IoT networks. By understanding how these techniques work, IoT developers can implement effective security measures to protect users' data and ensure the overall security of IoT networks.
