### Access Control for the notes of the Unit 3 - IDENTITY & ACCESS MANAGEMENT SOLUTIONS FOR IOT in the subject of PRIVACY AND SECURITY IN IOT

1. Access control is a security technique that regulates who or what can view or use resources in a computing environment.
2. It is a fundamental concept in security that minimizes risk to the business or organization.
3. There are two types of access control: physical and logical. Physical access control limits access to campuses, buildings, rooms and physical IT assets. Logical access control limits connections to computer networks, system files and data.
4. In the context of IoT, access control is crucial for ensuring the security and privacy of data collected and transmitted by IoT devices.
5. Access control mechanisms for IoT can include authentication, authorization, and accounting (AAA) frameworks, as well as encryption and secure key management.
6. Authentication verifies the identity of a user or device attempting to access a resource, while authorization determines whether the authenticated user or device has the necessary permissions to access the resource.
7. Accounting tracks and records user or device activity for auditing and compliance purposes.
8. Effective access control for IoT requires a comprehensive and multi-layered approach, taking into account the unique challenges and risks posed by the large number of connected devices and the potential for security vulnerabilities.
