### Security Requirements in IoT

The Internet of Things (IoT) is a rapidly growing network of connected devices that collect and exchange data. As the number of connected devices increases, so does the need for security. Here are some key requirements for IoT security:

1. **Device and data security**: This includes authentication of devices and ensuring the confidentiality and integrity of data.

2. **Security operations at IoT scale**: Implementing and running security operations at the scale of IoT is crucial.

3. **Compliance**: Meeting compliance requirements and requests is important for IoT security.

4. **Performance**: Meeting performance requirements as per the use case is also a key requirement for IoT security.

5. **Confidentiality and integrity of data**: Ensuring the confidentiality and integrity of data traveling between connected devices is important to make sure that data is not tampered with while it’s in transit between devices.

6. **Verifiable identity**: Providing each device a verifiable identity through digital certificates and signatures is a key security requirement for IoT.

7. **Security compliance from the beginning**: All IoT devices must be secure in their design. Therefore, infrastructure (servers, routers, and so on) and software must be coordinated.

These are some of the key requirements for IoT security. It is important to consider these requirements when designing and implementing IoT systems to ensure the security of the devices and the data they collect and exchange.
