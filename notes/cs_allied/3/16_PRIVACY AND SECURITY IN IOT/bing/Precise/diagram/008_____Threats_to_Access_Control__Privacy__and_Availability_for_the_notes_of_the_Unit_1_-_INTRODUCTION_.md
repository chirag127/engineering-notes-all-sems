### Threats to Access Control, Privacy, and Availability

#### Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION: SECURING THE INTERNET OF THINGS

1. **Access Control Threats**: Access control is the process of granting or denying specific requests to obtain and use information and related information processing services. Threats to access control can include unauthorized access, privilege escalation, and insider threats.

2. **Privacy Threats**: Privacy is the ability of an individual or group to seclude themselves or information about themselves, and thereby express themselves selectively. Threats to privacy can include data breaches, surveillance, and tracking.

3. **Availability Threats**: Availability refers to the ability of a system or service to be accessible and usable upon demand by an authorized entity. Threats to availability can include denial of service attacks, network congestion, and hardware or software failures.

These threats can compromise the security of the Internet of Things (IoT) and must be addressed to ensure the privacy and security of IoT devices and systems.
