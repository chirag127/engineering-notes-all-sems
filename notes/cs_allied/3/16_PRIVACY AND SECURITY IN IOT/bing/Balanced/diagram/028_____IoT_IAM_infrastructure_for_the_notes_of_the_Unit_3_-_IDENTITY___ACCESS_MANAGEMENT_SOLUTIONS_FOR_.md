### IoT IAM infrastructure

- IoT IAM infrastructure is the set of technologies and processes that enable identity and access management for IoT devices, data, and services.
- IoT IAM infrastructure aims to provide secure and scalable authentication, authorization, and auditing for IoT entities, such as devices, users, applications, and platforms.
- IoT IAM infrastructure typically relies on public key infrastructures (PKI) to generate, distribute, and manage cryptographic keys and certificates for IoT devices and services .
- PKI enables IoT devices and services to establish trust, verify identity, and encrypt and sign data using public and private keys and digital certificates.
- IoT IAM infrastructure also involves identity and access management services, such as AWS IoT Core, that provide APIs and tools to manage IoT identities, policies, and permissions .
- IoT IAM services allow IoT administrators to define and enforce granular access rules for IoT resources, such as topics, shadows, jobs, and streams, based on the identity and attributes of the requester.
- IoT IAM services also support federated identity and access management, which allows IoT devices and users to authenticate and authorize using external identity providers, such as social media platforms, mobile network operators, or enterprise directories .
- Federated IAM enables IoT devices and users to access multiple IoT platforms and services with a single identity, reducing the complexity and overhead of managing multiple credentials.
- IoT IAM infrastructure is essential for ensuring the security, privacy, and compliance of IoT systems, as it prevents unauthorized access, data breaches, and malicious attacks on IoT devices and services.
