## Unit 3 - IDENTITY & ACCESS MANAGEMENT SOLUTIONS FOR IOT

Unit 3 - Identity & Access Management Solutions for IoT in a subject focuses on the different technologies and solutions that are used to manage identities and access in the Internet of Things (IoT). This unit covers the following topics:

1. Identity Management: This includes an overview of the different identity management technologies and solutions that are used to manage identities in IoT, such as digital certificates, public key infrastructure (PKI), and biometric authentication.

2. Access Management: This includes an overview of the different access management technologies and solutions that are used to manage access to IoT devices and systems, such as role-based access control (RBAC), access control lists (ACLs), and attribute-based access control (ABAC).

3. Security protocols: This includes an overview of the different security protocols that are used to secure communication and transactions in IoT, such as Transport Layer Security (TLS), Secure Sockets Layer (SSL), and Internet Protocol Security (IPSec).

4. Threats and vulnerabilities: This includes an overview of the different threats and vulnerabilities that exist in IoT, such as unauthorized access, data breaches, and denial-of-service (DoS) attacks, and the solutions and technologies that are used to mitigate these threats.

5. Case studies: This includes an overview of real-world case studies that demonstrate the use of identity and access management solutions in IoT, and the impact these solutions have had on the security and privacy of IoT systems and devices.

By understanding the different technologies and solutions that are used to manage identities and access in IoT, students can gain a deeper appreciation for the technology and its impact on privacy and security in IoT. This understanding can help students to develop the skills necessary to design, implement, and maintain identity and access management solutions for IoT systems and devices.
