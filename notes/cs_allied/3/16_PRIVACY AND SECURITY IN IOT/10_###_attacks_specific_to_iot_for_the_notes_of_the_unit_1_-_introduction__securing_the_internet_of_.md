### Attacks Specific to IoT for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION: SECURING THE INTERNET OF THINGS in the subject of PRIVACY AND SECURITY IN IOT

Attacks specific to the Internet of Things (IoT) are security threats that are unique to IoT devices and systems. These attacks can compromise the security and privacy of IoT devices and systems, and can result in serious consequences, such as data theft, device compromise, and network disruption.

The following are some of the key attacks specific to IoT:

1. IoT botnets: An IoT botnet is a network of compromised IoT devices that are controlled by a malicious actor. These devices can be used to launch attacks, such as Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS) attacks, on other devices and systems.

2. IoT malware: IoT malware is malicious software that is designed to infect and compromise IoT devices. This malware can be used to steal sensitive information, compromise device functionality, and launch attacks on other devices and systems.

3. Man-in-the-Middle (MITM) attacks: A MITM attack is a type of attack in which an attacker intercepts and manipulates communication between two devices. In the context of IoT, this attack can be used to steal sensitive information, compromise device functionality, and launch attacks on other devices and systems.

4. IoT device hijacking: IoT device hijacking is the unauthorized control of an IoT device by a malicious actor. This can result in the compromise of sensitive information, the disruption of device functionality, and the launch of attacks on other devices and systems.

To protect against these and other attacks specific to IoT, it is important to implement security measures and best practices, such as secure communication protocols, secure device management, and secure data storage and transmission.

Overall, attacks specific to IoT are a critical concern in the Internet of Things, and it is important to understand these attacks and the measures that can be taken to protect against them. By understanding the attacks specific to IoT, students can gain a deeper appreciation for the importance of security in IoT and its impact on privacy and security.
