### Security Requirements in IoT for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION: SECURING THE INTERNET OF THINGS in the subject of PRIVACY AND SECURITY IN IOT

Security is a critical concern in the Internet of Things (IoT), as IoT devices are often connected to the internet and can be vulnerable to attacks and breaches. To ensure the security of IoT systems and devices, it is important to understand the security requirements and best practices for securing IoT.

The following are some of the key security requirements in IoT:

1. Confidentiality: Confidentiality refers to the protection of sensitive information from unauthorized access or disclosure. This is critical in IoT, as many IoT devices collect and transmit sensitive data, such as personal information, financial data, and health data.

2. Integrity: Integrity refers to the protection of data from unauthorized modification. This is important in IoT, as malicious actors may attempt to modify data to cause harm or steal sensitive information.

3. Availability: Availability refers to the ability of authorized users to access and use the system and its data. This is critical in IoT, as many IoT systems and devices are critical to the functioning of other systems and devices.

4. Authentication: Authentication is the process of verifying the identity of a user or device. This is important in IoT, as it helps to ensure that only authorized users and devices can access sensitive data and systems.

5. Authorization: Authorization is the process of granting access to specific resources or data based on the identity of the user or device. This is important in IoT, as it helps to ensure that only authorized users and devices can access sensitive data and systems.

To ensure the security of IoT systems and devices, it is important to implement security measures and best practices, such as secure communication protocols, secure data storage and transmission, and secure device management.

Overall, security is a critical concern in IoT, and it is important to understand the security requirements and best practices for securing IoT systems and devices. By understanding the security requirements in IoT, students can gain a deeper appreciation for the importance of security in IoT and its impact on privacy and security.
