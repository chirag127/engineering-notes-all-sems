IoT/M2M systems are composed of multiple layers that work together to provide a complete solution for connecting devices and transmitting data. These layers include:

1. Devices: The physical devices that are connected to the IoT network.

2. Network: The communication infrastructure that connects the devices and enables data transmission.

3. Data management: The systems and processes that are used to collect, store, and analyze data from the connected devices.

4. Applications: The software applications that are used to interact with the data and provide insights and information to users.

Design standardization is an important aspect of IoT/M2M systems, as it helps to ensure that devices and systems are interoperable and can work together seamlessly. Standardization helps to reduce the complexity of the system and makes it easier to design and implement connected devices.

Communication technologies are a critical component of IoT/M2M systems, as they enable devices to communicate with each other and with the network. There are several communication technologies that are used in IoT/M2M systems, including Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Zigbee, and cellular networks.

Data enrichment and consolidation are important aspects of IoT/M2M systems, as they help to provide a more complete and accurate picture of the data that is being collected from the connected devices. This data can be used to gain insights into the behavior and performance of the devices and the systems that they are connected to.

Ease of designing and affordability are also important considerations for IoT/M2M systems. The goal is to make it as easy as possible to design and implement connected devices, while also keeping the cost of the system as low as possible. This requires careful consideration of the components and technologies that are used in the system, as well as the design and implementation of the system itself.

Overall, the design of IoT/M2M systems is a complex and multi-disciplinary field that requires a deep understanding of the technology, the devices, and the data that is being collected and analyzed. By considering the layers and design standards of the system, organizations can ensure that their IoT/M2M systems are reliable, efficient, and effective.
