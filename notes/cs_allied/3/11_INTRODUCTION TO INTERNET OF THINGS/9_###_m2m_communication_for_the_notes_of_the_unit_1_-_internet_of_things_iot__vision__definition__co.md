### M2M Communication for the notes of the Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT): Vision, Definition, Conceptual Framework, Architectural view, technology behind IoT, Sources of the IoT, M2M Communication, IoT Examples. M2M (Machine-to-Machine) communication refers to the direct communication between devices without human intervention. In the context of the Internet of Things (IoT), M2M communication enables connected devices to exchange data and interact with each other to perform specific tasks. 

M2M communication is crucial for the functioning of IoT as it enables devices to work together seamlessly and perform complex tasks. M2M communication can be achieved through various communication technologies such as Wi-Fi, Zigbee, Bluetooth, NFC, and cellular networks. 

Design principles for connected devices in IoT/M2M systems include:
1. IoT/M2M systems layers and design standardization to ensure interoperability and compatibility between devices
2. Communication technologies to enable efficient and reliable data exchange between devices
3. Data enrichment and consolidation to improve the quality and value of the data generated by connected devices
4. Ease of designing and affordability to encourage widespread adoption and deployment of IoT/M2M systems

Examples of IoT include smart homes, connected vehicles, wearable devices, and industrial automation systems. These systems use M2M communication to exchange data and perform tasks such as controlling lighting, monitoring temperature, tracking location, and managing energy consumption.
