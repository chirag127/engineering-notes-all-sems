Unit 2 of Hardware for IoT in the subject of Introduction to Internet of Things covers the topic of wireless sensor networks. Wireless sensor networks are networks of wireless sensor nodes that are used to collect, process, and transmit data in IoT systems.

The following topics are covered in this unit:

1. Introduction to Wireless Sensor Networks: An overview of wireless sensor networks, including their working principle, applications, and limitations.

2. Network Architecture: An introduction to the different types of network architectures used in wireless sensor networks, including star, mesh, and hierarchical networks.

3. Communication Protocols: An overview of the different types of communication protocols used in wireless sensor networks, including Zigbee, Z-Wave, and Wi-Fi, among others.

4. Power Management: An introduction to the different methods and techniques used for power management in wireless sensor networks, including energy harvesting and power-saving modes.

5. Security: An overview of the security issues and challenges associated with wireless sensor networks, including data privacy, data integrity, and authentication, among others.

6. Deployment and Management: An introduction to the deployment and management of wireless sensor networks, including network configuration, network monitoring, and network maintenance.

In conclusion, Unit 2 of Hardware for IoT in the subject of Introduction to Internet of Things provides a comprehensive overview of wireless sensor networks, including their architecture, communication protocols, power management, security, and deployment and management. This knowledge is essential for designing and implementing wireless sensor networks that are capable of collecting, processing, and transmitting data in real-world IoT applications.
