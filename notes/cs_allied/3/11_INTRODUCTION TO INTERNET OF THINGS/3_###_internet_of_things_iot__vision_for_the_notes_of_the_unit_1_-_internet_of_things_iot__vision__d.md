Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT): Vision covers the vision, definition, conceptual framework, architectural view, technology behind IoT, sources of IoT, M2M communication, IoT examples, and design principles for connected devices.

The following are key aspects of IoT:

1. Vision: The vision for IoT is to create a world where physical objects are connected to the internet and can communicate with each other to improve our lives.

2. Definition: IoT refers to the network of physical objects that are connected to the internet and can communicate with each other.

3. Conceptual Framework: The conceptual framework for IoT includes the devices, networks, and services that make up the IoT ecosystem.

4. Architectural View: The architectural view of IoT includes the layers of the IoT stack, including the devices, networks, and services.

5. Technology Behind IoT: The technology behind IoT includes sensors, actuators, communication technologies, and cloud computing.

6. Sources of IoT: The sources of IoT include connected devices, such as smartphones and wearable devices, and the internet.

7. M2M Communication: M2M communication refers to communication between machines and devices in the IoT ecosystem.

8. IoT Examples: IoT examples include smart homes, connected cars, and industrial IoT.

9. Design Principles for Connected Devices: Design principles for connected devices include IoT/M2M systems layers and design standardization, communication technologies, data enrichment and consolidation, ease of designing and affordability.

By understanding these concepts, organizations can effectively understand the vision, definition, and design principles for IoT, and can effectively design and implement IoT solutions to improve our lives.
