### Architectural view for the notes of the Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT): Vision, Definition, Conceptual Framework, Architectural view, technology behind IoT, Sources of the IoT, M2M Communication, IoT Examples. The architectural view of the Internet of Things (IoT) describes the various components and layers that make up the IoT system. The following are the key components of the IoT architecture:

1. Devices: Physical objects that are embedded with electronics, software, and connectivity, such as smart home devices, wearable devices, and industrial machines.

2. Sensors and Actuators: Devices that collect data from the environment and control physical processes, such as temperature sensors and light switches.

3. Communication: The communication protocols and technologies that enable devices to connect and exchange data, such as Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and cellular networks.

4. Data Management: The process of collecting, storing, and analyzing data generated by IoT devices, including data storage, data processing, and data analysis.

5. Applications: The software applications that run on top of the IoT architecture, providing services such as remote monitoring, control, and automation.

In summary, the architectural view of the IoT describes the components and layers that make up the IoT system, including devices, sensors and actuators, communication, data management, and applications. These components work together to enable the collection, storage, analysis, and use of data generated by IoT devices.
