Unit 2 - Hardware for IoT focuses on the various hardware components that are used to build IoT systems. One of the key components of IoT systems are sensors, which are used to gather data from the physical world. Sensors can be analog or digital, and they measure various physical properties such as temperature, light, sound, and motion.

Digital sensors are a type of sensor that converts analog signals into digital signals. This allows the data generated by the sensor to be processed and analyzed by digital devices, such as computers and microcontrollers. Digital sensors are commonly used in IoT systems because they are more accurate and reliable than analog sensors, and they are easier to interface with digital devices.

In addition to sensors, IoT systems also use actuators, which are used to control physical devices. Actuators can be used to turn on and off lights, control motors, and perform other actions.

Radio Frequency Identification (RFID) technology is another important component of IoT systems. RFID is used to identify and track objects using radio waves. This technology is commonly used in supply chain management, inventory control, and asset tracking.

Wireless Sensor Networks (WSNs) are another important component of IoT systems. WSNs are networks of wireless sensors that are used to gather data from the physical world. WSNs are commonly used in environmental monitoring, industrial automation, and healthcare systems.

Participatory Sensing Technology is a type of IoT technology that involves the participation of individuals in gathering data. This technology is commonly used in environmental monitoring, crowd-sourced traffic monitoring, and other applications.
