## Unit 3 - Network & Communication aspects in IoT: Wireless Medium access issues, MAC protocol survey, Survey routing protocols, Sensor deployment & Node discovery, Data aggregation & dissemination

The network and communication aspects of the Internet of Things (IoT) are critical to the success of IoT systems. These aspects include the wireless medium access, MAC protocols, routing protocols, sensor deployment and node discovery, data aggregation and dissemination.

Wireless medium access is a key issue in IoT, as it determines how devices access the wireless network and communicate with each other. Different MAC protocols have been developed to address this issue, including carrier sense multiple access (CSMA), time division multiple access (TDMA), and frequency division multiple access (FDMA).

Routing protocols are another important aspect of IoT, as they determine how data is transmitted from one device to another. There are several routing protocols that have been developed for IoT, including ad hoc on-demand distance vector (AODV), dynamic source routing (DSR), and destination-sequenced distance-vector (DSDV).

Sensor deployment and node discovery are important aspects of IoT, as they determine how sensors are deployed and how nodes are discovered in the network. There are several approaches to sensor deployment and node discovery, including random deployment, grid deployment, and clustering.

Data aggregation and dissemination are also critical aspects of IoT, as they determine how data is collected and processed from the sensors, and how it is transmitted to the end-users. There are several approaches to data aggregation and dissemination, including data fusion, data compression, and data forwarding.

Overall, the network and communication aspects of IoT are complex and multi-disciplinary, and require a deep understanding of the technology, the devices, and the data that is being transmitted. By considering these aspects, organizations can ensure that their IoT systems are reliable, efficient, and effective.
