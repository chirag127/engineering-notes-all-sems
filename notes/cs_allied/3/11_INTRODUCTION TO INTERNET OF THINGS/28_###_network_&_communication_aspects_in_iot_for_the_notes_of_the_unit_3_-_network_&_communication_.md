### Network & Communication aspects in IoT for the notes of the Unit 3 - Network & Communication aspects in IoT: Wireless Medium access issues, MAC protocol survey, Survey routing protocols, Sensor deployment & Node discovery, Data aggregation & dissemination in the subject of INTRODUCTION TO INTERNET OF THINGS

The network and communication aspects of IoT play a critical role in enabling connected devices to communicate and exchange data. This involves the use of wireless communication technologies, such as Wi-Fi, Zigbee, and cellular networks, and the implementation of various protocols and techniques to support communication and data exchange.

The following are some of the key network and communication aspects of IoT:

1. Wireless Medium Access Issues: This refers to the challenges of managing access to the wireless medium, including issues such as interference, congestion, and limited bandwidth.

2. MAC (Media Access Control) Protocols: These protocols are used to manage access to the wireless medium, and include protocols such as CSMA/CA (Carrier Sense Multiple Access with Collision Avoidance) and TDMA (Time Division Multiple Access).

3. Routing Protocols: These protocols are used to route data between devices in a network, and include protocols such as AODV (Ad Hoc On-Demand Distance Vector) and DSR (Dynamic Source Routing).

4. Sensor Deployment & Node Discovery: This involves the deployment of sensors in a network, and the discovery of other nodes in the network.

5. Data Aggregation & Dissemination: This involves the aggregation of data from multiple sensors, and the dissemination of this data to other devices in the network.

Overall, the network and communication aspects of IoT play a critical role in enabling connected devices to communicate and exchange data, and are essential for the development of IoT applications and systems.
