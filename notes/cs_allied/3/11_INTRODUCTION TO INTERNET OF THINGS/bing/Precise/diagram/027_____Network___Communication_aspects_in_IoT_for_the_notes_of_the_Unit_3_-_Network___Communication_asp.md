### Network & Communication aspects in IoT

Unit 3 of the subject "Introduction to Internet of Things" covers the Network & Communication aspects in IoT. The following points are discussed in this unit:

1. **Wireless Medium access issues:** This topic covers the challenges and issues related to accessing the wireless medium in IoT networks. It includes topics such as interference, channel allocation, and energy efficiency.

2. **MAC protocol survey:** This topic provides an overview of the various Medium Access Control (MAC) protocols used in IoT networks. It covers protocols such as TDMA, CSMA/CA, and hybrid MAC protocols.

3. **Survey routing protocols:** This topic covers the various routing protocols used in IoT networks. It includes protocols such as AODV, DSR, and OLSR.

4. **Sensor deployment & Node discovery:** This topic covers the deployment of sensors in IoT networks and the discovery of nodes. It includes topics such as node localization, coverage, and connectivity.

5. **Data aggregation & dissemination:** This topic covers the aggregation and dissemination of data in IoT networks. It includes topics such as data fusion, data compression, and data dissemination protocols.

These are the main topics covered in Unit 3 of the subject "Introduction to Internet of Things". It is important to have a good understanding of these topics to have a strong foundation in the field of IoT.
