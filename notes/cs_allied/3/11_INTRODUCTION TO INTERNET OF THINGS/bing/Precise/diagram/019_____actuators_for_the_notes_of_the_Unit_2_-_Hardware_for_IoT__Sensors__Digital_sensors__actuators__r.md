### Actuators

Actuators are devices that convert electrical energy into mechanical energy. They are used to control or move mechanical systems. In the context of IoT, actuators are used to control or manipulate the physical environment based on the data collected by sensors.

There are several types of actuators, including:

1. **Linear actuators**: These actuators convert electrical energy into linear motion. They are commonly used in applications such as opening and closing valves or moving objects along a straight line.

2. **Rotary actuators**: These actuators convert electrical energy into rotational motion. They are commonly used in applications such as opening and closing doors or rotating objects.

3. **Electromagnetic actuators**: These actuators use electromagnetic fields to generate motion. They are commonly used in applications such as controlling the position of read/write heads in hard disk drives.

4. **Piezoelectric actuators**: These actuators use the piezoelectric effect to generate motion. They are commonly used in applications such as precision positioning or generating high-frequency vibrations.

Actuators are an essential component of IoT systems, as they allow the system to interact with and manipulate the physical environment. They are used in a wide range of applications, from home automation to industrial control systems.
