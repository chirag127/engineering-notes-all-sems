### Digital Sensors

Digital sensors are electronic devices that convert physical quantities such as temperature, humidity, pressure, or light intensity into digital signals that can be processed by a computer. These sensors are commonly used in IoT (Internet of Things) applications to collect data from the environment.

Some common types of digital sensors used in IoT include:

1. **Temperature sensors:** These sensors measure the temperature of the environment and convert it into a digital signal. Examples include thermistors and digital temperature sensors.

2. **Humidity sensors:** These sensors measure the amount of moisture in the air and convert it into a digital signal. Examples include capacitive and resistive humidity sensors.

3. **Pressure sensors:** These sensors measure the pressure of a fluid or gas and convert it into a digital signal. Examples include piezoresistive and capacitive pressure sensors.

4. **Light sensors:** These sensors measure the intensity of light and convert it into a digital signal. Examples include photodiodes and phototransistors.

Digital sensors are an essential component of IoT systems, as they allow the system to collect data from the environment and make decisions based on that data. They are commonly used in applications such as home automation, environmental monitoring, and industrial control.
