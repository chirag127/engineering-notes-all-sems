### Communication Technologies for IoT

Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT): Vision, Definition, Conceptual Framework, Architectural view, technology behind IoT, Sources of the IoT, M2M Communication, IoT Examples.

#### Design Principles for Connected Devices:

- IoT/M2M systems layers and design standardization: IoT systems are typically composed of multiple layers, including the device layer, the network layer, and the application layer. Standardization of these layers is important to ensure interoperability between different devices and systems.

- Communication technologies: Various communication technologies are used in IoT systems, including cellular, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and others. The choice of communication technology depends on factors such as range, power requirements, and data transfer rates.

- Data enrichment and consolidation: IoT devices generate large amounts of data, which must be enriched and consolidated to provide meaningful insights. This involves processing the data to extract relevant information and combining data from multiple sources.

- Ease of designing and affordability: IoT devices should be easy to design and affordable to produce, to enable widespread adoption of the technology.

#### M2M Communication:

Machine-to-Machine (M2M) communication refers to the exchange of data between two or more machines, without the need for human intervention. M2M communication is a key component of IoT systems, enabling devices to communicate with each other and with the cloud.

#### IoT Examples:

- Smart homes: IoT devices such as smart thermostats, lighting systems, and security systems can be used to automate and control various aspects of a home.

- Wearables: IoT-enabled wearable devices such as fitness trackers and smartwatches can be used to monitor health and fitness metrics.

- Smart cities: IoT technologies can be used to improve the efficiency and sustainability of urban environments, through applications such as smart traffic management and waste management.

- Industrial IoT: IoT technologies can be used to improve the efficiency and productivity of industrial processes, through applications such as predictive maintenance and supply chain management.
