### Sensors

Sensors are devices that detect and respond to changes in the environment. They are an essential component of the Internet of Things (IoT) as they enable devices to collect data from the physical world.

There are two main types of sensors: analog and digital. Analog sensors produce a continuous output signal that is proportional to the quantity being measured. Digital sensors, on the other hand, produce a discrete output signal that represents the measured quantity in binary form.

Actuators are devices that convert electrical signals into physical actions. They are often used in conjunction with sensors to control physical systems.

Radio Frequency Identification (RFID) technology is a wireless communication technology that uses radio waves to identify and track objects. It is commonly used in inventory management and supply chain management.

Wireless Sensor Networks (WSNs) are networks of spatially distributed sensors that are used to monitor environmental conditions. They are commonly used in applications such as environmental monitoring, agriculture, and healthcare.

Participatory sensing technology is a type of sensing technology that relies on the participation of individuals to collect data. It is commonly used in applications such as traffic monitoring and air quality monitoring.

Embedded platforms for IoT are hardware platforms that are designed to support the development of IoT applications. Some popular embedded platforms for IoT include Arduino, Netduino, Raspberry Pi, BeagleBone, Intel Galileo, and ARM Cortex. These platforms provide a range of features and capabilities that enable developers to build a wide variety of IoT applications.
