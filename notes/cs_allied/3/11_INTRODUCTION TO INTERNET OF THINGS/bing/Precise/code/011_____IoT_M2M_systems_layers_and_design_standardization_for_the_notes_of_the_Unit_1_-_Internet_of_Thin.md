### IoT/M2M systems layers and design standardization

IoT/M2M systems consist of several layers, each with its own set of design standards. These layers include:

1. **Device Layer:** This layer consists of the physical devices and sensors that collect data. Design standards for this layer focus on ensuring that devices are reliable, secure, and able to communicate with other devices and systems.

2. **Network Layer:** This layer is responsible for transmitting data from the devices to the cloud or other systems. Design standards for this layer focus on ensuring that data is transmitted securely and reliably.

3. **Cloud Layer:** This layer is responsible for storing and processing data collected by the devices. Design standards for this layer focus on ensuring that data is stored securely and can be accessed and processed quickly.

4. **Application Layer:** This layer is responsible for presenting data to users and allowing them to interact with the system. Design standards for this layer focus on ensuring that the user interface is intuitive and easy to use.

Design standardization is important in IoT/M2M systems to ensure that devices and systems from different manufacturers can communicate and work together seamlessly. This is achieved through the use of common communication protocols and data formats.

In addition to design standardization, other important design principles for connected devices include ease of designing and affordability. This means that devices should be easy to design and manufacture, and should be affordable for consumers to purchase and use. This can help to drive the adoption of IoT/M2M technologies and make them more accessible to a wider range of users.
