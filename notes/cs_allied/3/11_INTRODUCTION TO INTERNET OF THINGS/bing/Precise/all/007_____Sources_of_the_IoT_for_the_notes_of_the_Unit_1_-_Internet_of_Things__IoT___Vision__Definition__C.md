# Sources of the IoT

The Internet of Things (IoT) is a network of interconnected devices that can communicate with each other and with the internet. The sources of the IoT are the devices, sensors, and systems that make up this network. These sources can include:

1. **Sensors**: Sensors are devices that detect and measure physical properties such as temperature, humidity, light, and motion. They can be embedded in a wide range of devices, from home appliances to industrial machinery.

2. **Actuators**: Actuators are devices that convert electrical signals into physical actions, such as opening or closing a valve. They can be used to control the behavior of IoT devices.

3. **Smart devices**: Smart devices are electronic devices that can connect to the internet and interact with other devices. Examples include smartphones, smartwatches, and smart home appliances.

4. **Embedded systems**: Embedded systems are computer systems that are integrated into other devices or products. They can be used to control the behavior of IoT devices and to process data from sensors.

5. **Wireless communication technologies**: Wireless communication technologies such as Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and cellular networks are used to connect IoT devices to the internet and to each other.

6. **Cloud computing**: Cloud computing provides the infrastructure for storing and processing data from IoT devices. It enables the development of applications and services that can analyze and act on this data.

These sources work together to enable the IoT to collect, process, and act on data in real-time, providing new opportunities for automation, monitoring, and control.
