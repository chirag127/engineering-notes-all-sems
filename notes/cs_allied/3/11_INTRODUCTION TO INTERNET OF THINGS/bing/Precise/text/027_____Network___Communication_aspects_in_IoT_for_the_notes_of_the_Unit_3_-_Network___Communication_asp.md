### Network & Communication aspects in IoT

Unit 3 of the subject "Introduction to Internet of Things" covers the Network & Communication aspects in IoT. The following points are discussed in this unit:

1. **Wireless Medium access issues:** This topic covers the challenges and issues related to accessing the wireless medium in IoT networks. It includes the discussion of interference, congestion, and other factors that can affect the performance of wireless communication in IoT.

2. **MAC protocol survey:** This topic provides a survey of the various Medium Access Control (MAC) protocols used in IoT networks. It includes a discussion of the strengths and weaknesses of different MAC protocols and their suitability for different IoT applications.

3. **Survey routing protocols:** This topic provides a survey of the various routing protocols used in IoT networks. It includes a discussion of the strengths and weaknesses of different routing protocols and their suitability for different IoT applications.

4. **Sensor deployment & Node discovery:** This topic covers the methods and techniques used for deploying sensors and discovering nodes in IoT networks. It includes a discussion of the challenges and issues related to sensor deployment and node discovery in IoT.

5. **Data aggregation & dissemination:** This topic covers the methods and techniques used for aggregating and disseminating data in IoT networks. It includes a discussion of the challenges and issues related to data aggregation and dissemination in IoT.
