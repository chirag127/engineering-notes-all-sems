### Sensor deployment & Node discovery

Sensor deployment and node discovery are important aspects of network and communication in the Internet of Things (IoT). These processes involve the placement of sensors and the identification of nodes within a network.

1. **Sensor deployment:** This refers to the process of strategically placing sensors within an environment to collect data. The goal of sensor deployment is to ensure that the sensors are placed in locations that will provide the most accurate and useful data. Factors that can affect sensor deployment include the type of data being collected, the environment in which the sensors are being placed, and the power and connectivity requirements of the sensors.

2. **Node discovery:** This refers to the process of identifying and locating nodes within a network. In the context of IoT, nodes can refer to sensors, actuators, or other devices that are connected to the network. Node discovery is important for ensuring that all nodes within a network are able to communicate with each other and share data. This can be achieved through various methods, such as broadcasting messages or using a centralized directory service.

Both sensor deployment and node discovery are crucial for ensuring the effective operation of an IoT network. Proper sensor deployment can ensure that data is collected accurately and efficiently, while effective node discovery can facilitate communication and data sharing between nodes. These processes are essential for enabling the various applications and services that are made possible by IoT technology.
