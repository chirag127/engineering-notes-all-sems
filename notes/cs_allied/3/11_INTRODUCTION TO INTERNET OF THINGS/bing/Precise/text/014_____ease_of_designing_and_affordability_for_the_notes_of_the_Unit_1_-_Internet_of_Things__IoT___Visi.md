### Ease of Designing and Affordability for IoT Devices

- When designing connected devices for IoT applications, services, and business processes, the designer considers the ease of designing the device's physical, data link, adaptation layers, and gateway.
- IoT proves to be a valuable technology for many organizations and sectors. If organizations can develop and standardize certain aspects of the IoT architecture, they can tap into the full potential of their investments.
- A key feature of an IoT platform should be ease of use. It should be intuitive and easily accessible for both tech-savvy and not-so-technically sound workforce. The system must integrate with existing processes to provide maximum value.
- Scalability is a key factor to consider when designing IoT infrastructure. You should ask whether scaling up will require time-consuming configuration efforts or significant investments in new hardware.
- Affordance is an important design parameter that defines the physical quality of an object that suggests to a user the appropriate actions to use it.
