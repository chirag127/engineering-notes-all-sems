### Arduino Platform Boards Anatomy

1. **Microcontroller:** The microcontroller is the brain of the Arduino board. It is responsible for executing the code uploaded to the board. The most commonly used microcontroller in Arduino boards is the Atmel AVR microcontroller.

2. **Digital and Analog Pins:** The digital and analog pins are used to interface with external devices. The digital pins can be used for digital input or output, while the analog pins can be used for analog input or output.

3. **Power Supply:** The Arduino board can be powered through the USB connection or an external power supply. The external power supply can be an AC-to-DC adapter or a battery.

4. **USB Connection:** The USB connection is used to upload code to the board and to power the board. It can also be used for serial communication between the board and a computer.

5. **Reset Button:** The reset button is used to reset the microcontroller. Pressing the reset button will restart the code uploaded to the board.

6. **LEDs:** The Arduino board has several LEDs that can be used to indicate the status of the board. The most commonly used LED is the built-in LED connected to digital pin 13.

7. **Voltage Regulator:** The voltage regulator is used to regulate the voltage supplied to the board. It ensures that the board receives a stable voltage, even if the input voltage varies.

8. **Crystal Oscillator:** The crystal oscillator is used to generate a clock signal for the microcontroller. The clock signal is used to synchronize the operations of the microcontroller.

9. **Headers:** The headers are used to connect external devices to the board. The headers are typically male or female and can be used to connect sensors, actuators, and other devices to the board.
