### Design Principles for Connected Devices

Design principles for connected devices in the context of the Internet of Things (IoT) and Machine-to-Machine (M2M) communication are essential for creating effective and efficient systems. These principles include:

1. **IoT/M2M systems layers and design standardization:** It is important to have a standardized approach to the design of IoT/M2M systems, with clearly defined layers and interfaces. This allows for interoperability between different devices and systems, and makes it easier to develop and deploy new applications.

2. **Communication technologies:** The choice of communication technology is crucial for the performance and reliability of IoT/M2M systems. Factors to consider include range, bandwidth, power requirements, and security.

3. **Data enrichment and consolidation:** IoT/M2M systems generate large amounts of data, which must be processed and analyzed to extract useful information. Data enrichment and consolidation techniques can be used to improve the quality and usefulness of the data.

4. **Ease of designing and affordability:** The design of IoT/M2M systems should be as simple and straightforward as possible, to reduce the barriers to entry for developers and users. Additionally, the cost of the devices and systems should be kept as low as possible, to make them accessible to a wide range of users.

These design principles are important for creating effective and efficient IoT/M2M systems, and should be considered when designing and deploying connected devices.
