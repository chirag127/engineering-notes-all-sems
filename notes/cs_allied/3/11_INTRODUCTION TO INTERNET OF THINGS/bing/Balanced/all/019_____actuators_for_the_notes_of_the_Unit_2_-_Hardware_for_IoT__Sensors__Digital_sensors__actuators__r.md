# Actuators for IoT

- An actuator is a device that converts energy into motion. It does this by taking an electrical signal and combining it with an energy source.
- In IoT, actuators enable a physical action based on data that originates with one or more sensors. The conversion of sensor data to activity follows this sequence:
  - Sensors detect an event in the physical environment.
  - The sensor data is transmitted to a gateway or cloud platform.
  - The gateway or cloud platform processes the data and sends a command to the actuator.
  - The actuator executes the command and performs the desired action.
- Examples of actuators in IoT are :
  - Servo motors: These are rotary or linear actuators that allow for precise control of angular or linear position, velocity and acceleration. They are used for applications such as robotics, drones, cameras, etc.
  - Stepper motors: These are DC motors that move in discrete steps. They are used for applications such as 3D printers, CNC machines, etc.
  - DC motors: These are continuous rotation motors that can vary their speed and direction. They are used for applications such as fans, pumps, toys, etc.
  - Linear actuators: These are devices that convert rotational motion into linear motion. They are used for applications such as door locks, valves, switches, etc.
  - Thermal/magnetic actuators: These are actuated by thermal or mechanical energy. They are used for applications such as thermostats, circuit breakers, etc.
  - Mechanical actuators: These are devices that execute movement by converting rotary motion into linear motion. They are used for applications such as levers, gears, pulleys, etc.
  - Soft actuators: These are devices that deform or change shape in response to stimuli such as heat, light, electricity, etc. They are used for applications such as artificial muscles, soft robots, etc.
  - Light activated actuators: These are devices that respond to light intensity or wavelength. They are used for applications such as solar panels, optical switches, etc.
- Actuators are essential components of IoT systems as they enable interaction with the physical world and provide feedback to the users. They can also improve efficiency, safety, convenience, and quality of life.
