### IoT/M2M systems layers and design standardization

- IoT/M2M systems are composed of interconnected devices and applications that communicate and exchange data over the internet or other networks.
- IoT/M2M systems can be divided into three major domains: the application layer, the service layer, and the network layer .
- The application layer is the domain where the end-user interacts with the IoT/M2M system through various applications, such as smart home, smart city, smart health, etc. The application layer provides the user interface, the business logic, and the data analysis for the IoT/M2M system.
- The service layer is the domain where the IoT/M2M system provides common functionalities and services, such as device management, data management, security, discovery, orchestration, etc. The service layer enables interoperability and scalability of the IoT/M2M system across different devices, applications, and networks.
- The network layer is the domain where the IoT/M2M system connects the devices and applications through various communication technologies, such as cellular, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Zigbee, etc. The network layer provides the data transmission, routing, addressing, and quality of service for the IoT/M2M system.
- IoT/M2M systems layers and design standardization is the process of developing and adopting common protocols, interfaces, and architectures for the IoT/M2M system domains, in order to ensure compatibility, reliability, security, and efficiency of the IoT/M2M system.
- IoT/M2M systems layers and design standardization can be done at different levels, such as global, regional, national, or industry-specific, depending on the scope and objectives of the IoT/M2M system.
- IoT/M2M systems layers and design standardization can be done by different organizations, such as international standards bodies, industry consortia, research institutes, or government agencies, depending on the expertise and authority of the organization.
- Some examples of IoT/M2M systems layers and design standardization are:
  - oneM2M, a global initiative that develops service layer standards for IoT/M2M systems .
  - 3GPP, a global partnership that develops network layer standards for IoT/M2M systems, such as LTE-M, NB-IoT, and EC-GSM-IoT.
  - OGC, an international industry consortium that develops application layer standards for IoT/M2M systems, such as Sensor Web Enablement (SWE) and SensorThings API.
  - NIST, a US government agency that develops frameworks and guidelines for IoT/M2M systems, such as the Cyber-Physical Systems (CPS) Framework and the IoT-Enabled Smart City Framework.
