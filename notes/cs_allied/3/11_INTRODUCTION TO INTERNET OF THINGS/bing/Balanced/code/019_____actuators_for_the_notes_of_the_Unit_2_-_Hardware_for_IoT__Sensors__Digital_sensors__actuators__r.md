# Actuators for IoT

- An actuator is a device that converts energy into motion. It does this by taking an electrical signal and combining it with an energy source.
- In an IoT system, the actuator can act on data collected by sensors to create an outcome as determined by the chosen settings of the user.
- For example, a smart thermostat can use a temperature sensor to detect the room temperature and send a signal to an actuator that controls the heating or cooling system.
- There are different types of actuators for IoT, depending on the energy source, the movement type, and the application. Some common types are :
  - Servo motors: These are rotary or linear actuators that allow for precise control of angular or linear position, velocity and acceleration. They are often used in robotics, automation, and CNC machines.
  - Stepper motors: These are DC motors that move in discrete steps. They can be controlled by pulses of current and have a fixed number of steps per revolution. They are used for applications that require high accuracy, such as 3D printers, scanners, and cameras.
  - DC motors: These are continuous rotation motors that run on direct current. They can vary their speed and direction by changing the polarity and magnitude of the current. They are used for applications that require high torque, such as fans, pumps, and toys.
  - Linear actuators: These are devices that convert rotary motion into linear motion. They can be mechanical, hydraulic, pneumatic, or electric. They are used for applications that require linear movement, such as doors, windows, valves, and lifts.
  - Soft actuators: These are devices that use soft materials, such as polymers, gels, or fluids, to create deformation or movement. They can be activated by thermal, magnetic, electric, or light stimuli. They are used for applications that require flexibility, adaptability, and biocompatibility, such as wearable devices, soft robots, and biomedical devices.
