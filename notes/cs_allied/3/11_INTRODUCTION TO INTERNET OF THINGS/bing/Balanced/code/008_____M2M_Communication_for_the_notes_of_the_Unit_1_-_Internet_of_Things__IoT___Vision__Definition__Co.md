### M2M Communication

- M2M stands for Machine to Machine, and it refers to the automated exchange of data between devices or machines without human intervention.
- M2M is a broad concept that encompasses many different technologies, including IoT, which is a specific subset of M2M that relies on IP-based networks to connect smart devices to the internet and each other .
- M2M communication can be done over various types of networks, such as cellular, wired, wireless, or optical, depending on the application and the requirements .
- M2M communication enables various use cases and benefits, such as remote monitoring and control, automation, efficiency, security, and data analytics .
- M2M communication is the foundation for IoT, as it provides the basic functionality of data collection and transmission from devices to other devices, gateways, cloud, or middleware platforms .
- M2M communication is also influenced by the design principles for connected devices, such as IoT/M2M systems layers and design standardization, communication technologies, data enrichment and consolidation, ease of designing and affordability.
- Some examples of M2M communication in IoT are smart meters, smart home devices, wearable devices, industrial sensors, vehicle telematics, and healthcare devices .
