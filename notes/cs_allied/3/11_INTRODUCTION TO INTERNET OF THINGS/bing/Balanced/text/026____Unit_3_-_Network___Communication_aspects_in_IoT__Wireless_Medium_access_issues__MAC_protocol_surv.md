## Unit 3 - Network & Communication aspects in IoT

- Network and communication aspects in IoT refer to the methods and protocols that enable IoT devices to exchange data and interact with other devices, applications, and services over the internet or local networks.
- IoT devices can communicate using different types of communication, such as device-to-device, device-to-cloud, device-to-gateway, and device-to-application.
- IoT devices can use different types of communication protocols, such as wired or wireless, standard or proprietary, and application-specific or general-purpose.
- Some of the common communication protocols in IoT are:

  - Wireless Medium Access Issues: These are the challenges and solutions for managing the access of multiple wireless devices to a shared medium, such as radio frequency spectrum or optical fiber. Some of the issues include interference, collision, congestion, security, and energy efficiency. Some of the solutions include contention-based, reservation-based, or hybrid medium access control (MAC) protocols, such as CSMA/CA, TDMA, FDMA, CDMA, or OFDMA.
  - MAC Protocol Survey: This is a review of the existing MAC protocols for wireless IoT networks, such as IEEE 802.11 (Wi-Fi), IEEE 802.15.4 (Zigbee), IEEE 802.15.1 (Bluetooth), LoRaWAN, Sigfox, NB-IoT, LTE-M, and 5G. The survey compares the features, advantages, and disadvantages of each protocol in terms of scalability, reliability, latency, throughput, energy consumption, and security.
  - Survey Routing Protocols: This is a review of the existing routing protocols for wireless IoT networks, such as proactive, reactive, or hybrid routing protocols, such as OLSR, AODV, DSR, ZRP, or RPL. The survey compares the features, advantages, and disadvantages of each protocol in terms of scalability, reliability, latency, throughput, energy consumption, and security.
  - Sensor Deployment & Node Discovery: These are the methods and techniques for deploying and discovering IoT sensors in a network, such as random, deterministic, or adaptive deployment, and passive, active, or hybrid discovery. The methods and techniques aim to optimize the coverage, connectivity, redundancy, and cost of the sensor network.
  - Data Aggregation & Dissemination: These are the methods and techniques for collecting and distributing data in IoT networks, such as in-network, hierarchical, or distributed aggregation, and unicast, multicast, or broadcast dissemination. The methods and techniques aim to reduce the communication overhead, energy consumption, and latency of the data transmission.
