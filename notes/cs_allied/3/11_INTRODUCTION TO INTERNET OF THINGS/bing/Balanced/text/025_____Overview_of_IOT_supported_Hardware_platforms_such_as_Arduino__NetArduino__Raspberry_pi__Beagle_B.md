### Overview of IOT supported Hardware platforms such as Arduino, NetArduino, Raspberry pi, Beagle Bone, Intel Galileo boards and ARM cortex

- IoT hardware platforms are devices that enable the development and deployment of IoT applications. They typically consist of microcontrollers, sensors, actuators, communication modules, and other components that can interact with the physical world and the internet.
- Some of the common IoT hardware platforms are:

  - **Arduino**: Arduino is an open-source platform that consists of a series of microcontroller boards and a software development environment. Arduino boards can be programmed using a variety of languages, such as C, C++, Python, and Java. Arduino boards can also be extended with various shields and modules that provide additional functionality, such as wireless connectivity, GPS, LCD, etc. Arduino is widely used for prototyping and education purposes in IoT projects .
  - **Netduino**: Netduino is a platform that is compatible with Arduino, but runs on the .NET Micro Framework. Netduino boards can be programmed using C# and Visual Studio. Netduino boards can also use Arduino shields and modules, as well as .NET libraries and NuGet packages. Netduino is suitable for developing IoT applications that require more processing power and memory than Arduino.
  - **Raspberry Pi**: Raspberry Pi is a single-board computer that runs Linux or other operating systems. Raspberry Pi can be programmed using various languages, such as Python, Java, C, C++, etc. Raspberry Pi can also be connected to various peripherals and modules, such as cameras, keyboards, mice, sensors, etc. Raspberry Pi is ideal for developing IoT applications that require high performance and complex functionality .
  - **BeagleBone**: BeagleBone is a single-board computer that runs Linux or Android. BeagleBone can be programmed using various languages, such as Python, Java, C, C++, etc. BeagleBone can also be connected to various peripherals and modules, such as LCD, Ethernet, USB, etc. BeagleBone is suitable for developing IoT applications that require low power consumption and real-time processing .
  - **Intel Galileo**: Intel Galileo is a single-board computer that runs Linux or Windows. Intel Galileo can be programmed using various languages, such as C, C++, Python, Java, etc. Intel Galileo can also be connected to various peripherals and modules, such as Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Ethernet, USB, etc. Intel Galileo is compatible with Arduino and can use Arduino shields and modules. Intel Galileo is designed for developing IoT applications that require high performance and scalability .
  - **ARM Cortex**: ARM Cortex is a family of microcontroller cores that are widely used in embedded systems and IoT devices. ARM Cortex cores can be programmed using various languages, such as C, C++, Assembly, etc. ARM Cortex cores can also be integrated with various peripherals and modules, such as sensors, actuators, communication modules, etc. ARM Cortex cores are optimized for low power consumption and high efficiency in IoT applications .

- These are some of the popular IoT hardware platforms that can be used for developing IoT applications. However, there are many other platforms available in the market that can suit different needs and preferences. The choice of the platform depends on various factors, such as the cost, the functionality, the compatibility, the availability, the support, the community, etc. of the platform. Therefore, it is important to research and compare different platforms before selecting one for a specific IoT project.
