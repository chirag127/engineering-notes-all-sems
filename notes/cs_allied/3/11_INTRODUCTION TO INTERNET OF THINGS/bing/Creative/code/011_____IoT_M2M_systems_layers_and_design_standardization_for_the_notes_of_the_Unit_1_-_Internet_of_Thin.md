# IoT/M2M systems layers and design standardization

- IoT/M2M systems are composed of devices, networks, platforms, and applications that enable communication and data exchange between machines and humans.
- IoT/M2M systems can be divided into three major domains: the application layer, the service layer, and the network layer .
- The application layer is the domain where the end-user interacts with the IoT/M2M system, such as through a web interface, a mobile app, or a dashboard. The application layer provides the functionality and logic for the specific use case of the IoT/M2M system, such as smart home, smart city, or smart agriculture.
- The service layer is the domain where the IoT/M2M system manages the data and resources of the devices, networks, and applications. The service layer provides common services and capabilities for the IoT/M2M system, such as device management, data aggregation, security, and analytics. The service layer also enables interoperability and integration between different IoT/M2M systems and platforms, such as through standardized APIs and protocols.
- The network layer is the domain where the IoT/M2M system connects the devices and applications through various communication technologies, such as cellular, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, LoRa, or Zigbee. The network layer provides the connectivity and transmission of data and commands between the devices and applications, as well as between different IoT/M2M systems and platforms.
- IoT/M2M systems layers and design standardization are important for ensuring the scalability, reliability, security, and interoperability of the IoT/M2M systems and platforms. Standardization also facilitates the development and deployment of IoT/M2M systems and applications, as well as the innovation and collaboration among different stakeholders and sectors.
- Some of the organizations and initiatives that are involved in IoT/M2M systems layers and design standardization are:
  - oneM2M: a global initiative that aims to develop a common service layer for IoT/M2M systems and platforms, based on a set of specifications and APIs that support various communication technologies and use cases  .
  - Open Geospatial Consortium (OGC): an international industry consortium that develops open standards for geospatial and location-based services, including sensors, data models, and web services for IoT/M2M systems and applications.
  - 3GPP: a collaboration of standards organizations that develops specifications for cellular technologies, including LTE-M, NB-IoT, and EC-GSM-IoT, which are designed to support low-power, wide-area, and cost-effective IoT/M2M connectivity.
  - ETSI: a European standards organization that develops standards for various technologies and domains, including smart M2M communications, cybersecurity, and radio spectrum management for IoT/M2M systems and platforms.
  - IEEE: a global technical professional organization that develops standards for various technologies and domains, including wireless communications, networking, and cloud computing for IoT/M2M systems and applications.
