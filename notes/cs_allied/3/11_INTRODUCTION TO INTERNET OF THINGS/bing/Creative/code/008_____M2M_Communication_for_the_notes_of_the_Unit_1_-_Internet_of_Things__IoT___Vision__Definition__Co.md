# M2M Communication

- M2M stands for Machine to Machine, and it refers to the automated exchange of data between devices, sensors, machines, or software applications without human intervention.
- M2M is a broad concept that encompasses many different technologies, including IoT, which is a specific subset of M2M that relies on IP-based networks to connect devices to the internet and each other .
- M2M communication can be done over various types of networks, such as cellular, wired, wireless, or optical, depending on the requirements and capabilities of the devices and applications involved .
- M2M communication enables various use cases and benefits, such as remote monitoring and control, automation, optimization, security, and analytics, across different domains and industries, such as healthcare, agriculture, manufacturing, transportation, energy, and smart cities .
- M2M communication is also the foundation for IoT, as it provides the basic functionality and infrastructure for data collection, transmission, and processing from the connected devices.
- M2M communication is evolving and expanding with the advancement of technologies, such as 5G, cloud computing, edge computing, artificial intelligence, and blockchain, that can enhance the performance, scalability, security, and intelligence of M2M systems .
