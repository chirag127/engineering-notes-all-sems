### Actuators for IoT

- An actuator is a device that converts energy into motion. It does this by taking an electrical signal and combining it with an energy source.
- In IoT, actuators enable a physical action based on data that originates with one or more sensors.
- Actuators can be classified into different types based on the type of energy they use, the type of motion they produce, and the type of application they serve.
- Some common types of actuators in IoT are:

  - Servo Motors: A servomotor is a rotary actuator or linear actuator that allows for precise control of angular or linear position, velocity and acceleration. Servo motors are widely used in robotics, automation, and industrial applications.
  - Stepper Motors: Stepper motors are DC motors that move in discrete steps. They can be controlled by pulses of current and can achieve high torque and precision. Stepper motors are used in printers, scanners, cameras, and CNC machines.
  - DC Motors (Continuous Rotation Motors): DC motors are two wire (power & ground), continuous rotation motors. They can vary their speed and direction by changing the polarity and magnitude of the current. DC motors are used in fans, pumps, toys, and vehicles.
  - Linear Actuators: Linear actuators are devices that produce linear motion by converting electrical, hydraulic, or pneumatic energy. They can be used to lift, push, pull, or adjust objects. Linear actuators are used in medical devices, industrial machinery, and home automation.
  - Thermal/Magnetic Actuators: These are actuated by thermal or mechanical energy. Shape Memory Alloys (SMAs) or Magnetic Shape Memory Alloys (MSMAs) are materials that can change their shape when heated or magnetized. They can be used to create valves, switches, or sensors.
  - Mechanical Actuators: A mechanical actuator executes movement by converting rotary motion into linear motion. It uses gears, screws, or levers to achieve this. Mechanical actuators are used in valves, switches, or locks.
  - Soft Actuators: Soft actuators are made of flexible or elastic materials that can deform or bend when stimulated by electricity, light, or chemicals. They can mimic biological muscles or organs and can be used for soft robotics, wearable devices, or biomedical applications.
  - Light Activated Actuators: These are actuated by light energy. Photovoltaic cells or solar panels are devices that can convert light into electricity. They can be used to power other actuators or devices. Light activated actuators are used for renewable energy, environmental monitoring, or smart windows.
