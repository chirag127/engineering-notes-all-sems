### Sensor deployment & Node discovery

- Sensor deployment is the process of placing sensor nodes in a target area to monitor physical phenomena, such as temperature, humidity, pressure, sound, etc.
- Sensor nodes are small devices that have sensing, computing, communication, and power capabilities. They can be deployed in various environments, such as indoor, outdoor, urban, rural, etc.
- Sensor deployment can be done in different ways, such as random, deterministic, manual, or automated. The deployment method depends on the application requirements, such as coverage, connectivity, reliability, cost, etc.
- Node discovery is the process of identifying and locating sensor nodes in a network. Node discovery is essential for establishing communication links, routing data, and managing the network.
- Node discovery can be done in different ways, such as proactive, reactive, or hybrid. The discovery method depends on the network characteristics, such as size, density, mobility, heterogeneity, etc.
- Sensor deployment and node discovery are important aspects of IoT, as they enable the collection and transmission of data from the physical world to the digital world.
- Some examples of IoT applications that use sensor deployment and node discovery are:

  - Healthcare: Wireless Body Area Network (WBAN) is a network of wearable or implantable sensor nodes that monitor the vital signs and activities of a human body   . Node discovery is needed to identify the sensor nodes and their locations on the body, and to establish secure and reliable communication links among them.
  - Smart city: Smart city is a network of sensor nodes that monitor and manage various aspects of urban life, such as traffic, pollution, energy, water, etc. Sensor deployment is needed to ensure adequate coverage and connectivity of the target area, and to optimize the use of resources and infrastructure. Node discovery is needed to locate the sensor nodes and their status, and to enable efficient data aggregation and dissemination.
