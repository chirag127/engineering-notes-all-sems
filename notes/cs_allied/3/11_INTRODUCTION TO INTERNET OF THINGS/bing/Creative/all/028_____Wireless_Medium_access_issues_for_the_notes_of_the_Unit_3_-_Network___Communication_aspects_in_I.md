# Wireless Medium Access Issues for IoT

- Wireless medium access issues refer to the challenges and problems that arise when multiple IoT devices share the same wireless channel for data transmission and reception.
- These issues include interference, collision, congestion, energy consumption, latency, scalability, and security.
- To address these issues, various medium access control (MAC) protocols have been proposed and developed for IoT applications.
- MAC protocols are responsible for coordinating and regulating the access of IoT devices to the wireless medium, as well as providing reliable and efficient data delivery.
- MAC protocols can be classified into different categories based on their design principles, such as contention-based, reservation-based, hybrid, cognitive, and cooperative MAC protocols.
- Each category of MAC protocols has its own advantages and disadvantages in terms of performance, complexity, and suitability for different IoT scenarios and requirements.
- Some of the existing MAC protocols for IoT are IEEE 802.15.4, IEEE 802.11ah, LoRaWAN, Sigfox, NB-IoT, LTE-M, and Wi-Fi HaLow.
