# Unit 2 - Hardware for IoT

## Sensors, Digital sensors, actuators

- Sensors are devices that provide a usable output in response to a specified measurement. They are used for sensing things and devices in the physical world and converting them into digital signals that can be processed by IoT systems.
- Digital sensors are sensors that produce a discrete output, such as a binary signal, that can be directly read by a microcontroller or a computer. Examples of digital sensors are switches, buttons, encoders, etc.
- Actuators are devices that convert an electrical signal into a physical action or movement. They are used for controlling things and devices in the physical world based on the commands from IoT systems. Examples of actuators are motors, solenoids, relays, LEDs, etc.

## Radio frequency identification (RFID) technology, wireless sensor networks, participatory sensing technology

- RFID technology is a wireless communication technology that uses radio waves to identify and track objects, devices, or people. RFID systems consist of RFID tags, which are small electronic devices that store information and can be attached to objects, and RFID readers, which are devices that can read the information from the tags and communicate with a network.
- Wireless sensor networks (WSNs) are networks of interconnected sensors that cooperate to gather and provide information from the environment. WSNs can be used for various applications, such as environmental monitoring, smart agriculture, health care, etc. WSNs can communicate with each other and with a network using wireless protocols, such as Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, ZigBee, etc.
- Participatory sensing technology is a type of sensing technology that involves human participation in the data collection and processing process. Participatory sensing technology can leverage the sensors embedded in smartphones, wearable devices, or other personal devices that people carry or use in their daily lives. Participatory sensing technology can be used for various applications, such as social networking, urban planning, traffic management, etc.

## Embedded Platforms for IoT: Embedded computing basics, Overview of IOT supported Hardware platforms such as Arduino, NetArduino, Raspberry pi, Beagle Bone, Intel Galileo boards and ARM cortex

- Embedded computing is a type of computing that involves designing and implementing software and hardware systems that are dedicated to a specific function or application. Embedded computing systems are usually constrained by factors such as size, power, cost, performance, reliability, etc.
- Embedded platforms for IoT are hardware platforms that can support the development and deployment of IoT applications and systems. Embedded platforms for IoT usually consist of a microcontroller or a microprocessor, memory, input/output interfaces, sensors, actuators, and communication modules.

  - Netduino: Netduino is a platform that consists of a series of microcontroller boards that are compatible with the Arduino form factor and can be programmed using the .NET Micro Framework, which is a subset of the .NET Framework. Netduino boards can also be extended with various shields, as well as modules that support the .NET Gadgeteer standard, which is a plug-and-play system for creating electronic devices.
