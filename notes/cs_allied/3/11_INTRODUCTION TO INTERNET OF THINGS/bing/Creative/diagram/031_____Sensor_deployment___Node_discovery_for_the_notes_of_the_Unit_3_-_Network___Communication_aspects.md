### Sensor deployment and node discovery

- Sensor deployment is the process of placing sensor nodes in a target area to monitor physical phenomena, such as temperature, humidity, sound, etc.
- Sensor nodes are small devices that have sensing, computing, communication, and power capabilities. They can form a wireless sensor network (WSN) by communicating with each other and with a base station or a gateway.
- Node discovery is the process of identifying and locating sensor nodes in a WSN. It is essential for network initialization, topology maintenance, routing, data aggregation, and security.
- Sensor deployment and node discovery can be challenging in IoT scenarios, where the sensor nodes may be heterogeneous, mobile, or deployed in harsh or inaccessible environments.
- Some of the factors that affect sensor deployment and node discovery are:
  - The number and type of sensor nodes
  - The coverage and connectivity requirements
  - The energy consumption and lifetime of sensor nodes
  - The mobility and dynamics of sensor nodes and the target area
  - The security and privacy of sensor nodes and the sensed data
- Some of the techniques that can be used for sensor deployment and node discovery are:
  - Deterministic deployment: Sensor nodes are placed at predefined locations according to a plan or a grid. This method can ensure full coverage and connectivity, but may not be feasible or scalable in some IoT scenarios.
  - Random deployment: Sensor nodes are scattered randomly in the target area. This method can be easy and cost-effective, but may result in coverage holes and connectivity gaps.
  - Self-organization: Sensor nodes can adjust their positions or roles based on local information or feedback from other nodes or the base station. This method can improve the network performance and adapt to dynamic changes, but may incur additional overhead and complexity.
  - Localization: Sensor nodes can estimate their locations using various methods, such as GPS, triangulation, multilateration, or proximity. This method can facilitate node discovery and routing, but may require additional hardware or infrastructure support.
  - Neighbor discovery: Sensor nodes can exchange messages with their nearby nodes to discover their identities, locations, and capabilities. This method can enable network formation and data aggregation, but may consume energy and bandwidth.
