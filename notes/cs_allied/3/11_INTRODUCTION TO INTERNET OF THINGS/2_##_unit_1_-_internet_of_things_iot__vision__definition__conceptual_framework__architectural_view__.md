## Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT): Vision, Definition, Conceptual Framework, Architectural view, technology behind IoT, Sources of the IoT, M2M Communication, IoT Examples. Design Principles for Connected Devices: IoT/M2M systems layers and design standardization, communication technologies, data enrichment and consolidation, ease of designing and affordability

Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT) provides an overview of the IoT concept, its definition, and its various components. The following topics are covered in this unit:

1. Vision: The vision of the IoT, including its goals and objectives.

2. Definition: A definition of the IoT, including its key components and characteristics.

3. Conceptual Framework: The conceptual framework for the IoT, including its architecture, components, and communication protocols.

4. Architectural view: An overview of the IoT architecture, including its various layers and components.

5. Technology behind IoT: The technologies behind the IoT, including sensors, actuators, communication protocols, and data management.

6. Sources of the IoT: The sources of the IoT, including connected devices, applications, and data sources.

7. M2M Communication: The role of machine-to-machine (M2M) communication in the IoT, including its benefits and challenges.

8. IoT Examples: Real-world examples of the IoT, including smart homes, connected cars, and industrial IoT.

9. Design Principles for Connected Devices: The design principles for connected devices, including IoT/M2M systems layers and design standardization, communication technologies, data enrichment and consolidation, ease of designing and affordability.

In summary, Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT) provides an overview of the IoT concept, its definition, and its various components, including its vision, conceptual framework, architecture, technology, sources, M2M communication, real-world examples, and design principles for connected devices.
