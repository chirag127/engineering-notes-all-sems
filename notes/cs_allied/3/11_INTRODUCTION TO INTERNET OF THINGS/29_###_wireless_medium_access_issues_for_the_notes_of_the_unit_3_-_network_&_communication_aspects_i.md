Wireless medium access issues are a critical aspect of IoT network and communication, as they determine how devices access the wireless medium and transmit data. These issues include:

1. Interference: Interference from other devices or signals can impact the quality of the wireless signal and reduce the reliability of data transmission.

2. Capacity: The capacity of the wireless medium is limited, and it is important to ensure that it is used efficiently to maximize the available bandwidth.

3. Latency: Latency is the time it takes for a signal to travel from the source to the destination, and it is important to minimize latency in IoT applications to ensure that data is transmitted in real-time.

To address these issues, several MAC (Media Access Control) protocols have been developed, including:

1. Carrier Sense Multiple Access (CSMA): A protocol that uses carrier sensing to determine when the wireless medium is free and to prevent collisions between devices.

2. Time Division Multiple Access (TDMA): A protocol that divides the wireless medium into time slots and assigns each device a specific slot for transmission.

3. Frequency Division Multiple Access (FDMA): A protocol that divides the wireless medium into frequency bands and assigns each device a specific band for transmission.

There are also several routing protocols that have been developed for IoT, including:

1. Flooding: A protocol that floods the network with data and relies on each node to forward the data to its neighbors.

2. Directed Diffusion: A protocol that uses data-driven information dissemination to route data in the network.

3. Geographic Routing: A protocol that uses the geographic location of nodes to route data in the network.

Sensor deployment and node discovery are also important aspects of IoT network and communication, as they determine how devices are deployed in the network and how they discover other devices. Data aggregation and dissemination are also important, as they determine how data is collected and processed in the network, and how it is distributed to the appropriate devices.

Overall, wireless medium access issues, MAC protocols, routing protocols, sensor deployment and node discovery, and data aggregation and dissemination are critical components of IoT network and communication, and play a key role in ensuring that devices can communicate and exchange data effectively.
