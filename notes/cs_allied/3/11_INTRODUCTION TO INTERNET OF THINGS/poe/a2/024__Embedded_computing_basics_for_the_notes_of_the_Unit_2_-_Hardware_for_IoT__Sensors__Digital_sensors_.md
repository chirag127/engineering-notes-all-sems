### Embedded computing basics

- Embedded systems are special purpose computer systems designed to perform dedicated functions.
- They are embedded as part of a complete device including hardware and mechanical parts.
- Embedded systems are constrained by limited resources like memory, processing power, and energy.
- They are real-time systems that must respond to events within a strict time limit.
- Examples: Consumer electronics, automotive control systems, medical devices, industrial control systems, etc.

### Sensors and actuators

- Sensors detect physical quantities like temperature, pressure, motion, etc. and convert them into electrical signals.
- Actuators convert electrical signals into mechanical movements or actions like moving a motor.
- Sensors and actuators are connected to embedded systems to interface with the physical world.

### Wireless technologies

- Wireless technologies like Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Zigbee, etc. are used to connect embedded systems and IoT devices.
- They eliminate the need for cables and provide more flexibility in system design.
- However, they can be susceptible to interference and security vulnerabilities.

### Embedded platforms

- Hardware platforms with embedded processors and I/O interfaces are used to prototype and build embedded systems and IoT devices.
- Popular options include:
-- Arduino: open-source with easy-to-use hardware and software.
-- Raspberry Pi: inexpensive, flexible, and can run a full operating system.
-- BeagleBone: community-supported platform with cloud connectivity.
-- Intel Galileo and Edison: support for Intel processors and frameworks.
-- ARM Cortex boards: wide range of boards using ARM Cortex-M and Cortex-A processors.
