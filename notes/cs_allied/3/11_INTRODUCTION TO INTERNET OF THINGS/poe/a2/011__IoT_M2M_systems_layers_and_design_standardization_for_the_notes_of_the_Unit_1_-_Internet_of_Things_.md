 Here are the points on IoT/M2M systems layers and design standardization for the notes of Introduction to Internet of Things:

### IoT/M2M systems layers

1. Perception layer: Sensors and devices that collect data from the physical world. Eg: Temperature sensors, motion sensors etc.
2. Network layer: Connectivity between devices and servers. Eg: WiFi, Bluetooth, cellular networks etc.
3. Application layer: Software that makes use of the data collected. Eg: Data analytics software, automation software etc.

### Design standardization

1. Open standards: Standards developed by consortiums/organizations to enable interoperability between devices and systems from different vendors. Eg: MQTT, CoAP, HTTP etc.
2. Ease of adoption: Systems should be easy to configure and onboard new devices. Standard protocols and simple interfaces help in this.
3. Scalability: Systems should be able to handle large numbers of connected devices and high volumes of data and scale accordingly.
4. Security: Systems should implement strong security and authentication mechanisms to handle threats and vulnerabilities.
5. Affordability: Components and systems should be reasonably priced to enable mass adoption of IoT.

The above points cover the key layers in an IoT system and important design considerations for IoT/M2M systems. The standardization of components and interfaces helps in interconnecting disparate devices and building robust, scalable and secure systems in a cost-effective manner.
