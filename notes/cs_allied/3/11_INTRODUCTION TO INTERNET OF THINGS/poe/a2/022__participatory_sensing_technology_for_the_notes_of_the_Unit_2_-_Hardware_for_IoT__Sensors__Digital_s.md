Participatory Sensing Technology:
- Participatory sensing refers to the collective sensing of a phenomenon by a group of individuals using portable computing devices.
- Individuals contribute data by measuring environmental parameters, their location, body metrics, or other phenomena using sensors built into mobile phones or other personal devices.
- The collected data can be aggregated and analyzed to gain insights into events, conditions, and activities in the real world.
- Participatory sensing enables large-scale data collection with low-cost by leveraging sensors and computing capabilities of consumer mobile devices.
- Examples: Measuring air/noise pollution, traffic congestion, spread of diseases, etc. using sensors in smartphones carried by many individuals.
