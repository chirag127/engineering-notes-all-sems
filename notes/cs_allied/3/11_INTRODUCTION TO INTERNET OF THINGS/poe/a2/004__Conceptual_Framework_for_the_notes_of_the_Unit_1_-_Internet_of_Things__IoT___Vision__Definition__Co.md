### Conceptual Framework for the notes of the Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT):

- Vision: Connecting everyday physical objects and devices to the internet for data exchange to provide value-added services and automation.
- Definition: The interconnection of heterogeneous networked devices and systems via the internet, enabling data and information exchange to provide advanced and intelligent applications and services.
- Conceptual Framework: Perception layer, Network layer, Application layer. The perception layer senses the physical world. The network layer provides connectivity. The application layer offers IoT services.
- Architectural view: Thing/Device layer, Network/Transport layer, Service/Application layer. The thing/device layer contains sensors and actuators. The network/transport layer provides connectivity. The service/application layer offers services to users.
- Technology behind IoT: Embedded systems, Wireless/ wired networks, Cloud computing, Fog/Edge computing.
- Sources of the IoT: Radio-frequency identification (RFID), Wireless sensor networks (WSN), Machine-to-machine (M2M) communication.
- M2M Communication: Communication between machines/devices without human interaction to exchange data and take actions.
- IoT Examples: Smart homes, Smart cities, Smart healthcare, Smart manufacturing, Smart transportation, Smart agriculture.

Design Principles for Connected Devices:

- IoT/M2M systems layers and design standardization: Perception, network, application, security, management layers with standards like MQTT, CoAP.
- Communication technologies: WiFi, Bluetooth, Zigbee, Z-Wave, Cellular, RFID, NFC.
- Data enrichment and consolidation: Data aggregation, fusion, cleaning, and preprocessing.
- Ease of designing and affordability: Simple and open designs, minimal cost.
