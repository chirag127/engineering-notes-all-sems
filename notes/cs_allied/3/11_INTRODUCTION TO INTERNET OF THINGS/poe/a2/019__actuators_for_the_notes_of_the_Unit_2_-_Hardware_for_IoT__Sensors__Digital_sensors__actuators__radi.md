 Here are the points on actuators for the notes of the Unit 2 - Hardware for IoT:

1. Actuators convert electrical signals into motion/action. They are output devices that perform actions based on the input received from sensors.
2. Common types of actuators used in IoT devices:
- Motors: Servomotors, stepper motors, etc. to control motion of devices.
- Relays: To switch on/off electrical devices.
- Solenoids: Convert electrical signal into linear motion to control valves, locks, etc.
- Buzzers/Loudspeakers: Convert electrical signal into sound.

3. Actuators enable IoT devices to perform physical actions in the real world based on the data received from sensors and processed by microcontrollers/microprocessors.
4. Combining sensors and actuators enables IoT devices to sense the environment and accordingly trigger actions, enabling automation and control.
