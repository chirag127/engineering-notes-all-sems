 Here are the notes on Design Principles for Connected Devices:

1. Standardization: Follow common standards for interoperability between devices and systems. This includes communication protocols, data formats, and infrastructure interfaces. Standardization enables devices and systems from different vendors to interact and exchange data.

2. Modularity: Adopt a modular design that divides the system into independent, interchangeable modules with standard interfaces. This enables flexibility to swap components and extend or upgrade the system easily.

3. Simplicity: Keep the design as simple as possible. Minimize complexity in devices, networking, and infrastructure. This makes the system more robust, secure, and affordable.

4. Scalability: The system should be able to scale to large numbers of devices and high volumes of data. The networking and infrastructure should be able to handle expansion and growth efficiently.

5. Affordability: Choose technologies and components that are cost-effective to enable large-scale deployment of connected devices and systems. This is critical for adoption and impact at a massive scale.

6. Limited Resources: Design for constrained devices and limited bandwidth networks. Optimize for energy efficiency and minimal resource usage since many IoT devices are battery-powered and networks can become congested with high volumes of data.

7. Interoperability: Enable devices and systems from different vendors to integrate and work together. This requires compatibility of communication technologies, data formats, and system interfaces. Interoperability is crucial for IoT solutions to provide value.

8. Security: Apply strong security safeguards to protect devices, data, and systems. This includes authentication, encryption, access control, and other measures to prevent unauthorized access and cyber attacks. Security is vital for user trust and system reliability.

9. Privacy: Protect personal and private data in compliance with regulations. Enable users to control how their data is collected and used. Privacy protections are necessary as IoT systems gather and share increasing amounts of personal data.
