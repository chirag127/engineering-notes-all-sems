### Definition for the notes of the Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT):

1. Vision: The Internet of Things (IoT) refers to the interconnection of uniquely-identifiable embedded computing devices within the existing Internet infrastructure.
2. Definition: The IoT is a dynamic global network infrastructure with self-configuring capabilities based on standard and interoperable communication protocols where physical and virtual 'things' have identities, physical attributes, and virtual personalities and use intelligent interfaces, and are seamlessly integrated into the information network.
3. Conceptual Framework: The key components of an IoT system are: (1) connected devices or things, (2) gateways to connect devices, (3) network services to handle data transmission and applications, and (4) cloud-based services to store and process data as well as provide added-value services.
4. Architectural view: The architecture of IoT systems follows a layered approach: (1) device/gateway layer, (2) network/transport layer, (3) service/application layer, and (4) business/customer layer.
5. Technology behind IoT: The key technologies enabling IoT include: (1) IPv6 and addressing schemes for things, (2) low-power wireless networking (e.g., Zigbee, Bluetooth Low Energy, RFID), (3) service-oriented architecture and Web services, (4) cloud computing and big data analytics, and (5) embedded systems and cyber-physical systems.
