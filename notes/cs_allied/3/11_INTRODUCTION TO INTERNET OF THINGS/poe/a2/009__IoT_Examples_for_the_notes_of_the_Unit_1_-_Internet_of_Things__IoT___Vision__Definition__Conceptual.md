### IoT Examples

- Smart homes: IoT devices like smart thermostats, smart lighting, smart locks, etc. can be used to automate home controls and monitoring.
- Smart cities: IoT devices can be used to monitor and control city infrastructure like traffic and transportation systems, utility supply, waste management, etc. Smart meters and smart grids can help monitor energy usage.
- Industry 4.0: IoT devices and sensors can be used in manufacturing to monitor assets and improve processes. This includes tracking of inventory, monitoring equipment performance, and optimizing the supply chain.
- Healthcare: IoT devices can be used to monitor patients and enhance medical care. This includes medical wearables to monitor health in real-time, connected medical devices, and smart medical supplies management.
- Retail: IoT can enhance the retail customer experience through features like smart shopping carts, smart mirrors, beacon technology, surveillance, and smart payment systems.

### Design Principles for Connected Devices

- Scalability: The IoT system should be able to scale as more devices get connected. The system should be able to handle huge volumes of data and connections.
- Interoperability: The system should allow different devices and technologies to interconnect and exchange data. Standard protocols and interfaces are required to enable interoperability.
- Security: Robust security is required to protect the devices, data, and networks from threats. This includes authentication, encryption, access control, and other security measures.
- Privacy: The system should protect the privacy of users and their data. Privacy controls and anonymization techniques should be implemented.
- Reliability: The system should be reliable and resilient to failures. Redundancy and error handling capabilities should be incorporated in the IoT system design.
- Ease of use: The system should be easy to setup and use. Complexity should be hidden from the end-users. Plug and play capabilities can improve ease of use.
- Affordability: The IoT system and connected devices should be affordable to enable mass adoption and scale. Cost optimization is important for the commercial viability of IoT solutions.
