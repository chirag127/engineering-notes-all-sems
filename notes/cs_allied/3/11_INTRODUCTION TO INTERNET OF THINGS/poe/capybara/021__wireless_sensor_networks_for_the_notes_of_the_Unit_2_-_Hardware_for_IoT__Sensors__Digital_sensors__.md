### Wireless Sensor Networks

- A wireless sensor network (WSN) is a network consisting of spatially distributed autonomous devices using sensors to monitor physical or environmental conditions. 
- These sensors are connected through wireless links and can be used to monitor different parameters like temperature, humidity, pressure, and more.
- WSNs are used in various applications such as environmental monitoring, healthcare, and industrial automation.
- The nodes in a WSN are small, low-cost, low-power devices that can be deployed in large numbers to cover a wide area.
- WSNs have the ability to self-organize and self-heal, making them suitable for applications where manual intervention is difficult or impossible.
- The nodes in a WSN communicate with each other using wireless protocols such as Zigbee, Bluetooth, and WiFi.
- The data collected by the sensors is sent to a base station or sink node for processing and analysis.
- The base station can be connected to the internet to enable remote monitoring and control of the WSN.
- WSNs can be used in conjunction with other IoT devices, such as actuators, to create a complete IoT system.
- Participatory sensing technology, which involves the use of mobile devices to collect and share data, can also be integrated with WSNs to enhance their capabilities.

### Embedded Platforms for IoT

- Embedded computing is the integration of computing systems into everyday objects to enable them to perform specific tasks.
- IoT devices are based on embedded computing platforms that enable them to connect to the internet and communicate with other devices.
- Popular IoT hardware platforms include Arduino, NetArduino, Raspberry pi, Beagle Bone, Intel Galileo boards, and ARM cortex.
- Arduino is an open-source hardware platform that is easy to use and has a large community of users and developers.
- NetArduino is a version of Arduino that includes a built-in Ethernet interface for internet connectivity.
- Raspberry Pi is a low-cost, credit-card-sized computer that can be used for a wide range of IoT applications.
- Beagle Bone is another low-cost, open-source hardware platform that is suitable for IoT applications.
- Intel Galileo boards are based on the Intel Quark SoC X1000 processor and are designed for IoT and embedded applications.
- ARM cortex is a family of processors that are widely used in IoT devices due to their low power consumption and high performance.
- These platforms provide a wide range of interfaces and connectivity options, making them suitable for a variety of IoT applications.
- The choice of platform depends on the specific requirements of the application, such as processing power, memory, and connectivity options.
