### Embedded Platforms for IoT

In this section, we will discuss the embedded platforms that are used for the Internet of Things (IoT). The following are some of the key points that you need to know about embedded platforms for IoT:

- Embedded computing basics: Embedded computing is the integration of computing systems into other devices or systems. This is done to control and monitor the operation of these devices or systems. Embedded computing systems are typically low-power devices that are designed to perform specific tasks.

- Overview of IoT supported hardware platforms: There are several hardware platforms that are used for IoT. Some of the most popular ones include:

  - Arduino: Arduino is an open-source hardware platform that is used for building electronics projects. It is based on a microcontroller and can be easily programmed using the Arduino software.

  - NetArduino: NetArduino is similar to Arduino, but it includes a built-in Ethernet interface. This makes it easier to connect to the internet and other devices.

  - Raspberry Pi: Raspberry Pi is a small, affordable computer that is designed for educational purposes. It can be used for a variety of IoT projects, such as home automation and robotics.

  - Beagle Bone: Beagle Bone is a single-board computer that is designed for building embedded systems. It has a powerful processor and can be used for a variety of IoT applications.

  - Intel Galileo boards: Intel Galileo boards are a series of development boards that are designed for IoT applications. They are based on the Intel Quark processor and come with a variety of features, such as Wi-Fi and Bluetooth connectivity.

  - ARM Cortex: ARM Cortex is a family of processors that are used in a variety of IoT devices. They are low-power processors that are designed for use in embedded systems.

- Sensors, digital sensors, actuators, radio frequency identification (RFID) technology, wireless sensor networks, participatory sensing technology: In addition to embedded platforms, IoT also relies on a variety of sensors and other technologies. These include digital sensors, which can detect changes in temperature, pressure, and other environmental factors. Actuators are used to control the operation of devices, such as motors and valves. RFID technology is used for tracking and identification of objects, while wireless sensor networks allow devices to communicate with each other. Participatory sensing technology involves the use of smartphones and other mobile devices to collect data from individuals.

In conclusion, embedded platforms are a key component of IoT. They provide the computing power and connectivity that is needed to control and monitor devices and systems. By understanding the different types of embedded platforms and their capabilities, you can better design and implement IoT solutions.
