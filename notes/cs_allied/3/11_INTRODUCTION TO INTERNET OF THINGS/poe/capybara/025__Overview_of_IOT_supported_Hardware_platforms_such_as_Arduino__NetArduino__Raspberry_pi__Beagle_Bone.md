### Overview of IOT supported Hardware platforms

The following are some of the hardware platforms that are supported in the Internet of Things (IoT):

- **Arduino**: This is one of the most popular open-source hardware platforms for IoT. It is based on a simple microcontroller board and comes with an easy-to-use software platform that allows developers to program and interact with the board.
- **NetArduino**: This is another open-source platform that is built on top of the Arduino architecture. It provides networking capabilities to the Arduino board, making it suitable for IoT applications.
- **Raspberry Pi**: This is a credit-card-sized computer that can be used for a variety of IoT applications. It has a powerful processor and comes with a range of connectivity options, including Ethernet, Wi-Fi, and Bluetooth.
- **BeagleBone**: This is a low-cost, high-performance platform that can be used for IoT applications. It comes with a range of connectivity options, including Ethernet, Wi-Fi, and Bluetooth. It also has a powerful processor and can run a range of operating systems.
- **Intel Galileo**: This is a single-board computer that is designed for IoT applications. It comes with a range of connectivity options, including Ethernet, Wi-Fi, and Bluetooth. It also has a powerful processor and can run a range of operating systems.
- **ARM Cortex**: This is a family of microcontroller and microprocessor cores that are widely used in IoT applications. They are designed for low-power applications and come with a range of connectivity options.
