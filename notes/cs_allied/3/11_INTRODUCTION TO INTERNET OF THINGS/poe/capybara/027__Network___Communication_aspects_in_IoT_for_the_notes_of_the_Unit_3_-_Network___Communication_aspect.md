### Network & Communication Aspects in IoT

The following points cover the key aspects of network and communication in IoT:

- Wireless Medium Access Issues: IoT devices require wireless access to connect to the network. This requires proper management of the wireless medium to ensure efficient communication. Some of the key issues that need to be addressed include interference, congestion, and collision avoidance.

- MAC Protocol Survey: The Media Access Control (MAC) protocol is essential for managing the communication between IoT devices. A survey of different MAC protocols can help in selecting the most appropriate one for the specific IoT deployment.

- Survey Routing Protocols: Routing protocols are used to determine the most efficient path for data to travel between IoT devices. A survey of different routing protocols can help to determine the best option for the specific IoT deployment.

- Sensor Deployment & Node Discovery: IoT devices typically consist of sensors that collect and transmit data. Proper deployment of these sensors is crucial for effective data collection. Node discovery is also important for identifying new devices that may join the network.

- Data Aggregation & Dissemination: The large volume of data generated by IoT devices requires efficient aggregation and dissemination. This involves processing and filtering the data and then transmitting it to the appropriate destination.

In conclusion, network and communication aspects are critical for the success of IoT deployments. Proper management of wireless medium access, selection of appropriate MAC and routing protocols, effective sensor deployment and node discovery, and efficient data aggregation and dissemination are all essential components of a successful IoT network.
