### Hardware for IoT

IoT devices are composed of hardware and software components that work together to collect, store, and transmit data. In this unit, we will focus on the hardware components of IoT systems. Here are some important concepts to keep in mind:

#### Sensors

- Sensors are devices that detect and measure physical properties such as temperature, light, sound, and motion.
- Digital sensors are sensors that convert physical signals into digital signals that can be processed by a computer or microcontroller.
- Actuators are devices that convert electrical signals into physical action, such as movement or heat.

#### Radio Frequency Identification (RFID) technology

- RFID technology is a wireless communication technology that enables data exchange between a tag and a reader.
- RFID tags can be passive or active, depending on whether they have a power source.
- RFID technology is used in supply chain management, inventory control, and asset tracking.

#### Wireless Sensor Networks

- Wireless sensor networks are composed of a large number of small, low-power sensors that communicate with each other wirelessly.
- Wireless sensor networks are used in environmental monitoring, industrial automation, and smart homes.

#### Participatory Sensing Technology

- Participatory sensing technology enables individuals to collect, analyze, and share data using mobile devices.
- Participatory sensing technology is used in environmental monitoring, public health, and disaster response.

#### Embedded Platforms for IoT

- Embedded computing refers to the use of computer systems that are integrated into other devices or systems.
- Arduino, NetArduino, Raspberry Pi, Beagle Bone, Intel Galileo boards, and ARM cortex are some of the platforms that support IoT development.
- These platforms provide hardware and software components that enable developers to create IoT applications.

In summary, understanding the hardware components of IoT systems is essential for developing and deploying IoT applications. By leveraging sensors, RFID technology, wireless sensor networks, participatory sensing technology, and embedded platforms, developers can create innovative solutions for a wide range of use cases.
