### Sensors

Sensors are devices that detect and measure physical or chemical properties of the environment and convert them into electrical signals. These signals can then be processed by a microcontroller or a computer to extract useful information about the environment. Some common types of sensors used in IoT applications are:

- Digital sensors: These sensors convert physical or chemical properties into binary signals, such as on/off or high/low. Examples include temperature sensors, humidity sensors, light sensors, and motion sensors.

- Actuators: These devices are used to control physical systems based on the signals received from sensors. Examples include motors, valves, and relays.

- Radio Frequency Identification (RFID) technology: This technology uses radio waves to identify and track objects. RFID tags can be attached to objects and read by RFID readers, which can be integrated with IoT systems to track inventory or monitor the movement of goods.

- Wireless Sensor Networks (WSNs): These networks consist of multiple sensors that communicate with each other wirelessly to collect data from the environment. WSNs can be used for environmental monitoring, smart agriculture, and industrial automation.

- Participatory Sensing Technology: This technology involves using mobile devices, such as smartphones, to collect data from the environment. Users can contribute to data collection by installing apps that collect data from sensors on their devices and upload it to a central server.

### Embedded Platforms for IoT

Embedded platforms are hardware systems that are designed to perform specific functions and are integrated with sensors, actuators, and other components to form an IoT system. Some common embedded platforms used in IoT applications are:

- Arduino: An open-source platform for building electronics projects. Arduino boards are easy to use and can be programmed using a simple language.

- NetArduino: A variant of Arduino that is designed for networking applications. NetArduino boards have built-in Ethernet ports and can be used to build IoT systems that communicate over the Internet.

- Raspberry Pi: A credit-card sized computer that can be used for a variety of IoT applications. Raspberry Pi boards run a full-featured operating system and can be programmed using a variety of programming languages.

- Beagle Bone: A small, low-cost computer that is designed for embedded applications. Beagle Bone boards have a variety of built-in sensors and can be used for data collection and analysis.

- Intel Galileo: An Arduino-compatible board that is designed for IoT applications. Intel Galileo boards have built-in Ethernet ports and can be programmed using the Arduino development environment.

- ARM Cortex: A family of microcontroller and microprocessor cores that are widely used in embedded systems. ARM-based boards are powerful and energy-efficient and are commonly used in IoT applications.
