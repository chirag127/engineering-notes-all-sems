
### Wireless Medium Access Issues

* Wireless medium access issues are an important aspect of network and communication in the Internet of Things (IoT). 
* Medium access control (MAC) protocols are used to manage access to the wireless medium, and can be divided into two main categories: contention-based protocols and contention-free protocols. 
* Contention-based protocols, such as the 802.11 protocol, allow multiple devices to access the medium at the same time, while contention-free protocols, such as the 802.15.4 protocol, allow only one device to access the medium at a time. 
* Sensor deployment and node discovery are important aspects of IoT networks, as they allow devices to be placed in the environment and connected to the network. 
* Data aggregation and dissemination are also important aspects of IoT networks, as they allow data to be collected from multiple sources and distributed to multiple destinations. 
* Routing protocols are used to determine the best path between two nodes in a network, and can be divided into two main categories: proactive protocols and reactive protocols. 
* Proactive protocols, such as the OSPF protocol, use periodic messages to maintain up-to-date routing tables, while reactive protocols, such as the AODV protocol, use on-demand messages to update routing tables.
