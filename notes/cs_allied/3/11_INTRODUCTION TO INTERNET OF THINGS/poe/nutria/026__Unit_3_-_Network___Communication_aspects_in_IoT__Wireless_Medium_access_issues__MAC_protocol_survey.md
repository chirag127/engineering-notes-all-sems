
## Unit 3 - Network & Communication aspects in IoT:

* Wireless Medium access issues: Access to wireless medium is a critical issue in IoT networks. This is because the wireless medium is a shared resource and can be accessed by multiple nodes. To ensure that nodes can access the medium in a fair and efficient manner, different types of medium access control protocols have been developed. 

* MAC protocol survey: There are various types of MAC protocols for wireless networks, such as CSMA/CA, TDMA, FDMA, etc. Each of these protocols has its own advantages and disadvantages, and they can be used in different scenarios. 

* Survey routing protocols: Routing is another important aspect of IoT networks. Different types of routing protocols have been developed for different types of networks, such as Ad-hoc, Mesh, and Mobile Ad-hoc Networks. Each of these protocols has its own advantages and disadvantages, and they can be used in different scenarios. 

* Sensor deployment & Node discovery: Sensor deployment and node discovery are two important aspects of IoT networks. Sensor deployment involves the deployment of sensors in the environment, while node discovery involves the discovery of nodes in the network. 

* Data aggregation & dissemination: Data aggregation and dissemination are two important aspects of IoT networks. Data aggregation involves the aggregation of data from multiple sources, while data dissemination involves the dissemination of data to multiple destinations.
