
## Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT): 

- Vision: The Internet of Things (IoT) is a vision of a world in which physical objects are connected to the internet and can interact with each other and with the people who use them.

- Definition: IoT is a network of physical objects (devices, machines, people, animals, etc.) that are connected to the internet and can communicate with each other and with the people who use them.

- Conceptual Framework: The conceptual framework of IoT includes the following components: sensors, actuators, communication networks, data storage and analytics, and applications.

- Architectural View: The architectural view of IoT consists of four layers: physical layer, network layer, application layer, and service layer.

- Technology Behind IoT: The technology behind IoT includes sensors, actuators, communication networks, data storage and analytics, and applications.

- Sources of the IoT: The sources of the IoT are the physical objects that are connected to the internet and can communicate with each other and with the people who use them.

- M2M Communication: Machine-to-machine (M2M) communication is the communication between two or more machines, such as between a sensor and an actuator.

- IoT Examples: Examples of IoT include connected cars, smart homes, wearable devices, and connected medical devices.

- Design Principles for Connected Devices: The design principles for connected devices include IoT/M2M systems layers and design standardization, communication technologies, data enrichment and consolidation, ease of designing and affordability.
