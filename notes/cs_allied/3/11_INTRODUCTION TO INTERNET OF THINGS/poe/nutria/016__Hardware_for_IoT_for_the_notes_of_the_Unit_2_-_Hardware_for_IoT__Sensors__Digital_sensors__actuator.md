
### Hardware for IoT

* Sensors: Devices used to detect physical properties such as temperature, pressure, light, sound, etc.
* Digital sensors: Sensors that convert physical properties into digital signals, which can be read by computers.
* Actuators: Devices used to actuate physical objects, such as motors, pumps, valves, etc.
* Radio Frequency Identification (RFID) Technology: A technology for identifying objects using radio waves.
* Wireless Sensor Networks: Networks of sensors connected to each other wirelessly.
* Participatory Sensing Technology: Technology that allows people to collect data from their environment and share it with others.
