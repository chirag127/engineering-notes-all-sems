
### IoT/M2M Systems Layers and Design Standardization

The Internet of Things (IoT) is a vision of a world in which physical objects, such as machines and devices, are connected to the internet and can communicate with each other and with people. This vision requires the development of a set of technologies, such as communication technologies, data enrichment and consolidation, and design principles for connected devices that make it easier to design and deploy IoT solutions.

IoT/M2M systems layers and design standardization are essential components of the IoT vision. The layers of an IoT/M2M system typically include physical devices, networks, applications, and services. These layers must be designed to work together in order to create a secure, reliable, and efficient IoT solution.

Design standardization is also important in order to ensure that IoT solutions are affordable and interoperable. Standardization helps to reduce the cost of developing and deploying IoT solutions, and it also helps to ensure that different IoT solutions can interact with each other.

In order to ensure that IoT solutions are secure, reliable, and affordable, it is important to develop and adhere to design standards for the layers of an IoT/M2M system. These standards should include protocols for communication, data management, and security. Additionally, standards should be developed for the design of the physical devices, networks, and applications that make up an IoT/M2M system.
