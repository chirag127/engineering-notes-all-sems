
### Internet of Things (IoT): Vision

The Internet of Things (IoT) is a vision of a world where physical objects can be connected to the internet and communicate with each other. It is a concept that has been around since the early 1990s, and has since been gaining traction due to the rise of connected devices and the ubiquity of the internet.

The IoT has the potential to revolutionize the way we interact with the world, as it will enable us to monitor and control physical objects remotely. It has the potential to improve efficiency, reduce costs, and increase safety in many aspects of life.

The following are some of the key concepts related to the IoT:

* **Definition:** The IoT is defined as a network of physical objects, such as devices, vehicles, buildings and other items, that are embedded with electronics, software, sensors, and network connectivity, allowing them to collect and exchange data.

* **Conceptual Framework:** The IoT is based on a conceptual framework that consists of three layers: the physical layer, the communication layer, and the application layer.

* **Architectural View:** The IoT architecture is composed of four components: the device, the gateway, the cloud, and the application.

* **Technology Behind IoT:** The technology behind the IoT includes sensors, wireless communication, cloud computing, data storage and analytics, machine learning, and artificial intelligence.

* **Sources of the IoT:** The sources of the IoT include the internet, cellular networks, Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, and satellite networks.

* **M2M Communication:** M2M (machine-to-machine) communication is a type of communication in which two or more machines communicate with each other without human intervention.

* **IoT Examples:** Examples of the IoT include smart home devices, connected cars, and wearable technology.

* **Design Principles for Connected Devices:** Design principles for connected devices include IoT/M2M systems layers and design standardization, communication technologies, data enrichment and consolidation, ease of designing and affordability.
