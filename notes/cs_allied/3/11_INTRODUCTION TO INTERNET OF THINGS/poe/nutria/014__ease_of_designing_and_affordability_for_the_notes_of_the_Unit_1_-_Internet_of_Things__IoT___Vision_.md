
### Introduction to Internet of Things

Internet of Things (IoT) is a concept that refers to the interconnection of physical devices, vehicles, buildings and other items embedded with electronics, software, sensors, actuators, and network connectivity which enable these objects to collect and exchange data. It is a system of interrelated computing devices, mechanical and digital machines, objects, animals or people that are provided with unique identifiers and the ability to transfer data over a network without requiring human-to-human or human-to-computer interaction.

The concept of IoT has been around for many years, but its potential has only recently been realized. IoT enables connected devices to communicate with each other, providing data that can be used to improve efficiency and productivity.

#### Vision and Definition of IoT

The vision of IoT is to create a world where physical objects are seamlessly integrated into the information network, and where the physical environment can be sensed and controlled remotely across existing network infrastructure.

The definition of IoT is the network of physical objects that contain embedded technology to sense or interact with their internal states or the external environment. These objects can be uniquely identified through their embedded computing system and can interact with other connected devices.

#### Conceptual Framework and Architectural View of IoT

The conceptual framework of IoT is based on the idea of a distributed computing system, where multiple connected devices can communicate and exchange data with each other. The architecture of IoT consists of a network of connected devices, a communication infrastructure, and a platform that enables the integration of the devices.

The communication infrastructure of IoT consists of various technologies such as Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Zigbee, and NFC. This infrastructure enables the devices to communicate with each other and exchange data.

The platform of IoT enables the integration of the devices and provides the necessary tools to develop applications that can be used by the devices.

#### Technology Behind IoT

The technology behind IoT consists of various components such as sensors, actuators, controllers, and networks. Sensors are used to detect changes in the environment, such as temperature, pressure, and motion. Actuators are used to control devices, such as motors and lights. Controllers are used to manage the devices and networks. Networks are used to connect the devices and enable communication.

#### Sources of the IoT

The sources of the IoT can be divided into two categories: hardware and software. The hardware sources include sensors, actuators, controllers, and networks. The software sources include operating systems, middleware, and applications.

#### M2M Communication

Machine-to-machine (M2M) communication is a type of communication between two or more machines, where the machines can exchange data and information without the need for human intervention. M2M communication is used in IoT to enable communication between the connected devices.

#### IoT Examples

IoT is used in many different applications and industries. Some examples of IoT applications include smart homes, connected cars, smart cities, healthcare, and industrial automation.

#### Design Principles for Connected Devices

When designing connected devices, there are several principles that should be taken into consideration. These principles include:

- IoT/M2M systems layers and design standardization: This includes the design of the layers of the system, such as the hardware, software, and communication layers.
- Communication technologies: This includes the selection of the appropriate communication technologies for the system.
- Data enrichment and consolidation: This includes the enrichment and consolidation of data from the connected devices.
- Ease of designing and affordability: This includes the design of the system to be easy to use and cost-effective.
