
## Unit 2 - Hardware for IoT

1. Sensors: Sensors are devices that detect physical or environmental changes and provide input to the system. Examples of sensors include temperature, light, motion, and pressure sensors. 
2. Digital sensors: Digital sensors are sensors that convert physical or environmental changes into digital signals. Examples of digital sensors include digital temperature sensors, digital light sensors, digital motion sensors, and digital pressure sensors. 
3. Actuators: Actuators are devices that convert digital signals into physical or environmental changes. Examples of actuators include motors, valves, and relays. 
4. Radio Frequency Identification (RFID) Technology: RFID technology is a wireless communication technology that uses radio waves to identify and track objects. 
5. Wireless Sensor Networks: Wireless sensor networks are networks of sensors that can communicate with each other wirelessly. 
6. Participatory Sensing Technology: Participatory sensing technology is a type of technology that allows people to collect data from the environment. 
7. Embedded Computing Basics: Embedded computing is the use of computer technology in embedded systems. Embedded systems are electronic systems that are designed to perform specific tasks. 
8. Overview of IOT Supported Hardware Platforms: There are several hardware platforms that are used in the development of IoT applications. These platforms include Arduino, NetArduino, Raspberry pi, Beagle Bone, Intel Galileo boards, and ARM cortex.
