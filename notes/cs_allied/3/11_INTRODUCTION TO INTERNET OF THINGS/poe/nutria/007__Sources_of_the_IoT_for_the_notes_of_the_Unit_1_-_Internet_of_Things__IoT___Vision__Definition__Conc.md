
### Sources of the IoT

The Internet of Things (IoT) is a network of physical objects that are connected to the internet and can communicate with each other. These objects can be anything from everyday items such as phones and fridges, to more complex systems such as industrial machinery and medical equipment. The sources of the IoT are varied and include:

- **Sensors:** Sensors are used to detect changes in the environment and transmit data to the IoT. Examples of sensors include temperature sensors, motion sensors, and light sensors.

- **Connectivity:** Connectivity is the technology that allows devices to communicate with each other. Examples of connectivity technologies include Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and cellular networks.

- **Data storage and processing:** Data storage and processing are important components of the IoT. Data is stored in the cloud and processed by computers to create insights and enable automated actions.

- **Software:** Software is the instructions that tell devices how to operate and communicate with each other. Examples of software include operating systems, applications, and firmware.

- **Hardware:** Hardware is the physical components of the IoT, such as processors, memory, and sensors.

The design principles for connected devices include:

- **IoT/M2M systems layers and design standardization:** IoT/M2M systems should be designed using layers, with each layer responsible for a specific function. Standardization of the layers helps ensure interoperability and scalability.

- **Communication technologies:** Communication technologies such as Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and cellular networks are used to connect devices to the internet.

- **Data enrichment and consolidation:** Data enrichment and consolidation involve gathering data from multiple sources and combining it to create a unified view of the environment.

- **Ease of designing and affordability:** Connected devices should be designed in a way that makes them easy to use and affordable. This includes using standard components and open-source software.
