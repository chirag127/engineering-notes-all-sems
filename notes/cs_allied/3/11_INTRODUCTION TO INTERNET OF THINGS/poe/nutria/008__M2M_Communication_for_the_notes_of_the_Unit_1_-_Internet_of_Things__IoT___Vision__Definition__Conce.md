
### M2M Communication for the notes of the Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT):

* Machine-to-Machine (M2M) communication is a form of communication between devices, such as computers, sensors, and other electronic devices, over a network. 
* M2M communication is used in various fields such as manufacturing, healthcare, transportation, energy, and smart cities.
* M2M communication enables the exchange of data between two or more devices, allowing them to interact with each other and with other systems.
* It facilitates the connection of devices to the internet, allowing them to be monitored and controlled remotely.
* M2M communication is used in various applications, such as smart homes, connected cars, and industrial automation.
* It is also used in the Internet of Things (IoT), which is a network of connected devices that can communicate with each other and with other systems.
* IoT/M2M systems layers and design standardization involve the use of protocols such as MQTT, CoAP, and DDS for communication between devices.
* Communication technologies such as Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, NFC, and Zigbee are used to connect devices to the network.
* Data enrichment and consolidation involve the use of data analytics and machine learning to process and analyze data.
* Ease of designing and affordability are key factors in the development of connected devices.
