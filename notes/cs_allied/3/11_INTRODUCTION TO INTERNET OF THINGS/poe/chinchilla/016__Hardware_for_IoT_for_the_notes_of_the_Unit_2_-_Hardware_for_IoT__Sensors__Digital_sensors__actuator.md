### Hardware for IoT

The hardware used in IoT systems is an essential component that enables the collection, processing, and transmission of data. Here are some of the key components of IoT hardware:

#### Sensors

Sensors are devices that detect and measure physical or environmental conditions, such as temperature, humidity, light, and motion. These sensors can be analog or digital and can be used in a variety of IoT applications.

#### Digital Sensors

Digital sensors are a type of sensor that convert physical signals into digital signals that can be processed by a computer. Examples of digital sensors include accelerometers, gyroscopes, and magnetometers.

#### Actuators

Actuators are devices that convert electrical signals into physical actions, such as turning on a light or opening a door. These devices are used in IoT systems to control physical objects and enable automation.

#### Radio Frequency Identification (RFID) Technology

RFID technology is used to identify and track objects using radio waves. This technology is commonly used in inventory management and asset tracking applications.

#### Wireless Sensor Networks

Wireless sensor networks are collections of sensors that communicate wirelessly to enable remote monitoring and control of physical systems. These networks can be used in a variety of IoT applications, including environmental monitoring and industrial automation.

#### Participatory Sensing Technology

Participatory sensing technology is a type of IoT application that enables individuals to collect and share data using their mobile devices. This technology is commonly used in environmental monitoring and citizen science projects.

#### Embedded Platforms for IoT

Embedded platforms are computing systems that are designed to be integrated into physical systems. These platforms are used in IoT applications to enable data collection, processing, and transmission. Some of the popular IoT-supported hardware platforms include:

- Arduino
- NetArduino
- Raspberry Pi
- BeagleBone
- Intel Galileo boards
- ARM Cortex

#### Embedded Computing Basics

Embedded computing is a type of computing system that is designed to perform a specific function within a larger system. These systems are typically low-power and have limited processing capabilities. Understanding the basics of embedded computing is essential for developing IoT applications.
