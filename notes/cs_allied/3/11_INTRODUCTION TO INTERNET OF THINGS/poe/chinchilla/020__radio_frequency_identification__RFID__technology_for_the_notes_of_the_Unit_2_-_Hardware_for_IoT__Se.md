### Radio Frequency Identification (RFID) Technology

Radio Frequency Identification (RFID) is a wireless technology that uses radio waves to identify and track objects. It is widely used in various industries, including supply chain management, retail, healthcare, and transportation. In the context of IoT, RFID technology is used to collect data from objects and send it to other devices for processing and analysis. Here are some key points about RFID technology:

- RFID technology consists of two components: an RFID reader and an RFID tag. The reader sends out a radio signal that is received by the tag, which then responds with its unique identifier.
- RFID tags can be either passive or active. Passive tags do not have a power source and rely on the energy from the reader to transmit their data. Active tags have their own power source and can transmit data over longer distances.
- RFID technology can be used to track and manage inventory, monitor the movement of goods, and identify and authenticate products.
- RFID technology is also used in access control systems, such as in hotels and office buildings, to control entry and exit.
- In healthcare, RFID technology is used to track equipment and supplies, monitor patient movement, and ensure medication compliance.
- RFID technology is also used in agriculture to track livestock and monitor crop growth.

Overall, RFID technology is a powerful tool for collecting and managing data in a variety of industries. Its ability to track and identify objects in real-time makes it an essential component of many IoT applications.
