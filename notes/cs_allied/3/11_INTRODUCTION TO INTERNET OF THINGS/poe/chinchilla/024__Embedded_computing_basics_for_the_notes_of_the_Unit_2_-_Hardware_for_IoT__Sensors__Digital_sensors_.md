### Embedded Computing Basics for IoT

In the world of the Internet of Things (IoT), embedded computing plays a vital role. It is a technology that enables the communication between devices and the internet. In this unit, we will learn about the basics of embedded computing and various hardware platforms that support IoT.

#### Embedded Computing Basics

1. Embedded computing is a technology that is used to develop specialized computer systems that are designed to perform a specific task.

2. These systems are usually small, low-cost, and have limited resources such as memory, processing power, and storage.

3. Embedded systems are commonly used in devices such as home appliances, medical equipment, automobiles, and industrial automation systems.

4. The embedded system consists of three main components: hardware, software, and firmware.

5. Hardware includes components such as microprocessors, memory, and input/output interfaces.

6. Software is the set of instructions that the system follows to perform a specific task.

7. The firmware is a type of software that is embedded in the hardware and is responsible for controlling the hardware components.

#### IoT Supported Hardware Platforms

1. Arduino is an open-source hardware platform that is used for building electronic projects. It consists of a microcontroller board and a software development environment.

2. NetArduino is a wireless sensor network platform that is based on the Arduino platform.

3. Raspberry Pi is a low-cost, credit-card-sized computer that can be used for a variety of purposes, including IoT projects.

4. BeagleBone is a single-board computer that is designed for use in embedded applications. It is built on the ARM architecture and has a variety of connectivity options.

5. Intel Galileo is an open-source platform that is designed for IoT projects. It is based on the Intel Quark processor and has a variety of connectivity options.

6. ARM Cortex is a family of microprocessors that are designed for use in embedded applications. They are widely used in IoT devices due to their low power consumption and high processing power.

In summary, embedded computing is a critical technology for the Internet of Things. It enables communication between devices and the internet and allows for the development of specialized computer systems that are designed to perform specific tasks. There are various hardware platforms that support IoT, including Arduino, NetArduino, Raspberry Pi, BeagleBone, Intel Galileo, and ARM Cortex. Understanding the basics of embedded computing and these platforms is essential for developing IoT projects.
