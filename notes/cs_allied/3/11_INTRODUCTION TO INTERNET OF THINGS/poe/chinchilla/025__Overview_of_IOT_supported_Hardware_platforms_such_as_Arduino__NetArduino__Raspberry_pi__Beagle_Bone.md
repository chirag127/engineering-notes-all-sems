### Overview of IOT Supported Hardware Platforms

The Internet of Things (IoT) has revolutionized the way devices communicate with each other, making it possible to collect and analyze data in real-time. To enable this, there are several hardware platforms that are designed to support IoT. In this article, we will discuss some of the popular IoT-supported hardware platforms such as Arduino, NetArduino, Raspberry Pi, Beagle Bone, Intel Galileo boards, and ARM Cortex.

#### Arduino

Arduino is an open-source hardware and software platform that is designed for building electronics projects. It is based on a microcontroller board that can be programmed using the Arduino Integrated Development Environment (IDE). Arduino is popular among hobbyists and professionals alike because it is easy to use and has a large community of developers.

#### NetArduino

NetArduino is a variant of the Arduino platform that is designed for building IoT applications. It is based on the same microcontroller board as Arduino, but it also includes an Ethernet shield that enables it to connect to the internet. NetArduino can be programmed using the Arduino IDE, and it supports a wide range of sensors and actuators.

#### Raspberry Pi

Raspberry Pi is a credit-card sized computer that is designed for building IoT applications. It runs on the Linux operating system and is capable of running a wide range of applications. Raspberry Pi is popular among hobbyists and professionals alike because it is easy to use and has a large community of developers.

#### Beagle Bone

Beagle Bone is a single-board computer that is designed for building IoT applications. It is based on the ARM Cortex-A8 processor and runs on the Linux operating system. Beagle Bone is popular among hobbyists and professionals alike because it is easy to use and has a large community of developers.

#### Intel Galileo Boards

Intel Galileo Boards are a series of single-board computers that are designed for building IoT applications. They are based on the Intel Quark SoC X1000 processor and are capable of running a wide range of applications. Intel Galileo Boards are popular among hobbyists and professionals alike because they are easy to use and have a large community of developers.

#### ARM Cortex

ARM Cortex is a family of microcontrollers that are designed for building IoT applications. They are based on the ARM architecture and are capable of running a wide range of applications. ARM Cortex is popular among hobbyists and professionals alike because it is easy to use and has a large community of developers.

### Conclusion

In conclusion, there are several hardware platforms that are designed to support IoT. These platforms are designed to make it easy to build and deploy IoT applications, and they have a large community of developers who are constantly developing new libraries and frameworks to make building IoT applications even easier. Whether you are a hobbyist or a professional, there is an IoT-supported hardware platform that is right for you.
