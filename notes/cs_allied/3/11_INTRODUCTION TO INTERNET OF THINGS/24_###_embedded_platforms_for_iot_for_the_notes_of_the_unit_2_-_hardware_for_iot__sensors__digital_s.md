Embedded platforms for IoT are specialized computer systems that are designed specifically for use in IoT applications. These platforms are designed to be compact, low-power, and highly integrated, making them ideal for use in IoT devices and systems. 

Embedded computing basics include understanding the hardware and software components of embedded systems, including microcontrollers, memory, and input/output devices. 

There are several popular hardware platforms that are supported by IoT, including:

1. Arduino: An open-source platform that is widely used for IoT applications due to its ease of use and low cost.

2. NetArduino: A network-enabled version of the Arduino platform that includes Ethernet connectivity.

3. Raspberry Pi: A low-cost, high-performance platform that is popular for IoT applications due to its versatility and ease of use.

4. BeagleBone: An open-source platform that is designed for high-performance IoT applications.

5. Intel Galileo boards: A range of boards that are designed for use in IoT applications and are based on the Intel x86 architecture.

6. ARM Cortex: A range of processors that are designed for use in IoT applications and are based on the ARM architecture.

Embedded platforms for IoT are critical components of IoT systems, as they provide the computing power and connectivity that are necessary for devices to communicate and exchange data. By choosing the right platform for their IoT applications, organizations can ensure that their devices and systems are reliable, efficient, and effective.
