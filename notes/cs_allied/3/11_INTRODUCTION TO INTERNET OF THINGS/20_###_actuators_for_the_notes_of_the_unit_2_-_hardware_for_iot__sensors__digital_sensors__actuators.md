Actuators are devices that convert an electrical signal into a physical action. In the context of the Internet of Things (IoT), actuators are used to control and manipulate physical devices, such as motors, lights, and valves. They are essential components of IoT systems, allowing connected devices to interact with the physical world.

Actuators can be classified into two main categories:
1. Electro-mechanical actuators: These actuators use electrical signals to control mechanical motion, such as motors and solenoids.
2. Electro-thermal actuators: These actuators use electrical signals to control temperature, such as heating elements and cooling fans.

Actuators can be controlled by various types of signals, including digital signals, analog signals, and PWM signals. The choice of actuator depends on the specific requirements of the IoT application, such as the type of physical action required and the level of precision needed.

Overall, actuators are an important component of IoT systems, allowing connected devices to interact with the physical world and perform specific tasks. They play a crucial role in the development of IoT applications, such as smart homes, connected vehicles, and industrial automation systems.
