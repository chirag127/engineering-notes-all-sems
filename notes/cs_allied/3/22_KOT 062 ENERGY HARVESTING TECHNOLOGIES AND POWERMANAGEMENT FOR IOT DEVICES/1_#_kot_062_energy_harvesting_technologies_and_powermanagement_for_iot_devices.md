# KOT 062 ENERGY HARVESTING TECHNOLOGIES AND POWERMANAGEMENT FOR IOT DEVICES

"KOT 062 Energy Harvesting Technologies and Power Management for IoT Devices" is a course that focuses on the design and implementation of energy harvesting and power management systems for Internet of Things (IoT) devices. The course is designed to provide students with an understanding of the challenges and opportunities associated with energy harvesting and power management in the context of IoT, as well as the skills and knowledge needed to design and implement energy harvesting and power management systems for IoT devices.

In the course, students will learn about the various energy harvesting technologies, including solar, kinetic, thermal, and radio frequency (RF) energy harvesting, and will explore the use of these technologies in the context of IoT. Students will also learn about the different types of power management systems, including voltage regulators, battery management systems, and power management integrated circuits (PMICs), and will explore the use of these systems in the context of IoT.

In addition, students will learn about the challenges and opportunities associated with energy harvesting and power management in IoT, including issues related to energy efficiency, reliability, and scalability, as well as the challenges of integrating energy harvesting and power management systems into IoT devices.

The course will also cover the use of energy harvesting and power management tools and platforms, including simulation tools, prototyping platforms, and evaluation boards, and will provide hands-on experience with these tools through the use of case studies and practical exercises.

Overall, "KOT 062 Energy Harvesting Technologies and Power Management for IoT Devices" is a comprehensive course that provides students with a deep understanding of the design and implementation of energy harvesting and power management systems for Internet of Things (IoT) devices, and the skills and knowledge needed to design and implement these systems in real-world applications.
