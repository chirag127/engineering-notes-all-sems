Photovoltaic (PV) cell technologies refer to the methods and technologies used to convert sunlight into electricity. These technologies are an important aspect of the Unit 1 - Energy Harvesting Systems in the subject of KOT 062 Energy Harvesting Technologies and Power Management for IoT Devices KCS.

PV cells are made up of semiconductor materials, such as silicon, that absorb photons from sunlight and convert them into electrons. These electrons are then collected and used to generate electricity. The efficiency of a PV cell is determined by the ability of the cell to convert sunlight into electricity, and by the materials used in the cell.

There are several types of PV cell technologies, including monocrystalline, polycrystalline, and thin-film PV cells. Each type of cell has its own advantages and disadvantages, such as cost, efficiency, and durability.

It is also important to understand the challenges associated with PV cell technologies, such as the cost of production, the limited availability of materials, and the need for efficient and effective power management systems to ensure that the electricity generated by the cells is used in an efficient and sustainable manner.
