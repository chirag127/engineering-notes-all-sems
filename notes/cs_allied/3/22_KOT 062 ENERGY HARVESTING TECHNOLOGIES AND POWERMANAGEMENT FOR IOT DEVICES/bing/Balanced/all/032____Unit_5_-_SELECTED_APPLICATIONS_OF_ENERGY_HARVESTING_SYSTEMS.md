## Unit 5 - SELECTED APPLICATIONS OF ENERGY HARVESTING SYSTEMS

- Energy harvesting systems are devices that collect ambient energy from the environment and convert it into electrical energy for powering wireless devices, sensors, and autonomous systems.
- Energy harvesting systems can extend the battery life, reduce the maintenance cost, and improve the reliability of remote and inaccessible systems that rely on batteries or external power sources.
- Energy harvesting systems can use various types of ambient energy sources, such as solar, thermal, kinetic, electromagnetic, and chemical energy.
- Some of the selected applications of energy harvesting systems are:

  - Industrial applications: Energy harvesting systems can be used for remote monitoring, predictive maintenance, and energy-efficient solutions in industrial sectors, such as manufacturing, oil and gas, mining, and agriculture. For example, energy harvesting systems can power wireless sensors that measure temperature, pressure, vibration, humidity, and gas levels in industrial equipment and environments .
  - Consumer electronics applications: Energy harvesting systems can be used for powering wearable devices, smart watches, fitness trackers, and wireless keyboards and mice that require low power and frequent charging. For example, energy harvesting systems can use body heat, motion, or light to power wearable devices that monitor health, fitness, and activity levels .
  - Building and home automation applications: Energy harvesting systems can be used for controlling lighting, heating, ventilation, air conditioning, security, and fire detection systems in buildings and homes. For example, energy harvesting systems can use light, motion, or temperature differences to power wireless switches, sensors, and actuators that regulate the indoor environment and save energy .
  - Wireless sensor network applications: Energy harvesting systems can be used for enabling self-powered and autonomous wireless sensor networks that collect and transmit data for various purposes, such as environmental monitoring, disaster management, smart grid, and military surveillance. For example, energy harvesting systems can use solar, wind, or ocean waves to power wireless sensors that monitor weather, air quality, soil moisture, seismic activity, and oceanographic parameters .
  - Security system applications: Energy harvesting systems can be used for powering security devices, such as locks, alarms, cameras, and biometric sensors that require high reliability and low maintenance. For example, energy harvesting systems can use light, motion, or vibration to power security devices that detect intruders, fire, smoke, or unauthorized access .
