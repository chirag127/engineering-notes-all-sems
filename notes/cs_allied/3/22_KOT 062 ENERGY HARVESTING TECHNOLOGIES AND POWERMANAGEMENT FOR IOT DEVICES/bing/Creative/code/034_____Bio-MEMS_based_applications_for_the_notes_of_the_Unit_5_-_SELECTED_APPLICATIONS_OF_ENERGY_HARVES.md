### Bio-MEMS based applications

Bio-MEMS are micro-electro-mechanical systems that are used for biomedical or biological purposes. They may or may not include any electrical or mechanical functions. They can be classified into four main categories:

- Biomedical transducers: These are devices that convert one form of energy into another, such as pressure, temperature, or chemical signals into electrical signals. They are used for sensing, monitoring, or diagnosing various physiological parameters or biomarkers. Examples are blood pressure sensors, glucose sensors, and DNA microarrays.
- Microfluidics: These are devices that manipulate small volumes of fluids, such as blood, urine, or saliva, in microchannels or chambers. They are used for performing various laboratory processes or experiments on a single chip, such as mixing, separation, detection, or reaction. Examples are lab-on-a-chip, micro-total analysis systems, and organ-on-a-chip.
- Medical implants: These are devices that are implanted into the body to perform a therapeutic function or to replace a damaged organ or tissue. They are made of biocompatible materials and often have wireless communication or power capabilities. Examples are pacemakers, cochlear implants, and drug delivery systems.
- Microsurgical tools: These are devices that are used to perform minimally invasive surgery or manipulation of cells or tissues. They are often integrated with optical or electrical components to provide feedback or control. Examples are microscalpels, microneedles, and microgrippers.

Bio-MEMS have many advantages over conventional biomedical devices, such as miniaturization, integration, accuracy, sensitivity, specificity, low cost, and low power consumption. They also have some challenges, such as biocompatibility, sterilization, reliability, and scalability. Bio-MEMS are expected to have a significant impact on the fields of medicine, biology, and biotechnology in the future. The global Bio-MEMS market is projected to grow from $1.9 billion in 2012 to $6.6 billion in 2018.
