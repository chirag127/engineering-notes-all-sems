### Micro fabricated coils and magnetic materials

- Micro fabricated coils are miniature coils that are fabricated using microelectromechanical systems (MEMS) technology. They can be used for various applications, such as magnetooptical data storage, magnetic circuit integration, electromagnetic energy harvesting, and magnetic neurostimulation.
- Micro fabricated coils can be classified into two types: planar and three-dimensional. Planar coils are fabricated on a single substrate layer, while three-dimensional coils are fabricated on multiple substrate layers or using a sacrificial layer technique.
- The performance of micro fabricated coils depends on several factors, such as the coil geometry, the coil material, the magnetic core material, the insulation layer, and the fabrication process. The main parameters that characterize the coil performance are the inductance, the resistance, the quality factor, and the magnetic field strength.
- The advantages of micro fabricated coils over conventional wire-wound coils are the higher integration density, the lower parasitic capacitance, the higher reliability, and the possibility of batch fabrication. However, micro fabricated coils also face some challenges, such as the lower inductance, the higher resistance, the lower quality factor, and the fabrication limitations.
- Magnetic materials are materials that exhibit magnetic properties, such as magnetization, permeability, coercivity, and remanence. They can be classified into three types: diamagnetic, paramagnetic, and ferromagnetic. Diamagnetic materials are repelled by a magnetic field, paramagnetic materials are weakly attracted by a magnetic field, and ferromagnetic materials are strongly attracted by a magnetic field and can retain their magnetization after the field is removed.
- Magnetic materials can be used for various applications, such as magnetic recording, magnetic sensors, magnetic actuators, and magnetic shielding. The main properties that determine the suitability of a magnetic material for a specific application are the saturation magnetization, the coercivity, the remanence, the permeability, and the Curie temperature.
- The most common magnetic materials used for micro fabricated coils are soft magnetic materials and hard magnetic materials. Soft magnetic materials have low coercivity and high permeability, which means they can be easily magnetized and demagnetized by an external magnetic field. They are used for magnetic cores, magnetic sensors, and magnetic actuators. Hard magnetic materials have high coercivity and low permeability, which means they can retain their magnetization without an external magnetic field. They are used for permanent magnets, magnetic recording, and magnetic energy harvesting.
