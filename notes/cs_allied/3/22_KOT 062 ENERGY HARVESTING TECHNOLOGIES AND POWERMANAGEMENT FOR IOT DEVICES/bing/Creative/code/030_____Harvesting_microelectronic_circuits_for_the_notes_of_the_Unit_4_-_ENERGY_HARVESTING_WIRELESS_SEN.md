### Harvesting Microelectronic Circuits

- Harvesting microelectronic circuits are circuits that can convert ambient energy sources, such as light, heat, vibration, or radio waves, into electrical energy for powering wireless sensors and other low-power devices  .
- Harvesting microelectronic circuits consist of three main components: a transducer, a rectifier, and a regulator  .
  - A transducer is a device that converts one form of energy into another, such as a photovoltaic cell, a thermoelectric generator, a piezoelectric element, or an antenna  .
  - A rectifier is a circuit that converts alternating current (AC) into direct current (DC), such as a diode bridge, a voltage doubler, or a charge pump  .
  - A regulator is a circuit that maintains a constant output voltage or current, such as a linear regulator, a switching regulator, or a maximum power point tracking (MPPT) circuit  .
- Harvesting microelectronic circuits have several design challenges, such as low input power, variable input voltage, high conversion efficiency, low quiescent power, and small size  .
  - Low input power means that the harvested energy is often insufficient to directly power the load, and requires intermediate energy storage, such as a capacitor or a battery  .
  - Variable input voltage means that the output voltage of the transducer depends on the ambient conditions, such as the intensity of light, the temperature difference, the frequency of vibration, or the strength of radio waves  .
  - High conversion efficiency means that the harvesting circuit should minimize the power losses in the rectifier and the regulator, and maximize the power transfer from the transducer to the load  .
  - Low quiescent power means that the harvesting circuit should consume as little power as possible when the load is not active, and avoid draining the energy storage  .
  - Small size means that the harvesting circuit should fit within the dimensions of the wireless sensor or the low-power device, and use microelectronic components and techniques  .
- Harvesting microelectronic circuits have several applications, such as wireless sensor networks, biomedical implants, wearable devices, smart buildings, and environmental monitoring  .
  - Wireless sensor networks are networks of distributed sensors that communicate wirelessly and collect data from the environment, such as temperature, humidity, pressure, or motion  .
  - Biomedical implants are devices that are implanted in the human body and perform functions such as monitoring, diagnosis, or therapy, such as pacemakers, cochlear implants, or glucose sensors  .
  - Wearable devices are devices that are worn on the body and provide functions such as fitness tracking, health monitoring, or entertainment, such as smart watches, smart glasses, or smart clothing  .
  - Smart buildings are buildings that use sensors, actuators, and controllers to optimize the energy consumption, comfort, and security, such as smart lighting, smart heating, or smart security  .
  - Environmental monitoring is the observation and measurement of the physical, chemical, and biological conditions of the environment, such as air quality, water quality, or soil quality  .
