### Examples for the notes of the Unit 4 - ENERGY HARVESTING WIRELESS SENSORS in the subject of KOT 062 ENERGY HARVESTING TECHNOLOGIES AND POWERMANAGEMENT FOR IOT DEVICES KCS

- Energy harvesting wireless sensors are devices that can collect and convert ambient energy from their environment into electrical energy for powering themselves and transmitting data .
- Energy harvesting wireless sensors can use various sources of energy, such as solar, thermal, vibration, radio frequency, and human motion .
- Energy harvesting wireless sensors have several advantages over battery-powered sensors, such as:
  - They eliminate the need for battery replacement and maintenance, which can reduce the cost and environmental impact of sensor networks  .
  - They enable the deployment of sensors in hard-to-reach or hazardous locations, where battery replacement is impractical or dangerous  .
  - They increase the reliability and lifetime of sensor networks, as they do not depend on the availability and capacity of batteries  .
- Energy harvesting wireless sensors have various applications in different domains, such as:
  - Smart buildings: Energy harvesting wireless sensors can monitor and control temperature, humidity, lighting, occupancy, and air quality in buildings, and optimize energy efficiency and comfort .
  - Industrial IoT: Energy harvesting wireless sensors can measure and transmit parameters such as pressure, flow, level, vibration, and torque in industrial machines and processes, and enable predictive maintenance and remote diagnostics .
  - Healthcare: Energy harvesting wireless sensors can be embedded in wearable devices or implanted in the human body, and monitor vital signs, activity, and posture, and provide feedback and alerts .
  - Environmental monitoring: Energy harvesting wireless sensors can be deployed in large-scale and distributed networks, and collect and transmit data on weather, soil, water, air, and wildlife, and support disaster prevention and management .
