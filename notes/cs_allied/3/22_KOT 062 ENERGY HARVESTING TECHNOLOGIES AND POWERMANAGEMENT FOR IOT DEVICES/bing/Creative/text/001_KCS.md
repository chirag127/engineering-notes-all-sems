# KCS

KCS stands for Knowledge-Centered Service, a best practice methodology for service organizations to work more effectively with knowledge. 

## Benefits of KCS

- It improves service delivery by capturing, structuring, and reusing knowledge as part of the support process. 
- It reduces costs by eliminating redundant work and increasing efficiency. 
- It increases customer satisfaction by providing faster and more accurate solutions. 
- It enhances employee engagement by empowering them to create and share knowledge. 
- It fosters continuous improvement by measuring and improving knowledge quality and usage. 

## Principles of KCS

- Knowledge is a by-product of solving problems. 
- Knowledge should be captured and structured in the context of the customer. 
- Knowledge should be created as a collaborative effort. 
- Knowledge should be reused and improved over time. 
- Knowledge should be accessible to anyone who needs it. 

## Practices of KCS

- KCS has four main practices: Capture, Structure, Reuse, and Improve. 
- Capture: This practice involves capturing knowledge in the workflow, as close to the source as possible, using the customer's context and words. 
- Structure: This practice involves structuring knowledge in a consistent and logical way, using templates, metadata, and keywords. 
- Reuse: This practice involves reusing existing knowledge to solve problems, and updating or flagging it as needed. 
- Improve: This practice involves measuring and improving the quality, relevance, and effectiveness of knowledge, using feedback, analytics, and governance.
