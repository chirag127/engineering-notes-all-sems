### Micro and macro scale implementations for the notes of the Unit 3 - ELECTROMAGNETIC ENERGY HARVESTING AND NON-LINEAR TECHNIQUES

Electromagnetic energy harvesting (EMEH) is a technique that converts ambient mechanical vibrations into electrical energy using electromagnetic induction. EMEH devices can be used to power wireless sensor nodes, wearable electronics, biomedical implants and other low-power applications. EMEH devices can be classified into two categories based on their scale: micro and macro.

- Micro-scale EMEH devices are typically fabricated using microelectromechanical systems (MEMS) technology and have dimensions in the order of micrometers to millimeters. They can harvest energy from high-frequency vibrations (above 100 Hz) and have power outputs in the range of nanowatts to microwatts. Micro-scale EMEH devices can be integrated with CMOS circuits and sensors to form self-powered systems. Some examples of micro-scale EMEH devices are:

  - Cantilever-based EMEH devices: These devices consist of a flexible beam with a permanent magnet attached to its tip and a coil placed underneath. The beam vibrates due to external excitation and induces a voltage in the coil. The power output depends on the resonance frequency, damping ratio, magnet size and coil parameters of the device. Cantilever-based EMEH devices can be designed to have linear or nonlinear behavior by using different beam shapes, materials and boundary conditions.
  - Spring-mass-damper EMEH devices: These devices consist of a mass attached to a spring and a damper, and a coil wound around the mass. The mass oscillates due to external excitation and induces a voltage in the coil. The power output depends on the resonance frequency, damping ratio, mass size and coil parameters of the device. Spring-mass-damper EMEH devices can be designed to have linear or nonlinear behavior by using different spring and damper elements.
  - Magnetic levitation EMEH devices: These devices consist of a magnet levitated by another magnet or an electromagnet, and a coil placed around the levitated magnet. The levitated magnet vibrates due to external excitation and induces a voltage in the coil. The power output depends on the levitation gap, magnetic force, damping ratio and coil parameters of the device. Magnetic levitation EMEH devices can have broadband or narrowband behavior depending on the levitation stability and the excitation frequency.

- Macro-scale EMEH devices are typically fabricated using conventional machining and assembly techniques and have dimensions in the order of centimeters to meters. They can harvest energy from low-frequency vibrations (below 100 Hz) and have power outputs in the range of milliwatts to watts. Macro-scale EMEH devices can be used to power large-scale systems such as buildings, bridges, vehicles and industrial machines. Some examples of macro-scale EMEH devices are:

  - Linear generator EMEH devices: These devices consist of a movable permanent magnet and a fixed coil. The magnet moves linearly due to external excitation and induces a voltage in the coil. The power output depends on the stroke length, magnet size, coil parameters and load resistance of the device. Linear generator EMEH devices can be designed to have linear or nonlinear behavior by using different magnet arrangements, springs and dampers.
  - Rotary generator EMEH devices: These devices consist of a rotating permanent magnet and a fixed coil. The magnet rotates due to external excitation and induces a voltage in the coil. The power output depends on the rotation speed, magnet size, coil parameters and load resistance of the device. Rotary generator EMEH devices can be designed to have linear or nonlinear behavior by using different magnet arrangements, gears and dampers.
  - Hybrid EMEH devices: These devices combine linear and rotary generators to harvest energy from both translational and rotational vibrations. The power output depends on the coupling mechanism, magnet size, coil parameters and load resistance of the device. Hybrid EMEH devices can be designed to have linear or nonlinear behavior by using different coupling mechanisms, springs and dampers.
