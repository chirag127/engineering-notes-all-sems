### Basic principles for the notes of the Unit 3 - ELECTROMAGNETIC ENERGY HARVESTING AND NON-LINEAR TECHNIQUES in the subject of KOT 062 ENERGY HARVESTING TECHNOLOGIES AND POWERMANAGEMENT FOR IOT DEVICES KCS

- Electromagnetic energy harvesting (EMEH) is a process of converting ambient mechanical vibrations into electrical energy using electromagnetic transducers .
- EMEH devices typically consist of a coil and a magnet, which induce a voltage across the coil when they move relative to each other due to external vibrations .
- EMEH devices can be classified into linear and nonlinear types, depending on the restoring force of the system.
- Linear EMEH devices have a constant restoring force, which results in a fixed natural frequency and a narrow bandwidth of operation. They are suitable for applications where the vibration frequency is known and stable.
- Nonlinear EMEH devices have a variable restoring force, which results in a frequency-dependent natural frequency and a wide bandwidth of operation. They are suitable for applications where the vibration frequency is unknown or variable.
- Nonlinear EMEH devices can be further classified into different categories, such as bistable, monostable, Duffing, magnetic levitation, etc., depending on the nonlinear mechanism involved  .
- Nonlinear EMEH devices can achieve higher power output and efficiency than linear EMEH devices, especially at lower frequencies  .
- Nonlinear EMEH devices require appropriate interface circuits and power management circuits to optimize the energy conversion and storage .
- Nonlinear EMEH devices can be integrated with other energy harvesting mechanisms, such as thermoelectric, piezoelectric, electrostatic and triboelectric, to enhance the performance and reliability of the system.
