# Unit 4 - ENERGY HARVESTING WIRELESS SENSORS

- Energy harvesting wireless sensors are devices that can collect and convert ambient energy from their environment into electrical energy for powering themselves and transmitting data  .
- Energy harvesting wireless sensors can eliminate the need for batteries and wires, which can reduce maintenance costs, environmental impact and installation complexity  .
- Energy harvesting wireless sensors can use various sources of energy, such as solar, thermal, vibration, radio frequency, wind, etc. depending on the availability and suitability of the energy source  .
- Energy harvesting wireless sensors typically consist of four main components: an energy harvester, an energy storage unit, a sensor and a wireless transceiver .
- Energy harvesting wireless sensors can be used for various applications, such as smart buildings, industrial automation, environmental monitoring, healthcare, agriculture, etc. where conventional power sources are not feasible or desirable   .
