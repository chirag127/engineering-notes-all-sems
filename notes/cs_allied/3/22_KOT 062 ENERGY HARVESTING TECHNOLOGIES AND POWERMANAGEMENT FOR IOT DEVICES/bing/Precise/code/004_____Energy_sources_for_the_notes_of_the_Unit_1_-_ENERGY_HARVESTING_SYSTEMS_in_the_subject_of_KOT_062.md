### Energy sources for the notes of the Unit 1 - ENERGY HARVESTING SYSTEMS in the subject of KOT 062 ENERGY HARVESTING TECHNOLOGIES AND POWERMANAGEMENT FOR IOT DEVICES KCS

1. Solar energy: Solar energy is a renewable energy source that can be harvested using photovoltaic cells to convert sunlight into electricity.
2. Wind energy: Wind energy is another renewable energy source that can be harvested using wind turbines to convert the kinetic energy of wind into electricity.
3. Hydro energy: Hydro energy is a renewable energy source that can be harvested using water turbines to convert the kinetic energy of flowing water into electricity.
4. Thermal energy: Thermal energy is a renewable energy source that can be harvested using thermoelectric generators to convert temperature differences into electricity.
5. Mechanical energy: Mechanical energy is a renewable energy source that can be harvested using piezoelectric materials to convert mechanical stress or strain into electricity.
6. Radio frequency energy: Radio frequency energy is a renewable energy source that can be harvested using antennas to convert electromagnetic waves into electricity.
