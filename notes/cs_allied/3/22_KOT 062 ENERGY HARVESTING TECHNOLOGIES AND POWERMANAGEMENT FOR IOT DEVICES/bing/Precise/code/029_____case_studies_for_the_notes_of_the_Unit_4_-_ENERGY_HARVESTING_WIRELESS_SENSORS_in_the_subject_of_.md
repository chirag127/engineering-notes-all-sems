### Case Studies for the Notes of the Unit 4 - ENERGY HARVESTING WIRELESS SENSORS in the Subject of KOT 062 ENERGY HARVESTING TECHNOLOGIES AND POWERMANAGEMENT FOR IOT DEVICES KCS

1. **Electric Field Energy Harvesting Powered Wireless Sensors for Smart Grid**: In this case study, the harvested average power is about 47 µW. The energy can be harvested without removing the insulating sheath, making the proposed harvesting technology applicable to power sensor nodes in wireless ubiquitous sensor networks and smart grid systems.

2. **Energy Harvesting in Wireless Sensor Networks**: This taxonomic survey discusses the attention that wireless sensor networks (WSNs) have received due to their extensive deployment in the emerging fields of the Internet of Things (IoT) and self-driven devices. However, a major bottleneck in WSN technologies is their limited energy.

3. **Energy Harvesting for Wireless Sensor Networks**: This book is organized into four major parts. The first part introduces essential fundamentals and methods, while the second part focuses on vibration converters and hybridization. The third part is dedicated to wireless energy transfer, including both RF and inductive energy transfer.

4. **Solar Energy Harvesting System for Wireless Sensors**: This case study presents the general block diagram of a solar energy harvesting system for wireless sensors. The energy storage device is used to store generated energy as well as buffer the power required by sensor nodes.
