### Harvesters

Harvesters are devices that convert ambient energy sources into usable electrical energy. In the context of Unit 2 - Piezo-Electric Energy Harvesting and Electromechanical Modeling, of the subject KOT 062 Energy Harvesting Technologies and Power Management for IoT Devices KCS, harvesters refer to piezoelectric energy harvesters.

Piezoelectric energy harvesters convert mechanical strain into electrical energy through the use of piezoelectric materials. These materials generate an electric charge when subjected to mechanical stress. This property makes them ideal for use in energy harvesting applications where ambient mechanical vibrations or strain are present.

Some key points to consider when studying piezoelectric energy harvesters include:

1. The type of piezoelectric material used and its properties, such as its piezoelectric coefficient and mechanical strength.
2. The design of the harvester, including its shape, size, and the arrangement of the piezoelectric material.
3. The frequency and amplitude of the ambient mechanical vibrations or strain, and how they match the resonant frequency of the harvester.
4. The electrical load connected to the harvester and how it affects the energy conversion efficiency.
5. The use of power management techniques to optimize the energy conversion and storage process.

In summary, piezoelectric energy harvesters are devices that convert ambient mechanical vibrations or strain into usable electrical energy through the use of piezoelectric materials. The design of the harvester, the properties of the piezoelectric material, and the characteristics of the ambient mechanical vibrations or strain are all important factors to consider when studying piezoelectric energy harvesting. Additionally, power management techniques can be used to optimize the energy conversion and storage process.
