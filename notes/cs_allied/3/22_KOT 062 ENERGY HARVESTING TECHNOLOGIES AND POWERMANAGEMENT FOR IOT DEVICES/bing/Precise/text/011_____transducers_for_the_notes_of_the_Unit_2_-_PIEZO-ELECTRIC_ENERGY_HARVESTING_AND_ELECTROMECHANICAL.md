### Transducers

A transducer is a device that converts one form of energy into another. In the context of Unit 2 - Piezo-Electric Energy Harvesting and Electromechanical Modeling, in the subject of KOT 062 Energy Harvesting Technologies and Power Management for IoT Devices KCS, transducers are used to convert mechanical energy into electrical energy.

Some key points to note about transducers in this context are:

1. Piezoelectric transducers are a type of transducer that converts mechanical energy into electrical energy using the piezoelectric effect.
2. The piezoelectric effect is the ability of certain materials to generate an electric charge in response to applied mechanical stress.
3. Piezoelectric transducers are commonly used in energy harvesting applications, where they can convert vibrations or other mechanical energy into electrical energy.
4. Electromechanical modeling is used to understand and predict the behavior of piezoelectric transducers, allowing for the design of more efficient energy harvesting systems.
5. The performance of a piezoelectric transducer depends on factors such as the material used, the geometry of the transducer, and the frequency and amplitude of the mechanical energy being converted.
