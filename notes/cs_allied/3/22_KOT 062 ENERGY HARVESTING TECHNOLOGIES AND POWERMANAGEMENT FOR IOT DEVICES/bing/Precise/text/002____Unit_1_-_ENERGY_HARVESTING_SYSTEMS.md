## Unit 1 - ENERGY HARVESTING SYSTEMS

Energy harvesting, also known as power harvesting or energy scavenging, is the process of capturing and storing small amounts of energy from external sources such as solar, wind, thermal, and kinetic energy. This energy is then used to power small electronic devices such as sensors, wireless communication devices, and wearable technology.

1. **Solar Energy Harvesting:** Solar energy harvesting involves capturing energy from the sun using photovoltaic cells. These cells convert sunlight into electricity, which can be used to power electronic devices.

2. **Wind Energy Harvesting:** Wind energy harvesting involves capturing energy from the wind using wind turbines. These turbines convert the kinetic energy of the wind into mechanical energy, which is then converted into electricity.

3. **Thermal Energy Harvesting:** Thermal energy harvesting involves capturing energy from temperature differences. This can be done using thermoelectric generators, which convert temperature differences into electricity.

4. **Kinetic Energy Harvesting:** Kinetic energy harvesting involves capturing energy from motion. This can be done using piezoelectric materials, which generate electricity when subjected to mechanical stress.

Energy harvesting systems can be used in a variety of applications, including remote sensing, wireless communication, and wearable technology. These systems can provide a sustainable and renewable source of energy for small electronic devices, reducing the need for traditional power sources such as batteries.
