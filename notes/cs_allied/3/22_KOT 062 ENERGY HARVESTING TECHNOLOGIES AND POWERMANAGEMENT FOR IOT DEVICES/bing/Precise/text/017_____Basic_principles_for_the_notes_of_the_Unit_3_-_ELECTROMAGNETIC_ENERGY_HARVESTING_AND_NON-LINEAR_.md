### Basic principles for the notes of the Unit 3 - ELECTROMAGNETIC ENERGY HARVESTING AND NON-LINEAR TECHNIQUES in the subject of KOT 062 ENERGY HARVESTING TECHNOLOGIES AND POWERMANAGEMENT FOR IOT DEVICES KCS

1. Electromagnetic energy harvesting is the process of capturing and converting ambient electromagnetic energy into usable electrical energy.
2. This energy can be harvested from sources such as radio waves, solar radiation, and even the Earth's magnetic field.
3. Non-linear techniques refer to methods that do not follow a linear relationship between input and output, and can be used to improve the efficiency of energy harvesting systems.
4. Some common non-linear techniques used in electromagnetic energy harvesting include rectification, voltage multiplication, and impedance matching.
5. These techniques can help to maximize the amount of energy that is captured and converted, making the energy harvesting system more effective.
6. Electromagnetic energy harvesting and non-linear techniques are important for the development of IoT devices, as they can provide a sustainable and reliable source of power for these devices.
7. By incorporating these technologies into IoT devices, it is possible to reduce their reliance on traditional power sources and improve their overall efficiency and sustainability.
