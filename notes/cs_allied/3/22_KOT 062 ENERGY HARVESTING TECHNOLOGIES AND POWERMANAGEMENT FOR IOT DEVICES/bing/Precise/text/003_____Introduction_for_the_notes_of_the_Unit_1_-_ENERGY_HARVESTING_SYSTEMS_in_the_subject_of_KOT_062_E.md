### Introduction for the notes of the Unit 1 - ENERGY HARVESTING SYSTEMS in the subject of KOT 062 ENERGY HARVESTING TECHNOLOGIES AND POWERMANAGEMENT FOR IOT DEVICES KCS

1. Energy harvesting, also known as power harvesting or energy scavenging, is the process of capturing and storing small amounts of energy from external sources such as solar, thermal, wind, and kinetic energy.
2. This energy is then converted into electrical energy and used to power small electronic devices such as sensors, wireless communication devices, and wearable technology.
3. Energy harvesting systems are becoming increasingly important for the Internet of Things (IoT) devices, as they can provide a sustainable and maintenance-free power source for these devices.
4. In this unit, we will explore the different types of energy harvesting systems, their components, and how they can be used to power IoT devices.
5. We will also discuss the challenges and limitations of energy harvesting systems and how they can be overcome through effective power management techniques.
