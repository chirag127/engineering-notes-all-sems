1. Energy harvesting from vibration is one of the methods used in wireless sensors.
2. Hybrid vibration energy converters, electromagnetic transducers, piezoelectric transducers, and magneto-electric transducers are some of the energy harvesting technologies used in wireless sensors.
3. Wireless sensor networks (WSNs) have gained attention due to their extensive deployment in the emerging fields of the Internet of Things (IoT's) and self-driven devices.
4. Energy harvesting from ambient motion, strain, or light is used to maintain an energy reserve in wireless sensors.
5. Recent applications of energy harvesting wireless sensors include piezoelectric powered damage tracking nodes for helicopters and solar powered strain and seismic sensor networks for bridges.
