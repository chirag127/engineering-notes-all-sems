### Harvesting Microelectronic Circuits

1. Energy harvesting is the process of capturing and storing energy from external sources such as light, heat, and motion.
2. This energy can be used to power microelectronic circuits in wireless sensors, reducing the need for batteries or wired power sources.
3. There are several methods for harvesting energy, including photovoltaic, thermoelectric, piezoelectric, and electromagnetic.
4. Photovoltaic harvesting converts light energy into electrical energy using solar cells.
5. Thermoelectric harvesting converts temperature differences into electrical energy using the Seebeck effect.
6. Piezoelectric harvesting converts mechanical strain into electrical energy using piezoelectric materials.
7. Electromagnetic harvesting converts changes in magnetic fields into electrical energy using electromagnetic induction.
8. Energy harvesting circuits typically include a power management unit to regulate the voltage and current output of the harvesting device.
9. These circuits can be integrated into wireless sensors to provide a self-sustaining power source for the sensor.
10. Energy harvesting technology has the potential to greatly extend the lifetime and reduce the maintenance requirements of wireless sensors.
