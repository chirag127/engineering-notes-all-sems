## Unit 2 - PIEZO-ELECTRIC ENERGY HARVESTING AND ELECTROMECHANICAL MODELING

1. **Piezoelectric energy harvesting** is the process of converting mechanical energy, such as vibrations or strain, into electrical energy using piezoelectric materials.
2. These materials have the ability to generate an electric charge in response to applied mechanical stress.
3. This technology can be used to power small electronic devices, such as sensors, by harvesting energy from ambient vibrations.
4. **Electromechanical modeling** is the process of developing mathematical models to describe the behavior of electromechanical systems.
5. These models can be used to predict the performance of the system and to design control strategies to optimize its operation.
6. In the context of piezoelectric energy harvesting, electromechanical modeling can be used to design efficient energy harvesting devices and to optimize their performance.
