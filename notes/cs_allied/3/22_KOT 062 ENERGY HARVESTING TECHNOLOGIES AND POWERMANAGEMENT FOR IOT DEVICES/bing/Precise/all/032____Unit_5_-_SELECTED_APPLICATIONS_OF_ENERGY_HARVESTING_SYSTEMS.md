## Unit 5 - SELECTED APPLICATIONS OF ENERGY HARVESTING SYSTEMS

Energy harvesting systems are used to convert ambient energy into usable electrical energy. These systems are used in a variety of applications, including:

1. **Wireless Sensor Networks:** Energy harvesting systems can be used to power wireless sensor networks, which are used to monitor and collect data from remote locations. These systems can be used in a variety of applications, including environmental monitoring, structural health monitoring, and industrial process monitoring.

2. **Consumer Electronics:** Energy harvesting systems can be used to power consumer electronics, such as mobile phones, laptops, and wearable devices. These systems can help to extend the battery life of these devices, reducing the need for frequent charging.

3. **Building Automation:** Energy harvesting systems can be used to power building automation systems, which are used to control and monitor various building systems, such as lighting, heating, and air conditioning. These systems can help to improve energy efficiency and reduce energy costs.

4. **Transportation:** Energy harvesting systems can be used to power transportation systems, such as trains, buses, and cars. These systems can help to reduce the reliance on fossil fuels and reduce emissions.

5. **Medical Devices:** Energy harvesting systems can be used to power medical devices, such as pacemakers and hearing aids. These systems can help to improve the reliability and longevity of these devices.

These are just a few examples of the many applications of energy harvesting systems. These systems have the potential to revolutionize the way we use and consume energy, and their use is expected to continue to grow in the coming years.
