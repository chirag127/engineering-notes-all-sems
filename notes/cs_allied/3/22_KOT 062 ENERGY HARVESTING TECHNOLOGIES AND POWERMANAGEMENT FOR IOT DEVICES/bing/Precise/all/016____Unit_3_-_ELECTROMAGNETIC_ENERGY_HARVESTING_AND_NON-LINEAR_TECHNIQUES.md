## Unit 3 - ELECTROMAGNETIC ENERGY HARVESTING AND NON-LINEAR TECHNIQUES

1. Electromagnetic energy harvesting is the process of capturing and converting ambient electromagnetic energy into usable electrical energy.
2. This energy can be harvested from sources such as radio waves, solar radiation, and even the Earth's magnetic field.
3. Non-linear techniques are used to improve the efficiency of energy harvesting systems.
4. These techniques include the use of non-linear elements such as diodes and transistors to rectify the harvested energy and convert it into a usable form.
5. Other non-linear techniques include the use of resonant circuits to enhance the capture of electromagnetic energy and the use of non-linear materials to improve the conversion efficiency.
6. Electromagnetic energy harvesting has many potential applications, including powering remote sensors, wireless devices, and even implantable medical devices.
7. Non-linear techniques can help to improve the performance of these systems and make them more practical for real-world applications.
