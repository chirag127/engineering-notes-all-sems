# Unit 2 - PIEZO-ELECTRIC ENERGY HARVESTING AND ELECTROMECHANICAL MODELING

1. **Piezoelectric energy harvesting** is the process of converting mechanical energy into electrical energy using piezoelectric materials.
2. **Piezoelectric materials** are materials that generate an electric charge when subjected to mechanical stress or strain.
3. **Electromechanical modeling** is the process of creating mathematical models that describe the behavior of electromechanical systems.
4. **Electromechanical systems** are systems that combine mechanical and electrical components to perform a specific function.
5. **Piezoelectric energy harvesting** can be used in a variety of applications, including powering wireless sensors, charging batteries, and generating electricity from vibrations or movements.
6. **Electromechanical modeling** can be used to design and optimize piezoelectric energy harvesting systems, as well as to predict their performance.
7. **Piezoelectric energy harvesting** has the potential to provide a sustainable and renewable source of energy, reducing our reliance on traditional energy sources.
