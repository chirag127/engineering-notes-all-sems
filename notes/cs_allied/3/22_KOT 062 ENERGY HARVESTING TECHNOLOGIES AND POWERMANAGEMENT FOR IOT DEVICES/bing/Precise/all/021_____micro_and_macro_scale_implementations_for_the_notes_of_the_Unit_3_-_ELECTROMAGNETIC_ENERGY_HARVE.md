# Micro and Macro Scale Implementations for Electromagnetic Energy Harvesting and Non-Linear Techniques

Electromagnetic energy harvesting is a technique that converts ambient electromagnetic energy into usable electrical energy. This technique can be implemented on both micro and macro scales.

## Micro Scale Implementations

1. **RF Energy Harvesting:** Radio frequency (RF) energy harvesting is a micro scale implementation of electromagnetic energy harvesting. It involves capturing the energy from ambient RF signals, such as those emitted by cell phone towers or Wi-Fi routers, and converting it into usable electrical energy.

2. **Piezoelectric Energy Harvesting:** Piezoelectric energy harvesting is another micro scale implementation of electromagnetic energy harvesting. It involves converting mechanical energy, such as vibrations or pressure changes, into electrical energy using piezoelectric materials.

## Macro Scale Implementations

1. **Wind Energy Harvesting:** Wind energy harvesting is a macro scale implementation of electromagnetic energy harvesting. It involves converting the kinetic energy of wind into electrical energy using wind turbines.

2. **Hydro Energy Harvesting:** Hydro energy harvesting is another macro scale implementation of electromagnetic energy harvesting. It involves converting the kinetic energy of flowing water into electrical energy using hydro turbines.

## Non-Linear Techniques

Non-linear techniques are used to improve the efficiency of electromagnetic energy harvesting. These techniques involve using non-linear components, such as diodes or transistors, to rectify the harvested energy and improve its conversion efficiency.

1. **Voltage Multiplier:** A voltage multiplier is a non-linear technique used to increase the voltage of the harvested energy. It involves using a series of diodes and capacitors to multiply the voltage of the input signal.

2. **Synchronous Rectification:** Synchronous rectification is another non-linear technique used to improve the efficiency of electromagnetic energy harvesting. It involves using a switch, such as a MOSFET, to control the flow of current and improve the conversion efficiency of the harvested energy.

These are some of the micro and macro scale implementations and non-linear techniques used in electromagnetic energy harvesting. They can be used to convert ambient electromagnetic energy into usable electrical energy for a variety of applications.
