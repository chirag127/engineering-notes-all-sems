# Introduction for the notes of the Unit 1 - ENERGY HARVESTING SYSTEMS in the subject of KOT 062 ENERGY HARVESTING TECHNOLOGIES AND POWERMANAGEMENT FOR IOT DEVICES KCS

1. Energy harvesting is the process of capturing and storing small amounts of energy from external sources such as solar, thermal, wind, and kinetic energy.
2. This energy can be used to power small electronic devices, such as sensors and wireless communication devices, which are commonly used in the Internet of Things (IoT).
3. Energy harvesting systems are designed to convert ambient energy into usable electrical energy, store it, and manage its distribution to the device.
4. These systems are becoming increasingly important as the demand for low-power, portable, and wireless devices continues to grow.
5. Energy harvesting technologies can provide a sustainable and renewable source of power for IoT devices, reducing their dependence on batteries and the need for frequent maintenance and replacement.
6. In this unit, we will explore the different types of energy harvesting systems, their components, and their applications in IoT devices.
