# Unit 5 - SELECTED APPLICATIONS OF ENERGY HARVESTING SYSTEMS

## Harvesting for RF Sensors and ID Tags

- Radio Frequency (RF) sensors and ID tags are devices that use radio waves to communicate and transmit information.
- These devices can be powered by harvesting energy from the environment, such as light, heat, or motion.
- One common method of harvesting energy for RF sensors and ID tags is through the use of solar cells, which convert light into electricity.
- Another method is through the use of thermoelectric generators, which convert temperature differences into electricity.
- Piezoelectric materials can also be used to harvest energy from motion or vibrations.
- Energy harvesting can provide a sustainable and low-maintenance power source for RF sensors and ID tags, making them ideal for use in remote or hard-to-reach locations.
- Energy harvesting can also reduce the need for batteries, which can be costly and environmentally harmful to dispose of.
- In summary, energy harvesting for RF sensors and ID tags can provide a sustainable and low-maintenance power source, reducing the need for batteries and making these devices ideal for use in remote or hard-to-reach locations.
