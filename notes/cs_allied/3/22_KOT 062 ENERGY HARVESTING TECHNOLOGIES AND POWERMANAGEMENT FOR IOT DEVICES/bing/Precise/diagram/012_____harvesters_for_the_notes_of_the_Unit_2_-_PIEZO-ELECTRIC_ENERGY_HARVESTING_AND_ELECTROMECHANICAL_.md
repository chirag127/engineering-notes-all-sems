### Harvesters

Harvesters are devices that convert ambient energy sources into usable electrical energy. In the context of Unit 2 - Piezo-Electric Energy Harvesting and Electromechanical Modeling, of the subject KOT 062 Energy Harvesting Technologies and Power Management for IoT Devices KCS, harvesters refer to piezoelectric energy harvesters.

1. Piezoelectric energy harvesters convert mechanical strain into electrical energy through the piezoelectric effect.
2. The piezoelectric effect is the ability of certain materials to generate an electric charge in response to applied mechanical stress.
3. Piezoelectric energy harvesters are commonly used in applications where mechanical vibrations are present, such as in machinery, vehicles, and infrastructure.
4. The design of piezoelectric energy harvesters involves modeling the electromechanical behavior of the piezoelectric material and the mechanical system in which it is integrated.
5. Electromechanical modeling is used to optimize the performance of the harvester and to predict its behavior under different operating conditions.
