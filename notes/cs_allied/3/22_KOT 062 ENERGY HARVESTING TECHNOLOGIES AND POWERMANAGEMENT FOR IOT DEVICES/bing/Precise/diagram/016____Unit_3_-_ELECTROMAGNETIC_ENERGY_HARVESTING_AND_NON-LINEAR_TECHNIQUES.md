# Unit 3 - Electromagnetic Energy Harvesting and Non-Linear Techniques

1. Electromagnetic energy harvesting is the process of capturing and converting ambient electromagnetic energy into usable electrical energy.
2. This energy can be harvested from sources such as radio waves, microwaves, and light waves.
3. Non-linear techniques are used to improve the efficiency of energy harvesting by optimizing the conversion of electromagnetic energy into electrical energy.
4. These techniques include the use of non-linear materials and circuits, as well as the use of advanced control algorithms.
5. Electromagnetic energy harvesting has applications in a wide range of fields, including wireless sensor networks, wearable electronics, and renewable energy.
6. Research in this field is ongoing, with the goal of improving the efficiency and scalability of electromagnetic energy harvesting systems.
