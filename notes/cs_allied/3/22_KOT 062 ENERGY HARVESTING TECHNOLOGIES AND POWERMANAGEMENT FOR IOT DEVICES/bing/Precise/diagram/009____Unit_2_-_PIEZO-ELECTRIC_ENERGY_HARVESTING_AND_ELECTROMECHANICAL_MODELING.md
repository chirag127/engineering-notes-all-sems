## Unit 2 - PIEZO-ELECTRIC ENERGY HARVESTING AND ELECTROMECHANICAL MODELING

Piezoelectric energy harvesting is a process that converts mechanical energy into electrical energy using the piezoelectric effect. This effect occurs when certain materials, such as quartz or ceramics, are subjected to mechanical stress, causing them to generate an electric charge.

Electromechanical modeling is the process of creating mathematical models that describe the behavior of electromechanical systems. These models can be used to predict the performance of the system and to design control systems that optimize its operation.

In this unit, we will cover the following topics:

1. The principles of piezoelectric energy harvesting
2. The materials used in piezoelectric energy harvesting
3. The design of piezoelectric energy harvesting systems
4. The applications of piezoelectric energy harvesting
5. The basics of electromechanical modeling
6. The use of electromechanical models in the design of control systems.

By the end of this unit, you should have a good understanding of the principles and applications of piezoelectric energy harvesting and electromechanical modeling. You should also be able to apply these concepts to the design of electromechanical systems.
