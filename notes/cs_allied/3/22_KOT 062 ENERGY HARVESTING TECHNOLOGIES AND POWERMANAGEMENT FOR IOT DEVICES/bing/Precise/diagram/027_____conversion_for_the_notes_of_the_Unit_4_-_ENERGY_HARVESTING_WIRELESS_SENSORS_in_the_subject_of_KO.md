### Unit 4 - ENERGY HARVESTING WIRELESS SENSORS

Energy harvesting wireless sensors are devices that can convert ambient energy sources into electrical energy to power themselves. These sensors are used in the Internet of Things (IoT) devices to reduce the need for battery replacement or external power sources.

Some of the key points to note about energy harvesting wireless sensors are:

1. Energy harvesting wireless sensors can convert various forms of ambient energy into electrical energy. These energy sources can include light, heat, vibration, and radio frequency (RF) waves.

2. The conversion of ambient energy into electrical energy is achieved through the use of various energy harvesting technologies such as photovoltaic cells, thermoelectric generators, piezoelectric materials, and RF energy harvesting circuits.

3. Energy harvesting wireless sensors can be used in a wide range of IoT applications, including environmental monitoring, structural health monitoring, and wearable devices.

4. The use of energy harvesting wireless sensors can reduce the need for battery replacement or external power sources, which can reduce maintenance costs and improve the sustainability of IoT devices.

5. Power management is an important aspect of energy harvesting wireless sensors, as the amount of energy that can be harvested from ambient sources can vary. Power management techniques such as energy buffering and power-aware scheduling can be used to ensure that the sensor can operate reliably even when the amount of harvested energy is low.

Overall, energy harvesting wireless sensors are an important technology for enabling the development of sustainable and low-maintenance IoT devices. By converting ambient energy sources into electrical energy, these sensors can reduce the need for battery replacement or external power sources, which can improve the sustainability and reduce the maintenance costs of IoT devices.
