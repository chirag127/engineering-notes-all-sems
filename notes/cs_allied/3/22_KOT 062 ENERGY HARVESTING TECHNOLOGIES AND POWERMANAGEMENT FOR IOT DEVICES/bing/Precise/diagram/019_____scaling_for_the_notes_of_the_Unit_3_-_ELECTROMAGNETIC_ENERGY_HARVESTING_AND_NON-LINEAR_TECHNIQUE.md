### Scaling

Scaling is an important concept in the study of electromagnetic energy harvesting and non-linear techniques, which is covered in Unit 3 of the subject KOT 062 Energy Harvesting Technologies and Power Management for IoT Devices KCS.

1. Scaling refers to the process of adjusting the size or magnitude of a system or its components in order to achieve a desired performance or behavior.
2. In the context of electromagnetic energy harvesting, scaling can be used to optimize the performance of energy harvesting devices by adjusting their size, shape, and other parameters.
3. Scaling can also be used to improve the efficiency of non-linear techniques, such as rectification and frequency conversion, by optimizing the size and shape of the components involved.
4. Understanding the principles of scaling is essential for the design and optimization of energy harvesting systems and the application of non-linear techniques in the field of IoT devices.
