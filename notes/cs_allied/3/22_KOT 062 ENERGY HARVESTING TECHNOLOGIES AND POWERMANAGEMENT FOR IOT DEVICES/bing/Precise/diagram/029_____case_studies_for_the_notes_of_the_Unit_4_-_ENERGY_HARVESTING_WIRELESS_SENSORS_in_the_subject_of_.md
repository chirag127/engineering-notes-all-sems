### Unit 4 - ENERGY HARVESTING WIRELESS SENSORS

#### Case Studies

1. **Case Study 1: Energy Harvesting Wireless Sensor Network for Structural Health Monitoring**

In this case study, an energy harvesting wireless sensor network was developed for structural health monitoring. The system used piezoelectric energy harvesters to convert mechanical vibrations into electrical energy, which powered the wireless sensors. The sensors were used to monitor the structural health of a bridge, providing real-time data on its condition.

2. **Case Study 2: Solar-Powered Wireless Sensor Network for Environmental Monitoring**

In this case study, a solar-powered wireless sensor network was developed for environmental monitoring. The system used solar panels to harvest energy from the sun, which powered the wireless sensors. The sensors were used to monitor various environmental parameters, such as temperature, humidity, and air quality, providing real-time data on the environment.

3. **Case Study 3: Thermoelectric Energy Harvesting Wireless Sensor Network for Industrial Applications**

In this case study, a thermoelectric energy harvesting wireless sensor network was developed for industrial applications. The system used thermoelectric generators to convert temperature differences into electrical energy, which powered the wireless sensors. The sensors were used to monitor various industrial processes, providing real-time data on their performance.

These are some examples of case studies for energy harvesting wireless sensors. They demonstrate the potential of energy harvesting technologies to power wireless sensors, enabling them to operate in a wide range of applications.
