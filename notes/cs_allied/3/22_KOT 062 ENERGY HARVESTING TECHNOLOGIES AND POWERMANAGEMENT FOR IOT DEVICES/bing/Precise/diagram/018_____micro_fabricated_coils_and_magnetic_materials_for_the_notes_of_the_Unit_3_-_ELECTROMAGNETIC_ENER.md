### Micro Fabricated Coils and Magnetic Materials

Micro fabricated coils are small, intricate coils that are used in various applications, including magnetooptical data storage and magnetic neurostimulation. These coils are typically embedded in insulating material and may be electroplated with alternating magnetic and electrically insulating layers .

The structure of a planar micro coil usually includes upper and lower magnetic films, a metal wire coil layer in the middle, and an insulating layer used to isolate the magnetic core material and the wire. The main structural parameters include the outer diameter of the micro coil, winding number, line width, coil spacing, and thickness .

In the context of electromagnetic energy harvesting, the characteristics of wire-wound and micro-fabricated coils, as well as the properties of typical magnetic materials, are important to consider. The scaling of electromagnetic energy harvesters and the design limitations imposed by micro-fabrication processes are also important factors .

Micro-coil magnetic stimulation of brain tissue presents new challenges for MEMS micro-coil probe fabrication. The main challenges are low coil resistance for high power efficiency, low leak current from the probe into the in vitro experimental set-up, and adaptive MEMS process technology because of the dynamic research .
