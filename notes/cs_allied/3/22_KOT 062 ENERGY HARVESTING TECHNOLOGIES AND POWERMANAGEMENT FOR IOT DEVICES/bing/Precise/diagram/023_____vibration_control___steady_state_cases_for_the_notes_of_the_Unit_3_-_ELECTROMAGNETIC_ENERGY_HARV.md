### Vibration Control & Steady State Cases

Vibration control is a technique used to reduce or eliminate unwanted vibrations in a system. This is important in the context of energy harvesting, as vibrations can cause energy loss and reduce the efficiency of the harvesting process. There are several methods for controlling vibrations, including the use of dampers, absorbers, and isolators.

In steady state cases, the system has reached a state of equilibrium where the input and output energies are balanced. This means that the energy being harvested is equal to the energy being consumed by the system. In this state, the system is able to operate continuously without the need for additional energy input.

These concepts are important in the study of electromagnetic energy harvesting and non-linear techniques, as they play a crucial role in the efficiency and effectiveness of the energy harvesting process. Understanding these concepts is essential for the successful design and implementation of energy harvesting systems.

Here are some key points to remember:
- Vibration control is used to reduce or eliminate unwanted vibrations in a system.
- Methods for controlling vibrations include the use of dampers, absorbers, and isolators.
- In steady state cases, the system has reached a state of equilibrium where the input and output energies are balanced.
- Understanding vibration control and steady state cases is essential for the successful design and implementation of energy harvesting systems.
