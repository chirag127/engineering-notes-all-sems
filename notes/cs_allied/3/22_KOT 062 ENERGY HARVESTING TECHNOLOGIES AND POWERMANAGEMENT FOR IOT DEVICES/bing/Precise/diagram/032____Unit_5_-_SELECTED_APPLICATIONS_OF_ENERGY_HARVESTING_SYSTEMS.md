## Unit 5 - SELECTED APPLICATIONS OF ENERGY HARVESTING SYSTEMS

Energy harvesting systems are used to collect small amounts of ambient energy to power wireless devices. This technology is very promising for applications where batteries are impractical, such as body sensor networks and inaccessible remote systems.

The energy harvesting systems market finds its application in various sectors such as:
- Industrial: remote monitoring, predictive maintenance, and energy-efficient solutions .
- Consumer electronics .
- Building and home automation .
- Wireless sensor networks (WSN) .
- Security systems .
- Others .

Energy harvesting is a great way to extend the battery life of remote sensors found in commercial, industrial, and medical applications. Some systems convert motion, such as that of ocean waves, into electricity to be used by oceanographic monitoring sensors for autonomous operation. Kinetic energy harvesting converts movement or vibrations into electrical energy, enables battery-free operation of wireless sensors and autonomous devices, and facilitates their placement in locations where replacing a battery is not feasible or attractive.
