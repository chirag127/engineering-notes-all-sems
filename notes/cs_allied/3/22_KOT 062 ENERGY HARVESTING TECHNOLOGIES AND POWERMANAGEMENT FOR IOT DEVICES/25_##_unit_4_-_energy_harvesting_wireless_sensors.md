## Unit 4 - ENERGY HARVESTING WIRELESS SENSORS

Energy harvesting wireless sensors are devices that are used to capture and store energy from ambient sources such as light, heat, vibration, or electromagnetic radiation, and to use this energy to power wireless sensors. These devices are an important aspect of the Internet of Things (IoT) and are used to provide a sustainable source of energy for IoT devices, which often have limited energy resources.

Energy harvesting wireless sensors typically consist of a energy harvester, a battery or energy storage device, and a wireless sensor that is used to collect and transmit data. The energy harvester captures energy from ambient sources and converts this energy into electrical energy, which is then stored in the battery or energy storage device. The wireless sensor uses this stored energy to collect and transmit data, which can then be used for a variety of purposes, such as monitoring environmental conditions, tracking the location of assets, or collecting data for scientific research.

In the context of the study of energy harvesting technologies and power management for IoT devices, it is important to understand the principles of energy harvesting wireless sensors, and to be familiar with the different types of energy harvesters and wireless sensors that are used in these devices. This may include understanding the principles of energy conversion, energy storage, and wireless communication, as well as the design and implementation of energy harvesting wireless sensors.

In addition, the course may cover the optimization of energy harvesting wireless sensors, which involves the use of mathematical models and simulation tools to predict the performance of these devices under different conditions, and to optimize the design of these devices for maximum energy conversion efficiency and wireless communication performance.

Overall, energy harvesting wireless sensors are an important aspect of energy harvesting technologies and power management for IoT devices, and understanding the principles and implementation of these devices is an important aspect of the study of this subject.
