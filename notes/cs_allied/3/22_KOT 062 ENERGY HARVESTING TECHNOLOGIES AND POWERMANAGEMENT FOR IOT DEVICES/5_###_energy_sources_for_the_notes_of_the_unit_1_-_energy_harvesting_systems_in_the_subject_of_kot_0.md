### Energy sources for the notes of the Unit 1 - ENERGY HARVESTING SYSTEMS in the subject of KOT 062 ENERGY HARVESTING TECHNOLOGIES AND POWERMANAGEMENT FOR IOT DEVICES KCS

Energy harvesting is the process of capturing and storing energy from ambient sources such as light, heat, vibration, or radio frequency (RF) energy. Energy harvesting systems are used to power IoT devices, which often have limited energy resources and rely on energy harvesting to provide a sustainable source of power.

There are several energy sources that can be used for energy harvesting, including:

1. Solar energy: Solar energy can be harvested using photovoltaic cells, which convert light into electrical energy. Solar energy harvesting systems are commonly used to power IoT devices in outdoor environments.

2. Thermal energy: Thermal energy can be harvested from ambient heat sources, such as the heat generated by electronic devices, or from renewable sources such as geothermal energy. Thermal energy harvesting systems use thermoelectric generators to convert heat into electrical energy.

3. Vibrational energy: Vibrational energy can be harvested from sources such as machinery, vehicles, or human motion. Vibrational energy harvesting systems use piezoelectric materials to convert mechanical vibrations into electrical energy.

4. RF energy: RF energy can be harvested from sources such as radio waves, microwaves, or Wi-Fi signals. RF energy harvesting systems use rectifiers to convert RF energy into electrical energy.

In the context of KOT 062 Energy Harvesting Technologies and Power Management for IoT Devices, it is important to understand the different energy sources that can be used for energy harvesting, and to be familiar with the different energy harvesting systems and technologies that are used to capture and store energy from these sources.

Overall, energy harvesting is an important technology for powering IoT devices, and understanding the different energy sources that can be used for energy harvesting is an important aspect of the study of KOT 062 Energy Harvesting Technologies and Power Management for IoT Devices.
