## Unit 5 - SELECTED APPLICATIONS OF ENERGY HARVESTING SYSTEMS

In this unit, we will explore some selected applications of energy harvesting systems. Energy harvesting systems can be used in various fields, including healthcare, automotive, aerospace, and consumer electronics. Here are some examples of their applications:

1. **Healthcare:** Energy harvesting systems can be used to power medical devices and implantable sensors. For example, a self-powered pacemaker can be developed by using an energy harvesting system that converts the energy from the heart's motion into electrical energy.

2. **Automotive:** Energy harvesting systems can be used to power various electronic devices in a car, such as tire pressure monitoring systems and security systems. They can also be used to power the car's battery, reducing the load on the alternator and improving the fuel efficiency.

3. **Aerospace:** Energy harvesting systems can be used to power sensors and communication devices in satellites and spacecraft. They can also be used to power unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) and other autonomous vehicles.

4. **Consumer Electronics:** Energy harvesting systems can be used to power various consumer electronics devices, such as smartphones, smartwatches, and fitness trackers. For example, a smartwatch can be developed that uses solar energy to power its display and sensors.

5. **Industrial Automation:** Energy harvesting systems can be used to power sensors and control devices in industrial automation systems. For example, a wireless sensor network can be developed that uses energy harvesting to power its sensors, eliminating the need for batteries.

6. **Structural Health Monitoring:** Energy harvesting systems can be used to power sensors that monitor the health of structures such as bridges, buildings, and pipelines. These sensors can detect and report any damage or changes in the structure, allowing for timely maintenance and repair.

In summary, energy harvesting systems have a wide range of applications in various fields, and their use is growing rapidly. By converting ambient energy into electrical energy, these systems can power various devices and sensors, reducing the need for batteries and improving the efficiency and sustainability of various systems.
