### Power Maximizations for the Notes of Unit 3 - Electromagnetic Energy Harvesting and Non-Linear Techniques in the Subject of KOT 062 Energy Harvesting Technologies and Power Management for IoT Devices KCS

In this unit, we will discuss various ways to maximize power in electromagnetic energy harvesting systems and non-linear techniques. Here are some key points to remember:

1. **Use optimal load resistance:** The load resistance should be chosen to match the output impedance of the energy harvester. This ensures maximum power transfer from the harvester to the load.

2. **Use impedance matching network:** An impedance matching network can be used to match the output impedance of the energy harvester and the load impedance. This improves power transfer efficiency and reduces losses.

3. **Use voltage boosters:** Voltage boosters can be used to increase the output voltage of the energy harvester. This allows the use of low-voltage energy harvesters in high-voltage applications.

4. **Use multiple energy harvesters:** Multiple energy harvesters can be used in parallel or series to increase the overall power output. This is useful in applications where a single energy harvester cannot provide enough power.

5. **Use non-linear techniques:** Non-linear techniques such as voltage multiplication and frequency up-conversion can be used to improve power output. These techniques convert the low-frequency and low-voltage output of the energy harvester to a higher frequency and voltage, which can be used to power electronic devices.

6. **Optimize harvester placement:** The placement of the energy harvester should be optimized for maximum power output. This includes choosing the optimal location and orientation of the harvester with respect to the energy source.

7. **Use energy storage devices:** Energy storage devices such as capacitors and batteries can be used to store energy harvested by the energy harvester. This allows the energy to be used later when needed, and ensures a constant power supply to the electronic device.

By following these tips, you can maximize the power output of electromagnetic energy harvesting systems and non-linear techniques. This is crucial in applications where a constant and reliable power supply is required for IoT devices.
