### Micro Fabricated Coils and Magnetic Materials

In the field of energy harvesting, micro fabricated coils and magnetic materials play a crucial role in converting mechanical energy into electrical energy. These components are commonly used in the development of electromagnetic energy harvesting systems, which can be used to power IoT devices and other low-power electronics.

Here are some key points to understand about micro fabricated coils and magnetic materials in energy harvesting:

1. Micro Fabricated Coils: 
   - These are small coils that are typically made using microfabrication techniques such as photolithography and etching.
   - They are designed to be highly efficient at converting mechanical energy into electrical energy.
   - Micro fabricated coils are often used in electromagnetic energy harvesting systems due to their small size and high power density.
   - They are also used in other applications such as wireless power transfer and sensing.

2. Magnetic Materials:
   - Magnetic materials play a critical role in energy harvesting systems, as they are used to generate a magnetic field that interacts with the micro fabricated coils.
   - Common magnetic materials used in energy harvesting include ferromagnetic materials such as iron and nickel, as well as rare-earth magnets.
   - The choice of magnetic material depends on factors such as the required magnetic field strength, the operating temperature range, and the cost.

3. Non-Linear Techniques:
   - Non-linear techniques can be used to increase the efficiency of energy harvesting systems that use micro fabricated coils and magnetic materials.
   - One example of a non-linear technique is magnetic resonance, which involves tuning the frequency of the magnetic field to match that of the micro fabricated coil.
   - Another non-linear technique is frequency up-conversion, which involves using a non-linear material to convert the frequency of the electrical output from the micro fabricated coil to a higher frequency.

In conclusion, micro fabricated coils and magnetic materials are essential components in the development of electromagnetic energy harvesting systems. By understanding the principles behind these components and their use in non-linear techniques, engineers can design more efficient and effective energy harvesting systems for a variety of applications.
