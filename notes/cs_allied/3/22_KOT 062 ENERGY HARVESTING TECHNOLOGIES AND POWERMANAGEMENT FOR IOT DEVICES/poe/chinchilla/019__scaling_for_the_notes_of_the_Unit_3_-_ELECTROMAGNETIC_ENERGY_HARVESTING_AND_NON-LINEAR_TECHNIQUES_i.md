In this unit, we will discuss the scaling considerations for electromagnetic energy harvesting and non-linear techniques. Here are the important points to remember:

- The performance of an electromagnetic energy harvester depends on several factors, including the size of the harvester, the frequency of the input signal, and the efficiency of the energy conversion process.
- As the size of the harvester is reduced, the available power output also decreases due to the reduction in the effective area of the harvester. Therefore, scaling down a harvester requires careful consideration of the application and the required power output.
- The frequency of the input signal is another important consideration for scaling an electromagnetic energy harvester. The resonant frequency of the harvester should match the frequency of the input signal to maximize power output. However, reducing the size of the harvester can shift the resonant frequency, which may require adjustments to the design.
- Non-linear techniques can be used to improve the efficiency of electromagnetic energy harvesting by exploiting non-linearities in the energy conversion process. These techniques can be particularly effective at low power levels where conventional linear techniques may not be effective.
- One example of a non-linear technique is the use of a diode in the energy harvesting circuit. The diode allows current to flow in only one direction, which can improve the overall efficiency of the energy conversion process.
- Scaling non-linear techniques can be challenging because the non-linearities may not scale in the same way as the linear components of the harvester. Therefore, careful consideration of the design is required to ensure that the non-linear techniques are effective at the desired power levels.
- Another consideration for scaling energy harvesting systems is the power management circuitry. As the power output of the harvester is reduced, the power management circuitry may become less efficient, which can reduce the overall efficiency of the system. Therefore, careful consideration of the power management circuitry is required when scaling energy harvesting systems.

In summary, scaling electromagnetic energy harvesting and non-linear techniques requires careful consideration of several factors, including the size of the harvester, the frequency of the input signal, the efficiency of the energy conversion process, and the power management circuitry. By understanding these considerations, it is possible to design effective energy harvesting systems for a wide range of applications.
