# KCS

KCS (Knowledge Centered Service) is a methodology developed by the Consortium for Service Innovation that aims to improve knowledge management and support processes in service organizations. KCS is based on the idea that knowledge is a valuable asset that should be shared and reused within the organization to improve service delivery and customer satisfaction.

Here are some key points to understand about KCS:

- KCS is a knowledge management methodology that focuses on capturing, structuring, and reusing knowledge in the context of service delivery.
- KCS is a customer-centric approach that aims to solve customer issues quickly and efficiently by empowering service agents with the knowledge they need to resolve issues on the first contact.
- KCS is a continuous improvement process that creates a culture of knowledge sharing and learning within the organization.
- KCS is based on two main principles: "Capture in the flow of work" and "Evolve content based on demand and usage". These principles encourage service agents to capture knowledge as they solve issues and to continuously improve the knowledge base based on customer demand and usage patterns.
- KCS provides a set of practices and tools to support the knowledge management process, including the KCS article format, the KCS workflow, and the KCS performance metrics.
- KCS has been adopted by many service organizations across various industries, including technology, healthcare, and finance, and has been shown to improve service quality, reduce costs, and increase customer satisfaction.

In summary, KCS is a knowledge management methodology that helps service organizations to capture, structure, and reuse knowledge in the context of service delivery, creating a culture of continuous learning and improvement. By adopting KCS practices and tools, service organizations can improve service quality, reduce costs, and increase customer satisfaction.
