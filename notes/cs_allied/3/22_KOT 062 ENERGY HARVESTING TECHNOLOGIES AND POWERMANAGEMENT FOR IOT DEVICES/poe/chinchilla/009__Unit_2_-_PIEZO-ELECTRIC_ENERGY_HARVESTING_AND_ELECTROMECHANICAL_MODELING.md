## Unit 2 - PIEZO-ELECTRIC ENERGY HARVESTING AND ELECTROMECHANICAL MODELING

Piezoelectric energy harvesting is a method of generating electrical energy from mechanical vibrations. It works on the principle of converting mechanical energy into electrical energy using piezoelectric materials. In this unit, we will learn about the various aspects of piezoelectric energy harvesting and electromechanical modeling.

### Piezoelectric Effect

Piezoelectric effect is a phenomenon where certain materials produce an electric charge when subjected to mechanical stress. The effect was discovered by Pierre and Jacques Curie in 1880. Piezoelectric materials are used in a variety of applications such as sensors, actuators, and energy harvesting.

### Piezoelectric Energy Harvesting

Piezoelectric energy harvesting is a process of converting mechanical energy into electrical energy using piezoelectric materials. The process involves the following steps:

1. Mechanical vibrations are applied to the piezoelectric material.
2. The mechanical vibrations cause a deformation in the material, which generates an electric charge.
3. The electric charge is collected and stored in a capacitor or a battery.

Piezoelectric energy harvesting has several advantages over other methods of energy harvesting, such as high efficiency, low cost, and wide range of applications.

### Electromechanical Modeling

Electromechanical modeling is the process of modeling the behavior of piezoelectric materials under mechanical and electrical loads. The modeling involves the following steps:

1. Developing a mathematical model of the piezoelectric material.
2. Simulating the behavior of the material under different mechanical and electrical loads.
3. Analyzing the results and optimizing the design for maximum energy harvesting efficiency.

Electromechanical modeling is an important tool for designing and optimizing piezoelectric energy harvesters.

### Applications of Piezoelectric Energy Harvesting

Piezoelectric energy harvesting has several applications in various fields, such as:

1. Wireless sensors: Piezoelectric energy harvesting can be used to power wireless sensors in industrial and environmental monitoring applications.
2. Wearable devices: Piezoelectric energy harvesting can be used to power wearable devices such as smartwatches and fitness trackers.
3. Automotive industry: Piezoelectric energy harvesting can be used to power sensors and electronics in vehicles.
4. Aerospace industry: Piezoelectric energy harvesting can be used to power sensors and electronics in spacecraft and satellites.

### Conclusion

Piezoelectric energy harvesting is an efficient and cost-effective method of generating electrical energy from mechanical vibrations. Electromechanical modeling plays a crucial role in designing and optimizing piezoelectric energy harvesters. The applications of piezoelectric energy harvesting are diverse and include wireless sensors, wearable devices, automotive industry, and aerospace industry.
