### Examples for the Notes of Unit 4 - Energy Harvesting Wireless Sensors

In this unit, we will discuss the various examples of energy harvesting wireless sensors. These sensors have the capability to harvest energy from the environment and use it to power the wireless communication modules. Some of the examples are as follows:

1. Solar-Powered Wireless Sensors: These sensors make use of solar panels to harvest energy from the sun. The solar panels convert the sunlight into electrical energy, which is then used to power the wireless communication module. These sensors are widely used in outdoor applications where sunlight is abundant.

2. Thermal-Powered Wireless Sensors: These sensors make use of the temperature difference between the ambient environment and the object being measured to generate power. The thermoelectric generator converts the temperature difference into electrical energy, which is used to power the wireless communication module. These sensors are used in industrial applications where there is a temperature gradient.

3. Vibration-Powered Wireless Sensors: These sensors make use of the vibrations in the environment to generate power. The piezoelectric generator converts the vibrations into electrical energy, which is used to power the wireless communication module. These sensors are used in applications where there are constant vibrations, such as in machinery and vehicles.

4. Wind-Powered Wireless Sensors: These sensors make use of wind turbines to generate power. The wind turbines convert the kinetic energy of the wind into electrical energy, which is used to power the wireless communication module. These sensors are used in outdoor applications where there is consistent wind.

5. RF-Powered Wireless Sensors: These sensors make use of radio waves to harvest energy. The rectenna converts the radio waves into electrical energy, which is used to power the wireless communication module. These sensors are used in indoor applications where there is a strong radio frequency signal.

In conclusion, energy harvesting wireless sensors have the potential to revolutionize the IoT industry by eliminating the need for battery replacements and reducing maintenance costs. The examples mentioned above are just a few of the many possibilities of energy harvesting technologies. As technology continues to advance, we can expect to see even more innovative solutions for powering wireless sensors.
