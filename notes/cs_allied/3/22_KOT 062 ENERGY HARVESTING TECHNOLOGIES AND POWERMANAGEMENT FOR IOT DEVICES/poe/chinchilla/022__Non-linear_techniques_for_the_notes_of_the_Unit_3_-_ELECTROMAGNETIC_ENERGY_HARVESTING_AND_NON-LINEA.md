### Non-linear techniques for the notes of the Unit 3 - ELECTROMAGNETIC ENERGY HARVESTING AND NON-LINEAR TECHNIQUES in the subject of KOT 062 ENERGY HARVESTING TECHNOLOGIES AND POWERMANAGEMENT FOR IOT DEVICES KCS

In the study of energy harvesting technologies, non-linear techniques play an important role in improving the efficiency of energy conversion. In this unit, we will discuss the different non-linear techniques used in electromagnetic energy harvesting.

1. **Non-linear Inductors**

    Non-linear inductors are used to increase the voltage output of an energy harvester. They are made by winding a wire around a ferromagnetic core that has a non-linear magnetization curve. When the magnetic field strength is low, the inductance is high, and when the magnetic field strength is high, the inductance is low. This helps in increasing the voltage output of the energy harvester.

2. **Non-linear Capacitors**

    Non-linear capacitors are used to increase the capacitance of an energy harvester. They are made by using a dielectric material that has a non-linear permittivity. When the electric field strength is low, the capacitance is high, and when the electric field strength is high, the capacitance is low. This helps in storing more energy in the capacitor and increasing the overall efficiency of the energy harvester.

3. **Non-linear Resonant Circuits**

    Non-linear resonant circuits are used to improve the efficiency of energy transfer from the harvester to the load. They are made by using non-linear elements such as diodes or transistors in the resonant circuit. These non-linear elements help in shaping the resonance curve, which helps in improving the energy transfer efficiency.

4. **Multi-frequency Harvesting**

    Multi-frequency harvesting is a non-linear technique used to capture energy from multiple sources of frequencies. This is done by using a circuit that has multiple resonant frequencies. When the energy harvester is exposed to multiple sources of frequencies, the circuit resonates at the corresponding frequencies and captures energy from all sources.

5. **Non-linear Energy Harvesting with Piezoelectric Materials**

    Non-linear energy harvesting with piezoelectric materials is a technique used to improve the efficiency of energy harvesting from mechanical vibrations. This is done by using a piezoelectric material that has a non-linear response to the mechanical vibrations. This helps in capturing more energy from the vibrations and increasing the overall efficiency of the energy harvester.

These non-linear techniques are important in improving the efficiency of energy harvesting technologies. Understanding and implementing these techniques can help in designing more efficient energy harvester systems for IoT devices.
