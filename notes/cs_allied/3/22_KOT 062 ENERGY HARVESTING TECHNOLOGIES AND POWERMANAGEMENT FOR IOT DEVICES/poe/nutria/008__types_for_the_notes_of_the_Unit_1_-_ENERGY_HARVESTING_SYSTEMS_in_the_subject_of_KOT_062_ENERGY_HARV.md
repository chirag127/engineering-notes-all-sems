
### Unit 1 - Energy Harvesting Systems

* Energy harvesting systems are systems that capture energy from the environment and convert it into usable electrical energy.
* Common sources of energy that can be harvested include solar, wind, thermal, and mechanical energy.
* Solar energy harvesting systems use solar cells to convert sunlight into electricity.
* Wind energy harvesting systems use turbines to convert wind energy into electricity.
* Thermal energy harvesting systems use thermoelectric materials to convert temperature differences into electricity.
* Mechanical energy harvesting systems use piezoelectric materials to convert mechanical vibrations into electricity.
* Power management systems are used to regulate the amount of power generated by energy harvesting systems and ensure that it is used efficiently.
* Power management systems can also be used to store excess energy for later use.
