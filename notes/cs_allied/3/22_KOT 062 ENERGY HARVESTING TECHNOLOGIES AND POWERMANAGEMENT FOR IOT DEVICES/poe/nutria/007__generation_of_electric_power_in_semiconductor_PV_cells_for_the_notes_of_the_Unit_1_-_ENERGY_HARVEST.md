
### Generation of Electric Power in Semiconductor PV Cells

1. Photovoltaic (PV) cells are semiconductor devices that convert solar radiation into electrical energy.
2. PV cells are made up of two or more layers of semiconductor material, such as silicon, gallium arsenide, and cadmium telluride.
3. When photons from the sun hit the PV cell, they excite the electrons in the semiconductor material and generate an electric current.
4. The electric current is then collected by metal contacts on the surface of the cell, and can be used to power electrical devices.
5. PV cells can be used in a variety of applications, including solar panels, solar hot water systems, and even space satellites.
6. The efficiency of PV cells can vary greatly depending on the type of semiconductor material used, the size of the cell, and the amount of sunlight available.
