
### Microgenerators for the Notes of the Unit 2 - Piezo-Electric Energy Harvesting and Electromechanical Modeling 

1. Piezo-electric energy harvesting is a process that uses the mechanical energy of a piezoelectric material to generate electricity. 
2. Piezoelectric materials are materials that generate a voltage when they are subjected to mechanical stress or vibration. 
3. The piezoelectric effect is used in a variety of applications, such as sensors, microphones, and actuators. 
4. Piezoelectric energy harvesting can be used to generate power for low-power electronic devices, such as IoT devices. 
5. Electromechanical modeling is a technique used to analyze the behavior of a system that involves both electrical and mechanical components. 
6. The model is based on the physical laws that govern the behavior of the system. 
7. The model can be used to predict the behavior of the system under various conditions. 
8. Electromechanical modeling can be used to design efficient energy harvesting systems.
