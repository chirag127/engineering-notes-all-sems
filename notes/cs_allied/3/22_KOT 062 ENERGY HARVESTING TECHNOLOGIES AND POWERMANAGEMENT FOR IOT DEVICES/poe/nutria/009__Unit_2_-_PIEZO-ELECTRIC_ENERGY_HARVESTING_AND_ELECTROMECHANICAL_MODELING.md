
## Unit 2 - PIEZO-ELECTRIC ENERGY HARVESTING AND ELECTROMECHANICAL MODELING

1. Piezoelectric energy harvesting is the process of converting mechanical energy into electrical energy using piezoelectric materials. 
2. Piezoelectric materials are materials that produce a voltage when a mechanical stress is applied. 
3. Piezoelectric energy harvesting can be used to power small electronic devices such as sensors and wireless communication modules. 
4. Electromechanical modeling is a method of analyzing the behavior of piezoelectric materials under mechanical and electrical loads. 
5. Electromechanical modeling can be used to predict the performance of piezoelectric energy harvesting systems. 
6. The performance of piezoelectric energy harvesting systems is affected by the material properties, the geometry of the system, and the loading conditions. 
7. The most commonly used piezoelectric materials for energy harvesting are lead zirconate titanate (PZT) and quartz. 
8. Piezoelectric energy harvesting systems can be used to power small electronic devices such as sensors and wireless communication modules. 
9. Piezoelectric energy harvesting systems can also be used for vibration-based energy harvesting and self-powered sensors.
