

### Power Sources for WSN

Wireless Sensor Networks (WSNs) require power sources in order to function properly. The most common sources of power for WSNs are:

1. Primary Batteries: Primary batteries are the most common power source for WSNs. They are reliable, easy to use, and provide a consistent power supply. However, they are expensive and need to be replaced periodically.

2. Solar Cells: Solar cells are an increasingly popular source of power for WSNs. They are relatively inexpensive, easy to install, and provide a steady supply of energy. However, they are dependent on the amount of sunlight available and can be unreliable in cloudy or overcast conditions.

3. Fuel Cells: Fuel cells provide a reliable and efficient source of power for WSNs. They are more expensive than primary batteries and solar cells, but they are capable of providing a steady supply of energy over a long period of time.

4. AC Power: AC power is the most reliable source of power for WSNs. It is also the most expensive, and requires the installation of an AC power outlet.

5. Radio Frequency: Radio frequency (RF) energy harvesting is a relatively new technology that is becoming increasingly popular for WSNs. It is a reliable source of power, and can be used to power WSNs in remote locations.
