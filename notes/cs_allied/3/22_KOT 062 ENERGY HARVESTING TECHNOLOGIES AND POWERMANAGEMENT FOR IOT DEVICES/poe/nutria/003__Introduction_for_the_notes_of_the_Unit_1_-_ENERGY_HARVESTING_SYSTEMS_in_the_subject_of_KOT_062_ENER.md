
### Introduction for the notes of Unit 1 - ENERGY HARVESTING SYSTEMS

1. Energy harvesting is the process of collecting energy from the environment and converting it into usable electrical energy.
2. It is a key technology for powering Internet of Things (IoT) devices, as it eliminates the need for batteries or wired power sources.
3. Energy harvesting systems typically use solar cells, thermoelectric generators, piezoelectric materials, kinetic energy harvesters, and other technologies to convert energy from the environment into electrical energy.
4. These systems are used in a wide variety of applications, including consumer electronics, medical devices, industrial automation, and military applications.
5. The key components of an energy harvesting system include the energy source, energy converter, and energy storage device.
6. The energy source is the energy source that is used to power the system, such as solar, wind, or kinetic energy.
7. The energy converter is the device that converts the energy source into usable electrical energy.
8. The energy storage device is the device that stores the energy for later use.
9. The design of an energy harvesting system must take into account the available energy sources, the efficiency of the energy converter, and the capacity of the energy storage device.
10. Energy harvesting systems can be used to power a variety of IoT devices, including sensors, actuators, and communication devices.
