
### Power Generation for the Notes of the Unit 4 - Energy Harvesting Wireless Sensors in the Subject of KOT 062 Energy Harvesting Technologies and Power Management for IoT Devices KCS

1. Power generation for IoT devices is the process of harvesting energy from the environment and converting it into electrical energy to power the device.

2. Energy harvesting technologies for IoT devices include solar, thermal, mechanical, and RF energy harvesting.

3. Wireless sensors are used to detect and transmit data from the environment to the IoT device.

4. Power management for IoT devices is the process of managing the power consumption of the device, including the power generated from energy harvesting.

5. Power management techniques for IoT devices include power scheduling, power optimization, and power balancing.
