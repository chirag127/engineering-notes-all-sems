
### Case Studies for Unit 4 - Energy Harvesting Wireless Sensors

1. **Wireless Sensor Network (WSN)**: A WSN is a network of wireless devices that can sense, collect, and transmit data over a specific geographic area. The WSN is typically composed of a series of sensor nodes that are connected to a central hub for data collection and transmission.

2. **Energy Harvesting**: Energy harvesting is the process of capturing energy from the environment and converting it into useful electrical energy. This energy can then be used to power the wireless sensor nodes. Common forms of energy harvesting include solar, wind, and kinetic energy.

3. **Power Management**: Power management is the process of managing the power consumption of the wireless sensors in order to maximize the energy efficiency of the system. This involves optimizing the power consumption of individual nodes and the overall system.

4. **IoT Devices**: IoT devices are connected devices that can sense, collect, and transmit data over a network. IoT devices can be used in conjunction with a WSN to provide additional data points and insights.

5. **Case Studies**: Case studies are a great way to learn more about how energy harvesting and power management technologies can be used in real-world applications. Examples of case studies include smart cities, smart homes, and industrial applications.
