
# Knowledge Centered Support (KCS)

KCS is a methodology for providing customer service and technical support that is based on knowledge management and continuous improvement. It was developed by the Consortium for Service Innovation, and its main goal is to enable organizations to provide the best possible customer service and technical support by leveraging the collective knowledge of their employees.

KCS is built around the idea that the most effective way to provide customer service and technical support is to leverage the collective knowledge of the organization. This means that the organization should use the knowledge of its employees to create a shared repository of information that can be used to quickly and accurately respond to customer inquiries.

KCS also emphasizes the importance of continuous improvement. This means that organizations should strive to continuously improve their customer service and technical support by refining their knowledge base, as well as their processes and procedures.

KCS is based on five core principles:

1. Knowledge Capture: The organization should capture and store information about customer inquiries and solutions.
2. Knowledge Reuse: The organization should use the knowledge base to quickly and accurately respond to customer inquiries.
3. Knowledge Sharing: The organization should share knowledge between employees to ensure that everyone is working from the same source of information.
4. Knowledge Improvement: The organization should use feedback from customers, as well as analytics, to continuously improve the knowledge base.
5. Knowledge Application: The organization should apply the knowledge base to create better customer experiences.
