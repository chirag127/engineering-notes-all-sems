
### Power Maximizations for the Notes of the Unit 3 - ELECTROMAGNETIC ENERGY HARVESTING AND NON-LINEAR TECHNIQUES in the Subject of KOT 062 ENERGY HARVESTING TECHNOLOGIES AND POWERMANAGEMENT FOR IOT DEVICES KCS

1. Electromagnetic energy harvesting is the process of collecting and storing energy from electromagnetic fields. This energy can be used to power small devices such as sensors, actuators, and other IoT devices.

2. Non-linear techniques are methods of amplifying the energy collected from electromagnetic fields. This is done by using rectifiers, diodes, and other components to increase the efficiency of the energy harvesting process.

3. Power maximizations are techniques used to increase the amount of power that can be generated from an electromagnetic field. These techniques include increasing the area of the field, increasing the frequency of the field, and using non-linear techniques.

4. Power management for IoT devices is the process of controlling the amount of power used by the device. This is done by using power-saving techniques, such as reducing the amount of time the device is active, and using energy-efficient components.
