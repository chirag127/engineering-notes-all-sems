
## Unit 1 - ENERGY HARVESTING SYSTEMS

1. Energy harvesting systems are devices that capture and store energy from the environment. 
2. They can be used to power small electronic devices, such as sensors and actuators. 
3. Common energy sources used in energy harvesting systems include solar, wind, thermal, and kinetic energy. 
4. Solar energy harvesting systems use photovoltaic cells to convert light into electrical energy. 
5. Wind energy harvesting systems use turbines or blades to capture the kinetic energy of the wind. 
6. Thermal energy harvesting systems use thermoelectric generators to convert temperature differences into electrical energy. 
7. Kinetic energy harvesting systems use piezoelectric materials to convert mechanical energy into electrical energy. 
8. Energy harvesting systems are becoming increasingly popular due to their ability to provide renewable, clean, and reliable power sources. 
9. They are also cost-effective and require minimal maintenance.
