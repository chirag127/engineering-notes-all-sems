
### Basic Principles for the Notes of the Unit 3 - ELECTROMAGNETIC ENERGY HARVESTING AND NON-LINEAR TECHNIQUES in the Subject of KOT 062 ENERGY HARVESTING TECHNOLOGIES AND POWERMANAGEMENT FOR IOT DEVICES KCS 

1. Electromagnetic energy harvesting is a technology that uses a magnetic field to convert energy into electricity. 
2. Non-linear techniques are used to increase the efficiency of energy harvesting. 
3. Inductive energy harvesting uses an inductor to convert energy from a magnetic field into electrical energy. 
4. Capacitive energy harvesting uses capacitors to store and release energy from a magnetic field. 
5. Piezoelectric energy harvesting uses piezoelectric materials to convert mechanical energy into electrical energy. 
6. Photovoltaic energy harvesting uses solar cells to convert light into electrical energy. 
7. Thermoelectric energy harvesting uses thermoelectric materials to convert heat into electrical energy. 
8. Radio frequency energy harvesting uses radio waves to convert energy into electricity. 
9. Ultrasonic energy harvesting uses ultrasonic waves to convert energy into electricity. 
10. The energy harvested can be used to power a variety of devices, such as sensors, actuators, and microcontrollers.
