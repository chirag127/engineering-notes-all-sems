# KCS

KCS (Knowledge-Centered Service) is a methodology that aims to transform customer support by making knowledge the key asset of an organization. It is focused on creating and maintaining a knowledge base that can be used to resolve customer issues quickly and efficiently.

Here are some key points about KCS:

- KCS is a customer-centric approach to support that aims to create a self-sustaining system where customers can solve their own problems.
- The methodology is based on the "shift-left" principle, which means that the focus is on empowering customers to solve their own problems rather than relying on support staff to do so.
- KCS requires a strong emphasis on collaboration, with support staff working together to create and maintain the knowledge base.
- The knowledge base is not static; it is continually updated and refined based on new information and feedback from customers.
- KCS is not just about creating a knowledge base; it is also about ensuring that the knowledge is accurate, relevant, and up-to-date.
- The methodology emphasizes the importance of measuring the effectiveness of the knowledge base and using this information to continually improve the system.
- KCS can be applied to any industry or sector, from healthcare to financial services to technology.
- The KCS methodology is supported by a number of software tools and platforms that can help organizations implement the approach more effectively.

In summary, KCS is a customer-centric approach to support that emphasizes collaboration, continuous improvement, and the creation of a self-sustaining system where customers can solve their own problems. It requires a strong emphasis on knowledge management and measurement to be successful.
