Here are some important points to remember about Non-linear techniques for Electromagnetic Energy Harvesting:

- Non-linear techniques are used to improve the efficiency of electromagnetic energy harvesting systems by exploiting the non-linear properties of the system.
- The basic idea behind non-linear techniques is to match the impedance of the energy harvester to the output impedance of the energy source, which results in maximum power transfer.
- One of the most commonly used non-linear techniques is impedance matching, which involves adjusting the impedance of the energy harvester to match the impedance of the energy source.
- Another non-linear technique is resonant frequency tuning, which involves adjusting the resonant frequency of the energy harvester to match the frequency of the energy source.
- Non-linear techniques can also be used to improve the efficiency of rectifiers by using diodes with non-linear current-voltage characteristics.
- One of the challenges of using non-linear techniques is that they can introduce unwanted harmonics, which can reduce the efficiency of the energy harvesting system.
- To overcome this challenge, filters can be used to remove the unwanted harmonics and improve the overall efficiency of the system.
- Non-linear techniques are particularly useful in low-power energy harvesting applications, such as those used in IoT devices, where every bit of energy counts.
- It is important to carefully design and optimize non-linear techniques for each specific energy harvesting application to ensure maximum efficiency and performance.
