## Unit 4 - ENERGY HARVESTING WIRELESS SENSORS

Energy harvesting wireless sensors are used to collect data from various sources and transmit that data wirelessly to a central hub. These sensors are powered by energy harvesting techniques, which convert ambient energy from the environment into electrical energy.

Here are some key points about energy harvesting wireless sensors:

- Energy harvesting techniques include solar, thermal, and vibration energy harvesting.
- These sensors have a low power consumption and can operate for long periods of time without needing a battery change.
- They are used in various applications, such as environmental monitoring, structural health monitoring, and industrial automation.
- Energy harvesting wireless sensors can be deployed in remote or hard-to-reach locations, where it is difficult to replace batteries.
- They can transmit data wirelessly to a central hub or cloud-based platform, where the data can be analyzed and used for decision-making.
- Energy harvesting wireless sensors can be integrated with other technologies, such as artificial intelligence, to provide real-time insights and predictive analytics.
- These sensors can help reduce energy consumption and improve overall efficiency in various industries.

Overall, energy harvesting wireless sensors are a promising technology that can help address the challenges of remote monitoring and data collection. They offer a sustainable and cost-effective solution for collecting and transmitting data in various applications.
