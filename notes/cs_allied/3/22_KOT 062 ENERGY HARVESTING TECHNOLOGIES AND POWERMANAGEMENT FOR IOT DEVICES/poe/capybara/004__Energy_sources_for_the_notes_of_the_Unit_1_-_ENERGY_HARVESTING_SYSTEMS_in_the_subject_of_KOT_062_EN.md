### Energy sources

Here are some of the primary sources of energy that can be harvested to power IoT devices:

- Solar energy: This is the most common source of energy for IoT devices. Solar panels can be used to convert sunlight into electrical energy that can be used to power devices.
- Thermal energy: Heat can also be harvested to generate electrical energy. This is done using thermoelectric generators (TEGs), which convert the temperature difference between two surfaces into electricity.
- Mechanical energy: Mechanical energy can be harvested from vibrations, movements, and rotations. This energy can be converted into electrical energy using piezoelectric materials or electromagnetic generators.
- Radio frequency (RF) energy: Radio waves that are emitted by electronic devices can be harvested to generate electrical energy. This is done using RF antennas and rectifiers.
- Ambient energy: Energy can also be harvested from ambient sources such as light, sound, and electromagnetic fields. This energy can be converted into electrical energy using various types of sensors and transducers.

It is important to note that the amount of energy that can be harvested from these sources is typically very low, and therefore, energy harvesting systems must be designed to be highly efficient in order to generate enough power to operate IoT devices.
