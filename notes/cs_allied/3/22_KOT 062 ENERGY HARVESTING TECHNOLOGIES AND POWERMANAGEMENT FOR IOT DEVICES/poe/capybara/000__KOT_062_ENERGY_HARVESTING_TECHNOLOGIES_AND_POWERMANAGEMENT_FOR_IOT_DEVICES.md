# KOT 062 ENERGY HARVESTING TECHNOLOGIES AND POWER MANAGEMENT FOR IOT DEVICES

In this course, you will learn about the following topics related to energy harvesting technologies and power management for IoT devices:

## Energy Harvesting Technologies

- Definition and purpose of energy harvesting
- Types of energy sources for harvesting, including solar, thermal, kinetic, and electromagnetic
- Advantages and disadvantages of each energy source
- Basic principles of energy conversion and storage
- Energy harvesting system design considerations, including efficiency and reliability
- Case studies of energy harvesting applications in IoT devices

## Power Management for IoT Devices

- Power consumption analysis of IoT devices
- Techniques for reducing power consumption, including duty cycling, sleep modes, and voltage scaling
- Overview of power management architectures, including linear regulators, switching regulators, and DC-DC converters
- Trade-offs between power management techniques and system performance
- Real-world examples of power management in IoT devices

Overall, this course provides a comprehensive overview of energy harvesting technologies and power management for IoT devices. By the end of the course, you will have a solid understanding of the principles and techniques involved in designing and implementing energy-efficient IoT systems.
