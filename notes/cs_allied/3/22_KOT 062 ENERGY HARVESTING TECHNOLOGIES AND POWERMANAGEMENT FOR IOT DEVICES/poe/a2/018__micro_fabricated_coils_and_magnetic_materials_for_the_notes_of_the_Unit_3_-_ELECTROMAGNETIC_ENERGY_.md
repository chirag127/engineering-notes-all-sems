### Micro Fabricated Coils and Magnetic Materials

- Micro fabricated coils refer to coils that are manufactured at a micro scale using microfabrication techniques like photolithography. These coils have small dimensions but can have a high number of turns resulting in high inductance.
- Micro fabricated coils are used in electromagnetic energy harvesters to convert ambient mechanical energy into electrical energy. The small size and high inductance allow for efficient energy conversion even from low frequency ambient vibrations.
- Magnetic materials like ferrites and nanocrystalline alloys are used along with micro fabricated coils to increase the magnetic flux density and improve energy conversion efficiency. These magnetic materials have high permeability which concentrates the magnetic field and increases the inductance of the coils.
- The combination of micro fabricated coils and magnetic materials enables compact electromagnetic energy harvesters that can power low power IoT devices and wireless sensors. The small size and high efficiency make these harvesters suitable for applications where large batteries are not feasible.
