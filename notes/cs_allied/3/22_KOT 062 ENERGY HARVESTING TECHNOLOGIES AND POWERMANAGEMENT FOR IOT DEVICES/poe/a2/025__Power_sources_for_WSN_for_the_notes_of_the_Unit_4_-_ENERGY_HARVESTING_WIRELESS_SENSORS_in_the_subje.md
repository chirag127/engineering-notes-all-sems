### Power sources for WSN

- Batteries: Common power source for WSN. Limited lifetime and need replacement.
- Energy harvesting: Convert ambient energy into electric energy to power WSN. No need for battery replacement. Eg: solar, vibration, RF, thermal energy.
- Solar: Convert light energy into electric energy using photovoltaic cells. Unlimited and clean but depends on sunlight availability.
- Vibration: Convert vibrational energy from environment into electric energy using piezoelectric/electromagnetic transducers. Available in industrial environments but energy generated is little.
- RF: Convert RF signals from environment into DC voltage using rectenna. Unlimited but depends on RF signal strength.
- Thermal: Convert temperature gradients into electric energy using Seebeck effect. Available but amount of energy generated is less.

The chosen power source depends on application, deployment environment and energy requirements of the WSN. Energy harvesting enables self-sustained operation of WSN without frequent battery replacement. With advances, energy harvesting can be the primary power source for WSN.
