### Scaling for the notes of the Unit 3 - ELECTROMAGNETIC ENERGY HARVESTING AND NON-LINEAR TECHNIQUES

1. Electromagnetic Radiation Sources
- Natural sources: Sunlight, thermal radiation
- Artificial sources: Cell towers, Wi-Fi, RFID, TV/radio broadcasts
- Energy available depends on transmission power and distance from source

2. Antenna Design
- Match impedance of antenna to load for max power transfer
- Consider operating frequency and available space
- Omnidirectional vs directional antennas
- Antenna size affects radiation pattern and gain

3. Rectification and Conditioning
- Rectifier converts AC input to DC for storage/use (diodes/bridge rectifiers)
- Conditioning: Boost converter increases voltage, charge pump/buck converter for specific loads
- Limits: Circuit losses, ripple, voltage requirements of load

4. Energy Storage
- Capacitors: Fast charge/discharge, limited capacity
- Batteries: Higher capacity, slower charge rate
- Supercapacitors: In between capacitors and batteries
- Determine storage needs based on average power and peak power requirements

5. Maximum Power Point Tracking (MPPT)
- Track input power through varying load to optimize power harvested
- Perturb and observe, incremental conductance, constant voltage
- Important for varying/intermittent input power or varying load power needs
