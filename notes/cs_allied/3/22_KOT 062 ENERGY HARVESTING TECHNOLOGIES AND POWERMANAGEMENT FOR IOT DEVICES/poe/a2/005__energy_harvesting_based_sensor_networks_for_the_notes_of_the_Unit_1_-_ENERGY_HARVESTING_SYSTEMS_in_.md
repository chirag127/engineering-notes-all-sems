### Energy Harvesting Based Sensor Networks

- Sensor networks powered by batteries have limited lifetime. Energy harvesting provides a promising solution to power sensor nodes indefinitely.
- Energy harvesting sources like solar, wind, thermal, vibration can be used to generate energy to power sensor nodes.
- The energy generated is inconsistent and varies with environmental conditions. Energy storage elements like capacitors and rechargeable batteries are used to store the harvested energy and provide stable power supply to sensor nodes.
- Wireless sensor nodes have various components like sensors, microprocessor, transmitter, that require power. Efficient power management techniques are essential to optimize the utilization of the harvested energy.
- Topologies like cluster-based and chain-based are used in energy harvesting wireless sensor networks to improve efficiency. Data aggregation and efficient routing protocols can reduce energy consumption in data transmission.
- The performance of energy harvesting based sensor networks depends on the amount of energy harvested and scavenged from the ambient environment. More efficient energy harvesting and storage techniques are being researched to maximize the lifetime of wireless sensor networks.
