### Types of Energy Harvesting Systems

1. Solar Energy Harvesting Systems
- Photovoltaic cells convert sunlight into electrical energy
- Used in IoT devices deployed outdoor
- Provides intermittent and variable power depending on sunlight availability

2. Vibration Energy Harvesting Systems
- Harvest energy from ambient vibrations in the environment
- Use transducers like piezoelectric, electrostatic or electromagnetic to convert mechanical energy into electrical energy
- Used in IoT devices on rotating or vibrating machines
- Provides intermittent and variable power depending on vibration frequencies and amplitudes

3. Thermal Energy Harvesting Systems
- Convert waste heat into electrical energy using thermoelectric generators
- Used in IoT devices near hot spots or heat sources
- Provides intermittent and variable power depending on heat availability and temperature difference

4. Radio Frequency Energy Harvesting Systems
- Convert ambient RF signals into electrical energy
- Used in IoT devices near RF signal sources like Wi-Fi routers or cell towers
- Provides intermittent and variable power depending on RF signal strength

5. Wind Energy Harvesting Systems
- Use turbines to convert kinetic energy of wind into mechanical or electrical energy
- Used in IoT devices deployed outdoor in windy environments
- Provides intermittent and variable power depending on wind speed
