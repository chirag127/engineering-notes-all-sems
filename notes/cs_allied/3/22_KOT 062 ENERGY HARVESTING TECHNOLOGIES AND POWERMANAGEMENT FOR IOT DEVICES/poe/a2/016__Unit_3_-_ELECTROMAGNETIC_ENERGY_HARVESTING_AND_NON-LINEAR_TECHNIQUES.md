## Unit 3 - ELECTROMAGNETIC ENERGY HARVESTING AND NON-LINEAR TECHNIQUES

I. Electromagnetic Energy Harvesting

1. Ambient RF Energy - Radio frequency signals are abundant in the environment which can be harvested using rectenna (antenna coupled with rectifier). Components - antenna, matching network and rectifier.

2. Inductive Coupling - Transfer of energy between two coils placed close to each other. One coil is part of an AC source and the other coil is connected to load. Components - Transmitter coil and Receiver coil.

3. Vibrational Energy Harvesting - Kinetic energy from vibrations is converted into electrical energy using piezoelectric, electrostatic and electromagnetic transducers. Piezoelectric transducers generate charge proportional to strain. Electrostatic transducers vary capacitance with movement. Electromagnetic transducers generate current from magnetic field changes.

II. Non-linear Techniques

1. Pulse Generation - Rapid switching of components result in short pulses of high amplitude. Uses tunnel diodes, DTMOS, etc. Applications - Ultra wideband systems, radar, etc.

2. Frequency Multiplication - Output frequency is multiple of input frequency. Uses non-linear components like diodes and transistors.

3. RF Mixing - Combination of two input frequencies generate output at sum and difference frequencies. Uses transistors, diodes, etc. Applications - Modulation, demodulation, frequency conversion, etc.
