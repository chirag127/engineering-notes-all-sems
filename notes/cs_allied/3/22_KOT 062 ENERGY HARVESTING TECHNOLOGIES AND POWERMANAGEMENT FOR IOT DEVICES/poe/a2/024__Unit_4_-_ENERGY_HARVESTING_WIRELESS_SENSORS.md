## Unit 4 - ENERGY HARVESTING WIRELESS SENSORS

1. Introduction
- Wireless sensors are devices that sense and transmit data without any physical connections. They are used in various monitoring applications.
- However, wireless sensors are battery operated and have limited lifetime due to the power requirements of sensing, processing and wireless transmission tasks.
- Energy harvesting is a promising solution to power wireless sensors continuously by harvesting energy from the ambient environment. Some potential energy sources are solar, thermal, kinetic, radio frequency, etc.

2. Solar Energy Harvesting
- Solar panels can be used to convert solar energy into electrical energy for sensors. They generate power during daytime but are ineffective during nights.
- Rechargeable batteries are required as energy storage for continuous operation. MPPT (Maximum Power Point Tracking) techniques are used to get maximum power from the solar panel.

3. Thermal Energy Harvesting
- The temperature difference between the environment and a hot surface can be converted into electrical energy using thermoelectric generators. It can be used to power sensors monitoring hot spots.
- However, the amount of energy generated is generally small and highly dependent on the temperature difference. It may not be sufficient to power wireless transmission.

4. Kinetic Energy Harvesting
- Kinetic energy from vibrations or motions can be converted into electrical energy using piezoelectric materials or electromagnetic generators.
- They are suitable for sensors monitoring fast-moving machines or vehicles. However, the irregular and intermittent nature of kinetic energy makes energy storage necessary before it can be used to power sensors.

5. Radio Frequency Energy Harvesting
- The energy in radio frequency signals can be harvested using a rectifying antenna. It is suitable for powering sensors in RF-rich environments like wi-fi hotspots.
- However, the amount of energy available depends on the surrounding RF signals which are unpredictable. It cannot be used as the sole energy source and requires supplemental energy sources.
