### Power Maximizations for Unit 3 - Electromagnetic Energy Harvesting and Non-linear Techniques

1. Electromagnetic Energy Harvesting
- Electromagnetic induction: Generating electricity from moving magnets
- Vibration energy harvesting: Generating electricity from vibrating systems using springs and magnets
- Radio frequency (RF) energy harvesting: Generating electricity from ambient RF signals
- Advantages: High power density, wide bandwidth, compatible with CMOS
- Disadvantages: Requires large antennas and tuning circuits, sensitive to distance and antenna orientation

2. Non-linear Energy Harvesting Techniques
- Non-linear oscillators: Oscillators that harvest more energy than linear oscillators
- Bistable oscillators: Oscillators with two stable states that can achieve high energy efficiencies
- Ratchet oscillators: Oscillators that effectively "ratchet" energy in one direction to achieve high efficiencies
- Advantages: Can achieve higher energy efficiencies than linear oscillators
- Disadvantages: Complex systems that require more components and careful design
