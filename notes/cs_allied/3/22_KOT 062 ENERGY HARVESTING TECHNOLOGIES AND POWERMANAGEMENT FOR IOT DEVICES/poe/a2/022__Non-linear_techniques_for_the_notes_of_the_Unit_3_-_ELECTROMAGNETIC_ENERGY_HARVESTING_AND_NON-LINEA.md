### Non-linear techniques for the notes of the Unit 3 - ELECTROMAGNETIC ENERGY HARVESTING AND NON-LINEAR TECHNIQUES in the subject of KOT 062 ENERGY HARVESTING TECHNOLOGIES AND POWERMANAGEMENT FOR IOT DEVICES KCS

1. Rectification: Converting AC to DC
- Full-wave rectification: Uses both halves of the AC waveform, higher efficiency
- Half-wave rectification: Uses only one half of the AC waveform, lower efficiency

2. Boost conversion: Increases voltage, decreases current
- Used to increase voltage to required level for storage or load
- Output voltage higher than input voltage
- Uses inductors and capacitors to store and transfer energy

3. Buck conversion: Decreases voltage, increases current
- Used to decrease voltage to required level for load
- Output voltage lower than input voltage
- Also uses inductors and capacitors

4. Charge pumps: Transfer charge between capacitors to increase or decrease voltage
- Multiple stages of capacitors and switches are used to incrementally increase or decrease voltage
- Easy to integrate but can be inefficient for large voltage changes

5. Energy extraction and optimization
- Need to optimize extraction for varying input conditions and load requirements
- Maximum power point tracking: Adjusting extraction to get maximum power from source
- Load matching: Adjusting operating point to match load requirements
