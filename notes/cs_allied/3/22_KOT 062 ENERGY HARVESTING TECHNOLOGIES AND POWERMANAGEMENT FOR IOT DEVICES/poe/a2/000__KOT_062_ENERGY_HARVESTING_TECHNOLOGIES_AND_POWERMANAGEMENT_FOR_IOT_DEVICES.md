# KOT 062 ENERGY HARVESTING TECHNOLOGIES AND POWER MANAGEMENT FOR IOT DEVICES

1. Energy Harvesting Technologies
- Photovoltaic cells: Convert light energy into electrical energy. Used in outdoor IoT devices.
- Piezoelectric transducers: Generate voltage when mechanical stress/pressure is applied. Used in motion/vibration powered devices.
- Radio frequency (RF) energy harvesting: Captures RF signals from sources like TV/cell towers to generate power. Used in RF rich environments.
- Thermoelectric generators: Generate voltage from temperature gradients. Used in heat flux based devices.

2. Power Management for IoT Devices
- Low power modes: Sleep/hibernate modes reduce power consumption when devices are idle.
- Energy efficient components: Use of low power microcontrollers, memory, radios, etc. to minimize energy usage.
- Power conversion and distribution: DC-DC converters, regulators, etc. to efficiently store and distribute energy harvested/supplied.
- Alternative communications: Use of communication protocols requiring lesser power like Bluetooth Low Energy, Zigbee, etc. instead of WiFi/cellular.

3. Applications
- Wearable/implantable medical devices
- Smart home sensors
- Industrial monitoring sensors
- Wildlife tracking tags
- Smart city infrastructure
