### Transducers

- Piezoelectric transducers convert mechanical energy into electrical energy and vice versa.
- They contain piezoelectric materials that produce an electric charge in response to applied mechanical stress.
- The most common piezoelectric materials are crystals like quartz, tourmaline, and ceramics like lead zirconate titanate (PZT).
- When mechanical stress is applied, the atoms in the crystal become polarized and produce electrical charge. This is known as the direct piezoelectric effect.
- When an electric field is applied, the crystal becomes strained. This is known as the inverse piezoelectric effect.
- Piezoelectric transducers are used in devices like buzzers, sensors, actuators, and for energy harvesting.

- Electromagnetic transducers convert mechanical energy into electrical energy and vice versa using magnetic fields.
- They contain a coil of wire wrapped around a movable magnetic core.
- When the magnetic core moves relative to the coil, it induces a voltage. This is known as electromagnetic induction and is the basis for operation.
- The moving core can be the input with the coil sensing the motion, or a voltage can be applied to the coil to produce motion of the core.
- Common applications of electromagnetic transducers include microphones, speakers, inductive sensors, and energy harvesting devices.

- Triboelectric transducers convert mechanical energy into electrical energy by the contact and separation of two materials.
- When two materials are brought into contact and then separated, electrons flow between them which produces a voltage. This is known as the triboelectric effect.
- Common triboelectric material pairs include polymers like polytetrafluoroethylene and polyethylene with metals, rubber/silicon, and some ceramics/polymers.
- The amount of charge transferred depends on the materials, surface area, speed of contact/separation, temperature, and other factors.
- Triboelectric transducers are used for energy harvesting and sensing applications. They can generate high voltage but low current output.
