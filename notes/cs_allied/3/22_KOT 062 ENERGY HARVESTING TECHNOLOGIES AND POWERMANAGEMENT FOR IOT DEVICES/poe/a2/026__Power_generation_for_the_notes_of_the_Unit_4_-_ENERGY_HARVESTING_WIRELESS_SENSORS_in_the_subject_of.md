### Power generation for the notes of the Unit 4 - ENERGY HARVESTING WIRELESS SENSORS

1. Solar power generation:
- Solar panels convert the energy of light into electrical energy.
- Photovoltaic effect is utilized to generate electricity from the energy of sunlight.
- Main advantages: Renewable, abundant and eco-friendly.
- Challenges: Dependence on sunlight, efficiency reduced in bad weather conditions and at nights.

2. Vibration energy harvesting:
- Vibrations from the surroundings are converted into electrical energy.
- Piezoelectric, electromagnetic and electrostatic transducers are used.
- Main sources of ambient vibrations: Machinery, human movements, vehicles, etc.
- Advantages: Ubiquitous source, no dependence on environment conditions.
- Challenges: Low power density, specific transducers required for different frequencies.

3. Thermoelectric energy harvesting:
- The heat energy from the temperature difference between two surfaces is converted into electrical energy.
- Seebeck effect is utilized in thermoelectric generators.
- Sources of temperature gradient: Sunlight, vehicle exhaust, computer processors, etc.
- Advantages: Can harness waste heat energy, no moving parts.
- Challenges: Low efficiency and power density, high dependence on significant temperature gradients.

4. RF energy harvesting:
- The energy from RF signals in the environment is harvested and converted to electrical energy.
- RF waves from sources like Wi-Fi, cell towers, radio stations, etc. are tapped.
- Rectenna is used to convert the RF energy into DC electricity.
- Advantages: Ubiquitous energy source.
- Challenges: Low power density, complex and expensive harvesters.
