### Case Studies for Unit 4 - ENERGY HARVESTING WIRELESS SENSORS

1. Ambient RF Energy Harvesting:
- RF waves from various sources like TV/Radio towers, cell towers, WiFi routers can be harvested using rectenna arrays.
- The harvested RF energy can be used to power low power wireless sensors.
- Efficiency of RF energy harvesting is low but it is an attractive solution as the RF signals are ubiquitous.
- An example is the sensor network powered by TV tower radiation developed at University of Washington.

2. Vibration Energy Harvesting:
- Vibrations from machines, vehicles, civil structures can be converted into electrical energy using piezoelectric, electromagnetic or electrostatic transducers.
- The harvested vibration energy can be used to power wireless sensors to monitor machines or civil structures.
- Efficiency of vibration energy harvesting depends on the frequency and amplitude of ambient vibrations. It works well for certain applications like monitoring of vehicles or machines.
- Some examples are Tire Pressure Monitoring Systems powered by vibration energy and wireless sensors powered by wind turbine vibrations.

3. Solar Energy Harvesting:
- Solar energy can be harvested using photovoltaic panels and used to power wireless sensors.
- It is attractive for applications where sensors are deployed outdoor in sunlight.
- The energy output depends on the intensity of sunlight which varies with location, time of the day and weather conditions. Energy storage may be required for continuous operation during night or in low light conditions.
- Examples are solar powered wireless sensor networks for precision agriculture and solar powered traffic sensors.
