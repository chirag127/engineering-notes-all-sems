### Power Conditioning and Losses

- Power conditioning is required to convert the input power from the harvester to the required voltage and current levels needed by the load circuit. This is done using DC-DC converters.
- The conversion process is not 100% efficient and results in some losses. The efficiency of the power conditioning circuit depends on the type of converter used, the conversion ratio required and the operating conditions.
- Other losses that affect the overall efficiency of the energy harvesting system include:
-- Transduction losses: The energy conversion from ambient source to electrical energy is not perfect and results in some losses. Eg. The solar cell does not convert all incident light into electricity.
-- Storage losses: If energy storage is used, the charging and discharging processes are not 100% efficient and result in some losses. The loss depends on the type and capacity of the storage device.
-- Leakage losses: There are small leakage currents in the system even when the system is not in use. These contribute to energy losses. Proper system design can minimize leakage losses.
