## Unit 5 - SELECTED APPLICATIONS OF ENERGY HARVESTING SYSTEMS

Sure! Let's talk about Unit 5 - Selected Applications of Energy Harvesting Systems.

Unit 5 - Selected Applications of Energy Harvesting Systems focuses on the various applications of energy harvesting systems in different fields and industries. This unit provides students with a comprehensive understanding of the various applications of energy harvesting systems and how these systems can be used to solve real-world problems.

Some of the key topics covered in Unit 5 include:

1. Wearable Devices: Students will learn about the use of energy harvesting systems in wearable devices, such as smartwatches, fitness trackers, and health monitors. They will study the various types of energy harvesting systems used in these devices, including photovoltaic, thermoelectric, and piezoelectric systems, and will learn about the benefits and limitations of these systems.

2. IoT Devices: Students will learn about the use of energy harvesting systems in IoT devices, such as smart homes, smart cities, and industrial IoT systems. They will study the various types of energy harvesting systems used in these devices, including electromagnetic, thermoelectric, and photovoltaic systems, and will learn about the benefits and limitations of these systems.

3. Transportation: Students will learn about the use of energy harvesting systems in transportation, including the use of energy harvesting systems in electric vehicles, hybrid vehicles, and other forms of transportation. They will study the various types of energy harvesting systems used in these applications, including kinetic, thermoelectric, and photovoltaic systems, and will learn about the benefits and limitations of these systems.

4. Energy Storage: Students will learn about the use of energy harvesting systems in energy storage, including the use of energy harvesting systems to store energy from renewable sources, such as wind and solar energy. They will study the various types of energy storage systems used in these applications, including batteries, supercapacitors, and flywheels, and will learn about the benefits and limitations of these systems.

In conclusion, Unit 5 - Selected Applications of Energy Harvesting Systems provides students with a comprehensive understanding of the various applications of energy harvesting systems in different fields and industries. This unit will equip students with the knowledge and skills they need to design and implement effective energy harvesting systems for a wide range of applications.
