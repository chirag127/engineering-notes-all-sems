Energy harvesters, also known as energy scavengers, are devices that are used to capture and store energy from ambient sources such as light, heat, vibration, or radio frequency (RF) energy. Energy harvesters are used to power Internet of Things (IoT) devices, which often have limited energy resources and rely on energy harvesting to provide a sustainable source of power.

Piezoelectric energy harvesters are a type of energy harvester that use piezoelectric materials to convert mechanical vibrations into electrical energy. Piezoelectric materials, such as quartz, generate an electrical charge when subjected to mechanical stress. This property is used in piezoelectric energy harvesters to convert mechanical vibrations into electrical energy, which can then be stored and used to power IoT devices.
