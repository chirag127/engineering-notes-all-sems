Electromagnetic energy harvesting is a process by which electrical energy is captured and stored from ambient sources such as light, heat, and electromagnetic radiation. Electromagnetic energy harvesting is an important aspect of energy harvesting technologies and power management for IoT devices, as it provides a sustainable source of energy for these devices.

The basic principles of electromagnetic energy harvesting involve the conversion of electromagnetic energy into electrical energy using a transducer or energy converter. The transducer or energy converter typically consists of a material that exhibits a change in electrical resistance or impedance in response to changes in electromagnetic fields. This change in electrical resistance or impedance is used to generate an electrical voltage or current, which can then be stored and used to power IoT devices.
