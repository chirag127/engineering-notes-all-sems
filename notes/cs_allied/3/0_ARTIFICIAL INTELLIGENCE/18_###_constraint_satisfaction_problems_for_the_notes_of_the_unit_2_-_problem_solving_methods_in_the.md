### Constraint Satisfaction Problems for the notes of the Unit 2 - PROBLEM SOLVING METHODS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

Constraint Satisfaction Problems (CSPs) in Artificial Intelligence:
- Constraint Satisfaction Problems (CSPs) are problems in artificial intelligence where the goal is to find a solution that satisfies a set of constraints.
- CSPs are used in a variety of applications, including scheduling, planning, and configuration.
- CSPs are defined by:
  1. Variables: the unknowns in the problem.
  2. Domains: the possible values for each variable.
  3. Constraints: the restrictions that must be satisfied by the solution.
- CSPs can be solved using a variety of algorithms, including backtracking, forward checking, and constraint propagation.
- CSPs can be represented using a variety of data structures, including graphs, matrices, and tables.
- Understanding CSPs is important for the design and development of intelligent systems that can solve problems with constraints, such as scheduling and planning problems.
