### Perception for the notes of the Unit 5 - APPLICATIONS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

Perception in Artificial Intelligence:
- Perception refers to the process of acquiring, interpreting, and organizing sensory information from the environment.
- Perception is a critical component of many AI applications, including robotics, computer vision, and autonomous systems.
- Perception can be divided into several stages, including:
  1. Sensing: the acquisition of raw sensory data from the environment.
  2. Pre-processing: the processing of raw sensory data to remove noise and to extract relevant information.
  3. Feature extraction: the identification of salient features in the sensory data.
  4. Object recognition: the identification of objects in the environment based on their features.
- Perception can be implemented using a variety of techniques, including computer vision algorithms, machine learning, and deep learning.
- Understanding perception is important for the design and development of AI systems that can interact with and understand the environment.
