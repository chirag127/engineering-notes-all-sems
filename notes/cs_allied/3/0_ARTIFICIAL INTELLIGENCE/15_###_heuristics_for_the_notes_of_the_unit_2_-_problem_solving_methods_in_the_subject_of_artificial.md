Heuristics in Artificial Intelligence:
- Heuristics are techniques used in artificial intelligence to guide problem-solving and decision-making.
- Heuristics are used to estimate the cost of reaching a goal state, to prioritize the exploration of different states, and to make decisions about which actions to take.
- Heuristics can be based on a variety of information, including the structure of the problem, the properties of the states, and the goals of the system.
- Examples of heuristics include:
  1. Admissibility: a heuristic that estimates the actual cost of reaching a goal state.
  2. Consistency: a heuristic that ensures that the estimated cost of reaching a goal state is not greater than the actual cost.
  3. Monotonicity: a heuristic that ensures that the estimated cost of reaching a goal state is non-decreasing along any path.
- Heuristics can be used to improve the efficiency and effectiveness of search algorithms, such as informed search algorithms, and to make decisions in real-time systems.
- Understanding heuristics is important for the design and development of artificial intelligence systems that can solve problems efficiently and effectively.
