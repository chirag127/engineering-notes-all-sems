Stochastic Games

Stochastic games are a type of game where the outcome is uncertain and depends on random events. In these games, players make decisions based on the current state of the game and the probabilities of different outcomes. Stochastic games are used in a variety of applications, including decision-making, risk management, and game theory.

Stochastic games can be modeled as Markov decision processes, where the current state of the game determines the probabilities of different outcomes. Players make decisions based on the expected utilities of different actions, and the expected utilities are updated as the game progresses.

There are several types of stochastic games, including two-player zero-sum games, multi-player games, and cooperative games. Two-player zero-sum games are games where one player wins and the other player loses, and the total value of the game is zero. Multi-player games are games where there are multiple players, and the outcome depends on the decisions of all players. Cooperative games are games where players work together to achieve a common goal.
