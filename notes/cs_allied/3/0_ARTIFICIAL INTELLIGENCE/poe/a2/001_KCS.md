# KCS

- KCS stands for Knowledge Centered Service.
- It is a methodology to systematically capture, organize, and share knowledge and make it available for future use.
- The key components of KCS are:
- Knowledge base: A collection of information organized in a logical way. It contains solutions to common problems and captures institutional knowledge.
- Knowledge engineers: People responsible for researching, writing, organizing, and maintaining the knowledge base.
- Service channels: Methods to access the knowledge base such as self-service portals, chatbots, etc.
- Feedback loops: Mechanisms to continuously improve the knowledge base based on usage patterns and customer feedback.
- Benefits of KCS:
- Increased speed and accuracy of service delivery.
- Reduced dependence on individual employees. Knowledge is captured and available for all.
- Better utilisation of resources through a structured knowledge management process.
- Scalability and consistency through standardization of information and processes.
- Improved customer satisfaction due to faster resolution of queries.
