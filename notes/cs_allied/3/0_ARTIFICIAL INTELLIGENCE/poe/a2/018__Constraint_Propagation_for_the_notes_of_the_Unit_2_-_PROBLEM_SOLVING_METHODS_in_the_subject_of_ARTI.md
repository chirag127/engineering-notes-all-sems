### Constraint Propagation for the notes of the Unit 2 - PROBLEM SOLVING METHODS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

1. Constraint propagation is a technique used to prune the search space and reduce the complexity of problems.
2. It works by identifying and enforcing constraints between variables to deduce how they can or cannot be assigned.
3. As constraints are identified and enforced, values are eliminated from the possible assignments of variables, thus reducing the search space.
4. This process continues until no more deductions can be made or all variables have a single possible value.
5. If a contradiction is reached at any point (no possible assignments for a variable remain), then the initial constraints were inconsistent and no solution exists.
6. Otherwise, the remaining possible values represent solutions to the constrained problem.
7. Constraint propagation is commonly used in conjunction with search to both reduce the search space and detect failures early.
8. By identifying and enforcing constraints, we can potentially avoid extensive searching of portions of the space that cannot lead to solutions.
