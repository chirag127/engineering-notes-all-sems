### Moving for the notes of the Unit 5 - APPLICATIONS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

1. Expert Systems
- Systems that contain stored knowledge and inferencing capabilities to provide expert-level advice or decisions in a specific domain.
- Components: Knowledge base, inference engine, user interface.
- Limitations: Narrow domains, lack of common sense, rigidity.

2. Robotics
- Design and construction of robots that can perform a variety of physical tasks.
- Components: Mechanical system, sensors, controllers, programming.
- Applications: Manufacturing, space exploration, household appliances, surgery, etc.

3. Computer Vision
- Automatic analysis and understanding of images and videos.
- Tasks: Object recognition, scene understanding, depth perception, image segmentation, etc.
- Applications: Facial recognition, self-driving cars, surveillance, image retrieval, etc.

4. Natural Language Processing
- Automatic processing of human languages.
- Tasks: Text classification, sentiment analysis, machine translation, speech recognition, relationship extraction, etc.
- Applications: Virtual assistants, machine translation, spam detection, summarization, question answering, etc.

5. Fuzzy Systems
- Systems that can make decisions based on ambiguous or uncertain information.
- Components: Fuzzy sets, fuzzy logic, fuzzy rules.
- Applications: Control systems, pattern recognition, forecasting, decision making, etc.
