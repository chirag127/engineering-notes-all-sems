### Trust and Reputation in Multi-agent systems

- In multi-agent systems, agents interact with other agents and need to determine which agents are trustworthy and reliable to interact with.
- Trust and reputation models are used to determine trustworthiness of agents.
- Trust is the belief an agent has that another agent will behave in an expected manner. It is based on direct interactions and experiences with another agent.
- Reputation is the perception of an agent based on information from other agents. It is indirect and based on opinions of others.
- Combining trust and reputation provides a more robust measure of trustworthiness. Direct interactions are weighted higher than indirect opinions.
- Challenges in trust and reputation models include:
-- Handling conflicting opinions and subjective opinions
-- Bad mouthing and sybil attacks where malicious agents give dishonest ratings
-- Determining how much weight to give to direct vs indirect information
-- Scalability to large multi-agent systems
- Applications of trust and reputation include:
-- E-commerce: Determining trustworthy sellers and buyers
-- Social networks: Identifying spammers and honest users
-- Peer-to-peer systems: Identifying malicious peers
