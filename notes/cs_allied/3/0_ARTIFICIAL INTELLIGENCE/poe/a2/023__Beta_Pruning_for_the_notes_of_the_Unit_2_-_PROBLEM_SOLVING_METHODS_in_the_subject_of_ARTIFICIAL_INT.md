### Beta Pruning for the notes of the Unit 2 - PROBLEM SOLVING METHODS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

1. Beta pruning is a search control technique used in game playing programs.
2. It works by limiting the depth of the search by a constant called beta.
3. The value beta is chosen in such a way that the complete search up to that depth would give good results in most of the cases.
4. Beyond the depth beta, the positions are not evaluated and are simply assigned a rough estimate of the evaluation.
5. This technique saves a lot of time as the complete search space is very large in game playing programs.
6. The major disadvantage is that some good moves beyond depth beta may get missed.
7. But overall, beta pruning has been found to work reasonably well in practice, and gives a substantial increase in speed with only a small decrease in quality.
