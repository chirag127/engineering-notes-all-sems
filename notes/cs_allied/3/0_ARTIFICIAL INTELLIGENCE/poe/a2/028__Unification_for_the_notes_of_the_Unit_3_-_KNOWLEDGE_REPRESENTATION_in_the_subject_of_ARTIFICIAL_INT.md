### Unification for the notes of the Unit 3 - KNOWLEDGE REPRESENTATION in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

1. Knowledge Representation - It refers to the way knowledge is encoded and stored. It is about representing knowledge in symbols to enable automated reasoning.
2. Why is Knowledge Representation required? - To enable machines to understand the knowledge and perform tasks like:
    - problem solving
    - planning
    - learning
    - common sense reasoning
3. Types of Knowledge Representation -
    1. Logic-based representations - Prolog
    2. Frame-based representations - Frames, semantic networks
    3. Rule-based representations - Production rules
    4. Conceptual graphs
    5. Scripts and schemas
    6. Ontologies
4. Features of a good Knowledge Representation -
    - Expressivity - Able to represent a wide variety of knowledge
    - Clarity - Has a unambiguous semantics
    - Computational efficiency - Can be computed with in reasonable time and space bounds
    - Extensibility - Easy to extend to include new knowledge
    - Learnability - Easy to learn the representation language
