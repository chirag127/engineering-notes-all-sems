### Definition for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

1. Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the simulation of human intelligence in machines like computers.
2. AI makes it possible for machines to learn from experience, adjust to new inputs and perform human-like tasks.
3. The main objectives of AI are:
 - Making machines intelligent.
 - Developing computers that can solve problems in a smart way.
 - Creating computer systems that can perform tasks that normally require human intelligence.
4. The key characteristics of AI are:
 - Adaptation: AI systems can adapt themselves to the environment or inputs.
 - Natural Language: AI systems can understand human language as input.
 - Reasoning: AI systems can solve problems, take decisions and draw inferences.
 - Learning: AI systems can learn from data, experience and interactions.
 - Creativity: Some AI systems can think out of the box and surprise humans with their solutions or works.
5. The major types or branches of AI are:
 - Narrow AI or Weak AI: Specialized in one area. Eg: Playing chess.
 - General AI or Strong AI: As intelligent as a human across domains. Not developed yet.
 - Applied AI: Use of AI techniques to solve real-world problems. Eg: Spam filtering, recommendation systems.
