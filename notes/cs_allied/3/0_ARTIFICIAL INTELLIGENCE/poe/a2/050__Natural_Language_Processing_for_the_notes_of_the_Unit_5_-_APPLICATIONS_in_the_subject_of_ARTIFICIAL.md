### Natural Language Processing for the notes of the Unit 5 - APPLICATIONS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS.

1. Introduction
- Natural language processing (NLP) is a branch of artificial intelligence that deals with the interaction between computers and humans using the natural language.
- The goal of NLP is to enable computers to analyze, understand and generate human language as a way to interact with humans.

2. Components of NLP
- tokenization: Splitting input text into words, phrases, symbols, etc. (known as tokens)
- Stemming: Reducing related words to their word stem
- Lemmatization: Grouping together inflected forms of a word
- Part-of-speech tagging: Labeling words as nouns, verbs, adjectives, etc.
- Named entity recognition: Locating and classifying named entities in text
- Semantic analysis: Determining the sentiment, emotions, or intentions in user-generated text
- Relationship extraction: Identifying relationships between entities, topics, phrases, etc.
- Question answering: Automatically answering questions posed by humans in a natural language
- Machine translation: Translating text from one human language to another

3. Applications of NLP
- Sentiment analysis: Opinion mining to determine the attitude, opinions and emotions of a speaker or writer. Used in market research.
- Spam detection: Identifying and filtering unwanted emails.
- Summarization: Generating a concise summary of a document while preserving key information.
- Chatbots and virtual assistants: Software applications that can have automated conversations with humans.
- Machine translation: Translating text from one language to another. Used by companies to reach global audience.
- Information retrieval: Finding relevant information from a large corpus of documents in response to a user query. Used in search engines.
