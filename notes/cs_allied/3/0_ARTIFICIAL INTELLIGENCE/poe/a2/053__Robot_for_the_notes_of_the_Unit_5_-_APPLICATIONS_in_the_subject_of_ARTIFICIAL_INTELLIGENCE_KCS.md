### Robot

- A robot is a machine that can perform a complex series of actions automatically or with guidance. Robots are widely used in many industries and applications.
- Robots are used extensively in manufacturing for repetitive and dangerous tasks. They are used to assemble products, handle materials, pack and stack products, paint and spray coatings, etc. This increases productivity and reduces risk of injury to human workers.
- Robots are used in space exploration to collect data and samples from other planets. Space probes and rovers are robotic spacecraft and vehicles that can travel and explore space without human crew.
- Robots are used in the medical field for procedures like surgery, rehabilitation, prosthetics, etc. Surgical robots assist surgeons in performing complex surgeries with precision. Exoskeleton robots help patients with spinal cord injuries walk again.
- Robots are used in the military for reconnaissance, surveillance, bomb detection and disposal, rescue operations, etc. Unmanned aerial vehicles (drones) and unmanned ground vehicles are used to reduce risk to human soldiers.
- Self-driving cars use various sensors and AI algorithms to navigate roads and travel to destinations without human input. The technology is still being developed but autonomous vehicles could drastically change transportation in the future.
- Chatbots and virtual assistants are AI software programs that can understand language and respond to questions. They are being developed to assist users with a variety of everyday tasks like getting information, managing schedules and controlling home devices.
