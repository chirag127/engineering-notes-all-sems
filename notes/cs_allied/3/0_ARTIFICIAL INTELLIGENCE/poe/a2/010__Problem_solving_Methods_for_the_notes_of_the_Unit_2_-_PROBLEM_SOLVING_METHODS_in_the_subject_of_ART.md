### Problem solving Methods for the notes of the Unit 2 - PROBLEM SOLVING METHODS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

1. Blind Search:
- Exhaustive search of all possible solutions.
- Guarantees to find the optimal solution but time taken is exponential.
- Practical only for small problems.

2. Informed Search:
- Using heuristics to guide the search to promising areas of the search space.
- Reduces the search space to be explored.
- Does not guarantee optimal solution but gives good solutions in less time than blind search.
- Examples: Hill climbing, Beam search, A* search.

3. Constraint Satisfaction:
- Problems can be represented as a set of constraints that must be satisfied.
- Methods try to find values for variables to satisfy all constraints.
- Examples: Backtracking, Min-conflicts algorithm.

4. Reasoning:
- Logical deduction to reach conclusions from a set of facts or axioms.
- Examples: Forward and backward chaining, Resolution in first-order logic.
