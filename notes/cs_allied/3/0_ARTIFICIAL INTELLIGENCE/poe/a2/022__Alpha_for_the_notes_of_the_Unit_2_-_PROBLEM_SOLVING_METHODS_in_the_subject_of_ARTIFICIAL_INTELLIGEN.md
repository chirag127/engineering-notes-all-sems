### Alpha for the notes of the Unit 2 - PROBLEM SOLVING METHODS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS:

1. Uninformed Search: It is a brute-force approach where the algorithm searches the problem space without any heuristics. It evaluates every possible path and chooses the best solution.
2. Informed Search: It utilizes problem-specific knowledge (heuristics) to guide the search towards more promising solutions, thus reducing the search space.
3. Hill Climbing: It is a local search technique that starts with an initial solution and then attempts to find a better solution by incrementally improving the current solution.
4. Simulated Annealing: It is a randomized local search technique. It allows for probabilistic acceptance of worsening solutions, enabling the algorithm to escape from local optima.
5. Genetic Algorithms: It is an optimization technique inspired by biological evolution. It works with a population of candidate solutions and uses genetic operators like mutation and crossover to evolve better solutions.
