### Negotiation and Bargaining for the notes of the Unit 4 - SOFTWARE AGENTS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

- Negotiation is a process where two or more parties exchange goods, services, or valued interests in an attempt to agree upon courses of action, exchange, or resource allocation in a situation of conflict or mutual advantage.
- Bargaining is a type of negotiation where the parties in disagreement try to reach an acceptable agreement through an exchange of offers and counteroffers.
- The key steps in negotiation and bargaining are:
    - Define the problem and interests of parties.
    - Understand priorities and options.
    - Identify underlying interests.
    - Generate multiple options.
    - Evaluate options through objective criteria.
    - Claim and concession making.
    - Closing or agreement.
- Strategies in negotiation and bargaining include:
    - Competitive - trying to win at the other party's expense.
    - Accommodating - being cooperative and yielding to the other party.
    - Avoiding - not dealing with the conflict or negotiation.
    - Compromising - trying to find a middle ground.
    - Collaborating - working together to find a solution that satisfies both parties.
- Software agents can employ strategies and techniques from artificial intelligence such as heuristic search, machine learning from data, reasoning about knowledge, etc. to conduct automated negotiation and bargaining on behalf of humans. The agents can negotiate based on parameters and objectives set by the humans. The agents can achieve outcomes efficiently and continuously without human intervention. However, the results may not always align with human values and preferences. Humans would need to evaluate and monitor the agents' performance.
