### Knowledge Representation for the notes of the Unit 3 - KNOWLEDGE REPRESENTATION in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

1. Knowledge Representation refers to the ways of organizing and storing knowledge. It focuses on representing and organizing data, information, and knowledge using various forms.
2. The core goal of Knowledge Representation is to facilitate efficient processing, usage, and sharing of knowledge by computer agents in a systematic manner.
3. Some of the popular forms of Knowledge Representation are:
- Rules: If-Then statements that relate conditions and actions. E.g. If temperature is high, then switch on the fan.
- Frames: Representation of concepts and relationships in the form of frames (like data structures). Each frame contains slots (fields) that store attribute values.
- Semantic Networks: Concepts are represented as nodes and relationships as links. Links are labeled to indicate the relationship type.
- Concept Maps: Visual tools that represent concepts and relationships using a graph-like structure.
4. Choosing a suitable Knowledge Representation technique depends on:
- The type of knowledge that needs to be represented
- The operations that need to be performed on the knowledge
- Processing efficiency and scalability
- Ease of knowledge acquisition and sharing
