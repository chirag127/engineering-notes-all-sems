## Unit 2 - PROBLEM SOLVING METHODS

1. Brainstorming
- Generating as many solutions as possible without judging them
- Helps overcome inhibitions and encourages creativity
- Leads to new and innovative ideas

2. Trial-and-Error
- Trying potential solutions one by one until the correct one is found
- Useful when there are a limited number of options
- Requires trying systematic variations rather than random guesses

3. Means-End Analysis
- Starting with the goal and working backwards to find a solution
- Useful for complex problems with several sub-goals
- Helps determine the difference between the current state and the goal and finding ways to reduce the difference

4. Working Backwards
- Starting with the end result and thinking sequentially backwards
- Useful for planning and scheduling complex projects with interdependencies
- Helps identify prerequisites and critical paths

5. Divide-and-Conquer
- Breaking down a large problem into smaller subproblems
- Useful for reducing complexity and making problems more manageable
- The subproblems must be similar in nature and the solutions combined to obtain the overall solution
