### Speech Recognition for the notes of the Unit 5 - APPLICATIONS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

Speech recognition is a technology that enables machines to recognize human speech and convert it into text or commands. This technology has a wide range of applications in various fields, including artificial intelligence. In this unit, we will discuss the basics of speech recognition and its applications in AI.

Here are some key points to note:

- Speech recognition is the process of converting spoken words into text or commands that a computer can understand and act upon.
- The technology behind speech recognition involves acoustic and language modeling, which is used to recognize and interpret the sounds of human speech.
- There are two types of speech recognition: speaker-dependent and speaker-independent. Speaker-dependent speech recognition requires the system to be trained on the speaker's voice, while speaker-independent speech recognition can recognize any voice.
- Speech recognition has several applications in artificial intelligence, such as virtual assistants, speech-to-text transcription, voice-activated devices, and speech-based biometric authentication.
- Virtual assistants are AI-powered software applications that can understand and respond to voice commands. Examples of virtual assistants are Siri, Alexa, and Google Assistant.
- Speech-to-text transcription is the process of converting spoken words into written text. This technology is used in applications such as dictation software, closed captioning, and voice search.
- Voice-activated devices are electronic gadgets that can be controlled by voice commands. Examples of voice-activated devices are smart speakers, smart TVs, and home automation systems.
- Speech-based biometric authentication is a security technology that uses a person's voice as a unique identifier. This technology is used in applications such as phone banking, online transactions, and access control systems.

In conclusion, speech recognition is a crucial technology in artificial intelligence that has a wide range of applications. Understanding the basics of speech recognition and its applications can help us develop innovative AI-powered solutions that can make our lives easier and more efficient.
