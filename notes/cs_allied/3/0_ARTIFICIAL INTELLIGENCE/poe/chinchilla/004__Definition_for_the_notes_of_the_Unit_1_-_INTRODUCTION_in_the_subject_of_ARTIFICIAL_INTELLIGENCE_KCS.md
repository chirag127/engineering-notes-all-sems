### Definition for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a branch of computer science that deals with the study and development of intelligent agents that can perform tasks that typically require human intelligence, such as visual perception, speech recognition, decision-making, and language translation. In this unit, we will explore the basic concepts and principles of AI, including its history, applications, and future developments.

Here are some important definitions and concepts that you should understand in order to master this unit:

- **Artificial Intelligence:** The study and development of intelligent agents that can perceive the environment, reason, learn, and act autonomously to achieve specific goals.

- **Intelligent Agent:** A software program that can perceive the environment, reason about it, and take actions to achieve specific goals.

- **Machine Learning:** A subfield of AI that focuses on the development of algorithms and models that enable computers to learn from data without being explicitly programmed.

- **Deep Learning:** A form of machine learning that uses neural networks with multiple layers to learn patterns and relationships in data.

- **Neural Network:** A computational model inspired by the structure and function of the human brain, used for pattern recognition, classification, and prediction.

- **Natural Language Processing (NLP):** A subfield of AI that focuses on the development of algorithms and models that enable computers to understand, interpret, and generate human language.

- **Robotics:** The study and development of robots, which are intelligent agents that can perceive the environment, reason, learn, and act in the physical world.

- **Expert Systems:** Computer programs that use knowledge and inference rules to solve problems that normally require human expertise.

- **Data Mining:** The process of discovering patterns and relationships in large datasets, typically using machine learning algorithms.

- **Big Data:** Extremely large datasets that require advanced computing and storage technologies to process and analyze.

By understanding these concepts and their applications, you will be able to gain a deeper understanding of the field of AI and its impact on society. In the next units, we will dive deeper into the specific techniques and algorithms used in AI, and explore their real-world applications in various industries.
