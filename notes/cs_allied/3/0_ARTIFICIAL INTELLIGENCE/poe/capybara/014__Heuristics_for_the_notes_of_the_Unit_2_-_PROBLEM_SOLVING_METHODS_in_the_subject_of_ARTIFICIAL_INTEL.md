### Heuristics for the notes of the Unit 2 - PROBLEM SOLVING METHODS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

1. Define the problem: The first step in problem-solving is to identify and define the problem you are trying to solve. Clearly define the problem statement and its scope to avoid confusion.

2. Understand the problem: Once you have defined the problem, analyze it thoroughly to understand its root cause and its impact. This will help you to identify the best solution approach.

3. Generate creative solutions: Brainstorming is an effective way to generate creative solutions. List all possible solutions without judging them. Evaluate each solution based on its feasibility, cost, and effectiveness.

4. Analyze alternative solutions: Once you have generated possible solutions, analyze each one in detail. Consider the pros and cons of each solution and its impact on the problem.

5. Select the best solution: Based on your analysis, select the solution that is most feasible, effective, and affordable. Document your decision-making process to justify your selection.

6. Implement the solution: Once you have selected the solution, develop an action plan to implement it. Identify the resources required, the timeline, and the responsibilities of each team member.

7. Monitor and evaluate: After implementing the solution, monitor its effectiveness and evaluate its impact on the problem. If the solution does not work, go back to step 3 and generate new solutions.

8. Document the process: Document the problem-solving process, including the problem statement, analysis, solution selection, implementation plan, and evaluation. This will help you to improve the process in the future and to share your experience with others.

Remember, problem-solving is a continuous process that requires creativity, critical thinking, and collaboration. By following these heuristics, you can improve your problem-solving skills and become a more effective problem solver.
