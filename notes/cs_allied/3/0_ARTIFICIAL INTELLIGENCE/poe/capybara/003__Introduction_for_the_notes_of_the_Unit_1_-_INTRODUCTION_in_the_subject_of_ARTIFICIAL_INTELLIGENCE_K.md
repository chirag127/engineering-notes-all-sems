### Introduction

In this unit, we will be exploring the fundamental concepts of Artificial Intelligence (AI). AI is a branch of computer science that focuses on creating intelligent machines that can perform tasks that typically require human intelligence.

The following are some key points to keep in mind as we delve into the world of AI:

- AI is based on the idea that machines can learn from experience, adjust to new inputs, and perform human-like tasks.
- The goal of AI is to create machines that can think, reason, and problem-solve like humans.
- There are various subfields of AI, including machine learning, natural language processing, computer vision, and robotics.
- AI has a wide range of applications, including in healthcare, finance, transportation, and entertainment.
- Ethical considerations are important when it comes to AI, as the technology has the potential to impact society in significant ways.

Throughout this unit, we will be exploring these concepts in depth and gaining a deeper understanding of the exciting and rapidly advancing field of AI.
