
### Beta Pruning

Beta pruning is a technique used in artificial intelligence to improve the efficiency of problem-solving methods. It is a type of heuristic search that uses a “best-first” approach to limit the search space by pruning away any branches of the search tree that are not likely to lead to a solution. 

Beta pruning works by using a heuristic function to determine the “cost” of each branch of the search tree. If the cost of a branch is higher than the cost of the best solution found so far, then that branch is pruned away and the search continues on the remaining branches. This process is repeated until a solution is found or all branches have been exhausted.

The advantages of beta pruning include its ability to reduce the size of the search space and its ability to quickly find a solution. However, the downside of this technique is that it may not find the optimal solution and can be computationally expensive.
