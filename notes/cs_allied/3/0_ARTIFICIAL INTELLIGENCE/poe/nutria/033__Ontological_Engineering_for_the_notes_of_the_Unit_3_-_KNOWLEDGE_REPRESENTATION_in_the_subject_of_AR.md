
### Ontological Engineering

Ontological engineering is a branch of knowledge representation that focuses on the construction of ontologies. Ontologies are formal representations of a set of concepts within a domain and the relationships between those concepts. They are used to model a domain of knowledge in order to facilitate communication and reasoning between different agents.

* Ontologies are used to represent knowledge in a structured and organized manner.
* Ontologies are composed of concepts and relationships which are used to describe a domain of knowledge.
* Ontologies provide a shared understanding of a domain of knowledge between different agents.
* Ontologies can be used to facilitate communication and reasoning between different agents.
* Ontologies can be used to represent real-world objects and their properties.
* Ontologies can be used to represent the relationships between different concepts.
* Ontologies can be used to represent the meaning of words and phrases.
* Ontologies can be used to represent the structure of a document.
* Ontologies can be used to represent the structure of a database.
