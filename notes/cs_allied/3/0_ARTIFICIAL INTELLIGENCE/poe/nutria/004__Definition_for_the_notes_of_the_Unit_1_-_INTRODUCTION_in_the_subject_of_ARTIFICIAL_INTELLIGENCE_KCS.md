
### Definition for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

* Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a field of computer science that focuses on creating intelligent machines that can act and think like humans. It is an interdisciplinary field, combining elements from computer science, psychology, philosophy, linguistics, and other fields.

* AI systems are able to learn from their environment and experiences, and can adapt to changing conditions. This allows them to perform tasks that would be difficult or impossible for humans to do.

* AI systems can be used in a variety of applications, from medical diagnosis and autonomous vehicles to natural language processing and robotics.

* AI has the potential to revolutionize many industries, from healthcare to finance to transportation. However, there are ethical and legal concerns about the use of AI, such as privacy, safety, and accountability.
