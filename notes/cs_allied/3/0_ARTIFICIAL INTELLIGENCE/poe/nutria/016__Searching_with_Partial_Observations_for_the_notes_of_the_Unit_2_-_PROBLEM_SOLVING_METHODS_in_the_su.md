
### Searching with Partial Observations 

Searching with partial observations is a problem solving method used in artificial intelligence (AI). It is used when the AI has limited information about the environment in which it is operating.

The AI must use the limited information to make decisions and take actions that will lead to a successful outcome. This requires the AI to use heuristics, or rules of thumb, to determine the best course of action.

The AI must also consider the cost of its decisions. If the cost of a decision is too high, the AI may decide to wait for more information before taking action.

Searching with partial observations can be used in a variety of AI applications, such as robotics, natural language processing, and computer vision. It is also used in game playing, where the AI must make decisions based on limited information about its opponent's moves.
