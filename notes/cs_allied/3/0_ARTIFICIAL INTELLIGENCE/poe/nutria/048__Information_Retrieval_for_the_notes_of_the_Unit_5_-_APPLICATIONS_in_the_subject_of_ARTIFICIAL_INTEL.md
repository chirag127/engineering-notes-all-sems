
### Information Retrieval for the notes of the Unit 5 - APPLICATIONS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

1. Information Retrieval (IR) is the process of finding relevant information from a collection of documents or other sources.
2. IR techniques are used to search for documents, images, videos, and other digital content.
3. IR systems use algorithms to search for relevant information based on keywords or phrases.
4. IR systems can also use natural language processing (NLP) to understand the context of a query and return more relevant results.
5. IR systems can be used to search for information in a variety of fields, including medicine, law, education, and business.
6. IR systems can also be used to search for information in large databases, such as the World Wide Web.
7. IR systems can be used to identify trends in data, such as market trends or customer preferences.
8. IR systems can also be used to create personalized search results, such as recommending products or services to customers.
9. IR systems can also be used to detect plagiarism or copyright infringement.
