
### Constraint Satisfaction Problems

* Constraint satisfaction problems (CSPs) are a type of problem in artificial intelligence where a set of constraints must be satisfied in order to find a solution. 
* CSPs are used to model and solve a wide variety of problems, such as scheduling, resource allocation, and route planning. 
* In a CSP, the goal is to find a set of values that satisfies all of the given constraints. 
* Constraints are typically expressed as logical statements, such as “the start time must be before the end time” or “the total cost must be less than a certain amount”.
* CSPs can be solved using a variety of techniques, such as backtracking, forward checking, and constraint propagation. 
* Backtracking is a systematic way of exploring all possible solutions to a CSP, while forward checking and constraint propagation are methods of pruning the search space and making the search more efficient.
