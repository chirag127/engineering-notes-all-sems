
### Knowledge Representation

Knowledge representation is the process of representing knowledge in a way that can be understood and used by a computer. It involves encoding information in a format that can be used by machines to make decisions and to provide answers to queries. Knowledge representation is an important part of artificial intelligence, as it allows machines to understand the world around them.

* Representations are used to store and manipulate knowledge in a form that is easily accessible and understandable by the computer.
* Representations can be either symbolic or non-symbolic. Symbolic representations use symbols such as words, numbers, and images to encode knowledge, while non-symbolic representations use mathematical equations and algorithms to represent knowledge.
* Representations are used to represent knowledge in a form that can be manipulated by the computer, such as rules, frames, and ontologies.
* Representations are used to represent knowledge in a form that can be used by the computer to make decisions, such as expert systems and decision trees.
* Representations are used to represent knowledge in a form that can be used by the computer to answer queries, such as natural language processing and semantic networks.
