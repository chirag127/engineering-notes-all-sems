
### Language Models for the Notes of the Unit 5 - Applications in the Subject of Artificial Intelligence KCS

1. Language models are a type of statistical model used to predict the likelihood of a sequence of words.

2. Language models are used in natural language processing (NLP) tasks such as machine translation, speech recognition, text summarization, and question answering.

3. Language models are based on the idea of predicting the next word in a sequence given the previous words.

4. The most popular language models are n-gram models, which use probability to predict the next word based on the n-1 words that came before it.

5. Neural language models such as recurrent neural networks (RNNs) and transformers are more powerful and accurate than n-gram models and are used in many NLP applications.

6. Language models can also be used to generate text, such as generating a story or poem.
