
### Natural Language Processing for the notes of the Unit 5 - APPLICATIONS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

1. Natural Language Processing (NLP) is a branch of Artificial Intelligence (AI) that focuses on the analysis and understanding of human language.
2. NLP enables machines to interact with humans in their natural language, allowing for more natural conversations and better customer service.
3. NLP can be used to analyze text, extract key information, and interpret user intent.
4. NLP algorithms can be used to identify sentiment in text, detect topics of conversation, and generate natural language summaries.
5. NLP can also be used to identify and classify entities in text, such as people, places, and organizations.
6. NLP has applications in a wide range of fields, including customer service, healthcare, finance, and education.
