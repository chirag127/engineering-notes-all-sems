
### Events for the Notes of the Unit 3 - Knowledge Representation in the Subject of Artificial Intelligence KCS

1. In 1956, John McCarthy coined the term "Artificial Intelligence" and proposed the Dartmouth Conference to discuss the potential of AI.

2. In 1966, the first AI programming language, called LISP, was developed by John McCarthy.

3. In 1969, the first AI expert system, called Dendral, was developed at Stanford University.

4. In 1974, the first AI-based vision system, called the Perceptron, was developed by Marvin Minsky and Seymour Papert.

5. In 1980, the first AI-based natural language processing system, called SHRDLU, was developed by Terry Winograd.

6. In 1982, the first AI-based robotics system, called Shakey, was developed by the Stanford Research Institute.

7. In 1988, the first AI-based expert system, called MYCIN, was developed at Stanford University.

8. In 1989, the first AI-based neural network, called the Backpropagation Network, was developed by David Rumelhart and Geoffrey Hinton.

9. In 1997, the first AI-based game-playing program, called Deep Blue, was developed by IBM.

10. In 2006, the first AI-based robot, called ASIMO, was developed by Honda.
