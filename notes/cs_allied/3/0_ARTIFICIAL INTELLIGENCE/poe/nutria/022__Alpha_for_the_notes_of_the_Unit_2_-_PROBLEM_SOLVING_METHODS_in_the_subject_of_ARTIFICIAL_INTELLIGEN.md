
### Alpha for the notes of the Unit 2 - PROBLEM SOLVING METHODS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

1. Problem solving is the process of finding a solution to a problem. It is a key component of Artificial Intelligence (AI).
2. Problem solving methods involve the use of algorithms, heuristics, and search techniques to solve problems.
3. Algorithms are a set of instructions that are used to solve a problem. They are usually written in a programming language such as Python or Java.
4. Heuristics are rules of thumb that are used to help guide the problem-solving process. They are often used to reduce the search space and find a solution more quickly.
5. Search techniques are used to explore the problem space and find a solution. These techniques include depth-first search, breadth-first search, hill-climbing, and simulated annealing.
6. AI systems often use a combination of algorithms, heuristics, and search techniques to solve problems. This allows them to find optimal solutions in a reasonable amount of time.
