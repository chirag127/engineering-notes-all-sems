
### Problem Solving Methods for the Notes of the Unit 2 - Problem Solving Methods in the Subject of Artificial Intelligence KCS

* Search: Search algorithms are used to find solutions to a given problem. Examples include depth-first search, breadth-first search, uniform cost search, and A* search.
* Heuristics: Heuristics are used to guide search algorithms. Examples include hill climbing, best-first search, and simulated annealing.
* Constraint Satisfaction: Constraint satisfaction algorithms are used to find solutions to problems with constraints. Examples include backtracking and local search.
* Optimization: Optimization algorithms are used to find the best solution to a given problem. Examples include genetic algorithms, simulated annealing, and tabu search.
* Knowledge Representation: Knowledge representation is used to represent knowledge in a form that can be used by AI systems. Examples include frames, semantic networks, and production systems.
* Learning: Learning algorithms are used to improve the performance of AI systems. Examples include supervised learning, unsupervised learning, and reinforcement learning.
