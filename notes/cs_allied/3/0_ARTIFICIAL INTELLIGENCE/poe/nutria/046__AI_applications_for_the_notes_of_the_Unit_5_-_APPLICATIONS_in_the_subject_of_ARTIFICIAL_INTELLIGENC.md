
### AI Applications

1. Natural Language Processing (NLP): NLP is the field of artificial intelligence that focuses on the understanding of human language. It is used to process large amounts of text data and to give computers the ability to understand and respond to human language.

2. Image Recognition: Image recognition is a form of AI that is used to identify objects, people, and other features from digital images or videos. It is used in various applications such as facial recognition, autonomous vehicles, and medical imaging.

3. Robotics: Robotics is the field of AI that deals with the design and development of robots. It is used in various applications such as manufacturing, automotive, and medical.

4. Machine Learning: Machine learning is a form of AI that focuses on the development of algorithms that can learn from data and make decisions without being explicitly programmed. It is used in various applications such as predictive analytics, computer vision, and natural language processing.

5. Autonomous Agents: Autonomous agents are AI systems that can act on their own and make decisions without any human input. They are used in various applications such as autonomous vehicles, drones, and robots.
