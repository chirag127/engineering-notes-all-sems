
### Characteristics of Intelligent Agents

1. **Autonomy**: Intelligent agents are capable of operating independently, without external control or input.
2. **Reactivity**: Intelligent agents are able to respond to changes in their environment in a timely manner.
3. **Pro-activity**: Intelligent agents are capable of taking the initiative and making decisions without being prompted.
4. **Adaptability**: Intelligent agents are able to adjust their behavior based on the changing environment or user input.
5. **Learning**: Intelligent agents are able to learn from their mistakes and adapt their behavior accordingly.
6. **Social Ability**: Intelligent agents are able to interact with other agents and humans in a meaningful way.
