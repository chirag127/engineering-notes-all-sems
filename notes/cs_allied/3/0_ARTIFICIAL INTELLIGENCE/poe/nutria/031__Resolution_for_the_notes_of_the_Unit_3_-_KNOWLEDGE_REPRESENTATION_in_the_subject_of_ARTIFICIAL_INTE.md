
### Resolution for the Notes of the Unit 3 - Knowledge Representation in the Subject of Artificial Intelligence KCS

1. Knowledge Representation (KR) is a field of Artificial Intelligence (AI) that deals with how knowledge can be represented in a form that can be understood and used by machines.

2. The main goal of KR is to enable machines to understand and use knowledge in order to make decisions and solve problems.

3. KR is concerned with the representation of knowledge in a way that can be used by machines to reason about the world and make decisions.

4. KR techniques include:
    * Representing knowledge in a symbolic form, such as using a language like Prolog or a graphical representation like a decision tree.
    * Representing knowledge in a numerical form, such as using a neural network or a Bayesian network.
    * Representing knowledge in a logical form, such as using a rule-based system or a knowledge base.

5. KR techniques are used in a variety of applications, such as natural language processing, robotics, and computer vision.
