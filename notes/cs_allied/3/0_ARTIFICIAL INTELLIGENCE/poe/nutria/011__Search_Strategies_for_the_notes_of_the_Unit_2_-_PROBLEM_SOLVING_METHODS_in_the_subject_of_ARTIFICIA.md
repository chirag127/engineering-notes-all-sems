
### Search Strategies for Unit 2 - Problem Solving Methods in Artificial Intelligence KCS
1. **Uninformed Search Strategies**: Uninformed search strategies are search algorithms that do not make use of any domain-specific knowledge. Examples of uninformed search strategies include breadth-first search and depth-first search.

2. **Informed Search Strategies**: Informed search strategies are search algorithms that make use of domain-specific knowledge to guide the search. Examples of informed search strategies include best-first search, A* search, and greedy search.

3. **Heuristic Search Strategies**: Heuristic search strategies are search algorithms that make use of heuristics (approximations of the true cost) to guide the search. Examples of heuristic search strategies include hill climbing and simulated annealing.
