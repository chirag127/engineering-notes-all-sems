
### Hardware for the Notes of the Unit 5 - Applications in the Subject of Artificial Intelligence KCS

- Hardware is a critical component of any Artificial Intelligence (AI) system, as it is responsible for providing the computational power required to run AI algorithms.
- AI applications require specialized hardware, such as GPUs, FPGAs, or ASICs, to enable efficient and accurate processing of large datasets.
- GPUs are the most commonly used hardware for AI applications, as they provide the necessary computational power to run deep learning algorithms.
- FPGAs are also used for AI applications, as they can be programmed to perform specific tasks and can be reprogrammed for different tasks.
- ASICs are specialized chips designed for specific tasks, such as image recognition or natural language processing, and are used in AI applications that require specific hardware for their tasks.
- AI systems also require memory and storage hardware, such as RAM and hard drives, to store data and intermediate results.
- AI systems also require networking hardware, such as routers, switches, and modems, to allow for communication between different components of the system.
