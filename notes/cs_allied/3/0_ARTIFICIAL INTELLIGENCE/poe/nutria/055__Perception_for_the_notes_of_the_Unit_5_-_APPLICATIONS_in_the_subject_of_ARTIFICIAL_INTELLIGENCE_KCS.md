
### Perception

- Perception is the ability of an AI system to interpret data from its environment in order to make decisions and take action.
- Perception is a key component of AI systems, as it enables them to understand their environment and make decisions based on that understanding.
- Perception can be divided into two main categories: low-level and high-level. Low-level perception involves the interpretation of sensory data, such as images, sound, and touch. High-level perception involves the interpretation of more abstract data, such as language, facial expressions, and body language.
- AI systems use a variety of methods to interpret data, such as machine learning, computer vision, natural language processing, and robotics.
- Perception is important for AI systems to be able to interact with their environment and make decisions. It is also important for AI systems to be able to understand the context of their environment, as this can help them make better decisions.
