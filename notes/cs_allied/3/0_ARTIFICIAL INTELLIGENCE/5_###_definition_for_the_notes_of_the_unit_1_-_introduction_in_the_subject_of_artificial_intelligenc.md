### Definition for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

Definition of Artificial Intelligence (AI):
- Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a branch of computer science that deals with the development of intelligent machines that can perform tasks that typically require human intelligence.
- AI systems are designed to perform tasks such as perception, reasoning, learning, and problem-solving.
- AI systems can be trained to perform specific tasks, such as image recognition, speech recognition, and natural language processing.
- AI systems can also be programmed to perform more complex tasks, such as playing games, solving problems, and making decisions.
- AI systems can be based on a variety of algorithms, including rule-based systems, decision trees, neural networks, and genetic algorithms.
- AI systems can be used in a variety of applications, including robotics, computer vision, natural language processing, and expert systems.
- Understanding the definition of AI is important for the design and development of intelligent systems and for the application of AI in various fields.
