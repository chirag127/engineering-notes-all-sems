Informed Search in Artificial Intelligence (AI):
- Informed search is a type of problem-solving method in AI that uses additional information to guide the search for a solution.
- Informed search algorithms use heuristics, or estimates of the cost of reaching a goal, to prioritize the search and make it more efficient.
- Informed search algorithms include best-first search, A* search, and heuristic search.
- Informed search algorithms can be used to solve problems in a variety of domains, including games, robotics, and planning.
- Informed search algorithms can be more efficient than uninformed search algorithms, such as breadth-first search and depth-first search, because they use additional information to guide the search.
- Understanding informed search is important for the design and development of intelligent systems that can solve problems effectively and efficiently.
