### Characteristics of Intelligent Agents for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

Characteristics of Intelligent Agents:
- Intelligent agents are computer programs that can perceive their environment and act autonomously to achieve their goals.
- The characteristics of intelligent agents include:
  1. Autonomy: the ability to act independently and make decisions based on the information available.
  2. Proactivity: the ability to take the initiative and act without being explicitly told what to do.
  3. Reactivity: the ability to respond to changes in the environment and to new information.
  4. Bounded rationality: the ability to make decisions based on limited information and resources.
  5. Social ability: the ability to interact with other agents and with humans.
- Intelligent agents can be used in a variety of applications, including robotics, intelligent tutoring systems, and intelligent agents for the web.
- Understanding the characteristics of intelligent agents is important for the design and development of intelligent systems that can interact effectively with their environment and with humans.
