### Hardware for the notes of the Unit 5 - APPLICATIONS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

Hardware for Artificial Intelligence (AI) Applications:
- Hardware is a critical component for the implementation of AI systems and applications.
- Different AI applications require different hardware resources, including processing power, memory, and storage.
- The hardware requirements for AI systems depend on the size and complexity of the problem, the algorithms used, and the desired performance.
- There are several hardware options for AI systems, including:
  1. Central Processing Units (CPUs): traditional computing devices that are widely used for general-purpose computing.
  2. Graphics Processing Units (GPUs): specialized computing devices that are optimized for parallel processing and are used for high-performance computing.
  3. Field-Programmable Gate Arrays (FPGAs): programmable devices that can be customized for specific computing tasks.
  4. Application-Specific Integrated Circuits (ASICs): specialized computing devices that are designed for a specific application.
- Understanding the hardware requirements for AI systems is important for the design and development of AI systems that can perform effectively and efficiently.
