## Unit 4 - SOFTWARE AGENTS

Unit 4 - Software Agents in Artificial Intelligence:
- Unit 4 of the subject "Artificial Intelligence" focuses on the concept of software agents and their role in AI.
- Software agents are autonomous programs that can perceive, reason, and act in an environment.
- Unit 4 covers the following topics:
  1. Definition and properties of software agents, including autonomy, reactivity, proactivity, and social ability.
  2. Types of software agents, including mobile, web, and multi-agent systems.
  3. Agent communication and interaction, including message passing, negotiation, and coordination.
  4. Trust and reputation in multi-agent systems, including the modeling of trust and reputation and their role in managing interactions between agents.
  5. Agent architectures, including the design and implementation of software agents.
- Understanding software agents is important for the design and development of AI systems that can interact and cooperate with other agents and with the environment.
