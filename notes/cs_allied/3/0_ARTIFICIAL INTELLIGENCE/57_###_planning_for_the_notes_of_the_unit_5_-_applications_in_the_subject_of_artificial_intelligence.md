Planning

Planning is a process of determining a sequence of actions to achieve a specific goal. In artificial intelligence, planning is used to automate the process of decision-making and to generate solutions to problems. Planning is used in a variety of applications, including robotics, scheduling, and game playing.

Planning is typically modeled as a search problem, where the goal is to find a sequence of actions that will lead to the desired goal state. The search space for a planning problem is the set of all possible sequences of actions, and the search is guided by a set of rules that define the relationships between the actions.

There are several approaches to planning in artificial intelligence, including rule-based planning, heuristic planning, and probabilistic planning. Rule-based planning is a method of planning that uses a set of rules to generate a plan. Heuristic planning is a method of planning that uses heuristics, or rules of thumb, to guide the search. Probabilistic planning is a method of planning that uses probabilistic models to represent the uncertainty in the environment.
