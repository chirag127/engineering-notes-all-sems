## Unit 5 - APPLICATIONS

Unit 5 - Applications of Artificial Intelligence:
- Unit 5 of the subject "Artificial Intelligence" focuses on the applications of AI in various domains.
- AI has a wide range of applications, including robotics, natural language processing, computer vision, and machine learning.
- Unit 5 covers the following topics:
  1. Robotics: the application of AI to the design and development of robots.
  2. Natural language processing: the application of AI to the processing and understanding of human language.
  3. Computer vision: the application of AI to the processing and understanding of visual information.
  4. Machine learning: the application of AI to the development of algorithms that can learn from data.
  5. AI in medicine: the application of AI to the diagnosis and treatment of medical conditions.
  6. AI in finance: the application of AI to the analysis and management of financial data.
- Understanding the applications of AI is important for the development of intelligent systems that can solve real-world problems and for the application of AI in a wide range of fields.
