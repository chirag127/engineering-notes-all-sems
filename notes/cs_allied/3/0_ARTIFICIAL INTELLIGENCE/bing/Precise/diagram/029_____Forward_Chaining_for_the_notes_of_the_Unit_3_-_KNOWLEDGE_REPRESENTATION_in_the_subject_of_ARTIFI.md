### Forward Chaining

Forward chaining is a method of reasoning in artificial intelligence where the inference engine starts with the available data and uses inference rules to extract more data until a goal is reached. It is also known as data-driven reasoning or bottom-up reasoning.

Here are some key points to remember about forward chaining:

1. Forward chaining is a popular method used in rule-based expert systems.
2. The inference engine starts with the available data and uses the rules to draw new conclusions.
3. The process continues until a goal is reached or no new data can be inferred.
4. Forward chaining is a bottom-up approach, where the reasoning starts with the available data and moves towards the goal.
5. Forward chaining is useful when the system has a large amount of data and the goal is not clearly defined.
