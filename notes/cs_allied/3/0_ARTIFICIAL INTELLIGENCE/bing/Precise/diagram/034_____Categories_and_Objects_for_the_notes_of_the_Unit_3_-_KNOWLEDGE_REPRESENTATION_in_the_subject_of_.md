### Unit 3 - KNOWLEDGE REPRESENTATION
#### Categories and Objects

1. Categories and objects are fundamental concepts in knowledge representation.
2. A category is a group of objects that share common characteristics or properties.
3. Objects are instances of categories and can have specific properties and attributes that define them.
4. Categories can be organized into hierarchies, with more general categories at the top and more specific categories at the bottom.
5. Objects can belong to multiple categories and can inherit properties from their parent categories.
6. Categories and objects can be used to represent knowledge in a structured and organized manner, allowing for efficient storage, retrieval, and manipulation of information.
