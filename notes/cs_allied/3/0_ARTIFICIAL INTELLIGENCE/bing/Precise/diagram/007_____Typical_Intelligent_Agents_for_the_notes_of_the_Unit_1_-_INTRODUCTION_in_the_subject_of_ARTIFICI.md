### Typical Intelligent Agents

An intelligent agent is a system that can perceive its environment, reason about it, and take actions to achieve its goals. There are several types of intelligent agents, including:

1. **Simple reflex agents**: These agents select actions based on the current percept, ignoring the rest of the percept history. They are based on condition-action rules, where the condition part matches the current percept and the action part specifies the action to be taken.

2. **Model-based reflex agents**: These agents maintain an internal model of the world, which is used to keep track of the part of the world that is not currently visible. The agent uses this model to choose actions that are appropriate for the current situation.

3. **Goal-based agents**: These agents have explicit goals that they are trying to achieve. They choose actions based on the desirability of their outcomes, taking into account the current situation and the agent's goals.

4. **Utility-based agents**: These agents have a utility function that measures the desirability of different world states. They choose actions that are expected to maximize their utility, taking into account the current situation and the agent's knowledge about the world.

5. **Learning agents**: These agents can learn from their experiences and improve their performance over time. They use various learning algorithms to update their knowledge and behavior based on the feedback they receive from the environment.

These are some of the typical intelligent agents that are studied in the field of artificial intelligence. Each type of agent has its own strengths and weaknesses, and the choice of agent depends on the specific problem at hand.
