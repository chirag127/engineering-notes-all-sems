## Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION

1. Introduction is the first chapter of any subject.
2. It provides an overview of the subject and its importance.
3. It sets the tone for the rest of the subject and helps the reader understand what to expect.
4. It is important to read the introduction carefully to get a good understanding of the subject.
5. The introduction may include the definition of key terms, the scope of the subject, and its relevance to the reader.
6. It may also provide a brief history of the subject and its development over time.
7. The introduction is an essential part of any subject and should be read carefully to gain a good understanding of the subject.
