### Future of Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a revolutionary field of computer science that is ready to become the main component of various emerging technologies like big data, robotics, and IoT. It will continue to act as a technological innovator in the coming years.

There are several key trends that shape the future of AI. AI systems so far relied on these for improvement: increased computing power, availability of more data, better algorithms, and better tools. In all 4 areas, there is potential for dramatic improvements though it is hard to put these against a timeline.

Currently, AI is known as weak AI due to its limitations. However, the future of artificial intelligence is about building strong AI. Right now, AI can beat humans in some specific tasks only, but in the future, it is expected of AI to beat a human in all cognitive tasks.

AI is also shaping the future of humanity across nearly every industry. It is already the main driver of emerging technologies like big data, robotics, and IoT — not to mention generative AI, with tools like ChatGPT and AI art generators garnering mainstream attention — and it will continue to act as a technological innovator in the coming years.

In summary, the future of AI is bright and full of potential. It is expected to continue to drive technological innovation and change the way we live and work.
