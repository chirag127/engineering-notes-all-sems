### Stochastic Games

Stochastic games are a type of game theory model that extends the concept of a normal-form game to include probabilistic transitions between states. In a stochastic game, the game transitions between different states based on the actions of the players and the outcome of random events.

Some key points to consider when studying stochastic games include:

1. Stochastic games are played over multiple stages, with the game transitioning between different states at each stage.
2. The transition probabilities between states are determined by the actions of the players and the outcome of random events.
3. Players must make decisions based on the current state of the game and their expectations about future states.
4. The goal of the players is to maximize their expected payoff over the course of the game.
5. Stochastic games can be used to model a wide range of real-world scenarios, including economic, political, and social interactions.

Stochastic games are an important topic in the study of artificial intelligence and problem-solving methods, as they provide a framework for modeling complex, multi-stage decision-making processes. By understanding the principles of stochastic games, students can develop a deeper understanding of how to design and analyze algorithms for solving complex problems.
