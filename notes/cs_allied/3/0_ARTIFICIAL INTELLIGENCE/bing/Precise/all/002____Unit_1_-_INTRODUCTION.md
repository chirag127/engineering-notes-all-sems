## Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION

1. Introduction is the first chapter or unit of any subject or topic.
2. It provides an overview of the subject or topic being studied.
3. It sets the foundation for the rest of the material to be covered.
4. It is important to read and understand the introduction before proceeding to the rest of the material.
5. The introduction may include definitions of key terms, a brief history of the subject, and an explanation of the scope and purpose of the material.
6. It is important to pay attention to the introduction as it provides the context for the rest of the material.
7. The introduction may also include learning objectives and expected outcomes for the unit or chapter.
8. It is a good idea to review the introduction periodically as you progress through the material to ensure that you have a solid understanding of the foundational concepts.
