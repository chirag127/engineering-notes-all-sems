### Events

1. An event is a change of state or an occurrence that happens at a specific time and place.
2. Events can be represented using various formalisms such as predicate logic, temporal logic, and event calculus.
3. In artificial intelligence, events are used to model dynamic systems and reason about their behavior over time.
4. Event calculus is a formalism for representing and reasoning about events and their effects.
5. It is based on first-order logic and allows for the representation of actions, events, and their effects on the world.
6. Event calculus can be used to solve problems such as planning, scheduling, and diagnosis.
7. Temporal logic is another formalism for representing and reasoning about events and their temporal relationships.
8. It extends propositional and predicate logic with temporal operators that allow for the representation of temporal relationships between events.
9. Temporal logic can be used to verify the correctness of systems, reason about their behavior, and specify their desired properties.
10. In summary, events are an important concept in artificial intelligence and can be represented and reasoned about using various formalisms such as event calculus and temporal logic.
