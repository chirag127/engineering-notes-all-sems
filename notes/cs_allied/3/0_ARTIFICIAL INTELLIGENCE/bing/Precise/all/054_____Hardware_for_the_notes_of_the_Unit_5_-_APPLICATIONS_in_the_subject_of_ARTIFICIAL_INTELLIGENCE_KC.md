# Unit 5 - APPLICATIONS: Hardware

Hardware refers to the physical components of a computer system that are used to store, process, and interact with data. In the context of artificial intelligence (AI), hardware plays a crucial role in enabling the development and deployment of AI systems.

Some key hardware components used in AI include:

1. **Processors**: These are the central processing units (CPUs) that perform the calculations and logical operations required by the AI system. Many AI applications require high-performance processors, such as graphics processing units (GPUs), to handle the large amounts of data and complex calculations involved.

2. **Memory**: This refers to the storage devices used to hold data and instructions for the AI system. Memory can be either volatile (such as RAM) or non-volatile (such as hard drives or solid-state drives). AI systems often require large amounts of memory to store and process data.

3. **Input/Output Devices**: These are the devices used to interact with the AI system, such as keyboards, mice, touchscreens, and microphones. They allow users to provide input to the system and receive output from it.

4. **Networking**: This refers to the hardware and protocols used to connect the AI system to other devices and networks. Networking is important for AI systems that need to access data from external sources or communicate with other systems.

5. **Sensors**: These are devices that detect and measure physical properties, such as temperature, light, or motion. Sensors are often used in AI systems that interact with the physical world, such as robotics or autonomous vehicles.

In summary, hardware plays a crucial role in enabling the development and deployment of AI systems. Key hardware components used in AI include processors, memory, input/output devices, networking, and sensors. These components work together to store, process, and interact with data, allowing AI systems to perform their intended functions.
