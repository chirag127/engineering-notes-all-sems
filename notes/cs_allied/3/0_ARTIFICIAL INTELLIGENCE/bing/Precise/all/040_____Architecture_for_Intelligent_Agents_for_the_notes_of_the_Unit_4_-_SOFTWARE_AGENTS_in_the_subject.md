# Architecture for Intelligent Agents

Unit 4 of the subject Artificial Intelligence KCS focuses on Software Agents. The architecture for Intelligent Agents is one of the main topics covered in this unit. 

Some of the key points to consider when studying the architecture for Intelligent Agents are:

1. **Agent communication** – This refers to the ways in which agents interact and exchange information with each other.
2. **Negotiation and Bargaining** – This involves agents engaging in discussions to reach mutually beneficial agreements.
3. **Argumentation among Agents** – This refers to the process of agents presenting and defending their viewpoints in a structured manner.
4. **Trust and Reputation in Multi-agent systems** – This involves agents assessing the reliability and credibility of other agents in the system.

There are several different types of architectures that can be implemented by intelligent agents, including reactive, deliberative, blackboard, and mobile architectures . Each type of architecture has its own unique characteristics and is suited to different types of tasks and environments.
