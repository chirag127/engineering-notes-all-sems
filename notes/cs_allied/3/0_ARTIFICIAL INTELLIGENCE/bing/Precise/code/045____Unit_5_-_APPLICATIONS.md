## Unit 5 - APPLICATIONS

1. Applications refer to the use of a particular technology or concept in real-world scenarios.
2. Applications can range from simple, everyday uses to complex, specialized systems.
3. Some common applications of technology include communication, transportation, entertainment, and healthcare.
4. In the field of communication, applications such as email, instant messaging, and social media have revolutionized the way people interact with each other.
5. In transportation, applications such as GPS navigation and ride-sharing services have made travel more convenient and efficient.
6. In entertainment, applications such as streaming services and online gaming have changed the way people consume media.
7. In healthcare, applications such as telemedicine and electronic health records have improved the delivery of medical services.
8. The development of new applications is an ongoing process, driven by advances in technology and changing needs of society.
