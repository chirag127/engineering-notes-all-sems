### Constraint Propagation

Constraint propagation is a technique used in artificial intelligence, specifically in the field of constraint satisfaction problems. It is a method of reducing the search space by applying constraints to the problem and propagating their effects.

Here are some key points to remember about constraint propagation:

1. Constraint propagation is used to reduce the search space by eliminating values that are not consistent with the constraints of the problem.
2. It is an iterative process that continues until no more values can be eliminated or until a solution is found.
3. Constraint propagation can be used in conjunction with other search techniques, such as backtracking, to improve the efficiency of the search.
4. There are several algorithms for constraint propagation, including arc consistency, path consistency, and i-consistency.
5. Constraint propagation can be applied to a wide range of problems, including scheduling, resource allocation, and puzzle solving.
