### Information Extraction

Information Extraction (IE) is one of the applications of Artificial Intelligence (AI) that is covered in Unit 5 of the KCS 071 course. It is a type of language processing that involves automatically extracting useful information from unstructured data sources.

Some of the other AI applications covered in Unit 5 of the KCS 071 course include Language Models, Information Retrieval, Natural Language Processing, Machine Translation, Speech Recognition, Robotics, Hardware, Perception, Planning, and Moving.

IE can be used to process documents faster and simplify operational procedures, resulting in fewer errors and improved efficiency. This is achieved through the use of innovative AI techniques.
