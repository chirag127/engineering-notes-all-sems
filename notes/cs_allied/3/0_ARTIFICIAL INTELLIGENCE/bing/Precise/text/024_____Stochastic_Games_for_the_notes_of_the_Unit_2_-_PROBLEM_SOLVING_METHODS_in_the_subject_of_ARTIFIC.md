### Stochastic Games

Stochastic games are a type of game in which the outcome is determined by both the players' actions and chance. These games are often used to model situations in which the outcome is uncertain, such as in economics, finance, and artificial intelligence.

Here are some key points to remember about stochastic games:

1. Stochastic games are a generalization of Markov decision processes (MDPs), which are used to model decision-making in uncertain environments.
2. In a stochastic game, the game transitions from one state to another based on the actions of the players and chance.
3. The payoffs for each player depend on the current state of the game and the actions taken by all players.
4. Stochastic games can be used to model a wide range of situations, including competition, cooperation, and conflict.
5. Solution concepts for stochastic games include Nash equilibrium and correlated equilibrium.
6. Stochastic games can be solved using dynamic programming, reinforcement learning, and other methods.

These are some of the key points to remember about stochastic games. They are an important tool for modeling and solving problems in artificial intelligence and other fields.
