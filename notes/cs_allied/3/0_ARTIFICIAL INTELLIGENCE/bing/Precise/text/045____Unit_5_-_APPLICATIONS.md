## Unit 5 - APPLICATIONS

1. Applications refer to the use of a particular technology or concept in a practical setting.
2. Applications can range from simple, everyday uses to complex, specialized systems.
3. Some common applications of technology include communication, transportation, entertainment, and healthcare.
4. Advances in technology have led to the development of new applications and the improvement of existing ones.
5. The study of applications is important as it helps to understand the practical implications and potential benefits of a particular technology or concept.
