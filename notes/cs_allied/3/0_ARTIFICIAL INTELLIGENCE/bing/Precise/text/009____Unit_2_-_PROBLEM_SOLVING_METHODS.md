## Unit 2 - PROBLEM SOLVING METHODS

1. **Understand the problem**: The first step in solving a problem is to understand it. This involves identifying the problem, defining it, and determining its scope and limitations.

2. **Gather information**: Once the problem is understood, the next step is to gather information about it. This may involve researching the problem, collecting data, and consulting with experts.

3. **Generate possible solutions**: After gathering information, the next step is to generate possible solutions to the problem. This may involve brainstorming, using creativity, and considering multiple perspectives.

4. **Evaluate and select a solution**: Once possible solutions have been generated, they must be evaluated to determine which one is the best. This may involve considering the pros and cons of each solution, as well as their feasibility and potential impact.

5. **Implement the solution**: After a solution has been selected, it must be implemented. This may involve developing a plan, assigning responsibilities, and taking action.

6. **Monitor and evaluate the results**: After the solution has been implemented, it is important to monitor and evaluate the results to determine if the problem has been solved. If the problem persists, the process may need to be repeated.
