### Speech Recognition

Speech recognition is a technology that can identify words and phrases in spoken language and convert them into readable text. It is driven by Artificial Intelligence .

Some key features of effective speech recognition include the use of AI and machine learning, the integration of grammar, syntax, structure, and composition of audio and voice signals to understand and process human speech .

Speech recognizers are made up of a few components, such as the speech input, feature extraction, feature vectors, a decoder, and a word output. The decoder leverages acoustic models, a pronunciation dictionary, and language models to determine the appropriate output .

There are many speech recognition applications and devices available, with some of the more advanced solutions using AI and machine learning . One popular example is Google Assistant, which is an AI-enabled and voice-powered virtual assistant software .
