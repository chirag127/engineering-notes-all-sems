### Typical Intelligent Agents

An intelligent agent is a system that can perceive its environment, reason about it, and take actions to achieve its goals. There are several types of intelligent agents, including:

1. **Simple reflex agents**: These agents select actions based on the current percept, ignoring the rest of the percept history. They are based on condition-action rules, where the condition part matches the current situation and the action part specifies the action to be taken.

2. **Model-based reflex agents**: These agents maintain an internal model of the world, which is used to choose actions. The model is updated based on the percept history, and the agent uses it to keep track of the part of the world that is not currently visible.

3. **Goal-based agents**: These agents have explicit goals, and their actions are chosen based on the desirability of their outcomes. They use a process called goal formulation to generate specific goals based on their high-level desires.

4. **Utility-based agents**: These agents have a utility function that measures how desirable a particular state is. They choose actions based on the expected utility of their outcomes, taking into account both the desirability of the outcomes and their likelihood.

5. **Learning agents**: These agents can improve their performance over time by learning from their experience. They use various learning algorithms to update their knowledge or their decision-making process based on the feedback they receive from the environment.

These are some of the typical intelligent agents that can be found in the field of artificial intelligence. Each type of agent has its strengths and weaknesses, and the choice of agent depends on the specific problem at hand.
