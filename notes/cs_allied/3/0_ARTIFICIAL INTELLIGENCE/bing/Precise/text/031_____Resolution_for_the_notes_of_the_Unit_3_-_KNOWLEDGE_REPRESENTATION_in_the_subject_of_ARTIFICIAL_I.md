### Resolution for the notes of the Unit 3 - KNOWLEDGE REPRESENTATION in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

1. Knowledge representation and reasoning (KR, KRR) is the part of Artificial Intelligence concerned with AI agents thinking and how thinking contributes to the intelligent behavior of agents.
2. It is responsible for representing information about the real world so that a computer can understand.
3. An AI system must be capable of storing the knowledge in a knowledge base (both static and dynamic KB), applying the knowledge stored to solve problems, and acquiring new knowledge through experience.
4. In order to solve the complex problems encountered in AI, one generally needs a large amount of knowledge, and suitable mechanisms for representing and manipulating all that knowledge.
5. Knowledge can take many forms, such as simple examples like "John has an umbrella" or "It is raining".
