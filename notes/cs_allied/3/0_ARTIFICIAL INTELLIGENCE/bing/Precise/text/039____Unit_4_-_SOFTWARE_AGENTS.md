## Unit 4 - SOFTWARE AGENTS

1. **Definition**: A software agent is a computer program that acts on behalf of a user or another program to perform a specific task or set of tasks.
2. **Types**: There are several types of software agents, including intelligent agents, autonomous agents, and multi-agent systems.
3. **Intelligent Agents**: Intelligent agents are capable of learning and adapting to their environment, making decisions based on their observations and knowledge.
4. **Autonomous Agents**: Autonomous agents are capable of operating independently, without the need for human intervention.
5. **Multi-Agent Systems**: Multi-agent systems are composed of multiple agents that interact with each other to achieve a common goal.
6. **Applications**: Software agents are used in a variety of applications, including e-commerce, gaming, and data mining.
7. **Benefits**: Software agents can help automate repetitive tasks, improve efficiency, and provide personalized services to users.
8. **Challenges**: Developing effective software agents requires advanced knowledge of artificial intelligence, machine learning, and computer science. There are also ethical and legal considerations when using software agents, such as ensuring that they operate within the bounds of the law and do not harm users or other agents.
