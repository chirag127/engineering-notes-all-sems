### Perception for the notes of the Unit 5 - APPLICATIONS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

Perception is the process of acquiring, interpreting, selecting, and organizing sensory information. It is a crucial component of artificial intelligence (AI) systems, particularly in the field of robotics, where perception is used to enable robots to interact with their environment.

1. Perception in AI involves the use of sensors to gather data from the environment. This data is then processed and interpreted to extract meaningful information.
2. Common sensors used in AI perception include cameras, microphones, and various types of environmental sensors such as temperature, humidity, and pressure sensors.
3. Perception is used in a wide range of AI applications, including object recognition, speech recognition, and natural language processing.
4. Perception is also used in the development of autonomous systems, such as self-driving cars and drones, where the ability to perceive and understand the environment is essential for safe and effective operation.
5. Perception is an active area of research in AI, with ongoing efforts to improve the accuracy and reliability of perception systems.
