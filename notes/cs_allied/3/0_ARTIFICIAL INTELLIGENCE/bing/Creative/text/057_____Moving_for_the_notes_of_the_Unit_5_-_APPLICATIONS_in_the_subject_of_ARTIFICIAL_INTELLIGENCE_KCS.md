### Moving

- Moving is the process of changing the position or location of an object or agent in a physical or virtual environment.
- Moving is one of the basic tasks that artificial intelligence (AI) can perform to interact with the world and achieve goals.
- Moving can be classified into two types: navigation and manipulation.

#### Navigation

- Navigation is the ability of an agent to plan and execute a path from one point to another in a given environment, avoiding obstacles and optimizing some criteria (such as distance, time, energy, etc.).
- Navigation can be further divided into two subtypes: global and local.
- Global navigation is the process of finding a complete path from the start to the goal, using a map or a model of the environment.
- Local navigation is the process of following the global path, using sensors and actuators to perceive and react to the environment.
- Some of the applications of navigation in AI are:

  - Autonomous vehicles: AI can enable vehicles to drive themselves without human intervention, using cameras, radars, lidars, GPS, and other sensors to perceive the road, traffic, pedestrians, and other objects, and using actuators to control the steering, acceleration, and braking. AI can also use machine learning and deep learning to learn from data and improve the performance and safety of autonomous vehicles.
  - Robotics: AI can enable robots to move and operate in various domains, such as manufacturing, agriculture, health care, education, entertainment, and military. Robots can use sensors and actuators to perceive and manipulate the environment, and use planning and reasoning to achieve goals and tasks. Robots can also use machine learning and deep learning to learn from data and adapt to new situations and challenges.
  - Virtual reality and augmented reality: AI can enable immersive and interactive experiences in virtual and augmented reality, using computer vision, natural language processing, speech recognition, and gesture recognition to create realistic and responsive environments and characters. AI can also use machine learning and deep learning to generate and customize content and scenarios based on user preferences and feedback.

#### Manipulation

- Manipulation is the ability of an agent to change the shape, position, orientation, or state of an object or a part of the environment, using physical or virtual forces or actions.
- Manipulation can be further divided into two subtypes: grasping and manipulation planning.
- Grasping is the process of acquiring and maintaining a stable and secure contact between the agent and the object, using one or more effectors (such as fingers, claws, suction cups, magnets, etc.).
- Manipulation planning is the process of finding a sequence of actions or motions that can achieve a desired configuration or effect on the object or the environment, using a model or a representation of the object, the agent, and the constraints.
- Some of the applications of manipulation in AI are:

  - Industrial automation: AI can enable machines and robots to perform various tasks that involve manipulating objects, such as assembly, welding, painting, packaging, sorting, etc. AI can use computer vision, force and tactile sensing, and machine learning and deep learning to perceive and control the objects and the environment, and to optimize the quality and efficiency of the tasks.
  - Service robots: AI can enable robots to assist humans in various domains, such as health care, education, entertainment, and domestic. Robots can use computer vision, natural language processing, speech recognition, and gesture recognition to communicate and interact with humans, and use sensors and actuators to manipulate objects and perform tasks. Robots can also use machine learning and deep learning to learn from data and personalize their services and behaviors.
  - Computer graphics and animation: AI can enable realistic and expressive manipulation of images, videos, and 3D models, using computer vision, natural language processing, speech synthesis, and gesture synthesis. AI can also use machine learning and deep learning to generate and edit content and effects based on user inputs and feedback.
