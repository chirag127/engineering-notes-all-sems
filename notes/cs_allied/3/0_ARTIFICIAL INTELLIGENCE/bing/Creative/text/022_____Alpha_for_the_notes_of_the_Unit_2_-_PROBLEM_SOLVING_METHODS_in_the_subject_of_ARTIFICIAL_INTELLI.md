### Alpha

- Alpha is a term used to refer to a family of artificial intelligence systems developed by DeepMind, a subsidiary of Alphabet, the parent company of Google.
- Alpha systems use deep learning and reinforcement learning techniques to learn from data and improve their performance on various tasks, such as playing games, solving problems, and predicting protein structures.
- Some of the notable Alpha systems are:
  - AlphaGo: The first computer program to defeat a human professional Go player, and later the world champion, Lee Sedol, in 2016.
  - AlphaZero: A general game-playing system that can learn to master any two-player board game, such as chess, shogi, and Go, without any human knowledge or data, by playing against itself.
  - AlphaFold: A system that can predict the three-dimensional structure of proteins from their amino acid sequences, achieving unprecedented accuracy and speed, and solving a 50-year-old grand challenge in biology.
  - AlphaCode: A system that can generate and execute code for solving programming problems, by learning from a large corpus of existing code and natural language descriptions.
- Alpha systems demonstrate the potential of artificial intelligence for solving complex and challenging problems that require critical thinking, creativity, and generalization. They also pose ethical and social questions about the impact and implications of such systems for human society and well-being.
