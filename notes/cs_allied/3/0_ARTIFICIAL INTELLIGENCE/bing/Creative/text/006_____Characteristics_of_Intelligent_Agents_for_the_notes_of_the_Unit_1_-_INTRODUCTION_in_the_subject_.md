### Characteristics of Intelligent Agents

- An intelligent agent is a computer software system that can perceive its environment, act rationally, and learn from its experience  .
- Intelligent agents have the following distinguishing characteristics  :
  - **Autonomy**: They can perform certain tasks on their own, without direct human intervention or supervision.
  - **Adaptivity**: They can learn from their own actions and feedback, and improve their performance over time.
  - **Interactivity**: They can communicate and cooperate with other agents, humans, and systems, using various modes and protocols.
  - **Incrementality**: They can accommodate new rules and knowledge incrementally, without requiring a complete overhaul of their existing structure or behavior.
  - **Goal-orientation**: They can pursue specific objectives or outcomes, based on their preferences and beliefs.
  - **Knowledge-based**: They can store and manipulate information in various forms, such as facts, rules, models, and heuristics.
- Intelligent agents can be used for various applications in artificial intelligence, such as :
  - **Information access and navigation**: They can help users find, filter, and organize relevant information from large and complex data sources, such as the web, databases, or social media.
  - **Decision support and empowerment**: They can assist users in making informed and rational decisions, by providing suggestions, explanations, and feedback.
  - **Repetitive and tedious tasks**: They can automate and optimize tasks that are boring, time-consuming, or error-prone, such as scheduling, booking, or ordering.
  - **Personalization and recommendation**: They can tailor their services and products to the individual needs and preferences of users, by learning from their behavior and feedback.
  - **Entertainment and education**: They can provide fun and engaging experiences for users, such as games, simulations, or tutorials.
