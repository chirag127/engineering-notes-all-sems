### Information Extraction

- Information extraction (IE) is the task of automatically extracting structured information from unstructured and/or semi-structured machine-readable documents and other electronically represented sources.
- Information extraction can enable finding entities (such as names, dates, locations, etc.) as well as classifying and storing them in a database.
- Information extraction can also involve identifying relations, events, sentiments, opinions, and other types of information from text.
- Information extraction can be applied to various domains, such as news, social media, scientific literature, medical records, legal documents, etc.
- Information extraction can support various applications, such as search engines, question answering, knowledge graphs, text summarization, text analytics, etc.
- Information extraction can be divided into several subtasks, such as named entity recognition, entity linking, relation extraction, event extraction, coreference resolution, etc.
- Information extraction can use various techniques, such as rule-based methods, machine learning methods, deep learning methods, etc.
- Information extraction can face various challenges, such as ambiguity, noise, inconsistency, incompleteness, scalability, etc.
