### Events

- Events are the occurrence of things in the real world  .
- Events are actions that involve changes in the state of the world.
- Events can be described by their triggers, participants, objects, locations, and times.
- Events can be represented by logical formulas, frames, scripts, or event calculus.
- Events can be used to reason about causality, temporality, and counterfactuals.
