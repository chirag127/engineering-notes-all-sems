# Information Retrieval for the notes of the Unit 5 - APPLICATIONS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

- Information retrieval (IR) is the process of finding and organizing information that is relevant to a user's query from a large collection of documents, such as the web.
- Artificial intelligence (AI) is the field of computer science that aims to create machines and systems that can perform tasks that normally require human intelligence, such as reasoning, learning, planning, natural language processing, computer vision, etc.
- AI can be applied to IR to improve the effectiveness and efficiency of information access systems, such as search engines, recommender systems, question answering systems, etc.
- Some of the AI techniques that can be used for IR are:
  - Machine learning: the process of learning from data and making predictions or decisions based on the learned patterns or models. Machine learning can be used for various IR tasks, such as document indexing, query analysis, ranking, relevance feedback, etc.
  - Natural language processing: the process of analyzing, understanding, and generating natural language, such as text or speech. Natural language processing can be used for various IR tasks, such as query expansion, document summarization, sentiment analysis, entity extraction, etc.
  - Computer vision: the process of analyzing, understanding, and generating visual information, such as images or videos. Computer vision can be used for various IR tasks, such as image retrieval, face recognition, object detection, etc.
  - Knowledge representation and reasoning: the process of representing and manipulating knowledge using formal languages and logic. Knowledge representation and reasoning can be used for various IR tasks, such as semantic search, knowledge graph construction, inference, etc.
- Some of the applications of AI-based IR are:
  - Conversational search: a type of search that allows users to interact with information access systems using natural language dialogues, such as voice or text. Conversational search can provide more natural and personalized information access experiences for users, as well as enable complex and multi-turn queries. Examples of conversational search systems are Microsoft’s Bing/ChatGPT, Google/Bard and Meta/LLaMA.
  - Recommender systems: a type of information access system that suggests items or information that are likely to be of interest to a user, based on the user's preferences, behavior, or context. Recommender systems can help users discover new or relevant information, as well as increase user satisfaction and loyalty. Examples of recommender systems are Amazon, Netflix, Spotify, etc.
  - Question answering systems: a type of information access system that provides direct and concise answers to natural language questions, rather than a list of documents or links. Question answering systems can help users obtain specific and factual information, as well as reduce the cognitive load and effort of users. Examples of question answering systems are IBM Watson, Google Assistant, Siri, etc.
