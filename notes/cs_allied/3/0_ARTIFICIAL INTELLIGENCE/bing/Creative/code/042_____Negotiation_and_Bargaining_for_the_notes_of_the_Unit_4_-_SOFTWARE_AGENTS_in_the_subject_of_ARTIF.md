### Negotiation and Bargaining for the notes of the Unit 4 - SOFTWARE AGENTS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

- Negotiation and bargaining are processes of reaching an agreement between two or more parties that have conflicting interests or preferences.
- Negotiation and bargaining can be applied to various domains, such as business, politics, diplomacy, law, and personal relationships.
- Negotiation and bargaining can involve human-human, human-agent, or agent-agent interactions, where agents are software entities that can act autonomously and intelligently on behalf of their users or goals.
- Negotiation and bargaining can be classified into different types, such as distributive, integrative, cooperative, competitive, single-issue, multi-issue, one-shot, repeated, bilateral, or multilateral.
- Negotiation and bargaining can be influenced by various factors, such as the negotiation protocol, the negotiation strategy, the negotiation tactics, the negotiation outcome, the negotiation context, and the negotiation behavior.
- Negotiation and bargaining can benefit from the use of artificial intelligence (AI) techniques, such as machine learning, natural language processing, game theory, decision theory, and multi-agent systems  .
- AI can help negotiators and bargainers in various ways, such as providing information, advice, analysis, feedback, support, representation, automation, and optimization  .
- AI can also pose some challenges and risks for negotiation and bargaining, such as ethical, legal, social, and psychological issues, such as trust, fairness, transparency, accountability, and privacy .
- AI can also create new opportunities and scenarios for negotiation and bargaining, such as cross-cultural, cross-domain, and cross-modal negotiations, as well as negotiations with multiple and heterogeneous agents  .
