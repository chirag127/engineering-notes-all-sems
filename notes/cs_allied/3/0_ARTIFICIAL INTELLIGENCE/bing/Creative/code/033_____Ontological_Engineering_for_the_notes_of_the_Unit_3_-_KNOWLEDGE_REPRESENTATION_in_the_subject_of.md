### Ontological Engineering

- Ontological Engineering is a field that studies the methods and methodologies for building ontologies, which are formal and explicit representations of the concepts, properties and relations in a domain of interest .
- Ontologies can be used to enhance the semantic interoperability, reusability and integration of information and knowledge across different domains and applications.
- Ontological Engineering involves a set of tasks related to the ontology development process, such as:
  - Ontology specification: defining the purpose, scope and requirements of the ontology
  - Ontology conceptualization: identifying and organizing the relevant concepts, properties and relations in the domain
  - Ontology formalization: encoding the ontology in a formal language, such as OWL, RDF, or UML
  - Ontology implementation: implementing the ontology in a software system or a knowledge base
  - Ontology evaluation: assessing the quality, validity and usability of the ontology
  - Ontology documentation: documenting the ontology design, structure and semantics
  - Ontology maintenance: updating and evolving the ontology over time
- Ontological Engineering also involves the ontology life cycle, which describes the stages and activities of ontology development and use, such as:
  - Ontology reuse: reusing existing ontologies or ontology components
  - Ontology alignment: finding correspondences or mappings between different ontologies
  - Ontology merging: combining two or more ontologies into a new one
  - Ontology modularization: decomposing an ontology into smaller and independent modules
  - Ontology evolution: managing the changes and versions of an ontology
  - Ontology learning: extracting ontological knowledge from natural language texts or other sources
- Ontological Engineering requires the use of tools and languages that support the ontology development and use, such as:
  - Ontology editors: graphical or textual tools for creating and editing ontologies
  - Ontology reasoners: software systems that can infer new knowledge from ontologies
  - Ontology query languages: languages for querying and retrieving information from ontologies
  - Ontology visualization tools: tools for displaying and exploring ontologies
  - Ontology libraries and repositories: online collections of ontologies and ontology-related resources
