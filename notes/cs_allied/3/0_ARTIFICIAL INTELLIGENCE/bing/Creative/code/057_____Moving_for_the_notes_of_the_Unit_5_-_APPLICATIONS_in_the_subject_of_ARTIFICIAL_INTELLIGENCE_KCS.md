# Moving for the notes of the Unit 5 - APPLICATIONS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

- Artificial intelligence (AI) is a machine, program or system's ability to learn and adapt by experience.
- AI is a general purpose technology that has a multitude of applications in various industries and domains.
- Some of the common applications of AI are:

  - Language translation: AI can help translate documents, speech, text and images from one language to another faster and more accurately than humans . For example, Google Translate, Microsoft Translator and Amazon Translate are some of the popular AI-based translation services.
  - Image recognition: AI can help identify, classify, segment and annotate images based on their content and context . For example, Facebook, Instagram and Snapchat use AI to recognize faces, objects and scenes in photos and videos.
  - Credit scoring: AI can help assess the creditworthiness of borrowers and predict the likelihood of default based on their financial and behavioral data . For example, FICO, Experian and Equifax use AI to generate credit scores and reports for individuals and businesses.
  - E-commerce: AI can help optimize the online shopping experience by providing personalized recommendations, dynamic pricing, fraud detection and customer service . For example, Amazon, Alibaba and Netflix use AI to suggest products, movies and shows based on the user's preferences and behavior.
  - Cybersecurity: AI can help protect networks, devices and data from cyberattacks by detecting and preventing intrusions, malware, phishing and ransomware . For example, Cisco, Symantec and McAfee use AI to enhance their network and endpoint security solutions.
  - Chatbots: AI can help simulate natural and engaging conversations with humans using natural language processing and generation . For example, Siri, Alexa and Cortana are some of the popular AI-based virtual assistants that can answer questions, perform tasks and provide information.
  - Agriculture: AI can help improve crop yield, quality and sustainability by monitoring soil, weather, pests and diseases, and providing smart irrigation, fertilization and harvesting solutions. For example, John Deere, Bayer and IBM use AI to enhance their agricultural products and services.
