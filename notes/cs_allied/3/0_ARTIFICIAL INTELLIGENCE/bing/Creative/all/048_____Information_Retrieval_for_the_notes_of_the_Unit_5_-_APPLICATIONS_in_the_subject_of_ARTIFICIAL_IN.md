- Information retrieval (IR) is the process of finding and accessing relevant information from a large collection of documents, such as web pages, books, articles, etc.
- IR systems can benefit from the application of artificial intelligence (AI) techniques to account for the intrinsic uncertainty and imprecision that characterize the subjectivity of this task
- AI methods are employed throughout the standard IR process and for novel value added services
- Some of the applications of AI in IR are:

  - Pattern recognition: the ability to identify and classify patterns in data, such as text, images, audio, video, etc. Pattern recognition can be used for tasks such as document clustering, document classification, image retrieval, speech recognition, etc. 
  - Representation: the way of encoding and organizing the information to be retrieved, such as using keywords, vectors, graphs, ontologies, etc. Representation can affect the efficiency and effectiveness of the IR system, and can be enhanced by using AI techniques such as natural language processing, semantic web, neural networks, etc. 
  - Problem solving and planning: the ability to formulate and execute a sequence of actions to achieve a goal, such as answering a query, summarizing a document, generating a recommendation, etc. Problem solving and planning can be improved by using AI techniques such as logic, search, optimization, inference, etc. 
  - Heuristics: the rules of thumb or shortcuts that guide the decision making process, such as ranking, relevance feedback, query expansion, etc. Heuristics can help to overcome the limitations of the IR system, such as incompleteness, ambiguity, inconsistency, etc. Heuristics can be derived from AI techniques such as machine learning, data mining, statistics, etc. 
  - Learning: the ability to acquire and update knowledge from data and feedback, such as learning user preferences, document features, query patterns, etc. Learning can help to adapt the IR system to the changing needs and expectations of the users and the environment. Learning can be achieved by using AI techniques such as supervised, unsupervised, semi-supervised, reinforcement, etc. 

- Some of the examples of IR systems that use AI are:

  - Google: a web search engine that uses AI techniques such as natural language processing, machine learning, neural networks, etc. to provide relevant and personalized results for the users
  - Watson: a question answering system that uses AI techniques such as natural language processing, knowledge representation, inference, etc. to answer complex and open-ended questions from various domains
  - Netflix: a recommender system that uses AI techniques such as machine learning, data mining, statistics, etc. to provide personalized and tailored suggestions for the users based on their preferences and behavior
  - Siri: a voice assistant that uses AI techniques such as speech recognition, natural language processing, machine learning, etc. to understand and respond to the users' requests and commands
  - Spotify: a music streaming service that uses AI techniques such as machine learning, data mining, statistics, etc. to provide personalized and customized playlists and recommendations for the users based on their tastes and moods
