# Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION

## Definition of Artificial Intelligence

- Artificial intelligence (AI) is the **simulation of human intelligence in machines** that are programmed to **learn, reason, and perceive** .
- AI is also the **ability of a digital computer or computer-controlled robot** to perform tasks **commonly associated with intelligent beings** .
- AI is the **capability of a machine to imitate intelligent human behavior**.
- AI can be classified into **different types** based on the **level of intelligence** and the **scope of application**.
- Some examples of AI types are **weak AI, strong AI, narrow AI, general AI, super AI, artificial neural networks, expert systems, natural language processing, computer vision, machine learning, deep learning, etc.** .
- AI is being used across **different industries** including **finance, healthcare, education, entertainment, security, transportation, etc.** .
- Some examples of AI applications are **speech recognition, face recognition, chatbots, self-driving cars, recommendation systems, fraud detection, diagnosis, gaming, etc.** .
- AI has **advantages** such as **increasing productivity, efficiency, accuracy, innovation, creativity, etc.** .
- AI also has **challenges** such as **ethical, social, legal, technical, security, etc.** .
