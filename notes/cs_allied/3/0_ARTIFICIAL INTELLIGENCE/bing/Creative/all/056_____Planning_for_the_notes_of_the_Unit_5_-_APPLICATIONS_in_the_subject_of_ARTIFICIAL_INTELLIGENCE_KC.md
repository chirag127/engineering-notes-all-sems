## Expert systems

- An expert system is a computer program that uses knowledge and inference rules to solve problems that normally require human expertise.
- An expert system consists of three components: a knowledge base, an inference engine, and a user interface.
- The knowledge base contains facts and rules about a specific domain of knowledge, such as medicine, law, or engineering.
- The inference engine applies the rules to the facts in the knowledge base to derive new facts or conclusions.
- The user interface allows the user to interact with the expert system, ask questions, provide inputs, and receive outputs.
- Some examples of expert systems are MYCIN (a medical diagnosis system), DENDRAL (a chemical analysis system), and PROSPECTOR (a mineral exploration system).

## Natural language processing

- Natural language processing (NLP) is the branch of artificial intelligence that deals with the understanding and generation of natural languages, such as English, Chinese, or Hindi.
- NLP involves various tasks, such as speech recognition, speech synthesis, text analysis, text generation, machine translation, information extraction, question answering, sentiment analysis, and dialogue systems.
- NLP uses various techniques, such as parsing, semantic analysis, discourse analysis, pragmatics, lexicon, grammar, and ontology, to process natural language data.
- Some examples of NLP applications are Siri (a voice assistant), Google Translate (a machine translation system), and GPT-3 (a text generation system).

## Speech recognition and synthesis

- Speech recognition is the process of converting spoken words into text or commands that can be understood by a computer.
- Speech synthesis is the process of converting text or commands into spoken words that can be understood by a human.
- Speech recognition and synthesis are important components of natural language processing and human-computer interaction.
- Speech recognition and synthesis use various techniques, such as acoustic modeling, language modeling, speech segmentation, speech feature extraction, speech synthesis, and speech evaluation, to process speech data.
- Some examples of speech recognition and synthesis applications are Alexa (a voice assistant), Dragon NaturallySpeaking (a speech-to-text software), and Text-to-Speech (a speech synthesis software).

## Computer vision

- Computer vision is the branch of artificial intelligence that deals with the understanding and manipulation of visual data, such as images and videos.
- Computer vision involves various tasks, such as face recognition, object detection, scene understanding, optical character recognition, image segmentation, image enhancement, image synthesis, and video analysis.
- Computer vision uses various techniques, such as image processing, pattern recognition, feature extraction, machine learning, deep learning, and computer graphics, to process visual data.
- Some examples of computer vision applications are Face ID (a face recognition system), Google Lens (an image recognition system), and DeepFake (a video synthesis system).

## Robotics

- Robotics is the branch of artificial intelligence that deals with the design, construction, operation, and control of machines that can perform tasks that normally require human intelligence, skill, or physical ability.
- Robotics involves various aspects, such as mechanical engineering, electrical engineering, computer science, and cognitive science, to create robots that can interact with the environment and other agents.
- Robotics uses various techniques, such as sensors, actuators, controllers, algorithms, and artificial neural networks, to enable robots to perceive, plan, learn, and act.
- Some examples of robotics applications are Roomba (a vacuum cleaning robot), ASIMO (a humanoid robot), and Boston Dynamics (a robotics company).

## Machine learning

- Machine learning is the branch of artificial intelligence that deals with the creation and study of algorithms and models that can learn from data and improve their performance without explicit programming.
- Machine learning involves various tasks, such as classification, regression, clustering, dimensionality reduction, anomaly detection, and reinforcement learning.
- Machine learning uses various techniques, such as supervised learning, unsupervised learning, semi-supervised learning, and self-supervised learning, to train and evaluate machine learning models.
- Some examples of machine learning applications are spam filtering, face detection, recommender systems, and self-driving cars.
