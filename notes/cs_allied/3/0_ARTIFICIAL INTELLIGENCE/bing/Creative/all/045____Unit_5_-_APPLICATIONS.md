## Unit 5 - APPLICATIONS

- In this unit, we will learn about some of the applications of artificial intelligence in various domains and industries.
- We will explore how AI can be used to solve problems, enhance productivity, improve customer experience, and create new opportunities.
- We will also discuss some of the challenges and limitations of AI, as well as the ethical and social implications of its use.
- The topics covered in this unit are:

  - AI in Healthcare: How AI can assist in diagnosis, treatment, prevention, and research of diseases and medical conditions.
  - AI in Education: How AI can support learning, teaching, assessment, and administration in educational settings.
  - AI in Business: How AI can optimize business processes, decision making, marketing, and customer service in various sectors and industries.
  - AI in Entertainment: How AI can create and enhance content, such as music, art, games, and movies, and provide personalized and interactive experiences for users.
  - AI in Social Good: How AI can contribute to social and environmental causes, such as disaster relief, poverty alleviation, human rights, and sustainability.
