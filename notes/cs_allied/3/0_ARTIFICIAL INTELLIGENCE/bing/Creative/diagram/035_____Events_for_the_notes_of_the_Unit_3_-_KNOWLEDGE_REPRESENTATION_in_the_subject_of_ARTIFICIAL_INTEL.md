### Events

- Events are one of the types of knowledge representation in artificial intelligence that capture information about what happens in the world and how it affects the state of affairs.
- Events can be represented as tuples of attributes, such as `(agent, action, patient, time, location, manner, cause, effect, etc.)`, where each attribute can have a value or a variable.
- Events can be organized into scripts, which are sequences of events that describe typical scenarios or situations, such as going to a restaurant or taking a flight.
- Events can also be linked by causal, temporal, or logical relations, such as `before`, `after`, `because`, `if`, `then`, etc., to form complex event structures.
- Events can be learned from text corpora using natural language processing techniques, such as event extraction, event coreference, and event prediction.
- Events can be enhanced with external commonsense knowledge, such as the intents and emotions of the event participants, the preconditions and postconditions of the event, and the general world knowledge about the event domain.
- Events can be used for various applications in artificial intelligence, such as question answering, dialogue systems, story generation, planning, and reasoning.
