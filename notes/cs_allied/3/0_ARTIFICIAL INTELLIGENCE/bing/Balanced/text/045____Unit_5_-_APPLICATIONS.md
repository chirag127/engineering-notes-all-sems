## Unit 5 - APPLICATIONS

- In this unit, you will learn about some of the applications of artificial intelligence in various domains, such as health, education, entertainment, and business.
- You will also learn about some of the ethical, social, and legal implications of artificial intelligence, such as privacy, bias, accountability, and human dignity.
- The objectives of this unit are to:
  - Understand how artificial intelligence can be used to solve real-world problems and enhance human capabilities.
  - Identify some of the benefits and challenges of artificial intelligence in different sectors and contexts.
  - Evaluate some of the ethical, social, and legal issues raised by artificial intelligence and its impact on society and individuals.
- The topics covered in this unit are:
  - Artificial intelligence in health: how artificial intelligence can assist in diagnosis, treatment, prevention, and research of diseases and disorders.
  - Artificial intelligence in education: how artificial intelligence can support learning, teaching, assessment, and personalization of education.
  - Artificial intelligence in entertainment: how artificial intelligence can create and enhance various forms of entertainment, such as games, music, movies, and art.
  - Artificial intelligence in business: how artificial intelligence can improve productivity, efficiency, innovation, and decision making in various business domains, such as finance, marketing, and management.
  - Ethical, social, and legal implications of artificial intelligence: how artificial intelligence can affect human rights, values, norms, and laws, and what are some of the possible solutions and guidelines to ensure ethical and responsible use of artificial intelligence.
