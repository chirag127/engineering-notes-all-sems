# Negotiation and Bargaining

- Negotiation is a process of reaching an agreement between two or more parties that have conflicting interests or preferences .
- Bargaining is a subset of negotiation that focuses on the exchange of offers and counteroffers to determine the terms of the agreement.
- Negotiation and bargaining can be applied to various domains, such as business, politics, law, diplomacy, and personal relationships .
- Negotiation and bargaining can involve human-human, human-agent, or agent-agent interactions, where agents are software programs that can represent and act on behalf of humans or organizations  .
- Negotiation and bargaining can be classified into different types, such as distributive, integrative, cooperative, competitive, single-issue, multi-issue, one-shot, repeated, bilateral, or multilateral .
- Negotiation and bargaining can be influenced by various factors, such as the negotiators' preferences, beliefs, strategies, emotions, communication styles, cultural backgrounds, and ethical values .
- Negotiation and bargaining can have various outcomes, such as win-win, win-lose, lose-lose, or no agreement .
- Negotiation and bargaining can be supported or automated by artificial intelligence (AI) techniques, such as game theory, decision theory, machine learning, natural language processing, and computer vision  .
- AI can help human negotiators by providing information, advice, feedback, or suggestions, or by acting as mediators, representatives, or opponents  .
- AI can also negotiate with other AI agents to coordinate actions, allocate resources, or resolve conflicts  .
- AI can improve the efficiency, effectiveness, fairness, and transparency of negotiation and bargaining processes, but also pose challenges and risks, such as ethical, legal, social, and psychological implications  .
