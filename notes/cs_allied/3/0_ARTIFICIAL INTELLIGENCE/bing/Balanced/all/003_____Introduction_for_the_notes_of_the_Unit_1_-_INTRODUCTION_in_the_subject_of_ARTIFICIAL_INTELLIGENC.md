# Introduction

- Artificial intelligence (AI) is the branch of computer science that deals with creating machines or software that can perform tasks that normally require human intelligence, such as reasoning, learning, planning, decision making, natural language processing, computer vision, speech recognition, etc.
- AI can be classified into two main categories: weak AI and strong AI. Weak AI, also known as narrow AI, is the type of AI that is designed to perform a specific task or domain, such as playing chess, driving a car, or diagnosing diseases. Strong AI, also known as general AI or artificial general intelligence (AGI), is the type of AI that can perform any intellectual task that a human can do, such as understanding natural language, solving complex problems, or creating original art. Weak AI is currently more prevalent and achievable than strong AI, which is still a hypothetical and elusive goal.
- AI can also be classified into three main levels: artificial narrow intelligence (ANI), artificial general intelligence (AGI), and artificial superintelligence (ASI). ANI is the level of AI that can perform a specific task or domain better than humans, such as playing chess, driving a car, or diagnosing diseases. AGI is the level of AI that can perform any intellectual task that a human can do, such as understanding natural language, solving complex problems, or creating original art. ASI is the level of AI that can perform any intellectual task better than humans, including creating and improving itself. ANI is currently the most common and achievable level of AI, while AGI and ASI are still hypothetical and elusive goals.
- AI can also be classified into four main types: reactive machines, limited memory, theory of mind, and self-awareness. Reactive machines are the simplest type of AI that can only react to the current situation and do not have any memory or learning capabilities, such as Deep Blue, the chess-playing computer that defeated Garry Kasparov in 1997. Limited memory are the type of AI that can store some data from the past and use it to improve their performance, such as self-driving cars, facial recognition systems, or chatbots. Theory of mind are the type of AI that can understand the mental states, emotions, beliefs, and intentions of other agents, such as humans or animals, and interact with them accordingly, such as social robots, virtual assistants, or empathetic machines. Self-awareness are the type of AI that can have a sense of self, consciousness, and self-improvement, such as HAL 9000, the fictional AI from 2001: A Space Odyssey, or Sophia, the humanoid robot that was granted citizenship by Saudi Arabia in 2017.
- AI can also be classified into two main approaches: symbolic AI and sub-symbolic AI. Symbolic AI, also known as classical AI or rule-based AI, is the approach that uses symbols, rules, logic, and knowledge bases to represent and manipulate information, such as expert systems, natural language processing, or automated theorem proving. Sub-symbolic AI, also known as connectionist AI or neural network-based AI, is the approach that uses numerical values, matrices, vectors, and tensors to represent and manipulate information, such as deep learning, computer vision, or speech recognition. Symbolic AI is more suitable for tasks that require explicit and structured reasoning, while sub-symbolic AI is more suitable for tasks that require implicit and unstructured learning.
