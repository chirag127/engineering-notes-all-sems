## Unit 5 - APPLICATIONS

This unit covers some of the applications of artificial intelligence in various domains, such as:

- Natural language processing: the ability of computers to understand and generate natural language, such as speech and text. Some of the tasks in this domain are:

  - Speech recognition: converting spoken words into text or commands.
  - Speech synthesis: converting text or commands into spoken words.
  - Natural language understanding: extracting meaning and intent from natural language input.
  - Natural language generation: producing natural language output from structured or unstructured data.
  - Machine translation: translating text or speech from one language to another.
  - Question answering: answering natural language questions based on a given knowledge base or document.
  - Text summarization: producing a concise summary of a longer text.
  - Sentiment analysis: detecting the emotional tone or attitude of a text or speech.
  - Chatbots: conversational agents that can interact with humans using natural language.

- Computer vision: the ability of computers to perceive and understand visual information, such as images and videos. Some of the tasks in this domain are:

  - Image classification: assigning a label or category to an image based on its content.
  - Object detection: locating and identifying objects in an image or video.
  - Face recognition: identifying or verifying the identity of a person based on their face.
  - Scene understanding: inferring the context and semantics of a scene from an image or video.
  - Image generation: creating realistic or stylized images from scratch or based on some input.
  - Image segmentation: dividing an image into regions based on their pixels or features.
  - Image captioning: generating a natural language description of an image or video.
  - Optical character recognition: recognizing and extracting text from images or videos.

- Robotics: the branch of engineering and science that deals with the design, construction, operation, and control of machines that can perform tasks that require physical movement or manipulation. Some of the tasks in this domain are:

  - Locomotion: enabling robots to move in different environments and terrains, such as walking, crawling, flying, swimming, etc.
  - Manipulation: enabling robots to grasp and manipulate objects of various shapes, sizes, and materials, such as picking, placing, sorting, etc.
  - Navigation: enabling robots to plan and execute paths from one location to another, while avoiding obstacles and optimizing performance, such as mapping, localization, path planning, etc.
  - Perception: enabling robots to sense and interpret their surroundings, such as vision, sound, touch, etc.
  - Communication: enabling robots to communicate with other robots or humans, using natural language or other modalities, such as speech, gestures, facial expressions, etc.
  - Learning: enabling robots to acquire new skills or improve existing ones, based on feedback, experience, or demonstration, such as reinforcement learning, imitation learning, etc.
  - Cooperation: enabling robots to coordinate and collaborate with other robots or humans, to achieve a common goal, such as multi-agent systems, swarm robotics, human-robot interaction, etc.
