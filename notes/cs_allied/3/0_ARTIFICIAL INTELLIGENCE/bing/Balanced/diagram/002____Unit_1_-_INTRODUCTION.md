## Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION

- This unit introduces the basic concepts and principles of artificial intelligence (AI).
- AI is the study of how to create machines and software that can perform tasks that normally require human intelligence, such as reasoning, learning, planning, decision making, natural language processing, computer vision, etc.
- AI can be classified into different types, such as weak AI, strong AI, narrow AI, general AI, etc., depending on the scope and level of intelligence exhibited by the system.
- AI can also be categorized into different approaches, such as symbolic AI, connectionist AI, evolutionary AI, hybrid AI, etc., depending on the methods and techniques used to implement the system.
- AI has various applications and domains, such as expert systems, natural language processing, computer vision, robotics, games, etc., where it can provide solutions to complex and challenging problems.
- AI has various benefits and challenges, such as enhancing human capabilities, improving efficiency and productivity, creating new opportunities and innovations, etc., as well as raising ethical, social, and legal issues, such as privacy, security, accountability, bias, etc.
