### Definition for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

- Artificial intelligence (AI) is the field of computer science that studies and develops machines and systems that can perform tasks that normally require human intelligence, such as learning, reasoning, and perception .
- AI can be classified into two main types: weak AI and strong AI. Weak AI, also known as narrow AI, is the AI that is designed to perform a specific task or function, such as playing chess, recognizing faces, or driving cars. Strong AI, also known as general AI or artificial general intelligence (AGI), is the AI that can perform any intellectual task that a human can, such as understanding natural language, solving problems, or creating art .
- AI can also be categorized based on the degree of human intervention and guidance required for its operation. Supervised learning is the AI that learns from labeled data, such as images with captions or texts with categories. Unsupervised learning is the AI that learns from unlabeled data, such as images without captions or texts without categories. Reinforcement learning is the AI that learns from its own actions and rewards, such as a robot that learns to navigate a maze or a game agent that learns to play a video game .
- AI has many applications and benefits across different industries and domains, such as finance, healthcare, education, entertainment, and security. AI can help automate tasks, optimize processes, enhance decision making, improve customer service, and create new products and services. However, AI also poses some challenges and risks, such as ethical, social, legal, and technical issues, such as bias, privacy, accountability, security, and human-AI interaction  .
