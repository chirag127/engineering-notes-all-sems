## Unit 5 - APPLICATIONS

- In this unit, we will learn about some of the applications of artificial intelligence in various domains and industries.
- We will explore how AI can be used to solve problems, improve efficiency, enhance user experience, and create new opportunities in different fields.
- We will also discuss some of the challenges and limitations of AI, as well as the ethical and social implications of its use.
- The topics covered in this unit are:

  - AI for healthcare: how AI can help diagnose diseases, provide personalized treatment, monitor patients, and assist medical professionals.
  - AI for education: how AI can support learning, teaching, assessment, and feedback in various educational settings and contexts.
  - AI for entertainment: how AI can create and enhance content, such as games, music, movies, and art, and provide interactive and immersive experiences for users.
  - AI for business: how AI can optimize processes, increase productivity, reduce costs, and generate insights for decision making and innovation in various business sectors and functions.
  - AI for social good: how AI can address some of the global challenges and issues, such as poverty, hunger, climate change, and human rights, and contribute to the well-being and development of society.
