### Search Strategies for the notes of the Unit 2 - PROBLEM SOLVING METHODS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

Search Strategies in Artificial Intelligence:
- Search strategies are algorithms used in artificial intelligence to find solutions to problems.
- Search strategies are used in a variety of applications, including decision making, planning, and game playing.
- Search strategies can be classified into two main categories: uninformed search and informed search.
  1. Uninformed search: Uninformed search algorithms use only the problem definition and do not use any additional information about the problem. Examples include breadth-first search and depth-first search.
  2. Informed search: Informed search algorithms use additional information about the problem to guide the search. Examples include best-first search, A* search, and heuristic search.
- The choice of search strategy depends on the nature of the problem and the amount of information available about the problem.
- Understanding search strategies in artificial intelligence is important for the development of effective problem-solving algorithms and for the implementation of intelligent systems.
