Argumentation among Agents

Argumentation among agents refers to the process of exchanging and evaluating arguments between multiple software agents. In argumentation, agents present arguments to support their positions on a particular issue, and other agents evaluate these arguments to determine the validity and strength of the position.

Argumentation among agents is an important aspect of software agents in artificial intelligence because it allows agents to make decisions and take actions based on the collective wisdom of the group. This can lead to more accurate and effective decision-making, as multiple perspectives and considerations are taken into account.

There are several approaches to argumentation among agents, including formal argumentation frameworks, informal argumentation, and deliberation. Formal argumentation frameworks are structured approaches to argumentation that are based on formal logic and rules. Informal argumentation is a more flexible approach to argumentation that allows agents to present and evaluate arguments in a more natural and intuitive way. Deliberation is a process of discussion and exchange of arguments among agents to reach a decision.
