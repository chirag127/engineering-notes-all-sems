### Local Search Algorithms and Optimization Problems for the notes of the Unit 2 - PROBLEM SOLVING METHODS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

Local Search Algorithms and Optimization Problems in Artificial Intelligence:
- Local search algorithms are algorithms used in artificial intelligence to find solutions to optimization problems.
- Optimization problems are problems where the goal is to find the best solution from a set of possible solutions.
- Local search algorithms start with an initial solution and iteratively improve the solution by making small changes.
- Examples of local search algorithms include hill-climbing, simulated annealing, and tabu search.
- Local search algorithms are useful for solving problems where the solution space is large and the optimal solution is hard to find.
- The choice of local search algorithm depends on the nature of the problem and the desired trade-off between solution quality and computational efficiency.
- Understanding local search algorithms and optimization problems is important for the development of effective problem-solving algorithms and for the implementation of intelligent systems.
