### First Order Predicate Logic for the notes of the Unit 3 - KNOWLEDGE REPRESENTATION in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

First Order Predicate Logic in Artificial Intelligence:
- First Order Predicate Logic (FOPL) is a formal language used in artificial intelligence to represent knowledge and to perform reasoning.
- FOPL is based on first-order logic, which is a type of mathematical logic that allows the representation of objects, properties, and relations.
- FOPL provides a rich and expressive syntax for representing knowledge, including:
  1. Predicates: expressions that describe properties and relations between objects.
  2. Variables: symbols that stand for objects.
  3. Quantifiers: symbols that specify the scope of variables.
- FOPL can be used to perform automated reasoning, including:
  1. Unification: the process of finding a common representation for two expressions.
  2. Inference: the process of deducing new information from existing knowledge.
- Understanding FOPL is important for the design and development of intelligent systems that can reason about and manipulate knowledge and information effectively.
