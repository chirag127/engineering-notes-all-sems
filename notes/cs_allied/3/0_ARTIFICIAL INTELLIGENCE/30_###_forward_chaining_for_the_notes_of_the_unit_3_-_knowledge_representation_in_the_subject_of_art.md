### Forward Chaining for the notes of the Unit 3 - KNOWLEDGE REPRESENTATION in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

Forward Chaining in Artificial Intelligence:
- Forward chaining is a method of inference in artificial intelligence that starts with the available data and works forward to derive new conclusions.
- Forward chaining is used in rule-based systems, where the system uses a set of rules to make inferences and conclusions.
- Forward chaining works by applying the rules to the available data, and if a rule is triggered, the system adds the conclusion of the rule to the knowledge base.
- Forward chaining continues until no more rules can be triggered or until a stopping condition is reached.
- Forward chaining can be used in a variety of applications, including expert systems, decision support systems, and natural language processing.
- Understanding forward chaining is important for the design and development of rule-based systems and for the implementation of intelligent systems that use inference.
