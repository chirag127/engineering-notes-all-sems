## Unit 3 - KNOWLEDGE REPRESENTATION

Unit 3 - Knowledge Representation in Artificial Intelligence:
- Unit 3 of the subject "Artificial Intelligence" focuses on the representation of knowledge and information in a form that can be processed by computers.
- Knowledge representation is a critical aspect of AI, as it determines how knowledge and information can be represented, stored, and manipulated by AI systems.
- Unit 3 covers the following topics:
  1. Knowledge representation languages, including first-order logic, semantic networks, and frames.
  2. Representation of uncertainty and vagueness, including probabilistic reasoning and fuzzy logic.
  3. Representation of time and space, including temporal reasoning and spatial reasoning.
  4. Representation of action and change, including planning and decision making.
- Understanding knowledge representation is important for the design and development of AI systems that can reason about and manipulate knowledge and information effectively.
