### Introduction for the notes of the Unit 1 - Characterization of Distributed Systems in the subject of DISTRIBUTED SYSTEM

Distributed systems are a type of computer system that consists of multiple autonomous components that work together to provide a common goal. The goal of unit 1 in the subject of distributed systems is to provide an understanding of the characterization of distributed systems. This includes understanding the fundamental concepts, design principles, and challenges of distributed systems. The unit will cover topics such as the definition of distributed systems, the different types of distributed systems, and the challenges associated with designing and implementing distributed systems. Additionally, the unit will explore the various components that make up a distributed system, including nodes, communication networks, and algorithms for coordination and synchronization. Understanding the characterization of distributed systems is essential for designing and building reliable, scalable, and efficient distributed systems.
