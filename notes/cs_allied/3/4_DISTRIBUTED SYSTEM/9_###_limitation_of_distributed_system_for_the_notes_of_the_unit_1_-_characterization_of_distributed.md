### Limitation of Distributed system for the notes of the Unit 1 - Characterization of Distributed Systems in the subject of DISTRIBUTED SYSTEM

Limitations of Distributed Systems are the challenges that arise when designing, building, and maintaining these systems. Some of the limitations of distributed systems include:

1. Complexity: Distributed systems are inherently complex due to the need to coordinate the activities of multiple autonomous computers.

2. Latency: Communication between nodes in a distributed system can be slow, leading to increased latency in the processing of requests.

3. Bandwidth: The amount of data that can be transmitted between nodes is limited by the available bandwidth of the network.

4. Reliability: Distributed systems can be prone to failures, and it can be difficult to ensure that they are highly available and reliable.

5. Security: Distributed systems can be vulnerable to security threats, such as hacking and data theft.

6. Scalability: Distributed systems can be difficult to scale as the number of nodes increases, and it can be challenging to ensure that they continue to perform well as they grow.

7. Interoperability: Different nodes in a distributed system may use different protocols and technologies, making it difficult to ensure that they can communicate and work together effectively.
