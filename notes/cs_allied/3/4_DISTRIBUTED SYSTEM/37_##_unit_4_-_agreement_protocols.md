## Unit 4 - Agreement Protocols

Agreement protocols are algorithms used in distributed systems to ensure that all nodes agree on a single value or state. They are used to ensure consistency and reliability in the face of network failures and other challenges.

There are several types of agreement protocols, including:
1. Consensus protocols: ensure that all nodes agree on a single value or state. Examples include Paxos and Raft.
2. Atomic broadcast protocols: ensure that a message is delivered to all nodes in the same order. Examples include Total Order Broadcast and FIFO Broadcast.
3. Virtual Synchrony protocols: ensure that nodes have a consistent view of the system, even in the presence of failures. Examples include Isis and Horus.

Agreement protocols play a critical role in distributed systems, as they ensure that all nodes have a consistent view of the system, even in the presence of failures. They are used in a variety of applications, including:
- Distributed databases and data storage systems
- Distributed file systems
- Cloud computing and virtualization

In summary, agreement protocols are algorithms used in distributed systems to ensure that all nodes agree on a single value or state, and play a critical role in ensuring consistency and reliability in the face of network failures and other challenges.
