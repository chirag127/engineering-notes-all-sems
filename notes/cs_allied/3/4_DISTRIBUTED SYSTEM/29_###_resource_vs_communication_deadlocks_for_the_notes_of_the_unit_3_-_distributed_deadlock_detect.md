### resource Vs communication deadlocks for the notes of the Unit 3 - Distributed Deadlock Detection in the subject of DISTRIBUTED SYSTEM

Resource deadlocks and communication deadlocks are two types of deadlocks that can occur in a distributed system. 

1. Resource Deadlocks: Occur when multiple processes are waiting for access to resources that are held by other processes. This type of deadlock is caused by a cycle of resource allocation and waiting in the system.

2. Communication Deadlocks: Occur when multiple processes are waiting for messages from each other to proceed. This type of deadlock is caused by a cycle of message sending and waiting in the system.

The detection and resolution of resource deadlocks is typically easier than that of communication deadlocks, as resource deadlocks can be detected by examining the state of the system and the allocation of resources. Communication deadlocks, on the other hand, require a more sophisticated detection mechanism, as they involve the communication patterns between processes.

In summary, resource deadlocks and communication deadlocks are two types of deadlocks that can occur in a distributed system, with resource deadlocks being easier to detect and resolve compared to communication deadlocks.
