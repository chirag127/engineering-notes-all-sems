### Transactions for the notes of the Unit 8 - Transactions and Concurrency Control in the subject of DISTRIBUTED SYSTEM

Transactions are a unit of work in a distributed system that ensure the integrity and consistency of data. They are used to coordinate the execution of multiple operations, and ensure that the system remains in a consistent state even in the presence of failures.

The key features of transactions include:
1. Atomicity: transactions are atomic, meaning that they are executed as a single unit of work, and either all of the operations in the transaction are executed or none of them are.

2. Consistency: transactions ensure that the system remains in a consistent state, even in the presence of failures.

3. Isolation: transactions are isolated from each other, meaning that the execution of one transaction does not interfere with the execution of another transaction.

4. Durability: transactions are durable, meaning that the results of a transaction are persisted, even in the presence of failures.

In summary, transactions are a unit of work in a distributed system that ensure the integrity and consistency of data. They are atomic, consistent, isolated, and durable, and are used to coordinate the execution of multiple operations in a distributed system.
