## Unit 8 - Transactions and Concurrency Control

Unit 8 - Transactions and Concurrency Control covers the concepts and techniques used to manage transactions and concurrency in distributed systems. Transactions are a critical aspect of distributed systems, as they ensure that the system remains in a consistent state, even in the face of failures. Concurrency control is the process of managing access to shared resources in a distributed system, in order to ensure that the system remains consistent.

The following are key topics covered in Unit 8 - Transactions and Concurrency Control:

1. Transactions: Transactions are units of work that are executed in a distributed system. They ensure that the system remains in a consistent state, even in the face of failures.

2. Atomicity: Atomicity is the property that ensures that transactions are executed as a single, indivisible unit of work. This ensures that the system remains in a consistent state, even in the face of failures.

3. Consistency: Consistency is the property that ensures that the system remains in a consistent state, even in the face of failures. This is achieved through the use of transactions and concurrency control.

4. Isolation: Isolation is the property that ensures that transactions are executed in isolation from each other, in order to ensure that the system remains consistent.

5. Durability: Durability is the property that ensures that transactions are permanent and survive failures. This is achieved through the use of transactions and concurrency control.

6. Concurrency Control: Concurrency control is the process of managing access to shared resources in a distributed system, in order to ensure that the system remains consistent. This is achieved through the use of locks, semaphores, and other synchronization mechanisms.

By understanding the concepts of transactions and concurrency control, organizations can design and implement effective distributed systems that are reliable and maintain consistency, even in the face of failures. Unit 8 - Transactions and Concurrency Control covers the use of transactions and concurrency control in distributed systems, including the use of atomicity, consistency, isolation, durability, and concurrency control mechanisms.
