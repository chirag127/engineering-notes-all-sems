### Resource sharing and the Web Challenges for the notes of the Unit 1 - Characterization of Distributed Systems in the subject of DISTRIBUTED SYSTEM

Resource sharing refers to the ability of multiple users or devices to access and utilize shared resources such as storage, processing power, and network bandwidth. This can be achieved through various methods such as client-server architecture, peer-to-peer (P2P) networks, or cloud computing.

Web challenges in resource sharing include:
1. Scalability: The ability to handle increasing numbers of users and devices accessing shared resources.
2. Availability: Ensuring that resources are always accessible and available for use.
3. Security: Protecting shared resources from unauthorized access and ensuring data privacy.
4. Interoperability: Ensuring compatibility and seamless integration of different systems and devices.
5. Reliability: Ensuring that resources are consistently available and functioning as expected.
6. Performance: Ensuring that resources are used efficiently and effectively to meet user demands.
