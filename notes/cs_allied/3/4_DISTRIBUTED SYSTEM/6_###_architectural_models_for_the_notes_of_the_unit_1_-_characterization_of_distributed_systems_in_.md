### Architectural models for the notes of the Unit 1 - Characterization of Distributed Systems in the subject of DISTRIBUTED SYSTEM

Distributed systems can be characterized using various architectural models such as: 

1. Client-Server Model: In this model, clients request services from servers and servers provide the services. 

2. Peer-to-Peer Model: In this model, each node acts as both a client and a server. 

3. Three-Tier Model: In this model, the system is divided into three parts: client, application server, and database server. 

4. N-Tier Model: This is an extension of the three-tier model where there are more than three tiers. 

5. Microservices Model: In this model, the system is divided into small, independent services that communicate with each other through APIs. 

Each model has its own advantages and disadvantages, and the choice of a model depends on the requirements of the system being built.
