Cyber investigation is the process of collecting, analyzing, and preserving digital evidence to support a criminal or civil investigation. It is a complex and specialized field that requires a deep understanding of technology and the ability to identify, collect, and analyze digital evidence.

Cyber investigations can be conducted for a variety of reasons, including to support criminal investigations, to support civil litigation, to respond to security incidents, and to support internal investigations. The goal of a cyber investigation is to identify the cause of an incident, to determine the extent of the damage, and to preserve evidence for use in legal proceedings.

The process of a cyber investigation typically involves several stages, including:

1. Preparation: This involves preparing for the investigation by identifying the scope of the investigation, determining the objectives of the investigation, and gathering information about the systems and devices involved.

2. Collection: This involves collecting digital evidence from the systems and devices involved in the investigation. This may involve imaging hard drives, capturing network traffic, and collecting other types of digital evidence.

3. Analysis: This involves analyzing the digital evidence to identify the cause of the incident and to determine the extent of the damage. This may involve using forensic tools and techniques to recover deleted files, to analyze network traffic, and to identify malware.

4. Presentation: This involves presenting the results of the investigation to stakeholders, including law enforcement agencies, attorneys, and senior management.
