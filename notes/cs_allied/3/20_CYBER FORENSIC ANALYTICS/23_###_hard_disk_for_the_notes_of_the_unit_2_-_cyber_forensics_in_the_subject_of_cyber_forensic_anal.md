A hard disk is a type of data storage device that is commonly used in computers and other digital devices. The hard disk is made up of one or more rotating disks, called platters, that store data magnetically. The data is stored in tracks and sectors on the platter, and is read and written by a read/write head that is attached to an arm that moves across the platter.

In the context of cyber forensics, hard disks are often an important source of evidence in criminal investigations. Hard disks can contain a wealth of information about a person's activities, including emails, documents, images, and other files. This information can be used to help investigators understand the actions of a suspect, and to build a case against them.

To obtain this information, forensic analysts must use specialized tools and techniques to acquire, preserve, and analyze the data stored on the hard disk. This process is known as disk imaging, and it involves creating an exact copy of the hard disk, which is then used for analysis.
