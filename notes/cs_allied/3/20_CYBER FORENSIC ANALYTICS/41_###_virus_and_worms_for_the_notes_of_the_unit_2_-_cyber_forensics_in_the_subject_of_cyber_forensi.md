Virus and Worms are two types of malicious software (malware) that can cause harm to computer systems and networks.

A virus is a type of malware that attaches itself to a legitimate file or program and spreads to other computers when the infected file or program is executed. Once a computer is infected with a virus, the virus can cause a variety of problems, such as slowing down the computer, corrupting or deleting files, or stealing sensitive information.

A worm is a type of malware that is self-replicating and can spread itself to other computers without the need for a user to execute an infected file or program. Worms can cause harm to computer systems by consuming network bandwidth, slowing down or crashing computers, or by spreading to other computers and infecting them.

In the field of cyber forensics, it is important to understand the behavior and characteristics of viruses and worms, as well as the techniques used to detect and remove them.
