### Physical Evidence for Unit 4 - Evidence Management

1. Hard Drives
- Original hard drive containing digital evidence
- Images of hard drive containing hashes for authentication
- Clone of hard drive to preserve original

2. RAM
- Volatile memory chip containing running processes and data
- Must be extracted and imaged quickly before power down
- Contains decryption keys and malware in memory

3. Mobile Devices
- Smartphones, tablets, etc.
- May contain apps, data, GPS, Bluetooth, WiFi, etc. relevant to case
- Need specialized tools and techniques to extract data

4. Peripheral Devices
- USB drives, SD cards, etc.
- May contain digital evidence
- Images needed and originals preserved

5. Paper Documents
- Physical documents relevant to case
- Need proper handling and preservation to maintain integrity
- Photocopies or scans allow analysis while preserving originals
