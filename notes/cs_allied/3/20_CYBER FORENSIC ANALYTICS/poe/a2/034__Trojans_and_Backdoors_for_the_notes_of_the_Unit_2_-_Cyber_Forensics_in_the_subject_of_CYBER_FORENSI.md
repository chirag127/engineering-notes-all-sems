### Trojans and Backdoors

- Trojans are malicious programs that are disguised as legitimate software but perform malicious actions once installed or executed on a system.
- Trojans are often downloaded from malicious websites or sent as email attachments to trick users into installing them.
- Once installed, Trojans can do things like steal data, access the system, or install additional malware.
- Backdoors are methods of gaining remote access to a computer to bypass normal authentication procedures.
- Hackers install backdoors to maintain persistent access to compromised systems even after the initial attack vectors are closed.
- Backdoors are often installed once a system is compromised, allowing attackers to access the system whenever they want.
- Detecting trojans and backdoors requires analyzing system behavior, running scans, and monitoring network traffic for suspicious activity.
- Analysts can use tools like packet sniffers and system monitors to detect potential trojan and backdoor activity during a forensic analysis.
