### Criminal Behaviour for the notes of the Unit 1 - Cyber Crime in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

1. Cyber stalking: Repeatedly sending threatening or harassing messages to an individual via email or on social media.
2. Phishing: Tricking a user into providing personal information like account numbers, passwords, etc. by impersonating a trustworthy entity.
3. Malware distribution: Developing and spreading malware like viruses, worms, Trojans, ransomware, etc. to gain unauthorized access to systems or data.
4. Identity theft: Stealing someone's personal information and using it without their permission to impersonate the individual.
5. Online scams: Tricking users into sending money or goods through deceptive methods like fake lottery wins, fake job offers, fake charities, etc.
6. Online fraud: Obtaining money or goods illegally by lying, deceiving or tricking people online. Includes credit card fraud, tax refund fraud, investment fraud, etc.
7. Cyber espionage: Unauthorized access and theft of sensitive data/information from government agencies, corporations or individuals for political/economic gain.
8. Distributed denial of service (DDoS) attacks: Flooding a computer, server or network with a large amount of traffic in order to disrupt services and make resources unavailable to intended users.
