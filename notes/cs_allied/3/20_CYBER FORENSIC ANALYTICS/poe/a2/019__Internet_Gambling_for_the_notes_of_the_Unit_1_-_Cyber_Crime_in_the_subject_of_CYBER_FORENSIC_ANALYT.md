### Internet Gambling

- Internet gambling refers to gambling games like poker, casinos, sports betting, lotteries, etc. played over the internet.
- The ease of access to internet gambling websites makes it attractive to gamblers but also leads to financial and emotional distress for some.
- The illegal gambling industry makes huge profits from internet gambling as it is difficult to regulate and monitor. Some ways in which internet gambling is made lucrative for operators are:
- Allowing anonymous sign-ups without proper identity verification
- Targeting vulnerable groups like minors, problem gamblers, etc. through aggressive marketing
- Offering easy credit and loans to continue gambling
- Using deceitful practices to increase time spent on gambling websites
- Data from customer profiles and gambling behavior is collected and sold to third parties for targeted advertising and other purposes
- As internet gambling transcends physical borders, regulating it is challenging. International cooperation and technological measures are needed to curb illegal internet gambling activities and their negative impacts.
