### Regulation of certifying authorities for the notes of the Unit 5 - Cyber Laws and Authorities in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS.

1. Certifying authorities are regulated by the provisions of the Information Technology Act, 2000.
2. Section 24 of the IT Act, 2000 provides for the appointment of Controller of Certifying Authorities (CCA) by the Central Government.
3. The CCA has the power to suspend or revoke certifying authority in case of violation of the provisions of the Act.
4. According to Section 25 of the IT Act, 2000, every certifying authority shall comply with the standards for the certifying authorities, as may be specified by CCA.
5. Certifying authorities have to maintain the integrity of the electronic signatures and the certificates issued by them. Any fraudulent actions by them would be punishable under Section 25 and 26 of the Act.
6. Certifying authorities are also required to maintain proper accounts and other relevant records and arrange for their timely disposal in accordance with the regulations under the IT Act, 2000.
