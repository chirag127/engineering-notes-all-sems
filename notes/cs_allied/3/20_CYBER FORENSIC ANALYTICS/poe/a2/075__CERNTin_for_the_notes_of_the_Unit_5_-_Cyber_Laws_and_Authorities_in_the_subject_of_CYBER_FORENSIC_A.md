### CERNTin for the notes of the Unit 5 - Cyber Laws and Authorities in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

1. The Computer Emergency Response Team of India (CERT-In) is the nodal agency under Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) to deal with cyber security threats like hacking, phishing attacks and to fortify security-related defense of the Indian Internet domain.

2. CERT-In is responsible for collection and analysis of information on cyber incidents, assessment of threats, issuing guidelines, vulnerability notes and whitepapers relating to information security practices, procedures, prevention, response and reporting of cyber incidents.

3. CERT-In generates awareness among users, enterprises and organizations in Government and Public Sectors regarding ICT threats and how to prevent and respond to cyber attacks. It provides a single window for reporting cyber security incidents and related events.

4. CERT-In coordinates incident response measures with Internet Service Providers (ISPs), Internet giants, Critical Sectors (Power, Finance etc.), Researchers, Academia and other CERTs. It analyzes information collected from various sources about cyber attacks/ cyber crimes/ incidents and assesses damage caused due to such incidents.

5. CERT-In is covered under the provisions of Section 70 of the Information Technology Act, 2000 regarding the 'Power of the Central Government to prescribe rules for carrying out the purposes of this Act'. The Section empowers the Central Government to make rules to carry out the purposes of this Act.
