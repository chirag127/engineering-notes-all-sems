### Hard disk for the notes of the Unit 2 - Cyber Forensics in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

1. Hard disk is a non-volatile storage device that stores digital data on rapidly rotating platters with magnetic surfaces.
2. It is made up of one or more aluminum or glass platters with a magnetic coating, stacked on a spindle within an airtight enclosure.
3. The platters spin at high speeds of up to 7200 rpm or more, while read/write heads fly a tiny distance above the platters reading and writing data.
4. The hard disk components - platters, spindle, read/write heads and actuator arms - can get damaged or fail due to physical shocks, vibrations, wear and tear, etc. leading to hard disk failure.
5. When a hard disk fails, the stored data becomes inaccessible. Forensic analysts retrieve and analyze data from failed hard disks using various techniques like imaging, data carving, etc. to recover and investigate digital evidence.
6. The notes in this unit will cover in detail the various hard disk components, types of hard disk failures, and data recovery techniques from failed hard disks for cyber forensics.
