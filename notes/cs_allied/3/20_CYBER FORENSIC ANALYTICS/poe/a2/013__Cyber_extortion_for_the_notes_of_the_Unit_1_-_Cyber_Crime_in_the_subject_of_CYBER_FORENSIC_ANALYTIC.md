### Cyber Extortion

- Cyber extortion is a type of cybercrime where criminals threaten to damage a victim's computer system or reveal stolen personal information unless a ransom is paid.
- The criminals may claim that they have installed malware or stolen sensitive data from the victim's system and will only release the system or data once the ransom is paid.
- Even if the ransom is paid, there is no guarantee that the criminals will make good on their word and restore access or not misuse the stolen data.
- Cyber extortion attacks are often carried out using ransomware - malware that locks the victim out of their computer or files until a ransom is paid.
- To protect against cyber extortion, organizations should have strong security measures in place, including antivirus software, firewalls, employee training, and regular data backups to mitigate the impact of any attacks.
- If targeted by cyber extortion, the incident should be reported to the relevant authorities and paying the ransom is not recommended. It is better to restore systems and data using backups.
