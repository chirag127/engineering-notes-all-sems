### Cyber weapons

1. Cyber weapons are malware or malicious software designed to infiltrate and damage a computer system or network.
2. They are used to conduct cyber attacks to steal data, deny access to systems or networks, or cause physical damage.
3. Some examples of cyber weapons are:
- Stuxnet - Malware designed to sabotage Iranian nuclear facilities by damaging centrifuges.
- Duqu - Malware similar to Stuxnet that stole data from computers.
- Flame - Malware used for cyber espionage to steal data from infected computers.
- Shamoon - Malware that wiped data from thousands of computers at Saudi Aramco.

4. Cyber weapons require significant technical expertise and resources to develop and deploy effectively. They are being increasingly used by nation states and hacker groups to achieve strategic goals.
5. As cyber weapons become more sophisticated, they pose a serious threat to critical infrastructure, government systems and businesses. Developing effective defenses and responses to cyber attacks using cyber weapons is crucial to cyber security.
