### Internet Payment Services

- Payment gateways like PayPal, Stripe, Authorize.net, etc. facilitate online payments.
- These gateways act as an intermediary between the merchant and the customer's bank.
- Payment is completed once the gateway verifies and authenticates the transaction and transfers the funds to the merchant's account.
- However, these gateways can be vulnerable to attacks like account takeovers, transaction fraud, DDoS, etc. which can be investigated in cyber forensics.
- Other payment methods like credit/debit cards, internet banking, mobile wallets, etc. can also be misused for fraud which leaves digital footprints that can be analyzed.
- Analysis of logs, transaction details, account access details, etc. can help identify suspicious activity and trace criminals in such cases.
