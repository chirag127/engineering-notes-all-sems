### Jurisprudential Inconsistency for the notes of the Unit 1 - Cyber Crime in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

- Laws relating to cyber crimes are inconsistent across jurisdictions. Same cyber crime may be punished differently in different countries depending upon their laws. This creates challenges in investigating and prosecuting cyber crimes that span across multiple countries.
- For example, laws related to cyber stalking, cyber bullying, phishing, spamming, etc. are not consistent across countries. Some countries may not even have laws for certain types of cyber crimes. This inconsistency makes it difficult to take action against cyber criminals operating across borders.
- Lack of bilateral treaties and multilateral agreements on cyber crimes further adds to the inconsistencies. Though some efforts like Budapest Convention on Cybercrime aim to harmonize cyber crime laws but still more efforts are required to address jurisprudential inconsistencies.
- Such inconsistencies pose impediments in international cooperation on cyber crime investigation and create loopholes that can be exploited by cyber criminals to avoid legal consequences. Consistent laws and coordination between countries are required to effectively tackle the borderless cyber crimes.
