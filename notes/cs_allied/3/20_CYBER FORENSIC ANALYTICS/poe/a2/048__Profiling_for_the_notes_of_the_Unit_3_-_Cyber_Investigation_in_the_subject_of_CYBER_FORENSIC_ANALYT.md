### Profiling for the notes of the Unit 3 - Cyber Investigation in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

1. Profiling is creating attributes or characteristics of a person or a group that is based on a pattern of behavior and the collection of information related to the individual or group.

2. Profiling is an important step in cyber investigation to narrow down the suspect list and focus the investigation.

3. Profiling can be done based on:

- Digital footprint: Browsing history, online activities, posts, comments, likes, etc.
- Metadata: Data about data like file name, file size, creation date, modified date, etc.
- Artifacts: Temporary internet files, logs, etc.
- Writing style: Use of language, grammatical errors, punctuation, etc.

4. Limitations of profiling:

- It can be inaccurate if sufficient data is not available.
- It can be misleading if the perpetrator intentionally leaves false information or breadcrumbs.
- It should not be solely relied upon to draw conclusions. Other evidence and investigation techniques should also be used.

5. Profiling should be done by experienced investigators with the help of data scientists and linguists to achieve more accurate results.
