### Warranted searches for the notes of the Unit 3 - Cyber Investigation in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

1. Seizing and imaging digital devices: Warrants are required to seize and image digital devices that may contain evidence. The warrant must specify the devices to be seized and imaged.

2. Searching and seizing stored digital information: Warrants are required to search and seize digital information stored on a device or in the cloud. The warrant must specify the information to be searched and seized. Overbroad warrants that give law enforcement broad discretion to search and seize unspecified digital information are prohibited.

3. Compelling decryption: In some cases, law enforcement may seek to compel a subject to decrypt encrypted data. The legal requirements for such orders are unsettled. At a minimum, the government must show that it has a justified reason to believe the data contains evidence of a crime or that it is an instrumental to criminal activity.

4. Obtaining IP address or account information: Law enforcement may seek warrants or court orders to obtain IP address or account information to identify the person responsible for a crime or the location of criminal evidence. The legal requirements vary based on the type of information and entity possessing the information.

5. Obtaining cell phone location information: Law enforcement may seek warrants or court orders to obtain historical or real-time cell phone location information. The legal requirements depend on whether the request is for real-time tracking or historical cell-site location information. In both cases, the government must show probable cause that the information will reveal evidence of a crime or aid in apprehension of a suspect.
