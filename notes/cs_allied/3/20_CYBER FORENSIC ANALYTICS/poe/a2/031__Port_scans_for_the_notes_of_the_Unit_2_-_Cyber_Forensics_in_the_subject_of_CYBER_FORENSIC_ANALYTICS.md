### Port scans for the notes of the Unit 2 - Cyber Forensics in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

- A port scan is a series of messages sent by someone attempting to break into a computer to learn which computer network services the computer provides and the ports used by these services. This scanning technique is used as a first step in the hacking methodology against a target machine.
- The most common types of port scans used are:
- 1. Vanilla scan: This simply scans ports with no stealth techniques and is easy to detect.
- 2. Stealth scan: This uses techniques like scanning only a few ports at a time to avoid detection.
- 3. Fragmented packet scan: This splits the port scanning packets into fragments to avoid detection.
- Port scans can be detected by monitoring network traffic and looking for multiple connections to different ports originating from a single source. However, sophisticated scans may be hard to detect.
- As part of forensics analysis, it is important to analyze network traffic logs to detect any port scans and identify the source to understand if any hacking attempts were made. The data from port scans can provide valuable information to forensic analysts to understand the hacking methodology and techniques used.
