### Stylometric Techniques for the notes of the Unit 3 - Cyber Investigation in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

1. Stylometry is the application of the study of linguistic style, usually to written language, to infer authorship or other attributes of a text.
2. In forensics, stylometry is used to attribute authorship to anonymous or disputed documents.
3. The basic idea is that each person has a unique style of writing that can be detected through the use of statistical methods.
4. Some of the features commonly analyzed in stylometric studies include:
- Word length
- Sentence length
- Vocabulary richness and vocabulary choice
- Function word usage (e.g. prepositions, pronouns, etc.)
- Punctuation
- Syntactic complexity
5. Supervised machine learning techniques like classification and regression can be used to train models to distinguish authors based on their writing style.
6. However, stylometric techniques have limitations and do not always produce conclusive results due to factors like intentionally disguising one's writing style or collaboration between multiple authors.
7. In cyber forensic investigations, stylometry can be useful to attribute authorship of threat messages, ransom notes, malware code comments, etc. to suspects. But results should be interpreted cautiously in light of the limitations.
