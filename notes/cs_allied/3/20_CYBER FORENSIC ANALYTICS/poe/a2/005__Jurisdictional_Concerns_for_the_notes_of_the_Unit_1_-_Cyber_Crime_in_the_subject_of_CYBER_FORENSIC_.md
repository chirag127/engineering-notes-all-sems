### Jurisdictional Concerns for the notes of the Unit 1 - Cyber Crime in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

1. Given the borderless nature of cyberspace, determining which country has jurisdiction to investigate and prosecute cybercrimes can be difficult.
2. Jurisdiction is determined based on factors like:
    - Location of the perpetrator
    - Location of the victim
    - Location of servers used to carry out the crime
    - Location where the effects of the crime were felt
3. If jurisdiction is unclear, it may lead to:
    - Disputes between countries on who should handle the case
    - Criminals going unpunished if no country accepts jurisdiction
    - Inefficiencies if multiple countries claim jurisdiction and duplicate investigative efforts
4. International cooperation and cybercrime treaties can help address jurisdictional concerns by:
    - Defining acceptable bases for jurisdiction
    - Establishing mechanisms for multinational cooperation and information sharing
    - Enacting equivalent laws and agreeing on mutual assistance in investigating and prosecuting cybercrimes
