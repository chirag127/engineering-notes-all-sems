### Commercial Piracy

- Commercial piracy refers to the unauthorized copying and distribution of copyrighted material for commercial gain. This includes, but is not limited to, the sale of counterfeit media such as music, movies, software, and video games.
- Commercial piracy is illegal and unethical as it deprives content creators of revenue from legitimate sales. It can negatively impact innovation when companies are unable to profit from products or services.
- Tools and techniques used to investigate commercial piracy include:
- Analyzing distribution channels to trace the origin and flow of pirated goods. This could include investigating online marketplaces, payment records, and shipping information.
- Examining pirated physical and digital media for clues such as fingerprints, metadata, or watermarks that could identify the original source.
- Infiltrating piracy networks and groups to gather intelligence on their operations, members, and key figures. This may involve undercover operations and surveillance.
- International cooperation and coordination is often needed to effectively combat commercial piracy networks and shut down global distribution channels. Law enforcement must work with companies and organizations to gather evidence and build legal cases against commercial pirates.
