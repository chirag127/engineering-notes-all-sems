### Mobile Payments

1. Mobile payments refer to payment services operated under financial regulation and performed from or via a mobile device.
2. Instead of paying with cash, cheque, or credit cards, a consumer can use a mobile phone to pay for a wide range of services and digital or hard goods.
3. Mobile payment technology can also be used to deposit money into a bank account.
4. The most common mobile payment methods are:
- SMS-based payments: Customer sends a payment request via SMS text message. The charge is then added to their phone bill or deducted from their pre-paid balance.
- Direct mobile billing: Customer's payment is charged directly to their mobile phone account. This method is mainly used for digital goods.
- Mobile web payments: Customer uses their mobile web browser to complete a payment. Usually requires a card or account on file.
- NFC (Near Field Communication): Customer taps or waves their NFC-enabled mobile device over a payment terminal to complete an in-person payment.
- QR code payments: Customer scans a QR code with their mobile app to make an in-person or remote payment.
5. The benefits of mobile payments include convenience, security, and tracking of payments and expenses. However, there are also risks such as fraud, scams, and unauthorized access to accounts or devices.
