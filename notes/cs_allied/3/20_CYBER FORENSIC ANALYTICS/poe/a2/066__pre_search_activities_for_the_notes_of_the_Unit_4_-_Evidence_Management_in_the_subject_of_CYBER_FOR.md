### Pre-search activities for Unit 4 - Evidence Management notes

1. Understand the scope of evidence - Digital evidence can be found in various digital devices and locations. Determine the scope of devices and locations to search for relevant evidence.
2. Obtain legal authorization - Obtain proper legal authorization or warrant to conduct the search and seize digital evidence. Follow the laws of the land.
3. Plan the search - Plan the search process in a structured manner. Determine the order of searching devices and locations. Allocate proper resources and time.
4. Preserve the crime scene - Ensure proper handling of devices and locations to prevent spoliation or destruction of potential digital evidence. Document and photograph the crime scene.
5. Prepare to acquire and preserve evidence - Arrange for proper tools, equipment, and storage media to acquire, extract, and preserve digital evidence in a forensically sound manner.
6. Prepare to analyze the evidence - Plan for the digital forensic analysis process including the tools and techniques to employ as well as potential external help or resources required.

The above points cover the major pre-search activities to prepare for searching, acquiring, and analyzing digital evidence in a forensically adequate way. A proper pre-search planning leads to efficient and effective evidence management.
