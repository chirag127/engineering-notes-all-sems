

### Investigating Computer Intrusions

1. Computer intrusions are defined as unauthorized access to a computer system or network. They can be intentional or accidental, malicious or non-malicious.

2. Computer intrusions can be detected by monitoring network traffic for suspicious activities and looking for signs of malicious software.

3. To investigate a computer intrusion, forensic analysts must first identify the source of the intrusion. This can involve analyzing log files, network traffic, and system configuration information.

4. Once the source of the intrusion has been identified, forensic analysts must determine the extent of the intrusion. This includes determining what data was accessed, modified, or deleted, as well as any malicious software that may have been installed.

5. After the extent of the intrusion has been determined, forensic analysts can begin to analyze the evidence to determine the identity of the attacker. This can involve analyzing the malicious code, analyzing log files, and analyzing network traffic.

6. Finally, forensic analysts must determine the best course of action to prevent future intrusions. This can involve implementing stronger security measures, such as improved authentication, encryption, and intrusion detection systems.
