
### Pre Search Activities for Unit 4 - Evidence Management in CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

1. Understand the nature of the incident and the type of digital evidence that is likely to be found.
2. Identify the sources of digital evidence.
3. Determine the scope of the investigation.
4. Identify the type of tools to be used.
5. Identify the personnel required to conduct the investigation.
6. Create a list of keywords and phrases to be used in the search.
7. Create a timeline of events related to the incident.
8. Develop a plan to collect and preserve digital evidence.
9. Document the steps taken during the pre-search activities.
