
## Unit 4 - Evidence Management

1. Evidence management is the process of collecting, organizing, and maintaining evidence for use in legal proceedings.

2. Evidence can include physical objects, documents, audio recordings, video recordings, photographs, and other items.

3. It is important to maintain the integrity of evidence by preserving it in its original form and condition.

4. Evidence must be handled and stored properly to ensure that it is not contaminated or altered in any way.

5. Evidence must be documented and tracked throughout its life cycle, from collection to disposal.

6. Evidence must be securely stored in an appropriate environment to ensure its safety and security.

7. All evidence must be handled in accordance with the law and any applicable regulations.
