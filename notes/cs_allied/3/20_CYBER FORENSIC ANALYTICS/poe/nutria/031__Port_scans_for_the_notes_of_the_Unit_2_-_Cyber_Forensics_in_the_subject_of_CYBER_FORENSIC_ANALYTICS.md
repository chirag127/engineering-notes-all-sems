
### Port Scans for Unit 2 - Cyber Forensics

* A port scan is a process used to identify open ports on a networked device. 
* Open ports can be used to gain access to the device or its data, making port scans an important part of cyber forensics.
* There are several types of port scans, including TCP Connect, TCP SYN, UDP, ACK, FIN, Xmas Tree, and Null.
* Port scans can also be used to detect malware or malicious activity on a device.
* It is important to note that port scans are not always malicious and can be used for legitimate purposes, such as checking the security of a device.
* In order to protect a device from port scans, it is important to ensure that all ports are closed unless they are being used for a legitimate purpose.
