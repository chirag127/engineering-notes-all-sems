
## Unit 1 - Cyber Crime

* Cyber crime is a term used to describe any criminal activity that involves the use of computers and the internet. This includes activities such as hacking, phishing, identity theft, cyberbullying, cyberstalking, cyberterrorism, and more.

* Cyber criminals use a variety of methods to gain access to confidential information or to commit fraud. These methods include malware, social engineering, and exploiting software vulnerabilities.

* To protect yourself from cyber crime, it is important to be aware of the risks and to take steps to protect your computer and online accounts. This includes using strong passwords, avoiding phishing scams, and keeping your computer and software up-to-date.

* It is also important to be aware of the laws that govern cyber crime in your country or region. In many countries, cyber crimes are treated as serious offences and can carry heavy penalties.

* Additionally, it is important to be aware of the resources available to help victims of cyber crime. These resources can include legal advice, online support groups, and identity theft protection services.
