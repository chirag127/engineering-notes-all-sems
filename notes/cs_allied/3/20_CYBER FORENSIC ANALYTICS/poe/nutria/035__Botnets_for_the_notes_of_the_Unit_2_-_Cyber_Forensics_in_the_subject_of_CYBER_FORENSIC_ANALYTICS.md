
### Botnets for the notes of the Unit 2 - Cyber Forensics in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

1. A botnet is a network of computers infected with malicious software (malware) and controlled remotely by a hacker. 
2. Botnets can be used to send spam, launch distributed denial of service (DDoS) attacks, spread malware, and more. 
3. Botnets are usually created by exploiting vulnerabilities in the computers on the network. 
4. Botnets are often used to send large amounts of data or perform computationally intensive tasks. 
5. Botnets can be used to steal personal information, such as passwords and credit card numbers. 
6. Botnets can also be used to launch distributed denial of service (DDoS) attacks, which can disrupt websites and services. 
7. Cyber forensics is the process of collecting, analyzing, and reporting digital evidence in order to investigate cybercrimes. 
8. Cyber forensics analysts use a variety of techniques to analyze digital evidence and identify the source of a cybercrime. 
9. Cyber forensics analysts use a variety of tools to analyze digital evidence, such as forensic software and hardware. 
10. Cyber forensics analysts can use botnets to investigate cybercrimes, such as DDoS attacks, malware infections, and data breaches.
