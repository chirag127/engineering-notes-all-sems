
### Report Preparations for the notes of the Unit 4 - Evidence Management in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

1. Evidence management is a critical part of cyber forensics. It involves collecting, preserving, and analyzing digital evidence in order to identify, investigate, and prosecute cyber crime.
2. Digital evidence can be collected from a variety of sources, including computers, mobile devices, networks, and cloud services.
3. Before collecting digital evidence, it is important to establish a chain of custody. This ensures that the evidence is collected and stored securely, and the integrity of the evidence is maintained.
4. Digital evidence should be collected using appropriate tools and techniques. These tools and techniques should be documented in order to ensure that the evidence is collected in a consistent and reliable manner.
5. Digital evidence should be stored securely and backed up regularly. It is important to ensure that the evidence is not tampered with or destroyed.
6. Digital evidence can be analyzed using a variety of techniques, including forensic software, data recovery tools, and manual analysis.
7. Reports should be written in a clear and concise manner, and should include all relevant information regarding the digital evidence.
8. Reports should be reviewed and approved by a qualified expert prior to being submitted to the court.
