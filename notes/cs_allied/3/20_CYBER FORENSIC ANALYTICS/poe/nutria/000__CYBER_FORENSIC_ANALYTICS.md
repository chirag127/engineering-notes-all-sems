
# CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

1. Cyber Forensics is the practice of collecting, analyzing, and presenting digital evidence in a manner that is legally admissible. It is a branch of digital forensics that deals with the investigation of crimes committed using computers or the internet.

2. Cyber Forensics involves the application of investigative and analytical techniques to identify, preserve, recover, analyze, and present digital evidence in a manner that is legally admissible. It is used to investigate a wide range of computer-related crimes such as fraud, identity theft, intellectual property theft, and cyber terrorism.

3. Cyber Forensics requires a thorough understanding of computer systems and networks, as well as the ability to use specialized tools and techniques to collect, analyze, and present digital evidence. It is also important to understand the legal implications of collecting and presenting digital evidence in a court of law.

4. Cyber Forensics is a rapidly evolving field, as new technologies and techniques are developed to better identify, preserve, analyze, and present digital evidence. It is important for investigators to stay up-to-date on the latest developments in the field in order to effectively investigate cyber crimes.
