

### Electronic Governance for the notes of the Unit 5 - Cyber Laws and Authorities in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

* Electronic Governance is the use of digital technologies to support and facilitate the management of government functions. 
* It is used to increase the efficiency and effectiveness of government services, improve the transparency and accountability of government processes, and enhance the public's access to and engagement with government information and services. 
* Electronic Governance is also used to improve the security of government systems by implementing measures to protect information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. 
* Cyber Laws are laws that regulate activities related to the use of computers and the internet, such as the use of digital signatures, encryption, and data protection. 
* Cyber Laws also regulate the activities of hackers, cybercriminals, and other malicious actors. 
* Cyber Authorities are organizations or agencies responsible for enforcing cyber laws and protecting the public from cybercrime. 
* These organizations typically have the authority to investigate cybercrime, arrest and prosecute suspects, and impose civil or criminal penalties. 
* In some countries, Cyber Authorities are responsible for developing and enforcing policies and regulations related to the use of digital technologies in government and private sector organizations.
