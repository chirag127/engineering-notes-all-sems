
### Criminal Behaviour for the notes of the Unit 1 - Cyber Crime in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

1. Cybercrime is defined as any illegal activity that involves the use of computers or other digital devices, such as the internet, to commit a crime. 
2. Cybercriminals use a variety of methods to commit their crimes, such as hacking, phishing, malware, and other malicious activities. 
3. Cybercriminals often target vulnerable individuals or organizations, such as those with weak passwords or outdated software. 
4. Cybercriminals may also use social engineering techniques to gain access to confidential information or resources. 
5. Cybercriminals may also use distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) attacks to disrupt the operations of a website or other online services. 
6. Cybercriminals may also use ransomware to extort money from victims.
7. Cybercriminals may also use identity theft to gain access to bank accounts, credit cards, and other sensitive data. 
8. Cybercriminals may also use botnets to conduct malicious activities, such as sending spam emails or launching denial-of-service attacks. 
9. Cybercriminals may also use cryptocurrency to launder money and hide their identities. 
10. Cybercriminals may also use online marketplaces to buy and sell stolen data.
