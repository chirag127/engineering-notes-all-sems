
### Phreaking for the Notes of Unit 1 - Cyber Crime in CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

* Phreaking is a term used to describe the practice of manipulating phone networks to gain access to services without paying for them. 
* It is a form of cybercrime that has been used since the early days of the telephone system. 
* Phreakers use a variety of techniques to gain access to phone lines, including social engineering, exploiting weaknesses in the phone system, and using specialized hardware. 
* Phreaking is often used to make free long-distance calls, but can also be used to access voicemail, eavesdrop on phone conversations, and even control the phone system itself. 
* Phreaking can be a lucrative activity for criminals, as it can be used to steal money from unsuspecting victims. 
* To combat phreaking, phone companies have implemented measures to detect and prevent unauthorized access to their systems. 
* Law enforcement agencies have also taken steps to crack down on phreakers, and those caught engaging in the activity can face criminal charges.
