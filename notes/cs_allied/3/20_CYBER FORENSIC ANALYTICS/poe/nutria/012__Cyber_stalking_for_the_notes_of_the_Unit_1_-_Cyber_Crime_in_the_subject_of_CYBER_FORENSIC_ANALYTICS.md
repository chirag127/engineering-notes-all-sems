

### Cyber Stalking

1. Cyber stalking is the use of technology to harass, threaten, or monitor another person. It can involve sending unwanted emails, messages, or images, or tracking a person's online activities. 
2. Cyber stalking is a form of cyber crime and can be a criminal offense in many countries. It is important to be aware of the signs of cyber stalking and take steps to protect yourself from cyber stalkers. 
3. Victims of cyber stalking may experience emotional distress, fear, and anxiety as a result of the harassment. They may also experience physical symptoms, such as difficulty sleeping, headaches, and nausea.
4. To protect yourself from cyber stalking, it is important to be aware of your online activities and take steps to protect your personal information. This includes using strong passwords and two-factor authentication, not sharing personal information online, and avoiding online activities that could put you at risk. 
5. It is also important to be aware of the signs of cyber stalking and to report any suspicious activity to the authorities. Cyber stalking is a serious crime and should not be taken lightly.
