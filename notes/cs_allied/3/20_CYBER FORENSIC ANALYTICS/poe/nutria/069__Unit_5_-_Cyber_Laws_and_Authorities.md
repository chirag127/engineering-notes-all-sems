
## Unit 5 - Cyber Laws and Authorities

1. Cyber laws are laws that regulate the use of technology, including the internet, computers, mobile phones, and other electronic devices.

2. Cyber laws are intended to protect individuals and organizations from cybercrimes, such as identity theft, fraud, and data theft.

3. Cyber laws also protect the privacy of individuals and organizations by prohibiting the unauthorized collection and use of personal information.

4. Cyber laws also protect intellectual property by prohibiting the unauthorized use of copyrighted material.

5. Cyber laws are enforced by government agencies, such as the Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI) and the Department of Homeland Security (DHS).

6. In addition, many countries have established cybercrime units to investigate and prosecute cybercrimes.

7. Organizations are also encouraged to develop their own cyber security policies and procedures to protect their networks and data from cybercrimes.
