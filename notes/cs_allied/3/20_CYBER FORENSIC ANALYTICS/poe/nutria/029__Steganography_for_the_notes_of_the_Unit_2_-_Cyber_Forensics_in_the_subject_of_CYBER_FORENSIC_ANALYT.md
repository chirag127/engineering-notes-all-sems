
### Steganography for the Notes of the Unit 2 - Cyber Forensics in the Subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

1. Steganography is the practice of hiding information within other data, often in the form of images, audio files, and other digital media.
2. It is used to conceal sensitive information or to prevent unauthorized access to data.
3. Steganography is often used in cyber forensics to uncover hidden data that may have been used for malicious purposes.
4. There are various techniques employed to hide data, including least significant bit insertion, masking and filtering, and spread spectrum techniques.
5. To detect steganography, forensic investigators must use specialized tools to scan for hidden data.
6. Steganalysis is the process of analyzing digital media to detect the presence of steganography.
7. Steganalysis is an important tool for cyber forensic investigators, as it can reveal the presence of hidden data that may be used to commit a crime.
