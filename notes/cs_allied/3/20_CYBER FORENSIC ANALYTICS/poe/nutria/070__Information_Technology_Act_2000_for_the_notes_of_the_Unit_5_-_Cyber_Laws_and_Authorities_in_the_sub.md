
### Information Technology Act 2000

1. The Information Technology Act 2000 was enacted to provide legal recognition for transactions carried out by means of electronic data interchange and other means of electronic communication, commonly referred to as "electronic commerce".

2. The Act also seeks to facilitate electronic filing of documents with the Government agencies.

3. The Act provides for the legal framework so that legal sanctity is accorded to electronic records and digital signatures.

4. The Act provides for the establishment of a Controller of Certifying Authorities to regulate the issuance of digital signatures.

5. The Act also provides for the establishment of the Cyber Appellate Tribunal to hear appeals from any person aggrieved by any direction issued or decision made by the Controller.

6. The Act also provides for the setting up of a Cyber Regulations Advisory Committee to advise the Central Government on the formulation of policies and rules for the promotion of electronic commerce.

7. The Act also provides for the establishment of the Office of the National Cyber Security Coordinator to coordinate the activities of the Central Government and the State Governments in the field of cyber security.
