
### Types for the Notes of the Unit 4 - Evidence Management in the Subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS
1. Digital Evidence: Digital evidence is any type of evidence that can be stored on a computer or other digital device. This includes emails, documents, photos, audio, video, and other types of data.
2. Physical Evidence: Physical evidence is any type of evidence that can be obtained from a physical location. This includes items such as fingerprints, blood, hair, clothing, and weapons.
3. Network Evidence: Network evidence is any type of evidence that can be obtained from a network. This includes network traffic logs, system logs, and other types of data.
4. Mobile Device Evidence: Mobile device evidence is any type of evidence that can be obtained from a mobile device. This includes text messages, photos, audio, video, and other types of data.
5. Cloud Evidence: Cloud evidence is any type of evidence that can be obtained from a cloud-based service. This includes emails, documents, photos, audio, video, and other types of data.
6. Social Media Evidence: Social media evidence is any type of evidence that can be obtained from a social media platform. This includes posts, comments, messages, photos, audio, video, and other types of data.
