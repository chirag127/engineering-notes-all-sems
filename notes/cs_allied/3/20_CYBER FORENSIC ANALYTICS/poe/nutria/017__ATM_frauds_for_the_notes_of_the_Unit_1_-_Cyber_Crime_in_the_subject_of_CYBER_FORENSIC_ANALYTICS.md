
### ATM Frauds

ATM frauds are a type of cybercrime which involves the use of technology to steal money from automated teller machines (ATMs). These criminals use a variety of methods to gain access to the machines and steal cash or personal information from the users.

1. Skimming: Skimming involves the installation of a device on the ATM that reads and stores the user's card information. This information can then be used to create a duplicate card and access the user's account.

2. Phishing: Phishing involves sending emails or text messages that look like they are from a legitimate company or service. The messages will usually contain a link to a website that looks like the company's website, but is actually a fake site created by the criminals.

3. Malware: Malware is malicious software that is installed on an ATM and can be used to steal card information or access the user's account.

4. Card Trapping: Card trapping involves the installation of a device on the ATM that prevents the user's card from being returned after the transaction is complete. The criminals can then use the card to access the user's account.

5. Shoulder Surfing: Shoulder surfing is a form of social engineering that involves observing a user as they enter their PIN number at an ATM. The criminals can then use this information to access the user's account.

6. ATM Jackpotting: ATM jackpotting involves the use of malicious software to force an ATM to dispense all of its cash. The criminals can then collect the money before the machine can be shut down.
