
### Circumstantial Evidence for Unit 4 - Evidence Management in Cyber Forensic Analytics

1. Circumstantial evidence is the evidence that is used in criminal investigations to infer facts from the circumstances that are associated with the case.
2. It is also known as indirect evidence and is used to establish a link between the suspect and the crime scene.
3. Circumstantial evidence is used to establish a reasonable conclusion based on the facts of the case.
4. It is used to establish the timeline of events and to identify the perpetrator of the crime.
5. It can be used to prove the guilt or innocence of the suspect.
6. Circumstantial evidence can be in the form of physical evidence, witness testimony, or digital evidence.
7. Physical evidence includes items found at the crime scene such as fingerprints, DNA, weapons, and clothing.
8. Witness testimony is the testimony of people who were present at the time of the crime or who have knowledge of the crime.
9. Digital evidence includes emails, text messages, phone records, and other digital data that can be used to prove the guilt or innocence of the suspect.
10. In cyber forensic analytics, circumstantial evidence is used to identify the perpetrator of a cybercrime and to establish the timeline of events.
