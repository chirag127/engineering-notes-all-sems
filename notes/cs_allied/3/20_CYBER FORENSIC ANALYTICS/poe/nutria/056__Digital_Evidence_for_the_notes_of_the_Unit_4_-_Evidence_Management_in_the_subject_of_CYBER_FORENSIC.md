
### Digital Evidence for the notes of the Unit 4 - Evidence Management in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

1. Digital evidence is any type of evidence that is stored or transmitted in a digital format. This type of evidence can be used in criminal investigations, civil litigation, or other legal proceedings.

2. Digital evidence can be found on computers, mobile devices, servers, and other digital media. It includes emails, text messages, pictures, videos, documents, and other digital artifacts.

3. Digital evidence can be used to prove or disprove an allegation, identify a suspect, or corroborate witness testimony.

4. Digital evidence must be collected, preserved, and analyzed properly in order to be admissible in court.

5. Digital forensics is the process of collecting, preserving, and analyzing digital evidence. It involves the use of specialized tools and techniques to extract, analyze, and report on digital evidence.

6. Digital forensics experts are responsible for the collection, preservation, and analysis of digital evidence. They must be knowledgeable of the legal and ethical implications of their work.

7. Digital evidence can be used to investigate cybercrime, such as hacking, identity theft, and fraud. It can also be used to investigate other crimes, such as murder, kidnapping, and terrorism.

8. Digital evidence can be used to identify suspects, and to track their movements and activities. It can also be used to corroborate witness testimony and to establish timelines.

9. Digital evidence can be used to prove or disprove an allegation, or to identify a perpetrator. It can also be used to establish a link between a suspect and a crime.

10. Digital evidence must be handled properly in order to be admissible in court. This includes proper collection, preservation, and analysis.
