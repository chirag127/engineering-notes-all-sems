### Stylometric Techniques for the notes of the Unit 3 - Cyber Investigation in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

Stylometry is the study of linguistic style, including vocabulary, grammar, and syntax. It is a useful tool in Cyber Forensic Analytics to analyze text data and identify the author of the text. Here are some of the stylometric techniques that can be used to analyze the notes of the Unit 3 - Cyber Investigation in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS:

1. Word Frequency Analysis: This technique involves analyzing the frequency of words used in a text document. It is based on the assumption that each author has a unique vocabulary and writing style. By analyzing the frequency of words used in the notes of the Unit 3, it is possible to identify the author.

2. Sentence Structure Analysis: This technique involves analyzing the structure and syntax of sentences used in a text document. It is based on the assumption that each author has a unique way of constructing sentences. By analyzing the sentence structure of the notes of the Unit 3, it is possible to identify the author.

3. Part-of-Speech Tagging: This technique involves identifying the parts of speech used in a text document, such as nouns, verbs, adjectives, and adverbs. It is based on the assumption that each author has a unique way of using parts of speech. By analyzing the part-of-speech tagging of the notes of the Unit 3, it is possible to identify the author.

4. N-gram Analysis: This technique involves analyzing the frequency of n-grams, which are sequences of n words, used in a text document. It is based on the assumption that each author has a unique way of using n-grams. By analyzing the n-gram frequency of the notes of the Unit 3, it is possible to identify the author.

5. Authorship Attribution: This technique involves comparing the stylometric features of the notes of the Unit 3 with the stylometric features of known authors. It is based on the assumption that each author has a unique writing style that can be compared to other authors. By comparing the stylometric features of the notes of the Unit 3 with known authors, it is possible to identify the author.

In conclusion, stylometric techniques are useful tools in Cyber Forensic Analytics to analyze text data and identify the author of a text document. By using these techniques to analyze the notes of the Unit 3 - Cyber Investigation, it is possible to identify the author and gather important evidence for a cyber investigation.
