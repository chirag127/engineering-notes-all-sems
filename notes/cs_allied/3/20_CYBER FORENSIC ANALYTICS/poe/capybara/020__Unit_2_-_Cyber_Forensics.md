## Unit 2 - Cyber Forensics

Cyber Forensics is the process of collecting, analyzing, and preserving electronic evidence in a manner that is suitable for presentation in a court of law. It is a critical component of any cybercrime investigation and involves the use of various techniques and tools to uncover digital evidence. Below are some of the key aspects of Cyber Forensics:

- **Data Collection:** The first step in Cyber Forensics is to collect all relevant data that may be used as evidence. This includes electronic devices, such as computers, mobile phones, and tablets, as well as any other digital storage media, such as hard drives, USB drives, and memory cards.

- **Evidence Preservation:** Once the data has been collected, it is crucial to preserve it in a manner that ensures its integrity and authenticity. This involves creating a bit-by-bit copy of the original data and storing it in a secure location to prevent any changes or modifications.

- **Data Analysis:** The next step is to analyze the data to identify any relevant information that may be used as evidence. This includes searching for deleted files, hidden data, and other information that may not be easily accessible.

- **Forensic Tools:** To aid in the collection, preservation, and analysis of data, various forensic tools are used. These tools include software that can extract data from electronic devices, as well as hardware tools that can be used to image hard drives or other storage media.

- **Chain of Custody:** A critical aspect of Cyber Forensics is maintaining a chain of custody for all evidence collected. This involves documenting the location and handling of the evidence to ensure that it can be traced back to its source and that its integrity has not been compromised.

- **Report Writing:** Finally, the results of the analysis must be documented in a report that is suitable for presentation in a court of law. The report should include a detailed description of the evidence collected, the analysis performed, and the conclusions drawn from the analysis.

In conclusion, Cyber Forensics is a crucial component of any cybercrime investigation. It involves the use of various techniques and tools to uncover digital evidence, and the evidence collected must be preserved and analyzed in a manner that ensures its integrity and authenticity. By following proper procedures and using appropriate tools, Cyber Forensics specialists can help to bring cybercriminals to justice.
