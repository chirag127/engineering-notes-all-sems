### Soft Lifting for the Notes of Unit 2 - Cyber Forensics in the Subject of Cyber Forensic Analytics

In this unit, we will discuss the concept of soft lifting in cyber forensics. Soft lifting is a form of intellectual property theft where a person or organization illegally copies or uses software without proper licensing or permission. Let's dive deeper into this topic with the following points:

- Soft lifting can be done in various ways, such as using unlicensed software, copying software from another computer, or downloading pirated software from the internet.
- Soft lifting not only violates copyright laws but can also lead to security risks as pirated software may contain malware or other harmful viruses.
- Cyber forensics experts use various techniques to detect instances of soft lifting, such as examining the metadata of software, analyzing code signatures, and comparing software versions with license agreements.
- Soft lifting can have serious legal consequences, and organizations need to ensure that they have proper licenses for all software they use.
- It is crucial for individuals and organizations to understand the importance of software licensing and the risks associated with soft lifting.

In conclusion, soft lifting is a serious offense in the world of cyber forensics, and it is essential to take preventive measures to avoid any legal and security risks associated with it. Cyber forensic analysts play a crucial role in detecting instances of soft lifting and ensuring that organizations comply with software licensing laws.
