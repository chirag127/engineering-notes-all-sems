### Pre Search Activities for the Notes of the Unit 4 - Evidence Management in the Subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

In order to effectively manage digital evidence during an investigation, it is important to carry out pre-search activities. These activities help to ensure that the evidence collected is admissible in court and can be used to support the case. Some of the pre-search activities that should be carried out include:

- **Define the scope of the investigation:** Before beginning the search for evidence, it is important to define the scope of the investigation. This will help to narrow down the search and ensure that only relevant evidence is collected.

- **Identify potential sources of evidence:** Once the scope of the investigation has been defined, the next step is to identify potential sources of evidence. This could include computers, smartphones, tablets, and other digital devices.

- **Secure the scene:** Before collecting evidence, it is important to secure the scene. This involves ensuring that no one enters or alters the scene until the evidence has been collected.

- **Document the scene:** It is important to document the scene before collecting evidence. This could include taking photographs, videos, and notes about the location of devices and other relevant information.

- **Ensure chain of custody:** Throughout the evidence collection process, it is important to ensure chain of custody. This involves documenting who has had access to the evidence and ensuring that it is stored in a secure location.

- **Use appropriate tools and techniques:** When collecting evidence, it is important to use appropriate tools and techniques. This could include using write blockers to prevent data from being altered, and using forensic software to collect and analyze data.

By carrying out these pre-search activities, investigators can ensure that the evidence collected is admissible in court and can be used to support their case.
