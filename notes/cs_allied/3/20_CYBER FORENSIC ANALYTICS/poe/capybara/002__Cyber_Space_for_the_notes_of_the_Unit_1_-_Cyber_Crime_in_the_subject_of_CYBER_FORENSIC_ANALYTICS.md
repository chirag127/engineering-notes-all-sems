### Cyber Space

Cyber space refers to the virtual world created by computer systems and networks, where individuals and organizations interact and exchange information. It has become an essential part of our daily lives, enabling us to communicate, conduct transactions, and access information globally. However, the increasing dependence on cyberspace has also led to a rise in cybercrime. In this unit, we will explore the various aspects of cybercrime and the role of cyber forensic analytics in investigating these crimes.

Here are some important points to keep in mind about cyber space:

- Cyber space is a vast network of interconnected computer systems, servers, and devices that use the internet to communicate with each other.
- It includes various platforms such as social media, e-commerce websites, online banking, and cloud computing services.
- The anonymity and global reach of cyberspace have made it an attractive target for criminals to carry out various illegal activities such as identity theft, fraud, cyberstalking, cyberbullying, and hacking.
- Cybercrime not only affects individuals but also organizations and governments, causing significant financial and reputational damage.
- Cyber forensic analytics is the process of collecting, analyzing, and presenting digital evidence in a court of law to investigate cybercrime.
- Cyber forensic analysts use various tools and techniques such as disk imaging, data recovery, network forensics, and memory analysis to extract and analyze digital evidence.
- Cyber forensic analytics plays a crucial role in identifying cybercriminals, tracing their activities, and presenting evidence that can stand up in court.
- Cyber forensic analytics requires specialized skills and knowledge in computer science, law, and criminology. 

In conclusion, cyberspace is an integral part of our lives, but it is also vulnerable to cybercrime. Understanding the various aspects of cybercrime and the role of cyber forensic analytics is essential in combating this threat and maintaining the security of our digital world.
