### CERNTin for the notes of the Unit 5 - Cyber Laws and Authorities in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

Here are the key points to remember about Cyber Laws and Authorities in the subject of Cyber Forensic Analytics:

- Cyber laws are the legal measures designed to prevent cybercrime and provide appropriate punishment for offenders.
- The primary objective of cyber laws is to safeguard internet users from cyber attacks and ensure the safe use of the internet.
- The Information Technology Act, 2000 (IT Act) is the primary law for regulating cyber activities in India.
- The IT Act provides legal recognition to electronic documents and digital signatures, and it also defines cybercrime and its punishment.
- The IT Act has provisions for the investigation of cybercrime and the appointment of cybercrime authorities.
- The National Cyber Security Policy 2013 is a policy document that outlines the framework for protecting the cyberspace in India.
- The National Cyber Security Policy aims to create a secure cyber ecosystem in the country and strengthen the regulatory framework for cyberspace.
- The Computer Emergency Response Team-India (CERT-In) is the national nodal agency for responding to cybersecurity incidents in India.
- CERT-In works under the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology and provides technical support for cybersecurity incidents.
- The Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre (I4C) is a centralized cybercrime monitoring center that deals with cybercrime-related issues.
- The I4C is responsible for the coordination of all cybercrime investigations and provides technical assistance to law enforcement agencies.

Remembering these key points will help you understand the legal framework for cyber activities in India and prepare for exams in the subject of Cyber Forensic Analytics.
