## Unit 5 - Cyber Laws and Authorities

In this unit, you will learn about the following topics related to cyber laws and authorities:

1. Introduction to Cyber Laws
    - Definition and Meaning of Cyber Laws
    - Evolution of Cyber Laws
    - Objectives of Cyber Laws
2. Types of Cyber Crimes
    - Hacking
    - Cyber Stalking
    - Identity Theft
    - Phishing
    - Cyber Bullying
    - Cyber Terrorism
3. Cyber Law Enforcement Agencies
    - National Cyber Security Coordination Center (NCSCC)
    - National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal (NCCRP)
    - Cyber Crime Investigation Cell (CCIC)
    - Cyber Appellate Tribunal (CAT)
4. The Information Technology Act, 2000
    - Objectives of the IT Act
    - Key Provisions of the IT Act
    - Cyber Offences and Penalties under the IT Act
    - Adjudication of Cyber Disputes under the IT Act
5. Digital Signatures and Electronic Records
    - Definition and Meaning of Digital Signatures
    - Legal Recognition of Digital Signatures
    - Electronic Records and their Admissibility as Evidence
6. Cyber Security and Cyber Forensics
    - Importance of Cyber Security
    - Cyber Security Measures
    - Cyber Forensics and its Role in Cyber Crime Investigation
7. International Cooperation in Cyber Crime Investigation
    - Mutual Legal Assistance Treaty (MLAT)
    - International Cooperation in Cyber Crime Investigation
    - Cyber Crime Related Conventions

By the end of this unit, you will gain knowledge and understanding of the various aspects of cyber laws and authorities, including cyber crimes, cyber law enforcement agencies, the Information Technology Act, digital signatures, cyber security, cyber forensics, and international cooperation in cyber crime investigation.
