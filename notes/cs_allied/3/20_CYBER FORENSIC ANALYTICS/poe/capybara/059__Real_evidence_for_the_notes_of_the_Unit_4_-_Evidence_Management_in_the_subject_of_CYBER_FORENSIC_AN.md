### Real evidence for the notes of the Unit 4 - Evidence Management in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

Real evidence is a crucial aspect of cyber forensic investigations. It is physical evidence that is collected and preserved in a way that maintains its integrity and authenticity. Real evidence can be found in various forms, such as computers, hard drives, mobile devices, and other electronic devices.

Here are some important points to keep in mind when dealing with real evidence in cyber forensic investigations:

- Real evidence should be collected and preserved in a way that maintains its integrity and authenticity. This means that the evidence should not be altered, damaged, or contaminated in any way during the collection and preservation process.
- Chain of custody is critical when dealing with real evidence. This refers to the documentation of the movement and handling of the evidence from the time it is collected until it is presented in court. It is important to maintain a clear and accurate chain of custody to ensure the admissibility of the evidence in court.
- Real evidence should be collected using proper techniques and tools. This includes using specialized software and hardware to collect data from electronic devices. It is important to follow best practices and guidelines to ensure that the evidence is collected in a forensically sound manner.
- Real evidence should be analyzed and interpreted by qualified professionals. This includes forensic analysts and experts who have the necessary skills and knowledge to analyze the evidence and provide accurate and reliable results.
- Real evidence can be used to prove or disprove a hypothesis or theory. It can be used to identify suspects, establish timelines, and provide other valuable information that can help in the investigation.

In conclusion, real evidence is an essential element of cyber forensic investigations. It is important to collect, preserve, analyze, and interpret real evidence using proper techniques and tools to ensure its integrity and admissibility in court. Cyber forensic analysts and experts play a critical role in this process by providing the necessary skills and knowledge to effectively manage real evidence.
