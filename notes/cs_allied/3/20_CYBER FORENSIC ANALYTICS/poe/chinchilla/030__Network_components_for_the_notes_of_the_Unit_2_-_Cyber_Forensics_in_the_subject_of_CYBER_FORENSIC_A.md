### Network Components

In the field of Cyber Forensic Analytics, understanding the various network components is crucial to identify and investigate potential cyber crimes. The following are some of the key network components that forensic analysts should be familiar with:

1. **Switches**: Switches are devices that connect multiple devices within a network. They are responsible for directing data traffic between devices within the network.

2. **Routers**: Routers are responsible for directing data traffic between different networks. They can be used to connect devices to the internet, and to connect multiple networks together.

3. **Firewalls**: Firewalls are used to protect networks from unauthorized access. They can be hardware or software-based, and act as a barrier between the internal network and the outside world.

4. **Servers**: Servers are devices that provide services to other devices within the network. They can be used for file sharing, email, web hosting, and other purposes.

5. **Workstations**: Workstations are individual devices, such as computers or laptops, that are used by users within the network.

6. **Wireless Access Points**: Wireless Access Points (WAPs) are devices that allow wireless devices to connect to a network. They are commonly used in public places, such as coffee shops and airports.

7. **Network Attached Storage**: Network Attached Storage (NAS) is a type of storage device that is connected to a network. It can be used to store data and files that are shared between devices within the network.

8. **Virtual Private Networks**: Virtual Private Networks (VPNs) are used to create a secure connection between devices over the internet. They are commonly used to allow remote workers to access the network from outside the office.

9. **Intrusion Detection Systems**: Intrusion Detection Systems (IDS) are used to detect and prevent unauthorized access to the network. They can be used to monitor network traffic and identify potential security threats.

Understanding these network components is essential for forensic analysts to be able to investigate potential cyber crimes. By knowing how these components work together, they can identify potential vulnerabilities and take steps to protect the network from cyber attacks.
