### On Scene activities for the notes of the Unit 4 - Evidence Management in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

When responding to a cybercrime incident, the first step is to secure the area and gather evidence. This process involves a series of on-scene activities that are crucial to the success of the investigation. Here are some of the key on-scene activities for evidence management in cyber forensic analytics:

- Identification of the incident: It is necessary to identify the nature and scope of the incident. This information will help in determining the resources required and the approach to be taken.

- Preservation of the scene: The primary objective is to preserve the scene to minimize the possibility of contamination or destruction of evidence. This involves securing the area, restricting access, and documenting the condition of the scene.

- Collection of evidence: Careful collection of evidence is essential to ensure that it is admissible in court. This involves taking photographs and videos, collecting physical items such as computers and storage devices, and creating forensic images of digital evidence.

- Documentation of the evidence: It is important to document the evidence thoroughly, including its location, chain of custody, and any observations made during its collection. This documentation must be accurate and complete to ensure its admissibility in court.

- Analysis of the evidence: Once the evidence has been collected and documented, it must be analyzed to determine its significance in the investigation. This includes examining digital evidence for signs of tampering, identifying potential suspects, and developing a timeline of events.

- Reporting of the evidence: Finally, the evidence must be reported in a clear and concise manner, including any findings, conclusions, and recommendations. This report must be accurate and objective, and it must comply with any legal or regulatory requirements.

In conclusion, on-scene activities are a critical component of evidence management in cyber forensic analytics. These activities require careful planning, execution, and documentation to ensure that the evidence is admissible in court and that justice is served. By following these best practices, investigators can increase their chances of success and contribute to the fight against cybercrime.
