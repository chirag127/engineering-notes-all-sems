### Undercover Techniques for the notes of the Unit 3 - Cyber Investigation in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

In cyber forensic investigations, it is often necessary to go undercover in order to gather information and evidence. Undercover techniques can be used to gain access to information that would not be available through other means. Here are some common undercover techniques that may be used in cyber investigations:

1. Social Engineering: Social engineering is the art of manipulating people into divulging sensitive information. It is a commonly used technique in cyber investigations. Social engineering can be done through various methods such as phishing, pretexting, baiting, and tailgating. In phishing, an attacker sends an email pretending to be a legitimate organization to get sensitive information. Pretexting involves creating a fake scenario to get sensitive information. Baiting involves leaving a piece of malware-infected media in a public area to see who picks it up. Tailgating involves following someone into a secured area to gain access.

2. Online Personas: Online personas are fake online profiles that are created to gather information. These profiles can be used to gather information from social media, forums, and other online platforms. Investigators can use online personas to create relationships with suspects and gather information that may not be available through other means.

3. Covert Surveillance: Covert surveillance involves monitoring suspects without their knowledge. This can be done through various methods such as physical surveillance or monitoring online activity. Physical surveillance involves following a suspect and observing their behavior. Online surveillance involves monitoring a suspect's online activity such as their browsing history, emails, and social media activity.

4. Infiltration: Infiltration involves gaining access to a network or organization. This can be done through various methods such as hacking, exploiting vulnerabilities, or using social engineering techniques. Once access is gained, investigators can gather information and evidence.

5. Sting Operations: Sting operations involve setting up a fake scenario to catch suspects. This can be done through various methods such as creating a fake website or social media profile to lure suspects. Once suspects are caught, investigators can gather evidence and information.

In conclusion, undercover techniques are an important aspect of cyber forensic investigations. These techniques can be used to gather information and evidence that may not be available through other means. However, it is important to use these techniques ethically and legally to ensure that the evidence gathered is admissible in court.
