### Stylometric Techniques for the notes of the Unit 3 - Cyber Investigation in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

Stylometry is a technique that uses statistical analysis of linguistic and writing style features to identify the authorship of a document. It is commonly used in the field of cyber forensic analytics to determine the authorship of anonymous or disputed documents. Here are some important stylometric techniques used in cyber investigations:

1. **Word Frequency Analysis**: This technique involves analyzing the frequency of specific words or groups of words used in a document. The analysis can reveal the writing style, tone, and vocabulary of the author. It can also be used to compare the writing styles of different authors.

2. **N-gram Analysis**: This technique involves analyzing the frequency of sequences of words of length n in a document. By comparing the n-gram frequencies of different documents, it is possible to identify patterns that can help determine authorship.

3. **Part-of-Speech (POS) Tagging**: This technique involves tagging each word in a document with its part of speech (e.g. noun, verb, adjective, etc.). By comparing the POS tags of different documents, it is possible to identify similarities and differences in writing style that can help determine authorship.

4. **Syntactic Analysis**: This technique involves analyzing the structure of sentences in a document. By comparing the syntactic structures of different documents, it is possible to identify patterns that can help determine authorship.

5. **Machine Learning Techniques**: Machine learning techniques such as clustering and classification can be used to identify patterns in large datasets of documents. These techniques can help identify similarities and differences in writing style that can help determine authorship.

6. **Authorship Attribution Software**: There are several software programs available that use stylometric techniques to determine authorship. These programs can analyze large datasets of documents and provide statistical analysis of writing style features to identify the authorship of a document.

In conclusion, stylometric techniques are an important tool in cyber forensic investigations. By analyzing writing style features, it is possible to identify the authorship of anonymous or disputed documents. The techniques discussed above are just a few examples of the many techniques available to cyber forensic investigators. It is important to use a combination of techniques to ensure accurate and reliable authorship attribution.
