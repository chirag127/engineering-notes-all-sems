### Concepts of Investigation for the notes of the Unit 3 - Cyber Investigation in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

In Cyber Forensic Analytics, conducting an effective investigation is crucial to obtain evidence and identify the perpetrators. The following concepts of investigation are essential to ensure a successful outcome:

1. **Preservation of Evidence:** The first step in any investigation is to preserve the evidence. It involves securing the digital devices involved in the incident and preventing any changes to the data. This ensures that the data is admissible in court and can be used to prove the guilt of the perpetrator.

2. **Chain of Custody:** Chain of custody refers to the documentation of the movement and handling of evidence from the time it is collected until its presentation in court. It is important to maintain the chain of custody to ensure that the evidence has not been tampered with or altered.

3. **Search and Seizure:** Search and seizure involve the authorized search and collection of evidence from the scene of the incident. It is important to follow the legal procedures while conducting a search and seizure to ensure that the evidence is admissible in court.

4. **Data Acquisition:** Data acquisition involves the collection of data from digital devices. It is important to use forensically sound methods to acquire the data to ensure that the integrity of the data is preserved.

5. **Data Analysis:** Data analysis involves the examination of the collected data to identify relevant information. This can involve the use of specialized tools and techniques to identify patterns and anomalies in the data.

6. **Reporting:** Reporting involves documenting the findings of the investigation in a formal report. The report should include a summary of the incident, the evidence collected, the analysis conducted, and the conclusions drawn from the investigation.

7. **Expert Testimony:** Expert testimony involves presenting the findings of the investigation in court. It is important to have an expert witness who is knowledgeable in the field of cyber forensic analytics and can testify about the evidence collected and the analysis conducted.

In conclusion, the above concepts of investigation are essential to conducting an effective cyber investigation. It is important to follow the legal procedures and use forensically sound methods to ensure that the evidence collected is admissible in court. The analysis of the data should be conducted using specialized tools and techniques, and the findings should be presented in a formal report and expert testimony.
