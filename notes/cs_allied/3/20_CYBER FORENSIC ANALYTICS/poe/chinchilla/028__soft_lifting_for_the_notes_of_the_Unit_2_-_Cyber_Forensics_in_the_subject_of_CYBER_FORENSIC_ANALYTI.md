### Soft Lifting for the Notes of Unit 2 - Cyber Forensics in the Subject of Cyber Forensic Analytics

In this unit, you will learn about the concept of soft lifting, which is a type of cybercrime that involves the theft of proprietary or confidential information through non-technical means. Here are some important points to remember:

- Soft lifting involves the theft of information through means other than hacking or other technical methods. This can include stealing physical documents, copying files from unsecured computers, or using social engineering tactics to obtain sensitive information.
- Soft lifting can be difficult to detect, as it often involves individuals who are authorized to access the information they are stealing. This can make it more challenging for forensic investigators to determine who is responsible for the theft.
- Some common types of soft lifting include insider trading, corporate espionage, and theft of intellectual property. These crimes can have serious consequences for businesses, including financial losses and damage to their reputation.
- To prevent soft lifting, organizations can implement a number of security measures, such as restricting access to sensitive information, monitoring employee behavior, and conducting regular security audits.
- When investigating a suspected case of soft lifting, forensic analysts may use a variety of techniques, including examining physical evidence, analyzing computer logs, and conducting interviews with employees or other individuals who may have knowledge of the crime.
- It is important for organizations to have clear policies in place regarding the handling of sensitive information, as well as procedures for reporting suspected security breaches. This can help to prevent soft lifting and other types of cybercrime.

By understanding the concept of soft lifting and the tactics used by cyber criminals to obtain sensitive information, you can better protect yourself and your organization from these types of threats. Be sure to implement strong security measures and follow best practices for handling confidential information to minimize the risk of becoming a victim of soft lifting.
