### Steganography for the notes of the Unit 2 - Cyber Forensics in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

Steganography is a technique used to hide secret data within an ordinary, non-secret file or message. The goal of steganography is to conceal the existence of the message, so that it goes unnoticed by anyone except for the intended recipient.

Here are some important points to keep in mind when studying steganography for Cyber Forensic Analytics:

- Steganography is often used in conjunction with cryptography, which is the practice of encoding messages to keep them secret. Cryptography can be used to encrypt the message before it is hidden inside a file using steganography.

- Steganography can be used to hide information in various types of files, including images, audio files, and video files. The hidden information can be in the form of text, images, or other types of data.

- Steganography can be used for both legitimate and illegitimate purposes. For example, businesses may use steganography to protect sensitive information from competitors, while criminals may use it to hide information related to illegal activities.

- There are various steganography techniques that can be used to hide information within files. Some of the most common techniques include LSB (Least Significant Bit), DCT (Discrete Cosine Transform), and Spread Spectrum.

- Detecting the use of steganography can be a challenging task for digital forensics investigators. However, there are various tools and techniques available that can help identify the presence of hidden information within files.

- It is important for digital forensics investigators to be familiar with steganography and its various techniques in order to effectively investigate cases involving hidden information. This includes understanding the different types of steganography and the tools available for detecting it.

- Finally, it is important to note that steganography is not foolproof and can be detected if the appropriate tools and techniques are used. Therefore, it should not be relied upon as the sole means of keeping information secret. Instead, it should be used in conjunction with other security measures, such as encryption and access controls.

In conclusion, steganography is an important technique for hiding information within files, but it is not without its limitations. By understanding the various steganography techniques and the tools available for detecting it, digital forensics investigators can effectively investigate cases involving hidden information.
