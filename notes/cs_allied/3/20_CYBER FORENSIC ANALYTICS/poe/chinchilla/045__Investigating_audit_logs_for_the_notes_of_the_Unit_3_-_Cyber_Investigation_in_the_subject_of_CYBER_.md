### Investigating Audit Logs for the Notes of Unit 3 - Cyber Investigation in the Subject of Cyber Forensic Analytics

In cyber forensic investigations, audit logs are an essential source of information. Audit logs keep a record of all the activities that are performed on a system, network, or application. Audit logs are helpful in investigating cybercrime incidents and identifying the responsible party. In Unit 3 of the Cyber Forensic Analytics subject, we will learn how to investigate audit logs to gather evidence for cybercrime incidents. The following are some points that will help you understand the concept of investigating audit logs:

1. What are Audit Logs?
   - Audit logs are records of all the activities performed on a system, network, or application.
   - Audit logs contain information such as who accessed the system, what actions were performed, and when those actions were performed.
   - Audit logs are used for security and compliance purposes.

2. Why are Audit Logs Important in Cyber Forensic Investigations?
   - Audit logs can provide evidence of cybercrime incidents.
   - Audit logs can help identify the responsible party for a cybercrime incident.
   - Audit logs can be used to determine the scope of a cybercrime incident.

3. Where to Find Audit Logs?
   - Audit logs can be found on various devices such as servers, firewalls, routers, and workstations.
   - Audit logs can also be found in applications such as databases, email servers, and web servers.

4. How to Investigate Audit Logs?
   - The first step in investigating audit logs is to identify the relevant logs for the investigation.
   - The investigator must analyze the logs to identify any suspicious activity.
   - The investigator must correlate the information in the audit logs with other sources of information to build a complete picture of the incident.
   - The investigator must document their findings and preserve the audit logs as evidence.

5. Challenges in Investigating Audit Logs
   - Audit logs can be voluminous, making it difficult to identify relevant information.
   - Audit logs can be tampered with or deleted, making it difficult to determine the accuracy of the logs.
   - Audit logs may not provide complete information, making it difficult to build a complete picture of an incident.

In conclusion, audit logs are an essential source of information in cyber forensic investigations. Understanding how to investigate audit logs is critical for any cyber forensic investigator. By following the points mentioned above, you will have a better understanding of how to investigate audit logs for cybercrime incidents.
