### Cyber Weapons

In the world of cyber crime, attackers use a variety of tools and techniques to carry out their nefarious activities. These tools are often referred to as "cyber weapons" and can range from simple malware to sophisticated hacking tools. In this unit, we will explore some of the most common cyber weapons used by attackers.

Here are some of the most common cyber weapons:

1. Malware - Malware is a type of software that is designed to harm or exploit a computer system. Malware can take many forms, including viruses, worms, Trojans, and ransomware. Once installed on a victim's computer, malware can steal sensitive information, lock down files, or even take control of the entire system.

2. Phishing - Phishing is a technique used by attackers to trick victims into revealing sensitive information, such as passwords or credit card numbers. Phishing attacks typically involve the use of fake emails or websites that appear to be legitimate. Once the victim enters their information, the attacker can use it for their own purposes.

3. DDoS attacks - A DDoS (Distributed Denial of Service) attack is a type of cyber attack that floods a website or server with traffic, causing it to crash. DDoS attacks are often carried out using a network of infected computers, known as a botnet. Attackers can use DDoS attacks to extort money from businesses or to silence dissenting voices online.

4. Exploits - Exploits are vulnerabilities in software or hardware that can be exploited by attackers to gain access to a system. Attackers can use exploits to install malware, steal data, or take control of a system. Exploits can be found in a wide range of software, from web browsers to operating systems.

5. Password cracking - Password cracking is the process of guessing or cracking a password in order to gain access to a system. Attackers can use a variety of techniques to crack passwords, including brute force attacks and dictionary attacks. Once an attacker has gained access to a system, they can use it for their own purposes or sell access to other hackers on the dark web.

In conclusion, understanding the various cyber weapons used by attackers is an important part of cyber forensic analytics. By understanding these tools and techniques, forensic analysts can better identify and track cyber criminals, helping to prevent future attacks.
