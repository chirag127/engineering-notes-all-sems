Cyber extortion is a type of cybercrime in which an attacker threatens to harm an individual or organization unless a ransom is paid. Cyber extortion can take many forms, including:

1. Ransomware: This is a type of malware that encrypts the victim's data and demands a ransom in exchange for the decryption key.

2. DDoS attacks: This is a type of attack in which the attacker floods the victim's network with traffic, making it unavailable to users. The attacker may then demand a ransom in exchange for stopping the attack.

3. Email extortion: This is a type of cyber extortion in which the attacker threatens to harm the victim or release sensitive information unless a ransom is paid.

Cyber extortion can have serious consequences for individuals and organizations, including financial losses, loss of sensitive information, and damage to reputation.
