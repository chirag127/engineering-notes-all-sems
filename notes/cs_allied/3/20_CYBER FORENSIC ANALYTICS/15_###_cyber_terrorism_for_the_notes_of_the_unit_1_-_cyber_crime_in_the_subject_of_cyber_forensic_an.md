### Cyber terrorism for the notes of the Unit 1 - Cyber Crime in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

Cyber terrorism refers to the use of digital technologies and the Internet to carry out acts of terrorism. This type of crime is becoming increasingly prevalent as the use of technology and the Internet continues to grow, and it poses a significant threat to both individuals and organizations.

In the context of the Unit 1 - Cyber Crime in the subject of Cyber Forensic Analytics, it is important to understand the nature and scope of cyber terrorism, as well as the methods and tactics used by cyber terrorists. This may include understanding the motivations and goals of cyber terrorists, the types of targets they typically choose, and the types of attacks they may use, such as malware, phishing, and DDoS attacks.

It is also important to be familiar with the tools and techniques used by cyber forensic analysts to detect, prevent, and respond to cyber terrorism. This may include understanding the principles of digital forensics, the use of digital evidence, and the use of data analytics and machine learning algorithms to detect and respond to cyber attacks.

In the context of cyber terrorism, it is also important to understand the legal and ethical issues that arise, such as the need for privacy and security, the need for cooperation between law enforcement and technology companies, and the need for international cooperation in the fight against cyber terrorism.

Overall, cyber terrorism is a complex and evolving threat that requires a multi-disciplinary approach, including the use of technology, data analytics, and legal and ethical considerations. Understanding the nature and scope of cyber terrorism and the tools and techniques used by cyber forensic analysts is critical to the effective and ethical practice of cyber security and cyber forensics.
