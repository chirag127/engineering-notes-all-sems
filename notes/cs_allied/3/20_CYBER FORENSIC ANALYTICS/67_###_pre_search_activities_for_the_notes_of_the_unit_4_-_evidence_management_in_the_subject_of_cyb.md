### pre search activities for the notes of the Unit 4 - Evidence Management in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

Pre-search activities refer to the actions and procedures that are performed before conducting a search in the context of a cyber forensic investigation. Pre-search activities are critical to the success of a cyber forensic investigation, as they help to ensure that the search is conducted in a manner that is legal, ethical, and consistent with best practices in the field of cyber forensics.

In the context of cyber forensic analytics, pre-search activities typically include the following steps:

1. Planning: Planning is the process of defining the scope and objectives of the search, and of determining the resources and personnel that will be required to conduct the search. Planning is an important step in pre-search activities, as it helps to ensure that the search is conducted in an organized and efficient manner.

2. Legal compliance: Legal compliance is the process of ensuring that the search is conducted in accordance with applicable laws and regulations, including privacy laws, data protection laws, and electronic discovery laws. Legal compliance is an important step in pre-search activities, as it helps to ensure that the evidence obtained during the search is admissible in court.

3. Evidence preservation: Evidence preservation is the process of ensuring that the evidence is not altered or destroyed during the search. Evidence preservation is an important step in pre-search activities, as it helps to ensure that the evidence obtained during the search is reliable and can be used to support conclusions in the investigation.

4. Preparation of the search team: Preparation of the search team is the process of ensuring that the personnel who will be conducting the search are properly trained and equipped to perform their duties. This may include training in the use of forensic tools and techniques, as well as the provision of specialized equipment and software.

Overall, pre-search activities are an important aspect of cyber forensic analytics, and are critical to the success of a cyber forensic investigation. Understanding the role of pre-search activities and the steps involved in pre-search activities is an important aspect of the study of Cyber Forensic Analytics.
