Investigating computer intrusions is a critical aspect of cyber forensics, as it involves uncovering the cause and identifying the responsible parties for security breaches in computer systems. Computer intrusions can take many forms, including hacking, malware infections, and unauthorized access to computer systems. The process of investigating computer intrusions typically involves several steps, including:

1. Evidence collection: Collecting and preserving evidence related to the intrusion, including digital evidence such as computer systems, networks, and data storage devices.

2. Evidence analysis: Analyzing the evidence in order to determine the cause of the intrusion and to identify the responsible parties.

3. Interviews: Interviewing witnesses and suspects in order to gather additional information and to build a case.

4. Legal procedures: Following legal procedures and guidelines in order to ensure that the evidence is admissible in court.

5. Report writing: Writing a comprehensive report that summarizes the findings of the investigation and provides recommendations for preventing future intrusions.
