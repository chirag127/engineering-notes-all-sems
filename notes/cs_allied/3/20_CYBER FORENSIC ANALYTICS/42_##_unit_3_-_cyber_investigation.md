## Unit 3 - Cyber Investigation

Sure, here's a detailed explanation of Cyber Investigation:

Cyber Investigation is a branch of digital forensics that involves the collection, analysis, and preservation of digital evidence in order to investigate cybercrime and other related incidents. Cyber investigations can be used to identify the source of a cyberattack, determine the extent of the damage, and gather evidence that can be used in court.

Cyber investigations typically involve the use of specialized tools and techniques to collect and analyze digital evidence, including:

- Data collection: This involves the collection of digital evidence from various sources, such as computers, servers, mobile devices, and cloud services. This can be done through the use of forensic imaging, data extraction, and other techniques.

- Analysis: This involves the examination of the collected digital evidence to identify patterns, relationships, and other relevant information. This can be done through the use of various tools and techniques, such as network analysis, file system analysis, and memory analysis.

- Preservation: This involves the preservation of the digital evidence in a manner that ensures its integrity and authenticity, so that it can be used as evidence in court. This can be done through the use of digital signatures, hashing, and other techniques.

Cyber investigations can be complex and time-consuming, and often require specialized skills and knowledge. As such, they are typically carried out by trained professionals, such as digital forensics experts, law enforcement agencies, and private investigators.

In conclusion, Cyber Investigation is a branch of digital forensics that involves the collection, analysis, and preservation of digital evidence in order to investigate cybercrime and other related incidents. Cyber investigations typically involve the use of specialized tools and techniques to collect and analyze digital evidence, and are carried out by trained professionals with specialized skills and knowledge.
