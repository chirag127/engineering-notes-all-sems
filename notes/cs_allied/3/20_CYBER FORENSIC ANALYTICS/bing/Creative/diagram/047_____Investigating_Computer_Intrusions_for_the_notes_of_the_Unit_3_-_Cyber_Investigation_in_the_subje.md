### Investigating Computer Intrusions

- A computer intrusion is an unauthorized or illegal access or use of a computer system or network by an attacker.
- A computer intrusion investigation is a process of collecting, analyzing and presenting digital evidence related to a computer intrusion incident.
- The main objectives of a computer intrusion investigation are to:
  - Identify the source and extent of the intrusion
  - Determine the impact and damage caused by the intrusion
  - Recover the compromised data and systems
  - Prevent or mitigate future intrusions
  - Prosecute or deter the intruders
- The main steps of a computer intrusion investigation are:
  - Preparation: Establish the scope, objectives, resources and legal authority of the investigation. Define the roles and responsibilities of the investigation team. Gather the necessary tools and equipment. Secure the cooperation and consent of the affected parties.
  - Identification: Identify the signs and symptoms of the intrusion, such as unusual network activity, system anomalies, unauthorized access, data theft or modification, etc. Use intrusion detection systems, logs, alerts, reports and other sources of information to confirm the occurrence and timing of the intrusion.
  - Preservation: Isolate and protect the affected systems and networks from further damage or tampering. Make forensic copies of the relevant data and devices. Document and label the evidence and maintain a chain of custody. Use cryptographic hashes to verify the integrity of the evidence.
  - Analysis: Examine the evidence and reconstruct the events and actions of the intruders. Use forensic tools and techniques to recover deleted, hidden or encrypted data. Identify the methods, tools and vulnerabilities used by the intruders. Trace the origin and destination of the intrusion. Determine the motive, identity and affiliation of the intruders. Assess the impact and severity of the intrusion.
  - Presentation: Prepare and present the findings and conclusions of the investigation in a clear, concise and accurate manner. Use charts, graphs, tables, timelines and other visual aids to illustrate the evidence and support the arguments. Follow the legal and ethical standards and guidelines for reporting and testifying. Provide recommendations and suggestions for improving the security and resilience of the systems and networks.
  - Follow-up: Evaluate the effectiveness and efficiency of the investigation. Identify the lessons learned and best practices. Implement the corrective and preventive measures. Monitor and audit the systems and networks for any signs of recurrence or new intrusions. Update the policies and procedures for computer intrusion investigation.
