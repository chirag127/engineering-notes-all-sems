### Cyber weapons

- Cyber weapons are a piece of computer software or hardware used to commit cyberwarfare.
- Cyberwarfare is the use of cyberattacks to damage, disrupt, or destroy another nation's computers or information networks.
- Cyberattacks are malicious actions that exploit the vulnerabilities of computer systems, networks, or devices to compromise their confidentiality, integrity, or availability.
- Cyber weapons can be classified into two types: **kinetic** and **non-kinetic**.
  - Kinetic cyber weapons are those that cause physical damage or destruction to the target, such as Stuxnet, a computer worm that sabotaged Iran's nuclear centrifuges.
  - Non-kinetic cyber weapons are those that cause functional or mental harm to the target, such as ransomware, a type of malware that encrypts the victim's data and demands payment for decryption.
- Cyber weapons can have various objectives, such as espionage, sabotage, propaganda, coercion, or deterrence.
- Cyber weapons can also have various effects, such as denial of service, data theft, data manipulation, data destruction, or system hijacking.
- Cyber weapons can be employed by various actors, such as nation-states, terrorist groups, criminal organizations, hacktivists, or lone wolves.
- Cyber weapons pose various challenges and risks, such as attribution, escalation, proliferation, collateral damage, or ethical dilemmas.
