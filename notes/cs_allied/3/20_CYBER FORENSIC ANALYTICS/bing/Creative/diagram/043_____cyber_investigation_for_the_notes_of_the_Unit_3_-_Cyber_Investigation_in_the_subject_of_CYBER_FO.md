### Cyber Investigation

Cyber investigation is the process of investigating, analyzing, and recovering forensic data for digital evidence of a crime. Cyber investigation can be used to track criminals via the computer, such as in cases of computer crimes, identity theft, cyberstalking, or cyberterrorism . Cyber investigation can also be used to track records of criminals using computer forensics, such as in cases of fraud, money laundering, or homicide .

Some of the steps involved in a cyber investigation are:

- Identifying the type and scope of the cyber crime, such as hacking, phishing, malware, ransomware, denial-of-service, or extortion .
- Collecting and preserving digital evidence from various sources, such as computers, mobile devices, networks, servers, cloud services, or social media platforms .
- Analyzing and interpreting the digital evidence using various tools and techniques, such as encryption, decryption, hashing, data carving, or steganography .
- Reporting and presenting the findings and conclusions of the cyber investigation to the relevant authorities, such as law enforcement, prosecutors, or courts .

Some of the challenges and issues faced in a cyber investigation are:

- The dynamic and volatile nature of digital evidence, which can be easily altered, deleted, or overwritten .
- The legal and ethical implications of accessing, acquiring, and disclosing digital evidence, which may involve privacy, consent, jurisdiction, or chain of custody issues .
- The technical and operational difficulties of dealing with large volumes of data, complex systems, or sophisticated adversaries, who may use encryption, obfuscation, or countermeasures to evade detection or attribution .

Some of the skills and competencies required for a cyber investigator are:

- A strong background and knowledge in computer science, information technology, or cybersecurity, as well as in criminal justice, law, or criminology .
- A high level of analytical, logical, and problem-solving abilities, as well as attention to detail, accuracy, and objectivity .
- A good understanding and awareness of the current trends, threats, and best practices in cyber crime and cyber security, as well as the relevant laws, policies, and standards .
- A proficient and effective communication and collaboration skills, as well as the ability to work under pressure, adapt to changing situations, and handle confidential and sensitive information .
