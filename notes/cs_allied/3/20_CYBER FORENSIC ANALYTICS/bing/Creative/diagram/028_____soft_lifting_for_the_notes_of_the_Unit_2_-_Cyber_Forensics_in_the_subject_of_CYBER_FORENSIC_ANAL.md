### Softlifting

Softlifting is a type of software piracy that involves installing or copying a legally purchased software program onto more than one computer, in violation of the licensing agreement  . Softlifting does not involve selling copies of the software for profit, but rather providing the program to multiple users. Softlifting is also known as softloading or end-user piracy.

Some points to note about softlifting are:

- Softlifting is the most common type of software piracy.
- Softlifting often occurs in business or school environments, where one copy of the software is purchased and then installed on multiple computers to save money.
- Softlifting can also occur when a person lends or shares a software CD or DVD with others, or uploads or downloads the software from the internet.
- Softlifting can have negative consequences for the software developers, such as loss of revenue, reduced innovation, and increased costs .
- Softlifting can also expose the users to legal risks, such as fines, lawsuits, or criminal charges .
- Softlifting can also compromise the security and performance of the software, as the users may not receive updates, patches, or technical support .

Some ways to prevent or detect softlifting are:

- Reading and following the terms and conditions of the software license agreement .
- Using software asset management (SAM) tools to monitor and audit the software usage and compliance .
- Educating the users about the ethical and legal implications of software piracy .
- Reporting any suspected cases of software piracy to the appropriate authorities or organizations .
