# Evidence Management for the notes of the Unit 4 - Evidence Management in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

- Evidence management is the process of protecting, preserving, analyzing and presenting digital evidence in a cyber forensic investigation .
- Digital evidence is any information stored or transmitted in digital form that can be used to support or refute a hypothesis in a criminal or civil case .
- Cyber forensic analytics is the application of data analysis techniques and tools to identify, extract, interpret and present digital evidence in a cyber forensic investigation .
- The main steps of evidence management in cyber forensic analytics are   :
  - Identification: finding and selecting potential sources of digital evidence relevant to the case.
  - Collection: acquiring and documenting digital evidence from various devices and networks using appropriate methods and tools.
  - Preservation: securing and maintaining the integrity and authenticity of digital evidence using proper storage, handling and backup procedures.
  - Analysis: examining and interpreting digital evidence using various techniques and tools to extract relevant information and insights.
  - Presentation: reporting and communicating the findings and conclusions of the analysis in a clear, concise and accurate manner that can be understood by the intended audience.
- The main challenges of evidence management in cyber forensic analytics are   :
  - Volume: dealing with the large and increasing amount of digital data that can be relevant to a case.
  - Variety: handling the diverse and complex types and formats of digital data that can be encountered in a case.
  - Velocity: responding to the dynamic and fast-changing nature of digital data and cyber threats that can affect a case.
  - Veracity: ensuring the reliability and validity of digital data and analysis results that can be affected by errors, tampering, obfuscation and encryption.
  - Value: extracting and presenting the most relevant and useful information and insights from digital data that can support or refute a case.
