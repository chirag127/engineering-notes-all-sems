# Phreaking

- Phreaking is a slang term coined to describe the activity of a culture of people who study, experiment with, or explore telecommunication systems, such as equipment and systems connected to public telephone networks .
- Phreaking involves fraudulent manipulation of telephone signaling in order to make free phone calls or to access restricted services .
- Phreaking originated in the 1960s and 1970s, when phreaks used devices such as whistles or electronic boxes to generate specific tones that could trigger the automatic switches of the phone network .
- Phreaking was also motivated by curiosity, exploration, and the challenge of understanding and manipulating complex systems .
- Phreaking techniques have evolved over time, as phone companies have upgraded their security measures and switched to digital systems .
- Some modern phreaking methods include:
  - Caller ID spoofing: altering the caller ID information to disguise the origin of a call .
  - Voice over IP (VoIP) hacking: exploiting vulnerabilities in VoIP protocols or software to make free or cheap calls, eavesdrop, or launch denial-of-service attacks .
  - Cellular phone cloning: copying the identity of a legitimate phone to make unauthorized calls .
  - SIM swapping: tricking a phone company into transferring the phone number and service of a victim to a new SIM card controlled by the attacker .
- Phreaking is considered a form of cybercrime, as it violates the laws and regulations governing telecommunication systems and services .
- Phreaking can cause financial losses, privacy breaches, service disruptions, and security risks for phone companies, customers, and other entities .
- Phreaking can be detected and prevented by various methods, such as encryption, authentication, verification, monitoring, and auditing of telecommunication systems and services .
