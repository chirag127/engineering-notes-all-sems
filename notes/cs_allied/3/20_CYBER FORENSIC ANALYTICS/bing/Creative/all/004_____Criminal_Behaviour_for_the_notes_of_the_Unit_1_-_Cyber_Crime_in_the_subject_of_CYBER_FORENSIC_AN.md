# Criminal Behaviour

## Unit 1 - Cyber Crime

### Definition of Cyber Crime

- Cyber crime is the term used to describe criminal activities carried out by means of computers or the internet .
- Cyber crime can involve various types of illegal acts, such as fraud, theft, hacking, cyberstalking, cyberbullying, phishing, identity theft, child pornography, cyberterrorism, and more .
- Cyber crime can target individuals, corporations, or governments, and can affect their information, privacy, reputation, finances, or security .

### Types of Cyber Crime

- Cyber crime can be classified into two main categories: cyber-dependent and cyber-enabled crimes.
- Cyber-dependent crimes are those that can only be committed using computers or the internet, such as hacking, denial-of-service attacks, malware, ransomware, etc.
- Cyber-enabled crimes are those that can be committed offline as well, but are facilitated or amplified by the use of computers or the internet, such as fraud, identity theft, cyberstalking, cyberbullying, etc.

### Motives of Cyber Crime

- Cyber crime can be motivated by various factors, such as financial gain, personal revenge, ideological beliefs, political agenda, thrill-seeking, curiosity, boredom, or psychological disorders .
- Cyber criminals can be individuals, groups, or organizations, and can operate from anywhere in the world, making it difficult to trace and prosecute them .
- Cyber criminals can use various techniques and tools to carry out their attacks, such as phishing, social engineering, malware, encryption, anonymization, botnets, etc .

### Impacts of Cyber Crime

- Cyber crime can have serious and widespread impacts on individuals, businesses, and society, such as :
  - Loss of money, data, or intellectual property
  - Damage to reputation, trust, or confidence
  - Compromise of privacy, security, or safety
  - Disruption of services, operations, or infrastructure
  - Harm to physical or mental health
  - Influence on public opinion, elections, or policies
  - Threat to national or international security or stability

### Prevention and Response to Cyber Crime

- Cyber crime can be prevented and responded to by various measures, such as :
  - Raising awareness and education among users and organizations
  - Implementing strong security policies and practices
  - Using reliable and updated software and hardware
  - Encrypting and backing up sensitive data
  - Reporting and sharing information about cyber incidents
  - Cooperating and collaborating with law enforcement and other stakeholders
  - Developing and enforcing legal and ethical frameworks and standards
