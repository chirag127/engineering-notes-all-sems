### Wireshark for the notes of the Unit 2 - Cyber Forensics in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

- Wireshark is a free and open-source network protocol analyzer that can capture and examine packets from various network interfaces .
- Wireshark can be used for network forensics, which is the process of investigating network activities and events for evidence of malicious or unauthorized behavior  .
- Wireshark can help network forensic analysts to:
  - Monitor and troubleshoot network performance and functionality .
  - Identify and analyze network protocols, applications, and services .
  - Detect and investigate network attacks, such as denial-of-service, malware, phishing, and data exfiltration  .
  - Recover and reconstruct network data, such as files, emails, chat messages, and web pages .
  - Correlate and compare network data with other sources of evidence, such as logs, timestamps, and digital signatures .
- Wireshark has a graphical user interface (GUI) that allows users to view and filter packets in various ways, such as by source and destination addresses, ports, protocols, and content .
- Wireshark also has a command-line interface (CLI) that allows users to perform advanced tasks, such as scripting, automation, and customization .
- Wireshark supports hundreds of network protocols and formats, and can be extended with plugins and modules .
- Wireshark can work with live or offline data, and can save and export packets in various formats, such as pcap, csv, xml, and json .
- Wireshark is widely used by network professionals, security researchers, forensic examiners, and educators, and is considered an essential tool for network and cyber forensics    .
