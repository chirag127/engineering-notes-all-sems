### Cyber Investigation

Cyber investigation is the process of investigating, analyzing, and recovering forensic data for digital evidence of a crime. Cyber investigation can be used to track criminals via the computer, to investigate computer crimes, or to secure evidence from networked devices . Cyber investigation can also help to impose risk and consequences on cyber adversaries, such as criminals and nation-states, who compromise U.S. interests and security.

Some of the topics covered in cyber investigation are:

- Types of cyber crimes, such as hacking, phishing, malware, ransomware, denial-of-service attacks, extortion, identity theft, fraud, cyberstalking, cyberbullying, etc.
- Cyber crime investigation methods, such as digital forensics, network analysis, malware analysis, cryptography, etc.
- Cyber crime investigation tools, such as software, hardware, and online resources that can help to collect, preserve, analyze, and present digital evidence.
- Cyber crime investigation challenges, such as encryption, anonymity, jurisdiction, cooperation, ethics, etc.
- Cyber crime investigation case studies, such as examples of real-world cyber crimes and how they were investigated and solved.
