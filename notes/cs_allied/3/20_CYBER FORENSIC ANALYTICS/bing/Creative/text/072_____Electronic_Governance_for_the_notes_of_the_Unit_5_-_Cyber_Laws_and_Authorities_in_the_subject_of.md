### Electronic Governance

- Electronic governance or e-governance is the application of information technology for delivering government services, exchange of information, communication transactions, integration of various stand-alone systems between government to citizen (G2C), government-to-business (G2B), government-to-government (G2G), government-to-employees (G2E) as well as back-office processes and interactions within the entire governance framework .
- E-governance aims to enhance the efficiency, transparency, accountability, and participation of the public sector and to improve the quality of life of the citizens.
- E-governance can be classified into four main categories based on the type of interaction involved:
  - G2C: Government to citizen, where the government provides information and services to the citizens through various channels such as websites, portals, kiosks, mobile apps, etc.
  - G2B: Government to business, where the government facilitates the transactions and processes related to the business sector such as taxation, licensing, procurement, etc.
  - G2G: Government to government, where the government coordinates and cooperates with other government agencies at different levels such as local, state, national, or international.
  - G2E: Government to employees, where the government manages the human resources and internal functions of the public sector such as payroll, training, performance appraisal, etc.
- E-governance can also be classified into three main stages based on the level of sophistication and maturity of the ICT applications:
  - Information: The government provides basic information and data to the public through online platforms such as websites, portals, etc.
  - Interaction: The government enables two-way communication and feedback with the public through online platforms such as email, chat, forums, etc.
  - Transaction: The government enables the delivery of online services and transactions to the public through online platforms such as e-payment, e-forms, e-signature, etc.
- E-governance can bring various benefits to the public sector and the society such as :
  - Improving the accessibility, convenience, and responsiveness of the government services to the citizens and the business sector.
  - Reducing the cost, time, and corruption involved in the government processes and transactions.
  - Increasing the transparency, accountability, and trust of the government activities and performance.
  - Enhancing the participation, engagement, and empowerment of the citizens and the civil society in the decision-making and policy-making processes.
  - Promoting the innovation, collaboration, and integration of the government agencies and the stakeholders across different sectors and levels.
  - Supporting the social and economic development, inclusion, and equity of the society.
