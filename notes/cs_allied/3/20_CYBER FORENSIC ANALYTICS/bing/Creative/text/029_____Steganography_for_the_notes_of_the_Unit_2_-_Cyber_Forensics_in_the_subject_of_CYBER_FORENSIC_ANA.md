### Steganography

- Steganography is the art or practice of concealing a message, image, or file within another message, image, or file  .
- Steganography can be used along with cryptography as an extra-secure method in which to protect data.
- Steganography techniques can be applied to various types of media, such as text, image, audio, video, or network traffic  .
- Steganography can be classified into two main categories: technical steganography and linguistic steganography.
  - Technical steganography uses physical or digital means to hide the existence of a message, such as hiding a microdot in a printed document, embedding a watermark in an image, or modulating the least significant bits of an audio file.
  - Linguistic steganography uses natural language to conceal the message, such as using acrostics, ciphers, codes, or null ciphers.
- Steganography can be used for various purposes, such as protecting sensitive information, communicating covertly, enhancing security, or evading censorship .
- Steganography can also be used for malicious purposes, such as hiding malware, exfiltrating data, or spreading propaganda .
- Steganography can be detected and analyzed by using various methods, such as statistical analysis, visual inspection, steganalysis tools, or machine learning techniques .
