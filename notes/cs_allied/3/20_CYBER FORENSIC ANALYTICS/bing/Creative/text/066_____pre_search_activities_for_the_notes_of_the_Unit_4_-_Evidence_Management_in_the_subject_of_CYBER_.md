### Pre Search Activities for the Notes of the Unit 4 - Evidence Management in the Subject of Cyber Forensic Analytics

- Pre search activities are the steps that cyber forensic experts take before conducting a search for digital evidence in a cyber crime investigation.
- Pre search activities include the following tasks :
  - Identification: The first step is to identify what evidence is present, where it is stored, and in which format it is stored. This may involve interviewing the victim, the suspect, or the witnesses, examining the crime scene, or analyzing the network logs.
  - Preservation: The second step is to preserve the evidence from being altered, damaged, or destroyed. This may involve securing the physical devices, making copies or images of the data, or using write blockers or encryption tools.
  - Documentation: The third step is to document the evidence and the process of collecting it. This may involve taking photographs, notes, or videos of the devices, the data, or the crime scene, recording the chain of custody, or creating a report or a timeline of the events.
  - Analysis: The fourth step is to analyze the evidence and extract relevant information. This may involve using forensic tools, techniques, or methods to recover deleted, hidden, or encrypted data, to identify the source, origin, or author of the data, or to find patterns, anomalies, or correlations in the data.
  - Presentation: The fifth step is to present the evidence and the findings to the court, the client, or the stakeholders. This may involve preparing a summary, a report, or a testimony of the analysis, using charts, graphs, or visualizations to illustrate the results, or explaining the technical terms, concepts, or procedures in a clear and concise manner.
- Pre search activities are important for the following reasons :
  - They help to ensure the integrity, reliability, and admissibility of the evidence in the court of law.
  - They help to prevent the loss, contamination, or tampering of the evidence during the search process.
  - They help to establish the credibility, professionalism, and accountability of the cyber forensic experts.
  - They help to support the investigation, the prosecution, or the defense of the cyber crime case.
