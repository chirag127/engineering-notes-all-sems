## Unit 3 - Cyber Investigation

- Cyber investigation is the process of collecting, analyzing and presenting digital evidence from electronic devices and networks related to cybercrime or cyber incidents.
- Cyber investigation involves various steps, such as:
  - Identifying the scope and objectives of the investigation
  - Securing and preserving the digital evidence
  - Acquiring and analyzing the digital evidence
  - Reporting and presenting the findings and recommendations
- Cyber investigation requires specialized tools, techniques and skills, such as:
  - Digital forensics tools, such as EnCase, FTK, Autopsy, etc.
  - Network analysis tools, such as Wireshark, Nmap, Tcpdump, etc.
  - Malware analysis tools, such as IDA Pro, OllyDbg, Cuckoo Sandbox, etc.
  - Cryptography tools, such as Hashcat, John the Ripper, OpenSSL, etc.
  - Cyber law and ethics, such as the Computer Fraud and Abuse Act, the Electronic Communications Privacy Act, the General Data Protection Regulation, etc.
- Cyber investigation can be applied to various domains and scenarios, such as:
  - Cybercrime, such as hacking, phishing, identity theft, fraud, etc.
  - Cybersecurity, such as incident response, threat intelligence, vulnerability assessment, etc.
  - Cyberwarfare, such as cyber espionage, cyber sabotage, cyber terrorism, etc.
  - Cyberlaw, such as digital evidence admissibility, chain of custody, expert witness, etc.
