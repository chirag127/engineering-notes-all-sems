### Concepts of Investigation for the notes of the Unit 3 - Cyber Investigation in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

- Cyber forensics is a field that follows certain procedures to find the evidence to reach conclusions after proper investigation of matters.
- Cyber forensics is needed for the investigation of crime and law enforcement, especially when the computer system is the crime scene or the source of evidence.
- Cyber forensics involves the identification, preservation, extraction, analysis and presentation of digital evidence.
- Some of the common types of digital evidence are files, emails, logs, metadata, network traffic, etc.
- Some of the common techniques of cyber forensics are reverse steganography, file carving, live analysis, cross-drive analysis, etc.
- Cyber forensics must follow certain standards and guidelines to ensure the legal admissibility and reliability of the evidence.
- Cyber forensics requires skills such as technical knowledge, analytical thinking, problem-solving, communication, ethics, etc.
