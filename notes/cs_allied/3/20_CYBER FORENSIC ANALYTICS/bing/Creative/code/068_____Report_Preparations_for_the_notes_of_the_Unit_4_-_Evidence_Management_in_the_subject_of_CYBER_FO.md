### Report Preparations for the notes of the Unit 4 - Evidence Management in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

- Evidence management is the process of protecting, preserving, and documenting the digital evidence that is collected, examined, and presented in cyber forensics investigations.
- Digital evidence is any information stored or transmitted in binary form that may be relevant, reliable, and admissible in court. It can be found on various electronic devices, such as computers, mobile phones, smart devices, cloud servers, etc .
- Evidence management involves the following steps:
  - Identification: locating and recognizing the potential sources and types of digital evidence in a crime scene or a network.
  - Collection: acquiring the digital evidence using appropriate tools and techniques, such as imaging, hashing, logging, etc.
  - Preservation: ensuring the integrity, authenticity, and continuity of the digital evidence by preventing any alteration, loss, or damage. This includes using write blockers, encryption, tamper-proof seals, etc.
  - Analysis: extracting, interpreting, and correlating the digital evidence to reconstruct the events and activities related to the investigation. This includes using various software tools, such as hex editors, file viewers, data carving, etc.
  - Presentation: reporting and presenting the digital evidence and the analysis results in a clear, concise, and accurate manner to the relevant stakeholders, such as law enforcement, prosecutors, judges, etc. This includes using charts, graphs, tables, etc.
  - Disposition: storing, transferring, or disposing of the digital evidence according to the legal and ethical requirements and standards. This includes using secure storage facilities, chain of custody forms, destruction certificates, etc.
- Evidence management requires following the best practices and guidelines of the cyber forensics field, such as the NIST Special Publication 800-86, the ISO/IEC 27037, and the SWGDE.
- Evidence management is a critical facet of the cyber forensics field, as it ensures the validity, reliability, and admissibility of the digital evidence in court and helps to solve cyber crimes and recover important data .
