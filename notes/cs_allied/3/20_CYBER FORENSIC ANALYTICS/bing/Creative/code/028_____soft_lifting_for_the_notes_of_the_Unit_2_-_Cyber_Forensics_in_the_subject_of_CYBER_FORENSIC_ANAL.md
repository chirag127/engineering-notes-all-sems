# Soft Lifting

Soft lifting is a type of software piracy that involves installing or copying a legally purchased software program onto more than one computer, in violation of the software license agreement   . Soft lifting is also known as softloading or end-user piracy. Soft lifting does not involve selling copies of the software, but rather providing the software to multiple users, usually to save money. Soft lifting is the most common type of software piracy.

Some points to note about soft lifting are:

- Soft lifting is illegal and unethical, as it deprives the software developers of their rightful revenue and intellectual property rights.
- Soft lifting can also expose the users to security risks, as the software may not receive updates, patches, or technical support from the original vendor.
- Soft lifting can be detected and prevented by various methods, such as using software activation, digital rights management, license management, or anti-piracy software.
- Soft lifting can be prosecuted under civil or criminal law, depending on the jurisdiction and the severity of the infringement. The penalties may include fines, damages, injunctions, or imprisonment.
