### Hard Disk Forensics

- Hard disk forensics is the process of extracting actionable information from computer storage devices, such as hard disk drives, to be presented as evidence in criminal proceedings .
- Hard disk forensics often entails fetching information that was deleted or damaged and later on, reconstructing the same. This can be done by using specialized software tools that can recover data from various file systems, partitions, and sectors .
- Hard disk forensics can also analyze the metadata of files, such as timestamps, file names, and attributes, to determine the origin, history, and ownership of the data.
- Hard disk forensics can help to identify digital evidence, such as documents, images, videos, emails, logs, and malware, that can prove or disprove the involvement of a suspect in a cybercrime.
- Hard disk forensics can also help to assess the extent of damages inflicted by a cyberattack, such as data theft, data corruption, or data destruction.
- Hard disk forensics requires proper handling and preservation of the storage devices to avoid tampering, contamination, or loss of evidence. This includes following the chain of custody, documenting the procedures, and using write blockers to prevent accidental modification of the data.
