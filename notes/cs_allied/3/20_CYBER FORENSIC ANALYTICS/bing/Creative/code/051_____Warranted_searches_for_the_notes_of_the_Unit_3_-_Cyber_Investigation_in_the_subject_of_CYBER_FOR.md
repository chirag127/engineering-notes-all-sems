### Warranted searches for the notes of the Unit 3 - Cyber Investigation in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

- A warranted search is a search authorized by a court order based on probable cause that the target of the search contains or is related to a crime .
- A cyber investigation is the process of investigating, analyzing, and recovering forensic data for digital evidence of a crime.
- A warranted search for a cyber investigation may involve searching a computer or electronic media, such as a cellphone, a video game console, or a cloud storage service .
- Some of the legal considerations for a warranted search for a cyber investigation are :
  - Particularity: The search warrant must particularly describe the place to be searched and the things to be seized, such as the type of device, the location of the device, the data to be searched, and the crime to be investigated.
  - Time period: The search warrant must specify the permissible time period for examining seized electronic devices or storage media, which may vary depending on the complexity and volume of the data.
  - Retention: The search warrant must specify the retention policy for seized data, such as how long the data can be kept, how the data can be used, and how the data can be destroyed or returned.
- A warranted search for a cyber investigation may require special skills and tools, such as:
  - Knowledge of the relevant laws and procedures for obtaining and executing a search warrant.
  - Knowledge of the technical aspects of the device or media to be searched, such as the operating system, the file system, the encryption, and the network protocols.
  - Knowledge of the forensic methods and techniques for preserving, extracting, analyzing, and presenting digital evidence, such as using write blockers, hash values, metadata, and forensic software.
  - Knowledge of the best practices and standards for conducting a cyber investigation, such as following the chain of custody, documenting the steps, and reporting the findings.
