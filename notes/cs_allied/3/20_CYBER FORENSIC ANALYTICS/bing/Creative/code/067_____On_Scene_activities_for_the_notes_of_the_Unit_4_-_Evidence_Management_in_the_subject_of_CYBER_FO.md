### On Scene Activities for the Notes of the Unit 4 - Evidence Management in the Subject of Cyber Forensic Analytics

On scene activities are the actions and procedures that a cyber forensic analyst performs at the location where a cyber incident or a cybercrime has occurred. The main purpose of on scene activities is to identify, collect, preserve, and document digital evidence that can be used for further analysis and investigation. Some of the on scene activities are:

- **Securing the scene**: This involves establishing a perimeter around the area where the digital devices are located, preventing unauthorized access, and ensuring the safety of the personnel and the evidence. The analyst should also document the scene with photographs, sketches, notes, and logs .
- **Identifying the evidence**: This involves locating and recognizing the potential sources of digital evidence, such as computers, mobile phones, storage devices, network devices, cloud services, etc. The analyst should also determine the type, format, and relevance of the evidence, and prioritize the collection based on the risk of data loss or alteration .
- **Collecting the evidence**: This involves physically or logically acquiring the digital evidence from the devices or services, using appropriate tools and techniques. The analyst should follow the principles of forensics, such as minimizing the changes to the original data, using write blockers, hashing the data, and verifying the integrity of the copies .
- **Preserving the evidence**: This involves storing and transporting the digital evidence in a secure and controlled manner, using proper packaging, labeling, sealing, and chain of custody documentation. The analyst should also protect the evidence from environmental factors, such as heat, humidity, dust, etc., and avoid any physical or logical damage .
- **Documenting the evidence**: This involves creating and maintaining a comprehensive and accurate record of the on scene activities, including the date, time, location, personnel, actions, observations, findings, and evidence details. The analyst should also use standardized forms, templates, and reports, and follow the legal and ethical requirements of the jurisdiction .

On scene activities are crucial for the success of a cyber forensic investigation, as they ensure the quality, reliability, and admissibility of the digital evidence. Therefore, the analyst should follow the best practices and guidelines of the field, and use the appropriate tools and methods for each activity.
