## Unit 3 - Cyber Investigation

Cyber investigation is the process of collecting, analyzing and presenting digital evidence related to cybercrime or cyber incidents. Cyber investigation can be conducted by law enforcement agencies, private sector organizations, or individuals.

Some of the topics covered in this unit are:

- **Cybercrime and cyber incidents**: The types, categories, and characteristics of cybercrime and cyber incidents, such as hacking, phishing, malware, denial-of-service, cyber espionage, cyber terrorism, cyber warfare, etc.
- **Cyber investigation process**: The steps and stages of cyber investigation, such as identification, preservation, collection, examination, analysis, and presentation of digital evidence.
- **Cyber investigation tools and techniques**: The tools and techniques used for cyber investigation, such as digital forensics, network analysis, malware analysis, cryptography, steganography, etc.
- **Cyber investigation challenges and best practices**: The challenges and best practices for cyber investigation, such as legal and ethical issues, standards and guidelines, chain of custody, documentation, etc.
- **Cyber investigation case studies**: The examples and scenarios of cyber investigation, such as cyber attacks on critical infrastructure, data breaches, online fraud, cyber bullying, etc.
