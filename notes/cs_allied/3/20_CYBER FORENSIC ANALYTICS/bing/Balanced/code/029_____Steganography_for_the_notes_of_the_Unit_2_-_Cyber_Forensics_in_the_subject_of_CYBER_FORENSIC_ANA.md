### Steganography

Steganography is the art or practice of concealing a message, image, or file within another message, image, or file  . It is an encryption technique that can be used along with cryptography as an extra-secure method in which to protect data. Steganography techniques can be applied to images, a video file or an audio file. Typically, however, steganography is written in characters including hash marking, but its usage within images is also common.

Some points to note about steganography are:

- Steganography is different from cryptography, which is the science of making information unintelligible to anyone who does not have the key to decrypt it. Steganography does not alter the appearance or structure of the data, but rather hides it within another data.
- Steganography can be used for various purposes, such as hiding secret messages, watermarking digital media, protecting intellectual property, or evading censorship or surveillance . It can also be used for malicious purposes, such as embedding malware, spyware, or viruses within seemingly harmless files .
- Steganography can be classified into two types: technical and linguistic. Technical steganography uses physical or digital media to hide the data, such as images, audio, video, or text files. Linguistic steganography uses natural language to conceal the data, such as using code words, acronyms, or hidden meanings.
- Steganography can be detected and analyzed by various methods, such as statistical analysis, visual inspection, or steganalysis. Steganalysis is the science of finding and extracting hidden data from steganographic media. It can use various techniques, such as signature analysis, histogram analysis, or machine learning.
