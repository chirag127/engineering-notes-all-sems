### On Scene Activities for the Notes of the Unit 4 - Evidence Management in the Subject of Cyber Forensic Analytics

On scene activities are the actions and procedures that digital forensic investigators perform at the location where the digital evidence is found or suspected to be. On scene activities include:

- Identifying and securing the scene of the cyber incident or crime, such as a computer network, a server, a mobile device, or a cloud service.
- Assessing the risks and challenges of the scene, such as the presence of encryption, malware, remote access, or volatile data.
- Documenting and photographing the scene and the evidence, such as the physical layout, the connections, the labels, the serial numbers, and the timestamps.
- Collecting and preserving the evidence, such as the digital devices, the storage media, the network logs, and the metadata.
- Performing on-scene triage, which is the process of quickly analyzing the evidence to identify the most relevant and important data for further investigation.
- Reporting and transporting the evidence, such as creating a chain of custody, packaging and labeling the evidence, and delivering it to the forensic laboratory or storage facility.

On scene activities are crucial for ensuring the integrity, reliability, and admissibility of the digital evidence. They require careful planning, preparation, and execution, as well as following the legal and ethical standards of digital forensics. On scene activities are also dependent on the type, size, and complexity of the digital evidence and the cyber incident or crime.
