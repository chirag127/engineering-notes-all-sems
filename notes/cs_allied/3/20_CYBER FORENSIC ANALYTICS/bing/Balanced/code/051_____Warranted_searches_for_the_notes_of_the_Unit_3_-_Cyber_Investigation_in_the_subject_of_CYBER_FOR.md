### Warranted searches for the notes of the Unit 3 - Cyber Investigation in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

- A warranted search is a search authorized by a court order based on probable cause that the target of the search contains evidence of a crime .
- A warranted search of a computer or electronic media requires specifying the crime(s) to which the evidence pertains, the location and description of the device(s) to be searched, and the type and scope of the data to be seized .
- A warranted search of a computer or electronic media may involve on-site or off-site examination of the device(s), depending on the circumstances and the nature of the data.
- A warranted search of a computer or electronic media may encounter challenges such as encryption, password protection, remote storage, or data destruction.
- A warranted search of a computer or electronic media must follow the principles of legality, necessity, proportionality, and accountability to ensure the admissibility and integrity of the evidence.
- A warranted search of a computer or electronic media may be conducted by law enforcement agencies, such as the FBI, in response to various cyber threats, such as hacking, malware, identity theft, or espionage .
