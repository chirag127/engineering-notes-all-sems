### Softlifting

Softlifting is a type of software piracy that involves installing or copying a legally purchased software program onto more than one computer, in violation of the software license agreement  . Softlifting does not involve selling the software copies for profit, but rather providing the program to multiple users, usually in a business or school environment. Softlifting is also known as softloading, end-user piracy, or overinstallation  .

Some of the main points to note about softlifting are:

- Softlifting is the most common type of software piracy.
- Softlifting can result in legal consequences, such as fines, lawsuits, or criminal charges, for both the individual and the organization involved.
- Softlifting can also cause harm to the software industry, such as loss of revenue, reduced innovation, and increased security risks.
- Softlifting can be prevented by following the software license agreement, using software asset management tools, and educating users about the ethical and legal implications of software piracy.
