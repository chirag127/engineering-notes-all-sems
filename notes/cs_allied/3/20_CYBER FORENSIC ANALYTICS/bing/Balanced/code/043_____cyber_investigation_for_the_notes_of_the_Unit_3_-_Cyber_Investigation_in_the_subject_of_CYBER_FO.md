# Cyber Investigation

Cyber investigation is the process of investigating, analyzing, and recovering forensic data for digital evidence of a crime. Cyber investigation can be used to track criminals via the computer, such as in cases of computer crimes, cyber attacks, extortion, or identity theft . Cyber investigation can also be used to track records of criminals using computer forensics, such as in cases of fraud, terrorism, or child pornography .

Some of the objectives of cyber investigation are:

- To identify the source and nature of the cyber crime
- To collect and preserve the digital evidence
- To analyze and interpret the digital evidence
- To present the findings and conclusions in a court of law
- To impose risk and consequences on cyber adversaries

Some of the challenges of cyber investigation are:

- The complexity and diversity of cyber crimes and cyber criminals
- The anonymity and encryption of online activities and communications
- The jurisdictional and legal issues of cross-border cyber crimes
- The rapid evolution and obsolescence of technology and software
- The shortage of skilled and trained cyber investigators

Some of the skills and tools of cyber investigation are:

- Knowledge of computer systems, networks, and protocols
- Knowledge of cyber laws, ethics, and standards
- Knowledge of digital forensics techniques and software
- Ability to conduct online research and intelligence
- Ability to communicate and collaborate with other investigators and stakeholders
