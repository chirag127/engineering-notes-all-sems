### Pre Search Activities for the Notes of the Unit 4 - Evidence Management in the Subject of Cyber Forensic Analytics

- Pre search activities are the steps that cyber forensic experts take before conducting a search for digital evidence in a cyber crime investigation.
- Pre search activities include the following tasks:
  - Identification: The first step of cyber forensics experts are to identify what evidence is present, where it is stored, and in which format it is stored.
  - Planning: The second step of cyber forensics experts are to plan how to conduct the search, what tools and techniques to use, what legal and ethical issues to consider, and what resources and personnel to allocate.
  - Preparation: The third step of cyber forensics experts are to prepare the necessary equipment, software, and documentation for the search, as well as to secure the search location and obtain the proper authorization and consent.
- Pre search activities are important for the following reasons:
  - They help to ensure the integrity, reliability, and admissibility of the digital evidence collected.
  - They help to avoid unnecessary damage, loss, or alteration of the digital evidence.
  - They help to reduce the risk of legal or ethical violations, such as privacy breaches, trespassing, or tampering.
  - They help to optimize the efficiency and effectiveness of the search process.
- Pre search activities may vary depending on the type and scope of the cyber crime investigation, the nature and location of the digital evidence, and the specific tools and techniques used by the cyber forensic experts   .
