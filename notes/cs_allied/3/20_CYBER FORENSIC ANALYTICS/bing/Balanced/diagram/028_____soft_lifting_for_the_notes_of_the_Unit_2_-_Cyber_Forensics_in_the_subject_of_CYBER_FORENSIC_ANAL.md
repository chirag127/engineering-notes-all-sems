### Soft lifting

- Soft lifting is the act of purchasing a single copy of software and then downloading it onto more than one computer, despite agreement terms stating the software must only be downloaded once .
- Soft lifting does not involve selling copies of protected creative works, but it is still a form of software piracy and a violation of intellectual property rights .
- Soft lifting is the most common type of software piracy and it often occurs in business or school environments to save money .
- Soft lifting can be prevented by enforcing strict licensing policies, educating users about the legal and ethical implications of software piracy, and using software asset management tools to monitor and audit software usage.
