### Network components for the notes of the Unit 2 - Cyber Forensics in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

- Network components are the devices and software that enable the communication and data exchange between different nodes on a network.
- Some common network components are routers, switches, firewalls, network interface cards (NICs), cables, wireless access points, servers, clients, etc.
- Network components play a vital role in cyber forensics, as they can provide valuable evidence and information about the source, nature, and impact of cyberattacks on a networked environment.
- Network forensics is the science that centers on the discovery and retrieval of information surrounding a cybercrime within a networked environment.
- Network forensics involves the capture, recording, and analysis of network packets in order to determine the source of network security attacks.
- Network forensics can help draw several conclusions during an investigation, such as the identity and location of the attacker, the type and method of the attack, the extent and severity of the damage, the timeline and sequence of events, etc.
- Network forensics can also help in preventing future attacks, by identifying the vulnerabilities and weaknesses of the network, and recommending the appropriate countermeasures and security policies.
- Network forensics is one of the key components in a defense team’s arsenal, along with digital forensics and incident response.
- Digital forensics is the investigative branch of forensic science that collects, analyzes, and presents digital evidence, such as user activity and system data, from various devices, such as computers, mobile phones, tablets, etc.
- Incident response is the process of responding to and managing a security breach or attack, by containing the incident, eradicating the threat, recovering the system, and learning from the experience.
- Network forensics, digital forensics, and incident response work together to provide a comprehensive and effective solution to cybercrime investigations and prevention.
