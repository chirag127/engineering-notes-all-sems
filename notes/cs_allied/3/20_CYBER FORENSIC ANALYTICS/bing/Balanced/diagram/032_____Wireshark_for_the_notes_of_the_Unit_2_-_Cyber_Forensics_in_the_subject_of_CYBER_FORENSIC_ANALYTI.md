### Wireshark for the notes of the Unit 2 - Cyber Forensics in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

- Wireshark is a free and open-source network protocol analyzer that can capture and examine packets from a network connection .
- Wireshark can be used for network forensics, which is the study of network packets and their behavior to identify and investigate network-related incidents, such as cyberattacks, malware infections, data breaches, etc  .
- Wireshark can help network forensic analysts to:
  - Monitor and troubleshoot network performance and functionality .
  - Detect and analyze network anomalies and malicious activities  .
  - Extract and recover evidence from network traffic, such as files, passwords, commands, etc .
  - Reconstruct and replay network sessions and events .
- Wireshark has a graphical user interface (GUI) that displays the captured packets in a table format, with columns for packet number, time, source, destination, protocol, length, and info .
- Wireshark also has a command-line interface (CLI) called TShark that can perform similar functions as the GUI, but with more flexibility and automation .
- Wireshark can apply filters to the captured packets to narrow down the analysis to specific criteria, such as IP address, port number, protocol, keyword, etc .
- Wireshark can also use color codes to highlight different types of packets, such as TCP, UDP, HTTP, DNS, etc .
- Wireshark can export and import packets in various formats, such as pcap, pcapng, csv, json, etc .
- Wireshark supports hundreds of network protocols and can decode and dissect their fields and contents .
- Wireshark can also use plugins and extensions to add new features and functionalities, such as custom protocols, statistics, visualizations, etc .
