### Investigating audit logs for the notes of the Unit 3 - Cyber Investigation in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

- Audit logs are records of events that occur on a system or a network, such as user actions, system changes, network connections, security breaches, etc. 
- Audit logs are essential for cyber investigation because they can provide evidence of who did what, when, where, how and why on a system or a network.  
- Audit logs can help investigators to:
  - Identify the source and scope of an incident
  - Trace the actions and movements of an attacker
  - Determine the impact and damage of an incident
  - Recover from an incident and prevent future ones
  - Comply with regulatory and legal requirements   
- Audit logs can be classified into two types: system logs and user-level logs. 
  - System logs are generated by the operating system and the applications running on it, such as start up and shut down of the system, network connection changes or failures, changes to system security settings and controls, etc. 
  - User-level logs are generated by the actions of users on the system or the network, such as logins, file access, web requests, database queries, etc.  
- Audit logs can be stored in different formats and locations, such as text files, databases, cloud services, etc.  
- Audit logs require proper management and protection, such as:
  - Configuring the system or the network to generate relevant and sufficient audit logs
  - Collecting and aggregating audit logs from different sources and devices
  - Storing and retaining audit logs securely and for an appropriate period of time
  - Analyzing and reviewing audit logs regularly and systematically
  - Reporting and responding to audit log findings and alerts     
- Audit logs can be analyzed and reviewed using various tools and techniques, such as:
  - Log parsers and filters to extract and format audit log data
  - Log analyzers and dashboards to visualize and summarize audit log data
  - Log correlation and anomaly detection to identify and highlight suspicious or unusual audit log events
  - Log forensics and investigation to examine and verify audit log evidence
