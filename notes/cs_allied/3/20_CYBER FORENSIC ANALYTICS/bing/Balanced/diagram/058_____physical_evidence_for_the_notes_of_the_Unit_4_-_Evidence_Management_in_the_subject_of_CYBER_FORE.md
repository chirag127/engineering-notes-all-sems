### Physical Evidence

Physical evidence is any tangible object or material that can be used to prove or disprove a fact or hypothesis in a cyber forensics investigation. Physical evidence can be collected from various sources, such as:

- **Computer equipment**: This includes the hardware components of a computer system, such as the CPU, the hard drive, the RAM, the motherboard, the keyboard, the mouse, the monitor, the printer, the scanner, the webcam, the speakers, the microphone, the CD-ROM, the DVD-ROM, the USB drive, the external hard drive, the network card, the modem, the router, the switch, the hub, the firewall, the power supply, the battery, the fan, the heat sink, the cables, the connectors, the ports, the slots, the screws, the case, etc. These components can contain valuable information about the configuration, the performance, the usage, the history, the identity, the location, the ownership, the access, the modification, the deletion, the encryption, the decryption, the backup, the recovery, the infection, the damage, the tampering, the alteration, the destruction, the theft, the recovery, the disposal, etc. of the computer system and the data stored or processed on it  .

- **Removable media**: This includes any type of storage device that can be inserted or removed from a computer system, such as the floppy disk, the CD, the DVD, the Blu-ray, the USB flash drive, the memory card, the external hard drive, the tape, the zip drive, the optical disk, the magnetic disk, the solid state drive, the cloud storage, etc. These devices can contain valuable information about the data stored or transferred on them, such as the type, the format, the size, the name, the date, the time, the location, the ownership, the access, the modification, the deletion, the encryption, the decryption, the backup, the recovery, the infection, the damage, the tampering, the alteration, the destruction, the theft, the recovery, the disposal, etc. of the data and the device itself  .

- **Publications**: This includes any type of printed or digital material that can be used to communicate or disseminate information, such as the books, the magazines, the newspapers, the journals, the newsletters, the reports, the manuals, the guides, the brochures, the flyers, the posters, the banners, the stickers, the labels, the cards, the letters, the envelopes, the notes, the receipts, the invoices, the bills, the statements, the contracts, the agreements, the certificates, the licenses, the permits, the tickets, the vouchers, the coupons, the tokens, the stamps, the seals, the signatures, the logos, the symbols, the icons, the images, the photos, the videos, the audios, the podcasts, the web pages, the blogs, the forums, the social media, the emails, the chats, the messages, the texts, the emojis, the hashtags, the links, the attachments, the downloads, the uploads, the screenshots, the QR codes, the barcodes, etc. These materials can contain valuable information about the content, the context, the purpose, the source, the destination, the sender, the receiver, the author, the editor, the publisher, the distributor, the date, the time, the location, the ownership, the access, the modification, the deletion, the encryption, the decryption, the backup, the recovery, the infection, the damage, the tampering, the alteration, the destruction, the theft, the recovery, the disposal, etc. of the information and the material itself  .
