### Cyber Investigation

Cyber investigation is the process of investigating, analyzing, and recovering forensic data for digital evidence of a crime. Cyber investigation can be used to track criminals via the computer, to investigate computer crimes, or to secure evidence from networked devices found at the scene of a crime .

Some of the objectives of cyber investigation are:

- To identify the source and nature of cyber attacks, such as hacking, phishing, malware, denial-of-service, etc.
- To collect and preserve digital evidence in a forensically sound manner, following the principles of chain of custody, integrity, and authenticity.
- To analyze and interpret digital evidence using various tools and techniques, such as data carving, file recovery, encryption, hashing, etc.
- To present and report the findings and conclusions of the cyber investigation in a clear and concise manner, using appropriate formats and standards.
- To cooperate and coordinate with other law enforcement agencies, such as the FBI, Interpol, etc., in cross-border or multi-jurisdictional cyber cases.

Some of the challenges of cyber investigation are:

- The dynamic and evolving nature of cyber threats, which require constant updating of skills and knowledge.
- The large and complex volume of data involved in cyber cases, which require efficient and effective data management and analysis.
- The legal and ethical issues involved in cyber cases, such as privacy, jurisdiction, consent, etc., which require compliance with relevant laws and regulations.
- The technical and operational issues involved in cyber cases, such as encryption, anti-forensics, obfuscation, etc., which require advanced and specialized tools and techniques.
