### Jurisprudential Inconsistency

- Jurisprudential inconsistency refers to the lack of uniformity or coherence in the legal system or the legal principles that govern cybercrime and digital forensics investigations.
- Jurisprudential inconsistency can arise from various sources, such as:
  - The non-local and transnational nature of cybercrime, which challenges the traditional notions of territorial jurisdiction and sovereignty .
  - The intangibility and volatility of digital evidence, which complicates the processes of identification, collection, preservation, analysis, and presentation of evidence in court .
  - The diversity and complexity of cyber-attacks and cybercrimes, which require specialized knowledge and skills to investigate and prosecute effectively .
  - The lack of harmonization or standardization of laws, regulations, policies, procedures, and practices among different countries, regions, or jurisdictions that deal with cybercrime and digital forensics  .
- Jurisprudential inconsistency can have negative impacts on the effectiveness and efficiency of cybercrime and digital forensics investigations, such as:
  - Creating legal loopholes or gaps that can be exploited by cybercriminals to evade detection, prosecution, or punishment .
  - Reducing the reliability, validity, or admissibility of digital evidence in court, which can undermine the credibility of the investigation or the outcome of the case .
  - Increasing the costs, risks, or delays of the investigation or the legal proceedings, which can affect the resources, time, or reputation of the investigators, prosecutors, or judges .
  - Hindering the cooperation or coordination among different stakeholders, such as law enforcement agencies, judicial authorities, private sector, or international organizations, which can limit the sharing of information, expertise, or assistance  .
- Jurisprudential inconsistency can be addressed or reduced by various measures, such as:
  - Developing or updating the legal framework or the legal principles that can accommodate the specificities and challenges of cybercrime and digital forensics .
  - Establishing or enhancing the technical capabilities or the technical standards that can ensure the quality and integrity of digital evidence and digital forensics .
  - Promoting or facilitating the education or the training of the relevant personnel or the public on the issues and implications of cybercrime and digital forensics .
  - Strengthening or expanding the collaboration or the communication among different actors or entities that are involved or affected by cybercrime and digital forensics  .
