# CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

- Cyber forensic analytics is the application of data analysis techniques and tools to investigate cyber incidents and collect digital evidence for legal purposes .
- Cyber forensic analytics involves the following steps:
  - Identification: Identify the sources and scope of the cyber incident and the potential evidence to be collected.
  - Preservation: Secure and isolate the evidence from tampering or alteration.
  - Extraction: Extract the relevant data from the evidence using forensic tools and methods.
  - Analysis: Analyze the data to reconstruct the events, identify the actors, and discover the motives and methods of the cyber incident.
  - Presentation: Present the findings and conclusions in a clear and concise manner that can be understood by the intended audience, such as law enforcement, courts, or management.
- Cyber forensic analytics faces several challenges, such as :
  - The volume and complexity of data to be analyzed, which may require advanced tools and techniques, such as machine learning, artificial intelligence, and data visualization.
  - The encryption and obfuscation of data by malicious actors, which may hinder the extraction and analysis of evidence.
  - The dynamic and evolving nature of cyber threats, which may require constant updating of forensic knowledge and skills.
  - The legal and ethical issues of cyber forensic analytics, such as privacy, consent, jurisdiction, and chain of custody.
- Cyber forensic analytics is a vital component of cybersecurity, as it helps to detect, prevent, and respond to cyberattacks, as well as to hold the perpetrators accountable and deter future incidents  .
