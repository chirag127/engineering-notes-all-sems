### Investigating Computer Intrusions

- A computer intrusion is an unauthorized access to a computer system or network that violates the security policies or standard practices of the system or network.
- A computer intrusion investigation is a process of identifying, analyzing, and responding to the incidents or threats of computer intrusions.
- A computer intrusion investigation may involve the following steps  :
  - Notification: Informing the relevant authorities or stakeholders of the intrusion, such as the security division of the local telephone company, the law enforcement agencies, the system administrators, the owners or users of the affected systems, and the legal counsel.
  - Preservation: Securing the evidence and the crime scene, such as the computer systems, the network devices, the logs, the backups, the malware, and the physical access points. This may require isolating the systems from the network, making copies of the data, documenting the chain of custody, and applying encryption or hashing techniques.
  - Identification: Determining the scope, source, and method of the intrusion, such as the type of computer or computer system, the telephone connections, the computer security measures, the information accessed or compromised, the time and duration of the intrusion, the tools or techniques used by the intruder, and the possible motive or intention of the intruder.
  - Analysis: Examining the evidence and the intrusion, such as the system configuration, the network traffic, the user activity, the system vulnerabilities, the malware behavior, the intruder identity, and the impact or damage of the intrusion. This may require using forensic tools, techniques, and methods, such as disk imaging, file carving, memory analysis, network analysis, malware analysis, timeline analysis, and data recovery.
  - Response: Taking appropriate actions to mitigate, contain, or eliminate the intrusion, such as restoring the system functionality, removing the malware, patching the vulnerabilities, strengthening the security measures, notifying the affected parties, reporting the findings, and prosecuting the intruder.
  - Recovery: Restoring the normal operation of the system or network, such as verifying the integrity of the data, testing the system performance, monitoring the system activity, updating the security policies, and implementing the lessons learned.
