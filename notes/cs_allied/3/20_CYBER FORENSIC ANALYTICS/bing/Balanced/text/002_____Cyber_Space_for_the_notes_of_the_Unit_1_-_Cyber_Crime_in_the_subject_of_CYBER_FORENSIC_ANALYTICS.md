### Cyber Space

- Cyber space is a concept that describes the **notional environment** in which **communication over computer networks** occurs .
- Cyber space is also used to refer to the **online world** as a world 'apart', as distinct from everyday reality.
- Cyber space is a **global and dynamic domain** characterized by the combined use of electrons and the electromagnetic spectrum, whose purpose is to create, store, modify, exchange, share, and extract, use, eliminate information and disrupt physical resources.
- Cyber space is composed of various **layers** such as physical, logical, informational, and social.
- Cyber space is influenced by various **factors** such as technology, law, policy, culture, and ethics.
- Cyber space is the **arena** for various **activities** such as commerce, education, entertainment, social interaction, and crime.
- Cyber space is also the **source** and the **target** of various **threats** such as cyber attacks, cyber espionage, cyber terrorism, cyber warfare, and cyber crime.
- Cyber crime is the **illegal or unauthorized** use of cyber space for **harmful or malicious** purposes.
- Cyber crime can be classified into various **types** such as cyber theft, cyber fraud, cyber vandalism, cyber sabotage, cyber espionage, cyber terrorism, and cyber warfare.
- Cyber crime can have various **impacts** such as financial loss, reputational damage, identity theft, privacy violation, data breach, system compromise, and physical harm.
- Cyber crime can be **prevented** and **investigated** by various **measures** such as cyber security, cyber law, cyber policy, cyber ethics, and cyber forensics.
- Cyber forensics is the **application** of scientific methods and techniques to **collect**, **preserve**, **analyze**, and **present** digital evidence related to cyber crime.
- Cyber forensics can be performed on various **devices** such as computers, mobile phones, tablets, digital cameras, and storage media.
- Cyber forensics can be performed on various **levels** such as disk forensics, file forensics, network forensics, malware forensics, and memory forensics.
- Cyber forensics can be performed by various **roles** such as cyber forensic analysts, cyber forensic examiners, cyber forensic investigators, and cyber forensic experts.
- Cyber forensics can be performed by following various **standards** and **guidelines** such as ISO/IEC 27037, NIST SP 800-86, ACPO Good Practice Guide, and SWGDE Best Practices.
