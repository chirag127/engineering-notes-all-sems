## Unit 1 - Cyber Crime

- Cyber crime is any illegal or unethical activity that involves the use of a computer, a network, or the internet.
- Cyber crime can be classified into three main categories: cyber attacks, cyber fraud, and cyber theft.
- Cyber attacks are malicious actions that aim to disrupt, damage, or destroy computer systems, networks, or data. Examples of cyber attacks are denial-of-service attacks, ransomware attacks, hacking, and cyber warfare.
- Cyber fraud is the use of deception or manipulation to obtain money, goods, or services from unsuspecting victims online. Examples of cyber fraud are phishing, identity theft, online scams, and credit card fraud.
- Cyber theft is the unauthorized access, copying, or distribution of digital assets, such as software, music, movies, or personal information. Examples of cyber theft are piracy, data breaches, and espionage.
- Cyber crime can have serious consequences for individuals, organizations, and society, such as financial losses, reputational damage, privacy violations, and national security threats.
- Cyber crime can be prevented or mitigated by implementing various measures, such as encryption, authentication, firewalls, antivirus software, backup systems, and cyber security awareness and education.
