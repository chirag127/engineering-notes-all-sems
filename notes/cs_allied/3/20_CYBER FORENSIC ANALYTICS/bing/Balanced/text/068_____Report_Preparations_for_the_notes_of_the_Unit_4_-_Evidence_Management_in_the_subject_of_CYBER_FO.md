### Report Preparations for the notes of the Unit 4 - Evidence Management in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

- Evidence management is the process of collecting, preserving, analyzing, and presenting digital evidence in a forensically sound manner.
- Evidence management involves following the best practices and standards of digital forensics, such as:
  - Don’t break the law when collecting, processing, analyzing, or handling evidence .
  - Only mention the information that’s relevant to the case and the investigation objectives .
  - Focus on concrete facts rather than your personal opinions or interpretations .
  - Utilize any forensics notes you’ve made during the investigation itself, such as logs, timestamps, hashes, etc. .
  - Avoid using complex terms and focus on conveying the relevant information in a manner that is coherent and simple to understand .
  - Document and account for every step of the investigation, from the acquisition of the evidence to the analysis and reporting .
  - Create an identical copy of the evidence to ensure the original data is not lost or modified, and use the copy for analysis .
  - Verify the findings with the original evidence and cross-check with other sources if possible .
  - Present the report to the court in a clear, concise, and professional manner .
- Evidence management requires a combination of techniques and expert knowledge, such as:
  - Computer networking concepts, network security methodologies, risk management, computer and privacy laws, and data analytics.
  - Forensics for computers, smartphones, cloud, and networks, using various tools and methods .
  - An investigative mindset, with the ability to follow the evidence trail, identify anomalies, and draw logical conclusions .
- Evidence management is essential for ensuring the integrity, admissibility, and reliability of digital evidence in legal proceedings.
