### Cyber terrorism

- Cyber terrorism means the **politically motivated use of computers and information technology to cause severe disruption or widespread fear in society**.
- Cyber terrorism can target **vital computer systems** that control infrastructure, communication, transportation, finance, or security.
- Cyber terrorism can result in, or threaten, the **loss of life or significant bodily harm**.
- Cyber terrorism can be carried out by **subnational groups or clandestine agents** who seek to advance their political or ideological goals through threat or intimidation .
- Cyber terrorism can involve various types of attacks, such as **hacking, denial-of-service, ransomware, malware, phishing, or cyber espionage**.
- Cyber terrorism can pose serious challenges to **national security, public safety, civil liberties, and international cooperation**.
- Cyber terrorism can be prevented or mitigated by **enhancing cyber security, raising awareness, sharing information, enforcing laws, and cooperating with allies**.
