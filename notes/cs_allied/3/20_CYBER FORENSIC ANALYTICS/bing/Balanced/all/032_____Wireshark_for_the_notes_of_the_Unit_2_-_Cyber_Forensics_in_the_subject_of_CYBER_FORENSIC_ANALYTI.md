# Wireshark for the notes of the Unit 2 - Cyber Forensics in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

- Wireshark is a free and open-source network protocol analyzer that can capture and examine packets from a network connection .
- Wireshark is an essential tool for network forensics, as it can help to identify and analyze network threats, anomalies, and malicious activities   .
- Wireshark can display the details of each packet, such as the source and destination addresses, protocols, payload, and checksums .
- Wireshark can also filter, search, and highlight packets based on various criteria, such as protocol, port, IP address, or content .
- Wireshark can also reconstruct and export data from packets, such as images, files, or web pages.
- Wireshark can also perform statistical analysis and generate graphs and reports on the network traffic .
- Wireshark can also capture packets from multiple interfaces and sources, such as live network, offline files, or remote devices .
- Wireshark can also support various network protocols and standards, such as TCP/IP, Ethernet, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, or USB .
- Wireshark can also integrate with other tools and plugins, such as Nmap, Snort, or GeoIP.
- Wireshark can also be customized and extended by using scripts, filters, or dissectors .
