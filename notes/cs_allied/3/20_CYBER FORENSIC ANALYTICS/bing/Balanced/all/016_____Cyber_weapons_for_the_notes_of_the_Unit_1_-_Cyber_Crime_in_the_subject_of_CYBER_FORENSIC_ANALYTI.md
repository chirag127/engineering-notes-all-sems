# Cyber weapons

- Cyber weapons are pieces of computer software or hardware that are used to commit cyberwarfare .
- Cyberwarfare is the use of cyberattacks to damage, disrupt, or destroy the information systems or networks of another nation or organization.
- Cyberattacks are malicious actions that exploit the vulnerabilities of computers, networks, or data, such as hacking, phishing, denial-of-service, ransomware, or malware.
- Cyber weapons can have different objectives, such as:
  - Espionage: stealing or manipulating confidential or sensitive information from the target.
  - Sabotage: impairing or destroying the functionality or integrity of the target's systems or data.
  - Influence: altering or influencing the perception, behavior, or decision-making of the target or its audience.
- Cyber weapons can have different effects, such as:
  - Physical: causing physical damage or harm to the target's infrastructure, equipment, or personnel.
  - Functional: disrupting or degrading the performance or availability of the target's systems or services.
  - Mental: inducing fear, confusion, or doubt in the target or its audience.
- Cyber weapons can have different characteristics, such as:
  - Lethality: the degree of harm or damage that the cyber weapon can cause to the target.
  - Persistence: the duration or frequency of the cyber weapon's effect on the target.
  - Stealth: the ability of the cyber weapon to evade detection or attribution by the target or its defenders.
  - Scalability: the ability of the cyber weapon to affect multiple or diverse targets with the same or similar effect.
