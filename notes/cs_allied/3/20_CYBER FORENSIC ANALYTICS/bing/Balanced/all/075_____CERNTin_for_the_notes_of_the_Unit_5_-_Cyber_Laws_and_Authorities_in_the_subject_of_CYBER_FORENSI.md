# CERNTin for the notes of the Unit 5 - Cyber Laws and Authorities in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

- CERNTin stands for Cyber Emergency Response Team in India, which is a national agency that deals with cyber incidents and coordinates responses among various stakeholders.
- CERNTin was established in 2004 under the Information Technology Act, 2000, and operates under the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY).
- CERNTin's main functions are:
  - To collect, analyze and disseminate information on cyber incidents and vulnerabilities.
  - To issue alerts and advisories on cyber threats and best practices.
  - To provide incident response and resolution services to affected entities.
  - To conduct research and development on cyber security technologies and standards.
  - To collaborate with national and international organizations on cyber security matters.
- CERNTin's main objectives are:
  - To enhance the security and resilience of the Indian cyberspace.
  - To protect the critical information infrastructure of the country.
  - To promote awareness and capacity building on cyber security among various stakeholders.
  - To foster a culture of cyber security and compliance among the public and private sectors.
- CERNTin's main challenges are:
  - To cope with the increasing volume and sophistication of cyber attacks.
  - To coordinate with multiple agencies and jurisdictions on cyber incidents and investigations.
  - To balance the need for security and privacy in the digital domain.
  - To keep pace with the evolving cyber laws and regulations at the national and international levels.
