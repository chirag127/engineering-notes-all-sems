### Investigating Web Attacks

Unit 3 - Cyber Investigation in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

1. Web attacks are a common form of cybercrime that can result in the theft of sensitive information, disruption of services, and damage to systems and data.
2. Investigating web attacks involves identifying the source and nature of the attack, collecting and analyzing evidence, and taking appropriate action to mitigate the damage and prevent future attacks.
3. Some common types of web attacks include SQL injection, cross-site scripting (XSS), and distributed denial of service (DDoS) attacks.
4. To investigate a web attack, forensic analysts may use a variety of tools and techniques, including network traffic analysis, log analysis, and malware analysis.
5. It is important to follow proper forensic procedures and chain of custody when collecting and analyzing evidence to ensure its admissibility in legal proceedings.
6. Collaboration with law enforcement and other relevant parties may be necessary to identify and prosecute the perpetrators of web attacks.
7. Preventative measures, such as regular security updates and employee training, can help reduce the risk of web attacks.
