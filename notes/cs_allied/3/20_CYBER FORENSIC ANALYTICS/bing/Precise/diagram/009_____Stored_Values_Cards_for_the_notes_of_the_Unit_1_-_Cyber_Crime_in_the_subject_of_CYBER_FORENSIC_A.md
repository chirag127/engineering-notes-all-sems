### Stored Values Cards

Stored value cards are a type of payment card that has a monetary value stored on the card itself. They are commonly used for transactions where anonymity is desired, such as online purchases or gift cards. Here are some key points to remember about stored value cards in the context of Unit 1 - Cyber Crime in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS:

1. Stored value cards can be used to facilitate anonymous transactions, which can make it difficult for law enforcement to trace criminal activity.
2. Criminals may use stored value cards to launder money or to purchase illegal goods or services.
3. The use of stored value cards in criminal activity is an example of how technology can be used to facilitate crime.
4. Law enforcement agencies may need to develop new techniques and tools to investigate the use of stored value cards in criminal activity.
