### Cyber Space

Cyber space refers to the virtual environment where communication over computer networks occurs. It is a domain characterized by the use of electronics and the electromagnetic spectrum to store, modify, and exchange data via networked systems and associated physical infrastructures.

Some key points to consider when studying cyber space in the context of cyber crime and cyber forensic analytics are:

1. Cyber space is a global domain that transcends national borders and is accessible to anyone with an internet connection.
2. The anonymity and vastness of cyber space make it an attractive environment for criminal activities such as hacking, identity theft, and fraud.
3. Law enforcement agencies face challenges in investigating and prosecuting cyber crimes due to the transnational nature of cyber space and the difficulty in tracing criminal activities to specific individuals or locations.
4. Cyber forensic analytics is a field that focuses on the collection, analysis, and preservation of digital evidence in order to support the investigation and prosecution of cyber crimes.
5. The use of advanced technologies and techniques, such as data mining and machine learning, is essential in the field of cyber forensic analytics to effectively analyze large amounts of data and identify patterns of criminal behavior.
