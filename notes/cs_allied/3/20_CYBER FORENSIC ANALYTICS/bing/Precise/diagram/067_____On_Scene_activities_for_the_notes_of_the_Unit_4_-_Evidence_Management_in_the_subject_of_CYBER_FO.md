### On Scene Activities

On scene activities are crucial for evidence management in the field of cyber forensic analytics. These activities include:

1. **Securing the scene**: The first step in on scene activities is to secure the scene to prevent contamination or loss of evidence. This involves establishing a perimeter and controlling access to the scene.

2. **Documentation**: It is important to document the scene, including taking photographs and videos, making sketches, and taking notes. This documentation provides a record of the scene and can be used as evidence in court.

3. **Identification of evidence**: The next step is to identify potential evidence at the scene. This involves a systematic search of the scene to locate and identify items that may be relevant to the investigation.

4. **Collection of evidence**: Once evidence has been identified, it must be collected in a manner that preserves its integrity. This involves following proper procedures for handling, packaging, and labeling evidence.

5. **Preservation of evidence**: Evidence must be preserved to prevent degradation or loss. This involves storing evidence in a secure location and maintaining a chain of custody to document the handling of the evidence.

6. **Transportation of evidence**: Evidence must be transported from the scene to the laboratory for analysis. This must be done in a manner that preserves the integrity of the evidence and maintains the chain of custody.

These on scene activities are essential for ensuring that evidence is properly managed and can be used in court to support the investigation. Proper evidence management is critical for the success of a cyber forensic investigation.
