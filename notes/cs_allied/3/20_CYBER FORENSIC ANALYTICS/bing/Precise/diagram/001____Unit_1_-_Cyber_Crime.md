## Unit 1 - Cyber Crime

Cybercrime refers to criminal activities that are committed using the internet or other forms of digital communication technology. Some common types of cybercrime include:

1. **Hacking:** This involves unauthorized access to a computer system or network, often with the intent to steal or manipulate data.

2. **Identity theft:** This involves stealing someone's personal information, such as their name, address, and credit card details, in order to commit fraud.

3. **Phishing:** This involves sending fraudulent emails or messages that appear to be from a legitimate source, in order to trick people into revealing their personal information.

4. **Malware:** This refers to software that is designed to harm or disrupt computer systems. Examples include viruses, worms, and ransomware.

5. **Cyberstalking:** This involves using the internet or other forms of digital communication to harass or intimidate someone.

It is important to be aware of the risks associated with cybercrime and to take steps to protect yourself and your personal information. This can include using strong passwords, being cautious when clicking on links or downloading attachments, and being wary of unsolicited emails or messages.
