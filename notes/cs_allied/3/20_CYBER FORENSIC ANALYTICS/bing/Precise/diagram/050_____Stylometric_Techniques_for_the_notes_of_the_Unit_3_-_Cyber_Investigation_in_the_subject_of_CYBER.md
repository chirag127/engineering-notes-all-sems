### Stylometric Techniques

Stylometry is the statistical analysis of the style of written texts. It is used to identify the authorship of a text by analyzing the writing style. Stylometric techniques can be applied to various types of texts, including computer code and intrinsic plagiarism detection, which involves detecting plagiarism based on changes in writing style within the document .

The most important phase of the study is choosing the stylometric features of the text. Researchers identify a thousand features at different levels of analysis: lexical (including character and letter levels), syntactic, semantic, structural, and subject-specific . However, stylometric methods have one major drawback .

Stylometric techniques have a fundamental impact on various areas, including cybercrime investigation. The distributed nature of cyberspace provides an ideal anonymous channel for computer-mediated malicious activities, such as phishing scams . Stylometric analysis can help mitigate the effects of online anonymity abuse .
