### Wireshark

Wireshark is a free and powerful tool used for network forensics. It is the most widely used network and protocol analyzer and is also an essential tool in the field of network forensics. Wireshark is an effective open-source tool in the study of network packets and their behavior .

- Wireshark provides data single-handedly in network forensics and is useful for identifying threats through traffic analysis.
- It is more useful for network technicians than normal people, as network technicians can easily identify if anything is not normal with the network.
- Wireshark is a network protocol analyzer, or an application that captures packets from a network connection.
- Packet is the name given to a discrete unit of data in a typical Ethernet network, and Wireshark is the most often-used packet sniffer in the world.
