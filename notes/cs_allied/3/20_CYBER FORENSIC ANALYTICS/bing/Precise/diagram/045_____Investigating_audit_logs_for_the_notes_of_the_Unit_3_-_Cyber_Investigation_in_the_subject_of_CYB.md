### Investigating Audit Logs

Audit logs are records of events that occur on a computer system or network. They are used to track user activity, system changes, and other events that may be relevant to security or compliance. In the context of cyber investigations, audit logs can provide valuable information for identifying and analyzing security incidents.

Here are some key points to consider when investigating audit logs:

1. **Understand the log sources**: Different systems and applications generate different types of logs. It is important to understand the log sources and the type of information they contain in order to effectively analyze the data.

2. **Identify relevant events**: Not all events recorded in audit logs are relevant to an investigation. It is important to identify the events that are relevant to the incident being investigated and focus on those.

3. **Correlate events**: Events recorded in audit logs may not provide a complete picture of an incident on their own. It is often necessary to correlate events from multiple log sources in order to gain a better understanding of what occurred.

4. **Use tools to analyze the data**: Analyzing large volumes of log data can be challenging. There are many tools available that can help with this task, including log management and analysis tools, as well as specialized forensic analysis tools.

5. **Document findings**: As with any investigation, it is important to document the findings and the steps taken during the investigation. This can help with future investigations and can also provide evidence if legal action is necessary.

In summary, audit logs can provide valuable information for cyber investigations. It is important to understand the log sources, identify relevant events, correlate events from multiple sources, use tools to analyze the data, and document the findings. These steps can help investigators effectively analyze audit logs and gain a better understanding of security incidents.
