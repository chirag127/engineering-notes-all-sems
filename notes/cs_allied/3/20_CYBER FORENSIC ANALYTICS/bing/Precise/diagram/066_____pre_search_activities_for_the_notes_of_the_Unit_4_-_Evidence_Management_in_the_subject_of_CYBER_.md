### Pre-search activities for the notes of the Unit 4 - Evidence Management in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

1. **Define the scope of the search:** Before beginning the search for evidence, it is important to define the scope of the search. This includes identifying the devices, systems, and locations that will be searched, as well as the types of data that will be collected.

2. **Obtain legal authority:** In order to conduct a search, it is necessary to obtain the appropriate legal authority. This may include a search warrant, court order, or consent from the owner of the device or system.

3. **Prepare for the search:** Before beginning the search, it is important to prepare by gathering the necessary tools and equipment, such as forensic software, write blockers, and evidence bags. It is also important to ensure that the search team is properly trained and equipped to conduct the search.

4. **Document the search:** It is important to document the search process, including the date and time of the search, the personnel involved, and the actions taken during the search. This documentation will be important for maintaining the chain of custody and ensuring the integrity of the evidence.

5. **Secure the scene:** Before beginning the search, it is important to secure the scene to prevent any tampering or destruction of evidence. This may include securing the physical location, as well as taking steps to preserve any volatile data on electronic devices.
