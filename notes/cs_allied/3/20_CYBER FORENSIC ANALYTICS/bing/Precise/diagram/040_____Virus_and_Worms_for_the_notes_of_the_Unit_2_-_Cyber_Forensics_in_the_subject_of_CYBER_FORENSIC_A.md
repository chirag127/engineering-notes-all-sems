### Virus and Worms

Virus and worms are two types of malicious software (malware) that can harm computer systems. They are both self-replicating programs that spread from one computer to another.

- **Virus:** A virus is a type of malware that attaches itself to a host program or file and spreads by infecting other programs or files. It requires human action to spread, such as opening an infected email attachment or running an infected program.

- **Worm:** A worm is a type of malware that spreads by exploiting vulnerabilities in network security. Unlike a virus, a worm does not require human action to spread. It can replicate itself and spread to other computers on the network without any user intervention.

Both viruses and worms can cause harm to computer systems by consuming system resources, deleting or modifying files, and disrupting network traffic. They can also be used to create backdoors for hackers to gain unauthorized access to systems.

It is important to have up-to-date antivirus software and to practice safe computing habits to protect against viruses and worms. This includes not opening email attachments from unknown sources, keeping software and operating systems up to date with the latest security patches, and using strong passwords.
