### Prepaid Cards

Prepaid cards are a type of payment card that can be loaded with funds and used to make purchases. They are often used as an alternative to traditional credit and debit cards, and can be used for everything from gift certificates to disaster relief handouts. However, the use of prepaid cards has also been associated with certain risks in the context of cyber crime.

1. **Fewer Controls:** Prepaid cards have fewer controls on them than regular credit and debit cards issued by banks. This makes it easier for hackers to withdraw large amounts of money before detection  .

2. **Money Laundering:** Prepaid cards can be used to launder the proceeds of crime or fund terrorist plots. Criminals can use prepaid cards to parcel large sums of money into small amounts, helping to avoid arousing anti-money-laundering (AML) alerts when the funds are paid into the banking system and the wider economy. This practice is known as "smurfing" .

3. **Fraud Protection:** Branded prepaid cards from card networks such as Visa and Mastercard offer less risk, with the brands providing fraud protection and customer dispute options. The Financial Actions Task Force (FATF) has issued guidance regarding fraud and money laundering using prepaid cards .

It is important to be aware of these risks when using prepaid cards and to take appropriate precautions to protect against cyber crime.
