### Honey Pots

Honey Pots are a cybersecurity mechanism that is used to lure cybercriminals away from legitimate targets and gather intelligence about their identity, methods, and motivations. They are modeled after any digital asset, including software applications, servers, or the network itself .

- **Purpose**: Honey Pots are used to attract, detect, and deflect cybercriminals from hacking into legitimate targets .
- **Usage**: Honey Pots are mostly used by large companies and organizations involved in cybersecurity. They help cybersecurity researchers learn about the different types of attacks used by attackers .
- **Forensic purposes**: A research Honey Pot can also serve forensic purposes, quietly gathering data about the hackers who stole it .
- **Strategy**: While Honey Pots shouldn't serve as the centerpiece of a cybersecurity strategy, they should be considered a crucial component of one .
