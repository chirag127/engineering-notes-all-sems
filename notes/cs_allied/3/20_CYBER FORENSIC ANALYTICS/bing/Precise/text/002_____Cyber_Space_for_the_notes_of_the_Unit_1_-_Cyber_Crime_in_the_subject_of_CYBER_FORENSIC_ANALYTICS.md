### Cyber Space

- Cyber space refers to the virtual environment where communication over computer networks occurs.
- It is a global network of interconnected computer systems, where information is created, stored, and exchanged.
- Cyber space is not limited by physical boundaries and allows for the rapid exchange of information across great distances.
- It is a constantly evolving environment, with new technologies and applications being developed and implemented regularly.
- Cyber space is used for a wide range of activities, including communication, commerce, entertainment, and education.
- However, the open and interconnected nature of cyber space also makes it vulnerable to various forms of cyber crime, such as hacking, identity theft, and fraud.
- To combat these threats, various measures are taken to secure cyber space, including the use of encryption, firewalls, and anti-virus software.
- The study of cyber forensic analytics focuses on the detection, investigation, and prevention of cyber crime in cyber space.
