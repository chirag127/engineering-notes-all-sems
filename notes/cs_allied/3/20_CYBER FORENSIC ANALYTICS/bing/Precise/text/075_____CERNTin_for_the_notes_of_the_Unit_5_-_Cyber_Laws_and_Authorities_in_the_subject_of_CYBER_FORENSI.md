### CERNTin for the notes of the Unit 5 - Cyber Laws and Authorities in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

- CERNTin is an acronym that stands for Computer Emergency Response Team Information Network.
- It is a network of Computer Emergency Response Teams (CERTs) that work together to respond to computer security incidents.
- CERTs are responsible for responding to computer security incidents, such as hacking, viruses, and other malicious activities.
- CERNTin provides a platform for CERTs to share information and collaborate on incident response.
- CERNTin is an important tool for improving the overall security of computer systems and networks.
- By working together, CERTs can more effectively respond to security incidents and prevent future incidents from occurring.
- CERNTin is an important part of the overall cyber security infrastructure and plays a critical role in protecting against cyber threats.
