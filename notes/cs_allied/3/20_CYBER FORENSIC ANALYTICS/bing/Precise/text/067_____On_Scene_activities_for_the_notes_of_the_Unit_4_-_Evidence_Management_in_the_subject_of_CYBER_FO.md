### On Scene activities for the notes of the Unit 4 - Evidence Management in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

1. **Securing the Scene**: The first step in on-scene activities is to secure the scene to prevent contamination or loss of evidence. This involves establishing a perimeter, controlling access, and documenting the scene.

2. **Initial Survey**: The next step is to conduct an initial survey of the scene to identify potential evidence and to develop a plan for collecting and preserving it.

3. **Documentation**: It is important to thoroughly document the scene, including taking photographs, videos, and making sketches. This documentation should include the location and condition of all evidence.

4. **Evidence Collection**: The evidence should be collected in a systematic and careful manner to preserve its integrity. This involves properly packaging and labeling the evidence, as well as maintaining a chain of custody.

5. **Final Survey**: After the evidence has been collected, a final survey of the scene should be conducted to ensure that all evidence has been collected and that the scene has been thoroughly documented.

6. **Release of Scene**: Once all on-scene activities have been completed, the scene can be released. It is important to properly document the release of the scene and to ensure that all evidence has been properly collected and preserved.

These are some of the key on-scene activities that are involved in evidence management in the field of cyber forensic analytics. It is important to follow proper procedures and protocols to ensure the integrity of the evidence and the investigation.
