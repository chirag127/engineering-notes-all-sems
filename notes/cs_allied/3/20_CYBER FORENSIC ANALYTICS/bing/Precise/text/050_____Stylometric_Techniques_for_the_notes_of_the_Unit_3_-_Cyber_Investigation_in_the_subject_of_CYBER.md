### Stylometric Techniques

Stylometry is the application of the study of linguistic style, usually to written language. It is often used to attribute authorship to anonymous or disputed documents. Stylometry can also be used to identify characteristics of the author, such as gender, age, or ethnicity.

Here are some common stylometric techniques used in cyber investigations:

1. **Word frequency analysis:** This technique involves counting the frequency of words used in a document and comparing it to other documents to determine similarities or differences in writing style.

2. **Character frequency analysis:** Similar to word frequency analysis, this technique involves counting the frequency of characters used in a document and comparing it to other documents.

3. **Vocabulary richness:** This technique measures the diversity of words used in a document. A high vocabulary richness indicates a diverse vocabulary, while a low vocabulary richness indicates a limited vocabulary.

4. **Sentence length analysis:** This technique involves measuring the average length of sentences in a document and comparing it to other documents to determine similarities or differences in writing style.

5. **Function word analysis:** Function words are words that have little lexical meaning but serve to connect other words, such as prepositions, conjunctions, and pronouns. This technique involves analyzing the use of function words in a document and comparing it to other documents to determine similarities or differences in writing style.

These are just a few of the many stylometric techniques used in cyber investigations. These techniques can be used individually or in combination to help identify the author of a document or to determine similarities or differences in writing style between documents.
