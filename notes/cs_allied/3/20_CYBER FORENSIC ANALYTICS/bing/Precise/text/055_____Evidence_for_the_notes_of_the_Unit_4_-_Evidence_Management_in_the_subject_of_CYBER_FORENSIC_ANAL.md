### Evidence Management in Cyber Forensic Analytics

1. **Evidence Collection**: The first step in evidence management is the collection of evidence from various sources such as digital devices, network logs, and cloud storage. This involves the use of forensic tools and techniques to ensure that the evidence is collected in a forensically sound manner.

2. **Evidence Preservation**: Once the evidence is collected, it is important to preserve it in a manner that maintains its integrity and authenticity. This involves creating a chain of custody, making copies of the evidence, and storing it in a secure location.

3. **Evidence Analysis**: The next step is to analyze the evidence to extract relevant information and draw conclusions. This involves the use of specialized tools and techniques to examine the data and identify patterns and connections.

4. **Evidence Presentation**: The final step is to present the evidence in a clear and concise manner to support the investigation. This involves creating reports, visualizations, and other materials to communicate the findings to the relevant parties.

These are the key steps in evidence management in the field of cyber forensic analytics. It is important to follow these steps to ensure that the evidence is handled in a manner that is consistent with legal and ethical standards.
