### Digital device for the notes of the Unit 2 - Cyber Forensics in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

1. A digital device is any electronic device that can store, process, or transmit information.
2. Digital devices are commonly used in cyber forensics investigations to collect and analyze digital evidence.
3. Examples of digital devices include computers, smartphones, tablets, and external storage devices such as USB drives and hard drives.
4. In the context of cyber forensics, digital devices can be used to store and analyze data related to a cybercrime or incident.
5. Digital forensics tools and techniques can be used to extract and analyze data from digital devices to support an investigation.
6. It is important to follow proper procedures and guidelines when handling digital devices in a cyber forensics investigation to ensure the integrity and admissibility of the evidence.
7. Digital devices can also be used to document and report findings in a cyber forensics investigation.
8. The use of digital devices in cyber forensics is an important aspect of the field and is covered in Unit 2 of the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS.
