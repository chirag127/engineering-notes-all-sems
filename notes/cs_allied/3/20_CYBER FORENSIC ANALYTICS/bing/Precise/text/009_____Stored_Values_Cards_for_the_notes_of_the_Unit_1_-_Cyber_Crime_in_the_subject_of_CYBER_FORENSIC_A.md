### Stored Values Cards for the notes of the Unit 1 - Cyber Crime in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

- Stored value cards are a type of electronic payment card that stores a monetary value on the card itself.
- These cards can be used to make purchases or withdraw cash from ATMs.
- Stored value cards can be reloadable or non-reloadable.
- Reloadable cards can be reloaded with funds, while non-reloadable cards can only be used until the funds on the card are depleted.
- Stored value cards can be used for various purposes, such as gift cards, travel cards, and payroll cards.
- Stored value cards can be vulnerable to cybercrime, as they can be targeted by hackers to steal the funds stored on the card.
- To prevent cybercrime, it is important to use secure methods to load funds onto the card and to monitor the card for any unauthorized transactions.
- In the case of a cybercrime involving a stored value card, forensic analysis can be used to investigate the crime and gather evidence.
