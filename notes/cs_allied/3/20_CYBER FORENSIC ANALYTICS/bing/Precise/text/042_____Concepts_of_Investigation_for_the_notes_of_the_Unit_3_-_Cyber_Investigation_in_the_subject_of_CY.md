### Concepts of Investigation

Unit 3 - Cyber Investigation in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

1. **Identification**: The first step in any cyber investigation is to identify the incident or crime that has occurred. This involves gathering information and evidence to determine the nature and scope of the incident.

2. **Preservation**: Once the incident has been identified, it is important to preserve any evidence that may be relevant to the investigation. This includes securing the scene, collecting and documenting evidence, and taking steps to prevent further damage or loss of evidence.

3. **Collection**: The collection of evidence is a critical step in any cyber investigation. This involves gathering digital evidence from various sources, including computer systems, networks, and storage devices.

4. **Examination**: After the evidence has been collected, it must be examined to determine its relevance and reliability. This involves analyzing the data to identify patterns, connections, and other information that may be useful in the investigation.

5. **Analysis**: The analysis of evidence is the process of interpreting the data and drawing conclusions based on the evidence. This may involve using specialized tools and techniques to uncover hidden information or connections.

6. **Presentation**: The final step in any cyber investigation is the presentation of the evidence and findings. This involves preparing a report that summarizes the evidence and explains the conclusions reached during the investigation.
