### Mobile Payments

Mobile payments are a convenient and fast way to make transactions using a mobile device. However, with the increasing use of mobile payments, there are also increasing concerns about the security of these transactions.

- Mobile cybercrimes are still relatively rare, but major safety concerns, viruses and other mobile crimes have become major problems in recent years .
- 92% of ISOs believe that mobile payments will increase cybercrime significantly over the next few years .
- Tokenization is a technology that successfully promotes mobile payments, while protecting sensitive customer data against hackers and other cybersecurity threats .
- If a merchant system is compromised by a cyber attack, thieves will only be able to access tokenized data .
- Financial criminals aim to take advantage of increasing mobile payment traffic as companies undergo remote work, staffing changes, and other forms of pandemic-driven disruption .
- The risks attached to digital payments include hackers and scammers stealing bank account details, social security numbers and other personal information .

It is important to be aware of these risks and take necessary precautions to protect oneself while using mobile payments. Some measures that can be taken include using tokenization technology, being cautious while sharing personal information, and regularly monitoring bank statements for any suspicious activity.
