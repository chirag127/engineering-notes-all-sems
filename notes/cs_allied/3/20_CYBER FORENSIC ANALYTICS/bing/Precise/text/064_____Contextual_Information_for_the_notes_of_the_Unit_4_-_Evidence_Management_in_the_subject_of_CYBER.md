### Contextual Information for the notes of the Unit 4 - Evidence Management in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

1. Evidence management is a crucial aspect of cyber forensic analytics.
2. It involves the identification, collection, preservation, analysis, and presentation of digital evidence.
3. Digital evidence can include data from various sources such as computers, mobile devices, and cloud storage.
4. Proper evidence management is essential to ensure the integrity and admissibility of digital evidence in legal proceedings.
5. Chain of custody must be maintained to document the handling of evidence from the time of collection to presentation in court.
6. Standard operating procedures and best practices should be followed to ensure the proper handling of digital evidence.
7. Tools and techniques used in evidence management can include forensic imaging, hashing, and data carving.
8. It is important to stay up-to-date with the latest developments and advancements in the field of cyber forensic analytics to effectively manage digital evidence.
