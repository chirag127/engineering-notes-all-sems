### Hard Disk

- A hard disk is a data storage device that uses magnetic storage to store and retrieve digital information.
- It consists of one or more rigid rapidly rotating disks called platters, coated with magnetic material and with magnetic heads arranged to write data to the surfaces and read it from them.
- Hard disks are a type of non-volatile storage, meaning that the data stored on them remains even when the power is turned off.
- Hard disks are commonly used as the main storage device in computers, as they offer large storage capacity and fast access times.
- In the context of cyber forensics, hard disks can be an important source of evidence as they may contain data relevant to an investigation.
- Forensic analysts may use various techniques to recover data from hard disks, including deleted file recovery and file carving.
- It is important to follow proper procedures when handling hard disks in a forensic investigation to ensure the integrity of the evidence.
