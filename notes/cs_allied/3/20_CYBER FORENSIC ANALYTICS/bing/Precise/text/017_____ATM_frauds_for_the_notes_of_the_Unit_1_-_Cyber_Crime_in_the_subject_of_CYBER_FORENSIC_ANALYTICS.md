### ATM Frauds

ATM frauds are fraudulent activities that involve gaining illegal access to someone's ATM card and PIN to withdraw money from their account. Another kind of ATM fraud is breaking into an ATM and stealing money from the machine. However, with advanced technology, new methods and techniques of committing fraud have also arisen .

Some popular scams that thieves use include using a counterfeit device for access to the door to the ATM and using a false façade on the front of the machine. Some criminals can swipe data from free-standing ATMs using cracking programs .

Here are some common types of ATM frauds:
1. Skimming: This type of ATM scam involves a skimmer device that criminals place on top of or within the card slot .
2. Shimming: This is the latest update to skimming. Instead of reading your card number, criminals use a shim to read the chip on your card .

It is important to be aware of these types of frauds and take necessary precautions to protect oneself from becoming a victim.
