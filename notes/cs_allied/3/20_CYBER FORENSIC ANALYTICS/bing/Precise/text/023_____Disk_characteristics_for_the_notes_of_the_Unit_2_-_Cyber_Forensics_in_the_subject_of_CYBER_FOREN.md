### Disk Characteristics

1. **Storage Capacity:** The amount of data that can be stored on a disk.
2. **Data Transfer Rate:** The speed at which data can be transferred to and from the disk.
3. **Access Time:** The time it takes for the disk to locate and retrieve data.
4. **Form Factor:** The physical size and shape of the disk.
5. **Interface:** The method used to connect the disk to the computer.
6. **Power Consumption:** The amount of power the disk uses during operation.
7. **Reliability:** The likelihood of the disk failing or experiencing errors.
8. **Cost:** The price of the disk.

These are some of the key characteristics to consider when selecting a disk for use in cyber forensics. Each characteristic can impact the effectiveness of the disk in a forensic investigation. It is important to carefully evaluate these characteristics when selecting a disk for use in a forensic investigation.
