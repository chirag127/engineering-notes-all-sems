## Unit 2 - Cyber Forensics

1. **Introduction to Cyber Forensics:** Cyber Forensics is the process of collecting, analyzing, and preserving electronic evidence in order to investigate and prevent cybercrime.
2. **Types of Cybercrime:** Some common types of cybercrime include hacking, identity theft, phishing, and cyberstalking.
3. **Evidence Collection:** The first step in a cyber forensic investigation is to collect electronic evidence. This can include data from computer hard drives, mobile devices, and network logs.
4. **Evidence Analysis:** Once the evidence has been collected, it must be analyzed to determine its relevance to the case. This can involve using specialized software to recover deleted files, crack passwords, and identify patterns of behavior.
5. **Preservation of Evidence:** It is important to preserve electronic evidence in a way that maintains its integrity and admissibility in court. This can involve creating a forensic image of the data and maintaining a chain of custody.
6. **Legal Considerations:** There are many legal considerations that must be taken into account when conducting a cyber forensic investigation. These can include issues related to privacy, search and seizure, and the admissibility of evidence in court.
7. **Conclusion:** Cyber Forensics is a crucial tool in the fight against cybercrime. By collecting, analyzing, and preserving electronic evidence, investigators can help bring cybercriminals to justice and prevent future attacks.
