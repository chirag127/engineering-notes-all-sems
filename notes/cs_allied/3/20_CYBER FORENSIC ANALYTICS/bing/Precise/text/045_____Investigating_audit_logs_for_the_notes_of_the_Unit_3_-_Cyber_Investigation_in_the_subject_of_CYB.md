### Investigating Audit Logs for the Notes of the Unit 3 - Cyber Investigation in the Subject of Cyber Forensic Analytics

1. **Audit logs** are records of events that occur on a computer system or network. They can be used to track user activity, system changes, and security events.
2. In the context of cyber investigation, audit logs can provide valuable information for identifying and analyzing security incidents.
3. To investigate audit logs, it is important to have a clear understanding of the system or network being investigated, including its architecture, components, and normal behavior.
4. The investigation process typically involves collecting and analyzing log data, identifying patterns and anomalies, and correlating events to determine the cause of a security incident.
5. Tools such as log management and analysis software can be used to automate the collection and analysis of log data, making the investigation process more efficient.
6. It is important to ensure that audit logs are properly configured and maintained to provide accurate and complete information for investigation purposes.
7. In addition to investigating security incidents, audit logs can also be used for compliance and regulatory purposes, as well as for improving system and network security.
