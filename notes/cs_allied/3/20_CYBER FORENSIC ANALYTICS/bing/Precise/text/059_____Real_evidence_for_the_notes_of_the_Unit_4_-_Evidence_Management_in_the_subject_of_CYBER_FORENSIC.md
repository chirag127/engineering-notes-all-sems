### Real evidence for the notes of the Unit 4 - Evidence Management in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

1. Real evidence refers to physical objects that can be presented in court as evidence in a case.
2. In the context of cyber forensic analytics, real evidence can include digital devices such as computers, hard drives, and mobile phones.
3. These devices can contain valuable information that can be used as evidence in a case, such as emails, text messages, and other digital files.
4. The process of collecting, preserving, and analyzing this evidence is known as evidence management.
5. Proper evidence management is crucial in ensuring the integrity and admissibility of the evidence in court.
6. This involves following strict protocols and procedures to ensure that the evidence is not tampered with or altered in any way.
7. Chain of custody is an important aspect of evidence management, as it documents the handling and movement of the evidence from the time it is collected until it is presented in court.
8. Failure to properly manage evidence can result in the evidence being deemed inadmissible in court, which can have a significant impact on the outcome of a case.
