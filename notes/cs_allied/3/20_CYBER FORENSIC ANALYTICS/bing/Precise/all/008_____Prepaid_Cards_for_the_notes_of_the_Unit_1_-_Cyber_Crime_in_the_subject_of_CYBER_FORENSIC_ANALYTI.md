# Unit 1 - Cyber Crime: Prepaid Cards

Prepaid cards are payment cards that are preloaded with a specific amount of money. These cards can be used to make purchases or withdraw cash from ATMs, similar to a debit or credit card. However, unlike debit or credit cards, prepaid cards are not linked to a bank account.

Some key points to consider about prepaid cards in the context of cyber crime are:

1. Prepaid cards can be purchased anonymously, making them a popular choice for criminals to use in illegal activities.
2. Prepaid cards can be used to launder money, as they can be loaded with cash and then used to make purchases or withdraw cash without leaving a paper trail.
3. Prepaid cards can also be used in scams, where the scammer convinces the victim to load money onto a prepaid card and then provide the card details to the scammer.
4. Law enforcement agencies have difficulty tracking the use of prepaid cards, as they are not linked to a bank account and can be purchased anonymously.

It is important to be cautious when using prepaid cards and to be aware of the potential risks associated with their use. Always verify the legitimacy of any request to load money onto a prepaid card and be wary of any unsolicited offers or requests involving prepaid cards.
