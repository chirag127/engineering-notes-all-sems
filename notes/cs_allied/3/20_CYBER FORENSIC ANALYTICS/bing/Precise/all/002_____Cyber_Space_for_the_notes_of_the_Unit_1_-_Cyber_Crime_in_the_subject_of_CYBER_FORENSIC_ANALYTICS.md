# Cyber Space

Cyber Space refers to the virtual environment where communication over computer networks occurs. It is a domain created by the interconnection of computers and computer networks through the use of the internet. Some key points to consider when discussing Cyber Space in the context of Unit 1 - Cyber Crime in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS are:

1. Cyber Space is a global network of interconnected computer systems and information technology infrastructures.
2. It is a virtual environment where individuals and organizations interact and conduct business.
3. Cyber Space is not limited by physical boundaries and can be accessed from anywhere in the world.
4. The increasing reliance on technology and the internet has made Cyber Space an integral part of modern society.
5. Cyber Crime refers to illegal activities that take place in Cyber Space, such as hacking, identity theft, and online fraud.
6. The anonymity and global reach of Cyber Space make it an attractive environment for criminals to operate in.
7. Cyber Forensic Analytics is the application of scientific methods to collect, analyze, and present digital evidence in support of investigations into Cyber Crime.
8. Understanding the nature of Cyber Space and the threats it poses is essential for the prevention and investigation of Cyber Crime.
