# CERNTin

CERNTin is a topic in Unit 5 - Cyber Laws and Authorities in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS. Here are some key points to remember:

1. CERNTin stands for Computer Emergency Response Team in India.
2. It is an organization under the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology of the Government of India.
3. Its primary function is to respond to computer security incidents and to take measures to prevent such incidents from happening.
4. CERNTin also works to enhance the security of India's communications and information infrastructure.
5. It provides guidelines and best practices for organizations to follow in order to improve their cybersecurity.
6. CERNTin also conducts research and development in the field of cybersecurity to improve the overall security of the country's information infrastructure.
