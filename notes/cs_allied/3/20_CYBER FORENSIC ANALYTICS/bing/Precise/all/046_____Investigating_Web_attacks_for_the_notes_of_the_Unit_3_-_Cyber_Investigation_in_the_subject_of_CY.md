### Investigating Web attacks

Unit 3 - Cyber Investigation in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

1. **Identify the type of attack**: The first step in investigating a web attack is to identify the type of attack that has occurred. This could include attacks such as SQL injection, cross-site scripting (XSS), or distributed denial of service (DDoS).
2. **Collect evidence**: Once the type of attack has been identified, the next step is to collect evidence related to the attack. This could include log files, network traffic, and any other relevant data.
3. **Analyze the evidence**: After collecting the evidence, it is important to analyze it to determine the source and scope of the attack. This could involve using specialized tools and techniques to analyze the data and identify patterns or anomalies.
4. **Determine the impact**: Once the evidence has been analyzed, it is important to determine the impact of the attack. This could include identifying any data that has been compromised, as well as assessing the financial and reputational damage caused by the attack.
5. **Mitigate the damage**: After determining the impact of the attack, steps should be taken to mitigate the damage. This could include restoring any compromised data, implementing additional security measures, and notifying any affected parties.
6. **Prevent future attacks**: Finally, it is important to take steps to prevent future attacks. This could include implementing additional security measures, training employees on best practices, and regularly monitoring the system for any signs of attack.
