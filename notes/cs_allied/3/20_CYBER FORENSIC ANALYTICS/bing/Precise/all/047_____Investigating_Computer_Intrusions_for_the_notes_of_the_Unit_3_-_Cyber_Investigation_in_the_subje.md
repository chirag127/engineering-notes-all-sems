# Investigating Computer Intrusions

Unit 3 - Cyber Investigation in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

1. **Identify the intrusion:** The first step in investigating a computer intrusion is to identify that an intrusion has occurred. This can be done by monitoring system logs, network traffic, and user activity for any unusual or suspicious behavior.

2. **Contain the intrusion:** Once an intrusion has been identified, it is important to contain it to prevent further damage. This can be done by disconnecting the affected systems from the network, blocking the attacker's IP address, or disabling the compromised user accounts.

3. **Collect evidence:** After containing the intrusion, it is important to collect evidence to support the investigation. This can include system logs, network traffic, and any other relevant data that can help to identify the attacker and their methods.

4. **Analyze the evidence:** The collected evidence must be analyzed to determine the scope of the intrusion, the methods used by the attacker, and any data that may have been compromised.

5. **Remediate the intrusion:** After analyzing the evidence, steps must be taken to remediate the intrusion and prevent it from happening again. This can include patching vulnerabilities, improving security controls, and educating users on safe computing practices.

6. **Report the intrusion:** Finally, it is important to report the intrusion to the appropriate authorities and to any affected parties. This can help to prevent similar intrusions from happening in the future and can also aid in the prosecution of the attacker.
