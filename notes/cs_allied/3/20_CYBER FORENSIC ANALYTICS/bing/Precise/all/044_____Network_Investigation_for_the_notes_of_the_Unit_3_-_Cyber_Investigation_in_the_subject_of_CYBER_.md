### Network Investigation

Network investigation is a crucial aspect of cyber forensic analytics and is covered in Unit 3 - Cyber Investigation. It involves the collection, analysis, and preservation of digital evidence from networked environments. Here are some key points to consider when studying network investigation:

1. **Network traffic analysis:** This involves the examination of data packets that are transmitted over a network. Investigators use tools such as packet sniffers and protocol analyzers to capture and analyze network traffic.

2. **Log analysis:** Network devices such as routers, firewalls, and servers generate logs that record information about network activity. Investigators can analyze these logs to identify suspicious activity and reconstruct events.

3. **Intrusion detection:** Intrusion detection systems (IDS) are used to monitor network traffic for signs of unauthorized access or malicious activity. Investigators can use the alerts generated by an IDS to identify and respond to security incidents.

4. **Network forensics:** This involves the collection and analysis of network-based evidence. Investigators use specialized tools and techniques to recover and analyze data from network devices, including volatile data that may be lost when a device is powered off.

5. **Legal considerations:** Network investigations must be conducted in accordance with applicable laws and regulations. Investigators must ensure that they have the necessary legal authority to collect and analyze network-based evidence.

These are some of the key points to consider when studying network investigation as part of Unit 3 - Cyber Investigation in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS. It is important to have a thorough understanding of these concepts in order to effectively conduct network investigations.
