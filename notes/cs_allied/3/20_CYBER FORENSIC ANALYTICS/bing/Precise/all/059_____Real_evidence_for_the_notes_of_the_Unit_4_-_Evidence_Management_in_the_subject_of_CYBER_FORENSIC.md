### Real evidence for the notes of the Unit 4 - Evidence Management in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

1. Real evidence refers to physical objects that are relevant to the case and can be presented in court as evidence.
2. In the context of cyber forensic analytics, real evidence can include digital devices such as computers, hard drives, and mobile phones, as well as physical documents and other tangible items.
3. The process of collecting, preserving, and analyzing real evidence is crucial in building a strong case.
4. Evidence management involves the proper handling, storage, and documentation of evidence to ensure its integrity and admissibility in court.
5. Chain of custody is an important aspect of evidence management, as it documents the handling of evidence from the time it is collected until it is presented in court.
6. Proper evidence management can help prevent tampering, contamination, or loss of evidence, and can help ensure that the evidence is admissible in court.
