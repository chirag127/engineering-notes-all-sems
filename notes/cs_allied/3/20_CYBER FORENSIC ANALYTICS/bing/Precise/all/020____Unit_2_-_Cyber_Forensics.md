## Unit 2 - Cyber Forensics

1. Cyber Forensics is the process of collecting, analyzing, and preserving electronic evidence in order to investigate and prevent cybercrime.
2. The goal of cyber forensics is to provide evidence that can be used in a court of law to prosecute individuals or organizations involved in cybercrime.
3. Cyber Forensics involves the use of various techniques and tools to recover data from electronic devices, including computers, smartphones, and storage media.
4. The process of cyber forensics includes the identification, preservation, extraction, interpretation, and documentation of electronic evidence.
5. Cyber Forensics is a multidisciplinary field that involves expertise in computer science, law, and criminal justice.
6. Some common types of cybercrime that may require the use of cyber forensics include hacking, identity theft, cyberstalking, and online fraud.
7. Cyber Forensics is an important tool in the fight against cybercrime, as it allows law enforcement agencies to gather evidence and prosecute offenders.
8. The field of cyber forensics is constantly evolving, as new technologies and techniques are developed to keep pace with the changing nature of cybercrime.
