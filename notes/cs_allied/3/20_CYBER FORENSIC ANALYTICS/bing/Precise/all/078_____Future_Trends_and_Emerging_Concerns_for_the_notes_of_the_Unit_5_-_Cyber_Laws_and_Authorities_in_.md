# Future Trends and Emerging Concerns

Unit 5 - Cyber Laws and Authorities in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

1. **Increased use of Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning:** With the advancement of technology, the use of AI and ML in cyber forensics is expected to increase. This will help in automating the process of data analysis and identification of cyber threats.

2. **Rise of Internet of Things (IoT) devices:** The number of IoT devices is expected to increase significantly in the coming years. This will lead to an increase in the number of cyber attacks targeting these devices.

3. **Increased use of encryption:** The use of encryption is expected to increase in the future, making it more difficult for cyber forensic analysts to access and analyze data.

4. **Emergence of new cyber threats:** New cyber threats are expected to emerge in the future, requiring cyber forensic analysts to constantly update their knowledge and skills.

5. **Changes in cyber laws and regulations:** Cyber laws and regulations are expected to change in the future to keep up with the rapidly evolving technology landscape. Cyber forensic analysts will need to stay up-to-date with these changes to ensure compliance.

6. **Increased collaboration between organizations:** There is expected to be an increase in collaboration between organizations to share information and resources to combat cyber threats.

7. **Increased focus on privacy and data protection:** There is expected to be an increased focus on privacy and data protection in the future, with organizations implementing measures to protect the personal data of their customers.

These are some of the future trends and emerging concerns in the field of cyber forensic analytics. It is important for cyber forensic analysts to stay up-to-date with these trends and concerns to effectively combat cyber threats.
