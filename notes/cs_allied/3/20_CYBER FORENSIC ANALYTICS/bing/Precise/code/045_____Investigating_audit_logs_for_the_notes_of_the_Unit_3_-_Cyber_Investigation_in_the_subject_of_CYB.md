### Investigating Audit Logs for the Notes of the Unit 3 - Cyber Investigation in the Subject of Cyber Forensic Analytics

1. Audit logs are records of events that occur on a computer system or network.
2. They can provide valuable information for cyber investigations, including details about user activity, system changes, and security events.
3. To investigate audit logs, it is important to have a clear understanding of the system or network being investigated, including its architecture, operating system, and security controls.
4. The first step in investigating audit logs is to identify the relevant logs and their location. This may involve reviewing system documentation or consulting with system administrators.
5. Once the relevant logs have been identified, they can be collected and analyzed using specialized tools and techniques.
6. Analysis of audit logs may involve searching for specific events or patterns of activity, correlating data from multiple sources, and visualizing data to identify trends or anomalies.
7. The results of the audit log analysis can provide valuable evidence for a cyber investigation, and may be used to support further investigative actions or legal proceedings.
8. It is important to follow proper procedures when collecting and analyzing audit logs to ensure the integrity and admissibility of the evidence.
