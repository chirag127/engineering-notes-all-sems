### Types for the notes of the Unit 4 - Evidence Management in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

1. **Physical Evidence:** This type of evidence includes any physical object found at the crime scene or related to the crime. Examples include fingerprints, DNA samples, and computer hardware.

2. **Digital Evidence:** This type of evidence includes any digital data that can be used to support or refute a claim in a legal case. Examples include emails, text messages, and social media posts.

3. **Documentary Evidence:** This type of evidence includes any written or printed material that can be used to support or refute a claim in a legal case. Examples include contracts, invoices, and receipts.

4. **Testimonial Evidence:** This type of evidence includes any statements made by witnesses or other individuals with knowledge of the case. Examples include eyewitness accounts and expert testimony.

5. **Demonstrative Evidence:** This type of evidence includes any visual aids or physical objects used to help explain or demonstrate a point in a legal case. Examples include photographs, diagrams, and models.
