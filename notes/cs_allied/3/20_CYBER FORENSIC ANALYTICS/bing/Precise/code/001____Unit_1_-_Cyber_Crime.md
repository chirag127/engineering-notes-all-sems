## Unit 1 - Cyber Crime

1. **Definition:** Cybercrime refers to criminal activities that are committed using the internet or other forms of digital communication technology.
2. **Types of Cybercrime:** Some common types of cybercrime include hacking, identity theft, phishing, cyberstalking, and online fraud.
3. **Prevention:** To prevent cybercrime, it is important to use strong passwords, keep software and systems up to date, be cautious when clicking on links or downloading attachments, and be aware of common scams and tactics used by cybercriminals.
4. **Impact:** Cybercrime can have a significant impact on individuals, businesses, and society as a whole. It can result in financial loss, damage to reputation, and loss of personal information.
5. **Law Enforcement:** Law enforcement agencies around the world are working to combat cybercrime, through investigation, prosecution, and prevention efforts. Cooperation between different agencies and countries is essential in the fight against cybercrime.
