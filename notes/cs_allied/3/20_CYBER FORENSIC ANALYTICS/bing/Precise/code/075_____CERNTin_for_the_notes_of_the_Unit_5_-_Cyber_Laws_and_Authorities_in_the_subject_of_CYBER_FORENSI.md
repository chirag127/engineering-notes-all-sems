### CERNTin for the notes of the Unit 5 - Cyber Laws and Authorities in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

- CERNTin is an acronym for the **Computer Emergency Response Team**.
- It is a team of information security experts responsible for the protection against, detection of, and response to an organization's information security incident.
- The main function of CERNTin is to provide early warning of cyber threats, vulnerabilities, and to coordinate incident response.
- CERNTin is responsible for analyzing and responding to cyber threats, providing technical assistance to system administrators, and disseminating information to the public.
- CERNTin also plays a key role in coordinating the response to large-scale cyber incidents, such as those affecting critical infrastructure.
- CERNTin is an important component of an organization's overall information security strategy, and its effectiveness is critical to the security of the organization's information systems.
