### Jurisprudential Inconsistency

Jurisprudential inconsistency refers to the lack of consistency in the application of legal principles and the interpretation of the law. In the context of cybercrime and cyber forensic analytics, this can manifest in several ways.

1. **Lack of experience of judges:** Jurisprudential inconsistencies may be due mainly to the lack of experiences of the judges on computer related crimes, cyber-crimes, or crimes committed with high-tech devices and/or against them, etc.

2. **Admissibility of digital evidence:** Digital Forensics investigations jurisprudence is the theory and philosophy of the study of law and the principles upon which a law is based. For digital evidence to appear at court and be legally admissible, the evidence must be authentic, accurate, complete, and convincing to the jury.

3. **Complexity of digital forensic tasks:** With the increase in the number of computer crimes, law enforcement agencies in the field of digital forensics are expected to investigate more cases than before. Due to the complexity of digital forensic tasks, various investigation models have been developed to provide a structured approach to the investigation process.

These are some of the ways in which jurisprudential inconsistency can arise in the context of cybercrime and cyber forensic analytics. It is important for legal professionals and investigators to be aware of these issues and work towards addressing them to ensure that justice is served in a fair and consistent manner.
