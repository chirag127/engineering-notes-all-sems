### Concepts of Investigation for the notes of the Unit 3 - Cyber Investigation in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

1. **Identification**: The first step in any investigation is to identify the potential sources of evidence. This includes identifying the devices, systems, and networks that may contain relevant information.

2. **Preservation**: Once the potential sources of evidence have been identified, it is important to preserve the data in a forensically sound manner. This includes creating a forensic image of the data and ensuring that the data is not altered or destroyed.

3. **Collection**: The next step is to collect the data from the identified sources. This may involve physically acquiring the devices or remotely accessing the data.

4. **Examination**: Once the data has been collected, it must be examined to identify relevant information. This may involve using specialized forensic tools to analyze the data and recover deleted or hidden information.

5. **Analysis**: After the data has been examined, it must be analyzed to determine its significance to the investigation. This may involve correlating data from multiple sources and drawing conclusions based on the evidence.

6. **Presentation**: The final step in the investigation is to present the findings in a clear and concise manner. This may involve preparing a report or presenting the evidence in court.
