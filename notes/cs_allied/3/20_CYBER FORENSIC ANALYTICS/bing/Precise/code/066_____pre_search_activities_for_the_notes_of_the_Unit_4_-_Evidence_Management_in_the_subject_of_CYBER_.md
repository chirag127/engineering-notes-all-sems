### Pre-Search Activities

Pre-search activities are the steps taken before conducting a search in the context of evidence management in cyber forensic analytics. These activities are crucial in ensuring that the search is conducted in a lawful and effective manner. Below are some of the key pre-search activities:

1. **Obtaining Legal Authority:** Before conducting a search, it is important to obtain the necessary legal authority, such as a search warrant or court order. This ensures that the search is conducted in compliance with the law and that any evidence obtained is admissible in court.

2. **Defining the Scope of the Search:** The scope of the search should be clearly defined to ensure that the search is focused and relevant. This includes identifying the devices or systems to be searched, the type of data to be searched for, and the time frame of the search.

3. **Planning the Search:** A detailed plan should be developed for conducting the search, including the personnel involved, the tools and techniques to be used, and the procedures to be followed. This helps to ensure that the search is conducted in an organized and efficient manner.

4. **Securing the Search Area:** Before conducting the search, it is important to secure the search area to prevent any tampering or destruction of evidence. This may involve physically securing the area, taking photographs or videos of the scene, and documenting the chain of custody of any evidence collected.

5. **Preparing the Tools and Equipment:** The necessary tools and equipment should be prepared and tested before the search to ensure that they are in good working order. This may include forensic software, hardware write blockers, and other specialized tools for collecting and analyzing digital evidence.

These pre-search activities are essential in ensuring that the search is conducted in a lawful and effective manner, and that any evidence obtained is admissible in court. It is important to follow established procedures and best practices in conducting pre-search activities to ensure the integrity of the evidence and the overall investigation.
