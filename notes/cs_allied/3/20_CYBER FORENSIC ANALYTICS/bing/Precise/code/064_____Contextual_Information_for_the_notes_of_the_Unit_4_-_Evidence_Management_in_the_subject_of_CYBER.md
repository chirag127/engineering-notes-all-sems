### Contextual Information for the notes of the Unit 4 - Evidence Management in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

1. Evidence management is a crucial aspect of cyber forensic analytics.
2. It involves the identification, collection, preservation, analysis, and presentation of digital evidence.
3. Digital evidence can include data from computers, mobile devices, and other electronic devices.
4. Proper evidence management is essential to ensure the integrity and admissibility of digital evidence in legal proceedings.
5. Chain of custody must be maintained to document the handling of evidence from the time it is collected until it is presented in court.
6. Standard operating procedures and best practices should be followed to ensure the proper handling of digital evidence.
7. Evidence management also involves the use of specialized tools and software to analyze and present digital evidence.
8. Proper training and certification are important for individuals involved in evidence management to ensure they have the necessary skills and knowledge to handle digital evidence effectively.
