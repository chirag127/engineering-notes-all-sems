### Disk Imaging

Disk imaging is the process of creating an exact copy of a computer's hard drive or other storage media. This copy, known as an image, can be used for a variety of purposes, including:

1. **Backup and Recovery:** Disk images can be used to create a backup of a computer's data, allowing for easy recovery in the event of data loss or corruption.

2. **Forensic Analysis:** Disk images can be used in the field of cyber forensics to preserve the contents of a storage device for analysis. This allows investigators to examine the data without altering the original evidence.

3. **System Deployment:** Disk images can be used to quickly deploy a standard configuration to multiple computers, saving time and ensuring consistency.

There are several tools and techniques available for creating disk images, including hardware and software solutions. It is important to choose a method that is appropriate for the specific use case and to follow best practices to ensure the integrity of the image.
