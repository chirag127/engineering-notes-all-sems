### Report Preparations for the notes of the Unit 4 - Evidence Management in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

1. **Introduction**: Evidence management is a crucial aspect of cyber forensic analytics. It involves the collection, preservation, analysis, and presentation of digital evidence in a manner that is admissible in a court of law.

2. **Collection of Evidence**: The first step in evidence management is the collection of digital evidence. This involves identifying potential sources of evidence, such as computer systems, storage devices, and network logs, and using appropriate tools and techniques to acquire the data.

3. **Preservation of Evidence**: Once the evidence has been collected, it must be preserved in a manner that maintains its integrity and authenticity. This involves creating a forensic image of the data, which is an exact copy of the original data, and storing it in a secure location.

4. **Analysis of Evidence**: The next step is to analyze the evidence to extract relevant information. This involves using specialized tools and techniques to recover deleted files, search for keywords, and identify patterns of behavior.

5. **Presentation of Evidence**: The final step is to present the evidence in a manner that is admissible in a court of law. This involves preparing a report that documents the steps taken during the investigation, the findings, and the conclusions drawn.

6. **Conclusion**: Evidence management is a critical component of cyber forensic analytics. It involves the collection, preservation, analysis, and presentation of digital evidence in a manner that is admissible in a court of law. Proper evidence management is essential for ensuring the integrity and authenticity of the evidence and for building a strong case.
