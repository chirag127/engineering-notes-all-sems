### Cyber Extortion

Cyber extortion is an online crime in which hackers hold your data, website, computer systems, or other sensitive information hostage until you meet their demands for payment. It often takes the form of ransomware and distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) attacks, both of which could paralyze your business.

Cyber extortion attacks are about gaining access to an organization's systems and identifying points of weakness or targets of value. The hacker then demands money in return for allowing the organization to either regain control or stop the attack.

It is important to note that cyber extortion is a crime and can have serious consequences for both the victim and the perpetrator. It is important to take steps to protect your organization from cyber extortion and to report any instances of cyber extortion to the appropriate authorities.
