### Digital device for the notes of the Unit 2 - Cyber Forensics in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

- A digital device is any electronic device that can store, process, or transmit information.
- In the context of cyber forensics, digital devices can include computers, smartphones, tablets, and other storage media such as USB drives and external hard drives.
- Digital devices are important in cyber forensics because they can contain valuable evidence in the form of digital data.
- This data can include emails, text messages, documents, and other files that can be used to support or refute a legal case.
- Digital forensics involves the collection, preservation, analysis, and presentation of digital evidence.
- This process must be done in a manner that is forensically sound, meaning that the evidence must be collected and analyzed in a way that maintains its integrity and admissibility in a court of law.
- Digital forensics can be used in a variety of cases, including criminal investigations, civil litigation, and internal corporate investigations.
- It is important for forensic analysts to have a thorough understanding of the digital devices they are working with, including their hardware, software, and operating systems.
- This knowledge allows them to effectively collect and analyze digital evidence and to provide expert testimony in court.
