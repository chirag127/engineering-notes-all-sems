### Network Investigation

Network investigation is a crucial aspect of cyber forensic analytics, particularly in the context of Unit 3 - Cyber Investigation. Here are some key points to consider when studying this topic:

1. Network investigation involves the analysis of network traffic and data to identify and investigate potential security incidents or breaches.
2. This process can involve the use of various tools and techniques, such as packet sniffers, network flow analysis, and intrusion detection systems.
3. Network investigators must have a strong understanding of network protocols and architecture, as well as the ability to analyze large amounts of data.
4. The goal of network investigation is to identify the source and scope of a security incident, and to provide evidence that can be used in legal proceedings.
5. Network investigation is an ongoing process, and investigators must stay up-to-date with the latest threats and vulnerabilities in order to effectively protect their networks.
