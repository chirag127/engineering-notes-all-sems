Stored Value Cards (SVCs) are a type of prepaid payment card that can be used to make purchases, withdraw cash, or pay bills. They are similar to debit cards, but are not linked to a bank account and instead have a pre-loaded balance that can be used for transactions. SVCs are commonly used for a variety of purposes, including gift cards, payroll cards, and travel cards.
