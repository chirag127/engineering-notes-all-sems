### ATM frauds for the notes of the Unit 1 - Cyber Crime in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

ATM fraud is a type of cybercrime that involves the unauthorized use of Automated Teller Machines (ATMs) to steal money or sensitive information from individuals or financial institutions. It is an important aspect of the Unit 1 - Cyber Crime in the subject of Cyber Forensic Analytics.

There are several types of ATM fraud, including card skimming, card trapping, and ATM hacking. Card skimming involves the use of a device attached to an ATM that reads the magnetic stripe on a user's debit or credit card, allowing the attacker to steal the user's card information. Card trapping involves the use of a device that blocks the user's card in the ATM, forcing the user to leave the card behind, which the attacker can then retrieve and use to steal money or information. ATM hacking involves the use of malware or other malicious software to gain unauthorized access to the ATM or its network, allowing the attacker to steal money or information.
