### Investigating Web attacks for the notes of the Unit 3 - Cyber Investigation in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

Sure, here's a detailed explanation of investigating web attacks for the notes of Unit 3 in the subject of Cyber Forensic Analytics:

Investigating web attacks is a critical component of cyber forensic analytics, as web attacks are a common method used by attackers to gain unauthorized access to sensitive information and systems. Web attacks can take many forms, including cross-site scripting (XSS), SQL injection, and cross-site request forgery (CSRF).

When investigating a web attack, it is important to follow a systematic approach, including the following steps:

1. Collection of Evidence: The first step in investigating a web attack is to collect evidence from the affected systems and networks. This may include log files, network traffic captures, and other relevant data.

2. Analysis of Evidence: The collected evidence is then analyzed to determine the nature and extent of the attack. This may involve reviewing log files, network traffic captures, and other data to identify patterns and anomalies that indicate an attack.

3. Identification of Attack Vectors: Once the nature and extent of the attack have been determined, the next step is to identify the attack vectors used by the attacker. This may involve reviewing code, reviewing network traffic, and other relevant data to determine how the attacker gained access to the affected systems and networks.

4. Determination of Impact: The next step is to determine the impact of the attack on the affected systems and networks. This may involve reviewing system logs, network traffic captures, and other relevant data to determine the extent of the damage caused by the attack.

5. Mitigation and Remediation: The final step is to implement mitigation and remediation measures to prevent similar attacks from occurring in the future. This may involve patching vulnerabilities, implementing security controls, and other relevant measures.

In conclusion, investigating web attacks is a critical component of cyber forensic analytics. When investigating a web attack, it is important to follow a systematic approach, including the collection of evidence, analysis of evidence, identification of attack vectors, determination of impact, and mitigation and remediation. By following this approach, it is possible to effectively investigate web attacks, determine the extent of the damage, and prevent similar attacks from occurring in the future.
