Cyber forensic analytics is the process of using data analytics and other techniques to investigate and analyze digital evidence in support of a cybercrime investigation. The goal of cyber forensic analytics is to uncover and understand the activities of cyber criminals, and to provide evidence that can be used in a court of law.

Cyber forensic analytics involves a wide range of techniques, including data mining, network analysis, and digital forensics. Data mining is used to analyze large datasets to identify patterns and correlations that may indicate criminal activity. Network analysis is used to visualize and analyze the relationships between different devices and users in a network, and to identify potential sources of cybercrime. Digital forensics is used to collect, preserve, and analyze digital evidence, such as hard drives, mobile devices, and cloud storage, to uncover evidence of cybercrime.

In order to perform cyber forensic analytics, forensic analysts must have a deep understanding of the technology and techniques used by cyber criminals, as well as the laws and regulations that govern cybercrime investigations. They must also have the necessary tools and software to perform complex data analysis and digital forensics, such as data visualization tools, network analysis software, and forensic imaging tools.

Cyber forensic analytics is a critical component of cybercrime investigations, and is used to uncover and understand the activities of cyber criminals, and to provide evidence that can be used in a court of law. It is a complex and challenging field that requires a combination of technical expertise and legal knowledge, and is essential for protecting organizations and individuals from cybercrime.

In conclusion, cyber forensic analytics is the process of using data analytics and other techniques to investigate and analyze digital evidence in support of a cybercrime investigation. It involves a wide range of techniques, including data mining, network analysis, and digital forensics, and is a critical component of cybercrime investigations, essential for uncovering and understanding the activities of cyber criminals and providing evidence that can be used in a court of law.
