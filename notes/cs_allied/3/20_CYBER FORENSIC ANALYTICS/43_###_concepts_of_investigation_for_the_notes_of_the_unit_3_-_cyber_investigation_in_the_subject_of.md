1. Evidence collection: Collecting and preserving evidence related to the cybercrime, including digital evidence such as computer systems, networks, and data storage devices.

2. Evidence analysis: Analyzing the evidence in order to determine the cause of the cybercrime and to identify the responsible parties.

3. Interviews: Interviewing witnesses and suspects in order to gather additional information and to build a case.

4. Legal procedures: Following legal procedures and guidelines in order to ensure that the evidence is admissible in court.

5. Report writing: Writing a comprehensive report that summarizes the findings of the investigation and provides recommendations for preventing future cybercrimes.
