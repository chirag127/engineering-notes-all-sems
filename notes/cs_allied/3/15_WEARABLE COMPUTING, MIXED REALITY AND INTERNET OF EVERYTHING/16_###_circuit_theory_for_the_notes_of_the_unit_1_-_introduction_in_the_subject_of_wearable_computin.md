### circuit theory for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

Circuit theory is a branch of electrical engineering that deals with the analysis and design of electrical circuits. It is an important aspect of wearable computing, mixed reality, and Internet of Everything (IoE) fields, as these technologies often involve the design and implementation of complex electrical circuits.

The following are some of the key concepts in circuit theory:

1. Ohm's Law: This states that the current flowing through a conductor between two points is directly proportional to the voltage across the two points, and inversely proportional to the resistance between them.

2. Kirchhoff's laws: These laws describe the conservation of energy and charge in electrical circuits. Kirchhoff's current law states that the sum of the currents entering a node in a circuit must equal the sum of the currents leaving the node. Kirchhoff's voltage law states that the sum of the voltage drops in a circuit must equal the sum of the voltage rises.

3. Network analysis: This involves the analysis of the behavior of electrical circuits, including the determination of voltages, currents, and power.

4. Circuit design: This involves the design of electrical circuits, including the selection of components and the calculation of circuit parameters.

Overall, circuit theory is an important aspect of wearable computing, mixed reality, and IoE fields, as these technologies often involve the design and implementation of complex electrical circuits. Understanding the key concepts in circuit theory is important for effective design and development of wearable computing, mixed reality, and IoE devices.
