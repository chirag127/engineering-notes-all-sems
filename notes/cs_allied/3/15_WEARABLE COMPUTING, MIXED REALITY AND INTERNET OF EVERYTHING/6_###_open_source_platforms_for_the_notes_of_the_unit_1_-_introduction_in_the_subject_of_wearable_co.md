### Open Source Platforms for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

Open source platforms are software platforms that are made available to the public, with the source code freely available for use, modification, and distribution. They are an important aspect of the wearable computing, mixed reality and Internet of Things (IoT) fields, as they provide a foundation for innovation and development.

The following are some of the key benefits of open source platforms in the wearable computing, mixed reality and IoT fields:

1. Flexibility: Open source platforms allow for customization and modification to meet specific needs and requirements.

2. Innovation: The ability to modify and improve the platform encourages innovation and development.

3. Collaboration: Open source platforms foster collaboration and sharing among developers and communities.

4. Cost-effectiveness: Open source platforms are often free and can save significant costs compared to proprietary alternatives.

5. Interoperability: Open source platforms can be used with a variety of hardware and software components, promoting interoperability and integration.

Some of the popular open source platforms in the wearable computing, mixed reality and IoT fields include:

1. Android: An open source operating system for mobile devices, including wearable computing devices.

2. Raspberry Pi: A low-cost, single-board computer that can be used for a variety of IoT and wearable computing applications.

3. Arduino: An open-source electronics platform that is widely used for building a variety of electronic projects, including IoT devices and wearable computing devices.

4. Processing: A flexible software platform for creating interactive graphics, animations, and simulations, including for wearable computing and mixed reality applications.

Overall, open source platforms play a critical role in the wearable computing, mixed reality, and IoT fields, providing a foundation for innovation, collaboration, and cost-effectiveness.
