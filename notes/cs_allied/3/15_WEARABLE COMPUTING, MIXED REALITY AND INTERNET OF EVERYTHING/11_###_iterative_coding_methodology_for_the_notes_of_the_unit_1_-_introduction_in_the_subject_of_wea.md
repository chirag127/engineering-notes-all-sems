### Iterative coding methodology for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

Iterative coding methodology is a software development approach that emphasizes continuous iteration and improvement of code. This methodology is used in the development of wearable computing, mixed reality, and Internet of Things (IoT) applications.

Components of Iterative Coding Methodology:

1. Prototyping: Prototyping is the process of creating a working model of a software application. In iterative coding methodology, prototypes are created quickly and inexpensively, and they are used to test and refine the design and functionality of the application.

2. Testing: Testing is a critical component of iterative coding methodology. Testing is used to identify and fix bugs and to validate that the application meets the requirements and specifications.

3. Refinement: Refinement is the process of improving the design and functionality of the application based on the results of testing and feedback from users. In iterative coding methodology, refinement is a continuous process that occurs throughout the development cycle.

Applications of Iterative Coding Methodology:

1. Wearable Computing: Iterative coding methodology is used in the development of wearable computing devices, such as smartwatches and fitness trackers, to quickly create prototypes and test the functionality and design of the devices.

2. Mixed Reality: Iterative coding methodology is used in the development of mixed reality experiences, such as augmented reality and virtual reality, to quickly create prototypes and test the user experience and functionality of the experiences.

3. Internet of Things (IoT): Iterative coding methodology is used in the development of IoT applications, such as smart home systems and industrial control systems, to quickly create prototypes and test the functionality and design of the applications.

In conclusion, iterative coding methodology is a software development approach that emphasizes continuous iteration and improvement of code. This methodology is used in the development of wearable computing, mixed reality, and Internet of Things (IoT) applications, and it involves the use of prototyping, testing, and refinement to create high-quality software applications.
