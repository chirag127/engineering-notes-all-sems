### Raspberry Pi for the notes of the Unit 2 - SOFTWARE HARDWARE FRAMEWORKS in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

Raspberry Pi is a single-board computer that is designed to be low-cost, portable, and easy to use. It is widely used in the field of wearable computing, mixed reality, and the Internet of Things (IoT) for building and programming electronic devices and systems.

Unit 2 - Software Hardware Frameworks in the subject of Wearable Computing, Mixed Reality and Internet of Everything focuses on the different software and hardware frameworks that are used to build and program wearable devices, mixed reality systems, and IoT devices.

Some of the key features of Raspberry Pi include:

1. Single-board computer: Raspberry Pi is a complete computer on a single board, with a processor, memory, storage, and input/output interfaces.

2. Low-cost: Raspberry Pi is designed to be low-cost, making it accessible to a wide range of users, including students, hobbyists, and educators.

3. Portable: Raspberry Pi is small and portable, making it easy to carry and use in a variety of settings.

4. Versatile: Raspberry Pi is designed to be versatile, with a wide range of software and hardware support available, including operating systems, programming languages, and input/output interfaces.

By understanding Raspberry Pi and its role in wearable computing, mixed reality, and IoT, students can gain a deeper appreciation for the technology and its impact on these fields. This understanding can help students to develop the skills necessary to design, build, and program wearable devices, mixed reality systems, and IoT devices using Raspberry Pi and other related technologies.
