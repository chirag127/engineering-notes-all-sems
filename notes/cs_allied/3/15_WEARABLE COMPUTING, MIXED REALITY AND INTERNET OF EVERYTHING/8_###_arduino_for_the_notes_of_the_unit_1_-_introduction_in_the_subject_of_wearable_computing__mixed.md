### Arduino for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

Arduino is an open-source electronics platform that is widely used for building a variety of electronic projects, including wearable computing, mixed reality, and Internet of Everything (IoE) devices. It is a microcontroller-based platform that provides a user-friendly interface for programming and interfacing with sensors, actuators, and other components.

The following are some of the key features of the Arduino platform:

1. User-friendly interface: The Arduino Integrated Development Environment (IDE) provides a user-friendly interface for writing, uploading, and debugging code.

2. Wide range of components: The Arduino platform supports a wide range of components, including sensors, actuators, and communication modules, making it easy to build complex systems.

3. Open-source: The Arduino platform is open-source, allowing for the development of custom components and the sharing of code and projects.

4. Low cost: The Arduino platform is low cost, making it accessible to a wide range of users, including hobbyists, students, and professionals.

5. Community support: The Arduino platform has a large and active community, providing support and resources for users.

Arduino is a powerful tool for building wearable computing, mixed reality, and IoE devices. Its ease of use, low cost, and wide availability make it an ideal platform for hobbyists, students, and professionals alike.
