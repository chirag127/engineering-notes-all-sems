### Raspberry Pi for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

The Raspberry Pi is a low-cost, single-board computer that is widely used in the fields of wearable computing, mixed reality, and the Internet of Everything (IoE).

Features of Raspberry Pi:

1. Low Cost: The Raspberry Pi is a low-cost computer, making it accessible for a wide range of users and applications.

2. Small Form Factor: The Raspberry Pi is a small, compact computer that can be easily integrated into wearable devices and other IoT applications.

3. High Performance: Despite its small size, the Raspberry Pi is a high-performance computer that can run a wide range of software and applications.

4. Interconnectivity: The Raspberry Pi is equipped with a range of connectivity options, including Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and Ethernet, making it easy to connect to other devices and networks.

Applications of Raspberry Pi in Wearable Computing, Mixed Reality, and IoE:

1. Wearable Computing: The Raspberry Pi is widely used in wearable computing applications, such as smartwatches and fitness trackers, as a low-cost, high-performance computing platform.

2. Mixed Reality: The Raspberry Pi is used in mixed reality applications, such as augmented reality and virtual reality, to provide a platform for creating and running mixed reality experiences.

3. Internet of Everything (IoE): The Raspberry Pi is used in the Internet of Everything (IoE) to create connected devices and systems, such as sensors and actuators, that can collect and transmit data over the internet.

In conclusion, the Raspberry Pi is a low-cost, high-performance single-board computer that is widely used in the fields of wearable computing, mixed reality, and the Internet of Everything (IoE). The Raspberry Pi's small form factor, high performance, and interconnectivity make it an ideal platform for a wide range of applications in these fields.
