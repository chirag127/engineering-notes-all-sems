### push and pull for the notes of the Unit 3 - CYBERNETICS AND HUMANISTIC INTELLIGENCE in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

Push and pull are two communication models that are commonly used in the context of wearable computing, mixed reality, and the Internet of Everything (IoE). These models refer to the way data is transmitted between devices in a network.

Push communication is a model where data is actively pushed from one device to another. In this model, the sending device takes the initiative to send the data, and the receiving device passively receives the data. Push communication is often used in real-time applications, such as in the case of wearable devices, where data needs to be transmitted in real-time to provide an immersive experience.

Pull communication, on the other hand, is a model where data is passively pulled from one device to another. In this model, the receiving device takes the initiative to request the data, and the sending device actively sends the data. Pull communication is often used in applications where data needs to be retrieved on demand, such as in the case of IoT devices, where data is collected and stored in a database, and then retrieved and analyzed when needed.

In the context of wearable computing, mixed reality, and the IoE, both push and pull communication models have their advantages and disadvantages, and the choice between the two models depends on the specific requirements of the application. For example, push communication is well-suited for real-time applications, such as in the case of wearable devices, where data needs to be transmitted in real-time to provide an immersive experience. Pull communication, on the other hand, is well-suited for applications where data needs to be retrieved on demand, such as in the case of IoT devices, where data is collected and stored in a database, and then retrieved and analyzed when needed.

In conclusion, push and pull are two communication models that are commonly used in the context of wearable computing, mixed reality, and the IoE. Both models have their advantages and disadvantages, and the choice between the two models depends on the specific requirements of the application. By understanding the differences between push and pull communication, developers can make informed decisions about which model is best suited for their specific needs.
