### Arduino for the notes of the Unit 2 - SOFTWARE HARDWARE FRAMEWORKS in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

Arduino is an open-source electronics platform based on a microcontroller board and a software development environment. It is widely used in the field of wearable computing, mixed reality, and the Internet of Things (IoT) for building and programming electronic devices and systems.

Unit 2 - Software Hardware Frameworks in the subject of Wearable Computing, Mixed Reality and Internet of Everything focuses on the different software and hardware frameworks that are used to build and program wearable devices, mixed reality systems, and IoT devices.

Some of the key features of Arduino include:

1. Microcontroller: The Arduino board is based on a microcontroller, which is a small, low-power computer that is used to control and automate electronic devices and systems.

2. Open-source: Arduino is open-source, which means that the hardware and software design files are freely available, and the community can contribute to the development of the platform.

3. Easy to use: Arduino is designed to be easy to use, with a simple programming environment and a wide range of libraries and support resources available.

4. Interoperability: Arduino is designed to be interoperable with other electronic devices and systems, making it easy to integrate with other components and systems.

By understanding Arduino and its role in wearable computing, mixed reality, and IoT, students can gain a deeper appreciation for the technology and its impact on these fields. This understanding can help students to develop the skills necessary to design, build, and program wearable devices, mixed reality systems, and IoT devices using Arduino and other related technologies.
