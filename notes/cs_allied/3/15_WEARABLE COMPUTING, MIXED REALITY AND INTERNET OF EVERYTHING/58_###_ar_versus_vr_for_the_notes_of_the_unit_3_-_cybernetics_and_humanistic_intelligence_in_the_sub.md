Augmented Reality (AR) and Virtual Reality (VR) are two technologies that are used in the field of wearable computing, mixed reality, and the Internet of Everything (IoE). Both AR and VR are used to create immersive and interactive experiences, but they differ in the way that they interact with the real world.

Virtual Reality (VR) is a technology that creates a fully-immersive artificial environment that replaces the real world. VR is typically experienced through a headset that covers the user's eyes and ears, creating a sense of presence in the virtual environment. VR is often used for gaming, simulation, and training applications.

Augmented Reality (AR) is a technology that enhances the real world with virtual elements. AR is typically experienced through a device such as a smartphone or tablet, which displays virtual elements over the real world. AR is often used for applications such as navigation, product visualization, and gaming.

The choice between AR and VR will depend on the specific requirements of the application. VR is typically used for applications that require a fully-immersive experience, while AR is typically used for applications that require a more subtle and integrated interaction with the real world.
