### Data persistence for the notes of the Unit 3 - CYBERNETICS AND HUMANISTIC INTELLIGENCE in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

Data persistence refers to the ability of data to persist or remain stored even after the system or application that generated the data has been shut down. In the context of wearable computing, mixed reality, and the Internet of Everything (IoE), data persistence is a critical issue, as it allows data generated by wearable devices and IoT devices to be stored and retrieved for later use.

Data persistence is achieved by storing the data in a persistent storage medium, such as a hard drive, solid-state drive, or cloud storage. The data can then be retrieved and used by the system or application at a later time, even after the system or application has been shut down.

Data persistence is important in wearable computing, mixed reality, and the IoE for several reasons. For example, data generated by wearable devices, such as health data or location data, can be used to track the wearer's health or location over time. Data generated by IoT devices, such as temperature and humidity readings, can be used to monitor and control the environment.

In addition to providing a means of storing data, data persistence also provides a means of sharing data between different systems and applications. For example, data generated by a wearable device can be shared with a healthcare provider, or data generated by an IoT device can be shared with a home automation system.

In conclusion, data persistence is a critical issue in wearable computing, mixed reality, and the IoE. By providing a means of storing and retrieving data generated by wearable devices and IoT devices, data persistence allows organizations to track and monitor data over time, and to share data between different systems and applications. This helps to ensure that the data generated by wearable devices and IoT devices is used effectively and efficiently.
