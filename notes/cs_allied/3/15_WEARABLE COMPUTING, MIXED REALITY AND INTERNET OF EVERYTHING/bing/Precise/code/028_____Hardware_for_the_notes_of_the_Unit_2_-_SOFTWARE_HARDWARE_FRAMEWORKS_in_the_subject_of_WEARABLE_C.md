### Hardware for the notes of the Unit 2 - SOFTWARE HARDWARE FRAMEWORKS in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

1. Hardware refers to the physical components of a computer or electronic system.
2. In the context of wearable computing, mixed reality, and the Internet of Everything, hardware can include sensors, processors, displays, and communication devices.
3. Sensors can detect and measure various physical phenomena, such as motion, temperature, and light.
4. Processors are responsible for executing instructions and performing calculations.
5. Displays present visual information to the user.
6. Communication devices enable the exchange of data between devices and systems.
7. The choice of hardware can impact the performance, functionality, and user experience of wearable computing, mixed reality, and Internet of Everything systems.
8. Hardware design and selection should consider factors such as power consumption, size, weight, and durability.
9. Advances in hardware technology, such as miniaturization and increased processing power, have enabled the development of increasingly sophisticated wearable computing, mixed reality, and Internet of Everything systems.
10. The integration of hardware with software and frameworks is essential for the effective operation of wearable computing, mixed reality, and Internet of Everything systems.
