### Basic Electronics for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

1. **Electronics** is the branch of science and technology that deals with the study of flow and control of electrons and the use of electronic devices.
2. **Basic electronics** refers to the fundamental concepts and theories of electronics, including the behavior of electrons, the nature of electric and magnetic fields, and the principles of circuit analysis.
3. **Wearable computing** refers to the integration of advanced electronic technologies into clothing and accessories to enhance the functionality and convenience of everyday life.
4. **Mixed reality** is the merging of real and virtual worlds to produce new environments and visualizations where physical and digital objects co-exist and interact in real time.
5. **Internet of Everything** is the concept of connecting any device with an on and off switch to the Internet and/or to each other, including people, processes, data, and things.
