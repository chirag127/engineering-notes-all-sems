## Unit 3 - CYBERNETICS AND HUMANISTIC INTELLIGENCE

Cybernetics is the study of control and communication in systems, including living organisms and machines. It is concerned with understanding how systems regulate themselves and how they can be designed to achieve specific goals.

Humanistic intelligence, on the other hand, is a concept that refers to the integration of human cognitive abilities with technology. This can be achieved through the use of wearable computers, augmented reality, and other technologies that enhance human capabilities.

Some key points to consider when studying this topic include:

1. Cybernetics is a multidisciplinary field that draws on concepts from engineering, biology, and psychology.
2. The goal of cybernetics is to understand how systems regulate themselves and how they can be designed to achieve specific goals.
3. Humanistic intelligence is a concept that refers to the integration of human cognitive abilities with technology.
4. Wearable computers, augmented reality, and other technologies can be used to enhance human capabilities and achieve humanistic intelligence.
5. The study of cybernetics and humanistic intelligence can have important implications for the design of intelligent systems and the future of human-computer interaction.
