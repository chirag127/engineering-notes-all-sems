### Push and Pull

Push and pull are two fundamental concepts in the field of cybernetics and humanistic intelligence, particularly in the context of wearable computing, mixed reality, and the Internet of Everything.

1. **Push** refers to the act of sending information or data to a device or system without the user actively requesting it. This can include notifications, alerts, or updates that are automatically sent to the user's device.

2. **Pull**, on the other hand, refers to the act of the user actively requesting information or data from a device or system. This can include searching for information, requesting updates, or accessing specific data.

3. In the context of wearable computing, mixed reality, and the Internet of Everything, push and pull can be used to enhance the user experience by providing relevant and timely information to the user.

4. For example, a wearable device may use push notifications to alert the user to an incoming call or message, while the user may use pull to access information about their fitness or health data.

5. The balance between push and pull is important in ensuring that the user is not overwhelmed with information, while still providing them with the data they need to make informed decisions.

6. By understanding and utilizing push and pull, designers and developers can create more effective and user-friendly systems in the field of wearable computing, mixed reality, and the Internet of Everything.
