### Smart Cities and Wearable Computing as a form of urban design

Smart cities and wearable computing are two emerging trends in urban design that aim to improve the quality of life for citizens and enhance the efficiency of city services.

1. **Smart cities** are urban areas that use advanced technology and data analysis to improve the delivery of services, such as transportation, energy, and public safety. This can include the use of sensors and other devices to collect data on traffic patterns, air quality, and energy usage, which can then be analyzed to make informed decisions about city planning and resource allocation.

2. **Wearable computing** refers to the use of small, portable devices that can be worn on the body, such as smartwatches, fitness trackers, and augmented reality glasses. These devices can collect data on the user's health, activity levels, and location, and can provide real-time information and feedback to the user.

3. The integration of smart cities and wearable computing can provide numerous benefits for urban design. For example, data collected from wearable devices can be used to inform city planning decisions, such as the placement of bike lanes or the allocation of resources for public health initiatives.

4. Wearable devices can also provide real-time information to city officials, allowing them to respond quickly to emergencies or other events. For example, if a large number of people in a particular area are reporting high levels of air pollution through their wearable devices, city officials can take immediate action to address the issue.

5. In addition, the use of wearable technology can improve the accessibility of city services for citizens. For example, a person with a disability could use a wearable device to navigate public transportation or access information about city services.

Overall, the integration of smart cities and wearable computing represents an exciting opportunity for urban design, with the potential to improve the quality of life for citizens and enhance the efficiency of city services. It is an important topic in the study of wearable computing, mixed reality, and the internet of everything.
