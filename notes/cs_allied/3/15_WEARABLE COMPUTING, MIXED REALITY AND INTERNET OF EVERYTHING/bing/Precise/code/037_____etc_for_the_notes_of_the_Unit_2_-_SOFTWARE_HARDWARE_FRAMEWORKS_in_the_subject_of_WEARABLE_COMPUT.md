### Unit 2 - SOFTWARE HARDWARE FRAMEWORKS

In the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING, Unit 2 focuses on the software and hardware frameworks that enable these technologies.

1. **Wearable Computing:** Wearable computing refers to the integration of advanced electronic technologies into clothing and accessories that can be worn on the body. These devices can perform a range of functions, from simple notifications to complex data processing and analysis.

2. **Mixed Reality:** Mixed reality is the merging of real and virtual worlds to produce new environments and visualizations where physical and digital objects co-exist and interact in real-time. This technology is enabled by advanced hardware and software frameworks that allow for the seamless integration of virtual and real-world elements.

3. **Internet of Everything:** The Internet of Everything (IoE) is the networked connection of people, process, data, and things. It is an extension of the Internet of Things (IoT) that includes not only connected devices but also the integration of advanced data analytics, machine learning, and artificial intelligence technologies.

4. **Software Frameworks:** Software frameworks are pre-built libraries and tools that provide a standardized way to build and deploy applications. In the context of wearable computing, mixed reality, and the Internet of Everything, these frameworks provide developers with the tools they need to create advanced applications that can interact with and process data from a wide range of connected devices.

5. **Hardware Frameworks:** Hardware frameworks refer to the physical components and systems that enable these technologies. This includes the sensors, processors, and communication technologies that are integrated into wearable devices, mixed reality headsets, and other connected devices.

In summary, Unit 2 of the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING focuses on the software and hardware frameworks that enable these advanced technologies. These frameworks provide the foundation for the development of advanced applications that can interact with and process data from a wide range of connected devices.
