### Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION: WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

1. **Wearable Computing** refers to the integration of advanced electronic technologies into clothing and accessories to enhance the wearer's experience and capabilities.
2. **Mixed Reality** is the merging of real and virtual worlds to produce new environments and visualizations where physical and digital objects co-exist and interact in real-time.
3. **Internet of Everything** is the concept of connecting any device with an on and off switch to the Internet and/or to each other, including people, processes, data, and things.
4. These technologies have the potential to revolutionize the way we interact with the world around us, providing new opportunities for communication, entertainment, and productivity.
5. Wearable devices can range from smartwatches and fitness trackers to augmented reality headsets and smart clothing.
6. Mixed reality technologies can be used for a variety of applications, including gaming, education, and training.
7. The Internet of Everything has the potential to improve efficiency and productivity in industries such as healthcare, transportation, and manufacturing.
8. As these technologies continue to evolve, it is important to consider the ethical and societal implications of their widespread adoption.
