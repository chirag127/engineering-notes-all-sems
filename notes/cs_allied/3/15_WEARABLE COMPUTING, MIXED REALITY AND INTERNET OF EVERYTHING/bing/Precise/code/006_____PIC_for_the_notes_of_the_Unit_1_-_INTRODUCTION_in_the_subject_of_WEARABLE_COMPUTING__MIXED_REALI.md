### PIC for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

- PIC stands for Peripheral Interface Controller.
- It is a family of microcontrollers made by Microchip Technology.
- PICs are popular due to their low cost, wide availability, and large user base.
- They are commonly used in embedded systems and for prototyping.
- PICs can be programmed using assembly language or a high-level language such as C.
- They have a range of features including timers, analog-to-digital converters, and serial communication interfaces.
- PICs are used in a variety of applications including wearable computing, mixed reality, and the Internet of Everything.
- In the context of wearable computing, PICs can be used to interface with sensors and other peripherals to collect and process data.
- In mixed reality, PICs can be used to control and interact with virtual and augmented reality environments.
- In the Internet of Everything, PICs can be used to connect and control a wide range of devices and systems.
