### Oculus Rift

Oculus Rift is a line of virtual reality headsets developed and manufactured by Oculus VR, a division of Meta Platforms. It was released on March 28, 2016. In 2012 Oculus initiated a Kickstarter campaign to fund the Rift's development, after being founded as an independent company two months prior .

The Oculus Rift itself is a pair of virtual reality goggles, complete with headphones, that connect to a gaming PC or laptop . It is designed to provide an immersive gaming experience, with improved optics, tracking, and ergonomics .

It is important to note that the Oculus Rift is a discontinued line of virtual reality headsets . However, the Oculus Rift S, which is the most advanced PC-powered VR headset from Oculus, is still available .
