### Mixed Reality

Mixed Reality (MR) is a technology that combines the physical and digital worlds, creating a hybrid environment where physical and digital objects coexist and interact in real-time. It is a form of extended reality that includes both virtual reality (VR) and augmented reality (AR).

Some key points to consider when studying Mixed Reality for Unit 3 - CYBERNETICS AND HUMANISTIC INTELLIGENCE in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING are:

1. Mixed Reality is a combination of the physical and digital worlds, creating a hybrid environment.
2. It includes both virtual reality (VR) and augmented reality (AR).
3. MR allows for physical and digital objects to coexist and interact in real-time.
4. MR technology can be used in a variety of applications, including gaming, education, and training.
5. MR can enhance the user's experience by providing a more immersive and interactive environment.
