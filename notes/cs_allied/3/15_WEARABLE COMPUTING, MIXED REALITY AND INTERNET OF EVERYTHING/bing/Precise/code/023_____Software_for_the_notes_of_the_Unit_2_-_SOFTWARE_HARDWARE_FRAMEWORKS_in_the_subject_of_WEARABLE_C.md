### Software for the notes of the Unit 2 - SOFTWARE HARDWARE FRAMEWORKS in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

- **Windows Mixed Reality** is a software that can be downloaded and installed on a Windows computer. It is 1 GB in size and the download time will vary depending on the internet connection.
- **Virtual reality** is a combination of multiple technologies, including computer graphics technology, multimedia technology, sensor technology, human-computer interaction technology, network technology, 3D holographic projection technology, and simulation technology.
- **Mixed reality (MR)** is an integration of virtual content and the real world environment that enables interaction among elements of both.
- **Holography** is the creation of a 3D image in space that can be explored from all angles.
- **TelePresence** is a form of robotic remote control in which a human operator has a sense of being in another location.
- **Gesture recognition** can be conducted through wearable hand devices like data gloves. It is a wearable sensor input device of human–computer interaction applied for hand motion capture.
