# Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION: Measurements in Wearable Computing, Mixed Reality and Internet of Everything

- **Wearable Computing:** Wearable computing refers to the integration of advanced electronic technologies into clothing and accessories to enhance the functionality and convenience of the wearer. Measurements in wearable computing include the size and weight of the device, battery life, processing power, and sensor accuracy.

- **Mixed Reality:** Mixed reality is the merging of real and virtual worlds to produce new environments and visualizations where physical and digital objects co-exist and interact in real-time. Measurements in mixed reality include the resolution and field of view of the display, tracking accuracy, and latency.

- **Internet of Everything:** The Internet of Everything (IoE) is the networked connection of people, process, data, and things. Measurements in IoE include the number of connected devices, the amount of data generated and transmitted, and the speed and reliability of the network.

These measurements are important for the development and evaluation of wearable computing, mixed reality, and IoE technologies. They provide a means of comparing different devices and systems, and can help to identify areas for improvement and optimization.
