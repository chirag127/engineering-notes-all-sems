### Smart Cities and Wearable Computing as a form of urban design

- The research of the Smart Cities group focuses on intelligent, sustainable buildings, mobility systems, and cities. It explores the application of new technologies to enabling urban energy efficiency and sustainability, enhanced opportunity and equity, and cultural creativity .
- Wearables are presented as assistive technology to support persons with disabilities (PwD) to face the urban space in an autonomous and independently way .
- The smart cities concept argues technology should be at the front of design. Providing smartness within our cities does not just mean creating a digital interface, but it means using data and digital technology to make more informed decisions and improve quality of life by analyzing use and demand patterns .
- Urban systems design is a novel approach to shaping smart cities by integrating urban design, system science, and digital technologies .
- Urban computing has been defined as “a process of acquisition, integration, and analysis of big and heterogeneous data generated by diverse sources in urban spaces, such as sensors, devices, vehicles, buildings, and humans, to tackle the major issues that cities face .
- Smart energy city (SEC) has emerged as the latest urban development strategy in European countries. It can vastly accelerate cities’ decarbonization efforts, which can have several co-benefits. Capitalizing on advances in information and communication technologies (ICT), various SEC projects have been initiated and seek to integrate ICT into urban design .
