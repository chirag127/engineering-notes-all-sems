### Representing “reality” with computers

#### Unit 2 - SOFTWARE HARDWARE FRAMEWORKS

#### WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

- Computers can represent reality through the use of various hardware and software frameworks.
- These frameworks allow for the creation of virtual environments that can simulate real-world experiences.
- Wearable computing devices, such as smartwatches and fitness trackers, can collect data from the user and their environment to provide a more personalized experience.
- Mixed reality technologies, such as augmented and virtual reality, can overlay digital information onto the physical world or create entirely virtual environments.
- The Internet of Everything (IoE) connects people, processes, data, and things to enhance the relevancy of networked connections and provide more valuable experiences.
- These technologies can be used to create more immersive and interactive experiences, allowing users to interact with digital information in new and innovative ways.
- However, it is important to note that the representation of reality through computers is not always accurate and can be influenced by various factors, such as the limitations of the technology and the biases of the creators.
