### Unit 3 - CYBERNETICS AND HUMANISTIC INTELLIGENCE
#### Push and Pull

1. Push and pull are two fundamental concepts in the field of cybernetics and humanistic intelligence.
2. Push refers to the act of exerting force on an object in order to move it away from the source of the force.
3. Pull, on the other hand, refers to the act of exerting force on an object in order to move it towards the source of the force.
4. These two concepts are often used in the context of wearable computing, mixed reality, and the internet of everything, where devices and systems interact with each other and with the user in order to achieve a desired outcome.
5. For example, a wearable device may use push notifications to alert the user of an incoming message or event, while a mixed reality system may use pull technology to retrieve information from the internet in order to enhance the user's experience.
6. Understanding the concepts of push and pull, and how they can be applied in the context of cybernetics and humanistic intelligence, is essential for the development of effective and efficient systems in the field of wearable computing, mixed reality, and the internet of everything.
