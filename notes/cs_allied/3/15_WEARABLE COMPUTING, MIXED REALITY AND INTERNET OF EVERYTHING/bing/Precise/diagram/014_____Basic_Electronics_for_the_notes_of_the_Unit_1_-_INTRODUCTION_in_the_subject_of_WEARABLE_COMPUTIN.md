### Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION: Basic Electronics

Basic electronics is the study of electronic components, circuits, and systems. It is the foundation for understanding more advanced topics in electronics and electrical engineering. Here are some key concepts in basic electronics:

1. **Electricity:** Electricity is the flow of electrons through a conductor. It is measured in units of current (amperes), voltage (volts), and resistance (ohms).
2. **Electronic Components:** Electronic components are the building blocks of electronic circuits. Some common components include resistors, capacitors, inductors, diodes, and transistors.
3. **Circuits:** A circuit is a closed loop of conductive material that allows electricity to flow. Circuits can be simple, such as a single battery and a light bulb, or complex, such as a computer motherboard.
4. **Ohm's Law:** Ohm's Law is a fundamental principle in electronics that relates current, voltage, and resistance. It states that the current through a conductor between two points is directly proportional to the voltage across the two points, and inversely proportional to the resistance between them.
5. **Series and Parallel Circuits:** In a series circuit, components are connected end-to-end, so that the same current flows through all of them. In a parallel circuit, components are connected side-by-side, so that the voltage across each component is the same.

These are just a few of the basic concepts in electronics. Understanding these concepts is essential for further study in the field of wearable computing, mixed reality, and the Internet of Everything.
