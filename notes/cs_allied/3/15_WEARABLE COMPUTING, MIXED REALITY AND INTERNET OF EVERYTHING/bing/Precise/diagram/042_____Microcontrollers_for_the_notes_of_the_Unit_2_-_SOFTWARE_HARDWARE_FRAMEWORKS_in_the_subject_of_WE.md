### Microcontrollers

Microcontrollers are small, low-cost computers that are embedded in a wide range of devices and systems. They are used to control and manage the operation of these devices and systems, and are an essential component of many modern technologies.

Some key points to consider when studying microcontrollers in the context of Unit 2 - SOFTWARE HARDWARE FRAMEWORKS in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING are:

1. Microcontrollers are designed to be small, low-power, and inexpensive, making them ideal for use in wearable computing and other portable devices.
2. They are typically programmed using a high-level language such as C or C++, and can be interfaced with a variety of sensors and other hardware components.
3. Microcontrollers are often used to manage the flow of data between different components of a system, and to perform real-time processing and control tasks.
4. They are an essential component of many mixed reality and Internet of Everything systems, providing the computational power and control capabilities needed to support these technologies.
5. There are many different types of microcontrollers available, each with its own set of features and capabilities. Choosing the right microcontroller for a particular application requires careful consideration of factors such as processing power, memory, and connectivity options.
