# IoT and Wearables

IoT and Wearables are two rapidly growing fields that are closely related. Wearable technology is evolving into an important category of the Internet of things, with life-changing applications in medicine and other fields.

- Wearables and IoT combined produce outstanding results. They include sensors, chips, IoT protocols, and all types of technology. Wearables work along with apps, which process the information they collect.
- Wearables technology that use IoT sensors looks forward to giving personal information to the user. The more data the person collects in his smartphone, the better he feels about its health; it is a way to keep control of physical state (and maybe avoid going to the doctor).
- IoT wearables expand the functionality of ordinary objects. Thus, the whole new category of wearables for banking and finance emerge—connected cards, wristbands and even clothes and jewelry that enable easy payments and the management of consumer finances.
- Health- and fitness-oriented wearable devices that offer biometric measurements such as heart rate, perspiration levels, and even complex measurements like oxygen levels in the bloodstream are also becoming available. Technology advancements may even allow alcohol levels or other similar measurements to be made via a wearable device.

These are just a few examples of how IoT and Wearables are changing the way we interact with technology and the world around us. As these fields continue to grow and evolve, we can expect to see even more exciting developments in the future.
