# WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

Wearable computing, mixed reality, and the Internet of Everything are all interconnected technologies that are rapidly advancing and changing the way we interact with the world around us.

- **Wearable Computing:** Wearable devices connect people to the “internet of things” through direct contact with the body. These devices include smartwatches, fitness trackers, smart glasses, and virtual reality headsets .

- **Mixed Reality:** Wearable mobile computers like Magic Leap One or HoloLens 2 are capable of augmented reality (AR), mixed reality (MR), and spatial computing. They offer a new pattern of computing, with displays that feel vast when compared to screens because of their ability to project the digital into the physical space all around you .

- **Internet of Everything:** The Internet of Things (IoT) describes the growing network of internet-enabled devices. It is enabling smart cities and, in the future, driverless cars. Along with other emerging technologies such as AI, the IoT is part of the Fourth Industrial Revolution .

In the future, wearable technology could support the creation of an entire user-generated, non-verbal language. With a single swipe, consumers could send a loved one a message they can feel through unique light, sound, and vibration patterns on their wearable device . The WAT (Wearable Assistive Technology) enables elderly people to improve interactions with Mixed Reality (MR) and Internet of Things (IoT) technologies. It properly aids and assists elderly people in daily activities such as analyzing the environment, recognizing and searching for objects, wayfinding, and navigation .

These technologies are rapidly advancing and changing the way we interact with the world around us. They offer exciting new possibilities for communication, interaction, and exploration.
