### Unit 2 - SOFTWARE HARDWARE FRAMEWORKS
#### Digital vs. Analog circuits

1. **Definition**: Digital circuits represent and manipulate information using discrete values, typically 0 and 1, while analog circuits represent and manipulate information using continuous signals.
2. **Examples**: Digital circuits are commonly found in computers, smartphones, and other digital devices, while analog circuits are commonly found in audio and video equipment, sensors, and other devices that deal with continuous signals.
3. **Advantages of Digital Circuits**: Digital circuits are generally easier to design, more reliable, and less susceptible to noise and interference than analog circuits. They can also perform complex operations and store large amounts of data.
4. **Advantages of Analog Circuits**: Analog circuits can provide higher resolution and more accurate representation of continuous signals than digital circuits. They can also be faster and consume less power in some applications.
5. **Conversion**: Analog-to-digital converters (ADCs) and digital-to-analog converters (DACs) are used to convert between analog and digital signals.
6. **Hybrid Systems**: Many systems use a combination of analog and digital circuits to take advantage of the strengths of both technologies.
