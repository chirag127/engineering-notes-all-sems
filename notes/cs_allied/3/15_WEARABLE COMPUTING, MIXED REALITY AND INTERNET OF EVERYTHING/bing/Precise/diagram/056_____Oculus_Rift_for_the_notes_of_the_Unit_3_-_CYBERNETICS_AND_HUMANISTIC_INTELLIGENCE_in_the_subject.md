### Oculus Rift

Oculus Rift is a line of virtual reality headsets developed and manufactured by Oculus VR, a division of Meta Platforms. The Oculus Rift was released on March 28, 2016, after being funded through a Kickstarter campaign in 2012 .

The Oculus Rift is a pair of virtual reality goggles, complete with headphones, that connect to a gaming PC or laptop. It was invented by VR enthusiast Palmer Luckey, who later went on to found Oculus VR with Brendan Iribe, who became CEO .

The Oculus Rift allows users to experience VR's best games with improved optics, tracking, and ergonomics .
