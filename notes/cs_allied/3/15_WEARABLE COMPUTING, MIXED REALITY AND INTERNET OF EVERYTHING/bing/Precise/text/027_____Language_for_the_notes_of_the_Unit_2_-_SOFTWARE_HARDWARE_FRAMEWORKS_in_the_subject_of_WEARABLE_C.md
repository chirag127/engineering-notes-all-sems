### Language

- Language is a system of communication that uses symbols, such as words, gestures, or vocal sounds, to convey information or express thoughts and feelings.
- In the context of software and hardware frameworks for wearable computing, mixed reality, and the Internet of Everything, language refers to the programming languages used to develop software and the communication protocols used to transmit data between devices.
- Common programming languages used in these fields include C++, Java, and Python.
- Communication protocols, such as Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, and cellular networks, enable devices to exchange data and interact with each other.
- The choice of language and communication protocol can have a significant impact on the performance, functionality, and interoperability of a system.
- It is important for developers to have a strong understanding of the languages and protocols used in their field in order to design and implement effective systems.
