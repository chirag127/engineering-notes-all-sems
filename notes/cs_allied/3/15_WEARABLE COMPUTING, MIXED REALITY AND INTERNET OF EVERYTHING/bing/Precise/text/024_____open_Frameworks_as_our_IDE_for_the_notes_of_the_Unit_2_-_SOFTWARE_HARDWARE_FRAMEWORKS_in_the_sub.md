### Open Frameworks as our IDE for the notes of the Unit 2 - SOFTWARE HARDWARE FRAMEWORKS in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

- OpenFrameworks is an open-source C++ toolkit designed to assist the creative process by providing a simple and intuitive framework for experimentation.
- It is designed to work as a general purpose glue, and wraps together several commonly used libraries under a tidy interface: OpenGL for graphics, rtAudio for audio input and output, FreeType for fonts, FreeImage for image input and output, Quicktime for video playing and sequence grabbing.
- OpenFrameworks is designed to be a starting point for creative coding. It is not a finished product, but rather a work-in-progress, with an active community of developers and users contributing to its growth and development.
- OpenFrameworks is cross-platform and can be used to develop applications for Windows, macOS, Linux, iOS, Android, and Raspberry Pi.
- OpenFrameworks is well-suited for developing applications that involve real-time interaction with video, sound, and other media.
- OpenFrameworks is widely used in the fields of art, design, and research, and has been used to create a wide range of projects, from interactive installations to data visualizations to experimental games.
- OpenFrameworks is a powerful tool for developing applications that involve real-time interaction with media, and is an excellent choice for developers working in the fields of wearable computing, mixed reality, and the Internet of Everything.
