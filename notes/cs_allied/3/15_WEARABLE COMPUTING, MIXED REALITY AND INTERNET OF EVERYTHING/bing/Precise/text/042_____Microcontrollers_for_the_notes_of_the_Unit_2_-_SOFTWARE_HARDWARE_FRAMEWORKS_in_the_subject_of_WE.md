### Microcontrollers

Microcontrollers are small, low-cost computers that are embedded in a wide range of devices and systems. They are used to control and monitor the operation of these devices and systems, and to provide them with intelligence and interactivity.

Some key points to consider when studying microcontrollers in the context of Unit 2 - SOFTWARE HARDWARE FRAMEWORKS in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING are:

1. Microcontrollers are typically designed to be low-power, low-cost, and small in size, making them ideal for use in wearable computing and other portable devices.
2. They can be programmed to perform a wide range of tasks, from simple control functions to complex data processing and analysis.
3. Microcontrollers are often used in conjunction with sensors and other hardware components to collect data and provide feedback to the user.
4. They can also be used to provide connectivity to other devices and systems, enabling the creation of complex networks of interconnected devices.
5. Microcontrollers are an essential component of many wearable computing, mixed reality, and Internet of Everything systems, providing the intelligence and interactivity that these systems require.
