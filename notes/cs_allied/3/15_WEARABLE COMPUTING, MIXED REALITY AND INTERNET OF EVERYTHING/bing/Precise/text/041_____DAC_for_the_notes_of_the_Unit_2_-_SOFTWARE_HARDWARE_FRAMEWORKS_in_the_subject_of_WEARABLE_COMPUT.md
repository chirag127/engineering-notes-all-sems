### DAC

DAC stands for Digital-to-Analog Converter. It is a system that converts digital signals into analog signals. In the context of Unit 2 - SOFTWARE HARDWARE FRAMEWORKS in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING, DAC plays a crucial role in the conversion of digital data into analog signals that can be used by wearable devices and other hardware.

Some key points to note about DACs are:

1. DACs are used to convert digital data into analog signals that can be used by hardware devices.
2. The accuracy of the conversion depends on the resolution of the DAC.
3. There are different types of DACs, including binary-weighted, R-2R ladder, and thermometer-coded DACs.
4. The choice of DAC depends on the application and the required accuracy of the conversion.
