### History for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

1. Wearable computing refers to the use of small, portable, and often fashionable computing devices that can be worn on the body.
2. The concept of wearable computing has been around for several decades, with early prototypes and concepts dating back to the 1960s.
3. One of the earliest examples of wearable computing is the calculator watch, which was introduced in the 1970s.
4. In the 1980s and 1990s, research into wearable computing began to accelerate, with the development of head-mounted displays and other wearable devices.
5. Mixed reality refers to the merging of real and virtual worlds to produce new environments and visualizations where physical and digital objects co-exist and interact in real-time.
6. The concept of mixed reality has been around since the 1990s, with early research focusing on the use of virtual reality technology to enhance real-world experiences.
7. The Internet of Everything (IoE) refers to the interconnection of people, processes, data, and things, enabled by the Internet and other communication technologies.
8. The concept of the Internet of Everything has emerged in recent years as a result of the rapid growth of the Internet and the increasing number of connected devices.
9. The combination of wearable computing, mixed reality, and the Internet of Everything has the potential to revolutionize the way we interact with the world around us, enabling new forms of communication, entertainment, and productivity.
