### Raspberry Pi

Raspberry Pi is a series of small single-board computers developed in the United Kingdom by the Raspberry Pi Foundation. The purpose of the Raspberry Pi is to promote the teaching of basic computer science in schools and in developing countries.

Some key points to note about Raspberry Pi are:

1. It is a low-cost, credit-card sized computer that can be plugged into a computer monitor or TV.
2. It uses a standard keyboard and mouse.
3. It is capable of doing everything you would expect a desktop computer to do, from browsing the internet and playing high-definition video, to making spreadsheets, word-processing, and playing games.
4. It is also capable of interacting with the outside world and has been used in a wide array of digital maker projects, from music machines and parent detectors to weather stations and tweeting birdhouses with infra-red cameras.
5. Raspberry Pi runs on the Linux operating system and is compatible with a range of programming languages, including Python, Scratch, and C++.
6. There are several models of Raspberry Pi available, each with different specifications and capabilities.
