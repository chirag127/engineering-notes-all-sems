### Parts Identification for the Notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the Subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

1. **Wearable Computing:** Wearable computing refers to the integration of advanced electronic technologies into clothing and accessories that can be worn on the body. These devices can perform a wide range of functions, from simple information display to complex sensing and actuation.

2. **Mixed Reality:** Mixed reality is a technology that combines elements of both virtual reality and augmented reality. It allows users to interact with virtual objects in the real world, creating a seamless blend of the physical and digital environments.

3. **Internet of Everything:** The Internet of Everything (IoE) is a concept that describes the interconnection of all devices, systems, and services in the world. It encompasses not only traditional computing devices, but also everyday objects such as appliances, vehicles, and infrastructure.

4. **Parts Identification:** In the context of wearable computing, mixed reality, and the Internet of Everything, parts identification refers to the process of identifying and categorizing the various components and technologies that make up these systems. This can include hardware components such as sensors, processors, and displays, as well as software and networking technologies.
