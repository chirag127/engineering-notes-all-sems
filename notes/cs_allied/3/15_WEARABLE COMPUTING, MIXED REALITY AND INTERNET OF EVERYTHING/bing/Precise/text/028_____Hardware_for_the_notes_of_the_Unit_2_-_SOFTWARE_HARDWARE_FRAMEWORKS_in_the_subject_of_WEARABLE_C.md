### Hardware for the notes of the Unit 2 - SOFTWARE HARDWARE FRAMEWORKS in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

1. Hardware refers to the physical components of a computer or electronic system.
2. In the context of wearable computing, mixed reality, and the Internet of Everything, hardware can include sensors, processors, displays, and other components that enable these technologies to function.
3. Wearable computing hardware can include smartwatches, fitness trackers, and other devices that are worn on the body and provide various functions such as tracking fitness data or providing notifications.
4. Mixed reality hardware can include virtual reality headsets, augmented reality glasses, and other devices that enable users to interact with virtual or augmented environments.
5. The Internet of Everything refers to the interconnection of devices, systems, and services, and the hardware involved can include sensors, actuators, and other components that enable these devices to communicate and interact with each other.
6. The development of hardware for these technologies is an ongoing process, with new advancements and innovations being made regularly.
7. The choice of hardware can have a significant impact on the performance and capabilities of wearable computing, mixed reality, and Internet of Everything systems.
