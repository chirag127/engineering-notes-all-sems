### Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION: Sensors and Software

- Wearable computing, mixed reality, and the Internet of Everything rely on a combination of sensors and software to function.
- Sensors are devices that detect and respond to changes in the environment, such as temperature, light, or motion.
- Software is the set of instructions that tell the sensors how to respond to the changes they detect.
- Together, sensors and software enable wearable devices, mixed reality systems, and connected devices to interact with the world around them.
- Some common sensors used in these technologies include accelerometers, gyroscopes, magnetometers, and proximity sensors.
- Software for these technologies can range from simple programs that control the behavior of a single sensor to complex systems that integrate data from multiple sensors and other sources.
- The combination of sensors and software allows for the creation of sophisticated and responsive systems that can adapt to changing conditions and provide users with a seamless and immersive experience.
