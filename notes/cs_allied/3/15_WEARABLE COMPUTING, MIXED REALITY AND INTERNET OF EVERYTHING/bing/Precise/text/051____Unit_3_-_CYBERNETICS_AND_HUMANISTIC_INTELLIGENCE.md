## Unit 3 - CYBERNETICS AND HUMANISTIC INTELLIGENCE

1. Cybernetics is the study of control and communication in complex systems, including machines, animals, and organizations.
2. It is concerned with understanding how systems regulate themselves and how they can be designed to achieve specific goals.
3. Humanistic intelligence refers to the ability of humans to use their intelligence in a way that is empathetic, compassionate, and focused on the well-being of others.
4. It is often contrasted with artificial intelligence, which is focused on the development of machines that can perform tasks that would normally require human intelligence.
5. The integration of cybernetics and humanistic intelligence can lead to the development of systems that are both efficient and compassionate, capable of achieving goals while also taking into account the needs and desires of those affected by their actions.
6. This can have applications in fields such as healthcare, education, and social services, where the use of technology can be used to improve the lives of individuals and communities.
