## Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION

1. Introduction is the first chapter or unit of any subject or topic.
2. It provides an overview of the subject and its importance.
3. It sets the foundation for the rest of the subject and helps the reader understand the context and scope of the subject.
4. It may include definitions of key terms and concepts, historical background, and the objectives of the subject.
5. It is important to read and understand the introduction before proceeding to the rest of the subject.
