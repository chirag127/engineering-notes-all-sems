# Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION

### Basic Electronics

1. **Electricity** is the flow of electrons through a conductor.
2. **Voltage** is the measure of the electric potential difference between two points in a circuit.
3. **Current** is the flow of electric charge in a circuit, measured in amperes (A).
4. **Resistance** is the opposition to the flow of electric current, measured in ohms (Ω).
5. **Ohm's Law** states that the current through a conductor between two points is directly proportional to the voltage across the two points.
6. **Series circuits** have components connected end-to-end, so the same current flows through all components.
7. **Parallel circuits** have components connected side-by-side, so the voltage across each component is the same.
8. **Capacitors** store electric charge and can be used to smooth out voltage fluctuations in a circuit.
9. **Diodes** allow current to flow in one direction only and can be used to convert alternating current (AC) to direct current (DC).
10. **Transistors** can be used to amplify or switch electronic signals.
