# Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION
## Sensors and Software

- Wearable computing, mixed reality, and the Internet of Everything (IoE) rely on a combination of sensors and software to function.
- Sensors are devices that detect and respond to changes in the environment, such as temperature, light, motion, or sound.
- Software is the set of instructions that tell the sensors what to do and how to process the data they collect.
- Together, sensors and software enable wearable devices, mixed reality systems, and IoE devices to interact with the physical world and provide users with a seamless and immersive experience.
- Some common sensors used in these technologies include accelerometers, gyroscopes, magnetometers, and proximity sensors.
- These sensors can be used to track movement, orientation, and location, among other things.
- Software, on the other hand, can be used to process the data collected by the sensors, perform calculations, and provide feedback to the user.
- For example, a fitness tracker might use an accelerometer to track the user's steps, and software to calculate the number of calories burned.
- In summary, sensors and software are essential components of wearable computing, mixed reality, and the Internet of Everything, and they work together to provide users with a rich and interactive experience.
