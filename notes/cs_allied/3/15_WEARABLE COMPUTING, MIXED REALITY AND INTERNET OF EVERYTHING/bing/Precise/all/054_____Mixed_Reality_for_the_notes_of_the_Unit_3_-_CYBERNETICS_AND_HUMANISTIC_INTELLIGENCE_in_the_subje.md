# Mixed Reality

Mixed Reality (MR) is a technology that combines the real and virtual worlds to create a hybrid environment where physical and digital objects coexist and interact in real-time. It is a form of augmented reality that integrates virtual objects into the real world, allowing users to interact with both simultaneously.

Some key points to consider when studying Mixed Reality for Unit 3 - CYBERNETICS AND HUMANISTIC INTELLIGENCE in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING are:

1. Mixed Reality is a combination of Virtual Reality (VR) and Augmented Reality (AR).
2. It allows users to interact with both the real and virtual worlds simultaneously.
3. Mixed Reality can be experienced through various devices such as head-mounted displays, smartphones, and tablets.
4. It has applications in various fields such as gaming, education, healthcare, and entertainment.
5. Mixed Reality technology is constantly evolving and has the potential to revolutionize the way we interact with the world around us.
