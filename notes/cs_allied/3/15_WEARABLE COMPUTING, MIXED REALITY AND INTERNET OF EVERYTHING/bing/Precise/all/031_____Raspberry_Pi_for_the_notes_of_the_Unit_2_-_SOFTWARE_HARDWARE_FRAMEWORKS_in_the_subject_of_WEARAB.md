### Unit 2 - SOFTWARE HARDWARE FRAMEWORKS: Raspberry Pi

- Raspberry Pi is a series of small single-board computers developed in the United Kingdom by the Raspberry Pi Foundation.
- The original model became popular for its low cost and small size, making it suitable for use in education, robotics, and home automation projects.
- The Raspberry Pi runs on a variety of operating systems, including Raspbian, a Debian-based Linux distribution, and other Linux distributions such as Ubuntu and Arch Linux.
- The Raspberry Pi has a Broadcom system on a chip (SoC) which includes an ARM-compatible central processing unit (CPU) and an on-chip graphics processing unit (GPU).
- The Raspberry Pi has several models, with the most recent being the Raspberry Pi 4, which has improved processing power, memory, and connectivity compared to previous models.
- The Raspberry Pi can be used for a variety of projects, including media centers, game consoles, and home automation systems.
- The Raspberry Pi Foundation provides extensive documentation and support for the Raspberry Pi, making it accessible for beginners and experienced users alike.
- The Raspberry Pi has a large and active community of users and developers, who share projects, ideas, and support through online forums and social media.
