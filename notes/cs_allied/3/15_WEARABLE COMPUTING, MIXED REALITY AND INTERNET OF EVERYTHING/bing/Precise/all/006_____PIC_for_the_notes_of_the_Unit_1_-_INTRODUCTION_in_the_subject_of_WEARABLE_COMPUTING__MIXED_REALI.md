### PIC for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

- PIC stands for Peripheral Interface Controller.
- It is a family of microcontrollers made by Microchip Technology.
- PICs are popular due to their low cost, wide availability, and large user base.
- They are commonly used in embedded systems and for prototyping.
- PICs are available in a range of sizes and capabilities, from small 8-bit devices to 32-bit devices with advanced features.
- They are often used in wearable computing, mixed reality, and Internet of Everything applications due to their versatility and ability to interface with a wide range of sensors and other devices.
- PICs can be programmed using a variety of languages, including assembly, C, and BASIC.
- They are often used in conjunction with other components, such as sensors, displays, and wireless communication modules, to create complete systems.
- PICs are widely used in both hobbyist and professional applications, and there is a large community of users and resources available to support their use.
