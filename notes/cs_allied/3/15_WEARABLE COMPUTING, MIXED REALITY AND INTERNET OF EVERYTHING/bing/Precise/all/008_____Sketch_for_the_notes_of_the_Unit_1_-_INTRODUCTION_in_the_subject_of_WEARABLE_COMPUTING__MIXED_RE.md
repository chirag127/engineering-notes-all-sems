### Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION: WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

1. **Wearable Computing:** Wearable computing refers to the use of small, portable, and often fashionable computing devices that can be worn on the body. These devices can include smartwatches, fitness trackers, and smart glasses, among others.

2. **Mixed Reality:** Mixed reality is a technology that combines elements of both virtual reality and augmented reality. It allows users to interact with virtual objects in the real world, creating a more immersive experience.

3. **Internet of Everything:** The Internet of Everything (IoE) is a concept that refers to the interconnection of all devices, systems, and services in the world. This includes not only traditional computing devices, but also everyday objects such as home appliances, vehicles, and even clothing.

4. **Applications:** Wearable computing, mixed reality, and the Internet of Everything have numerous applications in various fields, including healthcare, entertainment, education, and transportation.

5. **Challenges:** Despite the potential benefits of these technologies, there are also challenges that must be addressed, such as privacy and security concerns, as well as the need for standardization and interoperability.

6. **Future Developments:** As these technologies continue to evolve, it is likely that we will see even more advanced and sophisticated applications, as well as new challenges and opportunities.
