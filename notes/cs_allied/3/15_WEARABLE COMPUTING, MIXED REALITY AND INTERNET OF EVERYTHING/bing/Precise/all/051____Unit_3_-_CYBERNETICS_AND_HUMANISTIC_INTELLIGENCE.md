## Unit 3 - CYBERNETICS AND HUMANISTIC INTELLIGENCE

1. Cybernetics is the study of control and communication in complex systems, including machines, animals, and organizations.
2. It is concerned with understanding how systems regulate themselves and how they can be designed to achieve specific goals.
3. Humanistic intelligence is a concept that refers to the integration of human intelligence with technology, in order to enhance human capabilities and improve the human experience.
4. This can involve the use of artificial intelligence, machine learning, and other technologies to augment human cognition and decision-making.
5. The goal of humanistic intelligence is to create systems that are more responsive to human needs and values, and that can help people achieve their full potential.
6. The field of cybernetics and humanistic intelligence is interdisciplinary, drawing on insights from computer science, psychology, neuroscience, and other fields.
7. Key concepts in this field include feedback, control, adaptation, and self-organization.
8. Applications of cybernetics and humanistic intelligence can be found in areas such as robotics, artificial intelligence, and human-computer interaction.
