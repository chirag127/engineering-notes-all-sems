# Introduction

Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

1. Wearable computing refers to the integration of advanced electronic technologies into clothing and accessories to enhance the user's experience and capabilities.
2. Mixed reality is a technology that combines elements of both virtual and augmented reality, allowing users to interact with digital content in the real world.
3. The Internet of Everything (IoE) is a concept that describes the interconnection of all devices, systems, and services to create a more intelligent and efficient world.

These technologies are rapidly evolving and have the potential to revolutionize the way we live, work, and interact with the world around us. In this unit, we will explore the basics of these technologies and their potential applications.
