### Unit 2 - SOFTWARE HARDWARE FRAMEWORKS

In the subject of Wearable Computing, Mixed Reality, and the Internet of Everything, Unit 2 focuses on the software and hardware frameworks that enable these technologies.

1. **Wearable Computing:** Wearable computing refers to the integration of advanced electronic technologies into clothing and accessories that can be worn on the body. These devices can perform a wide range of functions, from simple activity tracking to complex health monitoring.

2. **Mixed Reality:** Mixed reality is a technology that combines elements of both virtual and augmented reality. It allows users to interact with virtual objects in the real world, creating an immersive experience.

3. **Internet of Everything:** The Internet of Everything (IoE) is a concept that refers to the interconnection of all devices and objects, allowing them to communicate and share data with each other. This includes not only traditional computing devices, but also everyday objects such as home appliances and vehicles.

4. **Software Frameworks:** Software frameworks are pre-built structures that provide a foundation for developing applications. They can simplify the development process by providing common functionality, allowing developers to focus on the unique aspects of their applications.

5. **Hardware Frameworks:** Hardware frameworks refer to the physical components and systems that enable the operation of wearable, mixed reality, and IoE devices. These can include sensors, processors, and communication technologies.

In summary, Unit 2 of the Wearable Computing, Mixed Reality, and Internet of Everything course covers the software and hardware frameworks that enable these technologies to function and interact with each other. By understanding these frameworks, students can gain a deeper understanding of how these technologies work and how they can be applied in various contexts.
