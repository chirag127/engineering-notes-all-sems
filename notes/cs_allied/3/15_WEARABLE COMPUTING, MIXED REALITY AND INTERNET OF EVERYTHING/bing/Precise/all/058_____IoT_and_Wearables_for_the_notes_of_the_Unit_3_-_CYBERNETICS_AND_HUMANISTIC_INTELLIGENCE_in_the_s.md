# IoT and Wearables

IoT (Internet of Things) refers to the interconnection of physical devices, vehicles, home appliances, and other items embedded with electronics, software, sensors, and network connectivity, which enables these objects to collect and exchange data.

Wearables are electronic devices that are worn on the body, either as an accessory or as part of the material used in clothing. These devices are capable of sensing and processing data, and can communicate with other devices or networks.

Here are some key points to consider when studying IoT and Wearables in the context of Unit 3 - CYBERNETICS AND HUMANISTIC INTELLIGENCE in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING:

1. IoT and Wearables are closely related, as many wearable devices are part of the IoT ecosystem.
2. Wearables can collect and process data about the user's health, fitness, and other personal information, and can communicate this data to other devices or networks.
3. IoT and Wearables have the potential to improve healthcare, fitness, and overall quality of life by providing real-time monitoring and feedback.
4. The use of IoT and Wearables raises concerns about privacy and security, as personal data is collected and transmitted.
5. The development of IoT and Wearables requires a multidisciplinary approach, involving fields such as computer science, engineering, design, and psychology.
