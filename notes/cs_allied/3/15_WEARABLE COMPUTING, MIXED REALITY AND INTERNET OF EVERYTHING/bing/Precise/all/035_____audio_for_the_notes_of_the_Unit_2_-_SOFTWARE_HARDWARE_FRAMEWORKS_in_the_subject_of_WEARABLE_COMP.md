# Unit 2 - SOFTWARE HARDWARE FRAMEWORKS: Audio

- Audio is an important aspect of wearable computing, mixed reality, and the Internet of Everything.
- Audio can be used for communication, entertainment, and information delivery.
- In wearable computing, audio can be used for hands-free communication, such as with a Bluetooth headset.
- In mixed reality, audio can be used to enhance the immersive experience, such as with spatial audio.
- In the Internet of Everything, audio can be used for voice control of smart devices, such as with a smart speaker.
- Audio can be processed and transmitted using various hardware and software frameworks.
- Hardware for audio processing can include microphones, speakers, and audio processing chips.
- Software for audio processing can include audio codecs, digital signal processing algorithms, and voice recognition algorithms.
- Audio frameworks can be used to develop applications that utilize audio, such as voice assistants and music players.
- Audio frameworks can also be used to integrate audio into existing applications, such as adding voice control to a smart home app.
- Audio is an important aspect of the software-hardware framework in wearable computing, mixed reality, and the Internet of Everything. It provides a means of communication, entertainment, and information delivery, and can be processed and transmitted using various hardware and software frameworks.
