# Unit 3 - CYBERNETICS AND HUMANISTIC INTELLIGENCE

### Wearables

- Wearables are electronic devices that are worn on the body, either as an accessory or as part of the clothing.
- They are designed to perform a specific set of functions, such as tracking fitness, monitoring health, or providing information.
- Wearables can be categorized into several types, including smartwatches, fitness trackers, smart glasses, and smart clothing.
- Wearables are part of the larger trend of the Internet of Things (IoT), where everyday objects are connected to the internet and can communicate with other devices.
- Wearables can collect and transmit data, allowing for real-time monitoring and analysis.
- Wearables can also provide feedback to the user, such as vibrating to remind the user to stand up and move after sitting for too long.
- Wearables can be used for a variety of purposes, including fitness, health, entertainment, and productivity.
- Wearables can also be used in the workplace, for example, to monitor the safety of workers in hazardous environments.
- Wearables are still a relatively new technology, and there are concerns about privacy and security of the data collected by these devices.
- Wearables have the potential to revolutionize the way we interact with technology and the world around us. They can provide us with new insights into our health and behavior, and can help us to live more productive and healthy lives. However, it is important to use them responsibly and to be aware of the potential risks and challenges.
