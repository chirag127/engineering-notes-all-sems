# Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION: History of Wearable Computing, Mixed Reality and Internet of Everything

- Wearable computing refers to the integration of advanced electronic technologies into clothing and accessories that can be worn on the body.
- The concept of wearable computing can be traced back to the 1960s, when computer scientist Edward Thorp developed a wearable computer to predict the outcome of roulette games.
- In the 1980s, Steve Mann, a pioneer in the field of wearable computing, developed a wearable computer system that allowed him to control photographic equipment and other devices.
- Mixed reality is the merging of real and virtual worlds to produce new environments and visualizations where physical and digital objects co-exist and interact in real time.
- The term "mixed reality" was first introduced by Paul Milgram and Fumio Kishino in their 1994 paper "A Taxonomy of Mixed Reality Visual Displays".
- The Internet of Everything (IoE) is a concept that refers to the interconnection of all devices and objects, allowing them to communicate and share data with each other.
- The term "Internet of Everything" was first coined by Cisco in 2012 to describe the next wave of the Internet, where not only people and computers, but also everyday objects, are connected to the Internet.
- The development of the Internet of Things (IoT), which refers to the interconnection of everyday objects, laid the foundation for the emergence of the IoE.
