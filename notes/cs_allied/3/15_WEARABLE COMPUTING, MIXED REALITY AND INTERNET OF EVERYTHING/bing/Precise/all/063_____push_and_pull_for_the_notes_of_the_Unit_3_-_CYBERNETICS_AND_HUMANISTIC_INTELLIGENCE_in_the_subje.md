# Unit 3 - CYBERNETICS AND HUMANISTIC INTELLIGENCE

### Push and Pull

- Push and pull are two fundamental concepts in the field of cybernetics and humanistic intelligence.
- Push refers to the act of exerting force on an object in order to move it away from the source of the force.
- Pull, on the other hand, refers to the act of exerting force on an object in order to move it towards the source of the force.
- These two concepts are often used in the context of wearable computing, mixed reality, and the Internet of Everything, where they can be applied to the interaction between humans and technology.
- For example, a user may push a button on a wearable device to initiate an action, or pull data from a sensor to monitor their health.
- Push and pull can also be used to describe the flow of information in a system, where data is pushed from one component to another, or pulled from a source when needed.
- Understanding the concepts of push and pull is important for designing effective and efficient systems in the field of wearable computing, mixed reality, and the Internet of Everything.
