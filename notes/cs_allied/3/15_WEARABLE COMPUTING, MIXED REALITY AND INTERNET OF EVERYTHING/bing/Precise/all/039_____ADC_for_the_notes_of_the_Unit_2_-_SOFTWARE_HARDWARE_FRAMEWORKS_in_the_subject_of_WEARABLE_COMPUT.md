### ADC

- ADC stands for Analog to Digital Converter.
- It is a device that converts an analog signal into a digital signal.
- The analog signal is continuous in nature, while the digital signal is discrete.
- The conversion process involves sampling the analog signal at regular intervals and quantizing the samples to the nearest discrete value.
- The resolution of the ADC determines the number of discrete values that can be represented by the digital signal.
- The higher the resolution, the more accurate the digital representation of the analog signal.
- ADCs are used in a wide range of applications, including audio and video recording, data acquisition, and control systems.
- In the context of wearable computing, mixed reality, and the Internet of Everything, ADCs play a crucial role in interfacing sensors and other analog devices with digital systems.
