- Wearable computing refers to the devices that are worn on the body or embedded in clothing, such as smart watches, fitness trackers, smart glasses, etc. These devices can sense, process and communicate data, and provide various functions such as health monitoring, navigation, entertainment, etc.
- Mixed reality refers to the blending of real and virtual environments, where physical and digital objects coexist and interact. Mixed reality can be achieved by using devices such as head-mounted displays, augmented reality glasses, projection systems, etc. Mixed reality can enhance the perception, cognition and interaction of users, and enable new applications such as education, gaming, tourism, etc.
- Internet of everything refers to the network of physical and digital objects that are connected and can exchange data and services. Internet of everything includes internet of things, internet of people, internet of services, etc. Internet of everything can enable smart environments, automation, personalization, optimization, etc.
