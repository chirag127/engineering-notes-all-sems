### Database interface for the notes of the Unit 3 - CYBERNETICS AND HUMANISTIC INTELLIGENCE in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

- A database interface is a software component that allows users to interact with a database system, such as creating, querying, updating, or deleting data.
- A database interface can be designed for different types of users, such as end-users, administrators, developers, or analysts.
- A database interface can also support different types of data, such as structured, semi-structured, or unstructured data.
- A database interface can be implemented using various technologies, such as web-based, desktop-based, mobile-based, or voice-based interfaces.
- A database interface can be integrated with other systems, such as wearable computing, mixed reality, or internet of everything, to provide enhanced functionality, interactivity, and user experience.

- Wearable computing is a subfield of human-computer interaction that involves designing and using devices that are worn on the body, such as smart glasses, smart watches, smart clothing, or smart jewelry   .
- Wearable computing can enable humanistic intelligence, which is intelligence that arises when a human is part of the feedback loop of a computational process in which the human and computer are inextricably intertwined   .
- Humanistic intelligence can enhance human capabilities, such as perception, cognition, communication, or expression, by augmenting, mediating, or modifying the reality that the human experiences   .
- Humanistic intelligence can also enable new forms of artistic, social, or cultural expression, such as wearable art, wearable media, or wearable communities   .

- Mixed reality is a term that encompasses both virtual reality and augmented reality, which are technologies that create immersive and interactive environments that simulate or augment the physical world  .
- Mixed reality can be achieved by using various devices, such as head-mounted displays, handheld devices, projection systems, or spatial audio systems  .
- Mixed reality can provide users with different levels of immersion, interactivity, and realism, depending on the degree of alignment, occlusion, and consistency between the virtual and the real elements  .
- Mixed reality can also enable users to select, filter, or delete information intake through wearable processing devices, and to manipulate, annotate, or share their reality with others  .

- Internet of everything is a concept that refers to the interconnection of people, processes, data, and things through the internet, using various technologies, such as sensors, actuators, networks, cloud computing, or artificial intelligence  .
- Internet of everything can enable various applications and services, such as smart cities, smart homes, smart health, smart education, or smart entertainment, that can improve the quality of life, efficiency, productivity, or sustainability of individuals, organizations, or societies  .
- Internet of everything can also pose various challenges and risks, such as privacy, security, ethics, or governance, that need to be addressed by the stakeholders involved, such as users, developers, providers, or regulators  .
