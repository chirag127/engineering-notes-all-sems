### Raspberry Pi

- Raspberry Pi is a low-cost, low-power, credit-card-sized single-board computer that can connect to a variety of devices and sensors .
- Raspberry Pi was developed by the Raspberry Pi Foundation, a UK-based charity, to promote computing education and digital skills for people of all ages .
- Raspberry Pi can run various operating systems, such as Linux, Windows, and Android, and can be used for various purposes, such as web browsing, gaming, media streaming, robotics, and IoT  .
- Raspberry Pi has different models and versions, each with different specifications and features, such as CPU speed, RAM size, USB ports, wireless connectivity, and GPIO pins .
- Raspberry Pi can be plugged into a computer monitor or TV, and uses a standard keyboard and mouse. It can also be controlled remotely via SSH or VNC .
- Raspberry Pi can be programmed in various languages, such as Python, Scratch, C, Java, and JavaScript, and can interact with various libraries and frameworks, such as OpenCV, TensorFlow, PyGame, and Node-RED   .
- Raspberry Pi can be extended with various accessories and add-ons, such as cameras, touchscreens, hats, cases, and sensors, to enhance its functionality and capabilities  .
