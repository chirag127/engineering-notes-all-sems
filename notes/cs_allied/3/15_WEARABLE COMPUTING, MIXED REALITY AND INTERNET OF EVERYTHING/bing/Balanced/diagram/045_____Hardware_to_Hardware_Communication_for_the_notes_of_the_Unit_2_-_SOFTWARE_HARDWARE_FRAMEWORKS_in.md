### Hardware to Hardware Communication for the notes of the Unit 2 - SOFTWARE HARDWARE FRAMEWORKS in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

- Hardware to hardware communication refers to the process of exchanging data between different devices or components of a wearable computing system.
- Hardware to hardware communication can be achieved by using various methods, such as:
  - Interrupt request (IRQ): A signal sent by a hardware device to the CPU to request its attention and perform a specific task.
  - Wireless communication: A method of transmitting data using electromagnetic waves, such as radio, infrared, Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, etc. Wireless communication can enable wearable devices to connect to each other or to external networks  .
  - Serial communication: A method of transmitting data one bit at a time over a single wire or channel. Serial communication can be used to connect wearable devices to sensors, actuators, or other peripherals.
- Hardware to hardware communication can enable various applications and functionalities for wearable computing, such as:
  - Affective computing: The use of wearable devices to sense, recognize, and respond to human emotions and affective states.
  - Augmented reality: The use of wearable devices to overlay digital information or graphics on the user's perception of the physical world.
  - Remembrance agent: The use of wearable devices to provide timely and relevant information to the user based on their context and history.
  - Motion recognition: The use of wearable devices to capture and analyze the user's movements and gestures for interaction or feedback.
