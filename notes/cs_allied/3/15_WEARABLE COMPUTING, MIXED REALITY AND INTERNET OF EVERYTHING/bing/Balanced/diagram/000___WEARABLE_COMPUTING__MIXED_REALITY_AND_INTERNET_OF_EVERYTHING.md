# WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

- Wearable computing is the use of devices that can be worn on the body, such as smartwatches, fitness trackers, smart glasses, or virtual reality headsets. These devices can sense, process, and communicate data, and often interact with other devices or services via wireless networks .
- Mixed reality is the blending of the physical and digital worlds, where virtual objects or information can be overlaid on the real environment, or vice versa. Mixed reality can be experienced through wearable devices, such as head-mounted displays, that enable users to see and manipulate digital content in their surroundings .
- Internet of everything is the concept of connecting any person, device, or thing to the internet and to each other, enabling data exchange and communication across various domains and applications. Internet of everything encompasses the internet of things (IoT), which is the network of physical objects embedded with sensors, software, or other technologies that can collect and transmit data over the internet .
- The relationship between wearable computing, mixed reality, and internet of everything is that they are all technologies that can enhance human capabilities, experiences, and interactions in various contexts and scenarios. Wearable devices can connect people to the internet of everything, allowing them to access and control data, services, or devices remotely or locally. Mixed reality can augment the perception and understanding of the physical and digital worlds, creating new possibilities for learning, entertainment, or collaboration. Internet of everything can enable the integration and interoperability of different systems and domains, creating value and efficiency for individuals, organizations, and societies  .
