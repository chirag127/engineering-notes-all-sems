### OpenFrameworks as our IDE

- OpenFrameworks is an open source C++ toolkit for creative coding.
- It is designed to simplify the process of creating applications that use graphics, sound, video, computer vision, networking and other interactive elements.
- It supports multiple platforms, such as Windows, Linux, macOS, iOS, Android, Raspberry Pi, etc .
- It also supports multiple development environments, such as Visual Studio, Xcode, Code::Blocks, Eclipse, etc .
- To use OpenFrameworks, you need to download the appropriate version for your platform and IDE from the official website or GitHub.
- You also need to follow the setup guide for your platform and IDE, which will walk you through the installation and configuration steps.
- You can then use the examples and tutorials provided by OpenFrameworks to learn how to use the toolkit and create your own projects.
- You can also refer to the documentation for the reference of the classes, functions and addons of OpenFrameworks.
- You can also join the forum and the community to ask questions, share your work and get feedback.
- OpenFrameworks is distributed under the MIT License, which means you can use it for any purpose, modify it and redistribute it, as long as you give credit to the original authors and include the license notice .
