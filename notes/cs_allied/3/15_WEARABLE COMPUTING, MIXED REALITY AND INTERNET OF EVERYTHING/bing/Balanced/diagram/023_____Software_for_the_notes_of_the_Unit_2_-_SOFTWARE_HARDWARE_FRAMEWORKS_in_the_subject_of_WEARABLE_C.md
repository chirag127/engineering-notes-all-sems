### Software for the notes of the Unit 2 - SOFTWARE HARDWARE FRAMEWORKS in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

- Software hardware frameworks are the systems and tools that enable the development and deployment of applications for wearable computing, mixed reality and internet of everything (IoT) domains.
- Wearable computing is the integration of computing devices into clothing or accessories that can be worn on the body, such as smart watches, glasses, or earbuds.
- Mixed reality is the blending of physical and digital worlds, where virtual objects can interact with real environments and vice versa, such as augmented reality (AR) and virtual reality (VR).
- Internet of everything is the extension of internet connectivity to everyday objects and devices, such as sensors, cameras, or smart appliances, that can communicate and exchange data with each other and the cloud.
- Some of the common software hardware frameworks for these domains are:

  - Azure IoT: A cloud-based platform that provides services and tools for connecting, managing, and securing IoT devices and applications, as well as enabling advanced analytics, artificial intelligence, and digital twins.
  - UbiVis: A framework that tackles the challenges of using different hardware for mixed reality experiences, such as synchronization, calibration, and collaboration, and provides solutions for displaying virtual content in collaborative MR-applications.
  - MRCP: A secure mixed reality framework that allows users to seamlessly interact with headless IoT devices, such as smart parking meters or smart locks, using a vendor-neutral, open-source, cross-platform MR software.
  - Unity: A game engine and development platform that supports the creation of 2D, 3D, AR, and VR applications for various platforms, including wearable and IoT devices, and provides tools for scripting, animation, physics, and rendering.
  - Android: An open-source operating system that runs on a variety of devices, including smartphones, tablets, wearables, and IoT devices, and provides a rich set of APIs and libraries for developing applications and services.
