### Laptop for the notes of the Unit 2 - SOFTWARE HARDWARE FRAMEWORKS in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

- Wearable computing is the field of technology that involves devices that can be worn on the body, such as smartwatches, smart glasses, fitness trackers, etc.
- Wearable devices usually have microprocessors, batteries, sensors, and wireless connectivity to collect data and communicate with other electronics, such as mobile devices or laptops.
- Wearable computing can be used for various applications, such as health monitoring, entertainment, education, navigation, social interaction, etc.
- Some examples of wearable devices are:
  - WS50 Android Wearable Computer: a small, rugged, modular device that can be worn on the wrist, arm, or belt, and can run Android applications, scan barcodes, and connect to other devices via Bluetooth or Wi-Fi.
  - Zebra Ring Scanners: a series of wearable scanners that can be worn on the finger, and can capture 1D and 2D barcodes, RFID tags, and images, and transmit them to a wearable computer or a mobile device.
  - Google Glass: a smart eyewear that can display information, take pictures and videos, make calls, and access the internet via voice commands or touch gestures.
- A laptop for taking notes on the topic of wearable computing should have the following features:
  - A durable and rugged design that can withstand drops, spills, dust, and extreme temperatures.
  - A bright and high-resolution display that can be easily viewed indoors and outdoors.
  - A long-lasting battery life that can support hours of continuous use, and the option to swap batteries without shutting down the device.
  - A fast and reliable wireless connectivity that can sync data with other devices and access online resources.
  - A sufficient storage capacity and memory that can handle large amounts of data and run multiple applications.
  - A user-friendly and secure operating system that can support various software and hardware frameworks for wearable computing, such as Android, FreeRTOS, etc. 
- One possible laptop model that meets these criteria is the Getac B360, which is a fully rugged laptop with a 13.3-inch full HD touch display, dual hot-swappable batteries, a 10th generation Intel Core processor, up to 64 GB of memory, up to 1 TB of SSD storage, and a Windows 10 Pro operating system.
