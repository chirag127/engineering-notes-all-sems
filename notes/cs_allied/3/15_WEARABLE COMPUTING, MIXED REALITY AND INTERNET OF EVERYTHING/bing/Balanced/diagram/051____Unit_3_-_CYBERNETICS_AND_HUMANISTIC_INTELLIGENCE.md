## Unit 3 - CYBERNETICS AND HUMANISTIC INTELLIGENCE

- Cybernetics is the science of human-machine interaction that employs the principles of feedback, control and communication.
- Humanistic Intelligence is the intelligence that arises because of a human being in the feedback loop of a computational process, where the human and computer are inextricably intertwined.
- Cybernetics and Humanistic Intelligence are related fields that explore the relationship between humans and machines, and the implications for society, culture and ethics.
- Some of the topics covered in this unit are:

  - The history and development of cybernetics, from Norbert Wiener's seminal work on control and communication in animals and machines, to the contemporary applications of artificial intelligence, robotics and biotechnology.
  - The concept and examples of Humanistic Intelligence, such as wearable computing, augmented reality and brain-computer interfaces, and how they enhance human capabilities and experiences.
  - The philosophical and ethical issues raised by cybernetics and Humanistic Intelligence, such as the nature of human identity, agency and responsibility, the role of embodiment and emotion, and the potential risks and benefits of human-machine integration.
  - The social and cultural impacts of cybernetics and Humanistic Intelligence, such as the effects on communication, education, work, entertainment and politics, and the challenges and opportunities for human creativity, collaboration and diversity.
