### Arm Devices

- Arm devices are wearable computing devices that use Arm processors as the main computing unit.
- Arm processors are low-power, high-performance chips that are designed for mobile and embedded applications, such as smartphones, tablets, smartwatches, and augmented reality (AR) and virtual reality (VR) devices.
- Arm processors are based on the reduced instruction set computer (RISC) architecture, which simplifies the instruction set and reduces the complexity and power consumption of the processor.
- Arm processors are licensed by Arm Holdings to various chip makers, who can customize and optimize them for different use cases and markets.
- Arm devices can benefit from the Mbed development platform, which is an open source platform that provides a range of components, connectivity options, and operating systems for wearable computing development.
- Arm devices can enable a variety of wearable computing applications, such as fitness tracking, health monitoring, navigation, gaming, entertainment, education, and social interaction.
- Arm devices can also support mixed reality and internet of everything scenarios, where the wearable device acts as a gateway to the metaverse, a virtual environment that integrates physical and digital worlds.
