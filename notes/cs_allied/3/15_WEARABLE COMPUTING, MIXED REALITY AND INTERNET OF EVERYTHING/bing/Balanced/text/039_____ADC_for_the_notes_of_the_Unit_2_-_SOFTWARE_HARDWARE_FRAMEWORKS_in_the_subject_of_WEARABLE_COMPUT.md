### ADC

- ADC stands for Analog to Digital Converter. It is a device that converts analog signals, such as sound waves, into digital signals, such as binary numbers.
- ADC is an essential component of many wearable computing, mixed reality and internet of everything applications, as it allows the processing and transmission of analog data from sensors, cameras, microphones, etc.
- ADC can be classified into different types based on the method of conversion, such as successive approximation, flash, sigma-delta, dual-slope, etc.
- ADC can also be characterized by various parameters, such as resolution, sampling rate, accuracy, linearity, power consumption, etc.
- ADC can be integrated into a single chip or a module, or can be external to the main processor. The choice of ADC depends on the requirements and constraints of the application, such as speed, precision, cost, size, etc.
