### Data persistence for the notes of the Unit 3 - CYBERNETICS AND HUMANISTIC INTELLIGENCE in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

- Data persistence is the ability of data to remain accessible and usable over time, regardless of changes in the system or environment.
- Data persistence is important for the notes of the Unit 3 because it enables the following benefits:
  - **Retrieval**: The notes can be easily retrieved from different devices and platforms, such as wearable computing devices, mixed reality headsets, or cloud services.
  - **Analysis**: The notes can be analyzed using artificial intelligence (AI) techniques, such as natural language processing, sentiment analysis, or knowledge graph, to extract insights and generate summaries.
  - **Sharing**: The notes can be shared with other users or applications, such as blockchain, drones, or robotics, to enable collaboration and communication.
  - **Security**: The notes can be protected from unauthorized access or modification, using encryption, authentication, or digital signatures.
- Data persistence can be achieved using various methods and technologies, such as:
  - **File systems**: The notes can be stored as files in a hierarchical structure, such as folders and subfolders, on a local or remote storage device.
  - **Databases**: The notes can be stored as records in a structured or unstructured format, such as tables, documents, or graphs, on a relational or non-relational database system.
  - **Data streams**: The notes can be stored as events or messages in a sequential or parallel manner, such as queues, topics, or logs, on a streaming or messaging platform.
  - **Data lakes**: The notes can be stored as objects or blobs in a flat or nested structure, such as buckets, directories, or files, on a distributed or cloud-based storage system.
