### Case studies for the notes of the Unit 3 - CYBERNETICS AND HUMANISTIC INTELLIGENCE in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

- Cybernetics is the science of communication and control in living beings and machines. It studies the feedback loops that regulate the behavior of complex systems.
- Humanistic intelligence (HI) is a concept proposed by Steve Mann, who is considered the father of wearable computing. HI is intelligence that arises when a human is part of the feedback loop of a computational process in which the human and computer are inextricably intertwined   .
- Wearable computing is the design and implementation of computing devices that can be worn on the body, such as glasses, watches, clothing, jewelry, etc. Wearable computing can augment the human senses, cognition, memory, communication, and expression    .
- Mixed reality (MR) is the blending of the physical and digital worlds, where real and virtual objects coexist and interact in real time. MR can create immersive and interactive experiences that enhance the perception and understanding of reality.
- Internet of everything (IoE) is the network of people, processes, data, and things that are connected and communicate with each other. IoE can enable new forms of collaboration, innovation, and value creation across various domains and industries.

Some case studies that illustrate the concepts and applications of cybernetics, humanistic intelligence, wearable computing, mixed reality, and internet of everything are:

- **Digital Eye Glass**: This is a wearable computing device that resembles a pair of eyeglasses, but has a camera, a display, a processor, and a wireless connection. It can augment the vision of the wearer by overlaying digital information on the real world, such as text, images, graphics, etc. It can also record and transmit what the wearer sees and hears, creating a personal documentary of their life. The digital eye glass can be used for various purposes, such as education, entertainment, navigation, communication, security, etc. It can also create a new form of mixed reality, called augmediated reality, which is the ability to modify the appearance of reality by adding, subtracting, or reformatting visual information.
- **Smart Fabric Keyboard**: This is a wearable computing device that consists of a fabric keyboard embedded in a piece of clothing, such as a jacket, a shirt, or a glove. It can sense the touch and pressure of the fingers and send the corresponding signals to a connected device, such as a smartphone, a tablet, or a laptop. It can enable the wearer to input text, commands, or gestures without using a physical keyboard or a mouse. It can also provide haptic feedback to the wearer, such as vibration, heat, or cold. The smart fabric keyboard can be used for various purposes, such as gaming, music, education, communication, etc. It can also create a new form of human-computer interaction, called embodied interaction, which is the integration of the body and the environment in the interaction process   .
- **Cyborg Beetles**: These are living insects that have been implanted with microelectrodes and microcontrollers that can stimulate their nervous system and control their movements. They can be used as biological drones that can fly and carry sensors, cameras, microphones, or other devices. They can be remotely controlled by a human operator or an artificial intelligence system. They can be used for various purposes, such as surveillance, reconnaissance, exploration, rescue, etc. They can also create a new form of cybernetics, called biocybernetics, which is the application of cybernetics to living organisms.
