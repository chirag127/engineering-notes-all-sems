## Unit 3 - CYBERNETICS AND HUMANISTIC INTELLIGENCE

- Cybernetics is the science of human-machine interaction that employs the principles of feedback, control and communication.
- Humanistic Intelligence is the intelligence that arises because of a human being in the feedback loop of a computational process, where the human and computer are inextricably intertwined.
- Cybernetics and Humanistic Intelligence are related fields that explore the possibilities and implications of human-machine integration and augmentation.
- Some of the topics covered in this unit are:

  - The history and development of cybernetics and its applications in various domains such as biology, engineering, psychology, sociology, etc.
  - The concept of feedback and its role in regulating and adapting systems to achieve desired goals or outcomes.
  - The concept of control and its ethical and social implications in human-machine systems, such as autonomy, responsibility, accountability, etc.
  - The concept of communication and its importance in facilitating information exchange and coordination among agents in a system, such as humans, machines, animals, etc.
  - The concept of humanistic intelligence and its characteristics, such as embodiment, situatedness, personalization, etc.
  - The concept of wearable computing and its examples, such as smart glasses, smart watches, smart clothing, etc.
  - The concept of posthumanism and its challenges and opportunities for human identity, culture, and society in the age of artificial intelligence and biotechnology.
  - The concept of transhumanism and its visions and goals for enhancing and transcending human capabilities and limitations through science and technology.
