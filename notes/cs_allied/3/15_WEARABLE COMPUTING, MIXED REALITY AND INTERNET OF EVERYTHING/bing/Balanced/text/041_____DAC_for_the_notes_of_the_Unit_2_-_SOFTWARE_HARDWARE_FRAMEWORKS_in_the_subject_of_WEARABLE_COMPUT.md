### DAC

DAC stands for Digital-to-Analog Converter. It is a device that converts digital audio signals into analog audio signals that can be output to speakers, headphones or other analog devices. DACs are in everything, from cell phones to computers, and more.

Some of the topics related to DAC in the context of wearable computing, mixed reality and internet of everything are:

- The need for high-quality DACs in wearable devices that provide services such as collecting personal data, monitoring health conditions, and so on.
- The challenges of designing and implementing DACs for low-power, reliability, multicore/application specific/heterogeneous architectures, 3-D integrations, emerging device technologies, and security.
- The types and features of DACs that are suitable for different applications and scenarios, such as USB, portable and desktop DACs .
- The integration of DACs with other components and systems, such as Raspberry Pi, data acquisition (DAQ) products, data logger devices, and cloud-assisted services.
- The evaluation and comparison of DACs based on various criteria, such as performance, accuracy, resolution, sample rate, dynamic range, distortion, noise, and cost .
