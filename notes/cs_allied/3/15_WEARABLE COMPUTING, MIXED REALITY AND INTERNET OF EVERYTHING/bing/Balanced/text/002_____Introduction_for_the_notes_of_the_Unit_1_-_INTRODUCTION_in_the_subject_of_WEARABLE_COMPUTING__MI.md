### Introduction for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

- Wearable computing is the field of computer science that deals with the design, development and application of wearable devices that can sense, process and communicate information.
- Wearable devices are typically worn on the body or embedded in clothing or accessories, and can provide various functions such as health monitoring, fitness tracking, entertainment, navigation, social interaction, etc.
- Mixed reality is the field of computer science that deals with the creation and manipulation of digital content that is integrated with the physical environment and the user's perception.
- Mixed reality can be classified into two categories: augmented reality (AR) and virtual reality (VR).
- Augmented reality is the technology that enhances the user's view of the real world with digital information, such as images, text, sound, etc.
- Virtual reality is the technology that immerses the user in a simulated environment that is different from the real world, such as a game, a movie, a simulation, etc.
- Internet of Everything (IoE) is the concept that describes the interconnection of people, processes, data and things through the internet and other networks.
- IoE can enable new applications and services that can improve the quality of life, efficiency, productivity, security, etc.
- IoE can also pose challenges and risks such as privacy, security, ethics, etc.
