# Hardware for the notes of the Unit 2 - SOFTWARE HARDWARE FRAMEWORKS in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

- Hardware refers to the physical components of a computing system that enable the execution of software and the interaction with users and the environment.
- Hardware for wearable computing, mixed reality and internet of everything (IoE) applications must meet certain requirements, such as:
  - Low power consumption and long battery life
  - Small size and light weight
  - Wireless connectivity and data transmission
  - Sensing and actuating capabilities
  - User comfort and safety
  - Reliability and robustness
- Hardware for wearable computing, mixed reality and IoE can be classified into four categories, based on their functionality and location:
  - Body-worn devices: These are devices that are attached to or worn on the human body, such as smart watches, fitness trackers, smart glasses, head-mounted displays, earphones, etc. They can provide various functions, such as sensing, computing, communication, display, audio, etc.
  - Environmental devices: These are devices that are embedded in or attached to the physical environment, such as smart home appliances, sensors, cameras, speakers, etc. They can provide various functions, such as sensing, computing, communication, control, etc.
  - Mobile devices: These are devices that are portable and can be carried by the user, such as smartphones, tablets, laptops, etc. They can provide various functions, such as sensing, computing, communication, display, etc.
  - Cloud devices: These are devices that are located in remote servers or data centers, such as cloud computing platforms, databases, etc. They can provide various functions, such as storage, processing, analysis, etc.
- Hardware for wearable computing, mixed reality and IoE can also be classified into three layers, based on their role in the system architecture:
  - Device layer: This is the layer that consists of the physical devices that interact with the user and the environment, such as sensors, actuators, displays, etc. They can collect data, perform actions, or provide feedback to the user or the environment.
  - Network layer: This is the layer that consists of the devices that enable the communication and data transmission between the device layer and the cloud layer, such as routers, gateways, modems, etc. They can provide wireless connectivity, data encryption, data compression, etc.
  - Cloud layer: This is the layer that consists of the devices that provide the storage, processing, and analysis of the data collected from the device layer, such as servers, databases, cloud platforms, etc. They can provide data management, data mining, machine learning, etc.
