# PIC for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

- Wearable computing is a field of technology that involves electronic devices that can be worn on the body, either as accessories or as part of the clothing.
- Wearable devices can have various functions, such as health monitoring, fitness tracking, entertainment, communication, navigation, and augmented reality.
- Wearable devices can communicate with other devices or networks using wireless technologies, such as Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, cellular, or near-field communication (NFC).
- Mixed reality is a term that describes the combination of real and virtual environments, where physical and digital objects can coexist and interact.
- Mixed reality can be achieved using various devices, such as head-mounted displays, smart glasses, projectors, or holograms.
- Mixed reality can have various applications, such as education, gaming, entertainment, tourism, medicine, and engineering.
- Internet of Everything (IoE) is a concept that refers to the interconnection of people, processes, data, and things using the Internet.
- IoE can enable new capabilities, experiences, and opportunities by leveraging the power of networked devices, sensors, analytics, and artificial intelligence.
- IoE can have various benefits, such as improving efficiency, productivity, safety, quality, and sustainability.
- IoE can also pose various challenges, such as security, privacy, interoperability, scalability, and ethics.
