### Mixed Reality

- Mixed reality is a medium consisting of immersive computer-generated environments in which elements of a physical and virtual environment are combined.
- Mixed reality is a blend of physical and digital worlds, unlocking natural and intuitive 3D human, computer, and environmental interactions.
- Mixed reality is the next wave in computing followed by mainframes, PCs, and smartphones. Mixed reality is going mainstream for consumers and businesses. It liberates us from screen-bound experiences by offering instinctual interactions with data in our living spaces and with our friends.
- Mixed reality that incorporates haptics has sometimes been referred to as Visuo-haptic mixed reality.
- Mixed reality can be classified into two types: augmented reality and virtual reality. Augmented reality enhances the real world with digital information, while virtual reality creates a fully immersive digital world that replaces the real one.
- Mixed reality can be experienced using various devices, such as head-mounted displays, smart glasses, smartphones, tablets, and projectors.
- Mixed reality can be used for various applications, such as education, entertainment, gaming, health care, manufacturing, tourism, and social interaction .
- Mixed reality can also be used for cybernetics and humanistic intelligence, which are the fields of study that explore the interaction and integration of humans and machines. Cybernetics focuses on the feedback, control, and communication aspects of the human-machine system, while humanistic intelligence emphasizes the creativity, expression, and emotion aspects of the human-machine system.
- Mixed reality can enable cybernetics and humanistic intelligence by providing a platform for creating, manipulating, and sharing information and experiences in a natural and intuitive way. Mixed reality can also enhance the human-machine system by augmenting the human senses, cognition, and capabilities with digital information and feedback.
