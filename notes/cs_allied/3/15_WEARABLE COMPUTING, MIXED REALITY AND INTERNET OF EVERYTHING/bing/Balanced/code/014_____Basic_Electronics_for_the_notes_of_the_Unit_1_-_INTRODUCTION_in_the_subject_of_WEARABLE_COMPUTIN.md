# Basic Electronics for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

- Wearable computing is a field of technology that involves designing and developing electronic devices that can be worn on the body, such as smart watches, fitness trackers, smart glasses, etc.
- Wearable devices can collect and analyze data, communicate with other devices, and provide feedback or services to the user, such as health monitoring, navigation, entertainment, etc.
- Wearable devices are examples of the Internet of Things (IoT), which is a network of physical objects that can interact with each other and the internet through sensors, software, and connectivity.
- Mixed reality is a term that encompasses both augmented reality (AR) and virtual reality (VR), which are technologies that create immersive and interactive experiences by blending digital and physical elements.
- AR is a technology that overlays digital information or graphics on the real world, such as navigation directions, product information, or game characters.
- VR is a technology that creates a simulated environment that the user can explore and interact with, such as a virtual museum, a video game, or a training scenario.
- Mixed reality devices can be wearable, such as head-mounted displays, smart glasses, or gloves, or non-wearable, such as smartphones, tablets, or projectors.
- Basic electronics is a branch of engineering that deals with the design, analysis, and application of electronic circuits and components, such as resistors, capacitors, transistors, diodes, etc.
- Basic electronics is essential for understanding and developing wearable and mixed reality devices, as they involve various electronic functions, such as power supply, signal processing, data transmission, display, etc.
- Basic electronics concepts include voltage, current, resistance, Ohm's law, Kirchhoff's laws, AC and DC circuits, analog and digital signals, logic gates, etc.
- Basic electronics tools include multimeter, oscilloscope, breadboard, soldering iron, etc.
