### Data persistence for the notes of the Unit 3 - CYBERNETICS AND HUMANISTIC INTELLIGENCE in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

- Data persistence is the property of data that survives after the process with which it was created has ended.
- Data persistence is important for wearable computing devices, which are portable computers that can be worn on the body and are designed for everyday and continuous use.
- Wearable computing devices can collect, process, and store various types of data, such as biometric, environmental, location, and user activity data .
- Data persistence can enable wearable computing devices to provide various benefits, such as:
  - Enhancing the intellect or augmenting the senses of the user by providing timely and relevant information.
  - Monitoring the health and wellness of the user by analyzing the biometric and behavioral data over time.
  - Supporting the user's interaction with mixed reality and internet of everything applications by providing contextual and personalized data.
- Data persistence can also pose various challenges, such as:
  - Ensuring the security and privacy of the data collected and stored by wearable computing devices, especially when the data is uploaded to the cloud or shared with other devices.
  - Optimizing the battery life and performance of the wearable computing devices, as data persistence can consume more power and memory resources.
  - Managing the data quality and consistency of the wearable computing devices, as data persistence can introduce errors and conflicts due to synchronization, duplication, or corruption issues.
