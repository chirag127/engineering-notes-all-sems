## Unit 3 - CYBERNETICS AND HUMANISTIC INTELLIGENCE

- Cybernetics is the science of human-machine interaction that employs the principles of feedback, control and communication .
- Humanistic intelligence is the intelligence that arises because of a human being in the feedback loop of a computational process, where the human and computer are inextricably intertwined.
- Cybernetics and humanistic intelligence are related fields that explore the possibilities and implications of human-machine integration and augmentation.
- Some of the topics covered in this unit are:

  - The history and development of cybernetics and humanistic intelligence, from the pioneering work of Norbert Wiener, Alan Turing, Marvin Minsky, Ray Kurzweil, Steve Mann and others   .
  - The concepts and applications of feedback, control and communication in cybernetic systems, such as thermostats, robots, neural networks, artificial intelligence and cybernetic organisms  .
  - The concepts and applications of humanistic intelligence in wearable computing, such as personal imaging, mediated reality, sousveillance, lifelogging and cyborgs.
  - The ethical, social and philosophical issues raised by cybernetics and humanistic intelligence, such as the nature of human identity, agency, autonomy, responsibility, privacy, security and morality  .
