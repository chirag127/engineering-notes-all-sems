### Sensors and Software for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

- Wearable computing is the integration of advanced technologies into everyday clothing and accessories, such as smart watches, glasses, rings, and bracelets.
- Mixed reality is the combination of real and virtual worlds, where physical and digital objects coexist and interact, such as holograms, augmented reality, and virtual reality.
- Internet of everything is the network of physical objects, people, processes, and data that are interconnected and communicate through the internet, such as smart homes, smart cities, and smart healthcare.
- Sensors are devices that detect and measure physical phenomena, such as temperature, pressure, motion, and sound, and convert them into electrical signals.
- Software is the set of instructions and data that control the operation and behavior of a computer system, such as applications, operating systems, and firmware.
- Sensors and software are essential components of wearable computing, mixed reality, and internet of everything, as they enable the collection, processing, and transmission of data from the environment and the user  .
- Some examples of sensors and software for wearable computing, mixed reality, and internet of everything are:
  - Artificial intelligence (AI) hearing aids, which use sensors to capture sound and software to analyze and enhance it.
  - Google Glass and Microsoft's HoloLens, which use sensors to track the user's head movements and software to project images and information on a transparent display.
  - Motus Smart, which uses sensors to monitor the healing of bone fractures and software to alert the patient and the doctor of any complications.
  - Smart Sensor Technology, which uses sensors to detect hazardous gas, fire, or intruders and software to self-diagnose and repair any faults.
