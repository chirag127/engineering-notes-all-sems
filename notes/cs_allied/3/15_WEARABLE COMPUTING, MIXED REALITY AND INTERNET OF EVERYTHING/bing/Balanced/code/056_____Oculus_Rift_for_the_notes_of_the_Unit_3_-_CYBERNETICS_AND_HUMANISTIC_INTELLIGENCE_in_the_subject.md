### Oculus Rift

- Oculus Rift is a **virtual reality headset** that connects to a gaming PC or laptop and allows the user to experience immersive and interactive 3D environments .
- Oculus Rift was invented by **Palmer Luckey**, who later founded Oculus VR with **Brendan Iribe** .
- Oculus Rift was funded by a **Kickstarter campaign** in 2012 and released on **March 28, 2016**.
- Oculus Rift consists of a pair of **goggles** with a high-resolution display, a pair of **headphones** with spatial audio, and a pair of **controllers** with motion and touch sensors  .
- Oculus Rift uses **inside-out tracking** technology, which means it does not require external sensors or cameras to track the user's head and hand movements.
- Oculus Rift requires a **compatible PC** with a minimum of 8 GB of RAM, an Intel i3-6100 or AMD Ryzen 3 1200 processor, and a NVIDIA GTX 1050 Ti or AMD Radeon RX 470 graphics card.
- Oculus Rift can run a variety of **VR apps and games** from the Oculus Store, SteamVR, or other sources.
- Oculus Rift is a discontinued product as of **April 2020**, when it was replaced by the **Oculus Rift S**, which has improved optics, tracking, and ergonomics .
- Oculus Rift is an example of **cybernetics and humanistic intelligence**, as it enhances the human perception and interaction with the virtual world, and creates a feedback loop between the user and the system.
