### Measurements for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

- Wearable computing is the use of devices that are worn on the body or attached to clothing, such as smartwatches, fitness trackers, smart glasses, or virtual reality headsets.
- Mixed reality is the blending of the physical and digital worlds, where digital content can interact with the real environment and vice versa, such as holograms, spatial computing, or augmented reality.
- Internet of everything is the network of connections between people, things, data, and processes that provide general intelligence and improved cognition across the networked environment, such as smart homes, smart cities, or smart health.
- The main objectives of this unit are to:
  - Understand the concepts and applications of wearable computing, mixed reality, and internet of everything.
  - Learn about the benefits and challenges of these technologies for individuals, organizations, and society.
  - Explore the current and future trends and developments in these fields.
  - Analyze the ethical, legal, and social implications of these technologies.
- The main topics covered in this unit are:
  - Definition and history of wearable computing, mixed reality, and internet of everything.
  - Types and examples of wearable devices, mixed reality devices, and internet of everything devices.
  - Functions and features of wearable computing, mixed reality, and internet of everything.
  - Advantages and disadvantages of wearable computing, mixed reality, and internet of everything.
  - Use cases and scenarios of wearable computing, mixed reality, and internet of everything in various domains and industries.
  - Design principles and best practices for wearable computing, mixed reality, and internet of everything.
  - Challenges and opportunities for wearable computing, mixed reality, and internet of everything.
  - Ethical, legal, and social issues and considerations for wearable computing, mixed reality, and internet of everything.
