### Language

- Language is a system of communication that uses symbols, such as words, gestures, or signs, to convey meaning and express ideas.
- Language can be natural or artificial, spoken or written, formal or informal, and human or machine.
- Language can be used for various purposes, such as describing, instructing, persuading, questioning, or entertaining.
- Language can also be analyzed at different levels, such as phonology (sound), morphology (word structure), syntax (sentence structure), semantics (meaning), pragmatics (context), and discourse (text).

#### Natural Language

- Natural language is a language that is spoken or written by humans, such as English, Mandarin, or Hindi.
- Natural language is characterized by its complexity, ambiguity, variability, and creativity.
- Natural language can be divided into two main categories: spoken language and written language.

##### Spoken Language

- Spoken language is a natural language that is produced and perceived by the vocal organs and the auditory system.
- Spoken language is composed of sounds, such as vowels, consonants, and tones, that are organized into syllables, words, phrases, and sentences.
- Spoken language is influenced by factors such as pitch, stress, intonation, and prosody, which convey information about the speaker's attitude, emotion, and intention.
- Spoken language is also affected by the speaker's dialect, accent, register, and style, which reflect the speaker's social and cultural background, education, and situation.

##### Written Language

- Written language is a natural language that is represented by symbols, such as letters, numbers, or punctuation marks, that are displayed on a medium, such as paper, screen, or skin.
- Written language is composed of symbols, such as alphabets, logograms, or ideograms, that are organized into words, sentences, paragraphs, and texts.
- Written language is influenced by factors such as orthography, grammar, spelling, and punctuation, which convey information about the writer's accuracy, clarity, and coherence.
- Written language is also affected by the writer's genre, format, and style, which reflect the writer's purpose, audience, and tone.

#### Artificial Language

- Artificial language is a language that is designed or constructed by humans, such as Esperanto, Klingon, or Python.
- Artificial language is characterized by its simplicity, precision, consistency, and logic.
- Artificial language can be divided into two main categories: formal language and informal language.

##### Formal Language

- Formal language is an artificial language that is used for specific purposes, such as mathematics, logic, or programming.
- Formal language is composed of symbols, such as operators, variables, or keywords, that are organized into expressions, statements, or commands.
- Formal language is influenced by factors such as syntax, semantics, and pragmatics, which convey information about the structure, meaning, and function of the language.
- Formal language is also affected by the domain, paradigm, and framework, which reflect the scope, approach, and model of the language.

##### Informal Language

- Informal language is an artificial language that is used for general purposes, such as communication, entertainment, or education.
- Informal language is composed of symbols, such as emojis, abbreviations, or hashtags, that are organized into messages, posts, or tweets.
- Informal language is influenced by factors such as context, culture, and convention, which convey information about the situation, background, and norm of the language.
- Informal language is also affected by the platform, medium, and mode, which reflect the device, channel, and format of the language.
