# WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

- Wearable computing is the use of devices that can be worn on the body, such as smartwatches, fitness trackers, smart glasses, or virtual reality headsets, to perform various functions and interact with the user and the environment .
- Mixed reality is the blending of the physical and digital worlds, where virtual objects can coexist and interact with real ones, creating immersive and interactive experiences for the user  .
- Internet of everything is the concept of connecting not only devices, but also people, processes, and data, through the internet, to enable new capabilities, insights, and opportunities .
- Some of the benefits of these technologies are:
  - They can enhance the user's perception, cognition, communication, and collaboration with others and the environment .
  - They can provide personalized, context-aware, and adaptive services and information to the user  .
  - They can support various domains and applications, such as health, education, entertainment, tourism, and social interaction   .
- Some of the challenges of these technologies are:
  - They require high performance, low latency, and reliable connectivity and computing resources  .
  - They raise ethical, legal, and social issues, such as privacy, security, trust, and user acceptance  .
  - They need to address human factors, such as usability, ergonomics, accessibility, and user experience   .
