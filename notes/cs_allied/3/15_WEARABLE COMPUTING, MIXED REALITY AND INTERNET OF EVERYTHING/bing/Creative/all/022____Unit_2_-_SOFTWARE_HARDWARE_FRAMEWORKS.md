## Unit 2 - SOFTWARE HARDWARE FRAMEWORKS

- This unit covers the basic concepts and principles of software and hardware frameworks that enable the development and deployment of artificial intelligence (AI) systems.
- Software frameworks are collections of libraries, tools, and standards that provide a common platform for developing and running AI applications. Examples of software frameworks for AI include TensorFlow, PyTorch, Keras, Scikit-learn, etc.
- Hardware frameworks are architectures, devices, and interfaces that support the execution and optimization of AI algorithms. Examples of hardware frameworks for AI include CPUs, GPUs, TPUs, FPGAs, ASICs, etc.
- The main objectives of software and hardware frameworks for AI are to:
  - Simplify the design, implementation, and testing of AI models and systems.
  - Enhance the performance, scalability, and efficiency of AI computations.
  - Facilitate the interoperability, portability, and compatibility of AI solutions across different platforms and environments.
  - Support the integration, collaboration, and innovation of AI research and development communities.
- The main challenges of software and hardware frameworks for AI are to:
  - Balance the trade-offs between flexibility, functionality, and usability of AI frameworks.
  - Address the heterogeneity, complexity, and diversity of AI problems and applications.
  - Adapt to the rapid evolution, innovation, and competition of AI technologies and markets.
  - Ensure the reliability, security, and ethics of AI systems and data.
