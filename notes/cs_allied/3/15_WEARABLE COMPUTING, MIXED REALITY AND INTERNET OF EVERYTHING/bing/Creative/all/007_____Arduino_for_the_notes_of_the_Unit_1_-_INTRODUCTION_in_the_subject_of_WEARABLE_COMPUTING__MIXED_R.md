# Arduino

## Unit 1 - Introduction

- Arduino is an open-source electronics platform based on easy-to-use hardware and software  .
- Arduino boards are able to read inputs from sensors, buttons, or online sources and turn them into outputs such as motors, LEDs, or online actions .
- Arduino can be used for various purposes such as prototyping, interactive art, robotics, wearable computing, mixed reality, and internet of things  .
- Arduino consists of three main components: the board, the IDE, and the library.
  - The board is the physical device that contains the microcontroller, the input/output pins, and other components.
  - The IDE is the software tool that allows you to write, compile, and upload code to the board.
  - The library is the collection of pre-written code that provides various functions and features for your projects.
- Arduino supports various types of boards, such as Uno, Nano, Mega, and Due, each with different specifications and capabilities .
- Arduino also supports various types of shields, which are add-on modules that extend the functionality of the board, such as Ethernet, WiFi, Bluetooth, GPS, and LCD .
- Arduino uses a simplified version of C/C++ as its programming language, which is easy to learn and write .
- Arduino has a large and active community of users, developers, and educators, who share their projects, code, and tutorials online   .
