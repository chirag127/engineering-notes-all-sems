# Parts identification for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

- Wearable computing: The use of devices that are worn on the body or attached to clothing, such as smartwatches, fitness trackers, smart glasses, or virtual reality headsets, that can collect and process data, communicate with other devices, and provide various services to the user  .
- Mixed reality: The blending of the physical and digital worlds, where virtual objects can interact with real environments and vice versa, creating a seamless and immersive experience for the user . Mixed reality can be achieved by using wearable devices such as augmented reality (AR) or spatial computing glasses, or by using projection-based systems or holograms.
- Internet of everything: The extension of the internet of things (IoT), which connects devices and sensors to the internet and each other, to include people, data, and processes, creating a network of intelligence and value that can improve efficiency, productivity, and quality of life . Internet of everything can enable new applications and services that leverage the data and insights generated by wearable devices, mixed reality systems, and other smart objects.
