# Arm Devices

Arm devices are wearable computing devices that use Arm processors to provide low-power, high-performance and secure computing capabilities. Arm devices can be used for various applications, such as augmented reality, virtual reality, fitness tracking, health monitoring, smart clothing and more.

Some of the features and benefits of Arm devices are:

- They are based on the Arm architecture, which is a widely used and standardized instruction set for embedded systems.
- They can leverage the Arm ecosystem, which consists of over 300 software partners that provide operating systems, middleware, libraries, tools and applications for Arm devices.
- They can use the Arm Mbed development platform, which is a cloud-based service that allows device makers to easily create, test and deploy Arm devices with different components, connectivity options and operating systems.
- They can support various sensors, actuators, displays, cameras, microphones, speakers and other peripherals that enable rich and interactive user experiences.
- They can offer high security and privacy features, such as Arm TrustZone, which is a hardware-based isolation mechanism that protects sensitive data and code from unauthorized access.
- They can enable energy-efficient and long-lasting operation, as Arm processors are designed to optimize power consumption and performance for different workloads and scenarios.

Some of the examples of Arm devices are:

- Google Glass, which is a head-mounted display that provides information and communication features through voice and gesture commands.
- Samsung Gear, which is a series of smartwatches that offer fitness tracking, health monitoring, notifications, music playback and more.
- Oculus Quest, which is a standalone virtual reality headset that delivers immersive gaming and entertainment experiences.
- Nreal Light, which is a pair of augmented reality glasses that project realistic 3D images and videos onto the user's field of view.
- Hexoskin, which is a smart shirt that measures the user's heart rate, breathing rate, activity level and sleep quality.
