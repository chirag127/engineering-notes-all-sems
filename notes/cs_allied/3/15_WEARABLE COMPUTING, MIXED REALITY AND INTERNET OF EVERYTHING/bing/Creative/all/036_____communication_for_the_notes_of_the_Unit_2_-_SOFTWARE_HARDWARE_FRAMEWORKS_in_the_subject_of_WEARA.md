# Communication for the notes of the Unit 2 - SOFTWARE HARDWARE FRAMEWORKS in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

- Communication is the process of exchanging information and data between different devices, systems, or people.
- Communication in wearable computing, mixed reality and internet of everything involves the use of various technologies and protocols to enable seamless and ubiquitous interaction among different devices, sensors, users, and environments.
- Some of the key aspects of communication in these domains are:

  - **Wearable communication space**: This is a concept that uses wearable computers as a communication device to enable remote collaboration and awareness among multiple users. A wearable communication space can provide a body-stabilized virtual environment that allows the wearable user to interact with remote users and objects using gestures, voice, or gaze.
  - **Internet of Things (IoT)**: This is a network of physical objects that are embedded with sensors, actuators, and communication capabilities to collect, process, and exchange data with other devices, systems, or services over the internet. IoT can enable various applications and scenarios for wearable computing and mixed reality, such as health monitoring, smart home, smart city, smart factory, etc.
  - **Mixed reality (MR)**: This is a technology that combines the real world and the virtual world, creating immersive experiences where 3D digital images can be represented alongside physical objects. MR can provide a new way of communication and interaction that transcends the limitations of traditional media and interfaces. MR can also leverage the data and services from IoT to create context-aware and personalized experiences.
  - **Bitcoin**: This is a decentralized digital currency that uses peer-to-peer network and cryptography to enable secure and anonymous transactions without the need for intermediaries or central authorities. Bitcoin can provide a new paradigm of communication and exchange that challenges the existing legal and regulatory frameworks. Bitcoin can also enable new forms of value creation and distribution for wearable computing and mixed reality, such as micropayments, crowdfunding, digital art, etc.

- Some of the challenges and opportunities of communication in these domains are:

  - **Privacy and security**: Communication in wearable computing, mixed reality and internet of everything involves the collection, processing, and sharing of large amounts of personal and sensitive data, which poses significant risks to the privacy and security of the users and devices . Therefore, there is a need for developing and implementing effective mechanisms and policies to protect the data and the rights of the users and devices, such as encryption, authentication, access control, consent, etc .
  - **Interoperability and standardization**: Communication in wearable computing, mixed reality and internet of everything involves the interaction and integration of various devices, systems, and services that may have different architectures, protocols, and formats . Therefore, there is a need for developing and adopting common standards and frameworks to ensure the interoperability and compatibility of the devices, systems, and services, such as communication protocols, data formats, APIs, etc .
  - **User experience and acceptance**: Communication in wearable computing, mixed reality and internet of everything involves the creation and consumption of novel and immersive experiences that may have different effects and implications for the users and the society . Therefore, there is a need for understanding and evaluating the user experience and acceptance of the devices, systems, and services, such as usability, accessibility, satisfaction, trust, etc . There is also a need for designing and developing user-centric and ethical devices, systems, and services that can enhance the quality of life and well-being of the users and the society .
