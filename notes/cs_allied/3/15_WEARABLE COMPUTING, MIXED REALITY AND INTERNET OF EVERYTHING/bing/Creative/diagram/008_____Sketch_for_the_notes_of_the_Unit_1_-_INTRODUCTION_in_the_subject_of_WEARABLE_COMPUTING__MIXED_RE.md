# Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION

## Wearable Computing

- Wearable computing is the use of devices that can be worn on the body, such as smartwatches, fitness trackers, smart glasses, or virtual reality headsets.
- Wearable devices can collect and analyse data from the user's body, environment, or activities, and provide feedback, information, or services to the user or other devices.
- Wearable computing can enable new applications in health, fitness, entertainment, education, communication, and productivity.

## Mixed Reality

- Mixed reality is a type of immersive technology that combines the physical and digital worlds, creating a hybrid reality where virtual and real objects can coexist and interact.
- Mixed reality can be achieved by using wearable devices such as head-mounted displays (HMDs) or smart glasses that can project digital content onto the user's field of view, or by using spatial computing that can sense and map the physical environment and overlay digital information on it.
- Mixed reality can enhance the user's perception, cognition, and interaction with the physical and digital worlds, enabling new possibilities for learning, entertainment, collaboration, and creativity.

## Internet of Everything

- Internet of Everything (IoE) is a network of connections between people, things, data, and processes that provide general intelligence and improved cognition across the networked environment.
- IoE is an extension of the Internet of Things (IoT), which is the network of physical objects embedded with sensors, software, or connectivity that can communicate and exchange data with other devices or systems.
- IoE can enable new applications and services that leverage the data and insights from the connected devices, people, and processes, such as smart cities, smart homes, smart health, smart agriculture, and smart industry.
