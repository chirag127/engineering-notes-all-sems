### Raspberry Pi for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

- Raspberry Pi is a low-cost, low-power, credit-card-sized single-board computer that can connect to a variety of devices and sensors .
- Raspberry Pi was developed by the Raspberry Pi Foundation, a UK-based charity, to promote computing education and digital making for people of all ages .
- Raspberry Pi can run various operating systems, such as Raspberry Pi OS, Ubuntu, Windows 10 IoT Core, etc. It can also run programming languages like Python, Scratch, C, Java, etc .
- Raspberry Pi has different models and versions, each with different specifications and features. Some of the common models are Raspberry Pi 4 Model B, Raspberry Pi 3 Model B+, Raspberry Pi Zero, etc .
- Raspberry Pi can be used for various projects and applications, such as web servers, media centers, robots, smart home devices, gaming consoles, etc. It can also be used for learning and experimenting with electronics, coding, and hardware .
- Raspberry Pi can be plugged into a computer monitor or TV, and uses a standard keyboard and mouse. It can also be connected to the internet via Ethernet or Wi-Fi, and to other devices via USB, HDMI, GPIO, etc .
- Raspberry Pi can be integrated with wearable computing, mixed reality, and internet of everything technologies, such as sensors, cameras, displays, speakers, microphones, etc. It can also communicate with other devices and platforms, such as Arduino, Android, iOS, etc .
