### Raspberry Pi

Raspberry Pi is a small, low-cost, single-board computer that allows people to learn and explore computing, programming, and electronics. It can be used for various projects, such as robotics, gaming, home automation, and Internet of Things. Some of the features and benefits of Raspberry Pi are:

- It is about the size of a credit card and can be plugged into a monitor, keyboard, and mouse. It can also be powered by a USB charger or a battery pack.
- It uses a system on a chip (SoC) that integrates the CPU and GPU in a single integrated circuit, with the RAM, USB ports, and other components soldered onto the board.
- It has a 40-pin GPIO header that allows it to connect to sensors, motors, LEDs, and other devices. It also has a camera and a display interface for adding a camera module and a touchscreen.
- It runs on Linux, a free and open-source operating system that supports many programming languages, such as Python, Scratch, C, Java, and more. It also has a graphical user interface (GUI) and a web browser for accessing the internet.
- It is supported by the Raspberry Pi Foundation, a UK-based charity that provides educational resources, online courses, and community support for Raspberry Pi users. It also organizes events, such as Raspberry Jams, where people can share their projects and learn from each other.
- It is affordable and accessible for everyone, with prices ranging from $5 to $75 depending on the model and features. It is also widely available from online and offline retailers, such as Amazon, Adafruit, and Micro Center.
