### Cybernetics and Humanistic Intelligence
- Cybernetics is the science of communication and control in living organisms and machines.
- Cybernetics studies the principles of feedback, adaptation, self-organization, and learning in complex systems.
- Cybernetics can be applied to various fields, such as biology, psychology, sociology, engineering, management, etc.
- Humanistic intelligence is the ability to create and use technology that enhances human capabilities and values.
- Humanistic intelligence involves the integration of human and artificial intelligence, as well as the design of human-computer interfaces that respect human dignity and diversity.
- Humanistic intelligence can be achieved through wearable computing, mixed reality, and internet of everything.
- Wearable computing is the use of devices that are worn on the body, such as smart glasses, smart watches, smart clothing, etc.
- Mixed reality is the combination of real and virtual environments, such as augmented reality, virtual reality, holograms, etc.
- Internet of everything is the network of physical objects, people, and data that can communicate and interact with each other, such as smart homes, smart cities, smart health, etc.
