## Unit 3 - CYBERNETICS AND HUMANISTIC INTELLIGENCE

- Cybernetics is the science of human-machine interaction that employs the principles of feedback, control and communication.
- Humanistic intelligence is the intelligence that arises because of a human being in the feedback loop of a computational process, where the human and computer are inextricably intertwined.
- Some of the topics covered in this unit are:
  - The history and development of cybernetics, from Norbert Wiener's seminal work on control and communication in the animal and the machine to the contemporary applications of artificial intelligence and machine learning.
  - The concept and examples of humanistic intelligence, such as wearable computing, augmented reality, and brain-computer interfaces, and how they enhance human capabilities and experiences.
  - The ethical and social implications of cybernetics and humanistic intelligence, such as the questions of human identity, agency, responsibility, and autonomy in a world where machines are becoming more intelligent and powerful.
  - The challenges and opportunities of cybernetics and humanistic intelligence for the future of humanity, such as the possibility of uploading human consciousness into a computer, transcending biological limitations, and creating new forms of life and intelligence .
