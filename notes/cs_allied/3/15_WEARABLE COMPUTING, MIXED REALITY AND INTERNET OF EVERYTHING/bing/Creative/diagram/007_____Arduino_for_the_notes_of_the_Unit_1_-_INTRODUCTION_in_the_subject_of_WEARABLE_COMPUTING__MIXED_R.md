### Arduino

- Arduino is an open-source electronics platform based on easy-to-use hardware and software  .
- Arduino boards are able to read inputs from sensors, buttons, or online sources, and turn them into outputs such as motors, LEDs, or online actions .
- Arduino boards are programmed using the Arduino IDE, which is a cross-platform application that supports various languages and libraries  .
- Arduino boards are based on microcontrollers, which are small computers that can execute instructions and interact with other components .
- Arduino boards are designed to be compatible with various shields, which are expansion boards that add functionality such as wireless communication, GPS, LCD display, etc .
- Arduino boards are suitable for beginners, hobbyists, educators, and professionals who want to create interactive projects, prototypes, or products   .
- Arduino boards are part of the Arduino ecosystem, which includes online platforms, communities, events, and resources that support the Arduino users and developers  .
