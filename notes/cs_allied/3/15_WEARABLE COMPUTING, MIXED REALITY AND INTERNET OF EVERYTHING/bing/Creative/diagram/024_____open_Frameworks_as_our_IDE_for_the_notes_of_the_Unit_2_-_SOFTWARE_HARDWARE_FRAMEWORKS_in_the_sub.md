### openFrameworks as our IDE

- openFrameworks is an **open source C++ toolkit** for creative coding.
- It is designed to assist the creative process by providing a **simple and intuitive framework** for experimentation.
- It supports **cross-platform development** and works with various operating systems, such as Windows, Linux, macOS, iOS, Android, Raspberry Pi, etc .
- It wraps together several **commonly used libraries**, such as OpenGL, FreeType, FreeImage, OpenCV, etc.
- It also offers a **large collection of addons** that extend its functionality, such as ofxKinect, ofxOsc, ofxGui, etc.
- To use openFrameworks, you will need an **IDE (Integrated Development Environment)**, which is a software application that helps you write, compile, debug and run your code.
- openFrameworks supports **multiple IDEs**, such as Visual Studio, Xcode, Code::Blocks, Eclipse, etc.
- You can choose your preferred IDE and platform when you download openFrameworks from its official website.
- You can also find **setup guides**, **documentation**, **tutorials**, **examples** and **forum** on the website to help you learn and use openFrameworks .
- openFrameworks is distributed under the **MIT License**, which means you can use it for any purpose, even commercially, as long as you give credit to the original authors.
