# Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION
## Measurements
- Measurements are the process of quantifying or estimating the attributes or properties of objects, events, or phenomena in the real world.
- Measurements can be used for various purposes, such as describing, comparing, evaluating, controlling, predicting, or designing systems or processes.
- Measurements can be performed using different methods, instruments, units, standards, or scales, depending on the context and the desired accuracy or precision.
- Measurements can be classified into different types, such as direct or indirect, absolute or relative, discrete or continuous, nominal or ordinal, interval or ratio, etc.
- Measurements can also be categorized into different domains, such as physical, biological, social, or digital, depending on the nature of the objects, events, or phenomena being measured.

## Wearable Computing
- Wearable computing is a branch of computing that involves the use of wearable devices or systems that can be worn on the body or integrated into clothing or accessories.
- Wearable computing aims to provide ubiquitous, natural, and intuitive interaction with information and services, as well as to augment or enhance human capabilities, such as perception, cognition, communication, or health.
- Wearable computing can be applied to various domains, such as entertainment, education, health, fitness, sports, military, security, fashion, etc.
- Wearable computing can be implemented using different technologies, such as sensors, actuators, displays, cameras, microphones, speakers, batteries, wireless communication, etc.
- Wearable computing can also be integrated with other technologies, such as cloud computing, artificial intelligence, internet of things, mixed reality, etc.

## Mixed Reality
- Mixed reality is a branch of computing that involves the creation and manipulation of digital content that is integrated with the physical environment or reality.
- Mixed reality aims to provide immersive, interactive, and realistic experiences that can enrich or transform the perception and understanding of the real world, as well as to enable new forms of expression, communication, and collaboration.
- Mixed reality can be applied to various domains, such as entertainment, education, health, engineering, architecture, art, etc.
- Mixed reality can be implemented using different technologies, such as virtual reality, augmented reality, spatial computing, holograms, projection mapping, etc.
- Mixed reality can also be integrated with other technologies, such as wearable computing, internet of things, artificial intelligence, cloud computing, etc.

## Internet of Everything
- Internet of everything is a branch of computing that involves the interconnection and integration of people, things, data, and processes in a networked environment.
- Internet of everything aims to provide ubiquitous, intelligent, and adaptive services and solutions that can address various challenges and opportunities in the society, economy, and environment.
- Internet of everything can be applied to various domains, such as smart cities, smart homes, smart agriculture, smart transportation, smart health, smart energy, etc.
- Internet of everything can be implemented using different technologies, such as internet of things, internet of services, internet of data, internet of people, etc.
- Internet of everything can also be integrated with other technologies, such as cloud computing, artificial intelligence, wearable computing, mixed reality, etc.
