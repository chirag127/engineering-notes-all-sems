## Unit 3 - CYBERNETICS AND HUMANISTIC INTELLIGENCE

Cybernetics and humanistic intelligence are two related but distinct concepts that explore the interaction between humans and machines.

- Cybernetics is the science of human-machine interaction that employs the principles of feedback, control and communication. It was founded by Norbert Wiener, who investigated the interplay between human beings and machines in a world in which machines are becoming ever more computationally capable and powerful.
- Humanistic intelligence is the intelligence that arises because of a human being in the feedback loop of a computational process, where the human and computer are inextricably intertwined. It was coined by Steve Mann, who pioneered the field of wearable computing, which aims to augment human perception and cognition with wearable devices.
- The difference between cybernetics and humanistic intelligence is that cybernetics focuses on the general principles of feedback, control and communication that apply to any system, whether biological, mechanical or social, while humanistic intelligence emphasizes the role of the human being as the central agent in the system, and the importance of human values and ethics in the design and use of technology.
- Some of the topics that cybernetics and humanistic intelligence address are:
  - The nature and limits of artificial intelligence (AI) and how it compares and contrasts with human intelligence. AI is based on the idea of creating machines to mimic human intelligence and behavior, whereas cybernetics and humanistic intelligence study the interaction and integration of human and machine intelligence.
  - The possibility and implications of downloading human consciousness into a computer, or uploading a computer program into a human brain. This is a hypothetical scenario that challenges the notion of human identity and embodiment, and raises ethical and philosophical questions about the nature of life, death and consciousness.
  - The impact of technology on human society, culture and environment, and how humans can use technology to enhance their well-being, creativity and autonomy. Cybernetics and humanistic intelligence explore the potential benefits and risks of technology, and how humans can shape and adapt to the technological changes that affect their lives .
  - The integration of cognitive cybernetics into intelligent human systems, which are systems that involve human intelligence, cognition and emotion, as well as machine intelligence, computation and communication. Cognitive cybernetics help to bring the understanding of a triad (system – process – relationship) in intelligent human systems, dominated by the human intelligent being.
