### Wearables for the unit 3 - cybernetics and humanistic intelligence

- Wearables are devices that can be worn on the body or integrated into clothing, accessories, or implants, that have sensing, computing, and communication capabilities.
- Wearables can be used for various purposes, such as health monitoring, fitness tracking, entertainment, education, social interaction, augmented reality, and personal assistance.
- Cybernetics is the science of communication and control in living organisms and machines, that studies the feedback loops and self-regulation mechanisms that enable adaptation and learning.
- Cybernetics can be applied to wearables to design systems that can interact with the human body and the environment, and adjust their behavior accordingly.
- Humanistic intelligence is a concept that defines intelligence as the result of a human being in the feedback loop of a computational process, where the human and computer are inextricably intertwined.
- Humanistic intelligence can be embodied by wearables that augment the human senses, cognition, and expression, and create a symbiotic relationship between the human and the machine.
- Some examples of wearables that embody humanistic intelligence are:
  - Smart glasses, such as Google Glass, that provide information and assistance to the user through a head-mounted display and voice commands.
  - Smart watches, such as Apple Watch, that measure the user's vital signs, activity, and notifications, and allow the user to control other devices through gestures and touch.
  - Smart clothing, such as Levi's Commuter Jacket, that incorporate sensors and actuators into the fabric, and enable the user to interact with their smartphone or other devices through touch or voice.
  - Smart implants, such as cochlear implants, that restore or enhance the user's hearing ability by converting sound signals into electrical impulses that stimulate the auditory nerve.
  - Smart tattoos, such as DuoSkin, that use conductive ink to create interactive interfaces on the skin, that can sense touch, display information, and communicate with other devices.
