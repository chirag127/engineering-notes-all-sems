### Desktop for the notes of the Unit 2 - SOFTWARE HARDWARE FRAMEWORKS in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

- Wearable computing is the use of devices that are worn on the body or attached to clothing, such as smartwatches, fitness trackers, smart glasses, or virtual reality headsets.
- Mixed reality is the blending of the physical and digital worlds, where virtual objects can interact with real environments and vice versa, such as in augmented reality or spatial computing.
- Internet of everything (IoE) is the network of connections between people, things, data, and processes that provide general intelligence and improved cognition across the networked environment.
- Software hardware frameworks are the architectures and platforms that enable the development and deployment of wearable computing, mixed reality, and internet of everything applications and services.
- Some examples of software hardware frameworks are:
  - Android Wear: a version of Google's Android operating system designed for smartwatches and other wearables.
  - Magic Leap One: a wearable device that uses light field technology to create realistic 3D images that blend with the real world.
  - HoloLens 2: a mixed reality headset that uses holographic technology to project 3D objects and interfaces in the user's field of view.
  - Arduino: an open-source hardware and software platform that allows users to create interactive electronic projects using sensors, actuators, and microcontrollers.
  - Raspberry Pi: a low-cost, credit-card sized computer that can run various operating systems and be used for various IoT projects.
  - AWS IoT: a cloud-based platform that provides tools and services to connect, manage, and secure IoT devices and applications.
- Some challenges and opportunities of software hardware frameworks are:
  - Compatibility: ensuring that different devices, platforms, and standards can communicate and interoperate with each other.
  - Security: protecting the data and privacy of users and devices from unauthorized access, manipulation, or theft.
  - Scalability: handling the increasing number and diversity of devices, data, and users in the network.
  - User experience: designing intuitive, engaging, and comfortable interfaces and interactions for users of wearable computing, mixed reality, and internet of everything applications and services.
  - Innovation: exploring new possibilities and applications of wearable computing, mixed reality, and internet of everything technologies for various domains and purposes.
