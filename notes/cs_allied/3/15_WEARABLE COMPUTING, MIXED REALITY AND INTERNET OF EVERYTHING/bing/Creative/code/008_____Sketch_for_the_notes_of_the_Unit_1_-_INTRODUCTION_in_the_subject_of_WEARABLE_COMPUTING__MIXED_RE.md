# Sketch for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

- Wearable computing is the use of devices that are worn on the body or attached to clothing, such as smartwatches, fitness trackers, smart glasses, or virtual reality headsets. These devices can collect and analyse data, communicate with other devices or the internet, and provide various functions or services to the user.
- Mixed reality is the blending of the physical and digital worlds, where virtual objects or information can be projected onto the real environment, or real objects can be integrated into the virtual environment. Mixed reality devices, such as Magic Leap One or HoloLens 2, can create immersive and interactive experiences that enhance the user's perception and cognition.
- Internet of everything (IoE) is the extension of the internet of things (IoT), which connects devices, sensors, and data to the internet and each other. IoE also includes the connections between people, processes, and things, and the intelligence and value that can be derived from these connections. IoE can enable new applications, opportunities, and challenges in various domains, such as health, education, entertainment, or security.
- The relationship between wearable computing, mixed reality, and IoE is that they are all technologies that can augment the human capabilities and experiences, and create a seamless integration between the physical and digital worlds. Wearable computing can provide personal and contextual data, mixed reality can provide rich and engaging visualisation and interaction, and IoE can provide networked and intelligent services and solutions. Together, they can create a new paradigm of computing that is ubiquitous, natural, and human-centric.
