# PHP/Web for the notes of the Unit 3 - CYBERNETICS AND HUMANISTIC INTELLIGENCE in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

- PHP is a server scripting language, and a powerful tool for making dynamic and interactive Web pages.
- PHP is widely-used, free, and efficient alternative to competitors such as Microsoft's ASP.
- PHP runs on various platforms (Windows, Linux, Unix, Mac OS X, etc.) and is compatible with almost all servers used today (Apache, IIS, etc.).
- PHP supports a wide range of databases and is easy to learn and runs efficiently on the server side.
- Cybernetics is the science of communication and control in living organisms and machines.
- Cybernetics and systems bring together scientists from diverse disciplines and update them in important cybernetic and systems methods, while drawing attention to novel useful applications of these methods to problems from all areas of research, in the humanities, in the sciences and the technical disciplines.
- Wearable computing is the use of electronic devices that can be worn on the body, such as smartwatches, fitness trackers, smart glasses, virtual reality headsets, etc. .
- Wearable computing and cloud computing is all about collecting and analysing data and connecting devices both to the Internet and each other.
- Wearable computing is part of the Internet of Things (IoT), which refers to electronic devices that can be connected through internet networks.
- IoT enables wearables and sensors to help people interact with other devices and physical things around them, and to provide various services and assistance in daily activities .
- Mixed reality (MR) is the blending of the physical and digital worlds, where physical and digital objects co-exist and interact in real time.
- MR can be achieved by using various technologies, such as augmented reality (AR), virtual reality (VR), holograms, etc..
- MR can be integrated with IoT as an assistive technology for elderly people, to improve their interactions with the environment, recognise and search for objects, wayfinding, and navigation.
- MR and IoT can also provide various benefits for other domains, such as education, entertainment, health, etc. .
