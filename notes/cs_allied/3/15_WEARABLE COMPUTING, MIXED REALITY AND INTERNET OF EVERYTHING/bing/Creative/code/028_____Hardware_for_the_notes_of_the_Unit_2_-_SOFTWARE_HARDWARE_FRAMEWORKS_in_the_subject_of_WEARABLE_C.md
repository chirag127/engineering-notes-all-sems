### Hardware for the notes of the Unit 2 - SOFTWARE HARDWARE FRAMEWORKS in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

- Hardware refers to the physical components and devices that enable wearable computing, mixed reality and internet of everything applications.
- Hardware for wearable computing includes sensors, actuators, displays, batteries, processors, memory, communication modules, and input/output devices that can be worn on or embedded in the body.
- Hardware for mixed reality includes devices that can project digital information onto the physical environment, such as head-mounted displays, smart glasses, projectors, cameras, and speakers, as well as devices that can capture and track the user's position, orientation, gestures, and eye movements, such as accelerometers, gyroscopes, magnetometers, GPS, cameras, and eye trackers.
- Hardware for internet of everything includes devices that can connect to the internet and exchange data with other devices, such as smartphones, tablets, smart watches, smart bracelets, smart rings, smart clothing, smart shoes, smart jewelry, smart glasses, smart helmets, smart earphones, smart patches, smart implants, and smart tattoos, as well as devices that can sense and actuate the physical environment, such as RFID tags, NFC chips, QR codes, barcodes, sensors, actuators, and microcontrollers.
- Hardware for these applications should be designed to satisfy characteristics such as being small, light, wireless, low-power, robust, comfortable, ergonomic, aesthetic, and secure.
- Hardware for these applications should also be designed to support different interaction modalities, such as touch, gesture, voice, gaze, and brain-computer interfaces, and to enable natural and non-obtrusive means of interaction with the user and the environment.
- Hardware for these applications should also be designed to enable different levels of immersion, presence, and realism, depending on the application domain and the user's preferences.
- Hardware for these applications should also be designed to be compatible and interoperable with other devices and platforms, and to follow the standards and protocols of the web of things and the internet of things.
