### Raspberry Pi for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

- Raspberry Pi is a low-cost, low power consumption, credit-card-sized single-board computer that can connect to a computer monitor or TV, and use a standard keyboard and mouse .
- Raspberry Pi is developed by the Raspberry Pi Foundation, based in the U.K., with the aim of imparting knowledge about computing to even younger students at the cheapest possible price.
- Raspberry Pi can run various operating systems, such as Linux, Windows, and Android, and can be used for various purposes, such as web browsing, gaming, programming, robotics, and IoT .
- Raspberry Pi has different models and versions, each with different specifications, such as CPU speed, RAM, weight, and ports. The latest model is Raspberry Pi 4 Model B, which has a 1.5 GHz CPU and up to 8 GB of RAM.
- Raspberry Pi can connect to a huge variety of sensors and other modules, such as LCD displays, servos, and motors, using GPIO pins, USB ports, or wireless communication protocols . This enables users to create various projects and applications, such as smart home devices, wearable gadgets, and mixed reality systems.
