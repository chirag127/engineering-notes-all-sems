# Unit 2 - SOFTWARE HARDWARE FRAMEWORKS

## Wearable Computing
- Wearable computing is the use of devices that can be worn on the body, such as smartwatches, fitness trackers, smart glasses, or virtual reality headsets.
- Wearable computing can provide various benefits, such as convenience, personalization, health monitoring, entertainment, and productivity.
- Wearable computing can also pose some challenges, such as privacy, security, usability, compatibility, and battery life.
- Wearable computing can connect to the internet of things (IoT), which is a network of devices that can communicate and exchange data with each other and the cloud.
- Wearable computing can also enable mixed reality (MR), which is a combination of real and virtual environments that can interact with each other.

## Mixed Reality
- Mixed reality (MR) is a type of immersive technology that blends the physical and digital worlds, creating a hybrid reality that can be experienced through wearable devices, such as smart glasses or head-mounted displays.
- Mixed reality can offer various applications, such as education, gaming, tourism, healthcare, and industrial design.
- Mixed reality can also enhance human capabilities, such as multitasking, creativity, collaboration, and spatial awareness.
- Mixed reality can also face some challenges, such as technical limitations, user acceptance, ethical issues, and social implications.
- Mixed reality can leverage the internet of things (IoT) to connect with other devices and physical objects, creating a more interactive and contextualized experience.

## Internet of Everything
- Internet of everything (IoE) is an extension of the internet of things (IoT) that encompasses not only devices, but also people, data, and processes, creating a network of intelligence and value across the connected environment.
- Internet of everything can enable various opportunities, such as smart cities, smart homes, smart agriculture, smart healthcare, and smart manufacturing.
- Internet of everything can also generate various challenges, such as data management, security, interoperability, scalability, and governance.
- Internet of everything can integrate wearable computing and mixed reality to create a more personalized, immersive, and responsive experience for the users and the stakeholders.
