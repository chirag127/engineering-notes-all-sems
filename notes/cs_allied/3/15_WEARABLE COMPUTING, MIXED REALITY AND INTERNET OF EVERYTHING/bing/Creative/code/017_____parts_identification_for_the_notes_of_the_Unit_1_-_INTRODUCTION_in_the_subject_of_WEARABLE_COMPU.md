# Parts identification

- Wearable computing: A wearable device is a computer that can be worn on the body, either as an accessory or as part of the clothing. Wearable devices can perform various functions, such as sensing, computing, communicating, displaying, or interacting with the user or the environment. Examples of wearable devices are smartwatches, fitness trackers, smart glasses, head-mounted displays, smart clothing, etc.
- Mixed reality: A mixed reality (MR) is a type of virtual reality that combines real and virtual elements in the same environment. MR can create immersive and interactive experiences that blend the physical and digital worlds. Examples of MR devices are Microsoft HoloLens, Magic Leap One, Oculus Quest, etc.
- Internet of Everything: The Internet of Everything (IoE) is a concept that refers to the interconnection of people, processes, data, and things through the Internet. IoE can enable new possibilities for innovation, efficiency, and value creation across various domains, such as health, education, transportation, energy, etc. Examples of IoE applications are smart cities, smart homes, smart grids, smart healthcare, etc.
