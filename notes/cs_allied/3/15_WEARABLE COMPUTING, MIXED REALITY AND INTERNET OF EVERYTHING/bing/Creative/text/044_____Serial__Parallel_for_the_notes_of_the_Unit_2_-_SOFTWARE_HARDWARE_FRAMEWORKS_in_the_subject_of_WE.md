### Serial and Parallel Communication

- Serial communication is the process of sending data one bit at a time, sequentially, over a communication channel or computer bus.
- Parallel communication is the method of conveying multiple binary digits (bits) simultaneously using multiple conductors.
- The basic difference between serial and parallel communication is the number of electrical conductors used at the physical layer to convey bits.
- Serial communication has the advantages of lower cost, simpler wiring, longer distance, and less interference than parallel communication.
- Parallel communication has the advantages of higher speed, higher bandwidth, and synchronization than serial communication.
- Serial communication is suitable for applications that require low data rates, long distances, or low power consumption, such as sensors, modems, or microcontrollers.
- Parallel communication is suitable for applications that require high data rates, short distances, or high performance, such as printers, hard disks, or graphics cards.
