### Case studies for the notes of the Unit 3 - CYBERNETICS AND HUMANISTIC INTELLIGENCE in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

- Case study 1: Digital Eye Glass
  - A wearable computing device that augments the user's vision with digital information and images.
  - Developed by Steve Mann, a pioneer of wearable computing and humanistic intelligence.
  - Uses a head-mounted display, a camera, a computer, and a wireless connection to overlay digital information on the user's view of the physical world.
  - Enables applications such as augmented reality, personal cybernetics, mediated reality, and intelligent imaging.
  - Examples: EyeTap, Google Glass, Microsoft HoloLens, etc.

- Case study 2: Norbert Wiener and Cybernetics
  - A scientific discipline that studies the communication and control of systems, both natural and artificial, and their interactions with humans.
  - Founded by Norbert Wiener, a mathematician and philosopher, in the mid-20th century.
  - Explores the similarities and differences between biological and mechanical systems, and their implications for human society and ethics.
  - Influenced fields such as artificial intelligence, robotics, information theory, systems theory, cyberculture, etc.
  - Examples: feedback loops, thermostats, neural networks, cybernetic organisms, etc.
