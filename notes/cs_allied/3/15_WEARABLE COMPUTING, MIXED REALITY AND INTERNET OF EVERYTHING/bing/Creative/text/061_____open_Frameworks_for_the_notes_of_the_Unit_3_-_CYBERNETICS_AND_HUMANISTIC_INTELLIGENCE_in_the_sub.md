### Open Frameworks for the notes of the Unit 3 - CYBERNETICS AND HUMANISTIC INTELLIGENCE in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

- **Cybernetics** is the science of communication and control in living organisms and machines. It studies the principles of feedback, adaptation, self-organization, and learning in complex systems.
- **Humanistic Intelligence** is a concept proposed by Steve Mann that describes the intelligence that arises from the interaction between a human and a wearable computer. It is based on the idea that the human and the computer are inextricably intertwined and form a single cognitive unit.
- **Open Frameworks** is a cross-platform toolkit for creative coding that enables artists, designers, and programmers to create interactive applications using various media and sensors. It is based on open source software and hardware, and supports a variety of languages and platforms.
- Some of the applications of open frameworks in the context of cybernetics and humanistic intelligence are:
  - **Augmented Reality** (AR): AR is the technology that overlays digital information onto the physical world, enhancing the user's perception and interaction with the environment. Open frameworks can be used to create AR applications that use computer vision, graphics, sound, and spatial mapping to create immersive and interactive experiences.
  - **Wearable Computing** (WC): WC is the technology that integrates computing devices into the user's clothing or body, enabling constant and seamless access to information and communication. Open frameworks can be used to create WC applications that use sensors, actuators, displays, and wireless communication to create personalized and adaptive systems.
  - **Internet of Everything** (IoE): IoE is the concept that describes the network of physical objects, people, and processes that are connected and communicate with each other through the internet. Open frameworks can be used to create IoE applications that use data, analytics, cloud computing, and artificial intelligence to create smart and efficient solutions.
