## Unit 3 - CYBERNETICS AND HUMANISTIC INTELLIGENCE

- Cybernetics is the science of human-machine interaction that employs the principles of feedback, control and communication .
- Humanistic intelligence is the intelligence that arises because of a human being in the feedback loop of a computational process, where the human and computer are inextricably intertwined.
- Cybernetics and humanistic intelligence are related fields that explore the possibilities and implications of human-machine integration and augmentation.
- Some of the topics covered in this unit are:

  - The history and development of cybernetics and its applications in various domains such as biology, engineering, psychology, sociology, etc.
  - The concept of feedback and its role in regulating and adapting systems to achieve desired goals or outcomes.
  - The concept of control and its types, such as open-loop, closed-loop, feedforward, feedback, etc.
  - The concept of communication and its modes, such as analog, digital, verbal, non-verbal, etc.
  - The concept of information and its measures, such as entropy, redundancy, noise, etc.
  - The concept of computation and its models, such as Turing machines, cellular automata, neural networks, etc.
  - The concept of artificial intelligence and its branches, such as machine learning, natural language processing, computer vision, etc.
  - The concept of humanistic intelligence and its characteristics, such as embodiment, situatedness, sociality, creativity, etc.
  - The concept of wearable computing and its devices, such as smart glasses, smart watches, smart clothing, etc.
  - The concept of posthumanism and its visions, such as transhumanism, cyborgism, singularity, etc.
  - The ethical, social, and philosophical issues and challenges of human-machine interaction and integration.
