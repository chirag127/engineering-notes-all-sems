### Arm Devices

- Arm devices are wearable computing devices that use Arm processors to provide low-power, high-performance and secure computing capabilities.
- Arm processors are based on a reduced instruction set computer (RISC) architecture that enables efficient and flexible processing for a wide range of applications.
- Arm devices can be used for augmented reality (AR), virtual reality (VR), mixed reality (MR) and other metaverse experiences, as well as for fitness tracking, health monitoring, smart clothing and other wearable applications.
- Arm devices can leverage the Mbed development platform, which allows device makers to mix and match components, connectivity options and operating systems, and to access a large ecosystem of software partners.
- Some examples of Arm devices are Google Glass, Samsung's smartwatches, Oculus Quest 2, Magic Leap One and NXP's wearable solutions.
