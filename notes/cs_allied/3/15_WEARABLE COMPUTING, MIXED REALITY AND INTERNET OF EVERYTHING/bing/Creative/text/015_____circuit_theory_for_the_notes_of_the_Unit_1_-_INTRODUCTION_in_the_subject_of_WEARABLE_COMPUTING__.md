### Circuit Theory for the Notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the Subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

- Circuit theory is the study of the behavior of electrical circuits, which are composed of components such as resistors, capacitors, inductors, diodes, transistors, and sources of voltage and current.
- Circuit theory can be used to analyze and design various types of circuits, such as analog, digital, linear, nonlinear, active, passive, AC, DC, and mixed-signal circuits.
- Circuit theory can also be applied to the fields of wearable computing, mixed reality, and internet of everything, which are emerging technologies that integrate computing, communication, and sensing into everyday objects, environments, and experiences.
- Wearable computing refers to the devices and systems that can be worn on the body or embedded in clothing, such as smart watches, fitness trackers, smart glasses, and wearable cameras. Wearable computing can provide various functions, such as health monitoring, personal assistance, entertainment, and social interaction.
- Mixed reality refers to the combination of real and virtual worlds, where physical and digital objects coexist and interact in real time. Mixed reality can be achieved by using devices such as head-mounted displays, augmented reality glasses, projectors, and cameras. Mixed reality can create immersive and interactive experiences, such as gaming, education, training, and tourism.
- Internet of everything refers to the network of physical and virtual objects that are connected and exchange data through the internet. Internet of everything can include people, processes, data, and things, such as sensors, actuators, devices, machines, and systems. Internet of everything can enable new capabilities, richer experiences, and unprecedented economic opportunities, such as smart cities, smart homes, smart agriculture, and smart industry.
