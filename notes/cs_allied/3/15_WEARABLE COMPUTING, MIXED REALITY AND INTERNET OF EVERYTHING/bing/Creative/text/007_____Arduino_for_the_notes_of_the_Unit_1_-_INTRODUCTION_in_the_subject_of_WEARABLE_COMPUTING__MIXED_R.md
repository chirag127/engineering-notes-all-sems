### Arduino

- Arduino is an open-source electronics platform based on easy-to-use hardware and software .
- Arduino boards are able to read inputs from sensors, buttons, or online sources, and turn them into outputs such as activating motors, LEDs, or publishing data online.
- Arduino consists of both a physical programmable circuit board (often referred to as a microcontroller) and a piece of software, or IDE (Integrated Development Environment) that runs on your computer, used to write and upload computer code to the physical board.
- Arduino is a company, a project, and a user community that designs and manufactures single-board microcontrollers and microcontroller kits for building digital devices.
- Arduino is compatible with a wide range of sensors, actuators, displays, communication modules, and other components that can be used to create interactive and wearable projects .
- Arduino is based on the C/C++ programming language, and provides a simple and intuitive syntax for writing code .
- Arduino software is open-source and can be downloaded for free from the official website . The software can be used with any Arduino board, and supports multiple operating systems.
- Arduino hardware is also open-source and can be modified, extended, or customized according to the user's needs . There are many types of Arduino boards, each with different features and capabilities.
- Arduino is a popular and widely used platform for hobbyists, educators, students, artists, designers, and makers who want to create interactive and innovative projects with electronics  .
- Arduino is also a part of the larger maker movement, which encourages people to learn, share, and collaborate on DIY projects that involve technology, creativity, and problem-solving .
