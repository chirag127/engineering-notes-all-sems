### Parts identification for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

- Wearable computing: A branch of computing that involves devices that can be worn on the body, such as smartwatches, fitness trackers, smart glasses, and virtual reality headsets. These devices can collect and analyse data, interact with other devices and the internet, and provide various functions and services to the user. 
- Mixed reality: A type of immersive technology that combines elements of augmented reality and virtual reality, creating a hybrid environment where digital and physical objects can coexist and interact. Mixed reality devices, such as HoloLens and Magic Leap, use spatial computing to project digital content into the user's physical space, allowing them to manipulate and interact with it.
- Internet of everything: A concept that extends the internet of things (IoT) to include not only devices and things, but also people, data, and processes. The internet of everything aims to provide general intelligence and improved cognition across the networked environment, enabling new applications and opportunities in various domains, such as health, education, security, and entertainment.
