### Introduction for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

- In this unit, we will learn about the concepts, applications and challenges of wearable computing, mixed reality and internet of everything.
- Wearable computing is the field of computer science that deals with the design, development and use of devices that can be worn on the body, such as smartwatches, fitness trackers, smart glasses, etc.
- Mixed reality is the field of computer science that deals with the integration of real and virtual environments, such as augmented reality, virtual reality, etc.
- Internet of everything is the field of computer science that deals with the interconnection of people, processes, data and things through the internet, such as smart homes, smart cities, smart grids, etc.
- These fields are related to each other and have many potential benefits and challenges for various domains, such as health, education, entertainment, security, etc.
- In this unit, we will cover the following topics:

  - The history and evolution of wearable computing, mixed reality and internet of everything
  - The types and characteristics of wearable devices, mixed reality devices and internet of everything devices
  - The applications and use cases of wearable computing, mixed reality and internet of everything in different domains
  - The design principles and guidelines for wearable computing, mixed reality and internet of everything
  - The technical and social challenges and issues of wearable computing, mixed reality and internet of everything
  - The future trends and opportunities of wearable computing, mixed reality and internet of everything
