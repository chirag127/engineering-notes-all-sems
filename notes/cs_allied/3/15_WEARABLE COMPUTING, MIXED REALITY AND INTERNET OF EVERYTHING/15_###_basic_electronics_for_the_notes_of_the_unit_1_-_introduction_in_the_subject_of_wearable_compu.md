### Basic Electronics for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

Basic electronics is the study of electronic components and circuits and their applications in wearable computing, mixed reality, and Internet of Things (IoT) systems. It covers the fundamental principles of electronics, including voltage, current, resistance, and capacitance, and it provides a foundation for understanding how electronic components and circuits can be used to create wearable computing devices, mixed reality experiences, and IoT systems.

Components of Basic Electronics:

1. Electronic Components: Electronic components are the building blocks of electronic circuits. Examples of electronic components include resistors, capacitors, diodes, and transistors.

2. Circuits: Circuits are arrangements of electronic components that are used to perform specific functions, such as amplifying signals, filtering signals, and regulating voltage.

Applications of Basic Electronics in Wearable Computing, Mixed Reality, and IoE:

1. Wearable Computing: Basic electronics is used in the development of wearable computing devices, such as smartwatches and fitness trackers, to create electronic circuits and components that perform specific functions, such as monitoring heart rate, tracking movement, and displaying information.

2. Mixed Reality: Basic electronics is used in the development of mixed reality experiences, such as augmented reality and virtual reality, to create electronic circuits and components that interact with the physical world and provide a seamless user experience.

3. Internet of Things (IoT): Basic electronics is used in the development of IoT systems, such as smart home systems and industrial control systems, to create electronic circuits and components that collect and transmit data over the internet.

In conclusion, basic electronics is the study of electronic components and circuits and their applications in wearable computing, mixed reality, and Internet of Things (IoT) systems. It provides a foundation for understanding how electronic components and circuits can be used to create wearable computing devices, mixed reality experiences, and IoT systems, and it is a critical component of the development of these systems.
