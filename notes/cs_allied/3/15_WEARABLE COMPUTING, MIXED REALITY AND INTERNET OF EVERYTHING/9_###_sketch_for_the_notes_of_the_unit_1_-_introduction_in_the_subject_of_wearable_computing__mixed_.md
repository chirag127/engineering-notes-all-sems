### Sketch for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

Sketch is a vector graphics editing software that is widely used for creating and editing graphics, illustrations, and designs. It is a popular tool for creating user interfaces, icons, and other graphical elements for wearable computing, mixed reality, and IoT applications.

The following are some of the key features of Sketch:

1. Vector graphics editing: Sketch allows for the creation and editing of vector graphics, which are scalable and can be resized without losing quality.

2. User interface design: Sketch is widely used for designing user interfaces for wearable computing and IoT devices, and includes features such as grid and alignment tools, symbol libraries, and the ability to create reusable components.

3. Collaboration and sharing: Sketch includes features for collaboration and sharing, including the ability to share designs with others and work on designs together in real-time.

4. Plugins and integrations: Sketch has a large community of developers who have created a wide range of plugins and integrations, including integrations with other design tools and services, and plugins for specific tasks such as exporting graphics for use in web and mobile applications.

Overall, Sketch is a powerful and flexible tool for creating and editing graphics and designs for wearable computing, mixed reality, and IoT applications. Its ease of use, wide range of features, and large community of developers make it an ideal tool for designers, developers, and other professionals working in these fields.
