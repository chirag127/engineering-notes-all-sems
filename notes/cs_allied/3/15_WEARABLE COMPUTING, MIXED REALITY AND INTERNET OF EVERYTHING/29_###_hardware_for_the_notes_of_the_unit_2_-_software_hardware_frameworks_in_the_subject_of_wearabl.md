### Hardware for the notes of the Unit 2 - SOFTWARE HARDWARE FRAMEWORKS in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

Hardware is a critical component of wearable computing, mixed reality, and the Internet of Everything (IoE). It involves the physical components and devices that make up wearable devices, mixed reality systems, and IoE devices.

In Unit 2 - Software Hardware Frameworks in the subject of Wearable Computing, Mixed Reality and Internet of Everything, students will learn about the different types of hardware that are used in wearable devices, mixed reality systems, and IoE devices, including:

1. Sensors: Sensors are used to collect data from the environment and to provide input to wearable devices and mixed reality systems. Sensors can include accelerometers, gyroscopes, magnetometers, and other types of sensors.

2. Actuators: Actuators are used to control and manipulate the environment. Actuators can include motors, solenoids, and other types of actuators.

3. Microcontrollers: Microcontrollers are small, low-power computers that are used to control and automate wearable devices and mixed reality systems. They can include microprocessors, microcomputers, and other types of microcontrollers.

4. Connectivity: Connectivity refers to the ability of wearable devices and mixed reality systems to communicate and exchange data with other devices and systems. Connectivity can include Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, and other types of connectivity.

5. Power: Power refers to the ability of wearable devices and mixed reality systems to operate and function. Power can include batteries, power supplies, and other types of power sources.

By understanding the different types of hardware that are used in wearable devices, mixed reality systems, and IoE devices, students can gain a deeper appreciation for the technology and its impact on wearable computing, mixed reality, and the Internet of Everything. This understanding can help students to develop the skills necessary to design, build, and maintain wearable devices, mixed reality systems, and IoE devices.
