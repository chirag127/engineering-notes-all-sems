### History for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

Wearable computing, mixed reality, and the Internet of Everything (IoE) are rapidly evolving fields that are changing the way we interact with technology. The history of these fields can be traced back to the early days of computing and the development of wearable technology.

1. Wearable Computing: The concept of wearable computing can be traced back to the 1960s, with the development of the first wearable computer, the "Memory Augmentation System." This system was used to store and retrieve information, and it was worn on a belt.

2. Mixed Reality: The concept of mixed reality, which combines the physical and digital worlds, can be traced back to the 1990s, with the development of virtual reality and augmented reality technologies.

3. Internet of Everything (IoE): The concept of the IoE, which involves the integration of physical devices with the internet, can be traced back to the early days of the internet and the development of the World Wide Web.

In conclusion, the history of wearable computing, mixed reality, and the Internet of Everything (IoE) can be traced back to the early days of computing and the development of wearable technology, virtual reality, and the internet. These fields have evolved rapidly over the years, and they are changing the way we interact with technology and the world around us.
