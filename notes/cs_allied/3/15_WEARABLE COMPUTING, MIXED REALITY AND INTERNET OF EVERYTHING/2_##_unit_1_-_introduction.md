## Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION

Unit 1 - Introduction is the first unit in a course or subject, and is typically used to provide an overview of the course content, objectives, and structure. This unit is usually designed to provide students with a basic understanding of the subject matter and to help them become familiar with the course materials, resources, and expectations.

The introduction unit typically covers the following topics:

1. Overview of the course: This section provides a brief overview of the course content, objectives, and structure.

2. Course objectives: This section outlines the specific learning outcomes that students are expected to achieve by the end of the course.

3. Course structure: This section provides an overview of the course structure, including the number of units, the topics covered in each unit, and the expected workload.

4. Course resources: This section provides information about the course materials, resources, and tools that students will need to complete the course, including textbooks, online resources, and software.

5. Course expectations: This section outlines the expectations for student participation, engagement, and performance, including attendance, assignments, and exams.

The introduction unit is an important component of the course, as it provides students with the information and resources they need to succeed in the course. By understanding the course objectives, structure, resources, and expectations, students can develop a clear understanding of what is expected of them and can plan their time and effort accordingly.
