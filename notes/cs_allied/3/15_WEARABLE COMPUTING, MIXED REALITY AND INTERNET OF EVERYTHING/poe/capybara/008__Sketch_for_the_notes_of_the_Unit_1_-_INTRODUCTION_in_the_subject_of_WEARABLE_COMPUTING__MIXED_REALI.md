### Sketch for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING.

Wearable Computing:

- Wearable computing is a technology that allows users to wear electronic devices on their bodies as accessories or implants.
- It is a form of ubiquitous computing, which means that the technology is integrated into the user's environment and is always accessible.
- Wearable computing devices can be used for a variety of purposes, from monitoring health and fitness to accessing information and controlling other devices.

Mixed Reality:

- Mixed reality is a technology that blends the physical world with virtual elements.
- It can be achieved through the use of devices such as smart glasses or head-mounted displays.
- Mixed reality can be used for a variety of applications, from gaming and entertainment to education and training.

Internet of Everything:

- The Internet of Everything is a concept that refers to the integration of everyday objects with the internet and other networks.
- It involves the use of sensors, software, and other technologies to connect objects to the internet and enable them to interact with each other.
- The Internet of Everything has the potential to revolutionize industries such as healthcare, transportation, and manufacturing.

Conclusion:

- Wearable computing, mixed reality, and the Internet of Everything are all emerging technologies that have the potential to transform the way we interact with the world around us.
- Understanding these technologies is essential for anyone who wants to stay up-to-date with the latest developments in the field of technology.
- In the following units, we will delve deeper into these topics and explore their applications in various industries.
