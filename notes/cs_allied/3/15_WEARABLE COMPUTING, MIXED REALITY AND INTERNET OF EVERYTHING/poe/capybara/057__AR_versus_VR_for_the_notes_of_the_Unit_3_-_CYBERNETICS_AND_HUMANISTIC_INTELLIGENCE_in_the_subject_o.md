### AR versus VR

When it comes to wearable computing, mixed reality, and the internet of everything, two of the most popular technologies that come to mind are Augmented Reality (AR) and Virtual Reality (VR). Both technologies have their own unique features and advantages. In this section, we will discuss the differences between AR and VR.

#### Augmented Reality (AR)

Augmented reality is a technology that overlays digital content onto the real world. It enhances the user's perception of the real world by adding digital elements to it. Here are some key points about AR:

- AR technology uses the camera on a device to capture the real world and add digital objects to it.
- AR enhances the user's perception of reality by adding digital objects to the real world.
- AR can be used in various fields such as gaming, education, healthcare, and retail.

#### Virtual Reality (VR)

Virtual reality is a technology that creates a simulated environment that the user can interact with. It immerses the user in a digital world, blocking out the real world. Here are some key points about VR:

- VR technology uses a headset to create a simulated environment that the user can interact with.
- VR creates a fully immersive experience that blocks out the real world.
- VR can be used in various fields such as gaming, education, healthcare, and military training.

#### Differences between AR and VR

Although AR and VR are both immersive technologies, they have some key differences. Here are some of the main differences between AR and VR:

- AR enhances the real world, while VR creates a new world.
- AR allows the user to interact with the real world, while VR creates a closed environment.
- AR is more practical for everyday use, while VR is more immersive for entertainment and gaming.

In conclusion, AR and VR are both powerful technologies that have their own unique features and advantages. Understanding the differences between AR and VR can help you choose the right technology for your needs.
