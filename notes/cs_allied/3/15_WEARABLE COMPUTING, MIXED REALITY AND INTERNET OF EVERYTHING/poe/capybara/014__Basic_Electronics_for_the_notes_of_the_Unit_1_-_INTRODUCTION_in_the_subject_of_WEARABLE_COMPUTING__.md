### Basic Electronics

In this unit, we will be discussing the basics of electronics that are essential to understanding the field of wearable computing, mixed reality, and Internet of Everything. Here are some important points to keep in mind:

- Electronics is the study of the behavior and movement of electrons in different materials.
- The basic components of electronics are resistors, capacitors, inductors, diodes, transistors, and integrated circuits (ICs).
- Resistors are used to regulate the flow of current in a circuit. They are measured in ohms (Ω).
- Capacitors store electric charge and are measured in farads (F).
- Inductors store energy in a magnetic field and are measured in henries (H).
- Diodes allow current to flow in only one direction.
- Transistors are used to amplify and switch electronic signals.
- ICs are complex circuits that contain multiple electronic components on a single chip.
- The most common type of IC is the microcontroller, which is used to control the behavior of electronic devices.
- Electronic circuits can be designed using a software program called a circuit simulator.
- Understanding the basics of electronics is essential to designing and building wearable computing and mixed reality devices.
- In addition to hardware components, software programming is also an important part of building electronic devices.

By mastering the fundamentals of electronics, you will be better equipped to design and build innovative devices that incorporate wearable computing, mixed reality, and Internet of Everything technologies.
