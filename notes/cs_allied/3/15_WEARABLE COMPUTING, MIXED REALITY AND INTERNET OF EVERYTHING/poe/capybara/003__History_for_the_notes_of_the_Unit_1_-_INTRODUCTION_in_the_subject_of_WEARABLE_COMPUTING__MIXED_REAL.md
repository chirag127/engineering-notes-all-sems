### History

- Wearable computing has its roots in the 1960s, with the invention of the first wearable computer by Edward Thorp and Claude Shannon. 
- The first commercial wearable device was the calculator watch, which was introduced in the 1970s. 
- In the 1980s, the first wearable computer for military use was developed by Steve Mann, who is often considered the father of wearable computing. 
- In the 1990s, wearable technology began to gain popularity with the introduction of devices such as the wristwatch pager and the first smartwatches. 
- The early 2000s saw the introduction of the first fitness trackers, such as the Fitbit, which allowed users to track their physical activity and monitor their health. 
- The 2010s saw a rapid growth in the wearable technology industry, with the introduction of smart glasses, virtual reality headsets, and smart clothing. 
- Today, the wearable computing industry continues to grow, with new and innovative devices being developed for a wide range of applications, from healthcare to entertainment.
