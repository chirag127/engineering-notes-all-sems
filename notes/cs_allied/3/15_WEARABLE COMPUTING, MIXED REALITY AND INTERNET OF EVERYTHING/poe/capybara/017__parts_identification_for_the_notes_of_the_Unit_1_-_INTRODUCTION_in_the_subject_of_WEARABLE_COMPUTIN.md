### Parts Identification for the Notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the Subject of Wearable Computing, Mixed Reality and Internet of Everything

Wearable computing, mixed reality, and the Internet of Everything (IoE) are rapidly growing fields that are changing the way we interact with technology. To better understand and work with these technologies, it is important to be familiar with the components and parts that make them work. Here are some key parts to know:

1. **Sensors:** These are devices that detect and respond to physical inputs or changes in the environment, such as motion, temperature, or light. Sensors are used in wearable devices to collect data and provide feedback to the user.

2. **Microcontrollers:** These are small computers that control the functions of a device. In wearable devices, microcontrollers are used to process sensor data and execute commands.

3. **Wireless Communication Modules:** These are devices that enable wireless communication between wearable devices and other devices, such as smartphones and computers. Bluetooth and Wi-Fi are common wireless communication standards used in wearables.

4. **Display Technologies:** These are technologies that enable visual feedback to the user. Wearables may use OLED, LCD, or other display technologies to provide information to the user.

5. **Power Management Systems:** These are systems that manage the power supply of a device. Wearable devices often have limited battery life, so power management systems are crucial to ensure that the device operates efficiently and conserves power.

6. **User Interfaces:** These are the means by which a user interacts with a device. Wearable devices may use touchscreens, voice commands, or gesture recognition as user interfaces.

7. **Cloud Computing:** This is a technology that enables data storage, processing, and access over the internet. Wearable devices may use cloud computing to store data, perform complex computations, or access external services.

By understanding these parts and their functions, you can better understand and work with wearable computing, mixed reality, and the Internet of Everything.
