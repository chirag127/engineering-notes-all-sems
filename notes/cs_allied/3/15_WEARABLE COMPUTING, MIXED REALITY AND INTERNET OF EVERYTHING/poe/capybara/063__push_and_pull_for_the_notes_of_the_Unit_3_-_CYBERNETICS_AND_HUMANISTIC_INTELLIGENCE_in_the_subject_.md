### Push and Pull for the Notes of the Unit 3 - Cybernetics and Humanistic Intelligence in the Subject of Wearable Computing, Mixed Reality and Internet of Everything

In this unit, we will explore the concepts of cybernetics and humanistic intelligence and how they relate to wearable computing, mixed reality, and the internet of everything. Below are some key points to keep in mind:

- Cybernetics is the study of communication and control in living organisms and machines. It involves the feedback loop, which is a process where information is sent back to the source for analysis and adjustment. Cybernetics is important in wearable computing and mixed reality because it helps to create systems that can adapt and learn from user feedback.

- Humanistic intelligence is the ability of machines to understand and respond to human emotions, intentions, and values. This is important in wearable computing and mixed reality because it allows for more natural and intuitive interactions between humans and machines.

- Push technology is a method of delivering information to users without them requesting it. This is commonly used in wearable computing and mixed reality to provide users with notifications and updates without them having to actively seek out the information.

- Pull technology is a method of allowing users to request information when they need it. This is important in wearable computing and mixed reality because it allows users to access specific information and services when they need them.

- The internet of everything refers to the idea of connecting everything in the world to the internet, including people, devices, and machines. This is important in wearable computing and mixed reality because it allows for seamless communication and interaction between different devices and systems.

- Overall, understanding the concepts of cybernetics and humanistic intelligence, as well as the use of push and pull technology and the internet of everything, is crucial in developing effective and user-friendly wearable computing and mixed reality systems.
