
### Smart Cities and Wearable Computing as a Form of Urban Design

1. Smart cities are cities that use technology to improve the quality of life for citizens and increase efficiency of city operations. Wearable computing is a form of technology that can be used in smart cities to improve urban design.

2. Wearable computing devices are devices that are worn on the body and can connect to the Internet. They can be used to collect data about the environment and the people in it, and then use this data to inform decisions about urban design.

3. Wearable computing can be used to monitor and analyze the behavior of people in a city, such as where they go, how they move, and what they do. This data can be used to optimize urban design, such as where to place public facilities and how to design public spaces.

4. Wearable computing can also be used to create interactive experiences in the city. For example, it can be used to create augmented reality experiences that overlay digital information onto physical spaces. This can be used to create interactive public art, or to provide information about the city’s history and culture.

5. Finally, wearable computing can be used to provide citizens with access to services and information. For example, it can be used to provide citizens with real-time information about traffic and public transportation, or to provide access to services such as healthcare and education.

6. Wearable computing is an important tool for creating smart cities and improving urban design. It can be used to collect data, create interactive experiences, and provide citizens with access to services and information.
