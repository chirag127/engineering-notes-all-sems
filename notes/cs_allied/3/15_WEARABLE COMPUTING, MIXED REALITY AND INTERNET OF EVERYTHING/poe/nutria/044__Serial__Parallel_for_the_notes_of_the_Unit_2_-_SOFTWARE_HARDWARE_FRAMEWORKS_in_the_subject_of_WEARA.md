

### Serial & Parallel for the notes of the Unit 2 - SOFTWARE HARDWARE FRAMEWORKS in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

* Serial: Serial computing is a type of computing architecture that processes data in a linear fashion. It involves a single processing unit, such as a CPU, to process data sequentially. This type of computing is used in many applications such as embedded systems, robotics and automation.

* Parallel: Parallel computing is a type of computing architecture that processes data in parallel. It involves multiple processing units, such as multiple CPUs or GPUs, to process data simultaneously. This type of computing is used in many applications such as scientific computing, data analytics, and artificial intelligence.
