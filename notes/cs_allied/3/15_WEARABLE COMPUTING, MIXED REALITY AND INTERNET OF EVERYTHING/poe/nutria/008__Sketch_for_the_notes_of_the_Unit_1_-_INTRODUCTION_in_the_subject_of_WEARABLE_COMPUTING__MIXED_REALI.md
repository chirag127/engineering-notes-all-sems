
### Sketch for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

1. Wearable Computing: Wearable computing is a technology that enables the user to interact with the digital environment through the use of a device that is worn on the body. It is a form of technology that is designed to be worn on the body and used for a variety of tasks. Examples of wearable computing devices include smart watches, fitness trackers, augmented reality glasses, and virtual reality headsets.

2. Mixed Reality: Mixed reality (MR) is a combination of virtual reality (VR) and augmented reality (AR). MR combines the physical and digital worlds by blending real-world objects with virtual objects in a single environment. This technology has the potential to revolutionize the way we interact with digital content and the environment around us.

3. Internet of Everything (IoE): The Internet of Everything (IoE) is the concept of connecting all physical and digital objects to the internet. This includes everyday objects such as cars, appliances, and buildings, as well as digital objects such as websites and mobile apps. The IoE has the potential to revolutionize how we interact with the world around us, and how we use data to make decisions.
