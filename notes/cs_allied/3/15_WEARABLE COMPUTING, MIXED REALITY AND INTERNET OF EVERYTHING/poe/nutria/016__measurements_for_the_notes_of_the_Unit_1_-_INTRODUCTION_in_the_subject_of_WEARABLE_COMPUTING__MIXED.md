
### Measurements for the Notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction in the Subject of Wearable Computing, Mixed Reality and Internet of Everything

1. Wearable computing is a relatively new technology that enables people to interact with computers in a more natural and intuitive way.

2. Wearable computing devices can be worn on the body or even embedded within clothing and accessories.

3. Mixed reality (MR) is a technology that combines the physical world with the digital world, allowing users to interact with both real and virtual objects.

4. The Internet of Everything (IoE) is a concept that describes the connection of people, processes, data, and things to enable real-time communication and collaboration.

5. Wearable computing, mixed reality, and IoE can be used in a variety of applications, including healthcare, entertainment, and education.
