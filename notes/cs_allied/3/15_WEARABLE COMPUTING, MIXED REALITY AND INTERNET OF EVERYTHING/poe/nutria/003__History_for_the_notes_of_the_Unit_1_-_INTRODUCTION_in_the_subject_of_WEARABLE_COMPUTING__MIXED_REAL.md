
### History for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

1. Wearable computing has been around since the late 1970s when the first wearable computers were developed by the US military for use in the battlefield. 
2. Since then, the technology has been used in a variety of applications, from medical to entertainment. 
3. In the early 2000s, the development of smartphones and other mobile devices paved the way for the development of wearable computing. 
4. In the 2010s, the introduction of the Internet of Things (IoT) and the development of augmented reality (AR) and virtual reality (VR) technologies further boosted the development of wearable computing. 
5. In the 2020s, the introduction of 5G networks and the development of artificial intelligence (AI) technologies have enabled the development of smart wearables, such as smartwatches and smart glasses. 
6. These devices are increasingly used in a variety of applications, such as health and fitness tracking, navigation, and gaming. 
7. The development of Mixed Reality (MR) technologies is also enabling the development of more advanced wearable computing applications. 
8. With the introduction of 5G networks, the development of AI technologies, and the emergence of MR, the future of wearable computing is expected to be even more exciting.
