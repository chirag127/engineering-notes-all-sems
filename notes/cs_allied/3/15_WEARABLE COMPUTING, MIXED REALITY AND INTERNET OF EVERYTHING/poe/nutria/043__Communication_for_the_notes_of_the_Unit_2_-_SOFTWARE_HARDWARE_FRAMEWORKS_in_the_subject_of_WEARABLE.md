
### Communication for the notes of the Unit 2 - SOFTWARE HARDWARE FRAMEWORKS in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

- Communication is an essential part of Wearable Computing, Mixed Reality, and the Internet of Everything. 
- It enables devices to interact with each other and to exchange data and information. 
- To enable communication, a variety of software and hardware frameworks are used. 
- These frameworks include Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, and Near Field Communication (NFC). 
- Bluetooth is a wireless technology used for short-range communication. It is used for exchanging data between two or more devices over a short distance. 
- Wi-Fi is a wireless networking technology used for connecting devices to the Internet. It is used for exchanging data between two or more devices over a long distance. 
- Near Field Communication (NFC) is a short-range wireless technology used for exchanging data between two or more devices over a short distance. It is used for exchanging data between two or more devices over a short distance. 
- All of these frameworks are used to enable communication between Wearable Computing, Mixed Reality, and the Internet of Everything devices.
