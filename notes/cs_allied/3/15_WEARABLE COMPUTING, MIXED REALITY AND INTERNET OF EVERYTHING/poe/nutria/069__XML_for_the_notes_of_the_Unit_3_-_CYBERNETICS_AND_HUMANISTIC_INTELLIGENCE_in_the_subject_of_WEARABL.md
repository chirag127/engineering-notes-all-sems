- XML (eXtensible Markup Language) is a markup language used to describe structured data. It is used to store and transport data, and is often used in web development and software applications.
- XML is a human-readable language, which makes it easier to understand and interpret.
- XML documents are organized into elements, which are composed of tags, attributes, and values.
- XML documents can be validated, meaning that they are checked to make sure they conform to a set of rules. This can help ensure that the data is accurate and consistent.
- XML is used in a variety of applications, including web services, databases, and document management systems.
- XML is often used in conjunction with other technologies, such as XSLT, which is used to transform XML documents into other formats.
- Cybernetics is the study of communication and control systems in both living and non-living systems. It is an interdisciplinary field that studies the structure, behavior, and interactions of complex systems.
- Cybernetics is used to study how humans and machines interact, and how machines can be used to control or monitor processes.
