
## Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION

1. Introduction is a part of any study material, which provides an overview of the topics to be discussed.
2. It helps the reader to understand the context of the material and to make connections between the different topics.
3. It also assists in setting the tone for the rest of the material and provides a basic understanding of the content.
4. Introduction should be concise, clear and to the point. It should provide an overview of the material without going into too much detail.
5. Introduction should also include any relevant background information, such as the purpose of the material and the intended audience.
