
### I2C/IIC

I2C (Inter-Integrated Circuit) and IIC (I2C compatible) are two-wire serial communication protocols used for connecting electronic devices. They are widely used for connecting microcontrollers, sensors, and other electronic components.

I2C is a multi-master protocol, meaning that multiple devices can be connected to the same bus and communicate with each other. It is also a synchronous protocol, meaning that data is transferred in synchronous cycles.

I2C is a low-speed protocol, with a maximum speed of 400 kHz. It is also a low-power protocol, making it ideal for battery-powered devices.

I2C is used in a variety of applications, including:

* Wearable computing
* Mixed reality
* Internet of Things
* Automotive systems
* Industrial automation
* Home automation
* Robotics
* Medical devices
* Security systems
* Smartphones and tablets
* Wearable fitness trackers
