
### SQLite for the notes of the Unit 3 - CYBERNETICS AND HUMANISTIC INTELLIGENCE in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

* SQLite is an open-source, relational database management system (RDBMS) used for storing and retrieving data from a database.
* It is lightweight, compact and self-contained, making it ideal for embedded applications such as those used in wearable computing, mixed reality, and the Internet of Things (IoT).
* SQLite is written in the C programming language and is designed to be fast and efficient, with minimal resource requirements.
* SQLite supports a wide range of data types, including text, numbers, dates, and binary data.
* It also supports transactions, allowing multiple operations to be grouped together and either committed or rolled back as a single unit.
* SQLite is highly secure, with support for encryption, access control, and user authentication.
* The SQLite library is available for free, making it an attractive choice for developers who need a reliable and powerful database solution.
