
### PIC for the Notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction in the Subject of Wearable Computing, Mixed Reality and Internet of Everything

1. Wearable computing refers to the use of computers and technology that can be worn or carried by a user. This includes items such as smartwatches, fitness trackers, augmented reality headsets, and virtual reality goggles.
2. Mixed reality combines aspects of augmented reality and virtual reality. It is a technology that allows users to interact with digital content in a physical environment.
3. The Internet of Everything (IoE) is a term used to describe the interconnection of people, processes, data, and things. It is an interconnected system of devices, networks, and services that are able to communicate with each other.
4. Wearable computing, mixed reality, and the Internet of Everything are all related technologies that are becoming increasingly popular. They are being used in a variety of applications, from healthcare and entertainment to education and business.
5. Wearable computing and mixed reality are important components of the Internet of Everything. They allow users to interact with digital content in a physical environment, and enable the integration of data and services across multiple devices.
6. The Internet of Everything has the potential to revolutionize the way we interact with technology and the world around us. It can provide us with unprecedented levels of convenience, efficiency, and insight into our lives.
