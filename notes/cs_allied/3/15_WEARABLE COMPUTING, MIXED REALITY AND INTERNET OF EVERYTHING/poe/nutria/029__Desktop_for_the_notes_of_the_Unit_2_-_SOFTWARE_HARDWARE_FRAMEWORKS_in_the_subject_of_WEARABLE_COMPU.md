
### Desktop for the notes of the Unit 2 - SOFTWARE HARDWARE FRAMEWORKS in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

1. Software: Software is a set of instructions that tells a computer how to perform a task. It includes programs, applications, operating systems, and other components that enable computer systems to function.

2. Hardware: Hardware is the physical components of a computer system, including the motherboard, processor, memory, storage, and other components.

3. Frameworks: Frameworks are a set of tools and libraries that provide the basic structure for building an application. Examples of frameworks include Java, .NET, and Node.js.

4. Wearable Computing: Wearable computing is the use of computers and other electronic devices that are worn on the body. Examples of wearable computing devices include smartwatches, fitness trackers, and augmented reality glasses.

5. Mixed Reality: Mixed reality is the combination of virtual reality and augmented reality. It allows users to interact with virtual objects in a real-world environment.

6. Internet of Everything: The Internet of Everything is the connection of people, processes, data, and things to enable new levels of intelligence and efficiency. It includes the Internet of Things, cloud computing, artificial intelligence, and other technologies.
