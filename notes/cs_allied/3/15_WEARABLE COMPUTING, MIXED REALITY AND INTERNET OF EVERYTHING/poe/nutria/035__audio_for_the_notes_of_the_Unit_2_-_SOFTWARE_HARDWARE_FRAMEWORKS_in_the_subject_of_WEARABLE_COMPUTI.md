
### Audio for the Notes of the Unit 2 - Software Hardware Frameworks in the Subject of Wearable Computing, Mixed Reality and Internet of Everything

* Audio is the output of sound produced by a device, such as a computer or a smartphone. It is used to communicate information, provide entertainment, and create aural experiences. 
* Audio hardware components are the physical components that make up audio systems, such as speakers, microphones, amplifiers, and audio interfaces. 
* Audio software is the software used to create, manipulate, and store sound. Examples include audio editing software, digital audio workstations, and sound synthesis software. 
* Hardware frameworks are the physical components that make up a computing system, such as processors, memory, and storage. 
* Wearable computing is the use of computers that are worn on the body, such as smartwatches, fitness trackers, and augmented reality glasses. 
* Mixed reality is the combination of virtual reality and augmented reality, which allows users to interact with virtual objects in the real world. 
* The Internet of Everything is the concept of connecting people, processes, data, and things to create a network of interconnected systems.
