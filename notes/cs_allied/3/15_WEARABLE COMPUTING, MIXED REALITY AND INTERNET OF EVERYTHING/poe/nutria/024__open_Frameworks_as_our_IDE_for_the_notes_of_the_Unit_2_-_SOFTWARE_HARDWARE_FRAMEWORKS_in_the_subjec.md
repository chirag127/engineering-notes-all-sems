
### Open Frameworks as our IDE for the notes of the Unit 2 - SOFTWARE HARDWARE FRAMEWORKS in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

* Open Frameworks is an open source C++ library for creative coding. It is designed to facilitate the development of interactive applications and computer graphics. 
* It provides a wide range of features, such as an OpenGL wrapper, audio and video playback and recording, 3D graphics, and much more.
* It is cross-platform and supports a wide range of operating systems, including Windows, Mac OS X, Linux, iOS, and Android.
* It also includes a wide range of add-ons, such as OpenCV, OpenAL, and Bullet Physics.
* It is designed to be easy to use and to provide a flexible platform for creative coding.
