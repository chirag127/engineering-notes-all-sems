
### Basic Electronics for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

1. **Electricity and Electronics**: Electricity is the flow of electrical energy through a conductive material, such as a wire or circuit board. Electronics is the science and technology of controlling the flow of electrical energy through components such as resistors, capacitors, transistors, and integrated circuits. 

2. **Electronic Components**: Electronic components are devices that control the flow of electricity and are used to build circuits. Common components include resistors, capacitors, transistors, diodes, and integrated circuits. 

3. **Circuit Design**: Circuit design is the process of designing a circuit board or integrated circuit to perform a specific function. It involves selecting the appropriate components, connecting them together, and verifying that the circuit works as intended. 

4. **Electronic Prototyping**: Electronic prototyping is the process of creating a physical prototype of an electronic device. This is typically done by creating a circuit board and testing it to ensure that it works as expected. 

5. **Power Sources**: Power sources are devices that provide electrical energy to a circuit. Common power sources include batteries, solar cells, and AC/DC adapters. 

6. **Safety Precautions**: When working with electronics, it's important to take safety precautions to protect yourself and the components. This includes using proper tools, wearing safety goggles, and avoiding contact with exposed wires.
