
### Arduino

Arduino is an open-source electronics platform used for creating interactive objects and devices. It is widely used in wearable computing, mixed reality, and the Internet of Things (IoT). It can be programmed to read and write data from various inputs and outputs, such as sensors, motors, and displays.

Arduino boards consist of a microcontroller, a set of input/output pins, and a USB port for connecting to a computer. The microcontroller can be programmed using the Arduino IDE, a software development environment. Arduino code is written in C/C++ and is compiled and uploaded to the board.

Arduino boards are used in a variety of applications, including robotics, home automation, and consumer electronics. They are also popular in educational settings, allowing students to create projects that interact with the physical world.
