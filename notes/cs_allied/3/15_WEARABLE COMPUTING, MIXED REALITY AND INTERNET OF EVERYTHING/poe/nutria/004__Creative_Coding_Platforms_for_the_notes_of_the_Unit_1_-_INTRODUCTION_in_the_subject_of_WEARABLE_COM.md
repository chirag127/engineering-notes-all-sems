
### Creative Coding Platforms for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

* Creative coding platforms are software tools and applications that allow users to create digital content such as animations, interactive experiences, and games. 
* These platforms are used in various fields such as game development, media production, and interactive art. 
* Popular platforms include Unity, Unreal Engine, and Three.js.
* Unity is a powerful game development engine that allows users to create 3D and 2D games, simulations, and interactive experiences. 
* Unreal Engine is a game engine that is used to create high-quality 3D and virtual reality experiences. 
* Three.js is a JavaScript library that is used to create interactive 3D and 2D graphics in web browsers.
* Wearable computing, mixed reality, and the Internet of Everything are all areas of technology that are enabled by creative coding platforms. 
* Wearable computing is the use of computers and technology that can be worn on the body. 
* Mixed reality is the combining of the physical and digital worlds, allowing for the creation of immersive experiences. 
* The Internet of Everything is the interconnection of physical and digital devices, allowing for the exchange of data and information. 
* Creative coding platforms allow developers to create experiences that take advantage of these technologies, allowing for new and innovative applications.
