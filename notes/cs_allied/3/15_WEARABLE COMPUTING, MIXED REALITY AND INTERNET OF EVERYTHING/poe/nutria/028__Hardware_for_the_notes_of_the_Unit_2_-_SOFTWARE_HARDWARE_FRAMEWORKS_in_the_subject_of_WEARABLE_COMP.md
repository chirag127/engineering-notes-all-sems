
### Hardware for the Notes of the Unit 2 - SOFTWARE HARDWARE FRAMEWORKS in the Subject of Wearable Computing, Mixed Reality, and Internet of Everything

1. Wearable computing devices are hardware devices that are designed to be worn on the body. Examples include smartwatches, fitness trackers and augmented reality glasses.
2. Mixed reality is a technology that combines the physical and virtual worlds. It allows users to interact with digital objects in the real world and vice versa.
3. The Internet of Everything (IoE) is a concept that refers to the connection of physical objects, people, and data to the internet. It is a network of physical objects that are connected to the internet and can communicate with each other.
4. Software and hardware frameworks are the tools and technologies used to develop software and hardware applications. Examples include operating systems, programming languages, and development frameworks. 
5. Hardware components are the physical parts of a computer system, such as the processor, memory, storage, and input/output devices. 
6. Networking components are the hardware components used to connect computers and other devices to each other. Examples include routers, switches, and cables. 
7. Wearable computing applications are applications designed to run on wearable computing devices. Examples include health and fitness tracking apps, navigation apps, and augmented reality apps.
