
### Sensors and Software for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

* Wearable computing refers to the use of technology that can be worn on the body. These devices are designed to be unobtrusive and provide users with real-time access to data, information, and services.
* Wearable computing devices typically include sensors that measure and record data such as location, heart rate, temperature, and other biometric data.
* The data collected by wearable computing devices can be used to provide users with personalized information and feedback, as well as to provide insights into their health and fitness.
* Wearable computing devices are often connected to the internet, allowing users to access data and services from anywhere.
* Software is an integral part of wearable computing devices. This includes software for collecting, analyzing, and displaying data, as well as software for controlling the device and providing user feedback.
* Wearable computing devices can also be connected to other devices, such as smartphones and tablets, allowing users to access data and services from multiple devices.
