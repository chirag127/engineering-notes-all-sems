
### Digital to Analog Conversion 

Digital to analog conversion is the process of converting a digital signal into an analog signal. This is important in wearable computing, mixed reality and the internet of everything because it allows digital information to be translated into something that can be understood by the user. 

1. Digital signals are composed of binary 1s and 0s and are used to represent information. 
2. Analog signals, on the other hand, are continuous and are typically used to represent physical quantities such as voltage, temperature or sound. 
3. Digital to analog conversion is done using a device called a digital-to-analog converter (DAC). 
4. The DAC takes a digital signal and converts it into an analog signal by mapping the digital values to an analog voltage. 
5. The DAC is used in a variety of applications, such as audio and video playback, data transmission, and computer networking. 
6. There are several types of DACs, including voltage output DACs, current output DACs, and pulse code modulation (PCM) DACs. 
7. The accuracy of the conversion process is dependent on the accuracy of the DAC used. 
8. Digital to analog conversion is also used in robotics, where digital signals are converted into analog signals to control motors and other actuators.
