
# WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

* Wearable computing is technology that is designed to be worn by a person. It can include items such as smart watches, fitness trackers, and clothing with embedded sensors.

* Mixed reality is the combination of virtual reality and augmented reality. It combines real-world and virtual elements to create an immersive experience.

* The Internet of Everything (IoE) is the network of physical objects that are embedded with technology to connect and exchange data with other devices and systems over the internet. IoE includes connected devices, systems, and services that enable data collection and analysis.
