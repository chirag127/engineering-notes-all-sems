
### IoT and Wearables

* IoT (Internet of Things) and Wearables are physical devices that are connected to the internet and have the ability to collect, store, and analyze data.
* Wearables are typically used to monitor and track physical activities, such as steps taken, heart rate, and calories burned.
* IoT devices are used to control and automate everyday objects, such as thermostats, lighting, and security systems.
* Wearables and IoT devices can be used to improve quality of life and health, as well as to increase efficiency in the workplace.
