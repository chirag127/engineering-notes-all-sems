
### Microcontrollers 

Microcontrollers are small, low-cost, low-power computers that are used to control electronic devices. They are typically used in embedded systems, such as in wearables, mixed reality, and the Internet of Things (IoT). 

Microcontrollers are composed of several components, including: 

* A processor, which is responsible for executing instructions. 
* Memory, which stores data and instructions. 
* Input/output (I/O) ports, which allow the microcontroller to interact with external devices. 
* Peripheral devices, such as timers, counters, and analog-to-digital converters, which allow the microcontroller to interact with the environment. 

Microcontrollers are programmed using a variety of languages, including assembly language, C, and C++. Programming is typically done using a development board, which includes the microcontroller, memory, and other components. 

Microcontrollers are used in a wide variety of applications, including consumer electronics, automotive systems, medical devices, and industrial automation. They are also used in robotics, where they are used to control motors, sensors, and other components.
