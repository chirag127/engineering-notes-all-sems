
### Language for the Notes of Unit 2 - SOFTWARE HARDWARE FRAMEWORKS in Wearable Computing, Mixed Reality, and the Internet of Everything

1. **Software:** Software is a collection of instructions that enable the user to interact with a computer, its hardware, or perform tasks. Software can range from a simple calculator application to a complex operating system.

2. **Hardware:** Hardware refers to the physical components of a computer system, such as the motherboard, processor, memory, and storage.

3. **Frameworks:** Frameworks are software libraries that provide a standard way to build and deploy applications. Frameworks are used to create applications that are easier to develop, maintain, and scale.

4. **Wearable Computing:** Wearable computing is the use of computer technology embedded in clothing and accessories. Wearable computing can be used to monitor health and fitness, provide augmented reality experiences, and more.

5. **Mixed Reality:** Mixed reality is a combination of augmented reality and virtual reality that creates an immersive experience for the user. Mixed reality can be used to create interactive 3D environments, games, and more.

6. **Internet of Everything:** The Internet of Everything (IoE) is a term used to describe the connection of people, processes, data, and things. The IoE is enabled by the use of sensors, wireless networks, and other technologies that enable devices to communicate with each other.
