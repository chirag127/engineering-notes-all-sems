
### Oculus Rift

Oculus Rift is a virtual reality (VR) headset developed by Oculus VR, a division of Facebook Inc. It was released in March 2016 and is the first consumer-oriented VR headset to be released. The headset uses two OLED displays to provide a stereoscopic 3D image with a wide field of view and low latency motion tracking.

The Oculus Rift was the first major consumer-oriented VR headset to be released, and has been influential in the development of the VR industry. It has been used for gaming, entertainment, education, and other applications.

The Oculus Rift is a powerful tool for creating immersive VR experiences, and is compatible with a range of gaming platforms, including Windows, Mac, Xbox, and PlayStation. It is also compatible with a range of software development kits (SDKs), including Unity, Unreal Engine, and others.

The Oculus Rift is an important tool for exploring the potential of VR, and for developing applications in areas such as gaming, entertainment, education, and more. It is an exciting technology that is sure to have an impact on the future of computing.
