
### ADC for the notes of the Unit 2 - SOFTWARE HARDWARE FRAMEWORKS in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

* ADC stands for Analog-to-Digital Converter. It is a type of hardware device that converts analog signals into digital signals.
* ADC is used in many applications, such as in wearable computing, mixed reality and internet of things.
* ADC is used to convert analog signals into digital signals, which can be processed by a computer or other digital device.
* ADC can be used to measure physical parameters such as temperature, pressure, voltage, and current.
* ADC can be used to convert analog audio signals into digital audio signals, which can then be stored and processed by a computer.
* ADC can also be used to convert analog video signals into digital video signals, which can then be stored and processed by a computer.
