
### Arduino for the Notes of Unit 3 - CYBERNETICS AND HUM

1. Arduino is an open-source hardware and software platform used for building interactive objects and systems. It is based on a microcontroller board and a set of input/output components.

2. Arduino boards are programmed using the Arduino programming language, and the Arduino Integrated Development Environment (IDE). The language allows for the creation of complex programs and interactions with other devices.

3. Arduino boards can be used to control a variety of devices, from motors and sensors to LEDs and other electronic components. They can also be used to create interactive art pieces, robots, and other projects.

4. Arduino can be used to create projects that interact with the physical world, such as environmental monitoring systems, home automation systems, and robotic systems.

5. The Arduino platform is also used for teaching and research in fields such as cybernetics, robotics, and artificial intelligence.
