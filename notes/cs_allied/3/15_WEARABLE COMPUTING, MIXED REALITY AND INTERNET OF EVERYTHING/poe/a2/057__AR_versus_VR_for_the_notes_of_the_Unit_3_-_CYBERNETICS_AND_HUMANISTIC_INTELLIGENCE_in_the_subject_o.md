### AR versus VR for the notes of the Unit 3 - CYBERNETICS AND HUMANISTIC INTELLIGENCE in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

1. Augmented Reality (AR) - AR overlays digital information on the real world via devices like smart glasses or smartphones. It enhances the real world by adding layers of digital information on top of it. Users can still see the real world, but additional data is displayed through the device. AR is being applied in fields like education, retail, and business.

2. Virtual Reality (VR) - VR creates an entirely artificial environment. It transports users to a simulated 3D world and cuts them off from the physical world. Users wear headsets or goggles that provide immersive visual and audio experiences. VR is being explored for gaming, entertainment, education, social connection, and other applications.

3. Differences - The key differences between AR and VR are:

- AR supplements the real world while VR replaces it.
- AR uses see-through devices while VR uses occluded devices.
- AR integrates digital information into the real world while VR creates an entirely simulated world.
- AR is ideal for overlaying data and enhancing experiences while VR is better suited for fully immersive experiences.

4. Use cases - Some examples of use cases for AR and VR include:

AR:
- Smart glasses for factory workers to get instructions and share data
- Mobile apps for retailers to enable customers to view products in their home before buying
- Educational apps to teach students about the human body, solar system, and more

VR:
- Gaming and entertainment for immersive experiences
- Social connection through virtual meetings and hangouts
- Training simulations for practices that are dangerous, expensive, or impractical in the real world
- Therapy for rehabilitation, pain relief, and exposure therapy
