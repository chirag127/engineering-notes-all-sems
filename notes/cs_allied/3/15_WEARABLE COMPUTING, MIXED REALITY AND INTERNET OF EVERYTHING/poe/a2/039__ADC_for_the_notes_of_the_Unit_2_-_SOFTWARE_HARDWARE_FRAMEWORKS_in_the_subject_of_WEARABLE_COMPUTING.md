### ADC for the notes of the Unit 2 - SOFTWARE HARDWARE FRAMEWORKS in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

1. Analog-to-digital converters (ADCs) convert analog signals to digital signals that can be processed by digital devices.
2. ADCs are essential components in wearable and IoT devices that capture real-world analog data such as motion, sound, light, temperature, etc. and convert it to digital data for processing.
3. Important specifications for ADCs include:
- Resolution: Number of discrete values the ADC can produce. Higher resolution leads to more accurate digital representation of the analog input.
- Sampling rate: How frequently the ADC captures samples of the analog input. Higher sampling rate can capture higher frequency components of the input signal.
- Input range: The range of analog voltage values that can be converted. The input range determines the scale of phenomena that can be measured.
- Accuracy: How close the digital output is to the actual analog input value. Lower error leads to higher accuracy.
4. Types of ADCs include successive approximation register (SAR) ADCs, integrating ADCs, delta-sigma ADCs, etc. Selection depends on specifications, cost, and application.
