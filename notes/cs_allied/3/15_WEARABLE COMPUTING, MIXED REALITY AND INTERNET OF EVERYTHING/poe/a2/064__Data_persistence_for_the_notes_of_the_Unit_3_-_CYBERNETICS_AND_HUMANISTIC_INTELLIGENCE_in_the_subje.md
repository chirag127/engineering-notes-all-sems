### Data persistence for the notes of the Unit 3 - CYBERNETICS AND HUMANISTIC INTELLIGENCE in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING.

1. Data persistence refers to the ability of a digital system to store data for a long period of time. The data should remain accessible and usable during this time.
2. For the notes of the unit, the data should be stored in a digital format that can be accessed even after a long time. Some options for data persistence are:
- Saving the notes in a cloud storage service like Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive, etc. The notes will be accessible from any device as long as you remember the login credentials.
- Saving the notes in a local storage on your device like internal storage or SD card. The notes will be available as long as the device is functioning but there is a risk of data loss if the device is damaged or misplaced.
- Printing the notes and storing them physically. The notes will be accessible as long as the printouts are preserved but cannot be searched or edited digitally.
3. For the given context of wearable computing and mixed reality, cloud storage or local digital storage on devices would be more suitable as the notes would need to be accessed digitally. Using a reliable cloud service and maintaining multiple backups can ensure data persistence.
4. The data format of the notes should be future-proof like PDF, Markdown, etc. so that the notes remain accessible and readable even with changes in technology. Plain text format with minimal formatting is ideal if future accessibility is a priority.
