### Oculus Rift

- Oculus Rift is a virtual reality headset developed by Oculus VR, a subsidiary of Facebook Inc.
- It allows users to experience and interact with an immersive virtual environment.
- The headset tracks the user's movements and adjusts the perspective in correspondence, allowing them to look around the artificial world.
- Oculus Rift has two built-in stereoscopic OLED displays with a resolution of 1080x1200 per eye.
- It has a refresh rate of 90 Hz and a 100° field of view.
- Positional tracking is achieved via one or more externally mounted sensors that allow the system to locate the user's head motions in 3D space.
- The Oculus Touch controllers provide input for interaction with the virtual world and are tracked in a similar fashion.
- Oculus Rift was first announced in 2012, as a Kickstarter project to help realize the virtual reality headset concept.
- The first consumer version was released in 2016 and since then, its specifications have been gradually improved with newer models.
- Oculus Rift has enabled advancement in virtual reality technology and applications. Its impact has been significant in the gaming and entertainment industries as well as areas like social connection, business, and education.
