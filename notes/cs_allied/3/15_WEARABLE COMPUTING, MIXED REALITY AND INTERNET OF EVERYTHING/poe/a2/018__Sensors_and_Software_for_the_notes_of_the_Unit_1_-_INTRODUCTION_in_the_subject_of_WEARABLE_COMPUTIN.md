### Sensors and Software for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

1. Sensors: Sensors are devices that detect and respond to some type of input from the physical environment. They are used to collect data about the world. Some common sensors used in wearable devices and IoT are:
- Accelerometers: Detect acceleration and movement. Measure tilt, motion, vibration, and orientation.
- Gyroscopes: Detect rotation and orientation. Measure angular velocity and orientation.
- Magnetometers: Detect magnetic fields. Measure magnetic north and orientation.
- Pressure sensors: Detect air pressure, water pressure, etc. Measure altitude or depth.
- Temperature sensors: Detect temperature. Measure ambient temperature or body temperature.
- Humidity sensors: Detect humidity. Measure ambient humidity.
- Image sensors: Detect light and capture images. Take photos and videos and enable augmented reality.

2. Software: The data collected from sensors is processed and analyzed using software. Some common software components used are:
- Operating systems: Provide platform to run software and applications. Manage hardware and resources.
- Machine learning algorithms: Analyze data and detect patterns to make predictions or decisions without being explicitly programmed. Enable features like activity recognition, gesture recognition, etc.
- APIs and frameworks: Enable interaction between software components and access to hardware like sensors. Allow integration of machine learning models and algorithms.
- Applications: The end-user interfaces running on the devices that utilize the sensors and software to provide functionality like health monitoring, navigation, etc.
