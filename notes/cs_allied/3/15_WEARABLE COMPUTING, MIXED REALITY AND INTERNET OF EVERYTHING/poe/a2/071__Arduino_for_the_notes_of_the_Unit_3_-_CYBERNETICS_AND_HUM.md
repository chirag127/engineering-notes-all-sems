### Arduino for the notes of the Unit 3 - CYBERNETICS AND HUM

1. Arduino is an open-source hardware and software company, project, and user community that designs and manufactures single-board microcontrollers and microcontroller kits for building digital devices.

2. The Arduino boards are able to read inputs from a variety of sensors and control a variety of external devices by interfacing with the outside world via different shields or expansion boards. This makes the Arduino ideal for interactive projects.

3. The Arduino software is based on the Processing and Wiring projects. It includes a simple programming language and development environment based on the programming languages C and C++. The software is cross-platform and is used to write programs to control the board.

4. The Arduino project's initial goal was to create low-cost and easy tools for creating digital projects by non-engineers. The Arduino ecosystem has grown to cater to a wide variety of applications. There are many Arduino boards, including the Arduino Uno which is a very popular model.

5. Arduino boards can interface with a variety of sensors and actuators. They are able to control motors, LEDs, relays, etc. and can read data from sensors such as temperature sensors, light sensors, acceleration sensors, etc. This allows Arduino to be used in a wide variety of DIY applications and projects.
