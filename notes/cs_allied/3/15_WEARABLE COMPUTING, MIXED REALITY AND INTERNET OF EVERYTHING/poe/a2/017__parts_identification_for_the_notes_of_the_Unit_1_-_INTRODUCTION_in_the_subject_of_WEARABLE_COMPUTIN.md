 Here are the notes for Unit 1 - Introduction to Wearable Computing, Mixed Reality and Internet of Everything:

### Definitions
- Wearable computing: Embedding computing devices into clothing or accessories that can be worn on the body. E.g. smart watches, fitness trackers, smart glasses.
- Mixed reality: Merging of real and virtual worlds to produce new environments and visualizations where physical and digital objects co-exist and interact in real time. E.g. Hololens, Magic Leap.
- Internet of Everything: Network connections between people, process, data and things enabling advanced services and applications. E.g. connected smart home devices, smart cities.

### Key characteristics
Wearable computing:
- Portable and wearable
- Context-aware and adapts to user
- Limited display and input capabilities

Mixed reality:
- Combines real and virtual elements
- Interactive in real-time
- Immersive user experience

Internet of Everything:
- Interconnected network of people, devices and systems
- Exchange of data, information and actions
- Advance automation and analytics

### Applications and impacts
- Healthcare: Continuous health monitoring, augmented surgeries
- Industry: Smart factories, remote collaboration, skilled labor augmentation
- Entertainment: Immersive gaming, movies, social connections
- Daily life: Smart homes, cities, enhanced education

The technologies promise significant benefits but also raise ethical, privacy and legal concerns that must be addressed. Regulation and policies are needed to ensure safe, fair and ethical use.
