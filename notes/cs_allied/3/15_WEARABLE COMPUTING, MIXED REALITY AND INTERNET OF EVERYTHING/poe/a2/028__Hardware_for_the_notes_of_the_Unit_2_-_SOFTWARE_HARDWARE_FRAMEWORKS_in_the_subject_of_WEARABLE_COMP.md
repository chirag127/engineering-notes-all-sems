### Hardware

1. Sensors: Accelerometers, gyroscopes, magnetometers, heart rate monitors, etc. are sensors used to sense motion, orientation, physiological signals, etc. These sensors are used to capture data from the real world.
2. Microcontrollers: Microcontrollers like Arduino, Raspberry Pi, etc. are used to process the data from the sensors and perform actions. They are small computers that control the overall hardware system.
3. Actuators: Actuators like motors, LEDs, speakers, etc. are used to perform actions in the real world based on the data processed by the microcontrollers. They are used to display or output the results.
4. Power supply: Batteries or power banks are used to supply power to the overall hardware system. They provide the required electricity to power the sensors, microcontrollers and actuators.
5. Communication systems: Communication technologies like Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, cellular networks, etc. are used to transmit data between various hardware components or to the cloud for further processing and storage.
