### Analog to Digital Conversion

For the notes of the Unit 2 - SOFTWARE HARDWARE FRAMEWORKS in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING.

- Analog signals are continuous electrical signals which vary over time and can take on any value within a range.
- Digital signals have only two values, 0 and 1.
- Analog to digital conversion is the process of converting analog signals into digital signals.
- An analog to digital converter is used to sample an analog signal and convert it to digital data which can be processed by digital devices.
- The key steps in analog to digital conversion are:

1. Sampling - The analog signal is sampled at regular intervals to get a snapshot of the signal value at each interval.
2. Quantization - Each sample is approximated to the nearest value from a set of discrete values.
3. Encoding - The quantized values are encoded into binary data.
4. Reconstruction - The binary data is used to reconstruct the digital signal.

- The sampling rate and number of quantization levels impact the accuracy of digital representation of the analog signal. Higher sampling rate and more quantization levels lead to more accurate digital representation but also increase processing requirements.
- Analog to digital conversion is used in devices like sensors, audio and video equipment to convert physical quantities to digital data that can be processed by computers and other digital devices.
