### Communication

- Communication is the exchange of information between two or more entities.
- In wearable computing and IoT systems, communication happens between sensors, devices, and servers.
- The types of communication include:

#### Wired Communication

- Physical cables/wires are used to transmit data.
- Examples: USB, Ethernet
- Pros: Fast, reliable, secure
- Cons: Restricted mobility, prone to physical damage

#### Wireless Communication

- Data is transmitted wirelessly using radio waves or infrared light.
- Examples: Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, cellular networks
- Pros: Mobile, flexible
- Cons: Slower, less secure, prone to interference

#### Short-range vs Long-range

- Short-range: Bluetooth, infrared, NFC
- Limited range (meters to hundreds of meters)
- Low power
- Long-range: Wi-Fi, cellular
- Wide range (kilometers)
- Higher power

The appropriate communication technology is chosen based on requirements like speed, range, power, cost, etc. Hybrid systems using multiple technologies are also possible. Security and privacy are important considerations for wireless communication.
