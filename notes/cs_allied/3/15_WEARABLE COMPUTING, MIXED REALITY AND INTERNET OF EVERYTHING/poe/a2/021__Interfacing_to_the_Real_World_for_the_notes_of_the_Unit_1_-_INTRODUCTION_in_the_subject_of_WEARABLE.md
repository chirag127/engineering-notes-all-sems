### Interfacing to the Real World

- Sensors and actuators are used to interface wearable devices and mixed reality systems to the real world.
- Sensors detect parameters from the real world such as motion, location, biometrics, gestures, etc. and convert them into electrical signals which are processed by the system.
- Actuators convert electrical signals from the system into actions in the real world such as display, sound, vibration, etc.
- Different sensors are used for measuring various physical quantities. Some common sensors used are accelerometers, gyroscopes, magnetometers, pressure sensors, temperature sensors, biometric sensors, etc.
- Different actuators are used for different types of output. Some common actuators are displays, speakers, vibrational motors, etc.
- Sensor data can be fused using sensor fusion techniques to get more accurate measurements and contextual information.
- Precise and robust interfacing with the real world is crucial for wearable and mixed reality systems to function accurately.
