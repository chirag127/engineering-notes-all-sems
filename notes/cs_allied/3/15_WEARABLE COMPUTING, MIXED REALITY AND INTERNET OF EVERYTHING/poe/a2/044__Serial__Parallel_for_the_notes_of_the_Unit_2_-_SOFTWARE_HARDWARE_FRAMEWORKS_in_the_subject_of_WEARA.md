### Serial & Parallel

- Serial communication: Data is transmitted bit by bit, one after the other. Only one device can transmit at a time. Slower but simpler. Example: UART
- Parallel communication: Multiple bits are transmitted simultaneously on different wires. Faster but requires more wires. Example: Parallel buses in computers
- For wearable and IoT devices with limited connections, serial communication is preferred. Parallel can be used for high speed data transfer within a device.
- Serial interfaces: UART, SPI, I2C. Parallel interfaces: Parallel GPIO, parallel bus.
- Tradeoff between speed and complexity must be considered while choosing between serial and parallel communication.
