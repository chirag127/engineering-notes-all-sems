### Representing “reality” with computers for the notes of the Unit 2 - SOFTWARE HARDWARE FRAMEWORKS in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

Representing "reality" with computers is a central challenge in the field of wearable computing, mixed reality, and the Internet of Everything (IoE). It involves the use of computers and other digital technologies to create representations of the physical world, and to enable interactions between people and digital devices in the physical world.

In Unit 2 - Software Hardware Frameworks in the subject of Wearable Computing, Mixed Reality and Internet of Everything, students will learn about the different methods and technologies that are used to represent "reality" with computers, including:

1. Sensors: Sensors are used to collect data from the physical world, and to provide input to wearable devices and mixed reality systems.

2. Computer Graphics: Computer graphics is used to create digital representations of the physical world, and to enable interactions between people and digital devices in the physical world.

3. Virtual Reality (VR): VR is a technology that enables users to experience and interact with a computer-generated environment as if it were real.

4. Augmented Reality (AR): AR is a technology that enhances the physical world with digital information and experiences.

5. Mixed Reality (MR): MR is a technology that combines VR and AR to create a seamless and immersive experience that blends the physical and digital worlds.

By understanding the different methods and technologies that are used to represent "reality" with computers, students can gain a deeper appreciation for the technology and its impact on wearable computing, mixed reality, and the Internet of Everything. This understanding can help students to develop the skills necessary to design, build, and program wearable devices, mixed reality systems, and IoE devices that can interact with the physical world.
