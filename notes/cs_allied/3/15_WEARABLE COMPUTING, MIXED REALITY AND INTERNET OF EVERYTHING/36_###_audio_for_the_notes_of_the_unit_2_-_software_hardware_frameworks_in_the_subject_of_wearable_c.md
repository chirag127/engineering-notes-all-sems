### audio for the notes of the Unit 2 - SOFTWARE HARDWARE FRAMEWORKS in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

Audio is an important component of wearable computing, mixed reality, and the Internet of Things (IoT). It involves the creation, processing, and reproduction of sound and speech in wearable devices, mixed reality systems, and IoT devices.

In Unit 2 - Software Hardware Frameworks in the subject of Wearable Computing, Mixed Reality and Internet of Everything, students will learn about the different technologies and solutions that are used to create, process, and reproduce audio in wearable devices, mixed reality systems, and IoT devices, including:

1. Audio processing: Audio processing involves the processing of audio signals to extract information, such as speech recognition, audio compression, and audio equalization.

2. Audio reproduction: Audio reproduction involves the conversion of digital signals into analog signals that can be heard by the user. This can include speaker systems, headphones, and other audio output devices.

3. Audio input: Audio input involves the conversion of analog signals into digital signals that can be processed by the device. This can include microphones, audio input devices, and other audio input technologies.

4. Audio codecs: Audio codecs are used to encode and decode audio signals, and to reduce the size of audio files.

By understanding the different technologies and solutions that are used to create, process, and reproduce audio in wearable devices, mixed reality systems, and IoT devices, students can gain a deeper appreciation for the technology and its impact on wearable computing, mixed reality, and IoT. This understanding can help students to develop the skills necessary to design, build, and program wearable devices, mixed reality systems, and IoT devices that incorporate audio capabilities.
