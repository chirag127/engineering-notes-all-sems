### Introduction for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

Wearable computing, mixed reality, and the Internet of Everything (IoE) are rapidly growing fields that involve the integration of technology into everyday objects and environments. These fields involve the development of wearable devices, mixed reality experiences, and the integration of physical and digital systems to create new and innovative solutions.

1. Wearable Computing: Wearable computing refers to the development of computing devices that can be worn on the body, such as smartwatches, fitness trackers, and augmented reality glasses.

2. Mixed Reality: Mixed reality refers to the integration of digital content and information into the physical world, creating a seamless blend of the real and virtual environments.

3. Internet of Everything (IoE): The Internet of Everything (IoE) refers to the integration of physical devices, such as sensors, actuators, and other connected devices, into the internet, creating a network of connected systems.

In conclusion, wearable computing, mixed reality, and the Internet of Everything (IoE) are rapidly growing fields that involve the integration of technology into everyday objects and environments. These fields involve the development of wearable devices, mixed reality experiences, and the integration of physical and digital systems to create new and innovative solutions. The goal of these fields is to create more seamless and intuitive experiences for users, and to create new opportunities for innovation and progress.
