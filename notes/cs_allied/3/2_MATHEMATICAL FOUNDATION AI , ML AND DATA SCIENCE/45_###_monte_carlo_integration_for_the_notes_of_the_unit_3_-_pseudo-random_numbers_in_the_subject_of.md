- Monte Carlo Integration is based on the idea of randomly sampling points from a region and using these samples to estimate the value of an integral.
- Monte Carlo Integration can be used to estimate the value of integrals that cannot be solved analytically.
- Monte Carlo Integration is widely used in a variety of fields, including physics, engineering, and finance.
- Monte Carlo Integration can be performed using a variety of techniques, including the Monte Carlo method, the Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) method, and the Metropolis-Hastings algorithm.
