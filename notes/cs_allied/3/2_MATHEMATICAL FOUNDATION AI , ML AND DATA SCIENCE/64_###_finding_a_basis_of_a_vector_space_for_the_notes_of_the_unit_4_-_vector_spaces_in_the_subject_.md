Finding a Basis of a Vector Space

A basis of a vector space is a set of linearly independent vectors that can be used to represent all the vectors in the vector space. The concept of a basis is important in linear algebra, as it provides a way to represent a vector space in a compact and manageable form.

There are several methods for finding a basis of a vector space, including the Gram-Schmidt orthogonalization process and the row echelon form. The Gram-Schmidt orthogonalization process is a method for finding an orthogonal basis of a vector space, while the row echelon form is a method for finding a basis of a vector space that is in row echelon form.
