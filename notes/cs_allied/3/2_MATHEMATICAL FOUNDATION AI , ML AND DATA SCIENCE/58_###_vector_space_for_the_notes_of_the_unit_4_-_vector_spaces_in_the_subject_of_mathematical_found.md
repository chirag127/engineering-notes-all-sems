Vector Space in Mathematical Foundation of AI, ML and Data Science:
- Vector Space is a mathematical concept that refers to a collection of vectors and operations that can be performed on these vectors.
- Vector spaces have a set of axioms, including the properties of addition and scalar multiplication, that define the behavior of the vectors in the space.
- Vector spaces can be used to model a variety of mathematical objects, including points in a space, functions, and matrices.
- Vector spaces are used in a variety of fields, including linear algebra, physics, and engineering.
- Understanding vector spaces is important for the analysis and interpretation of data and for the development of data-driven solutions to real-world problems.
