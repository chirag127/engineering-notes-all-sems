Antithetic Variables/Control Variates in Pseudo-Random Numbers:
- Antithetic Variables and Control Variates are two techniques used to reduce the variance of Monte Carlo simulations.
- Antithetic Variables involves generating pairs of random variables that are negatively correlated, such that positive fluctuations in one variable are offset by negative fluctuations in the other.
- Control Variates involves using a known relationship between two random variables to reduce the variance of the Monte Carlo simulation.
- Antithetic Variables and Control Variates can be used to improve the efficiency of Monte Carlo simulations, as they reduce the number of simulations required to achieve a desired level of accuracy.
- Understanding Antithetic Variables and Control Variates is important for the development of data-driven solutions to real-world problems and for the analysis and interpretation of data.
