## Unit 1 - Descriptive Statistics

Unit 1 - Descriptive Statistics in Introduction to Data Analytics and Visualization:
- Unit 1 focuses on the study of descriptive statistics and its role in data analysis.
- Descriptive statistics is a branch of statistics that deals with the summarization and description of data.
- Topics covered in Unit 1 may include:
  1. Measures of central tendency: such as mean, median, and mode.
  2. Measures of dispersion: such as variance, standard deviation, and range.
  3. Frequency distributions: the distribution of data into categories.
  4. Graphical representations of data: such as histograms and box plots.
- Unit 1 may also cover the use of tools and technologies for descriptive statistics, such as R, Python, and Excel.
- Understanding descriptive statistics is important for the analysis and interpretation of data and for the development of data-driven solutions to real-world problems.
