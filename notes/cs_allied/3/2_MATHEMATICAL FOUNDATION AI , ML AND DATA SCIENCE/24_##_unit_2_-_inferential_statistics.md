## Unit 2 - Inferential Statistics

Inferential Statistics is a branch of statistics that deals with making generalizations about a population based on a sample.

1. Definition: Inferential Statistics is a branch of statistics that deals with making generalizations about a population based on a sample. Inferential Statistics involves using statistical methods to draw conclusions about a population based on a sample of data.

2. Sampling: Sampling is a key aspect of Inferential Statistics. Sampling involves selecting a subset of a population to represent the population as a whole. Sampling is used to make inferences about the population based on the sample data.

3. Estimation: Estimation is the process of using sample data to estimate population parameters. Estimation involves calculating point estimates and interval estimates for population parameters.

4. Hypothesis Testing: Hypothesis testing is a statistical method used to test claims about a population based on sample data. Hypothesis testing involves formulating a null hypothesis and an alternative hypothesis, and then using statistical methods to determine which hypothesis is supported by the data.

5. Confidence Intervals: Confidence intervals are a measure of the uncertainty associated with an estimate. Confidence intervals are calculated based on the sample data and are used to estimate the range of values that the population parameter is likely to fall within.

6. Significance Testing: Significance testing is a statistical method used to determine whether a sample is significantly different from the population. Significance testing involves calculating a p-value, which is the probability of observing the sample data if the null hypothesis is true.

7. Advantages: Inferential Statistics has several advantages, including the ability to make generalizations about a population based on a sample, the ability to estimate population parameters, and the ability to test claims about a population.

8. Limitations: Despite its advantages, Inferential Statistics also has several limitations, including the difficulty of ensuring the representativeness of the sample, the difficulty of ensuring the accuracy of the estimates, and the difficulty of ensuring the validity of the hypotheses.

9. Applications: Inferential Statistics is used in a wide range of applications, including market research, medical research, and social sciences. Inferential Statistics is also used in fields such as data science, machine learning, and artificial intelligence.

In conclusion, Inferential Statistics is a branch of statistics that deals with making generalizations about a population based on a sample. Inferential Statistics involves using statistical methods such as estimation, hypothesis testing, and significance testing to draw conclusions about a population based on a sample of data. Inferential Statistics has several advantages, including the ability to make generalizations about a population, but also has several limitations, including the difficulty of ensuring the representativeness of the sample. Inferential Statistics is used in a wide range of applications and plays an important role in fields such as data science and artificial intelligence.
