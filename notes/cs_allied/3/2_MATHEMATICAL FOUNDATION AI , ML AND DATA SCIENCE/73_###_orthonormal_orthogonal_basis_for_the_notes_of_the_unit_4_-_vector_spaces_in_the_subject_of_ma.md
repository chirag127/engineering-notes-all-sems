### Orthonormal (Orthogonal) Basis for the notes of the Unit 4 - Vector Spaces in the subject of MATHEMATICAL FOUNDATION AI , ML AND DATA SCIENCE

Orthonormal basis is a set of vectors that are mutually orthogonal (perpendicular) and have a unit length of 1. In a vector space, a set of orthonormal basis vectors can be used to represent any vector in the space. 

Orthonormal basis has several important properties, including:

1. Linear independence: Orthonormal basis vectors are linearly independent, meaning that no vector in the set can be expressed as a linear combination of the others.

2. Orthogonality: The vectors are orthogonal, meaning that the dot product of any two basis vectors is equal to 0.

3. Normalization: The vectors have a unit length of 1, meaning that their magnitude is equal to 1.

Orthonormal basis is widely used in linear algebra, computer graphics, and other fields. It is particularly useful for representing and manipulating vectors in high-dimensional spaces, as it provides a compact and efficient representation of the vectors.

In addition, orthonormal basis is a key component of several machine learning algorithms, including principal component analysis (PCA) and singular value decomposition (SVD). These algorithms use orthonormal basis to reduce the dimensionality of data and extract the most important features.
