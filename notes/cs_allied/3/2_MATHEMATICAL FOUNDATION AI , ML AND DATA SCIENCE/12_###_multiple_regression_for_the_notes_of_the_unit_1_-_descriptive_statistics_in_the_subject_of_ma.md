Multiple Regression in Mathematical Foundation of AI, ML, and Data Science:
- Multiple regression is a statistical technique used to model the relationship between a dependent variable and one or more independent variables.
- The goal of multiple regression is to find the best linear combination of independent variables that predicts the dependent variable.
- Multiple regression can be used for a variety of tasks, including:
  1. Predictive modeling: the process of using historical data to make predictions about future data.
  2. Causal inference: the process of determining the cause-and-effect relationship between variables.
  3. Model selection: the process of choosing the best model from a set of candidate models.
- Multiple regression can be performed using a variety of techniques, including ordinary least squares (OLS) regression, ridge regression, and lasso regression.
- Understanding multiple regression is important for the analysis and interpretation of data and for the development of data-driven solutions to real-world problems.
