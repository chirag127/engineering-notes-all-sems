### Change of Basis for the notes of the Unit 5 - Linear Transformations in the subject of MATHEMATICAL FOUNDATION AI , ML AND DATA SCIENCE

Change of Basis is a mathematical concept that refers to the process of transforming a vector from one basis to another. A basis is a set of vectors that can be used to represent any vector in a vector space. The process of change of basis involves expressing a vector in a different set of basis vectors.

In linear algebra, change of basis is used to simplify the representation of a vector or a linear transformation. For example, if a vector is expressed in a basis that is not orthogonal, it may be difficult to perform operations such as dot products or projections. By changing the basis to an orthogonal basis, these operations become much simpler.

The process of change of basis involves using a matrix to transform the vector from one basis to another. The matrix is called the change of basis matrix and is determined by the relationship between the old and new basis vectors.

Change of basis is also used in machine learning and data science to transform data from one representation to another. For example, it can be used to transform data from a high-dimensional space to a lower-dimensional space, making it easier to visualize and analyze.

Overall, change of basis is a fundamental concept in linear algebra and has numerous applications in mathematics, machine learning, and data science.
