Metropolis-Hastings Algorithm

The Metropolis-Hastings algorithm is a Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) algorithm used to generate a sequence of random numbers that approximate a target distribution. The algorithm is used to sample from a target distribution that is difficult to sample from directly, by constructing a Markov Chain that converges to the target distribution.

The Metropolis-Hastings algorithm consists of two steps: the proposal step and the acceptance-rejection step. In the proposal step, a candidate sample is generated from the current state of the Markov Chain. In the acceptance-rejection step, the candidate sample is either accepted or rejected based on a probability calculated using the target distribution and the proposal distribution.

The Metropolis-Hastings algorithm is widely used in machine learning and data science, as it provides a way to generate random numbers from a target distribution that is difficult to sample from directly. For example, the algorithm can be used to generate samples from a posterior distribution in Bayesian inference, or to generate samples from a complex distribution in Monte Carlo simulations.
