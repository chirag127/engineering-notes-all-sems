### Linear Combination for the notes of the Unit 4 - Vector Spaces in the subject of MATHEMATICAL FOUNDATION AI , ML AND DATA SCIENCE

Linear combination is a fundamental concept in linear algebra and is used extensively in the fields of artificial intelligence, machine learning, and data science. It refers to a linear combination of vectors, which is a weighted sum of the vectors.

A linear combination of vectors can be written as:

c1v1 + c2v2 + ... + cn vn = v

where c1, c2, ..., cn are scalars (numbers) and v1, v2, ..., vn are vectors. The result of the linear combination, v, is also a vector.

Linear combinations are used to describe a wide range of mathematical concepts, including linear transformations, subspaces, and eigenvectors. They are also used in machine learning algorithms, such as linear regression and principal component analysis, to model relationships between variables.
