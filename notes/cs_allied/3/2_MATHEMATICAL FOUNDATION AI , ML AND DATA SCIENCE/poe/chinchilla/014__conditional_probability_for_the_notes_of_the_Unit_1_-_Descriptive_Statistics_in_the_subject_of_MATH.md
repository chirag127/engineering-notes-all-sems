### Conditional Probability

Conditional Probability is the probability of an event occurring, given that another event has already occurred. 

It is denoted by P(A|B), which means the probability of event A occurring given that event B has already occurred.

Some important points to remember about Conditional Probability are:

- The probability of event A given event B is defined as the probability of both A and B occurring divided by the probability of event B occurring. This can be expressed as: P(A|B) = P(A and B) / P(B)
- The denominator of the conditional probability is always non-zero as we are assuming that event B has already occurred.
- Conditional probability is a way of updating our knowledge or beliefs about an event based on new information.
- The concept of conditional probability is used in many areas of mathematics, science, and social sciences, including statistics, machine learning, and data science.

There are some important theorems related to Conditional Probability:

1. The Multiplication Rule:

The multiplication rule states that the probability of two events A and B occurring together is the product of the probability of A occurring and the conditional probability of B occurring given that A has occurred. This can be expressed as: P(A and B) = P(A) x P(B|A)

2. Bayes' Theorem:

Bayes' Theorem is used to update the probability of an event based on new information. It states that the probability of event A given event B can be calculated as the product of the probability of event B given event A and the probability of event A, divided by the probability of event B. This can be expressed as:

P(A|B) = (P(B|A) x P(A)) / P(B)

3. Law of Total Probability:

The Law of Total Probability is used to calculate the probability of an event by considering all possible ways it can occur. It states that the probability of event A is equal to the sum of the probability of A given each possible value of another event B, multiplied by the probability of B occurring. This can be expressed as:

P(A) = ∑ P(A|B) x P(B)

In conclusion, Conditional Probability is an important concept in statistics, machine learning, and data science. It is used to update our beliefs about an event based on new information and is essential in making predictions and decisions based on data. Understanding the theorems related to Conditional Probability is crucial in these fields and can help us make better decisions.
