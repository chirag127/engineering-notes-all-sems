### Estimation and Hypothesis Testing for the notes of the Unit 2 - Inferential Statistics in the subject of MATHEMATICAL FOUNDATION AI , ML AND DATA SCIENCE

Inferential statistics is a branch of statistics that allows us to draw conclusions about a population based on a sample of data. Estimation and hypothesis testing are two important techniques used in inferential statistics. Let's understand these techniques in detail:

#### Estimation

Estimation is the process of using a sample to estimate the value of an unknown parameter in a population. The parameter can be a mean, proportion, or standard deviation of a variable in the population. Estimation is important when it is not possible to measure the entire population, and we have to rely on a sample to make inferences about the population.

There are two types of estimation techniques:

1. Point estimation: Point estimation involves estimating a single value for the parameter of interest. The most common point estimator for the population mean is the sample mean, and for the population proportion, it is the sample proportion. Point estimates are generally accompanied by a measure of uncertainty called a confidence interval.

2. Interval estimation: Interval estimation involves estimating a range of values for the parameter of interest. Confidence intervals are the most common interval estimators for population parameters. A confidence interval is a range of values that we can be confident will contain the true population parameter with a specified level of confidence.

#### Hypothesis Testing

Hypothesis testing is a statistical technique used to test the validity of a claim or hypothesis about a population parameter. The hypothesis is typically stated in terms of the null hypothesis (H0) and the alternative hypothesis (Ha). The null hypothesis is the hypothesis that there is no significant difference between the sample and the population, while the alternative hypothesis is the hypothesis that there is a significant difference between the sample and the population.

The steps involved in hypothesis testing are:

1. State the null hypothesis and the alternative hypothesis.

2. Choose a significance level (alpha) to determine the critical value or p-value.

3. Collect data and calculate the test statistic.

4. Determine the p-value of the test statistic.

5. Compare the p-value to the significance level.

6. Reject or fail to reject the null hypothesis based on the p-value.

Hypothesis testing is used in various fields like healthcare, economics, and social sciences, to test the effect of a new drug or policy, or to understand the behavior of a population.

In conclusion, estimation and hypothesis testing are two important techniques used in inferential statistics. Estimation is used to estimate the value of an unknown parameter in a population, while hypothesis testing is used to test the validity of a claim about a population parameter. These techniques help us make inferences about a population based on a sample and are widely used in data science, machine learning, and artificial intelligence.
