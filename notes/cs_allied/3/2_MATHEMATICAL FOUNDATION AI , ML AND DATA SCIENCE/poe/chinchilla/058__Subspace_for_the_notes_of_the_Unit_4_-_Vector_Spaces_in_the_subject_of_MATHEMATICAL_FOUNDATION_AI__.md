### Subspace for the notes of the Unit 4 - Vector Spaces in the subject of MATHEMATICAL FOUNDATION AI , ML AND DATA SCIENCE

Vector spaces are an essential topic in the field of mathematics and its applications in AI, ML, and Data Science. In this unit, we will explore Subspaces, which are subsets of a vector space that satisfy specific conditions. Let's dive deeper into Subspaces:

1. Definition of Subspaces
   - A subset W of a vector space V is a subspace of V if and only if:
     - W is non-empty, i.e., it contains at least one element.
     - W is closed under addition and scalar multiplication, i.e., if u and v are in W and a is a scalar, then u+v and au are also in W.

2. Properties of Subspaces
   - The intersection of two subspaces of V is also a subspace of V.
   - The union of two subspaces of V is not necessarily a subspace of V.
   - The only subspaces of a one-dimensional vector space are the zero subspace and the entire vector space. 

3. Examples of Subspaces
   - The solution set of a system of homogeneous linear equations is a subspace of the vector space of all solutions.
   - The set of all polynomials of degree less than or equal to n is a subspace of the vector space of all polynomials.
   - The set of all symmetric matrices is a subspace of the vector space of all matrices.

4. Basis and Dimension of Subspaces
   - A basis for a subspace W of V is a set of vectors that spans W and is linearly independent.
   - The dimension of a subspace W of V is the number of vectors in a basis for W.
   - The dimension of a subspace W of V is always less than or equal to the dimension of V.

5. Linear Transformations and Subspaces
   - A linear transformation T:V→W maps a vector space V to a vector space W and preserves the operations of addition and scalar multiplication.
   - The image of a subspace under a linear transformation is a subspace of the image space.
   - The kernel of a linear transformation is a subspace of the domain space.

In conclusion, Subspaces are an important concept in vector spaces that help us understand the structure of a vector space and its subsets. Knowing the properties and examples of subspaces, as well as their relationship with linear transformations, is crucial in the field of AI, ML, and Data Science.
