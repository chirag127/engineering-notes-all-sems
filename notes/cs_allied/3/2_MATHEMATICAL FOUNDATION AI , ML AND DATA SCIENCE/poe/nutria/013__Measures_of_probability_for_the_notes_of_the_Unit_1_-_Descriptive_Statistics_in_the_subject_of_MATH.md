
### Measures of Probability

1. Probability is a measure of the likelihood that a certain event will occur. It is expressed as a number between 0 and 1, where 0 indicates that the event is impossible and 1 indicates that the event is certain to occur.

2. The probability of an event can be calculated using the formula P(E) = n/N, where n is the number of outcomes favorable to the event and N is the total number of possible outcomes.

3. Probability theory is a branch of mathematics that deals with the analysis of random events. It is used to quantify the likelihood of certain outcomes in a variety of situations.

4. Probability distributions are used to describe how likely it is that a given event will occur. Commonly used probability distributions include the normal distribution, the binomial distribution, and the Poisson distribution.

5. Bayes' theorem is a mathematical theorem used to calculate the probability of an event given certain conditions. It is used in many areas of science and engineering, including machine learning and artificial intelligence.

6. The law of large numbers states that the average of a large number of independent and identically distributed random variables converges to its expected value. This is an important concept in probability theory and is used in many areas of science and engineering.
