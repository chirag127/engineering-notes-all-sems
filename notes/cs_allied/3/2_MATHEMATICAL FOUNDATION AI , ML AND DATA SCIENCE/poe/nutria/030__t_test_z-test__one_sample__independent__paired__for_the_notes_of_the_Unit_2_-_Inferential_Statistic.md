
### t-Test/z-Test (One Sample, Independent, Paired)

* A t-test is a type of inferential statistic used to determine if there is a significant difference between the means of two groups.
* The t-test is commonly used to determine if a sample mean is significantly different from a population mean.
* A one-sample t-test is used to compare the mean of a sample to a known population mean.
* An independent t-test is used to compare two independent samples.
* A paired t-test is used to compare two samples where each observation in one sample can be paired with an observation in the other sample.
* The z-test is a type of hypothesis test used to determine if a sample mean is significantly different from a population mean.
* The z-test is a special case of the t-test when the population standard deviation is known.
* The z-test is used when the sample size is large (more than 30 observations).
* The t-test and z-test are used to test the null hypothesis that there is no difference between the means of two groups.
