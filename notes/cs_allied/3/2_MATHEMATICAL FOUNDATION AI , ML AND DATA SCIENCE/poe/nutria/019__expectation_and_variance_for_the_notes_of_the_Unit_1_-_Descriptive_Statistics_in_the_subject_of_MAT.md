
### Expectation and Variance for Unit 1 - Descriptive Statistics in Mathematical Foundation AI, ML and Data Science

- Expectation is a measure of the central tendency of a random variable. It is the expected value of the random variable, and is calculated by taking the mean of all the possible outcomes.
- Variance is a measure of the spread of a random variable. It is the average of the squared differences of the values from the mean.
- The formula for expectation is: $$E[X] = \sum_{i=1}^n x_i p_i$$
- The formula for variance is: $$Var[X] = \sum_{i=1}^n (x_i - \mu)^2 p_i$$
- Where $$\mu = E[X]$$ is the expectation of the random variable, and $$p_i$$ is the probability of the ith outcome.
- The variance of a random variable can be used to measure the spread of the values, and can be used to compare different distributions.
- The expectation and variance of a random variable can be used to calculate the probability of certain outcomes. For example, the expected value of a Bernoulli random variable is the probability of success, and the variance is the probability of failure.
