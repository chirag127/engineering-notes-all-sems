
### Probability for the notes of the Unit 1 - Descriptive Statistics in the subject of MATHEMATICAL FOUNDATION AI , ML AND DATA SCIENCE

1. Probability is a measure of the likelihood of an event occurring. It is expressed as a number between 0 and 1, where 0 indicates impossibility and 1 indicates certainty. 
2. Probability theory is the branch of mathematics that deals with the analysis of random phenomena. 
3. Probability distributions are used to model the occurrence of random events. 
4. The most common probability distributions are the normal, binomial, and Poisson distributions. 
5. The Central Limit Theorem states that the sum of a large number of independent random variables is approximately normally distributed. 
6. Descriptive statistics are used to summarize and describe data. 
7. Measures of central tendency, such as the mean, median, and mode, are used to describe the center of a data set. 
8. Measures of dispersion, such as the range, variance, and standard deviation, are used to describe the spread of a data set. 
9. The correlation coefficient is used to measure the strength of the linear relationship between two variables. 
10. The chi-square test is used to test the independence of two categorical variables.
