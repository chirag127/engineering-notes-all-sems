
### Monte Carlo Integration for Unit 3 - Pseudo-Random Numbers in Mathematical Foundation AI, ML and Data Science

1. Monte Carlo Integration is a numerical method used to calculate integrals by randomly sampling points within the integration domain.

2. It is a useful tool to approximate integrals when analytical solutions are not available, or when the integrand is too complex to be solved analytically.

3. The basic idea behind Monte Carlo Integration is to randomly sample points within the integration domain, and use the average value of the sampled points to approximate the integral.

4. To use Monte Carlo Integration, a pseudo-random number generator must be used to generate the random points.

5. The accuracy of the integration depends on the number of random points used, and can be improved by increasing the number of points.

6. Monte Carlo Integration is a powerful tool for solving integrals in many areas of mathematics, including probability theory, numerical analysis, and statistical mechanics.
