
### Subspace for the Notes of the Unit 4 - Vector Spaces in the Subject of Mathematical Foundation AI, ML and Data Science

1. A subspace is a subset of a vector space which contains all the vectors that can be created through linear combinations of its elements. 
2. Subspaces are important in mathematics and computer science, as they allow us to work with smaller, more manageable sets of data. 
3. In vector spaces, a subspace is defined by a set of linearly independent vectors. 
4. These vectors span a subspace, which is the set of all linear combinations of the vectors. 
5. The dimension of a subspace is the number of linearly independent vectors that span it. 
6. Subspaces can be used to represent linear transformations and to solve systems of linear equations. 
7. The concept of subspaces is used in the study of linear algebra, machine learning, and data science.
