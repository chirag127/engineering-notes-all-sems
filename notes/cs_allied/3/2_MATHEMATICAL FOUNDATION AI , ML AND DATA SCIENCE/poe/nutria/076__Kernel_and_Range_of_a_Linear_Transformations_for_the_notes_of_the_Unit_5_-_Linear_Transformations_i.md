

### Kernel and Range of a Linear Transformations

* The kernel of a linear transformation is the set of all vectors in the domain of the transformation that are mapped to the zero vector.
* The range of a linear transformation is the set of all vectors in the codomain of the transformation that are mapped to by some vector in the domain.
* The kernel and range of a linear transformation are related in that the dimension of the kernel is equal to the dimension of the codomain minus the dimension of the range.
* A linear transformation is invertible if and only if its kernel is the zero vector, and its range is the entire codomain.
* The kernel and range of a linear transformation can be determined by solving a system of linear equations.
