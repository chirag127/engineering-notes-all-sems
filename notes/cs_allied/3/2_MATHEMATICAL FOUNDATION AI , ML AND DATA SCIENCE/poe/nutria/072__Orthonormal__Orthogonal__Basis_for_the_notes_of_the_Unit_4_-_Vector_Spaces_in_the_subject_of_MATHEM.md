
### Orthonormal (Orthogonal) Basis for the notes of the Unit 4 - Vector Spaces in the subject of MATHEMATICAL FOUNDATION AI , ML AND DATA SCIENCE

* Orthonormal basis is a set of vectors in a vector space which are mutually orthogonal and normalized. 
* Orthogonal vectors are vectors that are perpendicular to each other.
* Normalized vectors have a magnitude of 1.
* An orthonormal basis can be used to represent any vector in the space. 
* Any vector in a vector space can be expressed as a linear combination of the vectors in an orthonormal basis.
* Orthonormal bases are useful for solving linear equations and computing inner products.
* An orthonormal basis is also known as an orthogonal basis or an orthonormal coordinate system.
