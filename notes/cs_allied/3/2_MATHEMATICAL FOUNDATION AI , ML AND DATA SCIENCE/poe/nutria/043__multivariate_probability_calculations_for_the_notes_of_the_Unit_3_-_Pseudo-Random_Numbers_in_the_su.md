* Multivariate probability calculations are a way to calculate the probability of an event occurring when there are multiple variables involved.
* The most common approach to multivariate probability calculations is to use the law of total probability. This law states that the probability of an event occurring is the sum of the probabilities of each of the individual events.
* Another approach to multivariate probability calculations is to use Bayes' Theorem. This theorem states that the probability of an event occurring is the product of the probability of each of the individual events.

* Linear congruential generators generate pseudo-random numbers using a linear equation. These generators have a limited range of numbers and are not suitable for applications that require a large number of random numbers.
* Fibonacci generators generate pseudo-random numbers by using a sequence of Fibonacci numbers. These generators have a wider range of numbers and are suitable for applications that require a large number of random numbers.
* Mersenne Twister generators generate pseudo-random numbers by using a sequence of Mersenne Twister numbers. These generators have a large range of numbers and are suitable for applications that require a large number of random numbers.
