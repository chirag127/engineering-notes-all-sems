
### Inner Product for the Notes of Unit 4 - Vector Spaces in the Subject of Mathematical Foundation AI, ML and Data Science

* An inner product is a way of taking two vectors and combining them to create a single scalar value. It is a type of product operation, which is a mathematical operation that takes two vectors and produces a scalar value.
* Inner products are used in a variety of fields such as linear algebra, calculus, and machine learning.
* In linear algebra, inner products are used to define the length and angle between two vectors. They are also used to determine the orthogonality of two vectors.
* In calculus, inner products are used to calculate the integral of a function over a region in space.
* In machine learning, inner products are used to calculate the similarity between two vectors. This can be used to classify data points or to make predictions.
* Inner products can be defined in different ways, depending on the context. Common definitions include the dot product, the Euclidean inner product, and the cosine similarity.
* The dot product is the most commonly used inner product and is defined as the sum of the products of the corresponding elements of two vectors.
* The Euclidean inner product is defined as the sum of the squares of the elements of two vectors.
* The cosine similarity is defined as the cosine of the angle between two vectors.
