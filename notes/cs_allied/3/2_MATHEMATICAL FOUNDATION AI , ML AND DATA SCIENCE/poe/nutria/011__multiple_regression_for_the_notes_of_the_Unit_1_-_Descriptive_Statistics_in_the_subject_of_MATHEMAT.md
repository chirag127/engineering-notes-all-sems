
### Multiple Regression for Unit 1 - Descriptive Statistics

1. Multiple regression is a statistical technique that models the relationship between multiple independent variables and a single dependent variable.
2. It is used to identify and quantify the effect of multiple independent variables on the dependent variable, while controlling for other variables.
3. The most common form of multiple regression is linear regression, in which the dependent variable is assumed to have a linear relationship with the independent variables.
4. Multiple regression can also be used to identify non-linear relationships between the independent and dependent variables.
5. The coefficients of the independent variables in a multiple regression model can be used to measure the strength of the relationship between the independent and dependent variables.
6. Multiple regression can also be used to test hypotheses about the relationships between the independent and dependent variables.
7. The assumptions of multiple regression include linearity, homoscedasticity, and normality of the residuals.
8. The model can also be used to identify outliers and influential observations that may lead to biased results.
