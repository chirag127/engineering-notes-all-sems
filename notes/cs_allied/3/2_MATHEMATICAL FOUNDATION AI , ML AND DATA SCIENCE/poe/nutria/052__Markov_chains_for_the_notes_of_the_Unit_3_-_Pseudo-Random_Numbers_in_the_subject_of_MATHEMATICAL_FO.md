
### Markov Chains

Markov Chains are a type of stochastic process that can be used to generate pseudo-random numbers. They are composed of a set of states and a set of transitions between the states. Each transition has an associated probability, which determines the likelihood of the transition occurring.

The most common application of Markov Chains is in the generation of pseudo-random numbers. By defining a set of states and transitions, the Markov Chain can be used to generate a sequence of pseudo-random numbers.

Markov Chains can also be used to model real-world phenomena. For example, they can be used to model the spread of a virus, the stock market, or the movement of people in a city.

Markov Chains are an important tool for AI and Machine Learning, as they allow for the generation of pseudo-random numbers, which can be used to generate data for training algorithms. They can also be used to model real-world phenomena, allowing for the development of more accurate models.
