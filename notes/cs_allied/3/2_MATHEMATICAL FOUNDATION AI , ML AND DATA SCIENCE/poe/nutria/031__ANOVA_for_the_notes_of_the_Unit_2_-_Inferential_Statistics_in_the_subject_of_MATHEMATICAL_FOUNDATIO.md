
### ANOVA for the Notes of Unit 2 - Inferential Statistics in the Subject of Mathematical Foundation AI, ML, and Data Science

1. ANOVA stands for Analysis of Variance and is used to compare two or more groups to determine if there is a significant difference between them.

2. ANOVA is a parametric test, meaning that it assumes that the data follows a normal distribution.

3. ANOVA can be used to compare the means of two or more independent groups.

4. ANOVA tests the null hypothesis, which states that there is no significant difference between the means of the groups being compared.

5. If the null hypothesis is rejected, then the alternative hypothesis is accepted, which states that there is a significant difference between the means of the groups being compared.

6. The F-statistic is used to determine if the null hypothesis should be rejected.

7. The F-statistic is calculated by dividing the variance between the groups by the variance within the groups.

8. If the F-statistic is greater than the critical value, then the null hypothesis is rejected and the alternative hypothesis is accepted.

9. ANOVA is a powerful statistical tool that can be used to compare multiple groups with a single test.
