
### Chi-Square for the Notes of the Unit 2 - Inferential Statistics in the Subject of Mathematical Foundation AI, ML and Data Science

* Chi-square is a statistical test used to determine if there is a significant difference between the expected frequencies and the observed frequencies in one or more categories.
* The chi-square test is used to determine the relationship between two categorical variables.
* The chi-square test can be used to compare the observed frequencies with the expected frequencies in a two-way table.
* The chi-square test is used to determine if the two variables are independent or dependent.
* The chi-square test is used to determine the goodness of fit of a set of data to a theoretical distribution.
* The chi-square test is used to test hypotheses about population proportions.
* The chi-square test is used to test the hypothesis that two variables are independent.
* The chi-square test is used to test the hypothesis that two variables are related.
