
### Orthogonal Vectors for the Notes of the Unit 4 - Vector Spaces in the Subject of Mathematical Foundation AI, ML and Data Science

* Orthogonal vectors are two vectors that are perpendicular to each other. They have a dot product of zero and a magnitude of one.
* The concept of orthogonality is essential in linear algebra and vector spaces. It is used to solve linear equations, calculate the angle between two vectors, and define the dimensions of a vector space.
* Orthogonal vectors can be used to define a coordinate system. Any two vectors that are perpendicular to each other can be used to define the two axes of a coordinate system.
* Orthogonality is also used in linear transformations. A linear transformation is a mapping from one vector space to another. An orthogonal transformation is a linear transformation that preserves the angle between two vectors.
* Orthogonality is also used in signal processing. Orthogonal signals are signals that have a zero correlation. This means that they are uncorrelated and do not interfere with each other. Orthogonal signals can be used to transmit multiple signals through the same medium.
