
### Independent Event

1. An **independent event** is a type of event where the outcome of one event does not affect the outcome of another event.
2. In statistics, an independent event is used to calculate the probability of the occurrence of two or more events.
3. The probability of the occurrence of two independent events is calculated by multiplying the probability of each event.
4. For example, if the probability of event A is 0.4 and the probability of event B is 0.3, then the probability of both events occurring is 0.4 x 0.3 = 0.12.
5. Descriptive statistics is the branch of statistics that deals with the summarization and analysis of data.
6. Descriptive statistics can be used to describe the distribution of data, such as the mean, median, mode, and range.
7. Descriptive statistics can also be used to identify patterns in data, such as correlations and relationships between variables.
8. Descriptive statistics can also be used to compare and contrast different sets of data.
