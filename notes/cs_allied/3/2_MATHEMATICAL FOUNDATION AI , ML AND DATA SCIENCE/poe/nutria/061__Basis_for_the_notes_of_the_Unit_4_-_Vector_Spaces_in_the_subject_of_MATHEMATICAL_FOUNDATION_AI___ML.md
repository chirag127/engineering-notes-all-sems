
### Basis for the Notes of Unit 4 - Vector Spaces in the Subject of Mathematical Foundation AI, ML and Data Science

1. Vector Spaces: Vector spaces are mathematical objects consisting of a set of vectors, which are objects that can be added together and multiplied by scalars. Vector spaces are used in many areas of mathematics, including linear algebra, calculus, and topology.

2. Linear Independence: A set of vectors is said to be linearly independent if no vector in the set can be expressed as a linear combination of the other vectors in the set.

3. Spanning Sets: A set of vectors is said to span a vector space if any vector in the vector space can be expressed as a linear combination of the vectors in the set.

4. Basis: A basis for a vector space is a set of vectors that are linearly independent and span the vector space.

5. Orthogonality: Two vectors are said to be orthogonal if their dot product is equal to zero.

6. Orthonormal Bases: An orthonormal basis is a basis for a vector space in which the vectors are orthogonal to each other and have unit length.

7. Inner Product Spaces: An inner product space is a vector space equipped with an inner product, which is a function that takes two vectors and produces a scalar.

8. Orthogonal Projections: An orthogonal projection is a linear transformation that takes a vector and projects it onto a subspace of the vector space.
