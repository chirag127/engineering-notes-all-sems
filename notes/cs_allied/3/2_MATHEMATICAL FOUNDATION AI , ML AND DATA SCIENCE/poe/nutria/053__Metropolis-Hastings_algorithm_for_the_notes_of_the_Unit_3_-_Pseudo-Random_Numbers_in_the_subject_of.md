
### Metropolis-Hastings Algorithm

Metropolis-Hastings algorithm is a Markov chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) algorithm used to generate samples from a probability distribution. It is used in a variety of fields, including machine learning, artificial intelligence, and data science.

The algorithm works by taking a step in a random direction and then accepting or rejecting the step based on the probability of the new state. The probability of acceptance is based on a comparison between the current state and the new one. The algorithm is iterative, meaning that it repeats these steps until it reaches a desired sample size.

The algorithm is useful for generating samples from complex distributions, such as those used in machine learning and artificial intelligence. It can also be used to sample from distributions with unknown parameters.

The algorithm consists of two parts: the Metropolis-Hastings step and the acceptance probability. The Metropolis-Hastings step is a random walk in which the current state is compared to the proposed state and a decision is made on whether to accept or reject the proposed state. The acceptance probability is a function of the current and proposed states, and is used to determine the probability of acceptance.
