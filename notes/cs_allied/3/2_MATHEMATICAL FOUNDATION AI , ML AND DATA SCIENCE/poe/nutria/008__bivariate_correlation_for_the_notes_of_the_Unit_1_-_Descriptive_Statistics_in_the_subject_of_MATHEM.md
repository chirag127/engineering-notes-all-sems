
### Bivariate Correlation for Unit 1 - Descriptive Statistics in MATHEMATICAL FOUNDATION AI, ML AND DATA SCIENCE

1. Bivariate correlation is a measure of the relationship between two variables. It is used to determine if a linear relationship exists between two variables.

2. The correlation coefficient is a measure of the strength of the linear relationship between two variables. A value of 1 indicates a perfect positive correlation, while a value of -1 indicates a perfect negative correlation.

3. Correlation coefficients can range from -1 to 1. A value of 0 indicates no linear relationship between the two variables.

4. Correlation coefficients can be calculated using the Pearson product-moment correlation coefficient, the Spearman rank-order correlation coefficient, or the Kendall rank-order correlation coefficient.

5. Correlation coefficients are used to assess the strength and direction of the linear relationship between two variables.

6. It is important to note that correlation does not imply causation. Correlation simply indicates that two variables are related, but does not indicate which variable is causing the relationship.
