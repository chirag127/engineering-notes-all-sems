
### Inverse-transform for the Notes of Unit 3 - Pseudo-Random Numbers in the Subject of Mathematical Foundation AI, ML and Data Science

1. An inverse-transform is a method used to generate pseudo-random numbers from a given probability distribution.

2. The basic idea is to use the cumulative distribution function (CDF) of the desired distribution to generate random numbers.

3. The CDF is a function that gives the probability of a random variable taking a value less than or equal to a given value.

4. The inverse-transform method works by generating a random number between 0 and 1, and then using the CDF to find the corresponding random variable.

5. The inverse-transform method is simple and efficient, and can be used to generate pseudo-random numbers from any given probability distribution.

6. However, it is limited to distributions whose CDF is known or can be calculated.
