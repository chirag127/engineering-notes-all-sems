
### Multiple Regression Analysis

Multiple regression analysis is a statistical technique used to model the relationship between multiple independent variables and a single dependent variable. It is used to understand the impact of multiple independent variables on a single dependent variable.

1. Definition: Multiple regression analysis is a statistical technique used to model the relationship between multiple independent variables and a single dependent variable. 
2. Purpose: The purpose of multiple regression analysis is to understand the impact of multiple independent variables on a single dependent variable.
3. Assumptions: Multiple regression analysis assumes that the independent variables are linearly related to the dependent variable, that the errors are normally distributed, and that there is no multicollinearity among the independent variables.
4. Application: Multiple regression analysis is used in various fields such as finance, economics, psychology, and marketing to understand the relationship between independent variables and a dependent variable.
5. Methodology: Multiple regression analysis uses the least squares method to estimate the parameters of the model. The least squares method minimizes the sum of the squared errors between the observed values and the predicted values.
6. Interpretation: The interpretation of multiple regression analysis involves understanding the coefficients of the model, the statistical significance of the coefficients, and the overall fit of the model.
