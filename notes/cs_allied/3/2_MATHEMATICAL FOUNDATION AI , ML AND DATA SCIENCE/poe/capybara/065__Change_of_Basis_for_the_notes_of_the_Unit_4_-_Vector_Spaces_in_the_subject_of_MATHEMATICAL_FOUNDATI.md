### Change of Basis

Vector spaces are an essential concept in mathematics that are used in many fields such as Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Data Science. In this unit, we will dive deep into the concepts of vector spaces and learn about the change of basis.

Here are some key points to understand about the change of basis:

- In a vector space, a basis is a set of vectors that can be used to represent any other vector in the space. 
- Changing the basis means changing the set of vectors used to represent the vectors in the space.
- The change of basis is represented by a matrix called the transition matrix. 
- To change the basis, we need to multiply the vector by the transition matrix. 
- The transition matrix is a square matrix whose columns are composed of the coefficients of the new basis vectors expressed in terms of the old basis vectors.
- The transition matrix is invertible, meaning that we can easily switch back to the old basis by multiplying the vector by the inverse of the transition matrix.
- Changing the basis of a vector does not change its length or direction, only its representation in terms of the new basis vectors.

In summary, the change of basis is a fundamental concept in vector spaces that allows us to represent vectors in different ways. By understanding the concepts of the change of basis, we can solve many problems in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Data Science.
