### Cauchy-Schwarz Inequality

The Cauchy-Schwarz Inequality is an important concept in linear algebra and is used extensively in various fields of mathematics, including AI, ML, and data science. 

Here are some key points to keep in mind:

- The Cauchy-Schwarz Inequality states that for any two vectors, their dot product is less than or equal to the product of their magnitudes.

- The Cauchy-Schwarz Inequality is important in various applications, including optimization problems, signal processing, and probability theory.
- In AI and ML, the Cauchy-Schwarz Inequality is used in various algorithms, such as support vector machines and principal component analysis.
- The inequality has various extensions and generalizations, such as the Cauchy-Bunyakovsky-Schwarz Inequality and the Holder's Inequality.
