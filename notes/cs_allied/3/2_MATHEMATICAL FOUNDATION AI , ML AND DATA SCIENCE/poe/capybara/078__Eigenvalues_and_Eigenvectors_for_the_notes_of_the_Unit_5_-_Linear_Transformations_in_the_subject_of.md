### Eigenvalues and Eigenvectors

In linear algebra, Eigenvalues and Eigenvectors are important concepts that are used to study linear transformations. In this unit, we will learn about Eigenvalues and Eigenvectors and their applications in solving problems related to linear transformations.

Here are some key points to keep in mind while studying Eigenvalues and Eigenvectors:

- Eigenvalues and Eigenvectors are used to study linear transformations in which the transformation of a vector is proportional to the vector itself, i.e., the transformed vector is a scalar multiple of the original vector.
- An Eigenvector is a non-zero vector that is transformed only by a scalar factor when a linear transformation is applied to it. The scalar factor is called the Eigenvalue.
- The Eigenvalues and Eigenvectors of a matrix are important in solving problems related to linear transformations, such as finding the direction of the transformation and the rate of the transformation.
- The Eigenvectors of a matrix are linearly independent, which means that they form a basis for the vector space.
- The Eigenvalues of a matrix can be found by solving the characteristic equation of the matrix. The characteristic equation is obtained by subtracting the identity matrix multiplied by the Eigenvalue from the original matrix and then taking the determinant.
- The Eigenvectors of a matrix can be found by solving the system of linear equations obtained by setting the matrix multiplied by the Eigenvector equal to the Eigenvalue multiplied by the Eigenvector.
- The Eigenvectors of a matrix can also be used to diagonalize a matrix, which means that the matrix is transformed into a diagonal matrix by a change of basis.

In conclusion, Eigenvalues and Eigenvectors are important concepts in linear algebra that are used to study linear transformations. They have many applications in solving problems related to linear transformations, and their understanding is essential for anyone studying linear algebra.
