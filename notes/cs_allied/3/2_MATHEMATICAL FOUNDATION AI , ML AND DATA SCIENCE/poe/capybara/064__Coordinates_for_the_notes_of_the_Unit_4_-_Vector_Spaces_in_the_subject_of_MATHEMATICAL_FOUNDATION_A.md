### Coordinates for the notes of the Unit 4 - Vector Spaces in the subject of MATHEMATICAL FOUNDATION AI , ML AND DATA SCIENCE

Here are the key points to keep in mind when studying the coordinates for the notes of Unit 4 - Vector Spaces:

- In vector spaces, we use coordinates to represent vectors as tuples of numbers. These numbers are called the coordinates of the vector, and they tell us how much of each basis vector is needed to create the vector.
- The coordinates of a vector depend on the choice of basis. A different basis will give us different coordinates for the same vector.
- To find the coordinates of a vector with respect to a given basis, we need to express the vector as a linear combination of the basis vectors and then read off the coefficients. These coefficients are the coordinates of the vector.
- The process of finding the coordinates of a vector is called coordinate representation or coordinate transformation.
- In a finite-dimensional vector space, the dimension is the number of basis vectors. Any set of linearly independent vectors can be extended to form a basis, and any spanning set can be reduced to a basis.
- The standard basis for R^n is the set of n unit vectors along the coordinate axes. Any vector in R^n can be written as a linear combination of these vectors, and the coefficients are the coordinates of the vector.
- The change of basis formula tells us how to convert coordinates from one basis to another. It involves computing the matrix of the change of basis transformation and multiplying it by the column vector of coordinates in the old basis.
- The matrix of the change of basis transformation has the old basis vectors as columns and the new basis vectors as rows. Its inverse is the matrix that converts coordinates from the new basis to the old basis.
- The change of basis formula is useful for solving problems involving vector spaces, linear transformations, and matrices. It allows us to work with vectors and matrices in different bases and compare results.
