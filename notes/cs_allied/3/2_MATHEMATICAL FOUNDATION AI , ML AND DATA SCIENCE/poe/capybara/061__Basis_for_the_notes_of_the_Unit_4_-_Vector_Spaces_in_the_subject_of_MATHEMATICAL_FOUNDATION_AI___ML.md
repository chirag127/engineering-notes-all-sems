### Basis for the notes of the Unit 4 - Vector Spaces in the subject of MATHEMATICAL FOUNDATION AI , ML AND DATA SCIENCE

Vector spaces are an essential concept in mathematics and have many applications in AI, ML, and data science. In this unit, we will cover the basis of vector spaces.

Here are the key points to keep in mind:

- A vector space is a set of vectors that satisfy certain axioms, such as closure under addition and scalar multiplication.
- The dimension of a vector space is the number of vectors in a basis for that space. A basis is a set of linearly independent vectors that span the space.
- Linear combinations of vectors in a basis can represent any vector in the space.
- The span of a set of vectors is the set of all linear combinations of those vectors. The span of a basis for a vector space is the entire space.
- A subspace of a vector space is a subset that is also a vector space under the same operations.
- The intersection of two subspaces is also a subspace.
- The sum of two subspaces is the set of all vectors that can be expressed as the sum of a vector from each subspace. The sum of two subspaces is also a subspace.
- A linear transformation is a function that maps vectors from one vector space to another while preserving certain properties, such as linearity.
- The matrix representation of a linear transformation depends on the choice of bases for the two vector spaces involved.

Understanding vector spaces is crucial for many concepts in AI, ML, and data science, such as dimensionality reduction, clustering, and linear regression. It is important to master the basics of vector spaces to be able to apply these concepts effectively.

In summary, this unit covers the foundation of vector spaces, including the definition of vector spaces, bases, linear combinations, spans, subspaces, and linear transformations. It is essential to understand these concepts to apply them effectively in AI, ML, and data science.
