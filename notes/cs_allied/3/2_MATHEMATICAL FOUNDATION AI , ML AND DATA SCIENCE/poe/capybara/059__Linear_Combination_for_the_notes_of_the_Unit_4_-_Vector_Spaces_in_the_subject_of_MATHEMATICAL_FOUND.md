### Linear Combination for the notes of the Unit 4 - Vector Spaces in the subject of MATHEMATICAL FOUNDATION AI , ML AND DATA SCIENCE

Vector spaces form the basis of various mathematical concepts and real-world applications in AI, ML, and data science. In this unit, we will discuss linear combinations, an essential concept in vector spaces.

Here are the key points to understand about linear combinations:

- A linear combination is the sum of scalar multiples of vectors.
- Let V be a vector space, and let v1, v2, ..., vn be vectors in V. Then, any vector v in V can be expressed as a linear combination of the vectors v1, v2, ..., vn, i.e., v = a1v1 + a2v2 + ... + anvn, where a1, a2, ..., an are scalars.
- The scalars a1, a2, ..., an are called the coefficients of the linear combination.
- Linear combinations are closed under addition and scalar multiplication, which means that the sum of two linear combinations of vectors is also a linear combination of vectors, and multiplying a linear combination by a scalar also results in a linear combination of vectors.
- A set of vectors is said to be linearly independent if no vector in the set can be expressed as a linear combination of the other vectors in the set. Otherwise, the set is linearly dependent.

Understanding linear combinations is crucial in various applications, such as finding the solution to a system of linear equations, determining the span of a set of vectors, and understanding the geometry of vector spaces.

In conclusion, linear combinations are a fundamental concept in vector spaces, and understanding them is essential for anyone working in the field of AI, ML, and data science.
