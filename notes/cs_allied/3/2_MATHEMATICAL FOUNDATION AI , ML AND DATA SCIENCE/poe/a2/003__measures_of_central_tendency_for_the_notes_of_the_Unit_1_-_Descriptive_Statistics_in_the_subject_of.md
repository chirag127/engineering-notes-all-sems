### Measures of Central Tendency

- Mean: Sum of all values divided by total number of values. Calculates the central point of the data. Sensitive to outliers.
- Median: Middle value in a sorted list of values. Not sensitive to outliers. Useful for skewed data.
- Mode: Most frequent value in the data. Useful for categorical variables.

To summarize the measures of central tendency:

- Mean: Average, central point. Sensitive to outliers.
- Median: Middle value, not sensitive to outliers. Useful for skewed data.
- Mode: Most frequent value. Useful for categorical variables.

Choose the measure based on the characteristics of the data and the analysis objective. Mean is the most commonly used measure but median and mode are also useful in certain scenarios.
