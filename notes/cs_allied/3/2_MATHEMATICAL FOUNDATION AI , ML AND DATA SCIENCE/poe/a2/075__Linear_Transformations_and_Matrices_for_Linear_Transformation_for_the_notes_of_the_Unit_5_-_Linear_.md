### Linear Transformations and Matrices for Linear Transformation

1. A linear transformation is a function that transforms vectors into vectors while preserving the vector addition and scalar multiplication. In other words, it maps linear combinations to linear combinations.
2. The standard form of a linear transformation between two vector spaces V and W is:
T : V → W
3. The matrix of a linear transformation is a matrix A such that T(v) = Av for all v in V. The number of columns of A equals the dimension of V and the number of rows of A equals the dimension of W.
4. If V and W have the same dimension, then the matrix of a linear transformation is a square matrix. In this case, the matrix completely characterizes the linear transformation.
5. Two linear transformations T : V → W and S : V → W are equal if and only if they have the same matrix with respect to the standard bases of V and W.
6. The composition of two linear transformations is linear and the matrix of the composition is the product of the matrices of the individual transformations.
7. The inverse of a linear transformation is linear and its matrix is the inverse of the matrix of the original transformation. The inverse exists if and only if the matrix is invertible.
