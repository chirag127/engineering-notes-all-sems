## Unit 1 - Descriptive Statistics

1. Introduction
- Descriptive statistics refers to the analysis of data that summarizes and organizes the data in a meaningful way.
- It helps us understand the shape, distribution, variability, pattern, and key features of the data.
- Some common descriptive statistics include mean, median, mode, standard deviation, variance, percentiles, etc.

2. Measures of Central Tendency
- Mean: Sum of all values divided by number of values. Appropriate for normally distributed data.
- Median: Middle value in a sorted list of values. Appropriate for skewed data.
- Mode: Most frequently occurring value.

3. Measures of Dispersion
- Range: Difference between maximum and minimum value.
- Variance: Average squared deviation from the mean.
- Standard Deviation: Square root of variance. Measure of spread or variability.
