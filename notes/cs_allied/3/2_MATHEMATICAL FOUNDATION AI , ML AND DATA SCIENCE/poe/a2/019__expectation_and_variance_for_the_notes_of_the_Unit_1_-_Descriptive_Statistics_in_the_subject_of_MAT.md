### Expectation and Variance

Notes for Unit 1 - Descriptive Statistics

- Expectation: The expected value (or mean) of a random variable X is a weighted average of the possible values that X can take, where the weights are the probabilities of those values occurring.
- Variance: The variance of a random variable measures the spread or dispersion of the probability distribution. It is the square of the standard deviation and calculates the average squared deviation from the mean. A high variance indicates that the data is spread out over a wider range of values, while a low variance indicates that the data is clustered closely around the mean.
- Formulae:
Expectation: E(X) = ∑xP(X=x)
Variance: Var(X) = E[(X - μ)2] = ∑(x - μ)2P(X=x)
Where μ is the mean (expectation) of X.
