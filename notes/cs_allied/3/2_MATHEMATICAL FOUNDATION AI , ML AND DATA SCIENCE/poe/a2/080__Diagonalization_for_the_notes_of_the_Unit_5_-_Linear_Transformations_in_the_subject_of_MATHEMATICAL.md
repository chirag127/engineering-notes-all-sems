### Diagonalization for the notes of the Unit 5 - Linear Transformations in the subject of MATHEMATICAL FOUNDATION AI , ML AND DATA SCIENCE

1. A linear transformation T from a vector space V to itself is called diagonalizable if there exists a basis of V consisting of eigenvectors of T.
2. A diagonalizable transformation can be represented by a diagonal matrix in the basis of eigenvectors. The eigenvalues are the diagonal elements.
3. The matrix representation of a linear transformation is diagonalizable if and only if it has n linearly independent eigenvectors.
4. If T is diagonalizable, then T^k is diagonalizable for every positive integer k and (T^k)^i = T^(ki) for all positive integers i and k.
5. The eigenvalues of a diagonalizable linear transformation are the diagonal elements of its matrix with respect to any eigenvector basis. The multiplicity of an eigenvalue is the same for all such bases.
6. A linear transformation is diagonalizable if and only if its minimal polynomial is a product of linear polynomials. The degree of each linear factor is the multiplicity of the corresponding eigenvalue.
