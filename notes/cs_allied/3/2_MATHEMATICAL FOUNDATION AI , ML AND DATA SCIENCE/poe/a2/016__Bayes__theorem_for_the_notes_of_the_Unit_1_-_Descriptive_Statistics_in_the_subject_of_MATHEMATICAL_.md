### Bayes' Theorem

- Bayes' theorem is a mathematical formula that describes the probability of an event, based on prior knowledge of conditions that might be related to the event.
- It is stated as:
P(A|B) = P(B|A) x P(A) / P(B)
Where:
- P(A|B) is the posterior probability of A given B
- P(B|A) is the likelihood which is the probability of B given A
- P(A) is the prior probability of A
- P(B) is the prior probability of B
- Bayes' theorem is useful in calculating updated probabilities when new evidence is introduced. It allows you to update probabilities as you get more information.
- For example, if there is a 80% chance of rain (prior probability), and a weather forecast says there is a 70% chance of rain (likelihood), Bayes' theorem can be used to calculate the updated probability of rain (posterior probability).
- Bayes' theorem is widely used in machine learning and artificial intelligence for classification tasks. It allows algorithms to learn and improve their accuracy as they get exposed to more data.
