### Change of Basis for the notes of the Unit 4 - Vector Spaces in the subject of MATHEMATICAL FOUNDATION AI , ML AND DATA SCIENCE

1. Given a vector space V over a field F, a basis for V is a finite set of vectors that span V and are linearly independent.
2. Two bases for the same vector space may have a different number of elements and the elements themselves may be different vectors.
3. A change of basis is a process of expressing vectors (and other objects) from one basis to another basis of the same vector space.
4. If {v1, v2, ..., vk} and {w1, w2, ..., wk} are two bases for a vector space V, and v = a1v1 + a2v2 + ... + akvk for some scalars a1, a2, ..., ak, then there exist unique scalars b1, b2, ..., bk such that w = b1w1 + b2w2 + ... + bkwk.
5. The process of finding b1, b2, ..., bk given a1, a2, ..., ak is called a change of basis. The scalars (b1, b2, ..., bk) are called the coordinates of the vector w with respect to the basis {w1, w2, ..., wk}.
