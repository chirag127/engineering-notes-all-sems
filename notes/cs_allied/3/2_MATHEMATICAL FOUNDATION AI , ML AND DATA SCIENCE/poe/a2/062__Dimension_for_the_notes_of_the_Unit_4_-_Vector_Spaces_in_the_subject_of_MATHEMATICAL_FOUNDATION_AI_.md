### Dimension

- Dimension of a vector space V is the minimum number of vectors required to span V.
- Basis of a vector space is a set of linearly independent vectors that spans the vector space. Basis contains minimum number of vectors.
- Every vector space has a basis.
- Two bases of a vector space must have same number of elements. This common number is the dimension of the vector space.
- Zero vector space has dimension 0.
- The dimension of Rn is n.
- If W is a subspace of V, then dim(W) <= dim(V).
- If dim(W) = dim(V), then W = V.
- Direct sum of vector spaces is the smallest vector space containing the given vector spaces. Its dimension is the sum of dimensions of the vector spaces.
