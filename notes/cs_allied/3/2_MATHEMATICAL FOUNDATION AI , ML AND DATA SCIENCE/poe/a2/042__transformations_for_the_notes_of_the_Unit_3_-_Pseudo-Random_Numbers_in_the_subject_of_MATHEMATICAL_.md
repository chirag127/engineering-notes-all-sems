### Transformations for the notes of the Unit 3 - Pseudo-Random Numbers

1. Linear Transformation: It involves scaling and shifting the numbers in a random sequence. For example, multiplying each number in the sequence by a constant a and then adding another constant b. This produces a new random sequence.
2. Non-Linear Transformation: It involves more complex transformations on the random numbers like exponentiation, logarithms, trigonometric functions, etc. For example, calculating the square or cube of each number in the random sequence. This produces a new random sequence with different properties.
3. Permutation: It involves reordering or shuffling the random numbers in the sequence. This produces a new random sequence with the same elements but in a different order.
4. Combining Multiple Transformations: We can apply a combination of the linear, non-linear and permutation transformations on a random number sequence to get a new random sequence with desired properties. The transformations can be applied sequentially or simultaneously on the numbers.

The transformations help in controlling and modifying the properties of pseudo-random number sequences as per the requirements. They are useful for various applications of random numbers like simulations, sampling, machine learning, etc.
