### Probability for the notes of the Unit 1 - Descriptive Statistics in the subject of MATHEMATICAL FOUNDATION AI , ML AND DATA SCIENCE

1. Probability is a measure of the likelihood that an event will occur. It is quantified as a number between 0 and 1.
2. The probability of an impossible event is 0. The probability of a certain event is 1.
3. Probabilities can be expressed as proportions, fractions, decimals or percentages.
4. The basic rules of probability are:
- The probability of an event is between 0 and 1.
- The probability that at least one of two or more mutually exclusive events will occur is the sum of their probabilities.
- The probability that two or more events will all occur is the product of their probabilities.
5. Conditional probability is the probability that an event occurs given that another event has already occurred. It is written as P(A|B) and is calculated using the formula:
P(A|B) = P(A and B) / P(B)
