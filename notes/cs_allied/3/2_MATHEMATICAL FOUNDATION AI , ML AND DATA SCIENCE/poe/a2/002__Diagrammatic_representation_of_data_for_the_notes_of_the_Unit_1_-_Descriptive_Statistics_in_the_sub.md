### Diagrammatic representation of data for the notes of the Unit 1 - Descriptive Statistics in the subject of MATHEMATICAL FOUNDATION AI , ML AND DATA SCIENCE

1. Histogram: Used to visualize the distribution of data. Shows the shape of the distribution (symmetric, skewed, etc.). Divides the x-axis into intervals and draws bars to show the number of data points that fall into each interval.
2. Box plot: Displays quartiles and outliers of a dataset. A box is drawn from the first quartile to the third quartile. The median is marked with a line inside the box. Outliers are marked with dots. Helps visualize skewness and detect outliers.
3. Bar plot: Compares the quantities of different categories of data. Uses rectangular bars to represent the quantities. The height of each bar corresponds to the quantity of the category. Useful for categorical data.
4. Scatter plot: Shows the relationship between two quantitative variables plotted on x and y axes. Each data point is represented by a dot. Pattern of the dots reveals correlation (positive, negative, or none) and other relationships.
5. Pie chart: Represents data as sectors of a circle. The size of each sector shows the proportion of the category compared to the whole. Useful for representing percentages and comparing categories.
