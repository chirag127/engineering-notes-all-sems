### Inner Product Spaces for the notes of the Unit 4 - Vector Spaces in the subject of MATHEMATICAL FOUNDATION AI , ML AND DATA SCIENCE

Inner product spaces are mathematical structures that generalize the concept of dot products. They are used to define the notion of angle and length in a vector space. 

An inner product space is a vector space V over a field F, equipped with a function (called an inner product) that maps pairs of vectors to scalars, denoted as <u,v>. The inner product must satisfy the following properties:

1. Bilinearity: The inner product is bilinear, meaning that <au + bv, w> = a<u,w> + b<v,w> for all scalars a, b and vectors u, v, w in V.

2. Conjugate Symmetry: The inner product is conjugate symmetric, meaning that <u,v> = <v,u>* for all vectors u, v in V, where * denotes complex conjugation.

3. Positive Definiteness: The inner product is positive definite, meaning that <v,v> > 0 for all non-zero vectors v in V.

Inner product spaces are used in many areas of mathematics and computer science, including linear algebra, optimization, and machine learning. They are particularly useful for defining distance and similarity measures, as well as for solving optimization problems.
