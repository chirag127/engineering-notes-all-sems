### expectation and variance for the notes of the Unit 1 - Descriptive Statistics in the subject of MATHEMATICAL FOUNDATION AI , ML AND DATA SCIENCE

Expectation and variance are two important concepts in probability and statistics.

Expectation, also known as the mean or average, is a measure of the central tendency of a random variable. It is defined as the weighted average of all possible values of the random variable, where the weights are given by the probabilities of each value. The expectation is often used to describe the average behavior of a random variable.

Variance is a measure of the spread or dispersion of a random variable. It is defined as the expected value of the squared deviation of the random variable from its mean. The variance provides information about how much the values of the random variable deviate from its mean.

In summary, expectation and variance are two important concepts in probability and statistics that provide information about the central tendency and dispersion of a random variable, respectively.
