t-test/z-test in Inferential Statistics:
- t-test and z-test are statistical tests used to compare the mean of a sample to a known or hypothesized value.
- There are three types of t-tests/z-tests:
  1. One-sample t-test/z-test: used to compare the mean of a sample to a known value.
  2. Independent samples t-test/z-test: used to compare the means of two independent samples.
  3. Paired samples t-test/z-test: used to compare the means of two related or paired samples.
- t-tests and z-tests are used to make inferences about population parameters based on sample data.
- Understanding t-tests and z-tests is important for the analysis and interpretation of data and for the development of data-driven solutions to real-world problems.
