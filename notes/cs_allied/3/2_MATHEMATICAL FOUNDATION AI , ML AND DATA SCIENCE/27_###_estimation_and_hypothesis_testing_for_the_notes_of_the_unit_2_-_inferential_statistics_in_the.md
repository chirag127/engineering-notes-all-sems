Estimation and Hypothesis Testing in Inferential Statistics:
- Estimation and Hypothesis Testing are two important concepts in inferential statistics.
- Estimation refers to the process of using a sample of data to make inferences about a population.
- Hypothesis Testing refers to the process of testing a claim or hypothesis about a population using a sample of data.
- Estimation and Hypothesis Testing are used to make inferences about populations based on sample data, and to make decisions about populations based on the results of these inferences.
- Topics covered in Unit 2 may include:
  1. Point estimation: the process of using a sample of data to estimate a population parameter.
  2. Interval estimation: the process of using a sample of data to estimate a range of values for a population parameter.
  3. Hypothesis testing: the process of testing a claim or hypothesis about a population using a sample of data.
  4. Type I and Type II errors: errors that can occur in hypothesis testing.
- Understanding estimation and hypothesis testing is important for the analysis and interpretation of data and for the development of data-driven solutions to real-world problems.
