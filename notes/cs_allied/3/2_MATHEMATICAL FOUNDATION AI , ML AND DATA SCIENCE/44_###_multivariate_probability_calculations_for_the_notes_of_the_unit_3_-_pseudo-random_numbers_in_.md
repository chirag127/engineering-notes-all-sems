Multivariate Probability Calculations in Mathematical Foundation for AI, ML, and Data Science:
- Multivariate probability calculations refer to the calculation of probabilities for multiple variables.
- In multivariate probability, the relationships between variables can be modeled using joint distributions, marginal distributions, and conditional distributions.
- Joint distributions describe the probability of multiple events occurring together.
- Marginal distributions describe the probability of a single event occurring, given the occurrence of one or more other events.
- Conditional distributions describe the probability of an event occurring, given the occurrence of one or more other events.
- Understanding multivariate probability is important for the analysis and interpretation of data and for the development of data-driven solutions to real-world problems.
