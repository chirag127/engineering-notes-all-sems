Bivariate Correlation

Bivariate correlation refers to the relationship between two variables. The goal of bivariate correlation is to determine if there is a relationship between two variables, and to quantify the strength and direction of that relationship.

Bivariate correlation can be measured using a variety of techniques, including Pearson's correlation coefficient, Spearman's rank correlation coefficient, and Kendall's tau. Pearson's correlation coefficient measures the linear relationship between two variables, while Spearman's rank correlation coefficient measures the monotonic relationship between two variables. Kendall's tau measures the ordinal relationship between two variables.

Bivariate correlation can be represented graphically using a scatter plot, which is a plot of the values of two variables. A scatter plot can be used to visually inspect the relationship between two variables, and to identify any outliers or patterns in the data.
