Probability is the measure of the likelihood of an event occurring.

1. Definition: Probability is the measure of the likelihood of an event occurring, expressed as a number between 0 and 1. An event with probability 0 is impossible, while an event with probability 1 is certain.

2. Probability Axioms: The study of probability is based on three axioms: (1) the probability of any event is a non-negative number, (2) the probability of the sample space (the set of all possible outcomes) is 1, and (3) the probability of the union of two disjoint events is the sum of their individual probabilities.

3. Conditional Probability: Conditional probability is the probability of an event occurring given that another event has already occurred. It is calculated as the probability of the joint event divided by the probability of the given event.

4. Bayes' Theorem: Bayes' theorem is a mathematical formula that relates the conditional probability of an event to the prior probability of the event and the likelihood ratio of the event.

5. Random Variables: A random variable is a variable that takes on a value based on the outcome of a random event. Probability is used to describe the distribution of a random variable, which is a function that maps the possible values of the random variable to their probabilities.

6. Discrete and Continuous Probability Distributions: Probability distributions can be either discrete or continuous. A discrete probability distribution is used to describe the distribution of a discrete random variable, while a continuous probability distribution is used to describe the distribution of a continuous random variable.

7. Advantages: Probability has several advantages, including the ability to model and analyze random events, the ability to make predictions about future events, and the ability to make decisions based on uncertain information.

8. Limitations: Despite its advantages, probability also has several limitations, including the difficulty of accurately modeling complex and highly uncertain events, the difficulty of accurately estimating probabilities, and the difficulty of accurately modeling interdependent events.

9. Applications: Probability is used in a wide range of applications, including finance, engineering, and the natural sciences. Probability is also used in fields such as machine learning, data science, and artificial intelligence.
