Markov Chains in Mathematical Foundation for AI, ML, and Data Science:
- Markov chains are a type of mathematical model used to describe the evolution of a system over time.
- A Markov chain is a sequence of states in which the probability of moving from one state to another depends only on the current state, not on the history of the system.
- Markov chains can be used to model a variety of real-world systems, including financial markets, weather patterns, and computer algorithms.
- Markov chains can be used to make predictions about the future state of a system, and to evaluate the long-term behavior of a system.
- Understanding Markov chains is important for the analysis and interpretation of data and for the development of data-driven solutions to real-world problems.
