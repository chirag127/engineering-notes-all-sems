# Probability

- Probability is a measure of the likelihood of a statement or a theoretical expectation is correct.
- Probability is also the measure of chances for the occurrence of some event or the estimation of uncertainty of any event.
- Probability is denoted by the symbol P and ranges from 0 to 1, where 0 means impossible and 1 means certain.
- For example, when we flip a coin in the air, what is the possibility of getting a head? The answer to this question is based on the number of possible outcomes.
- The number of possible outcomes is called the sample space and is denoted by S. For a coin, the sample space is S = {H, T}, where H stands for head and T stands for tail.
- The number of favorable outcomes is called the event and is denoted by E. For getting a head, the event is E = {H}.
- The probability of an event E is calculated by the formula:

P(E) = (number of favorable outcomes) / (number of possible outcomes)

- In the coin example, P(E) = 1 / 2 = 0.5, which means there is a 50% chance of getting a head.
- There are different types of probability, such as theoretical probability, experimental probability, conditional probability, and joint probability, which will be discussed in the following units.
