### Gibbs sampling

- Gibbs sampling is a Markov chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) algorithm for obtaining a sequence of observations which are approximated from a specified multivariate probability distribution, when direct sampling is difficult .
- Gibbs sampling is based on the idea of sampling from the conditional distributions of each variable given the current values of the other variables, and iterating this process until convergence .
- Gibbs sampling can be used as a means of statistical inference, especially Bayesian inference, where it can be used to approximate the posterior distribution of unknown parameters or latent variables  .
- Gibbs sampling has some advantages over other MCMC methods, such as simplicity, ease of implementation, and convergence properties .
- Gibbs sampling also has some limitations, such as dependence on the initial values, slow mixing, and difficulty in assessing convergence .
