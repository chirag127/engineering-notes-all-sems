### Orthogonal Vectors

- Orthogonal vectors are vectors that are perpendicular to each other, i.e., they form a right angle .
- Orthogonal vectors have a dot product of zero, i.e., u · v = 0 .
- Orthogonal vectors are linearly independent, i.e., they cannot be expressed as a linear combination of each other.
- Orthogonal vectors can form an orthogonal basis for a vector space, i.e., a set of linearly independent vectors that span the space.
- Orthogonal vectors can be normalized to form orthonormal vectors, i.e., vectors that have unit length and are orthogonal to each other.
- Orthogonal vectors have some applications in statistics, geometry, linear algebra, and computer science .
