# Eigenvalues and Eigenvectors

- Eigenvalues and eigenvectors are important concepts in linear algebra that are related to linear transformations and matrices.
- An eigenvalue of a matrix is a scalar value that satisfies the equation `Ax = λx`, where `A` is the matrix, `x` is a nonzero vector, and `λ` is the eigenvalue   .
- An eigenvector of a matrix is a nonzero vector that satisfies the equation `Ax = λx`, where `A` is the matrix, `x` is the eigenvector, and `λ` is the corresponding eigenvalue    .
- The eigenvalue and eigenvector are said to be associated with each other, or the eigenvector is said to be a `λ`-eigenvector of the matrix.
- A matrix can have multiple eigenvalues and eigenvectors, and each eigenvalue can have multiple eigenvectors associated with it.
- The set of all eigenvalues of a matrix is called the spectrum of the matrix, and the set of all eigenvectors of a matrix is called the eigenspace of the matrix.
- Eigenvalues and eigenvectors have many applications in various fields of mathematics, science, and engineering, such as differential equations, matrix decomposition, linear systems, stability analysis, optimization, graph theory, quantum mechanics, and data analysis.
