# Estimation and Hypothesis Testing

Estimation and hypothesis testing are two important concepts in inferential statistics, which is a branch of statistics that deals with making inferences about a population based on a sample of data. These concepts are used to make decisions and draw conclusions about the population based on the sample data.

## Estimation

Estimation is the process of using sample data to estimate a population parameter. There are two types of estimation: point estimation and interval estimation.

### Point Estimation

Point estimation is the process of using sample data to calculate a single value that is used as an estimate of the population parameter. The most common point estimator is the sample mean, which is used to estimate the population mean.

### Interval Estimation

Interval estimation is the process of using sample data to calculate an interval within which the population parameter is likely to fall. The most common interval estimator is the confidence interval, which provides a range of values that is likely to contain the population parameter with a certain level of confidence.

## Hypothesis Testing

Hypothesis testing is the process of using sample data to test a claim about a population parameter. The claim is stated in the form of a null hypothesis and an alternative hypothesis. The null hypothesis is the claim that is being tested, while the alternative hypothesis is the claim that is accepted if the null hypothesis is rejected.

Hypothesis testing involves calculating a test statistic and comparing it to a critical value. If the test statistic falls within the critical region, the null hypothesis is rejected and the alternative hypothesis is accepted. If the test statistic does not fall within the critical region, the null hypothesis is not rejected.

Hypothesis testing is used in many fields, including medicine, psychology, and economics, to make decisions and draw conclusions about populations based on sample data. It is an important tool in inferential statistics and is widely used in research and data analysis.
