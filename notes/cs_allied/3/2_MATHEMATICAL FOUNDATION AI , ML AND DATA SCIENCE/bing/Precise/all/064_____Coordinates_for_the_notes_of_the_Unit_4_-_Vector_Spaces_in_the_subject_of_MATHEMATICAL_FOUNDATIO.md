# Unit 4 - Vector Spaces

## Coordinates

1. A **coordinate system** is a way to assign a unique set of numbers to each point in a space.
2. In a **vector space**, a coordinate system can be defined by choosing a **basis** for the space.
3. A **basis** is a set of vectors that are linearly independent and span the space.
4. Once a basis is chosen, each vector in the space can be uniquely represented as a linear combination of the basis vectors.
5. The **coordinates** of a vector with respect to a given basis are the coefficients of the linear combination that represents the vector.
6. Changing the basis of a vector space changes the coordinates of the vectors in the space.
7. The process of changing from one coordinate system to another is called a **change of basis**.
8. A **change of basis matrix** can be used to transform the coordinates of a vector from one basis to another.
9. The **dimension** of a vector space is the number of vectors in a basis for the space.
10. The **standard basis** for an n-dimensional vector space is the set of n unit vectors along the coordinate axes.
