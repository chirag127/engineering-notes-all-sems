### Variance Reduction

Variance reduction is a technique used in Monte Carlo simulations to improve the accuracy of the estimates. It is used to reduce the variance of the estimates obtained from the simulation, which in turn reduces the number of simulations required to achieve a given level of accuracy.

There are several variance reduction techniques that can be used in Monte Carlo simulations, including:

1. **Control Variates:** This technique involves using a known quantity to reduce the variance of the estimate. The known quantity is used to adjust the estimate, reducing the variance.

2. **Antithetic Variates:** This technique involves generating pairs of random numbers that are negatively correlated. The pairs are used in the simulation, reducing the variance of the estimate.

3. **Importance Sampling:** This technique involves changing the probability distribution used to generate the random numbers in the simulation. The new distribution is chosen to reduce the variance of the estimate.

4. **Stratified Sampling:** This technique involves dividing the sample space into strata and sampling from each stratum. The strata are chosen to reduce the variance of the estimate.

These techniques can be used individually or in combination to reduce the variance of the estimates obtained from Monte Carlo simulations. They are an important tool in the field of Pseudo-Random Numbers and are widely used in the study of Mathematical Foundations of AI, ML, and Data Science.
