# Permutations and Randomization Test

Permutations and randomization tests are important concepts in the field of inferential statistics, which is a unit in the subject of Mathematical Foundation AI, ML and Data Science.

## Permutations

- A permutation is an arrangement of objects in a specific order.
- The number of permutations of n distinct objects taken r at a time is given by the formula nPr = n! / (n-r)! where n! denotes the factorial of n.
- Permutations are used to calculate the number of possible outcomes in situations where order matters.

## Randomization Test

- A randomization test is a statistical test that uses the process of randomization to determine the probability of obtaining the observed data or more extreme data, assuming the null hypothesis is true.
- The test involves generating many random permutations of the data and calculating the test statistic for each permutation.
- The p-value is then calculated as the proportion of permutations for which the test statistic is as extreme or more extreme than the observed test statistic.
- Randomization tests are used to test the significance of the observed data and to make inferences about the population from which the data was sampled.

These are some key points to remember about permutations and randomization tests in the context of inferential statistics. They are important concepts to understand for the study of Mathematical Foundation AI, ML and Data Science.
