### Acceptance-Rejection

The acceptance-rejection method is a technique used to generate random numbers from a given distribution. It is a part of Unit 3 - Pseudo-Random Numbers in the subject of Mathematical Foundation AI, ML, and Data Science. Here are some key points to remember about the acceptance-rejection method:

1. The acceptance-rejection method is used to generate random numbers from a distribution that is difficult to sample directly.
2. The method involves generating random numbers from a simpler distribution, known as the proposal distribution, and then accepting or rejecting these numbers based on a certain criterion.
3. The acceptance probability is determined by the ratio of the target distribution to the proposal distribution.
4. The efficiency of the acceptance-rejection method depends on the choice of the proposal distribution. A good proposal distribution should be easy to sample from and have a similar shape to the target distribution.
5. The acceptance-rejection method can be used to generate random numbers from both continuous and discrete distributions.
