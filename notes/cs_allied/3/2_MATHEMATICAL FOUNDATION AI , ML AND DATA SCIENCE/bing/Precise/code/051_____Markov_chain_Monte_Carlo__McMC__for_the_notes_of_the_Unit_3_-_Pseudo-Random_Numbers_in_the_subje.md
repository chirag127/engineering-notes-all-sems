### Markov Chain Monte Carlo (McMC)

Markov Chain Monte Carlo (McMC) is a class of algorithms used for sampling from probability distributions based on constructing a Markov chain that has the desired distribution as its equilibrium distribution. It is widely used in the field of AI, ML, and Data Science.

Here are some key points to note about McMC:

1. McMC methods are used to generate samples from complex probability distributions, where direct sampling is difficult.
2. The Markov chain is constructed in such a way that it converges to the desired distribution as its stationary distribution.
3. The samples generated by the Markov chain are not independent, but are correlated with each other.
4. McMC methods are widely used in Bayesian statistics, where the posterior distribution is often complex and difficult to sample from directly.
5. Some common McMC algorithms include the Metropolis-Hastings algorithm, the Gibbs sampler, and the Hamiltonian Monte Carlo algorithm.
6. McMC methods can be computationally expensive, and the convergence of the Markov chain to the stationary distribution can be slow.
