### Permutations and Randomization Test

Permutations and randomization tests are important concepts in the field of inferential statistics, which is a unit in the subject of Mathematical Foundation AI, ML, and Data Science.

1. **Permutations** refer to the arrangement of objects in a particular order. It is a way to count the number of possible arrangements of a set of objects.
2. A **randomization test** is a statistical test that uses the idea of random assignment to determine the probability of obtaining the observed data or more extreme data, assuming the null hypothesis is true.
3. Randomization tests are often used in situations where traditional parametric tests are not appropriate, such as when the data does not meet the assumptions of normality or homoscedasticity.
4. The basic idea behind a randomization test is to randomly assign the observed data to different groups and calculate the test statistic for each assignment. This process is repeated many times to generate a distribution of the test statistic under the null hypothesis.
5. The p-value of the test is then calculated as the proportion of the test statistics from the random assignments that are as extreme or more extreme than the observed test statistic.
6. Randomization tests are often considered to be more robust than traditional parametric tests, as they do not rely on assumptions about the underlying distribution of the data.

These concepts are important for understanding and applying inferential statistics in the field of AI, ML, and Data Science. They provide a foundation for making decisions and drawing conclusions from data.
