### Gibbs Sampling

Gibbs sampling is a Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) technique for obtaining a sequence of observations which are approximated from a specified multivariate probability distribution, when direct sampling is difficult. This method is commonly used in Bayesian statistics, specifically in the context of graphical models.

Here are some key points to remember about Gibbs sampling:

1. Gibbs sampling is used to generate samples from a multivariate distribution when direct sampling is difficult.
2. It is an iterative algorithm that generates a Markov chain of samples.
3. At each iteration, one variable is updated, conditioned on the current values of the other variables.
4. The sequence of samples generated by Gibbs sampling converges to the target distribution.
5. Gibbs sampling is commonly used in Bayesian statistics, specifically in the context of graphical models.
