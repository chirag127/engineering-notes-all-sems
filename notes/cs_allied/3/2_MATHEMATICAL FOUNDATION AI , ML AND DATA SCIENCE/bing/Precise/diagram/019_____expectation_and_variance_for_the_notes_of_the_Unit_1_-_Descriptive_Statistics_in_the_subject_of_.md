### Expectation and Variance

Expectation and variance are two important concepts in descriptive statistics, which is a part of the subject of Mathematical Foundation AI, ML, and Data Science.

#### Expectation

The expectation of a random variable is the weighted average of all possible values that the variable can take on. In other words, it is the long-run average value of repetitions of the experiment it represents. The expectation is denoted by E(X) where X is the random variable.

#### Variance

Variance is a measure of how spread out a set of data is. It is calculated by taking the average of the squared differences between each data point and the mean. The variance is denoted by Var(X) where X is the random variable.

Variance is useful in understanding the variability of a dataset and can be used to compare the spread of two or more datasets. A high variance indicates that the data points are spread out over a wider range of values, while a low variance indicates that the data points are clustered closely around the mean.

In summary, expectation and variance are important concepts in descriptive statistics that help us understand the central tendency and variability of a dataset. These concepts are essential for the study of AI, ML, and Data Science.
