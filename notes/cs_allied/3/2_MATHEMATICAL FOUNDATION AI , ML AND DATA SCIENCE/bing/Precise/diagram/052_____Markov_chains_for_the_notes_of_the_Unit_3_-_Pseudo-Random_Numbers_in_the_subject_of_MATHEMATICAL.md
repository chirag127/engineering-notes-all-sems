### Markov Chains

Markov chains are a type of mathematical model used to represent systems that change over time. They are named after the Russian mathematician Andrey Markov, who introduced the concept in the early 20th century.

Here are some key points to remember about Markov chains:

1. A Markov chain is a sequence of random variables, where the future state depends only on the present state and not on the past states. This is known as the Markov property.

2. Markov chains are used to model systems that change over time, where the future state of the system depends only on the present state and not on the past states.

3. Markov chains can be represented using a state diagram, where each state is represented by a node and the transitions between states are represented by directed edges.

4. The probabilities of transitioning from one state to another are represented by the weights on the edges of the state diagram.

5. Markov chains can be used to model a wide range of systems, including physical, biological, and social systems.

6. Markov chains are widely used in the fields of artificial intelligence, machine learning, and data science, particularly in the areas of natural language processing, speech recognition, and image processing.

7. Markov chains can be used to generate pseudo-random numbers, which are used in simulations and other applications where random numbers are required.
