2. Advantages: Stratified sampling has several advantages, including the ability to ensure that the sample is representative of the population, the ability to ensure that the sample is representative of each stratum, and the ability to control the sampling error.

3. Limitations: Despite its advantages, stratified sampling also has several limitations, including the difficulty of accurately dividing the population into strata, the difficulty of accurately selecting a sample from each stratum, and the difficulty of accurately estimating population parameters.
