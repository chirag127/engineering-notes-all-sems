
### 5 Vs of Big Data

Big Data is a term used to refer to data sets that are so large or complex that traditional data processing applications are inadequate to deal with them. The 5 Vs of Big Data are Volume, Variety, Velocity, Veracity, and Value.

* **Volume**: Big Data is characterized by its large data sets. It is often measured in petabytes or even exabytes.

* **Variety**: Big Data is composed of different types of data, such as structured, semi-structured, and unstructured data.

* **Velocity**: Big Data is characterized by its high speed of data generation and analysis.

* **Veracity**: Big Data is characterized by its uncertain and incomplete data.

* **Value**: Big Data is characterized by its ability to generate insights and value from data.

Mnemonics:

* **V**olume, **V**ariety, **V**elocity, **V**eracity, **V**alue  (VVVVV)
