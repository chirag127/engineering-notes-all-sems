

#### IBM Big Data Strategy

* IBM Big Data strategy is a comprehensive approach to data management that leverages the power of analytics, cloud computing, and cognitive computing to help organizations gain insights from their data.
* IBM's Big Data strategy is focused on helping organizations to store, manage, analyze, and visualize data in order to gain valuable insights.
* IBM's Big Data strategy includes a variety of products and services, such as IBM Watson Analytics, IBM Cloud Object Storage, IBM BigInsights, and IBM Big SQL.
* IBM Big Data strategy also includes a suite of software tools and services to help organizations manage and analyze large amounts of data.
* IBM Big Data strategy also includes a number of services to help organizations gain insights from their data, such as IBM Watson Explorer, IBM Watson Machine Learning, and IBM Watson Cognitive Computing.
* IBM Big Data strategy also includes a number of open source projects, such as Apache Hadoop, Apache Spark, and Apache Kafka, to help organizations build and deploy data-driven applications.
* IBM Big Data strategy also includes a number of industry-specific solutions, such as IBM Watson Health, IBM Watson Financial Services, and IBM Watson Retail.
* IBM Big Data strategy also includes a number of Mnemonics and learning tricks to help organizations remember and understand the data-driven insights they gain from their data. For example, the acronym "BIG DATA" stands for "Business Intelligence, Gaining Insights, and Driving Action".
