

### NoSQL Databases

NoSQL databases are a type of database that offer scalability, performance and availability for applications with large datasets and high throughput. They are often used in big data applications and are becoming increasingly popular in the enterprise.

NoSQL databases are characterized by their lack of a predefined schema, meaning that data can be stored in a variety of formats. This makes them well-suited for applications that require flexibility and scalability.

Some of the most popular NoSQL databases include MongoDB, Cassandra, HBase, and Redis. Each of these databases have their own advantages and disadvantages, so it’s important to evaluate which one is best for your application.

Mnemonics and Learning Tricks for NoSQL Databases

* MongoDB: "My Only MongoDB"
* Cassandra: "Cassandra Always Speaks"
* HBase: "Highly Beneficial and Scalable Engine"
* Redis: "Reliable and Easy Data Store"
