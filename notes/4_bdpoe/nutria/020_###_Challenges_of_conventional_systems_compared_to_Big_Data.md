
### Challenges of Conventional Systems Compared to Big Data

1. **Scalability**: Conventional systems are limited in their ability to scale up to handle an increasing amount of data. Big Data systems, on the other hand, are designed to be highly scalable and can handle large amounts of data with ease.

2. **Cost**: Conventional systems require expensive hardware and software to store and process data. Big Data systems are more cost-effective and can be deployed with minimal upfront costs.

3. **Data Variety**: Conventional systems are limited in the types of data they can process. Big Data systems can handle a wide variety of data types, including structured, semi-structured, and unstructured data.

4. **Data Volume**: Conventional systems are limited in the amount of data they can process. Big Data systems can process large volumes of data in a short amount of time.

5. **Data Velocity**: Conventional systems are limited in their ability to process data in real time. Big Data systems are designed to process data quickly and can provide insights in near real-time.

6. **Data Quality**: Conventional systems are limited in their ability to ensure data accuracy and completeness. Big Data systems are designed to ensure data quality and can detect and correct errors in data.

7. **Data Security**: Conventional systems are limited in their ability to protect data from unauthorized access. Big Data systems are designed to protect data from unauthorized access and can provide robust security measures.
