
### History of Big Data Innovation

Big Data has been around for decades, but it has only recently become a major part of our lives. Here is a brief history of Big Data innovation:

* In the late 1990s, Big Data began to emerge as a concept with the development of large-scale databases. 
* In the early 2000s, the term “Big Data” was coined by Doug Laney of Gartner. 
* In the mid-2000s, the Hadoop open source project was launched to make processing large amounts of data easier. 
* In the late 2000s, companies like Google and Amazon began to use Big Data to improve their operations. 
* In the early 2010s, the term “Big Data” became mainstream, and companies began to use it to gain insights into their customers. 
* In the mid-2010s, the emergence of the Internet of Things (IoT) and the development of machine learning algorithms made it easier to gain insights from data. 
* In the late 2010s, Big Data was used to create predictive models and to automate processes. 
* In the early 2020s, the use of Big Data has become increasingly widespread, with more companies leveraging its power to gain insights and improve decision-making.
