### Challenges of conventional systems compared to Big Data

1. Volume - Conventional systems are not designed to handle huge volumes of data that Big Data systems can. Big Data systems use distributed and parallel processing to store and process large volumes of data.
2. Velocity - Conventional systems cannot match the speed at which data is generated and processed in Big Data systems. Big Data systems are optimized for fast ingestion and processing of data.
3. Variety - Conventional systems are designed to handle structured data. Big Data systems can handle structured, unstructured and semi-structured data. They use schema-on-read approaches and store the data in its raw form first.
4. Complexity - Big Data systems are designed to handle complex analytical queries on large datasets which conventional systems struggle with. Big Data systems use advanced analytics and machine learning algorithms to discover patterns and insights which are challenging for conventional systems.

Some mnemonics to remember the 3 V's:
Volume - Visualize a huge volume of water to remember Volume
Velocity - Visualize a cheetah running at high velocity to remember Velocity
Variety - Visualize different variety of fruits, shapes, colors to remember Variety

Advantages: Handle huge datasets, faster processing, can handle diverse data, enable advanced analytics and insights
Disadvantages: Require significant resources, complex to set up and manage, lack of skillset to work with Big Data systems

Examples: Recommendation systems, fraud detection, sentiment analysis, predictive analytics
Applications: E-commerce, healthcare, finance, manufacturing, etc.
