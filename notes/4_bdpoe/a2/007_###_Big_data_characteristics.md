### Big data characteristics

The characteristics of big data are commonly referred to as the "V's" of big data. Here are the main characteristics of big data:

- Volume: Refers to the huge amount of data generated and stored. The volume of data is doubling every two years as per industry reports.

- Velocity: Refers to the speed at which the data is generated and processed. The massive volume of data is streaming in at an unprecedented rate.

- Variety: Refers to the various types and formats of data. Data today comes in all types of formats like structured, unstructured, text, audio, video, etc.

- Veracity: Refers to the bias, noise, and abnormality in data. Big data analytics requires automated methods to detect and remove errors and inconsistencies in the data.

- Value: Perhaps the most important characteristic. The ultimate goal is to extract value from the data and enable better decisions and strategic business moves.

Some Mnemonics to remember the Big Data V's:

- Very Very Valuable Views (Volume, Velocity, Variety, Veracity, Value)
- Violent Velocity and Volume (Velocity, Volume)

The value of big data lies in processing the huge volume, variety, and velocity of data to gain insights and make impactful business decisions. Successful use cases of big data analytics across industries have demonstrated the potential value that can be generated from big data.
