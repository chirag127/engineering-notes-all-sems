## Unit 1 - Introduction to Big Data

- Big data refers to extremely large data sets that may be analyzed computationally to reveal patterns, trends, and associations, especially relating to human behavior and interactions.
- The challenges include capture, storage, search, sharing, transfer, analysis, and visualization.
- The four V's of Big Data:
- Volume: Refers to the huge amount of data. The size of data is in petabytes and exabytes.
- Velocity: Refers to the speed at which the data is generated and processed. The data streams in at an incredible rate.
- Variety: Refers to the different types and formats of data. The data could be structured, semi-structured or unstructured.
- Veracity: Refers to the biases, noise and abnormality in the data. The data may be incomplete, inconsistent, inexact or misleading.

**Mnemonics:**
- For the 4 V's - Think of a Very Very Fast car with a Variety of parts generating Volume of data.

**Learning Tricks:**
- Remember the 4 V's using the mnemonic and understand the meaning of each V in terms of Big Data. This will help in recalling the characteristics fast.
- Understand the challenges with the help of examples around the 4 V's. This develops intuition for the complexity and issues with Big Data.

**Advantages:**
- Faster insights and decisions
- Improved operational efficiency
- Increased revenue opportunities
- Predictive analytics for future trends

**Disadvantages:**
- Privacy concerns and ethical issues
- Bias in data can lead to flawed analysis
- Costs involved in data storage and analysis
- Lack of skilled professionals

**Applications:**
- Recommendation systems
- Sentiment analysis
- Fraud detection
- Precision medicine
- Supply chain management
