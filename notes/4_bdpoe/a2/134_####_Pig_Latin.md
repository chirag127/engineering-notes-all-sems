#### Pig Latin

Pig Latin is a game of permutations played on the English language game. It is not an actual separate language but just a cipher and can be used to disguise English and make it unintelligible to those not familiar with the rules.

The basic rules of Pig Latin are:

- Take the first letter of a word and put it at the end of the word followed by "ay".
- If the word begins with a vowel, add "way" to the end of the word.

For example:

- Hello -> Ellohay
- Apple -> Appleway
- Computer -> Omputercay

Some additional rules and variations:

- For words beginning with consonant clusters like "str" or "ch", move the whole cluster to the end of the word. For example, "string" -> "ingstray", "change" -> "angechay".
- For words beginning with "qu", move just the "q" to the end. For example, "queen" -> "eenquay".
- Some versions omit the "ay" for monosyllabic words like "I" or "a". So these would be unchanged.
- The game can be made more complex by nesting or recursion where translating a Pig Latin word yields another Pig Latin word.

Advantages:

- Fun game to play with words and permutation combinations.
- Can be used as a basic cipher for disguising English.

Disadvantages:

- Very easy to crack as the rules are simple permutations.
- Does not provide strong security or encryption.
- Mainly just a recreational lingual game.

Examples:

- I speak Pig Latin -> Iay eakspay Igpay Atinlay
- This is fun -> Histay isway unfunay

Applications:

- Creative writing and poetry
- Language learning - understand permutations and word order
- Recreational games with words and languages
