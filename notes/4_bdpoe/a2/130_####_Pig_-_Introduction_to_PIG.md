#### Pig - Introduction to PIG

Pig is a high-level scripting language that is used with Apache Hadoop. It is designed to be easy to learn and makes it possible to process large data sets efficiently. Pig Latin is Pig's scripting language which converts the scripts into MapReduce jobs which are executed on Hadoop clusters.

Some key points about Pig are:

- Pig Latin is a procedural language with a syntax resembling SQL and JavaScript.
- Pig Latin provides a variety of complex data types like tuples, bags, and maps.
- Pig Latin has the ability to nest complex data types within each other.
- Pig Latin provides a variety of builtin functions to operate on data. Users can also create their own functions for reusability and modular programming.
- Pig Latin programs are executed in two phases:

1. Compilation - The Pig Latin script is compiled into a series of MapReduce jobs.
2. Execution - The MapReduce jobs are executed on the Hadoop cluster.

Advantages of Pig:

- It is easy to learn with a simple syntax like SQL.
- It can handle complex data types and nested structures easily.
- It provides a variety of builtin functions to operate on data. Custom functions can also be created.
- The scripts are converted to MapReduce jobs which can be optimized and executed efficiently on Hadoop clusters.

Disadvantages of Pig:

- The simple syntax can mask the complexity of the underlying MapReduce jobs and make debugging difficult.
- The additional abstraction layer can lead to a performance hit as compared to writing customized MapReduce jobs.
- The scripts are bound to the Hadoop ecosystem and cannot be run independently.
