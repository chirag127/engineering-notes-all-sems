#### Auditing of Big Data

Auditing of Big Data refers to the process of analyzing and verifying the accuracy, completeness, and reliability of big data. Some key points about auditing Big Data are:

-   **Volume:** Due to the large volume of data, it is not feasible to audit 100% of the data. Audit sampling techniques are used to select a representative sample of the data to audit. The sample size is determined based on the risks and error tolerance levels.
-   **Variety:** The variety of data adds to the complexity of auditing. The audit team must have knowledge of the different data types (structured, semi-structured, unstructured) and associated technologies to audit the data.
-   **Velocity:** The high velocity of data generation and processing requires real-time auditing techniques to provide timely insights and recommendations. Traditional audit approaches may not be suitable for auditing high-velocity big data.
-   **Veracity:** The veracity or truthfulness of the data must be verified to ensure the data is accurate, complete, and reliable. Data profiling and data quality analysis techniques are used to assess the veracity of the data.
-   **Value:** The value of the insights and decisions derived from the data must be audited to ensure the big data analytics processes are effective and the results are useful and impactful. The audit may assess if the right data is being analyzed to address the key business questions.

Some Mnemonics and learning tricks for remembering the key points:

-   **5 V's:** Remember the 5 V's - Volume, Variety, Velocity, Veracity, Value
-   **SAMPLING:** Think of the word SAMPLING to remember audit sampling is used due to VOLUME
-   **REAL-TIME:** Think of REAL-TIME to remember real-time auditing is required due to high VELOCITY
-   **PROFILE and QUALITY:** Remember PROFILE and QUALITY to recall data profiling and data quality analysis for VERACITY
-   **RIGHT DATA, RIGHT QUESTIONS:** Think of RIGHT DATA, RIGHT QUESTIONS to remember assessing if the right data is analyzed to address key questions for VALUE
