### IBM Big Data strategy

IBM is a leading player in the world of big data, and the company has developed a comprehensive strategy for leveraging data to drive business success. The IBM Big Data strategy includes the following key components:

1. **Data Collection and Integration:** IBM Big Data strategy focuses on collecting data from various sources, including social media, sensors, and other digital devices. The collected data is then integrated with existing data sources to create a comprehensive view of the business.

2. **Data Management and Governance:** IBM Big Data strategy emphasizes the importance of managing and governing data to ensure its accuracy, completeness, and security. This involves implementing policies, procedures, and tools to manage data throughout its lifecycle.

3. **Data Analysis and Insights:** IBM Big Data strategy provides advanced analytics capabilities to extract insights from data. The company uses various technologies and techniques, including machine learning, natural language processing, and predictive analytics, to uncover patterns, trends, and anomalies in data.

4. **Data Visualization and Reporting:** IBM Big Data strategy includes tools for visualizing and reporting data insights. This helps users to understand data more easily and make informed decisions based on the insights gained.

5. **Cloud-based Data Services:** IBM Big Data strategy leverages cloud-based data services to streamline data management and analytics. The company provides various cloud-based data services, including data warehousing, data lakes, and data science platforms.

6. **Security and Compliance:** IBM Big Data strategy ensures the security and compliance of data by implementing various security measures and complying with industry regulations and standards.

Mnemonics and Learning Tricks:

1. Remember the acronym CDMA - Collect, Data Management, Analysis, Cloud-based services, and Security.
2. Think of IBM's strategy as a cycle: Collect data, Manage and govern data, Analyze data, Visualize and report insights, and ensure Security and compliance.

Overall, the IBM Big Data strategy provides a comprehensive approach to leveraging data for business success. By following this strategy, organizations can collect, manage, analyze, and visualize data to gain valuable insights that can inform key business decisions.
