## Unit 4 - Agreement Protocols
Agreement protocols are algorithms that ensure that a network of nodes can reach consensus on a shared state in a distributed system. They are used to ensure that the network reaches a consistent state in the face of node failures, network partitions, and other types of failures. Examples of agreement protocols include Paxos, Raft, and Zab. These protocols are designed to be fault-tolerant and ensure that the network can continue to operate even if some nodes fail. They are used in a variety of applications, including distributed databases, cloud computing, and blockchain systems.
