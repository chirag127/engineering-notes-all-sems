### Introduction for the notes of the Unit 1 - Characterization of Distributed Systems in the subject of DISTRIBUTED SYSTEM
Distributed systems are a collection of autonomous computing elements that work together to achieve a common goal. Unit 1 focuses on the characterization of these systems, including their fundamental concepts, design principles, and models. Topics covered include the definition and properties of distributed systems, communication, coordination, consistency and replication, and fault tolerance. Understanding these concepts is crucial for the design and implementation of reliable and scalable distributed systems.
