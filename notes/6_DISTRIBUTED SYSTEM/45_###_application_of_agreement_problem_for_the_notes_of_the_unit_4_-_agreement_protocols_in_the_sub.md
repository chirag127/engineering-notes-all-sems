### Application of Agreement problem for the notes of the Unit 4 - Agreement Protocols in the subject of DISTRIBUTED SYSTEM
The Agreement Problem in distributed systems refers to the challenge of ensuring that all nodes in a network reach a consensus on some value or decision. In Unit 4 of the distributed systems subject, Agreement Protocols are studied as a solution to this problem. These protocols are algorithms that allow nodes in a network to reach a consensus on a value or decision, even in the presence of failures or network partitions. Some common examples of Agreement Protocols include Paxos, Raft, and Zab. These protocols are widely used in distributed systems, such as distributed databases, cloud computing systems, and other large-scale systems that require coordination between multiple nodes. In conclusion, the study of Agreement Protocols is crucial for understanding how to build reliable and scalable distributed systems.
