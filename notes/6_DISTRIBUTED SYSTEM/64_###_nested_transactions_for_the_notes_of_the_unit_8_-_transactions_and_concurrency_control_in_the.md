### Nested transactions for the notes of the Unit 8 - Transactions and Concurrency Control in the subject of DISTRIBUTED SYSTEM
Nested transactions refer to a transaction that is executed inside another transaction. The inner transaction is treated as a sub-transaction and is dependent on the outer transaction. If the outer transaction is committed, the inner transaction is also committed, but if the outer transaction is rolled back, the inner transaction is also rolled back. Nested transactions are used to provide atomicity and consistency to complex operations that involve multiple sub-operations. They are used in distributed systems to ensure that all sub-transactions are executed in a consistent manner, even if one or more sub-transactions fail. Nested transactions are implemented using two-phase commit protocols to ensure that all sub-transactions are committed or rolled back together.
