### Consensus problem for the notes of the Unit 4 - Agreement Protocols in the subject of DISTRIBUTED SYSTEM
Consensus problem in distributed systems refers to the challenge of achieving agreement among nodes on a single value or state in a network where nodes may experience failures or delays in communication. It is a fundamental issue in distributed systems as it affects the reliability and consistency of data. There are various consensus algorithms such as Paxos, Raft, and Byzantine Fault Tolerance, which aim to solve the consensus problem by providing a mechanism for nodes to reach agreement on a value in a distributed system. These algorithms differ in their approach, complexity, and fault tolerance. Understanding the consensus problem and its solutions is crucial for designing and implementing reliable and scalable distributed systems.
