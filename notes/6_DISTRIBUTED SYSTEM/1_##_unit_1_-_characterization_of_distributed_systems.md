## Unit 1 - Characterization of Distributed Systems
Distributed systems are computer systems that consist of multiple autonomous computers that communicate through a network to achieve a common goal. Unit 1 of the course focuses on characterizing these systems by examining their properties and design decisions. This includes understanding the trade-off between consistency, availability, and partition tolerance in distributed systems, as well as the role of communication protocols and system architecture in achieving desired properties. Additionally, unit 1 covers topics such as consistency models, distributed algorithms, and fault tolerance.
