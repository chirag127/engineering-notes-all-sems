### Introduction for the notes of the Unit 4 - Agreement Protocols in the subject of DISTRIBUTED SYSTEM
The Unit 4 - Agreement Protocols in the subject of Distributed Systems deals with the methods and techniques used to reach a consensus among the nodes in a distributed system. The objective is to ensure that all nodes have a common understanding of the state of the system, even in the presence of failures. The unit covers various types of agreement protocols, including two-phase commit, Paxos, and Raft, as well as their applications and limitations. Additionally, the unit explores the trade-offs between consistency, availability, and fault tolerance in distributed systems.
