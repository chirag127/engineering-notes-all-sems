### Transaction recovery for the notes of the Unit 9 - Distributed Transactions in the subject of DISTRIBUTED SYSTEM
Transaction recovery refers to the process of restoring the state of a failed transaction to a consistent state. In a distributed transaction, multiple systems are involved and must all agree on the outcome of the transaction. If one system fails, the transaction may be left in an inconsistent state. Recovery involves restoring the state of the failed system and ensuring that all systems involved in the transaction agree on the outcome. This can be done through a variety of methods, such as logging and checkpointing, or by using a two-phase commit protocol.
