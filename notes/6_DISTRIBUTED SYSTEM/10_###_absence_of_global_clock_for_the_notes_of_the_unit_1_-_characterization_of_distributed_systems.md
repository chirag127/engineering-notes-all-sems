### absence of global clock for the notes of the Unit 1 - Characterization of Distributed Systems in the subject of DISTRIBUTED SYSTEM
A global clock in a distributed system refers to a shared notion of time across all nodes in the system. The absence of a global clock means that there is no single, agreed-upon time source for all nodes. This can lead to difficulties in coordinating activities and maintaining consistency in the system, as nodes may have different views of the current time. To address this issue, distributed systems often use techniques such as clock synchronization algorithms or logical clocks to provide a consistent view of time across the system.
