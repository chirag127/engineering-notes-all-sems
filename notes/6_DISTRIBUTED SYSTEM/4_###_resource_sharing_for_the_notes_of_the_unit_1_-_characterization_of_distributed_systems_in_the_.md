### Resource sharing for the notes of the Unit 1 - Characterization of Distributed Systems in the subject of DISTRIBUTED SYSTEM
Resource sharing refers to the ability of multiple processes in a distributed system to access shared resources such as files, printers, and databases. This is achieved through the use of communication protocols and resource coordination mechanisms. Resource sharing enables distributed systems to provide services that are more efficient and scalable than those provided by traditional centralized systems. Some of the key challenges in resource sharing include managing concurrent access to shared resources, ensuring consistency and reliability, and maintaining security. To address these challenges, distributed systems use techniques such as locking, transactional memory, and replication, among others. These techniques help to ensure that shared resources are accessed and modified in a consistent and coordinated manner, even in the presence of failures or network delays.
