### Causal ordering of messages for the notes of the Unit 1 - Characterization of Distributed Systems in the subject of DISTRIBUTED SYSTEM
Causal ordering of messages refers to the order in which events occur in a distributed system. In a distributed system, messages may be sent between processes in different orders, and it is important to ensure that messages are delivered in the correct order to maintain consistency. There are several algorithms that can be used to enforce causal ordering, including vector clocks, Lamport timestamps, and total ordering algorithms. These algorithms are used to determine the order in which messages are delivered, ensuring that messages are delivered in the correct order and that the system remains consistent.
