### causal order for the notes of the Unit 1 - Characterization of Distributed Systems in the subject of DISTRIBUTED SYSTEM
Causal order in distributed systems refers to the order in which events occur and their relationship to each other in terms of causality. It is a fundamental concept in the design and implementation of distributed systems, as it helps to ensure that events are processed in a consistent and predictable manner. In a distributed system, events can occur concurrently and in different orders on different nodes, making it necessary to establish a causal order to ensure that events are processed correctly. This is typically achieved by using a combination of timestamps and vector clocks to track the causality of events and ensure that they are processed in the correct order. Understanding the concept of causal order is important for designing and implementing reliable and scalable distributed systems.
