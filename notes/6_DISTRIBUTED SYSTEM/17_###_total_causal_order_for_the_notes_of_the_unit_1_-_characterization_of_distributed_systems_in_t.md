### total causal order for the notes of the Unit 1 - Characterization of Distributed Systems in the subject of DISTRIBUTED SYSTEM
Total causal order is a fundamental concept in distributed systems, which refers to the order in which events occur in a distributed system. It is used to ensure that events are processed in a consistent and predictable manner, even when they occur concurrently. The total causal order of events is determined by the relative causality between events, which is determined by the dependencies between events. In a distributed system, events can be caused by messages sent between processes, and the total causal order of events is determined by the order in which these messages are delivered. This is important because it ensures that the system remains consistent and predictable, even when events occur concurrently.
