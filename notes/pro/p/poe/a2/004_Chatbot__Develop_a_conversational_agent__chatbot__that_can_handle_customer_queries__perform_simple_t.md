Chatbot: Develop a conversational agent (chatbot) that can handle customer queries, perform simple tasks and make recommendations.

I. Introduction
- Chatbot: A software application that is able to communicate with humans through text or speech.
- Conversational agent: A chatbot that can conduct a multi-turn dialogue with humans on a specific topic.
- Natural language processing: The ability of a computer program to analyze, understand and generate human language.

II. Technologies
- Natural Language Processing (NLP): To understand user intent and extract context from conversational text.
- Python: To implement NLP and machine learning models. Popular NLP libraries - NLTK, SpaCy.
- Dialogflow: To design conversational flows and handle intents and entities.
- TensorFlow: To train machine learning models for more complex NLP tasks.

III. Capabilities
- Handle customer queries: Understand questions and queries, and provide relevant responses and information.
- Perform simple tasks: Complete tasks such as weather info, sports scores, reminders, etc. on the user's request.
- Make recommendations: Provide personalized recommendations based on the user's interests and past interactions.

IV. Challenges
- Understanding complex language: Handling metaphors, contextual references, spelling/grammatical errors.
- Large scale personalization: Catering to diverse users and remembering personal details/preferences of many users.
- Seamless conversations: Maintaining context throughout multi-turn conversations and providing relevant responses.

V. Conclusion
- Chatbots and conversational agents can automate communication and reduce human effort.
- Advancements in NLP and machine learning enable chatbots to understand complex language, personalize responses and engage in more engaging conversations.
- Python and cloud-based development platforms facilitate easy development of AI-powered chatbots.
