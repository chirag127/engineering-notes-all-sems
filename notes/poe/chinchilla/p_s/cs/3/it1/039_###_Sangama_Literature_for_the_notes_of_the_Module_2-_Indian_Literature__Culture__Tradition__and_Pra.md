### Sangama Literature

Sangama literature is a form of Kannada literature that originated in the Sangama dynasty of the Vijayanagara Empire. It is a unique form of literature that contains works in prose and verse, and is known for its rich language and literary style. Here are some important points to understand about Sangama literature:

- The Sangama dynasty ruled the Vijayanagara Empire from 1336 to 1485 CE, and was known for its patronage of art, literature, and culture.
- The Sangama literature was composed in Kannada language and was primarily written during the 14th and 15th centuries.
- The literature is broadly classified into three categories: Vachana Sahitya, Shatpadi Sahitya, and Ragale Sahitya.
- Vachana Sahitya refers to devotional poetry written in the form of short, free verses. These poems were written by Veerashaiva poets and were dedicated to Lord Shiva. They were meant to convey a message of social reform and spiritual enlightenment.
- Shatpadi Sahitya refers to poetry composed in six-line stanzas. These poems were written by Jain poets and were primarily focused on moral values and ethics.
- Ragale Sahitya refers to long narrative poems that were written in the form of ballads. These poems were composed by court poets and were meant to entertain the royal audience.
- The Sangama literature is known for its rich language, literary style, and use of metaphors and similes.
- The literature was deeply influenced by the Bhakti movement and the teachings of the Veerashaiva and Jain sects.
- Some of the notable works in Sangama literature include Basava Purana by Palkuriki Somanatha, Shanti Purana by Ratnakaravarni, and Raghavanka's Harishchandra Kavya.
- Sangama literature played a significant role in shaping the Kannada language and literature and is considered as an important part of Karnataka's cultural heritage.

In summary, Sangama literature is a unique form of literature that originated in the Sangama dynasty of the Vijayanagara Empire. It is known for its rich language, literary style, and use of metaphors and similes. The literature is broadly classified into three categories: Vachana Sahitya, Shatpadi Sahitya, and Ragale Sahitya, and played a significant role in shaping the Kannada language and literature.
