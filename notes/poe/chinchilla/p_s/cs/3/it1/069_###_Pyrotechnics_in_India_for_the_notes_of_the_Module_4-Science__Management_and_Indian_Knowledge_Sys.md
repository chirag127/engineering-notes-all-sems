### Pyrotechnics in India

Pyrotechnics or fireworks have been an integral part of Indian culture and traditions for centuries. From Diwali to weddings and other celebrations, fireworks are used to add to the joy and festivity of these occasions. In this section, we will discuss pyrotechnics in India in detail.

#### History of Pyrotechnics in India

The use of fireworks in India can be traced back to ancient times. It is believed that the Chinese introduced fireworks to India during the Tang dynasty (618-907 AD). Over the years, Indian pyrotechnicians have developed their own unique style and technique of fireworks.

#### Types of Fireworks

There are various types of fireworks used in India, some of which are as follows:

1. Rockets - These are cylindrical in shape and are propelled into the air using a stick. They explode in the air, producing a loud noise and bright colours.

2. Flower Pots - These are spherical in shape and produce a burst of colourful sparks when ignited.

3. Chakras - These are circular in shape and spin around on the ground, producing a display of bright sparks.

4. Sparklers - These are handheld fireworks that produce a stream of sparks when lit.

#### Pyrotechnics Industry in India

The pyrotechnics industry in India is largely unorganized and is dominated by small-scale manufacturers. However, there are some large-scale manufacturers as well, who produce fireworks for both domestic and international markets. The major production centers for fireworks in India are Sivakasi in Tamil Nadu and Noida in Uttar Pradesh.

#### Safety Concerns

Fireworks can be dangerous if not handled properly. It is important to follow safety guidelines while handling fireworks. Some of the safety guidelines are as follows:

1. Keep fireworks out of reach of children.

2. Always light fireworks in an open space away from buildings, trees, and other flammable objects.

3. Do not hold fireworks in your hand while lighting them.

4. Keep a bucket of water or sand nearby to extinguish any fires that may start.

#### Conclusion

Pyrotechnics in India is a rich and vibrant tradition that has been passed down from generation to generation. However, it is important to handle fireworks with care and follow safety guidelines to avoid any mishaps.
