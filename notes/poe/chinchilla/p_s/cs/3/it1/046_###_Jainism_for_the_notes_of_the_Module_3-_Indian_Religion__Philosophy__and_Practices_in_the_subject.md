### Jainism

Jainism is an ancient religion that originated in India around the same time as Buddhism. It is based on the teachings of Mahavira, who lived in the 6th century BCE. Jainism is a non-theistic religion that emphasizes the importance of non-violence, self-discipline, and the pursuit of enlightenment.

#### Key beliefs and practices

- Non-violence (ahimsa) is the most important principle of Jainism. Jains believe that all living beings have a soul and that harming any living being, even unintentionally, is wrong.
- Jainism also emphasizes the importance of self-discipline, particularly in controlling one's thoughts, speech, and actions.
- Jains believe in karma, the idea that our actions have consequences that affect our future lives. By living a virtuous life, one can accumulate positive karma and eventually achieve enlightenment.
- Jainism also places great importance on meditation and self-reflection as a means of achieving enlightenment.
- Jains practice a strict vegetarian diet and avoid foods that involve the killing of animals, such as meat, eggs, and dairy products.
- Jains also often practice fasting as a means of spiritual purification.

#### Jain philosophy

- Jain philosophy is based on the idea of anekantavada, which means "non-one-sidedness." This principle teaches that reality is complex and cannot be fully understood from a single perspective. Instead, truth is seen as a combination of multiple perspectives.
- Another important principle of Jain philosophy is syadvada, which means "the theory of conditioned predication." This principle teaches that all statements about reality are only partially true and that the full truth can only be understood by taking into account all perspectives.

#### Jain literature

- The primary Jain texts are the Agamas, a collection of scriptures that were orally transmitted for centuries before being written down.
- The most important Jain text is the Kalpa Sutra, which tells the story of Mahavira's life and teachings.

#### Jainism today

- Jainism is a minority religion in India, with only about 4.2 million adherents.
- However, Jainism has had a significant impact on Indian culture and society, particularly in the areas of vegetarianism and non-violence.
- Jainism has also spread to other parts of the world, particularly in the United States and the United Kingdom, where there are significant Jain communities.
