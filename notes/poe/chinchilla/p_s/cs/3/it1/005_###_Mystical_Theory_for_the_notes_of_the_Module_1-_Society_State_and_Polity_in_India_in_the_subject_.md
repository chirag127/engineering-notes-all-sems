It has given rise to the concept of dharma, which emphasizes the moral and ethical duties of individuals towards society and the state. It has also influenced the idea of non-violence, which has been a prominent feature of Indian political movements such as the freedom struggle led by Mahatma Gandhi.

5. Criticism of Mystical Theory: Despite its popularity, the mystical theory has also faced criticism from various quarters. Some argue that it is anti-scientific and promotes blind faith. Others claim that it has been used to justify social and political inequality in Indian society.

In conclusion, the mystical theory is an important aspect of Indian society, state, and polity. It has influenced various aspects of life and has given rise to diverse spiritual traditions and practices.
