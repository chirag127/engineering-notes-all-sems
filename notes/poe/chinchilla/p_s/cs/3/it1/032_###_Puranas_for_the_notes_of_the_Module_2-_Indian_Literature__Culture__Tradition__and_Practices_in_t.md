### Puranas 

Puranas are a genre of ancient Hindu texts that contain stories about the creation of the universe, gods and goddesses, and various religious rituals and practices. They were written in Sanskrit and are considered an important part of Indian literature, culture, tradition, and practices.

The following are some important points about Puranas that you should know:

- There are 18 Puranas in total, and they are divided into three categories: Sattva Guna (goodness), Rajas Guna (passion), and Tamas Guna (ignorance).
- Each Purana contains a specific set of stories and teachings, and they are named after the primary deity that is worshipped in that particular Purana.
- Some of the most famous Puranas include the Vishnu Purana, Shiva Purana, Bhagavata Purana, and Markandeya Purana.
- In addition to stories about gods and goddesses, Puranas also contain information about various religious practices such as meditation, yoga, and rituals.
- Puranas also provide valuable insights into the social and cultural practices of ancient India, including marriage customs, social hierarchies, and the roles of women in society.
- The Puranas were traditionally passed down orally from generation to generation, but they were eventually written down in the form of manuscripts.
- The Puranas continue to be an important part of Hindu culture and are still studied and revered by many people today.

Overall, studying the Puranas can provide valuable insights into the religious, social, and cultural practices of ancient India, and can help us better understand the rich cultural heritage of the country.
