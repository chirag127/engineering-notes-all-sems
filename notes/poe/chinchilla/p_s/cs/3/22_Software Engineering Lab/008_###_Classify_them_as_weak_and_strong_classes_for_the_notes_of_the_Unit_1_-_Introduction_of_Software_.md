#### What are Classes?

Classes are the building blocks of object-oriented programming (OOP). They are used to represent real-world objects and their behavior. A class is a blueprint for creating objects. It defines the properties and methods that an object can have. In OOP, objects are created from classes.

#### What are Weak Classes?

Weak classes are those classes that have a low level of cohesion and a high level of coupling. Cohesion refers to the degree to which the elements inside a class are related to each other. Coupling refers to the degree to which a class is dependent on other classes. A weak class has a low level of cohesion because its elements are not related to each other in a meaningful way. It has a high level of coupling because it is dependent on other classes.

#### What are Strong Classes?

Strong classes are those classes that have a high level of cohesion and a low level of coupling. A strong class has a high level of cohesion because its elements are related to each other in a meaningful way. It has a low level of coupling because it is not dependent on other classes. In other words, a strong class is self-contained and does not rely on other classes to perform its functions.

#### Advantages of Strong Classes

- Strong classes are easier to understand and maintain.
- Strong classes are more reusable because they are self-contained.
- Strong classes are less prone to errors because they are not dependent on other classes.

#### Disadvantages of Weak Classes

- Weak classes are harder to understand and maintain.
- Weak classes are less reusable because they are not self-contained.
- Weak classes are more prone to errors because they are dependent on other classes.

#### Examples of Weak and Strong Classes

Consider a simple example of a bank account. The bank account has properties such as account number, balance, and account holder name. It also has methods such as deposit, withdraw, and check balance.

A strong class for a bank account would have all the properties and methods related to the bank account. It would not rely on other classes to perform its functions.

A weak class for a bank account would have some of its properties and methods related to the bank account, while others would be related to other classes such as a database or a user interface. It would rely on other classes to perform its functions.

#### Applications of Strong Classes

Strong classes are used in software development to create reusable code. They are used to create libraries, frameworks, and components. Strong classes are also used in software testing to create testable units.

In conclusion, the classification of classes as weak and strong classes is an important concept in Software Engineering Lab. Strong classes are self-contained and have a high level of cohesion and a low level of coupling, while weak classes are not self-contained and have a low level of cohesion and a high level of coupling. Understanding this concept is important for creating reusable, maintainable, and error-free software.
