### NP-Completeness and Approximation Algorithms with Examples Such as Travelling Salesman Problem

NP-Completeness is a concept used in computational complexity theory that refers to the difficulty of solving certain computational problems. It is used to classify problems as "hard" or "easy" based on the amount of resources required to solve them.

Travelling Salesman Problem is an example of an NP-Complete problem that involves finding the shortest possible route that visits a set of cities and returns to the starting point. It is a classic problem in computer science and is used as an example to illustrate the concept of NP-Completeness.
