## Unit 5 - NP-Completeness and Approximation Algorithms with Examples Such as Travelling Salesman Problem, Graph Coloring, n-Queen Problem, Hamiltonian Cycles and Sum of Subsets.

In this unit, we will learn about NP-Completeness and Approximation algorithms. NP-Completeness is a class of computational problems that are believed to have no efficient solutions. Approximation algorithms are algorithms that provide solutions that are close to optimal but not necessarily optimal.

### NP-Completeness

NP-Completeness is a class of computational problems that are believed to have no efficient solutions. These problems are called NP-Complete problems. The most famous of these problems is the Traveling Salesman Problem (TSP).

The TSP is a problem where a salesman must visit a set of cities and return to his starting point, traveling the minimum distance possible. This problem is NP-Complete because it is believed to be impossible to find an efficient algorithm that will always provide the optimal solution.

Other examples of NP-Complete problems include Graph Coloring, n-Queen Problem, Hamiltonian Cycles, and Sum of Subsets.

### Approximation Algorithms

Approximation algorithms are algorithms that provide solutions that are close to optimal but not necessarily optimal. These algorithms are used when finding the exact solution is not feasible or when the problem is NP-Complete.

The most famous of these algorithms is the Greedy Algorithm. The Greedy Algorithm is an algorithm that makes the locally optimal choice at each step with the hope of finding a global optimum. The Greedy Algorithm is often used to solve the TSP.

Other examples of Approximation Algorithms include Linear Programming, Dynamic Programming, and Randomized Algorithms.

### Advantages and Disadvantages

The advantages of NP-Completeness and Approximation Algorithms are that they provide solutions to problems that would otherwise be impossible to solve. The disadvantages are that the solutions provided are not always optimal and may require a lot of computational resources.

### Applications

NP-Completeness and Approximation Algorithms are used in many applications, including logistics, scheduling, optimization, and resource allocation. For example, the TSP is used in logistics to find the shortest route for a delivery driver to visit a set of locations.

In conclusion, NP-Completeness and Approximation Algorithms are important concepts in computer science. These algorithms provide solutions to problems that would otherwise be impossible to solve. They are used in many applications, including logistics, scheduling, optimization, and resource allocation.
