### Sequence Diagrams for the notes of the Unit 2 - Basic Structural Modeling in the subject of Object Oriented System Design

Sequence diagrams are one of the important structural modeling techniques used in Object Oriented System Design. They are used to represent the interaction between objects in a system in a specific sequence. In this section, we will learn about sequence diagrams, their advantages, and how to create them.

#### What are Sequence Diagrams?
Sequence diagrams are a type of behavioral diagrams that depict the interaction between objects in a system in a specific sequence. They show the sequence of messages exchanged between objects to complete a specific task or scenario. Sequence diagrams are used to analyze, design and test the behavior of a system.

#### Advantages of Sequence Diagrams
- Sequence diagrams help in understanding the flow of messages between objects in a system.
- They help in identifying the objects and their interactions in a system.
- Sequence diagrams are used to visualize the dynamic behavior of a system.
- They are used to identify potential problems and bottlenecks in a system.

#### Creating Sequence Diagrams
Sequence diagrams are created using a set of standard symbols and notations. These symbols and notations represent the objects, messages, and their interactions in a system. The steps to create a sequence diagram are as follows:
1. Identify the participating objects in the system.
2. Identify the messages exchanged between objects.
3. Determine the order of the messages exchanged between objects.
4. Draw the sequence diagram using the standard symbols and notations.

#### Applications of Sequence Diagrams
Sequence diagrams are used in various applications such as:
- Designing and testing of software systems.
- Identifying and resolving problems in existing systems.
- Documenting and communicating the behavior of a system to stakeholders.
