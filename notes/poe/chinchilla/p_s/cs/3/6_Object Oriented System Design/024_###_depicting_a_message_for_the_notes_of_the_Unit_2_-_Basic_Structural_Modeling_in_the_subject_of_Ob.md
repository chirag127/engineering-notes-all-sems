Welcome to the Unit 2 - Basic Structural Modeling in Object Oriented System Design. This unit is an essential part of the subject and requires your utmost attention. In this unit, we will focus on the fundamental concepts of structural modeling in object-oriented system design.

Here are some important points to keep in mind for this unit:

1. Definition of Structural Modeling: Structural modeling is a technique used to represent the structure of a system in terms of its components and their relationships. It helps us to understand the system's architecture and design.

2. Basic Concepts of Structural Modeling: There are some basic concepts that you need to understand before moving forward with structural modeling. These include:

   * Classes and Objects
   * Attributes and Methods
   * Inheritance and Polymorphism
   * Association and Aggregation

3. UML Diagrams: UML (Unified Modeling Language) is a graphical language used to represent the structural model of a system. The important UML diagrams used for structural modeling are:

   * Class Diagrams
   * Object Diagrams
   * Composite Structure Diagrams
   * Package Diagrams

4. Advantages of Structural Modeling: Some advantages of structural modeling are:

   * It helps in understanding the system's architecture and design.
   * It makes it easier to maintain and modify the system.
   * It helps in identifying and resolving design issues early in the development process.

5. Disadvantages of Structural Modeling: Some disadvantages of structural modeling are:

   * It can be time-consuming and expensive.
   * It may not always be possible to accurately model the system's behavior.

6. Applications of Structural Modeling: Structural modeling is used in various domains such as:

   * Software development
   * Business process modeling
   * System engineering
   * Database design

In conclusion, understanding the basic concepts of structural modeling and UML diagrams is crucial for a successful career in software development, system engineering, and related fields. Practice makes perfect, so make sure to practice creating different UML diagrams to master the technique.
