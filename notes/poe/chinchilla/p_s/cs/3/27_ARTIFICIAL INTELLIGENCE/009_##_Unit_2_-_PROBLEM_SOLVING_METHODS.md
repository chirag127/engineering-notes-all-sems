## Unit 2 - PROBLEM SOLVING METHODS

Problem-solving methods are essential skills that enable individuals to solve complex problems in different fields. These methods are used in various disciplines, including engineering, computer science, mathematics, and business. This unit aims to introduce the different problem-solving methods and techniques, and their applications in various fields.

The following are the topics covered in this unit:

1. **Problem-Solving Techniques:** This topic covers the different techniques used in problem-solving, including brainstorming, mind mapping, SWOT analysis, and root cause analysis. Each technique is explained in detail, and their advantages and disadvantages are discussed.

2. **Algorithmic Problem Solving:** This topic covers the use of algorithms in problem-solving. The concept of an algorithm is explained, and different algorithms are discussed, including sorting algorithms, searching algorithms, and graph algorithms. The advantages and disadvantages of each algorithm are also discussed.

3. **Heuristic Problem Solving:** This topic covers the use of heuristic methods in problem-solving. The concept of a heuristic is explained, and different heuristics are discussed, including hill climbing, simulated annealing, and genetic algorithms. The advantages and disadvantages of each heuristic are also discussed.

4. **Problem Decomposition:** This topic covers the process of breaking down a complex problem into smaller, more manageable sub-problems. The different techniques used in problem decomposition are discussed, including functional decomposition, object-oriented decomposition, and task decomposition.

5. **Creativity and Innovation in Problem Solving:** This topic covers the importance of creativity and innovation in problem-solving. The different techniques used in promoting creativity and innovation are discussed, including brainstorming, lateral thinking, and design thinking.

6. **Decision Making:** This topic covers the process of making decisions in problem-solving. The different types of decisions, including programmed decisions and non-programmed decisions, are discussed. The different decision-making models, including the rational decision-making model and the bounded rationality model, are also discussed.

In conclusion, this unit covers the different problem-solving methods and techniques used in various fields. By the end of this unit, learners will have a better understanding of how to solve complex problems using these methods and techniques.
