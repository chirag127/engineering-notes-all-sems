## Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION

Welcome to Unit 1 of our course! In this unit, we will cover the basics of the course and introduce you to some of the core concepts that you will need to understand in order to succeed in this class. 

In this unit, you will:

- Learn about the course structure and expectations
- Understand the importance of computer programming and its applications in various fields
- Explore the history of computer programming and its evolution.
- Learn about the different types of programming languages and their uses
- Explore the different tools and resources that are available to help you learn and practice programming
- Understand the basic building blocks of programming such as variables, data types, and control structures
- Learn how to write and run your first program

At the end of this unit, you should have a good understanding of what computer programming is, why it is important, and how to get started with writing your own programs. You will also have a basic understanding of the different tools and resources available to help you along the way.

In the next few weeks, we will dive deeper into the world of programming and explore more advanced concepts and techniques. But for now, let's focus on getting a strong foundation in the basics. 

Remember to take your time and practice, practice, practice! Programming is a skill that takes time and effort to master, but with dedication and hard work, you can become a great programmer. 

Good luck!
