### Heuristics for the notes of the Unit 2 - PROBLEM SOLVING METHODS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

Problem-solving is a crucial aspect of Artificial Intelligence, and it involves finding solutions to complex problems using various techniques. Heuristics is one of the problem-solving methods that are frequently used in AI. Heuristics is a problem-solving strategy that involves using a practical approach to solve problems that may not have a guaranteed solution. In this section, we will explore the different heuristics techniques that can be applied in problem-solving.

#### 1. Hill Climbing

Hill Climbing is a heuristic technique that involves choosing the best possible solution at each step of the problem-solving process. The method starts with an initial solution and moves towards the best solution by making incremental changes. Hill Climbing is a simple and efficient method but can get stuck in local optima.

#### 2. Simulated Annealing

Simulated Annealing is a heuristic technique that is used to overcome the local optima problem that is faced by Hill Climbing. The method simulates the annealing process used in metallurgy to cool down metals. The technique involves accepting worse solutions initially and gradually reduces the acceptance of worse solutions as the algorithm proceeds. Simulated Annealing is more effective than Hill Climbing in finding global optima but is computationally expensive.

#### 3. Genetic Algorithms

Genetic Algorithms is a heuristic technique that is inspired by the principles of evolution. The method involves encoding the problem domain into a chromosome and then applying recombination and mutation to generate new chromosomes. The algorithm selects the fittest chromosomes to produce the next generation of solutions. Genetic Algorithms are effective in solving problems with large search spaces but can be computationally expensive.

#### 4. Tabu Search

Tabu Search is a heuristic technique that is used to overcome the problem of getting stuck in local optima. The method involves keeping track of recently visited solutions and marking them as taboo. The algorithm avoids visiting taboo solutions and explores new solutions. Tabu Search is more effective than Hill Climbing in finding global optima but is computationally expensive.

#### 5. Ant Colony Optimization

Ant Colony Optimization is a heuristic technique that is inspired by the behavior of ants. The method involves simulating the behavior of ants that lay pheromones to mark their paths. The algorithm uses the pheromone trails to generate new solutions and gradually improves the quality of the solutions. Ant Colony Optimization is effective in solving problems with a large search space and is computationally efficient.

In conclusion, heuristics is a powerful problem-solving technique that can be used in Artificial Intelligence. The choice of heuristic technique depends on the problem domain and the available computational resources. It is essential to understand the different heuristic techniques and their advantages and disadvantages to apply them effectively in problem-solving.
