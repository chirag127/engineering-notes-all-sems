### Problem Solving Approach to Typical AI problems
Problem-solving is one of the most critical aspects of Artificial Intelligence. In AI, problem-solving refers to the process of finding solutions to complex problems using algorithms and computational methods. The problem-solving approach is essential in AI because most AI problems are complex and require a systematic approach to solve them. In this section, we will discuss the problem-solving approach to typical AI problems.

#### Steps in Problem Solving Approach
The following are the steps involved in the problem-solving approach to typical AI problems:
1. Define the problem: The first step in the problem-solving approach is to define the problem. In this step, we need to identify the problem or the task that needs to be solved. We need to be clear about what we want to achieve and what the problem is.

2. Analyze the problem: The next step is to analyze the problem. In this step, we need to understand the problem and identify the factors that are contributing to the problem. We need to identify the inputs, outputs, constraints, and other relevant factors that are relevant to the problem.

3. Identify the solution approach: In this step, we need to identify the solution approach. We need to decide which algorithm or method we will use to solve the problem. We need to choose the approach that is most appropriate for the problem.

4. Implement the solution: The next step is to implement the solution. In this step, we need to write the code or develop the algorithm to solve the problem. We need to test the solution and make sure that it works correctly.

5. Evaluate the solution: The final step is to evaluate the solution. In this step, we need to test the solution and measure its performance. We need to analyze the results and determine if the solution meets the requirements and if it is efficient.

#### Advantages of Problem Solving Approach
- It provides a systematic approach to solving complex problems.
- It helps in identifying the factors that contribute to the problem.
- It helps in choosing the most appropriate solution approach.
- It ensures that the solution meets the requirements and is efficient.

#### Disadvantages of Problem Solving Approach
- It can be time-consuming.
- It requires a high level of expertise and knowledge.
- It may not always result in the best solution.
