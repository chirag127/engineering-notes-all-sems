## Unit 4 - SOFTWARE AGENTS

Software agents are autonomous entities that are capable of performing tasks on behalf of their users. They are essentially software programs that can interact with other software programs or users to achieve a specific goal. In this unit, we will explore the concept of software agents and their applications in various fields.
