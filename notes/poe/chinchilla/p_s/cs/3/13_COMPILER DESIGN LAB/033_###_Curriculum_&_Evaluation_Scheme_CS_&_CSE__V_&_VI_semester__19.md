### Curriculum & Evaluation Scheme CS & CSE (V & VI semester) 19

The curriculum and evaluation scheme for Computer Science and Computer Science Engineering (CS & CSE) in the fifth and sixth semesters provides a comprehensive understanding of various computer science concepts and technologies. This scheme has been designed to equip students with the necessary knowledge and skills to succeed in their careers.

The following topics are covered in the fifth and sixth semesters:

#### V Semester
- Design and Analysis of Algorithms
- Database Management System
- Computer Networks
- Operating Systems
- Object-Oriented Programming
- Lab-III (DBMS Lab)
- Lab-IV (Networks Lab)

#### VI Semester
- Compiler Design
- Artificial Intelligence
- Web Technologies
- Software Engineering
- Elective - I
- Lab-V (Compiler Lab)
- Lab-VI (Web Technologies Lab)

In addition to the above courses, students are also required to undertake a project in their final year. The project is aimed at providing hands-on experience to students in the implementation of software systems.

Evaluation of students is based on a combination of internal and external assessments. Internal assessment includes assignments, quizzes, and lab work, while external assessment is based on a final examination.

Advantages of this curriculum and evaluation scheme include:

- Comprehensive coverage of important computer science concepts and technologies
- Emphasis on hands-on experience through lab work and projects
- Assessment based on both internal and external evaluations, providing a well-rounded evaluation of student performance

Disadvantages of this curriculum and evaluation scheme include:

- Potentially high workload due to the number of courses and assignments
- Limited scope for specialization due to the fixed set of courses

Examples of applications of the concepts covered in this curriculum and evaluation scheme include:

- Designing and developing algorithms for various applications
- Developing and managing databases to store and retrieve data
- Configuring and managing computer networks
- Developing software systems using object-oriented programming techniques
- Designing and developing compilers for programming languages
- Developing artificial intelligence systems for various applications
- Developing web applications using various technologies

Overall, the curriculum and evaluation scheme for CS & CSE (V & VI semester) provides students with a strong foundation in various computer science concepts and technologies, and prepares them for a successful career in the field.
