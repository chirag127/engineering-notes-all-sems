## Unit 4 - Transaction Processing Concept

Transaction processing is a vital aspect of information systems that deals with the collection, processing, and storage of transactional data. It involves the recording and processing of business transactions in an organization, including sales, purchases, and payments.

- Types of transactions
- Properties of transactions

- Definition of transaction management in relational databases
- Importance of transaction management in relational databases
- Techniques for transaction management in relational databases
- Advantages and disadvantages of transaction management techniques in relational databases

By the end of this unit, you should have a clear understanding of the concepts and techniques involved in transaction processing, including the importance of ACID properties, concurrency control, recovery and undo/redo, distributed transaction processing, and transaction management in relational databases. This knowledge will be essential for anyone working with information systems and databases in a business environment.
