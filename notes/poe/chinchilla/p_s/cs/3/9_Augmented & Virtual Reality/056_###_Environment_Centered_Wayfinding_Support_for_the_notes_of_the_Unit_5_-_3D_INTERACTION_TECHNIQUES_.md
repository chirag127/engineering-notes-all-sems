### Environment Centered Wayfinding Support

Environment Centered Wayfinding Support is a technique used in Augmented & Virtual Reality to provide effective navigation assistance to users in unfamiliar environments. Here are some important points to note about this technique:

- The technique is used to provide users with navigational assistance by mapping the environment around them and highlighting the best possible path to their destination.
- The technique primarily focuses on providing the user with the most direct path to their destination, while taking into account the user's comfort and safety.
- The technique makes use of various visual cues such as arrows, trails, glowing pathways, etc. to guide the user through the environment.
- The technique can be applied to both indoor and outdoor environments, and can be customized to suit the needs of different users.
- One of the key advantages of this technique is that it helps users navigate through complex environments with ease, without the need for additional navigation tools or assistance.
- The technique can be applied to a wide range of scenarios, including museums, airports, shopping malls, theme parks, hospitals, and more.
- The technique can be used in conjunction with other 3D interaction techniques, such as object manipulation, object selection, and gesture recognition, to enhance the overall user experience.
- However, one of the potential disadvantages of this technique is that it may lead to over-reliance on technology, which may limit the user's ability to navigate through unfamiliar environments on their own.
- Another potential disadvantage is that the technique may require a significant amount of computing power, which may limit its use on low-end devices.

Overall, Environment Centered Wayfinding Support is an important technique that can greatly enhance the user experience in Augmented & Virtual Reality applications. By providing effective navigational assistance to users, it can help users navigate through complex environments with ease and comfort, while also enhancing the overall user experience.
