### Taxonomy for the notes of the Unit 8 - Augmented and Mixed Reality in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

In this unit, we will be exploring the concepts of Augmented Reality (AR) and Mixed Reality (MR). AR and MR are two technologies that have gained immense popularity in recent years due to their unique ability to enhance the user's perception of reality. In order to fully understand AR and MR, it is important to first understand their taxonomy. 

Here are some important points to keep in mind when studying the taxonomy of AR and MR:

1. AR vs MR: Augmented Reality (AR) is a technology that overlays digital information onto the physical world, while Mixed Reality (MR) is a technology that combines virtual objects with the physical world in a seamless manner. 

2. Spatial Mapping: Spatial mapping is the process of creating a digital representation of the physical environment. This is an important aspect of AR and MR as it allows the virtual objects to be accurately placed in the physical world. 

3. Depth Perception: Depth perception is the ability to perceive the distance of objects. In AR and MR, depth perception is important in order to accurately place virtual objects in the physical world. This is typically achieved through the use of sensors such as cameras and infrared sensors. 

4. Tracking: Tracking refers to the ability to track the user's position and orientation in the physical world. This is typically achieved through the use of sensors such as GPS, accelerometers, and gyroscopes. 

5. Display: Display refers to the way in which the AR or MR content is presented to the user. This can be through a variety of devices such as smartphones, tablets, AR glasses, and MR headsets. 

6. User Interaction: User interaction refers to how the user interacts with the AR or MR content. This can be through gestures, voice commands, or even brain-computer interfaces. 

7. Applications: AR and MR have a wide range of applications in various industries such as gaming, education, healthcare, and entertainment. Some popular examples of AR and MR applications include Pokemon Go, Microsoft HoloLens, and Snapchat filters. 

Overall, understanding the taxonomy of AR and MR is crucial in order to fully grasp the capabilities and limitations of these technologies. By studying this taxonomy, we can better understand how AR and MR are able to enhance our perception of reality and create new opportunities for innovation and creativity.
