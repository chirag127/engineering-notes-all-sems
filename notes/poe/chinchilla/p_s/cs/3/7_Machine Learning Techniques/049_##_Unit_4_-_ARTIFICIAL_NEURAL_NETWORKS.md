## Unit 4 - ARTIFICIAL NEURAL NETWORKS

Artificial neural networks (ANNs) are a subset of machine learning that is based on the structure and function of the human brain. ANNs are used to model complex nonlinear relationships between inputs and outputs, and they have been successfully applied to a wide range of tasks including image recognition, speech recognition, natural language processing, and control systems.

### Architecture of Artificial Neural Networks

- An ANN is composed of a large number of interconnected processing elements, called neurons, which are organized into layers.
- The input layer receives input signals from the environment, and the output layer produces output signals.
- The hidden layers, which are sandwiched between the input and output layers, perform computations on the input signals before passing them on to the next layer.
- Each neuron in an ANN receives input from other neurons or external sources, processes the input using a nonlinear activation function, and then sends its output to other neurons or to the output layer.

### Training ANNs

- ANNs are trained using a process called backpropagation, which involves adjusting the weights and biases of the neurons in the network to minimize the difference between the network's outputs and the desired outputs.
- The training process involves feeding the network with a set of input-output pairs, called training examples, and adjusting the weights and biases to minimize the difference between the network's outputs and the desired outputs.
- The training process continues until the network's performance on a validation set of examples stops improving.

### Advantages of ANNs

- ANNs can learn complex nonlinear relationships between inputs and outputs.
- ANNs can generalize well to new inputs that are similar to the inputs used during training.
- ANNs can be used to model a wide range of problems, including classification, regression, and control.

### Disadvantages of ANNs

- ANNs can be computationally expensive to train and evaluate, especially for large datasets.
- ANNs can be prone to overfitting, where the network learns the training data too well and fails to generalize to new inputs.
- ANNs can be difficult to interpret, making it challenging to understand how the network is making its predictions.

### Applications of ANNs

- ANNs are used in image and speech recognition, natural language processing, and control systems.
- ANNs are used in finance for predicting stock prices and in healthcare for diagnosing diseases.
- ANNs are used in robotics and autonomous vehicles for perception and control.

Overall, ANNs are a powerful tool for modeling complex nonlinear relationships between inputs and outputs. However, they require careful training and validation to avoid overfitting and can be challenging to interpret. ANNs have a wide range of applications in fields such as image recognition, speech recognition, natural language processing, finance, healthcare, robotics, and autonomous vehicles.
