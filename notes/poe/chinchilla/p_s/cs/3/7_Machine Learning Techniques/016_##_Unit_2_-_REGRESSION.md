## Unit 2 - REGRESSION

Regression is a statistical method that is used to estimate the relationship between two or more variables. It is used to predict the value of a dependent variable based on the value of one or more independent variables. Regression is widely used in various fields, such as economics, finance, psychology, and sociology, to name a few. In this unit, we will study regression in detail.

### 1. Linear Regression

Linear regression is a statistical method used to estimate the relationship between a dependent variable and one or more independent variables. It is one of the most widely used methods in regression analysis. Linear regression can be of two types, namely, simple linear regression and multiple linear regression. Simple linear regression involves only one independent variable, while multiple linear regression involves more than one independent variable.

#### Advantages of Linear Regression
- It is easy to understand and implement.
- It is widely used in various fields.
- It is robust and works well even if the data is not normally distributed.
- It provides a measure of the strength of the relationship between the variables.

#### Disadvantages of Linear Regression
- It assumes a linear relationship between the variables.
- It may not work well if the data is highly skewed or has outliers.
- It may not work well if there is multicollinearity among the independent variables.

### 2. Logistic Regression

Logistic regression is a statistical method used to estimate the relationship between a dependent variable and one or more independent variables. It is used when the dependent variable is binary or categorical. Logistic regression can be used to predict the probability of an event occurring, such as the probability of a person buying a product or the probability of a person voting for a particular candidate.

#### Advantages of Logistic Regression
- It is widely used in various fields, such as marketing, healthcare, and finance.
- It provides a measure of the probability of an event occurring.
- It can handle both continuous and categorical independent variables.

#### Disadvantages of Logistic Regression
- It assumes a linear relationship between the variables.
- It may not work well if there is multicollinearity among the independent variables.
- It may not work well if there are too many independent variables.

### 3. Nonlinear Regression

Nonlinear regression is a statistical method used to estimate the relationship between a dependent variable and one or more independent variables when the relationship is not linear. Nonlinear regression can be used to model various types of relationships, such as exponential, logarithmic, and power relationships.

#### Advantages of Nonlinear Regression
- It can model a wide range of relationships.
- It can handle both continuous and categorical independent variables.
- It can provide a better fit to the data than linear regression.

#### Disadvantages of Nonlinear Regression
- It can be difficult to understand and implement.
- It may require a large amount of data to estimate the parameters accurately.
- It may not work well if the initial parameter values are not chosen carefully.

### 4. Applications of Regression

Regression is widely used in various fields, such as economics, finance, marketing, healthcare, and engineering, to name a few. Some of the applications of regression are as follows:
- Predicting sales based on advertising spending.
- Estimating the effect of education on income.
- Predicting the probability of a patient having a particular disease based on their age, gender, and other factors.
- Estimating the effect of temperature on the growth of plants.

In conclusion, regression is a powerful statistical method that is widely used in various fields. Linear regression, logistic regression, and nonlinear regression are the three main types of regression that we have studied in this unit. Each type has its advantages and disadvantages, and their choice depends on the nature of the data and the research question.
