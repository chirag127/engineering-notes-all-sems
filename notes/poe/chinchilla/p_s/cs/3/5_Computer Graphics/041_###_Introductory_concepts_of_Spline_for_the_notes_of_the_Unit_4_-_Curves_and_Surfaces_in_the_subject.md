### Introductory Concepts of Spline for the Notes of Unit 4 - Curves and Surfaces in the Subject of Computer Graphics

Spline is a mathematical function used in the field of computer graphics to create smooth curves and surfaces. It is widely used in various applications such as animation, computer-aided design (CAD), and video game development. This section will provide an overview of the introductory concepts of Spline for the notes of Unit 4 - Curves and Surfaces in the subject of Computer Graphics.

#### Definition of Spline

A Spline is a smooth curve defined by a set of control points. The curve passes through the control points and is interpolated in between them. Spline curves are widely used in computer graphics for modeling and animation. They are defined by a set of polynomial functions that are smoothly joined together.

#### Types of Splines

There are different types of Splines that are used in computer graphics, including:

- B-spline: It is a type of Spline that is defined by a set of control points and a degree. B-splines are widely used in computer graphics because they can represent complex shapes and can be efficiently evaluated.

- NURBS: Non-Uniform Rational B-Spline (NURBS) is a type of Spline that is widely used in computer graphics for modeling and animation. NURBS curves and surfaces are defined by a set of control points, weights, and knots.

#### Advantages of Splines

- Splines are smooth and continuous curves that can be used to represent complex shapes and surfaces.

- They are flexible and can be easily modified by adjusting the control points.

- Splines can be used to create animations and simulations.

- They can be efficiently evaluated and rendered by computer hardware.

#### Disadvantages of Splines

- Splines can be computationally expensive to evaluate and render, particularly for complex shapes and surfaces.

- They may not accurately represent sharp corners or edges.

#### Applications of Splines

- Splines are widely used in computer graphics for modeling and animation.

- They are used in computer-aided design (CAD) to represent complex shapes and surfaces.

- Splines are used in video game development to create smooth animations and realistic environments.

#### Conclusion

Splines are an important mathematical function used in computer graphics to create smooth curves and surfaces. They are widely used in various applications such as animation, CAD, and video game development.
