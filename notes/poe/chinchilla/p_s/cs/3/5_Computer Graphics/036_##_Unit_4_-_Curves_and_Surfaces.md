## Unit 4 - Curves and Surfaces

This unit focuses on the study of curves and surfaces in computer graphics. The following topics will be covered in this unit:

### 1. Introduction to Curves and Surfaces

- Definition of curves and surfaces
- Applications of curves and surfaces in computer graphics
- Types of curves and surfaces

### 2. Parametric Curves

- Definition of parametric curves
- Representation of parametric curves using Bezier curves and B-splines
- Advantages and disadvantages of Bezier curves and B-splines
- Examples of parametric curves in computer graphics

### 3. Parametric Surfaces

- Definition of parametric surfaces
- Representation of parametric surfaces using Bezier surfaces and B-spline surfaces
- Advantages and disadvantages of Bezier surfaces and B-spline surfaces
- Examples of parametric surfaces in computer graphics

### 4. Non-parametric Curves and Surfaces

- Definition of non-parametric curves and surfaces
- Representation of non-parametric curves and surfaces using polygonal meshes and subdivision surfaces
- Advantages and disadvantages of polygonal meshes and subdivision surfaces
- Examples of non-parametric curves and surfaces in computer graphics

### 5. Surface Reconstruction

- Definition of surface reconstruction
- Techniques for surface reconstruction from point clouds and mesh data
- Advantages and disadvantages of surface reconstruction techniques
- Examples of surface reconstruction in computer graphics

Overall, this unit provides a comprehensive understanding of curves and surfaces in computer graphics and their applications. It also covers various techniques and representation methods used in computer graphics for modeling and rendering curves and surfaces.
