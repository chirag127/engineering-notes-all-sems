## Unit 3 - Three Dimensional

Three-dimensional (3D) refers to objects or images that have three dimensions or appear to have depth, height, and width. This unit focuses on the study of 3D geometry and its applications. Here are the topics that will be covered in this unit:

### 1. Three-Dimensional Coordinate Systems
- Definition of 3D coordinate systems
- The Cartesian coordinate system
- The cylindrical coordinate system
- The spherical coordinate system
- Conversion between coordinate systems
- Applications of 3D coordinate systems

### 2. 3D Shapes
- Definition of 3D shapes
- Types of 3D shapes (prisms, pyramids, cylinders, cones, spheres)
- Properties of 3D shapes (surface area, volume, Euler's formula)
- Applications of 3D shapes in real life (architecture, engineering, design)

### 3. Transformations in 3D
- Definition of transformations in 3D
- Types of transformations (translation, rotation, scaling)
- Matrix representation of 3D transformations
- Composition of transformations
- Applications of transformations in computer graphics and animation

### 4. 3D Graphics
- Definition of 3D graphics
- Rendering techniques (ray tracing, rasterization)
- 3D modeling software (Blender, SketchUp, 3ds Max)
- Applications of 3D graphics in video games, movies, and virtual reality

It is important to understand the concepts and apply them to real-life examples to fully grasp the topics covered in this unit. Practice problems and exercises will be provided to reinforce understanding and prepare for exams.
