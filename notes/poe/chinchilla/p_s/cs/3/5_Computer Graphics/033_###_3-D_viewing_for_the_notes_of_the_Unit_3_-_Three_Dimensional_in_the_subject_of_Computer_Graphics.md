### 3-D Viewing for the Notes of the Unit 3 - Three Dimensional in the Subject of Computer Graphics

In computer graphics, 3-Dimensional viewing is the process of transforming a 3D object in the world coordinate system into a 2D image that can be displayed on a 2D display device. The process involves various stages, including projection, clipping, and visibility determination. In this unit, we will discuss the concepts of 3-D viewing in detail. 

Below are some important points to understand 3-D viewing:

1. Projection: It is the process of converting 3D coordinates into 2D coordinates. The two most commonly used projections are Orthographic and Perspective projection. Orthographic projection involves projecting the 3D object onto a plane parallel to one of the coordinate planes. Perspective projection involves projecting the object onto a plane that is perpendicular to the viewing direction.

2. Clipping: It is the process of removing the parts of the object that are outside the view volume. The view volume is defined by the position of the camera and the projection plane. The parts of the object that are not visible are removed from the image.

3. Visibility determination: It is the process of determining which parts of the object are visible and which parts are hidden from the viewer's perspective. The process involves considering the position of the camera, the projection plane, and the object's orientation.

4. Advantages of 3-D viewing: 

   * It provides a more realistic representation of the object.
   * It allows the user to view the object from different angles.
   * It enables the user to interact with the object in a more intuitive way.

5. Disadvantages of 3-D viewing:

   * It requires more processing power and memory compared to 2D graphics.
   * The complexity of the image may affect the rendering speed.
   * The viewer may experience motion sickness if the graphics are not rendered properly.

6. Examples of 3-D graphics applications:

   * Video games
   * Virtual reality
   * 3-D modeling
   * Medical imaging

In conclusion, understanding 3-D viewing is crucial for anyone interested in computer graphics as it plays an important role in creating realistic and interactive graphics. The concepts of projection, clipping, and visibility determination are essential to master to create high-quality 3D graphics.
