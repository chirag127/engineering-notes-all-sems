## Unit 5 - Hidden Lines and Surfaces

In computer graphics, Hidden Lines and Surfaces refer to the techniques used to hide or remove the unwanted parts of a 3D object that are not visible to the viewer. These techniques are important for creating realistic 3D images and models. In this unit, we will cover the following topics:

1. Hidden Line Removal
   - This technique is used to remove the edges and lines that are hidden from view. It is achieved by determining which edges are visible and which are not, and then removing the hidden edges. This can be done using various algorithms such as the Depth-first search algorithm, Back-face culling, and Ray casting algorithm.

2. Hidden Surface Removal
   - This technique is used to remove the hidden surfaces that are not visible to the viewer. It is achieved by determining which surfaces are visible and which are not, and then removing the hidden surfaces. This can be done using various algorithms such as the Z-buffer algorithm, Scan-line algorithm, and Painter's algorithm.

3. Advantages and Disadvantages of Hidden Line and Surface Removal
   - Advantages:
      - It makes the image look more realistic by removing unwanted parts.
      - It reduces the complexity of the image, making it easier to render.
      - It saves memory and processing power by removing hidden objects.
   - Disadvantages:
      - It can be time-consuming and computationally intensive.
      - It may not always work perfectly, resulting in artifacts and errors.
      - It may require additional resources such as memory and processing power.

4. Applications of Hidden Line and Surface Removal
   - This technique is used in various fields such as architecture, engineering, video game development, and movie production. It is used to create realistic 3D models and images of buildings, machines, vehicles, and characters.

In conclusion, Hidden Lines and Surfaces are important techniques used in computer graphics to create realistic 3D models and images. By removing the hidden parts, the image looks more realistic and less complex, making it easier to render. These techniques are used in various industries such as architecture, engineering, and entertainment to create stunning visuals.
