### Blobby Objects for the Notes of the Unit 4 - Curves and Surfaces in the Subject of Computer Graphics

In computer graphics, Blobby objects are used to represent complex shapes that are difficult to model using traditional geometric primitives. These objects are also known as metaballs or soft objects, and they are often used in applications such as medical imaging, special effects in movies, and video game design.

Here are some important points to understand about Blobby objects in computer graphics:

- Blobby objects are defined by a set of equations that describe the surface of the object. These equations are based on the physical properties of the material being represented, such as its density, viscosity, and elasticity.
- The surface of a Blobby object is not defined by a single mathematical equation, but rather by a collection of smaller functions called "blobs" that are combined to create the final shape of the object.
- Each blob is defined by a center point, a radius, and a weight. The weight determines how much influence the blob has on the final shape of the object. Blobs that are closer to the surface of the object have more influence than those that are farther away.
- The shape of a Blobby object can be easily manipulated by changing the parameters of the individual blobs. For example, increasing the weight of a blob will cause it to have a greater impact on the overall shape of the object.
- Blobby objects are often used to create organic shapes that are difficult to model with traditional geometric primitives. For example, they can be used to model the shape of a cloud or a tree, or to create a realistic representation of a human face or body.
- One advantage of Blobby objects is that they are very flexible and can be easily deformed or animated. This makes them ideal for applications such as character animation in video games or movies.
- However, one disadvantage of Blobby objects is that they can be computationally expensive to render. Because the surface of the object is defined by a large number of individual blobs, it can take a lot of processing power to calculate the final shape.
- Blobby objects are often used in combination with other techniques such as ray tracing or polygonal meshes to create complex, realistic scenes in computer graphics.

Overall, Blobby objects are a powerful tool for modeling complex shapes in computer graphics.
