#### Walk Through (Walkthrough) Static testing strategy

Walkthrough is a static testing technique that is used to review the work product. The review is performed by a group of people who examine the document or code to find errors, defects or other issues. The review is performed manually by the team members and does not involve the use of any tools or automated scripts.

The walkthrough technique can be used for reviewing any work product including requirements, design documents, code, test cases, etc. The main objective of the walkthrough technique is to identify defects early in the development life cycle and reduce the cost of fixing them later.

The following are the steps involved in the walkthrough technique:

1. Planning - The first step is to plan the walkthrough. This includes identifying the work product to be reviewed, the team members who will participate in the review, and the schedule for the review.

2. Preparation - The next step is to prepare for the walkthrough. This includes distributing the work product to be reviewed to the team members, scheduling a meeting time, and ensuring that all team members are familiar with the work product.

3. Walkthrough - The walkthrough meeting is held where the team members review the work product. The team members identify defects, errors, and other issues and document them.

4. Follow-up - After the walkthrough is complete, the team members follow-up on the defects and issues identified during the review. The issues are assigned to the appropriate team member for resolution.

Advantages of Walkthrough:

- Early identification of defects and issues
- Reduction in the cost of fixing defects
- Improved quality of the work product
- Improved team communication and collaboration

Disadvantages of Walkthrough:

- Time-consuming process
- Requires a skilled team to perform the review
- May not find all defects and issues

Examples of Walkthrough:

- Reviewing requirements documents
- Reviewing design documents
- Reviewing code
- Reviewing test cases

Applications of Walkthrough:

- Software development
- Quality assurance
- Project management

In conclusion, the walkthrough technique is a valuable static testing strategy that can help identify defects early in the development life cycle. It requires a skilled team to perform the review and may be time-consuming, but it can improve the quality of the work product and reduce the cost of fixing defects.
