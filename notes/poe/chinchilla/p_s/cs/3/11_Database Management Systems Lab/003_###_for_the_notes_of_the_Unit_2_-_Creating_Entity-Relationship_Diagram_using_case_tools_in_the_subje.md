#### Network performance and transmission impairments in Computer Networks

Computer networks are used for data transmission over a long distance. The performance of a network is measured by its throughput, delay, and packet loss. The transmission impairments are the factors that affect the network's performance. Here are some of the network performance and transmission impairments in computer networks:

1. **Bandwidth:** It is the amount of data that can be transmitted over a network in a given time. The bandwidth of a network can be limited by the physical medium or the network devices. The bandwidth is measured in bits per second (bps) or bytes per second (Bps).

2. **Latency:** It is the time taken to transmit data from the sender to the receiver. The latency is affected by the distance between the sender and the receiver, the speed of the transmission medium, and the network devices. The latency is measured in milliseconds (ms).

3. **Jitter:** It is the variation in the delay of the packets that are transmitted over a network. The jitter is caused by the difference in the time taken by different packets to reach the destination. The jitter is measured in milliseconds (ms).

4. **Packet loss:** It is the loss of packets during data transmission. The packet loss can be caused by network congestion, errors in the network devices, or the physical medium. The packet loss is measured in percentage (%) of the total packets transmitted.

5. **Noise:** It is the unwanted signal that interferes with the transmission of data over a network. The noise is caused by the external factors such as electromagnetic interference, radio frequency interference, and crosstalk. The noise is measured in decibels (dB).

To overcome these impairments, various techniques are used such as error correction, flow control, congestion control, and quality of service (QoS). The QoS ensures that the network resources are allocated to the critical applications to ensure their performance.

In conclusion, the network performance and transmission impairments are the factors that affect the performance of a computer network. The understanding of these impairments is essential for the network administrators to ensure the smooth functioning of the network.
