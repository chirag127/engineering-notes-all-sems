### Components for the notes of the Unit 1 - DIGITAL IMAGE FUNDAMENTALS in the subject of Image Processing

Digital Image Fundamentals is an important topic in the subject of Image Processing that lays the foundation for understanding various aspects of digital images. In this unit, we will study the various components of digital images that are essential to understand the principles of image processing. Below are the components that we will cover in this unit:

1. Pixels: 
    - A pixel is the smallest unit of a digital image.
    - It is represented by a binary value that determines its color and intensity.
    - The resolution of an image is determined by the number of pixels it contains.
    - Each pixel can be represented in various color models such as RGB, CMYK, and HSL.

2. Image Size:
    - The size of an image is determined by the number of pixels it contains.
    - It is measured in terms of width and height in pixels.
    - The aspect ratio of an image is the ratio of its width to its height.

3. Bit Depth:
    - Bit depth refers to the number of bits used to represent each pixel.
    - Common bit depths are 1, 8, 16, and 32 bits.
    - Higher bit depth images can represent more colors and have better image quality.

4. Color Models:
    - A color model is a mathematical representation of colors.
    - RGB, CMYK, and HSL are the most commonly used color models.
    - Each color model has its advantages and disadvantages.

5. Image File Formats:
    - Image file formats determine how images are stored.
    - Common image file formats are JPEG, PNG, GIF, and BMP.
    - Each file format has its advantages and disadvantages.

6. Image Enhancement:
    - Image enhancement techniques are used to improve the quality of images.
    - Techniques such as histogram equalization, contrast stretching, and noise reduction are commonly used.
    - Image enhancement can improve the visual appearance of images and make them easier to analyze.

In conclusion, understanding the components of digital images is crucial for understanding the principles of image processing. This unit provides a solid foundation for further study in the field of image processing.
