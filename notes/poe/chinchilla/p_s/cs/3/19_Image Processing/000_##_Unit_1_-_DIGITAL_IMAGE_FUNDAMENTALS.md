## Unit 1 - DIGITAL IMAGE FUNDAMENTALS

Digital Image Fundamentals is a crucial topic in the field of digital image processing. It covers the basics of digital image processing, which includes the representation of images, image acquisition and sampling, image quantization, and image enhancement. In this unit, we will learn about the following topics:

### 1. Image Representation
- Definition of digital images
- Types of digital images
- Image resolution
- Image size and aspect ratio
- Image depth and color models

### 2. Image Acquisition and Sampling
- Image acquisition process
- Sampling theorem
- Sampling rate
- Aliasing
- Image interpolation

### 3. Image Quantization
- Quantization process
- Uniform and non-uniform quantization
- Bit depth and dynamic range
- Gray-level quantization
- Color quantization

### 4. Image Enhancement
- Histogram equalization
- Contrast stretching
- Image filtering
- Noise reduction
- Edge detection

By studying this unit, you will gain a solid understanding of the fundamental principles of digital image processing. Additionally, you will learn about the following concepts:

- Advantages and disadvantages of digital image processing
- Applications of digital image processing in various fields such as medical imaging, surveillance, and remote sensing
- Basic image processing algorithms and techniques
- Image compression techniques

In conclusion, Digital Image Fundamentals is an essential topic that forms the foundation of digital image processing. Understanding the concepts covered in this unit is crucial for anyone interested in pursuing a career in image processing or related fields.
