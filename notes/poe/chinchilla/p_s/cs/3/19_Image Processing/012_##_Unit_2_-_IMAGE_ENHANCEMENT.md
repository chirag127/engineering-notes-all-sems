## Unit 2 - IMAGE ENHANCEMENT

Image enhancement is the process of improving the quality of an image to make it visually more appealing and informative. In this unit, we will learn about various techniques of image enhancement. 

### Topics to be covered in this unit:

1. Basic concepts of image enhancement
2. Point processing operations
3. Histogram equalization
4. Spatial filtering
5. Frequency domain filtering
6. Edge detection
7. Noise reduction techniques

- Objective of image enhancement
- Image representation and its characteristics
- Image processing systems

### Applications of image enhancement:

- Medical imaging
- Forensic analysis
- Satellite imaging
- Photography
- Video processing
- Computer vision

Image enhancement is an important field of image processing that has its applications in various domains. Understanding the concepts and techniques of image enhancement is essential for anyone working in the field of image processing.
