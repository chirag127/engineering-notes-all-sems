### Electronic mail in application layer

Electronic mail, commonly known as email, is one of the most popular applications of the internet. Email is a method of exchanging digital messages between people using digital devices such as computers, tablets or smartphones. It is a part of the application layer protocols of the internet.

#### Architecture of Email System

The architecture of the email system comprises of the following components:

1. User Agent: It is an email client used by the user to compose, send, read, and organize emails. Examples of user agents include Gmail, Yahoo Mail, Outlook, etc.

2. Mail Server: It is a server that handles email storage and transfers. A mail server is responsible for receiving incoming messages, storing them in a mailbox, and delivering outgoing messages to the recipient's mail server.

3. Simple Mail Transfer Protocol (SMTP): SMTP is a protocol used for sending emails from one mail server to another. It is a client-server protocol that allows the user agent to transfer the email message to the mail server.

4. Post Office Protocol (POP) or Internet Message Access Protocol (IMAP): POP and IMAP are two protocols used for retrieving emails from the mail server. The POP protocol downloads the email to the user's device, whereas IMAP allows the user to read the email directly from the server.

#### Advantages of Email

1. Fast and Convenient: Email is a fast and convenient way to send messages to people around the world.

2. Cost-Effective: Email is a cost-effective way of communication as it eliminates the need for postage stamps and envelopes.

3. Easy to Use: Email is easy to use and does not require any technical knowledge.

4. Reliable: Email is a reliable way of communication as messages are stored on the mail server and can be accessed even if the device is lost.

#### Disadvantages of Email

1. Security: Emails can be intercepted and read by unauthorized persons, which can lead to data breaches.

2. Spam: Spam emails are unsolicited emails that can clutter the inbox and may contain malicious links or attachments.

3. Viruses: Emails can contain viruses that can infect the device and cause damage.

#### Examples of Email Applications

1. Gmail: It is a popular email service provided by Google.

2. Outlook: It is an email client developed by Microsoft.

3. Yahoo Mail: It is an email service provided by Yahoo.

#### Applications of Email

1. Personal Communication: Email is widely used for personal communication between friends and family.

2. Business Communication: Email is an essential tool for business communication as it allows employees to communicate with each other and clients.

3. Marketing: Email marketing is a popular way for businesses to reach out to their customers and promote their products and services.

In conclusion, email is a vital application of the internet that has revolutionized communication. It provides a fast, cost-effective, and convenient way of exchanging digital messages. However, it is essential to be aware of the security risks associated with email and take necessary precautions to protect personal and sensitive information.
