## Module 4-Science, Management and Indian Knowledge System

This module aims to provide an overview of the Indian Knowledge System and its relevance to science and management. It covers the following topics:

### Indian Knowledge System

- Definition and scope of the Indian Knowledge System
- Characteristics of the Indian Knowledge System
- Differences between the Indian and Western Knowledge Systems
- Importance of the Indian Knowledge System in contemporary times

### Science and Indian Knowledge System

- Role of Indian Knowledge System in the development of science
- Contribution of Indian scientists in various fields of science
- Comparison between Indian and Western scientific traditions
- Relevance of Indian Knowledge System to modern science

### Management and Indian Knowledge System

- Indian management philosophy and practices
- Comparison between Indian and Western management styles
- Importance of Indian Knowledge System in contemporary management practices
- Case studies of successful Indian companies and their management practices

Overall, this module provides a comprehensive understanding of the Indian Knowledge System and its relevance to science and management. It highlights the unique characteristics of the Indian Knowledge System and its contribution to modern science and management practices. It also provides a platform for students to explore and understand the rich heritage of Indian knowledge and its application to contemporary issues.
