## Unit 2 - Neural Networks-II (Back propagation networks)

Back propagation is a popular algorithm used in neural networks to train the model. It is based on the principle of error minimization and is used to adjust the weights of the network. This unit is focused on the backpropagation algorithm and its implementation.

### Topics Covered
1. Introduction to Backpropagation
2. Working of Backpropagation
3. Activation Functions
4. Gradient Descent
5. Backpropagation Algorithm
6. Applications of Backpropagation

### Introduction to Backpropagation
- Backpropagation is a supervised learning algorithm used to train neural networks.
- It involves adjusting the weights of the network to minimize the error between the predicted and actual output.
- It is based on the principle of gradient descent, which updates the weights in the direction of steepest descent.

### Working of Backpropagation
- The backpropagation algorithm works by propagating the error backward through the network.
- It starts with the output layer and calculates the error at each neuron.
- The error is then propagated backward through the hidden layers, and the weights are adjusted accordingly.
- This process is repeated until the error is minimized.

### Activation Functions
- Activation functions are used to introduce non-linearity into the neural network.
- They are applied to the output of each neuron in the network.
- Common activation functions include sigmoid, tanh, and ReLU.

### Gradient Descent
- Gradient descent is an optimization algorithm used to minimize the error between the predicted and actual output.
- It involves calculating the gradient of the error with respect to the weights and adjusting the weights in the direction of steepest descent.

### Backpropagation Algorithm
- The backpropagation algorithm involves two phases: forward propagation and backpropagation.
- In the forward propagation phase, the input is passed through the network, and the output is calculated.
- In the backpropagation phase, the error is calculated, and the weights are adjusted using gradient descent.

### Applications of Backpropagation
- Backpropagation is widely used in image recognition, speech recognition, and natural language processing.
- It is also used in finance for predicting stock prices and in healthcare for predicting diseases.

Overall, backpropagation is an important algorithm in the field of neural networks, and understanding its implementation is crucial for building and training effective models.
