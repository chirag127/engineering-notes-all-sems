### Solution for the notes of the Unit 2 - Neural Networks-II (Back propagation networks) in the subject of Application of Soft Computing

Back propagation networks are a type of artificial neural network that use supervised learning to train the network. The back propagation algorithm is used to adjust the weights of the connections between neurons in the network. This allows the network to learn patterns in the input data and make predictions.

Here are some important points to understand about back propagation networks:

- Back propagation networks consist of input, hidden, and output layers of neurons. The input layer receives data, the hidden layer processes the data, and the output layer produces a prediction.
- The back propagation algorithm works by calculating the error between the predicted output and the actual output. This error is then propagated backwards through the network, and the weights of the connections between neurons are adjusted to reduce the error in future predictions.
- Back propagation networks can be used for classification or regression problems. Classification problems involve predicting which class a data point belongs to, while regression problems involve predicting a continuous value.
- One advantage of back propagation networks is that they can handle complex patterns in the input data. They can also be used for tasks such as image recognition and speech recognition.
- However, back propagation networks can be slow to train and can sometimes get stuck in local minima. There are also other types of neural networks, such as convolutional neural networks and recurrent neural networks, that may be better suited for certain tasks.

Some examples of back propagation networks in action include:

- Predicting the price of a house based on features such as number of bedrooms, square footage, and location.
- Classifying images of animals based on their features.
- Recognizing speech and converting it to text.

In summary, back propagation networks are a powerful tool for machine learning and can be used to solve a variety of problems. However, they are not always the best choice and other types of neural networks should also be considered depending on the task at hand.
