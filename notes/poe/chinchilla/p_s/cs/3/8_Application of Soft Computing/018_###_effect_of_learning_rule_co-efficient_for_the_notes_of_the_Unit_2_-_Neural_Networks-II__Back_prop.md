### Effect of Learning Rule Co-efficient for the Notes of Unit 2 - Neural Networks-II (Back Propagation Networks) in the Subject of Application of Soft Computing

Learning Rule Coefficient is a crucial parameter in Back Propagation Neural Networks that determines the speed of convergence of the network during training. It is a scaling factor that is multiplied with the error gradient to adjust the weights of the network during backpropagation. In this section, we will discuss the effect of Learning Rule Coefficient on the performance of Back Propagation Networks.

#### Effect of Learning Rule Coefficient

Here are some of the effects of Learning Rule Coefficient on the performance of Back Propagation Networks:

1. **Speed of Convergence:** The Learning Rule Coefficient determines the speed of convergence of the network during training. A higher Learning Rule Coefficient leads to faster convergence, but it may also cause the network to overshoot the optimal weights and lead to instability. On the other hand, a lower Learning Rule Coefficient may result in slower convergence, but it may lead to a more stable network and better performance.

2. **Accuracy:** The Learning Rule Coefficient affects the accuracy of the network during training. A higher Learning Rule Coefficient may result in a higher accuracy rate, but it may also cause the network to overfit the training data and perform poorly on new data. A lower Learning Rule Coefficient may result in a lower accuracy rate, but it may lead to a more generalizable network that performs well on new data.

3. **Robustness:** The Learning Rule Coefficient affects the robustness of the network to noise and outliers in the training data. A higher Learning Rule Coefficient may make the network more sensitive to noise and outliers, while a lower Learning Rule Coefficient may make the network more robust to them.

4. **Computational Complexity:** The Learning Rule Coefficient affects the computational complexity of the network during training. A higher Learning Rule Coefficient may require more iterations to converge, which increases the computational time and resources required. On the other hand, a lower Learning Rule Coefficient may converge faster but may require more epochs to achieve the desired accuracy.

#### Choosing the Right Learning Rule Coefficient

Choosing the right Learning Rule Coefficient is crucial for the performance of Back Propagation Networks. Here are some guidelines to follow when choosing the Learning Rule Coefficient:

1. **Trial and Error:** The Learning Rule Coefficient should be chosen through trial and error by testing different values and observing their effect on the performance of the network.

2. **Learning Rate Schedules:** Learning Rate Schedules can be used to adjust the Learning Rule Coefficient during training. For example, the Learning Rule Coefficient can be decreased as the training progresses to avoid overshooting the optimal weights.

3. **Regularization Techniques:** Regularization Techniques such as Dropout or Weight Decay can be used to avoid overfitting caused by a high Learning Rule Coefficient.

4. **Problem-Specific:** The choice of Learning Rule Coefficient may be problem-specific and may depend on the nature of the data and the task at hand.

#### Conclusion

Learning Rule Coefficient is a crucial parameter in Back Propagation Networks that affects the speed of convergence, accuracy, robustness, and computational complexity of the network during training. Choosing the right Learning Rule Coefficient is crucial for the performance of the network, and various techniques such as trial and error, learning rate schedules, regularization techniques, and problem-specific approaches can be used to determine the optimal Learning Rule Coefficient.
