### Requirement Engineering Process

Requirement Engineering is the process of defining, documenting, validating and maintaining requirements throughout the software development life cycle. It is a crucial step in the Software Development Process as it helps to ensure that the software being developed meets the customer's expectations and is of high quality.

The Requirement Engineering Process involves the following steps:

1. Requirement Elicitation: In this step, the requirements are gathered from the stakeholders. This can be done through various techniques like interviews, questionnaires, brainstorming sessions, etc. The main aim of this step is to collect as much information as possible about the requirements.

2. Requirement Analysis: In this step, the requirements are analyzed to ensure that they are complete, consistent, and unambiguous. The requirements are also prioritized based on their importance.

3. Requirement Specification: In this step, the requirements are documented in a clear and concise manner. This documentation is called the Software Requirement Specification (SRS). The SRS should be easy to understand and should provide a clear picture of what the software will do.

4. Requirement Validation: In this step, the requirements are validated to ensure that they are correct and meet the customer's expectations. This can be done through various techniques like prototyping, simulations, etc.

5. Requirement Management: In this step, the requirements are maintained throughout the software development life cycle. Any changes to the requirements should be documented and approved by the stakeholders.

Advantages of Requirement Engineering Process:

1. Helps to ensure that the software being developed meets the customer's expectations and is of high quality.

2. Helps to identify and resolve any issues with the requirements early in the software development life cycle.

3. Helps to reduce the cost and time of software development by avoiding rework and ensuring that the software is developed right the first time.

Disadvantages of Requirement Engineering Process:

1. Can be time-consuming and costly.

2. Can lead to over-engineering if the requirements are not properly prioritized.

Examples of Requirement Engineering Process:

1. Developing a new mobile application.

2. Developing an online shopping portal.

Applications of Requirement Engineering Process:

1. Software Development.

2. Product Development.
