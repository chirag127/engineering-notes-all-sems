## Kannada Literature

Kannada literature is the body of literature written in the Kannada language, which is primarily spoken in the Indian state of Karnataka. Kannada language has a rich literary tradition that dates back to the 9th century AD. In this section, we will discuss the history, characteristics and the major works of Kannada literature.

### History of Kannada Literature

- Kannada literature has a rich history that dates back to the 9th century AD.
- The earliest known Kannada literary work is the Kavirajamarga, written by the Rashtrakuta King Amoghavarsha in the 9th century AD.
- During the medieval period, Kannada literature saw a lot of growth and development under the patronage of the Vijayanagara Empire.
- The 16th and 17th centuries saw the emergence of the Haridasa movement, which produced devotional literature in Kannada.
- In the modern era, Kannada literature has seen a lot of growth and development with the emergence of various literary movements.

### Characteristics of Kannada Literature

- Kannada literature is known for its rich cultural and linguistic heritage.
- Kannada literature is characterized by its unique literary genres such as vachana, tripadi, and shatpadi.
- Kannada literature also has a rich tradition of folk literature, which includes various forms of oral literature such as folk songs, ballads, and folktales.
- Kannada literature is known for its emphasis on social and moral values.

### Major Works of Kannada Literature

- Kavirajamarga: written by King Amoghavarsha in the 9th century AD, is the earliest known literary work in Kannada.
- Pampa Bharata: written by Pampa, is a 10th-century AD Kannada epic poem based on the Mahabharata.
- Vachana Sahitya: is a form of Kannada poetry composed by the Sharanas, who were 12th-century Lingayat saints.
- Haridasa Sahitya: is a form of Kannada devotional literature produced by the Haridasas in the 16th and 17th centuries.
- Jnanpith Award winners: Kuvempu, D.R. Bendre, U.R. Ananthamurthy, and Girish Karnad.

### Conclusion

Kannada literature has a rich heritage and is known for its unique literary genres and emphasis on social and moral values. It has produced many great literary works over the centuries and continues to thrive in the modern era. Kannada literature is an important part of the cultural and literary heritage of India.
