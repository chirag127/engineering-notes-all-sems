### Challenges of Conventional Systems for the Notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Big Data in the Subject of Big Data

Big Data is a rapidly growing field that deals with the processing and analysis of large and complex datasets. However, conventional systems used for storing and processing data face several challenges when it comes to handling Big Data. In this section, we will discuss the challenges of conventional systems for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Big Data in the subject of Big Data.

1. **Limited Storage Capacity:** Conventional systems have limited storage capacity, which makes it difficult to store and process large datasets. As the volume of data increases, conventional systems tend to become slow and inefficient.

2. **Slow Processing Speed:** Conventional systems are designed to process small datasets, and they tend to become slow when processing large and complex datasets. This can result in long processing times, which can be a major bottleneck when dealing with Big Data.

3. **Inflexibility:** Conventional systems are not flexible enough to handle the variety and complexity of Big Data. They are designed to work with structured data, and they struggle to process unstructured data such as social media posts, images, and videos.

4. **Costly:** Conventional systems can be expensive to set up and maintain. They require specialized hardware and software, which can be costly for small and medium-sized businesses.

5. **Security Concerns:** Conventional systems have security concerns when it comes to handling Big Data. As the volume of data increases, the risk of data breaches and cyber-attacks also increases. This can result in the loss of sensitive and confidential information.

6. **Lack of Real-Time Processing:** Conventional systems are not designed to handle real-time data processing. This can be a major challenge when dealing with Big Data, as real-time processing is essential for tasks such as fraud detection and predictive analytics.

In conclusion, conventional systems face several challenges when it comes to handling Big Data. As the volume and complexity of data continue to increase, it is important to adopt new technologies and tools that are specifically designed for handling Big Data. This will not only improve the efficiency and accuracy of data processing but also ensure the security and privacy of sensitive information.
