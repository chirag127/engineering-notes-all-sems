## Unit 1 - Introduction of Real Time System

Real-time systems are computer systems that are designed to react to events as they happen, without any delay. These systems are widely used in industries such as aviation, automotive, and manufacturing, where delays can result in catastrophic consequences. In this unit, we will learn about the basics of real-time systems, including their characteristics, advantages, and disadvantages, and their applications.

### Characteristics of Real-Time Systems

Real-time systems have the following characteristics:

- They must respond to events within a specified time limit, called a deadline.
- They must be predictable, meaning that their response time should be consistent.
- They must be reliable, meaning that they should work correctly and not fail.
- They must be able to handle multiple tasks simultaneously.

### Advantages of Real-Time Systems

Real-time systems have the following advantages:

- They can improve safety and reliability in critical applications.
- They can increase efficiency and productivity in manufacturing and other industries.
- They can provide real-time feedback and control in applications such as gaming and virtual reality.

### Disadvantages of Real-Time Systems

Real-time systems have the following disadvantages:

- They can be expensive to develop and maintain.
- They can be complex and difficult to design and implement.
- They can be limited in their flexibility and scalability.

### Applications of Real-Time Systems

Real-time systems are used in a variety of applications, including:

- Aviation and aerospace: Real-time systems are used to control aircraft and spacecraft, ensuring their safety and reliability.
- Automotive: Real-time systems are used in cars to control engine performance, braking, and other functions.
- Manufacturing: Real-time systems are used to control assembly lines and other manufacturing processes, improving efficiency and productivity.
- Gaming and virtual reality: Real-time systems are used to provide real-time feedback and control in gaming and virtual reality applications.

In conclusion, real-time systems are an important and growing area of computer science, with a wide range of applications in various industries. Understanding the basics of real-time systems is essential for anyone interested in working with these systems or studying computer science in general.
