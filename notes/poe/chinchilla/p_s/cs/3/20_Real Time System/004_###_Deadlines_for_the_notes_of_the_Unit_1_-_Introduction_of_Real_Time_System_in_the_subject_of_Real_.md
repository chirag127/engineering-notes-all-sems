### Deadlines for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction of Real Time System in the subject of Real Time System

Real Time System is a crucial subject in the field of computer science and engineering. It deals with the design and implementation of systems that need to respond to external events within a specific time limit. The subject is vast and requires a lot of attention and focus to understand it thoroughly. Unit 1- Introduction of Real Time System is the foundation of the subject and requires special attention to build a strong base for the rest of the subject. Here are the deadlines for the notes of the Unit 1- Introduction of Real Time System:

1. Introduction to Real Time System: The introduction of Real Time System is a critical component of the subject. It covers the basic concepts of real-time systems, such as hard and soft real-time systems, task deadlines, and scheduling. The deadline for this topic is two weeks from the start of the semester.

2. Characteristics of Real Time System: The characteristics of Real Time System include determinism, concurrency, and fault tolerance. These characteristics are essential for the proper functioning of real-time systems. The deadline for this topic is three weeks from the start of the semester.

3. Real Time Operating System: The Real Time Operating System is a crucial component of the subject. It covers the basic concepts of RTOS, such as scheduling algorithms, memory management, and device drivers. The deadline for this topic is four weeks from the start of the semester.

4. Task Management: Task management is another critical component of the subject. It covers the concepts of task creation, task scheduling, and task synchronization. The deadline for this topic is five weeks from the start of the semester.

5. Inter-Process Communication: Inter-Process Communication is an essential aspect of Real Time System. It covers the concepts of message queues, pipes, shared memory, and sockets. The deadline for this topic is six weeks from the start of the semester.

6. Real-Time System Design: Real-Time System Design is the final component of the unit 1. It covers the design of real-time systems using UML, statecharts, and other design methodologies. The deadline for this topic is seven weeks from the start of the semester.

It is essential to adhere to the deadlines to cover the entire syllabus of the subject of Real Time System. Understanding the fundamental concepts of Real Time System is crucial to excel in the subject. Therefore, it is recommended to study regularly and revise the topics periodically to have a better understanding of the subject.
