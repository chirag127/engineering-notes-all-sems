## Unit 4 - Real Time Communication

In this unit, we will learn about real-time communication (RTC), which is a term used to describe any form of communication that allows users to send and receive information in real-time. This includes technologies such as voice over IP (VoIP), video conferencing, instant messaging, and collaborative software.

### VoIP (Voice over IP)

- VoIP is a technology that enables voice communication over the internet.
- It uses a packet-switched network to transmit voice data in real-time.
- Advantages of VoIP include lower costs, improved call quality, and scalability.
- Disadvantages include the need for a reliable internet connection, potential security concerns, and the possibility of dropped calls.

### Video Conferencing

- Video conferencing is a technology that allows users to have face-to-face meetings over the internet.
- It enables remote collaboration and reduces the need for travel.
- Advantages of video conferencing include increased productivity, reduced costs, and improved communication.
- Disadvantages include the need for a high-speed internet connection, technical difficulties, and potential security concerns.

### Instant Messaging

- Instant messaging is a technology that allows users to send and receive messages in real-time.
- It is commonly used for informal communication and collaboration.
- Advantages of instant messaging include increased efficiency and accessibility.
- Disadvantages include the potential for distraction, the need for a reliable internet connection, and security concerns.

### Collaborative Software

- Collaborative software is a technology that allows multiple users to work together on a project or task.
- It can include features such as real-time editing, chat, and video conferencing.
- Advantages of collaborative software include increased productivity, improved communication, and reduced costs.
- Disadvantages include the need for a reliable internet connection, potential security concerns, and the need for training and support.

Overall, real-time communication technologies have revolutionized the way we communicate and collaborate in both personal and professional settings. It is important to understand the advantages and disadvantages of these technologies to make informed decisions about their use.
