### Timing Constraints for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction of Real Time System in the subject of Real Time System

Timing constraints play a major role in real-time systems. In a real-time system, tasks have to be completed within a certain time frame. This time frame is known as a deadline. If a task is not completed within its deadline, it may lead to system failure. Hence, timing constraints must be considered while designing real-time systems. In this section, we will discuss timing constraints in detail.

The following are the timing constraints that need to be considered in real-time systems:

1. Response Time: Response time is the time taken by a system to respond to an event. In real-time systems, the response time should be within a certain limit. If the response time exceeds the limit, it may lead to system failure.

2. Deadline: Deadline is the time at which a task should be completed. In real-time systems, each task has a deadline. If a task is not completed within its deadline, it may lead to system failure.

3. Jitter: Jitter is the variation in the delay of a task. In real-time systems, jitter should be minimized. If jitter is high, it may lead to system failure.

4. Periodic Tasks: Periodic tasks are tasks that are executed at regular intervals. The time between two successive executions of a periodic task is known as its period. In real-time systems, the period of a periodic task should be within a certain limit. If the period exceeds the limit, it may lead to system failure.

5. Schedulability: Schedulability is the ability of a system to meet its deadlines. In real-time systems, schedulability should be ensured. If a system is not schedulable, it may lead to system failure.

6. Preemption: Preemption is the ability of a higher priority task to interrupt a lower priority task. In real-time systems, preemption should be used judiciously. If preemption is not used judiciously, it may lead to system failure.

In conclusion, timing constraints play a crucial role in real-time systems. The above-mentioned constraints should be considered while designing real-time systems to ensure their proper functioning.
