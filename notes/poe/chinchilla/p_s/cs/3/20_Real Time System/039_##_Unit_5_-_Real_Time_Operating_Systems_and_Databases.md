Real-time operating systems and databases are critical components of modern embedded systems. Knowledge of these topics is essential for developers who are involved in the design and development of real-time systems. Understanding the architecture, components, scheduling, models, and examples of real-time operating systems and databases will enable developers to design and develop efficient and reliable systems. The integration of real-time operating systems and databases provides several advantages, including improved performance, increased reliability, and reduced development time and cost. However, there are also some disadvantages, such as increased complexity and the need for specialized knowledge. The applications of real-time operating systems and databases are vast and include aerospace and defense, automotive and transportation, medical devices, industrial automation, and robotics.
