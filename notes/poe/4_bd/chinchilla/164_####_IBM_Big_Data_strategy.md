#### IBM Big Data Strategy

IBM is a leading provider of big data and analytics solutions. The company's big data strategy focuses on providing customers with the tools and services they need to collect, manage, and analyze large volumes of data.

Here are some key points to understand about IBM's big data strategy:

1. IBM's big data strategy is focused on providing customers with a comprehensive set of tools and services that can help them collect, manage, and analyze large volumes of data.

2. The company offers a range of big data solutions, including data integration, data warehousing, data governance, and data analytics.

3. IBM's big data strategy is built around the IBM Cloud Pak for Data platform. This platform provides customers with a unified view of their data, allowing them to easily access and analyze it.

4. The IBM Cloud Pak for Data platform includes a range of tools and services, such as data virtualization, data science, and machine learning.

5. IBM's big data strategy also includes partnerships with other companies, such as Cloudera, Hortonworks, and MongoDB, to provide customers with access to additional tools and services.

6. One of the key advantages of IBM's big data strategy is its focus on open technologies. The company is committed to using open source technologies, such as Apache Hadoop and Apache Spark, to build its big data solutions.

7. IBM's big data strategy also includes a focus on data security and governance. The company offers a range of tools and services to help customers ensure the security and privacy of their data.

Mnemonics and learning tricks:
- Remember the acronym C-W-D for IBM's big data solutions: Collect, Warehousing, and Data governance.
- Think of IBM's Cloud Pak for Data platform as a "unified data hub" that brings together all of your data in one place.

In conclusion, IBM's big data strategy is focused on providing customers with a comprehensive set of tools and services to help them collect, manage, and analyze large volumes of data. The company's focus on open technologies, data security, and partnerships with other companies make it a strong player in the big data market. Remember the C-W-D acronym and the unified data hub concept to help you remember key points about IBM's big data strategy.
