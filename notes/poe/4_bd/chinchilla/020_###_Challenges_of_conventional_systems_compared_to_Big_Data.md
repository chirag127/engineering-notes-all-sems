### Challenges of conventional systems compared to Big Data

Conventional systems have been widely used in various industries for decades, but with the rise of Big Data, these systems face several challenges. In this section, we will discuss some of the main challenges that conventional systems face when compared to Big Data.

1. Data Volume: Conventional systems are not designed to handle large volumes of data. They often have limitations in terms of storage capacity and processing power. On the other hand, Big Data systems are designed to handle large volumes of data and can scale up or down as per the requirements.

2. Variety of Data: Conventional systems are designed to handle structured data, which is data that is organized in a specific format. However, Big Data includes a variety of data types, such as unstructured, semi-structured, and structured data. Handling these different data types can be a challenge for conventional systems.

3. Velocity of Data: Conventional systems are not designed to handle real-time data processing. This means that they may not be able to provide insights in real-time. On the other hand, Big Data systems are designed to handle real-time data processing, which enables organizations to make decisions in real-time.

4. Complexity of Data: Big Data includes complex data, such as graphs and networks, which can be difficult to process and analyze. Conventional systems may not have the necessary tools or algorithms to handle this type of data.

5. Cost: The cost of storing and processing large volumes of data can be high for conventional systems. Big Data systems, on the other hand, are designed to be cost-effective and scalable.
