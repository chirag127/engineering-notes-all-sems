### Challenges of conventional systems compared to Big Data

Conventional systems and Big Data are two different concepts that have their own challenges. In this section, we will discuss some of the challenges that conventional systems face in comparison to Big Data.

1. Data Size:
Conventional systems are not designed to handle large amounts of data. They have limitations in terms of storage capacity, processing power, and memory. On the other hand, Big Data systems are designed to handle large-scale data and can scale horizontally to accommodate more data.

2. Data Variety:
Conventional systems are designed to handle structured data such as numbers, text, and dates. They struggle with unstructured data such as images, videos, and audio. Big Data systems can handle both structured and unstructured data.

3. Data Velocity:
Conventional systems are not designed to handle real-time data processing. They are not equipped to handle the high volume of data that is generated in real-time. Big Data systems are designed to handle real-time data processing and can process data as soon as it is generated.

4. Data Complexity:
Conventional systems struggle with complex data such as graphs and networks. Big Data systems are designed to handle complex data and can process it efficiently.

5. Cost:
Conventional systems are expensive to scale up. They require expensive hardware and software licenses. Big Data systems, on the other hand, are designed to run on commodity hardware and open-source software. They are cost-effective and can be scaled up easily.

Mnemonics and Learning Tricks:
Remember the acronym SVDCC for the challenges that conventional systems face in comparison to Big Data. S-Data Size, V-Data Variety, D-Data Velocity, C-Data Complexity, and C-Cost.
