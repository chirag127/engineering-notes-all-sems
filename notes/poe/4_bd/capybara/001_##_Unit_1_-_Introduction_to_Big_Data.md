## Unit 1 - Introduction to Big Data

Big Data is a term used to describe the vast amount of structured and unstructured data that inundates a business on a day-to-day basis. It is characterized by its volume, velocity, and variety, and represents a significant challenge for organizations looking to make sense of the information they have at their disposal. In this unit, we will explore the key concepts and technologies that underpin Big Data, and explain why it is such an important area of study for businesses and individuals alike.

### 1.7 Advantages and Disadvantages of Big Data

- Advantages
  - Helps businesses make informed decisions
  - Enables predictive analytics
  - Improves customer experience
  - Can lead to new business opportunities
- Disadvantages
  - Privacy and security concerns
  - Requires significant investment in infrastructure and expertise
  - Can be difficult to integrate with existing systems
