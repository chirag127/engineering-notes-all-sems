### IBM Big Data strategy

- IBM's big data strategy revolves around its IBM Cloud and IBM Watson platforms.
- The IBM Cloud provides the infrastructure to store and process data at scale. It offers open source analytics and AI software along with IBM value-adds like Db2 and Datastage for data management and integration.
- IBM Watson is IBM's AI platform. It provides cognitive APIs and machine learning models to derive insights from data. Watson can understand natural language, speech, images, and more to solve complex problems.
- Together, the IBM Cloud and Watson provide an end-to-end big data and AI solution. Data can be ingested, stored, and processed at scale on the IBM Cloud and advanced analytics and AI can be performed on the data using Watson.
- Some key parts of IBM's big data strategy include:
    - Focus on data governance and security. IBM emphasizes governance, data management, and security to ensure data is used properly.
    - Invest in open source. IBM works with and contributes to open source big data projects like Apache Hadoop, Spark, and Kafka to advance the ecosystem.
    - Build AI and analytics on the cloud. IBM is integrating Watson and cognitive capabilities into its cloud platform to enable AI and advanced analytics for enterprises.
    - Focus on data integration and preparation. IBM acquired companies like Alteryx and Trifacta to help customers integrate and prepare data for analysis.
    - Partnerships with other tech companies. IBM partners with companies like Hortonworks, Cognizant, and HPE to expand its reach and serve customers better.

- Overall, IBM's big data strategy is to provide a robust, secure, and AI-powered cloud and software platform to help enterprises gain business value from their data. IBM is focused on covering the entire big data lifecycle from data integration to AI and is making significant investments to advance its offerings.
