#### Pig Latin

Pig Latin is a game of permutations played on the English language game. It is usually played by children as a fun way to disguise the English language and make it more challenging to understand.

To translate English into Pig Latin:

- Take all consonants before the first vowel and move them to the end of the word, followed by 'ay'.
- If the word begins with a vowel, simply add 'way' to the end of the word.

For example:

- Hello -> Ell-oh-hay
- Apple -> Apple-way
- Computer -> Omputercay-ay

Mnemonics to remember the rules:

- Consonants before vowels get shuffled to the end and 'ay' gets added.
- Words starting with vowels just get 'way' added.

Advantages:

- Fun way to play around with language as a game or puzzle.
- Can be used as a simple cipher to disguise English.

Disadvantages:

- Not a strong form of encryption as the rules are quite simple to decipher.
- The translation can make the words quite long and clumsy.

Applications:

- Creative writing and poetry.
- Language teaching - helps understand syllables and sounds.
- Light encoding for fun.
