
### Buddhism in India

* Buddhism is a religion and philosophy based on the teachings of Siddhartha Gautama, who is also known as the Buddha. It is one of the oldest and most widespread religions in the world, with more than 500 million followers.
* Buddhism was founded in India in the 6th century BCE, and has had a significant influence on the culture and history of the region.
* The core teachings of Buddhism are known as the Four Noble Truths:
    1. Life is suffering
    2. Suffering is caused by craving and attachment
    3. Suffering can be ended by overcoming craving and attachment
    4. The way to overcome craving and attachment is through the Eightfold Path
* The Eightfold Path is a set of principles and practices that Buddhists strive to follow in order to end suffering. It includes right understanding, right thought, right speech, right action, right livelihood, right effort, right mindfulness, and right concentration.
* Buddhism emphasizes the practice of meditation as a way to cultivate mindfulness and gain insight into the true nature of reality.
* Buddhism also teaches the importance of compassion and kindness towards all living beings.
* Buddhism has had a significant influence on Indian culture and philosophy, and has been a major source of inspiration for many Indian artists and writers.
* Buddhism has also been a major influence on other religions in India, such as Jainism and Hinduism.

* Right thought: Think Positively

* Right action: Act Responsibly
