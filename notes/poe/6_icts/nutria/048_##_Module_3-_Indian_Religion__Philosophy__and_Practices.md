
## Module 3- Indian Religion, Philosophy, and Practices

1. Hinduism: Hinduism is the oldest religion in India and is based on the Vedic texts. It is a polytheistic religion, with multiple gods and goddesses, and is divided into four main sects: Vaishnavism, Shaivism, Shaktism, and Smartism. Hinduism also has a strong belief in karma, reincarnation, and dharma.

2. Buddhism: Buddhism is a religion founded by Gautama Buddha in the 5th century BCE. It is based on the teachings of the Four Noble Truths and the Eightfold Path. Buddhism is a non-theistic religion, and does not believe in any gods or goddesses.

3. Jainism: Jainism is an ancient Indian religion that is based on the teachings of Mahavira. It is an atheistic religion, and does not believe in any gods or goddesses. Jainism is based on the principles of non-violence and ahimsa, and also has a strong belief in karma and reincarnation.

4. Sikhism: Sikhism is a monotheistic religion founded by Guru Nanak in the 15th century. It is based on the teachings of the ten Sikh Gurus, and is centered around the belief in one God. Sikhism also has a strong belief in karma and reincarnation.

5. Islam: Islam is a monotheistic religion founded by Muhammad in the 7th century. It is based on the teachings of the Qur'an, and is centered around the belief in one God. Islam also has a strong belief in the Day of Judgment and the afterlife.

6. Christianity: Christianity is a monotheistic religion founded by Jesus Christ in the 1st century. It is based on the teachings of the Bible, and is centered around the belief in one God. Christianity also has a strong belief in the resurrection of Jesus Christ and the afterlife.

7. Zoroastrianism: Zoroastrianism is an ancient religion founded by Zoroaster in the 6th century BCE. It is based on the teachings of the Avesta, and is centered around the belief in one God. Zoroastrianism also has a strong belief in the afterlife and the Day of Judgment.

8. Judaism: Judaism is an ancient monotheistic religion founded by Abraham in the 2nd millennium BCE. It is based on the teachings of the Torah, and is centered around the belief in one God. Judaism also has a strong belief in the afterlife and the Day of Judgment.
