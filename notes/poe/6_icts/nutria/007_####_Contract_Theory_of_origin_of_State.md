

### Contract Theory of Origin of State

The Contract Theory of Origin of State is a political theory that suggests that the state originated from an agreement or contract between individuals in a society. This theory is based on the idea that individuals in a society are rational and will agree to a contract that will benefit them the most.

The main proponents of this theory are Thomas Hobbes, John Locke, and Jean-Jacques Rousseau.

##### Thomas Hobbes

Thomas Hobbes proposed that the state originates from a “social contract” between individuals in a society. He argued that individuals in a state of nature would agree to a contract in order to protect themselves from the “war of all against all” that would arise in the absence of a state. This contract would create a sovereign power that would be responsible for protecting the rights of individuals in the society.

##### John Locke

He argued that individuals in a state of nature would agree to a contract that would create a government that would protect their natural rights, such as life, liberty, and property. He argued that this government would be limited in its power and would be accountable to the people.

He argued that individuals in a state of nature would agree to a “general will” that would create a government that would be responsible for protecting the rights of individuals in the society. He argued that this government would be based on the consent of the people and would be accountable to them.
