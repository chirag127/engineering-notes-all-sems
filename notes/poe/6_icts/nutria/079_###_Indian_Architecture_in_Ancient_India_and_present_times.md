
### Indian Architecture in Ancient India and Present Times

* Indian architecture has been shaped by a variety of influences over the centuries, from the ancient Indus Valley civilization to the modern day. 
* Ancient Indian architecture is characterized by its use of wood, stone, and brick in the construction of temples, forts, and other structures. 
* The most famous example of ancient Indian architecture is the Taj Mahal, built in the 17th century by Mughal emperor Shah Jahan.
* Ancient Indian architecture also included the use of carved stone and stucco, as well as the construction of stepped terraces and elaborate gateways.
* In modern times, Indian architecture has been heavily influenced by Western styles, such as the use of concrete and steel in the construction of skyscrapers and other large buildings. 
* Indian architecture also makes use of traditional motifs and styles, such as the use of domes, arches, and columns in the construction of temples and other religious structures.
* Indian architecture also includes the use of modern materials, such as glass and steel, as well as traditional materials, such as wood and stone.
* Indian architecture is also known for its use of vibrant colors and intricate designs, which can be seen in the many temples and palaces throughout the country.
* A good mnemonic for remembering Indian architecture is "Domes, Arches, and Columns: The Three Pillars of Indian Architecture". This mnemonic emphasizes the importance of the three architectural styles that are most commonly used in India.
