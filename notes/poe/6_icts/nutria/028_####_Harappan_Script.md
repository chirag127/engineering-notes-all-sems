
#### Harappan Script

- Harappan Script is an ancient script used by the Indus Valley Civilization in the 3rd millennium BCE. 
- It is the earliest known form of writing in India and one of the earliest known forms of writing in the world. 
- It is still undeciphered, and its true nature and purpose remain unknown.
- The script is written in an ancient Indus script and consists of about 400 symbols, which were used to write a variety of languages.
- It is believed to have been used for economic transactions, record keeping, and communication.
- The script is written from right to left and is composed of a variety of symbols, including abstract symbols, pictographs, and geometric figures. 
- Some of the symbols are believed to represent letters, numbers, and words.
- Mnemonics and learning tricks for the script include memorizing the symbols and their meanings, as well as associating them with words or phrases that are easier to remember.
- Harappan Script is an important part of the history of India and the world, and its study is essential for understanding the culture and history of the Indus Valley Civilization.
