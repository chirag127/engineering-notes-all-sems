
### Socio religious reform movement of 19th century in India

The 19th century in India saw a widespread socio-religious reform movement, which sought to reform the existing religious and social systems of the country. This movement was led by a number of prominent social reformers and religious leaders, who sought to bring about changes in the existing social and religious norms.

The reformers sought to bring about changes in the social structure of India, by eliminating caste-based discrimination, promoting the education of women, and by advocating for the rights of the lower classes. They also sought to bring about changes in religious beliefs and practices, by introducing new religious ideas and concepts, and by attempting to reconcile the different religious traditions in India.

The reformers also sought to bring about changes in the political and economic systems of India, by advocating for the rights of the lower classes and by advocating for the establishment of a more equitable society.
