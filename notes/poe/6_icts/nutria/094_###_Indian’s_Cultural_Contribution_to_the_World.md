
### Indian’s Cultural Contribution to the World

- India has made significant contributions to the world in terms of culture, literature, art, architecture, philosophy, science and technology. 
- India has a rich history of art and architecture, with the Taj Mahal being one of the most well-known examples.
- India is also home to some of the oldest and most influential religions in the world, such as Hinduism, Buddhism and Jainism.
- India has also produced some of the world's most renowned philosophers, such as Gautama Buddha and Mahavira. 
- Indian literature has a long and rich tradition, with the Ramayana and Mahabharata being two of the most famous works.
- India is also known for its contributions to mathematics, with the invention of the decimal system and the concept of zero.
- India has also made significant contributions to science and technology, with the discovery of the laws of motion by Aryabhata and the invention of the concept of zero by Brahmagupta.
- India has also been a leader in the field of medicine, with the Ayurvedic system of medicine being one of the oldest and most comprehensive systems of medicine in the world.
- India is also known for its contributions to music, with classical music being one of the most popular forms of music in the world.
- India is also known for its contributions to dance, with Bharatanatyam, Kathak and Odissi being some of the most popular forms of dance in the world.
- India is also known for its contributions to yoga, with yoga being one of the most popular forms of exercise in the world. 
- India is also known for its contributions to martial arts, with Kalaripayattu being one of the oldest and most respected forms of martial arts in the world. 
- India is also known for its contributions to cuisine, with Indian cuisine being one of the most popular and diverse cuisines in the world.
