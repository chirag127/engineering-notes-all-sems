
### Evolution of languages in India

India has a long and rich history of language evolution. Over the centuries, many languages have been spoken in India, and the evolution of these languages has been heavily influenced by the country's diverse cultures and religions.

* Sanskrit: Sanskrit is an ancient language that was used in India for religious and spiritual purposes. It is the oldest language in India and is said to be the mother of all Indian languages. Sanskrit is still used in some parts of India today, mostly in religious ceremonies and rituals.

* Prakrit: Prakrit is an ancient language that was spoken in India before the arrival of Sanskrit. It is believed to have originated from the Vedic language, and it is still used as a literary language in some parts of India.

* Pali: Pali is an ancient language that was used by Buddhist monks in India. It is believed to have originated from the Prakrit language and is still used in some parts of India today, mostly in Buddhist ceremonies and rituals.

* Dravidian Languages: Dravidian languages are spoken by the Dravidian people in India. These languages are believed to have originated in the Indus Valley Civilization and are still spoken in some parts of India today.

* Indo-Aryan Languages: Indo-Aryan languages are spoken by the Indo-Aryan people in India. These languages are believed to have originated from the Indo-Iranian language and are still spoken in some parts of India today.

* Modern Indian Languages: Modern Indian languages are the languages that are spoken in India today. These languages are derived from the ancient languages of the region and have evolved over time to become the languages that are spoken in India today.
