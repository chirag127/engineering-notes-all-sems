
## Module 1- Society State and Polity in India

* Society: This module covers the social structure of India, including its caste system, religious diversity, and the impact of urbanization. It also explores the role of family, gender, and other social institutions in Indian society.

* State: This module looks at the structure of the Indian government, its constitutional framework, and the role of the judiciary. It also examines the role of the central government, the state governments, and local government in the Indian political system.

* Polity: This module covers the political parties, elections, and the media in India. It also looks at the role of civil society and the impact of globalization on Indian politics.

* Mnemonics and Learning Tricks: 
  * To remember the structure of the Indian government, use the mnemonic "CJPM": Central, Judiciary, Political Parties, and Media. 
  * To keep track of the various branches of the Indian government, use the mnemonic "LEGIS": Legislature, Executive, Government, Judiciary, and State. 
  * To remember the key components of the Indian constitution, use the mnemonic "FRAME": Fundamental Rights, Directive Principles, Administrative Structure, and Miscellaneous.
