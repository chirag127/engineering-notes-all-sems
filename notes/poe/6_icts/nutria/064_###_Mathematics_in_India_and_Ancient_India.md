
### Mathematics in India and Ancient India

- Mathematics has been an integral part of Indian culture since ancient times. The earliest known mathematical texts are the Vedas, which date back to 1500 BCE.
- The Sulbasutras, which were written between 1000 and 800 BCE, contain the earliest known geometric proofs.
- The Shulba Sutras contain algorithms for constructing altars and calculating the area of a circle.
- The Jain mathematicians of ancient India developed the concept of zero and the decimal system. They also developed the concept of negative numbers and solved quadratic equations.
- The Indian mathematician Aryabhata developed the concept of infinity and worked on trigonometry and spherical geometry.
- The Indian mathematician Bhaskara II developed the concept of the differential calculus.
- The Indian mathematician Madhava of Sangamagrama developed the concept of infinite series and the Taylor series.
- The Indian mathematician Srinivasa Ramanujan developed the concept of modular forms and number theory.
- The Indian mathematician Srinivasa Aiyangar Ramanujan developed the concept of the Riemann zeta function.
- Indian mathematicians have made significant contributions to the field of mathematics and have been responsible for many important discoveries.
