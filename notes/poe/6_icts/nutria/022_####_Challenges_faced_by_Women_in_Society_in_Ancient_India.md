

#### Challenges faced by Women in Society in Ancient India

- Women in Ancient India faced a variety of challenges due to the patriarchal nature of the society. 
- Women were expected to adhere to traditional gender roles, including those related to marriage, motherhood, and domestic duties. 
- They were also denied access to education and political participation.
- Women were often treated as property and were subject to abuse, including dowry-related violence and female infanticide. 
- Women were also discriminated against in terms of inheritance rights and access to economic resources. 
- Women in Ancient India were expected to remain loyal to their husbands and in-laws and were not allowed to remarry if their husbands died. 
- They were also subject to restrictions on their movement and were not allowed to travel without permission from their husbands or male family members. 
- Women were also not allowed to own property and were not allowed to participate in any decision-making process within the family. 
- Women were also not allowed to participate in religious ceremonies and were not allowed to take part in public gatherings. 
- Women were also not allowed to attend public schools and were not allowed to pursue higher education. 
- Women were also not allowed to marry outside their caste or religion. 
- Women were also not allowed to pursue any profession or business and were not allowed to participate in any political activities. 
- Women were also not allowed to enter into any contracts or agreements. 
- Women were also not allowed to engage in any kind of physical activities or sports. 
- Women were also not allowed to pursue any kind of artistic activities. 
- Women were also expected to maintain a certain level of modesty and were not allowed to wear certain types of clothing. 
- In some cases, women were even forced into marriage and were not allowed to choose their own partners.
