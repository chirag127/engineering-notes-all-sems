
## Module 4-Science, Management and Indian Knowledge System

1. Science: Science is the systematic study of the physical and natural world through observation and experimentation. It is an organized body of knowledge and the application of this knowledge to the development of technology, medicines, and other products that benefit society.

2. Management: Management is the process of planning, organizing, directing, and controlling resources in order to achieve organizational goals. It involves setting objectives, developing strategies, and implementing tactics that enable an organization to reach its objectives.

3. Indian Knowledge System: Indian Knowledge System is a set of ancient Indian practices and beliefs that were developed over centuries. It includes traditional Indian medicine, philosophy, literature, astrology, and mathematics. Indian Knowledge System has been instrumental in the development of modern science and technology.

Mnemonics and Learning Tricks:

1. S.M.I.L.E: Science, Management, Indian Knowledge System, Literature, and Experiments.
2. M.I.S.T: Management, Indian Knowledge System, Science, and Technology.
3. I.K.S.T: Indian Knowledge System, Science, Technology, and Management.
