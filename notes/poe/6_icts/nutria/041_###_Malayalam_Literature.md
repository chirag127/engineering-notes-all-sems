
### Malayalam Literature
Malayalam literature is a form of literature written in the Malayalam language, which is a language spoken in the southern Indian state of Kerala. It is one of the 22 official languages of India and is the official language of the state. Malayalam literature is divided into four major genres: poetry, prose, drama and non-fiction.

Poetry: Poetry is the oldest form of Malayalam literature, with the earliest examples dating back to the 11th century. It is characterized by its use of simple language and imagery to convey complex emotions and ideas. Poetry in Malayalam is often written in the traditional metre of the language, which is called ‘Kavya’.

Prose: Prose is the most common form of Malayalam literature. It is typically written in the simple language of everyday speech, and is often used to tell stories or to discuss philosophical or spiritual topics. Prose in Malayalam is often written in the traditional metre of the language, which is called ‘Vruttam’.

Drama: Drama is a form of Malayalam literature that is performed on stage. It is typically written in the simple language of everyday speech, and is often used to tell stories or to discuss philosophical or spiritual topics. Drama in Malayalam is often written in the traditional metre of the language, which is called ‘Natakam’.

Non-Fiction: Non-fiction is a form of Malayalam literature that is written in the language of everyday speech and is used to discuss topics such as philosophy, religion, science, and history. Non-fiction in Malayalam is often written in the traditional metre of the language, which is called ‘Grantham’.

Mnemonics and Learning Tricks: Mnemonics are a useful tool for learning and remembering information. For Malayalam literature, one useful mnemonic is to remember the four main genres by the acronym ‘P-D-P-N’, which stands for Poetry, Drama, Prose and Non-Fiction. Additionally, it can be helpful to remember the traditional metres of the language by the acronym ‘K-V-N-G’, which stands for Kavya, Vruttam, Natakam and Grantham.
