
### Seals as Cultural Heritage of India

- Seals have been used to signify ownership and identity in India since ancient times.
- Seals can be found in the form of coins, seals, inscriptions, and sculptures.
- Seals were used to signify the ownership of land, goods, and other possessions.
- Seals were also used to authenticate documents and contracts, as well as to mark the boundaries of a kingdom or territory.
- Seals were also used as symbols of authority and power, and to indicate the status of a ruler or leader.
- Seals were also used as a form of communication between rulers and their subjects, as well as between different kingdoms and empires.
- Seals were also used to signify loyalty and allegiance to a particular ruler or kingdom.
- Seals were also used to identify individuals and to mark important events in history.
- Seals were also used to create a sense of unity among different communities and cultures.
- Seals have been found in many different shapes and sizes, and can range from simple to complex designs.
- Seals can be made from a variety of materials, including stone, wood, metal, and clay.
- Seals can also be inscribed with a variety of symbols, such as animals, plants, gods, and goddesses.
- Seals are an important part of India's cultural heritage and have been used for centuries to signify ownership and identity.
