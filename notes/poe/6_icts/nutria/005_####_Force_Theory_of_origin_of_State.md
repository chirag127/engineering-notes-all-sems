* According to the Force Theory, the state is the result of a group of people who have the power to impose their will on others. They use their power to create laws and enforce them. This is done by using military force, economic power, and political influence.
* The Force Theory also suggests that the state is necessary for the protection of the people and the maintenance of order. It also suggests that the state is necessary for the protection of the rights of the people.
* The Force Theory of origin of State has been criticized by many people. Some argue that the state is not necessary for the protection of the people and that it can be used for oppressive purposes.
