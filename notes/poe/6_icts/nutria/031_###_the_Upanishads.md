
### The Upanishads

* The Upanishads are the ancient spiritual texts of Hinduism. They form the core of its philosophical thought and are the foundation of the Vedanta school of Hinduism. 
* The Upanishads are considered to be the source of many of the ideas found in Hinduism and are believed to have been written between 800 BCE and 400 CE.
* The Upanishads are divided into three main categories: the Samhitas, the Brahmanas, and the Aranyakas. The Samhitas contain the main teachings of the Upanishads, while the Brahmanas and Aranyakas are commentaries on the Samhitas.

* The Upanishads focus on the concept of Brahman, which is the ultimate reality and the source of all existence. They also focus on the concept of Atman, which is the individual soul and its relationship to Brahman.
