

#### Theories of origin of State in India

1. **Aryan Theory**: This theory states that the origin of the state in India can be traced back to the Aryan invasion in the 2nd millennium BC. According to this theory, the Aryans brought with them the concept of state, which was based on the division of labour and a hierarchical social structure.

2. **Dravidian Theory**: This theory suggests that the origin of the state in India can be traced back to the Dravidian people who settled in the Indian subcontinent around the 4th millennium BC. The Dravidian people were organized into small chiefdoms and kingdoms, and these states were the precursors of the modern state in India.

3. **Vedic Theory**: This theory states that the origin of the state in India can be traced back to the Vedic period (1500-500 BC). During this period, the Vedic people developed the concept of state based on the division of labour and a hierarchical social structure.

4. **Feudal Theory**: This theory suggests that the origin of the state in India can be traced back to the feudal system that emerged in the Indian subcontinent during the medieval period (13th-18th centuries). The feudal system was based on the division of labour and a hierarchical social structure, and these elements were the precursors of the modern state in India.

5. **Colonial Theory**: This theory suggests that the origin of the state in India can be traced back to the British rule in India (1757-1947). The British introduced the concept of state based on the division of labour and a hierarchical social structure. This was the precursor of the modern state in India.
