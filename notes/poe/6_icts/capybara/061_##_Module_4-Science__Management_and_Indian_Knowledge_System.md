## Module 4- Science, Management, and Indian Knowledge System

This module is an essential part of the curriculum for any student who wants to gain knowledge about the relationship between science, management, and the Indian knowledge system. The module covers various topics that will help students understand the significance of science, management, and the Indian knowledge system in contemporary times. 

The following are the topics covered in this module:

1. Introduction to Science, Management, and Indian Knowledge System
    * The relationship between science, management, and the Indian knowledge system
    * Importance of studying this relationship

2. Scientific Management
    * Definition and Principles of Scientific Management
    * Advantages and Disadvantages of Scientific Management
    * Examples of scientific management in the industry

3. Indian Knowledge System
    * Definition and Types of Indian Knowledge System
    * Importance of Indian Knowledge System
    * Comparison between Indian Knowledge System and Western Knowledge System

4. Management Techniques in Indian Knowledge System
    * Definition and Principles of Management Techniques in Indian Knowledge System
    * Advantages and Disadvantages of Management Techniques in Indian Knowledge System
    * Examples of Management Techniques in Indian Knowledge System

5. Contemporary Issues in Science, Management, and Indian Knowledge System
    * Issues in Science and Technology
    * Issues in Management
    * Issues in Indian Knowledge System

6. Mnemonics and Learning Tricks for Module 4
    * Mnemonics for Scientific Management Principles
    * Learning Tricks for Indian Knowledge System Types

This module requires students to have a good understanding of basic management principles, scientific principles, and the Indian knowledge system. The module aims to help students understand the significance of the Indian knowledge system in the modern world and how it can be integrated with contemporary management and scientific principles.
