### Malayalam Literature

Malayalam literature is the literature of the Malayalam language, which is spoken by the people of the Indian state of Kerala and the union territory of Lakshadweep. Malayalam literature has a rich tradition, dating back to at least the 13th century. Here are some important points to know about Malayalam literature:

- The oldest known work of Malayalam literature is Ramacharitam, a version of the Ramayana written by Cheeraman in the 13th century.
- The medieval period of Malayalam literature saw the development of a unique style of poetry called Manipravalam, which combined Sanskrit and Malayalam.
- The modern period of Malayalam literature began in the late 19th century with the works of writers like Kumaran Asan and Vallathol Narayana Menon.
- Prominent Malayalam writers of the 20th century include Thakazhi Sivasankara Pillai, M.T. Vasudevan Nair, O.V. Vijayan, and Kamala Surayya.
- Malayalam literature includes a wide range of genres, including poetry, novels, short stories, and plays.
- Some of the famous works of Malayalam literature include:

  - Nalacharitham, a 17th-century play by Unnayi Warrier
  - Kundalata, a 19th-century novel by Appu Nedungadi
  - Chemmeen, a 1956 novel by Thakazhi Sivasankara Pillai that won the Sahitya Akademi Award
  - Randamoozham, a 1984 novel by M.T. Vasudevan Nair that retells the story of the Mahabharata from the point of view of Bhima.

- Malayalam literature has been influenced by various traditions, including Sanskrit literature, Tamil literature, and Arabic literature.
- There are many learning tricks to remember the important works of Malayalam literature. One trick is to remember the acronym "NKKR," which stands for Nalacharitham, Kundalata, Chemmeen, and Randamoozham - four of the most famous works of Malayalam literature.

Overall, Malayalam literature is a rich and diverse tradition that has produced many important works over the centuries. Studying Malayalam literature can provide insights into the history, culture, and language of the people of Kerala and the surrounding regions.
