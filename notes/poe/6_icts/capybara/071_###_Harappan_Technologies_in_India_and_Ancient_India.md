### Harappan Technologies in India and Ancient India

Harappa was an ancient city located in present-day Pakistan, and the Harappan civilization flourished between 2600 BCE and 1900 BCE. The Indus Valley Civilization or the Harappan civilization was one of the earliest civilizations in the world, and it was known for its advanced technological innovations.

Here are some of the Harappan technologies in India and ancient India that are worth noting:

1. Town Planning: The Harappan civilization had well-planned cities with well-designed drainage systems, brick-paved roads, and multi-story buildings.

2. Agriculture: The Harappans were skilled in agriculture and used advanced techniques like crop rotation, use of ploughs, and irrigation systems.

3. Pottery: The Harappans were skilled in pottery and created a variety of ceramics, including vases, bowls, jars, and figurines.

4. Trade and Commerce: The Harappans were skilled traders and had established trade links with Mesopotamia, Central Asia, and other parts of India.

5. Metallurgy: The Harappans were skilled in metallurgy and used copper, bronze, and gold to create ornaments and tools.

6. Textiles: The Harappans were skilled in textile production and created cotton and woolen fabrics.

7. Writing: The Harappans had their unique script, which is yet to be deciphered.
