## Module 3- Indian Religion, Philosophy, and Practices

This module focuses on the various Indian religions, philosophies, and practices that have evolved over the centuries. It is important to understand the basic concepts and beliefs of Indian religions as they have shaped the Indian society and culture.

### Hinduism

- Hinduism is one of the oldest religions in the world and has a vast number of followers in India and around the world.
- It is based on the belief in one ultimate reality called Brahman and the cycle of birth, death, and rebirth called Samsara.
- The religion is characterized by a diverse set of beliefs, practices, and rituals, including the worship of various deities, yoga, meditation, and the pursuit of knowledge and self-realization.
- Some of the major Hindu scriptures include the Vedas, Upanishads, Bhagavad Gita, and Puranas.

### Buddhism

- Buddhism was founded by Gautama Buddha in the 5th century BCE and has spread to many countries around the world.
- It is based on the Four Noble Truths and the Eightfold Path, which provide a framework for the pursuit of enlightenment and liberation from suffering.
- The religion emphasizes the importance of meditation, mindfulness, and compassion towards all living beings.
- Some of the major Buddhist scriptures include the Tripitaka, Mahayana Sutras, and Tibetan Book of the Dead.

### Jainism

- Jainism is an ancient Indian religion that emphasizes the importance of non-violence, self-control, and compassion towards all living beings.
- It is based on the belief in the existence of infinite souls or Jivas that are trapped in the cycle of birth, death, and rebirth called Samsara.
- The religion advocates the path of asceticism and self-discipline to achieve liberation from the cycle of Samsara.
- Some of the major Jain scriptures include the Agamas, Tattvartha Sutra, and Samayasara.

### Sikhism

- Sikhism is a monotheistic religion founded by Guru Nanak in the 15th century CE in the Punjab region of India.
- It is based on the belief in one ultimate reality called Waheguru and the pursuit of social justice, equality, and service to others.
- The religion emphasizes the importance of meditation, self-discipline, and the study of the holy scripture called Guru Granth Sahib.
- Some of the major Sikh scriptures include the Guru Granth Sahib, Dasam Granth, and Sarbloh Granth.

### Indian Philosophy

- Indian philosophy has a rich history and has contributed to the development of various philosophical schools of thought.
- Some of the major philosophical schools include the Nyaya, Vaisheshika, Samkhya, Yoga, Mimamsa, and Vedanta schools.
- These schools offer different perspectives on the nature of reality, knowledge, ethics, and the purpose of life.
- Some of the major philosophers include Shankara, Ramanuja, Madhva, and Patanjali.

### Learning Tricks and Mnemonics

- One mnemonic for remembering the major Indian religions is "HBJS," which stands for Hinduism, Buddhism, Jainism, and Sikhism.
- Another mnemonic for remembering the major philosophical schools is "NAVY MMV," which stands for Nyaya, Vaisheshika, Samkhya, Yoga, Mimamsa, and Vedanta.
