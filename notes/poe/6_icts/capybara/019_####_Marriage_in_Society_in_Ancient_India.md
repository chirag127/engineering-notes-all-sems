#### Marriage in Society in Ancient India

Marriage was an essential social institution in ancient India, and it played a significant role in shaping the Indian society's fabric. Here are some key points about marriage in ancient India:

1. **Types of Marriages:** There were eight types of marriages in ancient India, such as Brahma, Daiva, Arsha, Prajapatya, Asura, Gandharva, Rakshasa, and Paishacha.

2. **Brahma Marriage:** It was the most respectable type of marriage, where the father of the bridegroom arranged the marriage with a suitable bride.

3. **Daiva Marriage:** In this type of marriage, the bride's father would offer his daughter to a priest as a sacrificial offering.

4. **Arsha Marriage:** In this type of marriage, the bridegroom would offer a cow and a bull to the bride's father, and the marriage would take place with the bride's consent.

5. **Prajapatya Marriage:** It was a simple marriage where the bride and groom would exchange vows in the presence of a priest.

6. **Asura Marriage:** In this type of marriage, the bridegroom would pay the bride's father for the bride's hand in marriage.

7. **Gandharva Marriage:** It was a love marriage where the bride and groom would marry with mutual consent.

8. **Rakshasa Marriage:** It was a forced marriage where the bridegroom would abduct the bride and marry her against her will.

9. **Paishacha Marriage:** It was an offensive marriage, where the bridegroom would seduce an unwilling woman and marry her.

10. **Marriageable Age:** The minimum age for marriage was 16 for girls and 25 for boys.

11. **Polygamy:** Polygamy was prevalent in ancient India, and it was more common among the upper caste.

12. **Sati:** Sati was a practice where a widow would immolate herself on her husband's funeral pyre. It was mainly prevalent among the upper caste.

13. **Dowry:** Dowry was a prevalent practice in ancient India, where the bride's family would offer gifts and wealth to the groom's family.
