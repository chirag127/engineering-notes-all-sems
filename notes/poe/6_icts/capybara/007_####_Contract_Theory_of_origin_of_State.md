#### Contract Theory of origin of State

The Contract Theory of origin of State is a political theory that explains how states came into existence. According to this theory, the state is not a natural entity, but a product of a social contract between individuals who agreed to give up some of their rights and freedoms in exchange for protection and security provided by the state.

Here are some important points to understand the Contract Theory of origin of State:

1. Social Contract: The Contract Theory states that people in a state of nature would agree to give up some of their individual rights and freedoms to a government or ruler in exchange for protection and security. This is known as the social contract.

2. John Locke: One of the most famous proponents of this theory was John Locke, who believed that the government's power should be limited to protect individual rights.

3. Thomas Hobbes: Another important philosopher who contributed to the Contract Theory was Thomas Hobbes. He believed that the social contract was necessary to prevent a state of nature, which he described as a "war of all against all."

4. Legitimate Authority: According to the Contract Theory, the government's power comes from the consent of the governed. This means that the government's authority is legitimate only if people agree to be governed by it.

5. Consent of Governed: The concept of "consent of the governed" is crucial to the Contract Theory. It means that individuals have the right to choose their government and to hold it accountable for its actions.

6. Limitations on Government: The Contract Theory also emphasizes the importance of limitations on government power. This is because the government's primary role is to protect individual rights, and excessive government power can lead to the violation of these rights.

7. Mnemonic: A useful mnemonic to remember the Contract Theory is "SCLL," which stands for Social Contract, Consent of Governed, Legitimate Authority, and Limitations on Government.

Overall, the Contract Theory of origin of State is an important political theory that explains how governments came into existence and what their role should be in protecting individual rights and freedoms. Understanding this theory is crucial to understanding modern political systems and the relationship between individuals and the state.
