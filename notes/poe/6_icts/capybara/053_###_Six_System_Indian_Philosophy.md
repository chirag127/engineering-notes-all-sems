### Six System Indian Philosophy

Six System Indian Philosophy, also known as Shad-darsana, is a collection of six orthodox schools of Hindu philosophy that originated in ancient India. These six schools are:

1. Samkhya Philosophy
2. Yoga Philosophy
3. Nyaya Philosophy
4. Vaisheshika Philosophy
5. Mimamsa Philosophy
6. Vedanta Philosophy

Each of these schools has its own unique perspective on the nature of reality, the self, and the ultimate goal of human life. Here is a brief overview of each of these six schools:

1. Samkhya Philosophy: This school focuses on the dualistic nature of the universe, asserting that there are two fundamental entities - Purusha (consciousness) and Prakriti (matter). The ultimate goal of life is to attain liberation by realizing the difference between these two entities.

2. Yoga Philosophy: This school emphasizes the need for self-discipline and the practice of meditation to attain self-realization. The eight limbs of Yoga, as described by Patanjali, are the means to achieve this goal.

3. Nyaya Philosophy: This school is concerned with the nature of knowledge and logic. It asserts that knowledge is valid only if it is based on perception, inference, comparison, and testimony.

4. Vaisheshika Philosophy: This school focuses on the nature of reality and asserts that the universe is composed of nine substances - earth, water, fire, air, ether, time, space, mind, and soul.

5. Mimamsa Philosophy: This school is concerned with the interpretation of the Vedas and focuses on the rituals and sacrifices described in them. It asserts that the ultimate goal of human life is to attain heaven through these rituals.

6. Vedanta Philosophy: This school is based on the teachings of the Upanishads and focuses on the nature of the self and the ultimate reality. It asserts that the self (Atman) is identical to the ultimate reality (Brahman), and the ultimate goal of human life is to realize this identity.

Mnemonics and learning tricks for these six schools of philosophy are not readily available as they are complex and require in-depth study and understanding. However, it is helpful to remember the first letter of each school to recall them easily - S (Samkhya), Y (Yoga), N (Nyaya), V (Vaisheshika), M (Mimamsa), and V (Vedanta).

In conclusion, the Six System Indian Philosophy is an essential part of Hindu philosophy and provides a comprehensive understanding of the nature of reality, self, and the ultimate goal of human life. It is crucial to study and understand each school's unique perspective to gain a deeper understanding of Hinduism and its teachings.
