### Biology in India and Ancient India

2. **Sushruta**: Sushruta was an ancient Indian physician who is considered to be the father of surgery. He is credited with developing many surgical techniques and instruments, including the use of antiseptics and anesthesia.

3. **Charaka**: Charaka was another ancient Indian physician who is known for his work in the field of Ayurveda. He authored the Charaka Samhita, which is a comprehensive guide to Ayurvedic medicine.

4. **Yoga**: Yoga is an ancient Indian practice that involves physical, mental, and spiritual exercises. It is believed to promote health and well-being and has been shown to have many health benefits.

5. **Vedic Science**: Vedic science is a collection of ancient Indian texts that cover a wide range of subjects, including biology. These texts contain detailed descriptions of various plants, animals, and other living organisms.
