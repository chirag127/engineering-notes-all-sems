### India’s Dominance up to Pre-colonial Times and Ancient India

India’s dominance in pre-colonial times and ancient India can be understood through the following points:

1. Ancient India was home to one of the world’s oldest civilizations, the Indus Valley Civilization. This civilization was known for its advanced urban planning, trade, and art.

2. Ancient India was also known for its contribution to mathematics, science, and literature. The concept of zero and decimal system were invented in India, which revolutionized mathematics.

3. The Maurya Empire (321 BCE-185 BCE) was the largest empire in ancient India. Emperor Ashoka, a Mauryan ruler, is known for his policies of non-violence and religious tolerance.

4. The Gupta Empire (320 CE-550 CE) was known for its golden age of literature, art, and science. The decimal system, astronomy, and medicine flourished during this period.

5. The Indian subcontinent was a hub of trade and commerce, with the Silk Road passing through India. This led to the growth of cities, such as Varanasi, which became centers of learning and culture.

6. The caste system, which divided society into four main groups, developed during ancient India. This system governed social and economic relationships in India for centuries.

Mnemonic: Remember the acronym AIM CSG. It stands for Ancient India, Maurya Empire, Caste system, Silk Road, Gupta Empire.
