#### Marriage in Society in Ancient India

Marriage was an important institution in ancient Indian society. It was considered a sacred bond that connected two individuals and their families. The following points provide an overview of marriage in ancient India:

1. Types of Marriages: 
    - There were eight types of marriages mentioned in ancient Indian texts, including Brahma, Daiva, Arsha, Prajapatya, Gandharva, Asura, Rakshasa, and Paishacha.
    - The Brahma marriage was the most preferred form of marriage, where the bride's family gave her away to the groom in exchange for a dowry.

2. Age of Marriage: 
    - In ancient India, marriage was usually performed at a young age.
    - The age of marriage for boys was between 16-20, while for girls, it was between 14-16.

3. Arranged Marriage: 
    - Arranged marriages were the norm in ancient India.
    - The parents of the bride and groom would find a suitable partner for their child through social networks or matchmakers.

4. Importance of Dowry: 
    - Dowry was an important aspect of marriage in ancient India.
    - The groom's family would demand a dowry from the bride's family, which could include cash, jewelry, or other valuable items.

5. Role of Women: 
    - Women had an important role to play in ancient Indian marriages.
    - They were expected to be obedient wives and good homemakers.

6. Polygamy: 
    - Polygamy was allowed in ancient India, but it was not very common.
    - Kings and wealthy individuals were the only ones who could afford to have multiple wives.

7. Divorce: 
    - Divorce was allowed in ancient India, but it was not considered a favorable option.
    - It was usually the last resort after all other means of reconciliation had failed.
