### Telugu Literature

Telugu literature refers to the body of works written in the Telugu language, which is predominantly spoken in the Indian state of Andhra Pradesh and Telangana. Telugu literature has a rich history that spans over a thousand years, and it is known for its poetry, drama, and prose.

#### History of Telugu Literature

- Telugu literature can be traced back to the 11th century, with the works of Nannaya, Tikkana, and Yerrapragada.
- The Vijayanagara Empire (14th-16th century) was a golden age for Telugu literature, with the works of poets such as Srinatha and Potana.
- The 19th century saw the rise of the modern Telugu literature movement, with authors such as Gurajada Apparao and Sri Sri.

#### Major Forms of Telugu Literature

- **Poetry:** Telugu poetry can be divided into two categories: classical and modern. Classical poetry includes the works of Nannaya, Tikkana, and Yerrapragada, while modern poetry includes the works of Kaloji Narayana Rao and Sri Sri.
- **Prose:** Telugu prose includes novels, short stories, and essays. Some of the most famous Telugu novelists are Viswanatha Satyanarayana and Yandamuri Veerendranath.
- **Drama:** Telugu drama has a long history, with the earliest plays dating back to the 10th century. Modern Telugu drama is known for its realism and social commentary.

#### Mnemonics and Learning Tricks

- To remember the three major forms of Telugu literature (poetry, prose, and drama), you can use the mnemonic "PPD" (Poetry, Prose, Drama).
- To remember the famous Telugu novelists Viswanatha Satyanarayana and Yandamuri Veerendranath, you can use the mnemonic "SAY VY" (Satyanarayana, Andhra Pradesh, Yandamuri Veerendranath).

#### Conclusion

In conclusion, Telugu literature is a rich and diverse body of work that showcases the history and culture of Andhra Pradesh and Telangana. With its long history and talented authors, Telugu literature continues to captivate readers to this day.
