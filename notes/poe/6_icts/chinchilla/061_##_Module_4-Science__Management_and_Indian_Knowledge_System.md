## Module 4-Science, Management and Indian Knowledge System

This module is designed to provide the learners with an understanding of the relationship between science, management, and the Indian knowledge system. The module covers various aspects of science, management, and the Indian knowledge system, their interrelationships, and their applications in the contemporary world. The following topics are covered in this module:

1. Introduction to Science, Management, and Indian Knowledge System:
   - Definition and scope of science, management, and the Indian knowledge system
   - Interrelationships between science, management, and the Indian knowledge system

2. Science and Management:
   - Scientific management and its principles
   - Management of scientific research and development
   - Applications of science in management

3. Indian Knowledge System:
   - Concept of knowledge in Indian philosophy
   - Indian knowledge system and its components
   - Applications of the Indian knowledge system in contemporary times

4. Science and Indian Knowledge System:
   - Relationship between science and Indian knowledge system
   - Integration of traditional and modern knowledge systems
   - Examples of the application of the integrated knowledge system

5. Management and Indian Knowledge System:
   - Relationship between management and Indian knowledge system
   - Application of Indian knowledge system in management
   - Case studies of successful application of Indian knowledge system in management

Mnemonics/Learning Tricks:
- To remember the relationship between Science, Management, and Indian Knowledge System, we can use the acronym SMIKS (Science-Management-Indian Knowledge System).

Overall, this module provides learners with a comprehensive understanding of the relationship between science, management, and the Indian knowledge system. It equips them with the knowledge and skills to apply their learning in real-life situations and make informed decisions.
