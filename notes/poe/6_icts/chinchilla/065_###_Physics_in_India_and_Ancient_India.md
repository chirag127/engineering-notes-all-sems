### Physics in India and Ancient India

Physics has a rich history in India, dating back to ancient times. The ancient Indian scholars made significant contributions to the field of physics, including the discovery of zero, the concept of atoms, and the laws of motion. Here are some important concepts and scholars in the history of physics in India:

1. **Siddhantas**: The Siddhantas were a series of texts written by Indian astronomers and mathematicians. They contained knowledge about astronomy, mathematics, and physics. One of the most important Siddhantas was the Surya Siddhanta, which contained information about the solar system and the movement of the planets.

2. **Aryabhata**: Aryabhata was an Indian mathematician and astronomer who lived in the 5th century CE. He made significant contributions to the field of mathematics, including the invention of the decimal system and the concept of zero. He also wrote the Aryabhatiya, a text that contained information about astronomy and mathematics.

3. **Brahmagupta**: Brahmagupta was an Indian mathematician and astronomer who lived in the 7th century CE. He made significant contributions to the field of mathematics, including the invention of negative numbers and the concept of zero. He also wrote the Brahmasphutasiddhanta, a text that contained information about astronomy and mathematics.

4. **Bhaskara**: Bhaskara was an Indian mathematician and astronomer who lived in the 12th century CE. He made significant contributions to the fields of mathematics and astronomy, including the discovery of the principles of differential calculus and the development of the concept of gravity.

5. **Srinivasa Ramanujan**: Srinivasa Ramanujan was an Indian mathematician who lived in the early 20th century. He made significant contributions to the field of mathematics, including the discovery of new formulas for pi and the development of theories related to modular functions and hypergeometric series.
