### Harappan Technologies in India and Ancient India

The Harappan civilization, also known as the Indus Valley Civilization, flourished in the Indian subcontinent from around 2600 BCE to 1900 BCE. The civilization was known for its advanced technologies, which helped it to prosper and become one of the most prominent ancient civilizations in the world. Some of the most notable technologies developed by the Harappans include:

1. Urban Planning: The Harappans were skilled at urban planning and built their cities with a grid-like street system. They also had an advanced sewage system that included pipes, drains, and underground sewage systems.

2. Metallurgy: The Harappans were skilled metallurgists who were able to extract metals like copper, bronze, and tin from ores. They also developed advanced techniques for casting bronze objects.

3. Agriculture: The Harappans were one of the earliest civilizations to practice agriculture on a large scale. They developed techniques for irrigation and crop rotation to increase crop yields.

4. Trade: The Harappans were skilled traders who traded with other civilizations in the region. They developed an extensive trade network that extended to Mesopotamia and Oman.

5. Writing: The Harappans developed a system of writing that is still not fully understood today. The script, which is known as the Indus script, has not been deciphered yet, but it is believed to have been used for recording trade and commerce.

6. Art and Crafts: The Harappans were skilled at arts and crafts and produced a wide range of pottery, jewelry, and other decorative objects.
