#### Evolutionary Theory of origin of State

The Evolutionary theory of the origin of the state is a political theory that explains the emergence of the state as a natural and gradual process of social evolution. According to this theory, the state evolved from the primitive human societies and developed through a series of stages over time. This theory is also known as the organic theory of the state.

The evolutionary theory of the origin of the state can be explained through the following points:

1. Family: The first stage of the evolution of the state started with the formation of the family. The family was the first social institution that provided protection and security to its members. The family was also the first institution that laid the foundation for the formation of the state.

2. Clan: The second stage of the evolution of the state was the formation of the clan. The clan was a group of families that were related to each other. The clan provided a larger social group that could provide protection and security to its members.

3. Tribe: The third stage of the evolution of the state was the formation of the tribe. The tribe was a larger social group that was formed by the association of several clans. The tribe had a common language, culture, and tradition. The tribe provided protection and security to its members.

4. Chiefdom: The fourth stage of the evolution of the state was the formation of the chiefdom. The chiefdom was a political organization that was formed by the association of several tribes. The chiefdom had a central authority that had the power to make decisions for the entire community.

5. State: The final stage of the evolution of the state was the formation of the state. The state was a political organization that was formed by the association of several chiefdoms. The state had a central authority that had the power to make decisions for the entire community. The state also had a defined territory, a system of laws, and a complex bureaucracy.

Advantages of the Evolutionary Theory of origin of State:

1. The evolutionary theory of the origin of the state provides a comprehensive explanation of the emergence of the state from the primitive human societies.

2. The evolutionary theory of the origin of the state provides a historical perspective on the development of the state.

3. The evolutionary theory of the origin of the state emphasizes the gradual and natural process of the development of the state.

Disadvantages of the Evolutionary Theory of origin of State:

1. The evolutionary theory of the origin of the state is based on assumptions and generalizations about the primitive human societies.

2. The evolutionary theory of the origin of the state overlooks the role of conflict, power, and coercion in the formation of the state.

3. The evolutionary theory of the origin of the state does not provide a clear explanation of the factors that led to the emergence of the state.
