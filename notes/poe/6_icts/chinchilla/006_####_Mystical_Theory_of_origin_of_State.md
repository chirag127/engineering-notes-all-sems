#### Mystical Theory of origin of State

The Mystical Theory of origin of State is one of the oldest and most ancient theories that explain the origin of the State. According to this theory, the State is a creation of God or the divine power. This theory believes that the State is not a human invention, but rather a divine creation. Here are some important points to understand the Mystical Theory of origin of State:

1. Divine Creation: According to this theory, the State is a divine creation, and it is not a human invention. The State is created by God or the divine power to maintain law and order in society.

2. Theocracy: The Mystical Theory believes that the State is a theocracy, which means that the ruler is appointed by God or the divine power. The ruler is not elected by the people, but rather chosen by God.

3. Absolute Power: The ruler in the Mystical Theory has absolute power, and he is not accountable to anyone except God. The ruler is considered to be the representative of God on earth, and his decisions are considered to be the will of God.

4. Divine Right: The ruler in the Mystical Theory has a divine right to rule, which means that he has the right to rule because he is chosen by God or the divine power. The people do not have the right to question the ruler's authority, as it is considered to be the will of God.
