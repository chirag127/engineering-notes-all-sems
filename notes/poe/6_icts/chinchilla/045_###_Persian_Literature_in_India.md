### Persian Literature in India

Persian literature in India refers to the Persian language literature that was produced in the Indian subcontinent during the Mughal period (16th-19th centuries). Persian was the official language of the Mughal court, and the rulers and nobility of India patronized Persian literature, art, and culture. Persian literature in India had a significant impact on the development of the Urdu language and literature.
