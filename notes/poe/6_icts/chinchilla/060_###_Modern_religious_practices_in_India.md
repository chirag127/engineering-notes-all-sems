### Modern religious practices in India

India is a country with a diverse culture and rich traditions. Religion plays a significant role in the lives of people in India, and over time, new religious practices have emerged. Here are some of the modern religious practices in India:

1. Bhakti Movement: The Bhakti Movement is a devotional movement that originated in medieval India. It emphasizes the worship of a single god or goddess rather than the many gods and goddesses found in Hinduism. This movement has had a significant impact on modern Indian culture, inspiring many to adopt a more personalized and devotional approach to religion.

2. Art of Living: Art of Living is a spiritual organization founded by Sri Sri Ravi Shankar in 1981. It offers a variety of programs and courses aimed at promoting personal well-being and social change. The organization emphasizes the importance of meditation, yoga, and breathing techniques to help individuals achieve inner peace and happiness.

3. Brahma Kumaris: Brahma Kumaris is a spiritual movement founded in India in the 1930s. It emphasizes the importance of meditation and self-realization to achieve spiritual enlightenment. The movement is known for its emphasis on the empowerment of women and its teachings on non-violence.

4. Isha Foundation: Isha Foundation is a non-profit organization founded by Sadhguru Jaggi Vasudev in 1992. It offers a variety of programs and courses aimed at promoting physical, mental, and spiritual well-being. Its flagship program, Inner Engineering, teaches participants meditation and yoga techniques to help them achieve inner peace and happiness.

5. Falun Gong: Falun Gong is a spiritual movement that originated in China in the 1990s but has gained a following in India as well. It combines meditation, breathing exercises, and moral teachings to promote personal well-being and spiritual growth.

Mnemonics and learning tricks:

1. Remember the Bhakti Movement as the "Devotion Movement" that emphasizes the worship of a single god or goddess.

2. Think of the Art of Living as a way to "live artfully" by practicing meditation, yoga, and breathing techniques.

3. Remember the Brahma Kumaris as a movement that emphasizes "self-realization" through meditation.

4. Think of the Isha Foundation as a way to "find inner peace" through meditation and yoga techniques.

5. Remember Falun Gong as a practice that combines "meditation, breathing, and morals" to promote personal well-being and spiritual growth.
