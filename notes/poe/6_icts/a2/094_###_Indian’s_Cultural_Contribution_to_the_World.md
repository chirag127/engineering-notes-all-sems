### Indian’s Cultural Contribution to the World

1. Yoga and Meditation: India is the birthplace of Yoga and Meditation which are popular all over the world today for their health benefits. Yoga incorporates physical postures, breathing exercises, and meditation to bring harmony between the body and mind.

2. Ayurveda: Ayurveda is the ancient Indian system of medicine which utilizes herbs, oils, and lifestyle changes to heal the body and mind. The holistic concepts of Ayurveda regarding health and wellness have gained widespread popularity worldwide.

3. Mathematics: India has made seminal contributions to mathematics, including the invention of the decimal number system, the concept of zero, and calculus. The famous mathematical treatises by Aryabhata, Brahmagupta, and Bhaskara have had a profound impact on the field.

4. Chess: The popular board game Chess originated in India in the 6th century CE. Originally called 'Chaturanga', the Indian version incorporated dice and moved based on throws. The modern version of Chess as we know it today developed in Europe starting the 15th century.

5. Clothing: Indian clothing like the saree and dhoti have influenced fashion trends worldwide. The exotic colors, prints and embroideries of Indian fabrics and weaves have been adopted globally in various adaptations. Indian clothing highlights the cultural diversity and textile heritage of the country.
