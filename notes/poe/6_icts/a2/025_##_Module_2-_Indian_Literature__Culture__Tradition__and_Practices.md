## Module 2- Indian Literature, Culture, Tradition, and Practices

- Indian literature includes religious texts like Vedas, Upanishads, Ramayana, Mahabharata, Bhagavad Gita, etc. These texts are written in Sanskrit and are some of the oldest surviving texts in the world. They provide insights into ancient Indian culture, tradition, and practices.
- Indian culture is one of the oldest cultures in the world. It includes cultural elements like clothing styles, cuisine, festivals, music, dance, values, and traditions. Some examples are:
- Saree and dhoti are traditional Indian clothing styles.
- Cuisine varies by region but spices and variety are common. Some popular dishes are curries, biryanis, naan, chapati, etc.
- Diwali, Holi, Onam, Pongal, etc. are popular festivals celebrated with colors, lights, prayers, and traditions.
- Indian classical music includes Hindustani music of North India and Carnatic music of South India. Kathak, Bharatnatyam, Odissi, Kuchipudi, etc. are classical Indian dance forms.
- important Indian values include respect for elders, education, tolerance, non-violence, kindness, etc. Important traditions include joint families, arranged marriages, etc.
- Yoga and Ayurveda are some of the traditional Indian practices to achieve holistic health and well-being. Meditation, asanas, herbal medicines, Panchakarma, etc. are part of Yoga and Ayurveda respectively.
