### drama as Cultural Heritage of India

- Indian drama and theater has a long and rich tradition spanning over 2000 years. It includes musical, dance and theatrical performances.
- The origin of Indian drama can be traced back to ancient religious rituals and folk performances. The major components of Indian drama were:
-- Nāṭya (dramatic representations) including song and dance.
-- Rasa (sentiment, emotive flavor) referring to the aesthetic sentiment evoked in the audience.
-- Bhāva (mood) referring to the mental and emotional states expressed.

Learning tricks:
- Remember the components (Nāṭya, Rasa, Bhāva) and their meanings to understand the essence of Indian drama.
- Associate song and dance with dramatic representations to remember Nāṭya.
- Imagine feeling different emotions to remember the aesthetic flavors (Rasa) and mental states (Bhāva) evoked.

Advantages:
- Indian drama incorporated music, dance and theater which appealed to the senses and emotions of the audience.
- The dramatic performances were highly stylized with importance given to expressions and techniques to evoke the desired sentiment and mood.

Disadvantages:
- The rigid adherence to the principles of Rasa and Bhāva limited the scope of experimentation and innovation in Indian drama.
