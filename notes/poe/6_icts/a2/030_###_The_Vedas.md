### The Vedas

The Vedas are the oldest scriptures of Hinduism and are considered to be revealed texts by the seers (rishis) of the ancient Indian traditions. The Vedas are four in number - Rig Veda, Yajur Veda, Sama Veda and Atharva Veda.

**Rig Veda**: The Rig Veda is the oldest of the four Vedas and contains hymns praising the various deities. It is mainly focused on benedictions or boons for material prosperity. Some key deities are Indra, Agni, Soma, etc. A famous hymn from Rig Veda is the Creation Hymn or Nasadiya Sukta.

**Yajur Veda**: The Yajur Veda contains mantras or verses used in the sacrificial rituals. It consists of the prose passages called brähmanas which discuss the rituals in great detail. The two major recensions of the Yajur Veda are the Krishna Yajur Veda or Black Yajur Veda and the Shukla Yajur Veda or White Yajur Veda.

**Sama Veda**: The Sama Veda is the Veda of melodies and chants. It consists of hymns from the Rig Veda but set to musical tunes. The hymns are typically sung by priests during the soma rituals and sacrifices.

**Atharva Veda**: The Atharva Veda is different from the other three Vedas and contains hymns for magic spells and healing incantations. It focuses on remedies for diseases and calamities and contains philosophical ideas as well as scientific observations relating to astronomy, physiology, etc.

Some key points to remember:

- The Vedas are considered Śruti or revealed scriptures and are regarded as eternal truths
- The Vedas are written in Vedic Sanskrit and contain mantras or hymns addressed to various deities
- The four Vedas are Rig, Yajur, Sama and Atharva Veda with different focus areas like rituals, melodies, magic spells, etc.
- The Vedas contain many philosophical ideas and scientific observations apart from religious hymns and mantras.
