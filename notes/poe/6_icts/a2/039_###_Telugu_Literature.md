### Telugu Literature

- Telugu literature refers to the body of works written in the Telugu language. It consists of poems, novels, short stories, dramas, epics and others dating back to the 11th century CE.
- The earliest extant literature in Telugu is the epics of Kanva dynasty whose king Nannaya built the temple at Tirupati. They were composed in a poetic style known as Champu in metrical verses.
- Srinatha (1365–1441 CE) composed Mahaprasthanam, the earliest available Telugu poem in blank verse. He also wrote the Bhimakanda, a Telugu rendering of the Ramayana. Later, 'kandapaas' were added to the rendering by six other poets. This work is known as Kavitrayam (three great poets).
- Other notable literary works from this period include Basava puranam by Palkuriki Somanatha and Panduranga Mahatyam by Tenali Ramakrishna. Krishna Deva Raya (1509–1529 CE) composed Amuktamalyada in a mixture of Telugu, Kannada and Sanskrit languages, telling the story of Andal—the divine female saint, a devotee of Vishnu.
- Drama flourished in the 17th century under the patronage of the kings of Vijayanagar. Srinatha and Peddana enriched Telugu literature.
- In the 18th–19th centuries, translations of the great Indian epics Ramayana, Mahabharata and Bhagavatam were done by celebrated poets like Bhandaru Acchamamba and Gona Buddha Reddy.
- Modern period saw landmark poets like Gurazada Apparao who wrote play Kanyasulkam addressing social issues. Other modern poets are Sri Sri, Garikapati Narasimha Rao, Devulapalli Krishnasastri and C. Narayana Reddy.
- Telugu cinema gave scope to several lyricists and Telugu literature.
- Some key Mnemonics and learning tricks for Telugu Literature: Remember first 3 Telugu epics through PAK (Palkuriki Somanatha, Annaya, Kanva); Srinatha introduced blank verse - remember as Sri(natha)-blank; Kavitrayam added 6 poets to Bhimakanda - remember as kavi(6 poets) added to trayam(3 poets); Deva Raya composed in Telugu-Kannada-Sanskrit - remember as Deva used 3 languages; Translations of epics in 18-19th centuries - remember as Telugu rendered(Translated) India's epics (Ramayana, Mahabharata, Bhagavatam).
