# INDIAN TRADITIONS, CULTURAL AND SOCIETY

## Indian Family System
- Joint Family System: Multiple generations living together in a large family unit. Promotes bonding and sharing of responsibilities.
- Nuclear Family: Only parents and unmarried children living together as a separate unit. More independence but lesser support system.

## Indian Marriage Traditions
- Arranged Marriages: Marriages arranged by families based on matching horoscopes and social status. Seen as ensuring family approval and compatibility.
- Dowry System: Bride's family paying cash, gifts or property to bridegroom's family. Though illegal, still prevalent in some communities. Seen as a social evil.

## Indian Dressing Style
- Traditional Indian clothing like saree, salwar kameez, dhoti, kurta, sherwani, etc. reflect Indian culture and vary based on region, religion and community.
- Festive clothing is often elaborate, colorful and heavily embellished. Reflects the significance of festivals and occasions in Indian tradition.

## Indian Cuisine
- Indian cuisine is diverse with distinct spices, herbs and wide variety of regional cuisines reflecting the varied cultures.
- Staple foods include rice, roti, daal, sabji, curry. Sweets like gulab jamun, ras malai, etc. are popular desserts.
- Eating with hands is customary in some regions as it is considered more appetizing and promotes bonding during meals.

## Indian Festivals
- Diwali, Holi, Onam, Pongal, Eid, Christmas, etc. are some major festivals celebrated with fervor.
- Festivals are time for families and communities to come together, share gifts and food, wear new clothes and strengthen bonds.
- Many festivals have religious significance while some are harvest festivals. Reflect the diversity of Indian cultures and traditions.
