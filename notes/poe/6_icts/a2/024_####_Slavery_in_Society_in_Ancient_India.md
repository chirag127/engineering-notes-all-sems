#### Slavery in Society in Ancient India

- Slavery was prevalent in ancient Indian society and there were several types of slaves:
- **Prisoners of war**: Enemies captured in wars and battles were the most common type of slaves. They had no rights and could be used for hard labour.
- **Debt slaves**: People who were unable to repay debts were made slaves by creditors until they worked to repay the debt. This was known as 'debt bondage'.
- **Born slaves**: The children of slaves were also considered slaves and the status was hereditary.
- **Self-sale**: During famines and other calamities, some people would sell themselves or their children into slavery for food and survival.

- The rights and treatment of slaves varied based on the type of slavery and owners. Some slaves were treated as members of the household while others had difficult lives with hard labour.
- Slavery declined after the rise of Bhakti and devotional movements which preached equality and condemned the practice. The British also banned slavery in India in 1843.
