### Famous Sanskrit Authors in India

Some of the famous Sanskrit authors and their major works in ancient India are:

1. Kalidasa: He is considered as one of the greatest Sanskrit poets. His famous works are Abhijnanashakuntala, Kumarasambhava, Meghaduta, Raghuvamsa, etc. Abhijnanashakuntala is his most famous work which tells the story of Shakuntala and King Dushyanta.

2. Bharavi: He was a renowned Sanskrit poet who lived in the 6th century CE. His most famous work is Kiratarjuniya which describes the battle between Arjuna and Shiva disguised as a hunter.

3. Matanga: He was a Sanskrit author who lived in the 9th century CE. His most famous work is Brhat-Samhita which is an encyclopedia that covers various topics such as astrology, planetary motions, architecture, grammar, poetry, etc.

4. Amaruka: He was an ancient Sanskrit author who lived in the 2nd century CE. His most famous work is Amarakosha which is a thesaurus containing synonyms and antonyms used in poetry to create special effects. It is one of the most important reference works for Sanskrit poetry and literature.
