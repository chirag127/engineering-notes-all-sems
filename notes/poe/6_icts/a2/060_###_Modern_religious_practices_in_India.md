### Modern religious practices in India

- Hinduism: Hinduism is the dominant religion of India. Modern practices include:
-- Puja or daily worship at home altars or temples which may include meditation, yoga, religious songs & chants (bhajans), etc.
-- Celebration of major festivals like Diwali, Holi, Dusshera, etc. which are often community events with prayers, socializing & cultural activities.
-- Many Hindus follow religious teachings of modern gurus & swamis like Sathya Sai Baba, Ramdev, Sri Sri Ravi Shankar, etc. who teach spirituality, yoga & meditation.
-- ISKCON or Hare Krishnas are a modern Gaudiya Vaishnava religious movement which emphasizes bhakti or devotion through chanting, prayer & vegetarian diet.

- Islam: Islam is the second largest religion of India. Modern practices include:
-- Daily namaz or salat prayers are offered by faithful either at home or at mosques. Mosques are important community gathering spaces.
-- Celebration of Eid al-Fitr & Eid al-Adha with prayers at mosques, charity & feasting with family & community.
-- Some Muslims follow Sufi saints & dargahs which incorporate mystical practices like dhikr (chanting), qawwali singing & dhamal dance.
-- Reform movements like Deoband school & Tablighi Jamaat encourage a more orthodox practice of Islam focused on prayer, recitation of Quran & proselytization.
