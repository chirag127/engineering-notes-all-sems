### Various Philosophical Doctrines in India

The various philosophical doctrines that emerged in ancient India are:

1. Samkhya: It was propounded by Kapila. It is a dualistic philosophy and believes in two eternal realities - Prakriti (matter) and Purusha (soul). It considers Prakriti as the source of the world and Purusha as the passive witness. Liberation is attained by the separation of Purusha from Prakriti.
2. Yoga: It was founded by Patanjali. It is a practical discipline to achieve liberation through meditation and physical postures. The basic principles are derived from Samkhya philosophy. The eight limbs of Yoga are - Yama, Niyama, Asana, Pranayama, Pratyahara, Dharana, Dhyana and Samadhi.
3. Vaisheshika: It was founded by Kanada. It is an atomistic philosophy which postulates that all objects in the physical universe are reducible to a finite number of atoms. It enumerates five substances - earth, water, light, air and ether; and nine attributes - four primary: substance, quality, action, generality; and five secondary: number, magnitude, priority, posteriority, and being itself.
4. Nyaya: It was founded by Gautama. It is a logical and epistemological system. It consists of a methodology to acquire valid knowledge. It considers four sources of knowledge: perception, inference, comparison and verbal testimony. The Nyaya Sutras codify the method of dialectics for attaining liberation.
