### Political Ideals in Ancient India and their relevance in modern times

- Ancient Indian political thought focused on the ideals of achieving ideal state through Dharma (righteousness), Artha (material prosperity), Kama (pleasure) and Moksha (salvation).
- The major schools of political thought like Vedanta laid emphasis on an ideal state where there is welfare of all beings, prevalence of righteousness and contentment.
- In Arthashastra, Kautilya laid down principles of an ideal state with welfare, prosperity and security of people as core goals which could be achieved through principles of pragmatism. The text discusses statecraft, economy, diplomacy in detail to achieve the said goals. These ideals find resonance in modern concepts of welfare state.
- In Ramayana and Mahabharata, the ideals of righteous king, who works for the welfare of his subjects and ensures justice, find mention. These ideals have inspired and influenced modern concepts of democracy and constitutional monarchy.
- The concept of Gram Swaraj or village self-governance laid emphasis on self-sustained village communities. This ideal inspired Mahatma Gandhi and finds reflection in modern Panchayati Raj system in India.
- Thus, the political ideals in Ancient India which focused on welfare, righteousness, justice, sovereignty of people etc. have significant relevance in modern times and have inspired modern political concepts to a great extent.
