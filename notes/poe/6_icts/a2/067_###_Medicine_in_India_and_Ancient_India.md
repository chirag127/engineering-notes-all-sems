### Medicine in India and Ancient India

- Ancient Indian medicine or Ayurveda is one of the oldest medicinal systems, with a history of more than 3000 years.
- Ayurveda means 'science of life' and is a holistic system of health focusing on the whole body, mind and spirit.
- Some key principles of Ayurveda:

**The five elements:** Space, air, fire, water, earth. Imbalance in the elements leads to illness.
**Three doshas:** Vata, pitta and kapha. The doshas are energies that control bodily functions. Illness is due to imbalance in the doshas.
**Eight branches:** Internal medicine, surgery, ear nose throat, toxicology, psychiatry, pediatrics, geriatrics, fertility/virility.

- Key texts: Charaka Samhita and Sushruta Samhita which describe physiology, anatomy, medicine, surgery, toxicology, psychiatry, etc.
- Panchakarma is a key treatment involving purging, enema, nasal cleansing, bloodletting, and other techniques to restore balance in the body.
- Medicines are derived from plants, animals, and minerals. Examples: turmeric, neem, amla, ashwagandha, etc.
- Contributions: Classification of medicines, concepts of immunity and digestion, advanced surgery techniques like cosmetic surgery and cataract surgery, concepts of psychiatry, eight medical specialties, etc.
- Limitations: Lack of scientific validity for some concepts and remedies. Reliance on spirituality and supernatural reasons for illness. Some harmful practices like bloodletting.
- However, Ayurveda focuses on holistic long-term health and many of its concepts and medicines are still used today after scientific validation. It provides an important foundation for modern medicine.
