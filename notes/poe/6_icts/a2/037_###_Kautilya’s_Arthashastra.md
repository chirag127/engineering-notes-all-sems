### Kautilya’s Arthashastra

- Kautilya’s Arthashastra is an ancient Indian treatise on statecraft, economic policy and military strategy, written in Sanskrit by Chanakya, also known as Kautilya, the adviser to the first Mauryan emperor Chandragupta Maurya.
- It was written around 300 BCE, and discusses ideas of governance, economics, and military organization.
- Some key points from Arthashastra:
- The king's duty is to ensure the happiness and welfare of his subjects.
- The bureaucracy should be efficient and loyal.
- Economy should be regulated but not stifled. Land revenue is the main source of income for the state.
- Spies and secret services play an important role in surveillance and intelligence gathering.
- The army should be large, well trained and equipped.
- Forts and cities should be built for defense and security.
- Diplomacy and alliances are crucial aspects of foreign policy.
- Some useful mnemonics:
- 'The root of the Arthashastra is the Science of Polity, the Science of Polity has Wealth as its root, Wealth has the Earth as its root'
- 'In the happiness of the subjects lies the king's happiness, in their welfare his welfare. He shall not consider as good only that which pleases him but treat as beneficial to him whatever pleases his subjects.'
