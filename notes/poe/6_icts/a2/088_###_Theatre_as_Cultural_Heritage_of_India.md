### Theatre as Cultural Heritage of India

Theatre has been an integral part of Indian culture and heritage. Some key points about Theatre as Cultural Heritage of India are:

1. Origin of Theatre in India: Theatre in India originated from religious rituals and folk performances. Sanskrit theatre originated from religious rituals and ceremonies of the Vedic culture. Folk theatre forms originated from the festivals and events in villages. Many theatre forms like Bhavai from Gujarat, Nautanki from Uttar Pradesh originated from folk arts.

2. Ancient Indian Theatre: Sanskrit drama or Sanskrit theatre was popular in ancient India and flourished between the 2nd century BCE and the 8th century CE. Three famous playwrights of the era were Bhasa, Kalidasa, and Visakhadatta. Famous Sanskrit plays written during this period include Shakuntala, Mrcchakatika, and Abhijnanashakuntala.

3. Traditional Regional Theatre forms: India has a variety of traditional regional theatre styles. Some major regional theatre styles are:
- Kathakali from Kerala which uses elaborate costumes and face masks.
- Theyyam from Kerala which is ritual dance worship of gods.
- Yakshagana from Karnataka which is a dance drama including music, songs, and dialogue.
- Tamasha from Maharashtra which includes music, songs, and dance.
- Chhau from West Bengal, Odisha and Jharkhand which is a tribal martial dance drama.
- Nautanki from Uttar Pradesh which narrates tales from Indian epics with music and dance.
