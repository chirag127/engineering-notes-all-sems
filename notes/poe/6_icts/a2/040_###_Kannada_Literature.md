### Kannada Literature

- Kannada literature refers to the corpus of written forms of the Kannada language, particularly those produced roughly between the 10th and 20th centuries.
- The earliest existing examples of written Kannada are from the 5th century CE.
- Kannada literature has a rich heritage Online of influencing various forms of literature over the centuries. Refined forms of poetry evolved with time.
- The oldest known Kannada treaty (from around 450 C.E.) explains the forms, styles, and proportions of Kannada poetry and its composition.

Some important literary forms in Kannada are:

- Epic poetry: Kavya or Mahakavya - (example: Pampa's Vikramarjuna Vijaya ("Victory of Vikrama"))
- Lyric poetry: Vachana Sahitya - (examples: writings of Basavanna, Allama Prabhu)
- Religious literature: Dasa Sahitya (example: Purandaradasa's Kannada compositions)
- Plays: Yakshagana - (example: T. P. Kailasam's plays)
- Novels & short stories: (example: novels of Kuvempu)

A well-known anonymous 5th-century Kannada poem, the Kannada Kadamabaratha, uses the rare and complex champu b meter in the section 'Uttara Ramayana'.

Some key phrases & idioms to remember:
> Jaya chi au – Victory to Kannada
> Naada-geethe – State song (in Kannada)
> Maate indri - mother tongue (Kannada)
> Sa-kala varange - Wishing prosperity to all
> Chi-du chaapa - Excellent! Wonderful!
