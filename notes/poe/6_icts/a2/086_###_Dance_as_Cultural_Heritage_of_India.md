### Dance as Cultural Heritage of India

Indian dance is a cultural heritage of India with a history spanning more than 2000 years. The various classical and folk dance forms of India have been refined over many centuries into highly developed art forms. They represent the cultural values, traditions and customs of the regions from which they originate.

-   Bharatanatyam is a classical dance form originating from Tamil Nadu. It is characterized by fixed upper torso, legs bent, spectacular footwork, and a sophisticated sign language based on gestures of hands, facial expressions, and eye movements.
-   Kathak is a classical dance form from Uttar Pradesh characterized by fast rhythmic footwork and complex choreography. The dancers tell a story through their hand movements and expressive eyes.
-   Odissi has its origin in Odisha. It is characterized by the bent knee pose, unique hip movements, and intricate footwork. The theatrical elements of Odissi are predominant with costume, makeup, and stagecraft being an essential part of the dance.
-   Manipuri originated in Manipur and is characterized by smooth and graceful movements as well as unique costumes and makeup. The costumes are elaborate with a stiff cylindrical skirt.
-   Kuchipudi originated in Andhra Pradesh. It is characterized by poetic expressions and vigorous footwork and rhythmic patterns accompanied by Carnatic music. The dancers take on many different roles or characters in a single performance.

Some Mnemonics to remember the classical dance forms:

Bharatanatyam - Tamil Nadu, fixed upper torso
Kathak - Uttar Pradesh, fast footwork
Odissi - Odisha, bent knee pose
Manipuri - Manipur, smooth and graceful
Kuchipudi - Andhra Pradesh, vigorous footwork

The rich cultural heritage of India is showcased through the graceful mudras (hand gestures), rhythmic footwork, elaborate costumes, and expressive choreography of the diverse dance forms. They are living traditions that continue to evolve over time while retaining their distinct cultural identities.
