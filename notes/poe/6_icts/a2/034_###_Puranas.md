### Puranas

The Puranas are ancient Hindu religious texts eulogizing various deities, primarily the divine Trimurti God in Hinduism through divine stories. They contain legends, mythology, cosmological and genealogical details, stories of battles between good and evil, and detailed descriptions of Hindu cosmology, philosophy, and geography.

The Puranas are classified into Mahapuranas and Upapuranas. The 18 Mahapuranas are of particular importance in Hinduism while the 18 Upapuranas are lesser Puranas.

Mnemonics to remember the 18 Mahapuranas:

May Gopal Always Bless Kings Constantly
(Matsya, Garuda, Agni, Bhagavata, Bhavishya, Brahma, Brahmanda, Brahmavaivarta, Markandeya, Vishnu, Vayu, Varaha, Skanda, Kurma, Padma, Shiva, Linga, Narada)

Learning tips:

- Read the Puranas selectively focusing on topics of interest like mythology, cosmology or philosophy. Do not aim to read the entire texts as they are very extensive.
- Note down important characters, stories, events or concepts as you read for easy recollection.
- Relate the divine stories and legends to practical life lessons and ethics. They contain symbolic deeper meanings apart from the superficial storyline.
- Compare different Puranas to get perspectives on the same deity or legend. This reveals the diverse nature of beliefs even within Hinduism.
- Do not take the magical or supernatural elements literally. Look for the spiritual insights and teachings behind the fantastical descriptions.
