### Hindi Literature

- Hindi literature refers to literary works written in Hindi language, particularly by Northern Indian authors. It includes literature from the earliest Prakrit and Apabhramsha languages poetry of the 11th century to the modern literature.
- Early Hindi literature: The earliest works in Hindi began around the 11th century. During this period several regional dialects like Khari Boli developed into Hindustani. Early Hindi literature has an interesting mix of Sanskrit and Prakrit poems based on themes from Hindu epics and Bhakti poetry. The earliest example is Prithviraj Raso, a composite epic covering the life of Prithviraj Chauhan, written by Chand Bardai in the 12th century.
- Mughal period (1500-1800): The Mughal period saw the establishment of a variant of Hindustani as an official language in India with flourishing court patronage. Prominent poets like Kishan Singh, Bihari and Abdul Rahim Khan-I-Khana wrote in a mix of Persian and vernacular Hindi during this period. Bhakti poetry also flourished, including the works of Mirabai, Surdas and Tulsidas whose Ramcharitmanas has become a landmark of Hindi literature.
- 19th century (1800-1900): The late 19th century saw the influence of Western literary forms, social reforms and the rise of nationalism. Major writers include Mahavir Prasad Dwivedi, Maithili Sharan Gupt and Sundar Lal. Literary genres like novels, short stories and literary criticism developed during this period. Premchand was the most popular Hindi author of the 20th century. His works like Godaan and Nirmala highlight the social issues of rural India.
- Post-independence era (after 1947): After India's independence, Hindi literature absorbed concerns of contemporary India. Influences from the West as well as other Indian languages can be seen in the works of modern Hindi authors like Kamleshwar, Uday Prakash and Krishna Sobti. Apart from the earlier genres, genres like postmodernism, Dalit literature and Little magazine also found an audience. Several Hindi authors have also received international recognition like the Booker Prize or Sahitya Akademi Awards.
