# Engineering and Architecture as Cultural Heritage of India

India has a rich cultural heritage of engineering and architectural marvels which stand testimony to the advanced scientific and technical knowledge of ancient Indians. Some of the notable examples are:

## 1. Konark Sun Temple
- The Konark Sun Temple in Odisha is a 13th century temple dedicated to the sun god.
- It is considered an architectural and engineering feat with its ornate sculpture and intuitive proportions.
- The temple is designed as a giant chariot of the sun god with 12 pairs of elaborately carved stone wheels some of which are 3 meters wide and nearly 1 meter thick.
- The alignment of the temple is such that the first rays of sunrise strikes the principal entrance.
- *Mnemonic*: The Black Pagoda of the Sun - Konark Sun Temple with stone wheels.

## 2. Khajuraho Temples
- The Khajuraho Temples built in Central India between 950 to 1050 CE are famous for their nagara-style architecture and intricate sculptures.
- The temples are built on a raised platform and have a curvilinear tower or shikhara on top.
- The Lakshmana Temple is the best preserved with stunning sculptures depicting various gods and goddesses, celestial beings, and human couples in artistic postures.
- The temples are a testament to the engineering skills in terms of architectural balance, symmetry, and proportions as well as artistic excellence in sculpting.

## 3. Hampi Ruins
- Hampi in Karnataka contains the ruins of the once prosperous Vijayanagara Empire which was founded in the 14th century.
- The ruins are a collection of Dravidian style temples and palaces amidst large boulders and hills. Some of the major attractions are the Virupaksha temple, Vittala temple, and Hampi bazaar.
- The architecture is characterized by decorated pillars, immense courtyards, aquatic structures with channels, large monolithic sculptures, and elaborate frescoes.
- The ruins highlight the advanced engineering skills in temple and palace construction, hydraulics systems, and town planning of the Vijayanagara rulers.
