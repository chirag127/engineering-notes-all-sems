### Indian Cinema as a Performing Art in India

Indian cinema is one of the major performing arts of India. It has a long history and is widely popular in India and abroad.

Some key points about Indian cinema as a performing art:

1. India produces more films annually than Hollywood. Bollywood is the largest film producer in India based in Mumbai. Other major film producing centres are Chennai, Kolkata, Hyderabad, Bangalore, etc.
2. Indian films are known for their musicals, song and dance sequences. The music is an integral part of Indian films. Many films have 6-7 songs woven into the narrative.
3. Indian cinema uses dramatic elements and combines entertainment with cultural values and social issues. The films appeal to emotions and incorporate love stories, action, comedy, etc.
4. Acting in Indian films is also considered an art form. Actors are deeply respected and top actors have a huge fan following. Stars like Amitabh Bachchan, Rajinikanth are cultural icons.
5. Indian cinema has a variety of genres like mythologicals, devotionals, folklore, historicals, socials, etc. The storytelling techniques are heavily influenced by Indian theatre and folk arts.
6. The films are deeply rooted in Indian values and traditions but also reflect the modern transformations of Indian society. They portray cultural diversity by including regional languages, attires, music, etc.
7. Indian cinema has gained increasing global recognition and appreciation. Films by Satyajit Ray, Mrinal Sen, Adoor Gopalakrishnan have won major awards at international film festivals. This has established Indian cinema as an important art form on the global stage.
