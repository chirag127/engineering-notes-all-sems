## Module 3- Indian Religion, Philosophy, and Practices

- Hinduism: Oldest religion of India. Based on ancient Vedic traditions and scriptures like Vedas, Upanishads, Bhagavad Gita, Ramayana, Mahabharata. Core beliefs: Samsara, Karma, Dharma, Brahman, Atman. Main deities: Brahma, Vishnu, Shiva (Trimurti). Important practices: Puja, Yoga, Pilgrimage.
- Jainism: Founded by Mahavira (599 BCE). Based on non-violence, non-attachment, liberation of soul. Core beliefs: Karma, rebirth, asceticism. Main principles: Ahimsa, Aparigraha, Asceticism.
- Buddhism: Founded by Gautama Buddha (623 BCE). Based on Four Noble Truths, Eightfold Path. Core beliefs: Suffering, impermanence, non-self, Nirvana. Main principles: Three universal truths, Four sublime states, Three marks of existence. Important practices: Meditation, following Dharma.
- Sikhism: Founded by Guru Nanak Dev (1469 CE). Based on devotion and remembrance of the name of the creator. Core beliefs: Ek Onkar, Guruship, Congregation, Service, Remembrance of One God. Sacred scripture: Guru Granth Sahib. Important practices: Simran, Sewa, Three Pillars of Sikhism.

Mnemonics:
- Dharma: Righteous duty; Think of it as 'Dharma' for all
- Karma: Deeds; What goes around comes around (cause and effect)
- Atman: Soul; The self within
- Brahma, Vishnu, Shiva: Creator, Preserver, Destroyer; Like a circle of life

Learning tricks:
- Draw diagrams showing key beliefs and principles. Label them. This helps in visual learning and retention.
- Compare and contrast different religions to understand them better. Note down similarities and differences.
- Memorize important dates, scriptures, concepts using flashcards, acronyms, songs, etc.
- Solve previous year questions and discuss doubts to apply learning.
