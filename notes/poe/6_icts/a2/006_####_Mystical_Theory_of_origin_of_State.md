#### Mystical Theory of origin of State

- According to mystical theorists, the origin of the state is divine or supernatural. They believe that God created the state or the rulers derive their authority from God.
- The proponents of this theory claim that the authority of the state is sacred and the subjects should obey the rulers without question as disobedience will be punished by God.
- Examples of this theory can be found in the teachings of philosophers like St. Augustine and St. Thomas Aquinas who believed that the authority of the state comes from God. Similarly, the divine right theory proclaimed that the kings rule by the will of God and their authority is not to be challenged.
- The mystical theory was popular in the medieval period in Europe when religious influence was strong but now this theory has no takers and is rejected by modern political thinkers.

**Mnemonics**: "God chosen governors, Subjects have to follow orders" can be used to remember the key points of mystical theory of origin of state.

**Advantages**: It strengthens the authority of the state and ensures obedience from subjects. It gives a sacred sanction to the acts of the rulers.

**Disadvantages**: It discourages criticism of the acts of the government which can be despotic and tyrannical. It does not consider the will or consent of the people in delegating power to the government.

**Examples and applications**: The rule of monarchs in medieval Europe who claimed to rule by divine right. The enforcement of caste system and other social hierarchies in some societies based on divine commands.
