#### Challenges faced by Women in Society in Ancient India

- **Lack of education**: In ancient India, women did not have access to education. They were not allowed to study the Vedas or other religious texts. As a result, they were deprived of the opportunity to gain knowledge and improve their status.
- **Child marriage**: Child marriage was prevalent in ancient India. Girls were married off at a very young age, sometimes even before puberty. This denied them the chance to live their childhood and make important life decisions for themselves.
- **Sati system**: The oppressive Sati system forced widows to immolate themselves on their husband's pyre. Though voluntary sati was allowed earlier, forced sati became common, resulting in the death of thousands of women. It showed the inferior status of women in society.
- **Deprivation of property rights**: According to the texts like Manusmriti, women did not have the right to inherit property. All their property would be transferred to their male relatives after marriage. This made women dependent on men and they did not have financial independence.
- **Restrictions on freedom**: Women in ancient India faced several restrictions on their freedom. They were not allowed to go out of the house freely or interact with men. Their lives were confined to doing household chores and serving their husbands and in-laws. They did not have autonomy over their own lives.
