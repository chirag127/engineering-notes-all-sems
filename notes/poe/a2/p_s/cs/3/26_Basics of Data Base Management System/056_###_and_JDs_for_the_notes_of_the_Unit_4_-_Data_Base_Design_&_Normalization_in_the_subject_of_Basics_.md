### Data Base Design & Normalization

JD:

1. Data Base Design:
- The process of producing a detailed data model of a database.
- Requires determining the data types, structures and relationships to properly store & retrieve the data.
- Factors like data volume, query complexity & performance requirements are considered.
- Methodologies: Top-down, bottom-up, hybrid approach.
- Logical & Physical Design: Logical focuses on data relationships, Physical focuses on technical aspects.

2. Normalization:
- A process of analysing the given requirements & organizing the data in the database.
- Removes redundancy & inconsistent dependency.
- Makes the data flexible, scalable & consistent.
- 1NF, 2NF, 3NF & BCNF are the normal forms based on which normalization is done.
- Adv: Data consistency, Avoid data duplication, Flexibility, Scalability.
- Dis: may require complex queries & JOINS, may require disk space.
- Examples: Student(RollNo, Name, Course, Section) table can be normalized into Student(RollNo, Name) & Enrollment(RollNo, Course, Section) tables.
