## Unit 1 - Introduction

1. What is Artificial Intelligence?
- AI is the simulation of human intelligence in machines like computers.
- It is a branch of computer science that aims to create intelligent machines that can learn, reason, and carry out tasks like humans.
- The core goals of AI include reasoning, knowledge, planning, learning, natural language processing, and machine perception.

2. Why AI?
- AI has the potential to vastly improve many technologies and solve many real-world problems.
- Some of the application areas of AI include:
    - Automation: Automation of routine tasks like transportation, data processing, etc.
    - Healthcare: Diagnosing diseases, suggesting treatments, robot-assisted surgery, etc.
    - Education: Personalized learning, grading, etc.
    - Agriculture: Monitoring crops, livestock management, etc.
    - Business: Fraud detection, personalized recommendations, etc.

3. Types of AI
- Narrow AI: Specialized in one area like playing chess or solving equations. Examples: Siri, self-driving cars, etc.
- General AI: As intelligent as a human and has general problem-solving skills. Does not exist yet.
- Artificial Narrow Intelligence (ANI): Used for specific tasks. Examples: Siri, self-driving cars, etc.
- Artificial General Intelligence (AGI): Has general intelligence similar to a human mind. Does not exist yet.
