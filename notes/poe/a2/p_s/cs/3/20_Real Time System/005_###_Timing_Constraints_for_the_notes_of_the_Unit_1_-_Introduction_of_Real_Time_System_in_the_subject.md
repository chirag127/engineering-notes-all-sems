### Timing Constraints for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction of Real Time System

1. Hard Real Time System: In hard real time systems, meeting all deadlines is mandatory as missing a deadline can lead to catastrophic consequences. Example: Flight control system, Heart monitoring system.
2. Soft Real Time System: In soft real time systems, missing a deadline occasionally is tolerable but not desirable. Late completion of tasks can degrade performance but will not lead to catastrophic consequences. Example: Video conferencing, Streaming audio.
3. Firm Real Time System: In firm real time systems, tasks should be completed within a specified deadline and missing a deadline is undesirable but tolerable occasionally. Example: Printing a document, Robotic control system.

The key constraints for a real time system are:

1. Deadline: The time limit within which the task should complete its function. It can be hard, firm or soft.
2. Period: The frequency with which a task repeats itself. Shorter the period, higher the real time requirement.
3. Jitter: Variation in the actual deadline of completion from the required deadline. Low jitter is desirable for predictable performance.
4. Latency: The time delay between the occurrence of an event and the start of the appropriate response. Low latency is required for fast reaction to events.
