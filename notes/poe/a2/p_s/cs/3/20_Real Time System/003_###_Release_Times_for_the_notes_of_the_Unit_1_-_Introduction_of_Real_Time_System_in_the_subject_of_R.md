### Release Times for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction of Real Time System in the subject of Real Time System

1. Hard Real Time System:
- In hard real time systems, meeting deadline is mandatory for correct system operation. Missing deadline can lead to catastrophic consequences.
- Examples: Aircraft control system, Nuclear reactor control system, etc.
- Deadline miss in such systems is not tolerable.

2. Firm Real Time System:
- In firm real time systems, occasional deadline misses are tolerable but may degrade system performance.
- Examples: Video conferencing, Telephony systems, etc.
- Some amount of latency is acceptable but excessive delays are not desirable.

3. Soft Real Time System:
- In soft real time systems, missing deadlines sometimes is acceptable and will not cause catastrophic consequences.
- Examples: Multimedia systems, Some robot control systems, etc.
- Deadline flexibility is higher and degradation in performance due to latency is acceptable to some extent.

Advantages of Real Time Systems:
- Predictable and timely response.
- Increased automation and control.
- Increased safety and reliability.

Disadvantages of Real Time Systems:
- Complex to design and implement.
- Difficult to manage resource allocation and scheduling.
- Susceptible to temporal failures.
