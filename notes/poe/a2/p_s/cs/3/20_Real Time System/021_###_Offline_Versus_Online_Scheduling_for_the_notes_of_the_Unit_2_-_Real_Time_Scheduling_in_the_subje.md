**Offline Scheduling:**
- Task graphs and execution times are known a priori.
- Schedule is computed before execution.
- Optimal schedule can be computed.
- Not suitable for dynamic real-time systems where tasks arrive at random times.

**Online Scheduling:**
- Task graphs and execution times not known in advance.
- Schedule is computed at run time as tasks arrive.
- Finding optimal schedule is computationally hard.
- Suitable for dynamic real-time systems with unpredictable task arrivals.

** Differences:**
- Offline - full task set known, online - tasks arrive over time
- Offline - schedule computed in advance, online - schedule computed at run time
- Offline - optimal schedule possible, online - optimality harder to achieve
- Offline - static systems, online - dynamic systems

**Applications:**
- Offline - manufacturing, space missions
- Online - robotic control, process control

**Examples:**
- Earliest Deadline First (EDF)
- Least Laxity First

**Advantages and Disadvantages:**
Offline:
Advantage - Optimal schedule
Disadvantage - Not suitable for dynamic systems

Online:
Advantage - Suitable for dynamic systems
Disadvantage - Hard to achieve optimal schedule
