### Soft Real-Time Communication Systems

- Guarantee that messages will be delivered eventually but within bounded latency/delay.
- Latency/delay bounds can be violated occasionally as long as the violation rate is kept low.
- Example: Video streaming where occasional delayed frames are acceptable.
- Common protocols: Ethernet, UDP, etc.

### Hard Real-Time Communication Systems

- Guarantee that all messages will be delivered within strict latency/delay bounds.
- No message deadline can ever be missed.
- Examples: Control systems, robotics, safety-critical systems, etc.
- Common protocols: CAN, FlexRay, Time-Triggered Ethernet, etc.

Advantages of hard real-time communication systems:
- Predictable and guaranteed delivery timing.
- Low jitter/variation in latency.

Disadvantages:
- Typically more expensive and complex to implement.
- Lower bandwidth and scalability compared to soft real-time systems.

Applications of hard real-time communication systems:
- Automotive systems (braking, steering, engine control, etc.).
- Industrial control systems.
- Medical systems (patient monitoring, life support, etc.).
- Avionics/aerospace systems.
