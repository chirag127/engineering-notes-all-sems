## Unit 1 - Introduction of Real Time System

1. What is a Real Time System?
- A real time system is a system where the correctness of the system depends not only on the logical correctness of the outputs but also on the time at which the outputs are produced.
- In other words, in real time systems, the timeliness of the output is as important as the correctness of the output.
- Examples of real time systems: Aircraft control systems, Industrial control systems, etc.

2. Hard and Soft Real Time Systems
- Hard real time systems: In hard real time systems, it is mandatory that the critical tasks complete on time. If the tasks miss their deadlines, it can lead to catastrophic consequences. For ex: Aircraft control systems.
- Soft real time systems: In soft real time systems, although tasks have deadlines, missing a deadline occasionally does not lead to catastrophic consequences but can degrade the performance of the system. Ex: Multimedia systems.

3. Design Challenges in Real Time Systems
- Guaranteeing deterministic and timely responses.
- Dealing with concurrent processes and avoiding race conditions and deadlocks.
- Dealing with asynchronous external events.
- Limited resources and managing them efficiently.
- Predictability, reliability and fault tolerance.
