### Deadlines for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction of Real Time System in the subject of Real Time System

1. Hard Real-Time Systems: These systems have strict deadlines. If the system misses a deadline, it can lead to system failure or hazardous consequences. For example, in aircraft control systems, missile guidance systems, etc. Missing a deadline is unacceptable in these systems.

2. Soft Real-Time Systems: These systems have flexible or relaxed deadlines. Missing a deadline does not lead to catastrophic consequences but can degrade the system performance or quality of service. For example, video streaming, VoIP applications, etc. Some late tasks can be tolerated in these systems but not all late tasks.

3. Firm Real-Time Systems: These are in between hard and soft real-time systems. Some late tasks can be tolerated to an extent but eventually, they can also lead to system failure. The exact nature of consequences due to missing a deadline is well-defined in these systems. Many embedded systems fall under this category.

The key factors that determine if a system is hard, soft or firm real-time are:

- Consequences of missing a deadline
- Frequency of tasks and deadlines
- Determinism of the system

Advantages of Real-Time Systems:
- Predictable and reliable behavior
- Increased automation
- Increased efficiencies

Disadvantages of Real-Time Systems:
- Complex to design and implement
- Rigid system requirements
- Resource constraints can be challenging to meet
