### Single Layer Feed Forward Network

- It is the simplest type of neural network with just one layer of weights and biases besides the input and output layers.
- It takes input, processes it through activation function and produces the output.
- It can only solve problems with linearly separable patterns.
- It is limited in its capabilities due to the lack of hidden layers.
- Example: Perceptron model

Advantages:
- Simple architecture and easy to implement.
- Requires less training time.

Disadvantages:
- Limited modeling power due to single layer.
- Can only solve linearly separable problems.

Applications:
- Pattern classification problems with linear separation.
- As a building block for deep neural networks.

### Multilayer Feed Forward Network

- Also known as Multilayer Perceptron (MLP)
- Contains one or more hidden layers with weights and activation functions between the layers
- Enables tackling non-linear problems and more complex patterns
- Capable of approximating any function
- Requires more training due to additional parameters
- Example: 2-layer, 3-layer etc. neural networks

Advantages:
- Universal approximators - can model both linear and non-linear relationships.
- Able to learn complex patterns.

Disadvantages:
- More training time required due to additional layers and parameters.
- Prone to overfitting.
- Finding optimal architecture and hyperparameters challenging.

Applications:
- Classification
- Regression
- Time series forecasting
- Pattern recognition
- Computer vision
- Speech recognition
- Machine translation
- And many more!
