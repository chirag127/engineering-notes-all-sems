## Unit 1 - Neural Networks-I (Introduction & Architecture)

### Introduction
- Artificial Neural Networks(ANNs) are a type of machine learning algorithm inspired by the biological structure and functioning of the human brain.
- They are made up of interconnected nodes which operate like neurons firing and connections between those nodes which operate like synapses.
- ANNs receive inputs, process them and then produce outputs.
- They are great at tasks like pattern recognition, classification, regression, etc.

### Architecture
- The basic architecture of an ANN consists of three layers:
	1. Input Layer - Receives the input data and passes it to the next layer.
	2. Hidden Layer(s) - Processes the input and produces the output. Can have multiple hidden layers.
	3. Output Layer - Generates the output/predictions.
- The layers are made up of nodes/neurons that are interconnected.
- Each connection between nodes has a weight associated with it which can be adjusted during training to produce the desired output.
- The strength/activation of a node is determined by firing a weighted sum of its inputs through an activation function.
- The most common types of activation functions are ReLU, Sigmoid and Tanh functions.
