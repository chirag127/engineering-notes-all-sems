### Basic concepts for the notes of the Unit 5 - Genetic Algorithm(GA)

1. Genetic Algorithms(GA) are a part of evolutionary algorithms which are inspired by biological evolution.
2. They are adaptive heuristic search algorithms premised on the evolutionary ideas of natural selection and genetic processes.
3. The basic concepts of GA are:
    - Population: It refers to a set of solutions(called individuals) to an optimization problem.
    - Chromosome: It refers to a potential solution to the optimization problem and is represented by a string of elements(genes).
    - Genes: They are the elements of the chromosome string which encode the solution.
    - Fitness function: It evaluates the fitness/quality of each chromosome and provides a measure of how good it is.
    - Selection: The fitter chromosomes are selected from the population.
    - Crossover: New offspring chromosomes are produced from the selected parents.
    - Mutation: Random changes are made to the offspring chromosomes to introduce diversity.
4. The major steps in a basic GA algorithm are:
    - Generate initial population
    - Evaluate fitness of each chromosome
    - Select fitter chromosomes
    - Perform crossover to produce offspring
    - Perform mutation on offspring
    - Replace least fit population with offspring
    - Repeat steps 3-7 until stopping criteria is met
5. The advantages of GA are:
    - It is capable of handling both binary and real-valued data.
    - It is not dependent on gradient information and can search complex, multimodal landscapes.
    - It is a robust search technique and can produce near-optimal solutions to difficult problems.
6. The disadvantages of GA are:
    - It does not guarantee to find the global optimum.
    - The algorithm is time-consuming for large search spaces.
    - Fine-tuning the algorithm parameters is difficult.
