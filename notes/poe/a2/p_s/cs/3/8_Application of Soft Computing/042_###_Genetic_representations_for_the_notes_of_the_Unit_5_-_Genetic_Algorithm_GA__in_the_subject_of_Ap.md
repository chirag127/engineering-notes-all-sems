### Genetic representations for the notes of the Unit 5 - Genetic Algorithm(GA) in the subject of Application of Soft Computing

1. Chromosome representation:
- The solution to the problem is encoded in the form of a chromosome (string of bits/characters).
- The chromosome represents a candidate solution to the problem.
- The length of the chromosome is determined by the number of parameters required to specify a solution.
- Example: For the Traveling Salesman Problem (TSP), the chromosome can be a permutation of city numbers.

2. Genotype and phenotype representations:
- The internal representation of a solution is called the genotype representation.
- The genotype is mapped to the phenotype, which is the actual solution.
- The genotype representation is used by the GA for reproduction and creation of new offspring.
- The phenotype representation is required to evaluate the fitness of a solution.

3. Binary representation:
- Each parameter is represented using a fixed number of bits.
- The values are encoded using the binary number system.
- Example: If the parameter can take values between 0 and 15, it can be represented using 4 bits. The value 15 would be represented as (1111)2.
- Binary representations are widely used but the main disadvantage is that the strings become very long even for moderatley sized problems.
