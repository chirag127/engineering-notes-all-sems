## Unit 4 - Fuzzy Logic –II (Fuzzy Membership, Rules)

- Fuzzy membership functions: Membership functions are essential components of a fuzzy system. They translate crisp numerical inputs into degrees of membership, or fuzzy sets. The shape of the membership function controls the way crisp inputs get fuzzified. Common membership function shapes include triangular, trapezoidal, Gaussian, and sigmoid. The choice of membership function shape depends on the application and personal preferences. Triangular and trapezoidal shapes are popular because of their simplicity.
- Fuzzy rules: Fuzzy rules are IF-THEN statements that form the basis for the fuzzy inference system. The IF part contains conditions expressed in fuzzy terms, and the THEN part contains consequences expressed in fuzzy terms. The rules are evaluated in parallel, and their outcomes are combined to determine the system output. The maximum degree of fulfillment method is often used for combining multiple fuzzy rules. The resulting output fuzzy set is then defuzzified into a crisp output using various defuzzification methods such as the centroid method, mean of maxima, etc.
- Advantages: The key advantages of fuzzy logic are:
-- It is closer to human thinking and natural language.
-- It can handle vague and imprecise information.
-- It is flexible and can handle nonlinearities and complex relationships easily.
-- It is simpler to implement compared to other techniques.
- Disadvantages: Some of the disadvantages of fuzzy logic are:
-- It may lead to inexact conclusions.
-- It can be difficult to determine appropriate membership functions and fuzzy rules.
-- The system performance is highly dependent on the membership functions and fuzzy rules designed.
-- It can be less efficient compared to other techniques.
- Applications: Fuzzy logic has a wide range of applications in fields such as control systems, expert systems, pattern recognition, data classification, decision making, etc. Some examples are washing machine controllers, automobile cruise controllers, robotic systems, etc.
