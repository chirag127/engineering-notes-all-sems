### NoSQL Databases

- NoSQL databases are non-relational databases that do not require a fixed schema.
- They have a dynamic schema that is flexible and can be changed easily as the data grows.
- The data is stored in many ways - as documents, key-value pairs, graphs, or wide-column stores.
- Some popular NoSQL databases are MongoDB, CouchDB, Cassandra, HBase, etc.
- Advantages: Scalable, Distributed, Open source, Schema-less.
- Disadvantages: Limited query capabilities, Eventual consistency, Limited standardization.
- Applications: Content management systems, e-commerce applications, mobile applications, big data applications.

### INSTANCE-BASED LEARNING for the notes of the Unit 3 - DECISION TREE LEARNING in the subject of Machine Learning Techniques

- In instance-based learning, the training data is stored and the classifier searches through the training data to find the closest match to the test instance and assigns the label of the closest match to the test instance.
- The closest match is determined using a distance metric, eg. Euclidean distance. The training instances that are "close" to the test instance are weighted more heavily.
- The performance of this algorithm depends on the selection of the distance metric and the efficiency of the search process to retrieve closest matches.
- Examples of instance-based learners are nearest neighbor classifiers.
- Advantage: Simple to implement, no explicit training phase required.
- Disadvantage: Requires storage of all training data, inefficient search process when the training data is large.
