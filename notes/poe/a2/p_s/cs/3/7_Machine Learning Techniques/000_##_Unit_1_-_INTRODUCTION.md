## Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION

1. What is Computer Science?

- Computer Science is the scientific and practical approach to computation and its applications.
- It is the systematic study of algorithms and data structures that underlie all computer systems.
- It involves various aspects of computing such as theory of computation, programming languages, system software, architecture, software engineering, artificial intelligence, database systems, parallel and distributed systems, networking, computational biology, etc.
- The core goal of computer science is to develop efficient and reliable software and hardware systems to solve problems.

2. Why study Computer Science?

- Computational thinking: Computer Science teaches you how to think computationally to solve problems efficiently. This trains you to think logically and creatively.
- High demand for jobs: There is a huge demand for skilled computer scientists in areas like data science, machine learning, software engineering, etc. CS graduates have lucrative career opportunities.
- Impact on society: CS has a profound impact on society with applications including transportation, health care, education, environmental systems, and more. As a CS student, you can create technologies to help people.
- Challenging and rewarding: CS can be challenging to learn but solving complex problems and building applications is rewarding. There is a sense of achievement in making something work efficiently.
