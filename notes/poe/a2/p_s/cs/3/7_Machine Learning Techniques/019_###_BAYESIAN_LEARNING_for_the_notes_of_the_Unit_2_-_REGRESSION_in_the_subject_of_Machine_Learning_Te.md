### BAYESIAN LEARNING

- Bayesian Learning is a method of statistical inference in which Bayes' Theorem is used to update the probability estimate for a hypothesis as more evidence or information becomes available.
- In Bayesian Learning, we start with an initial probability distribution over the hypotheses and as we get more data or evidence, we update the probability distribution over the hypotheses using Bayes' Theorem.
- The initial probability distribution over the hypotheses is called the prior probability and the updated probability distribution over the hypotheses is called the posterior probability.
- The major advantage of Bayesian Learning is that it takes into account the uncertainty in the model and the data while making predictions. The more data we get, the more confident we become in the predictions.
- Some disadvantages of Bayesian Learning are:
    - It requires us to specify priors which can be difficult sometimes.
    - Bayesian computations can be intensive for complex models.
- Some examples of Bayesian Learning are:
    - Bayesian linear regression
    - Bayesian logistic regression
    - Naive Bayes classifier
- Bayesian Learning finds applications in various fields like:
    - Medical diagnosis
    - Image processing
    - Natural language processing
    - Robotics
    - Recommendation systems
