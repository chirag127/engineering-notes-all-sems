### Training of network for the notes of the Unit 4 - ARTIFICIAL NEURAL NETWORKS

1. Training a neural network involves adjusting the weights and biases of the network to produce the desired output.
2. The process of adjusting weights and biases is known as adapting or learning.
3. The most common training algorithm for neural networks is called **backpropagation**.
4. In backpropagation, the output of the network is compared with the desired output and an error is calculated. This error is then propagated back through the network, and the weights and biases are adjusted to minimize the error.
5. The strength of the adjustments is controlled by a **learning rate**. If the learning rate is too high, the algorithm may oscillate and become unstable. If the learning rate is too low, the algorithm may converge very slowly.
6. The process of feedforwarding to get outputs and backpropagating errors is repeated over and over again using new training data until the network reaches an acceptable level of performance. This process of repeating the training to reduce the error is known as **gradient descent**.
7. There are three types of errors that can be calculated :
    - Mean Squared Error (MSE)
    - Mean Absolute Error (MAE)
    - Cross Entropy Error
8. The training process can be summarized as follows:
    - Initialize weights and biases
    - Until stopping criteria is met:
        - Feedforward: Calculate outputs from inputs using weights and biases
        - Backpropagate: Calculate error and adjust weights and biases
        - Update weights and biases using gradient descent
