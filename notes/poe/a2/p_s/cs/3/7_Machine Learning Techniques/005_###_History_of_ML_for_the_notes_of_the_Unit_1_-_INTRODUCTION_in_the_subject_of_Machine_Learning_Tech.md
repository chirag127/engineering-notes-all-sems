### History of Machine Learning

- 1950s: The field of Machine Learning was born. Arthur Samuel defined ML as "Field of study that gives computers the ability to learn without being explicitly programmed". He wrote the first self-learning program for playing checkers.
- 1960s: ML research was limited due to limited computer power. The perceptron algorithm was introduced. Neural Networks were firstly proposed but faced problems so their popularity declined.
- 1970s: Development of ML algorithms that could automatically learn how to classify complex patterns. ML research focused on training Neural Networks with multiple layers - making them more powerful than single layer Perceptron.
- 1980s: Introduction of expert systems that contained encoded rules of human expertise. ML research in automatic speech & handwriting recognition, robot control and expert systems.
- 1990s-2000s: Increased data availability and increased computing power enabled the application of ML algorithms to various problems. Introduction of Support Vector Machines, Boosting, and applications like recommender systems.
- 2010-present: Increased interest in Deep Learning methods due to the success of training Deep Neural Networks with many layers. Deep Learning outperformed other Machine Learning approaches in tasks like computer vision, speech recognition, natural language processing leading to their widespread adoption.

Advantages: Automation, personalized experiences, accurate predictions.
Disadvantages: Require huge data, black-box models, risk of biased or unfair decisions.
Applications: Image classification, Speech recognition, Recommendation systems, Robotics, Medical diagnosis, etc.
