### Multiplexing in transport layer

- Multiplexing is the process of combining multiple signals or data streams into one signal over a shared medium.
- In the transport layer of the OSI model, multiplexing is used to allow multiple application-level messages to be transmitted over a single connection between two hosts.
- The sources and destinations of the messages are identified by port numbers. This is known as port-level multiplexing.
- The transport protocols (like TCP and UDP) provide interfaces to the applications to use different port numbers. The protocols then handle combining the messages over a single connection.
- Demultiplexing is the reverse process of separating the messages and directing them to the appropriate applications at the destination.
- Multiplexing increases the efficiency of using the available bandwidth and reduces the overhead of setting up multiple connections.
