### Railway Reservation System

- A railway reservation system is a computerized system used to store and retrieve passenger reservation records and information.
- It is used to reserve seats or berths in advance for passengers travelling in trains. Passengers can book tickets through reservation counters, travel agencies or through online reservation.
- The system maintains a database which stores information about train schedules, availability of seats, passenger details, booking details, etc.
- The system helps in efficient management of reservations and increases transparency in the booking process. It reduces errors and provides accurate information to passengers.
- Major components of a railway reservation system:

1. Train master: Contains data about trains like train number, names, departure and arrival time, etc.
2. Passenger master: Stores passenger details like name, age, contact, etc.
3. Reservation master: Stores booking details like PNR number, booked seats, fare, etc.
4. Availability status: Stores real-time availability of seats for different trains.
5. Reporting: Generates reports on demand like daily booking reports, unavailable seat reports, etc.

- Advantages: Convenience, efficiency, reduced errors and queues, web and mobile access, etc.
- Disadvantages: Initial setup cost, reliance on technology, hacking and fraud risks.
- Applications: Widely used by Indian Railways and railway systems worldwide.
