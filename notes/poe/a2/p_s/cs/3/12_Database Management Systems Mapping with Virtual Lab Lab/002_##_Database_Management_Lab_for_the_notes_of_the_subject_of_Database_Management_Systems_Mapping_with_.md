## Database Management Lab for the notes of the subject of Database Management Systems Mapping with Virtual Lab Lab

1. Introduction to Database Management Systems (DBMS)
- A DBMS is a software system that uses a standard method to store and organize data. It allows users to define, create, update, and manage a database.
- The main advantages of a DBMS are:
- Data Integrity - Ensures accuracy and consistency of data.
- Data Security - Prevents unauthorized access to data.
- Data Abstraction - Hides complexity of data structure and query languages from users.
- Data Independence - Shields users from changes in data structure.
- Concurrency Control - Allows multiple users to access the database simultaneously.
- Backup and Recovery - Provides mechanisms for recovery from system crashes and errors.

2. ER Modeling
- The Entity-Relationship (ER) model is a conceptual data model that views the real world as entities and relationships between entities.
- Entities are objects or concepts that exist independently of any attribute or relationship. They are represented by rectangles.
- Attributes are properties or characteristics of an entity that provide additional details about the entity. They are represented by ovals connected to entities.
- Relationships show how entities are connected and are represented by diamonds.
- The three main types of relationships are one-to-one, one-to-many, and many-to-many.
