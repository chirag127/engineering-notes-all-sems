## Statechart and Activity Modeling

Statechart and Activity modeling are behavioral modeling techniques used in Software Engineering to model the dynamic aspect of a system.

### Statechart Modeling

- Statechart is a graphical representation of states and transitions between states of a system.
- It shows the sequence of states that an object goes through during its lifetime in response to events, either internal or external.
- It consists of states, transitions between states, and events that trigger the transitions.
- States can be simple states, composite states which contain nested states, or pseudostates like initial state or final state.
- Transitions show the change of state and are labeled with events that trigger the transition.
- Statechart diagrams are useful to model reactive systems and depict the dynamic view of a system.
- They demonstrate the behavior and how the system responds to and interacts with its environment.
- Example - Statechart for a turnstile system showing states like locked, unlocked, coin_inserted, passage, etc. and transitions between them on events like insert_coin, push_turnstile, exit, etc.

Advantages -
- Model complex logic and sequencing of states
- Show parallel states and nesting of states
- Useful for reactive systems and event-driven systems

Disadvantages -
- Can become complex for large systems with many states and transitions
- Difficult to understand and follow the flow for complex statecharts

Applications - Reactive systems, Embedded systems, Event-driven systems, etc.
