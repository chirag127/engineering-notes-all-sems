## Unit 2 - Basic Structural Modeling

1. Structural Analysis - It is a method to determine the effects of loads on physical structures and their components. It is done to analyze stress, forces, deformation, and stability in the structures. The main types of structural analysis are:
- Static Structural Analysis - It analyzes structures under stationary loads. It is further classified into linear static analysis and nonlinear static analysis.
- Dynamic Structural Analysis - It analyzes the time-dependent behavior of structures under variable loads. It includes response spectrum analysis and time history analysis.

2. Modeling Approaches - There are two main approaches to model structures:
- Finite Element Method (FEM) - It models the structure as an assembly of small interconnected elements/components. It can model the complexity and details of the structures. It is more accurate but computationally expensive.
- Equivalent Frame Method - It models the structure as a combination of beam and column elements. It simplifies the complex structures into a equivalent skeletal frame. It is less accurate but computationally efficient for preliminary analysis.

3. Element Types - The basic structural elements commonly used in modeling are:
- Beams - They resist transverse loads and bending moments. They can be modeled as line elements.
- Columns - They resist axial loads/forces. They can be modeled as line elements.
- Plate/Shell - They resist in-plane forces. They are modeled as area elements.
- Solid - They resist volumetric forces. They are three-dimensional elements.
