# Natural Language Processing

## Introduction
- Natural Language Processing (NLP) is a branch of artificial intelligence that deals with the interaction between computers and humans using the natural language.
- NLP is a way for computers to analyze, understand and derive meaning from human language in a smart and useful way.
- The ultimate goal of NLP is to enable simple and natural communication between humans and computers.

## Tasks of NLP
- The core tasks of NLP are:

1. Text classification - Classifying text into predefined categories (e.g. spam/not-spam, positive/negative reviews)
2. Named entity recognition - Finding and classifying named entities in text into pre-defined categories (people names, organizations, locations, etc.)
3. Part-of-speech tagging - Tagging words in a sentence with their part-of-speech (noun, verb, adjective, etc.)
4. Dependency parsing - Analyzing relationships between words in a sentence
5. Sentiment analysis - Determining the sentiment (positive, negative, neutral) of a text
6. Summarization - Producing a concise summary of a longer piece of text
7. Question answering - Answering questions posed by humans in a natural language
8. Machine translation - Translating text from one human language to another

## Applications of NLP
- Some of the applications of NLP are:

1. Sentiment analysis - Analyzing sentiments on social media and reviews to understand people's opinions
2. Spam detection - Detecting and filtering spam emails and messages
3. Text classification - Classifying news articles, emails, etc. into categories
4. Speech recognition - Recognizing and converting speech to text
5. Machine translation - Translating text from one language to another
6. Question answering systems - Building systems that can answer questions
7. Text summarization - Summarizing long articles and texts
8. Named entity recognition - Recognizing names of people, organizations, locations, etc. in text
