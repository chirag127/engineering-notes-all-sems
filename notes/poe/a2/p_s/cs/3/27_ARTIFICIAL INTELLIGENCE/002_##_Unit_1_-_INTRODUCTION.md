## Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION

1. What is Artificial Intelligence?

- Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the field of computer science that attempts to build machines that can think and act intelligently, similar to a human.
- AI makes it possible for machines to learn from experience, adjust to new inputs and perform human-like tasks.
- The ultimate goal of AI is to create general artificial intelligence - a machine that can perform any intellectual task that a human can. However, we are not there yet. Current AI focuses on specialized systems that perform specific, limited tasks (like playing chess or identifying images).

2. Why is AI important?

- AI has a wide range of applications including:
- Automation of tasks: AI can take over repetitive and mundane tasks currently performed by humans and automate them. This can reduce costs and increase efficiency.
- Healthcare: AI can help analyze medical scans, detect diseases, suggest and personalize treatment plans, etc. This can improve accuracy and outcomes.
- Education: AI can grade assignments instantaneously, provide personalized feedback and adaptive learning experiences for each student, etc. This can make learning more engaging and effective.
- Transportation: AI powers self-driving vehicles, intelligent routing systems, computer vision for autonomous systems, etc. This can reduce accidents and congestion.
- Personal Assistance: AI powers virtual assistants and smart home devices that can understand speech and text and assist with various everyday tasks. This enables convenient automation of tasks.
