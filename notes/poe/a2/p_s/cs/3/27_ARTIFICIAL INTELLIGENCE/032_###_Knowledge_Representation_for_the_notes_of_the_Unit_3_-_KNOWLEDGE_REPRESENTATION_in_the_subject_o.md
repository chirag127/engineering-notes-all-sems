# Knowledge Representation

## Points:
1. Knowledge Representation refers to the ways/methods used to represent knowledge. It involves representing knowledge in a manner suitable for computations/processing by machines such as computers.
2. The key purposes of knowledge representation are:
- To enable knowledge/information sharing and reuse among AI systems and applications.
- To serve as a basis for automated reasoning and problem solving.
- To provide a common vocabulary and language for defining AI problems.
3. Some of the key Knowledge Representation techniques are:
- Logic-based methods: Propositional/first-order logic
- Frames: Frames are data structures to represent concepts and relationships. They consist of slots which take on values.
- Rules: IF-THEN rules for reasoning based on conditions and actions.
- Semantic Networks: Graphical structures/networks showing concepts and relationships.
- Conceptual Graphs: Graph-based method to represent knowledge consisting of nodes and links.
- Ontologies: Formal specifications/vocabularies of shared conceptualizations.
