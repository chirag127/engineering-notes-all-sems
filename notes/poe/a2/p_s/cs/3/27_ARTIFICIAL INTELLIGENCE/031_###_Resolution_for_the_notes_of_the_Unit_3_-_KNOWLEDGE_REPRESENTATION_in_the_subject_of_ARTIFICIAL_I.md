### Resolution for the notes of the Unit 3 - KNOWLEDGE REPRESENTATION in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

1. Knowledge Representation:
- Knowledge Representation is a way of representing knowledge in a formal manner that can be used to reason about the knowledge.
- It is about encoding concepts and relationships about the world into a format that a computer can use to solve complex tasks.
- The key goal of Knowledge Representation is to represent knowledge in a computer-interpretable form.

2. Why is Knowledge Representation required?
- To enable Intelligent Systems to use the knowledge. Without proper representation of knowledge, the systems cannot understand and reason about the knowledge.
- To facilitate Sharing and Reuse of knowledge. With common formats of representation, knowledge can be easily shared across systems and reused.
- To make incomplete and uncertain knowledge usable. Imperfect or uncertain knowledge can be represented using suitable formats so that systems can draw inferences even from such knowledge.

3. Requirements of a good Knowledge Representation:
- Expressiveness: Should be capable of expressing a wide variety of knowledge.
- Clarity: The representation should be clear and unambiguous.
- Knowledge Content Independence: The way of representing knowledge should be independent of the content of the knowledge.
- Extensibility: It should be easily extensible to accommodate more knowledge.
- Computational Efficiency: The representation should allow efficient computational processing.
- Usability: It should be easy for humans to use and understand the knowledge in the represented format.
