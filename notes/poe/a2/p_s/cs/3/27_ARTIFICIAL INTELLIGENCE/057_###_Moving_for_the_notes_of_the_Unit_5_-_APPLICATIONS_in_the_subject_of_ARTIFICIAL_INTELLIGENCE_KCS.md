1. Expert Systems - Systems that contain stored knowledge and inferential machinery for applying the knowledge to solve specific problems. They are designed to perform at the level of human experts in a given domain.
- Components: Knowledge base, Inference engine, User interface.
- Examples: Medical diagnosis, Technical troubleshooting, Financial analysis.
- Advantages: Provide expert advice rapidly, Available 24/7, Consistency.
- Disadvantages: Knowledge acquisition bottleneck, Lack of common sense, Narrow domains.

2. Robotics - Design and construction of robots that can perform tasks automatically. Robots are used to perform physical tasks with a high degree of accuracy and repeatability.
- Components: Sensors, Manipulators, Power supply, Computer.
- Examples: Assembly line robots, Surgical robots, Space probes.
- Advantages: Take over dangerous and repetitive tasks, Increase productivity and economic growth.
- Disadvantages: Initial cost, Limitations in agility and cognition.

3. Machine Learning - The scientific study of algorithms and techniques that can learn and improve from experience without being explicitly programmed. Machine learning powers many AI applications including speech and image recognition, recommendation systems, robotics, etc.
- Types: Supervised learning, Unsupervised learning, Reinforcement learning.
- Examples: Image classification, Sentiment analysis, Self-driving cars.
- Advantages: Automate complex tasks, Adapt to dynamic environments,Discover hidden patterns.
- Disadvantages: Require large amounts of data, Black box models, Lack of explainability.
