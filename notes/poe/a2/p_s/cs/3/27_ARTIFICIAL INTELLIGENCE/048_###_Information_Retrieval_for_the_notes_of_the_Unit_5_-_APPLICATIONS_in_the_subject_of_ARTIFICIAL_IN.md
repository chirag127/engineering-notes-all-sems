## Information Retrieval

- Information Retrieval (IR) is the process of obtaining relevant documents or data from a collection of documents for a user's query.
- The core functionality of an IR system is to retrieve information relevant to the user's query from a large repository of information.
- The key steps in an IR system are:

1. Indexing - In this step, each document in the collection is analyzed and indexed. The index contains keywords found in the document and pointers to the original documents.
2. Query processing - The user provides a query which is processed to retrieve relevant keywords and concepts.
3. Retrieval - The index is searched to obtain documents that are relevant to the query keywords and concepts.
4. Ranking - The retrieved documents are ranked in order of their relevance to the query. The most relevant documents are returned to the user.

- Some applications of IR are:

- Web search engines - To retrieve relevant web pages for user queries.
- Enterprise search - To enable employees to search through documents and data within an organization.
- Recommendation systems - To recommend products or content to users based on their past interactions and similarities with other users.
- Question Answering systems - To provide direct answers to user questions rather than a list of documents.

- Key Evaluation Metrics:

- Precision - Fraction of retrieved documents that are relevant. Higher precision is better.
- Recall - Fraction of relevant documents that are retrieved. Higher recall is better.
- F1-score - Harmonic mean of precision and recall. Higher F1-score is better.

- Some Pros and Cons:

Pros:
- IR enables users to access required information easily from large data collections.
- IR has a wide range of applications including web search and recommendations.

Cons:
- It is difficult to obtain high precision and high recall simultaneously. Improving one metric can lower the other.
- The outputs may contain irrelevant or duplicate information.
- The system performance depends on the quality of indexing and relevance metrics which can be challenging to optimize.
