1. Introduction
- Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the field of computer science that attempts to build machines that can think, learn, and act in an intelligent manner.
- AI systems are powered by algorithms that allow machines to learn from data, recognize patterns, adapt and make decisions with minimal human input.
- AI has a wide range of applications including expert systems, speech recognition, and machine vision.
- The primary goal of AI is to create computer systems and machines that are capable of intelligent behavior.

2. Search Methods
- Search methods are algorithms that systematically explore the solution space to determine the optimal path to the goal.
- The two most popular search methods are:
    - Breadth-First Search (BFS): Expands shallowest unexpanded node. Finds least cost path.
    - Depth-First Search (DFS): Expands deepest unexpanded node. Gets trapped in infinite loops.
- Examples: Maze solving, Chess, Constraint Satisfaction Problems.

3. Optimization Problems
- Optimization is finding the best solution among a number of possible alternatives.
- The two most common types of optimization methods are:
    - Local Search: Starts with an initial solution and iteratively moves to neighboring solutions that improve the objective.
    - Population-Based Search: Maintains a population of solutions and iteratively improves them using genetic operators like crossover and mutation.
- Examples: Travelling Salesman Problem (TSP), Vehicle Routing Problem (VRP), Job-Shop Scheduling.
