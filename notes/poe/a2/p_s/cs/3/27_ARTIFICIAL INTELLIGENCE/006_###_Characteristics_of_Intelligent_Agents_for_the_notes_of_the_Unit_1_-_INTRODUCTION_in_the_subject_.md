### Characteristics of Intelligent Agents

1. sensing: Ability to sense the external environment. The agents perceive the input from the environment through sensors.
2. autonomy: Ability to operate without direct human intervention. The agents perform tasks on their own.
3. goal-oriented: The agents work towards achieving a goal. They make decisions and perform actions to satisfy a goal.
4. adaptability: Ability to adapt to dynamic environments. The agents can vary their actions based on changes in the environment to achieve the goals.
5. learning: Ability to learn from experience. The agents can improve their performance over time based on the feedback from the environment.

The intelligent agents have the above characteristics to perceive the environment, achieve goals, adapt to changes and learn over time with limited or no human intervention. The amount of intelligence exhibited by an agent depends on how efficiently it can sense the environment, be autonomous, be goal-oriented, adaptable and capable of learning.
