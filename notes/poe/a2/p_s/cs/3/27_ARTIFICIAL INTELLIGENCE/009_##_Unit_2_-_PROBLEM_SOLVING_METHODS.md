## Unit 2 - PROBLEM SOLVING METHODS

Problem solving methods are systematic approaches to solve problems. Some of the popular methods are:

1. Trial and Error Method:
- It involves attempting various solutions and checking which works
- Advantage: Simple and guaranteed to find a solution
- Disadvantage: Time consuming and inefficient
- Example: Troubleshooting a malfunctioning device by trying different solutions one by one

2. Guess and Check Method:
- Making an intelligent guess and checking if it is correct
- Advantage: May lead to quick solutions for simple problems
- Disadvantage: May not be feasible for complex problems and multiple iterations may be required
- Example: Guessing input values to get desired output in a code or system

3. Brute Force Method:
- Trying all possible combinations and options to find a solution
- Advantage: Guaranteed to find an optimum solution
- Disadvantage: Very time and resource consuming making it impractical for most real world problems
- Example: Cracking a password by trying all combinations of letters, numbers and symbols
