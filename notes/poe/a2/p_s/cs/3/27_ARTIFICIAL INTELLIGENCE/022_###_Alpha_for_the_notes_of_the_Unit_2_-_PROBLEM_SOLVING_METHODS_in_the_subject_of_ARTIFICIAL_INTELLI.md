### Alpha
Problem Solving Methods refer to the approaches or techniques used to solve a problem. The various problem solving methods in AI are:

1. Brute Force Method: This method involves generating all possible solutions and checking which one satisfies the given problem. It is straightforward but time and resource consuming for complex problems.
Eg: Finding shortest path by trying out every possible path.

2. Hill Climbing: It starts with an initial solution and iteratively moves to better solutions. It stops at a local optimum.
Eg: Travelling salesman problem where nearby cities are visited first.

3. Beam Search: It is a variant of hill climbing which considers multiple candidate solutions at each step instead of a single solution. The candidates with highest evaluation functions are retained.
Eg: Game playing algorithms where next moves are evaluated and best options are explored.

4. Genetic Algorithms: They are inspired by natural selection and evolution. A population of solutions is iteratively improved using processes like selection, crossover and mutation.
Eg: Finding optimal weights in a neural network, optimizing schedules, etc.

5. Simulated Annealing: It is based on metallurgical process of annealing. A random change is made to the current solution and the new solution is accepted based on a probability which depends on the difference in evaluation functions and a temperature parameter. The temperature is decreased over time to reach global optimum.
Eg: Travelling salesman problem, scheduling problems, etc.
