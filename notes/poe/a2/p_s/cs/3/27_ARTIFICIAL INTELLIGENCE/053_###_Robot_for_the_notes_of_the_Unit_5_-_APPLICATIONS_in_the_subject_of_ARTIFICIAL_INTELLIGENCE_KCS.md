#### What is a Robot?

A Robot is a machine that can perform a complex series of actions automatically. Robots are widely used in many industries today to perform tasks that are dangerous for humans, repetitive and mundane, or require a high degree of precision and accuracy.

- Robots are made up of mechanical and electrical components which are programmed to carry out specific tasks.
- They are guided by computers and artificial intelligence systems that allow them to navigate their environment, manipulate objects, and even learn from experience.
- Robots are used extensively in manufacturing to perform tasks such as pick and place objects, packaging, processing, assembling objects on assembly lines, etc. This improves efficiency and reduces costs for companies.
- Robots are also used for tasks such as space exploration, bomb disposal, handling hazardous materials, surgery, rehabilitation therapy, etc.
- Some examples of robots include industrial robot arms, self-driving cars, unmanned aerial vehicles or drones, robotic vacuum cleaners, etc.

**Advantages of Robots:**
- Increased productivity and efficiency. Robots can work continuously without fatigue.
- Performs dangerous and repetitive jobs thereby reducing risk to human life and injury.
- Provides high precision and quality in manufacturing and other tasks.
- Reduces costs in the long run.

**Disadvantages of Robots:**
- Initial costs can be high to install and program robots.
- May reduce employment opportunities for humans and increase unemployment.
- May lack flexibility to adapt to change and perform multiple varied tasks.
- May malfunction or behave unexpectedly leading to errors, reduced quality or even accidents. This needs to be carefully monitored.
