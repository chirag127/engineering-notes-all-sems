**Expert Systems:**
- Expert systems are AI applications that incorporate the knowledge and inferencing capabilities of human experts to solve complex problems in a specific domain.
- They contain a knowledge base which stores domain-specific information and an inference engine which derives solutions to problems or diagnoses situations.
- The knowledge is represented in the form of if-then rules and the inference engine matches the rules to the known facts to deduce new conclusions.
- Examples: Medical diagnosis, Technical troubleshooting, Financial analysis, etc.

**Robotics:**
- Robotics is the design and construction of robots that can perform a variety of physical tasks with a high degree of autonomy and precision.
- Robots are programmed to perform tasks like manipulation, locomotion, perception, etc with mechanical, electrical and software components.
- Examples: Industrial robots for manufacturing, Surgical robots, Space exploration robots, Self-driving cars, etc.

**Machine Learning:**
- Machine Learning is a field of AI that uses statistical techniques to give computer systems the ability to "learn" with data, without being explicitly programmed.
- ML algorithms build a mathematical model based on sample data, known as "training data", in order to make predictions or decisions without being explicitly programmed to perform the task.
- Examples: Image classification, Speech recognition, Sentiment analysis, Recommendation systems, etc.
