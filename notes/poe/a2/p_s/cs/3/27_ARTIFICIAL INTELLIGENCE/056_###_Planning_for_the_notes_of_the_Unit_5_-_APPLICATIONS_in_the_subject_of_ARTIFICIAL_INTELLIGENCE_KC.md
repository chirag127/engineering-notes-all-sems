### Planning for the notes of the Unit 5 - APPLICATIONS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

1. Expert Systems -
- Built on knowledge base and Inference Engine
- Uses IF-THEN rules to make decisions and provide solutions to problems
- Examples - Medical diagnosis, Technical troubleshooting, Advisory systems
- Advantages - Provides solutions to complex problems, Capture expertise of human specialists
- Disadvantages - Difficult to build, Domain dependent, Require frequent updates, Cannot handle new situations well

2. Robotics -
- Electro-mechanical devices which can perform tasks automatically
- Use AI for navigation, object manipulation, vision, planning, learning
- Examples - Industrial robots, Space explorations, Self-driving cars
- Advantages - Automate tedious and dangerous tasks, Increase efficiency and production
- Disadvantages - Initial cost, Job risk for human labour, Lack of awareness about human presence

3. Natural Language Processing -
- Interaction between computers and humans using the natural language
- Uses AI concepts like classification, vector representations, clustering, summarization
- Examples - Chatbots, Spam detection, Sentiment analysis, Machine translation
- Advantages - Easy to use, Wide applications, Increased accessibility
- Disadvantages - Complex to implement, Context dependent, Require large datasets to train
