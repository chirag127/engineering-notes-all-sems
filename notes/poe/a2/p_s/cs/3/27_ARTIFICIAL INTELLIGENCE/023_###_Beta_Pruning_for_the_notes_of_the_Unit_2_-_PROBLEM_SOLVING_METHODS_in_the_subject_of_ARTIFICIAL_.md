## Beta Pruning

- Beta Pruning is a technique used in decision trees to avoid overfitting.
- It works by specifying a threshold beta value. If the benefit of splitting a node is less than the beta value, then the node is not split further.
- The beta value is chosen using cross-validation to get the best performance.
- Higher beta values lead to underfitting while lower beta values lead to overfitting.
- Choosing an optimum beta value is crucial for getting the best performance.
- Advantages:
    - Prevents overfitting and leads to simpler trees.
    - Faster training due to reduced tree size.
- Disadvantages:
    - Difficult to choose the best beta value.
    - May lead to underfitting if beta value is too high.
- Applications: Decision tree models like CART, C4.5, etc. use beta pruning to control tree size and avoid overfitting.
