### Software Quality Frameworks

Software quality frameworks provide a set of guidelines and quality standards to develop high quality software. Some of the popular software quality frameworks are:

- ISO/IEC 9126: This framework defines 6 characteristics of software quality - functionality, reliability, usability, efficiency, maintainability and portability. It provides metrics and models to evaluate the quality of software.
- McCall's Quality Model: It defines 3 levels of quality - primary, secondary and tertiary. Primary quality includes correctness, reliability and efficiency. Secondary quality includes maintainability, flexibility and testability. Tertiary quality includes functionality, usability, compatibility etc.
- Boehm's Quality Model: It relates quality to cost and risk. It proposes that higher quality software can be built by managing risk and controlling rework. It defines quality in terms of attributes - correctness, understandability, testability, modularity etc.

Advantages of using software quality frameworks:

- Provide standardized approach to evaluate and ensure software quality.
- Help identify key quality attributes and metrics to focus on.
- Aid in planning testing and risk management activities.
- Facilitate communication between stakeholders using common terminology.

Disadvantages:

- Can be difficult to implement due to large number of metrics and models.
- Quality is multi-dimensional and frameworks do not capture all aspects.
- Subjective nature of some metrics can lead to inaccurate evaluations.
