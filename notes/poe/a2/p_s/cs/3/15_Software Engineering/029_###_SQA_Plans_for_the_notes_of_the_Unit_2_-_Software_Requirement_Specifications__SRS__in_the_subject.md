### SQA Plans for Software Requirement Specifications (SRS)

- SQA (Software Quality Assurance) team will review the SRS document for correctness, consistency, and completeness. The key points to check in SRS are:
- All requirements are testable. Verifiable requirements are included.
- Requirements are consistent, not conflicting with each other.
- Requirements are unambiguous, clear, and understandable.
- All functions, interfaces, and performance criteria are specified.
- External interfaces are defined.
- Resource requirements and constraints are specified.
- Safety, security, and regulatory requirements are specified (if any).
- The SRS is kept updated if any changes are made to the requirements.
- The SRS is reviewed and baselined before design and coding starts.

The advantages of SQA review of SRS are:
- Faulty, inconsistent, and ambiguous requirements can be identified early.
- Missing requirements can be uncovered.
- Resources and time required for rework can be minimized.
- The overall quality of the software product can be enhanced.

The SQA team can use checklists, issue tracking tools, and other techniques for reviewing the SRS document thoroughly. The review comments and issues found need to be tracked and addressed before completing the SRS. The SRS review is critical to building the right product that meets the customer's needs.
