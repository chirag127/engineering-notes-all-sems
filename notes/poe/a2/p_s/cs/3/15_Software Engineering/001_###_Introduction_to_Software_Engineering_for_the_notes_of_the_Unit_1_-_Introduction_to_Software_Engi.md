### Introduction to Software Engineering

- Software Engineering is an engineering discipline which is concerned with all aspects of software production.
- It applies engineering principles to software development.
- It involves a systematic approach to the development, operation, maintenance, and retirement of computer software.
- The goals of software engineering are to produce software that is high quality, defect free, meets requirements, and is delivered on time and within budget.

**Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC):**
- The SDLC consists of the following phases:
	- Requirements Analysis: Gathering and analyzing system requirements.
	- Design: Coming up with a design that satisfies the requirements.
	- Implementation: Implementing the design in a programming language.
	- Testing: Testing the software to identify and fix defects.
	- Deployment: Deploying the software for user use.
	- Maintenance: Making modifications to fix bugs or enhance the software.

**Software Quality:**
- The degree to which software possesses desirable characteristics such as correctness, reliability, and ease of understanding.
- Includes factors such as correctness, reliability, efficiency, portability, maintainability, testability, usability, etc.
- Can be achieved through review, walkthroughs, iteration, and proper software engineering techniques.
