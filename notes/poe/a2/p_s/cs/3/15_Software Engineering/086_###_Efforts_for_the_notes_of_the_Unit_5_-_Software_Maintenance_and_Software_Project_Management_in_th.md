### Efforts for the notes of the Unit 5 - Software Maintenance and Software Project Management in the subject of Software Engineering

1. Software Maintenance
- It refers to the modification of a software product after delivery to correct faults, to improve performance or other attributes, or to adapt the product to a changed environment.
- Types: Corrective, Adaptive, Perfective
- Challenges: Limited understanding of existing system, unpredictable changes, resource constraints
- Best practices: Use documentation, Automate testing, Focus on most used features first

2. Software Project Management
- It is the art and science of planning and leading software projects.
- Project proposal: Feasibility study, Requirements analysis, Project plan
- Project planning: Estimation, Scheduling, Resource allocation
- Project tracking: Metrics, Monitoring, Control
- Project termination: Final report, Post-implementation review

Advantages: Help finish projects on time and budget, Increased visibility and control, Handle risks effectively
Disadvantages: Overhead cost, Inflexible, Not suitable for small projects
