### Feasibility Study for the notes of the Unit 2 - Software Requirement Specifications (SRS) in the subject of Software Engineering

1. Introduction
- A feasibility study is an assessment of the practicality of a proposed project or system.
- It is an evaluation and analysis of the potential of a proposed project which is focused on determining if it can be completed successfully.
- The primary objective of a feasibility study is to determine if the system under consideration is both practical and viable in terms of engineering and cost.

2. Types of Feasibility Study
- Technical Feasibility - evaluates if the system can be developed given the current technology and resources.
- Economic Feasibility - evaluates if the system is profitable and the costs do not outweigh the benefits.
- Operational Feasibility - evaluates if the system can be integrated into the organization and if it fulfills the user requirements.
- Schedule Feasibility - evaluates if the system can be developed and implemented within an acceptable time frame.

3. Steps in a Feasibility Study
1. Problem Definition - clearly define the problem and objectives of the proposed system.
2. Alternate Solutions - identify and evaluate various solutions to the problem.
3. Evaluate Criteria - determine evaluation criteria for judging alternatives such as technical, economic and operational feasibility.
4. Evaluate Alternatives - evaluate each alternative against the criteria and determine the pros and cons of each.
5. Recommend Solution - recommend the best alternative based on the evaluations and criteria.
6. Implementation Plan - develop a plan to implement the recommended solution.
