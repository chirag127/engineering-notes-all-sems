### ISO 9000 Models
- ISO 9000 is a family of quality management systems standards. It consists of standards and guidelines for quality management systems and is maintained by the International Organization for Standardization (ISO).
- The ISO 9000 family includes the following models:
- ISO 9001:2015 - requirements for quality management systems
- ISO 9004:2009 - guidelines for performance improvement of organizations
- ISO 19011:2011 - guidelines for auditing management systems
- Benefits of ISO 9000:
- Increased customer satisfaction through consistent quality
- Less waste and increased productivity
- Improved market image and credibility
- Better management and control of processes
- Common vocabulary and fewer misunderstandings
- Disadvantages of ISO 9000:
- Can be bureaucratic and encourage "paper compliance"
- Focuses on documentation more than actual improvement
- Difficult and time-consuming to implement and maintain
- May discourage innovation by insisting on consistency
- Applications of ISO 9000:
- Widely used for software and systems development processes
- Used for compliance with regulatory requirements
- Used as a starting point and then tailored as needed
- Examples of ISO 9000 implementations:
- Motorola's Six Sigma program derived from ISO 9000
- NASA's Software Assurance Standard derived from ISO 9000
