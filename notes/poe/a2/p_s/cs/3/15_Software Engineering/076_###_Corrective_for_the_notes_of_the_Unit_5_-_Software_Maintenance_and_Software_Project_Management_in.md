### Corrective for the notes of the Unit 5 - Software Maintenance and Software Project Management in the subject of Software Engineering

1. Software Maintenance
- Software maintenance is the modification of a software product after delivery to correct faults, to improve performance or other attributes, or to adapt the product to a modified environment.
- The major types of software maintenance are:
    - Corrective maintenance - fixing errors or bugs.
    - Adaptive maintenance - adapting the software to changes in the environment.
    - Perfective maintenance - improving the software.
    - Preventive maintenance - preventing errors or bugs.
- The maintenance process involves analyzing change requests, estimating costs and time required, and prioritizing, scheduling, and assigning maintenance tasks.
- Challenges with software maintenance:
    - Lack of documentation - Hard to understand the existing system and make changes.
    - Staff turnover - Loss of knowledge as original developers leave.
    - Complex software - Hard to understand and modify complex software systems.
    - Tight schedules - Little time available to perform proper testing and debugging.

2. Software Project Management
- Software project management involves planning, organizing, and controlling the development of an information system.
- The major software project management activities are:
    - Planning - Developing a software project plan which includes estimates of resource requirements and timelines.
    - Organizing - Coordinating human and physical resources and integrating their efforts.
    - Controlling - Monitoring progress and taking corrective action as needed.
- Important attributes of a software project plan include:
    - Scope - specifying what will and will not be included in the software product.
    - Schedule - specifying timing and sequence of events and activities.
    - Budget - specifying resource limits and estimating costs.
    - Quality - specifying appropriate levels of quality and evaluating the final product.
    - Risks - identifying and planning responses to potential problems.
- Tools for software project management include:
    - Gantt charts - for scheduling and monitoring project progress.
    - PERT charts - for analyzing uncertainty in scheduling.
    - Critical path method - for determining the minimum project duration and scheduling resources.
