1. Software Maintenance
- Definition: Modification of a software product after delivery to correct faults, to improve performance or other attributes, or to adapt the product to a modified environment.
- Types: Corrective, Adaptive, Perfective
- Problems: Limited documentation, staff turnover, inadequate testing
- Estimation techniques: Use of metrics like fault densities and fix times
- Management: Configuration management, impact analysis, automated tools

2. Software Project Management
- Definition: Planning and controlling the process of developing an information system.
- Objectives: Finish the project on time and on budget while meeting specifications.
- Activities: Estimation, risk analysis, scheduling, monitoring and control.
- Project life cycle: Initiation, planning, execution, monitoring and termination.
- Estimation techniques: Expert judgment, parametric models, bottom-up approaches.
- Scheduling techniques: PERT, CPM, Gantt charts.
- Risk management: Risk identification, risk analysis, risk response planning.
- Leadership, teamwork and communication are key project management skills.
