### Nature of Data

The nature of data in Big Data can be characterized in 3 V's:

1. Volume - The amount of data is huge. It is in terrabytes and petabytes. The data is so vast that traditional data processing applications are inadequate to deal with it.
2. Velocity - The speed at which the data is generated and processed is very high. The data streams in at an incredible rate from sources like social media, IoT devices, etc. Real-time processing is required to handle such high velocity data.
3. Variety - The data is present in all forms. It could be structured, semi-structured or unstructured. It could be text, audio, video, images, etc. The variety and complexity of data makes it difficult to store and process using traditional tools.

Some other characteristics of Big Data are:

- The data is exhaustive - It contains entire populations or complete sets of data rather than samples.
- The data is fine-grained - It is at a very granular level and of high resolution.
- The data is inclusive - It includes data that was previously ignored as trivial or useless. But now has potential value.
- The data is interconnected - The data points are linked and correlated with other data points.
- The data is dynamic - It keeps changing and evolving over time as new data comes in.

The heterogeneous and unstructured nature of Big Data makes it difficult to store, organize, manage, analyze and gain insights from the data. Hence, Big Data calls for special techniques and tools to systematically and efficiently extract value from it.
