### Challenges of conventional systems for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Big Data in the subject of Big Data

1. Scalability - The conventional systems are not capable of scaling up to handle the huge volumes of data in Big Data. The data in Big Data is enormous and rapidly increasing in size which the conventional systems cannot accommodate.
2. Complexity - The Big Data includes data from diverse sources and in varied formats like structured, unstructured and semi-structured. The conventional systems are not efficient in handling such complexity and diversity of data.
3. Velocity - The speed at which the data is generated and processed is very high in Big Data. The conventional systems cannot achieve the speed and real-time functionality required to process data at such high velocity.
4. Variety - The variety of data refers to the multiple formats of data and types of data. The traditional systems are designed to work with specific and structured formats of data which makes it difficult to handle the variety of data in Big Data.
5. Cost - The cost for storing and processing huge amounts of data is very high which makes the conventional systems inefficient and costly to work with Big Data.

The limitations of conventional systems in terms of scalability, complexity, velocity, variety and cost make it imperative to use Big Data architectures and frameworks to handle the challenges of the immense volumes and varieties of data in Big Data. The Big Data technologies are designed to overcome these challenges and enable efficient storage and processing of huge and diverse data.
