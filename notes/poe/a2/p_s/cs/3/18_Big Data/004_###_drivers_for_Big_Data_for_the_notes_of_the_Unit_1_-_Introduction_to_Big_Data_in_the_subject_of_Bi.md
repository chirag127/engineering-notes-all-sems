### Drivers for Big Data

The major drivers for Big Data are:

1. Increased data volume: The data volume has exploded over the recent years. Everything around us is getting connected to the internet and is generating data. This includes the huge amount of data generated from sources such as social media, ecommerce, internet of things devices, mobile devices, etc. This huge volume of data needs to be processed and analyzed to extract valuable insights and information.

2. Increased data velocity: The speed at which data is generated and processed has increased rapidly. This velocity of data ensures that the data becomes outdated quickly if not processed in real-time. Real-time data processing is required for applications such as fraud detection, personalized recommendations, etc. Tools and techniques are required to handle the increased data velocity.

3. Increased data variety: The types of data generated have increased in variety. Data today comes in variety of formats such as structured, unstructured and semi-structured data. This variety of data requires different techniques and tools to store, process and analyze them.

4. Low cost of data storage and computing: The costs of data storage and computing have reduced significantly with technological advancements. This has enabled even relatively small organizations to store and process huge amounts of data at a low cost. The reduced costs act as a driver for the increased adoption of Big Data technologies.

5. Need for better insights and decision making: There is a need for organizations to gain better business insights and make data-driven decisions to improve their products/services and business outcomes. The huge volume and variety of data now available can provide deeper insights to help in better decision making. This acts as a key driver for the adoption of Big Data technologies.
