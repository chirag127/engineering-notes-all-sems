### History of Hadoop

- Hadoop was created by Doug Cutting, the creator of Apache Lucene, in 2005.
- Cutting named it after his son's toy elephant. Hadoop had its origins in Apache Nutch, an open source web search engine.
- The initial development of Hadoop was supported by Yahoo, where Cutting worked at the time and it was deployed on thousands of nodes there.
- The Hadoop Distributed File System (HDFS) is inspired by the Google File System (GFS) paper published in 2003.
- MapReduce, the parallel processing framework, is inspired by Google's MapReduce paper published in 2004.
- In 2007, Hadoop's development moved to the Apache Software Foundation and it became a top-level Apache project in 2008.
- Hadoop 1.0 was released in 2011, followed by Hadoop 2.0 (YARN) in 2013, and Hadoop 3.0 in 2016.
- Today Hadoop is used by thousands of organizations for big data storage and analytics, including eBay, Facebook, IBM, Microsoft, and Twitter.
- Advantages of Hadoop: Scalable, Fault tolerant, Cost effective, Flexible.
- Disadvantages of Hadoop: Complex to use and manage, Slow compared to traditional databases.
- Applications: Log processing, Data warehousing, Machine Learning, Recommendation systems.
