## Unit 1 - Introduction

1. Introduction to Artificial Intelligence - AI is the field of computer science that attempts to build machines that can think and act intelligently, similar to a human. AI systems are powered by algorithms that allow machines to learn from data, recognize patterns, adapt and make decisions with minimal human input. Some examples of AI include:
    - Speech recognition systems that convert speech to text
    - Image recognition algorithms that can identify objects, faces or scenes in pictures
    - Virtual assistants that can understand voice commands and complete tasks
    - Machine translation that can translate between languages
    - Self-driving cars that can perceive and navigate the environment around them

2. A Brief History of AI - The concept of AI was first proposed in the 1950s. Some of the major milestones and breakthroughs in the field include:
    - In 1950, Alan Turing publishes his paper on the "Turing Test" to test a machine's ability to exhibit intelligent behavior equivalent to a human.
    - In 1956, the term "Artificial Intelligence" is coined at the Dartmouth Conference, where the concept of AI is extensively discussed.
    - In 1959, Arthur Samuel writes the first AI program for playing checkers.
    - In 1965, Herbert Simon predicts that machines will be capable of doing any work a human can do within 20 years.
    - In 1997, Deep Blue defeats Garry Kasparov in chess which is a major milestone.
    - In 2011, IBM's Watson defeats human champions at Jeopardy! which demonstrates the power of machine learning and natural language processing.
    - In 2016, AlphaGo defeats Lee Sedol in the game of Go, which is a major milestone in AI due to the complexity of the game.
    - In recent years, major progress has been made in deep learning, neural networks and reinforcement learning leading to huge strides in the capabilities of AI systems.
