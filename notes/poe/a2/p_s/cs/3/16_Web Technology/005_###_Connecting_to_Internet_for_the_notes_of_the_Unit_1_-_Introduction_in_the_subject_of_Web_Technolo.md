### Connecting to Internet

1.  Internet is a global network of computers and servers interconnected with each other using a standardized set of protocols.
2.  To access the resources and services available on the Internet, a computer needs to be connected to the Internet. There are various ways to connect a computer to the Internet:

-   Dial-up Connection: In this, the computer is connected to the Internet using a modem and a phone line. The modem converts the digital data from the computer to analog signals to be sent over the phone line. This is a slow connection and is not preferred nowadays.
-   Broadband Connection: This provides high-speed Internet access and is the most popular way of connecting to the Internet nowadays. The main types are:
    -   Fiber Optic Connection - Data is transmitted over optical fiber cables using light signals. It provides the fastest Internet speeds.
    -   Cable Connection - Data is transmitted over the cable TV network. Requires a cable modem.
    -   Digital Subscriber Line (DSL) - Data is transmitted over phone lines at high speeds. Requires a DSL modem.
-   Wireless Connection: This uses wireless data networks like Wi-Fi, 4G, and 5G networks to connect devices to the Internet. No cables or phone lines are required. Suitable for portable devices but speeds can be inconsistent.

The connection type to use depends on various factors like location, availability, and budget. Faster connections with high speeds and bandwidth are preferable for accessing high-bandwidth content and applications. Slower connections can result in a poor experience while browsing the Internet or using Internet-based services and apps.
