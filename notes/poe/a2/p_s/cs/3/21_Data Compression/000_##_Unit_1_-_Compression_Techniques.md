## Unit 1 - Compression Techniques

1. Lossless Compression
- Compression technique in which the original data can be perfectly reconstructed from the compressed data.
- Examples: ZIP, RAR, 7-ZIP
- Works by removing redundancy and unnecessary bits from the data.
- Advantage: Original data can be recovered exactly.
- Disadvantage: Achieves only moderate compression ratios.

2. Lossy Compression
- Compression technique in which the original data cannot be perfectly reconstructed from the compressed data.
- Examples: JPEG, MP3, MP4
- Works by permanently discarding unnecessary data.
- Advantage: Achieves very high compression ratios.
- Disadvantage: Original data cannot be recovered exactly.

3. Entropy Encoding
- A coding technique that assigns codes to signals/symbols based on their probability of occurrence.
- Examples: Huffman Coding, Arithmetic Coding.
- Assigns shorter codes to more probable symbols and longer codes to less probable symbols.
- Helps achieve additional compression.
- Often used along with other compression techniques.
