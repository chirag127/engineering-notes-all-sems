### Lossy Compression

- Lossy compression techniques remove some data from the original data in order to achieve higher compression ratios than lossless compression.
- The removed data cannot be recovered from the compressed data.
- Examples: JPEG image format, MP3 audio format, MPEG video format.
- The reduction in data is achieved by removing imperceptible or less important information.
- The compression ratio depends on the amount and type of information removed. Higher compression leads to more loss of information.
- Applications: Storage and transmission of audio, image and video files where some loss of information can be tolerated.
- Advantages: Much higher compression ratios than lossless compression.
- Disadvantages: Original data cannot be perfectly reconstructed from the compressed data.
