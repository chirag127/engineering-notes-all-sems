### Creating Emails in Cpanel for the notes of the Unit 5 - Web Hosting in the subject of Web Designing

1. Log in to your Cpanel account and go to Email Accounts section.
2. Click on Add New Account button to create a new email account.
3. Enter the Email Address, Password, and choose an appropriate Email Plan for the account.
4. Select the appropriate options for the account like:
    - Alias email addresses (if required)
    - Email forwarding (if required)
    - Autoresponders
    - Email filters (for spam, virus, etc)
    - Mailbox quotas (storage space allocation)
    - Email access protocols (POP3, IMAP, etc)
5. Click Create Account button to create the email account.
6. The email account will be created and will be displayed in the list of accounts where you can manage or access the account.

Advantages of creating email accounts in Cpanel:
- Easy to create, manage and access the email accounts.
- Provides various customization options for the accounts.
- Offers different email plans suitable for personal and business use.
- Provides security options like spam and virus filtering.
- Supports various email access protocols.

Disadvantages of creating email accounts in Cpanel:
- Limited storage space in free and lower plans.
- Limited customization options in free plan.
- Technical knowledge is required to set up the advanced options.

Applications of creating email accounts in Cpanel:
- For hosting personal websites and blogs.
- For small businesses to have professional email addresses.
- For web developers to create email accounts for their client's websites.
