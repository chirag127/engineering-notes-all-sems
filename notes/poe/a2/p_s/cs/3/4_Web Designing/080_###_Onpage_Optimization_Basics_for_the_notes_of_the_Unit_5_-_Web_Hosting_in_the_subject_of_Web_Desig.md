 Here is the content in markdown format for the topic ### Onpage Optimization Basics for the notes of the Unit 5 - Web Hosting in the subject of Web Designing:

1. Page Title - The page title is the first thing that search engines analyze to determine what a page is about. It should be descriptive and include the main keywords you want the page to rank for.

2. Meta Description - The meta description appears in search engine results pages to summarize what the page is about. It should be enticing and include keywords to attract clicks, but keep it under 156 characters.

3. Header Tags - Header tags (H1, H2, etc.) are used to structure content and indicate section headings. Include keywords in H1 and H2 tags, but don't keyword stuff. Use them naturally to structure your content.

4. Content - Having useful, unique content that includes your target keywords is critical for ranking in search engines. The content should be informative or provide value to the reader. Avoid duplicate content and keep your content updated.

5. Internal Linking - Link to other relevant content on your site to keep visitors engaged. Use relevant anchor text and don't link to low quality pages. This helps search engines crawl and understand your content.

6. Site Architecture - Having a logical and easy to navigate site architecture makes it easy for visitors to find content and search engines to crawl your site. Group related content together and use consistent category and tagging systems.

7. Load Time - Having a fast load time leads to a better user experience, higher rankings, and more conversions. Optimize images, minimize redirects, enable compression, and clean up code to improve load time.

8. Mobile-Friendliness - With increasing mobile usage, having a mobile-friendly site has become essential. Optimize your site to display well on mobile and include a responsive design or separate mobile version.

9. Schema Markup - Including schema markup helps search engines understand your content and display rich snippets in the search results. This can improve click-through rates and rankings. Implement markup for products, events, recipes, and more.

10. URL Structure - Having a clean URL structure makes it easy for users and search engines to understand your content. Keep URLs short, use keywords, and avoid special characters, sessions IDs, and dynamic parameters.
