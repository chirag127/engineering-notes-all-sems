### Importance of SEO for the notes of the Unit 5 - Web Hosting in the subject of Web Designing

1. SEO or Search Engine Optimization is important to rank the notes higher in search engines like Google when students search for the particular topic. Higher ranking of the notes will increase the visibility and more students will be able to access the notes easily.
2. Relevant keywords can be included in the headings and content of the notes which will be useful for the search engines to identify the most appropriate notes for the search query. For e.g. keywords like 'web hosting', 'shared hosting', 'dedicated hosting', 'cloud hosting' etc can be included for the notes on web hosting.
3. A clean and simple URL structure can be followed for the notes with relevant keywords in the slug to make it more SEO friendly. For e.g. `https://example.com/web-designing-notes/unit-5-web-hosting`
4. Internal linking between the notes can be added to keep the students engaged and help search engines identify the relevance of the content. For e.g. linking to the previous and next units notes at the beginning and end of the note.
5. Meta title, description and image can be optimized with relevant keywords to get higher rankings in search results.
6. The overall user experience can be improved by following a proper formatting structure with headings and fonts to make the notes easily scannable. This will lead to more time spent by students on the notes and reduction in bounce rate.

The above points can help in creating SEO friendly notes which will be more useful for the students to learn and prepare for their exams. Diagrams, examples and applications can also be added wherever required to enhance the learning experience.
