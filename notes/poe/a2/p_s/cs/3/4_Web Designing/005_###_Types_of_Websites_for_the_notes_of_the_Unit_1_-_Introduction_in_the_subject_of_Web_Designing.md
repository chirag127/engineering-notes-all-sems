### Types of Websites

The following are the major types of websites:

1. Personal Websites: Websites created by individuals to share their ideas, opinions, talent, achievements, etc. with others on the Internet. They usually contain personal information and content. For example, blogs, profiles, portfolios, etc.

2. Business Websites: Websites created by businesses, companies, organizations, etc. to promote their products, services, and other business activities on the Internet. They usually contain information like about the business, products and services, contact information, latest news and updates, etc. For example, e-commerce stores, product catalogs, corporate websites, etc.

3. News/Media Websites: Websites operated by news agencies, media organizations, journalists, etc. to publish and share news, current affairs, articles, videos, etc. on the Internet. For example, news websites, media/entertainment websites, etc.

4. Information Websites: Websites dedicated to providing information on various topics. They act as a repository of useful and informative content on the web. For example, Wikipedia, tutorial websites, knowledge portals, etc.

5. Community Websites: Websites that provide a platform for people to connect, interact, and collaborate with each other around common interests and topics. They allow people to start discussions, share and post content, upload media, comment, rate, network, etc. For example, forums, social networking websites, Q&A websites, etc.
