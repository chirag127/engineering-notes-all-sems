### Registering domains for the notes of the Unit 5 - Web Hosting in the subject of Web Designing

1. Domain name: The domain name is the unique name that identifies a website or an email address on the Internet. It consists of alphanumeric characters and is organized in a hierarchical system with domains at the top-level, second-level, and lower levels. For example, in the domain name "example.com", "example" is the second-level domain and "com" is the top-level domain.

2. Domain name registrars: Domain names are registered through domain name registrars that manage the reservation of domain names and assigning corresponding IP addresses. Some of the popular domain name registrars are GoDaddy, Namecheap, HostGator, etc. One can register a domain name by choosing an available domain name and completing the registration process on the registrar's website by providing personal details and paying the required registration fees.

3. Domain names extensions: The domain name extensions like .com, .net, .org, .in, .co, etc. determine the category of the domain. For example, .com and .net are generic top-level domains, .org is for non-profit organizations, .in is country code top-level domain for India, and .co is meant for companies. One can choose a suitable domain name extension as per the purpose and category of the website.

The key advantages of registering a domain name are:
1. It gives a unique identity to your website and helps in easy identification and recall.
2. It allows you to access your website at an easy-to-remember web address.
3. It enables you to have complete control over your domain name.

The registration fees and renewal charges for premium domain names can be high. Also, your desired domain name may already be taken. So, it is important to check the availability of your preferred domain name before registering it.

Overall, registering a domain name is important to establish your presence on the Internet and should be done carefully by choosing an appropriate domain name and reputed registrar.
