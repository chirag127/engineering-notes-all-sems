### Basics of SEO

SEO stands for Search Engine Optimization. It is the process of optimizing a website to rank higher in search engine results, especially Google search results. Higher search rankings mean more visitors to the website. SEO involves both technical and content aspects to achieve top rankings in search engines.

Some key points about SEO:

1. Choose relevant page titles and descriptions: The page title and meta description of a web page are very important for SEO. They should contain relevant keywords that describe the content of the page. This helps search engines understand the topic of the page and rank it appropriately.

2. Create useful content: The actual content of a web page is crucial for SEO. The content should revolve around relevant keywords and be useful for readers. It should answer questions and provide value to readers. Unique, well-structured content is preferred by search engines.

3. Optimize page speed: Slow loading web pages are not favored by search engines or users. Page speed optimization involves reducing page load time by minifying CSS and JS files, optimizing images, enabling compression, removing unnecessary plugins, etc. Faster pages lead to better user experience and SEO.

4. Build internal links: Internal links or backlinks help search engines crawl a website and understand the topic of each page. Relevant internal links from high quality pages can boost the search rankings of other pages on the website. The anchor text of internal links should also contain relevant keywords.

5. Earn high quality backlinks: Backlinks from other authoritative websites help to improve search rankings. However, only high quality backlinks from relevant websites are useful for SEO. Low quality or spammy backlinks can negatively affect SEO and may even lead to penalties. It is important to earn backlinks organically through solid content and relationship building.
