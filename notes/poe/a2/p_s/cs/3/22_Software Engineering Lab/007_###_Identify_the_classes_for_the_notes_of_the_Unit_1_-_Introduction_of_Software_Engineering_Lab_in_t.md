1. Software Engineering:
- Software Engineering is an engineering discipline which is concerned with all aspects of software production.
- It includes technical skills and methods encompassing the entire software development lifecycle.
- It includes processes, methods, and tools which are used to develop and maintain software.

2. Software Development Life Cycle:
- SDLC or Software Development Life Cycle refers to the process of developing software through business needs, analysis, design, development, testing, deployment, and maintenance.
- The SDLC aims to produce a high-quality software that meets or exceeds customer expectations, reaches completion within times and cost estimates.
- The SDLC usually consists of the following stages: Requirements Analysis, Design, Coding, Testing, Deployment, and Maintenance.

3. Requirements Analysis:
- The first stage of SDLC where the requirements of the software to be developed are analyzed.
- The requirements are gathered from the client, stakeholders, and domain experts.
- This stage determines what the software will do and who will use it. The requirements are documented in a Software Requirements Specification (SRS) document.

4. Design:
- The design of the software is prepared from the requirements specified in the SRS document.
- It includes architectural design, interface design, and component design.
- The design is transformed into diagrams and documentation for the development team to implement.
