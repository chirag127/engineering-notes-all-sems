### Recognition based on matching

- Pattern matching is a technique where the input image is matched with the stored templates to recognize the object.
- The templates are predefined patterns of the objects to be recognized.
- The input image is compared with each template to find the closest match. The object corresponding to the closest matching template is the recognized object.
- This is a simple and fast technique but has limitations like:
- It requires storage of templates of all possible objects.
- It fails to recognize objects with minor variations or distortions.
- It cannot handle occlusion or cluttered backgrounds.

Advantages:
- Simple and fast
- No training required

Disadvantages:
- Requires storage of many templates
- Fails for objects with variations or distortions
- Cannot handle occlusion or clutter

Applications:
- Recognition of printed characters
- Recognition of objects with consistent shape like coins, stamps, etc.
