### Texture

- Texture refers to the visual pattern or structure of an surface. It is a measure of the coarseness or smoothness of a surface.
- Texture analysis is used to classify surfaces and identify objects based on attributes like smoothness, coarseness, regularity etc.
- Some ways to analyze texture are:

1. Statistical methods: Calculate properties like mean, variance, correlation, entropy, etc. of the pixel intensities to characterize texture.
2. Structural methods: Analyze the spatial arrangement of texture primitives (like texels) to characterize texture. Texels are the fundamental repeating elements of a texture.
3. Model-based methods: Compare textures to prototype models like grids, lines, spots, etc. and classify based on closest match.

- Advantages: Texture analysis is rotation and scale invariant. It can classify objects even with distortions or changes in viewpoints.
- Disadvantages: It is difficult to extract precise texture features. The features are not very distinctive and different textures can produce similar features leading to misclassification.
- Applications: Texture analysis is used in applications like surface inspection, material classification, biomedical imaging, etc.
