### Notch Filters

- Notch filters are used to remove specific frequency components (notches) from an image.
- They are useful in removing periodic noise from an image.
- A notch filter can be designed by combining a lowpass and a highpass filter. The notch frequency is the frequency at which the gain of the notch filter is minimum.
- Notch filters are designed to have high attenuation at a specific notch frequency (or notch frequencies) and lesser attenuation at other frequencies.
- Examples of notch filters are:
    - Bandstop or band-reject filters - Removes a range of frequencies.
    - Band-notch filter - Removes a specific narrow range of frequencies.
- Applications:
    - Removal of periodic noise like power line interference.
    - Removal of specific texture from an image.
- Advantages:
    - Effective in removing specific frequency components.
    - Inexpensive and easy to design.
- Disadvantages:
    - May remove some desired frequency components along with noise.
    - May cause ripples or distortions in the frequency response away from the notch frequency.
