### SEI-CMM Model in SRS

- SEI-CMM stands for Software Engineering Institute-Capability Maturity Model. It is a framework that describes 5 levels of maturity for software development process which can be incorporated in Software Requirements Specification (SRS).
- The 5 levels of maturity are:

1. Initial: The software process is ad hoc and disorganized. Success depends on individual effort and heroics.
2. Repeatable: The software process follows a standard set of steps, but they are infrequently and inconsistently applied.
3. Defined: The software process is precisely defined, documented, and consistently used.
4. Managed: Detailed measures of the software process and product quality are collected. Both the software process and products are quantitatively managed.
5. Optimizing: Continuous process improvement is enabled by quantitative feedback from piloting innovative ideas and technologies.

- Following benefits can be achieved by incorporating SEI-CMM model in SRS:
- Increased customer satisfaction through on-time delivery of software meeting requirements.
- Improved quality and productivity through quantitative management and process optimization.
- Predictable costs and schedules through quantitative process management.
- Improved staff morale, training, and retention through process focus and institutionalization.
- Examples of including SEI-CMM levels in SRS can be:
- SRS will follow level 3 defined process as per SEI-CMM model.
- The development process defined in SRS conforms to level 4 managed process of SEI-CMM model.
