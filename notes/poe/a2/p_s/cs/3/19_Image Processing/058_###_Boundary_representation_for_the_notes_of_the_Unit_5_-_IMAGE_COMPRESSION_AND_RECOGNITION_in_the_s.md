### Boundary representation for the notes of the Unit 5 - IMAGE COMPRESSION AND RECOGNITION in the subject of Image Processing

- Boundary representation stores an object by representing its boundary. It represents the object's shape/contour using a closed polygonal line.
- The shape is described using a sequence of vertices (corners) and edges (line segments).
- Each vertex has an (x, y) coordinate, and edges are represented as ordered pairs of vertex indices.
- This is a compact way to represent shape and is useful for shape-based object recognition and analysis.
- However, boundary representation discards the region-based interior details/information of the object. It only encodes the outer shape/contour.
- Example: A circle can be represented by its boundary vertices and edges but the interior is not represented.
- Applications: Shape-based object recognition, Shape analysis, Shape decomposition, etc.
- Advantages: Compact representation, Invariant to translation/rotation/scale, Easy shape matching and comparison.
- Disadvantages: Lack of interior details, Sensitive to boundary deformations/noises, Can't represent objects with holes or nested objects.
