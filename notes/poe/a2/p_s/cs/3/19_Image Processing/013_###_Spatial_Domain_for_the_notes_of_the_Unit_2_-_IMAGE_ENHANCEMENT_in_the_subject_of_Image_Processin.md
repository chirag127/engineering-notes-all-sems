### Spatial Domain

The spatial domain refers to the aggregate of pixels in an image. Image enhancement techniques in the spatial domain deal with direct manipulation of pixel values in an image.

- Point Operations - Operations on each pixel independently, e.g. brightness/contrast adjustment, negative transformation, logarithmic transformation, etc.
- Neighborhood Operations - Operations on a pixel based on the neighboring pixels, e.g. smoothing, sharpening, edge enhancement, etc.

#### Advantages
- Simple and fast.
- Does not amplify noise.

#### Disadvantages
- Does not use frequency domain information.
- Limited types of enhancements possible.

#### Examples
- Brightness/Contrast adjustment
- Histogram Equalization
- Smoothing (Averaging, Gaussian, etc.)
- Sharpening (Unsharp Masking, High-boost filtering, etc.)

### Software Quality Assurance (SQA) in SRS

Software Quality Assurance (SQA) is the process of evaluating and monitoring software products and processes to ensure quality. Some key SQA activities to cover in the SRS are:

- Reviews: Both technical reviews and management reviews of requirements, design, code, etc.
- Audits: Formal evaluation of processes, procedures, methods, etc.
- Testing: Thorough testing of the software under development. The SRS should specify test plans and expected outcomes.
- Process monitoring: Monitoring adherence to defined processes and standards. Collecting metrics and measurements.
- Problem reporting and corrective action: Clearly specifying how defects, bugs, and other problems will be reported and handled.

Including robust SQA in the SRS helps ensure the final software product meets the necessary quality standards. It promotes building quality into the product rather than trying to test/inspect quality in after development.
