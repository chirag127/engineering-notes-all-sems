## Experiment 12 - To learn handling and configuration of networking hardware like RJ-45 connector, CAT-6 cable, crimping tool, etc.

1. Objective: To gain hands-on experience in assembling networking cables and connectors.

2. Requirements: RJ-45 connectors, CAT-6 Ethernet cable, crimping tool, wire stripper

3. Procedure:

- Cut the Ethernet cable to the required length.
- Strip off 1.5 inches of the outer jacket of the cable using the wire stripper to expose the wires.
- Arrange the wires in the correct order - Orange/White, Orange, Green/White, Blue, Blue/White, Green.
- Insert the wires into the RJ-45 connector and use the crimping tool to secure the wires in place.
- Test the cable using a cable tester to ensure it's making proper contact.

**Advantages:** Making your own networking cables is cost effective and the cables can be customized to the exact required length. It also helps in understanding the internal components of a networking cable.

**Disadvantages:** Incorrect wiring or improper crimping can lead to faulty cables which can cause network issues. Extra care must be taken to wire and assemble the cables properly.

**Applications:** Ethernet cables are used to connect devices like computers, routers, and switches to form a local area network. Assembled networking cables can be used in home or office networks.
