#### Statistical Graphics:

- Statistical graphics are visual representations of quantitative information, relationships or statistical data.
- They make use of visual cues to convey information and relationships effectively.
- Some common forms of statistical graphics are:

- Bar charts: Used to compare different categories of data. Rectangular bars represent the values of the data.
- Line charts: Used to show trends over time. Data is represented by line segments connecting the data points.
- Pie charts: Used to show proportions of a whole. The circle represents the whole and sectors show the proportions of the components.
- Scatter plots: Used to show relationships between two quantitative variables plotted on horizontal and vertical axes. The pattern of the points reveals trends.
- Area charts: Similar to line charts but the region under the line is filled with color to emphasize the magnitude of change.

Advantages:
- They are easy to understand and interpret.
- They effectively show patterns and trends in data.
- They are useful in communicating information to a wide audience.

Disadvantages:
- They may be misleading if the scales are chosen incorrectly.
- A large amount of data cannot be shown.
- Precise comparisons are difficult to make.

Applications:
- Used in data analysis and presentation.
- Used to analyze trends and make comparisons and inferences.
- Used in various fields like business, science, education, etc. to visually analyze and present data.
