## Unit 2 - Design process

The design process involves the following steps:

1. Identify the problem: This is the first and foremost step where the problem is thoroughly analyzed and understood. The problem could be to design a product, system or service that fulfills a need. The problem is cleared of any ambiguities and a concrete problem statement is formulated.

2. Gather requirements: The needs and requirements are gathered from the users or clients. Their inputs regarding the constraints, objectives and key performance indicators are collected. The requirements could be functional or non-functional.

3. Brainstorm solutions: Multiple solutions are thought of that could potentially solve the problem. This step encourages creativity and there are no limits to the number or types of solutions that can be proposed. Wild ideas are welcomed as they could lead to innovative solutions.

4. Evaluate solutions: The brainstormed solutions are evaluated based on the requirements, feasibility, costs, benefits, risks etc. The solutions are screened and weaker solutions are eliminated. The strengths and weaknesses of each solution are analyzed.

5. Select solution: The solution that best meets the requirements is selected. If multiple feasible solutions exist, they can be prioritized in case the top solution is not feasible. The selected solution is then implemented.

6. Implement and test: The solution is implemented and tested to ensure that it actually solves the problem. If the test results are unsatisfactory, the team goes back to step 3 or 4 to re-evaluate and select another solution. Once testing is complete, the solution is deployed.

7. Review and maintain: The solution is reviewed and monitored after deployment to identify any issues. Necessary improvements or modifications are made to the solution to adapt to changing needs or constraints. The cycle then repeats.

The above process is iterative in nature and helps in designing optimal solutions. It encourages constant feedback and learning.
