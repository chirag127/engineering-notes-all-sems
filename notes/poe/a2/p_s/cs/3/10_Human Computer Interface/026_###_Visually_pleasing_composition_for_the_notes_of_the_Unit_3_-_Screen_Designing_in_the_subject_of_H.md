### Visually pleasing composition for the notes of the Unit 3 - Screen Designing in the subject of Human Computer Interface

1. Alignment - Proper alignment of elements like headings, paragraphs, images, etc. makes the composition visually appealing and easy to read. Elements should be aligned to the left, right, center or justified as per the requirement. Improper alignment makes the content look clumsy and haphazard.

2. Spacing - Adequate spacing between elements gives a neat and organized look to the screen composition. Crowding too many elements in a small space makes it look cluttered whereas excessive spacing makes it look sparse. Appropriate margins and padding should be applied.

3. Contrast - Using contrast in elements like headings, images, buttons, etc. makes specific elements stand out and draw attention. This makes the key points or calls to action prominent. Contrast can be achieved using size, color, style, etc. Lack of contrast makes every element fight for attention, making the content difficult to read.

4. Consistency - Using consistent styling for similar type of elements gives a uniform and polished look. Inconsistent styling like using different sizes or colors for same types of elements makes the composition look messy and unprofessional. Consistency in styling should be maintained for headings, paragraphs, buttons, navigational elements, etc.

5. Simplicity - Keeping the composition simple with minimal elements, avoiding clutter and complexity makes it easy to read and understand. Complex compositions with too many elements and distracting backgrounds or images make the content difficult to comprehend. Simplicity should be the key for an effective screen design.
