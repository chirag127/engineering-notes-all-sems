### Analysis in Requirement Engineering Process in SRS

- Requirement analysis is the process of studying the requirements stated by the customer and identifying the real needs.
- It is done by questioning the customer in detail about the problem statement, making them elaborate on their requirements and noting down all possible requirements.
- Some techniques used for requirement analysis are:
-- Interviews: Direct, structured conversations with stakeholders to elicit requirements
-- Questionnaires: Written sets of questions distributed to stakeholders
-- Observation: Studying stakeholders in their environment to understand their needs
-- Prototyping: Quick construction of partial system to understand requirements
-- Brainstorming: Group activity to generate ideas for requirements
- The goal of requirement analysis is to produce a list of unambiguous and complete set of user requirements that can be validated and serve as an input to the next phase of the requirement engineering process which is requirement specification.
- Requirement analysis results in the identification of functional and non-functional requirements of the system to be developed.
- Some advantages of properly analyzing requirements are: reduced rework, increased customer satisfaction, improved productivity, decreased maintenance costs, etc.
