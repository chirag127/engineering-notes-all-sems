### Information Modelling in Software Requirement Specification (SRS)

- Information modelling is an important part of Software Requirement Specification (SRS). It involves identifying and structuring the information that flows in and out of the system.
- The inputs and outputs of the system are identified and structured into appropriate data formats and data elements. This results in defining the data objects and their interfaces.
- The key purposes of information modelling are:
- To discover and understand the key information/data that will be processed by the system.
- To establish a common vocabulary between the users and the developers.
- To organize the information into a coherent and consistent structure.
- To identify the relationships between the key data objects.
- The commonly used techniques for information modelling are:
- Entity-relationship modelling - focuses on identification of main entities and relationships between them.
- Data flow modelling - focuses on the flow of information through the system.
- Object modelling - based on object-oriented approaches and focuses on objects, attributes and methods.
- The advantages of information modelling are:
- It helps in understanding the information aspects of the problem domain.
- It leads to a consistent and structured specification of data and interfaces.
- It facilitates communication between users and developers.
- The disadvantages are:
- It can be time-consuming to perform detailed information modelling.
- The models can become complex for large systems.
- The notations used may be unfamiliar to some users.
