### Image and Video Displays

- Images and videos are essential components of graphical user interfaces to display information, content, data, etc. to the user.
- Some key points about image and video displays:

1. Image formats - Common image formats are JPEG, PNG, GIF, BMP, etc. Each has its own advantages and disadvantages in terms of compression, quality, color support, etc. The appropriate format is chosen based on the specific use case and requirements.
2. Video formats - Common video formats are MP4, AVI, MKV, WebM, etc. Similar to images, the format is chosen based on the specific requirements such as compression, playback, editing, etc.
3. Resolution - The pixel dimensions (height and width) of an image or video determine its resolution. Higher resolution means more pixels and hence more clarity and sharpness but also larger file sizes. Resolution is chosen based on the display size and requirements.
4. Aspect ratio - The ratio of height to width of images or videos is called the aspect ratio. Maintaining the correct aspect ratio is important for proper visibility and experience.
5. Encoding - Images and videos are encoded in specific formats and encodings to store and compress the data. The encoding scheme significantly affects the file size and other properties.
6. Display size - Images and videos can be displayed at various sizes depending on the use case. The appropriate display size is chosen such that the information is clearly visible as well as the system resources are not strained.

- Usage of images and videos in graphical user interfaces allows conveying more information to the user in an intuitive and easy to understand manner. However, the file sizes and system resource usage should be considered when using them. Appropriate formats and encodings can help reduce the resource usage while maintaining good display quality.
