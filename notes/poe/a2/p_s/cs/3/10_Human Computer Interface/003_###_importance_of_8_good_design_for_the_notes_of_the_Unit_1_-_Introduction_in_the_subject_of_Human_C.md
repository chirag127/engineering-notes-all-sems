### Importance of good design for the notes of Unit 1 - Introduction to Human Computer Interface

1. Organized structure: The notes should be properly organized into sections and subsections for easy understanding and navigation. This helps in easy reference and revision. The hierarchy of headings should be maintained throughout the notes.
2. Clear language: The language used should be simple, clear and easy to understand. Complex technical terms should be explained properly. The sentences should be short and to the point. This makes the notes easy to comprehend.
3. Focus on key concepts: The notes should focus on the key concepts and main points rather than going into too much detail. The core ideas should be highlighted which would make them easier to remember. Additional details can be added as footnotes or appendices if required.
4. Use of diagrams: Including diagrams and illustrations in the notes enhances understanding and memorability. They provide a visual representation of concepts and an alternative way of learning for visual learners. ASCII diagrams or image diagrams can be used as appropriate for the topics.
5. Examples: Including examples and case studies in the notes makes the concepts more relatable to real world scenarios. This helps in applying the concepts and strengthens understanding. The examples should be explained in detail with diagrams if required.
6. Code snippets: For technical topics, including relevant code snippets and syntax highlighting adds practical relevance to the notes. The students can see how the concepts are implemented and try out the code. Proper commenting and explanations should accompany the code.
7. active learning: The notes should encourage active learning rather than just passive reading. This can be achieved by including questions, exercises and activities at the end of topics or sections. This helps in reinforcing the understanding and assessing self learning.
8. Consistency: Using a consistent style and format throughout the notes in terms of headings, diagrams, highlighting, font styles, etc. makes the notes easy to follow. This gives a structured feel to the students and facilitates revision.

The notes can also include advantages and disadvantages of concepts, applications of the topics and other details as relevant to enhance understanding and learning. Markdown tables, images and other features can also be utilized to make the notes more interesting and interactive. The overall aim should be to make the notes engaging, easy to understand and useful for learning as well as reference.
