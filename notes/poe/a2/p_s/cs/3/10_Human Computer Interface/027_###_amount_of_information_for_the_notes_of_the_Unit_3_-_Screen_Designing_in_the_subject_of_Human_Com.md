### Software Quality Attributes

Software quality attributes are non-functional requirements that software systems must satisfy. Some key software quality attributes are:

- Reliability - The ability of a system to function correctly and consistently in specified environments and conditions. Includes fault-tolerance, recoverability, and maintainability.
- Usability - The ease with which users can learn and use the software. Includes aspects like learnability, memorability, error frequency, and satisfaction.
- Efficiency - The amount of system resources a system consumes (processor, memory, storage, etc.). Includes performance and scalability.
- Security - The system's ability to remain free from unauthorized access or malicious threats and attacks. Includes aspects like confidentiality, integrity, authentication, and non-repudiation.
- Maintainability - The ease of updating, correcting, or enhancing a software system. Includes aspects like analyzability, changeability, stability, and testability.
- Portability - The ability of a system to be transferred to a different computing environment. Includes adaptability, installability, and replaceability.
