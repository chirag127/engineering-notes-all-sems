### Information Retrieval on Web

- Information Retrieval is the process of searching and fetching relevant information from a large amount of unstructured data present on the web.
- The World Wide Web contains a huge amount of information in the form of web pages, documents, videos, images, etc. and it is unstructured in nature.
- To retrieve relevant information from this huge amount of unstructured data, we need accurate information retrieval techniques and tools.
- Some of the Information Retrieval techniques used are -

-- Crawling - The process of browsing and downloading web pages for further processing. Search engines use crawlers or spiders to crawl the web.
-- Indexing - The process of building an index of the words contained in the pages that were crawled. The index contains keywords and their locations in the pages.
-- Query Processing - The process of searching the index for relevant keywords and ranking the pages based on relevance to the user's query.
-- Ranking - The process of ordering the pages in the order of their relevance to the user's query using ranking algorithms.

- The accuracy of the retrieved information depends on the effectiveness of the crawling, indexing and ranking algorithms used.
- Some applications of Information Retrieval are web search engines, plagiarism detection, recommendation systems, etc.
- The advantages of Information Retrieval are easy access to large volumes of information and personalized results. The disadvantages are irrelevant results, lack of quality content, etc.
