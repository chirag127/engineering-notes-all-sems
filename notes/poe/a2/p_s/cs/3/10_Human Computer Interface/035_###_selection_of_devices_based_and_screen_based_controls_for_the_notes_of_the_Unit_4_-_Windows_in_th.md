### Elicitation in Requirement Engineering Process in SRS

Requirement elicitation is the process of gathering requirements from various stakeholders including users, customers and others which is an important part of Requirements Engineering process in Software Requirements Specification (SRS). Following points highlight the elicitation process:

1. Identify stakeholders: First step is to identify all the stakeholders who can provide inputs for requirements. This may include end users, customers, managers, etc.
2. Communicate with stakeholders: Then communicate with the identified stakeholders to gather their needs and expectations from the software. This can be done through interviews, questionnaires, observation, etc.
3. analyze stakeholder responses: Analyze the responses and information gathered from stakeholders to filter out valid and important requirements. Remove any inconsistencies or duplicate requirements.
4. Prioritize requirements: Prioritize the filtered list of requirements according to their importance and impacts. This helps in determining high priority requirements that can be implemented first.
5. Review and verify: Finally, review and verify the requirements with stakeholders again to confirm if all needs are gathered correctly and if any clarification is required.

**Advantages:** Helps in gathering actual user needs and building software as per the customer expectations.
**Disadvantages:** Can be time consuming and tedious process. Stakeholders may not be able to express their needs clearly.
**Examples:** Interviews, Questionnaires, Observation, Brainstorming sessions.
**Applications:** Requirement elicitation is commonly used in various software development methodologies like Agile, Waterfall, etc. to gather user requirements.
