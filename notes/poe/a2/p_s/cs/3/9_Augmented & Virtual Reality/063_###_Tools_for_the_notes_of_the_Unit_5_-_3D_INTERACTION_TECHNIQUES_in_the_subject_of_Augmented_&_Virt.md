### Tools for the notes of the Unit 5 - 3D INTERACTION TECHNIQUES in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

1. 3D Input Devices
- 3D Mice: Allows natural 3D interaction through physical manipulation in 3D space. It tracks the 3D movements and rotation. Eg: 3Dconnexion SpaceNavigator
- Data Gloves: Fabric gloves with sensors to track hand movements and gestures. Eg: P5 Glove
- Motion Trackers: Uses sensors to track the position and orientation of physical objects. Eg: Wii Remote, Kinect

2. 3D Selection Techniques
- Ray casting: Casting an invisible ray from a pointing device to select an object intersecting the ray.
- Cone casting: Creates a cone from the pointing device and selects the closest object inside the cone.
- Bounding volume: Creates a volume around the pointing device and selects the closest object overlapping the volume.

3. 3D Manipulation Techniques
- Translational manipulation: Directly moving objects in 3D space along x, y and z axes.
- Rotational manipulation: Directly rotating objects about x, y and z axes.
- Scaling manipulation: Directly scaling up or down the size of objects along x, y and z axes.
