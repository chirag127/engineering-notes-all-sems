### Special Purpose Input Devices

- Data Gloves: Data gloves are fabric gloves with sensors that detect finger bending and hand position. They are used to capture hand and finger movements to control objects in virtual environments. They provide a natural way to interact with 3D virtual worlds.

Advantages:
- Provides intuitive and immersive interface.
- Captures complex hand and finger movements.

Disadvantages:
- Expensive.
- Require calibration.
- Limited tactile feedback.

Applications:
- Used in virtual reality applications to manipulate virtual objects.
- Used for signing in virtual environments.

- Tracking Suits: Tracking suits are clothing with sensors that capture body movements and positions. They are used to track the movement of the entire body to control and interact with objects in virtual environments. They provide an immersive experience and allow natural ways of interaction.

Advantages:
- Captures natural body movements.
- Provides immersive experience.

Disadvantages:
- Expensive.
- Require frequent calibration.
- Limited tactile feedback.

Applications:
- Used in virtual reality applications for navigation, manipulation of objects, etc.
- Used in motion capture for animation, sports analysis, etc.
