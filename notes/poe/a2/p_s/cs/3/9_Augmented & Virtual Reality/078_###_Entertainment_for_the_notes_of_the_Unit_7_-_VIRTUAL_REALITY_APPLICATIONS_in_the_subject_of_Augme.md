### Entertainment

- VR headsets are being increasingly used for entertainment purposes like gaming, movies, etc. Some of the key entertainment applications of VR are:

- VR Gaming: VR headsets provide an immersive gaming experience with 3D visuals and surround sound. Popular VR gaming headsets include Oculus Quest 2, Valve Index, HTC Vive, etc. VR games include racing games, shooting games, puzzle games, etc. However, prolonged use of VR headsets can cause discomfort and health issues like eye strain and nausea.

- VR Cinema: VR headsets can be used to watch movies and videos in a virtual cinema setup with a huge screen and surround sound. This provides an immersive viewing experience from the comfort of home. However, the resolution and display quality of VR headsets is not as good as traditional displays yet.

- VR Theme Parks: VR theme parks with VR headsets and haptic suits are being set up which provide physical sensations and an immersive experience of thrill rides and activities like skydiving, etc. without actually doing them. These are costly but popular entertainment options.

- Advantages: Immersive and engaging entertainment experience. Feels like the real thing.
- Disadvantages: Can cause discomfort and health issues with prolonged use. Resolution and display quality limitations. Expensive options.
- Examples: Oculus Quest 2, Valve Index, VR theme parks, etc.
- Applications: Gaming, movies, thrill rides, etc.
