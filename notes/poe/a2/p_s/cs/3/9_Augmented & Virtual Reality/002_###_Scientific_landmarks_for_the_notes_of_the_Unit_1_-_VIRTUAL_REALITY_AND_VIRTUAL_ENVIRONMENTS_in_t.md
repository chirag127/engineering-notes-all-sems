 Here are the key scientific landmarks for Unit 1 - Virtual Reality and Virtual Environments:

1. Ivan Sutherland invents first Head-Mounted Display (HMD) in 1968. It was the first VR/AR headset that could track the user's head movements and update the visuals accordingly. This paved the way for immersive virtual environments.
2. Myron Krueger invents first virtual reality arcade game - Sensorama in 1970s. It included visual, audio and haptic feedback to provide an immersive experience. This was one of the first attempts at providing a multi-sensory virtual environment.
3. Jaron Lanier founds VPL Research in 1984 which developed DataGlove and the EyePhone head-mounted display. The DataGlove was one of the first devices to track hand movements and gestures. VPL Research played a key role in the early development of virtual reality.
4. NASA develops the Virtual Interface Environment Workstation (VIEW) in 1989. It could render stereoscopic 3D graphics in real-time and track the user's head movements. This system was used for virtual prototyping and manipulator control training.
5. Sega releases the Sega VR headset for arcade games in 1993. Though it was not a commercial success, it increased public interest in virtual reality gaming and paved the way for modern VR headsets.
