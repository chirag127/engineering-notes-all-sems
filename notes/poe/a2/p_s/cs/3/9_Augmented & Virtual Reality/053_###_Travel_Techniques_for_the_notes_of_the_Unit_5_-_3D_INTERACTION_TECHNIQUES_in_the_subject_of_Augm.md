### Travel Techniques

- Travel techniques allow the user to navigate and explore the 3D virtual environment.
- Some of the common travel techniques are:

1. Walking - The user can walk through the virtual environment using controls like keyboard, joysticks or motion controllers. The walking speed can be controlled by the user. This gives the most natural mode of navigation.
2. Flying - The user can fly through the air to navigate the virtual environment. The direction of flight can be controlled to explore the environment with a sense of freedom. But it may not seem natural in some scenarios.
3. Teleportation - The user can instantly teleport to any location in the virtual environment. The teleport locations can be predefined hotspots or the user can click on any location to teleport there. This is useful in large environments but lacks the sense of traveling through the space.
4. Vehicle - The user can navigate the environment by driving a vehicle (car, bike, spaceship etc.). The controls of the vehicle can be used to explore the environment. This can make the experience more fun but has limitations of pathways and access.
