 Here is the content in markdown format for the topic ### Education for the notes of the Unit 7 - VIRTUAL REALITY APPLICATIONS in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality:

1. Virtual Field Trips - Students can go on virtual field trips to places like museums, historic sites, national parks, etc. without leaving the classroom. This allows students to experience and learn things that wouldn't be possible otherwise due to constraints.
2. Virtual Laboratories - Virtual labs can be created for science experiments and simulations. This is useful when physical labs are not possible or to supplement them. Students can manipulate variables and see results in immersive virtual environments.
3. Virtual Training - VR can be used to train students on skills and processes. For example, medical students can practice surgical techniques or aircraft pilots can be trained in a virtual simulator. This allows for safe, repeatable, and scalable training.
4. Interactive Learning - VR enables highly interactive and engaging learning experiences. Students can interact with virtual objects, characters, and scenarios. This leads to improved learning outcomes compared to traditional methods.
5. Personalized Learning - VR can adapt experiences to individual students' skills, pace, interests, and learning styles. This personalized learning approach enables students to learn more effectively.
6. Immerse Disabled Students - VR helps disabled students participate and experience things they otherwise couldn't. For example, students with mobility disabilities can go on virtual field trips. This helps them be included and accommodated in the learning process.

* VR education advantages: highly engaging, personalized, accessible
* VR education disadvantages: expensive technology, isolating, simulation fidelity
* Examples: Google Expeditions, Labster, zSpace
* Applications: K-12, higher education, corporate training
