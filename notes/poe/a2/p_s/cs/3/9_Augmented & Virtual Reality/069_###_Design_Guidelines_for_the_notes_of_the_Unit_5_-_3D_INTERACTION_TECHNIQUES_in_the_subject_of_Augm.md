 Here are the Design Guidelines for the notes of the Unit 5 - 3D INTERACTION TECHNIQUES in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality:

1. Include headings and subheadings: Include main headings and subheadings to categorize the content and make it easy to navigate through the notes. For ex:

#### 3D Input Devices
- Data gloves
- Trackers (magnetic, optical, inertial)
- Motion Controllers

2. Use lists: Use ordered or unordered lists to present key points or steps. For ex:

To track hand movements:
1. Data gloves: Fiber optic/sensor gloves
2. Motion controllers: Controllers with motion sensors to track movement

3. Include diagrams and images: Include diagrams, images, charts, codes, etc. to visually represent concepts and make the notes more engaging and easy to understand. For ex: Include images of different 3D input devices and their working.

4. Write in simple and clear language: Use simple and clear language to explain the concepts and avoid complex technical jargon. Write short and concise sentences for easy understanding.

5. Include examples and applications: Include examples and applications of the concepts to relate the theory with practical implementations. This helps in understanding the relevance and importance of the topic.

6. Note down references: Note down references to books, research papers, websites, etc. at the end of the notes for students to explore the topics in more depth.
