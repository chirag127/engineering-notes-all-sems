## Unit 8 - Augmented and Mixed Reality

Augmented Reality (AR) and Mixed Reality (MR) are emerging technologies that overlay digital information on the real world via devices like smart glasses and head-mounted displays (HMDs).

### Augmented Reality (AR)

- AR overlays digital information on the real world via devices like smart glasses or smartphone apps.
- It enhances the real world by adding layers of digital information on top of what the user sees in the real world.
- The digital layers could include text, images, videos, 3D models, etc.
- Examples: Pokémon Go, IKEA Place app, etc.
- Advantages: Immersive learning, enhanced navigation, etc.
- Disadvantages: Privacy concerns, distraction, etc.

### Mixed Reality (MR)

- MR blends the real and digital worlds to produce new environments and visualizations where physical and digital objects co-exist and interact in real time.
- It incorporates elements of both AR and VR.
- The user can interact with digital elements while still being aware of the real world.
- Examples: Microsoft HoloLens.
- Advantages: Immersive experiences, collaborative interactions, etc.
- Disadvantages: Limited content, cost, etc.
