### Mobile Projection Interfaces

- Mobile projection interfaces refer to the techniques of projecting augmented reality content from mobile devices onto physical surfaces.
- The camera of the mobile device is used to track the surrounding environment and the projected visuals are aligned with the physical surfaces to create an augmented experience.
- Some of the mobile projection interfaces are -

1. Mobile Projected Displays - The display of the mobile device is projected onto a surface to create a larger display. The projected display can act as a secondary display or mirror the display of the mobile device.
2. Mobile Projected Overlays - Augmented reality overlays are projected from the mobile device onto physical surfaces. The overlays may include information, controls, etc. aligned with the physical environment.
3. Mobile Spatial Augmented Reality - Both the physical environment and augmented reality visuals are projected from the mobile device to enable interaction with augmented spaces. The projection allows augmentation of larger spaces and more natural interactions.

- Advantages - Enhanced display sizes, natural interactions, augmentation of physical spaces.
- Disadvantages - Requirements of projection equipment, diminishing brightness and resolution over distances, limited tracking accuracy.
- Applications - Entertainment, education, business, etc. The projection interfaces enable experiences that utilize larger display areas and more natural interactions with augmented content. They allow augmentation of physical environments at a larger scale compared to mobile AR viewed through displays.
