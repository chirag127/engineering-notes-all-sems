 Here are the Design Guidelines for the notes of the Unit 5 - 3D INTERACTION TECHNIQUES in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality:

### 1. Describe 3D Interaction Techniques
- Explain 3D Input Devices like Data Gloves, Motion Trackers, Gesture Recognition Systems, etc.
- Discuss 3D Selection Techniques like Ray Casting, Cone Casting, Spatial Partitioning, etc.
- Discuss 3D Manipulation Techniques like Point & Click, Drag & Drop, Two-Handed Manipulation, etc.
- Explain 3D System Metaphors like Virtual Hands, Tools, Virtual Objects, etc.

### 2. Compare different 3D Interaction Techniques
- Compare 3D Input Devices in terms of Accuracy, Cost, Usability, etc.
- Compare 3D Selection Techniques in terms of Precision, Speed, Task Suitability, etc.
- Compare 3D Manipulation Techniques in terms of Naturalness, Task Suitability, Learning Curve, etc.
- Discuss the Pros & Cons of different System Metaphors.

### 3. Provide Examples & Applications of 3D Interaction Techniques
- Explain the working of different 3D Interaction Techniques with examples.
- Discuss applications of 3D Interaction Techniques in various domains like Navigation, Design, Collaboration, Simulation, Exploration, etc.

### 4. Include Diagrams & Codes (If any)
- Include diagrams to explain the working of complex 3D Interaction Techniques.
- Include sample codes or algorithms if required to explain implementation details.
