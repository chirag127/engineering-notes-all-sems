### Computer Graphics

- Computer graphics is the field of computer science that studies methods for digitally synthesizing and manipulating visual content.
- It is the process of creating and manipulating images digitally and involves creating images in a computer that can be displayed on a monitor or printed.
- The images may be static or moving (animations).
- The input to the computer graphics system typically consists of geometry, viewpoint, texture, lighting, and motion information.
- The output is an image i.e. a drawing or animation.
    - Geometry specifies the shape of objects in the scene.
    - Viewpoint specifies the camera location and viewing transformation.
    - Texture specifies the surface appearance of objects.
    - Lighting specifies how objects in the scene are illuminated.
    - Motion specifies changes in geometry, viewpoint, texture, and lighting over time.
- The major areas of computer graphics research are photorealistic rendering, modeling, animation, virtual environments, and scientific visualization.
- Applications of Computer Graphics -
    - Video Games
    - Architectural Design
    - Advertising
    - Education
    - Movies
    - Medical Imagery
    - Simulations
    - etc.
