### Taxonomy for the notes of the Unit 8 - Augmented and Mixed Reality

1. Augmented Reality (AR)
- AR is the overlay of digital information on the real world via devices like smart glasses or mobile devices.
- It enhances our perception of reality by overlaying digital information on top of what we see in the real world.
- Examples: Pokémon Go, IKEA Place app, Google Translate, etc.
- Components: Marker, Sensor, Display, Tracking, Rendering, Registration
- Advantages: Immersive, engaging, efficient, cost-effective
- Disadvantages: Technical limitations, lack of context awareness, privacy and security concerns
- Applications: Entertainment, retail, education, business, healthcare, engineering, etc.

2. Mixed Reality (MR)
- MR is the merging of real and virtual worlds to produce new environments where physical and digital objects co-exist and interact in real time.
- It incorporates elements of both AR and VR to create a sense of immersion and interactivity.
- Examples: HoloLens, Magic Leap, etc.
- Components: Sensors, Cameras, Display, Tracking, Rendering
- Advantages: True immersion, contextual awareness
- Disadvantages: Expensive hardware, limited content
- Applications: Industries like engineering, architecture, healthcare education, design, gaming, etc.
