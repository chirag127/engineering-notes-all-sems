### The historical development of VR

1. 1960s - The concept of VR was first introduced in the 1960s. The first VR head-mounted display (HMD) was invented by Ivan Sutherland and his student Bob Sproull in 1968. It was called the Sword of Damocles because of the weight suspended from the ceiling.
2. 1970s - The 1970s saw the development of the first VR gloves to track hand movements and gestures by Thomas Zimmerman and others. The first VR arcade game and VR movie theater was developed.
3. 1980s - This decade saw the introduction of the term 'Virtual Reality' by Jaron Lanier. The DataGlove was invented, NASA and the military began using VR for training simulations, and the first VR company 'VPL Research' was founded by Jaron Lanier.
4. 1990s - The 1990s saw major improvements in graphics, with the introduction of the first consumer VR headsets with low-latency head and hand tracking. The Virtual Boy by Nintendo was the first portable VR headset launched although it turned out to be a commercial failure. VR started being used in medical training and simulations.
5. 2000s - Better and lighter VR headsets and motion controllers with 6 degrees of freedom tracking were introduced. Advancements in computing power, display technology, and graphics capabilities enabled higher quality VR experiences. VR started being used for gaming and entertainment.
6. 2010s - Major companies like Facebook (Oculus), HTC (Vive), Sony (PlayStation VR), and Microsoft invested in VR and launched their VR headsets or platforms. Mobile VR also became popular with the Gear VR and Google Cardboard. VR is being applied in various areas and the 'VR/AR industry' is experiencing rapid growth.
