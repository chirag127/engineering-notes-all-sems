## Technology behind Augmented Reality

- Marker-based AR: Use visual markers or triggers like QR codes, patterns, etc. to trigger the AR experience. The camera reads the marker and overlays the AR content on it. Example - QR code scanning AR.
- Markerless AR: Does not use any physical markers. The AR experience is triggered via GPS, compass, accelerometer, etc. or by scanning the environment. Example - Pokemon Go.
- SLAM (Simultaneous Localization and Mapping): The device tracks its own motion and location and maps the surrounding 3D environment to enable positional tracking and occlusion of digital objects. Example - AR games involving moving around.

## Key Features of Augmented Reality

- Combines the real and digital world: Blends digital visual elements with the real-world environment.
- Interactivity: Users can interact with digital AR elements.
- Real-time: Digital elements are rendered and overlaid on the real world in real-time via the device sensors and camera.
- Registration: AR elements are registered or aligned with the real-world elements. They appear like a natural part of the environment.
- Context-aware: AR experiences are contextual and customized based on the environment and inputs.
- Personalization: AR can be personalized for each individual user.
