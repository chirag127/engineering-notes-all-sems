### Big Data applications

Here are some of the major Big Data applications:

1. Targeted advertising - Analysing customer data and browsing history to target individuals with relevant ads. E.g. recommending products you might like based on your past purchases and searches.
2. Sentiment analysis - Analysing social media data and online reviews to determine public opinion and sentiment about a product, service or topic. Helps companies understand customer perceptions.
3. Predictive analytics - Analysing data to detect patterns and make predictions. E.g. predicting customer churn, forecasting sales and detecting fraud.
4. Recommendation systems - Recommending products, movies, music, etc. tailored to a user's interests based on their data and behaviour. E.g. product recommendations on Amazon and movie recommendations on Netflix.
5. Automated assistants - Systems that can understand speech and text to assist users in various tasks. E.g. Siri, Alexa, etc. rely on AI and NLP which use massive amounts of data to learn and improve.
6. Smart transportation - Analysing data from sensors and cameras to optimally route and navigate transportation. E.g. monitoring traffic and re-routing vehicles to avoid congestion.
7. Smart cities - Using data to improve infrastructure and services in a city. E.g. analysing data from sensors to monitor and optimize things like energy usage, water management, emergency response, etc.
8. Precision healthcare - Analysing genetic data and medical records to personalize healthcare treatment and predictions. E.g. personalised drug recommendations based on a patient's DNA, predicting disease risk, etc.
9. Industrial optimization - Analysing machine data to optimize and predict maintenance of equipment. E.g. predicting equipment failures and reducing downtime in industries like manufacturing, oil & gas, aviation, etc.
10. Education - Analysing data to personalize learning and provide tailored feedback for students. E.g. predicting learning needs, providing adaptive learning pathways and evaluating effectiveness.
