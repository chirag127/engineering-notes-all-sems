### Ellipsoid

- An ellipsoid is a surface that can be described as the set of points in 3D space that satisfy a quadratic equation of the form $Ax^2+By^2+Cz^2=1$ where $A,B$ and $C$ are constants.
- If $A=B=C$ then the ellipsoid is spherical, if $A=B\neq C$ then it is circular and if $A\neq B\neq C$ then it is a general ellipsoid.
- The axes of the ellipsoid are the vectors along the x, y and z directions with magnitudes equal to the square roots of $A,B$ and $C$ respectively. The lengths of these axes determine the shape and size of the ellipsoid.
- Some properties of ellipsoids are:
    - They are quadric surfaces as they are defined by a quadratic equation.
    - They are symmetrical about three orthogonal planes.
    - They are closed and bounded.
    - The intersection of a plane and an ellipsoid results in an ellipse.
- Applications of ellipsoids:
    - Modelling 3D objects like balls, eggs, etc.
    - Collision detection.
    - Approximating complex objects.
    - Lens and mirror design.
