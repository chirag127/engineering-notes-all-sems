## Unit 2 - Transformations

1. Translations: shifting an object's location without changing its orientation or size. It can be in x and/or y direction.

Example: Moving an object 5 units right and 3 units up.

Advantage: Used to position objects conveniently for calculations or simulations.

2. Reflections: flipping an object over a line of reflection. It can be over x-axis, y-axis or any arbitrary line.

Example: Flipping a shape over y-axis will result in a mirror image of the shape.

Application: Used to create symmetry and in coding for collision detection.

3. Rotations: spinning an object around a fixed point. Measure in degrees (°) counterclockwise.

Example: Rotating a shape 90° clockwise around its center.

Disadvantage: Angles can be difficult to visualize and calculate.

4. Dilations: resizing an object while proportionally adjusting its shape. It is characterized by a scale factor.

Example: Doubling the size of a shape.

Application: Used to resize images and models as required.
