### Raster Scan Displays

- A raster scan display is a two dimensional array of pixels arranged in rows and columns.
- In this type of display, the beam of an electron gun scans across the screen in a raster fashion, line by line, refreshing the phosphors to emit light and produce an image.
- The intensity of each pixel is determined by the video signal as the beam scans across the screen.
- Raster scan displays are commonly used in TVs and computer monitors.
- Advantages:
    - Inexpensive to produce.
    - Can display complex images with good resolution.
- Disadvantages:
    - Suffers from blurring and distortion, especially for moving images.
    - Resolution is limited by the number of pixels. Increasing resolution significantly increases cost.
