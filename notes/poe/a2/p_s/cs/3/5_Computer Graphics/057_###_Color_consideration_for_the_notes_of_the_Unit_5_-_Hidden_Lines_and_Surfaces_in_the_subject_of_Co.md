2. Some key points to consider while using colors for hidden lines and surfaces are:
- Use contrasting colors for hidden lines and surfaces: Hidden lines can be depicted in lighter shades while surfaces can be shown in darker shades. This contrast makes it easy to distinguish between the hidden elements.
- Use muted or dull colors: Since the hidden elements are not the focus of the graphics, muted or dull colors should be used instead of bright and vivid colors. This ensures that the visible elements get proper attention.
- Consider color schemes: The color scheme used for the overall graphics should be considered while choosing colors for hidden lines and surfaces. The colors should be chosen such that they go well with the color scheme and do not look out of place.
- Consider color conventions: If certain color conventions are followed for depicting hidden elements, then it is better to stick to those conventions. This makes the graphics more comprehensible as the elements can be easily identified.
