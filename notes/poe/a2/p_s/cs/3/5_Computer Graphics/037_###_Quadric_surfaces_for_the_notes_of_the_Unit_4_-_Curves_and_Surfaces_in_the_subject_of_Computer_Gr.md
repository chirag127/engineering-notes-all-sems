### Quadric surfaces for the notes of the Unit 4 - Curves and Surfaces in the subject of Computer Graphics

Quadric surfaces are the surfaces obtained by equating a quadratic polynomial to zero. They are important in Computer Graphics as they are used to model 3D objects. The various types of quadric surfaces are:

1. Ellipsoid: Formed by equating a quadratic equation of the form x^2/a^2 + y^2/b^2 + z^2/c^2 = 1. It is a 3D shape similar to an ellipse in 2D. Examples: Spheres and circular cylinders.
2. Hyperboloid: Formed by equating a quadratic equation of the form x^2/a^2 - y^2/b^2 - z^2/c^2 = 1. It has two sheets that are infinite in extent.
3. Elliptic paraboloid: Formed by equating a quadratic equation of the form x^2/a^2 + y^2/b^2 - z = 0. It is a U-shaped surface. Example: Parabolic reflector.
4. Hyperbolic paraboloid: Formed by equating a quadratic equation of the form x^2/a^2 - y^2/b^2 - z = 0. It is a saddle-shaped surface with a ridge along the z-axis.

Advantages:
- Can represent a wide variety of shapes.
- Mathematical equations are simple to handle.
- Can be tessellated easily for surface rendering.

Disadvantages:
- Difficult to construct complex shapes from only quadric surfaces.
- Mathematical equations can become complex for higher order surfaces.

#### Network devices in Computer Networks

The major network devices are:

1. Repeater: Boosts and regenerates the signal over the same network segment. It is used to extend the length of a network segment.
2. Bridge: Connects two network segments and filters and forwards traffic based on MAC addresses. It increases the size of a network.
3. Router: Connects two or more network segments and filters and forwards traffic based on IP addresses. It is used to connect two or more networks.
4. Switch: Connects network devices and filters and forwards traffic based on MAC addresses. It is used to connect multiple devices in a LAN.
5. Gateway: Connects two networks using different protocols and acts as an entrance and exit point between them. It is used to connect a LAN and a WAN.
6. Firewall: Protects a network from external threats by filtering inbound and outbound traffic based on applied security rules. It is used to protect a LAN or a network.
