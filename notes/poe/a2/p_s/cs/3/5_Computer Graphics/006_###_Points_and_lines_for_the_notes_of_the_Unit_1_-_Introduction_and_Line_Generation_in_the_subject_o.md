#### Goals and applications of networks and protocols

1. Connect devices: The primary goal of computer networks is to connect multiple devices to share resources and communicate with each other. Networks allow devices like computers, printers, servers, etc. to exchange data and access shared resources.
2. Share resources: Networks allow devices to share resources like internet connectivity, storage, computing power, applications, and services. For example, networks enable file sharing, shared printers, and access to server-based resources like emails.
3. Facilitate communication: Networks enable devices to communicate and exchange data. This could be devices within a local network or over the internet. Communication apps, emails, instant messages, video calls, etc. rely on networks and protocols to function.
4. Increase efficiency: Networks increase operational efficiency by allowing centralization of resources and functionalities. For example, storing files on a central server and accessing them from multiple devices increases storage utilization. Centralized management and security also increases efficiency.
5. Enable remote access: Networks enable remote access to resources from any location. This allows employees to access company resources from outside the office and allows you to access your home devices and data from any place with an internet connection.
