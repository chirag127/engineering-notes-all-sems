### Bezier Curves and Surfaces

- Bezier curves and surfaces are a important type of parametric curves and surfaces used in Computer Graphics to create smooth shapes.
- They are defined by a set of control points in space. The curve/surface passes through the first and last control points and is influenced by the other control points.
- The shape of the curve/surface depends on the positions of the control points. By moving the control points, diverse shapes can be created.
- The curve/surface is defined by a blending function that interpolates between the control points. The most common blending function is the Bernstein polynomial.
- The degree of the curve/surface determines the number of control points. Higher the degree, more control points and more flexibility in creating complex shapes.
- Bezier curves are 2D while Bezier surfaces are 3D, created by extending the 2D curves in the U and V parametric directions.
- Properties:
-- Pass through first and last control point
-- Determined by control points
-- Higher degree gives more flexible shapes
-- C2 continuous - curvature is continuous
-- Used in computer animation, CAD, etc.
