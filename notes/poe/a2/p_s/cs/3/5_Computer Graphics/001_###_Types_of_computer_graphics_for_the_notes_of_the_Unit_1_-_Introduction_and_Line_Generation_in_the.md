## Types of Computer Graphics

The major types of computer graphics are:

1. Raster Graphics:
- Raster graphics represents images as a grid of pixels.
- Each pixel has its own color and intensity value.
- Bitmap images and digital photos are examples of raster graphics.
- Raster graphics are resolution dependent and can result in jagged lines and curves if scaled to a higher resolution.
- However, raster graphics produce high quality images and are more photo-realistic.
- Examples: JPEG, PNG, GIF image formats.

2. Vector Graphics:
- Vector graphics represents images using geometrical primitives like points, lines, curves, and shapes.
- Resolution independent and can be scaled to any size without losing quality.
- However, vector graphics are not suitable for photo-realistic images and are more suitable for line drawings, illustrations, etc.
- Examples: SVG, PDF, EPS image formats.

3. 3D Computer Graphics:
- 3D computer graphics make use of 3D modeling techniques to produce 3D images that simulate the appearance of 3D objects.
- Involves concepts like 3D modelling, animation, rendering, etc.
- Used in applications like video games, movies, architectural visualizations, product design, virtual reality, etc.
- Examples: OpenGL, Direct3D, WebGL, etc.
