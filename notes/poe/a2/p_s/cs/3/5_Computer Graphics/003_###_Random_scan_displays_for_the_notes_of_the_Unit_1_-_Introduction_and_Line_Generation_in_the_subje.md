### Random scan displays

- Random scan displays do not display the pixels in a fixed sequential order. The pixels are illuminated in a random pattern.
- This eliminates annoying artifacts like flickering and jagged lines which are visible in raster scan displays.
- However, random scan displays require more complex circuitry and are more expensive to implement.
- Examples of random scan displays are plasma panels and LED displays.
- Advantages: Eliminates flickering and jagged line artifacts. Produces high quality displays.
- Disadvantages: Complex and expensive circuitry. Difficult to implement.
- Applications: Plasma panels, LED displays, etc.
