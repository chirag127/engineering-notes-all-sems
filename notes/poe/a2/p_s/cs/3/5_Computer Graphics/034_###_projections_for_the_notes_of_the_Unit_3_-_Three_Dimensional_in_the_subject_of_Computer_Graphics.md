### Projections for the notes of the Unit 3 - Three Dimensional in the subject of Computer Graphics

- Parallel Projection: Projection of a 3D object onto a plane such that all the projection rays are parallel to each other.
    - Orthographic Projection: Parallel projection with projection rays perpendicular to the projection plane. Results in same scale throughout. Used to get scaled orthographic views of objects.
    - Perspective Projection: Parallel projection with projection rays converging at a single point. Results in smaller scale for farther objects. Gives a 3D effect and sense of depth. Used to simulate how humans view 3D scenes.
- Central Projection: Projection of a 3D object onto a plane such that all the projection rays pass through a single point (the center of projection).
    - Perspective Projection: Central projection with the projection plane perpendicular to the central projection ray.

#### TCP/IP protocol suite in Computer Networks

- TCP/IP is a protocol suite comprising of multiple protocols to facilitate end-to-end communications over Internet Protocol (IP) networks.
- The main protocols in the suite are:
    - Transmission Control Protocol (TCP): Provides reliable, ordered, and error-checked delivery of streamed data over IP networks. Used for applications that require high reliability such as HTTP, SMTP, etc.
    - User Datagram Protocol (UDP): Provides fast but unreliable datagram delivery service over IP networks. Used for applications that require fast delivery such as video streaming, DNS, etc.
    - Internet Protocol (IP): The primary protocol that defines datagram structure and handles addressing and routing of datagrams over IP networks.
- Additional protocols include ICMP, IGMP, ARP, RARP, etc. to perform other network functions.
