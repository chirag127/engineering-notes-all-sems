### Viewing Transformations

For the notes of the Unit 2 - Transformations in the subject of Computer Graphics:

1. Viewing transformation is a transformation that is applied to 3D objects to change the view point. It is used to achieve different viewing positions and orientations to view the 3D scene.
2. There are two viewing transformations -

- Translation - Used to achieve different viewing positions by moving the eyepoint.
- Rotation - Used to achieve different viewing orientations by rotating the camera frame about the view direction.

3. Combination of translation and rotation transformations can be used to achieve different viewing positions and orientations and generate perspective and orthographic projections.
4. Examples of viewing transformations -

- Side view
- Top view
- Front view
- Isometric view
- Perspective view

5. Viewing transformations are helpful to -

- View the 3D objects from different angles.
- Achieve different projections like perspective and orthographic.
- Generate 2D views of 3D scenes for applications like CAD, animation, etc.
- Understand the shape and details of 3D objects better.
