## Unit 1 - Introduction to Data Analytics

1. What is Data Analytics?
- Data Analytics is the process of examining data and identifying patterns to extract insights and draw conclusions to make better decisions.
- It involves applying analytical techniques and tools on raw data to transform it into meaningful information and insights.
- The key steps in data analytics are:
-- Data Collection: Obtaining and storing data from various sources
-- Data Preparation: Cleaning and organizing the data
-- Data Analysis: Applying analytical techniques to understand the data
-- Data Interpretation: Deriving insights and conclusions from the analysis
-- Decision Making: Taking actions based on the insights to solve problems

2. Why is Data Analytics important?
- It helps businesses make data-driven decisions to improve processes, increase revenue, gain competitive advantage, etc.
- It enables personalized and optimized user experiences through recommendations and predictions.
- It powers innovations and discoveries in fields like healthcare, transportation, and artificial intelligence.
- It provides data-backed insights to solve complex problems and influences key strategies and plans.

3. Types of Data Analytics
- Descriptive Analytics: Summarizing and visualizing data to understand what happened
- Diagnostic Analytics: Analyzing data to understand why something happened
- Predictive Analytics: Using data to predict what may happen in the future
- Prescriptive Analytics: Recommending actions to influence future outcomes
