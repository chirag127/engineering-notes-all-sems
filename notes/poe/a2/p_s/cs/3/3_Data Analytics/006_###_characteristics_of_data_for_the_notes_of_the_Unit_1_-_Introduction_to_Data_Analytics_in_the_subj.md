 Here are the characteristics of data for the notes of Unit 1 - Introduction to Data Analytics:

### Volume
- The amount of data available for analysis is huge in data analytics. Data can be in terabytes or petabytes.
- The volume of data keeps increasing rapidly with time due to increase in technologies like social media, IoT devices, etc.
- Analysis of large volumes of data requires efficient ways to store and process the data like distributed systems.

### Velocity
- The speed at which data is generated and processed is very high in data analytics.
- Real-time data needs to be analyzed instantly to enable real-time responses and decisions.
- Fast processing of data requires technologies like stream processing and in-memory databases.

### Variety
- Data comes in all forms like numeric, text, audio, video, images, etc.
- Data can be structured, semi-structured or unstructured.
- Variety of data requires various techniques and tools to store, process and analyze different types of data.

### Veracity
- The quality and accuracy of data are questionable many times leading to uncertainty in decision making.
- Data needs to be cleaned and preprocessed to handle missing values, outliers, inconsistencies, duplications and noise.
- Verifying the veracity or truthfulness of data is critical for a data analytics project.
