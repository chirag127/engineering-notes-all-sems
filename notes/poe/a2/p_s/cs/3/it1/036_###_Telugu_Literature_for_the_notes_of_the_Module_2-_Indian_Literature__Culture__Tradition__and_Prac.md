### Telugu Literature

- Telugu literature refers to the body of works written in the Telugu language. It consists of poems, novels, short stories, dramas, and puranas.
- The earliest literary works in Telugu are the epics and puranas written by the poets in the royal courts of various kingdoms prior to 10th century CE. The historical records suggest that the royal courts of the Satavahanas, Ikshvakus, and the Eastern Chalukyas were famous for their literary contributions.
- The Vijayanagara and Qutb Shahi courts supported Telugu literature during the 14th to 16th centuries. Krishnadevaraya wrote Amuktamalyada in Telugu. Tenali Ramakrishna and Suranna were other notable poets of the Vijayanagara court.
- The Qutb Shahi sultans also patronized Telugu literature. Their court poets include Dhoyi, Peda Tirumalamba and Tallapaka Annamayya who composed sankeertanas and devotionals in Telugu.
- The modern period in Telugu literature is considered to begin with Krishnaswamy Shastri's Yudham (War), published in 1824. Chinnayasuri's Panduranga Mahatyam was the first Telugu novel. Other modern Telugu writers include Gurajada Apparao, Tripuraneni Ramaswamy, Sri Viswanatha Satyanarayana, and Sri Sri.
- Poetry remains a popular genre in Telugu literature. Well known forms such as padam, dwipada, sringara natakam and udyoga natakam were extensively used by poets such as Vemana, Kancherla Gopanna and writers in the 18th–19th centuries.
