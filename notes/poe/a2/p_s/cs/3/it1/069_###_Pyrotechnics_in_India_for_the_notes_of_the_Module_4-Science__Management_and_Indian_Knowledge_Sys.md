Pyrotechnics in India refers to the traditional craft of manufacturing and displaying fireworks in India. It has a long history in Indian culture and traditions. Some key points about Pyrotechnics in India are:

1. The ancient Indian text Arthashastra by Chanakya mentions the use of special burning oil for warfare. This indicates the knowledge of pyrotechnics in ancient India.
2. During festivals and celebrations, fireworks have been an important part of the festivities in India since ancient times. For example, fireworks are burst during Diwali festival.
3. Traditional Indian pyrotechnics include products like anaar (flower pot), chakri (spinning wheel), rocket, patakha (cracker), sutli bomb (string bomb), fuljhadi, etc. These are manufactured using potassium nitrate, charcoal, sulphur and other metals to produce different colors and effects.
4. The hub of traditional pyrotechnics in India is the town of Sivakasi in Tamil Nadu. It is a major producer of fireworks in India since the late 19th century. Around 90% of fireworks and matchboxes in India are produced in Sivakasi.
5. However, the traditional pyrotechnics industry in India faces challenges from the unorganized market and lack of safety norms. There have been several accidents highlighting the need to regulate this industry and implement proper safety measures.
