# INDIAN TRADITIONS, CULTURAL AND SOCIETY

## Indian Family System
- Joint family system: Multiple generations live together in a large family
- Importance of elders and family bonding
- Division of responsibilities among family members
- Mutual cooperation and support

## Indian Marriage System
- Arranged marriages: Matches made by families based on social status, compatibility, etc.
- Dowry system: Bride's family gives money, gifts, property to groom's family
- Wedding rituals: Mangal sutra, seven rounds around fire, tying magalsutra, etc.

## Indian Dressing Style
- Traditional attire like saree, salwar kameez, dhoti, sherwani, etc. based on region, religion, occasion, etc.
- Importance of modesty and covering head/shoulders in some communities and religions

## Indian Cuisine
- Diverse range of regional cuisines like South Indian, Punjabi, Gujarati, Maharashtrian, etc.
- Use of spices and herbs; dishes made of lentils, rice, flatbreads, curries, etc.
- Food connected to culture, religion and rituals; for e.g. fasting during festivals, prasad, etc.
