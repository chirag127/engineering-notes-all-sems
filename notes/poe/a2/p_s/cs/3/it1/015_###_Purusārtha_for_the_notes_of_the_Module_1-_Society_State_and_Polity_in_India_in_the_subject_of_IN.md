### Purusārtha

- Purusārtha refers to the four supreme goals or aims of human life according to Hindu philosophy. They are:

1. Dharma - Righteousness, duty and moral order. Following dharma leads to artha or wealth and happiness in life and next life.

2. Artha - Material prosperity and wealth. Earning artha should be through righteous means and for righteous purposes.

3. Kāma - Desire and pleasure. Kāma should be fulfilled in a controlled manner without transgressing dharma and artha.

4. Moksha - Liberation from cycle of birth and death. Moksha is achieved through spiritual practice and knowledge.

- These four Purusārthas provide a holistic framework to lead an ideal life that balances material prosperity and desires with righteousness and liberation. Achieving a harmonious balance between the four Purusārthas is key to happiness and well-being according to Hindu philosophy.

- The hierarchy of Purusārthas places Dharma as the foremost goal. Artha and Kāma are subordinate to Dharma. Moksha is the ultimate goal.
