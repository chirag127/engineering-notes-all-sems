### Malayalam Literature

- Malayalam literature refers to the body of literature created in the Malayalam language. It includes Malayalam poetry, prose, and drama. Malayalam literature started in the 13th century with the poet Ramban.
- The earliest extant literary works in Malayalam are the poems composed by the poet Ramban in the 15th century. The manipravalam meter was the dominant verse form during this period. Ravivarma Thampuran of the Travancore royal family and Unnayi Warrier are two important literary figures from this period.
- The modern period of Malayalam literature began in the late 19th century. Kerala Varma Valiya Koi Thampuran was a pioneer of modern prose literature in Malayalam. Other prominent figures of modern literature include Kumaran Asan, Vallathol Narayana Menon, Ulloor S. Parameswara Iyer, etc.
- The major literary form in Malayalam is poetry. It includes famous forms like Manipravalam, Ottanthullal, Koodiyattam, and Kathakali. Modern poetry is influenced by Romantic and Victorian poetry. The epics of Kerala including Ramacharitam also form an important part of Malayalam literature.
- Some important works in Malayalam literature include:
- Adhyatma Ramayana by Thunchaththu Ezhuthachan
- Krishnagatha by Cherusseri Namboothiri
- Kailasamatha Kalyanam by Unnayi Warrier
- Vrittantam by Kumaran Asan
- Chandrotsavam by Kumaran Asan
- Veena Poovu by Changampuzha Krishna Pillai
- Ente Gurunathan by Thoppil Bhasi
