## Indian's Cultural Contribution to the World

### 1. Yoga and Meditation
Yoga and meditation originated in ancient India. Yoga incorporates physical postures, breathing exercises, and meditation or relaxation. It has become popular all over the world as a form of exercise and stress relief. Meditation is a practice of focused attention that many people use to reduce stress and increase relaxation, awareness and wellbeing.

### 2. Ayurveda
Ayurveda is a system of traditional medicine that has originated in India. It uses a comprehensive holistic approach that incorporates herbal remedies, dietary advice, massage, yoga, meditation and other practices. Ayurveda has become popular as an alternative medicine system worldwide. Its practices are being incorporated into mainstream medical treatments.

### 3. Indian Cuisine and Spices
Indian cuisine and spices have become popular all over the world. Many Indian spices like turmeric, red chili, cumin, coriander, etc. have medicinal properties and are used in cuisines globally. Dishes like curry, biryani, naan, etc. have become popular internationally. The vibrant flavours, aroma and health benefits of Indian spices and cuisine are appreciated worldwide.

### 4. Indian Classical Music and Dance
Indian classical music has a long tradition and is based on melody and rhythm. It uses a variety of instruments like the sitar, sarod, tabla, etc. Indian classical dance forms like Bharatanatyam, Kathak, Odissi, Kuchipudi, etc. are gaining global recognition. They showcase a synthesis of rhythmic footwork, hand gestures and facial expressions to depict spiritual themes and stories.

### 5. Indian Textiles and Fashion
Indian textiles like cotton, silk, etc. and patterns like bandhani, ikat, etc. have gained worldwide popularity. Indian fashion designs incorporating traditional textiles, colors and embroidery are influencing international fashion. The diversity of Indian textiles and styles reflects the country's rich cultural heritage.
