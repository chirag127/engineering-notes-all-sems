### Puranas

- Puranas are ancient Indian religious texts which contain narrative accounts of cosmology, genealogy, myths, legends and other traditional knowledge.
- There are 18 Mahapuranas which highlight the essence of the 4 Vedas and are devoted to a particular deity - Vishnu, Shiva or Devi. Some of the major Puranas are:
    - Bhagavata Purana - focuses on Vishnu & Krishna, contains 10,000 slokas
    - Vishnu Purana - focuses on Vishnu, contains around 23,000 slokas
    - Shiva Purana - focuses on Shiva, contains around 24,000 slokas
    - Markandeya Purana - includes Devi Mahatmya, an important text dedicated to Goddess Durga, contains around 9,000 slokas
- Puranas typically contain 5 main topics known as Panchalakshana - Sarga (cosmogony), Pratisarga (secondary creation), Manvantara (genealogy of Manus), Vamsha (dynastic history) and Vamshanucharita (stories of royal dynasties).
- Puranas are important as they highlight the key beliefs, practices and essence of Hinduism in an easy to understand format through stories and legends. They have acted as a bridge between the scholarly Vedas and the common people, thereby helping in the preservation and propagation of Hindu culture and traditions.
