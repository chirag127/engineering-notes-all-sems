## Module 3- Indian Religion, Philosophy, and Practices

- Indian religion and philosophy dominated ancient and medieval Indian culture. This module will explore core beliefs and practices of major Indian religions including:
- Hinduism: Based on ancient Vedic tradition; centered on Brahman as the eternal, infinite, omnipresent, omniscient, omnipotent origin, that creates, preserves and dissolves all existence. Primary deities include Brahma, Vishnu, Shiva. Core beliefs: samsara, karma, dharma. Practice of meditation, yoga, and worship. Sacred texts include Vedas, Upanishads, Bhagavad Gita.
- Buddhism: Founded by Siddhartha Gautama (Buddha) around 500 BCE. Core beliefs: Life is suffering caused by desire and attachment; path to liberation is through moral behavior, meditation and wisdom. Four Noble Truths, Eightfold Path.practice of meditation.
- Jainism: Founded by Mahavira around 500 BCE. Core beliefs: Strict non-violence (ahimsa) and non-harm to all life. Karma and rebirth driven by actions and intentions. Path to liberation through asceticism and meditation. Primary practices include non-attachment, fasting, and meditation.
- Sikhism: Founded by Guru Nanak in 15th century. Core beliefs: There is one God (Ik Onkar) who is omnipresent and infinite. All people can achieve liberation through meditation on God, living an honest life, treating others equally. Primary practices include devotion, prayer, meditation, honest conduct and livelihood while living as part of a community.
