### Engineering and Architecture in Ancient India

- Ancient India has witnessed remarkable achievements in engineering and architecture. Some of the notable examples are:
- **Harappan Civilization (3300 BCE - 1300 BCE):** Well planned cities with advanced sanitation and drainage systems. Granaries and public baths. Baked bricks used for construction.
- **Mauryan Period (322 BCE - 185 BCE):** Ashoka built Royal Highway and many stupas, chaityagrihas and viharas. Rock edicts of Ashoka engraved on natural rock formations.
- **Gupta Period (320 CE - 550 CE):** Notable achievements in temple and palace architecture. Use of burnt bricks and mortar. Stone was also used. Temples were often made of brick with terracotta ornamentation.
- **Pallava Dynasty (275 CE - 897 CE):** Monolithic temples out of single rocks were constructed. Shore temple at Mahabalipuram is an example. It is a structural temple, built with blocks of granite, carved with sculptures and reliefs.
- **Chola Dynasty (300 BCE - 1279 CE):** Magnificent temples with towering gopurams were constructed. Brihadeeswara temple at Thanjavur is a finest example built by Raja Raja Chola I. It is an architectural and engineering marvel with its massive proportions and complexity.
- The ancient Indian engineers and architects had good knowledge of mechanics, metallurgy and construction techniques with locally available materials. Their creations have stood the test of time which demonstrates the skills and expertise they possessed. The monuments are an enduring legacy of the cultural heritage of India.
