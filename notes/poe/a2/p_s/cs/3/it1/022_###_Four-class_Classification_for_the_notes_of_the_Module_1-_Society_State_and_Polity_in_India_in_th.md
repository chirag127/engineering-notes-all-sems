### Four-class Classification for the notes of the Module 1- Society State and Polity in India in the subject of INDIAN TRADITIONS, CULTURAL AND SOCIETY

The Indian society and polity has been classified into four classes in ancient Indian texts like Manusmriti -

1. Brahmins - The priests and teachers who were placed at the top of the social hierarchy. They were responsible for religious duties and education.

2. Kshatriyas - The warriors and rulers who were responsible for administration, military and governance. They came after Brahmins in the social hierarchy.

3. Vaishyas - The traders and merchants who were involved in economic and business activities. They came after Kshatriyas in the social hierarchy.

4. Shudras - The laborers and service providers who were involved in serving the other three classes. They came at the bottom of the social hierarchy.

This four-fold classification was based on the functions and responsibilities of different sections of the society. However, this classification later got associated with the rigid social stratification and caste system in India which was based on birth and discouraged social mobility. The four-class system is no longer prevalent in its original form today but the legacy of social inequality still persists in the Indian society.
