### Harappan Technologies

The Harappan civilization was quite advanced in terms of their technological capabilities:

1. Town Planning and Architecture: The Harappans exhibited a highly evolved sense of town planning and architecture. Their cities were built on a grid system with well-laid out roads, drainage system and water supply system. The buildings were made of baked bricks of a standardized size.

2. Craft Specialization: There was a high degree of craft specialization as evident from the archaeological finds like Seal engravers, bead makers, brick makers, etc. This indicates a complex division of labour and trade.

3. Irrigation and Agriculture: The Harappans had a good irrigation system and agricultural practices. They grew wheat, barley, sesame, peas, dates, etc. Ploughs have been found indicating use of agriculture.

4. metallurgy: The Harappans knew the use of metals like copper, bronze, lead and tin and engaged in smelting and manufacturing objects from these metals. They even engaged in making objects like razors from copper.

5. Terracotta and Pottery: The Harappans produced a variety of terracotta and pottery objects with black and red ware, depicting a high sense of aesthetics. The pottery was made on wheel and in a variety of shapes and sizes indicating their skill.

6. Seals and Weights: The Harappans manufactured a variety of seals and weights made of stone, faience and terra-cotta. The seals were mainly used for trading purposes to stamp bags or goods. The weights were standardized and were used in calculating values.

7. Cotton and Fabric: The Harappans knew the cultivation of cotton and manufacture of cotton fabric. Spindle whorls have been found indicating that they knew the use of spinning wheel to spin cotton and weave fabric.

In conclusion, the Harappan civilization had developed various technologies in town planning, metallurgy, irrigation, craft specialization, etc which indicate their highly advanced knowledge and skills. These technologies were quite remarkable for their time and some like town planning are still inspiring today. The technological capabilities contributed to the growth and longevity of the Harappan civilization.
