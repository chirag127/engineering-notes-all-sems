 Here is the content in markdown format for the topic ### Astronomy in India:

1. Indian astronomy has a long history stretching from pre-historic to modern times. Some of the earliest roots of Indian astronomy can be dated to the Indus Valley Civilization or earlier.
2. The earliest text which refers to astronomy and astrology is the Rig Veda which is dated to the 2nd millennium BCE. Later developments in Indian astronomy occurred in the classical period variously dated from the 3rd century BCE to 2nd century CE. This period saw the rise of the Indian after the Greek and Hellenistic astronomy.
3. The classical Indian astronomy flowered in the Gupta era (4th to 6th centuries CE), during this period Indian astronomy was closely connected with Indian astrology where the same teacher was an astronomer and an astrologer. Some of the notable ancient Indian astronomers were Aryabhata, Brahmagupta, and Bhaskara II.
4. Aryabhata in the 5th century stated that the Earth was spherical and rotated on its axis which led to the alternation of day and night. He accurately calculated many astronomical constants, planetary positions and eclipses. Brahmagupta in the 7th century gave the famous Brahmagupta's theorem. Bhaskara II in the 12th century was the first to discover infinite descending chain of zeros in the decimal numerical system.
5. The Indian astronomy reached its zenith in the medieval period and the chief astronomers were Madhava of Sangamagrama, Nilakantha Somayaji, and Jyeshtadeva. The 12th century saw the rise of Kerala school of astronomy and mathematics. They made important discoveries in calculus and number theory.
6. After the classical period, Islamic and European astronomical traditions started influencing Indian astronomy. In the modern period, Jai Singh built observatories including the Jaipur observatory in the 18th century. In the early 20th century, Indian astronomy was studied at institutes like Ujjain, Pune and Kodaikanal observatories. The modern Indian astronomy is focused on cosmology and astrophysics.
