#### 1. Samkhya Philosophy:
- Proposed by Kapila Muni.
- Dualistic philosophy. Separates Purusha (consciousness) and Prakriti (primordial matter).
- Three Gunas - Sattva, Rajas, and Tamas. Explains how these Gunas interact to create the world.
- Liberation occurs when Purusha realizes its distinction from Prakriti.
- No place for God in this philosophy.

#### 2. Yoga Philosophy:
- Proposed by Patanjali.
- Eight limbs of yoga - Yama, Niyama, Asana, Pranayama, Pratyahara, Dharana, Dhyana, Samadhi.
- Liberation is through the disciplined practice of eight limbs of yoga which lead to the attainment of Samadhi (ultimate union of individual consciousness with universal consciousness).
- God is merely an efficient cause but not the material cause of the world.
