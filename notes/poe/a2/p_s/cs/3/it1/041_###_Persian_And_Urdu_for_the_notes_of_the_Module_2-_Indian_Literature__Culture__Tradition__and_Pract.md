### Persian And Urdu

- Persian was the court language during the Mughal rule in India. Many Persian words got incorporated into Hindi and other Indian languages.
- Urdu evolved from the Apabhraṃśa register of preceding Shauraseni language and is the ancestor of the modern Hindi language.
- Urdu has Persian vocabulary and Arabic influences due to the spread of Islam in South Asia.
- Many literary works such as poems, novels, short stories, plays were composed in Persian and Urdu. Examples: Rubaiyat of Omar Khayyam (Persian), Masnavi of Rumi (Persian), Diwan-e Shams-e Tabrizi (Persian), Shah Jo Risalo of Shah Abdul Latif Bhittai (Sindhi-Urdu).
- Famous Urdu poets: Mirza Ghalib, Muhammad Iqbal, Faiz Ahmad Faiz. Famous Persian poets: Rumi, Hafez, Omar Khayyam, Amir Khusraw.
- Calligraphy and ornamental designs using Arabic-Persian scripts are an important part of Persian and Urdu artistic traditions. Nastaliq is a popular calligraphic style for Urdu.
- Persian and Urdu languages have made significant contributions to the cultural heritage of South Asia. They have influenced literature, art, architecture, music as well as other aspects of life in the Indian subcontinent.
