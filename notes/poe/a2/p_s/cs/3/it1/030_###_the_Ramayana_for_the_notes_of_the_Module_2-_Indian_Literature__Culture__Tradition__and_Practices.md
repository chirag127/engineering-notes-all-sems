### The Ramayana

- The Ramayana is one of the two major Sanskrit epics of ancient India, the other being the Mahabharata.
- It was composed by the sage Valmiki around 500 BCE.
- The Ramayana narrates the story of Rama, the seventh avatar of the god Vishnu.
- It follows his wife Sita being abducted by the demon king Ravana and Rama's subsequent journey to rescue her.
- Along with the Mahabharata, it forms the Hindu Itihasa.
- The Ramayana consists of 24,000 verses in seven books (kandas) and 500 cantos (sargas), and tells the story of Rama, whose wife Sita is abducted by the demon (Rakshasa) king Ravana.
- The epic poem Ramayana is not just a story, but an integral part of Hinduism which provides spiritual, religious and philosophical foundations to the lives of millions of Hindus.
- The Ramayana teaches importance of values and duties in human life through the righteous path of truth, love, humility and sacrifice as shown by Lord Rama.
