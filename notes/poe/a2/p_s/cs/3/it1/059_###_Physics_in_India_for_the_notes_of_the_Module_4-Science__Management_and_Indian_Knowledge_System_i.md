### Physics in Ancient India

- Indian physicists made significant contributions to the field of physics, especially in the areas of optics, mechanics, and astronomy.
- Some of the important physicists and their contributions:

**Aryabhata (476 CE)** - Proposed that the earth is spherical and rotates on its axis. Calculated value of pi to 4 decimal places. Proposed theories on eclipses and the planets.
**Brahmagupta (598 CE)** - Proposed laws of gravitation and mechanics. Discovered methods to calculate the circumference and area of a circle. Proposed a method to calculate the position of planets.
**Bhaskara II (1114 CE)** - Discovered concepts of differential calculus and acceleration. Proposed proof that the earth is spherical, not flat. Discovered methods to calculate astronomical phenomena and planetary motion.

- Key achievements in ancient Indian physics include theories on:

- Motion (including concepts of velocity and acceleration)
- Gravitation (including concept of gravitational force and proof that earth is spherical)
- Mechanics (including dynamics and statics)
- Optics (including theory that light travels in straight lines)
- Astronomy (including planetary motion calculations and eclipse predictions)
