## Module 5- Cultural Heritage and Performing Arts

- Cultural heritage refers to the legacy of physical artifacts and intangible attributes of a group or society that are inherited from past generations, maintained in the present, and bestowed for the benefit of future generations. This includes tangible culture (such as buildings, monuments, landscapes, books, works of art, and artifacts), intangible culture (such as folklore, traditions, language, and knowledge), and natural heritage (including culturally significant landscapes, and biodiversity).
- The performing arts are a form of art in which artists use their voices, bodies or inanimate objects to convey artistic expression. It includes a range of disciplines which are performed in front of a live audience, including theatre, music, and dance. Performing arts range from vocal and instrumental music, dance and theatre to puppetry and performance art. Artists who participate in performing arts in front of an audience are called performers.
- Types of cultural heritage: Archaeological sites, historic districts, monuments, ancient structures, heritage sites, intangible culture heritage like oral traditions, performing arts, social practices, rituals, festive events, knowledge and practices concerning nature and the universe.
- Types of performing arts: Theatre (drama, opera, musical theatre), Dance (many genres exist), Music ( both vocal and instrumental), Magic, Circus arts, Puppetry, etc.
- Importance: Cultural heritage and performing arts provide a sense of identity by preserving history, representing cultural values and encouraging cultural diversity. They boost tourism, education and preserve ancient traditions and skills. They entertain, inspire, provoke thought and share emotions.
- However, cultural heritage sites are under threat due to neglect, lack of awareness, unplanned urban development, natural disasters, thefts and wars. Performing arts are challenged by lack of funds and audiences due to increased TV and digital media. Conservation efforts and promoting awareness can help address these challenges.
