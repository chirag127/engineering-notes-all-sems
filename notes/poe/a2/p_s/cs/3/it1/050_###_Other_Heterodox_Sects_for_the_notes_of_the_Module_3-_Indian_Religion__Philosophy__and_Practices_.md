 Here are the notes on ### Other Heterodox Sects for the Module 3- Indian Religion, Philosophy, and Practices in the subject of INDIAN TRADITIONS, CULTURAL AND SOCIETY:

I. Ajivikas:
- Followed by Makkhali Gosala, a contemporary of Mahavira and Buddha.
- Believed in determinism and fatalism. They denied the efficacy of human effort and karma.
- Followed strict asceticism and austerity. They believed that the cycles of rebirth can be escaped not through effort but through passive endurance and non-reaction.
- Almost disappeared now.

II. Charvakas:
- Materialists who rejected the idea of an afterlife or immortality of the soul.
- Believed that nothing exists except matter and the universe is the result of the accident.
- Rejected the Vedas as superstitions and emphasized on seeking happiness in this life through pleasure.
- Criticized religious practices and asceticism as vain and opposed sacrifices and rituals.
- Had little impact and their texts are almost lost now.

III. Sceptics:
- Questioned the possibility of attaining true knowledge and doubted all assertion or propositions.
- Rejected logic as the path to truth considering contradiction inherent in thought and language.
- Did not form any sect or school but only expressed doubts regarding various beliefs and doctrines.
- Played a significant role in stimulating debates and questioning assumptions which enriched Indian philosophy.
