Puppetry is a form of theatre or performance that involves the manipulation of puppets - inanimate objects, often resembling some type of human or animal figure, that are animated or manipulated by a puppeteer. Such a performance is often combined with music. The puppets are designed and created by puppeteers and they manipulate them by hand or by using rods or strings.

- Puppetry is an ancient form of storytelling and entertainment which was popular in India since the 12th century.
- The traditional Indian puppet theatre is known as Kathputli. Kathputli literally means "wooden dolls" which are manipulated through strings or rods to enact a play.
- The puppeteers are known as kathputliwala who speak and sing through the puppets. They convey social messages, narrate popular legends and enact folklores through the puppets.
- The puppets are made of wood and textile with joints that allow free movement of limbs. They are dressed in colourful attires and make-up. The dimensions of the puppets vary based on the character they represent.
- The most prominent styles of Indian puppetry are:
-- Rajasthani puppetry: Performances depict local legends and folk tales using ornate puppets supported by live music, songs and dance.
-- Bengali puppetry: Puppet performances narrate mythological and social stories and are accompanied by traditional music and songs. Leather puppets painted in vegetable dyes are used.
-- Andhra and Tamil puppetry: The puppeteers are known as Sendhavatu. The shows enact legends and myths using large wooden rod puppets and are accompanied by songs and music.

Advantages:
- It is an effective way to convey moral lessons and educate people especially in rural areas.
- It preserves the traditional art forms and folk culture through puppet making and performances.
- It provides a source of livelihood to the puppeteers and people involved in making the puppets.

Disadvantages:
- The traditional puppet theatre is declining due to the influence of television and digital media.
- Lack of patronage and funds have made it difficult for the puppeteers to sustain the art.
