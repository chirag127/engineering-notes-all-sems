### Geography

- Geography is the study of the physical features of the earth and its atmosphere, and the distribution of life on the earth, including human populations and resources.
- The Indian traditions and knowledge systems have a profound understanding of geography based on practical experiences and observations of nature and environment.
- Some key aspects of geography as per Indian traditions are:

* Classification of geographical regions (desha) based on rivers, mountains, vegetation, etc. For ex. Kuru, Panchala, Gandhara, etc.
* Understanding of spatial units like village, city, state, country and relationships between them. Village was the basic spatial unit.
* Understanding of ecological zones like mountains, forests, plains, coasts, etc. and their resources and occupations. Different regions had different primary occupations based on geography.
* Understanding of seasons, solar/lunar phases and their effect on climate, agriculture, water, etc. This reflects a nuanced understanding of time-space relationships.
* Navigation techniques using stars, understanding of winds, ocean currents, etc. This exhibits knowledge of physical geography and its application.
* Understanding of availability of natural resources across different regions and their sustainable use. This reflects a holistic view of environment and development.

The above aspects highlight how Indian traditions and knowledge systems had a comprehensive understanding of geography and applied this knowledge for sustainable living. The modern study of geography can benefit by incorporating insights from these knowledge systems.
