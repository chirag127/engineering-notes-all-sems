### Northern Indian Languages

- Hindi: Official language of India. spoken by nearly 425 million people. Devnagari script. Rich literature including poetry, novels, short stories, drama, etc.
- Punjabi: Spoken by nearly 100 million people in Punjab region. Gurmukhi script. Literature includes poetry, stories, and religious texts.
- Urdu: Official language of Pakistan. spoken by nearly 100 million people. Nastaliq script. Literature includes poetry like ghazals, nazms, novels, short stories, etc.
- Bengali: Official language of West Bengal. Spoken by nearly 250 million people. Bengali script. Literature includes poetry, novels, short stories, drama, etc. Nobel laureate Rabindranath Tagore wrote in Bengali.
- Other languages: Maithili, Magahi, Bhojpuri, etc.

### Northern Indian Literature

- Earliest literature include Vedas and Upanishads composed in Sanskrit.
- Epics Ramayana and Mahabharata composed in Sanskrit by Valmiki and Vyasa respectively.
- Classical Sanskrit literature flourished from 3rd century BCE to 8th century CE. Kalidasa, Bhavabhuti, etc. contributed to plays, poetry, prose, etc.
- Bhakti movement from 15th century emphasized devotional poetry in vernacular languages. Poets like Mirabai, Surdas, Tulsidas, Kabir, etc. composed in languages like Hindi, Punjabi, etc.
- Mughal period: Flourishing of Urdu, Persian literature. Poets like Ghalib, Iqbal, etc. Ghazal poetry popular.
- Modern literature: Short stories and novels gained popularity. Premchand, Sarat Chandra in Hindi. Rabindranath Tagore in Bengali.
- Other contributions: Amrita Pritam in Punjabi. Maithili Sharan Gupt in Hindi. Bankim Chandra in Bengali.
