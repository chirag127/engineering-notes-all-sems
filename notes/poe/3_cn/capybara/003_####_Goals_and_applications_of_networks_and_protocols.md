#### Goals and applications of networks and protocols

A network is a collection of devices that are connected together to allow communication between them. Networks allow people to communicate and share information, which is why they play a crucial role in our daily lives. Protocols are the set of rules and procedures that govern the way devices communicate with each other.

The main goals and applications of networks and protocols are:

1. Communication: The primary goal of networks and protocols is to enable communication between devices. Networks allow devices to send and receive data, voice, and video from one location to another. Protocols ensure that devices can communicate with each other seamlessly without any interference.

2. Resource sharing: Networks and protocols allow resources such as printers, scanners, and storage devices to be shared among multiple devices. This is especially useful in offices and homes where multiple people need access to the same resources.

3. Data security: Networks and protocols provide data security by encrypting data that is transmitted between devices. This ensures that the data cannot be intercepted by unauthorized users.

4. Remote access: Networks and protocols allow users to access resources and data from remote locations. This is especially useful for people who work from home or are traveling.

5. Entertainment: Networks and protocols allow people to stream music, videos, and movies from the internet. This is possible due to the high-speed internet connections that are provided by networks.

6. E-commerce: Networks and protocols are used for e-commerce transactions, such as online shopping and banking. They ensure that the transactions are secure and the information is encrypted.

7. Social media: Social media platforms such as Facebook, Twitter, and Instagram are made possible due to networks and protocols. They allow people to connect with each other and share information.
