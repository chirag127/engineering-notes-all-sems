#### Categories of networks in computer networks

Computer networks are mainly categorized into three categories, which are based on their geographical coverage, architecture, and ownership. These categories are:

1. Local Area Network (LAN)
2. Metropolitan Area Network (MAN)
3. Wide Area Network (WAN)

### Local Area Network (LAN)

A Local Area Network (LAN) is a network that covers a small geographical area, such as a home, office, or building. The LAN network connects devices like computers, printers, and servers, within a limited area to share resources like data, software, and hardware.

#### Advantages of LAN
- Fast data transfer rate
- Sharing of resources
- High security
- Easy to set up and manage

#### Disadvantages of LAN
- Limited coverage area
- Expensive to set up
- Limited scalability

#### Examples of LAN
- Home networks
- School networks
- Office networks

#### Applications of LAN
- File sharing
- Printer sharing
- Internet sharing
- Video conferencing

#### Mnemonic
LAN stands for Local Area Network, which is a network that is "local" to a small area like a home or office.

### Metropolitan Area Network (MAN)

A Metropolitan Area Network (MAN) is a network that covers a larger geographical area, such as a city or town. The MAN network connects Local Area Networks (LANs) within a metropolitan area to share resources like data, software, and hardware.

#### Advantages of MAN
- High-speed data transfer rate
- Sharing of resources
- Cost-effective
- Easy to set up and manage

#### Disadvantages of MAN
- Limited coverage area
- Limited scalability
- Expensive to set up

#### Examples of MAN
- Cable TV networks
- Telephone networks
- Municipal networks

#### Applications of MAN
- Interconnecting LANs
- Internet access
- Video conferencing

#### Mnemonic
MAN stands for Metropolitan Area Network, which is a network that covers a "metropolitan" area like a city or town.

### Wide Area Network (WAN)

A Wide Area Network (WAN) is a network that covers a wide geographical area, such as a country or the entire world. The WAN network connects computers and devices across long distances to share resources like data, software, and hardware.

#### Advantages of WAN
- Wide coverage area
- High-speed data transfer rate
- Sharing of resources
- Low-cost

#### Disadvantages of WAN
- Security issues
- Slow data transfer rate
- Complex to set up and manage

#### Examples of WAN
- Internet
- Company networks
- Banking networks

#### Applications of WAN
- Email
- Online shopping
- Social media

#### Mnemonic
WAN stands for Wide Area Network, which is a network that covers a "wide" area like a country or the entire world.

In conclusion, understanding the different categories of computer networks is crucial for network administrators and technicians. It helps in the efficient management, configuration, and troubleshooting of network issues.
