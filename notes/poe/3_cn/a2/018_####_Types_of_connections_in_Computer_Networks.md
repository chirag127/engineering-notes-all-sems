#### Types of connections in Computer Networks

The major types of connections used in computer networks are:

1. Point-to-point connection:
- Direct connection between two endpoints.
- Used for simplicity in small networks.
- Example: Direct serial cable connection between two computers.

Mnemonic: Point-to-point, two endpoints, simple and direct.

2. Multipoint connection:
- More than two endpoints connected in a network.
- Includes technologies like Ethernet, Wi-Fi, etc.
- Examples: LAN, WAN, Wi-Fi network, etc. connecting multiple computers.

Mnemonic: Multi-point, multi-computer network.

3. Circuit-switched connection:
- A dedicated end-to-end path is established before transmission starts.
- Example: Traditional telephone network where a call is established first before communication.

Mnemonic: First circuit then switch to communication.

4. Packet-switched connection:
- Data is transmitted in packets that can take different routes to reach the destination.
- Packets are reassembled at the destination in the correct order.
- Examples: Ethernet, Internet, etc.

Mnemonic: Split into packets, take any path, reassemble at destination.
