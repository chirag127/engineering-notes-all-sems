#### Network devices in Computer Networks

The major network devices facilitate the communication between devices in a computer network. Here are the key network devices along with some mnemonics and learning tricks to remember them:

1. Repeater: A repeater receives a digital signal and retransmits it at a higher level or higher power, so it can cover longer distances.
Mnemonic: Re- amplifies or Re- energises the signal.

2. Hub: A hub is a central connecting device that connects multiple devices in a star topology. It receives data packets from any connected device and broadcasts them to all other devices.
Mnemonic: Hub is where everything comes and goes (in a star network).

3. Bridge: A bridge connects two network segments and filters and forwards data packets between the segments based on MAC addresses. It helps reduce network traffic.
Mnemonic: Bridge over troubled traffic (it helps ease network traffic congestion between segments).

4. Router: A router determines the best path for data packets and forwards them between networks based on IP addresses. It helps connect multiple networks and route traffic efficiently.
Mnemonic: Route is the key (to connect networks and route traffic).

5. Switch: A switch filters and forwards data packets between network devices based on MAC addresses. It helps reduce network congestion by directing traffic only to intended destinations.
Mnemonic: Switch gives the right direction (to network traffic).

The key points to remember are:
1) Repeaters reamplify signals, hubs connect devices in a star topology
2) Bridges connect network segments and filter traffic based on MAC addresses
3) Routers connect networks and route traffic based on IP addresses
4) Switches filter and forward traffic to intended destinations based on MAC addresses
