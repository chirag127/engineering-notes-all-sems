## Unit 1 - Introductory Concepts of Computer Networks and Physical Layer

- Computer Network: A computer network is a group of computers that are interconnected and able to communicate with each other to share resources and data. Networks are classified based on scale (LAN, MAN, WAN), topology (bus, star, ring), and protocol (TCP/IP, UDP, FTP).
- Networks enable communication, access to remote resources, and sharing of resources like files/printers.
- Benefits of networking: Resource sharing, Communication, Access to information, Cost effectiveness.
- Components of a network: Computers/Devices, Communication medium (wired/wireless), Network devices (routers, switches, etc.).
- Physical layer: Deals with transmission of raw bit streams over a physical medium. It specifies mechanical, electrical, and functional requirements of the medium and the interfaces. Some concepts: Bandwidth, Throughput, Latency, Multiplexing (FDM, TDM), Error control (parity, CRC).
- Mnemonics: All People Seem To Need Data Processing (layers: Application, Presentation, Session, Transport, Network, Data Link, Physical)
- Learning tips: Understand the need for each layer, Learn functions of each layer, Practice identifying layers used in applications.
