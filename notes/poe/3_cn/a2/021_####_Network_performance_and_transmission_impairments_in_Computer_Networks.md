#### Network performance and transmission impairments in Computer Networks

The performance of a computer network depends on various factors like -

1. Bandwidth - The amount of data that can be transmitted through a network connection in a given amount of time. Higher bandwidth leads to faster data transfer speeds.
2. Latency - The time taken for a packet of data to be transmitted from one point to another. Lower latency means shorter delay and faster response times.
3. Jitter - Variation in the latency of received packets. Excessive jitter can affect the quality of streaming media.
4. Lost packets - Packets of data that do not arrive at the destination. This can lead to retransmission of packets and lower throughput.

The transmission of data over a network can face various impairments like -

1. Attenuation - The weakening of a signal over distance due to absorption and dispersion. This can lead to reduced signal strength and errors in data transmission.
2. Interference - Disruption of a signal by external signals leading to corrupted data. This can be due to natural phenomena or other devices using the same frequency band.
3. Noise - Random fluctuations in a signal that can interfere with data transmission. This can be due to natural phenomena or devices and lead to errors.

Mnemonics -

Bandwidth - Fat pipes can carry more water (data)
Latency - Waiting time, the slower the snail (packet) the longer the tale (delay)

To minimize impairments -

1. Use appropriate cable types and shielding.
2. Maintain proper distance between devices and cables.
3. Use error correction and detection techniques.
4. Choose frequencies with less interference.
5. Use modulation techniques suited for the medium.
