
### IPv6 in cn
- IPv6 is the sixth version of the Internet Protocol, and is the successor to IPv4. It allows for a larger address space, improved security, and better performance.
- IPv6 addresses are 128-bit numbers, written in 8 groups of 4 hexadecimal digits.
- IPv6 addresses can be represented in several different ways, including:
  - Full Address: 2001:0db8:85a3:0000:0000:8a2e:0370:7334
  - Compressed Address: 2001:db8:85a3::8a2e:370:7334
  - IPv4-Mapped Address: ::ffff:192.0.2.128
- IPv6 supports a number of different types of addresses, including unicast, multicast, and anycast.
- IPv6 supports a number of different protocols, such as ICMPv6, DHCPv6, and Neighbor Discovery Protocol (NDP).
- IPv6 has a number of advantages over IPv4, including:
  - A larger address space, which allows for more devices to be connected to the internet.
  - Improved security, with built-in authentication and encryption.
  - Better performance, with lower latency and higher throughput.
- IPv6 is the future of the internet, and it is important to be familiar with the protocol in order to understand how the internet works.
