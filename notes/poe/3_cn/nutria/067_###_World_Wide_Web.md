
### World Wide Web

The World Wide Web (WWW) is a system of interlinked hypertext documents accessed via the Internet. It is a vast collection of webpages that can be accessed by anyone with an internet connection. It is used for a variety of purposes, including communication, entertainment, research, and business.

Mnemonic: WWW stands for World Wide Web

Learning Tricks: The three W's stand for World Wide Web.

Advantages:
- Easy to access information from anywhere in the world
- Offers a wide range of services such as communication, entertainment, research, and business
- Allows users to access multimedia content such as videos, images, and audio
- Allows users to interact with each other through social media
- Provides a platform for businesses to advertise their products and services

Disadvantages:
- Can be difficult to find relevant information
- Can be difficult to verify the accuracy of information
- Can be vulnerable to cyber attacks
- Can be used to spread false information
- Can be used for illegal activities such as hacking and fraud
