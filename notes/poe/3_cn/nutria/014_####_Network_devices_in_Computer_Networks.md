
#### Network Devices in Computer Networks

Network devices are hardware components that enable communication between two or more computers. They are essential for establishing and maintaining a computer network. Common network devices include:

* Routers: Routers are used to connect two or more networks and route data packets between them. They are responsible for deciding which path data should take to reach its destination.

* Switches: Switches are used to connect multiple computers and other devices to a single network. They receive data from the source and forward it to the intended destination.

* Hubs: Hubs are used to connect multiple computers and other devices to a single network. They receive data from the source and broadcast it to all connected devices.

* Network Interface Cards (NICs): NICs are hardware components that enable computers to connect to a network. They are responsible for transmitting data over the network.

* Modems: Modems are used to connect computers to the internet. They are responsible for converting digital signals into analog signals for transmission over telephone lines.

* Access Points: Access points are used to create wireless networks. They are responsible for transmitting data over the airwaves.

Mnemonics and Learning Tricks:

* Routers: "Routing On The Edge"
* Switches: "Switching On The Inside"
* Hubs: "Hubs Are Broadcast Devices"
* Network Interface Cards (NICs): "NICs Are Network Interface Connectors"
* Modems: "Modems Make Digital To Analog"
* Access Points: "Access Points Provide Wireless Access"
