

### Types of Connections in Computer Networks

1. **Point-to-Point Connection**: A point-to-point connection is a direct connection between two devices. It is the simplest and most reliable type of connection. It is typically used for connecting two computers directly, or connecting a computer to a router or modem.

2. **Multipoint Connection**: A multipoint connection is a network connection that allows multiple devices to connect to a single network. It is typically used for connecting multiple computers to a single router or modem.

3. **Network Topologies**: Network topologies are the physical and logical arrangements of devices in a network. The most common topologies are bus, star, ring, and mesh.

4. **Network Protocols**: Network protocols are sets of rules and standards that allow devices in a network to communicate with each other. The most common protocols are TCP/IP, Ethernet, and Wi-Fi.

5. **Network Security**: Network security is the practice of protecting networks from unauthorized access, misuse, and disruption. Common security measures include encryption, authentication, and firewalls.

6. **Network Troubleshooting**: Network troubleshooting is the process of identifying and resolving issues with a network. Common troubleshooting techniques include packet sniffing, ping tests, and traceroute.
