
### Quality of Service in Transport Layer

* Quality of Service (QoS) in the transport layer is a set of techniques used to provide better service to certain types of network traffic. 
* It enables network administrators to prioritize certain types of traffic and allocate resources accordingly. 
* This helps in creating a more reliable network as it ensures that important traffic gets priority over regular traffic. 
* QoS can be implemented in both wired and wireless networks. 
* It is typically implemented in the form of a policy-based routing (PBR) system, which allows the network administrator to specify the priority of different types of traffic. 
* QoS techniques include traffic shaping, packet scheduling, congestion control, and rate limiting. 
* Traffic shaping is used to control the rate at which traffic is sent and received by the network. 
* Packet scheduling is used to prioritize certain types of traffic and allocate resources accordingly. 
* Congestion control is used to reduce the amount of traffic sent to the network in order to reduce congestion. 
* Rate limiting is used to limit the rate at which traffic is sent and received by the network. 
* QoS can be used to prioritize time-sensitive traffic such as voice and video, ensuring that these types of traffic get priority over regular traffic. 
* QoS can also be used to prioritize certain types of applications, such as those used for remote access or web browsing. 
* QoS can also be used to improve the performance of the network by ensuring that the most important traffic gets priority over regular traffic. 
* QoS can also be used to reduce the amount of congestion on the network by limiting the rate at which traffic is sent and received.
