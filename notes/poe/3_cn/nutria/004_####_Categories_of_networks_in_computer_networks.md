
#### Categories of networks in computer networks

* **Local Area Networks (LANs)**: A LAN is a network of computers that are connected to each other in a limited area, such as a home, office, or school. It is used for sharing resources such as printers, files, and applications.

* **Wide Area Networks (WANs)**: A WAN is a network of computers that are spread across a large area, such as a city or a country. It is used to connect different LANs together and to access the internet.

* **Metropolitan Area Networks (MANs)**: A MAN is a network of computers that are spread across a metropolitan area, such as a city or town. It is used to connect different LANs together and to access the internet.

* **Campus Area Networks (CANs)**: A CAN is a network of computers that are spread across a campus, such as a college or university. It is used to connect different LANs together and to access the internet.

* **Storage Area Networks (SANs)**: A SAN is a network of computers that are used for storage purposes. It is used to store large amounts of data and to access it from different locations.

* **Virtual Private Networks (VPNs)**: A VPN is a network of computers that are connected to each other over the internet. It is used to securely access resources from a remote location.

* **Wireless Networks**: A wireless network is a network of computers that are connected to each other without the use of physical cables. It is used to access the internet and to share resources, such as printers and files.

* **Mesh Networks**: A mesh network is a network of computers that are connected to each other in a mesh topology. It is used to provide reliable and secure communication between different nodes in the network.
