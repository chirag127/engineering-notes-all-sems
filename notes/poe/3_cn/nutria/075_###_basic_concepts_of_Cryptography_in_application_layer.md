
### Basic Concepts of Cryptography in Application Layer

1. **Symmetric-key cryptography**: Symmetric-key cryptography, also known as secret-key cryptography, is a type of cryptography where the same key is used to both encrypt and decrypt data. It is used for secure communication between two parties, and is generally faster than asymmetric-key cryptography.

2. **Asymmetric-key cryptography**: Asymmetric-key cryptography, also known as public-key cryptography, is a type of cryptography where two different keys are used to encrypt and decrypt data. It is used for secure communication between two parties, and is generally more secure than symmetric-key cryptography.

3. **Hash functions**: Hash functions are mathematical algorithms that are used to convert data into a fixed-length string of characters known as a hash. They are used to verify the integrity of data and to ensure that it has not been modified or tampered with.

4. **Digital signatures**: Digital signatures are used to verify the authenticity of digital documents. They are created using public-key cryptography and are used to ensure that the document has not been modified or tampered with.

5. **Public-key infrastructure (PKI)**: Public-key infrastructure (PKI) is a system of digital certificates and associated technologies used to securely exchange information over the internet. It is used to verify the identity of the sender or receiver of a message and to ensure that the message has not been modified or tampered with in transit.

6. **Cryptographic protocols**: Cryptographic protocols are algorithms that are used to securely exchange information over a network. They are used to ensure that the data is transmitted securely and that it has not been modified or tampered with in transit.

7. **Cryptographic algorithms**: Cryptographic algorithms are mathematical algorithms that are used to encrypt and decrypt data. They are used to ensure the confidentiality, integrity, and authenticity of data.

8. **Cryptographic keys**: Cryptographic keys are secret values that are used to encrypt and decrypt data. They are used to ensure that only authorized parties can access the data.
