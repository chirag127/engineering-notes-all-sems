
#### Link layer switches & bridges in local area network

* Switches and bridges are two types of devices used in local area networks (LANs) to connect computers, printers, and other devices.
* Switches are used to connect multiple devices on the same network. They are the most commonly used device in modern LANs, as they provide more flexibility and better performance than bridges.
* Bridges are used to connect two separate networks, such as two LANs or a LAN and a wide area network (WAN). They are less commonly used than switches, as they provide less flexibility and performance.
* Switches and bridges can be used together to create a more complex network.
* Switches and bridges operate at the link layer of the OSI model.
* Switches use the MAC address of each device to determine which device to send data to. They can also be used to segment a network into smaller subnets.
* Bridges use the IP address of each device to determine which device to send data to. They can also be used to connect two different types of networks (e.g. Ethernet and Token Ring).
* Switches and bridges can both be used to filter traffic, but switches are more commonly used for this purpose.
* Switches and bridges can both be used to create virtual LANs (VLANs).
* Switches and bridges can both be used to create redundant paths in a network, but switches are more commonly used for this purpose.
