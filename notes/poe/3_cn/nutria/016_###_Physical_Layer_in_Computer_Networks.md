
### Physical Layer in Computer Networks
* The physical layer is the lowest layer in the OSI (Open Systems Interconnection) model. It is responsible for providing reliable physical connections between nodes on a network.
* It defines the electrical, mechanical, and procedural characteristics of the physical link between two nodes.
* It is responsible for transmitting raw bits over a physical medium such as copper wires, optical fibers, or radio waves.
* It is also responsible for providing a reliable connection between two or more nodes.
* It provides physical addressing, line discipline, and error control.
* Physical layer protocols include Ethernet, Token Ring, Wi-Fi, and Bluetooth.
* It is also responsible for providing the means to transfer data from one node to another.
* It is responsible for providing the means to detect and correct errors that may occur during transmission.
* It also provides the means to detect and correct transmission collisions that may occur between two or more nodes.
* It is responsible for providing the means to control the flow of data across the network.
* It is also responsible for providing the means to synchronize the nodes on the network.
* Mnemonics for the physical layer include "Every Tom Cat Likes Eating Fish and Drinking Milk" which stands for Ethernet, Token Ring, CSMA/CD, Link Access, Error Control, Flow Control, and Synchronization.
