
#### Network Components in Computer Networks

1. Routers: Routers are network devices that connect two or more networks together. They are used to route data packets between networks, and can also be used to provide security and other services. 
2. Switches: Switches are network devices that connect two or more computers together. They are used to switch data packets between computers, and can also be used to provide security and other services. 
3. Hubs: Hubs are network devices that connect two or more computers together. They are used to broadcast data packets to all computers connected to the hub, and can also be used to provide security and other services.
4. Bridges: Bridges are network devices that connect two or more networks together. They are used to bridge traffic between networks, and can also be used to provide security and other services.
5. Firewalls: Firewalls are network devices that provide security to a network by controlling the traffic that is allowed to enter and leave the network. They can also be used to provide other services such as virus protection.
6. Access Points: Access Points are network devices that provide wireless access to a network. They are used to provide wireless access to a network, and can also be used to provide security and other services.
7. Network Interface Cards (NICs): Network Interface Cards (NICs) are network devices that provide physical connections between computers and networks. They are used to provide physical connections between computers and networks, and can also be used to provide security and other services.
