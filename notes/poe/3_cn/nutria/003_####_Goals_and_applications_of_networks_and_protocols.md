
#### Goals and applications of networks and protocols

* Networks and protocols are used to facilitate communication between two or more entities. 
* The main goal of networks and protocols is to provide reliable, secure and efficient communication between the entities. 
* Networks and protocols are used for a variety of applications such as file transfer, email, instant messaging, video conferencing, etc. 
* Networks and protocols are also used to connect different devices and systems to each other. 
* Networks and protocols also provide a platform for distributed computing and data storage. 
* Networks and protocols are also used to provide access to the internet and other online services.

Mnemonics and learning tricks: 
* Network Protocols: N - Networking, P - Protocols 
* Network Goals: G - Goals, A - Applications 
* Network Applications: A - Applications, C - Connectivity, S - Storage.
