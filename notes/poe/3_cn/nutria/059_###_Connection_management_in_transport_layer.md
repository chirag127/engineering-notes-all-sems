
### Connection Management in Transport Layer

Connection management in the transport layer is the process of establishing, maintaining, and terminating a connection between two devices. It is responsible for providing reliable end-to-end communication between the two devices. 

The main tasks of connection management include:

- Establishing the connection: This involves setting up the connection between the two devices, including verifying the connection parameters.

- Maintaining the connection: This involves ensuring that the connection remains active and that data is being transmitted correctly.

- Terminating the connection: This involves tearing down the connection between the two devices, including releasing any resources that were allocated for the connection.

Connection management is usually handled by the transport layer protocols such as TCP and UDP. These protocols provide reliable end-to-end communication by using flow control, error control, and congestion control mechanisms.

The main mnemonic for remembering the tasks of connection management is:

**E**stablishing the **C**onnection, **M**aintaining the **C**onnection, and **T**erminating the **C**onnection.
