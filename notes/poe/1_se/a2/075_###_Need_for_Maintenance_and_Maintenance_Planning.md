### Need for Maintenance and Maintenance Planning

- Maintenance is required to ensure continued operation of systems and equipment. Without proper maintenance, equipment can fail prematurely, leading to costly downtime and loss of productivity.
- Regular maintenance helps identify potential issues early and resolves them before they lead to catastrophic failures. This avoids unplanned outages and saves costs.
- Maintenance activities like cleaning, lubrication, and part replacement help equipment function efficiently and reliably, thereby improving performance and output.
- Maintenance planning involves forecasting what maintenance activities are required, when they are required, and what resources are needed to perform them. Good maintenance planning helps optimize costs and minimizes disruptions.
- Some key aspects of maintenance planning are:
	- Identifying maintenance requirements based on equipment age, usage, and manufacturer recommendations.
	- Scheduling preventive maintenance activities to minimize risk of unplanned downtime.
	- Estimating time, skills, and resources needed for maintenance work and procuring them in advance.
	- Coordinating maintenance with production schedules to minimize disruption.
	- Documenting and tracking maintenance activities and equipment conditions to identify trends and make improvements.

_Mnemonics_:
- "CPR for equipment: Clean, Prevent, Restore" - to remember the key goals of maintenance
- "6 Ps of maintenance: Proper Planning Prevents Poor Performance"

_Advantages_: Increased reliability, reduced costs, avoided downtime, optimized performance
_Disadvantages_: Requires time, effort, and resources to plan and execute maintenance
_Applications_: All technical systems/equipment, factories, facilities, vehicles, etc.
