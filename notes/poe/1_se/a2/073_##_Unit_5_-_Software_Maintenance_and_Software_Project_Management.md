## Unit 5 - Software Maintenance and Software Project Management

Software Maintenance:
- It refers to the activities required to keep a software system operational and functionally adequate after it has been delivered to the customer or client.
- It includes modifying the software to correct faults, improve performance or other attributes, adapt to a modified environment, and/or accommodate changing requirements.
- The key maintenance activities are: error correction, preventive maintenance, adaptive maintenance, and perfective maintenance.
- Challenges in software maintenance: Lack of documentation, Complexity of software, Frequency of changes, Knowledge transfer, Testing challenges.

Software Project Management:
- It refers to the application of knowledge, skills, tools, and techniques to project activities to meet the project requirements.
- The key project management processes are: initiating, planning, executing, monitoring and controlling, and closing.
- Project planning involves defining clear goals, identifying tasks and schedules, allocating resources, and planning budgets and risks.
- Project monitoring and control involves measuring progress, monitoring risks, taking corrective actions, and handling issues as they arise.
- Challenges in software project management: Estimating costs and timelines, Handling change requests, Managing people and teams, Managing quality and risks, Obtaining sponsorship and funding.

Some tips for learning:
- Understand the differences between software maintenance and software project management. Software maintenance focuses on managing changes to an existing software system whereas software project management focuses on planning and controlling the delivery of a new software system.
- Memorize the key activities/processes and challenges involved in software maintenance and software project management. This will help in identifying the focus of any questions on these topics.
- Solve case studies and examples to understand the concepts and processes in detail. This enhances comprehension and retention.
- Take mock tests and practice applying the concepts to reinforce learning.
