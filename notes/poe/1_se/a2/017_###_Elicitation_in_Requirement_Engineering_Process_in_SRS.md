### Elicitation in Requirement Engineering Process in SRS

The elicitation process in Requirement Engineering extracts requirements from stakeholders. Some key points about elicitation in RE process for SRS are:

1. Identify stakeholders - First step is to identify all stakeholders like users, customers, developers, testers, etc. who can provide requirements.
2. Conduct interviews - Interview stakeholders individually or in groups to understand their needs and gather requirements. Prepare interview questions in advance. Note down requirements.
3. Host brainstorming sessions - Bring stakeholders together for brainstorming sessions to generate new ideas and elicit requirements. Reduce inhibitions and encourage participation.
4. Use questionnaires - Prepare questionnaires or surveys for stakeholders to fill out requirements. Provides anonymity and collects standard data but may miss follow up questions.
5. Refer to similar systems - Analyze existing similar systems to identify their functional and non-functional requirements and get inspiration for the new system. But do not copy requirements blindly.
6. Observe users - Directly observe users in their environment and note down points where they face problems or requirements for a new system. But may miss non-observable requirements.

**Mnemonics:**
- `IDEAS` - Identify stakeholders, Discuss, Elicit, Analyze, Specify requirements
- `CATWOE` - Customers, Actors, Transformation process, Weltanschauung, Owner, Environmental constraints

**Advantages:** Captures hidden/implicit requirements, Considers users' perspectives, Generates new ideas
**Disadvantages:** Time consuming, Requires skilled facilitators, May be inconsistent/incomplete
