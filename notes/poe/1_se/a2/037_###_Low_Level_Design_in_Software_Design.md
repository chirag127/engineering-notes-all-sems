### Low Level Design in Software Design

Low level design focuses on the internal details of a system. It is the design of individual modules and components. Some key points to keep in mind while doing low level design are:

- Define interfaces and interactions between components. What are the inputs and outputs of each component?
- Reduce coupling and increase cohesion between components. Components should be loosely coupled and highly cohesive.
- Optimize for scalability and performance. Identify potential bottlenecks and come up with solutions to handle increased load.
- Use design patterns as applicable to solve common problems. Some examples are factory method, singleton, facade, etc.
- Come up with a modular decomposition of the system. Break the system into multiple modules and assign responsibilities to individual modules.
- Consider concurrency and think about making components thread-safe if multiple threads will access them.
- Discuss tradeoffs of different design decisions and evaluate pros and cons.
- Provide sequence diagrams or activity diagrams to depict the flow of control between components.

Some tips for learning low level design:

- Start with a high level overview of major components and their responsibilities. Then dive into details of individual components.
- Draw diagrams to visualize interactions between components. This helps in catching issues early.
- Come up with examples to demonstrate how the system will work in practice. This solidifies understanding.
- Discuss design decisions and evaluate alternatives to make a good choice.
- Read through existing systems' code to understand low level design patterns and best practices.

Advantages of low level design:

- It leads to a modular system that is extensible and maintainable.
- It results in a robust design that handles load and edge cases efficiently.
- It makes concurrency and parallelism easier to implement.
- It enables estimating system resources and performance.

Disadvantages:

- It can be time-consuming to figure out all the low level details.
- The design may be over-engineered if not done properly.
- It is difficult to change the low level design once implemented.
