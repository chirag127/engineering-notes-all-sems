### Software Characteristics

- Reliability: The ability of a system or component to perform its required functions under stated conditions for a specified period of time. It refers to the consistency of performance and the ability to recover from faults.
- Efficiency: The capability of the software product to provide appropriate performance relative to the amount of resources used, especially computer resources.
- Usability: The ease with which a user can learn to operate, prepare inputs for, and interpret outputs of a system or component. It refers to the friendliness and functionality of the software.
- Maintainability: The ease with which a software system or component can be modified to correct faults, improve performance or other attributes, or adapt to a changed environment.
- Portability: The ability of a system or component to be transferred from one hardware or software environment to another. This includes the ability to be installed on a different system and still function.
- Serviceability: The capability of the software product to be quickly and easily repaired or modified to correct faults or improve performance. It includes diagnosability, analyzability, stability, and changeability.
