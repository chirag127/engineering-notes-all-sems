### Resource Allocation Models (RAIM) in software project management

The following are the key Resource Allocation Models (RAIM) used in software project management:

1. **Fixed Price Model**: In this model, the resources (team members) are allocated for the entire project duration upfront. The budget and timelines are fixed. This model is suitable for smaller projects with well-defined requirements. However, any changes or delays can impact the budget and timelines.
2. **Time and Materials Model**: In this model, resources are acquired as needed and they are paid based on the time spent and materials used. While this model is flexible to changes, it can lead to budget overruns if not managed properly. Close monitoring of resources and timelines is required.
3. **Dedicated Team Model**: In this model, a dedicated team is allocated to work on the project. The team members work exclusively on the project. While this ensures focused efforts, it can lead to under-utilization of resources if the team size is more than required. Extra costs may be incurred in such cases.

_Mnemonics_:

- Fixed Price Model: Upfront fixed budget and timeline
- Time and Materials Model: Flexible but needs close monitoring
- Dedicated Team Model: Focused but can lead to under-utilization

_Applications_:

- Fixed Price Model: Small, well-defined projects
- Time and Materials Model: Projects with evolving requirements
- Dedicated Team Model: Projects requiring focused team efforts
