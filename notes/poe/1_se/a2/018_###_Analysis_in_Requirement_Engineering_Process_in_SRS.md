### Analysis in Requirement Engineering Process in SRS

- ** requirement analysis** - Understanding the actual needs of the customer and stakeholders. Translating those needs into a set of system requirements.
- ** feasibility analysis** - Checking if the proposed system is practical, viable and suits the budget and time constraints. This is done by evaluating alternatives and justifying the best option.
- ** domain analysis** - Studying the application area to understand the key concepts, terminology, constraints, and potential solutions. This results in creating a domain model.
- ** specification analysis** - Examining the requirements specifications to identify and resolve issues like incompleteness, inconsistency, ambiguity and redundancy.

**Mnemonics:** CAFT stands for Components, Actors, Features, Tasks which are analyzed in Requirement Analysis.

**Advantages:** Clearly understands customer needs, viable solution can be proposed, issues can be identified early and rectified.

**Disadvantages:** Time consuming, expensive,requires highly skilled analysts, customer may not be able to articulate all needs clearly.

**Examples:** Requirement analysis for designing a shopping cart system, student management system, etc.

**Applications:** Any software or system development project.
