
### Requirement Engineering Process in SRS

1. **Requirements Elicitation**: The process of gathering requirements from stakeholders, users, customers, and other sources. Requirements elicitation involves activities such as interviews, questionnaires, workshops, surveys, site visits, and observation.

2. **Requirements Analysis**: The process of analyzing the requirements gathered from stakeholders, users, customers, and other sources. Requirements analysis involves activities such as analyzing the requirements for consistency, completeness, feasibility, and accuracy.

3. **Requirements Specification**: The process of specifying the requirements in a formal document. The document should include the requirements gathered from stakeholders, users, customers, and other sources.

4. **Requirements Validation**: The process of validating the requirements specified in the formal document. Requirements validation involves activities such as reviews, inspections, and tests.

5. **Requirements Management**: The process of managing the requirements specified in the formal document. Requirements management involves activities such as tracking, monitoring, and controlling changes to the requirements.

6. **Mnemonics and Learning Tricks**: Mnemonics and learning tricks can be helpful when it comes to remembering and understanding the requirements engineering process. A few examples include: 
- REP: Requirements Elicitation, Requirements Analysis, Requirements Specification 
- RVM: Requirements Validation and Requirements Management 
- RICE: Requirements Identification, Clarification, Estimation, and Verification.
