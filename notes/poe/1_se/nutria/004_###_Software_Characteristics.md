
### Software Characteristics
Software characteristics are the properties of a software system that define its behavior and capabilities. These characteristics include: 

* **Functionality**: The ability of the software to perform its intended tasks. 
* **Usability**: The ease of use of the software.
* **Reliability**: The ability of the software to perform its intended tasks consistently and accurately.
* **Performance**: The speed and efficiency of the software.
* **Maintainability**: The ability of the software to be modified or extended to meet changing needs.
* **Security**: The ability of the software to protect data and resources from unauthorized access.
* **Portability**: The ability of the software to be used on different platforms and operating systems.
* **Scalability**: The ability of the software to expand its capabilities as the user's needs grow.
* **Flexibility**: The ability of the software to be adapted to different contexts and environments.
* **Testability**: The ability of the software to be tested for accuracy and reliability.
