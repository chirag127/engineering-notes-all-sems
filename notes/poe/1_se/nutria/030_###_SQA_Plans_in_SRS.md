
### SQA Plans in SRS

* Software Quality Assurance (SQA) plans are documents that outline the steps and processes that will be used to ensure the quality of a software product or system.
* These plans typically include details on how quality assurance activities will be performed, the roles and responsibilities of the team members involved, and the timeline for the activities.
* SQA plans are an important part of the software development process, as they provide a roadmap for quality assurance activities and help ensure that the software product meets the customer's quality requirements.
* SQA plans should include a detailed description of the software product, the quality goals and objectives, and the quality assurance activities that will be performed.
* SQA plans should also include a list of test cases, test data, and expected results, as well as any tools that will be used for testing.
* SQA plans should also specify the roles and responsibilities of the team members involved in the testing process, including the testers, the project manager, and the software developer.
* Finally, SQA plans should include a timeline for the activities, including when the tests will be performed and when the results will be reported.
