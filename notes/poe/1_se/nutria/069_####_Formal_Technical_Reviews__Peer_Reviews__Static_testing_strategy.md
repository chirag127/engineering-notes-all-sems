
#### Formal Technical Reviews (Peer Reviews) Static testing strategy

Formal technical reviews, also known as peer reviews, are an important part of static testing strategy. They involve a team of experts who review the code to identify any potential issues or bugs. This helps to ensure that the code is of the highest quality before it is released.

Peer reviews are typically conducted in a formal setting and involve a detailed review of the code. During the review, the experts will look for any errors or potential problems that could cause the code to fail in production. They will also look for any areas of improvement that could enhance the code's performance.

The review process typically involves the following steps:

1. The code is reviewed by a team of experts.
2. The experts identify any issues or potential problems.
3. The team discusses the issues and potential solutions.
4. The code is revised and improved.
5. The revised code is tested and reviewed again.

Peer reviews are an important part of static testing strategy as they help to ensure that the code is of the highest quality before it is released. They also help to identify any potential problems or areas of improvement that could enhance the code's performance.

Mnemonics and learning tricks for peer reviews include:

- R.E.V.I.E.W.: Read, Evaluate, Verify, Improve, Execute, and Write.
- P.R.E.P.: Plan, Review, Enhance, and Publish.
- B.U.G.S.: Breakdown, Understand, Generate, and Simulate.
