
### ISO 9000 Models in SRS

ISO 9000 is a set of international standards for quality management and quality assurance. It is designed to help organizations ensure that their products and services meet customer requirements and international standards. The ISO 9000 family of standards consists of three models:

1. Quality Management System (QMS)
2. Quality Assurance System (QAS)
3. Quality Control System (QCS)

**Quality Management System (QMS)**

The Quality Management System (QMS) is the foundation of ISO 9000 and is the most widely used model. It covers all aspects of quality management, including planning, design, development, production, installation, and service. It also includes processes for continuous improvement and customer satisfaction.

**Quality Assurance System (QAS)**

The Quality Assurance System (QAS) is a system of procedures and processes that are used to ensure that products and services meet the requirements of customers and other stakeholders. It includes quality control, quality assurance, and quality improvement.

**Quality Control System (QCS)**

The Quality Control System (QCS) is a system of procedures and processes that are used to ensure that products and services meet the requirements of customers and other stakeholders. It includes quality control, quality assurance, and quality improvement. It also includes processes for monitoring, measuring, and evaluating the effectiveness of the quality control system.

Mnemonics for remembering the three models:

* QMS: Quality Management System
* QAS: Quality Assurance System
* QCS: Quality Control System
