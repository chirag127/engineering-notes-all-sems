
### Analysis in Requirement Engineering Process in SRS

- Analysis in requirement engineering is the process of understanding, analyzing, and documenting the requirements of a system.
- It involves the identification of stakeholders, the determination of their needs, and the analysis of the system's environment.
- The goal of the analysis process is to ensure that the requirements of the system are accurately identified and documented.
- The analysis process is often divided into three stages:
    1. Requirements Gathering: This involves the identification of stakeholders and the determination of their needs.
    2. Requirements Analysis: This involves analyzing the requirements and determining how they will be implemented in the system.
    3. Requirements Documentation: This involves documenting the requirements in a format that is easy to understand and use.
- Mnemonics and learning tricks for analysis in requirement engineering process in SRS include:
    - RAG: Requirements Gathering
    - RAA: Requirements Analysis
    - RAD: Requirements Documentation
- Advantages of analysis in requirement engineering process in SRS include:
    - Ensuring that the system meets the stakeholders' needs.
    - Facilitating communication between stakeholders and system developers.
    - Improving the quality of the system by ensuring that all requirements are adequately addressed.
- Disadvantages of analysis in requirement engineering process in SRS include:
    - The process can be time consuming and expensive.
    - It can be difficult to accurately identify and document the requirements.
    - It can be difficult to ensure that all stakeholders are adequately represented in the process.
- Examples of analysis in requirement engineering process in SRS include:
    - Developing a web application to track customer orders.
    - Developing a database system to store customer information.
    - Developing a mobile application to provide customer support.
- Applications of analysis in requirement engineering process in SRS include:
    - Automation systems.
    - Business systems.
    - Web applications.
    - Mobile applications.
