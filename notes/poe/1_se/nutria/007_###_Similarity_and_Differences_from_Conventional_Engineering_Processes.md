
### Similarity and Differences from Conventional Engineering Processes

Conventional engineering processes and modern engineering processes have many similarities, but there are also some important differences. 

Similarities: 

* Both involve the application of scientific principles and knowledge to solve technical problems.
* Both involve the use of mathematics, engineering principles, and problem-solving strategies to identify and solve problems.
* Both involve the use of computer-aided design (CAD) software to create models and simulations.

Differences:

* Conventional engineering processes are typically linear, while modern engineering processes are often iterative.
* Conventional engineering processes focus on designing a single solution for a given problem, while modern engineering processes often involve designing multiple solutions in parallel.
* Conventional engineering processes are often more time-consuming and expensive than modern engineering processes.
* Conventional engineering processes typically involve manual labor, while modern engineering processes often involve automation.
* Conventional engineering processes often involve a single engineer, while modern engineering processes often involve multiple engineers working together.
