
### Low Level Design in Software Design

Low level design in software design is a process of creating a detailed design of a software system. It is concerned with the internal structure of a software system and how it will be implemented. It involves the design of the data structures and algorithms used in the software system.

The main goals of low level design are:

- To provide an efficient and effective solution to the problem.
- To ensure that the design is maintainable, extensible and portable.
- To create a design that is easy to understand and debug.

Low level design involves the following steps:

1. Analyzing the problem: The first step is to analyze the problem and identify the requirements of the software system.

2. Creating a data structure: The next step is to create a data structure that will be used to store the data and algorithms.

3. Designing algorithms: The next step is to design algorithms to solve the problem.

4. Testing the design: The next step is to test the design to ensure that it meets the requirements and is efficient.

Mnemonic: LLDIS (Low Level Design In Software)
