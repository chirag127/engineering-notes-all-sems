Advantages of MongoDB:

- Flexible data model allows for easy scaling and handling of unstructured data
- High availability and automatic sharding for improved performance
- Supports complex queries and indexing for faster data retrieval
- Easy integration with other Big Data tools such as Hadoop and Spark

Disadvantages of MongoDB:

- Limited support for transactions and joins
- Limited support for ACID (Atomicity, Consistency, Isolation, Durability) compliance
- Lack of maturity compared to traditional relational databases

Example use cases for MongoDB:

- Social media analytics
- Internet of Things (IoT) data management
- Mobile app development
- E-commerce platforms
