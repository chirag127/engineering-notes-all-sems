### Basic Concepts in Real time Communication

Real time communication is a type of communication that occurs in real time or live, without any delay. It involves the transfer of data, audio, and video among various devices. It is used in many fields, including telecommunications, video conferencing, and multimedia streaming. In this section, we will discuss some basic concepts related to real time communication.

1. Latency: Latency is the delay between the time a signal is sent and the time it is received. In real time communication, latency is critical because it can affect the quality of the data being transmitted. The lower the latency, the better the quality of the communication.

2. Jitter: Jitter is the variation in latency over time. It can affect the quality of real time communication because it can cause delays and interruptions in the transmission of data. A high level of jitter can result in degraded quality of communication.

3. Bandwidth: Bandwidth is the amount of data that can be transmitted over a network in a given time. In real time communication, a higher bandwidth is required to transmit large amounts of data, such as video and audio streams.

4. Quality of Service (QoS): Quality of Service refers to the ability of a network to provide a certain level of service to its users. In real time communication, a high level of QoS is required to ensure the quality of the communication.

5. Protocols: Protocols are a set of rules and standards that govern the communication between devices. In real time communication, protocols such as Real-time Transport Protocol (RTP) and Real-time Control Protocol (RTCP) are used to ensure the timely and accurate transmission of data.

6. Compression: Compression is the process of reducing the size of data by removing redundant information. In real time communication, compression is used to reduce the amount of data that needs to be transmitted, which can improve the quality of the communication.

7. Synchronization: Synchronization is the process of aligning the timing of different devices so that they can communicate effectively. In real time communication, synchronization is critical to ensure that audio and video streams are played in sync.

Real time communication is an important field that has many applications in various industries. Understanding these basic concepts is essential for anyone who wants to work with real time communication systems.
