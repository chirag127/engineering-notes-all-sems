### Model of Real Time Communication

Real time communication is the process of transmitting data instantly between two or more parties. It is a critical component in real time systems as it ensures timely delivery of information. The model of real time communication involves three main components:

1. Sender
2. Transmission Medium
3. Receiver

#### Sender

The sender is the device or application that initiates the transmission of data. It is responsible for packaging the data and sending it over the transmission medium. In a real time system, the sender must ensure that the data is transmitted within a specific time frame. This is achieved by using real time protocols and techniques such as prioritization, segmentation, and scheduling.

#### Transmission Medium

The transmission medium is the physical or virtual path through which the data is transmitted. It can be wired or wireless, and its characteristics determine the transmission speed, latency, and reliability of the communication. In a real time system, the transmission medium must be carefully selected to ensure that it meets the requirements of the application. For example, a wired Ethernet connection may be suitable for a real time video conferencing application, while a wireless Bluetooth connection may be suitable for a real time sensor network.

#### Receiver

The receiver is the device or application that receives the data transmitted by the sender. It is responsible for unpackaging the data and processing it. In a real time system, the receiver must ensure that the data is processed within a specific time frame. This is achieved by using real time protocols and techniques such as buffering, error checking, and synchronization.

#### Advantages of Real Time Communication Model

- Real time communication ensures timely delivery of information.
- Real time communication enables fast decision making.
- Real time communication improves collaboration and productivity.
- Real time communication reduces delays and errors in data transmission.

#### Disadvantages of Real Time Communication Model

- Real time communication requires high bandwidth and low latency transmission medium.
- Real time communication may be affected by network congestion and interference.
- Real time communication may require specialized hardware and software.
- Real time communication may be expensive to implement and maintain.

#### Example Applications

- Video conferencing
- Voice over IP (VoIP)
- Real time sensor networks
- Real time control systems
- Real time financial trading systems

In conclusion, the model of real time communication is a critical component in real time systems. It involves three main components: sender, transmission medium, and receiver. Real time communication enables timely delivery of information, fast decision making, and improved collaboration and productivity. However, it also has some disadvantages such as high bandwidth and low latency requirements, susceptibility to network congestion and interference, and the need for specialized hardware and software.
