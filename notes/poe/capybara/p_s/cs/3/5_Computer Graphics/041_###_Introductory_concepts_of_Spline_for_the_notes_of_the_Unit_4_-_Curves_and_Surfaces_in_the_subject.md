### Introductory concepts of Spline for the notes of the Unit 4 - Curves and Surfaces in the subject of Computer Graphics

Spline is a mathematical representation that is used to create smooth curves and surfaces in computer graphics. It is an essential tool for creating curves and surfaces, and it has a wide range of applications in computer graphics. Here are some of the introductory concepts of Spline that you need to know for the Curves and Surfaces unit in Computer Graphics:

1. Definition of Spline: 
   A spline is a mathematical function that is defined piecewise. It consists of several polynomial functions that are joined together to create a smooth curve or surface. The most common types of spline are Cubic Spline, B-Spline, and NURBS.

2. Cubic Spline: 
   A Cubic Spline is a type of spline that is defined by a set of control points. It is a piecewise polynomial function that is defined by a set of cubic polynomials. The curve passes through each control point and has a smooth continuity between the segments.

3. B-Spline: 
   A B-Spline is a type of spline that is defined by a set of control points and a degree. It is a piecewise polynomial function that is defined by a set of basis functions. The curve passes through the first and last control points and has a smooth continuity between the segments.

4. NURBS: 
   Non-Uniform Rational B-Splines (NURBS) are a type of B-Spline that uses weights to create more complex shapes. NURBS are widely used in computer graphics because they can represent complex shapes and surfaces.

5. Advantages of Spline: 
   - Spline curves and surfaces are smoother than other types of curves and surfaces.
   - Spline curves and surfaces are easy to manipulate and modify.
   - Spline curves and surfaces can be used to represent complex shapes and surfaces.

6. Disadvantages of Spline: 
   - Spline curves and surfaces can be computationally expensive to calculate.
   - Spline curves and surfaces can sometimes result in over-smoothed or unrealistic shapes.

7. Applications of Spline: 
   - Spline curves and surfaces are used in computer graphics for modeling 3D objects and characters.
   - Spline curves and surfaces are used in animation to create smooth motion paths.
   - Spline curves and surfaces are used in CAD (Computer-Aided Design) for creating complex shapes and surfaces.

In conclusion, Spline is an essential tool for creating smooth curves and surfaces in computer graphics. It has a wide range of applications, and it is important to understand the introductory concepts of Spline for the Curves and Surfaces unit in Computer Graphics.
