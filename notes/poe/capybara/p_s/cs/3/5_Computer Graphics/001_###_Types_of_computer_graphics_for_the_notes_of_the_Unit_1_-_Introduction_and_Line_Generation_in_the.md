### Types of Computer Graphics

Computer graphics is a field that deals with creating and manipulating visual images using computer software and hardware. There are two main types of computer graphics:

1. **Raster Graphics**: Raster graphics, also known as bitmap graphics, are made up of pixels arranged in a grid. Each pixel is assigned a color value, which determines its appearance. Raster graphics are commonly used for photographs and digital art.

Advantages of Raster Graphics:
- Can produce highly detailed images.
- Can create complex shading and color effects.

Disadvantages of Raster Graphics:
- Quality can be lost when the image is resized.
- File sizes can be large, which can be problematic for web and mobile applications.

2. **Vector Graphics**: Vector graphics are made up of mathematical equations and geometric shapes. They are resolution-independent, meaning that they can be resized without losing quality. Vector graphics are commonly used for logos, icons, and illustrations.

Advantages of Vector Graphics:
- Can be resized without losing quality.
- File sizes are usually smaller than raster graphics.

Disadvantages of Vector Graphics:
- Cannot produce highly detailed images.
- Complex shading and color effects are difficult to create.

Examples of Applications:
- Raster graphics: Photographs, digital art, web and mobile applications.
- Vector graphics: Logos, icons, illustrations, print media.

Overall, both types of computer graphics have their advantages and disadvantages, and choosing the right type depends on the specific needs of the project. Understanding the differences between raster and vector graphics is important for anyone working in the field of computer graphics.
