### Composite transformations for the notes of the Unit 2 - Transformations in the subject of Computer Graphics

Composite transformations are a set of transformations that are applied to an object in a specific sequence. These transformations include scaling, rotation, and translation, which are used to change the size, orientation, and position of an object. In computer graphics, composite transformations are widely used to create complex shapes and animations.

Here are some important points to understand about composite transformations:

1. Composite transformations are applied in a specific sequence. The order in which they are applied can significantly affect the final result.

2. The sequence of transformations is usually represented by a matrix. This matrix is multiplied with the original object matrix to obtain the final transformed object.

3. A composite transformation can be represented as a product of individual transformation matrices. For example, a scaling transformation followed by a rotation transformation can be represented as the product of the two individual transformation matrices.

4. Composite transformations can be used to create more complex animations. For example, a 3D animation can be created by applying a series of composite transformations to a 3D object.

5. One disadvantage of composite transformations is that they can be computationally expensive. This is because each transformation requires a separate matrix multiplication.

6. In some cases, composite transformations can lead to loss of precision. This is because each matrix multiplication can introduce small errors that accumulate over time.

7. Despite their disadvantages, composite transformations are widely used in computer graphics. They are essential for creating complex shapes and animations.

Here are some examples of composite transformations:

1. A car driving down a winding road can be created by applying a series of composite transformations to the car model. These transformations would include translation, rotation, and scaling to simulate the movement of the car.

2. A 3D model of a building can be created by applying a series of composite transformations to a set of 2D floor plans. These transformations would include translation, rotation, and scaling to create the different levels of the building.

3. A simple 2D animation of a bouncing ball can be created by applying a series of composite transformations to the ball object. These transformations would include translation, scaling, and rotation to simulate the bouncing motion of the ball.

In conclusion, composite transformations are an important concept in computer graphics. They are used to create complex shapes and animations by applying a sequence of transformations to an object. While they have some disadvantages, they are essential for creating realistic and engaging computer graphics.
