### Random Scan Displays for the Notes of Unit 1 - Introduction and Line Generation in Computer Graphics

Random scan displays, also known as vector displays, are a type of display device used in computer graphics. In this section, we will discuss the basics of random scan displays, their advantages and disadvantages, and their applications.

#### Introduction

Random scan displays are a type of display device that uses a beam of electrons to draw lines on the screen. The electron beam is moved across the screen in a random fashion, and the lines are drawn as the beam moves.

#### Line Generation in Random Scan Displays

To generate a line on a random scan display, we need to provide two end points of the line. These end points are then used to determine the direction and length of the line.

##### Bresenham's Line Drawing Algorithm

Bresenham's line drawing algorithm is a popular algorithm used to draw lines in random scan displays. The algorithm uses integer arithmetic to determine the coordinates of the pixels that the line passes through.

#### Advantages of Random Scan Displays

- Can display high-quality images with smooth lines and curves.
- Can display a large number of lines and curves at a high speed.
- Can be used for real-time applications like flight simulators and video games.

#### Disadvantages of Random Scan Displays

- Can only display lines and curves, not images or text.
- Can only display a limited number of colors.
- Can be expensive compared to other display devices.

#### Applications of Random Scan Displays

- Flight simulators
- Video games
- CAD/CAM software
- Medical imaging

In conclusion, random scan displays are a type of display device used in computer graphics to draw lines and curves. They have advantages like high-quality images and real-time applications, but also have disadvantages like limited color display and high cost. They are commonly used in flight simulators, video games, CAD/CAM software, and medical imaging.
