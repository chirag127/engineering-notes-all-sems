## Unit 4 - Curves and Surfaces

In this unit, we will study curves and surfaces in computer graphics. Curves and surfaces are used to represent objects in 3D space in computer graphics. They are essential tools for designing and modeling 3D objects. Here are some of the topics that we will cover in this unit:

### 1. Introduction to Curves and Surfaces
- Definition of curves and surfaces
- Advantages of using curves and surfaces in computer graphics
- Applications of curves and surfaces in computer graphics

### 2. Curves
- Types of curves: Bezier curves, B-spline curves, and NURBS curves
- Properties of curves: degree, control points, and knots
- Algorithms for generating Bezier and B-spline curves
- Advantages and disadvantages of Bezier and B-spline curves

### 3. Surfaces
- Types of surfaces: Bezier surfaces, B-spline surfaces, and NURBS surfaces
- Properties of surfaces: degree, control points, and knots
- Algorithms for generating Bezier and B-spline surfaces
- Advantages and disadvantages of Bezier and B-spline surfaces

### 4. Mesh Generation
- Definition of mesh
- Methods for mesh generation: Delaunay triangulation and Voronoi diagram
- Advantages and disadvantages of Delaunay triangulation and Voronoi diagram

### 5. Applications of Curves and Surfaces
- Designing and modeling 3D objects in computer graphics
- Animation of 3D objects in computer graphics
- Industrial design, automotive design, and product design

In conclusion, curves and surfaces are essential tools for designing and modeling 3D objects in computer graphics. By understanding the properties and algorithms of curves and surfaces, we can create complex 3D objects with ease. The applications of curves and surfaces in various fields make it a valuable tool to learn for anyone interested in computer graphics.
