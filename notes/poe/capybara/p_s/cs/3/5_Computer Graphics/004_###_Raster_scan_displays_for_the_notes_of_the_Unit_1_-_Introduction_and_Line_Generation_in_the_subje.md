### Raster scan displays for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction and Line Generation in the subject of Computer Graphics

Raster scan displays, also known as CRT displays, are widely used for displaying images in computer graphics. In this unit, we will learn about the basics of raster scan displays and how they are used for line generation.

#### What is a Raster Scan Display?

A raster scan display is an electronic display device that uses an electron beam to create an image on a phosphorescent screen. The electron beam scans the screen from left to right and top to bottom, creating a series of lines on the screen. These lines are called scan lines, and each scan line is made up of a series of pixels.

#### How Does a Raster Scan Display Work?

A raster scan display works by dividing the screen into a grid of pixels. Each pixel is a small dot on the screen that can be individually illuminated to create an image. The electron beam in the display scans each pixel one by one, illuminating each pixel as it goes. This creates the image on the screen.

#### Advantages of Raster Scan Displays

- Raster scan displays are widely used for displaying images in computer graphics because they are easy to implement and produce high-quality images.
- They are also relatively inexpensive to manufacture and are therefore widely available.
- Raster scan displays can display a wide range of colors and can be used to display complex images, including photographs and video.

#### Disadvantages of Raster Scan Displays

- Raster scan displays have a fixed resolution, which means that the image quality is limited by the number of pixels on the screen.
- They also suffer from flicker, which can cause eye strain and headaches in some people.
- Raster scan displays are also relatively bulky and consume a lot of power.

#### Applications of Raster Scan Displays

- Raster scan displays are used in computer monitors, televisions, and other electronic displays.
- They are also used in medical imaging equipment, such as X-ray machines and CT scanners.
- Raster scan displays are used in video game consoles, including the PlayStation and Xbox.
