### Projections for the Notes of the Unit 3 - Three Dimensional in the Subject of Computer Graphics

Computer graphics is a vast field that deals with the creation, manipulation, and rendering of images and visual content using computers. One of the key concepts in computer graphics is the projection of three-dimensional objects onto a two-dimensional surface. In this unit, we will explore the different types of projections used in computer graphics.

#### What is Projection?

Projection is the process of mapping a three-dimensional object onto a two-dimensional surface. This process is essential in computer graphics because most output devices, such as monitors and printers, are two-dimensional. Therefore, it is necessary to project three-dimensional objects onto a two-dimensional surface to display them.

#### Types of Projections

There are two main types of projections used in computer graphics: orthographic projection and perspective projection.

##### Orthographic Projection

Orthographic projection is a type of projection where the object is projected onto a plane parallel to one of the coordinate planes (x, y, or z). This type of projection is used to create two-dimensional representations of three-dimensional objects. Orthographic projection is also known as parallel projection because all the lines in the object that are parallel to the projection plane remain parallel in the projection.

##### Perspective Projection

Perspective projection is a type of projection where the object is projected onto a plane that is not parallel to any of the coordinate planes. This type of projection mimics the way the human eye sees objects in the real world. Perspective projection is used to create realistic images of three-dimensional objects.

#### Advantages and Disadvantages

Both types of projections have their advantages and disadvantages. Orthographic projection is useful when creating technical drawings or architectural plans because it accurately represents the size and shape of the object. However, orthographic projection does not provide a realistic view of the object. On the other hand, perspective projection provides a realistic view of the object, but it distorts the size and shape of the object.

#### Applications

Projections are used in various applications, such as:

- Architecture and engineering: Orthographic projections are used to create technical drawings and blueprints of buildings and structures.

- Video games and movies: Perspective projections are used to create realistic 3D environments and characters in video games and movies.

- Medical imaging: Projections are used to create 2D images of 3D structures in medical imaging, such as X-rays and CT scans.

#### Conclusion

In conclusion, projections are an essential concept in computer graphics. Orthographic and perspective projections are the two main types of projections used in computer graphics. Each type has its advantages and disadvantages, and they are used in various applications, such as architecture, video games, and medical imaging.
