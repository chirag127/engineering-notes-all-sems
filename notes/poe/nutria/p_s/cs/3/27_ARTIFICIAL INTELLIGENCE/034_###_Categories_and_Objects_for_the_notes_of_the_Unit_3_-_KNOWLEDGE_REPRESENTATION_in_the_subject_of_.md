
### Categories and Objects for the Notes of Unit 3 - Knowledge Representation in Artificial Intelligence KCS

Knowledge representation (KR) is a field of artificial intelligence that studies how to represent knowledge in a computer. KR is an important part of AI research, as it is used to model and reason about the world. KR involves representing knowledge using formal languages and logical reasoning. 

The most common KR techniques are: 

* Symbolic representation: Symbolic representations are used to represent knowledge in a way that can be understood by a computer. These representations are often based on logic, and they can be used to represent facts, rules, and relationships.

* Non-symbolic representation: Non-symbolic representations are used to represent knowledge in a way that is not easily understood by a computer. These representations are often based on images, diagrams, and natural language processing.

* Semantic networks: Semantic networks are used to represent knowledge in a way that is more easily understood by a computer. These networks are often based on graphs, which are used to represent relationships between different concepts.

* Frames: Frames are used to represent knowledge in a way that is more easily understood by a computer. These frames are often based on hierarchical structures, which are used to represent relationships between different concepts.

* Ontologies: Ontologies are used to represent knowledge in a way that is more easily understood by a computer. These ontologies are often based on taxonomies, which are used to represent relationships between different concepts.

KR is used in many AI applications, including natural language processing, robotics, and computer vision. KR is also used in many other fields, such as healthcare, finance, and engineering. KR is an important part of AI research, and it is used to model and reason about the world.
