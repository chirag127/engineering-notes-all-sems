
## Unit 4 - SOFTWARE AGENTS

Software agents are computer programs that can autonomously perform tasks on behalf of a user or organization. They are able to interact with their environment, make decisions and learn from their experiences. Agents are typically used to automate complex tasks or to provide a service.

### Advantages
- Agents can automate complex tasks and processes, making them more efficient and cost-effective.
- Agents can provide services, such as customer support, search and retrieval, and data mining.
- Agents can be used to monitor and manage resources, such as networks and databases.
- Agents can interact with their environment, making decisions based on their experiences.

### Disadvantages
- Agents can be difficult to design and implement, requiring a great deal of time and effort.
- Agents can be unreliable, as they may make mistakes or become confused in certain situations.
- Agents may not be able to adapt to changing conditions or environments.
- Agents may be vulnerable to malicious attacks, such as viruses and malware.

### Examples
- Autonomous robots, such as those used in manufacturing and warehousing.
- Virtual personal assistants, such as Apple's Siri and Amazon's Alexa.
- Chatbots, such as those used in customer service and online shopping.
- Autonomous vehicles, such as self-driving cars and drones.
