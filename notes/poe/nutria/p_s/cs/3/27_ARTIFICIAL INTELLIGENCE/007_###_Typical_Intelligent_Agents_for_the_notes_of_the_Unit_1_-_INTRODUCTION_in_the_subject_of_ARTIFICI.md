
### Typical Intelligent Agents for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

An Intelligent Agent (IA) is an autonomous entity that perceives its environment and takes actions that maximize its chances of success. Intelligent agents are the cornerstone of Artificial Intelligence (AI). They are used in a variety of applications, from robotics to games and natural language processing.

Intelligent agents can be divided into two broad categories: reactive agents and deliberative agents. Reactive agents are agents that do not have any internal state and respond to the environment only in terms of their current state. Deliberative agents are agents that have an internal state and can make decisions based on past experiences and knowledge.

Reactive agents are often used in robotics and games. They are designed to respond to their environment in a reactive manner. For example, a robotic arm may be programmed to move in a certain direction when it detects an object in its path.

Deliberative agents are used in a variety of applications, including natural language processing, decision making, and problem solving. They are designed to make decisions based on their current state, past experiences, and knowledge. For example, a natural language processing system may be programmed to recognize certain words and make decisions based on the context of the sentence.

Intelligent agents can also be categorized based on their learning capabilities. Supervised learning agents are agents that are trained on a set of labeled data. Unsupervised learning agents are agents that are not trained on any labeled data and are instead trained on a set of unlabeled data. Reinforcement learning agents are agents that learn by trial and error, and are often used in robotics and games.

In summary, Intelligent Agents are autonomous entities that perceive their environment and take actions that maximize their chances of success. They can be divided into two broad categories: reactive agents and deliberative agents. They can also be categorized based on their learning capabilities, such as supervised learning, unsupervised learning, and reinforcement learning.
