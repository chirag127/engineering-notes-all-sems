
## Knowledge Centered Service (KCS)
KCS is a knowledge management methodology that focuses on the use of knowledge to improve customer service. It is a set of principles and practices that enable organizations to deliver better customer service by leveraging their existing knowledge. KCS is based on the idea that knowledge is the foundation of customer service and should be used to improve customer experience.

KCS is a process-oriented approach to knowledge management. It focuses on the use of knowledge to improve customer service. The process involves creating, managing, sharing, and improving customer service knowledge.

### Principles
KCS is based on four key principles:
- **Knowledge is the foundation of customer service**: Knowledge is the foundation of customer service. It is the basis for providing quality customer service.
- **Knowledge should be used to improve customer experience**: Knowledge should be used to improve customer experience. It should be used to create better customer service processes, to provide better customer service, and to improve customer satisfaction.
- **Knowledge should be shared**: Knowledge should be shared with customers, employees, and other stakeholders. It should be shared in a way that is easy to understand and use.
- **Knowledge should be improved**: Knowledge should be improved over time. It should be improved by gathering customer feedback, analyzing customer service data, and using customer service best practices.

### Practices
KCS involves several practices that help organizations use knowledge to improve customer service:
- **Knowledge capture**: Knowledge capture is the process of creating, organizing, and storing customer service knowledge.
- **Knowledge sharing**: Knowledge sharing is the process of sharing customer service knowledge with customers, employees, and other stakeholders.
- **Knowledge improvement**: Knowledge improvement is the process of improving customer service knowledge by gathering customer feedback, analyzing customer service data, and using customer service best practices.
- **Knowledge management**: Knowledge management is the process of managing customer service knowledge. It involves creating, organizing, storing, sharing, and improving customer service knowledge.

### Benefits
KCS provides several benefits to organizations, including:
- Improved customer service: KCS helps organizations provide better customer service by leveraging their existing knowledge.
- Increased customer satisfaction: KCS helps organizations improve customer satisfaction by providing better customer service.
- Reduced costs: KCS helps organizations reduce costs by improving customer service processes and using customer service best practices.
- Increased productivity: KCS helps organizations increase productivity by making it easier to find and use customer service knowledge.
