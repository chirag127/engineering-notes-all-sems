
### Reasoning with Default Information

Default reasoning is a form of reasoning that allows for the drawing of conclusions from incomplete or uncertain information. It is based on the assumption that if no specific information is provided, then a default assumption should be made. Default reasoning is a fundamental concept in Artificial Intelligence (AI) and is used to make decisions and draw conclusions when only partial information is available.

**Definition**

Default reasoning is a form of non-monotonic reasoning that allows for the drawing of conclusions from incomplete or uncertain information by making assumptions about the state of the world. It is based on the assumption that if no specific information is provided, then a default assumption should be made.

**Applications**

Default reasoning is used in many AI applications, such as natural language processing, robotic navigation, and game playing. It is also used in decision making tasks, such as medical diagnosis, financial planning, and legal reasoning.

**Advantages**

Default reasoning has several advantages over other forms of reasoning. It allows for the drawing of conclusions from incomplete or uncertain information, which is often the case in real-world problems. It also allows for the incorporation of prior knowledge and experience into the reasoning process.

**Disadvantages**

Default reasoning also has some drawbacks. It can lead to incorrect conclusions if the default assumptions are not valid. It can also be computationally intensive, as it requires the reasoning system to consider all possible outcomes.

**Example**

Consider the following example: 

A patient comes to a doctor with a fever. The doctor has no other information about the patient's medical history. The doctor can use default reasoning to make an educated guess about the cause of the fever. The doctor might assume that the fever is caused by a viral infection, since that is the most common cause of fever. This assumption can then be tested by running additional tests.
