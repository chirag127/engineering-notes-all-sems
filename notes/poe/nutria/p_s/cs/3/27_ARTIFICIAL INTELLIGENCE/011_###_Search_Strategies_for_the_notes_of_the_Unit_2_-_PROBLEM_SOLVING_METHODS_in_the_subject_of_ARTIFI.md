
### Search Strategies for the notes of the Unit 2 - PROBLEM SOLVING METHODS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

Search strategies are techniques used to solve problems in artificial intelligence. These strategies are designed to help find the optimal solution to a problem by exploring all possible options. The most common search strategies include:

* Breadth-First Search (BFS): BFS is a strategy that starts at the root node and explores all of the neighboring nodes at the present depth before moving on to the nodes at the next depth level.
* Depth-First Search (DFS): DFS is a strategy that starts at the root node and explores as far as possible along each branch before backtracking.
* Iterative Deepening Search (IDS): IDS is a strategy that combines the benefits of both BFS and DFS. It starts with a shallow search and gradually increases the depth of the search.
* A* Search: A* is a strategy that uses an evaluation function to estimate the cost of reaching the goal from any given node. This evaluation function is used to determine which node should be explored next.

These strategies can be used to solve a variety of problems in artificial intelligence, including pathfinding, game playing, and constraint satisfaction. They can also be used to solve more complex problems, such as planning, scheduling, and natural language processing.
