

### Characteristics of Intelligent Agents

Intelligent agents are computer programs that can act autonomously to achieve a specific goal. They are used in a wide range of applications, from robotic agents that explore their environment to self-driving cars. Intelligent agents have certain characteristics that make them well-suited for these tasks.

1. **Perception**: Intelligent agents must be able to perceive their environment in order to act. This means they must be able to sense and interpret data from their environment.

2. **Learning**: Intelligent agents must be able to learn from their environment in order to become more efficient. This means they must be able to use past experiences to make better decisions in the future.

3. **Adaptability**: Intelligent agents must be able to adapt to changing conditions in their environment. This means they must be able to modify their behavior in response to changing conditions.

4. **Goal-Oriented**: Intelligent agents must be goal-oriented in order to be effective. This means they must be able to set and pursue goals in order to achieve their objectives.

5. **Robustness**: Intelligent agents must be robust in order to be reliable. This means they must be able to handle unexpected conditions and still be able to achieve their goals.

These characteristics make intelligent agents well-suited for a wide range of applications, from robotic agents to self-driving cars.
