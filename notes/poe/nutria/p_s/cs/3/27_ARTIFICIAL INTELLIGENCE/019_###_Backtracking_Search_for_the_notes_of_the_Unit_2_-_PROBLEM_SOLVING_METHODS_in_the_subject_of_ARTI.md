
### Backtracking Search for the notes of the Unit 2 - PROBLEM SOLVING METHODS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

Backtracking search is an algorithm for finding a solution to a problem. It is a form of depth-first search that systematically explores the potential solutions to a problem by trying all possible combinations until a solution is found. Backtracking search is useful for solving problems that require a combination of decisions to be made in order to reach a solution.

Backtracking search works by exploring all possible solutions until a solution is found. It can be used to solve a wide variety of problems, including:

* Puzzles such as Sudoku or the 8-Queens Problem
* Games such as chess or checkers
* Optimization problems such as the Traveling Salesman Problem

Backtracking search works by maintaining a list of decisions that have been made and exploring each possible combination of decisions until a solution is found. The algorithm starts with an initial set of decisions and then proceeds to make a decision and explore its consequences. If the decision leads to a solution, then the algorithm is done; otherwise, the algorithm backtracks to the previous decision and tries a different decision. This process is repeated until a solution is found.

Backtracking search is an efficient algorithm for solving problems, but it can be computationally expensive. It is also prone to getting stuck in infinite loops if the problem is not formulated correctly. For these reasons, it is important to ensure that the problem is correctly formulated before attempting to solve it using backtracking search.
