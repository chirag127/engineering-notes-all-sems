
### Stochastic Games
Stochastic games are a type of game in which players must make decisions in the face of uncertainty. This type of game is often used in artificial intelligence research to study how agents can make decisions in uncertain environments.

Stochastic games are composed of two or more players, each of whom has a set of possible actions that they can take. Each action has an associated payoff, which is determined by the state of the game. The state of the game is determined by the players' decisions, and can change over time.

Stochastic games can be used to model a wide range of real-world situations, such as economic decision-making or military strategy. They are also useful for studying the behavior of agents in complex, uncertain environments.

Advantages:
- Stochastic games provide a realistic way to model decision-making in uncertain environments.
- They can be used to study the behavior of agents in complex, uncertain environments.
- They can be used to model a wide range of real-world situations, such as economic decision-making or military strategy.

Disadvantages:
- Stochastic games can be difficult to analyze due to their complexity.
- The payoff structure of stochastic games can be difficult to determine.
- The behavior of agents in stochastic games can be difficult to predict.

Examples:
- Chess is an example of a stochastic game, as players must make decisions in the face of uncertainty.
- Poker is another example of a stochastic game, as players must make decisions based on the cards they are dealt.
- The stock market is an example of a stochastic game, as investors must make decisions based on uncertain market conditions.

Applications:
- Stochastic games can be used to study the behavior of agents in complex, uncertain environments.
- They can be used to model a wide range of real-world situations, such as economic decision-making or military strategy.
- They can be used to analyze the behavior of complex systems, such as the stock market.
- They can be used to develop strategies for decision-making in uncertain environments.
