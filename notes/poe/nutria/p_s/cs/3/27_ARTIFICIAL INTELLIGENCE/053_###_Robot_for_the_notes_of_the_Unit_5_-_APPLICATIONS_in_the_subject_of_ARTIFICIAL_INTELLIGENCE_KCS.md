
### Robot

Robot is an artificial agent, usually an electro-mechanical machine, which is guided by a computer program or electronic circuitry. Robots can be autonomous or semi-autonomous and range from humanoids such as Honda's Advanced Step in Innovative Mobility (ASIMO) and TOSY's TOSY Ping Pong Playing Robot (TOPIO) to industrial robots, medical operating robots, and even microscopic nano robots. 

Robots can be used in a variety of applications, including manufacturing, exploration, search and rescue, entertainment, and medical applications. In the field of Artificial Intelligence (AI), robots are used to perform tasks such as navigation, object recognition, and decision-making.

Robots can be used in a variety of ways in the field of AI, from simple tasks such as navigating a room to more complex tasks such as playing a game of chess. Robots can be used to explore unknown environments, to search for objects, to recognize objects, and to make decisions. In addition, robots can be used for medical applications, such as robotic surgery and robotic prosthetics.

Robots can also be used to create and maintain databases, to process information, and to provide feedback to users. Robots can also be used to interact with humans, such as by providing advice or assistance.

Robotics is an important field in Artificial Intelligence and is used to create intelligent machines that can interact with humans. Robotics is a rapidly growing field and is expected to continue to expand in the future.
