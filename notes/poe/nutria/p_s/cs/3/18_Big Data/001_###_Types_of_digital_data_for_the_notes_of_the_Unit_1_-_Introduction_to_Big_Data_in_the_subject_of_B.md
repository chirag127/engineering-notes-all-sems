
### Types of Digital Data

Digital data can be divided into two main categories: structured and unstructured data. 

#### Structured Data

Structured data is organized and stored in a specific format, such as a database, that can be easily searched and analyzed. This type of data is often used to analyze trends and make predictions. Examples of structured data include customer records, financial records, and sales data.

#### Unstructured Data

Unstructured data is unorganized and does not have a specific format. This type of data is often used to gain insight into customer behavior and preferences. Examples of unstructured data include images, videos, audio recordings, and text documents.

In the field of Big Data, both structured and unstructured data are important. Structured data is used to analyze trends and make predictions, while unstructured data is used to gain insight into customer behavior and preferences. By combining these two types of data, businesses can gain a better understanding of their customers and make more informed decisions.
