
### 5 Vs of Big Data 
Big Data is characterized by the 5 Vs, which are Volume, Variety, Velocity, Veracity and Value.

#### Volume
Volume refers to the amount of data, which can range from terabytes to exabytes. This data can come from a variety of sources, including customer data, social media, sensor data, mobile data, and more.

#### Variety
Variety refers to the different types of data that can be collected. This data can include structured data, semi-structured data, and unstructured data. Structured data is data that is organized in a specific way, such as a database. Semi-structured data is data that is not organized in a specific way, but still contains some structure. Unstructured data is data that is not organized in any way.

#### Velocity
Velocity refers to the speed at which data is generated and collected. This data can be generated from a variety of sources and can be collected in real-time.

#### Veracity
Veracity refers to the accuracy and completeness of the data. This data can be collected from a variety of sources, and it is important to ensure that the data is accurate and complete.

#### Value
Value refers to the value that can be derived from the data. This data can be used to gain insights into customer behavior, market trends, and more. Big Data can be used to make better decisions, improve customer experience, and increase efficiency.
