
### Analysis vs Reporting

Analysis and reporting are two distinct activities that are often used in the field of Big Data. 

**Analysis** is the process of examining data in order to gain insights and draw conclusions. It involves analyzing data to identify patterns and trends, and to identify correlations among variables. Analysis is often used to identify opportunities for improvement, and to inform decision-making.

**Reporting** is the process of communicating the results of analysis to stakeholders. It involves summarizing the findings of analysis and presenting them in a way that is easy to understand. Reports are often used to communicate the results of analysis to decision-makers, and to inform the public about the findings.

Analysis and reporting are both important activities in the field of Big Data. Analysis helps to identify patterns and trends in data, while reporting helps to communicate the results of analysis to stakeholders. Together, they can be used to inform decision-making and to gain insights from data.
