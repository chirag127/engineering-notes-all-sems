
### Challenges of Conventional Systems for the Notes of Unit 1 - Introduction to Big Data in the Subject of Big Data

* Conventional systems are unable to handle large amounts of data efficiently. This is because they are designed to process data in small batches. As a result, they are not suitable for handling large datasets.
* Conventional systems are not designed to handle data that is constantly changing. As a result, they are not able to keep up with the dynamic nature of big data.
* Conventional systems are not designed to process data in real-time. This means that they are not able to respond quickly to changes in data.
* Conventional systems are not designed to handle data from multiple sources. As a result, they are not able to integrate data from different sources.
* Conventional systems are not designed to process unstructured data. As a result, they are not able to process data from sources such as social media, blogs, and other sources of unstructured data.
* Conventional systems are not designed to process streaming data. As a result, they are not able to process data that is constantly changing and updating.
* Conventional systems are not designed to scale to meet the demands of big data. As a result, they are not able to process data in a timely manner.
