
### Big Data features – security, compliance, auditing and protection

Big Data is a powerful tool for businesses and organizations to gain insight into their operations. However, the sheer volume of data that can be collected and analyzed can make it difficult to ensure that the data is secure, compliant with relevant regulations, and properly audited. To ensure the safety and accuracy of Big Data, security, compliance, auditing, and protection measures must be taken.

#### Security

Security measures are necessary to protect data from unauthorized access and malicious actors. This can include encryption, authentication, access control, and other technologies to ensure that only authorized users can access the data.

#### Compliance

Organizations must ensure that their data is compliant with relevant regulations, such as privacy laws and industry-specific standards. This can include data minimization, data retention policies, and other measures to ensure that data is not misused or mishandled.

#### Auditing

Auditing is the process of verifying that data is accurate and up-to-date. This can include periodic checks of data accuracy, as well as checks of the data processing systems to ensure that they are functioning properly.

#### Protection

Data protection measures are necessary to ensure that data is not lost or corrupted. This can include data backups, data replication, and other measures to ensure that data is not lost or corrupted.
