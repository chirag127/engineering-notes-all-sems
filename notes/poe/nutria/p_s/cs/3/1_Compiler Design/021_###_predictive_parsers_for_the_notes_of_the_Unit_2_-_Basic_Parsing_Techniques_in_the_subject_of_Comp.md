
### Predictive Parsers for the Notes of the Unit 2 - Basic Parsing Techniques in the Subject of Compiler Design

Predictive parsers are a type of parser used to analyze the syntax of a given input string. They are commonly used in compiler design to determine if a given string is valid or not. Predictive parsers are also known as recursive descent parsers.

Advantages: 
- Predictive parsers are simple to implement and understand. 
- They are efficient in terms of time and space complexity.
- Predictive parsers are suitable for top-down parsing.

Disadvantages: 
- Predictive parsers are limited to LL(k) grammars. 
- They cannot handle left-recursive grammars.
- Predictive parsers cannot handle ambiguity.

Examples: 
- Predictive parsers are used in compiler design to determine if a given string is valid or not. 
- They can also be used in natural language processing applications to identify the structure of a sentence.

Applications: 
- Predictive parsers are used in compiler design to check the syntax of a given input string. 
- They are also used in natural language processing applications to identify the structure of a sentence. 
- Predictive parsers can be used in text editors to provide syntax highlighting.
