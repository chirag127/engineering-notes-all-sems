
### Priority Driven Approach for the Notes of Unit 2 - Real Time Scheduling in Real Time System

Priority Driven Approach is a scheduling algorithm used in Real Time Systems. It is used to assign priorities to tasks that need to be completed within a specific time frame. The tasks are then scheduled in order of priority, with the most important tasks taking precedence.

Advantages of Priority Driven Approach:

- Gives priority to tasks that are most important and need to be completed first.
- Ensures that tasks are completed within the specified time frame.
- Helps in avoiding any delays or bottlenecks.
- Can be used in applications with varying levels of complexity.

Disadvantages of Priority Driven Approach:

- Does not take into account the resource utilization of a task.
- Can lead to priority inversion, where a low priority task can prevent a higher priority task from being completed.
- May create a situation of starvation, where a low priority task is never completed.

Examples of Priority Driven Approach:

- Operating systems use Priority Driven Approach to assign priorities to processes and threads.
- Real-time embedded systems use Priority Driven Approach to assign priorities to tasks.
- Robotics applications use Priority Driven Approach to assign priorities to tasks.
