
### Offline Versus Online Scheduling

Real-time systems require scheduling algorithms to ensure that processes are executed in a timely fashion. In this unit we will be looking at two types of scheduling algorithms: offline and online. 

**Offline Scheduling** 

Offline scheduling algorithms are those that are used to plan ahead for the execution of processes. These algorithms are usually used to determine the order in which processes should be executed, as well as the amount of time each process should be allocated. Offline scheduling algorithms are usually designed to optimize the utilization of resources, such as CPU time and memory. 

The most common offline scheduling algorithms are: 

- Priority Scheduling 
- Earliest Deadline First 
- Rate Monotonic Scheduling 
- Least Laxity First 

**Online Scheduling** 

Online scheduling algorithms are those that are used to make decisions about the execution of processes in real-time. These algorithms are usually used to determine the order in which processes should be executed, as well as the amount of time each process should be allocated. Online scheduling algorithms are usually designed to optimize the utilization of resources, such as CPU time and memory. 

The most common online scheduling algorithms are: 

- First Come First Serve 
- Shortest Job First 
- Round Robin 
- Deadline Scheduling 

Both offline and online scheduling algorithms have their advantages and disadvantages. Offline scheduling algorithms are usually more efficient, as they can be used to plan ahead for the execution of processes. However, they are usually more complex to implement, as they require the use of a scheduling algorithm to determine the order in which processes should be executed. Online scheduling algorithms are usually simpler to implement, as they can be used to make decisions about the execution of processes in real-time. However, they are usually less efficient, as they are unable to plan ahead for the execution of processes. 

Overall, the choice of which scheduling algorithm to use depends on the specific requirements of the real-time system. In some cases, it may be beneficial to use a combination of both offline and online scheduling algorithms in order to optimize the utilization of resources.
