
### Processors and Resources for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction of Real Time System in the subject of Real Time System

* Processors in Real Time Systems: Processors in real-time systems are usually designed to process data in real time. They are usually designed to process data quickly and accurately. Examples of processors used in real-time systems are microcontrollers, microprocessors, and digital signal processors (DSPs).

* Resources in Real Time Systems: Resources in real-time systems are usually limited by the processor used. Examples of resources used in real-time systems are memory, I/O devices, and communication links.

* Advantages of Real Time Systems: Real-time systems have the advantage of providing timely responses to events. This is especially useful in safety critical applications such as controlling aircraft or medical equipment. Real-time systems also have the advantage of being able to handle large amounts of data quickly and accurately.

* Disadvantages of Real Time Systems: Real-time systems can be expensive to build and maintain. They can also be difficult to debug and troubleshoot. Additionally, real-time systems can be difficult to modify and upgrade.

* Examples of Real Time Systems: Examples of real-time systems include aircraft control systems, medical equipment, industrial control systems, and robotics.

* Applications of Real Time Systems: Real-time systems are used in a variety of applications such as aircraft control systems, medical equipment, industrial control systems, robotics, and video games.
