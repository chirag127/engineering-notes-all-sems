
### Overview of Commercial Real Time Databases 

Real Time Operating Systems (RTOS) and Databases are two important components of Real Time Systems. RTOS is responsible for providing a platform for the application to run on, while databases are responsible for storing and retrieving data. In this unit, we will discuss the overview of commercial real time databases and their importance in real time systems. 

Real time databases are designed to handle data that needs to be accessed and updated quickly. They are typically used in applications that require fast processing and response times, such as financial systems, medical systems, and embedded systems. Real time databases are designed to provide low latency, high throughput, and high availability. They are also designed to handle large volumes of data and provide scalability. 

Real time databases are typically based on a relational model. This means that data is stored in tables, and relationships between data elements are defined. The most popular real time databases are Oracle, Microsoft SQL Server, and IBM DB2. These databases are used in many different industries, including finance, healthcare, and retail. 

Real time databases are typically designed to be fault-tolerant. This means that they can continue to operate even if one or more components fail. They also provide data redundancy, which allows the system to continue to operate even if one or more components are lost. 

Real time databases also provide data security. They can be configured to encrypt data, and they can also be configured to provide access control. This means that only authorized users can access the data. 

Real time databases can also be used to store large amounts of data. This makes them ideal for applications that require large amounts of data, such as data warehouses and data lakes. 

Real time databases are also used in many embedded systems. Embedded systems are typically used in applications such as industrial automation, automotive, and aerospace. They require fast response times, and real time databases can provide this. 

Real time databases are an important part of real time systems. They provide low latency, high throughput, and high availability. They also provide fault-tolerance, data redundancy, and data security. They are also used in embedded systems, and can store large amounts of data.
