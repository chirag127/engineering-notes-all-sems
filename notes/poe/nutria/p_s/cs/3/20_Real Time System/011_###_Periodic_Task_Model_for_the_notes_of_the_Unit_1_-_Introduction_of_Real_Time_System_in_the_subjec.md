
### Periodic Task Model

The Periodic Task Model is a type of real-time system that is used to ensure that tasks are completed on time and in the correct order. This model is based on the concept of scheduling tasks at regular intervals and ensuring that they are completed within a certain period of time. The tasks are usually divided into two categories: hard and soft tasks. Hard tasks are those that must be completed on time and in the correct order, while soft tasks are those that can be delayed or reordered.
