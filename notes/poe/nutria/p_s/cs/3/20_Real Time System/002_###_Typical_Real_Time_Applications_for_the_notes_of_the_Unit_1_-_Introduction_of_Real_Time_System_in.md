
### Typical Real Time Applications 
Real-time systems are used in a variety of applications, such as industrial control systems, medical systems, military systems, and communication systems. 

#### Industrial Control Systems
Industrial control systems are used to monitor and control industrial processes. These systems are used to monitor and control temperature, pressure, flow, and other physical variables. Examples of industrial control systems include process control systems, distributed control systems, and programmable logic controllers. 

#### Medical Systems
Real-time systems are used in medical applications such as medical imaging, patient monitoring, and telemedicine. Medical imaging systems such as X-ray, CT, and MRI use real-time systems to acquire and process images. Patient monitoring systems use real-time systems to monitor the vital signs of patients. Telemedicine systems use real-time systems to provide medical services to remote locations. 

#### Military Systems
Real-time systems are used in military applications such as command and control systems, surveillance systems, and weapon systems. Command and control systems are used to coordinate and control military operations. Surveillance systems are used to detect and track enemy forces. Weapon systems are used to launch and guide missiles and other weapons. 

#### Communication Systems
Real-time systems are used in communication systems such as cellular networks, satellite networks, and data networks. Cellular networks use real-time systems to provide voice and data services. Satellite networks use real-time systems to provide global communication services. Data networks use real-time systems to provide data services such as streaming video and audio.
