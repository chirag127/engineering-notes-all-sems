
### Hard Real Time Systems 

Hard Real Time Systems are computer systems that are designed to guarantee the completion of certain tasks within a predetermined period of time. These systems are used in applications where a single task must be completed within a certain amount of time and failure to do so can result in catastrophic consequences.

#### Advantages

- Hard Real Time Systems are reliable and can guarantee that tasks will be completed within the specified time frame.
- Hard Real Time Systems can be used in safety-critical applications where failure to complete a task on time can have serious consequences.
- Hard Real Time Systems are designed to be efficient and can often complete tasks faster than other types of computer systems.

#### Disadvantages

- Hard Real Time Systems are expensive and require specialized hardware and software.
- Hard Real Time Systems are difficult to design and debug, making them time-consuming and costly to develop.
- Hard Real Time Systems are often limited in terms of the number of tasks they can handle at once.

#### Examples

- Air Traffic Control Systems: Air traffic control systems are hard real time systems as they must be able to accurately and reliably track aircraft in order to ensure the safety of passengers and crew.
- Automotive Systems: Automotive systems, such as those used in self-driving cars, must be able to process large amounts of data in real time in order to make decisions quickly and accurately.
- Industrial Control Systems: Industrial control systems are used to control and monitor industrial processes, such as manufacturing and assembly lines. These systems must be able to respond quickly and accurately in order to ensure the safety of personnel and equipment.

#### Applications

- Automotive Systems: Automotive systems are used in cars, trucks, and other vehicles to monitor and control various aspects of the vehicle, such as the engine, brakes, and suspension.
- Industrial Control Systems: Industrial control systems are used to monitor and control industrial processes, such as manufacturing and assembly lines.
- Robotics: Robotics systems are used in a variety of applications, from medical and industrial robots to consumer robots. These systems must be able to process large amounts of data in real time in order to make decisions quickly and accurately.
