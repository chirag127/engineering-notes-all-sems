
### Priority-Based Service and Weighted Round-Robin Service Disciplines for Switched Networks

Priority-based service and weighted round-robin (WRR) service disciplines are two of the most popular scheduling algorithms used in switched networks. They are used to determine the order in which packets are processed by the network.

#### Priority-Based Service

In priority-based service, each packet is assigned a priority level. Packets with higher priority levels are processed first, while packets with lower priority levels are processed last. This ensures that important packets are not delayed by less important packets.

The main disadvantage of priority-based service is that it is vulnerable to starvation. If a packet with a high priority level is constantly being sent, packets with lower priority levels may never get processed.

#### Weighted Round-Robin Service

In WRR service, each packet is assigned a weight. The higher the weight, the higher the priority of the packet. Packets with higher weights are processed first, while packets with lower weights are processed last.

WRR is more efficient than priority-based service because it prevents starvation. Since all packets are processed in a round-robin fashion, no single packet can monopolize the network.

#### Advantages

Both priority-based service and WRR service have the following advantages:

- They are both simple and easy to implement.
- They can both be adapted to any type of network traffic.
- They can both be used to prioritize certain types of traffic.

#### Disadvantages

The main disadvantage of priority-based service is that it is vulnerable to starvation, while the main disadvantage of WRR service is that it can be difficult to configure.

#### Examples

Priority-based service and WRR service are commonly used in switched networks. For example, they are used in Voice over IP (VoIP) networks, where they are used to prioritize voice traffic over other types of traffic. They are also used in wireless networks, where they are used to prioritize certain types of traffic, such as video streaming or gaming.

#### Applications

Priority-based service and WRR service are used in a variety of applications, including voice and video conferencing, streaming media, online gaming, and virtual private networks. They are also used in wireless networks to prioritize certain types of traffic.
