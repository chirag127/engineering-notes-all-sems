
### Soft Real Time Systems

Soft real time systems are computer systems that are designed to respond to events within a certain amount of time. They are used in applications where timing is critical, such as multimedia applications, computer-aided design, robotics, and embedded systems.

Soft real time systems have the following characteristics:

* They are designed to respond to events within a certain amount of time, usually within milliseconds.
* They are often event-driven, meaning that the system must respond to certain events as soon as they occur.
* They are usually not required to have a deterministic response time.
* They may be able to adjust the amount of time they take to respond to events, depending on the system's current load.

Soft real time systems are often used in applications such as multimedia applications, computer-aided design, robotics, and embedded systems. In multimedia applications, they are used to ensure that audio and video streams are played back in sync. In computer-aided design, they are used to ensure that the design process is completed within a certain time frame. In robotics, they are used to ensure that the robot responds to events quickly and accurately. And in embedded systems, they are used to ensure that the system responds to events quickly and reliably.

Advantages of soft real time systems include:

* They are more flexible than hard real time systems
* They can adjust the amount of time they take to respond to events, depending on the system's current load
* They are less expensive to develop than hard real time systems

Disadvantages of soft real time systems include:

* They are not as reliable as hard real time systems
* They may not be able to guarantee a deterministic response time
* They may not be able to handle large numbers of events in a short amount of time
