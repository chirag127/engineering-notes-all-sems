
### Temporal Parameters of Real Time Workload for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction of Real Time System in the subject of Real Time System

* Temporal parameters of real-time workloads are important for understanding and managing the performance of real-time systems. 
* These parameters include the deadlines, the priority of tasks, the resource requirements, and the preemption characteristics of tasks. 
* The deadline is the time by which a task must be completed. 
* The priority of a task is the importance of the task relative to other tasks. 
* The resource requirements of a task are the amount of CPU, memory, and other resources that the task requires. 
* The preemption characteristics of a task are the conditions under which the task can be interrupted or preempted by higher priority tasks. 
* The temporal parameters of real-time workloads must be taken into consideration when designing and implementing a real-time system. 
* These parameters can be used to ensure that the system meets its performance requirements and that the tasks are completed on time. 
* In addition, temporal parameters can be used to optimize the system's performance and minimize the amount of resources used. 
* For example, by setting deadlines and priorities appropriately, the system can ensure that the most important tasks are completed first and that the system is not overloaded with unnecessary tasks. 
* Temporal parameters can also be used to improve the system's response time and reduce the amount of energy consumed. 
* Finally, temporal parameters can be used to ensure fairness in the system by ensuring that all tasks have an equal chance of being completed on time.
