
## Unit 4 - Real Time Communication
Real time communication (RTC) is a type of communication that enables users to interact with each other in real-time. It is a type of communication that is used in many different fields, such as business, education, healthcare, and entertainment.

RTC is typically enabled through the use of audio, video, and text-based messaging. It is commonly used for two-way conversations, but can also be used for group conversations.

Advantages of RTC:
- Allows for immediate feedback and response
- Easier to stay connected with remote colleagues and customers
- Can be used to conduct virtual meetings and conferences
- Can be used to facilitate real-time collaboration

Disadvantages of RTC:
- Can be difficult to manage large groups of participants
- Can be disruptive to other activities
- Can be difficult to maintain focus
- Can be difficult to ensure privacy and security

Examples of RTC:
- Video conferencing
- Voice over IP (VoIP)
- Instant messaging
- Online gaming

Applications of RTC:
- Business: RTC is used for virtual meetings, conferences, and team collaboration.
- Education: RTC is used for online classes, lectures, and tutoring.
- Healthcare: RTC is used for telemedicine, remote diagnosis, and patient monitoring.
- Entertainment: RTC is used for video streaming, gaming, and social networks.

RTC has become an essential tool for businesses and organizations of all sizes. It has enabled organizations to stay connected and collaborate with remote colleagues and customers. It has also enabled organizations to conduct virtual meetings and conferences with ease.
