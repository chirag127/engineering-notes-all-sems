
## Unit 4 - ARTIFICIAL NEURAL NETWORKS

Artificial Neural Networks (ANNs) are a type of machine learning algorithm that mimics the behavior of biological neural networks. ANNs are composed of interconnected nodes, or neurons, that process information and pass it along to other neurons. The connections between neurons can be adjusted to learn from data and make predictions about new data.

ANNs have a wide range of applications, from image and speech recognition to natural language processing and autonomous vehicles. They are also used for forecasting, medical diagnosis, and financial analysis.

The basic components of an ANN are:

- Input layer: This layer takes in the input data.
- Hidden layers: These layers process the input data and pass it along to the output layer.
- Output layer: This layer produces the output of the network.

The network is trained by adjusting the weights of the connections between neurons. This process is called backpropagation and is based on the principle of gradient descent.

Advantages of ANNs:

- They can learn from data and make predictions about new data.
- They can be used for a wide range of tasks, from image and speech recognition to natural language processing and autonomous vehicles.
- They can be used for forecasting, medical diagnosis, and financial analysis.

Disadvantages of ANNs:

- They require a large amount of data for training.
- They can be difficult to interpret and debug.
- They are prone to overfitting.
