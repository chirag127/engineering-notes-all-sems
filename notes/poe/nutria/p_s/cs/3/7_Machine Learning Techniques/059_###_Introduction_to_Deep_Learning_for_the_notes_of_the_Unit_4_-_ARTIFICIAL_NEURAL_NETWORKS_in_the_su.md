

### Introduction to Deep Learning for the Notes of Unit 4 - ARTIFICIAL NEURAL NETWORKS

Deep Learning is a subfield of Machine Learning that uses Artificial Neural Networks (ANNs) to learn from data and make predictions. Deep Learning has become increasingly popular in recent years due to its ability to learn complex patterns from large datasets.

#### What is an Artificial Neural Network?
An Artificial Neural Network (ANN) is a type of Machine Learning algorithm that is inspired by the structure and functioning of the human brain. It consists of a network of interconnected nodes (or neurons) that process input signals and generate output signals. The nodes are connected to each other via weights, which are adjusted during the training process to optimize the network's ability to accurately predict outputs.

#### How Does an Artificial Neural Network Work?
An ANN works by taking in an input signal and passing it through a series of interconnected layers of neurons. Each layer of neurons processes the input signal and generates an output signal, which is then passed to the next layer. This process is repeated until the output signal is generated. The weights of the connections between the neurons are adjusted during the training process to optimize the network's ability to accurately predict outputs.

#### Advantages of Artificial Neural Networks
- ANNs are able to learn complex patterns from large datasets.
- ANNs can be used to classify data into different categories.
- ANNs are able to generalize from data, meaning they can make accurate predictions on unseen data.
- ANNs are able to perform tasks such as image recognition and natural language processing.

#### Disadvantages of Artificial Neural Networks
- ANNs require large amounts of data to train effectively.
- ANNs are computationally expensive and require powerful hardware to run effectively.
- ANNs are difficult to interpret, meaning it is difficult to understand how they make decisions.
- ANNs can be prone to overfitting, meaning they may not generalize well to unseen data.

#### Examples of Artificial Neural Networks
- Image recognition
- Speech recognition
- Natural language processing
- Machine translation
- Autonomous vehicles

#### Applications of Artificial Neural Networks
- Medical diagnosis
- Pattern recognition
- Image processing
- Text classification
- Time series analysis
- Financial forecasting
