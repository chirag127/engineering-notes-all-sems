
### Mutation for the Notes of Unit 5 - Reinforcement Learning in Machine Learning Techniques

Mutation is a technique used in reinforcement learning to alter the parameters of a policy in order to improve its performance. It is an evolutionary algorithm that works by making small random changes to the parameters of a policy, and then evaluating the results of these changes to determine if they are beneficial.

Mutation can be used to improve the performance of a policy by making small changes to its parameters. The idea is that small changes can lead to large improvements in performance. The process of mutation involves randomly changing the parameters of a policy, and then evaluating the results of the changes to determine if they are beneficial.

Mutation can also be used to explore new areas of a policy. By making small random changes to the parameters of a policy, it is possible to explore new areas of the policy space that may not have been explored before. This can lead to improved performance as well as new insights into the behavior of the policy.
