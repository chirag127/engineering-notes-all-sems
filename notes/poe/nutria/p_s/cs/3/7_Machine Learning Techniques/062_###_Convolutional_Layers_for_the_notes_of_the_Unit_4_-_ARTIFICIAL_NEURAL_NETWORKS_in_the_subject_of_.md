
### Convolutional Layers

Convolutional layers are a type of layer used in Artificial Neural Networks (ANNs). They are used to extract features from images and other data sources. Convolutional layers are composed of many neurons, each of which is connected to a small region of the input data. Each neuron takes a weighted sum of the values in its region and passes it through an activation function to produce an output.

#### Advantages

- Convolutional layers are highly efficient for feature extraction, as they are able to detect patterns in data using fewer parameters than other ANN layers. 
- They are also able to reduce the number of parameters needed to train a model, by sharing weights across neurons.
- Convolutional layers are well-suited for processing images, as they can identify patterns in the spatial arrangement of pixels, such as edges and shapes.

#### Disadvantages

- Convolutional layers are limited in their ability to detect patterns in data that is not arranged in a regular grid, such as text or audio data. 
- They are also limited in their ability to detect patterns that span multiple regions, such as objects in an image.

#### Examples

Convolutional layers are often used in computer vision applications, such as object recognition and image segmentation. They are also used in natural language processing applications, such as text classification and sentiment analysis.

#### Applications

- Image recognition 
- Image segmentation
- Object detection
- Text classification 
- Sentiment analysis 
- Time series forecasting 
- Speech recognition
