
### Training of Network for the Notes of Unit 4 - ARTIFICIAL NEURAL NETWORKS in the Subject of Machine Learning Techniques

* Artificial Neural Networks (ANNs) are a type of machine learning algorithm inspired by biological neural networks. They are used to solve complex problems, such as image recognition and natural language processing.
* Training an ANN involves providing it with a set of inputs and a set of desired outputs. The ANN then adjusts its weights and biases to produce the desired output when given the input.
* The training process is iterative, meaning that the ANN is constantly adjusting its weights and biases until it is able to accurately predict the desired output for a given input.
* The most common training algorithm used for ANNs is backpropagation. This algorithm works by calculating the error between the predicted output and the desired output, and then using this error to adjust the weights and biases of the ANN.
* There are several different techniques for training ANNs, such as supervised learning, unsupervised learning, and reinforcement learning. Each technique has its own advantages and disadvantages, and is best suited for different types of problems.
* ANNs can be used for a variety of tasks, such as image recognition, natural language processing, and even playing games. They can also be used to solve complex problems such as predicting stock prices or diagnosing diseases.
