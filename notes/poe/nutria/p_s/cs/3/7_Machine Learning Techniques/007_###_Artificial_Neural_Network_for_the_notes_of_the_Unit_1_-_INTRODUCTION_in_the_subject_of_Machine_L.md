
### Artificial Neural Network

An artificial neural network (ANN) is a type of machine learning algorithm based on the structure and functions of biological neural networks. It is composed of an interconnected group of artificial neurons that can receive, process, and transmit data to other neurons. ANNs are used to solve complex problems such as image recognition, natural language processing, and autonomous vehicle navigation.

Advantages:
* ANNs are capable of learning from data and generalizing from the examples they are given.
* ANNs can be used to model complex nonlinear relationships between inputs and outputs.
* ANNs are able to identify patterns in data that are too complex for traditional methods.

Disadvantages:
* ANNs are sensitive to the noise in the data, which can lead to incorrect results.
* ANNs require large amounts of data for training, which can be difficult and time consuming to obtain.
* ANNs are difficult to interpret, which can lead to difficulty in debugging and understanding the results.

Applications:
* Image recognition
* Natural language processing
* Autonomous vehicle navigation
* Medical diagnosis
* Fraud detection
* Recommendation systems
* Time series forecasting
