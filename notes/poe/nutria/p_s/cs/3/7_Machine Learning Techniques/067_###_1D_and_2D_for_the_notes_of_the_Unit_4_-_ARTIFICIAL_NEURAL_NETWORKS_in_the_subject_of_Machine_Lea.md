
### 1D and 2D Artificial Neural Networks

1D and 2D Artificial Neural Networks are types of neural networks that are used in machine learning techniques. They are used to model complex relationships between inputs and outputs, and are able to learn from data and make predictions.

1D Artificial Neural Networks:

1D Artificial Neural Networks are composed of a single layer of neurons, which are connected by weights and biases. These networks can be used for simple tasks such as predicting the outcome of a linear equation. They are generally used for simple problems that require a small number of inputs and outputs.

2D Artificial Neural Networks:

2D Artificial Neural Networks are composed of multiple layers of neurons, which are connected by weights and biases. These networks can be used for more complex tasks such as recognizing patterns in images or predicting the outcome of a non-linear equation. They are generally used for more complex problems that require a large number of inputs and outputs.

Advantages:

- They are able to learn from data and make predictions.
- They are able to recognize patterns in data.
- They are able to process large amounts of data quickly.
- They are able to handle non-linear relationships between inputs and outputs.

Disadvantages:

- They can be difficult to design and train.
- They can be computationally expensive.
- They can be prone to overfitting.

Examples:

- Image recognition
- Text classification
- Time series forecasting
- Speech recognition

Applications:

- Autonomous vehicles
- Natural language processing
- Recommendation systems
- Fraud detection
