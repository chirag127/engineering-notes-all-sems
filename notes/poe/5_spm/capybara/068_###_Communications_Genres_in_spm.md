### Communications Genres in SPM

In SPM, communication genres refer to different types of written and spoken communication that students are expected to be familiar with. These genres are important for effective communication in various settings including academic, professional, and personal contexts. Here are some of the main communication genres that you should know for your SPM exams:

1. Narrative
- A narrative is a type of writing that tells a story or describes an event in a chronological sequence.
- Mnemonic: Think of a narrative as a "narrated" story.

2. Descriptive
- Descriptive writing is used to create a vivid picture or sensory experience for the reader by using detailed language and sensory imagery.
- Mnemonic: Think of descriptive writing as painting a picture with words.

3. Expository
- Expository writing is used to explain or inform the reader about a topic by presenting ideas, facts, and evidence in a logical and organized way.
- Mnemonic: Think of expository writing as an "expose" of information.

4. Argumentative/Persuasive
- Argumentative or persuasive writing is used to convince the reader to accept a particular point of view or take a specific action by providing evidence and logical reasoning.
- Mnemonic: Think of argumentative writing as making an "argument" to persuade someone.

5. Informal/Formal Letter
- Letter writing is used to communicate thoughts, feelings, and ideas to someone else in a written format. In SPM, students are expected to be familiar with writing both informal and formal letters.
- Mnemonic: Think of letter writing as "letting" someone know your thoughts and feelings.

6. Speech/Talk
- A speech or talk is a type of oral communication that is used to convey ideas, persuade an audience, or inform listeners about a topic.
- Mnemonic: Think of a speech or talk as "speaking" your mind to an audience.

Overall, understanding the different communication genres in SPM is essential for effective communication in various settings. Knowing the key characteristics and purposes of each genre can help you choose the appropriate communication style for different situations, and effectively convey your message to your audience.
