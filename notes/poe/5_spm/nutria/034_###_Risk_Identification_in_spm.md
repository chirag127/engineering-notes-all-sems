
### Risk Identification in spm

* Risk identification is the process of identifying and analyzing potential risks that could affect a project, program, or organization. It is an important part of project management and should be done early in the project planning process.
* Risk identification should include identifying potential risks, assessing their probability, and determining the impact on the project.
* Common risks to consider include schedule, budget, scope, quality, and resource risks.
* Mnemonics such as "PESTLE" (Political, Economic, Social, Technological, Legal, Environmental) and "SWOT" (Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, Threats) can be used to help identify potential risks.
* Risks should be documented in a risk register. This should include a description of the risk, the probability and impact of the risk, and potential mitigation strategies.
* Risk identification should be an ongoing process throughout the project, as new risks may arise as the project progresses.
