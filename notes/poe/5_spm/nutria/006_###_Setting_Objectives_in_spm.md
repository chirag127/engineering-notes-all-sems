
### Setting Objectives in SPM 

* Setting objectives is the first step in creating a successful SPM project. It involves identifying the goals and objectives of the project and establishing a timeline for completion.
* It is important to consider the scope of the project and how it will fit into the overall plan. The objectives should be specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, and time-bound (SMART).
* When setting objectives, it is important to consider the resources available and the timeline for completion. It is also important to consider the budget and any potential risks or challenges.
* It is also important to consider the stakeholders involved in the project and their expectations. This will help ensure that the objectives are achievable and that the project meets the desired outcome.
* Mnemonics and learning tricks can be helpful in setting objectives. For example, the acronym SMART (specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, and time-bound) can be used to ensure that objectives are set correctly.
* Finally, it is important to review the objectives regularly to ensure that they are still relevant and achievable. This will help ensure that the project is completed on time and within budget.
