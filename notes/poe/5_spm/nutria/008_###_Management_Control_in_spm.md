
### Management Control in SPM

* Management control is a process used by organizations to ensure that resources are used effectively and efficiently in order to achieve the organization's goals and objectives. 
* It is a process of setting performance standards, measuring actual performance, and taking corrective action as needed.
* It involves setting objectives, developing plans to achieve them, and measuring and evaluating the results.
* It is used to monitor and control activities within an organization and to ensure that resources are used in the most effective and efficient way.
* Management control systems are designed to provide feedback on the performance of the organization and to help identify areas where performance can be improved.
* Management control systems can be used to measure the performance of individual employees, departments, and the organization as a whole.
* They can also be used to identify potential problems and areas of improvement.
* Mnemonics and learning tricks for management control in SPM include: 
  * P - Plan 
  * M - Monitor 
  * E - Evaluate 
  * A - Adjust 
  * C - Control 
* These mnemonics provide a simple way to remember the steps of management control and can be used to help understand and apply the concepts.
* Advantages of management control systems include improved efficiency, increased productivity, better decision making, and improved communication.
* Disadvantages of management control systems include increased costs, increased bureaucracy, and potential for abuse.
* Examples of management control systems include budgeting, performance management, and cost control.
* Applications of management control systems include monitoring and controlling costs, setting and measuring performance standards, and evaluating performance.
