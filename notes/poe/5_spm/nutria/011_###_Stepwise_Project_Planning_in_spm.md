

### Stepwise Project Planning in SPM

1. **Project Initiation and Definition**: This step involves defining the scope of the project, setting the timeline and budget, and identifying the project stakeholders.

2. **Work Breakdown Structure (WBS)**: This step involves breaking down the project into smaller tasks and activities. This will help in better understanding of the project and its deliverables.

3. **Resource Planning**: This step involves identifying the resources needed to complete the project. This includes human resources, materials, and equipment.

4. **Scheduling**: This step involves creating a timeline for the project and determining when tasks should be completed and by whom.

5. **Risk Management**: This step involves identifying potential risks and developing strategies to mitigate them.

6. **Monitoring and Control**: This step involves monitoring the project and making necessary changes to ensure that the project is completed on time and within budget.

7. **Closure**: This step involves evaluating the project and summarizing the results.
