

### Dynamic System Development Method in spm

Dynamic System Development Method (DSDM) is an iterative and incremental approach to software development that emphasizes continuous user involvement, rapid development of a working system, and frequent delivery of incremental system versions. It is one of the Agile methods for developing software and is used in the development of software products.

The main features of DSDM are:

- **Timeboxing**: The project is divided into multiple timeboxes, each with a fixed duration and a defined set of deliverables.
- **Iterative and Incremental Development**: The system is developed in multiple iterations, each of which adds new features and improves existing ones.
- **Focus on Business Needs**: The development process is driven by business needs and customer requirements.
- **Continuous User Involvement**: The customer is involved in the development process throughout the project.
- **Collaborative and Co-operative Approach**: The development team works together to ensure the successful delivery of the system.
- **Prototyping**: Prototypes are used to explore and refine the system requirements.
- **Flexibility**: The development process is flexible and can be adjusted to accommodate changes in the system requirements.

DSDM is a good choice for projects that require a rapid development cycle, such as those involving web-based applications or mobile applications. It is also well-suited to projects that involve a large number of stakeholders and require frequent interaction between the development team and the customer.
