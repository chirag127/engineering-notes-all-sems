
## Activities in SPM

1. **Memorizing:** Memorizing is a key activity in SPM, as it helps to remember the important topics and facts. To make the memorizing process easier, it is recommended to use mnemonics and other learning tricks.
2. **Studying:** Studying is an important activity in SPM as it helps to understand the material and refresh the memory. It is recommended to use various techniques such as active reading, highlighting and summarizing to make the studying process more effective.
3. **Practicing:** Practicing is essential in SPM as it helps to consolidate the knowledge and check the understanding of the material. It is recommended to practice with sample questions and mock tests to get familiar with the exam format.
4. **Revising:** Revising is a crucial activity in SPM as it helps to review the topics and refresh the memory. It is recommended to revise regularly, especially before the exam.
5. **Time Management:** Time management is an important activity in SPM as it helps to plan the study sessions and allocate the time effectively. It is recommended to create a study plan and stick to it.
