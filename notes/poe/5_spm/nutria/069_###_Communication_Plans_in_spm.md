
### Communication Plans in SPM

- **Communication Plans** are documents that outline how a project team will communicate with each other, stakeholders, and other interested parties.
- Communication plans should include the following:
    - **Goals**: What is the purpose of the communication plan? 
    - **Audience**: Who should receive the communication?
    - **Frequency**: How often should communication be sent?
    - **Format**: What format should the communication take?
    - **Content**: What information should be included in the communication?
    - **Responsibilities**: Who is responsible for creating and sending the communication?
- Communication plans should be tailored to the specific project and team, and should be regularly reviewed to ensure that it is still meeting the needs of the project.
- Mnemonics and learning tricks for Communication Plans in SPM include:
    - **C**: Create a **C**ommunication **P**lan
    - **A**: **A**udience, **F**requency, **F**ormat, **C**ontent and **R**esponsibilities
    - **M**: **M**ake sure the plan is **M**easurable and **M**anageable
    - **P**: **P**lan ahead and **P**rovide feedback
