
### Managing People in SPM

- Managing people in SPM involves understanding the different tasks, roles, and responsibilities of each individual involved in the project. 
- It requires being able to identify the skills and abilities of each team member, and then assigning roles accordingly. 
- It also involves developing and managing a team-based approach to project management, where each team member has a set of specific responsibilities. 
- It also involves understanding the different stages of the project, and the different tasks that need to be completed at each stage. 
- It also involves understanding the importance of communication and collaboration among team members, and how to effectively manage the project. 
- It also involves understanding the different tools and techniques available for managing people in SPM, such as project management software, project management templates, and project management frameworks. 
- Finally, it involves understanding the importance of training and mentoring team members, and how to effectively use these tools and techniques to ensure the success of the project.
