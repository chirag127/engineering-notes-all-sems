
### Forward Pass & Backward Pass Techniques in spm

- **Forward Pass:** This technique is used to calculate the output of a given neural network. It involves passing the input data through the network, layer by layer, until the output is obtained. At each layer, the weights and biases of the neurons are applied to the inputs to generate the outputs.

- **Backward Pass:** This technique is used to calculate the weights and biases of the neurons in the network. It involves propagating the error signal from the output layer back through the network, layer by layer, until the weights and biases of the neurons are updated.

- **Mnemonics:** To help remember the difference between forward and backward pass, it is useful to remember the phrase "Forward Pass is for Output, Backward Pass is for Weight".

- **Advantages:** The forward and backward passes are essential techniques in training a neural network. They allow the network to learn from its mistakes and improve its performance over time.

- **Disadvantages:** The forward and backward passes can be computationally expensive, especially when the network is large and complex.

- **Examples:** Forward and backward passes are used in a variety of machine learning tasks, such as image recognition, natural language processing, and robotics.

- **Applications:** Forward and backward passes are used in a variety of applications, such as computer vision, natural language processing, and robotics.
