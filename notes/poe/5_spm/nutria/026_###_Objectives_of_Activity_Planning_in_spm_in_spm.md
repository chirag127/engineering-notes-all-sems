
### Objectives of Activity Planning in SPM

1. To identify the activities necessary to complete the project and define the relationships among them.
2. To determine the duration of each activity.
3. To determine the resources (people, equipment, materials, etc.) needed for each activity.
4. To identify the critical activities and their paths.
5. To develop a schedule for the project that meets the project objectives.
6. To identify the slack time available in the project.
7. To identify the risk associated with the activities.
8. To create an effective monitoring and control system.

Activity planning is an important part of project management and involves the identification of all the activities required to complete the project. It involves the determination of the duration of each activity, the resources needed for each activity, and the relationships among the activities. Activity planning also helps to identify the critical activities and their paths, develop a schedule for the project, identify the slack time available in the project, and identify the risk associated with the activities. Finally, an effective monitoring and control system should be created to ensure that the project is completed on time and within budget.
