
## Unit - 1 - Project Evaluation and Project Planning for the notes of the course Software Project Management

Project Evaluation and Project Planning are two essential components of Software Project Management. 

**Project Evaluation** is the process of assessing the feasibility and viability of a project. It involves analyzing the project’s objectives, risks, and costs to determine if it is worth pursuing. 

**Project Planning** is the process of setting goals and objectives, creating a timeline, and assigning tasks to team members. It is important to plan out the project in order to ensure that it is completed on time and within budget. 

Mnemonics and learning tricks:

1. **P**lanning **E**valuate **R**isks **P**rioritizing **R**esources
2. **R**esearch **E**stimate **S**cheduling **P**lanning **E**xecution
3. **E**valuate **R**esources **P**rioritize **P**lan **E**xecute 

Advantages of Project Evaluation and Planning: 

1. It ensures that the project is completed on time and within budget.
2. It helps to identify and mitigate risks.
3. It helps to assign tasks and resources efficiently.
4. It helps to ensure that the project meets its objectives.

Disadvantages of Project Evaluation and Planning:

1. It can be time-consuming.
2. It can be expensive.
3. It can be difficult to accurately estimate the cost and duration of a project.

Examples of Project Evaluation and Planning:

1. A software development company is planning to develop a new mobile app. The project evaluation and planning process would involve researching the market, estimating the cost and duration of the project, and assigning tasks to team members.
2. A construction company is planning to build a new office building. The project evaluation and planning process would involve researching the local laws and regulations, estimating the cost and duration of the project, and assigning tasks to team members.
