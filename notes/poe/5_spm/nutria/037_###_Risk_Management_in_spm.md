
### Risk Management in SPM

Risk management in SPM is a process of identifying, assessing, and controlling potential risks to an organization's project management system. Risk management is an important part of the project management process, as it helps to ensure that the project is completed on time and within budget.

* Identifying risks: This involves identifying any potential risks that could affect the project, such as changes in the project scope, unexpected delays, or changes in the project's environment.

* Assessing risks: This involves assessing the potential impact of each identified risk on the project, such as the cost of mitigation or the potential impact on the project timeline.

* Controlling risks: This involves taking steps to reduce or eliminate the identified risks. This may involve changing the project scope, developing a contingency plan, or implementing additional controls.

Mnemonics and learning tricks:

* R - Recognize: Identify potential risks
* I - Investigate: Assess the potential impact of the risks
* S - Secure: Take steps to reduce or eliminate the risks
* K - Keep: Monitor and review the risks regularly
