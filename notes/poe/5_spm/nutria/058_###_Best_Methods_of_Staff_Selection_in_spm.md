
### Best Methods of Staff Selection in SPM

1. **Competency-based selection**: This method of staff selection involves assessing the skills, knowledge and abilities of potential candidates and hiring those who best meet the criteria. This is a useful approach for identifying qualified individuals who have the right skills to do the job.

2. **Behavioral interviewing**: This technique involves asking potential candidates questions about their past experiences and behaviors to identify how they would respond to certain situations and challenges. This is a useful approach for assessing how well a candidate will fit into the organization's culture.

3. **Psychometric testing**: This type of testing assesses a candidate's cognitive and personality traits to determine how well they would fit into the organization and the role. This can be a useful tool for selecting the most suitable candidates.

4. **Work sample tests**: This approach involves giving potential candidates a task to complete in order to assess their skills and abilities. This is a useful approach for assessing how well a candidate would perform in the job.

5. **Reference checks**: This involves checking the references of potential candidates to assess their suitability for the role. This is an important step in the selection process and can be a useful tool for identifying the best candidate.

6. **Mnemonics and learning tricks**: Mnemonics and learning tricks can be useful tools for helping candidates remember important information and processes. This can be a useful approach for selecting the most suitable candidates.
