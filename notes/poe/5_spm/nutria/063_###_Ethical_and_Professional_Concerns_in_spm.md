
### Ethical and Professional Concerns in SPM

1. **Data Privacy:** It is important to ensure that the data collected and stored is done in a secure manner, and that the privacy of the individuals involved is respected.
2. **Data Security:** It is important to ensure that the data collected and stored is secure, and that the data is not accessed by any unauthorized personnel.
3. **Data Integrity:** It is important to ensure that the data collected and stored is accurate and up-to-date.
4. **Data Ownership:** It is important to ensure that the data collected and stored belongs to the rightful owner, and that the owner has control over the data.
5. **Data Accuracy:** It is important to ensure that the data collected and stored is accurate and up-to-date.
6. **Data Accessibility:** It is important to ensure that the data collected and stored is accessible to those who need it.
7. **Data Sharing:** It is important to ensure that the data collected and stored is shared in a secure and appropriate manner.
8. **Data Protection:** It is important to ensure that the data collected and stored is protected from unauthorized access.
9. **Data Usage:** It is important to ensure that the data collected and stored is used in an ethical and responsible manner.
10. **Data Storage:** It is important to ensure that the data collected and stored is stored securely and in an appropriate manner.
