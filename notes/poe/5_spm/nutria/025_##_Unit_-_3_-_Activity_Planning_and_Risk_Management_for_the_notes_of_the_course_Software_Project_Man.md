
## Unit - 3 - Activity Planning and Risk Management for the notes of the course Software Project Management

Activity Planning and Risk Management are two important components of software project management. Activity Planning involves the identification of tasks and activities that need to be completed in order to achieve the project objectives. Risk Management involves the identification and assessment of potential risks that may affect the project and the implementation of strategies to minimize their impact.

### Mnemonics

A helpful mnemonic for remembering the steps of Activity Planning and Risk Management is:

**PRAIR**

* **P**lanning: Identify tasks and activities that need to be completed
* **R**isk Identification: Identify potential risks to the project
* **A**ssessment: Assess the potential impact of the identified risks
* **I**mplementation: Implement strategies to minimize the impact of the identified risks
* **R**eview: Regularly review the project and the identified risks

### Advantages

* Activity Planning and Risk Management help to ensure that the project is completed on time and within budget.
* They provide a structure and framework for the project, allowing the team to focus on the tasks that need to be completed.
* They help to identify potential risks and to develop strategies to address them.

### Disadvantages

* Activity Planning and Risk Management can be time-consuming and costly.
* They require detailed analysis of the project and potential risks, which can be difficult to complete.
* They can be difficult to implement in practice, as they require detailed knowledge and experience.
