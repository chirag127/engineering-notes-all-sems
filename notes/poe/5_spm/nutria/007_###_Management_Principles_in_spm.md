
### Management Principles in spm

- **Planning:** Planning is the process of setting goals, developing strategies, and outlining tasks and schedules to accomplish the goals. It is an important management principle as it helps to ensure that resources are used efficiently and that activities are completed in a timely manner.

- **Organizing:** Organizing is the process of assigning tasks, allocating resources, and establishing relationships among the members of the organization. It helps to ensure that the organization runs smoothly and that tasks are completed in a timely manner.

- **Leading:** Leading is the process of motivating, inspiring, and guiding individuals and groups to achieve organizational goals. It is an important management principle as it helps to ensure that the organization is able to reach its goals.

- **Controlling:** Controlling is the process of monitoring and evaluating the performance of the organization. It helps to ensure that the organization is able to reach its goals and that activities are completed in a timely manner.
