
### Leadership in SPM

* Leadership in SPM is the ability to identify and manage the resources, processes, and people needed to achieve a desired outcome. 
* It involves the ability to motivate and inspire others, to build consensus, to make decisions, and to manage change. 
* Leadership in SPM requires the ability to develop and manage relationships, to create a vision, and to communicate effectively.
* Leadership in SPM involves understanding and managing the dynamics of a project, including the stakeholders, the goals, and the resources. 
* It also requires the ability to analyze data and make decisions based on the available information.
* Leadership in SPM requires the ability to create and manage a project plan, and to identify and address risks.
* Good leadership in SPM also involves the ability to identify and manage conflicts, to delegate tasks, and to manage the team.
* A good leader in SPM should be able to foster collaboration and creativity, and to motivate and inspire the team.
* Good leadership in SPM also involves the ability to understand and manage the different personalities on the team, and to create an environment of trust and respect.
* Finally, good leadership in SPM requires the ability to recognize and reward the contributions of others, and to provide feedback and support.
