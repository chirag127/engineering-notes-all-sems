
### Project Schedules in SPM

* **Project Scheduling** is the process of determining the sequence, duration, and interdependencies of activities and tasks that must be completed in order to achieve a successful project outcome.
* **Program Management (SPM)** is the application of knowledge, skills, tools, and techniques to project activities in order to meet the project requirements.
* A **Project Schedule** is a document that outlines the sequence of activities and tasks, dependencies, and resource requirements that must be met in order to complete the project on time and within budget.
* Project Schedules in SPM are typically divided into three stages: **Planning**, **Execution**, and **Closure**.
* During the **Planning** stage, the project team will define the project scope, timeline, and budget. They will also identify the tasks, resources, and dependencies that are required to complete the project.
* During the **Execution** stage, the project team will monitor and control the progress of the project. They will also make adjustments as needed to ensure that the project is completed on time and within budget.
* During the **Closure** stage, the project team will review the project results and document any lessons learned. They will also close out any outstanding tasks and ensure that the project is completed according to the project plan.
* Mnemonics and learning tricks for Project Schedules in SPM include:
    * **P**lanning: Define the project scope, timeline, and budget.
    * **E**xecution: Monitor and control the progress of the project.
    * **C**losure: Review the project results and document any lessons learned.
