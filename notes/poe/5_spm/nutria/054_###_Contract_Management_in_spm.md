
### Contract Management in spm
Contract management in software project management (SPM) is the process of managing contractual agreements between two or more parties. It involves the review, negotiation, and execution of contracts. It also includes monitoring, tracking, and reporting on contract performance and compliance.

Contract management in SPM is an important part of the project management process. It ensures that contractual obligations are met, and that any changes to the contract are documented and agreed upon. It also helps to ensure that the project is completed on time and within budget.

Contract management in SPM includes the following steps:

1. Review: Reviewing the contract to ensure that all parties understand the terms and conditions of the agreement.

2. Negotiation: Negotiating the terms and conditions of the contract with the other parties involved.

3. Execution: Executing the contract, which includes signing and delivering the contract to all parties involved.

4. Monitoring: Monitoring the performance of the contract to ensure that all parties are meeting their obligations.

5. Tracking: Tracking the progress of the contract to ensure that all parties are meeting their obligations.

6. Reporting: Reporting on the performance of the contract to ensure that all parties are meeting their obligations.
