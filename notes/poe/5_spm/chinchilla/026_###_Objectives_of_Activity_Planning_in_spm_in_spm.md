### Objectives of Activity Planning in SPM

Activity planning is a crucial part of project management, and its objective is to identify the activities that need to be performed in order to achieve the project's goals. In SPM (Software Project Management), activity planning is a key process that involves the identification, sequencing, and scheduling of project activities. The objectives of activity planning in SPM are as follows:

1. To identify project activities: The first objective of activity planning is to identify all the activities that need to be performed in order to achieve the project's goals. This involves breaking down the project into smaller, more manageable tasks and identifying the dependencies between them.

2. To sequence project activities: Once all the activities have been identified, the next objective is to sequence them in the order they need to be performed. This involves identifying the logical relationships between activities and determining which activities need to be completed before others can begin.

3. To estimate activity durations: Another objective of activity planning is to estimate the time it will take to complete each activity. This involves considering factors such as the complexity of the task, the resources required, and the skill level of the team members who will be performing the task.

4. To allocate resources: Activity planning also involves allocating resources to each activity. This includes determining the number of team members needed, the equipment required, and any other resources necessary to complete the task.

5. To develop a schedule: The final objective of activity planning is to develop a schedule for the project. This involves determining the start and end dates for each activity and creating a timeline that shows when each activity will be performed.

Mnemonics and learning tricks:

- Remember the acronym ISEAS (Identify, Sequence, Estimate, Allocate, Schedule) to help you remember the objectives of activity planning in SPM.
- Use a flowchart to visualize the activity planning process and the objectives involved. This can help you better understand the sequence of tasks and how they relate to each other.

In conclusion, the objectives of activity planning in SPM are to identify, sequence, estimate, allocate, and schedule project activities. By achieving these objectives, project managers can ensure that their projects are completed on time, within budget, and to the satisfaction of their stakeholders.
