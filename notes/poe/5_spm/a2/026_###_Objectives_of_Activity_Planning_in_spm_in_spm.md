### Objectives of Activity Planning in spm

The main objectives of activity planning in software project management (spm) are:

1. Estimating the effort required - Activity planning helps in estimating the effort required for each task or activity. This helps in allocating resources and scheduling the activities properly.
2. Scheduling the activities - The activities are scheduled by considering their dependencies and the effort estimates. This results in a realistic project schedule.
3. Allocating resources - By knowing the effort estimates and schedule, resources can be allocated to each activity effectively. This avoids over or under allocation of resources.
4. Monitoring and controlling - The actual progress is monitored against the planned schedule and corrective actions are taken if required. This helps in meeting the project deadlines.

Some mnemonics to remember the objectives:

E - Estimate the effort
S - Schedule the activities
A - Allocate resources
M - Monitor and control

The objectives can also be remembered using the acronym ESAM.

Activity planning results in a detailed schedule showing start and end dates of each activity. It helps in managing the project in a systematic manner and increases the chances of project success. However, accurate estimates and realistic schedules are difficult to obtain due to inherent uncertainties in software projects.
