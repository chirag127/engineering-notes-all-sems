### Stress in spm

- Stress refers to the physical, mental, or emotional strain or tension experienced by an individual. In SPM, stress can have negative impacts on a student's performance.

- Causes of stress in SPM:
	- Exam pressure and fear of failure. The SPM exams are extremely important and stress inducing for students as their future paths depend on the results.
	- High expectations from parents, teachers and oneself. Students face pressure to achieve high scores which can lead to stress and anxiety.
	- Tight exam schedules and vast syllabi. The exam schedules are packed and students have to prepare a vast amount of course material in limited time leading to stress.
	- Transition from school to college/workplace. SPM is a turning point in a student's life and uncertainty about future prospects can induce stress.

- Effects of stress:
	- Anxiety, worry and difficulty concentrating.
	- Sleep problems, fatigue and loss of appetite.
	- Low confidence and self esteem.
	- Poor performance in exams due to inability to focus and cope with pressure.

- Coping strategies:
	- Prepare well and practice regularly to build confidence.
	- Take breaks in between study sessions to relax the mind.
	- Engage in exercise like walking or yoga to release tension and stress.
	- Talk to parents or teachers about worries and don't bottle up feelings.
	- Remind yourself that you have prepared well and will do your best. Stay positive.
	- Eat healthy and sleep well to maintain energy and concentration.

- Mnemonics:
	- PrePaRe - Prepare, Practice, Relax, Enjoy (the process)
	- TENSE - Talk, Exercise, Nutrition, Sleep, Enjoy
