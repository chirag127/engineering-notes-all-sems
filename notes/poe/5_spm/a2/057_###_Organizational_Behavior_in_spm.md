### Organizational Behavior in spm

Organizational behavior (OB) is the study of human behavior in organizational settings, how individuals and groups behave, and the effect of structure and processes on the people and organizations. Some of the key topics under OB are:

- Individual behavior: Personality, attitudes, values, perception, learning, motivation, etc.
- Group behavior: Teamwork, group dynamics, leadership, power and politics, conflict management, etc.
- Organizational structure: Departmentalization, centralization, decentralization, chain of command, span of control, etc.
- Organizational culture: Shared values, beliefs, norms and the effect on individual and group behavior.
- Change management: Coping with change, resistance to change and strategies to implement change.

Some useful mnemonics and learning tricks for OB concepts:

- PERSONALITY: Physical, Emotional, Religious, Socio-cultural, Occupational, Natural, Academic, Lifestyle, Interests.
- ARC of motivation: Attention, Relevance, Confidence. Employees must have attention on goals, see relevance to themselves and have confidence to achieve.
- TEAM: Together Everyone Achieves More. Effective teams have clear goals, roles, processes and trust.
- Power/Influence types: Legitimate, Reward, Coercive, Expert, Referent.
- Conflict management: Competing, Accommodating, Avoiding, Collaborating, Compromising.

Advantages of understanding OB:
- Increased efficiency and productivity.
- Better communication and teamwork.
- Enhanced problem-solving and decision making.
- Managing change and innovation.
- Aligning individual and organizational goals.
