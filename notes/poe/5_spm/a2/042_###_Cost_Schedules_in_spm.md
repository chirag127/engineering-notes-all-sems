### Cost Schedules in spm

A cost schedule is a table that shows the estimated costs of a project over time. It breaks down the total project cost into components and displays each component separately over the project timeline.

Cost schedules are useful for:

- Monitoring project costs and ensuring they remain within budget.
- Identifying potential cost overruns early so that corrective action can be taken.
- Comparing actual costs to estimated costs to analyze the accuracy of the initial cost estimates and identify areas where estimates were incorrect.
- Ensuring funds are available as needed for payments.
- Facilitating forecasting of cash flow requirements.

Keys to developing a good cost schedule:

- Break costs down into a work breakdown structure (WBS) to the level of detail needed for effective cost control.
- Obtain the most accurate cost estimates possible for each WBS component.
- Include all costs - direct costs as well as indirect costs.
- Include contingencies for unknown risks.
- Schedule costs according to the project schedule so that they align with the expected occurrence of work and payments.
- Update the cost schedule as the project progresses and costs are incurred or estimates change.
