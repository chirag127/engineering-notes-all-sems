### Managing People in spm

-   Communicate Clearly: Effective communication is key to managing people. Communicate the organization's goals and how each individual contributes to achieving them. Provide regular feedback and be open to listening to issues or concerns.
-   Empower Employees: Delegate responsibilities and trust your team to get the work done. Give them autonomy to work independently and make decisions. This will motivate them and allow them to develop their skills. However, be available to provide support if needed.
-   Recognize and Reward Good Work: Whether through bonuses, benefits or simply praise, recognizing and rewarding strong performance will encourage employees to continue their good work. Even small acts of appreciation can go a long way towards boosting morale and engagement.
-   Provide Training and Development: Invest in ongoing training and professional development to help your team improve their skills and advance their careers. This shows you are interested in their growth and will make them more effective employees. It can also help retain top talent.
-   Foster a Positive Company Culture: A positive work environment where employees feel supported and valued leads to a more engaged, productive team. Foster an open company culture where people feel comfortable sharing ideas and discussing issues. Promote work-life balance and organize team-building activities.
-   Handle Issues and Conflicts: Effectively addressing problems, conflicts or underperformance is an important part of people management. Have private conversations, actively listen to concerns and be open to feedback. Come prepared with specific examples and recommend constructive solutions. Take action to resolve issues in a timely manner.
- Mnemonics:
    - COMMS: Communicate, Offer growth, Motivate, Measure, Support
    - CLEAR: Communicate, Listen, Empower, Appreciate, Resolve
- Advantages: Increased productivity, engagement and retention. Happier, more motivated team. Opportunity for growth and development.
- Disadvantages: Time-consuming and challenging. Need proper training and skills. Can be costly (training, rewards, etc.).
