### Management Control in spm

Management control in spm refers to the process by which managers assure that resources are obtained and used efficiently and effectively in the accomplishment of the organization's objectives. Some key points about management control in spm are:

1. Establishing standards: Standards are set for key areas like quality, speed, and costs against which actual performance is measured. These standards are used to evaluate performance.
2. Measurement of performance: The actual performance is measured and compared against the standards. This is done through reports, inspections, and data collection.
3. Comparison of performance: The actual performance is compared with the standards and deviations are identified. This comparison helps in identifying problems or opportunities for improvement.
4. Taking corrective action: Appropriate action is taken to correct deviations and improve performance. This may involve changing strategies, reallocating resources, or taking operational actions to meet standards.
5. Feedback: The results of corrective actions are monitored to provide feedback to managers. This feedback is used to revise standards or redefine objectives if required.

Some mnemonics to remember the management control process are:

- S(tandards) E(stablish)
- M(easure) P(erformance)
- C(ompare)
- T(ake) C(orrective) A(ction)
- F(eedback)

The management control process is a continuous loop to ensure effective utilization of resources and achievement of objectives.
