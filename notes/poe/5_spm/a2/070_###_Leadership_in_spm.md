# Leadership in spm

Leadership refers to the ability of an individual to influence, motivate, and enable others to contribute toward the effectiveness and success of the organizations of which they are members.

The key attributes or traits of a good leader are:

1. Honesty and integrity - A good leader is trustworthy and responsible. He or she shows honesty and integrity in their actions and words. They lead by example.

2. Confidence - A good leader inspires confidence in others. He or she displays determination and confidence in the face of difficulties. They make decisive and timely decisions.

3. Humility - A good leader is humble. He or she is willing to accept mistakes and learn from them. They give credit to others and recognize their contributions and achievements.

4. Passion - A good leader is passionate about the work or mission and shows genuine enthusiasm and energy. They inspire and motivate others through their passion and optimism.

5. Communication - A good leader is a good communicator. He or she shares their vision clearly and concisely. They listen well and solicit input from others.

6. Commitment - A good leader shows commitment and dedication towards the mission or goal. They lead by example through hard work and sacrifices. They inspire others through their own commitment.
