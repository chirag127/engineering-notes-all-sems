### Health and Safety in spm

- Always wear protective gear like safety goggles, dust mask, gloves, ear plugs, respirator, etc. while working in the lab. Protective gear protects from harmful chemicals, fumes, dust, noise, etc.
- Tie long hair and loose clothes while working. They should not interfere with experiments and can catch fire.
- Know the location of safety equipment like fire extinguisher, eye wash, first aid kit, etc. and how to use them.
- Know how to handle chemical spills and burns. In case of spills, notify the teacher and use correct procedures to clean up. In case of burns, cool the burned area and seek medical attention.
- Never eat or drink in the lab. Only consume food and drinks in designated areas.
- Dispose waste properly in designated containers. Never pour chemicals down the drain.
- Never work alone in the lab. Always have a partner or work under teacher's supervision.
- Know basic first aid and emergency procedures.
- Follow all instructions carefully while conducting experiments. Never deviate from instructions without teacher's permission.
- Keep work area clean and organised.
- Inform the teacher immediately about any hazard, damage or injury.
- Be responsible and attentive in the lab. Horseplay and negligence can lead to accidents.

Some mnemonics to remember safety equipment locations -

* Fire extinguisher - Exit
* Eye wash - Near sinks
* First aid kit - Near exit

Advantages of following lab safety - Prevents injury and accidents, sets discipline, encourages responsibility.
Disadvantages of ignoring lab safety - Can lead to loss of life or permanent damage, legal repercussions on individuals and institutions.
