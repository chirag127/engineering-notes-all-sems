### Risk Assessment in spm

Risk assessment in software project management involves the following steps:

1. Identify risks:
- Brainstorming: Team members suggest possible risks.
- SWOT analysis: Analyzing strengths, weaknesses, opportunities and threats.
- Prior experience: Learn from past projects and identify potential risks.

Mnemonics:
- Think of 'what can go wrong'
- Consider PEST factors: Political, Economic, Social, Technological

2. Analyze risks:
- Assess probability of occurrence (low, medium, high)
- Assess impact on project (low, medium, high)
- Combine probability and impact to get risk rating (high, medium, low priority risks)

3. Develop risk response plans:
- Avoid: Eliminate risk completely
- Mitigate: Reduce probability/impact
- Transfer: Assign risk to third party (e.g. insurance)
- Accept: Take risk as-is and have contingency plans

4. Monitor and control risks:
- Track risk response plans and effectiveness
- Identify and analyze new risks
- Take corrective actions as required

Advantages:
- Prepares team for adverse events
- Allows for contingency planning
- Increases chances of project success

Disadvantages:
- Time consuming
- Difficult to predict all risks
- Subjective assessment of risks and responses
