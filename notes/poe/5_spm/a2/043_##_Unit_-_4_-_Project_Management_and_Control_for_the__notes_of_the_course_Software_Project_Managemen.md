## Unit - 4 - Project Management and Control

### Topic 1 - Project Monitoring and Control
- Project monitoring involves regularly measuring project performance using appropriate metrics and techniques and analyzing issues to identify risk and take corrective actions.
- Project control involves regulating project execution, monitoring project performance, and taking actions to ensure the project meets its objectives.
- Important monitoring and control techniques:
-- Earned value management (EVM) - measures project performance by comparing work completed vs work planned and budgeted.
-- Risk monitoring - identifying new risks, analyzing risk triggers andprobability/impact, and taking mitigation actions.
-- Issue management - identifying, logging, and resolving issues that affect project objectives.
-- Change management - managing requests for changes, analyzing impact, and approving/denying changes.
-- Quality management - inspecting and testing deliverables, tracking metrics, and taking actions to meet quality standards.

### Topic 2 - Agile Methodologies
- Agile methodologies take an iterative and incremental approach to software development. They focus on collaboration, adaptive planning, and rapid delivery.
- Popular agile methodologies:
-- Scrum - uses short iterations (sprints) to deliver incremental pieces of functionality. Roles include product owner, scrum master, and development team.
-- Kanban - focuses on visualizing work and limiting work in progress to reduce bottlenecks. Uses a Kanban board with columns for workflow states.
-- Extreme Programming (XP) - prescribes practices liketest-driven development, pair programming, and continuous integration for high-quality code.

Advantages of agile methodologies:
- Early and continuous delivery of valuable software
- Ability to adapt to changing requirements
- Higher customer satisfaction through collaboration and visibility

Disadvantages of agile methodologies:
- Can be difficult to estimate costs and timelines
- Relies on experienced, skilled team members
- Documentation can be lacking due to focus on working software over comprehensive documentation
