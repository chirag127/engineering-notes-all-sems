## Unit - 2 - Project Life Cycle and Effort Estimation

### Project Life Cycle
The project life cycle refers to the stages a project goes through from initiation to closure. The typical project life cycle includes:

1. Initiation - The project is proposed and discussed. If approved, a project charter is developed.
2. Planning - The project scope, schedule, and cost are determined. Plans to execute the project are developed.
3. Execution - The work of the project is performed according to the plans.
4. Monitoring and Control - The project is monitored and controlled to ensure it stays on track. Corrective actions are taken as needed.
5. Closure - The project is completed and closed. A post-implementation review is performed to identify lessons learned.

**Mnemonic:** PIECC stands for: Plan, Initiate, Execute, Control, Close
**Trick to remember:** Think of eating a pie in a sequence. First plan to get the pie, initiate by purchasing it, execute by eating the pie, control yourself from eating too fast, and finally close by finishing the pie.

### Effort Estimation
Estimating the effort required to complete a project is challenging but essential. Some techniques for effort estimation include:

- Expert Judgment - Rely on the estimates of individuals with experience
- Analogous Estimation - Base estimates on similar past projects
- Bottom-Up Estimation - Estimate individual work packages and roll up to the total
- Design Structure Matrices - Model dependencies between tasks and use to estimate effort
- Algorithmic Models - Use formulae and variables to calculate effort estimates

Consider advantages and disadvantages of different techniques and use multiple techniques to increase accuracy. Include buffers and contingencies in estimates to account for unknowns and risks.

**Mnemonic:** USE ABDM stands for: Use multiple techniques, Expert judgment, Analogous estimation, Bottom-up estimation, Design structure matrices
**Trick to remember:** Think of using all estimation techniques to get the job done. The first letters of the techniques form the word USE ABDM.
