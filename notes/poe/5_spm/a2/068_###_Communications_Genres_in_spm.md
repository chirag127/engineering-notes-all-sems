### Communications Genres in spm

The following are the major communication genres tested in SPM Bahasa Melayu Paper 1:

1. Surat Rasmi (Formal Letter)
- Format: Sender's address, Recipient's address, Salutation, Body, Complimentary close, Sender's name
- Use formal and polite language
- Common types: Complaint, Enquiry, Request
- Mnemonics: 'SCRiCeRS' - Sender, Recipient, Complimentary close, Request, Enquiry, Rasmi (Formal)

2. Karangan (Essay)
- Usually argumentative, persuasive or expository
- Have an introduction, body paragraphs that flow logically with supporting points and examples, and a conclusion that summarizes key points
- Use relevant facts and examples to support points
- Avoid grammatical, spelling and punctuation errors

3. Laporan (Report)
- Objectively inform or describe something
- Have a clear purpose or objective
- Use a standard report structure: Title, Introduction, Methodology, Results, Discussion, Conclusion
- Present information in logical order using sections and headings
- Use facts and data to support key points

4. Rekod Minit (Minutes of Meeting)
- Note down key discussion points and decisions made in a meeting
- Use a standard format: Date, Time, Venue, Attendees, Agenda, Discussion, Actions/Decision
- Write in past tense and third person
- Focus on objectives, outcomes and follow-ups, not verbatim record
