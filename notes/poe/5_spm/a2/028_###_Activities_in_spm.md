### Activities in spm

- Segregate materials topic-wise and subject-wise.
- Use dividers/bookmarks/labels to classify sections.
- Keep most frequently used materials close and easy to access.
- Make a table of contents or index for quick lookup.

- Prioritize important and lengthy topics/subjects.

- Plan breaks to avoid exhaustion.
- Be flexible to adapt to any changes/delays.
- Track progress and make necessary amendments to the timetable.

3. Revision - Revision is key to strengthening retention and recall. Some effective revision techniques:
- Revise notes/materials regularly, don't cram at the end.
- Practice connecting topics and themes across subjects.
- Solve previous years' questions to apply knowledge.
- Take breaks in between study sessions to relax the mind.
- Teach topics to someone else to identify weak areas.
- Use mnemonics, flashcards, mind maps, etc. to remember key terms and concepts.
