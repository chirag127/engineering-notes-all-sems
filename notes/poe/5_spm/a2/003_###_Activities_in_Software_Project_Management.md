### Activities in Software Project Management

The major activities involved in Software Project Management are:

1. Project Planning: This involves defining the scope, objectives, deliverables, schedule, budget, and resources required for the project. Some mnemonics to remember the key planning steps are:
- SCOPE: Specifying Client Objectives & Project Expectations
- SMART: Setting Measurable, Achievable, Realistic & Time-bound goals
- WBS: Creating Work Breakdown Structure
- Scheduling: Estimating time & sequencing activities
- Budgeting: Estimating & allocating costs to activities

2. Project Tracking & Control: This involves monitoring the progress, identifying variances, and taking corrective actions. Some tricks to remember are:
- EVM: Earned Value Measurement for comparing planned vs actual progress
- PCR: Performance Change Requests for handling issues & risks
- Status meetings: Regular reviews & updates with stakeholders

3. Supplier Management: This involves acquiring products & services from external suppliers. Some points to remember:
- Clear requirements: Provide detailed specifications to suppliers
- Evaluation: Assess suppliers on parameters like cost, quality, reliability, etc.
- Contracts: Formal agreements specifying rights & obligations of parties
- Oversight: Closely monitor supplier performance & take corrective actions if required
