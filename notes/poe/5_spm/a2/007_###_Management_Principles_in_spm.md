### Management Principles in spm

The following are the key management principles in software project management (spm):

1. Clearly Define Objectives and Scope - The objectives and scope of the project must be clearly defined in measurable terms. This helps in planning the project effectively and evaluating progress and success.

2. Get Requirements Right - The requirements of the project must be understood correctly. This involves detailed communication with the customer and stakeholders. Requirements changes must be handled properly through a formal change control process.

3. Plan the Work and Work the Plan - A detailed project plan must be created which schedules the various tasks and estimates the required resources and timelines. The project work must be executed as per the plan and the plan must be updated as required.

4. Manage Resources Effectively - The resources including funds, people, infrastructure, etc. must be acquired and utilized effectively and efficiently to complete the project. Resource constraints must be identified and handled proactively.

5. Manage Risks and Issues - Risks that can impact the project must be identified and corresponding mitigation plans must be created. Issues that arise must be identified and resolved quickly to avoid impact on the project.

6. Focus on Quality and Customer Satisfaction - The primary focus must be on meeting the required quality standards and ensuring customer satisfaction. Quality must be built into the product through good design, implementation, and testing practices.

7. Maintain Good Communication - Effective communication must be maintained with all stakeholders including customers and team members. This helps in ensuring that requirements and issues are clearly understood and resolved.

8. Monitor and Control - The project progress must be monitored continuously and corrective actions must be taken as required. Earned value management techniques can be used to measure and control cost and schedule variances.

Mnemonics:
PROPS - Plan, Requirements, Objectives, People, Schedule
QUALITY - Quality, Understanding, Achieving targets, Listening, Involving, Tracking, Yield
