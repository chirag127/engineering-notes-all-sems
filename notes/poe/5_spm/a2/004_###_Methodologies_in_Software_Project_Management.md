### Methodologies in Software Project Management

The following are the major methodologies used in Software Project Management:

1. Waterfall Model:
- Sequential design process consisting of stages: Requirements, Design, Implementation, Testing, Deployment
- Output of one stage is input to next stage
- Strictly follows sequential approach, no overlapping of stages
- suited for projects with clearly defined requirements
- Disadvantage: Not suitable for projects with undefined or changing requirements

Mnemonics: Think of a waterfall cascading down from top to bottom sequentially to remember the stages

2. Agile Methodology:
- Iterative and incremental approach
- Requirements and solutions evolve through collaboration between self-organizing teams
- Emphasizes on adapting to changes, regular adaptations and rapid feedback loops
- suited for projects with changing or undefined requirements
- Advantage: High customer satisfaction, adaptable to changes
- Disadvantage: Requires highly skilled teams, collaboration and communication intensive

3. Scrum Framework:
- Most popular Agile framework
- Work is done in sprints (1-4 weeks) with defined goals
- Daily stand-up meetings, sprint planning and review meetings
- Roles: Product Owner, Scrum Master, Team
- Artifacts: Product Backlog, Sprint Backlog, Increment
- Advantage: Predictable delivery, highly flexible and adaptable
- Disadvantage: Challenging for teams to adopt, lack of predictability in long term planning
