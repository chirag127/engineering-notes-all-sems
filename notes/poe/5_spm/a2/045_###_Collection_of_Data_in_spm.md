## Collection of Data in spm

- SPM involves collection of data from participants to study brain activity and understand psychological processes. The data collected can be of various types:

### Functional Magnetic Resonance Imaging (fMRI)
- Measures changes in blood oxygenation and flow that occur in response to neural activity.
- Participants lie in an MRI scanner while performing a task or being exposed to stimuli.
- The BOLD (Blood-Oxygen-Level Dependent) signal is used to identify regions of activation in the brain.
- Advantage: Non-invasive, good spatial resolution. Disadvantage: Poor temporal resolution, expensive.

Mnemonic: BOLD signal activates, deoxyhemoglobin dominates.

### Electroencephalography (EEG)
- Measures electrical activity on the scalp via electrodes.
- Participants wear a cap with electrodes that detect fluctuations in electrical potentials.
- Used to measure event-related potentials (ERP), oscillations, and other measures of brain electrical activity.
- Advantage: Excellent temporal resolution. Disadvantage: Poor spatial resolution.

Learning trick: Remember different EEG wave patterns - alpha, beta, theta, delta.
