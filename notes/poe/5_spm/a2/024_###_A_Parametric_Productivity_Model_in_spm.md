### A Parametric Productivity Model in spm

The Parametric Productivity Model (PPM) in Scrum Patterns Movement (spm) defines productivity as the function of three parameters:

1. Team Capacity: The total amount of time the Team has available to work on the Product Backlog Items. This is a function of the Team size and time allocated to the Sprint.
2. Work Required: The total amount of time required to complete the Product Backlog Items selected for the Sprint. This is a function of the effort estimates and the dependencies between the Product Backlog Items.
3. Waste: The time lost due to inefficiencies, dependencies, interruptions, and other impediments.

Productivity = (Team Capacity - Waste) / Work Required

Some key points about the PPM:

- It focuses on the time dimension of productivity.
- It considers the whole Team and all the work in the Sprint.
- It makes waste visible and quantifiable.
- It can be used to calculate a projected productivity and then compare against the actual productivity to identify and remove waste.
- It provides a basis for return on investment decisions regarding waste reduction.

The PPM enables the Scrum Team to measure, monitor, and improve productivity over time by reducing waste and balancing Team capacity and work required. It helps the Team achieve a sustainable pace and optimize the value delivered at the end of each Sprint.
