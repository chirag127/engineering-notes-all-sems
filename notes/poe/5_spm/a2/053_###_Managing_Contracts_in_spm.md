### Managing Contracts in spm

- Contracts in spm refers to the agreements or deals between the buyers and sellers.
- Following are the key points to manage contracts in spm:

1. Clearly define the terms of the contract: The terms of the contract like quantity, price, quality, delivery date, etc. should be clearly defined to avoid any confusion or dispute later. These terms can be defined in a formal contract document.

2. Set realistic deadlines: The deadlines for various phases of the contract like delivery, inspection, payment, etc. should be set in a realistic manner based on the scale and complexity of the contract. Unrealistic deadlines can lead to delays and disputes.

3. Continuous monitoring: The contract should be continuously monitored to ensure that the terms are being followed by both the parties. Regular meetings and inspections can help in monitoring the progress and addressing any issues proactively.

4. Formal documentation: Every communication and change related to the contract should be properly documented in a formal manner. This documentation can help in avoiding confusion and can act as a reference in case of any disputes.

5. Clear consequences: The consequences of not meeting the terms of the contract should be clearly communicated to both the parties. This can motivate the parties to meet their commitments and can also help in taking corrective actions in case of violations.

- The points mentioned above can help in effectively managing the contracts in a structured manner. Following proper processes and documentation along with continuous monitoring of the contract can lead to its successful execution.

- Mnemonics: CANCM - Clearly define terms, Set realistic deadlines, Continuous monitoring, Formal documentation, Clear consequences

- The mnemonic CANCM can be useful to remember the key points to manage contracts.
