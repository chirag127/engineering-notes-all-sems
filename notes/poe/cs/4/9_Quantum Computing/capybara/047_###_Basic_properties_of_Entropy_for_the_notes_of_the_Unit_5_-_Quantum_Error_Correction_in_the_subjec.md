### Basic Properties of Entropy for the Notes of Unit 5 - Quantum Error Correction in the Subject of Quantum Computing

Entropy is a measure of the disorder or randomness in a system. In quantum computing, entropy plays a crucial role in the study of quantum error correction. Here are some basic properties of entropy that are important to understand for the notes of Unit 5 - Quantum Error Correction in the subject of Quantum Computing:

1. Entropy is always non-negative: The entropy of a system cannot be negative. It can take a value of zero if the system is in a pure state, but it can never be negative.

2. Entropy is maximized for a uniform distribution: The entropy of a system is maximized when the system is in a state of maximum disorder or randomness. For example, if we toss a fair coin, the maximum entropy is achieved when the coin is equally likely to land on heads or tails.

3. Entropy is additive for independent systems: The entropy of a composite system made up of two independent subsystems is the sum of the entropies of the individual subsystems. Mathematically, if we have two systems A and B, then the entropy of the combined system AB is given by S(AB) = S(A) + S(B).

4. Entropy is preserved under unitary transformations: If we apply a unitary transformation to a quantum system, the entropy of the system remains unchanged. This property is important in quantum error correction because it allows us to perform unitary operations on a quantum system without affecting its entropy.

5. Entropy is related to the number of possible states: The entropy of a system is related to the number of possible states that the system can be in. A system with a large number of possible states will have a higher entropy than a system with a smaller number of possible states.

In summary, understanding the basic properties of entropy is crucial for studying quantum error correction in quantum computing. By understanding these properties, we can develop strategies to correct errors in quantum systems and build more robust quantum computers.
