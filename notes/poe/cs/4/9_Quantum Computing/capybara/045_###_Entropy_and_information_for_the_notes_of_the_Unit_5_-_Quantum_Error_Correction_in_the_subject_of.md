5. Information is a measure of the amount of knowledge that can be gained from a system. It is denoted by the symbol I and is measured in units of bits.
6. Information is related to the number of possible outcomes of a system. The more the number of outcomes, the higher the amount of information.
7. Information is related to the amount of uncertainty in a system. A system with high uncertainty will have a higher amount of information, while a system with low uncertainty will have a lower amount of information.
8. In Quantum Computing, the concept of information is used to measure the amount of information that can be transmitted through a quantum channel. It is also used to measure the amount of information that can be stored in a quantum memory.
