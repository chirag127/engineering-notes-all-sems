### Conditions for Quantum Computation

In order to perform quantum computation, we need to satisfy certain conditions. These conditions are as follows:

1. **Quantum Bits (qubits):** The basic building blocks of quantum computation are quantum bits or qubits. A qubit is a two-level quantum system that can be in a superposition of both states at the same time. Unlike classical bits, we cannot measure the state of a qubit without collapsing the superposition.

2. **Quantum Gates:** The operations performed on qubits are called quantum gates. These gates can be used to manipulate the state of a qubit. Some commonly used quantum gates include the Hadamard gate, Pauli-X gate, Pauli-Y gate, Pauli-Z gate, and CNOT gate.

3. **Superposition:** Quantum computation relies heavily on the concept of superposition. Superposition allows us to represent multiple states at the same time. For example, a qubit can be in a superposition of both 0 and 1 states at the same time.

4. **Entanglement:** Entanglement is a phenomenon where two or more qubits become correlated in such a way that the state of one qubit depends on the state of the other qubits. Entanglement is a crucial resource for quantum computation.

5. **Quantum Measurement:** Quantum measurement is the process of extracting information from a quantum system. However, measurement in quantum computation is different from classical measurement. In quantum computation, measurement causes the collapse of the superposition and gives us a classical bit.
