### Global Perspectives for the notes of the Unit 1 - Fundamental Concepts in the subject of Quantum Computing

Quantum computing is a field that is rapidly developing with the potential to revolutionize the world of computing. Understanding the fundamental concepts is essential to grasp the basics of quantum computing. In this unit, we will discuss the global perspectives for the notes of the Unit 1 - Fundamental Concepts in the subject of Quantum Computing.

Here are some key points to keep in mind:

1. Quantum computing is based on the principles of quantum mechanics, which is a fundamental theory in physics. It deals with the behavior of particles at the atomic and subatomic level.

2. The fundamental concepts of quantum computing include qubits, superposition, entanglement, quantum gates, and quantum algorithms.

3. Mnemonic: To remember the fundamental concepts of quantum computing, you can use the acronym "S.E.Q.G.A." where S stands for superposition, E for entanglement, Q for qubits, G for quantum gates, and A for quantum algorithms.

4. Qubits are the basic building blocks of quantum computing. They are the quantum equivalent of classical bits and can exist in a state of superposition, which means they can be in multiple states at the same time.

5. Superposition is a fundamental concept of quantum computing where a qubit can exist in multiple states at the same time. It is the key to the power of quantum computing.

6. Entanglement is a phenomenon where two or more qubits are linked in such a way that the state of one qubit depends on the state of the other qubits.

7. Quantum gates are the quantum equivalent of classical logic gates, and they are used to manipulate qubits.

8. Quantum algorithms are algorithms that are designed to run on a quantum computer. They are used to solve problems that are difficult or impossible to solve using classical computers.

9. One of the most famous quantum algorithms is Shor's algorithm, which is used to factor large numbers. This algorithm has the potential to break the encryption used in modern communication systems.

10. Applications of quantum computing include cryptography, optimization, simulation, and machine learning.

In conclusion, understanding the fundamental concepts of quantum computing is crucial to grasp the basics of this rapidly developing field. Mnemonics and learning tricks can be helpful in remembering the key concepts.
