
### Optical Photon Quantum Computer 

For the notes of the Unit 3 - Quantum Computers in the subject of Quantum Computing, an Optical Photon Quantum Computer (OPQC) is a quantum computer that uses photons as its qubits. The OPQC is a type of quantum computer that utilizes photons to represent and process information.

The main advantage of an OPQC is that it can be used to perform calculations faster than any classical computer. Additionally, OPQCs are more reliable than traditional computers since they can be used to perform calculations without errors.

Advantages: 

1. OPQCs are capable of performing calculations faster than traditional computers. 
2. OPQCs are more reliable than traditional computers since they can be used to perform calculations without errors. 
3. OPQCs are more energy-efficient than traditional computers. 
4. OPQCs are more secure than traditional computers since they are less vulnerable to attack. 

Disadvantages: 

1. OPQCs are expensive and difficult to build. 
2. OPQCs require specialized hardware and software to operate. 
3. OPQCs are not yet widely available.
