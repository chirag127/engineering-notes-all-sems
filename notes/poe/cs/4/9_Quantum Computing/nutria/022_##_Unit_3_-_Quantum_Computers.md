
## Unit 3 - Quantum Computers

* Quantum Computers are computers that harness the power of quantum mechanics to perform calculations and solve problems that are too complex for traditional computers.
* Quantum Computers are based on the principles of quantum mechanics, which describes the behavior of particles at the atomic and subatomic level.
* Quantum Computers use qubits, which are quantum bits that can exist in multiple states simultaneously. This allows them to process and store more information than traditional computers.
* Quantum Computers are able to solve problems that are too complex for traditional computers, such as factoring large numbers, solving optimization problems, and simulating quantum systems.
* Advantages of Quantum Computers include their ability to solve complex problems quickly and accurately, their potential to revolutionize the field of artificial intelligence, and their potential to revolutionize the field of cryptography.
* Disadvantages of Quantum Computers include their high cost, their complexity, and the fact that they are still in the early stages of development.
* Examples of applications for Quantum Computers include simulating quantum systems, factoring large numbers, and solving optimization problems.
* Quantum Computers are still in the early stages of development, but they have the potential to revolutionize the fields of artificial intelligence, cryptography, and many other areas.
