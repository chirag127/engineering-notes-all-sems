
### Theory of Quantum Error –Correction for the Notes of the Unit 5 - Quantum Error Correction in the Subject of Quantum Computing

1. Quantum Error Correction (QEC) is a set of techniques used to protect quantum information from errors due to noise and other effects. 
2. QEC is based on the idea of encoding quantum information into a protected subspace, which is immune to certain errors. 
3. QEC uses a combination of classical error-correcting codes and quantum operations to detect and correct errors. 
4. QEC codes are divided into two types: stabilizer codes and subsystem codes. 
5. Stabilizer codes use a group of commuting observables to encode the quantum information in a protected subspace. 
6. Subsystem codes use entanglement to encode the quantum information in a protected subspace. 
7. QEC codes can detect and correct errors that occur in the quantum information. 
8. QEC codes can also be used to detect and correct errors that occur in quantum operations. 
9. QEC codes are used in various applications, including quantum computing, quantum communication, and quantum cryptography.
