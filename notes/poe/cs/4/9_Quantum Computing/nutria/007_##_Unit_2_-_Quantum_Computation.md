
## Unit 2 - Quantum Computation

Quantum computation is the use of quantum-mechanical phenomena such as superposition and entanglement to perform computation. A quantum computer is a device that performs quantum computation.

**Advantages of Quantum Computation**
- Exponential speedup in certain problems
- Ability to solve certain problems that are intractable for classical computers
- Ability to simulate quantum systems

**Disadvantages of Quantum Computation**
- Extremely expensive and difficult to build
- Susceptible to noise
- Limited scalability

**Mnemonics and Learning Tricks**
- Remember that quantum computers are based on the principles of quantum mechanics, which describe how particles behave at the atomic and subatomic level.
- Think of a quantum computer as a powerful calculator that can solve certain problems exponentially faster than a classical computer.
- Visualize the qubits and their superpositions as points on a high-dimensional sphere.
- Remember that noise is a major issue in quantum computing, as it can cause errors in the calculations.

**Examples**
- Searching an unstructured database
- Cryptography
- Simulating quantum systems

**Applications**
- Machine learning
- Optimization
- Drug discovery
