
### Fault – Tolerant Quantum Computation for the notes of the Unit 5 - Quantum Error Correction in the subject of Quantum Computing

Fault-tolerant quantum computation is a method for performing quantum computation in the presence of errors. It relies on the use of quantum error-correcting codes to detect and correct errors in the computation. Fault-tolerant quantum computation is a key component of quantum computing, as it allows for the reliable execution of quantum algorithms in the presence of errors.

The main goal of fault-tolerant quantum computation is to ensure that the output of a quantum computation is the same as the input, regardless of errors that may occur during the computation. To achieve this, fault-tolerant quantum computation relies on the use of quantum error-correcting codes. These codes are designed to detect errors in the computation and correct them, ensuring that the output is the same as the input.

Fault-tolerant quantum computation can be used to perform a wide range of quantum algorithms, including Shor's algorithm, Grover's algorithm, and the quantum teleportation protocol. It is also used to build quantum computers, as it allows for the reliable execution of quantum algorithms.
