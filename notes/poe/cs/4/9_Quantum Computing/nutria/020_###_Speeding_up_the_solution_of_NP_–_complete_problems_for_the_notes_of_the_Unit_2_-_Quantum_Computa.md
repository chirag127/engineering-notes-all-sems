
### Speeding up the solution of NP – complete problems for the notes of the Unit 2 - Quantum Computation in the subject of Quantum Computing

NP-complete problems are a class of computational problems that are difficult to solve using traditional methods. In quantum computing, these problems can be solved faster and more efficiently by using quantum algorithms. 

**Advantages**
- Quantum computing can solve NP-complete problems in polynomial time, which is significantly faster than traditional algorithms. 
- Quantum algorithms can be used to solve complex problems that are difficult to solve using traditional methods. 
- Quantum algorithms can be used to solve problems that are impossible to solve using traditional methods. 

**Disadvantages**
- Quantum algorithms are still in their infancy, and there is still much to be learned about them. 
- Quantum computers are expensive and require specialized hardware and software. 
- Quantum algorithms are not yet widely available, and require specialized expertise to use.
