
### Phase Estimation for the Notes of the Unit 2 - Quantum Computation in the Subject of Quantum Computing

Phase estimation is an important concept in quantum computing. It is a technique used to measure the phase of a quantum system. This technique is used to find the eigenvalues of a quantum system, which can be used to solve certain problems.

The basic idea behind phase estimation is to use a quantum circuit to measure the phase of a quantum system. This is done by creating a superposition of the system's eigenstates, then measuring the phase of the superposition. The phase is then used to calculate the eigenvalues of the system.

One of the most important applications of phase estimation is to find the eigenvalues of a Hamiltonian. This can be used to solve certain problems, such as the ground state energy of a system.

- Think of the phase as the angle between the eigenstates of the system.
