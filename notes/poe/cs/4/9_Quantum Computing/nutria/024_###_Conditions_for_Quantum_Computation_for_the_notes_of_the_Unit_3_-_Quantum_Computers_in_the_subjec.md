
### Conditions for Quantum Computation for the notes of the Unit 3 - Quantum Computers in the subject of Quantum Computing

1. Quantum computation is the process of using a quantum computer to perform calculations. This is done by manipulating qubits, or quantum bits, which have the ability to exist in multiple states at the same time.

2. Quantum computers are able to solve problems that are intractable on conventional computers, due to the fact that they can explore a much larger space of possible solutions.

3. In order to perform quantum computation, certain conditions must be met. These conditions include:
    - A quantum computer must be able to maintain a certain level of quantum coherence, which is the ability of a quantum system to remain in a superposition of states.
    - The qubits must be initialized in a specific state, such as all zeros or all ones.
    - The qubits must be manipulated by applying quantum gates, which are operations that can change the state of the qubits.
    - The qubits must be measured to obtain the result of the computation.

4. Mnemonic: The four steps of quantum computation can be remembered with the acronym "QCIM": Quantum Coherence, Initialization, Manipulation, Measurement.
