
### Entanglement as a physical resource for the notes of the Unit 5 - Quantum Error Correction in the subject of Quantum Computing

Entanglement is an essential concept in quantum computing and quantum error correction. It is the phenomenon where two or more quantum particles are connected in such a way that they share the same properties, even when separated by large distances. Entanglement is used to create a quantum network, which allows for the transmission of quantum information between different locations.

Entanglement has a number of useful properties, such as the ability to create a secure channel for communication and to protect quantum information from the environment. It can also be used to create a quantum computing device, which can process quantum information faster than a classical computer.

Mnemonics and learning tricks for Entanglement:
- Entanglement can be thought of as a “quantum link” between two particles.
- Visualize entanglement as two particles connected by an invisible “quantum string.”
- Entanglement is a form of “quantum teleportation” which allows quantum information to be sent from one location to another.
- Entanglement is a powerful resource for quantum computing, allowing for faster and more secure processing of quantum information.

Advantages of Entanglement:
- Entanglement can be used to create a secure channel for communication, allowing for the transmission of quantum information without any risk of eavesdropping.
- Entanglement can be used to create a quantum computing device, allowing for faster processing of quantum information.
- Entanglement can be used to protect quantum information from the environment, allowing for the storage of quantum information for longer periods of time.

Disadvantages of Entanglement:
- Entanglement requires a large amount of energy to maintain, making it difficult to scale up to large quantum networks.
- Entanglement can be disrupted by environmental noise, making it difficult to create reliable quantum networks.
- Entanglement is fragile, making it difficult to maintain over long distances.
