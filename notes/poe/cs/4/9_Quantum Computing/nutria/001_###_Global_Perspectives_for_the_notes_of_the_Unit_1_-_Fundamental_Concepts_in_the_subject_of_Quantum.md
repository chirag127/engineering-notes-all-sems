
### Global Perspectives for the notes of the Unit 1 - Fundamental Concepts in the subject of Quantum Computing

Quantum computing is a rapidly advancing field of technology that has the potential to revolutionize the way we think about computing. It has been used in a variety of applications, from cryptography to artificial intelligence. In this unit, we will explore the fundamentals of quantum computing and its global perspectives.

- Quantum computing is based on the principles of quantum mechanics, which describe the behavior of particles at the atomic and subatomic level.
- Quantum computers use qubits, or quantum bits, instead of classical bits, which are used in traditional computers.
- Quantum computers are able to process information much faster than traditional computers, due to their ability to exploit quantum entanglement and superposition.
- Quantum computing has the potential to solve problems that are impossible for traditional computers to solve, such as simulating complex systems and analyzing large datasets.
- Quantum computing has applications in a variety of fields, such as cryptography, artificial intelligence, drug discovery, and financial services.
- Quantum computing is still in its early stages of development, and there are many challenges that need to be addressed before it can be used in the real world.
- One of the main challenges is developing algorithms that can take advantage of the unique features of quantum computers.
- Another challenge is developing hardware that is reliable and efficient enough to be used in real-world applications.
- Quantum computers are also expensive and require specialized skills to operate.
- In addition, quantum computers are vulnerable to interference from their environment, which can cause errors in the calculations.
- To address these challenges, researchers and engineers are working on developing new algorithms, hardware, and software to make quantum computing more accessible and reliable.
