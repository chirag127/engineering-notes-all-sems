
### Applications for the notes of the Unit 2 - Quantum Computation in the subject of Quantum Computing

* Quantum computing has a wide range of potential applications, including:
    * Optimization: Quantum computing can be used to solve complex optimization problems, such as scheduling and routing problems.
    * Cryptography: Quantum computing can be used to break existing codes, as well as create new encryption algorithms that are secure against quantum attacks. 
    * Simulation: Quantum computing can be used to simulate complex quantum systems, such as molecules, materials, and drugs.
    * Machine learning: Quantum computing can be used to develop more efficient machine learning algorithms.
    * Artificial intelligence: Quantum computing can be used to develop more powerful artificial intelligence algorithms.
    * Financial services: Quantum computing can be used to develop more efficient financial services algorithms.
    * Drug discovery: Quantum computing can be used to develop more efficient drug discovery algorithms.
    * Quantum communications: Quantum computing can be used to develop more efficient quantum communications algorithms.

* Mnemonics and learning tricks can be useful when studying quantum computing. For example, the acronym "QCC" can be used to remember the four main components of a quantum computer: qubits, control, couplers, and circuits. Additionally, the phrase "Quantum Computing Is Cool" can be used to remember the main elements of quantum computing: qubits, entanglement, interference, and measurement.
