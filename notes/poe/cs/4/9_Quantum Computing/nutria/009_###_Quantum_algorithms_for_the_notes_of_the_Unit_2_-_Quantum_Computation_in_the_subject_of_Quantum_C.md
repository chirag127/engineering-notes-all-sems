
### Quantum algorithms for the notes of the Unit 2 - Quantum Computation in the subject of Quantum Computing

Quantum algorithms are algorithms that make use of the principles of quantum mechanics to solve a problem faster than any classical algorithm. They are used in a variety of applications, including cryptography, quantum computing, and quantum simulations.

The most famous quantum algorithm is Shor's algorithm, which can factor integers in polynomial time. Other important quantum algorithms include Grover's algorithm, which searches an unstructured database in quadratic time, and the quantum Fourier transform, which is used in quantum computers to solve optimization and search problems.

Mnemonics and learning tricks:
- "Shor Factor": Shor's algorithm is used to factor numbers.
- "Grover Search": Grover's algorithm is used to search an unstructured database.
- "QFT": The quantum Fourier transform is used in quantum computers to solve optimization and search problems.
