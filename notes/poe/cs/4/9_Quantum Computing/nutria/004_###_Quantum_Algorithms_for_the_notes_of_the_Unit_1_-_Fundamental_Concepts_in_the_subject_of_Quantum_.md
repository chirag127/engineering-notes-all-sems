- Quantum algorithms are algorithms that make use of the principles of quantum mechanics to solve various problems. 
- They are used to solve problems that are intractable with classical computing, such as factorization and searching. 
- Quantum algorithms are typically much faster than classical algorithms, but require special hardware to run.
