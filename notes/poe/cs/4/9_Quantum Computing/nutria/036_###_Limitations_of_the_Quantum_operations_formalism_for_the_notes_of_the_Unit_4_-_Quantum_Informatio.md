
### Limitations of the Quantum operations formalism for the notes of the Unit 4 - Quantum Information in the subject of Quantum Computing

1. Quantum operations formalism is limited to systems that are described by a finite-dimensional Hilbert space, and thus cannot be applied to systems with an infinite number of degrees of freedom.

2. The quantum operations formalism does not take into account the effects of decoherence, which can cause a quantum system to lose its quantum properties over time.

3. The quantum operations formalism does not account for the effects of entanglement, which can lead to correlations between the states of different quantum systems.

4. The quantum operations formalism does not provide an explicit description of the dynamics of a quantum system, and so it cannot be used to describe the evolution of a quantum system over time.

5. The quantum operations formalism does not provide a method for computing the probability of a given measurement outcome, and so it cannot be used to make predictions about the outcome of a quantum measurement.
