
### Entropy and information for the notes of the Unit 5 - Quantum Error Correction in the subject of Quantum Computing

Entropy is a measure of the amount of information that can be stored in a quantum system. It is also known as the amount of uncertainty in a system. Entropy is important in quantum computing because it determines the amount of information that can be stored in a quantum system and the amount of noise that can be tolerated.

Quantum error correction is a method of protecting quantum information from errors due to noise. It is a technique of encoding information in such a way that errors can be detected and corrected. Quantum error correction is necessary for reliable quantum computing.

Mnemonic:

Quantum error correction: **QEC** - Quantum Entropy Control.

Learning Tricks:

1. Think of entropy as the amount of information that can be stored in a quantum system.
2. Quantum error correction is a way of protecting quantum information from errors due to noise.
3. Remember the mnemonic **QEC** - Quantum Entropy Control.
