When an atom is placed in an external magnetic field, its nucleus will align itself with the field and begin to spin. This spin can be detected and used to measure various properties of the molecule.

It can be used to determine the structure of a molecule, including its bond lengths, angles, and any other structural features. NMR can also be used to study the dynamics of a molecule, such as its rotational and vibrational motions.

NMR is also used to study quantum computing.
