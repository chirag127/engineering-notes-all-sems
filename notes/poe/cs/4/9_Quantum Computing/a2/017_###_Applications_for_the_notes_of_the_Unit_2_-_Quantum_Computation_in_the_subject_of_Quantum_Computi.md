### Applications for the notes of the Unit 2 - Quantum Computation in the subject of Quantum Computing

The following are the key applications and areas where Quantum Computation is applied:

1. Quantum Simulation - Quantum computers can simulate quantum systems to study their properties. This can be used to study quantum physics and develop new materials.
2. Quantum Machine Learning - Quantum algorithms like quantum support vector machine can be used to train machine learning models on quantum computers which can lead to improved accuracy and faster training times for complex problems.
3. Quantum Chemistry - Quantum computers can be used to simulate molecular interactions to aid in drug discovery and material design. Quantum algorithms like phase estimation can be used to calculate molecular energies.
4. Quantum Cryptography - Quantum cryptography uses quantum mechanical properties to encrypt and secure data. It allows for unconditional security and is used in quantum key distribution.
5. Quantum Search - Quantum algorithms like Grover's algorithm can be used to search an unstructured database in sqrt(N) time compared to linear time for classical algorithms leading to speedups in search problems.

The above are some of the key applications of quantum computation. However, quantum computing is still in its infancy and more applications can be explored as the technology matures. Quantum algorithms and quantum circuits would need to be designed for various problems to harness the power of quantum computers. Although promising, there are still challenges to scale quantum computers and reduce errors which can pave way for wider adoption of quantum computers in the future.
