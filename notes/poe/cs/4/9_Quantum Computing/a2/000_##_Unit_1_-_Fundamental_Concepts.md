## Unit 1 - Fundamental Concepts

1. Data vs Information:
- Data: Raw facts and figures without any context. Eg: Ages of students in a class.
- Information: Data that is organized, structured or presented in a meaningful context. Eg: Average age of students in a class.

Mnemonic: Data is raw, information is refined.

2. Bits and Bytes:
- Bit: Smallest unit of data in computers that can have a value either 0 or 1.
- Byte: Made up of 8 bits and can represent values from 0 to 255. Used to measure amounts of data.
Eg: 1 byte can represent a single character like 'A', 2 bytes can represent a character in some languages like Chinese, etc.

3. Analog vs Digital:
- Analog: Continuous electrical signals that can take on any value within a range. Eg: Sound waves.
- Digital: Discrete values that can only take on certain values. Eg: 0s and 1s in computers.

Mnemonic: Analogue is continuous, digital is discrete.
