### Quantum search algorithms for the notes of the Unit 2 - Quantum Computation in the subject of Quantum Computing

- Quantum search algorithms are quantum algorithms that solve the problem of searching an unsorted database faster than classical algorithms.
- The two major quantum search algorithms are:
    - Grover's algorithm - It provides a quadratic speedup over classical search algorithms. It takes `O(√N)` time to search an unsorted database of `N` items, compared to `O(N)` for classical algorithms.
    - Quantum adiabatic algorithm - It provides a solution to NP-complete problems by finding the minimum energy state of a system. It has an asymptotic runtime of `O(√(k/ε))` where `k` is a problem-specific constant and `ε` is the error tolerance.
- Advantages - Provide a speedup over classical algorithms. Have applications in machine learning, pattern matching, etc.
- Disadvantages - Require quantum hardware to implement. Error-prone and difficult to scale.
- Examples - Database search, optimization problems, machine learning.
- To remember - Think of Grover's algorithm as giving a 'boost' or speedup to searching by going to the square root of the search space. The adiabatic algorithm is like 'slowly coasting' to the minimum energy state.
