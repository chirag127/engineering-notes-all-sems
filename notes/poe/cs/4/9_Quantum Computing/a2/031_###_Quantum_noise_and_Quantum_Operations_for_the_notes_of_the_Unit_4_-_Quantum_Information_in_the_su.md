### Quantum noise and Quantum Operations

Quantum noise refers to the unwanted disturbances that affect the state of a quantum system and cause its quantum information to become degraded or lost. There are various sources of quantum noise:

- Decoherence: Interaction of the quantum system with its surrounding environment causes decoherence which leads to loss of quantum coherence. Decoherence converts pure states into mixed states and destroys quantum effects like entanglement.
- Dephasing: Fluctuations in the phase of the quantum state leading to loss of quantum interference effects. Dephasing reduces the off-diagonal elements of the density matrix representing the quantum state.
- Relaxation: Fluctuations that cause the state to decay from higher energy levels to lower energy levels. This leads to loss of energy from the quantum system.

To protect against quantum noise and operate on quantum systems, quantum operations are performed. Quantum operations are unitary transformations that act on the state of a quantum system. Some important quantum operations are:

- Quantum gates: Quantum logic gates like Hadamard gate, Pauli gates, CNOT gate, etc. that perform specific unitary transformations on quantum bits. Quantum gates are the building blocks for quantum circuits.
- Quantum teleportation: Transfer of quantum state from one system to another without transferring the physical matter. It uses quantum entanglement to transmit exact state information.
- Quantum error correction: Techniques like quantum ECC that detect and correct errors in quantum systems caused by quantum noise. This is required for building scalable quantum computers and networks.
- Quantum measurements: Process of obtaining information about the state of a quantum system. Measurements collapse the quantum state into a classical state. Precise quantum measurements are challenging to achieve.

Mnemonics and learning tricks:
- Think of decoherence as the enemy of coherence which destroys the quantumness of a system.
- Dephasing is like the system going out of phase or sync due to fluctuations.
- Relaxation is like the system relaxing from higher to lower levels, releasing energy.
- Quantum gates are the Legos of quantum circuits.
- Quantum teleportation is quantum state transfer without transfer of matter. Entanglement is the key.
- Quantum error correction is required to detect and fix errors from the noisy quantum world.
- Quantum measurements extract information and collapse the quantum state.
