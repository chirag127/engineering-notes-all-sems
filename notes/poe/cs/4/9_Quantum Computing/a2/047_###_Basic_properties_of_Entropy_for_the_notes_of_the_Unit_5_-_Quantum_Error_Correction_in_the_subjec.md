### Basic properties of Entropy for the notes of the Unit 5 - Quantum Error Correction in the subject of Quantum Computing

1. Entropy is a measure of randomness or uncertainty in a system. Higher the entropy, higher is the randomness and lower is the order.
2. For a quantum system, the entropy is given by - S = -Tr(ρlogρ) where ρ is the density matrix of the system.
3. Entropy is maximized for a completely mixed state where the density matrix is just the identity matrix scaled by 1/d (d being the dimension of the Hilbert space). This corresponds to maximum uncertainty about the state of the system.
4. Entropy is minimized for a pure state where the density matrix is a rank-1 projector. This corresponds to completely certain information about the state of the system.
5. Second law of thermodynamics states that the total entropy of an isolated system always increases over time and approaches a maximum value. This is also known as the increase in entropy principle.
6. Mnemonic - Entropy = Envelope of Randomness. Higher the envelope ( Entropy ), higher is the randomness.
