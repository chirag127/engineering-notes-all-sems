### Quantum algorithms for the notes of the Unit 2 - Quantum Computation in the subject of Quantum Computing

The quantum algorithms that will be discussed in this unit are:

1. Quantum Fourier Transform (QFT) - The QFT is a key subroutine in many quantum algorithms. It can be used to solve problems like order finding and discrete logarithms efficiently on a quantum computer.
Mnemonic: Think of QFT as a quantum version of discrete Fourier transform which can be used to solve problems related to frequencies and periodicity efficiently.

2. Simon's algorithm - Simon's algorithm is a quantum algorithm that can find the hidden period of a black-box function in time square-root of the period. It has applications in cryptography like finding a hidden subgroup.
Mnemonic: Think of Simon's algorithm as a way to find the hidden frequency of a black-box function in an efficient manner using quantum mechanics.

3. Shor's algorithm - Shor's algorithm is a quantum algorithm for integer factorization and discrete logarithms. It can be used to break the security of many public-key cryptosystems. It runs in polynomial time and is a very efficient quantum algorithm with important applications.
Mnemonic: Think of Shor's algorithm as a quantum method to factor numbers and solve discrete logarithm problems very quickly which can help break cryptographic systems that rely on the hardness of these problems.
