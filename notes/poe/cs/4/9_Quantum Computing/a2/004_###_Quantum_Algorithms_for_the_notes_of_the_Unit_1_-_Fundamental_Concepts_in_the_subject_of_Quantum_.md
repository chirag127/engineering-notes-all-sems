### Quantum Algorithms for the notes of the Unit 1 - Fundamental Concepts in the subject of Quantum Computing

* Quantum Fourier Transform (QFT) - The QFT is a quantum algorithm that converts a quantum state from the computational basis to the Fourier basis. It is a fundamental building block for many other quantum algorithms. Some key points about QFT are:

- It can be implemented efficiently using quantum gates.
- It helps in solving problems like order finding and Discrete Logarithm.
- It converts a quantum state from the computational basis to the Fourier basis.

* Deutsch-Jozsa algorithm - The Deutsch-Jozsa algorithm is a quantum algorithm that can determine whether a function is constant or balanced in just one evaluation of the function. Some key points about Deutsch-Jozsa algorithm are:

- It demonstrates the power of quantum superposition and quantum interference.
- It is exponentially faster than any classical algorithm.
- It can distinguish between constant and balanced functions in just one evaluation.

* Simon's algorithm - Simon's algorithm is a quantum algorithm that can find the period of a periodic function in just one evaluation of the function. Some key points about Simon's algorithm are:

- It is a black-box algorithm as it does not require any knowledge of the internal structure of the function.
- It can find the period in just one query compared to exponentially many queries required classically.
- It demonstrates the power of quantum algorithms in solving hard problems efficiently.

* Shor's algorithm - Shor's algorithm is a quantum algorithm for integer factorization. Some key points about Shor's algorithm are:

- It can factor integers in polynomial time which is exponentially faster than the best known classical algorithms.
- It utilizes the quantum Fourier transform and quantum interference.
- It has important implications for cryptography as it can break widely used RSA encryption.
