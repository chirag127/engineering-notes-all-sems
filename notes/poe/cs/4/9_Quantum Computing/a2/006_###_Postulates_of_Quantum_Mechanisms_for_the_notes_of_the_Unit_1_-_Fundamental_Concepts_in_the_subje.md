### Postulates of Quantum Mechanisms for the notes of the Unit 1 - Fundamental Concepts in the subject of Quantum Computing

1. Superposition: A quantum bit or 'qubit' can be in a superposition of states, where it holds a value of 0 and 1 at the same time with some probability of each. This is called the superposition principle. This allows a quantum system to perform certain computations faster than a classical system.

Mnemonic: A qubit can be here and there, 0 and 1 without a care. Superposition is the reason, quantum supremacy's season.

2. Entanglement: When two or more qubits are entangled, their states are dependent on each other, even if they are separated by a large distance. An action on one qubit will instantaneously influence the other, which seems to violate classical physics and special relativity. Entanglement is a key feature for many quantum algorithms and technologies like quantum cryptography.

Mnemonic: Entangled quite a pair, Action on one, the other shares. Separated by space, yet clandestinely embrace. Quantum correlation, nature's aberration.

3. Measurement: The state of a quantum system collapses from a superposition to a single definite state when measured or observed. This causes the loss of quantum coherence which is essential for quantum algorithms and technologies to function. Precise control and manipulation is required to minimize decoherence and measurement disturbance.

Mnemonic: Superposition succumbs to measurement's irritation. Coherence is lost, with no negation. Control the quantum, avoid decoherence situation.
