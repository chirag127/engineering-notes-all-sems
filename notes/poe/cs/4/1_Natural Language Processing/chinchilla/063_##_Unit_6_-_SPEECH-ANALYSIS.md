## Unit 6 - SPEECH-ANALYSIS

Speech analysis is a crucial aspect of the field of speech and language processing. It involves the use of various computational techniques to extract information from speech signals. Speech analysis plays a significant role in several applications such as speech recognition, speaker identification, emotion recognition, and speech synthesis. In this unit, we will learn about the different techniques used for speech analysis.

### Spectral Analysis

Spectral analysis involves the decomposition of a speech signal into its constituent frequency components. It provides information about the frequency content of the signal and helps in identifying different phonemes and syllables. Spectral analysis can be performed using techniques such as Fourier transform, short-time Fourier transform, and wavelet transform.

### Prosodic Analysis

Prosody refers to the rhythm, intonation, and stress patterns in speech. Prosodic analysis involves the extraction of these features from speech signals. It can be used to identify different types of speech, such as questions, statements, and exclamations. Prosodic analysis techniques include pitch tracking, stress detection, and intonation analysis.

### Phonemic Analysis

Phonemic analysis involves the identification of individual phonemes or speech sounds in a speech signal. It is a critical step in speech recognition systems. Phonemic analysis techniques include template matching, hidden Markov models, and neural networks.

### Speaker Identification

Speaker identification involves the identification of a specific person based on their voice. It is used in several applications such as security systems and forensic analysis. Speaker identification techniques include spectral analysis, prosodic analysis, and phonemic analysis.

### Emotion Recognition

Emotion recognition involves the identification of the emotional state of a speaker based on their speech. It can be used in several applications such as customer service and mental health diagnosis. Emotion recognition techniques include prosodic analysis and machine learning algorithms.

### Mnemonics and Learning Tricks

- To remember the different types of speech, use the acronym QSE: Questions, Statements, and Exclamations.
- To remember the different techniques for speech analysis, use the acronym PSPE: Prosodic analysis, Spectral analysis, Phonemic analysis, and Emotion recognition.

In conclusion, speech analysis is an essential aspect of speech and language processing. It involves the use of various computational techniques to extract information from speech signals. Spectral analysis, prosodic analysis, and phonemic analysis are some of the techniques used for speech analysis. Speaker identification and emotion recognition are two important applications of speech analysis. Mnemonics and learning tricks can help in remembering the different techniques and types of speech.
