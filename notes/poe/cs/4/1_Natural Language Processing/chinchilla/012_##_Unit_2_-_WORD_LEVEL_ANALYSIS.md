## Unit 2 - WORD LEVEL ANALYSIS

In natural language processing, the analysis of individual words is an important step towards understanding the meaning of a sentence or document. This unit covers various techniques and tools used for word level analysis.

### 1. Tokenization
Tokenization is the process of breaking down text into individual words or tokens. This is the first step in many natural language processing tasks. There are different approaches to tokenization such as whitespace tokenization, word-based tokenization, and regular expression-based tokenization.

### 2. Stemming and Lemmatization
Stemming and lemmatization are techniques used to reduce words to their base form. Stemming involves removing the suffixes from words to get to the stem. Lemmatization, on the other hand, uses a dictionary to convert words to their base form.

#### Learning Trick: 
Mnemonic for Stemming and Lemmatization - 
- Stemming: "Stem the word by cutting its end."
- Lemmatization: "Lemmatize the word to its root."

### 3. Part-of-Speech Tagging
Part-of-speech tagging is the process of identifying the part of speech of each word in a sentence. This is important for many natural language processing tasks such as sentiment analysis and text classification.

### 4. Named Entity Recognition
Named entity recognition is the process of identifying and classifying named entities in text such as people, organizations, and locations.

### 5. Word Embeddings
Word embeddings are a technique used to represent words as vectors in a high-dimensional space. This is useful for many natural language processing tasks such as language translation and sentiment analysis.

### 6. Word Sense Disambiguation
Word sense disambiguation is the process of identifying the correct meaning of a word based on the context in which it is used. This is important for many natural language processing tasks such as machine translation and text classification.

In summary, word level analysis is an important aspect of natural language processing. Techniques such as tokenization, stemming, lemmatization, part-of-speech tagging, named entity recognition, word embeddings, and word sense disambiguation are essential tools for analyzing text and understanding its meaning.
