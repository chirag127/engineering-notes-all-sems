### Rule-based for the notes of the Unit 2 - WORD LEVEL ANALYSIS in the subject of Natural Language Processing

Rule-based natural language processing (NLP) is an approach that involves creating a set of rules that can be used to analyze and process natural language data. This approach relies on linguistic knowledge and expertise to create rules that can capture the patterns and structures of language.

Here are some key points to keep in mind when studying rule-based NLP for word-level analysis:

1. **Tokenization:** Tokenization involves breaking down a sentence or document into individual words, phrases, or other units of meaning. The rules for tokenization can vary depending on the language, the domain, and the specific application.

2. **Stemming and Lemmatization:** Stemming and lemmatization are techniques used to reduce words to their base or root forms. This can help to reduce the complexity of language processing and improve the accuracy of analysis. Stemming involves removing the suffixes or prefixes from a word to get its base form, while lemmatization involves using a dictionary or other knowledge source to determine the base form of a word.

3. **Part-of-Speech Tagging:** Part-of-speech (POS) tagging involves assigning a grammatical tag to each word in a sentence, indicating its syntactic and semantic role. This can help to disambiguate words with multiple meanings and improve the accuracy of NLP tasks such as parsing, sentiment analysis, and named entity recognition.

4. **Named Entity Recognition:** Named entity recognition (NER) involves identifying and classifying named entities such as people, organizations, locations, and dates in a text. This can be useful for applications such as information extraction, question answering, and text summarization.

5. **Chunking and Parsing:** Chunking and parsing are techniques used to identify and group words into meaningful chunks or phrases. This can help to identify the syntactic structure of a sentence and extract relevant information. Chunking involves identifying groups of words that belong together based on their POS tags, while parsing involves analyzing the grammatical structure of a sentence using a formal grammar.

6. **Regular Expressions:** Regular expressions are a powerful tool for pattern matching and text manipulation. They can be used to define rules for tokenization, stemming, lemmatization, POS tagging, and other NLP tasks.

Mnemonics and Learning Tricks:

- To remember the difference between stemming and lemmatization, think of "stemming" as cutting off the branches of a tree to get to the trunk, while "lemmatization" is like using a dictionary to find the root word.
- To remember the different parts of speech, use the acronym "FANBOYS" for coordinating conjunctions (for, and, nor, but, or, yet, so), and "SWABIs" for subordinating conjunctions (since, when, although, because, if).
- To remember the syntax of a regular expression, think of it as a pattern that matches a sequence of characters. The pattern is defined using a set of rules or operators that specify how the characters should be matched. For example, the "." operator matches any single character, while the "*" operator matches zero or more occurrences of the preceding character.
