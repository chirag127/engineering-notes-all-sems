### Stochastic for the notes of the Unit 2 - WORD LEVEL ANALYSIS in the subject of Natural Language Processing

In natural language processing, Stochastic methods are widely used for word level analysis. Stochastic methods use probability theory to analyze and model language data. These methods are useful in various tasks such as language modeling, machine translation, and speech recognition.

Some important concepts related to stochastic methods in NLP are:

1. Probability Theory: Probability theory is the foundation of stochastic methods. It deals with the study of random events and their likelihood of occurrence. In NLP, probability theory is used to model the likelihood of different words or sequences of words in a given context.

2. Markov Models: Markov models are stochastic models that use probability theory to model the likelihood of different sequences of events. In NLP, Markov models are used to model the probability of different sequences of words in a given context. 

3. Hidden Markov Models: Hidden Markov models (HMMs) are a type of stochastic model that is widely used in NLP. HMMs are used to model sequences of events where the events are not directly observable. In NLP, HMMs are used for tasks such as part-of-speech tagging and speech recognition.

4. Naive Bayes: Naive Bayes is a probabilistic algorithm that is widely used in NLP tasks such as text classification and sentiment analysis. It uses Bayes' theorem to calculate the probability of a document belonging to a particular class.

5. Maximum Likelihood Estimation: Maximum likelihood estimation (MLE) is a method used to estimate the parameters of a probabilistic model. In NLP, MLE is used to estimate the parameters of language models such as n-gram models.

Mnemonics and Learning Tricks:

- To remember the concept of probability theory, you can use the acronym "PEMDAS" (Please Excuse My Dear Aunt Sally). This acronym represents the order of operations in probability theory (Parentheses, Exponents, Multiplication, Division, Addition, Subtraction).

- To remember the concept of Markov models, you can use the phrase "Memoryless Markov Model". This phrase emphasizes the fact that Markov models only consider the current state and do not take into account the history of previous states.

- To remember the concept of Hidden Markov Models, you can use the phrase "Obscure Outcomes Observable States". This phrase emphasizes the fact that in HMMs, the outcomes are not directly observable, but the states are observable.

- To remember the concept of Naive Bayes, you can use the phrase "Naive Bayes assumes independence". This phrase emphasizes the fact that Naive Bayes assumes that the features are independent of each other.

Stochastic methods are an important tool in NLP for word level analysis. By using probability theory and stochastic models, we can model and analyze language data more effectively.
