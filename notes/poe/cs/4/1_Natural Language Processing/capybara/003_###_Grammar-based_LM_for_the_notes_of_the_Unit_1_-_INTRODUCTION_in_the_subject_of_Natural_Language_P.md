### Grammar-based LM for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of Natural Language Processing

In Natural Language Processing, language modeling is a process of predicting the next word in a given sentence. A Language Model (LM) is a statistical model that estimates the probability of a sequence of words in a language. 

Grammar-based LM is one of the types of Language Models that uses a formal grammar to generate sentences. This LM uses a set of production rules to generate sentences that are syntactically and semantically correct. These rules are used to generate sentences based on the grammar of a language. 

#### Advantages of Grammar-based LM
- It can generate sentences that are grammatically correct.
- It can handle a large vocabulary of words.
- It can generate sentences that are semantically correct.

#### Disadvantages of Grammar-based LM
- It may not be able to handle the complexities of natural language.
- It may not be able to generate sentences that are contextually appropriate.

#### Examples of Grammar-based LM
- The Chomsky Normal Form (CNF) is a type of grammar that is often used in Grammar-based LM. It is a context-free grammar that has production rules in the form of A -> BC or A -> a, where A, B, and C are non-terminals, and a is a terminal symbol.
- The Earley parser is a type of parser that is often used with Grammar-based LM. It uses a chart to keep track of possible parse trees for a given sentence. 

#### Applications of Grammar-based LM
- It is used in Natural Language Generation systems to generate sentences that are grammatically and semantically correct.
- It is used in Machine Translation systems to generate translations that are syntactically and semantically correct.
- It is used in Speech Recognition systems to generate hypotheses of spoken sentences that are grammatically and semantically correct.

#### Learning Trick
A good mnemonic to remember Grammar-based LM is "Grammar rules the language." This can help students remember that Grammar-based LM uses a set of production rules to generate sentences in a language.
