## Unit 6 - SPEECH-ANALYSIS

Speech analysis is the process of extracting useful information from spoken words. This unit covers the basics of speech analysis, including the techniques used to extract features from speech signals, and the applications of speech analysis in various fields.

### Techniques of Speech Analysis

1. **Speech Segmentation**: This technique involves dividing the speech signal into smaller segments to identify and analyze various features.

2. **Feature Extraction**: Feature extraction involves identifying the useful characteristics of speech signals that can be used for further analysis. Some commonly used features include pitch, formants, and energy.

3. **Speech Recognition**: Speech recognition is the process of converting spoken words into written text. This technique is used in various applications, such as virtual assistants, speech-to-text software, and voice-activated systems.

4. **Speaker Recognition**: Speaker recognition involves identifying the speaker based on their unique voice characteristics, such as pitch, tone, and cadence.

### Applications of Speech Analysis

1. **Speech Therapy**: Speech analysis is used in speech therapy to diagnose and treat various speech disorders, such as stuttering, lisping, and voice disorders.

2. **Forensic Analysis**: Speech analysis is used in forensic investigations to identify suspects based on their voice characteristics.

3. **Emotion Recognition**: Speech analysis is used to recognize the emotional state of a person based on their speech signals. This technique is used in various applications, such as customer service and psychology research.

4. **Natural Language Processing**: Speech analysis is used in natural language processing to improve the accuracy of speech recognition and language understanding.

### Mnemonics and Learning Tricks

1. **Remember the 4 S's**: Speech Segmentation, Feature Extraction, Speech Recognition, and Speaker Recognition.

2. **Use Acronyms**: Create acronyms for the different techniques and applications to make them easier to remember. For example, SSFR could stand for Speech Segmentation, Feature Extraction, Speech Recognition, and Speaker Recognition.

3. **Practice with Speech Recognition Software**: Use speech recognition software to practice recognizing different speech signals and identifying the features that are important for analysis.

In conclusion, speech analysis is a vital field that has many applications in various fields. Understanding the techniques and applications of speech analysis is essential for anyone interested in the field. Using mnemonics and learning tricks can help make the material easier to remember for exams and real-world applications.
