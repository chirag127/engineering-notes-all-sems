### Issues in PoS tagging for the notes of the Unit 2 - WORD LEVEL ANALYSIS in the subject of Natural Language Processing

Part-of-Speech (PoS) tagging is a crucial task in Natural Language Processing (NLP). It involves assigning grammatical categories (such as noun, verb, adjective, etc.) to words in a sentence. However, PoS tagging is not an easy task and is accompanied by several issues. Some of the major issues are as follows:

1. Ambiguity: Many words in a sentence can have multiple PoS categories depending on the context. For example, the word "run" can be a verb (I like to run every day) or a noun (I went for a run in the morning). In such cases, it becomes difficult for the PoS tagger to assign the correct category.

2. Out of Vocabulary (OOV) words: Sometimes, a PoS tagger may encounter words that are not present in its training data. These words are called OOV words, and it becomes difficult to assign a PoS tag to them. 

3. Rare words: Some words occur very rarely in a language, and it becomes difficult to assign them a PoS tag. For instance, a word like "sesquipedalian" is rare and may not be present in the training data of the PoS tagger.

4. Homonyms: Homonyms are words that have the same spelling but different meanings. For example, the word "bow" can refer to a weapon or a gesture of respect. In such cases, it becomes difficult to assign the correct PoS tag without considering the context.

5. Idioms and Phrasal Verbs: Idioms and phrasal verbs are ubiquitous in natural language. They present a challenge for PoS tagging because they often involve multiple words that have to be tagged together. For example, in the idiom "kick the bucket," both "kick" and "bucket" have to be tagged together as a single unit.

Mnemonics and learning tricks can be helpful in remembering some of these issues. For example, for the issue of ambiguity, one can remember the phrase "Context is king" to emphasize the importance of considering the context while assigning PoS tags. Similarly, for the issue of OOV words, one can remember the phrase "Don't panic, use morphology" to emphasize the use of morphological analysis to assign PoS tags to unknown words.

In conclusion, PoS tagging is a challenging task in NLP, and there are several issues that need to be addressed. By understanding these issues and using appropriate techniques, we can improve the accuracy of PoS tagging and achieve better results in various NLP applications.
