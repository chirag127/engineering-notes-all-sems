### Word Senses for the notes of the Unit 4 - SEMANTICS AND PRAGMATICS in the subject of Natural Language Processing

In natural language processing, understanding the meaning of words is crucial. And to understand the meaning, we need to identify the different senses of a word. The same word can have multiple meanings depending on the context in which it is used. 

Let's explore the different word senses and how they are identified in NLP:

1. **Lexical Ambiguity:** It refers to the situation where a word has multiple meanings, and it is not clear which meaning is intended in a specific context. For example, the word "bank" can refer to a financial institution or the edge of a river.

2. **Polysemy:** It is a type of lexical ambiguity where a word has multiple related meanings. For example, the word "book" can refer to a written or printed work or to schedule an appointment.

3. **Homonymy:** It is a type of lexical ambiguity where a word has multiple unrelated meanings. For example, the word "bat" can refer to a flying mammal or a piece of sports equipment.

4. **Synonymy:** It refers to the situation where two or more words have the same or similar meanings. For example, "happy" and "joyful" are synonyms.

5. **Antonymy:** It refers to the situation where two words have opposite meanings. For example, "hot" and "cold" are antonyms.

To identify the different word senses, NLP techniques like WordNet, a lexical database, and Part-of-Speech (POS) tagging are used. 

Mnemonics and learning tricks can be helpful to remember the different word senses. For example, for polysemy, we can remember that "poly" means many, and "semy" comes from the Greek word "sema," which means a sign or meaning. So, polysemy means many meanings. Similarly, for antonymy, we can remember that "anti" means opposite, and "onym" comes from the Greek word "onoma," which means a name. So, antonymy means opposite names.

In conclusion, understanding the different word senses is crucial in NLP, and by using techniques like WordNet and POS tagging, we can identify these senses. Mnemonics and learning tricks can be helpful in remembering the different types of word senses.
