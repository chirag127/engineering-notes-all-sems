### Language Modeling for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of Natural Language Processing

Language modeling is an important concept in the field of Natural Language Processing (NLP). It involves predicting the probability distribution of words in a sentence or a sequence of words. Language models are used in various NLP tasks such as machine translation, speech recognition, and text summarization.

Here are some important points to keep in mind about language modeling:

1. Language Modeling is a statistical approach to language processing that predicts the likelihood of a given sequence of words.

2. It is based on the assumption that the probability of a word depends on the probability of its previous words in the sequence.

3. The most common approach to language modeling is n-gram modeling, which involves predicting the probability of the next word based on the previous n-1 words.

4. One of the challenges in language modeling is dealing with the sparsity problem, where the model encounters unseen or rare words in the training data.

5. Smoothing techniques such as Laplace smoothing and Good-Turing smoothing are used to handle the sparsity problem and improve the accuracy of language models.

6. Language models are evaluated based on perplexity, which measures how well the model predicts the next word in a sequence.

7. There are various types of language models such as unigram, bigram, trigram, and neural language models.

8. Neural language models use neural networks to learn the probability distribution of words in a sentence or a sequence of words.

9. Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs) are commonly used for language modeling tasks because they can capture the sequential nature of language.

10. Language models are used in various NLP tasks such as machine translation, speech recognition, and text summarization.
