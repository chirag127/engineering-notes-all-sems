### Grammar rules for English for the notes of the Unit 3 - SYNTACTIC ANALYSIS in the subject of Natural Language Processing:

Grammar rules are the foundation of any language, and English is no exception. In this unit, we will learn about the grammar rules that are used in English language processing. Here are some of the important grammar rules that you should know:

1. Parts of speech: There are eight parts of speech in English. They are nouns, verbs, adjectives, adverbs, pronouns, prepositions, conjunctions, and interjections. Each part of speech has its own function in a sentence.

2. Subject-Verb Agreement: The subject and verb in a sentence must agree in number. For example, if the subject is singular, the verb must also be singular. If the subject is plural, the verb must also be plural.

3. Tense: There are three main tenses in English - present, past, and future. The tense of a sentence tells us when the action took place.

4. Active and Passive Voice: In the active voice, the subject performs the action. In the passive voice, the subject receives the action.

5. Sentence Structure: A sentence in English consists of a subject and a predicate. The predicate contains the verb and any other words that modify the subject.

6. Phrases and Clauses: A phrase is a group of words that act as a single part of speech. A clause is a group of words that contain a subject and a predicate.

7. Punctuation: Punctuation marks, such as commas, periods, and question marks, are used to clarify the meaning of a sentence and to make it easier to read.

Mnemonics and Learning Tricks:

- To remember the eight parts of speech, use the acronym FANBOYS, which stands for: 

  F - conjunctions (for, and, nor, but, or, yet, so)
  
  A - adjectives
  
  N - nouns
  
  B - adverbs
  
  O - pronouns
  
  Y - verbs
  
  S - prepositions
  
  - To remember the three main tenses, use the acronym PPF:

  P - present
  
  P - past
  
  F - future
  
  - To remember the difference between active and passive voice, think of the subject as the doer in the active voice and the receiver in the passive voice.
  
By understanding and applying these grammar rules, you will be able to analyze and understand English sentences more effectively. Good luck with your studies!
