### Statistical LM for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of Natural Language Processing

Statistical Language Modeling (LM) is a technique used to predict the probability of occurrence of a given sequence of words in a language. It is an important aspect of Natural Language Processing (NLP) and finds its application in various fields such as speech recognition, machine translation, and text-to-speech synthesis.

#### Why Statistical LM?

The main reason for using Statistical LM is that it provides a way to model the probability of occurrence of words in a language. The probability of a word sequence can be used to predict the next word in the sequence. This is particularly useful in speech recognition where the system needs to predict the next word in the speech stream. Statistical LM can also be used to identify the language of a text and to generate text in a particular style.

#### Types of Statistical LM

There are two main types of Statistical LM:

1. N-gram models: These models are based on the assumption that the probability of a word in a sequence depends only on the previous N-1 words. For example, in a bigram model, the probability of a word depends only on the previous word. In a trigram model, the probability of a word depends on the previous two words.

2. Neural network-based models: These models use neural networks to model the probability of a word sequence. They are more complex than N-gram models but can capture more complex patterns in the data.

#### Advantages of Statistical LM

- Statistical LM is a widely used technique in NLP and has proven to be effective in various applications such as speech recognition and machine translation.
- It provides a way to model the probability of occurrence of words in a language, which is useful in predicting the next word in a sequence.
- It can be used to identify the language of a text and to generate text in a particular style.

#### Disadvantages of Statistical LM

- N-gram models suffer from the problem of sparsity, where the probability of a word sequence becomes zero if the sequence has not occurred in the training data.
- Neural network-based models are computationally expensive and require large amounts of training data.
