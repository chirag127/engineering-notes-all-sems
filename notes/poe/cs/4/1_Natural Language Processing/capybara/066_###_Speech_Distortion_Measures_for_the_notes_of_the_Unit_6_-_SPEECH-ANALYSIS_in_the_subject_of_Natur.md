### Speech Distortion Measures for the notes of the Unit 6 - SPEECH-ANALYSIS in the subject of Natural Language Processing

Speech distortion measures are used to evaluate the quality of speech signals. These measures assess the difference between the original and processed speech signals, and are important in assessing the effectiveness of speech enhancement techniques. In this unit, we will discuss some of the commonly used speech distortion measures.

#### Mean Opinion Score (MOS)

Mean Opinion Score (MOS) is a subjective measure used to evaluate the quality of a speech signal. MOS is used to obtain the average opinion of a group of listeners about the quality of a speech signal. Listeners are asked to rate the quality of the speech signal on a scale of 1 to 5, where 1 represents poor quality and 5 represents excellent quality. MOS is widely used in the industry to evaluate the quality of speech codecs.

#### Signal-to-Noise Ratio (SNR)

Signal-to-Noise Ratio (SNR) is a measure of the ratio of the power of the speech signal to the power of the noise signal. SNR is used to determine the level of noise in a speech signal. SNR can be measured in either the time domain or the frequency domain. In the time domain, SNR is calculated as the ratio of the energy of the speech signal to the energy of the noise signal. In the frequency domain, SNR is calculated as the ratio of the power of the speech signal to the power of the noise signal at each frequency band.

#### Perceptual Evaluation of Speech Quality (PESQ)

Perceptual Evaluation of Speech Quality (PESQ) is an objective measure used to evaluate the quality of a speech signal. PESQ is based on the human perception of speech quality. PESQ compares the original and processed speech signals and calculates a score based on the difference between the two signals. PESQ is widely used in the industry to evaluate the quality of speech codecs.

#### Short-Time Objective Intelligibility (STOI)

Short-Time Objective Intelligibility (STOI) is an objective measure used to evaluate the intelligibility of a speech signal. STOI measures the degree to which a speech signal can be understood in the presence of noise. STOI is based on the correlation between the original and processed speech signals. STOI is widely used in the industry to evaluate the effectiveness of speech enhancement techniques.

#### Learning Tricks

- To remember MOS, think of it as the average opinion of a group of listeners about the quality of a speech signal.
- To remember SNR, think of it as a measure of the ratio of the power of the speech signal to the power of the noise signal.
- To remember PESQ, think of it as an objective measure based on the human perception of speech quality.
- To remember STOI, think of it as a measure of the intelligibility of a speech signal in the presence of noise.

In conclusion, speech distortion measures are important in evaluating the quality of speech signals. MOS, SNR, PESQ, and STOI are some of the commonly used measures. By understanding these measures and their applications, we can evaluate the effectiveness of speech enhancement techniques and improve the quality of speech signals.
