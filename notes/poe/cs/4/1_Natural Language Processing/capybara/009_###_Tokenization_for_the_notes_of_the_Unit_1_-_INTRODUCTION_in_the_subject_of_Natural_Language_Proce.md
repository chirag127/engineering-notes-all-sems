### Tokenization for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of Natural Language Processing

Tokenization is the process of breaking down a piece of text into smaller units called tokens. In Natural Language Processing (NLP), tokenization is an important step as it allows us to analyze and manipulate the individual words or phrases that make up a piece of text. Here are some important points to keep in mind regarding tokenization:

- Tokenization can be performed at different levels depending on the requirements of the task. For example, we could tokenize a piece of text into individual words, or we could tokenize it into phrases or sentences.

- One of the most common methods of tokenization is to split a piece of text on whitespace characters, such as spaces or tabs. However, this method can result in inaccurate tokenization in certain cases, such as when dealing with contractions or hyphenated words.

- Another method of tokenization is to use regular expressions to match patterns in the text. This allows for more fine-grained control over the tokenization process, but can be more difficult to implement.

- A good mnemonic to remember when tokenizing is to think of breaking a piece of text into smaller pieces like breaking a chocolate bar into smaller pieces. Just like a chocolate bar can be broken into individual squares or groups of squares, a piece of text can be broken into individual words or groups of words.

- It's important to note that tokenization is not a perfect process and there may be cases where it is difficult to accurately tokenize a piece of text. Additionally, tokenization can be language-dependent, so different languages may require different tokenization methods.

Overall, tokenization is a crucial step in many NLP tasks and understanding the different methods and considerations involved can help improve the accuracy and effectiveness of these tasks.
