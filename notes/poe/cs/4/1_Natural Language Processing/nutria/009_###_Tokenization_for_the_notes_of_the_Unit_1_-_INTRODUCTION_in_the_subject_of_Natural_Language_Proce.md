
### Tokenization for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of Natural Language Processing

Tokenization is the process of breaking down a sentence into its individual words, or tokens. Tokenization is a key step in the process of Natural Language Processing (NLP). It helps to identify the structure and meaning of the text by dividing it into smaller units.

There are two types of tokenization: word tokenization and sentence tokenization. Word tokenization involves breaking down a sentence into its individual words, while sentence tokenization involves breaking down a document into its individual sentences. 

Word tokenization is used to identify the structure of a sentence by breaking it down into its individual words. This helps to identify the meaning of the sentence, as well as its grammar structure.

Sentence tokenization is used to identify the structure of a document by breaking it down into its individual sentences. This helps to identify the main ideas of the document and its structure.

There are several techniques used in tokenization. These include rule-based tokenization, statistical tokenization, and hybrid tokenization. Rule-based tokenization uses a set of rules to identify the tokens in a sentence. Statistical tokenization uses statistical models to identify the tokens in a sentence. Hybrid tokenization combines both rule-based and statistical tokenization techniques. 

Mnemonics and learning tricks can help to remember the process of tokenization. For example, the acronym "TOPS" (Tokenize, Organize, Parse, and Subdivide) can help to remember the steps in tokenization. Additionally, visualizing the process of tokenization can help to remember it more easily. 

Tokenization is an important step in the process of Natural Language Processing. It helps to identify the structure and meaning of the text by breaking it down into smaller units. By understanding the process of tokenization, it is possible to create more accurate and efficient NLP systems.
