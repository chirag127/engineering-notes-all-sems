
## Unit 4 - SEMANTICS AND PRAGMATICS

Semantics and pragmatics are two branches of linguistics that focus on the meaning of words and sentences. Semantics deals with the literal meaning of words and phrases, while pragmatics deals with the context in which words and phrases are used. 

Semantics looks at how words are used in a language and how they relate to each other. It is concerned with the meaning of words, how they are formed, and how they are used in different contexts. It also looks at how words can be combined to create meaning. 

Pragmatics looks at how language is used in real-life contexts. It examines the context in which words and phrases are used and how this affects their meaning. It also looks at how people use language to communicate with each other. 

The two branches of linguistics are related, but they have different focuses. Semantics looks at the meaning of words, while pragmatics looks at how language is used in real-life contexts. Both are important for understanding language and communication.
