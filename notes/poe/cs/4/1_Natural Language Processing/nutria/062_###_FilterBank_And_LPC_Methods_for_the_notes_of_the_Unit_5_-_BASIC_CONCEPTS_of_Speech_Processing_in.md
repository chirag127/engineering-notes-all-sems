
### Filter Bank And LPC Methods for the notes of the Unit 5 - BASIC CONCEPTS of Speech Processing in the subject of Natural Language Processing

* Filter Bank And LPC Methods are two techniques used for speech processing. 
* Filter Banks are used to divide a signal into multiple frequency bands and then process each band separately. 
* LPC (Linear Predictive Coding) is a method for analyzing and synthesizing speech. It uses a mathematical model of the vocal tract to predict the speech signal. 
* Filter Banks and LPC Methods are used in speech recognition, speech synthesis, and other speech processing applications.
* Filter Banks can be used to reduce the amount of data required for speech processing by dividing the signal into multiple frequency bands. 
* LPC is used to analyze and synthesize the speech signal. It uses a mathematical model of the vocal tract to predict the speech signal. 
* Filter Banks and LPC Methods can be used together to improve the accuracy of speech processing applications. 
* Filter Banks and LPC Methods can also be used to reduce the amount of data required for speech processing. 
* Mnemonics and learning tricks can be used to remember the concepts related to Filter Banks and LPC Methods. For example, the acronym "FBLPC" can be used to remember the concepts of Filter Bank and LPC.
