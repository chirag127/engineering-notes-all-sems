
## Unit 6 - SPEECH-ANALYSIS

Speech-Analysis is the process of analyzing spoken language in order to gain insights into the speaker's state of mind and intent. It is used in a variety of fields, such as psychology, marketing, and customer service. 

### Mnemonics and Learning Tricks

* SPEECH: Speech-Analysis is about analyzing the speaker's SPEECH.
* Listen: Listen carefully to the speaker's words, intonation, and vocal patterns.
* Analyze: Analyze the speaker's words, intonation, and vocal patterns to gain insights.
* Emotions: Look for clues to the speaker's emotions, such as anger, fear, or joy.
* Context: Consider the context of the conversation, such as the setting and the participants.

### Advantages

* Speech-Analysis can provide valuable insights into the speaker's state of mind and intent.
* It can be used to better understand customer needs and preferences.
* It can help to improve customer service and marketing efforts.

### Disadvantages

* Speech-Analysis can be subjective and open to interpretation.
* It can be difficult to accurately interpret the speaker's words and intent.
* It can be time-consuming and expensive to implement.
