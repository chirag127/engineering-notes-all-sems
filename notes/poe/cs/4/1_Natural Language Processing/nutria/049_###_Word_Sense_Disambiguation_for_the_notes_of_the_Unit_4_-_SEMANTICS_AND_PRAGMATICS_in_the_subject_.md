
### Word Sense Disambiguation for the notes of the Unit 4 - SEMANTICS AND PRAGMATICS in the subject of Natural Language Processing

Word Sense Disambiguation (WSD) is a process of identifying the correct sense of a word in a given context. It is an important task in Natural Language Processing (NLP) as it helps machines to understand the meaning of words in a sentence. WSD can be used to improve the accuracy of machine translation, information retrieval, and other NLP tasks.

The main goal of WSD is to identify the sense of a word in a given context. To do this, WSD algorithms use a variety of techniques, such as semantic analysis, knowledge-based methods, and machine learning.

#### Semantic Analysis

Semantic analysis is a method of WSD that uses the context of a sentence to identify the sense of a word. This method uses the meaning of words and the relationships between them to identify the sense of a word.

#### Knowledge-based Methods

Knowledge-based methods are a type of WSD that uses a knowledge base, such as a dictionary or thesaurus, to identify the sense of a word. This method uses the definitions of words in the knowledge base to identify the sense of a word.

#### Machine Learning

Machine learning is a type of WSD that uses algorithms to learn from data. This method uses a training dataset to learn the patterns of a language and then uses those patterns to identify the sense of a word.

In conclusion, Word Sense Disambiguation is an important task in Natural Language Processing as it helps machines to understand the meaning of words in a sentence. WSD algorithms use a variety of techniques, such as semantic analysis, knowledge-based methods, and machine learning, to identify the sense of a word in a given context.
