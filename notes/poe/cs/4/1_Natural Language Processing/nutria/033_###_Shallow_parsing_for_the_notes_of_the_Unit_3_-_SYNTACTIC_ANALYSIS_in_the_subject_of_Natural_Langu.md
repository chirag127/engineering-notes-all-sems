

### Shallow Parsing for the Notes of Unit 3 - Syntactic Analysis in the Subject of Natural Language Processing

Shallow parsing, also known as light parsing or chunking, is a process of analyzing a sentence to identify the constituents (nouns, verbs, adjectives, etc.) and label them according to their type. It is a type of syntactic analysis that involves a shallow analysis of a sentence to identify the phrases and assign them a label.

Shallow parsing is used to identify the main constituents of a sentence and the relationship between them. It is used to identify the parts of speech and the structure of a sentence.

Mnemonic for Shallow Parsing:

* **S**tructure - Identify the structure of the sentence.
* **H**ierarchy - Identify the hierarchy of the sentence.
* **A**nalysis - Analyze the sentence to identify the parts of speech.
* **L**abel - Label the parts of speech.
* **L**ink - Link the parts of speech to form a phrase.

Advantages of Shallow Parsing:

* It is a fast and efficient way to analyze sentences.
* It is useful for extracting information from unstructured data.
* It can be used to identify the main topics in a text.

Disadvantages of Shallow Parsing:

* It is not as accurate as deep parsing, as it does not take into account the context of the sentence.
* It is not suitable for tasks such as semantic analysis and machine translation.

Applications of Shallow Parsing:

* Information Extraction - Shallow parsing can be used to extract information from unstructured data.
* Text Summarization - Shallow parsing can be used to identify the main topics in a text and generate summaries.
* Question Answering - Shallow parsing can be used to identify the keywords in a question and find the answer.
* Natural Language Understanding - Shallow parsing can be used to identify the parts of speech and the structure of a sentence, which is useful for natural language understanding.
