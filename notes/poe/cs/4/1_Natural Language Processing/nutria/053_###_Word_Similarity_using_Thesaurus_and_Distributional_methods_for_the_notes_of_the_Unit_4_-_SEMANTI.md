
### Word Similarity using Thesaurus and Distributional methods for the notes of the Unit 4 - SEMANTICS AND PRAGMATICS in the subject of Natural Language Processing

Word similarity is a measure of how similar two words are in terms of their meaning. It is an important concept in natural language processing and is used to determine the relatedness of words. Word similarity can be calculated using either thesaurus-based or distributional methods. 

**Thesaurus-Based Methods**

Thesaurus-based methods use a thesaurus to compare words. The thesaurus contains a list of synonyms and antonyms for each word. The similarity between two words is determined by the number of shared synonyms or antonyms. Thesaurus-based methods are useful for determining the semantic relatedness of words, but they are limited in their ability to capture the nuances of word meaning. 

**Distributional Methods**

Distributional methods use a corpus of text to compare words. The corpus is a collection of documents that contain the words being compared. The similarity between two words is determined by the context in which they appear. Distributional methods are useful for determining the semantic relatedness of words, and they can capture the nuances of word meaning. 

Mnemonics and Learning Tricks: 
When trying to remember the differences between thesaurus-based and distributional methods for word similarity, it can be helpful to think of thesaurus-based methods as using a dictionary to compare words and distributional methods as using a book to compare words.
