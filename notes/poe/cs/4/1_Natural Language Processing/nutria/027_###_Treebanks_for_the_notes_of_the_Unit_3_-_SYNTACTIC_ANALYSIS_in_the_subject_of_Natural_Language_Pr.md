

### Treebanks for the Notes of the Unit 3 - Syntactic Analysis in the Subject of Natural Language Processing

* Treebanks are collections of annotated sentences that can be used to train and evaluate natural language processing (NLP) models.
* The annotations in a treebank provide information about the syntactic structure of the sentences, including the parts of speech, the syntactic relationships between words, and the syntactic structure of the sentence as a whole.
* Treebanks are used to train and evaluate models for syntactic analysis, such as part-of-speech tagging, parsing, and sentence segmentation.
* Treebanks are also used for other tasks such as machine translation, text summarization, and question answering.
* Treebanks are typically annotated manually, but there are also automatic methods for creating treebanks from existing text corpora.
* Treebanks can be used to evaluate the accuracy of NLP models, and to compare different models against each other.
* Mnemonic: A treebank is like a forest of annotated sentences.
