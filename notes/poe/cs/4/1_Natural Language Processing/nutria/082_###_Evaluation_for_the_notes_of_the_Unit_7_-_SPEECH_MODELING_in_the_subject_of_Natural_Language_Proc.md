
### Evaluation for the notes of the Unit 7 - SPEECH MODELING in the subject of Natural Language Processing

1. **Accuracy:** This refers to the percentage of correct predictions made by the model. It is calculated by dividing the number of correct predictions by the total number of predictions.

2. **Precision:** Precision is a measure of how many of the predictions made by the model are actually correct. It is calculated by dividing the number of true positives by the total number of predictions.

3. **Recall:** Recall is a measure of how many of the correct predictions made by the model are actually correct. It is calculated by dividing the number of true positives by the total number of actual positives.

4. **F-measure:** F-measure is a measure of the accuracy and precision of the model. It is calculated by taking the harmonic mean of precision and recall.

5. **AUC (Area Under the Curve):** AUC is a measure of how well the model is able to distinguish between positive and negative examples. It is calculated by plotting the true positive rate against the false positive rate and measuring the area under the curve.

6. **Mnemonic:** P-R-F-A-M is a helpful mnemonic to remember the five evaluation metrics for speech modeling. It stands for Precision, Recall, F-measure, AUC and Accuracy.
