
### Relations between Senses for the notes of the Unit 4 - SEMANTICS AND PRAGMATICS in the subject of Natural Language Processing

Semantics and pragmatics are two related fields of study in natural language processing (NLP). Semantics is the study of the meaning of words, phrases, and sentences, while pragmatics is the study of how language is used in context. The two fields are closely related, as the meaning of a sentence can change depending on the context in which it is used.

The relationship between semantics and pragmatics is complex and multi-faceted. Semantics is concerned with the literal meaning of words and phrases, while pragmatics is concerned with the interpretation of language in context. For example, the sentence "He is tall" has a literal meaning, but its interpretation can change depending on the context. In a conversation about basketball players, the sentence might refer to a person's physical height, whereas in a conversation about trees it might refer to the height of the tree.

In addition, the relationship between semantics and pragmatics can be seen in the way language is used to convey meaning. For example, a speaker may use metaphor or irony to convey a message that is different from the literal meaning of the words they use. This type of figurative language is often used to convey subtle nuances of meaning that cannot be expressed with literal language.

Finally, the relationship between semantics and pragmatics can also be seen in the way language is used to convey emotions. By using certain words, phrases, or tones of voice, a speaker can convey emotions such as anger, joy, or sadness. This type of communication is often referred to as "emotional language" and can be used to convey a speaker's feelings or intentions in a way that literal language cannot.

Overall, the relationship between semantics and pragmatics is complex and multi-faceted. Semantics is concerned with the literal meaning of words, while pragmatics is concerned with the interpretation of language in context. By understanding the relationship between the two fields, language users can better understand how language is used to convey meaning and emotion.
