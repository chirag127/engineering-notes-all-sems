

### Dependency Grammar for the notes of the Unit 3 - SYNTACTIC ANALYSIS in the subject of Natural Language Processing

* Dependency grammar is a type of syntactic analysis which examines the relationships between a set of words in a sentence. It is based on the idea that each word in the sentence is related to the other words in the sentence in a specific way.

* Dependency grammar looks at the relationships between the words in a sentence by examining the syntactic roles that each word plays. The syntactic roles are known as **dependency relations**. These relations are used to determine the meaning of the sentence.

* Dependency grammar is different from traditional grammar in that it does not rely on a set of rules. Instead, it uses a set of principles to determine the meaning of a sentence.

* Dependency grammar is a powerful tool for natural language processing and can be used to create text parsers, to generate natural language understanding and to create language models.

* A simple example of a dependency relation is the subject-verb relation. In this relation, the subject is the word that is performing the action and the verb is the action that is being performed.
