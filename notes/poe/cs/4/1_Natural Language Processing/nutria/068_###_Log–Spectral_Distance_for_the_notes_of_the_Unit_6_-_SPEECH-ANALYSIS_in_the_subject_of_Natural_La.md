
### Log–Spectral Distance for the notes of the Unit 6 - SPEECH-ANALYSIS in the subject of Natural Language Processing

Log–Spectral Distance (LSD) is a measure of similarity between two speech signals. It is based on the logarithmic power spectrum of the signals, and is used in speech recognition and speech synthesis.

LSD is calculated by taking the logarithmic power spectrum of two speech signals and subtracting them. The result is a measure of the difference between the two signals.

Advantages of LSD:

- It is easy to calculate and interpret.
- It is robust to noise and other distortions.
- It can be used to compare the similarity of two signals with different lengths.

Disadvantages of LSD:

- It is not suitable for comparing signals with different speaking styles.
- It is not suitable for comparing signals with different speaking rates.
