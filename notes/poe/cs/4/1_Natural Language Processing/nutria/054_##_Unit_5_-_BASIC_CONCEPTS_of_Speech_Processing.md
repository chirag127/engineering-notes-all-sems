
## Unit 5 - BASIC CONCEPTS of Speech Processing

1. **Speech Production:** The process of producing audible speech involves the movement of air from the lungs, through the vocal tract, and out of the mouth. The vocal tract is made up of the larynx, pharynx, and mouth. The larynx contains the vocal cords, which vibrate to produce sound. The pharynx is a tube-like structure that helps to shape the sound, and the mouth is where the sound is articulated. 

2. **Speech Perception:** Speech perception is the process of interpreting the sound waves that are produced by the speaker and converting them into meaningful linguistic units. This process involves the recognition of individual phonemes, syllables, and words.

3. **Speech Recognition:** Speech recognition is the process of using algorithms to identify spoken words. This can be done using a variety of techniques, such as Hidden Markov Models, Artificial Neural Networks, or Support Vector Machines.

4. **Speech Synthesis:** Speech synthesis is the process of creating speech from text. This is done by using text-to-speech algorithms, which convert text into speech.

5. **Speech Coding:** Speech coding is the process of compressing speech signals so that they can be transmitted over a limited bandwidth. This is done by using algorithms such as Linear Predictive Coding, Vector Quantization, and Adaptive Differential Pulse Code Modulation.

6. **Mnemonics and Learning Tricks:** Mnemonics are memory aids that can be used to help remember concepts and facts. For example, the acronym "LIPPS" can be used to remember the five components of speech production: larynx, ipsilateral, pharynx, palate, and sound.
