
### Requirements for Representation for the Notes of the Unit 4 - Semantics and Pragmatics in the Subject of Natural Language Processing

* Semantic representation is the process of representing the meaning of words, phrases, and sentences in a language. It is an important part of natural language processing (NLP).
* Pragmatics is the study of how language is used in context. It involves understanding the context of a conversation, the speaker’s intentions, and the social conventions that govern the use of language.
* Representation of natural language can be done using a variety of methods, such as semantic networks, frames, and ontologies.
* Semantic networks are graphical representations of the relationships between words, phrases, and concepts. They are often used to represent the meaning of words and phrases in a language.
* Frames are a type of semantic representation that consists of a set of slots and values. Each slot is associated with a particular concept, and the values associated with each slot represent the relationships between the concepts.
* Ontologies are formal representations of knowledge that can be used to represent the meaning of words and phrases in a language. They are often used in NLP to represent the relationships between words, phrases, and concepts.
* Mnemonics and learning tricks can be used to help remember the meaning of words and phrases in a language. Mnemonics involve creating associations between words and concepts, while learning tricks involve using memory aids such as repetition and visualization.
