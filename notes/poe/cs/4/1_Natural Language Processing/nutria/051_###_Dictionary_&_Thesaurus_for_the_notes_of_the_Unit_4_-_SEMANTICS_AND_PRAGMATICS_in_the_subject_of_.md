
### Dictionary & Thesaurus for the notes of the Unit 4 - SEMANTICS AND PRAGMATICS in the subject of Natural Language Processing

* A dictionary is a collection of words and their definitions, often organized alphabetically. A thesaurus is a collection of related words and their synonyms, often organized by category. 

* Semantics is the study of the meanings of words and phrases in a language. Pragmatics is the study of how context affects the meaning of words and phrases. 

* A dictionary can be used to look up the meaning of a word or phrase, while a thesaurus can be used to find synonyms for a word or phrase. 

* Dictionaries and thesauri can be used to improve understanding of language, as well as to improve writing and speaking skills. They can also be used to help with decision-making and problem-solving. 

* Mnemonics can be used to help remember the meanings of words and phrases. For example, the acronym “FANBOYS” can be used to remember the coordinating conjunctions: For, And, Nor, But, Or, Yet, So. 

* It is important to note that dictionaries and thesauri are not always 100% accurate. It is important to double-check the meanings of words and phrases before using them.
