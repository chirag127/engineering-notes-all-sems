

### Grammar Rules for English for the Notes of Unit 3 - Syntactic Analysis in the Subject of Natural Language Processing

1. **Parts of Speech**: English grammar is composed of 8 parts of speech, which are nouns, pronouns, verbs, adjectives, adverbs, prepositions, conjunctions, and interjections.
2. **Sentence Structure**: English sentences follow a subject-verb-object structure. The subject is the person or thing performing the action, the verb is the action being performed, and the object is the person or thing being acted upon.
3. **Verb Tense**: Verbs can be in the present, past, or future tense. The present tense is used for actions that are currently happening, the past tense is used for actions that have already happened, and the future tense is used for actions that will happen in the future.
4. **Clauses**: A clause is a group of words that contains both a subject and a verb. Clauses can be independent clauses, which can stand alone as complete sentences, or dependent clauses, which cannot stand alone as complete sentences.
5. **Phrases**: A phrase is a group of words that does not contain both a subject and a verb. Phrases can be noun phrases, verb phrases, adjectival phrases, adverbial phrases, or prepositional phrases.
6. **Morphology**: Morphology is the study of the structure of words. English words are composed of morphemes, which are the smallest units of meaning in a language.
7. **Syntax**: Syntax is the study of the structure of sentences. English sentences are composed of phrases and clauses, which are arranged in a specific order to form a complete sentence.
8. **Mnemonics and Learning Tricks**: Mnemonics are memory aids that can be used to help remember grammar rules. Examples include using acronyms, rhymes, and visual imagery.
