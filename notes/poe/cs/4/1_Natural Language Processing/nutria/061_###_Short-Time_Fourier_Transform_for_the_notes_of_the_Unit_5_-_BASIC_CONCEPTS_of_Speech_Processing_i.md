
### Short-Time Fourier Transform for the notes of the Unit 5 - BASIC CONCEPTS of Speech Processing in the subject of Natural Language Processing 

The Short-Time Fourier Transform (STFT) is a powerful tool used in the field of Natural Language Processing (NLP) to analyze and process speech signals. It is an important concept in speech processing and is used to analyze the frequency content of a signal over a short period of time. 

The STFT is a type of Fourier Transform that works by breaking up a signal into a series of overlapping windows and then calculating the Fourier Transform of each window. This allows the frequency content of the signal to be analyzed over short time intervals, allowing for more accurate analysis of the signal. 

The STFT has several advantages over other methods of analyzing a signal. It is fast and efficient, as it only requires a single pass through the signal. It is also very accurate, as it can detect very small changes in the signal over short time intervals. Finally, the STFT is highly flexible, as it can be used to analyze a wide range of signals.
