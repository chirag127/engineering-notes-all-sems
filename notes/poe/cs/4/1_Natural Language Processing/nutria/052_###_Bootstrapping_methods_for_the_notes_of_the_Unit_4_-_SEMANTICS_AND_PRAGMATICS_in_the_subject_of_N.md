
### Bootstrapping methods for the notes of Unit 4 - SEMANTICS AND PRAGMATICS in the subject of Natural Language Processing

Bootstrapping is a method of natural language processing (NLP) that uses existing knowledge to learn more. It is a method of unsupervised learning where a computer learns from existing data and develops its own rules for processing natural language. It is a way of teaching a computer to understand the meaning of words and phrases by using existing knowledge and data.

Bootstrapping methods can be used for a variety of tasks, including:

* Understanding the context of a sentence
* Extracting information from a text
* Generating natural language responses
* Automatically translating between languages

Mnemonics and learning tricks for Bootstrapping methods:

* **B**uild **O**n **O**ld **T**echniques: Bootstrapping methods build on existing techniques and knowledge to create new models.
* **S**tart **T**hinking **R**easonably: Bootstrapping methods use a reasonable approach to building models, rather than relying on intuition.
* **A**sk **P**roper **P**roblems: Bootstrapping methods ask the right questions to create models that are accurate and reliable.
* **S**tudy **T**he **R**esults: Bootstrapping methods study the results of the models to make sure they are accurate and reliable.
* **A**nalyze **P**erformance: Bootstrapping methods analyze the performance of the models to ensure they are accurate and reliable.
* **P**repare **I**nformation: Bootstrapping methods prepare the data and information needed to create models.
* **I**dentify **N**eeds: Bootstrapping methods identify the needs of the task at hand and create models accordingly.
