
### Syntactic Parsing for the notes of the Unit 3 - SYNTACTIC ANALYSIS in the subject of Natural Language Processing

Syntactic Parsing is the process of analyzing a text or sentence to determine its grammatical structure. It is a key component of Natural Language Processing (NLP) systems, as it is used to identify the relationships between words within a sentence. Syntactic Parsing can be used to determine the meaning of a sentence, or to generate new sentences with similar meaning.

Syntactic Parsing involves breaking down a sentence into its component parts, such as nouns, verbs, adjectives, and adverbs. It then identifies the relationships between these parts, such as the subject-verb-object relationship. Once the relationships between the words are identified, the parser can then generate a parse tree, which is a graphical representation of the sentence’s structure.

Syntactic Parsing can be used to identify the parts of speech in a sentence, as well as the relationships between them. It can also be used to identify the structure of a sentence, such as whether it is a simple sentence or a complex sentence. Additionally, Syntactic Parsing can be used to identify the types of phrases and clauses in a sentence, as well as the relationships between them.

Mnemonic for Syntactic Parsing:

SVO = Subject-Verb-Object

This mnemonic can be used to remember the basic structure of a sentence, which is Subject-Verb-Object. Knowing this structure can help you identify the parts of speech in a sentence, as well as the relationships between them.
