
## Unit 2 - WORD LEVEL ANALYSIS

Word Level Analysis is the process of analyzing the structure of a given language at the level of individual words. It involves the study of the components that make up a word and how they interact with each other. 

Mnemonics and Learning Tricks: 
1. **ROOMS** - This mnemonic can be used to remember the components of a word: R for Roots, O for Onsets, O for Offsets, M for Morphemes, and S for Suffixes. 
2. **ABC** - This mnemonic can be used to remember the levels of analysis: A for Acoustic, B for Behavioral, and C for Cognitive. 

Word Level Analysis involves the study of the following components: 

1. **Roots** - These are the core of a word, and they provide the basic meaning of the word. 
2. **Onsets** - These are the beginning of a word, and they provide a hint at the meaning of the word. 
3. **Offsets** - These are the end of a word, and they provide a hint at the meaning of the word. 
4. **Morphemes** - These are the smallest units of meaning within a word, and they provide a hint at the meaning of the word. 
5. **Suffixes** - These are the endings of a word, and they provide a hint at the meaning of the word. 

Word Level Analysis also involves the study of the following levels of analysis: 

1. **Acoustic Analysis** - This involves the study of how a word is pronounced. This includes the study of the phonemes, syllables, and stress patterns of a word. 
2. **Behavioral Analysis** - This involves the study of how a word is used in context. This includes the study of the syntax, semantics, and pragmatics of a word. 
3. **Cognitive Analysis** - This involves the study of how a word is processed in the brain. This includes the study of the mental representation of a word, and how it is stored and retrieved in the brain. 

Word Level Analysis is an important part of language study, as it helps us to better understand the structure of a language and how it is used. It also helps us to better understand how words are processed in the brain.
