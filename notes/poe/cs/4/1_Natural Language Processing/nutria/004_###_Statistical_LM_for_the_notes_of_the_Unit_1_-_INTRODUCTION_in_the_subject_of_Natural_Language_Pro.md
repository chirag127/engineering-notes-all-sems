
### Statistical LM for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of Natural Language Processing

Statistical Language Modeling (SLM) is a branch of Natural Language Processing (NLP) which deals with the probability of occurrence of a sequence of words in a sentence. The goal of SLM is to estimate the probability of a sentence or a sequence of words being generated by a language. SLM can be used to identify the probability of a sentence being grammatically correct or to identify the probability of a certain word appearing in a sentence.

SLM uses various methods such as Markov models, neural networks, and Bayesian networks to compute the probability of a sentence or a sequence of words. SLM can also be used to generate new sentences or words based on the probability of occurrence of a sequence of words.
