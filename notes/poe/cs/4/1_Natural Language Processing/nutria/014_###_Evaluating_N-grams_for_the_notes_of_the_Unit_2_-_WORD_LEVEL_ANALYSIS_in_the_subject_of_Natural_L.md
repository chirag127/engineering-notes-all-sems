
### Evaluating N-grams for the notes of the Unit 2 - WORD LEVEL ANALYSIS in the subject of Natural Language Processing

N-grams are a type of statistical language model used in Natural Language Processing (NLP) to predict the next word or phrase in a sentence. N-grams are a set of words or phrases that follow each other in a sequence. They are used to analyze text and identify patterns in language.

N-grams can be evaluated in a variety of ways. The most common method is to calculate the probability of the n-gram appearing in a given text. This is done by counting the number of times the n-gram appears in the text and dividing it by the total number of words in the text. The higher the probability, the more likely it is that the n-gram appears in the text.

Another way to evaluate n-grams is to calculate the frequency of the n-gram in the text. This is done by counting the number of times the n-gram appears in the text and dividing it by the total number of words in the text. The higher the frequency, the more likely it is that the n-gram appears in the text.

N-grams can also be evaluated using metrics such as precision, recall, and F-measure. Precision is the percentage of correct predictions made by the model. Recall is the percentage of correct predictions made by the model out of all possible predictions. F-measure is a combination of precision and recall, and is a measure of the accuracy of the model.

Finally, n-grams can be evaluated using measures of information such as entropy and perplexity. Entropy is a measure of the randomness of the text, while perplexity is a measure of how well the model predicts the next word in the text.

Mnemonics and learning tricks can be helpful when learning about n-grams. For example, the acronym "NLP" can be used to remember the term "Natural Language Processing". Additionally, visual diagrams can be used to understand how n-grams work.
