
### English Morphology for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of Natural Language Processing 

English Morphology is a branch of linguistics that studies the structure of words and their components. It is concerned with the way words are formed from smaller units of meaning and how these units interact to give words their meaning. It also looks at the way words change their form and meaning in different contexts.

Morphology is an important part of Natural Language Processing (NLP). NLP is the study of how computers can understand and process natural language. NLP systems use morphology to identify the components of words and to understand the meaning of words in different contexts.

Mnemonics and Learning Tricks:

* Morphology is the study of word structure and meaning.
* Morphology is used in NLP to identify the components of words and to understand the meaning of words in different contexts.
* Morphemes are the smallest units of meaning in a language.
* Inflectional morphemes are used to change the form of a word without changing its meaning.
* Derivational morphemes are used to create new words from existing words.
* Compounding is the process of combining two or more words to form a new word.
* Clitics are words that are attached to other words to form a single unit.
* Free morphemes are words that can stand alone, while bound morphemes must be attached to other words.
