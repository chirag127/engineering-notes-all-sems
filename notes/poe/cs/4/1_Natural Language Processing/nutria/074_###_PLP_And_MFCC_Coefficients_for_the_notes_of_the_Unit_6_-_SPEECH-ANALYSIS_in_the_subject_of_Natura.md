- **Perceptual Linear Prediction (PLP)** is a method used to analyse speech signals. It is based on the idea that the human auditory system is sensitive to the temporal and spectral characteristics of a sound. PLP is used to extract the spectral and temporal information from a speech signal and represent it in a compact form.

- **Mel Frequency Cepstral Coefficients (MFCCs)** are a set of coefficients that represent the spectral characteristics of a speech signal. They are derived from the Mel Scale, which is a logarithmic scale used to represent the frequencies of a sound. MFCCs are used to represent the spectral features of a speech signal in a compact form.
