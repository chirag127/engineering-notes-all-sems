
### Word Classes for the notes of the Unit 2 - WORD LEVEL ANALYSIS in the subject of Natural Language Processing

Word classes, also known as parts of speech, are categories of words that have similar syntactic properties. Word classes include nouns, verbs, adjectives, adverbs, pronouns, prepositions, conjunctions, interjections, and determiners.

* **Nouns**: Nouns are words that refer to people, places, things, or ideas. They can be used as the subject or object of a sentence. Nouns can be either proper (e.g. John, London) or common (e.g. boy, city).

* **Verbs**: Verbs are words that describe an action, state, or occurrence. They can be used as the main verb in a sentence. Verbs can be either transitive (e.g. eat, buy) or intransitive (e.g. run, sleep).

* **Adjectives**: Adjectives are words that describe or modify nouns or pronouns. They can be used to describe the quality, size, shape, color, or other characteristics of a noun or pronoun.

* **Adverbs**: Adverbs are words that modify or describe verbs, adjectives, or other adverbs. They can be used to describe the manner, time, place, degree, or other aspects of an action, adjective, or adverb.

* **Pronouns**: Pronouns are words that substitute for nouns or noun phrases. They can be used to refer to people, places, things, or ideas.

* **Prepositions**: Prepositions are words that link nouns, pronouns, and phrases to other words in a sentence. They can be used to indicate the location, time, direction, or other relationships between the nouns and other words in the sentence.

* **Conjunctions**: Conjunctions are words that connect words, phrases, or clauses. They can be used to join two independent clauses, or to join two or more items in a list.

* **Interjections**: Interjections are words that are used to express strong emotion or surprise. They are usually followed by an exclamation point.

* **Determiners**: Determiners are words that indicate the reference of a noun or pronoun. They can be used to indicate the number, gender, or other characteristics of a noun or pronoun.
