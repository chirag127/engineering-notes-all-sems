
### Viterbi Search for the notes of the Unit 7 - SPEECH MODELING in the subject of Natural Language Processing

The Viterbi search is an algorithm used in natural language processing (NLP) to find the most likely sequence of words in a given sentence. It is based on the dynamic programming approach and uses the Markov assumption, which states that the probability of a word being the next word in a sentence depends only on the previous word.

The Viterbi search is used to find the most probable sequence of words in a sentence, given a set of possible words and their probabilities. This algorithm is used in NLP for tasks such as part-of-speech tagging, named entity recognition, and machine translation.

The Viterbi search algorithm works by calculating the probability of each possible sequence of words in a sentence. It then chooses the sequence with the highest probability.

The algorithm works by first calculating the probability of the first word in the sentence. It then calculates the probability of each possible sequence of two words, and so on until the end of the sentence. The probability of a sequence is calculated by multiplying the probabilities of each word in the sequence.

The Viterbi search algorithm is an efficient way of finding the most likely sequence of words in a sentence. It is used in many NLP tasks, such as part-of-speech tagging, named entity recognition, and machine translation. It can be used to improve the accuracy of these tasks.
