

### Implementation Issues for the Notes of the Unit 7 - SPEECH MODELING in the Subject of Natural Language Processing

1. **Data Collection:** Speech modeling requires a large amount of data to be collected and labeled in order to train the model accurately. This can be a time-consuming task.

2. **Feature Extraction:** Feature extraction is the process of extracting meaningful features from the raw speech data. This is an important step in speech modeling as it helps to reduce the dimensionality of the data and make it easier to process.

3. **Model Selection:** There are many different types of models available for speech modeling. It is important to select the model that is best suited for the task at hand.

4. **Hyperparameter Tuning:** Hyperparameter tuning is an important step in speech modeling. It involves adjusting the model's parameters to optimize the performance of the model.

5. **Mnemonics and Learning Tricks:** Mnemonics and learning tricks can be used to help remember the important concepts related to speech modeling. For example, the acronym “GMM” can be used to remember the three components of a Gaussian Mixture Model (Gaussian Distribution, Mixture Model, and Maximum Likelihood Estimation).
