
### Feature Extraction And Pattern Comparison Techniques for the notes of the Unit 6 - SPEECH-ANALYSIS in the subject of Natural Language Processing

* Feature Extraction: It is the process of extracting meaningful features from the speech signal. It is used to represent the signal in a form which is easier to process and analyze. Different types of features can be extracted such as Mel-frequency cepstral coefficients (MFCCs), Linear Predictive Coding (LPC) coefficients, cepstral coefficients, etc. 

* Pattern Comparison: It is the process of comparing two speech signals to determine their similarity. It is used to identify the speaker or detect the spoken words. Different techniques can be used for pattern comparison such as Dynamic Time Warping (DTW), Hidden Markov Models (HMMs), Support Vector Machines (SVMs), etc. 

* Mnemonics: Mnemonics are memory aids which can help to remember the concepts related to feature extraction and pattern comparison techniques. For example, "MFCC" can be remembered as "Mel-Frequency Cepstral Coefficients". 

* Learning Tricks: Learning tricks are techniques which can help to understand and remember the concepts related to feature extraction and pattern comparison techniques. For example, drawing diagrams, writing summaries, making flashcards, etc. can be helpful to understand and remember the concepts.
