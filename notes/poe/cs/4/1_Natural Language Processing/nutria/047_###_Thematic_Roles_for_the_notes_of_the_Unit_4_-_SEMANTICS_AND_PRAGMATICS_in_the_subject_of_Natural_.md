
### Thematic Roles for the notes of the Unit 4 - SEMANTICS AND PRAGMATICS in the subject of Natural Language Processing

* Thematic roles are the core of semantic and pragmatic analysis. They are the basis for understanding the meaning of words in a sentence. 
* Thematic roles are used to represent the roles of the participants in a sentence. 
* They are also known as semantic roles, semantic functions, or semantic cases. 
* Thematic roles are divided into two main categories: agent and patient roles. 
* The agent role is assigned to the participant who is responsible for the action expressed in the sentence. 
* The patient role is assigned to the participant who is affected by the action expressed in the sentence. 
* Thematic roles can also be divided into two other categories: actor and experiencer roles. 
* The actor role is assigned to the participant who is performing the action expressed in the sentence. 
* The experiencer role is assigned to the participant who is receiving the action expressed in the sentence. 
* Thematic roles can also be divided into two other categories: source and goal roles. 
* The source role is assigned to the participant who is initiating the action expressed in the sentence. 
* The goal role is assigned to the participant who is receiving the action expressed in the sentence. 
* Mnemonics and learning tricks for the thematic roles can be helpful for better understanding and remembering the roles. For example, the mnemonic "AAPG" can be used to remember the four main thematic roles: Agent, Actor, Patient, and Goal.
