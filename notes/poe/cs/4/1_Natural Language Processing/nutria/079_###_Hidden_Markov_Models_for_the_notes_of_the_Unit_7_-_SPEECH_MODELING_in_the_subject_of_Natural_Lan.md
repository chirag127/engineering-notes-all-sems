- Hidden Markov Models (HMMs) are probabilistic models used to analyze and predict the probability of certain sequences of observations. 
- HMMs are used in many applications such as speech recognition, handwriting recognition, and biological sequence analysis.
- HMMs are composed of two main components: the observation sequence and the state sequence. The observation sequence is a sequence of observations that are assumed to be generated by a Markov process. The state sequence is a sequence of states, each of which has a certain probability of transitioning to the next state.
- HMMs can be used to model a variety of processes, such as speech, handwriting, and biological sequences.
