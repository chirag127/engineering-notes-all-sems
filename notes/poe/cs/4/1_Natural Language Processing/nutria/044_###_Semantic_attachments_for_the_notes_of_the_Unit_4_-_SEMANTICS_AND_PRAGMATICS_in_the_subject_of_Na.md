
### Semantic attachments for the notes of the Unit 4 - SEMANTICS AND PRAGMATICS in the subject of Natural Language Processing

Semantics and pragmatics are two interrelated aspects of natural language processing. Semantics involves understanding the meaning of words and phrases, while pragmatics involves understanding the context in which language is used.

#### Semantics

Semantics is the study of meaning in language. It is concerned with understanding how words and phrases are used to convey meaning. Semantics looks at how words are used in sentences and how they are related to each other. It also looks at how words are used in different contexts and how they are combined to form meaningful sentences.

#### Pragmatics

Pragmatics is the study of how context affects the interpretation of language. It looks at how language is used in a particular context and how this affects the meaning of words and phrases. Pragmatics looks at how the meaning of a sentence is affected by the context in which it is used. It also looks at how people use language to convey their intentions and how they interpret what others say.
