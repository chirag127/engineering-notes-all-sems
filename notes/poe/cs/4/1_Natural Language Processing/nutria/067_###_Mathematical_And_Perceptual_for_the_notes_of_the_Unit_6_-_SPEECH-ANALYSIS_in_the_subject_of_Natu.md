
### Mathematical And Perceptual for the notes of the Unit 6 - SPEECH-ANALYSIS in the subject of Natural Language Processing

1. Speech Analysis is the process of examining spoken language to understand its structure and meaning. It is a branch of Natural Language Processing (NLP) that focuses on the study of how people communicate with each other through spoken language.

2. Speech analysis involves the use of mathematical and perceptual techniques to identify and analyze the components of speech. These techniques include the use of signal processing techniques, such as Fourier analysis, and the use of statistical models, such as Hidden Markov Models (HMMs).

3. Speech analysis is used in many applications, such as speech recognition, speech synthesis, automatic speech recognition (ASR), natural language understanding (NLU), and natural language processing (NLP).

4. Speech analysis is also used in the development of intelligent agents, such as virtual assistants, which are able to understand and respond to spoken language.

5. Mnemonics and learning tricks for Speech Analysis include:
- "Fourier Analysis" - a mathematical technique used to analyze the frequency components of a signal.
- "HMM" - Hidden Markov Model, a statistical model used to identify patterns in a sequence of data.
- "ASR" - Automatic Speech Recognition, a technology used to recognize spoken language.
- "NLU" - Natural Language Understanding, a technology used to interpret and understand spoken language.
- "NLP" - Natural Language Processing, a technology used to process and analyze natural language.
