### Dictionary & Thesaurus for the notes of the Unit 4 - SEMANTICS AND PRAGMATICS in the subject of Natural Language Processing

- Dictionary: A dictionary is a listing of words in a language with their meanings, pronunciations, spellings, word origins, and usage examples. In NLP, dictionaries are used to tokenize the input text into words and lookup their meanings. Some examples of dictionaries used in NLP are:
- General English Dictionary: Contains common English words and their meanings. Used for basic tokenization and word sense disambiguation.
- Subject-specific Dictionary: Contains technical terms and jargons of a specific subject. Used when performing NLP on domain-specific text.
- Thesaurus: A thesaurus contains words grouped by meaning, similar to a dictionary. But instead of definitions, a thesaurus lists synonyms and related words. Thesauri are used in NLP for tasks like finding synonyms, measuring semantic similarity between words, expanding queries, etc.

Mnemonics/Learning Tricks:
- Dic. Defs: Dictionary has Definitions
- Thes. Synonyms: Thesaurus has Synonyms

Advantages:
- Provide meaning and usage of words which helps in tokenization and disambiguation.
- Help in expanding queries and finding related words.
- Aid in measuring semantic similarity.

Disadvantages:
- May not contain newly coined words and phrases.
- Subject-specific dictionaries have limited coverage.
- Thesauri group words based on human perception of similarity which may not always be accurate.

Examples: WordNet (lexical database of English), Medical Subject Headings (biomedical terms)

Applications: Query expansion, semantic similarity, speech recognition, machine translation, etc.
