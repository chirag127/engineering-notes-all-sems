### Speech Fundamentals for the notes of the Unit 5 - BASIC CONCEPTS of Speech Processing in the subject of Natural Language Processing

- Speech is produced by generating sound waves that are created by vibrations of vocal cords in the larynx. The rate of vibrations produce different pitch sounds. The shape of vocal tract modifies the sound produced by vocal cords to produce different speech sounds.
- The vocal tract acts as an acoustic filter that shapes the sound generated by the vocal cords into recognizable speech sounds based on the positions of lips, tongue, jaw, etc.
- There are consonant and vowel sounds in speech. Consonants are produced by obstructing or constricting the vocal tract in some way. Vowels are produced by shaping the vocal tract into certain positions.
- The study of how speech sounds are produced, modified and perceived is called Phonetics. The study of speech sounds of a language and their patterns of combination is called Phonology.
- Few important characteristics of speech are:
	- It is produced dynamically with time-varying signals.
	- It varies from person to person and based on emotions, speaking style, health conditions, etc.
	- It contains redundant and ambiguous information.
	- It has a hierarchical structure comprising of phonemes, syllables, words, phrases, sentences, etc.
- Some key advantages of speech are:
	- It is easy and convenient to use.
	- It is the most natural way of communicating.
	- It provides additional cues such as tone, emphasis, etc.
- Some key disadvantages of speech are:
	- It is transient and ephemeral.
	- It can be affected by noise and distortions.
	- Interpretation can be subjective and ambiguous.
	- Automatic speech processing is challenging due to variations and imperfections.

- Few applications of speech processing are: Speech recognition, Speaker recognition, Speech synthesis, Speech coding, Speech enhancement, Speech segmentation, etc.
