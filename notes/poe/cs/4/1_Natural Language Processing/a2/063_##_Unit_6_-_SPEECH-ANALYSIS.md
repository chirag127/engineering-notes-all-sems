## Unit 6 - SPEECH-ANALYSIS

**Speech Analysis** refers to the process of analyzing speech signals with the goal of identifying and extracting relevant information. It has many applications like automatic speech recognition, speaker identification, emotion recognition, etc. Some key steps involved in speech analysis are:

1. **Preprocessing:** Raw speech signals are preprocessed to remove noise, normalize amplitude, etc. Techniques like filtering, scaling, etc are used.

2. **Segmentation:** The speech signal is divided into meaningful units like phones, syllables, words, phrases, sentences, etc. This can be done using anchors like silence periods or acoustic cues.

3. **Feature Extraction:** Relevant features are extracted from the speech signal to represent its characteristics. Common features include MFCCs, PLP, etc which encode timbral and spectral properties of speech.

4. **Training a Model:** The extracted features are used to train a machine learning model to map speech signals to their transcriptions or other attributes like speaker identity or emotion. Different models like HMMs, Neural Networks, etc are used based on the task.

5. **Evaluation:** The performance of the speech analysis system is evaluated using metrics like Word Error Rate, Accuracy, F-score, etc. on test data and refined by tuning hyperparameters or changing the models/features.

Some tips for learning Speech Analysis:

- Understand the basic steps involved and their purpose. Knowing the overall flow will help understand the individual parts.
- Get familiar with common preprocessing, segmentation and feature extraction techniques. Practice implementing them on sample data to understand how they work.
- Learn about the different machine learning models used for speech analysis and their pros and cons. Focus on understanding the intuition behind them rather than mathematical details.
- Work through practical examples and datasets to apply your knowledge. This will help reinforce the concepts and expose you to real-world challenges.
