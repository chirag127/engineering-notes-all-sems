### Evaluation for the notes of the Unit 7 - SPEECH MODELING in the subject of Natural Language Processing

- Speech modeling involves analyzing the speech signal and extracting meaningful information from it. The evaluation of speech modeling techniques is done on the following criteria:
- Accuracy: The speech modeling technique should accurately extract the linguistic information from the speech signal. The words, phrases, syllables, etc. recognized should be correct. The speech features extracted should represent the speech signal properly. Higher accuracy in speech recognition and feature extraction is desirable.
- Robustness: The speech modeling technique should be robust to variations in speakers, speaking styles, background noises, etc. It should be able to handle noisy speech and variations between speakers to recognize the speech accurately. Higher robustness is required for speech modeling techniques.
- Real-time processing: For applications like speech recognition systems, the speech modeling technique should be able to process the speech signal in real-time and provide output with minimal latency. Faster processing speed and real-time output are necessary.
- Less resource usage: The speech modeling technique should be computationally efficient and require lesser memory for implementation. It should not be too complex and consume a lot of resources. Lower resource usage is preferred.
- Mnemonics:
- A ccurate - Extract information correctly
- R obust - Handle variations and noises
- R eal-time - Fast processing
- L ess resources - Efficient implementation

The evaluation of speech modeling techniques considers a combination of these factors to determine the best technique for an application. The requirements of accuracy, robustness, speed, and resources vary based on the application. Trade-offs are made between the factors while selecting a speech modeling approach.
