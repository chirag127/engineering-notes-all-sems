### Issues in PoS tagging for the notes of the Unit 2 - WORD LEVEL ANALYSIS in the subject of Natural Language Processing

Following are the major issues in Part-of-Speech (PoS) tagging:

1. Ambiguity: Words can have multiple parts of speech tags depending on the context. For example, "book" can be a noun or a verb. The PoS tagger has to determine the correct tag based on the context. This makes the task challenging.
2. Rare words: The tagger may come across words that are not in its dictionary or training data. This can lead to errors in tagging unknown or rare words.
3. Complex words: Words can be complex and composed of multiple parts like prefixes, suffixes, etc. For example, " unbelievable" has a prefix "un-" and suffix "-able". The tagger has to analyze these components and tag the word correctly which can be difficult.
4. Informal language: The input text can contain informal language, slang, abbreviations, etc. which may not be in the training data. This can hamper the performance of the tagger leading to errors.

Some tips to remember the issues:

* Ambiguity: Think of a "book" which can be a noun or a verb. Context is important.
* Rare words: Unknown or rare words can lead to errors as they may be missing from the dictionary or training data.
* Complex words: Break down complex words into prefixes, roots and suffixes to understand the issues in tagging them.
* Informal language: Think of informal abbreviations and slang which may not be in the training data and can cause errors.

Memorization tricks:

* For ambiguity: "Book" can be a noun or verb (ambiguous) depending on context.
* For rare words: Rare words are "rarely" seen so errors.
* For complex words: Complex words have "complex" components (prefixes, roots, suffixes) leading to issues.
* For informal language: Informal language is "not formal" so may be missing from training data leading to errors.
