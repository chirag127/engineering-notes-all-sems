### Acoustic Phonetics for the notes of the Unit 5 - BASIC CONCEPTS of Speech Processing in the subject of Natural Language Processing

- Acoustic phonetics is the study of speech sounds based on acoustic characteristics. It deals with the physical properties of speech sounds or phones.
- The basic units of acoustic phonetics are:
-- Vowels: Produced with an open vocal tract - /a/, /e/, /i/, /o/, /u/
-- Consonants: Produced with a constricted vocal tract - /p/, /t/, /k/, /b/, /d/, /g/ etc.
-- Suprasegmentals: Aspects of speech beyond individual sounds like stress, pitch, juncture etc.

Some key concepts in acoustic phonetics:

- Fundamental frequency (F0): Dominant frequency of vocal fold vibration which determines pitch.
- Formants: Concentrations of acoustic energy around particular frequencies in the vocal tract. Formants are coded for vowel identity.
- Voice onset time (VOT): Time interval between the release of a stop consonant and the start of vocal fold vibration. VOT is coded for stop consonant voicing.
- Spectrogram: Visual representation of speech sounds where the spectrum is plotted against time. It is used to study different acoustic characteristics of speech.

Mnemonics:
- 'A, E, I, O, U and sometimes Y and W' - Vowels
- 'If two vowels go walking, the first one does the talking' - Diphthongs (combination of two vowels in which the sound begins with one vowel and moves towards another)

Learning tricks:
- Listen to speech sounds and try to imitate them to get a feel of how to produce them
- Observe the shape of your mouth while producing different sounds to understand how the vocal tract is constricted/open for different phones
- Analyze spectrograms of speech sounds to understand their acoustic characteristics

Advantages: Gives an insight into the physical characteristics of speech which is necessary to understand speech perception and synthesis.
Disadvantages: Requires technical knowledge of acoustic analysis which can be difficult to understand for beginners.
Applications: Automatic speech recognition, speech synthesis, accent training, speech pathology etc.
