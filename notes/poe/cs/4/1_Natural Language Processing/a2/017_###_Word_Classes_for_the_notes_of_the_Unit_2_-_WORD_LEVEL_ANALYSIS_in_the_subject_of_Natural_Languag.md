### Word Classes for the notes of the Unit 2 - WORD LEVEL ANALYSIS in the subject of Natural Language Processing

The major word classes in English are:

- Nouns: Refer to persons, places, things, ideas, or concepts e.g. John, India, book, courage.
- Verbs: Refer to actions or states of being e.g. run, is, can.
- Adjectives: Modify or describe nouns e.g. big, old, green.
- Adverbs: Modify verbs, adjectives, or other adverbs e.g. quickly, extremely, very.
- Pronouns: Refer to persons or things e.g. I, you, he, she, it, we, you, they.
- Prepositions: Relate nouns or pronouns to other words in a sentence e.g. in, on, under, after, before.
- Conjunctions: Join words, phrases, or clauses e.g. and, but, or, so, because.
- Interjections: Express emotion e.g. Oh!, Alas!, Hurray!.

**Mnemonics:**

- Nouns name people, places, things: Person, Place, Thing.
- Verbs action words, something *active*: Action.
- Adjectives add *description*: Describe.
- Adverbs add more *description*: More Description.
- Pronouns replace nouns: Replace Nouns.
- Prepositions places relationships: Place Relationship.
- Conjunctions conjoin: Conjoin.
- Interjections show emotion: Emotion!

**Advantages of identifying word classes:**

- It helps in understanding the structure and organization of language.
- It is useful for syntactic and semantic analysis of sentences.
- It is a prerequisite for many NLP tasks like parsing, tagging, summarization, etc.
- It aids in machine translation and speech recognition systems.

**Disadvantages:**

- Classification of words into classes is not always unambiguous.
- The classes are not mutually exclusive i.e. a word can belong to more than one class e.g. 'fast' can be an adjective as well as an adverb.
- It requires extensive linguistic knowledge to identify word classes which is difficult to encode into machines.
