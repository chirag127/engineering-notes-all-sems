### Introduction to Group, Field, Finite Field of the Form GF(p), Modular Arithmetic, Prime and Relative Prime Numbers, Extended Euclidean Algorithm, Advanced Encryption Standard (AES) Encryption and Decryption, Fermat’s and Euler’s Theorem, Primarily Testing, Chinese Remainder Theorem, Discrete Logarithmic Problem, Principals of Public Key Crypto Systems, RSA Algorithm, Security of RSA

Group: A group is a set of elements with a binary operation that satisfies four properties - closure, associativity, identity, and inverse.

Field: A field is a set of elements with two binary operations - addition and multiplication, that satisfy certain properties such as associativity, commutativity, distributivity, and existence of additive and multiplicative inverses.

Finite Field of the Form GF(p): A finite field with p elements, where p is a prime number. It is denoted as GF(p) and has addition and multiplication operations defined over it.

Modular Arithmetic: Modular arithmetic is a system of arithmetic for integers where numbers "wrap around" after a certain value. It is used to perform operations in finite fields.

Prime and Relative Prime Numbers: Prime numbers are positive integers greater than 1 that have no positive integer divisors other than 1 and itself. Relative prime numbers are two numbers that have no common factor other than 1.

Extended Euclidean Algorithm: The extended Euclidean algorithm is an algorithm to find the greatest common divisor of two integers and also the coefficients of Bezout's identity.

Advanced Encryption Standard (AES) Encryption and Decryption: AES is a symmetric key encryption algorithm that uses a block cipher with a key length of 128, 192, or 256 bits. It is widely used in various applications, including wireless security, electronic payment systems, and VPNs.

Fermat’s and Euler’s Theorem: Fermat's theorem states that if p is a prime number and a is an integer not divisible by p, then a^(p-1) ≡ 1 (mod p). Euler's theorem is a generalization of Fermat's theorem for all positive integers n and states that if a and n are coprime, then a^(phi(n)) ≡ 1 (mod n), where phi(n) is Euler's totient function.

Primarily Testing: Primarily testing is a method to determine whether a given number is prime or composite.

Chinese Remainder Theorem: The Chinese remainder theorem is a theorem about solving systems of linear congruences. It states that if the moduli are pairwise relatively prime, then the system of congruences has a unique solution modulo the product of the moduli.

Discrete Logarithmic Problem: The discrete logarithmic problem is the problem of finding the exponent x in the equation a^x ≡ b (mod p), where p is a prime number, and a and b are integers.

Principles of Public Key Crypto Systems: Public key cryptography is a cryptographic system that uses a pair of keys - a public key and a private key. The public key is used for encryption, and the private key is used for decryption.

RSA Algorithm: The RSA algorithm is a public-key encryption algorithm that uses the product of two large prime numbers as the public key. It is widely used in various applications, including secure email, digital signatures, and SSL/TLS.

Security of RSA: The security of RSA depends on the difficulty of factoring large composite numbers into their prime factors. If the prime factors of the public key can be efficiently computed, the security of RSA is compromised. Therefore, the key size must be chosen large enough to resist attacks.
