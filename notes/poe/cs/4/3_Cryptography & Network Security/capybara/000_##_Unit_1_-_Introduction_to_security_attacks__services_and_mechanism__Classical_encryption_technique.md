### Introduction to Security Attacks, Services and Mechanism

1. Security attacks refer to the unauthorized access or manipulation of data, systems or networks by hackers or cybercriminals. 
2. Security services are mechanisms that protect against security attacks. The three main security services are confidentiality, integrity, and availability. 
3. Security mechanisms are techniques used to implement security services. Examples include access control, authentication, encryption, and digital signatures.

### Classical Encryption Techniques - Substitution Ciphers and Transposition Ciphers

1. Substitution ciphers involve replacing one letter or symbol with another. Examples include Caesar cipher, monoalphabetic cipher, and polyalphabetic cipher. 
2. Transposition ciphers involve rearranging the order of letters or symbols. Examples include rail fence cipher and columnar transposition cipher.

### Cryptanalysis

1. Cryptanalysis is the art of breaking encryption. It involves analyzing the ciphertext to reveal the original plaintext without knowing the key. 
2. Cryptanalysis techniques include frequency analysis, brute force attack, and known plaintext attack. 

### Steganography

1. Steganography is the practice of hiding a message within another message. 
2. Steganography techniques include hiding the message within an image, audio file or video file.

### Stream and Block Ciphers

1. Stream ciphers encrypt one bit at a time. Examples include RC4 and A5/1. 
2. Block ciphers encrypt a block of data at a time. Examples include Data Encryption Standard (DES), Advanced Encryption Standard (AES) and Blowfish.

### Modern Block Ciphers: Block Ciphers Principles, Shannon’s Theory of Confusion and Diffusion, Fiestal Structure, Data Encryption Standard (DES), Strength of DES, Idea of Differential Cryptanalysis, Block Cipher Modes of Operations, Triple DES

1. Block ciphers are cryptographic algorithms that operate on fixed-size blocks of data. 
2. Shannon’s theory of confusion and diffusion states that a good encryption algorithm should make the relationship between the plaintext and the ciphertext as complex as possible. 
3. Fiestal structure is a method of constructing block ciphers. 
4. Data Encryption Standard (DES) is a widely used symmetric encryption algorithm. 
5. DES has a key length of 56 bits, which makes it vulnerable to brute force attacks. 
6. Differential cryptanalysis is a technique for breaking DES encryption. 
7. Block cipher modes of operation include Electronic Codebook (ECB), Cipher Block Chaining (CBC), and Counter (CTR) mode. 
8. Triple DES is a variant of DES that uses three keys instead of one, making it more secure than DES.
