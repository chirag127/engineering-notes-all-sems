### Introduction to group

A group is a mathematical structure that consists of a set of elements and a binary operation that combines these elements in a specific way. The operation must satisfy four properties: closure, associativity, identity, and inverse. The study of groups is an important part of abstract algebra and has applications in various fields such as physics, chemistry, and computer science.

In this unit, we will cover the following topics related to groups:

- Field and Finite Field of the form GF(p): A field is a set of elements with two operations, addition and multiplication, that satisfy certain properties. A finite field is a field with a finite number of elements. GF(p) is a finite field with p elements, where p is a prime number.
- Modular arithmetic: Modular arithmetic is a way of performing arithmetic operations on integers that involves only the remainders of the numbers when divided by a specific integer called the modulus.
- Prime and relative prime numbers: A prime number is a positive integer greater than 1 that has no positive divisors other than 1 and itself. Two numbers are said to be relatively prime if they have no common factors other than 1.
- Extended Euclidean Algorithm: The Extended Euclidean Algorithm is an efficient algorithm for finding the greatest common divisor of two integers and also for finding integers x and y such that ax + by = gcd(a,b).
- Advanced Encryption Standard (AES) encryption and decryption: AES is a widely used symmetric key encryption algorithm that is used to protect sensitive data. It operates on blocks of data and uses a secret key to encrypt and decrypt the data.
- Fermat’s and Euler’s theorem: Fermat’s and Euler’s theorem are important theorems in number theory that provide a way to compute powers of integers modulo a specific integer.
- Primarily testing: Primarily testing is the process of determining whether a given integer is prime or composite.
- Chinese Remainder theorem: The Chinese Remainder theorem is a theorem in number theory that provides a way to solve a system of linear congruences.
- Discrete Logarithmic Problem: The Discrete Logarithmic Problem is a computational problem in number theory that involves finding an integer x such that a^x ≡ b (mod p), where a, b, and p are given integers.
- Principals of public key crypto systems: Public key cryptography is a cryptographic system that uses two keys, a public key and a private key, to encrypt and decrypt messages. The public key is used to encrypt messages, while the private key is used to decrypt messages.
- RSA algorithm: The RSA algorithm is a widely used public key encryption algorithm that is used to protect sensitive data. It is based on the difficulty of factoring large integers into their prime factors.
- Security of RSA: The security of RSA is based on the difficulty of factoring large integers into their prime factors. However, there are various attacks that can be used to break RSA encryption, such as brute force attacks, side-channel attacks, and chosen plaintext attacks.

Mnemonics and learning tricks can be helpful in remembering some of the concepts and formulas related to these topics. However, it is important to understand the underlying concepts and not rely solely on memorization. Practice problems and examples can also be helpful in understanding and applying these concepts.
