## Unit 2 - Introduction to group, field, finite field of the form GF(p), modular arithmetic, prime and relative prime numbers, Extended Euclidean Algorithm, Advanced Encryption Standard (AES) encryption and decryption, Fermat’s and Euler’s theorem, Primarily testing, Chinese Remainder theorem, Discrete Logarithmic Problem, Principles of public key crypto systems, RSA algorithm, security of RSA.

This unit covers various important concepts related to cryptography and number theory. It includes:

### Group, Field, and Finite Field

- A group is a set of elements along with an operation that satisfies certain properties like closure, identity element, inverse element, and associativity.
- A field is a set of elements along with two operations (addition and multiplication) that satisfy certain properties like closure, identity element, inverse element, and distributivity.
- A finite field of the form GF(p) is a field that contains p elements, where p is a prime number.
- Modular arithmetic is a type of arithmetic where integers "wrap around" upon reaching a certain value called the modulus. 

### Prime and Relative Prime Numbers

- A prime number is a natural number greater than 1 that has no positive divisors other than 1 and itself.
- Two numbers are said to be relatively prime if they have no common factors other than 1.

### Extended Euclidean Algorithm

- The Extended Euclidean Algorithm is an algorithm that finds the greatest common divisor of two integers and also finds integers x and y such that ax + by = gcd(a, b).

### Advanced Encryption Standard (AES) encryption and decryption

- AES is a symmetric encryption algorithm that is widely used for securing electronic data.
- It operates on blocks of plaintext and uses a key to encrypt and decrypt the data.

### Fermat’s and Euler’s theorem

- Fermat's Little Theorem states that if p is a prime number and a is any integer not divisible by p, then a^(p-1) ≡ 1 (mod p).
- Euler's Totient Theorem states that if a and m are two positive integers that are relatively prime, then a^φ(m) ≡ 1 (mod m), where φ(m) is Euler's totient function.

### Primarily testing

- Primality testing is the process of determining whether a given number is prime or composite.
- There are various algorithms for primality testing like the trial division method, Miller-Rabin test, and AKS primality test.

### Chinese Remainder theorem

- The Chinese Remainder Theorem is a theorem that gives a solution to a system of linear congruences with pairwise relatively prime moduli.

### Discrete Logarithmic Problem

- The Discrete Logarithm Problem is the problem of finding an integer x such that a^x ≡ b (mod p), where a, b, and p are given integers.

### Principles of public key crypto systems

- Public key cryptography is a cryptographic system that uses two keys (a public key and a private key) for encryption and decryption.
- It is widely used for secure communication over insecure channels.

### RSA algorithm

- RSA is a public key encryption algorithm that is widely used for secure communication.
- It involves the use of two keys, a public key and a private key, for encryption and decryption.

### Security of RSA

- The security of RSA depends on the difficulty of factoring large composite numbers.
- The security can be strengthened by using larger keys and by using additional security measures like padding and randomization.

In conclusion, this unit covers various important concepts related to cryptography and number theory. It is essential to have a good understanding of these concepts for designing and analyzing secure cryptographic systems.
