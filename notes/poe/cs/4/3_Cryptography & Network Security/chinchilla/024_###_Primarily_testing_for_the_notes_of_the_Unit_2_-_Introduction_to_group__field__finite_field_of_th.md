### Primarily Testing for Unit 2 - Cryptography & Network Security

Cryptography is the study of techniques for secure communication in the presence of third parties. It involves encryption and decryption of messages using various mathematical algorithms. In this unit of Cryptography & Network Security, we will cover several important topics related to cryptography such as group, field, finite field of the form GF(p), modular arithmetic, prime and relative prime numbers, Extended Euclidean Algorithm, Advanced Encryption Standard (AES) encryption and decryption, Fermat’s and Euler’s theorem, primarily testing, Chinese Remainder theorem, Discrete Logarithmic Problem, Principals of public key crypto systems, RSA algorithm, and security of RSA.

#### Group, Field, and Finite Field of the form GF(p)

- A group is a set of elements that satisfy certain properties such as closure, associativity, identity, and inverse. 
- A field is a set of elements that satisfy certain properties such as addition, multiplication, distributivity, and inverse. 
- A finite field of the form GF(p) is a field that has a finite number of elements, where p is a prime number. 
- These concepts are important in cryptography because they provide a mathematical framework for the design and analysis of cryptographic algorithms.

#### Modular Arithmetic, Prime and Relative Prime Numbers, Extended Euclidean Algorithm

- Modular arithmetic is a type of arithmetic where numbers "wrap around" after reaching a certain value called modulus. 
- Prime numbers are numbers that are only divisible by 1 and themselves. 
- Relative prime numbers are numbers that have no common factors other than 1. 
- The Extended Euclidean Algorithm is an algorithm for finding the greatest common divisor of two numbers and their respective multiplicative inverses modulo a given number. 
- These concepts are used in various cryptographic algorithms such as RSA and Diffie-Hellman key exchange.

#### Advanced Encryption Standard (AES) Encryption and Decryption

- AES is a symmetric encryption algorithm that uses a block cipher to encrypt and decrypt data. 
- It is widely used in various applications such as secure communication, data storage, and electronic commerce. 
- AES has several modes of operation such as ECB (Electronic Codebook), CBC (Cipher Block Chaining), and CTR (Counter).

#### Fermat’s and Euler’s Theorem, Primarily Testing

- Fermat’s and Euler’s Theorem are important theorems in number theory that provide a way to compute the remainder of a number raised to a power modulo a given number. 
- Primarily testing is a technique used to determine if a number is prime or composite. 
- These concepts are used in several cryptographic algorithms such as RSA and Diffie-Hellman key exchange.

#### Chinese Remainder Theorem, Discrete Logarithmic Problem

- The Chinese Remainder Theorem is a theorem that provides a way to solve a system of linear congruences. 
- The Discrete Logarithmic Problem is a problem that involves finding the logarithm of a number with respect to a given base modulo a given number. 
- These concepts are used in various cryptographic algorithms such as Diffie-Hellman key exchange and elliptic curve cryptography.

#### Principals of Public Key Crypto Systems, RSA Algorithm, Security of RSA

- Public key cryptography is a type of cryptography that uses a pair of keys - a public key and a private key - for encryption and decryption of data. 
- The RSA algorithm is a widely used public key encryption algorithm that is based on the difficulty of factoring large integers. 
- The security of RSA depends on the difficulty of factoring large primes. 
- Various attacks such as brute force attack, chosen plaintext attack, and side-channel attack can be used to compromise the security of RSA.

In summary, this unit covers several important topics related to cryptography such as group, field, finite field of the form GF(p), modular arithmetic, prime and relative prime numbers, Extended Euclidean Algorithm, Advanced Encryption Standard (AES) encryption and decryption, Fermat’s and Euler’s theorem, primarily testing, Chinese Remainder theorem, Discrete Logarithmic Problem, Principals of public key crypto systems, RSA algorithm, and security of RSA. Understanding these topics is crucial for designing and analyzing secure cryptographic algorithms.
