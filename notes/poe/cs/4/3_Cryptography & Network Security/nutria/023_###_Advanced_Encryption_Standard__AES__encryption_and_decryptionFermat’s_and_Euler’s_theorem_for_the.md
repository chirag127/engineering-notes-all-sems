##### Advanced Encryption Standard (AES) 
Advanced Encryption Standard (AES) is a symmetric-key algorithm used for data encryption. It is a block cipher, meaning that it takes a block of data and encrypts it using a key. AES is the most secure and widely used encryption algorithm in the world. AES is based on the Rijndael cipher developed by two Belgian cryptographers, Joan Daemen and Vincent Rijmen. AES is used for both encryption and decryption, and is designed to be secure and fast. 

##### Fermat’s and Euler’s Theorem
Fermat’s and Euler’s theorem are two important theorems in number theory. Fermat’s theorem states that if p is a prime number, then for any integer a, the equation ap-a will be divisible by p. Euler’s theorem states that if a and n are relatively prime, then a^φ(n) ≡ 1 (mod n), where φ(n) is the Euler totient function. These theorems are important in cryptography, as they are used to prove the security of RSA encryption.
