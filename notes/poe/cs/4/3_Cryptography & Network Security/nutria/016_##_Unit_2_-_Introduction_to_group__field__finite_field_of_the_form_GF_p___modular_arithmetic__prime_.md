
## Unit 2 - Introduction to group, field, finite field of the form GF(p), modular arithmetic, prime and relative prime numbers, Extended Euclidean Algorithm, Advanced Encryption Standard (AES) encryption and decryptionFermat’s and Euler’s theorem, Primarily testing, Chinese Remainder theorem, Discrete Logarithmic Problem,Principals of public key crypto systems, RSA algorithm, security of RSA

This unit covers a wide range of topics related to cryptography, including groups, fields, finite fields of the form GF(p), modular arithmetic, prime and relative prime numbers, Extended Euclidean Algorithm, Advanced Encryption Standard (AES) encryption and decryption, Fermat’s and Euler’s theorem, Primarily testing, Chinese Remainder theorem, Discrete Logarithmic Problem, Principals of public key crypto systems, RSA algorithm, and security of RSA.

- **Groups**: A group is a set of elements with a defined operation that satisfies certain properties. Examples of groups include the integers under addition, the non-zero rational numbers under multiplication, and the permutations of a finite set under composition.

- **Fields**: A field is a set of elements with two binary operations, addition and multiplication, which satisfy certain properties. Examples of fields include the real numbers and the complex numbers.

- **Finite fields of the form GF(p)**: A finite field of the form GF(p) is a field with a finite number of elements, p, where p is a prime number.

- **Modular Arithmetic**: Modular arithmetic is a form of arithmetic used in cryptography which involves taking the remainder when dividing by a certain number.

- **Prime and Relative Prime Numbers**: A prime number is a number that can only be divided by itself and one. A relative prime number is a number that shares no common factors with another number.

- **Extended Euclidean Algorithm**: The Extended Euclidean Algorithm is an algorithm used to find the greatest common divisor of two integers.

- **Advanced Encryption Standard (AES) Encryption and Decryption**: AES is a symmetric key encryption algorithm used to protect data confidentiality. It uses a key to encrypt and decrypt data.

- **Fermat’s and Euler’s Theorem**: Fermat’s and Euler’s Theorem are two theorems used in cryptography to find the multiplicative inverse of a number modulo a prime number.

- **Primarily Testing**: Primarily testing is a method used to determine whether a number is prime or not.

- **Chinese Remainder Theorem**: The Chinese Remainder Theorem is a theorem used to find the solution to a system of congruences.

- **Discrete Logarithmic Problem**: The Discrete Logarithmic Problem is a problem in cryptography which involves finding the discrete logarithm of a number.

- **Principals of Public Key Crypto Systems**: Public key crypto systems are cryptographic systems which use two keys, a public key and a private key, to encrypt and decrypt data.

- **RSA Algorithm**: The RSA Algorithm is an algorithm used to encrypt and decrypt data. It uses two keys, a public key and a private key.

- **Security of RSA**: The security of RSA is based on the difficulty of factoring large numbers. If a large enough number is used, it is believed that it is computationally infeasible to factor the number.
