### Consensus protocols for Permissioned Blockchains

In Permissioned Blockchains, consensus protocols are used to ensure that transactions are valid and agreed upon by the network participants. Here are some of the most commonly used consensus protocols in Permissioned Blockchains:

#### Practical Byzantine Fault Tolerance (PBFT)
- PBFT is a consensus protocol that was designed to tolerate malicious nodes in the network.
- In PBFT, a leader node is chosen to propose a new block of transactions, and other nodes in the network validate the proposed block.
- If a majority of nodes agree that the block is valid, it is added to the blockchain.
- PBFT is known for its high throughput and low latency, but it requires a large number of nodes to work efficiently.

#### Proof of Authority (PoA)
- PoA is a consensus protocol that relies on identity and reputation of the nodes in the network.
- In PoA, a group of nodes is designated as validators, and they are responsible for adding new blocks to the blockchain.
- Validators are chosen based on their reputation and trustworthiness, and they are held accountable for their actions.
- PoA is known for its fast consensus and low energy consumption, but it is less decentralized than other consensus protocols.

#### Delegated Proof of Stake (DPoS)
- DPoS is a consensus protocol that allows token holders to vote for delegates who are responsible for adding new blocks to the blockchain.
- Delegates are chosen based on their reputation and the number of votes they receive from token holders.
- DPoS is known for its fast consensus and low energy consumption, but it is less decentralized than other consensus protocols.

#### Practical Byzantine Fault Tolerance with Redundancy (PBFT-R)
- PBFT-R is a modified version of PBFT that adds redundancy to the network to increase fault tolerance.
- In PBFT-R, multiple leaders are chosen to propose new blocks, and a primary leader is selected to coordinate the consensus process.
- If the primary leader fails, a secondary leader takes over to ensure that the consensus process continues.
- PBFT-R is known for its high fault tolerance and low latency, but it requires a larger number of nodes than PBFT.

#### Learning tricks and Mnemonics
- There are no specific learning tricks or Mnemonics for remembering the consensus protocols for Permissioned Blockchains.
- However, it can be helpful to remember the key features and benefits of each protocol to understand when and where they are most appropriate to use.
