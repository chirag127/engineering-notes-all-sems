## Unit 8 - Use case 3

### Overview
Use case 3 is a technique used in software engineering to identify and describe functional requirements of a system. It involves the creation of scenarios that describe how a user interacts with the system to achieve a specific goal. Use case 3 helps to identify the actors involved in the system, the flow of events, and the outcome of each scenario.

### Mnemonic
No mnemonic or learning trick is commonly used for Use case 3.

### Steps to create Use case 3
1. Identify the actors: The first step is to identify the actors involved in the system. Actors can be people, other systems or devices that interact with the system.

2. Identify the goals: The next step is to identify the goals of the actors. Goals are what the actors want to achieve by interacting with the system.

3. Create scenarios: Based on the actors and their goals, create scenarios that describe how the actors will interact with the system to achieve their goals.

4. Identify flow of events: Identify the flow of events that occur in each scenario. The flow of events should describe the steps that the actors take to achieve their goals.

5. Describe outcomes: Describe the outcomes of each scenario. The outcome should describe what happens when the actors complete their goals.

### Advantages of Use case 3
- Helps to identify the functional requirements of a system.
- Provides a clear understanding of how the system will be used.
- Helps to identify potential issues and problems in the system.
- Provides a basis for testing and validation of the system.

### Disadvantages of Use case 3
- Can be time-consuming to create and maintain.
- May not capture all possible scenarios of system usage.
- Can become too complex and difficult to manage in large systems.

### Examples of Use case 3
- A mobile banking application use case for transferring money between accounts.
- A hospital management system use case for scheduling appointments.
- A customer service system use case for handling customer complaints.

### Applications of Use case 3
Use case 3 is commonly used in software engineering to identify and describe functional requirements of a system. It is also used in project management to identify project goals and objectives. Additionally, it can be used in business analysis to identify business requirements and processes.
