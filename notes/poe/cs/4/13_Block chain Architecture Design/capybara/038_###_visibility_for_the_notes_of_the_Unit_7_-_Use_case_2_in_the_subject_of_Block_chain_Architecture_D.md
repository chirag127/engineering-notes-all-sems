### Visibility for the Notes of Unit 7 - Use Case 2 in the Subject of Blockchain Architecture Design

Visibility in the context of blockchain architecture design refers to the ability to view and track the different transactions and activities that occur on the blockchain network. It is an essential feature for any blockchain network as it ensures that all parties involved in the network can access and monitor the blockchain activities.

In Unit 7 - Use Case 2 of the subject of Blockchain Architecture Design, visibility plays a critical role in ensuring the success of the blockchain network. Here are some key points to understand about visibility:

1. **Transaction Visibility:** Blockchain networks enable all participants to view and track all transactions that occur on the network. This feature ensures that all parties involved in the network can monitor the transactions and ensure their accuracy.

2. **Data Visibility:** Blockchain networks also provide data visibility, which enables all participants to view and track the data that is stored on the network. This feature ensures that all parties can access the data they need and verify its accuracy.

3. **Auditing and Compliance:** Visibility is crucial for auditing and compliance purposes. Audit trails can be created that allow all parties to track the changes made to the data on the blockchain network.

4. **Mnemonics and Learning Tricks:** Some mnemonics and learning tricks to remember the importance of visibility in blockchain architecture design are:

- "See it to believe it." This phrase emphasizes the importance of visibility in verifying the accuracy of transactions and data on the blockchain network.

- "Transparency is key." This phrase highlights the importance of transparency and visibility in blockchain networks.

- "Trust but verify." This saying emphasizes the importance of visibility in ensuring trust and verifying the accuracy of transactions and data on the blockchain network.

In conclusion, visibility is a critical feature in blockchain architecture design, and it plays a vital role in ensuring the success of blockchain networks. By enabling all participants to view and track transactions and data, visibility ensures the accuracy and transparency of the blockchain network.
