### Design goals for the notes of the Unit 3 - Permissioned Blockchains in the subject of Blockchain Architecture Design

The design goals for the notes of the Unit 3 - Permissioned Blockchains in the subject of Blockchain Architecture Design are as follows:

1. **Understanding the concept of permissioned blockchains:** The first design goal of these notes is to provide a clear understanding of permissioned blockchains. It includes the basics of permissioned blockchains, the difference between permissioned and permissionless blockchains, and the types of permissioned blockchains.

2. **Understanding the architecture of permissioned blockchains:** The second design goal is to provide a detailed understanding of the architecture of permissioned blockchains. This includes the components of permissioned blockchains, how they work together, and how they differ from the architecture of permissionless blockchains.

3. **Understanding the consensus mechanism:** The third design goal is to provide an understanding of the consensus mechanism used in permissioned blockchains. The notes will cover different consensus algorithms, their advantages and disadvantages, and how they are implemented in permissioned blockchains.

4. **Understanding the role of smart contracts:** The fourth design goal is to provide an understanding of the role of smart contracts in permissioned blockchains. The notes will cover what smart contracts are, how they work, and how they are used in permissioned blockchains.

5. **Understanding the security and privacy aspects:** The fifth design goal is to provide an understanding of the security and privacy aspects of permissioned blockchains. The notes will cover the security threats to permissioned blockchains, the measures taken to mitigate those threats, and the privacy features offered by permissioned blockchains.
