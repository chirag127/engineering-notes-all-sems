### Design Primitives for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Blockchain in the subject of Blockchain Architecture Design

Blockchain technology is a distributed ledger that records transactions in a secure and transparent manner. The design primitives are the fundamental building blocks that form the basis of blockchain architecture. These design primitives are important to understand before delving deeper into blockchain architecture design. Here are some of the design primitives for blockchain architecture design:

1. Cryptography: Cryptography is one of the most important design primitives in blockchain architecture. It involves the use of mathematical algorithms to secure the transactions made on the blockchain ledger. Cryptography ensures that the transactions are secure, private and tamper-proof.

2. Consensus Mechanisms: Consensus mechanisms are the protocols that enable participants in a blockchain network to agree on the validity of transactions. These mechanisms play a crucial role in ensuring the integrity of the blockchain ledger. Examples of consensus mechanisms include Proof-of-Work, Proof-of-Stake, and Delegated Proof-of-Stake.

3. Smart Contracts: Smart contracts are self-executing contracts with the terms and conditions of the agreement between the parties being directly written into lines of code. These contracts enable the automation of transactions and the removal of intermediaries, thereby reducing transaction costs and increasing transaction speed.

4. Decentralization: Decentralization is another important design primitive in blockchain architecture. It refers to the distribution of computing power and decision-making across the network. Decentralization ensures that there is no central point of control, making the blockchain network more secure and transparent.

5. Immutable Ledger: The blockchain ledger is immutable, meaning that once a transaction has been recorded on the blockchain, it cannot be altered or deleted. This ensures that the integrity of the blockchain ledger is maintained and that all transactions are transparent.

Mnemonics and Learning Tricks: 
1. Remember the acronym CCDSD (Cryptography, Consensus Mechanisms, Smart Contracts, Decentralization, Immutable Ledger) to help you remember the design primitives of blockchain architecture.
2. Think of the blockchain ledger as a digital diary. Once something is written in a diary, it cannot be erased or altered, just like the transactions on the blockchain ledger.
