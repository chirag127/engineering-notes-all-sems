## Unit 5 - Hyperledger Fabric (B)

Hyperledger Fabric is a blockchain framework that is highly modular, scalable, and secure. In this unit, we will dive deeper into Hyperledger Fabric and explore its various components and features.

### Components of Hyperledger Fabric

1. **Peer Nodes**: Peer nodes maintain the ledger and execute transactions. They communicate with each other to reach consensus on the state of the ledger.

2. **Ordering Service**: The ordering service receives transaction proposals from clients and orders them into a block. It then distributes the block to the peer nodes for validation and commitment to the ledger.

3. **Membership Service Provider (MSP)**: The MSP manages the identities of the participants in the network. It authenticates users and grants them appropriate access to the network.

4. **Chaincode**: Chaincode is the business logic that defines the rules for executing transactions on the network. It is deployed on peer nodes and executed upon request.

### Features of Hyperledger Fabric

1. **Privacy**: Hyperledger Fabric allows for private transactions where only the involved parties can see the details of the transaction.

2. **Scalability**: The modular architecture of Hyperledger Fabric allows for the network to scale to meet the demands of the application.

3. **Flexibility**: Hyperledger Fabric supports multiple consensus algorithms, allowing the network to be tailored to the specific needs of the application.

4. **Permissioned Network**: Hyperledger Fabric is a permissioned network, meaning that participants must be authenticated and authorized to access the network.

### Mnemonics and Learning Tricks

- Remember the acronym P.O.M.C. for the components of Hyperledger Fabric: Peer Nodes, Ordering Service, Membership Service Provider, and Chaincode.
- To remember the features of Hyperledger Fabric, think of the acronym P.S.F.P.: Privacy, Scalability, Flexibility, and Permissioned Network.

By understanding the components and features of Hyperledger Fabric, you will be able to design and deploy scalable and secure blockchain applications.
