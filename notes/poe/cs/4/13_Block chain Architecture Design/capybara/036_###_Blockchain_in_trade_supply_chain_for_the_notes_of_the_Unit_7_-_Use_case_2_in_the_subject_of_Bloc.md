### Blockchain in Trade/Supply Chain

In the world of trade and supply chain, blockchain technology has the potential to revolutionize the way transactions are conducted. Here are some key points to help you understand the use case of blockchain in trade/supply chain:

1. The traditional process of trade and supply chain involves many intermediaries, which results in delays, errors, and increased costs. In contrast, blockchain enables a decentralized and secure platform for transactions, eliminating the need for intermediaries and reducing costs.

2. Blockchain allows for greater transparency and traceability in the supply chain. Every transaction is recorded on the blockchain, creating an immutable ledger that can be accessed by all parties involved. This makes it easier to track the movement of goods and prevent fraud.

3. Smart contracts can be used to automate the execution of trade transactions, reducing the need for manual intervention and improving efficiency. For example, a smart contract could be used to automatically release payment to a supplier once the goods have been received and verified.

4. Blockchain technology provides a secure and tamper-proof platform for document management. For example, bills of lading and other trade documents can be stored on the blockchain, ensuring that they are authentic and cannot be altered.

5. Blockchain can also be used to facilitate cross-border trade by streamlining the process of customs clearance and reducing the need for intermediaries. This can help to reduce trade barriers and increase global trade.

Mnemonics and Learning Tricks:

- Think of blockchain as a digital ledger that keeps track of all transactions in a secure and transparent way.
- Smart contracts can be thought of as self-executing contracts that automatically enforce the terms of an agreement.
- Blockchain technology can be compared to a digital notary that ensures the authenticity and integrity of documents.
