## Unit 3 - Permissioned Blockchains

In this unit, we will be discussing permissioned blockchains, which are also known as private or consortium blockchains. These blockchains are different from public blockchains as access to them is restricted to a specific group of participants. 

### Characteristics of Permissioned Blockchains:

1. Access Controls: Permissioned blockchains are accessible only to a specific group of participants who have the necessary permissions to join the network.

2. Identity Management: Participants in a permissioned blockchain are required to undergo identity verification to ensure that only authorized users can access the network.

3. Consensus Mechanisms: Permissioned blockchains rely on a predetermined consensus mechanism that is agreed upon by all participants. 

4. Governance: Permissioned blockchains are governed by a set of rules and regulations that are enforced by the participants.

### Advantages of Permissioned Blockchains:

1. Improved Efficiency: Permissioned blockchains are faster and more efficient compared to public blockchains as they have a smaller network of participants.

2. Better Security: With access controls and identity management, permissioned blockchains provide better security than public blockchains.

3. Increased Privacy: As permissioned blockchains are only accessible to authorized participants, they provide increased privacy and confidentiality.

4. Customizable: Participants in permissioned blockchains have more control over the network's governance and consensus mechanisms, making them more customizable.

### Disadvantages of Permissioned Blockchains:

1. Centralization: Permissioned blockchains are more centralized compared to public blockchains, which can make them susceptible to a single point of failure.

2. Limited Decentralization: Permissioned blockchains have limited decentralization as they are controlled by a specific group of participants.

3. Cost: Permissioned blockchains can be more expensive to set up and maintain compared to public blockchains.

### Mnemonics and Learning Tricks:

1. To remember the characteristics of permissioned blockchains, you can use the mnemonic "AICG," which stands for Access Controls, Identity Management, Consensus Mechanisms, and Governance.

2. To remember the advantages of permissioned blockchains, you can use the mnemonic "EPPC," which stands for Efficiency, Security, Privacy, and Customizability.

Overall, permissioned blockchains have their own set of advantages and disadvantages. They are suitable for private networks where participants require increased privacy and confidentiality. However, they are not suitable for decentralized applications that require a high level of decentralization.
