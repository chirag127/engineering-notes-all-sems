### Digital Money to Distributed Ledgers for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Blockchain in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

Blockchain technology has revolutionized the way digital transactions are carried out. It has evolved from being just a digital currency to a distributed ledger technology that can facilitate secure and tamper-proof transactions.

Here are some points to understand the evolution of digital money to distributed ledgers:

1. Digital Currency: The first and foremost application of blockchain technology was Bitcoin, which is a digital currency. Bitcoin was created in 2009 as a peer-to-peer electronic cash system that eliminates intermediaries and allows for direct transactions between parties. It uses blockchain technology to ensure that transactions are secure and transparent.

2. Decentralized Applications: With the success of Bitcoin, developers started exploring other use cases of blockchain technology. They realized that blockchain technology could be used to create decentralized applications that can run on a distributed network without any central authority. This led to the creation of Ethereum, which is a blockchain-based platform for creating smart contracts and decentralized applications.

3. Distributed Ledger: The use of blockchain technology in digital currencies and decentralized applications led to the creation of a distributed ledger. A distributed ledger is a database that is spread across a network of computers, and each computer has a copy of the ledger. The ledger is updated in real-time, and all the copies are synchronized to ensure that they are the same.

4. Smart Contracts: Smart contracts are self-executing contracts that are encoded on the blockchain. They allow for the automation of contracts, which can reduce the need for intermediaries and increase efficiency. Smart contracts are used in various industries, including finance, real estate, and supply chain management.

5. Permissioned Ledgers: Permissioned ledgers are distributed ledgers that are only accessible to authorized participants. They are used in industries where privacy and security are critical, such as healthcare and government. Permissioned ledgers are designed to ensure that only authorized parties can access and update the ledger, which increases security.

Mnemonics and Learning Tricks:

1. DCDL - Digital Currency to Distributed Ledgers.
2. Think of blockchain as a digital ledger that can record anything of value, not just digital currency.
3. Smart contracts can automate contract execution, saving time and reducing costs.
4. Permissioned ledgers are like private clubs where only authorized members are allowed.
