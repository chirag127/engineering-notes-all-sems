
### Basic consensus mechanisms for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Blockchain in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

1. **Proof of Work (PoW)**: This consensus algorithm is the most widely used in blockchain technology. It requires miners to solve complex mathematical puzzles to verify transactions and add new blocks to the chain. This method is secure, but it is also energy-intensive, making it less efficient.

2. **Proof of Stake (PoS)**: This consensus algorithm is an alternative to PoW. Instead of miners, validators are selected based on their stake in the network. These validators are responsible for verifying transactions and adding new blocks to the chain. This method is more efficient than PoW, but it is also more vulnerable to attack. 

3. **Delegated Proof of Stake (DPoS)**: This consensus algorithm is a variation on PoS. Instead of selecting validators based on their stake, validators are elected by the community. This method is more efficient and secure than PoS, but it is also more centralized.

4. **Byzantine Fault Tolerance (BFT)**: This consensus algorithm is used in permissioned blockchains. It relies on a set of nodes that are responsible for verifying transactions and adding new blocks to the chain. This method is secure and efficient, but it is also more complex to implement.

5. **Mining Algorithms**: Mining algorithms are used to secure the blockchain. They are responsible for verifying transactions and adding new blocks to the chain. The most popular mining algorithms are SHA-256, Scrypt, and X11.

Mnemonic: PoW = Proof of Work; PoS = Proof of Stake; DPoS = Delegated Proof of Stake; BFT = Byzantine Fault Tolerance.
