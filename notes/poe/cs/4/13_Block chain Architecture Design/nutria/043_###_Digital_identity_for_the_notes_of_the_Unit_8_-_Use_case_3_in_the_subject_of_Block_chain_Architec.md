
### Digital identity for the notes of the Unit 8 - Use case 3 in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

Digital identity is the process of verifying an individual's identity in a digital setting. It is an important component of blockchain architecture design, as it is used to secure transactions and ensure that the parties involved in a transaction are who they say they are.

Digital identity can be verified in a variety of ways, including:
- Biometric data: Using biometric data such as fingerprints, iris scans, and facial recognition to authenticate an individual's identity.
- Digital certificates: A digital certificate is an electronic document that is used to prove the identity of an individual.
- Public-key cryptography: Public-key cryptography is a type of cryptography that uses two keys, one public and one private, to secure data.
- Digital tokens: Digital tokens are used to authenticate an individual's identity and can be used to control access to certain resources.

When designing a blockchain architecture, it is important to consider how digital identity will be used to secure transactions and to ensure that the parties involved in a transaction are who they say they are. Digital identity can also be used to create trust between parties, as well as to protect against fraud and malicious activity.
