
## Unit 2 - Consensus

* Consensus is a process by which a group of parties come to an agreement on a particular issue or set of issues. It is a form of collective decision-making, often used in organizations and communities.
* Consensus can be achieved through negotiation, discussion, and compromise. It is often used to resolve conflicts and to reach decisions on matters of policy or procedure.
* Consensus can also be used to develop a shared understanding of a situation or problem. This can be done through brainstorming and problem-solving techniques.
* Consensus can be used as a means of achieving collective action. It can help to ensure that decisions are made in the best interests of the group, rather than the interests of any one individual or faction.
* Consensus can also be used to build unity and trust among members of a group. It can help to create a sense of shared purpose and common goals.
* Mnemonics and learning tricks for consensus can be helpful in understanding the concept. For example, the acronym "C-O-N-S-E-N-S-U-S" can be used to remember the definition of consensus: Collaboration, Openness, Negotiation, Sharing, Equality, Non-judgmental, Solution-oriented, Understanding, and Support.
