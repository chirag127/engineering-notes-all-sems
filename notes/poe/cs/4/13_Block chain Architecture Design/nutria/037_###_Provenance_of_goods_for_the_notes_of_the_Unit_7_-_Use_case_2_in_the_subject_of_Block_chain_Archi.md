* The distributed ledger is used to store the transaction data, which is cryptographically secured and immutable.
* Smart contracts are used to automate the transfer of data between parties, and digital signatures are used to authenticate the transfer of goods.
* Blockchain-based provenance of goods systems provide several advantages, including increased transparency, improved security, and faster processing times.
