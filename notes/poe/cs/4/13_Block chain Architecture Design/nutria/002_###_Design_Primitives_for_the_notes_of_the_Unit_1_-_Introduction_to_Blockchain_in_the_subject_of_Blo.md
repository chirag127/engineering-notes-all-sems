
### Design Primitives for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Blockchain in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

Design Primitives are the fundamental building blocks of blockchain systems. They are the components that allow for the creation of complex blockchain architectures. These primitives include:

1. Consensus Algorithms: These algorithms are used to ensure that the data stored on the blockchain is valid and secure. Examples include Proof of Work (PoW) and Proof of Stake (PoS).

2. Cryptography: Cryptography is used to secure data on the blockchain. Examples include public-key cryptography, digital signatures, and hash functions.

3. Network Topology: Network topology is the arrangement of nodes in a blockchain network. It affects the speed and security of the network. Examples include private, public, and permissioned networks.

4. Smart Contracts: Smart contracts are self-executing contracts that are stored on the blockchain. They are used to automate processes and enforce agreements.

5. Tokenization: Tokenization is the process of creating digital tokens that represent real-world assets. These tokens can be used to facilitate transactions and create new markets.

In addition to these design primitives, blockchain systems can be built using a variety of other components, such as distributed data stores, distributed computing platforms, and distributed ledgers. These components can be used to create complex architectures that are tailored to specific use cases.
