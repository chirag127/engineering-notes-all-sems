
### Signature for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Blockchain in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

* A signature is a cryptographic technique used to verify the authenticity of digital documents. It is used to ensure that the document is not tampered with and that the sender is the one who they claim to be.
* In the context of blockchain architecture design, a signature is used to prove ownership of a certain asset. It is also used to prove that a transaction was made by a certain user.
* A signature is created using public-key cryptography. This means that each user has a public key, which is used to verify the authenticity of the signature, and a private key, which is used to create the signature.
* The signature is created by running the data of the transaction or asset through a cryptographic hash function. This produces a unique digital signature for the transaction or asset.
* The signature can then be verified by running the data of the transaction or asset through the same cryptographic hash function and comparing the result to the signature. If the two results match, then the signature is valid.
* Mnemonics and learning tricks for signature verification include remembering the acronym P.E.A.S (Public Key, Encryption, Authentication, Signature). This stands for the four steps involved in signature verification: public key, encryption, authentication, and signature.
