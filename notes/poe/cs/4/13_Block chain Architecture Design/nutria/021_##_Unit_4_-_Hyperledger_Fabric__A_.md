
## Unit 4 - Hyperledger Fabric (A)

Hyperledger Fabric is an open-source distributed ledger technology (DLT) platform for developing permissioned blockchain applications. It was created by the Linux Foundation in 2016 and is one of the most popular DLT platforms available.

Hyperledger Fabric is a permissioned blockchain platform that provides a modular architecture with a variety of components that can be configured to meet the needs of a particular use case. It supports smart contracts written in multiple programming languages and is designed to provide a high degree of confidentiality, resiliency, flexibility, and scalability.

**Mnemonics and Learning Tricks:**

* **F**lexible: Hyperledger Fabric is highly configurable and can be tailored to meet the needs of a particular use case.
* **A**dvanced: Hyperledger Fabric features a modular architecture and provides a high degree of confidentiality, resiliency, and scalability.
* **B**lockchain: Hyperledger Fabric is a permissioned blockchain platform.
* **R**obust: Hyperledger Fabric is designed to be robust and resilient to attacks.
* **I**nteroperable: Hyperledger Fabric supports multiple programming languages and provides APIs for interacting with other blockchain networks.
* **C**onsortium: Hyperledger Fabric supports the creation of consortiums, allowing multiple parties to collaborate on a shared network.

**Advantages:**

* Highly configurable and can be tailored to meet the needs of a particular use case.
* Provides a high degree of confidentiality, resiliency, flexibility, and scalability.
* Supports multiple programming languages and provides APIs for interacting with other blockchain networks.
* Supports the creation of consortiums, allowing multiple parties to collaborate on a shared network.

**Disadvantages:**

* It is not suitable for applications that require a high degree of anonymity.
* It is not suitable for applications that require a high degree of scalability.
* It is not suitable for applications that require a high degree of decentralization.
* It is not suitable for applications that require a high degree of speed.

**Examples:**

* Supply chain management: Hyperledger Fabric can be used to track and trace the movement of goods across a supply chain.
* Digital identity: Hyperledger Fabric can be used to store and manage digital identities, such as passports and driver’s licenses.
* Healthcare: Hyperledger Fabric can be used to securely store and manage healthcare data, such as patient records and medical images.
* Banking: Hyperledger Fabric can be used to securely store and manage banking data, such as customer accounts and transaction histories.
