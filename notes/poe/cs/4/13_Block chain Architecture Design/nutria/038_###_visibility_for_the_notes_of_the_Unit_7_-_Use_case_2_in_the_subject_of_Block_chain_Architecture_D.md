
### Visibility for the Notes of the Unit 7 - Use Case 2 in the Subject of Block Chain Architecture Design

1. Visibility is a key concept in the design of a blockchain architecture. It refers to the ability of users to view the contents of the blockchain, including its transactions, blocks, and other data.

2. Visibility is important because it allows users to verify the authenticity of the data stored in the blockchain. This can be done using cryptographic techniques such as digital signatures and hash functions.

3. Visibility also allows users to view the history of a particular transaction, which can be used to trace the origin of a transaction and detect any attempts at fraud or tampering.

4. Visibility also helps to ensure that transactions are properly recorded and stored in the blockchain. This can help to prevent double-spending and other malicious activities.
