
### KYC for the notes of the Unit 6 - Use case 1 in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

- **Know Your Customer (KYC)** is a process by which businesses verify the identity of their customers. This process is used by financial institutions, banks and other companies to ensure they comply with anti-money laundering (AML) regulations. 

- KYC is an important part of the blockchain architecture design process. It helps to ensure that all transactions are legitimate and that the blockchain is secure. 

- KYC involves collecting and verifying customer information such as name, address, date of birth, and other personal data. The information collected is used to create a customer profile, which can then be used to track transactions and detect any suspicious activity. 

- KYC can also be used to verify the identity of users before they can access certain services or resources. This helps to ensure that only authorized users have access to the system. 

- KYC can also be used to ensure that customers are not using the blockchain for illegal activities. This helps to protect the blockchain from being used for money laundering, terrorist financing, and other criminal activities. 

- To make KYC more effective, businesses should use a combination of automated and manual processes. Automated processes can help to quickly identify suspicious activity and detect any potential fraud. Manual processes can help to ensure that customers are properly identified and that their information is accurate. 

- Good mnemonics for remembering KYC include: "Keep Your Customers Informed" and "Know Your Customers Beforehand". These mnemonics help to remind businesses of the importance of KYC and why it is necessary. 

- By implementing KYC, businesses can ensure that their customers are who they say they are and that the blockchain is secure. This helps to protect the blockchain from being used for illegal activities and helps to ensure that all transactions are legitimate.
