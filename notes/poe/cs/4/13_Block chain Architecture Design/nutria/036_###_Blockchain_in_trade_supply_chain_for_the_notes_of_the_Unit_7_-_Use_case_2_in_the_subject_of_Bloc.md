
### Blockchain in Trade/Supply Chain for the Notes of Unit 7 - Use Case 2 in the Subject of Blockchain Architecture Design

- Blockchain technology is a distributed ledger system that enables secure, transparent, and tamper-proof transactions between multiple parties.
- It is immutable, meaning that once a transaction is recorded on the blockchain, it cannot be changed or reversed.
- Blockchain technology has the potential to revolutionize the way that trade and supply chain processes are managed.
- It can provide a secure, real-time view of the entire supply chain process, from the origin of a product to its final destination.
- It can also be used to track the movement of goods, verify product authenticity, and ensure that all parties involved in the transaction are following the agreed-upon rules and regulations.
- Additionally, blockchain technology can enable smart contracts, which allow for automated execution of transactions when certain conditions are met.
- This can help to reduce costs, minimize fraud, and streamline processes.
- Finally, blockchain technology can also be used to facilitate payments between parties, eliminating the need for intermediaries and reducing transaction costs.
