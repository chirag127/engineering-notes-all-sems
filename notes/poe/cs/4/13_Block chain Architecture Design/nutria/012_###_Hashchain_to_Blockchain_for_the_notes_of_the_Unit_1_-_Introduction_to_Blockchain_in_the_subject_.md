

### Hashchain to Blockchain for the Notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Blockchain in the Subject of Blockchain Architecture Design

- A hashchain is a chain of data blocks that are linked together using cryptographic hashing algorithms. Each block contains a cryptographic hash of the previous block, a timestamp, and transaction data. 
- The hashchain is used to create a secure and immutable ledger of transactions. It is used in many applications such as cryptocurrency, digital signatures, and electronic voting.
- Blockchain is an evolution of the hashchain concept. It is a distributed ledger technology that uses a peer-to-peer network to store and manage data. 
- Blockchain is composed of blocks that are linked together using cryptographic hashing algorithms. Each block contains a cryptographic hash of the previous block, a timestamp, and transaction data. 
- The blocks are secured by cryptographic algorithms, which makes it difficult to alter the data stored in the blocks. 
- The blockchain is used to create a secure and immutable ledger of transactions. It is used in many applications such as cryptocurrency, digital signatures, and electronic voting.
