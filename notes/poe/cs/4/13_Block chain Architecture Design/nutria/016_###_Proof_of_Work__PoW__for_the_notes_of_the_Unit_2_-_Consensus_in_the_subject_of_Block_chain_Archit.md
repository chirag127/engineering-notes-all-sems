
### Proof of Work (PoW) for the notes of the Unit 2 - Consensus in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

- Proof of Work (PoW) is a consensus algorithm which is used to validate transactions and produce new blocks on a blockchain. 
- It requires miners to solve complex mathematical puzzles in order to validate transactions and produce new blocks. 
- PoW is a computationally intensive process and requires a lot of energy to solve the puzzles. 
- The miners who solve the puzzles first are rewarded with a certain amount of cryptocurrency. 
- PoW is a secure and reliable consensus algorithm as it prevents double spending and other malicious activities on the network. 
- It is also a decentralized system as the miners are spread across the network and no single entity controls the network. 
- However, PoW is not energy efficient and requires a lot of electricity to run the miners. 
- It is also vulnerable to 51% attacks, where a single entity can control the majority of the network. 
- PoW is used in many popular cryptocurrencies such as Bitcoin, Ethereum, Litecoin, and many others.
