
### Permissions for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Blockchain in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

- Permissions in blockchain architecture design refer to the rights and privileges given to users to access and interact with the blockchain network. 
- Permissions can be divided into two categories: permissionless and permissioned. 
- Permissionless blockchains are public blockchains, where anyone can join the network and participate in the consensus process. Bitcoin and Ethereum are examples of permissionless blockchains. 
- Permissioned blockchains, on the other hand, require users to be granted permission to access the network. These networks are often used in enterprise settings, where the participants are known and trusted. 
- Permissions can also be divided into read and write permissions. Read permissions allow users to view the contents of the blockchain, while write permissions allow users to write data to the blockchain. 
- In permissioned blockchains, users are typically granted different levels of access depending on their role in the network. For example, a company may grant its employees read-only access to the blockchain, while only allowing its executives to write data to the blockchain. 
- Mnemonics and learning tricks for permissions in blockchain architecture design include: 
    - P for Permissions 
    - P for Public (permissionless) 
    - P for Private (permissioned)
    - R for Read 
    - W for Write 
    - L for Levels of Access
