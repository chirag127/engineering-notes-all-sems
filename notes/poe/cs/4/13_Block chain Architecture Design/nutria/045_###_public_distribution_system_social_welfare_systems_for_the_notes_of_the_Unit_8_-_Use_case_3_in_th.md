
### Public Distribution System Social Welfare Systems for the Notes of the Unit 8 - Use Case 3 in the Subject of Block Chain Architecture Design

* Public Distribution System (PDS) is a government-run system used to distribute basic necessities such as food and clothing to those in need.
* The use case for PDS in the context of Block Chain Architecture Design is to ensure the secure and efficient distribution of these necessities.
* The system works by using a distributed ledger technology (DLT) to track the distribution of goods and services.
* The benefits of using DLT for PDS include:
  * Increased transparency and accountability in the system, as all transactions are recorded on the ledger.
  * Improved security, as all transactions are cryptographically secured.
  * Improved efficiency, as the system can process transactions quickly and securely.
* The system also has some potential drawbacks, such as the need for a trusted third party to manage the ledger and the possibility of data tampering.
* Mnemonics and learning tricks for PDS in the context of Block Chain Architecture Design include:
  * PDS: Public Distribution System 
  * DLT: Distributed Ledger Technology 
  * TTP: Trusted Third Party 
  * DTA: Data Tampering Attack
