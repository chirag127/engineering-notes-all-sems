
## Unit 1 - Introduction to Blockchain

Blockchain is a distributed ledger technology that enables secure and immutable transactions over a network. It is a decentralized system that stores a continuously growing list of records, called blocks, which are linked and secured using cryptography. Each block contains a cryptographic hash of the previous block, a timestamp, and transaction data.

### Mnemonics and Learning Tricks

* BLOCK: Blockchain Ledger Of Cryptographically-secured Transactions
* CHAIN: Cryptographically Hashed And Immutable Network

### Advantages

* Increased security: Blockchain technology is highly secure and virtually impossible to tamper with.
* Increased transparency: All transactions on a blockchain are visible and traceable, providing increased transparency.
* Reduced costs: Blockchain technology reduces the need for intermediaries and their associated costs.

### Disadvantages

* Scalability: Scalability is a major issue for blockchain technology, as the number of transactions a blockchain can process is limited.
* Privacy: Although blockchain technology is secure and transparent, it does not provide the same level of privacy as traditional systems.
* Complexity: Blockchain technology is complex and requires a certain level of technical expertise to use.

### Applications

* Cryptocurrencies: Blockchain technology is the underlying technology behind cryptocurrencies such as Bitcoin and Ethereum.
* Smart contracts: Smart contracts are self-executing contracts that are stored on a blockchain and executed automatically when certain conditions are met.
* Supply chain management: Blockchain technology can be used to track the movement of goods through a supply chain in real-time.
