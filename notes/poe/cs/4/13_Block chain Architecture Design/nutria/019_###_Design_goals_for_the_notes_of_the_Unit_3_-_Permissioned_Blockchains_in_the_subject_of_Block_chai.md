
### Design goals for the notes of the Unit 3 - Permissioned Blockchains in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

- Permissioned blockchains are designed to be secure and reliable, while providing a platform for users to trust and transact with each other.
- The primary design goal of a permissioned blockchain is to create a secure and reliable environment for users to interact with each other in a trustless manner.
- The design of a permissioned blockchain should ensure that transactions are valid and secure, while also providing a platform for users to trust each other and transact without the need for a third-party intermediary.
- Permissioned blockchains should also provide a platform for users to securely store their data, as well as provide a platform for users to securely exchange digital assets.
- Additionally, permissioned blockchains should provide users with the ability to control access to their data, as well as the ability to audit the data in order to ensure the integrity of the system.
- Finally, the design of a permissioned blockchain should also provide a platform for users to securely store their private keys, as well as provide a platform for users to securely store their digital assets.
