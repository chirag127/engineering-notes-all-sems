
### Capital markets for the notes of the Unit 6 - Use case 1 in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

Capital markets are a key part of the global financial system. They enable companies to raise funds for investment and enable investors to access a wide range of assets. In the context of blockchain architecture design, capital markets are particularly relevant as they provide an efficient way to raise funds for projects that require large amounts of capital.

Capital markets are characterized by their large size, complexity, and high liquidity. They are typically divided into two main categories: primary markets and secondary markets. Primary markets are where new securities are issued and sold to investors, while secondary markets are where existing securities are traded.

In the context of blockchain architecture design, capital markets are particularly relevant as they provide an efficient way to raise funds for projects that require large amounts of capital. For example, companies can issue tokens to investors in exchange for capital. These tokens can then be traded on secondary markets, providing liquidity to the project.

In addition, capital markets provide investors with access to a wide range of assets, including stocks, bonds, and derivatives. This allows investors to diversify their portfolios and access different asset classes.
