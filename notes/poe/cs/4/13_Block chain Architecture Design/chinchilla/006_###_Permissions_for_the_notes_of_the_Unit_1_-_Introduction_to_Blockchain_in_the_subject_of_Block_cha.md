### Permissions for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Blockchain in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

In the study of blockchain architecture design, it is important to understand the various permissions granted to different entities in the blockchain network. These permissions determine who has the authority to perform certain actions and make changes to the blockchain. Here are some key permissions that you should be familiar with:

1. **Read permission**: This permission allows users to view the contents of the blockchain. It is the most basic permission and is usually granted to all users of the network.

2. **Write permission**: This permission allows users to add new transactions to the blockchain. Users with write permission can create new blocks and add them to the blockchain.

3. **Execute permission**: This permission allows users to execute smart contracts on the blockchain. Smart contracts are self-executing programs that can automate the execution of certain business logic.

4. **Admin permission**: This permission is usually reserved for network administrators who are responsible for managing the blockchain network. Users with admin permission can add or remove users, update network settings, and perform other administrative tasks.

5. **Ownership permission**: This permission is granted to the owner of a particular asset on the blockchain. For example, the owner of a cryptocurrency token has ownership permission over that token and can transfer it to other users.

Here are some mnemonics and learning tricks that can help you remember these permissions:

- **RAWEO**: This mnemonic stands for Read, Add, Write, Execute, and Owner. It can help you remember the five key permissions in the blockchain network.

- **WADEO**: This is another mnemonic that focuses on the four main permissions of Write, Add, Delete, and Execute.

- **Think of permissions as keys**: Just like how different keys unlock different doors, different permissions grant access to different actions on the blockchain network.

By understanding these permissions and their associated actions, you will be better equipped to navigate the blockchain network and understand how different users interact with it.
