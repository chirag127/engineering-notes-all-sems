### Blockchain in Trade/Supply Chain for the Notes of Unit 7 - Use Case 2 in the Subject of Blockchain Architecture Design

The use of blockchain technology in trade and supply chain management is gaining popularity due to its ability to provide a secure, transparent, and efficient solution for the exchange of goods and services. In this unit, we will explore the various aspects of blockchain in trade/supply chain management.

#### What is Trade/Supply Chain Management?

Trade/supply chain management involves the coordination and management of various activities involved in the production, transportation, and distribution of goods and services from the point of origin to the point of consumption. It includes activities such as procurement, inventory management, logistics, and distribution.

#### How Blockchain is Used in Trade/Supply Chain Management?

Blockchain technology can be used in trade/supply chain management in various ways:

1. Traceability: Blockchain can provide end-to-end traceability of products, from the point of origin to the point of consumption. This can help in ensuring the authenticity and quality of the products, preventing fraud and counterfeiting, and improving the efficiency of the supply chain.

2. Smart Contracts: Smart contracts can be used to automate various processes involved in trade/supply chain management, such as payment processing, quality control, and dispute resolution. This can help in reducing the time and cost involved in these processes.

3. Transparency: Blockchain provides a transparent and tamper-proof record of all transactions involved in the supply chain. This can improve the trust and collaboration between the various stakeholders involved in the supply chain.

#### Advantages of Using Blockchain in Trade/Supply Chain Management

The advantages of using blockchain in trade/supply chain management include:

1. Increased transparency and traceability of products
2. Improved efficiency and reduced costs
3. Enhanced security and reduced risk of fraud and counterfeiting
4. Improved collaboration and trust between the various stakeholders involved in the supply chain

#### Examples of Blockchain in Trade/Supply Chain Management

Some of the examples of blockchain in trade/supply chain management include:

1. IBM Food Trust: IBM Food Trust uses blockchain technology to provide end-to-end traceability of food products, from the point of origin to the point of consumption.

2. Walmart: Walmart is using blockchain technology to track the supply chain of fresh produce, ensuring quality and reducing waste.

3. Maersk: Maersk is using blockchain technology to automate various processes involved in the shipping industry, such as insurance claims and customs clearance.
