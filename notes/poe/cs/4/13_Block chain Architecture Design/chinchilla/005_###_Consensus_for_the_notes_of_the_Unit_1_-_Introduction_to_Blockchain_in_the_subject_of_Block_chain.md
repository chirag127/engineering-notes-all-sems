### Consensus for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Blockchain in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

Consensus is a critical aspect of blockchain technology that ensures the validity and integrity of transactions recorded on the blockchain. Consensus algorithms are used to ensure that all nodes on the network agree on the current state of the blockchain. In this section, we will discuss the various consensus algorithms used in blockchain technology.

#### 1. Proof of Work (PoW)

- PoW is the most widely used consensus algorithm and is used by Bitcoin and several other cryptocurrencies.
- In PoW, miners compete to solve a complex mathematical puzzle, and the first miner to solve the puzzle gets to add the next block to the blockchain.
- This process is computationally intensive and requires a lot of energy and computing power.
- The security of the blockchain is directly proportional to the amount of computational power dedicated to solving the puzzle.

#### 2. Proof of Stake (PoS)

- In PoS, validators are chosen to add new blocks to the blockchain based on the amount of cryptocurrency they hold.
- The more cryptocurrency a validator holds, the more likely they are to be chosen to add the next block.
- PoS is less energy-intensive than PoW and is used by several cryptocurrencies such as Ethereum.

#### 3. Delegated Proof of Stake (DPoS)

- In DPoS, token holders vote for a set of delegates who are responsible for validating transactions and adding new blocks to the blockchain.
- The delegates are chosen based on the number of votes they receive.
- DPoS is used by several blockchain platforms such as EOS and BitShares.

#### 4. Byzantine Fault Tolerance (BFT)

- BFT is a consensus algorithm that is used in permissioned blockchains.
- In BFT, a network of nodes reaches a consensus by exchanging messages and agreeing on the state of the blockchain.
- BFT is used in enterprise blockchain solutions such as Hyperledger Fabric.

#### 5. Practical Byzantine Fault Tolerance (PBFT)

- PBFT is an improvement over BFT and is used in permissioned blockchains.
- In PBFT, a network of nodes reaches a consensus by exchanging messages and agreeing on the state of the blockchain.
- PBFT is used in enterprise blockchain solutions such as Hyperledger Fabric.

#### 6. Proof of Authority (PoA)

- In PoA, validators are chosen based on their identity and reputation rather than their computational power or cryptocurrency holdings.
- PoA is used in private and consortium blockchains.

Mnemonic: 
- PoW: "Prove your Worth" - miners prove their worth through computational power
- PoS: "Proof of Stake" - validators prove their stake through the amount of cryptocurrency they hold
- DPoS: "Delegates with Power" - delegates are chosen based on the number of votes they receive
- BFT: "Byzantine Fault Tolerance" - used in permissioned blockchains
- PBFT: "Practical Byzantine Fault Tolerance" - an improvement over BFT
- PoA: "Proof of Authority" - validators are chosen based on their identity and reputation.
