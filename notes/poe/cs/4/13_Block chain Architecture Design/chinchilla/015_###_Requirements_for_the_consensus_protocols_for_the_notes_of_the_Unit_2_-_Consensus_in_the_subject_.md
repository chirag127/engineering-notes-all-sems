### Requirements for the Consensus Protocols for the Notes of the Unit 2 - Consensus in the Subject of Blockchain Architecture Design

Consensus protocols play a vital role in ensuring the integrity and security of a blockchain network. These protocols enable nodes in a distributed network to agree on a single version of the truth, even in the presence of malicious actors. In this unit, we will explore the different consensus protocols used in blockchain networks and their requirements. 

The following are the requirements for consensus protocols:

1. **Safety**: Consensus protocols should ensure that once a block is added to the blockchain, it cannot be modified, deleted, or rolled back. This requirement ensures the integrity of the blockchain and prevents double-spending and other malicious activities.

2. **Liveness**: Consensus protocols should ensure that the network can always reach a consensus, even in the presence of malicious actors or network failures. This requirement ensures that the blockchain network remains functional and can continue to process transactions.

3. **Decentralization**: Consensus protocols should ensure that no single entity has too much power or control over the network. This requirement ensures that the network is resilient to attacks and maintains its decentralized nature.

4. **Scalability**: Consensus protocols should be able to handle a large number of transactions and nodes without compromising the safety and liveness requirements. This requirement ensures that the blockchain network can scale to meet the demands of its users.

5. **Efficiency**: Consensus protocols should be able to reach a consensus quickly and with minimal computational resources. This requirement ensures that the network can process transactions efficiently and without incurring high costs.

Mnemonics and learning tricks:

One way to remember the requirements for consensus protocols is to use the acronym S.L.E.D.E.:

- S - Safety
- L - Liveness
- E - Efficiency
- D - Decentralization
- E - Scalability

Another way is to use the phrase "SLED is on the ice" to remember the acronym.

In summary, consensus protocols are critical for the functioning and security of blockchain networks. To ensure the safety, liveness, decentralization, scalability, and efficiency of these networks, consensus protocols must meet the above requirements. Remembering the acronym S.L.E.D.E. or the phrase "SLED is on the ice" can help you recall these requirements for exams and other learning purposes.
