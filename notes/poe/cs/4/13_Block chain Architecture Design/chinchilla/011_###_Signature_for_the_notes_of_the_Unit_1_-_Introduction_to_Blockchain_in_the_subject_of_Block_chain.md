### Signature for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Blockchain in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

In the world of blockchain, a signature plays a crucial role in ensuring the authenticity and integrity of the data. A signature is a digital representation of an individual's identity that can be used to sign and verify transactions on the blockchain. In this Unit, we will discuss the signature used in the blockchain and its importance in the architecture design of blockchain.

#### Public Key Cryptography

The signature used in blockchain is based on public key cryptography. It involves the use of two keys, a public key and a private key. The public key is used to encrypt the data, while the private key is used to decrypt the data. The private key is kept secret by the owner and is used to sign the transaction.

#### Digital Signature

A digital signature is created by combining the message to be signed with the private key of the signer. The resulting signature is unique and can be verified using the public key of the signer. A digital signature ensures the authenticity and integrity of the data.

#### Mnemonics

To remember the signature for the notes of Unit 1 - Introduction to Blockchain, you can use the following mnemonics:

- PKC: Public Key Cryptography
- DS: Digital Signature
- PP: Private key to sign, Public key to verify

#### Advantages of Signature

The signature used in the blockchain has various advantages, such as:

- Authenticity and Integrity: The signature ensures that the data is authentic and has not been tampered with.
- Non-repudiation: The signature ensures that the signer cannot deny signing the transaction.
- Security: The signature ensures that the data is secure and cannot be accessed by unauthorized parties.
