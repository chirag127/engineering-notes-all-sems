### Design goals for the notes of the Unit 3 - Permissioned Blockchains in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

Permissioned blockchains are becoming increasingly popular in industries where privacy and control are essential. These types of blockchains allow for a select group of individuals or organizations to have access to the network, ensuring that only trusted parties can validate transactions and maintain the integrity of the ledger. In this unit, we will discuss the design goals for permissioned blockchains and explore their unique features.

The following are the design goals for the notes of the Unit 3 - Permissioned Blockchains:

1. Security - Security is one of the primary design goals for permissioned blockchains. The network must be secure and protected from attacks, ensuring that only authorized parties can access the network. This is achieved through various security measures such as encryption, authentication, and authorization.

2. Privacy - Privacy is another critical design goal for permissioned blockchains. As these blockchains are limited to a select group of individuals or organizations, it is essential to ensure that the data stored on the blockchain is only visible to authorized parties. This is achieved through various privacy measures such as cryptographic techniques and data obfuscation.

3. Performance - Performance is a crucial design goal for permissioned blockchains, as they need to be able to handle a high volume of transactions efficiently. This is achieved through various performance optimization techniques such as consensus algorithms, parallel processing, and sharding.

4. Governance - Governance is another essential design goal for permissioned blockchains. As these blockchains are limited to a select group of individuals or organizations, it is essential to have a governance model that ensures that the network is managed and operated in a fair and transparent manner. This is achieved through various governance mechanisms such as voting, consensus, and rulemaking.

5. Interoperability - Interoperability is a design goal that aims to ensure that permissioned blockchains can communicate with other blockchains and networks. This is essential for industries that require collaboration between different organizations or networks, such as supply chain management.

Mnemonic: SPPII (Security, Privacy, Performance, Governance, Interoperability)
