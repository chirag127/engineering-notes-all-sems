### Public Distribution System Social Welfare Systems

Public Distribution System (PDS) is a social welfare system that provides food, fuel, and other essential commodities to the poor and marginalized sections of society. It is a government-run program aimed at ensuring food security for all citizens. In this unit, we will discuss how blockchain technology can be used to improve the efficiency and transparency of PDS.

#### Overview of Public Distribution System

The PDS system was introduced to ensure that the poor and needy people get access to essential commodities at affordable prices. The government procures essential commodities like rice, wheat, sugar, etc., from farmers at a fixed price and distributes them to the people through a network of ration shops. The ration shops are operated by the government-appointed dealers who sell the commodities at subsidized prices to the people. The system is plagued by several issues like leakages, corruption, inefficiencies, etc., which affect the intended beneficiaries.

#### Problems with the Current System

The current PDS system is riddled with several issues, which include:

- Leakage of commodities: The commodities meant for the beneficiaries are often diverted and sold in the open market.
- Corruption: The dealers and officials indulge in corrupt practices like hoarding, black marketing, etc., which leads to the diversion of commodities.
- Inefficiencies: The system is plagued by inefficiencies like delays in procurement, transportation, and distribution, which lead to delays in the delivery of commodities.
- Lack of transparency: The beneficiaries are often unaware of the distribution of commodities, which leads to confusion and chaos.

#### How Blockchain can help?

Blockchain technology can help solve the problems of the PDS system by providing transparency, efficiency, and security. The following are some of the ways in which blockchain technology can be used in the PDS system:

- Traceability: Blockchain technology can be used to track the movement of commodities from the farmers to the ration shops. This will ensure that the commodities are not diverted and reach the intended beneficiaries.
- Transparency: Blockchain technology can be used to provide transparency in the distribution of commodities. The beneficiaries can view the transactions on the blockchain and ensure that they receive the commodities they are entitled to.
- Efficiency: Blockchain technology can be used to improve the efficiency of the system by reducing delays in procurement, transportation, and distribution.
- Security: Blockchain technology can be used to provide security to the system by ensuring that the transactions are tamper-proof and cannot be altered.

In conclusion, the PDS system is an important social welfare system that aims to provide essential commodities to the poor and marginalized sections of society. However, the system is plagued with several issues, which affect its intended beneficiaries. Blockchain technology can be used to solve these problems by providing transparency, efficiency, and security to the system.
