### Basic Crypto Primitives for the Notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Blockchain in the Subject of Blockchain Architecture Design

In the world of blockchain, cryptography plays a significant role in ensuring the security and integrity of the data stored in the blockchain. Therefore, it is essential to have a fundamental understanding of the basic crypto primitives used in blockchain technology. In this section, we will cover some of the basic crypto primitives that are essential to understand for the notes of Unit 1 - Introduction to Blockchain in the subject of Blockchain Architecture Design.

1. Hash Functions:
   - A hash function is a mathematical function that takes an input (also known as a message) and produces a fixed-size output (also known as a hash value or digest). 
   - The hash function is a one-way function, which means that it is easy to compute the hash value for a given input, but it is practically impossible to generate the original input from the hash value. 
   - Hash functions are commonly used in blockchain technology to create a unique digital fingerprint of the data stored in a block. 
   - The most commonly used hash functions in blockchain technology are SHA-256 and SHA-3.

2. Digital Signatures:
   - A digital signature is a mathematical scheme for verifying the authenticity of digital messages or documents. 
   - It uses a combination of public and private keys to provide authentication, integrity, and non-repudiation of the message. 
   - Digital signatures are commonly used in blockchain technology to verify the authenticity of transactions and ensure that they have not been tampered with. 
   - The most commonly used digital signature algorithm in blockchain technology is the Elliptic Curve Digital Signature Algorithm (ECDSA).

3. Public Key Cryptography:
   - Public key cryptography is a cryptographic system that uses a pair of keys, a public key, and a private key. 
   - The public key is freely available to anyone, while the private key is kept secret. 
   - Public key cryptography is commonly used in blockchain technology to provide secure communication between nodes and to ensure the integrity of transactions. 
   - The most commonly used public key cryptography algorithm in blockchain technology is the Elliptic Curve Cryptography (ECC) algorithm.

4. Merkle Trees:
   - A Merkle tree is a tree data structure in which each leaf node is labeled with the hash of a data block, and each non-leaf node is labeled with the hash of the labels of its child nodes. 
   - Merkle trees are commonly used in blockchain technology to efficiently verify that a particular transaction is included in a block without having to download the entire block. 
   - Merkle trees are also used in blockchain technology to ensure the integrity of the block by verifying that each transaction in the block is authentic.

These are the basic crypto primitives that are essential to understand for the notes of Unit 1 - Introduction to Blockchain in the subject of Blockchain Architecture Design. Understanding these primitives will help you understand the underlying security mechanisms of blockchain technology and how they work together to provide a secure and decentralized system.
