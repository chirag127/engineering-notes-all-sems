### Visibility for the Notes of Unit 7 - Use Case 2 in the Subject of Blockchain Architecture Design

Visibility is a critical aspect of any blockchain system as it provides insight into the actions of various participants on the network. In the context of Use Case 2 in the subject of Blockchain Architecture Design, visibility is essential as it enables stakeholders to monitor the flow of goods and services across the supply chain.

Here are some key points to consider when studying visibility in the context of Use Case 2:

1. What is Visibility? 
Visibility refers to the ability of stakeholders to track the movement of goods and services across the supply chain. This includes tracking the location of goods, their condition, and any changes that occur during their transportation.

2. The Importance of Visibility 
Visibility is critical in Use Case 2 as it enables stakeholders to monitor the supply chain in real-time, identify potential issues or delays, and mitigate them before they become significant problems. It also helps to build trust between parties by providing a transparent view of the entire supply chain.

3. How is Visibility Achieved in Blockchain? 
Blockchain provides a transparent and tamper-proof record of all transactions that occur on the network. This means that stakeholders can easily track the movement of goods and services across the supply chain, as each transaction is recorded on the blockchain. Additionally, blockchain can be integrated with other technologies such as IoT sensors to provide real-time updates on the location and condition of goods.

4. Mnemonic for Understanding Visibility 
One useful mnemonic for remembering the importance of visibility in Use Case 2 is "See it to believe it." This emphasizes the importance of having a transparent and visible supply chain to build trust and mitigate potential issues.

In conclusion, visibility is a critical aspect of any blockchain system, and it is particularly important in Use Case 2 in the subject of Blockchain Architecture Design. By providing stakeholders with a transparent view of the supply chain, blockchain enables real-time monitoring, issue identification, and trust-building between parties.
