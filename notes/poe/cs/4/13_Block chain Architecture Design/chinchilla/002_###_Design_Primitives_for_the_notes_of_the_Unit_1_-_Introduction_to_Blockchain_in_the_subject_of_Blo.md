### Design Primitives for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Blockchain in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

Blockchain is a decentralized digital ledger technology that enables secure and transparent transactions between parties without the need for intermediaries. The blockchain architecture comprises several design primitives that determine its functionality and effectiveness. In this section, we will discuss the key design primitives of blockchain architecture that are crucial for understanding blockchain technology.

1. Consensus Mechanism: This primitive is responsible for validating transactions and ensuring that all nodes in the network agree on the state of the ledger. It is achieved through a consensus algorithm that enables nodes to agree on the order of transactions and the state of the ledger. Some popular consensus mechanisms include Proof of Work (PoW), Proof of Stake (PoS), and Delegated Proof of Stake (DPoS).

2. Smart Contracts: Smart contracts are self-executing contracts that contain the terms and conditions of an agreement between two or more parties. They are stored on the blockchain and can be executed automatically when certain conditions are met. Smart contracts enable trustless transactions and eliminate the need for intermediaries, making them a crucial design primitive in blockchain architecture.

3. Cryptography: Cryptography is used in blockchain architecture to ensure the security and privacy of transactions. It involves the use of mathematical algorithms to encrypt and decrypt data, making it difficult for unauthorized users to access or modify information on the blockchain.

4. Distributed Ledger Technology: The distributed ledger technology (DLT) enables the decentralized storage of transaction data across multiple nodes in the network. It ensures the immutability and transparency of transactions by making it difficult for any single node to modify the ledger without the consensus of other nodes.

5. Peer-to-Peer Networking: Peer-to-peer (P2P) networking enables nodes in the network to communicate and share data with each other without the need for a central server. It ensures that the network is decentralized and enables faster and more efficient communication between nodes.

6. Incentive Mechanisms: Incentive mechanisms are used to encourage nodes in the network to participate in the validation of transactions and the maintenance of the blockchain. They are usually in the form of rewards, such as cryptocurrency, and ensure that the network remains secure and functional.

Mnemonics and learning tricks:
- To remember the different consensus mechanisms, try remembering the acronym "PPD" which stands for Proof of Work, Proof of Stake, and Delegated Proof of Stake.
- To remember the role of cryptography, try remembering the phrase "Cryptography secures the chain" where "chain" refers to the blockchain.
- To remember the role of smart contracts, try remembering the phrase "Contracts that execute themselves" which highlights their self-executing nature.
