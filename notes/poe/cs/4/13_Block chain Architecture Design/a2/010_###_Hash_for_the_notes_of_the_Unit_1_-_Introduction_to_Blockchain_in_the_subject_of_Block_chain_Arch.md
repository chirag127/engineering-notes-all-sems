### Hash for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Blockchain in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

1. Blockchain is a distributed ledger that maintains a continuously growing list of records called blocks. Each block contains a timestamp and a link to the previous block. By design, blockchain is resistant to modification of the data. So the data in it is tamper-proof and immutable.

2. The blockchain is a decentralized, distributed, and public digital ledger that underlies cryptocurrencies like Bitcoin. The ledger is distributed across many nodes in a peer-to-peer network. The nodes constantly verify and reconcile transactions via the network. This makes the records tamper-resistant and transparent.

3. The blockchain is a distributed consensus system. It achieves consensus without a central authority. The consensus is achieved through Proof-of-Work or Proof-of-Stake mechanisms. The consensus ensures that the nodes agree on the order and validity of the transactions. It also ensures that the ledger is consistent across all the nodes.

4. The key aspects of blockchain are decentralization, immutability, transparency, and security. Due to decentralization, there is no single point of failure and no entity controls the data. Immutability ensures that the data cannot be altered or removed. The ledger is transparent as the data is visible to all the nodes. And the use of cryptographic hashes and digital signatures provides security to the blockchain.

5. Some of the applications of blockchain are cryptocurrencies, smart contracts, supply chain management, health records, etc. Blockchain has the potential to disrupt many digital transactions and processes. It can bring more transparency, security, and decentralization to various use cases. However, there are also scalability, interoperability, and energy consumption challenges with blockchain that need to be addressed.
