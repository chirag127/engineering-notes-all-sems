### Design goals for the notes of the Unit 3 - Permissioned Blockchains in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

The design goals for the notes on Permissioned Blockchains are:

1. To understand the basic concept of Permissioned Blockchains - Permissioned Blockchains are private blockchains where the participants are known and trusted. Only authorized nodes are allowed to participate in the consensus process. The consensus process can be simpler than public blockchains as the nodes are known. Examples include Hyperledger Fabric, R3 Corda, Quorum, etc.

2. To learn the advantages of Permissioned Blockchains - Some key advantages are faster transaction times, ability to use simpler consensus mechanisms leading to higher throughput, support for privacy and confidential transactions, support for regulatory compliance, etc.

3. To study the disadvantages of Permissioned Blockchains - Some disadvantages are less decentralization as only authorized nodes can participate, single organization controls the blockchain, more prone to security risks and attacks as the nodes are known, scaling limitations, etc.

4. To understand the components and working of a sample Permissioned Blockchain platform like Hyperledger Fabric - Cover topics like peers, orderers, channels, chaincode, etc. and how transactions are executed and consensus is achieved.

5. To explore some example use cases and applications of Permissioned Blockchains - For example, supply chain management, financial transactions, medical records, etc. where the participants are known and there are advantages of faster transactions and regulatory compliance.
