## Unit 8 - Use case 3

**Use case 3:** The system shall allow users to track stock levels of products and trigger reorder alerts.

- This use case allows the inventory management system to keep track of stock levels of products.
- The system maintains an inventory list of all products with their current stock quantity.
- As products are sold or used, the system decrements the stock quantity.
- Once the stock quantity reaches a predefined reorder level, the system triggers a reorder alert for procurement managers.
- The reorder alert can be in the form of an email notification or an in-app alert to notify the managers that it is time to place orders to replenish the stocks.
- Tracking stock levels and triggering timely alerts helps maintain optimum inventory and avoids stockouts which can lead to lost sales.
- *Mnemonic:* "Alerts for low stocks, no sales shocks!"

**Advantages:**

- Keeps inventory costs low by avoiding excess stocks.
- Prevents stockouts through timely alerts.
- Helps maintain optimum inventory levels.
- Increases sales by ensuring availability of products.

**Disadvantages:**

- Additional overheads to configure reorder alerts and monitoring stock levels.
- Risk of delays or errors in reordering which can still lead to stockouts.
- Frequency of alerts needs to be finely tuned to avoid excess alerts.
