### Public Distribution System Social Welfare Systems for the notes of the Unit 8 - Use case 3 in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

- A public distribution system (PDS) is a system established by the government to distribute essential commodities to the poor at subsidized rates. The commodities distributed include food grains, sugar, kerosene, etc.
- The block chain can be used to build a fair and efficient PDS by maintaining records of beneficiaries and entitlements on the block chain. This will reduce leakages and ensure timely delivery of supplies to actual beneficiaries.
- The block chain will maintain records of beneficiaries mapped to their Aadhaar numbers along with details of entitlements. These records will be transparent and tamper-proof.
- Supplies can be tracked from warehouses to ration shops to beneficiaries using the block chain. This will reduce diversion and black marketing of supplies.
- Smart contracts can be used to automate the process of distribution and payment. Once the beneficiary's entitlement is verified, the smart contract can facilitate automatic release and recording of supplies. This will reduce delays and human intervention.
- However, the use of block chain will require investment in infrastructure to implement the technology. There may also be challenges in getting all stakeholders on board the block chain-based system. Extensive training and capacity building will be required to adopt the technology.
- The key advantages of a block chain-based PDS would be reduction in leakages and corruption, timely delivery of supplies, transparent records, and automated processes. It has the potential to make PDS more efficient and effective in serving the poor.
