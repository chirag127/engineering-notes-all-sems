### Design Primitives for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Blockchain in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

1. Peer-to-Peer Network: Blockchain is a peer-to-peer network where the nodes in the network connect and communicate with each other directly. There is no central authority or centralized server. The nodes relay messages to the network and verify transactions through consensus.

Mnemonic: P2P like friends chatting with each other directly without going through any third person.

2. Distributed Ledger: The blockchain is a distributed ledger that maintains a continuously growing list of ordered records called blocks. The ledger is distributed and decentralized across many nodes in the peer-to-peer network. The distributed nature makes the records more transparent, secure, consistent and fault-tolerant.

Mnemonic: distributed ledger like maintaining accounts distributed across many friends rather than one person. More secure and transparent.

3. Consensus Mechanism: Since there is no central authority, the network uses consensus mechanisms to verify and validate transactions and to maintain consistency across the distributed ledger. The consensus ensures that the system can achieve agreement despite failures and adversarial manipulation. Some popular consensus mechanisms are Proof-of-Work, Proof-of-Stake, etc.

Mnemonic: Consensus like friends agreeing on something through discussion. Blockchain networks achieve consensus through algorithms.

4. Cryptography: Cryptographic techniques are used to ensure the integrity and security of the blockchain. Some of the cryptographic primitives used are digital signatures, hash functions, encryption schemes, etc. They are used for validation of transactions, proof of ownership, etc.

Mnemonic: Cryptography like encoding messages to conceal and secure them. Blockchain uses crypto to secure and validate transactions and data.
