### Hashchain to Blockchain for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Blockchain in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

- A hashchain is a sequence of blocks where each block contains the hash of the previous block. This forms a chain linking the blocks together through these hashes.
- The blockchain is a distributed ledger that maintains a continuously growing list of records called blocks. Each block contains a timestamp and a link to the previous block. By design, blockchains are resistant to modification of the data. This makes the blockchain suitable for recording events, medical records, and other records that need to be immutable and verifiable.
- The hashchain is a simple predecessor to the blockchain. The blockchain makes the hashchain more robust and secure by decentralizing it and using proof-of-work to achieve consensus on the transaction records and the order of the blocks in the chain. This makes the blockchain tamper-resistant and immutable.
- Mnemonics: "Hash links blocks" - remembers that hashes link blocks in a hashchain; "Blockchain is distributed, immutable, consensus-driven hashchain" - captures key properties of the blockchain building on the hashchain.
- The blockchain's key advantages are decentralization, immutability, consensus, and security. The key disadvantages are scalability issues and high energy consumption due to proof-of-work. The blockchain has applications in cryptocurrencies, smart contracts, and asset management.
