### Insurance for the notes of the Unit 6 - Use case 1 in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

- Block chain can be used in insurance to prevent fraud and increase transparency. Each transaction and claim can be recorded immutably on the block chain. This will provide an authoritative and permanent record of all the transactions which can be useful for regulatory purposes as well.
- Smart contracts can be used to automate the insurance process. The conditions and payouts of an insurance policy can be written in the form of a smart contract. Once the conditions are met the payout will be executed automatically without any manual intervention. This can speed up the claim process and reduce opportunities for fraud.
- A decentralized block chain based system will eliminate the need for an intermediary insurance company. Individuals can get into smart contracts with each other to insure against certain risks. The premiums and payouts will be handled via the smart contract in a transparent manner. However, this model may not scale well and individual consumers may not have the expertise to deal with complex insurance situations.
- Some of the advantages of block chain based insurance are - reduced fraud, faster claims, reduced administrative overhead, and increased transparency. However, the technology is still new and there are regulatory uncertainties. Widespread adoption would also require collaboration between insurance companies and a critical mass of users.
