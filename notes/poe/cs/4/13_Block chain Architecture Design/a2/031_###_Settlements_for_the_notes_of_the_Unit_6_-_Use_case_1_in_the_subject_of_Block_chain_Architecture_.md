### Settlements for the notes of the Unit 6 - Use case 1 in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

The settlements for the notes of Unit 6 - Use case 1 in Block chain Architecture Design are:

1. Blockchain network - The blockchain network consists of nodes that maintain a distributed ledger. The nodes continuously synchronize the ledger with each other and reach consensus on the valid transactions to be added to the ledger.
2. Transactions - Transactions are records of value/asset exchange between two parties added to the blockchain. They are broadcast to the network, verified and included in blocks that get added to the blockchain.
3. Mining - Mining is the process of creating new blocks in the blockchain and adding them to the chain. Miners solve a cryptographic puzzle to verify and record transactions on the blockchain. The miner who solves the puzzle first gets to place the next block on the chain and claim the block reward.
4. Consensus - Consensus is a mechanism that the nodes in the blockchain network use to agree on the valid blockchain and the order of transactions. Different blockchains use different consensus mechanisms like Proof-of-Work, Proof-of-Stake, Byzantine Fault Tolerance, etc. to maintain consistency across the network.
5. Smart contracts - Smart contracts are programs stored on the blockchain that execute when certain conditions are met. They are used to automate the execution of agreements/transactions so that all parties can be immediately certain of the outcome, without any intermediary's involvement or time loss.

- Mining is solving puzzles to earn block rewards.
- Consensus is agreement between nodes.
