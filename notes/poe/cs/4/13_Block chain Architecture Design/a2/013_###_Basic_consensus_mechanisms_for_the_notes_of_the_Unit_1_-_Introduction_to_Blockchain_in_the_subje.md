### Basic consensus mechanisms for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Blockchain in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

- Proof of Work (PoW): Miners solve a complex cryptographic puzzle to get the right to add a block to the blockchain. The miner that is first to find the solution to the puzzle gets to place the next block on the blockchain and claim the rewards. The main drawback is high energy consumption. Mnemonics: Proof of burning cpu cycles.
- Proof of Stake (PoS): Miners (forgers) stake their coins as collateral to validate transactions and get mining rewards. The more stake a forger has, the higher the chances to validate transactions. Energy efficiency is higher but it may lead to centralization as only people with more money can stake more. Mnemonics: Proof of having skin in the game.
- Delegated Proof of Stake (DPoS): Delegates/witnesses are elected by stakeholders to validate transactions and maintain consensus. The delegates that get the minimum required votes get the chance to validate transactions in a round-robin fashion. Higher throughput but criticisms around centralization as few top voted delegates end up having significant control. Mnemonics: Voting based proof of stake.
- Practical Byzantine Fault Tolerance (pBFT): Tolerates Byzantine faults with high probability using replication of nodes and consensus based on quorums. Multiple rounds of consensus building ensure high transaction finality. Drawbacks include lower throughput and more complex implementation. Mnemonics: Consensus despite malicious nodes.

The consensus mechanisms have their own pros and cons in terms of security, scalability, decentralization, etc. The choice of a particular consensus mechanism depends on the requirements and use case of the blockchain application.
