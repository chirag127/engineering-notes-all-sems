### Invoice Management Discounting for the notes of the Unit 7 - Use case 2 in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

- Invoice discounting is a financing technique used by suppliers or vendors to convert their accounts receivable into cash. In blockchain, the invoice data can be stored in a tamper-proof way and the invoice can be discounted instantly based on the credit score of the buyer. This brings liquidity to the suppliers and speed to the process.
- The key participants in blockchain based invoice discounting are:
-- Suppliers or vendors who want early liquidity
-- Investors who can fund the invoices and earn interest
-- Buyers who will eventually pay the invoices
- The process works as:
-- Supplier uploads the invoice details to the blockchain
-- Investors verify the invoice and credit score of the buyer
-- If approved, investor pays a discounted amount to the supplier
-- Buyer pays the full invoice amount to the investor on due date
-- Investor returns the surplus amount to the supplier after deducting interest
- Benefits: Fast process, low fees, secure data, global accessibility
- Challenges: Need for scalability, interoperability, and standardization
- Example: Tradeshift uses blockchain for invoice discounting and supply chain finance
- Applications: All B2B payment scenarios with longer payment terms
