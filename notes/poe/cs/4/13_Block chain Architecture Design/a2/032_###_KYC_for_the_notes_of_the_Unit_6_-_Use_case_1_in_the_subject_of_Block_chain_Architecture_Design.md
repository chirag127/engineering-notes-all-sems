### KYC for the notes of the Unit 6 - Use case 1 in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

Know Your Customer (KYC) is the process of a business verifying the identity of its clients and assessing their suitability, along with the potential risks of illegal intentions towards the business relationship. The following are the key points to note about KYC for the given use case:

- KYC is done at the time of on-boarding of a customer onto the blockchain network. The customer has to provide necessary identity proof and address proof documents to verify the identity.
- The verified identity and proofs are stored on the blockchain network. This ensures a single source of truth for identity and prevents duplicate KYC's.
- The KYC status can be used to control access to certain blockchain features. For example, only KYC verified customers can transact above a certain transaction limit. This helps in maintaining compliance.
- Smart contracts can be used to encode KYC verification logic and control access to network resources. This makes the KYC process automated and tamper-proof.
- Since the KYC data is stored on the blockchain, it is immutable and permanent. This can be a disadvantage as well since dynamic changes to KYC cannot be incorporated easily. Mechanisms like time-bound proofs may be required.
- KYC combined with other blockchain features like anonymity can be tricky to implement. There has to be a balance between the two for practical applications.
