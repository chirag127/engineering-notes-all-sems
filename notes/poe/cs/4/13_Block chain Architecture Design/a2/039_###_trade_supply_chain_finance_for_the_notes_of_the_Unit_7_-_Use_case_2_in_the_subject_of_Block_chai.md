### Trade/Supply Chain Finance for the notes of the Unit 7 - Use case 2 in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

- Trade finance refers to the financing of trade transactions for traders and banks. It includes lending, documentary collection, and other financial services needed to facilitate smooth functioning of international trade.
- Supply chain finance refers to a set of solutions designed to optimize cash flow and reduce risks for suppliers, buyers, and banks. It leverages the supply chain relationships between businesses to provide financing and working capital.
- Blockchain can bring significant improvements to trade finance and supply chain finance through:
- Increased transparency: Blockchain provides an immutable, secure, and transparent ledger of all transactions which can reduce fraud and build trust between parties. All parties have a secure and consistent view of transactions and fund flows.
- Reduced paperwork: Blockchain can eliminate the need for paper-based documentation like invoices and shipping documents. This can speed up processes and reduce errors and fraud.
- Smart contracts: Blockchain enables smart contracts which can automatically release funds once certain conditions are met. This can speed up financing and reduce settlement times.
- Some examples of blockchain applications in trade/supply chain finance include we.trade, Skuchain, and ModulTrade.
- However, there are also some challenges and limitations like lack of widespread adoption, interoperability issues, and governance complexities that need to be addressed for blockchain to reach its full potential in trade/supply chain finance.
