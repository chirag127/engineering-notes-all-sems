
### Channel Structure for the Notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction, Issues in Mobile Computing, Overview of Wireless Telephony: Cellular Concept, GSM

* Cellular Concept: 
  * Cellular concept is a type of wireless communication system which divides a geographical area into small cells, each of which is served by at least one fixed-location transceiver, known as a base station. 
  * Each cell is assigned a unique frequency or code, allowing multiple users to share the same frequency in different cells. 
  * This concept enables the network to provide coverage over a larger area than a single transmitter, as well as reduce interference.
  
* Global System for Mobile (GSM): 
  * GSM is a digital mobile network that is used by mobile phone users around the world. 
  * It is the most widely used mobile phone standard and provides voice and data services over a variety of radio frequencies. 
  * GSM phones use SIM cards to store user data, such as phone numbers, text messages, and contact information. 
  * GSM also supports data services such as SMS, MMS, and internet access. 
  * GSM networks are divided into different frequency bands, and each band is allocated to a specific region. 
  * GSM networks are used in more than 200 countries around the world, making it the most widely used mobile phone standard.
