
## Unit 2 - Wireless Networking, Wireless LAN Overview: MAC issues, IEEE 802.11, Blue Tooth, Wireless

* **MAC Issues**: Media Access Control (MAC) is the protocol used by wireless networks to control access to the shared medium. It is responsible for determining which station has the right to transmit at any given time. The MAC protocol also handles error detection and recovery. 

* **IEEE 802.11**: IEEE 802.11 is a set of standards for wireless networks developed by the Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers (IEEE). It defines the physical and data link layers of wireless networks. The most common version of IEEE 802.11 is 802.11b, which operates in the 2.4GHz frequency band and provides a maximum data rate of 11 Mbps.

* **Bluetooth**: Bluetooth is a wireless technology used for exchanging data over short distances. It is based on the IEEE 802.15 standard and operates in the 2.4GHz frequency band. Bluetooth devices can connect to each other without the need for cables or wires.

* **Wireless**: Wireless networks use radio waves to transmit data over a network. They are typically used in mobile applications such as laptops, tablets and smartphones. Wireless networks are usually based on IEEE 802.11 standards. They provide greater flexibility and mobility than wired networks.
