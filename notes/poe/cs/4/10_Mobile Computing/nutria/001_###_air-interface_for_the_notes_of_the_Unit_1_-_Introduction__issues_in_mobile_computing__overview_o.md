
# Air-Interface for the Notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction, Issues in Mobile Computing, Overview of Wireless Telephony: Cellular Concept, GSM: in the Subject of Mobile Computing

* **Air-Interface** is the physical connection between a mobile device and the wireless network. It is responsible for controlling the transmission of data between the two.

* **Cellular Concept** is the idea of dividing a geographic area into small cells, which are each served by a base station. This allows for multiple users to access the same network at the same time, as each user is assigned to a different cell.

* **GSM** (Global System for Mobile Communications) is the most widely used standard for mobile communications. It is used in over 200 countries, and is the basis for many other mobile communication standards.

* **Advantages of GSM** include its wide coverage area, its ability to support multiple users, and its ability to support data transmission.

* **Disadvantages of GSM** include its limited range, its limited data rate, and its susceptibility to interference.

* **Applications of GSM** include mobile banking, mobile commerce, and mobile telephony.
