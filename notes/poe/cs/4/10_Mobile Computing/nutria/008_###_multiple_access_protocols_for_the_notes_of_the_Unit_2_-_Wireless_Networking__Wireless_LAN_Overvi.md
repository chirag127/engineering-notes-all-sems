
### Multiple Access Protocols for the Notes of the Unit 2 - Wireless Networking, Wireless LAN Overview: MAC Issues, IEEE 802.11, Blue Tooth, Wireless in the Subject of Mobile Computing

* Multiple access protocols are used to control the way multiple devices access a shared medium such as a wireless network.
* The most common multiple access protocols are:
  * **IEEE 802.11**: This is the most widely used multiple access protocol for wireless networks. It is the basis for Wi-Fi networks and is used for both local area networks (LANs) and wide area networks (WANs).
* **Bluetooth**: This is a short-range wireless communication protocol used for connecting devices such as headsets, keyboards, and mice.
* **Wireless in the Subject of Mobile Computing**: This is a protocol used for connecting mobile devices such as smartphones and tablets to a wireless network.

Mnemonics and Learning Tricks:
* IEEE 802.11: The acronym stands for Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers 802.11. 
* Bluetooth: The name comes from the 10th century Danish king, Harald Bluetooth.
* Wireless in the Subject of Mobile Computing: Think of the acronym “WIMP” for wireless in the subject of mobile computing.
