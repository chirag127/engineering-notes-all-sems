
# Protocol Stack for the Notes of the Unit 2 - Wireless Networking, Wireless LAN Overview: MAC Issues, IEEE 802.11, Bluetooth, Wireless in the Subject of Mobile Computing

* **MAC Issues**: Media Access Control (MAC) is the protocol layer responsible for regulating access to the physical medium in a wireless network. It is responsible for determining when a device can transmit and receive data over the network. The MAC layer is responsible for error detection, flow control, and collision avoidance.

* **IEEE 802.11**: The IEEE 802.11 is a set of standards developed by the Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers (IEEE) for wireless local area networks (WLANs). The standards define the physical layer and the media access control layer of the wireless network.

* **Bluetooth**: Bluetooth is a wireless technology standard for exchanging data over short distances. It is a low-power, low-cost, and low-complexity wireless technology that allows devices to communicate without the need for cables.

* **Wireless**: Wireless networks are networks that use radio waves for communication instead of cables or wires. Wireless networks are typically used for mobile computing, such as laptops and smartphones, as well as for home networks.

Mnemonic:

* MAC Issues: Media Access Control
* IEEE 802.11: Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers
* Bluetooth: Basic Low-power, Low-cost, Low-complexity
* Wireless: Radio Waves for Communication
