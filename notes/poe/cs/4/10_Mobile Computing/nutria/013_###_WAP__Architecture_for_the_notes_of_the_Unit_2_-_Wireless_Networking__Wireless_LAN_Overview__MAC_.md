
### WAP: Architecture for the notes of the Unit 2 - Wireless Networking, Wireless LAN Overview: MAC issues, IEEE 802.11, Blue Tooth, Wireless in the subject of Mobile Computing

Wireless Access Point (WAP) is a device that acts as a bridge between wireless devices and a wired network. It allows wireless devices to connect to a wired network using radio frequency (RF) technology. WAPs are commonly used in homes, offices, and public areas to provide wireless access to the Internet.

Wireless LAN Overview: MAC issues, IEEE 802.11, Blue Tooth, Wireless

Wireless LANs (WLANs) are networks that use radio frequency (RF) technology to provide wireless access to a network. WLANs are typically used in homes, offices, and public areas to provide wireless access to the Internet. The most commonly used WLAN protocol is IEEE 802.11, which defines a set of standards for wireless LANs. These standards define the physical layer, the media access control (MAC) layer, and the management layer. The physical layer defines the physical properties of the network, such as the frequency of the radio waves used to transmit data. The MAC layer defines the rules and protocols used to control the flow of data on the network. The management layer defines the methods used to manage the network, such as authentication, encryption, and network access control.

Bluetooth is a wireless technology that allows devices to communicate with each other over short distances. It is used to connect devices such as keyboards, mice, and headsets to computers and other devices. Bluetooth also allows devices to transfer files and data between each other.

Wireless in the subject of Mobile Computing

Wireless technologies are used in mobile computing to enable users to access information and services while on the move. Wireless technologies such as Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and cellular networks are used to connect mobile devices to the Internet and to each other. Wi-Fi is used to connect mobile devices to local networks, while Bluetooth is used to connect devices to each other. Cellular networks are used to connect mobile devices to the Internet, allowing users to access the Internet and services while on the move.
