

### GPRS for the Notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction, Issues in Mobile Computing, Overview of Wireless Telephony: Cellular Concept, GSM: in the Subject of Mobile Computing

* GPRS stands for **General Packet Radio Service** and is a wireless data transmission service used by cellular networks. It is based on the GSM (Global System for Mobile Communications) network and is used to transmit data over the air.
* GPRS is a packet-switched technology, which means that data is sent in small packets and routed to the destination through the most efficient route. This makes it more efficient than circuit-switched technology, which is used in traditional telephone networks.
* GPRS is used in many applications such as mobile internet access, email, instant messaging, and location-based services.
* GPRS can be used to transmit data at speeds up to 115 kbps, which is much faster than traditional dial-up connections.
* GPRS also offers improved coverage and reliability compared to other wireless technologies.
* GPRS is a cost-effective solution for mobile data transmission, as it requires no additional hardware or software to be installed. 
* GPRS is also used for location-based services, such as tracking the location of a mobile device.
* GPRS can be used for mobile payments, such as paying for parking or buying tickets.
* GPRS is also used for mobile banking, allowing users to access their bank accounts and make payments on the go.
* GPRS is used in many other applications, such as telemetry, fleet management, and remote monitoring.
