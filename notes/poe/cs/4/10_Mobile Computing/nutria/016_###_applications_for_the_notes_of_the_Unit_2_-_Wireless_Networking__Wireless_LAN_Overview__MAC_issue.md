

# Applications for the Notes of the Unit 2 - Wireless Networking, Wireless LAN Overview: MAC Issues, IEEE 802.11, Blue Tooth, Wireless in the Subject of Mobile Computing

* **MAC Issues:** MAC stands for Media Access Control. It is a protocol that is used to control how devices on a network access the data and resources on the network. This protocol is used to ensure that only authorized devices can access the network resources.

* **IEEE 802.11:** This is a set of standards that define the physical layer, data link layer, and management of wireless networks. It is the most commonly used protocol for wireless communication. It is used in both home networks and corporate networks.

* **Blue Tooth:** This is a short-range wireless protocol that is used to connect devices over short distances. It is used for hands-free communication, streaming audio, and file sharing.

* **Wireless:** Wireless networks are networks that use radio waves to transmit data and information. They are used in home networks, corporate networks, and public networks. Wireless networks are becoming increasingly popular due to their convenience and ease of use.
