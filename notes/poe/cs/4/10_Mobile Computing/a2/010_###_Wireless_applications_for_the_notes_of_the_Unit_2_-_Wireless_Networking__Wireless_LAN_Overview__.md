### Wireless applications for the notes of the Unit 2 - Wireless Networking, Wireless LAN Overview: MAC issues, IEEE 802.11, Blue Tooth, Wireless in the subject of Mobile Computing

- Wireless LANs (WLANs) - Wireless local area networks allow devices to connect to the network using radio frequencies instead of wires. Devices can connect to the network from anywhere in the coverage area. Uses include internet access, file sharing, printing, etc.
- Wireless PANs (WPANs) - Wireless personal area networks have a short range (up to few meters) and are used to connect nearby devices like headphones to a device, wireless mice/keyboards, etc. Technologies like Bluetooth and Zigbee are used.
- Cellular Networks - Wireless mobile networks enable internet access and voice/data communication on the move using cellular technology. The coverage area is divided into small geographical areas called cells, each with its own radio frequency. Users stay connected as they move across cells. 4G LTE and 5G are the current cellular technologies.
- Wi-Fi - Wi-Fi or 802.11 is the most popular WLAN technology. It uses 2.4GHz/5GHz radio bands to create wireless networks with a range of up to 100m. Hotspots provide internet access and devices like laptops, tablets, smartphones can connect to the network. Wi-Fi Direct allows direct connection between devices without a wireless router.
- Bluetooth - Bluetooth is a popular WPAN technology operating at 2.4GHz. It can connect nearby devices to transfer data or establish wireless connections like wireless headphones. Bluetooth has a range of up to 10m and supports point-to-point and point-to-multipoint connections.

Advantages: Convenience, mobility, reduced infrastructure costs
Disadvantages: Security issues, interference, limited range
Applications: Internet access, wireless peripherals, wireless speakers, wearable devices, etc.
