### Applications for the notes of the Unit 2 - Wireless Networking, Wireless LAN Overview: MAC issues, IEEE 802.11, Blue Tooth, Wireless in the subject of Mobile Computing

- IEEE 802.11: Wi-Fi is the most popular wireless networking technology which is defined by the IEEE 802.11 standards. The different versions of Wi-Fi are 802.11a, b, g, n, ac, ax which work on different radio frequencies and have different bandwidths and ranges. Wi-Fi is used to provide wireless internet connectivity in homes, offices and public hotspots.
- MAC issues: The Medium Access Control (MAC) layer is responsible for coordinating access to the shared wireless medium. It uses protocols like CSMA/CA to ensure efficient use of the medium. Hidden node and exposed node problems can occur in wireless networks due to MAC.
- Bluetooth: Bluetooth is a short-range wireless technology standard for exchanging data over short distances using radio waves in the frequency range of 2.4 GHz. It is used to connect peripheral devices like wireless headphones, speakers, keyboards etc. to computing devices. Bluetooth uses a master-slave hierarchy and frequency hopping spread spectrum to prevent interference.
- Advantages: Wireless networks are convenient to use, flexible and inexpensive to deploy. They eliminate the need for cables and provide mobility to devices.
- Disadvantages: Wireless networks are susceptible to security threats like eavesdropping and man-in-the-middle attacks. They can also face interference from other devices operating in the same frequency band which can degrade performance. Reliance on batteries for some devices can also be a disadvantage.
