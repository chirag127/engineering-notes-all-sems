### Wireless applications for the notes of the Unit 2 - Wireless Networking, Wireless LAN Overview: MAC issues, IEEE 802.11, Blue Tooth, Wireless in the subject of Mobile Computing

Wireless technology has revolutionized the way we communicate and access information. In this unit, we will explore the various wireless applications and technologies used in mobile computing. 

#### Wireless Networking
Wireless networking refers to the use of wireless technology to connect devices and computers to a network. It eliminates the need for cables and allows for more flexibility and mobility. Some of the popular wireless networking technologies are:

- Wi-Fi (IEEE 802.11): The most commonly used wireless networking technology that provides high-speed internet connectivity over short distances. It uses radio waves to transmit data between devices and access points.

- Bluetooth: A wireless technology that enables communication between devices over short distances. It is often used for wireless headphones, speakers, and other peripherals.

- Zigbee: A low-power wireless networking technology that is used in home automation, industrial automation, and other applications.

#### Wireless LAN Overview: MAC issues, IEEE 802.11
Wireless LAN (WLAN) is a type of wireless networking that is used in organizations, homes, and public places. WLAN uses radio waves to transmit data between devices and access points. IEEE 802.11 is the standard that defines how WLAN works. 

- MAC Issues: WLAN uses a protocol called the Media Access Control (MAC) protocol to control access to the network. The MAC protocol ensures that only one device transmits data at a time to avoid collisions. 

- IEEE 802.11: The IEEE 802.11 standard defines the specifications for WLAN. It specifies the frequency, channel width, data rates, and other parameters required for wireless communication.

#### Bluetooth
Bluetooth is a wireless technology that enables communication between devices over short distances. It is often used for wireless headphones, speakers, and other peripherals. Here are some key points to remember about Bluetooth:

- Bluetooth uses radio waves to transmit data between devices. It operates on the 2.4GHz frequency band.

- Bluetooth devices use pairing to establish a secure connection between them. Pairing involves exchanging a passkey or PIN between the devices.

- Bluetooth has a limited range of about 10 meters or 33 feet.

#### Wireless in the subject of Mobile Computing
Mobile computing involves the use of mobile devices such as smartphones, tablets, and laptops. Wireless technology plays a crucial role in mobile computing. Here are some examples of how wireless technology is used in mobile computing:

- Wi-Fi hotspots: Wi-Fi hotspots provide wireless internet connectivity to mobile devices in public places such as cafes, airports, and hotels.

- Bluetooth peripherals: Bluetooth is used to connect wireless peripherals such as headphones, speakers, and keyboards to mobile devices.

- Mobile networks: Mobile devices connect to mobile networks to access the internet and make phone calls. Mobile networks use wireless technology to transmit data between devices and base stations.

#### Learning Tricks
Here are some learning tricks and mnemonics that can help you remember the key concepts and technologies related to wireless applications:

- To remember the popular wireless networking technologies, use the mnemonic "Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Zigbee" (W-B-Z).

- To remember the IEEE 802.11 standard, think of it as "8 bits, 0 errors, 2 transmit speeds, 11 channels" (802.11).

- To remember the limited range of Bluetooth, use the mnemonic "Blue-tooth, short-range" (BTSR).

- To remember the use of wireless technology in mobile computing, think of it as "Wireless on the go" (WOG).
