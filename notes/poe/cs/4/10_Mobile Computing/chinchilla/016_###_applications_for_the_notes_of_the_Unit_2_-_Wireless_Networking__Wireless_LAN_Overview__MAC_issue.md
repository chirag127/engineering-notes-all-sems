### Applications for the Notes of Unit 2 - Wireless Networking, Wireless LAN Overview: MAC issues, IEEE 802.11, Bluetooth, Wireless in the Subject of Mobile Computing

Wireless networking has become an integral part of modern-day computing, especially in the realm of mobile computing. This unit covers various aspects of wireless networking, including wireless LAN overview, MAC issues, IEEE 802.11, Bluetooth, and wireless in mobile computing. In this section, we will discuss the applications of these topics in mobile computing.

1. Wireless LAN Overview:
   - Wireless LANs (WLANs) are widely used in mobile computing as they provide the flexibility to access the network from anywhere within the range of the WLAN.
   - WLANs are used in various applications such as email, web browsing, file sharing, online gaming, and multimedia streaming.
   - WLANs are also used in enterprise environments where employees need to access the company's network from different locations within the office.

2. MAC Issues:
   - The media access control (MAC) protocol is used to govern access to the wireless medium by different devices.
   - A significant issue with the MAC protocol is the hidden terminal problem, where two devices cannot communicate directly due to a third device blocking the communication.
   - This issue can be resolved using the RTS/CTS (Request to Send/Clear to Send) mechanism, which allows devices to reserve the medium before transmitting data.

3. IEEE 802.11:
   - IEEE 802.11 is a set of standards for WLANs, which includes various protocols such as 802.11a, 802.11b, 802.11g, 802.11n, and 802.11ac.
   - These standards define the physical and data link layer specifications for wireless communication.
   - IEEE 802.11 is used in various mobile computing applications such as mobile devices, laptops, and tablets.

4. Bluetooth:
   - Bluetooth is a wireless technology used for short-range communication between devices.
   - It is commonly used in mobile computing to connect peripherals such as headsets, keyboards, and mice to devices such as smartphones and tablets.
   - Bluetooth is also used in applications such as wireless speakers, smartwatches, and fitness trackers.

5. Wireless in Mobile Computing:
   - Wireless technology has revolutionized mobile computing, providing users with the freedom to access the internet and network resources from anywhere.
   - Mobile computing devices such as smartphones and tablets use various wireless technologies such as WLANs, Bluetooth, cellular networks, and NFC (Near Field Communication).
   - The use of wireless technology in mobile computing has also led to the development of various applications such as mobile payment systems, location-based services, and augmented reality.

Mnemonics and Learning Tricks:
- To remember the IEEE 802.11 standards, you can use the mnemonic "a big giant never acquires speed" where the first letter of each word corresponds to the standard (a=802.11a, b=802.11b, g=802.11g, n=802.11n, ac=802.11ac).
- To remember the hidden terminal problem and RTS/CTS mechanism, you can use the analogy of a classroom where two students sitting at the back cannot communicate with each other due to a student sitting in between them. The teacher can use the raise hand mechanism (RTS/CTS) to allow the students to communicate by raising their hands before speaking.
