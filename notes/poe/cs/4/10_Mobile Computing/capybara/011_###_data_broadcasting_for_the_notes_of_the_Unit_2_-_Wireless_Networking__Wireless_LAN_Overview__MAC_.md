### Data Broadcasting for the Notes of Unit 2 - Wireless Networking, Wireless LAN Overview: MAC issues, IEEE 802.11, Bluetooth, Wireless in the Subject of Mobile Computing

Data broadcasting is a method of transmitting data from one source to multiple receivers simultaneously. In wireless networking, data broadcasting is commonly used to send data to all devices within the range of the wireless network.

#### MAC Issues in Wireless Broadcasting

In wireless broadcasting, MAC (Media Access Control) issues can arise due to the following reasons:

- Collision: When multiple devices transmit data simultaneously, a collision can occur, resulting in the loss of data.
- Hidden Terminal Problem: When two devices are out of range of each other, they may not be able to detect each other's signals, resulting in the loss of data.
- Exposed Terminal Problem: When one device is transmitting data, another device may be too far away to receive its signal, resulting in the loss of data.

#### IEEE 802.11

IEEE 802.11 is a set of standards for wireless LAN (Local Area Network) communication. It defines the protocols and procedures for data transmission in wireless networks.

The IEEE 802.11 standard specifies two modes of operation for wireless networks:

- Infrastructure mode: In this mode, devices communicate through an access point (AP) that acts as a central hub for all communication.
- Ad-hoc mode: In this mode, devices communicate directly with each other without the need for an access point.

#### Bluetooth

Bluetooth is a wireless technology used for short-range communication between devices. It is commonly used for connecting devices such as smartphones, headphones, and speakers.

Bluetooth uses a technique called frequency hopping to avoid interference with other wireless signals. It also uses a low-power mode to conserve battery life.

#### Wireless in Mobile Computing

Wireless communication is a key component of mobile computing. It enables devices to connect to the internet, transfer data, and communicate with other devices.

Some of the advantages of wireless communication in mobile computing include:

- Mobility: Wireless communication allows devices to be used anywhere, without the need for a wired connection.
- Flexibility: Wireless communication allows devices to be easily repositioned or moved around as needed.
- Scalability: Wireless communication allows multiple devices to be connected to a network without the need for additional cabling.

However, there are also some disadvantages of wireless communication in mobile computing, including:

- Security: Wireless communication can be vulnerable to hacking and other security threats.
- Interference: Wireless communication can be affected by interference from other wireless signals, such as microwaves and other electronic devices.
- Reliability: Wireless communication can be less reliable than wired communication, particularly in areas with poor signal strength.

Mnemonic: "WIFI" - Wireless communication, Infrastructure mode, Frequency hopping, Interference. This simple mnemonic can help remember some of the key concepts related to wireless communication in mobile computing.
