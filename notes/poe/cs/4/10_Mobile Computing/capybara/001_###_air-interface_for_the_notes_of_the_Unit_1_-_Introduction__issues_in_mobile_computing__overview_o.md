### Air-Interface for the Notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction, Issues in Mobile Computing, Overview of Wireless Telephony: Cellular Concept, GSM: in the Subject of Mobile Computing

The air-interface is the wireless communication link between a mobile device and the base station. It is a crucial component of mobile computing and wireless telephony. In this section, we will discuss the air-interface in detail, along with the cellular concept and GSM.

#### Cellular Concept

A cellular network is a type of wireless telecommunication system in which a large geographic area is divided into smaller cells, each of which is served by a base station. The cellular concept was introduced to overcome the problem of limited spectrum availability and to increase the capacity of the network.

#### Air-Interface

The air-interface is the communication link between the mobile device and the base station. It is responsible for transmitting and receiving voice and data signals. There are two types of air-interfaces: analog and digital.

##### Analog Air-Interface

Analog air-interface uses continuous signals to transmit voice and data. It is an older technology and has several drawbacks, such as poor voice quality, limited capacity, and susceptibility to interference.

##### Digital Air-Interface

Digital air-interface uses discrete signals to transmit voice and data. It is a newer and more advanced technology than the analog air-interface. It has several advantages over analog air-interface, such as better voice quality, higher capacity, and resistance to interference.

##### GSM

GSM stands for Global System for Mobile Communications. It is a digital cellular technology that is widely used around the world. GSM uses a combination of time-division multiplexing and frequency-division multiplexing to transmit voice and data signals.
