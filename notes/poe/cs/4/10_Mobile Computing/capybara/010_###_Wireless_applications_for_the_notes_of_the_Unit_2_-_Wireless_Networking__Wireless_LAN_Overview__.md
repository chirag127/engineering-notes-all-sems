### Wireless Applications for the Notes of Unit 2 - Wireless Networking, Wireless LAN Overview: MAC Issues, IEEE 802.11, Bluetooth, Wireless in Mobile Computing

Wireless networking has become increasingly popular in recent years, and has been widely adopted in various applications. It has brought about a significant impact on the field of mobile computing, which has led to the development of various wireless technologies. This article will discuss the different wireless applications in mobile computing that are relevant for the notes of Unit 2 - Wireless Networking, Wireless LAN Overview: MAC issues, IEEE 802.11, Blue Tooth, Wireless.

#### IEEE 802.11

IEEE 802.11 is the standard for wireless local area networks (WLANs) and is commonly known as Wi-Fi. It is widely used in homes, offices, and public places such as cafes, airports, and hotels. Some of the wireless applications that use IEEE 802.11 are:

- Video Conferencing: Wi-Fi enabled devices such as laptops, tablets, and smartphones can be used to conduct video conferencing over a wireless network.
- File Sharing: Wi-Fi enabled devices can share files over a wireless network, making it easier to transfer documents, images, and videos between devices.
- Online Gaming: Wi-Fi enabled devices can be used to play online games over a wireless network.

#### Bluetooth

Bluetooth is a wireless technology that is used for short-range communication between devices. It is widely used in mobile phones, laptops, and other portable devices. Some of the wireless applications that use Bluetooth are:

- Wireless Headsets: Bluetooth headsets can be used to make and receive calls wirelessly.
- Wireless Speakers: Bluetooth speakers can be used to listen to music wirelessly from a smartphone or laptop.
- Wireless Mouse and Keyboard: Bluetooth enabled mouse and keyboard can be used to control a computer wirelessly.

#### Wireless in Mobile Computing

Wireless technology has had a significant impact on mobile computing. Some of the wireless applications that are relevant to mobile computing are:

- Mobile Banking: Mobile banking applications can be used to transfer funds, pay bills, and check account balances wirelessly.
- Location-Based Services: Location-based services such as GPS and Google Maps use wireless technology to provide users with location-specific information.
- Mobile Payment: Mobile payment applications such as Apple Pay and Google Wallet use wireless technology to make payments wirelessly.

- To remember the applications of Bluetooth, remember the acronym HEADSET, which stands for Headsets, Entertainment, Audio, Data, Speakers, and Transfer.
- To remember the applications of IEEE 802.11, remember the acronym VFO, which stands for Video Conferencing, File Sharing, and Online Gaming.
