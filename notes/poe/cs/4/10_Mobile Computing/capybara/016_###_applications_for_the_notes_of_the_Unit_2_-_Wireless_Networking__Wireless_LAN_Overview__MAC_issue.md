### Applications for the Notes of the Unit 2 - Wireless Networking, Wireless LAN Overview: MAC issues, IEEE 802.11, Blue Tooth, Wireless in the Subject of Mobile Computing

Wireless networking has become an integral part of mobile computing. In this unit, we will learn about wireless LAN overview, MAC issues, IEEE 802.11, Bluetooth, and wireless technologies.

Here are some applications for the notes of Unit 2 that can help you understand the concepts better:

1. Mnemonics for IEEE 802.11: To remember the IEEE 802.11 standards, you can use the following mnemonic:

    - 802.11a - Always
    - 802.11b - Be
    - 802.11g - Good
    - 802.11n - Never
    - 802.11ac - Again
    - 802.11ax - Xtra

2. Advantages and Disadvantages of Bluetooth:

    - Advantages: Bluetooth is a low-power, short-range wireless technology that is widely used for personal area networks (PANs). It is easy to use, cost-effective, and supports multiple devices.
 
    - Disadvantages: Bluetooth has a limited range, slow data transfer rate, and can be affected by interference from other devices operating on the same frequency.

3. IEEE 802.11 Standards: The IEEE 802.11 standards are a set of protocols that define how wireless networks operate. These standards include:

    - 802.11a: This standard operates in the 5GHz frequency band and supports data rates up to 54 Mbps.

    - 802.11b: This standard operates in the 2.4GHz frequency band and supports data rates up to 11 Mbps.

    - 802.11g: This standard operates in the 2.4GHz frequency band and supports data rates up to 54 Mbps.

    - 802.11n: This standard operates in both the 2.4GHz and 5GHz frequency bands and supports data rates up to 600 Mbps.

    - 802.11ac: This standard operates in the 5GHz frequency band and supports data rates up to 7 Gbps.

    - 802.11ax: This standard operates in both the 2.4GHz and 5GHz frequency bands and supports data rates up to 10 Gbps.

4. MAC Issues: The MAC layer is responsible for controlling access to the wireless medium. Some of the MAC issues that can affect wireless networks include collisions, hidden terminal problem, and exposed terminal problem.

    - Collisions occur when two or more devices transmit data at the same time, causing interference and reducing network performance.

    - The hidden terminal problem occurs when two or more devices cannot detect each other's signals, leading to data loss and network congestion.

    - The exposed terminal problem occurs when a device hears another device transmitting and assumes the wireless medium is busy, even though it may not be.

5. Wireless LAN Overview: Wireless LAN (WLAN) is a type of wireless networking technology that is widely used for personal and business use. WLANs use radio waves to transmit data between devices, eliminating the need for cables and wires.
