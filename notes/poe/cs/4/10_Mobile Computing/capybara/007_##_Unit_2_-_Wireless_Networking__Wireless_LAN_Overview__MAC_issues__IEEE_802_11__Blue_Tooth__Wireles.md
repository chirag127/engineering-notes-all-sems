## Unit 2 - Wireless Networking, Wireless LAN Overview: MAC issues, IEEE 802.11, Blue Tooth, Wireless

Wireless LAN or WLAN is a type of local area network that uses wireless communication technology to transmit data over the air. In this unit, we will discuss MAC issues, IEEE 802.11, Blue Tooth, and Wireless in detail.

### MAC Issues
- MAC (Media Access Control) is a sub-layer of the Data Link layer in the OSI model that controls access to the network medium.
- In WLAN, the MAC layer is responsible for managing the wireless medium and controlling access to it.
- MAC issues in WLAN arise due to the limited bandwidth of the wireless medium, interference from other wireless devices, and the presence of hidden nodes.
- To overcome these issues, WLAN uses carrier-sense multiple access with collision avoidance (CSMA/CA) as the access control mechanism.

### IEEE 802.11
- IEEE 802.11 is a set of standards for WLANs published by the Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers (IEEE).
- The IEEE 802.11 standards define the physical layer (PHY) and MAC layer specifications for WLANs.
- The IEEE 802.11 standards support different frequency bands, data rates, and modulation schemes.
- The most commonly used standards in WLANs are 802.11b, 802.11g, and 802.11n.

### Blue Tooth
- Blue Tooth is a wireless technology that allows devices to communicate with each other over short distances.
- Blue Tooth operates in the 2.4 GHz frequency band and supports data rates up to 3 Mbps.
- Blue Tooth uses frequency-hopping spread spectrum (FHSS) as the access control mechanism to avoid interference from other wireless devices.
- Blue Tooth is widely used in mobile devices, such as smartphones, tablets, and laptops, for wireless communication with other devices, such as headphones, speakers, and smartwatches.

### Wireless
- Wireless communication refers to the transmission of data over the air without the use of physical cables or wires.
- Wireless communication can be achieved using different technologies, such as WLAN, cellular networks, satellite communication, and infrared communication.
- The advantages of wireless communication include mobility, flexibility, and scalability.
- The disadvantages of wireless communication include limited bandwidth, interference, and security issues.

In conclusion, this unit covered MAC issues, IEEE 802.11, Blue Tooth, and Wireless in detail. These concepts are essential to understand the functioning and applications of WLANs and wireless communication technology.
