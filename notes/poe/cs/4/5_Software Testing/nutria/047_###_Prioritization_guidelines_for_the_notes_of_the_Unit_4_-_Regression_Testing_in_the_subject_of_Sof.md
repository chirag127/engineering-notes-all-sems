
### Prioritization guidelines for the notes of the Unit 4 - Regression Testing in the subject of Software Testing
Prioritizing regression tests is a key part of software testing. In order to ensure that the tests are effective and efficient, they must be prioritized to focus on the most important areas. The following guidelines can help with this process:

1. Identify areas of the software that are most likely to be impacted by changes. This includes areas that are frequently changed, as well as areas that are likely to be affected by changes to other parts of the software.

2. Identify areas of the software that are most complex or have the most potential for errors. These areas are likely to require more testing and should be prioritized accordingly.

3. Identify areas of the software that are most critical to the success of the software. These areas should be given the highest priority when it comes to regression testing.

4. Identify areas of the software that are most difficult to test. These areas may require special attention and should be given a higher priority.

5. Establish a set of criteria for prioritizing regression tests. This may include factors such as risk, complexity, or importance.

6. Identify areas of the software that are most likely to be affected by changes in the near future. These areas should be given a higher priority so that any potential issues can be identified and addressed before the changes are implemented.

7. Use mnemonics and learning tricks to help remember the prioritization guidelines. For example, the acronym “RCCI” can be used to remember the criteria for prioritizing regression tests: Risk, Complexity, Criticality, and Impact.
