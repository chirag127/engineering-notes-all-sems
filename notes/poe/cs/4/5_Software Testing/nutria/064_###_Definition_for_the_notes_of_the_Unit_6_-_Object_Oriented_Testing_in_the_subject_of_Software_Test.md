
### Definition for the notes of the Unit 6 - Object Oriented Testing in the subject of Software Testing

Object-oriented testing (OOT) is a software testing methodology that focuses on testing the behavior of a system by examining the interactions between its objects. OOT is used to ensure that the system meets the requirements of the customer and is designed to be easy to maintain and extend. OOT is based on the object-oriented design principles of encapsulation, modularity, inheritance, and polymorphism.

The goal of OOT is to ensure that the system is working as expected. OOT is used to verify that the system is functioning properly and that it meets the requirements of the customer. OOT is also used to identify any defects in the system that may be difficult to detect using traditional testing methods.

OOT involves testing the behavior of objects in the system and the interactions between them. This includes testing the code that implements the behavior of the objects, as well as the data structures that store the objects’ state. OOT also involves testing the system’s performance, scalability, and reliability.

One of the most important aspects of OOT is its ability to detect defects early in the development process. By testing the system early, defects can be identified and fixed before they become major problems. OOT can also help to reduce the cost of development by identifying and fixing defects before they become expensive to fix.
