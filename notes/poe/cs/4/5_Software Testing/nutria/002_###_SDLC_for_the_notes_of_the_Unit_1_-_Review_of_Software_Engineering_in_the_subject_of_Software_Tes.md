
### SDLC for the notes of the Unit 1 - Review of Software Engineering in the subject of Software Testing

Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC) is a framework that describes the process of developing software. It is a set of activities and processes that are used to create a software product. It is also known as the System Development Life Cycle (SDLC).

The SDLC process consists of the following steps:

1. **Planning**: Planning is the first step in the SDLC process. This involves defining the scope and objectives of the project, gathering requirements, and creating a project plan.

2. **Analysis**: Analysis is the second step in the SDLC process. This involves analyzing the requirements, identifying potential risks and developing a solution.

3. **Design**: Design is the third step in the SDLC process. This involves designing the architecture, user interface, and database of the software.

4. **Implementation**: Implementation is the fourth step in the SDLC process. This involves coding the software and testing it to ensure that it meets the requirements.

5. **Testing**: Testing is the fifth step in the SDLC process. This involves testing the software to ensure that it meets the requirements and is free of bugs.

6. **Deployment**: Deployment is the sixth step in the SDLC process. This involves deploying the software to production and making it available to users.

7. **Maintenance**: Maintenance is the seventh and final step in the SDLC process. This involves providing ongoing support and making changes to the software as needed.

The SDLC is an important part of the software development process, as it helps to ensure that the software is of high quality and meets the requirements of the users. It also helps to ensure that the software is developed in an efficient and cost-effective manner.

Mnemonics for SDLC:

1. **PAPA**: Planning, Analysis, Design, and Implementation
2. **SADT**: System Analysis, Design, Testing, and Deployment
3. **DADT**: Design, Analysis, Development, and Testing
4. **PDCA**: Plan, Do, Check, and Act
5. **SDLC**: Software Development Life Cycle
