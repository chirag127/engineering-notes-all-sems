
### SRS Verification for the notes of the Unit 1 - Review of Software Engineering in the subject of Software Testing

Software Requirements Specification (SRS) Verification is a process used to ensure that the software meets the requirements of the customer. It is an important part of the software development life cycle and is used to ensure that the software meets the customer's needs and expectations.

The SRS Verification process involves the following steps:

1. Review the SRS: The first step in the SRS Verification process is to review the SRS document. This includes reviewing the requirements and making sure that they are complete, consistent, and accurate.

2. Verify the SRS: The second step in the SRS Verification process is to verify the SRS document. This includes verifying the requirements by testing the software against the requirements.

3. Validate the SRS: The third step in the SRS Verification process is to validate the SRS document. This includes validating the requirements by ensuring that the software meets the customer's needs and expectations.

4. Document the SRS: The fourth step in the SRS Verification process is to document the SRS document. This includes documenting the requirements and any changes that were made during the verification process.

5. Report the SRS: The fifth step in the SRS Verification process is to report the SRS document. This includes reporting the status of the verification process, any changes that were made, and any issues that were found.

These steps are important for ensuring that the software meets the customer's needs and expectations. By following these steps, the software development team can ensure that the software meets the customer's needs and expectations.
