### Priority category for the notes of the Unit 4 - Regression Testing in the subject of Software Testing

Regression testing is an essential part of software testing that ensures that any changes made to the software do not affect its previously working functionality. When it comes to prioritizing the regression test cases, the following categories can be considered:

1. High Priority: These test cases cover the critical and most important functionalities of the software. They must be tested first to ensure that the software is working as expected. Some Mnemonics to remember this category could be 'HPC' or 'VIP' (Very Important Points).

2. Medium Priority: These test cases cover the functionalities that are important but not critical. They can be tested after the high priority test cases are validated. Some Mnemonics to remember this category could be 'MPC' or 'IMP' (Important Points).

3. Low Priority: These test cases cover functionalities that are not critical and can be tested later. They can be tested after the high and medium priority test cases have been validated. Some Mnemonics to remember this category could be 'LPC' or 'NIP' (Not Important Points).

It is essential to remember that the priority of the test cases may change based on the changes made to the software. Hence, the priority should be reviewed periodically to ensure that the high-priority test cases are tested first.

In conclusion, prioritizing regression test cases is crucial to ensure the software's stability and reliability. By categorizing the test cases based on their priority, we can ensure that the most critical functionalities are tested first. Remembering the Mnemonics can help in quickly recalling the priority categories during the exam.
