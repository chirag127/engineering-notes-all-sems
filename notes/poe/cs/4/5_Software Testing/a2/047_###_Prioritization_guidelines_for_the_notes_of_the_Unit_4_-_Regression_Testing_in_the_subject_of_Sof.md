### Prioritization guidelines for the notes of the Unit 4 - Regression Testing in the subject of Software Testing

1. Test the major functional areas of the software:

- Prioritize testing core features and primary functions of the software. Focus on testing the major functional areas that were tested in previous iterations and release. These are the areas that are most likely to be affected by the changes.
- For e.g. Check login, checkout, payment, add to cart, search functions in an e-commerce application.

2. Test common paths through the software:

- Concentrate on the most commonly used paths and processes. These have a higher chance of being impacted by changes.
- For e.g. Check purchase of items, refund/return of items in an e-commerce application.

3. Test critical and complex features:

- Give priority to critical features and complex functionalities as they are more prone to errors due to changes.
- For e.g. Check calculation and taxation related features in billing software. Check sort and filter features in applications.

4. Consider recent changes and bug fixes:

- Focus on testing areas where recent changes or bug fixes were made. These portions are most likely to get affected by fixes or enhancements, so need re-testing.
- Review the change log to identify the changes and test accordingly.

5. Consider customer feedback and reported issues:

- Check areas of the software that have generated customer feedback or issues. Fixes made to address customer problems may affect other areas, so those portions should be re-tested.
- Go through customer feedback and support tickets to spot areas that need focus.

6. Automate regression tests:

- Automate regression tests to the extent possible. This makes repeated testing efficient, fast and less error-prone.
- Identify repetitive but critical tests and automate them. This helps in thorough regression testing in a shorter time span.
