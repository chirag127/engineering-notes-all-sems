### Decision Table Based Testing

- Decision table testing is a black box testing technique that uses a systematic approach to developing test conditions based on the conditions and actions of a system.
- It uses a tabular representation to design test cases efficiently by analyzing combinations of inputs and conditions.
- The inputs and conditions are listed in the rows and columns of the table respectively. The actions are listed in the table cells.
- This technique is useful for testing systems with multiple logical conditions and actions. It helps identify missing test conditions easily.
- Advantages:
  - Helps identify test conditions thoroughly considering all combinations of inputs and conditions.
  - Easy to learn and understand.
  - Useful for testing complex business logic with multiple conditions and actions.
- Disadvantages:
  - May not be feasible for large and complex systems with many inputs and conditions which can lead to explosion of test cases.
  - Tedious to create decision tables for complex scenarios.
- Examples: Testing an online shopping cart, loan eligibility system, etc.
- To remember: Arrange inputs, conditions and actions in rows and columns. Check for completeness of test coverage.
- For learning: Analyze scenarios with multiple conditions and actions. Identify inputs and conditions. Map actions to each combination. Create tables and identify missing test cases.
