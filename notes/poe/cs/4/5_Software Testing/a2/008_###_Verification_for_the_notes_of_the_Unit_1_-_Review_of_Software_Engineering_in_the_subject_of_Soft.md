### Verification for the notes of the Unit 1 - Review of Software Engineering in the subject of Software Testing

- Verification is the process of evaluating software to determine whether it satisfies the specified requirements. It involves reviews and meetings to evaluate documents, plans, code, and tests.
- Types of verification:
	- Inspections: Formal evaluation of work products by a team who identifies defects and suggests corrections.
	- Audits: Independent evaluation of processes and work products.
	- Walkthroughs: Step-by-step presentation of a work product by the author to a team who asks questions and identifies issues.
	- Reviews: Systematic examination of a work product by a team focusing on specific attributes.
- Advantages:
	- Finds and fixes defects early which is cost effective.
	- Ensures conformance to standards and requirements.
	- Provides opportunities for feedback and learning.
- Disadvantages:
	- Can be time-consuming and tedious.
	- Requires skilled individuals to be effective.
	- May delay project schedules if not planned properly.
- Mnemonics: VERRA - Verification, Evaluation, Review, Inspection, Audit.
- Learning trick: Remember VERRA by thinking verification allows you to eRRor check and review specifications.
