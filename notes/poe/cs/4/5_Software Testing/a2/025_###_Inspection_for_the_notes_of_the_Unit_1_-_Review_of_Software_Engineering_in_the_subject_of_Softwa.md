### Inspection for the notes of the Unit 1 - Review of Software Engineering in the subject of Software Testing

- Software Engineering deals with the systematic approach to the development, operation, maintenance, and retirement of computer software.
- The Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC) consists of the following phases: Requirements Analysis, Design, Implementation/Coding, Testing, Deployment, and Maintenance.
- Requirements Analysis phase involves understanding the exact needs of the customer and documenting them.
- Design phase involves converting the requirements into design and architecture of the software. It includes both logical and physical design.
- Implementation/Coding phase involves implementing the design in a programming language.
- Testing phase involves systematic testing of the software to identify and fix bugs or defects.
- Deployment phase involves delivery and installation of the software.
- Maintenance phase involves modifications and updations to the software as per the changing requirements.

Advantages of Software Engineering:
- Increased productivity and quality.
- Meets customer requirements.
- Reduced development and maintenance cost.
- Structured approach leading to high quality software.

Disadvantages of Software Engineering:
- Inflexible and rigid process which may not accommodate changes easily.
- Time-consuming and expensive process.
- Difficult to estimate costs and efforts.

Some important concepts in Software Engineering:
- Software Metrics - To measure the quality and productivity.
- Software Reusability - Using the same software multiple times to reduce effort.
- Software Reliability - Ability of the software to perform its intended functions under given conditions for a specified period of time.
- Software Quality - Degree to which software possesses desirable characteristics such as correctness, reliability, usability, and maintainability.
