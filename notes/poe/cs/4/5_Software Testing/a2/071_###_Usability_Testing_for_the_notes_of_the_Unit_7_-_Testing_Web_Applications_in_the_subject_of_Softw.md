### Usability Testing for the notes of the Unit 7 - Testing Web Applications in the subject of Software Testing

Usability testing is a technique used to evaluate a product by testing it on potential users. It aims to identify any problems with the user experience, interactions, and interface of the product.

Key points about Usability Testing:

- It is done by observing actual users using the product and recording their experiences, emotions, and difficulties faced.
- Small groups of 6-8 representative potential users are selected to perform specific tasks on the product.
- The users are asked to think aloud while using the product so that the observers can understand their thought process and reasons behind their actions.
- The tasks are assigned in a particular sequence to uncover usability issues.
- The observers note down all problems, confusions, difficulties, and negative emotions faced by the users.
- These observations are then analyzed to identify usability issues and make improvements to the product.

Advantages of Usability Testing:

- It provides direct feedback from real users which helps in understanding their expectations and experiences.
- It uncovers subtle usability issues that may go unnoticed by designers and developers.
- It is cost-effective as only small groups of users are required.
- It helps in making a user-centered design by focusing on user needs and experiences.

Disadvantages of Usability Testing:

- It requires availability of representative users to participate in the testing.
- The results may be subjective as different users may have varying experiences.
- The testing environment may affect user behavior and lead to inaccurate responses.
- Making changes based on usability testing feedback may not always lead to improved user experience.

Examples of Usability Testing:

- Testing a website or web application by assigning tasks like finding a product, checking order status, etc. to users and observing their actions and experiences.
- Testing a mobile app by having users perform various interactions and operations on the app and noting down usability issues faced by them.
- Testing hardware products like cameras or appliances by providing them to users and observing how they learn to use the products and any difficulties faced.

Applications of Usability Testing:

- Web design and development
- Mobile app design and development
- Product design and development
- Improving user experience and interface of software and hardware products
