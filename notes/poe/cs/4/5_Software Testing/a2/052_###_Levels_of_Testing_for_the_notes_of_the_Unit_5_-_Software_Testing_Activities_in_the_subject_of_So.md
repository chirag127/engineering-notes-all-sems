### Levels of Testing
The different levels of testing are:

- Unit Testing: Testing individual software components/units.
- Integration Testing: Testing integrated software modules/units to find interface defects between modules.
- System Testing: Testing the whole system as a whole to ensure that requirements are met.
- Acceptance Testing: Testing by the customer to accept or reject the software.
