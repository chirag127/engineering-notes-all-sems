### Prioritization guidelines for the notes of the Unit 4 - Regression Testing in the subject of Software Testing

Regression testing is a type of software testing that ensures that the recent changes made in the software do not affect the existing functionalities of the system. It is essential to prioritize the regression test cases to ensure the efficient use of resources and time. Here are some prioritization guidelines for regression testing that can help you in your software testing examinations:

1. Criticality of the functionality: Identify the critical functionalities of the software and prioritize regression test cases accordingly. Test cases related to critical functionalities must be given high priority as they can directly impact the user experience and affect the reputation of the software.

2. Frequency of use: Test cases related to frequently used functionalities must be given high priority as they are more likely to be impacted by the changes made in the software. Regression testing of frequently used functionalities ensures that the software is stable and reliable.

3. Complexity of the functionality: Test cases related to complex functionalities must be given high priority as they are more likely to be impacted by the changes made in the software. Regression testing of complex functionalities ensures that the software is working as expected.

4. Age of the functionality: Test cases related to recently added functionalities must be given high priority as they are more likely to be impacted by the changes made in the software. Regression testing of recently added functionalities ensures that the new functionalities are working as expected and do not impact the existing functionalities.

5. Failure rate of the functionality: Test cases related to functionalities with a high failure rate must be given high priority as they have a higher risk of being impacted by the changes made in the software. Regression testing of high failure rate functionalities ensures that the software is stable and reliable.

6. Business impact of the functionality: Test cases related to functionalities that have a high business impact must be given high priority as they can directly impact the revenue of the organization. Regression testing of high business impact functionalities ensures that the software is working as expected and meets the business requirements.

Mnemonics and learning tricks:
- CFC-AFB (Criticality, Frequency of use, Complexity, Age, Failure rate, Business impact) can be used as a mnemonic to remember the prioritization guidelines for regression testing.
- Another learning trick is to remember the acronym CRASH (Criticality, Recent, Age, Stability, High impact) which can help in remembering the prioritization guidelines for regression testing. 

In conclusion, the prioritization guidelines for regression testing are essential to ensure the efficient use of resources and time. By following these guidelines, software testers can ensure that the software is stable, reliable, and meets the business requirements. Remembering the mnemonics and learning tricks can help in recalling these guidelines during software testing examinations.
