### Verification for the notes of the Unit 1 - Review of Software Engineering in the subject of Software Testing

Verification is an essential process in software development that ensures that the software meets the specified requirements and standards. Verification is a process of evaluating the intermediate work products of software engineering to ensure that they meet the specified requirements and standards.

The following are the steps involved in the verification process:

1. Planning: The first step in the verification process is planning. In this step, the verification objectives are defined, and the verification plan is developed. The verification plan includes the verification methods, tools, and techniques that will be used to verify the software.

2. Review: The second step in the verification process is review. In this step, the intermediate work products are reviewed to ensure that they meet the specified requirements and standards. The review can be done manually or using automated tools.

3. Inspection: The third step in the verification process is inspection. In this step, the intermediate work products are inspected to ensure that they meet the specified requirements and standards. Inspection is a formal process that involves a team of people who are knowledgeable about the software and the verification process.

4. Testing: The fourth step in the verification process is testing. In this step, the software is tested to ensure that it meets the specified requirements and standards. Testing can be done manually or using automated tools.

5. Verification Reporting: The fifth step in the verification process is verification reporting. In this step, the results of the verification process are documented. The verification report includes the verification objectives, methods, tools, and techniques that were used, and the results of the verification process.

Mnemonics and learning tricks:

- Remember the acronym PRIVT for the five steps involved in the verification process: Planning, Review, Inspection, Testing, and Verification Reporting.
- Use the phrase "Verify with PRIDE" to remember the five steps involved in the verification process.

In conclusion, verification is a critical process in software development that ensures that the software meets the specified requirements and standards. The five steps involved in the verification process are planning, review, inspection, testing, and verification reporting. Remembering the acronym PRIVT or the phrase "Verify with PRIDE" can help in retaining the information for exams.
