### Limitation of Distributed system for the notes of the Unit 1 - Characterization of Distributed Systems in the subject of DISTRIBUTED SYSTEM

Some of the key limitations of distributed systems are:

1. Complexity: Distributed systems are more complex than centralized systems. They involve coordinating multiple components over a network which makes the system design, implementation and troubleshooting more difficult.
2. Partial failures: Since distributed systems have many components, the likelihood of some component failing is higher. The system must be designed to tolerate and recover from such partial failures.
3. Latency: The communication between different components over a network introduces latency. This can impact performance and must be accounted for in the system design.
4. Concurrency: Distributed systems involve concurrent processes and threads operating on shared data, which can lead to race conditions and other concurrency issues that must be handled.
5. Security: It is more difficult to ensure security in distributed systems due to the exposure of the system across a network and multiple components. Secure communication and authentication mechanisms must be implemented.

Some mnemonics to remember the limitations are:

- CCOLD: Complexity, Concurrency, Partial failures, Latency, Distributed
- SPID: Security, Performance, Interoperability, Debugging

The limitations can be mitigated through techniques like fault tolerance, caching, load balancing, asynchronous communication, etc. However, they can never be eliminated completely and must be considered in the system design.
