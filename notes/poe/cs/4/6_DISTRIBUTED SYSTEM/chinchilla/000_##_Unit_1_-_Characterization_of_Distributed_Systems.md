## Unit 1 - Characterization of Distributed Systems

Distributed systems are computer systems composed of multiple interconnected computers that work together to solve a common problem. These systems are commonly used in large-scale applications such as cloud computing, social media platforms, and online marketplaces. 

The characterization of distributed systems involves understanding the properties that define these systems, such as their structure, communication protocols, and fault tolerance mechanisms. This unit covers the following topics:

### 1. Introduction to Distributed Systems
- Definition of distributed systems
- Characteristics of distributed systems
- Advantages and disadvantages of distributed systems

### 2. System Models
- Architectural models of distributed systems
- Types of distributed systems based on architectural models
- Comparison of different architectural models

### 3. Network Basics
- Network topologies and architectures
- Network protocols and communication models
- Network devices and their roles in distributed systems

### 4. Interprocess Communication
- Message passing and shared memory models
- Synchronization mechanisms in distributed systems
- Remote procedure calls and distributed objects

### 5. Fault Tolerance and Resilience
- Types of faults in distributed systems
- Techniques for fault detection, recovery, and prevention
- Replication and redundancy in distributed systems

Mnemonics and Learning Tricks:
- To remember the types of faults in distributed systems, use the mnemonic "MURPHY" (Malfunction, Unreliability, Resource Exhaustion, Programming Errors, Human Errors, and Cyber Attacks).
- To remember the architectural models of distributed systems, use the acronym "P2P" (Peer-to-Peer, Client-Server, and Hybrid). 

In conclusion, understanding the characterization of distributed systems is essential for designing and developing reliable, efficient, and scalable distributed systems. This unit provides a comprehensive overview of the key concepts and techniques involved in the characterization of distributed systems.
