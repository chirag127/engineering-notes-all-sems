### Theoretical Foundation for Distributed System for the notes of the Unit 1 - Characterization of Distributed Systems in the subject of DISTRIBUTED SYSTEM

Distributed systems are a collection of independent computers that appear to the user as a single, coherent system. They are designed to handle large-scale problems by breaking them down into smaller, more manageable pieces that can be distributed across multiple machines. In this unit, we will focus on the theoretical foundation for distributed systems.

### Mnemonics and Learning Tricks:

- Remember the acronym "CAN" for the characteristics of distributed systems: Consistency, Availability, and Network partition tolerance.
- To remember the difference between synchronous and asynchronous communication, think of synchronous as "in sync" - both parties must be ready to communicate at the same time. Asynchronous is like leaving a message - the sender doesn't need an immediate response.
- To remember the types of middleware, think of "MOTORS" - Message-Oriented, Transaction Processing, and Object-Oriented middleware.
