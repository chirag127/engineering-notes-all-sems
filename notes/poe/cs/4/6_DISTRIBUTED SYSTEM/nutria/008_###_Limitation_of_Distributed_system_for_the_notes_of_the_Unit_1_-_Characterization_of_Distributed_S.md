
### Limitation of Distributed System for the Notes of the Unit 1 - Characterization of Distributed Systems in the Subject of DISTRIBUTED SYSTEM

1. **Communication Limitations:** As distributed systems are based on different computers connected over a network, communication between them is a major challenge. This is due to the fact that the communication channels are not always reliable and can be slow due to the physical distance between the computers. 

2. **Resource Limitations:** Distributed systems require multiple computers to run, and this can be costly. Additionally, each computer needs to have the necessary resources to process the data and run the system.

3. **Security Limitations:** Security is a major concern in distributed systems. As the data is distributed across multiple computers, it is more vulnerable to security threats.

4. **Scalability Limitations:** Distributed systems can be difficult to scale as they require multiple computers to work together. Each computer needs to be configured correctly in order to ensure that the system is running smoothly.

5. **Data Consistency Limitations:** As the data is distributed across multiple computers, it can be difficult to maintain data consistency. This is due to the fact that the data can be updated on one computer but not on the other.

6. **Programming Limitations:** Programming distributed systems can be difficult as it requires a deep understanding of the underlying technology. Additionally, debugging distributed systems can be difficult due to the complex nature of the system.
