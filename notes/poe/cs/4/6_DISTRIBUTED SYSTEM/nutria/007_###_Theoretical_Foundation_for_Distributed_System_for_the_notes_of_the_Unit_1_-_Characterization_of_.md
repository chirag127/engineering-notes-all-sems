
### Theoretical Foundation for Distributed System
Distributed systems are a type of computer system which is made up of multiple computers that are connected to each other over a network. They are designed to provide services that can be accessed by multiple users simultaneously. The theoretical foundation of distributed systems is based on the principles of distributed computing, which are designed to ensure that the system is reliable, secure and efficient.

#### Characterization of Distributed Systems
Distributed systems can be characterized in terms of the following parameters:

* **Communication:** This refers to the way in which different nodes in the system communicate with each other.
* **Scalability:** This refers to the ability of the system to handle increasing workloads.
* **Fault Tolerance:** This refers to the ability of the system to continue functioning even when one or more components fail.
* **Consistency:** This refers to the ability of the system to maintain data consistency across all nodes.
* **Security:** This refers to the ability of the system to protect data from unauthorized access and manipulation.
