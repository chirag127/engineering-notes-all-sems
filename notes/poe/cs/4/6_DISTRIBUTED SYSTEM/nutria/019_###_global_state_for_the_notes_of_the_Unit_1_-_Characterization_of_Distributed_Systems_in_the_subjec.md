
### Global State for the Notes of the Unit 1 - Characterization of Distributed Systems in the Subject of Distributed Systems

- Global state is a snapshot of the distributed system at a given moment in time.
- It includes the values of all variables and the states of all processes.
- It is important to understand the global state in order to properly characterize a distributed system.
- Global state can be used to detect and prevent inconsistencies in the system.
- It can also be used to detect and prevent deadlocks.
- Global state can also be used to detect and prevent race conditions.
- Global state can be used to identify and recover from failures.
- Global state can also be used to detect and prevent livelocks.
- Mnemonics and learning tricks: 
  - G: Global state
  - V: Variables
  - P: Processes
  - I: Inconsistencies
  - D: Deadlocks
  - R: Race conditions
  - F: Failures
  - L: Livelocks
