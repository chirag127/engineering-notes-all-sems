
### Transactions with Replicated Data for the Notes of the Unit 10 - Replication in the Subject of DISTRIBUTED SYSTEM

* Replication is a process of creating and maintaining multiple copies of data in a distributed system.
* Replication is used to improve the availability, reliability and scalability of data.
* Replication also helps in improving the performance of the system by reducing the latency of data access.
* Replication is of two types: synchronous and asynchronous.
  * Synchronous replication involves the creation of multiple copies of data in real time.
  * Asynchronous replication involves the creation of multiple copies of data at scheduled intervals.
* Replicated data is stored in multiple nodes of a distributed system.
* Transactions with replicated data involve the execution of operations on multiple copies of data.
* The operations can be reads, writes, updates, deletes, etc.
* The operations are executed atomically, i.e. they are executed as a single unit and either all of them succeed or none of them do.
* The operations are also executed in a consistent manner, i.e. the data remains consistent across all the nodes of the system.
* The operations are also executed in an isolated manner, i.e. the operations do not interfere with each other.
* The operations are also executed in a durable manner, i.e. the data is not lost even in the event of a system crash.
* The operations can also be executed in a distributed manner, i.e. the operations can be executed on multiple nodes simultaneously.
* The transactions with replicated data are executed using protocols such as two-phase commit and three-phase commit.
* The two-phase commit protocol involves the execution of the operations in two phases: prepare and commit.
* The three-phase commit protocol involves the execution of the operations in three phases: prepare, commit and confirm.
* Mnemonics and learning tricks for transactions with replicated data may include:
  * Two-phase commit: "PREPARE to COMMIT".
  * Three-phase commit: "PREPARE to COMMIT and CONFIRM".
