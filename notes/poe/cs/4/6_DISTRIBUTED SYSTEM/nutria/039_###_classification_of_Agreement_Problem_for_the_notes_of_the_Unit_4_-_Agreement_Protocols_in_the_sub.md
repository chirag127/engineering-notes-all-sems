
### Classification of Agreement Problem for the Notes of the Unit 4 - Agreement Protocols in the Subject of DISTRIBUTED SYSTEM 

Agreement problems in distributed systems can be classified into two major categories: 

1. Synchronous Agreement Problems: These problems involve reaching an agreement on a common decision within a given time frame. The two most common synchronous agreement problems are the Consensus Problem and the Atomic Broadcast Problem. 

- **Consensus Problem:** This is the problem of reaching a common agreement among all nodes in a distributed system. The consensus problem is solved by ensuring that all nodes agree on a single value. 

- **Atomic Broadcast Problem:** This is the problem of ensuring that all nodes in a distributed system receive the same set of messages in the same order. 

2. Asynchronous Agreement Problems: These problems involve reaching an agreement on a common decision without any time constraints. The two most common asynchronous agreement problems are the Byzantine Agreement Problem and the Byzantine Generals Problem. 

- **Byzantine Agreement Problem:** This is the problem of reaching a common agreement among all nodes in a distributed system, even if some of the nodes are faulty or malicious. 

- **Byzantine Generals Problem:** This is the problem of ensuring that all nodes in a distributed system receive the same set of messages, even if some of the nodes are faulty or malicious. 

In order to remember the different agreement problems, it can be useful to remember the mnemonic "CAPABG" which stands for Consensus, Atomic Broadcast, Agreement, Byzantine Agreement and Generals.
