
### Deadlock Prevention for the Notes of the Unit 3 - Distributed Deadlock Detection in the Subject of Distributed Systems

Deadlock prevention is an important concept to understand when studying distributed systems. This section will provide an overview of deadlock prevention techniques, including:

* Resource Allocation Graphs: Resource allocation graphs are used to detect deadlocks in distributed systems. This technique involves creating a graph of resources and processes and then analyzing the graph for cycles.
* Mutual Exclusion: Mutual exclusion is a technique used to ensure that only one process can access a resource at a given time. This technique is used to prevent deadlocks in distributed systems.
* Wait-For Graphs: Wait-for graphs are used to detect deadlocks in distributed systems. This technique involves creating a graph of processes and then analyzing the graph for cycles.
* Timeouts: Timeouts are used to detect deadlocks in distributed systems. This technique involves setting a timeout for each process and if the timeout expires, the process is terminated.
* Deadlock Avoidance: Deadlock avoidance is a technique used to prevent deadlocks in distributed systems. This technique involves assigning priorities to processes and resources and ensuring that the highest priority process is always granted access to the resource.
