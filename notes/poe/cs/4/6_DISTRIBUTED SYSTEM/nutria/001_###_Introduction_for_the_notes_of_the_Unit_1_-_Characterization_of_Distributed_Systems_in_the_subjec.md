
### Introduction for the notes of the Unit 1 - Characterization of Distributed Systems in the subject of DISTRIBUTED SYSTEM

Distributed systems are computer systems that are composed of multiple autonomous computers that communicate through a computer network. The computers interact with each other in order to achieve a common goal. Distributed systems can range from small, local networks to large, global networks.

Distributed systems are used for a variety of applications, including distributed computing, distributed databases, distributed file systems, distributed web services, distributed communication networks, and distributed control systems.

Characterization of distributed systems is the process of understanding the behavior of distributed systems. It involves analyzing the system's architecture, its components, and its interactions with other systems. Characterization of distributed systems is important in order to understand the system's behavior and its performance.

Characterization of distributed systems involves analyzing the system's architecture, its components, and its interactions with other systems. It also involves analyzing the system's communication protocols, its fault tolerance, its security, its scalability, and its performance.

* S - Scalability: Analyze the system's scalability.
* P - Performance: Analyze the system's performance.
