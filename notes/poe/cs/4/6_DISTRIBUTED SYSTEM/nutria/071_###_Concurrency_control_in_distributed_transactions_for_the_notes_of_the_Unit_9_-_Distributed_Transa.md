
### Concurrency Control in Distributed Transactions

Distributed transactions are transactions that are spread across multiple computers and databases. Concurrency control is the process of ensuring that multiple transactions can be executed concurrently without conflicting with each other.

##### Advantages

- Increased scalability: By allowing multiple transactions to be executed concurrently, distributed transactions can handle large amounts of data more efficiently.
- Improved reliability: By allowing multiple transactions to be executed concurrently, distributed transactions can ensure that data is not lost in the event of a system failure.

##### Disadvantages

- Increased complexity: Concurrency control requires complex algorithms and protocols to ensure that transactions do not conflict with one another.
- Increased overhead: Concurrency control requires additional resources to ensure that transactions do not conflict with one another.

##### Mnemonics and Learning Tricks

- **C**oncurrency **C**ontrol in **D**istributed **T**ransactions = **CCDT**
- **C**oncurrency **C**ontrol **M**ust **B**e **E**xecuted **C**orrectly = **CCMBEC**
