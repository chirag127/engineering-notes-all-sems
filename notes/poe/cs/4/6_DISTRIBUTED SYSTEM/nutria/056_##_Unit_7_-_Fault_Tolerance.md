
## Unit 7 - Fault Tolerance

Fault tolerance is a system's ability to maintain its normal operations despite the presence of hardware or software errors. Fault tolerance is an important part of any system that must remain operational despite the presence of errors.

There are three main types of fault tolerance:

1. **Redundancy:** Redundancy is the use of multiple copies of data or components to increase reliability. Redundancy can be used to increase the reliability of a system by providing a backup in case of a failure.

2. **Error detection and correction:** Error detection and correction is the use of algorithms and techniques to detect and correct errors. This can be used to ensure that data is accurate and reliable.

3. **Failover:** Failover is the process of switching from one system to another in case of a failure. This ensures that the system remains operational in the event of a failure.

In addition to these three main types of fault tolerance, there are several other techniques that can be used to increase reliability. These include load balancing, replication, and distributed computing.
