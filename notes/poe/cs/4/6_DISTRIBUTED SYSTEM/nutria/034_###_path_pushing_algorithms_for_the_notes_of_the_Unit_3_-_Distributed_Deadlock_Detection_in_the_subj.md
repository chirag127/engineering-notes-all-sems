
### Path Pushing Algorithms for the Notes of the Unit 3 - Distributed Deadlock Detection in the Subject of DISTRIBUTED SYSTEM

Path pushing algorithms are used for distributed deadlock detection. They are based on the idea of exchanging messages between processes in order to detect cycles in the system. In this algorithm, each process pushes its own path through the system and detects a deadlock if a cycle is present.

* Advantages:
  * Path pushing algorithms are more efficient than other deadlock detection algorithms.
  * They are able to detect deadlocks quickly and accurately.
  * They require less communication than other algorithms.

* Disadvantages:
  * They can be difficult to implement.
  * They require more memory than other algorithms.

* Mnemonics and Learning Tricks:
  * **P**ush **P**aths **T**hrough **S**ystem - PPTS
  * **D**etect **C**ycles **F**or **D**eadlock - DCFD
