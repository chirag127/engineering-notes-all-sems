### Introduction for the notes of the Unit 1 - Characterization of Distributed Systems in the subject of DISTRIBUTED SYSTEM

Distributed systems are an essential part of modern computing. They allow for the creation of robust, scalable, and fault-tolerant systems that can handle large amounts of data and traffic. In this unit, we will explore the fundamental concepts of distributed systems and their characteristics.

#### What is a Distributed System?

A distributed system is a collection of independent computers that work together to provide users with access to shared resources. These resources can include data, storage, and processing power. Distributed systems are designed to function as a single unit, even though they are composed of many separate computers.

#### Characteristics of Distributed Systems

Distributed systems have several characteristics that make them different from traditional centralized systems. Some of the most important characteristics of distributed systems include:

- **Concurrency:** Distributed systems allow multiple users to access resources simultaneously, which improves system performance and scalability.
- **Fault Tolerance:** Distributed systems are designed to be resilient to failures. If one component of the system fails, other components can take over its responsibilities to ensure smooth operation.
- **Scalability:** Distributed systems can handle large amounts of data and traffic by adding more computers to the system as needed.
- **Transparency:** Distributed systems hide the complexity of their design from users, making it easy for them to access resources without worrying about the underlying infrastructure.

#### Mnemonics and Learning Tricks

- Remember the acronym "CFTS" to recall the characteristics of distributed systems: Concurrency, Fault Tolerance, Scalability, and Transparency.
- Think of a distributed system as a team of superheroes working together to save the day. Each superhero has their own unique powers and abilities, but together they can accomplish great things.
