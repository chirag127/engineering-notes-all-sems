## Unit 4 - Agreement Protocols

In the world of computer science, an agreement protocol is a set of rules that governs the way in which multiple parties come to an agreement on a particular issue. This unit will cover some of the most commonly used agreement protocols, including the following:

1. Paxos
2. Raft
3. Byzantine Fault Tolerance

### Paxos

Paxos is a consensus algorithm that is used to reach agreement on a particular value among a group of distributed processes. It is widely used in distributed computing systems, particularly in distributed databases.

#### Mnemonic

A helpful mnemonic for remembering the Paxos consensus algorithm is to think of it as a group of people trying to agree on where to go for dinner. The group needs to agree on a restaurant, but there may be disagreements about the type of food or location. The group must come to a consensus on these issues in order to make a decision and move forward.

### Raft

Raft is a consensus algorithm that is designed to be easy to understand and implement. It is also used to reach agreement on a particular value among a group of distributed processes. Raft is particularly well-suited for use in systems that need to be highly available and fault-tolerant.

#### Mnemonic

A helpful mnemonic for remembering the Raft consensus algorithm is to think of it as a group of people on a raft trying to navigate a river. The group needs to work together to steer the raft in the right direction and avoid obstacles. In order to do this, they need to communicate effectively and come to a consensus on the best way to proceed.

### Byzantine Fault Tolerance

Byzantine Fault Tolerance (BFT) is a consensus algorithm that is designed to be able to tolerate faults, including malicious behavior, among distributed processes. It is particularly well-suited for use in systems that require high levels of security and reliability.

#### Mnemonic

A helpful mnemonic for remembering the Byzantine Fault Tolerance consensus algorithm is to think of it as a group of people trying to agree on a secret code word. The group needs to make sure that everyone knows the code word, but they also need to make sure that no one else knows it. In order to do this, they need to communicate securely and establish trust among the group.

Overall, understanding agreement protocols is essential for anyone working in the field of distributed computing. By learning about these protocols and their underlying principles, you will be better equipped to design and implement systems that are highly available, fault-tolerant, and secure.
