### Elasticity in Cloud for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction To Cloud Computing in the subject of Cloud Computing

Elasticity in Cloud Computing is the ability to automatically and quickly scale up or down the computing resources (e.g., processing power, storage, memory) based on the workload demands in real-time. This feature is one of the core benefits of cloud computing that helps organizations to save costs and improve their business agility. In this section, we will discuss the key concepts related to elasticity in cloud computing.

#### Key Concepts

1. Auto-scaling: Auto-scaling is a feature that allows cloud services to adjust the computing resources automatically based on the workload demands. It helps to maintain the desired performance levels and avoid over-provisioning or under-provisioning of resources. Auto-scaling is typically implemented using pre-defined policies or rules that specify the conditions for scaling up or down the resources.

2. Horizontal scaling: Horizontal scaling is the process of adding more computing resources (e.g., servers, instances, containers) to a cloud-based application to handle increasing workload demands. It helps to distribute the workload across multiple resources and improve the overall performance and availability of the application. Horizontal scaling is often used in conjunction with auto-scaling to provide seamless scalability and high availability.

3. Vertical scaling: Vertical scaling is the process of increasing or decreasing the computing resources (e.g., CPU, RAM) of a single instance or a virtual machine in a cloud environment. It is usually done to optimize the performance of a specific application or service. Vertical scaling can be done manually or automatically, depending on the workload demands.

4. Elastic Load Balancing: Elastic Load Balancing is a service provided by cloud providers that automatically distributes incoming network traffic across multiple compute resources (e.g., instances, containers) to improve the availability and fault tolerance of the application. It helps to ensure that no single resource is overloaded and provides a seamless user experience to the end-users.

#### Advantages of Elasticity in Cloud Computing

1. Cost savings: Elasticity allows organizations to pay only for the resources they need and avoid the costs associated with over-provisioning or under-provisioning of resources. This helps to reduce the overall IT costs and improve the ROI of cloud computing.

2. Improved business agility: Elasticity enables organizations to quickly respond to changing business requirements and adapt to new market conditions. This helps to improve the competitiveness and agility of the business.

3. Increased availability: Elasticity helps to ensure that the application is always available and responsive to the end-users, even during periods of high traffic or unexpected spikes in workload demands.

4. Better performance: Elasticity helps to optimize the use of computing resources and improve the overall performance and scalability of the application.

#### Mnemonic

A simple mnemonic to remember the key concepts related to elasticity in cloud computing is "AVEB" which stands for Auto-scaling, Vertical scaling, Elastic Load Balancing, and Business agility. This can be helpful to remember the core concepts and their benefits in a quick and easy-to-remember way.

#### Conclusion

Elasticity is a fundamental feature of cloud computing that provides organizations with the flexibility and scalability to meet their changing business requirements. It helps to optimize the use of computing resources, reduce costs, and improve the overall performance and availability of applications. Understanding the key concepts related to elasticity can help organizations to leverage this feature effectively and gain a competitive advantage in the market.
