### Google App Engine for the notes of the Unit 5 - Cloud Technologies And Advancements Hadoop in the subject of Cloud Computing

Google App Engine (GAE) is a service that lets you run web applications on Google's infrastructure. You simply upload your application and it scales automatically to serve web traffic.

Key points:

- It's a Platform as a Service (PaaS) offering. You don't need to manage servers or scaling - GAE does that for you.
- Applications run on Google's infrastructure.
- Supports apps written in several languages: Python, Java, PHP, Go and Ruby.
- High scalability - can scale to serve a lot of traffic.
- Offers high availability - Google handles replication and hardware failures for you.
- Free quotas to get started - you only pay for what you use.
- Suitable for medium-traffic web apps. If you need extremely high performance/scale, a IaaS solution may be better.

Advantages:

- Easy to get started - no infrastructure to manage.
- Scales easily to handle variable traffic.
- High availability/reliability (using Google's infrastructure).
- Supports several popular languages.

Disadvantages:

- Limited choice of languages and databases compared to other PaaS/IaaS options.
- Vendor lock-in - difficult to move away from GAE.
- Costs could be higher than a self-managed solution at very large scales.

Mnemonics/Learning tricks:

- Think "Get Application running Easily" -> Google App Engine
- "Just write code, we handle the rest" - GAE's main selling point is handling scaling/infra for you.
