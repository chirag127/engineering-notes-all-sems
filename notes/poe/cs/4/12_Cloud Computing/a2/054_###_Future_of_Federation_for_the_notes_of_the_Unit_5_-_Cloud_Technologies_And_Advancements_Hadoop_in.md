### Future of Federation

The future of federation in cloud computing looks promising with the following trends:

- Increased adoption of hybrid and multi-cloud strategies: As companies adopt hybrid and multi-cloud strategies, the need for federation increases to provide a unified view and manageability across clouds. Federation enables seamless data and application mobility across clouds.
- Growth in scale of data and computing resources: With the rapid growth in data volumes and scale of computing resources, federation will be essential to aggregate resources and services from multiple clouds to meet scalability and performance needs.
- Advancements in standards and interfaces: Emerging standards and interfaces like Openstack and OCCI are making cross-cloud interoperability and federation more feasible. Continued progress on standards will further boost federation.
- AI and Machine Learning advancements: As AI and Machine Learning techniques advance, federation will be crucial to bring together fragmented data sources and computing resources from multiple clouds to train powerful ML models.
- Event-driven and serverless computing growth: The increasing adoption of event-driven and serverless architectures will necessitate federation to compose functions and events across cloud providers. Federation will enable the construction of distributed event-driven systems spanning multiple clouds.

Some potential challenges for federation to address include security and access control spanning clouds, consistent management and monitoring, latency issues, and lack of support for certain cloud services across providers. However, federation has promising prospects to address hybrid and multi-cloud complexities and unlock more powerful use cases. Overall, federation is poised to grow in importance as a key functionality for the successful adoption of multi-cloud strategies.
