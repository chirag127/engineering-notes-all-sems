### On‐demand Provisioning

- On‐demand provisioning refers to the ability of cloud computing resources to be provided as and when required by the users without any delay.
- The resources like servers, storage, applications, services, etc. can be provisioned as soon as the user requests for them.
- This allows businesses and users to scale up or scale down the resources instantly as per the demands.
- The resources are available as a utility and the users only pay for what they use.
- This eliminates the need for excess procurement of resources and reduces wastage.
- The automatic provisioning of resources on‐demand increases the agility, flexibility and scalability of cloud computing.

**Mnemonics:** *Demand provision, utility pay, agility gain!*

**Advantages:**
- Scalability and elasticity: Resources can be scaled instantly on demand.
- Cost efficiency: Pay for only what is used. No wastage.
- Increased speed and agility: Fast provisioning increases business agility.
- Reduced planning: No need to predict and provision resources in advance.

**Disadvantages:**
- Vendor dependency: Completely dependent on the cloud provider for provisioning.
- Technical expertise: Requires technical knowledge to manage scaling of resources.
- Monitoring challenges: Difficult to monitor and optimize the use of resources.

**Examples:** Provisioning additional servers on demand to handle increased traffic on a website. Provisioning more storage on demand to store business data.
