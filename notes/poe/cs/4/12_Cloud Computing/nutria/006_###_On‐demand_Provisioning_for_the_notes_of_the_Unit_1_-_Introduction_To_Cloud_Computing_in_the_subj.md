

### On‐demand Provisioning for the Notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Cloud Computing in the Subject of Cloud Computing

On-demand provisioning is an important concept in cloud computing. It refers to the ability to quickly and easily access computing resources such as servers, storage, networks, and applications, as and when needed. This allows users to access the resources they need without having to purchase, configure, and manage them themselves.

The main advantages of on-demand provisioning are:

1. Cost savings: By using on-demand provisioning, users can access the resources they need without having to make a large upfront investment. This can help to reduce costs and improve efficiency.

2. Flexibility: On-demand provisioning allows users to access the resources they need when they need them, without having to commit to a long-term contract. This can help to ensure that resources are used efficiently and that users can access the resources they need when they need them.

3. Scalability: On-demand provisioning allows users to scale up or down their resources as needed, without having to purchase additional hardware or software. This can help to ensure that resources are used efficiently and that users can access the resources they need when they need them.
