Federation is a distributed computing model that allows for the seamless sharing of data, resources, and services across multiple systems. It is a key component of cloud computing and is used to enable scalability, reliability, and performance. In the future, federation will become even more important as cloud computing becomes more pervasive.

- Improved scalability: By allowing for the sharing of resources across multiple systems, federation can enable a much larger system than would be possible with a single system.
- Improved reliability: By allowing for the sharing of services across multiple systems, federation can enable a much more reliable system.
- Improved performance: By allowing for the sharing of data across multiple systems, federation can enable a much faster system.
- Reduced costs: By allowing for the sharing of resources across multiple systems, federation can reduce costs associated with individual systems.
