
### Elasticity in Cloud for the Notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Cloud Computing in the Subject of Cloud Computing

Elasticity in cloud computing is the ability of a cloud system to scale up or down its resources in order to meet the demands of its users. This is achieved by dynamically allocating resources such as computing power, storage, and networking capabilities. 

**Mnemonics and Learning Tricks**

* ELASTIC: Elasticity, Load Balancing, Availability, Scalability, Transferability, Isolation, and Cost-effectiveness
* EASE: Elasticity, Availability, Scalability, and Efficiency

**Advantages**

* Cost efficiency - Cloud services are typically charged on a pay-as-you-go basis, which can be more cost-effective than traditional IT solutions.
* Flexibility - Cloud services can be easily scaled up or down to meet changing demands.
* Reliability - Cloud services are hosted on highly reliable infrastructure and are backed up by multiple levels of redundancy.
* Security - Cloud services are protected by multiple layers of security, including encryption and identity management.

**Disadvantages**

* Lack of control - Cloud services are typically managed by the provider, so users have limited control over the service.
* Vendor lock-in - Cloud services are typically proprietary and may be difficult to switch from one provider to another.
* Network latency - Cloud services are typically hosted in remote locations and may be subject to latency issues.
* Compliance risks - Cloud services may not be compliant with all industry regulations.
