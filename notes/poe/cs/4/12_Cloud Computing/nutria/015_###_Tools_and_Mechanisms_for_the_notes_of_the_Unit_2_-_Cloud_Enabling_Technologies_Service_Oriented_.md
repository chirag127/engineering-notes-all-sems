
### Tools and Mechanisms for the notes of the Unit 2 - Cloud Enabling Technologies Service Oriented Architecture in the subject of Cloud Computing

* **Service Oriented Architecture (SOA)** is an architectural pattern used in software development. It is based on the concept of services, which are self-contained units of functionality that can be combined to create larger applications. SOA is used to create distributed applications that can be easily maintained and scaled. 

* **Web Services** are self-contained, modular applications that can be accessed over the internet using standard protocols such as HTTP. Web services are used to provide access to applications and data over the internet, and they are often used in SOA architectures.

* **Service Level Agreements (SLAs)** are agreements between two or more parties that define the level of service that will be provided. SLAs are used to ensure that services are provided in a timely and reliable manner.

* **Service Brokers** are systems that manage the delivery of services to clients. They are responsible for managing the availability of services, negotiating SLAs, and ensuring that the services are delivered according to the agreed-upon terms.

* **Service Discovery** is the process of locating services and determining their capabilities. It is used to identify services that can be used to fulfill a particular need.

* **Service Composition** is the process of combining multiple services to create a larger application. It is used to create complex applications from existing services.

* **Service Orchestration** is the process of managing the lifecycle of services. It is responsible for managing the deployment, configuration, and monitoring of services.

* **Service Virtualization** is the process of creating virtual services that can be used in place of physical services. It is used to create test environments and to reduce the cost of providing services.
