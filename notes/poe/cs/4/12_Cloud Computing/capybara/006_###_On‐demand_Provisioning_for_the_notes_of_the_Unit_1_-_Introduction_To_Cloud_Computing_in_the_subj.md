### On‐demand Provisioning for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction To Cloud Computing in the subject of Cloud Computing

On-demand provisioning is one of the key features of cloud computing that enables users to rapidly provision and de-provision computing resources as needed. This feature helps users to save time and money by only paying for resources that are being used.

Here are some important points to remember about on-demand provisioning:

- On-demand provisioning allows users to quickly and easily provision computing resources, such as virtual machines, databases, and storage, on an as-needed basis.
- Users can specify the amount of resources they need and the duration for which they need them, and the cloud provider will automatically allocate those resources.
- On-demand provisioning is a key feature of cloud computing because it allows users to avoid the upfront costs of buying and maintaining their own hardware and software.
- On-demand provisioning also enables users to scale their resources up or down as needed, depending on their changing needs.
- One mnemonic to remember the benefits of on-demand provisioning is "pay for what you use". This means that users only pay for the resources they actually use, rather than having to pay for a fixed amount of resources upfront.
- Another helpful trick to remember is to think of on-demand provisioning as a "virtual vending machine". Just as you can select and purchase items from a vending machine as needed, you can select and provision computing resources from the cloud as needed.

Overall, on-demand provisioning is a key feature of cloud computing that enables users to quickly and easily provision computing resources as needed, without incurring upfront costs or having to maintain their own hardware and software. By remembering the "pay for what you use" and "virtual vending machine" mnemonics, you can better understand the benefits of on-demand provisioning and its role in cloud computing.
