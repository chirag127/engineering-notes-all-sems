

### Intro to Neural Nets for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of Deep Learning

Neural networks are a type of machine learning algorithm that can be used to solve complex problems. They are based on the idea of artificial neurons, which are mathematical functions that can be used to approximate any function. Neural networks are composed of layers of neurons, which are connected together in a network. Each layer of neurons contains a set of weights and biases that are used to adjust the output of the network. 

A neural network can be used to solve a variety of tasks, such as image recognition, natural language processing, and forecasting. Neural networks are also used in many applications, such as self-driving cars, facial recognition, and fraud detection.

There are several types of neural networks, including convolutional neural networks, recurrent neural networks, and feedforward neural networks. Each type of neural network has its own advantages and disadvantages, and can be used in different applications.

Mnemonics and Learning Tricks:

- Neural networks are like a network of neurons, each of which is connected to the others and can adjust their output.
- Think of a neural network as a set of layers, each of which contains a set of weights and biases.
- Convolutional neural networks are used for image recognition, while recurrent neural networks are used for natural language processing.
- Feedforward neural networks are used for forecasting.
- Neural networks can be trained using backpropagation, which is a method of adjusting the weights and biases in the network.
