
## Unit 5 - CASE STUDY AND APPLICATIONS

Case studies are an important tool used in the field of applied research. They are used to describe and analyze a particular event or situation in order to gain insights into a larger problem or issue. Case studies are also used to evaluate the effectiveness of different interventions and to identify best practices.

Case studies are typically structured around a set of questions that guide the researcher in their investigation. These questions can include:

- What is the context of the case study?
- What are the key issues and challenges?
- What are the key stakeholders and how do they interact?
- What are the potential solutions and what are the implications of each?
- What is the best solution and why?

In addition to answering these questions, case studies can also be used to identify trends and patterns, to develop theories, and to assess the effectiveness of interventions.

When using case studies, it is important to keep in mind that the data collected may be limited and that the results may be subject to bias. It is also important to consider the ethical implications of conducting a case study.

One of the key advantages of case studies is that they can be used to explore a wide range of topics and to uncover new insights. They can also be used to identify best practices and to develop new strategies. They are also useful for evaluating the effectiveness of interventions and for developing theories.

Case studies can also be used to explore the implications of different interventions, to identify trends and patterns, and to assess the effectiveness of different strategies. They can also be used to develop hypotheses and to test them in the real world.

Case studies can be used in a variety of fields, including education, business, health, and social work. They can also be used to explore a range of topics, such as consumer behaviour, public policy, and the effectiveness of interventions.

Finally, case studies can be used to evaluate the effectiveness of different interventions, to identify best practices, and to develop theories. They can also be used to explore the implications of different interventions and to assess the effectiveness of different strategies.
