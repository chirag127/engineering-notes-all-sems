
### Recurrent networks for the notes of the Unit 4 - OPTIMIZATION AND GENERALIZATION in the subject of Deep Learning

* Recurrent networks are a type of artificial neural network that are used to process sequential data. They are able to remember previous inputs and use them to inform their current output. This makes them ideal for tasks such as language modeling and time series forecasting.

* Recurrent networks can be trained with backpropagation through time (BPTT), which is an extension of the standard backpropagation algorithm. This allows the network to learn from sequences of data by propagating errors back in time.

* Optimization is an important part of training recurrent networks. Common optimization algorithms include stochastic gradient descent (SGD), Adagrad, and RMSprop.

* Regularization is another important technique for training recurrent networks. Common regularization techniques include dropout, L2 regularization, and batch normalization.

* Generalization is the ability of a model to perform well on unseen data. In recurrent networks, generalization can be improved by using a larger training dataset and by using regularization and optimization techniques.

* Mnemonics and learning tricks can be useful for learning recurrent networks. For example, a common mnemonic for remembering the different types of recurrent networks is "LSTM: Long Short-Term Memory".
