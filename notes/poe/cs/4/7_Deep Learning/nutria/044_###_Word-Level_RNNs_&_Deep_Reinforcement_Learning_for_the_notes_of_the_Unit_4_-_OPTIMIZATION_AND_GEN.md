
### Word-Level RNNs & Deep Reinforcement Learning for the notes of the Unit 4 - OPTIMIZATION AND GENERALIZATION in the subject of Deep Learning

* Word-Level RNNs are a type of Recurrent Neural Network which are used to generate text. They are able to generate text one word at a time, which makes them suitable for tasks such as language translation, text summarization, and text generation.

* Deep Reinforcement Learning (DRL) is a type of machine learning that uses rewards and punishments to train an AI agent to complete a task. It is based on the idea of reinforcement learning, where an AI agent learns to complete a task by trial and error. DRL is used in applications such as robotics, autonomous driving, and game playing.

* Optimization is the process of finding the best solution to a problem. It is used in machine learning to minimize the cost function and maximize the accuracy of the model.

* Generalization is the ability of a machine learning algorithm to make accurate predictions on unseen data. It is a key factor in the success of a machine learning algorithm, as it allows the algorithm to make predictions on data that it has not seen before.

* Mnemonics and learning tricks are techniques used to help remember facts and concepts. These can be useful when learning about Word-Level RNNs and Deep Reinforcement Learning, as they can help to quickly recall important information.
