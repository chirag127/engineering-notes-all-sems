
### Neural networks as universal function approximates for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of Deep Learning

Neural networks are a powerful tool for approximating complex functions. They are commonly used in machine learning and deep learning algorithms to identify patterns in data, and to make predictions or decisions based on those patterns.

Neural networks are composed of neurons, each of which is connected to other neurons. Each neuron receives input from other neurons, processes the input, and then sends output to other neurons. This process of input-processing-output is repeated until the desired output is achieved.

Neural networks can be used to approximate any function, no matter how complex. This is because neural networks are made up of a large number of neurons that can be connected in any way. This allows the network to learn any function that it is exposed to.

Neural networks can be used for a variety of tasks, such as image recognition, language translation, and speech recognition. They are also used in robotics, where they are used to control the movements of robots.

Neural networks can be trained using various algorithms, such as backpropagation and gradient descent. These algorithms allow the network to adjust its weights in order to minimize the error between the desired output and the actual output.

Neural networks can also be used to solve problems that are too complex for traditional algorithms. This is because neural networks can learn to recognize patterns in data that traditional algorithms cannot.

Neural networks have many advantages, such as their ability to learn any function, their ability to recognize patterns, and their ability to solve complex problems. However, they also have some disadvantages, such as their need for large amounts of data, their high computational cost, and their tendency to overfit.
