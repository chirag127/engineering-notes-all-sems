
### What a shallow network computes for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of Deep Learning

A shallow network is a type of neural network that is composed of a single layer of neurons. The neurons are connected to each other, and the input is sent through the neurons to the output. The output is then used to make predictions and decisions.

Shallow networks are used for tasks such as classification and regression. They are particularly useful for tasks that require minimal complexity and are not too computationally intensive.

The main idea behind a shallow network is that it can be used to quickly and accurately classify data. The neurons in the network are trained to recognize patterns and make predictions based on the data that is inputted.

The advantages of shallow networks are that they are relatively easy to train and can be used for a variety of tasks. They are also relatively fast to train and can be used for real-time applications.

The disadvantages of shallow networks are that they are limited in their ability to learn complex patterns, and can be easily overfitted. They also require a large amount of data to train them effectively.

A good mnemonic to remember the concept of shallow networks is "Simple, Fast, and Accurate." This mnemonic emphasizes the advantages of shallow networks and can help you remember the concept.
