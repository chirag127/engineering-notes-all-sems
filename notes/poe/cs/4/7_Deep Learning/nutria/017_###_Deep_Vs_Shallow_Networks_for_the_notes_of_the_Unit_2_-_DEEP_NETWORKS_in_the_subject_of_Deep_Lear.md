
### Deep Vs Shallow Networks for the notes of the Unit 2 - DEEP NETWORKS in the subject of Deep Learning

Deep networks and shallow networks are two types of artificial neural networks (ANNs) used in machine learning and deep learning. Deep networks have more layers than shallow networks and are used to solve more complex tasks.

**Mnemonics:**

- **D**eep **N**etworks **A**re **D**eeper than **S**hallow **N**etworks

**Advantages of Deep Networks:**

- Deep networks can learn more complex patterns in data, allowing them to make more accurate predictions than shallow networks
- Deep networks can learn hierarchical representations of data, allowing them to capture more information than shallow networks
- Deep networks can learn non-linear relationships between inputs and outputs, allowing them to model more complex phenomena than shallow networks

**Disadvantages of Deep Networks:**

- Deep networks require more data to train than shallow networks
- Deep networks require more computing power to train than shallow networks
- Deep networks are more prone to overfitting than shallow networks

**Examples of Deep Networks:**

- Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs)
- Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs)
- Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs)

**Applications of Deep Networks:**

- Image recognition and classification
- Natural language processing
- Speech recognition
- Time series forecasting
- Autonomous driving
