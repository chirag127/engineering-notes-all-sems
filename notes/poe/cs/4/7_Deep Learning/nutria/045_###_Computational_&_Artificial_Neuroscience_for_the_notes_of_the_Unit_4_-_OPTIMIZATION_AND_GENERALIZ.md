
### Computational & Artificial Neuroscience for the Notes of the Unit 4 - OPTIMIZATION AND GENERALIZATION in the Subject of Deep Learning

* **Optimization** is a process of finding the best solution to a given problem. It involves finding the best combination of parameters, weights, and hyperparameters that will yield the best performance. Optimization can be used to improve the accuracy of a model, reduce its complexity, or reduce its training time.

* **Generalization** is the ability of a model to make accurate predictions on unseen data. A model that is able to generalize well will be able to make accurate predictions on data it has never seen before. Generalization is an important concept in deep learning and is essential for creating models that can be used in real-world applications.

* **Computational Neuroscience** is the study of the brain and how it works. It involves understanding the structure and function of the brain, as well as how it processes information. Computational neuroscience is a rapidly growing field and is used in the development of artificial intelligence and machine learning algorithms.

* **Artificial Neural Networks (ANNs)** are a type of machine learning algorithm that is based on the structure and function of the brain. ANNs are composed of neurons, which are connected to each other and form a network. The neurons in the network are able to learn by adjusting the weights of the connections between them. ANNs are used in many areas of deep learning, including computer vision, natural language processing, and reinforcement learning.

* **Backpropagation** is an algorithm used to train neural networks. It involves adjusting the weights of the connections between neurons in the network in order to minimize the error of the model. Backpropagation is an important algorithm in deep learning and is used to train deep neural networks.

* **Regularization** is a technique used to prevent overfitting in neural networks. Regularization involves adding a penalty to the loss function of the model, which encourages the model to generalize better. Regularization is an important technique for improving the accuracy and generalization of deep learning models.

Learning tricks are techniques used to help students understand concepts, such as using diagrams, analogies, and visualizations. Mnemonics and learning tricks can be used to help students understand and remember concepts in computational and artificial neuroscience.
