
## Unit 2 - DEEP NETWORKS

Deep networks are a type of artificial neural network (ANN) with multiple hidden layers between the input and output layers. They are used in a variety of applications, such as image and speech recognition, natural language processing, and robotics.

Deep networks are powerful because they can learn complex relationships between the input and output data. They are also more accurate than shallow networks, which have fewer layers.

Mnemonics and Learning Tricks:

1. Think of deep networks as a "deep" tree, with multiple layers of neurons branching off from the input layer. 
2. Each layer of neurons is responsible for recognizing a certain pattern in the data. 
3. The deeper the network, the more complex patterns it can recognize. 
4. The more layers there are, the more accurate the network will be. 

Advantages:

1. Deep networks can learn complex relationships between the input and output data. 
2. Deep networks are more accurate than shallow networks. 
3. Deep networks can handle large amounts of data. 
4. Deep networks are more efficient than shallow networks. 

Disadvantages:

1. Deep networks are more difficult to train than shallow networks. 
2. Deep networks require more data to train accurately. 
3. Deep networks are more computationally expensive than shallow networks. 
4. Deep networks are prone to overfitting. 

Examples:

1. Image recognition: Deep networks are used to classify images based on their content. 
2. Speech recognition: Deep networks are used to recognize speech and convert it into text. 
3. Natural language processing: Deep networks are used to understand natural language and generate responses. 
4. Robotics: Deep networks are used to control the movement of robots. 

Applications:

1. Autonomous driving: Deep networks are used to control self-driving cars. 
2. Medical diagnosis: Deep networks are used to diagnose diseases and recommend treatments. 
3. Recommendation systems: Deep networks are used to recommend products and services to users. 
4. Fraud detection: Deep networks are used to detect fraudulent transactions.
