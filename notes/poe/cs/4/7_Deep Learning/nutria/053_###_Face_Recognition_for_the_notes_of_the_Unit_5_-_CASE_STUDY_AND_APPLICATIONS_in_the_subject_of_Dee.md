
### Face Recognition for the Notes of the Unit 5 - CASE STUDY AND APPLICATIONS in the Subject of Deep Learning

Face recognition is a computer vision task of identifying and verifying a person from a digital image or video frame. It is one of the most popular applications of deep learning and is used in a variety of applications, such as security, surveillance, and computer vision-based human-computer interactions.

The face recognition process consists of two main steps:

1. **Feature Extraction**: This involves extracting facial features from the image or video frame, such as eyes, nose, and mouth. This is done using a variety of methods, such as Principal Component Analysis (PCA) or Linear Discriminant Analysis (LDA).

2. **Classification**: This involves using the extracted features to classify the face into one of the known categories. This is done using a variety of techniques, such as Support Vector Machines (SVMs), k-Nearest Neighbors (KNNs), or Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs).

In this unit, we will discuss the various techniques used for face recognition and their applications, as well as the various challenges faced in the field.

Mnemonic: **FACE** - Feature Extraction, Classification, Application, and Challenges.
