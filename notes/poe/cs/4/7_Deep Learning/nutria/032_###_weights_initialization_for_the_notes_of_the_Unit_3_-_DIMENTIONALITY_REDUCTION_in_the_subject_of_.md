
### Weights Initialization for the Notes of the Unit 3 - Dimensionality Reduction in the Subject of Deep Learning

* Weight initialization is an important step in deep learning. It determines the range of values that the weights of a neural network can take.
* The most common way to initialize weights is by sampling from a uniform or normal distribution.
* It is important to choose the right range of values for initializing the weights. If the range is too small, the network will not be able to learn complex patterns. If the range is too large, the network may not converge.
* Xavier initialization is a popular method for initializing weights. It is based on the assumption that the variance of the inputs and outputs of a layer should be the same.
* Another popular method is He initialization, which is similar to Xavier initialization but uses a different scaling factor.
* There are also other methods for weight initialization, such as LeCun initialization and Orthogonal initialization.
* It is important to note that the choice of weight initialization can have a significant impact on the performance of a neural network. Therefore, it is important to choose the right method for the task at hand.
