

### Generative Adversarial Networks (GAN) for the notes of the Unit 2 - DEEP NETWORKS in the subject of Deep Learning

Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) are a type of deep learning architecture that consists of two neural networks: a generator and a discriminator. The generator is trained to generate data that is indistinguishable from real data, while the discriminator is trained to distinguish between real and generated data.

GANs have been used for a variety of tasks, including image generation, image-to-image translation, text-to-image generation, and more. They are particularly useful for tasks that involve generating high-quality images or videos.

Mnemonics and learning tricks:

- GANs are like a game between two neural networks: the generator creates data and the discriminator evaluates it.
- GANs can generate data that is indistinguishable from real data.
- GANs can be used for a variety of tasks, such as image generation, image-to-image translation, and text-to-image generation.
- GANs are particularly useful for tasks that involve generating high-quality images or videos.
