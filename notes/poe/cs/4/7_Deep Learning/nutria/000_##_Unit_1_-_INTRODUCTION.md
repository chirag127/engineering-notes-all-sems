
## Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION

This unit provides an introduction to the fundamentals of computer science. It covers topics such as the history of computing, basic computer architecture, the different types of computer hardware and software, and how computers are used in today's world.

**History of Computing**

Computing has been around since the 1950s. The first computers were large and expensive and could only be used by large corporations and government agencies. Over time, computers have become smaller, faster, and more affordable. Today, computers are used in nearly every aspect of life, from education to business to entertainment.

**Basic Computer Architecture**

A computer consists of two main components: hardware and software. Hardware includes the physical components of a computer, such as the processor, memory, and storage devices. Software includes the programs and operating systems that run on a computer.

**Types of Computer Hardware**

Computer hardware includes the processor, memory, storage, and input/output (I/O) devices. The processor is the "brain" of the computer, and is responsible for performing calculations and executing instructions. Memory stores data and instructions for the processor to use. Storage devices store data and programs for long-term use. Input/output (I/O) devices are used to send data to and from the computer.

**Types of Computer Software**

Computer software includes the programs and operating systems that run on a computer. Programs are specific instructions written to perform a task. Operating systems manage the resources of a computer and provide an interface for users to interact with the computer.

**Uses of Computers**

Computers are used in a variety of ways in today's world. They are used in education to help students learn, in business to help manage data and operations, in communication to help people stay connected, and in entertainment to provide people with entertainment. Computers are also used in medicine, science, engineering, and many other fields.
