
## Unit 4 - OPTIMIZATION AND GENERALIZATION

Optimization and generalization are two important concepts in machine learning. Optimization refers to the process of finding the best parameters for a given model. Generalization is the ability of a model to perform well on unseen data.

### Optimization

Optimization is the process of finding the best parameters for a given model. There are many different optimization algorithms, such as gradient descent, stochastic gradient descent, and Adam. Each of these algorithms has its own strengths and weaknesses, and it is important to choose the right algorithm for the task at hand.

### Generalization

Generalization is the ability of a model to perform well on unseen data. This is an important concept in machine learning, as it allows a model to generalize from the training data and make accurate predictions on unseen data points.

### Mnemonics and Learning Tricks

There are a few mnemonics and learning tricks that can help to understand optimization and generalization.

- **O**ptimization is **O**btaining the **P**erfect **T**hings.
- **G**eneralization is **G**enerally **A**pplying **L**earned **I**deas.
- **O**ptimization is **O**btaining the **B**est **P**arameters.
- **G**eneralization is **G**enerally **A**pplying **L**earned **R**ules.
