
### Batch Normalization for the Notes of the Unit 3 - Dimensionality Reduction in the Subject of Deep Learning

Batch normalization is a technique used to improve the performance of deep neural networks. It is a form of regularization that helps to reduce overfitting and improve the generalization of models. Batch normalization works by normalizing the inputs of each layer, so that the mean and variance of the inputs are the same across all layers. This helps to reduce the internal covariate shift, which is the difference in the distribution of inputs between layers.

**Mnemonic:** 
*BN: Batch Normalize inputs to reduce Internal Covariate Shift.*

**Advantages:**
* Improves the performance of deep neural networks.
* Reduces overfitting and improves generalization.
* Helps to reduce the internal covariate shift.

**Disadvantages:**
* Can lead to increased computational complexity in some cases.
* Can be difficult to tune properly.

**Applications:**
* Image recognition
* Natural language processing
* Speech recognition

**Example:**
Let's say we have a neural network with two hidden layers. We can use batch normalization to normalize the inputs of each layer so that the mean and variance of the inputs are the same across all layers. This helps to reduce the internal covariate shift, which is the difference in the distribution of inputs between layers. In this way, batch normalization can help to improve the performance of the neural network.
