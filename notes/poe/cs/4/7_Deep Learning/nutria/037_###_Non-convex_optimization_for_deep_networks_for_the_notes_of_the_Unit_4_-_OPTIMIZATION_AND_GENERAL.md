
### Non-convex optimization for deep networks for the notes of the Unit 4 - OPTIMIZATION AND GENERALIZATION in the subject of Deep Learning

Non-convex optimization is an important part of deep learning, as it is used to optimize the parameters of deep neural networks. It is a difficult problem, as the optimization problem is highly non-linear, and the number of parameters is usually very large. 

There are several approaches to solving non-convex optimization problems, including gradient descent, stochastic gradient descent, Newton's method, and quasi-Newton methods. Each of these methods has its own advantages and disadvantages, and the choice of which to use depends on the problem at hand. 

Mnemonics and learning tricks can be helpful in understanding non-convex optimization. For example, one way to remember the difference between gradient descent and stochastic gradient descent is to think of gradient descent as “going down a hill”, while stochastic gradient descent is like “jumping down a hill”. 

In addition, it is important to understand the concept of regularization, which is used to prevent overfitting in deep neural networks. Regularization techniques can include L1 and L2 regularization, dropout, and early stopping. 

Finally, it is important to understand the concept of hyperparameter optimization, which is used to find the best set of hyperparameters for a given model. Hyperparameter optimization techniques can include grid search, random search, and Bayesian optimization. 

Overall, non-convex optimization for deep networks is an important concept to understand in order to be successful in deep learning. With the right mnemonics and learning tricks, it can be easier to understand and apply these concepts in practice.
