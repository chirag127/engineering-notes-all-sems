
### Stochastic Optimization for the notes of the Unit 4 - OPTIMIZATION AND GENERALIZATION in the subject of Deep Learning

Stochastic Optimization is a method of optimization that uses randomness to find an optimal solution. It is commonly used in machine learning and deep learning to optimize models. It is an iterative process that uses random samples to find an optimal solution.

- Stochastic optimization algorithms use random samples to explore the search space and find an optimal solution.
- The goal of stochastic optimization is to find the global minimum of a function.
- Stochastic optimization algorithms work well in high-dimensional spaces.
- They are often used in machine learning and deep learning to optimize models.
- Stochastic optimization algorithms are usually fast and require fewer iterations than other optimization algorithms.
- They are also robust to noise and can handle non-convex functions.
- Common stochastic optimization algorithms include stochastic gradient descent, simulated annealing, genetic algorithms, and particle swarm optimization.
- Advantages of stochastic optimization include speed, robustness, and the ability to handle non-convex functions.
- Disadvantages of stochastic optimization include the need for large amounts of data and the potential for local minima.
