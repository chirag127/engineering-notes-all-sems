### Audio Wave Net

- Audio Wave Net is a neural network architecture for generating raw audio waveforms.
- It is a type of autoregressive model that predicts the next sample of an audio waveform based on previous samples.
- The network is made up of convolutional layers and dilated causal convolutions that allow it to capture long-range dependencies in audio.
- Training the network on a large dataset of audio waveforms enables it to learn the patterns and statistical relationships between audio samples to then generate novel waveforms.
- Some key advantages of Audio Wave Net are:

- It can generate diverse and flexible audio with controllable styles or instruments.
- The generated audio sounds quite natural and compelling to human listeners.
- It is a flexible framework that can be adapted to various audio applications like speech synthesis, music generation, adding effects to audio, etc.

- However, some disadvantages are:

- Training can be quite slow due to the autoregressive nature of the model.
- It may not work well for very high quality or hi-fi audio generation.
- The generated audio can suffer from repetitive or looping patterns, or have indistinct sounds.

- Overall, Audio Wave Net is an impressive deep learning model for raw audio generation with the potential for various applications. More research is focused on improving the quality, diversity, and speed of audio generation from these types of neural networks.
