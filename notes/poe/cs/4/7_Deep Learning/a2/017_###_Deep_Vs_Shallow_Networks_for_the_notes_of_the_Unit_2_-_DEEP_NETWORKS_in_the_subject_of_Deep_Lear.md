### Deep Vs Shallow Networks

- Shallow networks have only 1-2 hidden layers while deep networks have 3 or more hidden layers.
- Shallow networks can learn simple patterns but deep networks can learn complex patterns and abstractions.
- Deep networks require more data and computation power but can model complex functions.
- Some mnemonics for remembering:
-- Deeper the network, deeper the learning
-- Shallow nets - simple learnings, deep nets - complex learnings

Advantages of deep networks:

- Can learn complex patterns and features.
- Demonstrate high performance on tasks like computer vision, speech recognition, etc.
- Have multiple levels of abstraction which helps in learning hierarchical features.

Disadvantages of deep networks:

- Require large amounts of data to learn complex features and patterns.
- Take more time to train due to multiple layers and parameters.
- Prone to overfitting due to large number of parameters. Needs regularization.
- Difficult to interpret due to presence of multiple hidden layers.

Examples of deep networks:

- Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs)
- Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs)
- Long Short Term Memory Networks (LSTMs)

Applications of deep networks:

- Image classification
- Object detection
- Speech recognition
- Machine translation
- Time series forecasting
- Medical diagnosis
- Robotics
- etc.
