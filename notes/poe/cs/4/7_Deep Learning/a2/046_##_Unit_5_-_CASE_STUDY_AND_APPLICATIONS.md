## Unit 5 - CASE STUDY AND APPLICATIONS

**Applications of Machine Learning**

- Machine Learning powers many technologies we use every day:
- Recommendation Systems: Recommend products you might like based on your past purchases and behavior. E.g. Amazon recommendation system.
- Spam Detection: Identify and filter out unwanted emails. E.g. Gmail spam filter.
- Image Recognition: Identify and label objects, scenes and people in images. E.g. Facebook photo tagging.
- Speech Recognition: Convert speech to text. E.g. Siri, Alexa, Google Assistant.
- Machine Translation: Translate between languages. E.g. Google Translate.
- Robotics: Navigate and manipulate robots.
- Diagnosis Systems: Identify diseases based on symptoms and test results. E.g. IBM Watson.
- Forecasting: Predict future trends and patterns. E.g. Stock price forecasting.

**Case Studies**

- Netflix Recommendation System: Uses customer data and watching patterns to recommend movies and shows. Uses deep learning and neural networks.
- IBM Watson: Question answering system that won Jeopardy! Uses machine learning and NLP to understand language and provide answers. Used in medical diagnosis.
- Self-Driving Cars: Use machine learning and computer vision to detect surroundings and navigate. Still in development by companies like Waymo and Tesla.
- AlphaGo: Learned to play the game of Go at superhuman level by training on large datasets of human games using deep learning and reinforcement learning. Created by DeepMind.
