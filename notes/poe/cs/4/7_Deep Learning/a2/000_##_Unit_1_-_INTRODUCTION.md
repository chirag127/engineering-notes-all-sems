## Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION

1. Data Science - Data science is an interdisciplinary field that uses scientific methods, processes, algorithms and systems to extract knowledge and insights from data in various forms, both structured and unstructured.

- Mnemonic: DEMSIS - Data Science uses Experiments, Models, Statistics, Informatics and Software systems to extract insights from data.

2. Data - Data refers to the raw facts, numbers, or symbols that are collected, stored, and analyzed to draw conclusions and gain knowledge. Data can be qualitative or quantitative, structured or unstructured.

- Examples: Sales records, temperature readings, customer feedback, images, videos, etc.

3. Data Analysis - Data analysis is the process of inspecting, cleaning, transforming and modeling data with the goal of discovering useful information, informing conclusions and supporting decision making.

- Steps: Data Collection, Data Preparation, Data Exploration, Modeling, Evaluation and Interpretation.

4. Why Data Science? - Data science has a wide range of applications in various fields and provides the following benefits:

- Identify patterns and trends: Data science techniques can identify hidden patterns and trends in data that can provide business value. E.g. Product recommendations, forecasting trends.

- Make predictions: Data science models can analyze historical data and make predictions. E.g. Predict customer churn, forecast sales.

- Automate processes: Machine learning algorithms can automate processes and tasks like classification and clustering. E.g. Spam detection, Image classification.

- Gain insights: Data science enables us to gain powerful insights from data and find solutions to complex problems. E.g. Diagnose diseases, optimize processes.
