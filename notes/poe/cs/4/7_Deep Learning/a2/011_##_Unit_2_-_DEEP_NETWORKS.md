## Unit 2 - DEEP NETWORKS

Deep Neural Networks are a type of machine learning algorithm that are inspired by the hierarchical structure and function of the human brain. They are made up of input and output layers, with multiple hidden layers in between. Each layer consists of a set of neurons that transform the input data and pass it to the next layer.

Some key points about Deep Networks:

1. They are fed large amounts of data and learn complex patterns in the data to make predictions or decisions on new data.
2. They are made up of multiple hidden layers of parameters/weights that are tuned during training to produce the desired output.
3. They can learn highly complex nonlinear relationships between inputs and outputs.
4. They often outperform traditional machine learning methods and require less feature engineering.
5. They have achieved state-of-the-art results in tasks such as computer vision, speech recognition, natural language processing, etc.

A few things to keep in mind:

- Deep Networks are data hungry and require large datasets to learn adequately.
- They can be time consuming to train due to the number of parameters and layers.
- They are prone to overfitting due to high complexity, so regularization techniques are important.
- Hyperparameter tuning is crucial to achieving good performance.
- There is no one perfect architecture, it depends on the problem and task.

Here are some helpful tips and mnemonics for learning Deep Networks:

- Think of the layers as transforming the data from simple to complex representations.
- Remember " Depth wins " - more layers can lead to better performance.
- " WIDEC " - stands for Weight Initialization, Depth/width of network, Epochs/iterations for training, Cost function, and Choice of optimizer.
- Draw diagrams of the network architecture with inputs, outputs, and hidden layers to understand the flow of information.
- Code and experiment with different types of Deep Networks to understand how they work in practice.
