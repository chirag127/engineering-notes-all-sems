### Word-Level RNNs & Deep Reinforcement Learning for the notes of the Unit 4 - OPTIMIZATION AND GENERALIZATION in the subject of Deep Learning

- Word-level RNNs: Recurrent Neural Networks can be trained on the character or word level to generate text. Training on the word level can produce more coherent text than training on the character level. In word-level RNNs, the input is a sequence of words and the output is a probability distribution over the next word in the sequence.
- Mnemonic: Think of word-level RNNs as predicting the next word in a sentence based on the previous words.
- Advantages: Can generate more coherent paragraphs of text than character-level models. Less noisy outputs.
- Disadvantages: Limited to predicting only the next most probable word. May not be able to generate very long or creative pieces of text.
- Applications: Text summarization, dialogue systems, machine translation.

- Deep Reinforcement Learning: Reinforcement learning uses feedback from the environment to determine the optimal way of achieving the highest reward. Deep reinforcement learning uses neural networks as the function approximator to learn the optimal policy. It has been used to solve complex control problems that were previously difficult to solve using conventional reinforcement learning.
- Mnemonic: The agent learns by trial-and-error using feedback from the environment.
- Examples: Playing games like chess and Go, robot control, network routing, etc.
- Advantages: Can solve complex problems that are difficult to solve analytically.
- Disadvantages: Require a large amount of data to train the neural networks. May be unstable or diverge if not configured properly.
