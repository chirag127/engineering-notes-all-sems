### Intro to Neural Nets

- A neural network is a series of algorithms that attempts to identify relationships between inputs and outputs and uses those relationships to make predictions or decisions on new data.
- They are inspired by biological neural networks in the brain that activate in response to stimuli.
- They are made up of interconnected nodes that operate like neurons firing and connections that operate like synapses.
- As the network is exposed to large amounts of data, it learns by adjusting the strengths of the connections between nodes in an iterative process.
- The strengths of the connections determine the output of the network based on a given input.
- The power of neural networks lies in their ability to automatically learn complex patterns in large data sets.

Some tips to remember:

- NN are inspired by the human brain
- They are made up of interconnected nodes and connections with adjustable strengths
- They learn by being exposed to lots of data and iteratively adjusting connections
- Their power lies in automatically learning complex patterns

Examples of applications:

- Image recognition
- Speech recognition
- Machine translation
- Predictive modeling
- Robotics control

Advantages:

- Can learn complex nonlinear relationships
- Do not require hand-engineering of features
- Have achieved state-of-the-art results in many domains

Disadvantages:

- Require large amounts of data to learn
- Can be opaque and hard to interpret
- May overfit to training data
- Training can be slow
