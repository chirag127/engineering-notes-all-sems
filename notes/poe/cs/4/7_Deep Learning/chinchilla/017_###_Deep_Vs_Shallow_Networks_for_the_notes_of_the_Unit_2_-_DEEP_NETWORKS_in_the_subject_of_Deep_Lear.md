### Deep Vs Shallow Networks for the notes of the Unit 2 - DEEP NETWORKS in the subject of Deep Learning

Deep learning is a subfield of machine learning that involves training artificial neural networks with multiple layers to perform complex tasks. A crucial aspect of deep learning is the depth of the neural network. In this section, we will discuss the differences between deep and shallow neural networks.

#### Shallow Networks

A shallow neural network has only one or two hidden layers between the input and output layers. Shallow networks are relatively easy to train, require less computational resources, and are suitable for tasks that involve simple pattern recognition. However, they may not be capable of modeling complex relationships between the input and output.

#### Deep Networks

Deep neural networks have multiple hidden layers between the input and output layers. These networks can model complex relationships between the input and output and are capable of learning representations of the input data at different levels of abstraction. Deep networks are suitable for tasks that require high accuracy, such as image and speech recognition. However, training deep networks can be challenging due to the vanishing gradient problem, which occurs when the gradient of the error function becomes too small, making it difficult to update the weights of the network.

#### Advantages of Deep Networks

- Deep networks are capable of modeling complex relationships between input and output.

- Deep networks can learn representations of the input data at different levels of abstraction, making them suitable for a wide range of tasks.

- Deep networks can achieve high accuracy on challenging tasks such as image and speech recognition.

#### Disadvantages of Deep Networks

- Training deep networks can be computationally expensive and time-consuming.

- Deep networks are prone to overfitting, which occurs when the model learns to fit the training data too well and fails to generalize to new data.

- Deep networks can suffer from the vanishing gradient problem, which can make training difficult.

#### Mnemonics and Learning Tricks

- A deep network is like a multi-story building, with each layer representing a level of abstraction.

- Shallow networks are like a single-story building, suitable for simple tasks, while deep networks are like tall skyscrapers, suitable for complex tasks.

- Think of a deep network as a hierarchy of features, with each layer learning more abstract representations of the input data.

In summary, deep neural networks are capable of modeling complex relationships between input and output and are suitable for a wide range of tasks. However, training deep networks can be challenging, and they are prone to overfitting and the vanishing gradient problem. Shallow neural networks are relatively easy to train and suitable for simple tasks but may not be capable of modeling complex relationships.
