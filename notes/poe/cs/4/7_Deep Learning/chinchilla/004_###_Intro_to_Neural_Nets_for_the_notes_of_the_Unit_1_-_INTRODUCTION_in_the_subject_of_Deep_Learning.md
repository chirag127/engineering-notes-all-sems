### Intro to Neural Nets for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of Deep Learning

Neural networks are a type of machine learning algorithm inspired by the structure and function of the human brain. They are used in a wide range of applications, including image recognition, natural language processing, and game playing.

In this unit, we will introduce the basics of neural networks, including their structure, training methods, and applications.

#### Neural Network Structure

A neural network is a collection of interconnected nodes or "neurons" that process information. The neurons are organized into layers, with the input layer receiving data from the outside world and the output layer producing the final result.

#### Activation Functions

Activation functions are used to determine the output of each neuron in the network. Common activation functions include the sigmoid function, the ReLU function, and the softmax function.

#### Training Methods

Neural networks are trained using a process called backpropagation, which involves adjusting the weights between neurons to minimize the error between the network's output and the desired output. The training data is typically split into a training set and a validation set to prevent overfitting.

#### Applications

Neural networks are used in a wide range of applications, including:

- Image recognition: Neural networks can be trained to recognize objects in images, such as faces or license plates.
- Natural language processing: Neural networks can be used to understand and generate human language.
- Game playing: Neural networks can be trained to play games, such as chess or Go, at a high level.

#### Advantages

- Neural networks can learn complex patterns and relationships in data.
- They can be used for a wide range of applications, from image recognition to natural language processing.
- They can be trained using large amounts of data, which can lead to better performance.

#### Disadvantages

- Neural networks can be computationally expensive to train and run.
- They can be prone to overfitting, especially if the training data is not representative of the real-world data.
- They can be difficult to interpret, making it challenging to understand how they are making decisions.

Overall, neural networks are a powerful tool for machine learning and are used in many real-world applications. Understanding their structure, training methods, and applications is essential for anyone interested in deep learning.
