### Computational & Artificial Neuroscience for the notes of the Unit 4 - OPTIMIZATION AND GENERALIZATION in the subject of Deep Learning

In deep learning, optimization and generalization are two critical concepts that play an essential role in the performance of a neural network. Computational and artificial neuroscience is a field of study that focuses on understanding the behavior of biological neural networks and applying the knowledge to the design and optimization of artificial neural networks. In this unit, we will explore how the principles of computational and artificial neuroscience can be used to optimize and generalize deep learning models.

#### Optimization

Optimization is the process of finding the best set of weights and biases for a neural network to minimize the loss function. There are various optimization algorithms available, such as Gradient Descent, Stochastic Gradient Descent, Adam, and Adagrad. The choice of optimization algorithm depends on the problem's complexity, number of parameters, and available computational resources.

Mnemonics:
- "GSD" to remember Gradient Descent 
- "ASAP" to remember Adam, which stands for Adaptive Moment Estimation.

#### Generalization

Generalization is the ability of a neural network to perform well on unseen data. Overfitting is a common problem that occurs when a neural network is trained on a limited dataset and fails to generalize to new data. Regularization techniques such as L1 and L2 regularization, dropout, and early stopping can help prevent overfitting and improve the generalization of a neural network.

Learning tricks:
- L1 regularization is like "Lasso" that removes less important features, and L2 regularization is like "Ridge" that reduces the impact of features but doesn't remove them completely.
- Dropout is like randomly dropping out a few students from a class to prevent overlearning.

In conclusion, understanding the principles of computational and artificial neuroscience can help us design and optimize deep learning models to achieve better performance and generalization. It is essential to choose the appropriate optimization algorithm and use regularization techniques to prevent overfitting and improve the model's generalization ability.
