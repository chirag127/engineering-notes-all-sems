### History of Deep Learning for the notes of the Unit 2 - DEEP NETWORKS in the subject of Deep Learning

Deep learning is a subset of machine learning that uses artificial neural networks with multiple layers to model and solve complex problems. The concept of deep learning dates back to the 1940s, but it wasn't until the 2000s that the technology and computational power caught up with the theory. Here is a brief history of deep learning:

1. Perceptron: In 1957, Frank Rosenblatt introduced the perceptron, which was the first artificial neural network capable of learning from data. It was a single-layer neural network that could classify linearly separable data.

2. Backpropagation: In 1974, Paul Werbos first introduced the backpropagation algorithm, which is used to train multi-layer neural networks. It was not widely adopted until the 1980s, when faster computers and better algorithms became available.

3. Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs): In the 1980s, Yann LeCun introduced the first CNNs, which are used for image recognition, natural language processing, and other tasks. CNNs are now widely used in computer vision applications.

4. Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs): In the 1980s, RNNs were introduced, which are used for processing sequential data, such as speech and text. RNNs have become popular in natural language processing and speech recognition.

5. Deep Belief Networks (DBNs): In 2006, Geoffrey Hinton introduced DBNs, which are multi-layer neural networks that use unsupervised learning to extract hierarchical representations of data. DBNs have been used for a variety of tasks, including image recognition and speech recognition.

6. Deep Neural Networks (DNNs): In the 2010s, DNNs became popular with the development of deep learning frameworks, such as TensorFlow and PyTorch. DNNs are used for a variety of tasks, including image recognition, natural language processing, and speech recognition.

In conclusion, the history of deep learning spans multiple decades, and it has been a long and fascinating journey to get to where we are today. With the rapid advancements in technology and the availability of massive amounts of data, deep learning is poised to continue its growth and revolutionize various industries.
