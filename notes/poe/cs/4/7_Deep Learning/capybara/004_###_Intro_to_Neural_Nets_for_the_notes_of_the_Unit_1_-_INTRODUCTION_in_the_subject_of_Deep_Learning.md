### Intro to Neural Nets for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of Deep Learning

Neural networks are one of the most important and widely used techniques in the field of deep learning. Neural networks are modeled after the human brain and are capable of learning from data.

Here are some key concepts to keep in mind when studying neural networks:

1. Neurons: The basic building blocks of a neural network are neurons. Neurons receive input signals, process them, and then generate output signals.

2. Layers: A neural network is composed of several layers of neurons. The input layer receives the input signals, while the output layer generates the final output. The layers in between are called hidden layers.

3. Activation functions: Activation functions are used to introduce non-linearity into the output of a neuron. Common activation functions include ReLU, Sigmoid, and Tanh.

4. Backpropagation: Backpropagation is a technique used to train neural networks. During the training process, the neural network is fed with input data and the corresponding output. The difference between the predicted output and the actual output is used to adjust the weights of the neurons.

5. Overfitting: Overfitting is a common problem in neural networks. It occurs when the network is trained too much on the training data and starts to learn the noise present in the data. This can lead to poor performance on new data.

Mnemonics and learning tricks:

1. To remember the function of neurons, think of them as tiny decision-makers in the network.

2. To remember the purpose of activation functions, think of them as the "go" or "no go" signals that a neuron sends out.

3. To remember the concept of backpropagation, think of it as "backtracking" to adjust the weights of the neurons.

4. To avoid overfitting, think of the neural network as a student preparing for an exam. The student should study the material thoroughly but not memorize it to the point where they cannot apply it to new problems.

Overall, understanding the basics of neural networks is crucial for anyone interested in deep learning. By mastering these concepts and techniques, you will be able to design and train your own neural networks for a variety of applications.
