### History of Deep Learning for the notes of the Unit 2 - DEEP NETWORKS in the subject of Deep Learning

Deep Learning is a subset of Machine Learning, which is based on artificial neural networks. It is used to develop complex models for pattern recognition and prediction. In this section, we will discuss the history of Deep Learning.

- In the 1940s, Warren McCulloch and Walter Pitts created the first mathematical model of a neuron. This model was the foundation for the development of neural networks.
- In the 1950s, Frank Rosenblatt created the first neural network, called the Perceptron. It was capable of learning and classifying patterns.
- In the 1960s, neural networks became popular, and researchers started exploring their potential. However, they were limited by the lack of computational power and data.
- In the 1970s, the Backpropagation algorithm was invented, which allowed neural networks to learn multiple layers of features. This was a significant breakthrough, as it enabled the development of deep neural networks.
- In the 1980s, neural networks were used in speech recognition and computer vision. However, they fell out of favor due to the rise of Support Vector Machines (SVMs) and Decision Trees.
- In the 2000s, Deep Learning made a comeback, thanks to advances in computational power and data availability. Geoffrey Hinton, Yann LeCun, and Yoshua Bengio were some of the researchers who worked on deep neural networks and made significant contributions to the field.
- In 2012, Alex Krizhevsky and his team won the ImageNet challenge, a competition for computer vision algorithms, using a deep neural network called AlexNet. This was a turning point in the field of Deep Learning, as it demonstrated the superiority of deep neural networks over traditional machine learning algorithms.
- Since then, Deep Learning has been used in various applications, such as natural language processing, speech recognition, image and video recognition, and self-driving cars.
