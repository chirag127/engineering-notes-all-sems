### Deep Vs Shallow Networks

Deep learning networks are complex neural networks that are composed of multiple layers. The number of layers in a network is referred to as the depth of the network. In contrast, shallow networks have a small number of layers. In this section, we will compare deep and shallow networks and discuss their advantages and disadvantages.

#### Shallow Networks

Shallow networks are neural networks that have only a few layers. They are relatively easy to train and require less computational resources than deep networks. Shallow networks are best suited for simple tasks such as image classification, speech recognition, and text classification.

Advantages of Shallow Networks:
- Easy to train
- Require less computational resources
- Suitable for simple tasks

Disadvantages of Shallow Networks:
- Limited capacity to learn complex patterns
- Cannot learn deep representations

#### Deep Networks

Deep networks are neural networks that have multiple layers. They are capable of learning complex patterns and can represent high-level abstractions. Deep networks are best suited for tasks such as image segmentation, object detection, natural language processing, and speech synthesis.

Advantages of Deep Networks:
- High capacity to learn complex patterns
- Can represent high-level abstractions
- Suitable for complex tasks

Disadvantages of Deep Networks:
- Difficult to train
- Require a large amount of computational resources
- Prone to overfitting

#### Mnemonic

A good mnemonic to remember the difference between deep and shallow networks is the following:

- Deep networks are like an onion, they have many layers
- Shallow networks are like a pancake, they have only a few layers

This mnemonic can help you remember that deep networks have many layers, while shallow networks have only a few layers.

In conclusion, deep and shallow networks have their own advantages and disadvantages. Deep networks are best suited for complex tasks that require high-level abstractions, while shallow networks are suitable for simpler tasks that require less computational resources. It is important to choose the appropriate network architecture based on the task at hand.
