## Unit 2 - DEEP NETWORKS

In this unit, we will learn about deep neural networks, which are a type of artificial neural network that consists of multiple layers. 

### 1. Introduction to Deep Networks

- Deep networks are used for tasks such as computer vision, speech recognition, and natural language processing.
- They consist of multiple layers of neurons, which allow them to model complex relationships between inputs and outputs.
- They can be trained using a variety of methods, including backpropagation and stochastic gradient descent.

### 2. Types of Deep Networks

- Convolutional neural networks (CNNs): used for image recognition tasks.
- Recurrent neural networks (RNNs): used for sequence data such as speech and text.
- Autoencoders: used for unsupervised learning tasks such as dimensionality reduction.
- Generative adversarial networks (GANs): used for generating new data based on existing data.

### 3. Training Deep Networks

- Deep networks are trained using large datasets and can take a long time to train.
- Techniques such as transfer learning can be used to speed up the training process.
- Regularization techniques such as dropout can be used to prevent overfitting.

### 4. Applications of Deep Networks

- Image and speech recognition
- Natural language processing
- Self-driving cars
- Robotics
- Fraud detection 
- Healthcare 

### Mnemonics and Learning Tricks

- "Deep" stands for multiple layers, so think of an ocean with multiple layers of depth. 
- Remember the types of deep networks using the acronym C-R-A-G: CNNs, RNNs, Autoencoders, GANs. 
- Think of training deep networks as building a skyscraper - it takes time and effort to construct, but the end result is impressive.
