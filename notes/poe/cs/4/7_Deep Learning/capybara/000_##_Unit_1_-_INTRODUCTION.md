## Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION

This unit provides an introduction to the field of computer science and covers the following topics:

1. What is Computer Science?
    - Computer Science is the study of computers and computational systems. It involves the design and analysis of algorithms, programming languages, hardware, and software systems.

2. History of Computer Science
    - Computer Science has evolved over time, from the development of the first computers in the 1940s to the modern-day use of artificial intelligence and machine learning.

3. Importance of Computer Science
    - Computer Science plays a crucial role in our lives today, from mobile phones to self-driving cars. It has revolutionized the way we communicate, work, and live.

4. Problem Solving and Algorithms
    - One of the fundamental aspects of Computer Science is problem solving. It involves breaking down a problem into smaller, more manageable subproblems and designing algorithms to solve them.

5. Programming Languages
    - Programming languages are used to write software programs that run on computers. There are many programming languages available, each with its strengths and weaknesses.

6. Hardware and Software Systems
    - Computer systems consist of hardware and software. Hardware includes the physical components of a computer, such as the CPU, memory, and input/output devices. Software refers to the programs that run on a computer.

7. Ethics in Computer Science
    - Computer Science raises ethical issues related to privacy, security, and the impact of technology on society. It is important for computer scientists to be aware of these issues and to develop ethical solutions.
