### What a shallow network computes for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of Deep Learning

A shallow neural network is a neural network that has only one hidden layer. In this section, we will discuss what a shallow network computes and how it works.

#### Computing in a Shallow Network

A shallow network is used to solve simple problems where the input and output are directly related. The network takes the input, applies weights to it, and then applies an activation function to produce an output. The output is then compared to the expected output to compute the error, which is used to adjust the weights. This process is repeated until the error is minimized.

#### Learning in a Shallow Network

A shallow network learns by adjusting the weights of the connections between the input and the output. The weights are adjusted in such a way that the output of the network is as close to the expected output as possible. This is done by minimizing the error between the output and the expected output.

#### Advantages of Shallow Networks

- Shallow networks are simple and easy to train compared to deep networks.
- They require less computational resources.
- They are suitable for solving simple problems.

#### Disadvantages of Shallow Networks

- They are limited in their ability to solve complex problems.
- They are not very good at recognizing patterns in the data.

#### Mnemonics and Learning Tricks

- To remember the concept of computing in a shallow network, remember the acronym WAO: Weights, Activation function, Output.
- To remember the concept of learning in a shallow network, remember the acronym WAE: Weights, Adjust, Error.

In conclusion, a shallow network is a simple neural network that is used to solve simple problems. It computes the output by applying weights to the input and applying an activation function. It learns by adjusting the weights to minimize the error between the output and the expected output. While shallow networks have their advantages, they are not suitable for solving complex problems.
