
### Types of Grids for the notes of the Unit 1 - Overview of Grid Computing Technology in the subject of High Performance Computing
There are three main types of grids used in High Performance Computing:

1. **Public Grids**: These grids are open to anyone and are not owned by any single organization. They are usually used for research and development purposes and are maintained by a group of volunteers.

2. **Private Grids**: These grids are owned and maintained by a single organization. They are usually used for business purposes and are more secure than public grids.

3. **Hybrid Grids**: These grids are a combination of public and private grids. They allow organizations to take advantage of both public and private grids in order to maximize the efficiency of their computing resources.
