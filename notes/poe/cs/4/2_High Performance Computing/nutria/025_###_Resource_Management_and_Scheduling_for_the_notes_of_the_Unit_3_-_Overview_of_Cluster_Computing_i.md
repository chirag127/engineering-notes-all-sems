
### Resource Management and Scheduling for the notes of the Unit 3 - Overview of Cluster Computing in the subject of High Performance Computing

Resource Management and Scheduling can be a complex topic in High Performance Computing. It is important to understand the basics of resource management and scheduling in order to effectively use cluster computing. 

1. Resource Management: This involves managing the resources of the cluster by allocating and deallocating resources based on the needs of the applications. It also involves managing the capacity of the cluster and ensuring that the resources are used efficiently. 

2. Scheduling: This involves determining when and where the applications should run on the cluster. It also involves determining how much resources should be allocated to each application and how the applications should be distributed across the cluster. 

3. Advantages: Resource management and scheduling can help to improve the performance of the cluster by ensuring that resources are allocated efficiently. It can also help to reduce the cost of running applications on the cluster. 

4. Disadvantages: Resource management and scheduling can be complex and time consuming. It can also lead to resource contention if not done properly. 

5. Examples: A common example of resource management and scheduling is the use of a job scheduler. A job scheduler can be used to manage and schedule the execution of applications on the cluster. 

6. Applications: Resource management and scheduling can be used in many different applications, such as scientific computing, data analysis, and web services. 

7. Mnemonics and Learning Tricks: To remember the basics of resource management and scheduling, try using the acronym RMS: Resource Management and Scheduling.
