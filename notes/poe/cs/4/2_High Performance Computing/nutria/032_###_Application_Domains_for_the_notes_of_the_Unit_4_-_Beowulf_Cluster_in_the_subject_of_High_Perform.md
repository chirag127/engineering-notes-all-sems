
### Application Domains for the notes of the Unit 4 - Beowulf Cluster in the subject of High Performance Computing

* High Performance Computing (HPC) is a field of computing that focuses on the design, development, and deployment of supercomputers and other high-performance computing systems.
* HPC systems are used for a variety of tasks, including scientific research, data analysis, machine learning, and artificial intelligence.
* Beowulf clusters are a type of HPC system that is composed of multiple computers connected together to form a single, powerful computing resource.
* Beowulf clusters are used in a variety of application domains, including scientific computing, engineering, and business analytics.
* In scientific computing, Beowulf clusters are used to solve complex problems in fields such as physics, chemistry, and biology.
* In engineering, Beowulf clusters are used to simulate complex physical systems, such as aircraft and automotive systems.
* In business analytics, Beowulf clusters are used to analyze large datasets and uncover patterns and trends.
* Beowulf clusters provide an efficient way to process large amounts of data in a short amount of time.
* Mnemonic: HPC = High Performance Computing; Beowulf = Multiple Computers Connected Together.
