### Infrastructure for the notes of the Unit 5 - Overview of Cloud Computing in the subject of High Performance Computing

1. Hardware: Powerful servers with lots of processors and memory
- Data centers with thousands of servers
- Network connectivity with high bandwidth
- Storage arrays with capacity and performance
- Redundancy and backup for high availability

2. Software:
- Virtualization technology to create and manage virtual machines
- Automated provisioning of virtual resources
- APIs to control and manage the infrastructure
- Operating systems, middleware and applications for users
- Layers of software to pool resources and enable self-service

3. Services:
- Compute as a service (VMs on demand)
- Storage as a service (scalable, redundant storage)
- Database as a service
- Network as a service (virtual networks, security)
- Serverless functions as a service
- Analytics, AI and ML as a service
- API access to all services

Advantages:
- Scalability and elasticity: resources can be scaled up or down as needed
- Self-service and fully automated
- High availability and redundancy
- Multi-tenancy and share resources
- Reduce cost through utility model
- Maintenance and management by the cloud provider

Disadvantages:
- Vendor lock-in: difficulty moving applications between cloud providers
- Reliance on internet connectivity
- Technical complexity is hidden
- Cost control and optimization is a challenge
- Performance variability and unpredictability

Applications:
- Websites and web applications
- Mobile backend
- Business applications
- Scientific and technical computing
- Storage of data and backups
- Analytics and artificial intelligence
