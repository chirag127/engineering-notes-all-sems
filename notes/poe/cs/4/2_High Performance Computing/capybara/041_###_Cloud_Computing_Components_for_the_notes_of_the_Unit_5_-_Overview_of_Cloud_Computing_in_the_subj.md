### Cloud Computing Components for the notes of the Unit 5 - Overview of Cloud Computing in the subject of High Performance Computing

Cloud computing is a rapidly growing field in the tech industry. It refers to the delivery of computing services, including servers, storage, databases, networking, software, and analytics, over the internet.

There are several components that make up cloud computing, including:

1. **Infrastructure as a Service (IaaS)** - This component provides the fundamental building blocks for cloud IT, including servers, storage, and networking. IaaS allows businesses to rent infrastructure from a cloud provider on a pay-as-you-go basis, rather than investing in their own data centers.

2. **Platform as a Service (PaaS)** - PaaS provides a platform for developers to build, test, and deploy applications. PaaS allows developers to focus on writing code without having to worry about the underlying infrastructure.

3. **Software as a Service (SaaS)** - SaaS provides applications that are accessed over the internet. These applications are usually provided by a third-party vendor and are delivered over the internet. Examples of SaaS applications include Salesforce and Microsoft Office 365.

4. **Public cloud** - Public clouds are owned and operated by third-party cloud providers. These providers offer their services over the internet and are accessible to anyone who wants to use them.

5. **Private cloud** - Private clouds are owned and operated by a single organization. These clouds are usually used by large organizations that have sensitive data or regulatory requirements that require them to have control over their own infrastructure.

6. **Hybrid cloud** - Hybrid clouds combine public and private clouds. This allows organizations to take advantage of the scalability and cost-effectiveness of public clouds while still maintaining control over their own infrastructure.

Mnemonics and learning tricks:

- For remembering the three main types of cloud services (IaaS, PaaS, SaaS), you can use the phrase "Infrastructure, Platform, Software - I, P, S."
- To remember the difference between public and private clouds, think of public clouds as being like public transportation (accessible to anyone) and private clouds as being like a personal car (only accessible to the owner).

In summary, understanding the components of cloud computing is essential for anyone looking to work in the field. Whether you're a developer, IT professional, or business owner, knowing the different types of cloud services and deployment models can help you make informed decisions about which cloud solutions are right for your organization.
