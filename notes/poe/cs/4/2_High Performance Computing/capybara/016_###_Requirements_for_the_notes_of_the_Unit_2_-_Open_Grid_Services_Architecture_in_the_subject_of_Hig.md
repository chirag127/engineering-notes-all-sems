### Requirements for the notes of the Unit 2 - Open Grid Services Architecture in the subject of High Performance Computing

The Unit 2 of the subject High Performance Computing deals with Open Grid Services Architecture (OGSA) which is an extension of the Grid Computing model. OGSA provides a standard architecture for grid services and their interactions. In order to understand this topic, you need to have a good understanding of the following requirements:

1. Understanding of Grid Computing: You need to have a clear understanding of Grid Computing and its architecture. OGSA is an extension of Grid Computing, and thus, having a solid foundation in Grid Computing is essential.

2. Knowledge of Web Services: OGSA is based on Web Services technology, and therefore, you need to have a good understanding of Web Services and their protocols. This includes knowledge of SOAP, WSDL, and UDDI.

3. Understanding of Service-Oriented Architecture (SOA): OGSA is a Service-Oriented Architecture (SOA), and thus, having a good understanding of SOA is crucial. You need to know the concepts of services, service interfaces, and service implementations.

4. Knowledge of Middleware: OGSA requires a middleware layer to provide the necessary infrastructure for communication between grid services. Therefore, you need to have a good understanding of middleware technologies such as JMS and CORBA.

5. Familiarity with OGSA Components: OGSA consists of various components, such as Resource Management, Data Management, and Security Management. You need to have a good understanding of these components and their interactions.

6. Familiarity with OGSA Implementations: There are various OGSA implementations available, such as Globus Toolkit and OGSA-DAI. You need to have a good understanding of these implementations and their capabilities.

Mnemonic/Trick: Remember the acronym SWAT-K: S for Service-oriented architecture, W for Web Services, A for OGSA components, T for Technology, K for Knowledge of OGSA implementations. 

By fulfilling these requirements, you can gain a good understanding of OGSA and its applications in High Performance Computing.
