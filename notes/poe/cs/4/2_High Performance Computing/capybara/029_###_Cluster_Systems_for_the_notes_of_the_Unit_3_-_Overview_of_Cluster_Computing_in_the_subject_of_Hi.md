### Cluster Systems for the notes of the Unit 3 - Overview of Cluster Computing in the subject of High Performance Computing

Cluster computing is a type of computing where a group of computers is connected together to work as a single system. In this system, the computers are programmed to work together to solve a problem or perform a task. Cluster systems are widely used in scientific research, financial modeling, weather forecasting, and many other applications that require a large amount of computing power. Here are some important cluster systems to keep in mind for the notes of the Unit 3 - Overview of Cluster Computing in the subject of High Performance Computing:

1. Beowulf Cluster
- A Beowulf cluster is a type of high-performance computing cluster developed by NASA researchers in the 1990s.
- It consists of a group of Linux computers connected by a high-speed network.
- The Beowulf cluster is used for scientific research, data analysis, and simulations.
- Mnemonic: The Beowulf cluster is named after the epic poem Beowulf, which tells the story of a hero who fights against a monster. Similarly, the Beowulf cluster is used to fight against complex problems.

2. Hadoop Cluster
- Hadoop is an open-source software framework used for distributed storage and processing of Big Data.
- It is designed to scale up from single servers to thousands of machines, each offering local computation and storage.
- Hadoop cluster is used for large-scale data analysis and processing.
- Mnemonic: Hadoop can be thought of as a big elephant that can store and process large amounts of data.

3. Kubernetes Cluster
- Kubernetes is an open-source container orchestration platform used for automating the deployment, scaling, and management of containerized applications.
- It allows users to deploy, scale, and manage applications in containers across a cluster of computers.
- Kubernetes cluster is used for cloud-native computing and microservices architecture.
- Mnemonic: Kubernetes can be thought of as a captain of a ship who manages the crew and ensures smooth sailing.

4. MPI Cluster
- MPI (Message Passing Interface) is a standard for parallel computing developed by a group of researchers in the 1990s.
- It allows multiple processors to communicate with each other and work together on a single task.
- MPI cluster is used for scientific computing, numerical simulations, and other applications that require parallel processing.
- Mnemonic: MPI can be thought of as a group of musicians who play together in harmony to create a beautiful piece of music.

In conclusion, Cluster systems are a powerful tool for high-performance computing that can be used to solve complex problems in various fields. By understanding the different types of cluster systems and their applications, you can choose the right system for your specific needs.
