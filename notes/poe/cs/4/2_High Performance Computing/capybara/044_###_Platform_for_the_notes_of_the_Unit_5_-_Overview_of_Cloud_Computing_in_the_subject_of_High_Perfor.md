### Platform for the notes of the Unit 5 - Overview of Cloud Computing in the subject of High Performance Computing

Cloud computing is an emerging technology that has revolutionized the way we access and distribute computing resources. Understanding cloud computing is essential for anyone who wants to work with high-performance computing. In this unit, we will cover the basics of cloud computing and its various platforms. Here are some important platforms for the notes of Unit 5:

1. Amazon Web Services (AWS): AWS is a cloud computing platform that offers a wide range of services, including computing power, storage, and databases, among others. It is one of the most widely used cloud computing platforms in the world. Mnemonic: "A - Always, W - Work, S - Smarter with AWS"

2. Microsoft Azure: Azure is another popular cloud computing platform that offers a variety of services, including virtual machines, storage, and databases, among others. It is widely used by businesses and organizations of all sizes. Mnemonic: "Azure - Always Zero Unplanned REstarts"

3. Google Cloud Platform (GCP): GCP is a cloud computing platform that offers a wide range of services, including computing power, storage, and databases, among others. It is known for its reliability and scalability. Mnemonic: "Google Cloud Platform - Good for Computing Power"

4. IBM Cloud: IBM Cloud is a cloud computing platform that offers a variety of services, including computing power, storage, and databases, among others. It is widely used by businesses and organizations of all sizes. Mnemonic: "IBM Cloud - Intelligent Business Machines"

5. Oracle Cloud Infrastructure (OCI): OCI is a cloud computing platform that offers a wide range of services, including computing power, storage, and databases, among others. It is known for its high-performance computing capabilities. Mnemonic: "OCI - Optimized for Computing Intensity"

Advantages of cloud computing platforms:

- Scalability: Cloud computing platforms offer scalability, which means that you can easily increase or decrease the amount of computing resources you need.

- Cost-effective: Cloud computing platforms are cost-effective since you only pay for the resources you use.

- Reliability: Cloud computing platforms are highly reliable since they have redundant systems in place to ensure that your data is always available.

Disadvantages of cloud computing platforms:

- Security: Cloud computing platforms can be vulnerable to security threats since your data is stored on a remote server.

- Dependency on the Internet: Cloud computing platforms require an Internet connection, which means that if your connection goes down, you will not be able to access your data.

Examples of cloud computing platforms in action:

- Netflix: Netflix uses AWS to store and stream its vast collection of movies and TV shows.

- Dropbox: Dropbox uses AWS to store its users' files and data.

- Spotify: Spotify uses GCP to store and stream its millions of songs to its users.

Applications of cloud computing platforms:

- Data storage and processing

- Web and mobile app development

- High-performance computing

In conclusion, understanding cloud computing platforms is essential for anyone who wants to work with high-performance computing. AWS, Azure, GCP, IBM Cloud, and OCI are some of the most popular cloud computing platforms, each with its own strengths and weaknesses. By understanding the advantages, disadvantages, examples, and applications of these platforms, you will be better equipped to work with them in the future.
