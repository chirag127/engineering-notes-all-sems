## Unit 4 - Network & Communication aspects in IoT

In this unit, we will learn about the network and communication aspects of IoT. We will explore the different types of networks that can be used for IoT devices and the various communication protocols that can be used to enable communication between these devices.

### Types of Networks for IoT Devices

There are several types of networks that can be used for IoT devices, including:

- **Local Area Networks (LANs):** LANs are used for devices that are located in close proximity to each other, such as in a home or office. These networks can be wired or wireless and can be used to connect devices such as laptops, smartphones, and IoT devices.

- **Wide Area Networks (WANs):** WANs are used for devices that are located in different geographic locations, such as across a city or even across the world. These networks typically use the internet as the backbone and can be used to connect devices such as servers, routers, and IoT gateways.

- **Personal Area Networks (PANs):** PANs are used for devices that are located within a very short range, such as within a person's body or immediate surroundings. Examples of PANs include Bluetooth and ZigBee networks.

- **Sensor Networks (SNs):** SNs are used for devices that are designed to collect data from the environment, such as temperature, humidity, and light level sensors. These networks can be wired or wireless and are typically used in industrial and environmental monitoring applications.

### Communication Protocols for IoT Devices

There are several communication protocols that can be used to enable communication between IoT devices, including:

- **Bluetooth:** Bluetooth is a wireless protocol that is commonly used for short-range communication between devices. It is often used for IoT devices that are located within the same room or area.

- **ZigBee:** ZigBee is a wireless protocol that is designed for low-power, low-data-rate applications. It is often used for IoT devices that are located in a PAN.

- **Wi-Fi:** Wi-Fi is a wireless protocol that is commonly used for LANs. It provides high-speed data transfer and is often used for IoT devices that require a high bandwidth connection.

- **Cellular:** Cellular communication is used for WANs and is typically used for IoT devices that are located in remote areas or are mobile. It provides a high-speed data transfer and is often used for applications such as vehicle tracking and remote monitoring.

### Mnemonics and Learning Tips

When it comes to learning about the network and communication aspects of IoT, it can be helpful to remember the following mnemonics and tips:

- **LANs, WANs, and PANs:** Think of these networks as being similar to the size of a room, a city, and a person's immediate surroundings, respectively.

- **Bluetooth and ZigBee:** Think of these protocols as being similar to the sound of buzzing bees or the blue light of a Bluetooth speaker.

- **Wi-Fi and Cellular:** Think of these protocols as being similar to the internet and cell phone networks that we use every day.

By keeping these mnemonics and tips in mind, you can better remember the different types of networks and communication protocols used in IoT.
