### Wireless Sensor Networks for the Notes of Unit 2 - Hardware for IoT in the Subject of Internet of Things

Wireless Sensor Networks (WSN) are a key component of the Internet of Things (IoT) as they provide the ability to collect data from a variety of sensors and transmit it wirelessly to a central location for analysis. Here are some important concepts to keep in mind when studying WSN:

1. Definition: Wireless Sensor Networks are a collection of autonomous sensors that communicate wirelessly with each other to monitor and collect data about the physical or environmental conditions of an area.

2. Components: A WSN typically consists of three main components: sensors, wireless communication devices, and a central monitoring system. The sensors are responsible for collecting data, while the wireless communication devices transmit the data to the central monitoring system for analysis.

3. Types of Sensors: There are various types of sensors used in WSN, including temperature sensors, humidity sensors, light sensors, pressure sensors, and motion sensors.

4. Wireless Communication Protocols: WSN use various wireless communication protocols to transmit data, including Bluetooth, Zigbee, Wi-Fi, and LoRaWAN.

5. Advantages of WSN: WSN have several advantages, including low power consumption, low cost, and the ability to collect data from remote or hard-to-reach locations.

6. Applications of WSN: WSN have a wide range of applications, including environmental monitoring, industrial automation, smart homes, and healthcare.

7. Mnemonic: A useful mnemonic to remember the components of WSN is "SCW" which stands for Sensors, Communication, and Wireless.

Overall, Wireless Sensor Networks are an essential aspect of IoT hardware, and understanding their components and applications is crucial for anyone studying IoT.
