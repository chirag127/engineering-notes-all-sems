### Survey Routing Protocols for the Notes of the Unit 4 - Network & Communication Aspects in IoT in the Subject of Internet of Things

Routing protocols play a vital role in the communication process in the Internet of Things (IoT). They are responsible for transmitting data packets from one device to another in a network. This is why it is important to have an understanding of the different routing protocols used in IoT.

In this section, we will discuss some of the commonly used routing protocols in IoT:

1. **RPL (IPv6 Routing Protocol for Low-Power and Lossy Networks)**

RPL is one of the most commonly used routing protocols for IoT devices. It is designed to operate in low-power and lossy networks (LLNs), which are common in IoT applications. RPL is an IPv6-based protocol that uses a proactive approach to build and maintain routes in a network.

Mnemonic: Remember RPL as 'Routing for Power and Loss'.

2. **6LoWPAN Routing Protocol**

6LoWPAN is another IPv6-based routing protocol that is commonly used in IoT devices. It is designed to operate in low-power and low-bandwidth networks. 6LoWPAN uses a mesh topology, which allows devices to communicate with each other directly or through intermediate devices.

Mnemonic: Remember 6LoWPAN as '6 Low-bandwidth and Power Area Network'.

3. **CoAP (Constrained Application Protocol) Routing Protocol**

CoAP is a lightweight application-layer protocol that is used for communication between IoT devices. It is designed to operate in resource-constrained environments, making it an ideal protocol for IoT devices. CoAP uses a client-server model and supports caching, observe, and discovery mechanisms.

Mnemonic: Remember CoAP as 'Communication in a Constrained Area Protocol'.

4. **MQTT (Message Queue Telemetry Transport) Routing Protocol**

MQTT is a publish-subscribe-based messaging protocol that is commonly used in IoT devices. It is designed to operate in low-bandwidth and unreliable networks. MQTT uses a broker to manage the communication between devices, making it an ideal protocol for IoT applications.

Mnemonic: Remember MQTT as 'Message Queue Telemetry Transport'.

Advantages and Disadvantages of RPL, 6LoWPAN, CoAP, and MQTT:

| Protocol | Advantages | Disadvantages |
| --- | --- | --- |
| RPL | Efficient in low-power and lossy networks, supports multiple routing metrics | Overhead involved in maintaining routing tables |
| 6LoWPAN | Supports mesh topology, low-bandwidth requirements | Limited scalability |
| CoAP | Lightweight, resource-efficient, supports caching and discovery mechanisms | Limited security features |
| MQTT | Publish-subscribe model, low-bandwidth requirements | Limited reliability in unreliable networks |

In conclusion, it is important to have a good understanding of the different routing protocols used in IoT. RPL, 6LoWPAN, CoAP, and MQTT are some of the commonly used routing protocols. Each protocol has its own advantages and disadvantages, and the choice of the protocol depends on the specific requirements of the IoT application.
