### Radio Frequency Identification (RFID) Technology for the Notes of the Unit 2 - Hardware for IoT in the Subject of Internet of Things

Radio Frequency Identification (RFID) is a wireless communication technology that is used for identifying and tracking objects or people. It consists of a reader and a tag, which communicate with each other using radio waves. The reader emits a radio signal, which is received by the tag. The tag then responds with its identification information, which is read by the reader.

#### Components of RFID System:

- Reader: It is a device that emits radio signals and receives information from the tag. It can be fixed or portable.

- Tag: It is a device that contains information about the object it is attached to. It consists of a microchip and an antenna.

- Antenna: It is a device that is used to transmit and receive radio signals.

#### Types of RFID Tags:

- Passive RFID Tags: They do not have a power source and rely on the reader's signal to power them. They are inexpensive and can be used for inventory tracking, asset management, and access control.

- Active RFID Tags: They have a power source and can send signals to the reader. They are more expensive than passive tags but can be used for real-time tracking of high-value assets.

- Semi-Passive RFID Tags: They have a battery to power the microchip but rely on the reader's signal to transmit data.

#### Advantages of RFID Technology:

- It can be used for real-time tracking of objects or people.

- It can be used in environments where barcodes or other identification technologies may not be suitable, such as dirty or wet environments.

- It can improve inventory accuracy, reduce theft, and improve supply chain management.

#### Disadvantages of RFID Technology:

- It can be expensive to implement.

- It can be difficult to read tags if they are not within range of the reader.

- It can raise privacy concerns if used for tracking people.

#### Applications of RFID Technology:

- Inventory tracking

- Asset management

- Access control

- Supply chain management

- Healthcare

- Smart agriculture

#### Examples of RFID Technology:

- RFID tags are used in retail stores to track inventory and prevent theft.

- RFID tags are used in hospitals to track medical equipment and supplies.

- RFID tags are used in livestock management to track animals and monitor their health.

#### Learning Trick:

A simple mnemonic to remember the components of an RFID system is RAT (Reader, Antenna, Tag).
