### Beagle Bone for the notes of the Unit 3 - Embedded Platforms for IoT in the subject of Internet of Things

Beagle Bone is a single-board computer developed by BeagleBoard.org. It is designed to provide an open-source development platform for embedded applications. Beagle Bone is widely used in the field of Internet of Things (IoT) due to its powerful hardware and software capabilities.

Here are some key points to remember about Beagle Bone:

1. Hardware: Beagle Bone is powered by a Sitara ARM Cortex-A8 processor clocked at 1GHz. It has 512MB of DDR3 RAM and 4GB of eMMC flash storage. In addition, it has a microSD card slot, USB host and device ports, Ethernet port, HDMI output, and 65 GPIO pins.

2. Operating System: Beagle Bone supports various operating systems including Debian, Ubuntu, Android, and Windows 10 IoT Core. The operating system can be loaded onto the eMMC flash or microSD card.

3. Programming: Beagle Bone supports various programming languages such as Python, C++, and Java. It also provides software libraries for hardware interfacing, such as the Adafruit BBIO library.

4. Mnemonic: A useful mnemonic to remember the GPIO pin layout of Beagle Bone is "P9 is mine and P8 is great". This means that the P9 header has 46 GPIO pins while the P8 header has only 33 GPIO pins.

5. Advantages: Beagle Bone is a powerful and versatile single-board computer that can be used for a wide range of embedded applications. It has a large number of GPIO pins and supports various operating systems and programming languages.

6. Disadvantages: Beagle Bone is relatively expensive compared to other single-board computers such as Raspberry Pi. It also has a steeper learning curve due to its more complex hardware and software.

7. Applications: Beagle Bone is widely used in IoT projects such as home automation, robotics, and industrial automation. It can also be used as a development platform for creating custom embedded systems.

In conclusion, Beagle Bone is a powerful and versatile single-board computer that is widely used in the field of IoT. It has a large number of GPIO pins, supports various operating systems and programming languages, and can be used for a wide range of embedded applications. Remembering the mnemonic "P9 is mine and P8 is great" can help in quickly identifying the GPIO pins on the board.
