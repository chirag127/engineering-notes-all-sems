### Overview of IOT Supported Hardware Platforms Such as Arduino for the Notes of the Unit 3 - Embedded Platforms for IoT in the Subject of Internet of Things

Internet of Things (IoT) is a rapidly growing field that involves the integration of physical devices, sensors, and software to collect and exchange data. Embedded platforms for IoT are an essential part of this field, and one such hardware platform that is widely used is Arduino.

Arduino is an open-source electronics platform based on easy-to-use hardware and software. It consists of a microcontroller board, a programming environment, and a community of users and developers. Arduino boards are programmable, and they can be used to control a wide range of electronic devices.

Here are some important points to keep in mind about IOT supported hardware platforms such as Arduino:

1. Arduino boards are easy to use and can be programmed using a simple language called Arduino programming language.

2. Arduino boards are versatile and can be used to control a wide range of electronic devices, including sensors, motors, and lights.

3. Arduino boards are affordable and widely available, making them an ideal platform for hobbyists, students, and professionals.

4. Arduino boards are compatible with a wide range of sensors and other electronic components, making it easy to build custom IoT solutions.

5. Arduino boards are highly customizable, and users can modify the hardware and software to meet their specific needs.

6. Arduino boards have a large community of users and developers who contribute to the platform, creating a wealth of resources and knowledge that users can tap into.

Mnemonics and learning tricks:

One simple mnemonic to remember the key features of Arduino is the acronym "EASY-C":

- Easy to use
- Affordable
- Versatile
- Compatible
- Customizable

Overall, IOT supported hardware platforms such as Arduino are an essential tool for developing IoT solutions. They are easy to use, versatile, and highly customizable, making them an ideal platform for hobbyists, students, and professionals. With a large community of users and developers contributing to the platform, there is no shortage of resources and knowledge available to help users get started with Arduino and build their own IoT solutions.
