### Sensor Deployment and Node Discovery for the Notes of Unit 4 - Network & Communication Aspects in IoT

In the Internet of Things (IoT), sensor deployment and node discovery play a vital role in ensuring the efficient communication of devices. This process involves the placement of sensors in a network and the discovery of nodes to establish communication between devices. Here are some of the important points to consider:

1. Sensor Deployment:

- The deployment of sensors requires a systematic approach to ensure proper functioning and coverage of the network.
- It involves selecting appropriate sensor nodes, configuring them, and placing them in designated locations to detect and transmit data.
- The sensors should be placed strategically to maximize coverage and minimize interference.
- Some mnemonics that can help remember the steps involved in sensor deployment are: 
    - "SCPD" (Select, Configure, Place, Detect) 
    - "PACT" (Place, Assign, Configure, Test)

2. Node Discovery:

- Node discovery involves the identification and connection of nodes in a network.
- It is essential to establish communication between devices and ensure the smooth flow of data.
- The discovery process can be done manually or automatically using protocols such as Simple Service Discovery Protocol (SSDP) and Constrained Application Protocol (CoAP).
- Some tricks to remember the node discovery process are: 
    - "Find, Connect, Communicate" 
    - "ADN" (Advertise, Discover, Negotiate)

In conclusion, sensor deployment and node discovery are crucial aspects of IoT networks. A proper understanding and implementation of these processes can ensure efficient communication and functionality of devices.
