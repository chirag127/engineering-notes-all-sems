## Unit 3 - Embedded Platforms for IoT

### Introduction

In this unit, we will learn about embedded platforms for IoT. An embedded platform is a combination of hardware and software that is designed to perform a specific function. In IoT, embedded platforms are used to connect devices to the internet and enable them to exchange data.

### Types of Embedded Platforms

There are various types of embedded platforms used in IoT. Some of them are:

1. Microcontroller Units (MCUs): MCUs are small computers that are designed to perform specific tasks. They are commonly used in IoT devices because of their low power consumption and cost-effectiveness.

2. System on a Chip (SoC): SoC is an integrated circuit that includes multiple components such as a processor, memory, and input/output interfaces. They are used in IoT devices because of their compact size and low power consumption.

3. Single-Board Computers (SBCs): SBCs are complete computers built on a single circuit board. They are used in IoT devices because of their flexibility and ease of use.

### Mnemonics and Learning Tricks

Remember the acronym "MSS" to recall the three types of embedded platforms: MCUs, SoCs, and SBCs.

### Advantages of Embedded Platforms

1. Low power consumption: Embedded platforms consume less power than traditional computers, making them ideal for IoT devices that run on batteries.

2. Cost-effective: Embedded platforms are less expensive than traditional computers, making them ideal for mass production of IoT devices.

3. Compact size: Embedded platforms are small in size, making them suitable for IoT devices that require portability.

### Disadvantages of Embedded Platforms

1. Limited processing power: Embedded platforms have limited processing power compared to traditional computers, making them unsuitable for complex applications.

2. Limited memory: Embedded platforms have limited memory, making them unsuitable for applications that require large amounts of data storage.

### Examples of Embedded Platforms

1. Arduino: Arduino is an open-source hardware and software platform that is used to create IoT devices.

2. Raspberry Pi: Raspberry Pi is a single-board computer that is used to create IoT devices.

### Applications of Embedded Platforms

1. Home automation: Embedded platforms are used in smart homes to control appliances and lighting.

2. Industrial automation: Embedded platforms are used in industrial settings to monitor and control machinery.

3. Healthcare: Embedded platforms are used in healthcare to monitor patients and collect data for analysis.

### Conclusion

In conclusion, embedded platforms are an essential component of IoT devices. They enable devices to connect to the internet and exchange data. Remember the types of embedded platforms - MCUs, SoCs, and SBCs - and their advantages and disadvantages. Also, remember the examples and applications of embedded platforms.
