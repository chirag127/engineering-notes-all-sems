### Raspberry Pi for the Notes of Unit 3 - Embedded Platforms for IoT in the Subject of Internet of Things

The Raspberry Pi is a popular series of small single-board computers that are commonly used in Internet of Things (IoT) projects. Here are some important points to keep in mind when studying the Raspberry Pi for Unit 3 - Embedded Platforms for IoT in the subject of Internet of Things:

1. Definition: The Raspberry Pi is a small single-board computer developed in the United Kingdom by the Raspberry Pi Foundation. It is designed to promote the teaching of basic computer science in schools and developing countries.

2. Features: The Raspberry Pi has a number of features that make it an ideal platform for IoT projects. Some of these features include a low price point, a wide range of input/output (I/O) options, and the ability to run a variety of operating systems.

3. Uses: The Raspberry Pi can be used in a wide range of IoT applications, including home automation, robotics, and environmental monitoring. Its versatility and low cost make it an ideal platform for hobbyists and professionals alike.

4. Setup: Setting up a Raspberry Pi can be a little daunting for beginners, but there are a number of resources available to help. The Raspberry Pi Foundation provides detailed tutorials and guides on their website, and there are also a number of online communities and forums where users can ask for help and advice.

5. Mnemonic Trick: Remember the acronym "GPIO" (General Purpose Input/Output) which refers to the pins on the Raspberry Pi that can be used to connect to other devices. A helpful mnemonic for remembering the GPIO pins is "BGE - Big Green Earth" which stands for "Broadcom, GPIO, Ethernet".

In summary, the Raspberry Pi is a versatile and affordable platform for IoT projects. By understanding its features, uses, and setup process, you can successfully incorporate it into your IoT projects. Remembering the GPIO pins can be made easier with the mnemonic trick "BGE - Big Green Earth".
