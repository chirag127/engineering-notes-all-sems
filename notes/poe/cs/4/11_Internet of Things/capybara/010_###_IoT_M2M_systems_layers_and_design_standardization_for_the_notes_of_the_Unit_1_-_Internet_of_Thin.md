### IoT/M2M Systems Layers and Design Standardization

The Internet of Things (IoT) is a network of interconnected devices that have the ability to communicate with each other and exchange data without human intervention. The design and standardization of IoT/M2M systems layers are crucial for ensuring that these devices can communicate effectively and efficiently with each other. In this section, we will discuss the different layers of IoT/M2M systems and the design standardization that is needed for effective communication.

#### IoT/M2M Systems Layers

The IoT/M2M systems layers are divided into four layers: 

1. Application Layer: This layer deals with the interface between the IoT devices and the user. It includes the applications that are used for controlling, monitoring, and analyzing the data that is collected by the IoT devices.

2. Service Layer: This layer provides the services that are required for the IoT devices to communicate with each other. It includes services such as data storage, data analysis, and device management.

3. Network Layer: This layer deals with the communication between the IoT devices and the network. It includes the protocols and technologies that are used for transmitting data between the devices.

4. Device Layer: This layer includes the IoT devices themselves. It deals with the hardware and software components that are required for the devices to function properly.

#### Design Standardization

Design standardization is crucial for ensuring that the different IoT/M2M systems can communicate effectively with each other. It involves the use of common protocols and technologies across different devices and networks. 

The following are some of the design standardization that is needed for IoT/M2M systems:

1. Communication Protocols: The use of common communication protocols such as MQTT, CoAP, and RESTful APIs ensures that the devices can communicate with each other effectively.

2. Data Formats: The use of common data formats such as JSON, XML, and CSV ensures that the data can be easily exchanged between different devices and networks.

3. Security: The implementation of common security measures such as encryption, authentication, and authorization ensures that the data is secure and protected from unauthorized access.

4. Interoperability: The use of common standards and protocols ensures that the different devices and networks can work together seamlessly.

Mnemonics and Learning Tricks:

- Remember the acronym "ASND" for the different layers of IoT/M2M systems - Application, Service, Network, and Device.
- Remember the acronym "CDSI" for the different design standardization that is needed for IoT/M2M systems - Communication Protocols, Data Formats, Security, and Interoperability.
