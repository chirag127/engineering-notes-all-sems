
### Digital Sensors for the Notes of the Unit 2 - Hardware for IoT in the Subject of Internet of Things

* Digital sensors are devices that measure physical properties such as temperature, pressure, humidity, acceleration, and other environmental conditions. They are used in a variety of applications in the Internet of Things (IoT), including home automation, smart cities, industrial automation, and healthcare.

* Digital sensors have several advantages, including:
    * High accuracy: Digital sensors are able to measure physical properties more accurately than analog sensors.
    * Low power consumption: Digital sensors require less power than analog sensors, making them more energy-efficient.
    * Easy integration: Digital sensors can be easily integrated into existing systems and networks.

* Digital sensors are available in a variety of form factors, including:
    * Wearable sensors: Wearable sensors are small, lightweight sensors that can be worn on the body. They are used in a variety of applications, such as fitness tracking and medical monitoring.
    * Environmental sensors: Environmental sensors measure environmental conditions such as temperature, humidity, and air quality. They are used in smart home systems, industrial automation, and other applications.
    * Motion sensors: Motion sensors detect movement and are used in security systems, home automation, and robotics.

* Digital sensors are often used in combination with other components, such as microcontrollers, actuators, and communication modules, to create complex systems.
