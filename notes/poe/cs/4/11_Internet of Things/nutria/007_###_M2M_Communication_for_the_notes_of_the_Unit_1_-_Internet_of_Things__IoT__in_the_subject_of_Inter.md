
### M2M Communication for the notes of the Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT) in the subject of Internet of Things

M2M (Machine-to-Machine) communication is an essential component of the Internet of Things (IoT). It refers to the direct communication between two or more devices, without any human involvement. M2M communication is used to enable data exchange and automation of processes between connected devices.

M2M communication is based on a set of protocols, standards, and technologies. These protocols enable devices to connect and exchange data with each other. The protocols used for M2M communication include:

- Bluetooth: Bluetooth is a wireless communication technology that enables two or more devices to connect and exchange data. It operates in the 2.4 GHz frequency range and is one of the most popular protocols used for M2M communication.

- Wi-Fi: Wi-Fi is a wireless networking technology that enables two or more devices to connect and exchange data. It operates in the 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz frequency range and is one of the most popular protocols used for M2M communication.

- Zigbee: Zigbee is a wireless communication technology that enables two or more devices to connect and exchange data. It operates in the 2.4 GHz frequency range and is one of the most popular protocols used for M2M communication.

- Near Field Communication (NFC): NFC is a wireless communication technology that enables two or more devices to connect and exchange data. It operates in the 13.56 MHz frequency range and is one of the most popular protocols used for M2M communication.

- Radio Frequency Identification (RFID): RFID is a wireless communication technology that enables two or more devices to connect and exchange data. It operates in the 125 kHz and 13.56 MHz frequency range and is one of the most popular protocols used for M2M communication.

M2M communication is used to enable a wide range of applications, such as home automation, smart energy management, remote monitoring, and industrial automation. It is also used to enable data exchange between connected devices and the cloud.

M2M communication is an important component of the Internet of Things (IoT). It enables data exchange and automation of processes between connected devices, thereby enabling a wide range of applications.
