

### Technology Behind IoT for the Notes of the Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT) in the Subject of Internet of Things

* IoT stands for the Internet of Things, which is a network of physical objects (devices, vehicles, buildings, etc.) that are embedded with sensors and electronics to enable the objects to collect and exchange data.
* IoT devices communicate with each other and other systems over the Internet, allowing for remote monitoring and control.
* The technology behind IoT includes various components such as sensors, actuators, gateways, communication protocols, and cloud computing.
* Sensors are used to detect and measure physical parameters such as temperature, humidity, pressure, and motion.
* Actuators are used to control the physical environment, such as turning on a light or adjusting the temperature.
* Gateways are used to connect the IoT devices to the Internet and to other systems.
* Communication protocols are used to enable the devices to communicate with each other and with other systems. Examples of communication protocols include Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, and ZigBee.
* Cloud computing is used to store and process the data collected from the IoT devices.

* Mnemonics:
  * IoT: Internet of Things
  * Sensors: Sense and Measure Physical Parameters
  * Actuators: Act to Control the Physical Environment
  * Gateways: Connect the IoT Devices to the Internet
  * Communication Protocols: Enable the Devices to Communicate
  * Cloud Computing: Store and Process Data
