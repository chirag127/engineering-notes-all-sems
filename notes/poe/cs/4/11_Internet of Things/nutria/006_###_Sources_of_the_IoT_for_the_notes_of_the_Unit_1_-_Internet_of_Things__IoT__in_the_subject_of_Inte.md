

### Sources of the IoT for the Notes of the Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT) in the Subject of Internet of Things

1. **Sensors**: Sensors are the most essential source of IoT. They are used to measure the physical parameters of the environment such as temperature, pressure, humidity, light, etc. They can also be used to detect motion, sound, and other environmental conditions.

2. **Actuators**: Actuators are the output devices that are used to control the environment. They can be used to control the speed of a motor, open or close a valve, turn on or off a light, etc.

3. **Networks**: Networks are used to connect the sensors and actuators to the cloud or the internet. The most common networks used are Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, ZigBee, and others.

4. **Hardware**: The hardware is the physical components of the IoT system. It includes the sensors, actuators, microcontrollers, and other components.

5. **Software**: The software is the set of programs and applications that are used to control the hardware and the network. It includes operating systems, middleware, and applications.

6. **Data Storage**: Data Storage is used to store the data collected from the sensors and actuators. It can be in the form of databases, files, or cloud storage.

7. **Mnemonics and Learning Tricks**: Mnemonics and learning tricks can be used to remember the different sources of the IoT. For example, the acronym “S.A.N.H.H.” can be used to remember the sources: Sensors, Actuators, Networks, Hardware, and Software.
