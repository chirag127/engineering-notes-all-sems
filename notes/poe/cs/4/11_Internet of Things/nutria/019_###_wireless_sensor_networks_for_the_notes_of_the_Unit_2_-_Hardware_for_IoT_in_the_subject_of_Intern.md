
### Wireless Sensor Networks for the Notes of the Unit 2 - Hardware for IoT in the Subject of Internet of Things

* Wireless sensor networks (WSNs) are composed of a large number of small, low-cost, and low-power devices that are used to monitor physical or environmental conditions such as temperature, pressure, humidity, and sound.
* WSNs are used in a variety of applications, such as environmental monitoring, industrial automation, and medical monitoring.
* WSNs are composed of three main components: sensor nodes, a communication network, and a base station.
* Sensor nodes are small, low-cost, and low-power devices that are used to measure physical or environmental conditions.
* The communication network is used to transmit the data collected by the sensor nodes to the base station.
* The base station is used to process the data and send it to the user.
* WSNs are often deployed in large numbers, with each node communicating with its neighbors.
* WSNs are typically deployed in harsh environments, making them vulnerable to a variety of attacks.
* To protect WSNs from attacks, various security measures such as encryption, authentication, and access control can be implemented.
* WSNs are an important part of the Internet of Things (IoT) and are used to monitor and control a variety of devices and systems.

Mnemonics:

* WSNs: Wireless Sensor Networks
* Nodes: Network of Devices
* Communication: Connecting to the Base Station
* Security: Encryption, Authentication, Access Control
