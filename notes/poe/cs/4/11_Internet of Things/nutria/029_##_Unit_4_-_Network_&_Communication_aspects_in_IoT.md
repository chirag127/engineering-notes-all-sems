

## Unit 4 - Network & Communication aspects in IoT

1. Networking is an essential component of the Internet of Things (IoT). It allows devices to communicate with each other and exchange data.
2. Networking can be wired or wireless, and can use a variety of protocols such as TCP/IP, Bluetooth, and Wi-Fi.
3. Networking can be used to enable remote monitoring, control, and data collection.
4. Communication between devices is essential for the proper functioning of an IoT system.
5. Communication protocols such as MQTT, CoAP, and XMPP are used to enable communication between devices.
6. Security is an important consideration in IoT networks. Security protocols such as TLS and DTLS are used to protect data transmitted over networks.
7. Networking and communication technologies such as LoRaWAN, NB-IoT, and Zigbee are used to enable low-power and long-range communication.
8. Network virtualization is used to enable multiple networks to share the same physical infrastructure.
9. Mnemonic: **N**etworking and **C**ommunication **A**spects of the **I**nternet of **T**hings (NCAIT)
