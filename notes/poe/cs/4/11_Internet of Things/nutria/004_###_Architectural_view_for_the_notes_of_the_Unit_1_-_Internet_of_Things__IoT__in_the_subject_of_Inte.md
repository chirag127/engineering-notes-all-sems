
### Architectural view for the notes of the Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT) in the subject of Internet of Things

The Internet of Things (IoT) is an interconnected network of physical objects, such as sensors and devices, that are able to communicate with each other over the internet. The architecture of the IoT is the foundation for how these objects interact with each other and how data is collected, stored, and analyzed. 

The architecture of the IoT consists of four main layers: the device layer, the communication layer, the platform layer, and the application layer. 

**Device Layer**: The device layer is the foundation of the IoT. It consists of the physical objects, such as sensors and devices, that are connected to the internet. These objects are able to collect, store, and transmit data. 

**Communication Layer**: The communication layer is responsible for the communication between the devices and the platform. It consists of communication protocols, such as Bluetooth and Wi-Fi, that allow the devices to communicate with each other and with the platform. 

**Platform Layer**: The platform layer is the middleman between the device layer and the application layer. It consists of the hardware and software that is used to store and process the data that is collected by the devices. 

**Application Layer**: The application layer is the layer that allows the user to interact with the IoT. It consists of applications that allow the user to view, analyze, and control the data that is collected by the devices. 

Mnemonic: 
IoT: Internet of Things 
DL: Device Layer 
CL: Communication Layer 
PL: Platform Layer 
AL: Application Layer
