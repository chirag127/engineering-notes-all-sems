
### Communication Technologies for the Notes of the Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT) in the Subject of Internet of Things

* Bluetooth: Bluetooth is a low-power, short-range wireless communication technology that is used to connect devices within a range of 10 meters. It is a popular technology used in IoT devices, as it has low power consumption and fast data transfer speeds.
* Wi-Fi: Wi-Fi is a high-speed, long-range wireless communication technology that is used to connect devices within a range of up to 100 meters. It is a popular technology used in IoT devices, as it has high data transfer speeds and is easy to set up.
* Zigbee: Zigbee is a low-power, short-range wireless communication technology that is used to connect devices within a range of up to 10 meters. It is a popular technology used in IoT devices, as it has low power consumption and is easy to set up.
* NFC: NFC stands for Near Field Communication and is a short-range wireless communication technology that is used to connect devices within a range of up to 4 cm. It is a popular technology used in IoT devices, as it is secure and has low power consumption.
* RFID: RFID stands for Radio Frequency Identification and is a long-range wireless communication technology that is used to connect devices within a range of up to 100 meters. It is a popular technology used in IoT devices, as it is secure and has low power consumption.

Mnemonics: 
* Bluetooth: "Be Low To Transfer High" 
* Wi-Fi: "Wireless Fidelity" 
* Zigbee: "Zigzag Bee" 
* NFC: "Near Field Communication" 
* RFID: "Radio Frequency Identification"
