
## Unit 2 - Hardware for IoT

* **Sensors**: Sensors are devices that measure physical quantities such as temperature, pressure, humidity, etc. and convert them into electrical signals. They are used to monitor and control the environment.

* **Actuators**: Actuators are devices that convert electrical signals into physical action. They are used to control the environment.

* **Microcontrollers**: Microcontrollers are small, low-cost computers that can be programmed to perform specific tasks. They are used to control the sensors and actuators.

* **Communication Protocols**: Communication protocols are used to send data between devices. Popular protocols include Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, and Zigbee.

* **Power Sources**: Power sources are used to supply electricity to the devices. Popular power sources include batteries, solar cells, and USB power.

* **Mnemonics**: To help remember the components of an IoT system, try using the acronym "SAPMCP", which stands for Sensors, Actuators, Microcontrollers, Communication Protocols, and Power Sources.
