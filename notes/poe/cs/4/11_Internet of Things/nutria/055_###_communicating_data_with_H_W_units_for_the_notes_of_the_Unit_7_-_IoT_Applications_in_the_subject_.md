
### Communicating Data with H/W Units for the Notes of the Unit 7 - IoT Applications in the Subject of Internet of Things

* Communication between hardware and software units is essential for the successful implementation of Internet of Things (IoT) applications.
* Communication between hardware and software units is often done through a serial port, which is a physical connection between the two units.
* Serial ports provide a reliable and secure way to send and receive data, and can be used for both short-distance and long-distance communication.
* For short-distance communication, the most commonly used protocol is the Universal Serial Bus (USB) protocol.
* For long-distance communication, the most commonly used protocol is the Ethernet protocol.
* Data can be transmitted over a serial port in two ways: synchronous and asynchronous.
* In synchronous communication, the data is sent in a continuous stream, with the data being sent and received at the same rate.
* In asynchronous communication, the data is sent in discrete blocks, with the data being sent and received at different rates.
* To ensure reliable communication between hardware and software units, it is important to use error-correcting protocols, such as the Cyclic Redundancy Check (CRC) and Checksum algorithms.
* To improve the efficiency of communication, compression algorithms, such as the Huffman coding algorithm, can be used.
* To ensure the security of the communication, encryption algorithms, such as the Advanced Encryption Standard (AES) algorithm, can be used.
* Mnemonics can be used to help remember the various protocols, algorithms, and techniques used in communicating data between hardware and software units. For example, the acronym "USB" can be used to help remember the Universal Serial Bus protocol.
