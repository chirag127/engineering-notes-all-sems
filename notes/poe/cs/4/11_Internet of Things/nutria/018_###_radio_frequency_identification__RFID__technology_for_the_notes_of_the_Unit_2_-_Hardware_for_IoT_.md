

### Radio Frequency Identification (RFID) Technology for the Notes of the Unit 2 - Hardware for IoT in the Subject of Internet of Things

RFID technology is a form of wireless communication that uses radio waves to identify and track objects. It is used in a variety of applications, including contactless payment, asset tracking, and inventory management. RFID technology is an important component of the Internet of Things (IoT) as it allows for the identification and tracking of objects in a networked environment. 

The following are some key points about RFID technology:

- RFID stands for Radio Frequency Identification.
- RFID technology uses radio waves to identify and track objects.
- RFID technology is used in a variety of applications, including contactless payment, asset tracking, and inventory management.
- RFID technology is an important component of the Internet of Things (IoT).
- RFID tags are small devices that contain a chip and an antenna.
- RFID readers are used to read the information stored in the RFID tag.
- RFID technology is used in a variety of industries, including retail, healthcare, and manufacturing.

Mnemonics and Learning Tricks: 
- RFID stands for Radio Frequency Identification. 
- RFID can be remembered as "Radio Frequency Identification Device". 
- RFID tags can be remembered as "Tiny Tags with a Chip and an Antenna". 
- RFID readers can be remembered as "Readers that Read the Information Stored in the RFID Tag".
