
### Conceptual Framework for the notes of the Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT) in the subject of Internet of Things

1. IoT is a network of physical objects, such as sensors and devices, that are connected to the internet and can communicate with each other. 
2. It enables the collection and exchange of data between the physical objects and other systems, such as the cloud. 
3. It is the foundation for the development of smart cities, smart homes, and smart factories. 
4. IoT systems are composed of three main components: sensors, actuators, and communication networks. 
5. Sensors are used to collect data from the physical environment, such as temperature, humidity, and pressure. 
6. Actuators are used to control the physical environment, such as turning on lights or adjusting the temperature. 
7. Communication networks are used to transmit data between the sensors and actuators. 
8. IoT systems can be used to monitor and control physical processes, such as monitoring a production line or controlling a heating system. 
9. They can also be used to monitor and control digital processes, such as tracking inventory or managing a supply chain. 
10. IoT systems can be used to improve efficiency, reduce costs, and increase safety. 
11. They can also be used to provide new services, such as predictive maintenance and remote monitoring. 
12. Security is a major concern for IoT systems, as they can be vulnerable to malicious attacks. 
13. To ensure the security of IoT systems, it is important to use secure communication protocols and encryption.
