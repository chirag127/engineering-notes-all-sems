
## Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT)

IoT is an interconnected network of physical devices, vehicles, home appliances and other items embedded with electronics, software, sensors, actuators, and connectivity which enable these objects to connect and exchange data. IoT is a rapidly growing field of technology that is transforming the way we interact with the world around us.

* **Mnemonics**: 
  * I - Internet 
  * O - of 
  * T - Things 

* **Advantages**: 
  * Increased convenience: IoT devices allow for greater convenience for users by automating many everyday tasks.
  * Improved accuracy: IoT devices can collect and analyze data much more accurately than humans, leading to improved decision-making and accuracy.
  * Improved safety: IoT devices can be used to monitor and control dangerous environments, improving safety for workers and the general public.
  * Cost savings: IoT devices can reduce costs by automating processes, leading to lower overhead and improved efficiency.

* **Disadvantages**: 
  * Security risks: IoT devices are vulnerable to hacking and data theft, leading to potential security risks.
  * Privacy concerns: IoT devices can collect and store personal data, leading to privacy concerns.
  * Complexity: IoT devices can be complex to set up and maintain, leading to confusion and frustration.

* **Applications**: 
  * Smart homes: IoT devices can be used to automate and control various aspects of a home, such as lighting, temperature, security, and more.
  * Smart cities: IoT devices can be used to monitor and control various aspects of a city, such as traffic, energy, water, and more.
  * Wearables: IoT devices can be used to track and monitor various aspects of a person’s health and fitness, such as heart rate, steps taken, calorie intake, and more.
  * Automotive: IoT devices can be used to monitor and control various aspects of a vehicle, such as fuel efficiency, engine performance, and more.
