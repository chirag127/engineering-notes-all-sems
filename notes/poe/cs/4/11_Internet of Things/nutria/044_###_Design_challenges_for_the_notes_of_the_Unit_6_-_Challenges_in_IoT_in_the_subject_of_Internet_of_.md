
### Design Challenges for the Notes of the Unit 6 - Challenges in IoT in the Subject of Internet of Things

* Security: Security is a major challenge in the Internet of Things. As more and more devices are connected to the Internet, the risk of malicious actors gaining access to sensitive data increases. To protect users, organizations must implement measures such as encryption, authentication, and authorization.
* Scalability: As the number of connected devices increases, the IoT infrastructure must be able to scale to meet the demands of the growing network. This includes ensuring that the network is robust enough to handle the large amounts of data generated by connected devices.
* Interoperability: To ensure that devices from different manufacturers can communicate with each other, organizations must ensure that the IoT infrastructure is interoperable. This requires developing standards for communication protocols and data formats. 
* Privacy: As more data is collected and shared, users must be assured that their data is kept secure and private. Organizations must implement measures such as encryption and authentication to protect user data.
* Reliability: As the number of connected devices increases, organizations must ensure that the IoT infrastructure is reliable. This includes ensuring that the network is robust enough to handle large amounts of data and that devices are able to communicate with each other without interruption.
* Cost: The cost of implementing and maintaining an IoT infrastructure can be high. Organizations must ensure that the cost of implementing and maintaining the IoT infrastructure is manageable and that the benefits outweigh the costs.

To help remember the challenges of the Internet of Things, try using the mnemonic “SIPRC” for Security, Interoperability, Privacy, Reliability, and Cost.
