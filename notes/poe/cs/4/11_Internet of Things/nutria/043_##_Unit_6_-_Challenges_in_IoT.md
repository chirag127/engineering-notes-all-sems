

## Unit 6 - Challenges in IoT

IoT (Internet of Things) is an ever-growing network of interconnected devices and systems. It enables us to control and monitor these devices remotely, allowing us to operate and manage them more efficiently and effectively. However, with the increased complexity of these systems, come a number of challenges. 

The following are some of the challenges associated with IoT:

* **Security:** IoT devices are often connected to the internet, making them vulnerable to cyber-attacks. Additionally, due to the sheer number of devices, it can be difficult to monitor and secure them all. 

* **Data Storage:** With the large amount of data generated by IoT devices, it can be difficult to store and manage the data efficiently.

* **Network Connectivity:** IoT devices often rely on wireless networks, which can be unreliable and prone to interference.

* **Data Privacy:** IoT devices can collect and store sensitive personal data, which can be vulnerable to misuse.

* **Hardware Compatibility:** Different IoT devices are often not compatible with each other, making it difficult to integrate them into a single system.

* **Power Consumption:** IoT devices often require a lot of power, which can be difficult to manage.

* **Cost:** IoT systems can be expensive to set up and maintain, due to the cost of the hardware and software.

To help remember these challenges, you can use the mnemonic: **SDPCHCP**, which stands for Security, Data Storage, Network Connectivity, Data Privacy, Hardware Compatibility, Power Consumption, and Cost.
