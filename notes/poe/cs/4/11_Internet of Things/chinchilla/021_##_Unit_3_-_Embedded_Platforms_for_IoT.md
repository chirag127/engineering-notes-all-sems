## Unit 3 - Embedded Platforms for IoT

Embedded platforms are the foundation of IoT systems. They are small, low-power, and low-cost computing devices that can be programmed to perform specific functions. The main purpose of embedded platforms is to collect data from sensors, process it, and send it to the cloud or other devices. In this unit, we will learn about the different types of embedded platforms used in IoT systems and their characteristics.

### Types of Embedded Platforms

1. Microcontrollers: Microcontrollers are small computers on a single chip. They are used in a variety of applications, including home automation, industrial control, and medical devices. Microcontrollers have low power consumption and are designed to perform specific functions.

2. Single-board computers: Single-board computers are complete computers on a single board. They are used in a wide range of applications, including robotics, home automation, and media centers. Single-board computers are more powerful than microcontrollers and can run a full operating system.

3. System on a Chip (SoC): SoC is a complete computer system on a single chip. It includes a microprocessor, memory, and other components necessary for a complete computer system. SoCs are used in a variety of applications, including smartphones, tablets, and IoT devices.

### Characteristics of Embedded Platforms

1. Low power consumption: Embedded platforms are designed to consume minimal power because they are often battery-powered and need to operate for long periods without recharging.

2. Small size: Embedded platforms are small in size and can be easily integrated into devices without taking up too much space.

3. Low cost: Embedded platforms are relatively inexpensive compared to traditional computing devices, making them suitable for mass production.

4. Real-time processing: Embedded platforms are designed to process data in real-time, which is essential in many IoT applications.

### Mnemonics and Learning Tricks

- For remembering the types of embedded platforms, you can use the mnemonic "MSS" - Microcontrollers, Single-board computers, SoC.

- To remember the characteristics of embedded platforms, you can use the mnemonic "SPLR" - Small size, Power consumption, Low cost, Real-time processing.

### Advantages of Embedded Platforms for IoT

1. Low cost: Embedded platforms are relatively inexpensive compared to traditional computing devices, making them suitable for mass production.

2. Low power consumption: Embedded platforms are designed to consume minimal power, making them ideal for battery-powered devices.

3. Real-time processing: Embedded platforms are designed to process data in real-time, which is essential in many IoT applications.

4. Compact size: Embedded platforms are small in size and can be easily integrated into devices without taking up too much space.

### Disadvantages of Embedded Platforms for IoT

1. Limited processing power: Embedded platforms may not have the processing power required for some applications.

2. Limited memory: Embedded platforms may have limited memory, which can limit the amount of data that can be processed.

3. Limited connectivity: Embedded platforms may have limited connectivity options, which can make it difficult to communicate with other devices.

### Examples of Embedded Platforms for IoT

1. Raspberry Pi: The Raspberry Pi is a popular single-board computer used in a variety of IoT applications.

2. Arduino: The Arduino is a microcontroller used in a wide range of applications, including robotics and home automation.

3. BeagleBone Black: The BeagleBone Black is a single-board computer used in industrial control and robotics.

### Applications of Embedded Platforms for IoT

1. Home automation: Embedded platforms are used in home automation systems to control lighting, heating, and security systems.

2. Industrial control: Embedded platforms are used in industrial control systems to monitor and control manufacturing processes.

3. Medical devices: Embedded platforms are used in medical devices such as blood glucose monitors and insulin pumps.

In conclusion, embedded platforms are the backbone of IoT systems, providing the necessary computing power to collect data, process it, and send it to the cloud or other devices. With their low cost, low power consumption, and small size, embedded platforms are ideal for mass production of IoT devices in a wide range of applications.
