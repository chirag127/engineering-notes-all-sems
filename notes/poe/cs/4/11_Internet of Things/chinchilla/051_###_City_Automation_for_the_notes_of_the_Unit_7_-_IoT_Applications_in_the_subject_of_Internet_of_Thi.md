### City Automation for the Notes of Unit 7 - IoT Applications in the Subject of Internet of Things

City automation refers to the use of Internet of Things (IoT) devices and sensors to automate various aspects of city life such as traffic management, waste management, energy management, and more. The use of IoT in city automation can lead to more efficient and sustainable cities.

#### Advantages of City Automation:

- Improved Traffic Management: IoT sensors can be used to monitor traffic flow, detect accidents, and adjust traffic signals accordingly. This can help reduce traffic congestion and improve overall traffic management.

- Efficient Waste Management: IoT sensors can be used to monitor waste levels in bins and containers, and schedule waste collection accordingly. This can lead to more efficient waste management and reduce overflowing bins.

- Energy Management: IoT sensors can be used to monitor energy usage in buildings and adjust heating, cooling, and lighting accordingly. This can lead to more efficient energy usage and reduce energy costs.

- Improved Public Safety: IoT sensors can be used to monitor public spaces and detect potential safety hazards. This can lead to quicker responses to emergencies and improve overall public safety.

#### Applications of City Automation:

- Smart Parking: IoT sensors can be used to detect available parking spots and guide drivers to them. This can help reduce traffic congestion and improve the parking experience for drivers.

- Smart Lighting: IoT sensors can be used to adjust the lighting in public spaces based on the time of day and the presence of people. This can lead to more efficient energy usage and improve public safety.

- Smart Waste Management: IoT sensors can be used to monitor waste levels in bins and containers and schedule waste collection accordingly. This can lead to more efficient waste management and reduce overflowing bins.

#### Learning Trick:

To remember the advantages of city automation, you can use the mnemonic "TWEEPS", which stands for Traffic Management, Waste Management, Energy Management, Efficiency, Public Safety, and Sustainability.
