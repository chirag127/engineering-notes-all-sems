#### Overview of ARM Cortex Processors

The ARM Cortex processors are a family of 32-bit and 64-bit RISC (Reduced Instruction Set Computing) processors that are designed for low power consumption and high processing power. These processors are widely used in various applications, including smartphones, tablets, digital cameras, and IoT devices. The ARM Cortex processors are classified into three main categories based on their performance and power consumption:

- Cortex-A: These processors are designed for high-performance applications and are used in smartphones, tablets, and other mobile devices.
- Cortex-R: These processors are designed for real-time applications and are used in automotive, aerospace, and industrial applications.
- Cortex-M: These processors are designed for low-power and cost-sensitive applications and are widely used in IoT devices, such as wearables, home appliances, and sensors.
