### Beagle Bone for the notes of the Unit 3 - Embedded Platforms for IoT in the subject of Internet of Things

BeagleBone is an open-source hardware platform designed to enable developers and hobbyists to create projects that involve electronics, sensors, and the internet of things. It is a single-board computer that runs Linux and is capable of interfacing with a wide range of hardware peripherals.

#### Features of BeagleBone

- Processor: BeagleBone has a 1GHz Sitara AM3358 ARM Cortex-A8 processor, which is capable of running at up to 1GHz.

- Memory: It has 512MB of DDR3 RAM, 4GB of onboard flash storage, and a microSD card slot for additional storage.

- Connectivity: BeagleBone has support for Ethernet, USB, and HDMI. It also has Wi-Fi and Bluetooth connectivity.

- GPIO pins: It has 65 digital I/O pins and 7 analog input pins, which can be used to interface with a wide range of sensors and other hardware.

- Operating System: BeagleBone runs on Linux, which makes it easy to develop software for the platform.

#### Learning Tricks and Mnemonics

- The BeagleBone has a bone-shaped design, which can help you remember its name.

- Remember that the processor is a Sitara AM3358 ARM Cortex-A8 processor, which you can remember as SAM3358.

- BeagleBone has 65 digital I/O pins, which you can remember as "65 DIO".

#### Advantages of BeagleBone

- BeagleBone is an open-source platform, which means that it is easy to find documentation, tutorials, and other resources online.

#### Disadvantages of BeagleBone
