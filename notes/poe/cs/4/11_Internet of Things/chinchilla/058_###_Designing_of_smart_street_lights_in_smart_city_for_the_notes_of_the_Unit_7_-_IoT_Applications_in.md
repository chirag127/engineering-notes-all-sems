### Designing of smart street lights in smart city for the notes of the Unit 7 - IoT Applications in the subject of Internet of Things

Smart street lights are a significant aspect of smart city development. IoT technology enables street lights to be connected to a network, making them "smart" and capable of communicating with one another. These lights can be designed to be energy-efficient and eco-friendly, saving money for the city and reducing carbon emissions. In this unit, we will discuss the designing of smart street lights in smart cities.

#### Components of Smart Street Lights

The designing of smart street lights in a smart city involves the following components:

1. LED lights - LED lights are energy-efficient and can be controlled remotely, making them suitable for smart street lights.
2. Sensors - Sensors can detect the presence of vehicles, pedestrians, and bikes and adjust the light accordingly. They can also detect the level of ambient light and adjust the brightness of the street light accordingly.
3. Communication network - A communication network is required to connect all the smart street lights with one another.
4. Control system - A control system is required to manage the smart street lights and monitor their performance.

#### Advantages of Smart Street Lights

The following are the advantages of smart street lights:

1. Energy-efficient - Smart street lights can be designed to consume less energy and save money for the city.
2. Eco-friendly - Smart street lights can reduce carbon emissions and help the city to achieve its sustainability goals.
3. Cost-effective - Smart street lights can reduce maintenance costs and save money for the city.
4. Improved safety - Smart street lights can detect the presence of vehicles, pedestrians, and bikes and adjust the light accordingly, making the streets safer.

#### Mnemonics and Learning Tricks

One mnemonic for remembering the components of smart street lights is LEDS-C, which stands for:

- LED lights
- Energy-efficient
- Detection sensors
- Communication network
- Control system

Another learning trick is to remember the advantages of smart street lights using the acronym EICC:

- Energy-efficient
- Eco-friendly
- Cost-effective
- Improved safety
- Connected

#### Conclusion

Smart street lights are a crucial component of a smart city. They can save energy, reduce costs, and improve safety. The designing of smart street lights requires the integration of various components such as LED lights, sensors, communication network, and control system. By using IoT technology, smart street lights can communicate with one another and improve the overall efficiency of the city's lighting system.
