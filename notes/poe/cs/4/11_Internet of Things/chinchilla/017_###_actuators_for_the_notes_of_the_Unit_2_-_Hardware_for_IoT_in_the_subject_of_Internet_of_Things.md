### Actuators for the Notes of Unit 2 - Hardware for IoT in the Subject of Internet of Things

Actuators are an essential component of the hardware for IoT systems. They are responsible for converting electrical signals or data received from sensors into physical actions or movements. In simple terms, they are the parts of a system that enable it to perform a task or function. Let's take a closer look at some of the most commonly used actuators in IoT systems.

#### 1. Motors
- Motors are the most common type of actuator used in IoT systems, and they are responsible for converting electrical energy into mechanical energy to produce movement.
- There are two main types of motors: DC motors and AC motors. DC motors are generally used for low-power applications, while AC motors are used for high-power applications.
- Some of the most common applications of motors in IoT systems include robotics, automation, and industrial control.

#### 2. Solenoids
- A solenoid is an actuator that converts electrical energy into linear motion. It consists of a coil of wire wrapped around a magnetic core.
- When an electrical current is applied to the coil, it creates a magnetic field that pulls a plunger or piston inside the solenoid towards the center of the coil.
- Solenoids are commonly used in applications such as door locks, valves, and switches.

#### 3. Servos
- Servos are actuators that are used to control the position of an object with high precision. They consist of a motor, a control circuit, and a feedback mechanism.
- The control circuit sends a signal to the motor, which rotates the output shaft to a specific position. The feedback mechanism then sends a signal back to the control circuit to indicate the actual position of the output shaft.
- Servos are commonly used in applications such as robotics, remote control devices, and camera stabilization systems.

#### 4. Piezoelectric Actuators
- Piezoelectric actuators are a type of actuator that uses the piezoelectric effect to convert electrical energy into mechanical energy.
- The piezoelectric effect is the ability of certain materials to generate an electric charge when subjected to mechanical stress.
- Piezoelectric actuators are commonly used in applications such as precision positioning systems, vibration control, and ultrasonic devices.

#### 5. Hydraulic and Pneumatic Actuators
- Hydraulic and pneumatic actuators are used to convert fluid pressure into mechanical force.
- Hydraulic actuators use a liquid such as oil or water to transmit pressure, while pneumatic actuators use compressed air or gas.
- These types of actuators are commonly used in applications such as industrial machinery, robotics, and heavy equipment.

Mnemonic: "MS SPPH" (Motor, Solenoid, Servo, Piezoelectric, Hydraulic/Pneumatic)

Learning Trick: Remember the acronym "MS SPPH" and associate each letter with the first letter of the five types of actuators. Repeat the acronym several times until it becomes easy to remember.
