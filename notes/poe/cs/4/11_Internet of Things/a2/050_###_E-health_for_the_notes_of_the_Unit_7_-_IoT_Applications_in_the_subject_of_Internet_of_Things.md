### E-health for the notes of the Unit 7 - IoT Applications in the subject of Internet of Things

E-health refers to the electronic systems used for health services and information. IoT has enabled advancements in e-health by allowing medical devices and applications to connect and exchange data over the Internet.

Some key e-health IoT applications are:

- Wearable devices: Devices like fitness trackers, smartwatches, etc. can monitor health metrics like heart rate, sleep, steps taken, etc. and provide personalized health insights.
- Remote patient monitoring: Sensors can monitor patients remotely and alert doctors/family in case of abnormalities. This allows patients to be monitored 24/7 from home and avoid unnecessary hospitalizations.
- Smart hospitals: IoT devices can automate various tasks in hospitals like asset tracking, handwashing compliance, equipment maintenance, etc. This improves operational efficiency and patient experience.
- Connected medical devices: Medical devices can connect to the Internet and exchange data to enable remote monitoring and consultations. For example, an ECG machine can send heart readings to a doctor in another city for diagnosis.

Some advantages of e-health IoT are increased accessibility, low-cost monitoring, and prevention of health issues. However, there are also risks like data breaches, device vulnerabilities, and increased dependence on technology which need to be addressed.

E-health has promising applications, but also many open challenges. With ongoing advancements in IoT and healthcare technologies, e-health IoT systems will likely transform the future of healthcare by enabling seamless monitoring, precise diagnostics, and personalized treatments.
