### Sources of the IoT for the notes of the Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT) in the subject of Internet of Things

The sources of the Internet of Things (IoT) are:

1.  Sensors: Sensors are devices that detect and respond to electrical or optical signals. They are used to sense the environment. For example, temperature sensors, motion sensors, pressure sensors, etc. These sensors collect data from the surrounding environment and send it to the network.
2.  RFID Tags: Radio-Frequency Identification (RFID) tags are small microchips that store information and transmit data wirelessly using radio waves. They are used to track and identify objects. For example, RFID tags are used for toll collection, tracking products in a supply chain, etc. These RFID tags send the data to the network.
3.  Devices: Various smart devices that are connected to the Internet form an IoT network. For example, smart home devices like smart speakers, smart displays, smart appliances, etc. These devices can send and receive data over the Internet.
4.  Gateways: Gateways act as an interface between the sensors/devices and the network. They collect the data from the sensors/devices and transmit it over the network. They also receive data or commands from the network and send it to the sensors/devices. For example, a smart home gateway connects the smart home devices to the home Wi-Fi network.
5.  Networks: The networks carry the data between the gateways/devices and the servers. It can be a local network (like home Wi-Fi) or a wide area network (like cellular data network). The data is transmitted over these networks to IoT platforms or servers for storage, processing, and analysis.

Some mnemonics to remember the sources are:

-   SSDRG: Sensors, RFID Tags, Devices, Gateways
-   NEWS: Networks, Ethernet, Wi-Fi, Sensors

The advantages of IoT are automation, remote monitoring, fast communication, etc. The disadvantages are privacy concerns, security issues, technical difficulties, etc.
IoT has applications in smart homes, smart cities, healthcare, transportation, etc.
