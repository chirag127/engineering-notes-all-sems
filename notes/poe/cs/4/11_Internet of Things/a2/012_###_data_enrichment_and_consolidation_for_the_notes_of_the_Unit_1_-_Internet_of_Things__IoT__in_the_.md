### Data enrichment and consolidation for the notes of Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT)

The following are some Mnemonics and learning tricks to remember the concepts:

- IoT: ISeeObjectsTalking - To remember that IoT is about devices/objects/things sensing and communicating.
- IP address: IPlayPingPong - To remember the four parts of an IP address - first byte, second byte, third byte and fourth byte.
- IPv4 vs IPv6: IPsVastlyImplementedTrialledFirst(IPv4), IPsVastlyIncreased(IPv6) - To remember that IPv6 was created to solve the limitations of IPv4 in addressing more devices.

Points to cover in the notes:

1. IoT definition: Interconnection of uniquely identifiable internet-connected objects/devices that can sense, communicate and act/react.
2. IoT architecture: Perception layer, network layer, application layer.
3. IoT enabling technologies: IoT platforms, networking technologies (WiFi, Bluetooth, cellular), identification technologies (RFID, QR codes), sensing technologies (sensors and actuators).
4. Growth and trends in IoT: Emergence of smart home devices, smart cities, industrial IoT, healthcare IoT, etc. Massive growth in connected devices and data generated.
5. Challenges and risks: Data security and privacy, device management, lack of standardization, high costs, etc.
6. Applications of IoT: Smart homes, smart cities, industrial automation, healthcare, transportation and logistics, environmental monitoring, etc.
