### M2M Communication for the notes of the Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT) in the subject of Internet of Things

M2M communication refers to direct communication between machines or devices. In the context of IoT, it enables connected devices to communicate with each other without human intervention. Some key points about M2M communication for IoT:

- Devices can communicate with each other over wired or wireless networks like WiFi, Bluetooth, cellular networks, etc.
- The communication is machine-generated and machine-interpreted. Humans are not involved in the communication process.
- The devices can send and receive data, detect and respond to events or triggers, and take actions or control other devices autonomously.
- M2M communication allows IoT devices to work together seamlessly and deliver smart functionality. For example, a motion sensor can send data to the smart lights to turn them on, a smoke detector can alert the fire alarm system, etc.
- Some applications of M2M communication in IoT include smart homes, smart cities, industrial automation, remote monitoring, etc.

**Mnemonics:**

- Machines talking to machines
- No humans in the loop
- Wired or wireless communication
- Enabling collaboration between connected devices

**Advantages:**

- Faster response times as human intervention is avoided
- Increased efficiency and automation
- Constant monitoring and control without human presence

**Disadvantages:**

- Prone to security and privacy threats as devices can be attacked or hacked
- Challenges in managing and updating software/firmware on large numbers of devices
- Reliance on stable networks and connectivity which can be impacted by issues
