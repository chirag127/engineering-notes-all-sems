## Unit 2 - Hardware for IoT

1. Microcontrollers - Microcontrollers are small computers on a single integrated circuit containing a processor core, memory, and programmable input/output peripherals. They are used to control electronic devices and machines. Some popular microcontrollers used in IoT are:
- Arduino: Open-source microcontroller boards useful for prototyping and learning IoT. Easy to program with an intuitive IDE.
- Raspberry Pi: Credit card-sized single-board computer running Linux. Can function as a microcontroller and be used for more complex IoT applications.
- ESP8266: Low-cost Wi-Fi microchip with full TCP/IP stack and microcontroller capability. Great for DIY IoT projects connecting to Wi-Fi.

2. Sensors - Sensors are input devices that convert physical phenomena into electrical signals which can be measured or recorded. Common sensors used in IoT include:
- Temperature sensors: Measure ambient temperature, detect heat, etc.
- Motion sensors: Detect movement, proximity, etc.
- Light sensors: Measure ambient light intensity.
- Pressure sensors: Measure air or liquid pressure.
- Humidity sensors: Measure ambient humidity.
