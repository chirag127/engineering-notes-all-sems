### Wireless Sensor Networks for the notes of the Unit 2 - Hardware for IoT

Wireless Sensor Networks (WSNs) consist of small nodes with sensors, embedded processors, and wireless transceivers. They are used to monitor physical or environmental conditions, such as temperature, sound, vibration, pressure, motion or pollutants and to cooperatively pass their data through the network to a main location.

- WSN components: Sensor nodes, Gateway node and Base station.
- Sensor nodes: Tiny devices with sensors, embedded processors and wireless transceivers. They sense data and transmit it.
- Gateway node: More powerful node that passes data from sensor nodes to the base station. It is used to connect different types of networks.
- Base station: Where data is collected, stored and analyzed. It is more powerful than other nodes.

Advantages:
- Easy to deploy as they are wireless.
- Inexpensive as they use low power components.
- Flexible as they can have various sensors for different applications.

Disadvantages:
- Limited power since they use batteries.
- Low range as they use low power transmitters.
- Vulnerable to attacks as they are wireless and distributed.

Applications:
- Environmental monitoring.
- Structural health monitoring.
- Machine monitoring.
- Medical applications.
- Tracking and navigation systems.
