### Participatory Sensing Technology for the notes of the Unit 2 - Hardware for IoT in the subject of Internet of Things

Participatory sensing refers to the collective utilization of sensors, computing devices and human intelligence to gain insights and useful information about the environment, community or issues of common interest. In participatory sensing for IoT, everyday objects and humans with sensor-equipped devices are engaged in collecting and sharing data to enable public applications and services.

Some key points about Participatory Sensing Technology:

- It utilizes sensors and sensor-equipped personal devices like smartphones to gather data about the environment, community and issues.
- The gathered data is shared, aggregated and analyzed to gain insights and useful information.
- It enables public IoT applications and services as it engages common people and everyday objects in data collection and sharing.
- The scale of data gathering can be very large as many participants and devices are involved.
- Privacy and security concerns are there due to personal devices and data being utilized. Appropriate measures need to be taken.
- It can be used for applications like air/water quality monitoring, traffic monitoring, noise level monitoring, etc.

Here are some mnemonics and learning tricks for Participatory Sensing Technology:

- Think of many "participants" with "sensors" sensing the environment - Participatory Sensing
- Personal devices and everyday objects sense and share - easy to remember
- Insights from shared and aggregated data - this is the key goal
- Applications for public welfare and common interest - the motivation

The advantages of large-scale data collection and insights for public interest need to be weighed against the privacy and security issues. With appropriate measures, participatory sensing can enable impactful IoT applications and services utilizing the power of human and technological collaboration.
