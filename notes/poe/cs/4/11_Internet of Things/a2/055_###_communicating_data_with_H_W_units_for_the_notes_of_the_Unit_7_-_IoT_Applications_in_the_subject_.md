### Communicating data with H/W units for the notes of the Unit 7 - IoT Applications in the subject of Internet of Things

- IoT devices and systems communicate with each other using various communication protocols and technologies like WiFi, Bluetooth, Zigbee, etc. These protocols help in establishing a connection and exchanging data between hardware units.
- Some key points to remember:

**WiFi:**

- Used for connecting to local networks and the internet.
- Has a range of 30-100 meters indoors and 300-500 meters outdoors.
- Requires more power than Bluetooth and Zigbee.
- Uses 2.4 GHz or 5 GHz frequency bands.

**Bluetooth:**

- Used for short-range wireless communication between devices.
- Has a typical range of 10 meters.
- Uses 2.4 GHz frequency band.
- Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) is optimized for low power IoT applications.

**Zigbee:**

- Used for short-range, low-power wireless communication.
- Has a range of 10-100 meters.
- Uses 2.4 GHz frequency band.
- Supports large networks of thousands of devices with low latency.

**RFID:**

- Used to exchange data over short distances using radio waves.
- Tags containing RFID chips are scanned by RFID readers to retrieve and store data.
- Used for applications like contactless payments, access control, etc.

Advantages: These communication technologies are wireless, low cost, and enable efficient data exchange.
