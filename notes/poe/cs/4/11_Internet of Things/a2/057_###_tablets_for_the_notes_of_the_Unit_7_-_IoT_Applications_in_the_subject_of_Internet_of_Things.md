1. Smart Homes
- IoT devices like sensors, actuators, microcontrollers, etc. are embedded in home appliances and connected to the Internet to automate home tasks like lighting, temperature, security, entertainment, etc.
- Examples: Smart speakers to control devices, smart thermostats to control temperature, smart locks, smart smoke detectors, etc.
- Mnemonics: Think of your home devices becoming smart and interconnected (Sensors-Actuators-Internet) to automate tasks and make living efficient.

2. Smart Cities
- IoT devices are installed in a city infrastructure to enable smart management of transportation, utilities, public services, environment, and more.
- Examples: Smart parking systems, smart traffic management systems, smart lighting systems, smart waste management systems, environmental monitoring systems, etc.
- Mnemonics: Imagine a futuristic city with various interconnected IoT systems (Sensors-Actuators-Internet) to automate and optimize the city operations.

3. Industrial IoT (IIoT)
- IoT devices are used in industries to enable smart monitoring and control of manufacturing equipment, supply chain, and business processes.
- Examples: Predictive maintenance of machines, automation of assembly lines, tracking inventory and assets, monitoring environment and quality, coordinating logistics, etc.
- Mnemonics: Think of industries getting smarter by connecting equipment and processes (Sensors-Actuators-Internet) to increase efficiency, reduce costs, avoid downtimes, and gain valuable insights.
