### Vision for the notes of the Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT) in the subject of Internet of Things

- Internet of Things (IoT) refers to the interconnection of everyday physical objects/devices via the internet. These devices are enabled with sensors, software, and networking connectivity that allows them to send and receive data.
- The vision of IoT is to enable everyday physical objects/devices to be connected to the internet and to each other to create opportunities for more precise monitoring, awareness, and control of the physical world.
- Some of the key components of the IoT system are:
    - Sensors: To sense the environment and collect data. Eg: Temperature sensors, motion sensors, etc.
    - Connectivity: To establish network connections between devices. Eg: WiFi, Bluetooth, RFID, NFC, etc.
    - Processors: To process the data collected by sensors. Eg: Microcontrollers, processors, etc.
    - Software: To send, receive and analyze data. Eg: Protocols, applications, etc.
    - Cloud: To store and exchange data.
- The core idea behind IoT is machine-to-machine (M2M) communication which allows devices to automatically transfer data and take actions based on the data received without human involvement.
- Some of the advantages of IoT are: automation, remote monitoring, predictive analysis, increased efficiency, etc.
- Some of the disadvantages of IoT are: security and privacy issues, complex infrastructure, lack of standards, high costs, etc.
- IoT has a wide range of applications such as smart homes, smart cities, industrial automation, healthcare, transportation, and more.
