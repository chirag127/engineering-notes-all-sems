### Definition for the notes of the Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT) in the subject of Internet of Things

The Internet of Things (IoT) refers to the interconnection of everyday physical objects/devices through the internet. These devices are equipped with sensors, software, and networking capabilities that allow them to send and receive data. IoT enables everyday objects to be sensing and responding entities that can be controlled remotely across existing network infrastructure.

Some key characteristics of IoT:

- Connectivity: IoT devices are connected to the internet and can send and receive data.
- Sensors: IoT devices have sensors that can detect and measure physical parameters from the surrounding environment like temperature, motion, pressure, etc.
- Computing: IoT devices have computing capabilities that can process the data from sensors and perform actions.
- Software: IoT devices run on software programs that can be remotely accessed and updated.
- Interoperability: IoT devices can interconnect and exchange data with various other devices and systems.

**Mnemonics**: Connecting Sensors, Computing software for Interoperability - remember this to recall the key characteristics of IoT.

**Advantages**: Increased efficiency, automation, remote monitoring, predictive analysis, etc.

**Disadvantages**: Security and privacy risks, complex architecture, high costs, etc.

**Applications**: Smart homes, smart cities, industrial automation, healthcare, transportation, and more.
