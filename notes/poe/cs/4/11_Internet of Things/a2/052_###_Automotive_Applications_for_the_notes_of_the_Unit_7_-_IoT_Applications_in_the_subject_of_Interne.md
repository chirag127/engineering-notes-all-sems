### Automotive Applications

- Connected cars - Cars are being equipped with IoT sensors and connectivity which helps in tracking location, diagnosing mechanical issues, providing navigation assistance, accessing car statistics like fuel level, tire pressure, etc. Examples - OnStar, Toyota's T-Connect, Hyundai BlueLink, etc.
- Smart parking - Sensors can detect available parking spots and navigate drivers to the location. It reduces time wasted in finding parking.
- Fleet management - IoT helps in tracking and monitoring commercial vehicles and manages fleet effectively. It tracks vehicle location, fuel level, efficiency, delivery status, etc.
- Intelligent traffic systems - IoT sensors and cameras can detect traffic and suggest alternate routes. They can control traffic signals to reduce congestion.
- Vehicle telematics - IoT helps monitor vehicle diagnostics to schedule maintenance and detect mechanical/engine issues proactively. It increases vehicle lifespan and reduces breakdowns.

Advantages - IoT makes driving efficient, convenient and safer. It reduces fuel usage and costs. It provides early detection of issues and prevents accidents.
Disadvantages - Expensive implementation and additional costs to consumers. Privacy and security concerns with large amount of data being collected and shared.

Examples -

- Smart parking bay sensors detect vacant spots and alert/navigate drivers to the location.
- In-vehicle IoT devices monitor engine oil, coolant level, tyre pressure, etc. and alert drivers or schedule maintenance.
- IoT cameras and sensors on roads track traffic and control signals to reduce delays.

Applications - The applications of IoT in automotive industry are rapidly increasing. More and more vehicles are being equipped with connectivity and an increasing number of smart city projects are including IoT powered intelligent traffic systems. IoT has the potential to radically change driving and transportation in the future.
