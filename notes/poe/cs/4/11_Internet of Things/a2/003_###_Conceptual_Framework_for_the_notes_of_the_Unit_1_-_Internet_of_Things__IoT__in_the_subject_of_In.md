### Conceptual Framework for the notes of the Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT) in the subject of Internet of Things

1. Internet of Things (IoT): The Internet of Things or IoT refers to the interconnection of everyday physical objects and appliances via the internet. These devices are equipped with sensors, software, and networking capabilities that allow them to send and receive data. IoT enables these devices to be sensed and controlled remotely across existing network infrastructure. This creates opportunities for integration of the physical world into computer-based systems, leading to efficiency improvements, economic gains, and improved human experiences.

Mnemonic: The Internet of Things connects Things to the Internet.

2. IoT Ecosystem: The IoT ecosystem consists of smart devices or things, connectivity, cloud services, and applications.

- Smart devices or things: These include objects like smart home devices, wearables, vehicles, etc. that are equipped with sensors, actuators, and computing capabilities to collect and exchange data.
- Connectivity: This refers to the networking technologies like Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, cellular data, etc. that provide connectivity between the smart devices and the Internet.
- Cloud services: The data collected by the smart devices is sent to cloud platforms for storage, processing, and analysis. The cloud services then send back commands to the devices to perform certain actions.
- Applications: The data insights and commands are delivered to the users through applications on devices like smartphones, tablets, etc. The applications could be for home automation, healthcare, transportation, etc.

3. IoT Architecture: The IoT architecture consists of three main layers - Perception layer, Network layer, and Application layer.

- Perception layer: The perception layer consists of sensors and actuators that collect data from the physical world and trigger actions.
- Network layer: The network layer facilitates connectivity between the perception layer and the application layer. It transmits the data over networks like Wi-Fi, cellular, etc.
- Application layer: The application layer consists of cloud services and applications that store, process, analyze the data and provide interfaces to the users.
