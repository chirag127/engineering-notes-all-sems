# Communication Technologies for IoT

## Wi-Fi

- Wi-Fi is a local area wireless technology that allows devices to connect over wireless local area networks (WLANs)
- Devices connect to a Wi-Fi network by associating with an access point
- Wi-Fi is best suited for short range communications and high bandwidth applications like streaming video or music
- Advantages: ubiquitous, high speed, easy to set up
- Disadvantages: limited range, security concerns, interference from other devices

**Mnemonic:** Think of Wi-Fi as *wireless fidelity* - it's wireless and provides high fidelity Internet connectivity.

## Bluetooth

- Bluetooth is a wireless technology standard for exchanging data over short distances using radio waves
- Bluetooth devices constantly scan for other Bluetooth devices in range and can connect automatically once paired
- Bluetooth is best suited for short range device connectivity and low bandwidth applications like wireless headphones or fitness trackers
- Advantages: low power, widely used, inexpensive
- Disadvantages: limited range, low bandwidth, security vulnerabilities

**Learning trick:** Remember that Bluetooth gets its name from a 10th century Danish king Harald Bluetooth who united Denmark and Norway - similarly, Bluetooth technology connects devices.

## Cellular (2G, 3G, 4G, 5G)

- Cellular networks provide wireless data connectivity using a grid of radio towers/base stations
- 2G, 3G, and 4G LTE are current cellular technologies with different data speeds and latencies
- 5G is the next generation cellular technology that will provide significantly faster speeds (10 to 100 Gbps) and lower latencies
- Cellular is best suited for wide area coverage and higher bandwidth applications but typically more expensive than Wi-Fi or Bluetooth
- Advantages: wide reach, high bandwidth, mobility
- Disadvantages: coverage gaps, speed variability, data caps, cost

**Mnemonic:** 2G is *second generation*, 3G is *third generation* and so on - easy to remember the generational progression of cellular technologies based on the number.
