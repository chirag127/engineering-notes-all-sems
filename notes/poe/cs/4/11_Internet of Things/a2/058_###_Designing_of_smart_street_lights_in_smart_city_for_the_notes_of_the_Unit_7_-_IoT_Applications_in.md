### Designing of smart street lights in smart city for the notes of the Unit 7 - IoT Applications in the subject of Internet of Things

- Smart street lights are IoT enabled street lights that can automatically adjust their brightness based on traffic and pedestrian movement. They have sensors and networking capabilities to communicate with each other and city's smart city platform.
- Key components of a smart street light:
    - LED light source: Energy efficient and long lasting
    - Sensors: Motion sensors to detect vehicle and pedestrian movement, ambient light sensors to detect external light conditions
    - Microcontroller: To control the lights and process data from sensors
    - Network interface: To connect to city's IoT network and communicate with other devices and platform
- Advantages:
    - Energy efficient: Lights up only when needed and adjust brightness based on requirement
    - Remote monitoring: Lights can be monitored and controlled centrally using city's smart city platform
    - Fault detection: Failure of individual lights can be detected automatically for maintenance
- Examples: Smart street lights are being implemented in many smart cities like San Diego, Copenhagen, etc. as part of smart city projects to enhance energy efficiency and safety.
- To learn: Mnemonics - 'SLEDS' can be used to remember key components (Sensors, Lights, microcontroller, network interface, LEDs). Application based examples and diagrams can help in understanding the working and benefits of smart street lights.
