### Ease of designing and affordability for the notes of the Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT) in the subject of Internet of Things

- IoT systems are easy to design and implement as they use simple and inexpensive components like sensors, microcontrollers, communication modules, etc. which are easily available in the market.
- The components required to build an IoT system are affordable as they are manufactured at large scales keeping the costs low.
- Open source hardware boards like Arduino, Raspberry Pi, etc. can be used to prototype and build IoT systems at a low cost.
- IoT protocols like MQTT, CoAP, etc. are lightweight and optimized for low bandwidth and resource constrained devices enabling easy and affordable implementation of IoT systems.
- Cloud platforms provide IoT services with generous free tiers allowing one to build and deploy IoT solutions at zero upfront costs.

**Mnemonics:** EAD - Ease of designing, Affordability of components, Free cloud services

**Learning trick:** Think of an IoT system as a combination of simple and inexpensive components and resources which can be easily put together to build innovative solutions. The ease of designing and affordability makes IoT an exciting field to explore.
