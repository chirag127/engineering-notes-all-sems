### Wireless Medium access issues for the notes of the Unit 4 - Network & Communication aspects in IoT in the subject of Internet of Things

The wireless medium access issues for IoT networks are:

1. Collision: When multiple devices transmit data at the same time, collision occurs which corrupts the data. This decreases the efficiency of the network. Collision avoidance and detection techniques are used to resolve this issue.

2. Interference: The wireless signals from other devices can interfere with the ongoing transmission process. This can be avoided by using different frequency bands for different devices and signal processing techniques.

3. Contention: When multiple devices want to access the medium at the same time, contention occurs. This can be resolved using contention-based protocols like CSMA/CA which make devices wait for a random time before transmission.

4. Bandwidth constraints: The wireless medium has limited bandwidth. Due to the huge number of IoT devices, this can become a bottleneck. Bandwidth allocation and scheduling techniques are required to address this issue.

5. Power constraints: IoT devices have limited power capabilities due to their battery-powered nature. This restricts their transmission range and other capabilities. Power efficient wireless protocols and power saving mechanisms are needed for IoT networks.

**Mnemonics:**

Collisions, Interference, Contention,
Bandwidth and Power constraints are the wireless medium access issues.
CICBP is the mnemonic to remember them.

** Disadvantages: ** unreliable, issues with medium access.
