### Actuators for IoT

Actuators are devices that convert an electrical signal into physical movement. They are used in IoT systems to perform actions based on data from sensors. Some common actuators for IoT applications are:

- motors - convert electrical energy into mechanical rotation or linear movement. Used to control devices like valves, doors, robot arms, etc.
- relays - electrically operated switches used to control high voltage devices using a low voltage signal. Used to turn devices on/off.
- solenoids - convert electrical energy into linear motion. Used to control locks, valves, etc.
- LEDs - convert electrical energy into light. Used for indicators or lighting.

Some key characteristics of actuators:

- Response time - how fast the actuator can respond to a control signal. Important for precision control.
- Accuracy - how accurately the actuator can be positioned or turned on/off. Important for precise control.
- Power requirements - the amount of electrical power required to drive the actuator. Important for selecting appropriate power sources.
- Durability - how long the actuator can last with repeated use. Important for reliability of IoT systems.

**Mnemonics:**

- Motors make things move
- Relays switch things on/off
- Solenoids push and pull
- LEDs light the way

**Advantages:** Allow IoT systems to take action based on data.
**Disadvantages:** Require electrical power and can be complex to control precisely.
**Applications:** Robots, smart home devices, industrial control systems, etc.
