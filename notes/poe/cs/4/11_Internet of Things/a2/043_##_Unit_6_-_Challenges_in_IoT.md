## Unit 6 - Challenges in IoT

The following are some of the major challenges in Internet of Things (IoT):

1. Interoperability: There are many protocols, standards and technologies used in IoT devices and systems. Getting all these devices and systems to interoperate and communicate with each other is a major challenge. Standardization of protocols and APIs is required to solve this challenge.

2. Security: IoT systems involve many devices and end points that capture and exchange sensitive data. This increases the attack surface and risks of cyber attacks like DDoS attacks, eavesdropping, data theft, etc. Implementing stringent security measures like encryption, access control, device authentication, etc. is critical to addressing security challenges in IoT.

3. Scalability: IoT systems involve connecting billions of devices and handling massive amounts of data. Accommodating the exponential growth in devices and data volumes poses a scalability challenge that needs to be addressed through technological advancements in communication, storage and processing capabilities.

4. Heterogeneity: IoT is a heterogeneous system with diverse devices, networks, platforms, applications and technologies. Coping with this diversity and heterogeneity in a seamless manner is a key challenge that requires open system architectures and extensive testing.

5. Privacy: The large-scale data collection and exchange in IoT can lead to serious privacy violations. Implementing privacy-preserving techniques that allow data to be utilized while protecting individuals' privacy is crucial to ensuring public trust in IoT systems.
