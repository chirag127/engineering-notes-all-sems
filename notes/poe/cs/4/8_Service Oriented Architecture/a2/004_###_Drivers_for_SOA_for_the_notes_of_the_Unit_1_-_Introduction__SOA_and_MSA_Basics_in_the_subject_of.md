### Drivers for SOA

The major drivers for Service Oriented Architecture are:

1. Reuse of services: Services once created can be reused by other applications thereby reducing redundancy and development effort.
2. Interoperability: Services use open standards and interfaces allowing heterogeneous systems and applications to interoperate.
3. Agility: The business logic is separated from the application logic thereby allowing flexible adaptation to business changes. New functionalities can be quickly developed and deployed as services.
4. Reduced Cost: Reuse of services and reduced redundancy leads to lower development and maintenance costs.
5. Business-IT alignment: SOA enables business processes and services to be modeled in a technology-neutral manner thereby aligning business and IT.

Some mnemonics or learning tricks for remembering the drivers for SOA:

- RIRAC: Reuse, Interoperability, Reduced Cost, Agility, Alignment (Business-IT)
- SOA drives ROI: Reuse, Interoperability and lower costs lead to higher Returns on Investment with SOA
