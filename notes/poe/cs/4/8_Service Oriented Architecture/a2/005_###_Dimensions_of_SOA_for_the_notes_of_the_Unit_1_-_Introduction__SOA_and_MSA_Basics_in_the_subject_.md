### Dimensions of SOA

The main dimensions of Service Oriented Architecture are:

1. __Granularity__: Refers to the size and complexity of the services. The services can be coarse-grained or fine-grained. Coarse-grained services perform larger and complex tasks whereas fine-grained services perform smaller and atomic tasks. Choosing the right granularity is important as very fine-grained services can lead to latency and very coarse-grained services can lead to inflexibility.

2. __Loose Coupling__: The services are loosely coupled which means that they have minimal dependencies on each other. They can be reused and reconfigured independently without impacting other services. This makes the SOA flexible, resilient and scalable.

3. __Abstraction__: The services abstract the underlying logic and implementation details from the consumers. The interfaces are published which hide the internal complexity of the services. This enables the services to be used without worrying about how they work internally.

4. __Reusability__: The services are designed to be reusable which means that they can be used by multiple applications and consumers. This avoids duplication of efforts and leads to more efficiency.

5. __Composability__: The services can be composed together in different ways to build new applications and solutions. This enables agility and reduces time-to-market.

6. __Autonomy__: The services are autonomous which means that they can be managed, updated, scaled and deployed independently. They have control over their data and logic. This makes the SOA robust, flexible and manageable.

7. __Discoverability__: The services can be discovered at runtime by the consumers. The service registries and directories can be used to locate the services. This makes the SOA dynamic.

8. __Interoperability__: The services are interoperable which means that they can work with each other and exchange data despite differences in platforms, languages, architectures, etc. Standards and protocols are used to enable interoperability. This makes the SOA heterogeneous and integration-friendly.
