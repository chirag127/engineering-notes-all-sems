### Approach for Enterprise-wide SOA Implementation

The following steps can be followed for Enterprise-wide SOA Implementation:

1. Define Business Services: Firstly, the key business services need to be identified that can be exposed as services. This can be done by analyzing the business processes and identifying the core functions that can be service enabled.
2. Define Governance: Strong governance needs to be established to ensure the proper development, management and usage of services. This includes setting up a centralized governance board with representatives from business and IT to review and approve services. Policies and processes need to be defined for service development, documentation, security, monitoring, versioning, etc.
3. Implement Infrastructure: The infrastructure for SOA needs to be implemented, including an Enterprise Service Bus (ESB) to support service communication, service registry to publish and discover services and a security infrastructure to control access to services.
4. Development of Services: The services can then be developed by service-enabling the core business functions. The services should be loosely coupled, platform independent, reusable and secure.
5. Usage of Services: The developed services can be used to build business applications. This allows for faster application development by reusing existing services. The consumption of services can be governed using the SOA governance processes.

Some advantages of an Enterprise-wide SOA Implementation are increased agility, reduced development time and costs, improved interoperability and governance. However, it can be a complex implementation and require organizational change management and significant upfront investment. Care needs to be taken to not create too many fine-grained services which can reduce performance.
