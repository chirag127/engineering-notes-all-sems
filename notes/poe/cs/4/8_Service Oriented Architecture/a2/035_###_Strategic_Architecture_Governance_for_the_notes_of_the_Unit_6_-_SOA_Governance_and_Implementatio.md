### Strategic Architecture Governance

- Strategic Architecture Governance involves defining and enforcing the overall vision and principles for the Enterprise Architecture. It provides oversight and decision-making for the key architectural decisions.
- The key responsibilities of Strategic Architecture Governance are:

1. Define the enterprise architecture vision, principles, and long-term roadmap.
2. Make decisions on standards, technologies, and frameworks.
3. Resolve conflicting requirements and priorities across projects and programs.
4. Oversee budget and resources for architecture initiatives.
5. Evaluate significant deviations from the architecture and provide guidance/approvals.
6. Monitor compliance with architecture principles and standards.

- The Strategic Architecture Governance board typically consists of senior IT and business executives responsible for governance of enterprise architecture.
- The benefits of effective Strategic Architecture Governance are:

1. IT investments and projects are better aligned with business goals.
2. Duplication of efforts is reduced leading to cost savings.
3. Interoperability and integration challenges are minimized.
4. Technical debt and rework are avoided by following standards and best practices.
5. Risks are mitigated by making prudent architecture decisions and avoiding shortcuts.

- To be effective, the Strategic Architecture Governance board needs to have the right composition of membership, sufficient authority, clear processes, and accountability. The board needs to meet regularly to provide leadership and actively participate in key architecture decisions and reviews.
