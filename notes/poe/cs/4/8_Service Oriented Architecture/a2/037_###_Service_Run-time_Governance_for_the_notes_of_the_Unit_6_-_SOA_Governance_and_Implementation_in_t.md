### Service Run-time Governance

- Service run-time governance involves managing and monitoring service performance and availability at run-time.
- It helps ensure that services meet their agreed quality of service (QoS) levels.
- Some important run-time governance activities are:

Monitoring service performance (latency, throughput, availability, etc.)
Enforcing service level agreements (SLAs)
Load balancing between service instances
Failover handling when service instances fail
Logging and auditing service requests and responses
Securing services
Version management when services evolve
Throttling requests to services if load thresholds are exceeded

- Good mnemonics: "MERA VLT" stands for Monitoring, Enforcing SLAs, Request throttling, Auditing, Versioning, Load balancing, Failover handling.
- These run-time governance mechanisms can be implemented using a variety of infrastructure and application monitoring tools, as well as capabilities provided by service delivery platforms.
- Overall, effective run-time governance is critical to ensuring that a SOA delivers business value with the necessary performance, security, and reliability levels.
