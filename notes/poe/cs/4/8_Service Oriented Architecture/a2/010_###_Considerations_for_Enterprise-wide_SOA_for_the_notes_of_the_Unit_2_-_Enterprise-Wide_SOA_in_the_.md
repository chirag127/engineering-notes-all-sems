### Considerations for Enterprise-wide SOA

The following are some key considerations for implementing an Enterprise-wide Service Oriented Architecture:

1. Achieve Business and IT Alignment: The services and processes should be aligned with the business goals and requirements. The SOA implementation should be driven by business needs and not by technology.
2. Take an Incremental Approach: An enterprise-wide SOA should be implemented incrementally by starting small, showing the value of SOA and then expanding slowly. This avoids risks and complexities of a big-bang approach.
3. Use an Integrated, Tool-based Approach: For large scale enterprise SOA, an integrated development environment with tools for service development, discovery, versioning, composition and monitoring should be used. This increases efficiencies and reduces redundancy.
4. Adopt Service-oriented Design and Development Approaches: Services should be designed using standard service-oriented design principles like loose-coupling, reusability, composition etc. This requires proper training for designers and architects.
5. Governance and Management: For the long term success of an enterprise SOA, proper governance and management is essential. This includes managing the service lifecycle, controlling quality, ensuring security and policies adherence, collecting metrics and monitoring, handling exceptions and changes etc.
6. Reduce Complexity and Use Standards: Standards should be used to reduce complexity wherever possible. This includes use of industry standards for service definition, data formats, security and other cross-cutting concerns. This increases interoperability across technologies and vendor products.
7. Consider Performance and Scalability: For enterprise-wide SOA that may have high volumes of service requests and data, performance tuning and scalability should be designed into the architecture. This includes caching, using asynchronous messaging, partitioning, clustering, load balancing etc.

The above are some key considerations to keep in mind when implementing an Enterprise-wide Service Oriented Architecture. A well-designed SOA that considers these factors can deliver significant business agility and IT efficiency for an enterprise.
