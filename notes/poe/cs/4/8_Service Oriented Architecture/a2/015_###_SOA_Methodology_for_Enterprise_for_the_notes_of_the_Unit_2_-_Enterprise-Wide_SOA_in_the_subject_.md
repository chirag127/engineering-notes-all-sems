### SOA Methodology for Enterprise for the notes of the Unit 2 - Enterprise-Wide SOA in the subject of Service Oriented Architecture

Following are the key steps in the SOA Methodology for Enterprise:

1. Identify Business Services: Identify the key business services that are needed based on the business processes and functions. These business services would be the core services that the SOA will provide.
2. Define the Services: Define the services in detail including the inputs, outputs, preconditions and effects. Also define the granularity level of the services. The services should be coarse-grained and not fine-grained.
3. Choose the Service Delivery Platform: Choose the required middleware and networking capabilities to deliver the services. This could include an Enterprise Service Bus (ESB), SOA gateways, messaging systems, etc.
4. Define the Service Contracts: Clearly define the service contracts which include the interfaces, service levels, security levels, governance, etc. The service contracts are crucial for service governance and consumption.
5. Implement the Services: Implement the services and test them to ensure they meet the service contracts. The services can be implemented using various languages and frameworks.
6. Publish the Services: Publish the services through a service registry or repository so that they can be discovered and consumed by service consumers.
7. Consume the Services: The business services are consumed by various service consumers including applications, systems, partners, etc. to automate business processes.
8. Monitor and Manage: Continuously monitor and manage the SOA to ensure service levels are met, optimize services, handle errors, apply governance, accommodate changes, etc.

Some key best practices and tips for the SOA Methodology:

- Start small and grow incrementally. Do not try to implement everything at once.
- Ensure business and IT alignment. Link the services to key business processes and functions.
- Define the right level of granularity for services. Services should be coarse-grained and not fine-grained.
- Standardize the service contracts and interfaces as much as possible. This enables easy connectivity and interoperability.
- Implement governance throughout the lifecycle to manage the SOA.
- Continuously monitor, optimize and accommodate changes to the SOA.
