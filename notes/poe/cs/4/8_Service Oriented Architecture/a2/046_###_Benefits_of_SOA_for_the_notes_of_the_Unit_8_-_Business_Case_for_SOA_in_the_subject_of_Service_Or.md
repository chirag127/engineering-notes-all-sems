### Benefits of SOA

Following are the key benefits of Service Oriented Architecture:

1. Increased Business Agility: SOA allows businesses to respond to changes quickly by reusing services to create new applications and processes. This increased agility and flexibility helps businesses to adapt to changing market conditions and user demands.
2. Reduced Cost: By reusing services and sharing common business logic across applications, SOA can significantly reduce development, maintenance and infrastructure costs.
3. Improved Interoperability: Services in SOA are designed to be interoperable so that they can be integrated easily with other systems and platforms. This interoperability enables businesses to integrate their systems and applications easily and gain a unified view of their data and processes.
4. Standardized Service Interfaces: SOA uses standard and common interfaces to expose business functionality as services. These standard interfaces enable easy integration and reuse of services.
5. Alignment with Business: SOA models business processes and functionality as a set of services which are aligned with the business requirements and functions. This alignment enables businesses to think in terms of business services and not complex software systems.
6. Independent Services: In SOA, services are designed to be independent so that they can be developed, deployed and scaled independently of each other. This independence brings more agility and flexibility to businesses.

Some useful mnemonics to remember the benefits are:

- AGILE: Agility, Growth, Interoperability, Lean, Efficiency
- SAVE$: Save costs, Agile, Vendor independence, Efficiency, $avings

The key benefits of SOA can be remembered easily using these mnemonics. However, these mnemonics may not be intuitive and easy to understand for everyone. So, it is best to understand the actual benefits of increased business agility, reduced cost, improved interoperability, standardized interfaces and alignment with business which I have explained in detail above.
