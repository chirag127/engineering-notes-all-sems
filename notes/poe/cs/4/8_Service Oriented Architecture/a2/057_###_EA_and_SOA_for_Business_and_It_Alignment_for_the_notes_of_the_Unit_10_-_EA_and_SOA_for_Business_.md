### EA and SOA for Business and IT Alignment

For the notes of the Unit 10 - EA and SOA for Business and IT Alignment in the subject of Service Oriented Architecture

- Enterprise Architecture (EA) is a holistic framework used to describe and map the business and IT landscape of an organization. It helps in aligning business and IT strategies by focusing on key principles, models, standards and best practices.
- Service Oriented Architecture (SOA) is a style of software design where applications are built as a set of independent services that can be used within multiple business processes. It aligns business and IT by identifying business processes and implementing them as interoperable services.
- The alignment of EA and SOA can provide the following benefits:
    - Increased agility: SOA's loosely coupled services and EA's standardized models enable quick responses to changing business needs.
    - Improved ROI: Reuse of services and consistent enterprise-wide decisions through EA lead to cost savings and better returns.
    - Better governance: EA provides a holistic view to govern SOA and streamline its development and management. SOA's modular services can be easily governed using EA frameworks.
- To align EA and SOA, the following steps can be taken:
    - Define business goals and key performance indicators (KPIs) to identify business processes and requirements.
    - Model the enterprise landscape using EA frameworks to map processes to capabilities.
    - Identify services and workflows from business processes and implement them in a SOA.
    - Ensure that SOA services comply with EA standards and best practices.
    - Continuously monitor goals and KPIs to optimize the EA and SOA alignment.
