### Strategic Architecture Governance for the notes of the Unit 6 - SOA Governance and Implementation in the subject of Service Oriented Architecture

When it comes to implementing Service Oriented Architecture (SOA), it is crucial to establish a governance framework to ensure the success and sustainability of the architecture. Strategic Architecture Governance is one such framework that helps organizations manage their SOA implementations efficiently.

Here are some key points to understand about Strategic Architecture Governance:

- Strategic Architecture Governance is a framework that helps organizations manage their SOA implementations by providing guidelines, policies, and procedures that ensure the architecture aligns with the organization's strategic goals and objectives.

- The framework includes a set of processes that ensure the SOA implementation is aligned with the organization's business goals, and that the architecture is flexible, scalable, and reusable.

- The Strategic Architecture Governance framework includes several key components, including a governance board, a governance model, and a governance process.

- The governance board is responsible for overseeing the SOA implementation, ensuring that it aligns with the organization's strategic goals and objectives, and making decisions about the architecture's future direction.

- The governance model outlines the roles and responsibilities of the various stakeholders involved in the SOA implementation, including the governance board, the project teams, and the business units.

- The governance process outlines the steps involved in implementing and maintaining the SOA architecture, including the development of policies, procedures, and standards, as well as the monitoring of the architecture's performance and effectiveness.

In addition to these key components, there are several best practices that organizations can follow to ensure the success of their Strategic Architecture Governance framework:

- Clearly define the roles and responsibilities of the various stakeholders involved in the SOA implementation.

- Establish a communication plan to ensure that stakeholders are kept informed about the SOA implementation's progress and status.

- Develop a set of policies, procedures, and standards that govern the development, deployment, and management of the SOA architecture.

- Monitor the performance and effectiveness of the SOA architecture regularly, and make changes as needed to ensure that it remains aligned with the organization's strategic goals and objectives.

Overall, Strategic Architecture Governance is a critical component of any successful SOA implementation. By establishing a framework that aligns the architecture with the organization's strategic goals and objectives, organizations can ensure that their SOA implementations are flexible, scalable, and reusable, and deliver value to the business.
