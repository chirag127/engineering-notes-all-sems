### Nonfunctional Properties for Services

Nonfunctional properties are the characteristics of a service that are not related to the functionality of the service itself, but rather to how the service is delivered. These properties include aspects such as performance, scalability, reliability, availability, security, and maintainability. In this section, we will discuss each of these nonfunctional properties in more detail.

#### Performance

Performance is the measure of how quickly a service can respond to a request. It is important to ensure that the response time of a service meets the requirements of the system. A mnemonic that can be helpful to remember when thinking about performance is "FAST":

- F - Fast
- A - Accurate
- S - Scalable
- T - Timely

#### Scalability

Scalability is the ability of a service to handle increasing amounts of load without compromising its performance. There are two types of scalability - horizontal and vertical. Horizontal scalability involves adding more instances of a service, while vertical scalability involves adding more resources to a single instance of a service. A mnemonic that can be helpful to remember when thinking about scalability is "HISE":

- H - Horizontal
- I - Increase
- S - Scalability
- E - Easily

#### Reliability

Reliability is the measure of how often a service is available and functioning correctly. It is important to ensure that a service is reliable, as it can impact the overall system's reliability. A mnemonic that can be helpful to remember when thinking about reliability is "SURE":

- S - Stable
- U - Uninterrupted
- R - Reliable
- E - Efficient

#### Availability

Availability is the measure of how often a service is accessible and operational. It is important to ensure that a service is available, as it can impact the overall system's availability. A mnemonic that can be helpful to remember when thinking about availability is "ABLE":

- A - Accessible
- B - Backed-up
- L - Load-balanced
- E - Elastic

#### Security

Security is the measure of how well a service is protected against unauthorized access, tampering, or theft. It is important to ensure that a service is secure, as it can impact the overall system's security. A mnemonic that can be helpful to remember when thinking about security is "SAFE":

- S - Secure
- A - Authenticated
- F - Firewalled
- E - Encrypted

#### Maintainability

Maintainability is the measure of how easy it is to update and maintain a service. It is important to ensure that a service is maintainable, as it can impact the overall system's maintainability. A mnemonic that can be helpful to remember when thinking about maintainability is "TEAM":

- T - Testable
- E - Easy
- A - Adaptable
- M - Modular

In conclusion, nonfunctional properties are an important aspect of service-oriented analysis and design. By understanding and considering these properties when designing services, we can ensure that the services are efficient, reliable, secure, and maintainable. Mnemonics can be helpful to remember these properties and their importance in service design.
