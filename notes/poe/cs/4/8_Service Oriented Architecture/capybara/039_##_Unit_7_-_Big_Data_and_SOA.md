## Unit 7 - Big Data and SOA

In this unit, we will learn about Big Data and SOA. Big Data refers to the large sets of data that cannot be processed by traditional data processing tools. SOA, on the other hand, is a software architecture that allows different services to communicate with each other over a network. Let us dive deeper into these topics:

### Big Data

1. Big Data refers to data sets that are too large, complex, and diverse to be processed by traditional data processing tools.
2. Big Data is characterized by the 3Vs - Volume, Velocity, and Variety.
3. Volume refers to the large amount of data that is generated every day. 
4. Velocity refers to the speed at which data is generated, collected, and analyzed. 
5. Variety refers to the different types of data that are generated such as structured, semi-structured, and unstructured data.
6. Some of the popular Big Data technologies are Hadoop, Spark, and NoSQL databases.
7. Big Data has numerous applications such as fraud detection, predictive analytics, and personalized marketing.

### SOA

1. SOA (Service Oriented Architecture) is a software architecture style that allows different services to communicate with each other over a network.
2. In SOA, services are loosely coupled and can be reused by other services.
3. SOA is based on the principles of separation of concerns, modularity, and network-based communication.
4. SOA uses standards-based technologies such as SOAP, REST, and XML.
5. SOA has numerous benefits such as increased flexibility, scalability, and reusability.
6. Some of the popular SOA technologies are Apache Axis, Microsoft WCF, and Oracle SOA Suite.

### Mnemonics

1. To remember the 3Vs of Big Data, you can use the mnemonic "VVV" where V stands for Volume, Velocity, and Variety.
2. To remember the benefits of SOA, you can use the mnemonic "FAR" where F stands for Flexibility, A stands for Agility, and R stands for Reusability.

In conclusion, Big Data and SOA are two important topics in the field of computer science. Understanding these topics can help in developing applications that can handle large amounts of data and communicate effectively with other services.
