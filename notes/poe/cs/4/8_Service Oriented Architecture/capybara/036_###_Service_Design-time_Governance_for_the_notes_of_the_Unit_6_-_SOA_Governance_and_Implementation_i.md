Service Design-time Governance is an essential aspect of the Service Oriented Architecture (SOA) governance and implementation process. It refers to the practice of managing and controlling the design and development of services within an organization. It ensures that services are designed and developed according to the organization's standards, policies, and guidelines. 

Below are some of the key points related to Service Design-time Governance:

- Service Design-time Governance is a proactive approach to govern the design and development of services. It helps to identify potential issues and risks before they occur, and prevent them from happening.

- The primary goal of Service Design-time Governance is to ensure that services are designed and developed to meet the organization's requirements, and are aligned with its objectives.

- Service Design-time Governance involves various activities, including service design review, service modeling, service testing, and service deployment. These activities ensure that services are designed and developed correctly, and meet the organization's quality standards.

- Service Design-time Governance also involves the use of various tools and techniques, such as service design patterns, service-level agreements (SLAs), and service-oriented architecture principles. These tools and techniques help to ensure that services are designed and developed in a consistent and standardized manner.
