### Enterprise Architecture for the notes of the Unit 10 - EA and SOA for Business and IT Alignment in the subject of Service Oriented Architecture

Enterprise Architecture (EA) is a strategic approach to aligning business and IT goals and objectives. It involves the creation and maintenance of a comprehensive view of an organization's structure, processes, information, and technology. The following points are important to understand Enterprise Architecture:

1. EA helps in identifying the gaps between the current IT infrastructure and business requirements.
2. EA determines the common business processes and information flows across an organization.
3. EA ensures that IT investments are aligned with business objectives.
4. EA provides a framework for managing complexity and change within an organization.
5. EA promotes standardization and reuse of IT assets.
6. EA is composed of four domains: Business, Data, Application, and Technology.

#### Mnemonics and Learning Tricks for Enterprise Architecture:

1. A good way to remember the four domains of EA is to use the acronym "BDAT" (Business, Data, Application, Technology).
2. Another helpful trick is to think of EA as a blueprint for building a house. Just as a blueprint outlines a structure's design and components, EA provides a comprehensive view of an organization's structure, processes, information, and technology.

In conclusion, Enterprise Architecture is a strategic approach that aligns business and IT goals and objectives. It ensures that IT investments are aligned with business objectives and provides a framework for managing complexity and change within an organization. Remembering the four domains of EA (BDAT) and thinking of it as a blueprint can help in understanding and applying this concept.
