### Stakeholder Objectives for the notes of the Unit 8 - Business Case for SOA in the subject of Service Oriented Architecture

In Service Oriented Architecture (SOA), stakeholders play a significant role in the success of the project. Stakeholders are individuals or groups who are interested in the outcome of the project, and they may have different objectives that they want to achieve through the project. Therefore, it is essential to identify the stakeholders and define their objectives to ensure that the project meets their expectations. In this section, we will discuss the stakeholder objectives in the Unit 8 - Business Case for SOA.

#### Stakeholders in SOA

Stakeholders in SOA can be categorized into three types:

1. Business stakeholders: These are individuals or groups who are interested in the business outcomes of the project. They may include the CEO, CFO, business analysts, and project sponsors.

2. IT stakeholders: These are individuals or groups who are interested in the technical outcomes of the project. They may include the CTO, system architects, developers, and IT managers.

3. End-users: These are individuals or groups who will use the system or application. They may include customers, employees, and partners.

#### Stakeholder Objectives

The following are the stakeholder objectives for the Unit 8 - Business Case for SOA:

1. Business stakeholders:

- Cost reduction: Business stakeholders want to reduce costs associated with IT projects. They want to see a positive return on investment (ROI) and increase their profitability.
- Improved business agility: Business stakeholders want to respond quickly to changing market conditions and customer needs. They want to be able to adapt their business processes and systems to meet these demands.
- Improved customer satisfaction: Business stakeholders want to improve customer satisfaction by providing better services and products. They want to achieve customer loyalty and retention.

2. IT stakeholders:

- Improved system integration: IT stakeholders want to integrate different systems and applications to provide a seamless experience for end-users. They want to ensure that the system is interoperable and scalable.
- Reduced maintenance costs: IT stakeholders want to reduce maintenance costs associated with the system. They want to ensure that the system is reliable and easy to maintain.
- Improved security: IT stakeholders want to ensure that the system is secure and protected from external threats. They want to ensure data privacy and confidentiality.

3. End-users:

- Improved user experience: End-users want to have a positive experience while using the system. They want the system to be easy to use, intuitive, and responsive.
- Access to real-time data: End-users want to have access to real-time data that is relevant to their job. They want to be able to make informed decisions based on this data.
- Improved productivity: End-users want to be able to complete their tasks more efficiently and effectively. They want the system to automate repetitive tasks and provide them with the necessary tools to perform their job.

#### Conclusion

In SOA, stakeholders play a critical role in the success of the project. Their objectives are different, and it is essential to identify them to ensure that the project meets their expectations. By understanding the stakeholder objectives, you can design and implement a system that meets their needs and achieves the project goals.
