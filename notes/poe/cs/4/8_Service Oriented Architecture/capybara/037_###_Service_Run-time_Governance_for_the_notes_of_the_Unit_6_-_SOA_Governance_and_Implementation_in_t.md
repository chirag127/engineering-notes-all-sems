### Service Run-time Governance

Service Run-time Governance is an important aspect of Service Oriented Architecture (SOA) Governance and Implementation. It involves managing the behavior and performance of the services during runtime. The main objective of Service Run-time Governance is to ensure that the services are performing as per the Service Level Agreements (SLAs) and are meeting the business requirements.

#### Importance of Service Run-time Governance

Service Run-time Governance is important for the following reasons:

- To ensure that the services are performing as per the SLAs and are meeting the business requirements.
- To monitor the service performance and behavior during runtime and take corrective actions if required.
- To enforce security policies and regulations to prevent unauthorized access to the services.
- To manage the service lifecycle and ensure that the services are available and reliable.

#### Service Run-time Governance Framework

Service Run-time Governance Framework includes the following components:

1. Monitoring and Management: This component involves monitoring the service performance and behavior during runtime and taking corrective actions if required. It also involves managing the service lifecycle and ensuring that the services are available and reliable.

2. Security and Compliance: This component involves enforcing security policies and regulations to prevent unauthorized access to the services. It also involves ensuring compliance with the regulatory and legal requirements.

3. Service Level Management: This component involves managing the SLAs and ensuring that the services are performing as per the SLAs. It also involves monitoring the service performance and behavior during runtime and taking corrective actions if required.
