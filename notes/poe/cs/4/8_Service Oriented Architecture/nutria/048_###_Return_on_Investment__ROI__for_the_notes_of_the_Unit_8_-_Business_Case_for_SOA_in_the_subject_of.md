
### Return on Investment (ROI) for the notes of the Unit 8 - Business Case for SOA in the subject of Service Oriented Architecture

* Return on Investment (ROI) is a measure of the financial performance of a Service Oriented Architecture (SOA) project. It is calculated by dividing the total cost of the project by the total benefits it generates.
* The main benefits of SOA include increased efficiency, scalability, and agility.
* To calculate the ROI of a SOA project, the total cost of the project must be estimated. This includes the cost of the software, hardware, and any other associated costs.
* The total benefits of a SOA project should also be estimated. This includes any cost savings, increased revenue, or other benefits that the project may generate.
* The ROI of a SOA project should be compared to other projects to determine if it is a worthwhile investment.
* Mnemonics and learning tricks to help remember the ROI of a SOA project include:
  * R - Return on Investment
  * O - Opportunity Cost
  * I - Investment Benefits
