
### Design of Activity Services (or Business Services) for the notes of the Unit 4 - Service-Oriented Analysis and Design in the subject of Service Oriented Architecture

Activity services (or business services) are the services that enable the organization to achieve its business goals. They are the services that are used to enable the organization to implement its business processes.

Activity services are designed to enable the organization to achieve its goals by providing the necessary functionality and data that is needed to execute the business process.

Activity services are typically designed around the concept of a service contract. A service contract defines the interface between the service and the client. It defines the operations that the service can perform, the data that it can access, and the format of the data it can return.

Activity services can also be designed to be extensible. This allows the service to be extended to provide additional functionality or data without changing the existing service.

Activity services are typically designed to be secure, reliable, and scalable. Security is important to ensure that only authorized users can access the service and that the data is protected from unauthorized access. Reliability is important to ensure that the service is available when needed and that it can handle large volumes of data. Scalability is important to ensure that the service can scale up to meet the needs of the organization.

2. **A**ctivity **S**ervices **P**rovide **F**unctionality and **D**ata **A**ccess **T**o **E**xecute **B**usiness **P**rocesses.
3. **A**ctivity **S**ervices are **D**esigned **A**round **S**ervice **C**ontracts.
4. **A**ctivity **S**ervices are **E**xtensible, **S**ecure, **R**eliable and **S**calable.
