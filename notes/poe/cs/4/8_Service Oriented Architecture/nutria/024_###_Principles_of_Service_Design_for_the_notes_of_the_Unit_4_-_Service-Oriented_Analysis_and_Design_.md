
### Principles of Service Design for the notes of the Unit 4 - Service-Oriented Analysis and Design in the subject of Service Oriented Architecture

1. **Service Abstraction**: This principle states that the services should be designed in such a way that they are abstracted from the underlying implementation. This helps to make the services more maintainable, reusable and extensible.

2. **Service Reusability**: This principle states that services should be designed to be reusable and extensible. This helps to reduce the cost of development and maintenance.

3. **Service Autonomy**: This principle states that services should be designed to be independent and autonomous. This helps to reduce the complexity of the system and makes it easier to maintain.

4. **Service Composability**: This principle states that services should be designed to be composable. This helps to reduce the complexity of the system and makes it easier to maintain.

5. **Service Statelessness**: This principle states that services should be designed to be stateless. This helps to reduce the complexity of the system and makes it easier to maintain.

6. **Service Loose Coupling**: This principle states that services should be designed to be loosely coupled. This helps to reduce the complexity of the system and makes it easier to maintain.

7. **Service Interface Standardization**: This principle states that services should be designed to have a standard interface. This helps to reduce the complexity of the system and makes it easier to maintain.

8. **Service Reliability**: This principle states that services should be designed to be reliable. This helps to reduce the complexity of the system and makes it easier to maintain.

9. **Service Security**: This principle states that services should be designed to be secure. This helps to reduce the complexity of the system and makes it easier to maintain.

10. **Service Performance**: This principle states that services should be designed to have high performance. This helps to reduce the complexity of the system and makes it easier to maintain.
