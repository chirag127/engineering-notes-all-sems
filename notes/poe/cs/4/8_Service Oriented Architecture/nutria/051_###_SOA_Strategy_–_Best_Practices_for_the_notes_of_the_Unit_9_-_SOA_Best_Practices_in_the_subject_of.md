
### SOA Strategy – Best Practices for the notes of the Unit 9 - SOA Best Practices in the subject of Service Oriented Architecture

1. Service-oriented architecture (SOA) is an architectural style that enables organizations to integrate different applications and services, regardless of their underlying technology. 
2. SOA encourages the use of loosely coupled services that can be reused and combined in different ways to meet the needs of the organization. 
3. SOA best practices include the use of standards-based technologies such as web services, XML and service-oriented messaging to build interoperable services. 
4. SOA also requires the use of enterprise service buses (ESBs) to provide the infrastructure for service-oriented communication and orchestration. 
5. SOA best practices also include the use of service registries to enable service discovery, and the use of service-level agreements (SLAs) to ensure service performance. 
6. Finally, SOA best practices emphasize the importance of governance to ensure that services are developed and managed in a consistent and reliable manner. 
7. A good way to remember the best practices of SOA is to think of the acronym “S.O.A.P.” which stands for Standards, Orchestration, Agreement, and Performance.
