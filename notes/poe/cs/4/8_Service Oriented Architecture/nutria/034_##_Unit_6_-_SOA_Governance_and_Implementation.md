
## Unit 6 - SOA Governance and Implementation

* Service-Oriented Architecture (SOA) Governance is a set of processes and principles that help organizations create, maintain and optimize their SOA-based solutions.
* SOA Governance focuses on creating a framework to ensure that the services are consistent and compliant with the organization's standards. It also helps to ensure that the services are secure, reliable and efficient.
* SOA Governance includes processes such as service design, service implementation, service testing, service deployment and service management.
* SOA Governance also includes principles such as service reuse, service integration, service scalability, service availability, service security, service reliability and service performance.
* SOA Governance is essential for organizations to ensure that their SOA-based solutions are compliant with the organization's standards and are secure, reliable and efficient.
* SOA Implementation is the process of deploying and managing SOA-based solutions. It involves the creation of services, the deployment of services and the management of services.
* SOA Implementation includes activities such as service design, service deployment, service testing, service monitoring and service management.
* SOA Implementation also includes principles such as service reuse, service integration, service scalability, service availability, service security, service reliability and service performance.
* Good mnemonics and learning tricks for SOA Governance and Implementation include:
  * SOA G: Service Design, Implementation, Testing, Deployment, Management
  * SOA I: Design, Deployment, Testing, Monitoring, Management
  * SOA S: Reuse, Integration, Scalability, Availability, Security, Reliability, Performance
