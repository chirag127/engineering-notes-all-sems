
### Dimensions of SOA for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction: SOA and MSA Basics in the subject of Service Oriented Architecture

The dimensions of Service Oriented Architecture (SOA) are often used to describe the various aspects of a service-oriented system. The dimensions of SOA include:

* **Functionality:** This dimension of SOA describes the capabilities of the services and how they can be used to solve business problems.

* **Data:** This dimension of SOA describes the data that is exchanged between services and how it is used to support the functionality of the services.

* **Interoperability:** This dimension of SOA describes how different services can interact with each other and how the data exchanged between them is understood.

* **Organization:** This dimension of SOA describes how services are organized and managed. It also describes how services are discovered and invoked.

* **Technology:** This dimension of SOA describes the technology used to implement services, such as the protocols, standards, and tools used.

* **Governance:** This dimension of SOA describes the policies, processes, and procedures that are used to ensure that services are deployed and managed in a consistent and secure manner.

Mnemonics and Learning Tricks:

To help remember the dimensions of SOA, you can use the mnemonic “FDIOTG”, which stands for Functionality, Data, Interoperability, Organization, Technology, and Governance.
