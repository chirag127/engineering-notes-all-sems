

### Drivers for SOA for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction: SOA and MSA Basics in the subject of Service Oriented Architecture

1. **Cost Reduction:** One of the primary drivers for SOA is cost reduction. By using a service-oriented approach, organizations can reduce the cost of development, maintenance, and deployment of software applications.

2. **Increased Agility:** SOA provides organizations with increased agility. By breaking down applications into smaller, more manageable services, organizations can quickly and easily develop and deploy new applications.

3. **Improved Quality:** SOA can help improve the quality of applications by making them more robust and reliable. By breaking down applications into smaller, more manageable services, organizations can ensure that each service is properly tested and maintained.

4. **Standardization:** SOA promotes the use of standards for communication between services. This helps to ensure that services are interoperable, allowing for the reuse of services across multiple applications.

5. **Reusability:** SOA promotes the reuse of services across multiple applications. By breaking down applications into smaller, more manageable services, organizations can ensure that services can be reused across multiple applications.

6. **Mnemonic:** SOA stands for Service-Oriented Architecture.
