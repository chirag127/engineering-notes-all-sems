
### Stakeholder Objectives for the notes of the Unit 8 - Business Case for SOA in the subject of Service Oriented Architecture

- Stakeholder objectives are the goals and objectives of the stakeholders involved in the Service Oriented Architecture (SOA).
- Stakeholders can include customers, suppliers, partners, employees, and other entities that have an interest in the success of the SOA.
- Stakeholder objectives should be clearly defined and communicated to all parties involved in the SOA.
- Stakeholder objectives should be measurable and achievable.
- Stakeholder objectives should be aligned with the overall objectives of the organization.
- Stakeholder objectives should be monitored and reported on a regular basis.
- Stakeholder objectives should be flexible and able to be adjusted as needed.
- Stakeholder objectives should be prioritized in order to ensure that the most important objectives are met first.
- Stakeholder objectives should be supported by the right resources and tools.
- Stakeholder objectives should be reviewed and updated regularly.
- Stakeholder objectives should be communicated to all stakeholders in a timely manner.
- Stakeholder objectives should be documented and tracked to ensure that they are being met.

A helpful mnemonic to remember these objectives is "CAMPFIRE":

- C: Clearly defined
- A: Aligned
- M: Measurable
- P: Prioritized
- F: Flexible
- I: Informed
- R: Reviewed
- E: Evaluated
