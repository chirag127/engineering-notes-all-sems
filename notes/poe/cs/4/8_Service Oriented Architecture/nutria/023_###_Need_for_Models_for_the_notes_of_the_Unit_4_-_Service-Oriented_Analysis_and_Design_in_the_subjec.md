
### Need for Models for the Notes of the Unit 4 - Service-Oriented Analysis and Design in the Subject of Service Oriented Architecture

- Service-Oriented Analysis and Design (SOAD) is an important part of Service Oriented Architecture (SOA).
- SOAD is a set of techniques and processes used to identify, analyze and design services that are used to build distributed applications.
- SOAD models are used to visualize the service requirements and design, and to identify the components of the service.
- SOAD models can be used to identify the service boundaries, the service components and the service interactions.
- SOAD models can also be used to identify the service dependencies, the service performance requirements, the service security requirements and the service scalability requirements.
- SOAD models are also used to identify the service quality requirements and the service availability requirements.
- SOAD models can also be used to identify the service architecture and the service implementation details.
- SOAD models are also used to identify the service deployment requirements and the service management requirements.
- SOAD models can also be used to identify the service testing requirements and the service monitoring requirements.
- SOAD models are also used to identify the service versioning requirements and the service lifecycle requirements.
- SOAD models are used to ensure that the services are designed and implemented in a way that meets the service requirements, and that the services are deployed and managed in a way that meets the service requirements.
