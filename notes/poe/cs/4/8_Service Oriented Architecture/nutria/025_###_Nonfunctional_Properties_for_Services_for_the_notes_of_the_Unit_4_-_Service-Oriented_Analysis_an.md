

### Nonfunctional Properties for Services for the notes of the Unit 4 - Service-Oriented Analysis and Design in the subject of Service Oriented Architecture

Nonfunctional properties of services are the characteristics of a service that can be measured, evaluated, and improved. These properties are also known as quality attributes. They are critical to the success of a service and can have a significant impact on the user experience.

The key nonfunctional properties of services are:

1. **Availability:** The ability of a service to be available when needed. This includes the ability to respond to requests in a timely manner.

2. **Reliability:** The ability of a service to perform its intended function consistently and accurately.

3. **Maintainability:** The ability of a service to be maintained in a cost-effective manner. This includes the ability to modify, upgrade, and fix the service.

4. **Scalability:** The ability of a service to scale up or down in response to changing demand.

5. **Security:** The ability of a service to protect data and resources from unauthorized access.

6. **Performance:** The ability of a service to meet its performance requirements. This includes response time, throughput, and resource utilization.

7. **Usability:** The ability of a service to be easy to use and understand.

8. **Testability:** The ability of a service to be tested quickly and accurately.

9. **Flexibility:** The ability of a service to adapt to changing requirements and environments.

10. **Portability:** The ability of a service to be deployed in different environments.
