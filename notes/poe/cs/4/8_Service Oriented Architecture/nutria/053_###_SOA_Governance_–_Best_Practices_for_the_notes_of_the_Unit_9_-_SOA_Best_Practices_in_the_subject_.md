
### SOA Governance – Best Practices for the notes of the Unit 9 - SOA Best Practices in the subject of Service Oriented Architecture

1. Establish Service Level Agreements (SLAs): SLAs are agreements between service providers and consumers that define the quality of service expected from the provider. SLAs should be established before any service is deployed to ensure that service providers and consumers are aware of their respective responsibilities.

2. Monitor Service Performance: Service performance should be monitored to ensure that service level agreements are being met. This can be done by collecting performance metrics such as response time and availability.

3. Establish Service Lifecycle Management: Service lifecycle management is the process of managing the entire lifecycle of a service, from its conception to its retirement. This includes activities such as service design, development, testing, deployment, and retirement.

4. Define Service Standards: Service standards are guidelines that define the quality of service expected from service providers. These standards should be established before any service is deployed to ensure that service providers are aware of their responsibilities.

5. Implement Service Security: Security is an important aspect of service governance. Service providers should implement security measures such as authentication, authorization, encryption, and auditing to ensure that services are secure.

6. Implement Service Versioning: Service versioning is the process of maintaining multiple versions of a service. This is important for ensuring that services are up to date and that service consumers are able to access the most recent version of a service.

7. Establish Service Policies: Service policies are rules and regulations that govern the use of services. These policies should be established before any service is deployed to ensure that service providers and consumers are aware of their respective responsibilities.

8. Implement Service Quality Assurance: Quality assurance is the process of ensuring that services meet the required quality standards. This can be done by testing services before they are deployed and by monitoring service performance.

9. Establish Service Catalog: A service catalog is a list of services that are available to service consumers. This catalog should include information such as service name, description, version, and cost.

10. Mnemonics and learning tricks: To help remember the best practices for SOA governance, try using the acronym SLAM-SDC-SQA-SCP-SV. This stands for Service Level Agreements, Monitoring, Service Lifecycle Management, Service Standards, Service Security, Service Versioning, Service Quality Assurance and Service Catalog.
