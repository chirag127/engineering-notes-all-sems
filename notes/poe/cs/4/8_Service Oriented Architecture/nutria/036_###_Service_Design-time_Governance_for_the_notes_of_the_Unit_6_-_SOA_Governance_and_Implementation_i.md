

### Service Design-time Governance for the notes of the Unit 6 - SOA Governance and Implementation in the subject of Service Oriented Architecture

Service Design-time Governance is a process that involves the management and enforcement of policies and standards related to the design and development of services. It is an important part of Service Oriented Architecture (SOA), as it helps to ensure that services are designed, developed, and deployed in a consistent and reliable manner.

**Mnemonic:** SOA GDI (Service Oriented Architecture Governance Design-time Implementation)

The main goals of Service Design-time Governance are to:

1. Establish and enforce standards for service design and development.
2. Ensure that services are designed and developed in a consistent manner.
3. Ensure that services are secure, reliable, and scalable.
4. Ensure that services are compliant with applicable laws and regulations.
5. Ensure that services are efficient and cost-effective.

The process of Service Design-time Governance involves the following steps:

1. Establish policies and standards for service design and development.
2. Develop a governance framework to ensure compliance with these policies and standards.
3. Monitor and enforce the policies and standards.
4. Review and update the policies and standards as needed.

The process of Service Design-time Governance must be tailored to the specific needs of the organization. It should take into account the organization's existing processes, tools, and technologies, as well as its specific business goals and objectives.

In addition, Service Design-time Governance must be closely aligned with the organization's overall SOA strategy. This will ensure that services are designed and developed in line with the organization's overall goals and objectives.

Finally, Service Design-time Governance should be closely integrated with the organization's overall IT governance processes. This will ensure that services are properly managed and maintained throughout their lifecycle.
