

### Strategic Architecture Governance for the notes of the Unit 6 - SOA Governance and Implementation in the subject of Service Oriented Architecture

- Service Oriented Architecture (SOA) is an architectural style that allows for the creation of services that are loosely coupled, meaning they can be consumed and reused without being heavily dependent on each other.
- SOA Governance is the process of managing and controlling the development and operations of services within an SOA environment.
- The purpose of SOA Governance is to ensure that services are developed and deployed in a consistent and secure manner, that they meet the requirements of the organization, and that they are managed in accordance with the organization's policies and standards.
- SOA Governance should include the following components: 
    - Service Design: This includes the design of services, service contracts, and service interfaces.
    - Service Development: This includes the development of services, service contracts, and service interfaces.
    - Service Deployment: This includes the deployment of services, service contracts, and service interfaces.
    - Service Operations: This includes the management and monitoring of services, service contracts, and service interfaces.
    - Service Security: This includes the implementation of security policies and procedures to ensure the security of services.
- The implementation of SOA Governance should include the following steps: 
    - Define the scope of governance: This includes the scope of services, the scope of the organization, and the scope of the governance process.
    - Define the goals of governance: This includes the goals of the organization and the goals of the governance process.
    - Define the roles and responsibilities of governance: This includes the roles and responsibilities of the organization and the roles and responsibilities of the governance process.
    - Define the process of governance: This includes the process of developing, deploying, and managing services, as well as the process of monitoring and enforcing the governance process.
    - Define the tools of governance: This includes the tools used to develop, deploy, and manage services, as well as the tools used to monitor and enforce the governance process.
- Mnemonics:
    - SOA Governance: Secure, Organize, Achieve.
    - Scope of Governance: Services, Organization, Process.
    - Goals of Governance: Organization, Process.
    - Roles and Responsibilities: Organization, Process.
    - Process of Governance: Develop, Deploy, Manage, Monitor, Enforce.
    - Tools of Governance: Develop, Deploy, Manage, Monitor, Enforce.
