
## Unit 8 - Business Case for SOA

* Service-Oriented Architecture (SOA) is a software design approach that enables organizations to build flexible, modular applications that can be easily integrated with other systems.
* SOA is based on the concept of a service, which is a discrete, reusable unit of functionality that can be accessed over a network. Services are typically self-contained and can be composed into larger applications.
* The benefits of SOA include increased agility, reduced development costs, and improved scalability.
* SOA also allows for better integration with other systems, allowing for the creation of more powerful applications.
* SOA can be used in a variety of contexts, from enterprise applications to web services.
* Mnemonics for SOA:
  * **S**ervice-oriented architecture 
  * **O**pen standards 
  * **A**dvanced integration 
* Advantages of SOA:
  * Increased flexibility and scalability 
  * Reduced development costs 
  * Improved integration with other systems 
  * Easier maintenance and upgrades 
* Disadvantages of SOA:
  * High initial setup costs 
  * Complexity of implementation 
  * Security concerns 
  * Difficulty of testing
