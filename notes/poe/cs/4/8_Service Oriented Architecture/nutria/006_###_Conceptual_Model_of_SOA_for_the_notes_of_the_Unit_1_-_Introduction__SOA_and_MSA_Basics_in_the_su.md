
### Conceptual Model of SOA for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction: SOA and MSA Basics in the subject of Service Oriented Architecture

* Service-oriented architecture (SOA) is an architectural style that allows different components of a system to communicate with each other in a loosely coupled fashion. 
* SOA enables applications to be developed and deployed independently of each other, while still being able to interact with one another. 
* SOA is based on the idea of creating small, reusable services that can be composed together to build larger applications. 
* Each service is self-contained and encapsulates a single business capability. 
* Services communicate with each other through a standardized interface and can be implemented using different technologies. 
* SOA enables organizations to develop applications quickly and easily, and makes it easier to maintain and modify existing applications.

* Microservices architecture (MSA) is an evolution of SOA. It is a distributed architecture that follows the same principles as SOA, but with a focus on creating small, independent services. 
* MSA services are typically much smaller than SOA services, and are designed to be independently deployed, scaled, and maintained. 
* MSA services can be implemented using different technologies and can be deployed on different platforms. 
* MSA enables organizations to develop and deploy applications quickly and easily, and makes it easier to maintain and modify existing applications. 
* MSA also makes it easier to scale applications as the demand for them increases.

Mnemonics and Learning Tricks: 
* SOA: Service-Oriented Architecture 
* MSA: Microservices Architecture
