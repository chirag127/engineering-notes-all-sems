
## Unit 10 - EA and SOA for Business and IT Alignment

* Enterprise Architecture (EA) is a framework that helps organizations align their business and IT strategies. It is a holistic approach to understanding how an organization's systems, processes, and people can be used to achieve its goals.

* Service-Oriented Architecture (SOA) is an architectural style that enables organizations to create, manage, and deploy services to meet their business needs. SOA allows organizations to build applications from reusable services that are independent of the underlying technology and platform.

* EA and SOA are closely related and can be used together to achieve better business and IT alignment. EA helps organizations understand their current state and develop a roadmap to achieve their desired future state. SOA helps organizations create, manage, and deploy services that can be used to implement the roadmap.

* EA and SOA can be used in combination to create a more efficient and agile organization. By understanding the current state of the organization and mapping out a roadmap to the desired future state, EA can help organizations create a plan for achieving their goals. SOA can then be used to create and deploy services that will enable the organization to reach those goals.

* There are several benefits to using EA and SOA together. By combining the two approaches, organizations can reduce costs, increase agility, and improve the quality of their services. Additionally, EA and SOA can help organizations create a more unified approach to their business and IT strategies, leading to better alignment and increased efficiency.
