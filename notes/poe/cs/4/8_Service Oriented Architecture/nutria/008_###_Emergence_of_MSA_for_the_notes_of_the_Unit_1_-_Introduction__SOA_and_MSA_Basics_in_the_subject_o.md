
### Emergence of MSA for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction: SOA and MSA Basics in the subject of Service Oriented Architecture

- Microservice Architecture (MSA) is an architectural style that structures an application as a collection of loosely coupled services.
- MSA is an evolution of Service Oriented Architecture (SOA) which emphasizes the use of services to build distributed and loosely coupled applications.
- MSA allows for the development of complex applications that are composed of small, independent services. Each service is responsible for a single, well-defined task and can be deployed and scaled independently.
- MSA offers many advantages over SOA such as: improved scalability, more efficient resource utilization, faster development cycles, better fault isolation, and increased flexibility.
- MSA is suitable for applications that require high availability and scalability, as well as applications that require rapid development cycles.
- Some of the key components of MSA include: service discovery, service composition, service orchestration, and service monitoring.
- MSA is becoming increasingly popular in the software engineering world and is being adopted by many organizations as the preferred architecture for their applications.
