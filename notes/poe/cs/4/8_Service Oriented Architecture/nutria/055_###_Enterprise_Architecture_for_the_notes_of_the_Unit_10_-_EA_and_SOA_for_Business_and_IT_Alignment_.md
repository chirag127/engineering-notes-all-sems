
### Enterprise Architecture for the notes of the Unit 10 - EA and SOA for Business and IT Alignment in the subject of Service Oriented Architecture

* Enterprise Architecture (EA) is the process of designing, planning, and managing the enterprise's IT infrastructure and applications. It is a framework for understanding the current and future state of the enterprise, and for aligning IT investments with the business objectives.

* Service Oriented Architecture (SOA) is a design pattern that enables the enterprise to develop, deploy, and manage services across different applications and systems. It is used to create loosely coupled services that can be easily scaled and reused.

* EA and SOA are closely related and are often used together to create an integrated solution for the enterprise. EA helps to define the structure and processes for the enterprise, while SOA provides the tools to implement and manage those processes.

* The main advantage of using EA and SOA together is that it enables the enterprise to create a flexible, scalable, and cost-effective IT architecture. This architecture can be quickly adapted to changing business needs, and can be used to create innovative solutions.

* Another advantage of using EA and SOA is that it helps to reduce the complexity of the enterprise's IT environment. By using a common set of services, the enterprise can reduce the number of systems and applications that need to be managed. This simplifies the IT infrastructure and reduces costs.

* One of the most important aspects of EA and SOA is the ability to create reusable services. By creating services that can be shared across different applications, the enterprise can reduce the amount of time and money spent on developing new services.

* Finally, EA and SOA can help the enterprise to create an integrated solution for the enterprise. By creating a unified architecture, the enterprise can ensure that all of its systems and applications are working together, and that the enterprise can take advantage of the best available technology.
