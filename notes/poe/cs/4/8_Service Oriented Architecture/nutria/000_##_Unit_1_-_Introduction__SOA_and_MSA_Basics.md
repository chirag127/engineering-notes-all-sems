
## Unit 1 - Introduction: SOA and MSA Basics

* SOA (Service Oriented Architecture): 
  * SOA is a software architecture style that enables the creation of applications by combining and connecting different services.
  * It allows for the reuse of existing services and the creation of new services that can be used by other applications.
  * SOA is based on the idea of software components communicating with each other through a well-defined interface.
  * Advantages of SOA include:
    * Increased flexibility and agility
    * Reduced development time and cost
    * Improved scalability
    * Improved reliability

* MSA (Microservice Architecture):
  * MSA is a type of SOA that focuses on creating small, independent services that can be deployed and managed independently.
  * MSA is based on the idea of breaking down large applications into smaller, more manageable services.
  * Advantages of MSA include:
    * Increased scalability
    * Improved reliability
    * Reduced development time and cost
    * Improved agility
    * Improved maintainability
