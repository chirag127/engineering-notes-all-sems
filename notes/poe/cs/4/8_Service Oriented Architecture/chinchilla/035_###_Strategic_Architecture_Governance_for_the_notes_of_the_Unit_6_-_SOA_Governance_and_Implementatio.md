### Strategic Architecture Governance for the notes of the Unit 6 - SOA Governance and Implementation in the subject of Service Oriented Architecture

In the context of Service Oriented Architecture (SOA), Strategic Architecture Governance refers to the process of managing and controlling the design, development, and deployment of SOA solutions. It involves the establishment of policies, standards, and procedures that ensure the alignment of SOA solutions with the organization's overall business objectives and IT strategies. The following are some key points to consider when it comes to Strategic Architecture Governance for SOA:

1. **Establish clear goals and objectives**: The first step in Strategic Architecture Governance is to establish clear goals and objectives for SOA solutions. This involves understanding the business requirements and IT strategies of the organization and defining the scope and vision for the SOA solutions.

2. **Define governance policies and standards**: Once the goals and objectives are established, it is important to define governance policies and standards that guide the development and deployment of SOA solutions. These policies and standards should cover areas such as service design, service development, service testing, service deployment, service management, and service security.

3. **Establish a governance framework**: A governance framework is a set of processes, roles, and responsibilities that facilitate the implementation of governance policies and standards. The governance framework should define the roles and responsibilities of the governance board, the SOA architects, the service owners, and other stakeholders involved in the development and deployment of SOA solutions.

4. **Implement governance processes**: Governance processes are the activities that enable the implementation of governance policies and standards. These processes may include service lifecycle management, service portfolio management, service catalog management, service level management, and service monitoring and reporting.

5. **Monitor and measure performance**: Monitoring and measuring the performance of SOA solutions is critical to ensuring that they meet the business requirements and IT strategies of the organization. This involves tracking key performance indicators (KPIs) such as service availability, service performance, service usage, and service satisfaction.

6. **Continuous improvement**: Continuous improvement is an important aspect of Strategic Architecture Governance for SOA. It involves reviewing and updating governance policies and standards, governance processes, and the governance framework based on feedback and lessons learned from the development and deployment of SOA solutions.

Mnemonics and learning tricks:

1. **GOSPIM**: This can be a useful mnemonic for remembering the key components of Strategic Architecture Governance for SOA - Goals and Objectives, Governance Policies and Standards, Governance Framework, Governance Processes, Performance Monitoring, and Continuous Improvement.

2. **Plan, Define, Implement, Monitor, Improve (PDIMI)**: This is a helpful acronym for remembering the five phases of the governance lifecycle - Plan, Define, Implement, Monitor, and Improve.
