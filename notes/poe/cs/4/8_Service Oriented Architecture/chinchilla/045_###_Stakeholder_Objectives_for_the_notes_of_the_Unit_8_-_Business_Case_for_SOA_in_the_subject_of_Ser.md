### Stakeholder Objectives for the notes of the Unit 8 - Business Case for SOA in the subject of Service Oriented Architecture

In the field of Service Oriented Architecture (SOA), one of the key considerations is understanding the objectives of the stakeholders involved in the implementation and usage of the system. Stakeholders are individuals or groups who have an interest in the success of the project, and therefore their objectives must be taken into account to ensure that the project meets their needs.

The following are the stakeholder objectives that must be considered when developing a business case for SOA:

1. Business Stakeholders: The primary objective of business stakeholders is to achieve their business goals and objectives. They want to increase revenue, reduce costs, improve customer satisfaction, and enhance business agility. They require a system that is flexible, scalable, and can respond quickly to changing business needs.

2. IT Stakeholders: IT stakeholders are responsible for the implementation, maintenance, and support of the system. Their objective is to ensure that the system is reliable, secure, and easy to maintain. They require a system that is easy to integrate with existing IT infrastructure, and that can be managed and monitored effectively.

3. End Users: End users are the individuals who will be using the system on a daily basis. Their objective is to have a system that is easy to use, intuitive, and provides the functionality they need to perform their job. They require a system that is accessible from any device and any location, and that provides a consistent user experience.

4. Regulators: Regulators are responsible for ensuring that the system complies with regulations and standards. Their objective is to ensure that the system is secure, auditable, and meets regulatory requirements. They require a system that can generate reports and audit trails, and that can be easily audited.

5. Partners: Partners are organizations that have a relationship with the business and may use or interact with the system. Their objective is to have a system that is easy to integrate with their own systems, and that provides the functionality they need to interact with the business. They require a system that is reliable, secure, and provides a consistent interface.

Mnemonics and Learning Tricks:

- Remember the acronym "BITREPS" to recall the objectives of each stakeholder: Business, IT, End Users, Regulators, and Partners.
- Think of the phrase "BITE the RAP" to remember the acronym and the order of the stakeholders.

In conclusion, understanding the stakeholder objectives is essential for developing a successful business case for SOA. By considering the needs of each stakeholder group, the system can be designed and implemented to meet their objectives, resulting in a system that is reliable, secure, and meets the needs of the business.
