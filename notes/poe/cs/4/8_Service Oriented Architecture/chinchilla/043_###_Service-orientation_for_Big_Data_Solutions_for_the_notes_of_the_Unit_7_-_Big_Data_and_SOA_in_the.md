### Service-orientation for Big Data Solutions for the notes of the Unit 7 - Big Data and SOA in the subject of Service Oriented Architecture

Service-orientation is a design paradigm that focuses on creating reusable and interoperable services that can be easily combined to build complex applications. It is a key aspect of Service Oriented Architecture (SOA) and is also essential for building Big Data solutions. In this article, we will discuss the key principles of service-orientation for Big Data solutions.

#### Principles of Service-orientation for Big Data Solutions

1. **Loose Coupling**: Services should be designed in such a way that they are loosely coupled with other services. This means that services should be independent of each other and should not be tightly bound to any other service. This helps in achieving better scalability, flexibility and maintainability of the system.

2. **Abstraction**: Services should be designed in such a way that they provide a high level of abstraction. This means that services should not expose their internal implementation details to other services. This helps in achieving better interoperability and reduces the impact of changes made to the implementation of a service.

3. **Reusability**: Services should be designed in such a way that they can be easily reused across different applications. This means that services should be designed in a generic and modular way, so that they can be easily combined to build complex applications.

4. **Composability**: Services should be designed in such a way that they can be easily composed together to form complex applications. This means that services should be designed to work together seamlessly, without any conflicts or dependencies.

5. **Autonomy**: Services should be designed in such a way that they have a high level of autonomy. This means that services should be self-contained and should not depend on any other service or component to perform their tasks.

6. **Statelessness**: Services should be designed in such a way that they are stateless. This means that services should not maintain any state information between requests. This helps in achieving better scalability and reliability of the system.

7. **Discoverability**: Services should be designed in such a way that they are easily discoverable by other services. This means that services should be registered in a central repository, which can be used by other services to locate and invoke them.

#### Mnemonics and Learning Tricks

- LARCSAD: This stands for Loose Coupling, Abstraction, Reusability, Composability, Autonomy, Statelessness and Discoverability. This can be used as a mnemonic to remember the key principles of service-orientation for Big Data solutions.

- Think Lego: Think of services as Lego blocks that can be easily combined to build complex applications. Each Lego block is self-contained and can be easily reused to build different applications.

#### Conclusion

Service-orientation is a key aspect of building Big Data solutions. By following the principles of service-orientation, we can build scalable, flexible, maintainable and interoperable systems. Remembering the mnemonic LARCSAD and thinking of services as Lego blocks can help in understanding and applying the principles of service-orientation effectively.
