### Service Run-time Governance for the notes of the Unit 6 - SOA Governance and Implementation in the subject of Service Oriented Architecture

Service Run-time Governance is a critical aspect of Service-Oriented Architecture (SOA) Governance. It involves monitoring and controlling the behavior of services during runtime to ensure that they comply with the defined policies and meet the organizational goals. 

Here are some important points to consider regarding Service Run-time Governance in SOA:

1. **Policy Enforcement:** Service Run-time Governance ensures that service policies are enforced during runtime. This includes monitoring the service behavior, identifying policy violations, and taking appropriate actions to rectify them.

2. **Service Monitoring:** Service Run-time Governance involves monitoring the service performance and availability in real-time. This helps to detect any issues or failures that could affect the service quality and take corrective measures proactively.

3. **Security Management:** Service Run-time Governance also includes managing the security of services. It involves ensuring that the services are secure and protected from unauthorized access, data breaches, and other security threats.

4. **Service Level Agreement (SLA) Management:** Service Run-time Governance involves managing the SLAs of services during runtime. It includes monitoring the service performance against the SLA and taking corrective actions if the service fails to meet the agreed-upon SLA.

5. **Service Versioning:** Service Run-time Governance also involves managing the service versioning during runtime. It includes ensuring that the services are backward compatible and there are no compatibility issues with the existing clients.

6. **Service Discovery:** Service Run-time Governance involves managing the service discovery during runtime. It includes ensuring that the services are discoverable and accessible to the clients.

7. **Service Virtualization:** Service Run-time Governance involves managing the service virtualization during runtime. It includes ensuring that the services are virtualized and can be accessed from multiple locations.

Mnemonics and learning tricks can be helpful in remembering the above points. For example, the mnemonic "PS3 SVD" can be used to remember the six key aspects of Service Run-time Governance:

- P: Policy Enforcement
- S: Service Monitoring
- S: Security Management
- S: SLA Management
- V: Service Versioning
- D: Service Discovery

In conclusion, Service Run-time Governance is an essential component of SOA Governance that helps to ensure the reliable and secure operation of services during runtime. By following the above points and leveraging appropriate tools and technologies, organizations can effectively manage their services and ensure their compliance with the defined policies and goals.
