### Introduction to Internet services

The Internet connects millions of computers and other devices from all over the world, enabling them to share information and services. Some of the major Internet services are:

1. **Email:** Email is one of the most popular Internet services. It enables users to send and receive messages through their email accounts hosted by email service providers like Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo, etc. Email uses protocols like SMTP, POP3, and IMAP to send and receive messages.

2. **World Wide Web:** The World Wide Web or WWW refers to the massive collection of interlinked web pages that can be accessed through browsers like Chrome, Firefox, Safari, etc. The web pages are connected using hyperlinks and are accessed using HTTP and URL protocols. The web has enabled access to information on almost any topic.

3. **File sharing:** There are services like Dropbox, Google Drive, and OneDrive that allow users to upload and share files, folders, photos, and other data with others over the Internet. This enables collaboration and access to files from anywhere. These services use cloud storage and syncing technologies.

4. **Streaming media:** Services like YouTube, Netflix, Spotify, etc. allow streaming of video and audio content over the Internet. This allows users to watch movies, TV shows, music videos, and listen to music on demand without downloading the entire media file. These services use streaming media protocols and technologies to deliver media continuously.

5. **Voice over IP:** Services like Skype, WhatsApp calls, and Facebook calls allow users to make voice and video calls over the Internet instead of traditional phone networks. This uses VoIP protocols to transmit voice and video data in the form of packets over the Internet. VoIP bypasses traditional telephone networks and can reduce costs.
