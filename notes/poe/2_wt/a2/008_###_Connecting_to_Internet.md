### Connecting to Internet

1. Dial-up Connection:
- Uses a modem to convert analog signals to digital and vice versa.
- Connects to Internet via phone line.
- Slow speed.
- Makes phone line busy.

Mnemonic: The annoying buzzing sound of the dial-up connection resembles an old dial phone.

2. Broadband Connection:
- Provides high speed Internet access.
- Types: Digital Subscriber Line (DSL), Cable, Fiber Optic, Wireless.
- DSL: Uses phone line, faster than dial-up.
- Cable: Uses cable TV line, fast speed.
- Fiber Optic: Uses optical fiber, highest speed.
- Wireless: Uses radio waves, convenient but slower speed.

Learning trick: Remember the broadband types using the mnemonic - DCFW (DSL, Cable, Fiber, Wireless).
