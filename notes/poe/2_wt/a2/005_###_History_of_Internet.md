### History of Internet

The internet has a long history behind its existence today. Here are the major milestones in the history of the internet:

1. 1960s: The concept of packet switching was created by Leonard Kleinrock and Paul Baran. Packet switching is a digital networking communications method that groups messages into packets that are then transmitted independently and reassembled at the destination. This was a crucial development towards the concept of the internet.

2. 1969: The US government funded the first experimental packet-switched network called NSFNET. It connected major supercomputers and was a testbed for developing protocols and networking technologies.

3. 1974: The TCP/IP communications protocols were created by Vint Cerf and Bob Kahn to enable multiple different networks to connect and form an "internet."

4. 1983: The TCP/IP protocol was adopted as the networking protocol of the ARPANET, an early packet-switching network funded by the U.S. government. This was a major step towards creating a real internet.

5. 1989: Tim Berners-Lee invented the World Wide Web while at CERN, creating the first web browser and website. This opened up the internet to massive public use for the first time.

6. 1990s: The early Internet and web browsers like Mosaic and Internet Explorer were created, and the internet becomes commercial and global. Companies like Amazon, eBay, and Google are founded, marking the start of the modern internet age.

The key developments in the history of the internet were packet switching networking, the TCP/IP protocols, the invention of the World Wide Web, and the first web browsers and commercial companies in the 1990s that made the internet globally popular. With continuing advancements, the modern internet has transformed numerous aspects of society.
