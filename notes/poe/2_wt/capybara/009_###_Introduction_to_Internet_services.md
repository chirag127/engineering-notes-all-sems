### Introduction to Internet services

The internet is a global network of interconnected computer systems that communicate with each other using standard communication protocols. Internet services are the various services and protocols offered by the internet that enable users to access information, communicate with others, and conduct various online activities.

Here are some of the most common internet services:

1. World Wide Web (WWW) - The World Wide Web is a system of interlinked hypertext documents that are accessed through the internet. It is the most widely used internet service and allows users to browse web pages, search for information, and access various online resources.

2. Email - Email (Electronic mail) is a system of exchanging messages over the internet. It is a fast and efficient way to communicate with others and send/receive messages, files, and documents.

3. File Transfer Protocol (FTP) - FTP is a protocol used to transfer files over the internet. It is commonly used to upload and download files to and from web servers.

4. Instant Messaging (IM) - Instant messaging is a real-time communication service that allows users to send and receive messages instantly. It is commonly used for personal and business communication.

5. Voice Over Internet Protocol (VoIP) - VoIP is a technology that allows voice communication over the internet. It is commonly used for video conferencing, online meetings, and voice calling.

6. Social Media - Social media is a platform that allows users to create and share content, connect with others, and communicate through various services and applications.

Mnemonics and learning tricks:

One helpful mnemonic to remember these internet services is the acronym "WE FIVS," which stands for WWW, Email, FTP, IM, VoIP, and Social Media. 

Another learning trick is to associate each internet service with a common activity. For example, WWW can be associated with browsing the web, Email with sending messages, FTP with file transfer, IM with instant messaging, VoIP with voice calling, and Social Media with creating and sharing content.

Overall, understanding the various internet services is essential for anyone who uses the internet regularly. Knowing how these services work and how to use them effectively can improve communication, productivity, and access to information.
