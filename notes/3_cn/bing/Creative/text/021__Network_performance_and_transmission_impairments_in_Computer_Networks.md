#### Network performance and transmission impairments in Computer Networks

- Network performance is a measure of how well a network can deliver data and services to its users. It can be evaluated by various metrics, such as throughput, delay, jitter, packet loss, and error rate.
- Transmission impairments are any factors that degrade the quality of a signal as it travels through a network. They can cause distortion, attenuation, or noise in the signal, which can affect the network performance and reliability.
- Some common types of transmission impairments are :
  - Noise: Any unwanted signal that gets added to the transmitted signal and interferes with its detection and interpretation. Noise can be caused by various sources, such as thermal noise, shot noise, impulse noise, crosstalk, etc.
  - Distortion: Any change in the shape or amplitude of the signal due to the different propagation delays of its frequency components. Distortion can be caused by the characteristics of the transmission medium, such as bandwidth limitations, dispersion, nonlinearity, etc.
  - Attenuation: Any decrease in the signal strength due to the resistance and absorption of the transmission medium. Attenuation can be caused by the distance, the number of devices, the environmental conditions, etc. Attenuation can be compensated by using amplifiers or repeaters.
- The impact of transmission impairments on network performance depends on the type and severity of the impairment, the characteristics of the signal and the network, and the requirements of the application. For example, in a wavelength-routed optical network, the signal may encounter crosstalk at the switches and amplified spontaneous emission (ASE) noise at the amplifiers, which can degrade the bit-error rate (BER) of the signal and affect the quality of service (QoS) of the network.
- To improve the network performance and reduce the transmission impairments, various techniques can be used, such as modulation, coding, filtering, equalization, error detection and correction, etc. These techniques can enhance the signal quality, increase the data rate, reduce the bandwidth, and improve the reliability of the network.
