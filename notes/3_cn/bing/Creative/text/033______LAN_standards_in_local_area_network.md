#### LAN standards in local area network

- A local area network (LAN) is a data communication network connecting various terminals or computers within a building or limited geographical area.
- The connection among the devices could be wired or wireless.
- LAN standards are the specifications and protocols that define the operation and performance of a LAN.
- The most widely used family of LAN standards is IEEE 802, which covers different types of LAN technologies, such as Ethernet, Token Ring, Wireless LAN, etc  .
- Each LAN standard has a number and a name, such as IEEE 802.3 for Ethernet, IEEE 802.11 for Wireless LAN, IEEE 802.15 for Wireless PAN, etc .
- Each LAN standard defines the physical layer and the data link layer of the network, as well as the media access control (MAC) method, the frame format, the addressing scheme, the error detection and correction, and the network management .
- LAN standards are important for ensuring interoperability, compatibility, and reliability of LAN devices and networks .
