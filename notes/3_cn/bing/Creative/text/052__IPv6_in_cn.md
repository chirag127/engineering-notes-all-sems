### IPv6 in China

- IPv6 is an upgrade of the internet protocol that allows for virtually unlimited number of unique addresses for electronic devices online.
- China has been promoting the large-scale deployment and application of IPv6 since 2017, with the goal of running a single-stack IPv6 network by 2030.
- China has set the following targets for IPv6 adoption by the end of 2023:
  - 700 million active IPv6 users;
  - 200 million IPv6 connections for the Internet of Things;
  - 15 per cent of fixed network traffic to use IPv6;
  - 50 per cent of mobile traffic to use IPv6;
  - 95 per cent IPv6 adoption by government and major commercial websites;
  - IPv6 to be enabled by default in all new home routers.
- China has also planned to accelerate the research and development of key technologies for IPv6 security, management, and supervision, and to participate in the formulation of international standards for the next-generation internet.
- China has the largest mobile network in the world, with over 900 million mobile subscribers and over 670 million 4G/LTE subscribers. IPv6 is essential for supporting the massive growth of mobile internet users and devices in China.
- China has also built a Future Internet Test Infrastructure, which consists of 31 nodes connected by 200Gbps links, to conduct experiments and tests on IPv6 and other next-generation internet technologies.
