### Basic internetworking in network layer

- Internetworking is the process of connecting different networks using intermediate devices such as routers or gateways.
- Internetworking enables communication among hosts that use different network technologies or protocols.
- Internetworking is implemented in the network layer of the OSI-ISO model, which is responsible for routing packets across multiple networks.
- The most notable example of internetworking is the Internet, which is a global network of networks that uses the Internet Protocol (IP) as the common communication protocol.
- There are three variants of internetworking, depending on who administers and who participates in them: extranet, intranet, and internet.
  - An extranet is a network of networks that is restricted to a single organization or entity, but that also has limited connections to the networks of one or more other organizations or entities, usually for business purposes.
  - An intranet is a network of networks that is under the control of a single administrative entity, such as a company or a university, and that uses IP-based tools such as web browsers and FTP clients. An intranet is usually isolated from the rest of the Internet by firewalls or other security measures.
  - An internet is a network of networks that is open to anyone who can access it, such as the public Internet or a community network. An internet may use different protocols or technologies, but it usually relies on IP as the common protocol.
