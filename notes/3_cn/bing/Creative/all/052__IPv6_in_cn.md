### IPv6 in cn

- IPv6 is the latest version of the Internet Protocol, which provides a larger address space, enhanced security, and improved performance than IPv4.
- China is one of the leading countries in IPv6 deployment and adoption, with a plan to achieve a single-stack IPv6 network by 2030.
- According to the latest statistics from ipv6-test.com, China has about 12.8 million IPv6 users, accounting for 9.2% of the total Internet users in the country.
- The major Internet service providers (ISPs) in China that support IPv6 include ChinaNet, China Mobile, China Unicom, China Telecom, CNGI-CERNET2, and Cloudflare Warp.
- China has allocated about 1.5 billion IPv6 addresses to various entities, such as government agencies, universities, research institutes, and enterprises .
- China has also built a Future Internet Test Infrastructure (FITI) with 31 nodes and 200Gbps links, which can be used for IPv6 testing and research.
- Some of the benefits of IPv6 for China include:
  - Addressing the shortage of IPv4 addresses and avoiding the cost and complexity of network address translation (NAT).
  - Enhancing the connectivity and interoperability of the Internet of Things (IoT) devices, which are expected to reach 400 million by 2025.
  - Improving the quality of service (QoS) and user experience of online applications, such as video streaming, gaming, and e-commerce.
  - Supporting the development of digital infrastructure and innovation, as part of the 14th Five-Year Plan.

- Some of the challenges of IPv6 for China include:
  - Upgrading the existing network equipment, software, and applications to support IPv6.
  - Ensuring the compatibility and coexistence of IPv4 and IPv6 during the transition period.
  - Providing adequate training and education for network engineers, developers, and users on IPv6.
  - Addressing the security and privacy issues of IPv6, such as spoofing, scanning, and tracking.

- Some of the mnemonics and learning tricks for IPv6 in cn include:
  - Remembering the format of an IPv6 address, which consists of eight groups of four hexadecimal digits, separated by colons, such as 2001:0db8:85a3:0000:0000:8a2e:0370:7334.
  - Using the zero compression and zero suppression rules to shorten an IPv6 address, such as 2001:db8:85a3::8a2e:370:7334 or 2001:db8::1.
  - Using the prefix notation to indicate the network portion of an IPv6 address, such as 2001:db8::/32 or ::1/128.
  - Using the special IPv6 addresses, such as :: for the unspecified address, ::1 for the loopback address, fe80::/10 for the link-local address, and ff00::/8 for the multicast address.
  - Using the IPv4-mapped IPv6 address to represent an IPv4 address in an IPv6 network, such as ::ffff:192.0.2.1 or ::ffff:c000:201.
