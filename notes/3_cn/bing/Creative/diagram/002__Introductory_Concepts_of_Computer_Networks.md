A computer network is a group of computers that are connected to each other and follow similar usage protocols for the purpose of sharing information and resources. A computer network can be classified by its size, topology, architecture, and purpose. Some of the common types of computer networks are LAN, WAN, MAN, PAN, SAN, and CAN.

An end device is a device that sends or receives the data in the network, such as a computer, a printer, a smartphone, or a sensor. The media provides connectivity between the end devices, such as cables, fiber optics, or wireless signals. The protocols enable communication between two or more end devices, such as TCP/IP, HTTP, FTP, or SMTP. The networking device is a device that facilitates the transmission and routing of data in the network, such as a router, a switch, a hub, or a firewall.
