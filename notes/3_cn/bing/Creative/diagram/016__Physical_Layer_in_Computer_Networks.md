The physical layer is the lowest layer in the OSI model of computer networking. It is responsible for transmitting and receiving bits over a physical medium, such as a cable, a wireless channel, or an optical fiber. It also defines the electrical, mechanical, and procedural aspects of the physical connection, such as the connectors, the voltage levels, the modulation schemes, and the encoding methods.
