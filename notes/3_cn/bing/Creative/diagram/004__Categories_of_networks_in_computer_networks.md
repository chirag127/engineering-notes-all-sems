Categories of networks in computer networks are classifications of networks based on their size, scope, topology, and purpose. There are many types of networks, but some of the most common ones are:

- Personal Area Network (PAN): A network that connects devices within the range of an individual, usually within 10 meters. Examples are Bluetooth, USB, and infrared connections.
- Local Area Network (LAN): A network that connects devices within a limited area, such as a home, office, school, or building. Examples are Ethernet and Wi-Fi connections.
- Metropolitan Area Network (MAN): A network that connects devices within a larger area, such as a city, campus, or region. Examples are cable, DSL, and fiber-optic connections.
- Wide Area Network (WAN): A network that connects devices across a large geographical distance, such as a country, continent, or the world. Examples are the Internet, satellite, and cellular connections.
