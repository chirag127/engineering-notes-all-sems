A network structure with reference to computer networks is the way network devices and services are structured to serve the connectivity needs of client devices. Network devices typically include switches and routers. Types of services include DHCP and DNS. Client devices comprise end-user devices, servers, and smart things.

There are different types of network structures or architectures, such as LAN, WAN, MAN, PAN, SAN, and CAN, depending on the geographic location, protocols, physical arrangement, traffic control, and purpose of the network.
