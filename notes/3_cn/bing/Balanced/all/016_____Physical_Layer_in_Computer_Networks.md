### Physical Layer in Computer Networks

- The physical layer is the first and lowest layer of the OSI model of computer networking.
- The physical layer provides an electrical, mechanical, and procedural interface to the transmission medium.
- The physical layer defines the means of transmitting a stream of raw bits over a physical data link connecting network nodes.
- The physical layer consists of the electronic circuit transmission technologies of a network.
- The physical layer maintains the data rate (how many bits a sender can send per second).
- The physical layer performs synchronization of bits.
- The physical layer helps in transmission medium decision (direction of data transfer).
- The physical layer helps in physical topology (mesh, star, bus, ring) decision (topology through which we can connect the devices).
- The physical layer is responsible for encoding, modulation, multiplexing, signal generation, transmission, reception, and demodulation of signals.
- The physical layer can be implemented through a great number of different hardware technologies with widely varying characteristics.
- Some examples of physical layer devices are hubs, repeaters, cables, connectors, and network interface cards.
- Some examples of physical layer standards are Ethernet, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, USB, and RS-232.

#### Mnemonics and learning tricks for the physical layer

- To remember the functions of the physical layer, you can use the acronym **EMT DR SED**:

  - **E**ncoding
  - **M**odulation
  - **T**ransmission
  - **D**ata rate
  - **R**eception
  - **S**ynchronization
  - **E**lectrical, mechanical, and procedural interface
  - **D**emodulation

- To remember the order of the OSI model layers, you can use the mnemonic **Please Do Not Throw Sausage Pizza Away**:

  - **P**hysical
  - **D**ata Link
  - **N**etwork
  - **T**ransport
  - **S**ession
  - **P**resentation
  - **A**pplication

- To remember some examples of physical layer devices, you can use the mnemonic **HRC CN**:

  - **H**ubs
  - **R**epeaters
  - **C**ables
  - **C**onnectors
  - **N**etwork interface cards

- To remember some examples of physical layer standards, you can use the mnemonic **EW BUS**:

  - **E**thernet
  - **W**i-Fi
  - **B**luetooth
  - **U**SB
  - **S**-232
