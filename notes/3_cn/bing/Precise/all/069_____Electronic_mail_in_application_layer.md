### Electronic mail in application layer

Electronic mail (email) is a method of exchanging messages between people using electronic devices. It operates across the internet or other computer networks. Email is one of the protocols included in the application layer of the OSI model and the TCP/IP protocol suite.

1. Email works by sending messages from the sender's email client to the recipient's email server, and then from the server to the recipient's email client.
2. The Simple Mail Transfer Protocol (SMTP) is the standard protocol for sending email messages between servers.
3. The Post Office Protocol (POP) and the Internet Message Access Protocol (IMAP) are two of the most common protocols for retrieving email messages from a server.
4. Email messages are typically composed of three main parts: the header, the body, and the attachments.
5. The header contains information about the sender, recipient, subject, and other metadata.
6. The body contains the actual message content, which can be in plain text or HTML format.
7. Attachments are additional files that are sent along with the email message, such as images, documents, or audio files.

A mnemonic to remember the email retrieval protocols is "PIMP" - Post Office Protocol (POP), Internet Message Access Protocol (IMAP).

Advantages of using email include:
- Speed: Email messages can be sent and received almost instantly.
- Convenience: Email can be accessed from anywhere with an internet connection.
- Cost-effective: Sending an email is typically free or very low cost.
- Record keeping: Email provides a written record of communication.

Disadvantages of using email include:
- Spam: Unsolicited email messages can clutter up an inbox.
- Security: Email messages can be intercepted and read by unauthorized parties.
- Miscommunication: The tone of an email message can be easily misinterpreted.

In summary, email is a widely used method of communication that operates at the application layer of the OSI model and the TCP/IP protocol suite. It has several advantages and disadvantages, and there are several protocols involved in sending and retrieving email messages. A mnemonic to remember the email retrieval protocols is "PIMP" - Post Office Protocol (POP), Internet Message Access Protocol (IMAP).
