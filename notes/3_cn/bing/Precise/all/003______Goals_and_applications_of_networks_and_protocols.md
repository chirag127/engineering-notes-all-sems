#### Goals and applications of networks and protocols

- Networks and protocols are designed to facilitate communication between devices and systems.
- The primary goal of networks and protocols is to enable the exchange of data and information between devices and systems.
- Networks and protocols are used to connect devices and systems, allowing them to communicate and share resources.
- Networks and protocols are used to provide reliable and secure communication between devices and systems.
- Networks and protocols are used to manage and control the flow of data and information between devices and systems.
- Networks and protocols are used to ensure that data and information are transmitted and received accurately and efficiently.
- Networks and protocols are used to provide access to shared resources, such as printers, files, and databases.
- Networks and protocols are used to support a wide range of applications, including email, file sharing, web browsing, and online gaming.
- Networks and protocols are used to support the development and deployment of new technologies, such as the Internet of Things (IoT) and cloud computing.
- Networks and protocols are used to support the growth and expansion of businesses and organizations, by enabling them to connect with customers, partners, and suppliers.
