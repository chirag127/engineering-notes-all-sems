#### Network topology design in Computer Networks

Network topology is used to describe the physical and logical structure of a network. It maps the way different nodes on a network, including switches and routers, are placed and interconnected, as well as how data flows. Diagramming the locations of endpoints and service requirements helps determine the best placement for each node to optimize traffic flows.

Network topology is how participating computers, antennas, sensors, and other devices are arranged and interact in a network. Several types of topology can be used to organize and direct the flow of data within networks. Selection of the most appropriate topology is a critical part of network design and takes into consideration various factors.

The two major categories of topology are physical and logical. A Network Topology is the arrangement with which computer systems or network devices are connected to each other. Topologies may define both physical and logical aspect of the network. Both logical and physical topologies could be same or different in a same network.
