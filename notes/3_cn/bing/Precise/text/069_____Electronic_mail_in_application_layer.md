### Electronic mail in application layer

Electronic mail, commonly known as email, is a method of exchanging digital messages between people using electronic devices. It operates across the internet or other computer networks. Here are some key points about email in the application layer:

1. Email is an application layer protocol that provides a system for composing, sending, receiving, and storing messages.
2. The application layer is the topmost layer in the OSI model and is responsible for providing services to the user.
3. Email uses various protocols in the application layer, such as SMTP (Simple Mail Transfer Protocol) for sending messages, and POP3 (Post Office Protocol version 3) or IMAP (Internet Message Access Protocol) for receiving messages.
4. SMTP is used to send messages from the sender's mail server to the recipient's mail server, while POP3 and IMAP are used to retrieve messages from the recipient's mail server to their email client.
5. Email messages are composed of two main parts: the header and the body. The header contains information such as the sender, recipient, subject, and date, while the body contains the actual message.
6. Email also allows for the attachment of files, such as documents, images, and videos, to be sent along with the message.
7. Email is widely used for both personal and business communication and has become an essential tool for communication in the modern world.
