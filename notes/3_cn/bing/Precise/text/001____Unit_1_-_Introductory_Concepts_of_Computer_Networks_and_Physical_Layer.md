## Unit 1 - Introductory Concepts of Computer Networks and Physical Layer

1. A computer network is a group of interconnected devices that can communicate with each other and share resources.
2. The physical layer is the first layer of the OSI model and is responsible for the transmission of data over a physical medium.
3. The physical layer defines the characteristics of the physical medium, such as the type of cable or wireless technology used, and the electrical and mechanical characteristics of the signals.
4. The physical layer also defines the methods for transmitting data over the physical medium, such as modulation and encoding.
5. Some common physical layer technologies include Ethernet, Wi-Fi, and Bluetooth.
6. The physical layer is important because it provides the foundation for all other layers of the network.
7. Without a properly functioning physical layer, data cannot be transmitted between devices on the network.
8. The physical layer is also responsible for error detection and correction, to ensure that data is transmitted accurately and reliably.
9. Understanding the concepts of the physical layer is essential for anyone working with computer networks, as it provides the foundation for all other aspects of networking.
