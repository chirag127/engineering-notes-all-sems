#### LAN standards in local area network

- LAN (Local Area Network) standards are technical specifications that define the protocols and technologies used in local area networks.
- These standards are developed and maintained by organizations such as the Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers (IEEE) and the International Organization for Standardization (ISO).
- Some common LAN standards include Ethernet (IEEE 802.3), Token Ring (IEEE 802.5), and Wireless LAN (IEEE 802.11).
- Ethernet is the most widely used LAN technology and uses a physical star topology with a central hub or switch.
- Token Ring is a LAN technology that uses a logical ring topology and was popular in the 1980s and 1990s.
- Wireless LAN (WLAN) technology allows devices to connect to a LAN wirelessly using radio waves. The most widely used WLAN standard is Wi-Fi (IEEE 802.11).
- LAN standards are important because they ensure interoperability between different devices and systems in a local area network.
- Adherence to LAN standards also ensures that data can be transmitted reliably and efficiently within a local area network.
