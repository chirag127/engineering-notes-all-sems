### Quality of Service in Transport Layer

The Quality of Service (QoS) in transport layer is a set of mechanisms that help to ensure the transmission of data over a network with certain guarantees. These guarantees are in terms of bandwidth, delay, jitter, and packet loss. In this section, we will discuss the various QoS mechanisms that are used in the transport layer.

* **Traffic shaping**: Traffic shaping is a technique used to control the amount of traffic that is sent through a network. It is used to ensure that the network does not become congested. Traffic shaping works by delaying packets that are sent through the network. This technique is used to ensure that the network does not become congested.

* **Bandwidth reservation**: Bandwidth reservation is a technique used to reserve a certain amount of bandwidth for a particular application. This technique is used to ensure that the application gets the required amount of bandwidth it needs. Bandwidth reservation is used in applications that require a certain level of bandwidth, such as video streaming.

* **Packet prioritization**: Packet prioritization is a technique used to prioritize packets that are sent through the network. This technique is used to ensure that packets that are more important are sent first. Packet prioritization is used in applications that require a certain level of service, such as VoIP.

* **Congestion avoidance**: Congestion avoidance is a technique used to avoid congestion in the network. This technique is used to ensure that the network does not become congested. Congestion avoidance works by detecting congestion in the network and reducing the amount of traffic that is sent through the network.

* **Congestion control**: Congestion control is a technique used to control the amount of traffic that is sent through the network. This technique is used to ensure that the network does not become congested. Congestion control works by reducing the amount of traffic that is sent through the network when congestion is detected.

In conclusion, QoS mechanisms in the transport layer are used to ensure that the network provides the required level of service. These mechanisms work together to control the amount of traffic that is sent through the network, prioritize packets, and avoid congestion.
