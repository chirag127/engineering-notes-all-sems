
### Quality of Service in Transport Layer

1. Quality of Service (QoS) is a set of techniques used to manage network traffic by prioritizing certain types of data.

2. QoS techniques are used to ensure that mission-critical applications, such as voice and video, are given priority over less important data, such as web browsing.

3. The transport layer of the OSI model is responsible for providing reliable, end-to-end data delivery.

4. QoS techniques at the transport layer include:
    * Flow control: A mechanism for regulating the amount of data sent between two hosts.
    * Error control: Techniques for detecting and correcting errors in transmitted data.
    * Congestion control: Mechanisms for managing network traffic by regulating the amount of data sent.

5. QoS techniques can be used to improve the performance of applications that require high throughput and low latency, such as streaming media and VoIP.
