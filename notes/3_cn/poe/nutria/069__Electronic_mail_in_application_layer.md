
### Electronic mail in application Layer

* Electronic mail (e-mail) is a method of exchanging digital messages from an author to one or more recipients. E-mail is one of the most widely used applications of the Internet. 
* E-mail is sent through an Internet server using Simple Mail Transfer Protocol (SMTP). The protocol defines the format of the messages and the process of sending and receiving mail. 
* The e-mail message is composed of two parts: the header and the body. The header contains the sender's and recipient's address, the subject of the message, and other information. The body contains the actual message. 
* E-mail clients are programs used to compose, read, and manage messages. Popular e-mail clients include Microsoft Outlook, Mozilla Thunderbird, and Apple Mail. 
* E-mail messages can be encrypted to protect the contents from unauthorized access. This is done by using a cryptographic protocol such as Pretty Good Privacy (PGP) or S/MIME. 
* E-mail can also be used to send attachments such as documents, images, and audio and video files.
