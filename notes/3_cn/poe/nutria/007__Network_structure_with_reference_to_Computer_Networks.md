
#### Network Structure with Reference to Computer Networks

1. Networks are composed of two main components: nodes and links. 
2. Nodes are the individual computers and other devices that are connected to the network.
3. Links are the physical or logical connections between the nodes.
4. Network topology is the arrangement of the nodes and links in a network. 
5. Common network topologies include star, bus, ring, and mesh.
6. The star topology connects all nodes to a central node, while the bus topology connects all nodes to a single, linear backbone. 
7. The ring topology connects all nodes in a circular arrangement, while the mesh topology connects each node to every other node.
8. Networks can also be classified by their physical and logical structure. 
9. Physical networks are based on the physical layout of the nodes and links, while logical networks are based on the logical relationships between the nodes. 
10. Network protocols are the rules and conventions that govern the communication between nodes on a network. 
11. Common network protocols include TCP/IP, UDP, and FTP.
12. Network security is the practice of protecting a network from malicious attacks and unauthorized access. 
13. Common security measures include firewalls, encryption, and authentication.
