
### Physical Layer in Computer Networks

1. The physical layer is the lowest layer of the OSI (Open Systems Interconnection) model. It is responsible for the transmission and reception of unstructured raw data in a physical medium. 
2. The physical layer is responsible for the transmission of bits over a physical medium. It is also responsible for establishing, maintaining and terminating connections between devices. 
3. The physical layer is responsible for the physical characteristics of the network, such as the type of cabling used, the speed of the connection, and the topology of the network. 
4. The physical layer defines the electrical, mechanical, procedural and functional specifications for activating, maintaining and deactivating the physical link between two systems. 
5. It defines the type of cable, the type of connectors, the signal voltage levels, the timing of the signals, and the transmission rates. 
6. It also defines the physical topology of the network, such as the star, bus, ring, mesh, and tree topologies. 
7. The physical layer is responsible for the transmission of data over the physical medium. It provides the physical means to send and receive data on a network. 
8. It is responsible for the transmission of bits over a physical medium and for the physical characteristics of the network, such as the type of cabling used, the speed of the connection, and the topology of the network. 
9. The physical layer is also responsible for the transmission of data over the physical medium, such as optical fiber, copper wire, and wireless media. 
10. The physical layer also provides the physical means to send and receive data on a network, such as the use of modems, cables, and connectors.
