
## Unit 4 - Transport Layer in Computer Networks

1. The transport layer is responsible for the end-to-end delivery of data and provides services to the application layer.
2. It is responsible for breaking the data into small units called segments, and then reassembling them at the destination.
3. The transport layer provides services such as reliability, flow control, congestion control, and error control.
4. It is responsible for segmenting the data into packets, and then reassembling them at the destination.
5. The transport layer also provides services such as connection establishment, connection termination, and connectionless service.
6. The transport layer protocols are TCP and UDP.
7. TCP provides reliable, connection-oriented service, while UDP provides connectionless, unreliable service.
8. Both protocols provide flow control, congestion control, and error control.
