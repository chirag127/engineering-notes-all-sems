

#### Network Components in Computer Networks

1. Routers: Routers are devices that forward data packets between computer networks. They use headers and forwarding tables to determine the best path for forwarding the packets, and they use protocols such as ICMP to communicate with each other and configure the best route between any two hosts.

2. Switches: Switches are devices that connect multiple devices together on the same network. They use MAC addresses to determine which devices are connected to which ports, and they use protocols such as Spanning Tree Protocol (STP) to prevent loops in the network.

3. Hubs: Hubs are devices that connect multiple devices together on the same network. They do not make any decisions about which packets should be sent where, but rather they just send all packets they receive out of all the ports.

4. Network Interface Cards (NICs): NICs are devices that allow computers to connect to a network. They can be either wired or wireless, and they use protocols such as Ethernet and Wi-Fi to communicate with the network.

5. Network Cables: Network cables are used to connect devices together on the same network. They can be either copper or fiber optic, and they use protocols such as Ethernet to transmit data.
