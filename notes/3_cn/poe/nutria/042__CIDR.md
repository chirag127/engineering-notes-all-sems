
#### CIDR

* CIDR stands for Classless Inter-Domain Routing and is an IP addressing scheme that replaces the older system based on classes A, B, and C.
* It allows for more efficient routing of IP packets by allowing variable-length subnet masks (VLSM).
* CIDR notation is a compact representation of an IP address and its associated routing prefix.
* CIDR notation consists of an IP address followed by a slash (‘/’) and the prefix length. For example, 192.0.2.0/24 represents the IPv4 address 192.0.2.0 and its associated routing prefix of 24 bits.
* CIDR is used to allocate IP addresses and route traffic on the Internet. It is also used by private networks to route traffic within their networks.
* CIDR has been instrumental in helping to alleviate the IPv4 address exhaustion problem by allowing more efficient use of the available address space.
