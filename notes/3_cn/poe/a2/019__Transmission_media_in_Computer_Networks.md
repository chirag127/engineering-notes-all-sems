#### Transmission media in Computer Networks.

1. Twisted pair cable:
- Consists of two insulated copper wires twisted together.
- Types: Unshielded twisted pair (UTP), Shielded twisted pair (STP).
- Used for telephone lines and local area network connections.
- Inexpensive and flexible but susceptible to interference.

2. Coaxial cable:
- Consists of an inner conductor surrounded by a flexible outer sheath.
- Used for cable television networks and some computer networks.
- Provides high bandwidth and is resistant to interference.
- More expensive and difficult to work with than twisted pair cable.

3. Fiber optic cable:
- Consists of one or more strands of glass or plastic fiber.
- Light pulses are used to transmit data with very low loss and high bandwidth.
- Immune to electromagnetic interference and secure from signal tapping.
- Expensive but can support very high data rates and long distances.

4. Wireless media:
- Transmission of data is via wireless electromagnetic waves.
- Common methods: Radio, Microwave, Infrared, Laser.
- Convenient since it does not require physical connections.
- Limited bandwidth and security/interference can be issues.
