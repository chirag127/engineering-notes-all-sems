#### Categories of networks in computer networks.

1. Local Area Networks (LANs): LANs connect computers and devices in a limited area such as an office building, a home or a small group of buildings. LANs are high speed networks allowing computers and devices to share resources such as file servers, printers, storage etc. Ethernet and Wi-Fi are common technologies used to implement LANs.

2. Metropolitan Area Networks (MANs): MANs consist of a network of cables and wireless links covering an area larger than a LAN but smaller than a WAN, such as a city. MANs are commonly used for communication between LANs and as a network service provider.

3. Wide Area Networks (WANs): WANs connect multiple LANs over longer distances. The Internet is the largest WAN which spans the globe, connecting multiple smaller networks and allowing global communication and access to resources. Other examples of WANs include connections between a company's offices or data centers. Technologies used include leased lines, fiber optic cables, satellites, and telephone lines.

4. Storage Area Networks (SANs): SANs are high-speed networks primarily used to access storage resources such as disk arrays and tape libraries. SANs are commonly used in data centers and storage virtualization. Fibre Channel is the most common technology used to implement SANs.
