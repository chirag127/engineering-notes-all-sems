### IPv6 in cn

1. Introduction to IPv6
 - IPv6 is the latest version of the Internet Protocol (IP)
 - It was developed to replace IPv4 due to depletion of IPv4 addresses
 - IPv6 uses 128-bit addresses as compared to 32-bit addresses in IPv4, providing a hugely expanded address space
 - IPv6 aims to simplify aspects of address assignment and network renumbering when compared to IPv4

2. IPv6 address
 - An IPv6 address is 128 bits long, written as 8 groups of 4 hexadecimal digits separated by colons (:)
 - For example, 2001:db8:85a3:0:0:8a2e:370:7334
 - Leading zeros in a group can be omitted, but a single group of consecutive zeros can be abbreviated with a double colon (::)
 - IPv6 addresses are classified into unicast, multicast and anycast addresses

3. IPv6 in China
 - China officially launched IPv6 commercialization in April 2011 and planned to implement IPv6 in three phases by 2025
 - As of June 2018, China has over 120 million IPv6 users which accounts for about 25% of the world's IPv6 users
 - The fast growth of IPv6 deployment in China is due to government support and efforts by large network operators like China Telecom, China Unicom and China Mobile to promote IPv6 commercialization
 - However, more work is needed to make IPv6 more widely adopted in China to meet the goal of large-scale commercial application by 2025
