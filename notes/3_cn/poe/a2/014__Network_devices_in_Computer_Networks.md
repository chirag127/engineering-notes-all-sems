#### Network devices in Computer Networks.

1. Routers: Routers connect two or more networks and forward packets between them based on network addresses. They use routing tables to determine the best path for packets to reach their destination network.

2. Switches: Switches connect devices on the same network and forward packets between ports based on MAC addresses. They are used to segment a network into multiple collision domains and increase bandwidth.

3. Bridges: Bridges connect two segments of the same network and forward packets between them based on MAC addresses. They filter unwanted traffic and reduce collisions. Bridges operate at the data link layer.

4. Gateways: Gateways connect networks using different protocols and act as translators between them. They convert packets from the protocol of one network to the protocol of another network. Gateways operate at multiple layers of the OSI model.

5. Repeaters: Repeaters boost the signal strength of networks and forward electrical signals between segments. They operate at the physical layer and copy bits from one signal and transmit them on the other port without altering them.
