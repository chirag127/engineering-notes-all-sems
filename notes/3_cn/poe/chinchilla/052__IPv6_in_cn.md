### IPv6 in CN

IPv6 is the latest version of the Internet Protocol (IP) that is designed to replace the current version, IPv4. In China, the adoption of IPv6 is gaining momentum as the government is promoting its use to address the shortage of IP addresses and to support the development of new applications and services. Here are some key points on IPv6 in China:

1. China is the world's largest Internet market with more than 900 million users, which makes it crucial for the country to adopt IPv6 to support the growth of the Internet.

2. In 2017, the Chinese government launched a national plan to promote the adoption of IPv6, which includes setting targets for IPv6 deployment and providing financial incentives to encourage its adoption.

3. The major telecom operators in China, including China Telecom, China Unicom, and China Mobile, have been actively deploying IPv6 in their networks to support the growing demand for Internet services.

4. As of 2021, China has the world's largest IPv6 deployment, with more than 40% of its Internet users using IPv6.

5. The government has also set up a national IPv6 testing center to provide technical support for the deployment of IPv6 in China and to promote the development of new IPv6-based applications and services.

6. The adoption of IPv6 in China is expected to have a significant impact on the global Internet community, as it will increase the demand for IPv6 addresses and encourage other countries to adopt IPv6 as well.

7. However, there are still challenges to the widespread adoption of IPv6 in China, such as the need for more IPv6-enabled devices and applications, and the need for more skilled professionals to manage IPv6 networks.

In conclusion, the adoption of IPv6 in China is an important step towards the development of a more robust and scalable Internet infrastructure. With the government's support and the efforts of the telecom operators and other stakeholders, the adoption of IPv6 is expected to continue to grow in China, which will benefit both the country and the global Internet community.
