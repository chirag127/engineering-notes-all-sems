## Unit 1 - Introductory Concepts of Computer Networks and Physical Layer

In this unit, we will discuss the introductory concepts of computer networks and the physical layer. Here are some key points to keep in mind:

### Computer Networks

- A computer network is a collection of interconnected devices that can communicate with each other.
- The main purpose of a computer network is to share resources and information between devices.
- Computer networks can be classified according to their size, topology, and communication technology.
- There are several types of computer networks, such as LAN, WAN, MAN, and PAN.
- LAN stands for Local Area Network, and it is used to connect devices in a small geographical area, such as an office or a home.
- WAN stands for Wide Area Network, and it is used to connect devices in a large geographical area, such as a city or a country.
- MAN stands for Metropolitan Area Network, and it is used to connect devices in a metropolitan area.
- PAN stands for Personal Area Network, and it is used to connect devices that are in close proximity to each other, such as a smartphone and a wearable device.

### Physical Layer

- The physical layer is the lowest layer of the OSI model, and it is responsible for transmitting raw data between devices.
- The main functions of the physical layer are to convert digital data into signals that can be transmitted over a communication channel and to ensure that the signals are received correctly.
- The physical layer is also responsible for defining the physical characteristics of the communication channel, such as the type of cable, the signal encoding, and the transmission rate.
- There are several types of physical media that can be used for communication, such as twisted pair cable, coaxial cable, fiber optic cable, and wireless media.
- The choice of physical media depends on factors such as the distance between devices, the bandwidth required, and the cost.
