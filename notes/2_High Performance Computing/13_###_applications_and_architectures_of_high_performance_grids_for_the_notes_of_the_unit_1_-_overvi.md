### Applications and Architectures of High Performance Grids for the notes of the Unit 1 - Overview of Grid Computing Technology in the subject of High Performance Computing
Applications:
1. Scientific simulations and data analysis.
2. Distributed storage and data management.
3. Resource sharing and virtual organizations.
4. Business and financial modeling.
5. Weather forecasting and climate modeling.

Architectures:
1. Client-Server: Centralized management of resources.
2. Peer-to-Peer: Decentralized and distributed resources.
3. Hybrid: Combination of client-server and peer-to-peer.
4. Service-Oriented: Resources provided as services.

Note: Grid computing refers to the use of multiple resources over a network to solve complex problems.
