### Types of Grids for the notes of the Unit 1 - Overview of Grid Computing Technology in the subject of High Performance Computing
1. Fixed Grid: A grid where resources are pre-allocated and dedicated to specific tasks or users.
2. Dynamic Grid: A grid where resources are allocated on-demand and can be reallocated to different tasks or users.
3. Community Grid: A grid created by a group of users or organizations who share resources for a common goal.
4. Enterprise Grid: A grid within a single organization or enterprise, used to manage and allocate internal resources.
5. Desktop Grid: A grid where resources are provided by individual desktops or laptops, often used for volunteer computing projects.
6. Utility Grid: A grid where resources are treated as a commodity and can be bought and sold on demand.
