Clients in cloud computing refer to devices or applications that access cloud-based services and resources over a network, typically the internet. They can be laptops, smartphones, tablets, or other devices. Clients interact with cloud-based servers to store, retrieve, and process data. The benefits of using cloud clients include access to powerful computing resources, scalability, and cost savings.
