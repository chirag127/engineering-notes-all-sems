### Components for Clusters for the notes of the Unit 3 - Overview of Cluster Computing in the subject of High Performance Computing
Components for Clusters:
1. Front-end node: acts as a gateway for users to access the cluster.
2. Compute nodes: perform the actual computation tasks.
3. Networking: interconnects nodes and enables communication between them.
4. Storage: provides shared access to data across the cluster.
5. Job scheduler: manages the allocation of tasks to nodes and monitors their progress.
6. Cluster management software: monitors and manages the overall operation of the cluster.
