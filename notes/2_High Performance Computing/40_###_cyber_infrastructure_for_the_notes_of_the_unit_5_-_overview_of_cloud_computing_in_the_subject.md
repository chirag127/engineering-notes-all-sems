### Cyber infrastructure for the notes of the Unit 5 - Overview of Cloud Computing in the subject of High Performance Computing
Cyber infrastructure refers to the combination of hardware, software, data, and people that support the use of cloud computing. It is the foundation for delivering scalable, secure, and reliable cloud services.

Some key components of cyber infrastructure for cloud computing include:

1. Data centers: physical facilities that house the computer systems and related components, such as telecommunications and storage systems, that make up the cloud infrastructure.

2. Networking: the communication pathways that allow data to be transmitted between the data centers and between the cloud and the end-users.

3. Virtualization: the abstraction of computing resources from the underlying physical hardware, allowing multiple virtual machines to run on a single physical server.

4. Storage: the ability to store vast amounts of data in a centralized location, accessible from anywhere with an internet connection.

5. Security: the measures taken to protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of the data and services in the cloud.

6. Management: the tools and processes used to manage the cloud infrastructure, including monitoring, provisioning, and maintenance.

7. Interoperability: the ability of cloud services to work together seamlessly, regardless of the underlying technology or vendor.

In summary, cyber infrastructure is critical to the success of cloud computing and must be designed, implemented, and managed with scalability, security, and reliability in mind.
