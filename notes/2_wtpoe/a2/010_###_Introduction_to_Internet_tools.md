### Introduction to Internet tools

The Internet has enabled various tools and services that make our lives easy. Here are some of the major Internet tools and their uses:

1. **Email**: Email is one of the most popular Internet services. It enables electronic exchange of messages between people. We can send text messages, files, images, videos, etc. using email. Popular email services are Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo Mail, etc.

Mnemonic: Every Mail Exchanges Love (EMEL)

2. **World Wide Web**: The World Wide Web or simply web refers to the collection of websites and webpages accessible through the Internet. We use a software called a web browser to access the web and navigate between websites and webpages. Popular web browsers are Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Safari, etc.

3. **Search Engines**: Search engines are Internet services that help us find information on the web. We can search for websites, images, videos, news, etc. using search engines. The most popular web search engine is Google Search. Other search engines are Bing, Yahoo Search, DuckDuckGo, etc.

4. **Social Media**: Social media platforms enable us to connect, communicate and share content with friends, family and people with common interests. Popular social media sites are Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, LinkedIn, Pinterest, etc. We can share status updates, photos, videos, messages, join interest-based communities, etc. on social media.
