### Introduction to Internet services

The Internet services refer to the services provided over the Internet. Some of the key Internet services are:

1.  World Wide Web (WWW): The WWW refers to the massive collection of interconnected web pages that can be accessed over the Internet. Websites and web applications are part of the WWW which allow us to access information on any topic. Mnemonic: The WWW has Spiders(web crawlers) and Webs(web pages).
2.  E-mail: E-mail or electronic mail allows us to exchange messages over the Internet. E-mail uses a client-server model where the e-mail clients connect to the e-mail servers to send and receive e-mails. Popular e-mail providers are Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo Mail, etc.
3.  File sharing: The Internet allows us to share files such as images, audio, video, software, etc. with others over the network. Services such as Dropbox, Google Drive, OneDrive, etc. allow cloud-based file sharing. Torrent is a popular peer-to-peer file sharing protocol.
4.  Streaming: We can stream and watch multimedia content such as videos and music over the Internet using streaming services such as YouTube, Netflix, Spotify, etc. The content is buffered and played on our devices as it is being downloaded.
5.  VoIP: VoIP or Voice over Internet Protocol allows us to make voice and video calls over the Internet instead of traditional phone lines. Popular VoIP services are Skype, WhatsApp calls, Facebook calls, etc.
6.  Online social networking: Services such as Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, LinkedIn, etc. allow us to socially connect and interact with people over the Internet. We can share updates, photos, messages, and more using these services.
7.  Cloud services: The Internet allows us to access computing resources and services from cloud providers such as Amazon Web Services, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud Platform. We can deploy applications, store data, and more using these cloud services.
