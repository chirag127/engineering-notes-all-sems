### Problem Solving Approach to Typical AI problems for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction in the subject of Artificial Intelligence
Problem Solving Approach to AI problems:
1. Define the problem: Clearly understand the problem and its requirements.
2. Formulate a plan: Develop a strategy to solve the problem.
3. Execute the plan: Implement the plan using AI techniques.
4. Evaluate the solution: Check if the solution meets the requirements and if it can be improved.
5. Repeat: Repeat steps 2-4 until a satisfactory solution is found.

Note: This approach can be applied to various AI problems such as search, decision making, pattern recognition, and natural language processing.
