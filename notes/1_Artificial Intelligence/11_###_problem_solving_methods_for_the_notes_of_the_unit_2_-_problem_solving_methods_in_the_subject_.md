### Problem solving Methods for the notes of the Unit 2 - Problem Solving Methods in the subject of Artificial Intelligence
Problem solving methods in AI can be broadly categorized into two types:
1. Search-based methods: These methods involve exploring the state space of a problem to find a solution. Examples include breadth-first search, depth-first search, and A* search.
2. Knowledge-based methods: These methods involve using knowledge about the problem domain to solve the problem. Examples include rule-based systems, decision trees, and expert systems.
3. Heuristic methods: These methods use problem-specific strategies to find a solution. Examples include hill climbing, simulated annealing, and genetic algorithms.

It is important to note that a combination of these methods may be used to solve a single problem in AI, depending on the nature of the problem and the available resources.
