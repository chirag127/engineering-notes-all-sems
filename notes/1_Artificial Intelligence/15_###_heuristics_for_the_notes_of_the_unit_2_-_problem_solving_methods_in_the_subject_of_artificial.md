### Heuristics for the notes of the Unit 2 - Problem Solving Methods in the subject of Artificial Intelligence
Heuristics are problem-solving strategies that use shortcuts or rules of thumb to find solutions quickly and efficiently.

1. Means-Ends Analysis: Breaking down the problem into smaller sub-problems and working towards the solution.

2. Generate and Test: Generating possible solutions and testing their feasibility until a solution is found.

3. Hill Climbing: Starting from an initial solution and iteratively improving it until a local optimum is reached.

4. Simulated Annealing: Similar to Hill Climbing, but with a random component to avoid getting stuck in local optima.

5. Constraint Satisfaction: Finding a solution that satisfies a set of constraints.

6. Local Search: Starting from an initial solution and making small changes to improve it.

7. Best-First Search: Exploring the most promising solutions first.

8. A* Search: Combines Best-First Search with a cost function to find the optimal solution.

9. Greedy Algorithm: Making the best choice at each step to find a solution.

10. Dynamic Programming: Breaking down a problem into smaller sub-problems and solving them in a bottom-up manner to find an optimal solution.
