### Constraint Propagation for the notes of the Unit 2 - Problem Solving Methods in the subject of Artificial Intelligence
Constraint Propagation is a problem-solving technique used in Artificial Intelligence to solve constraint satisfaction problems (CSPs) by reducing the number of possible values for variables. It involves iteratively applying constraints to reduce the search space and improve the efficiency of the solution. The process continues until either a solution is found or it is determined that no solution exists. Constraint Propagation is often used in combination with other search techniques, such as backtracking, to find a solution to a CSP.
