### First Order Predicate Logic for the notes of the Unit 3 - Knowledge Representation in the subject of Artificial Intelligence
First Order Predicate Logic (FOPL) is a formal language for representing knowledge and reasoning in AI. It consists of:
1) Predicates (e.g. "is a person", "is taller than")
2) Variables (e.g. "x", "y")
3) Terms (constants or variables)
4) Quantifiers (e.g. "for all", "there exists")
5) Sentences (formed using predicates, variables, terms, and quantifiers)
FOPL allows us to express complex relationships between entities and make inferences based on those relationships. It is widely used in AI for knowledge representation, theorem proving, and automated reasoning.
