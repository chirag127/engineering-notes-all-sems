### Uninformed for the notes of the Unit 2 - Problem Solving Methods in the subject of Artificial Intelligence
Uninformed search is a problem-solving method in Artificial Intelligence where the search algorithm does not have any information about the problem other than the initial state and the goal state. It is also known as blind search. The algorithm explores the search space systematically without having any prior knowledge of the solution. Examples of uninformed search algorithms include Breadth-First Search (BFS), Depth-First Search (DFS), and Uniform Cost Search (UCS). These algorithms are used to find a solution to a problem by searching through a tree or graph data structure. They differ in the order in which they explore the nodes in the search space and the way they handle repeated states. Uninformed search algorithms are used in AI applications such as game playing, pathfinding, and constraint satisfaction problems.
