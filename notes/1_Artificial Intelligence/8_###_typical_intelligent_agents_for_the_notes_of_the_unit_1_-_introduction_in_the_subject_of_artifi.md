### Typical Intelligent Agents for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction in the subject of Artificial Intelligence
Intelligent agents are computer systems that can perform tasks autonomously and adapt to changing environments. They can be simple rule-based systems or complex systems that use machine learning algorithms.

1. Simple reflex agents: respond to stimuli based on predefined rules
2. Model-based agents: maintain an internal model of the environment and use it to make decisions
3. Goal-based agents: have specific goals and take actions to achieve them
4. Utility-based agents: make decisions based on the expected utility of each action
5. Learning agents: improve their performance over time through experience.

Each type of agent has its own strengths and weaknesses and can be used in different applications, such as game playing, robotics, and information retrieval.
