### Game Playing for the notes of the Unit 2 - Problem Solving Methods in the subject of Artificial Intelligence
Game Playing in Artificial Intelligence:

- It involves creating AI agents that can play games such as chess, tic-tac-toe, Go, etc.
- Game playing can be seen as a problem-solving task, where the AI agent must find the best move to win the game.
- Popular approaches include Minimax, Alpha-Beta Pruning, Monte Carlo Tree Search, etc.
- Minimax is a decision-making algorithm that considers all possible moves and countermoves by both the AI agent and its opponent.
- Alpha-Beta Pruning is an optimization technique used in Minimax to reduce the number of nodes that need to be evaluated.
- Monte Carlo Tree Search is a more recent approach that uses random sampling to evaluate the potential outcomes of moves.
- Game playing can also provide a test bed for AI research, helping to advance the field by pushing the limits of AI algorithms.
