### Stochastic Games for the notes of the Unit 2 - Problem Solving Methods in the subject of Artificial Intelligence
Stochastic games are multi-player, multi-stage decision making problems where the outcome of each player's actions depends on the actions of all players. They are used to model situations where multiple agents interact and make decisions based on the current state of the game. The game has a set of states, actions, and transition probabilities that determine the outcome of each player's actions. The objective of each player is to maximize their reward or utility. The solution to a stochastic game is a Nash Equilibrium, which is a set of strategies for each player such that no player has an incentive to deviate from their strategy. Stochastic games have applications in various fields, including economics, game theory, and artificial intelligence.
