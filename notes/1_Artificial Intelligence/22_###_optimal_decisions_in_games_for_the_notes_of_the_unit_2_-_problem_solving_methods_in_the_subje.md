### Optimal Decisions in Games for the notes of the Unit 2 - Problem Solving Methods in the subject of Artificial Intelligence
Optimal Decisions in Games:
1. Game theory: Study of mathematical models of conflict and cooperation between intelligent rational decision-makers.
2. Nash Equilibrium: A state in which each player's strategy is the best response to the others' strategies.
3. Minimax algorithm: A decision rule used in game theory to find the optimal move for a player assuming the opponent will play optimally.
4. Alpha-beta pruning: An optimization technique for minimax algorithm to reduce the number of nodes evaluated.
5. Monte Carlo Tree Search: A heuristic search algorithm used in game AI to find the optimal move by simulating random playouts.
