## Unit 1 - Introduction
Unit 1 - Introduction is typically the first unit of a course or curriculum. Its purpose is to provide an overview of the topic and set the stage for what will be covered in the rest of the course. The introduction should include a brief history of the subject, its significance, and the objectives of the course. It should also provide an outline of the course structure and the topics that will be covered in each unit. The introduction should be engaging and provide context for the learner, helping them understand why the subject is important and how it relates to their lives or interests.
