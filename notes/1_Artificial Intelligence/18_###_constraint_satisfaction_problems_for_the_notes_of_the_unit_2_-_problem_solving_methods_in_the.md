### Constraint Satisfaction Problems for the notes of the Unit 2 - Problem Solving Methods in the subject of Artificial Intelligence
Constraint Satisfaction Problems (CSPs) are mathematical problems where variables are subject to constraints. These problems are used in Artificial Intelligence to model and solve real-world problems. CSPs are defined by a set of variables, each with a domain of possible values, and a set of constraints that restrict the values that the variables can take. The goal is to find an assignment of values to the variables that satisfies all the constraints. CSPs can be solved using techniques such as backtracking, forward checking, and arc consistency. These techniques involve searching through the solution space to find a solution that satisfies all the constraints. CSPs are widely used in areas such as scheduling, planning, and resource allocation.
