### Search Strategies for the notes of the Unit 2 - Problem Solving Methods in the subject of Artificial Intelligence
1. Keyword search: Use relevant keywords related to the topic to search through class notes, textbooks, and online resources.
2. Concept mapping: Create a visual representation of the topics and sub-topics to identify patterns and connections.
3. Mind mapping: Similar to concept mapping but allows for more free-form association and brainstorming.
4. Outline creation: Organize information into a hierarchical structure to identify main ideas and supporting details.
5. Divide and conquer: Break down complex problems into smaller, manageable parts to solve them more easily.
6. Abstraction: Identify the most important information and abstract it to a higher level of understanding.
7. Review and summarization: Regularly review notes and summarize the most important information to reinforce understanding.
