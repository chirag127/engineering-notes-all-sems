### Characteristics of Intelligent Agents for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction in the subject of Artificial Intelligence
1. Autonomy: Acts independently without human intervention.
2. Reactivity: Responds to changes in environment.
3. Proactivity: Takes initiative to achieve goals.
4. Learning: Improves performance through experience.
5. Social ability: Interacts with other agents and humans.
6. Bounded rationality: Makes decisions based on limited information.
7. Flexibility: Can adapt to changing circumstances.
