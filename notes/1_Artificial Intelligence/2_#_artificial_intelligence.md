# Artificial Intelligence
Artificial Intelligence (AI) refers to the simulation of human intelligence in machines that are designed to think and act like humans. These machines can be trained to perform tasks that typically require human intelligence, such as visual perception, speech recognition, decision-making, and language translation. AI can be classified into two categories: narrow or weak AI, which is designed to perform a specific task, and general or strong AI, which has the ability to perform any intellectual task that a human can. AI is achieved through various techniques including machine learning, deep learning, and natural language processing. AI has a wide range of applications, including self-driving cars, virtual personal assistants, and medical diagnosis. However, it also raises ethical concerns regarding job displacement and the potential misuse of AI technologies.
