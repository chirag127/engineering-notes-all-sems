## Unit 2 - Problem Solving Methods
Unit 2 focuses on Problem Solving Methods, which are systematic approaches to finding solutions to problems. These methods can be divided into two main categories: algorithmic and heuristic. Algorithmic methods follow a set of rules to find a solution, while heuristic methods use intuition and experience to find a solution. Common problem solving methods include trial and error, brainstorming, and root cause analysis. Effective problem solving requires clear definition of the problem, analysis of the situation, generation of alternative solutions, and evaluation of the solutions to select the best one.
