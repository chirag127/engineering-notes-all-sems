### Documentation in Requirement Engineering Process in SRS

The requirement engineering process results in many artifacts or documents. Proper documentation of these artifacts is essential for the success of the software project. Some of the key documents generated during requirement engineering and their importance are:

1. **Business Case Document**: It captures the business objectives and benefits of the proposed system. It is used to justify the investment in the project and judge the success of the project.
2. **Project Charter**: It formally authorizes the project and assigns resources to it. It includes information like scope, objectives, risks, constraints, etc.
3. **Vision and Scope Document**: It describes the vision and high-level requirements of the system. It includes information like purpose, features, and capabilities of the system.
4. **Use Case Diagrams**: They graphically capture the interaction between system and actors. They provide an overview of functional requirements and the scope of the system.
5. **Use Cases**: They capture the behavioral requirements of the system. They describe the interactions between the system and actors to achieve a goal.
6. **Domain Model**: It depicts the key concepts and relationships in the problem domain. It is a structural representation of the vocabulary associated with the problem domain.
7. **Data Models**: They show how data will be structured and organized in the system. They specify data entities and relationships between them. E.g. Entity-Relationship Diagrams.
8. **Activity Diagrams**: They depict the flow of control or workflow in the system. They are useful in visualizing the dynamic aspects of the system.
9. **Sequence Diagrams**: They capture the interaction between objects or system components. They show the order of messages exchanged between the objects and are useful in elaborating use cases.
10. **State Transition Diagrams**: They show the different states of an object or system and the transitions between those states. They are useful in specifying reactive systems.

The above documents/diagrams help in understanding and communicating the requirements efficiently. They also serve as input to the next phase of software development. Appropriate mnemonics and learning tricks can be devised based on the first letter of each document/diagram for easy remembrance. However, they should only be used if they are easy to remember.
