## Unit 1 - Introduction to Software Engineering

- Software Engineering is the systematic application of engineering approaches to software development. It involves a set of activities to develop and maintain software.
- The key activities in the software engineering process are: Requirements Analysis, Design, Implementation, Testing, and Deployment.
- Requirements Analysis: Determining what the customer wants and needs. Eliciting requirements from customers, analyzing requirements, and reviewing requirements.
- Design: Coming up with a design that satisfies the requirements. Includes architectural design, interface design, component-level design, and data structure design.
- Implementation: Implementing the design in a programming language.
- Testing: Testing the software to identify and fix bugs or defects. Includes unit testing, integration testing, and system testing.
- Deployment: Releasing the software and making it accessible to users. Includes installation, configuration, integration, and migration.

**Mnemonics:**
- `READY - Requirements, Design, Implementation, Testing, Deployment`
- `RAD - Rapid Application Development` methodology which emphasizes on building prototypes and iterative development.

**Advantages of Software Engineering:**
- Increased productivity and quality
- Reduced development and maintenance costs
- More reliable software
- Standardized process and methods

**Disadvantages of Software Engineering:**
- Inflexibility due to strict process
- Difficulty in estimating costs and time
- Requirement changes can be difficult to accommodate
