### Walk Through (Walkthrough) Static testing strategy

Walkthrough static testing strategy involves:

- Step-by-step execution of the program/system without actually running it.
- Checking for errors and design flaws while simulating execution.
- Led by a moderator through a series of sessions involving representatives from development and user areas.
- The representatives examine the product for conformance to specifications, standards and objectives.
- They analyse the product from a user's perspective and point out any difficulties, inaccuracies or other problems.

**Advantages:**

- Inexpensive and easy to perform.
- Non-technical staff can participate.
- Gives direct user feedback.

**Disadvantages:**

- Subjective and depends on individuals involved.
- May miss logical errors or timing/load related bugs.
- Not systematic and thus certain aspects can be overlooked.

**Mnemonics:**

- Think of walkthrough as "walking" step-by-step on paper.
- Users' "views" are important (user feedback).

**Learning tricks:**

- Imagine yourself as different users and step through the system. Try to find usability issues and explore different paths.
- Question everything and don't take things at face value. A fresh perspective can reveal more issues.

**Examples:**

- Walking through a process flow diagram.
- Simulating user actions on a prototype and discussing issues.

**Applications:**

- Requirements validation.
- Peer reviews of design.
- Training material reviews.
- Usability evaluations.
