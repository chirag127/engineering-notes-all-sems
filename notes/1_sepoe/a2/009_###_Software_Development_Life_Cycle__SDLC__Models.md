### Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC) Models

The Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC) is a process used by the software industry to design, develop and test high quality softwares. The SDLC aims to produce a high-quality software that meets or exceeds customer expectations, reaches completion within times and cost estimates.

There are several SDLC models followed in the industry:

1. Waterfall Model: This model follows a sequential design process, where progress is seen as flowing steadily downwards (like a waterfall) through the phases of requirements analysis, design, implementation, testing and deployment. The waterfall model is ideal for projects where requirements are very well understood. Disadvantages include difficulty in accommodating changes and lack of incremental releases.

Mnemonic: **RIDIOT - Requirements, Design, Implementation, Testing, Operation, Termination**

2. Iterative Model: This model focuses on repetitive iterations of software development lifecycle phases. Each iteration passes through the requirements, design, implementation and testing phases. Iterations continue until the software meets the necessary requirements. This model is flexible and can accommodate changes, but requires rigorous planning and project management to implement successfully.

3. Spiral Model: This model of SDLC combines the features of iterative and waterfall models. The spiral lifecycle model has four quadrants: planning, risk analysis, engineering and evaluation. The process passes through each quadrant in a spiral, allowing for incremental releases of the software. This model is preferred for large, expensive and complicated projects.

4. Agile Model: Agile methodology focuses on customer satisfaction and rapid, continual delivery of working software. Some popular agile frameworks are Scrum, Kanban and XP (Extreme Programming). Agile models promote teamwork, flexibility and rapid response to changes. However, they require experienced teams and do not work well for projects with clearly defined requirements.
