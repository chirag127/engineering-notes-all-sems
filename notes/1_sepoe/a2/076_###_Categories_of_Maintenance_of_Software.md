### Categories of Maintenance of Software

The following are the major categories of software maintenance:

1. Corrective Maintenance: Fixing errors or bugs that are detected in the software. This is the most common type of maintenance. The errors may be in the form of invalid outputs or flaws in the program logic. Corrective maintenance aims to locate and rectify the errors.

2. Adaptive Maintenance: Modifying the software to accommodate changes to its external environment. This includes changes to operating systems, hardware, or other interfaces. The software is adapted to operate in the changed environment.

3. Perfective Maintenance: Refining the software after it has been in operational use. This involves modifications to improve performance, maintainability, or other attributes of the software. It does not involve fixing errors but enhances the quality of the software.

4. Preventive Maintenance: Making modifications to software to prevent potential errors or failures. This involves analyzing the software and making changes to remove latent faults or weaknesses to reduce the likelihood of failures in the future.

**Mnemonic:** CAPP - Corrective, Adaptive, Perfective, Preventive

**Note:** The changes made in software maintenance need to be well documented for tracking modifications and maintaining integrity of the software. Proper testing needs to be done after any maintenance to ensure the software is working as desired.
