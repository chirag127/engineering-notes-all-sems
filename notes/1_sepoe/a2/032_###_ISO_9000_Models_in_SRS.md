### ISO 9000 Models in SRS

The ISO 9000 family of standards relates to quality management systems and is designed to help organizations ensure that they meet the needs of customers and other stakeholders. The ISO 9001 standard is the most popular standard in the ISO 9000 series and focuses on the requirements that organizations need to fulfill to demonstrate the ability to consistently provide products and services that meet customer and regulatory requirements.

The ISO 9000 standards are implemented using a process approach, evaluating the effectiveness of the processes, and continually improving them based on feedback. The ISO 9001:2015 standard follows the Plan-Do-Check-Act cycle and requires organizations to:

- Plan: establish the quality management system and its processes
- Do: implement the quality management system and its processes
- Check: monitor, measure, analyze, and evaluate the performance of the quality management system and its processes
- Act: take actions to improve the effectiveness of the quality management system and its processes

Some key highlights and requirements of ISO 9001:2015 are:

- Focus on risk-based thinking and risk management
- Increased emphasis on leadership engagement
- Consideration of interested parties and their requirements
- More focus on organizational knowledge
- Alignment with other key management system standards

The standard is intended to be applicable to any organization, regardless of size, type, or industry. It is a tool used by organizations to ensure that their products and services meet the needs of customers and other stakeholders. By adhering to the requirements of ISO 9001, organizations can demonstrate the ability to consistently provide conforming products and services and continually improve.
