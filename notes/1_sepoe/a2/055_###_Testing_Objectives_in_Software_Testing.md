### Testing Objectives in Software Testing

The primary objectives of software testing are:

1. To identify bugs, defects or faults in the software.

- Mnemonic: The FIT test - Find defects, Identify issues, Troubleshoot problems.

2. To ensure that the software meets its functional and non-functional requirements.

- Checking if the software provides the desired functionality and performance as per specifications.

3. To improve the quality of the software and provide a quality assurance.

- Software testing provides an objective, independent view of the software to allow the business to appreciate and understand the risks of software implementation.

4. To facilitate acceptance of the software.

- Through testing, the software is checked against the business requirements to enable the client to accept the software.

The secondary objectives of software testing include:

- Preventing defects by reviewing/inspecting software components early in the development cycle.
- Providing information to stakeholders on the status and risk of projects.
- Enabling the management to make decisions regarding software development and deployment.
- Increasing user satisfaction by providing software that meets functional and performance expectations.
