### Information Modelling in Software Requirement Specification (SRS)

- Information Modelling is a technique used in Software Requirement Specification (SRS) to represent the data requirements of the software to be developed.
- It involves identifying the key information entities and relationships between them. These entities become the data objects or data models in the software.
- The key information entities are identified from the problem statement and user requirements. They are represented in the form of entity-relationship diagrams or object diagrams.
- The relationships between the information entities are identified and represented in the diagrams. These could be 1:1, 1:M or M:N relationships.
- Mnemonic: "IMPLEMENTS" - Information, Models, Properties, Links, Entities, Attributes, Names, Types, Semantics
- Advantages: Provides a visual representation of data and relationships which is easy to understand. Facilitates identifying attributes and relationships. Avoids redundancies and inconsistencies in data representation.
- Disadvantages: Can become complex for large systems. Require skill to create accurate diagrams. Tool support may be required for complex diagrams.
- Applications: Widely used in database modelling, object-oriented modelling, etc. Forms an important part of SRS and database design.
- Examples: Entity-Relationship Diagram for a College Management System showing entities like Student, Course, Faculty, etc. and relationships between them.
