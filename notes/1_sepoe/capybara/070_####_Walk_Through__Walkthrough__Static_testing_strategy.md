#### Walk Through (Walkthrough) Static Testing Strategy

Walkthrough is a type of static testing strategy that is used to review software documents like requirements, design, code, and test cases. In this method, a group of people including developers, testers, and other stakeholders review the document and provide feedback.

##### Steps involved in Walkthrough Static Testing Strategy:

1. Planning: In this phase, the document to be reviewed is identified, and the participants are selected. The review objectives and guidelines are also defined.

2. Preparation: The participants of the review study the document beforehand and make a list of issues or questions they want to discuss during the review.

3. Review meeting: The review meeting is conducted where the participants discuss the document and provide feedback. The feedback is recorded and later used to make necessary changes in the document.

4. Rework: The document is modified based on the feedback received during the review meeting.

5. Follow-up: The follow-up phase involves verifying if the changes made to the document are satisfactory and meet the review objectives.

##### Advantages of Walkthrough Static Testing Strategy:

- Helps to identify defects early in the software development life cycle.
- Improves the quality of the software document and reduces the chances of errors in the final product.
- Enhances communication and collaboration among team members.
- Helps to share knowledge and experience among team members.

##### Disadvantages of Walkthrough Static Testing Strategy:

- Can be time-consuming if the document is large and complex.
- May require a lot of effort from team members to prepare for the review meeting.
- May not be effective if the participants lack expertise in the domain.

##### Example of Walkthrough Static Testing Strategy:

Suppose a team of developers is working on a software project to develop an e-commerce website. The team conducts a walkthrough of the requirements document to ensure that all the necessary features are included and the requirements are clear and complete.

##### Application of Walkthrough Static Testing Strategy:

Walkthrough is widely used in software development projects to review documents like requirements, design, code, and test cases. It can be used in any stage of the software development life cycle to improve the quality of the software product.
