#### Perfective Maintenance (PM) of Software

Perfective Maintenance (PM) is a type of software maintenance that focuses on improving the quality, performance, and functionality of the software. It involves making changes to the software that are not related to fixing bugs or errors. The main goal of perfective maintenance is to enhance the software's capabilities and make it more efficient and effective.

Here are some important points to remember about perfective maintenance:

- Perfective maintenance involves making changes to the software to improve its functionality, performance, and quality.
- PM is not concerned with fixing bugs or errors in the software.
- It aims to enhance the software's capabilities and make it more efficient and effective.
- PM can be performed on both new and existing software.
- The changes made during PM are usually based on the feedback received from users or stakeholders.
- The changes made during PM may involve adding new features, improving the user interface, or optimizing the code to make it more efficient.
- PM can help to extend the life of the software by making it more up-to-date and relevant to changing needs.
- The cost of PM may be lower than the cost of developing new software from scratch.

Here are some mnemonics and learning tricks that can help you remember the key points about perfective maintenance:

- PM is like a performance upgrade for your car. Just as you might upgrade your car's engine or tires to improve its performance, PM involves upgrading the software to improve its functionality and performance.
- PM is like a facelift for your software. Just as a facelift can make a person look younger and more attractive, PM can make the software look and function better.

Overall, perfective maintenance is an important aspect of software development that can help to improve the quality, performance, and functionality of software. By making changes to the software that are not related to fixing bugs or errors, PM can help to enhance the software's capabilities and extend its life.
