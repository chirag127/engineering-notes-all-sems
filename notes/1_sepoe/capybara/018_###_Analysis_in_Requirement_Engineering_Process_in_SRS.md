### Analysis in Requirement Engineering Process in SRS

Requirement analysis is an essential part of the requirement engineering process in software development. It is the process of identifying, understanding, and documenting the requirements of a software system. Requirements analysis is a crucial step in the software development process as it helps in ensuring that the software meets the needs of its users and stakeholders.

#### Mnemonic

One useful mnemonic to remember the steps involved in requirements analysis is the acronym FAST, which stands for:

- **F**unctionality: What the system should do
- **A**pplication domain: The context in which the system will be used
- **S**takeholders: The people who will use the system, and their needs
- **T**echnology: The tools and platforms that will be used to build the system

#### Steps involved in requirements analysis

The following are the steps involved in the requirements analysis process:

1. **Elicitation:** This involves gathering information about the requirements from various sources such as stakeholders, users, and existing documentation. Techniques such as interviews, surveys, and focus groups can be used to gather this information.

2. **Analysis:** This involves analyzing the information gathered during the elicitation process to identify the functional and non-functional requirements of the system.

3. **Specification:** This involves documenting the requirements in a clear and concise manner. The requirements should be specified in a way that is understandable to all stakeholders.

4. **Validation:** This involves ensuring that the requirements are complete, consistent, and feasible. This can be done through techniques such as peer reviews and prototyping.

5. **Management:** This involves managing the requirements throughout the software development process. This includes tracking changes to the requirements, prioritizing requirements, and ensuring that the requirements are met.

#### Advantages of requirements analysis

Some of the advantages of requirements analysis include:

- Helps in ensuring that the software meets the needs of its users and stakeholders
- Helps in identifying potential problems early in the software development process
- Helps in reducing the risk of project failure
- Helps in improving communication between stakeholders and the development team
- Helps in reducing development time and cost

#### Disadvantages of requirements analysis

Some of the disadvantages of requirements analysis include:

- Can be time-consuming and costly
- Can be difficult to gather complete and accurate requirements
- Can be difficult to manage changing requirements
- Can be difficult to balance conflicting requirements from different stakeholders

#### Example

For example, consider a requirement analysis for a banking software system. The functionality requirements may include features such as account management, transaction processing, and customer support. The non-functional requirements may include performance, security, and usability. The stakeholders may include customers, bank employees, and regulatory agencies. The technology requirements may include the use of a particular programming language, database system, or operating system.

#### Applications

Requirement analysis is a critical process in software development and is used in various industries such as healthcare, finance, and retail. It is used to ensure that software systems meet the needs of their users and stakeholders and to reduce the risk of project failure.
