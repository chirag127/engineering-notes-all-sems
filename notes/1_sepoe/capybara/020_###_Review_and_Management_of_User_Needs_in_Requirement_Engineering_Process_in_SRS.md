### Review and Management of User Needs in Requirement Engineering Process in SRS

Requirement engineering is a crucial part of software development, and one of its critical components is the review and management of user needs. The success of a software project heavily relies on understanding and meeting the needs of its stakeholders. Here are some points that will help you understand how to review and manage user needs in the requirement engineering process in SRS:

1. **Gather and analyze user needs:** The first step in the process is to gather and analyze user needs. The requirements should be collected from all stakeholders, including end-users, customers, and technical experts. A clear understanding of the user's requirements will help in developing an SRS that meets their needs.

2. **Document user needs:** Once the user needs are analyzed, they should be documented in an SRS document. The document should be clear and concise, and it should accurately reflect the user's needs. The SRS document should be reviewed by all stakeholders to ensure that it meets the requirements.

3. **Validate user needs:** The next step is to validate user needs. This can be done through reviews, walkthroughs, and inspections. The validation process ensures that the SRS document accurately reflects the user's needs and that there are no gaps or errors in the document.

4. **Manage changes to user needs:** During the development process, changes may need to be made to the user's needs. These changes should be managed carefully to ensure that they do not impact the overall project. Changes should be documented and reviewed by all stakeholders to ensure that they are valid and necessary.

5. **Ensure traceability of user needs:** It is essential to ensure traceability of user needs throughout the development process. This means that the SRS document should be linked to other project artifacts such as design documents, code, and test cases. Traceability ensures that the development process is aligned with the user's needs.

Mnemonic/learning trick:
One possible mnemonic to remember the above points is "GVDMT" which stands for Gather, Validate, Document, Manage, and Trace. This helps in remembering the key steps involved in reviewing and managing user needs in the requirement engineering process in SRS.
