
## Unit 1 - Introduction to Software Engineering

* Software engineering is the application of engineering principles to the design, development, maintenance, testing, and evaluation of software and systems. 
* It is an interdisciplinary field that combines the principles of computer science, mathematics, and engineering to create reliable and efficient software systems. 
* Software engineering focuses on the development of software products that are reliable, maintainable, and cost-effective.
* It is a process-oriented approach to software development that involves the use of well-defined and repeatable processes to ensure the quality of the software product. 
* The goal of software engineering is to create software that meets the requirements of the customer and is of high quality.
* The process of software engineering includes requirements gathering, design, coding, testing, maintenance, and documentation. 
* Requirements gathering is the process of gathering information about the software product from the customer and other stakeholders. 
* Design is the process of creating a model of the software product that meets the customer's requirements. 
* Coding is the process of writing the code to implement the design. 
* Testing is the process of ensuring that the software product meets the customer's requirements. 
* Maintenance is the process of updating and modifying the software product to keep it up to date. 
* Documentation is the process of creating documents that describe the software product and its use. 
* Software engineering is an important field in the modern world as it enables the development of reliable and efficient software products.
