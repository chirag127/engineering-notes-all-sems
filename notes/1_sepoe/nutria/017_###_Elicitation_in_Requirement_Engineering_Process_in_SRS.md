
### Elicitation in Requirement Engineering Process in SRS

Elicitation is a process used in requirement engineering to identify, understand, and document the requirements of the stakeholders of a system. This process involves the use of techniques such as interviews, questionnaires, observations, document analysis, and workshops. The main goal of elicitation is to ensure that the requirements of the stakeholders are accurately captured and documented.

**Techniques used in Elicitation:**

1. Interviews: Interviews are a common technique used to elicit requirements from stakeholders. They can be conducted in person, over the phone, or through video conferencing. During the interview, the interviewer asks questions that help to identify and understand the stakeholders' needs.

2. Questionnaires: Questionnaires are another popular technique used to elicit requirements. They can be used to gather information from a larger group of stakeholders in a short period of time. The questions should be designed to elicit the necessary information in a clear and concise manner.

3. Observations: Observations involve observing the stakeholders in their natural environment. This technique can be used to identify the tasks that the stakeholders perform and the tools they use to complete those tasks.

4. Document Analysis: Document analysis is a technique used to identify and understand the requirements from existing documents. This technique involves examining existing documents such as user manuals, system specifications, and other related documents.

5. Workshops: Workshops are a great way to elicit requirements from stakeholders. They provide an opportunity for stakeholders to discuss their requirements in detail and to identify any conflicts or inconsistencies.

**Mnemonics and Learning Tricks:**

1. E-E-D: Elicitation, Evaluation, and Documentation. This is a helpful mnemonic to remember the three main steps of the elicitation process.

2. Ask the Right Questions: It is important to ask the right questions during the elicitation process. This will help to ensure that all of the necessary information is gathered.

3. Listen Carefully: Listening carefully to the stakeholders is essential for understanding their requirements. It is important to take the time to listen and ask clarifying questions.

4. Document Everything: Documenting the requirements is an important step in the elicitation process. It is important to document all of the requirements accurately and in detail.
