
### Documentation in Requirement Engineering Process in SRS

1. Documentation in Requirement Engineering Process is a method used to ensure that all the requirements of a project are understood, documented and agreed upon by the stakeholders.
2. Documentation is an important part of the software development process and helps to ensure that the project is completed on time and within budget.
3. It is used to ensure that all stakeholders understand the requirements of the project, and that any changes to the requirements are documented and agreed upon.
4. The process typically involves gathering and analyzing the requirements from the stakeholders, documenting the requirements, and then validating the requirements with the stakeholders.
5. The goal of the process is to ensure that all stakeholders are in agreement on the requirements and that the project is completed according to the agreed upon requirements.
6. Mnemonics can be used to help remember the steps of the process, such as:
    - Gather (G): Gather the requirements from the stakeholders.
    - Analyze (A): Analyze the requirements to ensure they are complete and correct.
    - Document (D): Document the requirements in a clear and concise manner.
    - Validate (V): Validate the requirements with the stakeholders.
7. Advantages of the documentation process include:
    - Ensures that all stakeholders understand the requirements.
    - Helps to ensure that the project is completed according to the agreed upon requirements.
    - Prevents misunderstandings between stakeholders.
    - Increases the efficiency of the software development process.
8. Disadvantages of the documentation process include:
    - Can be time consuming.
    - Can be costly.
    - Can be difficult to keep up with changes in the requirements.
9. Examples of documentation in requirement engineering process include:
    - Use cases.
    - User stories.
    - Functional requirements.
    - Non-functional requirements.
    - System diagrams.
10. Applications of the documentation process include:
    - Software development.
    - Business process improvement.
    - System design and implementation.
