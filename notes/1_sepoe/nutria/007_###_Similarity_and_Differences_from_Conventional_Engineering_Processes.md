
### Similarity and Differences from Conventional Engineering Processes

* Conventional engineering processes involve the development of a product from start to finish, while Agile engineering processes involve continual development and improvement of the product over time.

* Conventional engineering processes rely heavily on upfront planning and design, while Agile engineering processes focus on delivering value to the customer quickly and iteratively.

* Conventional engineering processes are often characterized by long development cycles and large teams, while Agile engineering processes are characterized by shorter development cycles and smaller, cross-functional teams.

* Conventional engineering processes typically require detailed documentation and specifications, while Agile engineering processes rely more on oral communication and collaboration.

* Conventional engineering processes often involve a linear, sequential workflow, while Agile engineering processes involve an iterative, adaptive workflow.

* Conventional engineering processes are often more rigid and structured, while Agile engineering processes are more flexible and open to change.

* Conventional engineering processes often require extensive testing and validation, while Agile engineering processes focus on rapid feedback and continual improvement.

* Conventional engineering processes often involve a single team working on a single product, while Agile engineering processes involve multiple teams working on multiple products.
