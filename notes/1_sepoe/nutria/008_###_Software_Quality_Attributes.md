

### Software Quality Attributes

Software Quality Attributes are characteristics of a software system that are used to measure its overall quality. These attributes include: 

* **Reliability**: The ability of a system to perform its required functions correctly and consistently over time.
* **Availability**: The ability of a system to be available when needed.
* **Usability**: The ability of a system to be used by its intended users with minimal difficulty.
* **Maintainability**: The ability of a system to be modified and updated over time to meet changing requirements.
* **Security**: The ability of a system to protect its data and resources from unauthorized access.
* **Performance**: The ability of a system to meet its performance requirements.
* **Scalability**: The ability of a system to grow and adapt to changing user or system demands.
* **Testability**: The ability of a system to be tested in order to verify its correctness.

To remember these attributes, you can use the mnemonic **RUMSPS** (Reliability, Usability, Maintainability, Security, Performance, Scalability, Testability).
