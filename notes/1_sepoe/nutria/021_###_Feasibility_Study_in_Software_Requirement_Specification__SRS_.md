

### Feasibility Study in Software Requirement Specification (SRS)
A feasibility study in software requirement specification (SRS) is a process of assessing the viability of a proposed software system before it is developed. It is an important step in the software development process, as it helps to identify potential problems, risks, and opportunities that may arise during the development process.

The goal of a feasibility study is to determine if a proposed software system is feasible, practical, and cost-effective. The study will assess the technical, financial, and operational aspects of the proposed system and provide an analysis of the risks and benefits associated with the project.

The feasibility study should include a review of the existing system, the proposed system, and the resources required to complete the project. It should also include an analysis of the expected costs and benefits associated with the proposed system, as well as an assessment of the risks associated with the project.

The feasibility study should also include a review of the proposed system's user requirements, technical requirements, and system architecture. This will help to identify any potential issues or problems that may arise during the development process.
