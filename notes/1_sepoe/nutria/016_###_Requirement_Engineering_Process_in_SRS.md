
### Requirement Engineering Process in SRS

- Requirement engineering is the process of understanding and formalizing the requirements of a system. It involves gathering, analyzing, and documenting the needs of the stakeholders and users of the system.
- Requirements engineering is an iterative process which begins with the identification of stakeholders and their needs and requirements, followed by the analysis and specification of system requirements.
- The goal of the process is to create a set of requirements that can be used to guide the design, development, and implementation of a system that meets the needs of the stakeholders.
- Requirements engineering includes the following steps:
    - **Requirements Elicitation:** This step involves gathering the requirements from the stakeholders and users of the system. It includes activities such as interviews, focus groups, surveys, and document analysis.
    - **Requirements Analysis:** This step involves analyzing the requirements to identify any inconsistencies, ambiguities, and omissions. This can be done using techniques such as flowcharts, data flow diagrams, and use cases.
    - **Requirements Specification:** This step involves documenting the requirements in a formal language. This can be done using techniques such as natural language, structured English, and formal specifications.
    - **Requirements Validation:** This step involves validating the requirements to ensure that they are complete, consistent, and correct. This can be done using techniques such as prototyping and simulations.

- Mnemonics and learning tricks for the Requirement Engineering Process in SRS:
    - **R**equirements **E**licitation, **A**nalysis, **S**pecification, and **V**alidation (**REASV**).
    - **G**ather, **A**nalyze, **S**pecify, and **V**erify (**GASV**).
