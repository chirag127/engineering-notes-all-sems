
### ISO 9000 Models in SRS

ISO 9000 is a set of international standards that define quality management systems. It is a family of standards that provide guidance and tools for companies and organizations who want to ensure that their products and services meet the needs of their customers and other stakeholders while meeting statutory and regulatory requirements related to the product.

The ISO 9000 family of standards includes the following models:

* ISO 9000:2015 – Quality management systems – Fundamentals and vocabulary
* ISO 9001:2015 – Quality management systems – Requirements
* ISO 9004:2018 – Quality management systems – Guidance for performance improvement
* ISO 19011:2018 – Guidelines for auditing management systems

The ISO 9000 family of standards is designed to help organizations meet customer requirements and improve their performance. The standards provide a framework for quality management systems and processes, and are based on the principles of customer focus, leadership, engagement of people, process approach, improvement, evidence-based decision making, and relationship management.
