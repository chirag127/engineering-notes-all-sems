
### Evolutionary Development Models in SDLC

Evolutionary development models in software development life cycle (SDLC) are a type of iterative development model that are used to develop software in incremental steps. This type of model is based on the idea that software should be developed in a series of small, incremental steps that are continually tested and refined until the final product is ready.

The main advantage of evolutionary development models is that they allow software engineers to quickly develop and test new features without having to make major changes to the existing codebase. This makes it easier to make changes and fixes quickly, without having to go through a lengthy process of code review and testing.

The main disadvantage of evolutionary development models is that they can lead to code bloat, since each new feature or change is added to the existing codebase. This can lead to a decrease in performance and make the codebase more difficult to maintain.

Mnemonic: EDM-SDLC (Evolutionary Development Models in Software Development Life Cycle)

Learning Trick: The best way to remember evolutionary development models is to think of them as a series of small, incremental steps that are continually tested and refined until the final product is ready. This will help you to remember the main advantages and disadvantages of this type of model.
