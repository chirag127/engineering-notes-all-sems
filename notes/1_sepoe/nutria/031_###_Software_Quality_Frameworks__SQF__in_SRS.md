
### Software Quality Frameworks (SQF) in SRS

Software Quality Frameworks (SQF) are a set of tools and techniques that help to ensure the quality of software products and services. SQF is used to develop and maintain software systems, and to identify and address potential software issues.

The main components of SQF include:

* Quality Assurance (QA): Quality Assurance is the process of ensuring that software meets the customer's requirements. It includes testing, inspection, and review of software products and services.

* Quality Control (QC): Quality Control is the process of monitoring and evaluating the quality of software products and services. It includes testing, inspection, and review of software products and services.

* Quality Metrics: Quality Metrics are measures of software quality, such as defect density, defect severity, and defect rate. These metrics help to identify potential software issues and ensure that software meets the customer's requirements.

* Quality Models: Quality Models are frameworks that help to identify and address potential software issues. They include the Capability Maturity Model (CMM), the ISO/IEC 9126 Quality Model, and the Software Engineering Institute's (SEI) CMMI.

* Quality Standards: Quality Standards are guidelines that help to ensure the quality of software products and services. These standards include the ISO/IEC 9126 Quality Standard, the IEEE/EIA 12207 Standard, and the ISO/IEC 15504 Standard.

Mnemonics and learning tricks for SQF: 

* Quality Assurance (QA): Quality Assurance is Remembering to Always Test and Review.

* Quality Control (QC): Quality Control is Monitoring and Evaluating to Ensure Quality.

* Quality Metrics: Quality Metrics are Measuring Software Quality for Identification and Resolution.

* Quality Models: Quality Models are Frameworks for Identifying and Addressing Potential Software Issues.

* Quality Standards: Quality Standards are Guidelines for Ensuring Quality.
