#### Walk Through (Walkthrough) Static testing strategy

Walkthrough is a popular static testing technique that involves a review of the software product by a group of individuals. The primary objective of this technique is to identify defects and issues in the software early in the development cycle. This technique is widely used in the software industry to improve the quality of software products and reduce the cost of fixing defects.

The walkthrough static testing strategy involves the following steps:

1. Preparation: In this step, the team prepares for the walkthrough by reviewing the documentation and understanding the objectives of the review.

2. Kickoff meeting: The team meets to discuss the objectives of the review and the process of the walkthrough.

3. Review: The team reviews the software product in detail, looking for defects and issues. The team can use checklists or other review techniques to identify defects.

4. Follow-up: The team follows up on the defects and issues identified during the review to ensure that they are addressed.

Advantages of Walk Through Static testing strategy:

- Early detection of defects and issues in the software product.
- Improves the quality of software products.
- Reduces the cost of fixing defects.
- Enhances communication and collaboration between team members.

Disadvantages of Walk Through Static testing strategy:

- Time-consuming process.
- May not detect all defects and issues in the software product.
- Requires a skilled and experienced team.

Mnemonic: A helpful mnemonic to remember the steps involved in Walkthrough static testing strategy is "PKRF" which stands for Preparation, Kickoff meeting, Review, and Follow-up.
