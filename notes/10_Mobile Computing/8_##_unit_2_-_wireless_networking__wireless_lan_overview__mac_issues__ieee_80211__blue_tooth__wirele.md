## Unit 2 - Wireless Networking, Wireless LAN Overview: MAC issues, IEEE 802.11, Blue Tooth, Wireless
Wireless Networking refers to the use of wireless signals to connect computers and devices to each other, and to the internet. It involves the use of wireless access points (APs), which act as gateways between the wireless devices and the wired network.

Wireless LAN Overview:
- A wireless LAN (WLAN) is a local area network that uses wireless communication to connect devices.
- WLANs are often used in homes, offices, and public places to provide internet access.

MAC issues:
- MAC stands for Media Access Control and is the protocol used to control access to the wireless network.
- MAC issues can arise when multiple devices try to access the wireless network at the same time, leading to congestion and slow speeds.

IEEE 802.11:
- IEEE 802.11 is a set of standards for wireless local area networks (WLANs).
- It defines the physical and data link layers of the network and provides specifications for wireless communication.

Bluetooth:
- Bluetooth is a short-range wireless communication technology.
- It is used to connect devices such as headphones, speakers, and smartphones to each other.

Wireless:
- Wireless refers to the use of radio waves to communicate between devices without the use of cables.
- Wireless technology is used in many different types of devices, including smartphones, laptops, and smart home devices.
