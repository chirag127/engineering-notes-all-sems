### multiple access protocols for the notes of the Unit 2 - Wireless Networking, Wireless LAN Overview: MAC issues, IEEE 802.11, Blue Tooth, Wireless in the subject of Mobile Computing
Multiple access protocols are used to manage the communication between multiple devices in a wireless network.

1. MAC (Media Access Control) Issues: In wireless networks, the MAC layer is responsible for controlling access to the shared wireless medium. Issues such as hidden terminals, exposed terminals, and interference can arise in a wireless network, leading to poor performance.

2. IEEE 802.11: This is a popular standard for wireless local area networks (WLANs). It provides a set of protocols for managing the communication between devices in a wireless network, including the MAC layer.

3. Bluetooth: This is a short-range wireless communication technology that is used for connecting devices such as smartphones, laptops, and other devices. It uses a frequency hopping technique to avoid interference and provides a low-power, low-cost solution for wireless communication.

4. Wireless in Mobile Computing: Wireless networks and technologies play a crucial role in mobile computing, enabling devices to connect to the Internet and exchange data without being physically connected. Technologies such as Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and cellular networks are used to support wireless communication in mobile computing.
