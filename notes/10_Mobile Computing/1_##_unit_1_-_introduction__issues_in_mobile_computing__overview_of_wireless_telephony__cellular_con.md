## Unit 1 - Introduction, issues in mobile computing, overview of wireless telephony: cellular concept, GSM:
Unit 1 covers the introduction to mobile computing, including its issues such as security, power consumption, and limited resources. The overview of wireless telephony includes the cellular concept, which is a system of dividing a geographical area into small cells for efficient use of limited radio frequency spectrum. GSM (Global System for Mobile Communications) is the most widely used standard for mobile telephony, providing voice and data services to over 80% of the world's mobile users. It uses a circuit-switched system to connect users and supports multiple access technologies, including 2G, 3G, and 4G.
