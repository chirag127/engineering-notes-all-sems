### channel structure for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction, issues in mobile computing, overview of wireless telephony: cellular concept, GSM: in the subject of Mobile Computing
The channel structure for the notes of Unit 1 in the subject of Mobile Computing should cover the following topics: 
1. Introduction: Definition of mobile computing, its importance, and evolution.
2. Issues in Mobile Computing: Security, Interoperability, Scalability, and Energy Efficiency.
3. Overview of Wireless Telephony: Types of wireless networks and their characteristics.
4. Cellular Concept: Explanation of cell structure, cell splitting, and handoff.
5. GSM (Global System for Mobile communications): Overview of GSM architecture, services, and security.
