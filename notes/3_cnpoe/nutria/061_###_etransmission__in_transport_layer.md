
### Etransmission in Transport Layer

Etransmission is a type of data transmission that occurs in the transport layer of the OSI model. It is a reliable form of communication that ensures the delivery of data packets from one end of the network to the other.

**Advantages:**
- Reliable: Etransmission ensures that the data is delivered from one end of the network to the other.
- Efficient: Data is sent in small packets, which makes the process more efficient.
- Secure: Data is encrypted during transmission, making it secure.

**Disadvantages:**
- Cost: Etransmission requires specialized hardware and software, making it more expensive than other forms of communication.
- Latency: Etransmission can suffer from latency issues due to the need to send data in small packets.

**Mnemonics:**
- R.E.E.D: Reliable, Efficient, Encrypted, Data.

**Applications:**
- Etransmission is used in many applications, including voice and video conferencing, file sharing, streaming media, and more.
