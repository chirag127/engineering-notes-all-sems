

### Connection management in transport layer

* Connection management is a process in the transport layer of the OSI model which establishes and maintains a connection between two applications.
* It is responsible for setting up, managing and terminating the connection.
* Connection management involves the exchange of control information between two end systems, which is known as a handshake.
* The three-way handshake is the most common way of establishing a connection. It consists of a SYN (synchronize) packet, a SYN-ACK (synchronize-acknowledge) packet, and an ACK (acknowledge) packet.
* The connection management protocol provides a reliable connection between the two end systems. It ensures that all packets are delivered in the correct order and that no data is lost during transmission.
* It also ensures that data is transmitted at the correct rate and that there is no congestion on the network.
* Connection management protocols can also be used to provide security for the connection, by encrypting the data and authenticating the end systems.
* Connection management protocols include TCP, UDP, SCTP, and DCCP.
