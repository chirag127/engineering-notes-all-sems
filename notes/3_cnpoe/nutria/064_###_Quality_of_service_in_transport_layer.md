
### Quality of Service in Transport Layer

* Quality of Service (QoS) in the transport layer refers to the ability of a network to provide better service to selected network traffic over various underlying technologies, such as Ethernet, Wi-Fi, or mobile networks.
* QoS is used to prioritize certain types of traffic, such as voice or video, over other types of traffic, such as web surfing or file transfers.
* QoS can be implemented in several ways, including by using different types of traffic management techniques, such as traffic shaping, packet scheduling, and traffic policing.
* QoS can also be implemented using Quality of Service markers, such as Differentiated Services Code Point (DSCP) and Type of Service (ToS).
* QoS can be used to ensure that certain types of traffic are given priority over others, or to limit the amount of bandwidth that certain types of traffic can consume.
* QoS can also be used to provide guaranteed levels of service to certain types of traffic, such as voice or video.
* QoS can be used to provide better service to mission-critical applications, such as real-time voice or video applications, or to ensure that certain applications do not consume all available bandwidth.

Mnemonic: QoS stands for Quality of Service.
