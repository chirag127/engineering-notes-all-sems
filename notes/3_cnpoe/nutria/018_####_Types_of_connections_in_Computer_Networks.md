
#### Types of connections in Computer Networks

1. **Wired Connections**: Wired connections are the physical connections through which data is transmitted between two or more devices. These connections are made using cables, such as coaxial, twisted pair, fiber optic, and USB cables. The most common type of wired connection is Ethernet, which is used to connect computers, printers, and other devices to the same network.

2. **Wireless Connections**: Wireless connections use radio waves to transmit data between two or more devices. The most common type of wireless connection is Wi-Fi, which is used to connect computers, printers, and other devices to the same network. Other types of wireless connections include Bluetooth, cellular networks, and infrared.

3. **Dial-up Connections**: Dial-up connections use telephone lines to transmit data between two or more devices. Dial-up connections are slower than other types of connections and are used mostly for basic internet access.

4. **Broadband Connections**: Broadband connections are high-speed connections used for streaming media, downloading large files, and other activities that require a lot of bandwidth. The most common type of broadband connection is DSL, which is used to connect computers, printers, and other devices to the same network. Other types of broadband connections include cable, fiber optic, and satellite.

5. **Mnemonics and Learning Tricks**: 
- Wired connections: **CAT5** (Coaxial, Twisted Pair, Fiber Optic, USB cables)
- Wireless connections: **Wi-Fi** (Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Cellular networks, Infrared)
- Dial-up connections: **TEL** (Telephone lines)
- Broadband connections: **DSL** (DSL, Cable, Fiber Optic, Satellite)
