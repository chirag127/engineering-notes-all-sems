

### ISP

* Internet Service Providers (ISPs) provide access to the Internet. 
* ISPs provide their customers with access to the Internet through a variety of technologies, such as dial-up, cable, DSL, fiber optics, and wireless. 
* ISPs are responsible for maintaining the network infrastructure that enables customers to access the Internet. 
* ISPs also provide services such as email, web hosting, and domain name registration. 
* ISPs typically charge customers a monthly fee for access to their services. 
* ISPs are regulated by the Federal Communications Commission (FCC). 
* ISPs must provide customers with access to all legal content on the Internet and must not block or filter content. 
* ISPs must also provide customers with access to their own content and services. 
* ISPs must protect customers' privacy and security by providing encryption and other security measures. 
* ISPs must also provide customers with accurate information about their services, such as speed, reliability, and cost. 
* ISPs must also provide customers with customer service and technical support. 
* ISPs must also comply with laws and regulations, such as the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). 
* Mnemonics that can help remember the key points about ISPs include: 
  * I-Internet Service Providers 
  * S-Services, Security, Speed, and Support 
  * P-Privacy, Pricing, and Policies
