
#### Transmission media in Computer Networks

* Transmission media are physical channels or paths through which information is sent from one place to another. 
* Common types of transmission media include twisted pair cables, coaxial cables, optical fibers, and wireless media. 
* Twisted pair cables are the most common type of transmission media used in computer networks. They consist of two insulated copper wires twisted together to reduce electrical interference. 
* Coaxial cables are used in cable television systems and are made up of a single copper wire surrounded by insulation and a metal shield. 
* Optical fibers are thin strands of glass that are used to transmit light signals over long distances. They are used in high-speed networks and are more expensive than other types of transmission media. 
* Wireless media are used to transmit data without the use of cables. Examples of wireless media include radio waves, infrared light, and satellite systems.

* Advantages of transmission media include: 
  * Low cost 
  * High speed 
  * Easy to install 
  * Flexible 

* Disadvantages of transmission media include: 
  * Susceptible to interference 
  * Can be difficult to maintain 
  * Can be expensive 
  * Limited range 

* Examples of applications of transmission media include: 
  * Telecommunication 
  * Broadcasting 
  * Data transmission 
  * Networking
