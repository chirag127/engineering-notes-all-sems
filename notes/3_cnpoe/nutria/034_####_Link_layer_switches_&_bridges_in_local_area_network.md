
#### Link layer switches & bridges in local area network

1. **Switches**: Switches are layer 2 devices that forward data frames based on the MAC address. They are used to connect multiple devices in a local area network (LAN). Switches are also known as multiport bridges.

2. **Bridges**: Bridges are layer 2 devices that forward data frames based on the MAC address. They are used to connect two or more LANs together.

3. **Advantages of switches and bridges**: 
    - Switches and bridges increase the efficiency of the network by reducing collisions and increasing the speed of data transmission. 
    - They also allow for more devices to be connected to the network.
    - Switches and bridges also provide increased security by isolating the network and preventing unauthorized access to the network.

4. **Disadvantages of switches and bridges**: 
    - Switches and bridges can be expensive to purchase and maintain. 
    - They can also be difficult to configure and troubleshoot.

5. **Mnemonics and learning tricks**: 
    - A simple mnemonic to remember the difference between switches and bridges is "SWITCHes connect devices, BRIDGEs connect networks". 
    - Another mnemonic is "SWITCHes are for SWarms, BRIDGEs are for BRoads". 
    - A helpful learning trick is to think of switches as a hub with multiple ports and bridges as a router with two or more ports. 
    - Also, remember that switches forward data frames based on the MAC address, while bridges forward data frames based on the destination IP address.
