
#### Categories of networks in computer networks

1. **Local Area Network (LAN)**: A LAN is a computer network that connects computers and other devices in a relatively small area such as an office building, school, or home. It typically covers a single floor or building, and has a range of up to 10 kilometres.

2. **Metropolitan Area Network (MAN)**: A MAN is a computer network that covers a large geographic area, such as a city or town. It typically covers an area of up to 50 kilometres.

3. **Wide Area Network (WAN)**: A WAN is a computer network that covers a large geographic area, such as a country or even the entire world. It typically covers an area of more than 50 kilometres.

4. **Wireless Local Area Network (WLAN)**: A WLAN is a computer network that uses wireless technology to connect computers and other devices in a relatively small area such as an office building, school, or home.

5. **Wireless Metropolitan Area Network (WMAN)**: A WMAN is a computer network that uses wireless technology to connect computers and other devices in a large geographic area, such as a city or town.

6. **Wireless Wide Area Network (WWAN)**: A WWAN is a computer network that uses wireless technology to connect computers and other devices in a large geographic area, such as a country or even the entire world.

7. **Virtual Private Network (VPN)**: A VPN is a type of private computer network that uses public telecommunication infrastructure, such as the Internet, to provide remote users with secure access to their organization's network.

8. **Storage Area Network (SAN)**: A SAN is a computer network that connects computers and other devices to a shared storage system. It is typically used to provide high-performance, secure, and reliable access to data storage devices.

9. **Cloud Computing**: Cloud computing is a type of computing that uses a network of remote servers hosted on the Internet to store, manage, and process data, rather than a local server or a personal computer.

10. **Mesh Network**: A mesh network is a type of computer network that uses multiple interconnected nodes to share and relay data, rather than relying on a single centralized node.
