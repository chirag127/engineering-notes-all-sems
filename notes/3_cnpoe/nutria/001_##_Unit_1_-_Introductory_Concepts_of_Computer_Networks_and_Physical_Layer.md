
## Unit 1 - Introductory Concepts of Computer Networks and Physical Layer

* Physical layer: This is the lowest layer in the OSI model and is responsible for the transmission of data from one device to another. It is responsible for the physical connection between two devices and for the encoding and decoding of the data being transmitted.
* Data link layer: This layer is responsible for the reliable transmission of data across a physical link. It is responsible for the error-checking and flow control of the data being transmitted.
* Network layer: This layer is responsible for the routing of data across networks. It is responsible for the selection of paths for the data to travel and for the addressing of the data.
* Transport layer: This layer is responsible for the reliable and efficient delivery of data. It is responsible for the segmentation and reassembly of data, as well as the flow control and error-checking of the data.
* Session layer: This layer is responsible for the establishment and maintenance of communication sessions between two devices. It is responsible for the synchronization and management of the data being transmitted.
* Mnemonics and learning tricks:
    * Physical Layer: "Physical Layer Connects Devices"
    * Data Link Layer: "Data Link Layer Error-Checks and Flows"
    * Network Layer: "Network Layer Routes and Addresses"
    * Transport Layer: "Transport Layer Reliably and Efficiently Delivers"
    * Session Layer: "Session Layer Synchronizes and Manages"
