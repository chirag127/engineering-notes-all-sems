
### Point-to-point networks in network layer

Point-to-point networks are a type of computer network topology in which two nodes are connected directly to each other. This type of network is typically used for point-to-point communication between two nodes, such as a computer and a printer, or between two computers. 

Advantages: 

- Point-to-point networks are simple and easy to set up. 
- They are typically faster than other types of networks because they have less overhead. 
- Point-to-point networks are more secure than other types of networks because they are not susceptible to packet sniffing. 

Disadvantages: 

- Point-to-point networks are limited in the number of nodes that can be connected. 
- They are expensive to maintain and upgrade. 
- Point-to-point networks are not scalable and can become congested if too much traffic is sent through them. 

Mnemonic: 

P2P = Point-to-Point Network.
