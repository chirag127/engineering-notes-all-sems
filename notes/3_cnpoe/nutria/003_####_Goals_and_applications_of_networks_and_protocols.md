
##### Goals and applications of networks and protocols

1. Networking is a process of connecting two or more computers together in order to share information and resources. 
2. Protocols are the set of rules and standards that govern how computers communicate with each other over a network.
3. The main goal of networking is to enable users to access and share data, applications, and other resources.
4. Protocols are used to ensure that data is transmitted accurately and securely, and that the network is reliable and efficient.
5. Some of the most common applications of networks and protocols include email, instant messaging, file sharing, web browsing, and remote access.
6. Networks and protocols are also used to connect devices such as printers, scanners, and other peripherals to computers.
7. In addition, networks and protocols are used to connect computers to the Internet and to other networks.
8. Mnemonics and learning tricks for networks and protocols include:
    * OSI Model: Open Systems Interconnection Model
    * TCP/IP: Transmission Control Protocol/Internet Protocol
    * LAN: Local Area Network
    * WAN: Wide Area Network
    * DNS: Domain Name System
    * DHCP: Dynamic Host Configuration Protocol
    * NAT: Network Address Translation
    * VPN: Virtual Private Network
9. Advantages of networks and protocols include:
    * Increased productivity
    * Reduced costs
    * Improved communication
    * Improved security
    * Increased flexibility
10. Disadvantages of networks and protocols include:
    * Increased complexity
    * Increased vulnerability to security threats
    * Increased maintenance costs
