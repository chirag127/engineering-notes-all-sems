#### Categories of networks in computer networks

The major categories of networks in computer networks are:

1. Local Area Networks (LANs): LANs connect computing devices that are in close proximity to each other, such as in an office building, a home, or a small group of buildings. LANs are usually privately-owned and feature high-speed data transfer rates. Examples of LANs include Ethernet and Wi-Fi networks.

2. Metropolitan Area Networks (MANs): MANs cover a larger geographic area than LANs, such as an entire city or town. They are typically operated by a single organization. Examples of MAN technologies include WiMAX and fiber-optic networks.

3. Wide Area Networks (WANs): WANs connect LANs or MANs that are geographically separated over large distances. They are often operated by telecommunications companies and internet service providers. The internet is the world's largest WAN and allows networks around the globe to connect and share information. Other examples include cellular data networks and private leased lines.

Mnemonics to remember the categories:

- LANs - Local connections, small area
- MANs - City-wide connections
- WANs - Worldwide connections
