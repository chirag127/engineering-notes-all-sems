#### ISP (Internet Service Provider)

- An ISP or Internet Service Provider is a company that provides access to the internet and other related services.
- Common types of ISPs:
	- Dial-up ISP: Uses standard telephone lines to connect to the internet. Slow speed.
	- Broadband ISP: Uses high-speed connections like fiber optics, coaxial cables, DSL, etc. to provide fast internet access. Most common type of ISP nowadays.
- When you subscribe to an ISP, you get an internet connection and a unique IP address. The ISP is responsible for connecting you to the wider internet network and ensuring your data reaches the destined servers and websites.
- Some ISPs may provide additional services like web hosting, email, online storage, etc. along with the internet connection.
- Points to consider when choosing an ISP:
	- Connection speed and reliability
	- Data limits and bandwidth caps
	- Cost and subscription plans
	- Technical support
	- Additional services and features
- Mnemonic: "ISP connects us to the INTERNET and provides additional SERVICES"
- Pros: Convenient and easy access to the internet. Additional services. Technical support.
- Cons: Monthly subscription fees. Data limits and throttling. Technical issues can occur.
- Applications: Used by individuals and businesses to access the internet and related services.
