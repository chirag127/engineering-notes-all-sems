#### Types of connections in Computer Networks

The following are the major types of connections used in computer networks:

1. Point-to-Point Connection:
- It is a dedicated connection between two endpoints.
- Only two devices are connected in this type of network.
- Full bandwidth of the link is dedicated to these two devices.
- Example: Connection between two offices via leased lines.

Mnemonic: Point-to-Point means two points are connected directly.

2. Multi-Point Connection:
- It connects multiple endpoints.
- The bandwidth of the link is shared among multiple devices.
- Can be in the form of broadcast medium or a shared medium.
- Examples: Ethernet, Wireless LAN, etc.

Mnemonic: Multi-Point means many points are connected.

3. Switched Connection:
- It is a temporary dedicated connection between two endpoints.
- A switch is used to connect multiple links and provide dedicated connections.
- When a connection is not in use, the switch provides connectivity to other endpoints.
- Example: Ethernet Switched Networks.

4. Virtual Connection:
- It appears as a dedicated end-to-end connection between two endpoints but is not physically dedicated.
- The resources are allocated dynamically on a per connection basis.
- Example: Virtual circuits in Frame Relay, ATM, etc.
