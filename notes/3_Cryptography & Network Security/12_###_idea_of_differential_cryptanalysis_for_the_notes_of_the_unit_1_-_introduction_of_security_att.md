### Idea of differential cryptanalysis for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction of security attacks, services and mechanism in the subject of Cryptography & Network Security
Differential cryptanalysis is a method of analyzing and evaluating the security of cryptographic algorithms. It involves finding pairs of plaintexts that have a specific difference and observing the resulting ciphertexts to determine if the difference can be used to gain any information about the encryption key. The goal is to find pairs of messages that result in the same difference in the ciphertext, which can be used to mount an attack on the encryption system. The idea is to observe the effect of a chosen difference on the encryption process, and to use this information to determine the encryption key. This method has been used to attack several encryption algorithms, including DES, and is considered a powerful tool in the cryptanalyst's arsenal.
