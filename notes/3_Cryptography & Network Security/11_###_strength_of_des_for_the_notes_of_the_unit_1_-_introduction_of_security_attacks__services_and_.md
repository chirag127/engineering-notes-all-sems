### Strength of DES for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction of security attacks, services and mechanism in the subject of Cryptography & Network Security
DES (Data Encryption Standard) is a symmetric encryption algorithm that was widely used for secure data transmission. Its strength lies in the use of a 56-bit key, which was considered secure at the time of its development. However, with advancements in computing power, it is now considered vulnerable to brute force attacks. Despite this, DES remains in use in legacy systems and is sometimes used in combination with other encryption algorithms for added security.
