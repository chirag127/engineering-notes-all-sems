### cryptanalysis for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to security attacks, services and mechanism in the subject of Cryptography & Network Security
Cryptanalysis is the process of breaking or studying cryptographic systems to determine the hidden information. It is used to evaluate the security of cryptographic systems by discovering weaknesses and vulnerabilities. Cryptanalysis can be divided into two categories: classical and modern. Classical cryptanalysis involves techniques such as frequency analysis and brute force attacks, while modern cryptanalysis involves mathematical attacks, such as linear and differential cryptanalysis. Cryptanalysis is important in cryptography and network security because it helps to determine the security of cryptographic systems and identify potential weaknesses that can be exploited by attackers.
