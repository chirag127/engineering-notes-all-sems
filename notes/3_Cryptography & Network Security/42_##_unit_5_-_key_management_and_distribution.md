## Unit 5 - Key Management and distribution
Key Management refers to the process of generating, distributing, storing, and revoking cryptographic keys. It is a critical aspect of cryptography that ensures the confidentiality and integrity of data. Key distribution involves the secure transfer of keys from one entity to another, usually between a client and a server. This process is essential to establish secure communication between the two entities. Key management and distribution are usually achieved through the use of certificate authorities, key servers, and key management protocols. The most common key management protocols include SSL/TLS, IPSec, and SSH. It is important to implement strong key management practices to prevent unauthorized access to sensitive information.
