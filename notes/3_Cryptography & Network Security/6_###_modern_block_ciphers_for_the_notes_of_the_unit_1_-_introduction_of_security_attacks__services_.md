### Modern Block Ciphers for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction of security attacks, services and mechanism in the subject of Cryptography & Network Security
Modern block ciphers are symmetric encryption algorithms that operate on fixed-size blocks of plaintext and ciphertext. They are widely used for secure data transmission and storage. Examples of modern block ciphers include AES, Blowfish, and Twofish. These ciphers use substitution-permutation network (SPN) or Feistel network structures and employ key expansion, round functions, and modular arithmetic to encrypt and decrypt data. They are designed to be secure against various types of attacks, such as brute-force, differential, and linear cryptanalysis. Block ciphers are also used as building blocks for other encryption schemes, such as cipher block chaining (CBC) and counter (CTR) modes.
