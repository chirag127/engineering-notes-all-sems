### Optimal Decisions in Games for the notes of the Unit 2 - Problem Solving Methods in the subject of Artificial Intelligence
Optimal Decisions in Games:

1. Definition: The process of finding the best move or strategy for a player in a game to maximize their chances of winning.

2. Methods: 
a. Minimax Algorithm: a recursive method that evaluates all possible moves and their outcomes to determine the optimal move.
b. Alpha-Beta Pruning: an optimization technique for the minimax algorithm to reduce the number of nodes evaluated.
c. Monte Carlo Tree Search: a simulation-based method that uses random sampling to estimate the value of moves.

3. Applications: Used in game AI, decision making systems, and multi-agent systems.

4. Considerations: The complexity of the game, the number of possible moves, and the time constraints for decision making can impact the choice of method used.
