### Informed for the notes of the Unit 2 - Problem Solving Methods in the subject of Artificial Intelligence
Informed search methods in Artificial Intelligence use additional information to guide the search process towards more promising solutions. This information can be in the form of heuristics, which provide an estimate of the cost to reach the goal, or a problem-specific domain knowledge.

1. Best-first search: Selects the node with the lowest estimated cost to reach the goal.

2. A* search: Combines the cost to reach the node and the estimated cost to reach the goal.

3. Hill Climbing: Selects the node with the highest value and moves to a neighboring node with a higher value.

4. Beam search: Keeps a limited number of best nodes and expands them in the next iteration.

5. Branch and bound: Prunes branches that are not promising by bounding the cost of the optimal solution.

These methods help to reduce the search space and find solutions faster, but they may not guarantee finding the optimal solution. The choice of the informed search method depends on the specific problem and the available information.
