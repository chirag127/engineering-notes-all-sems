### Local Search Algorithms and Optimization Problems for the notes of the Unit 2 - Problem Solving Methods in the subject of Artificial Intelligence
Local Search Algorithms:
- A type of optimization algorithm that iteratively improves a solution by making small, local changes.
- Examples: Hill Climbing, Simulated Annealing, Genetic Algorithms.

Optimization Problems:
- A problem where the goal is to find the best solution among a set of possible solutions.
- Examples: Traveling Salesman Problem, Knapsack Problem, Minimum Spanning Tree Problem.

Note: Both Local Search Algorithms and Optimization Problems are used to solve complex problems in Artificial Intelligence and other fields. The choice of algorithm depends on the specific problem and constraints.
