### Information Extraction for the notes of the Unit 5 - Applications in the subject of Artificial Intelligence
Information Extraction (IE) is a process of automatically extracting structured information from unstructured data sources such as text, images, and audio. In the context of Artificial Intelligence, applications of IE include:

1. Named Entity Recognition (NER): Identifying and classifying named entities such as persons, organizations, and locations in text.

2. Relation Extraction: Identifying relationships between entities in text, such as "John works for Google."

3. Event Extraction: Identifying events and their attributes in text, such as "John gave a speech at the conference."

4. Coreference Resolution: Identifying and linking mentions of the same entity in text, such as "John, he, and his."

5. Sentiment Analysis: Determining the sentiment expressed in text, such as positive, negative, or neutral.

6. Summarization: Generating a concise summary of a text document.

IE is a crucial component in many AI applications, including natural language processing, machine translation, and question answering systems.
