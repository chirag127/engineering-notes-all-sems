### Stochastic Games for the notes of the Unit 2 - Problem Solving Methods in the subject of Artificial Intelligence
Stochastic Games are multi-agent decision making problems where agents interact with each other in a probabilistic environment. They are used to model situations where multiple agents have conflicting goals and must make decisions based on the actions of others. 

The game is played over a finite or infinite number of rounds, with each round consisting of the agents taking turns to make their moves. The outcome of each round is determined by the combination of the agents' actions and the probabilistic rules of the game. 

Stochastic games can be modeled as Markov Decision Processes (MDPs) with multiple decision makers. The solution to a Stochastic Game is a set of strategies for each agent, such that the expected sum of rewards for all agents is maximized. 

Applications of Stochastic Games include: resource allocation, network routing, and auction design.
