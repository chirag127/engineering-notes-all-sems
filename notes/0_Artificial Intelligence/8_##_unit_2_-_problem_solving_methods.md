## Unit 2 - Problem Solving Methods
Problem Solving Methods is a unit that teaches various techniques and strategies for solving problems. It covers topics such as problem definition, brainstorming, root cause analysis, decision making, and implementation. The unit emphasizes the importance of a structured approach to problem solving, including the use of tools such as flowcharts, cause-and-effect diagrams, and decision trees. It also covers the importance of testing and evaluating solutions, as well as the need to continually monitor and improve the problem-solving process. The unit is designed to help individuals develop critical thinking and problem-solving skills that can be applied in a variety of settings, including personal and professional contexts.
