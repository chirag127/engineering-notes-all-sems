### Constraint Propagation for the notes of the Unit 2 - Problem Solving Methods in the subject of Artificial Intelligence
Constraint Propagation is a technique used in Artificial Intelligence to solve problems by reducing the number of possible solutions through the use of constraints. It involves iteratively applying constraints to a problem until a solution is found or it can be determined that no solution exists. The process of constraint propagation can be thought of as propagating information from one constraint to another, reducing the number of possible solutions until a solution is found or it is determined that no solution exists. The technique is often used in search algorithms, such as constraint satisfaction problems and scheduling problems. Constraint propagation can greatly reduce the search space, making the problem-solving process more efficient.
