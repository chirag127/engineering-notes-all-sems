### Characteristics of Intelligent Agents for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction in the subject of Artificial Intelligence
1. Autonomy: operates independently and can adapt to changing environments.
2. Reactivity: respond to changes in the environment in real-time.
3. Proactivity: take the initiative to achieve goals.
4. Social ability: can interact with humans and other agents.
5. Learning: improve performance based on experience.
6. Knowledge representation: store and use information.
7. Reasoning: use logic and problem-solving skills.
8. Perception: process sensory input from the environment.
