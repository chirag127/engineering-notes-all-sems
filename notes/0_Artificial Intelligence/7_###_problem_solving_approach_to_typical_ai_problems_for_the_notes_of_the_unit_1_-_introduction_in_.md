### Problem Solving Approach to Typical AI problems for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction in the subject of Artificial Intelligence
Problem Solving Approaches to AI problems:
1. Search: A systematic exploration of the state space to find a solution.
2. Game Playing: Minimax, Alpha-Beta Pruning, Monte Carlo Tree Search.
3. Constraint Satisfaction: Backtracking, Forward Checking, Arc Consistency.
4. Knowledge Representation: First Order Logic, Bayesian Networks, Markov Networks.
5. Decision Making: Decision Trees, Random Forest, Naive Bayes, Support Vector Machines.
6. Reinforcement Learning: Q-Learning, SARSA, Deep Reinforcement Learning.
7. Unsupervised Learning: Clustering, Principal Component Analysis, Autoencoders.
8. Supervised Learning: Linear Regression, Logistic Regression, Neural Networks.
