### Language Models for the notes of the Unit 5 - Applications in the subject of Artificial Intelligence
Language models in AI:
- A language model is a statistical model that is trained to predict the likelihood of a sequence of words in a language.
- They are used in NLP tasks such as text classification, machine translation, and text generation.
- Pre-trained language models such as BERT, GPT-3, ELMo have significantly improved NLP performance.
- These models are trained on vast amounts of text data and can be fine-tuned for specific NLP tasks.
- They use techniques such as attention mechanisms and transformer architecture.

Applications:
- Text classification: predict the category of a given text (e.g. sentiment analysis).
- Machine translation: translate text from one language to another.
- Text generation: generate new text based on a given prompt or seed text.
- Question answering: answer questions based on a given context.
- Named entity recognition: identify named entities in text (e.g. people, organizations).
