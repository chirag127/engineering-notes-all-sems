### Backward Chaining for the notes of the Unit 3 - Knowledge Representation in the subject of Artificial Intelligence
Backward Chaining is a reasoning method used in Artificial Intelligence to deduce a conclusion based on a set of given premises. It starts with the desired conclusion and works backwards to find the necessary premises to support it. This method is also known as goal-driven or data-driven reasoning. It is used in rule-based systems and expert systems, where a set of rules is used to deduce new information. The process of Backward Chaining involves searching for the conclusion, checking if it can be deduced from the available information, and if not, searching for the premises that would support it. The process continues until either the conclusion is deduced or it is determined that the conclusion cannot be deduced from the available information.
