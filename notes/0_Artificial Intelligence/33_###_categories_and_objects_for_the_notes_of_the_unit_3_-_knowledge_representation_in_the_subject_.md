### Categories and Objects for the notes of the Unit 3 - Knowledge Representation in the subject of Artificial Intelligence
Categories:
1. Knowledge Representation Languages: languages used to represent knowledge, e.g. Prolog, RDF, OWL.
2. Knowledge Representation Formalisms: mathematical frameworks for representing knowledge, e.g. first-order logic, Bayesian networks, semantic networks.
3. Knowledge Representation Techniques: methods for representing knowledge, e.g. frames, scripts, ontologies.

Objects:
1. Instances: specific objects or entities, e.g. a person, a car, a city.
2. Classes: categories of objects or entities, e.g. animals, vehicles, locations.
3. Relations: connections between objects or entities, e.g. is-a, part-of, located-in.
4. Attributes: features or properties of objects or entities, e.g. color, size, weight.
