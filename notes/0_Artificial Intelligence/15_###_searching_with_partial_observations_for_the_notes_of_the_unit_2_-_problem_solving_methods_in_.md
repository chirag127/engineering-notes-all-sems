### Searching with Partial Observations for the notes of the Unit 2 - Problem Solving Methods in the subject of Artificial Intelligence
Searching with Partial Observations refers to the process of finding a solution to a problem when only a portion of the information is available. In Artificial Intelligence, this is a common scenario when solving problems that involve uncertainty, such as decision making under uncertainty, planning under uncertainty, and probabilistic reasoning. 

There are several methods used for searching with partial observations, including:
1. Heuristic search
2. Game playing
3. Constraint satisfaction
4. Bayesian networks
5. Markov decision processes

Each of these methods has its own strengths and weaknesses, and the choice of method depends on the specific problem being solved and the available information.
