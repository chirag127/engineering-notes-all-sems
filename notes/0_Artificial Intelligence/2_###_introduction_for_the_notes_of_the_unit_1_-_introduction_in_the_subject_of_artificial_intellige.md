### Introduction for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction in the subject of Artificial Intelligence
Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a branch of computer science concerned with creating machines that can perform tasks that normally require human intelligence, such as visual perception, speech recognition, decision-making, and language translation. The goal of AI is to develop algorithms and systems that can perform tasks that would normally require human intelligence. The field of AI has seen rapid progress in recent years due to advances in machine learning, deep learning, and big data. AI has applications in many industries, including healthcare, finance, and transportation. This unit will introduce the basics of AI, including its history, definitions, and applications. Additionally, we will cover some of the key techniques used in AI, such as machine learning and deep learning.
