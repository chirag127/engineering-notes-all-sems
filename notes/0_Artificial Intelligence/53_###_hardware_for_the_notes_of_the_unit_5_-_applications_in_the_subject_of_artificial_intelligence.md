### Hardware for the notes of the Unit 5 - Applications in the subject of Artificial Intelligence
Hardware for AI:
1. CPU (Central Processing Unit): The "brain" of the computer, performs basic arithmetic, logical, and input/output operations.

2. GPU (Graphics Processing Unit): Specialized hardware designed to perform complex mathematical operations, commonly used for training deep learning models.

3. TPU (Tensor Processing Unit): Google's custom-built ASIC (Application-Specific Integrated Circuit) designed specifically for machine learning workloads.

4. RAM (Random Access Memory): Temporary storage used by the CPU to store data and intermediate results of computations.

5. Storage: Hard disk drives (HDD) or solid-state drives (SSD) to store data and program files.

6. Motherboard: The main circuit board that connects all components of the computer.

7. Power Supply Unit (PSU): Converts AC power from the wall socket into DC power to run the computer components.

8. Network Interface Card (NIC): Enables communication between the computer and other devices on a network.
