### Architecture for Intelligent Agents for the notes of the Unit 4 - Software Agents in the subject of Artificial Intelligence
Architecture for Intelligent Agents:
1. Reactive: Respond to current environment with limited memory.
2. Deliberative: Consider goals, beliefs, and plans to make decisions.
3. Hybrid: Combination of reactive and deliberative.

Components of an Intelligent Agent:
1. Sensors: Perceive environment.
2. Actuators: Perform actions in environment.
3. Processor: Reason and make decisions.
4. Memory: Store beliefs and plans.

Types of Agents:
1. Simple reflex agents: React to stimuli.
2. Model-based reflex agents: Use internal model to make decisions.
3. Goal-based agents: Pursue specific goals.
4. Utility-based agents: Choose actions based on expected utility.
5. Learning agents: Improve performance through experience.
