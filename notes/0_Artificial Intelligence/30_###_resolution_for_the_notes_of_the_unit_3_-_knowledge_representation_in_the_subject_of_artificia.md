### Resolution for the notes of the Unit 3 - Knowledge Representation in the subject of Artificial Intelligence
The resolution of the notes for Unit 3 - Knowledge Representation in Artificial Intelligence encompasses the following key topics:

1. Representing knowledge in AI
2. Predicate logic and first-order logic
3. Knowledge representation languages (e.g. OWL, RDF, Prolog)
4. Reasoning and inference in AI
5. Representing uncertainty and probability in AI
6. Representing and reasoning with temporal information
7. Representing and reasoning with spatial information

Each of these topics provides a foundation for understanding how knowledge is represented and manipulated in AI systems, and how this representation and manipulation supports intelligent decision making.
