### Mental Events and Mental Objects for the notes of the Unit 3 - Knowledge Representation in the subject of Artificial Intelligence
Mental Events: Refers to the processes of thinking, perceiving, and feeling that occur in the mind. They are the mental representations of actions, events, and experiences.

Mental Objects: Refers to the mental representation of physical objects, abstract concepts, and relationships between objects. They are the mental representations of things, concepts, and relationships in the mind.
