Chatbot: Develop a conversational agent (chatbot) that can handle customer queries, perform simple tasks and make recommendations. Technologies: Natural Language Processing (NLP), Python, Dialogflow, TensorFlow.

A chatbot is a conversational agent that can handle customer queries, perform simple tasks and make recommendations. To develop a chatbot using NLP, Python, Dialogflow and TensorFlow, you need to follow these steps:

- Define your chatbot's use case and domain. For example, you may want your chatbot to provide weather information, book flights or order pizza.
- Choose a platform for your chatbot. You can use a messaging app like Telegram, Viber or Twilio, or you can integrate your chatbot into your website using a widget or Python code.
- Create a Dialogflow agent for your chatbot. Dialogflow is a Google service that provides natural language understanding and dialog management for chatbots. You can use Dialogflow's console to design your chatbot's intents, entities and contexts.
- Train your chatbot with data. You can use Dialogflow's built-in training tool to provide sample phrases and responses for your chatbot. You can also use TensorFlow to build custom models for more complex tasks like sentiment analysis or entity extraction.
- Test and deploy your chatbot. You can use Dialogflow's simulator to test your chatbot's functionality and performance. You can also use Python libraries like requests or Flask to connect your chatbot to the platform of your choice.
