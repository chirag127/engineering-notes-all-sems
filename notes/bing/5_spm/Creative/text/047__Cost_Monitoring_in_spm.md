### Cost Monitoring in SPM

Cost monitoring in Software Performance Management (SPM) is a process or function that monitors the expenses related to a software project, including hardware, development, maintenance and operations. 

Some of the objectives of cost monitoring are:

- To track the actual costs against the planned budget and identify any deviations or risks
- To measure the return on investment (ROI) and the value delivered by the software project
- To optimize the allocation of resources and reduce unnecessary or wasteful spending
- To support decision making and corrective actions based on accurate and timely data
- To improve the estimation and forecasting of future costs and benefits

Some of the techniques and tools for cost monitoring are:

- Earned Value Analysis (EVA): This is a method that integrates the scope, schedule, and cost of a project and compares the planned value, earned value, and actual cost of the work performed. EVA can help to calculate key performance indicators such as cost variance, schedule variance, cost performance index, and schedule performance index.
- Cost Breakdown Structure (CBS): This is a hierarchical representation of the costs of a project, organized by categories, subcategories, and elements. CBS can help to identify the sources and drivers of costs and to allocate them to different work packages or activities.
- Cost Reporting and Dashboard: This is a way of presenting and communicating the cost information of a project to the stakeholders, using charts, graphs, tables, or other visual aids. Cost reporting and dashboard can help to provide a clear and concise overview of the status and performance of the project in terms of costs.
