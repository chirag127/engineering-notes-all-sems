### Health and Safety in SPM

- SPM stands for Sahaviriya Plate Mill, a public company in Thailand that produces steel plates for various industries.
- SPM is committed to ensuring the health and safety of its employees, contractors, customers, and communities, as well as protecting the environment from any adverse impacts of its operations.
- SPM has implemented a safety and occupational health management system that complies with the TIS 18001 standards, which are based on the international OHSAS 18001 standards.
- SPM has also adopted the process safety management (PSM) approach, which is a systematic way of identifying, evaluating, and controlling the hazards of processes involving highly hazardous chemicals.
- SPM has established various policies, procedures, and programs to promote a culture of safety and health, such as:
  - Providing regular training and education for all employees and contractors on safety and health topics, such as safe patient handling and mobility (SPHM), fire prevention, emergency response, and personal protective equipment (PPE).
  - Conducting regular audits and inspections to monitor and improve the performance of the safety and health management system, as well as to identify and correct any non-conformities or potential risks.
  - Encouraging the participation and consultation of employees and contractors in safety and health matters, such as through safety committees, suggestion schemes, and reporting systems.
  - Recognizing and rewarding the achievements and contributions of employees and contractors in safety and health, such as through awards, incentives, and commendations.
  - Communicating and reporting the safety and health performance and activities of SPM to internal and external stakeholders, such as through newsletters, bulletins, and annual reports.
- SPM has achieved various milestones and accolades in safety and health, such as:
  - Reducing the lost time injury frequency rate (LTIFR) from 0.33 in 2005 to 0.03 in 2020, which is well below the industry average of 0.5.
  - Achieving zero fatalities and zero major accidents since 2005.
  - Receiving the Prime Minister's Industry Award for Safety Management in 2010, 2012, and 2014, which is the highest honor for industrial safety in Thailand.
  - Receiving the National Outstanding Industrial Establishment Award for Safety, Occupational Health, and Working Environment in 2011, 2013, and 2015, which is the second highest honor for industrial safety in Thailand.
  - Receiving the ASEAN Occupational Safety and Health Network (OSHNET) Award for Excellence in 2016, which is the regional recognition for outstanding safety and health performance in ASEAN countries.
