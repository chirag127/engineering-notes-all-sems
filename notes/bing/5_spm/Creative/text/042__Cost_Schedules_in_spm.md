### Cost Schedules in SPM

- Cost schedules are detailed and accurate estimates of the weekly or monthly costs of a software project over its life cycle.
- Cost schedules are used to analyze and forecast the project's total costs and help the project manager allocate the project's budget .
- Cost schedules also allow the project manager to keep track of actual costs vs. planned costs and identify any cost variances .
- Cost schedules are based on the project plan, which includes the work breakdown structure (WBS), the activity durations, the milestones, and the project completion date .
- Cost schedules are created by estimating the cost of each activity in the WBS, adding contingency reserves for risks and uncertainties, and aggregating the costs to obtain the total project cost.
- Cost schedules are updated and controlled throughout the project to reflect any changes in scope, schedule, resources, or quality.
- Cost schedules are important for software project management because they help the project manager to:
  - Plan and manage the project's cash flow and profitability .
  - Communicate the project's cost performance and status to stakeholders .
  - Evaluate the project's return on investment and value .
  - Make informed decisions and trade-offs regarding the project's scope, schedule, resources, or quality .
