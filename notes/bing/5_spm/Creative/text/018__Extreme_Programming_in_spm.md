### Extreme Programming in spm

- Extreme Programming (XP) is an agile software development framework that aims to produce higher quality software, and higher quality of life for the development team.
- XP is based on five values: communication, simplicity, feedback, courage, and respect .
- XP follows five rules: plan, design, code, test, and listen.
- XP implements 12 practices: the planning game, small releases, metaphor, simple design, test-driven development, refactoring, pair programming, collective ownership, continuous integration, 40-hour week, on-site customer, and coding standards  .
- XP is suitable for projects that have changing or unclear requirements, small and medium-sized teams, and customers who are willing to be involved in the development process  .
- XP aims to reduce the cost of changes in requirements by having multiple short development cycles, rather than a long one.
- XP emphasizes the importance of feedback, communication, and collaboration among the developers and the customers   .
