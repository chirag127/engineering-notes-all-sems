### Organizational Behavior in spm

- Organizational behavior (OB) is the study of how people behave and interact in organizational work environments. It draws on insights from various disciplines, such as psychology, sociology, and anthropology, to understand and predict human behavior at work.   
- OB covers three main levels of analysis: micro (individuals), meso (groups), and macro (the organization). Each level has its own theories, concepts, and phenomena that explain and influence organizational behavior.  
- OB is important for managers and employees because it helps them to improve organizational performance, effectiveness, and well-being. OB can also help to address various challenges and opportunities in the workplace, such as motivation, communication, diversity, leadership, conflict, ethics, and change.   
- OB is an applied and dynamic field that requires continuous learning and adaptation. OB can help to develop skills and competencies that are essential for personal and professional growth, such as critical thinking, problem-solving, teamwork, and communication.
