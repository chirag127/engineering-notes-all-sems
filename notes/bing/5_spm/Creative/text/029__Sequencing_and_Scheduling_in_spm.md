### Sequencing and Scheduling in SPM

- Sequencing and scheduling are two important techniques for planning and managing complex projects.
- Sequencing is the process of determining the order of tasks or activities that need to be performed in a project. It involves identifying the dependencies, constraints, and priorities among the tasks or activities.
- Scheduling is the process of allocating time and resources to the tasks or activities in a project. It involves estimating the duration, start and finish dates, and resource requirements of each task or activity.
- Sequencing and scheduling can be done using various tools and methods, such as bar charts, network diagrams, critical path method, Gantt charts, etc.
- Sequencing and scheduling help to optimize the project performance, reduce the project risk, and enhance the project quality.
- Sequencing and scheduling can be combined for smaller projects, where the tasks or activities are simple and sequential. However, for larger and more complex projects, sequencing and scheduling need to be separated and done as individual processes.
