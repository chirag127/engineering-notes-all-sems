### Objectives of Activity Planning in SPM

Activity planning is one of the most important management activities in software project management. It involves identifying, estimating, scheduling, and monitoring the tasks and resources needed to complete the project. Activity planning aims to achieve the following objectives  :

- To ensure that the appropriate resources will be available precisely when required;
- To avoid different activities competing for the same resources at the same time;
- To produce a detailed schedule showing which staff carry out each activity;
- To produce a detailed plan against which actual achievement may be measured;
- To produce a timed cash flow forecast;
- To provide a basis for project monitoring and control;
- To motivate the project team by setting clear and realistic goals;
- To coordinate the activities of different sub-projects or teams;
- To assess the feasibility of the project within the required timescales and resource constraints;
- To identify and manage the risks and uncertainties associated with the project.

Activity planning is an ongoing effort throughout the life of the project, as new information, changes, and issues arise. Activity planning requires the use of various tools and techniques, such as work breakdown structure, network planning models, critical path method, and Gantt charts. Activity planning also involves the use of software tools and applications that can help in creating, updating, and communicating the project plan. Activity planning is a key skill for software project managers and engineers, as it can help in delivering the project on time, within budget, and with the desired quality.
