### PERT Technique in SPM

- PERT stands for Program Evaluation and Review Technique. It is a scheduling technique used to plan, coordinate and track complex projects.  
- PERT involves identifying the activities and milestones of a project, estimating their durations, and arranging them in a network diagram that shows their dependencies and sequence. 
- PERT also uses a three-point estimation technique to account for uncertainty and variability in the activity durations. The three points are: optimistic, pessimistic and most likely. The weighted average of these three points is called the expected duration.  
- PERT helps to calculate the critical path of a project, which is the longest sequence of activities that must be completed on time to avoid delaying the project. The critical path determines the minimum possible duration of the project.  
- PERT also helps to identify the slack or float of each activity, which is the amount of time that an activity can be delayed without affecting the project duration. The slack can be used to allocate resources, manage risks and optimize the project schedule.  
- PERT is useful for projects that have high uncertainty, complexity and interdependence among activities. It can help to improve the accuracy and reliability of the project estimates and schedules.
