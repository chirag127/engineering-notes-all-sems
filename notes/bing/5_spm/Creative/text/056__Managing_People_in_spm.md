### Managing People in spm

- spm stands for software project management, which is the discipline of planning, organizing, executing, monitoring and controlling software projects.
- Managing people is one of the key skills of a software project manager, as the human factor is the main cause of project success or failure.
- Managing people involves selecting the best people for the project, motivating them, organizing them into effective teams, communicating with them, resolving conflicts, and providing feedback and recognition .
- Some of the best practices for managing people in spm are   :

  - Manage your own workload first, and set an example for your team members.
  - Define clear and realistic goals and expectations for the project and for each individual.
  - Delegate tasks and responsibilities according to the skills, interests and strengths of your team members.
  - Provide regular and constructive feedback, and celebrate achievements and milestones.
  - Encourage collaboration and communication among team members, and use appropriate tools and platforms to facilitate information sharing and coordination.
  - Support the professional development and learning of your team members, and provide opportunities for training, mentoring and coaching.
  - Empower your team members to make decisions and solve problems, and trust them to deliver quality work.
  - Respect the diversity and individuality of your team members, and foster a culture of inclusion and respect.
  - Address any issues or conflicts that arise promptly and constructively, and seek win-win solutions.
  - Align the project strategy and work with the organizational vision and values, and communicate the benefits and outcomes of the project to the stakeholders.
