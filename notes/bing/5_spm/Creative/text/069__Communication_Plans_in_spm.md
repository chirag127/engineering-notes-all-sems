### Communication Plans in SPM

- SPM stands for Strategic Performance Management, which is a process that guides an organization's leadership in designing and revising a system of strategic performance management.
- Communication is a critical component of the SPM process, as it helps to engage and collaborate with external and internal stakeholders, and to communicate important and ongoing project information .
- A communication plan is an outline of how, when, what, and to whom you are going to communicate during the SPM process.
- A communication plan should include the following elements:
  - Communication objectives: what are the goals and desired outcomes of your communication?
  - Communication audience: who are the key stakeholders and target groups of your communication?
  - Communication channels: what are the best ways and tools to reach your audience?
  - Communication messages: what are the main points and key messages you want to convey?
  - Communication frequency: how often and when will you communicate with your audience?
  - Communication roles and responsibilities: who is responsible for creating, delivering, and monitoring the communication?
  - Communication feedback: how will you collect and incorporate feedback from your audience?
  - Communication evaluation: how will you measure and report the effectiveness and impact of your communication?
- A communication plan should be aligned with the SPM process and its phases, such as visioning, goal setting, action planning, implementation, monitoring, and evaluation.
- A communication plan should be flexible and adaptable to changing circumstances and needs of the SPM process and its stakeholders.
