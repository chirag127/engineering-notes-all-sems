### Communications Genres in SPM

- Communication genres are the methods of communication that are selected and developed to deal with a particular need for project coordination .
- Communication genres can be classified into four categories based on the place and time of communication:
  - Same place, same time: This includes face-to-face communication such as meetings, interviews, presentations, etc.
  - Same place, different time: This includes communication that is left for others to access later such as noticeboards, pigeon-holes, documents, etc.
  - Different place, same time: This includes communication that is synchronous but not co-located such as telephone, instant messaging, video conferencing, etc.
  - Different place, different time: This includes communication that is asynchronous and not co-located such as email, voicemail, forums, blogs, etc.
- Communication genres have different purposes, advantages and disadvantages depending on the context and the audience .
  - Verbal communication is quick and effective, but it can be misunderstood, forgotten or distorted.
  - Written communication is clear and permanent, but it can be time-consuming, impersonal or ignored.
  - Visual communication is engaging and memorable, but it can be ambiguous, complex or misleading.
  - Non-verbal communication is subtle and expressive, but it can be misinterpreted, overlooked or inappropriate.
- Communication genres should be chosen and adapted according to the project needs, goals, stakeholders and risks .
  - The communication plan documents the arrangements for communication between stakeholders, such as the frequency, format, content and feedback mechanisms of communication.
  - The communication plan should consider the communication objectives, audience, message, medium, style, tone, timing and evaluation of communication .
  - The communication plan should be reviewed and updated regularly to reflect the changes and progress of the project .
