## Unit - 4 - Project Management and Control for the notes of the course Software Project Management in spm

- Project management and control is the process of monitoring and controlling the project activities to ensure that they meet the project objectives and deliverables.
- Project management and control involves the following aspects:
  - Creating a framework for management and control, which defines the roles, responsibilities, communication channels, reporting mechanisms, and tools for the project team and stakeholders.
  - Collecting the data, which involves gathering and analyzing the information about the project progress, performance, quality, risks, issues, and changes.
  - Visualizing the progress, which involves presenting the data in a clear and concise way, using charts, graphs, dashboards, and reports, to show the project status, trends, and deviations.
  - Cost monitoring, which involves tracking and controlling the project budget, expenses, and cash flow, and comparing them with the baseline and forecast values.
  - Earned value analysis, which is a technique that integrates the scope, time, and cost aspects of the project, and measures the project performance in terms of value earned, schedule variance, and cost variance.
  - Prioritizing monitoring, which involves focusing on the most critical and important aspects of the project, such as the key deliverables, milestones, risks, and issues, and allocating the resources and attention accordingly.
  - Getting the project back to target, which involves identifying and implementing corrective and preventive actions to address the project deviations, problems, and changes, and ensuring that the project is aligned with the objectives and expectations.
  - Change control, which is a process that ensures that any changes to the project scope, schedule, cost, quality, or resources are formally requested, evaluated, approved, and implemented, and that their impacts are assessed and communicated.
  - Software configuration management, which is a process that ensures that the software products and artifacts are identified, controlled, verified, and audited, and that their versions, baselines, and changes are tracked and documented.
  - Contract management, which involves managing the contractual relationships and obligations between the project and the external parties, such as the customers, suppliers, and subcontractors, and ensuring that the contract terms and conditions are fulfilled and the risks are minimized.

- Some mnemonics and learning tricks for the unit are:
  - The acronym **PVC** can be used to remember the three aspects of earned value analysis: **P**ercent complete, **V**alue earned, and **C**ost variance.
  - The acronym **CRUD** can be used to remember the four steps of change control: **C**hange request, **R**eview and approval, **U**pdate and implementation, and **D**ocumentation and communication.
  - The acronym **SMART** can be used to remember the characteristics of good project objectives and deliverables: **S**pecific, **M**easurable, **A**chievable, **R**elevant, and **T**ime-bound.
