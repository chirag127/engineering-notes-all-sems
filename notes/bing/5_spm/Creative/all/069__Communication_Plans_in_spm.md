### Communication Plans in SPM

- SPM stands for Strategic Performance Management, which is a multistep process that guides an organization's leadership in designing and revising a system of strategic performance management.
- Communication is a critical component of the SPM process, as it helps to engage and collaborate with external and internal stakeholders, and to communicate the vision, goals, and progress of the organization.
- A communication plan is an outline of how to communicate important, ongoing information to key stakeholders in the SPM process. It helps to ensure that everyone is on the same page, and that the communication is consistent, clear, and timely.
- A communication plan typically includes the following elements:
  - **Purpose**: The reason and objectives of the communication plan, such as informing, persuading, or motivating stakeholders.
  - **Audience**: The groups or individuals who need to receive the communication, such as employees, customers, partners, or media.
  - **Message**: The key points and information that need to be communicated, such as the vision, goals, achievements, or challenges of the organization.
  - **Channel**: The medium or method of communication, such as email, newsletter, website, social media, or meeting.
  - **Frequency**: The schedule or timing of the communication, such as daily, weekly, monthly, or quarterly.
  - **Responsibility**: The person or team who is in charge of creating and delivering the communication, such as the project manager, the communication manager, or the executive director.
  - **Feedback**: The mechanism or process of collecting and analyzing the responses and reactions of the audience, such as surveys, polls, comments, or reviews.
- A communication plan can be presented in a table or a matrix format, with each element as a column or a row, and each communication activity as a cell. For example:

| Purpose | Audience | Message | Channel | Frequency | Responsibility | Feedback |
|---------|----------|---------|---------|-----------|---------------|----------|
| Inform  | Employees | Vision and goals of the organization | Email | Quarterly | Executive director | Survey |
| Persuade | Customers | Benefits and features of the new product | Website | Monthly | Marketing manager | Comments |
| Motivate | Partners | Success stories and best practices | Newsletter | Weekly | Project manager | Reviews |

- A communication plan can help to improve the SPM process by:
  - Aligning the stakeholders with the vision and goals of the organization
  - Building trust and credibility among the stakeholders
  - Enhancing the collaboration and coordination among the stakeholders
  - Increasing the awareness and understanding of the organization's performance
  - Encouraging the feedback and input from the stakeholders
  - Celebrating the achievements and recognizing the contributions of the stakeholders
- A communication plan can be evaluated and revised based on the feedback and the changing needs of the organization and the stakeholders. A communication plan should be flexible and adaptable to the dynamic environment and the evolving SPM process.
