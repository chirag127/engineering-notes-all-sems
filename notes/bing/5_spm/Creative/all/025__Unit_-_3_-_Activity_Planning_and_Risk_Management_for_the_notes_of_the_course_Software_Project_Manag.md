## Unit - 3 - Activity Planning and Risk Management for the notes of the course Software Project Management

- Activity planning is the process of defining and sequencing the tasks, milestones, and deliverables of a software project.
- Activity planning aims to create a realistic and feasible schedule that meets the project objectives and stakeholder expectations.
- Activity planning involves the following steps:
  - Identify the project scope and objectives
  - Decompose the project into manageable activities or work packages
  - Estimate the effort, duration, and resources required for each activity
  - Identify the dependencies and constraints among the activities
  - Create a network diagram or a Gantt chart to visualize the activity sequence and critical path
  - Review and refine the activity plan with the project team and stakeholders
  - Monitor and control the activity progress and performance
- Activity planning is an iterative and dynamic process that requires constant updates and adjustments based on the project changes and feedback.

- Risk management is the process of identifying, analyzing, and responding to the uncertainties and threats that may affect the software project.
- Risk management aims to minimize the negative impacts and maximize the positive opportunities of the risks on the project objectives and outcomes.
- Risk management involves the following steps:
  - Identify the potential risks that may affect the project scope, schedule, quality, cost, or stakeholder satisfaction
  - Analyze the probability and impact of each risk and prioritize them based on their severity and exposure
  - Plan the appropriate strategies and actions to avoid, reduce, transfer, or accept each risk
  - Implement the risk response plan and assign roles and responsibilities for risk management
  - Monitor and review the risk status and effectiveness of the risk response plan
  - Communicate and document the risk information and lessons learned
- Risk management is a proactive and continuous process that requires collaboration and communication among the project team and stakeholders.

- A mnemonic to remember the steps of activity planning is **IDECRM** (Identify, Decompose, Estimate, Create, Review, Monitor).
- A mnemonic to remember the steps of risk management is **IAPIMC** (Identify, Analyze, Plan, Implement, Monitor, Communicate).
