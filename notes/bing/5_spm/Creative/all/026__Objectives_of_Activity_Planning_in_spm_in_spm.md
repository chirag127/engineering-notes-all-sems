### Objectives of Activity Planning in SPM

- Activity planning is the process of identifying, sequencing, estimating, and scheduling the activities or tasks required to complete a project.
- The objectives of activity planning in SPM are:

  - To define the scope and deliverables of the project in terms of activities or tasks.
  - To determine the logical order and dependencies of the activities or tasks.
  - To estimate the duration, effort, cost, and resources required for each activity or task.
  - To optimize the use of resources and minimize the risk of delays or conflicts.
  - To create a realistic and feasible schedule for the project that meets the stakeholder expectations and constraints.

  - **E**stimate
