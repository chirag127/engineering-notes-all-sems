### Earned Value Analysis in spm

- Earned Value Analysis (EVA) is a project management technique that allows to measure the performance and progress of a project based on the planned and actual costs and the work done  .
- EVA uses three key metrics to track the project status: Planned Value (PV), Earned Value (EV), and Actual Cost (AC)  .
  - PV is the budgeted cost of the work scheduled to be done by a certain date  . It is also known as Budgeted Cost of Work Scheduled (BCWS)  .
  - EV is the budgeted cost of the work actually completed by a certain date  . It is also known as Budgeted Cost of Work Performed (BCWP)  .
  - AC is the actual cost incurred for the work done by a certain date  . It is also known as Actual Cost of Work Performed (ACWP)  .
- EVA uses these metrics to calculate the following indicators of project performance  :
  - Schedule Variance (SV) = EV - PV. It shows whether the project is ahead or behind schedule  . A positive SV means the project is ahead of schedule, while a negative SV means the project is behind schedule  .
  - Cost Variance (CV) = EV - AC. It shows whether the project is under or over budget  . A positive CV means the project is under budget, while a negative CV means the project is over budget  .
  - Schedule Performance Index (SPI) = EV / PV. It shows how efficiently the project is using time  . An SPI of 1 means the project is on schedule, while an SPI greater than 1 means the project is ahead of schedule, and an SPI less than 1 means the project is behind schedule  .
  - Cost Performance Index (CPI) = EV / AC. It shows how efficiently the project is using resources  . A CPI of 1 means the project is on budget, while a CPI greater than 1 means the project is under budget, and a CPI less than 1 means the project is over budget  .
  - Estimate at Completion (EAC) = BAC / CPI. It shows the estimated total cost of the project at completion  . BAC is the original budget at completion  .
  - Estimate to Complete (ETC) = EAC - AC. It shows the estimated remaining cost to complete the project  .
  - Variance at Completion (VAC) = BAC - EAC. It shows the expected cost overrun or underrun at the end of the project  .
- EVA can help the project manager to monitor and control the project by providing an integrated view of the project's scope, time, and cost  . It can also help to identify and correct any deviations from the plan, forecast the project outcome, and communicate the project status to the stakeholders  .
- EVA requires the project to have a well-defined work breakdown structure (WBS), a realistic and detailed schedule, and a reliable and accurate cost estimation  .
