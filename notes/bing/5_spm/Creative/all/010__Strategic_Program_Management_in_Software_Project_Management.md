### Strategic Program Management in Software Project Management

- Strategic program management is a **strategic management approach** to executing and controlling multiple related projects that share a common goal or vision .
- The goal of strategic program management is to **drive benefits** to the entire program by sharing project resources, costs and activities . It also helps to **align** the projects with the strategic business goals and objectives of the organization.
- A strategic program manager is responsible for **coordinating** and **directing** the projects within the program, as well as **communicating** with the stakeholders and project managers . They also have to **view** and **manage** the interdependencies, risks and issues among the projects .
- A strategic program management office (PMO) is an **organizational component** that can support the strategic program management by providing **guidance**, **standards**, **tools** and **best practices** for project management. It can also help to **monitor** and **evaluate** the performance and outcomes of the projects and the program.
- Some of the **benefits** of strategic program management are:
  - It connects the projects to the strategic business goals and objectives of the organization.
  - It views and manages the interdependencies, risks and issues among the projects.
  - It simplifies the resource allocation and optimization across the projects.
  - It improves the quality and consistency of the project deliverables and outcomes.
  - It enhances the stakeholder engagement and satisfaction.
- Some of the **challenges** of strategic program management are:
  - It requires a high level of leadership, communication and collaboration skills.
  - It involves a complex and dynamic environment with multiple stakeholders and variables.
  - It demands a clear and shared vision and scope for the program and the projects.
  - It faces the risk of scope creep, cost overrun and schedule delay.
- A possible **mnemonic** to remember the key aspects of strategic program management is:

  **S**trategic: Aligns the projects with the strategic business goals and objectives.

  **P**rogram: Coordinates and directs multiple related projects with a common goal or vision.

  **M**anagement: Drives benefits to the entire program by sharing resources, costs and activities.

  **O**ffice: Supports the program management by providing guidance, standards, tools and best practices.
