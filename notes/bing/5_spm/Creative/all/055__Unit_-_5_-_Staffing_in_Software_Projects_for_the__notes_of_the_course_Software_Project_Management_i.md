## Unit - 5 - Staffing in Software Projects for the notes of the course Software Project Management in spm

Staffing in software projects is the process of selecting, assigning, and managing the human resources that will work on a software project. Staffing is a critical activity that affects the quality, cost, and schedule of the software project. Staffing involves the following steps:

- **Planning**: This step involves defining the staffing needs and requirements for the project, such as the number, skills, roles, and availability of the software developers. Planning also involves creating a staffing strategy that aligns with the project goals, scope, budget, and timeline. A staffing strategy may include the following aspects :
  - The type of software development methodology to use, such as agile, waterfall, or hybrid.
  - The type of software development team structure to adopt, such as functional, cross-functional, or matrix.
  - The type of software development outsourcing model to apply, such as onshore, offshore, nearshore, or hybrid.
  - The type of software development hiring model to follow, such as permanent, contract, or freelance.
  - The type of software development compensation model to offer, such as fixed, hourly, or performance-based.
- **Recruiting**: This step involves finding and attracting the best candidates for the software project, based on the staffing strategy and the job specifications. Recruiting may involve the following activities :
  - Posting job ads on various platforms, such as online job boards, social media, or referrals.
  - Screening and filtering the applicants based on their resumes, portfolios, or tests.
  - Conducting interviews and assessments to evaluate the candidates' technical and soft skills, fit, and motivation.
  - Negotiating and finalizing the terms and conditions of the employment contract, such as salary, benefits, or incentives.
- **Assigning**: This step involves allocating the selected software developers to the appropriate project activities, tasks, and roles, based on their skills, experience, and preferences. Assigning may involve the following activities :
  - Defining the roles and responsibilities of each software developer, such as project manager, analyst, designer, programmer, tester, or maintainer.
  - Establishing the communication and collaboration channels and tools among the software developers, such as email, chat, or video conferencing.
  - Setting the performance and quality standards and expectations for each software developer, such as deadlines, deliverables, or feedback.
  - Balancing the workload and the skill mix of the software developers, such as by using a constraint satisfaction or optimization-based approach .
- **Managing**: This step involves monitoring, evaluating, and improving the performance and satisfaction of the software developers throughout the project lifecycle. Managing may involve the following activities :
  - Providing regular feedback and recognition to the software developers, such as by using a performance appraisal or reward system.
  - Providing ongoing training and development opportunities to the software developers, such as by offering mentoring, coaching, or certification programs.
  - Providing adequate support and resources to the software developers, such as by resolving issues, conflicts, or challenges.
  - Retaining and motivating the software developers, such as by creating a positive and engaging work environment, culture, or values.

Some mnemonics and learning tricks for the unit are:

- Remember the four steps of staffing in software projects as **PRAM**: Planning, Recruiting, Assigning, and Managing.
- Remember the five aspects of a staffing strategy as **TTHHC**: Type of methodology, Type of team structure, Type of outsourcing model, Type of hiring model, and Type of compensation model.
- Remember the four activities of recruiting as **PSNC**: Posting, Screening, Negotiating, and Contracting.
- Remember the four activities of assigning as **DEBS**: Defining, Establishing, Setting, and Balancing.
- Remember the four activities of managing as **PERT**: Providing feedback, Providing training, Providing support, and Providing retention.
