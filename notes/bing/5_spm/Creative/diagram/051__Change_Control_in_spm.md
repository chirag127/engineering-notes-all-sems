Change control is a process that ensures that any changes to a project or a system are introduced in a controlled and coordinated way. Change control helps to minimize the risk of disruption, errors, or defects in the project or system. Change control in spm (strategic portfolio management) is a way of managing changes to the strategic objectives, plans, and initiatives of an organization. Change control in spm helps to align the work with the strategy, prioritize the most valuable initiatives, and respond quickly to changing needs or opportunities.

The diagram shows the basic steps of change control in spm:

- Propose Change: A change request is submitted by anyone who wants to introduce a change to the strategy, plan, or initiative. The change request should include the reason, scope, impact, and benefits of the change.
- Impact Summary: The change request is reviewed by the relevant stakeholders, such as the portfolio manager, the project manager, or the sponsor. The impact summary should assess the feasibility, cost, risk, and value of the change, as well as its alignment with the strategy and the dependencies with other initiatives.
- Make Decision: The decision to approve or reject the change request is made by the authorized person or group, such as the portfolio board, the steering committee, or the sponsor. The decision should be based on the impact summary and the strategic priorities of the organization.
- Make Change: If the change request is approved, the change is implemented according to the agreed plan and schedule. The change should be communicated to all the affected parties and monitored for its effectiveness and outcomes.
- Reject Change: If the change request is rejected, the reason for the rejection is communicated to the requester and the stakeholders. The change request is closed and archived for future reference.
- Close Change: Once the change is completed or rejected, the change request is closed and documented. The lessons learned from the change process are captured and shared for continuous improvement.
