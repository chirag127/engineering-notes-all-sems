The objectives of activity planning in software project management (SPM) are:

- To identify the activities that need to be performed to complete the project successfully.
- To estimate the duration, cost, and resources required for each activity.
- To sequence the activities in a logical order and identify their dependencies and constraints.
- To allocate the resources to the activities and assign responsibilities to the project team members.
- To produce a detailed schedule that shows the start and finish dates of each activity and the milestones and deliverables of the project.
- To monitor and control the progress of the activities and compare them with the planned schedule and budget.
- To communicate the plan to the stakeholders and obtain their approval and feedback.
