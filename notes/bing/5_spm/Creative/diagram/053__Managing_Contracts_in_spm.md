Managing contracts in spm is the process of monitoring and evaluating the performance of suppliers who provide services to an organization. SPM stands for supplier performance management, which is a method of assessing how well suppliers meet the expectations and requirements of their customers. SPM involves setting goals, measuring outcomes, providing feedback, and taking corrective actions if needed.

The diagram shows how the three main components of spm (contract management, supplier management, and service management) interact with each other and with the spm system. The spm system collects and analyzes data from various sources, such as contracts, invoices, surveys, audits, and service level agreements, to measure and evaluate the performance of suppliers. The spm system also generates and shares reports and dashboards that show the results of the analysis and the scores of the suppliers. The spm system also helps identify and implement improvement actions, such as renegotiating contracts, providing feedback, or terminating contracts, if the suppliers do not meet the expectations and requirements of the organization.
