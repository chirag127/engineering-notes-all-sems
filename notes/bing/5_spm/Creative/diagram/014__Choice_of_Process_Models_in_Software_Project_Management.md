### Choice of Process Models in Software Project Management

A process model is a representation of the activities, tasks, and resources involved in a software project. It provides a framework for planning, executing, monitoring, and controlling the project. Different process models have different advantages and disadvantages depending on the project characteristics, such as size, complexity, scope, quality, and risk.
