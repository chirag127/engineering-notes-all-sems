Ethical and Professional Concerns in spm are the personal and corporate rules that govern the behavior of software project managers and their teams. They are based on values such as honesty, respect, fairness, responsibility, integrity, and accountability. They help to ensure the quality, safety, and legality of the software products and services, as well as the trust and satisfaction of the stakeholders.

The diagram shows the following components and relationships:

- Code of Ethics: A set of principles and standards that guide the professional conduct of software project managers and their teams. It is usually defined by a professional organization, such as the Project Management Institute (PMI).
- Ethical Values: The core beliefs and ideals that shape the moral character and judgment of software project managers and their teams. They include honesty, respect, fairness, responsibility, integrity, and accountability.
- Ethical Dilemmas: The situations or problems that pose a conflict between two or more ethical values or principles. They may arise from the complexity, uncertainty, or ambiguity of the software project environment, or from the competing interests or expectations of the stakeholders.
- Ethical Behavior: The actions or decisions that software project managers and their teams take to resolve ethical dilemmas and uphold ethical values and principles. They may involve applying ethical frameworks, models, or codes, or consulting with experts, peers, or mentors.
- Ethical Choices: The outcomes or consequences of ethical behavior that reflect the ethical values and principles of software project managers and their teams. They may affect the quality, safety, and legality of the software products and services, as well as the trust and satisfaction of the stakeholders.
- Ethical Outcomes: The impacts or effects of ethical choices on the software project and its stakeholders. They may be positive or negative, intended or unintended, short-term or long-term. They may also provide feedback or learning opportunities for improving ethical behavior and choices in the future.
