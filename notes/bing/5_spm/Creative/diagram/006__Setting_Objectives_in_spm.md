Setting objectives in SPM (Strategic Portfolio Management) is the process of defining and aligning the goals of a project or a portfolio with the business strategy and priorities. Setting objectives helps to clarify the expectations, focus, motivation and engagement of the project team and stakeholders. It also helps to measure and track the progress and value of the project or portfolio.
