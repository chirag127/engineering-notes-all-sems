Strategic Program Management in Software Project Management is a process of managing multiple related software projects that contribute to a common strategic goal. It involves aligning the software projects with the business objectives, coordinating the project interdependencies, optimizing the resources and risks, and delivering the benefits and value to the stakeholders.
