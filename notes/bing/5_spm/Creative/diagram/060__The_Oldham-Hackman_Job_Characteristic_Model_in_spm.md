The Oldham-Hackman Job Characteristic Model in spm is a framework that proposes that certain job characteristics can enhance the intrinsic motivation, satisfaction, and performance of employees. The model identifies five core job dimensions, three critical psychological states, and four personal and work outcomes. The model also suggests that the relationship between the job dimensions and the outcomes is moderated by the individual's growth need strength, which is the degree to which a person values personal development and learning.
