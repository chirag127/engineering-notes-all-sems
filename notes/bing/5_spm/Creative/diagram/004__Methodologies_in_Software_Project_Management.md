### Agile
Agile is a flexible and iterative approach that delivers value to the customer in small increments. Agile teams work in short cycles called sprints, where they plan, design, develop, test and deliver a potentially shippable product or feature. Agile teams are self-organizing, cross-functional and collaborative, and they use feedback and adaptation to improve their processes and products. Agile is suitable for projects that have changing requirements, high uncertainty and fast delivery.
