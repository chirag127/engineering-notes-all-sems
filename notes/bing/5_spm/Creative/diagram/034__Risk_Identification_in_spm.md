Risk identification is the process of determining potential risks that may affect the project objectives, scope, schedule, budget, quality, or stakeholders. It is an essential step in risk management, which aims to minimize the negative impacts and maximize the positive opportunities of uncertainty.

There are various techniques for risk identification, such as brainstorming, interviews, checklists, questionnaires, SWOT analysis, cause-and-effect diagrams, etc. The choice of technique depends on the nature, size, and complexity of the project, as well as the availability of resources and information.

A common way to represent the risk identification process is by using a diagram that shows the inputs, tools and techniques, and outputs of the process.
