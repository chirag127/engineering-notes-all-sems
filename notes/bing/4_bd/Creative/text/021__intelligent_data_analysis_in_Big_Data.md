### Intelligent data analysis in Big Data

- Intelligent data analysis (IDA) is one of the most important approaches in the field of data mining, which attracts great concerns from the researchers.
- IDA aims to extract underlying patterns, trends, and insights from large, complex, and heterogeneous data sets, using advanced analytical techniques, such as data mining, statistical analysis, and predictive modeling .
- Big data is a term that refers to the massive volume, velocity, and variety of data that is generated by various sources, such as social media, sensors, mobile devices, and online transactions.
- Big data poses significant challenges for traditional data analysis methods, such as scalability, complexity, heterogeneity, and quality.
- Big data analytics (BDA) is the application of IDA on big data sets, using computational intelligence techniques, such as artificial intelligence (AI), machine learning, and deep learning, to transform raw data into valuable insights for decision making  .
- AI makes big data analytics simpler by automating and enhancing data preparation, data visualization, predictive modeling, and other complex analytical tasks that would otherwise be labor-intensive and time-consuming.
- AI helps users work with, manipulate, and surface actionable insights faster from large, complex data sets.
- BDA can provide various benefits for different domains and applications, such as business intelligence, customer behavior analysis, fraud detection, health care, smart cities, and recommender systems .
- BDA can also help to improve the performance, efficiency, and innovation of organizations and industries, by enabling data-driven decision making, optimizing business processes, and creating new products and services .
- BDA requires a new theoretical understanding of the capabilities, challenges, and opportunities of IDA in the big data environment, as well as the development of novel methods, tools, and frameworks for effective and efficient data analysis.
