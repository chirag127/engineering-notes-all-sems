### Spark

Spark is a word that has two distinct meanings. As a noun, it means a small fiery particle thrown off from a fire, alight in ashes, or produced by striking together two hard surfaces such as stone or metal. Or as a verb, it means emit sparks of fire or electricity.

Some of the features of spark as a noun are:

- It can be used to describe a small flash of light produced by a sudden disruptive electrical discharge through the air.
- It can also refer to an electrical discharge serving to ignite the explosive mixture in an internal combustion engine.
- It can indicate a trace of a specified quality or intense feeling, such as interest, anger, or excitement.
- It can denote a sense of liveliness and excitement, or a lively young fellow.

Some of the features of spark as a verb are:

- It can mean to produce sparks at the point where an electric circuit is interrupted.
- It can also mean to ignite something, such as a fire, a fuse, or a conflict.
- It can imply to provide the stimulus for something, such as an idea, a movement, or a reaction.
- It can signify to engage in courtship or flirtation.

Some examples of spark in a sentence are:

- A log fire was sending sparks onto the rug.
- Angry sparks were flashing in her eyes.
- There was a spark of light.
- A tiny spark of anger flared within her.
- There was a spark between them at their first meeting.
- The ignition sparks as soon as the gas is turned on.
- The explosion sparked a fire.
- The severity of the plan sparked off street protests.
- He went a sparking among the rosy country girls.
