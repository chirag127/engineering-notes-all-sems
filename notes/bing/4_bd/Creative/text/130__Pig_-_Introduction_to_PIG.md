#### Pig - Introduction to PIG

- Pigs are mammals of the family Suidae, which includes eight genera and 16 species of wild and domestic pigs .
- Pigs are also known as hogs or swine. Male pigs of any age are called boars; female pigs are called sows.
- Pigs are native to the Old World, but have been introduced to many parts of the world by humans .
- Pigs are omnivorous animals, meaning they eat both plants and animals . They have a well-developed sense of smell and can locate food underground.
- Pigs are social animals that live in groups called sounders. They communicate with each other using a variety of vocalizations and body language.
- Pigs are intelligent and can learn new skills quickly . They can also recognize themselves in mirrors and show empathy for other pigs.
- Pigs are raised by humans for various purposes, such as food, leather, glue, fertilizer, and medicine. They are also kept as pets by some people .
- Pigs have a significant impact on the environment, both positive and negative. They can help control pests and weeds, but they can also damage crops and habitats, spread diseases, and compete with native wildlife.
