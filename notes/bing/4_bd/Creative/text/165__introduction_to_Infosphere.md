#### Introduction to Infosphere

- Infosphere is a term that combines information and sphere, and refers to an environment that is populated by informational entities called inforgs .
- Infosphere can be understood as the whole system of services and documents, encoded in any semiotic and physical media, whose contents include any sort of data, information and knowledge.
- Infosphere is not limited to purely online environments, but can also include offline and hybrid ones, such as libraries, archives, museums, etc.
- Infosphere is a metaphysical realm of information, data, knowledge and communication, where inforgs interact with each other and with other agents, such as humans, animals, plants, etc.
- Infosphere is also a term used by IBM to refer to a set of products and solutions that provide data integration, data quality, data governance, master data management and data analytics capabilities  .
- Infosphere is a concept that can help us understand the nature, value and impact of information in the contemporary world, and the ethical and social implications of living in an information-rich environment.
