### IBM Big Data strategy

- IBM is a US-based computer hardware and software manufacturer that has implemented a Big Data strategy to offer solutions to store, manage, and analyze the huge amounts of data generated daily and equip large and small companies to make informed business decisions .
- IBM's Big Data strategy is aligned with its Smarter Planet initiative, which seeks to highlight how government and business leaders can capture the potential of smarter systems to achieve economic and sustainable growth and societal progress.
- IBM's Big Data strategy consists of six steps:
  - Understand the business objectives and connect the data strategy with the business strategy.
  - Assess the current state and identify the pain points, blockers, and gaps in the data landscape.
  - Map out the data strategy framework and define the target state, vision, and roadmap for the data transformation.
  - Design the data architecture and select the appropriate data platforms, tools, and technologies to support the data strategy.
  - Implement the data strategy and execute the data projects, initiatives, and use cases that deliver value and insights.
  - Govern and manage the data strategy and ensure the data quality, security, privacy, and compliance are maintained throughout the data lifecycle.
- IBM's Big Data strategy leverages its expertise and offerings in cloud, AI, and open source technologies to help its clients create enterprise-grade, secure, governed, and scalable data lakes and data warehouses that can handle structured, semi-structured, and unstructured data from various sources and formats.
