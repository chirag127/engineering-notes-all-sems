#### Schedulers in Hadoop Ecosystem

Schedulers are algorithms that manage the execution of tasks in a Hadoop cluster. They help in optimizing the utilization of resources and ensuring fair access to different users and applications. There are mainly three types of schedulers in Hadoop:

- **FIFO Scheduler**: This is the default scheduler in Hadoop. It places the applications in a queue and executes them in the order of their submission (first in, first out). It does not consider the priority or the resource requirements of the applications. It is simple and suitable for small clusters with homogeneous workloads .
- **Capacity Scheduler**: This scheduler allows multiple queues with different capacities and priorities. Each queue can have a minimum and a maximum capacity, and a weight to determine its share of resources. The scheduler allocates resources to the queues based on their capacities and weights, and within each queue, it uses FIFO scheduling. It also supports preemption, which means that it can reclaim resources from low-priority queues if they exceed their maximum capacity and give them to high-priority queues. This scheduler is designed for large clusters with multiple tenants and heterogeneous workloads  .
- **Fair Scheduler**: This scheduler aims to provide fair and equal access to resources for all users and applications. It creates a separate queue for each user or group, and allocates resources to the queues based on their demand and the available resources. It also supports preemption, which means that it can balance the resource allocation among the queues if some of them are underutilized or overutilized. It also supports hierarchical queues, weight-based shares, and pool-specific settings. This scheduler is flexible and adaptable for various scenarios and workloads  .
