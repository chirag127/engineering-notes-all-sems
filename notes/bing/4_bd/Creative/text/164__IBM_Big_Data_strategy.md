#### IBM Big Data strategy

- IBM Big Data strategy is a corporate initiative that aims to provide solutions for storing, managing, and analyzing the large volumes of data generated by various sources, such as social media, sensors, mobile devices, etc.
- IBM Big Data strategy is aligned with its Smarter Planet vision, which seeks to leverage smarter systems and technologies to achieve economic and social progress.
- IBM Big Data strategy consists of four key components: Big Data platform, Big Data services, Big Data solutions, and Big Data ecosystem.
- Big Data platform: IBM offers a comprehensive and integrated platform that supports various types of data, such as structured, unstructured, and streaming data, and enables data ingestion, processing, storage, and analysis. The platform includes products such as IBM InfoSphere BigInsights, IBM InfoSphere Streams, IBM PureData System for Analytics, IBM PureData System for Hadoop, IBM PureData System for Operational Analytics, IBM DB2 with BLU Acceleration, IBM Cloud Pak for Data, etc.
- Big Data services: IBM provides a range of services that help clients design, implement, and optimize their Big Data strategy, such as IBM Big Data and Analytics Services, IBM Big Data and Analytics Cloud Services, IBM Big Data and Analytics Managed Services, IBM Big Data and Analytics Consulting Services, etc.
- Big Data solutions: IBM delivers industry-specific and cross-industry solutions that address various business challenges and opportunities, such as customer analytics, fraud detection, risk management, operational efficiency, etc. The solutions include products such as IBM Cognos Analytics, IBM SPSS, IBM Watson, IBM Watson Studio, IBM Watson Machine Learning, IBM Watson OpenScale, IBM Watson Discovery, IBM Watson Assistant, etc.
- Big Data ecosystem: IBM collaborates with a network of partners, such as technology providers, solution providers, academic institutions, and research organizations, to enhance its Big Data capabilities and offerings, and to create value for its clients and stakeholders. Some of the partners include Cloudera, Hortonworks, MapR, MongoDB, Apache, SAS, SAP, Oracle, Microsoft, etc.
