### Drivers for Big Data

- Drivers for big data are the factors that motivate the need for collecting, storing, processing, and analyzing large and complex datasets.
- Some of the drivers for big data are:

  - **Data explosion**: The amount of data generated by various sources such as social media, sensors, mobile devices, e-commerce, etc. is growing exponentially. According to IDC, the global data volume is expected to reach 175 zettabytes by 2025, up from 33 zettabytes in 2018. This data explosion poses challenges for traditional data management systems, which are not designed to handle such volumes and varieties of data.
  - **Data value**: The data generated by various sources has potential value for various purposes such as business intelligence, decision making, innovation, customer satisfaction, etc. However, extracting value from data requires advanced techniques and tools that can handle the complexity and diversity of data. Big data analytics can help in discovering hidden patterns, trends, insights, and opportunities from data, and provide competitive advantages for organizations and individuals.
  - **Data democratization**: The availability and accessibility of data has increased significantly in recent years, due to the advancement of technologies such as cloud computing, open source software, and data sharing platforms. Data democratization refers to the process of making data available and understandable to a wider range of users, without requiring specialized skills or knowledge. Data democratization can empower users to leverage data for their own purposes, and foster a data-driven culture in organizations and society.
  - **Data regulation**: The increasing use and misuse of data has also raised concerns about the privacy, security, ethics, and governance of data. Data regulation refers to the rules and policies that govern the collection, storage, processing, and sharing of data, and protect the rights and interests of data owners, users, and subjects. Data regulation can also influence the design and implementation of big data systems, and require compliance with various standards and regulations.
