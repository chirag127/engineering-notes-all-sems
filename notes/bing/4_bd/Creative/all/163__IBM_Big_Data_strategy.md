### IBM Big Data strategy

- IBM Big Data strategy is a corporate initiative that aims to provide solutions for storing, managing, and analyzing the huge amounts of data generated daily by various sources, such as social media, sensors, mobile devices, etc.
- IBM Big Data strategy is part of the Smarter Planet vision, which seeks to leverage smarter systems to achieve economic and sustainable growth and societal progress.
- IBM Big Data strategy consists of four main components: Big Data platform, Big Data services, Big Data solutions, and Big Data ecosystem.
- Big Data platform: IBM offers a comprehensive and integrated platform that supports various types of data, such as structured, unstructured, semi-structured, streaming, etc. The platform includes products such as IBM InfoSphere BigInsights, IBM InfoSphere Streams, IBM PureData System for Analytics, IBM PureData System for Operational Analytics, IBM DB2 with BLU Acceleration, IBM Cognos Business Intelligence, IBM SPSS, etc.
- Big Data services: IBM provides consulting, implementation, and support services to help clients design, deploy, and optimize their Big Data projects. The services include IBM Big Data and Analytics Services, IBM Big Data and Analytics Cloud Services, IBM Big Data and Analytics Managed Services, IBM Big Data and Analytics Education Services, etc.
- Big Data solutions: IBM delivers industry-specific and cross-industry solutions that address various business challenges and opportunities, such as customer analytics, fraud detection, risk management, operational efficiency, etc. The solutions include IBM Customer Insight Solutions, IBM Counter Fraud Management, IBM Watson Foundations, IBM Content Analytics, IBM Social Media Analytics, etc.
- Big Data ecosystem: IBM collaborates with a network of partners, such as technology providers, solution providers, academic institutions, research organizations, etc., to foster innovation and co-creation in the Big Data domain. The ecosystem includes IBM Big Data and Analytics Hub, IBM Big Data University, IBM Big Data Innovation Centers, IBM Big Data Partner Program, etc.

- A possible mnemonic to remember the four components of IBM Big Data strategy is **P**latform, **S**ervices, **S**olutions, and **E**cosystem, or **PSSE**.
