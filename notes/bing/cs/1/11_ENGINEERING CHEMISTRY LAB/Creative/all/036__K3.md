### K3

- K3 is a synthetic, artificially produced form of vitamin K that doesn’t occur naturally .
- K3 is also known as menadione or vitamin K3 sodium bisulfite .
- K3 may be converted into K2 in your liver, which is the active form of vitamin K that regulates blood clotting and bone health .
- K3 has demonstrated anticancer and antibacterial properties in test-tube studies, but it has also been associated with toxicity and adverse effects in humans and animals .
- K3 is not approved for human use by the Food and Drug Administration (FDA) and is banned in many countries due to its potential harm .
- K3 is sometimes used as a supplement or additive in animal feed, especially for poultry and fish, to prevent vitamin K deficiency and bleeding disorders .
- K3 is also used as a tax form by the Internal Revenue Service (IRS) for partners in a partnership that have international transactions or activities.
- K3 is not a mountain peak in the Himalayas, unlike K2, which is the second highest mountain in the world.
