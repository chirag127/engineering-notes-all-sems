### 13. Preparation of Adipic acid / Paracetamol.

- Adipic acid is an important dicarboxylic acid with the formula (CH2)4(COOH)2. It is mainly used as a precursor for the production of nylon.
- Paracetamol is a widely used analgesic and antipyretic drug with the formula C8H9NO2. It is derived from p-aminophenol by acetylation.

#### Preparation of Adipic acid

- One of the methods to prepare adipic acid is by the oxidation of a mixture of cyclohexanone and cyclohexanol, known as KA oil, with nitric acid. This is a multistep pathway that involves the following reactions:

  - Cyclohexanone and cyclohexanol are oxidized to a mixture of cyclohexanone oxime and cyclohexanol nitrate.
  - Cyclohexanone oxime is rearranged to 6-nitrosohexanone.
  - 6-Nitrosohexanone is hydrolyzed to 6-nitrohexanoic acid.
  - 6-Nitrohexanoic acid is reduced to 6-aminohexanoic acid.
  - 6-Aminohexanoic acid is oxidized to adipic acid.

- Another method to prepare adipic acid is by the oxidation of cyclohexene with hydrogen peroxide or potassium permanganate. This is a simpler pathway that involves the following reactions:

  - Cyclohexene is oxidized to 1,2-cyclohexanediol.
  - 1,2-Cyclohexanediol is oxidized to adipic acid.

#### Preparation of Paracetamol

- One of the methods to prepare paracetamol is by the acetylation of p-aminophenol with acetic anhydride or acetic acid in the presence of a catalyst, such as sulfuric acid or phosphoric acid. This is a direct pathway that involves the following reaction :

  - p-Aminophenol is acetylated to paracetamol.

- Another method to prepare paracetamol is by the nitration of phenol, followed by the reduction of the nitro group and the acetylation of the amino group. This is a three-step pathway that involves the following reactions:

  - Phenol is nitrated to p-nitrophenol.
  - p-Nitrophenol is reduced to p-aminophenol.
  - p-Aminophenol is acetylated to paracetamol.

#### Mnemonics and learning tricks

- A possible mnemonic to remember the formula of adipic acid is: **A** **D**ouble **I**ce **P**ick **I**s **C**ool. The first letters of each word correspond to the number of carbon atoms in each segment of the formula: (CH2)4(COOH)2.
- A possible mnemonic to remember the formula of paracetamol is: **P**aracetamol **A**lways **R**elieves **A**ches **C**aused **E**specially **T**hrough **A** **M**igraine **O**r **L**umbago. The first letters of each word correspond to the symbols of the elements in the formula: C8H9NO2.
- A possible learning trick to remember the oxidation of cyclohexene to adipic acid is to think of the hydrogen peroxide or potassium permanganate as adding two oxygen atoms to each end of the cyclohexene ring, forming two carboxylic acid groups.
- A possible learning trick to remember the acetylation of p-aminophenol to paracetamol is to think of the acetic anhydride or acetic acid as replacing the hydrogen atom of the amino group with an acetyl group, forming an amide bond.
