### Viscosity, conductance of solution, chloride and iron content in the water

- Viscosity is a measure of the resistance of a fluid to deformation under shear stress. It is also known as the thickness or internal friction of a fluid. Viscosity depends on the intermolecular forces, temperature, pressure and composition of the fluid.
- Conductance of solution is a measure of the ability of a solution to conduct electric current. It is also known as the reciprocal of the resistance of the solution. Conductance depends on the concentration, type and mobility of the ions, temperature and solvent of the solution.
- Chloride and iron content in the water are measures of the amount of chloride and iron ions dissolved in the water. They are also indicators of the quality, salinity and hardness of the water. Chloride and iron content depend on the source, treatment and contamination of the water.

Some points to remember are:

- Viscosity decreases with increasing temperature and decreases with increasing pressure for most fluids. Viscosity increases with increasing concentration and molecular weight of the solute for solutions.
- Conductance increases with increasing temperature and increases with increasing concentration and mobility of the ions for solutions. Conductance decreases with increasing viscosity and decreases with increasing covalent character of the solute for solutions.
- Chloride content increases with increasing salinity and increases with increasing pollution and industrial waste for water. Chloride content decreases with increasing precipitation and decreases with increasing treatment and purification for water.
- Iron content increases with increasing corrosion and increases with increasing groundwater and soil leaching for water. Iron content decreases with increasing aeration and decreases with increasing filtration and disinfection for water.

Some mnemonics and learning tricks are:

- Viscosity is like honey, it flows slowly and resists shear. Conductance is like salt water, it flows easily and conducts electricity.
- Viscosity is inversely proportional to temperature and pressure. Conductance is directly proportional to temperature and concentration.
- Chloride content is high in sea water and low in rain water. Iron content is high in rusty pipes and low in filtered water.
