### Formaldehyde & Urea Formaldehyde Resin, Adipic Acid and Paracetamol

- Formaldehyde is a colorless, flammable, strong-smelling chemical that is used in building materials and to produce many household products.
- Urea formaldehyde (UF) is a nontransparent thermosetting resin or polymer that is produced from urea and formaldehyde. It is used in adhesives, plywood, particle board, medium-density fibreboard (MDF), and molded objects.
- Adipic acid is a white crystalline organic acid that is mainly used in the production of nylon. It is also used as a food additive, a plasticizer, a lubricant, and a solvent.
- Paracetamol, also known as acetaminophen, is a common pain reliever and fever reducer that is widely used in over-the-counter and prescription medicines. It is generally safe and effective when taken as directed, but it can cause liver damage if overdosed or combined with alcohol.

Some possible mnemonics and learning tricks for these topics are:

- Formaldehyde: **Form** a **de**adly **hyde**. Formaldehyde is a toxic chemical that can cause cancer and other health problems. It is often used to preserve dead bodies (hyde).
- Urea formaldehyde: **U**se **F**or **A**dhesives. Urea formaldehyde is a resin that is used for making adhesives and other products. The letters U, F, and A can help you remember its name and use.
- Adipic acid: **Adi**pose **p**roduces **ic**e. Adipic acid is derived from adipose tissue (fat) and it is used to make nylon, which is a synthetic fiber that can keep you warm (ice).
- Paracetamol: **Para**chute **c**an **e**ase **t**he **a**che **m**ore **ol**d-fashioned. Paracetamol is a painkiller that can help you feel better when you are injured or sick. It is a modern medicine that is more effective than some traditional remedies (old-fashioned).
