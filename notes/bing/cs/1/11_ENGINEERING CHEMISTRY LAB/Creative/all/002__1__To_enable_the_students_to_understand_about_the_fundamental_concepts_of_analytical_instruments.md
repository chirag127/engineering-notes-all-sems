### 1. To enable the students to understand about the fundamental concepts of analytical instruments

Analytical instruments are devices that are used to measure, analyze, or monitor physical, chemical, or biological properties of samples. Some examples of analytical instruments are spectrometers, chromatographs, microscopes, pH meters, and thermometers.

The fundamental concepts of analytical instruments include:

- **The purpose of analysis**: The purpose of analysis is to identify, quantify, or characterize the components or properties of a sample. The purpose of analysis determines the choice of analytical instrument, method, and technique.
- **The types of analysis**: The types of analysis can be classified into qualitative and quantitative analysis. Qualitative analysis is the identification of the components or properties of a sample, without measuring their amounts. Quantitative analysis is the measurement of the amounts or concentrations of the components or properties of a sample.
- **The principles of measurement**: The principles of measurement are the physical, chemical, or biological phenomena that are used to detect, measure, or manipulate the components or properties of a sample. The principles of measurement can be based on absorption, emission, scattering, fluorescence, polarization, refraction, diffraction, interference, ionization, separation, reaction, amplification, or transduction of light, sound, electricity, magnetism, heat, or force.
- **The components of analytical instruments**: The components of analytical instruments are the parts that perform the functions of sampling, preparation, separation, detection, measurement, and data processing. The components of analytical instruments can be classified into input, output, and intermediate components. Input components are the parts that introduce the sample into the instrument, such as syringes, pumps, valves, or injectors. Output components are the parts that display or record the results of the analysis, such as meters, indicators, printers, or computers. Intermediate components are the parts that perform the analysis, such as columns, cells, detectors, or sensors.
- **The performance characteristics of analytical instruments**: The performance characteristics of analytical instruments are the parameters that describe the quality, accuracy, precision, sensitivity, selectivity, resolution, range, speed, and reliability of the analysis. The performance characteristics of analytical instruments can be evaluated by using standards, calibrations, validations, or quality control procedures.
