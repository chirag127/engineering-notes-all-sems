Different analytical instruments are devices that are used to measure, analyze, or test various physical, chemical, or biological properties of samples. Some examples of analytical instruments are mass spectrometers, chromatographs, titrators, spectrometers, particle size analyzers, rheometers, elemental analyzers, thermal analyzers, etc.
