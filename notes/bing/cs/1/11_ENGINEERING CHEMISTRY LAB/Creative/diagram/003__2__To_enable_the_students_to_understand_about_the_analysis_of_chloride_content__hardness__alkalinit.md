The conductivity meter measures the ability of water to conduct electric current, which depends on the concentration and type of dissolved ions in water. The pH meter measures the acidity or alkalinity of water, which affects the solubility and availability of nutrients and pollutants. The Mohr method for chloride is a titration method that uses silver nitrate as a titrant and potassium chromate as an indicator to determine the chloride concentration in water. The EDTA method for hardness is a complexometric titration method that uses ethylenediaminetetraacetic acid (EDTA) as a titrant and eriochrome black T as an indicator to determine the total hardness of water, which is the sum of calcium and magnesium ions. The titration method for alkalinity is a neutralization titration method that uses sulfuric acid as a titrant and phenolphthalein and bromocresol green as indicators to determine the alkalinity of water, which is the capacity of water to neutralize acids.
