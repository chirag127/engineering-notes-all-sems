The following is a detailed ASCII diagram for the experiment of determination of the strength of ferrous ammonium sulfate using external indicator.

The experiment involves titrating a solution of ferrous ammonium sulfate (FAS) with a standard solution of potassium dichromate (K2Cr2O7) using potassium ferricyanide (K3Fe(CN)6) as an external indicator. The reaction is:

6Fe2+ + Cr2O7^2- + 14H+ -> 6Fe3+ + 2Cr3+ + 7H2O

The end point of the titration is detected by adding a drop of potassium ferricyanide solution on a filter paper. The drop turns blue when all the ferrous ions are oxidized to ferric ions. The blue color is due to the formation of Prussian blue complex:

Fe3+ + 3[Fe(CN)6]^3- -> Fe4[Fe(CN)6]3
