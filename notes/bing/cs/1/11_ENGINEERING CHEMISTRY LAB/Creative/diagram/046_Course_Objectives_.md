Course Objectives:

Course objectives are statements that describe what students should be able to do or know by the end of a course. They are usually written in measurable and observable terms, and they align with the course expectations and assessments. Course objectives help guide the students' learning and provide clarity and direction for the instructor.

An example of a course objective is:

Given a list of potential sources, students will be able to evaluate their credibility and relevance for a research topic.
