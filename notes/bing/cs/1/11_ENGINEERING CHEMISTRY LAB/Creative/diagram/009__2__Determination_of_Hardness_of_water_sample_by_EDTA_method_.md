The procedure for the determination of hardness of water by EDTA method is as follows:

1. Take a sample volume of 20ml (V ml).
2. Dilute 20ml of the sample in Erlenmeyer flask to 40ml by adding 20ml of distilled water.
3. Add 1 mL of ammonia buffer to bring the pH to 10±0.1.
4. Add 1 or 2 drops of the indicator solution.
5. Add EDTA titrant to the sample with vigorous shaking till the wine red colour just turns blue.
6. Note the volume of EDTA titrant used (A ml).
7. Repeat the steps 1 to 6 with a blank solution of distilled water.
8. Note the volume of EDTA titrant used for the blank solution (B ml).
9. Repeat the steps 1 to 6 with a standard solution of known hardness (C mg/L).
10. Note the volume of EDTA titrant used for the standard solution (S ml).
11. Calculate the hardness of the water sample by the formula:

Hardness (EDTA), as mg/L = (A-B) x C x 1000 / (S-B) x V
