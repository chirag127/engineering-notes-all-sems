The Job Client is the program that submits the MapReduce job to the Job Tracker. The Job Tracker is the master node that coordinates the execution of the Map and Reduce tasks on the slave nodes, which are the Task Trackers. The Input Data is the source of data that is processed by the Map Tasks, which apply a user-defined function to each input record and produce intermediate key-value pairs. The Reduce Tasks aggregate the intermediate values associated with the same intermediate key and produce the final output. The Job Client can also monitor the progress of the job and retrieve the results.
