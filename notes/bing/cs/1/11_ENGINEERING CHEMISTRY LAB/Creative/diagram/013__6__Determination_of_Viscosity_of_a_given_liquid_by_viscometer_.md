A viscometer is an instrument used to measure the viscosity of a fluid. There are different types of viscometers, such as capillary, rotational, falling ball, etc. A common type of viscometer is the capillary viscometer, which consists of a glass tube with a narrow section called the capillary. The fluid flows through the capillary under a pressure difference and the time taken for a fixed volume of fluid to pass through the capillary is measured. The viscosity of the fluid is proportional to the flow time and the pressure difference.
