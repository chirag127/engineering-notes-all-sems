The following is a detailed ASCII diagram for the determination of the strength of potassium dichromate using internal indicator.

The procedure involves titrating a known volume of ferrous ammonium sulphate (Mohr's salt) solution with a standard potassium dichromate solution in the presence of sulphuric acid, phosphoric acid and diphenylamine. The end point is marked by the appearance of a blue color due to the formation of diphenylamine sulphonic acid.

The reaction is as follows:

Cr2O7(2-) + 6 Fe(2+) + 14 H(+) --> 2 Cr(3+) + 6 Fe(3+) + 7 H2O
