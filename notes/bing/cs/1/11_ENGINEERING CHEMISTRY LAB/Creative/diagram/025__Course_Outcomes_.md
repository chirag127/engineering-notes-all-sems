Course outcomes are specific and measurable statements that define the knowledge, skills, and attitudes learners will demonstrate by the completion of a course. They are written with a verb phrase and declare a demonstrable action within a given time frame, such as by the end of the course. They describe both the skills and knowledge that learners will develop, and also describe how they will be demonstrated through summative assessment processes. Learning outcomes are specific, observable, and measurable (assessable) demonstrations of knowledge and skills . They describe how learners will achieve the goals of the course.
