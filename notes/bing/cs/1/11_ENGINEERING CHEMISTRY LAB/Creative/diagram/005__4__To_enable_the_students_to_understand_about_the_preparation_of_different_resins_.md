There are many types of resins with different sources, methods of preparation and applications. Some of the common types of resins are:

- Epoxy resin: A thermosetting resin made by reacting epoxide and amine. It is used for adhesives, coatings, composites, electronics, etc.
- UV resin: A resin that cures under ultraviolet light. It is used for jewelry, crafts, art, etc.
- Polyester resin: A thermosetting resin made by reacting polyol and acid. It is used for fiberglass, molding, casting, etc.
- Polyurethane resin: A thermosetting resin made by reacting polyol and isocyanate. It is used for foam, elastomers, coatings, etc.
- Phenolic resin: A thermosetting resin made by reacting phenol and formaldehyde. It is used for impregnation, brake linings, electrical components, etc.
- Alkyd resin: A thermoplastic resin made by reacting polyol and acid. It is used for paints, varnishes, enamels, etc.
