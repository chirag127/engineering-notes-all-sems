The procedure is as follows:

- Fill a burette with 0.02 N standard sulfuric acid (H2SO4) solution.
- Take 100 mL of water sample in a conical flask and measure its initial pH using a pH meter.
- Add 2 drops of phenolphthalein indicator to the water sample and titrate it with H2SO4 until the color changes from pink to colorless. Note the volume of H2SO4 used as V1.
- Add 2 drops of methyl orange indicator to the same water sample and continue the titration with H2SO4 until the color changes from yellow to orange. Note the volume of H2SO4 used as V2.
- Repeat the steps 2 to 4 for two more water samples and take the average of V1 and V2.
