A course objective is a statement of what a student should be able to do or know by the end of a course. Course objectives are usually derived from the program outcomes, which are the broader goals of a degree or certificate program. Course objectives should be specific, measurable, achievable, relevant and time-bound. They should also align with the course content, activities, assessments and teaching methods.
