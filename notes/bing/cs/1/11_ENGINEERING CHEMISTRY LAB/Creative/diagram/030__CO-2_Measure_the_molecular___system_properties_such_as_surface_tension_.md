- The carbon dioxide molecule (O=C=O) contains two double bonds and has a linear shape.
- It has no electrical dipole, and as it is fully oxidized, it is moderately reactive and is non-flammable, but will support the combustion of metals such as magnesium.
- The bond angle between the oxygen atoms and the carbon atom is 180 degrees, and the bond length is 116.3 pm.
- The carbon atom has a sp hybridization, and the oxygen atoms have a sp2 hybridization.
- The molecular weight of carbon dioxide is 44.009 g/mol, and its density is 1.977 kg/m3 at 0 °C and 1 atm.
- Carbon dioxide is soluble in water, in which it reversibly forms H2CO3 (carbonic acid), which is a weak acid.
- Carbon dioxide can undergo several chemical reactions, such as photosynthesis, combustion, reduction, and acid-base reactions.
