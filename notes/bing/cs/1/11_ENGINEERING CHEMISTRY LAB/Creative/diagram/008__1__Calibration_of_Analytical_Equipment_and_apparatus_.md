Calibration of analytical equipment and apparatus is the process of evaluating and adjusting the precision and accuracy of measurement equipment. Calibration helps ensure the quality and reliability of the results obtained by the equipment and apparatus. Calibration also helps reduce errors, costs, and risks associated with faulty measurements.

The following diagram illustrates the basic steps of calibration of analytical equipment and apparatus using a standard reference material (SRM) or a certified reference material (CRM) as a comparison.

The diagram shows that the equipment, apparatus, and SRM or CRM are first prepared and operated to produce outputs. The outputs are then compared with each other to determine the accuracy and precision of the equipment and apparatus. If the outputs are not within the acceptable range, the equipment, apparatus, and SRM or CRM are adjusted accordingly. The process is repeated until the outputs are consistent and accurate. The equipment and apparatus are then calibrated and ready to use for the intended purpose.
