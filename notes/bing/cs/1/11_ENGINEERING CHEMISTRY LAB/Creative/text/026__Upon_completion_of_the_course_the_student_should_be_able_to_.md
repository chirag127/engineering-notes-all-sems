### Upon completion of the course the student should be able to:

- Demonstrate a comprehensive understanding of the main concepts, theories and methods of the discipline.
- Apply the acquired knowledge and skills to analyze, evaluate and solve real-world problems in the relevant domain.
- Communicate effectively and professionally using appropriate oral, written and visual modes of expression.
- Conduct independent and collaborative research using appropriate sources, methods and tools of inquiry.
- Demonstrate ethical awareness and responsibility in academic and professional contexts.
- Reflect critically on their own learning process and outcomes, and identify areas for further improvement.
