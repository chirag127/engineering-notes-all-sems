### 1. To enable the students to understand about the fundamental concepts of analytical instruments.

- Analytical instruments are devices that are used to measure, analyze, or monitor physical, chemical, or biological properties of samples.
- Analytical instruments can be classified into different categories based on their principles, applications, or techniques, such as spectroscopy, chromatography, mass spectrometry, electrochemistry, microscopy, etc.
- Analytical instruments can be used for various purposes, such as quality control, research and development, environmental monitoring, forensic analysis, clinical diagnosis, etc.
- Analytical instruments consist of three main components: a sample introduction system, a detector, and a data processing system.
- A sample introduction system is the part of the instrument that prepares, transports, or manipulates the sample before it reaches the detector. It can include components such as pumps, valves, injectors, columns, reactors, etc.
- A detector is the part of the instrument that converts the physical, chemical, or biological signal from the sample into an electrical signal that can be measured and recorded. It can include components such as photodiodes, electrodes, ion sources, transducers, etc.
- A data processing system is the part of the instrument that analyzes, displays, or stores the data obtained from the detector. It can include components such as computers, software, printers, etc.
