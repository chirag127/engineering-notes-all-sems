### K3

- K3 is a term used to describe the third generation of Korean pop music, also known as K-pop.
- K3 is characterized by the increased use of social media platforms, such as YouTube, Twitter, Instagram, and TikTok, to promote and distribute K-pop content worldwide.
- K3 is also marked by the emergence of new subgenres and styles of K-pop, such as hip-hop, R&B, EDM, rock, and trot.
- Some of the notable K3 groups and artists include BTS, Blackpink, Twice, EXO, Red Velvet, NCT, IU, and Zico.
- K3 has achieved global recognition and popularity, especially in the US, Europe, and Southeast Asia, and has influenced the music and culture of other regions.
