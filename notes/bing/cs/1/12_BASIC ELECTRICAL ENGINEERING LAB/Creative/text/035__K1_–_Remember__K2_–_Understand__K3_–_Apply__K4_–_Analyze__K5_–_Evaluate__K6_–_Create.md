The topic you have chosen is K1 – Remember, K2 – Understand, K3 – Apply, K4 – Analyze, K5 – Evaluate, K6 – Create. These are the six levels of cognitive learning according to Bloom's taxonomy, a framework for classifying educational objectives and outcomes. Here is some content on this topic in markdown format:

#### K1 – Remember
- This level involves recalling facts, terms, definitions, concepts, or procedures from memory.
- Examples of verbs that indicate this level are: define, list, name, identify, label, state, recall, recognize, etc.
- Examples of questions or tasks that assess this level are:
  - Define what is a noun.
  - List the seven continents of the world.
  - Name the four basic operations in mathematics.
  - Identify the parts of a plant.
  - Label the diagram of the human digestive system.
  - State the formula for the area of a circle.
  - Recall the date of the American Civil War.
  - Recognize the flag of Canada.

#### K2 – Understand
- This level involves explaining, interpreting, summarizing, paraphrasing, or illustrating the meaning of information in one's own words.
- Examples of verbs that indicate this level are: describe, explain, summarize, paraphrase, illustrate, demonstrate, compare, contrast, classify, etc.
- Examples of questions or tasks that assess this level are:
  - Describe the main idea of a story.
  - Explain how photosynthesis works.
  - Summarize the main events of the French Revolution.
  - Paraphrase the definition of democracy.
  - Illustrate the life cycle of a butterfly.
  - Demonstrate how to solve a linear equation.
  - Compare and contrast the features of mammals and reptiles.
  - Classify the types of rocks according to their formation.

#### K3 – Apply
- This level involves using or applying knowledge, rules, methods, principles, or concepts to new or different situations or problems.
- Examples of verbs that indicate this level are: use, apply, solve, calculate, perform, implement, execute, etc.
- Examples of questions or tasks that assess this level are:
  - Use the Pythagorean theorem to find the length of the hypotenuse of a right triangle.
  - Apply the scientific method to conduct an experiment.
  - Solve a word problem involving fractions.
  - Calculate the interest rate of a loan.
  - Perform a dance routine following the instructions.
  - Implement a sorting algorithm in a programming language.
  - Execute a CPR procedure on a dummy.
