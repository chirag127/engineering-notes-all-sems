##### Virtual lab link: https://vlab.amrita.edu/?sub=1&brch=75&sim=332&cnt=1

- This link leads to a virtual lab simulation on the topic of **Determination of Young's Modulus of a Wire by Optical Lever Method**.
- The simulation is part of the **Virtual Labs** project initiated by the Ministry of Education, Government of India, under the National Mission on Education through Information and Communication Technology.
- The simulation allows the user to perform an experiment to measure the Young's modulus of a wire by observing the deflection of a light beam reflected from a mirror attached to the wire.
- The simulation provides the following features:
  - A virtual setup of the apparatus, including a wire, a mirror, a light source, a screen, a weight hanger, and a micrometer screw gauge.
  - A control panel to adjust the parameters of the experiment, such as the length and diameter of the wire, the distance between the mirror and the screen, and the mass of the weight hanger.
  - A data table to record the readings of the deflection of the light beam and the extension of the wire for different masses.
  - A graph plotter to plot the deflection-extension curve and calculate the slope, which is proportional to the Young's modulus of the wire.
  - A theory section to explain the principle and procedure of the experiment, as well as the sources of error and precautions.
  - A quiz section to test the user's understanding of the experiment and the concept of Young's modulus.
  - A feedback section to rate the simulation and provide suggestions for improvement.
- The simulation is useful for students who want to learn and practice the experiment without the need of a physical laboratory. It also helps to enhance the conceptual understanding and analytical skills of the students.
