##### 9. To study running and speed reversal of a three phase induction motor and record speed in both directions.

- A three phase induction motor is a type of electric motor that converts alternating current (AC) power into mechanical power by creating a rotating magnetic field in the stator and inducing currents in the rotor.
- The direction of rotation of a three phase induction motor depends on the phase sequence of the three phase AC supply. If the phase sequence is R-Y-B, the motor rotates in clockwise direction. If the phase sequence is R-B-Y, the motor rotates in anti-clockwise direction.
- To reverse the direction of rotation of a three phase induction motor, the phase sequence of the AC supply must be reversed. This can be done by interchanging any two phases of the supply. For example, if the phase sequence is R-Y-B, interchanging R and B will make it R-B-Y, and the motor will rotate in anti-clockwise direction.
- To study the running and speed reversal of a three phase induction motor and record speed in both directions, the following steps are required:

  - Connect the three phase induction motor to a three phase AC supply through a three phase switch and a wattmeter. The switch should have a reversing mechanism to change the phase sequence of the supply.
  - Connect a tachometer to the shaft of the motor to measure the speed of rotation in revolutions per minute (RPM).
  - Start the motor by closing the switch and observe the direction and speed of rotation. Note down the phase sequence, direction and speed of the motor.
  - Reverse the phase sequence of the supply by using the reversing mechanism of the switch and observe the change in direction and speed of rotation. Note down the new phase sequence, direction and speed of the motor.
  - Repeat the above steps for different values of supply voltage and frequency and record the corresponding speeds in both directions.
  - Plot the graphs of speed versus voltage and speed versus frequency for both directions of rotation and compare the results.
