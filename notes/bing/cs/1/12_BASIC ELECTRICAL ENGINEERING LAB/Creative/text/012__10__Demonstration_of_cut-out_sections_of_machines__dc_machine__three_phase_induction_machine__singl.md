##### 10. Demonstration of cut-out sections of machines: dc machine, three phase induction machine, single-phase induction machine and synchronous machine.

- The purpose of this demonstration is to study the various parts and construction of different types of electrical machines, such as dc machine, three phase induction machine, single-phase induction machine and synchronous machine.
- A cut-out section of a machine is a part of the machine that is cut or removed to expose the internal components and show how they are arranged and connected.
- A dc machine is a type of electrical machine that converts mechanical energy into direct current (dc) electrical energy or vice versa. It consists of two main parts: a stator and a rotor. The stator is the stationary part that contains the field windings, which produce a magnetic field when energized by a dc source. The rotor is the rotating part that contains the armature windings, which induce an emf when they cut the magnetic field lines. The rotor also has a commutator and brushes, which are used to connect the armature windings to the external circuit and reverse the direction of the current every half cycle.
- A three phase induction machine is a type of electrical machine that converts electrical energy into mechanical energy or vice versa. It consists of two main parts: a stator and a rotor. The stator is the stationary part that contains the stator windings, which are connected to a three phase ac supply and produce a rotating magnetic field. The rotor is the rotating part that contains the rotor windings, which are either short-circuited (squirrel cage rotor) or connected to an external resistance (wound rotor). The rotor windings induce an emf and a current when they cut the rotating magnetic field lines, which in turn produce a torque on the rotor.
- A single-phase induction machine is a type of electrical machine that converts electrical energy into mechanical energy or vice versa. It consists of two main parts: a stator and a rotor. The stator is the stationary part that contains the stator windings, which are connected to a single phase ac supply and produce a pulsating magnetic field. The rotor is the rotating part that contains the rotor windings, which are either short-circuited (squirrel cage rotor) or connected to an external resistance (wound rotor). The rotor windings induce an emf and a current when they cut the pulsating magnetic field lines, which in turn produce a torque on the rotor. However, a single-phase induction machine cannot start on its own, as it does not have a rotating magnetic field. Therefore, it needs a starting mechanism, such as a capacitor, a shaded pole, or a split phase.
- A synchronous machine is a type of electrical machine that converts electrical energy into mechanical energy or vice versa. It consists of two main parts: a stator and a rotor. The stator is the stationary part that contains the stator windings, which are connected to a three phase ac supply and produce a rotating magnetic field. The rotor is the rotating part that contains the rotor windings, which are energized by a dc source and produce a constant magnetic field. The rotor rotates at the same speed as the rotating magnetic field, hence the name synchronous. The rotor can be either salient pole (with projecting poles) or cylindrical (with smooth surface). The synchronous machine can operate as a motor or a generator, depending on the direction of the power flow.
