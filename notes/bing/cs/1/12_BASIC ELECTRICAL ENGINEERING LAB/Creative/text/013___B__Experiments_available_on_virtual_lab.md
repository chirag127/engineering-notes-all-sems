#### (B) Experiments available on virtual lab

Virtual lab is an online platform that allows students to perform experiments and simulations in various fields of science and engineering without the need for physical equipment or facilities. Some of the experiments available on virtual lab are:

- **Physics**: Students can explore topics such as mechanics, optics, electricity, magnetism, thermodynamics, quantum physics, and more. They can use interactive tools to measure, observe, and manipulate physical phenomena and test their hypotheses. For example, they can study the motion of a pendulum, the reflection and refraction of light, the Ohm's law, the magnetic field of a bar magnet, the heat transfer between two bodies, the photoelectric effect, etc.
- **Chemistry**: Students can learn about the structure and properties of matter, the chemical reactions and kinetics, the equilibrium and thermodynamics, the electrochemistry and spectroscopy, and more. They can perform virtual experiments to synthesize, separate, and analyze different compounds and substances. For example, they can prepare a buffer solution, perform a titration, extract caffeine from tea, measure the pH of a solution, determine the concentration of an unknown sample, etc.
- **Biology**: Students can study the diversity and functions of living organisms, the molecular and cellular biology, the genetics and evolution, the ecology and environment, the anatomy and physiology, and more. They can observe and interact with various specimens and models to understand their structures and behaviors. For example, they can examine the morphology of bacteria, fungi, and protists, the anatomy of a frog, the life cycle of a butterfly, the DNA replication and transcription, the Mendelian inheritance, the population dynamics, etc.
- **Engineering**: Students can apply the principles and methods of engineering to design, build, and test various systems and devices. They can use virtual tools to model, simulate, and optimize their solutions. For example, they can design a bridge, a circuit, a robot, a solar panel, a wind turbine, etc. They can also learn about the engineering disciplines such as civil, electrical, mechanical, computer, chemical, biomedical, etc.
