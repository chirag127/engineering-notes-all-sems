##### Virtual lab link: https://vlab.amrita.edu/?sub=1&brch=75&sim=313&cnt=1

- This link leads to a virtual lab simulation of the **Determination of the Molar Mass of Volatile Liquids by Vapor Density** experiment.
- The experiment is part of the **Physical Chemistry** branch of the **Chemistry** discipline of the **Virtual Labs** project.
- The **Virtual Labs** project is an initiative by the **Ministry of Education, Government of India**, under the **National Mission on Education through Information and Communication Technology**.
- The project aims to provide **remote access** to laboratories in various disciplines of science and engineering for students at all levels from undergraduate to research.
- The simulation allows the user to perform the experiment in a virtual environment, with the help of **instructions**, **theory**, **procedure**, **animation**, **video**, **quiz** and **references**.
- The user can select the **volatile liquid** (acetone, ethanol or methanol) and the **temperature** of the water bath, and then measure the **mass** and **volume** of the vaporized liquid using a **balance** and a **graduated cylinder**.
- The user can then calculate the **molar mass** of the volatile liquid using the **ideal gas equation** and compare it with the **theoretical value**.
- The user can also vary the **experimental parameters** and observe the **effect** on the **results**.
- The simulation helps the user to understand the **concept** and **application** of the **vapor density method** for determining the molar mass of volatile liquids, and to develop the **skills** and **confidence** for performing the experiment in a real laboratory.
