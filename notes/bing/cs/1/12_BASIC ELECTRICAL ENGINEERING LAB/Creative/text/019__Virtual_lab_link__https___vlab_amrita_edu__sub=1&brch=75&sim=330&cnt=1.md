##### Virtual lab link: https://vlab.amrita.edu/?sub=1&brch=75&sim=330&cnt=1

- This link leads to a virtual lab simulation of the **Determination of the Molar Mass of Volatile Liquids by Vapor Density** experiment.
- The experiment is based on the principle that the density of a gas at constant pressure is proportional to its molar mass.
- The objective of the experiment is to determine the molar mass of an unknown volatile liquid by measuring its vapor density at a known temperature and pressure.
- The simulation allows the user to select the unknown liquid from a list of options, and then follow the steps of the experiment as shown in the video.
- The user can also view the theory, procedure, self-evaluation, references and feedback sections of the virtual lab.
- The simulation is designed to enhance the understanding and skills of the students who perform this experiment in a real laboratory.
