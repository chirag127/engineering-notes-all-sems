##### Virtual lab link: http://vp-dei.vlabs.ac.in/Dreamweaver/measurement.html

- This link leads to a virtual lab on measurement, which is a part of the course on Engineering Physics offered by the Dayalbagh Educational Institute.
- The virtual lab consists of four experiments that demonstrate the concepts and applications of measurement in physics.
- The experiments are:
  - Measurement of length using vernier calipers and screw gauge.
  - Measurement of diameter of a thin wire using travelling microscope.
  - Measurement of thickness of a glass plate using Newton's rings method.
  - Measurement of wavelength of sodium light using diffraction grating.
- The virtual lab provides the following features for each experiment:
  - A brief introduction and theory of the experiment.
  - A list of objectives and learning outcomes.
  - A list of apparatus and materials required.
  - A step-by-step procedure with interactive animations and simulations.
  - A set of questions and answers to test the understanding of the experiment.
  - A feedback form to rate the virtual lab and provide suggestions for improvement.
- The virtual lab is designed to enhance the learning experience of students and teachers by providing a realistic and engaging environment for conducting experiments online.
