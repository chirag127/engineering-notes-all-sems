##### Virtual lab link: http://vlab.amrita.edu/?sub=3&brch=75&sim=217&cnt=2

- This link leads to a virtual lab simulation of the **Determination of the Molar Mass of Volatile Liquids by Vapor Density** experiment.
- The experiment is part of the **Physical Chemistry** branch of the **Chemistry** discipline of the **Virtual Labs** project.
- The experiment aims to demonstrate the **Dumas method** of determining the molar mass of a volatile liquid by measuring its vapor density at a known temperature and pressure.
- The experiment consists of the following steps:
  - Heating a volatile liquid in a flask and sealing it with a rubber stopper.
  - Weighing the sealed flask and noting the temperature and pressure of the room.
  - Submerging the flask in a water bath and heating it until the liquid vaporizes completely and the air is expelled from the flask.
  - Cooling the flask in an ice bath and drying it.
  - Weighing the flask again and noting the temperature and pressure of the water bath.
  - Calculating the mass, volume and density of the vapor using the ideal gas law and the measured values.
  - Calculating the molar mass of the liquid using the formula M = dRT/P, where M is the molar mass, d is the density, R is the gas constant, T is the temperature and P is the pressure.
- The experiment allows the user to choose from four different liquids: **acetone, ethanol, methanol and propanol**.
