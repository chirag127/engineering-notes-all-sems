A single phase ac series inductive circuit consists of a resistor, an inductor and a capacitor connected in series across an alternating voltage source. The power and power factor of such a circuit depend on the values of the components and the frequency of the source. The power factor can be improved by adding a capacitor in parallel with the circuit, which reduces the reactive power and increases the apparent power.
