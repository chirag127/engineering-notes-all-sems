The diagram shows how the components are connected in series and parallel. The user can adjust the values of the voltage source, the resistors and the switch using the sliders on the virtual lab interface. The user can also observe the readings of the ammeter and the voltmeter, which change according to the Kirchhoff’s Laws. The user can verify the Kirchhoff’s Voltage Law, which states that the sum of the voltages around any closed loop in a circuit is zero, and the Kirchhoff’s Current Law, which states that the sum of the currents entering any node in a circuit is equal to the sum of the currents leaving that node. The user can also perform experiments to find the equivalent resistance of the circuit and the power dissipated by the resistors. The virtual lab provides a graphical representation of the voltage and current values for each component, as well as a table of the results. The virtual lab also provides a theory section, which explains the concepts and formulas related to Kirchhoff’s Laws, and a quiz section, which tests the user’s understanding of the topic. The virtual lab is designed to enhance the learning experience of the students and to provide them with a hands-on approach to electrical circuits.
