Course outcomes are the statements that help the learners to understand the reason for pursuing the course and helps him to identify what he will be able to do at the end of the course. They are specific and measurable statements that define the knowledge, skills, and attitudes learners will demonstrate by the completion of a course. They are written with a verb phrase and declare a demonstrable action within a given time frame, such as by the end of the course.
