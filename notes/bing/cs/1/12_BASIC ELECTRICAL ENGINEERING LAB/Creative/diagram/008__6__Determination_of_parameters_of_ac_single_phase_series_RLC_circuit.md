An AC single phase series RLC circuit consists of a resistor, an inductor, and a capacitor connected in series to an AC voltage source. The current in the circuit is the same for all the components, but the voltages across them may differ in magnitude and phase. The phasor diagram of the circuit shows the relative angles between the current and the voltages, as well as the impedance of the circuit.
