The two wattmeter method is a technique for measuring the power in a three-phase circuit using two wattmeters. Each wattmeter has a current coil and a voltage coil. The current coils are connected in series with two of the phases, while the voltage coils are connected across the other two phases. The power measured by each wattmeter is the product of the current and the voltage in its coils.
