The virtual lab link is for an experiment on **Determination of the Refractive Index of a Prism using a Spectrometer**. The experiment is part of the **Optics Virtual Lab** of the **Physical Sciences** section of the **Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham Virtual Lab**. The virtual lab is an online platform that provides remote access to labs in various disciplines of science and engineering. The virtual lab simulates the real lab environment and allows the user to perform the experiment with interactive animations, graphics and videos.

The objective of the experiment is to determine the refractive index of a prism by measuring the angle of minimum deviation for different wavelengths of light using a spectrometer. The spectrometer is an instrument that splits light into its constituent colors and measures the angle of incidence and refraction of the light rays. The prism is a transparent solid object that refracts light at its faces. The angle of minimum deviation is the smallest angle between the incident and emergent rays when the prism is rotated.

The procedure of the experiment is as follows:

- Adjust the collimator slit width and the eyepiece of the telescope to get a clear image of the slit.
- Place the prism on the prism table and rotate it until the refracted ray is parallel to the base of the prism.
- Rotate the telescope to view the refracted ray and note down the reading of the vernier scale.
- Rotate the prism in the opposite direction until the refracted ray is again parallel to the base of the prism.
- Rotate the telescope to view the refracted ray and note down the reading of the vernier scale.
- Repeat the steps for different wavelengths of light by changing the color filter in the collimator.
- Calculate the angle of deviation for each wavelength by subtracting the readings of the vernier scale.
- Plot a graph of angle of deviation versus wavelength and find the minimum point on the curve.
- Calculate the refractive index of the prism using the formula:

n = (sin(A + Dm/2))/(sin(A/2))

where n is the refractive index, A is the angle of the prism, and Dm is the angle of minimum deviation.

Some mnemonics and learning tricks for the experiment are:

- Remember the acronym **SPAR** for the four parts of the spectrometer: **S**lit, **P**rism, **A**ngle, **R**efraction.
- Remember the formula for the refractive index as **n = sin(A + DAD)/sin(A/2)**, where **DAD** stands for **D**eviation **A**t **D**eviation **m**inimum.
- Remember that the angle of minimum deviation is the smallest angle between the incident and emergent rays, and it occurs when the refracted ray is parallel to the base of the prism.
