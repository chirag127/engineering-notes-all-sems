##### 5. Determination of parameters of ac single phase series RLC circuit.

- An ac single phase series RLC circuit is a circuit that consists of a resistor, an inductor, and a capacitor connected in series across an alternating voltage source.
- The voltage source has an emf of the form E = E0 sin ωt, where E0 is the peak voltage, ω is the angular frequency, and t is the time.
- The current in the circuit is the same for all the components and has the form I = I0 sin (ωt + φ), where I0 is the peak current, and φ is the phase angle between the current and the voltage.
- The phase angle φ depends on the relative values of the resistance R, the inductive reactance XL = ωL, and the capacitive reactance XC = 1/ωC, where L is the inductance and C is the capacitance of the circuit.
- The total impedance Z of the circuit is given by Z = √(R^2 + (XL - XC)^2), which is the ratio of the peak voltage to the peak current, Z = E0/I0.
- The power factor cos φ of the circuit is given by cos φ = R/Z, which is the ratio of the average power dissipated by the resistor to the average power supplied by the voltage source.
- The resonance frequency f0 of the circuit is the frequency at which the inductive reactance and the capacitive reactance are equal, XL = XC, and the phase angle is zero, φ = 0. The resonance frequency is given by f0 = 1/2π√LC.
- At resonance, the impedance of the circuit is minimum, Z = R, the current is maximum, I0 = E0/R, and the power factor is unity, cos φ = 1.
- The quality factor Q of the circuit is a measure of how sharp the resonance is, and is given by Q = ω0L/R = 1/ω0CR, where ω0 = 2πf0 is the angular resonance frequency.
- The bandwidth Δf of the circuit is the range of frequencies around the resonance frequency for which the power dissipated by the resistor is at least half of the maximum power. The bandwidth is given by Δf = f2 - f1 = R/2πL, where f1 and f2 are the lower and upper cutoff frequencies, respectively.

- A mnemonic to remember the formula for the resonance frequency is: **F**or **R**esonance, **L**et **C**urrent **B**e **M**aximum. The first letters of each word form the equation f0 = 1/2π√LC.
- A mnemonic to remember the formula for the quality factor is: **Q**uality **F**actor **I**s **L**arge **W**hen **R**esistance **I**s **S**mall. The first letters of each word form the equation Q = ω0L/R.
