#### Course Outcome (CO) Bloom’s Level

- Course Outcome (CO) is a statement that describes what a learner should be able to do at the end of a course or a unit of instruction.
- Bloom’s Level is a classification of the cognitive skills that learners need to demonstrate in order to achieve the CO.
- Bloom’s Level is based on Bloom’s Taxonomy, a framework that divides cognitive learning into six levels: remember, understand, apply, analyze, evaluate, and create.
- Each level of Bloom’s Taxonomy requires a higher degree of cognitive complexity and mastery than the previous one.
- The CO Bloom’s Level indicates the level of cognitive challenge and expectation for the learner in a course or a unit of instruction.
- The CO Bloom’s Level can be used to design learning objectives, assessments, and activities that align with the intended CO.
- The CO Bloom’s Level can also be used to measure the learner’s achievement and progress towards the CO.
- The CO Bloom’s Level can be expressed as a verb that describes the cognitive action that the learner should be able to perform at the end of the course or unit of instruction.
- For example, a CO Bloom’s Level of remember means that the learner should be able to recall or recognize factual information from the course or unit of instruction. A CO Bloom’s Level of create means that the learner should be able to produce or generate new or original ideas, products, or solutions based on the course or unit of instruction.
- Some examples of verbs for each level of Bloom’s Taxonomy are:

  - Remember: list, define, identify, name, recall, recognize, etc.
  - Understand: explain, describe, summarize, paraphrase, interpret, illustrate, etc.
  - Apply: use, demonstrate, solve, calculate, apply, perform, etc.
  - Analyze: compare, contrast, differentiate, categorize, classify, examine, etc.
  - Evaluate: judge, critique, assess, appraise, justify, argue, etc.
  - Create: design, invent, compose, construct, produce, generate, etc.

- A mnemonic to remember the six levels of Bloom’s Taxonomy is: **R**eally **U**gly **A**nts **A**re **E**ating **C**ookies. (Remember, Understand, Apply, Analyze, Evaluate, Create)
