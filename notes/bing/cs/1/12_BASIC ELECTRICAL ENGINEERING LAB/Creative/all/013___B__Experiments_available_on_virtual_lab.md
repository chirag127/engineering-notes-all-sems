#### (B) Experiments available on virtual lab

- Virtual labs are online simulations of real laboratories that allow students to perform experiments on various subjects such as physics, chemistry, biology, etc. without the need for physical equipment, materials, or safety precautions.
- Virtual labs can be used to supplement or replace traditional lab activities, enhance conceptual understanding, develop scientific skills, and foster inquiry-based learning.
- Some of the benefits of virtual labs are:
  - They are accessible anytime and anywhere with an internet connection.
  - They are cost-effective and environmentally friendly, as they reduce the use of resources and waste.
  - They are safe and ethical, as they eliminate the risks of accidents, injuries, or harm to living organisms.
  - They are flexible and customizable, as they allow students to manipulate variables, explore different scenarios, and repeat experiments as many times as they want.
  - They are interactive and engaging, as they provide immediate feedback, animations, sounds, and visualizations.
- Some of the challenges of virtual labs are:
  - They may not fully replicate the real-world conditions and phenomena, as they rely on mathematical models and algorithms.
  - They may not foster the development of manual skills, such as handling equipment, measuring, and recording data.
  - They may not promote social interaction and collaboration, as they are often done individually or remotely.
  - They may not motivate students to learn, as they may lack authenticity, relevance, or challenge.
- Some of the examples of virtual labs are:
  - ChemCollective Virtual Labs: A collection of interactive web-based chemistry tutorials that cover topics such as stoichiometry, equilibrium, kinetics, acid-base chemistry, and more. Students can design and perform their own experiments, or follow pre-designed scenarios and questions. 
  - Labster: A fully equipped virtual laboratory for Android that allows students to perform experiments on physics, chemistry, and biology. It was recognized with the 2020 award for Outstanding Contribution to Quality Science Education. Students can explore realistic 3D environments, conduct experiments, and solve problems. 
  - Nova Labs: A platform for teens and older learners to explore and examine cutting-edge issues in science and engineering, such as cybersecurity, evolution, energy, and more. Students can use real data, conduct investigations, and interact with experts. 
  - Molecular Workbench: A software that offers hundreds of virtual science labs for physics, biology, chemistry, and more. Students can manipulate atoms and molecules, observe phenomena, and run experiments. 
  - Mystery Science: A website that offers a wealth of virtual science labs, activities, and experiments for students in elementary school. From weather to astronomy, humans to animals, they’ve got you covered. Students can watch videos, answer questions, and do hands-on activities.
