### Note: A minimum of ten experiments from the following should be performed.

- The following is a list of possible experiments that can be performed in a laboratory course on data structures and algorithms. The experiments are designed to help students learn and practice the concepts and techniques of data structures and algorithms, such as arrays, lists, stacks, queues, trees, graphs, sorting, searching, hashing, etc. The experiments are not exhaustive and can be modified or extended according to the instructor's preference and the availability of resources.

- Experiment 1: Implement and compare the performance of different array operations, such as insertion, deletion, traversal, searching, sorting, merging, etc.

- Experiment 2: Implement and compare the performance of different types of linked lists, such as singly linked, doubly linked, circular linked, etc. Perform various operations on the linked lists, such as insertion, deletion, traversal, reversal, concatenation, etc.

- Experiment 3: Implement and compare the performance of different types of stacks, such as array-based, linked list-based, dynamic array-based, etc. Perform various operations on the stacks, such as push, pop, peek, isEmpty, isFull, etc.

- Experiment 4: Implement and compare the performance of different types of queues, such as array-based, linked list-based, circular queue, priority queue, double-ended queue, etc. Perform various operations on the queues, such as enqueue, dequeue, front, rear, isEmpty, isFull, etc.

- Experiment 5: Implement and compare the performance of different types of trees, such as binary tree, binary search tree, AVL tree, red-black tree, B-tree, etc. Perform various operations on the trees, such as insertion, deletion, traversal, searching, height, balance, etc.

- Experiment 6: Implement and compare the performance of different types of graphs, such as directed, undirected, weighted, unweighted, etc. Perform various operations on the graphs, such as adjacency matrix, adjacency list, degree, indegree, outdegree, etc. Implement and compare the performance of different graph algorithms, such as breadth-first search, depth-first search, shortest path, minimum spanning tree, topological sort, etc.

- Experiment 7: Implement and compare the performance of different sorting algorithms, such as bubble sort, selection sort, insertion sort, merge sort, quick sort, heap sort, radix sort, etc. Analyze the time and space complexity of the sorting algorithms.

- Experiment 8: Implement and compare the performance of different searching algorithms, such as linear search, binary search, interpolation search, exponential search, etc. Analyze the time and space complexity of the searching algorithms.

- Experiment 9: Implement and compare the performance of different hashing techniques, such as direct addressing, division method, multiplication method, folding method, etc. Perform various operations on the hash tables, such as insertion, deletion, searching, collision resolution, etc.

- Experiment 10: Implement and compare the performance of different dynamic programming algorithms, such as Fibonacci series, matrix chain multiplication, longest common subsequence, knapsack problem, etc. Analyze the time and space complexity of the dynamic programming algorithms.

- Experiment 11: Implement and compare the performance of different greedy algorithms, such as fractional knapsack problem, activity selection problem, job sequencing problem, etc. Analyze the time and space complexity of the greedy algorithms.

- Experiment 12: Implement and compare the performance of different backtracking algorithms, such as n-queens problem, sudoku solver, subset sum problem, etc. Analyze the time and space complexity of the backtracking algorithms.

- Experiment 13: Implement and compare the performance of different divide and conquer algorithms, such as binary search, merge sort, quick sort, closest pair of points, etc. Analyze the time and space complexity of the divide and conquer algorithms.

- Experiment 14: Implement and compare the performance of different branch and bound algorithms, such as travelling salesman problem, 0/1 knapsack problem, assignment problem, etc. Analyze the time and space complexity of the branch and bound algorithms.

- Experiment 15: Implement and compare the performance of different randomized algorithms, such as randomized quick sort, randomized select, reservoir sampling, etc. Analyze the time and space complexity of the randomized algorithms.
