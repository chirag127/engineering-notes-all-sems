##### 3. Study of phenomenon of resonance in RLC series circuit and obtain resonant frequency.

- Resonance in a series RLC circuit occurs when the inductive reactance (XL) is equal to the capacitive reactance (XC), or when XL - XC = 0 .
- At resonance, the circuit current is in phase with the applied voltage, and the impedance of the circuit is equal to the resistance (Z = R).
- The resonant frequency (fr) of a series RLC circuit is given by the formula :

  fr = 1 / (2π√LC)

  where L is the inductance, C is the capacitance, and π is the mathematical constant.

- At resonance, the series RLC circuit behaves like a pure resistive circuit, and the power factor is 1 .
- The voltage across the inductor and the capacitor is equal in magnitude and opposite in phase, and they cancel each other.
- The voltage across the resistor is equal to the applied voltage, and the current reaches its maximum value.
- The series RLC circuit has a high selectivity, meaning it can filter out signals of a certain frequency from a mixture of signals of different frequencies .
- The bandwidth of the series RLC circuit is the range of frequencies for which the power dissipated in the circuit is at least half of the maximum power. It is given by the formula :

  BW = R / L

  where R is the resistance and L is the inductance.

- The quality factor (Q) of the series RLC circuit is a measure of how sharp the resonance peak is. It is given by the formula :

  Q = 1 / R √L / C

  where R is the resistance, L is the inductance, and C is the capacitance.

- The quality factor is also equal to the ratio of the resonant frequency to the bandwidth :

  Q = fr / BW

- A higher quality factor means a narrower bandwidth and a more selective circuit .
- The series RLC circuit can be used for tuning radios, filters, oscillators, and other applications that require a specific frequency response .

- A possible mnemonic to remember the formula for the resonant frequency is:

  **F**ind **R**esistance **L**ike **C**hristmas

  where F stands for frequency, R for resistance, L for inductance, and C for capacitance. The first letters of each word match the symbols in the formula. The word Christmas can remind us of the circular shape of the pi symbol (π) and the fact that resonance occurs when the circuit is in phase (like a harmony).
