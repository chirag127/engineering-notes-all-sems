##### 4. Measurement of power in 3- phase circuit by two wattmeter method and determination of its power factor for star as well as delta connected load.

- The two wattmeter method is a technique for measuring the power in a three-phase circuit using two wattmeters.
- A wattmeter is an instrument that measures the power in a single-phase circuit by multiplying the voltage and current.
- In a three-phase circuit, the power is the sum of the powers in each phase, which can be measured by connecting three wattmeters in series with each phase and parallel with the load.
- However, using three wattmeters is not always convenient or economical, so the two wattmeter method is often used as an alternative.
- The two wattmeter method can be applied to both balanced and unbalanced loads, and to both star and delta connected loads.
- The principle of the two wattmeter method is based on the fact that the power in a three-phase circuit can be expressed as the sum of the products of the line voltages and the line currents, as shown below:

![formula](https://latex.codecogs.com/png.latex?P%3D%5Cfrac%7B1%7D%7B%5Csqrt%7B3%7D%7D%28V_%7BRY%7DI_%7BR%7D&plus;V_%7BYB%7DI_%7BY%7D&plus;V_%7BBR%7DI_%7BB%7D%29)

- If we connect the current coils of two wattmeters in series with the R and Y phases, and the potential coils of both wattmeters to the B phase, we can measure the power as follows:

![wattmeters](https://latex.codecogs.com/png.latex?P_%7B1%7D%3D%5Cfrac%7B1%7D%7B%5Csqrt%7B3%7D%7DV_%7BRY%7DI_%7BR%7D%5Cquad%20P_%7B2%7D%3D%5Cfrac%7B1%7D%7B%5Csqrt%7B3%7D%7DV_%7BYB%7DI_%7BY%7D)

- The total power is then the sum of the two wattmeter readings:
