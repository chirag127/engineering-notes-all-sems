### 1. Study of various types of Active & Passive Components based on their ratings.

- Active and passive components are the two main types of electronic circuit elements.
- Active components are devices that can produce or control the flow of electric current or voltage, such as transistors, diodes, integrated circuits, etc. They usually require an external power source to operate and can provide power gain or amplification to the circuit.
- Passive components are devices that can store or dissipate electric energy, such as resistors, capacitors, inductors, etc. They do not require an external power source to operate and cannot provide power gain or amplification to the circuit.
- The ratings of active and passive components are the specifications or limits that define their performance, quality, and reliability. They indicate the maximum or minimum values of certain parameters that the components can handle or operate under, such as voltage, current, power, resistance, capacitance, inductance, frequency, temperature, etc.
- The ratings of active and passive components are important to consider when designing, selecting, or using electronic circuits, as they affect the functionality, efficiency, and safety of the components and the circuit. Exceeding the ratings of the components can cause damage, failure, or malfunction of the components or the circuit.
- Some examples of ratings of active and passive components are:

| Component | Rating | Description | Example |
|-----------|--------|-------------|---------|
| Transistor | Vce | Collector-emitter voltage | The maximum voltage that can be applied between the collector and emitter terminals of the transistor. |
| Diode | Vf | Forward voltage | The voltage drop across the diode when it is forward biased. |
| Integrated circuit | Vcc | Supply voltage | The voltage that powers the integrated circuit. |
| Resistor | R | Resistance | The opposition to the flow of electric current through the resistor. |
| Capacitor | C | Capacitance | The ability to store electric charge. |
| Inductor | L | Inductance | The ability to generate an electromotive force when the current through it changes. |

- A mnemonic to remember the difference between active and passive components is: **A**ctive components **A**mplify, **P**assive components **P**ass.
