- CRO stands for Cathode Ray Oscilloscope. It is a device that displays the waveform of an electrical signal on a screen. It can measure voltage, frequency, phase, and other parameters of a signal. It consists of a cathode ray tube (CRT), a vertical and a horizontal amplifier, a time base generator, and a trigger circuit.
- Multi meter is a device that can measure multiple electrical quantities, such as voltage, current, resistance, capacitance, and continuity. It can be either analog or digital. It consists of a display, a selector switch, and probes. It can be used to troubleshoot circuits, test components, and measure battery levels.
- Function Generator is a device that can generate different types of electrical signals, such as sine, square, triangle, and pulse waves. It can vary the frequency, amplitude, and shape of the output signal. It consists of a signal generator, a frequency control, an amplitude control, and an output terminal. It can be used to test circuits, devices, and systems, and to simulate various signals.
- Power supply is a device that converts AC voltage to DC voltage. It can provide a constant and regulated voltage to a circuit or a device. It consists of a transformer, a rectifier, a filter, and a regulator. It can be either fixed or variable. It can be used to power circuits, devices, and systems, and to charge batteries.
- Active components are those that can control the flow of current or voltage in a circuit. They require an external source of power to operate. Examples of active components are transistors, diodes, LEDs, and integrated circuits. They can be used to amplify, switch, rectify, and modulate signals, and to perform logic operations.
- Passive components are those that do not control the flow of current or voltage in a circuit. They do not require an external source of power to operate. Examples of passive components are resistors, capacitors, inductors, and transformers. They can be used to limit, store, filter, and transfer energy, and to perform impedance matching.
- Bread Board is a device that allows the user to create temporary circuits without soldering. It consists of a plastic board with holes and metal strips. The holes are connected by the metal strips in rows and columns. The user can insert wires and components into the holes to form a circuit. It can be used to prototype, test, and debug circuits, and to learn about electronics.

- These test equipment can be demonstrated online from any lab of ECE department or physical mode is only option. Online demonstration can be done using simulation software, such as Multisim, Proteus, or CircuitLab. These software can create virtual circuits and components, and display the results on the screen. Physical mode demonstration can be done using real circuits and components, and measuring the results using the test equipment. Both modes have their advantages and disadvantages. Online mode is convenient, cost-effective, and safe, but it may not be accurate, realistic, or interactive. Physical mode is accurate, realistic, and interactive, but it may be inconvenient, expensive, and risky.
