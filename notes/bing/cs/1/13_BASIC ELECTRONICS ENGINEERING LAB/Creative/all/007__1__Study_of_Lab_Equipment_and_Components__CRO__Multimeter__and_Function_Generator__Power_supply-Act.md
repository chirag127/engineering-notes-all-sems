### 1. Study of Lab Equipment and Components: CRO, Multimeter, and Function Generator, Power supply-Active, Passive Components and Bread Board.

- CRO stands for **Cathode Ray Oscilloscope**, which is a device that displays the waveform of an electrical signal on a screen. It can measure voltage, frequency, phase, and other parameters of a signal. It consists of a cathode ray tube (CRT), which produces a beam of electrons that hits a phosphor-coated screen and creates a visible trace. The beam is deflected by two sets of plates, one for horizontal and one for vertical movement, which are controlled by the input signal and a time base generator. The CRO can have one or more channels, which allow it to display multiple signals simultaneously. Some of the applications of CRO are:

  - Testing and debugging of electronic circuits
  - Measuring the characteristics of signals, such as amplitude, frequency, phase, distortion, etc.
  - Displaying the waveform of audio, video, and other signals
  - Analyzing the frequency spectrum of signals using a spectrum analyzer

- Multimeter is a device that can measure multiple electrical quantities, such as voltage, current, resistance, capacitance, etc. It can be either analog or digital, depending on the type of display it uses. An analog multimeter uses a needle that moves along a scale, while a digital multimeter shows the numerical value on a screen. A multimeter can have different modes and ranges, which allow it to measure different types of signals and components. Some of the applications of multimeter are:

  - Testing and troubleshooting of electronic circuits and components
  - Measuring the voltage, current, and resistance of batteries, power supplies, resistors, etc.
  - Checking the continuity and polarity of wires and connections
  - Measuring the capacitance and inductance of capacitors and coils

- Function Generator is a device that can generate different types of electrical signals, such as sine, square, triangle, pulse, etc. It can vary the frequency, amplitude, and shape of the output signal. It can also modulate the signal with another signal, such as amplitude modulation (AM), frequency modulation (FM), etc. A function generator can have one or more channels, which allow it to produce multiple signals simultaneously. Some of the applications of function generator are:

  - Testing and calibrating of electronic circuits and devices
  - Simulating and generating different types of signals, such as audio, radio, etc.
  - Studying the response and behavior of circuits and systems to different inputs
  - Performing experiments and demonstrations in electronics and physics

- Power Supply is a device that converts the alternating current (AC) from the mains to a direct current (DC) of a desired voltage and current. It can be either fixed or variable, depending on the ability to adjust the output parameters. It can also have different types of outputs, such as regulated, unregulated, filtered, etc. A power supply can have one or more outputs, which allow it to supply power to multiple circuits or devices. Some of the applications of power supply are:

  - Providing power to electronic circuits and devices
  - Testing and measuring the performance and efficiency of circuits and devices
  - Charging and discharging of batteries and capacitors
  - Generating high voltage and current for special purposes, such as lasers, X-rays, etc.

- Active Components are the electronic components that can control the flow of current or voltage in a circuit. They usually require an external power source to operate. They can amplify, switch, or modify the input signal. Some of the examples of active components are:

  - Transistors, which can act as amplifiers or switches
  - Diodes, which can rectify, regulate, or emit light
  - Integrated Circuits (ICs), which can perform complex functions, such as logic, memory, etc.
  - Sensors, which can detect and convert physical quantities, such as temperature, light, etc.

- Passive Components are the electronic components that do not control the flow of current or voltage in a circuit. They do not require an external power source to operate. They can store, dissipate, or filter the input signal. Some of the examples of passive components are:

  - Resistors, which can limit or divide the current or voltage
  - Capacitors, which can store or filter the charge or voltage
  - Inductors, which can store or filter the magnetic flux or current
  - Transformers, which can step up or step down the voltage or current
