For example, if the primary coil has 100 windings and the secondary coil has 10 windings, the voltage transformation ratio is 10:1, meaning that the secondary voltage is 10 times smaller than the primary voltage.

The power rating indicates how much power the transformer can handle without overheating or damaging its components.
