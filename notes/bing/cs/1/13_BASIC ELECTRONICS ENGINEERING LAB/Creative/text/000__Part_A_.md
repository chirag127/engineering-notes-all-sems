## Part A:

- Part A is the first section of a document or a report that contains the introduction, the background, the objectives, and the scope of the work.
- Part A is important because it sets the context and the purpose of the document or the report for the reader. It also provides an overview of the main topics and the structure of the document or the report.
- Part A should be clear, concise, and informative. It should not include unnecessary details or information that belongs to other sections of the document or the report.
- Part A should answer the following questions:
  - What is the topic or the problem that the document or the report addresses?
  - Why is the topic or the problem important or relevant?
  - What are the main goals or objectives of the document or the report?
  - What are the limitations or the boundaries of the document or the report?
