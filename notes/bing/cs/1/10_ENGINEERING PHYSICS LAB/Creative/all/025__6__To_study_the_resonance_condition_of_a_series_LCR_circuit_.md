##### 6. To study the resonance condition of a series LCR circuit.

- A series LCR circuit consists of a resistor (R), an inductor (L) and a capacitor (C) connected in series to an alternating voltage source (V).
- The current (I) in the circuit is the same for all the components, but the voltage across each component may differ depending on their reactance.
- The reactance of an inductor is given by XL = 2πfL, where f is the frequency of the alternating voltage and L is the inductance.
- The reactance of a capacitor is given by XC = 1/(2πfC), where C is the capacitance.
- The total impedance (Z) of the circuit is given by Z = R + j(XL - XC), where j is the imaginary unit.
- The impedance is a complex quantity that has a magnitude and a phase angle. The magnitude of the impedance is given by |Z| = √(R^2 + (XL - XC)^2) and the phase angle is given by tan(φ) = (XL - XC)/R, where φ is the angle between the current and the voltage.
- The resonance condition of a series LCR circuit occurs when the inductive reactance (XL) is equal to the capacitive reactance (XC), or when XL - XC = 0. This means that the impedance is purely resistive and has a minimum value of R. The phase angle is also zero, meaning that the current and the voltage are in phase.
- The resonance frequency (fr) of a series LCR circuit is given by fr = 1/(2π√(LC)), where L and C are the inductance and capacitance of the circuit respectively.
- At resonance, the current in the circuit is maximum and is given by Ir = V/R, where V is the peak voltage of the source.
- The power dissipated in the circuit is also maximum at resonance and is given by Pr = V^2/R, where V and R are the peak voltage and the resistance of the circuit respectively.
- The quality factor (Q) of a series LCR circuit is a measure of how sharp the resonance is. It is given by Q = XL/R = XC/R = 1/(R√(LC)), where XL, XC and R are the inductive, capacitive and resistive reactances of the circuit respectively. A higher Q means a narrower bandwidth and a higher selectivity of the circuit.

Some mnemonics and learning tricks for the series LCR circuit are:

- To remember the formula for the resonance frequency, think of the word "furl", which sounds like "fr" and has the letters L and C in it.
- To remember the formula for the quality factor, think of the word "quirk", which sounds like "Q" and has the letters R, L and C in it.
- To remember the phase angle formula, think of the word "tax", which sounds like "tan" and has the letters X and R in it. The sign of the phase angle depends on whether XL is greater than XC or vice versa. If XL > XC, then the circuit is inductive and the phase angle is positive. If XL < XC, then the circuit is capacitive and the phase angle is negative. If XL = XC, then the circuit is resonant and the phase angle is zero.
