##### 10. To measure high resistance by leakage method.

- The leakage method is a technique to measure high resistance by using a capacitor, a ballistic galvanometer, and a stopwatch.
- The basic principle is to charge a capacitor through a high resistance and then discharge it through a ballistic galvanometer, which measures the charge by the deflection of a spot of light.
- The high resistance is calculated by using the formula:

  R = t / 2.3026 C log10 θ0 / θ1

  Where:

  - R is the high resistance in ohms
  - t is the time period of the leakage of the capacitor through the resistance in seconds
  - C is the capacitance of the capacitor in farads
  - θ0 is the first deflection of the spot of light when the capacitor is initially discharged through the galvanometer in degrees
  - θ1 is the first deflection of the spot of light when the capacitor is discharged through the galvanometer after a leakage of charge for time t through R in degrees

- The procedure is as follows:

  - Connect the capacitor, the high resistance, and a battery in series.
  - Close the switch to charge the capacitor through the high resistance for a few seconds.
  - Open the switch and connect the capacitor to the ballistic galvanometer.
  - Note the first deflection of the spot of light on the scale, which is θ0.
  - Disconnect the capacitor from the galvanometer and reconnect it to the high resistance.
  - Start the stopwatch and close the switch to allow the capacitor to leak through the high resistance.
  - After a known time t, open the switch and connect the capacitor to the galvanometer again.
  - Note the first deflection of the spot of light on the scale, which is θ1.
  - Repeat the experiment for different values of t and C and calculate the average value of R.

- The advantages of this method are:

  - It is very accurate and sensitive because of the low capacitance and the high resolution of the galvanometer.
  - It can measure very high resistances up to 10^18 ohms.
  - It does not require a constant voltage source or a standard resistance.

- The disadvantages of this method are:

  - It is time-consuming and tedious because of the multiple steps and measurements involved.
  - It is prone to errors due to leakage currents, temperature variations, and parallax errors.
  - It requires a dark room and a stable platform for the galvanometer and the scale.

- The applications of this method are:

  - It can be used to measure the insulation resistance of cables, wires, and transformers.
  - It can be used to measure the resistivity of materials such as semiconductors, ceramics, and polymers.
  - It can be used to study the dielectric properties of capacitors and the charge storage mechanisms of batteries.
