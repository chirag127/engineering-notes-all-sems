### 1. Practical Physics- K. K. Dey & B. N. Dutta (Kalyani Publishers New Delhi)

- This is a book that covers various topics in practical physics, such as mechanics, heat, optics, electricity, magnetism, electronics, and modern physics.
- The book is intended as a companion to any undergraduate course of practical work in physics, and it demonstrates the purposive and critical approach that should be made to all experimental work in physics.
- The book contains 434 pages and is divided into 12 chapters, each with a number of experiments, diagrams, tables, graphs, and questions.
- The book also provides some useful appendices, such as physical constants, conversion factors, error analysis, and logarithm tables.
- Some of the advantages of this book are:
  - It explains the theoretical background, experimental procedure, and analysis of results for each experiment in a clear and concise manner.
  - It provides many examples, illustrations, and numerical problems to help the students understand the concepts and applications of practical physics.
  - It covers a wide range of topics and experiments that are relevant to the current syllabus and trends in physics.
- Some of the disadvantages of this book are:
  - It does not provide any mnemonics or learning tricks for the students to remember the formulas, concepts, or results.
  - It does not provide any online resources or interactive features to supplement the learning process.
  - It may not be updated with the latest developments or discoveries in physics.
