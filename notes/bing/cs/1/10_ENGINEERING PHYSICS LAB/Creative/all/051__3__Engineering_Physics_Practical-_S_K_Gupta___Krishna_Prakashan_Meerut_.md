### 3. Engineering Physics Practical- S K Gupta ( Krishna Prakashan Meerut)

- This is a book that contains practical experiments and activities related to engineering physics for undergraduate students.
- The book covers topics such as optics, electricity, magnetism, electronics, mechanics, and modern physics.
- The book provides detailed instructions, diagrams, tables, graphs, and calculations for each experiment.
- The book also includes questions and answers, viva voce questions, and model test papers for revision and assessment.
- The book is written by S K Gupta, a professor of physics at Meerut Institute of Engineering and Technology, and published by Krishna Prakashan Media in 2014.
- The book is available in paperback and Kindle editions.

Some of the mnemonics and learning tricks for the book are:

- To remember the formula for refractive index of a prism, use the acronym AID: n = (A + D)/2
- To remember the formula for resolving power of a telescope, use the acronym ROD: R = D/d
- To remember the formula for magnetic field due to a current carrying wire, use the acronym BIL: B = mu_0 I/2 pi L
- To remember the formula for capacitance of a parallel plate capacitor, use the acronym CAKE: C = epsilon_0 A/d
- To remember the formula for frequency of a tuning fork, use the acronym FAN: f = n/2L
- To remember the formula for energy of a photon, use the acronym EHF: E = h f
