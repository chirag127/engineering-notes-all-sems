#### Level
- A level is a tool or device that is used to measure or indicate whether a surface is horizontal (level) or vertical (plumb).
- A level consists of a horizontal tube or vial that contains a liquid (usually alcohol or water) and an air bubble. The tube is slightly curved, so that the bubble moves to the center when the tube is parallel to the ground. The tube is mounted on a frame or a ruler that has markings to indicate the center position of the bubble.
- A level can be used for various purposes, such as:
  - Checking the alignment of walls, floors, ceilings, shelves, furniture, etc.
  - Hanging pictures, mirrors, paintings, etc. on the wall.
  - Installing doors, windows, cabinets, etc.
  - Measuring the slope or angle of a surface or a line.
  - Determining the elevation or height of a point or an object.
- A level can have different shapes and sizes, depending on the application and the accuracy required. Some common types of levels are:
  - Spirit level: A simple level that has one or more tubes or vials. It is also called a bubble level or a carpenter's level.
  - Laser level: A level that projects a laser beam or a dot on a surface or a wall. It can be used to create a straight line or a reference point over a long distance or in a dark area.
  - Digital level: A level that has a digital display that shows the angle or the inclination of the surface or the line. It can also have a sound or a light indicator that signals when the level is at zero or a preset angle.
  - Torpedo level: A small and compact level that has a magnetic base. It can be attached to metal surfaces or pipes. It is also called a plumber's level or a pocket level.
  - Line level: A level that is attached to a string or a wire. It can be used to measure the level of a surface or a line over a large area or an uneven terrain. It is also called a string level or a mason's level.
