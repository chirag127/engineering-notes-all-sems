#### CO CO Statement Bloom’s

- CO CO Statement Bloom’s is a mnemonic device to help remember the six levels of cognitive objectives in Bloom's taxonomy: Comprehension, Application, Analysis, Synthesis, and Evaluation.
- Bloom's taxonomy is a framework for classifying educational goals according to the type and level of cognitive skills required to achieve them.
- The six levels of cognitive objectives are:

  - **Comprehension**: The ability to understand and interpret information, such as facts, concepts, principles, or procedures.
  - **Application**: The ability to use learned information in new or practical situations, such as solving problems, performing tasks, or demonstrating skills.
  - **Analysis**: The ability to break down information into its component parts and examine their relationships, such as identifying causes, effects, patterns, or structures.
  - **Synthesis**: The ability to combine information from different sources or domains to create something new or original, such as generating ideas, hypotheses, plans, or products.
  - **Evaluation**: The ability to judge the quality, value, or effectiveness of information, arguments, or outcomes, based on criteria, standards, or evidence.
  - **Creation**: The ability to produce something novel or innovative that demonstrates a high level of cognitive skill and originality, such as designing, inventing, composing, or constructing.

- The mnemonic device CO CO Statement Bloom’s can be remembered by using the first letters of each level and forming a sentence: **C**omprehension **O**f **C**oncepts **O**pens **S**tatement **B**loom’s. Alternatively, one can use the acronym COCASB to recall the levels in order.
- CO CO Statement Bloom’s can be useful for teachers and students to design, assess, and improve learning outcomes and activities that align with the cognitive objectives of Bloom's taxonomy. For example, a teacher can use CO CO Statement Bloom’s to create questions or tasks that target different levels of cognitive skills, such as:

  - Comprehension: What is the main idea of this text?
  - Application: How can you apply this concept to a real-world situation?
  - Analysis: How does this argument compare and contrast with another one?
  - Synthesis: How can you combine these two ideas to form a new one?
  - Evaluation: How would you evaluate the validity of this claim?
  - Creation: How would you create a new product based on this information?

- Similarly, a student can use CO CO Statement Bloom’s to monitor and enhance their own learning process and outcomes, such as:

  - Comprehension: Do I understand the meaning and purpose of this information?
  - Application: Can I use this information to solve a problem or perform a task?
  - Analysis: Can I identify the main components and relationships of this information?
  - Synthesis: Can I integrate this information with other sources or domains to create something new?
  - Evaluation: Can I critically assess the quality and value of this information?
