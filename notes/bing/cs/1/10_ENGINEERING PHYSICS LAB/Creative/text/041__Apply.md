#### Apply

- Apply is a verb that means to put something into action or use, or to request something formally, such as a job, admission, or grant.
- Apply can also mean to spread or cover something with a substance, such as paint, cream, or glue.
- Apply has several synonyms, such as employ, implement, utilize, administer, assign, or petition.
- Apply can be used in different contexts and with different prepositions, such as:
  - Apply for a job, a visa, a scholarship, or a permit.
  - Apply to a college, a university, a program, or a position.
  - Apply something to a surface, a problem, a situation, or a wound.
  - Apply oneself to a task, a goal, a study, or a hobby.
- Apply can be used in different grammatical forms, such as:
  - Applied: the past tense and past participle of apply.
  - Applying: the present participle and gerund of apply.
  - Applies: the third person singular present tense of apply.
  - Application: the noun form of apply, meaning the act of applying or the document that is submitted when applying for something.
  - Applicable: the adjective form of apply, meaning relevant or suitable to a particular case or situation.
  - Applicant: the noun form of apply, meaning a person who applies for something.
