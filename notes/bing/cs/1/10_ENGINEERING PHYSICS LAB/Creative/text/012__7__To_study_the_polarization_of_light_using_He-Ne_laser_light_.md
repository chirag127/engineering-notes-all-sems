##### 7. To study the polarization of light using He-Ne laser light.

- Polarization of light is the phenomenon of restricting the vibrations of the electric field vector of a light wave to a certain plane or direction.
- A polarizer is a device that can produce polarized light from an unpolarized light source, such as a He-Ne laser.
- A He-Ne laser is a gas laser that uses a mixture of helium and neon as the active medium and emits a red light of wavelength 632.8 nm.
- The polarization properties of a He-Ne laser depend on the cavity design, the mode structure, and the mode competition of the laser.
- A linearly polarized He-Ne laser has a single transverse mode with a fixed orientation of the electric field vector.
- A circularly polarized He-Ne laser has two orthogonal transverse modes with equal amplitudes and a phase difference of 90 degrees, resulting in a rotating electric field vector.
- An elliptically polarized He-Ne laser has two orthogonal transverse modes with unequal amplitudes and a phase difference of 90 degrees, resulting in an elliptical electric field vector.
- To study the polarization of light using a He-Ne laser, one can use a polarizer and an analyzer, which are two polarizing filters that can transmit or block light depending on the orientation of their transmission axes.
- By rotating the polarizer and the analyzer, one can observe the changes in the intensity and the state of polarization of the transmitted light.
- The intensity of the transmitted light is given by Malus' law: I = I0 cos^2 θ, where I0 is the intensity of the incident light, θ is the angle between the transmission axes of the polarizer and the analyzer, and I is the intensity of the transmitted light.
- The state of polarization of the transmitted light can be determined by using a quarter-wave plate, which is a device that can introduce a phase difference of 90 degrees between two orthogonal components of a light wave.
- By placing a quarter-wave plate between the polarizer and the analyzer, one can convert a linearly polarized light into a circularly or elliptically polarized light, or vice versa, depending on the orientation of the quarter-wave plate.
- The state of polarization of the transmitted light can also be visualized by using a polarizing microscope, which is a device that can magnify and display the polarization patterns of a light wave.
