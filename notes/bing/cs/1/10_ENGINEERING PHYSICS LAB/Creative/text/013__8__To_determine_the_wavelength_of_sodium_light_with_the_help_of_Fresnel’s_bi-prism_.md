##### 8. To determine the wavelength of sodium light with the help of Fresnel’s bi-prism.

- Fresnel's bi-prism is a thin prism with a very small refracting angle, usually less than 1 degree. It is used to produce two coherent sources of light from a single source, by splitting the light beam into two parts that are deviated by the prism.
- The principle of the experiment is based on the interference of light waves from the two virtual sources created by the bi-prism. The interference pattern consists of alternate bright and dark fringes of equal width, which can be observed on a screen placed perpendicular to the bisector of the prism angle.
- The wavelength of the sodium light can be calculated from the following formula:

  `λ = Dd/D'`

  where `λ` is the wavelength, `D` is the distance between the slit and the screen, `d` is the distance between the two virtual sources, and `D'` is the distance between two consecutive bright or dark fringes.
- The experimental setup consists of the following components:

  - A sodium lamp as the source of monochromatic light.
  - A narrow slit to produce a parallel beam of light.
  - A convex lens to focus the light beam on the slit.
  - A Fresnel's bi-prism to split the light beam into two coherent beams.
  - A micrometer eyepiece to measure the fringe width on the screen.
  - A travelling microscope to measure the distance between the virtual sources.
- The procedure of the experiment is as follows:

  - Adjust the slit, the lens and the bi-prism such that the light beam is parallel and passes through the center of the prism.
  - Place the screen at a suitable distance from the bi-prism and observe the interference fringes.
  - Move the screen closer or farther from the bi-prism until the fringes are clear and sharp.
  - Measure the distance `D` between the slit and the screen using a meter scale.
  - Measure the fringe width `D'` using the micrometer eyepiece. Take at least five readings and find the average value.
  - Remove the screen and place the travelling microscope in its position. Adjust the microscope to focus on the bi-prism and measure the distance `d` between the two virtual sources. Take at least five readings and find the average value.
  - Calculate the wavelength of the sodium light using the formula `λ = Dd/D'`.
  - Repeat the experiment for different values of `D` and find the mean value of `λ`.
