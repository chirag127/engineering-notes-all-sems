### 3. Engineering Physics Practical- S K Gupta ( Krishna Prakashan Meerut)

- This is a book that contains practical experiments and activities related to engineering physics for undergraduate students.
- The book covers topics such as optics, electricity, magnetism, electronics, mechanics, heat, sound, and modern physics.
- The book provides detailed instructions, diagrams, observations, calculations, and results for each experiment.
- The book also includes viva voce questions, model test papers, and appendices with useful tables and formulas.
- The book is written by S K Gupta, a professor of physics at Meerut Institute of Engineering and Technology, and published by Krishna Prakashan Media in 2014.
- The book is available in paperback and Kindle editions, and has 280 pages and an ISBN of 978-81-8283-783-6.
