#### Apply

- Apply is a verb that means to make use of something for a specific purpose, or to request something formally or officially.
- Apply can also mean to put or spread something on a surface, or to be relevant or valid in a situation.
- Some examples of apply are:

  - You can apply sunscreen to protect your skin from the sun.
  - You can apply for a job by sending your resume and cover letter to the employer.
  - You can apply the Pythagorean theorem to find the length of the hypotenuse of a right triangle.
  - The rules of chess apply only when you are playing chess, not when you are playing checkers.
