##### 10. To measure high resistance by leakage method.

- The leakage method is a technique to measure high resistance values (such as insulation resistance) by applying a constant voltage source and measuring the leakage current through the resistance.
- The leakage current is proportional to the applied voltage and inversely proportional to the resistance value, according to Ohm's law.
- The leakage method requires a high voltage source, a sensitive ammeter, and a high resistance standard for calibration.
