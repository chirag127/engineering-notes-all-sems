#### Graphical and Computational Analysis

Graphical and computational analysis are two methods of solving problems involving functions, equations, inequalities, and data. They can be used to model real-world situations, explore patterns and relationships, and find optimal solutions.

- Graphical analysis involves using graphs to represent and analyze functions, equations, inequalities, and data. Graphs can show the domain, range, intercepts, zeros, asymptotes, extrema, intervals of increase and decrease, and end behavior of a function. Graphs can also be used to compare different functions, equations, inequalities, and data sets, and to find solutions to systems of equations and inequalities.

- Computational analysis involves using numerical methods, algorithms, and technology to perform calculations and operations on functions, equations, inequalities, and data. Computational analysis can be used to find exact or approximate solutions, to evaluate and simplify expressions, to perform transformations and compositions of functions, and to analyze data using statistics and probability.

- Graphical and computational analysis can complement each other and provide different perspectives on a problem. For example, graphical analysis can help visualize the behavior and features of a function, while computational analysis can provide precise values and measurements. Graphical and computational analysis can also be used together to verify and check the accuracy of solutions.
