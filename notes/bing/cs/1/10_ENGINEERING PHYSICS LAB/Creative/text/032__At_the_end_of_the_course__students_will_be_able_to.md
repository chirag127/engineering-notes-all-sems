### At the end of the course, students will be able to

- Demonstrate an understanding of the basic concepts and principles of the subject matter.
- Apply the learned skills and knowledge to solve problems and perform tasks related to the course objectives.
- Analyze and evaluate information, arguments, and evidence from various sources and perspectives.
- Communicate effectively and appropriately in written and oral forms, using the conventions and terminology of the discipline.
- Collaborate with others in a respectful and productive manner, respecting diversity and ethical standards.
- Reflect on their own learning process and outcomes, identifying strengths and areas for improvement.
