##### 4. To determine the focal length of the combination of two lenses separated by a distance

- The combination of two lenses separated by a distance can be treated as a single lens with an effective focal length given by the formula:

  1/f = 1/f1 + 1/f2 - d/f1f2

  where f is the effective focal length, f1 and f2 are the focal lengths of the individual lenses, and d is the distance between them.

- To determine the focal length of the combination, we can use the following steps:

  - Arrange the two lenses on an optical bench with a light source and a screen at the opposite ends. The distance between the lenses should be less than the sum of their focal lengths.

  - Adjust the position of the screen until a sharp image of the light source is obtained on it. Measure the distance between the light source and the screen. This is the object distance (u) for the combination.

  - Measure the distance between the first lens and the screen. This is the image distance (v) for the combination.

  - Use the lens formula 1/f = 1/u + 1/v to calculate the effective focal length (f) of the combination.

  - Repeat the experiment for different values of d and verify the formula for the effective focal length.
