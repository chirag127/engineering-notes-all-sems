##### 5. To determine resistance per unit length and specific resistance of a given resistance

- Resistance is the property of a material that opposes the flow of electric current through it. It is measured in ohms (Ω).
- Resistance per unit length is the ratio of resistance to the length of a material. It is measured in ohms per meter (Ω/m).
- Specific resistance or resistivity is the resistance of a material of unit length and unit cross-sectional area. It is measured in ohm-meter (Ωm).
- To determine the resistance per unit length and specific resistance of a given resistance, we need the following apparatus:
  - A resistance wire of known length and cross-sectional area
  - A meter bridge
  - A galvanometer
  - A battery
  - A jockey
  - A rheostat
  - A key
  - A resistance box
  - A screw gauge
  - A meter scale
- The procedure is as follows:
  - Connect the resistance wire between points A and B of the meter bridge, and the resistance box between points C and D.
  - Connect the galvanometer between the jockey and the point E, which is the midpoint of the bridge wire.
  - Connect the battery between points A and C, and insert the key in the circuit.
  - Adjust the rheostat to get a suitable current in the circuit.
  - Slide the jockey along the bridge wire and find the null point, where the galvanometer shows zero deflection.
  - Note the length of the bridge wire from point A to the null point, and call it l.
  - Repeat the experiment for different values of resistance in the resistance box, and note the corresponding values of l.
  - Plot a graph of l versus R, where R is the resistance in the resistance box.
  - The graph should be a straight line passing through the origin.
  - The slope of the graph gives the resistance per unit length of the given resistance wire.
  - Use the screw gauge to measure the diameter of the resistance wire, and calculate its cross-sectional area A.
  - Use the formula R = ρl/A, where ρ is the specific resistance, to calculate the specific resistance of the given resistance wire.
