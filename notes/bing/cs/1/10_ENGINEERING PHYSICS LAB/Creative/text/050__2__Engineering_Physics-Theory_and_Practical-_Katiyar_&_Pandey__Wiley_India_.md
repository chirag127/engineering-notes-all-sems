## 2. Engineering Physics-Theory and Practical- Katiyar & Pandey (Wiley India)

This is a textbook on engineering physics that covers both the theory and practical aspects of the subject. It is intended for undergraduate students of engineering and science. The book is divided into four parts:

- Part I: Mechanics and Properties of Matter
- Part II: Optics and Lasers
- Part III: Electricity and Magnetism
- Part IV: Modern Physics and Quantum Mechanics

Each part consists of several chapters that explain the concepts, principles, and applications of physics in engineering. The book also includes numerous solved examples, exercises, and numerical problems to enhance the understanding and problem-solving skills of the students. The book also provides a separate section on practical experiments that demonstrate the experimental verification of the theoretical concepts.

Some of the topics covered in the book are:

- Newton's laws of motion, work, energy, and power, rotational motion, gravitation, elasticity, fluid mechanics, and surface tension.
- Geometrical optics, interference, diffraction, polarization, optical fibers, holography, and lasers.
- Electrostatics, capacitance, current electricity, Kirchhoff's laws, magnetic effects of current, electromagnetic induction, alternating current, and electromagnetic waves.
- Atomic structure, photoelectric effect, X-rays, nuclear physics, radioactivity, and nuclear reactors.
- Wave-particle duality, de Broglie hypothesis, Heisenberg's uncertainty principle, Schrodinger equation, quantum numbers, atomic spectra, molecular spectra, and solid state physics.

The book is written in a simple and lucid language, with clear diagrams and illustrations. It is suitable for self-study as well as classroom learning. The book is designed to help the students develop a strong foundation in engineering physics and prepare them for their examinations and future careers.
