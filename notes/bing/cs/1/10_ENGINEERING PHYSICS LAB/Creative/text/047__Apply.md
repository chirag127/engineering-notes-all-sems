#### Apply

- Apply is a verb that means to use, put into action, or employ for a specific purpose or result.
- Apply can also mean to make a formal request or application for something, such as a job, a grant, or a visa.
- Apply can also mean to be relevant or applicable to a particular situation, person, or thing.
- Some examples of apply are:

  - You can apply sunscreen to protect your skin from the sun.
  - You can apply for a scholarship to fund your education.
  - You can apply the Pythagorean theorem to find the length of the hypotenuse of a right triangle.
  - You can apply the rules of grammar to write clear and correct sentences.

- Some synonyms of apply are:

  - Use
  - Implement
  - Utilize
  - Employ
  - Request
  - Seek
  - Submit
  - Relate
  - Pertain
  - Refer
