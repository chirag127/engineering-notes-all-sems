The formula for the focal length of a combination of lenses depends on whether the lenses are in contact or separated by some distance.

If the lenses are in contact, the combined focal length f is given by the sum of the reciprocals of the individual focal lengths f1 and f2:

1/f = 1/f1 + 1/f2

If the lenses are separated by some distance d, the combined focal length f is given by the following formula:

1/f = 1/f1 + 1/f2 - d/(f1f2)
