The Hall effect setup is a device that measures the Hall voltage generated by a magnetic field perpendicular to a current-carrying semiconductor material. The Hall voltage is proportional to the product of the current, the magnetic field, and the Hall coefficient of the material. The Hall coefficient depends on the type and density of the charge carriers in the material.
