The Hall effect is the phenomenon of a voltage difference (Hall voltage) generated across a conductor when a current flows through it in the presence of a magnetic field perpendicular to the current. The Hall effect can be used to measure the magnetic field strength, the sign and density of the charge carriers, and the mobility of the charge carriers in a material.
