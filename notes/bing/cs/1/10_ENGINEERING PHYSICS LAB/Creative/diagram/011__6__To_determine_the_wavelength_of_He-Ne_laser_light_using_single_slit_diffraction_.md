The single slit diffraction experiment is a setup where a coherent light source, such as a He-Ne laser, passes through a narrow slit and forms a diffraction pattern on a screen that is kept at a certain distance from the slit. The diffraction pattern consists of a bright central maximum and alternating dark and bright fringes on either side. The width and position of the fringes depend on the wavelength of the light and the width of the slit.
