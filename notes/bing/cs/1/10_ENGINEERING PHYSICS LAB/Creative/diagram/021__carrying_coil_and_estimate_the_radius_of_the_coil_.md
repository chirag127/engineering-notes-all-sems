A carrying coil is a coil of current-carrying wire that behaves like a magnet. It is also known as a solenoid or an electromagnet . The magnetic moment of the coil is determined by the number of turns, the current, and the area of the coil. The coil experiences a torque when placed in an external magnetic field, which depends on the angle between the magnetic moment and the field .

`A = L * W`

`C = 2 * pi * r`

`L * W = 2 * pi * r`

`r = L * W / (2 * pi)`
