A compound pendulum is a rigid body whose mass is not concentrated at one point and which is capable of oscillating about some fixed pivot (axis of rotation). In this experiment, we will determine the value of acceleration due to gravity (g) and the radius of gyration (k) for a compound pendulum rod containing holes.
