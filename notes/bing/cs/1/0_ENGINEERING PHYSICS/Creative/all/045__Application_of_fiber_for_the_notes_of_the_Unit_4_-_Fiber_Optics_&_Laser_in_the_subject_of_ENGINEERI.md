### Application of fiber for the notes of the Unit 4 - Fiber Optics & Laser in the subject of ENGINEERING PHYSICS

Fiber optics is the science and technology of transmitting data, voice, and images by the passage of light through thin, transparent fibers. Fiber optic cables are composed of one or more strands of glass or plastic, each with a core that carries the light signal and a cladding that reflects the light back into the core. Fiber optic cables have many advantages over conventional copper wires, such as higher bandwidth, lower attenuation, immunity to electromagnetic interference, and lighter weight.

Some of the major applications of fiber optics are:

- **Communication**: Fiber optics is widely used for voice, data, and video transmission in telecommunications, local area networks (LANs), industrial control systems, avionic systems, and military command, control, and communications systems. Fiber optics can carry more information over longer distances than copper wires, and can also provide more security and privacy by preventing wiretapping and signal leakage.
- **Medical**: Fiber optics is used for various medical purposes, such as endoscopy, surgery, dentistry, and biomedical sensing. Fiber optics can provide high-resolution images and illumination of internal organs and tissues, as well as deliver laser beams for cutting, cauterizing, or destroying abnormal cells. Fiber optics can also measure various physiological parameters, such as blood pressure, temperature, oxygen saturation, and glucose level.
- **Defence and Aerospace**: Fiber optics is used for data transmission in high-level data security fields of military and aerospace applications. These are used in wirings in aircraft, hydrophones for SONARs and Seismics applications, and optical sensors for navigation, guidance, and surveillance . Fiber optics can withstand harsh environments, such as high temperature, pressure, vibration, and radiation, and can also reduce the weight and power consumption of the systems.
- **Industrial**: Fiber optics is used for various industrial applications, such as process control, automation, robotics, welding, cutting, machining, and inspection. Fiber optics can provide fast and accurate data transmission and feedback, as well as deliver high-power laser beams for material processing and testing. Fiber optics can also enable remote and non-contact measurements of physical quantities, such as strain, displacement, temperature, and pressure.
- **Broadcasting**: Fiber optics is used for transmitting television and radio signals over long distances and to multiple locations. Fiber optics can offer higher quality and reliability of the signals, as well as lower cost and maintenance than coaxial cables or satellite links.
- **Lighting and Decorations**: Fiber optics is used for various lighting and decorative purposes, such as architectural, landscape, stage, museum, and underwater lighting, as well as fiber optic art, signs, and displays. Fiber optics can provide flexible, colorful, and safe lighting effects, as well as save energy and reduce heat generation.
