### Population inversion for the notes of the Unit 4 - Fiber Optics & Laser in the subject of ENGINEERING PHYSICS

- Population inversion is a necessary condition for all lasers, regardless of their power or wavelength, because all lasers operate on the same basic principle of a chain reaction.
- Population inversion occurs when a system (such as a group of atoms or molecules) exists in a state in which more members of the system are in higher, excited states than in lower, unexcited energy states.
- Normally, a system of atoms is in thermal equilibrium and there are always more atoms in low energy states than in higher ones, according to the Boltzmann distribution.
- To achieve population inversion, the system must be supplied with energy from an external source, such as an electric current or a flash lamp. This process is called pumping.
- The energy supplied by the pumping source excites some of the atoms from the ground state to higher energy levels. Some of these atoms may decay to lower energy levels by emitting photons spontaneously. This is called spontaneous emission.
- However, some of the atoms may remain in a metastable state, which is a long-lived excited state that does not decay easily. These atoms form the population inversion, as they outnumber the atoms in the ground state.
- When a photon of the same frequency and phase as the transition between the metastable state and the ground state passes by an atom in the metastable state, it can stimulate the atom to emit another photon of the same frequency and phase. This is called stimulated emission.
- The two photons then travel together in the same direction, forming a coherent beam of light. This beam can stimulate more atoms in the metastable state to emit more photons, creating a chain reaction of amplification. This is the basic mechanism of laser action.
- A mnemonic to remember the difference between spontaneous emission and stimulated emission is: Sponges soak up water, Stimuli trigger responses.
- A mnemonic to remember the steps of laser action is: Pump, Invert, Stimulate, Amplify.
