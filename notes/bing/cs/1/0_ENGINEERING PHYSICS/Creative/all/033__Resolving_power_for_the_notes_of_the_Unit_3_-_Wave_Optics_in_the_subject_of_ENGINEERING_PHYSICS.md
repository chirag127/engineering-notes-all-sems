### Resolving power for the notes of the Unit 3 - Wave Optics in the subject of ENGINEERING PHYSICS

- Resolving power is the ability of an optical instrument to distinguish between two closely spaced objects or sources of light.
- Resolving power depends on the resolution of the instrument, which is the minimum distance between two points that can be seen as separate by the observer or the detector.
- Resolving power is inversely proportional to the resolution, and directly proportional to the aperture (or diameter) of the instrument.
- Resolving power also depends on the wavelength of light used, and the refractive index of the medium in which the instrument is operating.
- Resolving power can be expressed by different formulas for different types of optical instruments, such as telescopes, microscopes, prisms, and gratings.
- For a telescope, the resolving power is given by:

  ![R_t = \frac{d}{1.22 \lambda}](https://latex.codecogs.com/png.latex?R_t%20%3D%20%5Cfrac%7Bd%7D%7B1.22%20%5Clambda%7D)

  where R_t is the resolving power, d is the diameter of the objective lens, and λ is the wavelength of light.

- For a microscope, the resolving power is given by:

  ![R_m = \frac{2n \sin \theta}{\lambda}](https://latex.codecogs.com/png.latex?R_m%20%3D%20%5Cfrac%7B2n%20%5Csin%20%5Ctheta%7D%7B%5Clambda%7D)

  where R_m is the resolving power, n is the refractive index of the medium between the objective lens and the specimen, θ is the half-angle of the cone of light entering the objective lens, and λ is the wavelength of light.

- For a prism, the resolving power is given by:

  ![R_p = \frac{\lambda}{d \lambda}](https://latex.codecogs.com/png.latex?R_p%20%3D%20%5Cfrac%7B%5Clambda%7D%7Bd%20%5Clambda%7D)

  where R_p is the resolving power, λ is the wavelength of the spectral line, and dλ is the difference between the wavelengths of two adjacent spectral lines.

- For a grating, the resolving power is given by:

  ![R_g = \frac{\lambda}{\Delta \lambda}](https://latex.codecogs.com/png.latex?R_g%20%3D%20%5Cfrac%7B%5Clambda%7D%7B%5CDelta%20%5Clambda%7D)

  where R_g is the resolving power, λ is the wavelength of the spectral line, and Δλ is the difference between the wavelengths of two adjacent spectral lines.

- Some mnemonics and learning tricks for resolving power are:

  - Remember that resolving power is the reciprocal of resolution, and resolution is the minimum distance between two points that can be seen as separate.
  - Remember that resolving power is directly proportional to the aperture (or diameter) of the instrument, and inversely proportional to the wavelength of light used.
  - Remember that resolving power also depends on the refractive index of the medium and the angle of the cone of light for microscopes.
  - Remember that resolving power is different for different types of instruments, and use the formulas accordingly.
