### Absent spectra for the notes of the Unit 3 - Wave Optics in the subject of ENGINEERING PHYSICS

- Absent spectra or missing order spectra are the phenomena of disappearance of certain orders of interference fringes in a thin film due to destructive interference of light waves.
- A thin film is a layer of material with thickness in the sub-nanometer to micron range. As light strikes the surface of a film, it is either transmitted or reflected at the upper surface. Light that is transmitted reaches the bottom surface and may once again be transmitted or reflected.
- The interference of light waves reflected from the upper and lower surfaces of a thin film depends on the thickness of the film, the wavelength of light, and the angle of incidence of light.
- The condition for constructive interference of light waves reflected from a thin film is given by:

    2nt = (m + 1/2)λ

    where n is the refractive index of the film, t is the thickness of the film, m is the order of interference, and λ is the wavelength of light in vacuum.

- The condition for destructive interference of light waves reflected from a thin film is given by:

    2nt = mλ

    where n, t, m, and λ are the same as above.

- When light of a certain wavelength or color satisfies the condition for destructive interference, it is absent from the reflected spectrum of the thin film. This is called absent spectra or missing order spectra.
- For example, if a thin film of air is formed between two glass plates, the condition for destructive interference of light waves reflected from the air film is:

    2t = mλ/n

    where t is the thickness of the air film, m is the order of interference, λ is the wavelength of light in vacuum, and n is the refractive index of air (approximately 1).

- If the thickness of the air film is equal to λ/4, then the first order (m = 1) of interference is absent for light of wavelength λ. This means that the color corresponding to that wavelength is missing from the reflected spectrum of the air film.
- Similarly, if the thickness of the air film is equal to 3λ/4, then the second order (m = 2) of interference is absent for light of wavelength λ, and so on.
- A mnemonic to remember the condition for destructive interference of light waves reflected from a thin film is:

    **D**ouble **n**umber **t**imes **t**hickness equals **m**ultiple **w**avelengths

    where D stands for destructive, n for refractive index, t for thickness, m for order, and w for wavelength.
