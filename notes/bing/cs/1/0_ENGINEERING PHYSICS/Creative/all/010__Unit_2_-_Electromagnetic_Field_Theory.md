## Unit 2 - Electromagnetic Field Theory

- Electromagnetic field theory is the study of the electric and magnetic fields and their interactions with matter and sources.
- It is based on the Maxwell equations, which describe how electric and magnetic fields are generated by charges, currents, and changes of each other.
- The Maxwell equations can be written in two forms: integral and differential. The integral form relates the flux or circulation of the fields through a closed surface or loop to the sources enclosed by the surface or loop. The differential form relates the divergence or curl of the fields at a point to the sources at that point.
- The Maxwell equations in vacuum are:

  - Gauss's law for electric field: $$\oint_S \mathbf{E} \cdot d\mathbf{A} = \frac{Q}{\epsilon_0}$$ or $$\nabla \cdot \mathbf{E} = \frac{\rho}{\epsilon_0}$$
  - Gauss's law for magnetic field: $$\oint_S \mathbf{B} \cdot d\mathbf{A} = 0$$ or $$\nabla \cdot \mathbf{B} = 0$$
  - Faraday's law of induction: $$\oint_C \mathbf{E} \cdot d\mathbf{l} = -\frac{d}{dt} \int_S \mathbf{B} \cdot d\mathbf{A}$$ or $$\nabla \times \mathbf{E} = -\frac{\partial \mathbf{B}}{\partial t}$$
  - Ampere's law with Maxwell's correction: $$\oint_C \mathbf{B} \cdot d\mathbf{l} = \mu_0 \left( I + \epsilon_0 \frac{d}{dt} \int_S \mathbf{E} \cdot d\mathbf{A} \right)$$ or $$\nabla \times \mathbf{B} = \mu_0 \left( \mathbf{J} + \epsilon_0 \frac{\partial \mathbf{E}}{\partial t} \right)$$

  where $\mathbf{E}$ is the electric field, $\mathbf{B}$ is the magnetic field, $\mathbf{J}$ is the current density, $\rho$ is the charge density, $Q$ is the total charge, $I$ is the total current, $\epsilon_0$ is the permittivity of free space, and $\mu_0$ is the permeability of free space.

- The Maxwell equations in a conducting medium are:

  - Gauss's law for electric field: $$\oint_S \mathbf{D} \cdot d\mathbf{A} = Q_f$$ or $$\nabla \cdot \mathbf{D} = \rho_f$$
  - Gauss's law for magnetic field: $$\oint_S \mathbf{B} \cdot d\mathbf{A} = 0$$ or $$\nabla \cdot \mathbf{B} = 0$$
  - Faraday's law of induction: $$\oint_C \mathbf{E} \cdot d\mathbf{l} = -\frac{d}{dt} \int_S \mathbf{B} \cdot d\mathbf{A}$$ or $$\nabla \times \mathbf{E} = -\frac{\partial \mathbf{B}}{\partial t}$$
  - Ampere's law with Maxwell's correction: $$\oint_C \mathbf{H} \cdot d\mathbf{l} = I_f + \frac{d}{dt} \int_S \mathbf{D} \cdot d\mathbf{A}$$ or $$\nabla \times \mathbf{H} = \mathbf{J}_f + \frac{\partial \mathbf{D}}{\partial t}$$

  where $\mathbf{D}$ is the electric displacement field, $\mathbf{H}$ is the magnetic field intensity, $\mathbf{J}_f$ is the free current density, $\rho_f$ is the free charge density, $Q_f$ is the total free charge, and $I_f$ is the total free current.
