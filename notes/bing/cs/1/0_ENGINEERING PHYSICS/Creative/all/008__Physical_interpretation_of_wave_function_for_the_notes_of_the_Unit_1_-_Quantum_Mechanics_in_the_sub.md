### Physical interpretation of wave function for the notes of the Unit 1 - Quantum Mechanics in the subject of ENGINEERING PHYSICS

- A wave function is a mathematical function that describes the quantum state of a particle or a system of particles.
- The wave function, at a particular time, contains all the information that anybody at that time can have about the particle or the system .
- But the wave function itself has no physical interpretation. It is not measurable. It is complex. It is expressed as \uD835\uDEBF (x, y, z, t) = a + ib and the complex conjugate of the wave function is expressed as \uD835\uDEBF × (x, y, z, t) = a – ib.
- However, the square of the absolute value of the wave function has a physical interpretation. It gives the probability density of finding the particle or the system in a certain location at a given instant  .
- The probability density is defined as the probability per unit volume. It is denoted by \uD835\uDC5C(x, y, z, t) and is given by \uD835\uDC5C(x, y, z, t) = |\uD835\uDEBF (x, y, z, t)|^2 = \uD835\uDEBF × (x, y, z, t) \uD835\uDEBF (x, y, z, t) .
- The probability of finding the particle or the system in a region V is obtained by integrating the probability density over that region. It is given by P(V) = ∫\uD835\uDC5C(x, y, z, t) dV .
- The wave function must satisfy the normalization condition, which states that the total probability of finding the particle or the system in the entire space is equal to one. It is given by ∫|\uD835\uDEBF (x, y, z, t)|^2 dV = 1 .
- The wave function must also satisfy the Schrödinger equation, which is a partial differential equation that relates the wave function to the energy and the potential of the particle or the system .
- The wave function can be used to determine probabilities and expectation values of physical observables, such as position, momentum, and kinetic energy.
- The wave function is not unique. It can be multiplied by any complex constant without changing the physical interpretation. This is called the phase ambiguity of the wave function.
- The wave function can also be expressed as a linear combination of other wave functions, called basis functions, that satisfy the same boundary conditions. This is called the superposition principle of the wave function.

#### Mnemonics and learning tricks

- A possible mnemonic to remember the physical interpretation of the wave function is: **W**ave function is **W**hat you can **W**onder about the particle, but its square is **W**hat you can **W**itness.
- A possible learning trick to remember the normalization condition is to think of the wave function as a probability distribution, and the normalization condition as a requirement that the total probability is one.
- A possible learning trick to remember the Schrödinger equation is to think of it as an energy conservation equation, where the total energy of the particle or the system is equal to the sum of the kinetic energy and the potential energy.
