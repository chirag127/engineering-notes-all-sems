### Compton effect for the notes of the Unit 1 - Quantum Mechanics in the subject of ENGINEERING PHYSICS

- The Compton effect is the phenomenon of the scattering of high-energy photons (such as X-rays or gamma rays) by electrons in matter .
- The Compton effect shows that photons behave like particles, as they transfer some of their energy and momentum to the electrons, and change their wavelength and direction .
- The Compton effect can be explained by the conservation of energy and momentum for the collision between a photon and an electron .
- The formula for the Compton effect is:

    ![Compton effect formula](https://latex.codecogs.com/png.latex?%5Clambda%27-%5Clambda%3D%5Cfrac%7Bh%7D%7Bm_ec%7D%281-cos%5Ctheta%29)

    where ![lambda](https://latex.codecogs.com/png.latex?%5Clambda) is the initial wavelength of the photon, ![lambda prime](https://latex.codecogs.com/png.latex?%5Clambda%27) is the final wavelength of the photon, ![h](https://latex.codecogs.com/png.latex?h) is the Planck's constant, ![m_e](https://latex.codecogs.com/png.latex?m_e) is the mass of the electron, ![c](https://latex.codecogs.com/png.latex?c) is the speed of light, and ![theta](https://latex.codecogs.com/png.latex?%5Ctheta) is the angle of scattering of the photon  .
- The Compton effect can be used to measure the energy of photons, the mass of electrons, and the size of nuclei .
- The Compton effect has applications in nuclear physics, astrophysics, and medical imaging .

Some mnemonics and learning tricks for the Compton effect are:

- Remember that the Compton effect is a **C**ollision between a photon and an electron, and the photon **C**hanges its wavelength and direction.
- Remember that the Compton effect formula has a **C**osine term, and the wavelength change is proportional to **1 - cos(theta)**.
- Remember that the Compton effect is an **inelastic** scattering process, as the photon loses some of its energy to the electron.
- Remember that the Compton effect shows the **particle** nature of photons, as they behave like billiard balls in the collision.
