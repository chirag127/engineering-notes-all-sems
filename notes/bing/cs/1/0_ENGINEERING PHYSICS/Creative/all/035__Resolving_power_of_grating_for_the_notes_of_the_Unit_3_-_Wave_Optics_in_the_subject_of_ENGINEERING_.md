### Resolving power of grating for the notes of the Unit 3 - Wave Optics in the subject of ENGINEERING PHYSICS

- Resolving power of grating is the ability of a grating to separate two spectral lines of very close wavelengths into distinct maxima.
- Resolving power of grating is measured by the ratio of the mean wavelength to the smallest difference in wavelengths that can be resolved, i.e. R.P. = λ/dλ.
- The resolving power of grating depends on the order of diffraction (n), the number of lines per unit length (N) and the total number of lines (Nt) on the grating.
- The expression for the resolving power of grating is derived as follows:

  - Consider two spectral lines of wavelengths λ and λ + dλ, which are diffracted by a grating at angles θ and θ + dθ, respectively, in the same order n.
  - The path difference between the rays from the two adjacent slits is given by d sin θ, where d is the grating element.
  - The condition for the maxima of the two spectral lines is given by nλ = d sin θ and n(λ + dλ) = d sin(θ + dθ).
  - Subtracting the two equations, we get ndλ = d cos θ dθ.
  - Dividing both sides by nλ, we get dλ/λ = cos θ dθ/n.
  - The two spectral lines are just resolved when the maxima of one line coincides with the minima of the other line, i.e. when the path difference between the rays from the two extreme slits is equal to λ/2.
  - The path difference between the rays from the two extreme slits is given by Nt d sin θ, where Nt is the total number of lines on the grating.
  - The condition for the just resolution of the two spectral lines is given by Nt d sin θ = λ/2.
  - Dividing both sides by d sin θ, we get Nt = λ/2d sin θ.
  - Substituting this value of Nt in the previous equation, we get dλ/λ = cos θ dθ/2Nt.
  - Rearranging the terms, we get dθ/dλ = 2Nt/λ cos θ.
  - The resolving power of grating is defined as the reciprocal of the smallest difference in wavelengths that can be resolved, i.e. R.P. = λ/dλ.
  - Substituting the value of dλ from the previous equation, we get R.P. = λ/dλ = dθ/dλ * λ/θ = 2Nt/λ cos θ * λ/θ = 2Nt/θ.
  - Since Nt = N/d, where N is the number of lines per unit length, we can write R.P. = 2Nt/θ = 2N/d * d/θ = 2N/θ.
  - Since θ = sin^-1(nλ/d), we can write R.P. = 2N/θ = 2N/sin^-1(nλ/d).
  - This is the expression for the resolving power of grating.

  - **R**esolving **P**ower = **2N**/sin^-1(**n**λ/**d**)
  - **R**eally **P**retty = **2N**/sin^-1(**n**ice λ/**d**ress)
