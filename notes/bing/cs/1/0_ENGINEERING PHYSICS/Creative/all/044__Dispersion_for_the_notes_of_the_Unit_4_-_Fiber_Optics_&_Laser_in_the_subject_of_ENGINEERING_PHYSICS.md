### Dispersion for the notes of the Unit 4 - Fiber Optics & Laser in the subject of ENGINEERING PHYSICS

- Dispersion is the spreading of light pulses when they travel through an optical fiber from one end to another .
- Dispersion causes distortion and limits the data rate of digital signals sent over fiber optic cable.
- Dispersion depends on the wavelength, material and geometry of the optical fiber.
- There are three main types of dispersion in optical fiber: material dispersion, waveguide dispersion and modal dispersion .

#### Material dispersion
- Material dispersion is caused by a change in the refractive index of the fiber optic material with different wavelengths.
- The higher the refractive index, the slower the light travels.
- Material dispersion is also called chromatic dispersion or intramodal dispersion.
- Material dispersion can be minimized by using single-mode fibers or dispersion-shifted fibers.

#### Waveguide dispersion
- Waveguide dispersion is due to the distribution of light between the core and the cladding of the fiber optic cable.
- The light in the core travels faster than the light in the cladding.
- Waveguide dispersion is also called intermodal dispersion or modal dispersion.
- Waveguide dispersion can be minimized by using graded-index fibers or dispersion-flattened fibers.

#### Modal dispersion
- Modal dispersion is caused by the different paths that light can take through a multimode fiber.
- The light that travels along the axis of the fiber arrives faster than the light that travels near the cladding.
- Modal dispersion is also called pulse broadening or intermodal distortion.
- Modal dispersion can be minimized by using single-mode fibers or laser sources.

#### Mnemonics and learning tricks
- A possible mnemonic to remember the types of dispersion is **M**aterial **W**aveguide **M**odal or **MWM**.
- A possible learning trick to understand the concept of dispersion is to imagine a group of runners starting a race at the same time but finishing at different times due to their different speeds and paths. The dispersion is the difference between the first and the last runner to finish the race. The more dispersion, the more spread out the group of runners is.
