### Poynting vector and Poynting theorem for the notes of the Unit 2 - Electromagnetic Field Theory in the subject of ENGINEERING PHYSICS

- Poynting vector is a vector quantity that represents the directional energy flux density (the rate of energy transfer per unit area) of an electromagnetic field.
- Poynting vector is denoted by **S** and is defined as the cross product of electric field vector **E** and magnetic field vector **H**:

  **S** = **E** x **H**

- Poynting vector is named after its discoverer, J.H. Poynting, who derived it in 1884.
- Poynting vector has the units of watts per square meter (W/m^2^) and is a measure of the power carried by the electromagnetic field.
- Poynting vector can be used to calculate the power flow in electromagnetic fields, such as the power radiated by an antenna or the power absorbed by a circuit.

- Poynting theorem is a statement of conservation of electromagnetic energy, which relates the power flow of electromagnetic fields to the work done on charges and the change in energy stored in electric and magnetic fields.
- Poynting theorem can be written as:

  ∇ • **S** = - ∂u/∂t - **J** • **E**

- Where ∇ • **S** is the divergence of Poynting vector, which represents the net power flow out of a unit volume; ∂u/∂t is the rate of change of electromagnetic energy density u; and **J** • **E** is the work done per unit volume by the electric field on the electric current density **J**.
- Poynting theorem can be integrated over a closed surface to obtain:

  ∫ **S** • d**A** = - dW/dt - dU/dt

- Where ∫ **S** • d**A** is the net power flow across the surface; dW/dt is the work done by the electromagnetic field on the charges enclosed by the surface; and dU/dt is the rate of change of electromagnetic energy stored within the surface.
- Poynting theorem can be used to analyze the energy balance of electromagnetic systems, such as the energy radiated by an antenna or the energy dissipated by a resistor.

- A mnemonic to remember the Poynting vector formula is:

  **S**ome **E**lephants **H**ave **T**runks

  Where **S** stands for Poynting vector, **E** for electric field, **H** for magnetic field, and **T** for cross product.

- A learning trick to understand the Poynting theorem is to think of an analogy with fluid mechanics, where the Poynting vector is like the velocity of the fluid, the electromagnetic energy density is like the pressure of the fluid, and the electric current density is like the mass flow rate of the fluid. The Poynting theorem then states that the net fluid flow out of a volume is equal to the decrease in pressure plus the work done by the fluid on the boundaries.
