### Laser applications for the notes of the Unit 4 - Fiber Optics & Laser in the subject of ENGINEERING PHYSICS

- A laser is a device that emits light through a process of optical amplification based on the stimulated emission of electromagnetic radiation. The term "laser" originated as an acronym for "light amplification by stimulated emission of radiation".
- Lasers have many applications in various fields of science, engineering, medicine, industry, and entertainment. Some of the common laser applications are:

  - **Laser cutting and welding**: Lasers can be used to cut and weld metals, plastics, ceramics, and other materials with high precision and speed. The laser beam melts and vaporizes the material at the point of contact, creating a smooth and clean cut or weld. Laser cutting and welding are widely used in manufacturing, aerospace, automotive, and medical industries .
  - **Laser printing and scanning**: Lasers can be used to print and scan images and text on paper, film, or other media. The laser beam modulates the intensity or color of the light according to the information to be printed or scanned, and then scans the beam across the surface of the media. Laser printing and scanning are widely used in office, education, and entertainment industries .
  - **Laser communication and sensing**: Lasers can be used to transmit and receive information and signals through optical fibers, free space, or the atmosphere. The laser beam carries the information or signal as a modulation of the intensity, frequency, phase, or polarization of the light. Laser communication and sensing are widely used in telecommunications, remote sensing, lidar, spectroscopy, and astronomy  .
  - **Laser surgery and medicine**: Lasers can be used to perform various surgical and medical procedures, such as cutting, cauterizing, coagulating, ablating, or stimulating tissues and organs. The laser beam can be focused to a very small spot, allowing precise and minimally invasive operations. Laser surgery and medicine are widely used in ophthalmology, dermatology, dentistry, oncology, and cardiology .
  - **Laser entertainment and art**: Lasers can be used to create various visual and audio effects, such as laser shows, holograms, laser pointers, laser harps, and laser microphones. The laser beam can be manipulated to produce different colors, shapes, patterns, and sounds. Laser entertainment and art are widely used in concerts, festivals, museums, and exhibitions .

- A mnemonic to remember some of the laser applications is **LAPCS** (pronounced as laps), which stands for **L**aser cutting and welding, **A**rt and entertainment, **P**rinting and scanning, **C**ommunication and sensing, and **S**urgery and medicine. This mnemonic can be easily remembered by associating it with the word "laps", which means to complete a circuit or a round in a race or a game. You can imagine that lasers are used to complete various tasks or challenges in different fields or domains.
