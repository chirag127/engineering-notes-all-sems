### Coherent sources for the notes of the Unit 3 - Wave Optics in the subject of ENGINEERING PHYSICS

- Coherent sources are sources that emit light waves with the same frequency, wavelength and phase or having a constant phase difference .
- Coherent sources produce sustained interference patterns when the waves superimpose and the positions of maxima and minima are fixed .
- Coherent sources are necessary for the phenomenon of interference to be observed .
- Coherent sources can be obtained by using a single source and splitting the light beam into two parts by using a beam splitter, a prism, a thin film, a pinhole, etc .
- Coherent sources can also be obtained by using two independent sources that are synchronized by an external signal or by using a common oscillator.
- Coherent sources have applications in holography, laser technology, optical communication, metrology, etc.

Some mnemonics and learning tricks for coherent sources are:

- To remember the definition of coherent sources, use the acronym **FAP** (Frequency, Amplitude, Phase).
- To remember the condition for interference, use the phrase **"Coherent sources interfere consistently"**.
- To remember some examples of devices that produce coherent sources, use the word **"BPTP"** (Beam splitter, Prism, Thin film, Pinhole).
