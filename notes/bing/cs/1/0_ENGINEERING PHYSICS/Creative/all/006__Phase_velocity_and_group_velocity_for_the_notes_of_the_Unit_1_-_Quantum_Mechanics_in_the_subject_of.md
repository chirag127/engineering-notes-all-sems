### Phase velocity and group velocity for the notes of the Unit 1 - Quantum Mechanics in the subject of ENGINEERING PHYSICS

- Phase velocity and group velocity are two important and related concepts in wave mechanics. They arise in quantum mechanics in the time development of the state function for the continuous case, i.e. wave packets.
- The phase velocity of a wave is the rate at which the wave propagates in any medium. This is the velocity at which the phase of any one frequency component of the wave travels.
- The group velocity of a wave is the velocity with which the overall shape of the wave's amplitudes—known as the modulation or envelope of the wave—propagates through space.
- Phase velocity and group velocity are related through Rayleigh's formula:

  `v_g = v_p + k dv_p/dk`

  where `v_g` is the group velocity, `v_p` is the phase velocity, `k` is the wave number, and `ω` is the angular frequency.
- If the derivative term is zero, group velocity equals phase velocity. In this case, there is no dispersion.
- Dispersion is the phenomenon in which the phase velocity of a wave depends on its frequency.
- In quantum mechanics, the phase velocity of a wave function is related to the total energy of the particle, while the group velocity is related to the kinetic energy of the particle.
- The phase velocity of a wave function can be greater than the speed of light, but this does not imply superluminal communication, because the phase velocity does not carry any information.
- The group velocity of a wave function can also be greater than the speed of light, but this does not violate causality, because the wave packet is not a localized particle, and the peak of the wave packet does not represent the position of the particle.
- A possible mnemonic to remember the difference between phase velocity and group velocity is:

  `Phase velocity is the pace of the wave, group velocity is the groove of the wave.`

- A possible learning trick to understand the concept of phase velocity and group velocity is to imagine a group of people walking along a road, and each person is waving a flag of a different color. The phase velocity is the speed at which each flag moves up and down, while the group velocity is the speed at which the group of people moves forward. The phase velocity depends on the frequency of the flag waving, while the group velocity depends on the shape of the group. If the group is uniform, the phase velocity and the group velocity are the same. If the group is spread out, the phase velocity and the group velocity are different. If the group is clustered, the phase velocity and the group velocity can be opposite.
