Absorption of radiation is the process by which electrons in the ground state absorbs energy from photons to jump into the higher energy level. 

The diagram below shows an atom with two energy levels E1 and E2, where E2 > E1. A photon with energy hν = E2 - E1 is incident on the atom and is absorbed by the electron in the lower energy level E1. The electron is then excited to the higher energy level E2.
