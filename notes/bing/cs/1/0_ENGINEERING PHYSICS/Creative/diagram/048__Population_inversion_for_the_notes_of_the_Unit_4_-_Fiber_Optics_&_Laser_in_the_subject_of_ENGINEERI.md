Population inversion is a condition in which more atoms or molecules are in an excited state than in a lower energy state. This is necessary for laser or maser action to occur, as it allows for stimulated emission of photons. Population inversion can be achieved by various methods, such as optical pumping, electric discharge, chemical reaction, or nuclear reaction.
