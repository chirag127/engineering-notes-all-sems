A solid state laser is a laser that uses a solid material as the gain medium, such as a ruby crystal or a neodymium-doped yttrium aluminum garnet (Nd:YAG) crystal. A gas laser is a laser that uses a gas as the gain medium, such as helium, neon, or carbon dioxide.
