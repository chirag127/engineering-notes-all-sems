The temperature dependence of critical field for superconductors is a phenomenon that describes how the magnetic field required to destroy the superconducting state varies with temperature. The critical field is usually denoted by H_c and the critical temperature by T_c. Different types of superconductors have different shapes of the H_c(T) curve, depending on the nature of their pairing mechanism and the presence of multiple bands or gaps.
