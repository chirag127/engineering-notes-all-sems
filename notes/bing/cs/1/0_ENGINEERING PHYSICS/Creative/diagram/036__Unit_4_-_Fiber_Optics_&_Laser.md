## Unit 4 - Fiber Optics & Laser

Fiber optics is the science of transmitting light through very thin glass or plastic fibers. Fiber optics has many advantages over copper wires, such as higher bandwidth, lower attenuation, immunity to electromagnetic interference, and lighter weight.

A fiber optic cable consists of a core, a cladding, and a protective coating. The core is the central part of the fiber that carries the light signal. The cladding is a layer of glass or plastic that surrounds the core and reflects the light back into the core. The coating is a material that protects the fiber from damage and moisture.

A laser is a device that produces a coherent and intense beam of light. A laser can be used as a light source for fiber optics, as it can generate a single wavelength of light with high power and low divergence.

A fiber laser is a type of laser that uses an optical fiber as the amplifying medium. A fiber laser has a spool of doped fiber that is pumped by one or more laser diodes. The doped fiber acts as a gain medium that amplifies the light signal. The fiber laser can produce high-power and high-quality laser beams with high efficiency and stability.
