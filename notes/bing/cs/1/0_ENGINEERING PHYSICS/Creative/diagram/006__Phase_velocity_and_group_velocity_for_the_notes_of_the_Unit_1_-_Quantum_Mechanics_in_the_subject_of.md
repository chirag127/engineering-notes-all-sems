### Phase velocity and group velocity

Phase velocity is the speed at which a point of constant phase travels as the wave propagates. Group velocity is the speed at which a wave packet or a group of waves travels. The relationship between phase velocity and group velocity depends on whether the wave is dispersive or non-dispersive. A dispersive wave is one in which the phase velocity varies with the frequency or the wavelength of the wave. A non-dispersive wave is one in which the phase velocity is constant for all frequencies or wavelengths.

The blue wave represents the envelope of the wave packet, which travels with the group velocity. The red wave represents the phase of the wave packet, which travels with the phase velocity. The dashed line shows the position of the wave packet at a given time.
