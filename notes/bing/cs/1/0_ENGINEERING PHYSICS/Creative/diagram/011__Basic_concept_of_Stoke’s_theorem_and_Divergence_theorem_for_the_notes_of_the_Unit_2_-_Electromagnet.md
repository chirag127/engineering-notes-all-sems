The basic concept of Stoke’s theorem and Divergence theorem is that they are both ways of relating the integrals of a vector field over different dimensions. Stoke’s theorem relates a surface integral to a line integral on the boundary of the surface, while Divergence theorem relates a volume integral to a surface integral on the boundary of the volume. Both theorems are generalizations of the fundamental theorem of calculus, which relates a line integral to a point evaluation of an antiderivative.
