A quantum well is a two-dimensional structure where the motion of electrons is confined in one direction, while they are free to move in the other two directions. A quantum wire is a one-dimensional structure where the motion of electrons is confined in two directions, while they are free to move in only one direction. Quantum wells and wires are made of thin layers of different semiconductor materials, and they have applications in nanoelectronics, optoelectronics, information technology, and biotechnology.
