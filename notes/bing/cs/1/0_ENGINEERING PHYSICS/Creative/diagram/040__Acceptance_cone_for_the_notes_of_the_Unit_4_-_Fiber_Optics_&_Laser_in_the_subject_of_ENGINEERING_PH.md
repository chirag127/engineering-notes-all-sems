The acceptance cone of an optical fiber is the cone within which optical power may be coupled into the bound modes of the fiber. The acceptance angle is the maximum angle with the axis of the fiber at which light can enter the fiber, in order to be propagated through it. The numerical aperture (NA) is a measure of the light-gathering ability and resolution of the fiber, and it is related to the acceptance angle by the formula NA = n0 sin θa, where n0 is the refractive index of the surrounding medium and θa is the acceptance angle.

The angle θa is the acceptance angle, and the cone formed by rotating it around the fiber axis is the acceptance cone. The light rays that are within this cone are accepted by the fiber core and propagated through it. The light rays that are outside this cone are not accepted by the fiber and lost. The larger the acceptance angle, the larger the numerical aperture, and the more light the fiber can accept and propagate.
