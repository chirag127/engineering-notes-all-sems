Attenuation in fiber optics is the reduction in intensity of the light beam (or signal) with respect to distance travelled through a transmission medium. It is usually expressed in decibels per kilometer (dB/km) at a specific wavelength. Attenuation depends on various factors, such as the intrinsic properties of the fiber material, the wavelength of the light, the presence of impurities or defects, the bending or twisting of the fiber, and the environmental conditions.
