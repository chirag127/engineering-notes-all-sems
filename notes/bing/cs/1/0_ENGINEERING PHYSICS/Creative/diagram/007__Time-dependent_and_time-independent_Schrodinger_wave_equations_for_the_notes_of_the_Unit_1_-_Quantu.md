The time-dependent Schrodinger wave equation describes the evolution of a quantum system over time. It has the form:

ih dψ/dt = Hψ

where i is the imaginary unit, h is the reduced Planck constant, ψ is the wave function, t is time, and H is the Hamiltonian operator.

The time-independent Schrodinger wave equation describes the stationary states of a quantum system. It has the form:

Hψ = Eψ

where E is the energy eigenvalue of the state ψ.
