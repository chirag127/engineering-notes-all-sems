A particle in a one-dimensional box is a simple model that describes the quantum mechanical behavior of a single particle confined inside an infinitely deep well. The particle can only move along a straight line and cannot escape the boundaries of the well. The potential energy of the particle is zero inside the well and infinite outside the well.

The particle is represented by a horizontal line inside the box. The box has a length L and the walls are represented by vertical lines. The potential energy of the particle is represented by the height of the diagram. Inside the box, the potential energy is zero and outside the box, the potential energy is infinite. This means that the particle cannot penetrate the walls of the box and is trapped inside. The position of the particle along the x-axis is denoted by x. The particle can have any value of x between 0 and L, but not outside this range.
