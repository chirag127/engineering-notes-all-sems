The dispersive power of the prism is given by the formula:

```
w = (μv - μr) / (μ - 1)
```

where w is the dispersive power, μv is the refractive index for violet light, μr is the refractive index for red light, and μ is the average refractive index for the medium.

The dispersive power depends on the material of the prism and the wavelengths of light used. Different materials have different dispersive powers, and different wavelengths of light have different refractive indices. The higher the dispersive power, the more the light is split into its component colors
