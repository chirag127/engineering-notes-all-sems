The Maxwell equations are a set of four partial differential equations that describe the behavior of electric and magnetic fields in vacuum and in conducting medium. They are:

- Gauss's law: ∇⋅E = ρ/ε0
- Gauss's law for magnetism: ∇⋅B = 0
- Faraday's law of induction: ∇×E = -∂B/∂t
- Ampere's law with Maxwell's correction: ∇×B = μ0(J + ε0∂E/∂t)

where E is the electric field, B is the magnetic field, ρ is the charge density, J is the current density, ε0 is the permittivity of vacuum, and μ0 is the permeability of vacuum.
