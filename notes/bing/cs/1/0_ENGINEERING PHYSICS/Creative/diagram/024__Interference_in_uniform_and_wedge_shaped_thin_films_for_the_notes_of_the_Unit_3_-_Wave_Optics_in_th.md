Interference in uniform and wedge shaped thin films occurs when light is reflected or transmitted by two parallel or nearly parallel surfaces that are separated by a thin layer of air or another transparent medium. The light waves undergo multiple reflections and interference within the film, producing bright and dark fringes depending on the path difference and the phase difference between the waves.
