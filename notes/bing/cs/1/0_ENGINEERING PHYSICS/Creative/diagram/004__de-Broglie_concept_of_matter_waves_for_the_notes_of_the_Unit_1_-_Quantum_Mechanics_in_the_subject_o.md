The de-Broglie concept of matter waves is based on the hypothesis that all matter exhibits wave-like properties and relates the observed wavelength of matter to its momentum. According to this concept, the matter has dual nature: when it is moving, it shows wave properties (like interference, diffraction, etc.) and when it is at rest, it shows particle properties. This concept was proposed by Louis de Broglie in 1924 to explain the quantization of the electron's angular momentum in the hydrogen atom, which was postulated by Bohr's quantum theory.
