An electromagnetic wave consists of an electric field and a magnetic field that are perpendicular to each other and to the direction of propagation of the wave. The electric field is represented by E and the magnetic field by B. The wave travels in the direction of the vector product of E and B, which is denoted by k. The magnitude of the electric field and the magnetic field are related by the equation c = E/B, where c is the speed of light.
