The Meissner effect is the expulsion of a magnetic field from a superconductor during its transition to the superconducting state when it is cooled below the critical temperature. This expulsion will repel a nearby magnet. The following diagram illustrates the Meissner effect using ASCII characters:

       in magnetic field         in magnetic field
       (T > Tc)                  (T < Tc)

    +---------------------+     +---------------------+
