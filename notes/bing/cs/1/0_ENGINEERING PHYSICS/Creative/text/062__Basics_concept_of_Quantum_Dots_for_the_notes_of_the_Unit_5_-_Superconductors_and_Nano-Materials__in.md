### Basics concept of Quantum Dots

- Quantum dots are **nanoscale particles** of semiconducting material that have **optical and electronic properties** that differ from those of larger particles as a result of **quantum mechanics** .
- Quantum dots can be embedded in cells or organisms for various experimental purposes, such as **labeling proteins**.
- Quantum dots have **discrete energy levels** that depend on their size, shape, and composition . This means that they can **emit or absorb light** of different wavelengths by changing their size or shape .
- Quantum dots have applications in **biomedical imaging**, **solar cells**, **quantum computing**, **LEDs**, **lasers**, and **sensors** .
- Quantum dots can be synthesized by various methods, such as **chemical vapor deposition**, **colloidal synthesis**, **electrochemical assembly**, and **self-assembly** .
- Quantum dots can be classified into different types, such as **core-shell quantum dots**, **metallic quantum dots**, **carbon quantum dots**, and **perovskite quantum dots**, based on their structure and material .
