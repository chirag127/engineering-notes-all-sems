### Solid state Laser (Ruby laser) and Gas Laser (He-Ne laser)

- A solid state laser is a laser that uses a solid material as the gain medium, rather than a liquid or a gas. 
- A ruby laser is a type of solid state laser that uses a synthetic ruby crystal as the gain medium. 
- A gas laser is a laser that uses a gas as the gain medium, such as helium, neon, argon, or carbon dioxide. 
- A He-Ne laser is a type of gas laser that uses a mixture of helium and neon gases as the gain medium. 

#### Ruby laser

- A ruby laser consists of a ruby rod with polished ends, a flash lamp, a power supply, and a resonator cavity. 
- The ruby rod is doped with chromium ions, which provide the energy levels for laser action. 
- The flash lamp emits a broad spectrum of light, which pumps the chromium ions from the ground state to the excited state. 
- The resonator cavity consists of two mirrors, one fully reflective and one partially reflective, which form an optical feedback loop. 
- The ruby laser is a three-level laser, which means that the lasing transition occurs between the first excited state and the ground state. 
- The ruby laser emits a pulsed red beam of light with a wavelength of 694.3 nm. 
- The ruby laser has applications in holography, medicine, industry, and research. 

#### He-Ne laser

- A He-Ne laser consists of a glass tube filled with a low-pressure mixture of helium and neon gases, two electrodes, a power supply, and a resonator cavity. 
- The electrodes are connected to a high-voltage alternating current, which creates an electric discharge in the gas mixture. 
- The electric discharge excites the helium atoms, which then collide with the neon atoms and transfer some of their energy to them. 
- The resonator cavity consists of two mirrors, one fully reflective and one partially reflective, which form an optical feedback loop. 
- The He-Ne laser is a four-level laser, which means that the lasing transition occurs between two metastable states of the neon atoms. 
- The He-Ne laser emits a continuous red beam of light with a wavelength of 632.8 nm. 
- The He-Ne laser has applications in metrology, spectroscopy, communication, and education.
