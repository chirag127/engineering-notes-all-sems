### Application of fiber

Fiber optics are thin, flexible strands of glass or plastic that can transmit light signals over long distances with minimal loss or interference. Fiber optics have many applications in various fields, such as:

- **Communication**: Fiber optics are widely used for voice, data, and video transmission, as they offer high bandwidth, low attenuation, and immunity to electromagnetic interference. Fiber optics are used for telecommunications, local area networks (LANs), industrial control systems, avionic systems, military command, control, and communications systems .
- **Medical**: Fiber optics are used for various medical purposes, such as endoscopy, laser surgery, dentistry, and biomedical sensing. Fiber optics allow for minimally invasive procedures, high-resolution imaging, and precise delivery of light or laser energy .
- **Defence**: Fiber optics are used for data transmission in high-level data security fields of military and aerospace applications. These are used in wirings in aircraft, hydrophones for SONARs and Seismics applications. Optical Fibres are also used for missile guidance, surveillance, and reconnaissance .
- **Industries**: Fiber optics are used for various industrial applications, such as process control, automation, instrumentation, sensing, and inspection. Fiber optics can withstand harsh environments, such as high temperature, pressure, vibration, and corrosion. Fiber optics are also used for fiber lasers, fiber amplifiers, and fiber optic gyroscopes .
- **Broadcasting**: Fiber optics are used for transmitting television signals, as they offer high quality, low noise, and long distance transmission. Fiber optics are also used for cable television, satellite television, and internet protocol television (IPTV) .
- **Lighting and Decorations**: Fiber optics are used for various lighting and decorative purposes, such as illumination, display, art, and architecture. Fiber optics can create colorful, dynamic, and aesthetic effects, as well as provide safety and security lighting .
- **Mechanical Inspections**: Fiber optics are used for inspecting hard-to-reach or inaccessible areas, such as pipelines, boilers, engines, and turbines. Fiber optics can provide high-resolution images and videos, as well as measure physical parameters, such as temperature, pressure, and strain .
