### Step index and graded index fibers

- Step index and graded index fibers are two types of multimode fibers that can transmit multiple modes of light along their length.
- Step index fiber has a core with a constant refractive index and a cladding with a lower refractive index. The core-cladding interface forms a sharp boundary or a step, hence the name step index fiber.
- Graded index fiber has a core with a varying refractive index that decreases gradually from the center to the edge. The core-cladding interface forms a smooth curve, hence the name graded index fiber.
- The main difference between step index and graded index fibers is the way they propagate light. In step index fiber, light travels in straight lines and reflects at the core-cladding interface. This causes different modes to travel different distances and arrive at different times at the output end. This phenomenon is called modal dispersion and it limits the bandwidth and distance of step index fiber .
- In graded index fiber, light travels in curved paths and refracts at the core-cladding interface. This causes different modes to travel at different speeds and arrive at the same time at the output end. This phenomenon reduces modal dispersion and increases the bandwidth and distance of graded index fiber .
- Some advantages of graded index fiber over step index fiber are  :
  - Higher transmission quality
  - Higher bandwidth
  - Longer distance
  - Better output beam profile
- Some disadvantages of graded index fiber compared to step index fiber are :
  - Higher cost
  - More complex fabrication
  - More sensitive to bending losses
- Step index fiber is mainly used for short-distance applications, such as local area networks (LANs), where cost and simplicity are more important than performance .
- Graded index fiber is mainly used for long-distance applications, such as telecommunication, where performance and quality are more important than cost and simplicity  .
- Step index fiber can be either single-mode or multimode, depending on the core diameter and the wavelength of light. Single-mode step index fiber has a very small core diameter (about 10 microns) and can transmit only one mode of light. Multimode step index fiber has a larger core diameter (about 50 to 100 microns) and can transmit multiple modes of light.
- Graded index fiber is always multimode, as the varying refractive index allows multiple modes of light to propagate. Graded index fiber can have different core diameters, ranging from 50 to 600 microns .
