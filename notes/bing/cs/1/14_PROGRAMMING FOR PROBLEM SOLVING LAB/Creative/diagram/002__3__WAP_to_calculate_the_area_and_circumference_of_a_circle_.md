A circle is a closed curve that consists of all points that are equidistant from a fixed point called the center. The distance from the center to any point on the circle is called the radius (r). The diameter (d) is the longest chord of the circle, which passes through the center and has both endpoints on the circle. The diameter is twice the radius, or d = 2r.

The area (A) of a circle is the amount of space enclosed by the circle. The formula for the area of a circle is A = pi * r^2, where pi is a constant that is approximately equal to 3.14.

The circumference (C) of a circle is the length of the boundary of the circle. The formula for the circumference of a circle is C = 2 * pi * r, or C = pi * d.
