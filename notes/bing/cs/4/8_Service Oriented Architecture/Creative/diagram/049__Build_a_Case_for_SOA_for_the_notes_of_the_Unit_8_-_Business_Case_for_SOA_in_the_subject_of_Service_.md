The following is a possible ASCII diagram for building a case for SOA for the notes of the Unit 8 - Business Case for SOA in the subject of Service Oriented Architecture. It is based on the Gartner framework and the CLLAX article.

The diagram shows the main components of a SOA business case, which are:

- The business drivers: the reasons why the organization needs to adopt SOA, such as increasing agility, reducing costs, improving quality, etc.
- The SOA benefits: the expected outcomes of implementing SOA, such as faster time to market, better customer satisfaction, higher reuse, etc.
- The SOA costs: the investments required to implement SOA, such as infrastructure, tools, training, governance, etc.
- The SOA risks: the potential challenges or pitfalls of implementing SOA, such as complexity, security, interoperability, etc.
- The SOA metrics: the measures used to evaluate the success of SOA, such as ROI, KPIs, SLAs, etc.

The diagram also shows the relationships between these components, such as:

- The business drivers influence the SOA benefits and the SOA metrics.
- The SOA benefits justify the SOA costs and mitigate the SOA risks.
- The SOA costs affect the SOA metrics and the SOA risks.
- The SOA risks impact the SOA benefits and the SOA metrics.
- The SOA metrics demonstrate the value of SOA and the achievement of the business drivers.
