The emergence of MSA is a result of the evolution of SOA. SOA stands for service-oriented architecture, which is a software design paradigm that views all components as services that can communicate with each other. MSA stands for microservice architecture, which is a refinement of SOA that focuses on building small, independent, and self-contained services that can be deployed and scaled independently.
