- Reduced development time: By reusing existing services, the service consumers can avoid developing new functionality from scratch, saving time and resources.
- Reduced maintenance cost: By maintaining a single version of a service, the service providers can avoid updating multiple copies of the same functionality, saving time and resources.
- Reduced integration cost: By using standard protocols and interfaces, the service consumers and providers can avoid complex and costly integration efforts, saving time and resources.
- Increased business value: By aligning the services with the business goals and processes, the SOA-based system can deliver more value to the stakeholders, increasing customer satisfaction and revenue.
