The following is a detailed ASCII diagram for Return on Investment (ROI) for SOA, based on the notes of the Unit 8 - Business Case for SOA in the subject of Service Oriented Architecture.

The diagram shows the three main components of SOA ROI: cost reduction, reuse, and agility. Cost reduction is achieved by reducing the complexity and duplication of IT systems and processes, and by enabling faster and cheaper integration. Reuse is achieved by creating and consuming reusable services and processes that can be leveraged across different business domains and applications. Agility is achieved by enabling faster and more flexible response to changing business needs and opportunities, and by facilitating innovation and collaboration.

The diagram also shows the factors that influence the SOA ROI, such as the number of services available for reuse, the degree of reuse, the service complexity, the integration complexity, the business value of the services, and the alignment of the services with the business goals and strategies.

The diagram uses the following symbols:

- S: Service
- P: Process
- C: Cost
- R: Reuse
- A: Agility
- V: Value
- N: Number
- D: Degree
- X: Complexity
- B: Business
- G: Goal
- E: Alignment
- +: Positive influence
- -: Negative influence
- *: Multiplication
