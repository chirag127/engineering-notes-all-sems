Service orchestration is the process of designing, creating, delivering, and monitoring service offerings in an automated way. It involves the coordination of multiple services across different domains, such as network, cloud, and application. Service orchestration can be achieved through various technologies, such as service orchestration and automation platforms (SOAPs), workload automation solutions (WLA), and enterprise job scheduling platforms.
