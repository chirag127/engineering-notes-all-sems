## Unit 1 - Introduction: SOA and MSA Basics

Service-oriented architecture (SOA) and microservice architecture (MSA) are two architectural styles for designing software systems using a software architecture that views all components as services. Services are self-contained, modular, and loosely coupled units of functionality that communicate with each other through standardized interfaces and protocols.
