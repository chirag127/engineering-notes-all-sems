The need for business and IT alignment is the need for ensuring that the IT strategy and capabilities support the business goals and objectives. This alignment is vital for market impact and growth, as it enables IT to respond more quickly and effectively to the changing business needs and opportunities. It also allows the business to leverage the technological advances and innovations that IT can provide  .

One way to achieve business and IT alignment is to use enterprise architecture (EA) and service-oriented architecture (SOA) as the guiding principles and frameworks for designing, developing, and managing the IT systems and services. EA is the practice of defining and documenting the current and desired states of the enterprise's structure, processes, information, and technology, and the roadmap for transitioning from one state to the other. SOA is the principle of organizing the software components that support the business operations as a set of reusable and interoperable services that can be composed and orchestrated to meet various business needs and scenarios    .
