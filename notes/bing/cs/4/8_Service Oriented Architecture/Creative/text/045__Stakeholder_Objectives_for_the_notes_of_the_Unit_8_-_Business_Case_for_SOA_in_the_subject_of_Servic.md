### Stakeholder Objectives for the notes of the Unit 8 - Business Case for SOA in the subject of Service Oriented Architecture

- Stakeholders are the people or groups who have an interest or influence in the outcome of a project or a system.
- Stakeholder objectives are the goals or expectations that the stakeholders have from the project or the system.
- In the context of Service Oriented Architecture (SOA), stakeholder objectives can be classified into four categories: business, technical, organizational, and social.
- Business objectives are related to the value proposition, return on investment, cost reduction, revenue generation, customer satisfaction, and competitive advantage of the SOA project or system.
- Technical objectives are related to the quality attributes, performance, scalability, reliability, security, interoperability, and maintainability of the SOA project or system.
- Organizational objectives are related to the alignment, governance, change management, collaboration, and culture of the SOA project or system.
- Social objectives are related to the ethical, legal, environmental, and social responsibility aspects of the SOA project or system.
- Stakeholder objectives can be conflicting, complementary, or independent of each other. For example, a business objective of reducing costs may conflict with a technical objective of improving performance, or a social objective of complying with regulations may complement a business objective of enhancing customer trust, or an organizational objective of fostering collaboration may be independent of a technical objective of ensuring security.
- Stakeholder objectives can be prioritized, measured, and evaluated using various methods and techniques, such as SMART criteria, balanced scorecard, key performance indicators, cost-benefit analysis, and risk assessment.
- Stakeholder objectives can be used to define the scope, requirements, design, implementation, testing, deployment, and evaluation of the SOA project or system.
- Stakeholder objectives can be communicated, negotiated, and managed using various tools and techniques, such as stakeholder analysis, stakeholder matrix, stakeholder map, stakeholder engagement plan, and stakeholder register.
