## Unit 6 - SOA Governance and Implementation

- SOA governance is the process of defining, implementing, and enforcing policies and standards for the development, deployment, and management of service-oriented architecture (SOA) solutions.
- SOA governance aims to ensure that the SOA solutions align with the business goals and requirements, adhere to the best practices and principles of SOA, and deliver the expected value and benefits.
- SOA governance involves the following aspects:
  - **Strategy**: defining the vision, objectives, and roadmap for SOA adoption and evolution in the organization.
  - **Architecture**: designing the SOA reference architecture, service models, and service contracts that guide the development and integration of services.
  - **Design**: applying the SOA design principles, patterns, and standards to the service development and composition.
  - **Development**: implementing the services using the appropriate technologies, tools, and frameworks, and ensuring the quality and security of the service code.
  - **Testing**: verifying the functionality, performance, reliability, and interoperability of the services and service compositions.
  - **Deployment**: deploying the services to the target environments, and managing the service versions, configurations, and dependencies.
  - **Operation**: monitoring, controlling, and optimizing the service runtime behavior, availability, and performance.
  - **Change**: managing the changes to the services and service compositions, and ensuring the compatibility and consistency of the service portfolio.
- SOA governance requires the establishment of a governance framework, which consists of the following elements:
  - **Governance model**: defining the roles, responsibilities, and relationships of the governance participants, such as service owners, service consumers, service developers, service managers, and governance board.
  - **Governance processes**: defining the workflows, activities, and tasks for the governance lifecycle, such as service identification, service design, service approval, service registration, service discovery, service consumption, service monitoring, and service change.
  - **Governance policies**: defining the rules, guidelines, and best practices for the governance aspects, such as service naming, service granularity, service reuse, service versioning, service security, service quality, and service level agreements.
  - **Governance metrics**: defining the measures, indicators, and targets for the governance aspects, such as service availability, service reliability, service performance, service usage, service compliance, and service value.
  - **Governance tools**: providing the support and automation for the governance processes, policies, and metrics, such as service repository, service registry, service catalog, service testing tool, service deployment tool, service management tool, and service dashboard.
- SOA governance implementation involves the following steps:
  - **Assess**: assessing the current state of the SOA maturity, readiness, and challenges in the organization, and identifying the gaps and opportunities for improvement.
  - **Plan**: planning the SOA governance strategy, architecture, design, development, testing, deployment, operation, and change, and defining the governance framework elements.
  - **Execute**: executing the SOA governance processes, policies, and metrics, and using the governance tools to support and automate the governance activities and tasks.
  - **Evaluate**: evaluating the SOA governance outcomes, impacts, and benefits, and using the feedback and lessons learned to improve the governance framework and practices.
