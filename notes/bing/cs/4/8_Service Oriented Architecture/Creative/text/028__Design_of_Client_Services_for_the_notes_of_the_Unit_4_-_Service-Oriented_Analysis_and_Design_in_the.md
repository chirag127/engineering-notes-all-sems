### Design of Client Services for Service-Oriented Analysis and Design

- Client services are the services that consume or invoke other services to provide a business functionality or a user interface.
- Client services can be classified into two types: **composite services** and **presentation services**.
- Composite services are services that orchestrate or coordinate other services to implement a business process or a workflow. They can be implemented using technologies such as BPEL, BPMN, or WS-CDL.
- Presentation services are services that provide a user interface or a front-end to other services. They can be implemented using technologies such as HTML, CSS, JavaScript, or AJAX.
- The design of client services involves the following steps:
  - Identify the business requirements and the user needs for the client service.
  - Identify the existing or potential services that can be reused or composed by the client service.
  - Define the service contract and the interface for the client service, specifying the inputs, outputs, operations, and quality of service requirements.
  - Define the service logic and the behavior for the client service, using a service composition language or a presentation technology.
  - Define the service policies and the governance for the client service, such as security, reliability, availability, and performance.
  - Test and deploy the client service, ensuring its interoperability and compatibility with other services.
