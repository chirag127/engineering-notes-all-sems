### Need for Business and IT Alignment

- Business and IT alignment is the process of ensuring that the IT strategy, capabilities, and investments support the business goals and objectives of the organization .
- Business and IT alignment is vital for market impact and growth, as it enables the organization to respond quickly and effectively to changing business needs and opportunities, and leverage IT as a source of competitive advantage .
- Business and IT alignment can improve customer satisfaction, operational efficiency, innovation, agility, and profitability of the organization .
- Business and IT alignment can also reduce risks, costs, and conflicts that may arise from misalignment or miscommunication between the business and IT stakeholders .
- Business and IT alignment requires a collaborative and continuous effort from both the business and IT sides, involving shared vision, goals, governance, communication, and metrics .
- Business and IT alignment can be facilitated by using frameworks and methodologies such as Enterprise Architecture (EA) and Service Oriented Architecture (SOA), which can help align the business processes, services, and systems across the organization.
