### Strategic Architecture Governance

- Strategic architecture governance is the process of defining, implementing, and monitoring the principles, policies, standards, and guidelines that govern the design, development, and evolution of service-oriented architecture (SOA) solutions.
- Strategic architecture governance aims to ensure that SOA solutions are aligned with the business goals, requirements, and expectations of the stakeholders, and that they adhere to the best practices and quality criteria of SOA.
- Strategic architecture governance involves the following activities:
  - Establishing the SOA vision, strategy, and roadmap that guide the SOA initiatives and projects.
  - Defining the SOA governance framework that specifies the roles, responsibilities, processes, and artifacts for governing SOA solutions.
  - Developing the SOA reference architecture that defines the common architectural elements, patterns, and standards for SOA solutions.
  - Creating the SOA governance registry and repository that store and manage the SOA artifacts, such as services, contracts, policies, and metrics.
  - Implementing the SOA governance tools and mechanisms that enable the enforcement, validation, and monitoring of SOA governance policies and rules.
  - Measuring and evaluating the SOA governance performance and outcomes using the SOA governance metrics and indicators.
  - Continuously improving the SOA governance practices and maturity based on the feedback and lessons learned from the SOA governance activities.
