### SOA Governance – Best Practices

SOA governance is the process of establishing and enforcing policies, standards, and guidelines for the design, development, deployment, and management of service-oriented architecture (SOA) solutions. SOA governance aims to ensure that the SOA solutions align with the business goals and objectives, meet the quality and performance requirements, and comply with the regulatory and security policies.

Some of the best practices for SOA governance are:

- Define a clear vision and strategy for SOA adoption and communicate it to all the stakeholders. The vision and strategy should outline the business drivers, benefits, challenges, and risks of SOA, as well as the scope, roadmap, and milestones for the SOA initiatives.
- Establish a SOA governance organization that consists of roles and responsibilities for governing the SOA lifecycle. The SOA governance organization should include representatives from the business, IT, and governance domains, and should have the authority and accountability to enforce the SOA policies and standards.
- Develop a SOA governance framework that defines the principles, policies, standards, and guidelines for the SOA lifecycle. The SOA governance framework should cover the aspects of service identification, design, development, testing, deployment, monitoring, management, and evolution. The SOA governance framework should also align with the enterprise architecture and IT governance frameworks.
- Implement a SOA governance toolset that supports the SOA governance framework and facilitates the governance activities. The SOA governance toolset should include tools for service registry and repository, service design and development, service testing and validation, service deployment and configuration, service monitoring and management, service security and compliance, and service governance reporting and analytics.
- Monitor and measure the SOA governance effectiveness and maturity using key performance indicators (KPIs) and metrics. The SOA governance KPIs and metrics should reflect the business value, quality, and performance of the SOA solutions, as well as the compliance and alignment of the SOA policies and standards. The SOA governance KPIs and metrics should also be used to identify the gaps, issues, and improvement opportunities for the SOA governance.
