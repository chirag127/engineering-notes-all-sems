### SOA Methodology for Enterprise

- SOA (Service-Oriented Architecture) is an integration architectural style and an enterprise-wide concept .
- SOA enables existing applications to be exposed over loosely-coupled interfaces, each corresponding to a business function, that enables applications in one part of an extended enterprise to reuse functionality in other applications.
- SOA is a particular construction technique that can be used to build enterprise IT. It describes a standard method for requesting services from distributed components and after that the results or outcome is managed.
- SOA is based on the following principles:
  - Services are reusable and can be composed into different applications.
  - Services are loosely coupled and can communicate with each other across platforms and languages.
  - Services are self-describing and can be discovered and invoked dynamically.
  - Services are stateless and do not retain information between requests.
  - Services are governed by policies and contracts that define their behavior and quality of service.
- SOA benefits include :
  - Increased agility and flexibility to respond to changing business needs and market opportunities.
  - Reduced complexity and cost of integration by using standard interfaces and protocols.
  - Improved reuse and sharing of functionality across the enterprise and beyond.
  - Enhanced scalability and reliability by leveraging distributed and fault-tolerant systems.
  - Improved alignment of IT with business goals and processes by using services as building blocks.
