### Emergence of MSA

- Microservice Architecture (MSA) is a software architectural style that consists of small, loosely coupled, and independently deployable services that communicate over well-defined APIs .
- MSA emerged as a response to the challenges of developing and maintaining large, complex, and highly scalable systems using traditional monolithic or tightly coupled Service Oriented Architecture (SOA) approaches .
- MSA enables better maintainability, testability, deployability, scalability, and resilience of software systems by allowing each service to have its own lifecycle, technology stack, and domain boundaries .
- MSA also enables faster and more agile development and delivery of software features by allowing cross-functional teams to work on different services independently and collaboratively .
- MSA is not a silver bullet, however, and it also introduces some challenges and trade-offs, such as increased complexity, network latency, data consistency, security, and monitoring .
