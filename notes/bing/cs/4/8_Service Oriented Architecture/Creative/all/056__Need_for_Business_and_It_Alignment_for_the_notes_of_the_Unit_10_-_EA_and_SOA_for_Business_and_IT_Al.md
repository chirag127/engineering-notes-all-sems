### Need for Business and IT Alignment for the notes of the Unit 10 - EA and SOA for Business and IT Alignment in the subject of Service Oriented Architecture

- Business and IT alignment is the process of ensuring that the IT strategy, goals, and activities are in sync with the business strategy, goals, and activities .
- Business and IT alignment is important because it can help the organization achieve better market impact, growth, innovation, customer satisfaction, and operational efficiency   .
- Business and IT alignment can also help the organization respond faster and more effectively to changing business needs, opportunities, and challenges, by leveraging the IT capabilities and resources  .
- Business and IT alignment can be achieved by using various methods and frameworks, such as:
  - Enterprise architecture (EA), which is the practice of designing, planning, and governing the IT systems and processes in alignment with the business vision, strategy, and goals.
  - Service-oriented architecture (SOA), which is an architectural style that enables the creation, integration, and reuse of loosely coupled, interoperable, and business-aligned services that can support the business processes and functions.
  - Business process management (BPM), which is the discipline of modeling, analyzing, optimizing, and automating the business processes to improve their performance, quality, and agility.
  - IT governance, which is the set of policies, standards, and mechanisms that define how IT decisions are made, implemented, and monitored in alignment with the business objectives and expectations .
  - IT service management (ITSM), which is the practice of delivering, supporting, and improving the IT services that meet the business needs and requirements .
- A possible mnemonic to remember the need for business and IT alignment is: **BIG IS A PIE**. This stands for:
  - **B**usiness and IT alignment can help the organization achieve **B**etter market impact, growth, innovation, customer satisfaction, and operational efficiency.
  - **I**T capabilities and resources can help the organization respond faster and more effectively to changing business **I**nterests, opportunities, and challenges.
  - **G**overnance, service management, process management, enterprise architecture, and service-oriented architecture are some of the methods and frameworks that can help achieve business and IT **G**oals alignment.
  - **IS** stands for **I**T **S**trategy, which should be in sync with the business strategy.
  - **A** stands for **A**lignment, which is the process of ensuring that the IT and business are in sync.
  - **PIE** stands for **P**erformance, **I**nnovation, and **E**fficiency, which are some of the benefits of business and IT alignment.
