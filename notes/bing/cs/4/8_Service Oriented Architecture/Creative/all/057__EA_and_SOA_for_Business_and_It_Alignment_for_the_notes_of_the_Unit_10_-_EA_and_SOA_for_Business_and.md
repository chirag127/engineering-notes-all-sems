### EA and SOA for Business and IT Alignment for the notes of the Unit 10 - EA and SOA for Business and IT Alignment in the subject of Service Oriented Architecture

- Enterprise Architecture (EA) is a framework that covers all the dimensions of IT architecture for the enterprise, such as business, data, application, and technology .
- Service Oriented Architecture (SOA) is an architectural strategy that uses the concept of "Services" as the underlying business-IT alignment entity .
- Services are self-contained, reusable, and loosely coupled components that provide business functionality and can be orchestrated to achieve business goals .
- EA and SOA share a similar goal of bridging the gap between business and IT through business-aligned services .
- However, EA and SOA are not the same thing. EA is a broader and more holistic view of the enterprise, while SOA is a specific approach to design and implement services .
- EA and SOA can complement each other and support each other's objectives. EA can provide the vision, strategy, principles, and standards for SOA, while SOA can provide the implementation, governance, and management of services for EA .
- EA and SOA can also impact each other's frameworks, methodologies, tools, and governance mechanisms .
- For example, EA can use SOA to define the service portfolio, service catalog, service contracts, and service level agreements, while SOA can use EA to align the services with the business processes, capabilities, and goals .
- EA and SOA can also use common tools and techniques, such as modeling, analysis, design, testing, monitoring, and auditing, to ensure the quality, consistency, and interoperability of services .
- EA and SOA can also use common governance structures, such as committees, roles, policies, and procedures, to ensure the alignment, compliance, and evolution of services .
- EA and SOA can benefit from each other's best practices, such as using standards, patterns, frameworks, and reference architectures, to guide the design and implementation of services .
- EA and SOA can also face similar challenges, such as complexity, scalability, security, performance, and change management, and can learn from each other's solutions and experiences .

- A possible mnemonic to remember the relationship between EA and SOA is:

**E**nterprise **A**rchitecture provides the **S**trategy, **O**rientation, and **A**lignment for **S**ervice **O**riented **A**rchitecture.

- A possible learning trick to understand the difference between EA and SOA is:

**E**nterprise **A**rchitecture is the **W**hat, **W**hy, and **W**ho of the enterprise, while **S**ervice **O**riented **A**rchitecture is the **H**ow, **W**hen, and **W**here of the services.
