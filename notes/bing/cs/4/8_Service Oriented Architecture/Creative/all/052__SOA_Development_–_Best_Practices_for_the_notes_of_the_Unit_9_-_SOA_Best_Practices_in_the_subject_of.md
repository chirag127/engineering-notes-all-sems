### SOA Development – Best Practices for the notes of the Unit 9 - SOA Best Practices in the subject of Service Oriented Architecture

SOA, or service-oriented architecture, defines a way to make software components reusable and interoperable via service interfaces. Services use common interface standards and an architectural pattern so they can be rapidly incorporated into new applications. SOA development requires careful planning, design, implementation, testing, and governance to ensure quality, performance, security, and business value. Here are some of the best practices for SOA development, based on the sources from the web search results:

- **Establish a strong executive sponsorship and a SOA evangelist.** SOA projects need support from high-ranking individuals from the business and/or IT, who can provide the vision, resources, and alignment for the SOA initiative. A SOA evangelist is someone who can promote the benefits and challenges of SOA to different stakeholders, and help overcome the resistance and skepticism that may arise.
- **Create a center of excellence (CoE).** A CoE is a team of experts who can provide guidance, standards, best practices, and governance for the SOA development life cycle . A CoE can help ensure consistency, quality, reuse, and compliance of the SOA services and processes .
- **Define completeness of work within services.** Services should be designed and implemented with a clear scope, functionality, and granularity, so that they can be reused and composed into different applications . Services should also have well-defined contracts, interfaces, and policies that specify the inputs, outputs, and behaviors of the services .
- **Ensure quality assurance.** SOA development involves testing the services at different levels, such as unit, integration, functional, performance, and security testing . Testing should be done early and often, using automated tools and frameworks, to ensure the reliability, availability, and scalability of the services .
- **Deliver substantial business value.** SOA development should be aligned with the business goals and needs, and deliver measurable outcomes and benefits, such as improved efficiency, agility, innovation, and customer satisfaction . SOA development should also be driven by the business processes and requirements, rather than by the technology or the existing systems .
- **Balance performance and security.** SOA development should consider the trade-offs and dependencies between performance and security, and use appropriate techniques and tools to optimize both aspects. For example, some of the techniques and tools include caching, compression, encryption, authentication, authorization, auditing, and monitoring.
- **Follow the standards and best practices.** SOA development should adhere to the industry standards and best practices for service design, implementation, integration, and governance, such as SOAP, REST, WSDL, UDDI, WS-* specifications, and SOA patterns . Standards and best practices can help ensure interoperability, compatibility, and quality of the services and the SOA architecture .
- **Learn from the experiences and feedback.** SOA development is an iterative and evolutionary process, that requires continuous learning, improvement, and adaptation. SOA development should leverage the experiences and feedback from the previous and ongoing SOA projects, and apply the lessons learned and the best practices to the future SOA projects.
