### Enterprise Architecture for the notes of the Unit 10 - EA and SOA for Business and IT Alignment in the subject of Service Oriented Architecture

- Enterprise Architecture (EA) is the organizing logic for business processes and IT infrastructure reflecting the integration and standardization requirements of the company’s operating model .
- EA is a framework that covers all the dimensions of IT architecture for the enterprise, such as business, data, application, and technology.
- EA helps to align the business strategy, goals, and objectives with the IT capabilities, resources, and services.
- EA provides a holistic view of the enterprise and its environment, and facilitates the communication, collaboration, and coordination among different stakeholders.
- EA supports the governance, planning, design, implementation, and evaluation of IT solutions and services that meet the business needs and expectations.
- EA also helps to manage the complexity, change, and risk associated with IT projects and programs.

- Service Oriented Architecture (SOA) is an architectural strategy that uses the concept of “Services” as the underlining business-IT alignment entity.
- SOA is based on the principles of loose coupling, reusability, interoperability, and composability of services.
- SOA enables the creation, discovery, invocation, and orchestration of services that encapsulate the business logic and functionality of the enterprise.
- SOA also enables the integration and collaboration of heterogeneous systems and applications across the enterprise and beyond.
- SOA supports the agility, flexibility, and scalability of the IT infrastructure and solutions in response to the changing business needs and demands.
- SOA also facilitates the governance, monitoring, and management of the service lifecycle and quality.

- The relationship between EA and SOA is that they share a similar goal of bridging the gap between business and IT through business-aligned services .
- EA provides the context, scope, and vision for SOA, and defines the business processes, capabilities, and requirements that drive the service identification and specification .
- SOA provides the implementation, realization, and delivery of the services that support the EA, and enables the service integration and orchestration that realize the business processes and capabilities .
- EA and SOA are complementary and interdependent, and should be aligned and coordinated at the strategy, architecture, and governance levels .
- EA and SOA should also be aligned and adapted to the operating model and culture of the enterprise, which determine the degree of integration and standardization of the business and IT domains .

- A possible mnemonic to remember the relationship between EA and SOA is:

  - EA is the **E**ye that sees the big picture and the **A**im that guides the direction.
  - SOA is the **S**olution that delivers the value and the **A**ction that makes it happen.
