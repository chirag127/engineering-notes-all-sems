### Cost Savings for the notes of the Unit 8 - Business Case for SOA in the subject of Service Oriented Architecture

- Service Oriented Architecture (SOA) is an architectural style that aims to achieve loose coupling among interacting software agents by using standard protocols and interfaces.
- SOA can provide cost savings for an organization by enabling the reuse of existing services, reducing the development and maintenance efforts, improving the agility and flexibility of the business processes, and enhancing the interoperability and integration of heterogeneous systems.
- Some of the cost savings aspects of SOA are:

  - **Reuse of services**: SOA promotes the design of reusable and granular services that can be composed and orchestrated to fulfill different business needs. This reduces the duplication of functionality and code, and leverages the existing investments in legacy systems. Reuse of services can also improve the quality and reliability of the software, as the services are tested and validated in multiple contexts. A mnemonic to remember this aspect is **R**educe, **R**euse, **R**ecycle.
  - **Reduced development and maintenance efforts**: SOA simplifies the development and maintenance of software by separating the concerns of the service providers and consumers, and by using standard protocols and interfaces to communicate. This enables the developers to focus on the business logic and functionality of the services, rather than the technical details and complexities of the underlying platforms and technologies. SOA also facilitates the modularization and decoupling of the software components, which makes them easier to modify and update without affecting the whole system. A mnemonic to remember this aspect is **S**eparation of **C**oncerns, **S**tandardization, **M**odularization.
  - **Improved agility and flexibility of the business processes**: SOA enables the alignment of the software architecture with the business goals and requirements, by providing a high-level view of the business processes and the services that support them. SOA also allows the dynamic discovery and binding of the services at runtime, which enables the adaptation and reconfiguration of the business processes according to the changing needs and demands of the customers and the market. A mnemonic to remember this aspect is **A**lignment, **A**daptation, **A**utomation.
  - **Enhanced interoperability and integration of heterogeneous systems**: SOA supports the integration and interoperability of diverse and distributed systems, by using standard protocols and interfaces to exchange data and messages. SOA also enables the abstraction and encapsulation of the service implementation details, which hides the complexity and heterogeneity of the underlying platforms and technologies. SOA also facilitates the mediation and transformation of the data and messages among the different formats and schemas, by using intermediaries and adapters. A mnemonic to remember this aspect is **I**ntegration, **A**bstraction, **M**ediation.
