There are many applications for wireless networking in different domains and scenarios. Some of the common applications are:

- Satellite system: This is a wireless communication system that uses artificial satellites to provide voice, data, and video services across the globe. Satellite systems can also be used for navigation, weather forecasting, and remote sensing.
- Television remote control: This is a device that uses infrared or radio signals to control the functions of a television set, such as changing channels, adjusting volume, and switching on or off.
- Wi-Fi: This is a wireless technology that uses radio waves to provide internet access and local area network (LAN) connectivity to devices such as computers, smartphones, tablets, and printers. Wi-Fi can also be used for wireless streaming, gaming, and smart home applications.
- Paging system: This is a wireless communication system that uses radio signals to send short messages or alerts to a receiver device, such as a pager or a mobile phone. Paging systems can be used for emergency, medical, or personal purposes.
- Wi-Max: This is a wireless technology that uses microwave signals to provide broadband internet access and wireless metropolitan area network (WMAN) connectivity to devices such as computers, laptops, and mobile phones. Wi-Max can also be used for backhaul, rural, and mobile applications.
- Security systems: These are wireless systems that use sensors, cameras, alarms, and other devices to monitor and protect a property or a person from unauthorized access, theft, fire, or other hazards. Security systems can also be controlled remotely via a wireless network or a mobile app.
- Cellphones: These are wireless devices that use cellular signals to provide voice, data, and multimedia services to users. Cellphones can also be used for messaging, browsing, social networking, and other applications.
- Computer interface devices: These are wireless devices that use Bluetooth, infrared, or radio signals to connect and communicate with a computer or a laptop. Examples of computer interface devices are keyboards, mice, headphones, speakers, and printers.
