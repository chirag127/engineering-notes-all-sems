## Unit 2 - Wireless Networking, Wireless LAN Overview: MAC issues, IEEE 802.11, Blue Tooth, Wireless

A wireless LAN (WLAN) is a network that connects two or more devices using wireless communication within a limited area such as a home, school, or office. A WLAN can be formed by a single cell or several cells, where each cell is controlled by a base station called an access point (AP). The AP connects to the wired network and acts as a bridge between the wireless and wired networks. The devices that are connected to the WLAN are called stations (STAs). Each STA has a wireless network interface controller (WNIC) that enables wireless communication with the AP or other STAs.
