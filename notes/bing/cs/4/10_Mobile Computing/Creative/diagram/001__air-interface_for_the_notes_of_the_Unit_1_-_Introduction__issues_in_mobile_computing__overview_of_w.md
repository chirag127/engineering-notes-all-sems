The air interface is the communication link between the mobile device and the base station in wireless telephony. It involves both the physical and data link layers of the OSI model. The air interface defines the frequency, modulation, coding, and signaling schemes used for transmission and reception of data and voice. Different wireless technologies have different air interfaces, such as GSM, CDMA, LTE, and 5G.
