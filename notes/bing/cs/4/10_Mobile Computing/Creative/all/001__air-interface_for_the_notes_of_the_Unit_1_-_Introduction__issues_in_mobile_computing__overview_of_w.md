### Air-interface for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction, issues in mobile computing, overview of wireless telephony: cellular concept, GSM: in the subject of Mobile Computing

- The air interface, or access mode, is the communication link between the two stations in mobile or wireless communication.
- The air interface involves both the physical and data link layers (layer 1 and 2) of the OSI model for a connection.
- The air interface is the wireless counterpart of the physical layer 1 in the OSI model .
- The air interface defines the frequency, channel bandwidth and modulation scheme .
- The air interface is also called a "radio interface" .
- In cellular telephone communications, the air interface is the radio-frequency portion of the circuit between the cellular phone set or wireless modem (usually portable or mobile) and the active base station.
- As a subscriber moves from one cell to another in the system, the active base station changes periodically.
- The air interface is one of the key factors that determine the performance, reliability and quality of service of a cellular network.
- Different cellular standards use different air interfaces, such as GSM, CDMA, TDMA, LTE, etc.
- The air interface can be classified into two types: narrowband and wideband.
  - Narrowband air interface uses a single carrier frequency and a narrow channel bandwidth (less than 200 kHz) for each user.
  - Wideband air interface uses multiple carrier frequencies and a wide channel bandwidth (more than 200 kHz) for each user.
  - Wideband air interface can support higher data rates and more users than narrowband air interface.
- The air interface can also be classified into two categories: circuit-switched and packet-switched.
  - Circuit-switched air interface establishes a dedicated connection between the sender and the receiver for the duration of the communication.
  - Packet-switched air interface divides the data into packets and sends them over a shared channel without a dedicated connection.
  - Packet-switched air interface can support more efficient and flexible data transmission than circuit-switched air interface.

#### Mnemonics and learning tricks for the air interface

- To remember the definition of the air interface, you can use the acronym AIR: Access mode, Interface, Radio-frequency.
- To remember the two layers involved in the air interface, you can use the rhyme: Physical and data link, make the air interface work in sync.
- To remember the two types of air interface, you can use the word NAW: Narrowband and Wideband.
- To remember the two categories of air interface, you can use the word CAP: Circuit-switched and Packet-switched.
- To remember the difference between narrowband and wideband air interface, you can use the phrase: Narrow is single and slow, wide is multiple and fast.
- To remember the difference between circuit-switched and packet-switched air interface, you can use the phrase: Circuit is dedicated and fixed, packet is shared and flexible.
