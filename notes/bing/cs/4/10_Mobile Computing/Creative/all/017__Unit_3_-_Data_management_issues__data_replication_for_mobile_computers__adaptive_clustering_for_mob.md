## Unit 3 - Data management issues, data replication for mobile computers, adaptive clustering for mobile

- Data management issues in mobile computing arise due to the characteristics of mobile devices and wireless networks, such as limited battery power, low bandwidth, high latency, frequent disconnections, and mobility .
- Some of the data management issues are:
  - Data availability: How to ensure that mobile users can access the data they need, even when they are disconnected from the network or the network is unreliable ?
  - Data consistency: How to maintain the consistency of data across multiple replicas, especially when updates occur in different locations and at different times ?
  - Data security: How to protect the data from unauthorized access, modification, or loss, especially when the data is stored on mobile devices or transmitted over wireless networks ?
  - Data synchronization: How to synchronize the data between mobile devices and the online database, or between different mobile devices, in an efficient and timely manner ?
  - Data adaptation: How to adapt the data to the varying capabilities and preferences of mobile devices and users, such as screen size, resolution, memory, bandwidth, and cost ?
- Data replication for mobile computers is a technique to improve data availability and performance by creating and maintaining multiple copies of data on different locations, such as mobile devices, servers, or other mobile devices  .
- Some of the benefits of data replication for mobile computers are:
  - It reduces the communication cost between mobile devices and the online database, as mobile users can access the data locally without connecting to the network .
  - It improves the data availability, as mobile users can access the data even when they are disconnected from the network or the network is unreliable  .
  - It enhances the data performance, as mobile users can access the data faster and with lower latency from local replicas  .
- Some of the challenges of data replication for mobile computers are:
  - It increases the storage and processing overhead on mobile devices, as they have to store and manage multiple replicas of data  .
  - It introduces the data consistency problem, as different replicas may have different versions of data due to concurrent or delayed updates  .
  - It requires the data synchronization mechanism, as the replicas have to be synchronized periodically or on demand to ensure data consistency and freshness  .
- Adaptive clustering for mobile is a technique to improve data replication and synchronization for mobile computers by dynamically grouping mobile devices into clusters based on their location, connectivity, or interest .
- Some of the benefits of adaptive clustering for mobile are:
  - It reduces the communication cost and latency for data replication and synchronization, as mobile devices can exchange data within clusters using short-range wireless communication, such as Bluetooth or Wi-Fi .
  - It improves the data availability and reliability, as mobile devices can access the data from nearby cluster members when they are disconnected from the network or the network is unreliable .
  - It enhances the data relevance and freshness, as mobile devices can replicate and synchronize the data that are of interest to them or their cluster members .
- Some of the challenges of adaptive clustering for mobile are:
  - It increases the complexity and overhead of cluster formation and maintenance, as mobile devices have to discover and join clusters, and update their cluster membership as they move or change their interest .
  - It introduces the data consistency and security issues, as mobile devices have to coordinate and agree on the data updates and synchronization within and across clusters, and protect the data from malicious or faulty cluster members .
