### CDMA

- CDMA stands for **Code Division Multiple Access**  , a form of radio technology that phones and phone networks use to transmit voice and data .
- CDMA works in **packet switching** mode, which means that it divides the signal into small packets and assigns each packet a unique code to identify the sender and receiver .
- CDMA allows multiple users to share the same frequency channel without interfering with each other, as each user's signal is separated by its code .
- CDMA is a **local** wireless technology, unlike GSM, which offers global usage. CDMA is mainly used in North America and some parts of Asia .
- CDMA phones are typically locked to a specific carrier and cannot be easily switched to another network . CDMA phones also do not use SIM cards, unlike GSM phones .
- CDMA is an older technology that is being phased out by newer standards such as LTE and 5G . CDMA networks are expected to shut down by 2023.
