### Wireless applications for the notes of the Unit 2 - Wireless Networking, Wireless LAN Overview: MAC issues, IEEE 802.11, Blue Tooth, Wireless

- Wireless applications are software programs that run on wireless devices, such as mobile phones, tablets, laptops, etc., and use wireless networks, such as Wi-Fi, cellular, Bluetooth, etc., to communicate, exchange and transmit information over the Internet or other networks.
- Wireless applications can be classified into different categories based on their functionality, such as:
  - Communication applications: These are applications that enable voice, text, video, or multimedia communication between wireless devices or between wireless devices and fixed networks. Examples are mobile phones, email, instant messaging, social media, video conferencing, etc.
  - Information applications: These are applications that provide access to various types of information, such as news, weather, sports, entertainment, education, health, etc., from online sources or local databases. Examples are web browsers, search engines, online maps, e-books, podcasts, etc.
  - Entertainment applications: These are applications that offer various forms of entertainment, such as games, music, videos, movies, TV shows, etc., to wireless users. Examples are online streaming services, online gaming platforms, media players, etc.
  - Business applications: These are applications that support various business processes, such as e-commerce, banking, finance, marketing, sales, customer service, etc., using wireless devices and networks. Examples are online shopping, mobile payment, mobile banking, mobile advertising, mobile CRM, etc.
  - Location-based applications: These are applications that use the location information of wireless devices to provide location-specific services, such as navigation, tracking, geofencing, geotagging, etc. Examples are GPS, online maps, location-based social networks, location-based games, etc.
  - Sensor applications: These are applications that use wireless sensors to collect, process, and transmit data about various physical phenomena, such as temperature, humidity, pressure, motion, sound, light, etc. Examples are wireless sensor networks, Internet of Things, smart home, smart city, smart grid, etc.
  - Artificial intelligence applications: These are applications that use artificial intelligence techniques, such as machine learning, deep learning, natural language processing, computer vision, etc., to perform various tasks, such as recognition, classification, prediction, recommendation, etc., using wireless devices and networks. Examples are voice assistants, chatbots, face recognition, image recognition, speech recognition, etc.
