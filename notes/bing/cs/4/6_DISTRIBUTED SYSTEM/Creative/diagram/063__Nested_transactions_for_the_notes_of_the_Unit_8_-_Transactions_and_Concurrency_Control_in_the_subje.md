A nested transaction is a transaction that is composed of sub-transactions, each of which may access data from different servers in a distributed system. A nested transaction has a single top-level transaction that initiates and terminates the whole transaction, and may have multiple levels of sub-transactions that can run concurrently or sequentially. A nested transaction is atomic, meaning that either all of its sub-transactions commit or all of them abort.
