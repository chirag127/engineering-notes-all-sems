### Thematic Roles for the notes of the Unit 4 - SEMANTICS AND PRAGMATICS in the subject of Natural Language Processing

- Thematic roles are the semantic roles that the arguments of a predicate (such as a verb or a noun) play in a sentence.
- Thematic roles capture the relationship between the predicate and its arguments, such as who did what to whom, when, where, how, etc.
- Thematic roles are also called theta roles, case roles, or semantic roles.
- There are different types of thematic roles, such as agent, patient, theme, experiencer, instrument, cause, source, goal, location, etc. The exact number and definition of thematic roles may vary depending on the theory or framework.
- Here are some examples of thematic roles and their definitions:

  - Agent: the entity that performs an action or initiates an event. Example: John broke the window. (John is the agent of the verb break)
  - Patient: the entity that undergoes a change of state or is affected by an action or event. Example: John broke the window. (The window is the patient of the verb break)
  - Theme: the entity that is moved or changes location in an action or event. Example: John threw the ball. (The ball is the theme of the verb throw)
  - Experiencer: the entity that perceives or feels an action or event. Example: John saw the movie. (John is the experiencer of the verb see)
  - Instrument: the entity that is used to perform an action or cause an event. Example: John cut the cake with a knife. (The knife is the instrument of the verb cut)
  - Cause: the entity that causes an action or event to happen. Example: The storm caused the flood. (The storm is the cause of the verb cause)
  - Source: the entity from which an action or event originates or begins. Example: John came from New York. (New York is the source of the verb come)
  - Goal: the entity to which an action or event is directed or ends. Example: John went to Paris. (Paris is the goal of the verb go)
  - Location: the entity where an action or event takes place. Example: John lives in London. (London is the location of the verb live)

- Some mnemonic devices or learning tricks to remember the thematic roles are:

  - A PET can be an Agent, a Patient, or a Theme. (A PET = Agent, Patient, Theme)
  - An ICE CREAM can be an Instrument, a Cause, an Experiencer, or a Goal. (ICE CREAM = Instrument, Cause, Experiencer, Goal)
  - A SLUG can be a Source or a Location. (SLUG = Source, Location)
  - Alternatively, you can use the acronym APETIC SLUG to remember all the thematic roles. (APETIC SLUG = Agent, Patient, Theme, Instrument, Cause, Source, Location, Goal)
