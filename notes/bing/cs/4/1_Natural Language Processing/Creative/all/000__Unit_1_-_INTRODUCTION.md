## Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION

- This unit introduces the basic concepts and principles of artificial intelligence (AI), which is the study of how to create machines and software that can perform tasks that require human intelligence, such as reasoning, learning, planning, decision making, natural language processing, computer vision, etc.
- The main objectives of this unit are:
  - To understand the definition, history, and scope of AI.
  - To learn about the different types of AI systems, such as symbolic, sub-symbolic, and hybrid.
  - To explore the main applications and challenges of AI in various domains, such as gaming, robotics, natural language processing, computer vision, etc.
  - To appreciate the ethical, social, and legal implications of AI for human society and the environment.
- The main topics covered in this unit are:
  - What is AI? Definition, history, and scope of AI.
  - Types of AI systems: Symbolic, sub-symbolic, and hybrid.
  - Applications of AI: Gaming, robotics, natural language processing, computer vision, etc.
  - Challenges of AI: Knowledge representation, reasoning, learning, planning, decision making, etc.
  - Ethical, social, and legal issues of AI: Bias, fairness, accountability, transparency, privacy, security, etc.

- Some mnemonics and learning tricks for this unit are:
  - To remember the types of AI systems, use the acronym SSH: Symbolic, Sub-symbolic, Hybrid.
  - To remember the main applications of AI, use the acronym GRAN: Gaming, Robotics, natural language processing, computer vision.
  - To remember the main challenges of AI, use the acronym KRLPD: Knowledge representation, Reasoning, Learning, Planning, Decision making.
  - To remember the main ethical, social, and legal issues of AI, use the acronym B-FAT PUS: Bias, Fairness, Accountability, Transparency, Privacy, Utility, Security.
