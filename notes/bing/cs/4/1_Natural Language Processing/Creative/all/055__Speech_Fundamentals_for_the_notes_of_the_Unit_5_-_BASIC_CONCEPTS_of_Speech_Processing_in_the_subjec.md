### Speech Fundamentals for the notes of the Unit 5 - BASIC CONCEPTS of Speech Processing in the subject of Natural Language Processing

- Speech processing is a subfield of natural language processing that deals with the analysis and synthesis of speech signals.
- Speech processing involves three main tasks: speech recognition, speech synthesis, and speech understanding.
- Speech recognition is the process of converting speech signals into text or commands that can be understood by a computer.
- Speech synthesis is the process of converting text or commands into speech signals that can be understood by a human.
- Speech understanding is the process of extracting the meaning and intention of speech signals, which may involve natural language understanding and dialogue management.
- Speech processing applications include voice assistants, speech translation, speech transcription, speech enhancement, speech emotion recognition, speech summarization, and speech generation.
- Speech processing relies on various techniques and models, such as signal processing, acoustic modeling, language modeling, speech feature extraction, speech segmentation, speech alignment, speech classification, speech clustering, speech decoding, speech encoding, speech synthesis, speech evaluation, and speech adaptation.
- Speech processing challenges include noise, variability, ambiguity, complexity, and context-dependence of speech signals and natural language.
- Speech processing can be evaluated using various metrics, such as accuracy, speed, naturalness, intelligibility, and user satisfaction.

Some mnemonics and learning tricks for speech processing are:

- To remember the three main tasks of speech processing, use the acronym RUS: Recognition, Understanding, and Synthesis.
- To remember the five canons of speech preparation, use the acronym AIRMD: Arrangement, Invention, Memory, Delivery, and Style.
- To remember some of the speech processing applications, use the acronym VESTS: Voice assistants, Emotion recognition, Summarization, Translation, and Synthesis.
- To remember some of the speech processing challenges, use the acronym NAVAC: Noise, Ambiguity, Variability, Complexity, and Context.
