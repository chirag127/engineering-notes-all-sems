## Unit 6 - SPEECH-ANALYSIS

Speech analysis is the process of extracting information from speech signals, such as the speaker's identity, emotions, language, accent, dialect, gender, age, etc. Speech analysis can also involve the recognition and understanding of the speech content, such as the words, phrases, sentences, topics, intents, etc.

Some of the main topics and concepts in speech analysis are:

- **Speech signal**: A speech signal is a time-varying waveform that represents the acoustic pressure variations produced by the vocal tract when a person speaks. A speech signal can be characterized by its amplitude, frequency, phase, and spectrum. A speech signal can be sampled and digitized to be stored and processed by a computer.

- **Speech feature extraction**: Speech feature extraction is the process of transforming a speech signal into a set of numerical values that capture the relevant information for a specific task, such as speaker identification, speech recognition, etc. Speech features can be extracted from different levels of the speech signal, such as the short-term spectrum, the pitch, the formants, the cepstrum, the mel-frequency cepstral coefficients (MFCCs), etc.

- **Speech segmentation**: Speech segmentation is the process of dividing a speech signal into smaller units, such as syllables, phonemes, words, etc. Speech segmentation can be based on acoustic cues, such as pauses, silences, energy, pitch, etc., or on linguistic cues, such as grammar, vocabulary, etc. Speech segmentation can be useful for speech recognition, speech synthesis, speech summarization, etc.

- **Speech recognition**: Speech recognition is the process of converting a speech signal into a sequence of symbols, such as letters, words, numbers, etc. Speech recognition can be performed by using statistical models, such as hidden Markov models (HMMs), neural networks, etc., or by using rule-based systems, such as finite state machines, grammars, etc. Speech recognition can be affected by factors such as noise, accent, dialect, vocabulary, etc.

- **Speech understanding**: Speech understanding is the process of extracting the meaning and the intention of a speech signal, such as the topic, the sentiment, the request, the command, etc. Speech understanding can be performed by using natural language processing (NLP) techniques, such as parsing, semantic analysis, dialogue management, etc. Speech understanding can be useful for speech-based applications, such as voice assistants, chatbots, etc.

- **Speaker recognition**: Speaker recognition is the process of identifying or verifying the identity of a speaker from a speech signal, based on the speaker's voice characteristics, such as the pitch, the timbre, the accent, etc. Speaker recognition can be performed by using statistical models, such as Gaussian mixture models (GMMs), support vector machines (SVMs), etc., or by using deep learning models, such as convolutional neural networks (CNNs), recurrent neural networks (RNNs), etc. Speaker recognition can be useful for security, authentication, personalization, etc.

- **Speech synthesis**: Speech synthesis is the process of generating a speech signal from a text or a symbolic representation, such as phonetic symbols, prosodic symbols, etc. Speech synthesis can be performed by using concatenative methods, such as unit selection, diphone synthesis, etc., or by using parametric methods, such as statistical parametric synthesis, neural parametric synthesis, etc. Speech synthesis can be useful for text-to-speech (TTS) applications, such as voice assistants, audiobooks, etc.

Some of the mnemonics and learning tricks for speech analysis are:

- To remember the main types of speech features, use the acronym **SPF**: Spectrum, Pitch, Formants.

- To remember the main types of speech segmentation, use the acronym **SWAP**: Syllables, Words, Acoustic, Phonemes.

- To remember the main types of speech recognition models, use the acronym **HNR**: HMMs, Neural networks, Rule-based.

- To remember the main types of speech understanding techniques, use the acronym **PSD**: Parsing, Semantic analysis, Dialogue management.

- To remember the main types of speaker recognition models, use the acronym **GSD**: GMMs, SVMs, Deep learning.

- To remember the main types of speech synthesis methods, use the acronym **CP**: Concatenative, Parametric.
