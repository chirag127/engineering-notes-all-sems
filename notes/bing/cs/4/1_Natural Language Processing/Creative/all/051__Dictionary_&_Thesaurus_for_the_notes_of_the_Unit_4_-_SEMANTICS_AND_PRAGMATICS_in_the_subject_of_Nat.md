### Dictionary & Thesaurus for the notes of the Unit 4 - SEMANTICS AND PRAGMATICS in the subject of Natural Language Processing

- A **dictionary** is a collection of words and their meanings, pronunciations, spellings, usage examples, etc. A dictionary can be used to provide information about the words in a natural language, such as their part of speech, synonyms, antonyms, hyponyms, hypernyms, collocations, etc.
- A **thesaurus** is a specialized dictionary that stores synonyms and antonyms of selected words in a language. A thesaurus can be used to find alternative words that have similar or opposite meanings, or to group words according to their similarity or difference.
- Both dictionaries and thesauruses are useful resources for natural language processing (NLP) tasks, such as word sense disambiguation, text summarization, text generation, sentiment analysis, information retrieval, etc.
- Some of the advantages of using dictionaries and thesauruses for NLP are:
  - They can help to enrich the vocabulary and diversity of the text, by providing synonyms and antonyms for words.
  - They can help to reduce the ambiguity and complexity of the text, by providing definitions and examples for words.
  - They can help to improve the accuracy and relevance of the text, by providing information about the usage and context of words.
  - They can help to enhance the readability and understandability of the text, by providing information about the pronunciation and spelling of words.
- Some of the disadvantages of using dictionaries and thesauruses for NLP are:
  - They may not cover all the words and meanings in a language, especially the new and rare ones.
  - They may not capture the nuances and variations of the words and meanings in different domains, genres, and registers.
  - They may introduce errors and inconsistencies in the text, by providing incorrect or outdated information about the words.
  - They may increase the complexity and difficulty of the text, by providing too many or too few alternatives for words.
- Some of the challenges of using dictionaries and thesauruses for NLP are:
  - How to select the most appropriate dictionary or thesaurus for a given task and text.
  - How to access and integrate the information from different dictionaries and thesauruses, especially when they are in different formats and languages.
  - How to deal with the conflicts and contradictions among the information from different dictionaries and thesauruses, especially when they have different perspectives and standards.
  - How to evaluate the quality and usefulness of the information from dictionaries and thesauruses, especially when they have different levels of coverage and reliability.
- Some of the examples of using dictionaries and thesauruses for NLP are:
  - The Lesk algorithm is a dictionary-based method for word sense disambiguation, which compares the definitions of the words in a sentence and selects the senses that have the most overlap .
  - WordNet is a large lexical database of English, which organizes words into sets of synonyms called synsets, and provides semantic relations among them, such as hypernymy, hyponymy, meronymy, etc.
  - Roget's Thesaurus is a classic thesaurus of English, which classifies words into six main categories (abstract relations, space, matter, intellect, volition, and affections), and further subdivides them into hierarchies of headings and subheadings.
  - Word2Vec is a neural network-based method for generating word embeddings, which are vector representations of words that capture their semantic and syntactic similarities and differences.
