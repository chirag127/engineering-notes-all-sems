## Unit 2 - WORD LEVEL ANALYSIS

Word level analysis is the process of analyzing natural language at the individual word level. It involves identifying and extracting the smallest meaningful units of a word, called morphemes, and applying grammatical rules to categories and groups of words, called parts of speech. Word level analysis is an important step in natural language processing, as it helps to understand the structure and meaning of text.

Some of the topics and techniques involved in word level analysis are:

- **Regular expressions**: A regular expression (RE) is a language for specifying text search strings. RE helps us to match or find other strings or sets of strings, using a specialized syntax held in a pattern. RE can be used to perform tasks such as tokenization, stemming, lemmatization, and pattern matching.

- **Tokenization**: Tokenization is the process of splitting a text into smaller units, called tokens, based on some criteria. Tokens can be words, sentences, paragraphs, or any other meaningful unit of text. Tokenization is useful for tasks such as word counting, indexing, and text classification.

- **Stemming**: Stemming is the process of reducing a word to its root form, by removing suffixes and prefixes. For example, the words "running", "runner", and "run" have the same root "run". Stemming can help to reduce the vocabulary size and normalize the text.

- **Lemmatization**: Lemmatization is the process of converting a word to its base form, by considering its part of speech and context. For example, the word "better" can be lemmatized to "good" as an adjective, or to "better" as a verb. Lemmatization can help to improve the semantic analysis of text.

- **Parts of speech tagging**: Parts of speech tagging (POS tagging) is the process of assigning a grammatical category to each word in a text, such as noun, verb, adjective, etc. POS tagging can help to identify the syntactic role and function of words in a sentence.

- **Named entity recognition**: Named entity recognition (NER) is the process of identifying and extracting the names of persons, organizations, locations, dates, etc. from a text. NER can help to extract useful information and facts from text.

- **Word sense disambiguation**: Word sense disambiguation (WSD) is the process of determining the meaning of a word in a given context, when the word has multiple possible meanings. For example, the word "bank" can mean a financial institution, a river shore, or a verb meaning to tilt. WSD can help to improve the semantic analysis of text.

Some of the mnemonics and learning tricks for word level analysis are:

- To remember the difference between stemming and lemmatization, think of the word "stem" as a part of a plant that can be cut off, while the word "lemma" as a logical proposition that can be proved.

- To remember the order of the steps in word level analysis, think of the acronym "RTLS-PNW":

  - R: Regular expressions
  - T: Tokenization
  - L: Lemmatization
  - S: Stemming
  - P: Parts of speech tagging
  - N: Named entity recognition
  - W: Word sense disambiguation

- To remember the types of named entities, think of the acronym "POLD":

  - P: Person
  - O: Organization
  - L: Location
  - D: Date
