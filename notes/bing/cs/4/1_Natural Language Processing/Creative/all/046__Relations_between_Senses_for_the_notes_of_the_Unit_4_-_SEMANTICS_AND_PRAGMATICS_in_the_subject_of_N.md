### Relations between Senses for the notes of the Unit 4 - SEMANTICS AND PRAGMATICS in the subject of Natural Language Processing

- Semantics is the study of the meaning of linguistic expressions, such as words, phrases, and sentences.
- Pragmatics is the study of the meaning of linguistic expressions in context, such as the speaker's intention, the listener's inference, and the situational factors.
- The relation between semantics and pragmatics is a matter of debate among linguists, but one possible way to view it is as follows:
  - Semantics provides the literal meaning of an expression, based on its structure, grammar, lexicon, and sounds.
  - Pragmatics provides the intended meaning of an expression, based on the speaker's purpose, the listener's background knowledge, and the relevant aspects of the situation.
  - Pragmatics can modify, enrich, or override the semantic meaning, depending on the context and the conversational implicatures.
- For example, consider the following sentence:

  > I have a headache.

  - The semantic meaning of this sentence is that the speaker is experiencing a pain in their head.
  - The pragmatic meaning of this sentence can vary depending on the context, such as:
    - The speaker is refusing an invitation to go out.
    - The speaker is asking for sympathy or help.
    - The speaker is expressing annoyance or frustration.
    - The speaker is lying or exaggerating.
- A traditional way of investigating the meaning of a word is to study the relationships between its meaning and the meanings of other words, which are called sense relations.
- Sense relations can be divided into two types: paradigmatic and syntagmatic.
  - Paradigmatic sense relations are the relations between words that can substitute for each other in a given context, such as synonyms, antonyms, hyponyms, hypernyms, etc.
  - Syntagmatic sense relations are the relations between words that can occur in construction with each other, such as collocations, selectional restrictions, semantic roles, etc.
- For example, consider the following sentence:

  > She is a beautiful woman.

  - The word beautiful has a paradigmatic relation with other words that can replace it, such as pretty, gorgeous, stunning, etc. These words are synonyms, meaning they have similar meanings.
  - The word beautiful also has a paradigmatic relation with words that have opposite meanings, such as ugly, hideous, repulsive, etc. These words are antonyms, meaning they have contrasting meanings.
  - The word woman has a paradigmatic relation with other words that belong to the same category, such as girl, lady, female, etc. These words are hyponyms, meaning they are more specific than woman.
  - The word woman also has a paradigmatic relation with words that include it as a category, such as human, person, mammal, etc. These words are hypernyms, meaning they are more general than woman.
  - The word beautiful has a syntagmatic relation with the word woman, meaning they can co-occur as an adjective and a noun. This is an example of a collocation, meaning a frequent or natural combination of words.
  - The word beautiful also has a syntagmatic relation with the word woman, meaning it can modify it semantically. This is an example of a selectional restriction, meaning a constraint on the possible meanings of words that can combine.
  - The word woman has a syntagmatic relation with the word she, meaning it can be referred to by it. This is an example of a semantic role, meaning a function or a relation that a word plays in a sentence.

- A possible mnemonic to remember the difference between paradigmatic and syntagmatic sense relations is to think of the words parallel and syntactic.
  - Parallel means running alongside or having the same direction, which can remind us of paradigmatic relations that hold between words that can replace each other or belong to the same category.
  - Syntactic means relating to the structure or arrangement of words, which can remind us of syntagmatic relations that hold between words that can occur together or have a specific function in a sentence.
