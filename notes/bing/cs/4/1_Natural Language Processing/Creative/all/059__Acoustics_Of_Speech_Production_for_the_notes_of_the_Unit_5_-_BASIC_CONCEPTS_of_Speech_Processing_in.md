### Acoustics Of Speech Production for the notes of the Unit 5 - BASIC CONCEPTS of Speech Processing in the subject of Natural Language Processing

- Acoustics of speech production is the study of how speech sounds are generated and modified by the human vocal tract.
- Speech sounds are produced by forcing air from the lungs through the trachea and the rest of the vocal tract, which consists of the larynx, pharynx, oral cavity, and nasal cavity.
- The larynx contains the vocal folds, which can vibrate to produce voiced sounds, such as vowels and some consonants. The vibration frequency of the vocal folds determines the pitch of the voice.
- The shape and configuration of the supralaryngeal vocal tract, which can be altered by the movements of the tongue, jaw, lips, and soft palate, affect the resonance properties of the vocal tract and filter the sound waves produced by the larynx. This results in a shaped spectrum with broadband energy peaks, called formants, that characterize different speech sounds.
- The acoustic theory of speech production is a mathematical model that relates the physical dimensions of the vocal tract to the acoustic properties of the speech signal. The model assumes that the vocal tract can be approximated by a series of connected tubes with varying cross-sectional areas, and that the sound waves propagate as plane waves within each tube segment.
- The acoustic theory of speech production can be used to calculate the transfer function of the vocal tract, which describes how the input sound (from the larynx) is transformed into the output sound (at the lips or nostrils). The transfer function can be represented by a frequency response curve, which shows the amplitude and phase of the output sound as a function of frequency.
- The acoustic theory of speech production can also be used to estimate the vocal tract shape from the speech signal, by applying inverse filtering techniques. This can be useful for speech analysis, synthesis, and recognition applications.
- A mnemonic to remember the three mechanisms of speech production is: **E**nergy, **S**ource, and **F**ilter. The first letter of each word corresponds to the first letter of each mechanism: **E**nergy from the lungs, **S**ource of sound from the larynx, and **F**ilter function from the vocal tract.
