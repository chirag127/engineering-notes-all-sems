### Tokenization for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of Natural Language Processing

- Tokenization is the process of breaking down a text into smaller units called tokens, such as words, punctuation marks, numbers, etc.
- Tokens are the basic building blocks of natural language processing (NLP) tasks, such as parsing, tagging, stemming, lemmatization, etc.
- Tokenization can be done at different levels of granularity, such as character-level, word-level, sentence-level, paragraph-level, etc.
- Tokenization can be done using different methods, such as rule-based, dictionary-based, statistical, machine learning, etc.
- Tokenization can be influenced by various factors, such as language, domain, genre, style, etc.
- Tokenization can have various challenges, such as handling abbreviations, contractions, hyphenated words, compound words, multi-word expressions, etc.

Some mnemonics and learning tricks for tokenization are:

- To remember the definition of tokenization, think of the word "token" as a small piece of something, such as a coin, a ticket, a stamp, etc. Tokenization is the process of splitting a text into such small pieces.
- To remember the different levels of tokenization, think of the acronym "C-W-S-P", which stands for Character, Word, Sentence, Paragraph. These are the four common levels of tokenization in NLP.
- To remember the different methods of tokenization, think of the acronym "R-D-S-M", which stands for Rule, Dictionary, Statistical, Machine learning. These are the four common methods of tokenization in NLP.
- To remember some of the factors that influence tokenization, think of the acronym "L-D-G-S", which stands for Language, Domain, Genre, Style. These are some of the factors that affect how a text is tokenized.
- To remember some of the challenges of tokenization, think of the acronym "A-C-H-C-M", which stands for Abbreviations, Contractions, Hyphenated words, Compound words, Multi-word expressions. These are some of the common problems that tokenization has to deal with.
