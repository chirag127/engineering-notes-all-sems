### Filter Bank And LPC Methods for the notes of the Unit 5 - BASIC CONCEPTS of Speech Processing in the subject of Natural Language Processing

- Filter Bank and LPC Methods are two techniques for feature extraction in speech processing, which aim to represent the speech signal in a compact and meaningful way.
- Feature extraction is the process of transforming the raw speech signal into a set of parameters that capture the relevant information for speech recognition, synthesis, coding, or analysis.
- Filter Bank and LPC Methods are based on different models of speech production, which assume that speech is generated by a source-filter system, where the source is the vocal cords and the filter is the vocal tract.
- Filter Bank Methods use a bank of band-pass filters to divide the speech signal into frequency bands, and then compute the energy or the logarithm of the energy in each band. The resulting features are called filter-bank energies (FBEs) or mel-frequency cepstral coefficients (MFCCs), depending on the type and number of filters used.
- LPC Methods use a linear prediction model to estimate the coefficients of a filter that approximates the vocal tract transfer function. The resulting features are called linear prediction coefficients (LPCs) or LPC-cepstral coefficients (LPCCs), depending on the way they are computed and transformed.
- Filter Bank Methods are more robust to noise and channel distortion, while LPC Methods are more efficient in terms of computation and storage.
- Filter Bank Methods are more suitable for speech recognition, while LPC Methods are more suitable for speech coding and synthesis.
- A mnemonic to remember the difference between Filter Bank and LPC Methods is: **F**ilter **B**ank for **F**requency **B**ands, **L**inear **P**rediction for **L**inear **P**arameters.
