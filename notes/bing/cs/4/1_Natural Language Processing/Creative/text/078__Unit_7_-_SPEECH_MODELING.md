## Unit 7 - SPEECH MODELING

- Speech modeling is the process of creating mathematical representations of speech signals and their underlying linguistic and acoustic properties.
- Speech modeling can be used for various applications, such as speech recognition, speech synthesis, speech enhancement, speech coding, speech analysis, and speech understanding.
- Speech modeling can be divided into two main categories: parametric and non-parametric models.
- Parametric models assume a specific structure or form for the speech signal, such as a linear combination of sinusoids, a source-filter model, or a hidden Markov model. Parametric models can be efficient and compact, but may not capture all the details and variations of speech.
- Non-parametric models do not assume a specific structure or form for the speech signal, but rather learn it from data, such as neural networks, Gaussian mixture models, or deep generative models. Non-parametric models can be flexible and powerful, but may require more data and computational resources.
- Speech modeling can also be classified based on the level of abstraction or granularity of the speech signal, such as waveform, spectral, phonetic, prosodic, or semantic models.
- Waveform models operate directly on the raw speech signal, such as waveform coding, waveform synthesis, or waveform generation.
- Spectral models operate on the frequency-domain representation of the speech signal, such as spectral analysis, spectral conversion, or spectral enhancement.
- Phonetic models operate on the discrete units of speech sounds, such as phonemes, allophones, or triphones. Phonetic models can be used for speech recognition, speech synthesis, or speech alignment.
- Prosodic models operate on the suprasegmental features of speech, such as pitch, intensity, duration, or stress. Prosodic models can be used for speech synthesis, speech emotion recognition, or speech summarization.
- Semantic models operate on the meaning or content of speech, such as words, phrases, or sentences. Semantic models can be used for speech understanding, speech translation, or speech generation.
