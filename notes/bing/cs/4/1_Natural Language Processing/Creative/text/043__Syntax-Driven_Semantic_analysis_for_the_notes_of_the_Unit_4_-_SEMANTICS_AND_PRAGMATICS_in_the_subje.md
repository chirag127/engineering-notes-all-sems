### Syntax-Driven Semantic Analysis

- Syntax-driven semantic analysis is a method of deriving the meaning of natural language sentences from their syntactic structure and lexical information.
- The main idea is to assign semantic representations to syntactic categories and rules, and then compose them according to the syntactic derivation of a sentence.
- Semantic representations can be expressed in various formal languages, such as first-order logic, lambda calculus, or feature structures.
- Semantic representations can capture various aspects of meaning, such as truth conditions, reference, quantification, scope, modality, tense, aspect, and presupposition.
- Syntax-driven semantic analysis can be implemented in various frameworks, such as generative grammar, categorial grammar, or head-driven phrase structure grammar.
- Syntax-driven semantic analysis can be applied to various tasks, such as natural language understanding, question answering, information extraction, and machine translation.
