## Unit 5 - BASIC CONCEPTS of Speech Processing

- Speech processing is the study of how humans produce, perceive, and understand speech, as well as how machines can process, synthesize, and recognize speech signals.
- Speech processing involves several subfields, such as speech analysis, speech synthesis, speech recognition, speech enhancement, speech coding, and speech translation.
- Speech analysis is the process of extracting features and information from speech signals, such as pitch, intensity, duration, formants, spectral envelope, etc.
- Speech synthesis is the process of generating speech signals from text or other symbolic representations, such as phonetic symbols, prosodic features, etc.
- Speech recognition is the process of converting speech signals into text or other symbolic representations, such as phonetic symbols, words, sentences, etc.
- Speech enhancement is the process of improving the quality and intelligibility of speech signals, such as reducing noise, reverberation, distortion, etc.
- Speech coding is the process of compressing speech signals for efficient transmission or storage, such as reducing bit rate, bandwidth, etc.
- Speech translation is the process of converting speech signals from one language to another, such as translating speech to text, text to speech, or speech to speech.
