### Syntactic Parsing

- Syntactic parsing is the process of analyzing the structure of a sentence according to a given grammar.
- A grammar is a set of rules that define the syntactic categories and relations in a language, such as parts of speech, phrases, clauses, and dependencies.
- A syntactic parser takes a sentence as input and outputs a parse tree or a dependency graph that represents the syntactic structure of the sentence.
- A parse tree is a hierarchical representation of the syntactic constituents and their relations in a sentence, such as noun phrases, verb phrases, prepositional phrases, etc.
- A dependency graph is a representation of the syntactic dependencies between the words in a sentence, such as subject, object, modifier, etc.
- Syntactic parsing can be performed by different methods, such as top-down, bottom-up, chart, probabilistic, or neural network based parsing.
- Syntactic parsing can be used for various natural language processing tasks, such as semantic analysis, information extraction, machine translation, question answering, etc.
