### Dictionary & Thesaurus

- A dictionary is a collection of words and their meanings, along with other information such as pronunciation, spelling, usage, synonyms, antonyms, etc.
- A thesaurus is a collection of words and their synonyms, along with other information such as usage, related terms, categories, etc.
- Both dictionary and thesaurus are useful resources for natural language processing, as they provide information about the lexical and semantic properties of words.
- Some of the applications of dictionary and thesaurus in natural language processing are:

  - Word sense disambiguation: the task of determining the correct meaning of a word in a given context, based on the dictionary definitions and thesaurus relations of the word and its surrounding words.
  - Text summarization: the task of generating a concise and informative summary of a longer text, based on the dictionary meanings and thesaurus synonyms of the key words and phrases in the text.
  - Text generation: the task of producing natural language text from a given input, such as a topic, a keyword, a query, etc., based on the dictionary and thesaurus information of the input and the output words.
  - Text analysis: the task of extracting useful information from a text, such as sentiment, topic, keywords, etc., based on the dictionary and thesaurus information of the words and phrases in the text.
  - Text simplification: the task of rewriting a text in a simpler and easier way, based on the dictionary and thesaurus information of the words and phrases in the text, and replacing complex words with simpler synonyms or definitions.
  - Text translation: the task of converting a text from one language to another, based on the dictionary and thesaurus information of the words and phrases in both languages, and finding the best equivalents or paraphrases for the source text.
