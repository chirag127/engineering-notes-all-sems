## Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION

- This unit introduces the basic concepts and principles of artificial intelligence (AI), which is the study of how to create machines and software that can perform tasks that require human intelligence.
- The unit covers the following topics:
  - What is AI and why is it important?
  - What are the main goals and challenges of AI?
  - What are the main branches and applications of AI?
  - What are the main methods and tools of AI?
  - What are the ethical and social implications of AI?
- The unit aims to provide a general overview of the field of AI and its relevance to various domains and disciplines.
- The unit also aims to stimulate the curiosity and interest of the students in learning more about AI and its potential impact on the world.
