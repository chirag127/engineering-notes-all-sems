### Statistical LM for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of Natural Language Processing

- A **statistical language model (LM)** is a probability distribution over sequences of words or symbols, such as sentences or texts.
- A statistical LM assigns a probability to every possible sequence, based on how likely it is to occur in a given corpus or domain of interest.
- A statistical LM can be used for various natural language processing (NLP) tasks, such as speech recognition, machine translation, text generation, spelling correction, information retrieval, etc.
- A statistical LM can be built from a large collection of text data, using various methods and techniques, such as n-grams, neural networks, hidden Markov models, etc.
- A statistical LM can be evaluated by various metrics, such as perplexity, log-likelihood, cross-entropy, etc.
- A statistical LM can be improved by various methods, such as smoothing, interpolation, back-off, etc.
