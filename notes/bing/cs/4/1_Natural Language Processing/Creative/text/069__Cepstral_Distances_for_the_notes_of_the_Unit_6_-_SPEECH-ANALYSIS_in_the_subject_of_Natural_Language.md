### Cepstral Distances for the notes of the Unit 6 - SPEECH-ANALYSIS in the subject of Natural Language Processing

- Cepstral distance is a measure of the similarity between two speech frames based on their cepstral coefficients .
- Cepstral coefficients are obtained by applying the inverse Fourier transform to the logarithm of the spectrum of a speech signal .
- Cepstral distance can be used for various applications in speech analysis, such as endpoint detection, isolated word recognition, and emotional speech recognition .
- Cepstral distance can be combined with other features, such as speech energy, to improve the performance of speech classification .
- Cepstral distance can be calculated using different methods, such as Euclidean distance, Mahalanobis distance, or cosine distance .
- Cepstral distance can be normalized or weighted to account for the variations in speech signals .
