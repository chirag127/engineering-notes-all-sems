## Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION

Unit 1 is about introducing the basic concepts and skills of critical thinking and logic. In this unit, you will learn how to:

- Define critical thinking and logic
- Identify and analyze arguments
- Evaluate the validity and soundness of arguments
- Recognize and avoid common fallacies
- Apply critical thinking to real-life scenarios
