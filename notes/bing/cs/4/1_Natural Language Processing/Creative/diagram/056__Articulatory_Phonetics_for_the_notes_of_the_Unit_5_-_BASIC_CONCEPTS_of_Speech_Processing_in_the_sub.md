Articulatory phonetics is the branch of phonetics that studies how speech sounds are produced by the human vocal tract. It involves the analysis of the movements and positions of the vocal organs, such as the tongue, lips, jaw, velum, glottis, and pharynx. Articulatory phonetics is also concerned with the aerodynamics and acoustics of speech production, that is, how the airflow and sound waves are affected by the articulators.
