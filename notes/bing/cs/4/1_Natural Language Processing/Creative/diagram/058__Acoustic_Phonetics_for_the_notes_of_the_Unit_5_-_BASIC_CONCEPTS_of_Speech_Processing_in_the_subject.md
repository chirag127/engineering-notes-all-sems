Acoustic phonetics is the branch of phonetics that deals with the acoustic aspects of speech sounds, such as their frequency, intensity, and duration. Acoustic phonetics also studies how these properties relate to other branches of phonetics, such as articulatory or auditory phonetics, and to abstract linguistic concepts, such as phonemes, phrases, or utterances.
