## Unit 2 - WORD LEVEL ANALYSIS

Word level analysis is the process of examining the structure and meaning of words in a text. It can involve various aspects, such as spelling, grammar, vocabulary, readability, sentiment, and complexity. Word level analysis can help to understand the text better, improve writing skills, and create appropriate materials for different audiences and purposes.

One possible way to represent the word level analysis of a text is to use a diagram that shows the different components and their relationships.
