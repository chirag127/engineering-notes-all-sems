Word classes are categories of words that share some common properties, such as syntactic function, morphology, or meaning. In natural language processing, word classes are often used to simplify the analysis of language data and to reduce the complexity of models. Some common word classes are nouns, verbs, adjectives, adverbs, pronouns, prepositions, conjunctions, and determiners.
