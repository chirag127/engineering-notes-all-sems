### On‐demand Provisioning for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction To Cloud Computing in the subject of Cloud Computing

- On-demand provisioning is one of the essential characteristics of cloud computing, which enables the users to access computing resources as and when they need them .
- On-demand provisioning means that the cloud service provider can dynamically allocate and deallocate resources according to the user's demand, without requiring human intervention.
- On-demand provisioning reduces the cost and complexity of managing IT infrastructure, as the users only pay for what they use and do not need to maintain idle or excess resources .
- On-demand provisioning also improves the scalability and elasticity of cloud services, as the users can easily scale up or down their resources according to the changing workload or performance requirements .
- On-demand provisioning can be implemented using various techniques, such as virtualization, containerization, orchestration, automation, and self-service portals.
- A mnemonic to remember the benefits of on-demand provisioning is **CARES**:

  - **C**ost-effective: Users only pay for what they use and save money on infrastructure and maintenance.
  - **A**vailable: Users can access resources anytime and anywhere over the internet.
  - **R**esponsive: Users can quickly adjust their resources to meet the changing demand or performance.
  - **E**fficient: Users can optimize their resource utilization and avoid waste or underutilization.
  - **S**imple: Users can easily request and release resources without involving IT staff or complex procedures.
