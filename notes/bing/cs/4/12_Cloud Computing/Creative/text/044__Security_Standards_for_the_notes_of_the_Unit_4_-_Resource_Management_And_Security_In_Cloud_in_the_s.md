### Security Standards

Security standards are guidelines and best practices for ensuring the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of data and resources in cloud computing. Security standards can help organizations to design, implement, and monitor their cloud security strategy and reduce the risk of security breaches and incidents. Some of the security standards that are relevant to cloud computing are:

- **ISO/IEC 27001/27002**: These are international standards for information security management systems (ISMS) that provide a framework for establishing, implementing, operating, monitoring, reviewing, maintaining, and improving the security of information assets. ISO/IEC 27001 specifies the requirements for an ISMS, while ISO/IEC 27002 provides a code of practice for information security controls .
- **ISO/IEC 27017**: This is a security standard that provides guidance for cloud service providers and consumers on the implementation of information security controls for cloud services, based on the ISO/IEC 27002 code of practice. It also specifies additional cloud-specific controls and responsibilities for both parties .
- **ISO/IEC 27018**: This is a security standard that provides guidance for cloud service providers on the protection of personal data in the cloud, based on the ISO/IEC 27002 code of practice. It also specifies additional cloud-specific controls and responsibilities for the protection of privacy and compliance with data protection laws and regulations .
- **General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)**: This is a regulation that applies to the processing of personal data of individuals in the European Union (EU) and the European Economic Area (EEA). It imposes strict obligations and responsibilities on data controllers and processors, as well as rights and remedies for data subjects. It also sets forth rules for the transfer of personal data outside the EU and EEA .
- **System and Organization Controls (SOC) Reporting**: This is a framework for reporting on the internal controls of service organizations that provide services to other entities, such as cloud service providers. It consists of three types of reports: SOC 1, SOC 2, and SOC 3. SOC 1 reports on the controls relevant to the financial reporting of the user entities, SOC 2 reports on the controls relevant to the security, availability, processing integrity, confidentiality, and privacy of the service organization's systems and data, and SOC 3 reports on the same controls as SOC 2 but in a general and less detailed manner .
- **Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard (PCI DSS)**: This is a security standard that applies to the processing, storage, and transmission of cardholder data and sensitive authentication data for payment transactions. It specifies 12 requirements for the protection of cardholder data and the prevention of fraud and identity theft. It also requires regular assessments and audits of the compliance status of the entities involved in the payment process .
- **Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA)**: This is a law that regulates the privacy and security of health information in the United States. It applies to covered entities, such as health care providers, health plans, and health care clearinghouses, and their business associates, such as cloud service providers, that create, receive, maintain, or transmit protected health information (PHI) on behalf of the covered entities. It requires the implementation of administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of PHI, as well as the notification of breaches and the provision of access and rights to individuals .
- **CIS AWS Foundations v1.2**: This is a security benchmark that provides guidance for configuring and securing Amazon Web Services (AWS) cloud environments, based on the best practices and recommendations from AWS and the Center for Internet Security (CIS). It covers 49 controls for identity and access management, logging and monitoring, networking, and encryption and key management .
