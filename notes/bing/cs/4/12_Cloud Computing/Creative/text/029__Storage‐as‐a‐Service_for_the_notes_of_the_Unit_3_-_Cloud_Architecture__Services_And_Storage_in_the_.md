### Storage‐as‐a‐Service

- Storage‐as‐a‐Service (STaaS) is a cloud service model that provides on-demand access to storage and compute resources over the internet or on-premises .
- STaaS allows users to store, manage, and access their data without investing in or maintaining their own storage infrastructure .
- STaaS is more cost-efficient and scalable than traditional storage solutions, as users only pay for the storage they need and can adjust their usage according to their demand .
- STaaS can be used for various purposes, such as multimedia storage, data repositories, data backup and recovery, and disaster recovery .
- STaaS is different from other cloud service models, such as Infrastructure‐as‐a‐Service (IaaS), Platform‐as‐a‐Service (PaaS), and Software‐as‐a‐Service (SaaS), in terms of the level of abstraction and control over the resources.
  - IaaS provides access to the underlying hardware and network components, such as servers, storage, and networking.
  - PaaS provides access to the software development and deployment environment, such as operating systems, databases, and middleware.
  - SaaS provides access to the software applications and services, such as email, CRM, and ERP.
  - STaaS provides access to the storage and compute resources, such as files, objects, and blocks .
