### Definition of Cloud

- Cloud computing is the **delivery of computing services** over the internet, such as servers, storage, databases, networking, software, analytics, and intelligence .
- Cloud computing enables **on-demand access** to computing resources, without the need for enterprises to procure, configure, or manage resources themselves .
- Cloud computing relies on some form of **virtualized IT infrastructure**, which abstracts the physical hardware and allows it to be pooled and divided irrespective of physical boundaries.
- Cloud computing offers **faster innovation, flexible resources, and economies of scale**, as users only pay for the services they use and can scale up or down as needed .
- Cloud computing can be classified into different **service models** and **deployment models**, depending on the level of abstraction and control, and the location of the resources  .
