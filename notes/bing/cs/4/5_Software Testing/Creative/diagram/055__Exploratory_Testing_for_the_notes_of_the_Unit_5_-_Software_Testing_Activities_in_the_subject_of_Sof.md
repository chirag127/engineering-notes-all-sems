Exploratory testing is an approach to software testing that is concisely described as simultaneous learning, test design and test execution. It is suited for specific testing scenarios, such as when someone needs to learn about a product or application quickly and provide rapid feedback. It helps review the quality of a product from a user perspective. Exploratory testing relies on the guidance of the individual tester to uncover defects that are not easily covered in the scope of other tests.
