Validation is the process of ensuring that the tested and developed software satisfies the client/user's needs. The business requirement logic or scenarios have to be tested in detail. All the critical functionalities of an application must be tested here.

Validation testing can be done at different levels of software development, such as unit testing, integration testing, system testing, and acceptance testing. Each level has a specific purpose and scope, and involves different types of test cases and techniques.
