The verification process consists of the following steps:

- The requirements are reviewed to check if they are clear, complete, consistent, and correct  .
- The system design is reviewed to check if it meets the requirements and follows the design principles  .
- The source code is reviewed to check if it conforms to the coding standards, follows the design specifications, and has no errors or defects  .
- The verification report is generated to summarize the findings, issues, and recommendations of the verification process .

The verification process is a static testing technique that aims to ensure that the software is well-engineered and error-free  . It is performed before the validation process, which is a dynamic testing technique that aims to ensure that the software meets the user's expectations and needs  .
