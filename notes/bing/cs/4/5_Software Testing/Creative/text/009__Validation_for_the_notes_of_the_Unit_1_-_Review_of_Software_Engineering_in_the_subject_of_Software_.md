### Validation for the notes of the Unit 1 - Review of Software Engineering in the subject of Software Testing

- Validation is the process of checking whether the software product meets the user requirements and expectations.
- Validation is also known as verification or quality assurance, as it ensures that the software is fit for its intended purpose and satisfies the customer needs.
- Validation can be performed at different stages of the software development life cycle, such as requirements analysis, design, coding, testing, and deployment.
- Validation can be done by various methods, such as reviews, inspections, walkthroughs, audits, testing, and evaluation.
- Validation can be classified into two types: static validation and dynamic validation.
  - Static validation is the process of checking the software product without executing it, such as by examining the documents, code, or models.
  - Dynamic validation is the process of checking the software product by executing it, such as by testing the functionality, performance, usability, or security.
- Validation can be measured by various criteria, such as correctness, completeness, consistency, reliability, usability, efficiency, maintainability, and portability.
- Validation can be improved by applying various techniques, such as standards, guidelines, checklists, metrics, tools, and best practices.
