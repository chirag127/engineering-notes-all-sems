### SDLC for the notes of the Unit 1 - Review of Software Engineering in the subject of Software Testing

- SDLC stands for Software Development Life Cycle. It is a process used by the software industry to design, develop and test high-quality software .
- The SDLC aims to produce a software that meets or exceeds customer expectations, reaches completion within time and cost estimates, and follows the best practices and standards of software engineering .
- The SDLC consists of several phases, each with its own activities, deliverables, and outcomes. The phases may vary depending on the type and size of the software project, but they typically include  :
  - Requirement analysis: This phase involves gathering and analyzing the needs and expectations of the stakeholders, such as the customers, users, developers, and managers. The output of this phase is a document that specifies the functional and non-functional requirements of the software .
  - Planning: This phase involves defining the scope, objectives, schedule, budget, resources, and risks of the software project. The output of this phase is a project plan that guides the development team throughout the SDLC .
  - Software design: This phase involves creating the architectural and detailed design of the software, such as the modules, components, interfaces, data structures, algorithms, and protocols. The output of this phase is a design document that describes how the software will be implemented .
  - Software development: This phase involves writing and testing the source code of the software, following the design document and the coding standards. The output of this phase is a software product that is ready for testing .
  - Software testing: This phase involves verifying and validating the quality and functionality of the software, using various types of testing techniques, such as unit testing, integration testing, system testing, and acceptance testing. The output of this phase is a software product that meets the requirements and specifications  .
  - Software deployment: This phase involves delivering and installing the software product to the target environment, such as the customer's site, the cloud, or the app store. The output of this phase is a software product that is available for use .
  - Software maintenance: This phase involves providing support and updates to the software product, such as fixing bugs, adding features, enhancing performance, and improving security. The output of this phase is a software product that is reliable and adaptable .
- The SDLC is not a fixed or rigid process, but rather a flexible and iterative one. There are different models or approaches of the SDLC, such as waterfall, agile, spiral, and prototyping, that suit different types of software projects and development teams  .
- The SDLC is important for software testing, as it helps to ensure that the software product is tested at every stage of development, from the requirements to the deployment. Testing is one of the most critical processes of the SDLC, as it helps to identify and eliminate defects, improve quality, reduce risks, and increase customer satisfaction  .
