### Verification for the notes of the Unit 1 - Review of Software Engineering in the subject of Software Testing

- Verification is the process of checking whether the software meets the specified requirements and design specifications.
- Verification can be done at different stages of the software development life cycle, such as planning, analysis, design, coding, testing, and maintenance.
- Verification can be performed by various methods, such as reviews, inspections, walkthroughs, audits, and formal methods.
- Verification aims to ensure that the software is built correctly, i.e., it conforms to the requirements and design specifications.
- Verification helps to detect and prevent errors and defects early in the software development process, which reduces the cost and effort of fixing them later.
- Verification also helps to improve the quality, reliability, and performance of the software.
