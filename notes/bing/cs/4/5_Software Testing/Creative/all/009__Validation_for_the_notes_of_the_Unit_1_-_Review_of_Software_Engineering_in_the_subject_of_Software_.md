### Validation for the notes of the Unit 1 - Review of Software Engineering in the subject of Software Testing

- Validation is the process of checking whether the software product meets the user's requirements and expectations.
- Validation answers the question: "Are we building the right product?"
- Validation is usually performed at the end of the software development life cycle (SDLC), after the software product has been built and tested.
- Validation involves activities such as user acceptance testing, beta testing, alpha testing, and reviews.
- Validation ensures that the software product is fit for its intended use and satisfies the user's needs.

Some mnemonics and learning tricks for validation are:

- **V**alidation **V**erifies the **V**alue of the software product to the user.
- **V**alidation is done by the **V**oice of the customer (VOC).
- **V**alidation is the **V**alid **V**ote of the user for the software product.
- **V**alidation is the **V**ery last step of the SDLC.
