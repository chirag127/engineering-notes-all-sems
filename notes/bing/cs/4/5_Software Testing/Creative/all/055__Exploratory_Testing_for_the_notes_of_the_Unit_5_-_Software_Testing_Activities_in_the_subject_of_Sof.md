### Exploratory Testing for the notes of the Unit 5 - Software Testing Activities in the subject of Software Testing

- Exploratory testing is an approach to software testing that is concisely described as simultaneous learning, test design and test execution.
- Exploratory testing is suited for specific testing scenarios, such as when someone needs to learn about a product or application quickly and provide rapid feedback. It helps review the quality of a product from a user perspective.
- Exploratory testing is widely used in Agile models and is all about discovery, investigation and learning. It emphasizes the tester's autonomy, skill and creativity .
- Exploratory testing is not ad hoc testing, but a structured and disciplined way of testing that requires critical thinking and exploration of the system under test.
- Exploratory testing can be performed at any stage of the software development life cycle, but it is especially useful when there is limited or no documentation, when the requirements are vague or changing, or when there is not enough time to design and execute detailed test cases.
- Exploratory testing can be combined with other testing techniques, such as risk-based testing, session-based testing, scenario-based testing, or pairwise testing, to increase the test coverage and effectiveness.
- Exploratory testing can be done individually or in pairs, where one tester acts as the driver and the other as the observer or navigator. Pair testing can enhance the communication, collaboration and learning among testers.
- Exploratory testing can be supported by various tools, such as test management tools, defect tracking tools, screen capture tools, note-taking tools, or mind mapping tools, to facilitate the documentation, reporting and analysis of the test results.
- Exploratory testing can be evaluated by various metrics, such as test charter coverage, defect density, defect severity, test session duration, test session debriefing, or tester satisfaction, to measure the quality and productivity of the testing process.

Some possible mnemonics and learning tricks for exploratory testing are:

- E.T. phone home: E.T. stands for Exploratory Testing, which is about phoning (testing) the system under test and finding out its home (features and functionalities).
- Explore more: Explore stands for Exploratory testing, which is about exploring more (learning, designing and executing tests) of the system under test.
- Test with zest: Test stands for Exploratory testing, which is about testing with zest (autonomy, skill and creativity) the system under test.
