### Verification Methods for the notes of the Unit 1 - Review of Software Engineering in the subject of Software Testing

Verification is the process of checking that a software achieves its goal without any bugs. It is the process to ensure whether the product that is developed is right or not. It verifies whether the developed product fulfills the requirements that we have. Verification is Static Testing .

Some of the methods of verification are:

- **Peer Reviews** – This is the easiest and informal way of reviewing the documents or the programs/software for the purpose of finding out the faults during the verification process. In this method, we give the document or software programs to others and ask them to review those and provide feedback. Peer reviews can be done by colleagues, friends, or experts in the same field. Peer reviews can help to improve the quality of the software, detect errors, and share knowledge.
- **Inspections** – This is a formal and structured way of reviewing the documents or the programs/software for the purpose of finding out the faults during the verification process. In this method, a team of reviewers, led by a moderator, examines the document or software programs in detail and identifies the defects, errors, and deviations from the standards. Inspections can be done by internal or external auditors, quality assurance teams, or independent reviewers. Inspections can help to ensure the compliance of the software, reduce the rework, and prevent the defects from reaching the customers .
- **Walk-throughs** – This is a semi-formal and interactive way of reviewing the documents or the programs/software for the purpose of finding out the faults during the verification process. In this method, the author of the document or software programs presents the work to a group of reviewers and explains the logic, design, and functionality of the software. The reviewers ask questions, provide feedback, and suggest improvements. Walk-throughs can be done by peers, managers, or customers. Walk-throughs can help to clarify the requirements, design, and implementation of the software, improve the communication, and resolve the issues .

A mnemonic to remember the verification methods is **PIW** (Peer reviews, Inspections, Walk-throughs). A learning trick to remember the mnemonic is to think of **PIW** as a sound that a laser gun makes. Verification is like shooting the software with a laser gun to find the bugs.
