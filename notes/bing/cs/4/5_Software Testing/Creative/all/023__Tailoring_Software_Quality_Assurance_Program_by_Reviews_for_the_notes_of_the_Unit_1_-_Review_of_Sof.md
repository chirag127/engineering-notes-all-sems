### Tailoring Software Quality Assurance Program by Reviews

- Tailoring Software Quality Assurance Program by Reviews is the process of adapting a generic software quality assurance (SQA) program to the specific needs and characteristics of a software project or organization.
- The purpose of tailoring is to ensure that the SQA program is relevant, effective, efficient, and feasible for the software under development or maintenance.
- Tailoring involves selecting, modifying, adding, or deleting SQA activities, standards, methods, tools, and metrics based on the software context, such as the size, complexity, criticality, domain, technology, methodology, and stakeholders of the software.
- Tailoring can be done at different levels of granularity, such as the organization level, the project level, the phase level, or the task level.
- Tailoring can be done at different stages of the software life cycle, such as the planning stage, the execution stage, or the evaluation stage.
- Tailoring can be done by different roles, such as the SQA manager, the SQA team, the project manager, the project team, or the customer.
- Tailoring can be done using different approaches, such as top-down, bottom-up, or hybrid.
- Tailoring can be done using different techniques, such as checklists, templates, guidelines, or tools.
- Tailoring can be done using different sources of information, such as standards, best practices, lessons learned, feedback, or benchmarks.

Some of the benefits of tailoring SQA program by reviews are:

- It improves the alignment of the SQA program with the software goals, requirements, and constraints.
- It increases the acceptance and commitment of the software stakeholders to the SQA program.
- It enhances the efficiency and effectiveness of the SQA program by eliminating unnecessary or redundant activities and focusing on the most important or risky ones.
- It reduces the cost and effort of the SQA program by optimizing the use of resources and minimizing waste and rework.
- It improves the quality and reliability of the software by preventing, detecting, and correcting defects and non-conformities.

Some of the challenges of tailoring SQA program by reviews are:

- It requires a clear understanding of the software context and the SQA program objectives and scope.
- It requires a balance between flexibility and consistency, as well as between customization and standardization.
- It requires a trade-off between the benefits and the risks of tailoring, as well as between the short-term and the long-term impacts of tailoring.
- It requires a systematic and documented process for tailoring, as well as a regular and objective evaluation of the tailoring results and outcomes.
- It requires a communication and coordination among the software stakeholders involved in or affected by the tailoring process.

A possible mnemonic to remember the steps of tailoring SQA program by reviews is:

- **T**arget: Identify the software context and the SQA program objectives and scope.
- **A**nalyze: Assess the suitability and applicability of the SQA program elements for the software context.
- **I**dentify: Select, modify, add, or delete the SQA program elements based on the analysis results and the software context.
- **L**og: Document the tailoring decisions and rationales, as well as the tailoring process and criteria.
- **O**perate: Implement and execute the tailored SQA program elements for the software project or organization.
- **R**eview: Evaluate and measure the effectiveness and efficiency of the tailored SQA program elements and the tailoring process.
- **I**mprove: Identify and implement the improvement actions for the tailored SQA program elements and the tailoring process based on the review results and feedback.
