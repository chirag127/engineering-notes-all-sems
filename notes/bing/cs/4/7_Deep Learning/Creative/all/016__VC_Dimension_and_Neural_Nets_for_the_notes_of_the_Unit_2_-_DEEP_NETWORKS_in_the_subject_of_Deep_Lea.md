### VC Dimension and Neural Nets for the notes of the Unit 2 - DEEP NETWORKS in the subject of Deep Learning

- VC dimension is a measure of the **model capacity** or **complexity** of a learning algorithm. It is defined as the maximum number of points that can be **shattered** (classified without error) by the algorithm in any possible way.
- VC dimension can be used to **bound the generalization error** of a learning algorithm, which is the difference between the training error and the test error. A low VC dimension means that the algorithm is **less likely to overfit** the training data, but it may also be **less expressive** and **underfit** the data. A high VC dimension means that the algorithm is **more expressive** and can fit complex patterns, but it may also be **more prone to overfitting** and have a large generalization error.
- The VC dimension of a neural network depends on the **architecture** and the **activation function** of the network. The architecture is described by a directed acyclic graph (DAG) where the nodes are the neurons and the edges are the connections. The activation function is the function that maps the input of a neuron to its output.
- For a neural network using **linear threshold** activation functions (such as sign or step functions), the VC dimension is at most **O(w * log w)**, where w is the total number of weights in the network. This means that the VC dimension grows **linearly** with the number of weights, but **slowly**.
- For a neural network using **sigmoid** activation functions (such as logistic or tanh functions), the VC dimension is at least **O(w)** and at most **O(E^2 * V^2)**, where E and V are the number of edges and nodes in the network, respectively. This means that the VC dimension grows **at least linearly** with the number of weights, but **potentially much faster**.
- A possible mnemonic to remember the bounds on the VC dimension of a neural network is: **linear threshold is low, sigmoid is high**. Alternatively, one can think of the shape of the activation functions: **linear threshold is flat, sigmoid is curved**. The more curved the activation function, the higher the VC dimension.
