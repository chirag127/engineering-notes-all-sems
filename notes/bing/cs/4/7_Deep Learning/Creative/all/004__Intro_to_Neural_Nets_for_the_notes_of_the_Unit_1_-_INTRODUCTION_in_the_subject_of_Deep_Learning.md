### Intro to Neural Nets for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of Deep Learning

- Neural networks are computational models that are inspired by the structure and function of biological neurons in the brain.
- Neural networks consist of artificial neurons, also called units or nodes, that are connected by weighted links, also called edges or synapses.
- Neural networks can learn from data by adjusting the weights of the links based on a learning algorithm, such as gradient descent or backpropagation.
- Neural networks can perform various tasks, such as classification, regression, clustering, dimensionality reduction, generative modeling, etc.
- Neural networks can be classified into different types based on their architecture, such as feedforward, recurrent, convolutional, etc.

Some mnemonics and learning tricks for the intro to neural nets are:

- A neural network is like a **net**work of **neurons** that can **learn** from data.
- A neuron is a **unit** that can **activate** based on its **input** and **weight**.
- A weight is a **link** that can **adjust** based on the **error** and **learning rate**.
- A learning algorithm is a **method** that can **optimize** the **weights** and **performance** of the network.
- A network architecture is a **design** that can **affect** the **complexity** and **capability** of the network.
