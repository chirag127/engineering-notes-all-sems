## Unit 5 - CASE STUDY AND APPLICATIONS

- A case study is a research method that involves an in-depth, detailed examination of a specific subject, phenomenon, or situation, using various sources of evidence.
- Case studies can be used to explore, describe, explain, or evaluate a topic, depending on the research question and purpose.
- Case studies can be applied to various fields and disciplines, such as business, management, health, social care, education, law, and more.
- Case studies can have different designs, such as single-case or multiple-case, holistic or embedded, descriptive or explanatory, and more.
- Case studies can use different methods of data collection, such as interviews, observations, documents, artifacts, surveys, and more.
- Case studies can use different methods of data analysis, such as thematic analysis, content analysis, pattern matching, cross-case synthesis, and more.
- Case studies can have different types of validity and reliability, such as construct validity, internal validity, external validity, and reliability.
- Case studies can have different strengths and limitations, such as providing rich and contextual insights, allowing for multiple perspectives, being flexible and adaptable, but also being time-consuming, resource-intensive, subjective, and difficult to generalize.

Some examples of case studies and applications are:

- A case study of Amazon.com, using the competitive forces and value chain models to analyze its business strategy, strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats.
- A case study of Athlete's Warehouse, using the SWOT analysis to evaluate its potential for success, growth, and expansion.
- A case study of health and social care, using the PIES framework to assess the physical, intellectual, emotional, and social needs of different service users.
- A case study of a multistate bank robbery, using the RICO statute to identify the elements of the crime, the roles of the participants, and the possible charges and penalties.
- A case study of physician documentation, using the ICD-10-CM and CPT codes to assign the appropriate diagnosis and procedure codes for different medical scenarios.

Some possible mnemonics and learning tricks for Unit 5 - CASE STUDY AND APPLICATIONS are:

- CASE: Context, Analysis, Synthesis, Evaluation. A four-step process for conducting and writing a case study.
- SWOT: Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, Threats. A four-quadrant framework for analyzing the internal and external factors affecting a business or organization.
- PIES: Physical, Intellectual, Emotional, Social. A four-dimensional framework for assessing the holistic needs of individuals in health and social care.
- RICO: Racketeer Influenced and Corrupt Organizations. A federal law that prohibits engaging in a pattern of racketeering activity as part of an enterprise that affects interstate or foreign commerce.
- ICD: International Classification of Diseases. A system of codes that classify diseases, disorders, injuries, and other health conditions.
- CPT: Current Procedural Terminology. A system of codes that describe medical, surgical, and diagnostic services performed by health care providers.
