### ResNet

- ResNet stands for **Residual Network**, a type of artificial neural network that uses **residual connections** or **skip connections** to ease the training of deep networks  .
- Residual connections allow the network to learn the **difference** or **residual** between the input and output of a layer, instead of learning the output directly . This helps to avoid the problem of **vanishing gradients** that occurs when the network becomes too deep and the gradients become too small to update the weights effectively .
- ResNet consists of several **residual blocks**, each containing two or more convolutional layers and a shortcut connection that bypasses some layers and adds the input to the output  . The shortcut connection can be either **identity** (when the input and output have the same dimensions) or **projection** (when the input and output have different dimensions and a linear transformation is applied to match them) .
- ResNet can be used as a **feature extractor** for many deep learning tasks like image classification, object detection, and image segmentation. ResNet can also be **pre-trained** on large datasets and **fine-tuned** on specific tasks to achieve better results.
- ResNet was introduced by **He et al.** in 2015 in their paper "Deep Residual Learning for Image Recognition" and won the **ImageNet Large Scale Visual Recognition Challenge (ILSVRC)** that year with a top-5 error rate of 3.57%, which was much lower than the previous state-of-the-art. ResNet has since become one of the most influential and widely used architectures in deep learning .
