### Intro to Neural Nets

- Neural networks are computational models that are inspired by the structure and function of biological neurons and synapses.
- Neural networks consist of layers of interconnected nodes, where each node performs some mathematical operation on its inputs and passes the output to the next layer.
- Neural networks can learn from data by adjusting the weights of the connections between the nodes, using optimization algorithms such as gradient descent.
- Neural networks can perform various tasks, such as classification, regression, clustering, dimensionality reduction, generative modeling, etc.
- Neural networks can be classified into different types, such as feedforward, recurrent, convolutional, attention, transformer, etc., depending on the architecture and functionality of the layers.
- Neural networks can be trained using different methods, such as supervised, unsupervised, semi-supervised, reinforcement, self-supervised, etc., depending on the availability and nature of the data and the desired outcome.
