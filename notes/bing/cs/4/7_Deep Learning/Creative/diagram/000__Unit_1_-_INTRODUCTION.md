## Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION

Unit 1 is an introduction to the topic of the course. It may cover the following aspects:

- The definition and scope of the topic
- The main concepts and terms related to the topic
- The historical background and development of the topic
- The current trends and issues in the topic
- The objectives and learning outcomes of the course
- The methods and tools for studying the topic
