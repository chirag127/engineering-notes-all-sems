### Entanglement as a physical resource for the notes of the Unit 5 - Quantum Error Correction in the subject of Quantum Computing

- Quantum entanglement is a phenomenon in which two or more quantum systems, such as qubits, share a quantum state and exhibit non-classical correlations that are not possible in classical systems .
- Quantum entanglement is a physical resource, like energy, that can be used to perform computational and cryptographic tasks that are impossible or inefficient for classical systems .
- Some of the tasks that can be enabled by quantum entanglement are:
  - Quantum cryptography: using entangled qubits to generate and distribute secure keys for encryption and decryption .
  - Superdense coding: using entangled qubits to transmit two classical bits of information with one qubit .
  - Quantum teleportation: using entangled qubits to transfer the quantum state of one qubit to another qubit at a distant location .
  - Quantum error correction: using entangled qubits to protect quantum information from decoherence and noise .
- Quantum entanglement can be measured, transformed, and purified using various methods and protocols .
  - Measurement: quantifying the amount or type of entanglement in a quantum state using measures such as entanglement entropy, concurrence, negativity, etc .
  - Transformation: manipulating the entanglement in a quantum state using operations such as local unitary transformations, local measurements, entanglement swapping, etc .
  - Purification: increasing the entanglement in a quantum state using techniques such as distillation, dilution, filtering, etc .
- Quantum entanglement is a valuable and scarce resource that needs to be carefully managed and optimized for quantum computing applications .
  - Valuable: entanglement can enhance the speed, security, and accuracy of quantum computing tasks .
  - Scarce: entanglement is difficult to create, maintain, and distribute in realistic quantum systems due to decoherence, noise, and loss .
  - Managed: entanglement can be controlled and allocated using protocols such as entanglement distribution, entanglement sharing, entanglement concentration, etc .
  - Optimized: entanglement can be maximized and utilized using methods such as entanglement generation, entanglement extraction, entanglement consumption, etc .
