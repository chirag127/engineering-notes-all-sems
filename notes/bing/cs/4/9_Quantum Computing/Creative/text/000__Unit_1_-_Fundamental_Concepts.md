## Unit 1 - Fundamental Concepts

In this unit, you will learn about some of the basic concepts and terminology related to computer science and programming. You will also learn about the history and evolution of computers, and the different types of computers and their applications.

Some of the topics covered in this unit are:

- What is computer science and what are its main branches and subfields?
- What is an algorithm and how can it be expressed using pseudocode and flowcharts?
- What is a programming language and what are the main categories and paradigms of programming languages?
- What are the basic components and functions of a computer system, such as the CPU, memory, input/output devices, and storage devices?
- What are the differences between hardware and software, and between system software and application software?
- What are the advantages and disadvantages of different types of computers, such as desktops, laptops, tablets, smartphones, and supercomputers?
- What are some of the current trends and challenges in computer science, such as artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, big data, cloud computing, and quantum computing?

By the end of this unit, you should be able to:

- Define and explain the key terms and concepts related to computer science and programming.
- Identify and describe the main branches and subfields of computer science and their applications.
- Design and analyze simple algorithms using pseudocode and flowcharts.
- Compare and contrast different programming languages and paradigms, and choose the appropriate one for a given problem or task.
- Recognize and describe the basic components and functions of a computer system, and how they work together to execute programs.
- Distinguish between hardware and software, and between system software and application software, and give examples of each.
- Evaluate the advantages and disadvantages of different types of computers and their suitability for different purposes and users.
- Discuss some of the current trends and challenges in computer science and their implications for society and the future.
