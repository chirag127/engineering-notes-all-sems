### Harmonic Oscillator Quantum Computer

- A harmonic oscillator quantum computer is a model of quantum computation that uses a finite subset of the energy eigenstates of a simple harmonic oscillator to represent quantum bits.
- A simple harmonic oscillator is a system that has a periodic motion with a restoring force proportional to the displacement from the equilibrium position.
- The quantum harmonic oscillator is the quantum-mechanical analog of the classical harmonic oscillator, and it is one of the most important model systems in quantum mechanics.
