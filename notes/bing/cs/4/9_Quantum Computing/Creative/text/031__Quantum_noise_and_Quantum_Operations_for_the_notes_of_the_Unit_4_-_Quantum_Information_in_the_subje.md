### Quantum noise and quantum operations

- Quantum noise is the term used to describe the unwanted effects of the environment and imperfections on the quantum state of a system, such as a quantum computer  .
- Quantum noise can cause errors in the computation, degrade the coherence and entanglement of qubits, and limit the scalability and performance of quantum devices  .
- Quantum operations are the mathematical descriptions of how quantum states are transformed by quantum gates, measurements, and noise .
- Quantum operations are represented by linear operators that act on the Hilbert space of the quantum system, and must satisfy certain properties, such as being completely positive and trace-preserving.
- Quantum operations can be characterized by different representations, such as matrices, Kraus operators, Choi-Jamiolkowski states, and process tomography.
- Quantum operations can be used to model and analyze the effects of noise on quantum circuits, and to design methods for noise mitigation, such as error correction and noise sensing  .
