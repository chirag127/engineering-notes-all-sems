### Guiding Principles for the notes of the Unit 3 - Quantum Computers in the subject of Quantum Computing

- The notes should provide an overview of the basic concepts and components of quantum computers, such as qubits, quantum gates, quantum circuits, quantum algorithms, and quantum error correction.
- The notes should explain the differences and similarities between classical and quantum computers, such as the advantages and limitations of quantum computation, the models of quantum computation, and the complexity classes of quantum problems.
- The notes should illustrate the applications and challenges of quantum computers in various domains, such as cryptography, optimization, machine learning, simulation, and metrology.
- The notes should include examples and exercises to help the students understand and apply the concepts and techniques of quantum computing.
- The notes should follow a logical and coherent structure, with clear definitions, explanations, diagrams, and references.
- The notes should use consistent and accurate notation, terminology, and conventions for quantum computing, such as the Dirac notation, the Bloch sphere, the quantum circuit diagram, and the quantum gate symbols.
