Quantum noise is the unwanted disturbance or error that affects the quantum state of a qubit or a quantum system. Quantum noise can be caused by imperfect control signals, interference from the environment, and unwanted interactions between qubits  . Quantum noise can lead to quantum decoherence, which is the loss of quantum coherence or the ability of a quantum system to exhibit quantum interference.

Quantum operations are the mathematical transformations that describe how a quantum system evolves under the influence of external factors, such as quantum gates, measurements, or noise . Quantum operations are also called quantum channels or quantum processes. Quantum operations can be represented by matrices, such as unitary matrices, Kraus operators, or superoperators .
