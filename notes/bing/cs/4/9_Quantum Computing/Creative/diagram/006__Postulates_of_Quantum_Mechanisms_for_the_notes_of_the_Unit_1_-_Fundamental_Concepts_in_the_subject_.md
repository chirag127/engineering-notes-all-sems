The postulates of quantum mechanics are the basic assumptions that underlie the theory of quantum mechanics. They describe how the state of a quantum system, the physical quantities that can be measured, and the outcomes of measurements are related to the mathematical objects of the theory, such as vectors, operators, and matrices.
