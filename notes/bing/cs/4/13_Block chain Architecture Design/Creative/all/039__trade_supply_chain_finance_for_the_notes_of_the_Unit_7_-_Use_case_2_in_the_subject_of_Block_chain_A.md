### Trade/supply chain finance for the notes of the Unit 7 - Use case 2 in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

- Trade/supply chain finance is the process of providing financing to businesses involved in the production, distribution and consumption of goods and services across the world.
- Trade/supply chain finance can help businesses to improve their cash flow, reduce their costs, mitigate their risks and access new markets.
- Blockchain is a distributed ledger technology that can record and verify transactions in a secure, transparent and immutable way.
- Blockchain can transform trade/supply chain finance by enabling the following benefits:

  - **Streamlining and automating processes**: Blockchain can eliminate the need for intermediaries, paper documents and manual checks in trade/supply chain finance transactions. This can reduce errors, delays, fraud and costs, and increase efficiency, speed and accuracy.
  - **Facilitating trust and continuity**: Blockchain can provide a single source of truth for all parties involved in trade/supply chain finance transactions. This can enhance the visibility, traceability and accountability of the goods and services, and the funds and data exchanged. Blockchain can also enable smart contracts, which are self-executing agreements that can enforce the terms and conditions of the transactions.
  - **Accessing new customer segments and products**: Blockchain can enable trade/supply chain finance providers, such as banks and other trade finance organizations, to reach new customers, especially small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs), and offer them new products, such as invoice financing, inventory financing and pre-shipment financing. Blockchain can also enable customers, such as importers and exporters, to access new markets and suppliers, and diversify their sources of financing.

- Some examples of blockchain platforms that are used for trade/supply chain finance are:

  - **we.trade**: A blockchain platform that connects buyers, sellers and banks in a network of trust for global trade. It offers standardized rules and simplified trading options that reduce risk and increase trading opportunities for banks and SMEs.
  - **Contour**: A blockchain platform that digitizes and streamlines the letter of credit process, which is a common form of trade finance that guarantees the payment of a buyer to a seller. It enables banks and corporates to exchange data and documents in real time, and reduces the time and cost of the process.
  - **Marco Polo**: A blockchain platform that provides an open and interoperable network for trade and working capital finance. It enables banks and corporates to exchange data and assets securely and efficiently, and offers various trade finance solutions, such as receivables discounting, payables finance and payment commitment.
