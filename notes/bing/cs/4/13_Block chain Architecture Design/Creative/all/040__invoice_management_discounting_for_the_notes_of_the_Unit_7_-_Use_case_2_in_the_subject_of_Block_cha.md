### Invoice management discounting for the notes of the Unit 7 - Use case 2 in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

- Invoice management discounting is a funding option available to small businesses to tide over cashflow vagaries.
- Under the invoice discounting arrangement, the supplier (business) uses the account receivable as collateral to access instant funds to improve the cash flow position.
- The supplier sells the invoice to a bank or a financial institution at a discounted rate and receives the funds immediately, instead of waiting for the customer to pay later.
- The bank or the financial institution collects the full amount from the customer when the invoice is due and earns the difference as profit.
- Invoice discounting is a market with a double-digit potential growth rate over the next years in Europe and worldwide.
- Blockchain technology can enhance the invoice discounting process by providing trust, security, transparency, and efficiency.
- Blockchain is a distributed ledger that records transactions in a peer-to-peer network, where each node has a copy of the ledger and can validate the transactions using consensus algorithms.
- Blockchain can enable businesses to upload their financial data on the chain and only share it with the entity they wish to show the data.
- This can reduce the risk of fraud, duplication, and manipulation of invoices, as well as the need for intermediaries and manual verification  .
- Blockchain can also enable smart contracts, which are self-executing agreements that can automate the invoice discounting process and trigger payments based on predefined conditions .
- Blockchain can also provide real-time visibility and traceability of the invoice status and the cash flow, as well as reduce the operational costs and the processing time  .

#### Mnemonics and learning tricks

- To remember the definition of invoice discounting, use the acronym **FACED**:

  - **F**unds: The supplier receives funds immediately from the bank or the financial institution.
  - **A**ccount: The supplier uses the account receivable as collateral to access the funds.
  - **C**ustomer: The bank or the financial institution collects the full amount from the customer when the invoice is due.
  - **E**arns: The bank or the financial institution earns the difference between the full amount and the discounted rate as profit.
  - **D**iscounted: The supplier sells the invoice to the bank or the financial institution at a discounted rate.

- To remember the benefits of blockchain for invoice discounting, use the acronym **STET**:

  - **S**ecurity: Blockchain reduces the risk of fraud, duplication, and manipulation of invoices, as well as the need for intermediaries and manual verification.
  - **T**ransparency: Blockchain provides real-time visibility and traceability of the invoice status and the cash flow.
  - **E**fficiency: Blockchain enables smart contracts, which can automate the invoice discounting process and trigger payments based on predefined conditions.
  - **T**ime: Blockchain reduces the operational costs and the processing time of invoice discounting.
