### Digital Money to Distributed Ledgers for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Blockchain in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

- Digital money is a form of electronic money that can be used to store value, measure transactions, and exchange goods and services online.
- Digital money can be issued by central banks, private entities, or decentralized networks, depending on the design and governance of the system.
- Distributed ledgers are databases that are shared, replicated, and synchronized among multiple nodes in a network, without the need for a central authority or intermediary.
- Distributed ledgers can record transactions, such as the transfer of assets or data, among the participants in the network, using cryptographic methods to ensure data integrity and security.
- Blockchain is a type of distributed ledger that uses a chain of blocks to store transactions in a chronological and immutable way. Each block contains a hash of the previous block, a timestamp, and a set of transactions that are validated by a consensus mechanism among the nodes.
- Blockchain was created to provide digital currency, such as Bitcoin, that operates in a decentralized and peer-to-peer manner, without the involvement of banks or intermediaries.
- Blockchain can also be used for other purposes, such as record management, process automation, smart contracts, digital identity, supply chain, etc.
- Blockchain and distributed ledger technology (DLT) have the potential to transform the financial sector, as well as other sectors, by making them more efficient, resilient, and reliable.

Some mnemonics and learning tricks for the topic are:

- To remember the three functions of money, use the acronym SUM: Store of value, Unit of account, Medium of exchange.
- To remember the difference between blockchain and distributed ledger, use the analogy of a square and a rectangle: A blockchain is a type of distributed ledger, but not all distributed ledgers are blockchains. A square is a type of rectangle, but not all rectangles are squares.
- To remember the components of a block in a blockchain, use the word HAT: Hash, Timestamp, Transactions.
