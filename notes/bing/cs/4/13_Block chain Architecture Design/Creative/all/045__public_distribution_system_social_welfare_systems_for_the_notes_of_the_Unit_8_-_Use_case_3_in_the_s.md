### Public distribution system social welfare systems for the notes of the Unit 8 - Use case 3 in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

- Public distribution system (PDS) is a system where the government creates a supply chain to reach towards the public such as ration card is used by public to gain the glossary form government.
- Social welfare systems are the programs that provide assistance to the poor, elderly, disabled, and other vulnerable groups in the society.
- Blockchain is a distributed ledger technology that records transactions in a secure, transparent, and immutable way.
- Blockchain can be applied to PDS and social welfare systems to improve their efficiency, accountability, and transparency.
- Some of the benefits of using blockchain for PDS and social welfare systems are:

  - It can reduce corruption, fraud, and leakage of the supplies by creating a traceable and verifiable record of the transactions .
  - It can enhance the quality and quantity of the supplies by ensuring that they are not tampered with or expired .
  - It can improve the delivery and distribution of the supplies by eliminating intermediaries and reducing costs .
  - It can empower the beneficiaries by giving them more control and choice over their entitlements and subsidies .
  - It can increase the trust and confidence of the stakeholders by providing them with real-time and accurate information.

- Some of the challenges of using blockchain for PDS and social welfare systems are:

  - It requires a high level of technical expertise and infrastructure to implement and maintain.
  - It faces legal and regulatory uncertainties and barriers in terms of data privacy, security, and governance .
  - It depends on the cooperation and coordination of multiple actors and agencies across different levels and sectors .
  - It may encounter social and cultural resistance and skepticism from the users and beneficiaries.
