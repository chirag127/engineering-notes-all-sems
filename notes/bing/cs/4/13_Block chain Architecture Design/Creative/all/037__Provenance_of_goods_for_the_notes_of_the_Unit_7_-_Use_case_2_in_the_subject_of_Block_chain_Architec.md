### Provenance of goods for the notes of the Unit 7 - Use case 2 in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

- Provenance of goods refers to the **chain of custody** of a product from the point of origin to the point of consumption .
- Blockchain technology can enable **traceability** and **transparency** of the provenance of goods by providing **full visibility** of goods-related data and transactions .
- Blockchain can also help **assure** the authenticity and origin of goods, **prevent** fraud and counterfeiting, and **improve** the efficiency and quality of the supply chain .
- Some of the use cases of blockchain for provenance of goods are:
  - **Art**: Blockchain can help verify the ownership, authenticity, and history of both digital and physical artworks, as well as protect the intellectual property rights of artists.
  - **Luxury goods**: Blockchain can help track the origin, quality, and value of high-end products such as jewelry, precious metals and stones, wine collections, and limited edition apparel and accessories.
  - **Land ownership**: Blockchain can help record and verify the ownership, transfer, and history of land titles, as well as reduce the costs and risks of fraud, corruption, and disputes.
  - **Supply chain information**: Blockchain can help monitor and share the information about the location, condition, and status of goods throughout the supply chain, as well as enable smart contracts and automated payments.
- A possible mnemonic to remember the benefits of blockchain for provenance of goods is **VAPPI**:
  - **V**isibility: Blockchain provides full visibility of goods-related data and transactions.
  - **A**ssurance: Blockchain helps assure the authenticity and origin of goods.
  - **P**revention: Blockchain helps prevent fraud and counterfeiting of goods.
  - **P**erformance: Blockchain helps improve the efficiency and quality of the supply chain.
  - **I**nnovation: Blockchain enables new business models and opportunities for goods.
