Provenance of goods is the process of tracing the origin, history, and ownership of a product or asset throughout its lifecycle in the supply chain. Blockchain technology can provide a secure and transparent way of recording and verifying the provenance of goods, by creating a distributed ledger of transactions that cannot be tampered with or altered. Blockchain can also facilitate the sharing of information and data among different parties involved in the supply chain, such as manufacturers, suppliers, distributors, retailers, and consumers.

A possible blockchain architecture for provenance of goods can consist of the following components:

- A blockchain network that consists of multiple nodes (computers) that store and validate the transactions on the ledger. The nodes can use a consensus mechanism, such as proof-of-work or proof-of-stake, to agree on the validity and order of the transactions. The nodes can also be organized into different groups or channels, depending on the level of access and privacy required by the participants.
- A smart contract that defines the business logic and rules for the provenance of goods. The smart contract can be written in a programming language, such as Solidity or Go, and deployed on the blockchain network. The smart contract can execute automatically when certain conditions are met, such as the transfer of ownership or the verification of quality. The smart contract can also emit events or notifications to inform the participants of the status and outcome of the transactions.
- A web or mobile application that provides a user interface for the participants to interact with the blockchain network and the smart contract. The application can allow the participants to register, authenticate, and authorize themselves on the network, as well as to create, view, update, and delete the transactions related to the provenance of goods. The application can also display the history and details of the transactions, such as the date, time, location, quantity, and quality of the goods.
- A blockchain explorer that provides a graphical or textual representation of the transactions and blocks on the blockchain network. The blockchain explorer can allow the participants to query and analyze the data on the ledger, as well as to monitor the performance and health of the network. The blockchain explorer can also provide statistics and charts to visualize the trends and patterns of the transactions.
