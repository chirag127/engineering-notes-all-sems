The diagram shows how different participants in the capital market, such as issuers, investors and regulators, can use blockchain technology to tokenize and securitize their assets, automate their business processes using smart contracts, and share a common distributed ledger that ensures transparency and trust. The blockchain also uses a consensus mechanism to validate transactions and maintain the integrity of the ledger. Some of the benefits of using blockchain in capital markets are:

- Reduced costs and risks by eliminating intermediaries and streamlining operations
- Increased liquidity and access by creating new markets and enabling fractional ownership
- Enhanced compliance and security by enforcing rules and regulations and protecting data privacy
- Improved efficiency and innovation by enabling faster settlement and enabling new business models
