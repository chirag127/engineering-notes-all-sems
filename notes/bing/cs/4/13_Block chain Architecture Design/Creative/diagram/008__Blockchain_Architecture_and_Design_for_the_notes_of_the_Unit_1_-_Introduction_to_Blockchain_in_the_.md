Each node is a user or a computer that has a complete copy of the blockchain ledger, which is a sequence of blocks that store transactions or other data. Each block has a unique identifier (hash) that links it to the previous and next block. The blocks are validated by a consensus mechanism, such as proof-of-work or proof-of-stake, that ensures the integrity and security of the blockchain. The blockchain architecture is decentralized, distributed, and immutable, meaning that no single entity can control or alter the data on the ledger. The blockchain architecture enables various applications, such as cryptocurrencies, smart contracts, supply chain management, and digital identity.
