## Unit 4 - Hyperledger Fabric (A)

Hyperledger Fabric is a permissioned blockchain framework that runs on a modular architecture (plug-and-play)  . Hyperledger Fabric leverages container technology to host its smart contract (Chaincode) that contains the application logic  . Hyperledger Fabric allows components, such as consensus and membership services, to be plug-and-play . Hyperledger Fabric is intended as a foundation for developing applications or solutions with a modular architecture .
