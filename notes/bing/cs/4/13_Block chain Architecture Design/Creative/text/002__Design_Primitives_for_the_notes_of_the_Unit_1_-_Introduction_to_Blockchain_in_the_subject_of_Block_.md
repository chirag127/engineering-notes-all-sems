### Design Primitives for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Blockchain in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

- Design primitives are the basic building blocks of a blockchain system that define its properties and functionalities.
- Some of the common design primitives are:

  - Consensus protocol: The mechanism that ensures all nodes in the network agree on the state of the ledger and the validity of transactions.
  - Cryptographic primitives: The mathematical tools that provide security, privacy, and authentication for the data and participants in the network.
  - Data structure: The way the ledger and transactions are organized and stored in the network.
  - Network topology: The structure and configuration of the nodes and connections in the network.
  - Incentive mechanism: The system that motivates and rewards the participants for their contributions and behaviors in the network.
  - Governance model: The rules and processes that determine how the network is managed and updated.

- Different design primitives can have trade-offs and implications for the performance, scalability, security, and usability of the blockchain system.
- The choice of design primitives depends on the requirements and objectives of the specific use case and application of the blockchain system.
