### Capital markets

Capital markets are financial markets where long-term debt or equity-backed securities are bought and sold. Capital markets channel the wealth of savers to those who can put it to long-term productive use, such as companies or governments making long-term investments.

Blockchain is a distributed ledger technology (DLT) that enables peer-to-peer transactions without intermediaries, using cryptography and consensus mechanisms to ensure data integrity and security. Blockchain has the potential to transform various aspects of capital markets, such as issuance, trading, clearing, settlement, custody, and asset servicing.

Some of the use cases of blockchain in capital markets are:

- **Issuance**: Blockchain can enable the issuance of digital tokens that represent securities, such as equity, debt, or derivatives. These tokens can be issued on a public or private blockchain, depending on the regulatory and compliance requirements. Blockchain can also facilitate the automation of corporate actions, such as dividends, voting, or splits, using smart contracts.
- **Sales and trading**: Blockchain can enable the trading of digital securities on decentralized exchanges (DEXs) or peer-to-peer platforms, without the need for intermediaries or brokers. Blockchain can also enable the creation of secondary markets for illiquid or alternative assets, such as real estate, art, or private equity, by tokenizing them and making them more accessible and divisible.
- **Collateral management**: Blockchain can enable the optimization of collateral management, which is the process of managing the assets that are pledged as security for loans or derivatives contracts. Blockchain can provide a single source of truth for the ownership and valuation of collateral, as well as automate the margin calls and collateral transfers using smart contracts.
- **Exchanges**: Blockchain can enable the creation of new types of exchanges, such as decentralized autonomous organizations (DAOs) or prediction markets, that can facilitate the exchange of value or information in a trustless and transparent way. Blockchain can also enable the integration of different types of assets, such as cryptocurrencies, fiat currencies, or commodities, on a single platform, using stablecoins or cross-chain solutions.
- **Clearing and settlement**: Blockchain can enable the near-instantaneous clearing and settlement of transactions, by eliminating the need for intermediaries or clearing houses. Blockchain can also enable the delivery versus payment (DvP) or payment versus payment (PvP) mechanisms, which ensure that the transfer of assets and payments are executed simultaneously and irrevocably.
- **Stablecoins**: Stablecoins are digital tokens that are pegged to a stable asset, such as a fiat currency, a commodity, or a basket of assets. Stablecoins can enable the transfer of value across different blockchain platforms, as well as the integration of blockchain with traditional financial systems. Stablecoins can also enable the creation of new types of financial products and services, such as lending, borrowing, or remittance, on blockchain.
- **Post-trade services and infrastructure**: Blockchain can enable the improvement of post-trade services and infrastructure, such as reconciliation, reporting, compliance, or risk management, by providing a shared and immutable record of transactions and events. Blockchain can also enable the automation of post-trade processes, such as confirmations, validations, or audits, using smart contracts or oracles.
- **Asset servicing**: Blockchain can enable the enhancement of asset servicing, which is the process of providing operational support and value-added services to asset owners and managers, such as fund administration, accounting, or tax reporting. Blockchain can provide a more accurate and timely data on the performance and characteristics of assets, as well as automate the asset servicing functions using smart contracts.
- **Mutual fund administration**: Blockchain can enable the simplification and streamlining of mutual fund administration, which is the process of managing the operations and compliance of mutual funds, such as fund creation, subscription, redemption, valuation, or distribution. Blockchain can provide a more transparent and efficient way of recording and updating the fund data, as well as automate the fund administration tasks using smart contracts.
- **Custody**: Blockchain can enable the innovation of custody services, which are the services that provide the safekeeping and management of assets for institutional or individual investors. Blockchain can enable the creation of self-custody or decentralized custody solutions, which allow the investors to control their own assets using cryptographic keys or smart contracts. Blockchain can also enable the integration of custody services with other blockchain-based platforms or applications, such as DEXs or DeFi.
