## Unit 6 - Use case 1

- Use case 1 is a scenario that describes how a user interacts with a system to achieve a specific goal.
- Use case 1 can be used to model the requirements of a system, to identify the actors and their roles, and to specify the main steps and alternative paths of the interaction.
- Use case 1 can be represented by a diagram that shows the actors, the system, and the relationships between them.
- Use case 1 can also be documented by a text that describes the preconditions, postconditions, main flow, and extensions of the interaction.
- Use case 1 can help to communicate the functionality and behavior of a system to the stakeholders, to validate the requirements, and to guide the design and testing of the system.
- Use case 1 can be derived from the user stories, the business goals, or the functional requirements of the system.
