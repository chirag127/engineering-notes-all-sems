### Trade/Supply Chain Finance

- Trade/Supply Chain Finance is the process of providing financing to facilitate the movement of goods and services along a supply chain, from the suppliers to the buyers.
- Trade/Supply Chain Finance involves various financial instruments and services, such as letters of credit, invoice financing, factoring, reverse factoring, inventory financing, etc.
- Trade/Supply Chain Finance aims to reduce the risks, costs, and inefficiencies associated with cross-border trade transactions, such as currency fluctuations, payment delays, fraud, disputes, etc.
- Trade/Supply Chain Finance can benefit various stakeholders in a supply chain, such as suppliers, buyers, banks, logistics providers, customs authorities, etc.
- Trade/Supply Chain Finance faces several challenges and limitations, such as lack of transparency, trust, standardization, interoperability, and regulatory compliance among different parties and jurisdictions involved in a trade transaction.
