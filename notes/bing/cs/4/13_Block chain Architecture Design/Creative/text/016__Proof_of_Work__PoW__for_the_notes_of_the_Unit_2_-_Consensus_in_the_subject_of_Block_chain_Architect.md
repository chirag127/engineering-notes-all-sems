### Proof of Work (PoW) for the notes of the Unit 2 - Consensus in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

- Proof of work (PoW) is a **decentralized system** used to **verify the accuracy of transactions** on the blockchain network    .
- Proof of work **removes the need for a central authority** like a bank, business, or government agency to monitor and manage transactions and their corresponding accounts .
- Proof of work lets blockchain networks operate by **consensus rules** rather than “trust.” Consensus rules are the agreed-upon protocols that determine how the network validates transactions and updates the shared ledger.
- Proof of work involves **miners**, who are the nodes (computers) that participate in the network and compete to **solve complex mathematical problems** using their computational power  .
- The mathematical problems are based on **hashes**, which are the unique identifiers of each block of transactions on the blockchain. A hash is generated from the data in the block and the hash of the previous block, creating a **chain of hashes** that links all the blocks together.
- The hash of each block must satisfy a certain **difficulty level**, which is determined by the network and adjusted periodically to maintain a steady rate of block creation . The difficulty level is the number of leading zeros that the hash must have.
- A **nonce** is a random number that miners add to the block data and hash to try to find a valid hash that meets the difficulty level. The nonce is the only variable that miners can change in the block data, and they have to try many different nonces until they find one that works.
- The first miner who finds a valid hash for a block is the **winner** and gets to **broadcast** the block to the rest of the network for verification  . The other miners then **validate** the block by checking the hash and the transactions in it.
- The verified block is then **added** to the blockchain and the miner receives a **reward** in the form of newly created cryptocurrency and/or transaction fees  . The reward serves as an incentive for miners to contribute their resources and secure the network.
- The process of finding a valid hash for a block is called **proof of work**, because it proves that the miner has done a lot of work to solve the problem and create the block  . Proof of work makes it **hard to create** blocks but **easy to verify** them, ensuring the **integrity** and **immutability** of the blockchain.
- Proof of work also makes it **costly and impractical** for anyone to **attack** the network or **reverse** the transactions, because they would have to redo the work for all the blocks and outpace the rest of the network, which would require more computational power and energy than the honest miners  .
- Proof of work is the **original** and **most widely used** consensus mechanism in blockchain, and it is the basis of cryptocurrencies like **Bitcoin** and **Ethereum**  . However, proof of work also has some **challenges** and **limitations**, such as:
  - High **energy consumption** and **environmental impact** of the mining process   .
  - Low **scalability** and **throughput** of the network, as the block creation rate and size are limited by the difficulty level and the network latency   .
  - High **barriers to entry** and **centralization risks** for the miners, as the mining competition favors those who have more resources and specialized hardware   .
