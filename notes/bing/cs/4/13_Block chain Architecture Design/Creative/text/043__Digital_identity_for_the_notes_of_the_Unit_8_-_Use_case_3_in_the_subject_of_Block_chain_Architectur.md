### Digital identity for the notes of the Unit 8 - Use case 3 in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

- Digital identity (D-ID) is the aggregated information that is collected by various parties and platforms when a user spends time and conducts activities online .
- D-ID can include personal data, preferences, behavior, reputation, credentials, and transactions.
- D-ID is often fragmented, insecure, and exclusive, as different platforms and parties have different standards and policies for collecting, storing, and sharing user data .
- Blockchain can enable more secure, unified, and interoperable management and storage of D-ID by providing a tamper-proof and decentralized infrastructure  .
- Blockchain can empower users to have greater control over their own D-ID, as they can choose what data to share, with whom, and for how long, and also monetize their own data  .
- Blockchain can also enable more efficient and trustworthy verification of D-ID, as users can issue and receive verifiable credentials that are cryptographically signed and anchored on the blockchain .
- Blockchain can support various use cases for D-ID, such as identity verification, access management, data privacy, digital rights, social media, e-commerce, e-government, and IoT  .
- Blockchain can leverage smart contracts and oracles to enable D-ID systems that are dynamic, context-aware, and responsive to real-world events .
- Blockchain can face some challenges and limitations for D-ID, such as scalability, interoperability, usability, regulation, and standardization  .
