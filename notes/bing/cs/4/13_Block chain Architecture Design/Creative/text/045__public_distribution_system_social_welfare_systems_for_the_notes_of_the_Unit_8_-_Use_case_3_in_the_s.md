### Public Distribution System Social Welfare Systems for the Notes of the Unit 8 - Use Case 3 in the Subject of Block Chain Architecture Design

- Public distribution system (PDS) is a system where the government creates a supply chain to reach towards the public, such as ration cards are used by the public to gain the glossary from the government.
- PDS is one of the key areas in which blockchain plays a major role, as it can provide security, transparency, efficiency, and speed to the transactions and records of the supplies  .
- Blockchain is an emerging technology that is applied in all real-world transactions to provide security and transparency. Blockchain is a distributed ledger that records transactions in a peer-to-peer network, where each node has a copy of the ledger and can validate the transactions using cryptography.
- Blockchain can prevent leakage, corruption, and fraud in the PDS by creating a traceable and immutable record of the supply chain, from the producer to the consumer, and by eliminating the intermediaries and middlemen who can manipulate the system  .
- Blockchain can also improve the efficiency and speed of the PDS by automating the processes and reducing the paperwork and manual errors. Blockchain can also enable smart contracts, which are self-executing agreements that can enforce the rules and conditions of the PDS .
- Blockchain can also enhance the user experience and satisfaction of the PDS by providing real-time information, feedback, and grievance redressal mechanisms. Blockchain can also empower the users by giving them more control and choice over their entitlements and preferences.
- Some of the challenges and barriers for implementing blockchain in the PDS are the lack of awareness, trust, and acceptance of the technology, the high cost and complexity of the infrastructure and maintenance, the regulatory and legal issues, and the social and cultural factors.
- Some of the enablers and facilitators for implementing blockchain in the PDS are the government support and policy, the stakeholder involvement and collaboration, the innovation and experimentation, and the education and training.
- A conceptual framework for implementing blockchain in the PDS can be based on the following steps:
  - Identify the problem and the scope of the PDS
  - Analyze the current state and the gaps of the PDS
  - Design the blockchain solution and the architecture of the PDS
  - Develop and test the blockchain prototype and the PDS
  - Deploy and monitor the blockchain system and the PDS
  - Evaluate and improve the blockchain performance and the PDS
