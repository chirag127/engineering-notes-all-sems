## Unit 8 - Use case 3

- Use case 3 is about designing and implementing a chatbot that can help customers book flight tickets online.
- The chatbot should be able to:
  - Greet the customer and ask for their name and preferred language.
  - Ask the customer for their travel details, such as origin, destination, date, time, number of passengers, and class of service.
  - Search for available flights that match the customer's criteria and present them in a table format with relevant information, such as flight number, airline, departure and arrival time, duration, price, and rating.
  - Allow the customer to select a flight or ask for more options if they are not satisfied with the results.
  - Confirm the customer's selection and ask for their payment details, such as credit card number, expiration date, and security code.
  - Process the payment and issue a confirmation number and a ticket to the customer via email or SMS.
  - Thank the customer for choosing the chatbot service and ask for their feedback or rating.
  - Handle any errors or exceptions that may occur during the conversation, such as invalid input, network failure, payment failure, or cancellation request.
  - End the conversation politely and gracefully.
