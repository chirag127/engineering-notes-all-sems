A Beowulf cluster is a type of high-performance computing system that consists of a group of computers that are connected by a network and work together to run parallel applications. A Beowulf cluster does not have any specific hardware or software requirements, but typically uses free and open source software, such as Linux, and commodity hardware, such as PCs or laptops.
