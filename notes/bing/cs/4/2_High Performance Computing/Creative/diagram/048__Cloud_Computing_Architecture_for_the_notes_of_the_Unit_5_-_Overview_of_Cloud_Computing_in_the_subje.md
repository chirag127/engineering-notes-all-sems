Cloud computing architecture is the design of the components and subcomponents required for cloud computing. It consists of a front-end platform, a back-end platform, a cloud-based delivery, and a network. The front-end platform is the interface that users interact with, such as a web browser, a mobile app, or a desktop application. The back-end platform is the collection of servers, storage, databases, and other resources that provide the cloud services. The cloud-based delivery is the method of delivering the cloud services to the users, such as software as a service (SaaS), platform as a service (PaaS), or infrastructure as a service (IaaS). The network is the communication medium that connects the front-end and the back-end platforms, such as the Internet, an intranet, or an intercloud.
