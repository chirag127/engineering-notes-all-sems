### Application Domains

- A Beowulf cluster is a type of high-performance computing system that consists of a collection of commodity computers connected by a private network and running a parallel programming environment.
- Beowulf clusters can be used for a variety of scientific and engineering applications that require high computational power, such as:
  - Transport phenomena, including fluid dynamics, heat and mass transfer, multi-phase flows, aerodynamics, etc.
  - Multi-million-atom molecular dynamics, and protein folding
  - Cellular automata to model phenomena from epidemiology to options trading
  - Graphics: distributed raytracing and rendering
  - Hard NP problems such as DNA sequence alignment (bioinformatics)
  - Financial market modeling, data mining and stream processing
  - Internet servers for audio and games
  - Product design and optimization, such as toothpaste tubes, soap dispensers, and diapers
  - Medical applications, such as proton-beam therapy for cancer treatment and radiation emission modeling
- Beowulf programs are usually written using languages such as C and FORTRAN. They use message passing to achieve parallel computations.
- Beowulf clusters are formed with the hardware components like switches, Ethernet adapters, PC cables, and Unix-like operating systems.
- Beowulf clusters differ from other clusters of workstations in their behavior of working like a single machine rather than a group of multiple workstations.
