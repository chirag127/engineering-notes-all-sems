### Clients

- A client is a hardware device or software that is used to access a cloud service.
- A client can be a computer system, a tablet, a navigation device, a home automation device, a mobile phone, a smart device, an operating system, or a browser .
- A client can be classified into three types based on the degree of dependency on the cloud service:
  - Thick client: A client that can perform most of the tasks locally without relying on the cloud service. For example, a laptop or a desktop computer.
  - Thin client: A client that can perform only minimal tasks locally and relies on the cloud service for most of the tasks. For example, a Chromebook or a web browser.
  - Zero client: A client that cannot perform any tasks locally and relies entirely on the cloud service. For example, a smart TV or a cloud gaming device.
- A client can also be classified into two types based on the type of cloud service it accesses:
  - Software as a Service (SaaS) client: A client that accesses a cloud-based application or software. For example, Gmail, Netflix, or Google Docs.
  - Platform as a Service (PaaS) or Infrastructure as a Service (IaaS) client: A client that accesses a cloud-based platform or infrastructure for developing, deploying, or managing applications or services. For example, AWS, Google Cloud, or DigitalOcean.
