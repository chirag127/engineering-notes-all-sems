### Desktop Grids for the notes of the Unit 1 - Overview of Grid Computing Technology in the subject of High Performance Computing

- Desktop Grids are a type of Grid Computing that use the idle CPU time of geographically distributed personal computers (PCs) to perform high-throughput computing tasks .
- Desktop Grids can provide a substantial computational resource when aggregated, as the number and power of PCs are increasing rapidly and the cost of computing is decreasing.
- Desktop Grids are based on the volunteer computing model, where PC owners voluntarily donate their computing resources to scientific or humanitarian projects.
- Desktop Grids rely on middleware software, such as BOINC (Berkeley Open Infrastructure for Network Computing), to manage the distribution, execution, and validation of tasks on the PCs .
- Desktop Grids can be used to solve various problems that require large-scale parallel or distributed computing, such as molecular docking, climate modeling, cryptography, image processing, etc .
- Desktop Grids have some advantages and disadvantages compared to other types of Grid Computing, such as:

| Advantages | Disadvantages |
|------------|---------------|
| Low cost and easy to deploy | Unreliable and heterogeneous resources |
| High scalability and availability | Security and privacy issues |
| Social and environmental benefits | Limited communication and coordination |
| Access to a large and diverse user base | Ethical and legal challenges |

- A possible mnemonic to remember the advantages and disadvantages of Desktop Grids is:

**LASH UP** for advantages: **L**ow cost, **A**vailability, **S**calability, **H**umanitarian, **U**ser base, **P**latform diversity.

**ULCE** for disadvantages: **U**nreliable, **L**imited communication, **C**hallenges (ethical and legal), **E**ncryption (security and privacy).

- An example of a Desktop Grid project is the World Community Grid, which supports various research projects on health, energy, and environment by using the donated computing power of over 700,000 PCs.
