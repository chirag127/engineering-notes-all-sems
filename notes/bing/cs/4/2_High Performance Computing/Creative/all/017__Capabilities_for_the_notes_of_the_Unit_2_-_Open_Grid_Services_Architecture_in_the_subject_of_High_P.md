### Capabilities for the notes of the Unit 2 - Open Grid Services Architecture in the subject of High Performance Computing

- Open Grid Services Architecture (OGSA) is a service-oriented architecture for a grid computing environment that defines a set of core capabilities and behaviors that address key concerns in grid systems .
- OGSA uses most of Web service technologies, notably WSDL and SOAP, but it aims to be largely agnostic in relation to the transport-level handling of data upon the grid.
- OGSA provides a common, flexible and open framework for implementing grid systems and applications that can interoperate across different domains and platforms.
- The Open Grid Services Architecture, Version 1.5 described these capabilities :

  - **Infrastructure services**: These are the basic services that support the creation, management and discovery of grid services. They include service lifecycle management, service naming and binding, service data access and notification, service groups and service metadata.
  - **Execution Management services**: These are the services that manage the execution of user tasks on the grid. They include job submission and scheduling, resource brokering and reservation, workflow and orchestration, and fault tolerance and recovery.
  - **Data services**: These are the services that manage the access, transfer, replication, and integration of data on the grid. They include data access and integration, data movement and replication, data virtualization and federation, and data provenance and quality.
  - **Resource Management services**: These are the services that manage the allocation and sharing of resources on the grid. They include resource discovery and monitoring, resource reservation and allocation, resource accounting and auditing, and resource policies and agreements.
  - **Security services**: These are the services that provide security mechanisms and policies for the grid. They include authentication, authorization, encryption, integrity, confidentiality, and auditing.
  - **Self-management services**: These are the services that enable the grid to adapt to changing conditions and requirements. They include self-configuration, self-optimization, self-healing, and self-protection.
  - **Information services**: These are the services that provide information about the grid and its components. They include service registry and directory, service metadata repository, grid monitoring and event service, and grid information modeling and ontology.

- A possible mnemonic to remember the seven capabilities of OGSA is **IDEaRSIS** (pronounced as "ideas is"), which stands for **I**nfrastructure, **D**ata, **E**xecution, **a**nd **R**esource, **S**ecurity, **I**nformation, **S**elf-management services.
