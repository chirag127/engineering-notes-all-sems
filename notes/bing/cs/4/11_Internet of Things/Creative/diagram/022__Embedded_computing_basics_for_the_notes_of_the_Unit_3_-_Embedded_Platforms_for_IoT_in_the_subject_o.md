Embedded computing is the use of specialized hardware and software to perform dedicated functions within a larger system. Embedded systems are often part of the Internet of Things (IoT), which is a network of devices that can communicate, sense, and interact with each other and the environment.

Some examples of embedded systems for IoT are:

- A smart thermostat that adjusts the temperature based on the user's preferences and the weather conditions.
- A wearable device that monitors the user's heart rate, blood pressure, and activity level.
- A smart lock that allows the user to remotely control the access to their home using their smartphone.
- A traffic light that changes the signal based on the traffic flow and the pedestrian crossing.
