An Arduino emulator is a software tool that can simulate the behavior of an Arduino board and its peripherals. It can be used for testing and debugging Arduino sketches without the need for physical hardware. There are many Arduino emulators available online and offline, such as Virtronics Simulator for Arduino, Emulare, Paulware's Arduino Simulator, Wokwi, and others     .
