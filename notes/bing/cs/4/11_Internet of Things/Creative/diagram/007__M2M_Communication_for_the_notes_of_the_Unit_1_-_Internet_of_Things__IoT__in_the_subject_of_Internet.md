M2M Communication is a term that refers to the direct communication between machines, sensors, and hardware over cellular or wired networks. It is a subset of IoT, which is a broader concept that involves the use of IP-based networks, cloud platforms, and middleware to collect, process, and analyze data from IoT-connected devices .

The diagram illustrates the basic architecture of a M2M Communication system in IoT. The IoT devices are the physical objects that have sensors, actuators, and embedded software to collect and transmit data. The M2M gateways are the devices that connect the IoT devices to the M2M servers over cellular or wired networks. The M2M servers are the devices that receive, store, and process the data from the M2M gateways. The M2M servers can also communicate with each other or with other applications and services. The M2M Communication system enables the IoT devices to exchange data and perform actions without human intervention  .
