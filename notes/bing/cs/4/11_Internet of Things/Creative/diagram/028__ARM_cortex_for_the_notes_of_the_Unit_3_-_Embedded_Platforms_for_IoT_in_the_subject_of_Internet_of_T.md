The ARM cortex is a family of processor cores that implement the ARM architecture, which is designed for high performance, low power consumption, and wide applicability. The ARM cortex family consists of three profiles: R, M, and A. The R profile is for hard real-time and safety-critical applications, the M profile is for microcontroller and embedded applications, and the A profile is for application and general-purpose computing.
