Tablets are portable devices that can be used for various IoT applications, such as data visualization, remote control, video conferencing, and smart pill dispensers. Tablets can communicate with IoT devices and cloud services using wireless technologies, such as Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, cellular, or NFC. Tablets can also run IoT software platforms, such as Blynk, that allow users to design and control IoT applications using graphical interfaces.
