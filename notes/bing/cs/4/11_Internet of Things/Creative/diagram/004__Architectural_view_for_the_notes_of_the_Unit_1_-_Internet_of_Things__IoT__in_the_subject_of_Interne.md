The media/device layer is the first layer of the IoT architecture and is responsible for collecting data from different sources. This layer includes sensors and actuators that are placed in the environment to gather information about temperature, humidity, light, sound, and other physical parameters.

The network layer of an IoT architecture is responsible for providing communication and connectivity between devices in the IoT system. It includes protocols and technologies that enable devices to connect and communicate with each other and with the wider internet. Some of the common protocols used in this layer are Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, ZigBee, LoRaWAN, and cellular networks.

The service and application support layer of an IoT architecture is responsible for providing the necessary services and functions to support the applications and users of the IoT system. It includes middleware, cloud computing, data analytics, security, and privacy mechanisms that enable data processing, storage, management, and sharing.

The application layer of an IoT architecture is responsible for providing the end-user interface and functionality of the IoT system. It includes various applications and domains that use the IoT data and services to provide value and benefits to the users. Some of the common applications and domains of IoT are smart home, smart city, smart health, smart agriculture, smart industry, and smart transportation.
