Some of the challenges are:

- Data Handling: IoT devices generate vast amounts of data that need to be stored, processed, and analyzed efficiently and reliably.
- Data Accuracy: IoT devices may not provide 100% accurate data due to various factors such as sensor errors, environmental noise, or network latency.
- Data Security: IoT devices and data are vulnerable to cyberattacks, unauthorized access, or data breaches that may compromise the privacy and integrity of the users and the system.
- Interoperability: IoT devices and platforms may not be compatible with each other due to different standards, protocols, or architectures, which may hinder the communication and integration of the system.
- Energy Efficiency: IoT devices may have limited battery life or power supply, which may affect their performance and functionality.
- Cost Estimation: IoT projects may involve high costs for the infrastructure, hardware, software, maintenance, and operation of the system, which may not be anticipated or budgeted properly.
