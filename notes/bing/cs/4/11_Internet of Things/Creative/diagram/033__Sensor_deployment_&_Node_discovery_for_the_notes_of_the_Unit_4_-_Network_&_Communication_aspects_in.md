Sensor deployment and node discovery are important aspects of IoT, especially for applications that involve wireless sensor networks (WSNs) or wireless body area networks (WBANs). Sensor deployment refers to the process of placing sensor nodes in the environment to monitor physical phenomena, such as temperature, humidity, sound, etc. Node discovery refers to the process of identifying and locating sensor nodes in the network, as well as establishing communication links among them.
