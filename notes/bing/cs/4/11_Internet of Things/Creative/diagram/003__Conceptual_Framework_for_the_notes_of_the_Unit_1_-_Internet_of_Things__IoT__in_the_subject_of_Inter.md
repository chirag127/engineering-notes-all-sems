The following is a possible ASCII diagram for the conceptual framework for Internet of Things (IoT) based on the search results. The diagram shows four layers of the framework: the physical layer, the virtualization layer, the context layer, and the service layer. The physical layer consists of heterogeneous devices and sensors that generate data. The virtualization layer abstracts the physical layer and provides a unified interface for accessing the data. The context layer processes the data and extracts relevant information for different applications. The service layer delivers dynamic and personalized e-services based on the context information.
