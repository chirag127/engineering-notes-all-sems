Some examples of communication technologies for IoT are:

- Wired networks, such as Ethernet
- Wireless networks, such as Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, 5G, LTE, RFID, NFC  
- Low-power wide-area networks (LPWANs), such as NB-IoT, LoRaWAN, Sigfox
