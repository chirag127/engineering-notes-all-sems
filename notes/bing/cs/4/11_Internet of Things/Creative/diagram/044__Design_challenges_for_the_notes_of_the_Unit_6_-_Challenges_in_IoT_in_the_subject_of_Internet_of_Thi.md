The diagram shows the main design challenges in IoT, which are:

- Interoperability: The ability of different systems, devices, or components to work together seamlessly and exchange data effectively.
- Security: The protection of sensitive data and systems from unauthorized access, theft, or damage.
- Scalability: The ability to handle the increasing number and diversity of IoT devices and data sources.
- Data Management: The collection, storage, processing, and analysis of large volumes of data generated by IoT devices and applications.
- Privacy: The respect for the personal information and preferences of the users and owners of IoT devices and data.
- Reliability: The assurance that IoT devices and systems will function correctly and consistently under various conditions and scenarios.
- Energy Efficiency: The optimization of the power consumption and battery life of IoT devices and systems.
- Quality of Service: The provision of satisfactory performance and functionality of IoT devices and systems to meet the expectations and requirements of the users and stakeholders.
- User Experience: The design of intuitive, engaging, and user-friendly interfaces and interactions for IoT devices and systems.
