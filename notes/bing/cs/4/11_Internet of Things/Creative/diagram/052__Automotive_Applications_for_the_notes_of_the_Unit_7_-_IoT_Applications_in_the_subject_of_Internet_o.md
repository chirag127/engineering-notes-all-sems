The diagram illustrates the basic architecture of a typical automotive IoT application, where a connected car collects data from various sensors and cameras and sends it to a cloud-based IoT platform. The IoT platform provides services such as data storage, analysis, security, sharing, insights and actions. The data can be used by different stakeholders, such as fleet managers, insurance companies, or other third-party service providers, to optimize their operations, reduce costs, improve safety, and enhance customer experience. For example, a fleet manager can use the data to monitor the location, performance, and maintenance of the vehicles, and schedule repairs or services accordingly. An insurance company can use the data to analyze the driving behavior, offer personalized pricing, and process claims faster. A connected car can also use the data to provide infotainment, navigation, and diagnostics features to the driver and passengers.
