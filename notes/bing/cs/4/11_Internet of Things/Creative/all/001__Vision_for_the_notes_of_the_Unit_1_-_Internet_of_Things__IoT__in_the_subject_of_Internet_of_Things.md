### Vision for the notes of the Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT) in the subject of Internet of Things

- The vision of the Internet of Things (IoT) is to create a network of physical objects, devices, vehicles, buildings, and other items that are embedded with sensors, actuators, software, and connectivity to enable them to exchange data and interact with each other, humans, and the Internet  .
- The vision of the IoT can be seen from two perspectives: Internet-centric and thing-centric .
  - The Internet-centric architecture involves Internet services as the main focus, as data is being generated by the "things". The things are passive and depend on the Internet for communication and processing .
  - The thing-centric architecture involves smart devices as the main focus, as they have the capabilities of sensing, computing, and networking. The things are active and can communicate and collaborate with each other and the Internet .
- The vision of the IoT is to enable various applications and scenarios that can benefit from the data and services provided by the things, such as smart homes, smart cities, smart health, smart agriculture, smart industry, smart environment, smart transportation, and smart energy  .
- The vision of the IoT also poses various challenges and issues that need to be addressed, such as scalability, interoperability, security, privacy, reliability, quality of service, energy efficiency, and social and ethical implications  .

Some possible mnemonics and learning tricks for the vision of the IoT are:

- To remember the two perspectives of the IoT architecture, think of **I**nternet-centric and **T**hing-centric as **IT**.
- To remember some of the applications and scenarios of the IoT, think of the acronym **SHAPES** for **S**mart **H**omes, **A**griculture, **P**roduction, **E**nvironment, and **S**ecurity.
- To remember some of the challenges and issues of the IoT, think of the acronym **SPRITES** for **S**calability, **P**rivacy, **R**eliability, **I**nteroperability, **T**rust, **E**nergy, and **S**ecurity.
