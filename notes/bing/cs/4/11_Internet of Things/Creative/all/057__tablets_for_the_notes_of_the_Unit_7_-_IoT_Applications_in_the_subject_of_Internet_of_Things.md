### Tablets for the notes of the Unit 7 - IoT Applications in the subject of Internet of Things

- Tablets are portable devices that have a touchscreen interface and can run various applications, such as web browsers, games, social media, etc.
- Tablets can also be used for IoT applications, which are applications that use the internet to connect and communicate with physical devices, sensors, and actuators.
- Some examples of IoT applications that can be run on tablets are:

  - **HMI Tablet**: A tablet that can act as a human-machine interface (HMI) for industrial and safety applications, such as controlling machines, monitoring processes, and displaying data. HMI tablets can improve the usability, visualization, and mobility of industrial IoT systems.
  - **Smart Pill Dispenser**: A tablet that can act as a smart pill dispenser, which is a device that can store, dispense, and track medication for patients. Smart pill dispensers can use IoT to connect to the cloud, send alerts, and collect data on medication adherence and health outcomes.
  - **Blynk**: A tablet that can run Blynk, which is a software platform that allows users to create IoT applications using a drag-and-drop interface and connect them to various devices, such as Arduino, Raspberry Pi, ESP8266, etc. Blynk can enable users to control and monitor their IoT devices from their tablets, as well as analyze and visualize the data collected by them.
  - **Contactless Payment**: A tablet that can enable contactless payment, which is a method of paying for goods and services without touching a physical terminal or card reader. Contactless payment can use IoT to communicate with payment devices, such as NFC tags, QR codes, or Bluetooth beacons, and process transactions securely and quickly.
  - **Custom Tablet**: A tablet that can be custom designed for specific IoT use cases, such as environmental monitoring, asset tracking, field service, etc. Custom tablets can have features such as rugged design, long battery life, cellular connectivity, and sensor integration, to meet the requirements of different IoT applications.

- Some advantages of using tablets for IoT applications are:

  - Tablets are portable, lightweight, and easy to use, which can improve the mobility, convenience, and user experience of IoT applications.
  - Tablets have large screens, high resolution, and touch capabilities, which can enhance the visualization, interaction, and feedback of IoT applications.
  - Tablets have powerful processors, memory, and storage, which can support the computation, analysis, and storage of IoT data.
  - Tablets have various connectivity options, such as Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, cellular, and NFC, which can enable the communication and integration of IoT devices and services.
  - Tablets have access to various applications, such as web browsers, social media, video conferencing, etc, which can enrich the functionality and value of IoT applications.

- Some disadvantages of using tablets for IoT applications are:

  - Tablets are expensive, fragile, and prone to theft or damage, which can increase the cost and risk of IoT applications.
  - Tablets have limited battery life, which can limit the operation and reliability of IoT applications.
  - Tablets have security and privacy issues, such as malware, hacking, and data leakage, which can compromise the safety and integrity of IoT applications.
  - Tablets have compatibility and interoperability issues, such as different operating systems, protocols, and standards, which can hinder the connectivity and integration of IoT devices and services.

- A possible mnemonic to remember the main points of this topic is:

  - **T**ablets are **T**ouchscreen devices that can run various **A**pplications, including **I**oT applications that use the **I**nternet to connect and communicate with physical devices, sensors, and actuators.
  - **T**ablets have **A**dvantages, such as **P**ortability, **V**isualization, **P**erformance, **C**onnectivity, and **F**unctionality, for IoT applications.
  - **T**ablets have **D**isadvantages, such as **C**ost, **R**isk, **B**attery, **S**ecurity, and **C**ompatibility, for IoT applications.
