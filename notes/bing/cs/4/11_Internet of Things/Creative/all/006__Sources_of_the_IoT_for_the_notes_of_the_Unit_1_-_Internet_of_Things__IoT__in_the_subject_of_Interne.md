### Sources of the IoT for the notes of the Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT) in the subject of Internet of Things

The Internet of Things (IoT) is a network of physical objects or devices that can communicate, sense, and interact with each other and other systems over the internet. The sources of IoT are the devices, sensors, platforms, and tools that enable the creation, management, and analysis of IoT data and applications. Some of the sources of IoT are:

- **IoT devices**: These are the physical objects or machines that have embedded sensors, actuators, processors, and communication modules that enable them to connect to the internet and exchange data. Examples of IoT devices are smart phones, smart watches, smart home appliances, smart meters, smart cars, smart cameras, etc.

- **IoT sensors**: These are the components that measure physical or environmental parameters, such as temperature, pressure, humidity, light, sound, motion, etc. and convert them into electrical signals that can be transmitted and processed by IoT devices or platforms. Examples of IoT sensors are thermometers, barometers, hygrometers, photodiodes, microphones, accelerometers, etc.

- **IoT platforms**: These are the software or cloud-based services that provide the infrastructure, middleware, and tools for connecting, managing, and analyzing IoT devices and data. Examples of IoT platforms are AWS IoT, Azure IoT, Google Cloud IoT, IBM Watson IoT, etc.

- **IoT tools**: These are the applications or libraries that facilitate the development, deployment, and operation of IoT solutions. Examples of IoT tools are Node-RED, OpenRemote, Flutter, Arduino, M2MLabs Mainspring, ThingsBoard, Kinoma, Kaa IoT Platform, etc.
