### MAC protocol survey for the notes of the Unit 4 - Network & Communication aspects in IoT in the subject of Internet of Things

- MAC (Medium Access Control) protocols are responsible for coordinating the access of multiple devices to the shared wireless medium in IoT networks.
- MAC protocols can be classified into two main categories: contention-based and contention-free.
- Contention-based MAC protocols allow devices to compete for the channel access, such as CSMA/CA, ALOHA, and IEEE 802.11.
- Contention-free MAC protocols assign the channel access to devices in a deterministic way, such as TDMA, FDMA, CDMA, and IEEE 802.15.4.
- Contention-based MAC protocols are more flexible and scalable, but suffer from collisions, overhead, and latency.
- Contention-free MAC protocols are more efficient and reliable, but require synchronization, reservation, and coordination among devices.
- IoT networks have some specific challenges and requirements for MAC protocols, such as energy efficiency, scalability, reliability, heterogeneity, mobility, and security.
- Several MAC protocols have been proposed or adapted for IoT networks, such as IEEE 802.11ah, IEEE 802.15.4e, LoRaWAN, SigFox, and NB-IoT.
- IEEE 802.11ah, also known as WiFi HaLow, is a scalable solution for medium-range communication in IoT. It leverages various innovative medium access techniques, such as restricted access window, target wake time, and group addressed transmission .
- IEEE 802.15.4e, also known as TSCH, is a time-slotted and channel-hopping MAC protocol for low-power and lossy networks. It provides high reliability, low latency, and energy efficiency by using a schedule-based access and frequency diversity.
- LoRaWAN is a low-power wide-area network protocol that uses a star-of-stars topology and a chirp spread spectrum modulation. It supports three classes of devices with different trade-offs between energy consumption and latency.
- SigFox is a low-power wide-area network protocol that uses an ultra-narrow band modulation and a random access scheme. It offers a low-cost and simple solution for IoT applications that require small data packets and low data rates.
- NB-IoT is a narrowband cellular technology that operates in licensed spectrum and uses orthogonal frequency division multiple access. It provides a high coverage, low power consumption, and high security for IoT applications that require reliable and bidirectional communication.
- A mnemonic to remember the main categories of MAC protocols is: **C**ontention-based and **C**ontention-free, or **C** and **C**.
- A mnemonic to remember the main contention-based MAC protocols is: **C**SMA/**C**A, **A**LOHA, and **I**EEE 802.11, or **C**AI.
- A mnemonic to remember the main contention-free MAC protocols is: **T**DMA, **F**DMA, **C**DMA, and **I**EEE 802.15.4, or **T**FCI.
- A mnemonic to remember the main MAC protocols for IoT networks is: **I**EEE 802.11ah, **I**EEE 802.15.4e, **L**oRaWAN, **S**igFox, and **N**B-IoT, or **I**ILSN.
