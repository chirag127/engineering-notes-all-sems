### Sensor deployment and node discovery for the notes of the Unit 4 - Network & Communication aspects in IoT in the subject of Internet of Things

- Sensor deployment is the process of placing and configuring sensor nodes in an IoT network to collect and transmit data from the physical environment.
- Sensor nodes are the devices that have sensing, processing, communication, and power capabilities, and can be wired or wireless, fixed or mobile, active or passive, etc.
- Sensor deployment can be done in various ways, such as random, deterministic, manual, or automated, depending on the application requirements, network topology, and environmental conditions.
- Sensor deployment affects the performance of the IoT network in terms of coverage, connectivity, reliability, scalability, energy efficiency, and security.
- Some of the challenges and issues in sensor deployment are:
  - How to determine the optimal number and location of sensor nodes to achieve the desired network objectives?
  - How to deal with the heterogeneity and diversity of sensor nodes and their capabilities?
  - How to cope with the dynamic and unpredictable changes in the network and the environment?
  - How to ensure the security and privacy of the sensor data and the network?
- Node discovery is the process of identifying and locating the sensor nodes in an IoT network and establishing communication links among them.
- Node discovery can be done in various ways, such as broadcasting, multicasting, unicasting, or querying, depending on the network architecture, communication protocol, and node characteristics.
- Node discovery enables the sensor nodes to exchange information, coordinate actions, and collaborate with each other and with other devices in the IoT network.
- Node discovery affects the functionality and efficiency of the IoT network in terms of data aggregation, routing, synchronization, load balancing, fault tolerance, and self-organization.
- Some of the challenges and issues in node discovery are:
  - How to discover the sensor nodes in a timely and accurate manner without consuming too much energy and bandwidth?
  - How to handle the mobility and variability of sensor nodes and their locations?
  - How to deal with the interference and noise in the wireless channel and the environment?
  - How to ensure the authenticity and integrity of the node discovery messages and the network?

- Some of the examples and applications of sensor deployment and node discovery in IoT are:
  - Wireless Body Area Network (WBAN) for healthcare applications, where sensor nodes can be deployed on or inside the human body to monitor vital signs, detect falls, and track environmental conditions .
  - Smart agriculture, where sensor nodes can be deployed on the soil, plants, animals, or vehicles to measure soil moisture, temperature, humidity, light, pH, etc., and optimize irrigation, fertilization, pest control, and harvesting.
  - Smart city, where sensor nodes can be deployed on the buildings, roads, bridges, vehicles, or people to monitor traffic, pollution, noise, weather, etc., and improve urban planning, transportation, safety, and quality of life.
  - Smart home, where sensor nodes can be deployed on the appliances, furniture, doors, windows, or users to control lighting, heating, ventilation, security, entertainment, etc., and enhance comfort, convenience, and energy saving.

- Some of the mnemonics and learning tricks for sensor deployment and node discovery in IoT are:
  - To remember the factors that affect sensor deployment, use the acronym **CCREES** (Coverage, Connectivity, Reliability, Energy Efficiency, Scalability, Security).
  - To remember the factors that affect node discovery, use the acronym **DARFSS** (Data Aggregation, Routing, Fault Tolerance, Synchronization, Self-organization).
  - To remember the types of sensor deployment, use the acronym **RAMA** (Random, Automated, Manual, Deterministic).
  - To remember the types of node discovery, use the acronym **BUMQ** (Broadcasting, Unicasting, Multicasting, Querying).
