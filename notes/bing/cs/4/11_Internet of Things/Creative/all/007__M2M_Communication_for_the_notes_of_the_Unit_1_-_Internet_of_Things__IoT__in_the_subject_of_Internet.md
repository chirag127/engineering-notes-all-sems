### M2M Communication for the notes of the Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT) in the subject of Internet of Things

- M2M stands for Machine to Machine communication and it refers to the direct exchange of data between devices, sensors, actuators, and hardware over wired or wireless networks  .
- M2M is a general concept that encompasses many different technologies, including IoT, which is a specific subset of M2M that relies on IP-based networks to send data collected from IoT-connected devices to gateways, the cloud, or middleware platforms .
- M2M communication can be used for various purposes, such as remote monitoring, control, automation, security, asset tracking, smart metering, and more   .
- M2M communication can be classified into two types: point-to-point and point-to-multipoint.
  - Point-to-point M2M communication involves a direct connection between two devices, such as a sensor and an actuator, or a smart meter and a utility company .
  - Point-to-multipoint M2M communication involves a connection between one device and multiple devices, such as a gateway and several sensors, or a server and several smart devices .
- M2M communication can also be classified into two modes: synchronous and asynchronous.
  - Synchronous M2M communication involves a request-response mechanism, where one device sends a request to another device and waits for a response, such as a smart thermostat sending a temperature reading to a cloud service and receiving a command to adjust the heating or cooling.
  - Asynchronous M2M communication involves a publish-subscribe mechanism, where one device publishes data to a network and another device subscribes to receive the data, such as a smart watch sending heart rate data to a mobile app and receiving notifications or recommendations.
- M2M communication can be enabled by various technologies, such as cellular, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Zigbee, RFID, NFC, and more   .
- M2M communication can be challenged by various factors, such as network availability, reliability, security, scalability, interoperability, and cost   .
- M2M communication can be improved by various solutions, such as edge computing, fog computing, cloud computing, blockchain, artificial intelligence, and more   .

#### Mnemonics and learning tricks

- To remember the difference between IoT and M2M, think of IoT as a subset of M2M that uses IP-based networks and cloud platforms, while M2M is a broader concept that uses any type of network and device  .
- To remember the types of M2M communication, think of point-to-point as a direct connection between two devices, and point-to-multipoint as a connection between one device and multiple devices .
- To remember the modes of M2M communication, think of synchronous as a request-response mechanism, and asynchronous as a publish-subscribe mechanism.
- To remember some of the technologies that enable M2M communication, think of the acronym CWBZRN, which stands for Cellular, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Zigbee, RFID, and NFC   .
- To remember some of the challenges that M2M communication faces, think of the acronym NRS SIC, which stands for Network availability, Reliability, Security, Scalability, Interoperability, and Cost   .
- To remember some of the solutions that M2M communication can benefit from, think of the acronym EFC BAB, which stands for Edge computing, Fog computing, Cloud computing, Blockchain, Artificial intelligence, and Big data   .
