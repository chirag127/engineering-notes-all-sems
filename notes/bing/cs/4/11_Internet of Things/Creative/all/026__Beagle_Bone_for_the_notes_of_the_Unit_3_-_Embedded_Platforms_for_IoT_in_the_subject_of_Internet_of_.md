### Beagle Bone for the notes of the Unit 3 - Embedded Platforms for IoT in the subject of Internet of Things

- Beagle Bone is a family of open-source, credit-card sized, Linux-based single-board computers (SBCs) that can be used for IoT projects and applications .
- Beagle Bone boards are based on ARM architecture and have various models, such as Beagle Bone Black, Beagle Bone Black Wireless, Beagle Bone AI, etc. with different features and capabilities .
- Beagle Bone boards have the following advantages for IoT development :
  - They are affordable, portable and easy to use and modify.
  - They support various operating systems, such as Android, Ubuntu, Debian, etc.
  - They have flexible peripheral interfaces, such as USB, Ethernet, HDMI, etc.
  - They have a proven ecosystem of feature-rich plug-in boards, called "Capes", that can extend the functionality and connectivity of the boards.
  - They have programmable real-time units (PRUs) and a Cortex-M3 microcontroller that can handle low-level tasks and offload the main processor.
- Beagle Bone boards can run lightweight Kubernetes, such as K3s, which is a powerful and scalable platform for deploying and managing IoT applications and services.
- Beagle Bone boards can be used for various IoT use cases, such as smart home, industrial automation, robotics, edge computing, etc  .
