### Designing of smart street lights in smart city for the notes of the Unit 7 - IoT Applications in the subject of Internet of Things

- Smart street lights are LED-based lighting systems that can be controlled and monitored remotely, using sensors, cameras, and wireless communication technologies.
- Smart street lights can provide various benefits for a smart city, such as:
  - Energy saving: Smart street lights can adjust their brightness and color temperature according to the ambient light, weather, traffic, and time of the day, reducing the power consumption and maintenance costs.
  - Data collection: Smart street lights can collect and transmit data about traffic, weather, air quality, noise, and other environmental parameters, enabling better urban planning and management.
  - Safety and security: Smart street lights can enhance the visibility and safety of pedestrians and drivers, as well as detect and report accidents, crimes, and emergencies, using cameras, motion sensors, and alarms.
  - Smart city services: Smart street lights can serve as the backbone of a network that connects and delivers various smart city applications, such as public Wi-Fi, electric vehicle charging, digital signage, and waste management.
- The architecture of a smart street light system consists of four main components:
  - Smart LED lamps: These are the light sources that can be dimmed, switched, and color-tuned remotely, using LED drivers and controllers. They can also be equipped with sensors, cameras, and communication modules to collect and transmit data.
  - Smart poles: These are the physical structures that support the smart LED lamps and provide power and network connectivity. They can also host other devices and equipment for smart city services, such as Wi-Fi routers, EV chargers, and digital displays.
  - Communication network: This is the wireless or wired network that connects the smart LED lamps and poles to a central management system. It can use various technologies, such as ZigBee, LoRa, NB-IoT, or cellular, depending on the bandwidth, range, and cost requirements.
  - Central management system: This is the software platform that monitors and controls the smart LED lamps and poles, as well as analyzes and visualizes the data collected from them. It can also integrate with other smart city systems and platforms, such as traffic management, emergency response, and environmental monitoring.
