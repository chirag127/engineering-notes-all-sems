### Survey routing protocols for the notes of the Unit 4 - Network & Communication aspects in IoT in the subject of Internet of Things

- Routing protocols are the rules that determine how data packets are transmitted from one node to another in a network.
- Routing protocols are essential for the Internet of Things (IoT), which is a network of physical objects that can sense, communicate and interact with each other and the environment.
- Routing protocols for IoT should be able to handle the challenges of scalability, heterogeneity, mobility, resource constraints, security and trust of the IoT devices and applications.
- There are different types of routing protocols for IoT, such as flat, hierarchical, geographic, context-based, swarm-based, security-based and trust-based routing protocols.
- Flat routing protocols are based on the assumption that all nodes have equal roles and capabilities in the network. They use flooding, gossiping or random walk techniques to disseminate data packets. Examples of flat routing protocols are AODV, DSR, OLSR, etc.
- Hierarchical routing protocols are based on the idea of dividing the network into clusters of nodes, where each cluster has a leader or a coordinator that is responsible for routing data packets within and between clusters. Examples of hierarchical routing protocols are LEACH, PEGASIS, HEED, etc.
- Geographic routing protocols are based on the location information of the nodes and the destination. They use geographic coordinates or landmarks to route data packets. Examples of geographic routing protocols are GPSR, GEAR, GAF, etc.
- Context-based routing protocols are based on the context information of the nodes and the application, such as temperature, humidity, light, sound, etc. They use context-awareness to route data packets. Examples of context-based routing protocols are CBRP, CAR, CTP, etc.
- Swarm-based routing protocols are based on the principles of swarm intelligence, which is the collective behavior of decentralized and self-organized systems. They use bio-inspired techniques such as ant colony optimization, bee colony optimization, particle swarm optimization, etc. to route data packets. Examples of swarm-based routing protocols are ACO, BCO, PSO, etc.
- Security-based routing protocols are based on the security requirements of the IoT applications, such as confidentiality, integrity, authentication, authorization, etc. They use cryptographic techniques or trust mechanisms to route data packets. Examples of security-based routing protocols are SPINS, TinySec, SIA, etc.
- Trust-based routing protocols are based on the trustworthiness of the nodes and the links in the network. They use trust models or reputation systems to evaluate and update the trust values of the nodes and the links. Examples of trust-based routing protocols are TBRP, TRUST, CONFIDANT, etc.
