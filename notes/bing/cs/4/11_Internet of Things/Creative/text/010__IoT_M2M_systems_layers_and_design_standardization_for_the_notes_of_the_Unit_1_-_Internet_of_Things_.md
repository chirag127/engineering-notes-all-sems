### IoT/M2M systems layers and design standardization

- IoT/M2M systems are composed of devices, networks, platforms, and applications that enable data exchange and service delivery among physical objects and digital systems.
- To ensure interoperability, scalability, and security of IoT/M2M systems, standardization is essential at different layers of the system architecture.
- One of the leading global initiatives for IoT/M2M standardization is oneM2M, which was launched in 2012 by ETSI and 13 other founding members.
- The oneM2M architecture divides IoT functions into three major domains: the application layer, the service layer, and the network layer  .
- The application layer is responsible for providing the business logic and user interface for IoT/M2M applications. It interacts with the service layer through IT-friendly APIs that support various protocols, such as HTTP, CoAP, MQTT, etc .
- The service layer is the core of the oneM2M architecture, which provides common services and functions for IoT/M2M applications, such as data management, device management, security, discovery, subscription, notification, etc  .
- The network layer is responsible for providing the connectivity and transport mechanisms for IoT/M2M devices and platforms. It supports various technologies, such as cellular, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, ZigBee, etc  .
- The oneM2M architecture also defines three types of nodes: application service node (ASN), common service node (CSN), and infrastructure node (IN). ASNs are the endpoints of the application layer, which host the application logic and interface with the service layer. CSNs are the main components of the service layer, which provide the common services and functions for IoT/M2M applications. INs are the components of the network layer, which provide the connectivity and transport services for IoT/M2M devices and platforms .
- The oneM2M architecture aims to achieve interoperability, scalability, and security of IoT/M2M systems by providing a standardized and modular framework that can support various use cases, technologies, and domains  .
