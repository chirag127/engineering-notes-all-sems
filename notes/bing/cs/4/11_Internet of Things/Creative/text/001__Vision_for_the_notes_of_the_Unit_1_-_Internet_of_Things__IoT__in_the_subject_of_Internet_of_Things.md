### Vision for the notes of the Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT) in the subject of Internet of Things

- The notes should provide an overview of the concept, definition, characteristics, and applications of IoT.
- The notes should explain the evolution and history of IoT, and the main drivers and challenges for its development and adoption.
- The notes should introduce the basic architecture and components of IoT systems, such as sensors, actuators, communication protocols, cloud computing, and data analytics.
- The notes should illustrate some examples of IoT domains and use cases, such as smart homes, smart cities, smart health, smart agriculture, and smart industry.
- The notes should discuss the benefits and opportunities of IoT for society, economy, and environment, as well as the risks and ethical issues associated with IoT, such as privacy, security, and social impact.
- The notes should include diagrams, tables, charts, and images to enhance the understanding and visualization of the concepts and examples.
- The notes should provide references and links to additional resources and readings for further learning and exploration of IoT.
