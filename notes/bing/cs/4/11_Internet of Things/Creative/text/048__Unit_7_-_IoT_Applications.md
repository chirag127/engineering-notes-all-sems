## Unit 7 - IoT Applications

- IoT applications are the use cases of IoT technology that provide various benefits and solutions to different domains and industries.
- IoT applications can be classified into three categories: consumer, enterprise and industrial.
- Consumer IoT applications are those that target individual users or households, such as smart home devices, wearable gadgets, personal assistants and entertainment systems.
- Enterprise IoT applications are those that aim to improve the efficiency, productivity, security and quality of business processes, such as smart buildings, smart cities, smart grids, supply chain management and traffic management.
- Industrial IoT applications are those that involve the use of IoT devices and sensors to monitor, control and optimize industrial operations, such as manufacturing, agriculture, mining, oil and gas, and healthcare.

- Some examples of IoT applications are:

  - Connected vehicles: Autonomous vehicles are one of the most notable examples of IoT in action. Self-driving cars and trucks use a slew of connected devices to safely navigate roadways in all sorts of traffic and weather conditions. The technologies in use include artificial intelligence (AI)-enabled cameras, motion sensors and onboard computers .
  - Environmental monitoring: IoT devices can be used to collect and analyze data on various environmental parameters, such as air quality, water quality, soil moisture, temperature, humidity, rainfall, etc. This can help to detect and prevent pollution, conserve natural resources, improve agricultural practices and respond to natural disasters .
  - Smart buildings and smart homes: IoT devices can be used to automate and optimize various aspects of building and home management, such as lighting, heating, ventilation, air conditioning, security, entertainment, etc. This can help to reduce energy consumption, enhance comfort, increase safety and provide convenience .
  - Smart cities: IoT devices can be used to improve the quality of life and services in urban areas, such as transportation, waste management, public safety, healthcare, education, etc. This can help to reduce traffic congestion, enhance public health, promote social inclusion and foster innovation .
  - Supply chain management: IoT devices can be used to track and monitor the movement and condition of goods and materials across the supply chain, from production to distribution to consumption. This can help to improve inventory management, reduce operational costs, increase customer satisfaction and ensure product quality .
  - Industrial, agricultural and commercial management: IoT devices can be used to monitor and control various aspects of industrial, agricultural and commercial operations, such as machinery, equipment, crops, livestock, energy, etc. This can help to improve efficiency, productivity, safety, quality and profitability .
