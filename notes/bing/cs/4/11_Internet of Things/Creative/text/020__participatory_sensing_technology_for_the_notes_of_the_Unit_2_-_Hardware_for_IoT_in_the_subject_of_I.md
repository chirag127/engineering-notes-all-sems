### Participatory Sensing Technology

- Participatory sensing is an approach to data collection and interpretation in which individuals, acting alone or in groups, use their personal mobile devices and web services to systematically explore interesting aspects of their worlds ranging from health to culture  .
- Participatory sensing can be seen as a form of crowdsourcing, where the crowd provides data and insights about their environment, activities, opinions, or experiences using sensors embedded in their smartphones or other wearable devices.
- Participatory sensing can enable various applications such as environmental monitoring, traffic management, urban planning, public health, social networking, citizen science, and civic engagement .
- Participatory sensing can also pose challenges such as data quality, privacy, security, incentives, and user engagement .
- Participatory sensing can benefit from the advances in hardware for IoT, such as low-cost, low-power, and miniaturized sensors, wireless communication, cloud computing, and edge computing .
