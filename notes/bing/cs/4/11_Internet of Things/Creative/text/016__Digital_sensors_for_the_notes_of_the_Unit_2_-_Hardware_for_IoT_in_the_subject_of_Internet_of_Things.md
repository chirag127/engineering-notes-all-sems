### Digital sensors for the notes of the Unit 2 - Hardware for IoT in the subject of Internet of Things

- Digital sensors are pieces of hardware that detect changes in an environment and collect data in a digital format .
- Digital sensors are different from analog sensors, which produce a continuous signal that varies with the physical quantity being measured.
- Digital sensors interact directly with an IoT microcontroller, which is a small computer that controls the functions of an IoT device.
- Digital sensors can measure various physical phenomena, such as temperature, pressure, motion, light, sound, humidity, etc .
- Digital sensors can be classified into different types based on their function, such as:
  - Binary sensors: These sensors have only two possible states, such as on/off, open/closed, etc. For example, a door sensor, a motion sensor, a smoke detector, etc.
  - Counting sensors: These sensors count the number of events or occurrences in a given period of time, such as pulses, revolutions, etc. For example, a flow sensor, a speed sensor, a vibration sensor, etc.
  - Measuring sensors: These sensors measure the value of a physical quantity, such as temperature, pressure, distance, etc. For example, a thermometer, a barometer, a ultrasonic sensor, etc.
- Digital sensors enable the Internet of Things (IoT) by collecting the data for smarter decisions and actions .
- Digital sensors are used in various applications, such as consumer devices, industry 4.0, medical devices, smart cities, smart homes, etc  .
- Digital sensors face some challenges in IoT, such as power consumption, data security, data quality, data integration, etc .
