### Raspberry Pi for the notes of the Unit 3 - Embedded Platforms for IoT in the subject of Internet of Things

- Raspberry Pi is a low-cost, credit-card sized computer that can run various operating systems, such as Linux, Windows 10 IoT Core, and Raspbian.
- Raspberry Pi can be used as an embedded platform for IoT projects, because it has a powerful CPU, wireless LAN and Bluetooth 4.1 radio, and a 40-pin GPIO connector for interfacing with external sensors.
- Raspberry Pi can also be used as a gateway device that collects data from other IoT devices, such as microcontrollers, and sends it to the cloud or a local server for processing and analysis.
- Raspberry Pi can support various IoT applications, such as smart gardening, air quality monitoring, smart energy management, home automation, and industrial IoT control.
- Raspberry Pi has a large and active community of developers and enthusiasts who share their projects, tutorials, and resources online, making it easy to learn and experiment with IoT.
