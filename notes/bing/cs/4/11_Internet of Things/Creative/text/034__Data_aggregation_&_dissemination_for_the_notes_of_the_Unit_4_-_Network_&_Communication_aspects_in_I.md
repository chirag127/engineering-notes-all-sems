### Data aggregation and dissemination for the notes of the Unit 4 - Network and Communication aspects in IoT in the subject of Internet of Things

- Data aggregation and dissemination are two fundamental services in Internet of Things (IoT) networks .
- Data aggregation is the process of collecting, filtering, compressing and summarizing data from multiple sources or nodes in the IoT network .
- Data dissemination is the process of transmitting, distributing and broadcasting data from a source node or a base station to multiple destination nodes or devices in the IoT network .
- Data aggregation and dissemination can improve the efficiency, reliability and scalability of IoT networks by reducing the communication overhead, energy consumption and network congestion .
- Data aggregation and dissemination can also enable various applications and services in IoT, such as data analysis, decision making, monitoring, control, security and privacy .
- Data aggregation and dissemination can be performed in different ways, such as centralized, distributed, hierarchical, cooperative, opportunistic, etc .
- Data aggregation and dissemination can face various challenges and issues in IoT, such as heterogeneity, interoperability, synchronization, coordination, security, privacy, reliability, etc  .
- Data aggregation and dissemination can be enhanced by using various techniques and methods, such as machine learning, optimization, encryption, compression, etc .
