### Conceptual Framework for the notes of the Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT) in the subject of Internet of Things

- The Internet of Things (IoT) is a network of physical objects or things that are embedded with sensors, actuators, software, and connectivity to enable data exchange and communication with other devices or systems over the Internet or other networks.
- The IoT enables various applications and services that can improve the quality of life, efficiency, productivity, and sustainability in different domains such as smart cities, smart homes, smart health, smart agriculture, smart industry, etc.
- The IoT is composed of four main components: things, communication, computation, and services.
  - Things are the physical objects or devices that have sensing, actuating, or computing capabilities and can interact with the environment or other things.
  - Communication is the process of transferring data or information between things or between things and other systems over wired or wireless networks using various protocols and standards.
  - Computation is the process of processing, analyzing, or storing data or information collected from things or other sources using cloud computing, edge computing, or fog computing platforms.
  - Services are the applications or functions that provide value or benefits to the users or stakeholders of the IoT system using the data or information from things or other sources.
- The IoT is characterized by some key features such as heterogeneity, scalability, interoperability, security, privacy, and intelligence.
  - Heterogeneity refers to the diversity and complexity of the things, communication, computation, and services in the IoT system, which require different technologies, protocols, standards, and platforms to work together.
  - Scalability refers to the ability of the IoT system to handle the increasing number and variety of things, communication, computation, and services without compromising the performance, quality, or reliability of the system.
  - Interoperability refers to the ability of the IoT system to exchange and use data or information from different things, communication, computation, and services regardless of their differences in technologies, protocols, standards, and platforms.
  - Security refers to the protection of the IoT system from unauthorized access, modification, or damage of the data or information, things, communication, computation, or services by malicious actors or threats.
  - Privacy refers to the protection of the personal or sensitive data or information of the users or stakeholders of the IoT system from unauthorized collection, disclosure, or use by the IoT system or other parties.
  - Intelligence refers to the ability of the IoT system to learn from the data or information, things, communication, computation, or services and to provide smart, adaptive, or autonomous solutions or actions to the users or stakeholders of the IoT system.
