### Mobiles

- Mobiles are devices that can communicate wirelessly with other devices or networks over short or long distances.
- Mobiles can be classified into three categories: smartphones, feature phones and basic phones.
- Smartphones are mobiles that have advanced computing capabilities and run on operating systems such as Android, iOS, Windows Phone, etc. They can access the internet, run applications, store data, take pictures, play music, etc.
- Feature phones are mobiles that have some limited computing capabilities and run on proprietary platforms such as KaiOS, Symbian, etc. They can access the internet, run some applications, store data, take pictures, play music, etc.
- Basic phones are mobiles that have minimal computing capabilities and run on simple platforms such as Java, etc. They can make calls, send messages, store contacts, etc.
- Mobiles can be used for various IoT applications such as:
  - Smart home: Mobiles can act as remote controllers or sensors for smart home devices such as lights, thermostats, security cameras, etc.
  - Smart health: Mobiles can monitor health parameters such as heart rate, blood pressure, glucose level, etc. and send data to doctors or caregivers.
  - Smart city: Mobiles can collect and share data about traffic, pollution, weather, etc. and help in improving urban planning and management.
  - Smart agriculture: Mobiles can control irrigation, fertilization, pest control, etc. and help in increasing crop yield and quality.
  - Smart industry: Mobiles can track inventory, production, maintenance, etc. and help in optimizing industrial processes and operations.
