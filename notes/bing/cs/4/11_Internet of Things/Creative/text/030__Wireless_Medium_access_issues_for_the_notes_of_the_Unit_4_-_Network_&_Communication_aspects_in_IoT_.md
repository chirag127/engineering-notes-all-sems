### Wireless Medium Access Issues for the Notes of the Unit 4 - Network & Communication Aspects in IoT in the Subject of Internet of Things

- Wireless medium access refers to the process of coordinating the transmission and reception of data among multiple devices that share a common wireless channel.
- Wireless medium access is challenging in IoT because of the following issues:
  - **Interference**: IoT devices may operate in the same frequency band as other wireless technologies, such as WiFi, Bluetooth, ZigBee, etc. This may cause interference and degrade the performance of IoT devices. For example, WiFi users may cause frequent packet drop and energy loss for IoT devices that use IEEE 802.15.4 standard.
  - **Energy efficiency**: IoT devices are often battery-powered and have limited energy resources. Therefore, wireless medium access protocols should minimize the energy consumption of IoT devices by reducing the idle listening, overhearing, collisions, retransmissions, etc. For example, IoT devices may use duty cycling techniques to switch between active and sleep modes periodically.
  - **Scalability**: IoT devices may be deployed in large numbers and have dynamic topology changes. Therefore, wireless medium access protocols should be able to handle the high network density and mobility of IoT devices without causing congestion, collisions, or synchronization problems. For example, IoT devices may use random access or group-based access schemes to avoid contention and collision among devices .
  - **Heterogeneity**: IoT devices may have different capabilities, requirements, and preferences. Therefore, wireless medium access protocols should be able to support the diversity and heterogeneity of IoT devices and applications. For example, IoT devices may use adaptive modulation and coding schemes to adjust the transmission rate and reliability according to the channel conditions and application needs.
  - **Security**: IoT devices may be vulnerable to various attacks, such as jamming, spoofing, eavesdropping, etc. Therefore, wireless medium access protocols should be able to provide security and privacy for IoT devices and data. For example, IoT devices may use encryption, authentication, and key management techniques to protect the data and identity of IoT devices.
