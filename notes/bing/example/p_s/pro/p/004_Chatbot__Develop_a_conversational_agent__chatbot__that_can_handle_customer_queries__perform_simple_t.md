Sure, I can provide some examples of chatbots for customer service. Chatbots are software applications that use natural language processing (NLP) and artificial intelligence (AI) to interact with customers via text or voice. They can help customers with common queries, perform simple tasks and make recommendations based on their preferences and needs.

Some examples of chatbots for customer service are:

- **Core dna**: This chatbot widget offers customer service to site visitors by answering their questions, providing product information and guiding them through the sales funnel.
- **HubSpot**: This chatbot platform enables businesses to create custom chatbots that can qualify leads, book meetings, provide support and more.
- **Slush**: This chatbot automates customer service for an event management company by providing event information, registration details, FAQs and feedback collection.
- **WHO**: This chatbot provides health information and updates on COVID-19 to people around the world via WhatsApp and Facebook Messenger.
