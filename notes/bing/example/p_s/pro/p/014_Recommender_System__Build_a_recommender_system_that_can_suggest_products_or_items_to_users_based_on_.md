Recommender System: Build a recommender system that can suggest products or items to users based on their previous purchases or preferences. You can use libraries like LightFM, Surprise, and Implicit to make this project.

Recommender systems are algorithms that suggest products or items to users based on their previous purchases or preferences. Some examples of recommender systems are:

- Netflix: It recommends movies and shows based on your ratings, watch history, and preferences.
- YouTube: It suggests videos based on your watch history, likes, subscriptions, and search queries.
- Tinder: It matches you with potential partners based on your location, profile, and swiping behavior.
- Amazon: It shows you products that are related to your purchases, searches, and browsing history .

These are just some of the many applications of recommender systems in different domains. You can use libraries like LightFM, Surprise, and Implicit to make your own recommender system project. These libraries provide various tools and methods to implement different types of recommender systems such as collaborative filtering, content-based filtering, hybrid filtering, etc.
