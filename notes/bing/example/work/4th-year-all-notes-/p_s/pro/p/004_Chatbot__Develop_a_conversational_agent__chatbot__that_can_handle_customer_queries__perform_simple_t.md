Sure, I can provide some examples of chatbots for customer service. Chatbots are software applications that use natural language processing (NLP) and artificial intelligence (AI) to interact with users via text or voice. They can help customers with common queries, perform simple tasks and make recommendations based on their preferences and needs.

Some examples of chatbots for customer service are:

- **Core dna**: This chatbot widget offers customer service to site visitors by answering FAQs, booking demos and providing relevant content.
- **HubSpot**: This chatbot helps customers with live chat support, product information, pricing plans and lead generation.
- **Slush**: This chatbot automates customer service by providing instant answers, collecting feedback and routing complex issues to human agents.
- **WHO**: This chatbot provides reliable information about COVID-19, such as symptoms, prevention tips and vaccine updates.
- **Sephora**: This chatbot helps customers find the best beauty products for their skin type, style and budget. It also offers tutorials, reviews and rewards.
