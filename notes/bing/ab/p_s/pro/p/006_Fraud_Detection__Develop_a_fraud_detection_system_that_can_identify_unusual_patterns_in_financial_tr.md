Fraud Detection: Develop a fraud detection system that can identify unusual patterns in financial transactions and alert authorities of potential fraud. Technologies: Python, Machine Learning, TensorFlow, Pandas, Numpy.

Fraud detection is a process that detects and prevents illegitimate activities related to financial transactions and data. It can be used to secure web, mobile and phone based transactions, as well as insurance claims and money laundering. Some of the benefits of fraud detection are   :

- It saves money and resources by avoiding losses due to fraud.
- It protects customer trust and loyalty by ensuring their data and accounts are safe.
- It enhances operational efficiency by reducing manual reviews and investigations.
- It ensures compliance with security and data privacy regulations by following best practices.

To develop a fraud detection system, you will need to use technologies such as Python, Machine Learning, TensorFlow, Pandas, Numpy. These technologies will help you to:

- Collect and preprocess data from various sources and formats.
- Build and train predictive models that can identify unusual patterns and anomalies in the data.
- Deploy and monitor the models in real time to flag suspicious transactions or events.
- Provide an investigative dashboard that can help you to review and resolve alerts.
