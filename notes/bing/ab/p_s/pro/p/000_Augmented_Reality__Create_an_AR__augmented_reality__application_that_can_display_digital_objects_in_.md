Augmented Reality: Create an AR (augmented reality) application that can display digital objects in the real world and interact with them. Technologies: Unity, ARKit, ARCore, Vuforia, C#.

Augmented reality (AR) is a technology that overlays digital information or objects onto the real world through a device such as a smartphone, tablet, or headset. AR can have many applications and benefits for different domains, such as:

- Education: AR can enhance learning by making it more immersive, interactive, and collaborative. Students can visualize and manipulate concepts that are otherwise difficult to understand or access .
- Communication: AR can improve communication and interaction by enabling new ways of expression, collaboration, and feedback. For example, AR can allow people to share their emotions, opinions, or ideas through virtual avatars or stickers .
- Business: AR can improve business processes by providing real-time information, guidance, and support. For example, AR can help customers make better buying decisions by showing them how products look or function in their environment .
- Navigation: AR can improve navigation and tourism by providing directions, landmarks, and contextual information. For example, AR can help travelers find their way around a new city or learn about its history and culture .
- Gaming: AR can improve gaming by making it more realistic, engaging, and fun. For example, AR can allow gamers to interact with virtual characters or objects in their surroundings .

To create an AR application that can display digital objects in the real world and interact with them, you will need some technologies such as:

- Unity: A cross-platform game engine that allows you to create 3D models, animations, sounds, and scripts for your AR application.
- ARKit: A framework for iOS devices that enables you to use the camera and sensors to track the position and orientation of the device and detect surfaces in the environment.
- ARCore: A platform for Android devices that provides similar features as ARKit but also supports environmental understanding and light estimation.
- Vuforia: A software development kit that enables you to use image recognition and tracking to create marker-based or markerless AR experiences.
- C#: A programming language that you can use to write scripts for your Unity project.
