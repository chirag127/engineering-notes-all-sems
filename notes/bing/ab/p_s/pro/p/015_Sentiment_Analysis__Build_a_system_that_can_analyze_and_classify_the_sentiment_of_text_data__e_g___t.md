Sentiment Analysis: Build a system that can analyze and classify the sentiment of text data (e.g., tweets, movie reviews) as positive, negative, or neutral. Technologies: Python, NLP, TensorFlow, Keras, NLTK.

Sentiment analysis is a technique that uses natural language processing (NLP) to analyze and classify the sentiment of text data as positive, negative, or neutral. Some of the applications and benefits of sentiment analysis are:

- Social media monitoring: You can use sentiment analysis to track what people are saying about your brand, product, or service on social media platforms like Twitter, Facebook, or Instagram. This can help you understand your customers' needs, preferences, feedback, and complaints. You can also compare your sentiment scores with your competitors' and identify your strengths and weaknesses.
- Customer support: You can use sentiment analysis to improve your customer service by analyzing the tone and emotion of customer queries, reviews, or feedback. This can help you prioritize urgent or negative issues, tailor your responses accordingly, and measure customer satisfaction .
- Brand monitoring and reputation management: You can use sentiment analysis to monitor how your brand is perceived by the public and the media. You can detect positive or negative mentions of your brand across different channels and sources, such as news articles, blogs, forums, etc. This can help you manage your brand image, respond to crises quickly, and leverage positive publicity .
- Market research and analysis: You can use sentiment analysis to conduct market research and analysis by collecting and analyzing opinions from surveys, focus groups, online reviews, etc. This can help you understand customer preferences, expectations,
and satisfaction levels for different products or services. You can also identify market trends,
opportunities,
and threats .
