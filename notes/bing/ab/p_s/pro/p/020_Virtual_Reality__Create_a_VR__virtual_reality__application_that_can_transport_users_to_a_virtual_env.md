Virtual reality (VR) is a technology that creates immersive and interactive simulations of real or imaginary environments. VR can have many applications and benefits for different domains, such as entertainment, education, health care, business, sports and more. Here are some examples of how VR can be used:

- VR can provide real-time training for novices who want to learn new skills or practice risky scenarios without endangering themselves or others .
- VR can enhance the experience of watching movies and playing games by making them more realistic and engaging .
- VR can provide therapy for people who suffer from phobias, anxiety, post-traumatic stress disorder (PTSD) or chronic pain by exposing them to controlled stimuli or relaxing environments .
- VR can help in computer-aided design (CAD) by allowing designers to visualize and manipulate their models in 3D space .
- VR can make education more interactive and fun by enabling students to explore different places, cultures, historical events or scientific concepts  .
- VR can save money by reducing the need for physical travel, equipment or facilities for various purposes .
- VR can provide a safe way for people to experience extreme sports, adventure activities or dangerous situations without risking injury or death .
- VR can create a virtual marketplace where consumers can try out products or services before buying them .
- VR can help paralyzed patients heal by stimulating their brain activity and restoring their sense of agency[^6].
