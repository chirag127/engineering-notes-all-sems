Augmented Reality: Create an AR (augmented reality) application that can display digital objects in the real world and interact with them. Technologies: Unity, ARKit, ARCore, Vuforia, C#.

Augmented reality (AR) is a technology that overlays digital information and objects onto the real world, creating a mixed reality experience. AR has many applications and benefits across different domains, such as education, communication, business, navigation, tourism, gaming and more   .

Some of the benefits of AR include:

- Enhancing learning and instruction by making them more immersive and interactive.
- Improving communication and interaction by introducing new ways of sharing information and collaborating with others.
- Increasing business efficiency and productivity by providing real-time data, guidance and feedback .
- Enabling navigation and tourism by offering contextual information and directions for travelers.
- Creating immersive video gaming experiences by blending virtual elements with real environments.

To create an AR application that can display digital objects in the real world and interact with them, you will need some technologies such as:

- Unity: A cross-platform game engine that supports AR development through various plugins and frameworks.
- ARKit: A framework for iOS devices that allows developers to create AR experiences using the device's camera, sensors and processors.
- ARCore: A platform for Android devices that enables developers to build AR apps using the device's camera, motion tracking and environmental understanding.
- Vuforia: A software development kit (SDK) that provides advanced computer vision features for AR applications on various platforms.
- C#: A programming language that is used to write scripts for Unity projects.
