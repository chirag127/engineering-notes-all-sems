### Estimation of Various Parameters such as Cost and Time in software project management

- Estimation is the process of predicting the required resources, effort, cost and duration of a software project based on available information and assumptions.
- Estimation is essential for planning, controlling and managing software projects, as well as for communicating with stakeholders and setting realistic expectations.
- Estimation can be done at different levels of detail and accuracy, depending on the stage of the project and the availability of information.
- Estimation can be done using various techniques, such as expert judgment, analogy, decomposition, parametric, or hybrid methods.
- Parametric estimation is one of the most widely used and reliable techniques for software project estimation. It involves using mathematical models, formulas, or algorithms to calculate the project parameters based on historical data and statistical relationships.
- Parametric estimation requires identifying the key variables or parameters that affect the project, such as size, complexity, quality, productivity, etc. These variables can be measured using different units, such as lines of code, function points, use cases, etc.
- Parametric estimation also requires collecting and analyzing historical data from similar projects or industry benchmarks to establish the values and ranges of the variables, as well as the coefficients and constants of the formulas or algorithms.
- Parametric estimation can be applied at different levels of granularity, such as project, phase, activity, or task level. It can also be adjusted or refined as the project progresses and more information becomes available.
- Parametric estimation can provide accurate and consistent results, as well as facilitate sensitivity analysis, risk analysis, and trade-off analysis. However, it also has some limitations, such as the need for reliable and relevant data, the validity and applicability of the models or formulas, and the uncertainty and variability of the variables and parameters.
- Some examples of parametric estimation models or methods for software projects are COCOMO, Function Point Analysis, Use Case Points, and Story Points.
