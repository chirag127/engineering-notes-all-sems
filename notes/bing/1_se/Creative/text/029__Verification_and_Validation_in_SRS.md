### Verification and Validation in SRS

Verification and validation are two important activities in the software requirements engineering process. They aim to ensure that the software requirements specification (SRS) document is complete, consistent, correct, realistic, unambiguous and verifiable. 

- Verification is the process of checking whether the SRS document conforms to the standards, guidelines and specifications established for it. It answers the question: "Are we building the product right?" Verification can be done by using various methods, such as peer reviews, walkthroughs, inspections, etc. Verification helps to detect errors, omissions, inconsistencies and ambiguities in the SRS document at an early stage of development. 

- Validation is the process of checking whether the SRS document meets the actual needs and expectations of the stakeholders. It answers the question: "Are we building the right product?" Validation can be done by using various methods, such as prototyping, simulation, testing, etc. Validation helps to ensure that the software system will perform its intended functions and satisfy the user requirements. 

Verification and validation should be done continuously throughout the development of requirements at every level and as part of baseline activities and reviewed during the System Requirements Reviews (SRR). Verification and validation help to improve the quality of the software system and reduce the risks of failure and rework.
