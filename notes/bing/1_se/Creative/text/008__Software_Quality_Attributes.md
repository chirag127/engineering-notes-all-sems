### Software Quality Attributes

Software quality attributes are the characteristics of a software system that affect its functionality, performance, usability, reliability, security, maintainability, portability, and other aspects. Software quality attributes are also known as non-functional requirements, quality characteristics, or quality factors.

Some examples of software quality attributes are:

- Functionality: The degree to which the software meets the specified requirements and provides the desired functions.
- Performance: The degree to which the software responds to user requests and executes tasks within acceptable time and resource limits.
- Usability: The degree to which the software is easy to learn, use, and understand by the intended users.
- Reliability: The degree to which the software operates correctly and consistently under normal and abnormal conditions.
- Security: The degree to which the software protects the data and resources from unauthorized access, modification, or damage.
- Maintainability: The degree to which the software can be modified, corrected, or improved by the developers or maintainers.
- Portability: The degree to which the software can be transferred, installed, or executed on different platforms, environments, or devices.

Software quality attributes are important for designing, developing, testing, and evaluating software systems. They help to define the quality goals and criteria for the software, and to measure and improve the quality of the software. Software quality attributes also influence the trade-offs and decisions that need to be made during the software development process, as different attributes may have conflicting or complementary effects on each other. For example, improving the performance of a software system may reduce its portability, or enhancing the security of a software system may affect its usability. Therefore, software quality attributes need to be carefully balanced and prioritized according to the needs and expectations of the stakeholders and the users of the software system.
