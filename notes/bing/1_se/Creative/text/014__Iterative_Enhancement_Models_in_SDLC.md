### Iterative Enhancement Models in SDLC

- Iterative Enhancement Models are a type of software development life cycle (SDLC) models that involve building software incrementally by adding more functionality in each iteration .
- In this model, the software is divided into smaller modules or components, each of which can be developed and tested separately .
- Each component is treated as a sub-project and goes through all the phases of the SDLC, such as planning, analysis, design, implementation, testing, and deployment .
- The first component is usually a basic or core version of the software that satisfies some of the essential requirements .
- The subsequent components add more features and enhancements to the software, based on the feedback from the users and stakeholders .
- The process of developing and integrating components is repeated until the software meets all the desired requirements and is ready for delivery .
- Iterative Enhancement Models are also known as Incremental Models or Iterative and Incremental Development (IID) Models  .
