### Software Quality Frameworks (SQF) in SRS

- Software Quality Frameworks (SQF) are the set of principles, guidelines, and best practices that aim to ensure the quality of software products and services.
- SQF can be applied at different stages of the software development life cycle, such as planning, analysis, design, implementation, testing, deployment, and maintenance.
- SQF can help to define the quality requirements, criteria, and metrics for the software system, as well as the methods and tools for measuring, monitoring, and improving the quality.
- SQF can also help to establish the roles and responsibilities of the stakeholders involved in the software quality assurance (SQA) process, such as developers, testers, customers, and managers.
- SQF can be based on various standards, models, and frameworks that have been developed and adopted by the software industry, such as ISO 5055, IEEE 830, CMMI, and Agile.
- SQF can be tailored to the specific needs and characteristics of the software project, such as the size, complexity, domain, and methodology of the software system.
- SQF can be documented in the software requirements specification (SRS) document, which is a formal and comprehensive description of the software system to be developed  .
- SQF can help to ensure that the SRS document is complete, consistent, clear, verifiable, and traceable, which are some of the quality characteristics of a good SRS.
- SQF can also help to align the expectations and objectives of the software project among the stakeholders, and to facilitate the communication and collaboration among the teams.
- SQF can ultimately contribute to the delivery of high-quality software products and services that meet the customer's needs and expectations, and that are reliable, secure, efficient, and maintainable.
