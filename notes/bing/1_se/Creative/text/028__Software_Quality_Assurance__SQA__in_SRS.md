### Software Quality Assurance (SQA) in SRS

- Software Quality Assurance (SQA) is the process of ensuring that the software products and processes meet the specified quality standards and requirements.
- SQA involves planning, implementing, monitoring, and improving the quality activities throughout the software development life cycle (SDLC).
- SQA aims to prevent defects, reduce rework, improve customer satisfaction, and enhance software quality and reliability.
- SQA in SRS refers to the quality assurance activities that are performed during the software requirements specification (SRS) phase of the SDLC.
- SRS is the document that defines the functional and non-functional requirements of the software system to be developed.
- SQA in SRS includes the following tasks:

  - Reviewing the SRS document for completeness, consistency, clarity, correctness, and testability.
  - Verifying that the SRS document conforms to the applicable standards, guidelines, and best practices.
  - Validating that the SRS document reflects the needs and expectations of the stakeholders and users.
  - Identifying and resolving any issues, ambiguities, or conflicts in the SRS document.
  - Tracking and managing the changes and revisions to the SRS document.
  - Conducting audits and inspections to ensure the quality of the SRS document and process.
  - Reporting and documenting the results and outcomes of the SQA in SRS activities.
