### Testing Objectives in Software Testing

- Testing objectives are the goals and expectations that guide the testing process and its outcomes.
- One of the main objectives of software testing is to **improve software quality** by finding and fixing defects before they reach the end users.
- Another objective of software testing is to **gain confidence** in and provide information about the level of quality and reliability of the software.
- A third objective of software testing is to **verify** whether the software meets the specified requirements and expectations of the stakeholders.
- A fourth objective of software testing is to **prevent** defects from occurring in the first place by applying best practices and standards throughout the software development life cycle.
- Testing objectives may vary depending on the type of testing technique and the software development model used. For example, unit testing aims to find as many defects as possible in individual components, while integration testing focuses on the interactions between components.
- Testing objectives should be **clear**, **measurable**, **realistic**, and **aligned** with the project goals and scope. They should also be communicated and agreed upon by all the stakeholders involved in the testing process.
