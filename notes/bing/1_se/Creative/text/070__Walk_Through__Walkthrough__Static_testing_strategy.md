#### Walk Through (Walkthrough) Static testing strategy

- A walkthrough is a type of static testing technique that involves a review of a document or a code by a group of people, usually led by the author or the owner of the work product.
- The purpose of a walkthrough is to find defects, clarify issues, gather feedback, or achieve a common understanding of the work product among the participants.
- A walkthrough is usually informal and does not follow a strict process or a predefined checklist. The participants can ask questions, make comments, or suggest improvements during the walkthrough session.
- A walkthrough can be applied to any type of document or code, such as requirements, design, test cases, user manuals, etc. It can also be performed at any stage of the software development life cycle (SDLC).
- The typical steps of a walkthrough are:

  - The author or the owner of the work product prepares the document or the code and invites the participants for the walkthrough session. The participants can be peers, managers, customers, or other stakeholders who have an interest or a role in the work product.
  - The author or the owner of the work product presents the document or the code to the participants and explains the main objectives, assumptions, and logic behind it. The participants can ask questions, make comments, or suggest improvements during the presentation.
  - The author or the owner of the work product records the feedback, issues, and defects identified by the participants and decides how to address them. The author or the owner of the work product may also assign actions to the participants or other responsible parties to resolve the issues or defects.
  - The author or the owner of the work product updates the document or the code based on the feedback, issues, and defects and verifies that they have been resolved. The author or the owner of the work product may also conduct a follow-up walkthrough session to confirm that the work product meets the expectations of the participants.

- The advantages of a walkthrough are:

  - It is a simple and flexible technique that can be applied to any type of document or code and at any stage of the SDLC.
  - It helps to improve the quality of the work product by finding defects early and preventing them from propagating to the later stages of the SDLC.
  - It helps to enhance the communication and collaboration among the participants and to achieve a common understanding of the work product.
  - It helps to reduce the cost and time of rework and testing by fixing the defects before they become more expensive and difficult to fix.

- The disadvantages of a walkthrough are:

  - It is a subjective and inconsistent technique that depends on the skills and experience of the participants and the author or the owner of the work product.
  - It may not cover all the aspects or scenarios of the work product and may miss some defects that are not obvious or visible during the presentation.
  - It may not follow a rigorous or a structured process and may not produce a formal or a detailed record of the feedback, issues, and defects.
  - It may consume a lot of time and resources of the participants and the author or the owner of the work product, especially if the work product is large or complex.
