#### Categories of networks in computer networks

A computer network is a collection of devices that are connected by communication channels to share data and resources. There are different ways to categorize computer networks based on their size, scope, topology, architecture, and purpose. Some of the common categories of networks are:

- **Personal Area Network (PAN)**: A PAN is a network that connects devices within a small area, such as a room or a desk. A PAN typically consists of a personal computer, a smartphone, a printer, a scanner, a headset, etc. A PAN can use wired or wireless technologies, such as USB, Bluetooth, infrared, etc. A PAN is used for personal or individual purposes, such as transferring files, printing documents, synchronizing data, etc.

- **Local Area Network (LAN)**: A LAN is a network that connects devices within a limited geographical area, such as a building, a campus, or a home. A LAN typically consists of multiple computers, servers, printers, routers, switches, etc. A LAN can use wired or wireless technologies, such as Ethernet, Wi-Fi, etc. A LAN is used for local or organizational purposes, such as sharing files, accessing the internet, hosting applications, etc.

- **Metropolitan Area Network (MAN)**: A MAN is a network that connects devices within a large metropolitan area, such as a city or a town. A MAN typically consists of multiple LANs, bridges, routers, etc. A MAN can use wired or wireless technologies, such as fiber optic, microwave, etc. A MAN is used for regional or public purposes, such as providing internet access, connecting government offices, broadcasting media, etc.

- **Wide Area Network (WAN)**: A WAN is a network that connects devices across a large geographical area, such as a country or a continent. A WAN typically consists of multiple MANs, routers, gateways, satellites, etc. A WAN can use wired or wireless technologies, such as telephone lines, cable, cellular, etc. A WAN is used for global or commercial purposes, such as connecting different branches of an organization, enabling e-commerce, supporting online gaming, etc.

- **Wireless Local Area Network (WLAN)**: A WLAN is a network that connects devices using wireless technologies, such as radio waves, infrared, etc. A WLAN typically consists of wireless access points, wireless routers, wireless adapters, etc. A WLAN can be a subset of a LAN or a MAN, or it can be a standalone network. A WLAN is used for wireless or mobile purposes, such as connecting laptops, tablets, smartphones, etc.

- **Storage Area Network (SAN)**: A SAN is a network that connects storage devices, such as hard disks, tape drives, optical disks, etc. A SAN typically consists of servers, switches, controllers, etc. A SAN can use wired or wireless technologies, such as SCSI, Fibre Channel, iSCSI, etc. A SAN is used for storage or backup purposes, such as storing large amounts of data, archiving data, recovering data, etc.

- **Virtual Private Network (VPN)**: A VPN is a network that connects devices using a secure and encrypted tunnel over a public network, such as the internet. A VPN typically consists of VPN servers, VPN clients, VPN protocols, etc. A VPN can use wired or wireless technologies, such as SSL, IPSec, PPTP, etc. A VPN is used for secure or remote purposes, such as accessing a private network, protecting data, bypassing censorship, etc.
