### World Wide Web

- The World Wide Web (WWW) is an information system that enables documents and other web resources to be accessed over the Internet.
- The WWW is not the same as the Internet, which is a global network of computers that can communicate with each other. The WWW is a service that operates over the Internet, just as email and Usenet do.
- The WWW consists of webpages that are linked by hyperlinks, which are references to other webpages or web resources. Users can navigate the WWW by clicking on hyperlinks or typing in web addresses (URLs) in web browsers.
- The WWW was invented by Tim Berners-Lee in 1989 at CERN, the European Organization for Nuclear Research. He proposed a system of hypertext documents that could be viewed by anyone on the Internet using a standard protocol called HTTP.
- The WWW gained rapid acceptance with the creation of a web browser called Mosaic, which was developed by Marc Andreessen and others at the National Center for Supercomputing Applications at the University of Illinois and was released in September 1993. Mosaic was the first graphical web browser that could display images and multimedia content along with text.
- The WWW has revolutionized the fields of communication, education, entertainment, commerce, and social networking. It has also raised issues of privacy, security, censorship, and digital divide.

Some mnemonics and learning tricks for the WWW are:

- WWW stands for World Wide Web, which can be remembered as W3 or 3W.
- WWW is a service that operates over the Internet, which can be remembered as WWW is a subset of the Internet, or WWW is inside the Internet.
- WWW consists of webpages that are linked by hyperlinks, which can be remembered as WWW is a web of links, or WWW is a network of pages.
- WWW was invented by Tim Berners-Lee, which can be remembered as WWW is TBL's baby, or WWW has three letters and TBL has three initials.
- WWW gained popularity with Mosaic, which can be remembered as WWW and Mosaic both start with M, or Mosaic made WWW more colorful.
