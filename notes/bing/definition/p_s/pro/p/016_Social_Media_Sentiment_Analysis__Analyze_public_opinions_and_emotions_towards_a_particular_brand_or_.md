Social Media Sentiment Analysis: Analyze public opinions and emotions towards a particular brand or product by using social media data. You can use libraries like Tweepy, TextBlob, and SentimentIntensityAnalyzer to make this project.

Social media sentiment analysis is the process of interpreting and determining whether the social media collected text data is positive, negative, or neutral. It can help you understand how your customers feel about your brand, product, or service based on their online posts and comments. To conduct a social media sentiment analysis, you need to:

- Keep track of your mentions. A mention is when a customer makes a social media post or message and names a specific company or business.
- Analyze the sentiment in the mentions. When analyzing the sentiment in the mentions, it's important to look for helpful keywords that can denote a positive or negative experience. You can also use libraries like Tweepy, TextBlob, and SentimentIntensityAnalyzer to automate this process using natural language processing techniques.
- Generate a report. A report can help you visualize and summarize the results of your analysis. You can use tools like Hootsuite Insights Powered by Brandwatch or Mentionlytics to create dashboards and charts that show the overall sentiment score, trends, topics, and influencers related to your brand.
