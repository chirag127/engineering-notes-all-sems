Human Pose Estimation is a way of identifying and classifying the joints in the human body from visual input data. It can be used for various applications such as personal trainers, robotics, motion capture, and more . To develop a system that can estimate the posture and movement of humans in real-time, you can use libraries like OpenCV, TensorFlow, and PyTorch to make this project. These libraries provide tools and frameworks for computer vision, deep learning, and image processing. You can also refer to some popular methods and papers on pose estimation such as OpenPose , HRNet, DeepCut, AlphaPose, etc.
