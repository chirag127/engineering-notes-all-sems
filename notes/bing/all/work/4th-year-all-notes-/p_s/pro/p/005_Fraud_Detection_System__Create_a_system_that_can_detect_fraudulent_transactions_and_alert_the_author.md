A fraud detection system is a software that monitors, investigates and blocks fraudulent activities on your website or other systems. Fraudulent activities are those that involve obtaining money or property through false means, such as using stolen cards or identities, hacking into accounts, creating fake invoices, etc.   

Some examples of fraud detection systems are:

- SEON: A granular, flexible and transparent fraud detection software that uses machine learning and data enrichment to detect and prevent various types of fraud across different industries. It can integrate with any platform and provide real-time risk scores and insights. 
- Signifyd: An automated fraud fighting software for merchants that uses artificial intelligence and a global network of data to guarantee every approved order against chargebacks. It can also handle customer verification, abuse prevention and revenue recovery. 
- Sift: A digital trust and safety platform that helps businesses protect themselves from fraudsters by using large-scale machine learning to analyze user behavior and device data. It can also automate workflows, optimize decisions and provide actionable insights. 

Some applications of fraud detection systems are:

- E-commerce: Fraud detection systems can help online retailers verify customers' identities, prevent chargebacks, reduce false positives and increase conversions. They can also help detect account takeover, refund abuse, promo abuse and loyalty fraud.  
- Banking: Fraud detection systems can help banks monitor transactions, detect anomalies, prevent identity theft, comply with regulations and enhance customer experience. They can also help detect credit card fraud, ATM fraud, wire transfer fraud and money laundering.  
- Insurance: Fraud detection systems can help insurance companies identify fraudulent claims, reduce losses, improve efficiency and increase customer satisfaction. They can also help detect policy application fraud, premium diversion fraud, claim padding fraud and staged accident fraud.  [5]

Some benefits of fraud detection systems are:

- Reduced costs: Fraud detection systems can help businesses save money by preventing losses due to fraudulent activities, reducing manual workloads and increasing operational efficiency.
- Increased revenue: Fraud detection systems can help businesses increase revenue by improving customer retention, loyalty and satisfaction; enhancing brand reputation; expanding into new markets; and optimizing pricing strategies.
- Improved security: Fraud detection systems can help businesses improve security by protecting sensitive data; complying with laws and regulations; preventing cyberattacks; mitigating risks; and resolving disputes.
