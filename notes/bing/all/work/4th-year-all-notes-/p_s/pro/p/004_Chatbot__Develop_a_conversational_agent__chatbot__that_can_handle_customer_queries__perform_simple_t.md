Chatbot: Develop a conversational agent (chatbot) that can handle customer queries, perform simple tasks and make recommendations. Technologies: Natural Language Processing (NLP), Python, Dialogflow, TensorFlow.

A chatbot is a computer program that uses artificial intelligence (AI) and natural language processing (NLP) to understand customer questions and automate responses to them, simulating human conversation . Chatbots can be embedded and used through any major messaging application, such as Facebook Messenger, WhatsApp, Telegram, etc. Some examples of chatbots are:

- Siri: A voice-based chatbot that can perform tasks such as setting reminders, making calls, playing music, etc. on Apple devices.
- Alexa: A voice-based chatbot that can control smart home devices, play games, order products, etc. on Amazon devices.
- Cortana: A voice-based chatbot that can help with web searches, calendar management, email sending, etc. on Microsoft devices.
- Watson Assistant: A text-based chatbot that can provide customer service, technical support, booking assistance, etc. for various businesses and organizations.

Chatbots have many applications and benefits for both customers and businesses. Some of them are:

- Customer satisfaction: Chatbots can provide 24/7 service, instant responses, personalized recommendations and solutions to customers' queries and problems.
- Cost reduction: Chatbots can reduce the need for human agents and save money on labor costs.
- Data collection: Chatbots can collect valuable data from customers' interactions and feedbacks and use it to improve their services or products.
- Brand awareness: Chatbots can increase brand visibility and engagement by reaching out to potential customers through various channels.

To develop a chatbot using Python , Dialogflow , TensorFlow , you need to follow these steps:

- Define the purpose and scope of your chatbot
- Design the conversation flow and user interface of your chatbot
- Choose a platform or framework to build your chatbot
- Create intents , entities , contexts , actions , parameters , fulfillment , etc. using Dialogflow
- Train a neural network model using TensorFlow to handle complex queries or tasks
- Test and deploy your chatbot on your chosen platform
