Fraud Detection: Develop a fraud detection system that can identify unusual patterns in financial transactions and alert authorities of potential fraud. Technologies: Python, Machine Learning, TensorFlow, Pandas, Numpy.

Fraud detection is the process of identifying and preventing fraudulent activities that can harm individuals, businesses or organizations. Fraud detection systems are software applications that use various techniques such as machine learning, data analysis, rule-based algorithms and anomaly detection to monitor transactions and events in real time and flag any suspicious or unusual patterns that may indicate fraud. Fraud detection systems can alert authorities or system administrators of potential fraud and provide them with tools to investigate and resolve the cases.

Some examples of fraud detection systems are:

- SEON, a granular, flexible and transparent fraud prevention platform that uses machine learning, device fingerprinting, IP analysis and behavioral biometrics to detect fraud across various industries such as e-commerce, gaming, banking and insurance.
- Signifyd, an automated fraud fighting solution for merchants that uses artificial intelligence, big data and a global network of experts to guarantee every approved order against chargebacks.
- Sift, a digital trust and safety platform that helps businesses prevent fraud, abuse and account takeover by using machine learning models that learn from billions of data points across industries.

Some applications of fraud detection systems are:

- Banking & Financial Services – Fraud detection systems are used in this industry to identify and prevent fraudulent activities such as money laundering, credit card fraud, identity theft, account takeover and loan fraud.
- E-commerce – Fraud detection systems are used in this industry to protect online merchants from fraudulent orders, chargebacks, payment fraud, coupon abuse and fake reviews.
- Healthcare – Fraud detection systems are used in this industry to detect and prevent healthcare fraud such as billing for services not rendered, upcoding or unbundling of services, duplicate claims and prescription drug fraud.

Some benefits of fraud detection systems are:

- Reduced losses – Fraud detection systems can help businesses save money by preventing fraudulent transactions from being completed or reversed.
- Improved customer satisfaction – Fraud detection systems can help businesses provide a better customer experience by reducing false positives (legitimate transactions being flagged as fraudulent), increasing approval rates (legitimate transactions being accepted) and enhancing security (protecting customer data from unauthorized access).
- Enhanced reputation – Fraud detection systems can help businesses build trust with their customers, partners and regulators by demonstrating their commitment to fighting fraud and complying with laws and regulations.
