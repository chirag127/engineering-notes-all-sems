A fraud detection system is a software that monitors, investigates and blocks fraudulent activities on your website or other systems. Fraudulent activities are those that involve obtaining money or property through false means, such as using stolen cards or identities  .

Some examples of fraud detection systems are:

- SEON: A granular, flexible and transparent fraud detection software that uses machine learning and data enrichment to detect and prevent various types of fraud, such as identity theft, account takeover, payment fraud and more.
- Signifyd: An automated fraud fighting software that uses artificial intelligence and a global network of merchants to protect online retailers from chargebacks, false positives and manual reviews.
- Sift: A fraud detection platform that leverages behavioral analytics, device fingerprinting and global trust data to help businesses reduce fraud losses and increase revenue.

Some applications of fraud detection systems are:

- E-commerce: Fraud detection systems can help online merchants verify customers' identities, prevent fraudulent transactions, reduce chargebacks and increase customer satisfaction .
- Banking: Fraud detection systems can help banks detect and prevent fraudulent transactions, such as money laundering, credit card fraud, ATM skimming and wire transfer fraud .
- Insurance: Fraud detection systems can help insurance companies identify and prevent fraudulent claims, such as staged accidents, inflated damages or fake injuries .

Some benefits of fraud detection systems are:

- Cost savings: Fraud detection systems can help businesses save money by reducing fraud losses, operational costs and legal fees associated with fraud investigations .
- Revenue growth: Fraud detection systems can help businesses increase revenue by improving customer trust, loyalty and retention; enhancing brand reputation; expanding into new markets; and optimizing pricing strategies .
- Risk mitigation: Fraud detection systems can help businesses mitigate risks by complying with regulatory requirements; avoiding fines and penalties; preventing data breaches; protecting intellectual property; and safeguarding customer privacy[^3 ^][ ^4 ^][ ^5 ^].
