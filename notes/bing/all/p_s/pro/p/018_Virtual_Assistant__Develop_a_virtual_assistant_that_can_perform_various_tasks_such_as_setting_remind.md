A virtual assistant is a device, app, or computer program that can respond to commands or questions and perform tasks electronically. It can also refer to a person who provides professional assistance with administrative or technical tasks while working remotely.

Some examples of virtual assistants are:

- Siri: A voice-controlled assistant that can perform tasks such as making phone calls, sending messages, setting reminders, playing music, and answering questions on Apple devices.
- Alexa: A voice-controlled assistant that can perform tasks such as playing music, controlling smart home devices, ordering products, setting alarms, and providing information on Amazon devices.
- Google Assistant: A voice-controlled assistant that can perform tasks such as searching the web, checking the weather, playing games, controlling smart home devices, and making reservations on Android devices and Google Home speakers.
- Cortana: A voice-controlled assistant that can perform tasks such as managing calendars, sending emails, setting reminders, and providing information on Windows devices and Microsoft apps.

Some applications of virtual assistants are:

- Personal use: Virtual assistants can help users with their daily activities such as managing schedules, booking travel arrangements, ordering food delivery, shopping online, and entertainment.
- Business use: Virtual assistants can help businesses with their administrative tasks such as scheduling meetings, sending invoices, organizing files,
researching data,
and customer service.

Some benefits of virtual assistants are:

- Convenience: Virtual assistants can provide users with instant access to information and services without requiring them to type or click anything.
- Efficiency: Virtual assistants can save users time and effort by performing multiple tasks at once or automating repetitive processes.
- Productivity: Virtual assistants can help users focus on their core activities by handling their secondary tasks or providing them with relevant suggestions.
- Accessibility: Virtual assistants can enable users to interact with their devices or services using natural language or voice commands.
