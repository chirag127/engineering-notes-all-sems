Chatbot: Develop a conversational agent (chatbot) that can handle customer queries, perform simple tasks and make recommendations. Technologies: Natural Language Processing (NLP), Python, Dialogflow, TensorFlow.

A chatbot is a software application that uses artificial intelligence (AI) and natural language processing (NLP) to understand customer questions and automate responses to them, simulating human conversation  . Chatbots can handle customer queries, perform simple tasks and make recommendations based on predefined rules or machine learning algorithms.

Some examples of chatbots are:

- Intercom: a chatbot that supports live chat, chat bots, and more to provide messenger-based experiences for prospects. Using machine learning and behavioral data, Intercom can answer up to 33% of queries and provide a personalized experience along the way.
- Watson Assistant: a chatbot that allows you to build conversational interfaces into any application, device or channel. Watson Assistant can understand natural language input and respond with relevant information or actions.
- ChatBot.com: a chatbot platform that lets you create custom chatbots for your website without coding. You can use templates, drag-and-drop elements and integrations with popular tools like Dialogflow and TensorFlow.

Some applications of chatbots are:

- Customer service: chatbots can provide instant answers to common questions, reduce wait times and improve customer satisfaction.
- E-commerce: chatbots can help customers find products, place orders, track deliveries and offer discounts or upsells.
- Education: chatbots can provide interactive learning experiences, quizzes, feedback and guidance for students.
- Entertainment: chatbots can create engaging stories, games, jokes and trivia for users.

Some benefits of chatbots are:

- Cost-efficiency: chatbots can reduce operational costs by automating repetitive tasks and scaling up without hiring more staff.
- Accessibility: chatbots can be available 24/7 across multiple channels and devices, reaching more customers and users.
- Personalization: chatbots can tailor their responses based on user preferences, behavior and context, creating more relevant interactions.
- Innovation: chatbots can leverage advanced technologies like NLP, AI and machine learning to provide new capabilities and features for users.
