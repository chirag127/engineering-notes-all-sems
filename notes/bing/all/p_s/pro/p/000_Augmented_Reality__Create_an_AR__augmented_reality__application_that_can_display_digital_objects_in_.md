Augmented Reality: Create an AR (augmented reality) application that can display digital objects in the real world and interact with them. Technologies: Unity, ARKit, ARCore, Vuforia, C#.

Augmented Reality (AR) is a technology that superimposes computer-generated content on a user's view of the real world, thus providing an enhanced and interactive experience  . The content can span multiple sensory modalities, such as visual, auditory, haptic, somatosensory and olfactory.

Some examples of AR applications are:

- Pokemon Go: A mobile game that allows players to catch virtual creatures called Pokemon in real-world locations using their smartphone's camera and GPS.
- IKEA Place: An app that lets users visualize how furniture items from IKEA would look like in their own homes by placing them on the floor or walls using their smartphone's camera.
- Snapchat Lenses: A feature that adds filters, stickers, animations and effects to users' selfies or videos using facial recognition and tracking algorithms.

Some applications of AR are:

- Education: AR can enhance learning outcomes by providing immersive and engaging experiences for students, such as exploring historical sites, anatomy models, chemical reactions and more.
- Healthcare: AR can assist medical professionals in diagnosis, surgery, training and patient care by providing relevant information and guidance overlaid on the patient's body or environment.
- Entertainment: AR can create new forms of entertainment and storytelling by blending digital content with reality, such as interactive games, movies, concerts and art exhibitions.

Some benefits of AR are:

- Increased engagement: AR can capture users' attention and interest by creating novel and fun experiences that stimulate their curiosity and imagination.
- Improved performance: AR can enhance users' skills and abilities by providing feedback, instructions and support that are tailored to their needs and goals.
- Reduced costs: AR can save time and money by reducing the need for physical materials, equipment and travel expenses that are otherwise required for certain tasks or activities.
