A chatbot is a computer program that uses artificial intelligence (AI) and natural language processing (NLP) to understand customer questions and automate responses to them, simulating human conversation . Chatbots can be embedded and used through any major messaging application, such as Facebook Messenger, WhatsApp, Telegram, etc.

Some examples of chatbots are:

- Siri: Apple's voice assistant that can answer questions, make calls, send messages, play music, etc.
- Alexa: Amazon's voice assistant that can control smart devices, order products, play games, etc.
- Cortana: Microsoft's voice assistant that can help with tasks, reminders, searches, etc.
- Watson Assistant: IBM's chatbot platform that can build conversational agents for various domains and industries.

Some applications of chatbots are:

- Customer service: Chatbots can provide 24/7 support to customers by answering FAQs, resolving issues, booking appointments, etc.
- E-commerce: Chatbots can help customers find products, make recommendations, process payments, track orders, etc.
- Education: Chatbots can assist students with learning materials, quizzes, feedbacks , etc.
- Entertainment: Chatbots can create engaging content such as stories , jokes , games , etc.

Some benefits of chatbots are:

- Cost-efficiency: Chatbots can reduce operational costs by handling multiple queries at once and reducing human labor .
- Customer satisfaction: Chatbots can improve customer experience by providing fast and accurate responses , personalization , and convenience .
- Data analysis: Chatbots can collect and analyze customer data to provide insights , trends , and feedbacks .
