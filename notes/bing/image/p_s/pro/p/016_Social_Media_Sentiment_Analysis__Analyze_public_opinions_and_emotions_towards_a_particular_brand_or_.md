Social media sentiment analysis is the process of interpreting and determining whether the social media collected text data is positive, negative, or neutral. It goes beyond just collecting and counting the number of mentions, comments, or hashtags. It can help businesses understand how customers feel about their products, services, or brands.

To conduct a social media sentiment analysis, you need to follow these steps:

- Keep track of your mentions. A mention is when a customer makes a social media post or message and names a specific company or business.
- Analyze the sentiment in the mentions. When analyzing the sentiment in the mentions, it's important to look for helpful keywords that can denote a positive or negative experience. You can also use libraries like Tweepy, TextBlob, and SentimentIntensityAnalyzer to make this easier.
- Generate a report. A report can help you visualize and summarize your findings. You can use charts, graphs, tables, or other tools to show the distribution of sentiments across different platforms, topics, or time periods.
