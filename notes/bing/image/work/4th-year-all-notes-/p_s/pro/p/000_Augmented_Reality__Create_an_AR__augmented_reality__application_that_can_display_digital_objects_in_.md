Augmented Reality: Create an AR (augmented reality) application that can display digital objects in the real world and interact with them. Technologies: Unity, ARKit, ARCore, Vuforia, C#.

Augmented reality (AR) is a technology that allows you to overlay digital objects and information on top of the real world. You can use AR to create immersive and interactive experiences for various purposes, such as education, entertainment, gaming, marketing, etc.

To create an AR application, you need to use a software platform that can handle the tasks of tracking, rendering, and interacting with the digital content. Some of the popular platforms are Unity, ARKit, ARCore, and Vuforia. You also need to use a programming language that can communicate with these platforms and control the logic of your application. One of the most common languages is C# .
