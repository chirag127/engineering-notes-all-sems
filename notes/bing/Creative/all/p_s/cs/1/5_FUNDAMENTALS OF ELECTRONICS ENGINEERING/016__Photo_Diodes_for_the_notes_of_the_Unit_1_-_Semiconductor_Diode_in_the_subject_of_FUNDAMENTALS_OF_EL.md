### Photo Diodes

- A photo diode is a light-sensitive semiconductor diode that produces current when it absorbs photons  .
- It is a special type of PN junction device that generates current when exposed to light.
- It is also known as a photo detector or a photo sensor .
- The package of a photo diode allows light (or infrared or ultraviolet radiation, or X-rays) to reach the sensitive part of the device. The package may include lenses or optical filters.
- A photo diode is a PIN structure or a p–n junction. When a photon of sufficient energy strikes the diode, it creates an electron – hole pair. This mechanism is also known as the inner photoelectric effect.
- A photo diode can operate in two modes: photo voltaic mode and photo conductive mode.
  - In photo voltaic mode, the photo diode is connected in a short circuit and no external voltage is applied. The photo current is proportional to the light intensity and flows in the opposite direction of the diode.
  - In photo conductive mode, the photo diode is connected in a reverse bias and an external voltage is applied. The photo current is proportional to the light intensity and flows in the same direction of the diode.
- A photo diode has many applications, such as optical communication, remote sensing, optical switching, solar cells, smoke detectors, etc .

Some possible mnemonics and learning tricks for the topic are:

- To remember the difference between photo voltaic and photo conductive modes, you can use the acronym VCR: Voltaic = Current Reverse, Conductive = Current Same.
- To remember the direction of the photo current in photo voltaic mode, you can use the phrase "Light makes the diode fight" (fight against the conventional current direction).
- To remember the direction of the photo current in photo conductive mode, you can use the phrase "Light makes the diode right" (right as in the same direction as the conventional current).
- To remember the types of photo diodes, you can use the acronym PAP: PIN, Avalanche, Phototransistor.
- To remember the characteristics of photo diodes, you can use the acronym RISE: Responsivity, Illumination, Sensitivity, Efficiency.
