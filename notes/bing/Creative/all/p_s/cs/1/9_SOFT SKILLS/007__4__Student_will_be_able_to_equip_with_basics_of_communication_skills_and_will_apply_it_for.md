### 4. Student will be able to equip with basics of communication skills and will apply it for

Communication skills are the abilities to convey and receive messages effectively in various contexts, such as verbal, nonverbal, written, and visual communication. Communication skills are essential for academic, personal, and professional success, as they help to build relationships, share ideas, solve problems, and persuade others.

Some of the basic communication skills that students should equip themselves with are:

- Listening: Listening is the ability to pay attention and understand what others are saying. Listening skills help to comprehend information, empathize with others, and respond appropriately. Listening skills can be improved by practicing active listening, which involves giving feedback, asking questions, and summarizing what was said.
- Speaking: Speaking is the ability to express oneself clearly and confidently in various situations, such as presentations, discussions, debates, and interviews. Speaking skills help to convey messages, opinions, and emotions effectively, as well as to persuade and influence others. Speaking skills can be improved by practicing pronunciation, intonation, vocabulary, and grammar, as well as by preparing and rehearsing the content and structure of the speech.
- Writing: Writing is the ability to produce coherent and accurate texts for different purposes, audiences, and formats, such as essays, reports, emails, and blogs. Writing skills help to communicate information, arguments, and perspectives in a structured and logical way, as well as to demonstrate knowledge and understanding of a topic. Writing skills can be improved by practicing spelling, punctuation, syntax, and style, as well as by planning, drafting, revising, and editing the text.
- Reading: Reading is the ability to comprehend and analyze various types of texts, such as books, articles, and websites. Reading skills help to acquire information, knowledge, and insights from different sources, as well as to evaluate and critique the arguments and evidence presented by the authors. Reading skills can be improved by practicing vocabulary, grammar, and comprehension, as well as by applying different reading strategies, such as skimming, scanning, and summarizing.
- Visual: Visual communication is the use of images, symbols, colors, and layouts to convey and interpret messages, such as graphs, charts, diagrams, and infographics. Visual communication skills help to present and organize data, information, and ideas in a clear and attractive way, as well as to enhance the impact and appeal of the message. Visual communication skills can be improved by practicing design principles, such as contrast, alignment, repetition, and proximity, as well as by using appropriate tools and software, such as PowerPoint, Photoshop, and Canva.

Students can apply these communication skills for various purposes, such as:

- Academic: Communication skills are vital for academic success, as they help students to learn, understand, and demonstrate their knowledge and skills in different subjects and disciplines. Communication skills also help students to participate in class activities, collaborate with peers and teachers, and seek feedback and guidance.
- Personal: Communication skills are important for personal development, as they help students to express themselves, build self-confidence, and develop their identity and values. Communication skills also help students to form and maintain relationships, cope with emotions, and deal with conflicts and challenges.
- Professional: Communication skills are essential for professional growth, as they help students to prepare for and perform in various career opportunities, such as internships, jobs, and entrepreneurship. Communication skills also help students to network with potential employers, colleagues, and clients, and to adapt to different work environments and cultures.

- To remember the four basic communication skills, you can use the acronym **LWRV**, which stands for **Listening, Writing, Reading, and Visual** communication.
- To remember the five design principles for visual communication, you can use the acronym **CARP**, which stands for **Contrast, Alignment, Repetition, and Proximity**.
- To remember the order of operations in algebra, you can use the acrostic **PEMDAS**, which stands for **Parentheses, Exponents, Multiplication, Division, Addition, and Subtraction**.
