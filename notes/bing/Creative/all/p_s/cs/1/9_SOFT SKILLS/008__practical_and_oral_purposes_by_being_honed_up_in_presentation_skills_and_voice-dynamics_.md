### Practical and oral purposes by being honed up in presentation skills and voice-dynamics

Presentation skills and voice-dynamics are essential for effective oral communication, which is an important learning skill and a professional asset. Here are some points to consider when honing up these skills:

- Presentation skills are the ability to use your voice confidently to communicate in "live" situations, such as delivering information verbally and "physically", engaging your audience, and thinking on your feet.
- Voice-dynamics are the aspects of vocal delivery that can enhance or detract from your message, such as rate, volume, pitch, articulation, pronunciation, and fluency.
- To improve your presentation skills, you should:
  - Know your purpose, audience, and message, and tailor your presentation accordingly.
  - Plan and structure your presentation logically, with a clear introduction, body, and conclusion.
  - Use visual aids, such as slides, charts, graphs, or images, to support your message and make it more memorable.
  - Practice your presentation several times, preferably in front of a mirror, a friend, or a video camera, and get feedback on your performance.
  - Use imagery to help keep calm, and visualize yourself delivering a successful presentation.
  - Learn strategies to build your self-confidence in general, such as affirming your strengths, setting realistic goals, and celebrating your achievements.
- To improve your voice-dynamics, you should:
  - Practise breathing deeply to fuel your voice, and avoid speaking from your throat or chest.
  - Vary your rate, volume, and pitch to create interest and emphasis, and avoid monotony and boredom.
  - Articulate every word clearly, and avoid mumbling, slurring, or dropping syllables.
  - Pronounce every word correctly, and avoid mispronouncing, misusing, or inventing words.
  - Speak fluently, and avoid hesitating, repeating, or using filler words, such as "um", "like", or "you know".
  - Think of some tongue-twisters and say them out loud, taking care to articulate every word.
  - Listen to good speakers and observe how they use their voice effectively.
  - Record yourself speaking and listen to your voice critically, and identify areas for improvement.

Improving your presentation skills and voice-dynamics will help you achieve practical and oral purposes, such as:

- Informing, persuading, or entertaining your audience with your message.
- Demonstrating your knowledge, competence, and credibility on a topic.
- Building rapport and trust with your audience, and inviting their feedback and participation.
- Enhancing your personal and professional image, and creating a positive impression.
- Developing your critical thinking, problem-solving, and decision-making skills.
- Expanding your network and opportunities for collaboration and career advancement.
