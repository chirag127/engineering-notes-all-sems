### 2. Students will be able to converse well with effective speaking and listening skills in English.

- Speaking and listening skills are essential for effective communication in any language, especially in English, which is widely used as a global language.
- Speaking skills involve the ability to produce clear, coherent, and appropriate speech in various contexts and purposes, such as expressing opinions, giving instructions, making requests, etc.
- Listening skills involve the ability to comprehend and respond to spoken messages in various contexts and purposes, such as following directions, taking notes, summarizing, etc.
- To converse well with effective speaking and listening skills in English, students need to:

  - Develop their vocabulary, grammar, pronunciation, and fluency in English.
  - Use appropriate strategies and techniques for speaking and listening, such as planning, organizing, rehearsing, monitoring, clarifying, paraphrasing, etc.
  - Be aware of the cultural and social norms and expectations of different situations and audiences, such as formal and informal, polite and impolite, etc.
  - Practice speaking and listening in authentic and meaningful contexts and tasks, such as dialogues, presentations, discussions, debates, etc.
  - Seek and provide feedback on their own and others' speaking and listening performance, such as strengths, weaknesses, areas for improvement, etc.
  - Reflect on their own and others' speaking and listening process and outcome, such as goals, strategies, challenges, achievements, etc.

- Some of the benefits of conversing well with effective speaking and listening skills in English are:

  - It enhances the students' confidence and competence in using English as a medium of communication.
  - It fosters the students' critical thinking and problem-solving skills, as they have to analyze, evaluate, and synthesize information from various sources and perspectives.
  - It promotes the students' interpersonal and intercultural skills, as they have to interact and collaborate with diverse people and cultures.
  - It prepares the students for academic and professional success, as they have to communicate effectively in various disciplines and domains.
