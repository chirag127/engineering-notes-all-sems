### 5. Students will be able build up personal traits that will make the transition from institution

The transition from institution to the workplace or further education can be challenging for many students. They need to develop personal traits that will help them cope with the changes and demands of their new environment. Some of the personal traits that students can build up are:

- **Self-awareness**: This is the ability to recognize one's own strengths, weaknesses, emotions, values, and goals. Self-awareness helps students to identify their interests, passions, and career aspirations, as well as their areas of improvement and learning needs. Self-awareness also helps students to manage their emotions, cope with stress, and communicate effectively with others.
- **Self-regulation**: This is the ability to control one's impulses, emotions, and behaviors. Self-regulation helps students to set realistic and attainable goals, plan and prioritize their tasks, monitor their progress, and adjust their strategies when needed. Self-regulation also helps students to deal with challenges, setbacks, and feedback, and to learn from their mistakes.
- **Self-motivation**: This is the ability to initiate and sustain one's own learning and work. Self-motivation helps students to pursue their goals with enthusiasm, curiosity, and perseverance, and to overcome obstacles and distractions. Self-motivation also helps students to seek and use feedback, resources, and opportunities for learning and improvement.
- **Self-efficacy**: This is the belief in one's own ability to succeed in a given task or situation. Self-efficacy helps students to have confidence in their skills and knowledge, and to take on challenges and risks. Self-efficacy also helps students to cope with failure and criticism, and to learn from their experiences.
- **Self-respect**: This is the respect and appreciation for oneself and one's dignity. Self-respect helps students to value their own worth, opinions, and rights, and to assert them in a respectful and constructive way. Self-respect also helps students to respect and appreciate the diversity and differences of others, and to avoid or resolve conflicts peacefully.
