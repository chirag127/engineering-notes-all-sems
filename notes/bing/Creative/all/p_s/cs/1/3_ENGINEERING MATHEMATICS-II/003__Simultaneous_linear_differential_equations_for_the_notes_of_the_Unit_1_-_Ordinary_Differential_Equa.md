### Simultaneous linear differential equations

- Simultaneous linear differential equations are a system of two or more linear differential equations that involve the same set of dependent and independent variables.
- A linear differential equation is an equation that can be written in the form

  `a_0(x)y + a_1(x)y' + a_2(x)y'' + ... + a_n(x)y^(n) = f(x)`

  where `y` is the dependent variable, `x` is the independent variable, `y', y'', ... , y^(n)` are the derivatives of `y` with respect to `x`, and `a_0(x), a_1(x), ... , a_n(x)` and `f(x)` are functions of `x` only.
- A solution of a linear differential equation is a function `y = y(x)` that satisfies the equation for all values of `x` in a given interval.
- A solution of a system of simultaneous linear differential equations is a set of functions `y_1 = y_1(x), y_2 = y_2(x), ... , y_m = y_m(x)` that satisfy all the equations in the system for all values of `x` in a common interval.
- There are different methods to solve simultaneous linear differential equations, depending on the form and the number of the equations. Some of the common methods are:

  - Elimination method: This method involves eliminating one or more variables from the system by adding or subtracting multiples of the equations, until a single equation in one variable is obtained. Then, the equation is solved for that variable, and the other variables are found by back-substitution.
  - Substitution method: This method involves expressing one variable in terms of another variable from one of the equations, and then substituting it into the other equations, until a single equation in one variable is obtained. Then, the equation is solved for that variable, and the other variables are found by back-substitution.
  - Matrix method: This method involves writing the system of equations in matrix form, and then using matrix operations to find the inverse or the determinant of the coefficient matrix, and then using them to find the solution vector.
  - Variation of parameters method: This method involves finding a particular solution of the system by assuming that the solution vector is a linear combination of some functions, and then finding the coefficients of the linear combination by solving a system of algebraic equations.
  - Undetermined coefficients method: This method involves finding a particular solution of the system by assuming that the solution vector has the same form as the right-hand side of the system, and then finding the coefficients of the solution vector by equating the coefficients of the corresponding terms in the system.

- Simultaneous linear differential equations have many applications in physics, engineering, chemistry, biology, economics, and other fields. Some examples are:

  - Modeling the motion of a mass-spring system with two masses and two springs
  - Modeling the electric current in a circuit with two resistors and two capacitors
  - Modeling the spread of a disease in a population with two groups of people
  - Modeling the growth of two competing species in an ecosystem
  - Modeling the supply and demand of two goods in a market

Some possible mnemonics and learning tricks for the topic are:

- To remember the order of operations for matrix multiplication, use the acronym **PEMDAS** (Parentheses, Exponents, Multiplication, Division, Addition, Subtraction).
- To remember the formula for the determinant of a 2x2 matrix, use the rhyme **cross the top, cross the bottom, subtract the two**.
- To remember the formula for the inverse of a 2x2 matrix, use the rhyme **switch the corners, change the signs, divide by the determinant**.
- To remember the method of variation of parameters, use the acronym **W** (Write the complementary solution, Write the Wronskian, Write the particular solution, Write the general solution).
- To remember the method of undetermined coefficients, use the acronym **A** (Assume the form of the solution, Apply the differential operator, Adjust the coefficients, Add the complementary solution).
