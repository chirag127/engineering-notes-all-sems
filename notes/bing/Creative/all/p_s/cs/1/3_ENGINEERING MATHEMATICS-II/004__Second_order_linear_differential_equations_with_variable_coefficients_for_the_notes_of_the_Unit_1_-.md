### Second order linear differential equations with variable coefficients

- A second order linear differential equation is an equation of the form

  `y'' + p(x)y' + q(x)y = f(x)`

  where `p(x)`, `q(x)` and `f(x)` are functions of the independent variable `x`.

- A second order linear differential equation is said to have variable coefficients if `p(x)` and `q(x)` are not constant functions.

- The general solution of a second order linear differential equation with variable coefficients is given by

  `y = c1y1 + c2y2 + yp`

  where `c1` and `c2` are arbitrary constants, `y1` and `y2` are two linearly independent solutions of the homogeneous equation

  `y'' + p(x)y' + q(x)y = 0`

  and `yp` is a particular solution of the non-homogeneous equation

  `y'' + p(x)y' + q(x)y = f(x)`

- To find the general solution, we need to find `y1` and `y2` by using one of the methods such as reduction of order, variation of parameters, power series, etc. and find `yp` by using one of the methods such as undetermined coefficients, variation of parameters, method of annihilators, etc.

- The second order linear differential equations with variable coefficients have many applications in physics, engineering, biology, etc. For example, the equation

  `y'' + xy' + y = 0`

  models the oscillations of a spring-mass system with a variable damping coefficient.

Some possible mnemonics and learning tricks for the topic are:

- To remember the general form of a second order linear differential equation, you can use the acronym **PYQF** (pronounced like "pique") which stands for **P**rime, **Y** (the dependent variable), **Q**uotient and **F**unction.

- To remember the general solution of a homogeneous equation, you can use the acronym **CYY** (pronounced like "see why") which stands for **C**onstant times **Y** (the dependent variable) times **Y** (the independent variable).

- To remember the general solution of a non-homogeneous equation, you can use the acronym **CYY + YP** (pronounced like "see why why plus why pee") which stands for **C**onstant times **Y** (the dependent variable) times **Y** (the independent variable) plus **Y** (the dependent variable) **P**articular.

- To remember the method of undetermined coefficients, you can use the acronym **GUESS** (pronounced like "guess") which stands for **G**uess a form of the particular solution, **U**se the differential equation to find the unknown coefficients, **E**valuate the particular solution, **S**ubstitute the particular solution into the general solution, and **S**implify the final answer.

- To remember the method of variation of parameters, you can use the acronym **VOP** (pronounced like "vope") which stands for **V**ary the parameters of the homogeneous solution, **O**btain a system of equations for the new parameters, and **P**roduce the particular solution by integrating the system of equations.
