- The comparison test is a method to determine the convergence or divergence of a series by comparing it with another series whose convergence properties are known.
- The comparison test can be applied to series with positive terms only. If a series has negative terms, it can be converted to a positive series by taking the absolute value of the terms.
- The comparison test can be used with geometric series and p-series, which are two common types of series whose convergence properties are well-known.
- A geometric series is a series of the form \\(\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}ar^{n-1}\\), where \\(a\\) and \\(r\\) are constants. A geometric series converges if \\(|r|<1\\) and diverges otherwise.
- A p-series is a series of the form \\(\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{1}{n^p}\\), where \\(p\\) is a constant. A p-series converges if \\(p>1\\) and diverges otherwise.
- The comparison test can be stated as follows:

  - If \\(0\leq a_n\leq b_n\\) for all \\(n\\) and \\(\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}b_n\\) converges, then \\(\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}a_n\\) also converges.
