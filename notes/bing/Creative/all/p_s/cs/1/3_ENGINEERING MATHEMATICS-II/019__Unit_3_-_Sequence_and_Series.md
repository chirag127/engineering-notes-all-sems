## Unit 3 - Sequence and Series

- A **sequence** is an ordered list of numbers or objects that follow a certain rule or pattern. For example, 1, 3, 5, 7, ... is a sequence of odd numbers.
- A **series** is the sum of the terms of a sequence. For example, 1 + 3 + 5 + 7 + ... is a series of odd numbers.
- Sequences and series are useful in mathematics and other fields to model patterns, growth, decay, approximation, etc.
- There are different types of sequences and series, such as arithmetic, geometric, harmonic, Fibonacci, etc. Each type has its own formula, properties, and applications.
- Some sequences and series have a finite number of terms, while others are infinite. For example, 1, 2, 3, 4 is a finite sequence with four terms, while 1, 2, 4, 8, ... is an infinite sequence that doubles each term.
- Some series have a finite sum, while others are divergent. For example, 1 + 2 + 3 + 4 = 10 is a finite series with a sum of 10, while 1 + 2 + 4 + 8 + ... is a divergent series that has no finite sum.
- To find the sum of a finite series, we can use various methods, such as adding the terms directly, using a formula, or applying a technique called partial sums.
- To find the sum of an infinite series, we can use various methods, such as finding a limit, using a formula, or applying a technique called convergence tests.
- Some examples of sequences and series are:

  - The sequence of natural numbers: 1, 2, 3, 4, ...
  - The series of natural numbers: 1 + 2 + 3 + 4 + ...
  - The sequence of even numbers: 2, 4, 6, 8, ...
  - The series of even numbers: 2 + 4 + 6 + 8 + ...
  - The sequence of squares: 1, 4, 9, 16, ...
  - The series of squares: 1 + 4 + 9 + 16 + ...
  - The sequence of powers of 2: 1, 2, 4, 8, ...
  - The series of powers of 2: 1 + 2 + 4 + 8 + ...
  - The sequence of Fibonacci numbers: 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, ...
  - The series of Fibonacci numbers: 1 + 1 + 2 + 3 + 5 + 8 + ...
  - The sequence of harmonic numbers: 1, 1/2, 1/3, 1/4, ...
  - The series of harmonic numbers: 1 + 1/2 + 1/3 + 1/4 + ...
  - The sequence of alternating signs: 1, -1, 1, -1, ...
  - The series of alternating signs: 1 - 1 + 1 - 1 + ...

Some possible mnemonics and learning tricks for the topic are:

- To remember the difference between a sequence and a series, think of a sequence as a queue of numbers and a series as a sum of numbers.
- To remember the formula for the sum of an arithmetic series, think of the acronym SALT: Sum = Average x Length x Two.
- To remember the formula for the sum of a geometric series, think of the acronym SIR: Sum = Initial x Ratio^(n-1).
- To remember the formula for the nth term of a Fibonacci sequence, think of the acronym FIB: F_n = (phi^n - psi^n) / sqrt(5), where phi and psi are the golden ratio and its conjugate.
- To remember the formula for the nth harmonic number, think of the acronym HEN: H_n = gamma + ln(n), where gamma is the Euler-Mascheroni constant.
- To remember the convergence tests for infinite series, think of the acronym LACT: Limit, Alternating, Comparison, and Test for Divergence.
