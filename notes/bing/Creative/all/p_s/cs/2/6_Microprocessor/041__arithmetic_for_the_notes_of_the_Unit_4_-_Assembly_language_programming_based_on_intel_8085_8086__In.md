### Arithmetic

Arithmetic instructions are used to perform basic mathematical operations on data, such as addition, subtraction, increment, decrement, multiplication, and division. The data can be in registers, memory locations, or immediate operands. The result of the arithmetic operation is usually stored in a register or a memory location, and may affect the flags register.

For example, `ADC AX, BX` adds the contents of BX, the carry flag, and AX and stores the result in AX. `ADC AL, 10H` adds the immediate data 10H, the carry flag, and AL and stores the result in AL.
- **SUB**: This instruction subtracts the source operand from the destination operand and stores the result in the destination operand.
