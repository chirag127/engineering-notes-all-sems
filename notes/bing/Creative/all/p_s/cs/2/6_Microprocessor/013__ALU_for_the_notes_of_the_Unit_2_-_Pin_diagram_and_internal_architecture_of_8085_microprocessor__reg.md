### ALU

- ALU stands for Arithmetic and Logic Unit. It is one of the functional units of the 8085 microprocessor  .
- It is an 8-bit register that performs arithmetic, logical, I/O and load/store operations.
- It is connected to the internal data bus and the accumulator.
- It can perform 8-bit operations such as addition, subtraction, increment, decrement, logical AND, OR, XOR, complement, rotate, compare and decimal adjust  .
- It can also perform 16-bit operations such as addition and subtraction with carry.
- It can generate flags such as sign, zero, parity, carry and auxiliary carry based on the result of the operation .
- It can also perform branch operations based on the flags.
- It is controlled by the control and status unit, which generates the appropriate signals to select the operation and the operands .
- It is implemented using combinational logic circuits and multiplexors.
