# KCS

KCS stands for Knowledge-Centered Service, a methodology that aims to integrate knowledge management into service delivery processes. KCS is based on the following principles:

- Knowledge is a by-product of solving problems.
- Knowledge should be captured, structured, and reused as close to the source of interaction as possible.
- Knowledge should evolve with demand and usage.
- Knowledge should be searchable and accessible by anyone who needs it.
- Knowledge should be measured by its impact and value, not by its volume or completeness.

Some of the benefits of adopting KCS are:

- Improved customer satisfaction and loyalty, as customers can find answers faster and more easily.
- Reduced operational costs and increased efficiency, as service agents can leverage existing knowledge and avoid duplication of work.
- Enhanced employee engagement and retention, as service agents can share their expertise and learn from others.
- Accelerated organizational learning and innovation, as knowledge is continuously updated and improved based on feedback and usage.

To implement KCS, organizations need to follow a set of practices and standards that cover four areas:

- Content: how to create, structure, and maintain knowledge articles that are findable, usable, and reusable.
- Process: how to integrate knowledge creation and reuse into the service workflow, and how to measure and improve knowledge quality and performance.
- People: how to motivate and reward service agents for participating in knowledge activities, and how to develop their skills and competencies.
- Technology: how to support knowledge processes with appropriate tools and systems, and how to enable knowledge access and collaboration across channels and platforms.
