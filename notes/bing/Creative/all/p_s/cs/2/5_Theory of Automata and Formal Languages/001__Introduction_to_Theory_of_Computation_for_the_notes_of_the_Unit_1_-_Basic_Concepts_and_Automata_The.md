### Introduction to Theory of Computation

- Theory of computation (TOC) is a branch of computer science that is concerned with how problems can be solved using algorithms and how efficiently they can be solved.
- TOC studies the mathematical models of computation, such as abstract machines, formal languages, computability, and complexity .
- TOC can be divided into three subareas: complexity theory, computability theory, and automata theory .
- Complexity theory deals with the classification of problems according to their inherent difficulty and the resources required to solve them, such as time and space.
- Computability theory deals with the limits of what can be computed and the existence of undecidable and uncomputable problems.
- Automata theory deals with the study of abstract machines that can recognize and generate formal languages, such as finite automata, pushdown automata, and Turing machines.
- TOC has applications in various fields, such as cryptography, artificial intelligence, compiler design, verification, logic, and programming languages .
