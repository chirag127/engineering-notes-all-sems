### Soft Real Time Systems

- A soft real time system is a system that can tolerate some degree of deadline misses without causing catastrophic consequences    .
- A soft real time system can still provide useful results even if they are delivered after the deadline  .
- The quality of service of a soft real time system may degrade with increasing tardiness, but the system can continue to function  .
- A soft real time system may have a small window of time for program completion rather than a precise moment due to some jitter from the operating system.
- Examples of soft real time systems are multimedia systems, digital audio systems, telephone switches, etc   .
- A soft real time system may use a soft real time operating system, which is an operating system that provides some relaxation in the time limit and does not guarantee strict deadlines.
- A soft real time system may have different levels of criticality for different tasks, and may use different scheduling algorithms to prioritize them.
- A soft real time system may have to deal with trade-offs between timeliness, accuracy, and resource utilization.
- A soft real time system may have to cope with uncertainties and unpredictabilities in the system behavior and the environment.
