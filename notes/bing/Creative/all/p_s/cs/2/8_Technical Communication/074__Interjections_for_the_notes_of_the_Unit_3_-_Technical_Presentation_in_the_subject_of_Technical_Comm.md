### Interjections

- An interjection is a word or phrase that expresses a sudden or strong feeling, such as surprise, pain, joy, anger, etc.
- Interjections are usually placed at the beginning or end of a sentence, and are often followed by an exclamation mark (!) or a comma (,).
- Interjections are not grammatically connected to the rest of the sentence, and do not modify or get modified by other words.
- Interjections are common in everyday speech and informal writing, but they are generally avoided in formal or technical communication, as they can convey hesitation, emotion, or bias, which may not be appropriate for the purpose or audience of the message.
- Some examples of interjections are:

  - Wow! That's amazing!
  - Ouch! That hurts!
  - Hey, stop that!
  - Well, I don't know.
  - Umm, let me think.

- Interjections can be classified into different types, depending on their function or meaning. Some of the common types are:

  - Greeting interjections: These are used to greet or welcome someone, such as hello, hi, hey, etc.
  - Response interjections: These are used to respond or react to something, such as yes, no, okay, sure, etc.
  - Filler interjections: These are used to fill pauses or gaps in speech, such as umm, err, hmm, etc.
  - Attention-getting interjections: These are used to attract or direct attention, such as hey, look, listen, etc.
  - Agreement interjections: These are used to express agreement or approval, such as yeah, right, exactly, etc.
  - Disagreement interjections: These are used to express disagreement or disapproval, such as no, wrong, nonsense, etc.
  - Surprise interjections: These are used to express surprise or astonishment, such as wow, whoa, gosh, etc.
  - Pain interjections: These are used to express pain or discomfort, such as ouch, ow, ouch, etc.
  - Joy interjections: These are used to express joy or happiness, such as yay, hooray, woohoo, etc.
  - Anger interjections: These are used to express anger or frustration, such as ugh, argh, grr, etc.
  - Fear interjections: These are used to express fear or anxiety, such as eek, yikes, oh no, etc.
  - Disgust interjections: These are used to express disgust or dislike, such as eww, yuck, gross, etc.
