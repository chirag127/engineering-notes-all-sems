### Sentences for Technical Communication

- A sentence is a group of words that expresses a complete thought. A sentence has two main parts: a subject and a predicate. The subject is the person, place, thing, or idea that the sentence is about. The predicate is the part of the sentence that tells something about the subject or shows the action of the subject.
- In technical communication, sentences should be clear, concise, coherent, and correct. Clear sentences are easy to understand and avoid ambiguity or confusion. Concise sentences are brief and to the point, without unnecessary words or details. Coherent sentences are logically connected and consistent with the purpose and tone of the document. Correct sentences follow the rules of grammar, punctuation, spelling, and style.
- Some examples of technical communication sentences are:

  - Communicating about technical or specialized topics, such as computer applications, medical procedures, or environmental regulations:

    - To create a new document in Microsoft Word, click the File tab and select New from the menu.
    - Before undergoing a colonoscopy, you need to follow a special diet and take a laxative to clear your bowel.
    - According to the Clean Air Act, all major sources of air pollution must obtain a permit from the Environmental Protection Agency.

  - Communicating by using technology, such as web pages, help files, or social media sites:

    - For more information about our products and services, please visit our website at www.example.com.
    - If you encounter any problems while using this software, refer to the online help file or contact our technical support team.
    - Follow us on Twitter and Facebook to get the latest updates and news from our company.

  - Communicating for performance appraisal, such as giving feedback, praise, or criticism:

    - He shows a high level of team spirit. He readily cooperates with his team members to get the job done.
    - She is very good at encouraging other members to do the job according to their best efforts.
    - He needs to improve his technical skills and knowledge. He often makes errors and misses deadlines.
