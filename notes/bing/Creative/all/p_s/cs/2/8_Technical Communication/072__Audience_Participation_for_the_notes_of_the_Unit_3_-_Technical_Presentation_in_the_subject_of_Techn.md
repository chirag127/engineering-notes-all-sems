### Audience Participation

Audience participation is the involvement of the audience in a presentation, such as by asking questions, giving feedback, sharing opinions, or taking part in activities. Audience participation can make a presentation more engaging, interactive, and persuasive, as well as help the speaker to assess the audience's understanding, interest, and attitude. However, audience participation also requires careful planning, preparation, and execution, as well as the ability to handle unexpected situations and responses. Here are some points to consider when designing and delivering a presentation that involves audience participation:

- **Schedule audience participation into your presentation.** Decide when, how, and why you want to interact with the audience, and allocate enough time for it. For example, you can start with a simple question to break the ice, use a poll or a quiz to check the audience's knowledge or opinion, or invite questions or comments at the end of each section or the whole presentation. Make sure that the audience participation is relevant to your topic, purpose, and objectives, and that it supports your main message and arguments.
- **Help people participate during your presentation.** Encourage the audience to interact with you by using clear and direct instructions, positive and respectful language, and nonverbal cues such as eye contact, gestures, and facial expressions. For example, you can say "Please raise your hand if you agree with this statement" or "I would love to hear your thoughts on this issue". You can also use incentives such as prizes, rewards, or recognition to motivate the audience to participate. For example, you can say "The first person to answer this question correctly will get a free copy of my book" or "Let's give a round of applause to our volunteer".
- **Think about attendee engagement as “Googling the audience”.** Use audience participation as an opportunity to learn more about your audience, such as their background, needs, expectations, preferences, or challenges. For example, you can ask the audience to introduce themselves, share their experiences, or express their opinions. You can also use audience participation as a way to tailor your presentation to the audience, such as by adjusting your level of technicality, providing examples or analogies, or addressing their concerns or objections.
- **Create a feedback loop.** Use audience participation as a way to get feedback from the audience, such as by asking them to rate your presentation, give suggestions for improvement, or ask questions. You can also use audience participation as a way to give feedback to the audience, such as by acknowledging their contributions, answering their questions, or summarizing their key points. For example, you can say "Thank you for your comment, that's a very interesting perspective" or "That's a great question, let me explain". You can also use feedback to reinforce your main message and arguments, such as by saying "As you can see from the poll results, most of you agree with me that..." or "As we discussed earlier, the main benefit of this solution is...".
- **Consider Live Polling, Interactive Q&A or Interactive Trivia.** These are some of the tools and techniques that you can use to create audience participation in your presentation, especially if you are using a digital platform or device. Live polling allows you to ask the audience multiple-choice or open-ended questions and display the results in real time. Interactive Q&A allows you to invite the audience to ask questions and answer them live or later. Interactive trivia allows you to test the audience's knowledge or memory with fun and challenging questions. These tools and techniques can help you to create a more dynamic, interactive, and engaging presentation, as well as to collect data and insights from the audience.

Here are some mnemonics and learning tricks for the topic of audience participation:

- To remember the benefits of audience participation, you can use the acronym **PIE**: Participation Increases Engagement. Audience participation can help you to increase the engagement, interest, and attention of the audience, as well as to create a positive and memorable impression.
- To remember the types of audience participation, you can use the acronym **FIRE**: Feedback, Interaction, Research, and Entertainment. Audience participation can help you to get feedback from the audience, interact with them, research their needs and preferences, and entertain them with fun and interactive activities.
- To remember the steps to plan and prepare for audience participation, you can use the acronym **SMART**: Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, and Timely. You should have specific and clear objectives for audience participation, measure and evaluate its effectiveness, make sure it is achievable and realistic, ensure it is relevant and aligned with your topic and purpose, and allocate enough time for it.
- To remember the tips to execute and manage audience participation, you can use the acronym **CARE**: Communicate, Appreciate, Respond, and Evaluate. You should communicate clearly and respectfully with the audience, appreciate and acknowledge their contributions, respond to their questions and comments, and evaluate their feedback and participation.
