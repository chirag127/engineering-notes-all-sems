### Audience Analysis for the notes of the Unit 3 - Technical Presentation in the subject of Technical Communication

- Audience analysis is the process of gathering and interpreting information about the audience of a technical report or presentation .
- Audience analysis helps the technical communicator to plan, write, and deliver the message in a way that meets the needs, expectations, and interests of the audience  .
- Audience analysis involves considering the following aspects of the audience :
  - Demographics: the characteristics of the audience, such as age, gender, education, occupation, culture, etc.
  - Prior knowledge: the level of understanding and familiarity of the audience with the topic, terminology, concepts, etc.
  - Attitude: the feelings, opinions, and beliefs of the audience towards the topic, the speaker, and the purpose of the communication
  - Context: the situation, environment, and occasion in which the communication takes place
  - Expectations: the goals, needs, and preferences of the audience for the communication
- Audience analysis can be conducted in different ways, such as :
  - Direct methods: involving interaction with the audience, such as surveys, interviews, focus groups, etc.
  - Indirect methods: involving observation or inference of the audience, such as analysis of existing data, documents, feedback, etc.
  - Adaptive methods: involving adjustment of the communication during the delivery, such as feedback, questions, cues, etc.
- Audience analysis can help the technical communicator to  :
  - Select and organize the content: by choosing the most relevant, accurate, and clear information for the audience
  - Design and format the document or presentation: by using the appropriate layout, style, visuals, and media for the audience
  - Use the language and tone: by adopting the suitable level of formality, technicality, and politeness for the audience
  - Establish the purpose and ethos: by stating the main idea, objective, and credibility of the communication
  - Engage and persuade the audience: by appealing to their interests, values, and emotions
- Audience analysis is an essential skill for technical communication, as it can improve the effectiveness, efficiency, and satisfaction of the communication  .

Some possible mnemonics and learning tricks for the topic are:

- To remember the aspects of the audience, use the acronym DPACE: Demographics, Prior knowledge, Attitude, Context, Expectations.
- To remember the methods of audience analysis, use the acronym DIA: Direct, Indirect, Adaptive.
- To remember the benefits of audience analysis, use the acronym SEDUCE: Select, Design, Use, Establish, Engage.
