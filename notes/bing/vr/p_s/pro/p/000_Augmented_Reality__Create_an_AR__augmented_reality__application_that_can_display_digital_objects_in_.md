Augmented Reality: Create an AR (augmented reality) application that can display digital objects in the real world and interact with them. Technologies: Unity, ARKit, ARCore, Vuforia, C#.

Augmented reality (AR) is a technology that overlays digital information or objects onto the real world, enhancing the user's perception and interaction. AR can be used for various purposes, such as entertainment, education, business, art, and more.

A visual representation for an AR application that can display digital objects in the real world and interact with them could look something like this:

```markdown
- A smartphone screen showing a camera view of a room with a couch and a coffee table.
- A menu on the bottom of the screen with icons for different digital objects, such as a lamp, a plant, a book, etc.
- The user taps on the lamp icon and drags it onto the coffee table on the screen.
- The screen shows a 3D model of a lamp on the coffee table that matches the lighting and perspective of the room.
- The user can pinch to zoom or rotate to adjust the size and orientation of the lamp.
- The user can tap on the lamp to turn it on or off or change its color.
```

To create such an AR application, you would need to use some technologies such as Unity (a game engine), ARKit (an iOS framework for AR), ARCore (an Android framework for AR), Vuforia (a platform for image recognition and tracking), and C# (a programming language). You would also need some 3D models and assets for your digital objects.

Some examples of similar AR applications are:

- Snapchat: A messaging app that lets users add filters and stickers to their photos and videos.
- Hololens: A headset that projects holograms onto the user's field of view.
- Pokemon Go: A game that lets users catch virtual creatures in their real surroundings.
