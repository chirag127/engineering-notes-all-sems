One possible visual representation for a chatbot that can handle customer queries, perform simple tasks and make recommendations is a logo that reflects the chatbot's name, purpose and personality. For example, you could use a combination of text and icons that relate to natural language processing (NLP), Python, Dialogflow or TensorFlow. You could also choose a color scheme that matches your brand identity and conveys a sense of trust, professionalism and friendliness.
