Drama is a form of performing arts that involves the enactment of stories using dialogue, music, gesture, and dance. Drama is an integral part of the cultural heritage of India, as it reflects the social and religious aspects of the Indian civilization. Drama in India has a long and rich history, dating back to the Vedic era, and has developed various forms and styles over the centuries. Some of the famous traditional dramas or theatres of India are:

- Sanskrit drama: This is the classical form of drama that originated in the ancient period and reached its peak during the Gupta dynasty. Sanskrit drama is based on the principles of Natyashastra, a treatise on dramaturgy written by Bharata Muni. Sanskrit drama is characterized by its poetic language, elaborate costumes, masks, and makeup, and the use of stock characters such as the hero, heroine, villain, clown, etc. Some of the famous Sanskrit playwrights are Kalidasa, Bhasa, Sudraka, and Vishakhadatta.
- Folk drama: This is the popular form of drama that emerged in the medieval period and is performed by the rural or village communities. Folk drama is based on the local myths, legends, and folklore, and is influenced by the regional languages, dialects, and cultures. Folk drama is characterized by its simple stage, colorful costumes, lively music, and dance, and the use of satire, humor, and social commentary. Some of the famous folk dramas or theatres of India are Nautanki, Tamasha, Jatra, Bhavai, Yakshagana, etc.
- Modern drama: This is the contemporary form of drama that emerged in the colonial and post-colonial period and is influenced by the Western and European styles of theatre. Modern drama is based on the themes of realism, nationalism, social reform, and experimentation, and is performed in various Indian languages such as Hindi, Urdu, Bengali, Marathi, etc. Modern drama is characterized by its realistic dialogue, minimal stage, and props, and the use of symbolism, irony, and allegory. Some of the famous modern playwrights are Rabindranath Tagore, Mohan Rakesh, Vijay Tendulkar, Girish Karnad, Mahesh Dattani, etc.
