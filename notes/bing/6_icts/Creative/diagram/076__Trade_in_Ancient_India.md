Trade in Ancient India was a flourishing activity that involved both domestic and foreign markets. India was known for its production and export of various commodities such as spices, textiles, metals, gems, ivory, and pearls. India also imported goods such as gold, silver, wine, silk, and horses from other regions. India had trade contacts with many countries and regions such as Mesopotamia, Egypt, Persia, Greece, Rome, China, Southeast Asia, and Africa.
