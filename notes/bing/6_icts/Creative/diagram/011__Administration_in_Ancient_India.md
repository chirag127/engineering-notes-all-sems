The king was the supreme head of the legislative, executive and judiciary branches. He was assisted by ministers and councilors who advised him on matters of state and government. The officials were the administrative staff who carried out the orders of the king and the ministers. The provinces and districts were the territorial divisions of the empire or the kingdom, each headed by a governor or a chief. The local bodies were the village assemblies or the republics that had some elements of democracy and self-governance.
