Indian Cinema is one of the most popular and influential forms of performing arts in India. It combines elements of drama, music, dance, comedy, romance, action, and social commentary. Indian Cinema has a rich and diverse history that spans over a century, from the silent era to the present day. Indian Cinema reflects the cultural, linguistic, religious, and regional diversity of India, as well as the changing socio-political and economic realities of the country.
