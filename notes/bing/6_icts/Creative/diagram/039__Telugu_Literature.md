Telugu literature is the body of works written in the Telugu language, a Dravidian language spoken in India. It consists of various genres, such as poetry, epics, short stories, novels, plays, and song lyrics. Telugu literature dates back to at least the 11th century, when the Mahabharata was first translated into Telugu by Nannaya. Telugu literature has been influenced by various literary traditions, such as Sanskrit, Tamil, Persian, Urdu, and English. Telugu literature can be broadly divided into four periods: ancient, medieval, modern, and contemporary.
