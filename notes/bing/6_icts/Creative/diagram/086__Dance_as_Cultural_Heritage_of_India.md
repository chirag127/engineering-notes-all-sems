Dance is an ancient and celebrated cultural tradition in India. It reflects the rich and diverse heritage of the country, as well as the deep-seated beliefs and values of its people. There are two main types of dance in India: classical and folk. Classical dances are based on ancient texts and codified rules, and are performed with grace, precision and devotion. Folk dances are more spontaneous and expressive, and are performed during festivals, weddings and other occasions. Both types of dance have regional variations and styles, and showcase the beauty and diversity of India.
