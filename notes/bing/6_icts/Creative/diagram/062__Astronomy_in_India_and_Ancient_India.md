Astronomy in India and Ancient India

Astronomy is the study of the celestial objects and phenomena in the sky. Astronomy in India and Ancient India developed to a sophisticated level as the sages of ancient India were keen observers of the sky generation after generation. They made many contributions in the field of astronomy, such as the concept of zero, the decimal system, the heliocentric model, the calculation of eclipses, the lunar and solar calendars, and the measurement of the Earth's circumference.
