### Jain Literature in Pali,Prakrit And Sanskrit

Jain literature refers to the literature of the Jain religion, which is a vast and ancient literary tradition that was initially transmitted orally. The oldest surviving material is contained in the canonical Jain Agamas, which are written in Ardhamagadhi, a Prakrit (Middle-Indo Aryan) language. Prakrit is a form of Sanskrit, which is the oldest and most influential language of ancient India. Pali is another form of Prakrit, which was used in Magadha and became the language of Buddhism .
