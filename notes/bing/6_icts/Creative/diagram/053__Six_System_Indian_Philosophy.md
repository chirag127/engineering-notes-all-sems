The Six Systems of Indian Philosophy are Nyaya, Vaisheshika, Samkhya, Yoga, Purva Mimamsa and Uttara Mimamsa or Vedanta. Each of these systems differs in one way or the other in terms of its concepts, phenomena, laws and dogmas. They are also known as the orthodox or astika schools of Hindu philosophy, as they accept the authority of the Vedas, the ancient scriptures of India.
