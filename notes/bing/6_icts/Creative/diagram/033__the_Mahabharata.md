The Mahabharata is one of the two great Sanskrit epics of ancient India, the other being the Ramayana. It is a story of the Kurukshetra War, a conflict between two branches of the Bharata dynasty, the Kauravas and the Pandavas, over the throne of Hastinapura. The Mahabharata also contains various philosophical and ethical discourses, such as the Bhagavad Gita, and is considered a sacred text by Hindus.
