The evolution of script in India is a fascinating topic that traces the development of various writing systems in the Indian subcontinent. The most ancient script of India is the Brahmi script, which was derived from a Semitic source around the 7th century BC. The Brahmi script was used to write various languages, such as Prakrit, Sanskrit, Pali and others. The Brahmi script was also the source of many other scripts that emerged in different regions and periods of India, such as Kharosthi, Gupta, Nagari, Siddham, Sharada, Grantha, Kannada, Telugu, Tamil, Malayalam, Sinhala, Tibetan, and many more. The Brahmi script and its derivatives are collectively known as the Brahmic scripts.
