Some explanations of the diagram are:

- Ayurveda, Siddha and Unani are the three major systems of traditional medicine in India. They have different origins, theories and practices, but they share some common features such as the use of natural substances, the belief in the balance of bodily elements, and the holistic approach to health and disease .
- Ayurveda is the oldest and most widely practiced system of medicine in India. It originated from the Vedic texts and developed into a sophisticated science of life. It uses various methods such as diet, lifestyle, herbal medicines, massage, yoga, meditation and surgery to prevent and treat diseases .
- Siddha is an ancient system of medicine that developed in the Tamil-speaking region of south India. It integrated elements of Ayurveda, Rasashastra, Yoga and Tantra and uses alchemically prepared metals along with medicinal plants. It also emphasizes the spiritual aspects of healing and the attainment of perfection.
- Unani is a system of medicine that originated from the teachings of the Greek physician Hippocrates and was later influenced by the Persian and Arab scholars. It is based on the theory of four humors (blood, phlegm, yellow bile and black bile) and their balance in the body. It uses herbs, minerals, animal products and other substances to treat diseases.
- Surgery, alchemy and humorism are some of the branches or methods of the three systems of medicine. Surgery is the art and science of treating diseases or injuries by manual or instrumental operations. Alchemy is the process of transforming metals or other substances into more valuable or useful forms. Humorism is the theory that the health and temperament of a person depend on the balance of four fluids or humors in the body  .
- Materia medica, metals and herbs are some of the sources or materials used by the three systems of medicine. Materia medica is the term for the medical material or substance used for diagnosis, treatment or prevention of diseases. Metals are the chemical elements that have metallic properties such as luster, conductivity and malleability. Herbs are the plants or plant parts that have medicinal, culinary or aromatic value  .
