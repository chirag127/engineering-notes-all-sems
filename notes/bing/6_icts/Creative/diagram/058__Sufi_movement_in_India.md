### Sufi movement in India

The Sufi movement in India was a socio-religious movement that emerged in the 13th century and spread across the Indian subcontinent. The Sufis were Muslim mystics who sought to attain union with God through love, devotion and self-discipline. They followed different orders or silsilas, each with its own spiritual leader, teachings and practices. The Sufis also interacted with the local cultures and religions, especially Hinduism and Sikhism, and influenced the development of the Bhakti movement and the syncretic traditions of India.
