Engineering and Architecture as Cultural Heritage of India

India has a rich and diverse architectural heritage that reflects its history, culture, and religion. Indian architecture can be classified into several styles and traditions, such as Hindu temple architecture, Indo-Islamic architecture, South Indian architecture, and Indo-Saracenic architecture.
