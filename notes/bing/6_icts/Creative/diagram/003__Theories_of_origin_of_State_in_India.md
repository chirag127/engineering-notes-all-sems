According to the web sources, there are three main theories of origin of state in ancient India: social contract theory, divine origin theory and organic theory. The social contract theory holds that the state emerged from a voluntary agreement among the people to form a political society and surrender some of their rights to a sovereign authority. The divine origin theory holds that the state was established by God or gods and the rulers derived their legitimacy from divine sanction. The organic theory holds that the state was a natural and inevitable outcome of the social evolution and the growth of human needs and capacities.
