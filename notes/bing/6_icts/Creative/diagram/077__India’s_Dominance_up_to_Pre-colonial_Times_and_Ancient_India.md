- Indus Valley civilization: This section covers the time from the rise to the decline of the Harappan civilization, which flourished along the Indus River and its tributaries. It is marked by the presence of urban settlements, sophisticated drainage systems, standardized weights and measures, and a script that remains undeciphered.
- Vedic period: This section covers the time from the arrival of the Indo-Aryan speakers to the formation of the Mahajanapadas, or the sixteen great kingdoms. It is marked by the composition of the Vedas, the oldest scriptures of Hinduism, and the development of the caste system, the varna system, and the concept of karma and dharma.
- Mauryan empire: This section covers the time from the rise to the fall of the Mauryan empire, which was the first pan-Indian empire. It is marked by the presence of a centralized administration, a large army, a network of roads, and the spread of Buddhism under the patronage of Emperor Ashoka.
