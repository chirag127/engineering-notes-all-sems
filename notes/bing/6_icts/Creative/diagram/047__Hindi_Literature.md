Hindi literature is the literature written in the Hindi language, which includes various dialects and scripts. Hindi literature can be divided into four broad periods: Adikal (the Early Period), Bhaktikal (the Devotional Period), Ritikal (the Scholastic Period) and Adhunik (the Modern Period).
