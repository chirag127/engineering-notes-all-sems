The Ramayana is a Hindu epic that narrates the life and adventures of Rama, the prince of Kosala, and his wife Sita. The main plot of the story is the abduction of Sita by the demon king Ravana and the rescue of her by Rama with the help of his brother Lakshmana and the monkey army led by Hanuman. The story also depicts the exile of Rama from his kingdom, his moral dilemmas, his battles with various enemies, and his eventual coronation as king.
