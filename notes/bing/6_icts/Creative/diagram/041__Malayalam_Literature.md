Malayalam literature is the literature written in Malayalam, a South-Dravidian language spoken in the Indian state of Kerala. It has a rich and diverse history that spans from the 12th century to the present day. Malayalam literature can be broadly divided into four periods: classical, medieval, modern and contemporary.
