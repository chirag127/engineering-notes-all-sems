Jainism is an ancient Indian religion that teaches the path to spiritual purity and enlightenment through nonviolence and reducing harm to all living beings. Jainism has a history of at least 2,500 years and has influenced Indian culture and ethics. Jainism is one of the minority religions in India, with about 4.5 million adherents, mostly concentrated in the states of Maharashtra, Gujarat, Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh and Karnataka.
