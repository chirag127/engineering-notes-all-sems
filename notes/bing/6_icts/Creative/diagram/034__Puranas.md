The Puranas are a vast genre of Indian literature that contain stories and legends about various aspects of Hinduism, such as creation, gods, sages, dynasties, and sacred places. They are considered as the fifth Veda and are divided into six categories based on the main deity they glorify: Vishnu, Shiva, Brahma, Shakti, Agni, and Surya. Each category has one major Purana and several minor Puranas. There are 18 major Puranas and 18 minor Puranas, making a total of 36 Puranas.
