The Council of Ministers in Ancient India was a group of advisers and officials who assisted the king in governing the kingdom. The council was also known as Mantri-parishad, Mantri-mandala or Mantri-gana. The council was headed by the Prime Minister, who was also called Mantrimukhya or Mahamantri. The council members were selected based on their qualifications, character and loyalty to the king. The council had various functions, such as making policies, executing orders, supervising administration, conducting diplomacy and advising the king on various matters.
