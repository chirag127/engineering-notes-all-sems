According to the web sources, there were three main types of states in ancient India: monarchy, republic and oligarchy. Monarchy was the most common form of state, where a king or a queen ruled over a territory. Republic was a form of state where the people elected their representatives or leaders. Oligarchy was a form of state where a few powerful people or groups controlled the state.
