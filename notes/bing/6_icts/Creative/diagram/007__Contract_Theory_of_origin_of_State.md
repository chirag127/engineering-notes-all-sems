The Contract Theory of origin of State is a theory that explains how the state or the society emerged from a voluntary agreement or contract between individuals who lived in a state of nature. The state of nature was a condition where there was no authority, law, or order, and people lived according to their natural instincts and desires. The contract theory assumes that people agreed to form a state or a society to escape the inconveniences and dangers of the state of nature, and to secure their rights, liberties, and properties. The contract theory has been developed by different philosophers, such as Thomas Hobbes, John Locke, and Jean-Jacques Rousseau, who had different views on the nature of the contract, the rights and duties of the individuals and the state, and the extent of the state's power and sovereignty.
