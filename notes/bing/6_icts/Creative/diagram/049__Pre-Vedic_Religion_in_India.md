Pre-Vedic religion in India refers to the religious beliefs and practices of the people who lived in the Indus Valley civilization before the arrival of the Indo-Aryan speakers who brought the Vedic religion. The Indus Valley civilization, also known as the Harappan civilization, flourished from about 2500 BCE to 1500 BCE, and had a highly developed urban culture, trade, and writing system. The religion of the Indus Valley people is not well understood, as their script has not been deciphered, and most of the evidence comes from archaeological remains and seals. However, some general features can be inferred from the available sources.
