North Indian literature is a broad term that encompasses the literary works produced in various languages and genres by the people of North India. It includes ancient Sanskrit epics, Puranas, dramas, and poetry, as well as modern novels, short stories, essays, and poetry in Hindi, Urdu, Punjabi, Bengali, and other languages. North Indian literature reflects the diverse cultural, religious, and historical influences that have shaped the region, such as Hinduism, Islam, Buddhism, Sikhism, Jainism, colonialism, nationalism, and globalization.
