The Mystical Theory of origin of State is a theory that claims that the state is created by God or some supernatural power, and that the ruler of the state is an agent of God or a chosen one. This theory is also known as the Divine Right Theory or the Theocratic Theory. According to this theory, the state is not a product of human effort or social contract, but a divine gift or a sacred institution. The people have no right to question or resist the authority of the ruler, who is accountable only to God or the higher power.
