Kautilya’s Arthashastra is a treatise on politics, economics, military strategy, and social organization attributed to Kautilya, who was a professor at Taxila University and later the Prime Minister of the Mauryan Empire . The Arthashastra covers various topics such as the duties of the king, the structure of the administration, the management of the treasury, the conduct of foreign affairs, the welfare of the people, and the methods of warfare  .
