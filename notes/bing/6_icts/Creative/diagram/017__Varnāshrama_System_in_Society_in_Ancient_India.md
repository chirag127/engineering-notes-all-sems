#### Varnāshrama System in Society in Ancient India

The Varnāshrama system was a social and moral order that divided the Hindu society into four classes (varnas) and four stages of life (ashramas). The four varnas were Brahman (priests and teachers), Kshatriya (kings and warriors), Vaishya (traders and merchants), and Sudra (servants and laborers). The four ashramas were Brahmacharya (student), Grihastha (householder), Vanaprastha (forest dweller), and Sannyasa (renunciate). Each varna and ashrama had specific duties and obligations (dharma) that were meant to ensure the harmony and welfare of the individual and the society.
