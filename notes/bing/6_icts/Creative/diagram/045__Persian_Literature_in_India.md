### Persian Literature in India

Persian literature in India is the product of the interaction between the Persian language and culture and the Indian subcontinent. It flourished under the patronage of various Muslim dynasties, such as the Delhi Sultanate, the Bengal Sultanate, the Bahmani Sultanate, the Mughal Empire and the Sikh Empire. Persian was the official language of administration, diplomacy, literature and poetry in these states, and it influenced the local languages and cultures, such as Hindi, Urdu, Bengali, Punjabi and Sindhi.
