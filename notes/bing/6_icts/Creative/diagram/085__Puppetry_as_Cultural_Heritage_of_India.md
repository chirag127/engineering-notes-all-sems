Puppetry is a form of theatre or performance that involves the manipulation of puppets. Puppetry is one of India's most diverse cultural heritages, with a variety of regional genres. Puppetry has been used in India as a means of entertainment and a form of cultural and religious dissemination. Puppetry in India can be classified into four main types: string puppets, shadow puppets, glove puppets and rod puppets. Each type has its own distinctive features, styles and themes.
