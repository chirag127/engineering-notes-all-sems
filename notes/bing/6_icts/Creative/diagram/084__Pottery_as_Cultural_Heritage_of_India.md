### Pottery as Cultural Heritage of India

Pottery is one of the most ancient and enduring forms of Indian art, dating back to the prehistoric times. Pottery reflects the cultural diversity and richness of India, as different regions have their own styles, techniques, motifs and colours of pottery. Pottery is also a functional craft that serves various purposes, such as storing water, cooking food, holding flowers, making toys, etc. Pottery is closely linked to the rural and tribal life of India, as many potters belong to these communities and practice their craft as a family tradition.
