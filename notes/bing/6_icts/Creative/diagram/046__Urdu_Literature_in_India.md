Urdu literature is the literature written in the Urdu language, which is spoken mainly by Muslims in Pakistan and northern India. It is influenced by Persian, Arabic, Turkish, Hindi, and other languages. Urdu literature has various genres, such as poetry, prose, drama, and fiction.
